# TKA Axial Alignment

*Recon · 40 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Relevance** — pre-op planning · component insertion · ligament balancing · prosthesis selection

### Normal anatomy

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| **Distal femur** (anatomic axis vs joint line) | **~9° valgus** |
| **Femoral anatomic vs mechanical axis** | **5–7° valgus** |
| **Proximal tibia** (anatomic axis vs joint line) | **2–3° varus** (tibial joint line ~3° varus to mechanical axis) |
| **Q angle** | **11 ± 7°** |

### Mechanical axis

**Limb mechanical axis** — ✓ **vertical line from femoral head centre through centre of knee to centre of ankle** · re-established at TKA\
✗ Wrong options: 5–7° valgus between shafts (anatomic axis) · 3° varus between shafts (varus knee) · passes 1.5 cm lateral (valgus knee) · 1.5 cm medial (varus knee)

![Mechanical vs anatomic axes](../../MCQ/attachments/q870-illa-mechanical-alignment-corrected.jpg)

### Technical goals

**Goals** — restore **mechanical alignment (0°)** · restore **joint line** (lets preserved ligaments e.g. PCL function) · **balanced flexion + extension gaps** · maintain **normal Q angle**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| View | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Standing AP + lateral knee** | Joint-space narrowing · collateral insufficiency (**lateral gapping in varus**, **medial gapping in valgus**) · femorotibial subluxation · bone defects |
| **Standing full-length AP + lateral** | Accurate **valgus cut angle** when **femoral/tibial deformity** or **very tall / short** stature |
| **Flexion + extension laterals** | — |
| **Sunrise** | Patellofemoral |

## 5 Management

### Femoral alignment

**Anatomic axis (AAF)** — bisects femoral canal · sets **IM guide entry point** · IM guide runs down AAF

**Mechanical axis (MAF)** — femoral head centre → point where AAF meets **intercondylar notch** · neutral → even medial/lateral condylar load

**Valgus cut angle (VCA)** — AAF–MAF difference (**~5–7°**) · distal cut perpendicular to MAF · jig usually **6°** from AAF · **tall → VCA <5°** · **short → VCA >7°** · measure on full-length standing AP

### Tibial alignment

**Anatomic axis (AAT)** — bisects canal · IM/EM guide parallel to it

**Mechanical axis** — centre of proximal tibia → **centre of talus** · tibial cut **perpendicular to mechanical axis**

**Usually** — AAT = mechanical axis → **IM jig** fine · **tibial deformity** (axes differ) → ✓ **extramedullary guide**

### Patellofemoral alignment

**Abnormal patellar tracking** — ✓ **most common complication of TKA** (not most serious)

**Most important variable** — preserve **normal Q angle (11 ± 7°)** · Q = ASIS–patella centre vs patella centre–tibial tuberosity

**↑ Q angle errors** — **IR of femoral component** · **medialised femoral component** · **IR of tibial component** · **lateral placement of patellar button** → lateral subluxation, pain, wear, dislocation

### Joint line preservation

**Goal** — implant thickness = bone + cartilage removed · fill defects so joint line not jeopardised

| | **Elevated joint line** (>8 mm → motion problems) | **Lowered joint line** |
|---|---|---|
| Effects | **Mid-flexion instability** · PF tracking problems · **pseudo patella baja** | **Lack of full extension** · **flexion instability** |

**Valgus knee** — never elevate joint line until balanced to full extension

### Deformity & risk (peroneal palsy)

**Valgus vs varus TKA** — valgus ↑ ✓ **peroneal nerve palsy** (lateral lengthening → traction) · also instability, **recurrent valgus**, wound breakdown, patellar maltracking · lateral contracture + medial laxity · lateral condyle / plateau deficiency → hard to find epicondylar axis → **femoral IR** → maltracking\
✗ Wrong options: recurrent varus · ↑ revision · PJI · longer stay

**67 yo, old ACL tear, varus with medial tibial deficiency + subluxation** → ✓ **TKA**\
✗ Wrong options: HTO (older) · UKA (ACL deficient) · interpositional (0/12 excellent) · closing wedge

**UKA criteria** — limited unicompartmental disease · fixed **≤10° varus or ≤5° valgus** · **intact ACL** · no ML subluxation

### Extra-articular deformity (McPherson rule) (TO)

**Principle** — the **closer** a coronal extra-articular deformity is to the knee, the **greater** knee malalignment (TO)

**McPherson rule** — ✓ deformity within **distal ¼ femur or proximal ¼ tibia** and **≥20°** → **concomitant osteotomy + TKA** (TO) · closing wedge osteotomy + **long diaphyseal-fit stem** (no extra fixation) · bone cuts alone → excessive resection threatening collaterals or incompetent ligaments (TO)

**Traps (TO)** — ✗ Wrong options: farther deformity → greater malalignment · farther deformity needs bigger intra-articular cut · deformity within distal/proximal ¼ easier to correct with cuts

### Robotic-assisted TKA

**Greatest positioning precision** — ✓ **robotic assistance** (↓ outliers)\
✗ Wrong options: spinal anaesthesia · TXA · IM / EM guides

**RA-TKA literature** — ✓ **better implant position + limb alignment, no difference in long-term outcomes** · no consistent difference in complications, revision, survivorship · surgical time normal after **15–20 case** learning curve · dissatisfaction after TKA **5–20%**\
✗ Wrong options: longer time for experienced · ↑ longevity

### Patient-specific instrumentation (TO)

**PSI** — 3D model from imaging → custom cutting jigs → sizing, resection, rotation, slope · ↓ steps → **less operative time** (TO)

**Uses (TO)** — **extra-articular deformity** · **retained femoral hardware** (IM guides impossible)

**EXCEPT (TO)** — ✓ **"fewer outliers than conventional TKR"** = false · PSI equal or **less accurate**, more outliers · uses **measured resection** for rotation, ignores soft tissue → femoral rotation errors (TO)

## How it's asked

**"Mechanical axis of a normal limb?"** → **vertical line femoral head → centre of knee → centre of ankle**

**"67 yo, old ACL tear, varus with subluxation?"** → **total knee arthroplasty**

**"Valgus knee vs varus knee — ↑ risk of?"** → **peroneal nerve palsy**

**"Greatest implant positioning precision?"** → **robotic assistance**

**"RA-TKA vs conventional — true?"** → **better positioning/alignment, no difference in long-term outcomes**

**"McPherson rule?"** → **deformity in distal ¼ femur / proximal ¼ tibia ≥20° → concomitant osteotomy + TKR** (TO)

**"Patient-specific TKR — EXCEPT?"** → **fewer outliers than conventional TKR** (TO)

**"Most common complication of TKA?"** → **abnormal patellar tracking**
