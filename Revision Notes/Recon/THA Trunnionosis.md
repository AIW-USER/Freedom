# THA Trunnionosis

*Recon · 36 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **mechanically assisted crevice corrosion (MACC)** · metal ion release from corrosion at the **femoral head–neck Morse taper** → hip pain + **adverse local tissue reaction (ALTR)**

**Occurs with any bearing** — ✓ **metal-on-polyethylene** as well as MoM · relatively rare · lucency between head and cup = PE liner → not MoM\
✗ Wrong options: only in MoM

**Modularity** — lets surgeon tune length + offset · extra junction = corrosion site · **dual-modular (modular neck) stems** → two junctions (head–neck + neck–stem) → **fretting corrosion** most common failure → revise to **monoblock stem**

### Risk factors

| Factor | ↑ Corrosion | ↓ Corrosion |
|---|---|---|
| **Head size** | ✓ **Larger CoCr heads** (↑ torque on trunnion) | Smaller heads |
| **Trunnion geometry** | ✓ **Shorter, thinner** taper (flexible, ↓ interference fit, ↑ micromotion, edge loading) | **Longer, wider, more rigid** taper |
| **Materials** | ✓ **Mixed alloy** taper/bore (CoCr head on Ti stem) | Similar alloy couple · **ceramic head** |
| **Head length** | ✓ **Long (high-length) heads** | Low-length heads |
| **Stem** | **High-offset** stems (↑ fretting) · low-rigidity stems | — |
| **Assembly** | **Impaction <4,000 N** · **wet taper** · impaction off-axis | **4,000 N**, dry, in line with taper |
| **Patient** | **High BMI**, ↑ cyclical loading | — |
| **Bearing** | MoM (ions contribute to taper wear) | — |

**Cemented vs cementless fixation** — unrelated to trunnionosis

## 2 Mechanism

### Mechanism – fretting & crevice corrosion

| Type | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Galvanic** | Electrochemical electron transfer between **dissimilar metals** in conductive medium (CoCr head on Ti stem) |
| **Fretting** ✓ | **Micromotion** between two materials → wear + ion release · disrupts **passivation (oxide) layer** · ✓ **micromotion at head–neck junction → fretting** · commonest in modular necks |
| **Crevice / pitting** | Physicochemical metal–environment interaction · low-O₂ area anodic, O₂-rich cathodic · in fatigue cracks · **316L stainless steel most prone**, **titanium least** |
| **MACC** | Fretting + crevice combined → passivation layer breaks, O₂-rich fluid enters crevices, acidic layer, ↑ ions → catastrophic failure |

**Self-passivation** — Ti and CoCr form adherent oxide coat → **protective**\
✗ Wrong options: cause of symptoms

**Adhesive wear** — PE adheres to bearing → osteolysis\
✗ Wrong options: a corrosion type in trunnionosis

**Tissue response** — **ALVAL / pseudotumour** = **lymphocyte**-mediated (type IV) · same as MoM · **macrophages** → PE debris osteolysis · neutrophils → infection

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **pain + limp** commonest · delayed groin/buttock/thigh pain · "clunking"

**Exam** — palpable pseudocyst if ALTR large · **abductor weakness / Trendelenburg** (attritional abductor destruction by ARMD) — not primary avulsion

**Catastrophic failure** — sudden pop, inability to walk → **head–neck dissociation** / stem fracture at trunnion · not dislocation from failed precautions

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — AP + lateral · ✓ **may be normal** · osteolysis (calcar, GT) late · **Judet / CT** if advanced osteolysis

**MARS MRI** — **periarticular pseudotumour**, fluid collection, capsular thickening, soft-tissue destruction · **US** — fluid collection adjacent to joint

![Pseudotumour on MARS MRI](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422821-pseudotumor.jpg)

### Genes & lab markers

**Rule out infection first** — ESR, CRP, WBC · aspiration with ✓ **manual cell count** (metal debris **falsely ↑ automated count**)\
✗ Wrong options: automated count confirms

**Metal ions** — ✓ **cobalt ↑ much more than chromium** · no pathognomonic level

| | **Co : Cr ratio** |
|---|---|
| **Trunnionosis** | **≥2:1** (Orthobullets) · **~5:1** / Co **4–5×** Cr (OB explanations) |
| **MoM bearing wear** | **~1:1** (1–2:1) |

**Cobalt threshold for MACC work-up** — **>1 ng/mL (ppb)** (Orthobullets; OB: MoP with ions >1.0 µg/L) · **≥1.6 ng/mL** suggested (OB) — read the options

**Elevated serum cobalt** — ✓ expected with CoCr head + fluid collection\
✗ Wrong options: pedestal (loosening) · macrophages (PE) · HO · LLD

## 5 Management

```
Suspected trunnionosis
├─ Asymptomatic → observe
├─ Infection not excluded → manual cell count, aspiration
└─ Painful, infection excluded → single-stage revision
       ├─ Stem + cup well fixed → ceramic head + Ti sleeve + new PE liner
       └─ Taper grossly damaged / stem failed → revise stem + ceramic head + new liner
```

### Revision – ceramic head with titanium sleeve

**Indication** — painful THA + risk factors + **Co elevated > Cr**, no infection

**Technique** — **frozen sections + cultures** · **extensive synovectomy / debridement** (remove metal debris, devitalised tissue → prevents recurrence) · inspect + **clean taper** · retain vs revise stem

**Head** — ✓ **ceramic head with titanium sleeve** · **sleeve prevents ceramic fracture** on damaged taper\
✗ Wrong options: CoCr → CoCr (recurrent ALTR) · CoCr + sleeve

**Liner** — ✓ **always exchange** (embedded metal debris)

**Lowest recurrence** — ✓ **ceramic-on-polyethylene**

**Prevention** — ✓ **ceramic femoral head** eliminates CoCr–Ti interaction\
✗ Wrong options: larger head · cemented fixation · diaphyseal stem

| Scenario | Answer |
|---|---|
| Large CoCr head, black fluid, Co 12/Cr 2, manual WBC 1,500 | ✓ **ceramic head + Ti sleeve + new PE** (✗ Wrong options: revise both components · spacer · bursa injection) |
| Intra-op corroded trunnion, well-fixed parts | ✓ **ceramic head + Ti sleeve** (✗ Wrong options: I&D only (recurrence) · 2-stage · IV antibiotics · cup revision) |
| Mild trunnion damage, 44 mm CoCr head | ✓ **clean trunnion + ceramic head + Ti sleeve + liner exchange** (✗ Wrong options: remove all components) |
| Catastrophic stem/trunnion failure, cup fixed | ✓ **revise femoral component + ceramic head + new liner** (✗ Wrong options: observe · ions first · sleeve only · revise cup) |
| Modular-neck stem with ALTR | **Revise entire femoral component to monoblock** |

### Trunnionosis vs other causes

| | **Trunnionosis** | **MoM wear** | **Aseptic loosening** | **PJI** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ions | **Co ≫ Cr** | Co ≈ Cr | Normal | Normal |
| Cells | **Lymphocytes** | Lymphocytes | **Macrophages** | **Neutrophils** |
| Imaging | Pseudotumour, may be normal X-ray | Pseudotumour | Subsidence, lucency, start-up pain | Variable |
| Revision | **1-stage** head + liner | Bearing revision | Loose component | **2-stage** |

**"Head–neck dissociation at 5 yr, infection excluded — true statement?"** → **micromotion and/or electrochemical transfer of metals**

**"↑ taper corrosion associated with?"** → **mixed alloy taper and bore**

**"Sudden pop, Co 15 / Cr 3, PE liner — cause?"** → **mechanically assisted crevice corrosion**
