# TKA Periprosthetic Fracture

*Recon · 52 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Sites** — **distal femur** · **proximal tibia** · **patella** · overall prevalence **0.39%** (OB)

**Decision drivers** — **fracture location** · **implant stability** · **bone stock** · comorbidity · implant design (CR / open-box PS / closed-box PS / stemmed)

### Risk factors & intraoperative fractures

| Category | Risk factors |
|---|---|
| **Bone quality** | **Osteopenia** · age **>70** · **female** · **chronic steroids** · **RA** · stress-shielding |
| **Stress risers** | Screw holes · **osteolysis** · stiffness · **revision TKA** · navigation pin sites |
| **Neurological** | **Epilepsy** · **Parkinson's** · cerebellar ataxia · myasthenia · **polio** · CP |
| **Femur-specific** | Modulus mismatch · **rotationally constrained** components · **anterior notching** — debatable |
| **Not risk factors** | **Male gender** · **anterior femoral notching** (TO: controversial; >3 mm ↓ strength in biomechanics, not clinically) |

**Notching conflict** — **not a risk factor** (TO) · **listed as risk** (OB210125, OB220387) · **debatable** (Orthobullets) — read the options

**Intra-op fractures** — ✓ **medial femoral condyle commonest** (incomplete sagittal cut between notch and MFC) → then **lateral condyle → supracondylar femur → proximal tibia** · ↑ in **PS (box cut)** · **femoral > tibial** · patella rare · most need **fixation** (wires/screws/plates) · occur in exposure/bone prep > trialing

**Navigation / robotics** — ✓ **femoral shaft fracture through tracker pin sites** = complication unique to navigation (TO) · ✓ **transcortical / bicortical diaphyseal, large pins** ↑ risk · safer: **two 3 mm unicortical metaphyseal pins**, self-drilling/self-tapping · fractures within ~**3 months**; thermal necrosis · 5 mm pin removes **2.78×** bone of 3 mm · drill holes ↓ bending strength up to **40%**\
✗ Wrong options: PS implant risk

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-rays** — diagnosis · **CT** — bone stock, **loosening**, planning · identify **CR (no box, notch recess) vs PS (box)** on X-ray

**Supracondylar periprosthetic femur** — within **15 cm** of joint line or **5 cm** of top of a stemmed component

**Acceptable outcome** — ROM **>90°** · shortening **<2 cm** · V/V **<5°** · F/E malalignment **<10°**

### Lewis & Rorabeck (distal femur) — most used

| Type | Displacement | Component |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Non-displaced** | Intact |
| **II** | **Displaced** | **Intact** |
| **III** | Displaced | **Loose / failing** |

### Su (distal femur) (TO)

| Type | Location | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Proximal** to femoral component | **Retrograde or antegrade IMN** / plate |
| **II** | Starts at **proximal edge of component**, extends proximally | **Retrograde IMN** or fixed-angle plate |
| **III** | Any part **distal to upper edge of anterior flange** | **Fixed-angle/locked plate** or **revision / DFR** if poor bone · no rIMN |

**Su used for** — ✓ **periprosthetic femur** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: patella · humerus · tibia

### Other femoral classifications

| System | Types |
|---|---|
| **Neer (1967)** | I non-displaced (<5 mm/<5°) · II >1 cm (IIa lateral, IIb medial shaft displacement) · III displaced + comminuted |
| **DiGioia & Rubash (1991)** | I extra-articular non-displaced · II extra-articular displaced (>5 mm/>5°) · III lost cortical contact / angulated 10°, ± intercondylar/T |
| **Chen (1994)** | I non-displaced · II displaced/comminuted |

### Felix (tibia)

| Type | Location | Subtype |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Tibial plateau** (implant interface) | **A** stable component |
| **II** | **Adjacent to stem** (meta-diaphysis) | **B** **loose** component |
| **III** | **Shaft distal** to component | **C** **intra-operative** |
| **IV** | **Tibial tubercle** | — |

**Felix treatment (TO)** — **IB, IIB → revision** (most postoperative I/II are B) · IIA, IIIA, IVA → usual fracture principles · IC → intra-op fixation · IIC → graft / immobilise / restricted WB · IIIC → conventional

**Unified Classification System (TO)** — A apophyseal · B implant bed · C clear of bed · D between two arthroplasties · E two bones, one arthroplasty · F faces unresurfaced implant

### Patellar classifications

| **Ortiguera & Berry** | EM | Component | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | Intact | **Stable** | **Cast/brace in extension** |

## 5 Management

```
Periprosthetic distal femur fracture
├─ Non-displaced + stable component → cast / brace
├─ Displaced + stable component → fix
│      (rIMN if CR / open box + Su I–II; locked plate if closed box, stem, Su III)
└─ Loose component / poor bone → revision (long stem) or DFR
       └─ Elderly, very distal, comminuted → distal femoral replacement
```

### Supracondylar femur – locked plating vs retrograde nail

**Retrograde IMN** — needs **CR or open-box PS** (8–12 mm recess) · Su I/II · ≥**2 distal interlocks**, end cap · insert **deep** (no patellar abrasion) · not with **closed-box PS**, **stemmed** femur, loose component, ipsilateral long antegrade/cephalomedullary nail or hip stem (stress riser), insufficient distal bone · not Su III (distal to flange) · ~**15% revision** (nonunion)

**rIMN malunion** — femoral component forces **posterior start point** → ✓ **extension (recurvatum) deformity** (OB) · **valgus + recurvatum** (TO) · distal **10° bend** nails improve alignment · hyperextension tolerated, valgus poorly tolerated (TO)

**Locked plate** — polyaxial screws around lugs/stems · independent of design + distal bone · **MIPO/submuscular** · not non-locked condylar buttress (no varus resistance; nonunion up to **50%**) · blade plate/DCS hard around PS

| Plate vs rIMN | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Union / nonunion** | ✓ **Equivalent** · similar secondary surgery |
| **Malunion** | **↑ with IMN** (plating ↓ malunion) · ✓ **IMN higher malunion** than ORIF or DFR |
| **Weight bearing** | **IMN faster** to full WB |
| **Soft tissue** | IMN less injury |

**Nonunion risk** — ✓ **extensile lateral approach > submuscular** · MIPO ↑ malunion (Orthobullets)\
✗ Wrong options: no BMP (not approved) · no lag/cerclage · plate vs antegrade nail · titanium vs steel

**Commonest reason ORIF fails** — ✓ **nonunion (persistent fracture line)** (~10% revised) → revision ORIF or DFR\
✗ Wrong options: aseptic loosening · PE wear · infection · dislocation

**Nail + lateral plate vs dual plating** — ✓ **earlier weight bearing** · dual plating stiffer (axial + torsion) + more medial dissection · infection same

![Posterior start point — extension deformity](../../MCQ/attachments/q218932-illc-illustration-c.jpg)

| Scenario | Answer |
|---|---|
| Far-distal fracture, well-fixed CR, community ambulator | ✓ **distal femoral locking plate** (✗ Wrong options: cast · buttress plate · DFR · antegrade nail) |
| PS TKA, displaced fracture just above, osteopenic, well fixed | ✓ **MIPO locked plating** (✗ Wrong options: cast/brace · rIMN (PS) · DFR in 62 yo) |
| **Su I, CR knee, well fixed** | ✓ **retrograde IMN** (✗ Wrong options: ex-fix · recon plate · stem revision · antegrade) |
| **Stemmed revision femur, well fixed** | ✓ **lateral locked plate** (✗ Wrong options: rIMN/antegrade (stem blocks)) |
| **PS box, 5 days post-op, well fixed** | ✓ **PS design → lateral locking plate** |
| Bridge plating vs rIMN | ✓ **similar nonunion, ↓ malunion** |

### Loose components or poor bone – revision / distal femoral replacement

**Loose femoral component** → ✓ **revision TKA** (long stem or DFR) · loose + medial condyle fracture → **ORIF + femoral revision**\
✗ Wrong options: nail · plate · cast · ex-fix

**DFR indications** — **elderly / sedentary** · **loose or malpositioned** component · **poor bone stock** · very distal / comminuted (Su III) · requires **rotating hinge** (collaterals lost) · tibia often revised too

**DFR vs ORIF** — ✓ **similar complication rate** · similar reoperation, function, return to ambulatory status · **immediate weight bearing** · shorter surgery · ROM similar or **slightly less** with DFR\
✗ Wrong options: better outcomes · higher reoperation · greater ROM

**84 yo walker user, very distal comminuted** → ✓ **DFR** (best chance ambulatory at 1 yr)\
✗ Wrong options: plate · dual plate · nail · ex-fix (seeding risk)

**Distal to flange, poor bone, medial condyle free** → ✓ **revision arthroplasty only (DFR)**

### Tibial periprosthetic fracture

**Incidence** — **0.4–1.7%** · risks: prior **TTO**, **loosening**, malposition, **long-stemmed** tibial components

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Non-displaced, stable | Cast/brace (non-WB) |
| Unstable fracture, stable component | **ORIF** / IMN |
| ✓ **Displaced + loose tibial component** (Felix IIB) | **Revision with long stem bypassing fracture** (TO) (✗ Wrong options: locking plate, LCP, LISS, ex-fix, IMN, brace) |
| Loose + poor bone, prior pain | ✓ **ORIF + revision TKA** (✗ Wrong options: cast · strut graft · IMN · proximal tibial replacement (salvage)) |

### Patella fractures

**Incidence** — resurfaced **0.2–21%** (Orthobullets) · **0.68–5.2%** (OB) · unresurfaced **0.05%**

**Risks** — patellar ON · asymmetric / over-resection · central **single peg** · **uncemented** · **metal backing** · **inset** component · lateral release · ↑ BMI

| Scenario | Answer |
|---|---|
| Displaced, **no SLR**, well-fixed component, good bone | ✓ **ORIF** (✗ Wrong options: patellectomy · allograft · component revision · brace) |
| Transverse, **active extension intact**, well fixed | ✓ **long-leg cast / brace in extension** (✗ Wrong options: immediate ROM · component removal · ORIF) |
| Displaced mid-patella, no SLR | ✓ **ORIF** (✗ Wrong options: hinged ROM · 2 wk immobilise · 6 wk cast (lag) · partial patellectomy (poles/comminution)) |
| Type II at 10 wk, stable button, good bone | ✓ **ORIF** (± mesh augmentation) |
| Intact EM, stable component | ✓ **knee brace locked in extension / cylinder cast** |
| Non-displaced transverse, SLR intact | ✓ **immobilise in extension 4–6 wk** |

**Type II/III outcomes** — **50% complications**, **20–40% reoperation** · **total patellectomy** = salvage, **>50% loss of extension strength**

### Extensor mechanism failure

**Chronic EM disruption after revision (6 mo, wide pole diastasis)** → ✓ **extensor mechanism allograft**\
✗ Wrong options: fusion (salvage) · patellectomy + repair · ORIF · cast
