# THA Rehabilitation

*Recon · 31 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Phases

**Team** — surgeon, PT, OT, case manager, nursing, patient + family

**Phases** — **pre-op teaching** → **inpatient acute** → **inpatient extended (rehab/SNF)** → **outpatient**

## 5 Management

### Preoperative teaching

**Pre-op PT** — ✓ **not shown to improve post-op outcomes**

**Hip precautions** — most useful if taught **before** surgery · hips dislocate in the **direction of the approach** (if implants well positioned)

| Approach | Avoid |
|---|---|
| **Posterolateral** | **Flexion >90°**, **extreme IR**, **adduction past midline** |
| **Anterolateral** | **Extension**, **extreme ER**, adduction past midline |
| **Direct anterior (Smith-Petersen)** | **Bridging**, **extension**, **extreme ER**, adduction past midline |

**Told to avoid extension, adduction, ER** → ✓ **Smith-Petersen (anterior)**\
✗ Wrong options: Southern/Kocher-Langenbeck (posterior) · Stoppa · Pfannenstiel

![Posterior hip precautions](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob5036-posterior-hip-precautions.jpg)

### Inpatient acute care

**Pain** — pre-op NSAIDs (± opioids) · **regional anaesthesia preferred over GA** · periarticular multimodal injection · **multimodal oral therapy = gold standard**

**PT progression** — sit upright → walker gait, out of bed → transfers, normal gait → independence

**Discharge home** — independent ambulation with aid · independent transfers · independent ADLs · stairs with supervision · home help

### Extended care

**Earlier discharge to rehab** → **improved outcomes** · same discharge criteria

### Outpatient – return to activity

**Sport** — **low-impact** preferred · **golf handicap barely changes after THA** (worsens after TKA) · **high-impact ↑ revision in patients <55**

**Driving** — **right THA 3–4 weeks** · **left THA <3–4 weeks** · ✓ **reaction time back to pre-op at 4–6 weeks** (OB answer: **4 weeks**) · influenced by side, lengthening, rehab, narcotics\
✗ Wrong options: 1–2 weeks · 2–3 months

**Work** — **within a month** if not manual

## How it's asked

**"When does driving reaction time return after THA?"** → **4 weeks (4–6 weeks)**

**"Avoid extension, adduction, ER — approach?"** → **Smith-Petersen**

**"Posterior approach precautions?"** → **no flexion >90°, no IR, no adduction past midline**

**"Does pre-op PT improve outcome?"** → **no**

**"High-impact sport after THA in <55 yr?"** → **↑ revision**
