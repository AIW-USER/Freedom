# THA Approaches

*Recon · 20 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Choice of approach

**Dictated by** — surgeon preference · prior incisions · obesity · dislocation risk · implant · deformity

**Standard** — direct anterior · anterolateral · direct lateral · posterolateral · **extensile** — trochanteric osteotomy · **MIS** variants

**Original Charnley THA** — ✓ **anterolateral** approach, supine, **greater trochanter osteotomy**, anterior dislocation (TO)

| **Anterolateral (Watson-Jones)** | **TFL – gluteus medius** (both SGN) | — | No — intermuscular only |
| **Direct lateral (Hardinge)** | **Splits gluteus medius + vastus lateralis** | — | No |

**Anterolateral approach (Smith-Petersen variant, TO)** — "7"-shaped incision · flap of **TFL, gluteus minimus, anterior medius**

**Harris (TO)** — U-incision + GT osteotomy · **Gibson** — anterior border of maximus, cut medius/minimus, dislocate **anteriorly** · **modified Gibson (Marcy–Fletcher)** — cut short ERs, dislocate **posteriorly** · **Osborn / Moore** — split maximus, cut short ERs, posterior capsule

## 5 Management

### Direct anterior – interval & LFCN injury

**Interval** — ✓ **TFL and sartorius** superficially · ✓ **internervous plane femoral + superior gluteal** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: sartorius–maximus · minimus–rectus · rectus–sartorius · TFL–rectus

**Bleeding in TFL–sartorius interval** — ✓ **ascending branch of LFCA** (~5 cm below hip) → ligate/coagulate\
✗ Wrong options: SGA · femoral · profunda · MFCA

**LFCN** — **L2–L3**, under inguinal ligament, pierces fascia **~1 cm distal to ASIS** near TFL–sartorius interval · ✓ **structure at greatest risk** (✗ Wrong options: femoral nerve/artery/vein (medial) · MFCA (posterior to neck)) · ✓ **most common complication** of anterior approach (81% altered thigh sensation, no functional loss) · ✓ **common, improves over time, no functional deficit** (✗ Wrong options: uncommon · intermediate femoral cutaneous)

**Fascial incision lateral, over TFL muscle belly** → ✓ **minimize LFCN injury**\
✗ Wrong options: protects femoral bundle · trajectory · PJI

**Advantages** — **↓ dislocation vs posterior** (~**0.5%**, regardless of capsule repair) · abductors intact · **no gait difference at 3 months** vs other approaches · unproven: less muscle damage/pain, faster recovery

**Disadvantages** — **steep learning curve (>100 cases)** · **wound infection ↑ in obese with large pannus** · **femoral nerve injury** from anterior retractor · femoral exposure difficult (special table) · **LFCN paraesthesia** · **↑ intraop fracture**

### Direct anterior – femoral complications

**Highest femoral-sided complication risk** — ✓ **direct anterior** (intraop **femur fracture, subsidence**, **femoral-sided revision for loosening**) · interval ✓ **femoral–superior gluteal plane** · superficial ✓ **TFL–sartorius**

**Distractors** — direct lateral = medius/vastus split → **Trendelenburg, HO** · posterior = maximus split → **dislocation** · anterolateral = TFL–medius → **limp**, lower dislocation

### Anterolateral (Watson-Jones)

**Interval** — ✓ **TFL (anterior) and gluteus medius (posterior)** (TO) · start midway ASIS–GT, expose SGN proximally · capsule incised along anterosuperior neck

**Pros/cons** — **lower dislocation** than posterior · **violates abductors → limp** · retractor through iliopsoas → **femoral nerve** risk, not instability

### Direct lateral (Hardinge) – abductor injury

**Detached from femur** — ✓ **vastus lateralis + gluteus medius (anterior ⅓–½) + gluteus minimus** (+ ligament of Bigelow)\
✗ Wrong options: psoas/sartorius · piriformis/obturator internus (posterior) · maximus/TFL · minimus/rectus

**Superior gluteal nerve** — runs between medius and minimus **4–6 cm above GT tip** · split ≤**4–5 cm** above GT (TO: **≤5 cm** from tip, or **≥2 cm** above acetabular lip — Frndak)

**Persistent limp + fatty atrophy of anterior abductors on CT** → ✓ **split >5 cm above GT tip** (SGN injury)\
✗ Wrong options: GT fracture · retractor (femoral nerve) · PT · offset

**Testing suspected SGN injury after extended incision (TO)** → ✓ **stand on operated limb** (Trendelenburg)\
✗ Wrong options: stand on other limb · ankle DF/PF

**Pros** — **lower dislocation** than posterior (OB: **lowest**) · anterior + posterior access without osteotomy · **cons** — abductor violation → **limp** · **HO common**

### Posterolateral (Moore / Southern)

**Most common** for primary + revision · abductors intact · excellent femur + acetabulum exposure · extensile

**Dislocation** — higher than anterior · ↓ with **capsule + short ER repair** · ✓ **Moore approach with capsulectomy = greatest dislocation risk** · overall dislocation **1–3%**\
✗ Wrong options: Smith-Petersen failed capsule repair · Watson-Jones with psoas penetration · Hardinge cup 40° · anterior with 15° anteversion

### Minimally invasive approach

**Mini-incision (≤10 cm)** — only proven advantage ✓ **better cosmesis**\
✗ Wrong options: pain · transfusion · LOS · analgesia

**Intraop femoral fracture during MIS** — ✓ **convert to extensile approach** to visualize + reduce · bypass by **2 cortical diameters** with long stem\
✗ Wrong options: limit WB alone · switch to cement · delay · autograft

**SuperPATH (TO)** — supercapsular percutaneously assisted · **modified posterolateral MIS** · interval ✓ **gluteus minimus–piriformis** · medius + minimus retracted anteriorly · **short ERs + ITB spared**, **no dislocation** · femoral canal prepared through **piriformis fossa** in situ · **accessory percutaneous portal for acetabulum** · ↓ LOS, blood loss, readmission\
✗ Wrong options: minimus–medius (the EXCEPT answer)

![SuperPATH interval](../../MCQ/attachments/TO21733194-1.png)

### Extensile – trochanteric osteotomy

**Types** — standard · **trochanteric slide** · **extended trochanteric osteotomy (ETO)**

**Pros** — excellent acetabular exposure · component removal · difficult primary/revision

**Complications** — nonunion · HO · trochanteric bursitis · abductor weakness · ✓ **fracture of osteotomy fragment = most common** · **ETO needs a diaphyseal-engaging stem**

### Surgical hip dislocation (Ganz) – TO

**Preserve** — ✓ **deep branch of MFCA** (protected by intact **obturator externus**) · **posterior approach → trochanteric flip osteotomy → anterior (Z) arthrotomy → anterior dislocation** ✓\
✗ Wrong options: posterior arthrotomy/dislocation

## X vs Y

| | **Direct anterior** | **Anterolateral** | **Direct lateral** | **Posterior** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Abductors | Intact | **Violated** | **Violated** | Intact |
| Dislocation | **Low (~0.5%)** | Low | **Lowest** (OB) | **Highest** |
| Signature complication | **LFCN**, femoral fracture/loosening, obese wound | Limp | **Limp, HO, SGN** | **Dislocation**, sciatic |
