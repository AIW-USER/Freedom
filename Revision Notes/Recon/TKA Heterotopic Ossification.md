# TKA Heterotopic Ossification

*Recon · 43 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — bone in soft tissues (not periosteum) after surgery, with or without a precipitating event · distinct from **myositis ossificans** (post-traumatic muscle injury)

**Incidence** — **1–42%** (Orthobullets) · **15–50%** (OB) — read the options · **<1%** clinically relevant · much **less** frequent / problematic than after THA

### Risk factors

| ✓ Risk factor | Not a risk factor |
|---|---|
| **Hypertrophic arthrosis** (pre-op osteophytes, often post-traumatic) | ✓ **Incorrect component sizing** |
| **Male gender** | **Valgus deformity** |
| **Obesity** | — |
| **History of trauma** | — |
| **Periosteal stripping** of anterior femur | — |
| Post-op **effusion / hematoma** | — |
| Post-op **forced manipulation** | — |

## 3 Presentation

**Usually asymptomatic** · rarely: ↓ motion, pain, **quadriceps snapping**, **PF instability**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**AP + lateral** — ossification along **anterior distal femur** and in **quadriceps mechanism** · visible **3–4 wk** · matures **1–2 yr**

### Furia & Pellegrini classification

| Type | Location | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **IA** | Island in **suprapatellar** soft tissue | **≤5 cm** |
| **IB** | Island in suprapatellar soft tissue | **>5 cm** |
| **IIA** | **Contiguous with anterior distal femur** | **≤5 cm** |
| **IIB** | Contiguous with anterior distal femur | **>5 cm** |

## 5 Management

**Observe** — most cases (rarely affects outcome)

**Excise** — **quadriceps snapping** · **PF tracking difficulty** · **PF instability**

**Prophylaxis** — **radiation** + **NSAIDs** used in high-risk **THA** · no evidence in TKA

## How it's asked

**"HO risk factors — EXCEPT?"** → **incorrect sizing of femoral/tibial components**

**"Indication for HO excision after TKA?"** → **quadriceps snapping / PF tracking problems / PF instability**
