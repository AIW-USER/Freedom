## 6 Complications

### Heterotopic ossification

**Frequency** — common **radiographic** finding (first year), rarely functional · **~15%** clinically significant stiffness (Brooker III/IV) · more after THA than TKA · 2–5% despite prophylaxis

**Biology** — mesenchymal cells in muscle/fascia → osteoblasts · starts **within 16 h** of insult, matures over >1 yr into lamellar bone

**Risk factors** — prolonged surgery · excessive soft-tissue handling · **hypertrophic OA** · ✓ **male** · ✓ **ankylosing spondylitis** · **prior HO** (other site or **contralateral hip**) · DISH · traumatic brain injury

| Brooker grade | Clinical significance |
|---|---|
| **I–II** | Usually asymptomatic, inconsequential (I not a pain source) |
| **III–IV** | Clinically significant stiffness, may need excision |

### HO prophylaxis

| Method | Regimen |
|---|---|
| **NSAIDs** | **Indomethacin** classic (other NSAIDs equivalent) · **7–21 days** (≥10 days; longer no benefit) · not 2 days · not in renal insufficiency/warfarin |
| **Radiation** | **600–800 cGy** (700–800 as effective as higher) · Orthobullets: within **24–48 h post-op** · OB: **within 24 h pre-op** or **72 h post-op** · Iorio/Healy: **≤6 h pre-op** or **≤4 days post-op** · pre-op = post-op efficacy (73% vs 76%) |
| **Not effective** | Drains · **etidronate (EHDP)** — delays mineralization only · radiation 1 week before surgery · 1,500 cGy (unnecessary) |

**Answers** — prior HO + renal insufficiency + warfarin → ✓ **800 cGy pre-op on morning of surgery** (✗ Wrong options: indomethacin 3–6 wk · 400 cGy day 2 · no treatment) · ankylosing spondylitis → ✓ **pre-op radiation 700 cGy** (✗ Wrong options: sciatic monitoring · 1,500 cGy · CPM · soft collar) · contralateral HO → ✓ **radiation 8 h post-op** (✗ Wrong options: indomethacin 2 days · drains · EHDP · RT 1 week pre-op)

**Prophylaxis candidates (Purcell–Lachiewicz)** — bilateral hypertrophic OA · OA + lumbar **DISH** · **AS** · HO after contralateral hip surgery

### HO treatment

**Early Brooker III at 6 weeks, asymptomatic** → ✓ **observe, repeat X-ray in 6 weeks**\
✗ Wrong options: indomethacin (too late) · RT (too late) · excision at 10 wk (immature, ↑ recurrence)

**Formed HO** — prophylaxis ineffective · ✓ **only surgical excision** eradicates

**Excision** — severe loss of motion · **wait ≥6 months** for maturation + capsule · confirm maturity: stable serial films, normal bone scan uptake, **normalized serum ALP** (non-specific) · **pre-op CT** for resection planning · **perioperative RT or NSAIDs** after excision ✓ (mature grade III/IV + severe limitation → **excision + post-op irradiation**) · instability after excision common → have **constrained liners** available · protect sciatic nerve

**Stiffness at 6 months with HO** → ✓ **first check ALP, ESR, CRP** (exclude PJI, assess maturity)\
✗ Wrong options: late indomethacin/RT · immediate excision · excision + constrained revision

### Squeaking

**Definition** — high-pitched audible sound with hip motion (e.g. extension from flexion on stairs)

**Incidence** — **CoC 0.5–10%** · **MoM 4–5%** · revision for squeaking **0.5%**

**Causes** — **impingement** · **edge loading** · **component malposition** · **loss of fluid-film lubrication** · **third-body particles** · **thin flexible titanium stem** · ✓ **NOT subclinical infection**

**Painless squeak, well-positioned implants** → ✓ **observe**\
✗ Wrong options: revise to PE · change cup abduction

**10-yr alumina CoC results** — ✓ **low osteolysis, squeaking and head fracture** (not eliminated)\
✗ Wrong options: same osteolysis as MoP · noisy hips ↑ osteolysis · fractures eliminated

### Metal wear & ARMD

**Pseudotumour risk (MoM)** — ✓ **edge loading** (most significant) · double heat treatment · low carbon content · **female**, **dysplasia**\
✗ Wrong options: obesity · age >65

**Large proximal femoral osteolysis, fixed MoM implants, normal ESR/CRP** → ✓ **adverse reaction to metal debris**\
✗ Wrong options: loosening · metastasis · PJI

### Stem fracture

**Mechanism** — ✓ **cantilever bending** fatigue: **loss of proximal (calcar) support + solid distal fixation** → **middle-third** fracture · modular fluted stems fracture at **modular junction** with poor proximal bone · body weight + cyclic stress\
✗ Wrong options: torsion · pistoning · subsidence

### Postoperative anaemia

**Best predictor of transfusion** — **low preoperative haemoglobin** · prevention **TXA** · transfuse at **Hb 7–8**

### Medical complications

**Mortality (TO)** — primary THR **~1% at 90 days** · revision THR **2.5–3× higher**

**Timing (TO)** — early: **DVT (most common)**, dislocation, infection · late: osteolysis, aseptic loosening, periprosthetic fracture · **IL-6 > CRP > procalcitonin** as infection marker (TO)

**Intraoperative death** — fat + marrow embolism during femoral preparation/cementing · lung histology may show ✓ **methylmethacrylate cement in pulmonary capillaries**\
✗ Wrong options: thrombus PE · infarct · granuloma · amyloid

**Ileus** — ✓ **~1%** of TJA · **THA > TKA** · risk: **older age, male, prior abdominal surgery**

**Ogilvie syndrome** (acute colonic pseudo-obstruction) — elderly, male, narcotics, hip arthroplasty · distended non-tender abdomen POD 3 → ✓ **abdominal radiograph** first\
✗ Wrong options: occult blood · NG lavage · US · CT

### Femoral nerve palsy (TO)

**Presentation** — weak SLR, **cannot actively extend knee** next day

**Causes** — retractor **anterior to iliopsoas** · **anterior capsulotomy/capsulectomy** · femoral retraction · **extruded cement** (acetabular pressurization) · **iliacus haematoma**

**NOT a cause** — ✓ **lengthening >3 cm** → **sciatic (peroneal)** palsy instead

### Postoperative shortening (TO)

**Shortening on POD 1** — ✓ **low femoral neck-shaft angle** (↓ vertical offset)\
✗ Wrong options: +8 head (lengthens) · high NSA (lengthens) · undersized stem (subsides later with ambulation)

### Persistent pain workup

**No improvement after THA, groin → medial thigh → past knee, stairs/uphill, normal labs** → ✓ **image lumbar spine** (stenosis) · stenosis ↑ nerve palsy risk · operative order debated (spine first ↑ hip dislocation)\
✗ Wrong options: aspiration · EMG first · bone scan · CT

### Osteolysis revision

**Failed cup with bone loss** → ✓ **cementless porous-coated acetabular revision** ± structural graft\
✗ Wrong options: bipolar · impaction grafting both sides · cemented jumbo cup · protrusio ring (Paprosky III only)

### Disparities

**Black non-Hispanic + Hispanic vs white non-Hispanic** — ↑ **LOS**, ↑ 30-day readmission, ↑ non-home discharge · ✓ **WNH shortest LOS**

## X vs Y

| | **HO prophylaxis** | **HO treatment** |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Peri-op (RT ±24–72 h, NSAIDs 7–21 d) | **≥6 months**, mature |
| Tool | Indomethacin / 600–800 cGy | **Excision** + RT/NSAID |

| | **Femoral nerve palsy** | **Sciatic (peroneal) palsy** |
|---|---|---|
| Deficit | Quadriceps (SLR, knee extension) | Foot drop, EHL |
| Cause | Anterior retractor, cement, iliacus haematoma | **Lengthening**, posterior approach, haematoma |
