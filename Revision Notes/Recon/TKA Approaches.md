# TKA Approaches

*Recon · 38 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Approach dictated by** — surgeon preference · **prior incisions** · degree of deformity · **patella baja** · obesity

**Simple primary** — medial parapatellar · midvastus · subvastus · minimally invasive

**Complex primary / revision** — medial parapatellar · **quadriceps snip** · **V-Y turndown** · **tibial tubercle osteotomy (TTO)**

**Outcome** — ✓ **no midterm/long-term difference** in function between medial parapatellar, midvastus, subvastus · similar **blood loss** · best long-term practice → ✓ **approach the surgeon is most comfortable with** · subvastus had higher mean ROM at 6 mo only (meta-analysis, 60 RCTs)\
✗ Wrong options: midvastus · mini-medial parapatellar · subvastus · standard MPP

### Prior incisions

**Skin blood supply** — mainly from **medial side** (saphenous a. + medial genicular perforators) → **lateral flap** at greatest necrosis risk

**Multiple longitudinal incisions** — ✓ use the **most lateral** (TO) (or most recently healed)\
✗ Wrong options: medial · transverse · new midline

**Dual-plated plateau fracture with medial + lateral scars** — ✓ **lateral one** (TO) · preserve medial flap (perforators) · avoid excess lateral subcutaneous dissection (TO)

**Single previous scar** — reuse if suitable · **transverse scar** → cross at **right angles** (perpendicular), avoid acute angles

**Skin bridge** — **≥5 cm** if old scar cannot be used (TO) · exact length controversial (Orthobullets)

**Old transverse incision years earlier** — standard anterior longitudinal incision usually fine (TO)

**Post-traumatic (plateau ORIF) TKA** — risk of TKA **5× controls (7.3% at 10 yr)** · remove only hardware that interferes with prosthesis · stiff knees → snip, V-Y, TTO, lateral release · bone loss → cement, graft, metal wedges · computer assistance helpful (TO)

## 5 Management

### Primary approaches

| Approach | Technique | Advantages | Disadvantages / relative CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Medial parapatellar** | Straight midline incision, arthrotomy medial to patella | Familiar · **excellent exposure** · easy patellar eversion | Medial capsular repair failure · lateral patellar subluxation · indirect lateral retinaculum access · **patellar circulation risk if lateral release added** |
| **Lateral parapatellar** | Lateral arthrotomy | **Fixed valgus** · preserves patellar blood supply · prevents lateral subluxation · direct lateral access | Technically demanding · **difficult medial patellar eversion** · may need TTO |
| **Midvastus** | Through **VMO muscle belly** · spares VMO insertion on quad tendon | Possibly faster rehab · better patellar tracking | Less extensile · hard in obese, flexion contracture · ✓ **partial VMO denervation** · CI: ROM <80°, obese, hypertrophic OA, previous HTO |
| **Subvastus** | **Vastus medialis belly lifted off intermuscular septum** (incision not through muscle) | **Patellar vascularity preserved** · extensor mechanism intact · minimal lateral release · accelerated rehab | ✓ **Least extensile** · CI: **revision**, large quads, previous HTO, obese, previous parapatellar arthrotomy |
| **Minimally invasive / quad-sparing** | Special instruments | — | Technically demanding · **↑ component malposition** · longer tourniquet time |

![Medial parapatellar, quad-sparing, subvastus, midvastus](../../MCQ/attachments/q219773-illa-median-para.jpg)

**Exposure tip (TO)** — always raise the **medial periosteal sleeve** + remove **patellar fat pad** · subvastus / midvastus not for **obese or revision** · **rectus snip** for stiff / revision knees

**Ankylosed knee (TO)** — needs **intact extensor mechanism** (else mesh / allograft) · **flexion > 90° hard to achieve**, high complication rate, poor prognosis

### Vastus approaches & denervation

**Greatest quadriceps denervation potential** — ✓ **midvastus** (abnormal EMG · blunt interval dissection recovers better than sharp)\
✗ Wrong options: subvastus · quad sparing · MPP · TTO

**Subvastus EXCEPT (TO)** — ✓ **"spares quad tendon, carried through belly of VMO"** = false (that is **midvastus**) · true: VM belly lifted off septum · accelerated rehab · not for revision (TO)

**Picture with incision not through muscle substance** → ✓ **subvastus** (TO)

![Subvastus — incision below VM, not through muscle](../../MCQ/attachments/TO17106215-2.jpg)

### Minimally invasive & quadriceps-sparing approaches

**Outcomes** — ✓ **no clinically significant improvement in PROs**, gait or quad strength\
✗ Wrong options: quickest rehab · normal gait sooner · shorter tourniquet / less pain (longer tourniquet) · no malalignment risk

**Higher component malposition vs MPP** — ✓ **quadriceps sparing**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral parapatellar · midvastus · snip · V-Y

**Mini-subvastus** — preserves all four quadriceps attachments to patella

**Convert to standard parapatellar when** — ✓ **jig alignment difficult / incision too small** · **patellar tendon starts peeling off tubercle**\
✗ Wrong options: subvastus · TTO · two-incision · ligament release

### Lateral parapatellar approach for valgus knee (TO)

**Approach of choice** — **fixed valgus** · lateral release integral to approach · preserves **medial** patellar blood supply · medial displacement of extensor mechanism internally rotates tibia → brings **posterolateral corner** into view · optimises PF tracking · tubercle can be medialised (TO)

**Medial arthrotomy + lateral release** — significant impairment of extensor mechanism blood supply (TO)

**EXCEPT (TO)** — ✓ **"more patellar necrosis than medial arthrotomy"** = false · true: less maltracking · difficult exposure (patellar eversion, may need TTO) · **fixed varus = contraindication** (TO)

### Extensile exposure in revision

**First steps** — extend medial release posteriorly (tibial ER → lateralises extensor mechanism) · ✓ **scar debridement recreating medial + lateral gutters + remove polyethylene** · scar under extensor mechanism commonest cause of failure to evert patella · need **~110° flexion** for safe implant removal

**Stepwise** — debridement → **quad snip** → **TTO (preferred)** or quad turndown → collateral release + hinge = **last bail-out**

**Lateral release alone** — for lateral patellar subluxation after implants · does not improve intra-articular exposure

| | **Quadriceps snip** | **V-Y turndown** | **Tibial tubercle osteotomy** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technique | Oblique cut from **apex of quad tendon at 45° into vastus lateralis** | MPP arthrotomy + diverging cut down **vastus lateralis tendon** toward lateral retinaculum (inverted V, closed as Y) | **6–10 cm** fragment cut **medial to lateral** (lateral bony hinge) · screws/wires |
| Exposure | Least extensile of three | **Excellent** (ankylosed knee) · lengthens quad tendon | **Excellent** · best for **well-fixed cemented tibial stem / cone** |
| Post-op | ✓ **No change** — **WBAT + ROM as tolerated**, same as MPP (TO) | **Immobilize in extension** until extensor heals | ROM limited **4–6 wk** · some limit WB |
| Problems | Minimal long-term · early quad weakness, no extensor lag | ✓ **Extensor lag** · quad weakness · **patellar devascularisation** (inferior geniculate) → necrosis/fracture | Tubercle avulsion fracture · **non-union (low — union ~100%)** · wound problems |

**Snip + proximal lateral release joined** = **quadriceps turndown** → extensor lag, patellar blood supply loss, worse function

**TTO needed for** — ✓ **well-fixed tibial cone + cemented stem**\
✗ Wrong options: maltracking · metaphyseal bone loss (cones/stems) · extensor lag (extensor reconstruction) · elevated joint line (distal femoral augments)

**V-Y vs TTO** — ✓ **V-Y → more extensor lag** · V-Y → higher patellar fracture risk · PROMs similar · TTO low non-union · V-Y starts ROM later

**Patella baja + exposure difficulty — which needs rehab change?** → ✓ **V-Y turndown**\
✗ Wrong options: MPP · lateral parapatellar · midvastus · quad snip (all standard rehab)

**Most difficult TKR (TO, FNB 2024)** — options Insall–Salvati <0.7, Caton–Deschamps <0.7, bipartite patella · answer **not provided by the source** · patella baja complicates exposure (Orthobullets)

### Bilateral TKA

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Simultaneous** | Two surgeons, both knees at the same time |
| **Sequential** | One surgeon, one knee then the other, **one anaesthetic** |
| **Staged** | Separate anaesthetics · **3 days to 1 year** apart |

**Antibiotic-loaded bone cement** — ↓ deep infection in **revision** · primary use controversial (theoretical ↓ strength) · not shown to ↑ aseptic loosening

## X vs Y

| | **Midvastus** | **Subvastus** |
|---|---|---|
| Plane | **Through** VMO belly | **Under** VM, off intermuscular septum |
| Risk | **Denervation** | **Least extensile** |
| Revision | Avoid if previous HTO, ROM <80° | **Contraindicated** |

| | **Medial parapatellar** | **Lateral parapatellar** |
|---|---|---|
| Use | Default, all knees | **Fixed valgus** |
| Patellar eversion | Easy | **Difficult** (may need TTO) |
| Patellar blood supply | Risked if lateral release added | **Preserved** |

## How it's asked

**"Quad-sparing MIS, jig alignment difficult — next step?"** → **convert to standard parapatellar arthrotomy**

**"Approach with higher component malposition?"** → **quadriceps sparing**

**"True about MIS quad-sparing approaches?"** → **no clinically significant improvement in PROs**

**"Approaches A, B, C (MPP, midvastus, subvastus) — accurate?"** → **similar blood loss and midterm function**

**"Best long-term practice for a new surgeon?"** → **the approach the surgeon is most comfortable with**

**"Subvastus — EXCEPT?"** → **spares quad tendon, carried through belly of VMO** (TO)

**"Picture of approach (incision not through muscle)?"** → **subvastus** (TO)

**"Greatest quadriceps denervation potential?"** → **midvastus**

**"Rehab after Q-snip?"** → **same as midline parapatellar** (TO)

**"Quad snip in revision — therapy regimen?"** → **no restriction in ROM or weight bearing**

**"TTO likely needed because?"** → **well-fixed tibial cone and cemented stem**

**"Quad snip in revision for aseptic loosening — instructions?"** → **WBAT, ROM as tolerated**

**"Stage-2 revision, stiff, cannot evert patella — next exposure step?"** → **scar debridement recreating gutters + remove polyethylene**

**"V-Y turndown (A) vs TTO (B)?"** → **A more likely to have extensor lag**

**"Two longitudinal + one transverse scar — which incision?"** → **lateral** (TO)

**"Prior dual plating, medial + lateral scars — incision?"** → **lateral one** (TO)

**"Patella baja, exposure needed — which technique changes rehab?"** → **V-Y turndown (Figure F)**

**"Lateral parapatellar for valgus — EXCEPT?"** → **more patellar necrosis than medial arthrotomy** (TO)
