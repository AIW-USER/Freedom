# TKA Other Complications

*Recon · 48 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Covered elsewhere** — neurovascular (vascular injury, peroneal nerve) · extensor mechanism (patellar loosening, clunk, maltracking, EM rupture) · PJI · periprosthetic fracture · metal hypersensitivity · wounds · tibiofemoral instability · stiffness

### Popliteus snapping

**Presentation** — **posterolateral** pain, popping, **palpable snapping at posterolateral joint line** · no effusion, normal gait · ~6 mo after TKA

**Cause** — ✓ **popliteal snapping syndrome** · tendon subluxes over a **retained lateral femoral condyle osteophyte**

**Traps** — ✗ Wrong options: **patellar clunk** (PS knees, patella jumps flexion → extension, anterior) · tight PCL · retained PMMA · tight lateral retinaculum

### Infrapatellar saphenous neuroma (TO)

**Nerve** — ✓ **infrapatellar branch of saphenous nerve (IPBSN)** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: superomedial, inferomedial, inferolateral genicular

**Anatomy (TO)** — saphenous n. exits **subsartorial canal** → **infrapatellar branch** (fat pad + anteromedial knee) + **descending branch** (anteromedial leg/foot to 1st MT)

**Causes (TO)** — **midline/parapatellar TKA approach** · more often ACL reconstruction, arthroscopy, tibial nailing

**Features (TO)** — sharp **medial** knee pain · ↓ ROM · medial tenderness · **Tinel +**

**Treatment (TO)** — conservative → **neuroma excision ± nerve graft**

## X vs Y

| | **Popliteus snapping** | **Patellar clunk** |
|---|---|---|
| Site | **Posterolateral** joint line | **Anterior** (suprapatellar nodule) |
| Cause | **Retained LFC osteophyte** | Fibrous nodule at quad tendon–patella junction |
| Implant | Any | **PS** knees |

## How it's asked

**"Posterolateral snapping 6 mo after TKA, no effusion?"** → **popliteal snapping syndrome**

**"Nerve causing painful neuroma after TKR?"** → **infrapatellar branch of saphenous nerve** (TO)
