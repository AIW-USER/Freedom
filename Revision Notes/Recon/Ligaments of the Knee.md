# Ligaments of the Knee

*Recon · 16 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Ligament function overview

| Ligament | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| **ACL** | Resists **anterolateral** tibial displacement | **Varus** at 0° (full extension) |
| **PCL** | Resists **posterior** tibial displacement, esp. **90°** | **Varus** at 0° |
| **LCL** | Resists **varus at 30°** | Posterolateral rotation at flexion **<~50°** |
| **PLC / popliteofibular** | Resists **posterolateral rotation** | Varus + posterior displacement |
| **MCL** | Resists **valgus** | Axial rotation (with ACL) |

### Rotational stabilizers (TO)

| Rotation | Main stabilizer |
|---|---|
| **Internal rotation 60–90°** | Anterolateral structures — **ITB** most significant |
| **Internal rotation near full extension** | ✓ **Posterior oblique ligament** (✗ Wrong options: LCL · popliteus/PFL · ACL+MCL) |
| **External rotation 60–90°** | **PLC — LCL, popliteus, popliteofibular** · ✓ **LCL = primary stabilizer to ER** (TO) (✗ Wrong options: ACL · PCL · MCL) |

### Layered anatomy

| Lateral layer | Contents |
|---|---|
| **1** | **ITB, biceps femoris** |
| *between 1 and 2* | ***Common peroneal nerve*** |
| **2** | Patellar (lateral) retinaculum |
| **3 superficial** | **LCL**, fabellofibular ligament, **ALL** |
| *between superficial and deep 3* | ***Lateral geniculate artery*** |
| **3 deep** | Arcuate ligament, coronary ligament, **popliteus tendon, popliteofibular ligament**, capsule |

| Medial layer | Contents |
|---|---|
| **1** | **Sartorius** + fascia (medial retinaculum) |
| *between 1 and 2* | ***Gracilis, semitendinosus, saphenous nerve*** |
| **2** | **Superficial MCL**, **MPFL**, **semimembranosus**, POL |
| **3** | **Deep MCL**, capsule, coronary ligament |

**Medial quadruple complex (TO)** — **MCL, semimembranosus, pes anserinus (ST, gracilis, sartorius), POL** · ✓ **not medial meniscus**

**Not attached to medial femoral condyle (TO)** — ✓ **popliteus** (lateral condyle, popliteal notch → posterior tibia above soleal line) · MFC attachments: MCL, MPFL, adductor magnus, PCL

**ITB (TO)** — **moves forward in extension, backward in flexion**, **tense in both** · in flexion **ITB, popliteus, LCL cross**; **ITB + biceps stay parallel**\
✗ Wrong options: lax in flexion · ITB/LCL parallel

### ACL

**Anatomy** — **extrasynovial, intracapsular** · origin **lateral femoral condyle** (medial wall, behind **resident's ridge**) · PL origin **posterior + distal** to AM · insertion broad, **anterior to / between intercondylar eminences** · **33 × 11 mm**

**AM bundle** — **tight in flexion**, loose in extension (Orthobullets/OB) · TO: **tight in both** flexion and extension · fibres parallel in extension, ER in flexion · **longer** · tibial attachment **anterior** to PL

**PL bundle** — **tight in extension**, loose in flexion · **prevents pivot shift** + IR near extension · shorter · isolated PL cut → ✓ **↑ translation + rotation at 30°**

**Blood supply** — **middle geniculate artery** · **innervation** — posterior articular branches of **tibial nerve** · mechanoreceptors (Ruffini, Pacini, GTO, free endings) → **proprioception**, quadriceps modulation

**Composition** — **90% type I, 10% type III** collagen

**Tensile strength** — **native ACL 2200 N < BPTB 3000 N < quadrupled hamstring 4000 N** · TO false statement: ✓ "native ACL > BPTB"

![ACL bundles in flexion and extension](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3001-acl-bundles-flexion-extension.jpg)

### PCL

**Anatomy** — **extrasynovial, intracapsular** · origin **medial femoral condyle** · insertion **tibial sulcus** · **38 × 13 mm** · strength **2500 N** · middle geniculate artery\
✗ Wrong options: intrasynovial

**AL bundle** — **shorter, thicker, stronger** · **tight in flexion** · double-bundle: tension **mid-flexion**

**PM bundle** — longer, thinner, weaker · **tight in extension** · tension in extension + high flexion (protects against **hyperextension**)

**Ridges** — **medial intercondylar ridge** = proximal border of femoral footprint · **medial bifurcate ridge** separates AL/PM

| | ACL | PCL |
|---|---|---|
| **Tight in flexion** | **AM** | **AL** |
| **Tight in extension** | **PL** | **PM** |

### Meniscofemoral ligaments

**Course** — **posterior horn lateral meniscus → medial femoral condyle** (lateral wall), into PCL substance

**Humphrey** — **anterior** to PCL · **Wrisberg** — **posterior** to PCL (more common) · one present in **71–100%**

**MRI** — sagittal: **small ovoid hypointense structures** just anterior (Humphrey) / posterior (Wrisberg) to PCL · coronal: hypointense band parallel to PCL · ✓ arrow = **ligament of Humphrey**\
✗ Wrong options: PCL · bucket-handle tear · ACL · intermeniscal ligament

### LCL

**Anatomy** — origin LFC **posterior + superior to popliteus** origin · runs **superficial to popliteus** · inserts **fibula anterior to popliteofibular ligament** · cord-like

**Biomechanics** — **tight in extension**, lax in flexion · **750 N**

### Posterolateral corner

**Function** — with PCL controls **external rotation + posterior translation**

**Structures** — **LCL (295 N)** · **popliteus (680 N)** · **popliteofibular ligament (229 N)** · lateral capsule · variable: **arcuate ligament**, ITB, fabellofibular ligament

**Dial test** — ✓ **↑ ER at 30° only → PLC** · ↑ at **30° + 90° → PLC + PCL**

**Posterior capsule** — closest structure ✓ **popliteal artery** · superficial → deep (lateral → medial): **tibial nerve → popliteal vein → popliteal artery** · CPN posterolateral · sural subcutaneous

### MCL

**Superficial MCL (tibial collateral)** — origin **3.2 mm proximal + 4.8 mm posterior** to medial epicondyle ✓ · inserts **periosteum of proximal tibia deep to pes**, ~6 cm below joint · layer 2, deep to gracilis + ST · **primary valgus restraint at all angles** · **57% at 5°, 78% at 25°** of medial stability · **4000 N**

**Deep MCL (medial capsular)** — separated by bursa · **meniscofemoral + meniscotibial** parts · attaches **medial meniscus** (coronary) · with posteromedial capsule = **secondary valgus restraint at full extension**

**Valgus opening 5 mm at 30°, other tests normal** → sMCL → ✓ origin **slightly posterior + proximal to medial epicondyle**\
✗ Wrong options: layer 1–2 · Gerdy's (ITB) · popliteal hiatus (popliteus) · Schöttle point (MPFL)

### Posteromedial corner – posterior oblique ligament

**Components** — **semimembranosus** insertion · **POL** · **oblique popliteal ligament** · posterior capsule · posterior horn medial meniscus

**POL** — **adductor tubercle** (just distal/posterior) → posteromedial tibia near articular margin · **posterior to MCL** · three arms (superficial, **central** — most important, capsular) · **tense near full extension**, lax with flexion

**Function** — ✓ **resists valgus + tibial internal rotation near full extension** · secondary restraint to posterior translation if PCL injured · mostly **nonoperative**; ↑ cruciate graft strain in multiligament\
✗ Wrong options: at 60° · ER · anterior translation (ACL) · varus/ER (PLC)

![Posteromedial corner — POL](../../MCQ/attachments/q218218-illa-posteromedial-corner-labeled.jpg)

### MPFL

**Function** — restrains **lateral patellar translation 0–30°** · low tension (**2–10 N**) · **isometric 0–90°**, slack >90° · fails at **200 N** (ACL **1725 N**)

**Anatomy** — **layer 2** · bundles: short oblique (superior pole), inferior straight · femoral: **distal to adductor tubercle, proximal to MCL** · **Schöttle's point** — **1.3 mm anterior** to posterior cortex line, **2.5 mm distal** to posterior origin of MFC, proximal to posterior Blumensaat line · patella: junction proximal–middle thirds of superomedial border

**Tears** — femoral > patellar · 2nd dislocation risk **13%**, 3rd after 2nd **50%**

### Anterolateral ligament

**Anatomy** — **layer 3** with LCL (TO ✓) · femoral **lateral epicondyle** → tibia **midway between Gerdy's tubercle and fibular head** · length **59 mm**, width 7 mm · attached to **lateral meniscus body** (meniscofemoral + meniscotibial) · **lateral inferior genicular vessels** between meniscus and ALL · **no ITB connection** · rotational stability

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Avulsion fractures – Segond, arcuate

| Fracture | Structure | Associated |
|---|---|---|
| **Segond** | **ALL** (meniscotibial part), lateral tibial plateau | **ACL tear 75%** · **IR + varus** |
| **Arcuate sign** | **Arcuate complex** — proximal fibula (LCL/biceps on fragment) | **Cruciate injury ~90%** |
| **Reverse Segond** | **Deep MCL** capsular component (medial) | PCL |
| Gerdy's tubercle | ITB | — |

### Rotatory instabilities (TO)

| Instability | Disrupted | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **Anteromedial** | Medial capsule, MCL, **POL**, ACL | Anterior drawer in 15° ER · valgus laxity at 30° |
| **Anterolateral (90°)** | ALL, ITB, ACL | Pivot shift |
| **Anterolateral (extension)** | ACL (primary) · ITB/Kaplan, lateral meniscus, ALL | **Jerk test, Slocum, MacIntosh pivot shift** |
| **Posterolateral** | LCL, arcuate, popliteus, lateral gastrocnemius ± PCL | **ER-recurvatum**, **reverse pivot shift** · varus thrust + hyperextension gait |
| **Posteromedial** | MCL, medial capsule, POL, PCL, posteromedial capsule, semimembranosus ± ACL | — |

## How it's asked

**"False about knee ligaments?"** → **native ACL stronger than BPTB** (TO)

**"Isolated PL bundle transection?"** → **↑ translation + rotation at 30°**

**"Bundle A anterior on tibia, tight in flexion?"** → **AM bundle; PL loose in flexion**

**"Main stabilizer of IR near full extension?"** → **POL** (TO)

**"Valgus laxity at 30°, injured structure — true?"** → **origin slightly posterior + proximal to medial epicondyle**

**"Resists internal tibial rotation in full extension?"** → **POL**

**"POL — true?"** → **static valgus restraint in full extension**

**"Structure A (posteromedial) — function?"** → **valgus + IR near full extension**

**"Primary stabilizer to ER?"** → **LCL** (TO)

**"Closest to posterior capsule?"** → **popliteal artery**

**"↑ ER at 30° only?"** → **posterolateral corner**

**"Arrow anterior to PCL on MRI?"** → **ligament of Humphrey** (TO)

**"Coronal T1 band from lateral meniscus to MFC?"** → **meniscofemoral ligament**

**"Two avulsions — player A and B?"** → **A = ALL (Segond), B = arcuate complex**

**"True about knee ligaments?"** → **ALL in layer 3 of lateral structures** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: PCL intrasynovial · AL tight in extension · AM prevents pivot shift

**"Medial quadruple complex EXCEPT?"** → **medial meniscus** (TO)

**"True about ITB?"** → **forward in extension, backward in flexion** (TO)

**"Not attached to MFC?"** → **popliteus** (TO)

**"Nerve between lateral layers 1 and 2?"** → **common peroneal nerve**
