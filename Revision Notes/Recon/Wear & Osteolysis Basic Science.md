# Wear & Osteolysis Basic Science

*Recon · 65 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Overview

**Osteolysis** — **histiocytic (macrophage) response to wear debris** → bone loss · same biology as aseptic loosening, faster + focal

**4 steps** — (1) **particulate debris formation** → (2) **macrophage-activated osteoclastogenesis + osteolysis** → (3) **prosthesis micromotion** → (4) **particulate debris dissemination**

**Aseptic loosening** — ✓ commonest cause of **late** THA failure · supra-acetabular osteolysis usually **>10 yr** after index surgery · ↓ since HXLPE

**Burden (TO)** — aseptic osteolysis = **most common cause of THR failure, ≈ 70% of revisions** · tribology = friction, lubrication + wear of surfaces in relative motion · septic vs aseptic osteolysis

**Closest correlate of osteolysis** → ✓ **linear wear rate** of the bearing couple · activity, cup position, PE oxidation, bearing all act by ↑ wear\
✗ Wrong options: weight · head material · offset

### Wear mechanisms (adhesion, abrasion, backside wear)

| Mechanism | What happens | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Adhesive** | Surfaces bond under load → weaker material (PE) pulled off | ✓ **Most common / most important** in THA osteolysis (TO) |
| **Abrasive (two-body)** | Harder, rough surface cuts softer — **cheese grater** | Rough head scratches PE · ploughing, cutting, fragmentation |
| **Third-body** | Trapped particles between bearing surfaces | Type of abrasive wear · **PMMA** mainly (bone, metal beads, HA, Al oxide, BaSO₄, taper corrosion debris) |
| **Fatigue / delamination** | Cyclic subsurface stress → microcracks, pits → large particles | ✓ **TKA** PE failure (TO) — max stress just **below** surface |
| **Fretting** | Small oscillation between surfaces not meant to move | **Backside wear** (shell–liner, tray–insert) |
| **Corrosive** | Sliding in corrosive / oxidative environment | — |

**Hip vs knee PE** — hip: ✓ **adhesion + abrasion** at the surface · knee: **fatigue cracking + delamination**\
✗ Wrong options: fretting · crevice corrosion (under screw heads) · oxidative for hip

**Third-body wear — NOT true** → ✓ **"particle size more important than hardness"** (TO) · hardness / composition, concentration matter more · implant hardness **<0.8×** particle → severe abrasion · **>1.2×** → resistant · vitamin E PE has better surface properties

**Wear modes (McKellop)** — **1**: bearing surfaces as intended · **2**: bearing vs non-bearing surface · **3**: third body between bearing surfaces · **4**: two non-bearing surfaces

**Wear stages** — primary (run-in) → secondary (steady state; most of life) → tertiary (rapid failure)

**Determinants of wear** → **wear factor**, **sliding distance**, **load** — ✓ **all independent determinants** (TO)

**PE cup wear pattern sequence** → ✓ **foldings first** (loading force) → **ripples** (loading-shear; adhesive; slowly worn cups) → **fibrils** (abrasive; rapidly worn cups) (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: fibrils · ripples · ditches

**Optimally positioned cup — wear site** → ✓ **anterosuperior quadrant** (TO) · excess inclination ↑ edge wear · greatly anteverted cup → posterior (neck impingement in extension)

### Linear vs volumetric wear

| | **Linear wear** | **Volumetric wear** |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | **Head penetration into the liner** (thickness lost) | Total volume removed → ✓ **number of particles** |
| Measure | **Livermore** — shortest distance head centre → acetabular surface | **V = πr²w** (∝ **radius²** of head) |
| Main factor | Orthobullets: independent of head size · TO: **22 mm > 32 mm** — read the options | ✓ **Head size** · **total contact area** (TO) |
| Failure | Wear-through of PE cup | **Osteolysis** |
| Threshold | **>0.1 mm/yr** → osteolysis + loosening (rare below) | — |

**Head size** — **22 mm**: ↑ linear, ↓ volumetric, ↑ central wear + dislocation · **32 mm**: ↓ linear, ↑ volumetric, thinner PE, ↑ ROM · **28 mm** = compromise → most systems (TO)

**Conventional PE** — 32 mm heads significantly more volumetric wear than 22 or 26/28 mm · **1 mm ↑ head size → ~10% ↑ volumetric wear**

**HXLPE** — wear **independent of head size 22–46 mm** → 32 vs 28 mm = ✓ **equivalent wear** · head **>40 mm** no proven longevity gain\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ dislocation · ↓ ROM · ↓ osteolysis · ↑ periprosthetic fracture

### Bearing wear rates

| Bearing | Wear rate / particles |
|---|---|
| **Conventional UHMWPE** | **0.1–0.2 mm/yr** (✗ Wrong options: 0.01–0.02) |
| **HXLPE** | Lower · smaller particles · penetration **0.003 vs 0.051 mm/yr** (McCalden) |
| **Ceramic-on-ceramic** | ✓ **Lowest** of any couple — **0.5–2.5 µm / component / yr** |
| **Ceramic-on-PE** | 0–150 µm |
| **Metal-on-metal** | **2.5–5.0 µm/yr** · **nanometre** particles, smaller than MoP |

**Order** — **CoC < MoM < MoP** (TO)

**TO lecture rates** — PE **0.1–0.5 mm/yr** = **90% of debris volume** (vs 0.1–0.2 mm/yr above — read the options) · metal **10–100× less** · ceramic **1000× less** · cement debris rare (old cemented hips) · **adhesive volumetric wear = prime cause of THR failure** · delamination commoner in **TKR**

**Conflict** — OB3311 calls **0.02–0.04 mm/yr** "conventional THA" wear (so 0.03 mm/yr ≈ conventional) · Orthobullets/OB218172: conventional UHMWPE **0.1–0.2 mm/yr** — read the options

**New liner at 0.03 mm/yr (RSA, 4 yr)** → ✓ **osteolysis + loosening minimal if rate continues**\
✗ Wrong options: greater than conventional · positioning irrelevant · significantly lower than conventional · better satisfaction

### Risk factors for wear

**Patient** — **age <50** · **male** · high activity · high BMI

**Implant / surgical** — PE thickness **<6 mm** · head **>32 mm** · ↑ contact stress · **cup inclination >45°** · component malalignment\
✗ Wrong options: >6 mm

**TKA, PE gamma-sterilised in air** — ↑ **shelf age** · younger patient · male · **rough tibial baseplate**\
✗ Wrong options: **PCL-retaining design** (not a risk)

### Particle type & size

**Commonest particle** — ✓ **UHMWPE** (up to **90%** of debris volume; colourless, mostly **spheroid**) · also PMMA, Al oxide, Zr oxide, CoCr, Ti, third body

**Size implicated** — ✓ **<1 µm (submicron)**, **0.1–1 µm** · mean PE **0.5 µm**, metal **0.7 µm** (Maloney) · most stimulatory **0.2–7/8 µm** · most particles **<5 µm**, random shape (TO) · active **0.5–10 µm** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: 10, 100, 1000 µm

**Size vs type** — **size more important than type** for biological activity (Orthobullets)

**Severity of response ∝** → ✓ **particle number** (dose-dependent)\
✗ Wrong options: size · composition · molecular weight

## 2 Mechanism

### Osteolysis biology (macrophages, RANKL, cytokines, particle size)

```
Submicron wear particle
├─ Macrophage phagocytosis / toll-like receptor contact → ↑ NF-κB
│      └─ TNF-α, IL-1, IL-6, IL-8, PGE2, MCP-1 → ↑ RANK + RANKL
└─ Osteoclast precursor + RANKL (from osteoblasts) → OSTEOCLAST BONE RESORPTION
       └─ Osteolysis → micromotion → more wear → LOOSENING
```

| Bearing / process | Cell driving response |
|---|---|
| **PE debris (MoP, CoP)** | ✓ **Macrophage (histiocyte)** — also fibroblasts, giant cells |
| **Metal-on-metal** | ✓ **Lymphocyte (T cell)** — type IV hypersensitivity, ALVAL |
| Infection | Neutrophil |
| Eosinophilic granuloma | Langerhans-type histiocytes (not wear) |

| **Promote osteolysis** | **Inhibit osteolysis** |
|---|---|
| ✓ **TNF-α** (key; from macrophages) · **IL-1** · **IL-6** · IL-8 · **PGE2** (COX-2) · PDGF · **RANKL** · **VEGF** · MCP-1 (recruits monocytes) · osteoclast activating factor · acid phosphatase · GM-CSF → ROS (H₂O₂, oxide radicals) | **OPG** · ✓ **interferon-γ** · **IL-4** · **IL-10** · calcitonin |

| Pro-osteolytic target | Inhibitor |
|---|---|
| RANKL | **Denosumab** (mAb) · OPG |
| TNF-α | **Etanercept** (decoy receptor) |
| COX-2 → PGE2 → IL-6 | **Celecoxib** / NSAIDs |
| IL-6 | **Tocilizumab** (mAb) |
| **IL-1** | **Anakinra** (receptor antagonist) (✗ Wrong options: "IL-10, anakinra") (IL-10 itself inhibits osteolysis) |
| Osteoclast | Bisphosphonates |

**RANK–RANKL–OPG** — RANKL made by **osteoblasts** (✗ Wrong options: osteoclasts) · RANK on **osteoclast precursors** · **OPG binds RANKL** (✗ Wrong options: RANK) · interleukins from **macrophages** (✗ Wrong options: osteoclasts) · RANK/RANKL transcription **↑** (✗ Wrong options: ↓) · TNF-α ↑ RANK

**UHMWPE-induced osteolysis** → ✓ **both VEGF and RANKL ↑** (VEGF enhances RANK/RANKL)

### Micromotion & dissemination

**Micromotion** — osteolysis → micromotion → ↑ particle wear + loosening · joint-fluid pressure → bone resorption

**Effective joint space** — anywhere joint fluid reaches bone · fluid + particles follow **pressure gradients / path of least resistance** · ↑ **hydrostatic pressure** (inflammation) spreads debris · above cup, **through screw holes**, down the stem · cementless cup → **expansile** lysis from implant–bone interface

**Effective joint space ↑ by (TO)** — **non-circumferential porous coating** · **excess screw holes** in shell · poor cement seal / gaps → prevent with circumferential coating, few screws, perfect press-fit or sealed mantle (Harris concept)

**Pelvic lysis around cup screws 10 yr after THA** → ✓ **PE particles tracking through the effective joint space**\
✗ Wrong options: osteosarcoma · galvanic corrosion · sepsis · occult fracture

**Systemic** — ↑ serum metal ions with MoM · **no clear link** to neoplastic, toxic or metabolic disease\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ sarcoma

### Osseointegration & micromotion (TO)

| Micromotion | Tissue formed |
|---|---|
| ✓ **<20 µm** | Predominantly **bone** (ideal) |
| **40–150 µm** | Bone + fibrous tissue |
| **>150 µm** | **Fibrous tissue** |

**Osseointegration (Albrektsson 1981)** — lamellar bone on implant without fibrous tissue · **4–12 wk**, continues up to **3 yr**

**Engh signs of osseointegration** — no radiolucent lines around porous surface · **spot welds** (new bone bridging endosteum → porous coating)

## 5 Management

### Highly cross-linked polyethylene

```
UHMWPE resin (MW 2–6 million)
├─ Shaped → COMPRESSION MOULDING / RAM EXTRUSION / ISOSTATIC PRESSING
├─ Gamma or e-beam 5–10 Mrad, no oxygen → CROSS-LINKS + FREE RADICALS
│      └─ Free radicals removed → REMELT / ANNEAL / VITAMIN E
└─ Terminal sterilisation → GAMMA, E-BEAM, ETHYLENE OXIDE, GAS PLASMA
```

| Property | **HXLPE vs conventional** |
|---|---|
| Wear resistance (adhesive + abrasive) | ✓ **↑** (TKA in vitro: wear ↓ **30–80%**) |
| Particle size | **Smaller** (more bioreactive) |
| Particle number | Fewer (process dependent) |
| Tensile strength | **↓** (✗ Wrong options: ↑) |
| Fatigue strength / crack-propagation resistance | **↓** |
| Fracture toughness | **↓** |
| Ductility / elongation to break | **↓** |
| 10-yr outcome | ✓ **↓ wear + ↑ survival** (✗ Wrong options: no difference) |

| PE generation (TO) | Process | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **1st (1990s)** | Gamma cross-linking + **annealing / remelting** | ↓ wear · ↓ toughness |
| **2nd (2000s)** | **Sequential annealing** / **vitamin E** | ↑ oxidation resistance, better strength · expensive |
| **3rd (2010s)** | Low-O2 processing, solid-state deformation | Wear **~0.02 mm/yr** · long-term data pending (TO — verify: compression moulding / ram extrusion are consolidation methods for all UHMWPE, not a generation) |

**Other materials (TO)** — **PEEK** under evaluation · **polyacetal + PTFE abandoned** (poor wear) · prevention: **3rd/4th-generation cementing**, PE **≥ 6 mm**, head **22–46 mm** (> 46 mm → thinner PE → ↑ wear), ceramic or MoP (avoid MoM) · drugs: **bisphosphonates**, **denosumab** (investigational), BMP-2
| Head size effect | None, **22–46 mm** |

**Toughness** — affects mechanical strength, **not** wear rate

**Conventional PE — the false statement** → "conventional generates smaller particles" is false (conventional = **larger**) · true: higher head penetration, more adhesive wear, ↑ toughness, ↑ tensile strength

| Free-radical step | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Remelting** (above **135 °C**) | Radicals **virtually eliminated** · **↓ crystallinity** → ↓ mechanical properties |
| **Annealing** (just below melt) | Less effective on radicals · crystallinity preserved |
| **Vitamin E** (α-tocopherol) soak / blend — 2nd gen | Scavenges radicals without remelting · ↑ oxidation + delamination resistance |
| Sequential irradiation + annealing ×3 — 2nd gen | ↓ radicals, good mechanics |
| ✗ Wrong option: **ethylene oxide / gas plasma** | Terminal **sterilisation** only |
| ✗ Wrong option: **electron beam / gamma irradiation** | **Creates** cross-links + free radicals |

**HXLPE insert — false** → ✓ **"sterilized by ether"** (TO) (✓ true: compression moulding, vitamin E, less delamination)

**Gamma in air** (historic) → oxidation → early failure

**Young active THA patient asks about longevity** → ✓ **HXLPE liners successful at 15 yr** in her age group\
✗ Wrong options: dual mobility for longevity (it is for stability) · 40 mm head · liner <3 mm (use **≥5–6 mm**) · vertical cup

### Stem design, stress shielding & fatigue failure (TO)

**Distal femoral osteolysis prevention** → ✓ **proximal circumferential porous coating** (proximal **33–40%** of stem; seals interface)\
✗ Wrong options: long stem · collared · fully cemented · diaphyseal fill · extended offset

| Coating | Osteolysis site |
|---|---|
| **Fully (extensively) coated** | ✓ **Proximal** (often no loosening) |
| **Proximal circumferential** | **Proximal** |
| **Patch-coated** | **Distal** — fluid reaches diaphysis |

**Stress shielding** — stiff stem carries load → proximal bone resorption (Wolff) · ✓ **cemented < uncemented** · **fully (HA) coated > proximally coated** · extensively coated: **25–30%** show shielding, calcar rounding, distal **spot welds**\
✗ Wrong options: fully coated ↓ shielding

**Stem material to ↓ stress shielding** → ✓ **titanium** (low modulus, highest fatigue strength) · CoCrMo **~10×** stiffer than femur · alumina **~19×** · flexible, **short, hollow** stems ↓ shielding

**Bending cantilever fatigue → stem fracture** → ✓ **proximal loosening of a cemented stem** with distal stem fixed (TO)

### Cemented component loosening (TO)

**Where loosening starts** — cemented **cup → cement–bone** · cemented **stem → cement–prosthesis** (mechanical) · site unspecified → ✓ **any site** (TO)

**Stem technique errors** — soft cancellous bone left on medial neck · thin / incomplete mantle · **removing all trabecular bone** · too little cement · no pressurisation · **stem motion while curing** · non-neutral stem · voids \
✗ Wrong options: **H₂O₂ canal preparation** (not a cause)

**Cup technique errors** — poor superior/posterior support · cartilage / soft tissue left, too few anchor holes · no pressurisation · cement not around entire cup · movement while curing · **undersized cup** in a large mantle · **malposition** (horizontal, not anteverted) \
✗ Wrong options: **serial reaming + removing all cartilage** (correct technique)

**Causes of aseptic loosening (THR)** — wear debris (major) · lack of primary osseointegration · insufficient press-fit \
✗ Wrong options: **decreased BMD** (TO)

### Clinical osteolysis & polyethylene failure

**Aseptic loosening TKA — ESR 3, CRP 0.1, aspirate 157 WBC, 18% PMN** → ✓ **one-stage revision**\
✗ Wrong options: DAIR + PE exchange · spacer · two-stage · antibiotics

**Osteolysis** — revise if **painful** · asymptomatic → monitor

**TKA 16 yr, distal femoral lysis** → ✓ **macrophage activation by PE particles**\
✗ Wrong options: PE toughness · more with HXLPE · infection · immunosuppression

**TKA 18 yr, no joint space, metal debris, well-fixed** → ✓ **polyethylene failure** (metal-on-metal articulation)\
✗ Wrong options: loose components · particle osteolysis

### Knee backside wear (TO)

**Backside wear** — micromotion tray ↔ insert → osteolysis **under the tibial tray + around screw holes**

**Cemented TKA, lysis under baseplate, no articular wear, no stem** → ✓ **backside fretting PE debris**\
✗ Wrong options: stress shielding (no stem) · articular PE or metal debris · cement debris

**↓ Backside wear** — **polished tray** · **robust locking mechanism** (most important — no micromotion) · **monoblock** · not **rough tray** (↑ wear) · obesity ↑ · modularity ↑

**Least backside wear, same insert** → ✓ **cruciate-retaining** (flat, less conforming, no cam-post) · most → **highly constrained** · PS intermediate

**Cementless tibia with patch porous coating + smooth tracks** → ✓ **↑ metaphyseal osteolysis** · continuous porous coating seals metaphysis\
✗ Wrong options: early loosening · hypersensitivity · fracture · instability

## 6 Complications

### Metal-on-metal debris & lymphocyte response

**MoM particles** — **nanometre**, ✓ **smaller than MoXLPE** · ↓ volumetric wear vs MoP · **↑ serum ions** · wear ↓ after **year 1** (steady state) · volumetric wear > metal-on-ceramic · no ↑ sarcoma\
✗ Wrong options: lower than MoXLPE

**Response** — ✓ **lymphocyte (T cell)** · particles + native proteins → **haptens** → **type IV delayed hypersensitivity** · perivascular lymphocytes + **ALVAL** (aseptic lymphocyte-dominated vasculitis-associated lesion) = pseudotumour histology

**Lymphocyte-driven soft-tissue reaction, well-positioned THA** → ✓ **metal-on-metal**\
✗ Wrong options: MoP, CoP (macrophage) · CoC (lowest wear)

**MoM failure** — **2–3×** higher than MoP · **4–5%** aseptic loosening at 6 yr

**↑ serum metal ions (MoM resurfacing)** → ✓ **smaller implant diameter** · **cup abduction >55°** (edge loading)\
✗ Wrong options: smaller abduction · anteversion 10–20° · function scores · preop OA severity

**MOMHRA pseudotumour mechanism** → ✓ **edge loading + loss of fluid-film lubrication** (malpositioned cup) · incidence **<1%**, usually asymptomatic\
✗ Wrong options: lymphocyte reactivity to nickel (no difference) · third body · P. acnes

**ALTR fluid** — watery, **yellow / grey**, **basic pH**, **low cell count**

**Titanium as bearing surface** — high failure: poor wear resistance + **notch sensitivity**

### Taper corrosion (MACC) & trunnionosis (TO)

**Trunnionosis** — wear at the femoral **head–neck** interface · risk: **modularity**, **large CoCr heads** (↑ torque), **taper design**, mixed alloys, MoM · **small head** is not a risk

**MACC** — mechanical abrasion + chemical corrosion at head–neck → ions + debris → **ALTR** · ✓ **also in MoP THR** (≥1 CoCr component) · **Co ↑ > Cr** · **Co >1 ppb ×2** diagnostic · **MRI** for pseudotumour\
✗ Wrong options: only MoM · Co >0.5 ppb · more with small heads

### Ceramic damage (stripe wear)

**Stripe wear** — CoC · ✓ **lift-off separation** of head (edge loading in gait / deep flexion)\
✗ Wrong options: "unique to metal-on-poly"\
✗ Wrong options: third body · neck–socket impingement · insertion · infection

**"Lead pencil" metal marks on ceramic head** → ✓ **recurrent dislocations** (metal transferred from shell) → ↑ head roughness + PE wear\
✗ Wrong options: infection · LLD · ceramic fracture · ALVAL

**CoC debris** — results from malposition / instability rather than causing failure
