# TKA Kinematic Alignment

*Recon · 46 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — TKA that restores the patient's **pre-arthritic limb alignment + joint lines** by **resurfacing** distal femur and proximal tibia · ✓ **"ligament-sparing"** — bone cuts, not releases, recreate native laxity

**Origin (TO)** — **Howell and Hull (2008)**, "custom-fit positioning" · driven by **~20%** dissatisfaction after mechanical alignment (TO)

**Individualised approaches (TO)** — ✓ **kinematic** + **functional** · fixed approaches = **mechanical** + **anatomical**\
✗ Wrong options: anatomical as individualised

### Anatomic rationale

**HKA axis** (varus / neutral / valgus) and **joint-line obliquity** (varus / neutral / valgus) are **independent** → **9 combinations**

**Only ~15%** of knees have 180° HKA with joint line perpendicular → MA alters **~85%** of patients

### Native alignment (TO / OB)

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| **Distal femur** | **0–4° valgus** (to mechanical axis) · **~9° valgus** to vertical |
| **Femoral anatomic vs mechanical axis** | **4–6°** valgus (OB) · **6°** (TO) |
| **Proximal tibia** | **1–5° varus** · ~3° varus to midline |
| **Overall limb** | Slight **varus ~3°** |
| **Joint line** | **~3° varus** (flexion + extension) |
| **Tibial slope** | **1–9°** posterior |

### Foundational principles

**Arthritis** — minimal **femoral** bone wear · minimal ligament contracture/attenuation · PCL usually intact · varus/valgus deformity = **cartilage loss + tibial wear**

**Balance** — laxity least in **extension** · in flexion **lateral more lax than medial** · avoid joint-line elevation/depression (baja/alta)

### The three kinematic axes of the femur

| Axis | Description |
|---|---|
| **Flexion–extension (cylindrical)** | Transverse axis across distal femur about which tibia flexes · **oriented differently from TEA** (Orthobullets) · **same as TEA** (TO) — read the options |
| **Internal–external rotation** | Longitudinal axis in **medial compartment** (**medial pivot**) · tibial IR in flexion → lateral condyle **roll-back** |
| **Patellar tracking** | Isometric axis across **medial condyle** · analogous to **Schöttle point** (MPFL) |

**All three** — parallel or perpendicular to the native femoral joint line → resurfacing femur restores them

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### CPAK classification & constitutional alignment (TO)

**Constitutional alignment** — ✓ **arithmetic HKA (aHKA)** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: mechanical HKA (changed by arthritis) · LDFA · MPTA alone

| Formula (TO) | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **aHKA = MPTA − LDFA** | **Negative = varus**, positive = valgus · unaffected by joint-space narrowing/subluxation · ignores JLCA |
| **JLO = MPTA + LDFA** | **180° = neutral** · **>180° apex proximal** · **<180° apex distal** |
| **mHKA** | Angle between femoral + tibial mechanical axes |
| **mLDFA** | Lateral angle, femoral mechanical axis vs distal femoral joint line |
| **mMPTA** | Medial angle, tibial mechanical axis vs proximal tibial joint line |

| Boundaries (TO) | Neutral | Varus / apex distal | Valgus / apex proximal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **aHKA** | **0 ± 2°** | **< −2°** | **> +2°** |
| **JLO** | **180 ± 3°** | **<177°** (apex distal) | **>183°** (apex proximal) |

| CPAK (TO) | aHKA | JLO | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | Varus | Apex distal | KA better from outset |
| **II** | **Neutral** | **Apex distal** | ✓ **Commonest (~40%)** → **anatomic alignment** |
| **III** | Valgus | Apex distal | Constitutional valgus |
| **IV** | Varus | Neutral | KA better from outset |
| **V** | **Neutral** | **Neutral** | ✓ **Best balanced by mechanical alignment** · only **~15%** |
| **VI** | Valgus | Neutral | Constitutional valgus |
| **VII–IX** | Varus / neutral / valgus | Apex proximal | — |

**CPAK I / IV under MA** — need varus recuts or extensive **MCL release** (TO)

**CPAK III / VI** — lateral femoral + tibial deficiency, femoral/tibial ER deformity, lateral contracture, later MCL attenuation (TO)

## 5 Management

### Surgical technique

**Femoral target** — ✓ **femoral component condyle thickness − 1 mm (saw kerf) − 2 mm (cartilage loss)**

**Tibial balancing** — after femoral resurfacing, adjust tibial **varus/valgus + slope** · no soft-tissue releases · no PCL sacrifice/recession · TO: only **tibial cut** used to balance (plus osteophytes, posterior capsule off femur, tibial ML shift)

| Gap | Shape |
|---|---|
| **Extension** | **Rectangular** (equal, negligible V/V laxity) |
| **Flexion** | **Trapezoidal** (lateral progressively looser) |

**Patella** — resect component thickness − 1 mm kerf − **4 mm** cartilage · leave **≥13 mm** bone

### Distal femoral cut angle

**LDFA 81°, anatomic–mechanical difference 6°** → femur in **9° valgus** at joint line → ✓ **9° valgus** to cut parallel to native joint line (KA) · MA would use **6°** · KA femoral component **2–4° more valgus**, tibia more **varus**\
✗ Wrong options: 0°, 3°, 6° valgus, 3° varus

### Component positioning goals

| Target | **Mechanical alignment** | **Kinematic alignment** |
|---|---|---|
| Femoral flexion | Similar | Similar |
| **Distal femoral cut** | ⊥ femoral mechanical axis | ∥ distal articular surface (after wear correction) |
| **Posterior femoral cut** | **3° ER** relative to posterior condyles | **Neutral (0°)** relative to posterior condyles |
| **Tibial cut** | ⊥ tibial mechanical axis | ∥ articular surface (after wear correction) |
| **Tibial slope** | **2–7°** posterior | **∥ medial plateau** |
| **Tibial axial rotation** | **Aligned with tibial tubercle** | **∥ long axis of lateral plateau** |
| Balancing | Ligament releases | Bone cuts (tibia) |

**NOT a KA goal (TO)** — ✓ **tibial rotation aligned with tubercle** (that is MA)

### Methods of performing KA

| Method | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Caliper-verified** | Any instruments/implant · cuts within **±0.5 mm** of target | Sagittal femoral flexion hard with IM rod · **tibial recuts** common · sizing hard pre-op |
| **Patient-specific guides** | Detailed planning · few trays | Recuts still needed · advanced imaging · closed vendor |
| **Navigation** | Planning + intra-op adjust, avoid recuts | Imaging · closed vendor · cost |
| **Robotics** | Planning + intra-op adjust | Imaging · closed vendor · equipment burden · cost |

### KA versus MA

**Varus knees** — varus tibial cuts / residual varus ✓ **not associated with bony or hardware failure**

**Valgus knees (KA view)** — **no hypoplastic LFC** · natural valgus joint line with comparable condyles · lateral cartilage + tibial wear (contrast: conventional teaching = LFC hypoplasia)

**Rotation** — **ER of femur/tibia not needed** for tracking · many native trochleae are **IR relative to TEA** · tracking from correct HKA, JLO, joint-line height + tubercle IR in flexion

**Implant** — **medial-pivot** design needed for normal kinematics · concern about shear on **PS cam-post**

**Outcomes (OB)** — loosening, reoperation equal · satisfaction + ROM equal or better

**Controversies (TO)** — needs PSI/technology · ACL + menisci sacrificed · non-weight-bearing kinematics differ · no limits on outliers → restrict to mild deformity · few long-term data

### Related techniques

| Technique | Principle |
|---|---|
| **Restricted KA** | Limits on component + HKA varus/valgus · safe zone (TO): **LDFA + MPTA 86–93°**, final **mHKA −5° varus to +4° valgus** · minor releases rather than exceed limits |
| **Functional alignment** | Adjust femoral + tibial cuts in all 3 planes for symmetric/asymmetric gaps · no releases · e.g. tibia up to **3° varus** for tight medial extension · **femoral IR** for tight lateral flexion · limb within **0 ± 3°** |
| **Inverse KA** | Predetermined tibial cut → femur gap-balanced · no releases |
| **Anatomical (Hungerford 1982)** | Tibia **3° varus** + femur extra 3° valgus (**7–10°** total) · for **CR** · risks asymmetric poly wear, LCL/popliteus damage (TO) |
| **Mechanical (Freeman & Swanson 1973)** | Both components ⊥ mechanical axis · releases balance gaps · varus knee: more lateral tibia + more distal/posterior MFC removed → MCL tight · **15–20%** remain symptomatic (TO) |

## X vs Y

| | **Mechanical** | **Kinematic** |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Neutral 0° HKA, horizontal joint line | Patient's pre-arthritic HKA + JLO |
| Balancing | **Soft-tissue release** | **Bone cuts** (tibia) |
| Femoral rotation | 3° ER to posterior condyles / TEA | 0° to posterior condyles |
| Best CPAK | **V** | I, III, IV, VI |
| Implant | Any | Medial pivot preferred |

## How it's asked

**"LDFA 81°, cut parallel to native joint line — guide setting?"** → **9° of valgus**

**"CPAK type best balanced by MA?"** → **CPAK V** (TO)

**"Constitutional alignment best defined by?"** → **arithmetic HKA** (TO)

**"Commonest CPAK type?"** → **neutral aHKA + apex distal JLO (type II)** (TO)

**"Individualised approach to alignment?"** → **kinematic alignment** (TO)

**"NOT a KA positioning goal?"** → **tibial rotation aligned with tibial tubercle** (TO)

**"Kinematic vs mechanical in varus knee — result?"** → **varus tibial + valgus femoral cuts**
