# THA Implant Fixation

*Recon · 24 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Types of fixation

**Cement** — **PMMA**, acts as **grout** (interlock, no adhesion)

**Biologic (cementless)** — **ingrowth** into porous coating · **ongrowth** onto grit-blasted surface

**Collared stem** — transfers load to **calcar**, ↓ early subsidence, controls depth · cemented or cementless · **protective against early periprosthetic fracture**

**History** — cement: **Gluck 1891**, **Charnley 1950s** (dental cement) · "cement disease" 1980s → cementless · **AML (1983)** first microporous ingrowth stem → proximal coating (thigh pain, osteolysis)

**Trend** — **93% of US THAs cementless** (2012)

**Templating** — calibrated radiograph · goal: **limb length + femoral offset**

### Dorr classification

**Ratio** — canal diameter **10 cm below mid-lesser trochanter ÷ inner diameter at mid-lesser trochanter**

| Type | Ratio | Features | Stem |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** ("champagne flute") | **<0.5** | Thick cortices AP + lateral · thick posterior **fin** · young, heavy, male | **Uncemented** (flat taper) |
| **B** | **0.5–0.75** | **Posterior cortex thinning**, fin eroded, **"rat bites"** | Uncemented · flat taper may be **rotationally unstable** → metadiaphyseal/diaphyseal or cemented |
| **C** ("stovepipe") | **>0.75** | Thin, fuzzy cortices both views · elderly, thin, female | **Cemented** |

**TO EXCEPT (Dorr B)** — ✓ **"ratio <0.5"** is false (that is A)

## 5 Management

### Operative criteria – cemented vs cementless

| Component | Cemented | Cementless |
|---|---|---|
| **Femur** | **Elderly / osteopenic** · **irradiated bone** · **Dorr C (stovepipe)** | Young, active males · old with good bone · **revision** (cemented revision stems fail more) |
| **Acetabulum** | Poor bone, complex revision · **fails more** (cement poor in **shear**) | **Preferred** (porous hemispheric) — except poor bone stock or irradiation |

**Elderly woman, minimize fracture + durable** → ✓ **hybrid (cementless cup + cemented stem)**\
✗ Wrong options: cemented cup (early loosening) · hemi · press-fit stem (fracture) · resurfacing

**Cemented vs cementless femur in elderly (registry)** → ✓ **lower early revision**, ↓ iatrogenic + early periprosthetic fracture, ↓ early aseptic loosening · LLD no different · intraop fracture **3.5% uncemented vs 0.4% cemented** · overall **3% vs 0.23%** (female, >65)

### Cemented fixation

**Generations**

| Generation | Technique |
|---|---|
| **1st** | Hand mixed, **finger packed**, no restrictor/preparation |
| **2nd** | **Cement restrictor**, **cement gun**, brush + dry canal |
| **3rd** | **Vacuum mixing** (↓ porosity), **pressurization**, **pulsatile lavage** |
| 4th (TO) | + **stem centralization** |

**Optimized by** — ↓ porosity (vacuum) · pressurization · **mantle >2 mm** (OB ~2 mm ideal; TO **2–4 mm**, >5 mm ↑ micromotion) · **stiff stem** · centralization · **clean dry bone** · **smooth stem, rounded corners** · **no mantle defects** (stem touching cortex) · neutral position\
✗ Wrong options: varus/valgus

**↑ Failure** — ✓ **PMMA-precoated stems** (extra interface)\
✗ Wrong options: collar–calcar contact · smooth corners · 2 mm mantle · stiff stem >115 GPa (stiffer improves)

**Osteopenia** — ✓ **stronger bone–cement interface** (porosity → deeper penetration) · young male dense bone → weaker interface · pressurization ↑ penetration + fracture resistance

### Barrack–Harris cement grading

| Grade | Appearance |
|---|---|
| **A** | Complete canal fill, **"white-out"** |
| **B** | Slight radiolucency (<50% of interface) |
| **C** | ✓ **Radiolucency >50%** or **incomplete mantle** |
| **D** | **Gross radiolucency** and/or **no cement around stem tip** |

### Bone cement properties (TO)

**Mechanics** — **strong in compression**, ✓ **weak in shear** (EXCEPT answer) · **E ≈ 2.2 GPa** · grout aiding load transfer

**Chemistry** — liquid **MMA monomer** + powder (PMMA–styrene co-polymer) · initiator **benzoyl peroxide** (powder) · accelerator **DmpT** (liquid) · stabilizer **hydroquinone** · radiopacifier **zirconium dioxide** / barium sulphate · ✓ **never 100% conversion** — **2–6% residual monomer** → 0.5% at 2–3 wk (EXCEPT answer) · glass transition **~70 °C** wet (above body temperature)

**Curing** — mixing → sticky → working → hardening · **heat, humidity, faster mixing ↓ working time** · pre-chilling prolongs it · antibiotics **<2 g** per pack keep strength

**Calcium phosphate cement** — no heat, osteoconductive · **not for load bearing** (EXCEPT answer) — weak, brittle, low cohesion in water

### Biologic fixation

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Pore size** | **50–300 µm**, ideally 50–200 (Orthobullets) · **50–400 µm** (OB) |
| **Porosity** | **40–80%** (more → ingrowth but weaker, metal shearing) |
| **Gaps** | **<50 µm** |
| **Micromotion** | **<150 µm** (>150 → fibrous) · initial rigid fixation **<50 µm** |
| **Cortical contact** | Maximal |
| **Bone** | Viable — **irradiation ↑ aseptic loosening** |

**Press-fit** — implant slightly larger than reamed (no screws needed) · **line-to-line** — same size, screws often added

**Coatings** — **proximal porous** (less stress shielding) · **extensive porous** (more proximal shielding; revision) · **grit-blasted** ongrowth — always extensively coated (weaker fixation) · **hydroxyapatite** — osteoconductive adjunct · ✓ **shorter time to biologic fixation in animals**, no clinical advantage · needs **high crystallinity**, optimal (not thick) thickness, rough surface

### Radiographic signs of stable fixation

**Cementless stem** — **spot welds** (endosteal bone to porous surface) · no lines around porous part · **proximal stress shielding** (extensively coated) · no subsidence

**Cementless cup** — no migration · no progressive lines · intact screws

## 6 Complications

**Aseptic loosening** — poor initial fixation, mechanical loss, particle osteolysis · cup → groin/buttock pain · stem → **thigh, start-up pain** · serial X-rays, bone scan · revise loose parts

**Stress shielding** — proximal bone loss around well-fixed stem · risk: ✓ **stiff stem (modulus mismatch; Co-Cr > Ti)** — most important · **large diameter** (biggest contributor to stiffness) · **extensive porous coating** · preoperative osteopenia · clinical significance unknown, no treatment

**Intraoperative fracture** — risk: **press-fit** · collar protective · **acetabular**: stable cup → add screws; unstable → remove, fix, reinsert with screws · **femoral**: stable stem → limit WB ± cerclage; unstable → remove, fix, stem bypassing by **2 cortical diameters**

## X vs Y

| | **Cemented femur** | **Cementless femur** |
|---|---|---|
| Patient | Elderly, osteopenic, **Dorr C**, irradiated | Young, active, Dorr A/B, **revision** |
| Fracture | **↓** | ↑ (press-fit) |
| Early revision (elderly) | **↓** | ↑ |
| Failure mode | Debonding, mantle defects | Fibrous ingrowth if micromotion >150 µm |

## How it's asked

**"Optimal pore size?"** → **50–400 µm** (OB) · 50–300 µm (Orthobullets)

**"HA-coated vs porous stems in animals?"** → **shorter time to biologic fixation**

**"Barrack–Harris — incomplete mantle, >50% lucency?"** → **grade C** (TO)

**"Bone cement EXCEPT?"** → **strong in shear** (TO)

**"Bone cement EXCEPT?"** → **hardening = polymerization of 100% MMA** (TO)

**"Calcium phosphate vs acrylic — false?"** → **better in load-bearing areas** (TO)

**"↑ cemented femoral failure?"** → **PMMA-precoated stems**

**"Osteopenia and bone–cement interface?"** → **improved mechanical integrity**

**"78-yr-old woman — minimize fracture, durable?"** → **hybrid: cementless cup + cemented stem**

**"Cemented vs cementless stem in elderly?"** → **lower risk of early revision**

**"Dorr B — EXCEPT?"** → **cortical thickness ratio <0.5** (TO)

**"Most important stress-shielding factor?"** → **stem stiffness (modulus mismatch)**

**"Micromotion threshold for bone ingrowth?"** → **<150 µm** (gaps <50 µm)
