# THA Leg Length Discrepancy

*Recon · 25 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Litigation** — **2nd most common cause of THA litigation** (after **nerve injury**)

**Prevention** — **preoperative planning/templating** ↓ incidence

### Mechanism

| Cause | Effect |
|---|---|
| **ABDuction contracture** | Involved hemipelvis **lower** → apparent **LONG** leg |
| **ADDuction contracture** | Involved hemipelvis **higher** → apparent **SHORT** leg |
| **Weak abductors** | Sensation of **long leg without true LLD** · resolves **3–6 months** |
| Unilateral OA | Pre-op shortening (cartilage loss, contracture) → equalized leg **feels long** |

### What changes length

| Lengthens | Does NOT change length |
|---|---|
| **Longer femoral neck** | ✓ **↑ femoral offset** (moves femur laterally → abductor tension, stability) |
| **High (proximal) neck cut** | **Medialized cup** (tension/JRF only) |
| **Incomplete seating of cemented stem** | Cemented vs cementless stem (if done properly) |
| **Inferior cup** (graft above to seat lower) | — |
| — | **Short-neck** component → **shortens** |

![Neck length vs offset effect on limb length](../../MCQ/attachments/q995-illb-picture1.jpg)

### Offset restoration

**Decreased offset** — femur closer to pelvis → ✓ **bone-on-bone impingement** (GT on pelvis) → levering, instability · **↓ abductor moment, ↑ JRF**, abductor weakness, soft-tissue laxity\
✗ Wrong options: trochanteric bursitis

**Increased offset** — **trochanteric pain / bursitis** · ↑ stability, ↓ impingement

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **perceived LLD** despite anatomic equality

**Tolerance (TO)** — **< 1 cm** unnoticed · **1–2.5 cm** may dissatisfy · **> 2.5 cm** symptomatic · lengthening **> 2.5–4 cm** → nerve injury risk · both over-lengthening and shortening ↑ contralateral hip OA risk (TO)

**True length** — **ASIS → medial malleolus**

**Apparent length** — includes contractures + pelvic obliquity · hard to measure

**Perceived LLD at 6 weeks, equal lengths on X-ray, normal motion/strength** → ✓ **perceived LLD**\
✗ Wrong options: flexion/adduction contracture (would make the operated leg feel short) · medialized cup · femoral malposition

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**AP pelvis** — compare limb lengths (inter-teardrop / ischial line to lesser trochanter) · ↑ neck length · **offset change does not alter length**

![Leg length discrepancy on AP pelvis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12115-lld.jpg)

## 5 Management

```
LLD after THA
├─ Perceived / apparent (contracture, weak abductors) → reassure, PT, wait 3–6 months
├─ True LLD persisting >6 months → shoe lift (most)
└─ Significant LLD affecting life >6–12 months → revision (rare; dislocation risk if shortening)
```

**Shoe lift** — adequate in most · **wait 6 months** for muscle relaxation

**Revision** — rare · significant LLD unresolved at **6–12 months** affecting quality of life · **shortening revision risks dislocation**

### Intraoperative factors

**Templating (TO)** — bilateral AP pelvis, hips **15° IR** · **inter-ischial line** + **top of lesser trochanter** each side → shortening · copy centre of rotation from normal side · cup: **inferior edge on teardrop line**, medial edge not past vertical teardrop line, ~**40°** · femur: GT + LT distances to head centre → neck cut + offset · femoral + acetabular centres at **same height** → equal length

| Intra-op check (TO) | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ranawat technique** | Greater trochanter / pelvic reference for length |
| **Shuck test** | Traction → **half the head stays in the socket** = adequate tension |
| **Drop-kick test** | Soft-tissue (rectus) tension |

**Restore anatomic length** — templating · variable neck lengths · intraoperative length check · clinical + radiographic pre-op assessment · ✓ **variable-offset modular system does NOT restore length** (EXCEPT answer)

## How it's asked

**"Help restore limb length EXCEPT?"** → **modular system with variable femoral offset**

**"Lengthening techniques EXCEPT?"** → **increasing femoral neck offset**

**"Most likely to lengthen the leg?"** → **very proximal neck cut**\
✗ Wrong options: medialized cup · high-offset stem · short neck · cemented stem

**"Equal lengths on X-ray, operated leg feels long at 6 weeks?"** → **perceived LLD**

**"Failure to restore offset (TO)?"** → **bone-to-bone impingement**

**"Abduction contracture causes?"** → **apparent long leg**

**"When to treat LLD with shoe lift?"** → **after 6 months**

**"Second most common THA litigation cause?"** → **LLD** (first: nerve injury)

**"Shuck test — adequate tension?"** → **half the femoral head stays in the acetabulum** (TO)

**"LLD after THR usually tolerated up to?"** → **≤ 2.5 cm** (TO)
