# THA Templating

*Recon · 35 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — anticipating **implant size + position** pre-op · reproduces hip biomechanics · minimizes **LLD**

**Accuracy** — **52–98%** within ± one size · improves with experience

**Order** — radiographs → patient data on template → landmarks → **LLD** → ✓ **acetabulum first** (sets new centre of rotation) → femur

**Tip** — aim for sizes in the **middle range** of the system · otherwise consider a different system

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Film | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **AP pelvis** | Centred over **pubic symphysis** |
| **AP hip** | **10–15° internal rotation** (Orthobullets) · **15–20° IR** (TO) — matches native anteversion · neck parallel to cassette |
| **Frog lateral hip** | — |
| **Magnification** | **20%** standard (tube 1 m, film 5 cm below table) · ↑ in obese, ↓ in slim · use magnification markers |

**Position of legs for templating** — ✓ **15° IR** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: 30° IR · 15° ER · 30° ER

**External rotation on film** — **falsely ↓ offset** · **valgus-appearing neck** · **falsely ↓ canal diameter**

**Secondary checks** — **pelvic obliquity** (spinal deformity → LLD issues) · **acetabular retroversion** (harder cup positioning)

**Pelvic sagittal tilt (TO)** — sacrococcygeal joint to top of symphysis · neutral **32 mm women (8–50)** · **47 mm men (15–72)** · ↑ when pelvis tilts forward · standing film with symphysis in line with sacrum (TO)

### Radiographic landmarks

| Side | Landmarks |
|---|---|
| **Femur** | Medullary canal · GT · LT · **saddle point** — most distal junction of superior neck and GT |
| **Acetabulum** | Roof · **teardrop** — superimposed distal medial wall + tips of anterior/posterior horns · lies behind **TAL**, at superior border of obturator foramen (TO) |
| **Pelvis** | **Ischial tuberosities** (LLD) |

![Teardrop landmark](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12124-teardrop.jpg)

### Limb length discrepancy

**Step 1** — horizontal line across **ischial tuberosities** (extend past medial femoral cortices) · alternative **inter-teardrop line** (may be **more accurate**)

**Step 2** — mark **proximal-most point of both lesser trochanters**

**Step 3** — LLD = difference in distance from the reference line to each LT

![LLD — ischial line to lesser trochanters](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12124-limb-length.jpg)

## 5 Management

### Template the acetabulum

**Abduction** — roughly **40°**

**Medial border** — approximates **ilioischial line**, close to **teardrop** · medial cup → ✓ **↓ JRF** + **↓ abductor force** to keep pelvis level\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ abductor force · ↑ leg length (set by cup + stem together) · ↑ / no change in JRF

**Inferior border** — at **inferior teardrop line**

**Then** — mark **acetabular centre of rotation (COR)**

**Acetabular offset (TO)** — acetabular COR to vertical line through teardrop · also: centre of femoral head to **true floor of acetabulum**

### Template the femur

**Size** — fills **medullary canal**

**Insertion depth** — set to equalize limb length

**Neck cut** — **posterior approach → lesser trochanter** reference · **anterior approach → saddle point**

**Femoral offset** — ✓ **femoral head COR to line bisecting long axis of femur** (TO) · ↑ offset → ↑ stability, ↓ JRF, ↓ impingement · ↓ offset → instability, abductor weakness, **gluteus medius lurch**

**Restore offset by** — stem with more/less offset · different **neck-shaft angle** · change **neck length**

### Templating effects on leg length & offset

**Rule** — acetabular COR is **fixed** · femoral COR moves to meet it intra-op

| Femoral COR relative to cup COR | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Superior** | **Lengthens** limb |
| **Inferior** | ✓ **Shortens** limb (directly inferior → shortens, **no offset change**) |
| **Medial** | **↑ offset** |
| **Lateral** | **↓ offset** |
| **Overlapping** | No change in length or offset |

| Cup COR vs femoral COR (TO table) | Cup medial to femur | Equal | Cup lateral to femur |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cup superior** | Short + ↓ offset | Shortened | Short + ↑ offset |
| **Equal** | **↓ offset** | No change | ↑ offset |
| **Cup inferior** | Long + ↓ offset | Lengthened | Long + ↑ offset |

**EXCEPT trap (TO)** — "femoral COR **inferior** to cup COR **lengthens**" = ✓ **false** · true statements: AP in 15° IR at 20% magnification · inferior → shortens · cup COR medial to femoral COR → ↓ offset

**↑ offset with same leg length** — ✓ femoral COR **medial** to cup COR at the **same level** (Figure E)

### Large femoral heads

**Associated with** — ✓ **acceptable wear with modern bearings** · ↓ dislocation · ↑ ROM before impingement · available in all bearing materials\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ dislocation · ↓ ROM · fewer materials · ↑ impingement

### Acetabular anteversion measurement (TO)

| Film | Method |
|---|---|
| **AP radiograph** | **Liaw** (sin⁻¹ tan β) · **Lewinnek** (arcsin D1/D2 — short/long axis of ellipse) · **Widmer** (short axis / total length) · **Hassan** · **Ackland** |
| **Cross-table lateral** | ✓ **Woo and Morrey** — measured directly · distinguishes anteversion vs retroversion |

**NOT an AP method** — ✓ **Woo and Morrey** (TO)

## X vs Y

| | **Femoral offset** | **Acetabular offset** |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Head COR → femoral shaft axis | Acetabular COR → teardrop vertical (or true floor) |
| ↓ when | Leg externally rotated on film | Cup medialized |

| | **Posterior approach** | **Anterior approach** |
|---|---|---|
| Neck-cut reference | **Lesser trochanter** | **Saddle point** |

## How it's asked

**"Templating truths — EXCEPT?"** → **femoral COR inferior to cup COR lengthens the limb** (TO)

**"Femoral offset definition?"** → **femoral head COR to line bisecting femoral long axis** (TO)

**"Leg position while templating?"** → **15° internal rotation** (TO)

**"Template that ↑ offset, same leg length?"** → **Figure E — femoral COR medial, same level**

**"Femoral COR directly inferior to cup COR?"** → **decreases limb length**

**"Femoral COR medial to cup COR at same level?"** → **length same, offset increased**

**"Cup moved medially (A → B)?"** → **decreases joint reactive force**

**"Large heads associated with?"** → **acceptable wear with modern bearings**

**"Not an AP-radiograph anteversion method?"** → **Woo and Morrey** (TO)
