# TKA Stiffness

*Recon · 60 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — ✓ **flexion <90°** or **flexion contracture 10–15°** · poor function + satisfaction

**Incidence** — **1.3–12%** (up to 6%; arthrofibrosis ~1%)

### Risk factors

| Phase | Factors |
|---|---|
| **Pre-op** | ✓ **Poor pre-op ROM (most important)** · **patella baja** · **younger age (<55)** · smoking · comorbidity (DM) · low pain tolerance · **prior surgery** (distal femur ORIF, plateau ORIF, multi-ligament surgery → 2–3× arthrofibrosis) · post-traumatic OA · socioeconomic |
| **Technical** | **Overstuffed PF joint** · **malrotation** · **tight flexion/extension gaps** · **joint-line elevation** · **extensor mechanism tightened at closure** (close in flexion) · **tight PCL** (CR) · oversized components · anterior tibial slope · inadequate resections |
| **Post-op** | Delayed rehab · poor effort · **infection** · HO · hamstring spasm (usually resolves by **6 mo**) |
| **Not** | Skin staples · SNF discharge · CPM use · PS vs CR · ipsilateral THA · no tourniquet |

### Predictors of postoperative ROM

**Most important determinant of final ROM** — ✓ **pre-op ROM** (TO, OB) · also intra-op ROM, flexion arc, lower tibiofemoral angle\
✗ Wrong options: CPM/rehab · slope + capsular release · balancing

**Gap errors (OB7594)** — **excess distal femoral resection + thick poly → tight flexion → loss of flexion** · **inadequate distal resection + retained osteophytes + tight posterior capsule → flexion contracture**\
✗ Wrong options: the reversed pairings

**Post-traumatic OA (prior plateau ORIF) TKA** → ✓ **limited knee flexion / stiffness needing MUA** · also wound problems\
✗ Wrong options: worse function (gain larger) · more loosening · HO · fatal PE

**Prior distal femur ORIF** → ✓ **arthrofibrosis 10–85° at 3 mo**\
✗ Wrong options: good pre-op ROM · PS · ipsilateral THA · no tourniquet

### Functional ROM requirements

| Activity | Flexion |
|---|---|
| **Stairs** | **90°** (TO, OB) · 95° (Orthobullets) |
| **Rise from chair** | ✓ **95–100°** (TO) · 105° (Orthobullets) — read the options |
| **Lifting an object** | **≥120°** |
| **Squat, kneel, cross-legged** | **110–165°** |
| **Efficient gait** | Near-full extension |

**Minimum flexion to rise from chair (TO)** → ✓ **95°**

## 3 Presentation

**Difficulty kneeling** · compare with pre-op ROM in records

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray / CT** — exclude malposition, malalignment, oversizing, notching

### Genes & lab markers

**ESR / CRP ± aspiration** — **exclude infection** before MUA

## 5 Management

```
Stiff TKA (flexion <90°)
├─ Always rule out infection (ESR, CRP, aspirate)
├─ <12 wk → manipulation under anaesthesia
├─ >12 wk / failed MUA → arthroscopic (or open) lysis + MUA
└─ Identifiable technical cause → revision TKA
```

### Manipulation under anaesthesia – indications & timing

**Indication** — **flexion <90° within 12 weeks** despite PT\
✗ Wrong options: 110° threshold

**Timing** — ✓ **best before 12 weeks / within 3 months** (6 wk no better than 12 wk; max gains 12–14 wk) · late (>3 mo) less effective, more risk

**Loss of flexion** responds better than loss of extension

**Gains** — average **67° → 111°** · early MUA +**20–30°** · resume aggressive PT immediately

**Risks** — **fracture** (<1–2%), **extensor mechanism disruption** · ✓ **notching** (undersized femur with **posterior referencing** → anterior over-resection; **3 mm** notch ↑ fracture risk; anterior femur stressed most in extension)\
✗ Wrong options: unequal gaps · ↑ offset · femoral IR · press-fit

| Scenario | Answer |
|---|---|
| ROM 0–60° at **2 mo** (pre-op 5–100°) | ✓ **best MUA indication** (✗ Wrong options: 0–60° at 8 mo · 30–120° (extension loss)) |
| 6 wk, good intra-op motion, poor flexion | ✓ **MUA** (✗ Wrong options: resurface patella · thinner poly · arthroscopy (>3 mo) · CPM) |
| 0–80° at 6 wk — MUA best before | ✓ **12 weeks** |
| 10–70° at 10 wk, normal ESR/CRP | ✓ **MUA** (✗ Wrong options: aspiration (labs normal) · steroid + PT · poly exchange · revision) |

### Late stiffness – workup & revision

**Deteriorating motion 9 mo after revision** → ✓ **investigate for PJI**\
✗ Wrong options: PT · MUA · revision · resection

**9 mo, 5–80°, painful, CR well aligned, infection negative** → ✓ **arthroscopic lysis of adhesions + manipulation**\
✗ Wrong options: dynamic splint · open release · MUA alone (late) · revision to CS

**Revision for stiffness** — only with **identifiable technical cause** · results modest (arc +~20°; 40% good–excellent; 66% satisfied) · ✓ **main post-op focus = regain near full extension** (more satisfying than arc gain)\
✗ Wrong options: 75–80% reach 0–90° · pre-op arc irrelevant · CR→PS much better · MUA reliable after revision

**Other options** — open adhesiolysis ± poly exchange, quadricepsplasty, component revision

### CPM

✓ **May improve early ROM, not final ROM** · no benefit in pain, ambulation, extension · cost questioned

## X vs Y

| | **< 12 weeks** | **> 12 weeks** |
|---|---|---|
| First choice | **MUA** | **Arthroscopic lysis + MUA** |
| Risk | Low | ↑ fracture, tendon rupture |

## How it's asked

**"When is MUA best indicated?"** → **0–60° at 2 months**

**"6 wk, poor flexion despite aggressive rehab?"** → **manipulation under anaesthesia**

**"MUA gives largest flexion gain if before?"** → **12 weeks**

**"10–70° at 10 wk, normal labs?"** → **manipulation under anaesthesia**

**"True about MUA?"** → **best results within 3 months**

**"Fracture during MUA — technical error?"** → **undersized femur with posterior referencing (notching)**

**"Most important factor for final ROM?"** → **preoperative ROM** (TO)

**"ROM after TKA — true?"** → **pre-op ROM is the principal predictor**

**"TKA after plateau ORIF — increased risk of?"** → **limited knee flexion**

**"10–85° at 3 mo — which history?"** → **prior distal femur ORIF**

**"Deteriorating motion after revision?"** → **investigate for infection**

**"Stiff revision candidate — counsel?"** → **focus on regaining near-full extension**

**"9 mo, 5–80°, painful, infection negative?"** → **arthroscopic lysis + manipulation**

**"Routine CPM outcome?"** → **may improve early ROM, not final ROM**

**"Minimum flexion to rise from chair?"** → **95°** (TO)
