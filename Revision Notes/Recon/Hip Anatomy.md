# Hip Anatomy

*Recon · 5 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Osteology

**Joint** — ball-and-socket diarthrodial · stability from **bony architecture**, augmented by **labrum + capsule**

**Acetabulum** — 3 ossification centres: **ilium, ischium, pubis** · **posterosuperior** cartilage thickest (weight-bearing)

**Acetabular orientation** — **anteverted 15°** · **abducted 45°** (caudal tilt)

**Femoral neck** — **anteverted 15°** (relative to condyles) · **neck-shaft angle 125°**

**Femoral head centre** — level with **tip of greater trochanter**

### Acetabular quadrants – screw safety

**Construction** — line **ASIS → acetabular centre** (anterior/posterior) + perpendicular bisector (superior/inferior)

| Quadrant | Structures at risk | Screws |
|---|---|---|
| **Posterior-superior** | **Superior gluteal nerve + vessels**, **sciatic nerve** | **Safest** |
| **Posterior-inferior** | **Inferior gluteal nerve**, **internal pudendal vessels** | Second safest |
| **Anterior-inferior** | **Obturator vessels** (and nerve) | Unsafe |
| **Anterior-superior** | **External iliac artery + vein** | Unsafe |

![Acetabular quadrant system](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12769-f001054f105-002-9780443069918.jpg)

### Capsule & ligaments

**Capsule** — anterior: **intertrochanteric line** · posterior: **intertrochanteric crest** · basicervical + intertrochanteric regions **extracapsular**

**Iliofemoral (Y ligament of Bigelow)** — **strongest** · AIIS → intertrochanteric line · **taut in full extension** · prevents **anterior dislocation / hyperextension**

**Ischiofemoral** — reinforces **posterior** capsule · **pubofemoral** — inferior/anterior

**Ligamentum teres** — cotyloid notch → fovea

**Transverse acetabular ligament** — **most inferior** labrum · landmark for **acetabular version** during THA reaming

### Femoral head blood supply

| Age | Main supply |
|---|---|
| **Birth–4 yr** | **MFCA + LFCA + ligamentum teres** |
| **4 yr–adult** | **Posterosuperior + posteroinferior retinacular vessels** from MFCA |
| **Adult** | **Medial femoral circumflex artery** ✓ (deep branch) |

**MFCA** — branch of **profunda femoris** · ✓ **major supply to femoral head** · **at risk in psoas tenotomy** · **avoid transecting quadratus femoris** in posterior approach (non-arthroplasty)\
✗ Wrong options: internal pudendal · IGA · SGA · LFCA

**Piriformis-start nails** — damage **posterosuperior retinacular vessels** → AVN

**Ligamentum teres artery** — **posterior division of obturator** · insignificant in adults

**Inferior gluteal artery** — internal iliac branch · exits greater sciatic notch **between piriformis and coccygeus**, runs **inferior to / along piriformis** · supplies **short ER + gluteus maximus** · **primary femoral head supply in up to 17%** → preserve in surgical dislocation, posterior dislocation, acetabular fracture · ✓ **found along border of piriformis tendon**

**Vessel-location distractors (OB)** — between pectineus + iliopsoas = **MFCA** · beneath TFL = **LFCA** portion · under gluteus medius = **superior gluteal** · between quadratus femoris + upper adductor magnus = **deep branch of MFCA**

### Vessels at risk

| Vessel | Origin | At risk |
|---|---|---|
| **External iliac → common femoral** | Aorta → external iliac | **Anterosuperior** quadrant screws |
| **LFCA ascending branch** | Profunda | **Direct anterior approach** |
| **MFCA** | Profunda | **Psoas tenotomy**, quadratus transection |
| **Obturator (posterior branch)** | Internal iliac | **Anteroinferior** screws + acetabular retractors · supplies TAL |
| **Superior gluteal** | Internal iliac | Supplies **abductors + TFL** |
| **Internal pudendal** | Internal iliac | Exits greater, **re-enters via lesser sciatic notch** |
| Femoral perforators | Femoral | Supply vastus lateralis |

**Corona mortis** — anastomosis between **inferior epigastric** (external iliac) and **obturator** vessels

### Nerves

**Lumbosacral plexus** — **T12–S4** · **posterior to psoas**

| Sciatic division | Branches |
|---|---|
| **Posterior** | **Peroneal** · superior gluteal · inferior gluteal · nerve to piriformis · posterior femoral cutaneous |
| **Anterior** | **Tibial** · nerve to quadratus femoris · nerve to obturator internus · pudendal · coccygeus |

**Peroneal division** — **lateral** to tibial · runs deep to long head of biceps · **most commonly injured nerve in THA**

**Obturator** — medial compartment · all adductors **except hamstring part of adductor magnus**

**Femoral** — **between iliacus and psoas**

**LFCN** — **L2–L3** · deep to inguinal ligament near ASIS

**Genitofemoral** — **L1–L2** · femoral + genital branches · pierces iliopsoas, runs on its **anteromedial** surface

### Muscles by action

| Action | Muscles |
|---|---|
| **Flexors** | Iliopsoas, rectus femoris, sartorius, pectineus, iliacus |
| **Adductors** | Adductor magnus (adductor part), longus, brevis, minimus, gracilis |
| **External rotators** | Gluteus maximus, piriformis, obturator externus + internus, gemelli, quadratus femoris |
| **Abductors** | Gluteus medius, gluteus minimus, TFL |
| **Extensors** | Gluteus maximus, semitendinosus, semimembranosus, long head biceps, adductor magnus (hamstring part) |

### MRI anatomy (TO)

**Piriformis** — muscle **just below the end of the sacrum** · **sciatic nerve between piriformis and obturator internus** · piriformis syndrome: oedema, accessory head, sciatic impingement

**LFCN on MRI** — small **fat triangle just below ASIS, between sartorius and TFL** → entrapment = **meralgia paraesthetica** (injection site)

**Rectus femoris** — **direct head from AIIS** · **indirect (reflected) head from supra-acetabular ridge**

**Gluteus medius** — **two parts** (anterolateral + posterior) · **fat plane between them is normal**, not a tear · **gluteus minimus** inserts anteriorly on GT

**Gluteus maximus** — ends in **ITB with TFL**, "never inserts directly on bone" (TO — verify: deep lower fibres insert on the gluteal tuberosity)

**Quadratus femoris** — between **GT and ischial tuberosity** · narrow space + oedema → **ischiofemoral impingement**

**Hamstrings** — semimembranosus + **conjoint tendon (long head biceps + semitendinosus)** · semitendinosus fleshy early

**Psoas** — tendon insinuates into iliacus · oedema without tear in dancer → **snapping psoas** · iliopsoas anterior to joint → **iliopsoas bursitis**

**Normal variants** — **lateral femoral tubercle** (not FAI) · physeal scar · small plica · little hip fluid

### Approaches

**Five** — anterior **Smith-Petersen** · anterolateral **Watson-Jones** · medial · direct lateral **Hardinge** · posterior **Southern / Moore**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### MRI sequences (TO)

| Sequence | Bright | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | **Fat only** (bladder urine dark) | Marrow replacement, **AVN line**, trabeculae, healed fractures, muscle atrophy, scarring |
| **T2** | **Fat + fluid** | General |
| **Fat-suppressed (STIR, PDFS, T2FS)** | **Fluid**, fat dark | **All acute conditions** — oedema, effusion, tears, strains |
| **GRE** | — | **Bone detail** — cortex, small calcified / bony lesions |

**Sequence trick** — look at the **urinary bladder** (TO)

**Request** — "**pelvis with right / left hip**" → adds dedicated **oblique axial** (along neck axis) + oblique sagittal sequences · orthogonal axials **never show the neck in continuity** · labrum / FAI need oblique axials (TO)

**Hamstring / rectus femoris tear** — request **hip AND thigh** MRI (pelvis scan stops at lesser trochanter) (TO)

**Labral tear on MRI** — **cleft (sulcus)** through labrum ± **paralabral cyst** (TO)

## 5 Management

### Surgical hip dislocation – capsulotomy (TO)

**Ganz safe dislocation** — ✓ **anterolateral + anteroinferior capsulotomy, hip dislocated anteriorly** (flexion + ER)\
✗ Wrong options: posterolateral/posteroinferior · dislocate posteriorly · anteromedial/anterosuperior

**Why** — first incision **anterolateral along neck axis** avoids **deep branch of MFCA** · anteroinferior limb kept **anterior to lesser trochanter** (main MFCA lies **just superior + posterior to LT**) · extended to rim, turned **posteriorly parallel to labrum** to retracted piriformis

**Preserve** — **IGA** along piriformis border (OB)

## X vs Y

| | **MFCA** | **LFCA** |
|---|---|---|
| Femoral head supply (adult) | **Major** | Minor |
| At risk | Posterior approach (quadratus), psoas tenotomy | **Ascending branch — direct anterior approach** |

## How it's asked

**"Main blood supply of femoral head in adult (femoral neck fracture)?"** → **medial femoral circumflex artery**

**"Surgical dislocation — inferior gluteal artery reliably found?"** → **along border of piriformis tendon**

**"Safe surgical dislocation — capsulotomy?"** → **anterolateral + anteroinferior, dislocate anteriorly** (TO)

**"Safest quadrant for acetabular screws?"** → **posterosuperior** (then posteroinferior)

**"Anterosuperior screw injures?"** → **external iliac vessels**

**"Anteroinferior screw / retractor injures?"** → **obturator vessels**

**"Ascending branch of LFCA at risk in?"** → **direct anterior approach**

**"Most commonly injured nerve in THA?"** → **peroneal division of sciatic**

**"Strongest hip ligament?"** → **iliofemoral (Y of Bigelow)**

**"Fat plane between two parts of gluteus medius on MRI?"** → **normal** (TO)

**"MRI sequence in which only fat is bright?"** → **T1** (TO)

**"Landmark for cup version?"** → **transverse acetabular ligament**
