# Adult Limb Deformity & Correction

*Recon · 2 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & aetiology

**Causes** — childhood growth impairment, metabolic bone disease, severe OA, **trauma / malunion / nonunion**

**Demographics** — **no gender predilection** · young → trauma / childhood growth abnormality · upper limb rare (elbow, wrist malreduction)

**Pathoanatomy** — altered anatomic axis → **mechanical axis deviation (MAD)** → abnormal contact pressure + ligament strain → degeneration → worsening alignment

**Childhood causes** — **Blount's**, focal fibrocartilaginous dysplasia, proximal tibial physeal injury, **Cozen's phenomenon**, SED, femoral anteversion, **LLD**

**Medical causes** — renal osteodystrophy, **rickets** (vitamin D deficient, hypophosphataemic), OI, CP, neurofibromatosis

**Site rule** — adult **genu varum → mostly tibial** (CORA proximal tibia) · adult **genu valgum → mostly femoral** (CORA distal femur)

### Anatomy

**Proximal femur** — neck-shaft angle **130 ± 7°** · anteversion **10 ± 7°** · **GT tip level with head centre** (start of anatomic femoral axis)

**Femoral anatomic vs mechanical axis** — differ by **5–7°** · tibia: **anatomic = mechanical** if no deformity

**Tibial tubercle** — **anterolateral** · triangular shaft

**sMCL** — just posterior to medial epicondyle → **6 cm** below joint line · LCL — posterior to lateral epicondyle → fibular head

**JLCA >2°** — **soft-tissue (ligamentous) laxity**

## 3 Presentation

**Pain by deformity** — **varus → medial knee pain** · **valgus → lateral knee pain**

**Exam** — coronal/sagittal deformity · **femoral anteversion → IR >70°** · residual ↑ external tibial torsion · LLD · hip/knee/ankle ROM, laxity

**Intermalleolar distance >8 cm** — **valgus** (OB)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **weight-bearing full-length AP + lateral** · **patellae facing directly anterior** (rotation) · tube **10 ft** away at knee level · plus WB views of each segment

**CT** — **rotational malalignment** · axial cuts for femoral version

**Mechanical axis (limb)** — **centre of femoral head → centre of ankle**

**Anatomic axes** — **bisect medullary canal** (femur from GT tip, exits just medial to distal femoral midpoint)

**MAD** — **lateral to knee centre = valgus** · **medial = varus**

**LLD** — **top of femoral head → centre of ankle**, compare sides · femur/tibia measured separately

### Deformity analysis – CORA

**CORA** — intersection of **proximal + distal mechanical axes** when deformity is purely angular

**Angulation + translation** — CORA **moves** from the axis intersection

**Multi-level deformity** — unrecognized intermediate segment → CORA may lie **outside bone**

**Osteotomy not at CORA** — induces **translation** in addition to angulation

**Proximity rule** — deformity **closer to joint → greater effect** on that joint's angulation

**Correction order** — **angulation → translation → length → rotation**

**Mechanical vs anatomic axis (TO)** — mechanical axis **always straight** · anatomic axis may be **curvilinear** (femur, sagittal) · **tibial mechanical axis lies lateral to anatomic axis** (TO)

**Normal MAD (TO)** — **1–15 mm** medial · **> 15 mm** = malalignment

**Malalignment test (Paley) (TO)** — MAD → **mLDFA** (abnormal = femoral) → **mMPTA** (abnormal = tibial) → **JLCA > 2°** (laxity / subluxation) → condylar non-parallelism (condylar deficiency)

**Functional CORA (TO)** — any point on the **bisector** of the deformity angle

| Paley osteotomy rule (TO) | Result |
|---|---|
| **Rule 1** — CORA = correction axis = osteotomy level | **Pure angulation**, no translation |
| **Rule 2** — CORA = correction axis, osteotomy elsewhere | Angulation + **relative translation** (bone realigns) |
| **Rule 3** — correction axis away from CORA | Angulation + **iatrogenic translation** |

**Open vs closed wedge (TO)** — open: simple, **lengthens**, needs **graft**, unstable, NV stretch · closed: **stable, no graft, safer for nerves**, **shortens** · dome: always some translation

**Oblique-plane deformity (TO)** — magnitude = **√(AP² + lateral²)**

**Scanogram set-up (TO)** — hip–knee–ankle on one film · **patella forward** regardless of foot · **level pelvis with shoe raise** in LLD

### Diagnostic criteria – varus vs valgus by angle

| | Varus (femoral source) | Varus (tibial source) | Valgus (femoral source) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Threshold | **mLDFA >90°** or **aLDFA >83°** | **MPTA <85°** | **mLDFA <85°** or **aLDFA <79°** · **MPTA >90°** also valgus |
| MAD | Medial | Medial | **Lateral** |

**OB trap values** — LDFA **88° = normal** · LDFA **91° = slight varus** · MAD **5–7° from femoral anatomic axis = normal**

## 5 Management

```
Adult limb deformity
├─ Mild / minimal deficit / unfit → bracing (KAFO), poorly tolerated
├─ Varus, CORA tibia (MPTA <85°) → valgus HTO (MOWHTO or LCWHTO)
├─ Varus, CORA femur (mLDFA >90°) → valgus DFO (lateral closing wedge)
├─ Valgus >12°, CORA femur → varus DFO (lateral opening / medial closing)
├─ LLD >2 cm → gradual lengthening ~1 mm/day
└─ Multicompartment OA → TKA ± extra-articular osteotomy
```

### Nonoperative

**Indications** — subtle deformity within normal parameters · minimal functional deficit · unfit for surgery

**KAFO** — AFO + metal uprights + mechanical knee joint + two thigh bands · bracing **poorly tolerated** · little data on progression

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Derotational femoral osteotomy** | No absolute · **excess femoral anteversion with <10° ER** · patellar maltracking/instability · gait (difficulty running) |
| **Valgus-producing HTO** | Varus MAD + **MPTA <85°**, CORA in tibia · **no arthritis in other 2 compartments** · young, not TKA candidate |
| **Valgus-producing DFO** | Varus MAD + **mLDFA >90° / aLDFA >83°**, CORA in femur · **joint-line obliquity after HTO** in combined deformity |
| **Varus-producing DFO** | Valgus MAD + **mLDFA <85° / aLDFA <79°** · valgus **>12°** · **no medial compartment arthritis** · **age <50** · isolated **lateral** OA · relative: genu valgum with ↑ Q angle, MCL stress |
| **Gradual lengthening** | LLD **>2 cm** (not absolute) |
| **TKA** | Deformity with **multicompartment** arthritis |

### Derotational femoral osteotomy

**Sites** — proximal (intertrochanteric/subtrochanteric, blade/locking plate — **also corrects flexion/extension + varus/valgus**) · diaphyseal (IM nail) · distal supracondylar (medial subvastus, locking plate)

**Diaphyseal IM technique** — derotate to **45° ER + 45° IR** with patella in line/slightly ER relative to ASIS

**Outcome** — **all forms improve outcomes** · ideal correction debated · may alter coronal alignment

**Complications** — undercorrection · overcorrection → retroversion + **out-toeing** (rare)

### Valgus-producing tibial osteotomy (HTO)

| | **Medial opening wedge** | **Lateral closing wedge** |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | Anteromedial | Anterolateral |
| Steps | Biplanar guide · hinge pin medial → lateral just below lateral plateau · protect patellar tendon · pack wedge | **Resect anterior fibular head** · cut proximal to tuberosity · **medial cortex = hinge** · staples/plate |
| Slope | **↑ tibial slope** | **↓ tibial slope** |
| Correction | May **lose correction** over time | — |

**HTO outcome** — **90–95% excellent at 5 yr → 50–70% at 10 yr** · **15–25% need TKA within 10 yr** · worse: **smokers, age >60**, other-compartment OA, obesity, prior failed arthroscopy, insufficient correction

### Distal femoral osteotomy – indications & outcomes

**Valgus-producing DFO** — **lateral closing wedge** · K-wires aimed at far medial cortex just above MFC · **hinge through medial cortex without perforating** → intact medial cortex = ↑ axial + rotational stability

**Varus-producing DFO** — ✓ **lateral opening wedge** (leave **1–2 cm medial hinge**, spacer + lateral plate) **or medial closing wedge** (metaphyseal–diaphyseal junction, medial locking plate)\
✗ Wrong options: lateral closing / medial opening (those make valgus)

**Candidate for lateral opening wedge DFO** — ✓ **young (35-yr) runner, isolated lateral joint-line pain, mechanical axis through fibular head**\
✗ Wrong options: PF/anterior pain · tricompartmental OA → TKA · prior MPFL/TTT anterior pain · varus/medial pain

**37-yr-old, 15° valgus, early lateral OA, failed NSAIDs + unloader brace** → ✓ **lateral opening wedge DFO**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral closing DFO + MOWHTO (varus) · HTO (varus) · TKA (too young)

**Survival** — ✓ **15-yr survival ~50%** (failure **48.5%**) · high mid/long-term deterioration + conversion to TKA

**Better outcome** — **higher preoperative function**\
✗ Wrong options: lower function

**TKA after varus DFO** — **conventional components** if bone stock adequate\
✗ Wrong options: stemmed/constrained in most

### Gradual limb lengthening

**Distraction osteogenesis** — **latency 5–7 days**, then **~1 mm/day**

**Devices** — external fixator (uniplanar, circular/hexapod) · **lengthening IM nail** ± ex-fix (for angular correction)

**Outcome** — LLD to **<1 cm** · can correct small diaphyseal/metaphyseal angulation

**Complications** — ex-fix: **pin-site infection**, scarring, cosmesis · IM: **poor regenerate**, joint contracture/dislocation, nail failure

### TKA with deformity

**Goal** — neutral mechanical alignment via distal femoral + proximal tibial cuts · **valgus cut angle 4–7°** (IM guide on AAF) · tibial IM = EM guides (AAT = MAT)

**Extra-articular deformity** — locate CORA · **CORA further from knee → easier** intra-articular correction · **severe or close-to-joint deformity → extra-articular osteotomy + TKA** · osteotomy better tolerated on **tibia** (equal effect on flexion/extension gaps)

**Constraint** — ↑ constraint / **stems** if collaterals compromised (laxity, prior osteotomy)

**Outcome** — consistent improvement · results **less reliable after prior extra-articular osteotomy**

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Under/overcorrection** | Unfavourable compartment loading · **opening wedge** may **lose correction** |
| **Nonunion** | **~5%** · risk: **smoking, age >60, obesity, far-cortex hinge fracture** |
| **Patella baja** | Proximal tibial osteotomy · **supra-tubercle > infra-tubercle** · pain, harder future TKA |
| **Tendon injury** | **Quadriceps** → femoral osteotomy · **patellar tendon** → tibial osteotomy |
| **Infection** | **1–4%** · ↑ nonunion · risk: smoking, inpatient admission |
| **DFO-specific** | Lateral opening: quadriceps tendon injury, **intercondylar fracture (too distal)**, lateral hinge fracture (↓ stability) · closing: medial hinge fracture |

## X vs Y

| | **Varus-producing DFO** | **Valgus-producing DFO** |
|---|---|---|
| Treats | **Genu valgum**, lateral OA | Genu varum with femoral CORA |
| Wedges | **Lateral opening** or **medial closing** | **Lateral closing** (medial hinge) |
| Criteria | mLDFA **<85°**, valgus >12°, age <50 | mLDFA **>90°** |

| | **Genu varum (adult)** | **Genu valgum (adult)** |
|---|---|---|
| Usual CORA | **Proximal tibia** | **Distal femur** |
| Pain | Medial | Lateral |
| Osteotomy | HTO | DFO |

## How it's asked

**"Young man, 15° valgus, early lateral OA, failed brace — best surgery?"** → **lateral opening wedge DFO**\
✗ Wrong options: HTO · TKA

**"Varus-producing DFO — true?"** → **15-yr survival ~50%**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral closing/medial opening · age >50 medial OA · needs constrained TKA later

**"Lateral opening wedge DFO — which patient?"** → **35-yr-old runner, isolated lateral pain, axis through fibular head**

**"16-yr-old, intermalleolar distance 10 cm, lateral opening DFO planned — alignment?"** → **MAD lateral to knee centre**\
✗ Wrong options: LDFA 88° normal · LDFA 91° varus

**"Normal anatomic–mechanical femoral axis difference?"** → **5–7°**

**"Valgus HTO indication?"** → **MPTA <85°, CORA in tibia, other compartments spared**

**"Order of correcting deformity?"** → **angulation, translation, length, rotation**

**"Osteotomy away from CORA causes?"** → **translation**

**"Opening wedge HTO changes tibial slope?"** → **↑ slope** (closing → ↓)

**"Distraction osteogenesis protocol?"** → **5–7 day latency, 1 mm/day**

**"Osteotomy at CORA with correction axis through CORA?"** → **pure angulation (Paley rule 1)** (TO)
