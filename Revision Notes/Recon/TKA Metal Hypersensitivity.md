# TKA Metal Hypersensitivity

*Recon · 47 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — allergic reaction to metallic TKA components → **persistent pain + stiffness** · **rare**

### Type IV hypersensitivity

**Mechanism** — ✓ **Type IV delayed-type, T-cell (cell-mediated)** hypersensitivity

**Allergen** — **nickel** (in **cobalt-chromium** alloys) · metal ions/wear lodge in tissue, **bind proteins** → initiate reaction · may initiate **early loosening**

| Gell–Coombs | Mediator | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | IgE | Anaphylaxis, bee sting |
| **II** | IgG (antibody, cytotoxic) | Haemolytic anaemia, Goodpasture, Graves |
| **III** | Immune complex | SLE, serum sickness, PAN |
| ✓ **IV** | **T cells, delayed** | **Metal allergy** |
| **V** | IgG stimulatory | — |

## 3 Presentation

**History** — dermatitis, eczema, atopy · **intolerance to cheap jewellery**

**Symptoms** — **vague persistent pain** · **persistent effusion / swelling** · **stiffness** · document ROM

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Genes & lab markers

| Test | Note |
|---|---|
| **Patch test** | Limited evidence of direct link |
| **Lymphocyte transformation test (LTT)** | — |
| **T-lymphocyte-rich immunohistopathology** | Synovium |
| **Infection work-up** | ✓ **Must be negative** (alpha-defensin, synovial fluid) |

**Diagnostic criteria (controversial)** — **positive patch test + positive immunohistology + relief after exchange** (strict) · or **diagnosis of exclusion** after infection + aseptic loosening ruled out

## 5 Management

**Implant exchange** — persistent symptoms affecting quality of life · **hypoallergenic femoral component** (e.g. nickel-free) + **all-polyethylene tibia** if possible · chronic inflammatory **synovitis** at surgery

## How it's asked

**"Jewellery intolerance — Gell–Coombs type of metal hypersensitivity?"** → **IV (delayed type)**

**"Painful TKA, negative infection work-up, jewellery reaction — pathophysiology?"** → **type IV delayed T-cell mediated**
