# THA Periprosthetic Fracture

*Recon · 27 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

| Type | Incidence |
|---|---|
| **Intraop femur — primary uncemented** | **3.5%** (OB: ~4%; 1–18% / 1.5–27.8% quoted) |
| **Intraop femur — cemented** | **0.4%** |
| Intraop femur — primary / revision THA | **0.1–5% / 3–21%** |
| **Intraop acetabulum** | **Cemented 0.2% · cementless 0.4%** |
| **Postoperative femur** | **0.1%** overall · **0.1–3%** primary cementless · most at **stem tip** |
| Overall (Abdel, 32,000 THAs) | **1.7%** · uncemented **3%** vs cemented **0.23%** · female, >65 |

**Definitions (TO)** — **prosthetic** = break in the implant · **periprosthetic** = bone around implant · **interprosthetic** = between two prostheses (ipsilateral THR + TKR) · fracture **distal to stem but in the same bone** = still periprosthetic

**Mechanism (TO)** — mostly **low-energy / trivial fall** in elderly

**Prevention** — **pre-operative templating** · adequate exposure · care with cementless stems in poor bone (RA, osteoporosis) · low threshold to cement

### Risk factors

| Intraoperative femur | Postoperative femur |
|---|---|
| **Female** ✓ · **cementless / press-fit** · osteoporosis · revision · impaction grafting · technical error · **MIS** (controversial) · sclerotic bone (sickle cell) · metabolic bone disease, Paget | Poor bone · **cementless** stems · compromised bone stock · revision · **collarless** stems |

**Other risks (TO)** — elderly, **female**, osteoporosis, **RA**, **chronic steroids** · **mini-incision** (limited exposure) · inadequate reaming · **implant size mismatch** · **uncemented + revision THR** (poor bone stock) · uncemented > cemented

**Timing pattern** — **cementless** stems fracture **early (<6 months)** — wedge-tapered → proximal, cylindrical fully coated → distal split · **cemented** stems fracture **late (~5 yr)** at/below tip

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — AP + lateral of hip **and whole femur** · CT for detail

**Loose-stem clues** — **prodromal thigh/start-up pain** · **subsidence** vs earlier films · **cement-column disruption** · long spiral fracture to stem tip

**Stem stability** — ✓ **intraoperative assessment = gold standard** · up to **20% discrepancy** pre- vs intraop · **B1 vs B2 hard to separate on X-ray**

### General principles (TO)

| Prosthesis | Fracture | Bone stock | Management (TO) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stable | Undisplaced | Good | **Non-op / ORIF** |
| Stable | Displaced | Good | **ORIF** (plate, nail or cerclage) |
| **Unstable** | Any | Good | **Revision** (long stem ± plate) |
| **Unstable** | Any | **Poor** | **Allograft–prosthesis composite / tumour (mega) prosthesis** |

**Goals (TO)** — fracture union + restore / maintain prosthesis stability · **Vancouver = most accepted** classification for THR

### Vancouver classification – postoperative

| Type | Description | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **AG** | **Greater trochanter** · often **osteolysis** | **<2 cm** displaced → nonop, partial WB, limit abduction · **>2 cm** (TO: **>2.5 cm**) → **ORIF claw plate / cables / TBW** · address osteolysis |
| **AL** | **Lesser trochanter** | **Nonoperative** (ORIF if large medial cortex — TO) |
| **B1** | Around/just below stem, **well-fixed** | **ORIF** — locking plate + cerclage cables ± strut |
| **B2** | Around/below stem, **loose**, **good proximal bone** | ✓ **Revision to long porous-coated cementless (or tapered fluted) stem** bypassing **2 cortical diameters** + fixation of fragments |
| **B3** | Loose + **poor / comminuted proximal bone** | **PFR (elderly, low demand)** · **APC / proximal femoral allograft (young)** · tapered fluted stem + struts |
| **C** | **Well below** stem | **ORIF plate**, leave arthroplasty alone |

**TO** — "Vancouver type 2" → ✓ **shaft around stem**\
✗ Wrong options: GT · LT · distal

### Vancouver classification – intraoperative

| Type | Location · subtype | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **A1** | Proximal metaphysis · **cortical perforation** | **Bone graft** (reamings) |
| **A2** | Proximal · **nondisplaced crack** (calcar) | ✓ **Cerclage before inserting stem** |
| **A3** | Proximal · **displaced unstable** | **Fully porous-coated or tapered fluted stem** |
| **B1** | Diaphyseal perforation (cement removal) | Fully porous stem bypassing **2 cortical diameters** ± strut |
| **B2** | Diaphyseal nondisplaced crack (hoop stress) | **Cerclage** if stable · fully porous stem if unstable ± strut |
| **B3** | Diaphyseal displaced | Fully porous stem bypassing ± strut |
| **C1** | Distal to tip, perforation | Morcellized graft, long stem, strut |
| **C2** | Distal nondisplaced | **Cerclage + strut** |
| **C3** | Distal displaced | **ORIF** |

## 5 Management

```
Postoperative periprosthetic femur fracture
├─ Trochanteric (A) → nonop if <2 cm · ORIF if >2 cm · treat osteolysis
├─ Around stem, stable (B1) or below stem (C) → ORIF (plate + cables)
├─ Around stem, loose, good bone (B2) → long cementless revision + fixation
└─ Loose + poor proximal bone (B3) → PFR (elderly) / APC (young)
```

### Intraoperative femoral (calcar) fracture

**Clue** — **change in resistance / audible change** · stem sinks further than final broach → ✓ **calcar fracture**

**Management** — ✓ **remove stem/broach → explore extent (intraop X-ray) → cerclage above lesser trochanter → reinsert SAME-size stem** · braided cables > wires · restores **"barrel hoop"** stability\
✗ Wrong options: continue insertion + cable · keep implant and cable · undersized stem (subsides) · convert to cement without cerclage (loses hoop stress) · delayed staged reconstruction · long stem without fixing calcar

**Same stem gives good outcome** — ✓ **female sex is a risk factor** · Berend: no revisions at 7.5 yr\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ subsidence/early revision · long stem better · press-fit/MIS not risk factors

**Weight bearing — conflict** — OB (Berend, 1349, 211416): ✓ **immediate full WB** after cerclage of minimal calcar fracture · OB (7026, 8433): **partial/protected WB 6 weeks** with 2-weekly X-rays — read the options

**Extends past lesser trochanter / displaced (A3)** → **fully porous-coated or tapered fluted** long stem · distal cracks found post-op → touch-down WB

**Mechanisms** — **proximal**: rasping, insertion, dimension mismatch · **mid**: exposure/preparation force · **distal**: straight-stem tip impacting **femoral bow**

### Intraoperative acetabular fracture

**Mechanism** — during **cup impaction** · ✓ **most common complication of press-fit cup** (TO) · risk: **under-reaming >2 mm** (most common cause), **elliptical/monoblock** cups, osteoporosis, cementless, dysplasia, radiation

**Stable cup** → ✓ **no change** or **add screws**, protected WB **8–12 wk** (OB: flat-foot 2 wk) · nondisplaced anterior column with stable cup → ✓ **multiple screws + brief WB restriction**\
✗ Wrong options: larger cup (propagates) · plating · cup-cage · 6 wk bed rest

**Unstable cup** → screws ± **jumbo cup** ± ORIF + revision · **posterior column displaced** → ✓ **remove cup, fix posterior column, antiprotrusio cage** (or cage-over-cup)\
✗ Wrong options: larger cup · graft · WB protection · cemented PE

### Acetabular periprosthetic – Peterson (TO)

**Timing** — usually **intraoperative** rather than postoperative (TO)

| Peterson | Features | Management (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **Type 1** | **Stable**, undisplaced, minimal pain | **Retain cup ± trans-acetabular screws** |
| **Type 2** | **Displaced / unstable** cup, pain, instability | **Revision + column plating ± antiprotrusio cage** |

### Vancouver A, B1 & C – ORIF

**B1** — ✓ **ORIF: lateral locking plate + proximal cables** · stable stem confirmed intraop\
✗ Wrong options: traction · revision · cemented stem

**B1 fixation details** — ✓ **locking screws proximally** (bicortical where possible; unicortical alone insufficient) · **2–3 cables** between LT and stem tip · **≥3 bicortical screws / 6–8 cortices distal** · strut allograft only for bone loss/migration\
✗ Wrong options: avoid cables · <3 distal screws

**B1 reoperation** — ✓ **nonunion** most common (B2 revision + ORIF → **infection**)

**Bridge plating (relative stability)** — ✓ **endochondral (secondary) healing**\
✗ Wrong options: intramembranous (needs compression)

**Ogden construct (TO)** — ✓ **periprosthetic femur fractures (Vancouver B)** · cables proximally + screws distally (now unicortical locking proximal, bicortical distal)

**Vancouver C (TO)** — **distal femoral LCP** · plate should **span to distal femoral metaphysis** to prevent re-fracture · interprosthetic → **Vancouver C** for the THR (**Su type I** for the TKR) → **long bridging plate ± bone graft**

**Surgical pearls (TO)** — **prepare for revision even in B1** (long stem + plates on the table) · pass cables **through muscle, not subperiosteal** · avoid periosteal stripping · stem **> 2 cortical diameters** beyond fracture

**Vancouver C** — ✓ **plate + proximal cables + distal screws** (✗ Wrong options: retrograde nail (no canal) · conventional plate · traction) · **interprosthetic** (THA + TKA) → ✓ **lateral locking plate spanning whole femur** (✗ Wrong options: nail · revision · strut alone · total femur)

**Below long revision stem, well fixed** → ✓ **ORIF plate + cables/wires**\
✗ Wrong options: strut + wires alone (no rotational control)

**FNB 2024** — mid-shaft fracture 3 months after THA, well-fixed stem → options nail / plating ± cerclage / traction / long-stem revision — answer not given (well-fixed stem → plating fits B1/C)

**AG >2 cm** → ✓ **ORIF trochanteric claw plate** · **AG with osteolysis + PE wear (eccentric head)** → ✓ **revision THA** (liner exchange, address lysis, fix GT)

### Vancouver B2 – long cementless revision stem

**Treatment** — ✓ **revise to long uncemented diaphyseal-engaging stem** (extensively porous-coated cylindrical or **modular tapered fluted titanium**) bypassing fracture by **≥2 cortical diameters** + **ORIF of fragments** (cables/plate)

**Why not cemented** — cement **extrudes into fracture → nonunion**, mid-term loosening, poorer results in active patients · proximally coated stem (no proximal fixation — ↑ loosening/nonunion) · ORIF alone (loose stem) · strut alone when bone stock adequate · traction · PFR (bone stock adequate) · antibiotic spacer (no PJI)

**Typical stems** — prodromal thigh pain + subsidence · cement column disruption · loose at 2 weeks with subsidence · "easily extractable" stem at surgery

**B2 ORIF alone (relative)** — low demand, **ASA ≥3**, **CCI ≥5**, anatomic reconstruction possible, no prior loosening/pain (González-Martín) · revision favoured: high demand, ASA <3, comminution / ≥2 zones, prior pain

**Loose cemented stem eroding lateral cortex (impending fracture)** → ✓ **cementless revision**\
✗ Wrong options: cemented ± strut · strut + cables · PFR

**Fracture around antibiotic spacer, infection cleared (WBC 500, α-defensin −)** → ✓ **reimplant uncemented diaphyseal-engaging stem + cables**\
✗ Wrong options: new spacer · traction + suppression · ORIF

**Normal ABI (0.9–1.3) with sluggish refill + B2** → ✓ **revision + ORIF**, serial exams\
✗ Wrong options: vascular consult/arteriogram · US · traction · exploration

![Vancouver B2 fracture](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob5013-shows-vancouver-b2-fx.jpg)

### Vancouver B3 – proximal femoral replacement

**Options** — **PFR / megaprosthesis / tumour prosthesis** (elderly, low demand, dementia, can't restrict WB → immediate WB) · **APC / proximal femoral allograft / impaction grafting** (young, restore bone) · diaphyseal cementless or tapered fluted stem + struts

**Not an option (TO)** — ✓ **long porous-coated stem with plate + cable** construct alone

**Typical answers** — 18 yr post-THA, loose cemented, poor bone → ✓ **PFR** (✗ Wrong options: NWB · ORIF · long cemented · fully porous + ORIF) · 88-yr-old with dementia → ✓ **PFR** (✗ Wrong options: proximally coated · ORIF · impaction grafting · strut) · Paget + coagulopathy → ✓ **PFR** (fast) · 91-yr-old minimally ambulatory with start-up pain → ✓ **PFR** · active 83-yr-old → ✓ **PFR** (✗ Wrong options: metaphyseal press-fit stem) · loose + progressive bone loss → **cementless tapered fluted modular stem + proximal strut**

**Loose stem + loose migrated cemented cup + poor bone (B3)** → ✓ **revise both components**

![Vancouver B3](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob5013-vancouver-b3.jpg)

### Fully porous-coated stem fracture

**Biggest risk** — ✓ **inadequate proximal bone stock** → distal ingrowth + no proximal support → **cantilever** fatigue mid-stem · also **stem <13.5 mm**, weight **>88 kg**, **young age (<60)**, ETO nonunion · revise via **ETO**, address proximal bone (graft/PFR)\
✗ Wrong options: older age · offset · exposure

## 6 Complications

### Associated neurovascular injury

**Loose stem, comminuted fracture, absent pulses, arteriogram occlusion** → ✓ **traction → vascular shunt → revision THA → definitive vascular repair**

**Cerclage cabling of proximal femur — bleeding** → ✓ **deep femoral (profunda) artery / perforators** (close to diaphysis, tethered by perforators) · **SFA** at risk **distally** (adductor canal) · **midshaft safest** · late pseudoaneurysm (swelling, falling Hct)
