# Knee Osteoarthritis

*Recon · 15 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence (symptomatic)** — **knee 240 / 100,000 / yr** · hip 88 / 100,000

**Modifiable** — **articular trauma** · occupation (repetitive bending) · muscle weakness · **large body mass** · **metabolic syndrome**

**Non-modifiable** — **female** > male · age · genetics · race

**Metabolic syndrome** — **central obesity**, **dyslipidaemia** (↑ TG, ↑ LDL), **hypertension**, **↑ fasting glucose** · ✓ **PVD is NOT part** · strong link to **knee** (not hip) OA · **BMI most predictive** · no CRP link

### Disparities in arthroplasty

**Race** — ✓ **African-American males have lower TJA rates than white or Hispanic males** · Hispanics < whites · not explained by symptom perception

### Pathophysiology

**Cartilage** — **↑ water**, ↑ **permeability**, ↓ proteoglycan (GAG), disorganized + ↓ collagen, ↑ **chondrocyte activity** incl. ✓ **↑ aggrecanase production**\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ GAG · ↓ permeability · ↓ IL-6 · ↓ water movement

**Synovium** — mild → hypervascular → thick + vascular

**Bone** — lytic lesions with **sclerotic edges** (vs RA) · cysts late · subchondral marrow oedema with **~97% ↑ intraosseous pressure**

**Osteophytes** — pathologic **endochondral ossification** via ✓ **Indian hedgehog (Ihh)** · Ihh inactive in normal adult cartilage\
✗ Wrong options: PPARγ (adipocytes) · RANKL · OPG · sclerostin (Wnt)

**Histology** — loss of superficial chondrocytes · **tidemark** replication + breakdown · fissuring · eburnation

### Genes & lab markers

**MMPs** — stromelysin, plasmin, **aggrecanase-1 (ADAMTS-4)** · **TIMPs** inhibit · **MMP/TIMP imbalance**

**Cytokines** — **IL-1** (catabolic), IL-6, **TNF-α**

**Genetics** — **non-Mendelian** · vitamin D receptor, oestrogen receptor 1, IL-1, IL-4, matrilin-3, BMP-2/5

**DMOADs (TO)** — monoclonal antibodies against **ADAMTS (aggrecanase, ADAMTS-5 > 4)**, **nerve growth factor (NGF)**, **VEGF** · ✓ **incorrect: "target fibroblast growth factor"** · anti-NGF: **tanezumab, fasinumab**, fulranumab (stopped 2016) · IV + SC routes (not oral) · AE: **abnormal peripheral sensation** (paraesthesia)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — function-limiting pain (walking distance) · night/rest pain · activity swelling · stiffness · instability, locking, catching

**Exam** — habitus · **limb alignment** · effusion · **flexion contracture >5°** · **flexion <110°** · ligaments

### Biomechanics – adduction moment & gait

**Knee adduction moment** — surrogate of **medial tibiofemoral contact force** · ✓ **↑ in medial compartment OA** (varus — GRF passes medial to knee) · large peak → ↑ pain + progression · ↓ by **offloader braces** · ↑ flexion moment also ↑ medial load

**Not linked to ↑ adductor moment** — cervical myelopathy · hip OA (slow, leans forward, pelvis drops) · ankle OA (↑ double support) · scoliosis

**Antalgic gait in knee OA** — knee held in **slight flexion** (quadriceps avoidance, ↓ capsular tension) · **toe walking** · ↓ stride · ↓ stance time · ✓ **not seen: walking predominantly on the heel**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **weight-bearing** AP · optional **sunrise** + **PA 30° flexion (Rosenberg)**

**Findings** — JSN · osteophytes · eburnation · subchondral sclerosis · cysts · pattern (medial, lateral, PF)

### Kellgren & Lawrence (AP weight-bearing)

| Grade | Findings |
|---|---|
| **0** | No JSN or reactive change |
| **1** | **Doubtful** JSN + possible osteophytic lipping |
| **2** | **Definite osteophytes** + possible JSN |
| **3** | **Moderate multiple osteophytes + definite JSN** + some sclerosis + possible bone-end deformity |
| **4** | **Large osteophytes + marked JSN + severe sclerosis + definite deformity** |

**Original** — coal miners, **1952** · grade ≤2 none/possible OA, >2 definite

![Kellgren–Lawrence grade 3](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12287-kellgren-3.jpg)

### Loose bodies & radiographic signs (TO)

**Most common source of loose body in knee** — ✓ **osteophytes (OA)** · young → **OCD** · multiple → **synovial chondromatosis**\
✗ Wrong options: damaged menisci

**Grand piano sign** — ✓ **knee**: anterior femoral resection surface in TKA → marker of **correct femoral rotation**

## 5 Management

```
Symptomatic knee OA
├─ All → exercise + education + oral/topical NSAIDs (strong)
├─ BMI >25 → weight loss · medial OA → unloader brace (moderate)
├─ Persisting → IA steroid (moderate) · RFA (genicular) · PRP > HA
├─ Young, unicompartmental + malalignment → HTO (varus) / DFO (valgus)
└─ Failed nonop, end-stage → TKA (UKA if isolated compartment)
```

### AAOS guidelines – nonoperative management

| Strength | 2021/2022 CPG (OB) |
|---|---|
| **Strong FOR** | **Exercise** (supervised, self-managed, aquatic) · **patient education** · **oral + topical NSAIDs** · **oral acetaminophen** |
| **Moderate FOR** | **Weight loss** (BMI >25) · **IA corticosteroid** · **unloader brace** · cane |
| **Limited FOR** | **PRP** · acupuncture, massage · supplements (turmeric, ginger, glucosamine, chondroitin, vitamin D) · **denervation (RFA)** |
| **Moderate AGAINST** | **IA hyaluronic acid** (NNT 17) · **arthroscopic lavage/debridement** |
| **Strong AGAINST** | **Oral narcotics incl. tramadol** · **lateral wedge insoles** |

| Modality | Older CPG positions |
|---|---|
| HA | 2009 inconclusive → **2013 strong against** (weakest evidence) → 2021 moderate against |
| IA steroid | 2013 **inconclusive** (for/against) → 2021 moderate for |
| Tramadol | 2013 **recommended** → 2021 **strong against** |
| 2008 CPG | **Low-impact aerobic exercise "A"** · glucosamine/chondroitin "A" against · arthroscopic debridement without mechanical symptoms "A" against · **lateral heel wedge "B" against** · valgus brace inconclusive |
| Orthobullets list | Acupuncture strong against · glucosamine/chondroitin strong against · viscosupplement moderate against · needle lavage moderate against · lateral wedge moderate against · **arthroscopic debridement/lavage strong against** · meniscal debridement inconclusive |
| Acetaminophen | Max dose **3000 mg/24 h** (was 4000) |

**Not recommended (TO)** — ✓ **HA injection**\
✗ Wrong options: NSAIDs · weight loss · self-management + exercise

**Exercise** — supervised + home best · **benefit lost 6 months after stopping**

**Medial OA brace** — ✓ **medial unloader (valgus)** for correctable varus\
✗ Wrong options: varus brace · Cho-Pat (patellar tendon) · ACL brace · sleeve

### Injections & orthobiologics

**IA steroid** — moderate for · short-term relief · **avoid repeat soon** (last 3 weeks ago, 10-day relief → discuss TKA)

**HA** — rooster-comb preparations → **contraindicated with chicken/egg allergy** ✓ · also joint/skin infection · no anti-inflammatory effect

**PRP** — ✓ **better clinical outcomes than HA** (**level 1** RCTs, meta-analyses) · best in **KL 2–3** (not KL 4) · **leukocyte-poor > leukocyte-rich** (more effective, safer) · injection site irrelevant · AAOS **limited** (downgraded)

**BMAC** — ✓ **highest IL-1 receptor antagonist** concentration (> LP-PRP, LR-PRP, HA)

**Genicular RFA** — ablates **superior lateral, superior medial, inferior medial** genicular nerves · ✓ **superior to corticosteroid** (RCTs) and to HA · safe · OK in **diabetes**, renal, CV, GI risk

**Glucosamine** — ✓ **substrate for proteoglycan** (chondroitin sulfate + hyaluronate)\
✗ Wrong options: COX-2 · viscosity · pain mediator · anti-apoptosis

### Arthroscopy

**Lavage/debridement vs placebo** — ✓ **no significant benefit** in chronic OA (1 wk–2 yr) · only for **mechanical** symptoms (meniscal tear, loose body)

### Surgical options – osteotomy, TKA

**HTO** — young, **medial** unicompartmental OA · AAOS limited evidence · ideal: **thin, active, stable, <60** → ✓ **outcomes approach TKA**\
✗ Wrong options: HTO faster recovery · more complete relief · better in old

**DFO** — 25-yr-old, **valgus + lateral JSN** → ✓ **varus-producing DFO** · most delay TKA ≥10 yr\
✗ Wrong options: varus HTO (joint-line obliquity) · lateral UKA · TKA · meniscectomy

**UKA** — isolated unicompartmental disease · **TKA has lower revision rates than UKA** · not tricompartmental

**TKA** — symptomatic OA + failed nonop · 70-yr-old with meniscal tear + tricompartmental change → ✓ **TKA** (✗ Wrong options: meniscectomy · HTO · UKA) · 62-yr-old, diffuse pain, no mechanical symptoms → ✓ **arthroplasty** (✗ Wrong options: PRP · meniscectomy · chondroplasty · allopurinol)

**TKA technique facts** — **CR = PS outcomes** · **patellar resurfacing**: no difference in pain/function, **↓ reoperation** · **drains not recommended** · **sensor-balancing device**: ✓ **no added benefit** in ROM/PROMs at 2 yr, ↑ time + cost
