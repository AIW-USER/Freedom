# TKA Peroneal Nerve Palsy

*Recon · 53 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **0.3–2%** (Orthobullets) · **0.3–1.3%** (OB) · **0.3–9.5%** after valgus TKR (TO)

**Prognosis** — **≥50%** improve/recover without further treatment

### Risk factors (valgus & flexion contracture)

| ✓ Risk factor | Note |
|---|---|
| ✓ **Valgus + flexion contracture** | **Highest risk** (✗ Wrong options: valgus only · varus ± FFD · FFD only) |
| **Valgus >12°** · **correction of 20° valgus** | Nerve tethered at fibular head → stretch |
| **FFD >10–15°** | Stretch |
| **Tourniquet >120 min** | — |
| **Post-op epidural analgesia** | Unprotected limb → compression |
| **Aberrant retractor** | — |
| **Pre-op neuropathy** (central or peripheral) · lumbar laminectomy (double crush) | — |
| ✗ Wrong option: **excessive medial release** | **Not** a risk factor |

**Lateral OA + FFD >15° + valgus >12° — counsel for** → ✓ **CPN injury** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: DVT · instability · quadriceps contracture

**Large valgus + FFD — higher risk than typical for** → ✓ **peroneal nerve injury**\
✗ Wrong options: DVT · wound · loosening · quad rupture

### Anatomy & release risk

**At tibial cut level** — CPN lies ✓ **superficial to (on) lateral head of gastrocnemius** (protected by it) · in interval between **biceps femoris and lateral gastrocnemius**\
✗ Wrong options: deep to arcuate ligament · on popliteus · on bone

**Distance from posterolateral tibia** — **9–15 mm** (Orthobullets) · mean **14.9 mm**, never <**9 mm**, ↑ in larger legs (OB MRI) · as close as **7–9 mm in extension** (cadaver)

**Greatest risk** — ✓ **release of posterolateral capsule with knee extended**\
✗ Wrong options: posterior capsule · PLC in flexion · ITB (anterior to nerve)

**Danger zone (TO)** — PLC release in triangle of **popliteus, tibial cut surface, posterior ITB fibres** · ITB pie-crusting = **safe zone** (TO)

## 3 Presentation

**Signs** — no ankle dorsiflexion or toe extension · ↓ sensation **dorsum of foot + first web space**

## 5 Management

```
Foot drop after TKA
├─ Recovery room → remove dressings + flex knee 20–30°
├─ Complete foot drop → AFO + PT (keep passive DF)
├─ No recovery at 3 mo → EMG; consider decompression / exploration (3–6 mo)
└─ Persistent palsy, tibialis posterior 5/5 → tibialis posterior transfer to dorsum
```

### Immediate management

✓ **Unwrap compressive dressings + flex knee**\
✗ Wrong options: AFO acutely · immediate EMG · exploration · reassurance

### Late management

**EMG** — after **3 months** if no improvement

**Exploration / neurolysis** — no recovery at **3–6 months** · explore at posterior border of biceps femoris

### Tendon transfer for persistent palsy

**Failed AFO + neurolysis, 18 mo, passive DF intact, inversion 5/5** → ✓ **tibialis posterior transfer to dorsum** (through interosseous membrane → **lateral cuneiform**)\
✗ Wrong options: continue AFO · repeat neurolysis · peroneus tertius ± TAL (same anterior compartment, paralysed)

### Valgus TKR technique (TO)

**Tibial cut** — referenced **6–8 mm off medial compartment** after removing lateral osteophytes (TO)

**Femur** — IM entry **more medial** · distal valgus cut **3°** (not 5–7°) · lateral distal resection minimal (1–2 mm) or none · medial ≤10 mm (usually 7–8) (TO)

**Rotation** — hypoplastic LFC → use **~6° ER** or anterior referencing · Whiteside line unreliable (trochlear dysplasia) → **TEA** or parallel to tibial cut (TO)

**Lateral release orders (TO)**

| Author | Sequence |
|---|---|
| **Ranawat** | **PCL** → PLC (at tibial cut, cautery) → ITB + LCL pie-crust · popliteus preserved |
| **Favorito** | **LCL** (tightest) → popliteus → PLC → ITB → lateral gastrocnemius |
| **Whiteside** | Tight in **flexion → LCL, popliteus** (near epicondyle) · tight in **extension → ITB, PLC** · LHG only for FFD |

## X vs Y

| | **ITB release** | **PLC release (extension)** |
|---|---|---|
| Nerve risk | **Safe zone** (ITB anterior to nerve) | ✓ **Danger zone** |

## How it's asked

**"Lateral OA, FFD >15°, valgus >12° — counsel for?"** → **CPN injury** (TO)

**"Deformity with most risk of peroneal palsy?"** → **valgus + flexion contracture**

**"Peroneal palsy risk factors — EXCEPT?"** → **excessive medial release**

**"Large valgus + FFD — higher risk of?"** → **peroneal nerve injury**

**"Recovery room, cannot dorsiflex?"** → **unwrap dressings and flex the knee**

**"Where is CPN at tibial cut level?"** → **superficial to lateral head of gastrocnemius**

**"Lateral tight in flexion + extension — greatest nerve risk during?"** → **posterolateral capsule release with knee extended**

**"Persistent foot drop at 18 mo, inversion 5/5?"** → **tibialis posterior transfer to dorsum of foot**
