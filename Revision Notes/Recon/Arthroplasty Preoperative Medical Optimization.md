# Arthroplasty Preoperative Medical Optimization

*Recon · 3 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Overview

**Five domains** — **medical optimization** · **preoperative counselling** · **VTE prophylaxis** · **anaesthesia plan** · **pain management plan**

**Goal** — identify modifiable **risk factors** for postoperative complications

### Genes & lab markers

**HbA1c** — screen hyperglycaemic patients · **HbA1c >6.7 → ↑ wound complications** · target roughly **<8** (varies by surgeon/hospital)

**Nicotine testing** — preoperative, to confirm cessation

**Toxicology screen** — failed screen → ↑ mortality, readmission, reoperation

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Cardiac & vascular preoperative evaluation

**Noninvasive cardiac testing** — **≥3 risk factors in a sedentary patient**: CAD/MI · heart failure · prior CVA · diabetes mellitus or chronic renal disease

**No stress test** — if patient can do **moderate activity (climb a flight of stairs)**

**Typical stem** — ✓ **67-yr-old for TKA with MI + CVA + DM**\
✗ Wrong options: spine patient with hypercholesterolaemia/HTN/DVT · healthy drinker · hip fracture with CRF/cancer/obesity · 81-yr-old who climbs stairs

**PVD + ↓ pulses before TKA** — next step ✓ **ankle-brachial index** of affected limb → vascular surgeon decides revascularization vs clearance\
✗ Wrong options: angiography first · venous Dopplers · MRI popliteal fossa · radiographs for calcified plaque

## 5 Management

### Medical optimization – risk factors

| Risk factor | Threshold / action |
|---|---|
| **Diabetes** | **HbA1c >6.7** ↑ wound complications · target ~**<8** |
| **Obesity** | **BMI >40** used in studies (continuous risk, no clear cut-off) · **>5% weight loss** may ↓ risk · bariatric surgery may help · ↑ AKI, CV, wound, infection |
| **Cardiac stents** | Delay elective surgery if DAPT stopped within **30 days of BMS** / **12 months of DES** |
| **Clopidogrel** | Discuss with cardiologist · **stop 7 days preop** → ↓ bleeding/transfusion, no ↑ CV events · restart ASAP |
| **Transfusion** | Perioperative transfusion → ↑ complications |
| **Dialysis** | **10–20× ↑ complications** · ESRD may do better with **kidney transplant before arthroplasty** (weigh dialysis septicaemia vs immunosuppression) |
| **MRSA** | Screen + decolonize (**mupirocin** nasal / **chlorhexidine** wipes) · universal decolonization may be cost-effective |
| **Smoking** | ↑ complications + infection · formal cessation programme · **6 weeks cessation** · nicotine testing |
| **Illicit drugs** | **5× ↑ mortality** · ↑ infectious + non-infectious complications |
| **Inflammatory arthritis** | **Hold biologics** before surgery |

### Smoking cessation

**Minimum cessation before elective THA** — **6 weeks** (Orthobullets) · TO (FNB 2024) asked this with options 3/4/6/8 weeks — answer not provided by the source

### Preoperative counselling

**Expectations** — satisfaction driven more by **expectations** than function · patients **over-optimistic** · set expectations for pain, function, complications

**Social support** — affects **LOS, readmission, non-home discharge** · psychological distress worsens postoperative pain · absence of anxiety → lower pain, better function

### VTE prophylaxis

| Body | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **ACCP** | **Mechanical compression + one agent**: warfarin, LMWH (enoxaparin), **aspirin**, factor Xa inhibitor (apixaban, rivaroxaban), fondaparinux, dabigatran |
| **AAOS** | **Pharmacologic, mechanical, or both** if no increased VTE risk |

**Aspirin** — favoured: oral, compliance, cost, **low bleeding**

### Anaesthesia

**Neuraxial (spinal)** — ↓ stress vs GA → ↓ surgical time, **infection**, CV events, **transfusion**, LOS

### Multimodal pain management

**Preoperative orals** — **NSAIDs (COX-2)** + **pregabalin** → ↓ opioid use, ↓ pain, ↑ ROM, **no ↑ bleeding**

**Postoperative** — continue above + judicious opioids · **opioid adverse events up to 50%** of post-arthroplasty complications

**Periarticular injection** — local anaesthetic + opioid + NSAID · TKA: **= femoral nerve block**, cheaper/safer/easier · THA: ↓ opioid, better pain + function

**Not effective** — ✓ **intra-articular pain catheter**\
✗ Wrong options: acetaminophen · opioids · gabapentin · NSAIDs (all valid components)

| Block | Joint | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Lumbar plexus / psoas, femoral, sciatic | THA | Good analgesia, may affect muscle function |
| **Femoral nerve** | TKA | **Quadriceps weakness** |
| **Adductor canal** | TKA | **Similar pain control to femoral block, no quad weakness** |
| Sciatic | TKA | Posterior knee pain · significant motor effect |

## How it's asked

**"Which patient needs preoperative noninvasive cardiac testing?"** → **sedentary, ≥3 of CAD/MI, CHF, CVA, DM/CKD**\
✗ Wrong options: able to climb stairs

**"PVD with ↓ pulses before TKA — next evaluation?"** → **ankle-brachial index**\
✗ Wrong options: angiography · venous Doppler

**"Not an effective part of a multimodal regimen?"** → **intra-articular pain catheter**

**"Smoking cessation before elective THA?"** → **6 weeks** (Orthobullets; TO answer not given)

**"HbA1c associated with wound complications?"** → **>6.7**

**"Delay elective surgery after DES?"** → **12 months** (BMS **30 days**)

**"Clopidogrel before TKA?"** → **stop 7 days preop**

**"Dialysis patient for THA — complication risk?"** → **10–20× ↑**

**"TKA block sparing the quadriceps?"** → **adductor canal**
