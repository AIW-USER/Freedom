# Knee Biomechanics

*Recon · 14 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Joint type (TO)

**Knee** — ✓ **ginglymus (hinge)** synovial joint · two joints: **tibiofemoral + patellofemoral**\
✗ Wrong options: condyloid · saddle · pivot

| Synovial type | Example |
|---|---|
| Ball & socket | Hip, glenohumeral |
| **Hinge (ginglymus)** | **Elbow, knee, IP joints** |
| Saddle | Thumb CMC |
| Plane | ACJ |
| Pivot | Atlantoaxial, proximal radioulnar |
| Condyloid (ellipsoid) | MCP, wrist |

**Fibrous** — sutures, gomphoses, syndesmoses (middle radioulnar/tibiofibular) · **cartilaginous** — synchondroses (physis, 1st rib), symphyses (pubis, disc)

### Patellofemoral mechanics

**Function** — transmits quadriceps tension to patellar tendon · **↑ extensor lever arm** · **patellectomy ↓ extension force 30%**

**Lever arm (TO)** — ✓ **greatest at 20–30° flexion → quadriceps force needed is minimum** · force ↑ markedly in **last 20° of extension** (less patella in trochlea)

**Motion** — sliding · patella moves **7 cm caudally** in full flexion · **maximum PF contact at 45° flexion**

| PFJRF | Orthobullets | TO |
|---|---|---|
| Walking | — | **0.5–1 × BW** |
| Vertical jump | — | **2.4–4.6 × BW** |
| Stairs | **Descending 2–3 × BW** | **3.5–6 × BW** (descent > ascent) |
| Running | — | **4.5–7 × BW** |
| Squat | **up to 7 × BW** | **7 ×**; **deep squat up to 20 × BW** |

**TO ascending order** — ✓ **walking < jumping < stair ascent < stair descent < running < deep squat** · PFJRF **↑ with ↑ flexion**\
✗ Wrong options: more going up stairs · less with more flexion

### Q angle

**Definition** — ASIS → centre of patella → tibial tuberosity

**Normal** — Orthobullets: extension **male 13°, female 18°**, flexion **8°** · TO: **male 14°, female 17°** (standing)

**↑ Q angle (TO)** — ✓ **↑ femoral anteversion** · **external tibial torsion** · **laterally displaced tubercle** · **genu valgum** · ↑ lateral pull → PF instability\
✗ Wrong options: medialized tubercle · internal tibial torsion · genu varum

### Tibiofemoral mechanics

**TFJRF** — **3 × BW walking** · **4 × BW climbing**

**ROM** — **3° hyperextension → 155° flexion** · limited by thigh–calf contact · **normal gait needs 0–70°**

## 2 Mechanism

### Knee kinematics – rollback, rotation

**Instant centre of rotation** — contact point · **polycentric**

**Medial pivot** — medial femoral contact **relatively stationary** · lateral contact **moves posteriorly** with flexion (**rollback**) → avoids impingement, ↑ flexion · ✓ **medial condyle translates much less than lateral**\
✗ Wrong options: equal · lateral less

**Beyond 120°** — **both** condyles roll back

**Tibial rotation with flexion** — ✓ **tibia internally rotates** · initial IR in first **~20°**, maintained, then **more IR past 90°** · **tibia ER with extension**\
✗ Wrong options: femur IR

**Paradoxical motion** — ✓ **contact point moves anteriorly during flexion** (ACL-deficient CR TKA fails to roll back)

### Screw-home mechanism

**Orthobullets** — **tibia ER 5° in last 15° of extension** · cause: **medial tibial plateau longer** than lateral · locks knee → ↓ quadriceps work standing

**TO** — **open chain** (foot in air): tibia **ER ~10° in last 30°** of extension · **closed chain** (foot planted): **femur IR ~10°** · factors: **medial femoral condyle longer** (30° lateral curve → 10–15° tibial ER in last 20°) + quadriceps lateral pull tensions ACL

**Unlocking** — **popliteus** · open chain: ✓ **tibia IR** to allow flexion · closed chain: ✓ **femur ER** during flexion

**TO answers** — ✓ "tibia must IR slightly to flex in open chain" · ✓ "with flexion normal tibia rotates internally" · ✓ "foot plantigrade — femur ER during flexion"\
✗ Wrong options: tibia IR in last 30° of extension · "brought by cruciates" (mainly bony anatomy)

### Ligament restraints

| Load | Primary restraint |
|---|---|
| **Varus** | **LCL** |
| **Valgus** | **Superficial MCL** |
| **Anterior translation** | **ACL** — AM bundle **tight in flexion**, PL bundle **tight in extension** · also axial rotation |
| **Posterior translation** | **PCL** — AL bundle **tight in flexion**, PM **tight in extension** |
| **External rotation** | **Posterolateral corner** |
| **Internal rotation** | **ALL + Kaplan fibres** (TO) |

**ACL** — origin **posteromedial lateral femoral condyle** (semicircular) → insertion **anterior to / between tibial eminences**

**PCL** — origin **anterolateral medial femoral condyle** → **tibial sulcus** below articular surface

**Kaplan fibres (TO)** — deep ITB → distal femur · proximal bundle **53.6 mm**, distal **31.4 mm** above lateral epicondyle (distal near **superior lateral genicular artery**) · ✓ **restrain internal rotation** · sectioning ↑ IR at **15–90°** (esp. 60–90°) vs ALL (15–30°) · **co-dominant with ALL** in anterolateral stability · control pivot shift\
✗ Wrong options: ER · anteromedial stability · recurvatum

### Injury mechanism – valgus collision (TO)

**Foot planted, hit on lateral distal leg** → ✓ **forced flexion + valgus → ACL tear**\
✗ Wrong options: isolated valgus MCL · varus LCL

**ACL** — **non-contact ~70%** · flexion-valgus + femoral adduction + tibial ER + ankle eversion (cutting) · stiff landing (<20° flexion) · deceleration · hyperextension · risk: poor core, weak hip abduction, ↑ valgus, heel-strike landing

**MCL** — **direct blow to lateral knee (valgus)** — commonest

**PCL** — **dashboard** (posterior force on flexed tibia) · **fall on flexed knee with foot plantarflexed** · hyperflexion · hyperextension (± ACL)

### Loading & rehabilitation forces

**Landing (basketball)** — ✓ **females → greater total valgus knee loading**

**Anterior tibial translation** — ✓ **terminal non-weight-bearing (open-chain) extension 60° → 0°** → avoid early after ACLR\
✗ Wrong options: WB extension/flexion · NWB flexion

### Terminal extension – VMO activity (TO)

**Open chain, terminal extension (15–20°) + tibia medially rotated** → ✓ **VMO maximal**\
✗ Wrong options: VL · VI · RF

**Closed chain** — **squats with external rotation** → max VMO
