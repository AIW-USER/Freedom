# TKA Polyethylene Wear & Manufacturing

*Recon · 54 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — macroscopic premature PE failure from excess loading + mechanical loosening

**Catastrophic failure** — typical of **TKA** (vs **osteolytic** failure in THA)

**Primary variables** — **PE thickness** · **articular geometry** · **kinematics** · **sterilisation** · **manufacturing** · **surgical technique** (tight flexion gap ↑ sliding wear; tight PCL + anterior tibial slope ↑ stress)

**Treatment** — revision TKA or isolated **PE exchange** if implants stable · X-ray: narrowing of tibiofemoral implant interface

### Polyethylene thickness

**Rule** — keep thinnest PE **>8 mm** · <8 mm → loads exceed PE yield strength (**12–20 MPa**) → catastrophic failure (older PE data)

**Labelled vs true** — "8 mm" insert may be **4–5 mm** PE on ~**3 mm** tray · deeper tibial cut avoids thin insert · young + active + thin PE = ↑ risk

### Articular surface design and geometry

| | **Congruent ("dished")** | **Flat** |
|---|---|---|
| Rollback | Sacrificed | ↑ rollback, ↑ flexion |
| Anatomy | Less anatomic | More anatomic, PCL sparing |
| Contact area | **↑** | ↓ |
| Contact stress | **↓** | **↑** → catastrophic wear |

**Best** — **biplanar congruency** (coronal + sagittal) · maximise contact area, minimise contact stress

### Insert conformity & stability

**Anterior-stabilised (AS) vs CR** — ✓ **↑ contact area + ↑ stability** in PCL-deficient knee · highly conforming + **large anterior lip** blocks anterior femoral translation · no box cut

### Kinematics

**Sliding wear** — **ACL sacrificed + PCL retained** · worst in **CR + flat insert** · least in **PS or AS + congruent insert**

**Varus alignment** → catastrophic failure · **excess rollback** → dyskinetic sliding → cracking

**Solutions** — correct varus · congruent design · ↑ posterior slope · **PS** for incompetent PCL · posterior contact point

## 2 Mechanism

### Sterilisation – gamma in air & oxidation

**Gamma radiation** — commonest sterilisation · breaks C–H bonds → **free radicals**

| Environment | Pathway | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Air (O₂ rich)** | **Oxidation** (chain scission) | ✓ **Early failure**: **subsurface delamination**, **pitting**, **cracking**, fatigue · ↑ wear + **osteolysis** · abandoned |
| **Inert (argon, nitrogen, vacuum)** | **Cross-linking** | ↑ resistance to **adhesive + abrasive wear** · ↓ ductility + fatigue resistance · ✓ **↓ pre-implantation oxidation** |
| **Ethylene oxide / gas plasma / H₂O₂** | No radicals, **no cross-linking** | No oxidation, but no wear benefit |

**Oxidised PE mechanics** — ✓ **↑ elastic modulus + ↓ strength** (and ↓ ductility) → brittle

**Gamma in nitrogen produces** — ✓ **free radicals**\
✗ Wrong options: annealing · gas plasma · uncross-linking · remelting

**Shelf life** — gamma-in-air packaged PE stored **5 yr** → ✓ **substantially oxidised** (still sterile, no creep) · oxidation ∝ time exposed to O₂ · (✗ Wrong options: OR FiO₂ matters (no radiation))

**Doses** — sterilisation **2.5–4 Mrad** (conventional 2.5 in air) · HXLPE **5–10 Mrad** (5–15 Mrad) in inert environment, separate earlier step · little benefit **>10 Mrad**

### Cross-linking & thermal processing (annealing vs remelting)

**Most improvement in wear** — ✓ **gamma irradiation in argon (inert)** · ✓ **radiation** has most positive effect on wear resistance\
✗ Wrong options: ram bar · gas plasma · EtO · air

**↑ Irradiation dose** — ✓ **↑ volumetric wear resistance** · ↓ tensile strength, fracture toughness, crack resistance · ✓ principal negative: **↓ fatigue strength**\
✗ Wrong options: yellow · unstable · absorbs fluid

**Radiation + heating** — ✓ **↓ wear but ↑ fracture risk** (↓ ductility) · forms interpenetrating network · fewer, smaller particles vs smooth counterface · **worse vs rough counterface** (↓ abrasive resistance)

**Free radicals removed by** — ✓ **thermal processing** (or **vitamin E**; ✗ Wrong options: vitamin C)

| | **Remelting** | **Annealing** |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature | **Above** melting point | **Below** melting point |
| Free radicals | ✓ **Most removed** (largest reduction) | **More remain** → oxidation risk |
| Crystallinity | **Partial crystalline → amorphous** | Preserved |
| Mechanical properties | **↓** | ✓ **Better maintained** |
| Wear | **Equivalent** | Equivalent |
| In vivo oxidation | Seen in both | Seen in both |

**Annealed vs remelted XPE** → ✓ **more free radicals, better mechanics, equivalent wear**

**Preferred** — annealing (better mechanics) (OB8309) · remelting most effective radical removal

**HXLPE vs conventional (THA RCT)** — head penetration **0.003 vs 0.051 mm/yr** · ✓ **superior wear** · ↓ osteolysis · larger head: volumetric wear equal or slightly higher

### Manufacturing – compression moulding

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Ram bar extrusion + machining** | Powder pushed through heated barrel → rods → machined · variable quality |
| **Calcium stearate additive** | **Fusion defects** · ↓ mechanics |
| **Sheet compression moulding** | Sheets up to 8" → machined |
| ✓ **Direct compression moulding (net shape)** | Powder → final mould · **no machining**, high gloss · **lowest fatigue crack formation + propagation** · wear **~50%** of machined (Orthobullets/Miller) · wear **equal** to ram extrusion (Comprehensive Ortho Review) — read the options · slow, costly |

**Machining** — shear stretches chains **1–2 mm** below surface (amorphous > crystalline) → radiation-susceptible → **white band of subsurface oxidation** → delamination + fatigue cracks

**Crystallinity** — ideal **~50%** · **>70%** ↑ failure

**"Perfect storm" for catastrophic wear** — metal-backed tray + **thin PE** · **flat coronal** design · **CR + flat insert** · **ram bar + calcium stearate** · **gamma in air** · **machined surface**

## X vs Y

| | **Oxidised PE** | **Cross-linked PE** |
|---|---|---|
| Made by | Gamma **in air** | Gamma in **inert** gas / vacuum |
| Wear | ↑ delamination, pitting | ↓ adhesive + abrasive |
| Mechanics | ↑ modulus, ↓ strength | ↓ ductility, ↓ fatigue strength |
