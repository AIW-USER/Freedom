# THA Stability Techniques

*Recon · 34 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Four determinants of stability

**1** — **component design** · **2** — **component position** · **3** — **soft-tissue tensioning** · **4** — **soft-tissue function**

### Component design – head size & head-neck ratio

**Head-neck ratio** — head diameter ÷ neck diameter · ✓ **larger ratio → greater arc of motion before impingement** (primary arc) · ✓ **larger head = most closely linked to ↑ ROM**\
✗ Wrong options: approach · extended offset · anteversion >18° · abduction <35°

**Skirts** — extend neck length but ✓ **↓ head-neck ratio → ↑ impingement + dislocation**\
✗ Wrong options: large ratio · 15° stem/cup anteversion · 50° abduction are safe

**Jump distance** — translation before dislocation (~½ head diameter) · larger heads seat deeper → ↑ jump distance → ↑ stability

**36 vs 28 mm head in revision** — ✓ **delays neck–socket impingement**\
✗ Wrong options: compensates abductor deficiency · ↓ volumetric wear (↑) · ↓ trunnion stress (↑) · compensates vertical cup

**Large head will NOT compensate for** — **abductor deficiency** or **vertical cup** · ✓ **high inclination negates the benefit of a larger head** (dislocates with minimal translation)

**Primary arc ↑ by** — ✓ **larger head**\
✗ Wrong options: collar · hooded liner · offset (affects JRF not arc) · more anteversion

### Neck-shaft angle

| Change vs native | Leg length | Offset |
|---|---|---|
| **↑ NSA (valgus)** | **↑** | **↓** |
| **↓ NSA (varus)** | **↓** | **↑** |

**Longer leg + less offset post-op** → ✓ **increased prosthetic neck-shaft angle**\
✗ Wrong options: medialized cup (↓ offset only) · standard offset stem (offset only) · longer neck (↑ both) · constrained liner

**Trendelenburg + 3 dislocations + already long leg** → ✓ **more varus (high-offset) stem** — ↑ offset without length\
✗ Wrong options: skirt · longer neck · valgus stem · medialized cup

### Acetabular liners

**Elevated (lipped) rim** — placed posteriorly ↑ stability · ↓ revision for instability

**Lateralized (offset) liner** — +4–10 mm acetabular offset (+ small lengthening) · ↑ tension · ✓ **↑ acetabular component loosening** (eccentric load, torsion at liner–shell + bone–implant interfaces, ↑ JRF + wear) — only liner type linked to early loosening

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Component position

| Component | Target |
|---|---|
| **Cup anteversion** | **5–25°** (Lewinnek 15 ± 10) |
| **Cup abduction (inclination)** | **30–50°** (Lewinnek 40 ± 10) |
| **Stem anteversion** | **10–15°** (hard to adjust in cementless) |
| **Combined anteversion** | **37°** (stiff spine: 35–40°) |

**Approach adjustments** — **posterior → more anteversion** · **anterior → less anteversion**

| Malposition | Result |
|---|---|
| **Excessive retroversion** | **Posterior** dislocation |
| **Excessive anteversion** | **Anterior** dislocation |
| **Excessive abduction (vertical, high theta)** | **Posterosuperior** dislocation · **eccentric PE wear**, late instability |
| **Excessive adduction (horizontal)** | **Flexion impingement**, **inferior** dislocation |

**Cup medialization** — ↓ body-weight moment arm → ↓ JRF ✓ (needs ↑ femoral offset to keep abductor tension) · not possible with resurfacing

**AIIS hypertrophy** — impingement + instability in straight flexion → resect · ✓ **direct head of rectus femoris** arises from AIIS (reflected head — groove above acetabular brim; sartorius — ASIS)

### Spinopelvic mobility & cup position

**Normal standing → sitting** — pelvis tilts posteriorly **20–35°** (15–20°) · femur flexes **55–70°** · sacral slope change **10–30°** (11–29°) · pelvic-femoral angle change **51–69°** · sitting anteinclination **41–63°** · standing PFA 165–195° · sitting CSI 162–198°

**Stiff spine** — ✓ **Δ sacral slope <10°** → **hip hypermobility** → ↑ dislocation · every 1° ↓ spinopelvic motion → 0.9° ↑ femoral motion

| Phenotype | Pelvis | Impingement → dislocation |
|---|---|---|
| **Stuck sitting** (APP tilted back 10–13°) | Fixed posterior tilt | **Posterior impingement → ANTERIOR dislocation** ✓ (even via posterior approach, if combined anteversion excessive) |
| **Stuck standing** (APP neutral) | Fails to tilt back when sitting | **Anterior impingement → POSTERIOR dislocation** |

**Stiff-spine targets** — Lum: inclination **45–50°**, anteversion **20–25°**, combined **35–40°** · McKnight (stuck sitting): **less inclination (35–40°) + less anteversion (15–20°)** · large head / **dual mobility**, restore offset (high-offset stem), navigation/robotics

## 5 Management

### Femoral offset & abductor tension

**Femoral offset** — ✓ **perpendicular distance from head centre to femoral shaft axis** (line 4) · **horizontal offset (label B)** most affects JRF (neck length also ↑ offset but lengthens leg; vertical height = length only)

| ↑ Offset | ↓ Offset |
|---|---|
| ↓ impingement · **↓ JRF** · ↑ soft-tissue tension **without ↑ length** · ↑ stability · ↓ wear, ↓ cup strain, ↓ limp | **Instability** · **abductor weakness** · **gluteus medius lurch / Trendelenburg** · ✓ **↑ osseous (bony) impingement** · ↑ JRF |
| Risk: lateral prominence → **trochanteric bursitis** | ↓ bursitis risk |

**High-offset stem** — ✓ **↑ soft-tissue tension** (✗ Wrong options: ↑ JRF · ↑ length · ↑ cup loosening (that is lateralized liner) · ↑ dislocation) · ✓ **improves stability by ↑ abductor tension** (✗ Wrong options: shorter neck · low offset · larger head · medialized cup)

**Exacerbates Trendelenburg** — ✓ **decreasing femoral offset**\
✗ Wrong options: extended offset neck · bigger head · longer neck · inferior cup

**↓ JRF** — ✓ **medialize cup** or ↑ offset\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ NSA · ↓ offset · ↑ femoral anteversion · head-neck ratio (stability only)

**Ways to ↑ offset** — longer neck · **↓ NSA** · medialize neck + lengthen · **trochanteric advancement** · lateralized liner

### Soft-tissue function

| System | Conditions impairing stability |
|---|---|
| **Central nervous** | Stroke, cerebellar disease, **dementia**, MS, **Parkinson**, myelopathy, delirium, **alcoholism** |
| **Peripheral nervous** | **Spinal stenosis** (medius = **L5**), neuropathy, radiculopathy, paresis |
| **Local tissue** | Trauma, myoligamentous disruption, deconditioning, ageing, irradiation, osteolysis, collagen disease, myopathy, malignancy, infection |

**Abductor integrity** — **gluteus medius** main abductor · chronic insufficiency → **gluteus maximus ± TFL transfer**

### Constrained & lipped liners

**Constrained indications** — absent abductors · recurrent dislocation with **well-positioned** implants · failed unconstrained solutions · lower threshold in elderly, low-demand, neurologically impaired

**Contraindication** — ✓ **poorly positioned cup** · young patient with large ROM (relative) · risks: loosening, ↓ ROM, mechanism failure\
✗ Wrong options: well-positioned cup in elderly · absent abductors · cage · central neurologic decline

### Recurrent instability revision

**Vertical (high theta) cup with recurrent dislocation** → ✓ **uncemented acetabular revision**\
✗ Wrong options: lipped liner · bigger head/thinner liner · cemented cup (↑ revision) · constrained (early failure on malposition)

**Posterior capsular insufficiency + irreparable medius tear (trunnionosis ALTR)** → ✓ **split gluteus maximus transfer** (one limb to anterior capsule / minimus, one to GT / medius; ± TFL — Whiteside)\
✗ Wrong options: constrained liner alone · PFR + direct repair · medialize cup (worsens) · high offset + dual mobility alone

**Dislocation rates** — primary **0.5–6%** · revision **2–20%** · patient risks: Parkinson, dementia, spasticity, alcoholism, previous surgery, ON, obesity, hip fracture

## X vs Y

| | **Larger head** | **↑ Offset** | **Cup medialization** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main effect | ↑ arc + jump distance | ↑ abductor tension, ↓ JRF | ↓ body-weight arm, ↓ JRF |
| Leg length | — | **No change** | — |
| Fixes abductor deficiency? | **No** | Partly | **No (worsens tension)** |

| | **Stuck sitting** | **Stuck standing** |
|---|---|---|
| Dislocation | **Anterior** | **Posterior** |
| Cup | Less anteversion + inclination | More anteversion |

## How it's asked

**"Increases ROM before impingement?"** → **larger femoral head-to-neck ratio**

**"CoC impingement — increase primary arc?"** → **larger femoral head**

**"Most likely to ↑ dislocation?"** → **skirted femoral head**

**"Impingement in straight flexion from labelled structure — muscle origin?"** → **direct head of rectus femoris (AIIS)**

**"36 mm vs 28 mm head in revision?"** → **delays neck–socket impingement**

**"High cup inclination negates benefit of?"** → **larger femoral head**

**"Template with reduced offset leads to?"** → **↑ osseous impingement**

**"Most closely related to ↑ post-op ROM?"** → **larger femoral head**

**"Worsens Trendelenburg?"** → **decreasing femoral offset**

**"↑ abductor tension for stability?"** → **high-offset femoral component**

**"Line representing femoral offset?"** → **head centre to femoral shaft axis (line 4)**

**"Adjust to ↓ JRF most?"** → **horizontal offset (B)**

**"High-offset stem leads to?"** → **↑ soft-tissue tension**

**"↓ JRF during THA?"** → **medialize the cup**

**"Trendelenburg, 3 dislocations, long leg — implant change?"** → **more varus femoral stem**

**"Longer leg + less offset post-op — cause?"** → **↑ prosthetic neck-shaft angle**

**"Stuck sitting — risk if ignored (posterolateral approach)?"** → **anterior dislocation**

**"Parameter indicating hypermobile hip?"** → **Δ sacral slope <10°**

**"Contraindication to constrained liner?"** → **poorly positioned cup**

**"Liner linked to early cup loosening?"** → **lateralized (offset) liner**

**"Recurrent dislocation, vertical cup?"** → **uncemented acetabular revision**

**"Capsular insufficiency + irreparable medius tear?"** → **gluteus maximus split transfer**
