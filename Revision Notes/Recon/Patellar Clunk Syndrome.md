# Patellar Clunk Syndrome

*Recon · 17 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Prevalence** — **3.5%** (mean) · up to **18% (12–21%)** of **PS TKAs** (TO) · rarer with newer designs

**Timing** — average **12 months** (Orthobullets) · **7–11 months** (OB) · **3–12 months**, occasionally up to 3 yr (TO) · more with patellar resurfacing

**First described** — **Hozack 1989** (TO)

### Pathophysiology

**Lesion** — **fibrous nodule at superior pole of patella / posterior surface of distal quadriceps tendon** (suprapatellar, articular side)\
✗ Wrong options: inferior pole

**Mechanism** — nodule **entrapped in intercondylar box during flexion** → **jerked out during active extension** → painful clunk at **30–45°** (~40°)

**Cause** — trauma to quadriceps tendon at patellar resection · impingement of tendon on **sharp anterosuperior edge of the box** · abrupt transition ridge between box and trochlea

### Risk factors

| Category | Factors |
|---|---|
| **Preoperative** | **Patella baja** / short patellar tendon · **valgus** alignment · fibrosynovial proliferation at quadriceps insertion (resect it) · **previous knee surgery** / revision |
| **Patellar component** | **Small** button · placed **low (distal)** — exposes superior pole · **over-resection / thin button** (↓ quadriceps offset) · uncovered cut bone (resect lateral facet) · implant overhang at distal pole |
| **Femoral component** | **PS design** · **↑ posterior condylar offset** · **smaller (undersized) femur** · **flexed** femoral component (posterior IM entry point — anterior femoral bow) · **high intercondylar box ratio** (trochlea extends proximal + anterior) / **larger box** |
| **Other** | **Thick PE insert** (raises joint line → relative baja) · excessive distal femoral resection · **anterior tibial tray** · forced flexion |

**Intercondylar box ratio (ICBR)** — box height ÷ AP height of femoral component · **high ICBR** → earlier quadriceps contact → ↑ clunk · **low ICBR** favourable

**Prevention** — low-ICBR design · side-specific femur (7° oblique groove) · trochlear deepening + lengthening · exact sizing · avoid downsized/flexed femur (**anterior IM entry point**) · avoid anterior tibial tray · patellar button **as proximal as possible, not beyond superior border** · avoid over-resection/thin button · **neutral or posterior tibial tray**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — patellofemoral pain · **popping, catching** · may be painless

**Exam** — palpable **"pop"/"catch" as knee extends from flexion at ~40°** (30–45°)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Diagnosis** — mostly **clinical** · **ultrasound** shows suprapatellar fibrous tissue · **MRI** can confirm (TO)

### Takahashi classification (TO)

| Type | Finding |
|---|---|
| **I** | **Isolated suprapatellar fibrous nodule** (true PCS) |
| **II** | Impinging **hypertrophic synovitis**, no nodule |
| **III** | Nodule **+** generalized hypertrophic synovitis |

### Related entities

| Entity | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Patellar clunk** | Discrete nodule · **clunk on extension 30–45°** |
| **Patellofemoral crepitus** | Fibrosynovial hyperplasia **without discrete nodule** · grinding at **30–60°** with loading · 2 months–2 yr |
| **Patellar crunch** | Scar **around the patellar component** · crunching going **extension → flexion** |
| **Intra-articular fibrous band** | Transverse band prevents patellar seating |

## 5 Management

```
Patellar clunk after PS TKA
├─ Mild → observe / quadriceps exercises (20–60% improve, TO)
├─ Severe or failed nonop → arthroscopic nodule resection
└─ Failed arthroscopy / component problem → open excision ± patellar revision
```

### Causes & management

**Conflict** — OB: **nonsurgical management rarely successful → surgical debridement** best ✓ · TO: **conservative quadriceps exercises help 20–60%**, so "conservative has no role, arthroscopy for all" is ✓ **incorrect** · Orthobullets: observe mild, resect severe\
✗ Wrong options: rest/NSAIDs · patellectomy · patellar revision · lateral release/realignment

**Arthroscopic resection** — good outcomes, **very low recurrence**, ↑ Knee Society scores · recurrence rare after arthrotomy + button revision

**Open excision** — failed arthroscopy · planned component revision / realignment · limited lateral incision under local

### Component rotation & patellar maltracking

**Patellar pain/subluxation/dislocation** — ✓ **internal rotation of tibial component** (lateralizes tubercle) · also **IR of femoral component**\
✗ Wrong options: lateralized tibial tray (moves tubercle medially, helps) · lateralized femur (moves trochlea laterally, helps) · femoral ER

**Correct rotation** — femur **parallel to epicondylar axis** at 90° · tibial tray centred on **medial third of tibial tubercle**

## X vs Y

| | **Patellar clunk** | **CAM jump** | **Midflexion instability** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cause | Suprapatellar nodule in PS box | **Large flexion gap** in PS knee | Elevated joint line, multiradius design, MCL laxity |

## How it's asked

**"True about PCS?"** → **undersized femoral component ↑ risk** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: more common in CR · inferior pole · grinding in flexion

**"PCS occurs?"** → **superior pole, knee extending from flexion** (TO)

**"PCS results from?"** → **scar at superior pole impinging on femoral component during extension** (TO)

**"Incorrect statement on PCS?"** → **conservative treatment has no role** (TO)

**"PS TKA, ↑ posterior offset, small patellar button — complication?"** → **patellar clunk**\
✗ Wrong options: maltracking · midflexion instability · CAM jump

**"Best management (OB)?"** → **surgical debridement**

**"Clunk on extension after PS TKA — associated?"** → **larger femoral box**\
✗ Wrong options: posterior tibial tray · oversized button · proximal button · ↓ offset

**"Patellar pain/dislocation from component orientation?"** → **internal rotation of tibial component**

**"Angle of clunk?"** → **30–45° (~40°) on extension**
