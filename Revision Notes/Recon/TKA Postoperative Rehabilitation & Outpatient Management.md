# TKA Postoperative Rehabilitation & Outpatient Management

*Recon · 56 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Phases** — **inpatient extended care** (rehab/SNF) · **outpatient care** · earlier discharge to rehab → better outcomes · home discharge criteria as in hospital

## 5 Management

### Rehabilitation (cryotherapy, home PT)

**Outpatient PT** — **2–3×/week for ≥2 weeks** · ✓ **closed-chain concentric** exercises · crutches → cane → unassisted

**Self-directed home PT vs formal outpatient PT** — ✓ **similar clinical outcomes** (ROM, MUA rates) + **cost savings** in selected (motivated, equipped) patients · up to **⅓** need formal PT later\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ cost · ↓ ROM · 50% need OPPT · OPPT saves money

| Modality | Point |
|---|---|
| **Cryotherapy** | Local temperature ∝ synovial **PGE2** · ✓ **better ROM at discharge** · ↓ blood loss but not transfusion · (✗ Wrong options: pain, swelling, analgesia · long-term ROM) · AAOS: moderate evidence no outcome benefit |
| **Aquatic** | Buoyancy ↓ compressive load + gives resistance |
| **Balance training** | Proprioception, postural control |
| **NMES** | Overrides CNS-related activation deficits |

### Opioid use risk factors

**↑ post-op opioid use** — ✓ **cigarette smoking** · **pre-op opioid** (chronic use) or **benzodiazepine** use · **↑ BMI** · psychiatric comorbidity · **younger age (<55)** ·\
✗ Wrong options: valgus alignment · robotic TKA may ↓ need

### Return to sport

**Preferred** — **low-impact closed chain**: elliptical, **biking**, **golf** · **running discouraged** (impact ↓ implant longevity)

**Golf after TKA** — ✓ **handicap change <5 strokes** · play frequency usually ↑ · drive length **↓ ~12 yd after TKA** · **↑ ~3 yd after THA** · lead (opposite-handed) knee stressed\
✗ Wrong options: unable to return · fewer rounds · >20 yd longer
