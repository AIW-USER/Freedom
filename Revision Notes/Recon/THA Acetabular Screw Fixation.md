# THA Acetabular Screw Fixation

*Recon · 19 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Acetabular quadrants & screw neurovascular risk

**Construction** — line 1: ✓ **ASIS → centre of acetabulum** (→ posterior fovea) · line 2: **perpendicular** at acetabular centre

| Quadrant | Zone | At risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Posterior-superior** | **Target / safe zone** — ideal for screws | **Sciatic nerve**, **superior gluteal nerve + vessels** (✓ SGA — TO) |
| **Posterior-inferior** | **Caution zone** — screws **≤20 mm** | **Sciatic nerve**, **inferior gluteal nerve + vessels**, **internal pudendal nerve + vessels** |
| **Anterior-inferior** | **Danger zone** | **Obturator nerve, artery, vein** |
| **Anterior-superior** | **"Death zone"** (Orthobullets/TO) · "danger zone" (OB) | ✓ **External iliac artery + vein** (vein more medial → more at risk) |

**General rule** — posterior quadrants safe except **long posteroinferior** screws · safe areas **superior + posterior** · external iliac at risk with **anteromedial** screws

**High hip centre** — anterosuperior + anteroinferior structures shift into the **posterosuperior quadrant** · safest = **peripheral half of posterosuperior** quadrant · elevated revision centre → **sciatic** risk with posterosuperior screws

## 6 Complications

### Screw injuries

**Foot drop + radicular pain after revision, screw in sciatic notch** → ✓ **posterior-superior quadrant** (long screw, high hip centre)

**Gluteus maximus lurch** (trunk extends at heel strike) after transacetabular screws → ✓ **posterior-inferior quadrant** → **inferior gluteal nerve** injury\
✗ Wrong options: posterior-superior (sciatic/SGN) · anterior-inferior (obturator) · anterior-superior (external iliac)

**Greatest vascular risk** → ✓ **anterior-superior**\
✗ Wrong options: superior · posterior-superior · posterior-inferior · superolateral

### Retractor placement & vascular injury

**External iliac vessels** — only **7 mm** from bone at **AIIS**, may lie on bone → **anterior retractor in anterosuperior quadrant** (✓) or anterosuperior screw

**Common femoral artery/vein** — separated from capsule only by **iliopsoas**, crosses hip near **AIIS** → anterior acetabular retractor (✓ position 2, anterosuperior) · esp. **anterolateral approach**

**Inferior retractor under transverse acetabular ligament** → **obturator** vessels/nerve

| Retractor position | Injury |
|---|---|
| **Anterior-superior** | **External iliac / femoral vessels** |
| **Anterior-inferior / directly inferior (under TAL)** | **Obturator** vessels + nerve |
| **Posterior-inferior** | Sciatic, inferior gluteal, internal pudendal |
| **Posterior-superior** | Relatively safe — superior gluteal, sciatic |

### Avoiding prosthesis impingement

**Proud anterior cup rim** → **iliopsoas impingement** · evaluate with **cross-table lateral** + **anaesthetic injection of iliopsoas sheath**

| Cause | Impingement type |
|---|---|
| **Medialized + raised cup** (↓ femoral offset) | **Bone-on-bone** |
| **Lateralized cup** | **Metal neck-on-metal cup** |
| **Head-to-neck ratio <2:1** (skirted heads, small heads) | Prosthetic impingement |

## How it's asked

**"Posterosuperior quadrant screw risks (TO)?"** → **superior gluteal artery**

**"Zone most risky for external iliac vessels?"** → **anterior-superior**

**"Line drawn to define quadrants?"** → **ASIS to centre of acetabulum (A and C)**

**"Foot drop, screw in sciatic notch after revision?"** → **posterior-superior quadrant**

**"Greatest risk of vascular injury?"** → **anterior superior**

**"Trunk extension at heel strike after transacetabular screws?"** → **posterior-inferior quadrant (inferior gluteal nerve)**

**"Posterior approach, bleeding from external iliac after retractor?"** → **anterior-superior**

**"Femoral artery bleeding from retractor?"** → **anterosuperior (position 2)**

**"Caution zone screw length?"** → **≤20 mm**

**"Obturator vessels at risk?"** → **anterior-inferior quadrant / retractor under TAL**
