# Prosthetic Joint Infection

*Recon · 18 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

| Procedure | Orthobullets / OB | OB (alternative) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary THA** | **0.3–1.3%** | 1.7% |
| **Primary TKA** | **1–2%** | 1–3% · 2.5% |
| **Revision THA** | **3–4%** | 3–5% |
| **Revision TKA** | **5–6%** (highest) | 5–10% |

**Order of risk** — ✓ **primary hip < primary knee < revision hip < revision knee** · knee higher (mobile, superficial) · most PJIs in first **1–2 yr** · aseptic revision ≪ revision for infection

**Early TKA revision (<2 yr)** — infection, instability, stiffness · ✓ **infection > stiffness** · late (>2 yr): **aseptic loosening**, infection (> instability) · PE wear now uncommon, occurs **late**

**Pathologic fracture salvage (PFR for metastasis)** — ✓ **deep infection = most common complication**

### Risk factors

| Category | Factors |
|---|---|
| **Preoperative** | **Active infection** (skin, deep, joint, septicaemia) · **previous surgery / prior infection** · active skin lesions (erysipelas, ulcer, folliculitis — contraindication until healed) |
| **Immunosuppressant drugs** | **Anti-TNF** (infliximab, etanercept, adalimumab, certolizumab, golimumab) · leflunomide · corticosteroids |
| **Diabetes** | **HbA1c >7** or **fructosamine ≥293 µmol/L** (Orthobullets) · OB: ✓ **HbA1c >8%** ↑ infection · fructosamine **≥292** |
| **Other medical** | CKD · acute liver failure · **malnutrition (albumin <3.5**, lymphocytes <800) · **HIV CD4 <400** · cancer/metastasis · depression |
| **Inflammatory arthropathy** | **RA** (seropositive men), psoriasis (controversial), AS |
| **Lifestyle** | **Morbid obesity (BMI >40)** · smoking · alcohol · IVDU · poor oral hygiene |
| **TO / OB extras** | **Male sex** · **BMI ≥30** · **allogeneic transfusion** · **hinged / constrained / PS** implant (vs CR) · indwelling catheter **>48 h** (UTI) · anticoagulation (haematoma) · trauma diagnosis |
| **Lower risk** | Uncemented TKA, **PFJ, UKA** vs cemented TKA · OA diagnosis · **age >65** (risk ↓ 1.2%/yr after 65 — Kaye) |

**Not a risk factor** — ✓ **asymptomatic bacteriuria** (TO) — no screening/treatment/delay needed · TO text elsewhere: a marker of risk but urine is not the source

**Not associated (TO)** — ✓ **age >65**\
✗ Wrong options: BMI ≥30 · transfusion · male

**RA THA (OB)** — ↑ **PJI** (e.g. 16,000 WBC, 88% PMN at 6 wk) · ↑ intraop fracture, revision, dislocation, wound infection · **no ↑ mortality or DVT**

**Stem-cell transplant** — arthroplasty ≥1 yr after (TO)

### Cutibacterium (Propionibacterium) acnes (TO)

**Organism** — **anaerobic gram-positive rod** (diphtheroid) · skin follicles · **shoulder**, men · ~**10%** of PJI (underestimated)

**Course** — ✓ **slow onset** · delayed (3–24 mo) / late · **CRP and WBC often normal**\
✗ Wrong options: high WBC

**Culture** — needs **anaerobic media, 7–15 days** (hold **≥14 days**) · sonication, molecular tests help\
✗ Wrong options: 48 h

**Antibiotics** — **penicillin G, ceftriaxone** first choice · **rifampin** active against biofilm · **resistant to metronidazole + fosfomycin** · aminoglycosides weak

### Biofilm & quorum

**Biofilm** — forms **within 4 weeks** · **15% cells, 85% polysaccharide (glycocalyx)** · adheres to implant, shields from immunity · infection invades **prosthesis–bone interface** · cannot be safely removed → **explant if >4 weeks**

**Antibiotics alone fail because** — ✓ **biofilm** · **sessile** bacteria more resistant than planktonic\
✗ Wrong options: resistance · polymicrobial · diabetes · planktonic bacteria

**Quorum** — ✓ **critical bacterial density needed to establish biofilm** → colony shifts **from replication to virulence/biofilm**\
✗ Wrong options: MIC · LD50 · empiric options index

### Prevention & prophylaxis

**Screening** — optimize risk factors · **nasal mupirocin** decolonization (MSSA/MRSA) · **no routine urine culture** unless UTI symptoms · **stop DMARDs 4–6 wk** · revision: **normal ESR/CRP off antibiotics** · reported penicillin/cephalosporin allergy → **allergy testing** (cephalosporins ↓ morbidity/mortality vs alternatives)

**Timing** — **within 30 min** of incision (Orthobullets) · **within 1 h** (OB ✓ cefazolin ↓ PJI) · **>10 min before tourniquet** · continue **24 h**

**Weight-based cefazolin** — **1 g <60 kg · 2 g 60–120 kg · 3 g >120 kg** (>120 kg often under-dosed)

**Aseptic revision** — ✓ **single weight-based dose + 3 more weight-based doses over 24 h** (q8h) (130 kg → 3 g)\
✗ Wrong options: 48 h (no benefit) · single dose · fixed 2 g

**Dilute povidone-iodine** — ✓ **cost-effective ↓ infection** · **0.35% for 3 min** · must be **rinsed out** (cytotoxic) · not an antibiotic (no resistance) · WHO/CDC/ICM support

**OR** — **vertical laminar airflow** · limit traffic · antiseptic skin wash · glove changes

**Antibiotic-loaded cement in primary TKA — conflict** — OB (2023): low-dose ALBC (1 g vanc + 1 g tobra / 40 g, <3%) → ✓ **↓ PJI** (no loosening, no AKI, ↑ cost) · OB (2021): routine ALBC in primary TKA **not shown to ↓ PJI** — read the stem

**No effect** — **staples vs sutures** · absorbable monofilament vs staples

**Dental prophylaxis** — depends on **host risk factors**

## 3 Presentation

**History** — bacteraemia · multiple surgeries · skin breach (IVDU, colonoscopy, dental work, ulcer, wound problem)

**Persistent pain + stiffness** — infection in **>90%** of such patients

**Acute** — swelling, tenderness, **drainage** · **chronic** — subtle, deteriorating function

**Sinus tract to joint** — **definite infection**

**Surgical site infection (with implant)** — within **1 year** of index surgery · 2 yr post-TKA sinus with pus → ✓ **"SSI" least accurate** term (✓ PJI, sinus, purulence, chronic)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Classification by timing

| System | Categories |
|---|---|
| **Orthobullets** | **Acute <3–6 wk** (CDC **<90 days**) — joint space only, no biofilm · **Chronic >3–6 wk** (CDC >90 days) — biofilm, bone–implant interface |
| **OB (DAIR)** | **Early postop ≤30 days** · **acute haematogenous** >30 days with symptoms **<3–4 wk** · **chronic** >30 days with symptoms **>1 month** |
| **Fitzgerald (OB)** | **I** acute postop, radiographically silent · **II** 6–24 mo, indolent, new bone formation · **III** >2 yr, **haematogenous** |
| **TO (P. acnes)** | Early <3 mo · delayed 3–24 mo · late >24 mo |
| **Source** | **Direct** (sinus, dehiscence) · **haematogenous** (dental, gallbladder) |

### Tsukayama (modified Coventry, Segawa) – TO

| Type | Definition | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Positive intraoperative cultures** (≥2) | **IV antibiotics (6 wk)** |
| **2** | **Early postop** (<4 wk) | **Debridement, prosthesis salvage** |
| **3** | **Acute haematogenous** | **Debridement, prosthesis salvage** |
| **4** | **Late chronic** (>4 wk) | **Prosthesis removal** |

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — **periosteal reaction** (lacey new bone, scalloping) · **patchy osteolysis** · **generalized resorption without wear** · transcortical sinus · loosening · TO: lucency **>2 mm** at bone–cement interface, component shift, cement fracture, motion on stress views · progressive lucency in **first year** → infection work-up

**Bone scan (Tc-99m)** — Orthobullets **99% sens, 30–40% spec** · TO **93% sens, 65% spec** (PPV 63%, NPV 94%) · hot for **years** after THA / at 6–7 months after TKA → not useful early · triple phase shows loosening, not sepsis

**Labelled WBC (In-111 / Tc-WBC)** — better to rule out · TO 2023: **Tc-99m WBC + bone scan = gold standard** for septic vs aseptic loosening (spec **86%**)

**FDG-PET** — **98% sens, 98% spec** (Orthobullets) · OB: no more accurate than ESR + CRP, no organism · normal scan excludes PJI

**MRI** — limited by metal (✓ TO "not sensitive due to metal") · MARS MRI knee **92% sens, 99% spec** · not first-line

**Septic vs aseptic loosening — conflict (TO)** — one TO item: ✓ **hip aspiration** (identifies + guides antibiotics; WBC scan can't definitively separate) · FNB 2023: ✓ **radiolabelled WBC scan** (aspiration sens 67%) — read the options/source

### Genes & lab markers – serum

| Marker | Facts |
|---|---|
| **CRP** | Peaks **2–3 d**, normal by **21 d** · threshold acute (<6 wk) **<100 mg/L**, chronic **<10 mg/L** · TO sens 94%, spec 74% · first-line with ESR |
| **ESR** | Peaks **5–7 d**, normal by **90 d** · chronic **<30 mm/h** · no acute cut-off · TO sens 91%, spec 72% |
| **IL-6** | Normal **<10 pg/mL** (TO <5) · peaks **8–12 h**, normal **48–72 h** · ✓ **highest correlation with PJI** · sens **100%**, spec **95%** (TO 95/87) · ✓ **most specific** (TO, vs PCT, CRP, IL-1) · false + **RA, MS, AIDS, Paget** |
| **Serum WBC** | Useless (sens **~20%**) |
| **D-dimer** | **>860 ng/mL** · outperforms ESR/CRP, good for indolent organisms |
| **Procalcitonin** | Spec **98%**, sens **33%** · good for septicaemia |
| **TNF-α** | High spec, low sens · unstable sample |
| **ICAM-1** | ✓ **Returns quickly to baseline** after clearance (✗ Wrong options: "stays elevated" (TO false statement)) |

**Not a correct constellation (TO)** — ✓ **ESR, CRP, WBC count, IL-1**

### Synovial fluid

| Setting | WBC / PMN cut-off |
|---|---|
| **Knee/hip, chronic** | **>3,000 cells/µL + >80% PMN** (Orthobullets) |
| **Knee, first 6 weeks after TKA** | **>10,000** ✓ (Parvizi 2018; Bedair 2011 used 27,800) |
| **MoM hip** | **>4,350 + >85%** · **manual count** (debris falsely ↑ automated) |
| **Hip antibiotic spacer** | **>1,166 + >64%** |
| TO best thresholds | Knee **1,630 + 60%** · hip **2,582 + 66%** → ✓ **2,500 cells/µL** "signifies infection" · early PJI often **>10,000 + >90%** |
| OB others | Chronic THA **3,966** · knee **>1,100** possible infection · knee PJI **>1,000 + >64%** |
| Native septic joint | **50,000** |

**Gram stain** — **specificity > sensitivity** · high false negatives in chronic PJI → **don't rely on it** (OB)

**Alpha-defensin** — ✓ **intra-articular antimicrobial peptide** (✗ Wrong options: serum · cytokine) · released by ✓ **neutrophils** (✗ Wrong options: macrophages · monocytes) · **100% sens, 98% spec** · ✓ **most sensitive + specific** marker (✗ Wrong options: frozen section · IL-6 · CRP · LE) · **>5.2 mg/L** · **unaffected by antibiotics** · accurate with blood · **false + in metallosis/ALTR** · ✓ **single most accurate predictor of chronic PJI**

**Leukocyte esterase strip** — sens **81%**, spec **97%** · cut-off **++** · blood → centrifuge · separates MoM failure from PJI

**Best combined accuracy (OB)** — ✓ **laboratory-based alpha-defensin + LE strips** · lab-based > lateral-flow alpha-defensin · synovial > serum > tissue tests

**Synovial CRP** — **>6.9 mg/L** · **high NPV, low PPV**\
✗ Wrong options: "high PPV"

### MSIS 2018 (Parvizi) criteria

**Major (either alone = infected)** — ✓ **sinus tract communicating with prosthesis** · **same pathogen from 2 separate cultures**\
✗ Wrong options: purulence alone (poly debris, MoM, gout mimic) · high WBC alone · CRP/ESR

| Minor — preoperative | Points |
|---|---|
| Serum **CRP >10 mg/L** or **D-dimer >860 ng/mL** | **2** |
| Serum **ESR >30 mm/h** | **1** |
| Synovial **WBC >3,000/µL** or **LE ++** | **3** |
| Synovial **alpha-defensin +** | **3** |
| Synovial **PMN >80%** | **2** |
| Synovial **CRP >6.9 mg/L** | **1** |
| **Score** | **≥6 infected · 2–5 inconclusive · 0–1 not infected** |

| Intraoperative (if 2–5 or dry tap) | Points |
|---|---|
| **Histology >5 PMN/hpf in 5 hpf (×400)** | **3** |
| **Purulence** | **3** |
| **Single positive culture** | **2** |
| **Combined pre + intraop** | **≥6 infected · 4–5 inconclusive · ≤3 not infected** |

**Performance** — **98% sens, 99.5% spec** · inconclusive → molecular tests (PCR, NGS)

**MSIS 2011** — major criteria **or 4 of 6 minor** (ESR + CRP, synovial WBC, PMN%, purulence, one culture, histology)

**Worked examples** — CRP 12 + ESR 45 + WBC 3,200 + PMN 78% → **6 points = infected** ✓ · WBC + alpha-defensin → **6** ✓ · WBC + PMN → 5 · D-dimer + synovial CRP → 3 · ESR + synovial CRP → 2

### Diagnostic workup – ESR/CRP then aspiration

```
Painful arthroplasty
├─ ESR + CRP normal → infection unlikely → observe / other causes
├─ ESR or CRP elevated → aspiration (cell count, diff, culture, crystals)
│      └─ On antibiotics / inconclusive → repeat after 2 wk off, or alpha-defensin
└─ Still equivocal → intraop frozen section ± sonication of implant
```

**First step** — ✓ **ESR + CRP** · AAOS: **strong recommendation to aspirate** if ESR and/or CRP abnormal\
✗ Wrong options: bone scan · MRI · CT for rotation · revision · metal ions · IV antibiotics first

**Aspiration before revision** — elevated ESR with borderline CRP → ✓ **aspirate** · instability + ↑ ESR/CRP → ✓ **aspirate first**\
✗ Wrong options: Tc scan · FDG-PET · straight to revision + frozen section

**Normal ESR + CRP** — ✓ **observe** / no aspiration (e.g. eccentric head with normal labs)

**MoM hip with fever after URI** — ✓ **aspirate** · MoM pain with ↑ ESR/CRP + normal ions → ✓ **arthrocentesis** · ions **Co:Cr 1:1 → MoM debris**, **2:1 → trunnionosis**\
✗ Wrong options: MARS MRI · metal ions · empiric antibiotics

**Equivocal aspirate + recent antibiotics** — ✓ **repeat aspiration** (after 1–2 weeks off) · on antibiotics now → ✓ **repeat aspiration for alpha-defensin today**

**Equivocal (CRP 13, ESR 19, WBC 1,000, PMN 90%, α-defensin −, synovial CRP 4)** — ✓ **OR for histology** (frozen section)

**Wait 2 weeks off antibiotics** before repeat aspiration (Orthobullets)

**Intraop frozen section** — **>5 PMN/hpf × 5 hpf** · **sens 85%, spec 90–95%** · equivocal cases

**Intraop cultures** — same organism on repeat aspirations or **≥3 of 5** specimens · **false-positive 8%** · **tissue > swabs**

**Sonication (TO)** — ✓ **low-frequency (40 kHz) ultrasound of explanted implant** dislodges biofilm → ↑ culture sensitivity, even after antibiotics\
✗ Wrong options: high frequency · treatment of overuse injuries

**Hemiarthroplasty, 18 months, rapid acetabular erosion + linear lucency** → ✓ **infection**\
✗ Wrong options: cement technique · oversized head (would extrude)

## 5 Management

```
Confirmed PJI
├─ Acute postop (<3–4 wk) or acute haematogenous (symptoms <3–4 wk) → DAIR + modular exchange
│      (not if loose implant, flap needed, MRSA/fungal/mycobacteria)
├─ Chronic (>4 wk), fit host → two-stage exchange (gold standard)
│      └─ Low-virulence, healthy host, no sinus → one-stage (Europe)
├─ Positive intraop cultures at aseptic revision (≥2/5) → 6 wk IV antibiotics
└─ Unfit / refuses → suppression · salvage: resection, fusion, AKA
```

### Acute infection – DAIR & liner exchange

**Indications** — **acute postop <3–4 wk** · **acute haematogenous** (ideally **<48–72 h** of symptoms; <3 wk) · well-fixed implant

**Technique** — **modular exchange** (PE, femoral head — removes fibrin nidus) · synovectomy · **6–9 L irrigation** · culture-guided IV antibiotics **2–6 weeks** (TO)

**Success** — **50–55%** · implants removed if reinfection

**Improved success** — modular exchange · **within 7 days** of symptoms · **rifampin** for staph · **fluoroquinolone** for gram-negatives (FQ-sensitive E. coli ~**79%**)

**Predict failure** — **MRSA, VRE, Pseudomonas**, S. epidermidis (biofilm), **fungi**, ✓ **mycobacteria** · host: **age >80**, male, COPD, CRF, cirrhosis

**Absolute contraindications** — **loose implants** (stem subsidence) · **poor soft tissue / flap needed** (>85% failure) · compromised cement mantle · chronic PJI (even with 12+ wk antibiotics)

**Best DAIR candidate** — ✓ **TKA 2 yr ago, 2-day haematogenous E. coli** · ✓ **THA 3 wk ago, WBC 10,700 + 91% PMN**\
✗ Wrong options: 8 mo MRSA · loose stem · flap · 6 mo symptoms · 1 yr with 2–3 mo symptoms

**Head + liner exchange appropriate** — ✓ **infection 3 days after systemic infection**\
✗ Wrong options: 4 months · 8 weeks · osteolysis · Vancouver A

**Persistent wound drainage 10–14 days post-op** → ✓ **I&D ± PE exchange** in OR · drainage at 3 wk with benign aspirate → **surgical exploration + possible PE exchange** · drainage after prior HTO incision → **urgent debridement + cultures + labs**\
✗ Wrong options: oral/IV antibiotics alone · ointment · immobilizer · sutures in clinic · VAC · wait for cultures

**Acute haematogenous (48 h, RA, dental work, 55K WBC)** → ✓ **operate now**\
✗ Wrong options: wait for cultures · α-defensin · IV antibiotics only · IR drain

**Possible acute PJI 3 wk post-TKA (fall, diverticulitis, effusion)** → ✓ **aspirate first**\
✗ Wrong options: book DAIR directly · reassure

**Acute but MRSA + BMI 50 + persistent drainage (5 wk)** → ✓ **two-stage** (I&D <50% success; failed I&D worsens later two-stage)

**FNB 2024** — bilateral TKA, infected at 3 wk, low-virulence sensitive organism → options suppression / DAIR / one-stage / two-stage — answer not given by source (DAIR fits acute <3–4 wk)

### Chronic infection – two-stage exchange

**Indication** — ✓ **gold standard for infection >4 weeks** · medically fit · adequate bone stock · preferred for **fungal**

**Stage 1** — explant · debride bone–implant interface + soft tissue · **antibiotic spacer** · **IV antibiotics 4–6 weeks** (TO: 6 wk, bactericidal titre **1:8**) · excise sinus with elliptical incision

**Before stage 2** — benign exam, **normal ESR/CRP/WBC**, ✓ **2-week antibiotic holiday then aspiration** · OB case: 1-month holiday\
✗ Wrong options: aspirate at completion of IV · wait 6 months (spacer breaks) · spacer exchange

**Reimplantation** — tissue for culture + **frozen section** · acute inflammation → **re-debride, new spacer** · antibiotic cement if cementing · **cementless better in hip**

| Reimplantation timing | Success |
|---|---|
| **Early, <2 weeks** | **35%** |
| **Delayed, >6 weeks** | **70–90%** (Orthobullets) · **80%** (OB) |

**Cure rates** — two-stage **90–93%** (OB) · **89–100%** (TO) · one-stage **75–80%** (OB) · **75–100%** (Orthobullets) · **~89%** (TO)

**Prognosis** — ✓ **MRSA worse than MSSA** after two-stage (eradication + function) · **BMI ≥40** ↑ reinfection · bilateral simultaneous resection + reimplant → ✓ **successful at 2 yr, <20% revision**

**Typical answers** — late haematogenous after colonoscopy (6 wk) → ✓ **two-stage** · 5 wk after URI, 15K WBC, GPC → ✓ **resection + antibiotics** · MR-CoNS 8 months → ✓ **remove implants + spacer** · inflamed tissue + frozen section 20 WBC/hpf at re-revision, Gram −ve → ✓ **cultures, remove implants, spacer** · sinus tract → ✓ **labs, aspirate, two-stage** (✗ Wrong options: wait · oral antibiotics · DAIR · one-stage) · culture-negative with ↑ markers → still ✓ **two-stage** · MRSA chronic → ✓ **two-stage** (✗ Wrong options: fusion · suppression · I&D · AKA)

**Failed two-stage (persistent infection)** — ✓ **repeat debridement + spacer** · devitalized patella (lateral release kills **superior lateral genicular**) → sequestrum → consider **patellectomy**\
✗ Wrong options: fusion · AKA · suppression in healthy 62-yr-old · arthroscopic I&D

### One-stage exchange

**Indications** — **Europe**, infected THA · **no sinus**, healthy host + soft tissue, no prolonged antibiotics, no bone graft · **low-virulence, sensitive** organism · antibiotic cement

**Pros/cons** — cheaper, single surgery, early mobility vs **↑ persistent infection** · not fungal (rarely succeeds)

### Antibiotic spacers & cement

| | **Static spacer** | **Articulating spacer** |
|---|---|---|
| Antibiotic dose | **Higher** (not premade) | Lower |
| Use | **Flap coverage, extensive bone loss, ligament deficiency** (gross laxity) | Default |
| Pros | Wound healing (no motion) | ↓ reimplantation exposure time · **joint space + ROM kept** · ↓ **quadriceps shortening** · satisfaction |
| Reinfection + function | **Equivalent** | **Equivalent** ✓ (✗ Wrong options: "↓ infection recurrence") |

**Spacer pros** — ↓ dead space, stability, high local antibiotic · **cons** — allergy, resistant organisms, **heat-stable antibiotics only**

| Cement dose (per 40 g) | Orthobullets / OB | TO |
|---|---|---|
| **Low (prophylaxis)** | **≤2 g** (commercial ALBC; OB: 0.5 g tobra/genta) | **≤2 g (<5%)** keeps strength |
| **Treatment / retained hardware** | — | **2–3.6 g** |
| **Spacer (high)** | **≥3.6 g** · practical **3 g vanc + 3.6–4 g tobra** · OB: **min 3.6 g tobra + 1 g vanc** | **4–8 g** · ✓ **max 8 g** |
| **Max without systemic toxicity** | **12.5 g tobramycin · 10.5 g vancomycin** | — |
| **Strength compromised** | — | ✓ **>3.6–4 g** |

**Elution** — burst first **24 h**, rapid decline, low by **5 wk** · ✓ **↑ porosity → ↑ elution** · **hand mixing** for spacers, **vacuum** for prophylaxis · **combination (vanc + aminoglycoside) ↑ elution of both** · vancomycin added **after 30 s** of mixing → ↑ elution · extra monomer **1.5–2 ampoules** · NPWT: no difference\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ strength · ↑ density

**Cement chemistry (TO)** — **liquid antibiotics contraindicated** → ✓ **interfere with polymerization (soft mass)** · antibiotic voids ↓ ✓ **compressive strength** · extra powder ✓ **↑ dough time** · ✓ **polymyxin B / colistin** trapped (poor elution) · penicillins heat-labile · ✓ **fosfomycin ↑ (not ↓) companion elution**, thermostable, voids · amphotericin weakens cement most

**Local antibiotics** — aminoglycosides (gram-negatives, synergy vs GPC, low systemic toxicity) · **vancomycin** (GPC, excellent elution) · **thermostable** · high-dose aminoglycoside spacer → **AKI** risk

**Empiric IV after cultures** — **1st-generation cephalosporin** · **vancomycin** if true penicillin allergy, MRSA history, or unidentified organism · tailor to sensitivity

### Fungal infection

**Features** — **Candida** (most common) · risk: immunosuppression, DM, CRF, IVDU · **indolent**, no fever, ESR/CRP minimally ↑

**Treatment** — ✓ **open I&D, two-stage exchange, antifungals 6–12 months** · **DAIR very high failure** ✓ · recurrence **20–25%** after two-stage · worse than bacterial\
✗ Wrong options: suppression · DAIR + suppression · resection + antimycobacterials · single-stage

**Fluconazole** — ✓ **oral = IV efficacy** · **heat-stable** (can go in cement) · **hepatotoxic** · **resistance rising** · no renal monitoring needed

### Positive intraoperative cultures at revision (TO)

**3/5 cultures CoNS after aseptic revision, afebrile, dry wound** → ✓ **IV antibiotics 6 weeks** (Tsukayama 1)\
✗ Wrong options: debridement + retention · one-stage · two-stage · observation

### Soft tissue coverage – gastrocnemius flap

**Large anteromedial knee defect with exposed implant** → **two-stage + flap at stage 1** · ✓ **medial gastrocnemius flap — medial sural artery**\
✗ Wrong options: free flap first · lateral gastrocnemius (lateral wounds, CPN risk) · soleus (middle/distal third) · fasciocutaneous (4–10 cm without exposed implant)

### Salvage options

| Option | Indications | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Chronic suppression** | Unfit, refuses, low-virulence organism sensitive to low-toxicity oral drug | **10–25%** eradication · **8–21%** complications |
| **Resection arthroplasty** | Poor bone/soft tissue · MDR recurrent · unfit · failed reimplantations · nonambulatory · **polyarticular RA, limited walking** (TO) | **TKA 50–89%** · **THA 60–100%** · short limb, poor function |
| **Arthrodesis** | Reimplantation not feasible (bone stock) · virulent recurrent · TO: young, high demand, single joint, deficient extensor, poor soft tissue | **71–95%** fusion + eradication |
| **Amputation** | Recalcitrant **TKA** infection, severe pain, soft-tissue/bone/vascular loss | **AKA** |

### Special situations

**Infected pseudotumour (MoM, neutrophils on H&E, ↑ ESR/CRP)** → debride, explant, spacer + ✓ **systemic antibiotics**\
✗ Wrong options: radiation · chemotherapy

**MoM THA with 6,000 WBC + 92% PMN (manual count), 3 months** → ✓ **explant + spacer**\
✗ Wrong options: DAIR · CoP revision (that is for metallosis without PJI) · MRI

**Chronic MRSA enterococcus + global laxity + sinus** → ✓ **two-stage with static spacer**\
✗ Wrong options: dynamic spacer with laxity · flap for small sinus

## X vs Y

| | **Acute / haematogenous PJI** | **Chronic PJI** |
