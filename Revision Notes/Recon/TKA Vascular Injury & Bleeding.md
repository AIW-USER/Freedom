# TKA Vascular Injury & Bleeding

*Recon · 62 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **0.017–0.2%** · rare, devastating · commonest = **direct popliteal artery injury**

**Risk factors** — **revision** · **peripheral vascular disease** (consider TKA without tourniquet)

### Popliteal artery anatomy & retractor injury

**Origin** — continuation of **SFA** at **adductor hiatus** · anchored by adductor magnus insertion

**At the joint** — ✓ **lateral** structure · directly behind **posterior capsule** · posterior to **posterior horn/root of lateral meniscus** · ✓ **posterolateral to PCL tibial insertion** · with popliteal vein + tibial nerve

**Relations** — ✓ **anterior to popliteal vein** · **9 mm** posterior to posterior tibial plateau at **90° flexion**\
✗ Wrong options: 15 mm · posterior/medial/lateral to vein

**Branches** — above joint: **medial + lateral sural**, cutaneous, **middle genicular** · at joint: **medial + lateral geniculars** · exits under **soleus** tendon · divides into **anterior + posterior tibial** at distal popliteus

**Flexion vs extension** — furthest from plateau at **60–90° flexion** · **hyperflexion** displaces it posteriorly ✓ · **hyperextension brings artery closer** + traction → injury during cuts

## 2 Mechanism

**Direct laceration** — sharp dissection in posterior compartment

**Blunt** — **posterior retractor lateral to PCL** or **>1 cm** into posterior soft tissue · hyperextension traction

**Thrombosis**

**Safe practice** — single-prong posterior retractor **medial**, never lateral to PCL, ≤1 cm deep

| Retractor | Structure at risk |
|---|---|
| ✓ **Posterior, lateral to PCL / posterolateral to PCL insertion** | **Popliteal artery** (✗ Wrong options: biceps · lateral meniscus root · semimembranosus · peroneal n.) |
| Posterior, 1 cm, **medial** to PCL | Safe |
| **Lateral, around fibular head 3 cm** | **Common peroneal nerve** (within 3 cm of posterior fibular head) |
| **Medial along tibial plateau 3 cm** | **Saphenous nerve** stretch (2–3 cm posterior to medial tibia) |
| Lamina spreader medial | Not linked |

## 3 Presentation

**Intra-op** — brisk bleeding · loss of pulses

**Post-op** — ischaemia · sensory change · **absent distal pulses** · **skin mottling** · severe pain + global motor loss

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Intra-op** — angiogram via vascular surgery

**Post-op** — **CT angiogram** for ischaemic signs\
✗ Wrong options: MR angiogram (delay)

## 5 Management

```
Suspected popliteal injury
├─ Intra-op bleeding / pulse loss → immediate vascular consult ± angiogram
└─ Post-op absent pulses → back to OR, vascular consult, intra-op arteriogram
       └─ Stent / bypass / endarterectomy ± prophylactic fasciotomy
```

### Management of absent pulses

**Asymmetric absent pulses + poor refill post-op** → ✓ **return to OR + vascular consult + intra-op arteriogram** (localise before exploring; stent possible)\
✗ Wrong options: observe 4 h · compartment pressures · MRA · blind exploration

**Valgus TKA, 5 h, pain, no DF/PF, asymmetric pulses after flexing + loosening dressing** → ✓ **vascular surgery consultation**\
✗ Wrong options: serial exams + EMG · ACB · peroneal exploration (global deficit) · thrombectomy without vascular decision

**Options (vascular)** — observation · **stent** · **bypass** · **endarterectomy** · **prophylactic fasciotomy** (reperfusion compartment syndrome)

**Complications** — compartment syndrome · amputation · mortality · ↑ LOS + cost

### Drain & transfusion

**Closed suction drain in primary TKA** → ✓ **↑ transfusion**\
✗ Wrong options: dehiscence · ↓ infection · ↓ hematoma return · ↓ pain

## X vs Y

| | **THA vascular risk** | **TKA vascular risk** |
|---|---|---|
| Vessel | External iliac (anterosuperior screw), obturator (TAL retractor), femoral (anterior retractor) | **Popliteal artery** (posterolateral to PCL) |
| Protective position | — | **Flexion** |

## How it's asked

**"Associated with closed suction drain?"** → **increased transfusion**

**"Popliteal artery anatomy?"** → **anterior to popliteal vein, 9 mm behind posterior tibial plateau at 90°**

**"Sharp Hohmann posterolateral to PCL — injury?"** → **popliteal artery**

**"Absent pulses in PACU, blunt popliteal injury — retractor?"** → **posterior retractor 1 cm deep, lateral to PCL**

**"Asymmetric absent pulses after TKA — next?"** → **OR + vascular consult + intra-op arteriogram**

**"Valgus TKA, global motor loss, asymmetric pulses after flexing knee?"** → **vascular surgery consultation**
