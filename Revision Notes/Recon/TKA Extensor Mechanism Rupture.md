# TKA Extensor Mechanism Rupture

*Recon · 42 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — periprosthetic **quadriceps tendon** rupture, **patellar fracture** or **patellar tendon** rupture · arguably the most devastating TKA complication

### Epidemiology

| Injury | Incidence |
|---|---|
| **Overall EM failure** | **0.1–2.5%** (↑ in revision) |
| **Patellar tendon rupture** | **0.17%** · ✓ **most common type** · ↑ **females** |
| **Quadriceps rupture** | **0.1%** (OB: ~1%) |
| **Patellar fracture** | **0.05%** unresurfaced · **0.2–21%** resurfaced · ↑ **men** |

**Age** — mean 65–75

### Risk factors

| Category | Factors |
|---|---|
| **Multiply operated knee** | **3–4 prior surgeries** (TKA, revision, realignment, HTO) |
| **Systemic** | **Renal disease**, **DM**, **RA**, PVD, obesity, chronic steroids |
| **Drugs** | **Testosterone replacement** · **fluoroquinolones** |
| **Technical** | Stiff knee + forced **patellar eversion** · **residual patella <12 mm** · **lateral release** (superior lateral geniculate a.) · **V-Y turndown** · **component malrotation** (IR femur/tibia), **lateralised button** · **joint-line elevation** (max acceptable **4 mm**) |
| **Implant** | **Single-peg** > tri-peg patella fracture risk · **thick anterior femoral flange** |

### Anatomy & blood supply

**Quadriceps tendon** — rectus femoris superficial (continues over patella), VM + VL deep, VI deepest · **watershed 1–2 cm proximal to superior pole** = commonest rupture site

**Patellar tendon** — inferior pole → tubercle · **4–6 mm thick, 5 cm long**

**Supply** — **geniculate anastomotic ring** (SM, SL, IM, IL) · **inferior geniculates + recurrent anterior tibial a.** → patellar tendon · ring disruption → **patellar AVN → stress fracture**

**PF forces** — walking **0.5× BW** · stairs **3.1× BW** · squatting **7× BW** · patella ↑ quad mechanical advantage **30–50%**

## 2 Mechanism

**Iatrogenic** — **tendon avulsion** from tubercle in forced flexion of stiff knee · excess lateral release · V-Y turndown · **MUA**

**Traumatic** — direct blow (patella fracture) · eccentric load on weak EM · fall · hyperflexion

## 3 Presentation

**Key sign** — ✓ **inability to straight-leg raise / active extension** · passive ROM preserved · extensor lag · palpable defect · effusion · pop

**Patellar height** — **patella alta** = **patellar tendon** rupture · **patella baja** = **quadriceps** rupture

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Use |
|---|---|
| **X-ray (AP, lateral, Merchant)** | **Insall–Salvati >1.2 → alta (PT rupture)** · **<0.8 → baja (QT rupture)** · normal 0.8–1.2 (lateral, 30° flexion) · posterior tibial subluxation · avulsions · compare old films for loosening, joint line |
| **CT** | **Component rotation** · patellar fragmentation + bone stock · loosening |
| **MRI (MARS)** | ✓ **Modality of choice** for extent of tendon tear (partial vs complete) · **early patellar AVN** |
| **US** | Bedside continuity, dynamic gap · no metal artefact, cheap |

### Genes & lab markers

**Always exclude PJI before reconstruction** — ESR, CRP, aspiration (cell count, Gram, culture) · alpha-defensin / leukocyte esterase · BMP, CBC, **HbA1c** for optimisation

### Ortiguera & Berry (periprosthetic patellar fracture)

| Type | EM | Component | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Intact** | **Well fixed** | **Non-op** (good–excellent) |
| **II** | **Disrupted** | Any | **ORIF** (adequate bone) ± EM reconstruction |
| **IIIa** | Intact | **Loose**, bone **≥10 mm** | ORIF + **revise patellar component** |
| **IIIb** | Intact | **Loose**, bone **<10 mm** | **Patellectomy** or component removal (patelloplasty) |

**Criteria** — EM integrity · patellar component fixation · bone stock

### Differential

| Condition | Distinguisher |
|---|---|
| **PJI** | ↑ ESR/CRP, positive aspirate |
| **Component loosening** | Pain, **no extensor lag** |
| **Patellar clunk** | Painful clunk **30–60°** flexion → extension · no lag · arthroscopic debridement |
| **Maltracking** | Tilt/subluxation on Merchant · **J-sign** · extension intact |
| **Arthrofibrosis** | ↓ passive **and** active ROM, no lag |

## 5 Management

```
EM disruption after TKA
├─ Partial, active extension kept / lag <20° undisplaced → immobilizer 6 wk
├─ Acute (<2 wk) quad tendon, intact patella → primary repair (± augment)
├─ Complete / chronic (>6 wk) / patellar tendon → reconstruction
│      (fresh-frozen allograft or synthetic mesh, tensioned in full extension)
└─ Multiple failed reconstructions ± infection → knee arthrodesis
```

### Nonoperative management indications

**Immobilizer 6 wk** — **partial rupture with active extension** · **type I** patellar fracture · non-displaced fracture with intact EM · **lag <20°** · active PJI / unfit · low-demand elderly

**Technique** — extension splint/cylinder cast · **drop-lock brace** if no intention of healing (only for those **unfit for reconstruction**) · WBAT in brace · X-ray every 4–6 wk · ROM after 6–8 wk · ~**69%** of patellar fractures managed conservatively

**Partial quadriceps rupture, weak active extension** → immobilizer 4–6 wk → ✓ **minor lag with acceptable flexion**\
✗ Wrong options: ↓ flexion · re-rupture · 20° lag · early failure

**Appropriate non-op** → ✓ **chronic displaced patella nonunion with only 5° lag**\
✗ Wrong options: acute PT rupture + dehiscence (I&D, poly exchange, repair) · QT rupture with 45° lag · PT rupture 30° lag (reconstruct) · partial QT + coronal instability (address instability)

### Primary repair

**Indication** — acute **<2 wk**, intact patella + component, good tissue · **quad tendon repair <2 wk may equal allograft**

**Avoid** — **chronic >6 wk** · poor bone stock · PJI · **patellar tendon** (largely abandoned)

| Repair | Complication rate |
|---|---|
| **Patellar tendon** | **63%** |
| **Quadriceps tendon** | **25%** |

**Technique** — #5 non-absorbable, anchors/tunnels · **allograft augmentation** advised · knee in **full extension** · cast 6–8 wk

**Why fails** — approach + dissection destroy **inferior genicular** pedicles to the tendon

### Reconstruction – allograft vs synthetic mesh

**Standard of care** for tendinous disruption — ✓ **reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: primary repair (high re-rupture/lag)

| Option | Key points |
|---|---|
| **Achilles allograft (calcaneal block)** | PT rupture with intact patella mobile within **3–4 cm** of joint line · chronic retracted QT · block in tibial trough |
| **Whole EM allograft** | **Deficient/absent patella**, failed prior reconstruction · needs **5 cm** tibial bone + **5 cm** quad tendon · allograft patella **not resurfaced** |
| **Synthetic (Marlex polypropylene, LARS)** | PT insufficiency with intact patella/component/QT · **immunocompromised** · tunnelled lateral to host tendon, distal end in tibial trough with screw/cement, covered by host flaps |
| **Medial gastrocnemius flap** | EM rupture + **poor anterior soft-tissue cover** · ↑ vascularity |
| **Semitendinosus autograft** | Option |

**Allograft rules** — ✓ **fresh-frozen** (✗ Wrong options: freeze-dried (↑ failure)) · ✓ **maximal tension in full extension** (✗ Wrong options: minimal tension → lag)

| Mesh vs allograft | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | ✓ **Mesh lower** |
| **Failure / revision / infection / function** | ✓ **Equivalent** (OB219493, OB217844 — 10–20° lag either) |
| **Extensor lag + walking aid** | **Mesh smaller lag + fewer walking aids** (OB219933) · equivalent (other OB) — read the options |
| **Disease transmission / availability** | Mesh: none / readily available |
| **Wound complications** | Not shown lower with mesh |

![Mesh reconstruction of the extensor mechanism](../../MCQ/attachments/q220113-a-extensor-mesh-recon.jpg)

**Chronic PT rupture, 60° lag, patella alta** → ✓ **bone-tendon allograft reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: hamstring-augmented repair · therapy · arthrodesis · orthotics

**Chronic QT rupture 4 mo, active patient** → ✓ **EM reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: repair via tunnels/anchors · hinged revision (fails without EM)

**QT rupture (baja), 30° lag, infection excluded** → ✓ **mesh reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: drop-lock + PT · lower joint line (for true baja) · Krackow repair · spacer

### Component malrotation & revision

**60° lag, failed PT repair, femur IR 9°, tibia IR 12°** → ✓ **revise femoral + tibial components + EM allograft tensioned tightly in full extension** · CT for rotation pre-op\
✗ Wrong options: PT · brace · poly exchange + hamstring · poly exchange + allograft

### Postoperative protocol

**After mesh/allograft reconstruction** — ✓ **long-leg cast in full extension 6–12 wk**, then gradual ROM in hinged brace · Orthobullets: cylinder cast **6–8 wk**\
✗ Wrong options: splint in 15° flexion · brace 2–4 wk · 4 months (stiffness) · immobilizer 4 wk

### ORIF of patella

**Indication** — type II with adequate bone · type IIIa (revise component) · screws or tension band · complications: AVN, **nonunion (high)**, wire migration to popliteal fossa, infection

### Knee arthrodesis salvage

**Indication** — ✓ **recurrent EM failure after multiple reconstructions + recurrent infection** · active PJI precluding reimplantation · massive bone loss (or AKA)

**Technique** — **long IM nail** preferred · ex-fix if infected/bone loss · position **0–15° flexion, 5–7° valgus**, neutral rotation

**Not arthrodesis** — failed DAIR → **2-stage** · loose distal femoral periprosthetic fracture in elderly → **DFR** · aseptic loosening + metaphyseal loss → **cones/sleeves** · flexion instability → **revision with posterior femoral augments**

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Rate / note |
|---|---|
| **Extensor lag >30°** | **Most common**, **45%** |
| **Re-rupture** | **25%** · ↑ repair alone, ↑ freeze-dried |
| **Infection** | **23%** after reconstruction → 2-stage |
| **Stiffness** | ROM **75–90°** after 6–8 wk immobilisation |
| **Instability / walking aid** | Common |
| **Patellar AVN** | Lateral release, V-Y |

**Prognosis** — functional success **50–68%** at 3–5 yr · **54%** 5-yr implant survival · good: acute <2 wk, fresh-frozen graft, intact component, good bone, no infection

## X vs Y

| | **Patellar tendon rupture** | **Quadriceps tendon rupture** |
|---|---|---|
| Incidence | **Commonest** (0.17%), ♀ | 0.1% |
| Patellar height | **Alta** (I-S >1.2) | **Baja** (I-S <0.8) |
| Primary repair | **63%** complications — abandoned | 25% · OK if <2 wk |
| Reconstruction | Achilles / whole EM / mesh | Achilles / whole EM / mesh |

## How it's asked

**"Pop at PT 3 mo, 60° lag, alta — treatment?"** → **bone-tendon allograft reconstruction**

**"Chronic QT rupture after fall, active male — best long-term?"** → **extensor mechanism reconstruction**

**"Mesh vs allograft — what is decreased?"** → **cost**

**"PT rupture after hyperflexion — true about treatment?"** → **synthetic mesh equivalent to allograft**

**"Baja, 30° lag, negative aspirate — treatment?"** → **tendon reconstruction with interwoven mesh**

**"Allograft vs synthetic — true?"** → **failure rates equivalent**

**"Mesh vs allograft, 40° lag with alta — true?"** → **mesh has lower cost**

**"Polypropylene graft — true?"** → **less cost than allograft**

**"Post-op protocol after mesh reconstruction?"** → **long-leg cast in full extension 6–12 wk, then gradual ROM**

**"Partial QT tear, immobilizer 6 wk — outcome?"** → **minor extension lag with acceptable flexion**

**"Which scenario suits non-op?"** → **chronic displaced patella nonunion with 5° lag**

**"60° lag, failed PT repair, IR components?"** → **revise both components + EM allograft tensioned in full extension**

**"Knee arthrodesis optimal in?"** → **recurrent EM disruption with recurrent PJI**
