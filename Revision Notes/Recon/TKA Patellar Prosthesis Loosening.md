# TKA Patellar Prosthesis Loosening

*Recon · 50 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **0.6–5%** (OB: 0.6–4.8%) · ↑ with **metal-backed** patellae

**Mechanism** — **maltracking** or **PF overstuffing** → high shear (↑ PFJRF at high flexion) · **subclinical infection**

**Risk factors** — **metal backing** · obesity · **lateral release** · **joint-line elevation** · **flexion >100°** · poor bone stock · **asymmetric resection** · **small pegs** (inadequate fixation) · **secondary osteonecrosis** + osteolysis

### Patellar blood supply & AVN

**Supply** — **anastomotic ring** · ✓ **medial-sided vessels contribute more** than lateral

| Step | Vascularity disrupted |
|---|---|
| **Medial arthrotomy** | **0–35%** |
| **Fat pad excision** | **0–16%** (anterior tibial recurrent a. + inferior ring, not medial/lateral vessels) |
| **Lateral release** | **53–75%** (superior + inferior lateral geniculates) |
| ✓ **Medial arthrotomy + lateral release** | **50–100%** (OB option) · **up to 75%** (Orthobullets/OB text) |
| **Lateral meniscectomy** | Does not disrupt ring |

**Patellar ON incidence** — up to **33% at 1 yr**, **10% at 10 yr** · direct link unproven

**Protect** — leave **≥15 mm medial cuff** · lateral release **distal to superolateral border**, **proximal to lateral joint line**, **≥15 mm lateral** to patella · care converting **lateral UKA → TKA** (prior lateral arthrotomy)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — persistent knee pain · swelling · pain with flexion · **no extensor lag** (vs EM rupture) · may see **subcutaneous component** through capsular defect

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| View | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Lateral** | **Loss of PF radiolucent space** — patellar bone touching femoral component · dissociated component |
| **Tangential** | Dissociated component superficial to patella · **thinning of polyethylene** |

**Aspirate 700 WBC, 55% PMN, no lag, component migrated subcutaneously** → ✓ **implant loosening**\
✗ Wrong options: infection · EM rupture (no lag) · fracture (would give haemarthrosis) · overstuffing (cause not diagnosis)

## 5 Management

**Revise patellar component** · if revision fails → **resect component** (patelloplasty)

## How it's asked

**"10 yr TKA, swelling, no lag, low WBC, component migrated — diagnosis?"** → **implant (patellar component) loosening**

**"Patellar blood supply — accurate statement?"** → **medial arthrotomy + lateral release can disrupt 50–100%**

**"Which side contributes more to patellar supply?"** → **medial**
