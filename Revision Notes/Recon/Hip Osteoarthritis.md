# Hip Osteoarthritis

*Recon · 9 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence (symptomatic)** — **hip 88 / 100,000 / yr** · knee 240 / 100,000 / yr

**Modifiable risks** — **articular trauma** · muscle weakness · heavy physical work · high-impact sport

**Non-modifiable** — **female** > male · ↑ age · genetics · **DDH, SCFE, Perthes**

### Cartilage pathophysiology

| Feature | **Osteoarthritis** | **Ageing** |
|---|---|---|
| **Water content** | **↑** (matrix disruption) ✓ | ↓ |
| Proteoglycan | **↓** quantity | ↓ content + size |
| Collagen | Disorganized · **↓ in severe OA** · ↓ cross-linking | **Normal** quantity · ↑ stiffness, ↑ cross-linking |
| **Chondrocyte activity** | **↑** (proliferation) | Unchanged |
| **Subchondral bone** | **↑ thickness** | Unchanged |
| Chondroitin : GAG | — | ↓ |

**Moderate OA cartilage** → ✓ **↑ water**\
✗ Wrong options: ↓ subchondral thickness · ↓ chondrocyte activity · ↑ proteoglycan · ↑ collagen

**Synovium** — early mild inflammation → middle **hypervascular** → late **thick + vascular**

**Subchondral bone** — remodelling → lytic lesions with **sclerotic edges** (unlike RA cysts) → **cysts** late

![OA vs ageing cartilage](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob5005-osteoarthritis-vs-aging.jpg)

### Genes & lab markers

**MMPs (degrade matrix)** — **stromelysin**, plasmin, **aggrecanase-1 (ADAMTS-4)** · zinc co-factor · stromelysin secretion **induced by PDGF**, **inhibited by TGF-β**

**TIMPs** — inhibit MMPs · **MMP–TIMP imbalance** in OA

**Cytokines (from synoviocytes, ↑ MMPs)** — **IL-1** (catabolic), IL-6, **TNF-α**

**Cartilage degeneration implicated** — ✓ **stromelysin**\
✗ Wrong options: TGF-β (anabolic) · aggrecan (compressive proteoglycan) · osteoprotegerin (RANKL decoy) · IGF-1 (anabolic)

**Inheritance** — **non-Mendelian** · candidate genes: **vitamin D receptor**, **oestrogen receptor 1**, IL-1, IL-4, matrilin-3, BMP-2, BMP-5

**Histology** — **loss of superficial chondrocytes** · **tidemark replication + breakdown** · fissuring · eburnation

### Rapidly progressive OA (RPOA)

**Causes** — **steroid injections** (high dose **>80 mg**, multiple) · cytokine/immune · autoimmune · **subchondral insufficiency fracture**

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — function-limiting · **groin** commonest · referred to lateral hip, **anterior knee**, thigh, buttock, low back · night/rest pain · stiffness · mechanical symptoms

**Exam** — gait, LLD, scars · **flexion contracture >5°** · **flexion <90–100°** · **↓ internal rotation** · **SLR negative**

**Intra-articular signs (OB)** — **Stinchfield** (pain with resisted hip flexion) · **C-sign** (groin cupping)

### Hip pain referred to knee

**Concomitant hip pathology** — **2–29%** of knee-pain patients

**Suspect hip when** — disability **disproportionate** to knee findings · fails cortisone/PT · **no improvement after knee surgery** · Trendelenburg gait

**Next step** — ✓ **hip radiographs** (✗ Wrong options: ESR/CRP first · more PT · revision TKA · pain specialist) · benign knee exam + pain on hip motion → assess for ✓ **hip OA** (✗ Wrong options: meniscus · occult fracture · septic knee · LLD)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **standing AP pelvis** + **AP and lateral hip** · optional **false-profile** (dysplasia)

**OA signs** — joint-space narrowing · osteophytes · subchondral sclerosis · subchondral cysts

**Pelvic obliquity** — spinal deformity → LLD issues

**Acetabular retroversion (crossover)** — makes cup positioning harder

### Tönnis classification

| Grade | Findings |
|---|---|
| **0** | Normal |
| **1** | Sclerosis of head + acetabulum · slight JSN · slight lipping |
| **2** | **Small cysts** · **moderate JSN** · moderate loss of head sphericity |
| **3** | **Large cysts** · obliterated / severe JSN · severe head deformity vs AVN |

![Tönnis grade 2](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob5005-tonnis-3.jpg)

### Protrusio acetabuli (TO)

**Definition** — ✓ **femoral head migrates past ilioischial (Köhler) line** · **CE angle of Wiberg >40°**\
✗ Wrong options: iliopectineal line · CEA >25° · ↓ CEA

**Köhler line** — most lateral pelvic brim → most lateral obturator foramen

| Unilateral causes | Bilateral causes |
|---|---|
| OA, septic + **TB** arthritis, AVN, **central fracture-dislocation**, fibrous dysplasia, **THA** (migrating prosthesis) | **RA**, **osteomalacia**, **ankylosing spondylitis**, **Paget**, renal osteodystrophy, rickets, osteoporosis, **Marfan** |

**Secondary causes (TO notes)** — RA, Paget, post-traumatic, **old hemiarthroplasty (Austin Moore / Thompson)**

**Otto pelvis (arthrokatadysis)** — **primary** protrusio · **bilateral** · **younger women** · early pain + stiffness · ✓ **not caused by osteomalacia** (that is secondary)

## 5 Management

```
Symptomatic hip OA
├─ First line → NSAIDs + weight loss/exercise (BMI >25) + contralateral cane
├─ Short-term relief → IA corticosteroid (also diagnostic)
├─ Young, dysplasia, concentric, mild–moderate OA → PAO ± femoral osteotomy
├─ Tönnis 2–3 → no arthroscopy (high conversion)
└─ End-stage, >50 yr → THA (≥3 months after ipsilateral injection)
```

### Nonoperative management

| Recommendation | Modality |
|---|---|
| **First line** | **NSAIDs** · **weight loss (BMI >25), activity modification, exercise/PT** · walking stick in **contralateral** hand |
| **Strongly supported (2013 AAOS CPG)** | **IA corticosteroid** short-term (therapeutic + diagnostic) · weight loss · low-impact exercise · NSAIDs |
| Controversial | Acupuncture · viscosupplement · glucosamine/chondroitin |
| **Against** | **Opioids incl. tramadol** · **IA hyaluronic acid** (strong against, 2017 CPG) · needle/arthroscopic lavage |

**Steroid mechanism** — ✓ **binds nuclear receptors → interrupts inflammatory cascade via mRNA**\
✗ Wrong options: hypothalamic antipyretic (acetaminophen) · cannabinoid receptor · synovial viscosity (HA) · Na⁺ channel block (lidocaine)

**OB note** — 2019 OB lists **tramadol** among supported options; Orthobullets lists tramadol as **recommended against** — read the stem

### Contraindication to joint preservation (Tönnis 2)

**Arthroscopy** — controversial · degenerative labral tears with little radiographic OA · ✓ **Tönnis ≥2 → high conversion to THA** · also **age >50**, obesity

**Not contraindications** — prior steroid injections · age >45 · large cam / 2B subspine · acetabular dysplasia (OB) · degenerative labral tear · alpha angle 52° · male sex · ligamentum teres tear

**PAO ± femoral osteotomy** — **symptomatic dysplasia**, adolescent/young adult, **concentric** hip, mild–moderate OA · mixed results, may delay THA

**Femoral head resection** — pathological lesions · painful subluxation

**Resurfacing** — **young (<50), active, male** · or severe proximal femoral deformity · ↓ use (MoM concerns)

### THA indications

**Indication** — end-stage/severe symptomatic OA · **preferred >50 yr** + advanced change · most predictable pain relief + function

**Typical stem** — 67-yr-old golfer, end-stage OA, failed NSAIDs/PT → ✓ **cementless THA**\
✗ Wrong options: steroid (transient + delays surgery 3 months) · HA · resurfacing · cemented hemi (acetabular disease)

**Hip–spine syndrome** — 1 week relief after IA injection + Stinchfield + C-sign → ✓ **recommend THA**\
✗ Wrong options: repeat injection · PT · lumbar fusion · osteochondroplasty (no cam/pincer)

**Injection timing** — wait **≥3 months** after ipsilateral IA injection (↑ PJI)

**Contraindications (TO)** — **absolute**: active infection (hip or elsewhere), unstable medical illness · **relative**: **morbid obesity**, **severe dementia**, tobacco, severe osteoporosis, untreated skin disease (psoriasis), **abductor insufficiency**

**Not a contraindication** — ✓ **asymptomatic bacteriuria** (TO) · HbA1c unreliable for predicting infection · Charnley: THA possible with chronic low-grade infection of **opposite** hip

### Combined hip & knee deformity – sequence (TO)

**Flexion deformity of hip + knee** — ✓ **hip first, then knee**\
✗ Wrong options: knee first · simultaneous · knee then hip 3 weeks later

**Reasons** — knee FFD ↓ after hip FFD corrected · knee easier with mobile hip · referred hip pain resolves · knee aligned to **new hip centre** · faster rehab

### Protrusio acetabuli – reaming (TO)

**Precaution** — ✓ **peripheral reaming only**\
✗ Wrong options: one size larger midway · one size smaller throughout · horizontal reaming

**Technique** — ream **periphery first** with larger reamers (1–2 sizes below final cup), **preserve subchondral bone** · medial floor: curette geodes, drill sclerotic bone with K-wires · **morselized graft** impacted on floor (none at rim) · cup **1–2 mm larger** than last reamer · ≥**50% host-bone contact**

**THA strategy (TO)** — difficult dislocation (head incarcerated) → remove posterior wall osteophytes · **double femoral neck osteotomy** (divergent cuts) to extract head · restore centre with **Köhler + Shenton** lines · rebuild medial wall: **morselized head autograft ± wire mesh / tantalum chips** · **antiprotrusio cage** for large defects · **reverse reaming** over graft · **err toward lateralization** (keep horizontal offset, avoid over-medialization)

## How it's asked

**"Characteristic cartilage change in moderate OA?"** → **↑ water content**

**"Implicated in cartilage degeneration?"** → **stromelysin**\
✗ Wrong options: TGF-β · IGF-1 · aggrecan · OPG

**"Mechanism of drug strongly recommended for short-term hip OA pain (2013 CPG)?"** → **corticosteroid — nuclear receptor, mRNA change**

**"Contraindication for THR — all except?"** → **asymptomatic bacteriuria** (TO)

**"67-yr-old golfer, end-stage OA, tournament in 5 months?"** → **cementless THA**\
✗ Wrong options: steroid injection (delays surgery 3 months)

**"Brief relief from hip injection, Stinchfield + C-sign — next?"** → **recommend THA**

**"What precludes arthroscopy for joint preservation?"** → **Tönnis grade 2**

**"Persistent knee pain after TKA, Trendelenburg gait — next?"** → **hip X-rays**

**"Benign knee exam, pain with hip motion — imaging looks for?"** → **hip OA**

**"Flexion deformity of hip and knee — sequence?"** → **hip first, then knee** (TO)

**"Protrusio acetabuli defined by?"** → **femoral head past ilioischial line** (TO)

**"Reaming precaution in protrusio?"** → **peripheral reaming only** (TO)

**"Not true about Otto pelvis?"** → **osteomalacia** (it is bilateral, primary, young women) (TO)

**"Steroid dose linked to RPOA?"** → **>80 mg**, multiple injections

**"THA in protrusio — cup position?"** → **err toward lateralization** (TO)
