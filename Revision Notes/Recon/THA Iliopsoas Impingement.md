# THA Iliopsoas Impingement

*Recon · 23 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Etiology

**Definition** — under-recognized cause of **recurrent groin pain** after THA (up to **4.3%** of painful THAs)

**Causes** — ✓ **malpositioned acetabular component with anterior overhang** (most common) · cup **too large**, **retroverted** or **lateral** · **excessively long screws** penetrating ilium · **retained cement** · LLD (Orthobullets lists; OB: not significant)

**Version trap** — anterior overhang from **retroversion** · (✗ Wrong options: excessive offset → **trochanteric bursitis**)\
✗ Wrong options: excessive anteversion

**Anatomy** — iliopsoas from lumbar transverse processes, passes **anterior to hip** → **lesser trochanter**

## 3 Presentation

**Onset** — average **20 months** after index THA\
✗ Wrong options: 2 weeks

**Pain** — **groin**, with **active hip flexion**: **ascending stairs** (not descending), getting into/out of car, rising from chair · **pain with passive extension**

**Exam** — subtle: slight limp, groin tenderness, rare palpable snap · ✓ **pain with resisted seated hip flexion or active straight-leg raise** · passive ROM/SLR painless

**Not iliopsoas** — **start-up groin pain** → cup loosening · start-up **thigh** pain → stem loosening · constant night pain + fevers → PJI

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — AP pelvis + AP/lateral hip · ✓ **cross-table lateral** shows **anterior cup prominence** (TO next step; ✗ Wrong options: MRI · PET · bursa aspiration)

**CT** — cup position, **anterior overhang**, excludes other causes · **MRI** — limited by artefact (shows tendon inflammation)

**Rule out** — infection (ESR, CRP, aspiration) + loosening first

### Diagnostic injection

**Image-guided local anaesthetic ± steroid into iliopsoas sheath** — ✓ **most reliable confirmation** · diagnostic **and** therapeutic\
✗ Wrong options: GT bursa injection · repeat aspiration · Ober test · knee films · bone scan · revision before diagnosis

## 5 Management

```
Groin pain with resisted hip flexion after THA
├─ Exclude infection + loosening → diagnostic iliopsoas sheath injection
├─ Cup well positioned (normal X-ray/CT) → arthroscopic iliopsoas tenotomy
└─ Excessive anterior overhang (>8 mm) → acetabular component revision
```

### Treatment – tendon release vs cup revision

**Nonoperative** — rest, NSAIDs, stretching, CT-guided steroid injection · **rarely sufficient** (relief in only **~50%**)

**Normal radiographs / well-placed cup** → ✓ **iliopsoas tenotomy** (arthroscopic)\
✗ Wrong options: indefinite activity modification · femoral/acetabular revision

**Anterior overhang** → ✓ **acetabular component revision** for permanent relief · **>8 mm prominence** → revision far better than tenotomy (Chalmers: persistent pain 50% nonop vs 24% operative)\
✗ Wrong options: tenotomy alone · lipped liner (worsens) · repeat injection · both components

**Arthroscopic tenotomy technique** — **extra-capsular** (at lesser trochanter, common after THA) or **trans-capsular** (between anterior labrum and zona orbicularis) · hip in slight flexion + ✓ **external rotation** → brings tendon toward surgeon, away from **femoral neurovascular bundle** · anterior portal ~**15 mm** from LFCN branches → shift portal lateral\
✗ Wrong options: IR (for deep gluteal space/sciatic) · MFCA (posterior)

## X vs Y

| | **Iliopsoas impingement** | **Trochanteric bursitis** | **Cup loosening** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain | Groin, **active flexion / stairs up** | **Lateral**, side lying | Groin **start-up** |
| Cause | **Anterior cup overhang**, long screws | ↑ offset | Osteolysis, micromotion |
| Test | **Sheath injection** | Bursa injection | Serial X-rays |

## How it's asked

**"Groin pain 3 months post-THA, painful active SLR, normal labs/X-ray — next (TO)?"** → **cross-table lateral X-ray**

**"Most likely to determine cause of pain (stairs, passive extension)?"** → **local anaesthetic injection of iliopsoas sheath**

**"Resisted seated flexion pain, well-placed components — next?"** → **image-guided lidocaine injection into iliopsoas sheath**

**"Structure labelled 1 (groin pain with stairs up)?"** → **iliopsoas tendon**

**"MRI inflamed iliopsoas — cause?"** → **anterior overhang of acetabular component**

**"Stair-climbing groin pain, prominent cup anteriorly?"** → **iliopsoas impingement**

**"Offset ↓ 2 mm, length ↑ 3 mm, prominent cup — likely?"** → **iliopsoas tendonitis**

**"History + exam typical of psoas impingement?"** → **pain with passive extension + resisted flexion, onset ~20 months**

**"Injection relieved pain, normal X-rays — next?"** → **iliopsoas tendon release**

**"Arthroscopic tenotomy — hip position?"** → **external rotation (protect femoral neurovascular bundle)**

**"Recurrent pain with confirmed anterior overhang — permanent fix?"** → **acetabular component revision**
