# THA Aseptic Loosening

*Recon · 21 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Pathophysiology

**Definition** — **macrophage-induced inflammatory** bone loss → implant loosening **without infection**

**Sequence** — **prosthesis micromotion** → **particulate debris** → **macrophage-activated osteolysis** (RANK/RANKL pathway ↑)

**Debris source** — conventional PE (submicron particles) · proximal femoral lysis around cemented stem → mostly **cement debris** (OB)

**Epidemiology** — ✓ **most common cause of late THA revision**

**Early (≤6 wk) cementless stem subsidence** — lack of initial stability → ✓ **high BMI (e.g. 44)** or **undersized stem**\
✗ Wrong options: oversized · Dorr A · tapered stem · modular neck

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — **groin** (cup), **thigh** (stem), knee · **activity related** · **start-up pain** (first steps) = loose femoral stem

**Exam** — minimal pain with ROM · pain with weight bearing · mild discomfort with SLR/rotation

### Genes & lab markers

**ESR + CRP normal** in aseptic loosening → rule out infection first; normal labs + loosening on X-ray → **no aspiration needed**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP + lateral · X-rays **insensitive**, **underestimate** osteolysis

**General** — **lucency >2 mm** at cement–bone or metal–bone interface · **progressive / continuous** radiolucent line · **component motion on stress views** · change in position on serial films · positive bone scan

**Femoral component** — **subsidence >1 cm** · **cement mantle fracture** · **debonding at lateral shoulder (Gruen zone 1)** · **pedestal** at stem tip · circumferential lucency · lateral cortical stress reaction

**Acetabular component** — migration / change in inclination · **PE liner wear → femoral head sits eccentric (superior)** in cup

![Aseptic loosening of THA](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12304-asl1.jpg)

**Resurfacing-specific (Orthobullets)** — change in **peg–neck angle** · ↓ peg tip–lateral cortex distance (also head collapse) · **neck narrowing >10% / after 3 yr** (also impingement)

### Roentgen stereophotogrammetric analysis (RSA)

**Accuracy** — **2°, 0.5 mm**

**Finding** — ✓ **migration >1–2 mm in the first year → ↑ risk of loosening** (varus or retroversion) · both cemented and cementless stems migrate\
✗ Wrong options: cemented do not migrate · any early migration = failure · abandoned

### Cemented stem failure modes (Gruen) – TO

| Mode | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **I — Pistoning** ✓ | Stem subsides within cement (or stem + cement within bone) · **linear lucency around stem and cement** · **commonest** · poor cementing |
| **II — Medial midstem pivot** | Proximal stem medial, distal stem lateral · weak calcar support |
| **III — Calcar pivot (distal toggle)** | Distal tip toggles medial–lateral ("windshield wiper") · poor distal support |
| **IV — Bending cantilever (fatigue)** | Proximal support lost, distal fixed → **stem fracture** |

![Gruen failure modes of cemented stems](../../MCQ/attachments/TO19356229-1.png)

### Cemented stem philosophies (TO)

| | **Force-closed / taper-slip** | **Shape-closed / composite beam** |
|---|---|---|
| Surface | **Polished** (Ra **<1 µm**) | **Rough** (Ra >1–2 µm), flanges, collar |
| Examples | **Exeter**, C-stem | **Charnley** |
| Subsidence | **Intended** (radial compression, no shear) · loosening if progressive **after 2 yr** or **>5 mm** total | **Not intended** · >**0.15 mm** after 2 yr → ↑ revision (**4% per 0.1 mm**) |
| Collar | **None** (would block subsidence) · use centralizer | Allowed (calcar load transfer) |
| Fracture | Slightly **↑ periprosthetic fracture** (hoop stress) | Anatomical stems fewer early fractures |

**Bone quality (TO)** — composite beam: collar, **good in osteoporotic bone** · taper slip: proximal wedge + hoop stress → **avoid in poor bone** (fracture risk)

**Earliest clinical sign (TO)** — **start-up pain** (groin / thigh on rising) · lucency typically appears **5–6 yr** post-op on serial films

**Cement mantle** — **2–4 mm**, continuous · thin → fissures · **>5 mm** → micromotion, creep · **over-broaching** (stem smaller than rasp, ≥2 mm mantle) vs **line-to-line ("French paradox")** thin mantle · **retrograde gun filling** · 4th-generation: centralize stem · **Barrack–Mulroy** grades mantle quality · setting faster with heat, humidity, longer mixing, handling

## 5 Management

```
Painful THA, suspected loosening
├─ ESR/CRP abnormal → aspirate (exclude PJI)
├─ Stable implant, minimal symptoms → observe (frequent X-rays)
└─ Loose / painful osteolysis / thinning cortex → revise loose component(s)
       ├─ Loose stem → cementless (long) stem
       └─ Loose cup, fixed stem → isolated acetabular revision
```

### Revision indications

**Revise** — pain from aseptic loosening · pain + osteolysis · **extensive osteolysis that would compromise future revision** · pathological / impending fracture

**Observe** — stable implant with minimal symptoms · ↑ frequency of radiographs if wear/lucency

**Untreated loose osteolytic stem** — highest risk ✓ **periprosthetic femoral fracture**\
✗ Wrong options: infection · pseudotumour (MoM) · acetabular fracture · dislocation

**Circumferential lucency + cement mantle fracture + thin cortex** → ✓ **revise to long-stem cementless** soon\
✗ Wrong options: observe · cane (use walker) · bisphosphonates (unproven, no effect on a loose stem) · long cemented stem

**Loose femoral stem, normal ESR/CRP, start-up thigh pain** → ✓ **cementless femoral revision**\
✗ Wrong options: aspiration · CT · MRI · cemented revision (high early failure)

**Acetabular osteolysis + loose cup, fixed stem** → ✓ **acetabular component revision**\
✗ Wrong options: head/liner exchange + grafting (loose cup) · 2-stage (not infected) · femoral · both

**Typical diagnoses** — hemiarthroplasty 5 yr with pedestal + large lucency, normal labs → ✓ **aseptic loosening** · cement mantle fracture + lateral shoulder debonding → ✓ **femoral loosening** (✗ Wrong options: Brooker I HO · Parkinsonism) · activity thigh pain + zone 1 debonding → ✓ **loose femoral component** (✗ Wrong options: PE synovitis · psoas · asymptomatic lysis · modulus mismatch)

## X vs Y

| | **Aseptic loosening** | **Septic loosening** |
|---|---|---|
| ESR / CRP | **Normal** | ↑ |
| Radiograph | Lucency, subsidence, pedestal | + periosteal reaction, patchy lysis, early lucency |
| Revision | **One-stage** of loose parts | **Two-stage** |

## How it's asked

**"Hemi 5 yr, thigh/knee pain, pedestal, normal labs?"** → **aseptic loosening**

**"RSA finding?"** → **migration >1–2 mm in first year ↑ loosening risk**

**"Cement debonding + mantle fracture?"** → **femoral loosening**

**"Zone 1 lucency after hybrid THA, activity pain?"** → **loose femoral component**

**"Loose osteolytic stem, refuses surgery — risk?"** → **periprosthetic femoral fracture**

**"Circumferential lucency + mantle fracture at 12 yr?"** → **revision to long-stem cementless**

**"Acetabular osteolysis, loose cup, stable stem?"** → **acetabular component revision**

**"Start-up thigh pain, continuous lucency, normal labs?"** → **femoral revision with cementless stem**

**"5 mm subsidence at 6 weeks — contributing factor?"** → **BMI 44** (undersizing)

**"Linear lucency around cemented stem — failure mode?"** → **pistoning** (TO)

**"Radiographic loosening threshold?"** → **lucency >2 mm**, subsidence >1 cm
