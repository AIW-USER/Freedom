# THA Postoperative Inpatient Management

*Recon · 28 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Team & phases

**Team** — surgeon ± hospitalist · PT · OT · case manager · nursing · patient + family

**Phases** — **pre-op teaching** → **inpatient acute care** → inpatient extended care (rehab/SNF) → outpatient/home

## 5 Management

### Pain management

| Phase | Measures |
|---|---|
| **Pre-op** | **NSAIDs + acetaminophen** · gabapentin/opioids (weaker data) |
| **Intra-op** | **Regional (spinal/epidural) preferred over GA** · **periarticular multimodal injection** |
| **Post-op** | **Multimodal oral therapy = gold standard** |

**Liposomal bupivacaine (FDA 2011)** — slow release, longer action, no systemic toxicity · ✓ **comparable to peripheral nerve blocks for opioid use** · not clinically superior to standard bupivacaine\
✗ Wrong options: same cost (expensive) · earlier ambulation · superior after 48 h (not even at 72 h) · shorter LOS vs fascia iliaca block

### Medical management

| Problem | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Hypertension** | >140/90 · check reversible causes (missed meds, pain, anxiety, constipation) · oral agents preferred |
| **Hypotension** | SBP <90 or drop ≥40 · ✓ **commonest cause: intravascular volume depletion** · hold antihypertensives, IV fluids ± CBC/BMP, ECG, troponin, CXR, CTPA |
| **Hypoxia** | ↓ cardiac output + O₂ uptake · **incentive spirometry for all** · consider ischaemia, COPD, CHF, **PE** · keep SpO₂ >90% · distress → ICU |
| **↓ Urine output** | Hypovolaemia, **urinary retention**, **AKI** · retention → straight cath, ↓ anticholinergics/opioids, **tamsulosin** · AKI → fix cause, euvolaemia, stop nephrotoxins |
| **Altered mental status** | Find cause · **reorient, normalize sleep–wake cycle** |
| **Chest pain** | Troponin + ECG if cardiac · aspirin + cardiology (ischaemia) · NSAIDs (pleuritic/wall) · PPI (dyspepsia) |
| **PONV** | Opioids, anaesthetics · antiemetics, fluids, electrolytes |
| **Ileus** | NG tube may be needed |
| **Fever** | **POD 1–2 cytokine fever** — observe if well · **after POD 3** → infectious (UTI, cellulitis, pneumonia, C. diff) or non-infectious (PE, withdrawal, transfusion/drug reaction) · antibiotics only for an identified source |

### Postoperative ileus & Ogilvie syndrome

**Ogilvie** — **acute colonic pseudo-obstruction** without mechanical cause · **laxatives may worsen** it

**Most severe complication** — ✓ **bowel ischaemia** (→ colectomy/laparotomy)\
✗ Wrong options: SMA syndrome · renal failure · cholangitis · appendicitis

**Treatment** — stop opioids · **NG tube** · IV fluids, electrolytes · **neostigmine** · failure → **colonoscopic decompression**

### Physical & occupational therapy

**Start day of surgery** — ↓ LOS, ↓ pain, ↑ function

**Exercises** — ankle pumps, knee bends, glute sets, quad sets, hip abduction, SLR → edge of bed → chair → walker → gait training

**Stairs** — **"up with the good, down with the bad"** (up: non-operated leg first · down: operated leg first)

**Walker** in immediate post-op period · OT: **raised toilet seat**, shower seat, raised chair

**Progression** — sitting → walker gait, out of bed → transfers, normal gait → independence

### Discharge criteria (home)

**Criteria** — **independent ambulation with aid 50–100 ft** · independent **transfers** · independent **ADLs** · **2 stairs with supervision** · **follows dislocation precautions** · adequate **home help**

### Extended care

**Earlier discharge to rehab** → **improved outcomes**

| | **SNF / subacute rehab** | **Inpatient rehab** |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Fail home criteria, don't qualify for IRF | Can do **3 h therapy/day** + (**>85 yr**, **BMI >50**, or **bilateral TJA**) |
| Cost | More cost-effective | Intensive, rarely used after TJA |

## How it's asked

**"Liposomal bupivacaine periarticular injection — true?"** → **comparable to nerve blocks for opioid consumption**

**"POD 4, distension, no BM 7 days — most severe complication?"** → **bowel ischaemia**

**"Most common cause of post-op hypotension?"** → **volume depletion**

**"Fever POD 1, patient well?"** → **observe (cytokine fever)**

**"Stairs after THA?"** → **up with the good, down with the bad**

**"Inpatient rehab eligibility?"** → **3 h/day therapy + age >85, BMI >50 or bilateral TJA**
