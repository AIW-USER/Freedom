# THA Pseudotumor (Metal on Metal Reactions)

*Recon · 30 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & epidemiology

**Pseudotumour = ALVAL** (aseptic lymphocyte-dominant vasculitis-associated lesion) — **mass-forming tissue reaction** to MoM wear · (semi-)solid or cystic periprosthetic mass **≥2 cm** not due to infection, tumour, bursa or scar (TO)

**Incidence** — **10–15%** of MoM THAs · resurfacing **0–39%** (OB) · ALVAL ~0.3% (TO) · **45–50% of MoM revisions** are for pseudotumour/ALTR · **~40% asymptomatic** · revision for symptomatic pseudotumour only **1.7–5.6%**\
✗ Wrong options: majority

**Seen with** — ✓ **MoM THA / resurfacing** (TO) · also **MoP with head–neck (trunnion) corrosion** → ALTR

### Risk factors

| Risk factors | Not risk factors |
|---|---|
| **↑ Cobalt + chromium** (Co **>5 µg/L** — TO) | ✓ **BMI >40** (TO EXCEPT) |
| **Female** | Metal allergy (TO) |
| **High cup inclination >55°** / ↑ anteversion → **edge loading** | Obesity |
| **Age <40** (TO) | — |
| Taper/trunnion corrosion even with good position | — |
| Large metal heads (some studies), low diametral clearance | — |

### Pathophysiology

**Two mechanisms** — **hypersensitivity to metal ions** · **local high wear debris** · ✓ pseudotumour associated with **↑ linear wear** of both components

**Hypersensitivity** — ✓ **type IV delayed (cell-mediated)** · metal ions form **haptens** with native proteins · ✓ **lymphocyte-mediated** in metal-allergic patients (jewellery reactions) (✗ Wrong options: macrophage-mediated · large/small particle wear) · ALVAL due to ✓ **hypersensitivity to metals** (✗ Wrong options: abrasion of shell · screws · stem corrosion)

**Cells — conflict** — Orthobullets/OB: macrophages + lymphocytes in all, **lymphocytes predominant** · high-wear cases → more macrophages, smaller lymphoid aggregates · TO: metal debris **cytotoxic to macrophages → macrophage depletion** → lymphocyte predominance · OB (DTH): effector = T-cell but **most participating cells are macrophages (lymphocytes ~5%)**\
✗ Wrong options: macrophage predominant

**Histology** — **dense perivascular lymphocytic infiltrate** (hallmark), T **and** B lymphocytes, lymphoid follicles/aggregates, histiocytes, giant cells, necrosis · ✓ dark areas = **T + B lymphocyte infiltrates** (✗ Wrong options: particle-laden macrophages (typical of MoP)) · MoP trunnion ALTR → ✓ **predominance of perivascular lymphocytes** (✗ Wrong options: neutrophils (PJI) · lipid-laden histiocytes/giant cells (PVNS))

**ALVAL true (TO)** — B lymphocytes · macrophage depletion · lymphoid aggregates · ✓ **false: "diagnosis is not made by macroscopic appearance"** (solid mass visible) · definitive diagnosis histological

## 3 Presentation

**History** — gradual or sudden groin pain · may be **asymptomatic** · mass causing discomfort

**Exam** — periprosthetic soft-tissue mass · **Trendelenburg gait** (abductor destruction) · groin pain with **flexion, IR, adduction** or rising from chair · NV usually normal

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — AP pelvis + AP/lateral hip · exclude fracture · **calcar erosion**

**MARS MRI** — ✓ **test that best correlates with prognosis** (TO) (✗ Wrong options: ESR/CRP/WBC · metal ions (don't correlate) · CT (bone loss only)) · ✓ **best test for painful MoM** (✗ Wrong options: CT · bone scan · ion levels alone) · findings: periarticular **fluid collection / solid mass**, synovial/capsular hypertrophy, **tendon avulsion**, bone oedema · **T1 ≈ bladder (transudate)** · **T2 hyperintense** (hypointense foci = necrosis/metal) · MAVRIC, SEMAC newer

**CT** — bony destruction, need for structural graft/augments

### Genes & lab markers

**Metal ions (Co, Cr)** — peak **12–24 months** (run-in) then fall · **>7 ppb (µg/L)** → MARS MRI · ions renally excreted · **activity does not change levels** · Co:Cr ≈ **1:1** = MoM bearing, **high Co:Cr (2:1)** = trunnionosis\
✗ Wrong options: pseudotumour

**Aspiration** — distinguishes **metallosis** from PJI · **"dishwater" fluid** (grey, watery, hazy) · ✓ **manual cell count** (fibrinous debris falsely ↑ automated) · MoM PJI cut-off **>4,350 WBC** · ARMD → **lymphocytes >40%**; PJI → **PMN >70%** (e.g. WBC >3,000 with low PMN, high lymphocytes = ✓ **pseudotumour hypersensitivity**; ✗ Wrong options: bacterial PJI · large-particle PE disease)

**PJI vs ALTR** — ALTR raises ESR, CRP, synovial WBC and gives **false-positive α-defensin** → ✓ **thorough work-up: cultures, manual WBC, %PMN (80% cut-off)** · α-defensin remains valid in RA\
✗ Wrong options: α-defensin alone diagnoses PJI · ESR/CRP confirm · ALTR excludes PJI

## 5 Management

```
MoM THA follow-up
├─ Asymptomatic, ions <7 ppb, normal films → observe (serial X-ray + ions)
├─ Ions >7 ppb (even asymptomatic) → MARS MRI
├─ Symptomatic + normal ions + normal MRI → close follow-up 3–6 months
└─ Pain/↑ ions + pseudotumour (or progressive osteolysis) → revise to CoP
       └─ Abductor loss → constrained liner ± gluteus maximus transfer
```

### Observation

**Indication** — well-functioning, **low ions**, no pseudotumour · serial radiographs (wear, loosening)

**Close follow-up allowed** — ✓ **symptomatic, Co 6 / Cr 5, normal ESR/CRP, MARS MRI normal**\
✗ Wrong options: asymptomatic with ions >7 (needs MRI) · symptomatic with fluid (revise) · asymptomatic + fluid + progressive osteolysis (revise)

### Revision indications

**Revise** — ✓ **MARS MRI with periarticular fluid + abductor destruction** · painful MoM + ↑ ions + pseudotumour · delay → more soft-tissue damage\
✗ Wrong options: CRP 6 · HO · aspirate 900 WBC · cobalt 5 ppb alone

**Technique** — revise to **ceramic-on-polyethylene** · single- or two-stage (if infection concern) · lateral or anterior approach · **bimodular (modular-neck) stem + monoblock cup** → ✓ **revise both components to CoP + I&D**\
✗ Wrong options: head/liner exchange (monoblock cup) · larger Co-Cr head · I&D alone · observe

**Abductor deficiency** — **Trendelenburg gait** → ✓ **gluteus maximus transfer** (extensor → abductor) ± **constrained liner**\
✗ Wrong options: flexion contracture · medial migration (cup-cage) · psoas · ER weakness

**Dislocation after head/liner exchange for pseudotumour** → ✓ **abductor insufficiency**\
✗ Wrong options: offset · larger head · vertical cup · impingement

**Infected pseudotumour** — neutrophils on histology + ↑ ESR/CRP → debride, explant, spacer, **systemic antibiotics**

## 6 Complications

**Revision outcome** — bone loss, soft-tissue destruction, abductor deficiency → harder revision, worse function · **↑ dislocation + infection**

**After revision** — instability · deep infection · aseptic loosening · persistent psoas tendinitis · persistent metal reaction · **14–20% re-operated within 5 yr**

**Femoral nerve palsy** — rare (case reports) · decompress + revise to CoP

## X vs Y

| | **ALTR / pseudotumour** | **PJI** |
|---|---|---|
| Synovial cells | **Lymphocytes >40%** | **PMN >70–80%** |
| α-defensin | **False positive** possible | Positive |
| Fluid | "Dishwater" grey | Purulent |
| Count | **Manual** | Automated fine |

| | **MoM bearing wear** | **Trunnionosis** |
|---|---|---|
| Co:Cr | ~1:1 | **Co ≫ Cr (2:1)** |
| Bearing | MoM | MoP or MoM with modular taper |
