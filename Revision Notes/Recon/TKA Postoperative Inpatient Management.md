## 1 Basics

**Team** — surgeon, PT, OT, case manager, nursing, patient + family

## 5 Management

### Pain management

| Phase | Measure |
|---|---|
| **Pre-op** | NSAIDs + opioids immediately before surgery ↓ post-op pain |
| **Intra-op** | **Regional (spinal/epidural) preferred** over GA · **peripheral nerve blocks** · **periarticular multimodal injection** (low risk) |
| **Post-op** | **Multimodal oral therapy = gold standard** |

| Drug class | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Opioids** | **Mu agonist** → neuronal hyperpolarisation, ↓ excitability |
| **NSAIDs** | Inhibit **COX-1 + COX-2** → ↓ PGs, TXA, AA mediators |
| **Selective COX-2** | Block AA → PG precursors · ↓ GI effects · may impair bone healing |
| **Gabapentin / pregabalin** | ↓ hyperexcitability of voltage-dependent **Ca²⁺ channels** · pregabalin better oral bioavailability |
| **SNRIs** | Inhibit serotonin + noradrenaline reuptake (CNS) |

### Regional anaesthesia – adductor canal block

**ACB vs femoral nerve block** — ✓ **ACB → earlier ambulation** · **equivalent analgesia** · ACB = **sensory** block (saphenous n. + nerve to vastus medialis, distal to quad motor branches) · FNB → **quad weakness, falls, delayed ambulation**\
✗ Wrong options: FNB better pain control · ACB blocks quads · longer spinal → earlier ambulation · CPM → early ambulation

### Tourniquet use

**Tourniquet-less TKA** — ✓ **↑ intra-op blood loss** (consistent) · no difference in 1-yr flexion, operative time, DVT · thigh pain with tourniquet · ~**90%** of surgeons use one

### Physical therapy & ROM

**Start therapy on day of surgery** — ✓ **↓ length of stay** (AAOS **strong**) · ↓ pain, ↑ function vs POD1 (moderate)

| Activity | Flexion needed |
|---|---|
| **Swing phase of gait** | **65°** |
| **ADLs** | **90°** |
| **Stairs** | **95°** |
| **Rising from chair** | **105°** |

### Continuous passive motion

![CPM — no long-term benefit](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422740-use-of-a-cpm-has-not-shown-to-have-any-long-term-benefit-fol.jpg)

**CPM** — ↑ **early** flexion only (2 wk) · ✓ **no difference in outcomes at 1 yr** · ✓ **no long-term ROM difference** · (✗ Wrong options: ↓ contracture · ↓ MUA · ↑ analgesic need · ↓ VTE (insufficient evidence) · ↑ passive flexion at 6 mo · ↓ SSI) · some studies ↓ LOS · Salter 1970

### AAOS guidelines (early rehab, drains)

| Recommendation (AAOS knee OA surgical CPG) | Strength |
|---|---|
| ✓ **Rehab on day of TKA ↓ LOS** | **Strong** |
| **CPM does not improve outcomes** | **Strong** (against CPM) |
| **No surgical navigation** (no outcome/complication difference) | **Strong** |
| **No difference with/without tourniquet** | **Strong** |
| **No difference patellar resurfacing vs not** | **Strong** |
| ✓ **Drains — no difference in complications/outcomes** (↑ cost, ↑ LOS) | **Strong** (OB213939) · **Moderate** against (2022 CPG, OB219801) — read the options |
| **Cryotherapy devices do not improve outcomes** | **Moderate** |
| **Pre-op PT improves pain/function** | **Limited** |
| **Tourniquet ↓ short-term function** | **Limited** |
| **Robotic vs conventional — no difference** | **Limited** |
| **Simultaneous bilateral TKA** — avoid in high cardiopulmonary risk | **Consensus** |
| **PCA** | No recommendation |

### Post-operative anaemia

**Tourniquet** ↓ intra-op blood loss · **earlier discharge to rehab** → better outcomes

### Outpatient TKR pathway (TO)

**Components (TO)** — **TXA** · periarticular injection (liposomal/plain bupivacaine ± ketorolac, morphine, dexamethasone) · **ACB + iPACK** (infiltration between popliteal artery and capsule) → best early function, ↓ LOS vs FNB · **PT at 2 h**, **WB at 6 h** · minimally invasive · ✓ **no tourniquet** (TO)

**NOT a component (TO)** — ✓ **tourniquet**

**Discharge criteria (TO)** — walk safely with 2 crutches, stairs, home exercises, self-care, **≥90° flexion** · no brace

**Patient selection (TO)** — **age <75**, **BMI <35**, no anaemia, COPD, CHF, cirrhosis, VTE, spinal stenosis, BPH, chronic narcotics · primary TKA, first/second case · RAPT **>10**, near hospital, private insurance

### Drain management

**Drains** — no difference in complications or outcomes · ↑ cost

### Discharge

| Destination | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Medically stable · **80–90° active flexion** · **ambulate 75–100 ft** · stairs |
| **Rehab / SNF** | Earlier discharge → better outcomes |

## X vs Y

| | **Adductor canal block** | **Femoral nerve block** |
|---|---|---|
| Nerves | Saphenous + nerve to VM | Femoral (motor + sensory) |
| Quads | Spared | **Weak** → falls |
| Ambulation | **Earlier** | Delayed |
| Analgesia | Equivalent | Equivalent |

## How it's asked

**"CPM after TKA — true?"** → **no difference in clinical outcomes at 1 yr**

**"CPM in uncomplicated TKA — true?"** → **no long-term difference in ROM**

**"Highest-strength AAOS recommendation?"** → **rehab on day of TKA reduces LOS**

**"Patient requests — strong evidence against?"** → **drain placement**

**"Moderate AAOS recommendation against?"** → **intra-articular closed suction drain**

**"ACB vs FNB — accurate?"** → **ACB gives earlier ambulation**

**"Tourniquet-less TKA — true?"** → **increased intra-op blood loss**

**"Not a component of outpatient TKR?"** → **tourniquet** (TO)
