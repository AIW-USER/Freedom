# TKA in Patella Baja (Prior HTO)

*Recon · 44 / 65*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — TKA in a knee with patella lowered relative to its normal position (patella infera), classically after **HTO**

### Causes of acquired baja

**Proximal tibial osteotomy** — ✓ **most frequent complication of both lateral closing-wedge and medial opening-wedge HTO** · patellar tendon **shortening** during osteotomy + post-op **scarring** · ↑ with post-op immobilisation · patellar height ↓ 89% (Insall–Salvati) / 76.3% (Blackburne–Peel) after HTO\
✗ Wrong options: fracture · peroneal palsy · compartment syndrome · infection

**Others** — **tibial tubercle slide/transfer** · proximal tibial trauma · **joint-line elevation at TKA** · **ACL reconstruction**

| Prior procedure | Converting to TKA → |
|---|---|
| **Medial opening-wedge HTO** | ✓ **Patella baja** |
| **Lateral closing-wedge HTO** | **Baja** · leg **shortening** |
| **Lateral distal femoral (closing) osteotomy** | **Patella alta** |
| **Medial UKA / PF arthroplasty** | not associated |

**Other HTO-to-TKA problems** — proximal tibial surface shifted relative to canal · retained hardware · old incisions · scar · **valgus** more frequent after HTO\
✗ Wrong options: severe varus · collateral instability · alta

### Pseudo-baja from joint line elevation

**Mechanism** — elevated joint line with normal tendon length = "equivalent" of baja

**Insall–Salvati 0.95 → 0.76 after TKA + lateral release** → ✓ **excessive distal femoral resection + lateral release**\
✗ Wrong options: notching · pre-op baja · tendon released off tubercle · excess tibial resection (lowers joint line)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — ✓ **anterior knee pain** (commonest after lateral closing-wedge HTO) · **stiffness**\
✗ Wrong options: lengthening · quad weakness (pre-existing) · infection (0–4%)\
✗ Wrong options: laxity

**Exam** — **mechanical block to flexion** (patella impinges on tibia) · ↓ patellar glides · pain with compression

**Closing-wedge HTO, burning anterior pain at 9 mo, ↓ patellar glides** → ✓ **patella infera**\
✗ Wrong options: alta · recurrent medial overload · nonunion

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP, lateral, **lateral at 30° flexion** · patella sits distal to trochlea

| Index | Baja | Normal | Alta |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Insall–Salvati** (tendon ÷ patella length) | ✓ **<0.8** (e.g. **0.7**) | **0.8–1.2** (~1:1) | >1.2 |
| **Caton–Deschamps** | — | — | **1.4** → alta |
| **Blackburne–Peel** | — | **0.6** normal | — |

**Distractors** — TT-TG **22 mm** → instability · patellar translation **>20 mm** abnormal (↓ after HTO scarring)

## 5 Management

**Non-op** — activity modification, PT · mild symptoms, younger

**TKA** — severe impingement, older with OA

### Techniques for baja at TKA

| Severity | Technique |
|---|---|
| **Mild** | **Patellar button placed superiorly** (smaller dome) + trim inferior patellar bone |
| **Moderate** | ✓ **Lower joint line** — **distal femoral augments** + **lower (more) proximal tibial cut** · may need revision system |
| **Moderate** | **Tibial tubercle transfer cephalad** — difficult near cemented tray · unpredictable healing, extensor lag |
| **Severe** | **Patellectomy** (partial to **10–12 mm**) + **cruciate-substituting** design |

**Avoid** — anything that **raises joint line** (thicker poly, under-resecting tibia) · **inferior** button · **caudad** tubercle transfer\
✗ Wrong options: downsizing femur (no effect)

**HTO + baja — intra-op technique** → ✓ **add distal femur augments**

## X vs Y

| | **True baja** | **Pseudo-baja** |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | Short / scarred **patellar tendon** (HTO, tubercle surgery, ACLR) | **Elevated joint line** (excess distal femoral cut + thick poly) |
| Insall–Salvati | **<0.8** | Normal tendon ratio, patella low relative to joint line |
| Fix | Lower joint line, superior button, tubercle transfer, patellectomy | Restore joint line |
