# THA Dislocation

*Recon · 22 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **1–3%** primary (OB: 0.5–10% primary, **up to 28% after revision**) \
✗ Wrong options: "5–10%"

**Timing** — **70% within first month** · 52% within 3 months · **majority within first year**, then constant lifelong rate · **late = >5 yr**

**Direction** — **75–90% posterior**

**TO notes** — incidence **~3%**, most within **first 3 months**

| Diagnosis | Relative dislocation risk vs OA (TO) |
|---|---|
| AVN | **× 2** |
| DDH | **× 3** |
| Fracture | **× 4** |
| Nonunion | **× 5** |
| **Revision** | **× 11** |

### Mechanism

| Dislocation | Position |
|---|---|
| **Posterior** (posterior approach) | ✓ **Flexion + internal rotation + adduction** — shoe tying, low seat/toilet, bending forward |
| **Anterior** (anterior approach) | **Extension + external rotation** |

### Risk factors

| Category | Factors |
|---|---|
| **Patient** | ✓ **Prior hip surgery (greatest; ~2×)** · **higher comorbidity burden** (Elixhauser/ASA) ✓ · **BMI <20 or >35** · neuromuscular/spastic (**Parkinson**), cognitive impairment · drug/alcohol abuse · female (OB) / gender conflicting (Orthobullets) · age >70 · inflammatory arthritis, AVN |
| **Spine** | ✓ **Prior lumbar(osacral) fusion / fixed spinopelvic alignment** |
| **Surgical** | **Posterior approach** (capsule + ER repair → near anterior rate) · component malposition (commonest cause of recurrent) · **↓ femoral offset** · ✓ **↓ head-to-neck ratio** |
| **Implant** | **MoP or MoM** bearings (vs CoP) · **cemented** components (vs uncemented) |
| **Late** | **PE wear (>2 mm)** — commonest late cause · progressive neuro decline · abductor insufficiency · loosening |

**Not risk factors** — male sex · cup 16° anteversion · 45° abduction · high offset (tightens abductors) · direct lateral approach (lowest) · MoM large head (lower dislocation)

**FNB 2024** — fold increase in dislocation after prior spinal fusion (options none/2/3/4×) — answer not given by source

### Spinopelvic mechanics

**Normal (TO)** — ✓ **pelvis retroverts when sitting upright**, anteverts during sit-to-stand · lumbar lordosis **↓ sitting**\
✗ Wrong options: pelvis anteverted sitting · lordosis ↑ sitting

**Sitting with mobile spine** — pelvis tilts posteriorly → **acetabular anteversion ↑** to accommodate femoral flexion · **sacral slope ↓**, pelvic tilt ↑

**Stiff spine** — **<10° change in sacral slope** standing → sitting · ✓ **lack of change (↓) in pelvic tilt** = fixed alignment → **anterior neck impingement → posterior dislocation**\
✗ Wrong options: ↓ lordosis · ↓ sacral slope · ↑ anteversion · pelvic incidence (fixed parameter)

**Flatback** — standing anterior pelvic plane **−10°** or **PI–LL mismatch >10°** → posterior tilt → **anterior dislocation** in standing

**Management** — lateral **sitting + standing** spine–pelvis–hip films · ✓ **↑ cup anteversion** ± ↑ offset, **dual mobility**, navigation/robotics\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ abduction (vertical cup) · retroverted stem · ↓ offset · constrained liner primary

**Highest-risk history** — ✓ **lumbosacral fusion**\
✗ Wrong options: kyphoplasty · radiation · laminectomy · ablation

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **AP + cross-table lateral** · **CT** for version/malposition

**Malposition** — inclination **>60°** · anteversion **>20°** · **retroversion**

**Eccentric femoral head** — **PE wear** → impending dislocation

### Acetabular safe zone

| Source | Abduction | Anteversion |
|---|---|---|
| **Orthobullets** | **40°** | **15°** |
| **Lewinnek (OB)** | **30–50°** | **5–25°** |
| OB other | 30–50° · 35–45° · 40° | 15–20° · 20° |
| Femoral stem | — | **10–15°** |

**Lewinnek (1978, TO)** — inclination **40° ± 10°** · anteversion **15° ± 10°** · radiographic anteversion = **sin⁻¹ (CD / AB)** · dislocation can still occur inside the safe zone (patient + soft-tissue factors)

**Navigation shows 20° abduction / 20° anteversion** → ✓ **increase abduction by 20°, keep anteversion** (too adducted → flexion impingement, inferior dislocation)

### Cup position and dislocation direction (TO)

| Cup | Dislocation |
|---|---|
| **Excessive anteversion** | **Anterior** with **extension, adduction, ER** (✓ TO false statement: "flexion, adduction, IR") |
| **Retroversion** | **Posterior** with flexion, adduction, IR |
| **Vertical (excess inclination)** | **Superior** with adduction (± adduction contracture, inferior osteophytes) |
| **Horizontal** | **Posterior** with early flexion impingement |

## 5 Management

```
THA dislocation
├─ First early dislocation, components well placed → closed reduction + education
├─ Recurrent (≥2) + malposition → revise malpositioned component
├─ Recurrent, late + PE wear → revision (liner/head ± cup)
└─ Recurrent, well positioned + abductor/soft-tissue deficiency → dual mobility or constrained liner
```

### Wera classification of instability (2012, TO)

| Type | Cause | Management (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Acetabular malposition** | **Cup revision** |
| **II** | **Femoral malposition** | **Stem revision** |
| **III** | **Abductor deficiency** | **Constrained liner** |
| **IV** | **Impingement** | Remove source / ↑ offset |
| **V** | **Late PE wear** | **Head + liner exchange** |
| **VI** | **Unknown cause** | Trochanteric advancement |

**Elevated-rim liner (TO)** — ↓ dislocation (**2.2% vs 3.8%**) but ↑ impingement + wear · **dual mobility** — ↑ stability, ↑ jump distance · cost: PE wear + **intraprosthetic dislocation**

### First dislocation management

**Closed reduction ± immobilization** — **two-thirds** of early dislocations · spica, abduction brace, knee immobilizer

**First dislocation at 4 weeks, components well placed** → ✓ **observation + education on precautions**\
✗ Wrong options: larger head · constrained · 3-month orthosis (only if cognitively impaired)

### Component malposition – acetabular revision

**Revision indication** — **≥2 dislocations** with malalignment, implant failure, or PE wear · malposition = **most common cause of recurrent dislocation** → correct first

**Answers** — vertical cup, 3 posterior dislocations → ✓ **revise acetabular component** (✗ Wrong options: spica · modular stem · brace · constrained liner on malposition) · 10° retroverted cup → ✓ **revise entire cup** to **15–20° anteversion, ~45° abduction** (✗ Wrong options: constrained · offset · 36 mm head · GT osteotomy) · 5° retroversion + 55° abduction → ✓ **acetabular revision** (✗ Wrong options: both · head-neck/length · brace) · underanteverted vertical cup, 3 dislocations bending forward → ✓ **revise cup + larger head/liner** (✗ Wrong options: head/liner exchange alone · PT · brace)

### Revision techniques to increase stability

| Technique | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Realign components** | Retroverted/vertical cup, short neck, low offset, retroverted stem |
| **Larger head** | ↑ head-neck ratio → ↑ ROM to impingement + jump distance |
| **Lateralized liner / extended offset stem** | ↑ offset → abductor tension |
| **Trochanteric advancement** | ↑ abductor tension + compression |
| **Elevated / lipped liner** | Rotate lip toward instability |
| **Dual mobility** | Larger effective head-neck ratio |
| **Constrained liner** | Mechanical capture |
| **Medializing cup** | ✓ **↓ stability** (shortens abductor arm, laxity) — the EXCEPT answer |

**Stem subsidence (cemented, 3 yr)** — effective **neck shortening** → ✓ **↓ abductor tension** → ↑ JRF + instability\
✗ Wrong options: ↓ excursion distance · ↑ arc · ↓ JRF · ↑ offset

**Posterior soft-tissue repair (TO)** — **single myocapsular sleeve (Yadav–Ashok)** = ✓ **capsule + short ERs sutured together to greater trochanter** · ↓ IR by 50%

### Late instability – PE wear & isolated liner exchange

**Late instability (>5 yr)** — ✓ **PE wear (eccentric head)** commonest · usually needs **surgery**\
✗ Wrong options: GT detachment · stem loosening · osteolysis · infection · HO

**Recurrent late dislocations + eccentric wear, normal labs** → ✓ **revision THA for PE wear**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · re-aspiration · observe · PT

**Isolated liner exchange** — stable, well-aligned implants with PE wear as **sole** cause · ✓ **most common complication = dislocation / instability**\
✗ Wrong options: osteolysis · shell–liner failure · PE fracture · sciatic injury · acetabular fracture

**Cementing a liner into a well-fixed shell** (broken locking tines) — **liner several sizes smaller** than shell (room for cement) ✓ · **score PE + smooth shell** · fully seat · avoid impingement · commonest complication still **dislocation**\
✗ Wrong options: "closely match liner and shell size"

### Constrained liners

**Indications** — ✓ recurrent instability with **well-positioned components** · **abductor deficiency / unrepairable capsule** · **neuromuscular / cognitive** disease · no identifiable cause · failed larger head + trochanteric advancement · elderly low-demand

**Contraindications** — **component malposition** ✓ (revise first) · **PE wear alone** ✓ (EXCEPT answer) · pelvic discontinuity · infection

**Limited because of** — ✓ **high failure rates** (fatigue, **ring/liner dissociation**) · ↑ bone–implant stress · ↓ ROM, worse function · last resort

**Answers** — cup 20° anteversion + 40° abduction, recurrent → ✓ **constrained** (✗ Wrong options: discontinuity · infected · 15° retroverted stem · 10° retroverted/60° cup) · bariatric weight loss, failed big head + GT advancement, aligned → ✓ **constrained** (✗ Wrong options: resection · brace · capsular ablation · bipolar) · abductors absent, components well placed → indication ✓ **incompetent abductors** · post-MoM deficient abductors, Trendelenburg → ✓ **constrained liner** (✗ Wrong options: medialize cup · offset · large ceramic head)

**Broken constrained ring** → ✓ **↑ dislocation risk** · dislocated constrained + malposition + Parkinson → ✓ **acetabular revision with constrained implant**\
✗ Wrong options: closed or open reduction

### Dual mobility & abductor deficiency

**Design** — small inner head captive in mobile PE liner, articulating with shell (Bousquet 1974, France) · Charnley low friction + McKee–Farrar ↑ head-neck ratio · ✓ **↑ arc of motion before impingement** + ↑ jump distance\
✗ Wrong options: locking ring (constrained) · ↓ jump distance · lipped liner · face-changing liner

**Unconstrained** — tripolar may add constraint ring · ✓ **"constrained design reduces dislocation" is NOT true** of DM (TO) · both articulations share centre · inner head dominates normal ROM, outer in high ROM

**Indications (TO)** — high instability risk: neuromuscular, cognitive, **ASA ≥3**, **>75 yr with prior hip surgery** · revision THA · THA after **femoral neck fracture** · tumour · ✓ **reduces instability in revision**\
✗ Wrong options: jumbo cup (↑ instability) · small head · capsular debridement · ↓ abductor tension

**Intraprosthetic dislocation (IPD)** — unique to DM · ✓ **wear of PE liner's retentive chamfer** · head articulates on metal shell → **acute shortening, limp**, metallosis · X-ray: **eccentric head** + **"bubble sign"** (liner in soft tissue) · can follow closed reduction attempt

![Dual mobility — bubble sign of intraprosthetic dislocation](../../MCQ/attachments/TO17099762-3.png)

### Salvage

**Conversion to hemiarthroplasty/bipolar** — soft-tissue deficiency · contraindicated if acetabular bone compromised · rarely used (DM) · groin pain, migration

**Resection arthroplasty** — options exhausted · bone + soft-tissue loss · **psychiatric secondary gain**

## X vs Y

| | **Dual mobility** | **Constrained liner** |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | ↑ ROM before impingement, ↑ jump distance | **Mechanical capture** (locking ring) |
| ROM | Large | **Restricted** |
| Failure | **Intraprosthetic dislocation** | **Ring/liner dissociation**, loosening |
| Use | High-risk primaries, revision | Last resort — abductor/soft-tissue deficiency |

| | **Early dislocation** | **Late dislocation (>5 yr)** |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | Malposition, soft tissue, noncompliance | **PE wear**, neuro decline, abductor loss |
| Treatment | Often closed reduction | Usually **surgery** |
