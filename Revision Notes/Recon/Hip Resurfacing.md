# Hip Resurfacing

*Recon · 12 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### History & design

**Early failures** — large head on **polyethylene** → ↑ **volumetric wear** → osteolysis

**Modern (FDA 2006, Birmingham — McMinn)** — **metal-on-metal** + **large femoral head** · popular in young (less femoral resection) · cemented femoral cap

![Birmingham hip resurfacing](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob5032-birmingham-hip-prosthesis.jpg)

### Advantages & disadvantages

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| **Preserves proximal femoral bone** ✓ (only proven advantage) | **No modularity** — cannot adjust **length or offset** |
| **Better stability** vs 22–32 mm THA · **dislocation <1%** (0.75% at 3 yr) | **Larger exposure** / soft-tissue release |
| Restores biomechanics · **lower LLD risk** · physiologic stress transfer | **Femoral neck fracture** (unique) |
| Easier revision than IM THA | **↑ metal ions**, metallosis, pseudotumour |
| Suits **proximal femoral deformity** | ↑ AVN of head · ↑ reoperation |

**vs THA** — ✓ **preservation of proximal femoral bone**\
✗ Wrong options: better compliance · more sport · more mobility · better pain relief

**vs large-head MoM THA** — ✓ **femoral bone preservation**\
✗ Wrong options: lower loosening · acetabular preservation · lower reoperation · lower wear (**same bearing**)

**Disadvantages EXCEPT** — ✓ **higher dislocation rate** (it is lower) · true disadvantages: ↑ periprosthetic fracture, ↑ metal ions, ↑ osteonecrosis, larger incision

## 5 Management

### Indications & contraindications

**Indications (controversial)** — advanced arthritis + **good proximal femoral bone** · best in **young, large males with OA** · proximal femoral deformity making THA hard

**TO indications** — ✓ **active men <60 with OA** (or post-traumatic) · male labourers who squat · prerequisite: near-normal proximal femur, **¾ of head intact** after contouring\
✗ Wrong options: osteopenia · AVN >30% head · malunited trochanter

| Absolute | Relative |
|---|---|
| **Femoral head/neck bone-stock deficiency** — **cysts** (1.5 cm) | **Coxa vara** (↑ neck fracture) |
| Small / bone-deficient **acetabulum** | **Significant LLD** (cannot correct) |
| | **Women of child-bearing age** (ions cross placenta; ↑ complications) |
| | **Renal failure** (cannot excrete ions) |
| | **Osteoporosis** (TO) · **ON >20–30% of head** (TO) |

**Contraindications EXCEPT** — ✓ **age <50**\
✗ Wrong options: head cysts · acetabular dysplasia · coxa vara · neck bone deficiency

**Osteonecrosis — conflict** — OB: **not a contraindication** if proximal bone stock preserved · OB (large collapse): **higher failure** → THA safer · TO: ON **>20–30%** head = contraindication

**38-yr-old woman, AVN with mild collapse — true?** → ✓ **female sex → ↑ complications** · Canadian series: **96.4% 5-yr survival**, failures = neck fracture + femoral loosening\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ ions = well-functioning · ON absolute contraindication · ↑ LLD · <50% success (success **>90%**)

**Outcomes** — **79–98%** success · better in young large males with **OA** than dysplasia/ON · some products withdrawn · recent RCTs: few differences vs THA

### Tönnis grading (TO)

**Used for** — ✓ **arthritis grading for hip-preservation surgery** · grade 0 none · 1 sclerosis, minimal JSN · 2 small cysts, moderate JSN · 3 large cysts, obliteration, head deformity\
✗ Wrong options: infection · FAI grading · resurfacing

## 6 Complications

### Femoral neck fracture – early reoperation

**Incidence** — **0–4%** (~1%) · **> THA**

**Timing** — **most common early complication** (first 3 yr) · ✓ **most common cause of revision <20 weeks / first 6 months** · unique vs THA → ✓ **transcervical neck fracture**\
✗ Wrong options: abductor rupture · dislocation · HO · stiffness · loosening · infection · pseudotumour

**Mechanism** — **osteonecrosis** of retained head · vertical line neck → lesser trochanter · groin pain

**Risk factors** — **femoral neck notching** (superior) · **varus positioning** ✓ · **osteoporotic** bone · large preexisting **AVN / cystic bone loss** · **incomplete seating** · neck impingement from malaligned cup (e.g. **excessive retroversion**) · **female** · older age · obesity\
✗ Wrong options: sclerotic

**Not a risk factor** — ✓ **excessive acetabular inclination** (EXCEPT question)

**Prevention** — femoral component in **slight valgus** (~10° relative valgus ↑ load to fracture) · avoid notching

**Treatment** — **convert to primary THA** · **cerclage above lesser trochanter** before stem insertion

![Femoral neck notching](../../MCQ/attachments/q215-illb-noching.jpg)

### Metal ions, pseudotumour & ARMD

**Metallosis** — ↑ **cobalt, chromium**, Co:Cr ratio in blood + urine · mostly **edge loading** · cup in **less abduction** than standard THA

**Causes of metallosis** — ✓ **edge loading** · malposition · third-body wear · impingement · cobalt sensitivity\
✗ Wrong options: activity-related wear · psoas tendinitis · PJI · stress fracture

**Mimics infection** — synovial WBC falsely ↑ by metal debris/corrosion → ✓ **manual cell count** next · also MARS MRI, ion levels, repeat aspiration · PJI + metallosis can coexist\
✗ Wrong options: 2-stage revision · isolated cup/stem revision · arthrodesis

**Pseudotumour** — solid/fluid periprosthetic mass (TO) · pain, swelling, DVT, abductor destruction · risk: ✓ **women ≤40**, **small components**, **dysplasia** · may be asymptomatic · symptomatic → **revision to non-MoM** bearing\
✗ Wrong options: large components · men ≥40 · primary OA

**Pseudotumour revision** — **poor prognosis** ✓ · soft-tissue/nerve destruction, high re-revision\
✗ Wrong options: neck fracture · ON · femoral loosening (revise easily to stem)

**Revision with pseudotumour, abductors intact, cup well placed** → ✓ **revise femoral component to stem + non-MoM bearing**\
✗ Wrong options: I&D + spacer · PE liner exchange (no PE) · MoM THA · constrained liner

**ALVAL** — **aseptic lymphocyte-dominant** vasculitis-associated lesion\
✗ Wrong options: "macrophage-dominant"

![Pseudotumour around resurfacing](../../MCQ/attachments/TO17907825-2.jpg)

### Other complications

**Complications EXCEPT** — ✓ **polyethylene debris** (MoM — no PE) · true: DVT, neck fracture, aseptic loosening, dislocation

**Aseptic loosening** — early loosening of **cemented femoral component** → convert to THA

**Heterotopic ossification** — **↑ vs THA** (wider exposure)

**Hemi-resurfacing** — acetabulum not resurfaced → **acetabular erosion / groin pain**

## X vs Y

| | **Hip resurfacing (MoM)** | **THA (CoP / MoP)** |
|---|---|---|
| Femoral bone | **Preserved** | Resected |
| Dislocation | **<1%** | Higher |
| Early reoperation | **Neck fracture** | Dislocation / infection |
| Unique risks | Neck fracture, **metal ions, pseudotumour, ALVAL** | PE wear (MoP) |
| Length/offset | **Fixed** | Adjustable |
| Best patient | **Young large male, OA** | Older, women, poor bone |

## How it's asked

**"Indication for hip resurfacing?"** → **active man <60 with OA** (TO)

**"Contraindications EXCEPT?"** → **age <50**

**"Disadvantages of resurfacing EXCEPT?"** → **higher dislocation rate**

**"Advantage vs THA?"** → **proximal femoral bone preservation**

**"Advantage vs large-head MoM THA?"** → **femoral bone preservation**

**"38-yr-old woman with AVN considering resurfacing — true?"** → **female sex ↑ complications**

**"Most common cause of revision <20 weeks?"** → **periprosthetic (femoral neck) fracture**

**"Neck fracture 2 months after resurfacing — risk factor?"** → **varus positioning**

**"Uniquely at risk vs father's CoP THA?"** → **transcervical femoral neck fracture**

**"Neck fracture risk EXCEPT?"** → **excessive acetabular inclination**

**"Masses around trochanter on MRI after resurfacing?"** → **pseudotumour** (TO)

**"Complications EXCEPT?"** → **polyethylene debris**

**"Poor prognosis after revising failed resurfacing?"** → **pseudotumour**

**"High risk of pseudotumour?"** → **women ≤40**

**"Synovial WBC 15,000, ↑ Co/Cr — next step?"** → **manual cell count**

**"Runner with ↑ Co/Cr after BHR — cause?"** → **edge loading**

**"Pseudotumour, abductors intact — surgery?"** → **revise femoral component, non-MoM bearing**

**"Tönnis grading is used for?"** → **arthritis grading before hip preservation** (TO)
