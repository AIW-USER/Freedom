# Hip Hemiarthroplasty Periprosthetic Fracture

*Recon · 8 / 65*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **1–4%** of hemiarthroplasties · **uncemented 1–7.4%** vs **cemented 0.2–1.3%**

**Risk factors** — ↑ age · **female** · **osteoporosis** · **uncemented stem** (RCTs + registries) · **undersized** component · **slip-taper stem** in cemented hemi

**Associated** — stress shielding, peri-implant osteopenia, infection, **osteolysis**, subsidence

**Mechanism** — **ground-level fall**

## 3 Presentation

**History** — fall · **start-up pain → antecedent loosening** · progressive pain, constitutional symptoms, wound problems → suspect **PJI**

**Exam** — pain, cannot bear weight · **shortened, externally rotated** limb · pain on log roll · check prior **scars**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — **AP + lateral femur** (pattern, displacement, implant position) · **AP pelvis** if revising

**CT** — implant stability, fracture morphology · **best for cement-mantle integrity**

**Stable stem signs** — proximal cement mantle intact · **no subsidence** · always **test stem stability intraoperatively**

### Vancouver classification & treatment

| Type | Description | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **AG** | Greater trochanter | ORIF if **>2 cm displaced** (abductor escape) · else nonop |
| **AL** | Lesser trochanter | **Nonoperative** |
| **B1** | Around stem, **well fixed** | **ORIF** |
| **B2** | Around stem, **loose**, good bone stock | **Revision** (diaphyseal-engaging) ± ORIF |
| **B3** | Around stem, loose, **poor bone stock** | **Proximal femoral replacement** |
| **C** | **Distal** to stem | **ORIF** |

## 5 Management

```
Hemiarthroplasty periprosthetic fracture
├─ Trochanteric: AL or AG <2 cm → protected WB, no active abduction 6–12 wk
│      └─ AG >2 cm → ORIF (claw plate, cables, TBW)
├─ Stem well fixed (B1) or distal (C) → ORIF (locking plate)
├─ Stem loose, good bone (B2) → revise to diaphyseal-engaging stem ± ORIF
└─ Stem loose, poor bone (B3) → proximal femoral replacement
```

### Treatment by stem stability

**Nonoperative (AG <2 cm, AL)** — protected WB · early walker WB in elderly · **restrict active abduction 6–12 weeks** (AG)

**ORIF (B1, C)** — as soon as medically optimized · lateral subvastus / **MIPO** · **polyaxial locking plate** · proximal fixation: cables, cerclage, screws · **drilling into cement ↑ pull-out strength but may compromise mantle** · allograft struts controversial · **15% complications**

**B2 ORIF alone (controversial)** — select **elderly, low-demand** · favourable: **simple pattern, anatomic reduction, no prior loosening, intact bone–cement interface**

**B2 revision** — ✓ **uncemented diaphyseal-engaging stem bypassing fracture by 2 cortical diameters** ± ORIF · monoblock cylindrical or **modular fluted tapered** · ± trochanteric bolt/claw · **up to 25% complications** (revision or ORIF) · revision → ↑ blood loss

**Long cemented stem (controversial)** — cement trapped in fracture → impaired healing · cement-in-cement may equal tapered stems in selected patients

**Metaphyseal-engaging components** — controversial in fracture setting

**B3** — resect fracture · cemented/uncemented **PFR** · reattach trochanter (suture/cable/wire)

![Vancouver B2 around a loose hemiarthroplasty stem](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob423186-hemivancb2.jpg)

**OB answer pattern** — B2 (6–8 wk post hemi, loose stem): ✓ **fracture fixation + diaphyseal-engaging revision**\
✗ Wrong options: protected WB (Vancouver A) · cerclage alone (B1) · locking plate ± strut (B1/C) · PFR (B3) · reuse original stem · **proximally coated stem** (needs press-fit in fractured zone) · bed rest

**B1 (5 yr post hemi, fracture at stem tip, proximal mantle intact)** → ✓ **ORIF alone**\
✗ Wrong options: long cemented revision (cemented stems not implant of choice in revision) · long uncemented ± ORIF (for B2) · PFR (salvage)

## 6 Complications

**List** — infection · dislocation · refracture · **stem subsidence** · implant failure · **trochanteric escape**

**Mortality** — **~25% at 1 yr**, up to **50% at 2 yr**

**Prognostic** — **early mobilization** critical · **surgical delay → ↑ mortality**

## X vs Y

| | **Cemented hemi** | **Uncemented hemi** |
|---|---|---|
| Periprosthetic fracture rate | **0.2–1.3%** | **1–7.4%** |
| Special risk | **Slip-taper** stem | Undersized stem |

| | **B1** | **B2** | **B3** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stem | Fixed | Loose | Loose |
| Bone stock | — | Good | **Poor** |
| Treatment | **ORIF** | **Diaphyseal revision ± ORIF** | **PFR** |

## How it's asked

**"Hemi 6 weeks ago, fall, loose stem — optimal treatment?"** → **fracture fixation + revision with diaphyseal-engaging stem** (B2)

**"Hemi 8 weeks, fall — best approach?"** → **ORIF + diaphyseal-engaging stem**\
✗ Wrong options: original stem · proximally coated stem

**"Hemi 5 years, fracture at stem tip, mantle intact proximally?"** → **ORIF alone** (B1)

**"Vancouver AG — ORIF threshold?"** → **>2 cm displacement**

**"Revision stem must bypass fracture by?"** → **2 cortical diameters**

**"Risk factor for hemi periprosthetic fracture?"** → **uncemented stem** (cemented: slip-taper)

**"Best imaging for cement mantle?"** → **CT**

**"Mortality at 1 year?"** → **~25%**
