## 1 Basics

### Anabolic steroids – mechanism & adverse effects (lipids, aggression, alopecia, surgical risk)

**What they are** — **synthetic derivatives of testosterone** (anabolic-androgenic steroids, AAS) · oral or injection · mimic testosterone + dihydrotestosterone → ↑ protein synthesis

**Primary mechanism of ↑ muscle** — **↑ production of messenger RNA**\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ cortisol production · VEGF · ↓ HDL (HDL falls but doesn't affect muscle)

**Other actions** — **stimulate GH release** · **reverse / attenuate cortisol (catabolic hormones)** → ↓ catabolism · more intense + sustained workouts · ↑ strength, ↓ recovery time · used to **gain muscle + lose fat** · ↑ erythropoiesis\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ fat

**Examples** — **methandrostenolone (Dianabol)**: first made 1960, most used + abused steroid worldwide · **stanozolol**: Winstrol (oral) / Winstrol Depot (IM), derived from **dihydrotestosterone** · **metandienone**, **DHMCT** (TO doping cases)

| AAS adverse effect (cardiometabolic) | Fact |
|---|---|
| **HDL** | **↓ HDL (20–70%)** + ↓ **apolipoprotein A1** (main HDL particle protein) ✓ (✗ Wrong options: ↑ HDL) |
| **LDL** | **↑ LDL > 20%** (OB) · **↑ or no effect** on LDL (OB) · ↑ LDL, hypercholesterolaemia (Orthobullets) |
| **Coronary risk** | Lipid changes → **CAD risk 3–6×** · premature CV events |
| **MI in young athlete** | **↑ LDL** (+ ↓ HDL) ✓ · ↑ O2 demand at peak exercise + accelerated atherosclerosis (✗ Wrong options: direct endothelial damage · ↓ BP · conduction change) |
| **Blood pressure** | **↑ systolic + diastolic** (hypertension) (✗ Wrong options: ↓ BP) |
| Heart | **LVH**, impaired diastolic filling, thrombosis |
| **Ventricular arrhythmia** | Cause of **sudden death** (✗ Wrong options: cause of CAD) · fatal cases in small case series |
| **Coagulation** | **↑ factor VII, IX + thromboxane** → hypercoagulable → **↓ bleeding time** (✗ Wrong options: ↑ bleeding time) |
| **Liver** | **Liver tumours** (Orthobullets) · **↑ liver enzymes** (OB) · oral AAS **induce hepatic enzymes** → less sensitive to anaesthetics (✗ Wrong options: impaired liver function) |
| **Fluid** | **Mineralocorticoid effect** + **diuretics used to mask** it → **fluid + electrolyte imbalance** ✓ |
| Ventilation | Large muscle mass + high calories → ↑ O2 use + CO2 → **↑ ventilatory requirement** (✗ Wrong options: lowered O2 requirement) |
| Spleen | **No effect** (✗ Wrong options: splenomegaly) |
| Immune | Immunosuppression (OB) |

| AAS adverse effect (endocrine, skin, MSK, psych) | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Gonadal** | **Testicular atrophy (40–51%)** · ↓ gonadotropins + sex hormones (oestrogen, testosterone) → **↓ BMD**, **oligo- / azoospermia (↓ sperm count)** · uterine atrophy |
| **Gynaecomastia** | **10–34%** |
| **Skin** | **Acne (40–54%)** · cutaneous striae (34%) · injection-site pain (36%) · **alopecia / male-pattern baldness** |
| Female | **Irreversible deepening of voice** (Orthobullets) |
| Child | **Growth retardation** |
| **MSK** | **Tendon + muscle injury / rupture** (e.g. **pectoralis major**) · osteonecrosis of hip (OB) · ↑ body weight |
| **Behaviour** | **Aggression, hostility, violence = most common** ✓ · psychosis, depression, mania **rare** · suicidal behaviour · **no drug dependence** (✗ Wrong options: like narcotics) |
| **Not linked** | **Decreased vision** ✓ (methandrostenolone EXCEPT question) |

**Reversibility** — **alopecia = only nonreversible effect** (OB) · Orthobullets also lists **irreversible female voice deepening** — read the options · on stopping: hypertrophy + training gains lost quickly, acne + personality effects reversible

**Lipids after stopping** — **HDL / LDL ratios return to pre-existing levels** (OB) · **changes persist after withdrawal** (Hartgens, OB) — read the options

**Surgery + anaesthesia in AAS user** — **fluid and electrolyte imbalance** ✓\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ bleeding time (actually ↓) · impaired liver function · lowered O2 requirement · splenomegaly

### Hormonal & metabolic effects (glucagon, endurance adaptations)

**Endurance training adaptations** — **↑ storage + utilisation of intramuscular lipids** ✓ → ↓ CHO contribution, **preserves muscle glycogen** · **type I** hypertrophy (✗ Wrong options: type II) · **↑ Krebs cycle enzymes** (✗ Wrong options: ↓) · **↓ rate of glycogen depletion** (depletion = fatigue) (✗ Wrong options: ↑) · **blunted catecholamine response** → ↓ HR at same intensity (✗ Wrong options: ↑ sympathetic activity)

### Creatine – side effects

**What it is** — from **glycine, arginine + methionine** · **supplement, not an anabolic steroid** · → **phosphocreatine → ATP source** (phosphate storage substrate) · off-season weightlifting

**Performance** — **mixed results** (Orthobullets) · ↑ work in **anaerobic** trials but **cannot ↑ peak force** (Orthobullets) · ↑ strength accrual + **↑ force / power in short maximal bouts** (Terjung, OB) — read the options · mild ↑ body composition + strength, limited proof of performance / recovery (OB) · greatest effect in **loading cycle**; not running / swimming (Branch, OB) · does not reduce muscle-damage markers (Rawson, OB)

**For endurance (TO)** — **not primarily endurance** · benefits **short bursts of power, high-intensity short-duration**

**Mechanism of dehydration** — intracellular creatine **pulls water from intravascular → intracellular space** → **dehydration** ✓

**Side effects** — **dehydration**, **muscle cramping** (✓ more common than with testosterone), water retention, nausea · strains, stiffness (Terjung) · ↑ muscle injury · **renal insufficiency (rare)**\
✗ Wrong options: tendon tears · ↓ HDL · hair loss, testicular atrophy, acne (testosterone)

**High-school football (McGuine, OB)** — **30%** use creatine · perceived benefit = faster recovery · biggest risk = **dehydration**

**Class** — **caffeine**, **ephedra**, **ephedrine**, **"ma huang"** (energy drinks), **amphetamines**, methylphenidate (TO)

**Stimulant risks** — **dehydration**, **impaired heat management**, **high blood pressure**, nervous system impairment

**Caffeine dose** — **2–3 mg/kg** improves performance · ↓ fatigue + ↑ alertness · stimulates CNS, **↓ perceived exertion** · ↑ endurance, reaction time, alertness · **↑ fat oxidation** (TO) \
✗ Wrong options: **"no proven effect"** (TO)

**Caffeine status** — previously banned by IOC, **now allowed up to 12 µg/ml urine** (Orthobullets) · removed from WADA list **January 2004** (Exercise Science chapter) · **2018 WADA Monitoring Program, not prohibited** (TO) — read the options

**Caffeine ban history (TO, PED lecture)** — "IOC banned 1962 → removed 1972" (TO — verify: caffeine was a restricted IOC substance 1984–2003, removed from WADA list **Jan 2004**) — read the options

**Ephedra** — **cardiac arrhythmias (can be fatal)** · psychiatric, autonomic, GI symptoms, palpitations (OB) · "apparently effective" + weight-loss supplement (TO)

**Other drugs (OB distractors)** — **furosemide = most commonly used banned diuretic** · **α-ketoglutarate** = supplement claimed to **remove excess ammonia** · **propranolol** → ↓ anxiety → steadier hands, even HR, focus

**Supplement regulation (TO)** — **DSHEA 1994**: no proof of safety / efficacy needed before marketing; FDA acts only after adverse events · choose **USP seal** / **NSF Certified for Sport** · best reason to take supplements = **aid recovery** · prohibited: androstenedione, Chinese ephedra

## 2 Mechanism

### Beta blockers – pharmacology (TO)

**Action** — **competitive antagonists** of adrenaline + noradrenaline at β-receptors · **β1: heart, kidney** · **β2: lungs, GI tract, vascular smooth muscle, skeletal muscle** · **β3: fat cells**

**Haemodynamics** — **↓ HR + ↓ cardiac output, no effect on stroke volume** · ↓ myocardial O2 demand

**In endurance sport** — ↑ perceived exertion, ↓ cardiac output + **VO2max**, **earlier fatigue + lactate threshold**, may worsen **EIB / asthma** → **avoid in asthmatics + endurance athletes**

**Why banned in precision sports** — **↓ HR, ↓ hand tremor, anxiolysis** → steadiness (**archery, shooting, billiards**)

### Gene doping (TO)

**Definition** — non-therapeutic gene therapy · DNA inserted via vector to **enhance performance** (✗ Wrong options: restore missing gene function) · **banned by WADA in 2003** ✓ (✗ Wrong options: 2001, 2006, 2008)

**Orthokine vs PRP** — both autologous blood · Orthokine **cultured at higher temperature** · **↑ growth factors** per procedure · **blocks IL-1 → anti-inflammatory** · FDA: too much manipulation → **unapproved drug** \

### Therapeutic use exemptions – glucocorticoids & IV fluids (TO)

### Team physician – travel, jet lag, media & medical bag (TO)

**Circadian system** — master clock = **suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN)** + peripheral clocks · synchronised by **zeitgebers**: **light (most critical)** via retinohypothalamic tract, sleep–wake, activity, social cues, meals · light timing, intensity, duration, spectrum matter
