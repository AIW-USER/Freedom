# Knee Imaging

*Knee & Sports · 25 / 61*

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings – high-yield signs

| Finding | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Segond sign** | Small **lateral tibial** avulsion → **ACL tear** |
| **Reverse Segond** | Small **medial tibial** avulsion → **PCL tear** |
| **Arcuate sign** | **Fibular head** avulsion → **PLC injury** |
| **Pellegrini-Stieda** | **Medial femoral condyle** avulsion / calcification → **chronic MCL** |
| Patella alta | Patellofemoral pathology |
| **Patella baja** | **Arthrofibrosis** |
| **Fairbanks changes** | Post-meniscectomy DJD: **square condyle, peaked eminences, ridging, narrowing** |
| Lateral MFC lesion | **OCD** |
| **Crescent-shaped MFC lesion** | **SONK** |
| **Square LFC + cupped lateral plateau** | **Discoid meniscus** (3 consecutive sagittal MRI slices) |
| Bipartite patella | Must differentiate from fracture |

### Weight-bearing flexed PA & mechanical axis views

**Weightbearing films** — preferred for joint space · **always** when patient can bear weight (shows mechanical loading)

**Rosenberg view = weight-bearing PA in 45° flexion** — **most sensitive for degenerative change** · best for **early tibiofemoral OA, posterior wear** · earliest cartilage loss in **30–60° flexion zone** (missed on extension films), esp. **lateral compartment**\
✗ Wrong options: non-WB AP · WB AP · non-WB PA · Merchant

**Dervin** — 45° PA > AP vs arthroscopy for lateral wear → **"screening radiograph of choice"** for knee OA

![Method for obtaining the PA flexion knee radiograph](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3004-method-for-obtaining-pa-knee-xray.jpg)

**Merchant / sunrise** — patellofemoral space, tilt, alignment

**Medial pain, varus, old arthroscopy, normal supine PCP films (OB)** — **next: mechanical axis + weight-bearing radiographs**\
✗ Wrong options: reassurance + unloader brace · steroid injection · MRI · PT (no diagnosis yet)

| Young medial OA option (OB) | When |
|---|---|
| Medial unloader brace, steroid injection | Non-surgical, isolated medial OA |
| **Valgus HTO** | Disrupted mechanical axis, excessive varus · medial overload after meniscectomy · osteochondral defects (Rossi) · also address slope |
| **Meniscal transplant** | Normal / corrected axis, stable, minimal arthritis (Amendola) |
| UKA | Some cases; arthroplasty usually avoided at this age |

**Brouwer** — valgus HTO ↑ pain + function in medial OA · no non-op control · no osteotomy proven better

### Stress radiographs

| Stress view | Use |
|---|---|
| **Varus–valgus** | **Physeal fractures in children** · MCL / LCL injuries |
| **Posterior stress** | **Isolated PCL: 10–12 mm** posterior displacement · **PCL + PLC: > 12 mm** |

### MRI

| Plane | Evaluates |
|---|---|
| Sagittal | Menisci, cruciates, cartilage, extensor mechanism |
| Coronal | Menisci, cruciates, **collaterals**, cartilage |
| Axial | **Patellofemoral joint**, cruciates, popliteal fossa |

| Sequence | Signal | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | **Water dark, fat bright** | **Anatomy**, not pathology |
| **T2** | **Fat dark, water bright** | **Oedema + pathology** |
| **STIR** | Fat suppression | Better imaging **with prostheses** |

**Meniscus** — **medial extrusion > 3 mm** → severe degeneration, large tear or **root tear** · radial tears ↑ after prior meniscal surgery (**32% vs 14%**) · **3.0 T MRI > 90%** accuracy / sensitivity / specificity

**ACL** — ↑ signal, thickening, cysts in / around ACL = common + **clinically insignificant** (no instability)

**Bone bruise** — near **sulcus terminalis** · osteochondral injury

**PLC** — LCL + popliteus tendon routinely seen (others rarely) · **oedema posterior to popliteus tendon** → underlying PLC injury

**Reading rules (TO)** — diagnose on **sagittal + coronal**; **axials key for patellofemoral + popliteus tendon** · normal **ACL greyer than PCL** (fibrofatty; AM taut, PL fibrillar) — high signal between intact taut fibres ≠ sprain · normal **PCL uniformly dark, buckled in extension** · fluid in popliteal sulcus + magic-angle signal normal · **fabella** in lateral gastrocnemius = normal

**Osteophyte vs enthesophyte (TO)** — osteophytes at **articular margins** · spurs at **tendon insertions = enthesophytes**

**Metal (TO)** — paramagnetic artefact mimics oedema → request **MAVRIC** (metal artefact reduction) MRI

**Partner injuries (TO)** — **isolated popliteus tear does not occur** → look for FCL, ALL, cruciate · **every ACL tear → look for lateral meniscus posterior root tear** (near ACL footprint; MFLs can hide it) · PCL injury → check **popliteal artery** flow void

### Ultrasound

**Uses** — **fluid collections**, bursae · **arthrofibrosis after TKA** → **synovial thickening + neovascularity**

## How it's asked

**"Most sensitive view for degenerative changes?"** → **weight-bearing PA in 45° flexion (Rosenberg)**

**"Medial pain, normal supine films from PCP — next step?"** → **mechanical axis + weight-bearing radiographs**

**"Lateral femoral condyle + inferomedial patella contusions (TO)?"** → **transient lateral patellar dislocation**

**"Loss of extension months after ACLR, graft intact (TO)?"** → **cyclops lesion**
