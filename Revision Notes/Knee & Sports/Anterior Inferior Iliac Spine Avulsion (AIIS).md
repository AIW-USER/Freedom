# Anterior Inferior Iliac Spine Avulsion (AIIS)

*Knee & Sports · 3 / 61*

## 1 Basics

**Who** — adolescents **14–17 yr**, **males > females** · **kicking sports** (soccer)

**Why adolescents** — **apophysis weaker than the musculotendinous unit** during forceful contraction

### Anatomy

**AIIS** — bony prominence **just above the acetabulum** → origin of the **direct head of rectus femoris** (**femoral n.**)

**Reflected head** — **supra-acetabular groove + anterior hip capsule**

**Insertion** — rectus femoris → **quadriceps tendon → superior pole of patella**

### Pelvic apophyseal avulsions — sites and muscles

| Site | Muscle | Frequency (Rossi, 203) |
|---|---|---|
| **Ischial tuberosity** | Hamstrings (BF long head, SM, ST) + adductor magnus | **\~50% — most common** |
| **AIIS** | **Rectus femoris (direct head)** | **25%** |
| **ASIS** | **Sartorius** + TFL | 20% |
| Superior corner of pubic symphysis | Adductors | 4% |
| **Iliac crest** | Abdominals | **1% — least common** |
| Lesser trochanter | **Iliopsoas** | — |
| Greater trochanter | Gluteus medius + minimus | — |

**Most common site** — **ischial tuberosity \~50%** (Rossi, OB) · **AIIS most common** (Schuett, 228 avulsions, OB — see ASIS chapter) — read the options

**Sports** — **soccer + gymnastics** most common

## 2 Mechanism

### Rectus femoris origin avulsion — mechanism, innervation & nonoperative care

**Mechanism** — **eccentric contraction of rectus femoris** **as hip extends + knee flexes** → avulses origin · **kicking** (missed ball, foot strikes ground) · hurdler's **trailing limb** striking hurdle

**MRI label** — rectus femoris = anterior central thigh muscle\
✗ Wrong options: vastus lateralis · vastus intermedius · vastus medialis · sartorius

**Nerve** — **femoral**\
✗ Wrong options: genitofemoral (cremaster) · pudendal (levator ani, bulbo- / ischiocavernosus) · obturator (obturator externus, adductor longus / brevis, gracilis) · superior gluteal (gluteus medius / minimus, TFL)

**Distractor insertions** — sartorius → **pes anserinus** · TFL → **Gerdy's tubercle** via ITB · iliopsoas → lesser trochanter · gluteus medius / minimus → greater trochanter

## 3 Presentation

**History** — **sudden "pop" in the pelvis**

**Exam** — antalgic gait · **anterior hip pain + hip flexion weakness** · pain on **resisted hip flexion**, local tenderness

**Differential** — SCFE, Perthes, congenital hip subluxation, malignancy, infection

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — AP pelvis / **AP + frog lateral hip** show the AIIS avulsion · **X-rays sufficient** to diagnose + decide\
✗ Wrong options: MRI before treatment

## 5 Management

```
AIIS avulsion
├─ Minimally displaced (almost all) → REST, ACTIVITY MODIFICATION, PT
└─ Selected cases → SURGERY
       ├─ Displaced > 2 cm / painful nonunion → ORIF
       └─ Symptomatic heterotopic ossification → ARTHROSCOPIC EXCISION
```

**Nonoperative** — **almost all succeed** · bed rest, ice, **activity modification**, NSAIDs, supervised PT · **hip flexed 2 wk** (↓ stretch on muscle + apophysis) → **guarded WB 4 wk**

**Pelvic avulsions (OB)** — protected WB **2–4 wk** → stretching + strengthening → normal activity **\~2 months**

**16-yr soccer, minimally displaced AIIS** — **rest, activity modification + physical therapy**\
✗ Wrong options: corticosteroid injection (impairs healing) · early aggressive strengthening (displacement / delayed union) · MRI · ORIF

### Operative criteria

| Surgery for | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Significant displacement** | **Commonly > 2 cm** |
| **Painful nonunion** | ORIF |
| **Symptomatic heterotopic ossification** | Excision (arthroscopic) |
| High-level athlete | Persistent limitation despite adequate nonop |

**Skeletally mature elite athletes** — complete / retracted proximal rectus femoris avulsion → surgery gives **faster RTP + lower recurrence** · individualise

**Post-traumatic rectus femoris HO** — **arthroscopic excision** → better pain, ROM, function at 2 yr

## 6 Complications

**Complications** — **loss of reduction** · **delayed union**
