# Lateral Patellar Compression Syndrome

*Knee & Sports · 26 / 61*

## 1 Basics

**Cause** — **tight lateral retinaculum** → **excessive lateral tilt without excessive patellar mobility**

**Miserable triad** — **femoral anteversion + genu valgum + external tibial torsion / pronated feet** → ↑ Q-angle, worse PF dysplasia

**Q-angle** — normal **17° in females** (OB) · 21° = increased

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain with **stair climbing** · **theatre sign** (pain sitting for long periods)

**Exam** — **pain on patellar compression**, moderate **lateral facet tenderness**, **cannot evert lateral edge of patella** · stable knee, no effusion

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnosis – patellofemoral pain syndrome

**Merchant / sunrise** — **lateral patellar tilt** (neutral or negative tilt)

![Lateral patellar tilt on Merchant view](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3021-merchants-min-tilt-moved.jpg)

**TT-TG (CT)** — trochlear trough line ⟂ posterior condylar line, superimposed on tubercle slice · **> 20 mm → medialising TTO** needed · 14 mm = normal

**13-yr-old gymnast, normal X-rays, negative tilt, pain on resisted open-chain extension** — **patellofemoral syndrome**\
✗ Wrong options: bipartite patella · distal femoral stress fracture · patella alta · baja

**PF syndrome (OB)** — no clear cause · quadriceps + **dynamic malalignment** contribute · X-rays + exam · MRI only if intra-articular / intra-osseous lesion suspected · **low-impact aerobic exercise**

## 5 Management

```
Lateral patellar compression
├─ Initial → NSAIDs, activity modification, closed-chain quads + VMO rehab (prolonged)
├─ Refractory + objective lateral tilt, no instability → arthroscopic lateral release
└─ Refractory + distal / lateral facet lesions, ↑ Q → realignment osteotomy
```

### Initial management – closed-chain strengthening

**Mainstay** — nonop for an **extensive period** · NSAIDs, activity modification · **VMO strengthening** + **closed-chain short-arc quads**

**First step (Q-angle 21°)** — **closed chain exercises with focus on quadriceps and hamstring strengthening** + isometrics\
✗ Wrong options: lateral release · tubercle elevation + medialisation · strict immobilisation / NWB · **open-chain seated leg extensions** (↑ PFJRF)

### Operative criteria – lateral retinacular release indications

| Lateral release indication | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Objective lateral tilt** | **Neutral or negative tilt** on Merchant |
| Refractory pain | After extensive rehab |
| **Cannot evert** lateral patellar edge | Tight lateral retinaculum |
| **No instability** | Ideal candidate |
| **Medial glide < 1 quadrant** | — |
| **Lateral glide < 3 quadrants** | — |

**9 months, Q 15°, < 1 quadrant medial glide, can't evert, TT-TG 14 mm** — **lateral retinacular release**\
✗ Wrong options: anterolateral TTO (↑ TT-TG) · **Maquet** (tightens lateral retinaculum, worsens negative tilt) · **Elmslie-Trillat** (TT-TG normal) · medial plica resection

**Arthroscopic findings** — superior portal: **medial facet does not articulate with trochlea at 40° flexion**

**Technique** — secure **haemostasis** · after release patella **passively tilts 80°**

**Outcome** — ↑ Lysholm in < 40 and > 40 yr · fibrosis at release site rare

**Complications** — persistent / worse pain · **medial patellar instability**

### Patellar realignment osteotomies

| Procedure | Movement | Indication | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Maquet** | **Anteriorisation** | **Distal pole lesions only** | Elevate **> 1 cm** → **skin necrosis** |
| **Elmslie-Trillat** | **Medialisation** | **Instability with lateral translation** (not isolated tilt) | **Medial facet arthrosis** |
| **Fulkerson** | **Anteromedialisation** | **Lateral + distal pole lesions**, ↑ Q-angle (controversial) | **Superior medial arthrosis** (scope first), **skeletal immaturity** |

## How it's asked

**"Anterior knee pain, Q-angle 21° — first step?"** → **closed chain quadriceps + hamstring strengthening**

**"Refractory, can't evert patella, TT-TG 14 mm?"** → **lateral retinacular release**

**"13-yr-old, normal radiographs, pain on resisted extension?"** → **patellofemoral syndrome**
