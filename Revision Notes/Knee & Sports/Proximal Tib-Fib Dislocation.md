# Proximal Tib-Fib Dislocation

*Knee & Sports · 47 / 61*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **rare**, rarer isolated · **often missed** · 2nd–4th decades

**Anatomy** — fibular head articulates with facet on lateral tibial cortex (**separate from knee joint**) · anterior + posterior ligaments of fibular head · **common peroneal nerve** distal to joint, **posterolateral fibular neck**

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanism** — **high energy** (horseback riding, parachuting) · fall on **flexed, adducted knee** · twisting of flexed knee in athletes (OB)

**Associated** — **posterior hip dislocation** (knee + hip flexed) · **open tibia–fibula fractures** · other knee / ankle fractures

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **lateral knee pain mimicking lateral meniscal tear** · instability · lateral prominence · popping / catching · dorsal foot numbness (peroneal)

**Exam** — fibular head tenderness · **compare both knees** (landmarks)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding / use |
|---|---|
| **AP + lateral of both knees** | **Contralateral comparison views essential** |
| CT | Clearly confirms / excludes dislocation |
| MRI | Normal cruciates + menisci → points away from intra-articular cause |

### Ogden classification

| Type | Fact |
|---|---|
| Subluxation | — |
| **Anterolateral dislocation** | **Most common** |
| Posteromedial dislocation | **Peroneal nerve injury usually with posterior** dislocations |
| Superior dislocation | — |

**4 types (Sekiya & Kuhn)** — subluxation + 3 dislocations · diagnosis frequently missed

## 5 Management

```
Proximal tib-fib dislocation
├─ Acute → closed reduction (knee flexed 80–110°)
│      (failed closed reduction → open reduction)
└─ Chronic pain / instability / missed / malreduced → surgery
       └─ soft-tissue (capsule) reconstruction, ORIF + pinning, arthrodesis, fibular head resection
```

**Closed reduction** — **flex knee 80–110°** (relaxes **LCL + biceps femoris**) → **pressure on fibular head opposite to dislocation** · usually successful

**Post-reduction** — immobilisation in extension vs early ROM → **controversial**

**2 wk after rugby fall, popping + dorsal foot numbness, normal MRI (OB)** — **closed reduction**\
✗ Wrong options: meniscal arthroscopy · ACL reconstruction · OCD arthroscopy · EMG for anterior tarsal tunnel (deep peroneal entrapment)

## 6 Complications

**Recurrence**

**Common peroneal nerve injury** — usually **posterior** dislocations · **> 35%** with concomitant proximal tibia fracture

**Arthritis** — rare, minimally symptomatic

## How it's asked

**"Lateral knee pop, catching, dorsal foot numbness, X-ray dislocation, normal MRI — next?"** → **closed reduction of dislocation**
