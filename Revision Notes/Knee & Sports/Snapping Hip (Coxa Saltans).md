# Snapping Hip (Coxa Saltans)

*Knee & Sports · 55 / 61*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Aka (TO)** — coxa saltans = **dancer's hip** · a **rare** disease (TO)

**Who** — athletes + dancers in **teens / twenties** · **ballet dancers** + athletes needing **repetitive extreme hip flexion + abduction** (TO)

**Common in (TO)** — **dancers** · applies to snapping hip generally **and** internal snapping\
✗ Wrong options: gymnasts · long-distance runners · cyclists

**Painless snapping** — up to **10% of general population** → **normal** (OB)

### Types & causes

| Type | Cause | Snap / pain |
|---|---|---|
| **External** — **most common** (Orthobullets, OB, TO) | **ITB over greater trochanter** · also **TFL or gluteus medius** over GT (OB) | **Lateral** · **visible** |
| **Internal** | **Iliopsoas** over **femoral head**, **iliopectineal ridge / eminence**, **lesser trochanter exostoses**, **iliopsoas bursa** · iliofemoral ligament (OB) | **Anterior** · **audible** click |
| **Intra-articular** | **Loose bodies** (traumatic, **synovial chondromatosis**), **labral tears**, osteochondral fragments (TO) | **Catching, locking**, clicking |

**Extra- vs intra-articular (TO)** — **extra-articular more common** · divided into internal + external · **external more common than internal**

**See vs hear** — external snapping **seen** from across the room · internal snapping **heard** from across the room

**Internal snapping cause (TO)** — **thickened iliopsoas tendon snapping over iliopectineal eminence**\
✗ Wrong options: labral tear · ITB over GT · FAI

**Iliacus (OB)** — may contribute as it overlies **anterior capsule + superior pubic rami**

### Internal snapping (iliopsoas) – anatomy

**Iliopsoas origin + insertion** — **anterior surfaces + lower borders of L1–L5 transverse processes + iliac fossa → lesser trochanter**\
✗ Wrong options: greater trochanter

**Distractor structures (OB)** — anterior → posterior acetabulum = **labrum** (tear → pain on **FADIR**) · **external lip of iliac crest → lateral tibial condyle (Gerdy's tubercle)** = **ITB**\
✗ Wrong options: medial condyle

**Snap mechanics** — iliopsoas tendon **lateral to iliopectineal eminence in full flexion** → **snaps medially** as hip returns to neutral

**Tendon bands (Philippon, OB)** — single **28.3%**, **double 64.2%**, triple 7.5% · **psoas major most medial**, primary iliacus tendon lateral → **more than one tendon may need release**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **snapping** in / around hip · **painful or painless** · patient can **reproduce** it · worse with activity

**Clicking / locking** — suggests **intra-articular** pathology

**Pain (TO)** — tendon inflamed → snapping becomes painful · internal → **anterior** pain + audible click · external → **lateral** pain + click

### External snapping (iliotibial band) – Ober test

**Exam** — palpate **greater trochanter** as hip **actively flexed** · **pressure stops the snapping** → confirms

**Ober test** — **limited hip adduction with hip extended** ✓ · lateral decubitus, **can't adduct across midline**\
✗ Wrong options: adduction in flexion · abduction flexed / extended · bow-legged gait

**Why** — **TFL flexes + abducts** hip → contracture limits **extension + adduction**

**Prone Ober (Gautam)** — stabilises pelvis, holds extension → **quantifies** contracture

![Ober test](../../MCQ/attachments/q2857-illa-ober-test-image.jpg)

### Internal snapping – provocative manoeuvres

| Source | Manoeuvre reproducing internal snap |
|---|---|
| **Orthobullets** | Passive **flexed + ER → extended + IR** |
| **OB (Yen)** | **Flexion-abduction-ER (FABER) → extension, adduction, IR** |
| **OB (Frank, JAAOS 2013)** | **Flexed + IR → extended + ER** |
| OB | Supine, **flex > 90° → extend to neutral**; accentuate with FABER in flexion, adduction + IR while extending |
| OB | **Flexion + extension**, or **flexion + ER + abduction** |
| **TO** | Rotate the **extended + adducted** hip IR / ER · **flex the extended hip** · **extend the flexed hip** |

**Manoeuvre conflict** — Orthobullets / Yen (**ER → IR** on extending) vs Frank (**IR → ER** on extending) — read the options; FABER → neutral is the commonest stem

**Dancer, popping on FABER → neutral** — **internal snapping** · no pain on IR of flexed hip → **labral tear unlikely**

**Released psoas tendon — pre-op finding** — **snapping with active hip flexion** ✓ · **FABER → neutral triggers popping** ✓

| Finding (OB distractor) | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| **Positive Ober** | **External** snapping (ITB / TFL) |
| Pain on IR + ER with hip + knee **extended** · IR only to neutral at 90° flexion, full ER | **Pincer FAI** |
| ↓ IR, limited **FABER → FADIR**, positive impingement (flexion, adduction, IR) | **Cam FAI** |
| ↓ active ROM, **catching, locking, grinding** on passive motion | **Intra-articular loose bodies** |
| **Loose bodies on X-ray** | **Synovial chondromatosis** → loose body removal |
| Labral tear on MRI | **Secondary to FAI** → repair vs debridement |
| **Pain on half sit-up + tender pubic ramus** | **Athletic pubalgia** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **clinical** for external + internal (TO: mainly clinical in extra-articular) · **advanced imaging** usually needed for **intra-articular**

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding / use |
|---|---|
| X-ray — AP pelvis / hip | **Usually normal** · **rule out synovial chondromatosis** · occasional **calcification near lesser trochanter** (OB) |
| **Dynamic ultrasound** | **Best to confirm internal snapping** (shows tendon in motion) · shows snapping band (internal or external) · **guides diagnostic injection** (trochanteric bursa / iliopsoas sheath / joint) |
| **MRI** (often **arthrogram**) | **Rule out intra-articular** pathology + mimics · inflamed bursa · dynamic USG / MRI help **intra-articular** diagnosis (TO) |
| **Iliopsoas bursography** | Contrast into bursa under **fluoroscopy** → tendon seen · ± therapeutic injection · **radiation to pelvis** |

**Confirm internal snapping (OB ×2)** — **ultrasound**\
✗ Wrong options: conventional MRI · CT arthrogram · weight-bearing X-ray · fluoroscopy without contrast · bone scan · CT · plain radiographs

**US anatomy (OB)** — **hyperechoic oval iliopsoas tendon** anterior to **superior pubic ramus**, posterior to **hypoechoic iliacus**

## 5 Management

```
Snapping hip
├─ Painless → no treatment
├─ Painful, acute (< 6 months) → activity modification
├─ Persistent, painful, limits ADLs → PT stretching + corticosteroid injection
└─ Failed nonop → surgery by type
       ├─ External → GT bursa excision + ITB Z-plasty
       ├─ Internal → iliopsoas tendon release / lengthening
       └─ Intra-articular (MRI loose body / labral tear) → hip arthroscopy
```

**Nonop (TO)** — **NSAIDs, ice, rest**, ultrasonic + interferential therapy, **stretching of iliopsoas + ITB** → **effective in majority** · **local anaesthetic + corticosteroid** into bursa / tendon sheath

**Resistant (TO)** — **surgical release + lengthening of iliopsoas tendon and ITB**

**Intra-articular (TO)** — **arthroscopic loose body removal + labral repair = treatment of choice**

### Operative criteria

| Indication | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **Painful external snapping**, failed nonop | **GT bursa excision + ITB Z-plasty** |
| **Snapping after THR** | **GT bursa excision + ITB Z-plasty** |
| **Painful internal snapping**, failed nonop | **Iliopsoas tendon release** |
| **Intra-articular**, failed nonop + **MRI loose bodies / labral tear** | **Hip arthroscopy** — loose body removal, labral debridement / repair |

### External snapping (iliotibial band) – Z-plasty

**Mainstay** — **stretching** (positive Ober = tight ITB) · often **PT alone** · occasional steroid injection for **profound GT bursitis**

**Runner pattern (OB)** — long symptom duration · **downward leg** (left leg running on left side of road)

**Most reliable surgery** — **Z-plasty of ITB** near **proximal origin** · may be **endoscopic**\
✗ Wrong options: **release at Gerdy's tubercle — not described** · iliopsoas release · labral debridement · FAI debridement

**Results (OB)** — Z-plasty good in 10 patients (**Faraj**) · release good in 16 patients / 17 hips (**White**)

![ITB Z-plasty lengthening](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3096-z-plasty.jpg)

### Internal snapping (iliopsoas) – release

**Nonop success (Gruen, OB)** — **63%** of 30 improve nonop · rest do well with **open lengthening**

**Open approaches** — anterior, medial, ilioinguinal, iliofemoral · **partial or complete** release · traditionally open → now **arthroscopic**

**Arthroscopic routes** — **endoscopic at lesser trochanter** · **transcapsular** at the hip joint: from **central compartment** (Orthobullets) · from **peripheral compartment** (OB) — read the options

**Ilizaliturri RCT (OB)** — **lesser trochanter (10) vs transcapsular (9)** → both ↑ WOMAC, **no difference** · both effective + reproducible

**Regeneration (Marquez Arabia, OB)** — 27 patients · tendon **regenerates in all** by 23 months (**84%** of original circumference) · **all 5/5 hip flexion** · muscle fibres attach directly to proximal femoral shaft → no retraction

**Post-op** — **no hip flexion strengthening for 6 wk**

## X vs Y

| | **External** | **Internal** | **Intra-articular** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | **ITB over GT** | **Iliopsoas over iliopectineal eminence / femoral head** | **Loose body, labral tear** |
| Frequency | **Most common** | Less | Least (extra > intra) |
| Site | **Lateral**, visible | **Anterior / groin**, audible | Deep, catching / locking |
| Key test | **Ober**, palpate GT during active flexion | **FABER → neutral (extension)** | FADIR (labrum), locking |
| Best imaging | Clinical | **Dynamic ultrasound** | **MRI arthrogram** |
| Surgery | **ITB Z-plasty** + GT bursa excision | **Iliopsoas release** | **Arthroscopy** |
