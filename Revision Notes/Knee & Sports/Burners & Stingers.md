# Burners & Stingers

*Knee & Sports · 7 / 61*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & epidemiology

**Definition** — **transient brachial plexus (or cervical root) neurapraxia** = "**dead arm syndrome**" · **mild traction injury of the brachial plexus (TO)** · reversible peripheral nerve injury

**Commonest** — **most common nerve injury in sportspersons (TO)** · most common UE neurologic injury in athletes (OB)

**Sports** — collision: **football**, rugby, ice hockey, wrestling (TO) · under-reported

**Recurrence** — **1 stinger → 3× risk of another** · **stinger in previous season = strongest risk factor** for injury + lasting symptoms (rugby: 33.9% had a history)

**Stenosis** — **Torg ratio < 0.8 → 3× stinger risk** (47.5% of stinger group vs 25.1%) · cervical canal + **foraminal stenosis** ↑ risk → work up recurrent burners

### Anatomy — C5-6 upper trunk

**Commonest level** — **C5-6 (upper trunk)** — greater midcervical mobility in young athletes\
✗ Wrong options: C3-4, C4-5, C6-7, C7-T1

| Root | Muscles tested |
|---|---|
| **C5** | **Deltoid, biceps**, rotator cuff (external rotators) |
| **C6** | Biceps (**elbow flexion + supination**), wrist extensors |
| ✗ Wrong option: C7 | Triceps, wrist flexors |
| ✗ Wrong option: C8 / C8-T1 | Finger flexors / interossei |

**Dorsal root ganglion** — fills most of the neural foramen → takes the brunt → **purely sensory** stinger possible

## 2 Mechanism

| Mechanism | How | Typical |
|---|---|---|
| **Traction** (commonest) | **Arm / shoulder depressed + neck bent AWAY** from injured side · TO: shoulder + head distracted opposite ways | **Younger** athletes — **plexus** |
| **Compression** | **Head turned / extended TOWARD affected side** → root pinched in neural foramen | **Older** athletes — **foraminal stenosis** |
| **Direct blow** | **Erb's point**, just superior to clavicle | Plexus |

![Traction mechanism — shoulder depression with neck flexed away](../../MCQ/attachments/q215512-illa-stinger-mech.jpg)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **unilateral** burning / stinging pain + paresthesia, shoulder → hand · **not dermatomal / not isolated to one area** ✓

**Weakness** — **C5-6: deltoid, biceps**, external rotators · often > 1 shoulder muscle group (ER, flexors, abductors) (TO)

**Never bilateral (TO)** — **bilateral or lower-limb symptoms → spinal cord injury (cervical cord neurapraxia)** until proven otherwise

**Neck exam** — **full, painless cervical ROM**, no tenderness · neck ROM **doesn't** reproduce symptoms · **positive Spurling's possible** ✓ · ROM often ↓ in chronic cases (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: neck pain on extension

**Course** — resolves quickly, **doesn't progress** · range: paresthesia → **complete arm monoplegia** · seconds → weeks if severe\
✗ Wrong options: progresses 20–25 min

| Duration of symptoms | Source |
|---|---|
| **1–2 min** | Orthobullets, OB |
| Seconds to several minutes | OB |
| **15–20 min** | OB |
| Several minutes; **5–10% last up to several weeks** | TO |
| "Resolution in **6–8 weeks**" accepted as **true** | TO (EXCEPT stem) — read the options |

**Subtle deltoid weakness** — mistaken for shoulder injury · **4/5 deltoid → withhold**\
✗ Wrong options: greater tuberosity tenderness = cuff contusion · AC pain · O'Brien's · labral shear (play on if strength full)

### Differentials

| Condition | Distinguishing fact |
|---|---|
| **Stinger** | Unilateral UE, C5-6, resolves in minutes, no residual |
| Acute transient spinal cord injury | **Temporary complete paralysis UE + LE**, resolves **< 24 h** |
| Central cord syndrome | **UE > LE**, mostly **elderly** |
| Nerve root avulsion | **Permanent** deficit, poor prognosis · **neurotmesis = most severe "stinger"** (arm-tackle traction) |
| Guillain-Barré | **Ascending** paralysis, legs → arms → face, **loss of DTRs** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Test | Indication / finding |
|---|---|
| **C-spine radiographs** | Usually normal · **recurrent stingers** → rule out **fracture + cervical stenosis** |
| **MRI C-spine** | **Bilateral symptoms** (not a stinger) → HNP, stenosis |
| **EMG** | **Persistent > 3 wk** (Orthobullets, OB) · **delay 3–4 wk** after injury (OB) · abnormal roots, cords, trunks, peripheral nerves |

**Formal work-up** — neurologic findings present, **persistent symptoms**, prior brachial plexus injury · = EMG + radiographs + MRI

**Not MRI triggers** — unilateral symptoms > 15 min · two prior unilateral episodes (→ **radiographs**) · transient deltoid + biceps weakness · **brief LOC → CT head**, not C-spine MRI

## 5 Management

```
Arm numbness / tingling after contact
├─ Bilateral or lower-limb signs → cord injury → MRI before any RTP
├─ Unilateral, fully resolved, full strength + painless neck ROM → same-day RTP
├─ Recurrent stingers → out for game → C-spine radiographs / imaging
└─ Weakness persists → withhold → EMG / NCS at 3–4 wk
```

### Return to play & red flags (bilateral or recurrent symptoms)

| RTP criteria | Contraindications to RTP |
|---|---|
| **Complete resolution of symptoms** | **Bilateral symptoms** (until MRI) |
| **Normal (5/5) strength**, normal sensation | **Multiple stingers** (until off-field work-up) |
| **Full, painless ROM of neck + shoulder** | **Recurring symptoms** (until C-spine X-rays) |
| Normal cervical exam — compression, **Spurling's, Adson's**, resisted head pressure | **Persistent** symptoms / weakness |

**Not RTP criteria** — fitting a brace · MRI excluding avulsion · normal EMG · partial sensation + arm overhead

| Number of stingers | RTP recommendation |
|---|---|
| **First** | RTP after **pain-free full ROM + normal strength** |
| **Second** | **Withhold rest of game** → re-examine → next game if no deficit |
| **Third** (any duration) | **Withhold rest of game + advanced C-spine imaging** (foraminal / uncovertebral stenosis, disc herniation) |
| Any, **persistent pain / weakness** | **Withhold** + **electrodiagnostic study** |

**Same-contest return** — **allowed after complete resolution** (Orthobullets, OB) · TO counts "**precludes participation in present contest**" as true — read the options

**3rd stinger (2 today + 1 last week), resolved in 5 min** — **withhold from the remainder of the game + pursue further off-field workup**\
✗ Wrong options: RTP after halftime · RTP once asymptomatic · 2 wk + EMG (only for persistent weakness)

**Biceps weakness > 10 min + 2 stingers last season** — **hold until off-field work-up incl. C-spine radiographs**\
✗ Wrong options: agility test · "benign" immediate RTP · graduated RTP protocol (not a concussion)

**Prevention** — **prevent recurrence** = key · neck collars / rolls (football) · avoid spear tackling · fitted equipment · recurrent stingers → possible **chronic proximal arm weakness + pain**

## X vs Y

| | **Stinger / burner** | **Cervical cord neurapraxia (transient quadriparesis)** |
|---|---|---|
| Lesion | **Root or brachial plexus** (peripheral) | **Spinal cord** |
| Limbs | **Unilateral upper limb** | **Bilateral**, > 1 limb ± legs |
| Mechanism | Traction, compression, Erb's point blow | Hyperextension, hyperflexion, **axial load** |
| Resolution | Minutes | **10 min – 48 h** (OB) · minutes – hours (Orthobullets) |
| Imaging | Radiographs if recurrent | **MRI** before RTP |
| Same-day RTP | **Yes** if fully resolved | **No** |

| | **Younger athlete** | **Older athlete** |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | **Traction** of plexus (midcervical mobility) | **Foraminal stenosis + extension → root compression** |

## How it's asked

**"All true about stingers EXCEPT (TO)?"** → **bilateral arm weakness + paresthesia**

**"Acute transient finding consistent with a stinger?"** → **unilateral upper-extremity pain + weakness**

**"Muscles most likely affected?"** → **deltoid + biceps**

**"Shoulder + finger numbness gone in 15 min, no residual?"** → **stinger / burner**\
✗ Wrong options: transient cord injury · central cord · root avulsion · GBS

**"Most commonly involved level?"** → **C5-6**

**"Finding consistent with a stinger?"** → **physical examination may show a positive Spurling's sign**

**"Where were the paresthesias (figure)?"** → **not isolated to any single area**

**"Indication for C-spine MRI before RTP?"** → **symptoms + signs in both upper extremities**

**"Condition before RTP after a stinger?"** → **no pain, normal sensation, full ROM, 5/5 strength**

**"Exam finding that keeps him out?"** → **full painless ROM but 4/5 deltoid strength**

**"Third stinger, resolved in minutes — next step?"** → **withhold from remainder of game, pursue further off-field workup**

**"Persistent biceps weakness + 2 prior stingers?"** → **hold until off-field work-up incl. C-spine radiographs**

**"Risk of another stinger after one?"** → **3×**
