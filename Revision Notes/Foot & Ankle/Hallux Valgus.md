# Hallux Valgus

*Foot & Ankle · 24 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Prevalence** — **\~23%** of 18–65 y · up to **30% of females** · **women up to 15:1** · **most common toe deformity** (TO)

**Forms** — **adult** vs **juvenile / adolescent** (see 5 Management)

**Shoe-wearing societies** — almost exclusively with constricting footwear (OB) · still occurs in **barefoot populations** → footwear **contributory, not sole cause** (TO)

### Genes & lab markers

**Family history** — **70%** (Orthobullets) · **60–70%** (TO) — **strongest risk factor**, **autosomal dominant, incomplete penetrance** (TO)

**COL5A1 mutation** — **Ehlers-Danlos** → hallux valgus → **MTP arthrodesis** (OB)

**Connective tissue** — **Ehlers-Danlos, Marfan** (ligamentous laxity) (TO)

### Risk factors

| Type | Factors |
|---|---|
| **Intrinsic (TO)** | **Genetic** (strongest) · **rounded (convex) 1st MT head** (flat / chevron-shaped head resists subluxation) · **metatarsus primus varus** (↑ IMA, **primary skeletal driver**) · **1st metatarsal-cuneiform hypermobility** · **pes planus** · **ligamentous laxity** · **long 1st MT (Egyptian foot)** |
| **Intrinsic (Orthobullets)** | **↑ DMAA** · 1st TMT instability · **2nd toe deformity / amputation** · RA · CP · metatarsus adductus |
| **Extrinsic** | **Narrow toe box + high heel** = most significant environmental factor (TO) |
| **Systemic (TO)** | **RA** (synovitis destroys medial capsule + collaterals) · **CP, poliomyelitis** (muscle imbalance) · **EDS, Marfan** (laxity) |
| **Not a risk factor** | **Pes cavus** (TO) · flexible cavovarus (OB) |
| **Miscellaneous (TO)** | **Obesity** · **plantar fasciitis** · degenerative arthritis · **2nd toe amputation** · **space-occupying lesion in 1st web space** · **gastrocnemius tightness** (Silfverskiöld) (TO) |

**Pes planus** — associated with **progression** · elevation + functional lengthening of 1st MT\
✗ Wrong options: cross-over toe · tarsal coalition · hammer toe · 1st MT supination

**Progression predictors** — **DMAA** + **AP talo–1st MT angle** at presentation (OB)

### Pathoanatomy & deforming forces (pronation, adductor, abductor hallucis, conjoined tendon)

**Cascade** — **valgus phalanx → varus 1st MT** → MT head drifts **medially** → sesamoid complex **laterally translated relative to MT head**

**Sesamoids** — sit in the two heads of **FHB**; attached to proximal phalanx base by sesamoid-phalangeal ligament / plantar plate

**Sesamoids don't move (TO)** — anchored to 2nd MT by **deep transverse intermetatarsal ligament** → **MT head migrates medially off them** = apparent "sesamoid subluxation" (TO) · Orthobullets: sesamoids displace **lateral** to MT head — read the options

**Medial** — capsule + **MCL stretched, attenuated** (TO) · early lesion = failure of medial sesamoid ligaments + MCL (OB)

**Lateral contracture** — lateral capsule + **adductor hallucis** + **lateral head FHB** → lateral **bowstring** (TO)

**Adductor hallucis** — **primary deforming force** · inserts **fibular (lateral) sesamoid + lateral base proximal phalanx**\
✗ Wrong options: abductor hallucis · EHB · FHB (more plantar, contributes less) · FHL (not part of sesamoid complex, not primary)

**Abductor hallucis** — inserts with **medial FHB tendon** into medial base proximal phalanx · migrates **plantar + lateral** → **flexion and pronation**\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ abduction · extension · pronation alone

**Conjoined tendon** — **FHB + adductor hallucis** at lateral base proximal phalanx · **do not release** → **iatrogenic hallux varus** · release adductor (transverse + oblique heads) **adjacent to lateral sesamoid** instead

**EHL + FHL** — bowstring **lateral to MTP axis** → dynamic lateral deviators, perpetuate deformity (TO) · Orthobullets: FHL + adductor = plantar-plate muscles that become deforming forces

**Plantar plate muscles (Orthobullets)** — abductor hallucis · FHB · **FHL (deforming)** · **adductor hallucis (deforming)**

**Hallux position** — **valgus + pronation** · nail faces **laterally** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: valgus + supination · varus

**1st MT rotation (TO)** — severe HV = **pronation (internal rotation) of the first metatarsal** → sesamoid displacement · **87%** pronated on CT (OB)\
✗ Wrong options: supination / external rotation · inversion of 1st ray · pure sagittal dorsiflexion

**Triplanar deformity (OB)** — valgus + **dorsiflexion** + **pronation** of 1st ray → offloads 1st MT head → load to lesser MTs

**Bunion (TO)** — **medial eminence** = normally covered medial MT head now uncovered · **not an exostosis** · **adventitious bursa** → painful bursitis · osteophytes later

**No muscle inserts on 1st MT head (TO)** — head vulnerable to deforming forces (narrow shoes) · progression: sesamoids sublux · **crista eroded** · flexors + extensors shift laterally → **pronation** · abductor hallucis loses mechanical advantage (TO)

**Sesamoid direction (TO lecture)** — "sesamoids sublux **medially**" (TO — verify: usual teaching = sesamoids lie **lateral** to the MT head, which drifts medially) — read the options

**Metatarsus primus varus (TO)** — set by **orientation of the MC joint** · **medial inclination up to 8° = normal** (TO)

**Windlass mechanism** — less effective → **transfer metatarsalgia**

**HV interphalangeus (TO)** — laterally angled distal articular surface of proximal phalanx · persists after MTP correction unless addressed

**2nd toe (TO)** — **crossover toe** · **2nd MTP instability** (plantar plate attenuation, dorsal subluxation) · hammer / claw · transfer metatarsalgia · lesser-toe crowding

**Associated** — hammer toe · callosities · pes planus · Marfan · EDS · metatarsus adductus

## 3 Presentation

**History** — slowly progressive deformity · pain with ambulation · **difficulty with shoe wear** (medial eminence)

**Commonest complaint** — **pain over the bunion** (TO) · bursitis, callus over medial eminence

**Lateral overload (TO)** — **transfer metatarsalgia**, **stress fractures**, **callosities of lateral MTs** · long-standing → **MTP OA** (TO)

**Digital nerve compression** — symptoms possible

**Exam** — hallux **valgus + pronated** · 1st MTP ROM, crepitus, **grind** · **1st TMT hypermobility** · callus · sesamoid pain · pes planus, lesser toes, midfoot / hindfoot

**Flexible vs fixed (TO)** — flexible = toe passively pulled to normal → nonop · **fixed** = cannot → operative (TO)

**Ligamentous laxity** — Beighton (thumb to forearm, knee hyperextension) + excessive passive PF / DF of 1st TMT → **Lapidus**

**Transfer metatarsalgia sign** — **plantar focal tenderness and keratosis under the second metatarsal head**, **improves with metatarsal pad**\
✗ Wrong options: Mulder click → Morton neuroma · insertional Achilles · fixed equinus with negative Silfverskiöld · fixed hindfoot varus on Coleman block (cavus is not a HV risk)

**DDx** — gout · **hallux rigidus** (stiff 1st MTP; gout, pseudogout, OA; **mostly bilateral**) (TO) · RA · turf toe · HV interphalangeus

### Bunionette (tailor's bunion)

**Site (TO)** — **lateral side of 5th metatarsal**\
✗ Wrong options: medial 1st MT · lateral 1st MTP · medial 5th MTP

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **weight-bearing AP, lateral, oblique** · non-weight-bearing films **underestimate** deformity (TO) · **axial sesamoid (skyline) view** — sesamoid position (TO)

| Measurement | Definition | Normal | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HVA** | 1st MT axis vs proximal phalanx axis | **< 15°** | MTP deformity; **> 15° diagnostic** (TO) |
| **IMA** | 1st vs 2nd MT axes | **< 9°** (normal **9°**, TO) | MT deformity (**metatarsus primus varus**) → decides distal vs proximal |
| **DMAA** | 1st MT axis vs line through base of distal articular cap | **< 15°** (Orthobullets) · **< 10°** (OB) · **< 6°** (TO) — read the options | **MTP congruence** (TO) |
| **HVI** | proximal vs distal phalanx | **< 10°** | **> 10° → Akin** |
| **Metatarsus adductus angle** | 2nd MT vs lesser tarsus axis (4th / 5th MT-cuboid reference) | **< 10°** | Predisposes; Sgarlato, Engel, Kilmartin methods |

![HVA, IMA and DMAA](../../MCQ/attachments/q215722-illa-hva-ima-and-dmaa-angles-illustrated.jpg)

**Measure of congruence** — **Distal Metatarsal Articular Angle (DMAA)** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: talar–1st MT angle · IMA · HVA

**Round sign (TO)** — rounded lateral MT head on AP = **pronation of the first metatarsal** (TO) · **squared / flat lateral border = normal rotation** (OB) · positive round sign → **recurrence risk** (OB)\
✗ Wrong options: subchondral cyst · sesamoid obliteration · AVN

**Sesamoid position (TO)** — **Hardy & Clapham grades 1–7** or tibial sesamoid position · position 1 = fully covered · 7 = completely lateral · **≥ position 4 significant**

**1st MC joint instability (TO)** — **joint-line obliquity**, **divergence of 1st / 2nd MT bases** → Lapidus · plantar-flexion **sag** at 1st TMT (OB)

**MTP arthritis** — narrowing, **dorsal osteophytes** (hallux rigidus component), sclerosis, cysts → arthrodesis (TO)

### Diagnostic criteria

| Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **Diagnosis** | **HVA > 15°** (TO) |
| **Surgery (symptomatic)** | **IMA > 10° + HVA > 20°** (Orthobullets, OB) |
| **Severe (TO)** | **HVA > 40° · IMA > 16° · HVI > 10°** · **DMAA up to 6° acceptable** (TO) |
| **Significant sesamoid subluxation** | **≥ position 4** (TO) |

### Congruent vs incongruent joint

| | **Congruent** | **Incongruent (subluxated)** |
|---|---|---|
| Joint surfaces | **Parallel**, matched; hallux deviates but no subluxation | Proximal phalanx **subluxed laterally** on MT head |
| Cause | **Bony** (↑ **DMAA**, articular tilt) | **Soft-tissue imbalance** (tight lateral, lax medial) |
| DMAA | **↑** (congruent HV needs ↑ DMAA) | Usually **low** |
| Treatment | **Osteotomy correcting DMAA** (biplanar chevron, Akin, double osteotomy) | **Lateral release + medial imbrication** (modified McBride) ± osteotomy |
| Trap | **Lateral release in congruent joint → creates incongruency → OA** (TO) | Akin **contraindicated** |

| Grade | TO | Orthobullets | OB explanations |
|---|---|---|---|

| **Moderate** | HVA **20–40°** · IMA **13–16°** (IMA grade **11–16°**) | HVA **30–40°** · IMA **13–20°** · proximal if HVA **> 25°**, IMA **> 13°** | **IMA > 13°** → proximal |
| **Severe** | HVA **> 40°** · IMA **> 16°** | HVA **> 40°** · IMA **> 20°** · double osteotomy HVA **41–50°**, IMA **16–20°** | HVA **> 40–41°** · IMA **> 15–16°** (**> 20°**, Robinson) |

**Read the options** — **IMA 13°** = the commonest distal vs proximal divide · HVA cut-offs vary most

## 5 Management

```
Symptomatic HV, failed shoe modification
├─ MTP arthritis / RA / gout / neuromuscular / EDS → 1st MTP ARTHRODESIS
├─ 1st TMT hypermobility or TMT arthritis → LAPIDUS + modified McBride
├─ IMA < 13° (mild) → DISTAL CHEVRON
│      └─ DMAA > 10° → BIPLANAR CHEVRON
└─ IMA > 13° (moderate–severe) → PROXIMAL / SCARF + modified McBride
       └─ + DMAA ↑ (congruent) → DOUBLE OSTEOTOMY
```

```
Joint congruence decides the HVA correction (TO)
├─ Incongruent (soft tissue) → LATERAL RELEASE + MEDIAL IMBRICATION
│      └─ IMA still ↑ → ADD BASAL (PROXIMAL) OSTEOTOMY
├─ Congruent (bony) → CORRECTIVE OSTEOTOMY (Akin / Chevron)
└─ Low-demand elderly, degenerate → ARTHRODESIS or KELLER
```

```
Add-ons to any correction
├─ HVI > 10° → AKIN
├─ Elderly / very low demand → KELLER
└─ Skin breakdown only → SIMPLE BUNIONECTOMY (medial eminence)
```

### Nonoperative (TO)

**Role (TO)** — **first-line for all** · definitive for mild symptoms, unfit, early deformity · **does not correct deformity** — relieves symptoms, slows progression

**Footwear (TO)** — **single most important** · **wide toe box**, adequate depth · **low heel < 2.5 cm (1 inch)** · soft uppers · custom / extra-depth for severe

**Pads + spacers (TO)** — bunion pads (silicone, felt, gel) · interdigital spacers · toe wedges / spacers worn **2–3 years** (TO)

**Orthoses** — help with **pes planus or metatarsalgia** (Orthobullets, TO) · **Morton's extension** limits MTP motion if hallux rigidus component (TO) · don't correct HV

**Night splints (TO)** — temporary relief only · **no evidence** they alter natural history

**Anti-inflammatory (TO)** — NSAIDs, ice · **steroid into bursa** short-term · repeated → **skin atrophy + capsule weakening**

**Physio (TO)** — intrinsic strengthening (towel curls, marble picks) · **gastrocnemius-soleus stretching** (tight → ↑ forefoot load)

**Surgery indication** — persistent symptoms despite shoe modification · **never for cosmesis alone** · choice by HVA, IMA, DMAA, congruency, arthritis, MC stability, age / demand (TO) · **> 150 procedures** described (TO) · > 130 (OB)\
✗ Wrong options: asymptomatic contralateral bunion

### General principles of correction (TO)

**Lateral release (TO)** — essential **except congruent deformity** · dorsal 1st web space or medial transarticular · **adductor from lateral sesamoid** + lateral capsulotomy + **lateral metatarsosesamoid ligament**

**Medial eminence resection (TO)** — **flush with medial shaft** · **over-resection → hallux varus**

**Medial capsulorrhaphy (TO)** — plication of attenuated medial capsule

**Sesamoid reduction (TO)** — sesamoids back under head in **crista** · persistent subluxation = inadequate lateral release or MT correction

### Lapidus (first TMT arthrodesis) – hypermobility indications & modified McBride

**Indications** — **1st TMT hypermobility** (laxity) · **1st tarsometatarsal arthritis** · severe HV (HVA **41–50°**, IMA **16–20°**, OB · HVA **> 40°**, IMA **> 15°**, OB · IMA **> 16°**, TO · IMA **> 20°**, Robinson) · metatarsus primus varus · recurrence with TMT pain · **concomitant pes planus** · adolescent bunion (physis closed)

**Not indications** — IMA **12°** · HVA **40°** alone · **age > 50** · **flexible cavovarus** · **MTP** (not TMT) hypermobility · MTP arthritis (→ MTP fusion) · spastic CP (→ MTP fusion)

**Contraindications (OB)** — **open physis** · already **shortened 1st MT**

**Typical answers** — **HVA 37, IMA 18 + TMT joint hypermobility in young patient** · **HVA 42, IMA 24 + TMT hypermobility** · laxity + hypermobile TMT + flexible planus → **tarsometatarsal joint arthrodesis with modified McBride** · large IMA + TMT laxity → **first tarsometatarsal (TMT) joint arthrodesis**\
✗ Wrong options: MTP fusion · **1st + 2nd TMT** (2nd not fused) · distal / proximal osteotomy (don't fix hypermobility)

**Technique** — 2 incisions or extended medial · correct with **adduction, supination, neutral plantar flexion** · screws ± plate · resect cartilage, **keep subchondral plate** (↓ shortening) · Lapidus 1934 (TO) · original = 1st–2nd MT bases + intercuneiform

**Better outcome** — **multiple screw fixation** · **bone grafting** · **correction of IMA** · failed conservative care · **anatomic plantarflexion of 1st MT**\
✗ Wrong options: **dorsiflexion unloading of the first metatarsal** → lesser MT overload

**Lapidus alone** — corrects valgus + dorsiflexion but **fails to correct pronation** → add **modified McBride** (derotates / supinates, \~10°) = "modified Lapidus"

**Modified McBride components** — **release of the adductor from the lateral sesamoid** · **lateral** capsulotomy · **medial** capsular imbrication\
✗ Wrong options: medial capsulotomy · lateral imbrication · resect base of proximal phalanx (Keller)

**Nonunion** — **\~10%** (Orthobullets, with MTP fusion) · **up to 12%** (OB) · **3–10%** (TO) · **4%** (OB) — read the options · \~33% symptomatic · NWB **6–8 wk** historically (TO)

### Juvenile hallux valgus – DMAA & double osteotomy

| | **Juvenile / adolescent** | **Adult** |
|---|---|---|
| Hallmark | **Metatarsus primus varus** (↑ IMA) | Soft-tissue + bony |
| DMAA | **↑**, **congruent** joint | Variable |
| Laterality | **Bilateral, familial** | — |
| Pain | Not the primary complaint | Primary |
| HVA | **Smaller** than adults | Larger |
| Bursa / medial eminence | **Less** prominent | Prominent |
| MTP joint | **Flexible** (↑ deformity) | not rigid in juvenile |
| Foot | **Flexible flatfoot** | — |
| Commonest complication | **Recurrence > 50%** | Recurrence 10–47% |
| **TO features** | **Hypermobile 1st MC joint** · **higher IMA, lower HVA** · **less pronation** · **bursal thickening uncommon** · **open physis** (TO) | — |

**Timing** — nonop **until physis closes** · surgery for symptoms with **IMA > 10° + HVA > 20°**

**Open physis** — **no proximal MT osteotomy** · severe → **first cuneiform (opening-wedge) osteotomy** · soft tissue alone fails

**Epidemiology (TO)** — HV 20–25% of people · **20–30% begin in childhood** · **guided growth (\~7–8 yr)**: lateral hemi-epiphysiodesis of 1st MT (screw/drill)

**Closed physis** — as adult, but congruent ↑ DMAA needs **second (distal) osteotomy** → **double first metatarsal osteotomy** (HVA **> 25°** moderate / **> 40°** severe + **DMAA > 15°**)

**Cases** — HVA 36, IMA 16, DMAA 21, physis closed → **double first metatarsal osteotomy** (✗ Wrong options: Ludloff + DSTP · cuneiform · MTP fusion · biplanar chevron alone) · ↑ IMA + congruent ↑ DMAA → **proximal and distal first metatarsal osteotomy** (✗ Wrong options: phalanx osteotomy · DSTP) · **HVA 32 + DMAA 25** → double (✗ Wrong options: HVA 32 + DMAA 12 · skeletal immaturity · neuromuscular · TMT hypermobility)

**Results (OB)** — double osteotomy corrects HVA \~22–24°, IMA \~8–9° · **4–5×** chevron correction · no recurrences

**Commonest complication (TO)** — **recurrent hallux valgus**\
✗ Wrong options: hallux varus · MTP OA · AVN · physeal arrest

**Causes of recurrence (TO)** — inadequate lateral release · insufficient medial plication · **missed metatarsus adductus** · failure to recognise **congruency** · **TMT hypermobility**

### Neuromuscular / connective tissue disorders – MTP arthrodesis

**Rule** — **CP, Down's, EDS (COL5A1), RA, gout, severe DJD**, neuromuscular → **MTP arthrodesis** (high recurrence after osteotomy) · even for mild–moderate deformity

(✗ Wrong options: **Traps** — Akin) · **Evans** (calcaneal lengthening for planovalgus) · modified McBride · **Keller** (elderly, cock-up toe)

**CP recurrence after distal osteotomy + McBride** — cause **cerebral palsy** → **1st metatarsophalangeal joint arthrodesis** · CP adolescents: no recurrence, 75% excellent (OB)\
✗ Wrong options: revision distal osteotomy · proximal osteotomy · "increased HVA"

### 1st MTP arthrodesis & salvage

| Position | Valgus | Dorsiflexion | Rotation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orthobullets | **5–10°** | **5–10°** | **Neutral** | flat platform simulates WB |
| OB | **5–10°** | **5–15° relative to floor** | **Neutral** | — |
| TO | **10–15°** | **10–15° relative to 1st MT** | — | — |

**Read the options** — DF reference differs (floor vs 1st MT)

**Technique** — avoid **EHL + dorsomedial cutaneous nerve** · dorsal plate + compression screw / crossed lag screws (TO)

**Gold standard (TO)** — HV + **MTP arthritis (HV with hallux rigidus)** · **failed prior surgery** · neuromuscular (CP, polio) · rheumatoid · very severe in elderly · also subluxed / dislocated MTP, forefoot inflammatory disease (OB)

**Outcomes** — **\~90% satisfaction** · eliminates pain, prevents recurrence · **no high heels** (TO) · superior to **metallic hemiarthroplasty** (24% hemi failed, OB)

**Keller (TO)** — shortened, **floppy, weak hallux** · loss of push-off · transfer metatarsalgia · replaced by MTP fusion · very elderly sedentary only

**MTP arthroplasty (TO)** — silicone (**Swanson**), hemi, total · wear, loosening, **osteolysis** (silicone) · only if motion wanted and fusion declined

### Recurrence – sesamoid reduction & IMA

**Risk factors** — **↑ preop IMA** + **↑ preop HVA** · **rounded lateral 1st MT head** (round sign = pronation) · **residual increased tibial sesamoid displacement** (lateral) · DMAA ↑ missed (> 10°) · **inadequate IMA correction** · **no lateral release / adductor not released** · **no medial capsule closure** · **Akin alone** for moderate–severe · juvenile · noncompliance with WB

**Sesamoids** — **unreduced sesamoids → 10× odds** of recurrence (OB) · reduction under head **prevents recurrence of the hallux valgus deformity**\
✗ Wrong options: extensor strength · sagittal correction · supination · nonunion

**Unrelated to recurrence** — **resection of the lateral sesamoid** → **hallux varus**, not recurrence (EXCEPT answer) · post-op IMA **15°** → recurrence\
✗ Wrong options: varus

**Revision target** — **adductor hallucis** (label D) left unreleased \
✗ Wrong options: FHL · abductor hallucis (pronator, not driver) · FHB bellies · remove lateral sesamoid (varus)

**Bilateral case** — right (squared head, tibial sesamoid reduced) recurred vs left (rounded, sesamoid overlapping lateral border) → **increased preoperative 1-2 intermetatarsal angle on the right compared to the left**\
✗ Wrong options: head rotation · sesamoid alignment (those favour left recurrence) · medial eminence resection → varus

**Persistent after isolated distal osteotomy** — **pronation of the first metatarsal** · rotation best corrected by **fusion / proximal osteotomy** (OB)\
✗ Wrong options: IMA · HVA · DMAA (distal osteotomy corrects these) · HVI 9° (normal)

**Scarf** — double-chevron diaphyseal osteotomy · **more stable** than others

### Iatrogenic hallux varus – causes (fibular sesamoid excision, over-release, medial eminence)

**Causes** — **fibular (lateral) sesamoid resection** = **commonest** · **excessive medial capsular reefing** (+ adductor + lateral FHB detached) = **second commonest** · **over-resection of medial eminence** · overcorrection of IMA (< 9° normal) · **excessive lateral release** / **conjoined tendon release** · medial tibial sesamoid subluxation · excessive postoperative bandaging · MTP OA · AVN collapse into varus · malunion of distal osteotomy · original McBride

**Mechanism** — fibular sesamoidectomy releases lateral FHB slip → **unopposed medial FHB + abductor hallucis** → medial rotation + translation · usually **with supination**\
✗ Wrong options: under-corrected pronation

**Not a cause** — **resection of the tibial sesamoid** (never resected in HV surgery; EXCEPT) · use of chevron / Akin per se · MTP arthritis (rarely varus) · complete medial sesamoidectomy · complete abductor release · **excessive lateral capsulorrhaphy** (it is **medial** capsulorrhaphy)

**Medial eminence cut** — saw blade placed too **lateral** (cut A) → **over-resection** → **hallux varus**\
✗ Wrong options: recurrence · cock-up (FHL / Keller) · transfer metatarsalgia (shortening) · AVN

**"Toe too straight"** — **complete excision of medial eminence**

```
Iatrogenic hallux varus
├─ Early, flexible → TAPING into valgus
├─ Flexible, no arthritis, failed taping → MEDIAL CAPSULAR LENGTHENING + SPLIT EHL TRANSFER
│      (or reverse scarf / chevron / Akin, EHB transfer)
└─ Rigid or MTP arthritis → DEFORMITY CORRECTION + 1st MTP FUSION
```

**Flexible painless varus** — **medial capsular lengthening and split extensor hallucis longus tendon transfer**\
✗ Wrong options: MTP fusion (salvage) · lateral sesamoid excision (worsens) · lateral closing wedge phalanx osteotomy (misses MTP) · soft-tissue release alone

**Varus + MTP arthritis, can't fit shoes** — **deformity correction and first metatarsophalangeal (MTP) fusion** · standard fusion **without structural graft**\
✗ Wrong options: cheilectomy · Keller · Moberg · Lapidus

### Nerve injury – dorsomedial cutaneous nerve

**Nerve** — **dorsomedial (medial dorsal) cutaneous nerve of the hallux** (DMCN) = **terminal branch of the superficial peroneal nerve** · sensation **dorsal + medial half of hallux** · lies directly over **medial eminence** · commonest nerve injured (\~3%)

**Injury** — **dorsal / dorsomedial approach** · medial capsular imbrication / MT osteotomy → numbness or **neuritic pain medial hallux**, painful neuroma against shoe · prefer **direct medial / mid-medial** incision · transverse vein **superficial + proximal** to nerve (OB)

**Course** — mostly **neuropraxia → resolves** over months · recalcitrant → **resect DMCN + bury in proximal 1st MT or medial cuneiform** (OB)

| ✗ Distractor nerve | Territory per explanations |
|---|---|
| **Deep peroneal** (medial branch) | **1st web space**, lateral hallux; extensors |
| **Dorsolateral cutaneous** | "Branch of deep peroneal" (OB) |
| **Saphenous** | Dorsomedial hindfoot · medial ankle · medial midfoot (OB, three wordings) |
| **Medial plantar / plantar hallucal** | Plantar medial foot (tibial) |
| **Intermediate dorsal cutaneous** | Mid-dorsum, doesn't reach medial hallux |
| **Sural** | Lateral foot + ankle |
| **Tibial** | Posterior to medial malleolus → medial + lateral plantar |

### Complications – osteonecrosis, dorsiflexion malunion, shortening & transfer metatarsalgia

**AVN — classic link** — **distal Chevron osteotomy** (✗ Wrong options: proximal osteotomies) · collapse → **osteonecrosis of the metatarsal head and MTP arthrodesis** (✗ Wrong options: PTOA dx · intra-articular osteotomy · varus / recurrence)

**AVN — blood supply conflict** — **medial capsulotomy = primary insult**; distal osteotomy + lateral release **do not ↑ risk** (Orthobullets, OB) · **laterally based head supply** → chevron + **extensive lateral release** ↑ risk (OB) — read the options

**AVN + shortening + lesser MT overload** — **metatarsophalangeal joint fusion with an autologous bone block** (restores length) · AVN with bone loss = only complication needing **bone block arthrodesis** (OB)\
✗ Wrong options: revision Lapidus · Keller (worsens overload) · Moberg · allograft MT head (experimental)

**Dorsiflexion malunion** — **first metatarsal proximal crescentic osteotomy** · inherently unstable · **smooth pins / single screw** · **plate** stronger (OB) · presents: **loss of MTP dorsiflexion**, **transfer metatarsalgia** ("marble" under 2nd–3rd MT heads)\
✗ Wrong options: AVN (distal) · cock-up · varus · neuroma

**Dorsiflexion malunion treatment** — **plantar flexion metatarsal osteotomy** · plantarflexion malunion → revision osteotomy ± MTP fusion\
✗ Wrong options: capsular release (bony block) · biplanar / double (DMAA) · phalanx osteotomy (HVI)

**Transfer metatarsalgia — causes** — **1st metatarsal shortening** (aggressive resection in MTP fusion, **modified Lapidus procedure** done incorrectly, chevron) · dorsiflexion malunion · residual pronation · **functional load transfer from altered forefoot biomechanics** after modified Lapidus · MTP fusion in **plantarflexion** (5° PF relative to floor) · **long 2nd MT** · equinus

**✗ Transfer metatarsalgia traps** — Cotton osteotomy (plantarflexion medial cuneiform for forefoot varus) · Akin · lateral sesamoidectomy (→ varus) · modified McBride (no shortening) · Morton's neuroma (Mulder click, paraesthesia) · **Freiberg** (2nd MT head AVN, visible on X-ray) · 1st MT nonunion · recurrence · pseudoarthrosis · TMT instability · cock-up toe

**Prevention** — **minimal bone resection** at 1st MT base · avoid shortening · fuse MTP in correct position

**First MT** — bears **half the forefoot weight** (OB)

### Weil osteotomy technique

**Indication** — 2nd MTP metatarsalgia / long 2nd MT (e.g. after MTP fusion) → **second metatarsal osteotomy (Weil) with extensor tendon and dorsal capsular release**\
✗ Wrong options: 2nd MTP fusion · Girdlestone-Taylor · **Helal** (shaft, ↑ nonunion) · head resection

**Weil** — **intra-articular** MT head osteotomy · longitudinal decompression by **shortening**

**Saw cut** — **parallel with the plantar surface of the foot** → minimises **plantar displacement** of capital fragment · remove wafer / thicker blade also ↓ plantar shift\
✗ Wrong options: perpendicular to shaft · parallel to MT inclination · 45° · 10°

### Sesamoid excision – FHB preservation

**Medial (tibial) sesamoidectomy** — preserve / repair **flexor hallucis brevis tendon** → prevents **hallux valgus**\
✗ Wrong options: FHL · abductor · adductor · EHB

**Complications of medial sesamoidectomy** — stiffness · claw toe · **hallux valgus** · (lateral sesamoidectomy → **hallux varus**)

### Prognosis

**Recovery** — return to work **6–12 wk** · improvement up to **1 yr** · patient-reported outcome not tied to degree of correction (OB)
