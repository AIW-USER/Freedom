# Hallux Varus

*Foot & Ankle · 25 / 48*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **medial deviation of the hallux relative to 1st metatarsal** · most often **iatrogenic overcorrection after bunion surgery**

**Incidence** — **2–14% after hallux valgus surgery** · **more common in women**

**Three components** — **medial deviation of hallux** at 1st MTP · **supination of phalanx** · **claw-toe deformity**

### Causes – excessive lateral release & over-correction

| Cause | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Congenital** | **Metatarsal physeal bracket** |
| **Iatrogenic (commonest)** | Overcorrection at bunion surgery |
| Trauma | — |
| Inflammatory | **RA**, **ankylosing spondylitis** |
| Neurological | **CMT**, **post-polio** |

**Iatrogenic pathoanatomy** — **excessive lateral soft-tissue / capsular release** · **fibular (lateral) sesamoid excision** · **excessive medial eminence resection** · **overplication of medial capsule** · **overcorrection of IMA** (e.g. **excessively large proximal medial opening-wedge osteotomy**) or of hallux valgus interphalangeus · loss of osseous support · malaligned osteotomy, soft-tissue laxity

**Medial release** — listed as a contributor (OB7610) · not **excessive medial capsular release** (OB220893 — lateral release + medial overplication cause it) — read the options

**Post-op dressing (TO)** — **overcorrection in post-op dressing** is a cause · also over-plication of medial capsule, excess medial eminence resection, tibial sesamoid displaced medially, **overpull of abductor hallucis** (TO)

**Not causes** — **tibial sesamoidectomy** · **inadequate** medial eminence resection · **1st TMT hypermobility** · **inadequate IMA correction** · failed pin fixation · choosing soft-tissue procedure over fusion

**After fibular sesamoid resection (TO)** — **unopposed pull of abductor hallucis** → **medial dislocation of tibial sesamoid** → medial hallux translation · also **MTP joint hyperextension** + **buttonholing of metatarsal head** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: **adductor hallucis**

## 3 Presentation

**Complaint** — **appearance** (toe "too straight" → excessive medial deviation) · **difficulty wearing shoes** · often **asymptomatic**

**Pain** — suggests **joint arthritis** or trauma · ↓ ROM, instability, weak push-off

**Exam** — varus angulation · **dorsal contracture of MTP** ± IP contracture · **EHL displaced medially → "bowstring"** · **tibial sesamoid displaced medially** · medial blister · **fixed vs flexible (passively correctable)** decides treatment

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| View / finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| **WB AP + lateral foot** | Standard · + NWB oblique + **sesamoid axial** |
| **Hallux valgus angle** | **< 0°** (normal **5–15°**) · e.g. HVA −19° |
| **IMA** | **Reduced** (overcorrected; e.g. 6°) |
| Medial eminence | **Excessively resected** |
| Sesamoids | **Tibial sesamoid subluxed medially** · **lateral (fibular) sesamoid absent** |
| Osteotomy | Overcorrected |
| Joints | **Degenerative change at MTP / IP** → fusion |
| **CT / MRI** | Rarely needed · **osteonecrosis of 1st metatarsal** |

## 5 Management

```
Hallux varus
├─ Early post-op (first weeks) → taping / splinting up to 3 months
├─ Flexible, asymptomatic / mild → wide toe box, padding
├─ Flexible (passively correctable), young → tendon transfer + medial release
│      └─ bony overcorrection → lateral closing-wedge osteotomy
└─ Fixed, arthritic, painful, or elderly → 1st MTP arthrodesis
```

### Treatment – reducible vs fixed deformity (tendon transfer vs MTP fusion)

**Taping / splinting** — **early post-op varus**; may correct if started **within first weeks**; continue **up to 3 months** / until soft tissues heal · not for fixed deformity

**Shoe modification** — **wider, flexible toe box** + padding · **flexible, longstanding, asymptomatic** · not for chronic fixed + arthritic · established varus → nonop role limited

**Lateral closing-wedge osteotomy** — **overcorrected proximal / distal MT or proximal phalangeal osteotomy** · ± scar release + lateral ligament repair · not when MTP degenerative

| Tendon procedure (flexible deformity) | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Adductor hallucis reattachment + medial release** | **Difficult after McBride** |
| **Abductor hallucis transfer → lateral base of proximal phalanx** | + reattach / reef **conjoined tendon** in web space |
| **EHL or EHB transfer + medial release ± IP fusion** | Under **transverse intermetatarsal ligament** → **distal MT neck** (lateral → medial) · **split EHL** + MT osteotomy + medial capsule release for **young, passively correctable** |

### Operative criteria

| 1st MTP arthrodesis | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Absolute** | **Fixed (not passively correctable) MTP** with significant deformity + non-functioning hallux · **painful MTP arthritis** |
| **Relative** | **Medial eminence resected beyond tibial sesamoid sulcus** |
| **Age** | **Elderly** — **72-yo, moderately reducible, painful** → **fusion** (soft tissue not indicated for age) |

**Fusion gives** — **most predictable pain relief, alignment, activity, shoe fit, no recurrence**

| ✗ Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|
| **MTP replacement / hemiarthroplasty** | Unpredictable; **radiolucency, subsidence**, high failure (hallux rigidus) |
| **Keller resection arthroplasty** | Unpredictable pain relief, activity, recurrence |
| **Interposition arthroplasty** | **No realignment** |
| **Lateral MT opening-wedge osteotomy** | Doesn't correct **arthritic, incongruent MTP** |
| **1st MT–cuneiform (Lapidus) fusion** | **Salvage for failed hallux valgus**, not varus |
| **Cheilectomy + medial release** / soft tissue alone | Won't correct fixed deformity |
| **Great-toe amputation** | Unreasonable in healthy patient |

## X vs Y

| | **Flexible (passively correctable)** | **Fixed / arthritic** |
|---|---|---|
| Patient | **Young** | **Elderly** or degenerative MTP |
| Surgery | **Tendon transfer + medial release** ± osteotomy | **1st MTP arthrodesis** |
| Splint / tape | Useful early | No |

## How it's asked

**"Hallux varus after fibular sesamoid resection — all EXCEPT?"** → **unopposed pull of adductor hallucis** (it's the abductor) (TO)

**"Progressive varus 2 yr after bunion surgery — factor?"** → **excessive lateral soft-tissue release**

**"HVA −19°, IMA 6° after bunionectomy — error?"** → **excessively large proximal metatarsal medial opening-wedge osteotomy**

**"Rigid varus, pain mid-range, 70-yo?"** → **hallux metatarsophalangeal joint fusion**

**"Not completely reducible, failed taping — predictable alignment + pain relief?"** → **MTP joint arthrodesis**

**"72-yo, moderately reducible, painful — most predictable?"** → **first MTP fusion**

**"Fixed varus + DJD after fibular sesamoidectomy?"** → **first MTP arthrodesis**
