# Ankle Ligaments

*Foot & Ankle · 8 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Osteology & biomechanics

**Ankle joint** — tibial plafond + medial malleolus + lateral malleolus + talus · main motion **plantar / dorsiflexion** · secondary inversion / eversion, rotation

**Distal tibiofibular joint** — distal fibula in **incisura fibularis** (concave distal lateral tibia) · fibula rotates in incisura during gait · **mortise widens from plantar → dorsiflexion** · **syndesmosis screws limit external rotation**

**Joint reaction force** — **5× body weight** walking on level ground

### Ligament overview

| Group | Ligaments |
|---|---|
| **Medial** | **Deltoid** · **calcaneonavicular (spring)** |
| **Lateral** | **Syndesmosis** (AITFL, PITFL, TTFL, IOL, ITL) · **ATFL** · **PTFL** · **CFL** · **LTCL** |

### Lateral ligaments

| | **ATFL** | **CFL** | **PTFL** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Function | **Primary restraint to inversion in plantar flexion** · resists **anterolateral talar translation** | **Primary restraint to inversion in neutral / dorsiflexion** · restrains subtalar inversion → limits talar tilt | Supplementary when ATFL + CFL intact · max strain in **dorsiflexion** · limits **posterior talar displacement + ER** |
| Strength | **Weakest** lateral | — | **Strongest** lateral |
| Origin | Anteroinferior fibula, **10 mm proximal to tip** | Anterior border fibula, **9 mm proximal to tip** | Posterior border fibula |
| Insertion | **Talar neck**, just distal to cartilage (**18 mm distal to joint line**) | Calcaneus **13 mm distal to subtalar joint**, **deep to peroneal sheaths** | **Posterolateral tubercle of talus** |
| Orientation | **45–90°** to tibial axis (Orthobullets) · **perpendicular in neutral → parallel in plantar flexion** (OB) | **Parallel to tibia in neutral** | **Perpendicular** to tibial axis |
| Injured by | **Inversion in plantar flexion** → commonest, **low ankle sprain** | **Inversion with foot in neutral** | **Complete talar dislocation** / severe sprains with other two torn |
| Exam | **Anterior drawer in 20° plantar flexion** (Orthobullets; **10° PF** = greatest translation, OB) vs other side · **> 8 mm forward shift** on lateral X-ray = ATFL tear | **Inversion (supination) test** in slight dorsiflexion · **talar tilt** (ATFL + CFL) | No specific test |

**PTFL if ATFL + CFL incompetent** — short fibres restrict IR + ER, talar tilt, dorsiflexion · long fibres only ER, talar tilt, dorsiflexion

**LTCL** — **lateral process of talus → lateral calcaneus** · **anterior + medial to CFL** · stabilises talocalcaneal joint · injured **with ATFL** · no specific test

### Syndesmosis

**Function** — **integrity of ankle mortise**

**Components** — **AITFL** · **PITFL** (deep part = **inferior transverse ligament**) · **TTFL** · **interosseous ligament (IOL)**

**Clinical** — **high ankle sprain / syndesmosis injury**, ankle fracture · AITFL + PITFL injured by **eversion + external rotation**

**Tests** — **external rotation test** · **squeeze test**

### Deltoid ligament

**Function** — **primary restraint to valgus talar tilt** · both layers resist hindfoot eversion · stabilises against **plantar flexion, ER, pronation**

| | **Superficial** | **Deep** |
|---|---|---|
| Crosses | **Ankle + subtalar** | **Ankle only** |
| Origin | **Anterior colliculus** | **Inferior + posterior medial malleolus** |
| Insertion | Navicular, talar neck, **sustentaculum tali**, posteromedial talar tubercle | Medial + posteromedial talus |
| Key | **Tibiocalcaneal (sustentacular) = strongest superficial** · **deep posterior tibiotalar = strongest overall** (TO), resists calcaneal eversion | **Primary medial stabiliser** · prevents **lateral displacement + ER of talus** |
| Test | **Eversion test** in neutral | **External rotation stress test** (+ syndesmosis) |

**Injury** — **pronation (eversion)** → forced ER + abduction · ± medial malleolus fracture · ankle fracture, high ankle sprain

### Spring ligament anatomy & flatfoot

**Spring (calcaneonavicular) ligament** — connects **calcaneus and navicular** · **sustentaculum tali → inferior navicular**\
✗ Wrong options: talus–navicular · calcaneus–cuboid · talus–calcaneus

**Components** — **superomedial** + **inferior** calcaneonavicular · **superomedial = largest + most important stabiliser of medial longitudinal arch**\
✗ Wrong options: inferior

**Superomedial attachments** — **anteromedial sustentaculum tali → broadly along medial navicular**\
✗ Wrong options: to medial cuneiform · from talus

**Function** — **static** stabiliser of **medial longitudinal arch + talar head** · arch = dynamic **PTT** + static ligaments / capsule + tarsal interlock · closely related to **deltoid**

**Untreated tear** — **flatfoot deformity**\
✗ Wrong options: syndesmotic widening · TMT widening · hallux valgus · talar tilt

**PTTD** — spring ligament **most likely attenuated in type II flatfoot** · significant disruption → **reconstruction**

**Acute spring tear** — forceful landing on flat foot · **tibialis posterior usually normal**

## 2 Mechanism

### Inversion injury – ATFL

| Mechanism | Injury |
|---|---|
| **Inversion in ~20° plantar flexion** | **ATFL** (commonest) |
| Inversion in neutral | **CFL** |
| Severe inversion (others torn) | PTFL |
| **Eversion + external rotation** | **AITFL / PITFL (syndesmosis)** |
| **Eversion** | **Anterior colliculus fracture** (deltoid avulsion) |

### Eversion injury – anterior colliculus

**Eversion** — **anterior colliculus fracture** = bony **deltoid** avulsion from medial malleolus

**✗ Wrong options (all inversion-associated)** — **osteochondral talar dome lesions** · **peroneal tendon splits** · **anterolateral soft-tissue (meniscoid) impingement** · **SPN stretch** (paraesthesia) · ATFL + CFL tears

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| **AP + mortise** | **Tibiofibular clear space < 5 mm** · **tibiofibular overlap > 10 mm (AP)** (Orthobullets) · clear space **< 6 mm**, overlap **> 6 mm AP / > 1 mm mortise** (OB) — read the options |
| **Weight-bearing mortise** | **Most accurate radiograph** for syndesmosis |
| **CT** | **Most accurate** syndesmosis assessment; no validated normals → **compare uninjured side** |
| **Anterior drawer stress X-ray** | **> 8 mm** = ATFL tear · stress views more accurate in **chronic** injury |
| **Talar tilt X-ray** | Plafond–talar dome angle **< 5° normal** · combined **ATFL + CFL** |
| **Ankle arthrogram** | **CFL rupture → dye leaks into peroneal tendon sheath** |
| **US** | ATFL — **more accurate than radiographs** |
| **MRI** | ATFL, CFL, spring ligament tear · PTFL rarely indicated |
| **Mortise / gravity stress** | **Medial clear space widening** → deltoid injury |
| Arthroscopy | Confirms ATFL tear |

![Tibiofibular clear space < 5 mm and overlap > 10 mm](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7005-tibfib-overlap.jpg)

### Syndesmotic ligament avulsion fragments (AITFL)

**Tillaux fracture** — **AITFL (anterior tibiofibular ligament)** avulses **anterolateral distal tibial epiphysis** · transitional fracture, **anterolateral physis closes last**\
✗ Wrong options: ATFL · CFL · EDB · EHB

**Fibular avulsion into syndesmotic space (CT)** — **AITFL** insertion → **syndesmosis injury** · repair fragment ± **syndesmotic screw / suture button** in most\
✗ Wrong options: excise fragment (incompetent syndesmosis) · Broström / allograft (ATFL / CFL) · deltoid (medial) · SPR (lateral fibular wall avulsion, peroneal subluxation)

## 5 Management

### Partial tear – physical therapy

**Isolated partial ATFL tear (grade I)** — stable · **RICE, early protected WB → physical therapy** (peroneal strengthening + proprioception) · brace for sport

**✗ ATFL partial-tear traps** — **cast + NWB 2–4 / 6–8 wk** (WB recommended) · **early open repair** (only for fracture / intra-articular lesion) · **early arthroscopic repair** (scope only for AITFL impingement / posteromedial impingement)

### Acute ankle sprain – taping (TO)

| Taping | Use (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Closed basket weave** | **Acute ankle injury, maximum support** ✓ (TO) · anchors (below calf + behind MT heads) → **stirrups medial → lateral** + **horseshoes behind heel** woven → **C-strips** → **heel locks** |
| **Open basket weave** | Acute sprain with **massive swelling** (TO) · **no overlap in front** → allows swelling + movement · heel locks with **elastic** tape · compression, ↓ inflammation |
| **Low dye** | **Zinc oxide** tape for **midfoot, plantar fascia, heel pain** (TO) · **not for ankle sprain** |
| Medial patellar | Knee (not ankle) |

## X vs Y

| | **ATFL** | **CFL** |
|---|---|---|
| Restrains inversion in | **Plantar flexion** | **Neutral / dorsiflexion** |
| Test | **Anterior drawer** (20° PF) | **Inversion test**, talar tilt |
| Orientation in neutral | Perpendicular (OB) | Parallel to tibia |

| | **Superficial deltoid** | **Deep deltoid** |
|---|---|---|
| Joints crossed | Ankle + subtalar | Ankle only |
| Test | Eversion | External rotation stress |

| | **Superomedial CN** | **Inferior CN** |
|---|---|---|
| Size | **Largest** | Smaller |
| Arch support | **Most important** | Less |

## How it's asked

**"Inversion with foot plantar flexed 20° — ligament?"** → **anterior talofibular**

**"Eversion mechanism associated with?"** → **anterior colliculus fracture**\
✗ Wrong options: peroneal splits · OCL · SPN palsy · anterolateral impingement

**"Isolated partial ATFL tear — treatment?"** → **physical therapy**\
✗ Wrong options: cast NWB · open / arthroscopic repair

**"Tillaux fragment — attached structure?"** → **anterior tibiofibular ligament**

**"Fibular avulsion into syndesmosis on CT — structure?"** → **anterior inferior tibiofibular ligament (AITFL)**

**"Spring ligament connects?"** → **calcaneus and navicular**

**"Spring ligament complex — true?"** → **superomedial ligament is the most important stabilizer of the longitudinal arch**

**"Untreated spring ligament tear on MRI leads to?"** → **flatfoot deformity**

**"Taping method in runner with acute ankle injury?"** → **closed basket weave taping** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: open (massive swelling) · low dye (plantar fascia)

**"Most accurate radiograph for syndesmosis?"** → **weight-bearing mortise view**
