# MTP Dislocations

*Foot & Ankle · 34 / 48*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **multiplanar instability of a lesser MTP joint** → **crossover toe** (dorsomedial deviation of 2nd toe)

**Commonest joint** — **2nd MTP**

### Plantar plate anatomy

**Structure** — broad, thick ligamentous plate on plantar MTP joint

**Origin** — **metatarsal head** via thin synovial attachment, just proximal to articular surface (**weakest point = origin at metatarsal neck**)

**Insertion** — **plantar base of proximal phalanx** (ruptures here and retracts proximally)

**Function** — **resists sagittal tensile load, esp. MTP dorsiflexion** · cushions joint, supports weight-bearing

![Plantar plate of a lesser MTP joint](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7053-plantar-plate.jpg)

## 2 Mechanism

### Pathoanatomic stages

| Stage | Event | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Plantar plate disrupted** — **chronic inflammation (commoner)** or traumatic rupture | Dorsal laxity |
| **2** | **Lateral collateral ligament fails** (OB: **LCL fails first**, plantar plate second — read the options) | **Medial deviation of 2nd toe**; plantar plate + flexors displace medially |
| **3** | **Medial structures contract** — lumbrical, interosseous, MCL, medial capsule | Adduction force |
| **4** | **Plantar plate fails completely** | **Hyperextension → dorsal instability / dislocation** |

**Trauma** — **hyperextension injury** of toe (push-off, spin) → plantar plate tear

**Chronic** — **repeated MTP dorsiflexion** attenuates plantar aponeurosis, plantar plate, capsule

**Accelerated by** — **cortisone injection** for misdiagnosed **Morton's neuroma** or MTP synovitis

**Once plate fails** — intrinsic + extrinsic forces dorsiflex the proximal phalanx · main deforming force = **EDL**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain at **toe-off / push-off** · **"walking on a marble / pebble / rock in shoe"** · better in padded shoes than barefoot

**Point tenderness** — **plantar aspect of 2nd MTP**; pain on hyperextension

**Calluses** — **plantar under MT head** (fat pad migrates **dorsally**, uncovering head) + **dorsal over PIP** (abuts toe box)

**Deformity** — **dorsomedial deviation (crossover toe)** · **hammertoe**: MTP extension + PIP flexion · crossover develops when **collateral ligament(s)** also injured

**Early instability** — before deformity, **mimics Morton's neuroma**

![Crossover second toe](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7053-crossover-toe.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Plantar plate injury & instability – drawer test

**MTP drawer / modified Lachman** — grip proximal phalanx (neutral) + MT head → **dorsally directed force** → **↑ sagittal (dorsal–plantar) translation / dorsal subluxation** · **> 50% subluxation** = marked instability

![MTP drawer test](../../MCQ/attachments/q2874-illa-mtp-drawer-test.jpg)

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| **WB AP, oblique, lateral** | AP: proximal phalanx dislocated — **medial > lateral** · lateral: **hyperextension + dorsal dislocation** of proximal phalanx |
| X-ray early | Often **normal** in plantar plate tear |
| **MRI** | **Early MTP synovitis / plantar plate tear**; rules out other pathology · not useful once dislocated |

### Clinical grading of plantar plate rupture (OB)

| Grade | Injury | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Sprain** | **Rest, ice, NSAIDs, stiff-soled shoe, MT pad** |
| **II** | **Partial tear** | **Nonop first** (± cast) |
| **III** | **Complete tear** | Repair if nonop fails |

### Surgical grading of plantar plate tears

| Grade | Tear pattern |
|---|---|
| **0** | Plate / capsular **attenuation** ± discolouration |
| **1** | **Transverse distal tear < 50%** (next to phalangeal insertion) ± midsubstance < 50% |
| **2** | **Transverse distal tear > 50%** ± midsubstance < 50% |
| **3** | **Extensive transverse ± longitudinal tear** (may involve collaterals) |
| **4** | **Extensive tear with button-hole (dislocation)**; little plate left to repair |

### Differentials by exam

| Finding | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| **↑ sagittal translation, + drawer / Lachman, MTP hyperextension pain** | **Plantar plate tear** ✓ |
| **Squeeze test + / palpable click (Mulder)**, numbness, **3rd web space** | **Morton's neuroma** |
| Point tenderness **2nd MT shaft** | **2nd MT stress fracture** |
| **Flexion contracture + dorsal ulceration** | **Hammer toe** |
| **AVN, flattening / collapse of 2nd MT head** from repetitive microfractures | **Freiberg's infraction** |
| Intrinsic weakness | **Claw toe** |
| TMT pain, X-ray arthritis | Midfoot arthritis |

## 5 Management

```
Lesser MTP instability / plantar plate tear
├─ Sprain, partial tear, first presentation → nonop (does not correct deformity)
├─ Failed nonop, painful instability → plantar plate repair + Weil osteotomy
└─ Severe subluxation / dislocation, fixed → Weil shortening osteotomy + soft-tissue balancing
       └─ Release + tendon lengthening fail → add MT shortening osteotomy
```

**Nonop (first line)** — **taping**, **shoe modification / extra-depth shoes**, **metatarsal pads**, well-padded liners, **Budin splint**, **stiff-soled shoe**, NSAIDs, Achilles stretching, ± cast · **won't correct deformity**

**Crossover toe 1 yr after hallux valgus, 1 month symptoms, > 50% drawer** — **orthotics with MT pads + shoe modification + taping + NSAIDs**\
✗ Wrong options: steroid injection (attenuates plate/capsule further) · revision HV correction · Weil + plate repair before nonop fails · MTP fusion

**Grade II plantar plate tear in athlete** — **rest, NSAIDs, stiff-soled shoe**\
✗ Wrong options: direct repair · repair + osteotomy · reconstruction (plate usually repaired) · arthrodesis

**Plantar plate repair indications** — failed nonop · (hallux) **sesamoid retraction, loose fragments** · lesser toe repair is done **with Weil osteotomy** (protects repair) · dorsal or plantar approach, both good

### Operative criteria

| Situation | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **Significant pain + loss of function, fixed deformity** | **Weil (distal oblique shortening) osteotomy** |
| Mild–moderate deformity | **Capsular release, collateral tightening, tendon lengthening, flexor transfer** |
| **Severe subluxation / dislocation, persists after release** | **MT shortening osteotomy** (decompresses joint, lengthens soft tissues) |
| Incompetent plantar plate | **Plantar plate repair** (sutures distal plate → drill holes in proximal phalanx) + osteotomy |
| Incompetent lateral structures | **EDB transfer under transverse intermetatarsal ligament** (dynamic stabiliser) |
| Dynamic flexion support | **FDL → EDL transfer (Girdlestone-Taylor)** — split FDL over proximal phalanx |
| Salvage | **DuVries metatarsal head resection arthroplasty** |

### Metatarsal shortening osteotomy (Weil/Helal) & floating toe

**Weil** — **intra-articular capital (metatarsal neck / head) osteotomy** · cut **parallel to plantar foot** · **longitudinal decompression by shortening** → joint reduces · **screw perpendicular to osteotomy** · with dorsal + medial capsular release, lateral reefing

**Helal** — **more proximal (shaft)** osteotomy · **no fixation**

| | **Weil** | **Helal** |
|---|---|---|
| Level | **MT neck / head (distal)** | **Shaft (proximal)** |
| Fixation | **Screw** | **None** |
| Satisfaction, AOFAS | **Higher** | Lower |
| Recurrent metatarsalgia, transfer lesions | **Fewer** | More |
| MTP reduction maintained | **Better** | Worse |
| **Malunion / pseudoarthrosis** | **Lower** | **Higher** |

**Weil vs Helal EXCEPT** — Weil does **NOT ↑ malunion / pseudoarthrosis**

**✗ Next-step traps (subluxation after release)** — **flexor tendon resection** (floppy toe, no dorsal correction) · **proximal phalangeal osteotomy** (distal to problem) · **lesser MTP arthrodesis** (poorly tolerated) · **distraction osteogenesis of MT** (lengthens → more instability)

## 6 Complications

**Floating toe** — **commonest complication of Weil osteotomy** · toe elevated in sagittal plane, **doesn't touch floor** standing · inability to flex MTP → **2nd toe dorsiflexion deformity** · from surgery, injury, or shortened MT\
✗ Wrong options: dorsal release + pin · silicone implant · excisional arthroplasty · Helal

**Toe vascular compromise** — correcting **chronic dislocation** stretches contracted vessels → **may need to reverse the procedure** to save the digit

## X vs Y

| | **Plantar plate rupture** | **Freiberg's infraction** | **Morton's neuroma** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pathology | Ligament failure → **dorsal instability** | **AVN of MT head subchondral bone** (repetitive microfracture) | Interdigital nerve |
| Exam | **+ drawer / Lachman**, ↑ sagittal translation | MT head pain | **Mulder click**, squeeze, numbness |
| Site | **2nd MTP** | **2nd MT head** | **3rd web space** |
| X-ray | Normal early | **Flattening / collapse** | Normal |

## How it's asked

**"Golfer, 2nd MTP hyperextension, calluses dorsal PIP + plantar head — all true EXCEPT?"** → **repetitive microfractures compromising MT subchondral blood supply** (that's Freiberg)

**"Plantar callus mechanism?"** → **dorsal displacement of fatty cushion under MT head**

**"MRI 2nd MTP plantar plate tear — exam finding?"** → **↑ sagittal translation (+ drawer)**\
✗ Wrong options: hyperflexion · squeeze pain · shaft tenderness

**"Grade II plantar plate tear, footballer — next step?"** → **rest, NSAIDs, stiff-soled shoe**

**"Rock in shoe, + modified Lachman, normal X-ray — diagnosis?"** → **plantar plate rupture**\
✗ Wrong options: Freiberg · Morton's · claw toe

**"Joints still subluxate after extensor lengthening + capsular release?"** → **metatarsal shortening osteotomy**

**"Weil vs Helal — all true EXCEPT?"** → **↑ malunion / pseudoarthrosis**

**"Floating toe — commonest complication of?"** → **Weil (metatarsal neck) osteotomy**

**"Crossover toe after HV surgery, 1 month symptoms?"** → **MT pads, shoe modification, taping, NSAIDs**\
✗ Wrong options: steroid injection

**"Commonest complication of MTP dislocation correction?"** → **floating toe**

**"Pale toe after correcting chronic dislocation?"** → **vascular stretch → reverse correction**
