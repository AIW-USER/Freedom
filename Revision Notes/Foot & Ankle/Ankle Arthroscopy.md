# Ankle Arthroscopy

*Foot & Ankle · 7 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Indications

**Indications** — **osteochondral lesions of talus** (microfracture of OCD) · post-traumatic synovitis debridement · **ATFL / AITFL anterolateral impingement** · **anterior tibiotalar spur resection (anterior bony impingement)** · **os trigonum excision** · loose bodies · cartilage debridement with ankle fusion

### Set-up

**Position** — **supine**, leg over padded bolster · tourniquet + exsanguinate

**Distension** — **external traction device** distracts tibiotalar joint (↓ cartilage injury, ↑ visualisation) · **20 ml normal saline** injected via marked AM portal site (TO)

**Entry** — **nick and spread** (skin nick, mosquito spreads subcutaneous tissue) → ↓ neurovascular injury

**Pre-tourniquet marking (TO)** — dorsalis pedis, DPN, tibialis anterior, great saphenous vein, **peroneus tertius**, malleoli, joint line

**Workhorse portals (TO)** — **anterolateral, anteromedial, posterolateral**

## 5 Management

### Portals – location & structures at risk

| Portal | Location | Role | At risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anteromedial (AM)** | **Medial to tibialis anterior**, lateral to medial malleolus, at joint line · between TA + saphenous vein | **Primary viewing portal, established first** · 2.7 mm 30° scope (TO) | **Saphenous nerve + great saphenous vein** (mean safe distance **7.4 mm / 9 mm**) · **tibialis anterior tendon** |
| **Anterolateral (AL)** | **Just lateral to peroneus tertius** (**\~4 mm lateral**, OB) + SPN, medial to lateral malleolus · made under vision with 25G needle | Viewing / **working portal** (TO) · lateral gutter | **SPN — dorsal intermediate cutaneous branch** (**commonest**) |
| **Anterocentral** | **Medial to EDL, lateral to EHL** · through extensor tendon if needed (TO) | Rarely used | **Dorsalis pedis artery + DPN** (anterior tibial NV bundle) |
| **Medial midline** | **Between EHL and tibialis anterior** | — | **Deep + superficial peroneal nn.** (well lateral to saphenous) |
| **Posterolateral (PL)** | **Just lateral to Achilles**, medial to peroneals · **2 cm proximal to LM tip** (Orthobullets) · **at level of line from LM tip parallel to sole** (TO) · prone / semi-decubitus | Posterior viewing (os trigonum) · **vision portal in hindfoot arthroscopy**, 4.0 mm 30° scope (TO) | **Sural nerve + short (small) saphenous vein** |
| **Posteromedial (PM)** | **Just medial to Achilles** | Posterior working (os trigonum) | **Posterior tibial artery** (+ tibial nerve, OB) |
| Trans-Achilles | — | **Abandoned** (TO) | Achilles injury / rupture |

### Anterior portals – superficial peroneal nerve injury

**SPN** — **most commonly injured nerve** in ankle arthroscopy · **anterolateral portal** · variable anatomy · branch of **common peroneal**, runs in **lateral compartment** → **motor to peroneus longus + brevis** · crosses fibula (lateral → anterior compartment) **\~11 cm (3–15 cm) proximal to LM tip** (OB) · fascial exit **10–12 cm** (OB) · **12–15 cm** (Orthobullets) — read the options · terminal **medial dorsal cutaneous (great toe)** + **intermediate dorsal cutaneous (dorsum of foot)**

**Protecting SPN** — **palpate**, **transilluminate** (shine scope light to see nerve under skin), blunt dissection, trace before incision · **4th toe flexion** used to protect nerve when making AL portal (TO; not 4th toe extension)

**Most commonly injured branch** — **dorsal intermediate cutaneous branch** (TO + Orthobullets)\
✗ Wrong options: dorsal medial cutaneous branch

**Dorsal foot numbness after scope** — SPN via **anterolateral portal**

**✗ SPN distractors** — between EHL + EDL = **DPN** (EDB/EHB motor, 1st web) · terminates as **lateral dorsal cutaneous n.** = **sural** · motor to abductor hallucis + FHB = **medial plantar** · terminal branch of femoral = **saphenous**

### Anteromedial & posterior portals – saphenous & sural nerves

**Saphenous nerve** — travels along distal medial tibia + ankle · **most at risk with anteromedial portal**\
✗ Wrong options: medial midline (well lateral to it)

**Sural nerve** — **posterolateral portal** (os trigonum resection) · permanent numbness risk

### Posterior ankle impingement (os trigonum, Stieda process) (TO)

**PAIS** — mechanical pinching in **terminal plantar flexion** · chronic > acute · ballet (**en pointe**), football, soccer, downhill running · **acute plantar hyperflexion** or **chronic microtrauma** (TO)

**Most common cause (TO)** — **os trigonum**\
✗ Wrong options: Stieda process · sloping posterior tibial lip · capsular calcification

| Osseous causes (TO) | Soft-tissue causes (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Os trigonum** · **Stieda process** · downward-sloping posterior tibial lip · posterior tibial osteophyte · prominent posterior calcaneal process · OCL, loose bodies, chondromatosis, subtalar coalition | Thickened posterior capsule · post-traumatic scar · **post-traumatic capsular calcification** · loose bodies · **FHL tenosynovitis** (commonest tendon involved) · abnormal muscles |

**Os trigonum** — **non-fusion of posterolateral talar secondary ossification centre** · lies **lateral to FHL groove** · appears **7–13 yr** · forms **11–13 yr boys / 8–10 yr girls** · fuses within **1 yr** (→ trigonal / Stieda process) · often bilateral · pain from abutment or **synchondrosis disruption** (TO)

**Os trigonum prevalence** — **7–14%** remain separate (TO) · **7%** don't fuse (TO) · **present in 10–25%** (OB) — read the options

**Stieda process (TO)** — **elongated posterolateral talar process** → **causes posterior ankle impingement**\
✗ Wrong options: ossification at MCL insertion (Pellegrini-Stieda) · unfused ossicle (os trigonum) · elderly head injury

**Imaging (TO)** — lateral X-ray: os trigonum / Stieda (may be normal) · **MRI best: bone marrow oedema** in talus, calcaneus or os trigonum

**DDx (TO)** — Achilles tendinosis / tear, **FHL tenosynovitis**, posterior talar process fracture, Haglund's, OCL, retrocalcaneal bursitis

**Treatment** — nonop (NSAID, activity modification, PT) → **excision open lateral or posterior arthroscopy** via posterolateral portal (**sural at risk**) · stress fracture ± FHL tenosynovitis / entrapment

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Neurological (overall)** | **Commonest reported complication** (OB, Orthobullets, TO) · **neuropraxia** commonest · nerve injury **3.5–10%** · overall complications **6–20%** |
| **SPN** | **Commonest nerve** · AL portal · **commonest complication of anterior arthroscopy** (TO + OB) |
| **Cartilage injury** | Up to **30%** (TO) · **anterolateral (zone 3) + central (zone 5) talus** · ↓ by distraction + correct portals · called **commonest complication of ankle arthroscopy** in one TO key |
| **Synovial cutaneous fistula** | "Somewhat common" · prevent by **immobilisation** until portals heal |
| Vascular | Rare · anterocentral portal · **anterior tibial artery aneurysm** (case reports) |
| Tendon / ligament | **EDC, EHL rupture** · ATFL + deltoid injured by accessory portals |
| **Posterior arthroscopy** | **Tibial + sural** = commonest nerve injuries · **FHL injury** · Achilles tightness |
| Other | DVT, infection (**very infrequent**), CRPS |

**Commonest complication — source conflict** — **SPN / neurological injury** (TO17136845, OB) · **cartilage injury** (TO17136847 explanation) — read the options; if SPN is an option it is the keyed answer

### Painful portal neuroma

**Burning at lateral portal, Tinel → medial foot / ankle, injection helped 2 wk** — **SPN medial branch entrapment** → **neuroplasty** (nerve intact) or **excision + burial** (cut / thinned)\
✗ Wrong options: sural neuroplasty (posterolateral portal) · DPN · repeat scope for OCD · anaesthetic patch

## How it's asked

**"Most common complication of anterior ankle arthroscopy?"** → **superficial peroneal nerve injury** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: cartilage · EDC · fistula

**"True regarding ankle arthroscopy?"** → **4th toe flexion is used to protect the nerve (CPN/SPN) while making the anterolateral portal** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: 4th toe extension · dorsal medial cutaneous branch · "commonest complication = CPN neurapraxia"

**"Most common complication with standard lateral portal?"** → **injury to the superficial peroneal nerve**\
✗ Wrong options: tibial · DPA · RSD · deep infection

**"Portal putting SPN at greatest risk?"** → **anterolateral portal**

**"Dorsal foot numbness after scope — portal?"** → **anterolateral**

**"Tendon closest to AL portal?"** → **peroneus tertius**

**"Transillumination protects?"** → **superficial peroneal nerve**

**"Most commonly injured nerve — characteristic?"** → **motor branches to peroneus longus and brevis** (SPN)

**"Lateral portal neuroma, Tinel to medial foot?"** → **neuroplasty or excision + burial of medial branch of SPN**

**"Saphenous nerve most at risk with?"** → **anteromedial portal**

**"Portal 2 (anteromedial) — at risk?"** → **saphenous nerve**

**"Os trigonum resection — portal riskiest for sural?"** → **posterolateral portal**

**"Most common cause of posterior ankle impingement?"** → **os trigonum** (TO)

**"True about Stieda's process?"** → **causes posterior ankle impingement** (TO)

**"Anterocentral portal at risk?"** → **dorsalis pedis artery (+ DPN)**

**"Posteromedial portal at risk?"** → **posterior tibial artery**
