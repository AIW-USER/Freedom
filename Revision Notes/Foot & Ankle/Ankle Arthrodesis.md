# Ankle Arthrodesis

*Foot & Ankle · 6 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Indications & causes

**Definition** — fusion of the **tibiotalar joint**; commonest for **end-stage ankle arthritis**; open or arthroscopic

**Commonest cause** — **post-traumatic arthritis** · **> 60%** of end-stage tibiotalar arthritis (OB)

**Other indications** — post-infection · chronic instability · **AVN of the talus** · inflammatory arthropathy · primary OA · **neuropathic arthropathy** · tumour resection · **salvage of failed ORIF** · **salvage of failed TAA**

**Talar AVN** — **trauma = commonest cause (75%)**, mainly talar neck / body fractures · early → core decompression, non-vascularised or vascularised bone graft · late (structural change) → **arthrodesis**

**Hindfoot OA causes** — calcaneus / talus fractures · RA · OA · end-stage PTTD · tarsal coalition · **Charcot-Marie-Tooth (cavus)**

**Talar neck fracture** — **varus malunion \~30%** → abnormal ankle loading → arthritis

### Effect of hindfoot fusion on motion (talonavicular)

| Joint fused | Effect on motion |
|---|---|
| **Talonavicular** | **Eliminates almost all hindfoot motion — > 90% ↓** (greatest cumulative effect) |
| **Subtalar (talocalcaneal)** | **↓ TN motion 74%** · **↓ CC motion 44%** |
| **Calcaneocuboid** | **↓ TN motion 33%** (OB) · **67%** (another OB) — read the options · ↓ subtalar 8% (little effect) |
| Naviculocuneiform | Little effect on hindfoot |
| Cuboid–5th MT | Limited effect |
| **Tibiotalar** | **Little effect on hindfoot ROM** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Decides |
|---|---|
| **Weight-bearing X-rays** ankle + foot | End-stage tibiotalar arthritis · coronal deformity (varus) · triple malunion (residual subtalar valgus, forefoot abduction) |
| **CT** | **Hindfoot (subtalar) degeneration** → spared = **isolated tibiotalar** · involved = **TTC** |
| **MRI** | **Talar AVN** — broad-based → TTC |
| **Aspiration** | Prior septic ankle — **no growth, WBC 9,800 = no active infection** → arthrodesis (✗ Wrong options: repeat I&D) |
| Post-op X-ray | **Tibial stress fracture at proximal interlocking screw** of hindfoot nail |

**Clinical localisation** — pain with **passive DF / PF** = tibiotalar · pain with **inversion / eversion** + sinus tarsi pain = **subtalar** · **Coleman block** rigid hindfoot = fixed

## 5 Management

```
End-stage ankle arthritis, failed bracing / NSAIDs / injections
├─ Subtalar spared → TIBIOTALAR ARTHRODESIS (screws)
├─ + Subtalar arthritis / broad talar AVN / bone loss → TTC ARTHRODESIS
│      (lateral transfibular · retrograde IM nail · femoral head allograft if bone loss)
├─ Infection / draining hardware → REMOVE HARDWARE + I&D → STAGED ARTHRODESIS
└─ Isolated subtalar arthritis, ankle painless → SUBTALAR ARTHRODESIS
```

### Operative criteria – arthrodesis vs TAA

| Favours **arthrodesis** | Favours **TAA** |
|---|---|
| **Young, high-demand / labourer** (construction worker, 36–45 yo) | **Elderly, less active** |
| **Peripheral neuropathy** (↑ TAA failure) · insensate foot | Preserved sensation |
| **Severe PVD** | — |
| **Charcot / neuropathic arthropathy** | — |
| **Osteonecrosis of talus** (TAA contraindicated) | Good talar bone stock |
| **Active or prior infection** (↑ periprosthetic infection) | No infection history |
| **Severe deformity** · soft-tissue compromise | Correctable deformity |
| **Gold standard**; most reliable pain relief | Keeps ROM → better cadence, **↓ adjacent joint degeneration**; reasonable 10-yr survivorship |

**Tibiotalar fusion** — **highest success rate** for post-ORIF tibiotalar arthritis\
✗ Wrong options: osteochondral allograft · interpositional soft tissue · arthroscopic debridement + microfracture · TTC

**Supramalleolar osteotomy** — only for **coronal deformity > 10°** with **mild / moderate** arthritis\
✗ Wrong options: end-stage

### Optimal fusion position

| Plane | Position |
|---|---|
| **Sagittal** | **Neutral (0°) dorsiflexion / plantarflexion — plantigrade** |
| **External rotation** | **5–10° ER** (Orthobullets, OB) · **10° ER** (OB, TO) · **ER \~5°** (TO explanation) — read the options |
| **Coronal** | **5° hindfoot valgus** (Orthobullets, TO) · **0–5° valgus** (OB) · **5–10° valgus** (another OB) — read the options |
| **Translation** | **5 mm posterior talar translation** under the plafond |

![Tibiotalar arthrodesis with crossed screws](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7052-ankle-fusion.jpg)

**Why each** — neutral flexion ↓ knee extension in gait + **MCL laxity** · slight valgus prevents **lateral column overload** · posterior talus ↓ **lever arm of foot**

**Avoid (TO)** — **extension** → excess pressure + **intractable heel pain** (slight flexion tolerated) · **any varus** · **anterior talar translation** → **"vaulting" gait + knee pain** (TO)

**Wrong positions** — **plantarflexion** → equinus gait, poor swing-phase clearance ("never the answer"; goal = plantigrade) · **too much DF or valgus (20°)** → altered gait, ↑ knee stress · **internal rotation** → abnormal foot progression angle · 0° ER · varus → poor outcome, adjacent joint degeneration

**Hindfoot arthrodesis** — aim **5° hindfoot valgus**

### Indications – tibiotalar vs tibiotalocalcaneal vs subtalar fusion

| Scenario | Answer | ✗ Traps |
|---|---|---|
| Post-traumatic ankle OA, 36-yo labourer, **CT: hindfoot spared** | **Tibiotalar arthrodesis with screws** | Arthroscopic debridement + steroid (adjunct only) · TAA · TTC IM / extramedullary |
| Varus end-stage ankle OA, **mild subtalar changes**, diabetic, no neuropathy | **Isolated tibiotalar fusion + slight posterior talar translation** | > 5° PF · IR · combined ST fusion · triple |
| End-stage ankle OA + **peripheral neuropathy**, no subtalar pain | **Tibiotalar arthrodesis** | TAA (↑ failure) · arthroscopy · subtalar fusion |
| **Prior septic arthritis + talar AVN**, aspirate sterile | **Ankle arthrodesis** | Repeat I&D · **TAA (contraindicated in talar AVN)** · SMOT · triple (fixed adult flatfoot) |
| Talar neck nonunion + **AVN + talar head collapse** | **TTC arthrodesis with femoral head allograft** | Steroid injection · TAA · subtalar fusion alone · triple |
| **Broad-based talar AVN** + tibiotalar + subtalar arthritis | **TTC via lateral transfibular approach** | Anterior approach (no subtalar access) · ankle fusion alone · TAA · **talectomy without arthrodesis** |
| **Pes cavus**, sinus tarsi pain, painful inversion / eversion, rigid on Coleman block, ankle painless | **Subtalar arthrodesis** | Broström–Gould (instability) · hindfoot scope + os trigonum excision (posterior impingement) · Chrisman–Snook (advanced instability) · **triple (TN + CC + ST arthritis)** |
| Isolated hindfoot arthritis from **chronic pes planus** | **Subtalar arthrodesis** | — |

### Techniques & fixation

**Arthroscopic arthrodesis** — **only if minimal deformity**

**Open** — **transfibular approach when deformity present** · fixation = **screws** · **plate + screws** · **external fixation**

**Staged approach** — **clear infection before definitive internal hardware**

**TTC fixation options** — Steinmann pins · plates + screws · ex-fix · **retrograde IM nail**

**Retrograde IM nail** — **load-sharing**; **↑ bending stiffness + rotational stability** vs plate-and-screw · useful with distal tibial / talar bone loss or when screw fixation suboptimal

![Tibiotalocalcaneal fusion with retrograde hindfoot nail](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7052-hindfoot-nail-moved.jpg)

**Nail entry point (TO)** — crossing of **bimalleolar (medial malleolus) bisector** (coronal) **+ sagittal line from tip of 2nd toe to centre of heel** (TO)

**Lateral transfibular approach** — dual joint preparation (**tibiotalar + subtalar**) + **local autograft** (talar osteonecrosis)

**TTC indications** — end-stage **ankle + subtalar arthritis** · **Charcot neuroarthropathy** · **hindfoot bone loss** (failed TAA, failed arthrodesis) · **osteonecrosis / global AVN of talus** · severe acute trauma · RA · flail ankle

### Contraindication – peripheral vascular disease

| Retrograde nail TTC | Items |
|---|---|
| **Absolute contraindications** | **Active infection** · **severe (profound) peripheral vascular disease** · **severe tibial malalignment** |
| **Relative contraindication** | **Normal subtalar joint** — may be sacrificed in non-compliant patients + **diabetic Charcot** for stability |
| **Indications (✗ Wrong options: as contraindication)** | **Osteonecrosis of talus** · **bone loss from failed TAA / arthrodesis** · **severe peripheral neuropathy with Charcot** |

### Salvage of infection/failed TAA – staged arthrodesis

**Infected TAA + varus instability + talar bone loss** — stage 1: **explant + antibiotic cement spacer + IV antibiotics** → stage 2: **ankle + hindfoot (TTC) arthrodesis with structural allograft**

**✗ Infected TAA traps** — revision TAA ± structural allograft (allograft remodels incompletely, fails under shear) · ankle arthrodesis in situ / with allograft (**talar bone loss → poor talar purchase**)

**Failed TAA salvage** — **conversion to fusion** or **transtibial amputation**

**Draining wound over old hardware + post-traumatic OA** — **removal of hardware, I&D, staged arthrodesis**\
✗ Wrong options: keep hardware · simultaneous arthrodesis · simultaneous arthroplasty

**Infected post-traumatic OA salvage** — arthrodesis or amputation · aggressive debridement + antibiotics before fusion

### Footwear after fusion – rocker sole

**Best gait aid after ankle fusion** — **rocker sole (heel-to-toe / rocker-bottom)** → ↓ heel-strike pressure, ↑ toe-off propulsion, "roll off" in late stance, dissipates force across fusion

| ✗ Option | Actual use |
|---|---|
| **SACH heel** | Mimics shock absorption of heel strike; less beneficial than rocker |
| **Extended steel shank** | Stiffens sole; worse unless combined with rocker |
| **Total contact insert** | Cushion / offload, correct flexible deformity |
| **Lateral sole flare** | Varus deformity / lateral ankle instability |
| **Morton's extension** (± carbon fibre) | Forefoot pain |
| **Medial heel wedge** | Not for well-aligned fusion |
| **AFO, Arizona, double-upright drop-lock** | Immobilise an already fused ankle |
| **Pantalar arthrodesis** | Unnecessary if hindfoot + transverse tarsal motion painless |

### Malunited triple arthrodesis – osteotomy

**Valgus–supination triple malunion** — residual subtalar valgus → **lateral subfibular impingement** + forefoot abduction / midfoot supination via TN joint

**Treatment** — **medial displacement calcaneal osteotomy + transverse tarsal (derotational) osteotomy**\
✗ Wrong options: arthroscopy + lateral ligament reconstruction · tendon transfer + lateral column lengthening + heel cord lengthening (PTTD flatfoot) · ankle arthrodesis with IM nail · TAA + deltoid reefing (ankle normal, patient young)

## 6 Complications

### Complications – adjacent arthritis, nonunion, stress fracture & nerve injury

| Complication | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Nonunion** | **Commonest complication** (OB) · **10%** (Orthobullets) · **0–47%** across foot and ankle fusions (OB) · **smokers 2.7× risk** |
| **Persistent nonunion after revision** | **Neuropathy = greatest risk factor** (✗ Wrong options: diabetes · prior femoral nonunion · post-traumatic arthritis · initial fixation method) |
| **Subtalar (adjacent hindfoot) arthritis** | **Commonest long-term complication once united**; nonphysiologic subtalar motion · **common complication (TO)** |
| **Lateral plantar nerve** | Hindfoot nail insertion at heel → **numb plantar-lateral foot + 4th / 5th toes** (✗ Wrong options: medial plantar · sural · SPN · DPN) |
| **Superficial peroneal nerve** | **Transfibular approach** |
| **Tibial stress fracture** | **Unique to IM nail** — **proximal interlocking screw = stress riser** |

**Subtalar arthritis (TO)** — **common complication** after ankle arthrodesis (TO)

**Most common complications (Orthobullets)** — **subtalar arthritis + nonunion**

**Not ↑ after fusion** — talar AVN (rare) · ipsilateral knee arthritis · contralateral ankle arthritis

**Adjacent joints** — hindfoot, midfoot, forefoot **compensate with ↑ motion** → accelerated arthrosis · TAA may protect

![Lateral plantar nerve near the plantar incision for hindfoot nail](../../MCQ/attachments/q2904-illa-hindfoot-nail-illustration.jpg)

**Nail stress fracture prevention** — **remove proximal screws after union** · **short rod avoiding diaphysis**\
✗ Wrong options: late rotatory deformity (controlled by distal transfixion screws) · talar osteonecrosis (minimal dissection) · hardware failure (any construct)

![Tibial stress fracture at the proximal interlocking screw](../../MCQ/attachments/q6709-a-011a.jpg)

**Failed TTC (RA, after failed TAA), unstable pseudoarthrosis, no infection** — **revision TTC arthrodesis**\
✗ Wrong options: conversion to TAA (subtalar arthrosis, poor talar stock) · supplemental screws alone · **talar resection** (keep talus for vascularity + local graft; femoral head allograft if insufficient) · total contact casting (neuropathic ulcers)

## X vs Y

| | **Tibiotalar** | **TTC** | **Subtalar** | **Triple** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Joints | Ankle | Ankle + subtalar | Subtalar | TN + CC + ST |
| Indication | Ankle OA, **hindfoot spared** | **Ankle + subtalar** OA, **global talar AVN**, Charcot, bone loss | Isolated subtalar OA (cavus, chronic planus) | **Fixed adult flatfoot** / TN + CC + ST arthritis |
| Fixation | **Screws**, plate, ex-fix | **Retrograde IM nail**, plate, ex-fix | Screws | — |
| Approach | Anterior / transfibular | **Lateral transfibular** | — | — |

| | **Primary fusion failure** | **Long-term after union** |
|---|---|---|
| Event | **Nonunion** | **Subtalar arthritis** |
| Risk factor | **Smoking 2.7×**, **neuropathy** (revision) | Nonphysiologic subtalar motion |

## How it's asked

**"Recommended ankle fusion position?"** → **neutral DF / PF, 10° ER, 5° valgus** (TO)

**"Ankle arthrodesis — ALL true EXCEPT?"** → **slight anterior translation of talus** (TO)

**"Entry point for retrograde TTC nail?"** → **bimalleolar bisector × line from 2nd toe tip to heel centre** (TO)

**"Optimal fusion position?"** → **0 degrees dorsiflexion / plantarflexion, 0–5 degree hindfoot valgus, 5–10 degree external rotation**

**"Optimal fusion position — talus?"** → **plantigrade, 10° ER, 5° valgus, posterior positioning of talus**

**"Varus post-traumatic ankle OA, mild subtalar change — procedure?"** → **isolated tibiotalar fusion with slight posterior translation of talus**

**"Post-ORIF tibiotalar arthritis — highest success?"** → **tibiotalar fusion**

**"Cavus, sinus tarsi pain, rigid hindfoot, ankle painless?"** → **subtalar arthrodesis**

**"Talar neck nonunion with AVN and talar head collapse?"** → **TTC arthrodesis with femoral head allograft**

**"Young construction worker, post-traumatic ankle OA, hindfoot normal on CT?"** → **tibiotalar arthrodesis with screws**

**"Prior septic ankle, sterile aspirate, talar AVN?"** → **ankle arthrodesis**

**"Broad-based talar AVN after subtalar dislocation?"** → **TTC arthrodesis using lateral transfibular approach**

**"Diabetic with neuropathy, end-stage ankle OA?"** → **tibiotalar arthrodesis**

**"Single joint fusion → greatest loss of hindfoot motion?"** → **talonavicular**

**"Fusion causing > 90% ↓ hindfoot motion?"** → **talonavicular joint**

**"Numb lateral sole + 4th / 5th toes after hindfoot nail?"** → **lateral plantar nerve**

**"RA, failed TTC after failed TAA, nonunion — next?"** → **revision TTC arthrodesis**

**"Complication unique to hindfoot IM nail?"** → **tibial stress fracture**

**"Greatest risk for persistent nonunion after revision?"** → **neuropathy**

**"United ankle fusion — long-term risk?"** → **subtalar arthritis**

**"Best footwear to restore gait after ankle fusion?"** → **rocker sole**

**"Pain-free fusion, hard walking, hindfoot motion painless?"** → **heel-to-toe rocker sole**

**"Altered gait after successful fusion — device?"** → **rocker-bottom sole**

**"Infected TAA with talar bone loss — second stage?"** → **ankle and hindfoot arthrodesis with structural allograft**

**"Draining post-traumatic ankle with hardware — plan?"** → **removal of hardware, I&D, staged arthrodesis**

**"Absolute contraindication to TTC nail?"** → **severe peripheral vascular disease**

**"Triple arthrodesis for coalition at 13, now lateral impingement?"** → **calcaneal osteotomy + transverse tarsal osteotomy**
