# Peroneal Tendon Tears and Instability

*Foot & Ankle · 38 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Spectrum** — tenosynovitis → tendinopathy → tears → instability (subluxation / dislocation)

**Tears in lateral ankle instability** — **23–77%** (Orthobullets) · "up to **77%**" (OB) → often **missed** in lateral ankle pain

**Prevalence** — tears = **4%** of ankle injuries

**Instability** — young, athletic · presents late, mistaken for **ankle sprain**

**PB vs PL** — **PB tears far commoner** (vulnerable between fibula + PL) · PL tears less frequent (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: "PL tear more common" (TO)

### Anatomy – brevis / longus relationship & course

| Level | Peroneus brevis (PB) | Peroneus longus (PL) |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | **Distal ⅔ fibula** + intermuscular septum (TO) | **Proximal ⅔ lateral fibula**, septum, **lateral tibial condyle** (TO) |
| Becomes tendinous | **2–4 cm** above fibular tip (Orthobullets) · **2–3 cm** (TO) | — |
| **Retromalleolar groove** | **Directly posterior to fibula**, flat (TO) · **anterior + medial (deep) to PL** | **Posterior to PB**, rounded (TO) |
| **Peroneal tubercle** | **Superior / dorsal** to tubercle (at sinus tarsi level) | **Inferior / plantar** → cuboid tunnel |
| **Insertion** | **Base of 5th MT** (dorsolateral) | **Plantar base 1st MT + medial cuneiform** (via cuboid tunnel between **long plantar ligament** + cuboid groove — TO) |
| Function | **Primary evertor** · **63% of eversion power** (TO) · assists PF | **Plantarflexes 1st ray** + **everts foot** · **secondary ankle PF** + **stabilises medial column** (TO) · **transverse arch** · antagonises TA |
| Nerve | **Superficial peroneal (S1)** | **Superficial peroneal (S1)** |

![Axial MRI: PB directly behind fibula, PL posterior to PB](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7023-brevis-and-longus.jpg)

**✗ Insertion traps** — navicular + medial cuneiform = **tibialis posterior** · base of hallux distal phalanx = **FHL** · distal phalanges 2–5 = **FDL** · 1st MT base + medial cuneiform = **PL** (not PB)

**✗ Course traps** — 90° turn at medial malleolus → navicular + plantar MT 2–4 = **tib post** · anterior ankle → medial cuneiform + 1st MT base = **tib ant** · between talar tubercles → hallux = **FHL** · inferior to tubercle → medial cuneiform + 1st MT = **PL**

**✗ Ligament traps** — fibula → talar neck = **ATFL** · posterior fibula → posterolateral talar tubercle = **PTFL** · fibula → calcaneus = **CFL**

**Common sheath (TO)** — **\~4 cm above to 1 cm below** lateral malleolus tip · splits into two sheaths at **peroneal tubercle** (TO)

**Retromalleolar groove** — fibro-osseous tunnel · deepened by **fibrocartilaginous ridge** (lateral) · only **\~5 mm** deep · bounded by **SPR, PTFL, CFL, PITFL** · surface ↑ laterally by fibrocartilaginous ridge, medially by osseous groove (TO)

**Superior peroneal retinaculum (SPR)** — **primary restraint** to peroneal subluxation · from **posterolateral ridge / distal 1–2 cm of fibula** (TO: distal 2 cm) → lateral calcaneus / **Achilles sheath** · secondary restraint to anterolateral ankle instability · single fibro-osseous tunnel

**Inferior peroneal retinaculum** — at **peroneal tubercle**, forms 2 separate sheaths · not involved in instability · "sheaths thicken to form the inferior extensor retinaculum" (TO wording)

**Blood supply** — vincula from **anterior + posterior tibial arteries**, **whole tendon vascularised, avascular zones disproven** (Orthobullets) · vincula from **posterior peroneal + medial tarsal arteries** (TO) · "**vascular watershed** just posterior to fibula" (OB) — read the options

**Peroneus tertius (TO)** — separate muscle in **anterior** compartment (TO)

### Variants – peroneus quartus, os peroneum, low-lying PB

| Variant | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Peroneus quartus** | Arises from **PB muscle belly, distal ⅓ leg** (✗ Wrong options: **from PL** (TO EXCEPT)) · runs posteromedial to tendons · inserts **retrotrochlear eminence** of calcaneus · incidence **up to 21%** (OB) · quartus or quintus **10–34%** (Orthobullets) · **mass effect** → pain, tears |
| **Low-lying PB belly** | Below superior margin of SPR · up to **33%** (TO) |
| **Enlarged peroneal tubercle** | **29%** · entraps PL → **PL tenosynovitis / tears** · Harris view |
| **Flat / convex retromalleolar sulcus** | **18%** (Orthobullets) · "flat or concave" (TO wording) |
| **Os peroneum** | Sesamoid in **PL** near **CC joint / lateral plantar cuboid** · radiographically as few as **5%** (TO) · uni / bi / multipartite · symptomatic = "**stepping on a pebble**" |
| **POPS (TO)** | **Painful os peroneum syndrome** → acute fracture … hypertrophied peroneal tubercle entrapping PL / os (TO) |

### Associated conditions & risk factors

**Risk factors** — **lateral ligament (ATFL / CFL) instability** · **cavovarus hindfoot** · **Charcot–Marie–Tooth** · low-lying PB · enlarged tubercle · quartus / quintus · flat sulcus · os peroneum · **calcaneal malunion + subfibular impingement**

**Not risk factors** — **pes planus / PTT insufficiency** (TO, OB) · 5th MT base fracture (↑ with cavovarus but doesn't cause tears) · talocalcaneal coalition · OLT

**PL tears associated with** — **diabetes**, RA, **hindfoot varus**, enlarged peroneal tubercle

## 2 Mechanism

**SPR tear (TO)** — **rapid forced dorsiflexion + inversion** → **reflex peroneal contraction** → SPR torn → subluxation / dislocation = **most common mechanism** (TO) · e.g. **ski tips caught**, foot caught on astroturf

**Mechanism wording** — **dorsiflexion + inversion** (Orthobullets, TO, most OB) · "dorsiflexion + eversion" in some OB explanations — read the options\
✗ Wrong options: plantarflexion + inversion (line of pull direct, little subluxation)

**PB split – two mechanisms (TO)** — **subluxation over posterolateral fibular edge** (SPR lax / torn, chronic instability) → attrition · **compression between fibula + PL** in inversion (TO)

**PB tear pattern (TO)** — **longitudinal split** at **tip of fibula** (tendon changes direction acutely + compression by PL) (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: radial · base of 5th MT

**PL tears** — direct trauma, sports · **cuboid notch / tunnel** = commonest PL site\
✗ Wrong options: musculotendinous junction (TO)

## 3 Presentation

**History** — **pop** / snap with **dorsiflexion** injury · lateral instability · pain + clicking returns after cast removal

**Pain** — lateral / **posterolateral** ankle, retrofibular · worse with **active eversion**, PF, **passive dorsiflexion**

**Inspection** — swelling behind lateral malleolus · **cavovarus** · "pseudotumour" · voluntary subluxation ± pop · tendons palpable as ridge over lateral fibula

| Test | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Apprehension test** | Subluxation / apprehension with **active DF + eversion against resistance** (most consistent) |
| **Palpable snapping** | **Tendon snapping over fibula during ankle dorsiflexion** (+ eversion) → **SPR injury** |
| **Compression test** | Pain / crepitus with **passive DF + eversion** |
| **Active circumduction** | Recreates instability |
| **Coleman block** | Hindfoot flexibility (cavovarus) |
| **Anterior drawer** | Concomitant ATFL laxity |

**✗ Exam traps** — +anterior drawer = **ATFL** · +ER stress = **syndesmosis** · anterolateral crepitus = arthritis / OCD / loose body · tenderness at **5th MT base with eversion = peroneal (PB) tear**, not SPR

**PL tendinopathy signs** — pain with **resisted eversion** + **resisted plantarflexion of 1st MT**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding / use |
|---|---|
| **X-ray (WB)** | Often normal · **fleck sign** = cortical avulsion of SPR off **distal / posterior lateral malleolar ridge** (rim fracture) · cavovarus: plantarflexed 1st MT, ↑ Meary angle |
| **Os peroneum migration** | **Proximal migration = pathognomonic of PL rupture** · best on **oblique + lateral** · compare contralateral |
| **Harris view** | Peroneal tubercle morphology |
| **Ultrasound** | **Dynamic subluxation**, intra-sheath subluxation · **90% accurate** for tears · user dependent |
| **CT** | Calcaneal malunion, subfibular impingement, groove / tubercle shape |
| **MRI** | **Axial, slightly plantarflexed ankle** (↓ **magic angle**) · **83–90% sens, 72–75% spec** · circumferential sheath fluid = tenosynovitis · fatty infiltration · **three structures behind fibula = longitudinal split** · **boomerang sign** (PB wraps around PL) · accessory muscles, low PB belly · ATFL / CFL, OCD |

![Fleck sign – SPR avulsion off the lateral malleolus](../../MCQ/attachments/q1144-illa-fleck-sign.jpg)

**✗ PL rupture traps** — avulsion at 5th MT base (PB) · lateral tenderness (sprain) · **inability to plantarflex** (gastrocsoleus still works) · **pes planovalgus** (PTTD)

| **2** | SPR separated from **cartilofibrous ridge** → tendons sublux between SPR and ridge | |

| **2** | **FCR + SPR + periosteum avulsed together** from anterior fibula | **33%** | |
| **3** | **Bony avulsion** of anterior fibula with FCR + SPR | **13%** | |

### Raikin classification – intra-sheath subluxation

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **1** | PL lies **deep** to PB |
| **2** | PL subluxes **through a PB tear** |

### Redfern & Myerson tear classification

| Type | Tendons | Treatment | Algorithm |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Both intact, partial tears** | **Debride + tubularise** | ![Redfern–Myerson algorithm](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7023-algorithm.jpg) |
| **II** | **One intact, other majority torn** | **Tenodesis** to intact tendon | |
| **IIIa** | **Both torn (unusable), no muscle excursion** | **FHL / FDL transfer** | |
| **IIIb** | **Both torn, muscle excursion present** | **Allograft** (one-stage) · scarred bed → **staged silicone rod** | |

**Other OB wording** — "Type I = < 50% one or both tendons, Type II = > 50% complex" — read the options

### Brandes–Smith zones (TO)

| Zone | Site | Typical tear |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Under **SPR** | Subluxation, mass effect of **quartus / low-lying PB** |
| **B** | **Inferior peroneal retinaculum** | **Peroneal tubercle hypertrophy** |
| **C** | **Cuboid notch** | **Commonest site of PL pathology** |

## 5 Management

```
Peroneal instability (SPR tear)
├─ Acute, nonprofessional → reduce + SLC 6 wk, slight PF + inversion
├─ Acute, high-level athlete → SPR repair ± groove deepening
├─ Chronic / failed nonop → SPR repair + groove deepening
└─ Recurrent, bony abnormality → bone block / rerouting (salvage)
       └─ hindfoot varus → add calcaneal osteotomy
```

```
Peroneal tendon tear (after failed rest, boot, PT)
├─ < 50% (50–60%) of width → debride + tubularise
├─ > 50% (> 60%), one tendon usable → excise + tenodesis
├─ Both > 50%, muscle excursion → interposition allo / autograft
└─ Both > 50%, no excursion → FHL / FDL transfer
```

### Operative criteria

| Situation | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **Tenosynovitis / tendinopathy**, recalcitrant, < 50–60% to resect | **Tenosynovectomy + debridement** (tendoscopic) |
| **Tear < 50%** (Orthobullets 50–60%) | **Debridement + tubularisation** |
| **Complex PB tear > 50%** (multiple splits, tendinosis; OB: **> 60%**) | **Excise + proximal + distal tenodesis PB → PL** · 70–80% success, RTS 12 wk |
| **Complex PL tear > 50%** | **Tenodesis PL → PB** |
| **Both > 50%, excursion preserved (IIIb)** | **Interposition graft** (hamstring auto / allo) |
| **Both > 50%, no excursion (IIIa)** | **FHL / FDL transfer** |
| **Acute dislocation, high-level athlete wanting quick return** · longitudinal tear | **SPR repair + groove deepening** |
| **Chronic / recurrent with bony abnormality / incompetent SPR** | **Groove deepening + soft-tissue transfer / bone block** (salvage) |
| **Rigid hindfoot varus / valgus** | **Add corrective osteotomy** |

### Peroneal subluxation – SPR repair ± groove deepening

**Acute, nonop** — **tendons reduced** + **short-leg cast 6 wk**, protected WB, **slight plantarflexion + inversion** (TO, Orthobullets) · **irreducible acute dislocation → surgery** · some advise acute repair in athletes (high nonop failure) (TO) · **U- or J-shaped pad** · effective in **\~50%** (OB) · **> 50% failure for chronic** · up to **83% failure for tears**

**No PT / rehab for unstable tendons** — may further damage them · no more casting or stirrup splint after failed cast

**Failed nonop / chronic subluxation** — **SPR repair ± fibular groove deepening**\
✗ Wrong options: ATFL repair · CFL repair · Broström–Gould with solid drawer end point · fibular shortening osteotomy · walker boot in PF

**Fleck sign + persistent popping, solid drawer** — **peroneal retinacular reconstruction**

**Dislocated tendons + convex fibular groove** — **débridement + fibular groove deepening + SPR repair**\
✗ Wrong options: tear repair alone (won't stop dislocation) · tenodesis alone

**Commonest procedure for chronic dislocation (TO)** — **SPR repair + groove deepening** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: CFL rerouting · distal fibular osteotomy + CFL rerouting · complete fibular osteotomy / bone block

**SPR repair + groove deepening** — **RTS 83–100%** (higher than SPR repair alone) · excellent PROMs

**Surgical categories (TO)** — SPR repair / replacement · groove deepening · bone block / osteotomy · soft-tissue rerouting · tendon graft (TO)

| Technique (TO) | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Direct SPR repair** | Chronic SPR redundant → **pants-over-vest** (anterior flap over posterior) · direct, bone tunnels or anchors |
| **Groove deepening – direct** | Elevate cartilaginous floor → burr / curette cancellous bone → tamp floor back |
| **Groove deepening – indirect** | **3.5 mm drill retrograde from fibular tip** parallel to groove → tamp floor into tunnel (preserves floor) |
| **Bone block (Kelly)** | Partial / complete fibular osteotomy, posterior displacement → barrier · firmer, but **fracture, nonunion, tendon irritation / adherence** |
| **CFL rerouting (Platzgummer)** | Tendons placed under CFL · salvage |
| **Tendon graft** | Achilles, PB, **plantaris** reinforce SPR via fibular tunnel |

**Bone block + rerouting (TO)** — nonunion, tendon adherence, **sural neuropathy**, instability → **salvage / revision only** (TO)

**Approach** — longitudinal incision over tendons, **avoid sural nerve** · SPR split longitudinally leaving cuff, or transected from fibula

### Tendon tears – debridement / tubularisation vs tenodesis vs transfer

**First line** — rest, activity modification, **boot then PT** (boot stops when rest pain resolves) · orthosis for varus · NSAIDs, bracing

**Tubularise (TO)** — after excising thin split, **> 50% of PB remains → tubularise** · **< 50% remains → tenodesis to PL** (TO)

**Severe degeneration (TO)** — **muscle excursion adequate → hamstring auto / allograft** · **scarred muscle → FHL / FDL transfer** · **fibrotic sheath → staged silastic rod → pseudosheath → repair** (TO)

**Single longitudinal PB tear, no tendinosis** — **core repair + tubularisation**

**PB: multiple splits, > 70% tendinosis over 3 cm** — **excise + proximal + distal tenodesis to PL**\
✗ Wrong options: core repair · excision without transfer · groove deepening · arthroscopic debridement

**PB tenodesis levels** — proximal **\~3 cm above** fibular tip · distal **\~2 cm below** tip, side-to-side

**30% PB + PL tear** — **debridement + tubularisation only**\
✗ Wrong options: 30% PB + shallow groove → add **groove deepening** · 60% PB → tenodesis · 60% both + excursion → graft · 60% both, no excursion → transfer

**SPR tear + complex PB 65%** — **SPR repair + groove deepening + PB → PL tenodesis**\
✗ Wrong options: tendinitis → tenosynovectomy · 30% → tubularise · both 70% → graft vs transfer

**PL tendinopathy (cuboid tunnel), PB normal** — **debride PL + tenodesis PL → PB** · proximal tenodesis just above fibular groove, distal just proximal to cuboid\
✗ Wrong options: PL / PB repair (chronic, no excursion) · PB allograft

**PL tear (MRI)** — **peroneus longus repair**\
✗ Wrong options: SPR repair + groove deepening (only if intact + dislocated)

**Failed PB repair, both tendons torn** — **FDL transfer to 5th MT** → balance + hindfoot mobility\
✗ Wrong options: **subtalar fusion** (salvage) · **PTT transfer** (loses inversion + arch) · **excise both** → imbalance → **forefoot varus**

**Persistent pain after boot + PT, eversion weakness** — **SPR repair + peroneal tendon repair / tenodesis**\
✗ Wrong options: recast · syndesmotic fixation · OLT excision · biopsy

**Tendoscopy** — viewing portal **2 cm distal**, working portal **3 cm proximal** to fibular tip

### Hindfoot varus – correction to prevent failure

**Hindfoot varus** — **risk factor for failure / recurrence** of peroneal stabilisation + repair → correct it\
✗ Wrong options: bunion · tarsal coalition · accessory navicular · talar OCD

**Correction** — **calcaneal osteotomy (lateral closing wedge, Dwyer)** or **lateral closing-wedge subtalar arthrodesis** (rigid / subtalar arthritis)

**PB tear + cavovarus NOT correcting on Coleman block** — **calcaneal osteotomy + 1st ray dorsiflexion osteotomy + PL → PB transfer**\
✗ Wrong options: 1st ray osteotomy alone (Coleman non-correcting) · PB repair alone · **PRP** · **amniotic augmentation**

**Supple hindfoot, chronic PB tear** — contributor = **hindfoot alignment (cavovarus)**

### Calcaneal malunion – subfibular impingement

**Anterior ankle pain + ↓ dorsiflexion** — horizontal talus impinges on tibia → **subtalar bone-block arthrodesis**

**Lateral pain** — peroneal dislocation, **subfibular impingement**, subtalar arthritis → **lateral wall exostectomy**

**Malunion + subtalar arthrosis + exostosis + height loss** — **subtalar bone-block arthrodesis + lateral wall exostectomy ± Achilles lengthening**\
✗ Wrong options: PTT transfer · Achilles lengthening + exostectomy alone · SPR repair ± sural neurolysis (only for isolated incompetent SPR) · TTC nail (needs tibiotalar arthritis)

| Stephens–Sanders type | Treatment (OB) |
|---|---|
| **I** | **Lateral wall exostectomy + peroneal tenolysis** |
| **II** | + **subtalar bone-block arthrodesis** (bone graft) |
| **III** | + **closing-wedge calcaneal osteotomy** for varus, or triple arthrodesis |

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Sural neuroma** | **Most common** complication after surgery |
| **Recurrent instability** | **Unaddressed malalignment (hindfoot varus)** → corrective osteotomy ± soft-tissue reconstruction |
| **Persistent pain** | **Over-tightened SPR** → tendon stenosis |
| **Tibial nerve compression** | After **FHL transfer** → release FHL–NV bundle adhesions |
| **FHL / FDL transfer** | Residual **eversion weakness** |
| **Bone block** | Fracture, nonunion, tendon irritation / adherence |

| | **Peroneal instability (SPR)** | **Peroneal tear** |
|---|---|---|
| Exam | Snapping / subluxation with **DF + eversion**, circumduction | Retrofibular tenderness, pain on resisted eversion, **no instability** |
| X-ray | **Fleck sign** | Usually normal (PL: os peroneum migration) |
| Surgery | **SPR repair ± groove deepening** | Tubularise / tenodesis / graft / transfer |

| | **SPR** | **Inferior peroneal retinaculum** |
|---|---|---|
| Attachment | Distal posterior fibula → Achilles sheath / lateral calcaneus | **Peroneal tubercle** of calcaneus |
| Role | **Primary restraint** to subluxation | Separates tendons into 2 sheaths |

|---|---|---|---|---|
