# Diabetic Foot Ulcers

*Foot & Ankle · 16 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **\~12% of diabetics** have foot ulcers · **commonest medical complication** making diabetics seek treatment

**Amputation** — ulcers cause **\~85% of lower-extremity amputations** · diabetes = **15–40× ↑ amputation risk** · **diabetic foot ulceration = most likely predictor of eventual amputation**

**PVD** — **60–70%** of patients with diabetes > 10 yr · **> 60% of diabetic ulcers** have ↓ blood flow

**Diabetic foot infection** — cause of amputation in **25–50%** · osteomyelitis ↑ amputation risk **up to 4×**

**Charcot** — **7.5% of neuropathic diabetics** · neuropathy in 30–50% of diabetics, neuropathic fracture only 1–2.5%

### Pathophysiology

**Neuropathy** — **largest effect** · **sensory → loss of protective sensation = primary risk factor** for ulcer · **autonomic → dry skin** (no glandular function) · motor → intrinsic dysfunction → dynamic toe deformity

**Angiopathy** — **lesser effect** than neuropathy

**Mechanical** — **equinus → forefoot (MT head) overload** · ↓ MTPJ motion → ↑ IP pressure on hallux · **peroneus longus overpull → plantarflexed 1st MT** → 1st MT head ulcer

**Three pillars of treatment** — **adequate blood supply** · **treat infection** · **pressure relief**

### Risk factors for ulceration & amputation (neuropathy, smoking)

**Most likely to ulcerate** — **cannot feel 5.07 Semmes-Weinstein monofilament** — vascular + nutrition tests predict **healing**, not ulcer formation\
✗ Wrong options: TcpO2 30 · ABI < 0.6 · albumin 3.1 · autonomic dry skin · Silfverskiöld result

**Concurrent variables in amputees** — **ulceration 84%** · neuropathy 64% · infection 59% · gangrene 55% · ischaemia 46% · + ↑ HbA1c, ↓ oxygen tension, ↓ ABI

**Least likely to predict amputation** — **ABI 1.07** (normal)\
✗ Wrong options: ulcer · monofilament loss · infection · HbA1c 10.7

**Only consistent modifiable risk factors** — **smoking + glycaemic control** (Rossboth)

**Single best programme to cut amputations** — **smoking cessation with counselling + pharmacotherapy**\
✗ Wrong options: annual plantar-pressure screening + custom footwear · mandatory TCC for all · routine arterial US · GLP-1 weight-loss clinic

**Infection risk factors** — ulcer **> 30 days** · recurrent ulcer · trauma · **probe to bone** · PAD

### Microbiology

**Usually polymicrobial** — **aerobic gram-positive cocci (S. aureus) commonest** · **gram-negatives ↑ in chronic wounds / recent antibiotics** · **obligate anaerobes with ischaemia / gangrene**

**Gold standard** — **deep cultures + bone biopsy** guide management

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — often **painless**

**Exam** — **depth (probe for bone)** · infection (cellulitis, pus, gangrene) · **Silfverskiöld** · **DP + PT pulses** · hair on toes (absent = poor flow)

**Signs of infection blunted** — ↓ WBC function, PAD, poor metabolic control, neuropathy · commonest signs = **↑ exudate + worsening glycaemic control** · ↑ WBC / ESR / CRP / fever **absent in 50%**

| Ulcer location | Driver | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Plantar MT heads (1st / 2nd)** | **Equinus** | **TCC + TAL / gastrocnemius recession** |
| **1st MT head** | Equinus **+ PL overpull** | **Gastrocsoleus recession + PL → PB transfer** |
| **Plantar hallux IP joint** | **Stiff / rigid MTPJ** | **Keller resection arthroplasty** |
| **Toe tip (apical)** | **Flexible claw / hammer toe** | **Percutaneous flexor tenotomy** |
| **Plantar midfoot** | **Charcot rocker-bottom** | **Exostectomy + TAL** |
| **Heel** | Bed-bound pressure · calcaneal osteomyelitis | Offloading / padding boots · **partial calcanectomy + TAL** |
| Medial malleolus | **Venous stasis** (rarely diabetic) | — |
| Dry toe gangrene | **Arterial insufficiency** | Toe disarticulation |

### Silfverskiöld test

**Technique** — **forefoot inverted, hindfoot subtalar neutral** (locks transverse tarsal joints) · compare passive DF **knee flexed vs extended**

| Finding | Contracture | Procedure |
|---|---|---|
| **DF improves with knee flexion** | **Isolated gastrocnemius** | **Gastrocnemius recession (Strayer)** |
| **DF equal with knee flexed + extended** | **Gastrocsoleus / Achilles** | **Achilles tendon lengthening (Hoke)** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnostic criteria

| Condition | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **Neuropathy (TO)** | **10 g (5.07 Semmes-Weinstein) monofilament** not perceived **all 3 times** (TO) |
| **Osteomyelitis** | **Bone biopsy with cultures + pathology = gold standard** · **exposed bone → presume osteomyelitis** |
| **Infected ulcer** | ↑ exudate · ↑ WBC / ESR / CRP · worsening glucose · erythema **persisting despite elevation** |
| **Healing potential** | **TcpO2 = gold standard** (see labs) |

**Monofilament technique (TO)** — **10 g monofilament** · eyes closed · **90° to skin**, pressed **until it buckles 1 cm** · patient says "yes" · **repeat 3 times** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: 1 g · 5 g · 1 g polyfilament

**Monofilament accuracy** — sensitivity **41–93% / 57–93%**, specificity **68–100% / 75–100%** · **3-site test: plantar great toe, 3rd MT, 5th MT** (Feng)

### Genes & lab markers – healing predictors (vascular studies & nutritional labs)

| Marker | Favours healing | Poor healing / risk |
|---|---|---|
| **TcpO2** (transcutaneous oxygen) | **> 30 mmHg** (or **40**, by source) — **gold standard** for healing potential | **< 30–40** · **< 20** · **15**, 24, 25 mmHg = poor |
| **ABI** | **> 0.45** (Orthobullets, most OB) · **> 0.60** to heal an ulcer (one OB) — read the options | **< 0.45** · **falsely ↑ by arterial calcification** (↓ compliance) |
| **Toe pressure** | **> 40 mmHg** (OB) · **> 45 amputation, > 60 ulcer** (Orthobullets) | **< 30 mmHg** (amputation wound risk) |
| **Albumin** | **> 3.0 g/dL** | **< 3.0** (most) · **< 3.5** (one OB) — read the options |
| **Total lymphocyte count** | **> 1,500 /mm³** | **< 1,500** |
| **Haemoglobin** (amputation) | **> 11.0 g/dL** | < 11 |
| **Total protein** (amputation) | **> 6.2 g/dL** | < 6.2 |
| **HbA1c** | — | **> 8.0** |
| Doppler / exam | **Triphasic waveforms**, hair on toes | Absent pulses, no hair |
| **Systolic BP > 140** | **Not predictive** | — |

**Next test with absent pulses** — ABI + TcpO2 (noninvasive vascular) · with **palpable pulses → Silfverskiöld**

**Pre-TMA labs — which is the risk?** — **albumin 2.9 g/dL**\
✗ Wrong options: Hb 12.5 · total protein 6.9 · TLC 1,700 · toe pressure 55

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use / sign |
|---|---|
| **X-ray** (AP, lateral, oblique foot + ankle) | Demineralisation · **periarticular erosions** · **cortical erosions = late, nonspecific** (confused with Charcot) · abnormal film LR+ 2.3 |
| **MRI** | **Best for abscess vs soft-tissue swelling** · **most sensitive for osteomyelitis** (↑ T2 marrow signal) · LR+ 3.8 / LR– 0.14, accuracy 89% · **hard to separate infection vs Charcot** · less accurate in **ischaemic** ulcers |
| **Bone scan** (Tc-99m, Ga-67, In-111) | Separates **soft-tissue infection vs osteomyelitis vs Charcot** · **↑ indium-labelled WBC = osteomyelitis** · **leukocyte scintigraphy** best for infection vs Charcot |
| **Charcot X-ray** | **Midfoot destruction, fragmentation, osteopenia, subluxation** · rocker-bottom |

**Midfoot plantar ulcer, metformin + gabapentin — X-ray?** — **Charcot midfoot**\
✗ Wrong options: Lisfranc · hallux valgus · CMT cavus · flatfoot without fragmentation

### Osteomyelitis – bone biopsy diagnosis

| Finding | Value |
|---|---|
| **Probe to bone** | **67%** osteomyelitis (Orthobullets) · **\~70%** / nearly ⅔ (OB) · **most specific clinical finding** · sens **66%**, spec **85%**, PPV 89%, NPV 56% · LR+ 6.4 |
| **Exposed bone** | Highly predictive — presume osteomyelitis |
| **Ulcer > 2 cm²** | LR+ 7.2 |
| **ESR > 70 mm/h** | LR+ **11** |
| **Bone biopsy (culture + histology)** | **Most accurate / most specific / gold standard** |
| **Wound / swab culture** | **Positive at all stages → not predictive** of osteomyelitis |
| CBC | Unhelpful (immunocompromise, vascular insufficiency) |
| CRP / ESR | Helpful, **not diagnostic** |

**No single history / exam finding excludes osteomyelitis**

**✗ Biopsy traps** — HbA1c > 15% · ESR + CRP · probe-to-bone · cortical erosions · CBC · technetium scan · WB radiographs

### Wagner classification & monofilament screening (TO)

| Grade | Orthobullets | TO table | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Skin intact, bony deformity — "**foot at risk**" | No open lesion; may have deformity or cellulitis | **Shoe modification + serial exams** |
| **1** | **Superficial** ulcer | Superficial, **partial or full thickness** (not exposing ligament, deep fascia, bone) | **Office debridement + TCC** |
| **2** | **Deeper, full-thickness** extension | To **ligament, tendon, joint capsule, deep fascia**; **no abscess / osteomyelitis** | **Operative debridement + TCC** |
| **3** | **Deep abscess or osteomyelitis** | + **joint sepsis** | **Operative debridement + TCC** (I&D + antibiotics before casting) |
| **4** | **Partial forefoot gangrene** | Gangrene of portion of forefoot **or heel** | **Local vs larger amputation** |
| **5** | **Extensive gangrene** | Entire foot | **Amputation** |

**Exposed tendons, no bone / abscess (TO)** — **Wagner grade 2** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: 1 · 3 · 4

**TO wording conflict** — TO text: ulcers "breaking through superficial fascia… with **exposed bone or tendon** = grade 2" · TO table + Orthobullets: **abscess / osteomyelitis = grade 3** — read the options

### Brodsky depth-ischaemia classification

| Depth | Definition | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | At-risk foot, no ulcer | Education, accommodative footwear, regular exams (**shoe modification**) |
| **1** | Superficial, not infected | **Offload — TCC**, walking brace, special footwear |
| **2** | **Deep, exposing tendons / joints** | Surgical debridement, wound care, offload, culture-specific antibiotics |
| **3** | **Extensive ulcer or abscess** (exposed bone, osteomyelitis) | Debridement / partial amputation, offload, culture-specific antibiotics |

| Ischaemia | Definition | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Not ischaemic | — |
| **B** | Ischaemia, no gangrene | Noninvasive vascular testing → **angioplasty / bypass** |
| **C** | **Partial forefoot gangrene** | Vascular reconstruction + **partial foot amputation** |
| **D** | **Complete gangrene** | Full vascular work-up + **major amputation** |

## 5 Management

```
Diabetic foot ulcer
├─ Infected / abscess / osteomyelitis → I&D + bone culture → culture-specific abx
│      └─ infection cleared → TCC ± TAL
├─ Clean plantar ulcer, perfused → wound care + TCC
│      └─ forefoot + equinus → add TAL / gastrocnemius recession
├─ Ischaemic → vascular studies → revascularise
└─ Gangrene → amputation (level by PT pulse + labs)
```

```
Forefoot ulcer with limited dorsiflexion
├─ Pulses absent → ABI / TcpO2 first
├─ DF improves with knee flexion → gastrocnemius recession (Strayer)
└─ No improvement with knee flexion → Achilles tendon lengthening (Hoke)
```

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **I&D + antibiotics before casting** | **Wagner ≥ 3** · deep full-thickness ulcer failing TCC |
| **Flexor tendon tenotomy** | **Flexible toe deformity + toe-tip ulcer** (high healing **if no osteomyelitis**) |
| **TAL / gastrocnemius recession** | **Plantar forefoot ulcer + equinus** (DF ≤ 5°) |
| **Ostectomy ± TAL** | **Bony prominence** causing internal pressure (TAL if tight Achilles) |
| **Partial calcanectomy ± TAL** | **Large heel ulcer + calcaneal osteomyelitis** |
| **Keller resection arthroplasty** | **Plantar hallux IPJ ulcer + stiff MTPJ, failed TCC** |
| **Deformity correction + TAL** | **Nonplantigrade, rigid Charcot deformity** with recurrent ulcers |
| **Syme amputation** | **Forefoot gangrene + palpable posterior tibial pulse** |

### Total contact casting, offloading & NPWT

**Wound care** — **first line** · moist environment · absorb exudate · barrier · offload

**TCC** — **gold standard for mechanical relief of plantar ulcers** · snug below-knee · protects **insensate** limb, keeps patient ambulatory · lower infection, admission, lost income

**TCC contraindications** — **absolute: infection** · relative: marginal arterial supply, poor compliance with cast care, cast claustrophobia

**TCC duration** — changed every **3–4 wk**, prevents daily wound care · total **several months (up to 4 months)** · 83% heal, 17% complications (skin irritation)\
✗ Wrong options: ≤ 4 weeks

**Recurrence after TCC** — typically **3–4 wk after removal** = **greatest risk within 1 month** (newly healed tissue weak to vertical + shear stress) · most within first 6 months

**Sequence** — **TCC (non-removable) → CROW (removable) → custom shoe / orthotic**

**Clean 3-month plantar ulcer, no bone, normal labs** — **total contact casting**\
✗ Wrong options: TCC + empiric IV antibiotics · debridement + antibiotics · CROW

**Infected heel ulcer — NOT indicated** — **total contact casting** · indicated: surgical debridement, tighter glycaemic control, imaging (MRI), culture-directed antibiotics

**✗ TCC traps** — replace weekly with active infection · cast ≤ 4 wk · avoid in insensate feet · custom insoles equally effective (orthoses **prevent**, TCC **treats**) · orthotics > TCC (TCC heals faster, compliance)

**Non-removable > removable** — non-removable casts heal better than removable casts / dressings · **pneumatic walker** = alternative, better wound surveillance, but **removable → compliance**; no evidence of superiority

**NPWT** — **↓ ulcer size + time to healing** · for perfused, uninfected, offloaded ulcers failing standard care / deep after debridement · granulation, ↓ oedema, removes exudate, ↑ local flow\
✗ Wrong options: custom shoes · electrophysical therapy · hyperbaric oxygen (mixed) · topical PDGF

**Without offloading** — debridement or NPWT alone **won't** heal · VAC does **not** reduce recurrence

### Achilles / gastrocnemius lengthening for forefoot ulcers (Silfverskiöld)

**Rationale** — equinus → cyclic forefoot overload → MT head ulcers · percutaneous TAL **↓ forefoot peak pressure \~25%**

**Mueller RCT (TCC ± TAL, DF ≤ 5°)** — healing **100% vs 88%** (no difference) · recurrence **↓ 75% at 7 mo, ↓ 52% at 2 yr** · **81% vs 38%** recurrence at 2 yr

**Lengthening effect** — **↓ recurrence**, not faster healing of active ulcers vs TCC alone · Colen **25% vs 2%** recurrence (94% RRR) — avoid over-lengthening

**TAL + TCC vs TCC alone** — **lower forefoot ulcer recurrence at 2 years** (✗ Wrong options: higher / same · ↑ BKA) · **↑ heel peak pressure → more heel-pad breakdown** (✗ Wrong options: lower)

**Static calf stretching** — **no effect** on ROM or plantar pressure

**Gastrocnemius recession (Strayer) benefits most** — **chronic plantar forefoot ulcer**\
✗ Wrong options: peroneal tendinitis · Freiberg · retrocalcaneal bursitis · calcaneal osteomyelitis

| Scenario | Answer | ✗ Traps |
|---|---|---|
| Forefoot ulcer, no bone, no infection, **palpable pulses** — what next? | **Silfverskiöld test** | MRI, ABI, TcpO2, HbA1c |
| Wagner 1, **failed TCC 8 mo**, gastrocnemius contracture | **Strayer procedure** | Integra + STSG, STSG, more TCC, Weil osteotomy |
| Bilateral **5th MT head** ulcers 2 yr, minimal DF | **Gastrocnemius release + wound care** | Plastazote cut-out, HBO + NWB, healing shoe, **TMA (salvage)** |
| **1st MT head** grade II, failed orthosis + TCC, no deformity | **Gastrocsoleus recession + PL → PB transfer** | Plastazote, 1st MT head resection, ray / TMA |
| DF 10° short, **no change with knee flexion**, failed 3 mo TCC | **Achilles lengthening** | 3 more months TCC, debridement, NPWT, MT head excision |
| MT head ulcer — heals + ↓ recurrence | **Achilles lengthening procedure** | Keep HbA1c 9.2, TcpO2 24, **narrow toe box**, flexor tenotomy |
| Progressed to toe gangrene, no Silfverskiöld change — early step? | **Achilles tendon lengthening** | FDL tenotomy, **isolated gastrocnemius recession**, Keller, pneumatic walker first |
| Healed recurrent forefoot ulcer, rigid equinus | **Ostectomy + Achilles tendon lengthening** | Ostectomy + gastrocnemius recession, revascularisation, TCC in neutral |

**Midfoot / forefoot gastrocsoleus recession** — 10/11 midfoot + 19/20 forefoot healed · add **PL lengthening (1st MT head)** or **tib post lengthening (5th MT head)** (Laborde)

### Osteomyelitis – treatment

**Highest success** — **IV antibiotics based on percutaneous bone biopsy culture sensitivity** · only factor affecting remission · remission only **64% at 1 yr**\
✗ Wrong options: empiric IV vancomycin · oral levofloxacin · ulcer-swab sensitivities · elevation + NWB

**Deep full-thickness ulcer, failed 2 mo TCC, erythema persists despite elevation** — **intra-operative debridement + irrigation with culture** → then antibiotics + casting\
✗ Wrong options: continue TCC · 6 wk empiric IV · office wound culture (nonspecific) · skin graft

**Surgery** — **remove infected + necrotic bone** · pathogens **polymicrobial or S. aureus**

**Greatest predictor of failure → amputation** — **presence of osteomyelitis** (e.g. sesamoid) · nonop sesamoid osteomyelitis → **15/18 needed surgery**\
✗ Wrong options: first ulcer (first ulcers heal better) · HbA1c 7.2 · TcpO2 48 · TAL

### Surgical offloading – exostectomy, deformity correction, phalangeal resection, partial calcanectomy

**Wagner 3 heel ulcer + calcaneal osteomyelitis, failed debridement + TCC** — **partial calcanectomy** (+ bone biopsy, debridement, antibiotics) · preserves limb length, ↓ morbidity vs Syme / BKA\
✗ Wrong options: ankle disarticulation · BKA · fasciocutaneous / free flap (only for uninfected ulcers)

**Partial calcanectomy technique** — remove posterior process; cut **1 cm posterior to subtalar + CC joints** · **Achilles released, retracts** · custom-moulded orthosis · 10/12 healed

![Partial calcanectomy — line of resection](../../MCQ/attachments/q751-illa-partial-calacanectomy.jpg)

**Charcot plantar midfoot ulcer, recurrent 2 yr, no osteomyelitis** — **midfoot exostectomy + Achilles tendon lengthening** (+ debridement, TCC)\
✗ Wrong options: CROW · VAC · in-situ midfoot / subtalar fusion (none ↓ recurrence)

**Nonplantigrade rigid Charcot, ulcer under talar head** — **surgical correction of deformity + TAL + therapeutic footwear** · recurrence likely unless corrected\
✗ Wrong options: depth-inlay shoes + orthosis · CROW · TAL + TCC + CROW · Syme

**Charcot surgery goal** — **stable, plantigrade, ulcer-free foot** · ulcer + deep infection → **15% major amputation**

**Hallux IP plantar ulcer, rigid MTPJ, failed 3× TCC** — **Keller: resect base of proximal phalanx** → restores MTPJ motion, ↓ IP pressure · K-wire keeps plantigrade toe, **5 mm MTP gap**, prevents cock-up\
✗ Wrong options: CROW · distal phalanx base · proximal phalanx head · MT head

### Toe-tip ulcers – flexor tenotomy

**Indication** — **apical toe ulcer + passively correctable claw / hammer toe** · dynamic **flexor stabilisation** (toes curl at heel lift) linked to apical ulcers · prophylactic with callus

**Outcome** — healing **> 95%** · complications **< 15%** · incision wound complications **< 5%** · **office / outpatient**, percutaneous needle

**Known complication** — **similar ulcer on adjacent toe (transfer lesion)** **8%** → adjacent tenotomy

**✗ Tenotomy traps** — > 50% wound complications · **MT head ulcers** (persist → gastrocnemius recession) · osteomyelitis irrelevant (failures mostly had osteomyelitis) · needs inpatient stay

### Footwear & orthoses (therapeutic shoe bill, rocker sole, depth-inlay, Plastazote)

**Shoe modification** — **prevention** (Brodsky 0 / Wagner 0) · deep / wide shoes, custom insoles, rocker soles · wide toe box + rocker → better healing, ↓ recurrence

**Best forefoot offloading shoe** — **rocker sole**\
✗ Wrong options: polyethylene foam insole · open-toe sandals · custom in-depth shoes · hard post-op shoe

**Healed first Wagner II ulcer + hammertoe, insensate** — moderate risk → **depth-inlay shoes + custom accommodative foot orthosis**\
✗ Wrong options: nothing until second ulcer · Oxford rubber-sole shoes · DF MT osteotomy · TAL

**Plastazote** — **cross-linked polyethylene** · **surface area distribution → ↓ peak pressure** · **heating + moulding ↓ properties** (stiffer)\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ stiffness · no cross-linking · ↑ peak pressure

### Amputation level selection

**Forefoot gangrene + palpable PT pulse, not septic** — **Syme amputation** → highest function\
✗ Wrong options: TMA (too near infected tissue, only to clear infection first) · BKA · AKA · debridement + antibiotics

**Syme** — **ankle disarticulation, malleoli removed, heel pad anchored** · heel pad fed by **PT artery** branches · needs **patent PT artery + stable heel pad** · success **85%** with palpable PT pulse vs 1/4 without · **90%** with triphasic / normal PT vs **57%**

**Sepsis resolved, albumin 1.9, ABI 0.70** — **local wound care, parenteral antibiotics, metabolic support, re-evaluate in 1 week** · albumin **≥ 2.5 g/dL** → good Syme healing\
✗ Wrong options: bypass · Syme now · guillotine / closed transtibial

**Most likely to fail + need revision** — **transmetatarsal amputation** in uncontrolled diabetic (**HbA1c > 8**) with forefoot ulcer · **HbA1c = main predictor of TMA success**, then need for debridement\
✗ Wrong options: long / short transtibial · transfemoral · Syme

**Surgical site infection** — complicated diabetics **7.25×** vs non-diabetics

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Ulcer recurrence after TCC** | **3–4 wk** after cast removal |
| **Heel-pad breakdown** | After TAL (↑ heel pressure) |
| **Transfer lesion** | Adjacent toe after flexor tenotomy (8%) |
| **TMA failure** | HbA1c > 8, persistent forefoot ulcer |
| **Amputation** | Infection 25–50% · osteomyelitis ↑ 4× · Charcot ulcer + infection 15% |
| **Partial calcanectomy** | Needs custom orthosis to cushion heel |
