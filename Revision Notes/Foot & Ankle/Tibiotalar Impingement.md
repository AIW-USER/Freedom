# Tibiotalar Impingement

*Foot & Ankle · 46 / 48*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — anterior ankle pain, **most often osteophyte impingement** in anterior tibiotalar joint · **"footballer's ankle"** (high incidence in soccer players)

**Who** — athletes on **turf / grass** or needing **maximum dorsiflexion**: **rugby, football, soccer, dancers (ballet), wrestlers**

**Associated (OB)** — **lateral ankle instability**, **osteochondral lesions**, **gastrocnemius contracture** → look for + treat concomitantly

## 2 Mechanism

**Causes** — **repetitive overuse** · trauma · degenerative sequelae · excess **anterolateral soft tissue** · posterior soft-tissue / osseous abnormalities

**Pain generator (OB)** — spurs of **talar neck + anterior tibia rarely touch** (cadaver) → **anterior synovial + capsular tissue impingement** is the source

| Subtype (OB) | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Anteromedial** | **Intra-articular exostoses of anterior tibia + talar neck** pinch interposed synovium / capsule → chronic inflammation |
| **Anterolateral** | **AITFL catching on talus** · or **after ATFL sprain** → **fibrotic tissue in anterolateral gutter** |

**Sources of anterior impingement pain** — **AITFL** · **tibial osteophyte** · **inflamed synovium + capsule** · **talar neck exostosis**\
✗ Wrong options: **os trigonum** = accessory ossification near **posterolateral talar process** → **posterior** impingement

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **anterior ankle pain** · worse **crouching** / squatting

**Exam** — **pain with forced dorsiflexion** · **limited dorsiflexion** (e.g. passive DF to **10°** reproduces pain) · **anterior joint-line point tenderness** · soft-tissue swelling, effusion · **subtalar joint pain-free**

**Anterolateral soft-tissue impingement** — old **ankle sprain** · pain **anterolaterally at end-arc passive dorsiflexion** · **normal talar drawer** · **no** tenderness at fibula, ATFL, CFL · no posterior pain with passive PF

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| **X-ray: AP, lateral, oblique** | **Spurs on anterior distal tibia** + **dorsal talus (talar neck)** · dorsiflexed lateral shows contact |
| **Oblique view** | Reveals **anteromedial talar spurs** · better than standard series (Tol & van Dijk) |
| **CT** | **Extent of bony osteophytes** |
| **MRI** | **Spurring + joint fluid** · fluid-sensitive: **anterior capsular thickening + tibial osteophyte** |
| **MR arthrogram** | **Anterolateral gutter soft-tissue mass** · MRI **78.9% accuracy** for anterolateral soft-tissue impingement (Ferkel) |

## 5 Management

```
Anterior ankle impingement
├─ First line → PT, activity / lifestyle change, NSAIDs, bracing
│      (steroid injection → soft-tissue impingement + synovitis pain)
├─ Failed nonop, bony spurs ± mild arthritis → arthroscopic debridement / cheilectomy
├─ Failed nonop, anterolateral soft tissue → arthroscopy + synovial debridement
└─ Severe arthritis → ankle arthrodesis (gold standard)
```

### Anterior bony impingement – arthroscopic debridement

**Rugby player, painful limited DF, tibial spur on dorsiflexed X-ray** — **arthroscopic tibial debridement**\
✗ Wrong options: chondral drilling · modified Broström · os trigonum excision · tibiotalar arthrodesis

**Impingement + mild arthritis** — **anterior ankle cheilectomy / debridement** (arthroscopic or open) · many still need **open approach** (Talusan)

**Wrestler, failed PT + NSAIDs + bracing, MRI capsular thickening + spur** — **arthroscopic debridement**\
✗ Wrong options: os trigonum excision (posterior) · percutaneous Achilles repair · more proprioceptive rehab (already failed) · loose body removal (none seen)

**Arthroscopy** — debride **bony abnormalities + inflamed synovium** · effective alternative to open · anteromedial: **97% return to prior sport** (Murawski & Kennedy)

**Technique** — **supine**, **external traction**, leg on padded bump · **knife cuts skin only → hemostat spread** for portals · **adequate view before burring** anterior tibia (protect dorsal NV bundle)

**Severe arthritis** — **ankle arthrodesis = gold standard** · **93–94% fusion with cancellous screws** (74% overall, 93% low risk) (OB) · **\~90% union** (Orthobullets, ankle arthritis) — read the options · **TAA** = technically demanding alternative

### Anterolateral soft-tissue impingement

**Military officer, old sprain, anterolateral pain at end-DF, stable drawer, failed nonop** — **ankle arthroscopy with synovial debridement**\
✗ Wrong options: Broström-Gould (anatomic ATFL + CFL shortening / reinsertion + inferior extensor retinaculum) · Chrisman-Snook (non-anatomic split peroneus brevis) · syndesmosis screw (diastasis) · loose body removal

**Outcome** — partial synovectomy + lateral-gutter scar debridement → **good–excellent at 2 yr** (Ferkel)

### Evidence-based indications for ankle arthroscopy

| Evidence (Glazebrook) | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Fair → recommended** | **Osteochondral lesions** · **ankle arthrodesis** |
| Indicated | **Anterior ankle impingement** (isolated bony) |
| **Limited** | Instability · septic arthritis · arthrofibrosis · **loose body removal** |
| **Not effective → not recommended** | **Debridement of diffuse degenerative cartilage (ankle arthritis)** |

## 6 Complications

| Portal / step | Structure injured |
|---|---|
| **Anterolateral portal** | **Superficial peroneal nerve** |
| **Anteromedial portal** | **Saphenous vein** |
| **Spur burring of anterior tibia** | **Dorsal neurovascular bundle** |

## X vs Y

| | **Anterior impingement** | **Posterior impingement** |
|---|---|---|
| Painful motion | **Forced dorsiflexion** | **Passive plantarflexion** |
| Bony cause | Tibial / talar neck spurs | **Os trigonum** (posterolateral talar process) |
| Sport | Soccer, rugby, wrestling, ballet | — |

| | **Anteromedial** | **Anterolateral** |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | **Tibia + talar neck exostoses** | **AITFL** on talus / **post-ATFL-sprain gutter fibrosis** |
| Imaging | Oblique X-ray spurs | MR arthrogram gutter tissue |
| Surgery | Arthroscopic spur + synovial debridement | **Arthroscopic synovial debridement** |

## How it's asked

**"Rugby player, painful limited DF, anterior tibial spur — surgery?"** → **arthroscopic tibial debridement**

**"Wrestler, failed nonop, MRI capsular thickening + spur?"** → **arthroscopic debridement**

**"NOT a source of anterior impingement pain?"** → **accessory ossification near posterolateral talar process (os trigonum)**

**"Old sprain, anterolateral pain at end-DF, stable drawer?"** → **ankle arthroscopy with synovial debridement**

**"Ankle arthroscopy indications EXCEPT?"** → **debridement of diffuse degenerative ankle cartilage**

**"Best plain view for anteromedial talar spurs?"** → **oblique**

**"Portal injuring SPN?"** → **anterolateral**

**"Real pain generator in bony impingement?"** → **impinged synovium + capsule** (spurs rarely touch)
