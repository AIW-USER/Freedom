# Plantar Fasciitis

*Foot & Ankle · 39 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Demographics** — **men = women** · typical age **40–70** (OB) · common **bilaterally** · **posteromedial heel**

**Risk factors** — **obesity (↑ BMI)** · **↓ ankle dorsiflexion** (tight calf / foot musculature) in non-athletes · **weight-bearing endurance activity** (running, dancing) · steep ↑ training volume

### Diagnosis & anatomy

**Plantar fascia** — nonelastic multilayered **connective tissue** originating on the **medial calcaneal tubercle** → inserts on **metatarsal heads** · **supports the arch** · **windlass mechanism** in late stance\
✗ Wrong options: abductor hallucis origin · Baxter's nerve · Achilles insertion on calcaneal tuberosity · tibialis posterior on navicular tuberosity

**Shared origin on medial calcaneal tubercle** — **abductor hallucis, FDB, quadratus plantae** (may be inflamed too)

**Pathology** — **degenerative**, chronic overuse → **microtears at fascia origin** · recurrent inflammation + periostitis

**Heel spur** — may coexist · **not the cause**

### Associated conditions

**Heel pain triad** — **plantar fasciitis** + **posterior tibial tendon dysfunction** + **tarsal tunnel syndrome**

**Others** — **calcaneal apophysitis** · **gastrocnemius–soleus contracture** · femoral anteversion · pes cavus · pes planus

## 3 Presentation

**"Start-up" pain** — insidious **inferior heel pain on first steps out of bed** / after rest · may walk on toes first · **eases with ambulation** · worse at **end of day** after prolonged standing

**Exam** — **tender medial calcaneal tuberosity** (plantar medial / anteromedial heel) · **↑ with dorsiflexion of toes + foot** · **↓ ankle DF** (tight Achilles · gastrocnemius contracture on **Silfverskiöld**)

**Abductor hallucis origin tenderness** — small subset → **Baxter's nerve** (first branch of lateral plantar nerve = **inferior calcaneal nerve**) entrapment · **pain persists after activity** rather than improving

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **clinical**; normal radiographs\
✗ Wrong options: navicular stress fracture · Freiberg · Baxter's entrapment · anterior tarsal tunnel

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use |
|---|---|
| **Radiographs** | **Not necessary on first visit** · often normal · may show **plantar heel spur** · optional WB axial + lateral hindfoot |
| **MRI** | **Surgical planning** |
| **Bone scan** | Quantifies inflammation · **rules out stress fracture** |
| **Labs** | Not routine · if **inflammatory arthritis / infection** suspected |
| **EMG** | **Rule out nerve entrapment** |

## 5 Management

```
Plantar fasciitis
├─ First line → PF-specific + Achilles stretching, prefabricated insert, NSAIDs
├─ Persists > 6 wk → night splint (± steroid injection, cast)
├─ > 6 mo, failed → ESWT (FDA-approved)
└─ Failed 6–12 mo (9 mo) → partial plantar fasciotomy (medial ⅓)
       └─ + tarsal tunnel compression → open release + distal tarsal tunnel decompression
```

### First-line treatment – stretching, night splints & orthoses

**Best initial** — **Achilles + plantar fascia stretching** with **prefabricated shoe insert** / heel cups\
✗ Wrong options: short leg cast · steroid injection · custom orthotic · surgical release

**Plantar medial heel pain + calcaneal spur** — **Achilles stretching**\
✗ Wrong options: walker boot · steroid · 50% release · **heel spur resection**

**Highest satisfaction at 8 wk** — **plantar fascia–specific stretching** (recreates **windlass**)\
✗ Wrong options: isolated Achilles stretching · steroid · ESWT · tarsal tunnel decompression + release

**NWB plantar fascia–specific stretching > WB Achilles stretching** · all stretching programs **equal satisfaction at 2 yr**

**Prefabricated vs custom** — **prefabricated more effective** (with stretching) + cheaper (Orthobullets, OB) · "Level III evidence supports custom orthotics" (OB) — read the options

**Chronic plantar fasciitis orthosis** — **dorsiflexion night splint** = **only orthosis proven effective**\
✗ Wrong options: gel heel cushion · full-length prefabricated / accommodative insert · **Morton's extension** (1st MTP pathology) · **recessed first ray / cavus orthotic** (cavus foot) · semi-rigid medial post insert

![Dorsiflexion night splint](../../MCQ/attachments/q3834-e-figure-e.jpg)

**Plateau at 6 wk with NSAIDs + PT** — add **night splints**, continue PT\
✗ Wrong options: stop PT for custom orthotics · ESWT · endoscopic fasciotomy · bipolar radiofrequency

**Second tier (> 6 wk)** — **night splints**, repeat **corticosteroid injection**, **cast immobilisation** (short leg cast **8–10 wk**, Orthobullets)

**Symptom duration** — up to **6–12 months** to resolve

### Shock wave therapy

**ESWT** — **second line** · **chronic heel pain > 6 months** failing other treatment · **FDA approved** · painful · effective at 6-month follow-up · alternative to surgery once nonop exhausted

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Plantar fasciotomy** (open or arthroscopic / endoscopic) | **Persistent pain > 9 months** of failed conservative care (Orthobullets, AAOS) · **6–12 months** (OB) · after **6 months of second-tier** modalities (OB) |
| **Release + distal tarsal tunnel decompression** | **Concomitant tibial nerve compression** in tarsal tunnel → **open** procedure · **70–90% success** |
| **Gastrocnemius recession** | **No clear indications** established |
| **Baxter's nerve release** | Release deep fascia of **abductor hallucis** → may improve outcomes |

**Release extent** — **medial ⅓ to ⅔** of fascia only (OB: **medial third**) → protects **medial longitudinal arch**

**Avoid complete release** — arch destabilisation · **lateral column overload** · **dorsolateral foot pain**

**Fasciotomy outcomes** — complications common · protracted recovery

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Lateral plantar nerve injury** | Surgical |
| **Complete release** | **Medial longitudinal arch collapse** · ↑ stress **dorsolateral midfoot** |
| Chronic pain | — |
| **Heel pad (fat pad) atrophy** | **Steroid injection** → shoe padding |
| **Plantar fascia rupture** | **Athletes**, **minimalist runners**, **steroid injection** |

### Plantar fascia rupture – steroid injection & immobilisation

**Steroid mechanism** — **inhibit heel pad fibroblast proliferation** → **fat pad atrophy**\
✗ Wrong options: decrease inflammation (PF is degenerative) · no rupture association · good medium / long-term relief · US guidance prolongs relief (not needed)

**Steroid evidence** — ↓ **short-term** pain only · **no high-quality evidence** · risks: fat pad atrophy, **PF rupture**, local reaction · used rarely / second line

**Rupture presentation** — acute **pop** in runner after steroid shot → can't bear weight · **loss of medial arch → pes planus**\
✗ Wrong options: os trigonum (posterior pain) · pes cavus + peroneal subluxation · PTTI (no heel pain) · lateral talar process (snowboarders)

**Plantar fascia avulsion on MRI (TO)** — pole vaulter landing on heel → marrow oedema · **avulsion of lateral cord** · oedema of **abductor digiti minimi + FDB** (TO)

**Rupture treatment** — **cast immobilisation 3–6 wk** (OB) · **CAM boot** sufficient (OB) · stretching **after** immobilisation\
✗ Wrong options: urgent / delayed primary repair (no evidence) · stretching first · steroid

## X vs Y

| | **Plantar fasciitis** | **Baxter's nerve entrapment** | **Calcaneal stress fracture** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain pattern | **Start-up**, eases with walking | **Persists after activity** | Worse with WB |
| Tender | Medial calcaneal tubercle | **Abductor hallucis origin** | **Heel squeeze** |
| Test | Clinical | **EMG** | **Bone scan** / MRI |

| Orthosis | Use |
|---|---|
| **Dorsiflexion night splint** | **Chronic plantar fasciitis** (only proven) |
| **Morton's extension** | 1st MTP pathology |
| **Recessed first ray** | Cavus foot |
| Gel heel cushion / prefabricated insert | Acute PF with stretching; not proven in chronic |

## How it's asked

**"Start-up heel pain, BMI 35, gastrocnemius contracture, normal X-ray?"** → **plantar fasciitis**

**"Structure causing morning plantar heel pain?"** → **connective tissue originating on medial calcaneal tubercle**

**"Runner, anteromedial heel pain — best initial?"** → **Achilles + plantar fascia stretching with prefabricated insert**

**"Plantar heel pain with calcaneal spur — initial?"** → **Achilles stretching exercises**\
✗ Wrong options: spur resection

**"Highest satisfaction at 8 weeks?"** → **plantar fascia–specific stretching program**

**"Chronic PF, failed stretching — which orthosis?"** → **dorsiflexion night splint**

**"Orthosis proven in chronic PF?"** → **night splint**

**"Improved 40% then plateaued at 6 weeks?"** → **night splints**

**"ESWT indicated when?"** → **> 6 months failed nonop** (FDA)

**"Fasciotomy — how much to release?"** → **medial third** (avoid arch collapse)

**"PF + tarsal tunnel compression — surgery?"** → **open plantar fascia release + distal tarsal tunnel decompression**

**"Steroid injection — mechanism / efficacy?"** → **inhibits heel pad fibroblast proliferation**

**"Pop after steroid shot in marathoner — exam / diagnosis?"** → **pes planus, plantar fascia rupture**

**"First line for plantar fascia rupture?"** → **cast immobilisation for 3–6 weeks**
