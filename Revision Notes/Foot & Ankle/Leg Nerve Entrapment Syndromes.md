# Leg Nerve Entrapment Syndromes

*Foot & Ankle · 29 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Entrapment sites – overview

| Nerve | Site of compression | Name / clue | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ilioinguinal** | Hypertrophied abdominal muscles (intensive training) | Hyperaesthesia · pain ↑ with **hip hyperextension** | Surgical release if nonop fails |
| **Obturator** | Well-developed adductors (**skaters**) | Chronic **medial thigh pain** · **nerve conduction studies** establish diagnosis | Supportive |
| **Lateral femoral cutaneous** | Tight belts, prolonged hip flexion | **Meralgia paresthetica** (lateral thigh pain) | PT, NSAIDs, posture, release compressive devices |
| **Sciatic** | **Ischial tuberosity** · **piriformis** | **Piriformis syndrome** | — |
| **Saphenous** | **Adductor (Hunter's, subsartorial) canal** | **Surfer's neuropathy** · prolonged kneeling · pain **inferomedial to knee** | **Knee pads** first line |
| **Common peroneal** | **Behind fibula / fibular neck** — ganglion cyst, direct blow, pressure | Foot drop | **Proximal tibiofibular joint fusion** to prevent cyst recurrence |
| **Superficial peroneal** | **Fascial exit \~10–12 cm** (OB) · **12–15 cm** (Orthobullets) **proximal to lateral malleolus** | Dorsal foot numbness ↑ with **PF + inversion** | Observation → fascial release |
| **Deep peroneal** | **Inferior extensor retinaculum** | **Anterior tarsal tunnel syndrome** · **1st web space** | — |
| **Posterior tibial** | **Flexor retinaculum** / tarsal tunnel | **Tarsal tunnel syndrome** · medial + plantar foot | — |
| **1st branch lateral plantar (Baxter)** | **Abductor hallucis deep fascia vs medial quadratus plantae** | **Distal tarsal tunnel syndrome** · runners | **Release abductor hallucis fascia** |
| **Medial plantar** | **Knot of Henry** (FDL × FHL) · **foot orthotics** | **Jogger's foot** | **Discontinue orthosis** |
| **Sural** | **12–15 mm distal to fibular tip** (equinus) · gastrocnemius–Achilles musculotendinous junction (calf) | Lateral dorsum + heel | Surgical release |
| **Interdigital** | — | **Morton's neuroma** | — |

### Tarsal tunnel anatomy

**Tarsal tunnel** — fibro-osseous tunnel **directly inferior to medial malleolus** · roof = **flexor retinaculum** · **abductor hallucis = plantar border** · compression by fascial septae, flexor retinaculum, abductor hallucis

**Tibial nerve** — divides into **medial plantar**, **lateral plantar**, **medial calcaneal** branches · plantar nerves = **terminal sensory branches** → most plantar sensation

| Dissection label (medial ankle) | Structure |
|---|---|
| **A** | **1st branch of lateral plantar nerve (Baxter)** — **most commonly entrapped** |
| B | Medial calcaneal nerve branches |
| C | Tibial nerve |
| D | Lateral plantar nerve |
| E | Medial plantar nerve (jogger's foot) |

**Dorsal sensation** — **SPN = dorsum except 1st web space** · **DPN = 1st web space** · **sural = lateral foot**\
✗ Wrong options: plantar surface

### Saphenous nerve entrapment (TO)

**Saphenous nerve (TO)** — **purely sensory** · **longest terminal branch of posterior division of femoral nerve** · **L3–L4**

**Course (TO)** — from below inguinal ligament with femoral artery · **deep to sartorius** throughout thigh · enters **subsartorial / adductor / Hunter's canal** (proximal third of thigh, per TO) — fibrous band **vastus medialis ↔ adductor magnus**

**Exit (TO)** — joins **saphenous branch of descending genicular artery** · both **pierce roof of the canal just proximal to the adductor hiatus**

**Commonest entrapment site (TO)** — **adductor canal**\
✗ Wrong options: below inguinal ligament · at adductor hiatus · below patella

### Common peroneal nerve compression (TO)

**Why CPN (TO)** — **most fixed nerve, minimum tolerance to mobility** · fixed at origin **under biceps femoris** → fixed to **fibular neck** as it winds around

**Unconscious, lying in lateral position, bruised lateral knee (TO)** — **CPN** injury\
✗ Wrong options: tibial · ulnar · trigeminal

![Course of the common peroneal nerve behind biceps femoris and around the fibular neck](../../MCQ/attachments/TO17138089-2.png)

**Branches** — **CPN → DPN + SPN** · SPN in **lateral compartment** → supplies **peroneus longus + brevis** → dorsal foot sensation

## 3 Presentation

### Baxter's nerve (first branch of lateral plantar nerve)

**Who** — **running athlete** · normally arched or **cavus** foot · responsible for **\~⅕ of hindfoot pain**, widely missed

**Symptoms** — **chronic plantar medial heel pain** (often 9–12 months) · start-up pain like plantar fasciitis **plus neuritic burning pain unrelated to loading** · worse at end of day / prolonged activity

**Signs** — **tenderness at abductor hallucis origin** · **Tinel's just proximal to abductor hallucis → radiates to lateral foot / 5th MT base** · **no Tinel's over posterior tibial nerve** · chronic → **abductor digiti minimi atrophy**

**Innervates** — **abductor digiti quinti (minimi)** (OB) · also **FDB + quadratus plantae** (one OB)\
✗ Wrong options: abductor hallucis · FDL · FHL · plantar fascia

**Course** — **deep to abductor hallucis + FDB** · **superficial to quadratus plantae** · **medial to calcaneus** · **just anterior to medial calcaneal tuberosity** / just superior to plantar fascia insertion

**Compression point** — between **deep fascia of abductor hallucis** and **medial caudal margin of medial head of quadratus plantae** (Orthobullets writes "abductor hallucis longus")

**Mechanism** — stretching + tethering in abductor hallucis fascial leashes · hypertrophy of small foot muscles · hindfoot microtrauma in runners

### Superficial peroneal nerve entrapment

**Mechanism** — **inversion injury** · **fascial defect** (± muscle herniation)

**Site** — where it **exits deep crural fascia of anterolateral leg**, **\~12 cm** (Orthobullets) · **10–12 cm** (OB) proximal to lateral malleolus · lateral → anterior compartment

**Symptoms** — **numbness + tingling over dorsum of foot** · ↑ with **passive plantarflexion + inversion** · ± eversion weakness

**Sridhara signs** — ↓ dorsal sensation **sparing 1st web space** · **soft-tissue bulge \~10 cm above lateral malleolus** · **Tinel's over bulge** · bulge ↑ with resisted dorsiflexion / WB · tenderness over bulge or terminal branches

**Variable course** — SPN in **lateral compartment 72%**, anterior 23% (Barrett) · **anterior in almost 50%** (Rosson) → **explore both compartments**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key diagnostic findings

| Test | Finding |
|---|---|
| **EMG** | Lateral plantar nerve entrapment positive in **88%** of neurogenic subcalcaneal heel pain |
| **MRI** | **Abductor digiti minimi atrophy** → Baxter's neuropathy |
| **Nerve conduction studies** | Help establish **obturator** entrapment |
| Electrical studies | Confirm **peroneal compression at fibular neck** |

## 5 Management

```
Baxter's nerve entrapment
├─ First → rest, ice, PT, orthotics, NSAIDs
└─ Persistent > 3 months → release abductor hallucis fascia
       (good–excellent 89%)
```

```
Superficial peroneal nerve entrapment
├─ Most → observation
└─ Refractory / elite athlete → fascial release + SPN neurolysis
       └─ fascial defect → release, never close (worsens)
```

### Operative treatment

| Condition | Operation | ✗ Traps |
|---|---|---|
| **Baxter's** (distal tarsal tunnel) | **Release of abductor hallucis fascia** → decompresses **lateral plantar nerve** branch | partial plantar fasciotomy / gastrocnemius recession (= recalcitrant **plantar fasciitis**) |
| **SPN** | **Fascial release + superficial peroneal neurolysis** (80% satisfied) | **repair herniation + close fascial defect** · 4-compartment fasciotomy · neurectomy · lumbar discectomy |
| **CPN ganglion** | Excision · **proximal tibiofibular fusion** prevents recurrence | — |
| **Medial plantar** (jogger's foot) | **Stop orthosis** | — |
| **Saphenous** | **Knee pads** first line | — |
| **Sural** | Surgical release | — |

**Baxter release complications** — persistent heel pain needing more surgery · neuroma · superficial infection · RSD

## 6 Complications

### Peroneal nerve palsy – order of recovery

**Short leg cast → foot drop** — compression of **peroneal nerve at fibular neck**

**Last muscle to recover** — **extensor hallucis longus** (innervated **most distally**)\
✗ Wrong options: EDL · peroneus longus · tibialis anterior · FDL (tibial nerve)

## How it's asked

**"Runner, heel pain, Tinel's at abductor hallucis origin radiating laterally?"** → **compression of the first branch of the lateral plantar nerve (Baxter's nerve)**

**"Nerve to abductor digiti quinti compressed under abductor hallucis fascia?"** → **first branch of the lateral plantar nerve**

**"Baxter's nerve — muscle affected?"** → **abductor digiti minimi**

**"Abductor hallucis fascia release relieved heel pain — which nerve?"** → **lateral plantar nerve**

**"Anatomy of the Baxter nerve?"** → **runs deep to the flexor digitorum brevis**\
✗ Wrong options: superficial to AbH · deep to quadratus plantae · branch of medial plantar

**"Medial ankle dissection — nerve most commonly entrapped?"** → **A = 1st branch of lateral plantar nerve**

**"Inversion injury, dorsal foot numbness ↑ PF + inversion — cause?"** → **compression of the superficial peroneal nerve by fascial band proximal to lateral malleolus**

**"Commonest SPN compression site?"** → **proximal to lateral malleolus, exiting fascia of anterolateral leg**

**"Marathoner, Tinel's over distal lateral fibula bulge — surgery?"** → **fascial release and superficial peroneal neurolysis**

**"Foot drop after short leg cast — last to recover?"** → **extensor hallucis longus**

**"Unconscious in lateral position, bruised lateral knee — nerve?"** → **CPN** (TO)

**"Commonest site of saphenous nerve entrapment?"** → **adductor canal** (TO)
