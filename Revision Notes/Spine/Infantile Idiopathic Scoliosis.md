# Infantile Idiopathic Scoliosis

*Spine · 29 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — idiopathic scoliosis presenting at **≤ 3 years**

**Incidence** — **4%** of idiopathic scoliosis

**Sex** — **males > females** (**3:1**, OB)

**Curve** — usually **left thoracic**

**Risk factor** — family history\
✗ Wrong options: predicts progression

### Genes & associations

**Inheritance** — **autosomal dominant, variable penetrance**

**Associations** — **plagiocephaly**, congenital defects (DDH, congenital heart disease), **neural axis anomalies** (**22% of curves > 20°**; 80% of these need neurosurgery; **syrinx ~20%**), **thoracic insufficiency syndrome**

### Early-onset scoliosis (EOS)

**Definition (TO)** — curve **> 10° with onset before age 10**, any aetiology (GSSG / CSSG) · categories (Vitale): **idiopathic, neuromuscular, congenital, syndromic** (Marfan, Down)\
✗ Wrong options: "< 5 yrs only" · better prognosis than older children

**Growth** — **T1-L5 grows fastest in first 5 yrs**; thoracic height doubles birth → maturity · **thoracic spine height: 12 cm at birth, 18 cm at 5 yrs, 27 cm at maturity** · thoracic volume **6% at birth, 30% at 5, 50% at 10** · target **≥ 22 cm T1–T12** before fusion

**Lungs (TO)** — **alveolar number ↑ until ~age 8, THEN volumetric growth** · earlier onset + larger curve → pulmonary failure · infantile/juvenile or severely lordotic curves cause pulmonary problems; typical AIS does not\
✗ Wrong options: reverse (EXCEPT answer)

**Thoracic insufficiency syndrome** — ↓ thoracic growth + lung volume → pulmonary hypertension, **cor pulmonale** · PFT impairment > 60°, cardiopulmonary issues > 90°

## 3 Presentation

**History** — age noticed, progression, perinatal, milestones; **drooling** → neuro cause

**Inspection** — **café-au-lait** (NF), hair patch, **dimple above gluteal fold** (below = benign), nevi, **plagiocephaly**

**Neuro** — milestones, **cavovarus**, **asymmetric abdominal reflexes (syrinx)**, clonus, Hoffmann, Babinski, gait

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Types** — **resolving** (most) vs **progressive**

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **standing PA + lateral**; **supine if can't stand (underestimates curve)**; exclude congenital anomalies

| Measure | Technique | Progression risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Cobb** | Standard | **> 20°** → progression |
| **Rib phase** | Convex apical rib head vs apical body on AP | **Phase 1** = no overlap · **Phase 2 = overlap → high risk** (✗ Wrong options: "phase 1 worse" (TO)) |
| **RVAD (Mehta)** | Perpendicular to apical endplate; rib-vertebral angles via rib head–neck line; **concave − convex** | **> 20° → progression** (83%); **< 20° → resolves** (83%) (✗ Wrong options: > 10°) |

![Rib-vertebral angle difference (Mehta)](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2055-rvad4.jpg)

**Mehta predictors** — **Cobb > 20° · RVAD > 20° · phase 2 rib**\
✗ Wrong options: age · sex · family history

**MRI (whole spine)** — **first**, before treatment: tether, cyst, tumour, **syrinx** · abnormal abdominal reflexes or < 10 yrs with significant curve → **MRI spinal axis**\
✗ Wrong options: PT · brace · observe · fusion

## 5 Management

```
Infantile idiopathic scoliosis (MRI normal)
├─ Cobb < 30° (< 20–25°) + RVAD < 20° + phase 1 → OBSERVE (~90% resolve)
├─ Flexible, Cobb > 30° (> 20–25°), RVAD > 20°, phase 2 → SERIAL MEHTA (EDF) DEROTATIONAL CASTING
│      (or TLSO); brace after casting / late flexible
└─ Cobb > 50–60° or failed casting → GROWING RODS (dual / magnetic) or VEPTR
       (fusion delayed to ~10 yrs)
```

### Mehta casting

**Elongation-derotation-flexion (EDF) casting** — corrects in 3D while allowing growth · **start early**: before 18 months–4 yrs → better chance of curve < 15° (worse if started > 4 yrs) · smaller initial Cobb + good first in-cast correction predict success · may cure idiopathic infantile curves; in **syndromic EOS → delays surgery, curve may still progress** but spine keeps growing · complications: skin necrosis, infection

**1-yr-old, 35°, RVAD > 20°, phase 2** — **serial derotational casting**\
✗ Wrong options: TLSO · observe · fusion · VBT

**22-month, RVAD 26°** → casting indication\
✗ Wrong options: Cobb 18°/28° · RVAD 16°/5°

### Growing constructs

**Growing rods** — anchors proximal + distal; **lengthen every 6–8 months**; permit up to **5 cm** growth; ↑ **FVC** · traditional or **magnetic** · 5-yr failing casting with ↓ FEV1/FVC → **growing rods**\
✗ Wrong options: more casts · TLSO · fusion

**Fusion before age 10** — **pulmonary compromise** → delay

**VEPTR (TO)** — rib-based distraction (rib hooks proximally; spine/pelvic anchors distally) → **EOS with rib anomalies / TIS**, also neuromuscular (SMA) · **facilitates** infant use · pelvic anchors in ambulators → **crouched gait** (limit to non-ambulators)\
✗ Wrong options: "pelvic anchors make infant use difficult" (EXCEPT answer)

**Growth-friendly classes (Skaggs)** — **distraction**: TGR, VEPTR, MCGR · **compression**: vertebral body stapling, VBT · **guided growth**: Luque trolley, Shilla

## 6 Complications

**Surgery** — high complication rate · progressive untreated curves can be **fatal** · **progressive by age 5 → > 50% exceed 70°**

## X vs Y

| | Infantile | Juvenile | Adolescent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age | **0–3** | 4–10 | 10–18 |
| Sex | **Male** | F (older) | **Female** |
| Curve | **Left thoracic** | Right thoracic | Right thoracic |
| Natural history | Most **resolve** | Most progress | Variable |
| Key Rx | **Mehta cast** | Brace / growing rods | Brace / PSF |

## How it's asked

**"Suggests progression in infantile scoliosis?"** → **rib overlap of apical vertebra (phase 2)**\
✗ Wrong options: RVAD > 10° · male · family history

**"Indication for Mehta casting / TLSO?"** → **RVAD 26°**

**"RVAD calculation?"** → **concave angle − convex angle (X − Y)**

**"VEPTR — incorrect statement?"** → **"pelvic anchors make use in infants with TIS difficult"** (TO)

**"5-yr failing Mehta casts, ↓ FEV1/FVC?"** → **traditional or magnetic growing rods**

**"Mehta casting for syndromic EOS — expected?"** → **continued progression but further spine growth**

**"1-yr-old, 35°, RVAD > 20°, phase 2 rib?"** → **serial derotational casting**

**"22-month, 48°, RVAD 24°, MRI normal — true?"** → **earlier EDF casting → better chance of curve < 15°**

**"6-yr painless curve, asymmetric abdominal reflexes?"** → **MRI spinal axis**

**"Lungs and spinal deformity — EXCEPT?"** → **"volumetric growth until 8 then alveolar increase"** (TO — reverse)

**"EOS definition?"** → **scoliosis developing before age 10** (TO)
