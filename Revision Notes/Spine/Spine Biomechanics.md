# Spine Biomechanics

*Spine · 59 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Functional spinal unit

**FSU** — cephalad + caudad vertebrae, disc, facet joints · physiologic motion + neural protection · intradiscal pressure varies with position

**Stability** — no abnormal strain or excess motion under physiologic load · maintained by FSU, muscle tension, abdominal/thoracic pressure, rib cage

![Intradiscal pressure by posture](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2003-intradiscal-pressures.jpg)

### Denis three columns

| Column | Structures |
|---|---|
| **Anterior** | **ALL**, **anterior 2/3** of body + annulus |
| **Middle** | **PLL**, **posterior 1/3** of body + annulus |
| **Posterior** | Pedicles, facets, **ligamentum flavum**, spinous process, **PLC** |

**Instability** — middle column injury (**widened interpedicular distance**, loss of posterior body height) · PLC disruption + anterior and/or middle column injury · model only moderately reliable

![Denis three columns](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2003-combined-2.jpg)

### Ligaments

**Ten ligaments** — restrict FSU motion, absorb energy · all **type I collagen** except **ligamentum flavum (mostly elastin)** · viscoelastic, non-linear

### Posterior ligamentous complex

**Components** — **supraspinous**, **interspinous**, **ligamentum flavum**, **facet capsules**\
✗ Wrong options: facet joints · PLL

**Ruptured if** — bony Chance fracture, interspinous widening, progressive kyphosis on non-op care, facet diastasis · ambiguous: MRI signal between spinous processes

**TLICS** — PLC intact 0 / indeterminate / ruptured 3 · burst without deficit + intact PLC = 2 → **non-op (< 4)** · + ruptured PLC = 5 → **surgery (> 4)**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Sagittal balance

| Segment | Normal |
|---|---|
| Cervical lordosis | **20–40°** |
| Thoracic kyphosis | Average **35°** (20–50°) |
| **Lumbar lordosis** | Average **60°** (20–80°) · **75% at L4–S1**, **47% at L5-S1** |

**Gravity line** — centre of C2 → anterior T7 → mid T12-L1 disc → posterior to L3 → posterosuperior S1 · radiograph: **C7 plumb line** to posterosuperior S1

**Negative balance** — axis **behind** sacrum (lumbar hyperlordosis) · **positive** — axis **in front** (hip flexion contracture, **flat back**)

**Best radiographic predictor of good outcome after adult deformity surgery** — **sagittal balance correction**\
✗ Wrong options: coronal · rotation · listhesis · PI

**Pelvic incidence** — fixed angle; **correlates with lumbar lordosis, thoracic kyphosis, sagittal alignment** (PT + SS change with pelvic rotation)

![Negative sagittal balance](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2003-negative-sagittal-balance-obq11-49.jpg)

### Facet orientation & motion

| Region | Coronal / sagittal angle | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| **Cervical (C3-7)** | **0° coronal, 45° sagittal** | Flex-ext, lateral bend, rotation |
| **Thoracic** | **20° coronal, 55° sagittal** | Rotation; little flex-ext (ribs) · protects heart/lungs |
| **Lumbar** | **50° coronal, 90° sagittal** | **Flex-ext**, minimal rotation |

### Instantaneous axis of rotation

**Definition** — **theoretical axis about which an FSU rotates at an instant** · not fixed; moves with bending/rotation · **3 axes, 6 degrees of freedom**

**Pathology** — **degeneration, fracture, ligament injury shift the IAR** outside the FSU (enlarged) \
✗ Wrong options: fixed · 12 DOF · from AP/lateral films

![IAR in degenerative disc](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2003-iar-of-degenerative-disc.jpg)

**Not IAR** — CORA (deformity correction) · vertebral incidence (L5) · plumb line (C7)

### Pedicle anatomy

| Region | Fact |
|---|---|
| C2, C7 | Pedicle screws viable |
| C3–C6 | Small pedicles → **lateral mass screws** |
| Thoracic wall | **Medial wall 2× thicker than lateral** |
| **T4** | **Narrowest diameter** + **shortest (14 mm)** (T1 20 mm, T10 20 mm) |
| T7 | Narrow on concave side in AIS |
| T12 vs L1 | T12 larger than L1 |
| Transverse angle | **10° mid-thoracic → 30° L5** |
| Sagittal angle | **15–17° cephalad** thoracic · neutral lumbar · caudal at L5 |
| Lumbar | **L1 smallest lumbar diameter** · medial angulation L1 12°, L5 30°, S1 39° · S1 \~19 mm |
| Landmarks | Transverse process midpoint (cranio-caudal) · lateral pars border (medio-lateral) |

## 5 Management

### Instrumentation (TO)

| System | Approach |
|---|---|
| Harrington rods | Posterior |
| Luque rods (sublaminar wires) | Posterior |
| Steffee plate + pedicle screws | Posterior |
| **Dwyer, Zielke** | **Anterior** (scoliosis) |
| Pedicle screw | Posterior, spans **all three columns** (Boucher 1959) |

### Sacropelvic fixation (TO)

| | Direct iliac screw | **S2 alar-iliac (S2AI)** |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | Posterior iliac crest near PSIS → ASIS | Between S1 and S2 foramina (Kebaish 2007) |
| Prominence | **Prominent** → pain, wounds, removal | **15 mm deeper** → seldom removed |
| Alignment | Needs **connector** | **In line** with lumbar screws |
| Muscle | Iliac devascularisation | Less |
| SI joint | Less violation | **More SI joint violation** (main drawback) |

## How it's asked

**"PLC components?"** → **supraspinous, interspinous, flavum + facet capsules**

**"Theoretical axis of FSU rotation at an instant?"** → **instantaneous axis of rotation**

**"IAR — true?"** → **altered by degeneration, fracture, ligament injury**

**"Best predictor of good outcome after adult deformity surgery?"** → **sagittal balance correction**

**"Angle correlating with lordosis, kyphosis, sagittal alignment?"** → **pelvic incidence (A)**

**"Not a posterior instrumentation?"** → **Dwyer-Zielke** (TO)

**"S2AI vs iliac screws — incorrect?"** → **"less SI joint violation"** (it's more) (TO)
