# Klippel-Feil Syndrome

*Spine · 32 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — congenital fusion of **≥ 2 cervical vertebrae** from **failure of segmentation (or formation) of cervical somites at 3–8 weeks**

**Incidence** — classically **1 in 40,000** · recent: 1 in 172 to 1 in 83 (asymptomatic) · **60–70% female**

**Commonest fusion levels** — **C2-C3 (71%)** > C5-C6 (68%) > C6-C7 (68%) > C3-C4 (29%) (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: C3-4

### Genes & inheritance

| Pattern | Gene |
|---|---|
| **Sporadic** | **Commonest**; isolated fusion |
| **Autosomal dominant** | **GDF6 (chr 8)**, **GDF3 (chr 12)**; SGM1 (chr 8) |
| **Autosomal recessive** | **MEOX1 (chr 17)** (encodes MOX1, regulates vertebral separation) |
| Other | Notch, Pax genes; **fetal alcohol** (50% have cervical fusions) |

### Associated conditions

| Association | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Congenital scoliosis** | **53.5–60%** (commonest orthopaedic association) |
| **Sprengel deformity** | 30% (Orthobullets) · 33% / 50% / 20% (TO) · 6.4–16.7% (Orthobullets exam) — read the options |
| **Renal (aplasia)** | **33%** → **renal US** |
| **Deafness** | **30%** |
| **Congenital heart (VSD)** | 15–30% (5–30%) → **echo** |
| **Atlantoaxial instability** | **\~50%** |
| **Adjacent-level degeneration** | **100%** |
| CNS anomalies | 19% (brainstem, Chiari, tethered cord, diastematomyelia) |
| Others | Basilar invagination, cervical stenosis, **synkinesis (mirror movements)**, cleft palate, absent ribs |

**Wildervanck (cervico-oculo-acoustic)** — **KFS + ossicular deafness + Duane syndrome** (abducens palsy with retraction bulbi); X-linked, female (Clarke KF4)

## 3 Presentation

**Classic triad (< 50%)** — **low posterior hairline**, **short (webbed) neck**, **limited cervical ROM**

**Most consistent finding (TO)** — **limitation of neck motion**\
✗ Wrong options: short neck · low hairline · high scapula

**Not a feature / not in triad (TO)** — **bilateral SCM contractures** · **head tilt (torticollis)** may coexist but not part of triad

**Symptoms** — neck stiffness, neck/back pain (onset \~16 yrs, worse \~28 yrs), radiculopathy/myelopathy from hypermobile adjacent segments · ↑ cervical myelopathy rate

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use |
|---|---|
| X-ray AP, lateral, open-mouth; **whole spine** | Fusions (craniocervical / subaxial); **basilar invagination** (dens above **McRae's line**); **AAI = ADI > 5 mm**; stenosis < 13 mm; disc calcifications (resolve in 6 months) |
| CT | Bony anatomy pre-op |
| MRI | Cord anomalies; smaller cords in larger canals |
| **Renal US + echo** | Renal aplasia, VSD |

### Classifications

| KFS (original) | Fusion | Inheritance |
|---|---|---|
| I | Most / all cervical | Sporadic |
| II | 1–2 vertebrae | AD |
| III | + thoracic / lumbar | AR |

| Samartzis (prognostic) | Pattern | Symptoms |
|---|---|---|
| **I** (25%) | Single-level fusion | Axial |
| **II** (50%) | Multiple **non-contiguous** | Myelopathy / radiculopathy |
| **III** (25%) | Multiple **contiguous** | Myelopathy / radiculopathy |

**Clarke** — KF1 (AR, only class with C1 fusion) · KF2 (AD, C2-3 dominant, SGM1) · KF3 (isolated, e.g. C5-6) · KF4 (X-linked, Wildervanck)

### High-risk fusion patterns (TO)

| Pattern | Problem |
|---|---|
| **1 — C1-2 fusion + occipitalised atlas** | Motion concentrated at atlantoaxial → odontoid hypermobile, posterior dislocation |
| **2 — Long fusion + abnormal occipitocervical junction** | Forces through abnormal dens / C1 ring → instability |
| **3 — Single open interspace between two fused blocks** | Hypermobile segment → instability + DJD |
| **Least symptomatic** | **Multiple short fusions separated by > 1 mobile interspace** (stress shared) |

**Above vs below C3** — fusions **above C3** → more symptoms, avoid contact sport · **below C3** → usually asymptomatic, normal life span

## 5 Management

### Contact sports participation

| Clearance | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Can play contact sport** | **Asymptomatic, 1–2 level fusion below C3** (e.g. C4-5), Samartzis II at/below C3 with full ROM, no instability/spondylosis |
| **No contact sport (absolute)** | **Fusion involving C2** (commonest presentation), **long / multilevel fusions**, any ↓ cervical motion, instability, spondylosis, occipitocervical anomaly, **after KFS surgery** |
| Relative | Samartzis II with prior transient quadriplegia |

**Surgery (decompression + fusion)** — **basilar invagination**, chronic pain, **myelopathy**, **atlantoaxial instability**, symptomatic adjacent disease, **progressive scoliosis** (82% of KFS with congenital scoliosis need surgery)

## 6 Complications

**Cervical SCI** — with contact sports (small cords); central cord after minor trauma

## X vs Y

| | Klippel-Feil | Congenital muscular torticollis |
|---|---|---|
| Lesion | Bony fusion | SCM fibrosis |
| Neck | Short, low hairline, ↓ ROM | Tilt + rotation, SCM mass |
| Associations | Renal, deafness, VSD, Sprengel, scoliosis | DDH, metatarsus adductus |

## How it's asked

**"Adolescent, short neck, renal aplasia, C4-5 fusion — football?"** → **can play** (TO)

**"10-yr, cervical fusion above C3, renal aplasia?"** → **Klippel-Feil, no contact sports**

**"14-yr, C2-4 fusion, lacrosse?"** → **refrain from contact sports**\
✗ Wrong options: after MRI · after PT · after fusion

**"NOT a feature of KFS?"** → **bilateral SCM contractures** (TO)

**"Fusion pattern least likely symptomatic?"** → **multiple short fusions separated by mobile joints** (TO)

**"Most consistent finding?"** → **limitation of neck motion** (TO)

**"Most commonly fused level?"** → **C2-C3** (TO)

**"Not part of triad?"** → **head tilt** (TO)
