# Cervical Lateral Mass Fracture Separation

*Spine · 14 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Demographics** — **M:F 2:1**, mean age **35** (20–70)

**Level** — **C6 > C5 > C7 > C4 > C3**

### Pathoanatomy

**Lesion** — fracture lines through **lamina + pedicle** → lateral mass (with its superior + inferior facets) floats free

**Two-level instability** — both the facet joint above and below are compromised → **fuse 2 levels** (vs facet dislocation = 1 level)

| Associated finding | Rate |
|---|---|
| Anterior listhesis of fractured vertebra | **77%** |
| Listhesis of vertebra above / below | 24% / 10% |
| Coronal translation | 33% |
| Body collapse | 33% (less in type A) |

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanism** — traffic accident, fall, heavy object on head → **hyperextension + lateral compression + rotation**

**Allen-Ferguson groups** — extension-compression · **lateral flexion → type B comminution** · flexion-distraction

## 3 Presentation

**Neuro symptoms** — common (**up to 66%**): radiculopathy, SCI / myelopathy (Frankel / ASIA)

**Exam** — **torticollis**, paravertebral spasm

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| **X-ray** (AP, lateral, oblique) | **Low sensitivity — 38%**; disc narrowing; instability = **> 3.5 mm** displacement, **> 10° kyphosis**, **> 10° rotation** vs adjacent level |
| **CT** | **Diagnosis**; fracture extends into adjacent facets, foramen transversarium, transverse process, pedicle, lamina; translation, uncovertebral subluxation |
| **MRI** | **ALL rupture 50–75%** · PLL 30–35% · ISL/SSL 10–75% · disc disruption, bone bruise |

### Kotani classification

| Type | Description | Same-level / adjacent translation |
|---|---|---|
| **A – Separation** | **2 fracture lines: unilateral lamina + pedicle** | 91% / 20% |
| **B – Comminution** | Multiple lines, lateral wedging (lateral flexion) | — / 50% |
| **C – Split** | Vertical coronal line; superior articular process of caudal vertebra invaginates | 80% / 0% |
| **D – Traumatic spondylolysis** | Bilateral horizontal pars fractures → anterior/posterior elements separate | 100% / 50% |

## 5 Management

**Non-op (Miami J / halo)** — stable, no deficit; **halo poorly controls hyperextension/rotation**; spontaneous fusion only **20%**; late instability + pain → surveillance

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Posterior decompression + 2-level instrumented fusion** | **Most cases** (injured structures posterior); lateral mass / pedicle screws or lateral mass plating; late instability after non-op |
| **Two-level ACDF** | **Minimally displaced**, no posterior reduction needed; **controls anterior collapse + rotation** |
| **Single pedicle screw** | **Type A** separation **without instability** |
| A + P | Extra anterior support; failed anterior reduction / late presentation |

**Posterior caveats** — anterior disc collapse + late kyphosis; **midline (wiring) fusion doesn't control rotation**

```
C5 lateral mass fracture-separation + ASIA C
├─ Posterior → INSTRUMENTED FUSION C4-6 (both levels)
│      (not C5-6 only or C5 corpectomy — body intact)
└─ Anterior alternative → 2-LEVEL ACDF C4-6
```

### Lateral mass screw safety

**Technique (TO)** — start **1 mm medial to pillar midpoint**, aim **30° lateral + 15° cephalad**

**Structure most at risk (TO)** — **nerve root** (exits anterolateral to facet; long screw)\
✗ Wrong options: vertebral artery (too medial) · cord (safe) · thoracic duct

## 6 Complications

**Vertebral artery injury** (pedicle screws) · **late kyphosis** · **late instability** · chronic neck pain · radiculopathy

## X vs Y

| | Lateral mass fracture-separation | Facet dislocation |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | **Hyperextension + lateral compression + rotation** | **Flexion-distraction** |
| Unstable levels | **2** | 1 |
| Fusion | **2 levels** (above + below) | 1 level |
| X-ray detection | 38% | Unilateral often missed |

## How it's asked

**"ASIA C, C5 lateral mass fracture-separation — management?"** → **posterior instrumented fusion C4-6**\
✗ Wrong options: C5-6 · corpectomy · 1-level ACDF

**"Structure most at risk with lateral mass screw?"** → **nerve root** (TO)

**"Why fuse two levels?"** → **superior + inferior facets of the floating mass both incompetent**
