# Sacroiliitis

*Spine · 51 / 69*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **inflammation** of the SI joint → LBP + posterior pelvic pain

**Demographics** — teens to middle age · **M > F**

**Causes** — chronic inflammation (**AS, Reiter's**), infection, trauma, overuse, **pregnancy** · may end in fibrosis + ossification

**Septic** — **IVDU**, haematogenous · gram-positive cocci (Staph ± Strep)

### Genes & lab markers

| Marker | Fact |
|---|---|
| **HLA-B27** | Ankylosing spondylitis · **1–2% of all people** carry it |
| RF | Usually **negative** in AS |
| **ESR / CRP** | **Usually ↑** |
| WBC | Usually normal; ↑ in infection |
| **Blood cultures** | Positive in **\~50%** of septic cases |

**Reiter's** — **oligoarthritis + conjunctivitis + urethritis**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain with prolonged standing, **difficulty climbing stairs**, generalised LBP, ipsilateral hip weakness, **morning stiffness**

**Exam** — **FABER → posterior (SI) pain** with otherwise painless hip ROM · AS: thoracolumbar flexion deformity

**Septic** — fever, leukocytosis, limp; may mimic hip sepsis (hip US/exam normal) · L5 root irritation possible

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| X-ray | **Non-specific erosions**, sclerosis, calcification |
| **MRI with gadolinium** | **Study of choice** — T2 **fluid / inflammation** in SI joint, **abscess** |
| Nuclear medicine | Historical |

## 5 Management

| Cause | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Infection** | **IV antibiotics** (until symptoms + CRP settle) → oral · **surgery only if antibiotics fail or large abscess** |
| IVDU septic shock (GPC clusters + chains) | **Vancomycin + clindamycin** (✗ Wrong options: 1st-gen cephalosporin alone · immediate I&D) |
| Trauma / overuse | Rest, activity modification, NSAIDs, steroid injection |
| **Pregnancy** | **Observe** — resolves after delivery |
| Spondyloarthropathy | Aggressive PT, NSAIDs, **TNF inhibitors** / biologics |

```
Septic sacroiliitis
├─ No abscess → EMPIRIC BROAD-SPECTRUM IV ANTIBIOTICS, narrow on cultures
└─ Failed antibiotics / large abscess → SURGICAL DEBRIDEMENT
```

**Prognosis** — most resolve

## X vs Y

| | Septic sacroiliitis | Septic hip | Transient synovitis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hip ROM | **Painless** | Painful | Painful |
| FABER | **Posterior pain** | Groin pain | — |
| Hip US | Normal | Effusion | Effusion |
| Treatment | **IV antibiotics** | I&D | NSAIDs |

## How it's asked

**"IVDU, septic shock, SI fluid without abscess, GPC clusters + chains?"** → **vancomycin + clindamycin**

**"12-yr febrile, painful FABER, normal hip, SI fluid on MRI?"** → **broad-spectrum IV antibiotics + monitor**
