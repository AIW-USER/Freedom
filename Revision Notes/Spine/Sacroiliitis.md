# Sacroiliitis

*Spine · 51 / 69*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **inflammation** of the SI joint → LBP + posterior pelvic pain

**Demographics** — teens to middle age · **M > F**

**Causes** — chronic inflammation (**AS, Reiter's**), infection, trauma, overuse, **pregnancy** · may end in fibrosis + ossification

**Septic** — **IVDU**, haematogenous · gram-positive cocci (Staph ± Strep)

**SpA family (TO)** — AS, psoriatic, reactive, **enteropathic**, juvenile AS, undifferentiated · shared: B27, RF-negative, sacroiliitis, **enthesitis (hallmark)**, uveitis, dactylitis, keratoderma blennorrhagica · first-degree relatives **52× AS risk** · TNF pathway (TACE/ADAM17), IL-1 genes · **isotretinoin** → bilateral sacroiliitis

**Joint (TO)** — sacral surface **hyaline (thick)**, iliac surface **fibrocartilage (thin) → iliac side changes first** · width 1–2 mm, \~17.6 cm² · motion < 4° rotation, < 2 mm translation

| Pattern (TO) | Causes |
|---|---|
| **Bilateral symmetric** | **AS, enteropathic (IBD)**, RA, hyperparathyroidism, **osteitis condensans ilii**, Whipple |
| **Bilateral asymmetric** | **Psoriatic, reactive**, gout, OA, Behçet |
| **Unilateral** | **Infection (pyogenic, TB, brucella)** — until proven otherwise · tumour · SAPHO · paraplegia |

| | AS / enteropathic (TO) | Psoriatic / reactive (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| Sacroiliitis | **Symmetric** | **Asymmetric** |
| Spine | Continuous ascending | **Discontinuous (skip)** |
| Syndesmophytes | **Delicate marginal** | **Bulky non-marginal** |

### Genes & lab markers

| Marker | Fact |
|---|---|
| **HLA-B27** | Ankylosing spondylitis · **1–2% of all people** carry it |
| RF | Usually **negative** in AS |
| **ESR / CRP** | **Usually ↑** |
| WBC | Usually normal; ↑ in infection |
| **Blood cultures** | Positive in **\~50%** of septic cases |

**Reiter's** — **oligoarthritis + conjunctivitis + urethritis**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain with prolonged standing, **difficulty climbing stairs**, generalised LBP, ipsilateral hip weakness, **morning stiffness**

**Exam** — **FABER → posterior (SI) pain** with otherwise painless hip ROM · AS: thoracolumbar flexion deformity

**Inflammatory back pain (TO)** — onset **< 35–40 yrs** · insidious · **morning stiffness > 30 min** · better with exercise, not rest · **alternating buttock pain** · pain in 2nd half of night

| Provocative test (TO) | Accuracy |
|---|---|
| **Thigh thrust** | **Sensitivity 88%**, specificity 69% |
| Sacral thrust | Sensitivity 63% |
| **Distraction** | **Specificity 81%** |
| FABER | Sensitive, not specific |
| Palpation | Low reliability |

**Septic** — fever, leukocytosis, limp; may mimic hip sepsis (hip US/exam normal) · L5 root irritation possible

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| X-ray | **Non-specific erosions**, sclerosis, calcification |
| **MRI with gadolinium** | **Study of choice** — T2 **fluid / inflammation** in SI joint, **abscess** |
| Nuclear medicine | Historical |

| Modality (TO) | Role |
|---|---|
| X-ray | Changes only after **\~5 yrs** of disease |
| **CT** | Best for **erosions, sclerosis, ankylosis** |
| **MRI (T1 + STIR)** | **Most sensitive for early sacroiliitis** · contrast separates synovitis from effusion |

**ASAS MRI positivity (TO)** — **bone marrow oedema mandatory** (STIR bright or T1FS enhancement), periarticular/subchondral, **≥ 2 lesions on one slice or 1 lesion on 2 slices** · structural lesions alone insufficient

**Acute vs chronic MRI (TO)** — acute: **subchondral BME (earliest, lower posterior joint)**, erosions (iliac first), synovitis, capsulitis, enthesitis · chronic: **subchondral fat (hallmark)**, sclerosis **≥ 5 mm** deep, erosions with **pseudo-widening**, bony bridges, ankylosis · MRI **corner sign**: oedema acute, fat chronic

| Criteria (TO) | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Modified New York (X-ray)** | Grade 0 normal · 1 suspicious · **2 erosion/sclerosis** · **3 + narrowing/widening, partial ankylosis** · 4 ankylosis · **positive = bilateral grade 2 or unilateral grade 3** |
| **ASAS axial SpA** | **< 45 yrs, back pain > 3 months** · imaging arm: sacroiliitis + **1** SpA feature · **HLA-B27 arm: B27 + 2** SpA features |
| **ESSG** | Inflammatory back pain or synovitis + ≥ 1 of family history, psoriasis, IBD, urethritis/diarrhoea, alternating buttock pain, enthesitis, sacroiliitis |

## 5 Management

| Cause | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Infection** | **IV antibiotics** (until symptoms + CRP settle) → oral · **surgery only if antibiotics fail or large abscess** |
| IVDU septic shock (GPC clusters + chains) | **Vancomycin + clindamycin** (✗ Wrong options: 1st-gen cephalosporin alone · immediate I&D) |
| Trauma / overuse | Rest, activity modification, NSAIDs, steroid injection |
| **Pregnancy** | **Observe** — resolves after delivery |
| Spondyloarthropathy | Aggressive PT, NSAIDs, **TNF inhibitors** / biologics |

**Infective sacroiliitis (TO)** — **S. aureus commonest** (MSSA) · **Pseudomonas commonest gram-negative** · consider in **pregnancy** · IV antibiotics weeks (e.g. cefuroxime 10 wks)

**Biologics (TO)** — TNF-α inhibitor if **BASDAI > 4 + failure of 2 NSAIDs** · **anti-IL-17A**: secukinumab (IgG1κ), ixekizumab (IgG4) — approved for non-radiographic axSpA · IL-23: risankizumab, guselkumab (p19), ustekinumab (p40)

```
Septic sacroiliitis
├─ No abscess → EMPIRIC BROAD-SPECTRUM IV ANTIBIOTICS, narrow on cultures
└─ Failed antibiotics / large abscess → SURGICAL DEBRIDEMENT
```

**Prognosis** — most resolve

## X vs Y

| | Septic sacroiliitis | Septic hip | Transient synovitis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hip ROM | **Painless** | Painful | Painful |
| FABER | **Posterior pain** | Groin pain | — |
| Hip US | Normal | Effusion | Effusion |
| Treatment | **IV antibiotics** | I&D | NSAIDs |

## How it's asked

**"IVDU, septic shock, SI fluid without abscess, GPC clusters + chains?"** → **vancomycin + clindamycin**

**"12-yr febrile, painful FABER, normal hip, SI fluid on MRI?"** → **broad-spectrum IV antibiotics + monitor**

**"Unilateral sacroiliitis — think?"** → **infection (pyogenic, TB, brucella) until proven otherwise** (TO)

**"ASAS MRI criterion requires?"** → **bone marrow oedema** (TO)
