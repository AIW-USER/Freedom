# Cervical Spine Trauma Evaluation

*Spine · 18 / 69*

## 1 Basics

**Rule** — every trauma patient has a C-spine injury **until proven otherwise**

**Clearance** — confirming **absence** of injury by exam ± imaging; do it **efficiently** (delay ↑ complications)

**Missed injuries** — permanent disability; causes: **inadequate imaging of the injured level**, LOC, multisystem trauma

**Noncontiguous spinal injuries** — **10–15%** (Orthobullets) · up to **16%** (OB) → **image the whole spine** once one fracture is found

**High-energy markers** — MVA **> 35 mph**, fall **> 10 ft**, closed head injury, neuro deficit, pelvis/limb fractures

**Mechanism clues** — elderly fall onto forehead / high-speed rear-end → **hyperextension**

**Comorbidities to identify** — **AS, DISH**, prior fusion, ligamentous laxity disorders

## 3 Presentation

**Primary survey** — ABC; **manual in-line traction** whenever collar off for airway; inspect whole spine; **seat-belt sign** → TL flexion-distraction

**Secondary survey** — remove collar, inspect face/scalp, head posture (rotation → **unilateral facet dislocation**), **palpate posterior midline** (no tenderness in alert patient → low probability of injury), **log roll**, full neuro exam

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnostic criteria – clearance

| Clinical clearance (no imaging) — ALL of | Radiographic clearance — ANY of |
|---|---|
| **Awake, alert, not intoxicated** | Intoxicated |
| **No neck pain, tenderness or neuro deficit** | Altered mental status / obtunded |
| **No distracting injury** | Neck pain or tenderness |
| — | Distracting injury |

**Paediatric** — **NEXUS-based clinical guidelines → fewer CTs** = most effective radiation reduction · < 3 yrs can't cooperate; X-ray screen first, CT if abnormal\
✗ Wrong options: fluoroscopy · low-dose CT · avoiding radiographs · MRI for all

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Lateral X-ray** | Must show **inferior endplate of C7** (Orthobullets) · **top of T1** (OB) |
| AP + **open-mouth** odontoid | Standard trio |
| **Swimmer's view (TO)** | **Cervicothoracic (C7-T1) junction** (✗ Wrong options: Hill-Sachs · Bankart · AO junction) |
| **CT** | Higher sensitivity, faster; must include **superior endplate of T1** (Orthobullets) · **inferior endplate of T1** (OB answer) — read the options |
| MRI | Ligament / cord / disc |
| Flexion-extension | Not in acute / known instability or deficit; only to exclude occult instability **after** clearance |

**Lateral X-ray lines** — anterior vertebral line · posterior vertebral body line · spinolaminar line

| Prevertebral / retropharyngeal soft tissue (TO) | Upper limit |
|---|---|
| **C2** | **6–7 mm** ("above cricoid" → **< 7 mm**) |
| C3 | 7 mm |
| **C6 (cricoid)** | **18–22 mm** (retropharyngeal **< 22 mm**) |
| Child at C6 | 14 mm |

**Obtunded / intubated after 3 days, X-rays negative, occipital skin erythema** — **definitive clearance with CT ± MRI**\
✗ Wrong options: keep collar until surgeries done · halo · wait to examine

**GCS 9, multitrauma** — **CT C-spine**\
✗ Wrong options: remove collar · wait without imaging · X-rays to C7 only · flex-ext

**Burst fracture found (C5 / C7)** — **CT of entire spine** before surgery\
✗ Wrong options: steroids · flex-ext · MRA · EMG · lumbar MRI

## 5 Management

**Collar** — from scene until directed exam

**Whiplash** — rear-end low-speed extension injury, normal neuro + X-rays → **soft collar + early ROM / PT** (✗ Wrong options: Philadelphia collar · halo · C2 screws) · persistent pain at 4 wks → **NSAIDs + activity modification + PT** (✗ Wrong options: observation · ESI · facet blocks · MRI)

**Injury → procedure (OB)** — **flexion teardrop** → **anterior corpectomy + strut + plate** · AAI with cord compression → posterior C1-2 fusion · bilateral facet dislocation → anterior or posterior reduction + fusion · central cord + spondylosis → ACDF or posterior decompression-fusion · C7 Chance (bony) → halo or posterior fusion

### Transient quadriparesis – return to play

**Recurrent transient quadriparesis + cervical stenosis on CT/MRI** — **no further contact sport** · stenosis **< 13 mm**, absolute **< 10 mm** · **Torg ratio has low predictive value** (Cantu)\
✗ Wrong options: neck roll · EMG · ESI · steroid pack

## 6 Complications

**Delayed clearance** — **aspiration**, ↓ respiratory function, **occipital / submandibular pressure ulcers**, ↑ ICP

### Prognosis

**Elderly (75 vs 60 yrs) incomplete cervical SCI** — **↑ mortality** · elderly: lower-energy falls with head strike, fewer deficits\
✗ Wrong options: more severe deficits · more paralysis · epidural haematoma · chronic pain

**Preadolescent SCI (e.g. 3-yr-old T2/3 paraplegia)** — **≥ 90% develop paralytic scoliosis**; bracing doesn't prevent it · uncommon post-adolescence

## X vs Y

| | Clinical clearance | Imaging clearance |
|---|---|---|
| Mental state | Alert, sober | Obtunded / intoxicated |
| Symptoms | No pain, tenderness, deficit | Pain, tenderness, deficit |
| Distracting injury | None | Present |

## How it's asked

**"C5 burst on X-ray, MRI shows retropulsion — before surgery?"** → **CT of entire spine**

**"Intubated 3 days, X-rays negative, occipital erythema?"** → **definitive CT and/or MRI clearance**

**"C7 burst on MRI with deficits — next study?"** → **CT cervical, thoracic + lumbar**

**"Most effective radiation reduction in paediatric trauma?"** → **clinical guidelines → fewer CTs**

**"GCS 9, collar on, multiple fractures?"** → **CT C-spine to T1**

**"Injury suited to anterior corpectomy + plate?"** → **flexion teardrop fracture**

**"< 5 mph rear-end, neck stiffness, normal X-rays?"** → **soft orthosis + early PT**

**"4 weeks of whiplash pain?"** → **NSAIDs, activity modification, PT**

**"75- vs 60-yr incomplete SCI — 75-yr has ↑?"** → **mortality**

**"3-yr-old with complete paraplegia — later deformity?"** → **≥ 90%**

**"Recurrent transient quadriparesis with stenosis?"** → **stop football**

**"Normal prevertebral space above cricoid?"** → **< 7 mm** (TO)

**"Normal retropharyngeal space at cricoid?"** → **< 22 mm** (TO)

**"Swimmer's view shows?"** → **cervicothoracic junction** (TO)
