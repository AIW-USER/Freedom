# Intervertebral Disc

*Spine · 30 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Composition & structure

**Function** — motion + stability; links bodies; **25% of spinal column height**

**Endplates** — **hyaline cartilage** attaches disc to bodies

| | **Annulus fibrosus** | **Nucleus pulposus** |
|---|---|---|
| Collagen | **Type I** (obliquely oriented, 30° alternating "criss-cross" lamellae); inner annulus also type II; minor type V | **Type II** (minor type XI) |
| Collagen : proteoglycan | **High collagen / low PG** | **Low collagen / high PG** |
| Water | Lower (\~65% outer annulus) | **\~88%** (80–90%) (✗ Wrong options: 60–70%) |
| Cells | **Fibroblast-like** (annulus only) | **Chondrocyte-like** (survive hypoxia); notochordal cells gone by \~10 yrs |
| Origin | **Sclerotome** | **Notochord** |
| Property | **Highest tensile stress / tensile modulus** — resists torsion, axial + tensile loads; prevents distraction | **Highest compressive stress**; compressibility; distributes load |

![Disc: annulus fibrosus and nucleus pulposus](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9020-disc.jpg)

**Aggrecan** — **most abundant proteoglycan**; hydrophilic → **maintains water content** (compressive strength) · GAGs: chondroitin + keratan sulphate · other PGs: decorin (fibrillogenesis), versican (adhesion), biglycan, lumican, fibromodulin (more in annulus)\
✗ Wrong options: tensile strength (collagen) · sensory transmission · cross-linking

**Aggrecan : collagen** — **nucleus > 1** · annulus < 1 · endplate < 1

### Nutrition & metabolism

**Largest avascular structure** — capillaries end at endplates; **nutrition by diffusion through pores in the (central) endplates** · outer third of annulus from epidural vessels (TO) · loading "pumps" diffusion\
✗ Wrong options: CSF · annular vessels · diffusion across annulus (not porous enough)

**Nucleus cells** — energy by **anaerobic glycolysis** → need **glucose**; die with ↓ glucose or acidic pH, not hypoxia\
✗ Wrong options: Krebs

**Endplate damage** (smoking, calcification, sclerosis) → degeneration

### Innervation

**Sinuvertebral nerve** — from **dorsal root ganglion** (Orthobullets, OB) · "branch of ventral ramus" (TO) → **superficial annulus only** (nucleus + inner annulus aneural) · painful degenerate discs: nerves grow to inner third (\~50%) with granulation tissue (neo-innervation) · central endplate also innervated

**Pain neuropeptides** — **substance P**, calcitonin (CGRP), **VIP**, CPON (neuropeptide Y flank)

### Biomechanics

**Viscoelastic** — **creep** (deformation over time) + **hysteresis** (energy absorption with repetitive loading; ↓ with age)

**Intradiscal pressure** — **lowest supine** < standing < **highest sitting flexed forward holding weights**; load held closer to body → lower pressure\
✗ Wrong options: highest standing (EXCEPT answer)

**Diurnal** — \~25% of water lost by day, regained at night\
✗ Wrong options: "water ↓ at night"

**After subtotal discectomy** — **extension** = most stable loading mode

## 2 Mechanism

### Degeneration biology

| ↓ Decreases with age / degeneration | ↑ Increases |
|---|---|
| **Water** | **Keratan : chondroitin sulphate ratio** |
| **Proteoglycans / aggrecan** (large aggregating PG degradation = **pathognomonic**) | **Lactate** |
| **pH** | **Degradative enzymes — MMPs** |
| Viable cell number, matrix synthesis, disc height | Fibroblast-like cell density (**annulus only** — not in nucleus) |
| **Nutritional transport** | Type X collagen |
| Elasticity, GAG : collagen ratio, sulphation | Inner annulus thickness |
| **No change: absolute collagen quantity** | Nucleus stays **type II** |

**Conversion to fibrocartilage** — annulus–nucleus distinction lost, endplate hyalinisation, fissures, **Schmorl nodes**, vascularisation of posterior annulus

**Annular tears** — commonest **posterolateral**\
✗ Wrong options: central

**Herniated disc** — spontaneous ↑ **IL-1β, OPG, RANKL, PTH**; **IL-1β** stimulates disc cells to ↑ **MMPs, nitric oxide, IL-6, PGE2** · **MMPs = most abundant product** of herniated disc cells → **ECM proteolysis**\
✗ Wrong options: caspases · decorin

### Kirkaldy-Willis stages (TO)

| Stage | Age | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Dysfunction** | 15–45 | Circumferential + radial annular tears, facet synovitis |
| **Instability** | 35–70 | Internal disruption, disc resorption, facet laxity/subluxation → **HNP** |
| **Stabilisation** | > 60 | Hypertrophic bone, segmental stiffening → **stenosis** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Vacuum phenomenon (TO)** — **nitrogen** gas in disc/vertebra: **DDD** (commonest), osteoporotic collapse, myeloma, Schmorl node, osteonecrosis (Kümmell), rarely infection \
✗ Wrong options: **endplate avulsion** (EXCEPT)

**MRI sequences (TO)** — **T1 = fat only** bright (CSF dark) · **T2 = fat + water** (CSF + healthy nucleus bright) · **STIR / T2 fat-sat = water only** → first look in trauma + infection · post-contrast T1 fat-sat → infection, tumour · **gradient** in cervical spine for disc/osteophyte

**T1 dark + T2/STIR bright** — **water** (oedema, infection), not fat or simple degeneration (TO)

**Annular fissure (TO)** — **bright T2 spot/line in dark annulus** · can cause severe pain without big disc

**Desiccation / black disc (TO)** — lost T2 nucleus signal ("white is young, black is old") · chronic LBP with black disc **does poorly with any treatment** → discography before fusion/TDR

**Schmorl node (TO)** — **vertical herniation through endplate** ± oedema · mimics infection (no constitutional symptoms) → analgesics + exercise, biopsy if doubt

| Modic type (TO) | Meaning | T1 | T2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Oedema** | Dark | Bright |
| **2** | **Fatty** | Bright | Bright |
| **3** | **Sclerosis** | Dark | Dark (mistaken for metastasis) |

### Pfirrmann classification (TO – **T2 MRI**)

| Grade | Signal | Nucleus vs annulus | Height |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | Homogeneous, bright white | Clear | Normal |
| **II** | Inhomogeneous, white ± grey band | Clear | Normal |
| **III** | Intermittent grey | Unclear | Normal / slightly ↓ |
| **IV** | Dark grey | Lost | Slightly–moderately ↓ |
| **V** | Black | Lost | **Collapsed** |

## 5 Management

**Biological therapy** — **BMP-2, BMP-7, TGF-β = morphogens**: ↑ **chondrogenic phenotype + matrix synthesis** without proliferation · **IGF-1, FGF = mitogens** (↑ proliferation)

**HNP natural history (TO)** — mostly favourable; surgical benefit mainly first year; non-progressive deficits (except CES) treated non-op; surgery can wait 6–12 wks

## X vs Y

| | Morphogen | Mitogen |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | BMP-2, BMP-7, TGF-β | IGF-1, FGF |
| Action | ↑ Chondrogenic phenotype, matrix | ↑ Cell number |
