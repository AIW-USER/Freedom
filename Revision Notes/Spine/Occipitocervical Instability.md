# Occipitocervical Instability

*Spine · 40 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Traumatic** — 15–30% of cervical injuries at the occipitocervical junction · in **19% of fatal** cervical injuries

**OCD epidemiology (TO)** — **5–12% of traffic fatalities** · OC region in **10–25%** of cord injuries in fatal RTAs · mortality **up to 80%** (brainstem / vertebral artery) · **commoner in children** (flatter condyles, ligament laxity)

**Acquired** — commonest in **Down syndrome** · usually asymptomatic, found on screening (surgery, Special Olympics)

**Synonyms** — atlanto-occipital dissociation (AOD), occipitocervical dislocation

### Pathophysiology

**Traumatic** — high-energy **translation or distraction** · head usually displaces **anteriorly**

**Acquired** — bony dysplasia, ligament laxity · RA, Down syndrome

**Associations** — atlantoaxial instability, neuro deficit, **vertebral / carotid artery injury**, Down syndrome

### Anatomy

**Occipital condyles** — lateral foramen magnum · near vertebral arteries, medulla, **CN IX–XII**

**Joints (6)** — anterior + posterior median atlanto-odontoid, paired atlanto-occipital, paired atlantoaxial

| Ligament | Attachment | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Transverse (TAL)** | Behind dens → C1 medial tubercles (horizontal limb of cruciate) | **Primary atlantoaxial stabiliser** · "strongest ligament of the spine" (TO) |
| **Alar** (paired) | Dens → occipital condyles (+ C1 lateral mass) | Strong · OC stability · **limits C1 rotation on C2** (TO) |
| Apical | Dens tip → foramen magnum | **Weak** |
| **Tectorial membrane** | Posterior C2 body → anterior foramen magnum | **Cephalad continuation of the PLL** · atlanto-occipital stability |
| Anterior AO membrane | Foramen magnum → C1 anterior arch | Continuation of ALL (TO) |
| Posterior AO membrane | Foramen magnum → C1 posterior arch | — |

**C1-2 joint (TO)** — near-horizontal facets → **60% of cervical rotation**

**Motion (TO)** — C0–C1 **"YES" joint**: \~25° flex-ext (30–40% of neck flex-ext), 5° rotation · C1–C2 **"NO" joint**: \~**50% of rotation** · stability is **ligamentous, not bony** · key stabilisers: **alar → C0–C1**, **transverse → C1–C2**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Measure | Normal / abnormal |
|---|---|
| X-ray (AP, lateral, odontoid) | **Low sensitivity (57%)** |
| **Powers ratio = BC / OA** (basion–posterior arch ÷ anterior arch–opisthion) | **\~1 normal** · **> 1 → anterior dislocation** · < 1 → posterior AOD, odontoid or atlas ring fracture |
| **Harris rule of 12** | **BDI or BAI > 12 mm** → dissociation |
| **Condyle–C1 interval (CCI)** | **Most sensitive + specific** · normal **< 2.5 mm** |
| **CT** | **Gold standard for bone** · midsagittal reconstruction |
| CT angiogram | Vertebral artery injury; anatomy before fusion |
| **MRI** | Ligament injury with preserved alignment, occult injury, deficits · AOD with normal CT measurements → **MRI** |
| **Basion–dens interval (TO)** | **> 2 mm → AOD** ("Pang criterion", TO — verify: Harris BDI > 12 mm; Pang uses CCI ≥ 4 mm in children) |

![Powers ratio](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2014-powers-ratio-ct-scan.jpg)

### Traynelis classification (direction)

| Type | Displacement |
|---|---|
| I | **Anterior** occiput |
| II | **Longitudinal** (distraction) |
| III | **Posterior** occiput |

**Traynelis (TO)** — I anterior = **flexion** · II vertical = **axial load / distraction (> 2 mm)** · III posterior = **extension** · **IV complex** (combined)

### Harborview classification (instability)

| Stage | Injury | Stability |
|---|---|---|
| I | Minimal / undisplaced unilateral ligament injury | Stable |
| II | Minimally displaced, significant MRI soft-tissue injury (traction test) | Stable or unstable |
| III | **Gross malalignment (BAI or BDI > 2 mm beyond normal)** | **Unstable** |

### Atlantoaxial instability criteria (TO)

| Measure | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **ADI (flexion lateral)** | **> 3 mm adult**, **> 5 mm child** → TAL rupture |
| **PADI / SAC** | Normal 17–29 mm · **< 14 mm → cord compression** |
| **Rule of Spence** (open mouth) | C1 lateral mass overhang **> 6.9 mm** → TAL disruption |

**Causes of AAI (TO)** — trauma (hyperflexion), **Morquio**, **Down**, skeletal dysplasia, **os odontoideum**, **Grisel** (infection), **RA**, NF

## 5 Management

```
Occipitocervical instability
├─ Traumatic → PROVISIONAL HALO / TONGS (no traction or prolonged collar)
│      └─ Definitive → OCCIPITOCERVICAL FUSION
│      (traction only considered in Harborview stage 2 with preserved alignment)
└─ Acquired (Down, RA)
       ├─ Asymptomatic → OBSERVE
       └─ Myelopathy / significant pain / RA basilar invagination / tumour → OCCIPITOCERVICAL FUSION
```

**Traumatic OC dissociation (intubated polytrauma)** — **instrumented occipitocervical fusion**\
✗ Wrong options: Philadelphia collar · halo 12 wks as definitive · ACDF

**AOD principles (TO)** — **traction contraindicated** · **halo/collar avoided** (can distract) · **occiput → C2 rigid arthrodesis** (plate, occipital bolts, C2/C3 screws) · most die before hospital

### Occipitocervical fusion technique

**Set-up** — prone, **Mayfield**, set **O-C2 angle** on lateral fluoroscopy before draping · Occiput–**C3** instrumentation

**C1 dissection** — stay **< 1 cm lateral to midline** above the C1 posterior arch → protects **vertebral artery**

**Occipital screws** — **unicortical** (dural **venous sinuses** under the external occipital protuberance) · safe zone: **2 cm lateral + 1 cm inferior to EOP** along superior nuchal line (Orthobullets) · triangle **2 cm lateral, 2 cm inferior to EOP** (OB) — read the options

**Thickest occiput** — **5 cm lateral to EOP** → too lateral for screws; **Mayfield posterior pin** site

![Occipital screw safe zone](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2014-heyworth-modified.jpg)

**C1 anterior cortex breach** — **internal carotid artery** (< 3 mm from C1) · also bicortical C1 or C1-2 transarticular screws\
✗ Wrong options: vertebral · basilar · spinal arteries

**C2** — pars, pedicle, transarticular or translaminar · **C3** lateral mass aimed cephalad + lateral · complications: **nonunion**, bleeding (internal carotid, vertebral artery)

**Anchors (TO)** — **C1 lateral mass screw** · **C2 pars screw = standard** · **high-riding vertebral artery → C2 translaminar screw** · bone graft essential

### Occipital condyle fractures (TO)

**Mechanism** — axial load ± lateral bending, direct blow · **> 90% unilateral** · often incidental on head-injury CT · **X-ray misses → thin-slice CT gold standard** (TO)

**Cranial nerves** — lower CN palsy **\~30%** · **Collet-Sicard syndrome = CN IX–XII palsy** (dysphagia, hoarseness, scapular winging, tongue deviation) · **hypoglossal canal → XII palsy** · Wallenberg syndrome (TO)

| Anderson & Montesano (TO) | Pattern | Stability | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Comminuted impaction** (axial load) | Stable | Rigid collar 6–8 wks |
| **II** | **Basilar skull fracture** extending into condyle | Stable | Rigid collar 6–8 wks |
| **III** | **Avulsion at alar ligament** attachment | **Unstable** | **Halo 12 wks** (Vishnu) · **posterior occipitocervical fixation** (Mohindra) — read the options |

| Tuli (TO) | Pattern | Stability |
|---|---|---|
| I | Basilar skull + condyle | Stable |
| II | Ring fracture of foramen magnum | Potentially unstable |
| III | Isolated condylar compression | Stable |
| IV | Alar avulsion with distraction | **Unstable** |

**Tuli scheme (TO — verify: Tuli 1997 is usually 1 non-displaced, 2A displaced stable, 2B displaced unstable)**

### Atlantoaxial subluxation – Fielding & Hawkins (TO)

| Type | Injury | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| I | Rotatory, **TAL intact** | Collar 6–8 wks |
| II | Anterior **3–5 mm**, one lateral mass pivot, TAL deficient | Collar / surgery |
| III | Anterior **> 5 mm** | **C1-2 fusion** (unstable) |
| IV | **Posterior** C1 on C2 (odontoid erosion) | **C1-2 fusion** (unstable) |

**Surgery (TO)** — adult: **ADI > 5 mm**, dynamic instability · child: deficit, **persistent ADI > 4 mm**, deformity > 3 months, recurrence despite 6 wks immobilisation

**AARS (TO)** — **"cock robin"** (20° tilt one way, 20° rotation other, 10° flexion) · Grisel syndrome · CT shows rotation · child, trivial fall, transient quadriparesis, recovered → **congenital atlantoaxial instability**\
✗ Wrong options: bifid atlas · ossified flavum · syrinx

## How it's asked

**"Down syndrome OC fusion — site for 8 mm unicortical screw?"** → **point B, within 2 cm lateral/inferior of the EOP**

**"C1 drill through anterior cortex endangers?"** → **internal carotid artery**

**"Tectorial membrane on MRI?"** → **posterior C2 body → anterior foramen magnum (PLL continuation)**

**"Intubated MVC, OC dissociation — definitive treatment?"** → **occipital-cervical fusion with instrumentation**

**"10-yr, trivial fall, transient quadriparesis?"** → **congenital atlantoaxial instability** (TO)

**"Collet-Sicard syndrome?"** → **CN IX–XII palsy from occipital condyle fracture / AOD** (TO)

**"Unstable occipital condyle fracture?"** → **Anderson-Montesano III (alar avulsion)** (TO)
