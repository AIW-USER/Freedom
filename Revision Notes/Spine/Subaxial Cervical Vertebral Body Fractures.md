# Subaxial Cervical Vertebral Body Fractures

*Spine · 61 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Fracture types

| Type | Features | Stability / treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Compression** | Anterior body fails, **posterior cortex intact**, no retropulsion · often PLC injury | Mild + intact PLC → **collar 6–12 wks** |
| **Burst** | Through **posterior cortex**, **retropulsion** · ± PLC injury · complete/incomplete SCI | **Unstable → surgery** |
| **Flexion teardrop** | Anterior column fails in flexion-compression, **posterior fragment retropulsed / retrolisthesis**, posterior column fails in tension · large anterior lip = **quadrangular fracture** | **Unstable → surgery** · SCI |
| **Extension teardrop avulsion** | Small fleck off anterior endplate (ALL/annulus), usually **C2**, no malalignment | **Stable → collar**, no SCI |

### Flexion teardrop (TO)

**Features** — triangle off **anteroinferior** body · commonest **C5** · **posterior displacement of the upper column** · **frequently with sagittal body + laminar fractures** · **anterior cord syndrome** characteristic\
✗ Wrong options: "unusual"

**Quadrangular fracture** — **flexion-compression** · anterior third fragment, posterior subluxation, kyphosis, interspinous widening, facet subluxation

### Mechanism

| Mechanism | Usual injury |
|---|---|
| Hyperextension | Central cord |
| Flexion-distraction | **Facet dislocation** |
| Rotation | Soft tissue / high cervical |
| **Pure axial load** | **Burst** |
| **Rear-end collision** | **Extension–distraction** (TO) |

**Commonest subaxial injury pattern (TO)** — **distractive flexion**, commonest **C6–C7** · stages: 1 PLC failure → 2 unilateral facet dislocation → 3 bilateral → 4 floating vertebra

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Classifications

| Allen-Ferguson (research, mechanism) |
|---|
| Flexion-compression · vertical compression · flexion-distraction · extension-compression · extension-distraction · lateral flexion |

**Clinical descriptive** — compression, burst, flexion-distraction, facet dislocation (uni/bilateral), facet fracture

### SLIC (TO example)

| Component | Example score |
|---|---|
| Morphology (translation/rotation) | 4 |
| Discoligamentous complex disrupted | 2 |
| Neurology (incomplete cord 3 + continuous compression 1) | 4 |
| **Total** | **10 → surgery** |

### Key radiology findings

**MRI** — **PLC status** · TO: before reduction of fracture-dislocation · MRI within **48 h** most sensitive for oedema/haemorrhage · obtain if CT shows prevertebral soft tissue **> 5 mm at C3**, disc-space widening > 1–2 mm, interspinous widening

## 5 Management

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| Collar 6–12 wks | **Mild compression** (intact PLC, no kyphosis), **extension teardrop avulsion** |
| Halo | Stable pattern |
| **Anterior corpectomy + strut + plate** | Compression with **≥ 11° angulation or 25% height loss** · **unstable burst/teardrop with cord compression** · minimal posterior injury |
| **Posterior decompression + fusion** | **Significant posterior element injury**, no anterior decompression needed |

**Timing** — **early decompression < 24 h improves neuro outcome** · delay → pulmonary complications, ulcers, DVT\
✗ Wrong options: steroids · delay to avoid "second hit" · collar

**TO conflict** — awake, alert C4-5 jumped facets within 30 min → **hard collar + MRI** first (TO) · ASIA C C4-5 fracture-dislocation with MRI done, SLIC 10 → **immediate reduction + stabilisation** (TO)

### Anterior approach (TO)

**Plane** — **carotid sheath (lateral) vs trachea + oesophagus (medial)** · no internervous plane

**Side** — **left preferred**: right RLN loops under subclavian, less protected · left RLN under aortic arch (ligamentum arteriosum) in tracheo-oesophageal groove

**Commonest complication** — **vocal cord palsy (RLN) → hoarseness**\
✗ Wrong options: superior laryngeal

### C5 palsy (TO)

**Definition** — deltoid ± biceps weakness ≥ 1 grade after decompression without myelopathy worsening · **mostly posterior surgery**, also anterior (similar incidence)

**Features** — half with C5 pain/sensory change · **92% unilateral** · within **1 wk** · **good prognosis** · theories: segmental cord ischaemia-reperfusion vs **root tethering** (favoured)

**Prevention / treatment** — prophylactic C5 foraminotomy, limit posterior cord shift + excess lordosis · physiotherapy, steroids

### Cervical disc replacement (TO)

**Pros** — preserves motion + IAR, avoids pseudarthrosis + adjacent segment disease · equal neuro outcomes, **fewer reoperations** than ACDF

**Ideal** — mature patient, 1–2 level radiculopathy ± myelopathy C3–C7 from soft disc / foraminal stenosis

**Avoid** — disc height **< 3 mm**, major spondylosis, axial neck pain alone, **facet arthrosis**, **≥ 3 levels**, instability (> 3 mm translation, > 11° rotation), osteoporosis, obesity, **kyphosis**

## How it's asked

**"Flexion teardrop — EXCEPT?"** → **sagittal body + laminar fractures are unusual** (TO)

**"Quadrangular fracture mechanism?"** → **flexion-compression**

**"Large anteroinferior fragment + retrolisthesis?"** → **unstable flexion compression injury**

**"Awake C4-5 jumped facets at 30 min?"** → **hard collar + MRI** (TO)

**"ASIA C C4-5 fracture-dislocation, MRI done?"** → **immediate surgery: reduction + stabilisation** (TO)

**"Diving C6 burst, ASIA C — best recovery?"** → **urgent decompression < 24 h**

**"Bilateral deltoid weakness day 2 after C5-6 surgery?"** → **C5 palsy** (TO)

**"Anterior cervical approach — side?"** → **left (right RLN more at risk)** (TO)

**"Hoarseness + unilateral cord palsy after ACDF?"** → **recurrent laryngeal nerve palsy** (TO)

**"Anterior cervical approach plane?"** → **carotid sheath laterally, trachea/oesophagus medially** (TO)

**"Rear-end crash, quadriparesis?"** → **extension–distraction** (TO)

**"Commonest subaxial injury type?"** → **distractive flexion** (TO)

**"Image after ACD with articulating device?"** → **disc replacement** (TO)
