# Cervical Disc Arthroplasty

*Spine · 12 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### History

**1966 Fernström** — stainless-steel **ball bearing**; device complications → abandoned as ACDF rose

**2002** — modern CDA; premise = maintain motion → ↓ adjacent segment disease

**2010 →** — RCTs: **superior to fusion for reoperation rates + quicker return to work**

### Benefits & risks

| Benefit | Risk |
|---|---|
| **Preserves motion** | **Hardware failure** → retropulsion into canal, **paralysis** |
| Pseudarthrosis not a concern | **Persistent facet-origin neck pain** |
| Quicker return to activities | — |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use |
|---|---|
| X-ray AP + lateral | Alignment, disc height |
| **CT** | Implant **positioning + sizing**; facet arthrosis |
| **MRI** | Central + foraminal stenosis |

## 5 Management

### Indications & contraindications

| Indications | Contraindication |
|---|---|
| **1- and 2-level radiculopathy** | **Significant facet degeneration (advanced facet arthrosis)** → persistent axial neck pain → fuse instead |
| **1- and 2-level myelopathy** (thorough decompression) | — |
| Revision CDA **within 2 weeks** = primary results | — |

**Not contraindications** — two-level disease · **loss of disc height** · neutral lordosis/kyphosis · bilateral Hoffmann (myelopathy)

### Evidence

| | vs ACDF |
|---|---|
| Neurologic improvement | **Equivalent** (1 + 2 level) |
| PROMs | **Equivalent** |
| **Reoperation** | **Superior (lower)**; CDA reoperations mostly **index-level, implant-related** |
| Adjacent segment disease | "Mounting evidence of ↓" (Orthobullets CDA note) · "no protective effect, possibly ↓ reoperation" (Orthobullets ASD note) — read the options |

**Single-level myelopathy, no fixed kyphosis (smoker, inverted brachioradialis)** — **CDA at C5-6** gives best function\
✗ Wrong options: NSAIDs/PT · laminoplasty C4-7 · laminectomy-fusion C4-7 (multilevel, ↑ NDI) · C6 corpectomy

### Technique

**Anterior approach** · **align centre of rotation in coronal + sagittal planes** (critical in 2-level)

## 6 Complications

**Hardware failure** — catastrophic retropulsion

**Facet pain** — if arthritic facets ignored

## X vs Y

| | CDA | ACDF |
|---|---|---|
| Motion | Preserved | Lost |
| Pseudarthrosis | N/A | Possible |
| Reoperation | **Lower** | Higher |
| Facet arthrosis | contraindicated | ✓ |
| Levels | 1–2 | Any |

## How it's asked

**"51-yr single-level myelopathy, no kyphosis — best functional outcome?"** → **C5-6 disc arthroplasty**

**"C7 radiculopathy — contraindication to CDA?"** → **advanced facet arthrosis**\
✗ Wrong options: two-level · disc height loss · neutral alignment · Hoffmann

**"Where is CDA superior to ACDF?"** → **reoperation rate**
