# Congenital Muscular Torticollis

*Spine · 21 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Commonest cause of infantile torticollis** — **0.3–2.0%**\
✗ Wrong options: Klippel-Feil · Chiari · AARD · paroxysmal torticollis

**Sex** — **M:F 3:2** · mostly **right** side (TO)

**Risk factors** — **first pregnancy**, **breech**, traumatic delivery, oligohydramnios (packaging)

**Muscular vs non-muscular (TO)** — **\~80% CMT · \~20% non-muscular** · ask congenital vs acquired, painful vs painless · **present AT birth → osseous** · appears **few weeks after birth (mass → band) → CMT** · acute → inflammatory/infective

**Natural history (TO)** — SCM "tumour" at **4–8 wk** → fibrous band → contracture by **6–8 mo** · SCM heads: sternal (tendinous), clavicular (muscular)

**Aetiology (TO)** — **compartment syndrome / packaging = most accepted** · residual mesenchymal fibroblasts · spinal accessory nerve denervation

### Pathophysiology

**Lesion** — **sternocleidomastoid (SCM) contracture / fibromatosis**\
✗ Wrong options: platysma · omohyoid · longus colli · trapezius

**Theories** — compression/stretch injury · **venous outflow obstruction → compartment syndrome** of SCM

**Mass** — palpable at birth or **within 4 weeks**, middle–lower third of sternal head; regresses over months → fibrous cord

### Associated packaging disorders

| Condition | Note |
|---|---|
| **DDH** | **5–15%** → examine hips |
| **Metatarsus adductus** | ✓ |
| Calcaneovalgus feet | ✓ |
| **Plagiocephaly** | **Contralateral** side |
| Congenital atlanto-occipital anomalies | ✓ |

### Anatomy – SCM

**Origin** — sternal head (manubrium) + clavicular head (medial third of clavicle)

**Insertion** — mastoid process + lateral occipital bone

**Nerve** — **spinal accessory (CN XI)** — at risk in release

**Action** — ipsilateral tilt/flexion + **contralateral rotation** of the chin

## 3 Presentation

**Posture** — **head tilt TOWARD** affected SCM + **chin rotated AWAY** ("cock-robin")

**Painless** passive motion · older child: tight band, ↓ rotation + lateral flexion

**Exam** — vision, hearing, neuro, **hips**, feet, spine

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Cheng classification (TO)**

| Group (TO) | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Sternomastoid tumour** | Palpable SCM mass |
| **Muscular torticollis** | Tight SCM band, no mass |
| **Postural torticollis** | No mass, no tightness |

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Indication / finding |
|---|---|
| **Ultrasound** | **Modality of choice with palpable mass** + ↓ ROM; enlarged **hyperechoic** (fibrotic) SCM; separates CMT from serious causes; follows fibrosis over time |
| **X-ray C-spine** | Tilt/rotation **without a mass**; exclude bony causes |
| Dynamic CT C1-2 | Exclude AARD |
| MRI brain + C-spine | Non-muscular / central causes |

**Ultrasound = most sensitive test for CMT (TO)** — homogeneous hyperechoic SCM mass · X-ray for osseous causes (> 4–5 yr) · ophthalmology review

## 5 Management

```
Congenital muscular torticollis
├─ < 1 yr, < 30° ROM loss → PASSIVE STRETCHING (90–95% respond in year 1)
│      (tilt away + rotate chin toward affected side)
└─ Failed ≥ 1 yr stretching → BIPOLAR RELEASE / Z-LENGTHENING of SCM
```

**Surgical timing** — after **≥ 1 yr failed stretching**; 92% success even in older children; facial asymmetry improves if done **before 10 yrs** (Orthobullets) · **after 3–4 yrs** — **release before 3 yrs → deep painful scarring** (TO)

**Technique** — short proximal incision behind ear (mastoid) ± distal sternal/clavicular incision · unipolar (clavicular) or **bipolar (preferred)**; Z-plasty clavicular head + mastoid release · CN XI at risk · botox for refractory (OB)

**Stretching (TO)** — **> 95%** effective before 1 yr (**Morrison & MacEwen**) · good prognosis: **< 1 yr**, rotation deficit **< 30°**, **no tight band / tumour** · technique: **bend to OPPOSITE side, rotate to SAME side** · adjuncts: **Botox + stretching** (Botox alone no help), **TOT collar**, helmet for plagiocephaly

**Surgery indications (TO)** — > 1 yr · rotation deficit **> 30°** · facial asymmetry · gaze deviation > 30° · **ideal age 5–12 yr (Shim & Jang)** — read the options (1–5 yr: small structures, more recurrence)

**Unipolar vs bipolar (TO)** — unipolar distal release: poorer (residual tilt, lost neck contour) · **bipolar (Ferkel, JBJS 1983)**: distal **Z-plasty of sternal head** (preserves neck column) + clavicular head release, proximal release at **mastoid bony insertion** (protect **CN XI**; facial + great auricular nerves at risk) · **\~92% good/excellent** vs other releases 15% good · post-op bracing largely obsolete → early exercises · endoscopic release option · **modified Lee (Ling) criteria** grade results

## 6 Complications

**Untreated** — permanent rotational deformity, **positional plagiocephaly**, craniofacial asymmetry / **hemihypoplasia**, compensatory scoliosis

## X vs Y

| | CMT | AARD / Grisel | Klippel-Feil | Paroxysmal torticollis of infancy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pain | **Painless** | **Painful** | Painless | Episodic |
| Mass | **SCM mass** | No | No | No |
| SCM spasm vs chin | **Opposite** side (SCM primary) | Same side (secondary) | — | — |
| Key | Breech, first-born, DDH | URTI / trauma | Short webbed neck, low hairline, ↓ ROM | Vestibular; pallor, ataxia; resolves by 2–3 yrs |
| Rx | Stretching | Collar → traction → fusion | — | Self-limiting |

| Non-muscular cause (TO) | Key | Rx |
|---|---|---|
| **Grisel syndrome** | Non-traumatic **AA rotatory subluxation after throat/neck infection** (group A strep), transverse ligament laxity, painful | Antibiotics, NSAID, collar → **> 1 wk no better: halter traction + muscle relaxants** |
| **Ocular torticollis** | Painless, no SCM tightness, full cervical ROM · **superior oblique (CN IV) weakness** commonest | Eye-muscle surgery |
| **Sandifer syndrome** | < 1 yr (or toddler), dystonic posturing **after feeds**, **GERD / hiatus hernia**, normal EEG | Treat reflux |
| Klippel-Feil / craniovertebral | Present at birth, short neck, low hairline · McGregor, McRae, Wackenheim lines | Evaluate stability |

## How it's asked

**"True about SCM contracture?"** → **surgery before 3 yrs → deep painful scarring** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: disappears after infancy · best age 1–2 · unipolar sternal release suffices

**"Treatment of choice in infancy?"** → **passive stretching** (TO)

**"Muscle implicated?"** → **sternocleidomastoid**

**"6-week infant, head tilt since birth + neck mass — commonest cause?"** → **congenital muscular torticollis**

**"Imaging with palpable mass?"** → **ultrasound**

**"Associated condition to screen?"** → **DDH**

**"Most sensitive test for CMT?"** → **ultrasound** (TO)

**"Commonest cause of ocular torticollis?"** → **superior oblique weakness** (TO)

**"Torticollis after feeds with normal EEG?"** → **Sandifer syndrome (GERD)** (TO)
