# Spine Surgical Site Infections

*Spine · 60 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**SSI** — commonest early post-op hospital-acquired infection · cost \~$200,000/patient · ↑ morbidity, reoperation, stay, mortality

| Procedure | Rate |
|---|---|
| Overall spine | **0.7–16%** (orthopaedic spine 2.0%) |
| **Microdiscectomy** | **0.7%** with prophylaxis · **1.4% with microscope** |
| Elective instrumented fusion | **2.8–6%** |
| **Trauma** | **Up to 10%** (multilevel, delay > 160 h, complete deficit, cognitive impairment) |
| Posterior vs anterior | **Posterior higher** · combined A/P not higher than posterior alone |

**Average time to infection** — 14 months

### Risk factors

| Category | Factors |
|---|---|
| Medical | **> 70 yrs**, ASA, **diabetes**, CVD, cancer, steroids, prior lumbar surgery, COPD, immunocompromise, prior infection, **pre-op stay > 1 wk**, prior radiation |
| Lifestyle | **Obesity**, **smoking**, alcohol, **malnutrition (15×)** |
| Intra-op | Transfusion, **instrumentation**, staged surgery, **levels fused**, OR traffic, **> 3 h**, **blood loss > 1 L** |

### Pathogenesis

**Implants** — seroma + inflammation · **glycocalyx biofilm** shields bacteria from host + antibiotics · metallosis granulomas

| Organism | Context |
|---|---|
| **S. aureus (73%)** | Commonest · **MRSA 5–18%** |
| S. epidermidis | Increasing |
| E. coli, Enterococcus | **Incontinence / faecal soiling** |
| **Cutibacterium acnes** | Low virulence, **late hardware infection**, immunocompromised |
| Gram-negative rods | Trauma (ISS > 18), **neuromuscular scoliosis** |
| Polymicrobial | Post-op wound contamination (neuromuscular) |

## 3 Presentation

**Commonest presentation** — **wound drainage** · pain increasing instead of settling · night sweats

**Fever** — commonest general sign · **> 39 °C** worrying for deep infection

**Sepsis / organ failure** — emergent debridement

**Wound** — superficial: erythema, discharge · **deep may look clean** · clear persistent drainage = seroma; purulent = infection

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Classifications

| Anatomic | |
|---|---|
| Superficial | Skin + subcutaneous, fascia intact |
| **Deep** | Fascia/muscle — **won't settle with antibiotics alone** |

| Timing | |
|---|---|
| Early | **< 3 wks** |
| Late | **> 4 wks** |
| Latent | Years |

| Mechanism | |
|---|---|
| Direct inoculation | > 10⁵ organisms; within 30 days |
| Early outside-in | Drains, seroma, soiling |
| Late haematogenous | Dental work, UTI, bacteraemia |

| Thalgott | Host | Infection |
|---|---|---|
| A / B / C | Normal / local-systemic disease (incl. smoking) / immunocompromised | — |
| 1 | — | Single organism |
| 2 | — | Deep, polymicrobial (\~3 debridements) |
| 3 | — | Deep, polymicrobial + **myonecrosis** (poor) |

### Labs

| Marker | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **CRP** | Peaks **day 2–3**, normal by **5–21 days (\~2 wks)** · **most sensitive marker** · **persistent rise or second peak = infection** |
| ESR | Peaks day 4–11, **elevated up to 6 wks** · rising after day 4 suggests infection |
| WBC | Unreliable |
| Malnutrition | **Albumin < 3.5 g/dL**, transferrin < 150 |
| HbA1c | Pre-op diabetics **< 7.0** |

**Resolution** — **CRP normalised + ESR improving**

**TO** — post-discectomy severe pain + spasm, no fever, normal TLC/ESR, **high CRP** → **infective discitis**

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Fact |
|---|---|
| X-ray | Early normal · late: disc-height loss, endplate erosion, **lucency around hardware** |
| CT | Fusion status, implant position, peri-screw lysis, pseudarthrosis |
| **MRI + gadolinium** | **Most useful** · **rim-enhancing large collection = pathognomonic** · epidural collection, bony destruction, progressive marrow change · post-op change mimics infection |
| WBC-labelled / bone scan | No MRI (pacemaker) |

![Rim-enhancing post-op collection](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12737-screen-shot-2020-01-27-at-8-45-56-pm.jpg)

**Cultures** — **intra-operative tissue = gold standard** (ideally before antibiotics) · superficial swabs unreliable · culture removed hardware in latent cases

**Differential** — adjacent segment disease, inadequate decompression, seroma, haematoma

### Failed back surgery syndrome (TO)

| Timing | Commonest cause |
|---|---|
| Immediate / early | **Incorrect diagnosis** |
| **Few weeks (e.g. 3 wks)** | **Spinal infection** |
| Months | Recurrent herniation, **epidural fibrosis**, arachnoiditis |
| Years | Degeneration, instability, stenosis, adjacent segment disease |

**Overall commonest** — poor patient selection · patient factors: workers' comp, smoking, obesity, stress

## 5 Management

```
Spine SSI
├─ Mild superficial → ORAL ANTIBIOTICS + WOUND CARE
│      (Keflex MSSA · Bactrim MRSA · clindamycin PCN allergy · Augmentin anaerobes)
└─ Deep / most cases → URGENT I&D + CULTURES → TARGETED IV ANTIBIOTICS 6 WKS
       ├─ Loose hardware / refractory / latent with solid fusion → REMOVE HARDWARE
       └─ Unstable / not fused → RETAIN (± upsize, extend) — titanium preferred
```

**Surgery indications** — most cases, antibiotic failure, deformity, deficit, MRI progression

**Debridement** — old incision, layered, pulse lavage, remove necrotic muscle + **loose graft** + loose hardware · multiple debridements · **NPWT**, flaps, SPY perfusion, non-braided tension-free closure, beads, drains

**Antibiotics** — **6 wks IV** (PICC), follow ESR/CRP · **serial MRI not needed** · prolonged IV antibiotics may allow fusion before hardware removal

## 6 Complications

**List** — wound breakdown, pseudarthrosis, neuro deficit, sepsis, organ failure, death, worse outcomes

### Prevention

| Phase | Measures |
|---|---|
| Pre-op | Weight loss, **stop smoking**, alcohol abstinence, **glucose control**, treat UTI, **povidone-iodine nasal swab for MRSA carriers** |
| Prep | **Clippers** (✗ Wrong options: razor), alcohol prep |
| **Antibiotics** | **Within 1 h of incision**, **redose if > 4 h** · **cefazolin 2 g** (3 g if > 100 kg / BMI > 35) · PCN allergy/MRSA risk: **vancomycin** (start > 1 h before) or clindamycin · **stop at 24 h** |
| Intra-op | ↓ time, blood loss, invasiveness · ↓ traffic · **dilute betadine 0.3% × 2 min** · **release retractors every 2 h** · debride necrotic muscle · haemostasis |
| **Vancomycin powder** | **↓ SSI** · if infection occurs → **↑ gram-negative** + culture-negative collections (✗ Wrong options: VRE · nonunion · ↑ cost) |
| Drains | Evacuate haematoma; NASS: not routine after single-level surgery |

## How it's asked

**"Post-discectomy pain + spasm, only CRP high?"** → **infective discitis** (TO)

**"Most sensitive marker of post-op inflammation?"** → **CRP**

**"Resolution of wound infection?"** → **CRP normalised, ESR improving**

**"Topical vancomycin powder leads to?"** → **more gram-negative SSI if infection occurs**

**"Commonest cause of FBSS at 3 weeks?"** → **spinal infection** (TO)
