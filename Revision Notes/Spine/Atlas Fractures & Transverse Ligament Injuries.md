# Atlas Fractures & Transverse Ligament Injuries

*Spine · 8 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **\~7% of cervical fractures** · 25% of CVJ injuries · 1–3% of all spinal injuries · **commonly missed**

**Incidence (TO)** — **5–10%** of cervical spine injuries (Mohindra) · **3–13%** of cervical fractures (Vishnu) · associated odontoid / hangman **40–50%** · mechanism: axial load → **"hoop stress"** bursts the ring

**Bimodal** — **20–30 yrs** high-energy axial load (\~85% MVC) · **elderly** low-energy ground-level fall (OA, osteoporosis, gait imbalance)

**Associated injuries** — **50%** other spine injury · **40% axis (C2) fracture** · head injury

**Neuro injury rare** — capacious canal: cord occupies only **\~35%** at C1 (vs 50% lower) · burst **increases** space at C1

### Mechanism

| Force | Injury |
|---|---|
| **Axial compression** (blow to vertex, diving) | **Jefferson burst** |
| **Hyperextension** (blow to forehead) | **Posterior arch** fracture; low-energy falls; ↑ with **odontoid fractures**; 30% less energy needed in extension |
| Lateral compression | Anterior arch fracture |
| Lateral distraction | Comminuted lateral mass |

### Anatomy

**Atlas** — ring with 2 lateral masses, **no body, no spinous process**; **3 ossification centres**

**Incomplete posterior arch** — common **congenital variant**, not trauma

**Vertebral artery** — C1 transverse foramen → bends posteromedially across **sulcal groove** (common site of posterior arch fracture)

| Ligament | Role |
|---|---|
| **Transverse (TAL)** | **Main stabiliser of C1-2** (TO); posterior dens → anterior arch, inserts on **lateral mass tubercles**; prevents dens migrating into canal |
| **Alar (paired)** | Dens → occipital condyles; strong (5–6 mm); occipitocervical stability |
| **Apical** | Weak midline, dens → foramen magnum |
| **Tectorial membrane** | Posterior C2 body → anterior foramen magnum; **cephalad PLL** |

**Layers anterior to dura (dorsal → ventral)** — tectorial membrane → cruciate ligament → odontoid (alar + apical) ligaments

**Motion** — **occiput-C1 \~50% of flexion-extension** · **C1-2 \~50% of rotation**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — neck pain, spasm, ↓ motion, suboccipital pain, sense of instability

**C2 / greater occipital nerve** — **occipital neuralgia** (TO: commonest nerve injury — emerges below posterior arch), occipital numbness, rarely alopecia, neck flexion/extension weakness

**Vertebral artery dissection** — LOC, diplopia, vertigo, blindness, ataxia, dysphagia, nausea

**Neuro deficit** — uncommon in isolated C1; ↑ with associated C2 fracture

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Measure | Normal / abnormal |
|---|---|
| **ADI** | Adult **< 3 mm** normal (child < 5) · **3–5 mm** = **TAL torn, alar + apical intact** · **> 5 mm** = TAL + alar + tectorial membrane (OB: **> 7 mm**) |
| **Sum of lateral mass displacement (open mouth / coronal CT)** | **> 6.9 mm (rule of Spence)** or **> 8.1 mm with magnification (rule of Heller)** → TAL rupture, unstable (OB: ≥ 7 mm / 8.2 mm) |
| SAC (PADI) | Normal ≥ 13–14 mm; doesn't predict TAL injury |
| Retropharyngeal soft tissue | **> 9.5 mm** → anterior arch injury |
| Basion-dens interval | < 12 mm normal (occipitocervical) |
| Power's ratio | Occipitocervical instability |
| C2 pars displacement | Hangman fracture |

![Jefferson fracture: lateral mass overhang on open-mouth view](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2015-xray-jefferson-fx.jpg)

**X-rays** — lateral, **60° obliques** (posterior arch), **open-mouth** · flexion-extension only for **late instability** after non-op (not in ED)

**CT** — every suspected injury; **sagittal** recon = occult anterior arch fracture · **axial** = Dickman II avulsion · **coronal** = LMD · CTA for vertebral artery

**Paediatric pseudospread** — asymmetric atlas vs axis growth → lateral mass overhang without fracture (CT)

**MRI** — every confirmed C1 fracture; **↑ T2 signal in TAL = intrasubstance tear**; **more sensitive than CT for TAL**

**LMD caveat** — measurements may underestimate due to **rebound** after injury

### Landells classification

| Type | Pattern | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Isolated anterior or posterior arch** — **commonest**; "plough" = isolated anterior arch from dens driven through it | **Hard collar** |
| **2** | **Jefferson burst** — bilateral anterior + posterior arch, axial load | TAL intact → **hard collar** · intrasubstance tear → **C1-2 fusion** · bony avulsion → **halo** |
| **3** | **Unilateral lateral mass** | TAL intact → hard collar · disrupted → halo |

| Gehweiler (TO) | Pattern | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Isolated **anterior arch** (hyperextension) | Collar 8–12 wks |
| **II** | Isolated **posterior arch** | Collar 8–12 wks |
| **IIIa** | Jefferson burst, **TAL intact** | Collar, close follow-up |
| **IIIb** | Jefferson burst, **TAL disrupted** | **Unstable → posterior C1-2 fusion / halo** |
| **IV** | **Lateral mass** | Collar; displaced → traction + halo |
| **V** | **Transverse process** | Stable, collar |

| Jefferson (TO) | Pattern | Stability |
|---|---|---|
| I | Bilateral posterior arch | Stable |
| II | Unilateral lateral mass | Stable |
| III | Burst — anterior + posterior arches | **Unstable** (TAL) |

### Dickman classification (TAL injury)

| Type | Lesion | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Intrasubstance tear** (won't heal) | **C1-2 fusion** |
| **II** | **Bony avulsion** of C1 lateral mass tubercle | **Halo** (75% success) |

**TAL injury management (TO)** — type I (intraligamentous/insertional) **heals poorly → early C1-2 or OC fusion** · type II (bony avulsion) **halo 12–16 wks**, surgery if nonunion

**Fixation (TO)** — **> 8 mm** combined LMD → C1-2 fusion / OC fixation · posterior C1-2 fixation **loses \~50% of neck rotation** · **anterior C1 ring fixation** preserves rotation

## 5 Management

```
Atlas fracture
├─ TAL intact (LMD < 7 mm, normal ADI) → RIGID COLLAR 6–12 WKS
│      (not soft collar or observation)
├─ TAL bony avulsion (Dickman II) → HALO 3 months
└─ TAL intrasubstance tear (Dickman I) → POSTERIOR C1-2 FUSION
       └─ Comminuted C1 / no C1 purchase → OCCIPUT-C2 FUSION
```

**Elderly** — **never halo** (↑ mortality) → hard collar / surgery · clear C-spine expeditiously

**Non-op** — reduce with halo traction first if displaced · **post-treatment flexion-extension films** for late instability

**Operative indications** — unstable type 2/3 (controversial) · **Dickman I** · **combined C1 + C2** fractures (type II odontoid, hangman) · some prefer occiput-C2 (lower revision)

**C1 internal fixation** — lateral mass split; preserves C1-2 motion (small series)

### Fusion technique

| Technique | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Harms / Goel (C1 lateral mass + C2 pedicle screws)** | **10° medial trajectory** protects **internal carotid** (bicortical) (✗ Wrong options: vertebral artery · cord (at risk if too medial)) |
| **C1-2 transarticular screw (Magerl)** | **Vertebral artery** at risk in C2 groove (\~10% on CT planning) (TO) |
| **Gallie sublaminar wiring** | Needs intact posterior arch → valid for **TAL disruption** (✗ Wrong options: OC dissociation · comminuted C1 · type I/III odontoid) |
| **Occiput-C2** | Comminuted C1; occipital plate |

**Motion loss** — C1-2 fusion → **\~50% rotation** · occiput-C2 → **50% rotation + 50% flexion-extension** (TO)

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| Vertebral artery | Displaced posterior ring through **sulcal groove** |
| **Cock-robin deformity** | Displaced **unilateral sagittal split lateral mass** → occipital condyle settles on C2 → **occipitocervical fusion ± osteotomy** |
| **Nonunion** | **\~20%** non-op |
| Neck pain / stiffness | 14–80% pain, 8–20% stiffness, 34% activity limits |
| Infection | ↑ with posterior approach |
| Prognosis | Worse PROMs with **> 7 mm** displacement |

## X vs Y

| ADI | Ligaments torn |
|---|---|
| < 3 mm | None |
| 3–5 mm | TAL only |
| > 5 mm (> 7 mm OB) | TAL + alar + tectorial membrane |

|---|---|---|

| Heals? | No | Yes (75%) |

## How it's asked

**"Main stabiliser of C1-C2?"** → **transverse ligament** (TO)

**"ADI > 7 mm implies?"** → **TAL + alar ruptured + tectorial membrane ruptured**

**"Jefferson fracture — which indicates TAL disruption?"** → **combined LMD 8.2 mm**\
✗ Wrong options: ADI 2 · PADI 16 · pars shift · Power's ratio

**"Diving injury, LMD 8.3 mm — true?"** → **TAL disrupted, unstable → rigid orthosis / halo / surgery**

**"Comminuted C1, which suggests TAL rupture?"** → **LMD 9 mm**\
✗ Wrong options: SAC 15 · ADI 2 · BDI 11 · PVS 5 mm

**"Occiput-C2 fusion — motion loss?"** → **50% rotation + 50% flexion-extension** (TO)

**"Gallie C1-2 wiring valid for?"** → **TAL disruption**

**"Jefferson with intact TAL?"** → **rigid collar 6–12 weeks**

**"MRI shows intrasubstance TAL rupture 2 weeks after injury?"** → **surgical stabilisation**\
✗ Wrong options: collar · halo

**"Elderly, stable C1 fracture after slip?"** → **hard collar**\
✗ Wrong options: halo · flex-ex films · fusion

**"10° medial C1 lateral mass screw protects?"** → **internal carotid artery**

**"Transarticular C1-2 screw — structure at risk?"** → **vertebral artery** (TO)

**"True about Jefferson fracture?"** → **greater occipital neuralgia** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: high neuro injury · type 1 → halo · intact TAL → fusion

**"Gehweiler type with TAL disruption?"** → **IIIb — unstable** (TO)
