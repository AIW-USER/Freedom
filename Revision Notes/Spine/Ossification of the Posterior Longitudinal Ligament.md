# Ossification of the Posterior Longitudinal Ligament

*Spine · 42 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Population** — **East Asian (Japanese)** commonest · Asian 2–3% (up to 4% of cervical patients) · Caucasian 0.2–0.7% (TO) · not European ancestry

**Sex / age** — **men \~2× women** (not prognostic) · peak 6th decade

**Level** — **C4-C6** (C4-5, C5-6) · **> 95% cervical** · then thoracic, lumbar

### Risk factors & labs

| Risk factor | Not a risk factor |
|---|---|
| **Hyperinsulinaemia** (even without diabetes) · **diabetes** | HLA-B27 (= AS) |
| **Obesity / BMI** (correlate with extent) | Traumatic brain injury (= HO) |
| **Fluoride intoxication (fluorosis)** — urinary fluoride ∝ severity (TO) | Female sex |
| Hyperparathyroidism, **hypophosphataemic rickets**, abnormal Ca metabolism | — |
| Growth hormone imbalance, recurrent minor trauma, infection (TO) | — |
| High-salt low-meat diet, poor Ca absorption, mechanical PLL stress | — |
| Genetic linkage | — |

**Pathology** — vascular infiltration → hypertrophy → **endochondral ossification** of PLL → lamellar bone

**Associations (TO)** — **DISH**, **ossified ligamentum flavum**, **ankylosing spondylitis**, fluorosis

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — often asymptomatic · **myelopathy** (hand clumsiness, gait instability, Hoffmann, hyperreflexia) · radiculopathy · **cord injury from minor trauma** (central canal stenosis)

**MRI clue** — compression at **mid-vertebral body levels**, not only discs

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Role |
|---|---|
| **Lateral X-ray** | **Diagnosis** + **sagittal alignment (lordosis)** · classification |
| **CT** | **Study of choice for extent + thickness** of ossification · screw planning, vertebral artery |
| **MRI** | **Study of choice for cord compression** |
| CT myelogram | MRI contraindicated / complex bony stenosis |

**Pre-op work-up (TO)** — **lateral + dynamic X-rays AND CT** (both) · laminectomy only if lordosis maintained

![OPLL on sagittal CT](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2046-ct-sagittal-moved.jpg)

### Classification (lateral X-ray) (TO)

| Type | Pattern |
|---|---|
| 1 **Continuous** | Long lesion over several bodies |
| 2 **Segmental** | Behind each body, interrupted at discs |
| 3 **Mixed** | Continuous + segmental |
| 4 **Localised** | Over disc space, 1–2 segments |

**Myelopathy risk** — **mixed + continuous** progress most · **occupancy ratio (OPLL ÷ canal AP) 30–60%** predicts myelopathy (TO)

## 5 Management

```
OPLL
├─ Mild symptoms, not a surgical candidate → OBSERVE
└─ Significant myelopathy → DECOMPRESS + STABILISE
       ├─ Lordotic spine → POSTERIOR LAMINOPLASTY or LAMINECTOMY + FUSION
       ├─ Kyphotic spine → ANTERIOR CORPECTOMY (OPLL "floated")
       └─ Dynamic myelopathy → INTERBODY FUSION WITHOUT DECOMPRESSION
```

**Posterior (laminoplasty / laminectomy + fusion)** — **needs lordosis** (cord drifts back) · **safer, preferred** (avoids peeling OPLL off dura) · fuse after laminectomy (prevents **post-laminectomy kyphosis**) · risk **OPLL growth**

**Anterior corpectomy** — **kyphotic** spine · **"float" the OPLL** in the corpectomy site instead of excising → avoids **dural tear / CSF leak**

## 6 Complications

**Post-laminoplasty C5 palsy** — **commonest root palsy (\~6%)** · **deltoid / lateral arm (C5 dermatome)** · short C5 root at apex of lordosis, cord drifts back

**Recurrence / growth** — less with complete resection or floating

## X vs Y

| | OPLL | DISH | AS | Fluorosis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ossification | **Posterior** longitudinal ligament | **Anterior**, flowing, ≥ 4 levels | **Marginal syndesmophytes** | **Diffuse** — PLL, flavum, interosseous membranes |
| Marker | Hyperinsulinaemia | Diabetes | **HLA-B27** | Endemic fluoride (Punjab) |
| Anterior osteophytes | Small, non-syndesmotic | Large | — | — |

### Ossified ligamentum flavum (TO)

**Features** — rare cause of **thoracic myelopathy**, East Asians · **posterior** compression → **spastic gait, LL hyperreflexia, normal UL reflexes**

**Imaging** — **T2 sagittal MRI** · **whole-spine screen mandatory** (tandem lesions) · fine CT for planning

**Treatment** — **decompression only** (non-op fails) · better prognosis than other thoracic myelopathy · prognosis ← preop severity + time to surgery

## How it's asked

**"65-yr from Punjab, myelopathy, MRI sagittal — diagnosis?"** → **OPLL** (TO)

**"65-yr from Punjab, myelopathy, CT neck — diagnosis?"** → **fluorosis** (TO) — read the image and options

**"Mixed OPLL with myelopathy — pre-op tests?"** → **lateral + dynamic X-rays and CT** (TO)

**"Known risk factor for OPLL?"** → **hyperinsulinaemia**

**"Commonest OPLL level?"** → **C4-6**

**"42-yr myelopathy, cord compressed at mid-body levels, thick ossified PLL?"** → **OPLL**

**"Image suggestive of OPLL?"** → **ossified band behind the vertebral bodies on CT**

**"Commonest root palsy after laminoplasty?"** → **C5**

**"Spastic gait, LL hyperreflexia, normal UL reflexes?"** → **thoracic ossified ligamentum flavum** (TO)
