# Atlantoaxial Rotatory Displacement (AARD)

*Spine · 7 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Aetiology

**Definition** — paediatric C1-2 rotatory instability: spectrum from mild subluxation / muscle spasm → **fixed facet dislocation** (mechanical block)

| Cause | Share |
|---|---|
| **Infection** (pharyngitis, otitis media, URTI, retropharyngeal abscess) | **~35%** — **Grisel's disease**; lymphatic oedema around C-spine |
| **Trauma** (even trivial — fall from bed) | **~24%** |
| **Recent head / neck surgery** (tonsillectomy) | **\~20%** |
| Idiopathic | — |
| Associated | **Down syndrome**, **RA**, tumours, congenital anomalies |

**Mechanism** — ligamentous laxity

**TAL intact** — canal stenosis only with **severe rotation + facet dislocation**

**TAL ruptured + anterolisthesis > 5 mm** — stenosis with **less rotation (45°)**; vertebral arteries at risk

### Anatomy

**C2 ossification** — **5 centres** · **subdental (basilar) synchondrosis** between dens + body fuses **~6 yrs** · secondary (ossiculum terminale) centre appears **~3 yrs**, fuses **~12 yrs**

**Ligaments** — **transverse** limits anterior translation of atlas · **apical + alar** limit rotation

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — head tilt, neck pain, headache, ↓ rotation

**Posture** — **"cock-robin"**: chin rotated **away from** the subluxed facet, head tilted toward it (≈ 20° tilt, 20° rotation, 10° flexion)

**SCM spasm** — on the **SAME side as the chin** (protective, secondary) — e.g. right facet subluxation → chin left → **left SCM spastic**

**5-yr after tonsillectomy** — right neck bend, left head turn, SCM spasm → AARS

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| **AP open-mouth** | Asymmetric **size + distance from midline** of lateral masses |
| **Lateral** | Facet appears **anterior + wedge-shaped** (not oval) |
| Flexion-extension | Exclude instability (hard to obtain) |
| **Dynamic CT** | **Gold standard** — neutral + maximal right + left rotation → **fixed C1-on-C2 rotation unchanged** with turning |
| MRI | Little value unless neuro signs |

**Diagnosis is primarily clinical** (OB) · CT/MRI for persistent cases · labs (CBC, ESR, CRP) for infection

### Fielding (& Hawkins) classification

| Type | Lesion | TAL | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Unilateral** facet subluxation; **odontoid = pivot** (one facet forward, one back) | **Intact** | **Commonest, benign** |
| **II** | Unilateral, **3–5 mm** anterior; one facet = pivot | Injured | — |
| **III** | **Bilateral** anterior **> 5 mm** | Disrupted | Rare; neuro risk / sudden death |
| **IV** | **Posterior** C1 displacement (odontoid fracture / hypoplastic dens) | — | Rare; neuro risk / sudden death |

## 5 Management

**Chronicity is the key factor** — earlier treatment → better conservative success; many reduce spontaneously before presentation

```
AARD by duration (Orthobullets)
├─ < 1 week → SOFT COLLAR + NSAIDs + exercises
├─ > 2 weeks / failed collar × 2 wks → HALTER TRACTION (~5 lb) + BENZODIAZEPINES → hard collar 3 months
├─ > 1 month / failed halter × 2 wks → HALO TRACTION → halo vest 3 months
└─ > 3 months / neuro deficit / failed halo × 2 wks / recurrent → POSTERIOR C1-2 FUSION
```

**Timing variants (OB)** — soft collar < 7 days · halter 1 wk–1 month · halo 1–3 months · or sequential **2-week trials** (collar → halter → skeletal traction → arthrodesis) — at least **2 weeks** of collar before traction

### Early management – collar, NSAIDs & traction

**Acute (3–5 days, post-URTI / tonsillectomy)** — **soft collar** + NSAIDs + antispasmodic\
✗ Wrong options: halter · halo · fusion · observation alone

**1 week of collar without resolution** — **continue collar ≥ 1 more week** then reassess\
✗ Wrong options: immediate halter · stretching

**Fixed torticollis 4 weeks after fall** — **cervical (halter) traction** · halo needed when child's skin can't tolerate halter weight\
✗ Wrong options: soft collar · SCM release · fusion

### Chronic / irreducible – C1-2 arthrodesis

**Failed halter + halo traction, still subluxed on dynamic CT** — **posterior atlantoaxial (C1-2) fusion**\
✗ Wrong options: no treatment · closed reduction under sedation/GA · occipitocervical fusion (loses motion) · more sedation · more weight · halo vest/C-collar unless reduced

**Halo vest** — only to **maintain** a reduction achieved by halo traction

## 6 Complications

**Missed / delayed diagnosis** — commonest problem

**Type III/IV** — neuro injury, vertebral artery compromise, death

## X vs Y

| | AARD | Congenital muscular torticollis |
|---|---|---|
| Primary lesion | C1-2 subluxation | SCM fibrosis |
| SCM spasm vs chin | **Same side** as chin | **Opposite** side to chin |
| Age | Child, acute after URTI / trauma / surgery | Infant |
| Pain | Painful | Painless |
| Rx | Collar → traction → C1-2 fusion | Stretching → SCM release |

## How it's asked

**"5-yr, fixed torticollis 4 weeks after fall, CT rotatory fixation — next?"** → **cervical traction**\
✗ Wrong options: soft collar · SCM release

**"6-yr, head turned 3 days after URTI, no deficit?"** → **soft collar**\
✗ Wrong options: halter · halo · arthrodesis

**"7-yr, 1 week of collar, still tilted?"** → **continue soft collar ≥ 1 week, re-evaluate**

**"Painful head tilt 5 days after tonsillectomy?"** → **cervical collar + NSAIDs + antispasmodic**

**"3-yr, 8 months of torticollis, failed halter + halo, still subluxed?"** → **posterior atlantoaxial fusion**\
✗ Wrong options: occipitocervical fusion

**"12-yr, 10 weeks, failed 2 wks halter + 3 wks halo?"** → **C1-2 arthrodesis**

**"Gold-standard diagnosis?"** → **dynamic CT**

**"Commonest Fielding type?"** → **type I — unilateral, TAL intact, dens pivot**

**"Right facet subluxation — chin + SCM spasm?"** → **chin left, left SCM spastic**
