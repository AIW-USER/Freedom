# DISH (Diffuse Idiopathic Skeletal Hyperostosis)

*Spine · 26 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Also** — **Forestier disease**; **enthesopathy** of spine + extremities; ossification of the **anterior longitudinal ligament** (TO: non-inflammatory, "degenerative")

**Incidence** — **6–12%** overall · uncommon < 50 · > 50: 25% M / 15% F · > 80: 28% M / 26% F · non-diabetics 1.6–13% vs **diabetics 13–49%**

**Race** — commonest in **Caucasians**; less in Black, Native American, Asian\
✗ Wrong options: "most common in African Americans" (EXCEPT answer)

**Location** — **thoracic (right side) > cervical > lumbar** · thoracic **T7–T11**, often isolated · **right-sided thoracic** because the **pulsatile aorta protects the left** · cervical + lumbar **symmetric**

### Genes & lab markers

| Marker | Fact |
|---|---|
| **HLA-B8** | Associated (esp. DISH + diabetes) |
| HLA-B27 | **Not** associated (✗ Wrong options: "90% B27 positive") |
| **↑ HbA1c / diabetes** | Image showing DISH → elevated HbA1c |
| **↑ Growth hormone + IGF-1** | ✓ |
| ESR | Normal |

**Risk factors** — **gout, hyperlipidaemia, diabetes**, coronary artery disease

### Associated conditions

**Lumbar** — **spinal stenosis**

**Cervical (large anterior osteophytes)** — **dysphagia**, stridor, hoarseness, **sleep apnoea**, difficult intubation, **myelopathy**

**Fracture** — ankylosed segments → **long lever arms**; **hyperextension** injuries; low-energy falls → unstable fractures

**Extra-spinal** — elbow and other entheses · **heterotopic ossification after THA 30–50%** (vs < 20% without DISH)

## 3 Presentation

**Often asymptomatic** (incidental)

**Thoracolumbar** — **mild chronic back pain** (ankylosis stabilises) + **stiffness worse in morning + cold weather**

**Cervical** — pain, stiffness, **dysphagia** (food "stuck"), stridor, hoarseness, sleep apnoea

**Exam** — ↓ spinal ROM; myelopathy / stenosis signs

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnostic criteria

| Criterion (Resnick) |
|---|
| **Flowing ossification along anterolateral aspect of ≥ 4 contiguous vertebrae** (= **non-marginal syndesmophytes at 3 successive levels**) |
| **Preserved disc height**; little degenerative change (no marginal sclerosis, no vacuum) |
| **No facet ankylosis**; **no SI joint erosion / sclerosis / fusion** |

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| **X-ray** | **"Flowing candle wax"** non-marginal ossification; thoracic **right-sided**; **lateral C-spine**: anterior bone with **preserved disc space** (best view to separate from AS) |
| Bone scan | ↑ uptake — may **mimic metastases** |
| **CT** | **Any neck pain after trauma** → rule out occult fracture |
| MRI | Ligament / cord injury |

**Dysphagia + neck stiffness in a diabetic** — **X-rays of cervical, thoracic, lumbar spine** first\
✗ Wrong options: HLA-B27 · MRI first · ESI · inflammatory labs

### Osteophyte vs syndesmophyte vs DISH (TO)

| Structure | Origin / orientation |
|---|---|
| **Traction osteophyte** | Small, horizontal, parallel to endplate, few mm from margin (Sharpey fibres) |
| **Claw osteophyte** | Larger, curved, may bridge — progression of traction osteophyte in degenerated disc (TO image answer) |
| **Syndesmophyte (AS)** | Ossified **annulus**, **vertical**, at the **endplate margin** (marginal) |
| **DISH** | Ossified **ALL**, undulating, away from endplate margin (non-marginal) |

**Osteophytes** — 80% men / 60% women by 50, 95% by 70; most at **L3-4 + T9-10**

## 5 Management

**Non-op (most)** — activity modification, PT, brace, NSAIDs, **bisphosphonates**

**Cervical traction (fracture)** — caution: **over-distraction** (compromised ligaments)

**Surgery** — for sequelae: **lumbar stenosis, cervical myelopathy, adult spinal deformity**

### Fractures – CT & long posterior fusion

```
DISH + trauma (even standing-height fall)
├─ Neck pain → CT C-SPINE (not soft/hard collar + discharge or flex-ext)
└─ Fracture into posterior elements → LONG POSTERIOR FUSION
       (≥ 3 levels above + 3 below, e.g. C6 → C3-T2)
       (not C5-7 short, anterior alone — failure, or halo / collar — ↑ mortality)
```

## 6 Complications

**Cervical trauma mortality** — **15% operative vs 67% non-operative** · ↑ morbidity + mortality vs non-ankylosed spines · vs AS: "higher" / "similar" (Orthobullets) · **equivalent** (OB) — read the options

**Neurologic injury with trauma** — **AS > DISH** (67% vs 40%); AS also more surgery + epidural haematoma

**HO after THA** — **30–50%**

## X vs Y

| | DISH | Ankylosing spondylitis |
|---|---|---|
| Syndesmophytes | **Non-marginal**, flowing candle wax | **Marginal**, bamboo spine, Romanus shiny corners |
| Disc space | **Preserved** | Ossified (cervical) |
| Bone density | Normal / ↑ | **Osteopenia** |
| HLA | **B8** | **B27** |
| Age | > 50 | Young (15–40) |
| SI joints | **Spared** (abnormality excludes DISH) | **Bilateral sacroiliitis** |
| Facets | Spared | Ankylosed |
| Diabetes | **Yes** | No |
| ESR | Normal | ↑ |
| Thoracic | **Right-sided** | Whole spine |
| Trauma neuro injury | Lower | **Higher** |

**"67-yr neck + back pain, anterior flowing ossification?"** → **DISH**\
