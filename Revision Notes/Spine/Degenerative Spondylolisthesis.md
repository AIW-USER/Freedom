# Degenerative Spondylolisthesis

*Spine · 23 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — anterior slip with an **intact pars** (Wiltse **type III**) (TO)

**Prevalence** — ~5% men, ~9% women (5–9%)

**Demographics** — **women > 40** (**~8×**; 5–10× in OB), **African Americans**, **diabetics**; hormonal ligamentous laxity

**Level** — **L4-5** (5× other levels)\
✗ Wrong options: L5-S1 = isthmic

**Risk factors** — **sacralisation of L5** (transitional L5) · **sagittally oriented facets**

### Pathoanatomy

**Cascade** — disc degeneration → facet capsule degeneration → microinstability → macroinstability → anterolisthesis (worse with congenitally sagittal facets)

**Grade** — rarely > Meyerding II (posterior arch intact → canal narrows as body slides)

### Wiltse classification (TO)

| Type | Name |
|---|---|
| I | Dysplastic (no pars defect) |
| II | Isthmic (lytic, elongated, acute pars) |
| **III** | **Degenerative** |
| IV | Traumatic (non-pars arch fracture) |
| V | Pathologic (e.g. OI) |
| VI | Iatrogenic (added later) |

## 2 Mechanism

| Stenosis | L4-5 slip compresses | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| **Central + lateral recess** | **Traversing L5** | Slip, **LF hypertrophy**, facet osteophytes |
| **Foraminal** | **Exiting L4** | Vertical: disc height loss + posterolateral body osteophyte pushes root against pedicle · AP: superior facet + posterior body changes |

## 3 Presentation

**Back pain** — **commonest**; mechanical, relieved by rest/sitting · **start-up pain** (sit → stand) suggests instability

**Neurogenic claudication** — 2nd commonest: buttock/leg pain with walking upright, **relieved by sitting / flexing** (shopping cart), **not by standing still** · stationary bike tolerated

**L4** — quadriceps (sit-to-stand), ↓ patellar reflex, dorsiflexion (heel walk)

**L5** — dorsiflexion (heel walk), **EHL**, gluteus medius (hip abduction) · EHL weakness = commonest documented deficit (L5)

**Hamstring tightness** — common; separate from neurogenic pain · cauda equina very rare

### Neurogenic vs vascular claudication

| | Neurogenic | Vascular |
|---|---|---|
| Relief | **Sitting / flexion** | **Standing still** |
| Stationary bike | **Tolerated** | Painful |
| Pulses / ABI | Normal (**ABI > 0.9**) | ↓ |
| Walking test | Stop + stand → no relief; sit → relief | Stop + stand → relief |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use |
|---|---|
| **Standing lateral + flexion-extension** | Slip; **instability = 4 mm translation or 10° angulation** vs adjacent segment |
| **MRI (T2 sagittal + axial)** | **Persistent leg pain after failed non-op**; neural compression |
| CT | Bony pathology; **most reliable for pseudarthrosis** after fusion |
| **CT myelogram** | MRI contraindicated (**pacemaker**) |

## 5 Management

**Non-op (most)** — activity restriction, NSAIDs, PT · **ESI second-line**

### Operative criteria

| Indication for surgery |
|---|
| **Persistent incapacitating pain after 6 months** non-op **incl. ESIs** (commonest) |
| **Progressive motor deficit** |
| Cauda equina syndrome |
| **Active smoker → smoking cessation first** (continue PT) |

```
Symptomatic L4-5 DS failing 6 months non-op
├─ Standard → DECOMPRESSION + INSTRUMENTED POSTEROLATERAL FUSION (± TLIF/PLIF)
├─ Medically frail, can't tolerate fusion → DECOMPRESSION ALONE
│      (~31% progressive instability)
└─ Revision pseudarthrosis → ALIF
```

**SPORT** — surgery → **greater pain relief + better function at 4 yrs** vs non-op

**Herkowitz & Kurz** — decompression + fusion **90% good/excellent** vs **44%** decompression alone · satisfaction ~79% with fusion vs ~69% decompression alone

**Not appropriate** — decompression alone (iatrogenic instability, ~30% progress) · microdiscectomy · TDR (doesn't address facet pain generator) · stand-alone ALIF (suboptimal fusion, cage extrusion; doesn't decompress) · fusion alone (must decompress lateral recess + foramen) · uninstrumented fusion (↑ nonunion)

### Fusion technique & outcomes

**Pedicle screws** — **↓ pseudarthrosis** (↑ fusion rate); outcome benefit inferred, not directly proven · slip reduction: limited role in adults, not correlated with outcome

**Solid fusion** — **most important for long-term relief** of back + leg pain (short-term relief similar with pseudarthrosis)\
✗ Wrong options: interbody device · rhBMP-2 · preserving facet capsules

**Interbody cage (TLIF/PLIF)** — **↑ hospital cost** without ↑ fusion, ODI, SF-36 or LOS (OB) · "may ↑ fusion + ↓ slip progression" (Orthobullets) — read the options

**Cortical bone trajectory (CBT) screws** — start **more medial + caudal**, aim **cephalad + lateral**, smaller screws · ↓ blood loss, smaller incision, ↓ 1-wk pain; fusion + function = pedicle screws; usually with PLIF/TLIF · e.g. single-level PLIF with **local autograft + CBT screws**

**Fusion after laminectomy (no DS/scoliosis)** — if destabilised: **pars fractured** or whole facet removed\
✗ Wrong options: dural tear repair · 2 levels · smoker · < half facets removed

### Smoking

**Smoking** — ↑ pseudarthrosis, infection, reoperation; **↓ functional outcome scores**\
✗ Wrong options: LOS · airway after corpectomy · haematoma

**Post-op cessation ≥ 6 months** → ↑ fusion rate vs continued smoking, but risk **stays above non-smokers** whether quitting pre- or post-op · pre-op cessation ≥ 4 wks ↓ wound complications · multilevel fusion ↑ nonunion risk in smokers

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Pseudarthrosis** | **5–30%**; **CT** best (✗ Wrong options: MRI · flex-ext (miss 60–80%) · bone scan · ESR/CRP) |
| **Adjacent segment disease** | 30–40% radiographic (Orthobullets) · **20–29% need surgery at 10 yrs** (18% at 8 yrs SPORT) · ↑ with **laminectomy at cranial adjacent level** (✗ Wrong options: 1-level fusion · DS itself · obesity) |
| **SSI** | **0.1–2%**; **I&D + retain hardware** (stability aids eradication) (✗ Wrong options: aspiration · antibiotics alone · remove hardware) |
| SSI risk factors | **Age > 60–65**, **diabetes**, **smoking**, ↑ BMI, alcohol, blood loss, operative time, levels, **staged A/P**, prior SSI (✗ Wrong options: **previous spine surgery** (EXCEPT)) |
| **LFCN palsy** | **Prone iliac bolster** → lateral thigh numbness (meralgia paraesthetica) |
| Ulnar nerve / brachial plexopathy | Arm positioning |
| Dural tear | — |
| **In-hospital complications** | **Age** (strongest), ≥ 3 comorbidities, blood loss, operative time, levels fused (✗ Wrong options: gender, race, hospital size) |

**Adjacent-level symptoms not matching MRI (vague radicular pain, L5/S1 weakness, L3-4 degeneration)** — **EMG** before surgery

## X vs Y

| | Degenerative | Isthmic |
|---|---|---|
| Level | **L4-5** | L5-S1 |
| Pars | **Intact** | Defect |
| Sex | **Female** | Male |
| Root | **Traversing L5** (+ exiting L4 foraminal) | Exiting L5 |
| Canal | **Narrowed** | Widened |
| Grade | ≤ II | Can be high |

## How it's asked

**"L4/5 DS, failed 6 months incl. ESIs — next?"** → **decompression + fusion**\
✗ Wrong options: TDR · microdiscectomy · decompression alone · fusion alone

**"Failed non-op, flexion-extension + MRI DS?"** → **laminectomy + partial facetectomy + foraminotomy + instrumented PSF**

**"DS with EHL weakness, failed ESIs?"** → **decompressive laminectomy + posterior instrumented fusion**

**"Pacemaker, CT myelogram DS, ABI 0.95, bikes painlessly?"** → **decompression + instrumented fusion**

**"70-yr, trouble heel-walking, failed non-op?"** → **laminectomy + instrumented fusion**

**"SPORT — surgery vs non-op at 4 yrs?"** → **greater pain relief + improved function**

**"Greatest effect on pain, function, ODI (4 mm translation)?"** → **decompression + instrumented fusion**

**"Laminectomy for stenosis — fuse if?"** → **pars fractured**

**"Best long-term improvement?"** → **open laminectomy + instrumented fusion**

**"Pedicle screws in posterolateral fusion?"** → **↓ pseudarthrosis**

**"Most important for long-term relief?"** → **solid L4/5 fusion**

**"Fusion vs pseudarthrosis outcome?"** → **better long-term back + leg pain relief**

**"Interbody spacer adds?"** → **increased hospital cost**

**"↓ blood loss + incision, high fusion?"** → **PLIF with local autograft + cortical trajectory screws**

**"Smoker quits post-op — nonunion risk?"** → **still higher than non-smokers**

**"Smoking associated with?"** → **lower functional scores**

**"Post-op cessation needed to ↑ fusion?"** → **≥ 6 months**

**"Heavy smoker, failed 8 months non-op, no deficit?"** → **continue PT + smoking cessation**

**"Six-week wound erythema, ↑ ESR, dorsal collection?"** → **debridement, delayed closure, retain hardware**

**"SSI risk factors EXCEPT?"** → **previous spine surgery**

**"Recurrent pain 1 yr after fusion, normal labs — confirm diagnosis?"** → **CT lumbar spine**

**"Complication risk factors?"** → **age + blood loss + levels fused (all)**

**"Lateral thigh numbness after prone fusion?"** → **positioning (LFCN)**

**"Highest in-hospital complication risk?"** → **age**

**"Highest risk of ASD needing surgery?"** → **laminectomy at cranial adjacent level**

**"Need for adjacent-level surgery at 10 yrs?"** → **20–29%**

**"Expected weakness (L4-5 DS)?"** → **EHL**

**"Vague radicular pain, symptoms don't match adjacent-level MRI?"** → **EMG**

**"Wiltse type 3?"** → **degenerative** (TO)
