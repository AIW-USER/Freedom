# Neuromuscular Scoliosis

*Spine · 39 / 69*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — spinal curvature from disorders of **brain, spinal cord or muscle**

### Neuromuscular vs idiopathic curves

| Neuromuscular curves | |
|---|---|
| Progression | **More rapid**; **may progress after maturity** |
| Pelvis | **Pelvic obliquity** |
| Length | **Longer**, more vertebrae (may reach cervical) — long C-shaped |
| Surgery | **↑ Pulmonary complications** |

![Long C-shaped neuromuscular curve with pelvic obliquity](../../MCQ/attachments/q5291-illa-367f01-1-moved.gif)

### Paralytic scoliosis after childhood SCI (TO)

**Cervical complete SCI before age 10** — paralytic scoliosis in **virtually 100%** (TO answer: 6-yr with C4-5 fracture-dislocation → **100%**) · risk depends on level + age at injury

## 5 Management

**Outcome** — **spinal fusion is the only treatment with proven benefit on deformity** · carers report excellent QoL improvement

### Classification & treatment

| Category | Disease | Non-op | Operative |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Upper motor neuron** | **Cerebral palsy** | Boston underarm brace until puberty (10–12), wheelchair modification | Group I → **PSF** · Group II → PSF ± ASF + **fusion to pelvis (Luque-Galveston)** · indications: **curve > 50°**, worsening **pelvic obliquity with sitting imbalance** |
| | **Rett syndrome** | Brace C-shaped curves | PSF — thoracic curve affecting sitting/balance, C-curves failing brace |
| **Muscle weakness** | **Spinal muscular atrophy** | Boston brace until puberty | **PSF to pelvis** (wheelchair sitting) · release **hip contractures first** · may transiently lose UE function |
| | **Muscular dystrophy** | **Bracing contraindicated** | PSF ± pelvis (controversial) · **curve > 20°** or progressive · operate early (**20–30°**) **before pulmonary function declines** |
| **Paralytic** | **Spina bifida, SCI** | **Bracing contraindicated** | **ASF + PSF** with pelvic fixation (**anterior fusion needed — deficient posterior elements**) |
| | Polio | Boston brace until puberty | PSF |

## 6 Complications

### Wound complication risk factors

| Risk factor | Not a risk factor |
|---|---|
| **Serum albumin < 3.5 g/dL** | **Hemiplegia** |
| **WBC / lymphocytes < 1,500 cells/µL** | Number of levels fused |
| **VP shunt** | Blood loss, transfusion |
| **Severe spastic quadriplegia, non-ambulatory, seizures** | Preoperative curve magnitude |
| — | Age, length of surgery |

**Infection rate** — **4–14%** (> idiopathic)

## How it's asked

**"Wound complication risk factors in CP — EXCEPT?"** → **hemiplegia**

**"Known risk factor for wound infection after NM fusion?"** → **albumin < 3.5 g/dL**\
✗ Wrong options: levels · blood loss · curve size

**"Paralytic scoliosis after complete C4-5 SCI at 6 yrs?"** → **100%** (TO)
