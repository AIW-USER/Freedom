# Pediatric Spinal Cord Injury

*Spine · 46 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **2–5% of all SCI** · **83% cervical** · M:F 1.5:1

**Incidence (TO)** — **5–10% of spinal injuries**, \~5% of cord injuries · **\~50% missed** · **2–5%** (Orthobullets) vs **5%** of SCI (TO)

| Age (TO) | Cause | Region |
|---|---|---|
| Newborn | Birth injury (breech) | C1–C2 |
| Infant | **Child abuse** | Cervical |
| **< 8 yrs** | Ligamentous | **C1–C3** |
| 8–10 yrs | — | Subaxial C3–C7 |
| **> 10 yrs** | Sport | **T12–L1** |

**Age pattern** — **< 8 yrs: cervical** (**large head-to-body ratio**) · **> 8 yrs: thoracolumbar** · upper cervical (C1-4) > lower (C5-7)

**Mechanism** — **MVA / RTA commonest** · falls, sport, **child abuse** · sledding + ATV → thoracolumbar

**Associations** — **head injury > 30%**, intrathoracic, intra-abdominal

### Injury patterns

| Region | Patterns |
|---|---|
| Cervical | OC / atlantoaxial dislocation, **atlantoaxial rotatory fixation**, odontoid (synchondrosis), subaxial ligamentous, **SCIWORA** |
| Thoracolumbar | **Compression (commonest)**, burst, flexion-distraction, fracture-dislocation |

### Anatomy & biomechanics

**Cord** — ends at **L3 at birth** → **L1-2** · adult size by **10 yrs**

**Flexibility** — ligamentous laxity, immature supports, **shallow horizontal facets**, nucleus with ↑ water + ↓ collagen cross-links · column stretches **5 cm** without rupture · forces pass through weak **physeal cartilage**

**Fulcrum (TO)** — **C2–C3 in children → C5–C6** with age · facet angle **30° at birth → 60–70° by 10 yrs** · column stretches **5 cm vs cord 5 mm** → SCIWORA

**Normal variants (TO)** — **synchondrosis** (no oedema on MRI) · physiological **wedging** (high disc height) · pseudosubluxation C2–3 **≤ 2 mm** (TO) vs **< 4 mm** (Orthobullets) — read the options

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use |
|---|---|
| X-ray AP + cross-table lateral of whole spine | **Multilevel injury common** · ± swimmer's, open-mouth, flexion-extension |
| CT | Polytrauma, high energy, altered mental state, head/face injury · **radiation risk** · **not a screening tool** |
| **MRI** | **Deficit with normal X-rays (SCIWORA)** · soft tissue, cord · no radiation |

**Retropharyngeal space (TO)** — **< 6 mm at C2** (adults + children) · at C6 **< 14 mm children**, **< 22 mm adults**

### SCIWORA (TO)

**Definition** — **spinal cord injury without radiographic abnormality** · deficit with **normal X-ray + CT** · often recovers (TO stem: quadriplegia, normal imaging, complete recovery in 3 months — answer not keyed by source; SCIWORA is the option matching the definition) · MRI is the study

**SCIWORA (TO)** — hyperextension + laxity → momentary dislocation, spontaneous reduction · **80% < 9 yrs** · **\~6%** of paediatric spinal trauma · deficit may appear **30 min–96 h** later · **brace 12 wks** + close monitoring

**Apophyseal ring injury (TO)** — adolescent · **mimics disc prolapse** (larger soft-tissue component) · CT/MRI: avulsed endplate fragment · rest 6 wks → decompression if persistent

**Odontoid synchondrosis fracture (TO)** — **< 7 yrs** · halo 6–8 wks · AOD in child: **avoid collars** (distraction) → OC fusion

## 5 Management

| Treatment | Indications |
|---|---|
| Analgesia + activity | **Apophyseal, spinous process, transverse process** fractures |
| **Collar 8–12 wks** | Stable odontoid, AAI, acute AARD, stable subaxial |
| **TLSO 8–12 wks** | **Compression < 50% height loss**, **burst < 50% retropulsion, no deficit**, **purely osseous flexion-distraction (Chance)** |
| Halo | Unstable cervical (**thin calvaria → skull penetration**) |
| **Cervical fusion** | OC, atlantoaxial, subaxial instability (OC fusion, transarticular screws, pedicle screws) |
| **Thoracolumbar fixation** | **Unstable burst, cord compression, irreducible fracture-dislocation, ligamentous flexion-distraction** · pedicle screws 1–2 levels above + below |

**Surgical indications (TO)** — progressive deficit · **kyphosis > 30°** (burst) · **> 50% height loss or canal compromise** · 3-column injury with rotation · lap-belt flexion-distraction (PLC) → stabilise

**Technique (TO)** — paediatric spine board (large head) · halo: **more pins, less torque** · pedicle screws feasible **> 4 yrs** (3.5–4 mm) · avoid long fusion in young → **crankshaft** · flexion-extension films before discarding brace

## 6 Complications

**List** — complete deficit, **progressive spinal deformity**, poor wound healing, CES

**Post-injury scoliosis** — cervical complete SCI **before age 10** → paralytic scoliosis in **virtually 100%** · follow all to maturity

**Recovery by MRI (TO)** — **cord oedema only \~75%** recover · minor haemorrhage \~40% · transection poor · early decompression ↓ post-traumatic syrinx + kyphoscoliosis

**Prognosis** — most paediatric SCI **incomplete** · SCI commoner + more lethal < 8 · recovery better > 8

## How it's asked

**"9-yr complete C5-6 SCI — scoliosis risk?"** → **100%**

**"Paediatric SCI — EXCEPT?"** → **"cervical injury in < 8 from small head-to-body ratio"** (it's large) (TO)

**"Child quadriplegic, normal X-ray + CT, recovers in 3 months?"** → **SCIWORA** (TO)

**"SCIWORA — commonest age?"** → **< 9 yrs (80%), hyperextension + ligamentous laxity** (TO)
