# Thoracolumbar Fracture-Dislocation

*Spine · 67 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **\~4% of SCI** at level 1 centres · **50–60% have SCI** · M:F 4:1

**Mechanism** — high energy (**MVA commonest**, falls, sport, violence) · **acceleration/deceleration → hyperflexion + rotation + shear**

**Site** — **thoracolumbar junction T10–L2** (kyphosis → lordosis transition; vulnerable to shear) · injures **cord, conus or cauda equina** by level

**Associations** — head, thoracic, abdominal injuries

## 3 Presentation

**Pre-hospital** — backboard + collar · ATLS · inspect for open injury, kyphosis · palpate step-off, crepitus · **ASIA score**, rectal exam, GCS

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Fact |
|---|---|
| AP + lateral X-ray | Fracture type + **dislocation** |
| CT | Pattern (unilateral facet dislocation), canal, screening in blunt trauma |
| **MRI** | Cord + ligaments (**PLL**) · deficit level not matching bony level |

![Thoracolumbar fracture-dislocation](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2024-img-1470.jpg)

### TLICS

**Thresholds** — **≤ 3 non-op · 4 indeterminate · ≥ 5 surgery**

**Translation/rotation** — 3 + PLC 3 = **≥ 6** → surgery

**Example (OB)** — T12 burst (2) + incomplete cord (3) + intact PLC (0) = **5 → posterior decompression + arthrodesis**

## 5 Management

**Posterior open reduction + instrumented fusion** — **most fracture-dislocations** · pedicle screws for distraction-reduction · **2 levels above + 2 below** · post-op CT/MRI for residual anterior compression · **early surgery > delayed or non-op**

**Avoid isolated anterior procedure** — ALL usually intact; anterior surgery further destabilises; anterior reduction difficult · no closed reduction + TLSO or bed rest

**With deficit** — **posterior fusion + instrumentation + posterolateral decompression**

**Reduction tricks (TO)** — nibble half of intact facets · towel clips on spinous processes (often fractured) · **temporary rod + rod holder** (more powerful) · flexion-distraction: screws 2 above + 2 below → **cantilever straight rod** closes posterior elements · reduction usually decompresses the canal

![Posterior reduction and instrumentation](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2024-img-1468.jpg)

### Pedicle screws (TO)

**Entry** — **intersection technique** commonest (free-hand) · lumbar: line just lateral to inferior articular process × TP bisector · thoracic: mid inferior facet × mid TP insertion · **S1: inferolateral base of superior articular process** · mammillary point more lateral than intersection, more lateral than pars point

**Osteoporotic bone — best pull-out** — **larger thread depth** · > 60% of hold from **pedicle cortex** · fine threads proximally, coarse distally · insertion at 0–10° better · **cement augmentation** (PMMA, CaP, HA), expandable screws\
✗ Wrong options: cannulation · larger core (reduces thread depth) · larger pitch (narrower pitch better)

**Failure** — loosening 0.6–11% · breakage 2.6–60% · pedicle screws span **all 3 columns** (Boucher 1950s, Roy-Camille 1980s)

### Left retroperitoneal lumbar approach (TO)

| Structure | Fact |
|---|---|
| Ureter | Adherent to posterior peritoneum, falls away from psoas |
| **Sympathetic trunk** | On the **medial** border of psoas (✗ Wrong options: lateral) → injury: **warm red ipsilateral (left) foot**, so the normal right foot seems **cold + pale** |
| Ilioinguinal nerve | Emerges from **lateral** psoas border → quadratus lumborum |

### Perched facets (TO)

**Mechanism** — **distractive flexion** (subaxial) · spectrum: sprain → perched → unifacet → bifacet dislocation → floating vertebra

## 6 Complications

**Post-traumatic pain** — **commonest**; ↑ with kyphosis

**Deformity** — scoliosis, **progressive kyphosis (missed PLL injury)**, **flat back** (pain, stooped posture, fatigue), post-traumatic syrinx

**Others** — neuro injury, CES, DVT, nonunion

## X vs Y

| | Burst | Fracture-dislocation |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | Axial load ± flexion | Flexion + rotation + shear |
| Alignment | Adjacent levels aligned | **Translation** |
| PLC | Variable | Disrupted |
| Treatment | Often non-op if intact | **Posterior reduction + fusion** |

## How it's asked

**"T12 burst, incomplete cord, PLC intact — TLICS + treatment?"** → **5; posterior decompression + arthrodesis**

**"Fracture-dislocation with quad weakness — definitive?"** → **posterior instrumented fusion + posterolateral decompression**

**"Commonest pedicle screw technique?"** → **intersection technique** (TO)

**"Pedicle screw entry point?"** → **mammillary process, lateral to pars** (TO)

**"Osteoporotic bone — screw property?"** → **larger thread depth** (TO)

**"Left retroperitoneal approach — EXCEPT?"** → **sympathetic trunk on the lateral psoas border** (it's medial) (TO)

**"Perched facets seen in?"** → **distractive flexion** (TO)
