# Cervical Spondylosis

*Spine · 19 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Prevalence & high-risk sports

**Onset** — 40–50 yrs · **85%** show spondylosis regardless of symptoms: **> 65 yrs** (Orthobullets) · **> 60 yrs** (OB) · men > women

**Levels** — **C5-6 > C6-7** (most subaxial flexion-extension) · risk: excessive driving, smoking, lifting, professional athletes

**Highest spinal degeneration in sport** — **jockeys (horse racing)** · also wrestling, rugby, ski-jumping, gymnastics\
✗ Wrong options: badminton · baseball · volleyball · golf

### Pathoanatomy

**Motion segment** — disc + **2 facet joints + 2 uncovertebral joints (Luschka)**

```
Degenerative cascade
├─ Disc → desiccation, height loss, bulge / herniation
├─ Joints → uncinate spurs + facet arthrosis
├─ Ligaments → LF thickening + infolding (height loss)
└─ Deformity → kyphosis → load shifts to facets/uncovertebral → more spurs
```

**Clinical syndromes** — axial / discogenic neck pain · radiculopathy · myelopathy

## 2 Mechanism

**Root compression (radiculopathy)** — foraminal spurs (facet + uncovertebral) · posterolateral disc / disc-osteophyte between uncinate and lateral PLL · foraminal soft disc → **exiting root** (C6-7 → C7)

**Central stenosis (myelopathy)** — canal **< 13 mm** (normal 17) · **worse in extension**: cord pinched between disc anteriorly and facets + infolded LF posteriorly

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Findings |
|---|---|
| **X-ray** | Uncovertebral + facet degeneration, osteophytes, disc narrowing, **endplate sclerosis**, ↓ sagittal diameter; **poor symptom correlation** · lateral: alignment, canal size |
| **Oblique** | **Foraminal stenosis** (uncovertebral arthrosis) |
| Flexion-extension | Angular / translational instability; compensatory subluxation next to stiff segment |
| **MRI (axial)** | Modality of choice; disc degeneration, myelomalacia; **28%** false positives > 40 yrs |
| CT myelogram | Bony detail + neural status; pacemaker / hardware |
| Discography | Rarely; oesophageal puncture, discitis |

## 5 Management

**Treatment** — by pain severity/chronicity, instability, neuro deficit: observation → medical → surgery

**Axial neck pain only, normal neuro (70-yr, 2 months)** — counsel that **85% of > 60s show these X-ray findings**\
✗ Wrong options: surgery to prevent decline · MRI for radiculopathy (clinical diagnosis) · "< 10% improve non-op"

## How it's asked

**"Sport with highest cervical + lumbar degeneration?"** → **jockeys**

**"70-yr axial neck pain, spondylotic X-ray, no deficits — counsel?"** → **85% of > 60-yr-olds have these findings**

**"Commonest level?"** → **C5-6**
