# Spinal Cord Anatomy

*Spine · 53 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Cord overview

**Length** — \~45 cm adult · foramen magnum (continuous with medulla) → **lower border of L1** (TO)

**Conus medullaris** — **L3 at birth → L1 at maturity** · lower L1 in women, L1 pedicle in men (OB)

**Filum terminale** — **modification of pia mater** (TO) · conus → adheres to dura → inserts on **first coccygeal segment** (TO)

**Thecal sac** — dura containing CSF, roots, cauda equina

**Meninges** — dura (outer) → arachnoid → pia (inner) · **CSF in subarachnoid space** (arachnoid–pia)

### Embryology

| Structure | Becomes |
|---|---|
| **Neural tube** | Spinal cord · failed closure cranially → anencephaly · distally → spina bifida occulta, meningocele, myelomeningocele |
| **Neural crest** (dorsal to tube) | PNS, **pia mater**, spinal ganglia, sympathetic trunk |
| **Notochord** (ventral to tube) | Vertebral bodies, **nucleus pulposus** (annulus from sclerotome) |

### Autonomic

**Sympathetic chain** — **22 ganglia** (3 cervical, 11 thoracic, 4 lumbar, 4 sacral) · cervical: superior, **middle**, stellate · **middle ganglion at C6**, medial edge of **longus colli** → injury → **Horner's**

**Hypogastric plexus** — S2–S4 parasympathetic + lumbar sympathetic splanchnics

### Functional tracts

| Tract | Function |
|---|---|
| **Dorsal columns** | **Deep touch, proprioception, vibration** |
| **Lateral spinothalamic** | **Pain + temperature** · cordotomy site |
| Ventral spinothalamic | Light touch |
| **Lateral corticospinal** | Main voluntary motor · **UE fibres medial (central)** → **central cord: arms > legs** |
| Ventral corticospinal | Voluntary motor |

### CSF

**Production** — **choroid plexus** (3rd, 4th, lateral ventricles) · ultrafiltrate of plasma · **\~500 mL/day** · total **\~150 mL** → turned over **3–4×/day** · mechanical + immunological protection

### Blood supply

| Vessel | Supply |
|---|---|
| **Anterior spinal artery (single)** | **Anterior 2/3** incl. lateral + ventral corticospinal tracts — **predominant supply** |
| **Posterior spinal arteries (paired)** | Posterior 1/3 — **dorsal columns** |
| **Artery of Adamkiewicz** | **Largest anterior segmental (radicular) artery** · from **left posterior intercostal** · **lower 2/3 of cord** via ASA |

**Adamkiewicz level** — **left T8–L1 in 75%** (Orthobullets) · **left T9–L1** (OB, TO) · **T9–T11 in 80%** (TO, Dommisse) · range T5–L4, either side · "anywhere between T8 and T12" (TO true statement) — read the options

**Adamkiewicz injury** — **paraplegia NOT common** (TO EXCEPT) · thoracic watershed ischaemia with **sacral sparing** · ASA infarct → **anterior cord syndrome** (motor loss, preserved sensation/proprioception) · e.g. after wide **left anterolateral** corpectomy approach

**Critical vascular zone (TO)** — **T4–T9**: narrowest canal + poorest supply → paraplegia risk

**Dommisse principles (TO)** — 3 longitudinal channels (1 anterior, 2 posterolateral) · largest at **cervical + lumbar enlargements** (grey matter demand) · 2–17 anterior and 6–25 posterior medullary feeders · **vertebral arteries supply 80% of neck radicular arteries** · reversible flow like circle of Willis

### Nerve root anatomy

| Region | Exit |
|---|---|
| **Cervical** | **Above** same-numbered pedicle (C5 above C5) · horizontal · **extra C8 root** |
| Thoracic | **Below** same-numbered pedicle (T4 between T4-5) |
| Lumbar | **Below** pedicle (L5 below L5) · descend vertically |
| Intrathecal lumbar | **Exiting roots ventral**; **motor fibres ventral** to sensory |

**Why thoracic/lumbar roots lie below their vertebrae (TO)** — **7 cervical vertebrae, 8 cervical nerves** · 31 pairs total

**Vertebral artery (TO)** — through transverse foramina **C1–C6**\
✗ Wrong options: C7 (foramen present but artery usually outside)

**Saddle anaesthesia, no other LL deficit (TO)** — lesion **below S2** (S3: inner thighs, buttocks, perianal)

### Neurogenic bladder (TO)

| Lesion | Bladder |
|---|---|
| **Above pons** (micturition centre in pons, **locus coeruleus**) | **Loss of voluntary inhibition**, mild detrusor overactivity, **no DSD**, normal volume + pressure, no hypertrophy |
| **Above S2 (suprasacral cord)** | **Detrusor-sphincter dyssynergia**, high pressures → functional outlet obstruction |
| **Below S2 / pelvic nerves** | **Detrusor areflexia**, no DSD, **high volume, low pressure** |

### Neuroanatomy – brain & cranial nerves (TO)

| Topic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Trigeminal-associated ganglia** | **Ciliary (V1), otic + pterygopalatine (V2 per TO), submandibular (V3) — all four** · trigeminal ganglion is sensory |
| Brodmann | **4 primary motor** · 6 premotor/SMA · 3,1,2 somatosensory · **17 visual** · 41,42 auditory · **44 Broca** · 8 frontal eye field · **Wernicke = 39 + 40** |
| **Internal acoustic meatus** | Transverse crest (horizontal) + **Bill's bar** (vertical, deeper) · **"7 up, Coke down"** — facial + nervus intermedius anterosuperior, **cochlear anteroinferior** (✗ Wrong options: superior), vestibular nerves posterior |
| **Vernet (jugular foramen)** | **IX, X, XI** — taste posterior 1/3, palate/pharynx/larynx, trapezius + SCM · not **tongue deviation (XII, hypoglossal canal)** |
| **Cavernous sinus** | **Inside: VI (abducens) + ICA + sympathetic plexus** · lateral wall: III, IV, V1, V2 · "OH TOM CAT" |
| Annulus of Zinn | Through: III (sup + inf), **VI**, **nasociliary**, optic nerve + ophthalmic artery · **outside: frontal, lacrimal, trochlear**, superior ophthalmic vein |
| **Facial nerve branches** | **1st = greater superficial petrosal** (geniculate) → stapedius → chorda tympani → posterior auricular, digastric, stylohyoid → 5 terminal |
| Facial muscles | Orbicularis oris/oculi, buccinator · not **lateral pterygoid (V3)** |
| Facial nerve blood | **External carotid** branches |
| Olfaction | Unmyelinated, cribriform plate, entorhinal cortex · **only sense not relaying in thalamus** (✗ Wrong options: VPL) |
| **Left optic tract** | **Right homonymous hemianopia** (temporal right + nasal left) |
| **Posterior pituitary** | **Neuroectoderm** (3rd ventricle floor) · anterior = Rathke's pouch |
| **Asterion** | Lambdoid + occipitomastoid + parietomastoid sutures |
| **Kocher's point** | **1 cm anterior to coronal suture**, 2–3 cm from midline (mid-pupillary) — EVD into frontal horn |
| **Vein of Labbé** | **Sylvian fissure (superficial middle cerebral vein) → transverse sinus** |
| **Pineal infratentorial supracerebellar approach** | Sacrifice **superior vermian + precentral cerebellar veins** · not basal vein of Rosenthal |

## How it's asked

**"Spinal anatomy — EXCEPT?"** → **vertebral artery through C1–C7 foramina** (it's C1–C6) (TO)

**"Artery of Adamkiewicz — EXCEPT?"** → **paraplegia after its injury is common** (TO)

**"Critical vascular zone?"** → **T4–T9** (TO)

**"Predominant cord blood supply?"** → **anterior spinal artery (A)**

**"Motor loss with preserved sensation after left anterolateral corpectomy?"** → **left-sided artery T9–L1 (Adamkiewicz)**

**"Filum extends to?"** → **first coccygeal segment** (TO)

**"Saddle anaesthesia only?"** → **below S2** (TO)

**"Why thoracic/lumbar nerves exit below their vertebrae?"** → **7 cervical vertebrae, 8 cervical nerves** (TO)

**"Nerve between T4-5?"** → **T4** (TO)

**"Correct root–foramen grouping?"** → **cervical roots above, lumbar below their pedicle (Group D)**

**"Conus level in adults?"** → **L1**

**"Ganglia associated with trigeminal?"** → **all of the above** (TO)

**"Veins sacrificed in infratentorial supracerebellar approach?"** → **superior vermian + precentral cerebellar** (TO)

**"Brodmann 4?"** → **primary motor cortex** (TO)

**"IAM — NOT true?"** → **cochlear nerve in superior-medial compartment** (TO)

**"Vernet syndrome — FALSE?"** → **tongue deviation** (TO)

**"Wernicke's area?"** → **Brodmann 39 + 40** (TO)

**"Direct content of cavernous sinus?"** → **6th cranial nerve** (TO)

**"Posterior pituitary develops from?"** → **neuroectoderm** (TO)

**"Junction of lambdoid, occipitomastoid, parietomastoid?"** → **asterion** (TO)

**"Not through annulus of Zinn?"** → **frontal nerve** (TO)

**"Cranial nerve supplied by ECA?"** → **VII** (TO)

**"Olfaction — false?"** → **relays to VPL of thalamus** (TO)

**"Facial nerve supplies all EXCEPT?"** → **lateral pterygoid** (TO)

**"First branch of facial nerve?"** → **greater superficial petrosal nerve** (TO)

**"Left optic tract lesion?"** → **temporal right + nasal left field loss** (TO)

**"Kocher's point?"** → **1 cm anterior to coronal suture** (TO)

**"Vein of Labbé connects?"** → **Sylvian fissure to transverse sinus** (TO)

**"Urgency, detrusor overactivity, no DSD, normal pressures?"** → **lesion above the pons** (TO)
