# Disc Space Infection - Pediatric

*Spine · 24 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Who** — children > adults · **males** · usually **< 5 yrs**

**Where** — **lumbar spine (50–60%)**

### Pathophysiology

**Why children** — vessels run from **cartilaginous endplate into nucleus pulposus** → direct haematogenous seeding of disc → spreads to endplate/body · **adults**: vessels reach **annulus only** → isolated discitis rare

| Organism | Note |
|---|---|
| **Staph aureus** | **> 80%** |
| **TB** | **Always consider** — esp. not improving on first-line antibiotics |
| **Salmonella** | **Sickle cell** |

## 3 Presentation

**Toddler** — **limp / refuses to sit or walk**, **loss of appetite**, abdominal pain, fever only **25%**

**Older child** — back pain with **point tenderness**, ↓ ROM

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Genes & lab markers

| Test | Fact |
|---|---|
| ESR, CRP, WBC | **High-normal or mildly ↑**; WBC normal in \~45% |
| **CRP** | **Most sensitive for monitoring treatment** (half-life **19 h**) (✗ Wrong options: ESR (3 wks to normalise) · WBC · blood cultures · procalcitonin) |
| Blood cultures | Identify organism (often negative) |

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — unreliable; earliest changes at **\~1 week**

| Order | Finding | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| **1 (earliest)** | **Loss of lumbar lordosis** | Before bony change |
| 2 | **Disc space narrowing** | **10–21 days** (Orthobullets) · from 1 wk (OB) |
| 3 | **Endplate erosion** | 10–21 days / 1–3 wks |
| Later | "Sawtooth" erosion 3–4 wks · endplate scalloping | Long-standing |
| Healed | **Vertebra magna**, permanent height loss, **block vertebra** (spontaneous fusion) | Resolved |

**MRI** — **diagnostic test of choice**

## 5 Management

```
Paediatric discitis
├─ Early, no abscess / thecal sac displacement → ADMIT: BED REST + IV ANTI-STAPH ABX
│      (7–10 d IV → total 4–6 wks; ± immobilisation; follow CRP)
├─ No response at 7–10 d → CT-GUIDED BIOPSY (rule out TB)
└─ Late / abscess + deficit / failed non-op → SURGICAL DEBRIDEMENT + ABX + BRACE
```

**Bacteraemic 5-yr, no collection** — **admit, bed rest + IV antibiotics**\
✗ Wrong options: oral antibiotics at home · surgery · cast alone (slow, recurrent)

**IV antibiotics** — speed resolution + ↓ recurrence

## 6 Complications

**Long-term** — disc space narrowing, **interbody fusion**, back pain

## X vs Y

| | Paediatric discitis | Spondylolysis |
|---|---|---|
| Labs | ↑ ESR/CRP | Normal |
| Exam | Pain flexion + extension, refuses to walk | Pain with **extension** (gymnast) |
| Rx | **IV antibiotics** | Activity restriction ± brace |

## How it's asked

**"Disc space infection in children — all true EXCEPT?"** → **loss of disc height is the earliest sign** (TO) — earliest = loss of lordosis

**"Order of radiographic findings?"** → **loss of lordosis → disc narrowing → endplate erosion**

**"6-yr gymnast, ↑ ESR/CRP, discitis on MRI — true?"** → **IV antibiotics usually resolve it**\
✗ Wrong options: urgent debridement · bracing first line (spondylolysis)

**"Best marker to monitor antibiotic response?"** → **CRP**

**"5-yr, gram-positive bacteraemia, no collection?"** → **admit for bed rest + IV antibiotics**
