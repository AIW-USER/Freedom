# Cervical Spine Anatomy

*Spine · 17 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Embryology

**Hox (homeobox) genes** — direct craniocaudal differentiation + segmentation

**Atlas (C1)** — **3 ossification centres**: 2 lateral masses (join posteriorly → posterior arch) + anterior arch (appears **\~1 yr**); lateral masses fuse to body at **7 yrs**

**Axis (C2)** — **5 centres**: 2 neural arches/lateral masses, 2 halves of dens (usually fused at birth; persistence = dens bicornis), body · **subdental (basilar / dentocentral) synchondrosis** open in **all children to 3 yrs**, half at 4–5, fused **\~6 yrs** · secondary tip centre (ossiculum terminale) appears **\~3 yrs**, fuses **\~12 yrs** (persistence = ossiculum terminale persistens, insignificant)

**3-yr-old with synchondrosis between dens and body** — **normal physiologic finding**\
✗ Wrong options: developmental anomaly · type 1/3 odontoid fracture

### Osteology

| Feature | Levels |
|---|---|
| **Transverse foramen** | **C1–C7** (all subaxial vertebrae in most people) |
| **Vertebral artery passes through** | **C6 → C1**; **C7 foramen usually empty** |
| **Bifid spinous process** | **C2–C6** (C7 not bifid) |
| **Carotid (Chassaignac) tubercle** | **C6** — anterior approach landmark |
| C8 | No vertebra; **C8 root** exists |
| Atlas | **No body, no spinous process** |

**Canal** — normal **17 mm**; **< 13 mm** → possible cord compression

**Alignment** — lordosis measured C2–C7

### Facets & kinematics

**Subaxial superior facets** — face **posteromedial at C3 → posterolateral at C7**\
✗ Wrong options: reverse

**Axial view** — **superior articular process is anterior** to the inferior articular process

**Motion** — greatest at **cephalad** subaxial levels (facet orientation)\
✗ Wrong options: caudal

| Joint | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Occiput-C1** | **\~50% of flexion-extension** |
| **C1-C2** (diarthrodial) | **50° of 100° rotation**, 10° of 110° flex-ext, 0° lateral bend |
| C2–C7 | 50° flex-ext, 50° rotation, **60° lateral bend** |

### Odontoid blood supply

**Watershed** between **apex (internal carotid branches)** and **base (vertebral artery branches)** → poor healing of **type II odontoid** fractures

### Ligaments

**Transverse** — limits anterior translation of atlas (strongest ligament of spine) · **apical + alar** — limit rotation

| Connects | Ligaments (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Atlas → occiput** | **Anterior atlanto-occipital membrane** (ALL extension) · **posterior atlanto-occipital membrane** · **ascending band of cruciate** |
| Axis (dens) → occiput | **Tectorial membrane** (PLL extension; C2–3 bodies → FM) · **alar** (dens → condyles) · **apical** (dens tip → FM, weak) |
| Axis → atlas | **Transverse atlantal ligament** · atlanto-alar part of alar · descending band of cruciate |

### Vertebral artery segments (TO)

| Segment | Course |
|---|---|
| V1 **Pre-foraminal** | Subclavian → front of C7 transverse process (between longus colli + scalenus anterior) |
| V2 **Foraminal** | Transverse foramina **C6 → C2** |
| V3 **Extradural (atlantic)** | Above C2 → **through C1 transverse foramen** → horizontal in occipital triangle → canal |
| V4 **Intradural** | Pierces dura → anterior medulla → joins opposite → **basilar** at lower pons |

**C1 transverse foramen contains** — **extradural segment of vertebral artery** + venous plexus + sympathetic plexus (TO)

**Medial / enlarged transverse foramen on CT before ACDF** — ↑ **vertebral artery injury** risk\
✗ Wrong options: IJV · ICA · vagus

### Pedicle & body dimensions (TO)

| Measure | Level |
|---|---|
| **Smallest transverse pedicle diameter** | **C3** (5.1 mm); cervical pedicles C2 largest, C3 smallest, ↑ C3 → C7 |
| Narrowest pedicle overall (average) | **T4** |
| Largest pedicle diameter / longest pedicle | **L5** |
| Shortest pedicle | T10 |
| Largest canal cross-section | **C2** (374 mm²) |
| Smallest canal | **T3–T8** |
| Smallest body | C3 + C5 · narrowest body width C4 |
| Tallest / shortest disc | L2-3 / T1-2 |
| Lumbar pedicles | Angle more medially caudally; L1 smallest lumbar |

### C1 lateral mass screw

**Trajectory** — **10° medial + 22° cephalad** · safe even with **anomalous vertebral artery** that precludes transarticular screws · medial angle protects **internal carotid**

### Muscles

**Sternocleidomastoid (TO)** — one side: **rotates head to OPPOSITE side** + tilts to same side · both: **flex neck + extend head**, lift head off pillow, accessory inspiration \
✗ Wrong options: rotate to same side (EXCEPT answer)

## X vs Y

| | C1 (atlas) | C2 (axis) | C7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body | No | ✓ + dens | Yes |
| Spinous process | No | Bifid | Non-bifid (prominens) |
| Ossification centres | 3 | 5 | — |
| Vertebral artery in foramen | Yes | Yes | Usually no |

## How it's asked

**"Structure in C1 transverse foramen?"** → **extradural segment of vertebral artery** (TO)

**"3-yr-old, synchondrosis between dens and body?"** → **normal**

**"Subaxial anatomy — true?"** → **all subaxial vertebrae have a foramen transversarium**\
✗ Wrong options: artery through all · rotation greatest caudally · facets posterolateral → posteromedial caudally · inferior process anterior

**"Medially placed, enlarged transverse foramen before ACDF — risk?"** → **vertebral artery injury**

**"C1 lateral mass screw trajectory?"** → **10° medial, 22° cephalad**

**"SCM functions EXCEPT?"** → **rotate neck to same side** (TO)

**"Smallest pedicle diameter (C2–C5)?"** → **C3** (TO)

**"Ligament connecting atlas to occiput?"** → **ascending band of cruciate** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: tectorial membrane · alar · TAL
