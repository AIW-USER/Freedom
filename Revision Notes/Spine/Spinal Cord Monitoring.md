# Spinal Cord Monitoring

*Spine · 55 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Modalities

| Modality | Monitors | Stimulate → record | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SSEP** | **Dorsal columns** (afferent; gracilis LE, cuneatus UE) | **Posterior tibial** (ankle) / peroneal; **ulnar/median** → **transcranial recording of somatosensory cortex** (or neck) | **25% sensitive, 100% specific** |
| **tcMEP** | **Lateral + ventral corticospinal tracts** (efferent; anterior 2/3, ASA territory) | **Transcranial motor cortex** → muscle (gastroc, soleus, EHL) | **100% sensitive, 100% specific** · **most sensitive early indicator** |
| **Spontaneous (mechanical) EMG** | Specific **nerve roots** | Surgical manipulation → muscle · **burst = contact (insignificant)** · **sustained train = significant traction/injury** | Over-sensitive |
| **Triggered (electrical) EMG** | **Pedicle breach** (medial/inferior wall → exiting root) | Stimulate screw → muscle · bone insulates | — |
| Stagnara wake-up test | Gross motor (move feet) | Intraoperative waking (amnestic) | Not continuous; delays |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Alert criteria

| Modality | Significant change |
|---|---|
| **SSEP** | **≥ 50% ↓ amplitude and/or ≥ 10% ↑ latency** (or loss) · not 10% amplitude / 50% latency |
| **MEP** | **> 50% ↓ amplitude** · **> 100 V ↑ threshold** (early injury) |
| **Triggered EMG** | **< 8 mA → breach** (Orthobullets, OB) · Stecker: < 10 mA inspect, **< 5 mA likely malpositioned** · **> 8 mA (e.g. 11, 12, 14 mA) = intact pedicle** |

**Lumbar (L4-5) surgery (TO)** — **triggered EMG**\
✗ Wrong options: SSEP · MEP · D-waves (cord monitors)

**Patient with existing motor deficit (TO)** — **transcranial MEPs**

## 5 Management

### Responding to signal loss

```
Significant signal change
├─ Check technical → LEADS, EQUIPMENT, interference (irrigation, cautery, fluoroscopy, warming blanket)
├─ Check anaesthesia → NO PARALYTIC / INHALATIONAL bolus, steady state
├─ Optimise physiology → RAISE MAP (80–90 TO · > 90 OB), Hb > 8, O2, reverse hypocapnia + hypothermia
├─ Surgical → REVERSE LAST MANOEUVRE (distraction/correction), REMOVE IMPLANT, widen decompression
│      (signals may take ≥ 10 min to return; close 3-column osteotomy)
└─ Still absent → STAGNARA WAKE-UP TEST
```

**TO EXCEPT** — ✗ Wrong options: **lower BP to reduce blood loss** (raising MAP recovers signals in \~50%)

**Corpectomy cage placed → 75% SSEP ↓ + MEP loss** — **remove the cage** and monitor

**Deformity correction → sustained 80% SSEP ↓, technical + anaesthetic causes excluded** — **reverse correction ± wake-up test**\
✗ Wrong options: steroids · remove all instrumentation · continue

**DMD child, diffuse 80% MEP ↓, MAP 55 on propofol** — **restore MAP > 90 and retest**\
✗ Wrong options: remove one screw (diffuse change) · switch to halogenated gas

**Most concerning in AIS correction** — **65% drop in LE MEPs**\
✗ Wrong options: 30% SSEP ↓ · 5% latency ↑ · 14 mA screw

**Most concerning in PSO** — **> 100 V MEP threshold rise**

### Reversible causes

**Commonest non-anaesthetic reversible cause** — **patient positioning** (nerve compression, plexus traction, neck alignment)\
✗ Wrong options: screw · ischaemia · retractor

**Unilateral cortical SSEP loss only during ACDF (subcortical + Erb's normal)** — **cerebral ischaemia from carotid retraction** (incomplete circle of Willis)

**Hypothermia SSEP drop (TO)** — **flood the field with warm saline** (fastest — exposed pathway)\
✗ Wrong options: warmer air, room, blankets

### Physiological effects on evoked potentials (TO)

| Variable | Effect |
|---|---|
| Cerebral blood flow | Normal to 20 · deteriorate 18–13 · **absent < 10–12 mL/100 g/min** |
| Hypotension | ↑ latency |
| ↑ ICP | ↑ latency, ↓ amplitude |
| PaO₂ | Deteriorate **< 40 mmHg** |
| Haematocrit | Latency ↑ at 10–15%; amplitude ↓ < 10% |
| PaCO₂ | Extreme hypocapnia **< 25 mmHg** |
| **Hypothermia** | **↑ latency**; no recording < 18 °C |
| Inhalational agents | Depress: **N₂O > isoflurane > halothane** |
| IV agents | **Propofol** no spinal effect (cortical depression recovers fast) · opioids, ketamine none · benzodiazepines mild |
