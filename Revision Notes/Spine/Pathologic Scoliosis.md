# Pathologic Scoliosis

*Spine · 44 / 69*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — scoliosis from a **benign bone tumour**, usually in the **posterior elements** · any age; OO + osteoblastoma typically **2nd decade**

**Mechanism** — painful **paraspinal muscle spasm**

| | Osteoid osteoma | Osteoblastoma |
|---|---|---|
| Size | **< 2 cm** nidus | **> 2 cm** |
| Site | **Apex of the concavity** of the curve · posterior elements > body | Posterior elements |
| Curve | **Rigid** (✗ Wrong options: flexible) | — |
| Pain | Severe, **night**, **relieved by NSAIDs** | Less severe, **NSAIDs help less** |
| Neuro | — | **May cause deficits** |
| Histology | **Same as peripheral OO** (✗ Wrong options: more malignant) | Less ossified; ± secondary ABC |

**NSAID mechanism** — nidus makes **PGE₂ + PGI₂**; **COX-2** high in nidal osteoblasts

### Syndromic scoliosis (TO)

| Syndrome | Scoliosis incidence |
|---|---|
| **Marfan (FBN1, fibrillin-1)** | **Two-thirds — highest** |
| Rett (**MECP2**) | Up to half |
| Prader-Willi, Down, Loeys-Dietz | Fewer |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **painful scoliosis**, back pain **worst at night**, NSAID relief · curve usually **mild**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| Standing AP + lateral | Level + severity · cortical thickening with **radiolucent nidus** |
| **Fine-cut CT** | **Best for outlining the lesion + planning** · low-attenuation nidus with central mineralisation, perinidal sclerosis |
| MRI | Neurovascular proximity · may show only oedema, **miss the nidus** |
| **Bone scan / SPECT** | **Markedly ↑ uptake** |

![Nidus on axial CT](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2059-scoli-axial.jpg)

### Differential of painful posterior-element lesions

| Lesion | Clue |
|---|---|
| **Osteoid osteoma** | Small sclerotic nidus, painful scoliosis |
| Osteoblastoma | Larger, less ossified, ± ABC |
| ABC | Purely lytic, no sclerotic nidus |
| **Eosinophilic granuloma** | **Vertebral body**, purely lytic, **vertebra plana** |
| Metastasis | Mixed blastic-lytic, no nidus |

## 5 Management

```
Pathologic scoliosis
├─ Minimal curve → NSAIDs + OBSERVE (up to 36 months to resolve)
│      (osteoblastoma rarely responds to NSAIDs)
└─ Painful, progressive scoliosis → EN BLOC RESECTION
       (RFA case by case — usually too near neural elements)
```

**Prognosis** — curve resolves after resection if done **within 15–18 months of curve onset** in a child **< 11 yrs**

## How it's asked

**"Spinal osteoid osteoma — true?"** → **occurs on the concave side of the curve**\
✗ Wrong options: flexible curve · never RFA · always body · more malignant

**"13-yr painful scoliosis, hot bone scan, sclerotic nidus in posterior element?"** → **osteoid osteoma**

**"Highest scoliosis incidence?"** → **Marfan syndrome** (TO)
