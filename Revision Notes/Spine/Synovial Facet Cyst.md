# Synovial Facet Cyst

*Spine · 62 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — rare · lumbar

**Level** — **L4-5 60–89% (most mobile segment)** · L3-4 \~14% · L5-S1 \~12% · cervical rare: C5-6, C7-T1\
✗ Wrong options: "L5-S1 commonest (sagittal facets", TO)

### Pathophysiology

**Cause** — **facet microinstability** → synovium extrudes through capsule · myxoid degeneration, fibroblast proliferation, ↑ hyaluronic acid · trauma controversial

**Associations (TO)** — facet OA (40.5%), **degenerative spondylolisthesis** (43.4%), disc degeneration (13.2%)

**Contents** — ganglion, flavum cysts; serous, mucoid, haemorrhagic

### Histology

| Cyst | Lining |
|---|---|
| **Synovial cyst** | **Synovial (cuboidal) cells over vascular granulation stroma** · clear/xanthochromic fluid |
| Ganglion | No synovial lining, gelatinous myxoid content |
| ✗ Traps | Concentric fibrocollagen lamellae = annulus · gelatinous matrix with oval chondrocytes = nucleus · nidus + halo = osteoid osteoma · storiform atypia = UPS |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **mechanical back pain**, **radicular leg pain** (lateral recess → **traversing root**, e.g. L5: EHL + hip abductor weakness), neurogenic claudication · **sudden deterioration = cyst haemorrhage** · CES rare

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| X-ray AP, lateral, **flexion-extension** | Usually normal · **segmental instability** |
| **MRI** | **Axial + sagittal T2** · **T2 hyperintense centre, hypointense rim** · T1 hypointense core · **rim enhancement** · contiguous with degenerative facet, narrowing lateral recess |
| Positional MRI | Supine 89% → **standing 97%** sensitivity (cysts enlarge standing) |
| T2 signal → aspiration | **High T2 (synovial fluid) → better CT rupture success** · low T2 (gelatinous/calcified) → worse |

![Facet synovial cyst on axial T2](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2066-facet-synovial-cyst.jpg)

**Differential** — ligamentum flavum, **Tarlov cyst**, pseudomeningocele, HNP

## 5 Management

```
Symptomatic facet cyst
├─ 1st line (6–8 wks) → NSAIDs, PT, rest, ESI
├─ 2nd line → CT-GUIDED CYST RUPTURE + facet steroid (50–75% relief at 1 yr; ~39–50% need surgery)
└─ Surgery
       ├─ Unilateral, stable → LAMINOTOMY / LAMINECTOMY + CYST EXCISION
       └─ Instability / spondylolisthesis, bilateral, central stenosis, recurrence → FACETECTOMY + INSTRUMENTED FUSION
```

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication / outcome |
|---|---|
| **Decompression + cyst excision** | Persistent symptoms, **unilateral** · **TO: surgery of choice without instability** · 80–90% success · risk: **high recurrent back pain + cyst within 2 yrs**, iatrogenic listhesis |
| **Facetectomy + instrumented fusion** | **Recurrence after laminectomy**, bilateral symptoms, central stenosis, **instability / degenerative spondylolisthesis** · some: **first-line surgery** · **lowest recurrent back pain + cyst recurrence** · 80–90% resolution |
| ✗ Wrong option: ALIF | Doesn't remove cyst or decompress lateral recess |
| ✗ Wrong option: uninstrumented fusion + facetectomy | ↑ pseudarthrosis |
| ✗ Wrong option: aspiration | High failure, short-term only |

**Technique** — unilateral laminotomy + medial facetectomy (burr) · develop dura–cyst plane, Allis traction, Epstein curette/Woodson · pedicle screw at **superior TP border × mid superior articular process**

## 6 Complications

**Recurrence** — high with excision alone → fusion

**Iatrogenic spondylolisthesis** — laminectomy without fusion → instrumented fusion

**Dural tear** — revision, **cyst adherent to dura (50–55%)** → **partial excision** (88% success, ↓ durotomy)

## How it's asked

**"Facet cyst — wrong statement?"** → **commonest at L5-S1** (it's L4-5) (TO)

**"Hip abductor + EHL weakness, MRI lesion off facet?"** → **facet synovial cyst**

**"Histology of this lesion?"** → **synovial cells over vascular granulation stroma**

**"Arrow on T2 at a degenerative facet, bright cyst in lateral recess?"** → **lumbar synovial cyst**

**"Recurrent synovial cyst after laminectomy?"** → **revision laminectomy + instrumented fusion**

**"Lowest recurrent back pain at 2 yrs?"** → **decompression + facetectomy + instrumented fusion**

**"Unstable L4-5 listhesis + cyst, EHL weakness — definitive?"** → **laminectomy + cyst excision + instrumented fusion**

**"Grade 1 listhesis + cyst — best long-term?"** → **L4-5 posterior instrumented fusion, laminectomy, cyst excision**
