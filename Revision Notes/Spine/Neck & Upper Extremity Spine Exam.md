### Cervical root anatomy

**Mismatch** — **C7 root exits below C6 pedicle** (C6 under C5) · extra C8 root (no C8 pedicle)

**Horizontal root** — **central and foraminal disc at same level hit the same root** (C6-7 disc → **C7**)

**Commonest degenerative levels** — **C4-5 (C5)** and **C5-6 (C6)**

## 3 Presentation

**Inspection** — sagittal/coronal alignment (kyphosis), scars (ulnar transposition, carpal tunnel), **café-au-lait** (NF), atrophy (deltoid + biceps in palsy)

**Normal cervical ROM** — flexion **50°** · extension **60°** · rotation **80°** · lateral bend **45°**

**Motor / sensory grading** — ASIA 0–5 for one muscle per root C5–T1 · sensory: always **pain (pin) + light touch**; vibration, temperature, 2-point for focused exam

### Gait

| Gait | Cause |
|---|---|
| Antalgic | Hip/knee pathology, severe radicular pain |
| Trendelenburg | Painful hip arthritis, **gluteus medius weakness** |
| **Wide-based shuffling** | **Myelopathy** / neurologic |
| Steppage / lateral swing | **Foot drop** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Provocative tests

| Test | Technique | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| **Spurling** | Rotate toward affected side + extend + **axial load**, 30 s → ipsilateral arm pain | **Specific, not sensitive** — acute **radiculopathy** |
| **Hoffmann** | Flick distal phalanx of long finger into extension → **thumb IP flexes** | **Sensitive, not specific** — **myelopathy** |
| **Lhermitte** | Neck flexion/extension → electric shock down spine/limbs | **Specific, not sensitive** — cord compression |

## X vs Y

| Root | Distinguishing finding |
|---|---|
| C5 | Weak shoulder abduction + ER |
| C6 | **Absent brachioradialis reflex**, **thumb numbness** |
| **C7** | **Weak wrist flexion + elbow extension**, middle-finger numbness, ↓ triceps |
| C8 | **↓ Grip** |

## How it's asked

**"Left foraminal C6-7 disc — exam?"** → **weak wrist flexion** (C7)\
✗ Wrong options: absent brachioradialis = C6 · thumb numbness = C6 · shoulder abduction/ER = C5 · grip = C8
