# Thoracic Spine Anatomy

*Spine · 65 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Alignment

**Thoracic kyphosis** — average **35°**, normal **20–50°** (✗ Wrong options: 40–50°) · apex **T5–T8** (T6-7) (✗ Wrong options: T4) · adolescents 30–40° (TO)

**Lumbar lordosis** — **majority below L3** (TO) · L4-S1 \~60% (TO) · 75% (Orthobullets, previous chapter) · **discs give 78%** (47°), bodies 12° · apex L3-L4 · range 20–60° (TO) · 20–80° (Orthobullets)

**Frontal plane** — straight; **> 10° = scoliosis**\
✗ Wrong options: < 20° normal

**Plumb line (sagittal vertical axis)** — from **C7 centre** → **posterosuperior corner of S1** (within 2 cm) · anterior = positive · posterior = negative · ideally from dens: anterior to thoracic, posterior to lumbar

**Pelvis** — sacral slope < 35° + low PI → flat short lordosis · > 45° + high PI → long curved lordosis (TO)

**Conus (TO)** — **lower border of L2 adult**, L3 neonate (Orthobullets: L1) · filum to first coccygeal segment

### Osteology

**Vertebra prominens** — C7 spinous process

**Costal facets** — bodies + transverse processes **T1–T9** · ribs → **most rigid region** of axial skeleton

**Bodies** — size ↑ T1 → T12 · canal dimension varies

### Facet orientation & motion

| Region | Coronal / sagittal | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| Cervical | 0° / 45° | All planes |
| **Thoracic** | **20° / 55°** (coronal facets), 6 DOF | Rotation; little flex-ext (ribs) · protects heart/lungs |
| Lumbar | 50° / 90° (sagittal facets) | Flex-ext; little rotation |

### Pedicles

| Measure | Fact |
|---|---|
| Wall | **Medial 2× thicker than lateral** |
| **Narrowest diameter** | **T4** (Orthobullets, OB) · T4–T6 dip; **T6** smallest among T1/T6/T12/L3/L5 (OB) |
| Largest | **T1** (upper) · **T12 (> L1)** at TL junction · T10, T11 > L1 · L5 largest lumbar · C7 largest cervical |
| T7 | Narrow on concave side in AIS |
| **Length** | T1 20 mm → **T4 14 mm (shortest)** → T10 20 mm |
| **Transverse angle** | **T1 25° medial, T2 15°, T3–T10 \~10°** (5–7°), minimal T11-12 (OB) · 10° mid-thoracic → 30° L5 (Orthobullets) |
| Sagittal angle | **15–17° cephalad** thoracic · neutral lumbar · caudal L5 |

### Interpedicular distance (TO)

**Minimum** — **T4–T8** · narrowed: **achondroplasia** (caudal narrowing), thanatophoric dysplasia · widened: **diastematomyelia**, burst fracture (middle column)

### Erector spinae

**Function** — trunk extension · **dorsal rami**

| Muscle (medial → lateral) | Attachment |
|---|---|
| **Spinalis** (medial) | Spinous → spinous process |
| **Longissimus** (intermediate) | Transverse → transverse process |
| **Iliocostalis** (lateral) | Ilium + ribs → ribs + TPs |

**Mnemonic** — "I Love Spine" lateral → medial

## 5 Management

### Thoracic approaches

| Approach | Access |
|---|---|
| Laminectomy / hemilaminectomy | Posterior (not for central disc) |
| Transpedicular | Posterolateral |
| Costotransversectomy | Lateral |
| Lateral extracavitary | Lateral, more ventral |
| **Anterior intracavitary** | **Thoracotomy / VATS** — central |

## How it's asked

**"Interpedicular distance minimal at?"** → **T4–T8** (TO)

**"Smallest average transverse pedicle diameter?"** → **T6** (of options)

**"Greatest pedicle isthmic width?"** → **T12**

**"Thoracic pedicle transverse angulation?"** → **25° T1, 15° T2, 10° T3–T10**

**"Plumb line passes through?"** → **posterosuperior margin of S1** (TO)

**"Correct normal alignment statement?"** → **majority of lumbar lordosis below L3** (TO)
