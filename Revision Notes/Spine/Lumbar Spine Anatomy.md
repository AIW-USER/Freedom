# Lumbar Spine Anatomy

*Spine · 37 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Alignment

**Lumbar lordosis** — average **60°** (range 20–80°) · apex **L3** · mostly from the **disc spaces**

### Osteology

**Vertebral bodies** — **largest** in the axial spine

| Part | Fact |
|---|---|
| Pedicles | Project posteriorly from posterolateral body corners |
| Laminae | Posteromedial from pedicles, join midline |
| **Mammillary processes** | **Separate ossification centres**; project posteriorly from the **superior articular facet** · pedicle screw landmark |
| **Pars interarticularis** | Bone between superior + inferior facets · **site of spondylolysis** |
| Facet (zygapophyseal) joint | From pedicle–lamina junction · **more coronal moving distally** |

![Facet orientation becomes coronal distally](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2071-facet-orientation.jpg)

### Ligamentum flavum

**Attachments (TO)** — origin **lower half of the anterior surface of the lamina above** · insertion **posterior surface + upper margin of the lamina below** · OB: origin ventral cranial lamina 60–70% of the way up (closer to superior edge), insertion superior edge of caudal lamina — read the options

**MRI** — **low signal on T2** · thickened flavum → stenosis\
✗ Wrong options: synovial cyst and epidural CSF are high signal

### Pedicle anatomy

**Landmarks** — superior-inferior midpoint = **midpoint of transverse process** · medial-lateral midpoint = **lateral border of pars**

**Pedicle screw entry (TO)** — **mammillary process, lateral to pars** · lumbar: intersection of line through lateral facet and line bisecting transverse process, \~1 mm below the facet joint · laterality: **mammillary > intersection > pars** start points

**Danger (TO)** — lumbar root lies in the **upper third** of the foramen, just **inferior to the medial pedicle wall** → **medial or inferior breach more dangerous** than lateral/superior

| Measure | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Medial angulation** | Increases distally: **L1 12° → L5 30° → S1 39°** · largest horizontal angle at L5 (TO) |
| **Smallest lumbar diameter** | **L1** (OB, Orthobullets) |
| Smallest overall | **T4** (Orthobullets) · **T5** horizontal (TO) — read the options |
| Widest | **L5** horizontal; **T11** sagittal · S1 \~19 mm |
| Sagittal angle | Caudal at L5, cephalad L3–T1 (TO) |
| Thoracic pedicle | 62–79% cancellous · **lateral wall thinner than medial** |

![Pedicle diameter by level](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2071-shows-pedicle-diameter.jpg)

### Cortical : cancellous ratio

| Highest → lowest |
|---|
| **Thoracic pedicles** |
| Lumbar pedicles |
| Vertebral bodies |
| **Sacrum** (lowest) |

### Cervical pedicles (TO)

**Size** — **C3 smallest**, C2 + C7 largest · C3–C5 need < 4.5 mm screws

**Angulation** — medial at all levels: **most C5**, least C2 + C7 · upward at C2-3, parallel C4-5, downward C6-7

**Vertebral artery** — at risk **C3–C6** (lateral cortex thinnest; medial cortex \~2× thicker) · less at risk C2 + C7-T1 (outside foramen at C7)

### Neural structures

**Root rule** — exits under the **same-numbered pedicle** · central/paracentral → traversing root · far lateral → exiting root

**Cervical vs lumbar** — cervical C6 under C5 pedicle (mismatch, extra C8 root) · lumbar L5 under L5 (match) · vertical lumbar roots → paracentral + foraminal discs hit **different** roots

**Cauda equina** — begins \~**L1**

### Pain innervation

**Disc** — **sinuvertebral nerve** → **outer annulus only** (nociception + proprioception) · discogenic pain: axial, worse sitting/bending, no radicular signs\
✗ Wrong options: nucleus · DRG direct

**Dura** — intradural communicating nerve (central herniation pain)

**Dorsal ramus branches** — **medial → facet capsule + multifidus** · intermediate → longissimus, spinalis · lateral → iliocostalis + skin

**Facet dual supply** — each facet from the **medial branch of the level above + same level** · **L4-L5 facet block = L3 + L4 medial branches**\
✗ Wrong options: ventral rami · lateral/intermediate branches

**Ventral rami** — anteromedial trunk

### Blood supply

**Segmental arteries** — dorsal branches → dura + posterior elements · tie off for corpectomy

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Lumbopelvic parameters

| Parameter | Definition | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **Pelvic incidence (PI)** | Line S1 endplate centre → femoral head centre vs **perpendicular to S1 endplate** | **PI = PT + SS** · **fixed, independent of pelvic position** |
| **Pelvic tilt (PT)** | Same line vs **vertical** | PT = PI − SS |
| **Sacral slope (SS)** | S1 endplate vs **horizontal** | SS = PI − PT |

**PI** — correlates with spondylolisthesis severity (**Meyerding grade**)

![PI = PT + SS](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2071-combined-pi-ss-pt-moved.jpg)

### Image-guided interventions

| Procedure | Use |
|---|---|
| **Selective nerve root injection** | Unilateral radicular symptoms · diagnostic + therapeutic · **transforaminal (outside-in)** |
| **Facet injection** | Confirms facet as pain generator · may be therapeutic |
| **Epidural injection** | **Lumbar spinal stenosis** |
| **Discography** | Controversial · **concordant** vs discordant pain · shows annular tears |

**Needle** — 22–25 G, CT or fluoroscopy

## 5 Management

### Surgical approaches

| Approach | Facts |
|---|---|
| Posterior midline | PLIF / TLIF |
| **Wiltse paraspinal** | Far lateral pathology |
| **Retroperitoneal (anterolateral)** | **Aortic bifurcation L4-5** · **superior hypogastric plexus on L5 body → retrograde ejaculation** |
| **Lateral transpsoas** | **Left side** (aorta tolerates retraction) · ideal **L1-2, L2-3, L3-4** · **L4-5** = highest plexus risk (hip flexion + knee extension weakness) · T12-L1 needs rib + diaphragm takedown |

### Transpsoas safe zone

**Cranial → caudal** — **safe zone shrinks because the lumbar plexus moves ventral** · triggered EMG helps\
✗ Wrong options: vessels move; great vessels roughly constant

| Nerve injured | Result |
|---|---|
| **Ilioinguinal + iliohypogastric** | **Delayed (\~2 wks) abdominal wall paresis** — bulge mimics hernia (CT excludes) · groin paraesthesia · resolves with observation |
| Genitofemoral | Genital numbness, **lost cremasteric reflex** |
| Femoral | Knee extension weakness, anterior thigh numbness |
| LFCN | Lateral thigh (aggressive retroperitoneal fat dissection) |
| Obturator | Medial thigh numbness, **adduction weakness** |

**Risk factors** — multilevel, long retraction, excessive bed break, hips not flexed

![Nerves at risk in transpsoas approach](../../MCQ/attachments/q5918-illa-lateral-transpsoas-nerves-labeled.jpg)

### Retroperitoneal relations (TO)

**Anterior to L3 body** — answer not keyed by source (options aorta, ureter, right iliac artery, IVC) · Orthobullets: aorta bifurcates at **L4-5**, so the iliac arteries lie below L3

## X vs Y

| | Cervical root | Lumbar root |
|---|---|---|
| Course | Horizontal | Vertical |
| Pedicle | C6 under C5 (mismatch) | L5 under L5 (match) |
| Central vs foraminal disc | **Same root** | **Different roots** |

## How it's asked

**"Entry point for pedicle screw?"** → **mammillary process, lateral to pars** (TO)

**"T12–L4 fusion — smallest screw diameter?"** → **L1**

**"Highest cortical : cancellous ratio?"** → **thoracic pedicles**

**"Pelvic parameter independent of pelvic position?"** → **PT + SS (= pelvic incidence)**

**"Transpsoas safe zone cranial → caudal?"** → **decreases — lumbar plexus moves ventral**

**"Abdominal bulge 2 wks after L3-5 lateral fusion?"** → **ilioinguinal / iliohypogastric injury**

**"Ligamentum flavum origin and insertion?"** → **lower half anterior surface of lamina above → posterior surface + upper margin of lamina below** (TO)

**"Arrow on T2 axial — thick low-signal band?"** → **ligamentum flavum**

**"Discogenic pain pathway?"** → **sinuvertebral nerve to the annulus fibrosus**

**"Block for L4-L5 facet RFA?"** → **medial branches of L3 + L4 dorsal rami**
