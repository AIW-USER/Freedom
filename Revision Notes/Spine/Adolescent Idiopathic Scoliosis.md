# Adolescent Idiopathic Scoliosis

*Spine · 1 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — coronal deformity, **Cobb > 10°**, age **10–18 yrs**; **commonest type of scoliosis**

**Incidence** — **3%** for 10–20° curves · **0.3%** for curves > 30°

**Sex ratio** — **1:1** small curves · **10:1 female** for curves > 30°

**Commonest pattern** — **right thoracic** curve + **thoracic hypokyphosis** (T5–T12 ≈ 8°; normal 20–50°)\
✗ Wrong options: right thoracic + hyperkyphosis · left thoracic

**Family history** — most patients **positive**

### Aetiology

**Pathophysiology** — **unknown / multifactorial**: hormonal (**melatonin**), brain stem, proprioception, platelet, **calmodulin**

**Neurocentral synchondrosis (NCS)** — cartilaginous plate between **centrum + posterior neural arches**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior arches

| NCS closure order | Age |
|---|---|
| **Cervical** | 5–6 yrs |
| **Lumbar** | 11–12 yrs (\~100% closed at age 10) |
| **Thoracic** | **14–17 yrs** (\~50% closed at age 10) |

**NCS order trap** — **cervical → lumbar → thoracic**\
✗ Wrong options: cervical → thoracic → lumbar

### Skeletal maturity & progression risk

**Best predictor of progression** — curve magnitude at **peak height velocity (PHV)**; **> 30° at PHV** → **> 70%** reach surgical range\
✗ Wrong options: > 20° at menarche · > 30° at Risser 2 · skeletal age 12

**PHV timing** — **before menarche** and **before Risser 1** (occurs during **Risser 0**) · menarche ≈ 1 yr after PHV · girls mature **\~1.5 yrs (18 months) after menarche**

**Skeletal method best matching curve acceleration / PHV** — **Tanner-Whitehouse III RUS** (radius, ulna, metacarpals + phalanges of digits 1, 3, 5) (✗ Wrong options: Greulich-Pyle · Oxford · Risser · Lenke) · TW **stage III** (✗ Wrong options: stage 2)

**PHV correlates closest with (TO)** — **olecranon apophysis fusion**; olecranon **stage 5 = PHV** (stage 1 = 3.0 yrs before, stage 7 = 3.4 yrs after)\
✗ Wrong options: triradiate closure · Risser 1 · menarche

**Other PHV surrogates (TO)** — modified Oxford score **19–21** · calcaneal apophysis **stage 3** · humeral head ossification between **stages 2–3** · Sauvegrain elbow score · TW2 = most accurate/reproducible, GP = simpler, less reliable in Asians

| Risk factor for progression | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Curve magnitude (immature)** | **> 25°** before maturity → continues to progress |
| **Curve magnitude (mature)** | Thoracic **> 50°** · lumbar **> 40°** → progress **1–2°/yr** |
| **Remaining growth** | Age **< 12 yrs** · Tanner **< 3** (F) · **Risser 0–1** · **open triradiate cartilage** · PHV |
| **Curve type** | Thoracic > lumbar · double > single |

| Risser | Iliac apophysis | Growth |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | No ossification | Covers **first 2/3 of pubertal spurt** → **greatest velocity of linear growth**; highest progression risk with open triradiate |
| **I** | 25% | Velocity descending |
| **II** | 50% | GT apophysis unites; **\~3 cm sitting growth** left, no more LL growth |
| **III** | 75% | \~2 cm sitting growth |
| **IV** | 100% ossified, not fused | \~1 cm sitting growth; = **skeletal maturity** for bracing |
| **V** | Fused | Complete maturity |

![Risser staging of the iliac apophysis](../../MCQ/attachments/q4696-illa-ossification-of-the-iliac-apophysis-creates-risser.jpg)

**Open triradiate + < 18 h brace wear** — factor **most correlated with progression** (Karol) → Risser 0 needs **≥ 18 h/day**\
✗ Wrong options: curves < 25° · Risser 1 · Sanders 6

**Karol – progression to ≥ 50° in brace** — Risser 0 **44.2%** · Risser 1 **6.9%** · Risser 2 **0%**

### Natural history into adulthood

**Untreated AIS (Weinstein 50-yr)** — ↑ **chronic back pain** (✓)\
✗ Wrong options: difficult vaginal delivery / ↑ caesarean · pulmonary dysfunction at 38°

**Cobb > 60° at maturity** — ↑ **acute + chronic back pain** vs controls\
✗ Wrong options: premature death · disability · depression · ADL limitation

**Curves < 30° at maturity** — rarely progress · thoracic **> 50°** most likely to progress

**Curves > 90°** — cardiopulmonary dysfunction, early death, pain, ↓ self-image

## 3 Presentation

**School screening** — **7° on scoliometer** (Adams forward bend) abnormal → correlates with **\~20° Cobb**

**Adams forward bending** — axial rotation (rib prominence) = **structural** curve · **sitting** forward bend eliminates **leg-length inequality**

**Back pain** — 25–30% of all adolescents; mild, intermittent, non-limiting + typical right curve → **stretching + lighter backpack** · atypical pain → MRI / bone scan\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · bone scan · TLSO for 20° · pain clinic

### Atypical features requiring MRI

| Exam finding (non-idiopathic) | Suggests |
|---|---|
| **Hairy patch** | Spina bifida / **diastematomyelia** |
| Subcutaneous tumours · pigmented lesions · **café-au-lait** | **Neurofibromatosis** |
| Excessive flexibility | **Ehlers-Danlos / Marfan** |
| Joint contractures | CP / arthrogryposis |
| Craniofacial anomalies | Syndromic scoliosis |
| Limb weakness | Neuromuscular scoliosis |
| **Cavovarus foot** | Neural axis anomaly → MRI |
| **Asymmetric abdominal reflexes** | **Syringomyelia** (curve with little rotation) |

**Truncal shift (TO)** — loss of coronal balance in **large idiopathic** curves → NOT a sign of non-idiopathic scoliosis (EXCEPT answer)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**First films (TO)** — **standing PA + lateral whole spine**; PA = ↓ breast radiation (AP gives better quality)\
✗ Wrong options: standing AP · supine · segment only

**Cobb angle** — lines along the **most tilted** end vertebrae · **> 10° = scoliosis** · error **3–5°**

| Radiographic term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Coronal balance** | **C7 plumb line** vs **central sacral vertical line (CSVL)** |
| **Sagittal balance** | C7 plumb vs posterosuperior corner of S1 |
| **Stable zone** | Between vertical lines from lumbosacral facets |
| **Stable vertebra** | Most proximal vertebra most closely **bisected by CSVL** |
| **Neutral vertebra** | **Rotationally neutral** (spinous process equidistant from pedicles) |
| **End vertebra** | **Most tilted** from horizontal apical vertebra |
| **Apical vertebra** | Disc / vertebra **deviated farthest** from centre |
| **Touched vertebra (TV)** | **Last cephalad vertebra touched by CSVL** |
| **Clavicle angle** | **Best predictor of post-op shoulder balance** |

**MRI** — **posterior fossa → conus**; rules out Chiari / syrinx / cord cyst / tether

| MRI indicated | NOT an MRI indication |
|---|---|
| **Left thoracic curve** | **Left thoracolumbar / lumbar curve** (TO – common pattern) |
| Short angular curve · **apical kyphosis** / excessive kyphosis | Age 10–18 |
| **Rapid progression** | Risser stage |
| Neurologic signs, pain (atypical) | Curve magnitude alone (without rapid progression) |
| **Asymmetric abdominal reflexes** · foot deformity (cavovarus) | Mild activity-related back pain |
| **Male**, structural abnormality | Routine initial work-up |

**Left thoracic curve in a neurologically normal child** — **total spine MRI** = next step\
✗ Wrong options: bracing · PT · bending films · CT

### Lenke classification

**Components (TO)** — **curve type 1–6** + **lumbar modifier A/B/C** + **sagittal (thoracic) modifier −/N/+**\
✗ Wrong options: "thoracic modifier A/B/C"

**Regions** — proximal thoracic: apex **T3–T5** · main thoracic: apex **T6 → T11–12 disc** · thoracolumbar: apex **T12–L1** · lumbar: apex **L1–2 disc → L4**

**Major curve** — largest Cobb, always structural

**Structural minor curve** — residual **≥ 25° on supine side-bending** OR kyphosis **≥ 20°** (T2–T5 for PT; **T10–L2** for TL/L)

| Lenke type | Name | Structural curves |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Main thoracic | MT only |
| **2** | **Double thoracic** (TO) | **MT (major) + PT** structural; TL/L non-structural |
| **3** | Double major | MT (major) + TL/L structural; PT non-structural |
| **4** | Triple major | All three structural |
| **5** | TL/L | TL/L only (major) |
| **6** | TL/L–MT | TL/L major (≥ 5° > MT) + MT structural |

| Lumbar modifier | CSVL at lumbar apex |
|---|---|
| **A** | Between pedicles |
| **B** | Touches apical pedicle |
| **C** | Completely medial to apical body/pedicle |

| Sagittal modifier (T5–T12) | Kyphosis |
|---|---|
| **−** | **< 10°** (hypokyphosis) |
| **N** | **10–40°** |
| **+** | **> 40°** (hyperkyphosis) |

![Lenke classification summary table](../../MCQ/attachments/TO19356190-8.png)

**King-Moe** — 5 thoracic curve types for **Harrington** instrumentation; coronal only, no sagittal plane → replaced by Lenke

## 5 Management

```
AIS by Cobb angle + maturity
├─ < 25° (any maturity) → OBSERVE, serial X-rays
├─ 25–45°, Risser 0–2 (flexible, immature) → BRACE
│      (goal = stop progression, not correct)
├─ 25–45°, Risser 4–5 / mature → OBSERVE
└─ > 45–50° → POSTERIOR SPINAL FUSION
       └─ Very young (Risser 0, girls < 10, boys < 13) / > 75° / stiff → ANTERIOR + POSTERIOR
```

### Bracing – indications, wear time & efficacy

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| **Cobb range** | **25–45°** (Orthobullets) · **20–40°** (OB) · **20–25° with documented progression** → brace — read the options |
| **Maturity** | **Risser 0, 1, 2** · Sanders 1–3 · age 10–15 |
| **Prescribed** | **16–23 h/day** until maturity or surgery |
| **Minimum effective (actual)** | **> 12 h/day** (12.9 h → **90–93% success**, BrAIST) (✗ Wrong options: 6–8 h · 9–10 h) |
| **Dose-response** | **17.7 h → 93%**; > 12 h → 82% no progression; < 7 h → 69% progressed; prescribed 16 vs 23 h had no effect |
| **Effect** | **50% ↓ need for surgery** · **NNT = 4** (compliant) |
| **Best predictor of brace success** | **Time in brace** (✗ Wrong options: in-brace correction · PT first) |

**Brace success / failure (TO)** — success = **< 5°** progression · failure = **≥ 6°** progression at discontinuation, **> 45°** before/at maturity, or switch to surgery\
✗ Wrong options: "success = correction > 5°"

**Skeletal maturity = stop brace** — **Risser 4** · **< 1 cm height change over 2 visits 6 months apart** · **2 yrs post-menarche**

**Poor brace prognosis** — poor in-brace correction · **hypokyphosis** (relative CI) · **male** · **obese** · non-compliant

| Brace (TO) | Curve apex |
|---|---|
| **Milwaukee (CTLSO)** | **Above T7** (TO, Orthobullets) · at/proximal to **T6** (OB) · double thoracic / apex above T8 do not respond to TLSO |
| **TLSO / Boston (underarm)** | **T7 or below** (e.g. apex T12) |
| **Charleston bending** | **Night-time** only |

**Sanders staging** — **AP hand X-ray** + curve magnitude → risk of progression **> 50° despite bracing** in **Lenke 1 + 3** curves · stage 3 = **complete capping of phalangeal epiphyses**

| 25° curve + Sanders stage | Risk > 50° despite brace |
|---|---|
| **1** | **99%** |
| **3** | **29%** → brace most likely to change natural history (✓) |
| **4–8** | **0%** |
| 45° curve | \~100% |

![Sanders skeletal maturity staging (hand radiograph)](../../MCQ/attachments/q8775-illa-sanders-classification.jpg)

**Bracing traps** — **scoliosis-specific exercises / PT** not effective · spinal manipulation unsupported · 12° curve with 6° progression → observe · Risser 4 / Sanders 7 + 30° → observe · boy with 50° → surgery · bracing large (> 45°) curves can still **delay** surgery to maturity · congenital curve → no bracing

### Fusion level selection

**PSF indications** — Cobb **> 45°** (Orthobullets) / **> 50°** (OB) · **gold standard** for thoracic + double major curves · usable for all idiopathic types

**ASF** — **thoracolumbar / lumbar** curves with **normal sagittal profile**; saves lumbar levels; fuse **end → end vertebra**; not if **thoracic hyperkyphosis > 40°** (↑ pseudarthrosis)

| Technique | Fusion levels |
|---|---|
| **Typical** | Proximal end vertebra → 1–2 levels cephalad to **stable** vertebra; double/triple major → **distal end vertebra** |
| **Harrington** | 1 above + 2 below end vertebrae if within **stable zone** |
| **Moe** | To **neutral** vertebra |
| **Lenke** | All major curves + minor curves that are **stiff or kyphotic** |
| **Touched vertebra** | Fuse **to or caudad to TV** → best correction; cephalad to TV → **truncal shift**, poor long-term outcome |

![Touched vertebra: last cephalad vertebra touched by CSVL](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2053-determination-of-tv.jpg)

**Avoid L4 / L5** — Cochran: ↑ **low back pain** with fusion to **L5** (less with L4) · pelvis almost **never** needed in AIS

**Lenke 1B (65° MT, flexible PT + lumbar)** — **PSF of thoracic curve only**\
✗ Wrong options: T2–L4 · brace · stapling · A/P

**Selective thoracic fusion** — **AVT ratio** = thoracic AVT (C7 plumb → thoracic apex) ÷ TL/L AVT (CSVL → lumbar apex); **> 1.2** → selective thoracic OK (e.g. 45/15 = **3.0** ✓)

**LIV to prevent DJK / adding-on** — include **stable sagittal vertebra** (≥ 50% of body anterior to **posterior sacral vertical line**) · **Lenke 1A-R (L4 tilted right)** → ↑ adding-on · e.g. TL kyphosis T8–L2 with L3 the first SSV → **LIV = L3**\
✗ Wrong options: T12 · L2 · L5 · pelvis

**Isolated long PSF indicated** — **13-yr Risser 3 with 55°**\
✗ Wrong options: JIS → needs anterior too · IIS → Mehta cast · hemivertebra + bar → bar excision · 27° → brace

**Vertebral body stapling** — small thoracic curves **< 35°**, immature

### Surgical technique

**Pedicle screws** — insertional torque ∝ **pullout resistance**; ↑ by **undertapping 1 mm** + **straightforward trajectory**; not tapping may be stronger still · segmental screws → ↑ coronal correction, ↓ need for anterior release\
✗ Wrong options: anatomic trajectory ↓ torque

**Pedicle subtraction osteotomy (PSO)** — posterior closing wedge hinged on **anterior column**, usually **L2 / L3** · commonest complication **pseudarthrosis 25–30%** · 1 PSO ≈ **3 Smith-Petersen** osteotomies, more blood loss\
✗ Wrong options: cord ischaemia · aorta < 1–2% · neuro deficit < 10%

**Opioid-sparing** — **short-course post-op steroids** ↓ opioid use\
✗ Wrong options: bracing · avoiding drain (drains ↓ opioids) · delaying PT · pre-op opioids ↑ use

**ED visits after PSF** — \~10%, mostly minor → **older age + more fusion levels**\
✗ Wrong options: BMI · Lenke type · ERAS ↓ visits · pelvic fixation/Ponte

### Neuromonitoring

**Standard of care** — **SSEP + MEP**; MEPs warn earliest of cord dysfunction, change faster than SSEPs

**Alert thresholds** — amplitude drop **> 50%** (Orthobullets) · critical: **MEP 80%**, **SSEP 50% / latency 10%** (OB)

```
Neuromonitoring signal drop
├─ Technical / anaesthetic cause? → CHECK leads + IV agents
├─ Not yet critical (e.g. MEP 60%, SSEP 30%) → REPOSITION, raise BP, check Hb
│      (do not deepen anaesthesia — suppresses MEPs)
└─ Persistent / critical → REVERSE CORRECTION
       └─ Still lost → STAGNARA WAKE-UP TEST → remove implants if spine stable
```

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Neurologic injury** | Paraplegia **1:1000**; ↑ with **kyphosis**, excessive correction, **sublaminar wires** |
| **Pseudarthrosis** (1–2%) | Late pain, progression, **rod breakage**; asymptomatic + no loss of correction → observe |
| **Infection** (1–2%) | **Late pain**, clean wound; **P. acnes** commonest delayed organism (**2-week** culture); I&D + retain hardware if not loose and < 6 months |
| **Flat back** | Loss of lumbar lordosis → fatigue + back pain; rare with sagittal rod contouring; Rx **posterior closing wedge osteotomies** (± anterior release) |
| **Crankshaft** | Continued **anterior growth** after **PSF alone** in very young → rotation; prevent with **anterior discectomy + fusion** |
| **SMA syndrome** | **3rd part of duodenum** compressed between SMA (arises **L1**) and aorta → bilious vomiting in **1st post-op week**, electrolyte disturbance; risk: height **< 50th**, weight **< 25th** centile, kyphosis; Rx **NG tube + IV fluids** |
