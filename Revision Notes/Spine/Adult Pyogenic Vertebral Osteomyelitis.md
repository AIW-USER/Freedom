# Adult Pyogenic Vertebral Osteomyelitis

*Spine · 3 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology – lumbar spine most common

**Spectrum (spondylodiscitis)** — discitis · vertebral osteomyelitis · epidural abscess

**Age** — median **50–60 yrs**

| Region | Share |
|---|---|
| **Lumbar** | **50–60%** |
| Thoracic | 30–40% |
| Cervical | **\~10%** (Orthobullets) · 20% (OB) |
| Sacrum | Rare |

**Order** — **lumbar > thoracic > cervical > sacrum**\
✗ Wrong options: lumbar = thoracic · cervical first

**Risk factors** — **IV drug use** · **diabetes** · **recent systemic infection (UTI, pneumonia)** · malignancy · immunosuppression · obesity · **malnutrition (albumin < 3 g/dL)** · trauma · smoking

### Organisms

| Organism | Setting |
|---|---|
| **Staph aureus** | **Commonest (50–65%)** |
| Staph epidermidis | 2nd commonest |
| Gram-negatives | ↑ last decade; GU + respiratory sources |
| **Pseudomonas** | **IV drug users** |
| **Salmonella** | **Sickle cell disease** |
| Fungal · TB | Immunocompromised |

**Sources (TO)** — **genitourinary 29%**, soft tissue 13%, upper respiratory 11% · haematogenous via **Batson plexus** commonest · blood/urine cultures positive 16–30% · deficit from epidural abscess or **arterial thrombosis**

**Commonest fungal infection of spine (TO)** — **Aspergillus**; Candida + Aspergillus = commensals entering via IV lines/implants/surgery · ↑ during COVID (steroid overuse) · Coccidioides + Blastomyces = geographic\
✗ Wrong options: Candida · Cryptococcus · Blastomyces

## 2 Mechanism

**Haematogenous seeding (commonest)** — arterial or venous → **endplate** (low-flow vascular anastomosis) → **disc** → adjacent endplate

**Direct inoculation** — penetrating trauma, open fracture, surgery

**Contiguous spread** — retropharyngeal + retroperitoneal abscess

**Neurologic deficit (10–20%)** — direct neural infection · **epidural abscess** compression · **instability**

**Epidural abscess** — pus/granulation between dura and epidural fat · in **\~18%** of spondylodiscitis · **50%** of abscess patients have neuro symptoms

**Psoas abscess** — associated

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — severe, **insidious**, unrelenting, worse with activity; **night pain** → infection / malignancy

**Fever** — **only 33%**

**History** — UTI, pneumonia, skin infection, transplant

**Neuro** — radiculopathy / myelopathy in 10–20% → careful neuro exam

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Genes & lab markers

| Test | Fact |
|---|---|
| **WBC** | ↑ in only **\~50%** → insensitive |
| **ESR** | ↑ in **90%**; serial monitoring, less reliable than CRP |
| **CRP** | ↑ in **90%**; **best for tracking treatment response** |
| **Blood cultures** | **All patients before antibiotics**; least invasive; positive **\~33% (25–66%)**; **85% accurate** when positive; ↑ yield off antibiotics + during fever |
| **CT-guided biopsy** | If blood cultures negative + no urgent surgery; diagnostic **68–86%** (Orthobullets) · needle \~70%, open > 80% (OB); send **aerobic, anaerobic, fungal, AFB** |
| **Open biopsy** | Non-invasive fails; anterior, costotransversectomy, transpedicular |

**Post-discectomy: severe pain + spasm, no fever, normal TLC + ESR, CRP very high (TO)** — **infective discitis**\
✗ Wrong options: instability · pseudomeningocele · residual disc

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Findings |
|---|---|
| **X-ray** | Delayed by weeks · **loss of psoas shadow** (first days) · **disc space narrowing at 7–10 days** · **endplate erosion/sclerosis at 10–21 days** · local osteopenia |
| **CT** | Bony destruction, abscess extent |
| **MRI with gadolinium** | **Gold standard**; **most sensitive (96%)** + specific (93%); **best to separate infection from tumour**; early scan may mimic degeneration → repeat |
| **Tc-99m bone scan** | If MRI impossible; **90% sensitive, not specific**; + gallium-67 → ↑ sensitivity + specificity |
| **Indium-111** | Poor sensitivity (**17%**) |

![Disc space narrowing with endplate erosion on lateral X-ray](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2025-case-a-xray-lumbar-lat-shows-disc-space-narrowing-mgh.jpg)

**Classic discitis MRI triad** — **T2 bright disc** (fluid/pus) · **T1 dark**, disc–body margin lost · **T1 fat-sat + gad: rim enhancement** of abscess + adjacent bodies → pathognomonic\
✗ Wrong options: lymphoma · primary epidural abscess · DDD · early AS

![Spondylodiscitis on T2 and T1 MRI](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2025-case-a-mri-t2-and-t1.jpg)

**MRI limit** — less useful than **labs** for treatment response

**Infection vs tumour** — infection = **disc involved**, endplate erosion, marked inflammation · tumour spares disc

**Discitis vs Modic I** — Modic I: T1 dark, T2 bright marrow but **disc desiccated (normal / dark T2)**, no endplate erosion, no soft-tissue mass, normal ESR/CRP

**Pyogenic vs TB on MRI (TO)** — collection with **relatively preserved disc** → **pyogenic** (acute, presents early) · **collapsed disc**, subligamentous spread → TB (late presentation) · end result of spinal TB = **bony ankylosis** (weight-bearing + cancellous surfaces)\
✗ Wrong options: fibrous ankylosis as in limb joints

## 5 Management

```
Suspected spondylodiscitis
├─ Septic / critically ill → CULTURES (± CT aspirate) THEN BROAD-SPECTRUM IV
│      (vancomycin + 3rd-gen cephalosporin)
├─ Stable, neuro intact → MRI + BLOOD CULTURES first
│      └─ Organism found → ORGANISM-SPECIFIC IV ABX
│      └─ Cultures negative → CT-GUIDED BIOPSY
└─ Deficit / instability / abscess / cervical → DEBRIDE + STABILISE
```

### Diagnosis – MRI, blood cultures & CT-guided biopsy

**Organism ID is mandatory** before targeted therapy · stable patient → **hold antibiotics** until cultures/biopsy\
✗ Wrong options: empirical IV antibiotics · INH-RIF-PZA before biopsy in suspected TB

**IVDU with UTI, back pain, ↑ ESR** — **admit + blood cultures + MRI with/without gad**\
✗ Wrong options: oral abx · bone scan · renal US

**Blood cultures negative, neuro intact** — **CT-guided closed biopsy**\
✗ Wrong options: IV abx · open surgery · repeat MRI

**MSSA in blood cultures** — **organism-specific IV antibiotics**\
✗ Wrong options: CT biopsy · debridement

### Treatment – organism-specific antibiotics

**Course** — **6–12 weeks** ± bracing · IV until improvement (\~**4–6 wks**) → oral · success **80%**

**Bracing** — pain + deformity prevention · **cervical → rigid cervicothoracic orthosis or halo**

**Resistant organisms** — MRSA, VRSA, VRE → **linezolid, daptomycin**

| Infection | Agent |
|---|---|
| Gram-positive | Cefazolin, vancomycin |
| Gram-negative | Fluoroquinolone (ciprofloxacin), 3rd-gen cephalosporin |
| **Fungal** | **Amphotericin B** first line (± azoles) |
| TB | **INH + rifampicin + PZA + ethambutol** |

### Operative criteria

| Debridement + stabilisation indicated |
|---|
| **Cervical** osteomyelitis / cervical epidural abscess |
| **Progressive neurologic deficit** |
| **Progressive deformity / gross instability** |
| **Refractory** to medical therapy |
| **Large abscess** |

**Open biopsy alone** — cultures + CT biopsy fail; lumbar disease without canal abscess

### Surgical technique

**Gold standard** — **anterior debridement + strut graft ± posterior instrumentation** · posterior debridement alone usually inadequate

**Graft** — autograft (tricortical iliac crest, rib, fibula) safe in acute infection · allograft OK · **titanium mesh cage + autograft + posterior instrumentation** → best deformity correction

**Instrumentation in active infection** — controversial (staged vs single stage) · **titanium > stainless steel**

**Posterior instrumentation** — severe kyphosis or multilevel anterior construct

**TO regimen** — IV antibiotics **6 wks → oral 6 wks** (culture-guided), monitor CRP · **posterior-only approaches now preferred** · epidural abscess: **alternate-level (limited) laminectomy + irrigation catheter** → less blood loss, less deformity

**Two-level cervical osteomyelitis + rapidly progressive gait loss + ↑ WBC/CRP/ESR** — **anterior corpectomy + posterior instrumentation (A + P)** · cervical abscess → early surgery (little space for cord) · lumbar abscess → often antibiotics alone

## X vs Y

| | Pyogenic | Tuberculous |
|---|---|---|
| Onset | Acute, painful, early | Insidious, late |
| Disc (TO) | **Relatively preserved** at presentation | **Collapsed / destroyed** |
| Spread | Endplate → disc → endplate | Subligamentous, skip lesions |
| End result | — | **Bony ankylosis** |

| | Infection | Tumour |
|---|---|---|
| Disc | **Involved** | Spared |
| Endplate | Eroded | Preserved |
| Best test | **MRI + gad** | MRI |

## How it's asked

**"Spondylodiscitis with collection but preserved disc?"** → **pyogenic** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: TB

**"End result of spinal TB?"** → **bony ankylosis** (TO)

**"IVDU, back pain, negative blood cultures, neuro intact — initial management?"** → **CT-guided closed biopsy**

**"IVDU, UTI, focal back pain, ↑ ESR — next step?"** → **admission + blood cultures + MRI with/without gadolinium**

**"Spondylodiscitis on MRI in a diabetic, no deficit — plan?"** → **needle biopsy + IV antibiotics + TLSO**\
✗ Wrong options: Modic I · antibiotics before biopsy · anterior surgery

**"HIV-positive man, suspected TB spondylitis, cultures negative?"** → **CT-guided biopsy + cultures**\
✗ Wrong options: start anti-TB drugs · broad antibiotics

**"IVDU, thoracic discitis, no sepsis or deficit — first step?"** → **blood cultures**\
✗ Wrong options: start IV antibiotics · corpectomy

**"T2 bright disc, T1 loss of margins, rim enhancement on gad — pathognomonic for?"** → **discitis**

**"Stable 17-yr with discitis after pyelonephritis — next step?"** → **MRI with contrast + blood cultures**\
✗ Wrong options: aspiration first · start broad abx

**"MSSA in 2 blood cultures, no deficit — next?"** → **organism-specific IV antibiotics**

**"Commonest fungal infection of spine?"** → **Aspergillosis** (TO)

**"Amphotericin B used for?"** → **fungal osteomyelitis**

**"Order of spinal involvement?"** → **lumbar > thoracic > cervical > sacrum**

**"Which scenario needs anterior + posterior fusion?"** → **diabetic with 2-level cervical osteomyelitis + rapid gait loss**

**"Post-discectomy pain, normal ESR + TLC, high CRP, afebrile?"** → **infective discitis** (TO)

**"Best lab to follow treatment response?"** → **CRP**

**"Most sensitive and specific imaging?"** → **MRI with gadolinium**\
