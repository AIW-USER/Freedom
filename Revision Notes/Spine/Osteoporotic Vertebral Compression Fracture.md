# Osteoporotic Vertebral Compression Fracture

*Spine · 43 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Commonest fragility fracture** — ✓ VCF · 700,000/yr US · 70,000 admissions · $15 billion\
✗ Wrong options: distal radius · femoral neck · intertrochanteric · proximal humerus

**Prevalence** — **25% > 70 yrs** · **50% > 80 yrs** (up to 40% of women > 80, OB)

**Strongest predictor of further vertebral fractures** — **history of 2 VCFs** (postmenopausal women)

### Osteoporosis

**Definition** — **normal bone quality, decreased quantity** · thin cortices, ↓ trabecular continuity\
✗ Wrong options: abnormal quality = osteomalacia

**WHO** — **T-score < −2.5** · after a fragility fracture → **treat regardless of T-score**

**Peak lumbar BMD** — **females 33–40** · **males 19–33** (variable)

**Screening** — all women **≥ 65** + younger women with osteoporotic fractures

### Associations & mortality

**Pulmonary** — kyphosis → **each VCF ↓ FVC up to 9%** → **↑ death from pulmonary causes** (pneumonia) · ↓ chest/abdominal volume → **early satiety**

**Comorbid** — associated with hypertension, smoking, calcium/vitamin D use · frailty drives both fracture + death

| Mortality | Value |
|---|---|
| **1 yr** | **\~15%** (7–15%) — **less than hip** (hip \~25%) |
| **2 yr** | **\~20% — equivalent to hip** |
| Ranking (1 yr) | **Hip > VCF > distal radius** (distal radius: no ↑ mortality) |
| Medicare cohort | \~2× controls · **men > women** · **younger age at fracture → greater risk** · HR 1.8 · survival 54% / 31% / 11% at 3 / 5 / 7 yrs |

**Further fracture risk** — **20%** after first VCF (OB) · **\~40% within 2 yrs without osteoporosis treatment** (OB) · **5× more likely** (OB) — read the options

**Progressive kyphosis** — **load transfers to the superior adjacent vertebra** · deformity progresses even when pain-free · worse gait, satiety, lungs

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — only **25%** seek attention · localised; may wrap around ribs dermatomally · tenderness on deep spinous palpation

**Neuro** — **< 1%** (SCI very rare; lumbar foraminal root deficits) · no chronic pain in majority

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use |
|---|---|
| **X-ray whole spine** | Concomitant fractures **20%** · height loss **≥ 20% or ≥ 4 mm** |
| CT | Inadequate films, LE deficit, posterior wall |
| **MRI** | **Acuity (STIR/T2 oedema = acute)**, PLC, cord compression, **benign vs metastatic** · ✓ next study after X-ray |
| Bone scan (3-phase) | Acuity if MRI contraindicated |
| No | Flexion-extension, tagged WBC scan |

### Benign vs malignant on MRI

| Osteoporotic (benign) | Metastatic (malignant) |
|---|---|
| **Fluid sign** (fluid cleft) | **Complete marrow replacement** (diffuse homogeneous low T1) |
| **Band-like low T1 / high T2 signal** | **Convex posterior border** |
| **Retropulsed fragment** | **Pedicle / posterior column involvement** |
| — | Paraspinal or epidural mass |

| Feature (TO) | Osteoporotic | Malignant |
|---|---|---|
| Posterior wall | **Angular retropulsed fragment** | **Convex posterior bulge** |
| Levels | Contiguous | **Skip lesions** |
| Marrow | **Some normal fat spared** | **Whole body replaced** |
| Posterior elements | Fracture lines only | **Pedicle involved** (Batson plexus) |
| Paraspinal | Smooth thin haematoma | **Thick, lobulated, enhancing mass** |

**Cardboard box (TO)** — normal = stuffed box · osteoporotic = **empty box** (flattens, sharp edges) · malignant = **styrofoam box** (contents bulge out)

**Acuity (TO)** — oedema = fresh · **no oedema = old healed** fracture

**Disc rule (TO)** — **disc involved = infection** · **disc spared = metastasis / osteoporosis** ("bad disc is good news")

![MR fluid sign](../../MCQ/attachments/q5643-illb-illus-b-mr-fluid-sign-sagittal-stir.jpg)

**Suspect metastasis** — **above T5**, atypical films, constitutional symptoms, **young without a fall**

### Labs

**Work-up** — CBC, BMP · **ESR** (infection) · **SPEP + UPEP** (myeloma)

## 5 Management

```
Osteoporotic VCF, neuro intact
├─ Acute → ANALGESIA + EARLY MOBILISATION ± TLSO
│      (calcitonin 4 wks if < 5 days; no bed rest)
├─ Severe pain > 6 wks / Kümmell → KYPHOPLASTY
└─ Progressive deficit / PLL injury / unstable → DECOMPRESSION + LONG FIXATION
       (multiple fixation points ± anterior support)
```

### Non-operative

**First line** — **oral analgesia (opioid acceptable) + gradual activity, walking unrestricted** ± TLSO for comfort · even **> 30° kyphosis or > 50% height loss if PLL intact**\
✗ Wrong options: strict bed rest → DVT, PE, deconditioning, bone loss

**AAOS 2010 guideline** — only **moderate** recommendation: **calcitonin 4 wks if presenting within 5 days** · only **strong** recommendation: **against vertebroplasty** · kyphoplasty = limited/inconclusive option · brace, bed rest + opiates, exercise, ibandronate/strontium, L2 nerve block = inconclusive

**TLSO** — RCTs: same pain, disability, radiographs with or without brace · not strongly recommended

**Calcitonin** — **directly inhibits osteoclasts** (cell-surface receptor)

**Medical** — **bisphosphonates** prevent further fractures (atypical femur fracture risk) · **teriparatide ↑ BMD without atypical femur fracture risk** · exercise ↑ BMD

### Vertebral augmentation

**Indication** — **severe pain after 6 wks** of non-op (narcotic-dependent, sedentary, constipated) · not first line · not for **healed VCF with kyphosis** (TO)

**Evidence** — **double-blind sham-controlled RCTs: vertebroplasty no benefit at any time point** · surgery meta-analysis (OB): **greater pain improvement**

| | Vertebroplasty | Kyphoplasty |
|---|---|---|
| Cavity | None | **Balloon cavity** |
| Cement viscosity | **Less viscous** | More viscous |
| Injection pressure | **Higher** | **Lower** (TO: "kyphoplasty higher pressure" = false) |
| Extravasation | **Higher** | Lower |
| Height restoration | No | Goal; partial reduction |
| Cost / equipment | Less | **More** |
| Pain relief | **Comparable** (TO) | Comparable |
| Future VCF risk | — | Yes (TO) |

**TO trap** — vertebroplasty cement: **high viscosity + more radiopaque than arthroplasty cement** (true statement in TO stem)

**Technique (TO)** — needle via pedicle (**3 o'clock right / 9 o'clock left**) on AP + lateral · inject when **semi-viscous** · **stop as cement nears the posterior wall** · vertebroplasty ↑ leak, kyphoplasty ↑ height correction

**Mechanism** — **eliminates micromotion**

![Kyphoplasty balloon](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2021-kyphoplasty.jpg)

### Kümmell disease

**Definition** — **delayed post-traumatic vertebral body osteonecrosis** · pain-free interval (months–years) → new pain, collapse, **intravertebral cleft** (pathognomonic) · thoracolumbar junction

**Treatment** — **kyphoplasty**\
✗ Wrong options: continued non-op · vertebroplasty (leak, no correction) · fusion without deficit

![Intravertebral cleft of Kümmell disease](../../MCQ/attachments/q214130-illa-intravert-cleft.jpg)

### Operative stabilisation

**Indications** — **progressive neuro deficit**, **PLL injury / unstable** (very rare) · not stable 30% height loss, 15° kyphosis · retropulsion makes cement risky

**Technique** — **long constructs**, multiple fixation points, consider anterior support

## 6 Complications

**Cement extravasation** — into canal → **neuro injury** · vertebroplasty > kyphoplasty · check posterior cortex defects

**Kümmell disease** — avascular necrosis + nonunion
