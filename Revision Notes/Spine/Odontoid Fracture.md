# Odontoid Fracture

*Spine · 41 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **commonest axis fracture** · 10–15% of cervical fractures · **commonest cervical fracture in the elderly (> 65)**

**Bimodal** — young: high-energy head trauma (hyperflexion/extension) · elderly: **simple falls**, often missed, ↑ morbidity + mortality · children: rare, **almost always at the basilar synchondrosis**

**Displacement** — anterior (hyperflexion) → TAL failure + AAI · posterior (hyperextension) → C1 anterior arch strikes dens

### Anatomy

**Ossification** — **5 centres**: body, 2 neural arches, odontoid, **secondary (tip) centre** · **subdental (basilar) synchondrosis fuses \~6 yrs** (5–7) · secondary centre **appears \~3, fuses \~12 yrs** → fused synchondrosis + unfused tip = **8–10 yrs**

**Ligaments** — **transverse = primary atlantoaxial stabiliser** (limits anterior C1 translation) · **alar + apical limit rotation**

**Blood supply** — **watershed at the waist**: apex from **internal carotid** branches · base from **vertebral arteries** → **type II nonunion**

![Odontoid watershed blood supply](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2016-blood-supply-3.jpg)

### Cervical kinematics

| Joint | Flex/ext | Rotation | Lateral bend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Occiput–C1 | 50 | 4 | 8 |
| **C1-2** | **10** | **50** | **0** |
| C3–C7 | 50 | 50 | 60 |
| **Total** | **110** | **100** | **68** |

**C1-2 fusion** — **\~50% loss of rotation**

### Os odontoideum

**Cause** — likely **old trauma** (not failed fusion) · looks like a type II fracture · **observe** if no deficit/instability · **C1-2 fusion** if deficit or instability

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — neck pain worse with **rotation** · **dysphagia** (retropharyngeal haematoma)

**Neuro deficit** — **very rare** (large canal at C1-2)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use |
|---|---|
| X-ray AP, lateral, **open-mouth** | Fracture best seen on **open-mouth view** |
| Flexion-extension | Type I (rule out **OC instability**) + os odontoideum · instability: **ADI > 10 mm**, **SAC < 13 mm** |
| **CT** | **Study of choice** — pattern + stability |
| **CT angiogram** | **Vertebral artery course before posterior screws** (aberrant VA up to 30%) |
| MRI | Only if neuro symptoms |

### Anderson & D'Alonzo

| Type | Fracture | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Oblique tip avulsion — alar ligament** | Rare; rule out **atlanto-occipital instability** (flexion-extension) |
| **II** | **Waist** | **Highest nonunion** (watershed) · 35% of C2 fractures |
| **III** | Into **cancellous C2 body** ± C1-2 joint | Good blood supply + large cancellous surface → heals |

![Anderson & D'Alonzo types](../../MCQ/attachments/q3389-illa-anderson-and-d-alonzo-classification.jpg)

### Grauer (type II)

| Subtype | Pattern | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **IIA** | Undisplaced / minimal, no comminution | **External immobilisation** |
| **IIB** | Displaced, line **anterosuperior → posteroinferior** ("anterior oblique") | **Anterior odontoid screw** (good bone) |
| **IIC** | **Anteroinferior → posterosuperior** or **comminuted** | **Posterior C1-2 stabilisation** |

![Grauer classification](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2016-grauer-classification.jpg)

### Nonunion risk factors (type II)

| Factor | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **Posterior displacement** | **> 2 mm (strongest predictor)** (Orthobullets) · **> 5 mm** (TO, OB) — read the options |
| **Displacement magnitude** | **≥ 5 mm (> 50% nonunion)** (Orthobullets) · **> 6 mm = best predictor** (OB) |
| **Age** | **> 40** (Orthobullets) · **> 50** (OB) · > 65 (TO) · not age < 40 |
| **Delay** | **> 4 days** (✗ Wrong options: 36–48 h) |
| **Angulation** | **> 10°** (≥ 11° OB) · not 7° |
| Gap | **> 1 mm** |
| Others | **Smoking**, comminution, diabetes, osteoporosis |
| No | Gender |

**Overall nonunion** — 33% (up to 88%) · elderly non-op **60–71%**

## 5 Management

| Type / patient | Treatment (Orthobullets) |
|---|---|
| Type I | **Collar** (soft collar acceptable, OB) |
| **Type II < 40** (no risk factors) | **Halo vest** (or hard collar) |
| **Type II 40–80** | **Surgery** |
| **Type II > 80** | **Collar** |
| **Type III** | **Collar 6–12 wks** (✗ Wrong options: halo advantage) |

```
Type II odontoid
├─ Young, no nonunion risk → HALO VEST (or hard collar)
│      (halo if displacement < 6 mm, reduced within 1 wk, age < 60 → > 90% union)
├─ Risk factors, reducible, oblique IIB, good bone, normal habitus → ANTERIOR ODONTOID SCREW
├─ Risk factors otherwise / elderly surgical candidate → POSTERIOR C1-2 FUSION
└─ Elderly, comorbid / > 80 → RIGID COLLAR (fibrous union acceptable)
```

### Elderly type II

**Halo** — **highest morbidity + mortality** (aspiration, pneumonia, dysphagia, pin problems) → **contraindicated** in elderly, especially dementia, COPD

**Posterior C1-2 fusion (surgical candidate)** — ↑ union + **↓ mortality vs non-op** · ↑ SF-36 · less dysphagia than halo · **higher fusion than anterior screw**

**Not a surgical candidate** — **rigid collar 6–8 wks** · **fibrous union likely** and usually stable enough

**> 80 yrs** — **high morbidity + mortality regardless of treatment** · collar recommended

**Nonop vs op in elderly** — nonop → ↑ **nonunion**\
✗ Wrong options: VA occlusion · myelopathy · radiculopathy

**Anterior screw in elderly** — avoid with **barrel chest (COPD)**, short neck, osteoporosis (cut-out), **dysphagia**, displaced fractures, fractures **> 4 wks old** · higher failure than posterior fusion

### Anterior odontoid screw

**Indications** — type II with nonunion risk **AND** acceptable/reduced alignment + **fracture line perpendicular to screw (anterior oblique, IIB)** + habitus allowing trajectory

**Pros** — **preserves C1-2 rotation**, fewer infections than posterior · single screw adequate

**Cons** — **higher failure than posterior C1-2 fusion** · not for transverse, posterior oblique, type I/III

![Anterior oblique fracture line suits odontoid screw](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2016-anterior-oblique-fracture-line-amenable-to-odontoid-screw.jpg)

### Posterior C1-2 fusion

**Indications** — type II with nonunion risk (50–80) · **type II/III nonunion** · os odontoideum with deficit/instability\
✗ Wrong options: PT · collar · halo · anterior screw

**C1 lateral mass screws** — **10° medial, 22° cephalad** · don't breach C1 anterior cortex (**internal carotid**)

**C2** — **pars (commonest)**, pedicle, laminar · Gallie/Brooks wiring needs halo (rare)

**Transarticular C1-2 screws** — **contraindicated with aberrant vertebral artery**

**Transoral odontoidectomy** — severe posterior dens displacement + cord compression + deficit · rarely; C1 laminectomy usually enough · combine with posterior fusion

### Halo

**Pin-site infection** — superficial → **tighten pins + oral antibiotics**

### Paediatric synchondrosis fracture

**Features** — **commonest C-spine fracture < 7 yrs** · through basilar synchondrosis · neuro deficit rare · nonunion rare

**Treatment** — **closed reduction (extension)** + **Minerva cast or halo** 6–8 wks\
✗ Wrong options: soft collar · screw · fusion initially

## 6 Complications

**Nonunion** — type II · symptomatic → **posterior C1-2 fusion**

**Mortality** — > 80 poor with any treatment, worst with halo

## How it's asked

**"Major nonunion risk in type 2?"** → **posterior displacement > 5 mm** (TO)

**"Nonunion risks EXCEPT?"** → **age < 40**

**"Greatest nonunion risk on images?"** → **type II with posterior displacement**

**"Best predictor of nonunion non-op?"** → **magnitude of displacement**

**"72-yr declines surgery — risk with non-op?"** → **fracture nonunion**

**"40-yr ladder fall, type II — consider?"** → **high nonunion from vascular watershed**

**"78-yr smoker, emphysema, delayed type II?"** → **high nonunion risk → C1-C2 posterior fusion**

**"Elderly type II — highest morbidity/mortality treatment?"** → **halo vest**

**"72-yr COPD, undisplaced type III?"** → **rigid cervical collar**

**"85-yr comorbid, displaced type II?"** → **cervical collar**

**"80-yr COPD + dementia, undisplaced type II?"** → **rigid orthosis 6–8 wks**

**"79-yr posterior C1-2 fusion — true?"** → **lower mortality than non-op**

**"75-yr non-ambulatory comorbid, < 50% displaced?"** → **rigid collar**

**"65-yr diabetic smoker COPD, type II?"** → **reduction + posterior C1-C2 fusion**

**"84-yr demented, undisplaced type II — true?"** → **fibrous union likely in hard orthosis**

**"71-yr posterior displaced horizontal type II?"** → **posterior C1-2 instrumented fusion**

**"81-yr, 5 mm posterior displacement — true?"** → **morbidity + mortality high regardless**

**"Young diabetic smoker, IIB, anterior screw — associated with?"** → **high union, few complications**

**"Aberrant vertebral artery — contraindicated?"** → **C1-C2 transarticular screws**

**"Symptomatic type II nonunion at 7 months?"** → **posterior C1-C2 fusion**

**"42-yr smoker, 5-day delay, favourable obliquity?"** → **anterior odontoid screw**

**"Single anterior screw suits?"** → **type II anterior oblique pattern**

**"37-yr type III?"** → **external orthosis 6–8 wks**

**"Rigid orthosis acceptable EXCEPT?"** → **Jefferson fracture with TAL disruption**

**"28-yr, 2 mm displacement, 7° angulation, 36 h?"** → **halo or hard orthosis (low nonunion risk)**

**"Fused synchondrosis, unfused tip centre — age?"** → **8–10 yrs**

**"Paediatric odontoid fracture?"** → **almost always at the basilar synchondrosis**

**"5-yr unstable synchondrosis fracture?"** → **closed reduction + Minerva cast or halo**
