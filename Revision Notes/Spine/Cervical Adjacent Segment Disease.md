# Cervical Adjacent Segment Disease

*Spine · 11 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Definitions

**Adjacent segment degeneration** — **radiographic** degeneration next to a fusion, ± symptoms

**Adjacent segment disease (ASD)** — **clinical** radiculopathy / myelopathy from the adjacent motion segment

**Adjacent level ossification development (ALOD)** — large anterior marginal osteophyte / ALL heterotopic bone next to a plated level; disc height preserved, no herniation; clinical significance unclear

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — clinically relevant ASD **1.6–2.4% / yr** (reoperation \~2.4%/yr) · **25.6% at 10 yrs** after ACDF

**Levels** — C4-5, **C5-6 (highest)**, C6-7 · **C2-3 lowest**

| Risk factor for ASD | |
|---|---|
| **Smoking** | **Strongest** |
| **Female** | Yes (✗ Wrong options: male) |
| **Age < 60** | Yes |
| **< 3 levels fused** | Yes (more remaining motion segments) |
| **Fusion ending next to C5-6 / C6-7** | Most mobile levels |
| Pre-op dural indentation / disc protrusion at C5-7 | Yes |
| Fusion vs non-fusion (foraminotomy, discectomy) | **No difference** |

### Pathophysiology

**ASD** — ↑ compensatory motion → ↑ intradiscal pressure, stress, shear → herniation, height loss, osteophytes → stenosis · also ALL / longus colli damage · **needle marker in wrong disc**

**Counter-evidence** — **CDA (motion-preserving) does not reduce ASD** (Orthobullets ASD note) — partly natural history of spondylosis

**ALOD** — **anterior plate within 5 mm of adjacent disc**; variable-angle screws allow subsidence toward the disc\
✗ Wrong options: diabetes · static screws (protective) · male · age > 60

### Park classification of adjacent level ossification

| Grade | Ossification |
|---|---|
| 0 | None |
| 1 | < 50% of disc space |
| 2 | > 50% of disc space |
| **3** | **Complete bridging** |

## 3 Presentation

**Radiculopathy** — dermatomal pain / weakness at adjacent root

**Myelopathy** — gait instability, hand clumsiness, urinary change

**Exam** — inspect wound · painful ROM arc · rule out **cubital / carpal tunnel** for sensory change · **Hoffmann** (myelopathy) · **Spurling** (radiculopathy) · **shoulder abduction relief** (radiculopathy) · **Lhermitte** (myelopathy)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use |
|---|---|
| X-ray AP, lateral, **flexion-extension** | Disc narrowing, foraminal stenosis, osteophytes, facet arthropathy; **poor correlation with symptoms** |
| **CT** | **Pseudarthrosis**, OPLL |
| **MRI** | Foraminal / central stenosis at adjacent level |

**Differential (top 5)** — **pseudarthrosis** · progressive DDD · **indolent infection** · myelopathy / radiculopathy at a non-adjacent level

**Prevalence context** — radiographic degeneration in **> 80%** of asymptomatic unfused people > 60 · in **25–91%** of fused patients

## 5 Management

```
Symptomatic ASD
├─ Radiculopathy / mild myelopathy → NSAIDs, gabapentin, PT, activity change ± brief collar
│      (avoid narcotics; NSAID caution in elderly)
└─ Failed non-op radiculopathy / any significant myelopathy → EXTEND FUSION
       └─ Single adjacent level → CERVICAL DISC REPLACEMENT (≈ ACDF)
```

**Extension of fusion** — approach by pathology location · **re-operate from the prior side** (assume ipsilateral RLN palsy unless ENT scope is normal → protects contralateral nerve) · include C5-6/C6-7 · fusing **> 3 levels** ↓ further ASD · plate **> 5 mm** from adjacent disc

**CDR for ASD** — single level; **equivalent to ACDF**; ↓ adjacent ossification vs plating; align centre of rotation · as index procedure **no clear ASD protection** (meta-analyses: possibly ↓ reoperation)

## 6 Complications

**Progression** — new adjacent segment degenerates after extension → further revision

**Reoperation** — overall **6.1–25.6%**; **0.7–3.7%/yr**

## X vs Y

| | Adjacent segment degeneration | Adjacent segment disease | ALOD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Definition | Radiographic | Clinical symptoms | Anterior ossification |
| Cause | Motion / natural history | Same | **Plate < 5 mm from disc** |
| Rx | Observe | Non-op → extend fusion / CDR | Observe |

## How it's asked

**"Asymptomatic anterior ossification 2 yrs after ACDF — associated factor?"** → **plate placed within 5 mm of adjacent disc**\
✗ Wrong options: diabetes · static screws · male · age > 60

**"Strongest risk factor for cervical ASD?"** → **smoking**

**"Level with highest ASD risk?"** → **C5-6**

**"Revision anterior approach — which side?"** → **same side as prior surgery** (unless ENT shows normal cords)
