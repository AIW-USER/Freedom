# Pediatric Cervical Trauma Overview

*Spine · 45 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Share** — uncommon, but **60% of paediatric spinal injuries** · **33% present with neuro deficit** · other organs in \~40%

**Level** — **< 8 yrs: 87% at C3 or above** · > 8 yrs: lower cervical (adult pattern)

**Mechanism** — **MVA commonest** · falls (toddlers, school age) · sports (adolescents)

### Why under-8s are vulnerable

**Anatomy** — **large head : trunk**, **horizontal facets**, **ligamentous laxity**, weak muscles · column stretches **5 cm** without rupture

**SCI** — commoner + more lethal < 8 · recovery better > 8

**Mortality** — **C1 injury 17%** · C4 \~4% · higher at C3 and above

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — full exam (associated injuries) · suspect C-spine with **head + facial trauma** · document sensation incl. **sacral sparing**, motor, reflexes · **repeat — 20% with fractures examine normal** initially

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Mandatory X-rays** — **AP, open-mouth odontoid, cross-table lateral** · oblique (facets) optional · **flexion-extension only under physician supervision**

### Normal paediatric lateral (diagnostic criteria)

| Parameter | Normal |
|---|---|
| **Prevertebral soft tissue** | **< 2/3 of adjacent body width** |
| **Retropharyngeal space** | **< 6 mm at C2** · at C6: **< 22 mm** (Orthobullets) · **< 14 mm in children, < 22 mm adults** (TO) — read the options |
| **Retrotracheal space** | **< 14 mm** |
| **ADI** | **< 5 mm child**, **< 3 mm adolescent** |
| **Pseudosubluxation C2-3 / C3-4** | **< 4 mm** if **posterior laminar line contiguous** |
| Contour lines | Smooth anterior + posterior body, spinolaminar, spinous tips · parallel facets |
| **C3 wedging** | Normal in **7%** |
| **Loss of lordosis** | Normal in **14%** |

![Normal paediatric lateral C-spine](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob2047-lateralcspine.jpg)

**CT** — upper cervical fractures, **atlantoaxial rotatory subluxation**, canal compromise · not as screening study

**MRI** — **obtunded / closed head injury**, canal compromise

## 5 Management

### Transport immobilisation

**Board** — **standard adult backboard → cervical flexion (kyphosis)** — avoid · use **occipital recess (head cut-out)** or **thoracic elevation (torso pad)** for < 8 yrs

**Alignment** — in-line, **external auditory meatus in line with the shoulders** (relative extension)\
✗ Wrong options: EAM anterior/posterior · adult board + sandbag under head

**Collars** — commercial collars often misfit → sandbags · paediatric collars acceptable

![Backboard with occipital recess vs standard](../../MCQ/attachments/q210134-illa-backboard.jpg)

### Treatment by injury

| Treatment | Indications |
|---|---|
| **Observation** | **C2-3 pseudosubluxation** |
| **Collar** | Stable odontoid fracture, AAI, **acute AARD**, stable subaxial injury |
| **Halo** (thin calvaria — many low-torque pins) | Unstable odontoid, OC instability, AAI, **subacute AARD**, **Jefferson**, unstable subaxial |
| **Surgery** | Unstable spine + SCI, AAI, **chronic AARD** |

### Car seat & airbag safety

| Stage | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Rear-facing**, rear centre | Until **≥ 1 yr and ≥ 20 lb** |
| **Forward-facing seat** | Until **≥ 4 yrs or ≥ 40 lb** |
| **Booster** | Until **60–80 lb and 8 yrs** |
| Seat belt, rear middle | 8–12 yrs |
| **Front seat** | From **13 yrs** · **airbag OFF if < 80 lb** |

## How it's asked

**"8-yr unconscious after fall — acute stabilisation?"** → **in-line, external auditory meatus in line with shoulders**

**"Avoid in young children after trauma?"** → **standard backboard**

**"Consistent car-safety recommendation?"** → **75 lb 13-yr in front seat — airbag OFF**
