# Halo Orthosis Immobilization

*Spine · 27 / 69*

## 1 Basics

### Biomechanics

**Principle** — fixes **skull to torso** → **most rigid external cervical immobilisation** (TO)

**Best controls** — **upper cervical** motion: **rotation at C1-2** / occipitocervical junction\
✗ Wrong options: subaxial flex-ext, rotation, lateral bend, CT junction

**Weakness** — **intercalated paradoxical motion** in subaxial spine; **lateral bending least controlled** → **"snaking"**: recumbent lateral shows mid-cervical **kyphosis**, upright shows lordosis → not ideal for **lower cervical** injuries (incl. facet fracture-dislocation — fails)

## 5 Management

### Indications

| Adult | Paediatric |
|---|---|
| **Occipital condyle fracture** | **Atlanto-occipital dissociation** |
| Occipitocervical dislocation | **Jefferson / atlas fractures** |
| **Stable Jefferson (type II atlas)** | **Unstable odontoid fractures** |
| **Type II odontoid in young** | **Persistent AARS** |
| **Type II / IIA hangman** | C1-2 dissociation; subaxial trauma |
| Post-op adjunct | **Pre-op traction** for deformity (halo-gravity) |

### Contraindications

| Absolute | Relative |
|---|---|
| **Cranial fracture** | Polytrauma, severe chest trauma, barrel chest, obesity |
| **Infection** | **Advanced age** — mortality **21% if > 79 yrs** (elderly → avoid halo) |
| Severe soft-tissue injury at pin sites | — |

**Pre-application CT** — suspected **cranial fracture** · **children < 10 yrs** (bone thickness, avoid **sutures**)\
✗ Wrong options: skull X-ray

### Pin technique

| | Adult | Child |
|---|---|---|
| **Pins** | **4** (2 anterior, 2 posterior diagonally opposite) | **6–8** (Orthobullets) · **8–12** (OB) — **more pins** |
| **Torque** | **8 in-lb** (Orthobullets, OB); 6–8 in-lb range | **2–4 in-lb** ("finger-tight") · **1–5 in-lb** (OB) — **lower torque** (✗ Wrong options: ft-lb · maximal hand tightening) |
| Vest | Standard | Custom vest > 2 yrs · **Minerva cast < 2 yrs** |
| Retighten | Day 2 | — |

**Anterior safe zone** — **1 cm above the orbital rim**, over the **lateral third** of the orbit (Orthobullets) · "lateral two-thirds", ≥ 4.5 cm from midline (OB) — **at/below skull equator** · **lateral to supraorbital nerve**, anterior + medial to **temporalis**

**Ring** — below widest part of skull, \~1 cm above ear helix · pins perpendicular to cortex

```
Anterior pin position
├─ Too MEDIAL → SUPRAORBITAL + SUPRATROCHLEAR NERVE injury, frontal sinus
│      (forehead / upper eyelid numbness)
├─ Correct (1 cm above lateral third, below equator) → NO NEURO COMPLICATION
└─ Too LATERAL (temporal fossa) → THIN BONE + TEMPORALIS → jaw pain, chewing pain, infection
```

## 6 Complications

**Overall** — **children \~70% (68%)** vs **adults \~35% (36%)**

| Complication | Rate / management |
|---|---|
| **Pin loosening** | **36% — commonest in adults**; retighten; persistent → exchange |
| **Pin-site infection** | **12–20%**; esp. posterior pin in **temporalis fossa**; **not loose → keep pin + oral antibiotics + local care** (✗ Wrong options: remove · tighten) · loose / deep → remove + new site, IV abx, drain abscess |
| Discomfort | 18%; release skin around pin |
| Dural puncture | 1%; meningitis if inner table breached |
| **Abducens (CN VI) palsy** | **Commonest cranial nerve** (✗ Wrong options: oculomotor · trochlear) — **traction injury**, not pin; diplopia + **loss of lateral gaze**; ↓ traction + **observe** (resolves) |
| Supraorbital / supratrochlear palsy | **Medial anterior pins** |
| Medical | Pneumonia, ARDS, arrhythmia; ↑ mortality elderly |

## X vs Y

| Nerve | Cause | Deficit |
|---|---|---|
| **Supraorbital** | Medial anterior pin | Upper eyelid / forehead / scalp numbness |
| **Supratrochlear** | More medial pin | Medial forehead + medial eyelid numbness |
| **Abducens (VI)** | **Traction** | Diplopia, ↓ lateral gaze |

## How it's asked

**"Anterior pins too medial — nerve at risk?"** → **supraorbital nerve**

**"Region for anterior pin?"** → **1 cm above lateral orbit, below equator**

**"Red (medial) zone — structures at risk?"** → **supraorbital + supratrochlear nerves**

**"Anterior pin position?"** → **1 cm above, within lateral third of superior orbital rim**\
✗ Wrong options: temporalis · medial third

**"6-yr, 6 pins at 3 in-lb, correctly placed anterior pins — complication?"** → **none**

**"2-yr halo — pin/torque?"** → **8 pins at 2–4 in-lb**

**"7-yr, 10 pins at 6 in-lb, pin complication — change?"** → **lower insertional torque**

**"Preadolescent halo?"** → **8–12 pins at 1–5 in-lb**

**"2-yr Down syndrome, O-C2 fusion + halo — recommended?"** → **pre-op skull CT**

**"Halo — all true EXCEPT?"** → **oculomotor is commonest injured CN** (TO) — it's abducens

**"Commonest neurologic complication of halo traction?"** → **deficit in lateral eye movement (CN VI)**

**"Lateral gaze loss after 2 wks halo traction?"** → **CN VI injury**\
✗ Wrong options: surgery needed

**"Erythematous, non-loose pin, afebrile?"** → **leave pin + oral antibiotics**

**"Halo best controls?"** → **atlantoaxial rotation**

**"Occipital condyle fracture in halo — true?"** → **pins tightened to 8 in-lb**\
✗ Wrong options: lower C-spine · lateral bend best controlled · middle third · trochlear nerve
