# Gluteus medius

*Basic Science · 41 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy

| | Gluteus medius |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Dorsal ilium below iliac crest |
| **Insertion** | Lateral + superior **greater trochanter** |
| **Action** | ✓ **Major hip abductor** · anterior fibres IR · posterior fibres ER |
| **Innervation** | **Superior gluteal nerve (L4, L5, S1)** |
| **Arterial supply** | Superior gluteal artery |

✗ Wrong options: gluteus maximus · obturator externus/internus

## 3 Presentation

### Trendelenburg sign after proximal femoral replacement

**Mechanism** — abductors detached and reattached to megaprosthesis · **tendon won't heal to metal** → abductor weakness

**Trendelenburg** — weak abductor on **stance** side → **pelvis drops on the contralateral side** in single-leg stance · trunk shifts over stance leg · ↑ **hip instability** risk

| Test | Detects |
|---|---|
| **Trendelenburg** | ✓ **Abductor deficiency** |
| FADIR | Impingement |
| Ober | Tight ITB |
| Patrick (FABER) | Hip vs SI pathology |
| Thomas | Hip flexion contracture |

## How it's asked

**"3 months after proximal femoral replacement for sarcoma — positive exam?"** → **Trendelenburg sign**\
✗ Wrong options: FADIR, Ober, Patrick, Thomas

**"Muscle producing the hip movement shown — abduction (TO)?"** → **gluteus medius**
