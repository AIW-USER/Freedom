# Level of Evidence

*Basic Science · 56 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Steps of evidence-based medicine

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Formulate an answerable question** |
| 2 | **Gather** the evidence |
| **3** | ✓ **Appraise** the evidence (levels of evidence used here) |
| 4 | **Implement** |
| 5 | **Evaluate** the process |

### Assigning level by study design & follow-up

| Level | Therapeutic study (JBJS/Oxford) |
|---|---|
| **I** | **High-quality RCT** (randomised, blinded, **>80% follow-up**, narrow CI) · systematic review/meta-analysis of **homogeneous Level I RCTs** · all-or-none |
| **II** | **Lesser-quality RCT** (<80% follow-up, no blinding, improper randomisation, crossover) · **prospective cohort / prospective comparative** · SR of Level II or heterogeneous Level I |
| **III** | **Retrospective cohort** · **case-control** · retrospective comparative · SR/meta-analysis of Level III |
| **IV** | **Case series** (no or historical control) |
| **V** | **Expert opinion**, case report, personal observation, bench/animal work |

**Meta-analysis level** — = **lowest level of included studies** (OB, Orthobullets) · one RCT with 70% follow-up in an SR → **Level II** · meta-analysis of retrospective cohorts → **Level III** · TO table: **meta-analysis of cohort studies = Level 2** (TO) — read the options

**RCT with >20% loss to follow-up (TO)** — ✓ **downgraded to Level II** (attrition bias) even with flawless randomisation, concealment, ITT, blinded assessors **(TO)**

**Reporting guidelines** — **QUORUM / PRISMA** (meta-analyses of RCTs) · **MOOSE** (observational meta-analyses) · **CONSORT** (RCTs)

**Study types** — **therapeutic** (results of treatment) · **prognostic** (patient characteristic → outcome) · **diagnostic** (test efficacy) · economic/decision

**Most common study type in ortho literature** — ✓ **Level IV case series** (58–64%)

### AAOS guideline recommendation strength

| Strength | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | ≥2 high-quality studies |
| Moderate | 1 high or 2 moderate |
| Weak | 1 moderate or ≥1 low |
| Consensus | Expert opinion — only for interventions preventing **loss of life or limb** |

### GRADE framework (TO)

**Baseline (TO)** — **RCTs start High** · **observational studies start Low** · separates **quality of evidence** (high → very low) from **strength of recommendation** (strong / weak)

| Downgrade (5) **(TO)** | Upgrade (3) **(TO)** |
|---|---|
| **Risk of bias** (no blinding, no concealment, attrition, early stopping) | **Large magnitude of effect** — RR >2 or <0.5 (+1) · RR >5 or <0.2 (+2) |
| **Inconsistency** (heterogeneity, high I²) | **Dose–response gradient** |
| **Indirectness** (PICO mismatch, surrogate outcome e.g. BMD vs fracture) | ✓ **All plausible confounders would reduce the observed effect** |
| **Imprecision** (small n, few events, wide CI) | — |
| **Publication bias** (funnel plot) | — |

**Not upgrade criteria (TO)** — explained heterogeneity · PROSPERO registration / PRISMA adherence · precise CI around small RR 1.2

## How it's asked

**"Level of meta-analysis of cohort studies (TO)?"** → **2**

**"RCT, blinded, ITT, flawless randomisation, 22% loss to follow-up (TO)?"** → **Level II — attrition >20%**

**"Level II study?"** → **prospective cohort**\
✗ Wrong options: case-control (III) · high-quality RCT (I)

**"Level III study?"** → **retrospective case-control**

**"Moseley sham-surgery RCT, >90% follow-up?"** → **therapeutic Level I**

**"SR of 15 homogeneous high-quality RCTs?"** → **Level I**

**"Meta-analysis of retrospective cohorts (QUORUM)?"** → **Level III**

**"Prospective non-randomised matched PRP cohort, >80% follow-up?"** → **Level II**

**"Prospective surgeon-preference IT fracture study, 85% follow-up, wide CI?"** → **Level II**

**"RCT, 20% crossover, unblinded surgeon assessor, 65% follow-up?"** → **Level II**

**"10 patients, new procedure, no control?"** → **case series**

**"Surgeon's 25-yr technique article?"** → **Level V**

**"SR of 6 RCTs, one with 70% follow-up?"** → **Level II**

**"Most common study type in ortho literature?"** → **Level IV case series**

**"Third step of EBM?"** → **appraise the evidence**

**"Upgrade Low → Moderate in GRADE (TO)?"** → **all plausible confounders would reduce the effect**
