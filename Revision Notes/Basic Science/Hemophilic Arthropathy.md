# Hemophilic Arthropathy

*Basic Science · 44 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — ✓ **↓ significantly due to home factor treatment** (recombinant factor VIII since 1992 — also removed HIV/hepatitis risk)

**Demographics** — young males, **3–15 yr**

**Site** — **knee** (most common) · elbow, ankle, shoulder, spine

### Prevention, factor replacement & inheritance

| Cause | Inheritance | Factor |
|---|---|---|
| **Haemophilia A** | X-linked recessive | ↓ **VIII** |
| **Haemophilia B (Christmas)** | X-linked recessive | ↓ **IX** |
| von Willebrand | **Autosomal dominant** | Abnormal VIII + platelet dysfunction · mucosal bleeding, rare joint bleeds |

**X-linked recessive bleeding disorders** — ✓ **both factor VIII and IX deficiency**\
✗ Wrong options: factor XI (AR) · vWD (AD)

**Pathoanatomy** — repeated minor trauma → haemarthrosis → **synovitis (hypertrophic, hyperplastic)** → enzymatic cartilage destruction → deformity

**Associations** — iliacus haematoma → **femoral palsy (L4 paraesthesia)** · LLD (epiphyseal overgrowth) · osteopenic fractures (normal healing) · **HIV up to 90%** (older cohorts)

## 3 Presentation

| Haemarthrosis | Features |
|---|---|
| **Acute** | Painful, **tense** effusion |
| **Subacute** | After **2 prior bleeds** |
| **Chronic** | **Contractures, arthritis** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Arnold–Hilgartner staging

| Stage | Radiographic finding |
|---|---|
| **1** | **Soft-tissue swelling** |
| **2** | **Osteoporosis** (± epiphyseal overgrowth) |
| **3** | **Subchondral cysts**, joint grossly intact |
| **4** | **Cartilage loss — joint narrowing** |
| **5** | **Severe arthritis** (end-stage, fibrous ankylosis) |

![Arnold–Hilgartner stage 5](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9051-stage-5-of-the-arnold-hilgartner-classification.jpg)

### Key radiology findings

**Knee** — **squared patella + condyles (Jordan's sign)** · ballooned distal femur · **widened intercondylar notch** · narrowing · long thin patella

**MRI** — early degenerative change, haemosiderin · **US** — follow muscle haematomas

### Labs

**Factor VIII inhibitor (IgG) screen** — ✓ **before surgery** (negates factor therapy) · found in **5–25%** · relative contraindication to surgery

**Exclude septic arthritis** — exam + aspiration

## 5 Management

| Indication | Factor VIII target |
|---|---|
| Vigorous PT | **20%** |
| Acute haematoma | **30%** |
| **Acute haemarthrosis, soft-tissue surgery, synovectomy** | **40–50%** |
| **Skeletal surgery, TJA, arthrodesis** | **100% wk 1 → >50% wk 2** |

**Before TKA (X-linked bleeding disorder)** — ✓ **factor VIII replacement**\
✗ Wrong options: protein C/S · vit K · vWF

**Desmopressin** — mild–moderate haemophilia A

**Non-operative** — compressive dressing, analgesia, short immobilisation → rehab, steroids, splints, PT

**Synovectomy** — recurrent haemarthroses despite medical management · ↓ recurrence, pain, swelling

**Synoviorthesis** — intra-articular radioactive **colloidal P-32 chromic phosphate**

**Arthroplasty** — end-stage · **arthrodesis** — ankle

## How it's asked

**"Main reason for ↓ haemophilic arthropathy over 10–20 yr?"** → **home factor treatment**\
✗ Wrong options: PT, steroid injections, debridement, splints

**"X-linked clotting disorder before TKR — replace?"** → **factor VIII**

**"Bleeding disorders that are X-linked recessive?"** → **factor VIII and IX deficiency**

**"Arnold–Hilgartner stage with subchondral cysts, joint intact?"** → **stage 3**
