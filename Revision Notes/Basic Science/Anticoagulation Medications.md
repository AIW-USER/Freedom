## 1 Basics

### Coagulation & Virchow's triad

**Cascade** — series of reactions → **fibrin** → platelet activation → clot · imbalance → thromboembolism / DVT

**Virchow's triad** — **venous stasis** · **endothelial damage** · **hypercoagulable state**

**Factor Ia** — = **fibrin** · broken down by **plasmin** (from plasminogen)

### Overview of anticoagulants

| Agent | Mechanism | Metabolism | Reversal | Advantage / risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Compression stocking | Mechanical | — | — | No bleeding · compliance |
| **Aspirin** | **Irreversible COX** acetylation in **platelets** → ↓ TXA2, PG | Renal | None (acts at platelet) | Convenient |
| **Unfractionated heparin** | **Activates ATIII** → inhibits **IIa, III, Xa** | **Hepatic** | **Protamine** | Reversible · **highest bleeding + HIT** |
| **LMWH** (enoxaparin, dalteparin) | ATIII → principally **Xa** (also IIa) | **Renal** | Protamine (**partial** — anti-Xa not normalised) | Fixed dose, no monitoring · t½ **4–5 h** |
| **Fondaparinux** (Arixtra) | **Indirect Xa** via ATIII · synthetic pentasaccharide · no IIa effect | **Renal** | — | No monitoring · ↓ DVT vs enoxaparin (hip fx, TKA) · **highest bleeding** · avoid with epidurals · t½ **17–21 h** |
| **Warfarin** | **Vitamin K 2,3-epoxide reductase (VKORC1)** → ↓ γ-carboxylation of **II, VII, IX, X, C, S** | **Hepatic** | **Vit K** (≤3 days) · **FFP** (immediate) | **Most effective** · hard to reverse/dose |
| Dextran | Dilutional · coats RBC/platelets/endothelium (↑ electronegativity) · ↓ vWF/VIII-Ag | — | — | Fluid overload |
| **Rivaroxaban, apixaban, edoxaban** | **Direct Xa** ("-xa-ban") | Liver (major) + kidney, gut | Andexanet alfa (see below) | **Oral** · peak **2–4 h** |
| **Dabigatran** (Pradaxa) | **Direct thrombin (IIa)**, reversible | **Renal** | **Idarucizumab** (FDA 2015) | GI upset, bleeding |
| Hirudin (leech), bivalirudin | Direct thrombin | — | — | Hirudin = most potent thrombin inhibitor |
| Clopidogrel | **ADP receptor** on platelets | — | — | — |

## 5 Management

### Warfarin mechanism & pharmacology

**Mechanism** — ✓ **inhibits vitamin K-dependent clotting factors** · blocks reduction of vit K epoxide → active vit K (hydroquinone) · **inhibits carboxylation**

**Factors** — **II (prothrombin), VII (first affected), IX, X** + proteins **C and S** · not ATIII, VIII, Xa directly

**Alcoholic / liver disease** — caution due to **factor VII** (narrow margin)

**Pharmacokinetics** — oral, rapid GI absorption · **affected by liver function + diet** · plasma **t½ 36–42 h** · therapeutic in **72–96 h** · INR not affected for **2–3 days**

**Target INR** — **2–3** for orthopaedic patients \
✗ Wrong options: 2.5–3.5

**Elective surgery** — stop **3–5 days** before

**Drug interactions** — rifampin · phenobarbital · diuretics · cholestyramine

### Heparin, LMWH & fondaparinux

**Activates ATIII** — ✓ **heparin**\
✗ Wrong options: warfarin, aspirin, rivaroxaban, dabigatran

**Heparin inhibits IIa (thrombin)** — ✓ heparin\
✗ Wrong options: fondaparinux (Xa only) · rivaroxaban (Xa only) · TXA

**Enoxaparin** — primarily **Xa** (antithrombin complex irreversibly inactivates Xa) · protamine less effective than for UFH

**Indirect Xa inhibitor** — ✓ **fondaparinux**\
✗ Wrong options: rivaroxaban, apixaban, edoxaban (direct) · dabigatran (direct IIa)

**Protamine reverses** — ✓ **LMWH** (partially), UFH\
✗ Wrong options: aspirin, clopidogrel (no medical reversal) · warfarin (vit K)

**Highest bleeding + thrombocytopenia** — ✓ **unfractionated heparin** (**HIT** — antibodies activate platelets → intravascular clot) · rare with LMWH\
✗ Wrong options: coumadin

### Factor Xa inhibitors

**Direct Xa** — ✓ **rivaroxaban (Xarelto)**, apixaban, edoxaban · antithrombin-independent\
✗ Wrong options: fondaparinux (indirect)

**Class facts** — **oral** · **max effect 2–4 h** · predictable → **no routine monitoring** · **low** food/drug interaction · excretion liver (major) + kidneys + intestine

**Half-life** — rivaroxaban **8 h** · apixaban **12 h** · urgent surgery delayed until a half-life has elapsed

**Reversal** — Orthobullets: **no current antidote, andexanet alfa under investigation** · OB: **FDA-approved coagulation factor Xa (recombinant) inactivated-zhzo** for life-threatening bleeding — read the options

### Tranexamic acid

**Mechanism** — synthetic **lysine** analogue · **competitively inhibits plasminogen activation** (binds lysine-binding site) · non-competitive plasmin inhibitor at high dose · ↓ **active plasmin** → ↓ fibrinolysis

**Potency** — **8–10×** ε-aminocaproic acid

![TXA blocks plasminogen → plasmin](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9054-txa-mechanism-of-action.jpg)

| Route | Fact |
|---|---|
| IV | 10–20 mg/kg bolus ± repeat every 3 h ×1–4 or infusion 1–10 mg/kg/h |
| **Topical** | **As effective as IV** · sprayed on wound at closure · **no detectable serum level** |
| **Oral** | **As effective as IV** · 1.95 g **2 h preop** · **cheaper** |

**Kinetics** — <5% metabolised · joint-fluid t½ **3 h** · in tissues up to **17 h**

**Safety** — **no ↑ thromboembolic events** (no route difference in DVT) · avoid with **cardiac stents, prior VTE/ischaemic stroke**

**Jehovah's Witness THA** — TXA ↓ active plasmin\
✗ Wrong options: epoetin alfa (↑ Hb preop) · dexamethasone (pain) · celecoxib

### Antiplatelet effects & supplements

**Aspirin's target cell** — ✓ **platelets** (irreversibly blocks TXA2)

| Supplement | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Ginkgo** (ginkgolide B displaces PAF) | ↑ bleeding — **platelets** |
| **Ginseng** (panaxynol) | ↑ bleeding — platelets · hypoglycaemia |
| **Garlic** (aged extract — ↑ cyclic nucleotides, ↓ fibrinogen binding) | ↑ bleeding — platelets |
| **Omega-3 fish oil** | ↑ INR (platelets + vit K factors) |
| **St John's wort** | ↓ INR (↑ warfarin catabolism) · CNS reuptake inhibition · **not a platelet effect** |
| Coenzyme Q10 · green tea | ↓ INR (green tea = direct warfarin antagonist) |
| Ephedra | Cardiovascular instability |
| Kava, valerian | ↑ sedative effect of anaesthetics |

### VTE prophylaxis guidelines

**CRISTAL (9,711 THA/TKA)** — symptomatic VTE **aspirin 3.45% vs LMWH 1.8%** → aspirin **failed non-inferiority** (1% margin) · **no difference in mortality**, joint readmission, major bleeding

**ACCP** — mechanical + pharmacological · minimum **10–14 days**, ideally up to **35 days** · LMWH preferred, aspirin acceptable

**Aspirin dose** — **81 mg BID** = efficacy of 325 mg BID with **less GI** · <4 weeks → ↑ mortality + bleeding

**No prophylaxis** — DVT >**50%** after TKA → mechanical alone not recommended

**Weight-based LMWH** — = therapeutic dose → ↑ bleeding/wound drainage (avoid without confirmed VTE)

| AAOS VTED guideline (elective THA/TKA) | |
|---|---|
| Postop duplex screening | **Not recommended** |
| Only risk factor clearly supported for VTE | **Previous VTE** |
| Only bleeding risk factors clearly supported | **Known bleeding disorder, active liver disease** |
| Antiplatelet agents | **Stop preop** |
| All patients | Pharmacological and/or mechanical |
| Previous VTE | Pharmacological **AND** mechanical |
| Bleeding disorder / liver disease | **Mechanical** |
| Anaesthesia | **Neuraxial** (spinal/epidural) |
| Mobilisation | Early |
| IVC filter | Not clearly supported |
| **Aspirin** | ✓ **Included** as a prevention option |

### Operative criteria — when chemoprophylaxis is not mandatory

**Below-knee injury, no risk factors** — e.g. healthy 37-yr-old, isolated rotational ankle ORIF → **not absolutely indicated** (DVT rate = placebo)

**Elective forefoot/midfoot surgery, low risk, immediate WB** — ✓ **no routine chemical prophylaxis** (ICM-VTE Foot & Ankle) · NWB = independent risk, mitigated by **>50% load-bearing**

**Risk factors that mandate it** — older age · obesity · prior DVT · **malignancy** · OCP · smoking · non-ambulatory · SCI / axial injury · hip fracture · THA

```
Patient needing VTE prophylaxis decision
├─ THA / TKA → MECHANICAL + PHARMACOLOGICAL
│      └─ Prior VTE → PHARMACOLOGICAL AND MECHANICAL
├─ Known bleeding disorder / liver disease → MECHANICAL ONLY
└─ Below-knee / forefoot, low risk, early WB → NO ROUTINE CHEMOPROPHYLAXIS
```

## How it's asked

**"Mechanism of warfarin?"** → **inhibits vitamin K-dependent clotting factors**\
✗ Wrong options: platelets · COX · ↓ ATIII

**"Inhibits carboxylation of clotting factors?"** → **warfarin**\
✗ Wrong options: enoxaparin, dalteparin, heparin, hirudin

**"Prevents γ-carboxylation of X, IX and prothrombin?"** → **warfarin**\
✗ Wrong options: clopidogrel (ADP receptor)

**"Alcoholic, venogram DVT — warfarin caution due to factor?"** → **VII**

**"Specific target of warfarin?"** → **vitamin K 2,3-epoxide reductase**\
✗ Wrong options: ATIII · protein C · VIII · Xa

**"Plasma half-life of warfarin?"** → **36–42 h**

**"DVT agent affected by liver function and diet?"** → **warfarin**\
✗ Wrong options: LMWH, fondaparinux (renal)

**"Protamine reverses?"** → **LMWH**

**"Highest risk of bleeding and thrombocytopenia?"** → **unfractionated heparin** (HIT)

**"Activates antithrombin III?"** → **heparin**

**"Inhibits the circled factor IIa?"** → **heparin**\
✗ Wrong options: fondaparinux · rivaroxaban (Xa only)

**"Where does enoxaparin act?"** → **factor Xa**

**"Indirect inhibitor of factor X?"** → **fondaparinux**

**"Direct factor Xa inhibitor?"** → **rivaroxaban**\
✗ Wrong options: fondaparinux (indirect) · dextran · coumadin

**"True of Xarelto class?"** → **maximal effect 2–4 h**\
✗ Wrong options: food interactions · IV/SC only · monitoring · mainly renal

**"Competitive inhibition of plasminogen activation?"** → **tranexamic acid**

**"Inhibits activation of the enzyme that breaks down factor Ia?"** → **TXA**

**"TXA in TKA — true?"** → **similar blood-loss reduction IV vs oral**\
✗ Wrong options: IV better · topical better · ↑ DVT

**"Jehovah's Witness THA — drug ↓ active plasmin?"** → **TXA**

**"Supplement affecting clotting via platelets?"** → **ginkgo**\
✗ Wrong options: vitamin D, ephedra, St John's wort, selenium

**"↑ bleeding by direct platelet effect EXCEPT?"** → **St John's wort**

**"Aspirin's primary cellular target (image)?"** → **platelets**

**"LMWH vs aspirin after THA/TKA — true?"** → **no difference in mortality**\
✗ Wrong options: aspirin non-inferior · aspirin fewer DVT · LMWH more bleeding/readmission

**"AAOS VTED recommendations EXCEPT?"** → **"aspirin not approved in low-risk patients"** (false — aspirin is included)

**"Uncomplicated TKA, BMI 39 — optimal prophylaxis?"** → **aspirin 81 mg BID for 6 weeks**\
✗ Wrong options: 325 mg × 2 wk · weight-based LMWH · INR 2.5–3.5 · SCDs only

**"DVT prophylaxis NOT absolutely indicated?"** → **healthy 37-yr-old, isolated ankle ORIF**

**"Healthy 49-yr-old, elective bunion surgery, immediate heel WB — VTE strategy?"** → **no routine chemical prophylaxis**
