# Piriformis

*Basic Science · 79 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy & gluteal nerves

| | Piriformis |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | **Anterior (ventral) surface of sacrum** (3 digitations) + margin of greater sciatic notch · lesser: SI capsule, sacrotuberous ligament |
| **Insertion** | **Superior border / apex of greater trochanter** (piriformis fossa) |
| **Action** | **External rotator** · abducts flexed hip |
| **Innervation** | **Nerve to piriformis (L5, S1, S2)** |
| **Arterial supply** | Superior + inferior gluteal, internal pudendal arteries |

**Only short rotator from ventral sacrum** — ✓ piriformis\
✗ Wrong options: obturator internus/externus (pubic ramus, obturator membrane) · quadratus femoris (near ischial tuberosity) · superior gemellus (near acetabulum)

**Sacral plexus** — forms on its **anterior surface**

**Gluteal nerves named by relation to** — ✓ **piriformis**\

|---|---|---|---|

| **Inferior gluteal** | L5, S1, S2 | **Below piriformis** | **Gluteus maximus** (sole motor) |

### Greater sciatic foramen contents (TO)

**Greater sciatic foramen** — notch closed by **sacrotuberous + sacrospinous** ligaments · piriformis fills upper part, divides into two **(TO)**

| Compartment (TO) | Vessels | Nerves |
|---|---|---|
| **Above piriformis** | **Superior gluteal vessels** | ✓ **Superior gluteal nerve** |
| **Below piriformis** | Inferior gluteal · **internal pudendal** | Inferior gluteal · **pudendal** · **sciatic** · **posterior femoral cutaneous** · nerve to obturator internus · nerve to quadratus femoris |

**Lesser sciatic foramen (TO)** — **obturator internus tendon** · nerve to obturator internus · internal pudendal vessels · pudendal nerve **(TO)**

✗ Wrong options: **Through sciatic notch** — ✓ **obturator vessels** (obturator canal) **(TO)**

## How it's asked

**"Superior and inferior gluteal nerves named for relation to?"** → **piriformis**

**"Muscle originating from ventral sacrum?"** → **piriformis**\
✗ Wrong options: obturator internus/externus, quadratus femoris, superior gemellus

**"Through greater sciatic foramen above piriformis?"** → **superior gluteal nerve** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: internal pudendal, posterior femoral cutaneous, nerve to obturator internus

**"Structure NOT passing through sciatic notch?"** → **obturator vessels** **(TO)**
