# Iliacus

*Basic Science · 48 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy

| | Iliacus |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Upper ⅔ iliac fossa, inner lip of iliac crest, lateral sacrum, ventral SI + lower iliolumbar ligaments |
| **Insertion** | **Lesser trochanter** (with psoas → iliopsoas) |
| **Action** | Flexes thigh on trunk (and trunk on thigh) |
| **Innervation** | **Muscular branch of femoral nerve (L1–L3)** |
| **Arterial supply** | Iliolumbar (iliac branch) from internal iliac |

### Cross-sectional anatomy at the femoral heads

| Structure | Position relative to iliacus |
|---|---|
| **Psoas tendon** | Immediately **medial** |
| Rectus femoris | Just anterior + slightly lateral |
| Sartorius | Superficial + anterior |
| Tensor fascia lata | Lateral + superficial |
| Obturator externus | Medial, deep to pectineus |

**Iliacus haematoma (haemophilia, anticoagulation)** — compresses **femoral nerve** (see Femoral nerve, Hemophilia chapters)

## How it's asked

**"Pelvic cross-section at femoral heads — arrowed muscle with psoas tendon just medial?"** → **iliacus**\
✗ Wrong options: rectus femoris, sartorius, obturator externus, TFL
