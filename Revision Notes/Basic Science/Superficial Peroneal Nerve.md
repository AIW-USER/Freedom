# Superficial Peroneal Nerve

*Basic Science · 103 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy & entrapment

**Course** — branch of **common peroneal** after crossing fibular head → **lateral compartment** → ✓ **motor to peroneus longus + brevis** → pierces **crural fascia ~10–12 cm proximal to fibular tip** → **intermediate + medial dorsal cutaneous nerves** (dorsum of foot + toes)

**At risk** — **lateral approach to ankle** (e.g. bimalleolar ORIF)

| Nerve | Supplies |
|---|---|
| **Superficial peroneal** | **Lateral compartment** (PL, PB) · dorsum of foot except 1st web |
| **Deep peroneal** | **Anterior compartment** · **EDB, EHB** · **1st dorsal web space** |
| **Sural** | **Lateral foot** sensation |

## 3 Presentation

**Entrapment** — runner · lateral distal leg pain + **dorsal foot dysaesthesia** · worse with **active/passive plantar flexion + inversion** (traction at fascial exit) · tender fullness (fascial hernia) **~10 cm above lateral malleolus** · **Tinel +** · no weakness · normal X-ray/MRI

## 5 Management

**Entrapment** — ✓ **fascial release + neurolysis**\
✗ Wrong options: **closure of fascial defect** (worsens, risk exertional compartment syndrome) · four-compartment fasciotomy (only for established compartment syndrome) · biopsy · epidural steroid

## How it's asked

**"Nerve at lateral ankle approach — true?"** → **proximally innervates lateral compartment**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior compartment · EDB · 1st web · purely sensory lateral foot

**"Runner, dysaesthesia dorsum foot with PF + inversion, Tinel 10 cm above malleolus?"** → **fascial release + neurolysis of SPN**\
✗ Wrong options: fascial closure · fasciotomy
