# Hemophilia

*Basic Science · 43 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & types

| | Haemophilia A | Haemophilia B (Christmas) |
|---|---|---|
| Factor | **VIII** | **IX** |
| Incidence | **1 : 5,000** males | **1 : 30,000** males |
| Inheritance | **X-linked recessive** | **X-linked recessive** |

**Acquired haemophilia** — rare, autoimmune, not inherited

**Family history** — affected **maternal uncles**

**Pathophysiology** — VIII + IX needed for **thrombin generation in the intrinsic pathway** → unstable clot

### Genes & lab markers

**Coagulation** — ✓ **aPTT prolonged, PT normal** · LFTs normal · CBC (Hb)

**Factor assays** — plasma factor VIII/IX activity + **inhibitor assay** (diagnosis)

**Bethesda assay** — quantifies inhibitor antibody if inhibitor positive · none / **<5 BU low** / **>5 BU high**

| Severity | Factor level |
|---|---|
| Mild | 5–25% |
| Moderate | 1–5% |
| **Severe** | **0–1%** |

| Bleeding disorder | Inheritance | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Factor VIII / IX deficiency** | ✓ **X-linked recessive** | Spontaneous haemarthroses |
| Factor XI (haemophilia C) | Autosomal recessive | Surgical bleeding, no spontaneous haemarthrosis |
| **von Willebrand** (commonest bleeding disorder) | Autosomal dominant (chr 12) | **Mucosal** bleeding · ↓ platelet adhesion |
| Protein C / S deficiency, factor V Leiden | AD | **Thrombosis** (not bleeding) |
| Vitamin K deficiency | Acquired | Diet, malabsorption, liver disease |

### DIC (fulminant)

**Labs** — ✓ **aPTT ↑** and PT ↑ · ↓ fibrinogen, ↓ platelets · ↑ FDPs, **↑ D-dimer** · heparin not proven · causes: massive transfusion, sepsis, burns, crush, liver disease, malignancy, obstetric

## 3 Presentation

**Bleeding** — joints (**knee most common** haemarthrosis; elbow, ankle, shoulder, spine) · muscles (quadriceps, hamstrings, **iliopsoas**, biceps, triceps) · mucocutaneous

### Orthopaedic manifestations

**Haemophilic arthropathy** — synovitis → cartilage destruction → deformity

**Intramuscular haematoma (pseudotumour)** — ✓ **iliacus haematoma → femoral nerve** compression → paraesthesia in **L4 / saphenous** distribution (medial distal tibia) · groin pain after sport (forceful hip flexion)

**Other** — LLD (**epiphyseal overgrowth**) · fractures (osteopenia, **heal in normal time**) · compartment syndrome

## 5 Management

### Management (factor replacement, synovectomy, haematoma)

| Situation | Target factor level |
|---|---|
| Vigorous physiotherapy | 20% |
| **Acute haematoma** | **30%** |
| **Acute haemarthrosis, soft-tissue surgery** | **40–50%** (OB: "within 60%") |
| **Skeletal surgery** | **100% for week 1 → >50% for week 2** |

**High-titre factor VIII inhibitor** — ✓ **factor VIIa** (bypasses VIII) for acute bleed, **compartment syndrome**, surgery

**Femoral fracture + compartment syndrome + high inhibitor titre** — ✓ **factor VIIa immediately → fasciotomy + IMN**\
✗ Wrong options: VIII replacement (won't work) · operate first · plate

**Non-life-threatening bleed** — analgesia, physio · no routine immobilisation or paracentesis

**Synovectomy** — ✓ **recurrent haemarthrosis despite optimal medical management** · ↓ bleeding but doesn't restore ROM or reverse cartilage damage · not for radiographic narrowing, stiffness, prophylaxis

**Radioactive synoviorthesis** — ↓ recurrence **more than surgical synovectomy**

**End-stage** — arthroplasty

### VTE prophylaxis

**Known bleeding disorder (factor VIII deficiency)** — ✓ **pneumatic compression only, no pharmacoprophylaxis** **(TO)** · also active liver disease\
✗ Wrong options: protein C/S deficiency, factor V Leiden (thrombophilias)

## 6 Complications

**Inhibitors (IgG)** — **4–20%** · any time · **relative contraindication to surgery** · screen preop

**Blood-borne infection** — HIV ~**10–15%** (this note) · up to 90% (arthropathy note, older cohorts) · recombinant factor VIII (1992) eliminated risk

**Allergy** to blood products

## How it's asked

**"Avoid pharmacoprophylaxis in favour of pneumatic compression (TO)?"** → **factor VIII deficiency**\
✗ Wrong options: protein C, S, factor V Leiden

**"Haemophilic boy, iliacus haematoma, medial distal tibia paraesthesia — nerve?"** → **femoral**\
✗ Wrong options: obturator, genitofemoral

**"Femur fracture + compartment syndrome + high VIII inhibitor?"** → **factor VIIa now, then fasciotomy + IMN**

**"Primary indication for synovectomy?"** → **recurrent bleeding despite optimal medical management**

**"Fulminant DIC finding?"** → **↑ aPTT**\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ fibrinogen · ↓ D-dimer · normal PT · heparin curative

**"Factor VIII deficiency inheritance?"** → **X-linked recessive**
