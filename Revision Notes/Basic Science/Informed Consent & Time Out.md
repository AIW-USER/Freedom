# Informed Consent & Time Out

*Basic Science · 50 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Elective consent

**Optimal** — ✓ **by the surgeon, in the office, a few days (within 7 days) before** surgery\
✗ Wrong options: nurse/surgeon in pre-op holding · risk manager · scheduler

**Disclose** — who will operate · risks, benefits, indications, alternatives · surgeon's **HIV/HBV/HCV status** for exposure-prone procedures (at scheduling) · **origin of implants** (bovine — Hindu · porcine — Jewish, Muslim)

**Adolescents** — AAP: **>14 yr** who **dissent** treated as having capacity, even though parental legal consent needed

### Emergency consent

**Life-threatening, no legal consent available** — proceed; **confirm + document necessity with a colleague**

**Non-life-threatening** — consent required first (language barrier, child)

**Next of kin order** — **spouse → children → parents → siblings → grandchildren**

### Research & privacy

**HIPAA** — no patient consent needed to share PHI with **other treating providers**

**IRB** — mandates clinical-trial consent process · levels: exempt (still needs chair certification), expedited, full board

**No IRB review needed** — ✓ **internal quality-improvement studies** (OR efficiency, preop delays) even with identifiers · becomes research (IRB) if published/presented nationally or applied beyond the institution

**IRB required** — any study using PHI (**medical record number is PHI**) · **vulnerable populations**: prisoners, pregnant women, fetuses, neonates, cognitively impaired, **children**, students, employees

### Surgical safety checklist

**WHO checklist (2009)** — ✓ ↓ **surgical mortality**, ↓ in-hospital complications, ↑ adherence in OR crises (haemorrhage, arrest)

## How it's asked

**"Optimal setting for elective written consent?"** → **surgeon in office within 7 days prior**

**"Which studies need no IRB review?"** → **internal QI studies (2 and 4)**\
✗ Wrong options: MRN retrospective study · children with CP

**"Next of kin first in line?"** → **spouse**
