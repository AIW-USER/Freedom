# Lead Toxicity

## 1 Basics

### PTHrP inhibition

**Mechanism** — lead inhibits ✓ **parathyroid hormone-related peptide (PTHrP)** signalling → **growth inhibition** (PTHrP normally delays chondrocyte hypertrophy in the physis)

**Bone effect** — **↓ bone mineral density** (animal studies)

**Traps** — ✗ Wrong options: TSH · growth hormone · IGF · GnRH

## How it's asked

**"Lead toxicity affects growth by inhibiting?"** → **PTHrP**
