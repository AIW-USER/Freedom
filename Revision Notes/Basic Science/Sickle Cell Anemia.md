# Sickle Cell Anemia

*Basic Science · 99 / 108*

## 1 Basics

**Epidemiology** — ~**2 million** Americans carry the gene · **1 in 12 African Americans**

| | **Sickle cell anaemia (disease)** | **Sickle cell trait** |
|---|---|---|
| Genotype | **Two HbS alleles** | One HbS + one HbA |
| Severity | **Most severe** | Usually asymptomatic · **most common** |
| Key risk | Crises, infarcts, AVN, infection | **Exertional sickling collapse** → sudden death |

**Pathophysiology** — low O₂ → **sickle-shaped RBCs** → can't traverse vessels → vascular occlusion

![Sickle cells](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4114-sicklecells.jpg)

**Infection susceptibility** — **hyposplenism / functional asplenia** · sluggish circulation · ↓ opsonisation / impaired complement · GI micro-infarcts → Salmonella entry

### Orthopaedic manifestations

| Manifestation |
|---|
| **Sickle cell crisis** |
| **Osteomyelitis** · septic arthritis |
| **Osteonecrosis of femoral + humeral heads** |
| **Bone infarcts** |
| Growth retardation / skeletal immaturity |
| **Dactylitis** (acute hand/foot swelling) |

## 3 Presentation

**Sickle cell crisis** — severe bone pain · begins ~**age 2–3** · mediated by **substance P** · may cause infarcts · **hydroxyurea** → pain relief

**Sickle cell trait in athletes** — ✓ **oxygen + oral/IV hydration + rest** for exertional sickling collapse (leading cause of death in college football) · other SCT risks: compartment syndrome, lumbar myonecrosis, splenic infarct, haematuria, hyposthenuria, VTE\
✗ Wrong options: warm clothes · extra analgesia / rest days (disease, not trait) · NSAID avoidance

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **haemoglobin electrophoresis**

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **bone infarcts** · **biconcave "fish-tail" vertebrae**

![Fish vertebrae in sickle cell](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4114-xr-fish-vertebra.jpg)

### Bone infarction vs osteomyelitis & Salmonella

| Test | **Infarct** | **Osteomyelitis** |
|---|---|---|
| **Bone scan** | Abnormal | Abnormal |
| **Bone marrow scan** | ✓ **↓ marrow uptake** | **Normal** marrow uptake |
| **MRI** | — | **Gadolinium T1** identifies infection |
| CBC, ESR, CRP | May be ↑ | May be ↑ |
| Definitive | — | **Aspirate + culture** |

**Infarct confirmed (↓ marrow uptake)** — ✓ **symptomatic care: NWB, IV hydration, haematology**\
✗ Wrong options: biopsy · debridement · oral antibiotics · steroid injection

**Osteomyelitis organisms** — **S. aureus + Salmonella** (which is commoner is unclear; S. aureus still most likely per OB3500/OB4399) · ✓ **Salmonella osteomyelitis occurs most commonly / almost exclusively in sickle cell** · often **diaphyseal** · may spread from gallbladder / gut micro-infarcts

| Osteomyelitis population | Organism |
|---|---|
| **Sickle cell** | ✓ **Salmonella** (characteristic) + S. aureus |
| Neonates <4 mo | S. aureus, Enterobacter, group A/B Strep |
| **Sneaker puncture** | **Pseudomonas** |
| IVDU, dialysis | Staph, Enterobacter, Pseudomonas, Strep |

**Salmonella osteomyelitis → associated finding** — ✓ **sickled cells** on smear\
✗ Wrong options: OI, child abuse, renal failure, thalassaemia (one case)\
✗ Wrong options: myeloma plasma cells · synovial sarcoma · GCT giant cells · Ewing pseudo-rosettes

## 5 Management

**Septic arthritis** — fever, bone pain, ↑ CRP/ESR · aspirate + culture · **I&D** — consider **pre-op oxygenation + exchange transfusion**

### Osteonecrosis

**Femoral head AVN** — may be **bilateral** · mechanism ✓ **HbS → sickling → vascular occlusion**\
✗ Wrong options: SCFE slip · Salmonella · GAG accumulation · COL5A1

**Screening** — ✓ **haemoglobin : haematocrit ratio** (proxy for **MCHC**) = **strongest predictor** of femoral head ON · high-risk → hip MRI for early ON / hip preservation

| AVN stage | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Initial | **Partial weight-bearing + ROM** |
| Failed nonop / intractable pain | **THA** — **poor results** (ongoing bone remodelling) |

**Humeral head ON prognosis** — ✓ **sickle cell = most benign course / best prognosis**\
✗ Wrong options: steroids (commonest non-traumatic cause) · alcohol · radiation · malunion

## How it's asked

**"Sickle cell thigh pain, abnormal bone scan, ↓ marrow uptake?"** → **symptomatic care, NWB, IV fluids, haematology** (infarct)

**"Salmonella osteomyelitis — associated histology (figure)?"** → **sickled red cells**\
✗ Wrong options: plasma cells · pseudo-rosettes

**"Salmonella haematogenous osteomyelitis commonest in?"** → **sickle cell disease**\
✗ Wrong options: neonates · IVDU · dialysis · sneaker puncture

**"3-yr-old with Salmonella osteomyelitis also has?"** → **sickle cell anaemia**\
✗ Wrong options: thalassaemia · OI

**"Sickle cell, 2-yr progressive hip pain, AVN — mechanism?"** → **abnormal HbS alleles**\
✗ Wrong options: SCFE · Salmonella

**"Humeral head ON with best prognosis?"** → **sickle cell disease**\
✗ Wrong options: steroids, alcohol, radiation

**"Purpose of Hb : Hct ratio in sickle cell?"** → **screen for femoral head osteonecrosis risk**

**"Football players with sickle trait may need?"** → **oxygen + hydration**
