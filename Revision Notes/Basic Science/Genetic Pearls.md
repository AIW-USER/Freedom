### Tumour suppressors & translocations (RB, p53, Li-Fraumeni)

**Tumour suppressor** — normally inhibits proliferation · loss → unregulated growth

| Gene | Type | Facts |
|---|---|---|
| **RB-1** | ✓ **Tumour suppressor** · **recessive** (both alleles) | Abnormal = **phosphorylated** (can't bind DNA) · 100% retinoblastoma · **35% osteosarcoma** |
| **p53** | Tumour suppressor · **dominant** (one allele) | Blocks entry to **S-phase** if DNA damaged · ~50% of all tumours · **osteosarcoma 20–65%** · chondrosarcoma · **Li-Fraumeni (germline, AD)** |

**Li-Fraumeni** — breast (25%) > **osteosarcoma (20%)** · soft-tissue sarcoma, leukaemia, brain, colon · homozygous p53 → 100% malignancy

**Oncogenes** — **FAK** (focal adhesion kinase) · **Erb-2** (EGF receptor variant)

| Sarcoma | Translocation | Fusion |
|---|---|---|
| **Ewing's** | **t(11;22)** | **EWS-FLI1** |
| **Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma** | **t(2;13)** (PAX3 70%) / t(1;13) (PAX7) | **PAX3-FKHR** |
| **Myxoid liposarcoma** | **t(12;16)** | **TLS-CHOP** |
| **Synovial sarcoma** | **t(X;18)** (>90%) | **SYT-SSX1/2/4** |

### Epigenetics & siRNA

**Epigenetics** — ✓ **inheritable alteration without DNA mutation** · **DNA methylation, histone modification, nucleosome location, non-coding RNA** · role in **OA** (methylation in chondrocytes/synoviocytes → proteases) · genomic imprinting (Prader-Willi, Angelman)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Transgene / transgenesis | Gene artificially introduced into embryo / another organism |
| Enhancer | Region ↑ transcription |
| Promoter | Controls transcription start |
| Transformation | Uptake of exogenous DNA (plasmid into bacterium) |
| Hybridisation | Annealing of complementary DNA/RNA |

## How it's asked

**"RB-1 is best characterised as?"** → **tumour suppressor**\
✗ Wrong options: oncogene, proto-oncogene

**"Translocation associated with osteosarcoma?"** → **none**\
✗ Wrong options: SYT-SSX1, CHOP-TLS, EWS-FLI1, FKHR-PAX3

**"Correctly paired sarcoma + translocation?"** → **clear cell sarcoma t(12;22)**

**"9-yr-old, destructive distal femur lesion, p53 mutation — histology?"** → **osteosarcoma**

**"Inherited p53 mutation causes?"** → **Li-Fraumeni**\
✗ Wrong options: Maffucci (IDH) · FD/McCune-Albright (GNAS)

**"Heritable change without DNA mutation (methylation, histones)?"** → **epigenetics**

**"Function of siRNA?"** → **blocks translation of mRNA**\
✗ Wrong options: Western (protein) · Southern (DNA)

**"DNA methylation in chondrocytes in OA?"** → **epigenetics**

**"Tool to detect translocations prenatally?"** → **cytogenetic analysis**

**"Serum marker for colorectal carcinoma?"** → **CEA**
