# Endochondral Bone Formation

*Basic Science · 30 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Where it occurs

**Three settings** — **longitudinal physeal growth** · **embryonic long-bone formation** · **non-rigid (secondary) fracture healing** (cast, brace, **IM nail**)

**Principle** — **cartilage model** → chondrocytes hypertrophy/calcify → osteoclasts resorb → osteoblasts lay bone **on** the cartilage framework (bone **replaces** cartilage, not converted from cartilage) · deposition on **metaphyseal side** · **type X collagen**

### Molecular regulation

**Sox-9** — master regulator of chondrogenesis · collagen II, IV, XI, aggrecan

**Low β-catenin** — ✓ mesenchyme → **cartilage** (↑ SOX9) → collagen II, IX, XI · anlage grows interstitially + appositionally **(TO)**

**High β-catenin (Wnt)** — chondrocytes stop growing, **hypertrophy** → express **RUNX2 + VEGF-A** → vessels, osteoblasts, osteoclasts → **primary ossification centre** **(TO)**

**Ihh–PTHrP loop** — prehypertrophic chondrocytes secrete **Indian hedgehog** → ↑ **PTHrP** → **delays hypertrophic differentiation**

| Mutation | Effect on hypertrophic maturation |
|---|---|
| **Deactivating PTHrP receptor** | ✓ **Accelerated** |
| Activating Ihh | Delayed (↑ PTHrP) |
| Activating TGF-β / SMAD-3 | Delayed (TGF-β inhibits hypertrophy, type X, ALP) |
| PGE2 | No physeal effect shown |

### Physeal blood supply

**Perichondrial artery** — **major nutrition of the physis**

## 2 Mechanism

### Physeal zones & pathology

![Physeal zones with associated diseases](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9006-zones-with-disease.jpg)

| Zone | Features | Diseases |
|---|---|---|
| **Reserve / resting / germinal** | Stores **lipids, glycogen, PG aggregates** · **max PG storage (TO)** · **low O₂** · supplies cells, organises plate | **Gaucher's**, diastrophic dysplasia, Kniest, pseudoachondroplasia |
| **Proliferative** | Chondrocyte division + **columns (stacking)** · ↑ O₂ inhibits calcification · **highest ECM production (TO)** | **Achondroplasia, gigantism**, MHE |
| **Hypertrophic** — maturation, degeneration (cells ↑ 5×), **provisional calcification** | Chondrocyte hypertrophy → death → Ca release → matrix calcifies · **type X collagen** + ALP · Ihh/PTHrP · **weakest zone (TO)** | **SCFE (non-renal)**, **rickets (provisional calcification)**, enchondromas, MPS, Schmid · **physeal fractures (SH I/II) through zone of provisional calcification** |
| **Primary spongiosa** (metaphysis) | Vascular invasion, transverse septa resorbed, osteoblasts on cartilage bars → woven bone | **Metaphyseal corner fracture (abuse)**, scurvy |
| **Secondary spongiosa** | Internal remodelling (woven → lamellar), funnelisation | **Renal SCFE** |

**Type X collagen** — produced by terminal **hypertrophic** chondrocytes · ✓ **abrupt appearance heralds ossification** **(TO)** · type II still most abundant in hypertrophic zone **(TO)**

**Longitudinal growth** — **chondrocyte hypertrophy** contributes most (cell volume/height) (OB8254) · proliferative zone "responsible for longitudinal growth" (OB3234) — read the options

### Physis periphery

| Structure | Function | Disease |
|---|---|---|
| **Groove (zone) of Ranvier** | Wedge of germinal cells · supplies chondrocytes to periphery · ✓ **appositional (circumferential) growth** · ↑ mechanical strength | **Osteochondroma** · SH VI injury (lawnmower, burns) |
| **Perichondrial ring of LaCroix** | Dense fibrous ring · **peripheral stability** · links Ranvier zone to metaphyseal periosteum | — |
| Perichondrial artery | Physeal nutrition | — |

### Growth regulation

**Hueter–Volkmann** — **sustained compression → decelerated longitudinal growth** · tension accelerates

**Law of ossification (TO)** — secondary centre that appears first **fuses first** — **exception: fibula**

| Growth contribution (TO) | % |
|---|---|
| Shoulder (proximal humerus) | **40%** (TO — verify: proximal humerus usually \~80% of humeral growth) |
| Elbow | **20%** |
| Wrist | 40% (TO — verify: distal radius usually \~75–80%) |
| Hip | 12% (TO — verify: proximal femur usually \~30%) |
| Knee | **70%** |
| Ankle | 18% (TO — verify: distal tibia usually \~45%) |

### Embryonic long-bone formation

```
Mesenchymal anlage (6th week in utero)
├─ Cartilage model → CHONDROBLASTS (hyaline precursor)
├─ Vascular buds invade → PRIMARY OSSIFICATION CENTRE (~8 wk) + MARROW
└─ Ends keep growing → SECONDARY OSSIFICATION CENTRES (epiphyses, physis)
```

### Non-rigid fracture healing

**Soft callus** = cartilage intermediate → proliferation → **hypertrophy** → calcification → bone · IM nail → both endochondral and intramembranous

## How it's asked

**"Physeal zone with maximum PG storage (TO)?"** → **resting zone**

**"Collagen whose abrupt appearance heralds ossification (TO)?"** → **type X**

**"SH I fractures occur through?"** → **zone of provisional calcification**

**"SH II distal radius — zone?"** → **zone of provisional calcification**

**"Gigantism affects?"** → **proliferative zone (C)**

**"Type X collagen prominent in?"** → **zone of hypertrophy**

**"Accelerated hypertrophic maturation?"** → **deactivating PTHrP-receptor mutation**\
✗ Wrong options: activating TGF-β, SMAD-3, Ihh

**"Found during chondrocyte differentiation / anlage formation (TO)?"** → **low β-catenin**\
✗ Wrong options: RUNX2, VEGF-A (hypertrophy/ossification) · RANKL

**"Sustained compression on physis?"** → **decelerated longitudinal growth**

**"Groove of Ranvier responsible for?"** → **appositional growth**

**"Process contributing most to longitudinal growth?"** → **chondrocyte hypertrophy**
