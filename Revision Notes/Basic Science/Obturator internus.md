# Obturator internus

*Basic Science · 69 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy

| | Obturator internus |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Internal surface of obturator membrane + posterior bony margin of obturator foramen |
| **Insertion** | **Medial greater trochanter** (with superior + inferior gemelli) |
| **Action** | External rotation · abducts flexed thigh |
| **Innervation** | **Nerve to obturator internus (L5, S1)** |
| **Arterial supply** | Internal pudendal, superior + inferior gluteal arteries |

### Cross-section at the greater trochanters

**Obturator internus** — projects **posteriorly around the ischium** (lesser sciatic notch) → posterior proximal femur **just below piriformis**

| Structure | Position |
|---|---|
| Obturator externus | **Anterior** to ischium |
| Pectineus | Posterior, deep to femoral vessels |
| Adductor longus / brevis | Just appearing anteriorly / behind longus |
| Adductor magnus | More distal (not at this level) |

## How it's asked

**"Pelvic cross-section at greater trochanters — muscle wrapping posteriorly around ischium?"** → **obturator internus**\
✗ Wrong options: obturator externus (anterior)
