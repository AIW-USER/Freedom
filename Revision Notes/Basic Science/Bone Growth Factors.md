# Bone Growth Factors

*Basic Science · 17 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Overview of growth factors

| Factor | Source | Receptor | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TGF-β** | Platelets, bone ECM, cartilage | **Serine-threonine kinase** | Pleiotropic · **undifferentiated MSC proliferation** · type II collagen + PG by MSCs · osteoblast collagen synthesis · in fracture haematoma · coats porous implants |
| **BMP** | Osteoprogenitors, osteoblasts, bone ECM | **Serine-threonine kinase** → **SMADs** | ✓ **MSC → chondrocytes + osteoblasts** · skeletal patterning |
| **FGF** (FGF-1, -2 most abundant) | Macrophages, MSCs, chondrocytes, osteoblasts | **Tyrosine kinase** | **Mitogenic** · angiogenesis · early fracture healing |
| **IGF-1** (somatomedin C) | Bone matrix, osteoblasts, chondrocytes | **Tyrosine kinase** | **Most abundant GF in bone** · GH–IGF-1 → physeal **proliferation without maturation** (linear growth) · intramembranous defects |
| **IGF-2** | — | Tyrosine kinase | **More potent than IGF-1** · type I collagen, cartilage matrix, proliferation, bone |
| **PDGF** | Platelets, osteoblasts | **Tyrosine kinase** | Mitogen · **macrophage/inflammatory-cell chemotaxis** to fracture |
| **PPAR-γ** | — | **Nuclear receptor** (binds DNA) | **Adipogenic** differentiation |
| **Wnt** | — | **LRP5/6** → β-catenin to nucleus | Osteoblast differentiation |

**Thyroid hormone** — nuclear receptor → physeal **proliferation and maturation**

**Morphogens vs mitogens (disc)** — BMP-2, BMP-7, TGF-β change phenotype (↑ chondrogenic phenotype + matrix) without proliferation · IGF-1, FGF ↑ proliferation

### BMP signalling (SMADs, serine-threonine kinase)

**Family** — TGF-β superfamily (with GDFs, AMH, activin, Nodal) · ~30 members

**Target** — undifferentiated **perivascular MSCs**

**Cascade** — BMP → heterodimer of **type I + II serine-threonine kinase receptors** → phosphorylate **SMAD 1, 5, 8** → complex with **SMAD 4** → nucleus → osteoblast genes · 8 SMADs known

![BMP receptor → SMAD signalling](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9086-bmp-and-smad.jpg)

| BMP | Fact |
|---|---|
| **BMP-2, 4, 6, 7** | **Osteoinductive** (BMP-2 potent osteogenic + osteoinductive) |
| **BMP-3** | **Not osteoinductive** · **antagonises BMP-2** · **most abundant BMP in DBM** · knockout → 2× trabecular bone · ↑ chondrogenic markers (type II collagen, aggrecan) |
| **BMP-1** | **Metalloprotease** (protease for collagen I, II, III) · not bone-inducing |
| **BMP-4** | Altered signalling → **fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva** |

### Lineage factors

| Factor | Drives |
|---|---|
| BMP, **RUNX2**, Wnt/β-catenin, ATF4 (with Runx2) | **Osteogenic** |
| **SOX9** | Chondrogenic |
| **PPAR-γ** | ✓ **Adipogenic** (pre-adipocyte → adipocyte) · inhibited by Wnt5a |

**Thiazolidinediones** — **PPAR-γ agonists** → ↑ marrow adiposity, **↓ osteoblastogenesis** → **↓ BMD** (femoral neck, hip), ↑ fracture risk

**GSK-3 inhibition** — ↑ β-catenin → ↑ osteoblasts\
✗ Wrong options: ↓ BMD

## 5 Management

### rhBMP-2 clinical use

| Product | FDA-approved use |
|---|---|
| **rhBMP-2** | **Single-level ALIF L2–S1** for DDD with **LT-Cage** |
| **rhBMP-2** | **Open tibial shaft fracture + IM nail, within 14 days** |
| rhBMP-7 (HDE) | Recalcitrant long-bone nonunion where autograft unfeasible + alternatives failed |
| rhBMP-7 (HDE) | Revision posterolateral lumbar fusion in compromised hosts (osteoporosis, smoking, DM) |

**Not approved (rhBMP-2)** — plates (MIPO tibia) · posterior/posterolateral lumbar · multilevel · **cervical**

**PLF in >60 yr** — rhBMP-2 **cost-effective** vs ICBG and ↓ revision surgery (off-label)

**Contraindications** — pregnancy · allergy to **bovine type I collagen** or rhBMP-2 · infection · tumour · **skeletal immaturity**

**Concerns** — **uncontrolled heterotopic bone** · cost

## How it's asked

**"All true of BMP-3 EXCEPT?"** → **"it is osteoinductive"**

**"BMP receptor class?"** → **serine-threonine kinase**\
✗ Wrong options: tyrosine kinase, MAPK, aurora, GSK-3

**"Phosphorylated directly after BMP binding?"** → **SMADs**\
✗ Wrong options: Myc · β-catenin (Wnt) · adenylate cyclase

**"Serine-threonine receptor, MSC metaplasia into bone-forming cells?"** → **BMP**\
✗ Wrong options: IGF-2, PDGF (tyrosine kinase) · OPG · RANKL

**"Osteoinduction and osteogenesis?"** → **BMP-2**\
✗ Wrong options: BMP-1 (metalloprotease) · BMP-3 · PPAR-γ · IGF-1

**"Promotes MSC → chondrocytes + osteoblasts?"** → **BMP**\
✗ Wrong options: TGF-β (proliferation) · FGF (mitogen)

**"↑ chondrogenic phenotype of disc cells?"** → **BMPs**

**"FDA-approved rhBMP-2 scenario?"** → **L5–S1 ALIF for DDD**\
✗ Wrong options: plated open tibia · posterolateral · cervical

**"rhBMP-2 true EXCEPT?"** → **"FDA indicated for single-level posterolateral lumbar fusion"**

**"Binds LRP5/6?"** → **Wnt**

**"Drives MSCs to adipose?"** → **PPAR-γ**\
✗ Wrong options: BMP, RUNX2, SOX9, Wnt

**"Thiazolidinedione effect on BMD?"** → **activates PPAR-γ → adipocytes ↑, osteoblastogenesis ↓, BMD ↓**

**"Altered BMP-4 signalling?"** → **fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva**
