# Bone Signaling & RANKL

*Basic Science · 20 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### RANK–RANKL–OPG axis

**Central pathway** — couples osteoblast and osteoclast activity · resorption stimulated by **RANKL (normal)** · **PTHrP (pathological — metastases)**

**RANKL** — major source = ✓ **osteoblasts** (+ marrow stromal cells, **activated T cells**, osteocytes) · binds **RANK** on osteoclast precursors + mature osteoclasts · RANKL + **M-CSF** → fusion into multinucleated osteoclasts · secreted in response to **inflammatory stimuli** · TNF superfamily

**OPG (osteoprotegerin)** — **decoy receptor** from **osteoblasts + stromal cells** · ✓ **binds and sequesters RANKL** → ↓ osteoclast differentiation, fusion, activation

**Primary stimulus for osteoclastic resorption** — ✓ **RANK–RANKL interaction**\
✗ Wrong options: PTH–osteoclast direct · OPG–RANKL

**Osteoclast transcription factors** — PU.1, NF-κB, AP-1, **NFATc1**, Mitf, Myc, Src · **DC-STAMP** required for fusion

### Osteoclast activators vs inhibitors

| Stimulate resorption | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **RANKL** | Binds RANK |
| **PTH** | Receptor on **osteoblasts** → **adenylyl cyclase/cAMP/PKA** → ↑ RANKL + M-CSF, ↓ OPG |
| **IL-1** | ↑ osteoclast differentiation |
| **1,25-(OH)₂D** | ↑ RANKL expression · terminal osteoclast differentiation |
| **PGE2** | Activates adenylyl cyclase |
| **IL-6, MIP-1α** | **Myeloma** |
| TNF-α | ↑ resorption (✗ Wrong options: not an inhibitor) |
| Thyroid hormone, GH | Systemic stimulators |

| Inhibit resorption | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **OPG** | Sequesters RANKL |
| **Calcitonin** | ✓ **Binds directly to receptor on osteoclast** → loss of ruffled border, retraction, ↓ number + activity · from thyroid **parafollicular cells** · ↑ by hypercalcaemia, gastrin, pentagastrin · on osteocytes: ↓ apoptosis, ↑ sclerostin |
| **Oestrogen** | ↓ RANKL · inhibits adenylyl cyclase · also anabolic |
| **TGF-β** | ↑ OPG |
| **IL-10** | Suppresses osteoclasts |
| Androgens | Systemic |
| Bisphosphonates | Osteoclast apoptosis |
| **Denosumab** | **Anti-RANKL IgG2** (OPG-like) |

**Dexamethasone / corticosteroids** — ↓ OPG → ↑ osteoclast formation + longevity → osteoporosis

### Wnt/β-catenin signalling (TO)

**Wnt** — binds **frizzled + LRP5/6** → β-catenin accumulates → binds **lymphoid enhancer factor (LEF)** → bone-formation genes + **OPG** **(TO)**

**Wnt antagonists** — **Dkk-1**, soluble frizzled-related proteins (**sFRP**), **sclerostin (SOST)** **(TO)**

| Favour formation (TO) | Favour resorption (TO) | Coupling factors (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **OPG, BMPs, Wnt, β-catenin–LEF complex** | RANK, RANKL, M-CSF, DC-STAMP, Dkk-1, sFRP, sclerostin | **TGF-β** (released + activated by acid during resorption) · **ephrinB2** (osteoclast) → **EphB4** (osteoblast) |

**Does NOT inhibit bone formation (TO)** — ✓ **β-catenin–LEF complex**\
✗ Wrong options: sclerostin, Dkk-1, RANKL

### Smad signalling

**BMP-2 cascade** — receptor phosphorylates **regulatory Smad 1, 5, 8** → complex with ✓ **co-Smad 4** (not phosphorylated) → nucleus

**Smad 6, 7** — **inhibitory** · Smad 2, 3 — TGF-β pathway

### Second messengers

**Adenylyl cyclase / cAMP** — ✓ **PTH** (Gαs/PKA in osteocytes) · PGE2

**Serine-threonine kinase** — BMP, TGF-β (→ SMADs) · **Tyrosine kinase** — IGF-II

### Osteoporosis drugs by target

| Drug | Target |
|---|---|
| **Calcitonin** | **Directly binds osteoclast** |
| **Denosumab** | RANKL antibody |
| **Romosozumab** | **Sclerostin antibody** → activates Wnt |
| **Teriparatide** | PTH analogue → **activates osteoblasts** |
| Vitamin D | Osteoclast differentiation + osteoid mineralisation |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Genes & lab markers

**Alkaline phosphatase** — from **bone (osteoblasts) + liver** · bone ↑: Paget's, metastases, osteomalacia · liver ↑: hepatitis, cirrhosis, **liver metastases**, biliary obstruction

**↑ ALP, negative bone scan, known cancer** — ✓ check **ALT/AST** (liver)\
✗ Wrong options: CEA · PSA · CT hip · MRI brain

**Antibodies to RANKL or sclerostin** — ↑ BMD

## 6 Complications

### Clinical implications

| Condition | RANKL-pathway link |
|---|---|
| **Osteoporosis** | **Loss of OPG function** → constitutive osteoclast activation · **OPG knockout mice → osteoporosis** |
| **Osteopetrosis** | Absent osteoclastic resorption · **RANKL knockout → osteopetrosis-like** · not OPG knockout |
| Paget's | Altered RANK cytoplasmic binding or **OPG mutations** |
| **Lytic metastases** | ✓ **Cancer cells directly produce RANKL** · OPG blockade ↓ metastasis (animals) · denosumab ↓ pathological fractures |
| **TJA osteolysis** | PE debris → macrophages → cytokines incl. **RANKL** → osteoclasts |
| **Inflammatory arthropathy** (RA pannus, gout tophi) | IL-6, IL-8, TNF-α · ↑ RANKL, ↓ OPG |

**OPG overexpression would help** — ✓ **osteoporosis** (also Paget's, metastases)\
✗ Wrong options: osteopetrosis, rickets, achondroplasia, scurvy

## X vs Y

| | RANKL | OPG | RANK |
|---|---|---|---|
| Made by | Osteoblasts, T cells, tumour | Osteoblasts, stromal cells | — (receptor) |
| Located | Ligand | Soluble decoy | Osteoclast precursors |
| Effect | ↑ resorption | ↓ resorption | Mediates differentiation |
| Knockout | **Osteopetrosis-like** | **Osteoporosis** | — |
