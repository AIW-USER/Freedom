## 1 Basics

### Innate & adaptive immunity

| | **Innate** | **Acquired (adaptive)** |
|---|---|---|
| Specificity | Non-specific (structures common to microbes) | Pathogen/antigen-specific |
| Memory | No | Yes |
| Components | Skin barrier, inflammation, **complement** | Antigen processing + presentation |
| Cells | **NK cells, mast cells, eosinophils, basophils, macrophages, neutrophils, dendritic cells** | **CD8+ T cells**, T helpers, γδ T cells, B cells, plasma cells |

**First step of acquired response** — ✓ **antigen processing + presentation to T cells** by **APCs (B cells, dendritic cells)** → peptides on **MHC** → TCR recognises MHC/antigen\
✗ Wrong options: T cells phagocytose antigen · disulfide cleavage occurs in APC

**MoM bearing reactivity** — innate (cell-mediated) per OB · metal implants → **type IV** (Orthobullets)

### Hypersensitivity

| Type | Mediator | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **I — immediate/anaphylactic** | **IgE** → mast cells, basophils | Allergy, **urticaria** (rapid hives) |
| **II — cytotoxic** | IgG, IgM | Transfusion reactions |
| **III — immune complex** | IgG, IgM complexes deposit | — |
| **IV — delayed (cell-mediated)** | **T cells**, monocytes, macrophages · 2–3 days (7–10 for dermatitis) | **TB skin test** · **metal implants (plate + screws)** · **topical antibiotic (bacitracin) contact dermatitis** |

**Bacitracin contact dermatitis** — **5–10%** · erythema, oedema, **blistering**, no pus/fluctuance → stop agent ± steroid cream

### Benign ethnic neutropenia

**Most common neutropenia worldwide** — **25–50% of people of African descent**, some Middle Eastern groups · lifelong low ANC **without ↑ infection risk**

**ANC <1.5 × 10⁹/L** — 4.5% African-Americans · 0.79% white · 0.38% Mexican-Americans

**More common in** — **males**, **athletes**, children <5 · hypothesis: malaria protection

### HLA associations & HIV arthropathy (TO)

✗ Wrong options: DR4, B5, B47

## How it's asked

**"First step of TCR–MHC acquired response?"** → **antigen processing + presentation to T cells**

**"First antibody class after antigen exposure?"** → **IgM** (IgG — most abundant)

**"Type IV hypersensitivity seen with?"** → **metal plate + screw placement**\
✗ Wrong options: transfusions · red man syndrome

**"Healthy patient, baseline WBC 1.5 for years?"** → **more likely African than European descent**\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ infection risk · female · non-athlete

**"NOT innate immune cells?"** → **CD8+ T lymphocytes**

**"Finger laceration + bacitracin → erythema, blistering, no pus at 1 wk?"** → **allergic contact dermatitis**\
✗ Wrong options: cellulitis · IgE urticaria

**"HIV arthropathy — all true EXCEPT (TO)?"** → **"symmetrical polyarthritis is the most common form"**

**"HLA in coeliac disease (TO)?"** → **HLA-DQ2**
