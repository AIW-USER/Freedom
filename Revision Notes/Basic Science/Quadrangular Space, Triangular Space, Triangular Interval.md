# Quadrangular Space, Triangular Space, Triangular Interval

*Basic Science · 88 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Contents of shoulder spaces

| Space | Borders | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **Quadrangular (quadrilateral) space** | Medial **long head triceps** · lateral **humeral shaft** · superior **teres minor** · inferior **teres major** | ✓ **Axillary nerve + posterior humeral circumflex artery** |
| **Triangular space** | Lateral **long head triceps** · superior teres minor · inferior teres major | **Scapular circumflex artery** (no major nerve) |
| **Triangular interval** | Medial long head triceps · lateral **lateral head triceps / humerus** · superior **teres major** | **Radial nerve + profunda brachii artery** |

## How it's asked

**"Correct pairing of shoulder space and contents?"** → **quadrangular space — axillary nerve + posterior humeral circumflex artery**\
✗ Wrong options: quadrangular (radial nerve/profunda) · quadrangular (scapular circumflex) · triangular space (axillary nerve)
