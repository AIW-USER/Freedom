# Abductor digiti minimi

*Basic Science · 1 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy — foot ADM (Orthobullets)

| | Abductor digiti minimi (foot) |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Calcaneal tuberosity |
| **Insertion** | Base of 5th toe |
| **Action** | Abducts small toe |
| **Innervation** | **Lateral plantar nerve** |
| **Arterial supply** | Lateral plantar artery |

![Abductor digiti minimi of the foot](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob10094-ll1-moved.jpg)

### Blood supply — hand ADM

**Hand ADM** — hypothenar · abducts 5th digit · **deep branch of ulnar nerve (C8, T1)**

**Main blood supply** — ✓ **ulnar artery** (deep branch) · 1 major + 2 constant minor pedicles · major pedicle leaves ulnar artery just distal to **pisiform**

**Flap use** — ADM can be used as a local flap in crushed hands

![Ulnar artery supplying ADM and FDM (hypothenar excised)](../../MCQ/attachments/q1267-illb-adm-ulnar-artery.jpg)

## X vs Y

| Artery | Supplies |
|---|---|
| **Ulnar artery** | ✓ **Hypothenar muscles (ADM, FDM)** |
| Radial artery | Thenar muscles |
| Posterior interosseous artery | Superficial + deep extensor compartments of forearm |
| Anterior interosseous artery | Forearm flexor compartment |
| Palmar metacarpal artery | Palmar interossei |

| | Hand ADM | Foot ADM |
|---|---|---|
| Nerve | Deep branch **ulnar** | **Lateral plantar** |
| Artery | **Ulnar** | Lateral plantar |

## How it's asked

**"Main blood supply to abductor digiti minimi (hand)?"** → **ulnar artery**\
✗ Wrong options: PIA · AIA · palmar metacarpal · radial

**"Nerve to abductor digiti minimi of the foot?"** → **lateral plantar nerve**
