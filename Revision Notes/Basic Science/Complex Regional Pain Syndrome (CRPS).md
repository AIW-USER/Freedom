# Complex Regional Pain Syndrome (CRPS)

*Basic Science · 27 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Synonyms** — **RSD** (type I) · **causalgia** (type II)

**Incidence** — 5.5 / 100,000 person-yr (US) · 6–26 / 100,000 (OB) · ↑ with age to 70

**Sex** — **female 4:1** (Orthobullets/OB) · 2–4× (TO)

**Site** — **upper limb 60%** (Orthobullets) · **70%** (TO) · type I **88%** vs type II 12% (TO)

**Postoperative rate** — 10–37% (OB)

### Risk factors

**Listed** — trauma with exaggerated response (**commonest cause of poor outcome after foot crush**) · surgery (multiple procedures) · **prolonged immobilisation**, tight dressings/casts (early cast complaints) · **anxiety/depression** · **ACE inhibitor at time of trauma** · migraine, asthma · **smoking** · **fibromyalgia (↑ up to 2.5×)** · RA, carpal tunnel

**Not a risk factor** — **male sex**

### Pathophysiology

**Triad** — ✓ **aberrant inflammatory response** · **vasomotor dysfunction** · **maladaptive neuroplasticity** → sustained sympathetic activity in a perpetuated reflex arc

**Mediators** — IL-1β, IL-6, TNF-α, **substance P** · ↑ osteoclasts → regional migratory osteoporosis / **bone marrow oedema**

### Genes & lab markers

**Genetics** — ↑ risk in siblings of young-onset cases · **TNF-α and ACE gene polymorphisms**

**Labs** — WBC, ESR, CRP **normal** (distinguishes from infection)

## 3 Presentation

**Cardinal signs** — **pain out of proportion** · **swelling** · **stiffness** · **skin discolouration**

**Exam** — vasomotor disturbance · trophic skin changes · **hyperhidrosis** · **allodynia, hyperalgesia, hyperpathia** · cool shiny skin · **flamingo gait** (knee) · **equinovarus** (ankle) · not **crepitus**

**Not typical** — ↓ fingertip perfusion (Raynaud) · ptosis/anhidrosis (Horner, preganglionic plexus) · fingertip ulcers (Buerger) · positive Spurling

**Natural course (TO)** — warm, ↑ hair/nail growth, oedema, erythema → later slow hair growth, brittle nails, weakness, **cold limb**

### Lankford & Evans stages

| Stage | Onset | Exam | Imaging |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Acute** | 0–3 mo | Burning pain, redness, swelling, warmth, hyperhidrosis | X-ray normal · **bone scan +** |
| **Subacute (dystrophic)** | 3–12 mo | ↑ pain, cyanosis, dry skin, stiffness, skin atrophy | **Subchondral osteopenia** |
| **Chronic (atrophic)** | >12 mo | ↓ pain, glossy skin, fibrosis, contractures, hair/nail loss | **Extreme osteopenia** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### IASP classification (type I vs II)

| | **Type I** | **Type II** |
|---|---|---|
| Nerve damage | ✓ **None identifiable** | **Identifiable nerve injury** |
| Frequency | **Most common** | Less common |
| Sympathetic block | Responds | **Minimal / no response** |
| Distribution | Whole region | Single nerve territory |

**Not distinguishing** — autonomic dysfunction · trophic changes · functional impairment · positive bone scan (all in both)

### Diagnostic criteria — Budapest

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Continuing pain disproportionate** to inciting event |
| 2 Symptoms | ≥1 symptom in **3 of 4** categories (**clinical**) · **4 of 4** (research) |
| 3 Signs | ≥1 sign in **≥2** categories at evaluation |
| 4 | **No other diagnosis** explains it |

| Category | Symptoms / signs |
|---|---|
| **Sensory** | Hyperaesthesia, **allodynia**, hyperalgesia (pinprick) |
| **Vasomotor** | **Temperature asymmetry**, skin colour change/asymmetry |
| **Sudomotor / oedema** | **Oedema, sweating change/asymmetry** |
| **Motor / trophic** | ↓ ROM, weakness, tremor, dystonia, hair/nail/skin changes |

**Budapest vs IASP** — IASP sensitivity 1.00, **specificity 0.41** · Budapest sensitivity 0.99, **specificity 0.68–0.79**

**Best diagnostic test** — ✓ **history + physical examination** (clinical) · confirmed by **relief with sympathetic block** · early diagnosis critical

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Finding |
|---|---|
| X-ray | Osteopenia (**patella** — commonest knee finding) · soft-tissue swelling · subperiosteal resorption · **joint spaces preserved** |
| **Three-phase bone scan** | ↑ uptake all phases · **phase III (2–3 h) most sensitive** · **high NPV** (rules out type I) · **most sensitive investigation (TO)** · TO note: high specificity, ↓ sensitivity vs Budapest, not confirmatory |
| MRI | **Diffuse bone marrow oedema** (warm phenotype) |
| Thermography | Limb temperature difference **≥1 °C** significant · questionable utility |
| EMG/NCV | Slowing in known nerve (type II, e.g. median) · not diagnostic for type I · myoclonus only 11–36% |

![Three-phase bone scan in CRPS](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6095-bone-scan-in-patient-with-rsd.jpg)

| Bone scan phase | Shows |
|---|---|
| I (2 min) | Arteriogram |
| II (5–10 min) | Cellulitis, synovitis |
| III (2–3 h) | **Bone** |
| IV (24 h) | Osteomyelitis vs cellulitis |

### Differential

**Listed** — soft-tissue infection · malingering · **clenched fist syndrome** (psychiatric) · neuropathic/chronic pain · **Raynaud** (digital vasospasm) · TOS · arterial insufficiency · erythromelalgia

**Clenched fist syndrome** — **non-organic somatoform** disorder · fixed finger flexion · **macerated foul-smelling palm**, paradoxical stiffness · psychiatric association, poor prognosis · normal imaging/EMG → therapy + psychiatry

## 5 Management

### Vitamin C prophylaxis

**Regimen** — ✓ **vitamin C 500 mg daily × 50 days** · 200 mg if renal impairment

**Evidence** — **Level I** (Zollinger, 2 RCTs) in **distal radius fractures** · Lancet: CRPS **7% vs 22%** placebo · JBJS: 200 mg 4.2%, **500 mg 1.8%**, 1500 mg 1.7% → 500 mg recommended · later studies conflicting · also ↓ **CRPS type I** after **foot & ankle surgery** (non-diabetics)

**Mechanism** — ↓ lipid peroxidation · scavenges hydroxyl radicals · protects capillary endothelium · ↓ vascular permeability

**Other prevention** — avoid tight dressings + prolonged immobilisation

### Treatment (physiotherapy, blocks, sympathectomy)

```
CRPS treatment ladder
├─ First line → PT (GENTLE / ACTIVE, desensitisation) + DRUGS
│      (avoid casting, immobilisation, aggressive PROM)
├─ Failed → SYMPATHETIC / NERVE BLOCK → CHEMICAL SYMPATHECTOMY
│      └─ Focal LE CRPS failing blocks → DRG STIMULATION (TO)
└─ Refractory → SURGICAL SYMPATHECTOMY
       └─ Type II with known nerve → DECOMPRESS NERVE (e.g. CTR)
```

**Early CRPS** — ✓ **immediate PT: desensitisation + active ROM + multidisciplinary follow-up**\
✗ Wrong options: restrict PT · LSB first · bone scan before treating

**Modalities** — **gentle physiotherapy** · contrast baths · **tactile discrimination training** · **graded motor imagery** · mirror therapy · splinting, elevation, massage · **TENS — positive only in type II** (OB)

**Graded motor imagery sequence (TO)** — ✓ **laterality restoration → explicit motor imagery → mirror therapy**

**Drugs** — NSAIDs · **α-blockers (phenoxybenzamine, prazosin)** · β-blocker (propranolol) · antidepressants · anticonvulsants · Ca-channel blockers · **gabapentin** · **bisphosphonates** · antiarrhythmics · corticosteroids · calcitonin · vitamin C

**Also used (TO)** — **ketamine** (NMDA antagonist, topical/IV) · **botulinum toxin A** · **mirror therapy** · naltrexone · plasma exchange · cannabis inhaler · **pramipexole — not used**

**Warm phenotype + bone marrow oedema on MRI (<6 mo, TO)** — ✓ **IV bisphosphonates (neridronate)** first-line pharmacological

**Post-TKA CRPS** — ✓ **α-blockers + PT + tactile discrimination + graded motor imagery**\
✗ Wrong options: explore knee · I&D/poly exchange · L-spine MRI

**Nerve stimulation** — TENS · peripheral nerve · spinal cord stimulator · indicated when symptoms mainly in **one peripheral nerve**

**Neuromodulation — focal LE CRPS after failed blocks (TO)** — ✓ **dorsal root ganglion stimulation** (ACCURATE trial: up to 80% pain ↓, fewer postural changes vs tonic SCS) · diffuse whole-limb → SCS

| Block / sympathectomy | Target |
|---|---|
| **Stellate ganglion** | **Upper limb** |
| **Lumbar sympathetic** | **Lower limb** |
| Agents (block) | Lidocaine, bupivacaine, bretylium, guanethidine |
| Agents (chemical sympathectomy) | **Phenol, alcohol** |

**Second line after failed early PT modalities (OB)** — ✓ **surgical sympathectomy** · best candidates = responded to sympathetic block\
✗ Wrong options: cast · boot NWB · exchange nail · SCS

**Best success** — find + treat an associated nerve problem (type II)

## 6 Complications

**Prognosis** — poor response to treatment · **better: upper limb, warm CRPS, children** · late CRPS refractory → permanent disability

## X vs Y

| | CRPS | Raynaud | Horner (preganglionic plexus) | Clenched fist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hallmark | Pain out of proportion, allodynia, sweating | Digital vasospasm in cold | Ptosis, miosis, anhidrosis | Fixed clenched fist, psychiatric |
| Mechanism | Inflammatory + vasomotor | Exaggerated arterial vasoconstriction | Sympathetic chain injury | Somatoform |

## How it's asked

**"Most sensitive investigation for CRPS (TO)?"** → **bone scan**\
✗ Wrong options: CT, MRI, US

**"Clinical features of LE CRPS EXCEPT?"** → **crepitus**

**"Commonest X-ray finding in knee CRPS?"** → **patellar osteopenia**

**"Healed DRF, now hyperalgesia, allodynia, hyperhidrosis — pathophysiology?"** → **aberrant inflammatory and vasomotor response**

**"Post-Kidner, allodynia, hyperalgesia, oedema of medial foot?"** → **RSD**

**"All common in CRPS EXCEPT?"** → **↓ perfusion to fingertips**

**"Fibromyalgia patient with DRF — at ↑ risk of?"** → **hyperalgesia, allodynia, oedema (CRPS)**

**"CRPS type I vs II differs by?"** → **no identifiable peripheral nerve involvement**

**"Whole-leg pain, shiny cool cyanotic skin after tibia ORIF — diagnosis + best test?"** → **CRPS type I; history and examination**\
✗ Wrong options: type II bone scan · EMG

**"Wrist sprain, allodynia, sweating, relief with sympathetic block?"** → **CRPS type I** (type II doesn't respond)

**"Vitamin C after DRF ORIF prevents?"** → **pain out of proportion + ↑ sweating (CRPS)**

**"Adjuvant improving outcome after DRF ORIF?"** → **oral vitamin C**

**"Level I evidence for vitamin C reducing CRPS in?"** → **distal radius fractures**

**"Vitamin C after F&A surgery ↓?"** → **CRPS type I**

**"Protocol shown to prevent CRPS?"** → **vitamin C 500 mg × 50 days**

**"NOT used in CRPS treatment (TO)?"** → **pramipexole**

**"Graded motor imagery sequence (TO)?"** → **laterality → explicit imagery → mirror therapy**

**"Focal knee CRPS after ACL, failed blocks — neuromodulation (TO)?"** → **DRG stimulation**

**"Post-TKA burning pain, oedema, ↑ hair, marrow oedema — first-line drug (TO)?"** → **IV bisphosphonate (neridronate)**

**"Positive effect in early CRPS?"** → **gentle physiotherapy**\
✗ Wrong options: casting, plyometrics

**"Post-TKA burning, sweating, purple leg, aspirate negative?"** → **α-blockers + PT + tactile discrimination + GMI**

**"Failed splinting, contrast baths, TENS — second line?"** → **surgical sympathectomy**

**"Early CRPS after ankle sprain — initial step?"** → **immediate PT, desensitisation + AROM, multidisciplinary follow-up**

**"Crush foot CRPS — risk factors EXCEPT?"** → **male gender**

**"Painful clenched hand, depression, normal studies?"** → **clenched fist syndrome**
