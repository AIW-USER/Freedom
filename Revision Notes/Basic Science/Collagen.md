# Collagen

*Basic Science · 26 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Overview

**Most abundant protein in humans** — ~25% of body protein · >25 types · made by **fibroblasts** · synthesis **inside and outside** the cell

**Structure** — **triple helix: two α1 + one α2** chains · repeats **Gly-X-hydroxyproline**, **Gly-Pro-X** (glycine every 3rd residue)

**Type I genes** — **COL1A1** (pro-α1) + **COL1A2** (pro-α2)

### Assembly

```
Collagen synthesis
├─ Intracellular → α-CHAINS → PROCOLLAGEN (2 α1 + 1 α2, triple helix)
└─ Extracellular → TROPOCOLLAGEN (enzymatic processing after Golgi)
       └─ Lysine/hydroxylysine aldehyde cross-links → FIBRIL → FIBRE
```

### Collagen types by tissue

| Type | Tissue | Disease |
|---|---|---|
| **I** (>90% of body collagen) | **Bone, tendon, ligament, meniscus, annulus fibrosus, skin, healed cartilage/fibrocartilage, scar, nerve** (tensile strength), deep calcified cartilage | **Osteogenesis imperfecta**, Ehlers–Danlos |
| **II** (90–95% of cartilage collagen) | **Articular/hyaline cartilage**, **nucleus pulposus**, **elastic cartilage** · very long half-life | — |
| **III** | **Skin, blood vessels** · **early tendon/ligament healing** (inflammatory + early regenerative) | **Ehlers–Danlos (vascular, type IV)**, **Dupuytren's** |
| **IV** | **Basement membrane** | Goodpasture, Alport |
| V, VI, IX | Small amounts in articular cartilage · **VI only pericellular** (lacunae, mechanotransduction) · V, VI in disc | — |
| **IX** | Cartilage | Deletion → **OA in women**, knockout mice · **COL9A1–3 → MED** |
| VII, VIII | Epithelial basement membrane | — |
| **X** | **Deep calcified cartilage** · only **hypertrophic chondrocytes** in endochondral ossification (physis, callus, HO) · ↑ **early OA** | **Schmid metaphyseal chondrodysplasia** |
| **XI** | **Adhesive holding cartilage collagen lattice together** | — |
| XII | Annulus only | — |

**Disc** — **annulus = type I** · **nucleus = type II** · type VI both

### Bone collagen

**Bone weight** — inorganic **60–70%** · water **5–8%** · organic remainder · collagen = 90% of organic = **20–25% of bone weight** · collagen gives **flexibility**

### Multiple epiphyseal dysplasia

**Genes** — **COMP (70%)** · COL9A1, COL9A2, COL9A3

**Features** — fragmented ossific centres · **coxa vara** · **genu valgum** · short stubby metacarpals

## 2 Mechanism

### Tendon & ligament healing

**Inflammatory + early regenerative phase** — ✓ **type III** predominates → **type I** in late regenerative phase

## How it's asked

**"Type I collagen in peripheral nerve provides?"** → **tensile strength**\
✗ Wrong options: conduction velocity/strength · compression

**"Collagen first produced in inflammatory phase of tendon/ligament healing?"** → **type III**

**"Major cartilage collagen, 90–95%?"** → **type II**

**"Collagen % of bone weight?"** → **20–25%**

**"Predominant collagen of nucleus pulposus?"** → **type II** (annulus — type I)

**"Type II most abundant in all EXCEPT?"** → **blood vessels** (type III)

**"Collagen of hypertrophic chondrocytes, ↑ in early OA, Schmid?"** → **type X**
