# Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS)

*Basic Science · 3 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Pathology

**Definition** — **Lou Gehrig's disease** · progressive motor neuron disorder · **degeneration of anterior horn cells** · commonest degenerative disease of the motor neuron system

**LMN component** — loss of motor neurons in **anterior horns** + motor cranial nerve nuclei

**UMN component** — loss of myelinated fibres in **corticospinal + corticobulbar** tracts

**Hallmark** — **abnormal motor conduction, normal sensory conduction**

### Genes & lab markers

**Familial** — ~**5%** · some map to **superoxide dismutase** gene on **chromosome 21** · cause otherwise unknown

**Labs** — **no lab test confirms ALS** · SPEP/immunoelectrophoresis only exclude differentials

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **painless weakness** of one limb → spreads · fasciculations · cramps · dysarthria/dysphagia · ↓ head control · breathing difficulty · urinary frequency/incontinence (late) · **sensation normal**

**Neck ptosis** — neck drop from neck-extensor weakness

| UMN signs | LMN signs |
|---|---|
| Spasticity | Atrophy |
| **Hyperreflexia** | Weakness |
| **(+) Hoffmann** | **Fasciculations** |
| **(+) Babinski** | Clumsiness |
| Spastic dysarthria | ↓ reflexes, ↓ tone |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnostic criteria

**Clinical** — **UMN + LMN signs in the same limb** · no pain, no sensory symptoms · ± cranial-nerve findings → highly indicative

**Period of observation** — documents progressive loss of UMN + LMN function

**Often misdiagnosed** — cervical myelopathy / radiculopathy

### Electrodiagnostics — definitive test

**Definitive diagnosis** — ✓ **electrodiagnostic studies (NCS + EMG)** · also exclude peripheral mimics

| EMG/NCS finding in ALS | |
|---|---|
| Motor | **↓ CMAP amplitude** widespread · slowed motor CV |
| Denervation | **Fibrillations + positive sharp waves** + ↓ recruitment in **≥3 extremities** |
| Spontaneous | Fibrillation + **fasciculation** potentials |
| Reinnervation | Present (**denervation + reinnervation**) |
| Sensory | **Normal SNAPs** |

**Not definitive** — genetic testing (only some familial types) · MRI brain/cord (rules out structural mimics) · muscle biopsy (not required) · SPEP

## 5 Management

**No cure** — supportive · maintain function + comfort

**Riluzole** — blocks **tetrodotoxin-sensitive Na⁺ channels** in damaged neurons · delays ventilator dependence · prolongs life **2–3 months**

## X vs Y

| | **ALS** | Peripheral compressive neuropathy (CTS/cubital) |
|---|---|---|
| UMN signs (Hoffmann, Babinski, hyperreflexia) | **Present** | Absent |
| Sensory symptoms | **None** | Present |
| SNAPs | **Normal** | Abnormal |
| Distribution | Spreads, multiple limbs | Single nerve territory |

## How it's asked

**"Most useful study for definitive diagnosis of ALS?"** → **electrodiagnostic studies**\
✗ Wrong options: genetic testing · MRI · muscle biopsy · SPEP

**"Hallmark EMG/NCS pattern?"** → **abnormal motor, normal sensory conduction** · denervation + reinnervation

**"Hand weakness + wasting + hyperreflexia/Hoffmann, normal sensation?"** → **ALS**\
✗ Wrong options: carpal/cubital tunnel

**"Riluzole mechanism?"** → **blocks tetrodotoxin-sensitive sodium channels** · +2–3 months survival
