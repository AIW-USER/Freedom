# Thromboembolism (PE & DVT) Prophylaxis

*Basic Science · 107 / 108*

## 1 Basics

**VTE** — DVT + PE · US incidence: DVT **200,000/yr**, PE **50,000/yr**

### Pathophysiology (Virchow triad, thromboplastin, fibrin, embolisation)

**Virchow's triad** — **venous stasis** · **hypercoagulability** · **endothelial (intimal) injury** \
✗ Wrong options: thrombocytopenia · platelet dysfunction

**Clot formation** — stasis → fibrin formation → clot retraction → propagation · DVT starts in **venous valve cusps**

**Surgical trigger** — ✓ **thromboplastin (tissue factor, factor III)** released during dissection → **extrinsic pathway** (VII → X → prothrombin → thrombin → fibrinogen → fibrin)

**Thrombus** — **fibrin + RBCs**, relatively **few platelets** (platelets key in **arterial** thrombi) · **fibrin = end product of coagulation cascade** (thrombin cleaves fibrinogen) · collagen starts intrinsic arm · fibrinolytic system breaks down fibrin

![Coagulation cascade](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9056-coagulation-cascade-aaos-moved.jpg)

**Cement pressurisation of femoral canal** — ✓ **greatest V/Q mismatch risk in THA** (MMA + fat emboli)\
✗ Wrong options: reaming, modular stem, SCD, posterior approach

### Thrombophilias (factor V Leiden, protein C/S)

| Inherited | Note |
|---|---|
| **MTHFR C677T/TT** | ✓ **Highest risk** (✗ Wrong options: "no impact") |
| **Factor V Leiden** | Gln-for-Arg substitution at protein C cleavage site → **factor Va resists inactivation by activated protein C** → ↑ thrombin · >50% get DVT with one extra risk (fracture, TJA) · (✗ Wrong options: platelet / endothelial / hepatic disorder · mandatory Greenfield filter) |
| **Antithrombin III deficiency** | ATIII normally inhibits thrombin + Xa |
| **Protein C / S deficiency** | Protein S = cofactor to activated protein C; inactivates Va + VIIIa · ✓ **deficiency → VTE** (✗ Wrong options: fibrinogen, VII, VIII, prothrombin deficiency) |
| Activated protein C resistance · ↑ factor VIII · hyperhomocysteinaemia · **prothrombin G20210A** | — |

| ✗ Wrong option | Actually |
|---|---|
| Familial dysfibrinogenaemia | Defective fibrin clot |
| Plasminogen deficiency | Can't lyse clots |
| Glanzmann thrombasthenia | Platelet defect → bleeding |
| von Willebrand disease | Bleeding disorder |
| Antiphospholipid syndrome | Autoimmune, antibodies to protein C/S → clotting |

### Risk factors (TO)

**Acquired** — **malignancy** (up to 20% of new VTE) · **hormones**: recombinant **EPO** (✓ thrombotic event before spinal fusion), **HRT**, OCP, late pregnancy · antiphospholipid (lupus anticoagulant, anticardiolipin) · prior VTE · **obesity** · CHF · MI · varicose veins · smoking · ↑ viscosity · thoracic outlet (UE DVT) · **type of surgery (TJA)** · **age** · family history\
✗ Wrong options: **hypercholesterolaemia**\
✗ Wrong options: protective

**Anaesthesia** — ✓ **general > spinal/epidural** (conversion spinal → GA ↑ risk) · AAOS: neuraxial anaesthesia not shown to ↓ VTE (↓ blood loss)

**Warfarin paradox** — rapid INR rise on unopposed warfarin → early **protein C fall** (t½ 6–8 h) before factor II (48–120 h) → transient hypercoagulability

**TXA** — ✓ **no ↑ DVT** (oral or IV)

**Paediatric** — rising (obesity, OCP, smoking) · ✓ **biggest risk = prior VTE** (+ thrombophilia, strong family history)\
✗ Wrong options: nephrotic, obesity, OCP, SCI (lesser)

**Caprini index** — ✓ **DVT risk assessment in trauma** · low/moderate/high/highest · **major LE fracture = highest** · **score ≥3 → prophylaxis** **(TO)**

## 3 Presentation

**DVT** — calf pain · palpable cord · pitting swelling · **50% with classic signs have no DVT** · **50% venogram-positive have normal exam** · ~20% clinically silent after TJA

**PE** — most **asymptomatic** · pleuritic pain · dyspnoea · tachypnoea · saddle embolus → death · post-vertebroplasty PE may be delayed (PMMA, fat, marrow)

**Timing** — most symptomatic VTE **after discharge, 2–6 wk** · TKA: ↑ DVT but ↓ symptomatic PE vs THA · no prophylaxis TKA → symptomatic PE ~8% · DVT ~60% without chemoprophylaxis\
✗ Wrong options: within 2 wk

**Classification** — acute <28 days · chronic >28 days · recurrent

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**DVT** — **duplex compression US** ("non-compressible vein", ~95% sens/spec) = **gold standard** (Orthobullets) · ✓ **venography = gold standard** (TO) — read the options · US best **non-invasive** test (97% proximal, ~75% calf) · symptomatic calf pain 2 wk post-TKA → ✓ **venous US** next **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · CTA · aspiration

**Screening tests (TO)** — **D-dimer**: sensitive, not specific (inflammation, malignancy, post-op) — rules out · **digital photoplethysmography** ~100% sensitive **(TO)**

**Routine post-op duplex screening** — ✓ **strong AAOS recommendation against** in asymptomatic elective arthroplasty, even with prior DVT / estrogen (cost ~$35,000 per extra DVT)

![Duplex US — non-compressible vein](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9056-dvt-us.jpg)

**PE** — ✓ **CT pulmonary angiography = gold standard** · **V/Q scan** if **contrast allergy** · CXR usually normal early (oligaemia, prominent hilum) → late wedge/plate atelectasis

![CTPA — saddle embolus](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9056-ctpa-showing-saddle-embolus.jpg)

### Diagnostic criteria — PE

| Finding | PE |
|---|---|
| **PaO₂ < 80 mmHg** | ✓ |
| **PaCO₂ < 35 mmHg** (hypocapnia) | ✓ · (✗ Wrong options: **PaCO₂ > 35**)|
| **A–a gradient > 20 mmHg** | ✓ |
| Tachypnoea with SpO₂ 99% | ✓ (pulse oximetry unreliable — hyperventilation) |
| Tachycardia + new **RBBB** | ✓ |
| **ECG** | **Sinus tachycardia (commonest**, 8–69%) · RAD · RBBB · **S1Q3T3 (McGinn-White)** 10–20% |
| Acid–base | **Respiratory alkalosis** (✗ Wrong options: hyperchloraemic metabolic acidosis) |

**Wells criteria** — clinical probability for DVT and PE **(TO)**

## 5 Management

### Prophylaxis guidelines (AAOS, ACCP, mechanical, combined) (TO)

**Risk stratification (ACCP 2008)** — ✓ **THA, TKA, hip fracture, major trauma, SCI = HIGH risk** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: "THR moderate risk"

**AAOS approach** — ✓ **pre-op assessment of VTE + bleeding risk** · weigh **bleeding vs PE**

| AAOS elevated risk of **major bleeding** | AAOS elevated risk of **PE** |
|---|---|
| **Bleeding disorder** (haemophilia, vWD) · **recent GI bleed** · **recent haemorrhagic stroke** · active liver disease | **Hypercoagulable state** (e.g. ✓ **protein C deficiency**) · **previous documented PE** |

### Operative criteria — who gets prophylaxis

| Procedure | Prophylaxis |
|---|---|
| **THA / TKA** | ✓ **All patients** — mechanical + pharmacological (moderate strength) · no optimal agent specified |
| Upper extremity · arthroscopy · isolated fracture knee and below (standard risk) | Not recommended |
| Foot & ankle | Not shown to ↓ VTE (20,043 patients) |
| **Spine** | No consensus · early mobilisation + IPC · chemo for long surgery, multilevel thoracolumbar, anterior TL · risk **epidural haematoma** |
| Shoulder arthroplasty | Mobilisation, mechanical, regional · LMWH only if ↑ risk |
| **Trauma** | ✓ **Combined mechanical + chemical → ↓ DVT AND PE** · standard: IPC + enoxaparin |
| **Liver failure / bleeding risk (INR 1.36)** | ✓ **Mechanical only** (✗ Wrong options: warfarin · none) |
| Contraindication to anticoagulation | IVC filter considered (✗ Wrong options: routine) |

**AAOS guideline points** — ✓ **mechanical compressive devices post-op** · aspirin post-op · ✓ **stop antiplatelets pre-op** · (✗ Wrong options: stockings alone sufficient · routine duplex · specific INR (2011)) · regional anaesthesia suggested · **early mobilisation (consensus)** · AAOS/ASH 2019: IPC > stockings, no IVC filter, aspirin or anticoagulant, DOAC > LMWH (conditional)\
✗ Wrong options: start aspirin 1 wk pre-op

**Shown to ↓ VTE after TKA** — early mobilisation · aspirin · coumadin · pneumatic boots \
✗ Wrong options: **neuraxial anaesthesia**

**IPC mechanism** — ✓ **↑ venous blood flow/return** · ↑ endothelial **fibrinolysis** · ↓ venous **compliance** + stasis\
✗ Wrong options: ↓ bleeding time · endothelial injury

**2012 ACCP convergence with AAOS** — ✓ **↑ aspirin monotherapy prescribing** · ACCP agents ≥ **10–14 days**: ASA, LMWH, fondaparinux, apixaban, dabigatran, rivaroxaban, LDUH, VKA · dual mechanical + chemo inpatient

**Compliance** — almost all receive prophylaxis but often not ACCP-compliant · LMWH compliant more than warfarin (THA 63 vs 33%) · VTE ↑ costs in- and post-discharge · most acute peri-op pulmonary events = **fat embolism / cardiac**

| Agent (TO) | Dose / start | Duration | Notes / reversal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aspirin** | 81 mg BID (favoured) or 160–325 mg · within 12–24 h | TKA 10–14 d · THA 28–35 d | AAOS 2007 + ACCP 2012 ✓ · **SIGN: not as sole agent** · platelets (no antidote) |
| **LMWH (enoxaparin)** | 30 mg BID (US) / 40 mg OD · 12–24 h | 10–14 d · THA up to 35 d | "Gold standard" (TO) · no aPTT monitoring · ↓ HIT, ↓ osteoporosis vs UFH |
| UFH | — | — | Monitor **aPTT** · replaced by LMWH |
| **Warfarin (VKA)** | INR **2–3, target 2.5** (ACCP) · **AAOS 2007: INR ≤ 2** | Up to 35 d | Vit K, PCC, FFP |
| **Fondaparinux** | **2.5 mg OD** | — | Anti-Xa · ✓ **no routine monitoring** |
| **Rivaroxaban** | **10 mg OD** · 6–10 h (TO) / 12–24 h (TO EPCAT table) | THA 35 d · TKA 14 d | Oral Xa · not for **hip fracture** · RECORD 1–4 · **andexanet alfa** · 20 mg = treatment dose |
| **Apixaban** | **2.5 mg BID** · 12–24 h | THA 35 d (32–38) · TKA 12 d (10–14) | Oral Xa · not for hip fracture · ADVANCE · **least bleeding** · **andexanet alfa** (✗ Wrong options: protamine) |
| **Dabigatran** | **220 mg OD** (half dose 1–4 h post-op; 110 mg if >75 or renal) | THA 28–35 d · TKA 10 d | **Direct thrombin (IIa) inhibitor** · RE-MODEL, RE-NOVATE · ✓ **idarucizumab** |

**Step-down (EPCAT II) (TO)** — ✓ **rivaroxaban 10 mg × 5 days → aspirin 81 mg** for 30 more days (THA) / 9 days (TKA) · non-inferior, less bleeding **(TO)**

**Switching rules (TO)** — do not **overlap LMWH + DOAC** · give first DOAC dose **when next LMWH dose due** (24 h after 40 mg OD; 12 h after 30 mg BID) · LMWH → aspirin day 4–6 · do not step up aspirin → rivaroxaban 20 mg · CrCl <30 → adjust/avoid · epidural: hold LMWH 12 h before catheter removal, next dose ≥4 h after **(TO)**

```
Elective THA / TKA
├─ All → MECHANICAL (IPC) + EARLY MOBILISATION
├─ Standard risk → ASPIRIN or ANTICOAGULANT (individualised)
├─ ↑ PE risk (prior PE, hypercoagulable) → PHARMACOLOGICAL ± MECHANICAL
└─ ↑ Bleeding risk (bleeding disorder, liver disease, recent GI bleed / haemorrhagic stroke) → MECHANICAL ONLY
       └─ anticoagulation contraindicated + high PE risk → CONSIDER IVC FILTER
```

### Treatment of VTE

**Isolated calf thrombus <5 cm** — rarely needs treatment → **serial US**

**Anticoagulation** — ~**3 months after DVT** · ~**12 months after PE**

**Other** — **early mobilisation** (dislodgement risk < further clot risk) · graduated compression hose → may prevent post-thrombotic syndrome · thrombolysis / thrombectomy controversial

**Suspected intra-op PE in trauma** — damage control: convert definitive (IMN) to provisional (ex-fix), get helical CT

## 6 Complications

**DVT** — PE · **post-thrombotic syndrome 43% at 2 yr** (30% mild, 10% moderate, 3% severe) · **recurrence 25%**

**PE** — sudden death · obstructive shock · PEA · arrhythmias · pulmonary hypertension · cor pulmonale · hypoxaemia · R→L shunt

**Mortality** — **6%** DVT and **12%** PE within 1 month · ~10% of PE die in first hour, 30% later from recurrence

**Bleeding on dabigatran** — ✓ **idarucizumab** **(TO)**
