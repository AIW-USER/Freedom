# Biceps femoris short head

*Basic Science · 11 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy & innervation

| | Biceps femoris short head |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Lateral lip of **linea aspera**, lateral supracondylar ridge, lateral intermuscular septum |
| **Insertion** | **Fibular head** (mainly) · also LCL + lateral tibial condyle |
| **Action** | Flexes knee · externally rotates tibia (long head also extends hip) |
| **Innervation** | **Common peroneal (peroneal division of sciatic) — L5, S1, S2** |
| **Arterial supply** | Perforators of profunda femoris, inferior gluteal, superior muscular branches of popliteal |

## X vs Y

| | Long head | **Short head** |
|---|---|---|
| Nerve | **Tibial division** of sciatic (L5–S3) | **Peroneal division** of sciatic (L5–S2) |
| Crosses hip | Yes (extends hip) | No |

**Only hamstring on peroneal division** — short head of biceps femoris

## How it's asked

**"Nerve to short head of biceps femoris?"** → **peroneal division of sciatic**\
✗ Wrong options: tibial division (long head) · femoral · superior gluteal · saphenous
