# Lumbosacral Plexus

*Basic Science · 58 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Nerve at risk — iliosacral screws

**Danger** — screw anterior/superior on the **sacral ala** (above the alar slope / iliac cortical density on lateral view) → ✓ **L5 nerve root**

**L5 deficit** — ✓ **loss of great-toe extension (EHL)** · also tibialis anterior, tibialis posterior, EDL, hamstrings, gluteus medius/maximus

| Fluoroscopic view | Checks |
|---|---|
| **Lateral** | Below **iliac cortical density / alar slope** → protects L5 |
| **Outlet** | **Above S1 foramen** |
| **Inlet** | Into **anterior** sacral body |

### Lateral femoral cutaneous nerve

**Course** — lumbar plexus → **surface of iliacus** → ✓ **under the inguinal ligament** (near ASIS) → pierces fascia lata · variable path

**Traps** — ✗ Wrong options: deep to psoas · medial to femoral vein · adjacent to femoral nerve · deep to iliopectineal fascia

## How it's asked

**"Iliosacral screw too anterior-superior on S1 ala — deficit?"** → **loss of great-toe extension (L5)**

**"Most common location of LFCN?"** → **under the inguinal ligament**
