# Bone Remodeling

*Basic Science · 19 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Remodelling laws & cortical vs cancellous turnover

| Law | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Wolff's law** | Bone **remodels/adapts to the loads (mechanical stress)** placed on it · ↑ load → stronger · ↓ load → osteopenia · osteocytes sense load |
| **Davis's law** | **Soft tissues** heal according to how they are mechanically stressed **(TO)** |
| **Hueter–Volkmann** | **Compression on physis slows growth**, tension/distraction accelerates (immature bone) |
| **Delpech's law** | **Tension on physis speeds growth** **(TO)** |
| **Piezoelectric** | **Compression side electronegative → osteoblasts (formation)** · tension side electropositive → osteoclasts |
| Maxwell's law | **Not a physeal law** **(TO)** |

**Law governing remodelling (TO)** — ✓ **both Wolff's and Davis's** (bone and soft tissue)

### Remodelling mechanism

**Basic multicellular unit (BMU, Frost)** — osteoclasts + osteoblasts + osteocytes in the remodelling cavity

| | **Cortical bone** | **Cancellous bone** |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | **Osteoclastic tunnelling — cutting cone** (head = osteoclasts → capillaries → osteoblasts fill = closing cone → cement line) | **Osteoclastic resorption on trabecular surface** + osteoblastic lamellae |
| Cycle (TO) | **Shorter — median ~120 days** | ~**200 days** |

![Cutting cone — osteoclasts lead, osteoblasts follow](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9008-cutting-cone-moved.jpg)

**Turnover extremes (TO)** — **~100 days** in thyrotoxicosis, 1° hyperparathyroidism · **>1,000 days** in myxoedema and **after bisphosphonates** (slower, not "faster")

### Sclerostin & Wnt pathway

**Sclerostin** — glycoprotein from **osteocytes** · ✓ **inhibits osteoblastogenesis → ↓ bone formation**

**Sclerostin + Dkk-1** — block Wnt binding to **LRP5/6** → ✓ inhibit **Wnt/β-catenin** pathway

**Wnt on** — β-catenin not phosphorylated → accumulates → nucleus → osteoblast genes · also ↑ **OPG**

**Knockout / inhibition of sclerostin or Dkk-1** — ↑ bone mass

### Age-related changes

**Ageing cortex** — **↓ cortical thickness/area**, **↑ medullary canal** volume (bone volume constant) → ↑ fracture risk

## 2 Mechanism

### Calcium, vitamin D & calcitonin

**Sunlight** — UV converts **7-dehydrocholesterol → cholecalciferol (D3)** in skin → liver 25-OH → kidney 1,25-(OH)₂D

**↓ Sun exposure** — ✓ **impaired GI calcium absorption** (no 1,25-D) · liver/kidney hydroxylation intact · PTH **↑**\
✗ Wrong options: ↓

**Phosphate ↓ urinary Ca** — ✓ **complexes with Ca in the intestine** (less absorbed) · also ↑ PTH, complexes in bone/soft tissue (↓ filtered load)

**Calcitonin** — ✓ **interferes with osteoclast maturation** (receptor on osteoclasts) · ↓ renal Ca + phosphate reabsorption · chondroprotective (MMP inhibition) · no osteoblast receptor · no major gut Ca effect · used in **Paget's when bisphosphonate-intolerant**

### Anabolic therapy — intermittent PTH

**Direct anabolic** — ✓ **intermittent PTH**\
✗ Wrong options: bisphosphonate, calcitonin (antiresorptive) · corticosteroid · TNF-α inhibitor

**Continuous ↑ PTH** — bone resorption

**TNF-α** — ↓ osteoblasts, ↑ osteoclastic resorption

**Excess cortisol** — ↓ gut Ca absorption, ↑ renal Ca loss, ↓ matrix formation, secondary hyperparathyroidism

### Bone healing

**Rigid compression plating (absolute stability)** — ✓ **primary healing by Haversian remodelling** (osteonal cutting cones cross fracture) · no visible callus

**Relative stability (IM nail)** — **secondary healing, callus**, endochondral ossification

## 5 Management

### Hueter–Volkmann principle

**Clinical uses** — **tension-band (8-) plate guided growth** · scoliosis progression (concave side compressed → slower growth) · **Scheuermann extension brace** (anterior bodies in tension → growth corrects wedging)

## X vs Y

| | Wolff | Hueter–Volkmann |
|---|---|---|
| Governs | **Remodelling** (mature + immature) | **Longitudinal growth** (immature physis) |
| Stimulus | Intermittent ↑ stress → apposition | Sustained compression → growth suppression |

| | Primary healing | Secondary healing |
|---|---|---|
| Stability | Absolute (compression plate) | Relative (nail, cast) |
| Mechanism | **Haversian / cutting cones** | **Callus, endochondral** |

## How it's asked

**"Direct anabolic effect on bone?"** → **intermittent PTH**\
✗ Wrong options: bisphosphonate, calcitonin, corticosteroid, TNF inhibitor

**"Inhibits osteoblastogenesis to ↓ bone formation?"** → **sclerostin**\
✗ Wrong options: RANKL, IL-1, Wnt, OPG

**"Sclerostin + Dkk-1 inhibit?"** → **Wnt/β-catenin**

**"Physeal growth modulation by compression/tension (TO)?"** → **Hueter–Volkmann**

**"Law NOT pertaining to growth plate (TO, FNB 2023)?"** → **Maxwell's law**

**"Scheuermann extension brace corrects wedging by?"** → **Hueter–Volkmann principle**\
✗ Wrong options: Hooke, Kirchhoff, Wolff

**"Tension-band plate relies on?"** → **Hueter–Volkmann**\
✗ Wrong options: cutting cones · Wolff · piezoelectric

**"Phosphate ↓ urinary Ca by?"** → **intestinal Ca–phosphate complex**

**"↓ Sun exposure harms bone by?"** → **↓ GI calcium absorption**

**"Calcitonin mechanism (Paget's)?"** → **interferes with osteoclast maturation**

**"Rigid compression plating — healing type?"** → **primary, Haversian remodelling**

**"25-yr-old vs 85-yr-old femur?"** → **thicker cortex, smaller canal**

**"Law governing remodelling (TO)?"** → **both Wolff's and Davis's**

**"True about remodelling (TO)?"** → **cortical cycle shorter than cancellous**\
✗ Wrong options: cutting cones in cancellous · bisphosphonates speed remodelling
