# Cartilage

*Basic Science · 23 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Types of cartilage

| Type | Location | Collagen | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hyaline (articular)** | Articular surfaces, ribs, nasal septum | **Type II** | ↓ friction, distributes load |
| **Fibrocartilage** | Tendon/ligament–bone junction, **pubic symphysis**, **annulus fibrosus**, menisci | **Type I** (predominantly) | Compressive strength · **repair tissue** after microfracture, drilling, abrasion arthroplasty |
| Fibroelastic | Menisci | — | Fewer PGs + glycoproteins than hyaline |
| **Elastic** | **Auricle, epiglottis, auditory tube** | **Type II** + elastin network | Tolerates repetitive deformation |
| **Epiphyseal (physeal)** | Between epiphysis + metaphysis | — | **Linear growth (endochondral ossification)** · remodelling potential |

**Physeal zones** — reserve → proliferative → hypertrophic → primary spongiosa → secondary spongiosa · ECM holds PGs + GFs (BMP-2)

### Cartilage biology

**Properties** — **avascular, aneural, alymphatic** · nutrients by **diffusion from synovial fluid** · pH **7.4** · metabolism regulated mechanically

**Composition (hyaline)** — water (65–80%) > collagen > PG > non-collagenous protein > cells · **stress-shielding** of solid matrix

**Loading** — physiological = chondroprotective · **underloading** → thinning, softening, ↓ PG → fibrillation, erosion · overloading → damage (**in vitro only**)

### SOX9 & chondrocyte differentiation

**SOX9** — ✓ **"master switch" transcription factor for chondrocytes** · HMG superfamily · binds type II collagen promoter/enhancer · ↑ **type II, IX, XI collagen, aggrecan, link protein** · not a tyrosine kinase receptor

### Matrix components (aggrecan, type II & IX collagen)

**Type II collagen** — ✓ **tensile strength + reinforcement of PG gel** · restrains swelling · superficial-zone collagen bears ~**20%** of load · degradation → fibrillation

**Aggrecan** — negative GAG charge (chondroitin, keratan sulfate) → ↑ osmolality → attracts water → compressive strength

**↓ Aggrecan expression** — ✓ **↓ water content** (and ↓ osmolality, ↓ compressive strength) · no direct AGE/collagen change

**Pericellular integrin anchors** — fibronectin, **type VI collagen**

### Genes & lab markers

**OA linkage in women** — ✓ **type IX collagen** (400-family study · knockout mice → early OA) · also **ADAMTS-5** (aggrecanase)

### PTH effect on cartilage

**PTH** — **chondroprotective** · ✓ **↓ cartilage degradation** · via **Indian hedgehog (IHH)** signalling · synergy with bFGF, IGF

**Traps** — ✗ Wrong options: ↑ MMP-13 (degradation) · ↑ type X collagen (end-stage hypertrophic/OA) · ↓ PG

## X vs Y

|---|---|---|---|
| Collagen | **II** | **I** | **II + elastin** |
| Example | Joint surface | Annulus, symphysis, meniscus, enthesis | Ear, epiglottis |

## How it's asked

**"SOX9 drives differentiation of?"** → **chondrocytes**\
✗ Wrong options: osteoclasts, osteoblasts, fibroblasts

**"SOX9 regulates which tissue (histology)?"** → **cartilage**\
✗ Wrong options: bone, fat, muscle, nerve

**"Strong genetic linkage with OA in women?"** → **type IX collagen**

**"Downregulate aggrecan genes → effect?"** → **↓ water content**

**"Primary biomechanical function of type II collagen?"** → **tensile strength, reinforcing PG matrix**\
✗ Wrong options: osmotic swelling (PGs)

**"PTH effect on cartilage?"** → **↓ degradation (chondroprotective)**
