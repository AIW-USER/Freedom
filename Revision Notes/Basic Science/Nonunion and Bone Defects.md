# Nonunion and Bone Defects

*Basic Science · 67 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Definitions

**Nonunion** — arrest of fracture repair, no further capacity to heal without intervention

**FDA definition (TO)** — ✓ **≥9 months since injury + no visible progressive healing for 3 months**

**Delayed union** — not healed in expected time · **failure to unite by 6 months** (Orthobullets) · **3–6 months** (TO) · varies with site and soft-tissue injury

**Large segmental defect** — functional nonunion

**Retrospective diagnosis** — defined by the **course of healing**, not one X-ray (TO)

**Pseudarthrosis** — **false joint** lined by **fibrocartilage + synovial-like tissue** from chronic motion (TO)

**Delayed union (TO)** — healing progressing but slower than expected · signs of union present but inadequate

**Critical-size defect** — will not heal spontaneously in a lifetime · **> 2.5 cm or > 25% cortical loss** (TO) · subcritical < 2.5 cm heals with graft / fixation

**Robinson guideline** — **> 25 mm circumferential loss → early bone grafting** · < 25 mm → observe (TO)

| OTA bone defect (Tetsworth) | Description (TO) |
|---|---|
| **D1** | Incomplete defect |
| **D2** | Minor / subcritical (**< 2 cm**) |
| **D3** | Segmental / critical (**> 2 cm**) |

### Causes

**Commonest** — **inadequate stabilisation + poor blood supply** · infection · **smoking (biggest modifiable risk)** · location (scaphoid, distal tibia, 5th MT base) · segmental/butterfly (intercalary blood supply)

**Smoking** — nicotine vasoconstriction + **CO binding Hb** · prior smokers also at risk · > NSAIDs, BMI >40, delay >24 h · **reamed nailing ↓ nonunion**\
✗ Wrong options: risk

**Spine fusion in smoker** — ✓ **maintain smoking cessation** best ↓ pseudarthrosis

| Risk category | Examples (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Fracture** | Open (haematoma loss), intra-articular, **precarious supply** (femoral neck, scaphoid, talus), comminution, bone loss |
| **Host** | Age, smoking, diabetes, PVD, **vitamin D deficiency**, malnutrition, immunosuppression, obesity, NSAIDs |
| **Treatment** | Poor reduction / fixation, **periosteal stripping**, distraction, implant loosening, infection |

**Smoking (TO)** — vasoconstriction, hypoxia, free radicals, ↓ angiogenesis · **2.2× nonunion risk** · nicotine itself delays union

**Diabetes (TO)** — **↑ RANKL/OPG** → ↑ osteoclastogenesis · **↓ RUNX2, BMPs** → ↓ osteoblast function

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Classification & biology

| Type | Biology | Mechanics | Appearance | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hypertrophic** | **Adequate** vascularity | **Inadequate stability** (too much motion) | **Abundant callus**, no bridging ("elephant foot") | ✓ **↑ mechanical stability only** (e.g. exchange nail, compression plate) — rarely needs graft |
| **Oligotrophic** | Usually adequate | **Malreduction/distraction** (displacement) · or **overly stiff construct** | Little/no callus | Improved fixation ± biological stimulation |
| **Atrophic** | **Inadequate blood supply** | Inadequate immobilisation (Orthobullets) · excessively stiff fixation (OB) | **No callus, sclerotic/rounded ends** | ✓ **Revision fixation + bone graft** (autologous ICBG gold standard · BMP · DBM, cancellous chips) + healthy soft tissue |
| **Septic** | Infected | — | Variable callus | Staged: debride, beads/VAC, flaps, transport · eradicate infection |
| **Synovial pseudarthrosis** | Sealed canals + fluid-filled pseudomembrane | — | False joint · **bone scan "cold cleft"** | Resect non-viable ends, stable fixation |

**Classification axes (TO)** — epiphyseal / metaphyseal / diaphyseal · septic / aseptic · hypertrophic / oligotrophic / atrophic · pseudarthrosis

**Weber–Cech** — based on **vascularity / viability (strontium-85 uptake)** · hypertrophic = hypervascular "elephant foot" · oligotrophic = moderate, minimal callus (poor reduction / distraction) · **atrophic = avascular, pencil-thin ends** (TO)

**Mnemonic** — **"high callus = motion problem; no callus = biology problem"** (TO)

| Paley (tibia) | Description (TO) |
|---|---|
| **A (< 1 cm bone loss)** | **A1 mobile** (lax) · **A2 stiff** — A2-1 no deformity, A2-2 deformity |
| **B (> 1 cm bone loss)** | **B1 bone defect** · **B2 shortening** · **B3 both** |

**Paley relevance** — stiff ≈ hypertrophic → **compression** · mobile ≈ atrophic → **graft** (TO)

| Umiarov (infected nonunion) | Features (TO) |
|---|---|
| I | Normotrophic, no shortening |
| II | Hypertrophic + shortening |
| III | Atrophic + shortening |
| IV | Atrophic + bone / soft-tissue defect |

| NUSS (Calori) | TO interpretation |
|---|---|
| **0–25** | Mechanical problem → improve fixation |
| 26–50 | Biology > mechanics → grafts / stimulators |
| 51–75 | Complex → transport, Masquelet, RIA |
| **> 75** | Poor host → **salvage** (arthrodesis, megaprosthesis, amputation) |

**NUSS bands** — TO labels as above (TO — verify: Calori bands are 0–25 standard care · 26–50 specialist · 51–75 specialist + complex · 76–100 salvage / amputation)

**Biopsy of aseptic nonunion after ex-fix** — ✓ **bland cartilage callus** (failed endochondral healing)\
✗ Wrong options: PMNs (infection) · Haversian/lamellar bone (healed)

**Locked plate distal femur — short working length, high screw density** — ✓ **high stiffness → low strain → oligotrophic nonunion → fatigue failure** · revised with long working length, healed **without graft**

### Work-up

**History** — mechanism, treatments, metabolic/nutritional/immune status, **NSAIDs, nicotine** · pain on axial loading

**Exam** — neurovascular, soft tissue, nonunion mobility, deformity

**Labs** — ✓ **CBC, ESR, CRP** (infection) · total protein, albumin · **vitamin D (deficient in 60–70%)**, TSH, PTH

**Most accurate test for septic nonunion** — ✓ **CRP** (rises in 6 h, peaks 2–3 days, normalises 5–21 days with treatment)\
✗ Wrong options: IL-1, IL-6, WBC, ESR

**First step when nonunion + risk of infection (open fracture, erythema)** — ✓ **labs: vitamin D, TSH, inflammatory markers** before revision

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — cornerstone · **4 views** · full-length standing films if LLD

**CT** — if union uncertain (hardware artefact)

**"GAP TORSO" (TO)** — **G**ap · **A**trophy · **P**seudarthrosis · **T**apered ends · **O**bliterated canal · **R**ounded edges · **S**clerosis · **O**bliquity / deformity

**Indirect signs (TO)** — broken / loose implant · **persistent fracture line on 3 of 4 cortices** · CT more accurate but **high false-positive** for nonunion

| RUST (per cortex, 4 cortices) | Score (TO) |
|---|---|
| No callus | 1 |
| Callus present | 2 |
| **Bridging callus** | 3 |
| Remodelled | 4 |

**RUST union** — **≥ 11 = united** (TO — verify: most series use ≥ 10)

## 5 Management

```
Nonunion (rule out infection: CRP, ESR)
├─ Hypertrophic → ↑ STABILITY (exchange nail / compression plate)
├─ Oligotrophic → IMPROVE FIXATION ± BIOLOGY
├─ Atrophic → REVISION FIXATION + AUTOGRAFT + SOFT-TISSUE ENVELOPE
└─ Septic → STAGED DEBRIDEMENT, BEADS, FLAP, TRANSPORT
```

**Atrophic ulna nonunion 6 mo after BBFA ORIF, normal CRP/ESR** — ✓ **revision ORIF with autograft**\
✗ Wrong options: observe, cast, IM nail, revision without graft

**Atrophic radius nonunion with gap** — ✓ **supportive soft-tissue envelope** essential · plating + cancellous graft treats defects **up to 6 cm** · **3.5 mm plates** adequate · poor envelope → vascularised graft + soft tissue

**Atrophic tibial nonunion 9 mo, normal labs** — cause ✓ **lack of biology** → exchange nail + graft

**Non-operative** — brace · **bone stimulators**\
✗ Wrong options: synovial pseudarthrosis · mobile nonunion · gap >1 cm

**Principle (TO)** — identify what is missing: **mechanics → better fixation** (nail, plate, ex-fix) · **biology → graft / substitute** · "when mechanics are right, biology follows"

**Non-operative (TO)** — well-aligned hypertrophic → **PTB cast**, WB · unfit → electrical stimulation, **LIPUS (30 mW/cm², 1.5 MHz) — ↑ endochondral ossification, not cell proliferation** · PEMF (delayed union) · combined magnetic field (long bones, scaphoid) · stop smoking, correct vit D

### Nailing — exchange & dynamisation (TO)

| Option | Indication / technique (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Exchange nailing** | Diaphyseal (femur / tibia), undersized nail, rotational instability · **over-ream 1–2 mm**, nail **\~2 mm larger**, static lock · reaming = **vascular stimulation + local autograft** · early WB |
| **Dynamisation** | Gap from resorption / distraction · at **3–6 months** · remove screws from the **longer fragment** end · not beyond 6 months |
| **Augmentation plate** | Nail left in + plate → resists nail fatigue, complex nonunion |
| **Plating** | Preferred for **metaphyseal** nonunion · absolute stability · cannot correct shortening |

**Ilizarov** — gold standard for **"DDD": defect, deformity, decreased length** · immediate WB, multiplanar · mono- / bi- / trifocal transport · LRS for transport (TO)

| Procedure | Indication (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Fibular osteotomy** | Tibial nonunion → allows compression |
| **Valgus osteotomy** (femoral neck) | Converts **shear → compression** |
| **Arthroplasty** | Elderly + periarticular nonunion |
| **Girdlestone / excision** | Infected femoral neck / radial head |
| Amputation | Non-reconstructable + neurovascular compromise |

| Articular nonunion | Treatment (TO) |
|---|---|
| Arthritis / instability, elderly | **Arthroplasty** |
| Arthritis, young | **Arthrodesis** / excision arthroplasty |
| No arthritis | Fixation, debridement |

### Gap nonunion & grafting techniques (TO)

| Technique | Key facts (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Masquelet (induced membrane)** | Stage 1: debride + **antibiotic cement spacer** · stage 2 at **6–8 wk**: remove cement, **cancellous graft** in membrane · spacer blocks fibrous ingrowth + local antibiotics |
| **Judet decortication (1972)** | Subperiosteal **osteoperiosteal chips** without muscle detachment + cancellous graft |
| **Papineau** | **Open cancellous grafting**: serial debridement → matchstick graft left open → closure / flap · **infected tibial nonunion** |
| **Phemister onlay** | Cancellous graft along nonunion without disturbing callus · fibrous nonunion, minimal motion |
| **Boyd dual onlay** | Grafts on both cortices |
| **Nicoll** | Cancellous insert (block) when other bone united |
| **Gill** | Sliding cortical graft |
| **Harmon posterolateral** | Graft on **interosseous membrane → tibiofibular synostosis** · scarred anterior skin |
| **Huntington** | **Fibular tibialisation** (two-stage) · infected / paediatric gap tibial nonunion |
| Vascularised / non-vascularised fibula | Segmental defects |

| Infected nonunion | Approach (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Conventional (two-stage)** | Radical debridement → soft-tissue cover → bone graft |
| **Active (single / modified)** | Simultaneous debridement + stabilisation ± graft |

**Exam answer** — write **conventional > active** (TO)

| Emerging therapy | Target (TO) |
|---|---|
| **BMP-2 / BMP-7** | Osteoinduction · FDA: **open tibia** (BMP-2), recalcitrant nonunion (BMP-7) |
| WNT / sclerostin inhibitors | Osteoblast survival |
| HIF-1α, GH | Angiogenesis |
| PRP, stem cells, gene therapy | Cellular / growth-factor delivery |

### Electrical stimulation

**Induction coupling** — ↑ **BMP-7, BMP-2, TGF-β1**, ↑ **osteoblast proliferation** · **not ↓ osteoclast differentiation**

| Mode | Effect |
|---|---|
| Direct current | ↓ O₂, ↑ pH → ↓ osteoclasts, ↑ osteoblasts |
| Capacitive coupling (AC) | cAMP, collagen, cartilage calcification |
| PEMF | Fibrocartilage calcification |
| Combined magnetic | — |

## How it's asked

**"Ulna fracture 12 mo, pain, abundant callus on X-ray (TO)?"** → **hypertrophic nonunion**

**"Nonunion after ex-fix, cultures negative — histology?"** → **bland cartilage callus**

**"Least likely to need added biology?"** → **abundant callus without bridging (hypertrophic)**

**"Tibial nail 9 mo, no bone formation, normal labs — cause?"** → **lack of biology (atrophic)**

**"Humerus ORIF, little callus, broken plate, normal labs?"** → **atrophic**

**"Distal femur locked plate nonunion healed with revision, no graft — initial problem?"** → **high stiffness**

**"Smoker having spine fusion — ↓ pseudarthrosis by?"** → **smoking cessation**

**"Obese smoker, NSAIDs, delayed nailing — highest nonunion risk?"** → **smoking**

**"Open subtroch femur, 9-mo pain, erythema, nonunion — next?"** → **vitamin D, TSH, inflammatory labs**

**"Septic nonunion — most accurate lab?"** → **CRP**

**"Atrophic ulna nonunion, normal CRP/ESR?"** → **revision ORIF with autograft**

**"Atrophic radius nonunion with gap — required besides revision + graft?"** → **supportive soft-tissue envelope**

**"Induction coupling effects EXCEPT?"** → **↓ osteoclast differentiation**

**"Nonunion classification based on vascularity (strontium-85)?"** → **Weber–Cech** (TO)

**"Critical-size bone defect?"** → **> 2.5 cm or > 25% cortical loss** (TO)

**"Two-stage induced-membrane technique?"** → **Masquelet — cement spacer, graft at 6–8 weeks** (TO)

**"Open cancellous grafting for infected tibial nonunion?"** → **Papineau** (TO)

**"Gold standard for defect + deformity + shortening?"** → **Ilizarov** (TO)
