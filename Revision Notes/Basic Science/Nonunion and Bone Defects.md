# Nonunion and Bone Defects

*Basic Science · 67 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Definitions

**Nonunion** — arrest of fracture repair, no further capacity to heal without intervention

**FDA definition (TO)** — ✓ **≥9 months since injury + no visible progressive healing for 3 months**

**Delayed union** — not healed in expected time · **failure to unite by 6 months** (Orthobullets) · **3–6 months** (TO) · varies with site and soft-tissue injury

**Large segmental defect** — functional nonunion

### Causes

**Commonest** — **inadequate stabilisation + poor blood supply** · infection · **smoking (biggest modifiable risk)** · location (scaphoid, distal tibia, 5th MT base) · segmental/butterfly (intercalary blood supply)

**Smoking** — nicotine vasoconstriction + **CO binding Hb** · prior smokers also at risk · > NSAIDs, BMI >40, delay >24 h · **reamed nailing ↓ nonunion**\
✗ Wrong options: risk

**Spine fusion in smoker** — ✓ **maintain smoking cessation** best ↓ pseudarthrosis

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Classification & biology

| Type | Biology | Mechanics | Appearance | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hypertrophic** | **Adequate** vascularity | **Inadequate stability** (too much motion) | **Abundant callus**, no bridging ("elephant foot") | ✓ **↑ mechanical stability only** (e.g. exchange nail, compression plate) — rarely needs graft |
| **Oligotrophic** | Usually adequate | **Malreduction/distraction** (displacement) · or **overly stiff construct** | Little/no callus | Improved fixation ± biological stimulation |
| **Atrophic** | **Inadequate blood supply** | Inadequate immobilisation (Orthobullets) · excessively stiff fixation (OB) | **No callus, sclerotic/rounded ends** | ✓ **Revision fixation + bone graft** (autologous ICBG gold standard · BMP · DBM, cancellous chips) + healthy soft tissue |
| **Septic** | Infected | — | Variable callus | Staged: debride, beads/VAC, flaps, transport · eradicate infection |
| **Synovial pseudarthrosis** | Sealed canals + fluid-filled pseudomembrane | — | False joint · **bone scan "cold cleft"** | Resect non-viable ends, stable fixation |

**Classification axes (TO)** — epiphyseal / metaphyseal / diaphyseal · septic / aseptic · hypertrophic / oligotrophic / atrophic · pseudarthrosis

![Hypertrophic nonunion — abundant callus, no bridging](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9069-hypertrophic-nonunion.jpg)

**Biopsy of aseptic nonunion after ex-fix** — ✓ **bland cartilage callus** (failed endochondral healing)\
✗ Wrong options: PMNs (infection) · Haversian/lamellar bone (healed)

**Locked plate distal femur — short working length, high screw density** — ✓ **high stiffness → low strain → oligotrophic nonunion → fatigue failure** · revised with long working length, healed **without graft**

### Work-up

**History** — mechanism, treatments, metabolic/nutritional/immune status, **NSAIDs, nicotine** · pain on axial loading

**Exam** — neurovascular, soft tissue, nonunion mobility, deformity

**Labs** — ✓ **CBC, ESR, CRP** (infection) · total protein, albumin · **vitamin D (deficient in 60–70%)**, TSH, PTH

**Most accurate test for septic nonunion** — ✓ **CRP** (rises in 6 h, peaks 2–3 days, normalises 5–21 days with treatment)\
✗ Wrong options: IL-1, IL-6, WBC, ESR

**First step when nonunion + risk of infection (open fracture, erythema)** — ✓ **labs: vitamin D, TSH, inflammatory markers** before revision

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — cornerstone · **4 views** · full-length standing films if LLD

**CT** — if union uncertain (hardware artefact)

## 5 Management

```
Nonunion (rule out infection: CRP, ESR)
├─ Hypertrophic → ↑ STABILITY (exchange nail / compression plate)
├─ Oligotrophic → IMPROVE FIXATION ± BIOLOGY
├─ Atrophic → REVISION FIXATION + AUTOGRAFT + SOFT-TISSUE ENVELOPE
└─ Septic → STAGED DEBRIDEMENT, BEADS, FLAP, TRANSPORT
```

**Atrophic ulna nonunion 6 mo after BBFA ORIF, normal CRP/ESR** — ✓ **revision ORIF with autograft**\
✗ Wrong options: observe, cast, IM nail, revision without graft

**Atrophic radius nonunion with gap** — ✓ **supportive soft-tissue envelope** essential · plating + cancellous graft treats defects **up to 6 cm** · **3.5 mm plates** adequate · poor envelope → vascularised graft + soft tissue

**Atrophic tibial nonunion 9 mo, normal labs** — cause ✓ **lack of biology** → exchange nail + graft

**Non-operative** — brace · **bone stimulators**\
✗ Wrong options: synovial pseudarthrosis · mobile nonunion · gap >1 cm

### Electrical stimulation

**Induction coupling** — ↑ **BMP-7, BMP-2, TGF-β1**, ↑ **osteoblast proliferation** · **not ↓ osteoclast differentiation**

| Mode | Effect |
|---|---|
| Direct current | ↓ O₂, ↑ pH → ↓ osteoclasts, ↑ osteoblasts |
| Capacitive coupling (AC) | cAMP, collagen, cartilage calcification |
| PEMF | Fibrocartilage calcification |
| Combined magnetic | — |

## How it's asked

**"Ulna fracture 12 mo, pain, abundant callus on X-ray (TO)?"** → **hypertrophic nonunion**

**"Nonunion after ex-fix, cultures negative — histology?"** → **bland cartilage callus**

**"Least likely to need added biology?"** → **abundant callus without bridging (hypertrophic)**

**"Tibial nail 9 mo, no bone formation, normal labs — cause?"** → **lack of biology (atrophic)**

**"Humerus ORIF, little callus, broken plate, normal labs?"** → **atrophic**

**"Distal femur locked plate nonunion healed with revision, no graft — initial problem?"** → **high stiffness**

**"Smoker having spine fusion — ↓ pseudarthrosis by?"** → **smoking cessation**

**"Obese smoker, NSAIDs, delayed nailing — highest nonunion risk?"** → **smoking**

**"Open subtroch femur, 9-mo pain, erythema, nonunion — next?"** → **vitamin D, TSH, inflammatory labs**

**"Septic nonunion — most accurate lab?"** → **CRP**

**"Atrophic ulna nonunion, normal CRP/ESR?"** → **revision ORIF with autograft**

**"Atrophic radius nonunion with gap — required besides revision + graft?"** → **supportive soft-tissue envelope**

**"Induction coupling effects EXCEPT?"** → **↓ osteoclast differentiation**
