# Psoriatic Arthritis

*Basic Science · 86 / 108*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **seronegative spondyloarthropathy** · DIP-predominant hand arthritis · silvery plaques · uveitis · nail dystrophy

**Incidence** — **5–20%** of psoriasis patients (Orthobullets, OB210216) · 5–10% (OB3340) · ~20% (OB3581) — read the options \
✗ Wrong options: "most psoriasis patients develop arthritis"

**Sex** — M = F

**Skin first** — plaques precede arthritis by years in **80–85%** · arthritis first in **~20%** **(TO)**

### Genes & lab markers

**HLA-B27** — positive in **~50%**\
✗ Wrong options: all patients

**RF and ANA** — usually **negative**

### Patterns (Wright & Moll) (TO)

| Pattern (TO) | Features |
|---|---|
| **Symmetrical polyarthritis** | ✓ **Most common** · resembles RA but milder · RF-negative **(TO)** |
| **Asymmetrical oligoarthritis** | **<5 joints**, small/medium · **2nd commonest** (DIP, PIP, MCP) **(TO)** |
| **Distal (DIP-predominant)** | DIP + PIP · **acro-osteolysis** of tufts + nail changes |
| **Arthritis mutilans** | Rare · extreme phalangeal + MCP destruction → telescoping · **opera-glass hand (doigt / main en lorgnette)** · pencil-in-cup |
| **Spondyloarthropathy** | ~1/3 · SI (usually **unilateral**) + spine · **cervical > thoracolumbar** (distinguishes from AS) **(TO)** |

## 3 Presentation

**Skin** — **silvery plaques** on extensor surfaces (elbows, knees) · scalp

**Hands** — **dactylitis (sausage digit)** — up to **50%**, toe > finger **(TO)** · **nail pitting (onychodystrophy)** · **onycholysis** (distal nail-plate lifting) · opera-glass hands

**Opera-glass hand** — shortened phalanges → excess skin folds transversely, digits retract like telescoping binoculars

**Enthesitis** — up to **40%** **(TO)** · **Achilles**, **posterior tibial tendonitis**, **plantar fasciitis** · heel, foot, hip pain

**Other** — chronic **uveitis** · ankylosis of small hand/foot joints more than RA **(TO)**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Pencil-in-cup** — ✓ **DIP**: destruction of middle phalanx head + expansion of distal phalanx base · **centripetal erosions → joint-space widening**\
✗ Wrong options: DJD

![Pencil-in-cup deformity](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9050-radiograph-of-pencil-in-cup-deformity.jpg)

**Other hand signs** — **acro-osteolysis** (distal tuft resorption) · **fluffy periostitis** (periosteal ossification) · small-joint erosions or **fusions** (PIP, MCP, wrist) · flail digits

**Spine** — sacroiliitis · syndesmophytes · paravertebral ossification · destructive discovertebral lesions

### Diagnostic criteria — CASPAR

**Requirement** — inflammatory articular disease (joint, spine, entheseal) + **≥3 points**

| CASPAR criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| **Current psoriasis** | **2** |
| Personal or family history of psoriasis | 1 |
| **Psoriatic nail dystrophy** (onycholysis, pitting, hyperkeratosis) | 1 |
| **Negative RF** | 1 |
| **Dactylitis** (current or recorded) | 1 |
| **Juxta-articular new bone formation** on hand/foot X-ray (✗ Wrong options: osteophytes) | 1 |

## 5 Management

**Medical (mainstay, like RA)** — NSAIDs · methotrexate · sulfasalazine · cyclosporine · **TNF-α inhibitors** · rheumatology referral

### Treatment — TNF inhibitors

**Failed NSAIDs, active disease** — ✓ **biologic DMARD — TNF inhibitor** (or IL-17, IL-12/23) = ACR first line → ↓ joint destruction + surgery

| ✗ Wrong option | Why not |
|---|---|
| NSAIDs | Symptomatic only, not disease-modifying |
| Weight loss / bariatric | does not treat inflammation |
| **Hydroxychloroquine** | not approved · **precipitates / worsens psoriasis** |
| **Oral prednisone** | Weak disease modification · **pustular flare on withdrawal** |
| csDMARDs (MTX, leflunomide, SSZ) | Limited effect on joint damage + skin |

**Operative** — advanced disease → **digit fusion vs resection arthroplasty** · osteotomy, arthroplasty · **high infection rates** reported

## Psoriatic vs other hand arthritis

| | **Psoriatic** | **RA** | **Erosive OA** | **Gout** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Joints | **DIP** | PIP, **MCP (2nd–3rd)**, ulnar styloid, triquetrum · no DIP | DIP, PIP, 1st CMC | Any |
| Erosions | **Centripetal**, pencil-in-cup | Marginal, subchondral cysts | **Central "gull wing"**, not marginal | **Punched-out "rat-bite"**, overhanging edges, sclerotic margins |
| Other | Periostitis, acro-osteolysis, telescoping | Ulnar drift, swan-neck, boutonnière | Osteopenia, fusiform swelling | Tophi |
| Serology | RF− · HLA-B27 ~50% | RF+ | — | — |

## How it's asked

**"Most common form of psoriatic arthritis?"** → **symmetrical polyarthritis** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: asymmetric oligoarthritis (2nd)

**"Sausage digits + pencil-in-cup?"** → **psoriatic arthritis**\
✗ Wrong options: gout · RA

**"Toe dactylitis, posterior tibial tendonitis, nail pitting?"** → **psoriatic arthritis**\
✗ Wrong options: scleroderma · pseudogout

**"Nail pitting + onycholysis — matching radiograph?"** → **telescoping / opera-glass destruction**\
✗ Wrong options: RA MCP ulnar drift · erosive OA gull-wing

**"Psoriatic arthritis — true?"** → **DIP erosions often centripetal**\
✗ Wrong options: most psoriasis patients get arthritis · all HLA-B27+ · RF/ANA positive · antifungal

**"Severe PsA after NSAID failure — what would have prevented TKA?"** → **TNF inhibitor**\
✗ Wrong options: hydroxychloroquine · prednisone
