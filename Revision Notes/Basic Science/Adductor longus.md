# Adductor longus

*Basic Science · 2 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy

| | Adductor longus |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Anterior body of pubis, just lateral to symphysis |
| **Insertion** | **Middle third of linea aspera** · between adductor magnus/brevis (medial) and vastus medialis origin (lateral) |
| **Action** | Adducts + flexes thigh · helps laterally rotate hip |
| **Innervation** | **Anterior division of obturator nerve (L2, L3, L4)** |
| **Arterial supply** | Obturator artery + medial circumflex femoral artery |

![Adductor longus](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob10065-adductor-longus.jpg)

### Cross-sectional anatomy — proximal thigh

**Adductor longus** — lies **just deep to the superficial femoral artery**

**Adductor brevis** — between longus and magnus · **deep to the deep femoral vessels**

**Adductor magnus** — largest, most posterior adductor

**Sartorius** — superficial · **covers the superficial femoral vessels**

**Gracilis** — medial + superficial

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### L3 radiculopathy vs femoral neuropathy

**Key test** — **hip adduction** (obturator nerve) · weak in **L2/L3 radiculopathy**, spared in **femoral neuropathy (L2–4)**

**Femoral-innervated** — quadriceps · iliacus · sartorius · psoas\
✗ Wrong options: distinguishing

## X vs Y

| Muscle | Nerve | Distinguishes L3 root from femoral nerve? |
|---|---|---|
| **Adductor longus** | **Obturator (ant. division)** | Yes |
| Quadriceps | Femoral | No |
| Iliacus | Femoral | No |
| Sartorius | Femoral | No |
| Psoas | Femoral (listed as such in the OB key) | No |

## How it's asked

**"L3 radiculopathy best differentiated from femoral neuropathy by testing?"** → **adductor longus** (obturator)\
✗ Wrong options: quadriceps · iliacus · sartorius · psoas

**"Muscle just deep to the superficial femoral artery on proximal-thigh cross-section?"** → **adductor longus**\
✗ Wrong options: magnus (large, posterior) · brevis (deep to profunda) · sartorius (covers SFA) · gracilis (medial)
