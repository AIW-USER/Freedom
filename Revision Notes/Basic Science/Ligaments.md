# Ligaments

*Basic Science · 57 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Function & composition

**Function** — restrict motion · stabilise joint · **proprioception** (mechanoreceptors, free nerve endings)

**Composition** — water · **type I collagen (70% dry weight)** · **elastin (more than tendon)** · lipids · proteoglycans · epiligament (some) · **fibroblasts** · low vascularity + cellularity

**Ligament vs tendon** — ligament: **less collagen**, **more PG + water**, less organised fibres, rounder fibroblasts

**Blood supply** — enters at **insertion** (unlike tendon) · uniform microvascularity · ACL/PCL: **middle geniculate artery**

### Insertion anatomy (direct, indirect, Sharpey fibres)

| | **Indirect (fibrous)** | **Direct (fibrocartilaginous)** |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | **Most common** | — |
| Mechanism | Superficial fibres → **periosteum** · deep fibres → bone via **Sharpey fibres** (type I collagen) | 4 transition zones of ↑ stiffness (force dissipation) |
| Example | **MCL into proximal tibia** | **Supraspinatus**, native ACL |

| Direct zone | Content |
|---|---|
| **1 Ligament/tendon** | Aligned type I collagen, decorin |
| **2 Fibrocartilage** (unmineralised) | Types **II + III**, small I, IX, X · aggrecan, decorin |
| **3 Mineralised fibrocartilage** | Type II + significant **type X**, aggrecan |
| **4 Bone** | Type I, high mineral |

## 2 Mechanism

### Viscoelasticity & mechanical behaviour

**Viscoelastic** — ✓ behaviour depends on **loading rate** (faster → **stiffer + stronger**) → control load rate when testing prosthetic ligaments

| Property | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Creep** | ↑ deformation over time under **constant load** |
| **Stress relaxation** | ↓ stress over time under **constant deformation** |
| **Hysteresis** | Unloading curve ≠ loading curve · area = energy dissipated |

**Load–elongation curve** — **toe region** (crimped fibres straighten, **non-linear**) → **linear region** (slope = stiffness / Young's modulus) → yield (elastic → plastic) → failure

**Ligament vs tendon curve** — ligament recruits fibres gradually → **longer toe region** · tendon → shorter toe, higher loads

**Anisotropic** — strong in **tension**, weak in compression · Young's modulus **< cancellous bone, > cartilage**

![Stress–strain curve — toe, linear, failure](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9016-stressstrain.jpg)

### Failure

**Mechanism** — sequential collagen fibre rupture · no plastic deformation

**Site** — **adults: midsubstance** · **children: bony avulsion** at junction of **mineralised + unmineralised fibrocartilage**

| Grade | Injury |
|---|---|
| I | Mild sprain |
| II | Moderate / partial tear |
| III | Complete tear |

## 5 Management

### Healing & graft incorporation

| Phase | Time | Events |
|---|---|---|
| **Inflammatory** | **1–7 d** | Neutrophils, macrophages · **type III collagen** · TGF-β1, IGF, PDGF, BMP-12/13, bFGF |
| **Proliferative** | **7–21 d** | → **type I collagen** · **weakest day 5–21** |
| Remodelling | >14 d | — |
| Maturation | ≤18 months | — |

**Healed MCL at 1 year vs normal** — ✓ **↑ number of fibres** · type III proportion back to normal · **cross-links only ~45%** · ↓ fibre mass + diameter · scar → ↓ ultimate strength, adhesions

**Impair healing** — **intra-articular** (ACL < MCL) · age · **immobilisation** · smoking · **NSAIDs** · DM · alcohol · ↓ bFGF, NGF, IGF-1 · ↓ procollagen I, COMP, tenascin-C, tenomodulin, scleraxis

**Improve (experimental)** — extra-articular · CD44 knockout · **IL-10** · IL-1 receptor antagonist · macrophage depletion · MSCs (tunnel healing) · PDGF-BB (↓ adhesions, no strength gain) · G-CSF (tunnel) · BMP-2/-12 (cuff) · collagen–PRP hydrogel for primary ACL repair · **CGRP** (MCL)

**Allograft vs autograft ACL** — allograft: ✓ **prolonged inflammation**, slower incorporation, more large-diameter fibrils · tendon grafts heal to tunnel by **fibrovascular scar → Sharpey-like fibres** · intra-articular part: necrosis → host synovial repopulation → revascularisation → **ligamentisation**\
✗ Wrong options: native 4-zone insertion

## How it's asked

**"Order of direct insertion zones?"** → **ligament → fibrocartilage → mineralised fibrocartilage → bone**

**"MCL tear — structure and insertion?"** → **ligament; Sharpey fibres (indirect)**

**"Why control loading rate testing a prosthetic ligament?"** → **ligaments are viscoelastic**

**"MCL sprain — mechanical property?"** → **non-linear stress–strain on initial loading**\
✗ Wrong options: isotropic · strongest in compression

**"Grade III MCL at 1 yr vs normal — ↑?"** → **gross number of fibres**\
✗ Wrong options: type III · cross-links · diameter

**"Allograft vs autograft ACL inflammatory phase?"** → **prolonged**
