# Fracture Healing

*Basic Science · 38 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Stem-cell sources

**Two major sources** — **periosteum** + **endosteum** · periosteal response ↑ by motion, ↓ by rigid fixation

### Primary vs secondary healing & strain theory

**Strain (definition)** — ✓ **change in length ÷ original length** (ΔL/L) · stress = force/area

**Perren's interfragmentary strain theory** — gap tissue differentiation depends on strain · **comminuted fractures tolerate more motion** (motion shared across many gaps) · simple transverse fractures in a nail → higher strain

| Strain | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **<2%** | **Primary healing** (intramembranous, **Haversian remodelling / cutting cones**) |
| **2–10%** | **Secondary healing** (endochondral, callus) |
| 10–100% | Fibrous tissue (or hypertrophic nonunion) |
| >100% | Nonunion |

**Tissue tolerance (TO)** — **cortical bone tolerates only 2%** · **lamellar bone up to 10%** (TO wording) · granulation tissue tolerates the **greatest** strain

**Strain bands – TO lecture (TO)** — < 2% primary · 2–10% secondary (callus) · **> 17% fibrous union** · **\~100% granulation tissue** · rule: **small gap = high strain, large gap = low strain**

**Locking plate (TO)** — secondary healing (strain 2–10%) · compression plate → primary healing

**Cancellous bone healing (TO)** — minimal callus → **creeping substitution** (direct osteoblastic activity); callus only if a gap exists

**Osteoblast toxins (TO)** — **hydrogen peroxide**, **povidone-iodine (Betadine)**, **bacitracin**

| Tissue | Yield strain (TO, Perren 1975) |
|---|---|
| Granulation | **100%** |
| Cartilage | **10%** |
| Bone | **2%** |

**Fibrous union threshold** — **10–100%** (Orthobullets) · **> 17%** (TO) — read the options

**Callus** — ∝ movement · inversely ∝ stability (TO)

**Osteoblasts** — stimulated at 0.3–2.8% strain · optimal interfragmentary motion **0.2–1 mm**

| Construct | Healing |
|---|---|
| **Compression plate**, **lag screw + neutralisation plate**, tension band, medial malleolar screws | **Primary — Haversian remodelling** (absolute stability, anatomic reduction, <2%) |
| **IM nail** (incl. CMN), **external fixator**, **bridge plate**, cast/brace/splint | **Secondary — endochondral** (relative stability, 2–10%) |
| **Locked compression plate (bridge mode)** | ✓ **Secondary (TO)** — "internal fixator" |

**Primary types (TO)** — **contact healing** (ends apposed → osteoclast **cutting cones** cross, osteoblasts follow) · **gap healing** (small gap → transverse woven bone → lamellar) · also unicortical/greenstick fractures

**Primary healing preferred** — periarticular · both-bone forearm · simple ankle fractures with syndesmotic disruption (callus would impair rotation/joint)

### Biomechanics of callus

**Peripheral callus** — ✓ **↑ moment of inertia → ↓ strain** · bending stiffness ∝ **diameter⁴** (2× diameter → **16×** stiffness) · ↑ torsional strength, peak torque

## 2 Mechanism

### Stages of secondary healing

| Stage | Events |
|---|---|
| **Inflammation** (haematoma) | Haematopoietic cells, macrophages, neutrophils, platelets → **PDGF, TNF-α, TGF-β, IL-1, 6, 10, 12** (from 24 h) · **BMPs ↑**, MSC migration (**SDF-1, G-CSF**) · granulation tissue · **COX-2 → Runx2/osterix** (NSAIDs act here) |
| **Repair** | **Primary callus within 2 wk** · stable → osteoblasts, unstable → chondrocytes · **type II collagen early → type I** · soft → hard callus (woven bone) · callus amount ∝ 1/immobilisation |
| **Remodelling** | Starts mid-repair, continues years · Ihh, PTHrP, FGF, BMP · **type X** collagen (hypertrophic chondrocytes, calcification) · chondrocyte apoptosis → **VEGF** → vessels · woven → **lamellar** (Wolff's law, piezoelectric) |

**Frost stages (1989, TO)** — **haematoma** → **granulation tissue** → **callus** (soft → hard, endochondral) → **modelling** (trabeculae reorient to stress) → **remodelling** (woven → lamellar, Wolff "form follows function")

| Perkins timetable (TO) | Callus visible | Union | Consolidation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Upper limb** | 2–3 wk | **4–6 wk** | 6–8 wk |
| **Lower limb** | 2–3 wk | **8–12 wk** | 12–16 wk |

| Growth factor | Action in healing (TO) |
|---|---|
| **BMP** | **Osteoinductive** · MSC → osteoblast |
| **TGF-β** | Type II collagen + PG synthesis in callus |
| **IGF-II** | Type I collagen + cartilage matrix |
| **PDGF** | **Chemotactic** — recruits inflammatory cells |

| Mediator | Role |
|---|---|

| **BMP-2** | ↑ in **inflammatory phase** · recruits MSCs · differentiation |
| **SDF-1 + G-CSF** | ✓ **MSC migration to fracture** (absence → nonunion) |

| RANKL | Osteoclasts |

|---|---|

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Variables that influence healing

**Blood supply — most important** — ↓ immediately → ↑ peaks **2 wk** → normal **3–5 months** · unreamed nails preserve endosteal supply · reaming devascularises inner **50–80%** · loose nails reperfuse faster

**Head injury** — may ↑ osteogenic response

**Mechanical** — soft-tissue attachments · stability/strain · location · bone loss · **segmental/butterfly** → ↑ nonunion (intercalary blood supply)

| Patient factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Vitamin D / Ca deficiency** | **84%** of nonunions metabolic · **>66% vitamin D deficient** |
| Protein malnutrition | ↓ callus strength · amino acids ↑ albumin, body mass, muscle protein, callus mineralisation (✗ Wrong options: **not IGF-1 mRNA**) |
| **Roux-en-Y gastric bypass** | **Duodenum bypassed** → ↓ Ca/vit D → **2° hyperparathyroidism** → nonunion (✗ Wrong options: sleeve · band · colectomy · nephrectomy) |
| **Diabetes** | ↓ callus cellularity, delayed endochondral ossification, weak callus · **1.6× longer** |
| **Nicotine** | ↓ angiogenesis, weak callus · **spine pseudarthrosis +500%** · open tibia **~70% longer** |
| **HIV** | Fragility fractures, delay — ARVs, poor circulation, **TNF-α deficiency**, nutrition |
| Bisphosphonates | Longer healing (wrist ORIF) · atypical femur fractures |
| Corticosteroids | **6.5%** higher IT nonunion · ↓ IGF-1, TGF-β |
| NSAIDs | COX inhibition |
| Quinolones | Chondrocyte toxicity |
| High-dose radiation | ↓ cellularity, long-term remodelling change |

**Detrimental (TO)** — age · malnutrition (**albumin <3.5, lymphocytes <1,500**) · smoking · **alcohol** · DM · cancer, osteomalacia, Cushing's, obesity · **anaemia** (iron for proline hydroxylation, mineralisation) · **PVD** · NSAIDs, chemo, steroids, BPs, quinolones · radiation · infection · open, intra-articular, segmental, comminuted, denervated fractures · **intact fellow bone** · soft-tissue interposition · inadequate reduction/fixation (too short, too stiff) · **nephrotic syndrome — not listed** **(TO)**

**Vicarious blood supply (TO)** — femoral neck · scaphoid waist/proximal pole · talar body · paediatric lateral condyle · 5th MT base · distal ⅓ tibia · distal ⅓ ulna

## 5 Management

### Nonunion (stability, metabolic causes)

```
Nonunion
├─ Hypertrophic (abundant callus) → ↑ MECHANICAL STABILITY
│      (cause: inadequate stability, biology intact)
├─ Atrophic / oligotrophic (poor biology) → TAKEDOWN + BONE GRAFT + STABILISE
└─ No infection signs → METABOLIC WORK-UP (25-OH vit D) → VITAMIN D + OBSERVE
```

**Oligotrophic distal tibia nonunion, no infection** — ✓ **observe + vitamin D** (keep weight-bearing)\
✗ Wrong options: NWB · suppressive antibiotics

**Lab of choice for vit D** — **25-hydroxyvitamin D**

### Bone stimulation (LIPUS, capacitive coupling)

| Modality | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **LIPUS** (30 mW/cm² pulsed) | ✓ **Nanomotion** (mechanical strain across cell wall) · ↑ vascularity, protein expression · ↑ callus torque/stiffness · delayed/nonunion healing ~**80%** |
| **Capacitive coupling** (AC) | ✓ **Ca²⁺ translocation via voltage-gated channels** → calmodulin → ↑ BMP, cAMP, TGF-β1 · collagen, cartilage calcification |
| **Direct current** | ↓ O₂, ↑ pH → ↓ osteoclasts, ↑ osteoblasts · inflammatory-like response |
| **PEMF** | Calcification of **fibrocartilage** (✗ Wrong options: fibrous tissue) |

**LIPUS Level I evidence** — ✓ radius shaft, distal radius, **casted tibia**, scaphoid\
✗ Wrong options: **tibia treated with reamed IM nail**

### Dynamisation

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Dynamisation | Gradually **destabilise** fixation to load bone |
| **Reverse dynamisation** | ✓ **Early micromotion → callus**, then convert to **rigid** fixation to accelerate union |
| Accordion method | Alternate distraction/compression of regenerate |
