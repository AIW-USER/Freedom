### Osteoblasts (Runx2, osteocalcin, RANKL, PTH)

**Structure** — ↑ ER, Golgi, mitochondria · **cuboidal**, line immature osteoid · most active at bone surface

**Products** — non-mineralised matrix: **type I collagen**, **alkaline phosphatase**, osteonectin, **osteocalcin** (↑ by 1,25-(OH)₂D)

**Most specific marker of mature osteoblast** — ✓ **osteocalcin** (not in immature, proliferating cells)\
✗ Wrong options: TGF-β · COL2A1 (chondrocyte) · c-Fos (osteoclast) · IL-1

**Differentiation signals** — **BMP** (MSC → osteoprogenitor) · **Runx2** · **stable β-catenin** (→ intramembranous bone) · PDGF · IGF

**Osteocalcin (TO)** — **most abundant non-collagenous protein** · marker of osteoblast activity

**Osteoblast differentiation (TO)** — RUNX2, BMP, **WNT/β-catenin**, PDGF, IGF · **cathepsin K NOT involved** (osteoclast enzyme)

**Porosity (TO)** — cortical **5–10%** · cancellous **75–95%**, turnover **8× faster**

| Signal on osteoblast | Effect |
|---|---|
| **PTH receptor** | ↑ ALP, type I collagen · → **RANKL** + M-CSF · **intermittent rhPTH (1-34) → anabolic** · chronic ↑ PTH → resorption |
| 1,25-(OH)₂D receptor | ↑ matrix, ALP, **osteocalcin** |
| Oestrogen | ↓ resorption, ↑ formation (inhibits adenylyl cyclase) |
| Glucocorticoids | ↓ collagen + matrix |
| Prostaglandins | ↑ resorption (activate adenylyl cyclase) |

**Coupling** — osteoblast **RANKL → RANK** on osteoclast precursor → resorption · osteoblast **OPG** = decoy for RANKL → ↓ osteoclasts

**Jagged1–Notch** — ✓ **PTH** induces **Jagged1** on osteoblasts → Notch on **HSCs** → HSC proliferation (immune/haematopoietic regulation)

### Osteoclasts (origin, cathepsin K, integrins, RGD)

**Appearance** — **multinucleated giant cell**, monocyte lineage, **ruffled border** · sits in **Howship's lacunae**

**Differentiation** — **M-CSF + RANKL** (progenitors express RANK) · also PU.1, CSF-1

**Activators** — **RANKL** (osteoblasts, tumour cells) · **IL-1** (next to loose TJA implants)

**Inhibitors** — **calcitonin** · IL-10 · OPG

**Acid phosphatase** — ✓ **osteoclast (TRAP)** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: osteoblast · osteocyte

| Step | Molecule |
|---|---|
| **Attachment (sealing zone)** | **Integrins** — **αVβ3 = vitronectin receptor** · bind **RGD (Arg-Gly-Asp)** sequence on vitronectin, fibronectin, osteopontin · **reversible** binding · anti-αVβ3/RGD block resorption |
| Polarisation | Ruffled border · functional secretory domain · basolateral membrane |
| **Mineral dissolution** | **HCl** into resorption lacuna · ATP proton pumps · H⁺ from **carbonic anhydrase II** · Cl⁻ channels · CA deficiency → no resorption |
| **Organic matrix** | **Cathepsin K** (major) · **MMP-9** · lysosomal cysteine proteinases |
| Removal | Transcytosis to FSD · **TRAP** in vesicles → reactive O₂ species destroy collagen |
| End | Apoptosis or return to non-resorbing state |

**Cathepsin K** — function = ✓ **bone resorption** · mutation → **pycnodysostosis** · inhibitors studied for osteoporosis

**Bisphosphonates** — block ruffled border + acid hydrolase production

![Osteoclast zones — sealing zone, ruffled border, resorption lacuna](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9002-osteoclast-zones.jpg)

**Disuse** — ↓ integrin expression → disuse osteoporosis

### Osteocytes

**Origin** — **former osteoblasts trapped in their own matrix** · **90%** of cells in mature skeleton

**Structure** — high nucleus:cytoplasm · long processes through **canaliculi** → **gap junctions** (connexins; cAMP, IP3 pass) · radial around Haversian canal

**Function** — maintain bone matrix · **Ca/P regulation** · no ALP

**Osteocyte stimulation (TO)** — **stimulated by calcitonin** · regulates Ca / PO₄

**Signals** — **stimulated by calcitonin**, **inhibited by PTH**

| Structure | Role |
|---|---|
| **Canaliculi** | ✓ **Osteocyte–osteocyte communication** |
| Haversian canal | Centre of osteon · vessels + nerves |
| Volkmann canal | Perpendicular · links Haversian canals + periosteum |
| Cement line | Boundary of osteon |

**Cement lines** — **weak points → common fracture planes** · **osteon = functional unit** of cortical bone (TO)

**Cortical bone** — **80% of skeletal mass** · slow turnover · resists **torsion + bending** · cancellous: vascular, high turnover, heals faster (TO)

| | Lamellar | Woven |
|---|---|---|
| Organisation | **Stress-oriented** (Wolff) | **Random** |
| Cellularity | **Hypocellular** | **Hypercellular**, ↑ turnover |
| Seen in | Normal cortical + cancellous | **Fracture callus, infection, malignancy, stress reaction** (TO) |

## X vs Y

| | Osteoblast | Osteoclast | Osteocyte |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin | Mesenchymal | **Myeloid (monocyte)** | Trapped osteoblast |
| Marker | **Osteocalcin**, ALP | **TRAP**, cathepsin K, calcitonin receptor | No ALP |
| PTH | Receptor ✓ (→ RANKL) | **No receptor** (indirect) | Inhibited |
| Calcitonin | — | Inhibited (receptor ✓) | Stimulated |

| Function | Molecule / cell |
|---|---|
| Osteoclast attachment | **Integrin αVβ3 / vitronectin / RGD** |
| Binds RANKL, prevents RANK activation | **OPG** |
| Chemotaxis in fracture haematoma | PDGF |
| Induces type II collagen + PG in callus | TGF-β |

## How it's asked

**"Acid phosphatase contained in (TO)?"** → **osteoclast**

**"Function of cathepsin K?"** → **bone resorption**\
✗ Wrong options: RANK activation · water absorption

**"Vitronectin receptor function?"** → **osteoclast attachment to bone**\
✗ Wrong options: RANKL interaction · chemotaxis (PDGF) · type II collagen (TGF-β)

**"MSCs can form all EXCEPT?"** → **osteoclasts**

**"Multinucleated giant cells, monocyte lineage, ruffled border?"** → **osteoclasts**

**"True about the cell in Figure A (osteoclast)?"** → **haematopoietic, macrophage lineage**\
✗ Wrong options: mesenchymal (osteoblast) · trapped osteoblast (osteocyte)

**"Integrins function?"** → **osteoclast attachment to bone**\
✗ Wrong options: binds RANKL (OPG)

**"RGD sequence allows?"** → **interaction with integrins**

**"Enzyme used by mature osteoclasts to resorb bone?"** → **cathepsin K**\
✗ Wrong options: ALP, TIMP, osteonectin, osteopontin

**"Lineage and mediator of osteoclast differentiation?"** → **myeloid / RANKL**\
✗ Wrong options: lymphoid · BMP · OPG

**"Cell of myeloid origin?"** → **preosteoclast**

**"Primary function of RGD bone protein?"** → **reversible osteoclast binding to bone**

**"True about normal remodelling (osteopetrosis patient)?"** → **Howship's lacunae form at resorption sites**\
✗ Wrong options: PTH acts directly on osteoclasts · OPG ↑ turnover · TRAP from osteocytes · lamellar → woven

**"Function of Cbfa1/Runx2?"** → **osteoblastic differentiation**

**"Releases RANKL?"** → **osteoblasts**

**"Most specific marker of mature osteoblasts?"** → **osteocalcin**

**"Intermittent rhPTH targets?"** → **osteoblasts**

**"Transcription factor committing to bone?"** → **Runx2**\
✗ Wrong options: PPARγ2, MyoD, Sox9, C/EBPα

**"Activates Jagged1–Notch pathway?"** → **PTH**

**"Osteocyte communication network?"** → **canaliculi**\
✗ Wrong options: Volkmann · cement lines · Haversian

**"Spindled cells in mature osteoid connected by canaliculi?"** → **osteocytes**
