# Axillary Nerve

*Basic Science · 9 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Course

**Origin** — **posterior cord** (with radial nerve) → just **inferior to subscapularis** → winds posteriorly through **quadrangular space** with **posterior circumflex humeral artery**

![Axillary nerve, posterior view](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob10136-axillary-posterior.jpg)

### Motor & sensory distribution

| Branch | Supplies |
|---|---|
| **Anterior branch** | Winds round **surgical neck** → **anterior (half) deltoid** + overlying skin |
| **Posterior branch** | **Teres minor** + **posterior deltoid** · skin over lower ⅔ of posterior deltoid (**superior lateral cutaneous nerve of arm**) |
| Collateral branch | Long head of triceps |

**Axillary nerve muscles** — ✓ **deltoid + teres minor**\
✗ Wrong options: teres major (**lower subscapular n.**) · supraspinatus (**suprascapular n.**) · latissimus (thoracodorsal)

## 3 Presentation

**Lateral deltoid numbness + weak Hornblower's (teres minor, ER in abduction)** — ✓ **posterior branch of axillary nerve** (e.g. after greater-tuberosity fracture)

**Deltoid palsy test** — weakness of **abduction with the arm internally rotated** (supraspinatus rotated anteriorly, contributes less)

**Posterior cord injury** — would cause wider deficit (radial nerve too)

## 6 Complications

**Iatrogenic** — large **anterior shoulder exposure** (e.g. **Latarjet**) · uncommon but debilitating

**Treatment** — **Leechavengvongs radial → axillary nerve transfer**: a triceps branch of the radial nerve (medial, lateral or long head) → axillary nerve, as proximal as possible on axillary nerve, maximal excursion of donor

## X vs Y

| Muscle | Nerve |
|---|---|
| Deltoid, **teres minor** | **Axillary** |
| **Teres major** | **Lower subscapular** |
| Supraspinatus, infraspinatus | Suprascapular |
| Coracobrachialis, biceps, brachialis (medial) | Musculocutaneous |

## How it's asked

**"Healed GT fracture, lateral-deltoid numbness, weak Hornblower's — structure injured?"** → **posterior branch of axillary nerve**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior branch · posterior cord · suprascapular · musculocutaneous

**"Axillary lesion weakens deltoid and?"** → **teres minor**\
✗ Wrong options: teres major · latissimus

**"Post-Latarjet weak abduction in IR, radial-to-axillary transfer — injured nerve supplies?"** → **deltoid + teres minor**
