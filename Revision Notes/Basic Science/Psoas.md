# Psoas

*Basic Science · 85 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy

| | Psoas |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Transverse processes **L1–L5** + bodies and discs **T12–L5** |
| **Insertion** | **Lesser trochanter** |
| **Action** | Flexes torso and thigh on each other |
| **Innervation** | **Direct fibres of lumbar plexus (L1–L3)** |
| **Arterial supply** | Lumbar branch of iliopsoas branch of internal iliac artery |

### Nerves at risk in lumbar approaches

| Nerve | Relation to psoas |
|---|---|
| **Genitofemoral (L1–L2)** | ✓ **Emerges through psoas at L3–L4 → runs on anteromedial surface** — met in anterior retroperitoneal approach |
| Ilioinguinal, iliohypogastric, LFCN | **Posterior** to psoas → on quadratus lumborum / iliacus |
| Femoral | **Posterior** to psoas → wraps laterally onto iliopsoas distally |

**Lumbar plexus in transpsoas (lateral) approach** — ✓ **more dorsal at L1–2, more ventral at L4–5**\
✗ Wrong options: constant position

## How it's asked

**"Anterior retroperitoneal approach — nerve on anteromedial psoas?"** → **genitofemoral**\
✗ Wrong options: ilioinguinal, femoral, LFCN, iliohypogastric

**"Lumbar plexus position in psoas?"** → **dorsal in upper lumbar, ventral in lower lumbar**
