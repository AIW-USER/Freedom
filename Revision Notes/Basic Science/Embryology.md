### Limb bud signalling (AER, ZPA, timing)

**Limb bud** — lateral plate + somatic mesoderm under ectoderm · Shh from notochord regulates formation · growth = **AER ↔ progress-zone mesoderm** interaction (FGF-driven)

**All UL bones endochondral** — except distal tufts of distal phalanges (membranous)

| Signalling centre | Order | Molecule | Axis | Defect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Apical ectodermal ridge (AER)** | **1st** | **FGF8 (obligatory, earliest)** · FGF4, 9, 17 redundant · forms under **FGF10** | **Proximal → distal** + interdigital necrosis | Removal/insult → **transverse truncation** (congenital transverse absence of forearm), central deficiency (cleft hand), radial club hand |
| **Zone of polarising activity (ZPA)** — posterior | **2nd** | **Sonic hedgehog (Shh)** — dose-dependent | **Anteroposterior (radioulnar)** | **Duplication/anterior graft → mirror hand (ulnar dimelia)** |
| **Non-AER dorsal ectoderm (Wnt centre)** | **3rd** | **WNT7a → Lmx1b** | **Dorsoventral** (all dorsal features incl. **nails**) | — |
| Ventral ectoderm | — | **Engrailed-1** (antagonises WNT7a) | Ventral patterning | — |

### Shh gradient

**Little finger** — **highest** Shh (posterior/ulnar) · **thumb** — **lowest** Shh (anterior/radial)

**↑ Shh posteriorly** — **ulnar (postaxial) polydactyly** (dose-dependent) · ↓ Shh posteriorly → loss of ulnar digits

**↑ Shh anteriorly** — **loss of thumb**

**Timing** — **posterior elements (ulna, little finger) form early**, anterior (radius, thumb) late → AP disruption loses **radius/thumb**

**Shh activates Gremlin** — inhibits BMPs that would block AER FGF

**Mesoderm markers** — anterior **ALX4** · posterior **Hox8**

### Chondrification & joints

**Chondrification factors** — **Sox-5, -6, -9** · TGF-β, BMP-2 · FGFs · retinoids · hedgehog · PTHrP · cadherins · WNT5a, WNT7a

**Joint formation** — repress chondrogenesis: **WNT4, WNT14, GDF5** (CDMP-1)

**Finger separation** — **Msx2** in interdigital mesenchyme → **BMP4**-mediated apoptosis · Hox-7

**Apert (acrocephalosyndactyly)** — **FGFR2** mutation · severe craniofacial + mild syndactyly (gain-of-function, FGF2) · mild face + severe syndactyly (binds FGF10)

### Somites & axial skeleton (Hox genes)

**Somites** — paired **paraxial mesoderm** beside notochord · **cranial → caudal** (paraxis gene) · **42 pairs (TO)**

| Somite layer | Becomes |
|---|---|
| **Sclerotome** | ✓ **Axial skeleton — vertebral bodies + annulus fibrosus** |
| Myotome | Muscle (dorsal epimere → dorsal muscles) |
| Dermatome | Dermis/skin |

**Vertebra precursor (TO)** — ✓ **somites** · each vertebra = **caudal half of one sclerotome + cranial half of the next** (two somite levels) · sclerotome cells engulf notochord in week 4

**Metameric shift** — spinal nerves, originally mid-sclerotome, exit **between** vertebral bodies

**Hox (homeobox) genes** — ✓ **regulate segmentation/identity of axial skeleton** (rostral → caudal) · with Shh, AP patterning

| Structure | Origin |
|---|---|
| **Nucleus pulposus** | **Notochord** |
| **Annulus fibrosus** | **Sclerotome** |
| Spinal cord | Neural tube |
| PNS, pia, spinal ganglia, sympathetic trunk | **Neural crest** |

**Neural tube DV patterning** — **Shh** ventral (floor plate, notochord) vs **Wnt/β-catenin** dorsal (roof plate)

**Vertebral primary ossification centres (3)** — **centrum** · **neural arch** (posterior elements, pedicles) · costal element

## How it's asked

**"Congenital transverse absence of forearm — vascular insult to?"** → **apical ectodermal ridge**\
✗ Wrong options: limb bud mesoderm · ectoderm · notochord

**"Duplication of ZPA causes?"** → **mirror hand**\
✗ Wrong options: preaxial/postaxial polydactyly, syndactyly, hypoplastic thumb

**"Transplant along AP axis → mirror hand — site + molecule?"** → **ZPA, Shh**\
✗ Wrong options: AER/FGF8 · WNT7a

**"Limb bud timing and US?"** → **forms 4 wk, transvaginal US 8 wk**

**"Sclerotome becomes?"** → **axial skeleton**

**"Function of homeobox genes in axial skeleton?"** → **regulate segmentation (somatisation)**\
✗ Wrong options: limb bud (Shh) · PD (FGF10) · DV (Wnt)

**"Neural plate formation by (TO)?"** → **18 days**

**"Embryological precursor of vertebrae (TO)?"** → **somites**\
✗ Wrong options: notochord (NP)
