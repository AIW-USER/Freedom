# Hypoparathyroidism

*Basic Science · 46 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Pathophysiology

**Cause** — ↓ PTH from chief cells · **thyroidectomy** (incl. parathyroids) = **most common**

**Effects of ↓ PTH** — ↓ renal phosphate excretion → **↑ serum phosphate** · ↓ 1α-hydroxylation → **↓ 1,25-(OH)₂D** → **↓ calcium**

### Genes & lab markers

| ↓ | ↑ | Normal |
|---|---|---|
| **PTH, calcium**, 1,25-D, urinary Ca | **Serum phosphate** | **ALP** |

**Alkalosis** — ↑ albumin binding of ionised Ca → hypocalcaemia

**ECG** — **prolonged QT**

### PTH & vitamin D lab patterns

| Condition | Ca | PO₄ | PTH | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hyperparathyroidism | ↑ | ↓ | ↑ | Adenoma |
| **Hypoparathyroidism** | **↓** | **↑** | **↓** | **Thyroidectomy** |
| Ectopic PTH(rP) | ↑ | ↓ | ↓ | Malignancy |
| Vitamin D malabsorption | ↓ | ↓ | ↑ | Coeliac, GI disease |
| ↓ Vit D + no renal PO₄ excretion | ↓ | ↑ | ↑ | **Renal failure**, pseudohypoparathyroidism |

## 3 Presentation

**Hypocalcaemia** — neuromuscular irritability: **Chvostek's sign**, **tetany**, **seizures** · **cataracts** · nail fungal infections · hair loss · vitiligo, blotchy skin

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Skull X-ray** — **basal ganglia calcification**

## 5 Management

**Calcium + vitamin D supplementation** · monitor labs · **no hormone replacement** available (Orthobullets)

### Renal disease & PTH

**Chronic renal disease** — ✓ **↑ PTH production** (Ca wasted, PO₄ retained, ↓ 1,25-D)\
✗ Wrong options: TSH unaffected

**Renal osteodystrophy** — high-turnover (↑ PTH, gland hyperplasia → osteitis fibrosa cystica, persists after correction) vs **low-turnover (more common on dialysis)** · metaphyseal enlargement, frontal bossing, bowing, genu varum

## How it's asked

**"Chronic renal disease — endocrine abnormality?"** → **↑ PTH**\
✗ Wrong options: ↓ PTH · TSH change

**"Post-thyroidectomy tetany, ↑ PO₄, ↓ Ca, ↓ PTH?"** → **hypoparathyroidism**
