# Operative Blood Loss & Transfusion

*Basic Science · 72 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Risk factors for transfusion

**Strongest predictor** — ✓ **preoperative haemoglobin**

**Others** — female · older age · larger patient · longer surgery · **lateral release in TKA**

## 5 Management

### Preoperative optimisation

| Measure | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Labs** | CBC + **MCV** (microcytic → iron; macrocytic → folate/B12), folate, B12 |
| **Iron / vitamin regimen** | **Iron 256 mg + vit C 1 g + folate 5 mg daily × 30–45 d** → ↓ transfusion (only if deficient) · iron s/e: constipation, reflux, pain |
| **Erythropoietin** | Renal pericapillary cells · > autologous donation efficacy · cost ≈ 4 units blood · high-risk patients · regimens: **600 IU/kg × 4 (days −21, −14, −7, 0)** · 300 IU/kg × 15 · 150 IU/kg × 9 |
| **Preop autologous donation** | 1–2 units **≥3 weeks** before · Hb >11, weight >50 kg (best if Hb >14) · not cost-effective, many units wasted |
| Preop embolisation | Vascular tumours (**renal cell**) within **24 h** of surgery |

### Intraoperative

| Method | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Acute normovolaemic haemodilution** | Blood drawn at surgery, replaced with colloid, reinfused · less planning · rarely used (OR time) |
| **Tourniquet** | SBP + 100–150 mmHg · **≤2 h** · Esmarch (✗ Wrong options: infection, tumour) · caution PVD: arterial insufficiency, absent pedal pulses, popliteal aneurysm, arterial calcification → vascular consult |
| **TXA** | **Lysine analogue** · blocks plasmin(ogen) binding sites on fibrin → **↓ fibrinolysis**, stabilises clot · ↓ blood loss + transfusion (TKA <3%, THA <10%) · **IV = oral = topical** · no ↑ DVT/PE · PT/PTT unchanged · s/e: **seizures** (glycine/GABA) |
| Aminocaproic acid | Lysine analogue, plasmin inhibitor · cheaper · no seizure-threshold effect |
| Collagen (microfibrillar) | Activates **intrinsic** pathway · dry powder, dry field + dry instruments |
| Fibrin sealant | Fibrin + factor XIII + thrombin + Ca²⁺ |
| PRP / platelet-poor plasma | Haemostasis, healing / autologous fibrin sealant with thrombin + Ca |
| **Gelfoam** | Animal-skin gelatin · mechanical for low-pressure bleeders · ± thrombin · **remove excess** (impairs bone healing) |
| **Gelatin–thrombin matrix** (Floseal, Surgiflo) | Porcine/bovine gelatin + human thrombin · **spine epidural bleeding** · end of cascade |
| **Cell saver** | Filter, wash, reinfuse **within 6–8 h** · complications: **coagulopathy** (FDPs, TNF-α, ILs — esp. unwashed), wound drainage, contamination, cost |

**Topical haemostats** — not cost-effective in TJA · useful in spine (near neural tissue)

### TXA dosing & contraindications (TO)

**TXA in TKR (TO)** — ✓ **oral and IV equal efficacy**\
✗ Wrong options: "topical for 2 min better than IV" · ↑ DVT · "IV best"

**Doses (TO)** — IV **10–15 mg/kg or 1 g** (20 min before tourniquet inflation ± 15 min before deflation) · topical **1.5–3 g in 100 mL** for **5 min** before tourniquet release · oral **2 g 2 h preop** + 2 doses postop (cheapest)

**Absolute contraindications (FDA)** — ✓ **current subarachnoid haemorrhage, active intravascular clotting, severe hypersensitivity** only · embolic stroke, DVT, AF, factor V Leiden are **not** absolute

**Relative (OB/TO)** — pregnancy, OCP, seizures, **coronary stent <6 mo**, prior VTE/CVA/MI (TO list) · renal dosing

**Topical TXA** — alternative route if MI, CAD, CVA history

### Postoperative transfusion

**1 unit PRBC** — ↑ Hb **~1 g/dL**

**Threshold** — consider at **Hb <8 g/dL** · no routine transfusion >8 (no ↑ mortality, even cardiac patients) · transfuse if **symptomatic** (orthostasis, fluid-resistant tachycardia)

**Allogeneic blood** — **immunomodulation** → ↓ cell-mediated immunity, ↑ infection

| Platelet count | Action |
|---|---|
| **<5,000** | Transfuse (spontaneous bleeding) |
| **<50,000** | Transfuse **before surgery** |
| <100,000 | Consider before surgery |
| >100,000 | Not needed |

**Platelets** — ✓ **blood product most likely contaminated** (stored at **room temperature**) · 10/million units · **gram-positive** commonest

**Mild postop anaemia** — oral iron

### Jehovah's Witness

**Allogeneic blood** — forbidden (some individuals consent)

**Acceptable** — ✓ **continuous closed-loop cell salvage** · colloids, coagulation factors (personal choice) · **bovine-derived haemostats** generally OK · human-plasma products individual decision\
✗ Wrong options: **batch-processed** (breaks continuity)

**Profound intraop haemorrhage** — ✓ **continuous closed-loop cell saver**\
✗ Wrong options: TXA infusion · EPO (too slow)

## How it's asked

**"True about TXA in TKR (TO)?"** → **oral and IV equal efficacy**

**"TXA true EXCEPT?"** → **"increases fibrinolysis"**

**"Absolute contraindication to TXA in THA?"** → **none of these** (embolic stroke, DVT, AF, FVL)

**"Jehovah's Witness, profound intraop bleeding?"** → **continuous closed-loop cell salvage**

**"Blood product most likely contaminated?"** → **platelets**

**"Strongest predictor of postop transfusion?"** → **preoperative haemoglobin**
