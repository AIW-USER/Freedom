# Brain Anatomy

*Basic Science · TO notes*

## 1 Basics

### General

**Weight** — **1300–1400 g** · \~**10 glial cells per neuron** (TO)

**Neurons vs glia** — neurons **cannot multiply**, glia can → **gliomas** among commonest brain tumours · lost neurons after head injury **not replaced** (TO)

**Coverings** — dura · arachnoid · pia (as for the cord) (TO)

**Parts** — cerebrum (2 hemispheres) · cerebellum · brainstem (midbrain, pons, medulla) · diencephalon (thalamus, hypothalamus, epithalamus + pineal) (TO)

**Contralateral control** — motor fibres **decussate in the medulla** (TO)

### Ventricles & CSF pathway

**CSF pathway (TO)** — lateral ventricle → **foramen of Monro** → 3rd ventricle → **aqueduct of Sylvius** → 4th ventricle → **foramina of Luschka + Magendie** → subarachnoid space + spinal central canal → absorbed by **arachnoid villi / granulations**

**Third ventricle** — between the thalami, above hypothalamus · **fourth ventricle** — between pons / medulla and cerebellum (TO)

**Obstruction anywhere** — **hydrocephalus** (TO)

![Ventricles and CSF pathway](../../TO notes/attachments/TOn-notes-anatomy-of-the-brain-aug-2026-p02-f004.jpg)

### Cerebrum

**Cortex** — superficial **grey matter**; white matter deep · grey grows faster → **gyri, sulci, fissures** (TO)

**Hemisphere connection** — **corpus callosum** · separated by **longitudinal fissure** (TO)

| Lobe | Position | Main functions (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **Frontal (largest)** | Anterior; **central sulcus** separates from parietal | **Motor cortex**, personality, **Broca** (motor speech), planning, emotion |
| **Parietal** | Top of head | **Sensory cortex** (touch, pressure, temperature, pain), shapes on skin, language interpretation |
| **Occipital** | Back | **Visual cortex** |
| **Temporal** | Sides, above ears | **Auditory cortex**, hearing + **smell**; posterior part → speech comprehension |

![Lobes of the cerebrum](../../TO notes/attachments/TOn-notes-anatomy-of-the-brain-aug-2026-p02-f005.jpg)

| Lobe / system | Lesion → (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Frontal** | Contralateral **hemiplegia** · **non-fluent** (Broca) aphasia |
| **Parietal** | **Sensory neglect** · **astereognosis** |
| **Temporal** | **Receptive** (Wernicke) aphasia · memory |
| **Occipital** | **Contralateral homonymous hemianopia** |
| **Limbic system** | Aggression · **amnesia** (emotion, behaviour, memory) |

**Landmarks** — **central sulcus** frontal / parietal · **lateral (Sylvian) sulcus** separates temporal lobe · **precentral gyrus = primary motor** · **postcentral gyrus = primary sensory** (TO)

### Cerebellum

**Size** — 2nd largest; posterior cranial fossa; **\~10% of brain weight but \~50% of neurons** (TO)

**Parts** — **vermis** (posture, sitting upright) · anterior + posterior lobes (subconscious movement control) · **flocculonodular lobe** (equilibrium, balance) · folia · white matter = **arbor vitae** (TO)

**Lesion** — **staccato ("scanning") speech** · incoordination, imbalance (TO)

| Peduncle | Connects cerebellum to (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Superior** | **Midbrain** |
| **Middle** | **Pons** |
| **Inferior** | **Medulla** |

### Brainstem

| Part | Key facts (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Midbrain** (\~2.5 cm) | Tectum: **superior colliculi = vision**, **inferior colliculi = hearing** · tegmentum (eye movements) · **substantia nigra** (dopamine; lesion = **Parkinsonism**) · crus cerebri (corticospinal + corticonuclear) · arousal, sleep–wake, temperature |
| **Pons** | Links cerebellum to brain · respiration, swallowing, **pontine micturition centre**, hearing, balance, eye movement |
| **Medulla** (\~3 cm) | Pons → foramen magnum · **motor decussation** · cardiovascular, respiratory rhythm, vasomotor centres · swallow, cough, sneeze, vomit · lower cranial nerve nuclei |

**Brainstem lesion** — drowsiness / **coma** (arousal centres) (TO)

| Part | Cranial nerve nuclei (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Midbrain** | **CN III, IV** · red nucleus · substantia nigra |
| **Pons** | **CN V–VIII** (facial sensation, hearing, balance) |
| **Medulla** | **CN IX–XII** · pyramids · olives |

**Reticular formation** — maintains **arousal + consciousness** (TO)

**Corticospinal decussation** — at the **medullary pyramids** → contralateral weakness (TO)

### Diencephalon

**Thalamus** — \~**80%** of diencephalon · **gateway to cortex**: relays nearly all sensory input (TO)

**Hypothalamus** — autonomic, temperature, water balance, food intake, **circadian rhythm**, hypothalamo-pituitary axis, HR + BP (with medulla) (TO)

**Pineal** — **melatonin in darkness** → sleep · melatonin used for disturbed sleep after head injury (TO)

| Thalamic nucleus | Relays (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Medial geniculate** | **Hearing** |
| **Lateral geniculate** | **Vision** |
| **Ventral posterior** | Taste + body sensation (touch, pain, cold) |

### Circumventricular organs

**Definition** — periventricular structures **without a blood–brain barrier** ("windows of the brain"), sensory + secretory (TO)

**"Medimix SOAP" (TO)** — **M**edian eminence · **S**ubfornical organ · **S**ubcommissural organ · **O**VLT · **A**rea postrema · **P**ineal · **P**osterior pituitary

### Arterial supply

**Cerebral blood flow** — **\~750 mL/min = 15% of cardiac output** · two pairs: **ICA (anterior)** + **vertebral (posterior) circulation** (TO)

| Artery | Supplies (TO) | Occlusion (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **ACA** | Medial frontal + parietal, **paracentral lobule** | Contralateral **leg > arm** weakness |
| **MCA** | Lateral hemisphere, **Broca + Wernicke** | Contralateral **face + arm** weakness · aphasia (dominant) · **commonest infarct** |
| **Anterior choroidal** | **Internal capsule**, optic tract, hippocampus | **Pure motor hemiplegia** |
| **PICA** | Medulla + inferior cerebellum | **Wallenberg** — dysphagia, hoarseness, **Horner**, **contralateral body pain/temp loss** |
| **AICA** | Pons + **inner ear** | — |
| **SCA** | Upper cerebellum + midbrain | — |
| **Basilar** | Vertebrals unite → bifurcates into PCAs | **Quadriplegia, locked-in syndrome** |
| **PCA** | Occipital lobe, visual cortex | **Hemianopia**, memory loss |

### Circle of Willis

**Components** — ACAs linked by **AComA** · **PComA** joins ICA → PCA · basilar from vertebrals (TO)

**Significance** — collateral flow if one artery occluded · **\~85% of intracranial aneurysms** around the circle · berry aneurysm sites **AComA > PComA > MCA bifurcation** (TO)

### Venous drainage

**Pathway** — superior sagittal sinus → transverse · inferior sagittal + **great vein of Galen → straight sinus** → confluence · transverse → sigmoid → **internal jugular** (TO)

**Cavernous sinus** — drains orbit + pituitary · contains **CN III, IV, V1, V2, VI** · **thrombosis = ophthalmoplegia + facial infection ("danger triangle")** (TO)

### Meninges & spaces

| Space | Location | Bleed (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **Epidural** (potential) | Skull–dura | **Middle meningeal artery** → EDH |
| **Subdural** (potential) | Dura–arachnoid | **Bridging veins** → SDH (elderly, trauma) |
| **Subarachnoid** | Arachnoid–pia | Contains CSF + vessels |

**Layers** — dura (tough, forms venous sinuses) · arachnoid (avascular) · pia (thin, **vascular**, adherent) (TO)

### CSF dynamics

| Parameter | Value (TO) |
|---|---|
| Production | **Choroid plexus** (mainly lateral ventricles) · **\~500 mL/day** |
| Volume | **150 mL** |
| Absorption | Arachnoid villi → **superior sagittal sinus** |
| Normal pressure | **10–18 cm H₂O** |
| Lumbar puncture | **L3–L4** (below conus) |

| Block site (TO) | Result |
|---|---|
| **Foramen of Monro** | **Unilateral** ventricular dilatation |
| **Aqueduct of Sylvius** | **Non-communicating** hydrocephalus (aqueductal stenosis) |
| **Arachnoid villi** dysfunction | **Communicating** hydrocephalus |

## 2 Development

### Embryology

**Neural ectoderm (TO)** — **neural tube** (brain + cord) · **neural crest** (most of PNS) · **placodes** (sensory ganglia, anterior pituitary, inner ear)

| Phase (TO) | Event | Defects |
|---|---|---|
| **Dorsal induction (neurulation)** | Neural plate **\~4.5–5 wk** → groove → folds fuse → tube; secondary neurulation → distal cord | Primary: **anencephaly, cephalocele, Chiari** · secondary: **spinal dysraphism** (spina bifida, meningomyelocele) |
| **Ventral induction** | Rostral expansion → brain vesicles (proximal **2/3 brain**, caudal **1/3 cord**; lumen = ventricles) | **Holoprosencephaly**, hydrocephalus, **aqueductal stenosis**, agenesis of corpus callosum, **Dandy-Walker** |
| **Proliferation, migration, histogenesis** | Neurons multiply + migrate to cortex | **Lissencephaly**, schizencephaly, polymicrogyria, heterotopia, micro / megalencephaly, focal cortical dysplasia |
| Myelination | Myelin sheaths form | — |

| Primary vesicle | Secondary vesicle | Adult derivative (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **Prosencephalon** | Telencephalon · diencephalon | Cerebral hemispheres · thalamus, hypothalamus, epithalamus |
| **Mesencephalon** | Mesencephalon | Midbrain |
| **Rhombencephalon** | **Metencephalon** · **myelencephalon** | **Pons + cerebellum** · **medulla** |

## 3 Surface Anatomy

### Cortical surface anatomy

**Central sulcus joins Sylvian fissure** — only **\~2%** (separated by **subcentral gyrus** in 98%) (TO)

**Parietal lobules** — superior + inferior, split by **intraparietal sulcus** · **supramarginal gyrus (area 40)** caps Sylvian fissure · **angular gyrus (area 39)** caps superior temporal sulcus (TO)

**Pars opercularis, triangularis, orbitalis** — **inferior FRONTAL gyrus** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: inferior temporal gyrus

### Brodmann areas

| Area | Function (TO) |
|---|---|
| **4** (precentral) | **Primary motor** |
| 6 | Premotor / supplementary motor |
| 8 | **Frontal eye field** |
| **3, 1, 2** (postcentral) | **Primary somatosensory** |
| **44, 45** (inferior frontal) | **Broca** — **non-fluent**, comprehension preserved |
| **22, 39, 40** | **Wernicke** — **fluent**, comprehension lost |
| **41, 42** (Heschl) | **Primary auditory** |
| **17** | **Primary visual** |

### Craniometric points

**Pterion** — frontal + parietal + temporal + **greater wing of sphenoid** · 2 fingerbreadths above zygomatic arch, thumb's breadth behind frontal process of zygoma · **thinnest skull** · overlies **Sylvian fissure** (TO)

**Pterion fracture** — **middle meningeal artery** → **extradural haematoma** (TO)

**Others** — asterion · lambda · **bregma (coronal + sagittal sutures)** · stephanion · glabella · opisthion · inion (TO)

**Superior sagittal sinus** — usually **slightly right** of the sagittal suture (TO)

![Craniometric points](../../TO notes/attachments/TOn-notes-anatomy-of-the-brain-aug-2026-p06-f016.jpg)

### Homunculus

**Large areas** — hand (thumb), face, lips, tongue, eyes · small: trunk, legs (TO)

**Layout** — **leg + genitals medial** (interhemispheric) · **face + hand lateral** → explains deficits by arterial territory (TO)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Cerebral aneurysms

| Type | Description | Common site (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **Saccular (berry)** | Congenital wall weakness | **AComA, PComA, MCA bifurcation** |
| **Fusiform** | Diffuse dilatation | **Basilar** |
| **Dissecting** | Intimal tear, blood in wall | Vertebral / basilar |

**SAH presentation** — **thunderclap headache** · vomiting, photophobia, neck stiffness · **CN III palsy = PComA aneurysm** (TO)

**Investigations** — **CT brain** (SAH) → CT/MR angiography → **DSA = gold standard** (TO)

**Berry aneurysm** — **commonest cause of spontaneous SAH** (TO)

## 5 Management

### Aneurysm treatment

| Approach | Principle (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Microsurgical clipping** | Neck clipped via craniotomy · **gold standard for accessible** lesions |
| **Endovascular coiling (GDC)** | Catheter coil packing · **preferred for posterior circulation / deep** |
| **Flow-diverter stent** | Redirects flow · **wide-neck** aneurysms |

**Adjuncts** — **nimodipine 60 mg 4-hourly × 21 days** (vasospasm) · **SBP < 140** (rebleed) · euvolaemia (TO)

**Triple-H therapy** — **H**ypertension (MAP 90–100) · **H**ypervolaemia · **H**aemodilution (vasospasm prevention) (TO)

## 6 Complications

### Aneurysm / SAH complications

| Complication | Timing / note (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Rebleeding** | **24–48 h** |
| **Vasospasm** | **Days 4–14** |
| Hydrocephalus | Obstructive |
| Seizures · CN palsy | Esp. **oculomotor** |

## How it's asked

**"Cortical surface anatomy — false statement?"** → **"pars opercularis, triangularis, orbitalis lie in inferior temporal gyrus"** (TO)

**"Central sulcus joins Sylvian fissure in?"** → **\~2%** (TO)

**"Cerebellar peduncles connect to?"** → **superior–midbrain · middle–pons · inferior–medulla** (TO)

**"Metencephalon gives rise to?"** → **pons + cerebellum** (TO)

**"Circumventricular organs?"** → **"Medimix SOAP"** (TO)

**"Pterion fracture → ?"** → **middle meningeal artery → extradural haematoma** (TO)

**"Substantia nigra lesion?"** → **Parkinsonism** (TO)

**"Commonest artery in cerebral infarct?"** → **MCA** (TO)

**"Dysphagia, hoarseness, Horner, contralateral pain/temp loss?"** → **PICA (Wallenberg)** (TO)

**"Basilar artery occlusion?"** → **locked-in syndrome** (TO)

**"Commonest berry aneurysm site?"** → **AComA > PComA > MCA bifurcation** (TO)

**"Nimodipine regimen after SAH?"** → **60 mg 4-hourly × 21 days** (TO)

**"Ophthalmoplegia + facial infection?"** → **cavernous sinus thrombosis** (TO)
