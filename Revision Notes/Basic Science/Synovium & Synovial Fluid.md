# Synovium & Synovial Fluid

*Basic Science · 104 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Synovium

**Function** — nutrient exchange between blood and joint fluid

**Structure** — vascularised, porous connective tissue · ✓ **no basement membrane**

| Synovial cell | Origin / features | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Type A** | **Macrophage-derived** · non-fixed · antigen-presenting · superficial | ✓ **Phagocytosis** |
| **Type B** | **Fibroblast-like** · rich RER, dendritic processes · deeper | ✓ **Produce synovial fluid** — hyaluronic acid, fibronectin, collagen |
| Type C | Intermediate · unknown origin/function | ? multipotent precursor of A or B |

### Synovial cells & lubrication

**Synovial fluid** — **ultrafiltrate of plasma** regulated by synovium · **~2 mL** in healthy knee · lubricates + **nourishes cartilage by diffusion**

| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| **Hyaluronan** | Synthesis needs **uridine diphosphoglucose dehydrogenase** |
| ✓ **Lubricin** | Glycoprotein (HA-binding) · **↓ coefficient of friction** · made by **superficial-zone chondrocytes** · deficiency → early arthritis |
| **Urokinase-type plasminogen activator** | Activates plasmin → **prevents intra-articular clot** (fibrinolysis) |
| Collagenases · prostaglandins | — |
| Proteolipids | Boundary lubrication |

| ✗ Wrong option | Actual role |
|---|---|
| Decorin | Commonest tendon proteoglycan · regulates collagen fibril diameter |
| Scleraxis | Tendon/ligament transcription factor · inhibits osteoblastogenesis |
| Aggrecan | Cartilage swelling pressure |
| Cathepsin | Osteoclast protease at ruffled border |

**Biomechanics** — ✓ **non-Newtonian** · viscosity not constant, not linear · **viscosity ↑ as shear rate ↓**

**Lipohaemarthrosis (supine)** — layer 1 **fat** · layer 2 **serum/synovial fluid** (✓ **uPA → inhibits clot**) · layer 3 **RBCs** (don't form fibrin clot)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Synovial fluid analysis in OA (TO)

| Parameter (TO) | Normal | **OA** | Gout | RA | Pyogenic | TB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Appearance | Straw/clear yellow | **Clear yellow** | Yellow–turbid milky | Yellow, cloudy | Purulent | Yellow, turbid |
| Viscosity | Normal | ✓ **Normal** | ↓ | ↓ | ↓ | ↓ |
| WBC/mm³ | ≤200 | ✓ **≤2000** | 2000–50,000 | 2000–50,000 | **>50,000** | 10,000–20,000 |
| PMN | <20% | ✓ **<20%** | 60–70% | 50–60% | **90%** | 60% |
| Crystals | — | — | **Urate** | — | — | — |
| Glucose | Slightly < plasma | — | ↓ | ↓ | ↓↓ | ↓↓ |

**OA fluid** — ✓ **normal viscosity, <20% neutrophils**\
✗ Wrong options: increased viscosity · WBC >2000 · decreased viscosity **(TO)**

## How it's asked

**"Cell producing synovial fluid?"** → **type B synovial cell**\
✗ Wrong options: type A (phagocytic) · fibrochondrocyte

**"Synovial component most reducing friction?"** → **lubricin**\
✗ Wrong options: decorin, scleraxis, aggrecan, cathepsin

**"Lipohaemarthrosis layers — true?"** → **layer 2 contains uPA, inhibits intra-articular clot**

**"OA synovial fluid — true statement?"** → **normal viscosity with <20% neutrophils** **(TO)**
