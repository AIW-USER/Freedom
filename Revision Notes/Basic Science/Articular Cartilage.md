## 1 Basics

### Forms of cartilage

| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| **Hyaline / articular** | Joint surfaces |
| **Fibroelastic** | Meniscus |

### Composition & function (aggrecan, water, type II collagen)

**Function** — ↓ friction · distributes load · **stress-shielding of the solid matrix** (high water, incompressible water, PG–collagen organisation) · **anisotropic**\
✗ Wrong options: isotropic

**% by weight** — **water > collagen > proteoglycan > non-collagenous protein > cells**

| Component | Amount | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Water** | **65–80%** of wet weight · **80% superficial, 65% deep** | Load-dependent deformation · ↓ with age · **↑ in OA** |
| **Collagen** | 10–20% · **type II = 90–95%** (minor V, VI, IX, X, XI) | **Tensile strength** · framework · **resists swelling** from aggrecan osmotic pressure |
| **Proteoglycans** | 10–15% | **Compressive strength** · attract water · fluid/electrolyte balance |
| **Chondrocytes** | **~2% of volume** (OB) · "<10%" / "up to 5%" (TO) | Make collagen, PG, enzymes · only cell in matrix |

**Type I collagen gives tensile strength** — **false (TO)** · it is **type II**

**Type X collagen** — **calcified cartilage** (mineralisation) · ↑ in **early OA** at subchondral interface (strain >2.5% induces Col X mRNA)

**Type IV collagen** — **basal lamina**\
✗ Wrong options: calcified cartilage

### Proteoglycans

**Aggrecan** — **most responsible for hydrophilic behaviour** · GAGs (**chondroitin sulfate, keratan sulfate**) on a core protein → aggrecan → **link protein** binds it to **hyaluronic acid** → **proteoglycan aggregate**

**Negative charge** — sulfated GAG chains → osmotic swelling pressure · holds **50× its weight** in water · swelling restrained ~20% by type II collagen · MW ~1,000,000\
✗ Wrong options: positive

![Aggrecan aggregate on hyaluronic acid](../../MCQ/attachments/q4453-illa-aggrecan-image.jpg)

| Molecule | Role |
|---|---|
| **Aggrecan** | ✓ **Hydrophilicity, swelling pressure** |
| Decorin, biglycan, fibromodulin | Small PGs · bind type II collagen · matrix stabilisation (decorin organises collagen) |
| Versican | Cell-surface PG |
| Matrilin-1 | Cartilage tissue organisation |
| Type IX collagen | Stability · links matrix molecules to type II |
| COMP | Regulates chondrocyte proliferation |
| Osteocalcin | Most prevalent non-collagenous protein of **bone** |

### Structure & zones

![Zones of articular cartilage](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9017-cartilage-layers.jpg)

| Zone | Collagen | Chondrocytes | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lamina splendens** | Cell-free, random flat fibril bundles | None | Surface layer |
| **Superficial (tangential, zone 1)** | **Parallel** to surface (resists shear) | **Flattened**, parallel to tidemark | **Highest collagen, lowest PG**, high water · thinnest · **only zone with progenitor cells** |
| **Transitional / intermediate (zone 2)** | Oblique / **random**, larger fibres | Round, active | **Thickest** · more PG |
| **Deep / radial / basal (zone 3)** | **Perpendicular**, **crosses tidemark**, largest fibrils | **Round, in columns** | **Highest PG, lowest water** (lowest collagen) |
| **Tidemark** | Basophilic line | — | Boundary **uncalcified / calcified** cartilage |
| **Calcified (zone 4)** | Type X | Small, metabolically inactive | Anchors to subchondral bone |

**Tidemark (TO)** — lies between **radial (deep) and calcified zone** · part of zone 4 \
✗ Wrong options: calcified/subchondral · superficial/transitional

**Tidemark (Orthobullets)** — separates true articular cartilage from remnant of **cartilage anlage** · divides nutritional sources · found only in joints, most prominent in adults

**Water vs depth (TO)** — as PG ↑ from superficial → deep, **water ↓**

**Mechanical descriptors (TO)** — **anisotropic** (varies with direction of load) · **biphasic** (solid + fluid) · **viscoelastic** (deforms under load, recovers on release)

**Coefficient of friction** — **\~0.003** · smoother than lubricated metal (TO)

**Minor collagens (TO)** — **type IX cross-links type II** · **type XI regulates fibril diameter** · **type X near calcified / hypertrophic zone**

**Composition per TO** — water **74%** · collagen 15% · PG 10% · chondrocytes **1%** (TO)

### Nourishment, growth factors

**Avascular** — fed by **synovial fluid** (surface) + **subchondral bone** (base) · ATP by **glycolysis**

**Immature cartilage** — has stem cells · mature does not

| Growth factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **TGF-β1** | **Most investigated** · ↑ PG + ECM synthesis · ↓ IL-1 + MMP catabolism · **synovial proliferation + fibrosis** · osteophytes |
| b-FGF (FGF-2) | ↑ chondrocyte DNA synthesis, PG · but ↑ inflammation, osteophytes · no defect healing |
| **IGF-1** | ↑ DNA + matrix synthesis (adult) · ↓ synovial inflammation · **additive with TGF-β** · ↓ in OA |
| PDGF | Laceration healing (from PRP data) · **no adverse or positive effect in vivo** |
| **IL-1** | Catabolic · ↑ **MMP**, COX-2, nitric oxide synthase |
| Glucosamine | Anabolic · prevents IL-1β-induced ↓ PG synthesis |

## 2 Mechanism

### Response to loading & mechanotransduction

| Stimulates matrix synthesis | Suppresses synthesis, ↑ chondrolysis |
|---|---|
| **Cyclic** stress **1–5 MPa** | Excess **>5 MPa** |
| Moderate frequency **0.1–1 Hz** | **Static** <0.01 Hz |
| Low rate <1000 MPa/s | High rate >1000 MPa/s |

**Mechanosensors** — **primary cilia** (chondrocytes + osteoblasts) · transduction via **integrins**

**Marathon runner, cartilage 2× thick** — ✓ **chondrocyte modulation via mechanotransduction**

**Moderate running** — ↑ thickness · ✓ **↑ GAG** production (T1 gadolinium MRI) · lateral femoral GAG > medial

**Strenuous loading** — thinning + PG loss

**Immobilisation / NWB** — thinning, softening, ↓ PG · **only in the off-loaded limb**\
✗ Wrong options: contralateral hypertrophy

### Lubrication (lubricin, boundary, fluid film)

| Type | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Elastohydrodynamic** | **Main** during dynamic function · elastic deformation + thin fluid film |
| **Boundary** | Non-deformable surfaces, lubricant partially separates · **lubricin** (superficial zone protein) + HA |
| Boosted | Fluid trapped in pools between contact regions |
| Hydrodynamic | Fluid separates sliding surfaces |
| Weeping | Fluid exuded under load → hydrostatic separation |
| Squeeze film | Fluid slowly squeezed out from between surfaces |

**Lubricin** — mucinous glycoprotein · binds **hyaluronic acid** → **boundary** layer \
✗ Wrong options: vitronectin (doesn't bind HA)

**Fluid-film in MoM THA** — ↑ by **larger head**, slight **radial clearance** (most important), low roughness · **complete congruence prevents fluid film**

**Wear mechanisms** — adhesion · abrasion · transfer · fatigue · third body

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Aging vs osteoarthritis changes

| | **Aging** | **Osteoarthritis** |
|---|---|---|
| **Water** | **↓** | **↑** (→ ↑ permeability, ↓ strength) |
| Modulus / stiffness | **↑** (less elastic, brittle) | **↓** (more elastic) · ↓ tensile + shear + compressive modulus |
| Chondrocytes | Fewer but larger (hypocellular) | Clusters / clones (stage 2) |
| GAG ratio | **↑ keratan sulfate : chondroitin** (KS ↑ until ~30 yr, CS ↓; C4S → C6S) | **↑ chondroitin 4-sulfate : keratan sulfate** |
| Proteoglycans | ↓ synthesis, ↓ size · ↑ decorin | **↑ degradation** · unbound from HA · **aggrecan ↑ early, ↓ late** |
| Collagen | **↑ cross-linking** (passive glycation) | **Disorganised** (↑ collagenase) |
| AGEs | **↑** (→ ↑ stiffness, brittleness, fatigue failure) | AGE accumulation thought to lead to knee/ankle OA |

**Common to both** — ✓ **↓ proteoglycan content**

**OA collagen** — ↓ collagen content (TO, FNB 2016) · "relative ↑ collagen concentration from PG loss" (TO second key) — read the options

**AGEs** — non-enzymatic glycation (glucose, fructose, ribose + lysine/arginine) · **pentosidine** · cross-link type II collagen · ↓ chondrocyte response to TGF-β, IGF-1, BMP-7

### OA stages

| Stage | Event |
|---|---|
| **1 (early)** | Matrix disruption → **↑ water** (hallmark), ↓ PG aggregation, ↓ aggrecan → ↓ stiffness |
| **2** | Chondrocytes detect damage → mediators → **↑ proliferation (clusters/clones)** + ↑ matrix synthesis **and** ↑ catabolism |
| **3** | ↓ proliferative + anabolic response → visible cartilage loss → clinical OA |

**Initiators** — inflammation · overload · ↓ matrix production

**Microscopy** — fibrillation of superficial + transitional zones → **tidemark breached by vessels** → subchondral remodelling

**Late / terminal** — water ↓ with degradation

**Molecular** — Wnt-induced signal protein 1 ↑ proteases · ↓ response to TGF-α, IGF-1 · ROS, senescence

## 5 Management

### Healing

**Deep laceration (through tidemark into subchondral bone)** — haematoma → marrow MSCs → **fibrocartilage (type I collagen)** · poor wear, ↓ stiffness

**Superficial laceration (above tidemark)** — chondrocyte proliferation but **no healing** (avascular)

## 6 Complications

### Post-traumatic OA & inflammatory mediators

**Initiating event** — ✓ **chondrocyte apoptosis** along superficial matrix cracks

**Direct mediator of chondrocyte death** — ✓ **caspases** (mainly **caspase-3**) · aspartate-specific cysteine proteases

**Cytokines after intra-articular fracture** — **IL-1β, TNF-α, nitric oxide, MMPs, aggrecans, DAMPs**

**Neutrophil recruitment** — ✓ **IL-8** (with IL-6) · IL-6 → MMPs, hypertrophy (STAT3/ERK)\
✗ Wrong options: IL-10 anti-inflammatory · RANKL osteoclasts

### Intervertebral disc degeneration

**DDD** — ✓ **↑ MMP-1**, **↓ aggrecan/PG**, **↓ water** → ↓ osmotic pressure → disc height loss · **↓ T2 signal** ("dark disc")
