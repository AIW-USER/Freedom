# Hyperparathyroidism

*Basic Science · 45 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **0.1%** · **women** > men · with malignancy = most cases of **hypercalcaemia**

**Cause (primary)** — **single adenoma 80–90%** · hyperplasia 10%

### Pathophysiology

**PTH acts indirectly** — binds **osteoblast** receptor → RANKL + M-CSF → osteoclasts → excess resorption · **cortical > cancellous**

**PTH effects (OB)** — ↑ renal tubular Ca reabsorption · ↑ osteoclast activity · ↑ active vitamin D → ↑ gut Ca

### Classification

| Type | Cause | Ca | Phos | PTH |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Adenoma / hyperplasia | **↑** | **↓** | **↑** |
| **Secondary** | Compensation for **↓ Ca / ↑ phosphate** — **CKD** (renal osteodystrophy), **malabsorption (coeliac)**, vit D deficiency | **↓ / N** | ↑ (renal) / ↓ (malabsorption) | **↑** |
| **Tertiary** | Autonomous glands after long secondary HPT / renal transplant | **↑** | **↑** | **↑** |
| Malignancy (PTHrP) | Ectopic | ↑ | ↓ | **↓** |

### Genes & lab markers

**Serum** — ↑ ALP · normal anion-gap metabolic acidosis (↓ HCO₃ reclamation)

**Urine (primary)** — **hypercalciuria (stones)** · ↑ cAMP

**ECG** — **short QT**

## 3 Presentation

**"Stones, bones, groans"** — renal stones · bone pain · constipation · weakness · often asymptomatic · rare 2° HTN · **fragility fracture** with abnormal labs

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Finding | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Subperiosteal resorption** | **Radial side of middle phalanges**, phalangeal tufts, distal clavicle, proximal tibia/humerus/femur |
| **"Salt and pepper" skull** | Calvarial trabecular resorption |
| **Rugger-jersey spine** | Sclerotic endplate bands (excess unmineralised osteoid) — **distinctive for HPT (TO)** |
| **Brown tumours** | Lytic, expansile → pathological fracture · **osteitis fibrosa cystica** (jaw) |
| Chondrocalcinosis | Menisci, articular cartilage |
| Looser zones | Renal osteodystrophy (osteomalacia) |

### Brown tumour histology

**Features** — **bland spindled fibrous stroma** + **small multinucleated giant cells** · **haemorrhage + haemosiderin** · variable lytic/dense multiple lesions

**vs GCT** — GCT: larger giant cells, round stromal nuclei identical to giant-cell nuclei

**vs myeloma/metastases** — serum markers (Ca, PTH) distinguish

## 5 Management

**Acute hypercalcaemia** — **IV fluids + loop diuretics**

**Symptomatic** — **parathyroidectomy** → watch **postop hypocalcaemia** (numbness, tingling, cramps) → **IV calcium gluconate**

## 6 Complications

**Peptic ulcer** (↑ gastrin) · **pancreatitis** (↑ lipase) · CNS (anxiety, confusion, coma) · **osteoporosis/fragility fractures**

## X vs Y

| Lab panel | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| ↑ PTH, ↑ ALP, ↑ Ca, ↓ PO₄, normal renal function | **Primary HPT** |
| ↑ PTH, ↑ ALP, ↓ Ca, ↑ PO₄ | Secondary (renal) |
| ↑ PTH, ↑ Ca, ↑ PO₄ | Tertiary |
| Normal PTH, **↓ ALP**, ↑ Ca, ↑ PO₄, ↑ urinary phosphoethanolamine | Hypophosphatasia |
| ↓ PTH, normal ALP, ↓ Ca, ↑ PO₄ | Hypoparathyroidism |

## How it's asked

**"Rugger-jersey spine X-ray characteristic of (TO)?"** → **hyperparathyroidism**

**"Dialysis patient, Looser zone, rugger jersey, salt-and-pepper skull — labs EXCEPT?"** → **hypophosphataemia** (phosphate ↑ in renal osteodystrophy)

**"25-yr-old, brown tumours, normal GFR — labs?"** → **↑ PTH, ↑ ALP, ↑ Ca, ↓ PO₄ (primary)**

**"Coeliac sprue, brown tumour fracture — expected?"** → **↑ PTH**\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ Ca, ↑ 1,25-D, ↑ PO₄, ↓ ALP

**"66-yr-old, multiple variable tibial lesions, spindle stroma + small giant cells + haemosiderin?"** → **hyperparathyroidism (brown tumours)**\
✗ Wrong options: myeloma, mets, GCT

**"Fragility fracture, ↑ Ca, ↑ ALP, ↓ PO₄, ↑ PTH — aetiology?"** → **PTH adenoma**\
✗ Wrong options: age-related osteoporosis (normal labs)
