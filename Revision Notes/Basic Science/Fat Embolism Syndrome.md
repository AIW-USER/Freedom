# Fat Embolism Syndrome

*Basic Science · 34 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **3–4%** isolated long-bone fracture · **10–15%** polytrauma · ~**90%** of long-bone fractures have some fat emboli (subclinical ↓ PaO₂ in almost all)

**Mortality** — up to **15%**

**Incidence — two sources** — **3–4%** isolated long bone (Orthobullets) · **2–4%** isolated femur, up to **10%** multiple long-bone / pelvis (TO) — read the options

**Mortality — two sources** — up to **15%** (Orthobullets) · **5–20% (avg 10%)** (TO) — read the options · fat embolism itself 65–99% (TO)

**History (TO)** — **Zenker 1862** (autopsy) · **von Bergmann 1873** (first clinical diagnosis) · Sevitt: subclinical / non-fulminant / fulminant

**Severe FES risk (TO)** — high-velocity trauma · **surgical delay > 10–12 h** · fixing multiple fractures in one sitting · lung contusion

**Causes** — long-bone fractures (femur, tibia, pelvis) · **IM nailing** · **hip & knee arthroplasty** · rare: crush, burns, marrow biopsy/transplant, liposuction, CPR, haemoglobinopathy, steroids, pancreatitis, fatty liver **(TO)**

**Risk factors (TO)** — young age · polytrauma, multiple fractures · **closed** fractures · **conservative** treatment of long-bone fractures · IM nailing: over-zealous nailing, reaming, ↑ reaming speed, ↑ nail–cortex gap

### Pathophysiology

| Theory | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Mechanical** | Marrow fat droplets enter venous system (shear of intraosseous vessels, reaming) → obstruct pulmonary vessels |
| **Biochemical** | ✓ **Lipoprotein lipase → free fatty acids + glycerol** → endothelial toxicity → cytokines → **ARDS-like** picture · ↓ surfactant |

**Arthroplasty emboli** — **fat + marrow** (obstructive, act like PE) vs free fat of fractures (chemical injury)

## 2 Mechanism

### Intraoperative embolisation in arthroplasty

**Peak intramedullary pressure** — **cement pressurisation + femoral component insertion** (whole canal pressurised) > reaming/broaching

**Uncemented hemiarthroplasty stem insertion → hypotension, hypoxia** — ✓ **intramedullary fat + marrow embolisation**

**TKA** — IM alignment rods → emboli · ✓ **extramedullary guides ↓ embolisation risk** · overdrilling the femoral canal entry ↓ risk

## 3 Presentation

**Onset** — within **24 h** (Orthobullets) · **24–72 h** (OB) · **12–72 h** (TO)

**Classic triad** — **respiratory distress (first) · cerebral signs (confusion) · petechial rash (last)**

**Petechiae** — **axillae**, upper chest, shoulders, **conjunctivae**, oral mucosa · appear within 36 h, gone within a week · RBC extravasation from distended capillaries · pathognomonic **(TO)**

**Other** — tachycardia, tachypnoea, fever (non-specific but near-universal) · jaundice, oliguria **(TO)** · respiratory recovery in ~3 days if no further embolism **(TO)**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnostic criteria

| Major (≥1) | Minor (≥4) |
|---|---|
| **Hypoxaemia (PaO₂ <60 mmHg)** + respiratory signs, radiographic changes | **Tachycardia >110** |
| **CNS depression** (unrelated to head injury) | **Pyrexia >38.5 °C** |
| ✓ **Petechial rash** | **Retinal** fat/petechiae |
| Pulmonary oedema (Orthobullets) | Fat in urine/sputum · renal changes · jaundice |
| — | **Thrombocytopenia** · ↓ Hct/Hb · ↑ ESR |

**Gurd & Wilson (TO)** — **1 major + 4 minor + fat macroglobulinaemia**

**Additional** — PCO₂ >55 · pH <7.3 · RR >35 · dyspnoea · anxiety

| FES criteria (TO) | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Gurd (TO, Tiwari)** | **2 major** or **1 major + 4 minor** |
| **Gurd–Wilson (TO, Mohindra)** | ≥ 1 major + ≥ 4 minor + 1 lab (↓ platelets > 50%, ↓ Hb > 20%, ↑ ESR, fat globules) |
| **Schonfeld fat embolism index** | Petechiae **5** · diffuse CXR infiltrates **4** · hypoxia **3** · fever, tachycardia, confusion 1 each → **score ≥ 5 diagnostic** |
| **Lindeque (respiratory only)** | **PaO₂ < 60** · **PaCO₂ > 55** · **RR > 35** · dyspnoea in sedated patient |
**Earliest vs confirmatory (TO)** — earliest = **fat in urine / sputum** (non-specific) · earliest confirmatory = **retinal lesions** or **fat-laden BAL macrophages** · most significant = **↓ PaO₂ / ↑ A–a gradient**
**Retina (TO)** — cotton-wool spots + flame haemorrhages (**Purtscher retinopathy**), \~50%

### Key radiology findings

**ABG (TO)** — early hypoxia + **hypocarbia, respiratory alkalosis** → later respiratory acidosis · ↑ A–a shunt within 24–48 h without ARDS

**CXR (TO)** — initially normal → diffuse fluffy bilateral infiltrates (**"snowstorm"**), bases + periphery

**CT angiogram** — negative for PE (distinguishes)

**HRCT (TO)** — ground-glass + septal thickening → **"crazy paving"** · CT head = diffuse cerebral oedema · TOE shows fat during nailing

## 5 Management

### Prevention

**Most important** — ✓ **early fracture stabilisation (<24 h)** of long bones\
✗ Wrong options: delay fixation, TO

**Techniques** — narrower-shaft reamers · overdrill femoral entry in TKA · **external fixation** in medically unstable patients

**Nailing precautions (TO)** — **venting hole (4.5 mm) in distal cortex** · canal lavage · **unreamed** nail (TO) · ex-fix first if borderline · **steroids before nailing — no role**
**Ventilation targets (TO)** — FiO₂ < 0.6 · SpO₂ > 90% · pressure-control + PEEP · prone helps oxygenation, no survival benefit

### Treatment

**Supportive (ICU)** — O₂ → **CPAP** (↑ PaO₂ without ↑ FiO₂) → **mechanical ventilation with high PEEP** if FiO₂ >60% + CPAP >10 cm needed **(TO)**

**Fluids** — **albumin** preferred (restores volume, lipophilic) **(TO)** · CVP monitoring (pulmonary HTN, RV failure)

**Not recommended (TO)** — **heparin** (bleeding in polytrauma) · **high-dose steroids** (insufficient evidence)

## 6 Complications

### Bone cement implantation syndrome (TO)

**Definition** — **hypoxia, hypotension** ± **unexpected loss of consciousness**, arrhythmia, ↑ PVR, cardiac arrest around **cementation, prosthesis insertion, reduction or tourniquet deflation** in cemented arthroplasty **(TO)**

**Not a feature** — **hypertension** **(TO)**

**Timing (5 stages)** — femoral reaming · acetabular/femoral cement · prosthesis insertion · joint reduction · (tourniquet deflation in TKA) **(TO)**

**Mechanism** — exothermic cement (up to **96 °C**) expands, traps air + marrow under high intramedullary pressure → embolisation **(TO)**

| BCIS grade (TO) | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **1** | SpO₂ **<94%** or SBP fall **>20%** |
| **2** | SpO₂ **<88%** or SBP fall **>40%** or unexpected LOC |
| **3** | **Cardiovascular collapse → CPR** |

**Risk factors (TO)** — old age · poor reserve · cardiopulmonary disease · **pulmonary hypertension** · osteoporosis · **bony metastases** · hip fracture (**pathological/intertrochanteric**) · **previously uninstrumented canal** · **long-stem** component

**Treatment (TO)** — IV fluids, inotropes, vasopressors → **ECMO** if no recovery

## X vs Y

| | FES | PE (DVT) | BCIS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timing | 12–72 h after fracture/nail | 1–6 wk postop | **Intraop** — cement, stem, reduction, tourniquet release |
| Rash | **Axillary petechiae** | No | No |
| CNS | Confusion | Rare | LOC |
| Test | Clinical · CTA negative | **CTA positive** | Clinical |

## How it's asked

**"Hypotension + hypoxia during uncemented hemi stem insertion?"** → **intramedullary fat and marrow embolisation**\
✗ Wrong options: DVT-PE · MI · fluid deficit

**"Benefit of extramedullary guides in TKA?"** → **↓ embolisation risk**

**"Cemented bipolar, death after hypoxia; marrow emboli in lungs — cause?"** → **femoral component insertion after cement pressurisation**\
✗ Wrong options: reaming · undersized broaches

**"Emboli during TKA composed of?"** → **fat and marrow**

**"Major criterion for fat embolism (TO)?"** → **petechial rash**\
✗ Wrong options: tachycardia, pyrexia, retinal changes (minor)

**"True regarding FES (TO)?"** → **CPAP and PEEP for hypoxia**\
✗ Wrong options: heparin · steroids · delay fixation

**"Bilateral femur + humerus fractures, tachycardia, hypoxia, confusion, axillary petechiae, CTA negative?"** → **fat embolism syndrome**

**"Day after reamed tibial nail: ↑ O₂, delirium, chest rash — mechanism?"** → **lipoprotein lipase activation → free fatty acids**\
✗ Wrong options: tissue factor/collagen (PE) · IgE · plasmin inhibition (TXA) · ↑ surfactant

**"TKR — hypotension + dyspnoea after tourniquet deflation (TO)?"** → **bone cement implantation syndrome**\
✗ Wrong options: fat embolism (IM rod insertion)

**"NOT a component of BCIS (TO)?"** → **hypertension**

**"Earliest confirmatory test for FES?"** → **retinal lesions / fat-laden BAL macrophages** (TO)
