# Median nerve

*Basic Science · 61 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Branches in the hand (TO)

**Carpal tunnel** — median nerve enters hand deep to flexor retinaculum → divides into **lateral** and **medial** divisions **(TO)**

| Division (TO) | Supplies |
|---|---|
| **Lateral (stouter)** | **Thenar 2½: APB, opponens pollicis, superficial head FPB** · **1st lumbrical** · sensation thumb + radial index |
| **Medial** (mainly sensory) | Rest of lateral 3½ digits · **2nd lumbrical** |

**Recurrent motor branch** — innervates **thenar muscles** → ✓ **at risk excising a thenar mass** (e.g. lipoma)\
✗ Wrong options: deep ulnar · AIN · palmar cutaneous ulnar · no "recurrent ulnar branch"

**AIN** — FPL, pronator quadratus, FDP index (± middle) · terminal branch → wrist capsule only

### Median nerve palsy — thenar function & opposition (TO)

**Loss of opposition (TO)** — ✓ **median nerve**\
✗ Wrong options: ulnar · radial · medial division

**Thumb abductors (TO)** — ✓ **median + radial nerve both** (APB — median · APL — radial/PIN)

## 3 Presentation

### High median palsy vs AIN

| Lesion | Attempted fist |
|---|---|
| **High median (arm)** | ✓ **Thumb + index can't flex** (FPL, FDP + FDS index lost) — "benediction" attitude · middle partly flexes |
| AIN syndrome | Loss of **DIP flexion thumb, index, middle** (FPL, FDP) — no sensory loss |
| PIN syndrome | Finger drop at MCPs, wrist extends in **radial deviation** |
| Radial nerve (arm) | **Wrist + finger drop** |
| Median at wrist (CTS) | Thenar weakness + sensory loss, long flexors intact |

## How it's asked

**"Excising thenar lipoma — nerve at risk?"** → **recurrent motor branch of median**

**"Can't flex thumb + index on making a fist?"** → **median nerve lesion in the arm**\
✗ Wrong options: AIN · PIN · CTS

**"Nerve supply to thumb abductors (TO)?"** → **median and radial both**

**"Nerve injury causing loss of opposition (TO)?"** → **median**
