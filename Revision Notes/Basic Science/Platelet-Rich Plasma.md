# Platelet-Rich Plasma

*Basic Science · 80 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & biology

**PRP** — plasma from own blood enriched with **autologous platelets** · controversial use to stimulate bone + soft-tissue healing

**Optimal concentration** — **3–5× whole blood** · **>5× inhibits healing** · not 10–15× (OB) · therapeutic working definition **≥1 million platelets/mL** **(TO)**

**Yield (TO)** — **30 mL** venous blood → **3–5 mL** PRP **(TO)**

**Proposed actions** — ↑ ECM deposition · ↓ pro-apoptotic signals · ↓ joint inflammation · acts via **growth factors**\
✗ Wrong options: aggrecan / structural polypeptide production

**Knee OA mechanism** — ✓ **↓ COX-2 and NF-κB** + **↑ hyaluronic acid by synoviocytes** · ↓ cartilage degradation · ↑ chondrocyte proliferation\
✗ Wrong options: NSAIDs (no HA ↑) · steroid (nuclear receptor) · HA injection (rooster comb/bacterial lubricant)

### Genes & lab markers

**Growth factors released** — **PDGF** · **TGF-β** · **VEGF** · **IGF-1** · **EGF** · **CTGF** · **FGF-2** · >30 bioactive proteins; 7 fundamental GFs **(TO)**

**Cell adhesion molecules (TO)** — **fibrin, fibronectin, vitronectin** **(TO)**

| Platelet granule (TO) | Contents |
|---|---|
| **α-granules** (unique to platelets, most abundant, **50–80/platelet**) | ✓ **Growth factors** (VEGF, FGF) · αIIbβ3, P-selectin · PF4, fibrinogen · vWF, factor V · chemokines (CXCL1, IL-8) · >300 proteins |
| **Dense (δ) granules** (2nd) | **Serotonin, histamine** · adenine nucleotides · polyphosphates · **calcium** |

**Degranulation (TO)** — secretory proteins activated (histones, carbohydrate side chains) → bind receptors on MSCs, osteoblasts, fibroblasts, endothelial + epidermal cells **(TO)**

**pH dependence (TO)** — ✓ GF effect **IS pH dependent** · PDGF highest at **pH 5.1** · TGF-β lower at pH 5.0 than 7.1/7.6 (Liu) **(TO)**

**Release kinetics (TO)** — **~70% of GFs in first 10 min** after activation · **~100% within 1 h** · small amounts over platelet lifespan (**8–10 days**) **(TO)**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Types of PRP (TO)

| Type (TO) | Platelets / leukocytes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Pure PRP / leukocyte-poor (LP-PRP)** | High / minimal | Tendon, cartilage — less inflammation · neutrophils ~**100 cells/mL** |
| **Leukocyte-rich (LR-PRP / L-PRP)** | High / high | Acute inflammatory response · **↑ IL-1β, TNF-α, MMPs** antagonise anabolic effect · neutrophils ~**5000 cells/mL** · most commercial products |
| **Platelet-rich fibrin (PRF)** | — | **No anticoagulant** → fibrin scaffold → slow sustained GF release |
| **Autologous conditioned plasma (ACP)** | ✓ **High platelet, low leukocyte** | Leukocyte-poor form · tendon, cartilage, OA |
| **PRGF** | Balanced GFs, minimal leukocytes | Controlled healing |

### Preparation (TO)

**Anticoagulant** — ✓ **citrate-based**: **acid citrate dextrose (ACD) A/B** · CTAD · sodium citrate → prevent platelet activation, preserve function\
✗ Wrong options: **EDTA damages platelet membrane** (EDTA still listed as used by one TO source) · heparin · hirudin **(TO)**

**Differential centrifugation** — sediments constituents by specific gravity · **soft spin 1500 rpm × 5 min → hard spin 6000 rpm × 15 min** **(TO)**

| Method (TO) | Steps | PRP location |
|---|---|---|
| **PRP method** | ACD tubes · **don't chill** · soft spin → transfer supernatant → hard spin | **Lower 1/3 = PRP**, upper 2/3 = PPP · platelet pellet at bottom → resuspend in 2–4 mL plasma |
| **Buffy coat method** | Store **20–24 °C** · **high-speed** spin first · remove supernatant · low spin / leucocyte filter to remove WBC | Bottom **RBC** · ✓ **middle = platelets + WBC (buffy coat)** · top **PPP** |

**Activation** — ✓ **calcium chloride** (Orthobullets/OB3629 key) · **thrombin + calcium chloride** both initiate activation (OB, TO)\
✗ Wrong options: citrate dextrose (= anticoagulant) · TGF-β, IL-8, PDGF (= released products)

**Unanswered TO item (TO24050608)** — "agent commonly used to activate platelets?" · options TGF-α / IL-2 / **calcium chloride** / **thrombin** · **answer key not provided by source** · sources support both CaCl₂ and thrombin — read the options

**Point of care** — in-office / in-OR devices, **minutes**\
✗ Wrong options: 24-h lab processing

**Dosing (TO)** — up to **3 injections, 3–6 wk apart**, **5–10 mL** **(TO)**

### Regulation

**FDA** — PRP + preparation devices **"cleared" (510(k)), not "approved"** · not a drug · **not FDA-approved for OA** **(TO)** · blood products exempt from HCT/P pathway (21 CFR 1271) → not regulated like other cellular/tissue products (OB) · one TO table labels PRP "FDA approved" vs ACS not — read the options

## 5 Management

### Clinical evidence

| Indication | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Lateral epicondylosis (tennis elbow)** | ✓ **Potential benefit** — PROs + pain |
| **BPTB donor sites** (patellar + tibial plug) | ✓ **Improved PROs of knee function**, ↓ patellar tendon gap |
| ACL graft ligamentisation / maturation | Not supported |
| **Rotator cuff repair augmentation** | **No added benefit** (possibly detrimental) · safe |
| Meniscal repair | No clear evidence |
| **Achilles tendinopathy** (midsubstance/insertional) | Not supported |
| Acute ligament/tendon/muscle injury, chronic tendinopathy | No consensus |
| **Osteoarthritis** | No consensus, lack long-term follow-up |
| Fracture healing / fusion | Limited evidence, some detrimental |

**TO indication list** — knee OA · partial tears (RC, hamstring, quadriceps, common extensor/flexor origin, triceps) · Achilles + chronic tendinosis · meniscus grade 1–2 ± parameniscal cyst · non-union / delayed union · partial ACL / ATFL tears **(TO)**

### Orthokine — autologous conditioned serum (ACS) (TO)

**Orthokine** — blood incubated with **pyrogen-free glass spheres** at **37 °C × 24 h** → leukocytes stimulated → ✓ **↑ IL-1 receptor antagonist (IL-1Ra)** + IGF-1, PDGF, TGF-β1 → cell-free serum injected\
✗ Wrong options: platelet concentration · stem cells · steroid **(TO)**

**Process (TO)** — 10–60 mL blood · spin 3000–3500 rpm · serum stored **−20 °C** · injected once–twice weekly · hypoxia-like (HIF-1α) response, not EPO **(TO)**

**WADA (TO)** — not banned; may need **TUE** (M1/M2 blood manipulation categories) **(TO)**

## ACS (Orthokine) vs PRP (TO)

| | **ACS** | **PRP** |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Whole blood + glass spheres, incubated | Centrifuged whole blood |
| Mechanism | **↑ IL-1Ra** (anti-inflammatory) | Platelet GFs (PDGF, TGF-β, VEGF) |
| Best for | OA, lumbar stenosis, disc prolapse, muscle injury | Tendon, ligament, cartilage, post-surgical |
| Processing | **24 h** incubation | **15–30 min** |
| Injections | Fewer | Multiple over weeks |
| FDA (per TO table) | Not approved | "Approved" (✗ Wrong options: per other TO key) |
