# General and Regional Anesthesia in Orthopaedics

*Basic Science · 39 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Components of anaesthesia

**Five A's** — amnesia · anxiolysis · analgesia · akinesia · attenuation of autonomic responses

### General anaesthesia

**Inhalational** — isoflurane, sevoflurane, desflurane · **nitrous oxide → gaseous abdominal (bowel) distension** (diffuses in, can't leave) → hinders fluoroscopy in pelvic/spine surgery

**IV non-opioids** — propofol, etomidate, benzodiazepines, dexmedetomidine, ketamine · **opioids** — fentanyl family, morphine, hydromorphone

| Neuromuscular blocker | Agents | Reversal |
|---|---|---|
| **Depolarising** (depolarises + blocks ACh-R) | **Succinylcholine** (short only) | — |
| **Non-depolarising** | Intermediate: **rocuronium, vecuronium**, atracurium, cisatracurium · long: pancuronium | **Neostigmine** · **sugammadex** (rocuronium) |

## 5 Management

### Upper-limb blocks

| Block | Site | Use | Key complication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Interscalene** | Brachial plexus between **anterior + middle scalenes**, cricoid level | **Shoulder**, upper arm, elbow | **Phrenic block → hemidiaphragm paresis** (very common side effect) · **sensory neuropathy / temporary paraesthesia ≤6 mo (2.3%)** = commonest complication · Horner's · RLN · pneumothorax · vertebral artery · epidural/spinal spread |
| **Supraclavicular** | Above clavicle | Lower humerus → hand | **Hemidiaphragmatic paralysis > infraclavicular** · pneumothorax · subclavian artery |
| Infraclavicular | Below clavicle | Lower humerus → hand | Less phrenic involvement |
| Axillary | Axilla | Elbow, forearm, wrist, hand | — |
| **Bier block** | IV regional (lidocaine distal IV, tourniquet) | Short (<60 min) hand/forearm (CTR) | **Deflate ≥30 min** → avoid LAST |

![Interscalene block](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9075-interscalene-block.jpg)

**Interscalene contraindications** — ✓ **moderate–severe lung disease (FEV1 45%)** (diaphragm paresis ↓ lung volumes ~30%) · contralateral phrenic injury · LA allergy · infection · uncontrolled seizures · coagulopathy · pre-existing nerve injury\
✗ Wrong options: age, aspirin, obesity alone, 1st-degree heart block

**Hemidiaphragm paresis, SpO₂ 97% on 2 L** — ✓ **observe + monitor**\
✗ Wrong options: ABG, CTPA, chest tube, intubation

**Postop dyspnoea after supraclavicular block, normal CXR** — ✓ **hemidiaphragmatic paralysis** (sniff test) · phrenic nerve (C3–5) on anterior scalene

**Interscalene catheter → ptosis, miosis (TO)** — ✓ **Horner's syndrome → remove catheter** (reversible) · stellate ganglion paralysis from high LA concentration **(TO)**

### Lower-limb blocks

| Block | Target / landmark | Use | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lumbar plexus (psoas compartment) | L1–4 (obturator, LFCN, femoral) · 3–5 cm lateral to L4 spinous process | Hip, thigh, knee | Retroperitoneal haematoma, epidural/intrathecal spread, **falls** |
| Posterior quadratus lumborum | Behind QL → thoracolumbar fascia | Hip | Deep, technically hard |
| **Fascia iliaca** | Femoral, obturator, LFCN · 1 cm caudal to lateral ⅓ / medial ⅔ of pubic tubercle–ASIS line | Hip fracture, thigh, knee | ✓ **↓ postoperative delirium in geriatric hip fracture** |
| **Femoral** | Lateral to femoral artery (L2–4) | Knee | **Prolonged quadriceps weakness, falls** |
| **Adductor canal** | Saphenous + nerve to VMO (distal to quad motor branches) | **TKA** | ✓ **= pain control, better mobility** than femoral |
| **iPACK** | Between popliteal artery + posterior capsule | TKA | **Motor-sparing** · US-guided · ✓ **iPACK + ACB > ACB alone** |
| Sciatic | GT–PSIS / GT–sacral hiatus lines | Leg, ankle, foot | Heel ulcers, delayed nerve-injury diagnosis |
| Obturator | 2 cm inferior + 2 cm lateral to pubic tubercle | Adductor spasm, hip OA pain, knee adjunct | Anterior branch → hip/inner thigh · posterior → knee |
| Popliteal | Sciatic ~10 cm above popliteal crease | Foot/ankle (+ saphenous) | Foot drop, pressure sores |
| Saphenous | Behind sartorius | Medial knee/leg/ankle | — |
| Periarticular injection | — | TKA/THA | Better TKA pain control than iPACK (Orthobullets) |

**Geniculate blocks** — chronic OA pain before TKA, not perioperative

### Regional techniques — haematoma block & tourniquet (TO)

**Haematoma block** — LA **injected directly into the fracture site** · distal radius, ankle fracture-dislocation · not "**around nerves at target site**" (false) **(TO)**

| Tourniquet facts (TO) | |
|---|---|
| Cuff | **Widest possible** (arm 10 cm, thigh ≥15 cm) — effective at lower pressure |
| Pressure | Arm SBP + **100** · thigh SBP + **100–150** mmHg · best: **limb occlusion pressure (LOP)** + margin (LOP <130 → +40 · 131–190 → +60 · >190 → +80) |
| Time | **2 h** safe limit · if longer, deflate **10 min** |
| Commonest complication | **Nerve injury** · UL > LL · **radial nerve** most susceptible |
| Nerve injury cause | **Cuff pressure > duration** (✗ Wrong options: "duration more than pressure" (false)) · direct pressure > ischaemia · too low pressure (venous congestion) also |
| Post-tourniquet syndrome | **Ischaemia** · oedema, redness, pain, dysaesthesia, stiffness |
| Relative contraindications | PVD, DVT, severe infection, poor cardiac status · avoid exsanguination in infection/malignancy |
| Prep solutions | Prevent seepage under cuff (chemical burns) |

### Blood management

**Hypotensive epidural anaesthesia (HEA)** — ✓ **best ↓ blood loss + transfusion** in bilateral THA · block to **T2** (cardio-accelerator fibres) → **MAP ~50 mmHg** · bradycardia → low-dose **epinephrine** · normal HR, CVP, SV, CO · usable in HTN, poor cardiac function, CKD · beats spinal, normotensive epidural, hypotensive TIVA, GA

**Other** — preop EPO · autologous donation · acute normovolaemic haemodilution · cell saver

## 6 Complications

### Malignant hyperthermia & local anaesthetic toxicity

| | **Malignant hyperthermia** | **LAST** |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | **AD, RYR1** · triggered by **volatile agents + succinylcholine** | **Intravascular bupivacaine** (Na-channel block) |
| Features | Rigidity, **masseter spasm**, ↑ **EtCO₂**, ↑ temp, tachycardia, dark urine (myoglobin) | CNS: perioral numbness, metallic taste, seizures, coma · CVS: arrhythmia, **asystole/VF** |
| Treatment | Stop triggers, hyperventilate O₂, cool + ✓ **dantrolene** | ✓ **IV 20% lipid emulsion** + ACLS |
| Incidence | 1 : 15,000–50,000 | — |

| Antidote | For |
|---|---|
| Naloxone | Opioids |
| Flumazenil | Benzodiazepines |
| Protamine | Heparin |
| Neostigmine / sugammadex | Non-depolarising NMB (rocuronium) |
| Atropine, isoproterenol | β-blocker bradycardia |

**Bone cement implantation syndrome** — hypotension + hypoxaemia → IV fluids, vasopressors, 100% O₂

**Epidural morphine + steroid paste after laminectomy** — ✓ **surgical site infection**

## How it's asked

**"Interscalene catheter, facial discomfort, ptosis/miosis (TO)?"** → **Horner's — remove catheter**\
✗ Wrong options: facial palsy

**"Most likely complication of interscalene block?"** → **sensory neuropathy**\
✗ Wrong options: motor neuropathy, CRPS, pneumothorax

**"Relative contraindication to interscalene block?"** → **FEV1 45%**\
✗ Wrong options: age, aspirin, obesity, 1st-degree block

**"Most common complication after interscalene block?"** → **temporary paraesthesia up to 6 months**

**"Post-interscalene high-riding diaphragm, SpO₂ 97%?"** → **observe and monitor**

**"Post-supraclavicular block dyspnoea, normal CXR?"** → **hemidiaphragmatic paralysis**\
✗ Wrong options: pneumothorax · LAST

**"Best describes iPACK?"** → **ACB + iPACK > ACB alone**\
✗ Wrong options: femoral branches (ACB) · motor-sacrificing

**"Block C (adductor canal) vs A (femoral)?"** → **equivalent pain control, better mobility**

**"Cardiac arrest during TKA, resuscitated with 20% lipid?"** → **bupivacaine**

**"Family history, rigidity, ↑ EtCO₂ after succinylcholine — antidote?"** → **dantrolene**

**"Nitrous oxide effect?"** → **gaseous abdominal distension**

**"Bilateral THA — best ↓ transfusion?"** → **hypotensive epidural anaesthesia**

**"Epidural morphine + steroid paste after laminectomy — complication?"** → **surgical site infection**

**"False about haematoma block (TO)?"** → **"LA injected around nerves at target site"**

**"False about tourniquet palsy (TO)?"** → **"related more to duration than pressure"**
