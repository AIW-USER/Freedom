# Peroneus tertius

*Basic Science · 78 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy

| | Peroneus tertius |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | With EDL from **distal third of extensor (medial) fibular surface** + anterior intermuscular septum |
| **Insertion** | **Dorsal base of 5th metatarsal** (± fascia over 4th interosseous space) |
| **Action** | ✓ **Dorsiflexion + eversion** (with EDL; also abducts foot) |
| **Innervation** | **Deep peroneal nerve (L5, S1)** · anterior compartment |
| **Arterial supply** | Anterior tibial artery |

![Peroneus tertius](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob10082-peroneus-tertius.jpg)

### Function

**Absent** — in **10%** of population · unique to humans · proximally migrated deep extensor of 5th toe

**Clinical use** — **arthroscopic portal landmark** · insertion point for tendon transfers to assist dorsiflexion

## How it's asked

**"Function of peroneus tertius (portal landmark)?"** → **dorsiflexion and eversion**\
✗ Wrong options: dorsiflexion alone · eversion alone · 5th / lesser toe extension
