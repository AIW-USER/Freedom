# Cerebral Vascular Malformations

*Basic Science · TO notes*

## 1 Basics

### McCormick classification (1966)

| Type | Description | Prevalence (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **AVM** | Direct artery–vein connection · **high flow** | **44–60%** |
| **Cavernous malformation** | Cluster of dilated capillaries ("caverns") | 19–31% |
| Capillary telangiectasia | Dilated capillaries, normal brain between | 4–12% |
| **Developmental venous anomaly** (venous angioma) | Congenital venous drainage pattern | 9–10% |
| Mixed / unclassified | Angiographically occult | \~11% |

**AV fistulas** — artery → vein **without a nidus** · vein of Galen malformation · **dural AVF** · carotid–cavernous fistula (TO)

**Commonest vascular malformation** — **AVM** (TO — verify: DVA usually commonest in autopsy / imaging series) — read the options

### AVM — pathophysiology

**Structure** — **nidus** (tangle of dysplastic vessels) + multiple **feeder arteries** + **red draining veins** (oxygenated) · **no capillary bed** (TO)

**Haemodynamics** — artery \~120 → capillary \~0 → vein 5–15 mmHg · in AVM vein sees **arterial pressure** → dilatation + rupture (TO)

**Origin** — **congenital**, but enlarges and ↑ flow with age (TO)

| Anatomical type | Example (TO) |
|---|---|
| Parenchymal | Cortical / subcortical |
| Dural (AVF) | Carotid–cavernous fistula |
| Mixed | Dural + parenchymal |

**Location** — **supratentorial 85%** (**parietal** commonest lobe) · **posterior fossa 15%** · commonest artery **MCA** (TO)

**Venous drainage** — superficial (cortical → sagittal, transverse, sigmoid) · **deep (vein of Galen, internal cerebral, basal vein of Rosenthal) → higher Spetzler–Martin grade + bleed risk** (TO)

**Epidemiology** — prevalence \~0.14% · mean age **30–35** · slight male predominance (TO)

**HHT (Osler–Weber–Rendu)** — telangiectasia lips / mucosa, epistaxis, GI bleed · **15–20% have cerebral AVMs** (TO)

### Cavernous malformation — basics

**Synonyms** — cavernoma · cavernous angioma / haemangioma (TO)

**Nature** — benign **vascular hamartoma** · sinusoidal channels, **no intervening brain** · **no large feeders or draining veins** · stagnant blood, thrombus of varying age · **low flow** (TO)

**Gross** — **"mulberry"** · **"haemorrhoid of the brain"** · 1–5 cm · may arise de novo, grow, shrink (TO)

| Parameter | Finding (TO) |
|---|---|
| Share of malformations | 5–13% |
| Sites | Supratentorial 48–86% · **brainstem 4–35%** · basal ganglia 5–10% |
| **Multiple** | **23–50%** |
| Spinal CM | **42% have ≥ 1 intracranial lesion** → **brain MRI screening** |
| Risk factor | **Craniospinal radiotherapy** |
| **Familial** | **Autosomal dominant**, 3 genes |

### Dural AVF — basics

**Definition** — **acquired** AV shunt **within dural leaflets** → drains into dural sinus or cortical veins · fed by **meningeal branches** of ICA, ECA, vertebral (TO)

| Parameter | Finding (TO) |
|---|---|
| Incidence | **10–15% of intracranial AVMs** |
| Age | **40–59** (rare in children) |
| Sex | **Female (\~60%)** |
| Predisposition | **Venous sinus thrombosis** |

**Pathogenesis** — sinus thrombosis → ↑ venous pressure → meningeal feeders recruited → shunt → **cortical venous hypertension**, ↓ CSF absorption → ↑ ICP · severe: global oedema, communicating hydrocephalus (TO)

**Mechanisms** — dormant embryonic AV channels · de novo angiogenesis · post-thrombotic recanalisation (TO)

| Site | Note (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Transverse–sigmoid** | **63%**, commonest, slightly left |
| Tentorial / petrosal | 2nd commonest · aggressive |
| **Anterior fossa / ethmoidal** | Often **aggressive** |
| Cavernous sinus | **Carotid–cavernous fistula** |
| Superior sagittal sinus | Cortical reflux |
| Foramen magnum | Circular sinus |

## 3 Presentation

### AVM

| Presentation | Frequency (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Haemorrhage** (ICH / IVH / SAH) | **\~50% — commonest** |
| Seizures | 20–40% |
| Headache / focal deficit (steal, mass effect) | 10–20% |
| Incidental | 10% |

**Bleed risk** — unruptured **2.3%/yr** · after rupture **4.3%/yr** · rebleed highest in **1st year** (TO)

**High-risk features** — **deep venous drainage** · Spetzler–Martin IV/V · **prior haemorrhage** · **associated aneurysm** · female (TO)

**Seizures** — gliotic cortex around nidus · temporal / superficial cortical · **nidus > 3 cm** · post-haemorrhagic scar (TO)

| Associated aneurysm | Share (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Feeder artery** | **75%** |
| Intranidal | 15% |
| Draining vein | 10% |

### Cavernous malformation

| Presentation | Frequency (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Seizures** | **50% — commonest** |
| Haemorrhage | 25% |
| Focal deficit | 25% |
| Hydrocephalus / incidental | 20–50% |

**Epileptogenic zone** — **haemosiderin ring** · 5-yr first-seizure risk: symptomatic \~6%, incidental \~4% (TO)

| Haemorrhage risk | Value (TO) |
|---|---|
| Definition | Symptomatic blood **outside the haemosiderin ring** |
| **First bleed** | **0.08%/yr** (TO — verify: usual quoted 0.25–1%/yr) |
| Rebleed | **5–6× higher** after 1st bleed · declines over time |
| **Brainstem** | **4.4%/yr** recurrent |
| Familial | 4–6%/yr |
| Spinal | 2.1%/yr |

**No ↑ bleed risk with** — sex, size, multiplicity, pregnancy, anticoagulants, activity (TO)

### Dural AVF

| Symptom | Frequency (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Pulsatile tinnitus** | **92% — commonest** |
| Occipital bruit (mastoid) | 89% |
| Headache | 40% |
| Visual loss / papilloedema | 25–30% |

**Bleed type** — **subdural** = "hallmark" of DAVF (TO — verify: cortical-reflux DAVFs usually bleed intracerebral / SAH) · aneurysm → SAH · AVM → ICH (TO)

### Other malformations

| Lesion | Key facts (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Vein of Galen malformation** | AVF of vein of Galen · **neonatal high-output CCF**, hydrocephalus, seizures · prenatal US **midline tubular "keyhole / comet-tail"** · **type III commonest** |
| **DVA (venous angioma)** | Medullary veins → central vein, **normal brain between** · low pressure, no shunt · **"caput medusae"** in **venous phase** |
| **AOVM** | Not seen on DSA (low flow / small) · younger patients |
| **Capillary telangiectasia** | Dilated capillaries, normal brain between · **HHT**, ataxia telangiectasia |
| **Carotid–cavernous fistula** | **Pulsatile proptosis** (✓ commonest cause), chemosis, diplopia, **CN III, IV, VI** palsy, ↑ IOP |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Lesion | Best test | Classic sign (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **AVM** | **DSA (gold standard)** | **Draining veins in arterial phase** · flow voids on T2 ("popcorn / Medusa head" per TO — verify: popcorn = cavernoma, caput medusae = DVA) |
| **Cavernoma** | **MRI GRE / SWI** | **"Popcorn / mulberry"** mixed core + **dark haemosiderin rim** · **DSA usually negative** · CT: calcification |
| **Dural AVF** | **DSA — 6-vessel study** (bilateral ICA + ECA + vertebral) | **Early venous filling in arterial phase** · plain CT / MRI often normal |
| DVA | MRI / DSA | **Caput medusae (venous phase)** |

**MRA for AVM** — non-invasive, shows nidus + feeders **≥ 1 mm** · CTA useful acutely (TO)

### Spetzler–Martin grading (AVM)

| Parameter | Criteria | Points (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **Size** | < 3 / 3–6 / > 6 cm | **1 / 2 / 3** |
| **Eloquence** | Non-eloquent / eloquent | 0 / 1 |
| **Venous drainage** | Superficial / **deep** | 0 / 1 |

**Grades** — I–V (higher = riskier) · **grade VI = inoperable** · **Lawton–Young** adds **age + rupture status** (TO)

### Borden classification (DAVF)

| Type | Drainage | Nature (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Dural sinus, antegrade | Benign |
| **II** | Sinus + **retrograde cortical venous reflux** | Aggressive |
| **III** | **Direct to cortical vein** (no sinus) | Highly aggressive |

### Cognard classification (DAVF)

| Type | Drainage | Nature (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Sinus, antegrade | Benign |
| IIa | Sinus, retrograde | Benign |
| IIb | Sinus antegrade + **cortical reflux** | Aggressive |
| IIa+b | Retrograde + reflux | Aggressive |
| III | Direct cortical venous drainage | Aggressive |
| IV | **Cortical vein ectasia** | Aggressive |
| **V** | **Spinal perimedullary** drainage | Aggressive (myelopathy) |

**Aggressiveness markers** — **cortical venous reflux** (key) · deep drainage · high flow · **tentorial / anterior fossa** site (TO)

**Natural history** — benign: **98% stable at 3 yr** · aggressive (4 yr): 8% bleed, 7% deficit, **10% mortality** (TO)

### Barrow classification (CCF)

| Type | Feeders (TO) |
|---|---|
| **A** | **Direct ICA → cavernous sinus** · **traumatic, high flow** |
| B | Meningeal branches of **ICA** |
| C | Meningeal branches of **ECA** |
| D | ICA + ECA meningeal branches |

## 5 Management

### AVM

```
AVM — Spetzler–Martin grade
├─ I–II → MICROSURGICAL EXCISION
├─ III → MULTIMODAL (surgery + embolisation / SRS)
└─ IV–V → CONSERVATIVE / SELECTIVE
       └─ < 3 cm, deep or eloquent → SRS
```

**Aneurysm + AVM** — ✓ **treat the aneurysm first** (TO)

| Modality | Indication | Key facts (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **Microsurgery** | **Low grade (I–II)**, superficial, non-eloquent · ruptured (with clot evacuation) | **Immediate cure** · sequence: **feeders → nidus → draining vein last** |
| **SRS** (Gamma Knife / CyberKnife) | **< 3 cm, deep, eloquent** | **80% obliteration in 2–3 yr** · **latency 1–3 yr** · radiation necrosis · endothelial damage → thrombosis |
| **Embolisation** | **Adjunct** before surgery / SRS · palliative | **Onyx, NBCA glue** · incomplete, recurrence, multiple sessions |

| Follow-up | Imaging (TO) |
|---|---|
| Surgery | DSA post-op → **1 + 5 yr** |
| SRS | MRI 6-monthly × 3 yr → **DSA to confirm** obliteration |
| Embolisation | MRI + angiography annually |

### Cavernous malformation

| Situation | Recommendation (TO) |
|---|---|
| Asymptomatic / deep | **Observe** · MRI every 2–3 yr |
| **Superficial + symptomatic** (seizure / bleed) | **Excision** |
| **Brainstem — 1 bleed** | **Observe** |
| **Brainstem — ≥ 2 bleeds** / progressive deficit | **Surgery** ("operate when it speaks twice") |
| Temporal lobe + seizures | Excision after **EEG correlation** |
| Deep / eloquent, surgery risky | **SRS** |

**Surgical principles** — least-eloquent corridor · **remove lesion + haemosiderin ring** · **early (< 6 wk) after bleed** · no surgery for deep asymptomatic (TO)

**SRS** — ≈ natural history for 2 yr, ↓ bleeds after 2 yr · **may induce de novo cavernomas** (TO)

**DVA** — **observe** · **surgery contraindicated** unless associated cavernoma / bleed (TO)

**Vein of Galen** — **endovascular embolisation**: types I–III **trans-arterial**, type IV **trans-venous** · surgery only **after 6 months** (TO)

### Dural AVF

| Modality | Key facts (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Manual carotid compression** | Low-flow benign lesions · **opposite hand** (avoid ipsilateral ischaemia) · 10 min/day, ↑ · **22% cure** |
| **Endovascular embolisation** | ✓ **First line** · trans-arterial (NBCA, Onyx, coils) for isolated feeders · **trans-venous preferred with cortical venous reflux** / sinus occlusion · goal: obliterate venous outflow |
| Microsurgery | Failed / inaccessible endovascular · pre-op embolisation · **torrential bleeding on dural opening** |
| SRS | **Adjunct only** for residual feeders · 58–70% obliteration · latency 2–3 yr |

**Treatment priority** — **eliminate cortical venous reflux** (TO)

**CCF** — low flow: conservative / carotid compression · high flow: **trans-venous balloon / coil** · surgery if endovascular fails · **SRS not preferred** (optic apparatus) (TO)

## 6 Complications

**AVM** — **normal perfusion pressure breakthrough** (hyperaemia after resection) · rebleed · seizures · radiation necrosis / cyst · residual nidus → recurrence (TO)

**DAVF** — venous rupture → SDH · deficits (cortical venous hypertension) · CN III, IV, VI palsy (CCF) · stroke (balloon / stent migration) · recurrence if venous outflow not occluded (TO)

## X vs Y

| | AVM | Cavernoma | Dural AVF |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin | **Congenital** | Hamartoma, familial AD | **Acquired** (sinus thrombosis) |
| Flow | **High** | **Low** | Shunt in dura |
| Commonest presentation | **Haemorrhage** | **Seizures** | **Pulsatile tinnitus** |
| Best test | **DSA** | **MRI GRE / SWI** (DSA negative) | **DSA 6-vessel** |
| Key grading | **Spetzler–Martin** | — | **Borden / Cognard** |
| Mainstay | Surgery (low grade) | Observe / excise if symptomatic | **Endovascular** (TO) |

| | Cavernoma | DVA |
|---|---|---|
| Brain between vessels | **None** | **Normal brain** |
| Sign | **Popcorn + haemosiderin rim** | **Caput medusae** (venous phase) |
| Surgery | If symptomatic | **Contraindicated** alone (TO) |

## How it's asked

**"AVM — gold-standard investigation?"** → **DSA (draining veins in arterial phase)** (TO)

**"Spetzler–Martin parameters?"** → **size, eloquence, venous drainage** (TO)

**"AVM with feeder aneurysm — treat first?"** → **aneurysm** (TO)

**"Commonest presentation of cavernoma?"** → **seizures (50%)** (TO)

**"Brainstem cavernoma — surgery when?"** → **≥ 2 haemorrhages or progressive deficit** (TO)

**"Commonest symptom of dural AVF?"** → **pulsatile tinnitus** (TO)

**"Marker of DAVF aggressiveness?"** → **cortical venous reflux** (TO)

**"Commonest cause of pulsatile proptosis?"** → **carotid–cavernous fistula** (TO)

**"'Caput medusae' in venous phase?"** → **developmental venous anomaly — observe** (TO)
