# Multiple Sclerosis

*Basic Science · 63 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & pathophysiology

**Incidence** — 5 / 100,000 (US) · **20–40 yr** · **women > men** · **northern latitudes**

**Risk factors** — stress · smoking · **↓ sunlight / low vitamin D** · not considered hereditary

**Mechanism** — CNS **demyelination + axonal injury** · **T-cell–mediated autoimmune** · genetic + environmental + infectious factors

**Orthopaedic associations** — **↑ fracture risk** (falls + ↓ BMD) · **osteoporosis** (inactivity, ↓ vit D, immunomodulators) · gait abnormality (foot drop) · spasticity, contractures

## 3 Presentation

**Clinical definition** — **≥2 episodes of neurological dysfunction separated in time and space** (brain, cord, optic nerve)

**Symptoms** — **optic neuritis**, diplopia, nystagmus · fatigue, depression · dysarthria, dysphagia · weakness, ataxia, falls · paraesthesia, **peculiar sensations (wetness)** · bowel/bladder dysfunction · relapsing–remitting **transient limb numbness** (non-dermatomal)

**Exam** — wide-based gait, limb ataxia, slapping foot · **spasticity, hyperreflexia, Babinski, Hoffmann** · **internuclear ophthalmoplegia (MLF)** · **Lhermitte's sign**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**MRI brain + spinal cord with gadolinium** — ✓ **multiple focal demyelinating (T2) lesions** · **asymmetric periventricular plaques** · cord lesions commonest in cervical spine

![MS — periventricular plaques](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9079-ms.jpg)

### Genes & lab markers

**CSF** — **↑ IgG (80%)** · **oligoclonal bands** · mononuclear pleocytosis (25%) · routine bloods normal (exclude mimics)

**Differential** — cervical myelopathy · CNS mass · B12 deficiency · sarcoidosis · CNS infection

## 5 Management

**Young woman, transient arm numbness, UMN signs, multifocal T2 cord lesions** — ✓ **neurology referral**\
✗ Wrong options: laminoplasty · plasmapheresis/IVIG (GBS) · B12 (subacute combined degeneration) · lumbar MRI

**Acute relapse** — **corticosteroids** · **prophylaxis** — **interferon-β** (↓ relapses, slows RRMS)

**Spasticity** — baclofen, gabapentin, clonazepam, **botox** · PT: gentle stretching, progressive resistance · osteoporosis treatment

## 6 Complications

**Course** — ✓ **relapsing–remitting (commonest)** · primary progressive · secondary progressive · progressive relapsing

**Life expectancy** — 5–10 yr shorter

## How it's asked

**"29-yr-old woman, relapsing arm numbness, brisk reflexes, bilateral Hoffmann, multifocal cord lesions — next?"** → **neurology referral**
