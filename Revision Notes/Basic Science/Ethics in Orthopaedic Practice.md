*Basic Science · 31 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Ethical principles

| Pillar | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Beneficence** | Duty to do good |
| **Non-maleficence** | Duty not to harm |
| **Respect for autonomy** | Patient self-determination |
| **Justice** | **Fair, equitable, appropriate** treatment (✗ Wrong options: "everyone gets the **same** treatment") |
| Confidentiality | Patient's right + doctor's duty to protect information |

## 5 Management

### Industry relationships & conflicts of interest

| AAOS Standards of Professionalism | |
|---|---|
| **Gifts** | Market value **<$100** (except textbooks, patient-education material) · dinners **≤$100/person** with useful education |
| **Industry non-CME course** | ✓ Tuition, travel, modest hospitality acceptable (✗ Wrong options: "must decline non-CME courses") |
| **Faculty at industry meeting** | ✓ Travel + hotel paid (contribution must match expenses) |
| **CME event** | Corporate subsidy **to the sponsor** OK · not OK: direct industry reimbursement to the surgeon, spouse's travel |
| **On-site training** (company HQ workshop, new technique/implant) | ✓ **Expenses reimbursable** |
| **Disclosure** | All industry relationships → **patients, colleagues, institution** · **even without financial compensation** |
| **Royalties** | **Disclose full relationship incl. royalties to patient** before surgery · may still use own device |
| Kick-backs | No payment for using a product ($500/knee, "consultant" after 25 implants) |
| **Stark II** | No referral to centres in which physician has a **financial interest** |
| Consulting | Defined duties + work product · fair pay for time · no thinly veiled payment |
| Residency grants | **Unrestricted** for CME ✓ · (✗ Wrong options: restricted to industry course)|
| Personal deals (e.g. rep buys 90% of a boat) | **Reject — unacceptable conflict** (equal share doesn't fix it) |

**Implant selection** — ✓ surgeon responsible for knowing the **implant's clinical track record** · (✗ Wrong options: rely on sales rep) · publish negative results too

### Research conduct

**Industry-funded RCT** — ✓ paid fair market value, disclose, register (**ClinicalTrials.gov — FDA law for implanted devices**), regulatory binder, publish\
✗ Wrong options: **company statistician analysing data** (bias → use independent blinded statistician)

**Sunshine Act** — all industry payments to physicians reported to **CMS**

**Publication** — government-funded studies must publish regardless of outcome · private studies need only report serious adverse events to FDA (publication bias)

### Advertising

**Who regulates** — AMA/AAOS **cannot stop** physicians advertising (FTC Act) · **FTC, AAOS, state medical boards, consumer protection agencies** sanction false advertising

| Avoid | ✓ Acceptable |
|---|---|
| "**Cure**" (if no true cure) | "**Relatively little pain**" |
| "**Painless / pain-free**", "**bloodless**" | "**Less invasive / minimally invasive**" (no outcome claims) |
| "Board certified in joint replacement" (doesn't exist) | "Relatively safe" |
| "World renowned" · "safe surgery for all" | — |
| "Return to all sports after TJA" | — |

### Interpreters & consent

**Preferred** — ✓ **certified (professional) interpreter**, on-site or telephonic (medico-legal)

**Family members** — not as interpreters (confidentiality) unless patient offers/agrees · **minors** not as interpreters except emergencies

**Interpreter summarising (short phrases)** — consent invalid → ✓ **repeat consent with qualified interpreter**\
✗ Wrong options: another nurse · proceed

**Interpretation style** — conduit, literal, first person, no omissions

### Patient transfer — EMTALA

**Requirements** — patient **screened** (+ stabilised) at original ED · **benefit of transfer > risk** · **accepting facility knows and formally accepts** · ✓ **exiting facility lacks resources to treat** (OB key)

**Not required** — insurance discussion · full lab work-up · cost of transport

### Team physician obligations

**Team-employed physician** — ✓ **agent of the team → must disclose findings to employer** · record = employment record, **not HIPAA-protected**

**Private physician** — HIPAA applies · no obligation to disclose to prospective teams without athlete consent

## 6 Complications

### Patient complaints, communication & litigation

**Leading cause of litigation** — ✓ **poor communication** · ↓ litigation with **effective communication**, standard of care, thorough informed consent, documentation

**Orthopaedic surgeons rated** — highest for **technical skills** · lowest for **empathy**, listening, communication ("high tech, low touch") · doctors interrupt within **23 s**

**Most common patient complaint** — ✓ **lack of empathy**

**Formal written complaint about care** — written response letter to patient

**Formal medical-board complaint** — ✓ **written response to the board with all relevant records** · governed by **state boards** · (✗ Wrong options: automatic licence suspension · contact patient's attorney) · attorney not required\
✗ Wrong options: federal

### Physician burnout & suicide

**Burnout** — loss of enthusiasm, cynicism (depersonalisation), ↓ personal accomplishment · ✓ **45–55%** of US physicians · rising · **1.5–2×** general population · ortho among higher-risk specialties · Maslach Burnout Inventory

**Suicide** — ✓ orthopaedic surgeons = **highest number among surgical subspecialties (28%)** · surgeons **6%** more likely than general population

### Disparities & diversity

**Cultural competency goal** — ✓ better patient–physician relationships, **access**, quality of care\
✗ Wrong options: quotas · stereotypes · foreign languages · travel abroad

**Implicit bias** — unconscious prejudice influencing care · ✓ **determinant of healthcare disparity**

**Paediatric access** — determined by **type of insurance**
