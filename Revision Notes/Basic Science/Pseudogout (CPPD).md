# Pseudogout (CPPD)

*Basic Science · 84 / 108*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — inflammatory arthritis from intra-articular **calcium pyrophosphate dihydrate (CPPD)** crystal deposition

**Demographics** — **elderly (>60)** · rare in young unless associated disease

**vs gout** — **older** · **more proximal joints** · **positively** birefringent crystals

### Associated conditions

| Associations |
|---|
| **Hyperparathyroidism** |
| **Haemochromatosis** |
| Wilson's disease |
| Gout · RA · SLE |
| Haemophilia |
| Long-term **haemodialysis** (pyrophosphate-like deposition) |

**Chondrocalcinosis** — present in **7%** · high association with knee **OA** (relationship unclear)

## 3 Presentation

**Acute attack** — warm, erythematous, tender, **monoarticular** · mimics gout + septic arthritis · ± superficial mineral deposits

| Joint frequency | |
|---|---|
| **1st** | ✓ **Knee** |
| **2nd** | **Wrist** |
| 3rd | 2nd + 3rd MCP joints |

**Course** — can cause rapidly progressive joint destruction · inflammatory markers normal / minimally ↑

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Chondrocalcinosis** — ✓ **linear calcification of fibrocartilage + articular cartilage** · **menisci (knee)** · **TFCC (wrist)** · distinguishes CPPD from other secondary OA (gout, haemochromatosis, RA, ochronosis)

### Diagnostic criteria — aspiration

**Crystals** — ✓ **weakly positively birefringent, rhomboid-shaped** · WBC moderate (**<50,000**) · culture negative

![Weakly positive birefringent rhomboid crystals](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7040-crystal-analysis-showing-weakly-positive-birefringent-rhombo.jpg)

## 5 Management

| Phase | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Acute** | **NSAIDs** · splint (comfort) · **intra-articular steroid** · rest |
| **Chronic / recurrent** | **Colchicine 0.6 mg PO bid** (prophylaxis ↓ recurrence) · **intra-articular yttrium-90** |

## 6 Complications

**Complications** — permanent joint damage · renal disease

## CPPD vs differentials

| Finding | Disease |
|---|---|
| **CPPD crystals, chondrocalcinosis** | ✓ **Pseudogout** |
| Monosodium urate, **strongly negative needle** crystals, punched-out erosions | Gout |
| Destructive **pannus**, symmetric narrowing, osteopenia | RA |
| Empty osteocyte lacunae | Osteonecrosis |
| Recurrent haemarthroses, wide notch, big condyles | Haemophilia |
| Iron deposition (cirrhosis, pigmentation, DM, cardiac) | Haemochromatosis |
| Homogentisic acid (homogentisic acid oxidase deficiency) | Ochronosis |

## How it's asked

**"Knee pain, hyperparathyroidism, meniscal calcification — finding?"** → **CPPD crystals**\
✗ Wrong options: urate · pannus · empty lacunae · haemarthroses

**"Linear cartilage calcification on knee AP?"** → **chondrocalcinosis (pseudogout)**\
✗ Wrong options: gout, haemochromatosis, ochronosis

**"TFCC calcification — other joint most involved?"** → **knee**\
✗ Wrong options: 1st MTP
