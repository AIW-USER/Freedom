# Intramembranous Bone Formation

*Basic Science · 52 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Definition

**Bone formed without a cartilage model** — undifferentiated mesenchymal cells aggregate → osteoblast differentiation → organic matrix deposition

**Also called** — contact healing, Haversian remodelling (in fracture healing)

### Sites of intramembranous ossification

| ✓ Intramembranous **(TO)** | Not endochondral **(TO)** |
|---|---|
| **Embryonic flat bones** — skull, maxilla, mandible, pelvis, **clavicle**, subperiosteal surface of long bones | Longitudinal physeal growth |
| **Distraction osteogenesis** | Embryonic long bones |
| **Rigid fixation (compression plate)** | Non-rigid (secondary) fracture healing |
| Perichondrial bone formation | Epiphyseal secondary centres |
| **Blastema bone** (children's amputations) | **Fracture callus** |
| One component of IM-nail healing | Degenerating OA cartilage, calcifying cartilage tumours, **DBM-induced bone** |

### Regulation

**Signalling** — canonical **Wnt** + Hedgehog · **β-catenin** → osteoblasts

**Transcription factors** — **CBFA1 / RUNX2** · **osterix (OSX)**

### Sclerostin

| Sclerostin | Fact |
|---|---|
| Gene | **SOST** |
| Source | **Osteocytes** |
| Action | ✓ **Inhibits Wnt** (✗ Wrong options: activates) → ↓ osteoblasts |
| Overexpression | **↓ bone mass** |
| Loss | **Sclerosteosis, van Buchem disease** (high bone mass) |

## 6 Complications

### Genes & lab markers

**Cleidocranial dysplasia** — **defective intramembranous ossification** · **CBFA1 (RUNX2)** on **chromosome 6** · AD · absent clavicles, skull defects

## How it's asked

**"Sclerostin — all true EXCEPT?"** → **"activates Wnt pathway"**\
✗ Wrong options: SOST product · osteocyte-derived · sclerosteosis/van Buchem

**"Intramembranous formation does NOT occur in (TO)?"** → **callus formation during fracture healing**\
✗ Wrong options: compression plating, distraction osteogenesis, flat bones
