# Musculocutaneous Nerve

*Basic Science · 65 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Origin & course

**Origin** — **C5–7** · superior trunk → **lateral cord** (terminal branch) · leaves axilla at inferior border of pectoralis minor **(TO)**

**Course** — ✓ **pierces coracobrachialis** **3–8 cm** distal to coracoid (Orthobullets) · **5–8 cm** (TO) · 1.5–9 cm (brachial plexus note) → runs **between biceps (superficial) and brachialis (deep)** → pierces deep fascia **lateral to biceps tendon** above elbow → **lateral antebrachial cutaneous nerve**

| Motor | Sensory |
|---|---|
| **Coracobrachialis, biceps, brachialis** (C5–6) · articular branches to humerus, elbow **(TO)** | **Lateral antebrachial cutaneous** — lateral (+ volar) forearm |

## 6 Complications

### Injury after coracoid transfer

**Mechanism** — iatrogenic in **deltopectoral approach** · dissection/retraction **medial to the conjoint tendon** · ✓ **vigorous retraction of conjoint tendon (short head biceps + coracobrachialis) → neurapraxia** **(TO)** · Latarjet / coracoid transfer, open shoulder reduction

**Lateral forearm numbness after coracoid transfer** — ✓ **musculocutaneous nerve**\
✗ Wrong options: axillary (lateral arm) · radial (dorsal wrist) · median

### Sensory & motor deficit (TO)

| Expected finding **(TO)** | Not expected **(TO)** |
|---|---|
| **↓ elbow flexion + supination** | Not **weak pronation** (pronators — median) |
| **Lateral (+ volar) forearm sensory loss** | Not elbow extension weakness (triceps — radial) |
| **Weak/absent biceps reflex** | — |
| **Small-amplitude LABCN SNAP**, ↓ biceps CMAP | — |
| Biceps atrophy | — |

**Pitfall (TO)** — complete division can be missed: **brachioradialis** still flexes elbow → palpate biceps during testing

## How it's asked

**"Vigorous conjoint-tendon retraction endangers (TO)?"** → **musculocutaneous nerve**\
✗ Wrong options: axillary · anterior circumflex humeral artery

**"Lateral forearm numbness 1 wk after coracoid transfer?"** → **musculocutaneous**

**"After open shoulder reduction, lateral forearm numbness — unlikely finding (TO)?"** → **weak supination AND pronation** (pronation normal)
