# Pectoralis Major

*Basic Science · 77 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy

| | Pectoralis major |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | **Clavicular head** — anterior medial half of clavicle · **sternocostal head** — sternum, superior 6 costal cartilages, external oblique aponeurosis |
| **Insertion** | **Lateral lip of intertubercular (bicipital) groove** |
| **Action** | **Adducts + medially rotates** humerus · draws scapula anteriorly/inferiorly · clavicular head flexes, sternocostal head extends |
| **Innervation** | **Lateral + medial pectoral nerves (C5–T1)** |
| **Arterial supply** | Pectoral branch of **thoracoacromial trunk** |

### Humeral insertion

| Insertion relative to long head of biceps | Muscle |
|---|---|
| **Just lateral** | ✓ **Pectoralis major** |
| Medial (floor / medial lip) | **Teres major, latissimus dorsi** |
| Lesser tuberosity, medial | **Subscapularis** |
| ✗ Wrong option: humerus | Short head of biceps — **originates on coracoid** |

## How it's asked

**"Tendon inserting just lateral to long head of biceps?"** → **pectoralis major**\
✗ Wrong options: teres major, latissimus dorsi (medial) · subscapularis (lesser tuberosity)
