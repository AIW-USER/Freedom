# PTH & Vit D Physiology

*Basic Science · 87 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Overview

**Organs in Ca homeostasis** — skin · liver · parathyroid · kidney · bone · small intestine · ↑ PTH + ↑ vit D → ↑ serum Ca

### Vitamin D metabolism & serum markers

| Step | Site | Product |
|---|---|---|
| **7-dehydrocholesterol** + **UV-B** | **Skin** | Previtamin D₃ → **cholecalciferol (D₃)** |
| Diet / supplements | Gut | D₂ (**ergocalciferol** — vegetables, supplements) · D₃ (oily fish, fortified foods) — both inert |
| Carried on **vitamin D-binding protein** → **25-hydroxylase** | **Liver** | **25-(OH) D₃ = calcidiol** |
| **1α-hydroxylase** (↑ by PTH, ↓ Ca, ↓ PO₄) | **Kidney** | ✓ **1,25-(OH)₂ D₃ = calcitriol — active** **(TO)** |
| **24-hydroxylase** | Kidney | **24,25-(OH)₂ D₃ — inactive** · 1,24,25-(OH)₃ D₃ |

![Vitamin D pathway — A D₃, B 25-OH D, C 1,25-(OH)₂ D, D 24,25-(OH)₂ D, E 1,24,25-(OH)₃ D](../../MCQ/attachments/q210168-a-path.jpg)

**Self-regulation** — high 1,25-D → ↑ **24,25-D** (inactive) · negative feedback on itself

**Skin conversion** — ↓ with age · nearly absent in darkly pigmented individuals

**Vitamin D actions** — ↑ intestinal Ca + PO₄ absorption · ↑ bone resorption (Ca + PO₄ release; osteoclastic, TO) → **↑ serum Ca AND ↑ PO₄** · OB219175: vit D (with **FGF23**) **inhibits osteoclast activity** → ↑ bone mass — read the options

### PTH functions (TO)

**Source** — **chief cells** of parathyroid · trigger **hypocalcaemia / hypomagnesaemia**

| PTH action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bone | ↑ **osteoclastic** resorption — receptor on **osteoblasts** → **IL-1** → osteoclasts |
| Kidney | ↑ **Ca reabsorption (DCT)** · **↓ phosphate reabsorption** |
| Kidney | ↑ **1α-hydroxylation** of 25-OH D → ↑ 1,25-D |
| Net | **↑ serum Ca, ↓ serum PO₄** |

**PTH function — EXCEPT answer (TO)** — **"↑ osteoblastic resorption of bone"** — no such thing; resorption is osteoclastic **(TO)**

### Genes & lab markers

**Best test for deficiency** — ✓ **25-OH vitamin D** — long half-life, circulating level **~1000×** 1,25-D\
✗ Wrong options: 1,25-D (estimates stores poorly; only a fraction converted)

| Half-life | 25-OH D | 1,25-(OH)₂ D |
|---|---|---|
| OB4653 | **2–3 wk** | **4–6 h** |
| OB210168 | **15 days** | **15 h** |

**Unit conversion** — 1 nmol/L = 0.4 ng/mL · 1 ng/mL = 2.5 nmol/L

| 25-OH D level | Status |
|---|---|
| **<30 nmol/L (<12 ng/mL)** | At risk of **deficiency** (NIH, OB217497) |
| 30–50 nmol/L (12–20 ng/mL) | Possible inadequacy |
| **≥50 nmol/L (≥20 ng/mL)** | Sufficient for most |
| **75 nmol/L (30 ng/mL)** | ✓ **Normal** (OB217497) · Endocrine Society: **>75 nmol/L (>30 ng/mL)** needed for max bone/muscle effect |
| **<20 ng/mL** | **Deficient** (OB219590) |
| **20–30 ng/mL** | ✓ **Insufficient** (OB219590) |
| 30–80 ng/mL | Normal range (OB7541) |

**RDA vitamin D₃** — **400 IU** infants <1 yr · **600 IU** age 1–70 (incl. pregnancy/lactation) · **800 IU** adults >70

## 5 Management

### Supplementation & fracture healing

**Vitamin D in bone healing** — ✓ **regulates inflammatory response** at injury site (macrophages, neutrophils, T cells express **VDR**, convert vit D locally) · ↑ **VEGF**-mediated vasculogenesis · ↑ **MSC commitment to osteoblasts** (matrix adhesion, integrins) · ↑ ECM: **collagen I, osteocalcin, osteopontin** · prevents infection, ↑ muscle/soft tissue

**Deficiency** — ↑ fracture + arthrodesis **nonunion** · ↑ falls in elderly (supplement ↓ falls) · atrophic nonunion work-up → **25-OH D**

**Insufficiency fracture + 25-OH D 14 ng/mL, normal Ca** — ✓ **high-dose vitamin D**\
✗ Wrong options: observation (at risk elsewhere) · calcium alone · bisphosphonate before correcting mineral imbalance · surgery

**Supplement form** — **D₃ preferred** · D₂ most available by prescription (US) · hypervitaminosis D rare, high doses tolerated

## Vitamin D vs PTH

| | **1,25-(OH)₂ vit D** | **PTH** |
|---|---|---|
| Serum Ca | ↑ | ↑ |
| **Serum PO₄** | **↑** | **↓** |
| Gut | ↑ Ca + PO₄ absorption | Indirect (via 1,25-D) |
| Kidney | — | ↑ Ca reabsorption, ↓ PO₄ reabsorption, ↑ 1α-hydroxylase |
| Disorders | Rickets / osteomalacia | Hypoparathyroidism · pseudohypoparathyroidism · renal osteodystrophy |

## How it's asked

**"Most active vit D metabolite?"** → **1,25-dihydroxycholecalciferol** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: 25-OH · 24,25

**"Correct regarding vit D?"** → **24,25-dihydroxycholecalciferol is inactive**\
✗ Wrong options: 1,25-D best lab test · 1,25-D longer half-life

**"Serum marker after vit D repletion (pathway letter)?"** → **B — 25-hydroxyvitamin D**

**"Post-hip fracture 25-OH D 75 nmol/L (30 ng/mL)?"** → **normal level**

**"Atrophic humeral nonunion — value showing insufficiency?"** → **25-OH D 25 ng/mL**\
✗ Wrong options: 35 / 45 ng/mL · any 1,25-D value

**"Sacrococcygeal insufficiency fracture, 25-OH D 14 ng/mL?"** → **high-dose vitamin D**

**"Primary function of vit D in osteoporotic fracture healing?"** → **regulates inflammatory response at injury site**\
✗ Wrong options: activates RANKL · ↓ VEGF · inhibits osteocalcin

**"NOT a function of PTH?"** → **↑ osteoblastic resorption of bone** **(TO)**
