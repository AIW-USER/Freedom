# Rickets

*Basic Science · 93 / 108*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — defective mineralisation of osteoid/cartilage from inadequate **Ca + PO₄** **before physeal closure** · after closure = **osteomalacia**

**Primary problem** — ✓ **low calcium** available for mineralisation (Ca/PO₄ homeostasis)\
✗ Wrong options: primarily a physeal (proliferative zone) disorder · type I collagen (OI) · EXT-1 (MHE) · dysplastic fibrous bone (FD)

**Site** — poor calcification at **zone of provisional calcification** → ↑ **physeal width** + cortical thinning/bowing · fastest-growing bones (tibia, distal femur, distal radius) · rarely hand tubular bones

**Causes** — vit D deficiency · vit D malabsorption · **renal osteodystrophy** · **X-linked hypophosphataemia** \
✗ Wrong options: **vitamin K deficiency**

**Activation** — ✓ **PTH** activates renal **1α-hydroxylase** (low Ca) → calcitriol · most potent effect: ↑ intestinal Ca absorption

### Classification

| Type | Inheritance / gene | Mechanism | Age |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vit D-resistant (familial hypophosphataemic, XLH)** | ✓ **X-linked dominant — PHEX** (commonest **heritable** rickets) | **Renal tubules can't reabsorb PO₄** · ↑ **FGF23** → PO₄ wasting + suppressed 1α-hydroxylase · GFR normal | **1–2 yr** |
| ADHR | **AD — FGF23** (cleavage site) | ↓ FGF23 degradation · **iron deficiency triggers** expression **(TO)** | — |
| ARHR | **AR — DMP1** (also ENPP1, FAM20C) | Impaired osteocyte maturation, ↑ FGF23 | — |
| **Nutritional (vit D-deficient)** | — | ↓ intake → ↓ gut Ca → ↑ PTH → resorption → ↑ ALP | **6 mo–3 yr** |
| **VDDR I** | **AR — renal 1α-hydroxylase** (12q14; CYP27B1) | ↓ **calcitriol** | — |
| **VDDR II** | **AR — vit D receptor** (inactivating) | ↑ **calcitriol** · end-organ resistance · alopecia (2A) | — |
| HHRH | **SLC34A3** (NaPi-IIc) | FGF23-**independent** PO₄ wasting **(TO)** | — |

**XLH inheritance** — affected father → **100% daughters carriers/affected**, sons unaffected · more severe in males

**Nutritional risk factors** — premature infants · ✓ **dark-skinned (African American) breastfed >6 mo without vit D** · malabsorption (coeliac), TPN · Asian immigrants · vegetarian diets · rare since milk fortified

| FGF23-dependent hypophosphataemia (TO) | FGF23-independent (TO) |
|---|---|
| **XLH**, ADHR, ARHR · tumour-induced osteomalacia · epidermal naevus · osteoglophonic dysplasia · fibrous dysplasia · iron-infusion · **α-klotho translocation** · **Dent's** (per TO key) · NPT2a, NHERF1 mutations · post-transplant / hepatic resection | ✓ **Fanconi syndrome** · ✓ **HHRH (hereditary hypophosphataemic rickets with hypercalciuria)** |

**FGF23 (TO)** — **high** → ↓ PO₄, ↓ 1,25-D, osteomalacia · ✓ **low** → ↑ PO₄, ↑ 1,25-D, soft-tissue calcification, **hyperostosis** **(TO)** · signals via FGFR1 + **Klotho**; phosphaturia partly PTH-dependent **(TO)**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — listlessness · irritability · weakness

**Signs** — **tibial bowing** (wide proximal tibial physes) · **genu varum** (commonest) · **rachitic rosary** (costochondral enlargement) · **widened wrists** + ankles · skull flattening · **kyphosis (cat back)** · ligamentous laxity · hypotonia · waddling gait · delayed dentition, enamel defects · pathological fractures · short stature

![Tibial bowing in rickets](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9031-11-moved.jpg)

**XLH** — short stature · **genu varum** · windswept deformity · medial tibial torsion · genu valgum in some (e.g. 4-yr-old knock knees) · no myopathy

**VDDR** — like nutritional but **more severe** · type I: hypotonia, seizures, fractures in infancy · type II: + bone pain, dental caries/hypoplasia, **alopecia**

| Calcipenic vs phosphopenic (TO) | **Calcipenic** | **Phosphopenic** |
|---|---|---|
| Muscle weakness | Present | **Absent** (present in TIO) |
| Bone pain | Common | Uncommon |
| Limbs | All equal | **Predominantly lower limbs** |
| Tetany · enamel hypoplasia | May occur | Absent |
| Dental abscess | Absent | May occur |
| Ca | Low/normal | Normal |
| ALP | Markedly ↑ | Mild–moderate ↑ |
| PTH | ↑ | Normal / minimally ↑ |
| **Osteopenia, osteitis fibrosa** | Present | ✓ **Absent** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + lateral)** — ✓ **physeal widening** · **metaphyseal cupping**, irregular metaphyses · loss of zone of provisional calcification definition · osteopenia · **Looser zones** (compression side) · ✓ **anterior rib widening (rachitic rosary)** · **genu varum** · codfish vertebrae · dorsal kyphosis

![Rachitic rosary](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9031-598b04743e587575e91d114605fc6b-gallery.jpg)

| Radiographic mimic | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Hyperparathyroidism** | **Punched-out** lesions, **subperiosteal resorption**, erosion around physis edge |
| **Jansen metaphyseal chondrodysplasia** | **Rounded epiphyses**, wide epiphysis–metaphysis separation · ligand-independent **PTHR1** activation |
| **Renal osteodystrophy** | **Rugger jersey** sclerotic vertebral endplates |

**Extreme physeal widening + osteopenia + deformity** — ✓ pathognomonic for rickets → **labs (Ca, PO₄, ALP, vit D) + endocrinology first**\
✗ Wrong options: osteotomy / guided growth before medical control (high recurrence)

### Looser zones (TO)

**Looser zone** — pseudofracture; indentation of soft bone by vascular crossings **(TO)**

| Seen in (TO) | Not seen |
|---|---|
| **Osteomalacia** (characteristic) · renal osteodystrophy · fibrous dysplasia · hyperthyroidism · **Paget's** · **XLH** · OI | ✓ **Lead poisoning** |

### Oncogenic (tumour-induced) osteomalacia (TO)

**TIO** — paraneoplastic · adolescents/young adults · mesenchymal tumours secrete **FGF23 ("phosphatonin")** → phosphaturia **(TO)**

**Tumours (TO)** — **haemangiopericytoma (commonest)** · GCT · chondromyxoid fibroma · chondroblastoma · osteosarcoma · osteoblastoma · NOF / metaphyseal fibrous defect · fibrous dysplasia · MFH · fibrosarcoma · haemangioma \
✗ Wrong options: **multiple myeloma** **(TO)**

**TIO labs (TO)** — ✓ **↓ PO₄ (severe), normal Ca, low/normal 1,25-D, ↑ FGF23** · ↑ ALP · low TmP · PTH less raised than dietary osteomalacia **(TO)**

**TIO work-up + Rx (TO)** — localise: **octreotide scan**, FDG-PET/CT, MRI/CT · **excise tumour** → PO₄ normalises within hours · supplement PO₄ + vit D until normal **(TO)**

### Renal tubular rickets & Fanconi syndrome (TO)

**Renal tubular rickets (TO)** — ✓ **↑ urine PO₄, ↓ urine Ca, normal 1,25-D, ↓ serum PO₄, normal PTH** · normal Ca · low urinary pH · early infancy, severe deformity, no myopathy or hypocalcaemia · RTA, Fanconi, XLH **(TO)**

**Fanconi syndrome (TO)** — proximal tubular defect → wasting of **PO₄, amino acids, glucose, bicarbonate** **(TO)**

| Fanconi (TO) | Finding |
|---|---|
| Blood | **Metabolic acidosis** · ↓ PO₄ · ↓ 25(OH)D · ↑ ALP · ↑ PTH · (✗ Wrong options: **↑ serum Ca**) |
| Urine | ↑ PO₄ · ↑ K⁺ · calciuria |
| Bone | ↓ BMD, osteoporosis |

## 5 Management

| Type | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **XLH** | **Calcitriol + phosphate** (phosphate must be with calcitriol — prevents 2° HPT) · **burosumab** |
| **Nutritional** | **Vitamin D 5000 IU/day × 6–10 wk** |
| **VDDR I** | **Calcitriol** (physiological dose) + calcium — excellent response **(TO)** |
| VDDR 1B (25-hydroxylase) | Calcidiol **(TO)** |
| **VDDR II** | Partial resistance → **vit D** (very high calcitriol) · total → **calcium** · severe hypocalcaemia → ✓ **prolonged high-dose IV calcium** (alopecia doesn't respond) **(TO)** |
| **HHRH** | **Oral phosphate ONLY** · **active vit D contraindicated** (worsens hypercalciuria, stones) **(TO)** |
| ADHR | PO₄ + calcitriol · **iron repletion** may normalise FGF23 **(TO)** |
| ARHR | PO₄ + calcitriol · risk enthesopathy, vascular calcification (ENPP1) **(TO)** |

**Doses** — calcitriol **20–30 μg/kg/day** (children, as printed) · 0.5–0.75 μg/day adults · phosphate **20–40 mg/kg/day** in 3–5 doses (children) · 750–1000 mg/day adults

**Burosumab** — ✓ **fully human mAb neutralising circulating FGF23** → restores PO₄ reabsorption + 1α-hydroxylase · (✗ Wrong options: PHEX chaperone · Klotho agonist **(TO)**) · approved **≥1 yr** (Orthobullets) · first line **>6 months** + adults (TO) — read the options

**Conventional XLH therapy risks (TO)** — **nephrocalcinosis**, tertiary HPT → renal US · nephrocalcinosis → ✓ **switch to burosumab** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: loop diuretic · more phosphate

**XLH medical therapy** — early calcitriol + phosphate ✓ **↓ need for osteotomies**, improves height, ↓ recurrence · (✗ Wrong options: normalises mineralisation completely) · continue into **adulthood** (pain, insufficiency fractures) · (✗ Wrong options: calcium alone) · OB2930 (Loeffler): adding phosphate to vit D gave no extra benefit — read the options

### Operative criteria

| Deformity | Surgery |
|---|---|
| Growth remaining (**<11 yr**) | **Hemiepiphysiodesis / guided growth** — effective once medically treated |
| **>11 yr** / severe tibial bowing | **Multilevel corrective osteotomy** (K-wires, plates, IM nails, ex-fix) |
| Before metabolic control | No — high recurrence |

**XLH outcome** — staged surgery better, but early degenerative joint disease

**Healing assessment (TO)** — best marker: **X-ray** · lecture calls the healing line "white line of Frankel" (TO — verify: Frankel line is a scurvy sign; rickets healing = recalcified zone of provisional calcification)

**Timing of osteotomy (TO)** — only after **serum ALP normalises** · never in active rickets · angular deformity seen after 2 yr, **genu varum > valgum**

**Windswept deformity (TO)** — valgus one knee + varus the other · children: rickets · adults: RA
