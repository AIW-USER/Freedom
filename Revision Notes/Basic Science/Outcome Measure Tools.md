## 1 Basics

### SF-36 & patient-reported outcomes

**SF-36** — **generic** short-form health survey, **36 questions** · most widely applied general health instrument · **self-administered** (office, mail, phone) · validated with **normative population data** + multilingual translations

**SF-36 structure** — **8 scaled scores** (each 0–100, equal item weight) · physical functioning · role physical · bodily pain · general health · vitality · social functioning · role emotional · mental health · measures functional ability, well-being, overall health

**SF-36 limitation** — ✓ **ceiling and floor effects** · bias **lower > upper extremity**\
✗ Wrong options: "no normative data" · ignores patient perspective · must be on-site

**SF-36 use** — compare disease burden · **polytrauma with foot injury → lower SF-36** than polytrauma without foot injury (≥12 mo)

**SF-12** — 12-item short version · physical + mental health · pre-injury pain-related disability predicts pain at 6 mo

**VR-12** — Veterans Rand 12-item, derived from SF-36 · physical + mental summary scores · HRQOL, disease burden

**EQ-5D** — generic QOL · **5 dimensions** (mobility, self-care, usual activities, pain/discomfort, anxiety/depression) + **VAS** · **243 health states** · index **0 = death, 1 = perfect health** · 170+ languages

**PROMs vs physician-reported** — ✓ **better assessment of patient satisfaction**\
✗ Wrong options: cost-effective care · ambulation · disposition · analgesia needs

### PROMIS

**PROMIS** — NIH (2004 Roadmap) · **general + disease-specific** · adult + paediatric · domains **physical, mental, social**

**Method** — **computer adaptive testing (CAT)** + **item response theory (IRT)** → next item chosen from previous answers → **fewer questions**, high precision

**Scoring** — **T-scores**, population mean **50, SD 10**

**Advantage** — ✓ **reduced floor and ceiling effects** · quickest, broadest population, highly reliable · high correlation with ODI + SF-36\
✗ Wrong options: more items · low reliability/validity

**Validated in** — ✓ **spine · foot & ankle · upper extremity**

**Foot & ankle** — PROMIS ✓ **superior** to FAOS / OMAS at detecting clinically significant change at **6 and 12 mo** after ankle fracture\
✗ Wrong options: needs 24 mo follow-up

**Legacy measures** — SF-36, EQ-5D, HHS, KOOS, DASH, QuickDASH, FAAM, ODI · not IRT-based · validated only in specific populations

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Joint-specific scores

| Region | Scores |
|---|---|
| **Knee** | **KSS** · IKDC · **KOOS** · **WOMAC** · Lysholm · Tegner · Oxford knee **(TO)** |
| **Hip** | Merle d'Aubigné–Postel · **Harris Hip Score** · modified HHS · HOOS · Oxford hip · Lequesne · WOMAC **(TO)** |
| **Shoulder** | **DASH** · Constant · Oxford shoulder · WOSI / WORC / WOOS · Simple shoulder test · UCLA · SPADI · ASES **(TO)** |

**"Not a knee score"** — ✓ **DASH** (upper limb) **(TO)**

**WOMAC** — hip + knee **OA** · **24 items**: **pain 5 · stiffness 2 · function 17** · sum 0–20 / 0–8 / 0–68 · **higher = worse** · TO key: **knee** (OA commoner in knee than hip) **(TO)** · also back pain, RA, JRA, SLE, fibromyalgia

**KOOS** — young/middle-aged, post-traumatic OA (also TKA) · **5 domains** (pain, symptoms, ADL, sport/recreation, QOL) · 42 items 0–4 → 0–100 (**100 = no problems**)

| Harris Hip Score (THA) | Points |
|---|---|
| **Pain** (none) | **44** |
| **Function** (gait: no limp, no aid, >6 blocks) | **33** |
| Functional activities | 14 |
| Physical exam (ROM) | 9 |
| **Rating** | **90–100 excellent · 80–90 good · 70–79 fair · 60–69 poor · <60 failed** |

![Harris Hip Score](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9087-hhs.jpg)

**FAOS** — lateral ankle instability, Achilles tendinosis, plantar fasciitis · **5 subscales** (pain, symptoms, ADL, sport, QOL) · last week · 0–4 → normalised **100 = no symptoms**

| Constant shoulder score (0–100) | Max |
|---|---|
| Pain (subjective) | **15** |
| Function / ADL (subjective) | **20** |
| **Range of motion** (objective) | **40** |
| **Strength** (objective) | **25** |

![Constant score](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9087-constant-score.jpg)

**UCLA shoulder** — **5 sections**: pain · function · active forward flexion · strength of forward flexion · **patient satisfaction** · max **35** · **>27 = good/excellent**

**DASH** — **30-item** self-report upper limb · disability/symptom (30 items, 1–5) + optional sport/music/work (4 items) · score = **[(sum/n) − 1] × 25** · not calculable if **>3 missing** · optional module not scored if any missing

**QuickDASH** — **11 items**, 5-point Likert · not scored if **>1 missing** · 0–100, higher = more disability

**Oxford Shoulder Instability Score** — 12 questions · **12 (best) to 60 (worst)** · uni- + multidirectional instability

### Spine scores

**ODI** — **gold standard** low back functional outcome · **10 sections × 5** · % = scored / possible × 100 (16/50 = **32%**; one section missed → 16/45 = **35.5%**)

**NDI** — modified ODI · 10 items · commonest neck pain measure (chronic neck pain, whiplash, radiculopathy) · sum / 50 × 100 · 0% none → 100% complete disability

**ASIA** — spinal cord injury

| Cervical myelopathy | Based on |
|---|---|
| **mJOA** (17 points) | UE motor · LE motor · sensory · **bladder** |
| **Nurick** | **Gait** · employment · cord symptoms |

## 5 Management

### MCID — statistical vs clinical significance

**p-value** — chance of the observed difference if no true difference · **p < 0.05** = statistically significant · says nothing about clinical relevance

**MCID** — ✓ must be identified to judge **clinical significance** (e.g. KOOS difference, p = 0.04)\
✗ Wrong options: "significant → better outcomes" · larger sample needed

**ODI 38.4 vs 34.1, p = 0.002** — ✓ **statistically significant but likely below MCID** (4.3 points) · (✗ Wrong options: NNT (applies to risk)) · 80% power / β 0.2 = standard · non-randomised → **confounding**\
✗ Wrong options: Hawthorne = behaviour change when observed

### Predictors of pain & patient satisfaction

| Predicts moderate/severe pain at 6 mo after MSK injury | Not a predictor |
|---|---|
| **Failure to complete high school** · **pre-injury pain-related disability** · **disability compensation** · **moderate/severe pain at discharge** | ✓ **Age, gender (male)** |

**↑ Satisfaction AND quality** — ✓ **interpreter for limited English proficiency**\
✗ Wrong options: requested hydrocodone · early MRI · waiting-room amenities · early pain referral

**HCAHPS** — inpatient satisfaction survey · concern it may not reflect quality

## Generic vs Specific

| | **Generic** | **Disease/joint-specific** |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | SF-36, SF-12, VR-12, EQ-5D, PROMIS | WOMAC, KOOS, HHS, DASH, Constant, ODI, NDI |
| Strength | Compare burden across diseases | Sensitive to joint change |
| Weakness | Ceiling/floor effects (SF-36) | Population-limited |

## How it's asked

**"Instrument with general + disease-specific measures, validated in spine, foot & ankle, upper extremity?"** → **PROMIS**\
✗ Wrong options: SRS-22, ODI, SF-12, PHQ-9

**"PROMIS vs other tools in spine?"** → **reduced floor and ceiling effect**\
✗ Wrong options: more items · poor correlation

**"IRT-based, quickest, broadest, reliable PROM?"** → **PROMIS**\
✗ Wrong options: DASH, QuickDASH, FAAM, SF-36

**"PROMIS vs legacy in foot & ankle?"** → **superior at detecting change at 6 and 12 mo**

**"ODI difference 4.3, p = 0.002?"** → **statistically significant, likely below MCID**

**"KOOS improvement, p = 0.04 — next?"** → **identify MCID to judge clinical significance**

**"Polytrauma with Lisfranc injury vs without at 12 mo?"** → **lower SF-36**

**"Limitation of SF-36?"** → **ceiling and floor effects**

**"Benefit of PROMs (WOMAC) over physician-reported?"** → **better assessment of patient satisfaction**

**"NOT a predictor of pain 6 mo after MSK injury?"** → **male gender**

**"↑ Both satisfaction and quality?"** → **interpreter**

**"NOT a knee functional score?"** → **DASH** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: WOMAC, KSS, KOOS

**"WOMAC is used for which joint?"** → **knee** (TO key; hip + knee OA) **(TO)**
