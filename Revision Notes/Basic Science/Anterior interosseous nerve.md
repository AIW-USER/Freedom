# Anterior interosseous nerve

*Basic Science · 4 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Innervated muscles

**AIN muscles** — **FPL** · **FDP index + long** · **pronator quadratus**

**Motor only** — no cutaneous sensation

![AIN branching from the median nerve](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob10107-graphic-2-large.jpg)

### Gantzer muscle (TO)

**Gantzer muscle** — ✓ **accessory head of FPL** (or of FDP) · anatomical variant in up to **70%** **(TO)**

**Origin** — deep surface of FDS, coronoid process or medial epicondyle **(TO)**

**Insertion** — FPL or FDP **(TO)**

**Innervation** — exclusively **AIN** **(TO)**

**Traps** — ✗ Wrong options: accessory popliteus · accessory biceps femoris · accessory rectus femoris **(TO)**

## 3 Presentation

**Supracondylar humerus fracture (extension type)** — **AIN = commonest nerve injury** in children

**Muscle affected** — ✓ **pronator quadratus** (most common electrophysiological abnormality) · also FPL, FDP index/long

**Often misdiagnosed** — as tendon injury (only 3 of 13 correct initially in Seror's series)

![Inability to make an "OK" sign — AIN lesion](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob10107-anterior-interosseous-lesion1345527186191.jpg)
