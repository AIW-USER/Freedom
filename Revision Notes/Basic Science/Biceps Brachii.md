# Biceps Brachii

*Basic Science · 10 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy & innervation

| | Biceps brachii |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | **Short head:** tip of **coracoid** · **Long head:** **supraglenoid tubercle** |
| **Insertion** | **Radial tuberosity** + forearm fascia via **bicipital aponeurosis** |
| **Action** | **Supinates** + flexes forearm |
| **Innervation** | **Musculocutaneous nerve (C5, C6)** |
| **Arterial supply** | Muscular branches of brachial artery |

### Long head in the rotator interval

**Rotator interval** — superior **supraspinatus** · inferior **subscapularis** · medial **glenoid**

**Sagittal MRI** — LHB seen in cross-section in the interval between subscapularis + supraspinatus → from superior labrum laterally → turns inferiorly into **bicipital groove**

**MGHL on sagittal MRI** — runs obliquely anteroinferiorly from superior labrum, **deep (posterior) to subscapularis**

![Sagittal MRI — arrow on the long head of biceps in the rotator interval](../../MCQ/attachments/q6457-a-31.jpg)

**Arthroscopy (posterosuperior portal)** — LHB tendon from supraglenoid tubercle → muscle innervated by **musculocutaneous**

## How it's asked

**"Sagittal shoulder MRI, structure between subscapularis and supraspinatus?"** → **long head of biceps**\
✗ Wrong options: MGHL (oblique, deep to subscap) · CA ligament

**"Arthroscopic tendon from supraglenoid tubercle — nerve to its muscle?"** → **musculocutaneous**\
✗ Wrong options: axillary, median, radial, ulnar
