# Sciatic nerve

*Basic Science · 95 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy at the hip & posterior approach

**Exit** — ✓ **greater sciatic notch** · emerges **anterior (deep) to piriformis** · passes between **piriformis and superior gemellus** (>80%) → posterior to superior gemellus, obturator internus, inferior gemellus, quadratus femoris → deep to biceps femoris

**Variants** — passes above or **pierces piriformis** · commonest rotator variant: runs **between capsule and short external rotators**, exiting below superior gemellus

**Posterior THA** — ✓ **deep to piriformis, superficial to short external rotators** · primary THA: identify by **palpation** · revision: palpation + direct visualisation

**Lesser sciatic notch** — **obturator internus tendon**\
✗ Wrong options: sciatic nerve

### Peroneal division innervation

**Thigh** — **tibial division → all hamstrings** (long head biceps, semitendinosus, semimembranosus) · ✓ **peroneal division → short head of biceps femoris only** · both gastrocnemius heads = tibial

**Peroneal division** — roots **L4–S3** (✗ Wrong options: L3–S1) · separates from tibial in upper popliteal fossa · crosses **posterior** to lateral gastrocnemius head · **deep peroneal** starts **lateral** to anterior tibial artery → crosses medial · commonest injury: **lateral** (✗ Wrong options: anterior) knee dislocation

**Localising** — thigh-level lesion → **denervates short head biceps + tibialis anterior** · knee-level lesion **spares short head biceps** → test short head biceps (EMG)

**Transection** — definitive repair at **1–3 wk** (zone of injury declares)

## How it's asked

**"Posterior THA — sciatic nerve location?"** → **deep to piriformis, superficial to short external rotators**

**"Sciatic nerve exit from pelvis?"** → **greater sciatic notch, between piriformis and superior gemellus**\
✗ Wrong options: lesser notch · obturator foramen

**"Posterolateral approach — nerve passes between?"** → **piriformis and superior gemellus**

**"Peroneal division supplies in thigh?"** → **short head of biceps femoris**

**"Peroneal division — true?"** → **thigh injury denervates short head biceps + tibialis anterior**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior knee dislocation · L3–S1
