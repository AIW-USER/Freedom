# Ochronosis

*Basic Science · 71 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Alkaptonuria — homogentisic acid oxidase

**Defect** — ✓ **deficiency of homogentisic acid oxidase** (rare inborn error, tyrosine/phenylalanine pathway) → **homogentisic acid** accumulates in fibrillar collagen

**Ochronosis** — **bluish-black discoloration of cartilage + connective tissue** · acid polymerises in joints → **early degenerative arthritis**

**Tendons** — deposition in **Achilles, patellar tendons** → spontaneous rupture

## 3 Presentation

**Black urine** (homogentisic acid polymerises on standing) · early large-joint OA · back pain

**Ochronotic spondylitis** — 4th decade · progressive degeneration

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Spine** — **disc calcification + disc-space narrowing** (multilevel)

**Knee** — chondrocalcinosis, early OA

![Ochronotic spondylitis — calcified, narrowed discs](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9046-ochronotic-spondylitis.jpg)

### Genes & lab markers

**Diagnosis** — **homogentisic acid in urine**

## 5 Management

**No specific medical treatment** (Orthobullets) · treat as OA: observation, NSAIDs, tramadol, steroids · **early arthroplasty** for severe disease

## X vs Y

| Finding | Disease |
|---|---|
| **Black cartilage, homogentisic acid** | **Alkaptonuria (ochronosis)** |
| Urate crystals | Gout |
| Lead | Plumbism |
| Hepcidin failure → iron overload | Haemochromatosis |
| LDLR mutation | Familial hypercholesterolaemia |
| Flowing anterior ossification | DISH |
| Syndesmophytes, SI fusion | Ankylosing spondylitis |

## How it's asked

**"Back pain, calcified discs, chondrocalcinosis, dark urine?"** → **ochronosis (alkaptonuria)**\
✗ Wrong options: DISH, AS, homocystinuria

**"Black cartilage at TKA — cause?"** → **homogentisic oxidase deficiency**
