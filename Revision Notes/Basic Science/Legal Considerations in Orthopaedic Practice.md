# Legal Considerations in Orthopaedic Practice

*Basic Science · 55 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Medical negligence — four elements

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Duty** | Obligation to meet the professional standard of care (same specialty) |
| **Breach of duty** | ✓ **Failure to exercise the diligence and care a reasonable, prudent person would under similar conditions** — deviation from standard of care |
| **Causation** | Breach **directly caused** the injury |
| **Damages** | Money awarded for injury |

**Standard of care (legal)** — ✓ **reasonable treatment showing knowledge, skill, diligence and care** (what a reasonable physician would do)\
✗ Wrong options: majority norm · minimum · average · "what you'd want for family"

**Corrected error, good outcome, lawsuit** — ✓ **defend: error occurred but was corrected, little/no damages**\
✗ Wrong options: settle · claim no error · contact patient directly

### Litigation statistics

**Trend** — ortho suits ↑ **13%** 2003–2008 (ageing population) · ortho **7th** most sued specialty · **~33%** of claims pay out · defence ~$47,000

**Causes** — "**improper performance**" 45% · procedures: joints (non-fusion), open/closed reductions · diagnosis: **OA (21%)**, joint disorder, **femur fracture**

**Subspecialty** — ✓ **arthroplasty most sued (29.9%)** > spine (22%) > trauma (17.7%) · most cases won by surgeon · highest plaintiff success: **fasciotomy (48%)**, infection (43%)

**Highest total indemnity by fracture** — ✓ **tibia** (> femur despite fewer cases)

**Wrong-site claims** — **84%** pay indemnity vs 30% of other ortho claims

### Legislation

| Law | Content |
|---|---|
| **Anti-Kickback Statute** (1987) | **Criminal** — no remuneration to induce referrals for federal programmes |
| **Stark law** (Social Security Act §1877; 1989/1993) | ✓ **No self-referral** to entities with physician financial relationship (PT, lab, radiology, DME) · fines to **$100,000** per scheme · "in-office" exceptions |
| Fee-splitting provisions | Sharing revenue for referrals |
| Certificate of Need | State control of new facilities |
| ACA (2010) | Insurance fairness/affordability (Sunshine Act passed within it) |
| **Physician Payments Sunshine Act** (2010) | ✓ **Industry payments >$10 reported to CMS** (Open Payments) |
| **HIPAA** (1996) | PHI protection · **no consent needed to share with other treating providers** (referral records, treating chiropractor) |

### IRB

**Origin** — **Nuremberg Code** (Nazi physicians) · dignity, autonomy, informed consent, fair selection, equal burden/benefit · mandates trial consent process

**Vulnerable populations** — **fetuses, pregnant women, children, prisoners**, cognitively impaired, students, employees\
✗ Wrong options: "prisoners are not"

**No IRB needed** — ✓ **internal QI study** (even with name, DOB, MRN) · IRB needed: MRN-based research, phase III device trial, CP children, RCT

## 5 Management

### Informed consent (emergencies, minors, interpreters, competency)

**Five elements (research + procedures)** — **voluntariness** · **provision of information** (risks, benefits, alternatives, no-treatment consequences, lay terms) · **competency** · **understanding** · **decision** \
✗ Wrong options: jurisdiction

**Late-stage dementia excluded from trial** — ✓ **competency**

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| **Unconscious, life/limb-threatening, no family** | ✓ **Confirm + document necessity with a colleague of similar expertise**, then operate |
| GCS 10, fiancé present, IIIC tibia | **Two surgeons of similar expertise** (fiancé has no legal standing) |
| **Intoxicated, compartment syndrome, refusing, no translator** | ✓ **Fasciotomy after documenting necessity** (lacks capacity; friend can't consent) |
| **Non-emergent (closed femur), patient lacks capacity** | ✓ **Delay; obtain next-of-kin consent** (daughter before mother) |
| 13-yr-old, urgent tibia fracture, parents abroad (German) | ✓ **Call parents with a German interpreter** (✗ Wrong options: cousin · child translating) |
| Limited English | ✓ **Certified live interpreter → certified telephone** (✗ Wrong options: family · minors · own limited Spanish) |
| Jehovah's Witness | ✓ **Crystalloid, starch (Hetastarch), colloid** · (✗ Wrong options: whole blood, RBC, platelets, plasma, autologous/donor-directed) · cell saver = individual choice |
| Consent says resident only retracts, attending leaves before cementing | ✓ **Ethically unsound — patient misled** ("ghost surgery" → battery) |

**Next of kin** — legal guardian → **spouse → children → parents → siblings → grandchildren**

**Implant origin** — **bovine → Hindu** · porcine → Jewish, Muslim

**Surgeon seropositive** — disclose **HIV, HBV, HCV** at scheduling of exposure-prone procedure · needlestick transmission: HIV ~0.3% · HCV ~1.8% · HBV 6–30% (30% if HBeAg+)

### Wrong-site surgery & Universal Protocol

**Joint Commission Universal Protocol (2004)** — **pre-procedure verification** (documents, labelled imaging displayed, blood/implants/equipment available) · **site marking** · **time-out**

**Site marking** — surgeon's **initials (or "YES")**, permanent marker, **with the patient**, in pre-op holding, **visible in the surgical field after draping** · (✗ Wrong options: "X" · "NO" on other limb · plantar foot for TKA) · may be delegated to resident/PA/APRN (LIP accountable)

**Correct sequence** — ✓ **read consent with patient → mark site → regional block → time-out → incise**

**JCAHO time-out** — correct **patient, side, site, procedure** · antibiotic not mandated · **all team members speak** · any member can lead · attending present in room\
✗ Wrong options: only surgeon

**Two teams (ortho + plastics) both present** — **single time-out** sufficient · otherwise repeat

**Late-arriving resident** — greet the team · no mandate to repeat time-out

**Wrong side about to be incised (resident)** — ✓ **ask surgeon to revisit safety checklist**

**Trend** — reported wrong-site surgeries ✓ **increased** since 1997/2003 protocols (↑ reporting) · 12.7/million · **ortho + podiatry 41%**

**WHO checklist (2009)** — sign-in (before induction: ID, site, consent, airway, **blood-loss risk**) · time-out (team introductions, **antibiotics within 60 min**, **imaging displayed**) · sign-out · "implants + rep present" not a component · ↓ mortality, complications, ↑ crisis adherence · **surgeon most effective member**

### Errors & disclosure

| Error | Response |
|---|---|
| Wrong-side incision noticed | ✓ **Immediately discuss with patient + family** (then risk management) · never alter record |
| Wrong knee incised in TKA | ✓ **Close wound, complete correct TKA, tell family after surgery** |
| Wrong spinal level (T7 not T6) | ✓ **Complete correct level**, document, apologise, inform after · (✗ Wrong options: abort)|
| Intraop MCL laceration | ✓ **Tell the patient the MCL was injured and plan adjusted** |
| Documentation error | ✓ **Addendum only** — altering record is illegal, no one can authorise it |

**Full disclosure + apology + responsibility + prevention plan** — ✓ **↑ patient satisfaction** (not ↑ litigation, not guaranteed prevention) · accept responsibility ≠ blame

**Second opinion** — **ethical (not legal)** duty to disclose error/standard-of-care breach to **patient** · no legal duty to testify (but subject to subpoena) · no duty to tell original surgeon · only patient can transfer care

**Impaired colleague (drinking attending)** — ✓ **express concern to the attending** → escalate to chief if needed\
✗ Wrong options: operate unsupervised

### Communication & patient safety

**Communication breakdown** — ✓ **most common cause of errors harming patients** · leading cause of **wrong-side surgery, medication errors, diagnostic delays, loss to follow-up** · 70% of sentinel events · ↑ costs + complications\
✗ Wrong options: SSI

**Communication affects** — patient satisfaction, adherence, physician satisfaction, malpractice \
✗ Wrong options: **Stark II litigation**

**Best ↓ sentinel events** — ✓ **culture where all team members feel empowered to speak** (flattened hierarchy)

**Retained sponge after shift change** — ✓ **OR-wide crew resource management training**\
✗ Wrong options: blame nurses · single policy

**CPOE** — ✓ **↓ medication errors** (incl. allergy errors)

**Physicians interrupt** — **23 seconds**

**Child lost to follow-up after casting (Spanish-speaking)** — ✓ **instructions not communicated** · discharge transitions need clear follow-up

### Malpractice process

**Summons received** — ✓ **notify liability carrier first** (assigns attorney)\
✗ Wrong options: call patient · discard notes

**Formal complaint** — **written response + all records to medical board** \
✗ Wrong options: automatic suspension · mandatory attorney

**Insurance** — ✓ **occurrence policy** (covers incidents during employment whenever filed) · claims-made needs **tail** · **nose** = prior acts if previous claims-made without tail

**Terminating care** — written notice + **30–45 day** grace period · without it = **abandonment**

### Workers' compensation & disability

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Maximum medical improvement** | Further restoration not anticipated → ✓ **claim can be settled** (declared by treating physician) |
| **Impairment** | ✓ **Loss of function from anatomic/physiologic derangement** (medical) |
| **Disability** | Limitation in meeting personal/social/occupational demands (legal) |
| Apportionment | Share of disability from each injury |
| FCE | Tests ability to function (work) |

**Choice of physician** — ✓ **state statute**

**Suspected malingering / fear** — elicit with ✓ **empathy** (lean forward, open questions, few notes)

### Team physician & athlete confidentiality

**Employed team physician** — inform **team and player** of athletically relevant issues\
✗ Wrong options: media

**Life-threatening risk (transient quadriplegia + stenosis)** — ✓ **ban from participation** · no constitutional right to play · **scholarship kept** · waivers don't protect

**Share without consent** — ✓ **treating chiropractor** (treatment)\
✗ Wrong options: mother, coach, reporter, fiancée

### Employment & billing

**Independent contractor** — ✓ **contractor determines means + methods**; employer determines result · employer pays no taxes, insurance, retirement, training

**Injection + separate osteoporosis management same day** — ✓ **procedure code + E&M with modifier 25**

### Access to care

**Paediatric access** — ✓ **type of insurance** (Medicaid vs private)
