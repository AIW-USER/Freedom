# Bone Matrix

*Basic Science · 18 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Composition of bone

| Component | % dry weight | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **Organic** | **40%** | Collagen (90% of organic) · proteoglycans · matrix proteins · cytokines/GFs |
| **Inorganic** | **60%** | **Calcium hydroxyapatite Ca₁₀(PO₄)₆(OH)₂** · osteocalcium phosphate (**brushite**) |

### Osteocalcin & matrix components

| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| **Type I collagen** | **Tensile strength** · triple helix: **two α1 + one α2** chains |
| **Proteoglycans** | ✓ **Compressive strength** (with HA) · **inhibit mineralisation** · bind growth factors · GAG–protein complexes |
| **Hydroxyapatite** | **Compressive strength** |
| **Osteocalcin** | **Most abundant non-collagenous protein** (10–20%) · by **mature osteoblasts** · promotes mineralisation · regulates bone density · attracts osteoclasts · ↑ by **1,25-(OH)₂D₃**, ↓ by **PTH** · **marker of bone formation/turnover** (serum, urine) |
| **Osteonectin** | **Glycoprotein that binds calcium** · from **platelets + osteoblasts** · regulates Ca / organises mineral |
| **Osteopontin** | **Cell-binding** protein |
| Cytokines / GFs | IL-1, IL-6, IGF, TGF-β, BMPs |

**Osteocalcin clinical** — **most specific marker of mature osteoblast** · serum levels track **BMD gain on osteoporosis treatment** and anabolic trials (teriparatide)

## X vs Y

| | Osteocalcin | Osteonectin |
|---|---|---|
| Source | **Osteoblasts** (✗ Wrong options: osteoclasts) | Platelets + osteoblasts |
| Key fact | Most abundant non-collagenous protein | **Ca-binding glycoprotein** |

| Strength | Provided by |
|---|---|
| **Tensile** | **Type I collagen** |
| **Compressive** | **Proteoglycans + hydroxyapatite** |

## How it's asked

**"Osteocalcin true EXCEPT?"** → **"glycoprotein that binds calcium"** (that is osteonectin)

**"Osteocalcin true EXCEPT (2)?"** → **"secreted by osteoclasts"**

**"Most responsible for compressive strength?"** → **proteoglycans**\
✗ Wrong options: type I collagen (tensile) · osteocalcin · osteonectin · osteopontin
