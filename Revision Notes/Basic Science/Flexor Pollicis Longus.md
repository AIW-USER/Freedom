# Flexor Pollicis Longus

*Basic Science · 37 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy

| | Flexor pollicis longus |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Anterior radius + adjacent interosseous membrane |
| **Insertion** | Base of **distal phalanx of thumb** |
| **Action** | Flexes thumb phalanges (IP joint) |
| **Innervation** | **Anterior interosseous nerve (C8, T1)** |
| **Arterial supply** | Anterior interosseous artery |

## 6 Complications

### AIN injury

**AIN** — branches **posteriorly from median nerve deep to pronator teres** → at risk in forearm fracture fixation

**Weak FPL + FDP index after both-bone forearm fixation** — ✓ **AIN injury**\
✗ Wrong options: PIN, radial, ulnar (don't supply) · LABCN (sensory)

## How it's asked

**"Weak FPL and index FDP after both-bone ORIF?"** → **anterior interosseous nerve**
