# Bone Circulation

*Basic Science · 15 / 108*

## 1 Basics

### Overview

**Share of cardiac output** — **5–10%** · intraosseous flow **5–20 mL/min/100 g**

**Tenuous supply** — **scaphoid** · **talus** · **femoral head** · **odontoid**

**Three sources (long bone)** — nutrient artery · metaphyseal–epiphyseal · periosteal

![Arterial vascularisation of a long bone](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9005-arterial-vascularization-of-long-bone.jpg)

### Blood supply to physis & diaphysis

| System | Pressure | Supplies |
|---|---|---|
| **Nutrient artery** | **High** | Enters via nutrient foramen → medullary canal → ascending/descending → **inner ⅔ of mature cortex** (Haversian) |
| Metaphyseal–epiphyseal | — | From periarticular plexus (e.g. geniculates) |
| **Periosteal arterioles** | **Low** | **Outer ⅓ of diaphyseal cortex** · linked by Volkmann (perpendicular) + Haversian (parallel) |
| **Perichondrial artery** (of LaCroix) | — | ✓ **Major source of nutrition of growth plate** |
| Epiphyseal artery | — | Proliferative zone of physis |

**Emissary system** — **venous** only · no "emissary artery"

**Venous flow after reamed nailing (TO)** — normally **centripetal** → reverses to **centrifugal after reamed IM nailing** (TO — verify: standard teaching is that **arterial** flow becomes **centripetal** (periosteal) after reaming)

**Periosteum (TO)** — outer fibrous + **inner cambium (osteogenic)** layer · anchored by **Sharpey's fibres** · **absent over articular surfaces + sesamoids (patella)**

### Direction of flow

| | Arterial | Venous |
|---|---|---|
| **Mature bone** | **Centrifugal** (inside → out; nutrient high-pressure dominates) | **Centripetal** (cortical capillaries → sinusoids → emissary veins) |
| **Immature bone** | **Centripetal** (periosteal dominates) | — |

**↑ Bone blood flow** — hypoxia · hypercapnia · sympathectomy

## 2 Mechanism

### After fracture

| Phase | Flow |
|---|---|
| **Immediate** | **↓ flow** · becomes **centripetal** (nutrient system disrupted, periosteal dominates) |
| Hours–days | **↑ flow** (regional acceleratory phenomenon) · **peaks 2 wk** · normal by **3–5 months** |

### Intramedullary nails

**Unreamed nail** — preserves endosteal supply

**Reaming** — devascularises **inner 50–80%** of cortex · delays endosteal revascularisation

**Loose-fitting nail** — spares cortical perfusion, faster reperfusion · **tight-fitting** → compromised, slow

## How it's asked

**"Disruption interrupts major nutrient supply to growth plate?"** → **perichondrial artery**\
✗ Wrong options: diaphyseal, metaphyseal, synovial

**"Supplies outer third of diaphysis?"** → **periosteal arterioles**\
✗ Wrong options: nutrient (inner ⅔) · emissary artery (doesn't exist) · perichondral (physis)

**"Direction of arterial flow in mature bone?"** → **centrifugal** · immature → **centripetal**

**"Immediately after fracture, flow becomes?"** → **↓ and centripetal**
