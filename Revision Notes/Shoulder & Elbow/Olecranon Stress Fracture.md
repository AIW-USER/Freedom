# Olecranon Stress Fracture

*Shoulder & Elbow · 30 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & location

**Frequency** — **rare**; predominantly **throwing athletes** (**baseball pitchers**), other high-level athletes; overuse injury

**Location** — predominantly the **olecranon epiphyseal plate** (persistent apophysis); line propagates **transverse or oblique**

**Age** — adolescent pitchers; may persist into **late adolescence** (**delayed closure of olecranon epiphysis**, 16–20 y; other elbow physes closed)

**Associated throwing injuries** — **UCL tear** · **medial epicondyle avulsion fracture**

## 2 Mechanism

| Theory (3 accepted) | Note |
|---|---|
| **Posteromedial olecranon impingement** | — |
| **Triceps traction force** | Repetitive forceful triceps contraction |
| **Valgus extension overload** | Part of **valgus-extension overload syndrome** |

**Torg & Moyer** — triceps contraction + **varus and valgus torques** → **olecranon epiphysis separates from epiphyseal plate**

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — **posteromedial olecranon pain** of throwing arm; **worse with throwing** (after several innings), **better with rest**

**Exam** — mild swelling, **localised tenderness over proximal olecranon** (medial, distal to epicondyle); full ROM; no mechanical symptoms

**Valgus stress test** — **instability → associated UCL injury**; **normal** in isolated stress fracture

**Late picture** — acute worsening + **inability to throw** after failed nonop

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Detail |
|---|---|
| **X-ray** | AP, lateral, oblique; optional **valgus stress** + **contralateral comparison** |
| X-ray findings | **Physeal widening, delayed fusion, fragmentation**; medial joint space widening if UCL injured |
| **CT** | Early confirmation; **best on lateral (sagittal) views**; **originates at articular surface → runs dorsal-proximal** |
| **MRI** | Early confirmation; **suspected UCL tear** → coronal **T2 fat-sat**: **T-sign** = UCL tear |

### Differential

| ✗ Distractor | Why wrong (11-yo pitcher, posteromedial tenderness, normal valgus stress) |
|---|---|
| Olecranon bursitis | No swelling/infection |
| OCD capitellum | No clicking / mechanical symptoms |
| UCL insufficiency | **Valgus stress normal** |
| Medial epicondylitis | Tenderness distal to epicondyle, over olecranon |

## 5 Management

```
Olecranon stress fracture
├─ First line → NSAIDs + rest ± splint (4–6 wk), no throwing up to 3 months
│      └─ delayed union / failed rest → ORIF
├─ Undisplaced → single large compression (cannulated) screw
└─ Displaced → treat as olecranon fracture (screw ± TBW or plate)
```

### Nonoperative

**Regimen** — short-term **NSAIDs**, **rest ± temporary splint**; **4–6 wk rest**, **removal from sport up to 3 months**; progressive ROM; **avoid valgus loading (throwing)**

**Adjuncts** — **electrical bone stimulation**; **vitamin D + calcium**

### Operative criteria – compression screw fixation

| Indication | Fixation |
|---|---|
| **Delayed union** / failed rest (e.g. **9 months**, or **two 6-wk rest periods**) | **Large compression screw** (intramedullary **cannulated**) |
| Undisplaced | **Single large screw** (e.g. **7.0 mm**) |
| Displaced | Screw **± tension band wire**, or plate |

**Plate fixation** — option once displaced (OB) · **not indicated** for this injury (another OB) — read the options

**✗ Next-step traps** — elbow arthroscopy · open osteophyte removal · ulnar nerve transposition · **cast + strengthening** after failed rest · physiotherapy · **long-arm cast** (stiffness; only reasonable briefly at onset) · **hinged-elbow brace** (no healing benefit)

![Compression screw + tension band construct, healed](../../MCQ/attachments/q1408-illa-olecranon-stress-fracture-fixation.jpg)

### Outcomes

**Rettig (7.0 mm screw, 5 pitchers)** — union mean **15.4 wk**; return to throwing mean **29.4 wk**

**Charlton & Chandler (16–20 y, delayed epiphyseal closure)** — UCL intact in all; ORIF with **TBW, screw ± autograft** → all **asymptomatic at 32 months** despite **> 30 months** pre-op pain

**Surgery** — reliable results when rest fails

## X vs Y

| | **Olecranon stress fracture** | **UCL insufficiency** |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | **Posteromedial olecranon** | — |
| Valgus stress test | **Normal** (unless UCL also injured) | **Positive / instability** |
| Imaging | Physeal widening, CT fracture line | Medial joint widening; MRI **T-sign** |

| | **Undisplaced** | **Displaced** |
|---|---|---|
| Fixation | **Single large compression screw** | Screw ± TBW or plate (as olecranon fracture) |

## How it's asked

**"19-yo pitcher, posterior elbow pain 9 months despite nonop, acute worsening — next?"** → **internal fixation with compression screw**\
✗ Wrong options: arthroscopy · osteophyte removal · ulnar transposition · cast

**"11-yo pitcher, posteromedial pain, tender over proximal olecranon, normal valgus stress — diagnosis?"** → **olecranon stress fracture**\
✗ Wrong options: UCL insufficiency · OCD · medial epicondylitis · bursitis

**"15-yo pitcher, two 6-wk rest periods failed, other physes closed — next?"** → **cannulated screw fixation**\
✗ Wrong options: plate · hinged brace · long-arm cast · physio

**"Mechanism theories?"** → **posteromedial impingement, triceps traction, valgus extension overload**

**"First-line treatment?"** → **NSAIDs + rest ± brief splint, no valgus loading**

**"Indication for ORIF?"** → **delayed union**
