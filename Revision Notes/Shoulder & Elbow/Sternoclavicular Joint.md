# Sternoclavicular Joint

*Shoulder & Elbow · 52 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Joint anatomy

**Type** — **diarthrodial saddle** joint, **incongruous (~50% contact)**; **fibrocartilage**; contains **intra-articular disc**\
✗ Wrong options: hyaline/synovial cartilage

**Motion** — arm elevation to **90° → 30° rotation** at SC joint

![SC joint: disc, anterior/posterior SC, costoclavicular, interclavicular ligaments](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3034-sternoclavicular-joint.jpg)

### Stabilising ligaments

| Ligament | Function |
|---|---|
| **Posterior SC capsular ligament** | **Primary restraint to anterior–posterior stability** |
| **Anterior SC ligament** | Primary restraint to **superior displacement** of medial clavicle |
| **Costoclavicular (rhomboid)** — anterior fasciculus | Resists **superior rotation + lateral displacement** |
| **Costoclavicular** — posterior fasciculus | Resists **inferior rotation + medial displacement** |
| **Intra-articular disc ligament** | **Prevents medial displacement**; secondary restraint to superior displacement |

### Medial clavicle anatomy

**First to ossify** — **clavicle**, **5th–6th week of gestation**

**Last to fuse** — **medial clavicular epiphysis**, closes **20–25 y** (last physis)

**Before 25 y** — most "SC dislocations" are **medial clavicular physeal fractures** → distinguish

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Serendipity view** — **40° cephalic tilt** of sternum + clavicles; best plain radiograph

![Serendipity view: 40° cephalic tilt](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3034-serendipity-view.jpg)

**CT** — **most sensitive + specific**

## 5 Management

### Instability after medial clavicle resection – figure-of-eight reconstruction

**Cause** — medial clavicle resection (e.g. for **SC degenerative joint disease**) compromising **costoclavicular ligament** → **medial clavicular instability**: ↑ pain, **medial bump**, digit tingling

**Treatment** — **semitendinosus figure-of-eight graft** through **clavicle + manubrium** → best **peak load-to-failure**

(✗ Wrong options: **Distractors** — subclavius tendon transfer) · medial clavicular osteotomy · further medial resection · SC arthrodesis

## X vs Y

| | **SC dislocation** | **Medial clavicle physeal fracture** |
|---|---|---|
| Age | Adult (physis closed) | **<25 y** (physis closes 20–25) |
| Frequency <25 y | Less common | **Most injuries** |

## How it's asked

**"Instability after medial clavicle resection (medial bump, tingling) — procedure?"** → **semitendinosus figure-of-eight graft**\
✗ Wrong options: subclavius transfer · further resection · arthrodesis

**"Primary restraint to AP translation of SC joint?"** → **posterior SC capsular ligament**

**"Ligament preventing medial displacement of clavicle?"** → **intra-articular disc ligament**

**"Best plain view of SC joint?"** → **serendipity view (40° cephalic tilt)**; **CT** most sensitive

**"Last physis to close?"** → **medial clavicle, 20–25 y**
