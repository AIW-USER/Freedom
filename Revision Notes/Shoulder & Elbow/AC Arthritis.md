# AC Arthritis

*Shoulder & Elbow · 1 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Frequency** — **most common cause of AC joint pain**

**Demographics** — ↑ with age; can start by **2nd decade**

**Classic patient** — **weight-lifters** + overhead throwing athletes (constant heavy overhead activity)

**Risk factors** — trauma · post-traumatic (**clavicle fracture, AC instability**) · **distal clavicle osteolysis** · inflammatory (RA) · post-infectious (septic arthritis)

### Pathophysiology

**Mechanism** — **large axial loads through a small contact area** → **repetitive microtrauma** → OA (**same mechanism as distal clavicle osteolysis**)

**Aggravators** — articular disc degeneration + oblique joint surface orientation → ↑ contact forces

### Anatomy – AC joint & ligaments

**AC joint** — **diarthrodial**; scapula ↔ clavicle; **fibrocartilaginous intra-articular disc** (analogous to knee meniscus); small surface, high axial + rotational loads

| Ligament | Stability provided | Injury → |
|---|---|---|
| **AC ligaments** | **Anterior–posterior (horizontal)** | AP instability |
| **Posterior + superior AC ligaments** | **Most important** AC stabilisers | **Posterior instability** of distal clavicle |
| Anterior + superior AC ligaments | Anterior | **Anterior instability** (one OB explanation) · **no instability** (another OB explanation) — read the options |
| **CC ligaments (conoid + trapezoid)** | **Superior–inferior (vertical)** | **Superior instability** |

![Axial view – AC joint ligaments; posterior + superior AC ligaments highlighted](../../MCQ/attachments/q211920-a-ac-joint.jpg)

**Not AC stabilisers** — **coracohumeral ligament** (rotator interval) · **coracoacromial ligament** (anterior acromion; released in subacromial decompression) · deltotrapezial fascia (only elevated in open DCE)

**Rare instabilities** — **inferior** (CC ligaments resist superior displacement) · **rotational** (rotation is normal baseline load)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — activity-related **superior shoulder pain**; overhead activity, **pressing (bench press, push-up)**, lying on affected side

**Palpation** — **direct AC joint tenderness**; prominent distal clavicle (osteophytes)

**Provocative** — **cross-body adduction test** (most classic) · **O'Brien active compression** (90° forward flexion) → AC pain

![Cross-body adduction test](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3049-crossarm.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Finding |
|---|---|
| **X-ray – Zanca view (15° cephalic tilt; 10–15° in TO/OB)** | **Best view**; osteophytes, **joint space narrowing**, **distal clavicle osteolysis** |
| X-ray caveat | Changes often present **without symptoms** (poor clinical correlation) |
| **MRI** | ↑ signal + **oedema** in AC joint; shows associated cuff / LHB pathology |

![Zanca view – AC osteophytes and joint space narrowing](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3049-zanca.jpg)

### Diagnostic injection

**AC joint injection (corticosteroid / local anaesthetic, e.g. bupivacaine)** — **diagnostic + therapeutic**; relief confirms AC source

**Accuracy** — joint hard to access, **often missed** → **ultrasound ↑ accuracy**; most get **no long-term relief**

## 5 Management

```
Symptomatic AC arthritis
├─ First line → activity modification (avoid pressing) + PT (shoulder girdle) + NSAIDs
├─ Persistent → corticosteroid injection (US-guided)
└─ Failed nonop, severe symptoms → distal clavicle excision (Mumford)
       ├─ Arthroscopic → resect 0.5–1 cm, evaluate + treat GH pathology
       └─ Open → meticulous deltotrapezial fascia repair
```

### Distal clavicle excision – preserving posterosuperior capsule

**Mumford procedure** — distal clavicle resection, arthroscopic (burr) or open; indication = **severe symptoms, failed nonop**

| Resection amount | Source |
|---|---|
| **0.5–1.0 cm** (lateral clavicle) | Orthobullets, OB |
| **4–8 mm** may suffice arthroscopically (preserves superior AC ligaments → less bone than open) | OB (Nuber) |
| **>1–1.5 cm** = aggressive → **instability** | Orthobullets, OB |

**Structure to preserve** — **posterosuperior joint capsule / posterior + superior AC ligaments** → prevents **AP (posterior) instability**

**Over-resection** — AC or CC ligament damage → instability; even correct length + posterosuperior damage → AP instability

**Under-resection** — persistent pain

![Arthroscopic distal clavicle excision](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3049-dcr.jpg)

### Arthroscopic vs open distal clavicle excision

**Outcomes** — similar; choice = surgeon preference

**Only proven arthroscopic advantage** — **evaluation of the glenohumeral joint** + treat associated pathology (**rotator cuff, LHB, labrum / SLAP**)

**Missed pathology with open** — **15 missed SLAP lesions** in failed open DCE (Berg) → do **diagnostic arthroscopy even with open DCE**

**Not proven arthroscopic advantages** — lower complication rate · lower infection rate · ↓ surgical time · preservation of CC or inferior AC ligaments

**Other arthroscopic pluses (OB)** — direct view of clavicle end, less soft-tissue violation, shorter rehab, quicker return

**Open DCE** — requires **meticulous deltotrapezial fascia repair**

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Cause |
|---|---|
| **AP instability** | **Over-resection >1–1.5 cm** · sacrificing **posterior + superior AC ligaments** |
| **Superior–inferior instability** | Iatrogenic; resection compromising **CC ligaments** |
| **Persistent pain** | **Incomplete resection** – most commonly **posterior-superior distal clavicle** |
| Heterotopic ossification | |
| Stiffness, infection | Causes of failed DCE (OB) |
| **Deltoid dehiscence** | Inadequate **deltotrapezial fascia repair** after **open** DCE |

**Failed DCE management** — conservative, revision, or **ligament reconstruction**

## X vs Y

| | **AC ligaments** | **CC ligaments** |
|---|---|---|
| Components | Superior, posterior, anterior, inferior | **Conoid + trapezoid** |
| Plane | **Anterior–posterior** | **Superior–inferior** |
| Injured by | Aggressive DCE / debridement | Over-aggressive resection (iatrogenic) |
| Instability | **Posterior** (post + sup injured) | **Superior** |

| | **Arthroscopic DCE** | **Open DCE** |
|---|---|---|
| GH evaluation | **Yes** | No (add scope) |
| Key closure step | — | **Deltotrapezial fascia repair** |
| Specific risk | Under-resection (visualisation) | **Deltoid dehiscence**, missed SLAP |

## How it's asked

**"Weightlifter, superior shoulder pain with bench press — best X-ray view?"** → **Zanca (15° cephalic tilt)**

**"Weightlifter, persistent popping after arthroscopic DCE, posterosuperior ligaments damaged — instability direction?"** → **posterior**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior · inferior · rotational · superior = CC ligaments

**"Structure most important to preserve during arthroscopic DCE?"** → **posterosuperior joint capsule**\
✗ Wrong options: coracohumeral · coracoacromial · anterosuperior capsule · deltotrapezial insertion

**"Only documented benefit of arthroscopic over open DCE?"** → **ability to evaluate the glenohumeral joint**\
✗ Wrong options: lower complications · ↓ surgical time · CC / inferior AC ligament preservation

**"Persistent pain after DCE — commonest cause?"** → **incomplete resection of posterior-superior distal clavicle**

**"Deltoid weakness after open DCE?"** → **inadequate deltotrapezial fascia repair**

**"How much clavicle to resect arthroscopically?"** → **0.5–1 cm** (4–8 mm may suffice; >1–1.5 cm → instability)
