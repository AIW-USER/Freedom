# Acromioclavicular Joint

*Shoulder & Elbow · 2 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Osteology & articular disc

**Joint type** — **diarthrodial**; capsule + intra-articular synovium + articular cartilage interface

**Intra-articular disc** — **fibrocartilaginous**; usually **meniscoid-like** · complete disc **very rare** (TO); variable size/shape

**Cartilage (TO)** — hyaline → **fibrocartilage**: **acromial side by age 17** · **clavicular side by age 24**

### Motion

**Clavicle** — rotates **40–50° posteriorly** with shoulder elevation

**Through AC joint** — only **8°** of rotation; remainder = **scapular rotation + SC joint motion**

**Joint itself** — **gliding motions only**; most motion from the bones, not through the joint

### Stability – AC vs CC ligaments

| Restraint | Direction | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **AC ligaments / capsule** (superior, inferior, anterior, posterior) | **Horizontal (AP)** | **Superior strongest**, then **posterior**; constrain posterior clavicular rotation; small-displacement vertical stability (TO) |
| **Trapezoid** (CC; **anterolateral**) | Horizontal + vertical loads | Inserts **3 cm** from clavicle end; **>3× thicker at clavicular end** (TO); origin **posterior half of coracoid dorsum** (TO) |
| **Conoid** (CC; **posteromedial**) | **Vertical** | Inserts **4.5 cm** from clavicle end, **posterior border**; **more important** CC ligament; clavicular insertion **~2× wider/thicker** than coracoid → **inverted cone** (TO); origin more posterior on **coracoid base** (TO) |
| Deltotrapezial fascia, capsule, deltoid, trapezius | Additional | Secondary stabilisers |

![Coracoclavicular ligaments – trapezoid and conoid](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3033-cc-ligaments-v4.jpg)

**Normal CC distance** — **11–13 mm** (superior coracoid → inferior clavicle)

### Biomechanics of the AC ligaments (TO)

**AP vs vertical (TO)** — AC capsule + ligaments give **3× more control in AP** than vertical direction

**Thickness (TO)** — **1–5 mm**; inner osseous insertion **3–4 mm from joint line**, peripheral fibres farther

**Superior ligament** — **most robust**; principal horizontal stabiliser; **~50%** of AP strength (OB); **thickest posteriorly**

**Posterior ligament** — adds **~25%** of AP strength (OB)

**Two-bundle model (TO)** — **superoposterior bundle** resists **posterior** clavicular translation · thin **anteroinferior bundle** limits **anterior** displacement

## 5 Management

### Distal clavicle excision – posterosuperior AC ligaments

**Mumford procedure** — (arthroscopic) distal clavicle resection for **AC arthrosis**; resection length **debated**

**Must preserve** — **superior + posterior AC ligaments / capsule** → prevents post-op **AP (posterior) instability**

**Over-resection sign** — popping + pain + **mild posterior instability**, no deformity on X-ray (e.g. 8 mm excised in weightlifter) → **posterior + superior AC ligaments compromised**

**✗ Wrong option: anterior + superior AC** — opposite answer; these resist **anterior** translation

**✗ Conoid / trapezoid** — **vertical** restraints; injured in **grade III or higher** separations → superior clavicular migration

**✗ Wrong option: coracohumeral ligament** — not an AC stabiliser

### AC separation – reduction & stabilisation

| AC separation | Feature | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Type III** | — | **Controversial** |
| **Type V** | Clavicle elevated **100–300%** + **extensive soft-tissue stripping** | **Surgical AC reduction + stabilisation** |

![Type V AC separation – marked clavicular prominence](../../MCQ/attachments/q8711-a-14a.jpg)

**✗ Type V distractors** — sling · NSAIDs + rapid return · arthroscopic partial claviculectomy · acromionectomy

## X vs Y

| | **AC ligaments** | **CC ligaments (conoid + trapezoid)** |
|---|---|---|
| Plane | **Horizontal / AP** | **Vertical** |
| Strongest component | **Superior** (then posterior) | **Conoid** (more important) |
| Threatened by | **Distal clavicle excision** (too much) | **Grade ≥III** AC separation |
| Failure → | **Posterior instability**, popping | **Superior clavicle migration** |

| | **Trapezoid** | **Conoid** |
|---|---|---|
| Position | **Anterolateral** | **Posteromedial** |
| Distance from clavicle end | **3 cm** | **4.5 cm** |
| Shape (TO) | >3× thicker at clavicular end | **Inverted cone** |
| Role | Horizontal + vertical | **Vertical** (primary) |

## How it's asked

**"Structure responsible for AP stability of AC joint?"** → **posterosuperior part of capsule** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: anteroinferior capsule · trapezoid · conoid

**"Arthroscopic distal clavicle excision — preserve what to prevent AP instability?"** → **superior + posterior AC capsule**\
✗ Wrong options: trapezoid · conoid · coracohumeral

**"Popping + posterior clavicle instability after Mumford — what was compromised?"** → **posterior + superior AC ligaments**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior + superior

**"Quarterback, clavicle tenting under skin (type V) — management?"** → **AC reduction + stabilisation**\
✗ Wrong options: sling · NSAIDs · claviculectomy · acromionectomy

**"Primary vertical stabiliser of AC joint?"** → **conoid ligament**

**"Strongest AC ligament?"** → **superior** (~50% AP strength), then posterior (~25%)
