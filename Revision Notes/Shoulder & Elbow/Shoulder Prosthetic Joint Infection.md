# Shoulder Prosthetic Joint Infection

*Shoulder & Elbow · 50 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Significance** — **major cause of revision within first 2 years** after shoulder arthroplasty

**Incidence** — **~4% primary TSA + RSA** (Orthobullets, OB) · 3–4% · 0–5% · up to 4–5% (other OB explanations) — read the options · **~15% revision** TSA/RSA

**aTSA vs rTSA** — **no difference** in primary shoulders without prior surgery · ↑ in primary **rTSA after ≥1 prior non-arthroplasty operation** (Florschütz)

### Risk factors & prevention (smoking, steroid injection, nasal decolonisation)

| Risk factor | Note |
|---|---|
| **Male** | **2.5×** PJI rate · ↑ C. acnes colonisation |
| **Arthroplasty for fracture / trauma** | **3×** vs elective |
| **Revision surgery** | ~15% |
| **Intra-articular steroid injection within 3 months** | Injection **4 months** (or 15 months) before = **not** a risk factor |
| **Smoking** | ↑ infection + fracture + loosening |
| **Young age** | — |
| Postoperative haematoma | — |
| **Coagulopathy**, **ESRD**, **lupus**, **RA**, uncontrolled comorbidities | Medical comorbidities |

**Pre-op smoker** — **smoking cessation programme next** (smoking = suggested contraindication to TSA/RSA) \
✗ Wrong options: PT trial (pseudoparalysis CTA) · tramadol (= 1st line knee OA) · CBC/ESR/CRP (infection unlikely) · rheumatology referral

**Complication risk (patient)** — younger age · ↑ BMI · RA · diabetes · smoking

**C. acnes colonisation correlates** — **male** · **native hair at surgical site** · **humeral loosening**\
✗ Wrong options: age >70 · CKD · female · glenoid lucent lines

| ✓ Validated prevention | Detail |
|---|---|
| **MRSA (methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus) screening + nasal decolonisation** | If nares colonized → application of **5% povidone-iodine solution to each nostril** pre-operatively (**10 s/nostril, 1 h pre-op**) · or **2% mupirocin BID × 5 days** |
| IV antibiotic prophylaxis | Within **30 min** of incision, continue **24 h** |
| Penicillin allergy prophylaxis | **Clindamycin for C. acnes** (Orthobullets, OB) · **vancomycin, not clindamycin** (OB) — read the options |
| Skin prep | **Chlorhexidine (ChloraPrep)** · hydrogen peroxide prep |
| Hair removal | **Electric clippers** |
| Intra-op | Frequent **saline irrigation** · **dilute povidone-iodine** before closure |
| Closure / dressing | Dermabond · **silver-impregnated** dressings |

| Not validated | Note |
|---|---|
| **Axillary hair shaving** | No meaningful ↓ |
| **Sterile helmets/hoods** | May **↑** infection |
| **Laminar flow** | Not validated |

## 2 Mechanism

### C. acnes – microbiology, prolonged culture & antibiotic choice

**Most common organism** — **Cutibacterium acnes** (formerly Propionibacterium acnes) · others: S. aureus, S. epidermidis

**Description** — **gram-positive, anaerobic rod** (OB) · **gram-positive rod, facultative anaerobe** (OB) · "gram-positive, facultative, aerotolerant, anaerobic rod" (Orthobullets) · **ferments lactose → propionic acid** · non-spore-forming

**Habitat** — **axilla**, dermal **sebaceous glands**; commensal skin flora

**Biofilm** — forms in **18–90 h** on implant + synovium (>> planktonic) → **aspiration only 17% sensitive**

**Behaviour** — indolent, low-grade, low virulence · usually **no swelling, erythema, fever, pus** · inflammatory markers **often normal**

**Culture** — mean incubation **7–21 days** · usually negative in first **3–5 days** · may take **up to 3 weeks**

![C. acnes – gram-positive rods](../../MCQ/attachments/q218951-e-figure-e.jpg)

| ✗ Organism distractor | Description |
|---|---|
| **S. aureus** | GP cocci, facultative anaerobe, **coagulase +** · commonest in **hip/knee** PJI |
| **S. epidermidis** | GP cocci, facultative anaerobe, **coagulase −** |
| **E. coli** | GN rod, facultative anaerobe · rare in shoulder |
| **Klebsiella** | GN **encapsulated** rod |
| N. gonorrhoeae | GN diplococci |
| C. diphtheriae | GP **club-shaped**, nonmotile, noncapsulated bacillus |
| Streptococci · fungi | Rare · exceedingly rare |

### Biofilm & timing

**Biofilm (all bacteria)** — on implant **within 4 weeks** · **15% cells + 85% polysaccharide (glycocalyx)** → adherence + immune protection · no safe removal → **explant if >4 wk**

**Source** — **direct** (sinus tract, wound dehiscence) · **haematogenous** (dental work, gallbladder) in longstanding infection-free joint

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **persistent pain (most common)** · **draining sinus (2nd)** · systemic (fever, chills, sweats) · **stiffness**

**Sinus tract to joint** — **diagnostic**

**Typical C. acnes stem** — months–years after arthroplasty, **gradually ↑ pain**, normal WBC/ESR/CRP, humeral lucency

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Classification – time of onset

| | **Acute** | **Chronic** |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | **<3–6 wk** (CDC **<90 days**) | **>3–6 wk** (CDC **>90 days**) |
| Biology | Confined to joint; no bone–implant invasion; less biofilm | **Biofilm**; invades **prosthesis–bone interface** |
| Treatment | **I&D + retention (DAIR)** | **2-stage revision** |

**DAIR window** — **<4 wk** (Orthobullets treatment) · **within 5–6 wk** (OB) · <3–6 wk (Orthobullets classification) — read the options

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — normal early · osteopenia · **periprosthetic lucencies** · pseudosubluxation of head · effusion · **endosteal scalloping** · bony resorption

**Humeral vs glenoid loosening** — **glenoid loosening = most common cause of TSA failure**, not specific for infection · **humeral loosening → suspect PJI**

![Glenoid radiolucency after TSA](../../MCQ/attachments/q216841-a-tsa-poly.jpg)

**US / MRI** — fluid collections (US: loculated away from GH joint)

**Bone scan** — not routinely (efficacy not shown)

**PET** — adjunct; **98% sensitivity + 98% specificity**

### Genes & lab markers

| Marker | Fact |
|---|---|
| WBC | Not sensitive/specific; **normal in C. acnes** |
| **CRP** | Peaks **2–3 d** post-op → normal **14–21 d**; may be normal in C. acnes |
| **ESR** | Peaks **5–7 d** → normal **90 d**; may be normal |
| ESR/CRP overall | **Insufficient sensitivity/specificity** for PJI |
| Alpha-defensin | Investigational |
| Synovial WBC | **>50,000** |
| Crystals | **Don't exclude** infection |
| Gram stain | Often **negative**; + in ~75% |

### Diagnostic work-up – aspiration

**Synovial tissue culture** — **gold standard** (+ ~80%) · **≥4 specimens** at surgery · **aerobic + anaerobic + broth** media

**Hold cultures** — **14–28 days** (Orthobullets) · **14–21 days / 2–3 weeks** (OB) — read the options · fungal **4 wk** · mycobacterial **8 wk**

**Aspiration** — **low sensitivity + low NPV** · **dry tap common** → **does not rule out** · **+ S. aureus culture = high specificity + PPV**

**Fluoroscopic / US guidance** — ensures intra-articular placement · **no ↑ PPV/NPV or yield**, operator-dependent, ↑ cost

**Antibiotics** — stop **2 weeks before aspiration**\
✗ Wrong options: current antibiotics don't affect yield

**No growth at 5 days** — **observe cultures additional 2 weeks** before revision\
✗ Wrong options: revision · arthroscopic I&D

## 5 Management

```
Shoulder PJI
├─ Unfit / unwilling → antibiotic suppression (fails 60–75%)
├─ Acute (<4 wk; 5–6 wk OB) → I&D + component retention (DAIR)
│      └─ exchange mobile parts (glenosphere, poly liner, tray)
└─ Chronic
       ├─ fit, unknown organism, adequate bone → 2-stage exchange (gold standard)
       ├─ low-virulence known organism, healthy tissue → 1-stage revision
       └─ frail / poor bone / recalcitrant → resection arthroplasty
```

### Antibiotics

**C. acnes drug of choice** — **penicillin** (low MIC) · penicillin G, amoxicillin \
✗ Wrong options: TMP-SMX · meropenem · vancomycin (not 1st line)

**Penicillin allergy** — **ceftriaxone or clindamycin**

**Duration** — prolonged IV; guided by cultures + ID consult

| Mechanism of action | Antibiotic |
|---|---|
| **Binds transpeptidase (PBP) → inhibits cell wall synthesis** | **Penicillin** ✓ |
| Reversibly binds **50S** | Macrolides |
| **Irreversibly** binds **30S** | Aminoglycosides |
| Blocks **30S–50S initiation complex** | **Linezolid** |
| Binds cell wall, disrupts peptidoglycan **cross-linking** | **Vancomycin** |

### Operative criteria

| Option | Indications | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Antibiotic suppression** | Severely ill, unwilling, multiple comorbidities | **Fails 60–75%** |
| **I&D + retention** | **Acute** infection | **50% failure** |
| **Single-stage revision** | Uncommon; **low-virulence** organism with good sensitivity; healthy patient + soft tissue | Up to **90%**; ↓ stay, cost, antibiotic time · consider **RSA** (cuff sacrificed) |
| **2-stage exchange** | **Gold standard**; medically fit; **unknown organism**; adequate bone stock; eradication before stage 2 | **60–90%**; some keep spacer |
| **Resection arthroplasty** | Frail/complex, **insufficient bone stock**, recalcitrant | Poor function; **pain relief >50%**; **tuberosity preservation** predicts better result |
| Arthrodesis | Rare (bone stock poor) | Pain ↓ |
| Amputation | **Life-threatening** infection | — |

**Spacer** — antibiotic-PMMA; local antibiotics, **pseudocapsule**, maintains soft-tissue tension, ↓ pain, allows PT · stem from **Steinmann pin + chest tube**

![Resection arthroplasty](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12757-resection-arthroplasty.jpg)

**Resection** — **antero-superior subluxation** if tuberosities + cuff removed

**Arthrodesis** — S-shaped incision · resect cuff, biceps tenodesis, decorticate glenoid/head/acromion undersurface · **10-hole 4.5 mm pelvic recon plate** · **30° abduction, 30° flexion, 30° IR** · complications: infection, nonunion, malposition, prominent hardware, humeral shaft fracture

**Approach** — **deltopectoral** for all

## 6 Complications

**Complications** — **failure to eradicate** (inadequate clearance after suppression, I&D, 1-stage) · recurrent infection (2-stage) · humerus/glenoid fracture · poor bone stock

## X vs Y

| | **Shoulder PJI** | **Hip/knee PJI** |
|---|---|---|
| Commonest organism | **C. acnes** | **S. aureus / S. epidermidis** |
| Inflammatory markers | **Often normal** (C. acnes) | ↑ with S. aureus / MRSA |
| Aspiration value | Less clear: low sensitivity, dry taps | Clearer |
| Gold-standard treatment | 2-stage | 2-stage |

## How it's asked

**"rTSA, drainage at 4 wk, I&D + poly exchange, cultures negative at 10 d — MOA of best antibiotic?"** → **binds transpeptidase, inhibits cell wall synthesis (penicillin)**\
✗ Wrong options: 50S macrolide · 30S aminoglycoside · linezolid · vancomycin

**"Normal labs, painful aTSA, aspiration — hold cultures 2–3 wk for?"** → **gram-positive, anaerobic bacteria**\
✗ Wrong options: GP aerobic cocci · GN rods · diplococci · club-shaped

**"18-mo TSA, lucent lines, normal labs, no growth at 5 d — next step?"** → **observe cultures additional 2 weeks**\
✗ Wrong options: arthroscopic I&D · revision

**"Independent correlate of P. acnes colonisation?"** → **native hair at surgical site**\
✗ Wrong options: age >70 · CKD · female · glenoid lucent lines

**"aTSA 2 mo ago, infection confirmed — organism characteristics?"** → **gram-positive rod, facultative anaerobe**\
✗ Wrong options: coagulase + / − cocci · GN rods

**"C. acnes grown — antibiotic of choice?"** → **penicillin**\
✗ Wrong options: TMP-SMX · clindamycin = allergy only · meropenem · vancomycin

**"Smoker with CTA planned for arthroplasty — next step?"** → **smoking cessation programme**

**"rTSA infection — NOT a risk factor?"** → **steroid injection 4 months before surgery** (✓ risk: coagulopathy, fracture, male, smoking)

**"Validated PJI prevention before rTSA?"** → **5% povidone-iodine to each nostril if MRSA-colonised**\
✗ Wrong options: axillary shaving · helmets · laminar flow · clindamycin for allergy

**"rTSA 2 y, ↑ ESR/CRP — most common organism (Gram picture)?"** → **gram-positive rod (C. acnes)**\
✗ Wrong options: staph · strep · E. coli · fungus

**"Shoulder aspiration — true statement?"** → **positive S. aureus culture = high specificity**\
✗ Wrong options: high sensitivity/NPV · dry tap rules out · fluoroscopy ↑ PPV/NPV · antibiotics don't matter

**"Gold standard for chronic shoulder PJI?"** → **2-stage exchange**

**"Acute PJI within weeks of surgery?"** → **I&D + component retention (DAIR)**
