# Suprascapular Neuropathy

*Shoulder & Elbow · 54 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy – suprascapular nerve course

**Origin** — **C5–C6**, **superior (upper) trunk** of brachial plexus → across **posterior triangle of neck** → superior border of scapula

**Course** — under **superior transverse scapular (suprascapular) ligament** at **suprascapular notch** → supraspinous fossa (**supraspinatus**) → around lateral border of scapular spine through **spinoglenoid notch** (under **spinoglenoid ligament**) → infraspinous fossa (**infraspinatus**)

**Suprascapular ligament** — from medial base of coracoid over notch · **artery ABOVE, nerve BELOW**

**Function** — **motor only** (SS + IS) · **no sensory** → no sensory deficit

**Transglenoid fixation risk** — nerve lies **1.5 cm medial** to glenoid labrum at spinoglenoid notch

![Suprascapular nerve — sites of compression](../../MCQ/attachments/q213127-illa-suprascapular-nerve-sites-of-compression.jpg)

| Site | Level | Muscles weak |
|---|---|---|
| **Suprascapular notch** | **Proximal** | **Supraspinatus + infraspinatus** |
| **Spinoglenoid notch** | **Distal** (SS branch already given off) | **Infraspinatus only** |

**TO wording** — "infraglenoid notch" compression → infraspinatus only (= spinoglenoid notch)

**Atraumatic compression sites** — **suprascapular + spinoglenoid notches** (fascial bands, ganglion cysts) \
✗ Wrong options: scalenus anterior · cervical rib · conjoined tendon · subcoracoid

### Epidemiology & associations

**Who** — **overhead athletes**: **volleyball** (classic), baseball pitchers/catchers, tennis · also weightlifters, rowers, linebackers

**Associated** — **SLAP tears** · posterior labral tears · internal impingement

## 2 Mechanism

### Suprascapular notch – causes

**Causes** — **ganglion cyst** (often with labral tear) · **transverse scapular ligament** entrapment/thickening · **fracture callus**

### Spinoglenoid notch – causes

**Causes** — **posterior labral tear → cyst** · spinoglenoid ligament · spinoglenoid notch ganglion · **traction** (**45% of volleyball players**) · transglenoid fixation

**Cyst mechanism** — labral tear = **one-way valve** → synovial fluid exits joint, can't drain → paralabral cyst

**Cyst frequency** — ganglion cysts at **spinoglenoid notch** > **less commonly suprascapular notch** · cyst origin: transverse scapular ligament, scapular fibrous tissue, or GH joint

**Labral source** — **SLAP** most often, posterior labrum less (OB) · **posterior labral tear** (Orthobullets, OB) — read the options; posterior SLAP / **posterosuperior** labral tear fits both

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **deep, diffuse, posterolateral shoulder pain** · weakness, fatigue with overhead activity · ↓ velocity · no numbness

**Suprascapular notch** — tender notch · **supraspinatus weak**: abduction 90° + 30° FF + IR (**Jobe +**) · **infraspinatus weak**: **ER with elbow at side** · atrophy above + below spine

**Spinoglenoid notch** — **isolated weak ER with arm at side** · **atrophy below scapular spine** (posterior shoulder) · **supraspinatus normal** (empty can negative) · O'Brien + (associated SLAP)

![Isolated infraspinatus atrophy](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3063-clinical-image-infraspinatus-atrophy-smaller.jpg)

**Most specific exam finding (spinoglenoid cyst)** — **weakness of ER**\
✗ Wrong options: impingement sign · apprehension · active compression · abduction weakness

**ER in elevation** — isolated IS palsy: **full ER with arm elevated** · combined SS + IS: **can't ER in forward elevation**

### Distinguishing shoulder tests

| Test | Muscle / lesion | In SSN entrapment |
|---|---|---|
| **Jobe / empty can** | **Supraspinatus** | Abnormal at **suprascapular notch** ✓ |
| **ER with arm at side** | **Infraspinatus** | Abnormal at **both** sites ✓ |
| **Hornblower** (90° abduction + 90° ER, falls into IR) | **Teres minor** (axillary) | Normal |
| **Belly press · bear hug · lift-off** | **Subscapularis** | Normal |
| **Anterior load and shift** (supine, 40–60° abduction + FF) | Anterior instability | Normal |
| **Kim test** (seated, 90° abduction → 45° FF + axial + posteroinferior force) | **Posterior instability** | Normal |
| **Jerk test** | **Posterior labral tear** | May be + (cyst source) |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**MRI** — identify **compressive mass / cyst** + associated **labral lesion** · cyst: well-defined, **low T1, high T2** · denervation: ↓ bulk + **fatty infiltration** (SS ± IS)

**Paralabral cyst** — most commonly with **labral tears**

![Spinoglenoid notch cyst](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3063-spinoglenoid-notch-cystplain.jpg)

![Suprascapular notch cyst](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3063-suprascapular-notch-cyst.jpg)

### Diagnosis – EMG & compression sites

**EMG/NCV** — **diagnostic**; with MRI = investigations of choice · localises compression

**Isolated ER weakness + atrophy, MRA normal (no cyst, no tear)** — **EMG/NCV first** → found notch entrapment with early SS denervation\
✗ Wrong options: cervical MRI first · subacromial steroid · notch release before EMG · CRP/ESR

**C5 radiculopathy** — weakness out of proportion; not when specific SSN pattern

| Lesion (distractor) | Nerve / site | Deficit |
|---|---|---|
| **Quadrangular (quadrilateral) space** | **Axillary + PHCA** | **Teres minor** atrophy, deltoid (forward elevation, abduction) |
| **Triangular interval** | **Radial nerve + profunda brachii** | Elbow + wrist extension |
| Subscapular recess | Anterior to subscap, below coracoid | Loose bodies |
| Upper/lower subscapular nerves | Subscapularis | IR (lift-off) |
| Long thoracic (C5–7 roots) | Serratus anterior | Not affected by spinoglenoid cyst |

**Brachial plexus figure** — suprascapular = **A (upper trunk)** · dorsal scapular · medial pectoral · lower + upper subscapular = wrong options

## 5 Management

```
Suprascapular neuropathy
├─ No structural lesion on MRI → activity modification + rehab ≥6 months
│      └─ fails ~1 year → surgical decompression (notch release / spinoglenoid ligament release)
├─ Suprascapular notch cyst → decompression at suprascapular notch
└─ Spinoglenoid cyst + labral tear → arthroscopic labral repair + cyst decompression
```

### Operative criteria

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **No structural lesion** | **Nonop ≥6 months** (posterior capsule stretching for spinoglenoid) |
| No lesion + failed **~1 year** nonop | Suprascapular notch release · or **spinoglenoid ligament release** (posterior approach) |
| **Structural lesion (cyst) at suprascapular notch** | **Nerve decompression at notch** |
| **Labral tear + spinoglenoid cyst** | **Arthroscopic labral repair + cyst decompression** ✓ |

### Treatment – labral repair with cyst decompression

**Young competitive athlete, posterior labral tear + spinoglenoid cyst** — **cyst decompression + posterior labral repair**\
✗ Wrong options: 6 wk rest · PT · suprascapular notch decompression · cuff repair + acromioplasty

**Catcher, posterosuperior tear + large cyst, severe ER weakness** — **arthroscopic repair + decompression**\
✗ Wrong options: aspiration + steroid · rest · acromioplasty · rehab

**Labral tear key** — address tear (cause of cyst) · **no isolated cyst decompression** when tear present

**Labral repair alone** — not enough (doesn't decompress cyst, atrophy may progress — OB) · "good outcomes with labral repair alone; needle aspiration also used" (one OB explanation) — read the options; best answer = **repair + decompression**

**Failed 6 wk PT with cyst + IS atrophy** — operate\
✗ Wrong options: another 6 wk PT · cervical MRI + EMG · tenodesis

**Mechanical compression** — surgical decompression usually required

## 6 Complications

### Iatrogenic injury

**Arthroscopic SSN release at suprascapular notch** → injured nerve → weak **abduction, forward elevation + ER**, wasting **above + below scapular spine** = suprascapular notch level\
✗ Wrong options: spinoglenoid = IS only · axillary · spinal accessory · dorsal scapular

**SLAP repair** — anchors/instruments **medial to glenoid** → SSN injury

## X vs Y

| | **Suprascapular notch** | **Spinoglenoid notch** |
|---|---|---|
| Muscles | **SS + IS** | **IS only** |
| Weakness | **Abduction (Jobe) + ER** | **ER at side** only |
| Atrophy | Above + below spine | **Below spine** |
| Usual cause | Cyst, **transverse scapular ligament**, callus | **Paralabral cyst (SLAP/posterior labrum)**, traction |
| Treatment (lesion) | Decompress at notch | **Labral repair + cyst decompression** |

| | **Spinoglenoid cyst** | **Quadrilateral space syndrome** |
|---|---|---|
| Nerve | Suprascapular | **Axillary** |
| Atrophy | **Infraspinatus** | **Teres minor** (± deltoid) |
| Test | ER at side weak | Hornblower; forward elevation/abduction weak |
