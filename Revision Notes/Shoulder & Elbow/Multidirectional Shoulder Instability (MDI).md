# Multidirectional Shoulder Instability (MDI)

*Shoulder & Elbow · 28 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & epidemiology

**MDI** — symptomatic instability in **≥2 planes** (anterior, posterior, inferior) from **capsular redundancy**; **one plane must be inferior** (TO, OB)

**Age** — peaks **2nd–3rd decades**

**Who** — overhead athletes: **swimmers, volleyball, gymnasts, throwers**, dancers

**Generalised laxity** — in **45–75%** of MDI patients undergoing surgery

### Torn loose / worn loose / born loose (TO)

| Type (TO) | Mechanism | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Torn loose** | Trauma | Traumatic dislocation (TUBS) |
| **Worn loose** | **Microtrauma / overuse** | Overhead athletes → **MDI** |
| **Born loose** | **Lax ligaments / capsule** | **Collagen disorders: Ehlers-Danlos, Marfan** → **MDI** |

### TUBS vs AMBRI (Matsen) (TO)

| **TUBS** | **AMBRI** |
|---|---|
| **T**raumatic dislocation → recurrent instability | **A**traumatic (minimal trauma) |
| **U**nidirectional | **M**ultidirectional |
| **B**ankart lesion most common | **B**ilateral (frequently) |
| **S**urgery mainstay | **R**ehabilitation first |
| — | **I**nferior capsular shift for nonresponders |

### Pathoanatomy

**Cause (TO)** — **inferior capsular redundancy**\
✗ Wrong options: Bankart · Hill-Sachs · cuff tear

**Hallmarks** — **patulous inferior capsule** (IGHL anterior + posterior bands) on MRI · **rotator interval deficiency**

**Add-on lesions (TO)** — from traumatic events: **Bankart** (anteroinferior labrum) · **Kim lesion** (posteroinferior labral avulsion) · glenoid erosion · Hill-Sachs, chondral, SLAP — **less often** than in unidirectional; rarely change treatment

### Glenohumeral stability

| Static restraints | Dynamic restraints |
|---|---|
| Glenohumeral ligaments | **Rotator cuff** — compresses head into glenoid (primary role) |
| Glenoid labrum | Biceps |
| Articular congruity + version | **Periscapular muscles** |
| **Negative intra-articular pressure** (release → head subluxes inferiorly) | |

**MDI = static deficiency** → therapy builds **dynamic** stabilisers to compensate

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **pain > instability** often · weakness · **paraesthesia / transient numbness** · crepitus · instability **during sleep** · atraumatic, self-reduced dislocations, never needed ED

**Impingement signs <20 y** — think **MDI** (TO)

**Voluntary dislocator** — "popping shoulders in and out" → **PT**; **contraindication to surgery**

### Diagnosis – sulcus sign, generalised laxity & capsular redundancy

| Test | MDI finding |
|---|---|
| **Sulcus sign** | **≥2+**; inferior traction at **0° and 45° abduction** (TO) → dimple below acromion; **most specific** for MDI (**2 cm → up to 97% specificity**) |
| **ER with arm adducted** (and at 90° abduction) | **↑ ER** = **incompetent rotator interval** |
| Anterior + posterior **load and shift** | **≥2+** both directions; less specific |
| Apprehension / relocation | Common in **both** traumatic + MDI |
| **Jerk / Kim tests** | Posterior traumatic **and** MDI |
| Neer / Hawkins | Impingement in **<20 y** → MDI |

![Sulcus sign – dimple below acromion with inferior traction](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3052-sulcus-sign.jpg)

| Sulcus grade (TO) | Acromiohumeral interval |
|---|---|
| **1+** | **<1 cm** |
| **2+** | **1–2 cm** |
| **3+** | **>2 cm** |

**2+/3+ sulcus + ↑ ER at side (TO)** — **pathologic rotator interval** + patulous inferior capsule

**Sulcus reflects** — **patulous inferior capsule + rotator interval** (TO, Orthobullets) · "incompetent rotator interval" (OB)

### Beighton score (generalised laxity)

| Item (9 points) | Points |
|---|---|
| **Palms to floor**, knees straight | 1 |
| **Genu recurvatum** | 2 |
| **Elbow hyperextension** | 2 |
| **Small-finger MCP hyperextension** (passive >90°) | 2 |
| **Thumb to ipsilateral forearm** | 2 |
| **Not included** | **Excessive forearm supination** |

**Cut-offs** — generalised laxity **>4/9** (Orthobullets) · **>6** associated with **connective tissue disorder** (Marfan, Ehlers-Danlos; OB)

![MCP hyperextension – generalised ligamentous laxity](../../MCQ/attachments/q937-a-mp-hyperextension.jpg)

**✗ Exam distractors** — cross-body adduction (**AC joint**) · O'Brien (**SLAP**) · **axillary webbing** (**pec major rupture**) · Jobe's (**supraspinatus**)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Finding |
|---|---|
| **X-ray** | Trauma series (**AP-IR, AP-ER, true AP, axillary, scapular Y**); **often normal** |
| **MR arthrogram** | Needed to assess **capsular volume**; **patulous inferior capsule** / capsular redundancy (e.g. posterior), **intact labrum** |
| MRI add-ons | Bankart (traumatic anterior) · Kim lesion (traumatic posterior) · glenoid bony erosion (chronic anterior) |
| **Arthroscopy** | **Drive-through sign**: scope passes easily between head + glenoid at **anterior band of IGHL** (also laxity) |

### Diagnostic criteria

| MDI requires | |
|---|---|
| Instability in **≥2 planes** | One **inferior** |
| **Sulcus ≥2+** | ± ↑ ER at side |
| **Load and shift ≥2+** | Anterior + posterior |

### Differential diagnosis

| Diagnosis | Clue |
|---|---|
| Anterior / posterior (traumatic) instability | TUBS, Bankart, **glenoid bone loss**, inverted pear |
| **Long thoracic nerve palsy** | Serratus weakness, **medial winging** |
| **Buford complex** | Normal variant: **absent anterosuperior labrum + cordlike MGHL** to base of biceps anchor |
| **GIRD** | IR deficit **25°** (OB) · **>20°** (TO, Orthobullets) vs contralateral → sleeper stretches |
| **Little leaguer's shoulder** | **Salter-Harris I** proximal humeral physis, excessive pitch counts |
| C-spine disease, brachial plexitis, TOS | Neurologic |

## 5 Management

```
MDI
├─ All patients first → dynamic stabilisation PT (cuff + periscapular)
│      └─ still within PT trial (e.g. 8 wk) → continue PT
├─ Failed extensive PT + pain/instability limiting ADLs/sport → capsular shift / plication + RI closure
├─ Voluntary dislocator → no surgery
└─ Collagen disorder / refractory → allograft capsular reconstruction
```

### Initial treatment – prolonged rotator cuff & periscapular strengthening

**First line** — **all patients**, incl. bilateral, voluntary, collagen disorders: **dynamic stabilisation PT** → **rotator cuff + periscapular** (serratus anterior, rhomboids) strengthening, proprioception, humeral head control, scapulohumeral rhythm + **home exercises**

**Early rehab** — **closed kinetic chain exercises** → safe co-contraction of scapular + cuff muscles

**Duration** — **3–6 months** (Orthobullets, TO, OB) · **≥6 months** (several OB) · **6–12 months** (one OB) — read the options

**✗ Initial distractors** — **immobilisation / sling** (not effective; comfort only) · posterior capsular stretching (= GIRD) · bracing · steroid injections · labral repair · HAGL repair · thermal capsulorrhaphy · open/arthroscopic shift before PT trial

**Outcome** — most patients satisfactory with nonop (pain + recurrence)

### Operative criteria

| Surgery for MDI | |
|---|---|
| **Indications** | **Failure of extensive PT** (sole indication, TO) · pain + instability interfering with ADLs / sport |
| **Contraindication** | **Voluntary dislocators** |
| Procedure of choice | **Inferior capsular shift** (open or arthroscopic) |
| Results | Good, but **less predictable** than traumatic anterior stabilisation |

### Surgery – capsular shift & rotator interval closure

**Inferior capsular shift** — capsule shifted **superiorly** → addresses patulous inferior capsule

**Capsular plication** — loose capsule to labrum, **balanced anterior + posterior** → ↓ capsular volume

**Rotator interval closure** — plicate **SGHL to MGHL** (anterosuperior capsule) → **eliminates sulcus sign** / inferior subluxation · greatest effect = **↓ ER with arm at side (0° abduction)** (~10°) \
✗ Wrong options: restricts ER in cocking · IR at 90° · flexion/cross-body

**Capsule rule** — tight **anterior** capsule → ↓ **ER** · tight **posterior** capsule → ↓ **IR**

**Do not eliminate sulcus** — SLAP repair · Bankart repair (anteroinferior only) · supraspinatus repair · SAD

**Open approach** — deltopectoral; subscapularis tenotomy vs split; also fix any labral pathology

**Allograft capsular reconstruction** — rare; refractory, collagen disorders

**Arthroscopic vs open** — similar outcomes; arthroscopic advantages

| Post-op rehab | |
|---|---|
| **0–4/6 wk** | Sling / immobiliser |
| **6–10 wk** | ADLs, **45° limit** abduction + ER |
| **10–16 wk** | Gradual ROM |
| **>16 wk** | Strengthening |
| **>10 months** | **Contact sports** (only after normal strength + motion) |

### Recurrent instability with glenoid bone loss – bony augmentation

**Clues to bone loss** — multiple recurrences · long history · instability in **mid-range (45–60° abduction + ER)**

| Glenoid bone loss | Management |
|---|---|
| **10%** | Arthroscopic repair still OK |
| **15%** + labral tear, first-time (contact athlete) | **Surgical stabilisation** (✗ Wrong options: prolonged PT) |
| **>20–25%** | **Bony augmentation (Latarjet**: coracoid + conjoint tendon) — arthroscopic/open Bankart fail |

**✗ Bone loss distractors** — arthroscopic Bankart · subscapularis advancement · open capsular shift · hemiarthroplasty (no arthritis)

## 6 Complications

### Thermal capsulorrhaphy – complications & failure

**Status** — **historical / contraindicated**; abandoned for high complication + failure rates

**Failure** — **high failure** for **anterior, posterior and MDI** at 2 + 5 y · recurrence peaks **2–3 wk** post-op (capsule weakest) · good 1-y result does not protect from later recurrence

**Capsule** — revision finds capsule **thin + patulous** · **capsular necrosis / attenuation / insufficiency**\
✗ Wrong options: thick fibrotic

**Shrinkage** — depends on **total thermal energy + rate of delivery** · denatured tissue **does heal**\
✗ Wrong options: fixed for peak temp

**Revision after thermal failure** — **open revision** → capsular attenuation = **contraindication to arthroscopic revision**\
✗ Wrong options: arthroscopic

**Axillary nerve injury** — **heat-induced**; can't actively raise arm, full passive ROM, normal X-ray

**Chondrolysis** — heat from **monopolar RF / laser** → progressive pain, ROM never regained, joint space loss

![Glenohumeral chondrolysis after thermal capsulorrhaphy](../../MCQ/attachments/q7888-a-100.jpg)

| ✗ Chondrolysis distractor | Actually causes |
|---|---|
| Supraspinatus tear | Cuff tear arthropathy, superior head migration |
| **Subscapularis dehiscence** | Open deltopectoral → **anterior instability** |
| **Anterior humeral circumflex coagulation** | **Osteonecrosis** (main head supply) |
| Non-compliance with PT | Stiffness, pain (✗ Wrong options: chondrolysis) |

### Surgical complications

**Recurrence** — **most common** after arthroscopic or open capsulorrhaphy

**Subscapularis deficiency** — after **open anteroinferior shift**; **+ lift-off**, excessive ER; late **anterior subluxation** on axillary view

**Loss of motion** — over/asymmetric tightening → ↓ ER; treat **Z-lengthening subscapularis**; rare

**Axillary nerve** — iatrogenic neurapraxia → observe; **abduction + ER moves nerve away** from glenoid

**Capsulorrhaphy arthropathy** — **posterior glenoid wear**, posterior head subluxation, **glenoid retroversion**, **IR contracture**; **Putti-Platt, Magnuson-Stack**

## X vs Y

| | **TUBS (traumatic)** | **AMBRI (MDI)** |
|---|---|---|
| Onset | Trauma | **Atraumatic** |
| Direction | Unidirectional | **≥2 incl. inferior** |
| Lesion | **Bankart**, bone loss | **Capsular redundancy, RI deficiency** |
| Side | Unilateral | **Bilateral** often |
| Treatment | **Surgery** | **Rehab first** → inferior capsular shift |

| | **MDI** | **GIRD / internal impingement** |
|---|---|---|
| Exam | Sulcus, ↑ ER, load-shift both ways, Beighton + | **IR deficit ≥25°** vs other side |
| Treatment | **Cuff + periscapular strengthening** | **Posteroinferior capsular (sleeper) stretching** |

## How it's asked

**"Cause of MDI?"** → **inferior capsular redundancy** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: Bankart · Hill-Sachs · cuff tear

**"Atraumatic recurrent dislocator, hyperextensible MCP — etiology?"** → **capsular redundancy**\
✗ Wrong options: inverted pear · LTN palsy · Bankart · Buford

**"Connective tissue feature EXCEPT?"** → **excessive forearm supination** (not Beighton)

**"Gymnast, self-reduced dislocations, normal MRA — exam finding?"** → **↑ ER in adduction**\
✗ Wrong options: cross-body · O'Brien · axillary webbing · Jobe

**"Most specific exam for MDI?"** → **sulcus sign**\
✗ Wrong options: apprehension · load-shift · jerk · Kim

**"Voluntary popper, 2 cm bilateral sulcus — initial treatment?"** → **cuff + periscapular strengthening**\
✗ Wrong options: posterior stretching · plication · thermal · HAGL repair

**"Swimmer, 2+ load-shift, 1.5 cm sulcus — next?"** → **dynamic stabilisation therapy**\
✗ Wrong options: bracing · labral repair

**"Bilateral MDI volleyball, impingement signs?"** → **physical therapy**\
✗ Wrong options: injections · staged surgery

**"17-y ♀ MDI — initial?"** → **PT + home exercises**\
✗ Wrong options: sling · thermal

**"Swimmer, 2+ translation, elbow hyperextension, thumb to forearm?"** → **PT cuff + scapulothoracic**\
✗ Wrong options: open shift · thermal · sling

**"13-y pitcher, Beighton signs, sulcus, 10° IR deficit?"** → **MDI; periscapular training**\
✗ Wrong options: GIRD sleeper stretches · little leaguer's shoulder

**"Who gets prolonged PT rather than surgery?"** → **17-y swimmer, Beighton 5 + sulcus**\

**"Dancer, grade III sulcus, 8 wk PT done?"** → **continue PT**\
✗ Wrong options: shift · Bankart · Latarjet

**"Rotator interval closure — true?"** → **restricts ER at 0° abduction**

**"Failed nonop MDI — successful surgery includes?"** → **rotator interval closure**\
✗ Wrong options: SLAP repair · capsular advancement into Hill-Sachs · IGHL repair

**"Procedure that eliminates sulcus sign?"** → **rotator interval closure**\
✗ Wrong options: Bankart · SLAP · SAD

**"Contraindication to arthroscopic revision?"** → **capsular attenuation from thermal capsulorrhaphy**\
✗ Wrong options: 10% bone loss · ALPSA · GLAD · SLAP + Bankart

**"Can't actively elevate after thermal capsulorrhaphy, normal X-ray?"** → **axillary nerve injury**\
✗ Wrong options: chondrolysis

**"Stiff painful shoulder 3 y after MDI scope, joint space loss?"** → **monopolar RF thermal capsulorrhaphy** (chondrolysis)

**"Thermal shrinkage — true?"** → **high failure for anterior, posterior and MDI**\
✗ Wrong options: thick fibrotic capsule · no healing

**"6-y recurrent instability, mid-range apprehension, 3D-CT bone loss?"** → **bony glenoid augmentation**\
✗ Wrong options: arthroscopic Bankart · hemiarthroplasty
