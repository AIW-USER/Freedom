# Rotator Cuff Arthropathy

*Shoulder & Elbow · 40 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Demographics** — **♀ > ♂** · **7th decade** · **dominant** shoulder

**Risk factors** — **rotator cuff tear** · **RA** · **crystal-induced arthropathy** · **haemorrhagic shoulder** (haemophilia, elderly on anticoagulants)

**History** — term **cuff tear arthropathy** coined by **Neer (1983)**

### Defining features

**3 common features (all patients)** — **non-functioning rotator cuff** · **GH degenerative changes** · **superior migration of humeral head**

**Full picture** — + **subchondral osteoporosis** · **humeral head collapse** · **femoralisation** of head · **acetabularisation** of coracoacromial arch

**Why diagnosis matters** — definitive treatment depends on whether **cuff functions**

## 2 Mechanism

### Pathogenesis – loss of force couples/concavity compression & anterosuperior escape

**Concavity compression** — cuff compresses head into glenoid → concentric rotation → **primary mechanism centring head against upward pull of deltoid**

**Force coupling** — muscle balance centres the net force vector on glenoid → stable **fulcrum** for deltoid + periscapular muscles · loss → **instability, eccentric wear, CTA**

**Supraspinatus + infraspinatus** — depress head caudally; dynamic stabilisers

**Pathway** — cuff deficiency → **loss of dynamic compression** → abnormal joint forces → **superior migration** → **mechanical cartilage wear** → **acetabularisation of acromion**

| ✗ Concept trap | Truth |
|---|---|
| Absent supraspinatus → centring impossible | **Subscapularis + infraspinatus** can still centre head |
| Cuff repair restores stability after concavity lost | **False** — cannot fully restore once **effective glenoid concavity lost** |
| Intact biceps prevents migration | **False** — biceps is **not a head depressor** |
| Cuff vector optimally depresses head | **False** — not optimally oriented |

**Mechanical factors** — loss of concavity (compression effect), ↓ ROM + function, head migration, instability ± recurrent dislocation

**Nutritional factors** — **loss of watertight joint space** → ↓ joint fluid → **cartilage atrophy** (↓ water + GAG) + **subchondral collapse** (disuse osteoporosis)

**Crystal-induced** — synovial degradation proteins destroy cuff + cartilage → end-stage **calcium phosphate crystal** deposits

### Pseudoparalysis mechanism

**Pseudoparalysis** — inability to **actively forward flex/elevate** with **full passive ROM** · cuff **can't DEPRESS** head → deltoid pulls humerus up → **anterosuperior subluxation / escape** · deltoid lever arm ↓\
✗ Wrong options: can't elevate

**Injection test** — **no change** in active elevation after GH local anaesthetic → **pain is NOT the cause** → mechanical (RSA)

| ✗ Pseudoparalysis distractor | Why wrong |
|---|---|
| Posteroinferior / inferior subluxation | Deltoid pulls **anterosuperiorly** |
| Deltoid atrophy | Not the classic chronic-tear mechanism |
| Suprascapular nerve palsy | Weak supra + infraspinatus but **cuff still checkreins** head → no pseudoparalysis |
| **C5 radiculopathy** | Weak deltoid + cuff, **paraesthesias**, no pseudoparalysis |
| Fibroblastic capsular thickening | **Adhesive capsulitis** (passive ROM lost) |

| ✗ Pathogenesis distractor | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| Haemosiderin, synovial hyperplasia, cytokines | **Haemophilic arthropathy** (can cause massive cuff tears) |
| GPC proliferation, PMNs | **Septic arthritis** (P. acnes = **gram + bacillus**, may have **normal markers**) |
| Microcirculation occlusion, osteocyte death | **AVN** (subchondral lucency/collapse, no superior migration) |
| T-cell autoimmune | **RA** (**central** glenoid erosion, periarticular osteopenia) |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain incl. **night pain** · subjective weakness + stiffness

**Inspection** — **supra/infraspinatus atrophy** · **anterosuperior escape** (head prominence anteriorly on elevation) · **subcutaneous effusion** (capsular fluid leak, "geyser")

**ROM** — ↓ active ± passive · **crepitus** GH/subacromial · **pseudoparalysis**

**Acute-on-chronic** — low-energy injury → **acute decompensation** of compensated chronic tear → pain + disability

| Test | Finding | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| **ER lag sign** | Can't hold passive ER, elbow 90° | **Massive infraspinatus** tear |
| **Hornblower** | Can't ER / hold ER at **90° abduction + 90° elbow flexion** | **Teres minor** dysfunction |
| Belly press | Normal → subscapularis intact | Subscapularis |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP, axillary, **Grashey (true AP)**

**X-ray** — **↓ acromiohumeral interval** · **acromial acetabularisation** · **femoralisation** of head · **asymmetric superior glenoid wear** · **lack of osteophytes** · **osteopenia** · **"snowcap" sign** (subchondral sclerosis) · **anterosuperior escape**

![Cuff tear arthropathy, high-riding head](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3044-rotator-cuff-arthropathy.jpg)

**MRI** — irreparable tear: **massive fatty infiltration** + **severe retraction** · **not needed** if X-ray already shows anterosuperior escape

**Labs** — normal WBC/ESR/CRP (vs septic, RA)

### Hamada classification

| Grade | Findings |
|---|---|
| 1 | **AHI ≥6 mm** |
| 2 | **AHI ≤5 mm** |
| 3 | AHI ≤5 mm + **acetabularisation of acromion** |
| 4A | **GH arthritis without acetabularisation** (AHI <7 mm) |
| 4B | **GH arthritis with acetabularisation** (AHI ≤5 mm) |
| 5 | **Humeral head collapse** |

![Hamada classification](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3044-hamada-classification.jpg)

**Hamada versions** — stage 2+ AHI **≤5 mm**, stage 5 **head collapse** (Orthobullets) · AHI **<7 mm**, stage 5 **osteonecrosis** (TO) — read the options

### Seebauer classification

| Type | Pattern | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **IA** | **Centred, stable** | Intact anterior restraints; minimal superior migration; dynamic stabilisation; **femoralisation + acetabularisation** |
| **IB** | **Centred, medialised** | Intact/compensated anterior restraints; compromised stabilisation; **medial glenoid erosion** |
| **IIA** | **Decentred, limited stability** | Compromised anterior restraints; **superior translation**; minimal CA-arch stabilisation |
| **IIB** | **Decentred, unstable** | Incompetent anterior restraints; **anterosuperior escape**; no dynamic or CA-arch stabilisation |

## 5 Management

```
Rotator cuff arthropathy
├─ Function preserved / first visit → NSAIDs, steroid injection, PT (deltoid, scapular, cuff)
├─ Pain only, intact CA arch + deltoid, young active → hemiarthroplasty
├─ Pseudoparalysis / elderly >70 / anterosuperior escape → reverse TSA
│      ├─ + ER pseudoparalysis (Hornblower +, ER lag) → + latissimus dorsi transfer
│      └─ deltoid / axillary nerve dysfunction → no RSA
└─ Infection / poor soft tissue → resection arthroplasty (salvage)
```

### Nonoperative care when function is preserved

**Indication** — **first-line**; esp. **good active elevation** (e.g. 150°), first presentation

**Regimen** — activity modification · **NSAIDs** · **subacromial steroid injection** · PT (**deltoid**, scapular, cuff strengthening) · not straight to surgery

**Acute decompensation** — rest, PT, injection often settle the painful phase

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indications / facts |
|---|---|
| **Arthroscopic debridement** | **Controversial**, unpredictable; **preserve CA arch** (✗ Wrong options: acromioplasty · CA ligament release) |
| **Hemiarthroplasty** | **Anterior deltoid preserved** + **CA arch intact** (else **anterosuperior / subcutaneous escape**) · **younger, active** · relieves pain (good–excellent **~75%**) but **not function** (elevation **40–70°**) |
| **Reverse TSA** | **Pseudoparalytic CTA** · **elderly (>70)**, low demand · **anterosuperior escape** · needs **functioning deltoid (axillary nerve)** + **good bone stock** · improves **pain + function** |
| **Latissimus dorsi transfer** | **ER pseudoparalysis** · with RSA |
| **Pectoralis major transfer** | **IR deficiency / subscapularis insufficiency** (± aTSA for massive anterior tear + arthritis) |
| **Resection arthroplasty** | **Salvage** — chronic osteomyelitis, infection, poor soft tissue |
| **Glenoid resurfacing / anatomic TSA** | **Contraindicated** — superior glenoid **shear / edge loading** ("**rocking horse**") → **glenoid loosening** (#1 cause of aTSA revision) |
| **Cuff repair** | Only if no end-stage changes, active, **minimal fatty infiltration**; **not with supraspinatus atrophy** |
| **SCR / tendon transfer** | Younger, irreparable tear, must follow strict rehab |
| **Ream-and-run** | **Young labourer, GH arthritis, intact cuff** (✗ Wrong options: CTA) |

### Reverse arthroplasty for pseudoparalysis & outcomes

**Biomechanics** — moves **centre of rotation inferior + medial** → ↑ **deltoid mechanical advantage / lever arm** → deltoid powers elevation

**Outcome** — **improves pain and range of motion even in setting of preoperative pseudoparalysis** · low loosening with modern constructs · durability > hemi

**Can't elevate after successful local anaesthetic** → **RSA**\
✗ Wrong options: biceps tenolysis, open debridement, hemi (pain-reducing only)

**Young vs old** — younger: cuff repair, **SCR**, or hemi · **older, less active: RSA**

**Implant options** — convertible platform stem, central post or screw baseplate, **short cementless stem** (osteopenia) all OK · **severe osteoporosis / low deltoid tuberosity index → cemented stem** · **metal-backed polyethylene glenoid (aTSA) = contraindicated**

### RSA with latissimus/teres major transfer for loss of external rotation

**Indication for LDT with RSA** — **pseudoparalysis of ER** (deltoid restores elevation but **not ER**) from infraspinatus + teres minor dysfunction

**ER dysfunction criteria** — **+ Hornblower** · **ER lag >15° vs contralateral** · **teres minor fatty degeneration ≥ stage 2** (Goutallier / Fuchs)

**Not LDT indications** — negative Hornblower · <5° ER lag · **IR pseudoparalysis** · supraspinatus retraction medial to glenoid · isolated supraspinatus tear\
✗ Wrong options: RSA either

**RSA contraindications** — **axillary nerve neurotmesis** · **severe deltoid dysfunction** (e.g. stroke)

**Post-traumatic arthritis, normal motor** — TSA, no transfer

### Pectoralis major transfer for subscapularis insufficiency – nerve risk

**Technique** — upper part or whole pec major tendon → near **lesser tuberosity**; route **deep to conjoint tendon, superficial to musculocutaneous nerve**

**Nerve at risk** — **musculocutaneous** (tension neurapraxia) → **↓ elbow flexion** (biceps, brachialis)

| ✗ Wrong option: weakness | Nerve |
|---|---|
| Elbow extension | Radial |
| Shoulder ER | Suprascapular (infraspinatus), axillary (teres minor) |
| Shoulder adduction | Medial/lateral pectoral, thoracodorsal, lower subscapular |
| Shoulder abduction | Suprascapular, axillary |

### Traumatic subscapularis tear mechanism

**Mechanism** — fall, grasping to stop fall → **forced external rotation** against resistance\
✗ Wrong options: IR · extension · abduction · flexion

## 6 Complications

### Implant choice & complications (superior glenoid wear, metal-backed glenoid)

**Scapular notching** — humeral poly abuts **inferior glenoid in adduction** → bone loss, osteolysis, loosening, **lower outcome scores**

| Notching | Factor |
|---|---|
| **↑ Risk** | **CTA with superior glenoid wear** → glenosphere too **superior** / **superior tilt** · **anterosuperior (deltoid-splitting) approach** |
| **↓ Risk** | **Lateralised humeral implant** with **lower neck-shaft angle 132.5°** (vs Grammont **155°**) · **~10° inferior tilt** · **inferior-offset glenosphere** with **~3 mm inferior overhang** · deltopectoral approach |

![Scapular notching after RSA](../../MCQ/attachments/q217907-a-figure-a-notching.jpg)

**Acromial / scapular spine stress fracture** — mechanism = **deltoid tensioning by implant design** · **~4–5%**, mean **9 mo** · ↑ **onlay humeral component**, **lateralisation**, **osteoporosis**, **female**, medialised pre-op COR · **nonop union 40–50%** \
✗ Wrong options: bisphosphonate mechanism · C. acnes · superior tilt (= notching)

| Complication | Note |
|---|---|
| **Instability** | **More common after hemi**, rare after RSA |
| Infection | C. acnes → indolent infection |
| Neurovascular injury | Musculocutaneous with pec major transfer |
| **Deltoid dysfunction** | — |
| **Glenoid loosening** | aTSA in CTA (rocking horse) |

## X vs Y

| | **Hemiarthroplasty** | **Reverse TSA** | **Anatomic TSA** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patient | Younger, active | **Elderly >70**, low demand | Not in CTA |
| Needs | **Intact CA arch + anterior deltoid** | **Functioning deltoid (axillary n.)**, bone stock | Functioning **supraspinatus + subscapularis** |
| Pain | Relieved (~75%) | Relieved | — |
| Function | **Poor (40–70°)** | **Improved**, even pseudoparalysis | Glenoid loosening |

| | **CTA** | **RA** | **AVN** | **Septic (P. acnes)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Glenoid wear | **Superior**, asymmetric | **Central** erosion | — | — |
| X-ray | **↓ AHI**, acetabularisation, **no osteophytes**, snowcap | **Periarticular osteopenia** | Subchondral lucency / **head collapse**, no superior migration | — |
| Labs | Normal | ↑ inflammatory markers | — | **May be normal** |

## How it's asked

**"Remote cuff repair, normal labs, high-riding head — pathogenesis?"** → **loss of dynamic compression → abnormal forces → mechanical cartilage wear**\
✗ Wrong options: haemosiderin · GPC · AVN · T-cell

**"Untreated progression of cuff tear on MRI (figure)?"** → **cuff tear arthropathy (Figure E)**\
✗ Wrong options: chondrosarcoma · OA · dislocation · fracture

**"True about CTA before RSA (Hamada 4a)?"** → **concavity compression is primary centring mechanism vs deltoid**

**"Full passive ROM, can't flex, no change after injection — pathomechanics?"** → **anterosuperior subluxation of humeral head**\
✗ Wrong options: posteroinferior · deltoid atrophy · SSN · failure to elevate

**"Pseudoparalysis after ladder fall, acetabularisation — process?"** → **inability of cuff to depress head**\
✗ Wrong options: capsulitis, SSN, C5, inferior subluxation

**"75 y, subcutaneous fluid, 80° active / 170° passive, CTA X-ray — next?"** → **reverse TSA**\
✗ Wrong options: biopsy · aspiration · anatomic TSA · syrinx MRI

**"75 y, high-riding head, severe arthritis — treatment?"** → **reverse shoulder arthroplasty**\
✗ Wrong options: resurfacing · hemi · TSA · debridement

**"30° active elevation, AHI 0 mm — best function?"** → **reverse shoulder arthroplasty**

**"Can't raise arm even after injection relieved pain?"** → **reverse TSA**

**"82 y fall, acute-on-chronic cuff tear, failed 4 mo — surgery?"** → **reverse TSA**\
✗ Wrong options: cuff repair · ream-and-run · hemi · aTSA

**"72 y, walks + housework, CTA — implant (figure)?"** → **reverse TSA (Figure F)**

**"True about best option for CTA, 70° elevation?"** → **improves pain + ROM even with pseudoparalysis**\
✗ Wrong options: needs CA arch · pain only · high loosening

**"Pain on top of shoulder 9 mo after RSA, CT fracture — mechanism?"** → **deltoid tensioning by implant design**\
✗ Wrong options: bisphosphonates · ↓ lateralisation · C. acnes · superior tilt

**"RSA + latissimus transfer most appropriate?"** → **70 F, pseudoparesis of elevation + ER, acetabularisation**

**"Pseudoparalysis + Hornblower +, belly press normal — treatment?"** → **RSA + latissimus dorsi transfer**\
✗ Wrong options: aTSA + pec transfer = anterior tears

**"Indication for concurrent latissimus transfer?"** → **pseudoparalysis of external rotation**\
✗ Wrong options: axillary neurotmesis · negative Hornblower · IR

**"82 F, 1 mo pain, elevates to 150° — management?"** → **trial of anti-inflammatory medication or cortisone injection and/or deltoid strengthening**

**"Iatrogenic nerve injury after pec major transfer — weak movement?"** → **elbow flexion** (musculocutaneous)

**"Traumatic subscapularis tear mechanism?"** → **forced external rotation**

**"Risk factor for scapular notching?"** → **CTA with superior glenoid wear**\
✗ Wrong options: lateralised 132.5° · 10° inferior tilt · inferior offset · deltopectoral

**"Implant feature contraindicated in CTA?"** → **metal-backed polyethylene glenoid component**\
✗ Wrong options: convertible stem · central post · medialised COR · short cementless stem
