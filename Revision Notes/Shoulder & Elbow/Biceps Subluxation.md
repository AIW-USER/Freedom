# Biceps Subluxation

*Shoulder & Elbow · 6 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Association with subscapularis tears

**Definition** — LHBT subluxes/dislocates **medially** out of the bicipital groove → recognised cause of **anterior shoulder pain**

**Most common associated pathology** — **subscapularis tear** (even **partial**); biceps subluxation is **almost always** associated with a subscapularis tear

**Most important medial restraint** — **subscapularis tendon** = most important restraint to medial subluxation/dislocation of LHBT

**Walch series** — dislocated LHBT associated with partial/complete **subscapularis tear in 96%**

**Other cause** — **disruption of the biceps sling (pulley)**

**Not the cause** — **subacromial impingement** does not cause LHBT instability · isolated degenerative tendinosis (instability is from the subscapularis tear) · SLAP

### Biceps pulley stabilisers (coracohumeral & transverse humeral ligaments)

**Origin** — LHBT arises from **supraglenoid tubercle + superior labrum** → intra-articular → bicipital groove

**Function** — **dynamic stabiliser**; shoulder flexion, abduction

| Biceps sling / pulley (intra-articular) | Rotator interval tissue? |
|---|---|
| **Coracohumeral ligament (CHL)** | **Yes** |
| **Superior glenohumeral ligament (SGHL)** | **Yes** |
| **Subscapularis** fibres | Contributes to sling |
| **Supraspinatus** (anterior fibres) | Contributes to sling |

![Biceps sling: CHL, SGHL, subscapularis, supraspinatus over LHBT](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3046-screen-shot-2019-11-24-at-9-07-02-pm.jpg)

**Transverse humeral ligament (THL)** — stabilises LHBT in the groove **at the level of the greater + lesser tuberosities** (distal to pulley); **not rotator interval tissue**

**THL (TO)** — old concept of THL as the LHB stabiliser is **obsolete**; **medial pulley = subscapularis + SGHL + CHL** prevents medial subluxation — read the options

**Comma sign (TO)** — in **subscapularis tears**: torn CHL + SGHL + subscapularis pulled together into a comma shape → **pulley lesion**

| Habermeyer pulley lesion (TO) | Injury |
|---|---|
| **I** | **SGHL** → anterior LHB instability |
| **II** | SGHL + **anterior supraspinatus** tear |
| **III** | SGHL + **cranial subscapularis** tear (**comma sign**) |
| **IV** | SGHL + anterior supraspinatus + cranial subscapularis → **anteroposterior instability** |

**Bicipital groove** — length 81–87 mm · width 7.7–11.6 mm · depth 3.7–6 mm · **bottleneck ~2/3 down** from top → THL may constrain groove → anterior pain; tenodesis **below the groove** may be preferred

| ✗ Distractor | Why wrong |
|---|---|
| **Coracoacromial ligament (CAL)** | Not rotator interval; no LHBT stability at tuberosities |
| **IGHL** | Not rotator interval; not an LHBT stabiliser |
| **AIGHL** | Not associated with biceps subluxation (injured in anterior dislocation) |
| Superior labrum / infraspinatus / posterior labrum | Not associated with **medial** biceps subluxation |

**Imaging finding = injured structure** — MRI medial biceps subluxation → **CHL disruption** (sling)

**Anterosuperior cuff tear** — sling disrupted → LHBT subluxes medially → **signals subscapularis tear** (often missed on MRI)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **anterior** shoulder pain ± **clicking**

**Yergason test** — anterior pain with **resisted supination**, arm at side, elbow flexed 90°

**Speed test** — anterior pain with **resisted forward flexion**, shoulder 90° flexed, elbow extended, palm up

**Palpable click** — with **abduction + external rotation** as tendon subluxes/dislocates out of groove

**Clinical clues (TO)** — anterior pain · clicking/snapping · MRI medial subluxation/dislocation · **often missed** unless subscapularis/cuff evaluated

**Always test subscapularis strength** — **lift-off** (Gerber), belly press, push-off

**Lift-off test (Gerber)** — back of hand held off mid-lumbar spine in IR; hand falls or patient extends elbow/shoulder to compensate → **subscapularis tear**

| ✗ Exam distractor | Tests |
|---|---|
| Drop arm | **Supraspinatus** |
| External rotation lag | **Infraspinatus** |
| Hornblower's | **Teres minor** |
| Kim test | **Posteroinferior labral tear** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**MRI** — ↑ **T2 signal** + LHBT **displaced out of bicipital groove** (axial: on lesser tuberosity → fully medial); coincides with **subscapularis tear**

**MRI limitation** — misses subscapularis tears unless tear extends **≥½ cranio-caudal** length → small tears missed → test lift-off

**LHBT subluxation on MRI as predictor of full-thickness subscapularis tear (Shi)** — sensitivity **82%** · specificity **80%** · PPV **35%** · **NPV 97%** → value lies in **NPV** (no subluxation → full-thickness subscapularis tear unlikely); correlates with tear severity

**Ultrasound** — **dynamic test** of biceps instability

**Arthroscopy** — ↓ **biceps–glenoid angle** associated with subluxation (**87°** vs **90°**; Koh) → statistically significant but **not clinically relevant**

## 5 Management

```
Biceps subluxation
├─ Initial → NSAIDs + PT + steroid injection
│      (injection near, not into, the tendon)
└─ Refractory → arthroscopic/open tenotomy or tenodesis
       └─ subscapularis tear → + subscapularis repair
```

### Nonoperative

**First line** — **NSAIDs, PT strengthening, activity modification, steroid injection** (adjacent to tendon, **not into it**)

### Operative

**Indication** — **refractory** symptoms

**Options** — arthroscopic vs open **biceps tenotomy vs tenodesis** ± **subscapularis repair**; instability can be tested intra-op

**Acute traumatic medial LHBT instability (Maier)** — open subscapularis repair + LHBT stabilisation = equal function to tenotomy/tenodesis, **better cosmesis, less cramping**

**THL release** — routine release at tenotomy/tenodesis may ↓ post-op anterior pain

### Biceps tenotomy for irreparable cuff tear

**Indication** — large/massive **irreparable** cuff tear, **pain** main complaint, **good active elevation** (e.g. 120°), **normal articular surfaces** → **biceps tenotomy**

| Alternative | When |
|---|---|
| **Latissimus dorsi transfer** | Severe **ER deficit** + deficient **teres minor** |
| **Reverse TSA** | **Cuff tear arthropathy + pseudoparalysis** |
| ✗ Wrong option: SLAP repair | Contraindicated in this age group |

## X vs Y

| | **Biceps sling (pulley)** | **Transverse humeral ligament** |
|---|---|---|
| Level | Intra-articular, rotator interval | Bicipital groove at **tuberosities** |
| Components | CHL, SGHL + subscapularis, supraspinatus | Single ligament, superficial roof of groove |
| Rotator interval tissue | **CHL + SGHL = yes** | **No** |

| | **Tenotomy for massive cuff tear** | **Latissimus transfer** | **Reverse TSA** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Key feature | Pain, elevation preserved, no arthritis | ER deficit, teres minor deficient | Arthropathy + pseudoparalysis |

## How it's asked

**"62F, anterior pain, + Yergason/Speed, MRI LHBT on lesser tuberosity then dislocated — true statement?"** → **biceps pathology due to partial subscapularis tear**\
✗ Wrong options: SLAP repair · isolated tendinosis · impingement

**"MR arthrogram, biceps out of groove — arthroscopy will show?"** → **subscapularis tear + biceps subluxation**\
✗ Wrong options: THL disruption · absent CHL · short head rupture

**"Skier, MRI dislocated biceps tendon — most important exam?"** → **lift-off test**\
✗ Wrong options: drop arm · ER lag · Hornblower · Kim

**"Post-dislocation, MRI medial biceps subluxation — injured structure?"** → **coracohumeral ligament**\
✗ Wrong options: AIGHL · superior labrum · infraspinatus · posterior labrum

**"Stabilises LHBT at tuberosities, not rotator interval?"** → **transverse humeral ligament**\
✗ Wrong options: CHL · SGHL = interval · CAL · IGHL

**"Main diagnostic value of LHBT subluxation on MRI?"** → **negative predictive value (97%)**

**"Massive irreparable cuff tear, FE 120°, normal joint, MRI biceps subluxed?"** → **biceps tenotomy**\
✗ Wrong options: latissimus transfer · reverse TSA

**"Most important medial restraint of LHBT?"** → **subscapularis tendon**

**"Comma sign at arthroscopy indicates?"** → **subscapularis tear with biceps pulley lesion** (TO)

**"Habermeyer type III pulley lesion?"** → **SGHL + cranial subscapularis tear** (TO)
