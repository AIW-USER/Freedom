# Triceps Rupture

*Shoulder & Elbow · 59 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — rare · **0.8%** of tendon ruptures

**Demographics** — **♂ 2:1** · age **30–50** · competitive **weightlifting** (bench press), bodybuilding, **football**

### Risk factors & diagnosis (flake sign, MRI)

| Risk factor | Yes / No |
|---|---|
| **Anabolic steroids** | ✓ (established link) |
| **Local steroid injection** | ✓ |
| **Fluoroquinolones** | ✓ |
| **Renal osteodystrophy** / renal disease, **hyperparathyroidism** | ✓ |
| OI, RA, type I DM, Marfan | ✓ |
| Chronic olecranon bursitis, prior triceps surgery | ✓ |
| **Creatine supplements** | **not associated** |

### Anatomy – lateral expansion & anconeus

| Head | Origin |
|---|---|
| **Long** | **Infraglenoid tuberosity** |
| **Lateral** | Posterior humerus between teres minor insertion and **above spiral groove**, lateral border, lateral IM septum |
| **Medial** | Posterior humerus **distal to spiral groove**, medial humerus, medial IM septum |

**Triceps** — pennate · **only muscle of posterior arm compartment** · **radial nerve (C6–C8)** · primary elbow extensor

**Insertion footprint** — **1.1 cm distal to olecranon tip** · width **1.9–4.2 cm** · triceps tendon proper (all 3 heads → olecranon) + **lateral triceps expansion** (posterior ulnar crest · **ECU fascia + anconeus deep fascia** · antebrachial fascia)

**Anconeus** — **secondary elbow extensor**; terminal **15° extension** + supination · **compensates for torn triceps** → resisted extension may persist

**Anconeus vs distractors** — brachioradialis, brachialis = flexion · LCL = varus stabiliser · pronator teres = pronation

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanism** — **forceful eccentric contraction** (bench press, muscle-ups) · **FOOSH** · direct blow

**Site** — most often **osseous insertion** (medial or lateral head) · less often muscle belly / MTJ

## 3 Presentation

**History** — **painful pop**

**Exam** — posterior elbow pain, swelling, ecchymosis · **palpable defect** (not always)

**Resisted extension** — inability to extend against resistance **not always present**: **intact lateral expansion** or **anconeus** compensation · complete tear → can't extend **against gravity** (OB)

**Modified Thompson squeeze** — **prone**, elbow at table edge, forearm hanging → squeeze triceps → **no extension** = complete rupture of **triceps proper + lateral expansion**

**Missed** — **10/23** missed initially (van Riet) · **nearly 50%** misdiagnosed (Yeh)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + lateral)** — **"flake sign"** on lateral = **pathognomonic** of triceps avulsion (olecranon tip fleck)

![Flake sign: avulsed fleck at olecranon tip](../../MCQ/attachments/q217442-a-flake-of-bone-typical-sign-on-lateral-x-ray-image-.jpg)

**MRI** — location + severity; confirms, classifies, guides treatment · **partial** = small fluid-filled defect in distal tendon · **complete** = **large fluid-filled gap**, paratricipital oedema · use when too painful to examine or partial suspected

![MRI: distal triceps tear](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3071-picture2.jpg)

**✗ Flake-sign distractors** — distal biceps, brachialis, LUCL, MUCL tears · elbow arthritis · olecranon bursitis · anconeus avulsion (= **lateral epicondyle** fracture)

### Descriptive classification

| Feature | Options |
|---|---|
| Degree | Complete · partial · intact |
| Location | Muscle belly · MTJ · tendinous insertion · avulsion |
| Lateral expansion | Intact · torn |

**Formal system** — none

## 5 Management

```
Triceps rupture
├─ Partial, extends vs gravity / low-demand, poor health → splint 30° flexion 4 wk
├─ Acute complete → primary repair (best within 3 wk)
├─ Partial >50% + significant weakness → primary repair
└─ Chronic / delayed → reconstruction ± tendon graft
```

### Operative criteria

| Indication | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Acute complete tear** (active, medically fit) | **Primary repair** |
| **Partial >50% + significant weakness** | **Primary repair** |
| Partial, extends against gravity | Splint |
| Low demand, poor health | Splint |
| Late (>3 wk) | Reconstruction, tendon graft |

**Approach** — posterolateral

| Tear site | Technique |
|---|---|
| MTJ | **V-Y advancement** ± plantaris augmentation |
| Tendinous insertion | **Bunnell / Krackow** non-absorbable whipstitch → **transosseous tunnels**, periosteal flap, or **suture anchors** |
| Bony avulsion | Tension band · screw + washer |

**Tunnels vs anchors** — **no difference** in biomechanical strength or function · **transosseous: ↑ re-rupture + complications**

**Post-op** — 30–45° flexion **2 wk** · active ROM at **4 wk** · no weightlifting **4–6 mo**

**Outcome** — early diagnosis + repair → excellent; anatomic footprint repair

## 6 Complications

**General** — elbow stiffness/weakness · **ulnar nerve injury** · repair failure

**Repair-specific** — olecranon bursitis · flexion contracture · re-rupture

## X vs Y

| | **Partial tear** | **Complete tear** |
|---|---|---|
| Extension vs gravity | Preserved | Lost (unless anconeus / lateral expansion compensate) |
| Thompson squeeze | Extension present | **No extension** |
| MRI | **Small fluid-filled defect** | **Large fluid-filled gap**, paratricipital oedema |
| Treatment | Splint 30° × 4 wk · repair if **>50% + weak** | **Primary repair** |

## How it's asked

**"Triceps rupture associated with all EXCEPT?"** → **creatine supplements**\
✗ Wrong options: anabolic steroids · local steroid · renal osteodystrophy · fluoroquinolones

**"40 y weightlifter, pop on bench press, lateral X-ray fleck — MRI shows?"** → **triceps tendon tear**\
✗ Wrong options: distal biceps · brachialis · LUCL · MUCL

**"44 y, FOOSH rollerblading, posterior pain, olecranon flake?"** → **triceps rupture**\
✗ Wrong options: arthritis · bursitis · LCL · anconeus avulsion

**"Muscle-ups, positive Thompson squeeze — structure assisting resisted extension?"** → **anconeus (E)**
