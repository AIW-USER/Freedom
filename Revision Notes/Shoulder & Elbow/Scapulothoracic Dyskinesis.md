# Scapulothoracic Dyskinesis

*Shoulder & Elbow · 43 / 60*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **abnormal scapular motion** during coordinated scapulohumeral movement → impingement + dysfunction · rare

**Who** — athletes, **baseball pitchers** · sequela of prior shoulder injury disrupting **scapular stabiliser activation**

**Incidence (TO)** — in **61–100%** of athletes with shoulder injury (throwers: baseball, cricket, volleyball, javelin, tennis) · often a **symptom, not a diagnosis** → treat underlying cause (TO)

| Normal scapula at rest (TO) | Value |
|---|---|
| Internal rotation | **30–45°** |
| Upward rotation | 5–10° (20° in another TO lecture) |
| Anterior tilt | 10–15° |
| Vertebral border | **2–3 cm** from spine |
| Scapular plane | 30–45° anterior to frontal plane |

**During elevation (TO)** — upward rotation **~60°** (with 120° humeral) · **posterior tilt 20°** · ER 15–20° · rhythm **2:1**

| Muscle (TO) | Scapular action |
|---|---|
| **Serratus anterior** | Upward rotation + **posterior tilt** |
| **Lower trapezius** | Depression + upward rotation |
| Upper trapezius | Elevation + upward rotation |
| Rhomboids, levator scapulae | Downward rotation + retraction |
| **Pectoralis minor** | **Anterior tilt** + IR |

**Scapular roles (TO)** — stable base · kinetic-chain link · **shock absorber in deceleration** · glenoid orientation · elevation via upward rotation + posterior tilt

### Aetiology

**Causes (multifactorial)** — neurologic injury · thoracic **kyphosis** · **periscapular muscle fatigue** · **poor throwing mechanics** · pain (shoulder, neck)

**Mechanisms (TO)** — weak **serratus / lower trapezius** + tight **pec minor / levator** · GH instability · kyphosis · kinetic-chain disruption · posterior capsule tightness (anterior tilt) · nerve: **long thoracic → serratus (winging)**, **spinal accessory → trapezius**

**Associated lesions (TO)** — internal impingement · SLAP · GIRD · subacromial impingement

## 2 Mechanism

**Pathoanatomy** — scapulothoracic **power imbalance** → **scapular protraction** → altered GH mechanics → ↑ stress on **anterior capsule + posterosuperior labrum**

**Injury risk** — labrum · rotator cuff (**articular-surface tears**) · capsule

**Thrower's cascade** — **posteroinferior capsular tightness** + scapular dyskinesis → articular-side cuff tear + **posterosuperior labral tear**

### SICK scapula

**SICK** — muscular **fatigue syndrome** of throwers

| Letter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **S** | **Scapular malposition** |
| **I** | **Inferior medial border prominence** |
| **C** | **Coracoid pain + malposition** (tender coracoid = **pectoralis minor contracture**) |
| **K** | dys**K**inesis of scapular movement |

**GIRD** — **loss of internal rotation** (e.g. 15°) = posterior capsule tightness

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — shoulder pain/dysfunction **worse with arm elevation** · **loss of throwing velocity** · posterosuperior ± anterior pain

**Exam** — **scapulothoracic crepitus** · scapula **lower + protracted** · **symptoms relieved by scapular stabilisation** (diagnostic)

**Associated** — coracoid tenderness · IR deficit · positive relocation test

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **clinical**

| Kibler type (TO) | Pattern | Muscle | Pathology |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Inferomedial border** prominence ("wing") | Weak lower trap / serratus | **Labral tear**, impingement |
| **II** | **Medial border** prominence | Weak rhomboids | **GH instability** |
| **III** | **Superior translation, shrug** | Overactive upper trap, weak lower trap | **Rotator cuff** |
| **IV** | Normal symmetric | — | Normal |

| Test (TO) | Positive |
|---|---|
| **Scapular assistance (SAT)** | ↓ pain / ↑ ROM with manual upward rotation + posterior tilt |
| **Scapular retraction (SRT)** | ↑ supraspinatus strength with manual retraction · **most sensitive for dynamic dyskinesis** (TO) |
| **Lateral scapular slide** | **>1.5 cm** side difference |
| Wall push-up | Medial border prominence (serratus weakness) |

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (complete shoulder series)** — **usually unremarkable**

**MRI** — often no intra-articular lesion

## 5 Management

```
Scapular dyskinesis
├─ Mainstay → NSAIDs, PT, local injections
└─ Rehab (Kibler, proximal → distal)
       ├─ Stage 1 → full scapular motion + coordinate scapula with trunk + hips
       ├─ Stage 2 → strengthen scapular muscles
       └─ Stage 3 → shoulder flexibility, closed-chain, sport-specific
```

### Rehabilitation – kinetic chain, pectoralis minor & posterior capsule stretching

**Principle** — **proximal → distal** (kinetic chain); progress by **function, not timetable**; acute → recovery → maintenance phases

**Initial stage** — **coordination of scapular motion with trunk + hip movements**\
✗ Wrong options: isometric · isokinetic · closed chain · axial loading (later)

**PT emphasis** — **core** strengthening · scapular stabilisers (**serratus anterior, trapezius**) · rotator cuff · proper mechanics in throwers

**SICK / GIRD initial regimen** — **stretch posterior capsule** (IR deficit) **+ pectoralis minor** (coracoid tenderness)

| Rehab (TO) | Technique |
|---|---|
| Stretch **pec minor** | Doorway / corner |
| Stretch levator | Contralateral flexion + rotation |
| Stretch posterior capsule | Cross-body adduction |
| Strengthen **serratus** | **Push-up plus**, dynamic hugs |
| Strengthen **lower trapezius** | **Prone Y raises**, wall slides |
| Rhomboids | Prone retraction, rows |
| Kinetic chain | **Closed chain first** → open-chain overhead; "proximal stability for distal mobility" |

**Return to play (TO)** — full pain-free ROM, no winging · strength **≥90%** of other side · no kinetic-chain deficit · **negative SAT/SRT**

| ✗ Regimen distractor | Why wrong |
|---|---|
| Posterior capsule + **subscapularis strengthening** | Misses pec minor (coracoid pain) |
| Posterior capsule + **plyometrics** | Misses pec minor (coracoid pain) |
| **Anterior capsule** stretching (any) | IR deficit = **posterior** capsule tightness |

## How it's asked

**"Pitcher, posterior pain, scapular dyskinesis, normal MRI — emphasise first in rehab?"** → **coordination of scapular motion with trunk + hip movements**\
✗ Wrong options: isometric · isokinetic · closed chain · axial loading

**"Pitcher, 15° IR loss, coracoid tenderness, + relocation, normal MRI — initial regimen?"** → **stretch posterior capsule + pectoralis minor**\
✗ Wrong options: subscapularis strengthening · plyometrics · anterior capsule stretching

**"SICK scapula — coracoid pain due to?"** → **pectoralis minor contracture**

**"Diagnostic exam finding?"** → **symptoms relieved by scapular stabilisation**

**"Scapular position in dyskinesis?"** → **lower + protracted**

**"Kibler type III dyskinesis?"** → **superior translation / shrug** (TO)

**"Lateral scapular slide test abnormal if?"** → **>1.5 cm side difference** (TO)
