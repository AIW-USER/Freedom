# Elbow Stiffness and Contractures

*Shoulder & Elbow · 16 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Functional range of motion

| Motion | Functional range |
|---|---|
| **Flexion–extension** | **30°–130°** = **100° arc** (Orthobullets, OB) · **90° arc** (one OB explanation) — read the options |
| **Pronation–supination** | **50°–50°** |

**Tolerance** — **30° loss of extension well tolerated** · **flexion loss causes more dysfunction than extension loss**

### Causes

**Aetiology** — **OA**, **trauma (fractures)**, surgery, **CP**, **TBI**, **burns**, prolonged immobilisation, recurrent haemarthroses, congenital (**arthrogryposis**, **congenital radial head dislocation**)

| Type | Causes |
|---|---|
| **Intrinsic** (intra-articular) | Joint incongruity, synovitis, **loose bodies**, intra-articular fracture, malunion, OCD, post-traumatic arthritis (**coronoid + olecranon tip osteophytes**, radiocapitellar narrowing) |
| **Extrinsic** (extra-articular) | Burn **eschar**, **HO**, **capsular adhesions/contracture**, ligament contracture (**posterior oblique MCL**) |
| **Mixed** | Late effect of intrinsic → extrinsic stiffness |

### Elbow ligaments

| Ligament | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **Anterior bundle MUCL** (anterior oblique) | **Isometric**; **primary valgus + distraction stabiliser**; highest tensile strength; **Tommy John** reconstruction · do not release → **valgus instability** |
| **Posterior bundle MUCL** (posterior oblique) | **Dorsal to axis of rotation**; lengthens **9 mm between 60° and 120°** flexion · contracture → **loss of flexion** · **release → ↑ flexion without valgus instability** |
| Transverse band MUCL | Release **does not improve ROM** |
| Radial collateral ligament | Do not release |
| **LUCL** | Do not release → **PLRI** (pivot shift) |
| Annular ligament | — |

**Anterior capsule contracture** — **loss of extension**

![Medial ligaments: A anterior band, B posterior band, C transverse](../../MCQ/attachments/q4701-a-fig-a-medial.jpg)

**Ulnar nerve** — close to joint → at risk when contracted / during flexion gain

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — **mid-arc pain → intra-articular** pathology · **pain at extremes** → extrinsic contracture (bony impingement + soft-tissue stretch)

**Function** — ↓ motion limits **ADLs**

**Exam** — **scars**, skin quality, inflammation · flexion/extension + pronation/supination vs contralateral · **median, radial, ulnar nerve** function

**Flexion <90–100°** — **posterior band of MCL contracted** → release (+ consider ulnar nerve decompression/transposition)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **AP, lateral, oblique**; **serial X-rays if HO**

![Heterotopic ossification, elbow](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3087-xrhoelbow.jpg)

**CT** — **loose bodies**, nonunion, joint incongruity, abnormal bony anatomy; HO planning

**MRI** — rarely indicated

**HO maturity** — **well-corticated margins + no change on serial X-rays** · **labs not needed** · matures **12–18 mo** (OB) → **no bone scan** years after injury

## 5 Management

```
Elbow stiffness
├─ Most / contracture <40° → NSAIDs + supervised PT (active + passive ROM)
│      └─ failed PT + flexion contracture >30° or flexion <130° → static progressive splint
├─ Plateau 4–6 mo, extrinsic, congruent joint → capsular release ± posterior band MCL
└─ Intrinsic arthritis → choose by extent + demand
       ├─ confined to olecranon fossa → osteophyte excision + capsular release
       ├─ diffuse, young high-demand → distraction interposition arthroplasty
       └─ diffuse, elderly low-demand → TEA
```

### Static progressive splinting

**Indications** — after **failed PT** with **flexion contracture >30°** or **flexion <130°**

**Best initial option (OB)** — **supervised therapy + static progressive (turnbuckle) splinting** over **6 mo** (6–12 mo) → greatest ↑ **DASH** + functional ROM

**Not indicated** — **mechanical block** (esp. pronation/supination) · **ankylosis** (extensive HO) · functional arc already (e.g. 10–140°) · routinely after arthroscopic loose body removal

| ✗ Alternative | Why wrong |
|---|---|
| **Self-directed exercise** | **Poor results** |
| Supervised therapy alone | First line, but response **variable, often unsatisfactory** |
| **CPM** | Limited role in **established** contractures; no end-range gain |
| **Closed manipulation (MUA)** | **Worsens stiffness**: tearing, **haemarthrosis**, more fibrosis |

![Turnbuckle static progressive splint](../../MCQ/attachments/q3026-illa-turnbuckle.jpg)

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Capsular release ± posterior band MCL** | **Extrinsic** contracture + **normal joint congruency** (most predictable) · arthritis (less predictable) · ankylosis → less predictable |
| **Osteophyte excision** (+ capsular release) | **Intrinsic** contracture, **arthritis confined to olecranon fossa**; bony block to terminal motion → remove **coronoid, coronoid fossa, olecranon, olecranon fossa** |
| **Distraction interposition arthroplasty** | Intrinsic, **diffuse arthritis**, **young high-demand** |
| **TEA** | Intrinsic, diffuse arthritis, **elderly low-demand** · high failure in young · **permanent 5-lb lifting limit** |
| **Musculocutaneous neurectomy** | **Neurogenic** contracture, **flexion deformity <90°** |
| **Timing of release** | **4–6 mo** post-injury/surgery once ROM **plateaued** despite splinting/therapy |
| **HO excision** | At **maturity** |

**Contraindications to release** — **Charcot elbow**, **neurologic elbow disorder** · **poor skin** (relative → rotational flap)

### Release – posterior bundle of MCL for flexion

**Flexion gain** — capsular release + **posterior oblique band of MUCL**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior band · transverse · RCL · LUCL

**Flexion <100° preop** — consider **ulnar nerve transposition/decompression** (↓ nerve injury)

### Arthroscopic vs open release (prior ulnar transposition, HO)

**Arthroscopic** — technically demanding · posterior: olecranon fossa/osteophyte debridement + posterior capsular release (**no suction medially** — ulnar nerve) · anterior: coronoid fossa debridement + anterior capsulotomy/capsulectomy

**Nerves at risk with portals** — **radial (most)** > ulnar > median (stiffness note) · overall most common = **transient ulnar**, most common permanent = **PIN** (TO, arthroscopy MCQs) — read the options

**Relative contraindications to arthroscopic release** — **prior ulnar nerve transposition** (variable nerve position) · **HO** · obesity · severe loss of pronation–supination · muscular contracture (**CP**)

**Not contraindications** — age >40, gender, **osteophytes / arthritis** (unpredictable outcome only), heavy labour

| Open approach | Key points |
|---|---|
| **Lateral column (Morrey)** | Lateral or posterior skin incision · elevate **ECRL + BR** off anterior supracondylar ridge, triceps posteriorly · brachialis off anterior capsule · **robust anterior access** → capsular release + **HO excision**; coronoid + olecranon tip/fossa, radiocapitellar joint |
| **Medial "over the top" (Hotchkiss)** | Extrinsic contracture, **MCL calcification**, **baseline ulnar nerve symptoms** · with ulnar nerve decompression/transposition · **release posterior band MCL** · work anterior to flexor-pronator mass |
| **Combined medial + lateral** | Single **posterior incision** → both columns |

### Rehabilitation & outcomes

**Post-op** — **regional block**, **CPM through full ROM**, compressive dressing, active + active-assist ROM, extension / dynamic / static progressive splinting as needed

**Compliance** — critical

**Outcome** — variable; **retain ~two-thirds of motion gained** at surgery

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Heterotopic ossification** | Prophylaxis **low-dose radiation** or **indomethacin** · no radiation with **acute fracture** (nonunion risk) |
| **Transient ulnar neuropraxia** | After release (nerve close to contracted joint) |
| **Ulnar nerve damage** | Transpose if **preop flexion <100°** |
| **Recurrent contracture** | — |

## X vs Y

| | **Posterior band MUCL** | **Anterior band MUCL** |
|---|---|---|
| Length change | **+9 mm (60°→120°)** | **Isometric** |
| Contracture → | **Loss of flexion** | — |
| Release → | **↑ flexion**, stable | **Valgus instability** |
| Other | — | Valgus restraint in throwing; Tommy John; strongest |

| | **Arthroscopic release** | **Open release** |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Osteophytes, capsular contracture, loose bodies | **Extensive HO**, **prior ulnar transposition**, severe contracture |
| Risk | **Radial nerve** (portals) > ulnar > median | Ulnar neuropraxia / damage |

## How it's asked

**"Static progressive turnbuckle splint most appropriate?"** → **3 mo post distal humerus ORIF, arc 45–100°, plateaued**\
✗ Wrong options: radial head block to supination · 10–140° arc · ankylosis with HO · after arthroscopic loose body removal

**"2.5 mo after fracture-dislocation fixation, arc 75°, DASH 45 — best initial?"** → **supervised therapy + static progressive splinting**\
✗ Wrong options: self-directed · CPM · MUA

**"True about posterior oblique MCL?"** → **released to gain flexion in post-traumatic contracture**\
✗ Wrong options: strongest · Tommy John · valgus restraint = anterior bundle · pivot shift = LUCL

**"Lacks 40° extension, flexion to 80° — release capsule plus?"** → **posterior band MCL (B)**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior band → valgus instability · LUCL → PLRI

**"Relative contraindication to arthroscopic release?"** → **prior ulnar nerve transposition**\
✗ Wrong options: age, gender, osteophytes, labour

**"Terrible triad 5 y ago, arc 60–95°, extensive HO — treatment?"** → **open capsular release + HO excision via lateral column approach**\
✗ Wrong options: bone scan · arthroscopy · downsize radial head · interposition arthroplasty

**"Nerve most at risk with elbow arthroscopy portals?"** → **radial**

**"Functional elbow ROM?"** → **30–130° (100° arc)**; pronation/supination **50–50°**
