# Glenohumeral Joint Anatomy, Stabilizer, and Biomechanics

*Shoulder & Elbow · 19 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Scapulohumeral rhythm

**GH : ST ratio (TO)** — **2:1**\
✗ Wrong options: 1:2 · 2.5:1 · 3:1

**First 30° elevation (TO)** — largely **glenohumeral**; scapulothoracic small + inconsistent → after 30° both move **simultaneously**

**180° abduction** — **120° GH (⅔) + 60° scapulothoracic (⅓)**

**Rhythm variation (TO thrower lecture)** — ratio shifts toward **1:1 at ~60°**, ~**5:4** near 120° · without scapular rotation → deltoid active insufficiency → abduction limited to **60–70°**

**Scapular plane** — **30° anterior** to coronal plane

**Abduction needs ER** — clears greater tuberosity from acromion → **IR contracture → cannot abduct >120°**

### Osteology

| Structure | Key numbers |
|---|---|
| **Humeral head** | Spheroidal in 90% · diameter **43 mm** · **inclination 130°** to shaft · tuberosities = cuff attachments |
| **Glenoid** | **Pear-shaped** · upward tilt **5°** · covers only **25–30% of head** (TO) |
| **Coracoid** | **"Lighthouse"** of deltopectoral approach · coracobrachialis, pec minor, short head biceps |
| **Acromion** | 3 ossification centres: **pre- (tip), meso- (mid), meta-acromion (base)** |
| **Acromiohumeral interval** | **7–8 mm** |

### Glenoid & humeral version

| | **Glenoid** | **Humeral head** |
|---|---|---|
| Orthobullets | **5° retroversion** avg (scapular body axis); **7° retro → 10° ante** | **~20° retroversion** (transepicondylar axis) |
| TO | **Near 0°**, more often **slight retroversion**; <10° either way | **Retroverted 26° ± 11°** |
| TO (bone-loss lecture) | **~7° retroversion** to scapular plane · **~5° superior inclination** | **~30° retroversion** · neck–shaft **130°** · diameter **~43 mm** |

**Humeral retroversion** — **20°** (Orthobullets) · **26° ± 11°** (TO) — read the options

**Humeral retroversion (TO bone-loss lecture)** — **30°** — read the options

**False (TO)** — **"both glenoid and humeral head anteverted"**

### Humeral head blood supply

**Main supply** — **posterior humeral circumflex artery** (current literature) → injury most likely → **AVN**

**Why PHCA?** — **AHCA disrupted in ~80%** of proximal humerus fractures yet AVN infrequent

**Ascending branch of AHCA** — runs **parallel to lateral aspect of LHB** in bicipital groove → **arcuate artery** = intraosseous continuation into head; do not injure when plating

### Labral anatomy & blood supply

**Composition** — **fibrocartilage** (TO)

**Function** — **50% of socket depth** · cavity-compression · ↑ depth, ↑ articular surface area, **centralises head**, **seal → intra-articular pressure** (TO)

**Embryology (TO)** — labrum forms **~8th week**; LHB + capsule insertion defined by **9th week**

**Blood supply** — **suprascapular** · **circumflex scapular** (TO) / anterior humeral circumflex (Orthobullets) · **posterior humeral circumflex**; from **capsule + periosteal vessels**, not underlying bone

**Poorest blood supply** — **anterosuperior labrum** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: posterosuperior

**Biceps attachment (TO)** — to labrum, **mainly posterior**

**Weak links** — anterior labrum anchors **IGHL** → **Bankart** · superior labrum anchors **biceps** → **SLAP**

## 2 Mechanism

### Static vs dynamic stabilisers

| **Static** | **Dynamic** |
|---|---|
| **Glenohumeral ligaments** | **Rotator cuff** (main) |
| **Glenoid labrum** | Rotator interval |
| Articular congruity + version | **Long head biceps** (humeral head depressor) |
| **Negative intra-articular pressure** (release → head subluxes **inferiorly**) | Periscapular muscles |

**Bony articulation** — **inherently unstable**

**Posterior capsule** — thin (**<1 mm**), **no ligaments**

### Glenohumeral ligaments – function by arm position (IGHL, MGHL, SGHL, CHL)

| Arm position | Anterior restraint | Inferior restraint | Posterior restraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0° (side) + adduction** | | **SGHL / CHL** | |
| **45° abduction + ER** | **MGHL** | | **MGHL** |
| **90° abduction + ER** | **Anterior band IGHL** | **Anterior band IGHL** | **Posterior band IGHL** |
| **90° flexion, adduction, IR** | Anterior band IGHL | | **SGHL / CHL** |

![Arthroscopic view: 1 SGHL, 2 subscapularis, 3 MGHL, 4 IGHL, 5 biceps](../../MCQ/attachments/q4514-illa-arthroscopy-question-3.jpg)

**Anterior static stability (TO)** — **anterior band IGHL = main restraint** (90° abduction + ER) · **MGHL** (45° abduction + ER) · **SGHL** (arm at side)

**Hammock (TO)** — at **90° abduction** IGHL = hammock: **anteroinferior band = anterior** support, **posteroinferior band = posterior** support

**Abduction + ER main stabiliser (TO)** — **IGHL**

**>45° abduction, anterior + posterior stress (TO)** — **anterior inferior GHL**\
✗ Wrong options: SGHL · CHL · anterosuperior labrum

**SGHL** — anterosuperior labrum → humerus · **inferior translation at 0° abduction / adduction** (neutral rotation) → **greatest stress = inferiorly translated, arm adducted** · restrains **ER of adducted arm** · prevents **anteroinferior translation of LHB** (biceps pulley)

**MGHL** — **anterior (and posterior) translation in midrange ~45° (45–60°) abduction + ER** · from labrum, crosses **subscapularis obliquely (~45°)** → **just medial to lesser tuberosity** · do not confuse with subscapularis

**Anterior band IGHL** — **anterior/inferior translation at 90° abduction + maximal ER** (**late cocking**) · anchors anterior labrum → **Bankart weak link**

**Posterior band IGHL** — **posterior subluxation at 90° flexion + IR** (forward flexed, adducted) · restraint in **posterior instability** (Kim test) → must repair · **tightness → internal impingement** + ↑ shear on superior labrum (SLAP)

**Superior band IGHL** — **most important static stabiliser** (Orthobullets); **100% ↑ strain with SLAP lesion**

**CHL** — coracoid → **rotator cable** · limits **posterior translation in flexion, adduction, IR** · limits **inferior translation + ER in adduction**

**Primary restraint to inferior translation** — **CHL** (OB; Bigliani: inferior capsule + IGHL also contribute) · **SGHL/CHL** at 0° (Orthobullets, OB) — read the options

### Ligament anatomy (TO)

| Ligament | Origin → insertion (TO) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **CHL** | **Proximal ⅓ of horizontal coracoid** → variable: superficial fibres **greater tuberosity**; only **15–50%** to lesser tuberosity | Blends with supraspinatus + subscapularis |
| **SGHL (Flood ligament)** | **Superior glenoid tubercle** → **fovea capitis** just above lesser tuberosity | Key **stabiliser of LHB** in groove |
| **MGHL** | Just beneath/medial to SGHL → **just medial to lesser tuberosity** | Blends with subscapularis |
| **IGHL** | Anterior + posterior bands + **axillary pouch** (reinforced by **fasciculus obliquus**) → humeral neck like a **collar** under articular margin | Hammock |

**Foramina (TO)** — **Weitbrecht** = between SGHL + MGHL · **Rouvière** = between MGHL + IGHL

**IGHL anterior band origin** — **2–4 o'clock** (one TO) · **3–5 o'clock** (another TO); posterior band **7–9 o'clock** — read the options

### Stabilisers by abduction, neutral rotation (TO)

| Abduction | Anterior | Posterior | Inferior |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rest** | Supraspinatus | Posterior capsule | **Superior capsule, CHL, SGHL**, supraspinatus, biceps |
| **0–45°** | Subscapularis, **MGHL**, IGHL | Posterior capsule, IGHL | IGHL, MGHL |
| **45–90°** | **IGHL**, posteroinferior capsule, biceps (IR), cuff, deltoid | Posterior capsule, IGHL, biceps (ER) | IGHL, MGHL |
| **>90°** | **IGHL, axillary pouch**, subscapularis, infraspinatus | Posterior capsule, IGHL | — |

**Glenoid inclination** — also listed among stabilisers in the TO table

### Rotator interval contents

**Definition** — triangular space between **upper border of subscapularis** + **anterior margin of supraspinatus** (TO); bridged by capsule

| Boundary | Structure |
|---|---|
| **Base (medial)** | **Coracoid** (lateral base) |
| Superior | Anterior edge **supraspinatus** |
| Inferior | Superior border **subscapularis** |
| **Apex (lateral)** | **Transverse humeral ligament** |

**Contents** — **CHL + SGHL** + LHB + thin capsule; CHL + SGHL = most constant structures of fibrous capsule\
✗ Wrong options: MGHL

**Layers (TO)** — 4 layers incl. **superficial CHL** · supraspinatus + subscapularis fibres · **deep CHL**

**Arthroscopy** — area between **biceps + subscapularis tendon** = **rotator interval**; biceps deep to interval

**Contracture** — **adhesive capsulitis** → release · **laxity** → **sulcus sign** (inferior laxity in adduction) → **RI closure / CHL imbrication** improves inferior stability (Harryman)

### Dynamic stabilisers – concavity compression & rotator cuff

**Rotator cuff** — **main dynamic stabiliser**; **compresses humeral head into glenoid**; works most in **midrange**, not extremes

**Concavity compression** — **most important midrange stabilising mechanism**; depends on **depth of concavity + magnitude of compressive force**\
✗ Wrong options: isometric ligaments · capsule tension · deltoid

| ✗ Distractor | Actual role |
|---|---|
| SGHL, MGHL | **Static** stabilisers |
| Teres major | Adducts + IR; not a significant stabiliser |
| Deltoid | Abducts; not main stabiliser |

### Free body analysis of deltoid

**Arm 90° abduction** — A (deltoid lever) = 3 cm, B = 30 cm → (A × D) − (B × 0.5W) = 0 → **D = 5W**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Normal variants (Buford complex, cord-like MGHL) – do not repair

| Variant | Frequency | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Normal** (labrum on rim + flat/broad MGHL) | Most common | Cord-like MGHL quoted in **86%** (Orthobullets) |
| **Sublabral recess (sulcus)** (TO) | **Most common** variant | **11–1 o'clock** at biceps–labral complex; smooth, **<2 mm**; follows rim **medially**; **does not extend posterior to biceps anchor** |
| **Sublabral foramen (hole)** (TO) | **~12%** (Orthobullets) · **11%** (TO) | Anterosuperior labrum detached **1–3 o'clock**, anterior to biceps anchor; smooth, **<1.5 mm** |
| Sublabral foramen + cord-like MGHL | | |
| **Buford complex** | **~1.5%** | **Cord-like MGHL + absent anterosuperior labrum** (MGHL attaches to base of biceps anchor) |
| Meniscoid labrum | 1% | |

![Anterosuperior labral variants: (a) normal, (b) sublabral foramen, (c) foramen + cord-like MGHL, (d) Buford](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3032-variants-in-anterosuperior-labrum-anatomy-a-normal-b-sublabr.jpg)

**Buford (TO)** — **normal variant**, 1.5% · cord-like **MGHL** (✗ Wrong options: SGHL) + absent **anterosuperior** (✗ Wrong options: anteroinferior) labrum · not abnormal · **should not be repaired** → ↓ ROM

**Repairing Buford to glenoid** — **painful loss of ER** (and elevation) · **loss of ER with arm at side** > at 90° abduction (MGHL tighter at side)

**Sublabral foramen vs tear (TO)** — true anterosuperior tear **propagates superiorly into biceps anchor or inferiorly into IGHL** attachment

**Tubercle of Assaki (TO)** — ridge of subchondral bone at **centre of glenoid** with focal **cartilage thinning** → do not mistake for cartilage defect

**Subacromial pseudospur (TO)** — prominence of acromial angle at **CA ligament** attachment → mimics caudal osteophyte; also at deltoid tendon attachment → mimics enthesophyte

### Key radiology findings

**Sublabral recess** — **best seen with arthrography** (TO)

**AHI** — **7–8 mm**; normal on X-ray but ↓ on supine MRI (arm weight removed) → **multiple-tendon cuff tear**

**HAGL on MRI** — tear of **humeral attachment of IGHL**

### Acromial morphology

| Type | Shape |
|---|---|
| I | **Flat** |
| II | **Curved** |
| III | **Hooked** |

### HAGL lesion

**Mechanism** — **abduction + hyperextension + ER** (e.g. 15-year-old wrestler)

**HAGL** — humeral avulsion of IGHL; **much less common than Bankart** (anteroinferior labral tear)

| ✗ Distractor | Why |
|---|---|
| Lesser tuberosity avulsion | Very rare; **12–13-year-olds** |
| Subscapularis tear | Same mechanism, **older** patients |

## 5 Management

### Arthroscopic vs open repair – external rotation preservation

**Advantage of arthroscopic** — **preservation of ER** (spares subscapularis; no anterior capsular incision)

**Not advantages** — ↓ healing time · outpatient (both) · faster return (recovery identical) · ↓ recurrence (arthroscopic **slightly higher or equal**)

### Shoulder arthrodesis position

| Plane | Angle |
|---|---|
| Abduction | **15–20°** |
| Forward flexion | **20–25°** |
| Internal rotation | **40–50°** |

**Other sources** — **30-30-30** (Orthobullets arthrodesis note) · ~40° abd / 35° FF / 20° IR (OB) — read the options

## How it's asked

**"Most important static stabiliser at 45° abduction + ER?"** → **MGHL** (TO)

**"Main stabiliser in abduction + ER?"** → **IGHL** (TO)

**"At 90° abduction, main stabiliser against anterior dislocation?"** → **anterior-inferior GHL** (TO)

**">45° abduction, restraint to anterior + posterior stress?"** → **anterior inferior GHL** (TO)

**"Primary static restraint to anterior translation at 90° abduction?"** → **IGHL complex**\
✗ Wrong options: bony shape · negative pressure

**"SGHL under greatest stress?"** → **inferiorly translated, arm adducted (5°)**

**"Asterisk structure (crosses subscapularis) — function?"** → **prevents anterior translation at 45° abduction** (MGHL)

**"Labelled ligament restraining anterior translation at 45° abduction + ER?"** → **MGHL (D)**

**"Primary restraint to inferior translation?"** → **coracohumeral ligament**\
✗ Wrong options: MGHL · subscapularis · CAL

**"90° abduction, full ER — resists anterior translation?"** → **anterior band of IGHL complex**

**"Arrowed structure, posterior portal — function?"** → **resists anterior + posterior translation at 45° abduction** (MGHL)

**"Posterior instability, + Kim test — structure to repair?"** → **posterior band IGHL**\
✗ Wrong options: AIGHL · CHL

**"Besides biceps, rotator interval contains?"** → **CHL + SGHL**\
✗ Wrong options: MGHL

**"Arthroscopic area between biceps and subscapularis?"** → **rotator cuff interval**

**"Dynamic stabiliser compressing head into glenoid?"** → **rotator cuff muscles**\
✗ Wrong options: teres major · deltoid

**"Most important midrange stabilising mechanism?"** → **concavity compression**

**"Buford complex?"** → **normal variant: cord-like MGHL + absent anterosuperior labrum** (TO)

**"Repairing Buford complex causes?"** → **loss of external rotation**

**"Probe on cord-like MGHL repaired to glenoid — result?"** → **loss of ER with arm at side**

**"Asterisk, posterior portal, anterior structure crossing subscapularis?"** → **MGHL**

**"15-year-old wrestler, abduction-hyperextension-ER, MRI?"** → **tear of humeral insertion of IGHL (HAGL)**\
✗ Wrong options: lesser tuberosity avulsion · Bankart

**"AVN after proximal humerus fracture — artery?"** → **posterior humeral circumflex (4)**

**"Advantage of arthroscopic anterior stabilisation?"** → **preservation of external rotation**

**"False about glenoid labrum?"** → **posterosuperior labrum has poorest blood supply** (TO) (anterosuperior is poorest)

**"GH : ST motion ratio?"** → **2:1** (TO)

**"False about glenoid/humeral version?"** → **both anteverted** (TO)
