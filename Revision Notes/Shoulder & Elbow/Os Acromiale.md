# Os Acromiale

*Shoulder & Elbow · 31 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Definition

**Os acromiale** — **unfused secondary ossification centre** of the acromion (**failure of fusion of anterior acromial apophysis**, OB) → impingement, cuff tendonitis

### Epidemiology

**Prevalence** — **8%** (Orthobullets, OB) · **1–15%** (another OB) — read the options

**Bilateral** — **60%** (Orthobullets) · **33%** (Sammarco skeletons, OB) — read the options

**Race** — **Black > White** (**13.2% vs 5.8%**)

**Sex** — **♂ > ♀** (**8.5% vs 4.9%**)

**Usually incidental** — found on imaging for other reasons, often **trauma**

### Anatomy – failed fusion of meso- and meta-acromion

| Ossification centre | Position | Origin of |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-acromion** | **Tip** | **Anterior deltoid + coracoacromial ligament** |
| **Meso-acromion** | **Mid** | **Middle deltoid** |
| **Meta-acromion** | **Base** | **Posterior deltoid** |
| Basi-acromion | (4th centre, OB) | — |

**Number of centres** — **3** (Orthobullets, OB) · **4: pre-, meso-, meta-, basi-** (another OB) — read the options

**Fusion age** — ossify medially toward clavicular facet **by 18 y** (OB) · **15–18 y** (Hurst, OB)

**Most common site** — **junction of meso- and meta-acromion** = commonest **symptomatic** form\
✗ Wrong options: meso–pre · meta–pre

**Blood supply** — **acromial branch of thoracoacromial artery** → preserve at surgery

![Ossification centres: pre- (PA), meso- (MSA), meta-acromion (MTA)](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3036-os-acromiale-drawing.jpg)

### Pathological mechanism

**✓ Mechanism** — **failure of fusion between ossification centres**

| ✗ Mechanism distractor | Condition |
|---|---|
| Failure of mesenchymal segmentation | **Tarsal coalition** |
| Defect in chondrocyte proliferation | **Achondroplasia** |
| Glycine substitution, abnormal collagen cross-linking | **Osteogenesis imperfecta** |
| Failure of cartilaginous differentiation | **Congenital radioulnar synostosis** |

### Associated conditions

**Impingement + rotator cuff disease** — **meso-acromion** → cuff tendonitis + **full-thickness tears in 50%**

## 3 Presentation

**Impingement pain** — anterior fragment **flexes / displaces downward** with deltoid contraction + arm elevation → ↓ subacromial space

**Synchondrosis pain** — **motion at nonunion site**

**Symptoms** — **superior shoulder pain**, **overhead activity**, **lying on affected side**; tenderness **anterolateral / superior acromion**

**Trigger** — **trauma** can make a previously silent os symptomatic

**Diagnostic injection** — **1% lidocaine** into site when pain source unclear (Spiegl)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Use |
|---|---|
| **Axillary lateral X-ray** | **Best view** for diagnosis |
| **CT** | Nonunion site; **degenerative change (cysts, sclerosis, hypertrophy)** |
| **Axial MRI** | Shows os (superior pain + overhead vignette) |

**Os vs fracture** — os: **rounded edges + corticalisation of entire fragment** · fracture: acute

**Fused os acromiale** — may be mistaken for a free ossicle (Sammarco)

| ✗ Diagnosis distractor | |

| Type IV AC separation | Posterior clavicle |

| AC OA / AC separation / PTRCT / SLAP | MRI shows os |

## 5 Management

```
Os acromiale
├─ Incidental / mild symptoms → observe, NSAIDs, PT, subacromial steroid
│      └─ fails 6–12 months → surgery
├─ Symptomatic os + impingement → two-stage fusion
├─ Impingement ± cuff tear, os incidental + nontender → ASD + acromioplasty
└─ Small symptomatic pre-acromion / failed ORIF → fragment excision
```

### Nonoperative

**Regimen** — observation, **NSAIDs**, activity modification, therapy, **subacromial corticosteroid** → **majority**

**Duration** — surgery only after **6–12 months** of nonop

### Operative criteria – treatment of symptomatic os acromiale (fusion)

| Procedure | Indication | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Two-stage fusion** | **Symptomatic os + impingement**, failed nonop | **Stage 1: fuse ± bone graft** (rigid fixation: **TBW, sutures, cannulated screws**) → **Stage 2: acromioplasty** |
| **Arthroscopic SAD + acromioplasty** | **Impingement ± cuff tear, os incidental + nontender** | — |
| **Fragment excision** (open/arthroscopic) | **Symptomatic pre-acromion, small fragment** · **salvage after failed ORIF** | Arthroscopic: less periosteal + deltoid detachment; **better results with pre-acromion** |

**Direct excision of large fragment** — **deltoid dysfunction** → prefer fusion

**Warner (15 shoulders)** — **cannulated screws + tension band + autograft** → union in **all but one**; 8 had cuff tears

**Harris** — commonest concurrent procedure **cuff repair (59%)**, then **distal clavicle excision (25%)**; all techniques improve outcomes

**Abboud** — meso-os + cuff pathology satisfactory in **4 of 8**; **80%** satisfactory overall vs **22%** in **workers' compensation**

**44-yo, failed PT after cuff repair, symptomatic os + impingement** — ✗ Wrong options: observation · more PT · ASD alone (os not incidental) · excision alone

## 6 Complications

**Deltoid weakness** — after **fragment excision**

**Persistent pain / weakness**

**Prognosis** — **poorer outcomes after cuff repair** with **meso-os acromiale**; os may worsen outcome of unrelated shoulder surgery (Hurst)

## X vs Y

| | **Two-stage fusion** | **ASD + acromioplasty** | **Excision** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Os status | **Symptomatic, tender** + impingement | **Incidental, nontender** | Small **pre-acromion** / failed ORIF |
| Deltoid | Preserved | Preserved | **Risk of dysfunction** (large fragments) |

## How it's asked

**"27-yo, shoulder pain, X-ray shows os — cause?"** → **failure of fusion meso- and meta-acromion**\
✗ Wrong options: meso–pre · acute fracture · type IV AC · deltoid avulsion

**"23-yo, superior shoulder pain overhead + lying on side, axial MRI?"** → **os acromiale**\
✗ Wrong options: AC OA · PTRCT · SLAP

**"Most common location?"** → **junction of meso- and meta-acromion**

**"Pathological mechanism?"** → **failure of fusion between ossification centres**\
✗ Wrong options: segmentation = coalition · glycine = OI

**"44-yo, failed PT after cuff repair, symptomatic os with impingement — treatment?"** → **two-stage fusion**\
✗ Wrong options: ASD · excision only

**"Os incidental + nontender, impingement ± cuff tear?"** → **arthroscopic SAD + acromioplasty**

**"Best view?"** → **axillary lateral**

**"Complication of excision?"** → **deltoid weakness**
