# Osteochondritis Dissecans of Elbow

*Shoulder & Elbow · 32 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Presentation & population (capitellum, gymnasts/throwers)

**Definition** — localised injury → **separation of articular cartilage + subchondral bone** of the **capitellum**

**Population** — **gymnasts + throwing athletes** (pitchers) · repetitive overhead or **upper-limb weight bearing** · also cheerleaders\
✗ Wrong options: swimmers/divers · linemen · rugby · cyclists

**Age** — **>10 y** (adolescent) (Orthobullets, OB) · "typically **>13 y**" (one OB explanation)

**Sex** — **boys > girls**

**Site** — **capitellum** of **dominant** arm (most common) · occasionally **radial head** (OB) \
✗ Wrong options: "capitellum or lateral trochlea"

**Juvenile vs adult** — **juvenile OCD better prognosis**

### Anatomy – blood supply

**Capitellum** — supplied by **2 end arteries**: **radial recurrent** + **interosseous recurrent** → vulnerable to ischaemia

![Capitellar blood supply – radial recurrent + interosseous recurrent arteries](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3085-blood-supply.jpg)

### Pathophysiology

**Mechanism** — **repetitive compression** (lateral compression + axial loading) of **immature capitellum** → **vascular insufficiency** + **repetitive microtrauma** → osteochondral necrosis → separation

**Throwers** — **repetitive valgus overload of radiocapitellar joint**, **late cocking** phase

**Panner's + OCD** — likely a **continuum** (OB)

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — insidious, **activity-related lateral elbow pain**, dominant arm · posterolateral pain with throwing

| Sign | Timing |
|---|---|
| **Loss of extension** (mild, ~15°) | **Early** sign = typical early presentation |
| **Catching / locking / grinding / clicking / popping** | **Late** — loose bodies; **poorer prognosis** |
| Lateral tenderness, mild **effusion**, ± crepitus | Any |
| Painful click on passive rotation, painful pronation/supination | Unstable lesion |

**Instability testing** — normal\
✗ Wrong options: MUCL laxity

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + lateral; slightly flexed-elbow view)** — confirms diagnosis: **well-defined subchondral lesion** in capitellum · radiolucency, **flattening**, sclerosis, fragmentation, loose bodies · initial films may be **negative**

![Capitellar OCD – flattening and lucency](../../MCQ/attachments/q216869-c-capitellar-ocd.jpg)

**MRI** — best for **size, extent of oedema, cartilage status** · OCD: **separation of cartilage from capitellum + chondral fissuring** · stable: **intact cartilage + subchondral signal change**, T2 no signal around lesion

**MR arthrogram** — contrast **outlining margins** of lesion = **unstable** fragment → **poor** prognostic sign\
✗ Wrong options: good

**Os supratrochleare dorsale** — normal variant, incidental\
✗ Wrong options: prognostic

### Classification – radiographic & arthroscopic

| Type | Findings | Image |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Intact cartilage**; bony stability may or may not be present | ![Type I](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3085-type-1-ocd.jpg) |
| **II** | **Cartilage fracture** with bony collapse or displacement | ![Type II](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3085-ocd-elbow.jpg) |
| **III** | **Loose bodies** in joint | ![Type III](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3085-type-3-lesion-elbow.jpg) |

### Stability criteria (Takahara)

| **Stable** (heals with rest) — all of | **Unstable** (surgery better) — any of |
|---|---|
| **Open capitellar physis** | **Closed** capitellar physis |
| Localised flattening or radiolucency of subchondral bone | **Fragmentation** |
| Good elbow motion | **Restricted motion ≥20°** |

## 5 Management

```
Capitellar OCD
├─ Type I stable → stop activity 3–6 wk → graded return over 6–12 wk
├─ Unstable type I / stable type II → arthroscopic microfracture or drilling
├─ Large, incompletely displaced → arthroscopic reduction + fixation
├─ Unstable type II / type III → arthroscopic debridement + loose body excision
└─ Large type II–III engaging radial head → OATS
       └─ uncontained → size-matched fresh allograft
```

### Stable lesions – activity cessation

**First line** — **cessation of offending activity** ± immobilisation · **>90% success**

**Duration** — **cessation of activity 3–6 weeks**, followed by slow progression back to activities over next **6–12 weeks** (Orthobullets, OB) · "**minimum 6 wk**" / "**6 wk**" (other OB explanations) — read the options

**Return to play** — resumption when **asymptomatic** + examination shows **restoration of painless range of motion**\
✗ Wrong options: wait for complete radiographic healing (residual X-ray abnormality persists even when asymptomatic)

**No participation** until symptoms resolve + motion recovers\
✗ Wrong options: continue until worse

**Avoid casting 6–8 wk** — too long → stiffness

**Avoid immediate throwing programme** · no surgery before trial of nonop (stable, no loose body)

**Nonop healing** — >50% of stable lesions heal with activity restriction; **smaller lesions without cyst-like change** heal better

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication | Post-op |
|---|---|---|
| **Arthroscopic microfracture / drilling** (extra- or transarticular) | **Unstable type I** · **stable type II** · symptomatic lesion with **intact articular surface** (in situ drilling) | Protected ROM; strengthen 2 mo; throw/WB **4–6 mo** |
| **Fixation** (arthroscopic reduction) | **Large**, **incompletely displaced** (partial separation) | As above; results variable |
| **Arthroscopic debridement + loose body excision** | **Unstable type II** · **type III** · mechanical symptoms | Early ROM ± brace; no throwing/WB **3 mo** |
| **OATS** (autograft/allograft) | **Large type II–III engaging radial head**; uncontained → fresh allograft | Strengthen 3 mo; throwing 5–7 mo |
| Lateral column osteotomy | **Investigational**, salvage (offloads lateral compression) | — |

**Surgery overall** — failed extended nonop · mechanical symptoms · loose bodies on imaging · unstable lesions

**OATS (single plug)** — **93%** graft incorporation, high return to sport, low donor morbidity · large unstable lesions: **fixation/reconstruction > simple excision**

**Microfracture** — among the most commonly performed + studied

### Prognosis – skeletal maturity

**Best predictor** — **skeletal age / open physes**\
✗ Wrong options: symptom duration · gender · activity-related symptoms · anatomic variant

**Healing** — most heal in **6–18 months** of nonop (open physes)

**Poor nonop healing** — **radial head enlargement** · skeletal age difference · closed physis

**Long term** — **~50%** residual ADL symptoms (Takahara: 50% nonop, 46% fragment removal) · **up to 50% arthritis**

## 6 Complications

**Complications** — **stiffness** · pain · **failure to return to sport** · **arthritis**

## X vs Y

| | **OCD of capitellum** | **Panner's disease** |
|---|---|---|
| Age | **>10 y** (adolescent) | **<10 y** (first decade) |
| Lesion | **Focal, well-defined subchondral** lesion | **Entire capitellar ossific nucleus** fragmented; irregular epiphysis |
| MRI | Cartilage separation + chondral fissuring | — |
| Loose bodies | Common | Rare |
| Course | Variable; arthritis up to 50% | **Benign, self-limiting**, normal regrowth, no sequelae |
| Surgery | May be needed | **Contraindicated** |

| Distractor (X-ray / stem) | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| Calcification at common extensor origin | **Lateral epicondylitis** |
| **Widened medial epicondyle physis** | **Little Leaguer's elbow** |
| Incarcerated medial epicondyle fragment | Medial epicondyle fracture |
| **Olecranon osteophyte**, posterior pain in extension | **Valgus extension overload** |
| Medial pain + instability | UCL (Tommy John) |
| After dislocation | PLRI |
| Posterior pain in extension | Olecranon stress fracture |
| Lateral pain, rarely effusion/mechanical | Plica |

## How it's asked

**"13-y pitcher, lateral pain, effusion, painful click on rotation?"** → **OCD**\
✗ Wrong options: Tommy John · PLRI · stress fracture · plica

**"Population for capitellar OCD?"** → **gymnasts + throwing athletes**

**"Characteristic of elbow OCD?"** → **MRI: cartilage separation from capitellum + chondral fissuring**\
✗ Wrong options: whole ossific nucleus · normal regrowth · <10 y · MUCL laxity

**"Typical early presentation?"** → **loss of extension**\
✗ Wrong options: catching/locking = late · boys <10 = Panner · MR arthrogram outline = poor sign

**"14-y gymnast, can't fully straighten, popping — which X-ray?"** → **capitellar flattening + lucency**\
✗ Wrong options: extensor calcification · physeal widening · olecranon osteophyte

**"12-y gymnast, 15° extension loss, 3 × 7 mm stable lesion, no loose bodies?"** → **no gymnastics until symptoms resolve + motion recovers**

**"Little League pitcher, catching, lateral pain — initial management?"** → **rest; resume when asymptomatic + painless full ROM**\
✗ Wrong options: wait for X-ray healing · drilling · osteotomy

**"11-y pitcher, lacks terminal extension, first visit?"** → **stop pitching 3–6 wk + slow return**\
✗ Wrong options: medial epicondyle ORIF · OATS · debridement

**"Type I OCD on MRI, full ROM — initial treatment?"** → **cessation 3–6 wk then progression over 6–12 wk**\
✗ Wrong options: casting 6–8 wk · throwing programme

**"Intact cartilage + subchondral signal change?"** → **cessation of activity for 6 wk**\
✗ Wrong options: microfracture · fixation · OATS

**"Unstable type I or stable type II?"** → **microfracture / drilling**

**"Large lesion engaging radial head?"** → **OATS**

**"16-y cheerleader, closed physes — largest prognostic factor?"** → **skeletal age**
