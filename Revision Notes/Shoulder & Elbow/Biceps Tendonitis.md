# Biceps Tendonitis

*Shoulder & Elbow · 7 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Pathoanatomy

**Nature** — **tendinosis** (degenerative) > true inflammation; inflammatory, traumatic or degenerative causes

**Associations** — usually **secondary** to other shoulder pathology: **subacromial impingement**, **bicipital groove stenosis**, **rotator cuff tears — esp. subscapularis**

**Hourglass biceps** — chronic inflammation → **hypertrophied intra-articular tendon** → **incarcerated in joint** during motion, esp. **elevation**

![Hourglass biceps incarcerated in the joint](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3045-hourglassbiceps.jpg)

### Long head of biceps anatomy – labral attachment, rotator interval & pulley

**Origin (TO)** — **supraglenoid tubercle + superior labrum**; most commonly **50% labrum / 50% tubercle**

**Insertion** — with short head → **radial tuberosity** · action: **elbow flexion + forearm supination** · **no shoulder ROM/strength contribution**

**Synovial status (TO)** — **intra-articular but extrasynovial**; synovial sheath communicates with GH joint → extends into groove

### Tuoheti classification – LHB attachment on labrum (TO)

| Type | Attachment | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Completely posterior | — |
| **2** | **Predominantly posterior** | **Most common** |
| 3 | Anterior + posterior | — |
| 4 | Anterior | **Least common** |

### Rotator interval (TO)

| Feature | Structure |
|---|---|
| **Superior border** | **Supraspinatus** |
| **Inferior border** | **Subscapularis** |
| **Medial edge (base)** | **Coracoid process** |
| Ceiling | Joint capsule |
| Floor | Articular surface of humeral head |
| Reinforced | **CHL laterally**, **SGHL medially** |
| Contents | **LHB** (on the base, covered by capsule), CHL, SGHL, RI capsule |

**✗ Boundary trap (TO)** — **CHL is NOT a boundary** (content/reinforcement)

**Shape (TO)** — triangular **space** (3-D), not 2-D area

**Jost 4 layers (TO)** — 1 superficial CHL fibres (coracoid → greater + lesser tuberosities) · 2 CHL blended with cuff tendons · 3 deep CHL fibres · 4 **SGHL + capsule** mesh

**CHL (TO)** — arises **outside joint**, lateral base of **coracoid** → inserts **greater + lesser tuberosities** → **spans bicipital groove** · superficial layer covers articular surface of supra/infraspinatus · **deep layer thicker** → greater tuberosity

### Biceps pulley & stabilisers (TO)

**Biceps pulley (TO)** — **SGHL + CHL** fuse along lateral half of RI with **subscapularis + supraspinatus** fibres → cradle intra-articular LHB

| LHB portion | Stabilisers (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Intra-articular** | **Labrum + capsuloligamentous structures**, **CHL**, **SGHL**, **supraspinatus**, **subscapularis** |
| **Extra-articular** | **Transverse humeral ligament**, **pectoralis major** muscle/tendon, **bicipital groove** |

**✗ Stabiliser traps (TO)** — **transverse humeral ligament = extra-articular** (✗ Wrong options: intra-articular) · CHL/SGHL = **intra-articular** stabilisers (✗ Wrong options: extra-articular)

### Rotator cable vs crescent (TO)

| | **Rotator cable** | **Rotator crescent** |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | **Thick bundle** of fibres | **Thin, crescent-shaped sheet** of cuff |
| Location | Avascular zone of CHL; **perpendicular to supraspinatus fibres** | **Distal** supraspinatus + infraspinatus insertions |
| Relationship | Proximal margin of crescent | Bounded proximally by cable |

**"Suspension bridge" (TO)** — cable + crescent span supraspinatus + infraspinatus insertions · swapping the two definitions = classic trap

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **anterior shoulder pain** ± radiating down biceps; mimics cuff/impingement pain

**Groove tenderness** — over **bicipital groove**; worse with arm **internally rotated 10°**

| Test | Manoeuvre | Positive |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | Forward elevate against resistance, **elbow extended + forearm supinated** | Pain in bicipital groove; **also + in SLAP** |
| **Yergason** | Active **supination against resistance**, **elbow 90°**, forearm **pronated** | Pain in bicipital groove |
| Popeye | Distal bulge of biceps muscle | **Rupture** |

![Popeye deformity](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3045-popeye.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**US** — **thickened tendon** within bicipital groove

**MRI** — evaluates **concurrent pathology** (subscapularis, cuff) · **thickening + tenosynovitis** of proximal LHB · **↑ T2 signal around tendon** · longitudinal split tears

![Axial MRI, fluid around LHB in groove](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3045-8-1-2012-8-08-59-pm.jpg)

**Diagnostic US-guided injection** — groove steroid → temporary relief supports LHB as pain generator (e.g. 90% relief × 4 wk)

## 5 Management

```
Biceps tendonitis
├─ First line → NSAIDs + activity modification + PT + steroid injection
│      (injection near, not into, tendon)
└─ Refractory to nonop → arthroscopic / open tenotomy or tenodesis
       ├─ faster, simpler, less rehab; ↑ Popeye → tenotomy
       └─ natural contour; drilling / retraction nerve risk → tenodesis (supra- or subpectoral)
```

### Nonoperative

**Regimen** — NSAIDs, **PT strengthening**, activity modification, **steroid injection** (in proximity, **not into tendon**)

### Tenodesis vs tenotomy

**Only proven difference** — **Popeye (cosmetic) deformity ↑ with tenotomy**

**No difference** — **strength**, **pain (VAS)**, **ASES / patient-reported outcomes**, **shoulder + elbow ROM**, **supination** (level 1 evidence)

**Cramping** — tenodesis **possibly ↓ arm cramping** (surgeon belief) · **not shown in high-level studies** — read the options

**Tenotomy pros** — faster, simpler, **less rehab**, **faster return** to activity

**Tenodesis pros** — natural muscle **contour**; but extra risks: nerve injury (**bicortical drilling**, **musculocutaneous retraction**)

**Bite study (OB)** — tenotomy vs suprapectoral tenodesis with cuff repair: differences small; non-inferiority **statistically inconclusive** on Constant-Murley at 1 y

### Tenodesis technique – suprapectoral vs subpectoral, arthroscopic vs open

| | **Arthroscopic suprapectoral** | **Open subpectoral** |
|---|---|---|
| Fixation site | **Proximal** in groove | **Distal** groove, at **pectoralis major insertion** |
| Rationale | — | Removes tendon from groove (eliminates **groove pain**) |
| Residual / groove pain | **Similar** | **Similar** |
| Popeye, ROM, strength, PROMs, revision | **Similar** (revision very low) | **Similar** |
| **Nerve injury** | Lower (**unicortical** drilling) | **↑ transient neuropraxia** · **axillary n.** (bicortical drilling) · **medial NV** structures (retraction) |

**Complications** — overall complication rates **similar** (OB) · **subpectoral ↑ transient neuropraxia** (OB) — read the options

**Groove pain** — Orthobullets lists tenodesis → possible "**groove pain**"; recent prospective studies → **no difference** supra vs subpectoral

![Subpectoral tenodesis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3045-tenodesis-moved.jpg)

### Postoperative function after tenodesis

**Rehab** — full active shoulder ROM; **avoid active supination with elbow 90°** + **resisted elbow flexion** until healed

**Long-term** — **no change in shoulder or elbow strength**\
✗ Wrong options: ↓ elbow flexion · ↓ supination · ↓ shoulder abduction/elevation

## 6 Complications

| Procedure | Complication |
|---|---|
| **Tenotomy** | **Popeye deformity** · ? cramping |
| Tenodesis | Groove pain · **nerve injury** (axillary, musculocutaneous, medial NV) · fixation failure |

## How it's asked

**"Tenodesis vs tenotomy — possibly lower incidence of?"** → **arm cramping**\
✗ Wrong options: elbow flexion weakness · stiffness · shoulder weakness

**"True comparing tenodesis to tenotomy?"** → **↓ Popeye deformity with tenodesis**\
✗ Wrong options: strength, pain, supination, ROM differences

**"Primary outcome difference tenodesis vs tenotomy?"** → **cosmetic deformity**\
✗ Wrong options: ASES, ROM, strength, VAS

**"Photographer worried about strength + deformity (figure D)?"** → **tenotomy ↑ cosmetic deformity, not inferior in PROMs**\
✗ Wrong options: tenodesis superior

**"Suprapectoral vs subpectoral — true?"** → **similar residual anterior shoulder pain**\
✗ Wrong options: ROM, cosmesis, nerve, PROMs differences

**"Subpectoral differs from suprapectoral in?"** → **higher rate of nerve injury**\
✗ Wrong options: PROMs, strength, revision, ROM

**"Arthroscopic suprapectoral vs prior open subpectoral — counselling?"** → **equivalent patient-reported outcomes**

**"Post-op avoid active supination at 90° — long-term function?"** → **no change in shoulder or elbow strength**

**"Rotator interval boundaries EXCEPT"** → **CHL** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: coracoid, subscapularis, supraspinatus

**"True about rotator interval structures?"** → **intra-articular LHB is extrasynovial** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: cable/crescent swapped · CHL/SGHL extra-articular

**"NOT a stabiliser of intra-articular LHB?"** → **transverse humeral ligament** (TO)

**"Tuoheti — most common LHB attachment?"** → **predominantly posterior** (TO)

**"Speed test also positive in?"** → **SLAP lesion**
