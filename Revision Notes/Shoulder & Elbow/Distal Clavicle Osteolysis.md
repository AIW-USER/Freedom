# Distal Clavicle Osteolysis

*Shoulder & Elbow · 11 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Demographics** — **20s**, mostly **♂**; **weightlifters** (bench press, push-ups), overhead athletes, wrestlers

**Risk factor** — history of **trauma** (acute injury) or **repetitive microtrauma**

**Adolescents (OB)** — mean age 15.9 y in one series; overhead sports / weightlifting

### Anatomy

**Clavicle** — S-shaped; **last bone to fuse** (medial physis fuses early 20s)

**AC joint** — **diarthrodial** with **fibrocartilage meniscus**

| Ligament | Stability |
|---|---|
| **AC ligaments** (esp. posterosuperior capsule) | **Horizontal** |
| **CC ligaments** | **Vertical** |

## 2 Mechanism

**Pathoanatomy** — **repetitive stress + micro-fractures** of distal clavicle → **osteopenia + bone resorption**

**Load** — bench press / manoeuvres with **elbows posterior to torso** → traction through AC joint

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — **distal clavicle / anterosuperior shoulder**; **insidious**; ↑ with repetitive loading (bench press, push-ups); mimics AC arthritis

**Exam** — **point tenderness at AC joint / distal clavicle** · **pain with cross-body adduction** · pain without weakness on resisted forward flexion + adduction · **full ROM**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **AP clavicle** + **Zanca view (15° cephalad tilt)** (Orthobullets) · **10–15°** (TO, OB) — read the options

![Zanca view technique](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3048-zanca-view-method.jpg)

**X-ray (distal clavicle only, not acromion)** — **cysts**, **osteopenia**, **resorption + erosion**, **tapering** of distal clavicle, **AC joint widening**

![Distal clavicle osteolysis, AP](../../MCQ/attachments/q636-a-distal-clavicle-osteolysis.jpg)

**MRI** — **↑ T2 signal + bone marrow oedema** in distal clavicle / AC joint (fluid-sensitive)

**Bone scan** — **↑ uptake** distal clavicle; **earlier than X-ray changes**

**Follow-up MRI (OB)** — mean **5 mm AC widening**

## 5 Management

```
Distal clavicle osteolysis
├─ First line → rest + NSAIDs + PT + modify lifting technique
│      └─ persistent → corticosteroid injection (diagnostic + therapeutic; US-guided)
└─ Failed nonop → distal clavicle excision (open or arthroscopic)
       └─ address cuff + LHB pathology at same time
```

### Nonoperative

**Activity modification** — avoid aggravating lifts; **narrow hand grip**; **stop bar descent 4–6 cm above chest**; avoid elbows behind torso

**Steroid injection** — **diagnostic + therapeutic**; **more accurate with US**

**Success (OB)** — **93%** respond to conservative treatment (adolescent series)

### Distal clavicle excision

**Indication** — persistent symptoms after **failed nonop** (rest, PT, cortisone)

**Mumford (arthroscopic)** — resect **only 0.5–1 cm**; **too much → AC instability**

**Open vs arthroscopic** — surgeon preference; arthroscopic **comparable pain relief**, ↓ morbidity, fewer restrictions, **quicker return**, better cosmesis, **evaluates GH joint** + treats concomitant pathology

**Open** — meticulous **deltoid-trapezial fascia** repair

**Results (OB)** — Auge: **10 weightlifters**, limited arthroscopic resection → **return to sport 3.2 d**, weight training **9.1 d** · Zawadsky: 22 excellent, 16 good, **3 failures — all traumatic aetiology**

![After arthroscopic distal clavicle excision](../../MCQ/attachments/q210103-illb-postop.jpg)

## 6 Complications

**Horizontal instability** — **violating posterosuperior capsule / AC ligaments** during excision

**AC instability** — **over-resection** (beyond 0.5–1 cm)

## X vs Y

| | **Distal clavicle osteolysis** | **AC separation** | **SLAP / biceps (wrestler)** | **Cuff tear** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Key clue | AC tenderness, cross-body pain, **no weakness**, full ROM | **Instability** | Common in wrestlers, but exam ≠ AC | **Weakness** |
| Imaging | Lysis, cysts, tapering; marrow oedema | — | Labral pathology | Tendon tear |
| Surgery | **Distal clavicle excision** | **CC ligament reconstruction ± DCE** | SLAP repair / tenodesis | Cuff repair |

## How it's asked

**"Bodybuilder, cross-body pain, AC tenderness, X-ray lysis — most successful treatment?"** → **arthroscopic resection of the distal clavicle**\
✗ Wrong options: GH injection · periscapular strengthening · labral repair · capsular release

**"Wrestler, failed rest + PT + cortisone, AC tenderness, MRI marrow oedema — next step?"** → **distal clavicle excision**\
✗ Wrong options: SLAP repair · subpectoral tenodesis · CC reconstruction

**"Best early imaging before X-ray changes?"** → **bone scan**

**"Complication of excessive / posterosuperior capsular resection?"** → **horizontal AC instability**

**"How much clavicle to resect?"** → **0.5–1 cm**
