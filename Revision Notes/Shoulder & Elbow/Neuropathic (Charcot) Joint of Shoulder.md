# Neuropathic (Charcot) Joint of Shoulder

*Shoulder & Elbow · 29 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **very rare** (~70 cases reported)

**Demographics** — mean age **~50** · **♂:♀ 2:1**

**Sites** — shoulder · elbow · foot & ankle

**Syrinx → Charcot** — **25% of syrinx patients** develop neuropathic arthropathy, **80% in upper limb** (Orthobullets, OB) · **25% of all Charcot joints** due to syringomyelia (Orthobullets associated conditions) — read the options

### Syringomyelia – most common cause & MRI work-up

| Cause | Note |
|---|---|
| **Syringomyelia** | **#1 cause of upper-limb (shoulder) Charcot**; **cervical syrinx = 75%** of Charcot shoulders; monoarticular (**shoulder > elbow**); Hatzis: syrinx in **5/6** shoulders |
| **Arnold-Chiari malformation** | **#1 cause of syringomyelia** |
| **Hansen's disease (leprosy)** | **2nd** most common cause in upper limb |
| **Neurosyphilis (tabes dorsalis)** | Usually **knee**; can be **polyarticular**; no longer the commonest |
| **Diabetes** | **#1 cause in foot & ankle**; reported in shoulder but less common; always rule out |
| **Chronic alcoholism** | Reported (Hatzis: 1/6) |
| Others | MS, ESRD, adhesive/TB arachnoiditis, meningomyelocele, traumatic SCI, familial sensory neuropathy |
| ✗ Wrong option: **uremia** | **No documented association** (OB) |

### Pathophysiology

**Syrinx** — **fluid-filled cavity in spinal cord** → damages **decussating fibres of lateral spinothalamic tract** → **loss of pain + temperature**

**Dissociated anaesthesia** — pain/temperature lost · **proprioception + motor preserved** (early)

**Syrinx enlarges** — dorsal column + **anterior horn** → **areflexia**, weakness, atrophy

**Neurotraumatic theory** — ↓ sensation/proprioception → **repetitive microtrauma** + abnormal joint pressures

**Neurovascular theory** — dysregulated reflexes → **↑ blood flow (hyperaemia)** → **↑ osteoclastic resorption**

### Genes & lab markers

**Molecular** — **RANK/RANKL/OPG** pathway implicated

**Labs** — **ESR + WBC may be ↑** → hard to separate from osteomyelitis · can be **normal** (OB stem: CBC, ESR, CRP normal)

**Histology** — synovial hypertrophy · **detritic synovitis** (cartilage + bone fragments in synovium)

## 3 Presentation

**History** — trauma as inciting event in **30%**

**Symptoms** — swollen shoulder · **50% painless** · loss of function · instability · progressive **painless weakness** + ↓ ROM

**Inspection** — **swollen, warm, erythematous** (intermittent) → **mimics infection**

**Motion** — **loss of motion = most common finding (90%)** · crepitus · laxity/mechanical instability

**Neuro** — ↓ strength · **cape-like** sensory + temperature loss (back + arms) · asymmetric reflexes · **areflexia late**

**Most common findings (OB)** — **pain, loss of motion, instability**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + scapular Y)** — **gold standard / initial** diagnosis

| Stage | X-ray finding |
|---|---|
| Early | Degenerative changes **mimic OA** |
| Late | **Superomedial flattening of humeral head** |
| Late | **Periarticular soft-tissue calcifications** |
| Late | **Glenoid sclerosis** |
| Late | **Extensive bone resorption** (humeral head "amputated"), fragmentation |
| Late | Destruction of **both sides** of joint → subluxation/dislocation |

![Charcot shoulder – resorption of humeral head, destruction both sides of joint](../../MCQ/attachments/q216787-a-shoulder-neuropathic2.jpg)

**MRI cervical spine** — **next step in every Charcot shoulder** → syrinx (signal ≥ CSF on T1)

![Cervical syrinx on MRI](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9044-syrinx-mri.jpg)

**CT** — if concern for osteomyelitis: intraosseous gas, cortical destruction, sequestra

| Scan | Charcot | Osteomyelitis |
|---|---|---|
| **Technetium bone scan** | **Hot** | Hot |
| **Indium-labelled WBC scan** | **Cold (negative)** | **Hot (positive)** |

**Best test to separate from osteomyelitis** — **indium WBC scan**

### Differential diagnosis

| ✗ Distractor (OB) | Why not |
|---|---|
| **AVN** (loss of humeral head blood supply) | **Sclerosis + flattening** of head only |
| **Metastasis** (distant primary) | Variable; no obliteration of **both sides** of joint |
| **Cartilage tumour** | ↑ calcification throughout lesion |
| **Bone-forming tumour** | ↑ ossification throughout lesion |
| **Osteomyelitis / septic joint** | Same swelling, warmth, ↑ WBC/ESR, hot Tc scan → **indium scan hot** |

**Others** — synovial chondromatosis · soft-tissue sarcoma · tumoral calcinosis · Winchester syndrome · **Gorham's disease** · **Milwaukee shoulder**

## 5 Management

```
Charcot shoulder
├─ All → MRI C-spine + treat underlying disease
│      └─ cervical syrinx → neurosurgical decompression
├─ First line → rest, NSAIDs, sling, activity restriction, gentle PT
│      └─ severe pain → GH corticosteroid injection
└─ Failed nonop
       ├─ classic → shoulder arthrodesis
       └─ underlying condition controlled → off-label arthroplasty
```

### Nonoperative

**First line** — rest, NSAIDs, **sling immobilisation** (slows ligamentous laxity), activity restriction, treat cause, gentle PT/passive stretching · **50% improve**

**Corticosteroid injection** — severe pain · **glenohumeral** most effective · temporary **80%** pain ↓ (case reports)

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication | Outcome / technique |
|---|---|---|
| **Neurosurgical decompression** | **Cervical syrinx present** | Slows progression, improves function + bone (**glenoid regrowth**); posterior fossa decompression, craniotomy, **syringoperitoneal shunt**, laminectomy |
| **Arthrodesis** | Severe pain, **failed nonop**; historically **only** option | **S-shaped incision** scapular spine → over acromion → anterolateral arm |
| **Arthroplasty** (hemi, TSA, rTSA, resurfacing) | Failed nonop **+ underlying condition treated** | **Off-label** (Charcot = **strict FDA contraindication**, loosening) · **70%** improved function at 5 y |

**Fusion position ("30-30-30")** — **20–30° abduction · 20–30° forward flexion · 20–30° internal rotation** → hand to mouth

**No emergent ORIF / I&D** — not indicated (afebrile, normal labs) → **C-spine MRI first**

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Risk factor |
|---|---|
| **Infection** | Surgery **without managing underlying condition** |
| **Upper-limb DVT** | Any surgery |
| **Acromial stress fracture** | **Reverse TSA** for Charcot |
| **Progressive glenoid erosion** | **Hemiarthroplasty** |

**Missed diagnosis** — often missed → **inappropriate surgery** (Hatzis)

## X vs Y

| | **Charcot shoulder** | **Osteomyelitis** | **AVN humeral head** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain | Often **painless** (50%) | Painful | Painful |
| Warm, red, swollen | Yes (intermittent) | Yes | No |
| X-ray | **Resorption + destruction both sides**, calcifications | Destruction, sequestra | **Sclerosis + flattening** (head only) |
| Indium WBC | **Cold** | **Hot** | — |
| Next step | **MRI C-spine** | Aspirate/CT | MRI shoulder |

| Charcot cause | Classic joint |
|---|---|
| Syringomyelia | **Shoulder** (upper limb) |
| Diabetes | **Foot & ankle** |
| Tabes dorsalis | **Knee** (polyarticular) |

## How it's asked

**"Chronic shoulder pain, X-ray of destroyed/fragmented humerus — most common cause?"** → **syringomyelia**\
✗ Wrong options: diabetes · syphilis · alcoholism · uremia

**"Most common cause of upper-extremity neuropathic arthropathy?"** → **syringomyelia**\
✗ Wrong options: Hansen's = 2nd · diabetes = foot · neurosyphilis = knee

**"Painless progressive weakness, SCI, normal CBC/ESR/CRP, Charcot X-ray — early management?"** → **cervical spine MRI**\
✗ Wrong options: HIV test · repeat labs · emergent ORIF · emergent I&D

**"Painless stiff shoulder, intermittent warmth, destroyed joint — work-up will show?"** → **fluid-filled cavity in spinal cord**\
✗ Wrong options: AVN · metastasis · cartilage/bone tumour

**"Tract damaged by syrinx?"** → **decussating fibres of lateral spinothalamic tract** (pain + temperature)

**"Most common cause of syringomyelia?"** → **Arnold-Chiari malformation**

**"Test to differentiate Charcot from osteomyelitis?"** → **indium WBC scan (cold in Charcot)**

**"Syrinx found — treatment?"** → **neurosurgical decompression**

**"Shoulder fusion position?"** → **20–30° abduction, flexion, IR each**

**"Complication of rTSA in Charcot shoulder?"** → **acromial stress fracture**
