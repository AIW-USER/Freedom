# Lateral Ulnar Collateral Ligament Injury (PLRI)

*Shoulder & Elbow · 22 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Primary elbow stabilisers (coronoid)

**Primary constraint (TO)** — release alone → laxity · **secondary** — release alone insufficient, ↑ laxity only after primary divided

| Primary constraints (TO) | Secondary constraints (TO) |
|---|---|
| **Ulnohumeral articulation (coronoid)** | **Radiohumeral articulation** (radial head) |
| **Anterior MCL (AMCL)** | Common **flexor-pronator** tendon |
| **Lateral collateral ligament complex (LCLC)** | Common **extensor** tendon |
| | **Capsule** |

**Coronoid (TO)** — **main stabiliser** of elbow; transection affects stability most · **loss of ≥50% → instability**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior band MCL · LCL · radial head

**Main osseous stability (TO)** — trochlea ↔ ulnar olecranon articulation; LCL + MCL blend into capsule

### LUCL anatomy & failure site (humeral avulsion)

**LCL complex (4)** — **radial collateral (LRCL)** · **annular** · **accessory lateral collateral** · **lateral ulnar collateral (LUCL)**

**LUCL** — **primary stabiliser to varus + external rotation** (supination) stress; **most robust** of LCL complex; prevents ER subluxation of ulna on humerus

**Origin → insertion** — **lateral epicondyle** → **tubercle of supinator crest (crista supinatoris)** of ulna

**Failure site** — **humeral origin (lateral epicondyle)**, just anterior + distal to it; common extensor origin may also avulse; **radioulnar articulation intact**\
✗ Wrong options: ulnar attachment

![Lateral ligament complex: RCL, annular, accessory, LUCL](../../MCQ/attachments/q217443-illa-lateral-elbow-ligs.jpg)

| ✗ Distractor (labelled diagrams) | Role |
|---|---|
| **Annular ligament** | Stabilises radial head in pronation/supination; does not need repair; not part of LUCL recon |
| Radial collateral / accessory collateral | Less important for PLRI |
| **Orbicular** ligament | Much less important laterally |
| Anterior band MCL | **Primary valgus** restraint (medial) |
| Posterior band MCL | Disrupted in **VPMRI** |
| **Transverse ligament of Cooper** | Coronoid → olecranon; **no stability role** |
| Posterior capsule | Contributes but does not cause PLRI |
| Anconeus | Does not need repair |

| ✗ Location distractor | Site |
|---|---|
| Supinator crest | **LUCL insertion** |
| Radial tuberosity | Biceps insertion |
| Medial epicondyle | **MUCL origin** |
| **Sublime tubercle** | **MUCL insertion** |

## 2 Mechanism

**Definition** — PLRI = **posterolateral subluxation/dislocation of radiocapitellar + ulnohumeral joints**; ulna **supinates (ER) away from humerus**, radial head subluxes **posteriorly on capitellum** in **supination + extension**

**Traumatic (most common)** — **elbow dislocation**: FOOSH → **supination + axial load + valgus** (posterolateral) + extension → LCL complex fails first → **anterior capsule / coronoid** → semilunar notch externally rotates off trochlea

![Mechanism: axial compression, valgus, supination](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3129-mechanism.jpg)

**Iatrogenic** — lateral elbow surgery (**lateral epicondylitis / ECRB debridement**, arthroscopic or open) going **posterior to equator of radial head** → excessive resection → **stay anterior to equator**

**Chronic attenuation** — **cubitus varus malunion** → lateral thrust + abnormal **triceps vector** stretch LUCL

**Radial head fractures** — usually **valgus posterolateral rotatory load** → concomitant LUCL injury common (avulsed from epicondyle)

### O'Driscoll stages (circle of Horii)

| Stage | Disruption | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **LUCL** ± partial/total LCL complex | **PLRI** (pain with varus stress) |
| **2** | + **anterior capsule** | Incomplete posterolateral dislocation |
| **3a** | All soft tissue incl. **posterior MCL**, **except anterior bundle** | Dislocates posteriorly pivoting on **anterior bundle** |
| **3b** | **Complete MCL** | Full dislocation |

**Sequence** — **lateral → medial**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **pain** (vague, poorly localised) · **mechanical: clicking, catching, snapping, clunking, locking** with extension · **frank dislocation is not the most common presentation**

**Classic** — **pushing off armrests to rise from chair** (axial load + supination + valgus) · difficulty with **triceps dips**, **push-ups**

**History** — prior **elbow dislocation** ("never felt right"), or prior **tennis elbow surgery**

**Causes of chronic lateral instability (TO)** — **neglected trauma** (fracture fixed, **LUCL not addressed**) · **steroid injections for lateral epicondylitis** (ligament attenuation) · chronic varus load

**Symptoms (TO)** — apprehension/weakness **reaching out** (varus load) · painful clicking/snapping · apprehension on **axial load in supination + extension** (chair armrests) · unremitting lateral pain + swelling

**Exam** — often benign; **tenderness over LUCL**, **varus instability** (no firm endpoint); full ROM

### Diagnosis – lateral pivot-shift, chair push-up & clinical signs

**Lateral pivot-shift (O'Driscoll)** — supine, arm **overhead** (shoulder fully ER), forearm **supinated**, **valgus + axial load**, elbow from **full extension → flexion** (to **40°** — Orthobullets; to 90° — OB)

**Pivot-shift findings** — subluxed in extension → **reduces with flexion (triceps tension)** with **clunk**; max rotatory/posterior displacement **~40° flexion**; **skin dimple** proximal to radial head · awake = **apprehension**, anaesthetised = **clunk** (more reliable) · analogous to ITB in ACL-deficient knee

![Lateral pivot-shift: supination, valgus, axial compression](../../MCQ/attachments/q5751-illb-illus-b.jpg)

| Test | Technique / positive |
|---|---|
| **Posterolateral rotatory drawer** | Supine, overhead, supinated; finger under radial head → **posterior force + ER → radial head subluxes** behind capitellum |
| Apprehension | Overhead, supinated, valgus while flexing |
| **Chair rise (chair push-up)** | Pain/apprehension pushing up with forearms supinated (**= positive pivot shift**) |
| **Table-top relocation** | |
| **Floor / prone push-up** | **Can't push up with forearm supinated** |

| ✗ Wrong option: valgus (MUCL) tests | Technique |
|---|---|
| Valgus stress | Valgus at **30° flexion**, medial pain |
| **Milking manoeuvre** | Elbow 90°, supinated, thumb pulled posteriorly |
| **Moving valgus stress** | Ranged **70–120°**, supinated, valgus |
| Symptom | **Pain late cocking / acceleration** = valgus instability |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + lateral)** — rule out fractures, confirm **concentric reduction**; often **normal / little value** in PLRI; may show **impaction defect of posterolateral capitellum**

**Fluoroscopy / stress views** — during pivot-shift → **radial head subluxation** (radiocapitellar + ulnohumeral)

**X-ray clues (TO)** — **"drop sign"** on lateral (~**4 mm ulnohumeral gap**) · radiocapitellar gapping on AP · small avulsion/ossification at lateral epicondyle · **varus stress view under IITV widens radiocapitellar joint** · MRI confirmatory

**Drawer at high flexion (TO)** — positive at **80–90°** flexion = **posterolateral capsule laxity** too, not just LCL · pivot shift dimple + clunk at **~40°**

**MRI** — identifies **acute LUCL avulsion**; poor in recurrent/attenuated LUCL (**oblique course**); detects LUCL pathology in only **50%**

### Elbow instability types

| Instability | Cause / association |
|---|---|
| **PLRI** | **LUCL** — after dislocation; lateral pivot shift |
| **Posteromedial rotatory (VPMRI)** | **Varus** + **anteromedial coronoid facet** fracture |
| **Valgus** | **MUCL** — throwers, overuse |
| **Varus** | Rare; **lateral gapping** |
| **Longitudinal forearm** | **Essex-Lopresti** |

| | **VPMRI** | **VPLRI** |
|---|---|---|
| Radial head | **No** fracture | **Fracture** |
| Coronoid | **>15%, anteromedial facet** | **<15%, tip** |
| MCL | **Posterior band** ruptured; anterior band intact (on AM facet) | **Anterior band** ruptured |
| LCL | LCL complex (LUCL) avulsion | LCL complex (LUCL) avulsion |
| Exam | Valgus stress, moving valgus, milking | **Varus stress, chair rise, lateral pivot shift** |

## 5 Management

```
LUCL injury / PLRI
├─ Acute simple dislocation, stable after reduction → splint 5–7 d → early ROM
├─ Unstable after reduction / entrapment / complex → ORIF + LUCL repair
├─ Mild chronic, low-demand → brace + extensor strengthening, avoid varus
└─ Chronic PLRI, failed nonop → LUCL reconstruction with graft
```

### Acute dislocation – splinting position

**Splint** — posterior splint **5–7 days** · **90° flexion** (Orthobullets) · **within stable arc, no specific angle** (OB) — read the options

| Ligaments disrupted | Forearm position |
|---|---|
| **LCL disrupted, MCL intact** (usual) | **Full pronation** (tightens lateral structures) |
| **LCL + MCL** | **Neutral** |
| Isolated MCL | Supination — **not in PLRI** (LCL always torn) |

**Then** — early active ROM; full pronation/supination from 90° → full flexion; **↑ extension by 30°/week in full pronation**; **full supination in extension after 6 wk**

**✗ Wrong options:** sling only, no immobilisation · splint at 45°/90° in supination

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Open reduction + fracture fixation + LUCL repair** | **Osteochondral fragment / soft-tissue entrapment** blocks concentric reduction · **complex dislocation** (fractures) · **acute instability** (e.g. unstable 0–60° after reduction) |
| **LUCL reconstruction with graft** | **Chronic PLRI** (tissue **attenuated**, not amenable to repair) |
| Bracing, extensor strengthening, avoid gravity varus | **Mild chronic PLRI, low-demand** |
| MCL reconstruction / hinged ex-fix | Only if lateral restoration fails — **rare** |

**Chronic ✗ traps** — direct repair, isolated plication (less reliable) · **thermal shrinkage** (long-term effect unclear) · continued bracing after failed nonop · ECRB debridement · RCL reconstruction · MUCL (Tommy John, docking) reconstruction

**Most common lateral ligament needing repair after posterolateral dislocation** — **LUCL** (reliably torn, off humerus)

### Reconstruction & postoperative protocol (avoid varus, pronation, splinting position)

**Approach** — **posterior midline** or **Kocher**

**Graft** — **palmaris longus** (most common), gracilis, triceps fascia; auto- or allograft

**Configuration** — tunnel at **supinator crest** → **"Y" tunnel** in humerus, tied over lateral column; graft must cover **> posterior 25% of radial head** (sling) · **+ capsuloligamentous plication**

**Tensioning** — **neutral rotation + 45° flexion** (Orthobullets)

**Tensioning (TO)** — **45–60° flexion + full pronation** (reduces residual posterolateral subluxation) — read the options

**TO reconstruction details** — supine, Kocher or **EDC split** · graft **palmaris longus** (may be short), **half FCR**, **gracilis** (best diameter + length) · ulnar tunnels: one at **proximal margin of radial head**, a few mm posterior to joint, second **~2 cm distal over supinator crest** · humeral tunnel **just anterior + distal to isometric point** (anteroinferior lateral epicondyle) → exits posterolateral cortex, **cortical button** · graft **extracapsular** · **box-loop / sling** technique when both collaterals need reconstruction

![LUCL reconstruction: ulnar tunnels at supinator crest, humeral tunnel at isometric point](../../TO notes/attachments/TOn-notes-approaches-around-elbow-chronic-elbow-and-forearm-inst-p07-f019.jpg)

**Fixation** — **docking** (humeral Y tunnels) · interference screws both sides · overlay (sutures over bone)

![Docking LUCL reconstruction: Y tunnel humerus, supinator crest tunnel](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3129-docking.jpg)

**Coronoid** — **large fragment → screw from dorsal ulna** (ACL-type guide) · **small → excise + suture plication of anterior capsule** to tip

**Post-op** — **locked hinge brace**; **avoid varus** and **shoulder abduction** (gravity varus) **6–12 wk**; ROM with extension block → full extension + supination by **6–8 wk**

**Forearm in rehab** — **full pronation** during ROM / extension exercises until 6 wk (least stress on lateral repair); forearm most stable in pronation

**Post-op avoid** — **varus + supination**\
✗ Wrong options: valgus + pronation

| Ligament compromised | Extension exercises with forearm |
|---|---|
| **LUCL (lateral)** | **Pronation** |
| **MUCL (medial)** | **Supination** |
| **Both** | **Neutral** |

**Radial head arthroplasty + lateral repair** — extension exercises in **pronation**\
✗ Wrong options: avoid motion 6 wk → stiffness

### Iatrogenic injury after lateral epicondylitis surgery

**Presentation** — after ECRB release/debridement: ↑ lateral pain, **catching/locking**, painful rising from chair, **+ pivot shift**

**Injured** — **LUCL**\
✗ Wrong options: annular · orbicular · LRCL · anterior MCL

**Prevention** — keep debridement **anterior to equator of radial head**

## 6 Complications

**Recurrent instability** — **3–8%** (Orthobullets) · **<10%** after repair (OB)\
✗ Wrong options: >10%

**Cutaneous nerve injury** — ↓ with **posterior midline** approach

**Others** — infection · **↓ ROM**

## X vs Y

| | **PLRI (LUCL)** | **Valgus instability (MUCL)** |
|---|---|---|
| Patient | After dislocation / lateral surgery | **Thrower** |
| Symptom | **Clicking pushing off chair** | **Pain late cocking / acceleration** |
| Test | **Lateral pivot shift**, drawer, chair rise | **Milking**, moving valgus, valgus at 30° |
| Splint/rehab forearm | **Pronation** | **Supination** |
| Post-op avoid | **Varus, shoulder abduction** | Valgus |
