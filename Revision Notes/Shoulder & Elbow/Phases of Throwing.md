# Phases of Throwing

*Shoulder & Elbow · 34 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### The phases

**Number of phases** — **5**: wind-up · cocking (early + late) · acceleration · deceleration · follow-through (Orthobullets, TO) · **4**: wind-up · cocking · acceleration · deceleration/follow-through (one TO) — read the options

**Six phases (TO biomechanics lecture)** — wind-up · **stride** · cocking (early + late) · acceleration · deceleration · follow-through — read the options

**Timing** — whole throw **~2 s**; wind-up + acceleration ≈ **75% (1.5 s)** (Orthobullets) · **wind-up + cocking = 80% (~1500 ms)**, acceleration **2% (50 ms)**, deceleration **18% (350 ms)** (TO) — read the options

| Phase | Mechanics | Peak muscle activity | Associated pathology |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wind-up** (500–1000 ms, TO) | Body rotates, hips + shoulders 90° to target; "controlled fall"; minimal shoulder force | **Rotator cuff inactive** | — |
| **Early cocking** | Arm loading; max valgus not yet reached | **Deltoid** | — |
| **Late cocking** (500–1000 ms, TO) | **Max shoulder ER** ("loaded", apprehension position), **high torque**; anterior capsule coiled | **Supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor** (rotator cuff); subscapularis late cocking → early acceleration | **MUCL injury** (**max elbow valgus**) · **internal impingement** · **GIRD** · **SLAP (peel-back)** |
| **Acceleration** (**50 ms**, shortest, TO) | Max ER → **ball release (~ear level)**; elastic capsular recoil + IR muscles | Early **triceps** → late **pec major, latissimus dorsi, serratus anterior** | Enormous elbow valgus at release (TO) |
| **Deceleration** (350 ms, TO) | Eccentric braking; centre of gravity over plantar foot | **Eccentric contraction of all muscles**; **highest torque** | **Most harmful**: **SLAP**, **biceps**, **brachialis**, **teres minor** |
| **Follow-through** | Body rebalances, stops forward motion | Muscles **return to resting** | — |

**Most harmful phase** — **deceleration** (Orthobullets, TO) · "**late cocking + deceleration** put greatest force on shoulder" (TO)

**Rotator cuff greatest stress** — **deceleration** (eccentric slowing of arm)

**Minimal throughout** — short head of biceps + coracobrachialis

### Scapular kinematics & muscle activation

**Scapula + humerus** — must work **in concert** for GH stability

**Scapular rotation** — must **rotate in cocking + acceleration** → elevate / clear acromion → prevents cuff impingement

**Retraction / protraction** — **maximal retraction in late cocking** (acromion starts to elevate) · **protraction in acceleration + ball release** (**serratus anterior, long thoracic nerve**)

**✗ Scapula distractors** — max retraction at ball release · protracts in late cocking · fixed during throw (must be dynamic) · no effect on concavity-compression\
✗ Wrong options: — centres head → ↑ concavity-compression

**Kibler – 5 scapular roles** — stable GH articulation · retraction + protraction · **acromial elevation** · base for muscle attachment · **link in kinetic chain**

**Kinetic chain (TO)** — **~½ of kinetic energy** transferred through **scapulothoracic** joint

### Kinetic chain & return to throwing (TO)

**Links (TO)** — feet → lower limbs → hip/pelvis → trunk → scapulothoracic → shoulder → elbow → wrist → hand · **proximal-to-distal sequencing**: each distal link fires as proximal velocity peaks

**Hip + core (TO)** — generate **~50%** of throwing energy · "if the hip is weak, the shoulder will suffer" · ↓ hip IR linked to shoulder injury

| Dysfunction (TO) | Example |
|---|---|
| Anatomical disruption | **GIRD** or ↓ hip IR → shoulder overload |
| Force-distribution error | Over-reliance on arm (tennis / pitcher's shoulder) |
| Inefficient motor pattern | Scapular dyskinesis → ↓ velocity, impingement |

**Hip–shoulder separation (TO)** — rotational angle hips vs torso in stride/wind-up · **30–55°** (peak **67°**) · stores elastic energy (rubber band)

**Catch-up phenomenon (TO)** — stride deficiency → compensatory shoulder load · stride faults → lumbar stress fracture, disc degeneration

**Kinetic chain tests (TO)** — one-leg stability (pelvic drop, valgus collapse) · **Y-balance** (anterior, posteromedial, posterolateral reach) · **prone hip IR** · core/balance

**Thrower's paradox (TO)** — capsule loose enough for ER yet stable enough to avoid subluxation → improve hip/thoracic ROM rather than more GH rotation

**Thrower's shoulder (TO)** — overuse + **microinstability** + adaptive GIRD · triad: **concavity-compression, cam effect, peel-back**

**Bony values (TO)** — humeral retroversion **~30°** · glenoid retroversion **~7°** · articular offset **~2 mm** · ABER → ↑ congruence + contact area, ↓ pressure

### Throwing athlete adaptations (TO)

**Shoulder** — **↑ ER range** (anterior capsule/IGHL stretch) → anterior instability + secondary impingement

**IR:ER strength** — normal **3:2**; exaggerated in throwers → ER weakness ↑ injury risk

**Bony** — ↑ **humeral retrotorsion** (↓ anteversion) · ↑ **glenoid retroversion**

**Elbow** — breakdown of medial stabilisers (MCL, capsule, flexors) → **↑ carrying angle**; anterior capsular strain, posterior impingement, flexor strain → **fixed flexion deformity**

## 3 Presentation

### Internal impingement & GIRD (TO)

**Internal impingement (TO)** — late cocking (**ABER**): posterior cuff undersurface pinched between **greater tuberosity/humeral head and posterosuperior glenoid** → **posterosuperior labral tear** (hallmark) + **articular-sided (PASTA) supraspinatus** tear

**GIRD (TO)** — adaptive **loss of IR** in throwing shoulder; caused by **late cocking** (repetitive cocking)

**GIRD pathology (TO)** — **posteroinferior capsule + posterior band IGHL contracture** (thicker posterior capsule) ± posterior cuff tightness → ↑ GH contact pressure + impingement

**GIRD symptoms (TO)** — stiffness · prolonged warm-up · **loss of velocity ("dead arm")** · vague **posterior** pain · late cocking provocative

**GIRD associations (TO)** — posterior labral tears · posterosuperior PASTA tears · **SLAP (peel-back in ABER)** · scapular dyskinesia · **UCL tears**

**Sulcus sign in GIRD (TO)** — **~⅔ of pitchers**; rotator interval laxity (**CHL + SGHL**)

**Cuff tears in throwers (TO)** — mostly **degenerative partial/full** from impingement (**internal > external**) · minority **acute traumatic** from **deceleration** overload

## 5 Management

```
Throwing shoulder with GIRD
├─ First line → posterior capsular stretching + scapular strengthening
│      └─ sleeper stretch, cross-body stretch, passive prone stretch
└─ Severe GIRD + ↓ TRM, failed extensive nonop → arthroscopic posteroinferior capsular release
```

### Throwing athlete rehabilitation (TO)

**Mainstay (TO)** — **posterior capsular stretching** + **scapular** strengthening

**Sleeper stretch (TO)** — lie on **affected side**, shoulder + elbow **90°**, scapula stabilised → other arm pushes forearm to floor (**IR**); **30 s × 5**

![Sleeper stretch](../../MCQ/attachments/TO21733265-3.png)

**Cross-body stretch (TO)** — standing/sitting, shoulder flexed 90° → other hand pulls arm across chest (**horizontal adduction**); **30 s × 5**

**Not appropriate for GIRD (TO)** — **latissimus dorsi stretch** (✓ sleeper, ✓ cross-body, ✓ passive prone shoulder stretch)

**Stretching benefits (TO)** — ↑ IR · ↑ TRM · ↑ **acromiohumeral distance**

**Surgery (TO)** — only after exhausted nonop; SLAP repair + articular cuff tear treatment **controversial** (variable RTP); **arthroscopic posteroinferior capsular release** for severe GIRD

### Follow-through & injury prevention (TO)

**Long follow-through (TO)** — **decreases** chance of muscle injury\
✗ Wrong options: increases injury · ↑ fatigue · no relation

**Why** — dissipates deceleration forces; forward torso rotation reacquires energy → ↓ burden on serratus + scapular stabilisers

### Ball-flight physics (Magnus effect) (TO)

**Magnus effect (TO)** — **spinning ball** drags boundary layer of air → **high pressure** where boundary layer opposes passing air · **low pressure** where they move together → **lift (Magnus) force** high → low → **curved flight path**

**Sports (TO)** — **tennis**, cricket, soccer, baseball, golf\
✗ Wrong options: sprints · swimming · basketball
