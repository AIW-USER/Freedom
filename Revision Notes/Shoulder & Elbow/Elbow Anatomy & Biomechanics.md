# Elbow Anatomy & Biomechanics

*Shoulder & Elbow · 12 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Joints & function

**3 articulations** — **ulnohumeral** (hinge) · **radiocapitellar / radiohumeral** (pivot) · **proximal radioulnar**

**Function** — lever arm positioning the hand · fulcrum for forearm lever

### Range of motion & load sharing

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Total flexion–extension | **0–150°** |
| **Functional flexion–extension** | **30–130°** |
| **Functional pronation/supination** | **50° each** |
| Carrying angle (valgus) | **♂ 5–10° · ♀ 10–15°** (anatomy note) · ~11° ♂ / ~13° ♀ (exam note) · 6–11° (UCL note); ↓ with flexion |
| Axial load, extended elbow | **40% ulnohumeral · 60% radiohumeral** |

### Osteology

**Spiral groove** — posterior humeral shaft; **radial nerve**; **~13 cm** proximal to trochlear articular surface

**Distal flare** — medial + lateral epicondyles; flare = half of elbow joint · **medial + lateral columns**

**Trochlea** — spool-shaped, **medial** · **capitellum** — lateral

**Sublime tubercle** — medial **coronoid** process; distal attachment of **anterior bundle of MCL**

### Arthrology

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Articular surface **anterior tilt** | **~30°** relative to humeral shaft |
| Valgus | **6°** |
| Rotation | **5° internal** |
| **Axis of flexion** | Centre of **trochlea + capitellum**; passes through **anteroinferior medial epicondyle** |
| **Radial head cartilage** | Covers **~240°**; **lateral 120° bare** (safe zone for radial head fixation) |
| **Capsule** | **Max distension 70–80° flexion**; anterior capsule inserts **6 mm distal to coronoid tip** → **coronoid intra-articular** |
| Coronoid fossa | Receives coronoid tip in deep flexion |
| **Coronoid tip** | **Buttress against posterior dislocation** |

### Muscles of the elbow

**Flexors** — **biceps** (radial tuberosity) · **brachialis** (inserts **11 mm distal to coronoid tip**) · brachioradialis

**Extensor** — triceps

### Collateral ligaments – MCL anterior bundle & LCL complex

| Stabiliser tier | Structures |
|---|---|
| **Primary static** (3) | **Ulnohumeral joint (coronoid)** · **MCL (anterior bundle)** · **LCL complex** |
| **Secondary static** | **Radiocapitellar joint** · **capsule** · **flexor + extensor origins** |
| **Dynamic** | Muscles crossing elbow: **anconeus, brachialis, triceps, biceps** → compression |

**Coronoid** — loss of **≥50% height** → instability

**MCL** — anterior, posterior, transverse bundles; resists **valgus + distraction**; origin **anteroinferior medial epicondyle** → insertion **sublime tubercle**

| MCL bundle | Role |
|---|---|
| **Anterior** | **Most important valgus restraint** (prime medial stabiliser); esp. in flexion (Morrey) |
| **Posterior** (posterior oblique) | **Floor of cubital tunnel** · **primary valgus restraint in maximal flexion** · secondary medial stabiliser · tension ↑ **60–120° flexion** → contracture **limits flexion** |
| **Transverse** | **No role** in valgus stability or ROM |

**2nd most important valgus restraint** — **radial head**\
✗ Wrong options: ulnotrochlear articulation

![MCL – A anterior bundle, B posterior oblique band, C transverse, D annular, E oblique cord](../../MCQ/attachments/q5863-a-medial.jpg)

**LCL complex** — **RCL** · **LUCL** · **accessory collateral ligament** · **annular ligament**; whole complex = **capsular thickening**; arises from **isometric point on lateral capitellum**

| LCL component | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **RCL** | **Originates at axis of rotation for flexion** |
| **LUCL** | **Primary restraint to varus + external rotation** through full arc; origin **posterior lateral epicondyle** → insertion **crista supinatoris** (✗ Wrong options: sublime tubercle) |
| Accessory collateral | ± substantial lateral stability (with RCL) |
| **Annular** | **PRUJ** stability |

**LCL repair** — optimal stability with **appropriately tensioned** repair

![LCL complex – A RCL, B annular, C LUCL](../../MCQ/attachments/q6194-a-21.jpg)

**Not LCL complex** — ulnohumeral joint (primary but not LCL) · radiocapitellar joint (secondary) · anterior bundle UCL · **transverse ligament (MCL complex)**

**LUCL injury** — abnormal **supination of ulna** on humerus → radial head subluxes **posteriorly** → **PLRI** (external rotatory subluxation of ulna + valgus on trochlea)

**LCL disruption** — **uniform** in dislocations + fracture-dislocations; commonest pattern = **avulsion from distal humerus** (McKee)

**Radiocapitellar joint** — important valgus constraint; radial head = **~30% of valgus stability**, most at **0–30° flexion / pronation**

**Capsule** — greatest stability contribution **in extension** · anterior capsule resists varus-valgus **in extension, not flexion** (Morrey)

### Nerves of the elbow

| Nerve | Origin | At the elbow |
|---|---|---|
| **Musculocutaneous** | Lateral cord | Between **biceps + brachialis** (supplies both); exits laterally distal to biceps tendon → **LABC deep to cephalic vein** |
| **Radial** | Posterior cord | **Triangular interval** (teres major, long head triceps, humerus) → spiral groove **13 cm** above trochlea → pierces **lateral IM septum 7.5 cm** above trochlea (middle/distal third) → between **brachialis + brachioradialis** → superficial to capsule at radiocapitellar joint |
| **Median** | Medial + lateral cords | With brachial artery (lateral → medial); superficial to brachialis; **no branches in arm** |
| **Ulnar** | Medial cord | Medial to brachial artery → pierces medial IM septum (**arcade of Struthers**) → cubital tunnel; **no branches in arm**; first FCU branch **distal to elbow** |

### Blood supply

**Brachial artery** — medial in arm → enters cubital fossa → splits into **radial + ulnar** arteries

**Cubital fossa** — contents lateral → medial: **biceps tendon, brachial artery, median nerve** · borders: **brachioradialis** (lateral), **pronator teres** (medial), distal humerus (proximal)

**Branches** — superior/inferior ulnar collateral · nutrient/muscular · supratrochlear

## 2 Mechanism

### Axis of forearm rotation

**Flexion–extension axis** — centre of trochlea; line through isometric points of capitellum + trochlea

**Pronation–supination axis** — **centre of radial head → fovea of ulnar head** (capitellum → radial head → distal ulna); forms a **cone**

**✗ Axis distractors** — radial head–radial styloid · radial head–ulnar styloid · coronoid–sigmoid notch · coronoid–radial styloid

**Forearm rotation** — radiocapitellar + PRUJ + DRUJ + IOM + forearm muscles · **pronators**: pronator teres + quadratus · **supinators**: biceps + supinator

### Biomechanics calculation – moment arms & anchor strength

**Joint reaction force** — large; **short, inefficient lever arms** (biceps inserts near COR) → degenerative change · static loads ≈ body weight · **dynamic loads > body weight**

**Free body** — ΣM = 0 → **B × 5 cm = W × 15 cm → B = 3W**

![Free body diagram – biceps 5 cm, weight 15 cm](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3078-elbow-moved.jpg)

**Anchor problem** — arm 20 N at 15 cm, biceps 5 cm → biceps force **60 N** × safety factor 3 = **180 N** minimum pullout

## 5 Management

### Posterior bundle of MCL – release for flexion

**Release for flexion loss** — **posterior (oblique) band of MCL + posterior capsule**; flexion 89° → **127°** (Wada; medial incision, ulnar nerve freed)

**Limited extension** — **anterior capsulectomy**

**✗ Release distractors** — **anterior band MCL** (→ valgus instability) · transverse band (no ROM gain) · **annular ligament** (never) · oblique cord of IOM

### Arthrodesis position

| Situation | Position |
|---|---|
| **Unilateral** | **90° flexion**, **0–7° valgus** |
| **Bilateral** | One at **110°** (feeding) · other at **65°** (perineal hygiene) |

### Diagnostic injection

**Soft spot** — triangle of **lateral epicondyle + olecranon + radial head** → intra-articular injection

### Complex instability (OB)

**Simple dislocation** — closed reduction + short immobilisation; recurrent instability **uncommon**

**Complex / persistent instability** — fix or replace bony elements + ligament repair ± **hinged fixation**

## X vs Y

| | **MCL (anterior bundle)** | **LCL complex (LUCL)** |
|---|---|---|
| Resists | **Valgus** + distraction | **Varus + external rotation** |
| Origin | **Anteroinferior medial epicondyle** | **Posterior lateral epicondyle** / isometric point capitellum |
| Insertion | **Sublime tubercle** | **Crista supinatoris** |
| Failure | Valgus instability | **PLRI** |
| Backup | **Radial head** (2nd valgus restraint) | — |

| | **Posterior bundle MCL** | **Anterior bundle MCL** |
|---|---|---|
| Valgus role | Primary only in **max flexion** | **Most important overall** |
| Release for stiffness? | **Yes — restores flexion** | **No —** valgus instability |
| Cubital tunnel | **Floor** | — |
