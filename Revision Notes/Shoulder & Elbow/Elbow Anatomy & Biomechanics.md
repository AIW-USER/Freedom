# Elbow Anatomy & Biomechanics

*Shoulder & Elbow · 12 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Joints & function

**3 articulations** — **ulnohumeral** (hinge) · **radiocapitellar / radiohumeral** (pivot) · **proximal radioulnar**

**Function** — lever arm positioning the hand · fulcrum for forearm lever

### Range of motion & load sharing

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Total flexion–extension | **0–150°** |
| **Functional flexion–extension** | **30–130°** |
| **Functional pronation/supination** | **50° each** |
| Carrying angle (valgus) | **♂ 5–10° · ♀ 10–15°** (anatomy note) · ~11° ♂ / ~13° ♀ (exam note) · 6–11° (UCL note); ↓ with flexion |
| Axial load, extended elbow | **40% ulnohumeral · 60% radiohumeral** |

### Osteology

**Spiral groove** — posterior humeral shaft; **radial nerve**; **~13 cm** proximal to trochlear articular surface

**Distal flare** — medial + lateral epicondyles; flare = half of elbow joint · **medial + lateral columns**

**Trochlea** — spool-shaped, **medial** · **capitellum** — lateral

**Sublime tubercle** — medial **coronoid** process; distal attachment of **anterior bundle of MCL**

### Arthrology

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Articular surface **anterior tilt** | **~30°** relative to humeral shaft |
| Valgus | **6°** |
| Rotation | **5° internal** |
| **Axis of flexion** | Centre of **trochlea + capitellum**; passes through **anteroinferior medial epicondyle** |
| **Radial head cartilage** | Covers **~240°**; **lateral 120° bare** (safe zone for radial head fixation) |
| **Capsule** | **Max distension 70–80° flexion**; anterior capsule inserts **6 mm distal to coronoid tip** → **coronoid intra-articular** |
| Coronoid fossa | Receives coronoid tip in deep flexion |
| **Coronoid tip** | **Buttress against posterior dislocation** |

### Muscles of the elbow

**Flexors** — **biceps** (radial tuberosity) · **brachialis** (inserts **11 mm distal to coronoid tip**) · brachioradialis

**Extensor** — triceps

### Collateral ligaments – MCL anterior bundle & LCL complex

| Stabiliser tier | Structures |
|---|---|
| **Primary static** (3) | **Ulnohumeral joint (coronoid)** · **MCL (anterior bundle)** · **LCL complex** |
| **Secondary static** | **Radiocapitellar joint** · **capsule** · **flexor + extensor origins** |
| **Dynamic** | Muscles crossing elbow: **anconeus, brachialis, triceps, biceps** → compression |

**Coronoid** — loss of **≥50% height** → instability

**MCL** — anterior, posterior, transverse bundles; resists **valgus + distraction**; origin **anteroinferior medial epicondyle** → insertion **sublime tubercle**

| MCL bundle | Role |
|---|---|
| **Anterior** | **Most important valgus restraint** (prime medial stabiliser); esp. in flexion (Morrey) |
| **Posterior** (posterior oblique) | **Floor of cubital tunnel** · **primary valgus restraint in maximal flexion** · secondary medial stabiliser · tension ↑ **60–120° flexion** → contracture **limits flexion** |
| **Transverse** | **No role** in valgus stability or ROM |

**2nd most important valgus restraint** — **radial head**\
✗ Wrong options: ulnotrochlear articulation

![MCL – A anterior bundle, B posterior oblique band, C transverse, D annular, E oblique cord](../../MCQ/attachments/q5863-a-medial.jpg)

**LCL complex** — **RCL** · **LUCL** · **accessory collateral ligament** · **annular ligament**; whole complex = **capsular thickening**; arises from **isometric point on lateral capitellum**

| LCL component | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **RCL** | **Originates at axis of rotation for flexion** |
| **LUCL** | **Primary restraint to varus + external rotation** through full arc; origin **posterior lateral epicondyle** → insertion **crista supinatoris** (✗ Wrong options: sublime tubercle) |
| Accessory collateral | ± substantial lateral stability (with RCL) |
| **Annular** | **PRUJ** stability |

**LCL repair** — optimal stability with **appropriately tensioned** repair

![LCL complex – A RCL, B annular, C LUCL](../../MCQ/attachments/q6194-a-21.jpg)

**Not LCL complex** — ulnohumeral joint (primary but not LCL) · radiocapitellar joint (secondary) · anterior bundle UCL · **transverse ligament (MCL complex)**

**LUCL injury** — abnormal **supination of ulna** on humerus → radial head subluxes **posteriorly** → **PLRI** (external rotatory subluxation of ulna + valgus on trochlea)

**LCL disruption** — **uniform** in dislocations + fracture-dislocations; commonest pattern = **avulsion from distal humerus** (McKee)

**Radiocapitellar joint** — important valgus constraint; radial head = **~30% of valgus stability**, most at **0–30° flexion / pronation**

**Capsule** — greatest stability contribution **in extension** · anterior capsule resists varus-valgus **in extension, not flexion** (Morrey)

| Stability (TO) | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| **Valgus** | **MCL anterior bundle** | Radial head, flexor-pronator mass |
| **Varus** | **Coronoid process** (TO) | LUCL, extensor mass |
| **Posterolateral rotatory (PLRI)** | **LUCL** | Radial head, MCL |
| Posteromedial rotatory (PMRI) | Coronoid (anteromedial facet) | MCL (TO table) |

**Dynamic (TO)** — **flexor-pronator mass resists valgus** · **extensor-supinator mass resists varus**

**Coronoid (TO)** — anterior buttress against posterior dislocation · fracture **>50% height → instability** · fractured in **10–15%** of dislocations

**Radial head safe zone (TO)** — **"90° arc between radial styloid and Lister's tubercle" (Caputo)** · "posterolateral 80° non-articular; 280° articular" (other TO) · lateral **120°** bare (above) — read the options

**Horii cycle (TO)** — FOOSH valgus + axial + supination: **LUCL → anterior/posterior capsule → MCL** · **reverse Horii**: varus + pronation → **PMRI**, coronoid anteromedial facet + LCL

### Nerves of the elbow

| Nerve | Origin | At the elbow |
|---|---|---|
| **Musculocutaneous** | Lateral cord | Between **biceps + brachialis** (supplies both); exits laterally distal to biceps tendon → **LABC deep to cephalic vein** |
| **Radial** | Posterior cord | **Triangular interval** (teres major, long head triceps, humerus) → spiral groove **13 cm** above trochlea → pierces **lateral IM septum 7.5 cm** above trochlea (middle/distal third) → between **brachialis + brachioradialis** → superficial to capsule at radiocapitellar joint |
| **Median** | Medial + lateral cords | With brachial artery (lateral → medial); superficial to brachialis; **no branches in arm** |
| **Ulnar** | Medial cord | Medial to brachial artery → pierces medial IM septum (**arcade of Struthers**) → cubital tunnel; **no branches in arm**; first FCU branch **distal to elbow** |

### Blood supply

**Brachial artery** — medial in arm → enters cubital fossa → splits into **radial + ulnar** arteries

**Cubital fossa** — contents lateral → medial: **biceps tendon, brachial artery, median nerve** · borders: **brachioradialis** (lateral), **pronator teres** (medial), distal humerus (proximal)

**Branches** — superior/inferior ulnar collateral · nutrient/muscular · supratrochlear

### Elbow imaging – X-ray & MRI (TO)

**Condyles (TO)** — **lateral condyle (larger)** carries **capitellum + lateral epicondyle** · medial = medial epicondyle + trochlea → "medial condyle fracture" is really a **medial epicondyle or trochlear** injury

**Fossae (TO)** — **coronoid fossa (anterior)** receives coronoid in flexion · **olecranon fossa (posterior)** receives olecranon in extension

**CRITOE (TO)** — capitellum **1** · radial head **3** · internal (medial) epicondyle **5** · trochlea **7** · olecranon **9** · external epicondyle **11** (or 2-4-6-8-10-12) — **order matters** · **trochlea visible but medial epicondyle not in place → avulsion with entrapment**

| X-ray sign (TO) | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Anterior "sail" / visible posterior fat pad** | **Haemarthrosis** |
| **Fat–fluid level** | **Lipohaemarthrosis → intra-articular fracture** |
| **Anterior humeral line** | Normally through **middle third of capitellum**; posterior displacement → anterior part / misses |
| Small lateral flake (child) | May hide **lateral condyle fracture** into cartilage → **MRI** |
| Wider physis vs other side | **Epicondyle avulsion** (adolescent athlete) |

**Occult radial head/neck fracture (TO)** — after any elbow trauma: **AP + lateral + external oblique** · **Coyle view** = lateral with beam **45°** → radial head free of ulna · negative X-ray + suspicion (esp. children) → **screening MRI** (also coronoid tip fracture)

**CT (TO)** — little role except **deformity**

**MRI technique (TO)** — **prone "superman" position**, elbow in magnet centre (large FOV with chest → respiratory artefact) · **distal biceps protocol = 2 scans** (shoulder–arm supine, arm–elbow prone) · coronal T1 + PD FS, optional **GRE** (trabeculae, faint calcification), sagittal PD FS, axial PD ± FS

| MRI structure (TO) | Normal / pitfall |
|---|---|
| **UCL** | Anterior band (key) · posterior band = **cubital tunnel floor** · transverse rarely seen · **grey fan-shaped humeral end = normal**; dark cord at sublime tubercle |
| RCL | Cord-like; small radial sulcus normal |
| LUCL | Lateral epicondyle → behind radius → ulna; **often hard to see even when intact** |
| Radiocapitellar plica | Normal synovial fold |
| **Capitellar pseudodefect** | **Normal posterior** capitellar groove — **not OCD** |
| Ulnar "bare area" | Normal cartilage gap in trochlear notch |
| Radial head fovea | Central cartilage thinning can be normal |
| Annular ligament | From ulnar tubercle around radial head |

![Sagittal PD FS: radiocapitellar joint, normal capitellar pseudodefect and plica](../../TO notes/attachments/TOn-notes-elbow-imaging-x-ray-and-mri-apr-2026-p04-f010.jpg)

**Distal biceps on MRI (TO)** — short + long heads stay **separate to the radial tuberosity** · lacertus runs medially · don't confuse **brachial artery (flow void)**, veins or median nerve with the tendon · rupture = fluid in biceps–brachialis plane, no insertion → **measure retraction**

| MRI case (TO) | Finding |
|---|---|
| Medial elbow pain | Oedematous common flexor origin + faint calcification on GRE = **calcific tendinosis** |
| Pain on supination | Thick distal biceps + fluid = **radiobicipital bursitis / tendinosis** |
| Lateral elbow pain | **Empty common extensor origin**, retracted extensors, ligaments intact |
| Young athlete | **Capitellar OCD = ANTERIOR** cartilage/subchondral irregularity (pseudodefect = posterior) |

## 2 Mechanism

### Axis of forearm rotation

**Flexion–extension axis** — centre of trochlea; line through isometric points of capitellum + trochlea

**Pronation–supination axis** — **centre of radial head → fovea of ulnar head** (capitellum → radial head → distal ulna); forms a **cone**

**✗ Axis distractors** — radial head–radial styloid · radial head–ulnar styloid · coronoid–sigmoid notch · coronoid–radial styloid

**Forearm rotation** — radiocapitellar + PRUJ + DRUJ + IOM + forearm muscles · **pronators**: pronator teres + quadratus · **supinators**: biceps + supinator

### Biomechanics calculation – moment arms & anchor strength

**Joint reaction force** — large; **short, inefficient lever arms** (biceps inserts near COR) → degenerative change · static loads ≈ body weight · **dynamic loads > body weight**

**Free body** — ΣM = 0 → **B × 5 cm = W × 15 cm → B = 3W**

![Free body diagram – biceps 5 cm, weight 15 cm](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3078-elbow-moved.jpg)

**Anchor problem** — arm 20 N at 15 cm, biceps 5 cm → biceps force **60 N** × safety factor 3 = **180 N** minimum pullout

## 5 Management

### Surgical approaches to the elbow (TO)

| Group | Approach (TO) | Plane / key step |
|---|---|---|
| Posterior | **Campbell triceps split** | 15 cm midline incision; triceps split to bone; lateral half with anconeus, medial half with flexor fascia |
| Posterior | **Campbell aponeurosis tongue** | Tongue of triceps aponeurosis reflected proximally → **V-Y lengthening** for **extension contracture** |
| Posterior | **Wadsworth** (extensile posterolateral) | Triceps tongue + **anconeus elevated from lateral epicondyle** → radial head + posterior humerus |
| Posterior | **Olecranon osteotomy (MacAusland, Müller)** | **~2 cm from tip** · best articular exposure · fixed TBW (K-wire TBW commonest), TBW over screw, plate |
| Posterior | **Bryan–Morrey** | Triceps + forearm fascia + periosteum + anconeus reflected **medial → lateral** as one sleeve → **only posterior approach keeping extensor mechanism in continuity** · triceps must be repaired |
| Lateral | **Kaplan** | **ECRB ↔ EDC** · close to PIN → forearm pronated · mainly PIN release |
| Lateral | **Column** (extensile proximal lateral) | **BR + ECRL elevated off lateral supracondylar ridge** · extensile |
| Lateral | **Kocher** | **Anconeus ↔ ECU** · **most common lateral approach** · **cannot extend distally (PIN)**; annular ligament = distal limit |
| Medial | **Molesworth / Campbell** | **Medial epicondylectomy**, flexor origin reflected with fragment; ulnar nerve isolated |
| Medial | **Hotchkiss "over the top"** | **Split flexor-pronator mass between PT + FCU**, brachialis reflected → **coronoid** · protect **MABCN** |
| Anterior | **Henry** | **Mobile wad ↔ biceps tendon**; supinator elevated from radius |

![Cross-section map of approaches around the elbow](../../TO notes/attachments/TOn-notes-approaches-around-elbow-chronic-elbow-and-forearm-inst-p02-f004.jpg)

| Olecranon osteotomy (TO) | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Transverse (MacAusland)** | **Most commonly performed** |
| Müller | Only triceps-insertion part cut; residual ulna blocks view → rarely used |
| **Chevron** | V-cut → **rotational control + larger healing area** |

**Lateral boundaries (TO)** — every lateral approach lies between **radial nerve (anterior)** and **LUCL (posterior)** · **pronate forearm** → PIN moves anterior, away from dissection (Kaplan + Kocher)

**Radial nerve course (TO)** — pierces lateral IM septum at **middle/distal third junction** → brachialis–ECRL → brachialis–BR → winds around proximal radius **between two heads of supinator** (PIN)

**Medial approach indications (TO)** — **coronoid fixation when intact radial head blocks lateral view** · medial osteophyte excision

**Triceps-split/reflecting approaches (TO)** — poor view of intra-articular distal humerus

### Posterior bundle of MCL – release for flexion

**Release for flexion loss** — **posterior (oblique) band of MCL + posterior capsule**; flexion 89° → **127°** (Wada; medial incision, ulnar nerve freed)

**Limited extension** — **anterior capsulectomy**

**✗ Release distractors** — **anterior band MCL** (→ valgus instability) · transverse band (no ROM gain) · **annular ligament** (never) · oblique cord of IOM

### Arthrodesis position

| Situation | Position |
|---|---|
| **Unilateral** | **90° flexion**, **0–7° valgus** |
| **Bilateral** | One at **110°** (feeding) · other at **65°** (perineal hygiene) |

### Diagnostic injection

**Soft spot** — triangle of **lateral epicondyle + olecranon + radial head** → intra-articular injection

### Complex instability (OB)

**Simple dislocation** — closed reduction + short immobilisation; recurrent instability **uncommon**

**Complex / persistent instability** — fix or replace bony elements + ligament repair ± **hinged fixation**

## How it's asked

**"Primary stabiliser against valgus (labelled figure)?"** → **anterior bundle MCL**\
✗ Wrong options: posterior bundle = secondary · transverse · annular · biceps

**"Structure A on lateral ligament figure?"** → **radial collateral ligament** (B annular, C LUCL; transverse = MCL complex)

**"Sublime tubercle is the insertion of?"** → **anterior bundle MCL**

**"True statement about elbow stability?"** → **RCL originates at axis of flexion**\
✗ Wrong options: LUCL on sublime tubercle · anterior bundle primary in max flexion · ulnotrochlear 2nd valgus restraint

**"Varus + external rotation instability — which primary stabilisers incompetent?"** → **LUCL, accessory collateral, annular, RCL**\
✗ Wrong options: ulnohumeral · radiocapitellar · transverse ligament

**"Release to restore flexion?"** → **posterior oblique band MCL (B)**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior band · annular

**"Axis of forearm rotation?"** → **radial head → ulnar head**

**"Anchor pullout, 20 N at 15 cm, biceps 5 cm, safety 3?"** → **180N**

**"Posterior elbow blow, olecranon spur, painless effusion?"** → **student's elbow** (olecranon bursitis; key not provided, TO)

**"Optimal unilateral arthrodesis?"** → **90° flexion**

**"Only posterior approach preserving extensor mechanism continuity?"** → **Bryan–Morrey** (TO)

**"Kocher lateral approach interval?"** → **anconeus and ECU** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: ECRB–EDC = Kaplan · mobile wad–biceps = Henry

**"Safe zone for radial head fixation?"** → **lateral 120° non-articular**

**"Primary varus stabiliser of the elbow?"** → **coronoid process** (TO)

**"Posterior capitellar groove on sagittal MRI?"** → **normal pseudodefect, not OCD** (OCD is anterior) (TO)

**"View for occult radial head fracture?"** → **external oblique / Coyle (45°) view** (TO)
