# Little League Elbow

*Shoulder & Elbow · 23 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Medial epicondyle apophysitis – pathogenesis & diagnosis

**Definition** — spectrum of **medial elbow** injuries in **adolescent pitchers**: **medial epicondyle stress fracture / apophysitis** · **UCL injury** · **flexor-pronator mass strain**

**Most common cause of medial elbow pain in adolescent pitcher** — **Little League elbow (medial apophysitis)** (TO stem; answer not given by TO source — OB: apophysitis far commoner than other throwing elbow conditions)\
✗ Wrong options: golfer's elbow · olecranon bursitis · capitellar OCD

**Age rule** — **younger / open physis → apophysitis or avulsion** > UCL sprain; medial epicondyle apophysis **fuses at 16–18 y**

**Pathogenesis** — **repetitive valgus loading** → **tension overload** of medial structures; **repetitive flexor-pronator contraction stresses chondro-osseous origin → apophysitis**

| ✗ Pathogenesis distractor | Condition |
|---|---|
| Acute fragmentation of **entire capitellar ossific nucleus** | **Panner disease** |
| Microtraumatic **vascular insufficiency of capitellum** (compression + rotation) | **Capitellar OCD** |
| Rupture anterior band of anterior bundle UCL | UCL tear |
| Repetitive triceps contraction | Olecranon (posterior) |

### MCL (UCL) anatomy & biomechanics (TO)

**MUCL components (TO)** — **anterior oblique ligament (AOL / anterior bundle)** = strongest, **primary valgus restraint** · **posterior oblique / posterior bundle** · **transverse** ligament

**Anterior bundle (TO)** — **medial epicondyle → sublime tubercle**; primary valgus stabiliser **30–120°** (when throwing valgus is highest)

**Bands of AOL (TO)** — **reciprocal**: **anterior band tight in extension**, **posterior band tight in flexion**

| Band (TO) | Primary range |
|---|---|
| **Anterior band** | Up to **90°** (one TO) · **20–120°** (another TO) — read the options |
| **Posterior band** | **>90°**, taut **90–120°** |
| **POL** | **>120°** |

**Other valgus stabilisers (TO)** — **radial head** + **flexor-pronator mass** (dynamic)

**Degeneration in pitcher's elbow (TO)** — **posterior band of anterior bundle** (max force in **acceleration at 90–100°** flexion)\
✗ Wrong options: anterior band · posterior bundle · anteromedial olecranon

**Posteromedial olecranon (TO)** — repetitive microtrauma → **osteophytes at posteromedial** olecranon\
✗ Wrong options: anteromedial

**LCL complex (TO)** — LCL, annular, LUCL

## 3 Presentation

**Triad** — **medial elbow pain** in throwing arm · **↓ throwing speed (velocity), accuracy, distance**

**Exam** — tenderness **medial epicondyle** (inferior aspect) · **pain with valgus stress** · **valgus instability = more severe** (check through flexion → extension)

### Valgus tests for MUCL (TO)

| Test (TO) | Technique | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| **Valgus stress test** | Elbow **20–30° flexion** (**unlocks olecranon**) + **forearm supinated**, humerus ER, valgus | Sens **66%**, spec **60%** |
| **Milking manoeuvre** | Pull **thumb** → valgus; **forearm supinated**, **elbow 90°** | |
| **Moving valgus stress (O'Driscoll)** | Upright, shoulder **90° abduction + max ER**, elbow max flexion → extend to 30° under constant valgus; **pain 70–120°** (late cocking → early acceleration) | Sens **100%**, spec **75%** |

**AOL integrity test (TO)** — **valgus stress at 20° flexion**\
✗ Wrong options: full extension · varus · milking in pronation

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray AP + lateral** — often normal · **physeal widening** of medial epicondyle · **fragmentation / avulsion**; **compare contralateral** elbow

![Medial epicondyle physeal widening and fragmentation](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3086-me.jpg)

**MRI** — more sensitive; **oedema of medial epicondyle apophysis**; differentiates **bony/physeal vs ligament** injury; confirms **UCL insufficiency**

![MRI: oedema of medial epicondyle apophysis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3086-mrime.jpg)

**First step** — **rule out MCL injury**: **medial widening on stress X-ray / dynamic US**, or valgus instability on exam → **no widening + tender + physeal widening/oedema = apophysitis**

| ✗ Diagnosis distractor | Clue |
|---|---|
| Displaced medial epicondyle avulsion | Displaced fragment → ORIF |
| MUCL tear | Medial widening on stress / instability |
| **Valgus extension overload** | **Olecranon (posteromedial) osteophytes** |
| Ulnar neuritis | Typical throwing condition but much less common than apophysitis |

## 5 Management

```
Adolescent thrower, medial elbow pain
├─ Apophysitis / stress fracture / strain → rest + activity mod + PT
│      (↓ innings, minimal immobilisation, coach + parent education)
├─ Displaced medial epicondyle avulsion → ORIF (cannulated screws)
└─ UCL disruption + insufficiency → UCL reconstruction (palmaris)
```

### Treatment – rest & activity modification

**Mainstay** — **rest, activity modification, ↓ innings pitched per week, PT** (also for acute medial pain + valgus pain after a pitch)

**PT aims** — strengthen **dynamic stabilisers** (flexor-pronator), shoulder + elbow ROM, **core**; gradual return with proper mechanics + safe pitching rules

**Immobilisation** — **minimal** (maintain ROM); no long-arm cast 6–8 wk → **stiffness**

**Avoid fragment excision** — preserve medial epicondyle (**ligament + soft-tissue attachments**, stability)

**No UCL reconstruction** in Little League elbow — ligament **competent**

**✗ Wrong option: arthroscopy / microfracture** — for **capitellar osteochondral lesions**, not medial apophysitis

### Capitellar OCD in young thrower

**Lateral elbow tenderness, nondisplaced capitellar OCD** — **cessation of throwing** (heals if activity curtailed)

**Cast** — only for **noncompliant**; **≤6 weeks**

**Surgery** — **loose bodies or cartilage flaps**\
✗ Wrong options: excision/drilling for intact lesion · steroid injection

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **ORIF medial epicondyle** | **Displaced medial epicondyle avulsion fracture** |
| **UCL reconstruction (palmaris longus autograft)** | **UCL disruption + insufficiency** |

### MCL (UCL) injury – reconstruction (TO)

| UCL reconstruction indications (TO) |
|---|
| **Acute complete rupture** in **competitive thrower** wishing to remain active |
| **High-grade distal tear** |
| **Chronic pain/instability** after **≥4–6 wk** supervised nonop |

**Midsubstance tear + degeneration, wants to play (TO)** — **MCL reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: repair · brace/physio · arthroscopy

**Nonop (TO)** — partial tears; **"active" rest 2–3 months** (no throwing, no valgus stress) + **flexor-pronator strengthening**; also for athletes not returning to sport and asymptomatic in daily life

**Paediatric UCL reconstruction** — similar to adult; **palmaris longus autograft**

## 6 Complications

**List** — **ulnar nerve neuropathy** · continued pain + instability · loss of motion · **inability to return to same level**

## X vs Y

| | **Little League elbow (medial)** | **Capitellar OCD (lateral)** |
|---|---|---|
| Force | **Tension** (valgus, flexor-pronator pull) | **Compression + rotation** (radiocapitellar) |
| Pathology | Apophysitis / avulsion / stress fracture | **Microtraumatic vascular insufficiency** of capitellum |
| Tenderness | **Medial epicondyle** | **Lateral** elbow |
| Treatment | Rest + activity mod + PT | **Stop throwing**; surgery for loose body / cartilage flap |
