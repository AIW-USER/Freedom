# SLAP Lesion

*Shoulder & Elbow · 51 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — uncommon: **<5%** of shoulder injuries · yet a common cause of pain + weakness in throwers

**Who** — **dominant shoulder of overhead/throwing athletes** · older patients after traumatic falls

**Risk factor** — **GIRD**

**Associations** — internal impingement · GIRD · **articular-sided** cuff tears · (subtle) instability · scapular dyskinesis · paralabral cyst · sublabral clefts, Buford complex

### Anatomy – biceps anchor, Buford complex & vascularity

**LHB attachment** — **~50% to supraglenoid tubercle, ~50% to labrum** (predominantly posterior labrum) · tubercle **>6 mm medial** to glenoid surface

**Posterior / posterior-dominant attachment** — **~70%** of shoulders (✗ Wrong options: 15% · 30% · 100%) · most common: **posterior to 12 o'clock** (✗ Wrong options: "anchored anteriorly")

| Tuoheti type | Biceps attachment | Share |
|---|---|---|
| I | Completely posterior | I + II **>70%** |
| II | Predominantly posterior | |
| III | Anterior + posterior | 25% |
| IV | Anterior | 5% |

**Biceps function** — torsional + horizontal stability in **late cocking** · **poorest blood supply** in intra-articular course

**Labrum** — fibrocartilage; **chock block** to subluxation · superior labrum attaches more **medially** + anchors biceps = **weak link**

**Labral blood supply** — **suprascapular, circumflex scapular, posterior humeral circumflex** arteries via **capsule + periosteum** · **anterosuperior labrum = poorest** → degenerative tears, poor healing\
✗ Wrong options: bone · single source

| Variant | Feature | Pitfall |
|---|---|---|
| **Sublabral recess / foramen** | ± thickened MGHL | Mistaken for tear on MRI |
| **Buford complex** | **Cord-like MGHL + absent anterosuperior labrum**; **normal variant**, **1.5%** | **Do not repair → significant loss of ER** + pain |
| **Meniscoid labrum** | **1%** | — |
| Superior MGHL attachment | On glenoid | — |

![Buford complex — arthroscopic view](../../MCQ/attachments/q101-illa-buford-comples-scope-view.jpg)

**Arthroscopy tell** — erythema + tearing **under** labrum = tear (vs normal recess)

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanisms** — **repetitive overhead activity** · FOOSH with **tensed biceps** · traction on arm

**Thrower pathoanatomy** — tight **posterior IGHL** → GH contact shifts **posterosuperiorly** → ↑ shear on superior labrum → **"peel-back"**

**Consequence** — SLAP **↑ strain on anterior band IGHL** → ↓ stability

## 3 Presentation

**History** — "pop" during overhead activity or trauma · **lag** between injury + symptoms

**Symptoms** — **vague deep shoulder pain** · popping/clicking · weakness, fatigue, ↓ performance

**Exam** — bicipital groove tenderness · check **IR + total arc** vs contralateral · SS ± IS **atrophy** (suprascapular neuropathy from cyst) · apprehension positive in **85%**

**Tests** — **no single specific test**

| Test group | Tests |
|---|---|
| Biceps provocation | **Speed's**, **Yergason's**, Kim biceps load |
| SLAP | **O'Brien (active compression)**, **crank** (full abduction, load + rotate), **dynamic labral shear**, Kibler anterior slide |

### Related topics (distal clavicle excision, biceps dislocation)

**Arthroscopic vs open DCE** — advantage = **evaluate GH joint** · failed open DCE: **15/20 had SLAP** (9/15 good–excellent after arthroscopic treatment)\
✗ Wrong options: fewer complications · infection · time

**Arthroscopic DCE** — sacrifices **inferior** AC ligament, **preserves superior** AC ligament

**Medial LHB dislocation** — **subscapularis tear**\
✗ Wrong options: supraspinatus (LHB rupture) · transverse ligament · shallow groove · type I SLAP; type II SLAP can cause instability

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnosis – MR arthrogram

**X-ray** — usually normal

**Study of choice** — **MR arthrogram** · **gold standard = arthroscopy**\
✗ Wrong options: US · EMG · labs · PT first when suspicious

**MRI sign** — T2 signal between superior labrum + glenoid, **lateral to glenoid rim, posterior to biceps**

**Accuracy** — sens **~50%**, spec **~90%**, ↑ with arthrogram (Orthobullets) · linear high signal on oblique coronal T2: sens **41%**, spec **86%**, accuracy 63% (OB) — read the options

**Paralabral ganglion cyst** — **highly specific for labral tear**; usually **spinoglenoid notch**\
✗ Wrong options: suprascapular notch

**Overdiagnosis** — concern (↑ SLAP repairs in ABOS case logs)

**Arthroscopic peel-back test** — labrum peels back at **90° abduction + ER**

### SLAP classification

| Type | Orthobullets description (share) | Clock face (TO) | TO description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | Labral + biceps **fraying, anchor intact** (11%) | 11–1 | Fraying superior labrum |
| **II** | Fraying + **detached biceps anchor** (**41%**, most common) | 11–1 | Detachment of biceps anchor |
| **III** | **Bucket handle, intact anchor** (33%); biceps separates from fragment | 11–1 | **Bucket handle, intact biceps** |
| **IV** | **Bucket handle + detached anchor** (15%); biceps stays with fragment | 11–1 | Bucket handle with **biceps extension** |
| V | II + **anteroinferior extension (Bankart)** | **11–5** | Bankart + SLAP |
| VI | II + **unstable flap** | 11–1 | Flap tear of bucket-handle SLAP |
| VII | II + **MGHL injury** | **11–3** | Extension into MGHL |
| VIII | II + **posterior extension** | **11–7** | Posterior extension |
| IX | **Circumferential** | **11–11** | Pan-labral |
| X | II + posteroinferior extension (**reverse Bankart**) (Orthobullets) | **11–1+** | SLAP + **noncontiguous** posterior labral tear (TO) |

![SLAP types I–IV](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3053-classification-illustration-moved.jpg)

**Systems** — **Snyder** = I–IV · **Maffet** added V–VII · high inter/intra-observer variability

**Type X wording** — "posteroinferior extension / reverse Bankart" (Orthobullets) · "noncontiguous posterior labral tear" (TO) — read the options

### Classification & variants (type III, Andrews lesion) (TO)

**Dynamic labral shear + tear 11–1 o'clock, intact biceps (TO)** — **type III** SLAP\
✗ Wrong options: type II = biceps anchor detached · Bankart

**Andrews lesion (TO)** — **anterior + superior labral detachment** **without extension posterior to biceps** footplate · **throwers** \
✗ Wrong options: SLAP II · posterosuperior · anterior + posterior · posterior IGHL avulsion

## 5 Management

```
SLAP lesion
├─ First line → rest + PT + NSAIDs (scapula, cuff; treat GIRD)
├─ Failed nonop, type I / III / IV <1/3 biceps → arthroscopic debridement
├─ Failed nonop, type II, young overhead athlete → SLAP repair
│      └─ >40 y (failure >36 y) → debridement + biceps tenodesis/tenotomy
└─ Type IV >1/3 biceps → biceps tenodesis/tenotomy ± labral repair/debridement
```

### Treatment – nonoperative vs repair vs tenodesis (age, type IV) & return to sport

**Initial ✓** — **rest + rehab for scapular mechanics + cuff strengthening** (+ NSAIDs; address GIRD)\
✗ Wrong options: aggressive **concentric biceps** strengthening (aggravates) · surgery first

**Labral tear in thrower** — initial: **posterior capsular stretching + cuff strengthening**

### Operative criteria

| Situation (failed nonop, severe symptoms) | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **Type I, III, IV <1/3 biceps** | **Arthroscopic debridement** (III → debridement ± repair of fragment) |
| **Type II, young overhead athlete** | **Labral repair** (most amenable to repair) |
| **Type II, >40 y** · biceps fraying **>50%** | **Debridement + biceps tenotomy/tenodesis** (✗ Wrong options: repair (stiffness)) |
| **Type IV with >1/3 (>30%) biceps** | **Biceps tenodesis** ± labral repair/debridement |
| Type V (II + Bankart) · II + posterior extension | **SLAP + Bankart repair** (active patient) |
| Paralabral cyst + SLAP II | **Cyst decompression + SLAP repair** |

**Age thresholds** — tenodesis consensus **>40 y** (Orthobullets, OB) · repair failure **>36 y** (Orthobullets, OB) · stiffness risk **>35 y** (OB) — read the options

**Tenodesis in young** — viable even **<25 y** athletes (recent studies)

**Repair failure factor** — **age >36**\
✗ Wrong options: recreational swimming · male · sedentary job · type II

### Return to sport

| Group / treatment | Rate |
|---|---|
| Same level after repair (overall) | **60–70%** |
| Return to prior play after repair | **50–60%** |
| Non-pitchers returning to baseball after repair | **65–85%** |
| Nonop: return to play | **~40%** |
| Nonop: return to prior play | **20–30%** |

**Pitchers vs non-pitchers** — **non-pitchers return at higher rates** after repair · RTP similar for repair vs tenodesis · elite throwers: variable, unpredictable

### Repair technique

**Steps** — debride flaps · **decompress cysts** · accessory/percutaneous portals for anchors near rim · **sutures anterior to biceps → stiffness**

**Tenodesis** — all-arthroscopic or mini-open; tenotomise near labrum

### Postoperative rehabilitation after SLAP repair

| Period | Allowed | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Wk 0–4** | **Passive + active-assisted flexion in scapular plane**; passive **0–90° FE** | **Passive ER at 90° abduction** (~4 wk), extremes of abduction + ER, **resisted biceps** |
| Wk 4–6 | Active ROM, isometrics; **isotonic cuff** 4–6 wk | **Resisted elbow flexion until 6 wk** |
| Wk 6–12 (to 8) | Functional + light strengthening | — |
| Wk 8–16 / 12+ | **Sport-specific** strengthening | — |
| **~6 months** | Return to sport | — |

**Limit after repair** — **abduction + ER** — max tension on repair\
✗ Wrong options: IR

**✗ Rehab traps** — immediate full active ROM · **6 wk sling, no motion** (stiffness) · cuff strengthening by wk 2 · eccentric biceps at wk 2 · latissimus pull-downs

## 6 Complications

### Stiffness

**Most common** after SLAP repair (~78%, Orthobullets) · risk: incidental SLAP repaired in **>45 y** during cuff repair · **post-surgical adhesive capsulitis**

**Treatment** — early pendulum/AAROM from wk 1 → **capsular release** if fails >6 months PT

### Suprascapular nerve – anchor placement & paralabral cyst

**Mechanism** — deep drilling / anchors from **anterior or anterosuperior portal** · instrumentation **medial to glenoid** · overdrilling glenoid

**Distance** — SSN **1.5–2.0 cm** from glenoid cortex at scapular spine level

**Origin** — SSN from **superior (upper) trunk**\
✗ Wrong options: middle trunk (no branches) · C5–7 roots = long thoracic · posterior/medial cord

**✗ At risk distractors** — axillary nerve, thoracoacromial artery, thoracodorsal nerve, superior thoracic artery

![Spinoglenoid paralabral cyst](../../MCQ/attachments/q9037-illa-mr-spinoglenoid-cyst.jpg)

| Cyst location | Muscles affected |
|---|---|
| **Suprascapular notch** | **SS + IS** |
| **Spinoglenoid notch** | **IS only** |

**Cyst management** — no structural lesion → rehab · with labral tear → **decompression + labral repair**

### Failed repair

**Causes** — chondral injury, hardware failure, **anchor pullout** · age **>36** → tenodesis better

## X vs Y

| | **Buford complex** | **Sublabral foramen** | **SLAP II** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Labrum | **Absent** anterosuperior | Present; recess/foramen beneath | Detached at biceps anchor |
| MGHL | **Cord-like** | ± thickened | — |
| Key point | **Do not repair** (↓ ER) | MRI pitfall — not a tear | Repair / tenodesis |

| | **SLAP III** | **SLAP IV** | **Andrews (TO)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Labrum | Bucket handle | Bucket handle | Anterosuperior detachment |
| Biceps | **Intact** | **Torn into** | Not posterior to footplate |

| Distractor lesion | Key feature |
|---|---|
| **HAGL** | Humeral avulsion of anterior IGHL |
| **ALPSA** | Capsulolabral periosteal sleeve displaced **medially** on glenoid neck (axial) |
| **Bankart** | Anteroinferior labrum, after anterior dislocation |
| Hill-Sachs | Anterior instability; engaging defects → surgery |
| Reverse Hill-Sachs | Posterior instability; surgery if **>20%** of articular surface |

## How it's asked

**"MRI tear 11–1 o'clock, intact biceps, positive dynamic labral shear?"** → **SLAP type III** (TO)

**"Andrews lesion?"** → **anterior + superior labral tear** (TO)

**"Buford complex?"** → **normal variant: cord-like MGHL + absent anterosuperior labrum**

**"% posterior/posterior-dominant biceps attachment?"** → **70%**

**"True about the superior labrum (SLAP on MRI)?"** → **anterosuperior portion most susceptible to vascular insufficiency**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior anchor · single artery · suprascapular notch cyst · limit IR after repair

**"FOOSH, overhead athlete, MRI superior labral signal?"** → **SLAP tear**\
✗ Wrong options: HAGL · ALPSA · Bankart

**"Pitcher, O'Brien + Jobe relocation, total arc 180° — next?"** → **MR arthrogram**

**"47-year-old, type II SLAP, biceps fraying >50%, failed 8 months nonop?"** → **debridement + biceps tenotomy/tenodesis**\
✗ Wrong options: SLAP repair

**"Factor associated with SLAP repair failure?"** → **age >36**

**"Third-baseman, type II SLAP — counsel?"** → **better return to same level than pitchers**

**"38-year-old, O'Brien/Speed/Yergason +, initial treatment?"** → **rest + rehab for scapular mechanics + cuff**

**"Finding favouring tenodesis over repair?"** → **type IV with 40% biceps involvement**

**"Rehab after type II SLAP repair?"** → **limited passive 4 wk → active to 8 wk → sport-specific to 12–16 wk**

**"Avoid at 2 weeks after SLAP repair?"** → **passive ER at 90° abduction**

**"Immediate rehab after SLAP repair?"** → **passive + active-assisted flexion in scapular plane**

**"Anchor drilling from anterosuperior portal — structure at risk?"** → **suprascapular nerve**

**"SLAP + cyst on MRI — treatment?"** → **supraglenoid cyst decompression + SLAP repair**

**"Instrument passed medial to glenoid — nerve origin?"** → **superior trunk**

**"Arthroscopic DCE advantage?"** → **evaluation of GH joint**

**"Medial biceps dislocation cause?"** → **subscapularis tear**
