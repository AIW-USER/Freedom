# Shoulder Exam

*Shoulder & Elbow · 46 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Exam components

**Sequence** — inspection · palpation · ROM · neurovascular · provocative tests · always **compare both sides**

**Inspection** — skin, scars, symmetry, swelling, atrophy, hypertrophy, **scapular winging**

**Palpation** — C-spine spinous processes, SC joint, clavicle, **AC joint**, acromion, coracoid, scapular spine · paraspinals, periscapular, deltoid, **greater tuberosity (cuff insertion)**, **LHB in groove**

### Range of motion

| Plane | Normal |
|---|---|
| **Forward elevation** | **180°** |
| **Abduction (scapula stabilised)** | **90°** |
| **ER at side** | **80°** |
| ER at 90° abduction | Compare sides |
| **IR to vertebral level** | **T4–T8** |
| IR at 90° abduction | Compare sides |

**ROM rule** — active vs passive, both sides; normal relative to contralateral

### Neurovascular

| Root | Motion | Muscle | Reflex |
|---|---|---|---|
| C4 | Scapular stabilisation (winging) | Upper serratus anterior | — |
| **C5** | Shoulder abduction · elbow flexion palm up | Deltoid · biceps | **Biceps** |
| **C6** | Elbow flexion thumb up · wrist extension | Brachioradialis · ECRL | **Brachioradialis** |
| **C7** | Elbow extension · wrist flexion | Triceps · FCR | **Triceps** |
| **C8** | **Finger flexion, grip, thumb extension** | **FDS** | — |
| T1 | Finger abduction | Interossei (ulnar) | — |

**Sensation** — C4–T1 dermatomes · **pulses** brachial, radial, ulnar

### Anterior band of IGHL – restraint in abduction/external rotation

| Ligament | Restrains |
|---|---|
| **Anterior band IGHL** | **Anterior translation at 90° abduction + full ER** (moves anteriorly) |
| **MGHL** | Anterior translation at **45° abduction** |
| **SGHL + CHL** | **Inferior translation of adducted arm** + ER in adduction |
| **Posterior band IGHL** | **Posterior translation in IR** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Impingement & biceps tests (Hawkins, Speed)

**Impingement syndrome (TO)** — anterior pain in **60–120° abduction** (painful arc) · greater tuberosity slides under acromion, bursa smooths it · causes: **subacromial bursitis**, **hooked acromion**, cuff pathology (supraspinatus tendinitis, calcific tendinitis, partial tear), **GT fracture** (TO)

| Test | Technique | Positive / meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Neer sign** | Stabilise scapula, **passive forced forward elevation** | Anterior pain (GT under acromion **70–110°**) · needs **full ROM** · sens **75–86%**, spec **50%** · also + in stiffness, OA, instability, bone lesions |
| **Neer test** | Repeat Neer after **subacromial lidocaine** | **Marked pain relief** = subacromial impingement |
| **Hawkins-Kennedy** | **Flex 90°, elbow 90°, forcible IR** (GT under CA ligament) | Pain · **sensitive, not specific** · sens **75–92%**, spec **45%** · = **subacromial bursitis** (TO) |
| **Scapular assistance (TO)** | Push **inferomedial scapular angle** during elevation (upward rotation + posterior tilt) | **Relief** of impingement pain + ↑ arc |
| **Internal impingement** | 90° abduction + max ER + extension (late cocking) | **Posterior** throwing pain · relieved by relocation / elbow in front of body |

![Hawkins-Kennedy test](../../MCQ/attachments/TO17140070-1.png)

**Neer test injectate** — **5 mL 1% lidocaine** (TO) · **4 cc 1% lidocaine + 4 cc 0.5% bupivacaine + 2 cc steroid** (Orthobullets) — read the options

| Biceps test | Technique | Positive |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | Resisted forward elevation, **elbow extended, forearm supinated** | Pain in **bicipital groove** |
| **Yergason** | Elbow 90°, pronated → **resisted supination** | Groove pain = biceps tendon pathology |
| **Popeye** | Bulge in arm | **LHB proximal rupture** |
| Groove tenderness | Palpation | Inflamed LHB or **SLAP** |

**Speed + / empty can −** — **bicipital tendinitis** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: supraspinatus tendinitis · SLAP · impingement

### Rotator cuff strength & lag tests (lift-off, hornblower, ER lag)

| Muscle | Tests |
|---|---|
| **Subscapularis** | **Lift-off**, **belly-press / Napoleon**, **bear hug**, **IR lag**, ↑ passive ER |
| **Supraspinatus** | **Jobe empty can**, **drop arm (Codman)** |
| **Infraspinatus** | **ER lag sign** (specific), drop sign (Hertel), ER resistance stress |
| **Teres minor** | **Hornblower (Patte)**, ER strength at 90° abduction |

**Lift-off (Gerber) (TO)** — dorsum of hand on lumbar spine (IR) → can't lift off = **subscapularis tear** · specific for **IR deficit** (TO) · best for **inferior subscapularis** · more accurate if hand reaches opposite scapula · confounded by other muscles\
✗ Wrong options: abduction · ER deficit · anterior instability

![Lift-off test](../../MCQ/attachments/TO17140081-1.png)

**Belly-press (Gerber) / Napoleon (TO)** — palm presses abdomen, elbow in front of trunk · weak → **elbow drops behind trunk** (shoulder extension) = belly-press · **wrist flexes** = **Napoleon sign** · best for **superior subscapularis**

![Belly-press test](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3037-belly-press.jpg)

**Bear hug (Burkhart & De Beer) (TO)** — palm on **opposite shoulder**, elbow in front, examiner pulls hand away **anteriorly** · can't resist = positive · **most sensitive**: detects tears **<50%** (upper third) missed by lift-off/belly-press (TO) · positive if **≥20% weaker** than other side (Orthobullets)

**Most sensitive + specific subscap test** — **IR lag sign** (Orthobullets, OB) · **bear hug** most sensitive (TO) — read the options

**IR lag sign** — behind patient, elbow 90°, shoulder 20° elevation + 20° extension, lift hand off lumbar spine to near-max IR, release wrist → lag

**Not subscap testing** — **isolated IR at side** (pec major + latissimus contribute)

**Subscapularis insertion** — **lesser tuberosity** → **positive bear hug after trauma = lesser tuberosity avulsion**\
✗ Wrong options: GT fracture = drop arm · superior migration = CTA · dislocation can't be positioned · AC separation

**Jobe empty can (TO)** — **90° abduction in scapular plane (30° forward), IR thumb down**, resist downward push · **weakness = supraspinatus tear** (TO) · **pain only** = subacromial bursitis/irritation, tear only with true drop arm (Orthobullets)

**Drop arm (Codman) (TO)** — arm elevated overhead → lower slowly · sudden pain or uncontrolled lowering = **supraspinatus tear** · passive 90° scapular plane → drop = **most specific for full-thickness tear (98%)** (Orthobullets)

**ER resistance stress (TO)** — arms at side, elbows 90°, **ER 60°**, resist examiner's IR push → pain/weakness = **infraspinatus + teres minor**

**ER lag sign (TO)** — seated, elbow 90°, shoulder **20° elevation in scapular plane**, full ER → hold · angular drop = **infraspinatus tear** (specific)\
✗ Wrong options: subscapularis · teres minor · adhesive capsulitis

**Drop sign (TO)** — same as ERLS but **90° elevation** in scapular plane · infraspinatus · ERLS slightly more sensitive + specific · not the Orthobullets "drop sign" (= drop arm, supraspinatus)

**Hornblower (Patte) (TO)** — arm **90° abduction in scapular plane**, elbow 90° → attempt ER against resistance · can't = hornblower posture · **most accurate for teres minor** · TO option: "test for **infraspinatus and teres minor**" · Orthobullets: hold 90° abd + 90° ER → **arm falls into IR** · also + in **posterior supraspinatus** tears

![Positive hornblower's sign](../../MCQ/attachments/TO17140078-1.png)

**Teres minor** — **axillary nerve**, ER

| ✗ Distractor | Tests |
|---|---|
| Jobe | Supraspinatus weakness ± impingement |
| Belly-press, IR lag | Subscapularis |
| Hawkins | Impingement |
| "Jones test" | not supraspinatus test |

### Lesser tuberosity fracture – subscapularis testing

**Bear hug after ATV accident** — can't hold palm on opposite shoulder vs anterior pull → **lesser tuberosity fracture** on X-ray

### Labral tests (Porcelli)

| Test | Technique | Positive |
|---|---|---|
| **O'Brien (active compression)** | **90° flexion, elbow extended, 10–15° adduction, pronated (thumb down / full IR)** → resist downward force · repeat supinated/ER | **Deep GH pain pronated, not supinated = SLAP** · **superficial AC pain = AC joint** |
| Crank | Abducted, passive rotation + axial load | Click/pain = labrum |
| **Modified dynamic labral shear (TO)** | Standing, **abduct 120°** scapular plane, elbow 90°, ER → lower arm keeping ER | **Painful click posterior joint line 120°→90°** = SLAP |
| **Uppercut (TO)** | Neutral rotation, elbow 90°, supinated fist → punch to chin vs resistance | Pain = **SLAP** |
| **Porcelli (TO)** | See below | **Posterior labral tear** |

**Porcelli test (TO)** — Morey 2016 · **posterior labral tear** · **sens 100%, spec 99%**, inter-examiner reliability **>0.8** · outperformed jerk, Kim, load-and-shift · **not yet validated**\
✗ Wrong options: SLAP · subscapularis · PASTA

**Porcelli technique (TO)** — part 1: **90° flexion, 10–15° adduction, max IR (thumb down)**, examiner behind stabilises scapula, resisted elevation → note pain/strength · part 2: same, examiner's **thumb on posterior joint line** pushing anteriorly (prevents posterior subluxation) · **pain ↓/gone in part 2 = positive** · pain persists = **AC joint** pathology

### Instability tests

| Direction | Test | Technique / positive |
|---|---|---|
| Anterior | **Apprehension** (Jobe, TO) | Supine, **90° abduction + full ER** (behind coronal plane, elbow 90°) → apprehension |
| Anterior | **Relocation** | Posterior force on head → apprehension relieved |
| Anterior | **Anterior release** | Release hand in abd/ER → subluxation · **"3 in 1"** (apprehension + relocation also +) |
| Anterior | Anterior load & shift / drawer | Supine, **40–60° abd** (TO drawer: 60°, neutral rotation, axial load) · grade = laxity, pathologic only if symptomatic |
| Posterior | **Jerk (Jahnke)** | Sitting/standing, **flex 90°, IR, adducted**, axial load at elbow → posterior jerk; abduct → reduces (TO) · clunk or pain |
| Posterior | **Kim** | Seated, 90° abduction → flex to 45° + axial load + posteroinferior force · **pain** |
| Posterior | **Posterior stress** | Flexion, adduction, IR + posterior force → pain + instability |
| Posterior | Posterior load & shift / drawer | Increased translation vs other side |
| Posterior | **Loss of ER** | Locked in IR = posterior subluxation/dislocation |
| Inferior / MDI | **Sulcus** | Pull arm down (TO: at **0° and 45° abduction**) · persists at **2+/3+ with ER at side = pathologic rotator interval** |

| Translation grade (drawer / load & shift) | Finding |
|---|---|
| 1+ | **To glenoid rim** |
| 2+ | **Over rim, reduces spontaneously** |
| 3+ | **Dislocates / locks out**, no spontaneous reduction |

| Sulcus grade | Acromiohumeral interval |
|---|---|
| 1+ | **<1 cm** |
| 2+ | **1–2 cm** |
| 3+ | **>2 cm** |

## How it's asked

**"Full-thickness teres minor tear — positive test?"** → **Hornblower's sign**\
✗ Wrong options: Jobe · belly-press · IR lag · Hawkins

**"90° abduction + full ER — ligament resisting anterior translation?"** → **anterior band IGHL**\
✗ Wrong options: CHL · SGHL · MGHL · posterior band

**"ATV crash, positive bear hug — X-ray?"** → **lesser tuberosity fracture**

**"Correct statement for test in figure (arm in hornblower posture)?"** → **Hornblower's test for infraspinatus and teres minor** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: Jones · O'Brien · IR lag

**"Muscle tested in figure (hand behind back)?"** → **subscapularis** (lift-off) (TO)

**"Lift-off test is specific for?"** → **internal rotation deficit** (TO)

**"ER lag sign suggests?"** → **infraspinatus tear** (TO)

**"Anterior pain on IR, positive lift-off — muscle?"** → **subscapularis** (TO)

**"Diagram: 90° flexion + forced IR?"** → **Hawkins-Kennedy test** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: Neer · empty can · ER lag

**"Positive Hawkins sign indicates?"** → **subacromial bursitis** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: cuff tear · subscap tear · brachial plexus

**"Positive Speed + negative empty can?"** → **bicipital tendinitis** (TO)

**"Porcelli test detects?"** → **posterior labral tear** (TO)
