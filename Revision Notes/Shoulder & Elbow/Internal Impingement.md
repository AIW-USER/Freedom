# Internal Impingement

*Shoulder & Elbow · 20 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Who** — major cause of shoulder pain in **throwing + overhead athletes**; very common in **elite baseball pitchers** · also swimming, volleyball, javelin

**Definition** — repetitive contact of **undersurface (articular side) of posterior rotator cuff** + greater tuberosity against **posterosuperior glenoid/labrum**

**Impinged structure** — **posterior rotator cuff**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior cuff · GH ligaments · biceps

**Contact** — may be **physiologic or pathologic** in throwers

### Anatomy – glenohumeral stability & cuff force couples

| Restraint | Structures |
|---|---|
| **Static** | **GH ligaments**, labrum, articular congruity + version, negative intra-articular pressure |
| **Dynamic** | Rotator cuff, biceps, periscapular muscles |

| Force couple plane | Balance |
|---|---|
| **Coronal** | **Inferior cuff (IS, TM, subscapularis)** vs superior moment of **deltoid** |
| **Transverse** | **Subscapularis (anterior)** vs **IS + TM (posterior)** |

**Cuff role** — dynamic stability, centres head → stable fulcrum; goal of cuff repair = restore equilibrium

![Glenohumeral ligaments — posterior band of IGHL](../../MCQ/attachments/q212172-illa-glenohumeral-ligs.jpg)

| Ligament | Role in internal impingement |
|---|---|
| **Posterior band IGHL (PIGHL) + posterior capsule** | **Tight + thickened** ✓ (target of sleeper stretch) |
| Anterior band IGHL | **Lax** (✗ Wrong options: tight) |
| Anterior axillary pouch, MGHL, SGHL | Not implicated |
| Superior band IGHL | Most strain in **SLAP** lesions |

## 2 Mechanism

### Pathomechanics – late cocking, abduction–external rotation & posterosuperior contact

**Position** — **abduction + maximal ER**\
✗ Wrong options: adduction/IR · abduction/IR · flexion/ER

**Phase** — **late cocking** (+ early acceleration); max ER\
✗ Wrong options: wind-up · early cocking · deceleration · follow-through

**Contact** — **posterior articular supraspinatus** (± anterior infraspinatus) between greater tuberosity + **posterosuperior glenoid**

**Peel-back** — posterosuperior labrum peeled back by biceps → SLAP

**Internal vs external** — internal = **articular side** · subacromial ("external") = **bursal side**

![Internal vs external impingement](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3054-internalvsexternal.jpg)

### Posterior capsular contracture & GIRD – stretching

**Most clearly associated cause** — **posterior capsular contracture** (✓ primary process)\
✗ Wrong options: anterior capsular laxity (variable, less direct) · CA arch stenosis (subacromial) · Bennett lesion (no causal link)

**Contracture effect** — blocks normal anteroinferior translation in late cocking → **obligatory posterosuperior translation** → posterosuperior labral + articular cuff pathology

**Etiology** — tight **posterior band IGHL** + **anterior micro-instability** · multifactorial: altered biomechanics, subtle instability, posterior contracture, scapular dyskinesia

**Primary etiology** — **posterior capsular contracture** (OB, Orthobullets) · **GH instability** commonly primary (one OB explanation) — read the options

**Anterior labrum** — may become **thinned** (obligate translation → anterior micro-instability)

**Associated conditions** — **GIRD**, SLAP tears, **SICK scapula** + dyskinesia, biceps tendinitis, anterior instability

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — diffuse **posterior shoulder** pain (posterior deltoid), deep-seated · worse with throwing, esp. **late cocking + early acceleration** · ↓ velocity

**Inspection** — proximal humeral retroversion · tender along **infraspinatus**

**ROM** — **↑ ER + ↓ IR** · IR loss **>20° at 90°** (Orthobullets) · stabilise scapula · total arc **preserved** (Orthobullets, OB) · total arc **↓** (one OB stem) — read the stem

**Strength** — rotator cuff weakness often (may be 5/5)

**Isolated finding** — ↓ IR with **no other positive provocative test**\
✗ Wrong options: sulcus sign · Speed's · ↓ ER · ↓ abduction

| Test | Technique | Positive |
|---|---|---|
| **Apprehension test** (internal impingement) | Max ER + abduction + extension | **Posterior** pain, relieved in neutral |
| **Whipple** | Forward flexion + adduction + scapular retraction, resist | Pain → **partial supraspinatus tear** |
| Sulcus sign | Downward pull, arm adducted | **Inferior instability** (superior GH laxity) |
| Speed's | 90° FF, supinated, resist downward | **Biceps tendinitis** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Classification** — **none formal**

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — complete shoulder series; usually normal · **Bennett lesion** on AP

![Bennett lesion](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3054-bennet-lesion.jpg)

**Bennett lesion** — **mineralisation / exostosis of posteroinferior glenoid** = **traction spur** of posteroinferior capsule (PIGHL) · formed in **deceleration + follow-through** · **baseball pitchers** · **22%** of MLB pitchers, **none needed surgery**\
✗ Wrong options: football, swimmers, rowers

**MRI / MRA** — **PASTA** (partial articular supraspinatus–infraspinatus tendon avulsion), fraying, tear · signal at **greater tuberosity** (posterolateral head edema, **cysts**) + **posterosuperior labrum** · ↑ anterior capsular volume · thickened **PIGHL**

**ABER view** — reproduces impingement position; dynamic humeral + glenoid sides

![PASTA lesion](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3054-pasta.jpg)

### Associated lesions & diagnosis (articular-sided cuff tears, posterosuperior labral fraying)

| Lesion (spectrum) | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Articular-sided partial cuff tear** | **Supraspinatus–infraspinatus interval** (✗ Wrong options: bursal = external) |
| **Posterior + superior labral lesion** | **SLAP with posterior extension**; posterosuperior fraying |
| **Bennett lesion** | Posterior capsule/glenoid hypertrophy + scarring (calcified) |
| **Chondral injury** | **Posterior glenoid** |
| Greater tuberosity | Cystic changes |
| Capsule | **Lax anterior**, **tight posterior** |

**Not part of it** — subscapularis tear · bursal-sided tear · Bankart · Hill-Sachs · tight anterior capsule · lax posterior capsule · full-thickness tear (needs significant trauma)

**Bennett + ↓ arc + deep pain + posterosuperior labral fraying** — **internal impingement**\
✗ Wrong options: SLAP alone · subacromial = Hawkins + · calcific tendinitis

**Javelin thrower, failed PT, SLAP with posterior extension** — **internal impingement**\
✗ Wrong options: EDS/MDI · traumatic anterior instability · osteonecrosis

### Asymptomatic MRI findings in pitchers

**Common asymptomatic findings** — partial cuff tear · SLAP / posterior-superior labral tear · **↑ humeral retroversion** · thick posterior capsule · posterolateral head cysts

**Asymptomatic pitcher** — **continue pitching**\
✗ Wrong options: rest · posterior release · cuff repair · rotational osteotomy

**GIRD definition** — **IR deficit 15° + total arc deficit ≥10°** (OB) · **>20° IR loss** (Orthobullets) · **>25°** (Orthobullets GIRD note) — read the options

## 5 Management

```
Internal impingement
├─ First line → stop throwing + PT (posterior capsule stretching)
├─ Failed extended PT + PASTA <50% / Bennett / peel-back → arthroscopic debridement
├─ Failed PT + partial >50% / full-thickness / unstable labrum → repair (arthroscopic or mini-open)
└─ Persistent posterior contracture / anterior instability → add posterior release or anterior plication
```

### Nonoperative

**Regimen** — break from throwing until pain-free → supervised return with proper mechanics · **sleeper + cross-body stretches**, cuff strength balancing, scapular stabilisation, kinetic chain · **6 months** stretching

**Stretch target** — **posteroinferior capsule** · **posterior band IGHL**\
✗ Wrong options: superior, anterior, anteroinferior, inferior

**Prevention ✓** — **posterior capsular stretching** ↓ shoulder + elbow injury risk\
✗ Wrong options: cuff strengthening · periscapular strengthening (less direct) · SLAP / partial cuff repair

**Outcome** — correlates with **compliance**; surgery results **unpredictable** → only after extended failed PT

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication (after failed nonop) |
|---|---|
| **Arthroscopic debridement** (cuff ± labrum) | **Partial tear <50%** · **Bennett lesion** · **peel-back labral lesion** |
| **Cuff ± labral repair** (arthroscopic / mini-open) | **Partial >50%** or full-thickness · **unstable labral tear** |
| **Posterior capsule release vs anterior stabilisation** | Persistent posterior contracture or anterior instability (adjunct) |

### Techniques

**Debridement** — EUA + diagnostic arthroscopy (intra-articular + subacromial) · shaver · **accelerated rehab**, shorter immobilisation

**Cuff repair** — arthroscopic addresses labrum too · bursectomy · **no acromioplasty** if no bursal pathology · footprint abrasion · **portal of Wilmington** · partial: **in situ trans-tendinous** (pulley; functionally shortens tendon) or complete the tear → single/double row

**Posterior release** — cautery / shaver · **axillary nerve risk** at inferior border of infraspinatus

**Anterior stabilisation** — **capsular plication** most common

## 6 Complications

**Progression to full-thickness tear** — small risk after debridement alone

**Return to play** — **delayed / worse after cuff repair** in throwers

**Axillary nerve injury** — posterior release, **inferior border of infraspinatus**

## X vs Y

| | **Internal impingement** | **Subacromial (external) impingement** |
|---|---|---|
| Cuff side | **Articular** (posterior SS/IS) | **Bursal** |
| Pain | **Posterior**, ABER, late cocking | **Anterolateral**, IR in abduction |
| Test | Posterior apprehension pain, ↓ IR | **Hawkins / Neer** + |
| Cause | Posterior capsular contracture, micro-instability | Hooked acromion, CA arch stenosis |

| | **Bennett lesion** | **Hill-Sachs** |
|---|---|---|
| Site | **Posteroinferior glenoid** | Posterosuperior humeral head |
| Nature | Traction spur (PIGHL) | Impaction fracture |
| Context | Pitchers | Anterior dislocation |
