# Thoracic Outlet Syndrome

*Shoulder & Elbow · 55 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — compression of **brachial plexus ± subclavian vessels** between neck and axilla (first rib–clavicle); heterogeneous, potentially disabling

**Incidence** — **1–2%** of population

**Demographics** — **♀ > ♂ (3:1)**; **thin, long neck, drooping shoulders**; age **20–60**

| Type | Frequency | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Neurogenic** | **95% (most common)** | Lower plexus |
| **Venous** | **4%** | Paget-Schroetter; athletic ♂ > ♀ |
| **Arterial** | **<1%** | Aneurysm, emboli; athletic ♂ > ♀ |

### Anatomy – thoracic outlet spaces

**Boundaries (OB)** — **clavicle, first rib, subclavius, costoclavicular ligament, anterior scalene**

| Space | Borders | Contents | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Interscalene triangle** (proximal) | **Ant: anterior scalene · Post: middle scalene · Inf: 1st rib** | **Plexus trunks + subclavian artery** (✗ Wrong options: vein (runs **beneath anterior scalene**)) | **Adson**, supraclavicular pressure |
| **Costoclavicular space** (middle) | Ant: clavicle + **subclavius** · Post: 1st rib + scalenes · Med: **costoclavicular ligament** · Lat: upper scapular border | **Divisions + subclavian artery + vein** | **Costoclavicular manoeuvre** |
| **Retropectoralis minor** (subcoracoid; distal) | Sup: **coracoid** · Ant: **pectoralis minor** · Post: **ribs 2–4** | **Cords + axillary artery + vein** | **Wright** |

![Three spaces of the thoracic outlet](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3064-tosanatomy.jpg)

**Most affected (OB)** — **subclavian artery, vein + lower trunk (C8–T1)**

## 2 Mechanism

**Pathogenesis** — **anatomic predisposition + superimposed neck trauma** (acute or repetitive)

**Split** — **soft tissue 70%** · **osseous 30%**

| Soft tissue | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Scalene abnormalities** | **Anterior scalene hypertrophy** · plexus **through** anterior scalene · **middle scalene anterior insertion** on 1st rib |
| **Scalenus minimus** | Accessory; **30–50%** of TOS; C-transverse process → 1st rib **between subclavian artery + T1 root** |
| **Fibromuscular bands** | ↑ stiffness, ↓ compliance; congenital band → lower trunk |
| **Costoclavicular ligament** | Abnormal insertion → **Paget-Schroetter** |
| **Tumours** | **Pancoast** (1–3% lung cancers; **lacks cough/haemoptysis/dyspnoea**), neuroblastoma, plexus schwannoma |
| Pectoralis minor | Abnormal |

| Osseous | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cervical rib** | **<1%** population; from **C7** |
| **Prominent C7 transverse process** | |
| Clavicle / 1st rib | Displaced fracture, **hypertrophic callus**, malunion |
| AC / SC injury | Dislocation |
| Tumours | 1st-rib metastasis (**breast, prostate, kidney**), osteoid osteoma |

### Cervical rib types

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Complete**, articulates with 1st rib or manubrium |
| 2 | Incomplete, **free bulbous tip** |
| 3 | Incomplete, **fibrous band** distally |
| 4 | **Short bar** (mm) beyond C7 transverse process |

**Overuse** — repetitive overhead lifting, **hyperabduction**; **weightlifting, rowing, swimming**

**Vascular damage** — aneurysm, thrombosis, emboli, limb-threatening ischaemia

### Paget-Schroetter syndrome

**Definition** — **venous TOS** in **well-developed young athletes** → **upper-limb DVT**

**Cause** — intermittent **subclavian vein** obstruction in **costoclavicular space** by **abnormal costoclavicular ligament** or **anterior scalene hypertrophy**

## 3 Presentation

### Neurogenic

**Pain** — neck, **trapezius (92%)**, chest, shoulder, arm

**Paraesthesia** — **98%**; **non-radicular**, **wide plexus distribution** · **lower plexus (C8–T1) or combined (C5–T1) in 90%** → **ring + small fingers**, medial forearm

**Other** — weakness, numbness, **heaviness with overhead activity** · activity-related + **night symptoms** (↓ pressure → returning sensation felt as pain) · uni- or bilateral

**Posture** — rounded shoulders, ↑ kyphosis, depressed/downward-rotated scapula

### Gilliatt-Sumner hand (TO)

**Definition (TO)** — rare, severe sign of **true neurogenic TOS**; **chronic lower-trunk (C8–T1)** compression by **cervical rib or congenital fibrous band**

**Pattern** — **preferential APB atrophy, relative hypothenar sparing** (TO, ✓) · atrophy of **APB + hypothenar + interossei** (Orthobullets, TO Elvey list) — read the options

**Why APB (TO)** — **T1-innervated** muscles hit harder (median + ulnar both get lower-trunk fibres)

**Red flag (TO)** — thenar wasting usually = CTS, but in TOS **+ medial forearm sensory change** + milder ulnar intrinsic involvement

(✗ Wrong options: **Distractors (TO)** — symmetric interossei/hypothenar wasting sparing thenar) · claw hand from ulnar compression at humerotricipital groove · thumb/index C6 sensory loss

**Diagnosis (TO)** — EDX (neurogenic changes) + MRI/US

**Treatment (TO)** — **surgical decompression** → stops progression of atrophy + pain; partial motor recovery possible

**Differential (TO)** — **CTS, ALS (motor neuron disease), Hirayama disease**

![Gilliatt-Sumner hand: thenar (APB) wasting](../../MCQ/attachments/TO24921390-1.png)

### Vascular

**Venous** — **episodic cyanosis + swelling**, distended veins, deep arm ache, **heaviness after activity**

**Arterial** — **unilateral Raynaud-type**: coolness + **pallor → cyanosis → erythema**, worse in cold; **hand** predominantly; ulcers/microemboli (rare)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Provocative tests & neurogenic TOS

**All provocative tests** — **high false-positive rate**

| Test | Technique | Positive | Space |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supraclavicular pressure** | Squeeze upper trapezius + anterior scalene **30 s**, arm at side | Pain/paraesthesia | Interscalene |
| **Adson** | Arm slightly abducted + ER (TO: abducted + **extended**), elbow extended; **neck extended + rotated TOWARD tested side**; inhale + hold | ↓/absent radial pulse ± symptoms; **51% of normals** ↓ pulse; TO: **low sensitivity + PPV** (modified: tilt to opposite side / symptom aggravation) | **Interscalene** |
| **Costoclavicular (military / "attention")** | Shoulders retracted + depressed, chest out; shoulder extended ~30° **1 min** | ↓ pulse ± symptoms | **Costoclavicular** |
| **Wright (hyperabduction)** | Neck rotated **away**; shoulder ER + **max abduction above head** **1 min** (TO: 1–2 min) | ↓ pulse (**7% of normals**) ± symptoms | **Retropectoralis minor** |
| **Roos / EAST** | Orthobullets: **abduct 90° + elbow 90°**, open/close hands **3 min** · TO: arms **abducted, ER, elevated overhead**, open/close fingers **1–3 min** | **Reproduction of symptoms**; can't complete 3 min; relief on dropping arms | **Entire outlet** |
| **Cyriax release** | Examiner behind, elevates shoulder girdle passively **3 min** | Symptoms reproduced (unloading) | Unloading |
| **Halstead (TO)** | Downward traction on arm, **neck hyperextended + rotated to opposite side** | **Radial pulse disappears** | |
| **ULTT of Elvey (TO)** | Abduct 90°, elbow extended, **wrist dorsiflexed**, then **ear to contralateral shoulder** | Ipsilateral symptoms = **neurogenic TOS** | Plexus stretch |
| ✗ Wrong option: **Spurling (TO)** | Neck extension + lateral flexion + **axial compression** | **Radicular** pain | **Cervical radiculopathy** |

### Key radiology findings

| Test | Use / finding |
|---|---|
| **CXR + C-spine X-ray** | **Cervical rib**, prominent C7 TP, low shoulder girdle, **Pancoast** (or normal) |
| CT | Osseous lesions, rib/clavicle malunion |
| MRI | Soft-tissue anomalies |
| **EMG/NCV** | Historically equivocal (normal until permanent damage) · early: **↓ NCV in medial antebrachial cutaneous + median motor to APB** (C8–T1) |
| **Doppler US** | Subclavian vein obstruction/thrombosis — **95% sensitive, 92% specific** for venous TOS |
| CTA/MRA · arteriography | **Embolic disease / aneurysm** |
| **Venography** | Suspected **subclavian/axillary vein thrombosis** |

**Normal neuro exam + EMG/NCS** — does **not** exclude TOS (✓ cervical rib vignette)

### Diagnosis – cervical rib

**Classic vignette** — **bilateral ring + small finger tingling**, worse **overhead**, normal neuro exam + EMG/NCS, cervical ribs on X-ray → **TOS**\
✗ Wrong options: C5–6 / C6–7 disc · cubital tunnel · radial tunnel

## 5 Management

```
Thoracic outlet syndrome
├─ Neurogenic → PT, activity modification, NSAIDs
│      └─ fails 6 mo / progressive atrophy → decompression
├─ Scalene contracture → US-guided scalene block (lidocaine / Botox)
└─ Vascular → by lesion (see table)
```

### Nonoperative

**First line** — avoid provocative activity (overhead, ± change job) · NSAIDs, muscle relaxants · **PT**: core/back + shoulder-girdle strengthening, **posture**, relaxation · TENS

**Worse results** — **obese** · **workers' compensation** · **double crush** (carpal/cubital tunnel)

**Anterior scalene block** — **US-guided lidocaine or botulinum toxin**; successful block → **14% higher** good surgical outcome

**Leffert (OB)** — reserve surgery for **intolerable** symptoms (complications significant)

### Operative criteria

| Indication | |
|---|---|
| **Failed nonop 6 months** | Decompression |
| **Progressive atrophy / worsening neuro deficit** | Decompression |
| Embolic events · stenosis with pain + insufficiency · **subclavian aneurysm** · thrombosis with critical ischaemia | Vascular intervention |

### Decompression procedures

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **1st rib resection + anterior + middle scalenectomy + neurolysis** | **Most common**; **95% good outcomes**; combined transaxillary + supraclavicular |
| **Isolated scalenectomy** | **Upper plexus** symptoms · **no bony anomaly** · muscular/obese · **recurrence after 1st rib resection** |
| **Isolated pec minor tenotomy** | Symptoms reproduced at **retropectoralis minor** space |
| Cervical rib resection · band release · costoclavicular ligament resection · clavicle malunion ORIF | Per cause |

### Vascular intervention

| Lesion | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Acute embolism** | **IV heparin** ± embolectomy ± thrombectomy ± **tPA** + anticoagulation |
| Small-vessel embolism | **tPA + anticoagulation** |
| Large/proximal embolism | **Embolectomy + anticoagulation** |
| Mild stenosis | **Endovascular stent** |
| **Subclavian aneurysm** / severe stenosis or thrombosis + critical ischaemia | **Resection** ± repair / saphenous / arterial / synthetic graft |
| Chronic emboli + critical ischaemia | **Bypass** |

### Approaches

| | **Transaxillary** | **Supraclavicular** | **Posterior** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Use | **Most common**; **best 1st rib exposure** | **Upper plexus**, scalenes, 1st rib neck, vessels | **Recurrent TOS**, **prior neck surgery** |
| Best for | 1st rib, cervical rib, bands, **lower plexus (C7–T1)** neurolysis | **Isolated scalenectomy + arterial reconstruction** | Proximal plexus |
| Pros | **Cosmetic scar**, no NV retraction for rib | | |
| Cons | **Plexus injury risk** | Poor 1st rib view (needs plexus/vessel retraction) | Extensive dissection → shoulder dysfunction; **LTN, dorsal scapular, accessory nerve** risk |

## 6 Complications

**Pneumothorax** — **one of the most common** after 1st rib resection

## X vs Y

| | **Neurogenic TOS** | **Cervical radiculopathy** | **Cubital / carpal tunnel** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distribution | **Non-radicular, wide (plexus)**, C8–T1 | **Radicular** | **Clear dermatomal/peripheral** distribution |
| Provocation | Overhead, Roos, Adson, Wright | **Spurling** | |
| EMG | Often normal early; MABC + APB motor ↓ | | |

| | **Gilliatt-Sumner (TOS)** | **CTS** |
|---|---|---|
| Thenar (APB) wasting | **Yes** (preferential) | Yes |
| Medial forearm sensation | **Abnormal** | Normal |
| Lesion | **Lower trunk C8–T1** | Median at wrist |

## How it's asked

**"35-y, bilateral ring/small tingling overhead, normal EMG, X-ray shown?"** → **thoracic outlet syndrome (cervical rib)**\
✗ Wrong options: C5–6/C6–7 disc · cubital tunnel · radial tunnel

**"Arms abducted, ER, overhead; open-close fingers 1–3 min — test?"** → **Roos test** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: Adson · Wright · Spurling

**"Gilliatt-Sumner hand in true neurogenic TOS?"** → **preferential APB atrophy, relative hypothenar sparing** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: symmetric interossei sparing thenar · claw from ulnar at humerotricipital groove · C6 thumb/index loss

**"Shoulder ER + max abduction above head 1 min, neck turned away — test and space?"** → **Wright test; retropectoralis minor space**\
✗ Wrong options: Adson = interscalene · costoclavicular

**"Most common complication of 1st rib resection?"** → **pneumothorax**
