# Elbow Arthroscopy: Indications & Approach

*Shoulder & Elbow · 14 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Indications

| Source | Indications |
|---|---|
| **Orthobullets** | **Loose body removal**, osteophyte debridement, **synovectomy**, **capsular release (stiffness)**, **OCD capitellum**, lateral epicondylitis, **septic arthritis** debridement |
| **TO** | Loose bodies · **OCD capitellum** · osteochondral lesions radial head · osteophytes humerus/olecranon · **RA (partial synovectomy)** · stiff elbow (debridement, lysis of adhesions) · **tennis elbow release** · evaluation of painful elbow · **inflamed olecranon bursa** · **intra-articular fractures** |

**Osteocapsular arthroplasty** — young patient with degenerative elbow where **TEA inappropriate**, or significant contracture

**Advantages** — better articular visualisation, ↓ post-op pain, **faster recovery**

**Disadvantages** — technically demanding; **high risk of NV injury** (proximity to joint)

### Contraindications (prior ulnar nerve transposition)

| Source | Contraindications |
|---|---|
| **Orthobullets** | Prior trauma, surgical scarring, **previous ulnar nerve transposition** |
| **TO** | **Bony ankylosis** · **severe fibrous ankylosis** (scope can't enter safely) · **active infection** · surgery altering anatomy e.g. **anterior ulnar nerve transposition (relative)** |
| **OB** | **Ulnar neuropathy with prior submuscular transposition** = contraindication |

**Not contraindications (OB)** — **Panner disease** · loose body ulnohumeral joint · **s/p radial head ORIF** · elbow stiffness · **osteoarthritis**

**Ulnar nerve subluxation** — **not an absolute contraindication**; identify pre-op (esp. prior submuscular/intramuscular transposition)

### Capsular anatomy & distension (TO)

**Capsule (TO)** — attached to articular margins; fibres blend with **annular ligament**; anterior → above coronoid + radial fossae; posterior → above olecranon fossa

**Tension (TO)** — taut **anteriorly in extension**, **posteriorly in flexion**

**Maximum distension (TO)** — **70–80° flexion**; capacity **25–30 mL**\
✗ Wrong options: 90–120° · 10° · 120–150°

## 5 Management

### Positioning & joint distension (TO)

**Positions** — **supine** (suspended), **prone**, **lateral decubitus**

**Most commonly used (TO)** — **lateral**, elbow hanging freely on support (**flexed**)

**All positions = elbow flexed (TO)** — **elbow extended, prone** = not a position

**Anaesthesia** — **general** (muscle relaxation, tolerates prone/lateral) · regional: **no immediate post-op nerve check**, poor tolerance of position

### Portal technique

**Distend first** — **fully distend joint through lateral soft spot** → NV structures move away from trocar

**Nick and spread** — skin only with blade, **hemostat spread** to capsule, **blunt trocar**

**Elbow position** — **anterior portals at 90° flexion** · posterior portals in some extension

**Kit** — standard **30° scope** · tourniquet · **solid cannulas** → maintain distension, ↓ extravasation\
✗ Wrong options: trephinated

**Landmarks** — olecranon, both epicondyles, radiocapitellar joint, **ulnar nerve**; **mark before insufflation** (distension distorts)

**Order** — distend → viewing portal (medial or lateral) → working portal under **direct vision + needle localisation**

**First portal** — **anterolateral** after distension (TO, OB) · establishing a **medial portal first** (before swelling) may be advantageous (Orthobullets) — read the options

**Viewing vs working (TO)** — **anterolateral = viewing**; **proximal anteromedial = most common working portal** + most commonly used medial portal

**Posteromedial portal** — **avoided** (lies over **ulnar nerve**)

### Factors that ↓ NV injury (TO)

| ✓ Manoeuvre (TO) | Protects |
|---|---|
| **90° flexion + joint distension** | Radial nerve moves anteriorly |
| **Pronation** | **PIN** |
| **Keep lateral portal more proximal** | **Radial nerve / PIN** |
| Avoid deep knife; hemostat + blunt trocar | Lateral + posterior antebrachial cutaneous |
| Draw landmarks, **palpate ulnar nerve** | Ulnar |
| **Low-flow infusion 40–45 mL/min or gravity** (✗ Wrong options: pressurised) | Compartment syndrome |
| Instrument tips always in view; no suction near nerves | All |

**NOT a protective manoeuvre (TO)** — **more distal lateral portal** (risk ↑ with distal placement)

### Portals & nerves at risk

| Portal | Location | Use | Nerves at risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Proximal anterolateral** | **1–2 cm proximal, 1 cm anterior** to lateral epicondyle | Views anteromedial joint; **safest anterolateral** (TO) | **Radial** (↓ risk more proximally) |
| Mid anterolateral (TO) | Just proximal, ~1 cm anterior to radiocapitellar joint | — | — |
| **Distal anterolateral** | **1–3 cm distal, 1 cm anterior** to lateral epicondyle; anterior to radial head/neck | 1st portal supine; radial head, medial elbow, coronoid, trochlea, brachialis insertion, coronoid fossa | **PIN** + **lateral antebrachial cutaneous**; **radial at maximum risk** (TO) → rarely used |
| **Direct lateral (soft spot)** | Triangle **olecranon – radial head – lateral epicondyle** | **Initial distension**; posterior compartment (capitellum, radial head, radioulnar joint) | **Relatively safe**; LABC |
| **Anteromedial** (direct medial) | **2 cm anterior + 2 cm distal** to medial epicondyle; through flexor-pronator | Augments proximal AM; medial recess; place under direct vision | **MABC + median** (+ basilic vein, TO) |
| **Proximal anteromedial (superomedial)** | **2 cm proximal** to medial epicondyle, **anterior to IM septum** | Entire anterior compartment, radial head, capitellum, coronoid, trochlea; **most common working portal** (TO) | **MABC**; ulnar (7 mm, posterior to septum) + median (OB, TO) |
| **Straight posterior (transtriceps)** | **3 cm proximal** to olecranon, midline through triceps | Partial extension; **olecranon osteophytes**, posteromedial loose bodies | **Posterior antebrachial cutaneous**; ulnar **15–25 mm** away (OB) |
| **Posterolateral** | **2–3 cm proximal** to olecranon, **just lateral to triceps** (centre of anconeus triangle) | **20–30° flexion** (relax triceps); **best posterior compartment**, radiocapitellar (**OCD capitellum**), olecranon fossa | PABC, medial brachial cutaneous, ulnar (Orthobullets) · PABC + **lateral brachial cutaneous** (TO) |
| ✗ Wrong option: **posteromedial** | Medial to triceps | **Not used** | **Ulnar nerve** — unacceptably high |

**Proximal anterolateral location** — **1–2 cm proximal** (Orthobullets) · **2 cm proximal** (TO, OB) · **3 cm proximal** (one OB explanation) — read the options

**Distal anterolateral location** — **1–3 cm distal** (Orthobullets) · **2–3 / 3 cm distal** (TO) · **1–2 cm distal** (OB)

**Straight posterior location** — **3 cm proximal** (Orthobullets, OB) · **2–3 cm proximal, 2 cm medial to posterolateral** (TO)

**Posterior compartment workhorses** — **straight posterior + posterolateral**, both **3 cm proximal to olecranon tip**\
✗ Wrong options: any posteromedial portal

### Nerve distances (OB)

| Portal | Nerve – distance |
|---|---|
| Anterolateral (joint line) | **Radial 4.8 mm** · PABC 12.6 mm |
| **Proximal anterolateral** | **Radial 4–7 mm** · PIN 1–14 mm |
| Anteromedial | **MABC 1 mm · median 7 mm** (one OB) · MABC 8.9 · median 12.9 · ulnar 22.1 mm (another OB) |
| Proximal anteromedial | MABC 1 mm · **median 12 mm** · ulnar 7 mm (posterior to septum) |
| Posterolateral | Radial 4.8 mm (3–8) (OB) |
| Straight posterior | Ulnar **~20 mm (15–25)** |

### Radial nerve & anterior capsulectomy

**Radial nerve course** — on the **anterior capsule at mid-capitellum** (OB), runs longitudinally **medial to capitellum** → at risk in capsular excision; seen near probe when viewing radiocapitellar joint from **anteromedial** portal

**Protective layer** — **brachialis (~4 mm)** between capsule and radial nerve **proximal to radial neck**\
✗ Wrong options: anconeus · brachioradialis · biceps · pronator teres

**Danger zone** — distal capsule **over radial head/neck** → transect capsule **≥2–3 cm proximal to radial head**

## 6 Complications

### Nerve injury

**Rate** — **nerve palsy 1–5%**; neurologic = **most common** complication, mostly **transient**

**Greatest risk** — **rheumatoid arthritis**, **elbow contracture**

| Ranking | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Most common complication / nerve (TO, OB)** | **Transient (sensory) ulnar nerve palsy** |
| 2nd | **Radial nerve palsy** (Orthobullets, OB) · **persistent wound drainage**, then transient sensory radial palsy (TO) |
| **Most common permanent nerve palsy (TO)** | **PIN**, then ulnar |
| **Least involved nerve (TO)** | **Median** |

**Mechanism** — **direct** (trocars, instruments, no blunt dissection → neuromas) · **indirect** (**compartment syndrome** from aggressive distension/extravasation; **local anaesthetic extravasation** — transient)

### Deficit → nerve → portal

| Deficit | Nerve | Portal |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrist drop: no wrist + MCP extension** | **Radial nerve proper** | **Proximal anterolateral** |
| Weak wrist extension + **ulnar deviation** (lost ECRL) | Radial nerve proper | Proximal anterolateral |
| **No finger (MCP) extension, partial wrist extension; can't extend thumb IP**; wrist extends in **radial deviation**; no paraesthesia | **PIN** (ECRL spared) | **(Distal) anterolateral** |
| **Paraesthesia dorsal 5th digit**, intrinsic weakness, ↓ finger ab/adduction | **Ulnar** | **Posteromedial** |
| Paraesthesia **medial volar forearm** | MABC | Anteromedial |
| Can't flex wrist | Median | Anteromedial |
| Sensory deficit only | Superficial radial | — |

**PIN palsy** — EPL (thumb IP extension) + finger extensors; **ECRL** innervated **proximal to bifurcation** → spared

**✗ PIN traps** — index abduction = **1st DI (ulnar)** · APB = **median** (APL = PIN) · "wrist extension + radial deviation lost" = radial nerve proper

### Other complications

**HO / ankylosis** — **less than open**; minimise bleeding

**Infection** — **sinus tract (posterolateral portal)**; ↑ risk with **intra-articular steroid at time of arthroscopy**; possibly ↑ with **inflammatory arthritis**

### Infection prevention – avoid intra-articular steroid

**✓ Prevent infection** — **avoid intra-op steroid injection**

**✗ Traps** — avoid posterior midline portals · avoid scope in OA · absorbable subcuticular suture only (no difference) · restrict ROM 3–4 wk (↑ stiffness)

## X vs Y

| | **Proximal anterolateral** | **Distal anterolateral** |
|---|---|---|
| Location | 1–2 cm **proximal**, 1 cm anterior to LE | 1–3 cm **distal**, 1 cm anterior to LE |
| Nerve | **Radial nerve proper** (wrist drop) | **PIN** (finger + thumb IP extension), LABC |
| Safety | **Safer** → more commonly used | Riskier → rarely used |

| | **Anteromedial** | **Proximal anteromedial** |
|---|---|---|
| Location | 2 cm anterior + 2 cm distal to ME | 2 cm proximal to ME, anterior to septum |
| Nerves | **MABC (1 mm) + median (7 mm)** | MABC (1 mm), ulnar (7 mm), median (12 mm) |
| Role | Augment PAMP, medial recess | **Most common working portal** |

## How it's asked

**"First portal in elbow arthroscopy?"** → **anterolateral** (TO)

**"Most common nerve involved?"** → **ulnar** (TO)

**"Most common complication?"** → **transient sensory ulnar nerve palsy** (TO)

**"Most common permanent nerve palsy?"** → **PIN** (TO)

**"Least involved nerve?"** → **median** (TO)

**"NOT a manoeuvre to avoid nerve injury?"** → **more distal lateral portal** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: pronation + 90° · distension · low flow

**"Most commonly used working portal?"** → **proximal anteromedial** (TO)

**"NOT a position for elbow arthroscopy?"** → **elbow extended, prone** (TO)

**"Maximum elbow distension at?"** → **70–80° flexion** (TO)

**"Portal generally not used?"** → **posteromedial** (ulnar nerve)

**"Shaver through anterolateral portal, finger drop — structure?"** → **PIN**

**"Structure nearest probe, radiocapitellar joint from anteromedial portal?"** → **radial nerve**

**"Greatest risk with proximal anterolateral portal?"** → **radial nerve**

**"Two safest posterior compartment portals?"** → **straight posterior + posterolateral, 3 cm proximal to olecranon**

**"Portal 2 cm proximal, 1 cm anterior to LE — deficit if nerve injured?"** → **loss of wrist + MCP extension**

**"Can't extend thumb IP post-op — portal?"** → **anterolateral**\
✗ Wrong options: direct lateral = LABC · posterolateral = PABC

**"Portal 2 cm anterior + 2 cm distal to ME — risk?"** → **MABC + median**

**"AL, AM and posteromedial portals used — likely deficit?"** → **paraesthesia dorsal 5th digit** (ulnar)

**"Protects radial nerve in anterior capsulectomy?"** → **brachialis**

**"Prior radial head ORIF, AL portal — iatrogenic finding?"** → **can't extend thumb IP**\
✗ Wrong options: wrist extension + radial deviation = radial proper

**"Contraindication to elbow arthroscopy?"** → **prior submuscular ulnar nerve transposition**\
✗ Wrong options: Panner · loose body · stiffness · s/p radial head ORIF

**"Prevent infection after elbow arthroscopy?"** → **avoid intra-op steroid injection**
