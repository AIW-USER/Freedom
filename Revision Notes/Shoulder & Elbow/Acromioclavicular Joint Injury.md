# Acromioclavicular Joint Injury

*Shoulder & Elbow · 3 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Frequency** — **9% of shoulder girdle injuries** · **40–50% of shoulder injuries in sports** (OB)

**Demographics** — **♂ > ♀**, **athletes**, young

**AKA** — "shoulder separation" = disruption of AC ± CC ligaments

### Anatomy

**AC joint** — **diarthrodial**; medial acromion + lateral clavicle; oblique surface

**Intra-articular disc** — **fibrocartilaginous** (≈ knee meniscus); involutes, **gone by age 40**

**Motion** — mainly **gliding**; clavicle rotates **40–50° posteriorly** with elevation but only **~8° through AC joint** (synchronous scapuloclavicular motion)

**Dynamic stabilisers** — **anterior deltoid + trapezius** (deltotrapezial fascia)

### AC vs CC ligament function (horizontal vs vertical stability)

| Ligament | Controls | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **AC ligaments** (sup, inf, ant, post) | **Horizontal = anterior–posterior** stability | **Posterior + superior (posterosuperior) most important** → preserve in distal clavicle resection |
| **Conoid** (CC) | **Vertical = superior–inferior** | **Most important for vertical stability**; **conoid tubercle, posteromedial**; inserts **4.5 cm** medial to lateral clavicle edge |
| **Trapezoid** (CC) | Vertical | **Trapezoid tubercle, anterolateral**; inserts **3 cm** medial to lateral edge |
| Coracoacromial (CA) | — | **Spared** in AC injuries |

**✗ Ligament traps** — ✗ Wrong options: anterosuperior AC = vertical · conoid = horizontal · **inferior AC** = no significant horizontal role · trapezoid "most posteromedial" (that is conoid) · **anterior + inferior AC** most important · trapezoid at 4.5 cm

![AC, CA and CC (conoid, trapezoid) ligaments](../../MCQ/attachments/q214098-illa-ligaments-labeled.jpg)

**Normal CC distance** — **11–13 mm** (top of coracoid → undersurface of clavicle)

**AC joint type (TO)** — **atypical synovial** with fibrocartilaginous disc · **AC ligament = horizontal**, **CC = vertical** stability · conoid = medial/central, trapezoid = lateral · conoid–trapezoid gap **~1.5 cm** · CC distance **>25% increase = disruption** (TO)

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanism** — **direct blow to the shoulder** (fall onto **adducted** shoulder); bike falls, contact sport

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain over AC joint ± referred to **trapezius**; visible step-off

**Exam** — AC/lateral clavicle tenderness; abnormal contour vs other side

**Stability testing** — **horizontal (AP) = AC ligaments** → cross-body adduction; **vertical (sup–inf) = CC ligaments**

**Horizontal instability (ISAKOS IIIB)** — may need more aggressive treatment

**Type IV clue** — **skin tenting + posterior fullness**

**Type V clue** — **severe shoulder droop, not corrected by shrug**

**Type VI clue** — rare, associated injuries, **paraesthesias**

### Provocative tests

| Test | Technique | Positive = |
|---|---|---|
| **O'Brien (active compression)** | **Resisted flexion at 90° flexion, 10° adduction, full IR** | **Superficial pain → AC joint** · **deep pain → SLAP** |
| **Cross-body adduction** | Arm adducted across chest | AC joint pain |
| ✗ Wrong option: Jobe / empty can | Resisted abduction 90° scapular plane, pronated | Supraspinatus tear |
| ✗ Wrong option: apprehension | Supine abduction + ER | Anterior instability |
| ✗ Wrong option: belly press | Palm on abdomen, resisted IR | Subscapularis tear |
| ✗ Wrong option: Neer | Passive forward elevation in IR | Subacromial impingement |

**Persistent AC pain after nonop type III (6 wk)** — **O'Brien / cross-body adduction** → if pain + instability → **AC + CC reconstruction**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| View | Technique | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Bilateral AP AC joints** | **1/3 penetration** of standard shoulder AP | Compare **CC distance** with contralateral (III vs V) |
| **Axillary lateral** | — | **Required to diagnose type IV (posterior)** |
| **Zanca** | **10–15° cephalad tilt, 50% penetrance** | **Most accurate view of AC joint** |
| **Cross-body adduction (Basmania)** | Scapular Y with cross-body adduction stress | Horizontal stress view |
| **Alexander** | Scapular Y centred on AC joint, **15° cranial tilt** | **Posterior displacement** (also superior) |
| Weighted stress views | — | Rarely used; II vs III |
| CT | — | Degree/direction of displacement; often unnecessary |
| ✗ Wrong option: **Serendipity** | **40° cephalic tilt** | **SC joint** |
| ✗ Wrong option: Stryker notch · West Point · supraspinatus outlet · Velpeau | — | Not for AC joint |

**Penetration** — AC joint needs **1/3–1/2** of glenohumeral exposure → standard AP **over-penetrates (dark)** AC joint

**Zanca tilt** — **10–15°** (Orthobullets, OB) · **15°** (one OB explanation)

**TO views** — AP routine · **15° cephalic tilt (Zanca)** clears thoracic shadow · **stress view with 5 kg hanging** → subtle AC separation · axillary lateral → anterior/posterior displacement

### Rockwood classification & ligament injury pattern

| Type | AC lig | CC lig | Exam | Radiograph | Reducible | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | Sprain | Normal | Tender, **no instability** | Normal | Yes | Sling |
| **II** | **Torn** | **Sprain** | **Horizontal instability** | AC disrupted; **CC ↑ < 25%** | Yes | Sling |
| **III** | Torn | **Torn** | — | **CC ↑ 25–100%** | Yes | **Controversial** |
| **IIIA** (ISAKOS) | Torn | Torn | **Vertical instability, no horizontal** | CC ↑ 25–100% | Yes | — |
| **IIIB** (ISAKOS) | Torn | Torn | **Vertical + horizontal instability** | CC ↑ 25–100% | Yes | **May need more aggressive** |
| **IV** | Torn | Torn | **Skin tenting, posterior fullness** | Clavicle **posterior through trapezius** on **axillary** | **No** | Surgery |
| **V** | Torn | Torn | **Severe droop**, not improved by shrug | **CC ↑ > 100%** | **No** | Surgery |
| **VI** | Torn | Torn | Rare; associated injuries; paraesthesias | **Inferior** — **subacromial or subcoracoid**, tethered by **conjoint tendon** | **No** | Surgery |

![Rockwood classification types I–VI](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3047-illustration-rockwood-classification-2-moved.jpg)

**Type III CC threshold** — **25–100%** (Orthobullets, OB) · **20–100%** (one OB explanation) — read the options

**Type I trap** — written in one OB explanation as "AC sprain + CC sprain, no instability" (Orthobullets: CC normal)

**Types III–VI** — **both AC + CC torn**; IV–VI = **irreducible**

**Type V irreducible** — clavicle **buttonholed through deltotrapezial fascia** (most common culprit)

**✗ Interposed-structure traps** — conjoint tendon (coracoid tip) · CC ligaments (ruptured, not incarcerated) · CA ligament

**Type IV and trapezius** — clavicle **pierces trapezial fascia** → muscle innervated by **CN XI (spinal accessory)**

**✗ Nerve traps** — axillary (deltoid, teres minor) · CN VII (face, platysma) · pectoral nerves · suprascapular (SS, IS)

**ISAKOS consensus** — Rockwood misses nuances → added **biomechanical stability (IIIA vs IIIB)**

### Differential (mimics)

| Mimic | Distinguishing feature |
|---|---|
| **Base of coracoid fracture** | Superiorly displaced clavicle but **normal CC distance** (CC ligs intact) |
| **Distal clavicle fracture Neer 2A** | Ligaments stay on distal fragment; medial fragment displaces |
| **Distal clavicle fracture IIB** (OB) | Fracture **between conoid + trapezoid** → **only conoid** disrupted; distal fragment CC distance intact |
| Paediatric distal / medial clavicle physeal injury | Physis |

**AC separation vs IIB clavicle fracture** — AC separation = **conoid + trapezoid** both injured (CC ↑ 25–100%)

## 5 Management

```
AC joint injury (Rockwood)
├─ Type I–II → sling, ice, early ROM + PT
├─ Type III, most patients → nonop (good if displacement < 2 cm)
│      └─ laborer / elite athlete / cosmetic concern / failed nonop → surgery
└─ Type IV, V, VI (acute) → ORIF / CC reconstruction
```

### Low-grade injuries – sling & early motion

**TO nonop** — sling **3–4 wk** → early motion · **persistent pain at 2–3 months → distal clavicle excision (1–2.5 cm)** · type III **controversial**: nonop generally, **operative for young / athletes / manual workers** · IV–VI operative

**Nonop regimen** — brief sling (**2–4 wk** for I–III, OB), ice, activity modification, **early ROM**, PT; ± steroid injection

**Timeline (nonop)** — functional motion by **6 wk** · normal activity at **12 wk**

**20% ↑ CC distance** — = **type II** → **sling + early PT**\
✗ Wrong options: CC reconstruction · AC capsule repair · retrograde pinning · distal clavicle excision

**58-yo accountant, non-dominant, II vs III, no AP translation** — **sling + early ROM**\
✗ Wrong options: Weaver-Dunn · CC recon

**Nonop complications** — AC arthritis, chronic subluxation/instability

### Operative criteria

| Indication | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Acute type IV, V, VI** | Recent studies: no functional difference op vs nonop even for high grade (IV–V may be treated nonop, OB) |
| **Acute type III** | **Laborers, elite athletes, cosmetic concerns** |
| **Chronic type III** | **Failed nonop** |
| Contraindications | Poor rehab compliance; skin problems over approach |

**Timing** — acute stabilisation usually **within first 2 wk** (OB) · surgery **after 6 wk failed nonop = same outcome as immediate** (Orthobullets)

**Old teaching** — acute → ORIF, chronic → CC reconstruction (no longer supported)

### Type III injuries – operative vs nonoperative

| Outcome | Operative | Nonoperative |
|---|---|---|
| **Complications** | **Higher** (hardware commonest) | Lower |
| Return to work/sport | **Slower** | **Faster** |
| **Early function (6 wk, 3 mo)** | Worse | **Better** |
| Function / PROs / QoL at 1 yr | **Equal** | **Equal** |
| Return to athletics at 1 yr | Equal | Equal |
| **Cosmesis** | **Better** (only consistent benefit) | Poorer |
| **Radiographic alignment** | **Better** (hook plate, COTS RCT) | Worse |
| Disability at 2 yr | **Both improve significantly** | **Both improve significantly** |
| ROM / strength / endurance | No difference | No difference |
| Secondary surgery | Higher (hardware removal) | Lower |
| Cost | Higher | Lower |

**Early-score wording** — nonop "higher DASH at 6 wk & 3 mo" (Orthobullets; ↑ DASH = worse) · nonop **better** early scores/function at 6 wk (OB) — read the options

**Spencer systematic review** — surgery **not better**, **higher complication rate**, **longer recovery**

**Canadian (COTS) RCT** — hook plate: **better radiographic alignment**, **not clinically superior**; nonop better early scores

### Surgical techniques

| Technique | Pros | Cons / complications |
|---|---|---|
| **CC screw (Bosworth)** — clavicle → coracoid, sup → inf | Rigid | Out of favour; screw too long → critical structures; **routine removal 8–12 wk** (breakage from clavicle–scapula motion); **failure at coracoid thread purchase** |
| **CC suture fixation** — around/through clavicle + around coracoid base (or anchors) | **No hardware failure/migration** | Weaker than screw; **careful passage under coracoid** (NV structures); **suture erosion → distal clavicle fracture** |
| **AC pin (Phemister)** — wire across AC joint | Percutaneous | **High pin migration** (catastrophic); rarely done |
| **Hook plate** | Rigid | **2nd surgery** for removal; **acromial erosion**, hook pullout |
| **Modified Weaver-Dunn** — **distal clavicle excision + CA ligament transfer** to clavicle | — | **CA ligament only 20% as strong** as CC; failure without internal fixation |
| **Free tendon graft CC reconstruction** — figure-of-8 around coracoid, clavicle tunnels | **Closest to native CC strength** | Graft morbidity; needs internal fixation backup |
| Cortical flip button (Dog Bone) ± arthroscopy | — | — |

**Grafts** — autograft **palmaris longus, semitendinosus** · allograft **tibialis anterior**

![Hook plate fixation](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3047-hook-plate.jpg)

**Post-op rehab** — sling **6 wk, no ROM** · full activity at **6 months**

| Technique (TO) | Concept |
|---|---|
| Phemister K-wire | **Obsolete** (migration) |
| **Hook plate** | Hook **under posterior acromion** (anterior placement → impingement) · **remove 8–10 wk** |
| Bosworth screw | CC screw · **remove 8–10 wk** (vs 8–12 wk above) |
| **TightRope / Endobutton** | FiberWire suspensory CC fixation keeping **~1.2 cm** CC distance |
| Suture anchor | Coracoid + clavicle tunnels at **3 cm (trapezoid) + 4.5 cm (conoid)** |
| Weaver-Dunn | CA ligament transfer + distal clavicle excision — chronic |
| **Hamstring autograft / Mazzocca** | **Anatomic conoid + trapezoid reconstruction → gold standard for chronic** (TO) |

### AC joint cyst (Geyser sign) (TO)

**Entity (TO)** — **synovial cyst of AC joint**; uncommon; elderly; chronic shoulder pain, ↓ ROM, large lump, cosmetic concern

| Type (TO) | Cause | Treatment (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **Type 1** | **AC joint chronic arthropathy** | **Excision + lateral clavicle resection** |
| **Type 2** (**most common**) | **Chronic rotator cuff tear** | Mild/moderate pain + reparable cuff → **distal clavicle resection + cuff repair** · severe pain + irreparable cuff (elderly) → **shoulder arthroplasty (RSA > hemi)** |

**Mechanism (TO)** — chronic cuff tear → **superior humeral head migration** → erodes **inferior AC capsule** → fluid escapes into AC joint + subcutaneous tissue

**Geyser sign (TO)** — fluid jet through AC joint on **MRI / arthrogram / USG**; MRI reliably shows cyst type

(✗ Wrong options: **Distractors (TO)** — AC exostosis) · SLAP type 4 · biceps sheath GCT

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Residual AC pain** | **30–50%** |
| **AC arthritis** | **More common after surgery** than nonop (nonop: no articular contact → no arthritic symptoms; surgery: cartilage injury + incongruity) |
| **Hardware failure** | CC screw breakage/pullout **at coracoid** |
| **Pin migration** | AC pinning (Phemister) |
| **Coracoid fracture** | **Coracoid tunnel drilling** |
| **Distal clavicle fracture** | Suture erosion |
| Acromial erosion | Hook plate |
| Neurological injury | **Musculocutaneous nerve** closest to coracoid (✗ Wrong options: axillary (runs at inferior subscapularis, distant)) |

### Surgical complications (Weaver-Dunn AP translation, AC arthritis)

**Failed modified Weaver-Dunn** — persistent symptoms from **anterior–posterior (horizontal) translation**\
✗ Wrong options: sup–inf translation · inadequate resection · over-reduction · SC instability

**Prevention** — **anatomic** repair/reconstruction **preserving distal clavicle** → less horizontal instability

**CC screw statement traps** — ✗ Wrong options: "more migration than AC pinning" · "fails at clavicle" (coracoid) · "avoid removal" (remove at 8–12 wk) · "axillary nerve"

## How it's asked

**"Type V on X-ray — ligaments torn?"** → **AC + CC**\
✗ Wrong options: SC · CA

**"CC distance 13 mm contralateral, type III — which labelled ligaments torn?"** → **AC + conoid + trapezoid**\
✗ Wrong options: CA ligament (spared)

**"Type II AC injury?"** → **AC torn, CC sprain, horizontal instability**\
✗ Wrong options: no instability = type I · CC torn = III

**"IIB distal clavicle fracture vs AC separation?"** → **fracture = conoid only; AC separation = conoid + trapezoid**

**"Posterior clavicle displacement after fall over handlebars?"** → **type IV**

**"Type V, irreducible, skin tenting — interposed structure?"** → **deltotrapezial fascia**\
✗ Wrong options: conjoint · CC · CA

**"Type IV, posterior fullness — nerve to muscle involved?"** → **CN XI** (trapezius)

**"Most important for horizontal stability?"** → **posterosuperior AC ligament**

**"Most important for vertical stability?"** → **conoid**

**"Trapezoid inserts how far from lateral clavicle?"** → **3 cm** (conoid 4.5 cm)

**"Best extra view for AC joint?"** → **Zanca**\
✗ Wrong options: Stryker · West Point · outlet · Velpeau · Serendipity = SC

**"Type III — surgery vs nonop?"** → **higher complication rate** with surgery

**"Type III non-athlete — only benefit of fixation?"** → **improved cosmesis**

**"Type III, 19-yo wants surgery — true?"** → **surgery ↑ complications + nonop faster return**\
✗ Wrong options: better function

**"Type III nonop vs op?"** → **nonop better function at 6 wk**; equal at 1 yr

**"Hook plate RCT — true?"** → **both groups improve significantly by 2 yr**\
✗ Wrong options: op faster return · op worse cosmesis · equal alignment

**"Type III nonop, still painful at 6 wk — exam?"** → **O'Brien: resisted flexion at 90° flexion, 10° adduction, full IR**

**"20% ↑ CC distance, symptomatic — treatment?"** → **sling + early PT**

**"58-yo accountant, non-dominant AC separation, no AP translation?"** → **sling + early ROM**

**"Persistent symptoms after modified Weaver-Dunn?"** → **anterior–posterior translation**

**"CC screw fixation — true post-op?"** → **AC arthritis more likely than nonop**

**"Elderly, large shoulder lump, MRI Geyser sign?"** → **AC joint cyst** (TO)

**"Commonest AC cyst type?"** → **type 2 — chronic cuff tear** (TO)

**"Geyser sign, severe pain, irreparable cuff, elderly?"** → **arthroplasty (RSA > hemi)** (TO)

**"Correct hook-plate position?"** → **hook under the posterior acromion** (anterior → impingement) (TO)

**"Gold standard for chronic AC dislocation?"** → **anatomic CC tendon-graft reconstruction** (TO)
