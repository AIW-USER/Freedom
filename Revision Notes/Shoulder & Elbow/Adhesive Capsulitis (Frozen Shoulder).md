# Adhesive Capsulitis (Frozen Shoulder)

*Shoulder & Elbow · 4 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Prevalence (TO)** — **2–5%** of population · incidence in **diabetics 10–36%**

**Demographics** — **♀ > ♂**, **40–60 y**; **nondominant** arm most often; **minorities > Caucasians**\
✗ Wrong options: Caucasian ↑ risk

**Bilateral** — **age <50** and **diabetes** → ↑ risk of bilateral disease; **sequential, not simultaneous**

### Risk factors & associations (diabetes, thyroid disease)

**Risk factors (TO)** — **female**, **age >40**, **preceding trauma**, **HLA-B27 positivity**, **prolonged immobilisation** of GH joint

**Associations (TO)** — **diabetes**, **thyroid disease**, **cerebrovascular disease**, **coronary artery disease**, **autoimmune disease**, **Dupuytren's**

**Other associations** — atherosclerotic disease, **cervical disc disease**, **history of MI**, **Crohn's**, **breast cancer treatment / axillary dissection**, previous surgery

**Diabetes (both types)** — **most common association**; stiffness may be **first manifestation of DM** → work up · often **bilateral** · **worse outcomes regardless of treatment** (nonop + surgical)

**Insulin-dependent (TO)** — **more severe** limitation + **more resistant** to nonop than non-insulin diabetics

**Risk ↑ within diabetics** — **older age (>50)** · **duration of DM** · **autonomic neuropathy** · **history of MI**

**Not risk factors in diabetics** — **type of diabetes** (↑ in **both type I and II**) · diabetic regimen (insulin vs non-insulin) · glycaemic control

**Thyroid** — autoimmune aetiology; **hypothyroidism** (↑ TSH) classic

**✗ Distractors** — **menopause** (no association) · **steroid use** (steroids treat it) · epilepsy · renal disease · prior dislocations

### Genes & lab markers

| Test | Finding in AC | ✗ Distractor meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **TSH** | **↑** (hypothyroidism) | ↑ **free T4** = hyperthyroidism (wrong option) |
| **HbA1c** (hemoglobin A1C) | **↑** (diabetes) | — |
| **HLA-B27 (TO)** | Positivity = risk factor | — |
| Uric acid | Normal | ↑ = gout |
| ESR / CRP | Normal | ↑ = infection / inflammatory arthropathy |
| RF / anti-CCP | Normal | ↑ = RA |
| ANA | Normal | + = lupus |

**Workup** — metabolic panel + endocrine labs (**TSH, HbA1c**) before choosing treatment

### Pathophysiology – fibroblastic proliferation & reduced capsular volume

**Forms** — **primary/idiopathic** (most) · **post-traumatic** (proximal humerus fracture, immobilisation) · **post-surgical** (RCR, axillary dissection)

**Pathoanatomy** — inflammation (subsynovial layer, TO) → **fibroblastic proliferation** of capsule → thickening, fibrosis, adherence of capsule to itself + humerus → **mechanical block** · **↓ intracapsular volume**

**Cell type** — **fibroblasts / myofibroblasts**\
✗ Wrong options: adipocytes · granulocytes · lymphocytes · monocytes

**Collagen** — abundant **type III collagen**

**Essential lesion** — **coracohumeral ligament (CHL)** + **rotator interval capsule**

**Why ER lost first (TO)** — changes start in **subsynovial layer of capsule in rotator interval**

**✗ Wrong options (other processes)** — isolated posterior capsular tightness = **GIRD** · ↓ blood supply = **AVN** · degenerative tendon tear = **cuff tear** (active loss only)

### Rotator interval anatomy

| Boundary | Structure |
|---|---|
| **Superior** | **Anterior edge of supraspinatus** |
| **Inferior** | **Superior edge of subscapularis** |
| Medial (base) | **Coracoid process** |
| Lateral (apex) | **Transverse humeral ligament** |
| **Contents** | **LHB tendon, SGHL, CHL, RI capsule** |

**✗ RI traps** — LHB = content · AIGHL + **axillary pouch** lie **inferior** to RI · MGHL is not the inferior border (subscapularis is)\
✗ Wrong options: superior border

**RI clinical** — **closed** for instability · **contracted** in AC → **released**

![Rotator interval borders and contents](../../MCQ/attachments/q2556-illa-rotatorinterval.jpg)

### Capsuloligamentous restraints

| Structure | Restraint | Release effect |
|---|---|---|
| **RI (SGHL + CHL + capsule)** | **Inferior translation + ER in adduction** | ↑ **ER at side** + inferior translation |
| **SGHL** | **Inferior translation at 0° abduction** | — |
| **MGHL** | **Anterior (± posterior) translation at ~45° abduction** + ER | ↑ anterior translation at 45° |
| **AB-IGHL** | **Anterior + inferior translation at 90° abduction + max ER** | ↑ anteroinferior translation at 90° |
| **Posterior capsule** | IR + **cross-body adduction** | ↑ **IR + cross-body adduction** |

**Normal role** — check reins at extremes of motion; IGHL complex = anterior band + axillary fold + posterior band

## 3 Presentation

### Clinical diagnosis – loss of active and passive motion

**Symptoms** — **insidious** diffuse pain **preceding** loss of motion · **night pain / difficulty sleeping** · ADL difficulty (reaching overhead)

**Exam** — **symmetric loss of both active AND passive ROM** · **tethered endpoint** · pain throughout arc or at terminal motion (stage-dependent)

**First movement lost (TO)** — **external rotation**\
✗ Wrong options: IR · flexion · abduction

**Most common + most sensitive** — **passive ER deficit vs contralateral** shoulder

**Provocative tests** — impingement, biceps, SLAP often **positive** (false); cuff testing limited by stiffness

**Diagnosis** — **clinical** (normal X-ray + painful active + passive loss); MRI not necessary

| ✗ Wrong option: exam pattern | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| Active < passive (e.g. active FF 100°, passive 150°) | **Weakness** — cuff tear |
| Pain 0–30° active abduction, full passive | Supraspinatus tendinosis/tear |
| Empty can + pain/weakness, full passive | Rotator cuff tear |
| Speed's +, full ROM | Biceps tendinopathy |
| Isolated IR loss (posterior capsule) | GIRD |

### Clinical stages

| Stage | Features | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Freezing / painful** | Gradual diffuse pain at rest + motion, night pain | **6 wk – 9 mo** |
| **2 Frozen / stiff** | Pain lessens; ↓ ROM affecting ADLs | **4 – 9 mo or more** |
| **3 Thawing / resolution** | Pain gone; gradual return of motion (less than normal) | **5 – 26 mo** |

**Total duration** — self-limited; average **3–6 mo**, up to **18 mo – 2 y** (OB) — read the options

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray views** — **AP in neutral rotation**, **scapular-Y**, **axillary lateral** (± AP in IR/ER) · **mandatory** to exclude **OA, posterior dislocation, hardware**

**X-ray finding** — normal or **disuse osteopenia**; no high-riding head

**MRI (± arthrography)** — not needed for diagnosis; excludes other pathology · **loss of axillary recess** (capsular contracture) · thickened CHL

**MR arthrogram** — **↓ intracapsular volume**

| MRI sign of AC (TO) | Value |
|---|---|
| Axillary recess thickening | **≥1.3 cm** (TO) |
| **Anterior capsule thickening** | **>3.5 mm** + hyperintensity |
| Synovial thickness | **>4 mm** |
| RI | Soft tissue thickening + signal change; scarring |
| Biceps anchor | Encased by abnormal soft tissue |
| **CHL** | **Thickened** |
| **Subcoracoid triangle sign** | **Obliteration of fat** in subcoracoid space |
| Inferior capsular fold | **Obliterated**; capsule hypervascular |

**NOT seen (TO)** — **widening** of axillary pouch (pouch is contracted/obliterated)

**CHL on MRI** — thickened (Orthobullets) · **not better visualised** than normal (OB wrong option) — read the options

**Incidental partial cuff tear** — common in **asymptomatic >60 y** → still treat AC with stretching

![Coronal MRI, loss of axillary recess](../../MCQ/attachments/q4266-illa-coronalmrit2frozen.jpg)

| ✗ MRI distractor | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| Fluid extravasation down humerus | **HAGL** |
| Hypertrophied synovium, papillary projections | **PVNS** |
| Massive retracted tear | Cuff tear |

### Arthroscopic stages

| Stage | Finding |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Patchy, fibrinous synovitis** |
| 2 | **Capsular contraction + fibrinous adhesions** |
| 3 | Increasing contraction, **synovitis resolving** |
| 4 | **Severe contraction** |

## 5 Management

```
Adhesive capsulitis
├─ Early (freezing) → reassurance + gentle stretching ± IA steroid + NSAIDs
│      (treat diabetes / thyroid)
├─ Failed nonop (3–6 mo) → MUA and/or arthroscopic capsular release
│      └─ diabetic / post cuff or labral repair → release (MUA 50% fail)
└─ Residual IR / cross-body loss → add posterior capsular release
```

### Nonoperative management – stretching, injection & natural history

**First-line** — **PT + NSAIDs ± IA steroid injection** ± heat/cryotherapy; often effective, **majority** succeed

**PT program** — **gentle, pain-free stretching + moist heat**; supervised; **3–6 mo**; stress **motion, not strengthening**

**Home exercise program** — **as helpful as organised therapy**

**IA steroid** — ↓ inflammation → allows stretching; **earlier** return of ROM, **no long-term difference** (OB, TO) · stage II → **IA steroid + stretching**

**PT detail (TO)** — stretching then strengthening = mainstay **regardless of stage**; **avoid abduction initially** (impingement)

(✗ Wrong options: **Wrong early** — **sling** (↑ contracture, prolonged recovery)) · **immediate aggressive therapy** (counterproductive unless pain controlled) · **PRP** (no evidence) · MUA / arthroscopy before conservative trial · cuff strengthening

**Distension arthrography** — rarely performed

**Natural history** — **self-limited**; most resolve without surgery

**Most common outcome after nonop stretching** — **decreased range of motion vs contralateral shoulder**\
✗ Wrong options: limiting pain · recurrence (low) · surgery (rare, **>90% satisfied**)

### Stretching exercise mechanics

**ER wand exercise (arm adducted, elbow flexed)** — stretches **anterior-superior capsule, SGHL, CHL, IGHL** · **LEAST stretch on posterior capsule**

**Most commonly contracted** — **anterior-superior capsule (RI)** → limits **ER in adduction**

### Operative criteria

| Factor | Threshold / rule |
|---|---|
| Failed therapy before release | **3 mo** (Orthobullets) · **3–6 mo** (OB) · **≥6 mo** (OB) · **4–6 mo** compliant therapy (TO) — read the options |
| Indication | **Progressive loss of motion** despite prolonged PT |
| MUA contraindication | **Diabetics (50% failure)** · **after RCR or labral repair** · freezing phase (controversial) |
| Arthroscopy (TO) | Reserve for **recalcitrant** cases (✗ Wrong options: RCT superiority over MUA) |

### Manipulation under anaesthesia

**Technique** — **document ROM** first · **full muscle paralysis** · indwelling regional catheter · **steady force** · **early PT**

**FEAR sequence (TO)** — **F**lexion, **E**xtension, **A**bduction, adduction, external + internal **R**otation

**Post-MUA (TO)** — **20 ml 0.5% bupivacaine + corticosteroid** IA

**Results (TO)** — **up to 94%** subjective relief, sustained long term

### Arthroscopic capsular release – structures & outcomes

**Portals** — slightly **higher** than normal (contracted capsule)

**Rotator interval release** — from **anterior biceps to superior subscapularis** → CHL visualised + released → restores **ER at side**

**Released** — **SGHL, MGHL, IGHL, CHL** (circumferential) · **not coracoacromial ligament** (= landmark for complete RI release)

**Posterior capsular release** — ↑ **IR + cross-body adduction**

**Subacromial** — **bursectomy + adhesion release**; **no acromioplasty**

**Sequence** — MUA before or after release; TO: release before manipulation allows inspection + precise capsulotomy; **anterior release always ± posterior** (posterior: early benefit, similar outcome)

**Advantage over open** — **spares subscapularis**; releases intra-articular + subacromial adhesions

**Not done** — completion + repair of small partial tear (that is for **>6 mm or 50%**) · **capsular plication** (instability) · **distal clavicle resection** (AC arthritis)

**Outcome** — gains in ROM + function **maintained at long-term follow-up**

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Detail / prevention |
|---|---|
| **Residual / recurrent stiffness** | **Most common** after release → **immediate ROM + PT**, **no sling**, ± CPM, regional analgesia |
| **Axillary nerve injury** | **Inferior capsular release** → stay **close to glenoid rim**; rare |
| Infection, instability, impingement | Less common |
| **MUA (overzealous, osteoporotic bone)** | **Proximal humerus fracture**, dislocation, **cuff + labral tears**, **brachial plexus palsy** |

**Prognosis** — self-limited · **worse in diabetics**

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Active ROM | ↓ | ↓ | ↓ IR | ↓ |
| Passive ROM | **↓ (= active)** | **Normal** | ↓ IR only | ↓ |
| First / key loss | **ER** | Strength (weakness) | **IR** (posterior capsule) | — |
