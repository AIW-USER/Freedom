# Posterior Labral Tear

*Shoulder & Elbow · 35 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — **reverse Bankart** lesion / attenuation of posterior capsulolabral complex

**Frequency** — relatively **rare** vs anterior labral pathology

**Athletes** — **weightlifters (bench press)** · **football linemen (blocking)** · **wrestlers** · swimmers · gymnasts · **baseball batters**

### Anatomy

**Posterior labrum** — **fibrocartilage** · cavity-compression · **50% of socket depth** · **posterior stability** · **anchors PIGHL**

**Static restraints to posterior translation (flexion, adduction, IR)** — **PIGHL**, augmented by **SGHL**

| ✗ Distractor | Actual restraint role |
|---|---|
| Supraspinatus | **Dynamic**; prevents **inferior** instability |
| MGHL | Static; anterior + posterior at **45° abduction** |
| Subscapularis | **Dynamic**; posterior translation in **ER** |
| AIGHL | Static; **anterior** at **90° abduction + ER** |

**Most important factors in posterior instability (Bäcker)** — **capsular lesions + IGHL rupture**

## 2 Mechanism

### Mechanism & provocative position (flexion–adduction–internal rotation)

**Most common mechanism** — **repetitive microtrauma** to posterior capsulolabral complex

**Provocative position** — **posteriorly directed force** with arm **flexed, ADducted, internally rotated** (net posterior vector)

**✗ Wrong option: flexion + ABduction + ER** — anterior instability → **anteroinferior** labral tear

**Batter's shoulder** — **acute posterior subluxation** on **adducted lead shoulder** while swinging a bat → acute posterior labral tear\
✗ Wrong options: recurrent anterior events · anabolic steroids

**Posterior capsular tightening** — → **GIRD**\
✗ Wrong options: cause of batter's shoulder

**Glenoid retroversion** — **~17° → 6× more likely** posterior instability vs ~7°

**Kim lesion** — **incomplete, sometimes concealed avulsion of posteroinferior labrum**

**First-time posterior dislocation** — **less likely to recur** than first-time anterior

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **vague, deep, nonspecific posterior shoulder pain** (most common) · **↓ strength** (bench press, **batting power**) · clicking/popping · instability less common

**Aggravated by** — posterior force: **pushing heavy doors, bench press, push-ups, blocking, grappling**

**Throwing pain** — **late cocking** (Orthobullets) · late cocking/early acceleration = **anterior** instability (OB) — read the options

**Exam** — posterior joint-line tenderness; **full ROM**; large patients hard to examine

### Diagnosis – jerk/Kim tests & MRI

| Test | Technique | Positive / meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Posterior apprehension** | **90° flexion + adduction**, stabilise scapula anteriorly, push humerus posteriorly | Pain / instability |
| **Posterior load and shift** | Arm at side; posterior force on proximal humerus | Graded translation (below) |
| **Jerk (Jahnke) test** | **90° abduction + full IR** (arm flexed, IR), **axial load while adducting across body** (push elbow posteriorly, scapula anteriorly) | **Clunk** = head subluxes off posterior glenoid → **posterior labral tear** |
| **Kim test** | **90° abduction + 45° forward flexion**, axial load on elbow, **posteroinferior** force | **Pain** → **posteroinferior labral tear** (most sensitive) |
| Push-pull test | (OB) | Pain from posterior labrum |

**Jerk/Kim accuracy** — **highly sensitive + specific** (Orthobullets) · sensitivity/specificity **"under question"** (OB) — read the options

![Kim test: abduction with forward flexion, axial + posteroinferior force](../../MCQ/attachments/q213968-illa-kim-test.jpg)

| Load-and-shift grade | Translation |
|---|---|
| 0 | None |
| 1 | To **edge** of glenoid |
| 2 | **Over edge**, **spontaneously relocates** |
| 3 | Over edge, **does not relocate** |

| ✗ Exam distractor | Indicates |
|---|---|
| Forward elevation in scapular plane | **Impingement** |
| **ER + abduction** | **Anterior apprehension** |
| Flexion + abduction | Nonspecific |
| O'Brien active compression | SLAP / labrum |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **true AP + scapular Y + axillary**; **axillary required** (posterior dislocation missed on AP alone); often **normal**

**Chronic X-ray (axillary)** — **glenoid retroversion** · **posterior glenoid erosion / bone loss**

**MRI** — **study of choice**; **intra-articular contrast (MR arthrogram)** ↑ sensitivity · alternative **CT arthrogram**

**MR arthrography for labrum** — **sensitivity 89%, specificity 91%, accuracy 90%** (Bencardino); standard MRI often misses labral lesions → **next step after failed nonop + labral test**\
✗ Wrong options: diagnostic arthroscopy · CT · stress views

**SLAP on MRA** — coronal oblique: deep cleft between superior labrum + glenoid extending below biceps anchor

**Incidental** — posterior labral tears common on MRI of **asymptomatic throwers** → treat only if history + exam fit

![Axial MR arthrogram: posterior labral tear](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3057-posterior-labral-tear.jpg)

| ✗ Diagnosis distractor (bodybuilder, MRI arthrogram) | |
|---|---|
| Pectoralis major rupture · supraspinatus PT tear · SLAP · LHB tendinitis | Not on MRI |

## 5 Management

```
Posterior labral tear
├─ First line → activity mod + NSAIDs + PT
│      (cuff + deltoid strengthening, periscapular stabilisation)
└─ Failed extensive nonop / competitive athlete → arthroscopic repair + capsulorrhaphy
       ├─ posterior glenoid bone loss → address bone (augmentation)
       └─ excess retroversion → ± corrective osteotomy
```

### Treatment – therapy vs arthroscopic repair, recurrence & postoperative restrictions

**Nonop** — **first line**; cuff + deltoid strengthening, **periscapular stabilisation**

**Thrower with anterior follow-through pain, end-of-game, full ROM, small posterior labral contrast** — **rotator cuff deconditioning** → **PT with throwing program**\
✗ Wrong options: labral repair · debridement · capsular release · IR stretching (ROM is full)

**Operative indication** — **failed extensive nonop** (or competitive athletes)

**Arthroscopic > open** — avoids extensive posterior dissection; **more reliable return to play**

**Suture anchors + capsulorrhaphy** — **fewer recurrences + revisions** than non-anchored repair

**Probe posterior labrum** — to rule out subtle **Kim lesion**

**Post-op** — **avoid adduction + internal rotation** (stress posterior repair) → failure risk ↑ if not avoided

| Post-op restriction | Repair |
|---|---|
| **Avoid adduction + IR** | **Posterior labrum** |
| Avoid ER | **Subscapularis**, **anterior labrum** |
| No restriction (stiffness risk) | Calcific tendinitis decompression, distal clavicle resection |

**Outcome** — generally good; **overhead throwers** return less reproducibly

### Posterior glenoid bone loss

| Posterior glenoid bone loss | Failure of isolated capsulolabral repair |
|---|---|
| **11%** | **10.4×** |
| **15%** | **24.4×** |

**"Sub-critical" bone loss** — even small posterior loss → **recurrence after isolated arthroscopic repair**\
✗ Wrong options: "not critical, no bony procedure"

**Glenoid width vs version** — **width more predictive** of recurrence than version

**Obligate translation** — tightening posterior tissues → anterior translation = **therapeutic**\
✗ Wrong options: causes anterior instability

**Corrective osteotomy** — for excessive retroversion; **does not obviate bone block** when bone loss present

## 6 Complications

### Complications – axillary nerve injury

**Axillary nerve (posterior branch)** — within **1 mm of inferior capsule + glenoid rim** → injured by **suture passage at posteroinferior glenoid**

**Posterior branch gives** — **superolateral brachial cutaneous** nerve + **nerve to teres minor** → **lateral arm burning/numbness** + **teres minor ER weakness**

**Overtightening posterior capsule** — **anterior subluxation** or **coracoid impingement**

**Recurrence** — ↑ if **posterior bone loss** not addressed

## X vs Y

| | **Posterior labral tear (reverse Bankart)** | **Anterior labral tear (Bankart)** |
|---|---|---|
| Position | **Flexion + adduction + IR**, posterior force | **Abduction + ER**, anterior force |
| Who | Linemen, lifters, wrestlers, batters | — |
| Painful activity | **Bench press, push-ups, blocking** | Late cocking, spiking, overhead serve |
| Test | **Jerk, Kim, load-and-shift** | Apprehension, relocation |
| Ligament | **PIGHL** (+ SGHL) | **AIGHL** |
| Post-op avoid | **Adduction + IR** | **ER** |

| | **Jerk test** | **Kim test** |
|---|---|---|
| Position | 90° abduction + IR, adduct across body with axial load | 90° abduction + 45° flexion, axial + posteroinferior force |
| Positive | **Clunk** | **Pain** |
| Best for | **Posterior** labral tear | **Posteroinferior** labral tear |

## How it's asked

**"Bodybuilder, vague deep pain, weak bench press, MR arthrogram — etiology?"** → **posterior labral tear**\
✗ Wrong options: pec major · SLAP · LHB tendinitis

**"Old 'pop', never reduced, axial MRI posterior labrum — painful activity?"** → **heavy bench press**\
✗ Wrong options: late cocking · volleyball spike = anterior · curl = SLAP

**"Wrestler, + Kim + jerk — structure augmenting PIGHL in flexion-adduction-IR?"** → **superior glenohumeral ligament**\
✗ Wrong options: MGHL · AIGHL · subscapularis

**"Wrestler, posterior labral tear MRI — position increasing incidence?"** → **flexion, ADduction, internal rotation**

**"Baseball player, ↓ batting power, + Kim/jerk — etiology?"** → **acute posterior subluxation while batting**\
✗ Wrong options: posterior capsular tightening = GIRD

**"Ex-pitcher, failed nonop, + O'Brien — next diagnostic step?"** → **MRI-arthrography**\
✗ Wrong options: diagnostic arthroscopy · CT

**"Lineman, pain with blocking, axial MRI — manoeuvre reproducing pain?"** → **flexion, adduction, internal rotation**\
✗ Wrong options: ER + abduction = apprehension

**"Flexed IR arm pushed posteriorly, girdle anteriorly, clunk — injured structure?"** → **posterior labrum (C)** (jerk test)

**"Quarterback, anterior pain in follow-through, end of game, small posterior labral contrast — initial?"** → **PT with throwing program**

**"Post-op: avoid adduction + IR — initial diagnosis?"** → **posterior labral tear (Figure C)**

**"NFL tackle, posterior glenoid bone loss on CT — true?"** → **capsulolabral repair alone may lead to recurrence**\
✗ Wrong options: obligate anterior instability · version more predictive than width · osteotomy obviates bone block

**"Burning lateral arm pain after arthroscopic posterior labral repair — nerve?"** → **axillary**\
✗ Wrong options: suprascapular · radial
