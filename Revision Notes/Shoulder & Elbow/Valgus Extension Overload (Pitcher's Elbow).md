# Valgus Extension Overload (Pitcher's Elbow)

*Shoulder & Elbow · 60 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — **posteromedial elbow pain** from repetitive microtrauma in **throwing athletes**

**Prevalence** — up to **65%** of elite baseball players with symptomatic elbows

**Who** — **dominant arm** of throwers (pitchers, quarterbacks) · also swimmers, volleyball, gymnasts, racquet sports, golfers

**Location** — **posteromedial olecranon** (✓ label D) · posterior trochlea · olecranon fossa

**Association** — **cubital tunnel syndrome ~25%**

## 2 Mechanism

**Forces of pitching** — **medial tension (MCL)** · **lateral radiocapitellar compression** · **posterior extension overload** · **shear on medial olecranon tip + fossa**

**Phases (OB)** — **valgus stress in acceleration** (esp. with **UCL insufficiency**) + **extension impaction in deceleration**

**Medial laxity** — any cause → **compensatory ↑ stress posteromedially** → osteophytes / loose bodies

**Sequence** — repetitive impaction of olecranon into fossa → **chondromalacia/cartilage injury** → **osteophytes (posteromedial olecranon + humerus)** → **loose bodies** (fragmentation) · **capitellar osteochondral lesions** · **UCL attenuation** with repetitive strain

## 3 Presentation

### Diagnosis – posteromedial olecranon osteophytes, examination & throwing phase

**Pain** — **posteromedial**, at **full extension**; **deceleration / follow-through** (✓; after ball release) · rarely acceleration\
✗ Wrong options: wind-up, early/late cocking

**Function** — **loss of terminal extension** (osteophytes block olecranon seating) · ↓ accuracy · pain after ~25 pitches

**Exam** — **tender posteromedial olecranon** · **flexion contracture** (e.g. 10°) · **crepitus** (loose bodies, synovitis) · **pain with forced extension**

**Provocative tests** — **arm bar test** (sustained extension) · **bounce test** (repeated terminal extension)

**UCL assessment** — check competency (failure → valgus instability); moving valgus stress may be painful **without instability** in VEO

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Finding |
|---|---|
| **X-ray AP, lateral, oblique** | **Posteromedial olecranon osteophyte** (AP key; medial olecranon) · loose bodies (capitellar fragmentation) · **MCL calcification** · **humeral hypertrophy** (↓ room in fossa) |
| **CT** | **Best osseous detail**; 3D for planning |
| **MRI** | Not needed for diagnosis · **associated MCL tear** (partial/complete) · capitellar OCD |

**Diagnosis** — **clinical** (posteromedial pain ↑ with extension) + imaging of posteromedial osteophyte

## 5 Management

```
Valgus extension overload
├─ First episode → NSAIDs + active rest 10–14 d → graduated throwing
├─ Repeat episode → active rest 4–6 wk → lengthened return
├─ Fails nonop / ↓ performance → arthroscopic osteophyte + loose body excision
│      └─ ulnar nerve symptoms / subluxing → ± decompression / transposition
└─ MCL insufficiency → not olecranon debridement alone (address UCL)
```

### Nonoperative

**First line** — NSAIDs, **stop throwing**, correct mechanics, ± steroid injection (**paucity of data**) · also **mid-season** or **end of career**

**PT** — **flexor-pronator strengthening** (✓ next step) · shoulder, forearm + kinetic chain

| Patient | Active rest | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **First-time symptomatic** | **10–14 days** | Graduated throwing |
| **Repeat symptomatic** | **4–6 weeks** | Lengthened gradual return |

### Operative criteria

| Factor | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Indication** | **Persistent symptoms after failed (extended) nonop** · diminished performance |
| **Relative contraindication** | **MCL insufficiency** (for olecranon debridement alone) |
| **Ulnar nerve release / transposition** | **Significant ulnar symptoms** · nerve **subluxes** over medial epicondyle (transposition for symptomatic unstable nerve) |

### Arthroscopic osteophyte resection

**Preferred** — arthroscopic (✓) or open **cheilectomy**; generally **excellent results** if no over-resection

**Position** — lateral decubitus, prone or supine (**supine + articulating holder** if combined with **MCL reconstruction**)

**Steps** — mark ulnar nerve course · diagnostic arthroscopy, remove loose bodies · **posterolateral viewing portal** · **direct posterior portal** (through triceps) · clear fibrous tissue (RF + shaver) · free fractured osteophyte (switch portals → remove via posterolateral) · **contour to native margin only** · **arthroscopic valgus stress test** (medial gapping = incompetent MCL)

**Also** — chondromalacia debridement/drilling · lateral meniscoid lesion / posterolateral plica

### Excessive olecranon resection – unmasking UCL insufficiency

**Rule** — remove **osteophyte only, not native olecranon** → else **loss of bony restraint → ↑ MCL strain + valgus angulation**

**Threshold** — **≥9 mm** resection → **UCL rupture** under valgus (cadaveric; Orthobullets, OB) · **>3 mm** / past native margin → ↑ MCL stress + valgus instability (Orthobullets complications) — read the options

**Result** — **valgus elbow instability** (✓) · **MCL damaged**\
✗ Wrong options: LUCL · RCL · annular · arcuate

**Presentation** — after debridement: **medial pain (late cocking)**, laxity + pain on valgus stress, **positive milking manoeuvre**, **negative arm bar**, new UCL tear on MRI

**✗ Failure-cause distractors** — accelerated return-to-throw · lateral instability (rare) · posterior bundle (dominant UCL structure = **anterior band of anterior bundle**) · anatomic variant · ulnar neuritis · capitellar OCD · olecranon stress fracture

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Prevention |
|---|---|
| **Valgus instability** | Avoid over-resection (see thresholds) |
| **Ulnar nerve injury** | Mark nerve before medial portals · **"nick and spread"** · limit/remove **shaver suction** in posteromedial gutter |

## X vs Y

| | **VEO** | **UCL injury** | **Medial epicondylitis** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain | **Posteromedial** | **Medial** | Medial, **5–10 mm distal to epicondyle** |
| Phase | **Deceleration / follow-through** | **Acceleration** (late cocking) | |
| Test | **Arm bar, bounce**; crepitus | **Valgus stress at 20–30°**, **milking manoeuvre**, moving valgus stress | **Resisted flexion/pronation** |
| X-ray | **Posteromedial osteophyte** | No fracture, **no posteromedial osteophyte** | No VEO osteophytes |
| Contracture | **Yes** | | Unlikely |

| Differential | Key feature |
|---|---|
| **Acute UCL rupture** (pop, ↓ velocity, MRA full-thickness) | **UCL reconstruction** in competitive thrower (✗ Wrong options: isolated ulnar transposition · osteophyte excision without osteophyte) |
| Partial UCL tear, non-thrower / low-risk sport | Nonop: activity modification + PT |
| **MCL sprain** (recreational thrower) | Medial pain **only when throwing**, ↓ speed + distance, no instability, normal X-ray |
| **PLRI** | Lateral; **pivot shift, chair rise**; LUCL; dislocation or lateral epicondylitis debridement |
| **Capitellar OCD** | Lateral pain, swelling, **catching/locking** |
| Ulnar nerve subluxation | Palpable in flexion; neuritis to hand |
| Pronator syndrome | **Volar forearm** pain on resisted pronation; not throwing-related |
| Medial epicondyle avulsion | **Skeletally immature** |
| Olecranon stress fracture | **Posterior** pain + swelling; lateral X-ray/MRI |
| ✗ Wrong option: varus extension overload | Distractor — **valgus** EO (not varus) mimics MCL injury |

## How it's asked

**"Typical location of osteophytes in VEO?"** → **posteromedial olecranon (D)**

**"Pitcher, posteromedial pain after ball release, normal milking — diagnosis + next step?"** → **VEO; PT with flexor-pronator strengthening**\
✗ Wrong options: UCL reconstruction · PRP · LUCL reconstruction · extension splint

**"Pitcher, 10° flexion contracture, posteromedial tenderness, no instability?"** → **valgus extension overload**\
✗ Wrong options: medial epicondylitis · olecranon stress fracture · trochlear OCD · epicondyle avulsion

**"Exam findings in VEO?"** → **posteromedial pain, crepitus, flexion contracture**\
✗ Wrong options: pivot shift · distal epicondyle tenderness · lateral locking

**"Throwing phase most painful in VEO?"** → **follow-through / deceleration**\
✗ Wrong options: acceleration · late cocking

**"Excessive normal olecranon resection → on return to play?"** → **valgus elbow instability**

**"Valgus instability 9 mo after osteophyte removal — structure?"** → **medial collateral ligament**\
✗ Wrong options: LUCL · annular

**"Medial pain in late cocking + valgus laxity after olecranon debridement?"** → **excessive olecranon resection**

**"New UCL tear after VEO arthroscopy + accelerated rehab — cause?"** → **excessive olecranon resection**\
✗ Wrong options: accelerated protocol · posterior bundle

**"Pitcher, pop, full-thickness UCL tear on MRA, wants to return?"** → **UCL reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: ulnar transposition · osteophyte excision

**"Recreational thrower, medial pain only throwing, ↓ distance, normal X-ray?"** → **MCL sprain**\
✗ Wrong options: ulnar neuritis · pronator syndrome · medial epicondylitis · varus extension overload
