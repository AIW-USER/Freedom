# Reverse Shoulder Arthroplasty

*Shoulder & Elbow · 38 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Concept & history

**Design** — **convex glenoid hemisphere (glenosphere)** + **concave humeral cup** (semiconstrained)

**History** — early reverse designs failed (**COR too lateral** → torque → glenoid loosening) · **Grammont 1985** = first successful reverse (TO) · popular in Europe; North America since **1990**

**Grammont's 2 innovations (OB)** — **large glenoid hemisphere with no neck** + **small, almost horizontal humeral cup at 155°** (non-anatomic valgus) covering **< half the glenosphere**

**Indications summary** — **cuff tear arthropathy (CTA)**, comminuted **4-part proximal humerus fractures**, **failed arthroplasty**

**TO indications** — CTA · irreparable cuff + arthritis · fracture with **tuberosity nonunion** · failed TSA/HA · **RA with cuff involvement** · prerequisites: **functional deltoid + adequate bone stock** · contraindications: active infection, **deltoid paralysis**, poor bone stock, neurological disorder

## 2 Mechanism

### Biomechanics – medialised/inferior centre of rotation & deltoid tensioning

**COR shift** — **medial + inferior (distal)** (✓ TO, OB)\
✗ Wrong options: superior · lateral · ↑ lateral humeral offset · posterior · proximal

**Standard prosthesis without cuff (TO)** — deltoid contraction → humeral head **subluxes superiorly**; reverse **corrects the abnormal vector** and re-creates a **fulcrum**

**Medial COR** — ↑ **deltoid moment arm** (less force for same torque) · ↓ **torque/shear at glenoid interface** (✓ glenoid stability; force vector passes through component) · ↓ articular shear stress

**Distal COR** — **lengthens/tensions deltoid** (↑ resting length + tone) · humerus lowered relative to acromion · recruits **anterior + posterior deltoid** as abductors

| COR shift (TO) | Effect |
|---|---|
| **10 mm medial** | ↑ deltoid moment **~20%** |
| **10 mm distal** | ↑ deltoid efficacy **~30%** |

**Grammont advantages (OB)** — **medialisation of COR + lengthening deltoid + ↑ deltoid moment arm**\
✗ Wrong options: lateralisation · shortening deltoid

**Matched radius of curvature** — glenosphere + humeral cup → **stable fixed fulcrum** for elevation; shear converted to **compression**

**Motions gained** — **forward elevation + abduction** ↑ · **IR, ER (at side or at 90°), adduction** not significantly improved → **latissimus dorsi transfer** for ER\
✗ Wrong options: not normal

**↑ deltoid moment (OB)** — **place glenosphere more inferiorly** (✓) · anything moving COR medial/inferior or lengthening deltoid\
✗ Wrong options: constrained poly (no effect) · short stem (no effect) · **smaller NSA (varus) shortens deltoid**

## 3 Presentation

**Pseudoparalysis** — **inability to actively elevate arm** with **free passive ROM**, **no neurological lesion**; irreparable cuff tear (± GH arthritis)

**Pseudoparalysis without arthritis (TO)** — loss of fulcrum when cuff fails → head translates superiorly → deltoid loses lever · **first line = rehabilitation** (recruit accessory muscles) → **RSA only after PT fails**

**Anterosuperior escape** — **incompetent coracoacromial arch** → humeral head escapes into subcutaneous tissue (esp. after **HA + acromioplasty**); **anterosuperior bulging** on attempted elevation

**Failed HA (cuff failure)** — pseudoparalysis, lateral deltoid pain, **no crepitus**, near-normal passive ROM · weakness of ER at side\
✗ Wrong options: infection → passive ROM also ↓

| Scapular-Y | |

**Hamada ≥3** — **acetabularisation of acromion**\

## 5 Management

```
Massive irreparable cuff tear ± GH arthritis
├─ Balanced shoulder (isolated SST tear + arthritis) → unconstrained TSA
├─ Full active elevation retained → no RSA
├─ Pseudoparalysis, no arthritis → PT first
│      └─ PT fails → RSA
└─ CTA / pseudoparalysis, elderly, working deltoid → RSA
       └─ also no active ER (abducted) → RSA + latissimus dorsi transfer
```

```
Failed hemiarthroplasty / aTSA
├─ Infection signs → 2-stage revision
├─ Cuff failure / anterosuperior escape → convert to RSA
│      (not RCR, aTSA or tendon transfer — poor results)
└─ Intractable pain / persistent infection → resection arthroplasty
```

### Indications – cuff tear arthropathy, pseudoparalysis, failed arthroplasty or cuff repair, fracture

| Indication | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cuff tear arthropathy** | Severe GH arthritis + **superior escape** + massive cuff tear; **best results** |
| **Pseudoparalysis** | Irreparable cuff tear (± arthritis, TO) |
| **Anterosuperior escape** | Incompetent CA arch |
| **Proximal humerus fracture in elderly** | **3- or 4-part, age >70** · head-splitting · osteopenia (GT poor healing) |
| **Cuff insufficiency "equivalent"** | **Tuberosity nonunion/malunion** after trauma or arthroplasty |
| **Failed arthroplasty** | Prior HA or aTSA with **cuff insufficiency**; options exhausted |
| **Failed cuff repairs** | Irreparable tear after ≥2 repairs |
| **Rheumatoid arthritis** | **Only if glenoid bone stock sufficient** |
| Others (OB) | Proximal humeral tumours, B2 glenoid |

**Patient factors** — **low demand** · **physiological age >70** ("exclusively >70 y", OKU 9) · **sufficient glenoid bone stock** · **working deltoid (intact axillary nerve)**

**Not ideal** — **40-y labourer** (glenoid loosening in young/active) · 40-y painful malunion · arthritis with **active overhead motion** · intact cuff → **aTSA** · 30-y locked posterior dislocation

**Not indications (TO)** — **massive cuff tear + arthritis with full active elevation** · **isolated supraspinatus tear + arthritis** → balanced → **unconstrained TSA**

**Revision options — wrong (OB)** — revision SST repair after 2 failed (doesn't address subscap) · **HA in subscap + SST insufficiency → instability + anterosuperior escape** · **latissimus transfer = younger, no arthritis** · cortisone/PT (doesn't restore function)

**HA best results** — marked glenoid bone loss, younger, **RA with forward elevation >90°**

**HA failure reasons** — **progressive glenoid degeneration + cuff deficiency** (✓ cuff failure; not advanced glenoid wear when pseudoparalytic without crepitus)

**Instability after HA** — elderly fall → **rotator cuff injury** → **convert to RSA**\
✗ Wrong options: labrum

**Humeral stem loosening** — most commonly linked to **infection**

**Sirveaux (OB)** — 80 shoulders: **96% no/minimal pain**, 3 revisions; ↑ forward elevation at 44 mo

### Proximal humerus fracture – RSA vs alternatives

| Option | Elderly 3/4-part fracture |
|---|---|
| **Nonoperative** | **Majority** of geriatric fractures |
| **rTSA** | **Best surgical option** in osteoporotic elderly; **better outcomes + lower reoperation than HA**; doesn't rely on tuberosity healing |
| HA | Outcome **depends on tuberosity healing** (cuff function) |
| ORIF | High demand + expected tuberosity healing; elderly → **= nonop function, ↑ complications** |
| IMN | 2/3-part; ≈ ORIF; **high failure in osteoporosis** |
| PHR | **Last resort** — no bone stock; failed revisions, tumour |

### Fracture RSA – greater tuberosity healing

**GT fixation** — ↑ **forward elevation + ER** (✓ OB); **anatomic GT repair** → ↑ ER, function, satisfaction vs resection/malunion · not mandatory (deltoid still powers elevation)

**No effect on outcome** — press-fit vs cemented stem · inlay stem · LTO (LT already fractured) · suprascapular decompression

**Humeral height in fracture** — calcar/tuberosity; if calcar missing → **pectoralis major insertion 5.6 cm** below top of humeral head

| **C5–C6 root avulsion · posterior cord palsy** | → complete deltoid palsy |
| **Acromion deficiency** | Contraindication (Orthobullets) · **relative** (OB) |
| **Glenoid osteoporosis / insufficient bone stock** | |
| **Active infection** (glenoid osteomyelitis) | |
| Complete brachial plexus injury · prior arthrodesis | |
| Severely impaired deltoid · isolated SST tear · full active elevation (TO) | |

**NOT contraindications** — **RA** (sufficient glenoid stock; excellent short/midterm results) · **pseudoparalysis** (= indication) · **prior infection** (if not active) · **prior partial acromioplasty** · **absent GH joint-space narrowing** · **age >70** · **incompetent CA arch** (= indication)

**Deltoid wasting + scapular dysrhythmia** — **not a candidate**\
✗ Wrong options: "candidate due to CTA" · "not candidate due to age/massive tear"

| Factor | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Deltoid | **Functioning** (intact axillary nerve) |
| Age | **Physiological >70**, low demand |
| Glenoid | **Sufficient bone stock** |
| Cuff | Irreparable / deficient (pseudoparalysis) |

### Approach

| | **Deltopectoral** | **Anterosuperior (superolateral)** |
|---|---|---|
| Deltoid | **Preserved** | **Anterior deltoid split/violated** |
| Subscapularis | **Take-down needed** | **Preserved** |
| Glenoid | **Inferior pole exposed** (✓ baseplate position) | ↑ exposure; **risk excess height / superior tilt** |
| Instability | Extensive capsular release → ↑ instability (TO: ↑ vs superolateral) | ↓ (anterior stabilisers kept) |
| Axillary nerve | **↓ risk** | **↑ risk** (distal branches) |
| Notching | Not associated | **↑ notching** (insufficiently inferior baseplate) |
| Other | Extensile; **LD transfer possible**; poor posterior glenoid access; stiffness (immobilise for subscap) | Easier humeral axial prep + **GT fixation** |

### Technique

**Humerus** — osteotomy **0–30° retroversion (typically 20°)**; more retroversion → ↑ post-op ER · LHB tenotomy/tenodesis · ream + broach as TSA\
✗ Wrong options: no effect on notching

**Glenoid** — excise labrum, **circumferential capsular release**, expose **inferior glenoid** subperiosteally · central guidewire by best bone stock · **baseplate as inferior as possible + inferior tilt** (↓ loosening + notching) · navigation

**Screws** — **superior → coracoid base**, **inferior → scapular body** · unicortical screws to coracoid may ↓ acromial fracture; 2 screws may suffice

**TO technique** — deltopectoral · humeral cut **30° retroversion, 45° inclination** · concave poly liner · **metaglene baseplate with 10–15° inferior tilt** → glenosphere · **4 parts**: stem, poly liner, metaglene, glenosphere · inferior tilt ↓ notching + ↑ longevity

**Glenosphere size** — **36 mm** females/smaller · **40 mm** large men

**Newer prostheses (OB)** — **locking peripheral screws + 15° inferior tilt** · NSA **155° → 143° or 135°** → ↓ notching, ↓ instability, ↓ need for subscap repair

### Humeral component design (inlay vs onlay)

| | **Inlay** (Grammont) | **Onlay** |
|---|---|---|
| Stem | Straight, horizontal inlay tray | Curved |
| Bone | **More metaphyseal reaming** → **↑ GT fracture risk**; ↑ bony contact | **Preserves proximal bone** |
| Cut | | **More varus cut** → preserves GT, **minimises cuff damage** |
| Offset | | **↑ humeral offset / lateralisation** |
| ROM | | **↑ adduction, extension, ER** |
| Conversion | | **Convertible to/from HA + aTSA** |
| Scapular spine fracture | **↓ risk** (✓ prevention, OB) | **↑ risk** (humeral lateralisation) |

**TO trap** — "onlay has more **valgus** cut and more cuff damage" = **FALSE** (onlay = **varus** cut, less damage)

### Latissimus dorsi transfer with RSA

**Indication** — **loss of active elevation + ER** → RSA restores elevation, **LD restores ER**; primary or revision (✓ **failed HA, no active ER with arm abducted**)

**✗ Wrong options** — deltoid dysfunction (RSA contraindicated) · brachial plexus injury/arthrodesis · CTA with **active ER >50°** (posterior cuff functioning → RSA or CTA head alone) · AVN + partial infraspinatus tear (nonop → resurfacing) · OA with 10° retroversion (TSA, ream anterior glenoid)

### Rehabilitation

**Early** — sling; passive / active-assisted only

**Sling duration** — **3 wk if subscap NOT repaired** · **6 wk if repaired**; limit passive ER + active IR

**Avoid** — **pushing out of chair** (extension + adduction + IR) · TO: avoid these **8–12 wk**

**Early ROM** — not ↑ dislocation vs 6 wk immobilisation (OB)

**Subscap re-tear** — **anterior instability** → **early exploration + repair**

### Outcomes

**Best indication** — **CTA** (failed arthroplasty = **highest complications**)

**Deterioration** — radiographic after **6 y**, clinical after **8 y**

**Survivorship** — **~90% at 10 y**

### Surgeon learning curve & postoperative surveillance

**Learning curve** — early complications ↓ after **18–45 cases** (✓ 40; ✗ Wrong options: 10, 75, 100, 200)

**Asymptomatic notching** — **observe, routine follow-up with serial X-rays** (✓) · (✗ Wrong options: aspirate for C. acnes · sling for acromial fracture · revise baseplate) · IR to **L5 = excellent** for RSA

## 6 Complications

### Overview – which is "most common"

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| **Scapular notching** = most common (radiographic) complication | OB, TO, Orthobullets incidence |
| **Dislocation** = most common complication | OB (2 questions) — read the options |
| **Instability/dislocation** = most common cause of **early failure / early revision** | Orthobullets, TO, OB |
| **Glenoid loosening** = most common **mechanism of failure / long-term** complication | Orthobullets, TO |
| **Glenoid loosening/fracture** = most common after **revision RSA** | OB |

**TO ranking (early)** — **notching > instability > acromion/scapular spine fracture > infection > axillary neurapraxia**; overall complication rate **~15%**

**Complications ∝ indication** — highest for **failed shoulder arthroplasty** (dislocation, loosening, ↓ function); low for CTA / failed RCR

### Scapular notching – superior baseplate, neck-shaft angle & lateralisation

**Definition** — erosion of **axillary border of inferior scapular neck** (scapular pillar) from **medial rim of humeral cup in adduction**; unique to RSA

**Incidence** — **44–96%** Grammont (Orthobullets) · **51–96%** (OB) · **68%** (Lévigne, OB) · **62%** (Lévigne, 326 pts, OB); appears early (**1–2 y**), variable progression

**TO notching** — **40–90%** · humeral poly impinges on scapular neck in adduction · risk: medialised COR, superior placement/tilt · prevention: **inferior tilt 10–15°**, inferior baseplate, **larger glenosphere (↑ lateral offset)** — mnemonic **"lateralise & inferiorise"** · Nerot–Sirveaux: 1 scapular neck only → 2 up to inferior screw → 3 beyond inferior screw → 4 baseplate loosening (TO)

**TO complications** — instability (soft-tissue tensioning) · infection (thin soft-tissue cover) · **weak ER from teres minor deficiency** → combine **LD transfer with RSA if teres minor deficient** · **acromial stress fracture** (deltoid over-tensioning)

**Most common technical error** — **superior placement of glenoid component** (✓) → **inferior** scapular notching\
✗ Wrong options: "superior notching"

| ↑ Notching | ↓ Notching (protective) |
|---|---|
| **Superior baseplate placement** (upper half) | **Inferior placement / inferior overhang** — **greatest reduction**, least adverse effect |
| **Superior tilt** of glenosphere | **Inferior tilt** (more even force, impingement-free arc) |
| **155° NSA** (Grammont; medialises humeral component) | **Varus NSA (135°)** — **most important factor** (Gutiérrez) |
| **Medialised COR** / medialised glenosphere | **Lateralised offset** (bone/metal augment) — **protective** (✓ TO "all except") |
| **Superior glenoid erosion (E2)** → base plate in valgus | **Larger glenosphere** (OB) |
| **Anterosuperior / superolateral approach** | ↓ **poly depth** (↑ ROM, ↓ notching) |
| ↓ **scapular neck length** · CTA with narrow AHD · **infraspinatus FI grade 3/4** · high BMI · ↑ activity · duration | |

**Gutiérrez (sawbones)** — **NSA** = largest effect on **inferior impingement** → then glenosphere position → lateral offset → glenosphere size · **COR offset** = largest effect on **ROM** · **15° inferior tilt** → most uniform compression, least micromotion

**No effect on notching** — humeral retroversion · stem length · horizontal humeral cut · retentive liner · cemented stem · locking screws · over-reaming

**Source conflicts** — **inferior/posterior tilt**: protective (Orthobullets, TO, most OB) · **minimal effect, may ↑ notching** (OB7681; low position = biggest effect) · **glenosphere size**: larger ↓ notching (OB) · **no effect** (OB214573) — read the options

**Consequences** — **↓ ROM, strength, Constant scores** (✓ with preop superior erosion, Lévigne) · "not linked to detrimental outcomes" (same OB item) · particulate debris → osteolysis → **implant failure**; association with **baseplate failure** · rarely needs revision · not ↑ dislocation · not ↑ aseptic loosening

**Baseplate lateralisation** — ↓ notching but **↑ baseplate loosening** (more stress)

| Sirveaux grade | Extent |
|---|---|
| 0 | No notch |
| 1 | **Limited to scapular pillar** |
| 2 | **Contact with inferior screw** of baseplate |
| 3 | **Beyond inferior screw** |
| 4 | **Under baseplate, approaching central peg** |

### Instability/dislocation – risk factors & positions to avoid

**Incidence** — **2–3.4%** (Orthobullets) · **3.4% avg, 2–14%** (TO) · **2–4%** (OB) · **2–3%** (OB) · **1.5–31%** (OB) — read the options

**Direction (TO)** — **exclusively anterior / anterolateral**

**Position** — **extension + adduction + internal rotation** (pushing out of chair, tucking shirt) · TO keys: **adduction + extension** and **adduction + IR** · not abduction + ER (native shoulder; deltoid "wrap effect" stabilises RSA)

**✗ Position distractors** — flexion + abduction (RCR) · flexion + IR + adduction (**posterior labral repair**) · ER + abduction (**anterior labral repair**) · IR at 90° abduction (face/mouth hygiene)

| Risk factor | Note |
|---|---|
| **Irreparable / inadequate subscapularis** | **Strongest** (Orthobullets); ↑ with medialised prosthesis (OB) · **less of an issue with newer implants** (OB4801) — read the options |
| **Proximal humeral bone loss** / loss of tuberosities | |
| **Failed prior arthroplasty / revision** | **Most significant** in revision vignette |
| **Proximal humeral nonunion / malunion** | Tuberosity failure |
| **Fixed preop GH dislocation** | |
| **Male sex** · **BMI >30 / obesity** | (✗ Wrong options: female) |
| Previous shoulder surgery (open, extensive capsulotomy) | Not arthroscopic / mini-open biceps tenodesis |
| Axillary nerve injury · Grammont-style implant | |
| Deltopectoral (vs superolateral) · subscap **FI > Goutallier 3** (TO) | |
| **Inadequate humeral length (↓ compression)** → shallow socket | **Main biomechanical** parameters (TO) |
| ✗ Wrong option: **rheumatoid arthritis** | Not a risk factor |

**Stability determinants (TO)** — soft-tissue balance (humeral length), glenosphere size, humeral joint-line inclination, humeral version, metaglene position

**Prevention** — **lateralise baseplate/glenosphere** (✓) · ↑ glenosphere size · **eccentric baseplate** · lateralise/distalise humeral component · **constrained implant**\
✗ Wrong options: valgus stem (medialises humerus, ↓ deltoid wrapping) · subscap tenotomy without repair

**Treatment** — **immediate closed reduction + sling** (OB) · CR can be tried but **redislocation usual → open reduction + correct the surgical error** (TO) — read the options

**✗ Dislocation treatment distractors** — EMG (deltoid weakness) · I&D + poly exchange (**acute infection**) · I&D + antibiotic spacer (**chronic infection**) · ORIF (periprosthetic fracture)

├─ Closed reduction successful → immobilise in abduction 4–6 wk
├─ CR fails → open reduction
│      ├─ soft-tissue laxity → thicker PE ± metal spacer ± retentive PE ± glenosphere change
│      └─ adequate tension → immobilise in abduction 4–6 wk

```

```
Recurrent RSA instability – cause
├─ ↑ ESR/CRP + positive aspirate → <3 mo: I&D + PE exchange · >3 mo: 1- or 2-stage revision
├─ Humeral shortening
│      ├─ <15 mm → thicker PE ± metal spacer ± retentive PE
│      └─ >15 mm → humeral revision ± proximal humeral allograft
├─ Humeral medialisation
│      ├─ <15 mm → larger glenosphere ± ↑ lateral offset

### Acromial & scapular spine stress fractures

**Incidence** — **4%** (Orthobullets, OB) · **4–5%** / **~5%** (OB) · **4–8%** (OB) · **10.2%** with **lateralised glenosphere** (Levy, TO)

**✓ Glenoid** — **universally stable glenoid implant** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: "fall on abducted shoulder"\
✗ Wrong options: loose glenoid

| Risk factor | Source |
|---|---|
| **Female sex** (up to 100% of series) · age 70–80 | Orthobullets, OB, TO |
| **Osteoporosis** | ✓ OB |
| **Medialised preop COR** (✓ OB) → **RA** (central wear) | Orthobullets, OB |
| **Superior baseplate screw** (✓ TO) · posterior screw | TO, OB |

### Periprosthetic fracture after a fall

**Fall 8 mo post-RSA → acromial fracture** — ✓ **osteoporosis is a risk factor**\
✗ Wrong options: incidence 15% (≈5%) · chronic pain likely · nonunion <5% nonop · equal revision rate

### Infection & complications after revision

**Incidence** — **1–2%** deep infection; large **subacromial dead space**

**Organisms** — **C. acnes + staphylococci** · C. acnes → **vancomycin + clindamycin**

**Risk factors** — **younger age (<65) + male = greatest** (✓ 45-y male RSA) · **primary reverse > aTSA** · **arthroplasty for trauma** · failed arthroplasty\
✗ Wrong options: BMI/obesity, race, ASA, smoking, RA, diabetes

**Treatment** — **2-stage revision = gold standard** · acute → I&D + poly exchange · chronic → I&D + antibiotic spacer

**Revision RSA** — complication rate **11–36%** after failed arthroplasty; **glenoid loosening/fracture ↑ (~25% at 5 y)** (✓) — high metal : small glenoid bone ratio; **periprosthetic glenoid** fractures commonest · also dislocation/recurrent instability\
✗ Wrong options: humerus

**Glenoid loosening treatment** — **staged**: fill glenoid with **autograft** + **hemiarthroplasty** → await incorporation → new glenosphere

### Nerve injury during glenoid preparation

**Most commonly injured** — **axillary nerve** (✓ label B)\
✗ Wrong options: radial (A), musculocutaneous (C), median (D), ulnar (E)

**Neurapraxia** — **0.5–1%**; risks **anterosuperior approach** (deltoid retraction/levering) + **humeral lengthening** · direct laceration during glenoid implantation · **usually transient → observe**

**Lädermann** — subclinical EMG changes (mainly axillary) at 3.6 wk; **8 of 9 resolved <6 mo**; arm lengthening implicated

### Baseplate failure – central peg error

**Scenario** — RSA + **iliac crest graft behind baseplate** → early baseplate migration → **central peg error** (peg in graft, not native glenoid; peg too short) (✓)\
✗ Wrong options: tilt · dissociation · small glenosphere (larger = instability option)

**Glenosphere dissociation** — rare; intra-op failure to engage glenosphere on baseplate
