# Shoulder Hemiarthroplasty

*Shoulder & Elbow · 47 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Definition

**Hemiarthroplasty (HA)** — humeral articular surface replaced with **stemmed humeral component**; glenoid left native

**Most common indication** — GH arthritis with **inadequate glenoid bone stock** for TSA

**Mechanics** — relies on **rotator cuff compression** for motion + strength; **CA arch** = barrier to proximal migration

**Use** — less common since RSA; still for young patients/laborers, CTA, osteonecrosis, complex proximal humerus fractures

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Use |
|---|---|
| **True (Grashey) AP** | **30–40° oblique** to coronal plane; extent of DJD, fracture pattern |
| **Axillary** | **Posterior glenoid wear**; fracture displacement |
| **CT** | **Glenoid version + glenoid bone stock**; unclear fracture pattern |
| **MRI** | **Rotator cuff** status |

**↓ Acromiohumeral distance** — superior head migration → **cuff deficiency** → evaluate cuff (MRI / CT arthrogram)

## 5 Management

### Indications & limitations (cuff integrity, osteonecrosis, CTA head, ream-and-run)

| Indication | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Primary arthritis** | **Cuff deficient** · glenoid stock inadequate · **high glenoid loosening risk (young, active laborers)** |
| **Rotator cuff arthropathy** | **HA > rTSA if forward flexion > 90°** |
| **Osteonecrosis** | **Without glenoid involvement** |
| **Proximal humerus fracture** | 3-part with poor bone · **4-part** · **head-split** · major articular destruction |

| Contraindication | Note |
|---|---|
| Infection | — |
| **Neuropathic (Charcot) joint** | e.g. syrinx |
| Unmotivated patient | — |
| **CA ligament deficiency** | CA arch + cuff deficient → **superior (anterosuperior) escape** |
| RA · capsulorrhaphy arthropathy | Listed as contraindications (one OB) |

**RA / capsulorrhaphy arthropathy** — contraindication (OB) · RA results "satisfactory in most, ↓ ROM" (TO) · ream-and-run works in capsulorrhaphy arthropathy (OB) — read the options

### Outcomes by diagnosis

**Best results (TO)** — **humeral head AVN / osteonecrosis**: consistent pain relief in **90%**, near-normal ROM

**Less good (TO)** — **RA, OA, glenoid dysplasia, post-traumatic arthrosis**: satisfactory in most, **↓ ROM**

**Most influential factor on function** — **status of the rotator cuff**\
✗ Wrong options: CA ligament · glenoid wear · inferior osteophyte · AC arthritis

**Irreparable cuff** — **TSA contraindicated** → HA (limited function) or **RSA**

**Fractures** — **excellent pain relief** in majority; outcome **inversely proportional to age + time from injury to surgery**

### HA vs TSA for GH OA

| Statement | Truth |
|---|---|
| **HA outcomes with eccentric glenoid wear** | **Inferior to TSA** ✓ |
| HA function overall | Inferior to TSA |
| HA revision rate | **Equal or greater** than TSA (mid–long term); higher with eccentric wear |
| HA outcomes eccentric vs concentric | **Worse with eccentric** wear |
| Glenoid | **Progressive erosion** may occur |

### Extended (CTA) head

**Theoretic advantage** — **metal-to-bone articulation with the acromion** → greater arc of smooth metal contact with glenoid + acromion

**Evidence** — unproven; results **comparable to standard HA**; **no other biomechanical advantage**\
✗ Wrong options: superior stability · fixed fulcrum · ↑ deltoid moment arm · ↑ offset

### Ream-and-run

**Definition** — HA + **concentric glenoid reaming** (**non-prosthetic glenoid arthroplasty**) with **nubbed spherical reamer** → single smooth concavity

**Advantage** — avoids **prosthetic glenoid failure** → **activity beyond that recommended for TSA**

**Ideal patient** — **young, active, motivated** (e.g. 42-yo weightlifter)

**Works in** — OA, **capsulorrhaphy arthropathy**, **post-traumatic arthritis**\
✗ Wrong options: contraindicated

**Rehab** — **therapy is critical**\
✗ Wrong options: "TSA needs more therapy"

**Outcomes** — excellent 2-year functional + radiographic results

### Technique

**Approach** — **deltopectoral**

**Head cut** — start at **medial insertion line of supraspinatus**

### Humeral stem version

| Version | Result |
|---|---|
| **30° retroversion** ✓ (broach + stem, TO) | **Ideal**; lateral fin **slightly posterior to bicipital groove** |
| **Excess anteversion** | **Anterior dislocation** |
| **Excess retroversion** (e.g. 45°, TO) | **Posterior dislocation** (✗ Wrong options: anterior instability) |
| ✗ Wrong options: neutral · 10° anteversion · neutral–10° retroversion | Wrong (TO) |

**45° retroversion (TO)** — FNB 2024 item, answer not provided by source; options: anterior instability · limit IR · best function · shaft fracture

### Height, head size & fixation

**Greater tuberosity height** — **7–8 mm below top of head** (Orthobullets) · **6–8 mm** (OB) — read the options

**Why** — maintain **cuff + biceps tension**; recreate **medial calcar** contour

**Height landmark** — top of head → **upper border of pectoralis major = 56 mm**; cement prosthesis proud

**Head size** — contralateral X-ray or **measure resected head**; too large → **overstuffing**

**Fixation** — **cemented = standard of care** (better QoL, ROM, strength vs uncemented)

### Tuberosity malposition after fracture hemiarthroplasty

**Importance** — **tuberosity migration = commonest cause of failure** in fracture HA; non-anatomic placement → variable outcomes, **rotation deficit**

**Technique** — secure tuberosities **to each other + to shaft**; **autograft from resected head** → ↓ pull-off, ↑ healing

| Malposition | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **GT too proximal** (above head) | **GT impingement on acromion** in abduction |
| GT too distal | **Over-tensioned cuff**, stiffness, ↓ motion |
| **LT too lateral** (close to GT) | **↑ Subscapularis tension → ER deficit** |

**✗ GT position traps** — does **not** change nonunion risk · does **not** cause overstuffing

![Tuberosity fixation around hemiarthroplasty](../../MCQ/attachments/q216885-illa-hemi-repair.jpg)

### Rehab

**Fracture HA** — **early passive motion 6–8 wk** until healed → **strengthening once tuberosity healed**

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Detail | Consequence / treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Progressive glenoid arthrosis** | Young, active | **Conversion to TSA** |
| **Tuberosity displacement / malunion** | Commonest in fracture HA | Reposition + bone graft |
| **Overstuffing** | Head too large | → **stiffness**, **accelerated glenoid arthritis** |
| **Subcutaneous (anterosuperior) escape** | **Cuff + CA arch deficient** | **RSA > anatomic TSA** |

![Anterosuperior escape after hemiarthroplasty](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3074-anterosuperior-escape.jpg)

### Failed hemiarthroplasty – glenoid erosion & conversion to TSA/RSA

```
Painful hemiarthroplasty
├─ Infection excluded? (WBC 1900, 20% PMN, cultures −ve 3 wk) → aseptic
├─ Intact cuff + glenoid wear → keep stem, revise head + add glenoid (TSA)
└─ Pseudoparalysis + superior escape → convert to RSA
```

**Glenoid arthritis, intact cuff, well-fixed stem** — **modular**: retain stem, **revise head + prosthetic glenoid**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · two-stage spacer · explant stem · RSA

**Cuff failure + superior escape (pseudoparalysis)** — **conversion to RSA**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI (cuff failure implied) · cuff repair + TSA · fusion if glenoid stock good · revision HA · PT

**Why not TSA for cuff-deficient failed HA** — poor function, **continued superior migration**, **glenoid loosening (rocking-horse)**

**Cuff arthropathy after HA** — bony erosion, superior head migration, **acromion + AC joint erosion**

## X vs Y

| | **Hemiarthroplasty** | **Anatomic TSA** | **Reverse TSA** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glenoid | Native | Prosthetic | Prosthetic (reverse) |
| Cuff needed | **Yes** | **Yes** | No |
| Best for | **AVN, 4-part fracture**, young/laborer, poor glenoid stock | OA, **eccentric glenoid wear** | Cuff deficiency, **superior escape** |

## How it's asked

**"Best results of hemiarthroplasty?"** → **humeral head AVN** (TO)

**"Recommended stem position?"** → **30° retroversion** (TO)

**"Lesser tuberosity placed too close to GT — problem?"** → **ER deficit** (subscap over-tension)

**"GT above superior head after fracture HA — risk?"** → **tuberosity impingement**\
✗ Wrong options: nonunion · overstuffing

**"Function after HA or TSA most affected by?"** → **rotator cuff integrity**

**"Extended CTA head advantage?"** → **metal-to-bone articulation with acromion**

**"66-yo — hemiarthroplasty most indicated for?"** → **humeral head osteonecrosis**\
✗ Wrong options: Charcot · CA ligament deficiency · RA · capsulorrhaphy arthropathy

**"42-yo with GH OA — HA vs TSA?"** → **HA inferior with eccentric glenoid wear**

**"42-yo weightlifter — ream-and-run?"** → **allows activity beyond TSA limits**

**"Painful HA 10 y, intact cuff, aseptic aspirate — next?"** → **revise head + prosthetic glenoid, retain stem**

**"Pseudoparalysis + superior escape after fracture HA?"** → **convert to RSA**

**"Contraindication to HA?"** → **CA ligament deficiency** (superior escape)
