# Little Leaguer's Shoulder

*Shoulder & Elbow · 24 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Definition

**Little Leaguer's shoulder (LLS)** — **proximal humeral epiphysiolysis** = overuse **Salter-Harris type I** stress injury of proximal humeral physis

**✗ Wrong option: SH II** — LLS does **not** produce a Salter-Harris II fracture

### Epidemiology

**Who** — **skeletally immature overhead athletes**: **youth baseball pitchers** (dominant arm); occasionally **tennis**, football, racket sports

**Sex** — **♂ > ♀**

**Age** — **11–16 y** (Orthobullets, OB) · **11–14 y**, peak proximal physeal growth (OB)

**Throwing** — **10%** of paediatric shoulder pain is throwing-related

### Physeal zone affected

**Zone** — **hypertrophic zone** = **weakest** part of growth plate

**✗ Zone distractors** — metaphysis (not a physeal zone) · provisional zone of calcification · **proliferative** · reserve

![Physeal zones — LLS injures the hypertrophic zone](../../MCQ/attachments/q217347-illa-physis-labeled.jpg)

## 2 Mechanism

**Forces** — repetitive **rotational (torsional) + distraction** stresses (± shear) on unossified physeal cartilage; **rotation > distraction** in importance

| Throwing phase | Physeal stress |
|---|---|
| **Late cocking** (max ER, just before acceleration) | **Extreme rotatory torque ~400% greater** than physis tolerates → **commonest cause** |
| **Deceleration** | Forward arm motion vs cuff → **excessive eccentric** physeal stress |
| ✗ Wrong option: early cocking | Not the peak |

![Pitching phases — late cocking at max ER](../../MCQ/attachments/q218568-illa-phases-of-throwing-3.jpg)

### Risk factors

**Most important** — **number of pitches thrown** + **frequency of pitching** (innings, throwing days, pitches/innings per week–month–year, **multiple teams**, year-round play)

**Breaking pitches** — **implicated** (Orthobullets) · **refuted / not worse than fastballs** (OB) — read the options; "% curveballs = most important factor" is wrong

**Mechanics / body size** — poor throwing kinematics part of accepted theory (OB) · **no clear relationship** with weight/height or mechanics (another OB)

## 3 Presentation

**History** — **↓ pitch velocity**, **↓ accuracy / control**; recent ↑ pitching (camp → season, two "select" teams)

**Pain** — diffuse arm/shoulder pain **with throwing**, **anterolateral/lateral**; worse in **late cocking / deceleration**; resolves with rest → may progress to ADL / rest pain

**Exam** — **point tenderness lateral proximal humerus over physis** · pain with rotation · **GIRD** (e.g. 30° IR difference) · ROM often full · no atrophy, no neuro deficit

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Finding |
|---|---|
| **X-ray views** | **AP in external rotation**, scapular Y, axillary |
| **Subtle cases** | **AP in ER** (changes clearest **anterolateral physis**) + **contralateral comparison** |
| **X-ray findings** | **Widened (lateral) proximal humeral physis vs contralateral** · metaphyseal sclerosis · demineralisation/lucency · osteopenia · metaphyseal calcification · **fragmentation** · cystic change |
| **Normal X-ray** | **17%** |
| **MRI** | **Metaphyseal oedema adjacent to physis**; rules out **labral tear**, **partial articular-sided cuff tear** (rare concomitant) |
| MRI necessity | **Not needed** for diagnosis when X-ray classic |

### Diagnosis – physeal widening, imaging & throwing phase

**Classic vignette** — 11–16-yo pitcher, lateral shoulder pain with throwing, ↓ velocity, physeal tenderness, **widened physis** → **LLS**

**15-yo, pain on throwing, ↓ IR, physeal widening (TO)** — **Little League shoulder**\
✗ Wrong options: adhesive capsulitis · pectoralis major tear · subacromial impingement

| ✗ Differential distractor | Why wrong |
|---|---|
| Septic arthritis | No infection |
| SLAP tear / internal impingement | Not this picture |
| External impingement | Subacromial bursitis, cuff tear |
| Chronic retracted supraspinatus tear | Age > 40 |
| Fallen cortical fragment in empty cyst | **UBC** pathological fracture |
| Acromion tip stress fracture | LLS = **proximal humeral physeal** stress fracture |
| Deltoid strain | MRI oedema is **bony metaphyseal** |

## 5 Management

```
Little Leaguer's shoulder
├─ All → stop throwing + PT (nonoperative)
│      └─ symptom-free → progressive throwing programme
├─ Return → short low-velocity tosses → ↑ distance + velocity
└─ Surgery → never indicated
```

### Treatment – cessation of throwing & graded return

**Mainstay** — **cessation of throwing / pitching** until **completely asymptomatic** → **PT** → **progressive throwing programme**

**Rest duration** — **3 months** (Orthobullets; "minimum 3 months", OB) · **2–3 months** (OB) · **6–12 wk** (OB) · **6–8 wk** then progressive throwing (OB) · brief immobilisation + no pitching **for remainder of season** (OB) — read the options

**PT** — **rotator cuff strengthening**, **posterior capsule stretching**, core strengthening

**Throwing programme** — start **only after symptom resolution**; short, low-velocity tosses → slowly ↑ distance + velocity

| ✗ Wrong management | Why |
|---|---|
| **In situ screw fixation**, ORIF (tension band), **CRPP** | Surgery not indicated; risks physis |
| Arthroscopic debridement / Bankart / labral repair | No labral pathology |
| **Subacromial corticosteroid** | Not impingement |
| **Biopsy** | Changes classic, not neoplastic |
| CT / MRI arthrogram as next step | Diagnosis already made |
| **Continue playing** with ↓ pitch count / breaking balls | Physis must heal |
| PT + continue pitching as tolerated | Risks growth arrest |
| **Sling 4 wk + NWB** | Unnecessary; ADLs allowed |
| Avoid throwing **until skeletal maturity** | Excessive |

**Prognosis** — **large majority return to pre-injury level**\
✗ Wrong options: "unlikely to reach pre-injury level"

### Prevention

**Measures** — **proper mechanics (pitching coach)** · **discourage breaking balls until skeletal maturity** · **enforce pitch counts + rest days** · **avoid year-round pitching** · **≤ 2 games/week**

| Age (y) | Pitches/game | Max games/week |
|---|---|---|
| 8–10 | 52 | 2 |
| **11–12** | **68** | **2** |
| 13–14 | 76 | 2 |
| 15–16 | 91 | 2 |
| 17–18 | 106 | 2 |

### Recurrence risk factors (IR deficit, length of rest)

**GIRD** — **3× more likely** recurrent symptoms; ↑ physeal injury risk

**Inadequate rest** — **length of activity cessation** (e.g. only 1 month) = recurrence risk

**> 2 games/week** — recurrence risk

**Not recurrence risks** — pitch count within age limit (68 at 12 y) · avoiding breaking balls · pitching coach

## 6 Complications

**Premature physeal closure** — **growth arrest** + **angular deformity** of proximal humerus

**Cause** — **continuing to pitch** / not managed properly
