# Subacromial Impingement

*Shoulder & Elbow · 53 / 60*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Frequency** — **most common cause of shoulder pain** · **44–65%** of shoulder disorders · with biceps tendinitis, among commonest causes

### Anatomy – acromion ossification

**Ossification centres** — **pre-acromion** (tip) · **meso-acromion** (mid) · **meta-acromion** (base)

**Os acromiale** — failure of centres to fuse

![Acromial ossification centres](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3041-shows-the-ossification-centers-of-the-acromion.jpg)

### Pathophysiology – metalloproteases

**Extrinsic compression** — cuff between humeral head and **anterior acromion**, **CA ligament**, **AC joint**

**Intrinsic degeneration** — supraspinatus attrition → can't balance head → **superior migration** → narrowed subacromial space

**Bursitis** — abutment → inflamed subacromial bursa → anti-inflammatory treatment rationale

### Genes & lab markers

| Marker | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Metalloproteases (MMP)** ↑ | **Subacromial bursitis / impingement** ✓ |
| TNF, **COX-1 + COX-2** ↑ | Subacromial bursitis |
| TSH (hypothyroid), **HbA1c** (diabetes) | Wrong — tests for **adhesive capsulitis** |
| WBC | Wrong — tests for **septic bursitis** |
| ALP | Wrong — tests for osteoblastic tumours, Paget, hyperparathyroidism |
| AFP, PSA, CEA, CA-125 | Wrong — tumour markers |

**Histology (tendinopathy)** — **disorganised collagen** · **mucoid degeneration** · inflammatory cells · bursa: high MMP + cytokines

## 2 Mechanism

**Continuum (Neer)** — **impingement + bursitis (stage 1)** → tendinitis/fibrosis → partial → full-thickness → **massive tear** → **cuff tear arthropathy**

**Associated conditions** — **hooked (type III) acromion** · **os acromiale** · **posterior capsular contracture** · **scapular dyskinesia** · tuberosity malunion · instability

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — **insidious** · **overhead activity** + lifting away from body · **night pain** = **poor predictor of nonop success**

**Strength** — **usually normal**; full passive ROM

| Test | Technique | Positive / meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Neer sign** | Passive FF **>90°** | Pain |
| **Neer test** | Subacromial **lidocaine** then repeat | **Pain relieved** |
| **Hawkins** | **IR + passive FF to 90°** | Pain |
| **Jobe (empty can)** | Resisted pronation + FF 90° | **Supraspinatus** pathology |
| **Painful arc** | Abduction in scapular plane | Pain **60–120°** |
| **Yocum** | Hand on opposite shoulder, raise elbow | **Sensitive, nonspecific** |
| Internal impingement | ABER | Pain |
| Speed's | — | Biceps tendinitis |

**O'Brien pain not relieved in neutral** — equivocal → **not labral**; don't order MRA first

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **True AP** | **Acromiohumeral interval** — normal **7–14 mm** |
| **30° caudal tilt** | **Subacromial spur** |
| **Supraspinatus outlet** | **Acromial morphology** (Bigliani) |
| **Axillary lateral** | **Os acromiale** (best) |

**X-ray findings** — proximal humeral migration (CTA) · traction osteophytes · **CA ligament calcification** · GT cysts · **type III hooked acromion** · os acromiale

**MRI** — degree of cuff pathology; subacromial/subdeltoid bursitis · CT arthrogram + US also image cuff accurately

**MRI indications** — acute injury · failed nonop · **weakness** / suspected large tear\
✗ Wrong options: first with benign exam

### Acromial morphology – os acromiale

**Types** — pre- · **meso-** · meta- os acromiale (named by unfused centre)

**MRI (axial + sagittal)** — unfused segment ± **edema** in skeletally mature adult → **meso os acromiale**\
✗ Wrong options: type I/III acromion

## 5 Management

```
Subacromial impingement (no cuff tear)
├─ First line → PT (cuff + periscapular) + NSAIDs ± subacromial steroid
│      (scapular dyskinesia → integrated kinetic-chain rehab)
├─ Failed ≥4–6 months nonop → subacromial decompression ± acromioplasty
└─ Os acromiale → stage 1 fuse with graft → stage 2 acromioplasty
```

### Nonoperative management – physical therapy & PRP

**Mainstay** — **PT** (aggressive cuff strengthening + periscapular stabilisation), NSAIDs, subacromial injection

**Benign exam (full strength, Jobe/Speed painful)** — **PT** before imaging\
✗ Wrong options: AC injection · ASD · MRA · MRI

**Outcome** — **most improve with PT alone** (even with type III acromion) · but **tendinitis tends to recur** \
✗ Wrong options: **"most fail nonop"**

**PT vs surgery** — PT **as effective** as subacromial decompression ± debridement (tendinitis, partial tears) · ASD + PT not better at 2 y

**PRP** — **no effect** on pain, function, ROM at **1 y** vs therapy alone · not harmful · does not prevent arthropathy · meta-analysis: insufficient evidence

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Subacromial decompression / acromioplasty** | Impingement **failed ≥4–6 months** nonop |
| **Two-stage os acromiale** | Fuse with bone graft → later acromioplasty (✗ Wrong options: direct excision → deltoid dysfunction) |
| **Avoid acromioplasty + CA ligament release** | **Massive irreparable cuff tear** (preserve CA arch → avoid anterosuperior escape) |

### Technique – acromioplasty

**Step 1** — **anterior acromionectomy** (arthroscopic: limited by **anterior deltoid origin**, keep intact)

**Step 2** — **anteroinferior acromioplasty** smooths undersurface (rasp open; shaver/burr arthroscopic) · open → meticulous **deltoid repair** to bone

### Subacromial decompression outcomes (workers' compensation)

**Lower satisfaction** — **workers' compensation** (also longer return to work) · **anxiety + depression** \
✗ Wrong options: dominant arm · male · smoker · age <60

### Massive cuff tears – irreparability & SCR contraindications (TO)

**Irreparable (TO)** — can't reach tuberosity despite release (size, retraction, atrophy, fatty infiltration)

| Irreparability marker (TO) | Value |
|---|---|
| Massive tear | **>5 cm** (Cofield) or **≥2 tendons** |
| Pattern | **Posterosuperior (SS + IS)** > anterosuperior (SS + subscap) |
| Fatty atrophy | **Goutallier III–IV** |
| **Acromiohumeral distance** | **<6 mm** |
| Function | **Chronic pseudoparalysis** (can't elevate **>90°**; some say >45°) |
| MRI | **Positive tangent sign** |

**Tangent sign (TO)** — sagittal oblique; line **top of scapular spine → top of coracoid** · SS belly **below line = positive** → **fatty atrophy, irreparable supraspinatus** / poor repair outcome\
✗ Wrong options: subscapularis · retraction medial to glenoid · biceps dislocation

### Superior capsular reconstruction (TO)

**Concept (TO)** — passive restraint to **superior head translation** → restores force couples, ↓ subacromial impingement, ↓ pain

**Graft (TO)** — folded **fascia lata 6–8 mm** or **acellular dermal 3–4 mm** · anchors on **superior glenoid rim** + **medial tuberosity**

| SCR (TO) | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Indications** | Failed nonop **massive irreparable** tear (SS + IS) · **Hamada 1–2** · **intact/reparable subscapularis** · **functional deltoid** · intolerable pain · pseudoparalysis |
| **Contraindications** | **Irreparable / torn subscapularis** ✓ · **Hamada ≥3** · GH OA |

**✗ Contraindication distractors (TO)** — Hamada I · pseudoparalysis · massive tear failed conservative treatment (= indications)

### Young patient, massive irreparable posterosuperior tear (TO)

| Option (TO) | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Latissimus dorsi transfer** | Retracted posterosuperior tear, **Goutallier ≥3** SS + IS; not if subscap insufficient or deltoid atrophy |
| **Lower trapezius transfer** | **More anatomical** for posterosuperior; promising |
| **Pectoralis major transfer** | **Anterosuperior** (SS + subscap) — most common |
| **SCR** | Young, no significant arthritis |
| **Reverse TSA** | **Older** patients with cuff arthropathy → **least preferred in 35-year-old** ✓ |

**Tendon transfer prerequisites (TO)** — young, active, no significant GH arthritis, **intact subscapularis**

## 6 Complications

**Deltoid dysfunction** — failed repair after open acromioplasty · **excessive acromionectomy** arthroscopically · **direct excision of os acromiale**

**Anterosuperior escape** — acromioplasty + CA ligament release in **massive irreparable** tear

| | **Subacromial impingement** | **Adhesive capsulitis** | **Septic bursitis** |
|---|---|---|---|
| ROM | **Full passive** | ↓ | — |

|---|---|---|
| Tear | **Posterosuperior** | **Anterosuperior** |

## How it's asked

**"Insidious overhead pain, full ROM, MRI bursitis — elevated marker?"** → **metalloproteases**\
✗ Wrong options: TSH · HbA1c · WBC · ALP

**"Markers contributing to subacromial bursitis pain?"** → **metalloproteases**\
✗ Wrong options: AFP, PSA, CEA, CA-125

**"Partial SS tear, lidocaine relieved 90%, asks for PRP?"** → **no effect on pain, function, ROM at 1 year**

**"Type III acromion, Hawkins + empty can +, 5/5 — true about nonop?"** → **most improve with PT alone**\
✗ Wrong options: most fail · ASD better at 2 y · PRP better · never recurs

**"Golfer, Jobe + Speed's painful, full strength — next?"** → **PT for cuff strengthening**\
✗ Wrong options: ASD · MRI · MRA · AC injection

**"Lower satisfaction after ASD?"** → **workers' compensation**

**"Axial + sagittal MRI unfused acromion with edema?"** → **meso os acromiale**

**"Contraindication to SCR?"** → **irreparable subscapularis tear** (TO)

**"35-year-old, pseudoparalysis, AHD <6 mm, Goutallier 3 — least preferred?"** → **reverse shoulder arthroplasty** (TO)

**"Positive tangent sign predicts?"** → **irreparable supraspinatus (fatty atrophy)** (TO)

**"Best view for os acromiale?"** → **axillary lateral**

**"Night pain significance?"** → **poor predictor of nonop success**

**"Massive irreparable tear — avoid?"** → **acromioplasty + CA ligament release** (anterosuperior escape)
