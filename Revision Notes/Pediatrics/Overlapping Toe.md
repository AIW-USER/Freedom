# Overlapping Toe

*Pediatrics · 78 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — congenital overlap of a toe from **extensor digitorum longus contracture** · rare

**Epidemiology** — **5th toe** most common · usually **bilateral** · M = F · often **familial**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain with **shoe wear**

**Exam** — toe **adducted + slightly externally rotated**, overlapping 4th · **MTP dorsiflexed** ("cock-up") · **smaller nail plate**

## 5 Management

### Surgical treatment

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| First line | **Passive stretching + buddy taping** |
| Failed, still symptomatic | **Butler procedure** |
| Alternatives | EDL tenotomy · dorsal capsulotomy · **syndactylization to 4th toe (McFarland)** |
| Skeletally mature only | **Proximal phalanx resection** (induces secondary deformity) |

**Butler** — **dorsal + plantar racquet-shaped incision**, **EDL tenotomy**, **circumferential MTP release**, toe translated plantarly · addresses chronic MTP dislocation (needs plantar release) · main problem = **recurrence**\
✗ Wrong options: tenotomy alone · V-Y + dorsal capsulotomy

## How it's asked

**"9-yr, painful cock-up overlapping 5th toe with shoes?"** → **Butler procedure: racquet incision, EDL tenotomy, circumferential MTP release**\
✗ Wrong options: EDL tenotomy alone · phalangeal resection
