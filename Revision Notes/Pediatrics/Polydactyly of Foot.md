# Polydactyly of Foot

*Pediatrics · 84 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **1 in 500** (Orthobullets) · postaxial ~1 in 200 (OB) · **postaxial (lateral) most common** · African American > Caucasian · foot less common than hand

**Cause** — failure of differentiation of the **apical ectodermal ridge** in the 1st trimester

### Inheritance & associations

**Inheritance** — **autosomal dominant** (family history)\
✗ Wrong options: X-linked · AR · mitochondrial · spontaneous only

**Isolated postaxial with normal exam** — **normal motor milestones** · **no cardiac / renal work-up** · no systemic association

### Venn-Watson classification

| Type | Patterns |
|---|---|
| **Postaxial** (lateral) | **Y** metatarsal · **T** metatarsal · wide MT head · complete duplication |
| Central (added) | Duplicated 2nd, 3rd or 4th toe |
| **Preaxial** (medial) | Short block 1st MT · wide MT head |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**X-ray** — MTs visible at birth · **delay films** until phalanges ossify for planning

![Postaxial polydactyly radiograph](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4078-polyrad.jpg)

## 5 Management

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Well-aligned postaxial / central | **Observation** (unless foot much wider) |
| **Malaligned toe, esp. preaxial** | **Ablate extra (usually border) digit at 9–12 months** |
| Small skin tag | Remove in newborn nursery |
| Cosmesis / shoe wear | Elective, any age per family wishes (OB) |

**Natural history** — shoe-fitting problems, angular toe deformity

## How it's asked

**"10-yr postaxial foot polydactyly, father similar, normal exam?"** → **normal milestones, no further work-up**\
✗ Wrong options: cardiac/renal work-up · spontaneous mutation

**"7-month-old postaxial polydactyly — inheritance?"** → **autosomal dominant**
