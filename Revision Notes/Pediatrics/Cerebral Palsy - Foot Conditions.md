# Cerebral Palsy - Foot Conditions

*Pediatrics · 14 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Foot deformity** — **most common deformity in CP** · forms: **equinus**, **hallux valgus**, **equinoplanovalgus**, **equinocavovarus**

| Deformity | Typical CP type | Muscle imbalance |
|---|---|---|
| **Equinus** | All; **isolated in hemiplegia** | Spastic/contracted **gastrocsoleus** vs dorsiflexors |
| **Equinoplanovalgus** | **Spastic diplegia + quadriplegia**, bilateral | **Spastic peroneals + heel cord**, **weak PTT**, lax ligaments |
| **Equinocavovarus** | **Spastic hemiplegia** | **Invertors (PTT ± TA) overpower peroneals** |
| **Hallux valgus** | **Diplegics** with equinus + planovalgus | **Adductor hallucis overactivity** + external forces |

**Lever-arm dysfunction** — planovalgus and cavovarus both disrupt push-off / second rocker

## 3 Presentation

### Equinus

**Symptoms** — shoe wear, **tripping** (poor clearance), instability

**Exam** — forefoot calluses · **toe walking, no heel strike** · compensatory **knee hyperextension** · late equinoplanovalgus

**True equinus gait** — toe walking with **lack of heel strike** at initial contact · **plantarflexion moment** in stance\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ DF in stance/swing · DF moment

**Silfverskiöld** — DF ↑ with **knee flexed** → **gastrocnemius** tight · equal → **Achilles** tight · DF is **worse with knee extended** when gastroc spastic (crosses knee)

**Planovalgus trap** — uncontrolled subtalar joint **overestimates** ankle DF → **correct heel valgus first** before testing for Achilles contracture

### Equinoplanovalgus

**Findings** — bilateral · **valgus heel** · **talar head callus** in arch · **midfoot break** · **hallux valgus develops over time**

### Equinocavovarus

**Findings** — lateral-border / **5th MT calluses** (ulcers) · in-toeing · foot drop if TA weak · supination on TA firing = TA is main source

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Deformity | Views | Findings |
|---|---|---|
| Equinus | Not required unless hindfoot malalignment | — |
| **Equinoplanovalgus** | **WB AP ankle** (rule out **ankle valgus**) + foot | **↓ calcaneal pitch** · **negative talo–1st MT angle** · talar head uncovering |
| Equinocavovarus | WB foot + ankle | **Metatarsal overlap** · **↑ calcaneal pitch** |
| Hallux valgus | WB series | ↑ HVA, IMA, DMAA, HVI |

## 5 Management

### Equinus – botulinum toxin, serial casting & gastrocnemius recession

| Treatment | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Serial casting** | Mild, dynamic, young · **unbraceable foot** (can't reach neutral) after botox → cast, then brace |
| **Botulinum toxin A** (gastrocnemius) | Mild spasticity, delays surgery · **prevents presynaptic release of acetylcholine (ACh)** · not more often than **every 3 months** |
| **Hinged / articulated AFO** | Mild, passively correctable, no myostatic contracture · not if excessive midstance DF |
| **Solid AFO** | Mild–moderate, partly correctable, mild contracture · not if heavy, ≥12 yr, rigid malalignment |
| **Gastrocnemius recession** | **Silfverskiöld positive** (isolated gastroc) · less immobilisation, **higher recurrence** |
| **TAL** | Gastrocsoleus (Achilles) tight · rarely isolated **except in hemiplegia** · walking cast 4–6 wk |

**Botox mechanism distractors** — ✗ Wrong options: binds nicotinic receptor (curare, succinylcholine) · destroys receptor (myasthenia) · sarcolemma instability (dystrophy)

**Surgery timing** — delay equinus surgery until **≥4 yr** (OB) · **≥6 yr** to prevent recurrence (OB) — read the options

**Hemiplegic 3-year-old, toe-walking, DF short of neutral after botox** → **serial casting** of affected side\
✗ Wrong options: bilateral AFOs (unaffected side toe-walks in compensation) · heel-cord lengthening · repeat botox at 1 month · baclofen pump (diplegia)

**Gastrocnemius recession technique** — posterior/posteromedial incision · protect **sural nerve** · incise aponeurosis transversely or U-shape · cast in neutral

### Equinovarus – split tibialis posterior transfer

```
Flexible equinocavovarus (hemiplegia)
├─ PTT EMG continuous → SPOTT (split PTT to peroneus brevis), age 4–7
├─ PTT EMG out-of-phase → whole PTT transfer to dorsum (lateral cuneiform)
├─ Spastic TA (supination on TA firing) → SPLATT ± TAL + PTT lengthening
└─ Fixed deformity → add calcaneal osteotomy / arthrodesis
```

**SPOTT** — **passively correctable** · **age 4–7** · **spastic hemiplegia** · **continuous PTT EMG** · posterior half rerouted behind tibia (anterior to NV bundle) → woven into **PB** · more consistent than whole transfer\
✗ Wrong options: athetosis (unpredictable) · rigid deformity · quadriplegic/diplegic valgus

**SPLATT** — passively correctable + **spastic TA** · lateral half → **cuboid** tunnel · not for **weak TA / foot drop**

**Bony** — **lateral slide or lateral closing-wedge calcaneal osteotomy** (fixed varus) · **triple arthrodesis** (severe fixed; recurs if soft tissues not balanced)

**Bracing** — supple only · rarely successful, worsens calluses

### Equinoplanovalgus

**Flexible** → **bracing**

**Pain / pressure sores despite bracing** → soft tissue + bone

| Soft tissue | Bone |
|---|---|
| TAL · **peroneus brevis lengthening** · **PTT advancement** | **Medial slide calcaneal osteotomy** · **lateral column lengthening (Evans)** (trapezoidal graft anterior to middle facet) · **Grice** (extra-articular subtalar, **does not affect tarsal growth**) · **subtalar arthroereisis** (plug; breakdown) |

![Grice extra-articular subtalar arthrodesis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12120-grice-moved.jpg)

### Hallux valgus – first MTP arthrodesis

**Painless, shoes OK** → observe

**Painful (failed accommodative shoes)** → **1st MTP arthrodesis** — **highest success** in ambulatory + nonambulatory CP\
✗ Wrong options: double/crescentic MT osteotomy · modified McBride · night orthotics (recurrence unacceptably high)

**Valgus interphalangeus** → **Akin** proximal phalanx osteotomy

## 6 Complications

**Planovalgus surgery** — **overcorrection into varus (most common)** · **sural nerve injury** · **overlengthened lateral column → lateral forefoot overload** · CC joint destabilisation

**Cavovarus** — **recurrence** if all components not addressed

## X vs Y

| | **Equinoplanovalgus** | **Equinocavovarus** |
|---|---|---|
| CP type | **Diplegia / quadriplegia** | **Hemiplegia** |
| Imbalance | Spastic peroneals, weak PTT | **PTT ± TA > peroneals** |
| Callus | **Talar head** | **Lateral border / 5th MT** |
| Calcaneal pitch | **↓** | **↑** |
| Surgery | LCL, medial slide, Grice, arthroereisis | **SPOTT / SPLATT**, lateral calcaneal osteotomy |

## How it's asked

**"5-year-old hemiplegic, flexible equinocavovarus, lateral ulcers, continuous PTT EMG?"** → **split posterior tibial tendon transfer**\
✗ Wrong options: PTT to lateral cuneiform (out-of-phase) · triple · calcaneal slide (fixed)

**"Best candidate for SPOTT?"** → **6-year-old spastic hemiplegic, flexible equinovarus**\
✗ Wrong options: athetosis · rigid

**"Spastic diplegic with true equinus gait — expected?"** → **toe walking, no heel strike**

**"Botulinum toxin mechanism?"** → **prevents presynaptic ACh release**

**"3-year-old hemiplegic, DF −5° after botox 1 month ago?"** → **serial casting** of affected side

**"Isolated gastrocnemius recession best when?"** → **DF 15° knee flexed vs neutral knee extended**\
✗ Wrong options: too-many-toes · Thompson · equal DF · no single heel rise

**"15-year-old CP community ambulator, painful hallux valgus?"** → **1st MTP arthrodesis**
