# Popliteal Cyst in Children

*Pediatrics · 85 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — herniated posterior knee capsule synovium between **semimembranosus** and **medial head of gastrocnemius** · common soft-tissue mass · **unlike adults, usually NOT linked to meniscal tears**

## 3 Presentation

**Findings** — usually **painless**, mobile, soft · posteromedial, distal to knee crease · **most prominent with knee extended** · **transilluminates**

![Popliteal cyst](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4048-clinical-photo-miller-colorado-moved.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — normal · **US** — cystic · **MRI** — fluid-filled simple cyst (confirms)

## 5 Management

### Treatment - observation

**Observation** — mainstay · Dinham: of 70 untreated cysts, **51 resolved spontaneously** over a mean **1 yr 8 months**

**Aspiration** — may work but **high recurrence** · **excision** only if significant discomfort or failure to resolve\
✗ Wrong options: radical excision · arthroscopic resection first · CT · bone scan

## How it's asked

**"10-yr, soft painless posteromedial mass, MRI simple cyst?"** → **observation**
