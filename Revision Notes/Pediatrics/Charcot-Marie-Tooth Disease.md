# Charcot-Marie-Tooth Disease

*Pediatrics · 19 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **1 in 2500** · no ethnic predisposition · **most common inherited neuromuscular disease** seen by orthopaedists

**Sex** — more common in **males**; females often worse (OB)

**Name** — **HMSN** (hereditary motor sensory neuropathy) · peroneal muscular atrophy · most common HMSN (others: Dejerine-Sottas)

### Genetics & pathophysiology (PMP22 duplication, myelin, autosomal dominant)

**Most common** — **autosomal dominant duplication on chromosome 17p12** → **PMP22** (peripheral myelin protein 22) expressed in **Schwann cells** · PMP duplication in **~70%** (OB)

**Structure affected** — **myelin sheath** (CMT1)\
✗ Wrong options: perineural vessels · cell body · dendrite · NMJ

**PMP22 deletion** (same region) → **HNPP** (hereditary neuropathy with liability to pressure palsies)

| Type | Inheritance | Pathology | NCV | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CMT1** | **AD** | **Demyelinating** | **Slowed** (<38 m/s) | **50%** (Orthobullets, OB) · 80% (OB) · 1A = PMP22 duplication · onset 1st–2nd decade · **cavus** foot · motor > sensory · normal lifespan |
| **CMT2** | AD (AR-CMT2 subtype) | **Axonal — Wallerian degeneration**, intact myelin | **Normal** (>45 m/s), ↓ CMAP/SNAP amplitudes | ~20% · onset 2nd decade+ · **less disabled** · flaccid foot |
| CMT4 | **AR** | Demyelinating | Slowed | — |
| CMTX | **X-linked** | — | — | — |

**HMSN look-alikes** — ALS (UMN + LMN death) · Refsum (branched-chain fatty acid oxidation) · **Dejerine-Sottas** (severe, rapidly progressive, early) · Parkinson (midbrain)

### Deformity mechanism – plantar-flexed first ray (PL over TA) & PTT

| Strong (preserved) | Weak | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Peroneus longus** | **Tibialis anterior** | **Plantarflexed 1st ray = initial deformity** (cavus) |
| **Tibialis posterior** | **Peroneus brevis** | **Hindfoot varus**, foot adduction |
| Long toe extensors (recruited for DF) | **Intrinsics** | **Claw toes** |

**Predominant deforming muscles** — **posterior tibialis + peroneus longus**

**Weakest first** — **peroneals** first + most profound (Orthobullets) · initial presentation = **weak tibialis anterior** (OB) → foot drop, later fixed equinus

**Sequence** — plantarflexed 1st MT + pronated forefoot → **secondary hindfoot varus** → later fixed + equinus

**Bilateral progressive cavovarus** → **HMSN/CMT** (78% of bilateral cavovarus)\
✗ Wrong options: DMD (equinovarus) · CP (equinus/equinovalgus) · myelomeningocele (clubfoot/CVT/calcaneus) · Werdnig-Hoffmann

## 3 Presentation

**Motor** — distal weakness + atrophy · **high-steppage gait** (foot drop) · tripping, falls · **chronic ankle instability / sprains** · **"inverted champagne bottle"** legs · lateral foot pain · lateral shoe wear

**Sensory** — stocking-glove loss · ↓ proprioception → imbalance · burning/numbness

**Foot** — **bilateral symmetric cavovarus** + claw/hammer toes · flexible → rigid

**Reflexes** — **hyporeflexia/areflexia** (absent ankle + knee jerks)

**Hand** — **intrinsic wasting** (1st dorsal interosseous) · **weak pinch** (grasp, dexterity spared)

**Spine** — scoliosis on Adams test

![Pes cavus in CMT](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4086-pes-cavus.jpg)

**Coleman block** — corrects → **forefoot-driven** · doesn't → **hindfoot-driven/rigid**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnosis & imaging

**NCS/EMG** — classifies demyelinating vs axonal · **demyelinating cut-off 38 m/s** · axonal **>45 m/s** with ↓ CMAP/SNAP · **prolonged distal latencies** in peroneal, ulnar, median nerves

**Genetic testing** — **PCR for PMP22** · chr 17 duplication

**Radiograph** — **high-arched cavus foot** (↑ calcaneal pitch)\
✗ Wrong options: accessory navicular · CN coalition · ball-and-socket ankle (fibular hemimelia)

**Pathology** — simultaneous demyelination + remyelination ("onion bulb")

![CMT1 demyelination](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4086-image-showing-demyelination.jpg)

## 5 Management

### Orthoses – lateral forefoot & heel posting

**Flexible cavovarus** → **lateral forefoot + lateral heel posting** (forefoot post lets 1st MT flex; heel post corrects varus) · (✗ Wrong options: medial heel wedge (worsens) · medial flare · SACH) · metatarsal pad = pain relief only

**Lateral hindfoot posting** suits **flexible cavovarus**\
✗ Wrong options: accessory navicular · middle-facet coalition · flexible flatfoot · metatarsus adductus

**Other** — accommodative shoes (mild) · semi-rigid insole with 1st-ray recess + lateral wedge (flexible adult) · SMO · **AFO for foot drop** (best if equinus dynamic) · lace-up brace

**24-yr HMSN, corrects with Coleman, no arthritis** → **custom orthotic with lateral forefoot + heel posting** first\
✗ Wrong options: medial posting · osteotomies

### Surgery – flexible (PL to PB, first MT osteotomy, PTT transfer, calcaneal osteotomy) vs rigid (triple arthrodesis)

```
CMT cavovarus — failed orthoses
├─ Flexible (Coleman corrects) → plantar release + PL→PB + 1st MT DF osteotomy ± PTT to dorsum
├─ Partial / no correction → + lateralising calcaneal osteotomy (proximal translation)
└─ Rigid + arthritic (TC, CC) → triple arthrodesis
       (poor long-term in CMT — last resort)
```

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Plantar release** (fascia ± Steindler) | Cavus |
| **PL → PB transfer** | Plantarflexed 1st ray · ↓ 1st-ray PF, restores eversion |
| **1st MT dorsiflexion osteotomy** (or 1st MC dorsal closing wedge) | **Flexible hindfoot** (Coleman normal) |
| **PTT transfer through IOM to dorsum** (lateral foot) | **Flexible deformity, TA weak, PTT strong** → ↓ varus + gives DF · **improved outcomes** |
| Gastrocnemius recession / TAL | Equinus (gastroc recession less weakness, with plantar release) |
| **Lateralising calcaneal osteotomy** (± proximal translation) | **Hindfoot not correcting on Coleman** · proximal shift improves triceps lever arm |
| **Triple arthrodesis** | **Rigid + arthritic** hindfoot · **poor outcomes** in CMT (Wetmore & Drennan) |
| **Jones (EHL → 1st MT neck + IP fusion)** | Symptomatic claw hallux failing nonop |

**Distractors** — ✗ Wrong options: PB → PL (wrong direction) · SPLATT (TA weak) · TA → PB (TA weak) · PTT → TA tendon (out-of-phase; go **lateral**) · FDL transfer (PTT is the deforming force) · medializing calcaneal osteotomy (↑ varus) · LCL (flatfoot) · botox (weakens push-off) · ankle/subtalar fusion in supple young foot

**25-yr chr-17 duplication, progressive foot drop, supple joints, equinus, strong PF + inversion** → **PTT transfer to dorsum + gastrocnemius lengthening**

**23-yr, partial Coleman correction, 1st MT callus, weak TA + PB** → **PL → PB + 1st MC dorsal closing wedge + lateralising calcaneal osteotomy with proximal translation**

### Claw toes

**Mechanism** — weak DF → **toe extensor recruitment** + weak intrinsics → claw · flexible → rigid

**Treatment** — **Jones procedure** (EHL to 1st MT neck + IP arthrodesis) for symptomatic claw hallux

### Associated hip dysplasia

**Prevalence** — **<10%** (Orthobullets) · **6–8%** (OB) · often **asymptomatic** → **screen** · presents in adolescence in ambulators

**Cavus foot + hip dysplasia in a teen** → **CMT**\
✗ Wrong options: OI · Down (planovalgus) · achondroplasia · cleidocranial

**Treatment** — **pelvic osteotomy** · ↑ **sciatic nerve palsy** risk · outcomes comparable to DDH

### Scoliosis

**Prevalence** — **10–20%**

**Distinguishing** — **left thoracic** + **kyphotic** curve (vs idiopathic)

**Bracing** — rarely effective · **fusion if >50°** and progressive

## 6 Complications

### Complication – transfer lesion after bunionette resection

**5th MT head resection in CMT** → **transfer lesion to 4th MT head** (lateral overload of cavovarus)\
✗ Wrong options: fixed 5th claw (toe flail) · ulcer (no bone) · peroneal atrophy · Charcot

**Rigid deformity** — untreated progression → arthritis

## X vs Y

| Onset | 1st–2nd decade | 2nd decade+ |
| Disability | More | **Less** |
| Foot | **Cavus** | Flaccid |

| | **CMT** | **Friedreich ataxia** |
|---|---|---|
| Gene | PMP22, chr 17 | GAA repeat, chr 9 |
