# Proximal Femoral Focal Deficiency

*Pediatrics · 87 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — defect of the **primary ossification centre (cartilage anlage)** of proximal femur · spectrum: **absent hip · femoral neck pseudarthrosis · absent femur · short femur**

**Epidemiology** — **bilateral 15%** · mostly **sporadic** · rare AD form (dysmorphic facies)

### Genes & lab markers

**Gene** — **sonic hedgehog** (limb bud)

### Associated ACL deficiency & knee instability

**Associated** — **fibular hemimelia (50%)** · **ACL deficiency / absent cruciates** · underdeveloped tibial spines · **coxa vara** · knee flexion contractures · acetabular dysplasia · ball-in-socket ankle

**ACL deficiency** → **knee subluxation during femoral lengthening** → examine with **Lachman**\
✗ Wrong options: dial · Galeazzi · Thessaly · varus stress

**Not associated** — clubfoot · café-au-lait · webbed neck (Turner) · scapular winging (Sprengel)

**Associations (TO)** — **fibular hemimelia \~45%** · **clubfoot** (TO lists it — conflicts with the "not associated" line above; read the options) · congenital heart disease · spinal and facial dysplasia

**ACL deficiency most likely in** — **PFFD**\
✗ Wrong options: cleidocranial (RUNX2) · Apert (FGFR2) · achondroplasia (FGFR3) · radial head dislocation

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — severe shortening · **% shortening constant with growth** · short bulky thigh **flexed, abducted, externally rotated** · feet usually normal

**Exam (TO)** — **knee flexion contracture** · foot often at level of **contralateral knee** · **skin dimple over greater trochanter**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Aitken classification

| Class | Femoral head | Acetabulum |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Present | Normal |
| **B** | Present | Mildly dysplastic |
| **C** | **Absent** | Severely dysplastic |
| **D** | **Absent** | **Absent** |

**Femoral head ossifies ~6 months** → defer hip decisions until then

**Aitken — TO version** — A: head present, short femur · B: **femoral neck pseudarthrosis** · C: **absent head** · D: **absent head + acetabulum** · **Paley**: group A congenital short femur → B–D increasing discontinuity

**Options (TO)** — orthosis · lengthening (selected Paley A; hip + knee instability makes it hard) · valgus osteotomy for coxa vara · **knee fusion + extension prosthesis** · rotationplasty · amputation

## 5 Management

**Individualise by** — final LLD · foot · musculature · **proximal (hip) stability**

```
PFFD
├─ Hip unstable (Aitken C/D) → femoral-pelvic fusion (Brown)
└─ Hip stable (A/B or stabilised)
       ├─ LLD <20 cm, femur >50%, functional foot → lengthening ± contralateral epiphysiodesis
       ├─ Foot at level of normal knee → Van Ness rotationplasty
       └─ Foot above normal knee → Syme + knee arthrodesis → AK prosthesis
```

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Lengthening ± contralateral epiphysiodesis** | Predicted LLD **<20 cm** · stable hip + functional foot · **femur >50%** of other side · **head present (Aitken A/B)** · not with unaddressed coxa vara, neck pseudarthrosis, acetabular dysplasia |
| **Knee arthrodesis + foot ablation (Syme)** | Foot **proximal to opposite knee** · prosthetic knee won't sit below opposite knee · Syme at 10–14 months, knee fusion 3–4 yr (OB) |
| **Femoral-pelvic fusion (Brown)** | **Femoral head absent (Aitken C/D)** |
| **Van Ness rotationplasty** | **Foot at level of opposite knee** · ankle **>60% motion** · absent head (C/D) · **180° rotation** → ankle DF becomes knee flexion → BK prosthesis |
| **Amputation** | Femur **<50%** · keep length · **through joint** (avoid overgrowth) · prosthesis after 1 yr |
| Observation / extension prosthesis | Bilateral PFFD |

### Treatment - rotationplasty

**11-week-old, ankle will reach normal knee level** → **delayed Van Ness rotationplasty + hip stabilisation at 1 yr if head deficient**\
✗ Wrong options: immediate femoral-pelvic fusion (head status unknown) · Syme + knee fusion (too short) · immediate lengthening

## How it's asked

**"ACL most likely deficient in?"** → **PFFD**

**"PFFD — test for condition causing knee subluxation with lengthening?"** → **Lachman**

**"6-yr PFFD — other finding?"** → **knee ligamentous laxity**\
✗ Wrong options: clubfoot · café-au-lait

**"Infant PFFD, ankle at normal knee level at maturity?"** → **delayed Van Ness rotationplasty, hip stabilisation at 1 yr if needed**
