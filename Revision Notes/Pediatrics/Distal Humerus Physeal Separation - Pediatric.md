# Distal Humerus Physeal Separation - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 36 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Also called** — **transphyseal distal humerus fracture**

**Age** — **<3 years** (physis weaker than bone–ligament interface → physeal fracture, not dislocation)

**Mechanism** — **birth trauma** (vaginal delivery, shoulder dystocia; C-section traction) · **non-accidental trauma** (rotational/twisting shear — **up to 50% battered child**) · FOOSH, fall from bed/couch

**Displacement** — **posteromedial** (most) · rarely anterior

| Age | SH type |
|---|---|
| **<3 yr** | **SH I** (pure physeal) |
| >3 yr | SH II (metaphyseal fragment) |
| Rare | SH III/IV — mimics lateral condyle fracture |

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — **pseudoparalysis** / not using arm · swelling, ecchymosis · NV compromise rare

**NAT red flags** — unwitnessed, inconsistent history, multiple injuries, burns, bites, bruises (**skin lesions most common NAT finding**)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnosis & non-accidental trauma association (skeletal survey)

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **AP + lateral centred on elbow** · stress views may help · **skeletal survey if abuse suspected**\
✗ Wrong options: "babygram" (misses it)

**Findings** — **radius + ulna displaced posteromedially relative to humerus** (may be the only sign in infants) · **forearm not aligned with humeral shaft** · **radiocapitellar alignment maintained** (capitellum stays with radius — definitive if ossified) · effusion, swelling

**Capitellum ossifies** — **1–2 yr** (first elbow centre)

**Ultrasound** — uncertain dx, **no sedation** · static (cartilage absent on metaphysis) / dynamic instability

**Arthrogram** — uncertain dx, in OR with CRPP · **direct posterior portal** (olecranon fossa) in young · shows pin start on capitellum + anterior humeral line

**MRI** — not routine (sedation)

**Missed** — up to **50%** by radiologists · delayed up to 1 wk

## 5 Management

```
Transphyseal distal humerus fracture (<3 yr)
├─ Suspect NAT → skeletal survey + child protective services
├─ Displaced (most) → arthrogram + closed reduction + percutaneous pinning
└─ Nondisplaced / late presentation → long-arm splint/cast 2–3 wk
       (late: deformity → osteotomy later)
```

**After immobilisation, 8-month-old** → **contact child protective services**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · CT elbow/head · emergent fixation (not emergent without open/NV injury)

**NAT team consult** — most likely for **distal humerus physeal separation in 16-month-old**\
✗ Wrong options: BBFF 5-yr · type II supracondylar 4-yr · femur 10-yr · Tillaux 13-yr

### Treatment – closed reduction & pinning

**Displaced (even if NAT ruled out)** → **CRPP** under GA with **arthrogram**\
✗ Wrong options: observe · sling · rigid immobilisation · compartment monitoring · ORIF / plate · hinged ex-fix

**Reduction** — gentle traction (little force) → correct translation/rotation → flex

**Acceptable** — **no cubitus varus** · **anterior humeral line bisects capitellum** · no malrotation

**Pins** — **2–3 × 0.062" lateral, divergent**, bicortical, wide spread · test under live fluoro

**Post-op** — splint · X-ray 1 wk · **remove pins at 3 wk** · follow **2–4 yr** (arrest, deformity, AVN)

**Nonop** — limited: **nondisplaced** · **late-presenting** (→ later osteotomy)

## 6 Complications

**Cubitus varus** — **up to 70%** — **more common than supracondylar** · from **medial condyle AVN**, malunion (missed/lost reduction), growth arrest → **lateral closing-wedge osteotomy**

**Medial/lateral condyle AVN** — fishtail deformity

**Growth disturbance** — observe, operate **>5 yr** (bigger limb, cooperative, one-stage)

**Loss of motion** — rarely functional

**NOT associated** — **tardy ulnar nerve palsy** (→ lateral condyle nonunion + cubitus valgus)

## X vs Y

| | **Transphyseal separation** | **Elbow dislocation** |
|---|---|---|
| Age | **<3 yr** | Older (almost never <3) |
| Displacement | **Posteromedial** | **Posterolateral** |
| Radiocapitellar line | **Intact** | Disrupted |
| NAT | **Strong association** | — |

## How it's asked

**"10-month-old, posteromedial displacement — all EXCEPT?"** → **tardy ulnar nerve palsy** (✓ abuse, cubitus varus, medial condyle AVN)

**"Injury most commonly from child abuse?"** → **distal humerus physeal separation**\
✗ Wrong options: Monteggia · proximal femur · toddler's · supracondylar

**"8-month-old after immobilisation — next?"** → **contact child protective services**

**"2-year-old, no injury history, transphyseal separation — true?"** → **skeletal survey**\
✗ Wrong options: posterolateral · nonop reliable · 6–8 yr · incarcerated fragment (medial epicondyle)

**"NAT team present — your consult?"** → **distal humerus physeal separation in 16-month-old**

**"7-month-old swollen elbow, forearm displaced on humerus?"** → **closed reduction + percutaneous pinning**

**"12-month-old, NAT ruled out?"** → **operative percutaneous pinning**

**"9-month-old fell down steps?"** → **CRPP in OR, immobilise 3–4 wk**

**"8-month-old displaced transphyseal fracture?"** → **closed reduction with pinning**
