# Congenital Pseudoarthrosis of Clavicle

*Pediatrics · 29 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **failure of fusion of medial and lateral ossification centres** of the clavicle

### Right-sided middle third

**Side** — **right ~90%** · **left only with dextrocardia / situs inversus** · **bilateral <10%** (Orthobullets) / ~10% (TO) — bilateral linked to **bilateral cervical ribs**

**Site** — **middle third**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral third · left · both equally

**Cause** — extrinsic compression / pulsation of **subclavian artery** (higher on right)

**Genetics** — no clear involvement

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — **painless, nontender mass** on clavicle · present at birth, **no callus**, no trauma history · ± scapular winging

![Clavicular pseudarthrosis — painless bump](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4039-clavicular-pseudarthrosis-physical-exam.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**AP clavicle** — **rounded, sclerotic bone ends** at pseudarthrosis · no callus (vs fracture)

![Congenital pseudarthrosis of clavicle](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4039-congenital-pseudoarthosis-of-clavicle.jpg)

## 5 Management

**Observation** — minimal symptoms / cosmetic only

### Operative criteria

**ORIF + iliac crest bone graft at 3–6 yr** — pain · functional impairment · cosmesis · union usually achieved · **avoid bone-graft substitutes** (↑ nonunion)

## X vs Y

| | **Congenital pseudarthrosis** | **Birth fracture** | **Cleidocranial dysplasia** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Side | **Right**, middle third | Either | Bilateral |
| Pain | **Painless** | Painful | Painless |
| X-ray | **Rounded sclerotic ends**, no callus | Callus by ~1 wk | Absent / hypoplastic clavicles |
| Other | — | — | Skull, pubis, teeth |

## How it's asked

**"Congenital pseudarthrosis of clavicle — side?"** → **right** (TO)

**"Almost always presents?"** → **right side, middle third**

**"Painless clavicle mass with rounded sclerotic ends — treatment?"** → **observe; ORIF + iliac crest graft at 3–6 yr if symptomatic**
