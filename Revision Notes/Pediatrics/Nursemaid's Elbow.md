# Nursemaid's Elbow

*Pediatrics · 72 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & recurrence

**Definition** — radial head subluxation / **annular ligament interposed between radial head and capitellum**

**Age** — **1–4 yr**, average **28 months** (Orthobullets) · peak **2–3 yr** (TO) · TO text also says peaks in first 2 yr · **rare after 5**\
✗ Wrong options: "peak 3–5 yr" · "common in infants"

**Why rare after 5** — thicker, stronger distal annular ligament attachment (Orthobullets) · radial head ossified (TO)

**Sex / side** — **slightly more common in girls** (✓ TO true statement) · left arm (TO)

**Mechanism** — **sudden longitudinal traction on hand, elbow EXTENDED + forearm PRONATED** (✗ Wrong options: supinated) · lifting / swinging by the hand · occasionally a fall · annular ligament tears or slips over radial head (✗ Wrong options: LCL tear)

**Recurrence** — **up to 5%** (TO true statement) · 5–39% reported (TO) · esp. **<3 yr** · capsule stays stretched for months\
✗ Wrong options: "recurrence is rare"

## 3 Presentation

**History** — **click** felt by the person pulling · child cries, **refuses to use arm**

**Exam** — **elbow slightly flexed, forearm pronated** · lateral elbow tenderness · full flexion/extension · **pain / restricted supination**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-rays** — **not needed** with classic history + age ≤5 + typical exam · when taken: **normal** · **25%** radiocapitellar line slightly lateral to capitellar centre

**Ultrasound** — when mechanism unclear or exam inconclusive · ↑ echo-negative area between capitellum + radial head · **sensitivity 64.9%, specificity 100%** · no radiation

**Diagnosis of exclusion** — supracondylar, olecranon, radial neck, lateral condyle fractures · contusion · septic arthritis · congenital radial head dislocation · synostosis

## 5 Management

### Reduction - supination & flexion

**Rule** — exclude fracture before manipulating

**Supination technique** — supinate forearm → **flex elbow** (maximally per Orthobullets · 60–90° OB · 90° TO) · thumb on radial head → **click**

**Hyperpronation technique** — hyperpronate forearm in flexion

**After reduction** — child uses arm within minutes · **no immobilization after first episode**\
✗ Wrong options: long arm cast (stiffness) · pinning · MRI · observation · longitudinal traction

**Recurrent** — **cast in flexion + neutral or supination** (lets annular ligament heal)

**Open reduction** — rare: chronic irreducible symptomatic subluxation

![Supination–flexion reduction](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4012-nursemaid-flexion-supination.jpg)

### Misfiled TO – Weil osteotomy

**Weil osteotomy** — distal oblique metatarsal head osteotomy (dorsal-distal → proximal-plantar, ~30°, screw) for **metatarsalgia**\
✗ Wrong options: Perthes · SCFE · genu valgum

**Indications** — long / plantarflexed metatarsal · transverse-plane MTP deformity · MTP subluxation/dislocation · recalcitrant metatarsalgia

**Complications** — **dorsiflexed (floating) toe** · painful/↓ MTP dorsiflexion · PIP contracture

## How it's asked

**"2-yr, elbow flexed + pronated, normal X-rays?"** → **supination and flexion reduction maneuver**\
✗ Wrong options: + long arm cast · CRPP · MRI

**"3-yr, arm pulled crossing street, guarding?"** → **closed reduction via supination and flexion**\
✗ Wrong options: aspiration · longitudinal traction · splint

**"Pulled elbow — true?"** → **recurrence in 5% of cases** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: peak 3–5 yr · supinated forearm · LCL tear

**"Nursemaid's elbow — true?"** → **more common in girls** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: infants · recurrence rare

**"Weil osteotomy used for?"** → **metatarsalgia** (TO)
