# Osteogenesis Imperfecta

*Pediatrics · 75 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Genetics & type I collagen defect

**Type I collagen** — main structural protein of bone, skin, tendon, dentin, sclera · triple helix = **2 α1 chains (COL1A1) + 1 α2 chain (COL1A2)** · **glycine every 3rd residue** allows helix

**Mutation** — **COL1A1 / COL1A2** in **90%** (Orthobullets) · **85%** (OB) · **missense glycine substitution** in procollagen → abnormal cross-linking · **dominant-negative** effect → severe phenotype\
✗ Wrong options: nonsense

**Pathophysiology** — **↓ collagen secretion** + **abnormal collagen** → **insufficient osteoid** by physeal + **periosteal osteoblasts** → **bone can't remodel**\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ secretion · "uninterrupted remodeling"

**Quantitative vs qualitative** — **quantitative (half normal amount, haploinsufficiency) → mild (type I)** · **qualitative (glycine substitution) → severe (II, III, IV)**

**Inheritance** — **AD most common** · AR forms (VI, VII, CRTAP / LEPRE1 — severe/lethal, no primary collagen defect) · **not X-linked recessive** · AR forms more severe

| Defect (distractors) | Disease |
|---|---|
| **Glycine substitution in procollagen** | **OI** |
| Gsα | Fibrous dysplasia |
| Vitamin C (hydroxylation) | Scurvy |
| Sulfate transporter | Diastrophic dysplasia |
| Homogentisic acid oxidase | Alkaptonuria |
| Osteoclast (carbonic anhydrase / M-CSF) | Osteopetrosis |
| Activating FGFR3 | Achondroplasia |
| Type V collagen (basement membrane) | Ehlers-Danlos (OB) |
| N-Ac-Gal-6-sulfate sulfatase | Morquio |
| EXT (prehypertrophic chondrocytes) | MHE |
| COMP / type II collagen | Pseudoachondroplasia / MED, SED |

### Sillence classification

| Type | Inheritance | Collagen | Sclera | Features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | AD | **Quantitative** | **Blue** | **Mildest, most common** · preschool (tarda) · **hearing loss 50%** · A (no DI) / B (DI) |
| **II** | AD (Orthobullets, TO) · AR (OB) | Qualitative | Blue | **Perinatal lethal** |
| **III** | AD (Orthobullets, TO) · AR (OB) | Qualitative | **Normal** | **Most severe survivable** · fractures at birth · progressive short stature |
| **IV** | AD | Qualitative | **Normal** | **Moderate** · bowing, vertebral fractures · hearing normal · A/B by teeth |
| **V** | AD | **No collagen defect** (IFITM5) | — | **Congenital anterolateral radial head dislocation** · **hypertrophic callus** · **IOM ossification** (forearm, leg) |
| **VI** | AR | No collagen defect (SERPINF1) | — | Like IV · ↑ ALP · fish-scale lamellae · responds poorly to bisphosphonates → denosumab (TO) |
| **VII** | AR | No collagen defect (CRTAP) | — | Rhizomelia, coxa vara · severe |

**90%** fall into types I + IV · TO adds VIII (LEPRE1), IX (PPIB), X (SERPINH1), XI (FKBP10, Bruck), XV (WNT1)

**Recurrent fractures + hearing loss in a child** → **type I** (TO) · hearing loss 50–92%, rare in type IV

## 3 Presentation

**Orthopaedic** — fragility fractures (heal normally, **don't remodel** → bowing, **saber shin**) · ligamentous laxity · short stature · scoliosis · **codfish vertebrae** · **basilar invagination** · **coxa vara (10%)** · congenital radial head dislocation

**Olecranon apophyseal avulsion** — **most common first presenting sign** · minor trauma (e.g. pushing a sibling) · **bilateral** → work up OI · high contralateral risk · ORIF if displaced\
✗ Wrong options: abuse · Noonan · vitamin D

**Non-orthopaedic** — **blue sclera** · triangular facies · **hearing loss** (50% adults; conductive, sensorineural, mixed) · **dentinogenesis imperfecta** (brown/blue opalescent teeth, primary > secondary) · **wormian bones** · **hypermetabolism → malignant hyperthermia risk** · thin skin, bruising · MVP, aortic regurgitation

**Fracture course** — fewer with age, usually stop after puberty

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Radiographic recognition

**X-ray** — **thin cortices** · generalized osteopenia · **saber shins** / bowing · **wormian bones** on skull · coxa vara

**Bisphosphonate zebra lines** — **dense parallel horizontal metaphyseal bands** (growth arrest lines) after **8–10 wk** of cyclical therapy · migrate to diaphysis, vanish after physeal closure\
✗ Wrong options: from growth hormone (TO false statement)

**Diagnosis** — **family history + clinical + radiographic** · no commercial test · labs normal (mildly ↑ ALP) · **skin biopsy / fibroblast culture** for collagen (diagnostic per TO; positive in 80% of type IV) · iliac crest biopsy · **prenatal PCR on chorionic villi at 8–12 wk** (TO)

**Histology** — ↑ Haversian canal + lacunar diameter · replicated cement lines · ↑ osteoblasts + osteoclasts · ↓ trabeculae · thin cortex · woven bone, crowded osteocytes (TO)

![Bisphosphonate growth arrest lines](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4102-growth-arrest-lines-associated-with-long-term-bisphosphonate.jpg)

## 5 Management

### Bisphosphonates

**Indication** — most OI · ↓ fracture rate, ↓ pain (1–6 wk), ↑ ambulation, ↑ lumbar BMD

**Mechanism** — **inhibit osteoclast resorption** (N-bisphosphonate → **FPP synthase** → osteoclast apoptosis) · endosteal resorption ↓ while periosteal formation continues → **cortex thickens**\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ mineralization · better collagen · ↑ turnover · RANKL antibody

**Effect** — cortical diameter **↑88%** · cancellous volume **↑46%** · **no effect on scoliosis**

**Pamidronate** — 1 mg/kg/day × 3 days every 2–4 months · zoledronic acid every 6 months (TO)

**IM rodding** — **bisphosphonate-free period** around surgery (impairs **osteotomy** healing >> fracture healing)

**Others** — growth hormone (adjunct) · BMT (some success) · denosumab (type VI, refractory) · teriparatide **adults only** (osteosarcoma risk) · early bracing to limit deformity

### Fracture management - intramedullary rodding

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **<2 yr** | Treat as non-OI child (closed) |
| **>2 yr** | **Telescoping rods** (Bailey-Dubow, Sheffield, Fassier-Duval) — grow with child · **preferred** (TO) |
| Deformity beyond tolerance after reduction | **Load-sharing IM device** |
| **Severe bowing** | **Sofield-Miller (shish-kebab / "seekh kebab") multiple osteotomies + IM rod** |

**IM rod over plate** — plates → **stress shielding + periprosthetic fracture** · OI **not** at higher nonunion risk

**Teen forearm (14–15 yr)** — >10° won't remodel → **open reduction + IM rod**\
✗ Wrong options: plate · cast · realignment osteotomy (that's for chronic deformity)

**Non-telescopic** — Rush, Williams rods

![Sofield-Miller shish-kebab osteotomy](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4102-photo-shishkebab-osteotomy-moved.jpg)

### Spine (basilar invagination, scoliosis)

**Scoliosis** — curves early + progress · VC falls to **40% at 60°**

| Curve | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **<45°** | Observe |
| **>45° mild** OI | **PSF** |
| **>35° severe** OI (thin bones) | **PSF early** |
| Bracing | **Ineffective, not recommended** (rib fragility) |

**PSF** — allograft (little autograft) · large blood loss · **ASF only in very young** (crankshaft) · no anterior approach (pulmonary) · not telescoping rods (long bones)

**Basilar invagination** — odontoid into foramen magnum · **severe forms** · 3rd–4th decade (teens possible) · **apnea, altered consciousness, ataxia, myelopathy** (finger escape, Hoffmann) · → brainstem compression, hydrocephalus, syrinx · **transoral decompression + posterior fusion** · also Klippel-Feil, achondroplasia, Morquio, SED\
✗ Wrong options: AAI (Down)
