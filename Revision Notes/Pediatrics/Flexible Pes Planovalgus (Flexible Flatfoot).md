# Flexible Pes Planovalgus (Flexible Flatfoot)

*Pediatrics · 48 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — ↓ medial arch + **hindfoot valgus** + forefoot abduction **on weight-bearing**, from **ligamentous laxity**

**Prevalence** — **44%** of school-age children (↓ with age) · 20–25% adults · normal toddler variant

**Associated** — generalised laxity · **gastrocsoleus contracture (25%)** · rotational problems · accessory navicular (controversial)

**Lever arm dysfunction (TO)** — planus + **hindfoot valgus** + **forefoot abduction** → foot CORA shifts medially, ↓ plantarflexion lever arm, ↑ energy at push-off

**Developmental (TO)** — infant fat pad + laxity · **arch develops by \~6 yr** · Staheli: "flatfeet are usual in infants, common in children, unusual in adults" · flatfoot in **90% of infants, 25% of adults** (TO)

### Types

| Type | Note |
|---|---|
| **Hypermobile flexible** | **Most common**, familial, bilateral |
| Flexible with **tight heel cord** | Stretch |
| **Rigid flatfoot / tarsal coalition** | Least common · hindfoot valgus **doesn't correct** on toe-standing |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — usually **asymptomatic** · arch or pretibial pain

**Exam** — **flat only on standing**; arch **reconstitutes on toe-standing, hallux dorsiflexion (Jack test), or hanging** · hindfoot valgus → **varus on toe-rise** · **normal painless subtalar motion** · check **Silfverskiöld** for tight heel cord

![Arch reconstitutes on toe standing](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4069-20171003-135410.jpg)

**History clues (TO)** — morning stiffness → inflammatory · activity pain → coalition / accessory navicular · recurrent sprains · family: familial flatfoot, **hereditary spastic paraplegia** · progressive → neurological · **progressive unilateral flatfoot = red flag for tethered cord** (spread buttocks: sacral dimple/sinus)

**Exam (TO)** — **toe-walking test** + **Jack's (great-toe dorsiflexion) test** restore the arch in flexible feet · subtalar inversion-eversion normal **20–30°** · **Silfverskiöld**: ↓ DF **> 10°** with knee extended = gastrocnemius tightness · **lock the subtalar joint** (avoid pseudo-DF)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Paediatric foot angle (TO, weight-bearing) | View | Normal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Talus–1st MT** | AP | **0 to \~7–10°** | Negative = adductus · > 10° = abduction |
| **Talocalcaneal (Kite)** | AP | **25–40°** (20–40° < 5 yr) | > 40° hindfoot valgus · < 20–25° varus |
| Calcaneus–4th MT | AP | — | Midfoot abduction |
| **Calcaneal pitch** | Lateral | **10–15°** | High = cavus / calcaneus · low/negative = equinus, flatfoot, CVT |
| **Meary (talus–1st MT)** | Lateral | **0–4°** | Apex dorsal = cavus · apex plantar = planus |

**Principles (TO)** — standing AP + lateral ("one view is no view") · exam first · **most foot ossification centres by \~5 yr** · **1st MT physis at its BASE**, other MT physes at heads

**Indications** — **painful** flatfoot or **rigid** flatfoot only\
✗ Wrong options: painless flexible

**Views** — **WB AP** (talar head coverage, TC angle) · **WB lateral** (Meary's) · **WB oblique** (coalition) · ± plantarflexion lateral (exclude CVT) · ± ankle AP/lateral (ankle valgus — myelodysplasia)

**Meary's angle** — **apex plantar** · **>4°** = arch collapse

![Meary's angle — apex plantar](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4069-pediatric-flex-flatfoot-normal-mearys-angle-moved.jpg)

**Differentials** — tarsal coalition · CVT · accessory navicular

## 5 Management

### Nonoperative management & observation

**Asymptomatic** — **observation + education** · arch develops with age\
✗ Wrong options: arch supports · X-rays · PT "to help feet develop"

**Tight heel cord, painless** — **gastrocnemius stretching**\
✗ Wrong options: spine MRI (that's cavus) · X-rays · night AFO · UCBL without pain

**Orthotics** — **do NOT change natural history** · use/stop by **comfort** (no harm, no benefit) · soft arch support for symptoms · **UCBL** for symptomatic advanced cases (rigid → poorly tolerated)

**Short-foot exercises** — intrinsics, medial arch (TO)

**Painful flexible flatfoot (TO)** — causes: **obesity**, **tight TA** → weight loss + TA stretching with subtalar locked · asymptomatic: reassurance, **no orthotics**, shoes don't change natural history

**Age-based (TO)** — **0–3 yr: supervised neglect** · **3–12 yr: orthotics + physiotherapy** · adolescents / adults: guided by clinical + radiological findings · only symptomatic feet need surgery (TO)

**Orthotics in childhood** — **do not change natural history; no orthotics if asymptomatic** (Orthobullets) · **3–12 yr: orthotics + physiotherapy** (TO) — read the options

**EOTTS / sinus tarsi stent (TO)** — extra-osseous talotarsal stabilisation · corrects **talar plantarflexion + inversion** · for **selected adolescent flexible flatfeet** (TO)

### Operative criteria

| Indication | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **Painful + tight heel cord refractory to stretching** | **TAL / gastrocnemius recession** |
| **Refractory pain** (talar head, sinus tarsi) after extensive nonop | **Calcaneal (lateral column) lengthening — Evans** ± cuneiform osteotomy, peroneal lengthening |
| Hindfoot valgus | Medial **sliding calcaneal osteotomy** |
| **Forefoot supination** | **Plantar-based closing-wedge osteotomy of medial cuneiform** |

### Surgical - calcaneal lengthening osteotomy

**Evans lateral calcaneal lengthening** → **flexible pes planovalgus**\
✗ Wrong options: clubfoot · cavus · hallux valgus · Köhler

**12-yr, 2 yr pain, failed UCBL + orthoses, supple** → **calcaneal neck lengthening osteotomy**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral slide · PL→PB · 1st MT DF osteotomy · PTT to dorsum (all cavus procedures)

**Isolated medial calcaneal slide without cuneiform osteotomy** → **persistent forefoot supination**\
✗ Wrong options: persistent heel valgus

![Evans calcaneal lengthening](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4069-evans.jpg)

**Mosca procedure (TO)** — **Evans-modified calcaneal lengthening** with **tricortical iliac crest graft** · restores lateral column, corrects lever-arm dysfunction

**Triple C osteotomy (TO)** — **cuboid opening wedge + medial cuneiform closing wedge + calcaneal sliding** osteotomy

## X vs Y

| | **Flexible flatfoot** | **Rigid flatfoot (coalition)** | **CVT** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Toe-rise | Arch + heel varus **return** | **No correction** | Rigid |
| Subtalar motion | Normal | **↓** | — |
| Pain | Rare | Often | — |
| X-ray | Only if painful | Oblique / CT | Forced PF lateral |

## How it's asked

**"5-yr painless flexible flatfeet, DF neutral knee extended / 15° flexed?"** → **gastrocnemius stretching**

**"2-yr flatfoot, arches reconstitute, normal ROM?"** → **observation and education**

**"3-yr, mother asks about custom inserts?"** → **continue/discontinue by comfort; resolves with time**

**"Evans osteotomy for?"** → **flexible pes planovalgus**

**"12-yr refractory painful flexible flatfoot?"** → **calcaneal neck lengthening osteotomy**

**"Calcaneal slide without medial cuneiform osteotomy?"** → **persistent forefoot supination**
