# Pediatric Abuse

*Pediatrics · 80 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Most common presentations & epidemiology

**Burden** — **>1 million** substantiated victims/yr (US) · 15–42 per 1,000 children · >1,200 deaths/yr · **~⅓ of abused children see an orthopaedic surgeon**

**Mortality** — **2nd most common cause of death in children >1 yr** (after accidental injury) · after SIDS at 1–5 months (OB) · death usually from **head trauma**

**Age** — **90% of abuse fractures <5 yr** · **50% of fractures <1 yr** · **30% of fractures <3 yr** (Orthobullets) · 10–15% <3 yr (Kempe, TO) · most common <3 yr

**Femur** — **NAT = most common cause of femur fracture in non-ambulatory infant** · NAI up to **35% of femur fractures <3 yr**, **40–90% <1 yr** (OB)

| Type (overlap) | % |
|---|---|
| **Neglect** (✓ most common) | **78%** |
| Physical | 18% |
| Sexual | 9% (~10% TO) |
| Psychological | 8% |

| Presentation | Rank |
|---|---|
| **Skin lesions** (bruises in various stages, burns, cuts) | **Most common** (✗ Wrong options: fractures) |
| **Fractures** | **2nd** |
| **Head injury** | **Most long-term morbidity** (✗ Wrong options: burns) |

**Fracture location** — **humerus > tibia > femur** · **diaphyseal 4× more common than metaphyseal** · single transverse diaphyseal fracture classic (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: metaphyseal 4×

### Risk factors

**Child** — **first-born** · unplanned · **premature** · **disability (CP, neurocognitive syndromes, Down)** · **step-child** · age <3 yr

**Parent / social** — single parent · recent stressor (**job loss**, move) · unemployment · drugs · abused as child · low SES · no support · age <20 · Medicaid (⅔)

**NOT risk factors** — **female sex** · **no siblings**

## 3 Presentation

**Red flags** — inconsistent / no history · **delay in seeking care** · **long-bone fracture in a non-ambulatory child** (e.g. "fell running" at 6 months) · unwitnessed injury · multiple carers · bruises in various stages · **pinna bruising, cigarette burns** (highly specific)

### Fracture patterns suggestive of abuse

| Specificity | Fractures |
|---|---|
| **High** | **Classic metaphyseal lesion (corner + bucket handle)** · **posteromedial rib** · **long bone in non-ambulatory child** · **transphyseal distal humerus** (moderate per OB220897) · scapula · sternum · spinous process |
| Moderate | **Multiple fractures in various stages of healing** · vertebral body · digital · complex skull |
| Low | **Toddler's fracture** (non-displaced spiral tibia, ambulatory 9 months–3 yr) · clavicle · supracondylar humerus · SCFE |

**Classic metaphyseal lesion** — through **primary spongiosa** at metaphysis-physis junction · **torsion / traction-shear** (pull, twist, shake) · **corner fracture** = discrete metaphyseal avulsion · **bucket handle** = same seen en face · present in **40–50%** of abused infants (TO) · "almost pathognomonic" (TO) · **most suspicious femur pattern**\
✗ Wrong options: midshaft spiral/transverse

**Femur NAI vs accident** — **mid-diaphyseal most common in both** · NAI children **younger**\
✗ Wrong options: corner fracture most common in NAI

**Rib fractures** — **posteromedial** · highest specificity (OB) · 70% chance of abuse · 51% of fractures in fatal abuse · anterior rib not pathognomonic

**Transphyseal distal humerus separation** — rotatory shear · ≤3 yr · radiocapitellar line preserved (vs dislocation) · arthrogram/US/MRI if unossified · CRPP like supracondylar

**Shaken baby syndrome** — **subdural hematoma + retinal hemorrhage + posterior rib fractures** (TO) · chest ecchymosis · seizures

**No fracture is pathognomonic** — judge age, ambulation, pattern, mechanism, psychosocial picture

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Skeletal survey** — **study of choice / gold standard** · AP appendicular + AP/lateral axial (skull, spine, ribs) · **mandatory <2 yr** with suspected abuse (AAP) · **repeat at 2 wk** for healing injuries\
✗ Wrong options: SPECT · CT CAP · brain/spine MRI · isolated forearm film

**Add** — lateral thoracolumbar <12 months · **head CT <1 yr** or neuro signs · ophthalmology if shaking suspected

**Bone scan** — adjunct >1 yr · ↑ sensitivity for ribs, subtle shaft fractures, periosteal elevation · **useless for metaphyseal + skull fractures** · less efficient than survey

**OI vs abuse (TO)** — **DEXA 4–6 months apart** (density rises without metabolic disease, not in OI) · fibroblast culture / collagen analysis if unclear

### Differential

**List** — accidental injury · **OI** · osteopenia of prematurity · scurvy · copper deficiency · **Menkes** · disuse osteopenia · chronic renal/liver disease · rickets

**X-ray mimics** — rickets (physeal widening, metaphyseal fraying) · wormian bones (OI, rickets; normal in 10% Caucasians, 80% Asians) · bisphosphonate zebra lines (vs Park-Harris arrest lines — persist, parallel to physis) · **Caffey disease**

### Differential – Caffey disease (infantile cortical hyperostosis)

**Features** — infant · **solid wavy periosteal new bone** along long-bone shafts (ulna, femur), **clavicles, mandible**, ribs, scapulae · **resolves spontaneously** within ~a year

**TO stems** — infant with forearm cortical hyperostosis (OI / congenital syphilis / Caffey / infantile osteomyelitis) · diffuse ulnar thickening + clavicle + femur, resolved in a year (scurvy / Ewing / Garré / Caffey) · **answers not provided by the source**

## 5 Management

### Management - skeletal survey & CPS

**Rule** — **report reasonable suspicion** → **child protective services** · **admit** for safety + multidisciplinary evaluation (pediatrics, social work, CPS, radiology) · document + photograph

**Order** — CPS/NAT work-up **before definitive fracture plan or discharge**\
✗ Wrong options: cast and send home · next-week clinic · metabolic labs first · genetic testing first · talk home safety · restrain father / police

**Fracture care** — usually splint/cast until callus (Pavlik / spica for infant femur) · surgery rarely needed · still treat appropriately (e.g. forearm: **closed reduction + cast + skeletal survey + CPS**)

**Toddler's fracture alone** — not abuse · **plus corner fracture or suspicious skin lesions** → **admit / report to CPS**

**If missed** — **30–50% repeat abuse**, **5–10% death**

### Legal duty to report

**Physicians = mandated reporters** · need only **reasonable suspicion** · **immune from civil + criminal liability in good faith** · **failure to report can be a crime**\
✗ Wrong options: prove cause · call daycare first

**Sexual abuse disclosure (minor)** → **child protective services**\
✗ Wrong options: parents · school board · athletic director · counselor

### Medical child abuse - multidisciplinary evaluation

**Munchausen by proxy (caregiver-fabricated illness)** — usually biologic mother with medical knowledge · repeated doctor shopping, unwarranted tests/surgery · normal exam + X-rays → **early multidisciplinary evaluation** (medical, psychosocial, CPS, legal)\
✗ Wrong options: CT · casting · coalition excision · another opinion

### Developmental milestones

| Milestone | Age |
|---|---|
| **Sits** | **9 months** (not sitting at 15 months → work up) |
| Walks | Up to **15 months** still normal |
| **Hand dominance** | **3rd year (~30 months)** · strong preference <2 yr → neuro deficit |
| Shoes on correct feet, buttons | 4 yr |
| Hops on one foot, heel-to-toe walk | 5 yr |

### Cast saw burns

**Reduce burns** — vacuum · **thinner cast** · **"up and down" technique** · check blade often · **avoid fiberglass** (more heat than plaster)
