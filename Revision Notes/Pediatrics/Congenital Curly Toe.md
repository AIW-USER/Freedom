# Congenital Curly Toe

*Pediatrics · 24 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Deformity** — **flexion + varus of the interphalangeal joints** · toe curls under its medial neighbour

**Location** — **lateral three toes** (3rd + 4th most often) · usually **bilateral**

**Cause** — contracture of **FDL** or **FDB** (most common)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — usually **asymptomatic** · if symptomatic: pain, shoe-wear difficulty, **nail problems**

**Natural history** — ~**25% improve spontaneously** (Sweetnam) · more likely to **persist in older child (e.g. 6 yr)**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — clinical

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — **not indicated**

## 5 Management

```
Congenital curly toe
├─ Infant / asymptomatic → observation
└─ Symptomatic (pain, shoes, nail) after age 3 → flexor tenotomy
```

**Observation** — first line (same success as strapping, less painful) · **toe strapping/taping not beneficial** (no change or temporary)

### Operative criteria

| Indication | Procedure |
|---|---|
| Severe toe or **nail-bed** deformity, symptomatic, **>3 yr** | **Flexor tenotomy** (FDL ± FDB) — **85–90%** effective |
| — | Tenotomy **= FDL transfer to extensor hood** in efficacy |
| Rarely | PIP arthrodesis |

**Techniques** — open tenotomy of both FDB slips + FDL · tenotomy of one FDB slip · FDL → extensor hood transfer

## How it's asked

**"8-month-old with curly toes, first surgeon offered FDB tenotomy + volar plate release?"** → **observation**\
✗ Wrong options: FDL transfer · FDB tenotomy · taping

**"Symptomatic curly toe in a 6-year-old?"** → **flexor tenotomy**\
✗ Wrong options: observe · tape 8 wk · transfer (no better) · PIP fusion

**"Cause?"** → **FDL / FDB contracture**
