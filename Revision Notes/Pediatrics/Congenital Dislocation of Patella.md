# Congenital Dislocation of Patella

*Pediatrics · 25 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — rare, **irreducible lateral (posterolateral) patellar dislocation** · present at birth (often missed) or reducible at birth → subluxation → **fixed dislocation in childhood**

### Pathoanatomy

**Bone** — **small or absent patella** · **hypoplastic trochlea** · **external tibial torsion**

**Soft tissue** — **thick, tight lateral structures (ITB, retinaculum)** · **tight quadriceps** (superior subluxation, patella alta)

**Associated** — **Larsen** · arthrogryposis · diastrophic dysplasia · **nail-patella** · **Down** · Ellis–van Creveld

### Pathomechanics

**Quadriceps action** — laterally displaced extensor mechanism → **quadriceps becomes a knee flexor**\
✗ Wrong options: extension · internal rotation (it's external) · varus (it's valgus) · posterior translation

**Patella anatomy** — largest sesamoid · ossifies **3 yr (♀) / 4–5 yr (♂)** · blood supply **distal → proximal** (geniculates, supreme geniculate, anterior tibial recurrent)

## 3 Presentation

**History** — associated syndromes · **delayed walking** (mimics **cerebral palsy**)

**Exam** — **genu valgum** · **knee flexion contracture** · external tibial torsion · **"smiley face" kneecaps** · prominent condyles · small, laterally placed patella hard to palpate · limited active extension

**Pain** — all patients **≥7 yr** had pain (Gordon & Schoenecker)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**<3 yr** — patella unossified → radiographs unhelpful → **ultrasound or MRI**

**>3 yr** — **AP, lateral, sunrise** · dislocated patella · hypoplastic trochlea

![Sunrise view — laterally dislocated patella](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4047-cdp-sunrise.jpg)

## 5 Management

**Observation** — **not recommended** (long-term functional deficit, malalignment, pain)

**Surgery early** — allows trochlear remodelling

```
Congenital patellar dislocation
└─ Early surgical reduction (Andrish)
       ├─ Soft tissue: lateral release + VL advancement + quad lengthening + MPFL (semitendinosus)
       └─ Distal realignment if needed → Roux-Goldthwait (open apophysis)
```

**Andrish soft-tissue steps** — lengthen lateral retinaculum · detach VL from septum, advance proximally · release distal patellomeniscal ligaments · **lengthen quadriceps tendon, shorten patellar tendon** (alta) · **MPFL reconstruction** (semitendinosus rerouted through MCL to patella)

**Bony** — distal realignment rarely needed · tubercle transfer limited by apophysis → **Roux-Goldthwait** (lateral half patellar tendon medialised)

## 6 Complications

**Recurrence**

## X vs Y

| | **Congenital dislocation** | **Recurrent patellar instability** |
|---|---|---|
| Age | Birth / early childhood | Older child, activity |
| Patella | **Fixed, irreducible** | Reducible; normal between episodes |
| Associated | Syndromes (Down, nail-patella, Larsen) | Ligamentous laxity |
| Treatment | **Surgical reduction** | Nonop → MPFL |

## How it's asked

**"10-day-old, laterally dislocated patella on US — quadriceps now does?"** → **flexes the knee**

**"Imaging under age 3?"** → **ultrasound / MRI**

**"Treatment?"** → **early surgical reduction (lateral release, quad lengthening, MPFL)**

**"Distal realignment with open apophysis?"** → **Roux-Goldthwait**
