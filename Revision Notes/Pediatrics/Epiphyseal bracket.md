# Epiphyseal bracket

*Pediatrics · 43 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Also called** — **longitudinal epiphyseal bracket (LEB)**, delta phalanx / delta bone

**Incidence** — **2–14%** of congenital hand + foot deformities · **11% in great toe**

**Pathology** — incomplete primary ossification · **aberrant physeal cartilage runs longitudinally along the diaphysis ("C-shaped")** → tethers growth → short, wide, **triangular / trapezoidal** bone

**Bones** — short tubular bones (phalanges, metatarsals — esp. **1st MT**)

### Genes & lab markers

**Genetics** — **AD when in hand**, otherwise sporadic

**Associated** — **hallux varus** (feet) · **clinodactyly**, polydactyly (hands) · Rubinstein-Taybi · fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva · Apert

## 3 Presentation

**Deformity** — angulated, short digit / ray (hallux varus, medial great-toe deviation)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **trapezoidal / triangular** bone · **"D-shaped" metatarsal** · wedge-shaped middle phalanx

**Ultrasound** — shows **pre-ossified** bracket

**MRI** — **diagnoses** LEB

![Longitudinal epiphyseal bracket — C-shaped physis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12781-f132-024ac-9780323081764.jpg)

### Radiographic staging

| Stage | Feature |
|---|---|
| **1** | Delta-shaped diaphysis, radiolucent cartilaginous epiphysis |
| **2** | Secondary ossification at proximal + distal ends |
| **3** | Complete ossification of bracket along one side |
| **4** | Physeal closure |

## 5 Management

```
Longitudinal epiphyseal bracket
├─ Young child, open bracket → early excision (physiolysis) + fat / PMMA interposition
└─ Older, bracket closed → opening/closing wedge osteotomy ± lengthening
```

### Treatment - bracket excision

**Early open bracket excision + fat graft (or PMMA) interposition** — best; restores near-normal longitudinal growth, ↓ deformity\
✗ Wrong options: percutaneous epiphysiolysis (open needed) · Syme · ex-fix lengthening as index · fusion (macrodactyly) · observation · ray resection

**Osteotomy** — **after bracket epiphysis closes** (older) · secondary realignment/lengthening if growth insufficient

![Physiolysis of bracket](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12781-11832-2013-544-fig4-html.jpg)

## 6 Complications

**Untreated** — forefoot deformity, short bone, clinodactyly

**Operative** — recurrence, incomplete correction

## How it's asked

**"4-yr with metatarsal LEB — next step?"** → **bracket excision + fat graft interposition**

**"3-yr, first-ray bracket on X-ray?"** → **excision of the abnormal structure**
