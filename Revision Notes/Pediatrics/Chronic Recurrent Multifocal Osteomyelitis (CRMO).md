# Chronic Recurrent Multifocal Osteomyelitis (CRMO)

*Pediatrics · 20 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **1 in 250,000** · prevalence 1 in 160,000

**Age** — children/adolescents · **peak 10 years**

**Sex** — **girls > boys**

**Site** — **tubular long bones** (metaphyses) + **clavicle**

### Definition & associations

**Also called** — chronic **non-bacterial** osteomyelitis · sterile, **rheumatologic** bone inflammation · multifocal, recurrent over years

**Associations** — **pustulosis palmoplantaris** (red patches + pustules on palms/soles → desquamation days later) · **SAPHO** (synovitis, acne, pustulosis, hyperostosis, osteitis) · **psoriasis** · **inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn's)**

**Syndromic forms** — **Majeed syndrome** · **DIRA** (deficiency of IL-1 receptor antagonist)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — localised pain + swelling, often **worse at night** · episodic fever, malaise · partial relief with NSAIDs

**Exam** — focal tenderness

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnostic criteria

| Criterion |
|---|
| **Multiple sites** of apparent osteomyelitis |
| **Pathology + cultures negative** |
| **No response to antibiotics** |

**Diagnosis of exclusion** — biopsy essential

### Genes & lab markers

**Labs** — **WBC normal** · **ESR / CRP mildly elevated**

**Biopsy** — inflammation, **no atypia** (✗ Wrong options: osteosarcoma), **no small round blue cells** (✗ Wrong options: Ewing) · cultures negative

![CRMO biopsy — bland inflammation](../../MCQ/attachments/q213915-c-crmo-biopsy.jpg)

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **eccentric lytic metaphyseal lesions** abutting physis with **surrounding sclerosis + new bone**

**Bone scan** — finds other sites

**MRI** — if malignancy suspected (soft-tissue involvement)

![CRMO of distal tibiae](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4036-crmo-distal-tibias.jpg)

## 5 Management

**Symptomatic during exacerbations** — **NSAIDs** · corticosteroids · **pamidronate** (symptoms, vertebral remodelling) \
✗ Wrong options: antibiotics (no response)

## 6 Complications

**Growth disturbance** — rare

**Natural history** — remissions + exacerbations · resolves in **3–5 years**

## X vs Y

| | **CRMO** | **Infectious osteomyelitis** | **Osteoid osteoma** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sites | **Multiple** | — | **Solitary** |
| Cultures | **Negative** | Infection evident | — |
| Antibiotics | **No response** | Respond | — |
| X-ray | Metaphyseal lysis + sclerosis | — | **Nidus** |
| NSAIDs | Help | — | Help |

## How it's asked

**"9-yr girl with Crohn's, night pain in forearm + clavicle, normal WBC, ↑ ESR/CRP, bland biopsy?"** → **chronic recurrent multifocal osteomyelitis**\
✗ Wrong options: osteosarcoma · Ewing · osteomyelitis · osteoid osteoma

**"CRMO criteria?"** → **multifocal, culture-negative, no antibiotic response**

**"Skin association?"** → **palmoplantar pustulosis / SAPHO**

**"Treatment?"** → **NSAIDs ± pamidronate**
