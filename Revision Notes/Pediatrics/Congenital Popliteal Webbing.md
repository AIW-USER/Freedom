# Congenital Popliteal Webbing

*Pediatrics · 28 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Also called** — **popliteal pterygium syndrome** · affects face, limbs, genitalia

**Incidence** — rare, **1:300,000** · **M = F**

### Genes & lab markers

**Inheritance** — **autosomal dominant** (or sporadic — **advanced paternal age**)

**Gene** — **IRF6** — transcription factor for head/face, skin, genital development

### Anatomy of the web

| Structure | Position |
|---|---|
| **Subcutaneous fibrous band** | Superficial, along **free edge** (ischium → calcaneus) |
| **Sciatic / peroneal nerve** | **Deep to band**, ~halfway between free edge and apex · often **shortened** |
| **Popliteal artery/vein** | Deep in popliteal space |

## 3 Presentation

**Web** — behind knee from **ischial tuberosity to heel** · usually bilateral · limits extension, abduction, rotation → **knee flexion contracture**

**Other** — **cleft lip/palate** · syndactyly · triangular skin fold over hallux nail · **ankyloblepharon** (eyelids), **syngnathia** (jaws) · hypoplastic labia / **cryptorchidism, bifid scrotum** · cognitive delay · spina bifida

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP/lateral)** — knee flexion contracture

**MRI** — **pre-op planning** to locate neurovascular structures (wide variation, injury risk)

![MRI of popliteal pterygium](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422805-mri.jpg)

## 5 Management

### Femoral shortening with soft-tissue release

| Contracture | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **<20°** | Nonoperative |
| **20–40°** | Hamstring lengthening + post-op splinting |
| **Up to 60°** | **Popliteal Z-plasties** + post-op serial casting (avoid NV tension) |
| **>60°** | **Femoral shortening osteotomy + soft-tissue releases** (Z-plasty, band excision, hamstring lengthening, posterior capsulotomy) · or gradual ex-fix — **recurs fast** without formal soft-tissue procedures + splinting |

**4-month-old, bilateral 100° fixed contractures** → **femoral shortening + soft-tissue releases**\
✗ Wrong options: serial casting · PT/splints · soft tissue alone · frame without release

**Technique** — resect/tenotomise fibrous band · **Z-plasty** skin · sciatic nerve grafting or tendon lengthening if needed

## How it's asked

**"100° popliteal pterygium contractures — most likely to succeed?"** → **femoral shortening osteotomy + soft-tissue releases**

**"Gene?"** → **IRF6, autosomal dominant**

**"Pre-op imaging?"** → **MRI to locate sciatic nerve and vessels**

**"Where is the sciatic nerve?"** → **deep to fibrous band, halfway between free edge and apex**
