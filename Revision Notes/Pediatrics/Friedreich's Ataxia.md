# Friedreich's Ataxia

*Pediatrics · 49 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — inherited **spinocerebellar degeneration**

**Incidence** — **1 in 50,000** · onset **7–25 yr** (Orthobullets) / **7–15 yr** (OB) · earlier with more GAA repeats

### Genes & lab markers

**Inheritance** — **autosomal recessive**

**Gene** — **GAA trinucleotide repeat** in **frataxin (FXN)**, **chromosome 9q13** · frataxin = mitochondrial protein (iron metabolism, oxidative stress)

**Pathology** — **dorsal root ganglia neuronopathy** + loss of peripheral sensory fibres + **posterior column** degeneration · not anterior horn α-motor neuron loss (= **SMA**)

**EMG** — motor + sensory defects, ↑ polyphasic potentials · ↓ NCV in upper limbs

## 3 Presentation

**Classic triad** — **ataxia** (wide-based staggering gait — presenting symptom) · **areflexia** · **positive Babinski (extensor plantar)**

**Other** — weakness · nystagmus · ↓ proprioception · **cardiomyopathy**

**Orthopaedic** — **cavovarus foot** (very high arch, **rigid**, progressive, claw toes; strong plantar reflex + PTT pull into equinovarus) · **scoliosis**

![Cavovarus foot — Coleman block assessment](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4087-colman-block-test-flexible-hindfoot-courtesy-miller-moved.png)

**Most likely condition** — **foot deformity**\
✗ Wrong options: hip dysplasia (CMT) · malignant hyperthermia (myelomeningocele) · tibia vara (Blount)

![Scoliosis in Friedreich's ataxia](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4087-scoli-fre.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **genetic testing** (frataxin)

### Key radiology findings

**Standing scoliosis series** · AP + lateral foot if cavovarus

## 5 Management

**Cardiomyopathy** — **cardiology review before surgery** · coenzyme Q (antioxidant) slows cardiac decline, not ataxia

### Operative criteria

| Problem | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Cavovarus, **early, ambulatory** | **Plantar release + tendon transfers ± MT/calcaneal osteotomy** (tenotomies + bracing early) |
| Cavovarus, **rigid / late / nonambulatory** | **Triple arthrodesis** (bracing/stretching ineffective) |
| Scoliosis **<40°**, no progression predictors | Observe |
| Scoliosis **40–60°** | Grey zone |
| Scoliosis **>60°** or rapid progression with predictors | **PSF + segmental instrumentation**, upper thoracic → lower lumbar (**usually not to pelvis**) |

**Scoliosis progression predictors** — **disease onset <10 yr** · **scoliosis onset <15 yr**

```
Friedreich ataxia
├─ Cavovarus: ambulatory early → soft-tissue releases/transfers ± osteotomy
│      └─ Rigid / nonambulatory → triple arthrodesis
└─ Scoliosis: <40° observe · >60° or predictors → PSF (not to pelvis)
```

## 6 Complications & prognosis

**Wheelchair** — by ~**30** (Orthobullets) / 1st–2nd decade of disease (OB)

**Death** — **cardiomyopathy** (± pneumonia, aspiration), 4th–5th decade (~50)

## X vs Y

| | **Friedreich ataxia** | **CMT** | **SMA** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gene | **Frataxin (AR)** | PMP22 (AD) | Survival motor neuron protein |
| Lesion | DRG + posterior columns + cerebellum | Peripheral myelin | **Anterior horn cells** |
| Reflexes | Areflexia + **Babinski +** | Areflexia | Areflexia, fasciculations |
| Foot | Rigid cavovarus | Cavovarus (flexible → rigid) | — |
| Heart | **Cardiomyopathy** | — | — |

## How it's asked

**"Friedreich's ataxia — all true EXCEPT?"** → **loss of anterior horn α-motor neurons** (SMA)

**"Patients most likely have?"** → **foot deformity (cavovarus)**

**"Scoliosis surgery threshold?"** → **>60° (observe <40°)**

**"Gene + inheritance?"** → **frataxin GAA repeat, autosomal recessive**
