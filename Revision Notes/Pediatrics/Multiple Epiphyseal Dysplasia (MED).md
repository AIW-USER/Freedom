# Multiple Epiphyseal Dysplasia (MED)

*Pediatrics · 69 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — failure of formation of **secondary ossification centres** → irregular, **delayed epiphyseal ossification** at multiple sites → secondary articular cartilage deformity

**Epidemiology** — ~**1 in 10,000** · presents **5–14 yr**

**Sites** — **proximal femur + proximal humerus** most · **hips** = most common joint

### Diagnosis & genetics (COMP)

| Inheritance | Gene | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Autosomal dominant** (most common) | **COMP**, chromosome **19** | **Most common + most severe** |
| AD | **Type IX collagen** (COL9A1/2/3) | Link protein for type II collagen → articular cartilage |
| AD | **Matrilin 3** | — |
| **Autosomal recessive** | **SLC26A2** (diastrophic dysplasia sulfate transporter) | ≥1 finding at birth: clubfoot, cleft palate, clinodactyly |

**Up to ½** of clinical MED have none of the typical mutations

## 3 Presentation

**Features** — **short-limbed, disproportionate dwarfism** (mild) · joint pain · **waddling gait** / bilateral gluteus medius lurch · valgus knees · early OA · contractures · **short stubby fingers + toes**

**Normal** — **spine** · neuro exam · **intelligence** · facies

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**General** — **irregular, delayed ossification at multiple epiphyses** · multiple **OCD** lesions

**Hip** — **bilateral, symmetric** proximal femoral epiphyseal flattening · **early acetabular changes** · **no metaphyseal cysts** · acetabular dysplasia · may have concurrent AVN

**Knee** — valgus · flattened condyles · **double-layer patella**

**Hand / foot** — short stunted metacarpals · hyperextensible fingers · short metatarsals

### Workup - skeletal survey

**Bilateral symmetric femoral head flattening** → **skeletal survey**\
✗ Wrong options: bone scan · MRI · CT · CBC/CRP

**Unilateral flattening** → Perthes (no survey needed)

**Symmetric bilateral + family history of early OA + myopia** → **skeletal survey + COL2A1 genetic screening** (type II collagen disorder mimicking LCPD)\
✗ Wrong options: MRI for AVN · steroids for JIA · Perthes care

**Skeletal survey not for** — CP windswept hips · SCFE · hamstring avulsion · unilateral LCPD

## 5 Management

| Problem | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Early OA | **NSAIDs + PT** |
| Childhood acetabular dysplasia | Often resolves by maturity |
| **Progressive genu varum / valgum** | **Realigning osteotomy or hemiepiphysiodesis** |
| Severe hip arthritis (**by age ~30**) | **THA** |

## X vs Y

| | **MED** | **Bilateral Perthes** | **SED** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hips | **Symmetric, same stage** | **Asymmetric, different stages** (bilateral 10–15%) | Involved |
| Acetabulum | Early changes | Late | — |
| Metaphyseal cysts | **No** | Yes (head-at-risk) | — |
| Spine | **Normal** | Normal | **Involved**, AAI, myelopathy, sharp curve |
| Gene | **COMP**, type IX collagen | — | **Type II collagen** |

## How it's asked

**"9-yr short, valgus knee, waddling, COMP mutation?"** → **multiple epiphyseal dysplasia**\
✗ Wrong options: achondroplasia · diastrophic · Perthes

**"Which X-ray needs a skeletal survey?"** → **symmetric bilateral femoral epiphyseal flattening (MED)**\
✗ Wrong options: unilateral Perthes · SCFE

**"5-yr, mild short stature, bilateral gluteus medius lurch, flat femoral heads?"** → **skeletal survey**

**"6-yr, symmetric bilateral head irregularity, family early OA + myopia?"** → **skeletal survey + COL2A1 sequencing**
