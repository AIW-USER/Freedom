# Metatarsus Adductus

*Pediatrics · 66 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — forefoot **adduction at the tarsometatarsal joint** with **normal hindfoot** · **packaging** disorder (intra-uterine position)

**Epidemiology** — **1 in 1,000** · M = F · **bilateral ~50%** · ↑ late pregnancy, **first pregnancy**, **twins**, **oligohydramnios**

**Associated** — **DDH (15–20%)** · torticollis

## 3 Presentation

### Diagnosis

**History** — parents notice **in-toeing in first year**

**Exam** — adducted forefoot · **convex lateral border** · **medial soft-tissue crease → more rigid** · **normal hindfoot + subtalar motion** · tickle foot to test **active** correction\
✗ Wrong options: straight

**TO case** — 5-month-old, **no medial crease**, gap between 1st/2nd toes, adduction correctable to 10° abduction · options: metatarsus adductus / constriction band / metatarsus primus varus / positional MA · **answer not provided by the source** · no crease + correctable = flexible deformity

### Causes of intoeing

| Condition | Key finding |
|---|---|
| **Metatarsus adductus** | Forefoot medially deviated, **hindfoot normal** · heel bisector lateral |
| **Internal tibial torsion** | **Thigh–foot angle >10° internal** (normal 0–20° external) |
| **Femoral anteversion** | Prone **IR >70°** (normal 30–60°), **ER <20°** · patella points in |

**Work up intoeing if** — pain · LLD · progression · family history of rickets / dysplasia / MPS · rotational profile **>2 SD** from normal

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Bleck classification (heel bisector)

| Grade | Heel bisector passes through |
|---|---|
| **Normal** | **2nd–3rd web space** |
| Mild | 3rd toe |
| Moderate | 3rd–4th web space |
| Severe | 4th–5th web space |

![Heel bisector line](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4061-heel-foot-bisector-2-moved.jpg)

### Berg classification

| Type | Features |
|---|---|
| Simple MTA | MTA |
| Complex MTA | MTA + **lateral shift of midfoot** |
| **Skew foot** | MTA + **valgus hindfoot** |
| Complex skew foot (**serpentine**) | MTA + lateral shift + valgus hindfoot |

**Serpentine foot** — TMT adductus + **talonavicular lateral subluxation** + hindfoot valgus · **casting ineffective**\
✗ Wrong options: like simple MTA

**X-rays** — only in older children

**Other foot differentials** — clubfoot · **atavistic great toe** (congenital hallux varus) · skewfoot

## 5 Management

### Treatment - observation & stretching

**Natural history** — **90% resolve by age 4** · another 5% in early walking years · flexible: **90–95% spontaneous correction**

| Flexibility | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Actively** correctable to midline | **No treatment — reassurance + observation** |
| **Passively** correctable | **Serial stretching by parents** |
| **Rigid**, medial crease | **Serial casting** (goal straight lateral border) · **before 6 months** (OB) |

![Rigid metatarsus adductus with medial crease](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4061-rigid-metatarsus-adductus-courtesy-miller-moved.png)

**Shoes, braces, splints** — efficacy not established · (✗ Wrong options: Ponseti (clubfoot) · Achilles lengthening unless tight heel cord)\
✗ Wrong options: dynamic AFO

### Residual deformity – tarsometatarsal capsulotomy

| Indication | Surgery |
|---|---|
| **Age 2–4 yr**, failed non-op | **TMT capsulotomies** |
| **Age >5 yr**, resistant (medial crease), pain / shoe problems | **Lateral column shortening (cuboid closing wedge) + medial column opening (cuneiform) wedge** · multiple metatarsal osteotomies · abductor hallucis recession (atavistic toe) |
| Symptomatic **serpentine** foot | Calcaneal osteotomy + midfoot osteotomies + **multiple metatarsal osteotomies with pinning + TMT release (Hamen)** · often accepted / observed |

**Surgery timing (OB)** — >3 yr or rigid unresponsive to casting · medial release if symptomatic beyond 4 yr

**Indications (TO)** — pain · objectionable appearance · **difficulty fitting shoes** after the age for stretching/casting has passed

**Prognosis** — residual MTA **not linked to pain or ↓ function** · late **medial cuneiform obliquity**\
✗ Wrong options: hallux valgus

## X vs Y

| | **Metatarsus adductus** | **Clubfoot** | **Skewfoot** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forefoot | Adducted | Adducted | Adducted |
| Hindfoot | **Normal** | **Varus + equinus** | **Valgus** |
| Ankle | Full DF/PF | Equinus | — |
| First line | Observe / stretch / cast | Ponseti | Often accept; osteotomies if symptomatic |

## How it's asked

**"Newborn MTA, corrects actively and passively?"** → **parental reassurance and observation**\
✗ Wrong options: stretching · casting · Ponseti

**"Newborn mild–moderate MTA — initial treatment?"** → **observation with possible stretching**\
✗ Wrong options: serial casting · medial release · dynamic AFO

**"3-yr, moderate MTA, failed stretching/casting, shoe problems?"** → **tarsometatarsal capsulotomies** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: observation · more casting · metatarsal osteotomies
