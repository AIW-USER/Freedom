# Anterolateral Bowing & Congenital Pseudoarthrosis of Tibia

*Pediatrics · 5 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — extremely rare · **1:140,000–190,000** (Orthobullets) · **1 in 250,000** live births (TO)

**Continuum** — **anterolateral bowing → congenital pseudarthrosis of tibia (CPT)** = same disease\
✗ Wrong options: calcaneovalgus · fibular hemimelia · CTEV · CVT

**Pathophysiology** — pseudarthrosis is **not congenital** · develops **post-natally** from fracture nonunion

### NF1 association & natural history (fracture/pseudarthrosis)

**NF1** — **most common association** · in **50–55%** of anterolateral bowing/CPT (Orthobullets, TO) · 50–90% in large series (TO)

**Reverse** — only **6–10% of NF1** patients have anterolateral bowing (TO: **~6%**)\
✗ Wrong options: 20% · 50% · NF2

**Fibrous dysplasia** — **15%**

**First sign** — presents in **1st year** → may be **first recognised manifestation of NF1** · NF does **not** change union rate or outcome (TO)

**NF1 genetics** — **neurofibromin**, **chromosome 17**, usually **spontaneous** mutation · café-au-lait spots

**NF1 orthopaedic manifestations** — **scoliosis** (dystrophic + non-dystrophic), **CPT**, soft-tissue tumours · **nerve sheath tumours** (neurofibromas) → "not associated with NF1?" → **none of the above** (TO)

**Natural history** — does **not** self-resolve · **spontaneous fracture** at site of bowing → nonunion · once fractured, low chance of spontaneous healing\
✗ Wrong options: resolution by 2 yr · no change · spread

### Three types of tibial bowing

| Bowing | Cause | Natural history |
|---|---|---|
| **Anterolateral** | **NF1 / CPT** | **Fracture → pseudarthrosis**; no remodelling |
| **Posteromedial** | **Physiologic** (intrauterine packing) · **calcaneovalgus** foot | Resolves over **5–7 yr** · **LLD 3–4 cm** · rarely fractures |
| **Anteromedial** | **Fibular hemimelia (deficiency)** | — |
| Posterolateral | No specific association | — |

## 3 Presentation

**Onset** — bowing in **first year** of life

**Exam** — anterolateral deformity · **café-au-lait spots** + other NF stigmata

**Fibula** — dysplastic fibula / fibular pseudarthrosis in **two-thirds** (TO) · **one-third** (other TO explanation) — read the options

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — anterolateral bow · **intramedullary sclerosis / narrowed or obliterated canal** at apex (**prefracture stage**) · cystic change · hourglass constriction · tapered, sclerotic ends

### Treatment-guiding criteria

**Classifications** — Boyd, Andersen, Crawford: **none guide management or predict outcome**

**Two practical criteria** — **fracture present or absent** · **age at fracture: early onset <4 yr, late onset >4 yr**

### Boyd classification (TO)

| Type | Feature | Prognosis |
|---|---|---|
| I | Anterior bowing + tibial **defect at birth** | — |
| **II** | Anterior bowing + **hourglass constriction**; fracture **<2 yr** ("high-risk tibia"), sclerotic, canal obliterated | **Most common**, **NF-associated**, **poorest** |
| III | **Congenital cyst** (middle/distal third junction) | Recurrence less than II |
| IV | **Sclerotic segment**, no narrowing; stress fracture | **Good** |
| V | **Dysplastic fibula** | Good if confined to fibula |
| VI | Intraosseous **neurofibroma / schwannoma** | Extremely rare |

### Crawford classification (TO) — all anterolateral

| Type | Feature | Prognosis |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Preserved canal**, cortical thickening at apex | **Good**, may never fracture |
| II | Thinned canal, cortical thickening, trabeculation defect | — |
| III | **Cystic** lesion | **Early fracture** → early treatment |
| IV | Established pseudarthrosis (tibia ± fibula) | — |

**Andersen** — dysplastic, cystic, sclerotic, clubfoot types · **El-Rosasy–Paley–Herzenberg** — I atrophic mobile, no surgery · II atrophic mobile, previous surgery · III broad ends, stiff · **Choi fibula** — A (fibula intact) · B (fibular pseudarthrosis, B1–B3)

## 5 Management

```
Anterolateral bowing / CPT
├─ Bowing, no fracture, ambulatory → brace (clamshell / PTB / AFO anterior shell) to maturity
│      (TO: 8-plate guided growth for cystic, unfractured tibia)
├─ Fracture or pseudarthrosis → resect, realign, graft, IM rod ± frame / vascularised fibula / 4-in-1
└─ Multiple failed unions, severe LLD → Syme / Boyd amputation
```

### Bracing to prevent fracture

**Indication** — ambulatory (weight-bearing) child with **bowing, no fracture/pseudarthrosis**

**Brace** — **clamshell** orthosis · **patellar tendon bearing (PTB)** orthosis · **AFO with anterior shell** · until **skeletal maturity**

**Goal** — **prevent further bowing + fracture**\
✗ Wrong options: correct deformity (bowing is not brace-correctable)

**Contraindicated** — **osteotomy for bowing alone** (→ nonunion)\
✗ Wrong options: observation · IM rod · vascularised fibula · PT

**Posteromedial bowing case** — "pseudarthrosis, brace" is the **false** statement · observation only, LLD, calcaneovalgus association (TO)

### Growth modulation for deformity

**Unfractured anterolateral bowing with cystic change** — **distal tibial anterolateral 8-plate (guided growth)** → gradual cyst resolution + correction (TO, Laine 2020)\
✗ Wrong options: osteotomy + IM nail/plate (nonunion) · 4-in-1 (for established pseudarthrosis)

### Surgical indications & NF1 association

**Surgical indication** — **pseudarthrosis or fracture** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: bowing >45° · failure of brace to correct

**Goals** — resect pseudarthrosis to normal bone · correct alignment · bone graft + stabilise · **intramedullary splinting**

| Technique | Key point |
|---|---|
| **IM rod + bone graft** | Resect, **shorten tibia**, rod, graft |
| **Free vascularised fibula (Farmer)** | **Contralateral** fibula (ipsilateral involved) · when IM nailing + graft fails |
| **Ilizarov / circular frame** | Lengthening or bone transport |
| **4-in-1 (Choi)** | Resect both sclerotic ends + cortical onlay grafts → **tibiofibular cross-union** · for Choi **B2/B3** fibula · + Ilizarov lengthening if shortening >2–3 cm (TO) |
| **Fibula** | Resect fibular pseudarthrosis (↑ union) · osteotomise intact fibula |

**Amputation** — after **multiple failed unions**, severe LLD, dysfunctional deformity · **Syme or Boyd** (ankle disarticulation) preferred over amputation at pseudarthrosis

## 6 Complications

**Refracture** — **≥50%** even after union · decreases with age, ceases after maturity

**Other** — **valgus** deformity · **LLD ~5 cm** at maturity · no treatment gives predictable results

## X vs Y

| | **Anterolateral** | **Posteromedial** | **Anteromedial** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Association | **NF1**, fibrous dysplasia | **Calcaneovalgus foot** | **Fibular deficiency** |
| Fracture risk | **High** | Rare | — |
| Treatment | **Brace** until maturity | **Observe** | Per fibular deficiency |
| Sequel | Pseudarthrosis, refracture | **LLD 3–4 cm** | LLD |

## How it's asked

**"All true about CPT EXCEPT?"** → **20% of NF patients have pseudarthrosis** (✓ anterior angulation commonest, ✓ NF commonest association, ✓ dysplastic fibula) (TO)

**"Nonunited tibial fracture + café-au-lait spots — prior deformity?"** → **anterolateral bowing** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: anteromedial (fibular deficiency) · posteromedial (physiologic)

**"Not associated with NF1?"** → **none of the above** (nerve sheath tumour, CPT, scoliosis all associated) (TO)

**"True statement about NF1 and CPT?"** → **6% of NF1 have CPT** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: 50% of NF1 · NF2

**"Anterolateral bowing is associated with?"** → **congenital pseudarthrosis of tibia**

**"5-month-old, anterolateral bow with medullary sclerosis — natural history?"** → **spontaneous fracture**

**"Chromosome 17 mutation, pseudarthrosis after casting — bowing type?"** → **anterolateral**

**"6-month-old posteromedial bow — NOT true?"** → **"pseudarthrosis, protective bracing"** (TO)

**"2-year-old ambulatory, prefracture CPT?"** → **PTB orthosis**\
✗ Wrong options: osteotomy + rod (nonunion)

**"12-year-old NF, anterolateral bow, intact cortices?"** → **AFO with anterior shell** (brace to maturity)

**"Anterolateral bow with cysts, no fracture — ideal surgery?"** → **growth modulation (8-plate)** (TO)

**"Indication for surgery in CPT?"** → **pseudarthrosis (or fracture)** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: bowing >45° · brace failure
