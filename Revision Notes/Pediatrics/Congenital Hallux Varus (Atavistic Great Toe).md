# Congenital Hallux Varus (Atavistic Great Toe)

*Pediatrics · 27 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — rare idiopathic **varus (medial) deviation of the great toe at the MTP joint** · noticed **after walking age** · few degrees → 90°

**Pathoanatomy** — **firm, fibrous, band-like abductor hallucis** · **short, thick 1st metatarsal** · **longitudinal epiphyseal bracket (LEB)** of 1st MT or proximal phalanx · skeletal dysplasia (**diastrophic dwarfism**)

**Associated** — **polydactyly** (often)

### Longitudinal epiphyseal bracket

**LEB** (delta phalanx / bracket epiphysis) — anomalous **C-shaped secondary ossification centre** along the **medial diaphysis** → blocks transverse/longitudinal growth → progressive shortening + angulation

**Frequency** — 2–14% of congenital hand/foot deformities · **bilateral ~75%** · hand → clinodactyly, foot → **hallux varus**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — cosmetic · **difficult shoe wear** · usually painless

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP, lateral, oblique foot

**Findings** — **short, thick 1st metatarsal** · bracket (if unossified → **ultrasound** shows cartilaginous C-shaped structure)

![Congenital hallux varus — short thick 1st MT](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4076-chv.jpg)

**Differentials** — **metatarsus adductus** · **metatarsus primus varus** (1st MT varus, **no MTP deformity**)

## 5 Management

**Observation** — rarely; deformity **worsens with age** → surgery often in infancy

### Operative criteria

| Situation | Procedure |
|---|---|
| Mild, resistant | **Abductor hallucis release** |
| **Epiphyseal bracket** | **Excision of central bracket (physiolysis)** ± **PMMA / fat interposition** — **early, ideally <1 yr, before ossification** · growth often resumes · may need later realignment/lengthening |
| Moderate–severe | **Farmer** — **syndactylise hallux to 2nd toe** to hold correction |
| Older, ossified bracket, big deformity | Distraction / angular osteotomy |

![Farmer procedure — syndactyly to 2nd toe](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4076-f029-018-9780323374620.jpg)

### Longitudinal epiphyseal bracket – excision

**9-month-old, bilateral hallux varus, unossified C-shaped bracket on US** → **immediate excision of preossified bracket ± PMMA interposition**\
✗ Wrong options: observe until ossified then osteotomy · wait until 3–4 yr · ex-fix distraction · serial casting (soft-tissue deformities only)

## 6 Complications

**Recurrence** · **incomplete correction**

## How it's asked

**"Infant hallux varus with preossified bracket?"** → **early bracket excision ± PMMA**

**"Muscle causing atavistic great toe?"** → **tight band-like abductor hallucis**

**"X-ray finding?"** → **short, thick 1st metatarsal**

**"Moderate–severe deformity — procedure that holds correction?"** → **Farmer (syndactyly to 2nd toe)**
