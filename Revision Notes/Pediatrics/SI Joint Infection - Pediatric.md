# SI Joint Infection - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 97 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Epidemiology** — rare · **1–2% of septic arthritis** · children >10 yr more than adults

**Adult risk factors** — IV drug use · immunosuppression · pregnancy · trauma · endocarditis · sepsis · TB

### Causative organism

| Organism | Note |
|---|---|
| ***S. aureus*** | **Most common** (✗ Wrong options: GBS (neonatal osteomyelitis) · H. influenzae (vaccine)) |
| *M. tuberculosis* | Skeletal TB = 3–5% of TB; ~10% of that at SIJ |
| *Salmonella* | Sickle cell disease |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — progressive **low back + buttock pain** · **can't bear weight** on that side · **fever**

**Exam** — pain with **iliac wing compression** + SI palpation · **normal hip ROM** · **FABER strongly positive**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — AP/lateral pelvis ± inlet/outlet · late: destruction, fusion, periarticular osteopenia, widening, cavitation

**MRI** — **most sensitive** (> CT) · acute: fluid/gas, subchondral edema, enhancement · chronic: marrow reconversion, pannus, erosion, sclerosis, ankylosis

**Labs** — ↑ WBC · **ESR >50** · **CRP >70** · **blood cultures before antibiotics** (often guide treatment; SIJ aspiration usually unnecessary)

## 5 Management

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **First line** | **Organism-targeted IV antibiotics** (cover S. aureus if cultures negative) |
| Abscess · failed antibiotics · sequestrum | **Debridement** (rare in children) |
| Chronic · sequestrum with joint destruction · recurrent | **Debridement + SI arthrodesis** (longer, more complications) |

![SI joint arthrodesis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4033-si-1.jpg)

## 6 Complications

**List** — chronic pain · recurrence · wound healing delay · hardware problems · sepsis

## How it's asked

**"Most common organism in acute pediatric SI septic arthritis?"** → **Staphylococcus aureus**\
✗ Wrong options: GBS · TB · Salmonella
