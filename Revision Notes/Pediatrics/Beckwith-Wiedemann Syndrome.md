# Beckwith-Wiedemann Syndrome

*Pediatrics · 7 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **1 in 13,700** births · M:F **1:1**

**Type** — **overgrowth syndrome**

### Genes & lab markers

**Locus** — **chromosome 11p15.5**

**Mechanism** — overactivity of **IGF-2** (growth factor) and/or loss of active **CDKN1C** (cell-proliferation inhibitor)

**Inheritance** — **sporadic 85%** · **AD with incomplete penetrance 15%**

**Lab** — **neonatal hypoglycemia** (✗ Wrong options: hyperglycemia) from **pancreatic islet cell hypertrophy** (✗ Wrong options: pancreatic insufficiency) → repeated bouts → **spasticity**, altered development

## 3 Presentation

### Diagnostic criteria

| Major | Minor |
|---|---|
| **Overgrowth** (large for gestational age, gigantism) | **Hemihypertrophy** — **10–20%** (Orthobullets) · **~15%** (OB) |
| **Abdominal wall defects** incl. **omphalocele** | **Ear anomalies** (linear lobe creases) |
| **Macroglossia** | **Neonatal hypoglycemia** |
| | **Nephromegaly** |

**Exam** — macroglossia · midface hypoplasia · infraorbital creases · anterior linear ear-lobe creases · **visceromegaly** · exophthalmos · **hemihypertrophy** (whole side incl. foot)

**No skin markings** — absence of nevi / vascular markings excludes **NF, Proteus, Klippel-Trenaunay** as causes of hemihypertrophy

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **physical exam + genetic testing**

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — **comparison view** of affected vs contralateral limb (LLD)

**Bone age** — inaccurate when very young · later useful to **time epiphysiodesis**\
✗ Wrong options: as initial study at age 2

## 5 Management

### Wilms tumour screening

**Predisposition** — **embryonal tumours**: **Wilms tumour** (most common), **hepatoblastoma**, adrenal neoplasm · hemihypertrophy from BWS → **40% malignancy risk** (OB)

**Screening** — **renal + abdominal ultrasound every 3–6 months until 5–8 years** (Orthobullets) · until **~7 years** (OB) · intervals ≤4 months ↓ late-stage disease

**Tumour side** — may be **contralateral** to hemihypertrophy

```
Child with hemihypertrophy (BWS)
├─ Screening → abdominal/renal US q3–6 mo until 5–8 yr (Wilms)
└─ LLD → serial limb radiographs, bone age later
       └─ Predicted significant LLD → epiphysiodesis of affected (longer) limb
```

### Operative criteria

**Epiphysiodesis** — **growth arrest of the affected (overgrown) limb** for LLD

**Multidisciplinary** — orthopaedic, abdominal and overgrowth issues

## How it's asked

**"Large-for-dates neonate with hypoglycemia, now right hemihypertrophy + 2 cm LLD — next imaging?"** → **renal and abdominal ultrasonography**\
✗ Wrong options: left-wrist bone age · MRI spine/brain · hip US

**"11p15.5 mutation, hemihypertrophy, macroglossia, omphalocele — screen every 3–6 months for?"** → **Wilms tumor**\
✗ Wrong options: hearing loss · hyperglycemia · pancreatic insufficiency · vision loss

**"Cause of infantile hypoglycemia in BWS?"** → **pancreatic islet cell hypertrophy**

**"Treatment of BWS limb overgrowth?"** → **epiphysiodesis** of affected limb
