# Tibial Shaft Fractures - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 107 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Epidemiology** — **3rd most common pediatric long-bone fracture** · **15% of pediatric fractures** · boys > girls · **mean age 8 yr** · **39% mid-diaphysis** · fibula fractured in 30%

**NAT** — **2nd most common bone fractured in non-accidental trauma** · suspect abuse in non-ambulatory child

**Anatomy** — triangular, apex anterior · anteromedial border subcutaneous · distal flare mostly cancellous · **posterior tibial a.** → nutrient + periosteal vessels · **anterior tibial a.** at risk through interosseous membrane · fibula carries 6–17% of load

## 2 Mechanism

**Adolescent** — **pedestrian vs vehicle (50%)** · direct blow

**Toddler** — low-energy twist / fall → spiral or oblique ("toddler's fracture")

## 3 Presentation

**Findings** — pain, bruising · **refuses to bear weight** · tenderness · **pain on ankle dorsiflexion** · always think **compartment syndrome**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **AP + lateral tib/fib** · **image ipsilateral knee + ankle** · ± contralateral · toddler's fracture may be **normal initially** → repeat at **2 wk** for callus / periosteal reaction

**CT** — physeal / intra-articular extension (distal third) · pathologic lesion

**MRI** — pathologic / stress fracture · occult fracture

**Bone scan** — occult fracture

| Pattern | Description |
|---|---|
| Incomplete | **Greenstick** of tibia ± fibula |
| Complete | Tibia ± fibula fracture or plastic deformation |
| **Toddler's fracture** | **Nondisplaced spiral / oblique tibia, intact fibula, age <2.5 yr** |

### Toddler's fracture

**Typical** — afebrile · normal WBC / ESR / CRP · distal tibial tenderness · X-rays often negative · usually unwitnessed

**Treatment** — **long leg cast** · heals **3–4 wk**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · aspiration · vitamin D / metabolic labs · child services · chromosomal analysis

**Not** — osteomyelitis / subperiosteal abscess (afebrile, normal labs) · NOF · NAT (age + story fit)

## 5 Management

### Operative criteria

**Acceptable alignment** — translation **50%** (Orthobullets, OB: <50% = >50% cortical overlap) · shortening **<1 cm** · angulation **<5–10°** sagittal + coronal (Orthobullets) · **<10°** (OB210163) · **<5°** (OB218688) — read the options

| Method | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Long leg cast** | Toddler's · greenstick |
| **Closed reduction + long leg cast** | **Most traumatic fractures** with acceptable reduction |
| **External fixator** | Extensive soft-tissue injury · length unstable · polytrauma |
| **Flexible IM nails** | Open / closed in skeletally immature · multiple long bones · **floating knee** |
| Percutaneous pins | Noncomminuted unstable oblique (± cast) |
| **Rigid IM nail** | **At / near skeletal maturity** (✗ Wrong options: open physes) |
| Plate | Physeal / articular extension · length unstable · nonunion / malunion |

**Reasons to operate (Ho)** — open fracture · obesity · compartment concern · segmental · polytrauma · floating knee · failed non-op

### Treatment - casting vs flexible nailing

**Casting technique** — sedation / GA · cast **to groin, knee flexed 30°**, molded · ± bivalve · loss of reduction → **cast wedging**

**Early weight bearing** — WB at **1 wk** in long leg cast (knee ~10°) is **safe and may speed healing** (Jenkins: +0.3 days healing per day delayed)\
✗ Wrong options: NWB 4–6 wk · short leg cast · flexible nails first

**Distal tibia fractures** — **high secondary displacement** → **follow-up within 1–2 wk** · most non-op\
✗ Wrong options: >1 month

**Casting vs flexible IMN** — only real difference: **casting = prolonged immobilization** (>3 months) · **compartment syndrome more with IMN** (up to 20%) · union, malunion, LLD similar

**Elastic nails** — **period of casting / splint + NWB** after (nails don't control rotation, bending) · **no 50-kg contraindication** (unlike femur) · open fractures OK\
✗ Wrong options: soft dressing + WBAT · added ex-fix

| Complication | Rate |
|---|---|
| Ex-fix nonunion | ~2% · **refracture** · pin tract infection |
| Flexible nail nonunion | ~10% |

### Valgus deformity

| Fibula | Drift |
|---|---|
| **Intact** | **Varus** |
| **Fractured** (both bones) | **Valgus + recurvatum (procurvatum, OB)** |

**Why valgus** — lateral compartment pull + pes anserinus · **gentle varus mold** (too much → varus malunion) · residual → wedging

## 6 Complications

### Compartment syndrome

**Compartment syndrome** — less common than adults · open + closed · signs in kids: **3 As — analgesia ↑, anxiety, agitation** · firm leg with pulses → **4-compartment fasciotomy**\
✗ Wrong options: more narcotics · nerve block · PCA · observe

**LLD** — age <10 · comminution → overgrowth · pins → growth arrest, **recurvatum from tibial tubercle arrest**

**Angular deformity** — complex · **valgus + apex posterior** · physeal extension · osteotomy for rotational malunion / symptomatic

**Nonunion** — **25% of open fractures** · ↑ age, wound severity · hypertrophic → graft + rigid fixation

**Healing** — **3–4 wk toddler's** · **6–8 wk** others

## How it's asked

**"23-mo refuses to bear weight, normal labs, X-rays negative?"** → **long leg cast**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI pelvis · knee aspiration · vitamin D

**"3-yr won't bear weight, afebrile, normal CRP/ESR — diagnosis?"** → **nondisplaced oblique / spiral tibia, intact fibula**\
✗ Wrong options: osteomyelitis · NAT · NOF

**"2-yr 11-mo fall, spiral distal tibia?"** → **long leg cast**\
✗ Wrong options: child services · MRI

**"Casting vs flexible IMN — casting has more?"** → **prolonged immobilization**\
✗ Wrong options: compartment syndrome · nonunion · LLD

**"Titanium elastic nailing — TRUE?"** → **may need a period of casting**\
✗ Wrong options: higher union · >50 kg contraindicated · avoid in open

**"13-yr ATV, elastic nails — post-op?"** → **NWB in splint or cast**\
✗ Wrong options: soft bandage + WBAT

**"7-yr tree fall — maximize healing?"** → **closed reduction + long leg cast, WB at 1 wk**\
✗ Wrong options: NWB 4–6 wk · short leg cast · nailing

**"11-yr distal tibia, long leg cast — TRUE?"** → **follow-up within 1–2 wk (secondary displacement)**\
✗ Wrong options: most need surgery · rigid nail

**"9-yr tib + fib fracture in cast — likely deformity?"** → **valgus**\
✗ Wrong options: varus · procurvatum

**"3-yr, 30 h after fracture, firm leg, pulses present, pain despite morphine?"** → **4-compartment fasciotomy**\
✗ Wrong options: nerve block · PCA · observe
