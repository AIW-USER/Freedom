# Lateral Condyle Fracture - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 58 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **2nd most common pediatric elbow fracture** (after supracondylar) · 17% of distal humerus fractures · **11%** of elbow injuries · peak age **6 yr**

**Signature** — **highest nonunion rate** about the pediatric elbow · ↑ malunion, AVN

**Physeal pattern** — usually **Salter-Harris IV** (Orthobullets, OB) · TO: Milch II "is really SH II", Milch I = SH IV — read the options

### Pathoanatomy

**Mechanism** — **pull-off** (avulsion by common extensors) · **push-off** (FOOSH → radial head impacts lateral condyle)

**Fracture line** — starts **posterior** distal humeral metaphysis → distal + anterior across physis + epiphysis into joint · may extend into **trochlear groove** → unstable, prone to dislocation

**Posteromedial dislocation** — rare association · FOOSH, slight flexion + **adduction** · **LCL stays intact**, attached to condylar fragment + radial neck\
✗ Wrong options: LCL repair

### Blood supply - avoid posterior dissection

**Supply** — distal humerus mainly from **posterior** anastomotic vessels · brachial artery anterior

**ORIF rule** — **avoid posterior dissection** → **AVN of lateral condyle** (and trochlea)

### Ossification ages

| Centre | Appears (yr) | Fuses (yr) |
|---|---|---|
| **Capitellum** | **1** | 12–14 |
| **Radial head** | **3** | 14–16 |
| **Internal (medial) epicondyle** | **5** | 16–18 |
| **Trochlea** | **7** | 12–14 |
| **Olecranon** | **9** | 15–17 |
| **External (lateral) epicondyle** | **11** | 12–14 |

**OB ages** — capitellum first 6 months · radial head 3–6 · medial epicondyle 5–7 · trochlea 8 · olecranon 10 · **lateral epicondyle last, 12–14 yr in males** (OB answer) · **females ~1 yr earlier**

**Trap** — appearance and fusion are independent events

## 3 Presentation

**History** — FOOSH · lateral elbow pain + swelling · may be subtle

**Exam** — **no obvious deformity** · lateral tenderness · pain with resisted wrist flexion/extension · crepitus\
✗ Wrong options: supracondylar look

**Lateral ecchymosis** — tear of **brachioradialis aponeurosis** → **unstable** fracture

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **AP, lateral + INTERNAL oblique** · internal oblique shows **maximum displacement** (fracture is **posterolateral**)\
✗ Wrong options: external oblique (TO EXCEPT trap)

**Findings** — capitellum displaced laterally vs radial head · **posterior Thurston-Holland** fragment on lateral

**Arthrogram** — minimally displaced fractures · assess cartilage when epiphysis unossified · dynamic · **preferred over MRI**

**MRI** — cartilaginous trochlear integrity · planning of delayed/nonunion · expensive, needs sedation

**CT** — rarely, only if fracture type unclear · stress views **not** recommended

![Internal oblique view shows true displacement](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4009-internal-oblique-view-demonstrating-a-fracture-of-the-latera.jpg)

### Milch classification

| Type | Fracture line | Stability |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Lateral** to trochlear groove | Stable · less common |
| **II** | Into **trochlear groove** | **Unstable** · **more common** (95%, TO) |

![Milch classification](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4009-milch-classification.jpg)

### Weiss classification & treatment

| Type | Displacement | Cartilage | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **<2 mm** | Intact hinge | **Long arm cast** |
| **2** | **2–4 mm** (Orthobullets) · >2 mm (TO) | **Intact** on arthrogram | **CRPP** |
| **3** | **>4 mm** (Orthobullets) · >2 mm (TO) | **Disrupted** | **Open reduction + fixation** |

**Prognosis (TO)** — type 3 complications **>3×** type 2 · type 3 = **highest nonunion risk** (✓ even after K-wires)

## 5 Management

```
Lateral condyle fracture
├─ <2 mm all views, hinge intact → long arm cast 4–6 wk, weekly X-ray ×3 wk
├─ 2–4 mm, cartilage intact → CRPP
└─ >4 mm / incongruity → open reduction + fixation
       (anterolateral approach, no posterior dissection)
```

### Operative criteria

**Cast** — only if **<2 mm in ALL views** + **intact medial cartilaginous hinge** · elbow ~90° · **weekly X-rays (incl. internal oblique) for 3 wk** · occasionally >6 wk

**Surgery** — **>2 mm on any view** = unstable → fixation

### Treatment (ORIF displaced)

**Approach** — **anterolateral** (blood supply posterior) · dissection often done by injury · **see joint reduction directly** · metaphysis may not fit perfectly (plastic deformation)

**Fixation** — **2 smooth pins** (3 if comminuted), parallel or **divergent** (most stable) · **smooth pins** because fixation **crosses the physis** · single **cannulated screw** for large fragment / nonunion → compression + early motion · no plate · 3–6 wk above-elbow cast

**Closed reduction** — rarely succeeds for type 3 · CRPP can't inspect articular surface\
✗ Wrong options: CRPP in widely displaced

**With posteromedial dislocation** — **open reduction + percutaneous or internal fixation**\
✗ Wrong options: LCL repair · cast

## 6 Complications

### Nonunion, cubitus valgus & tardy ulnar palsy

**Stiffness** — **most common** · may be early sign of delayed/nonunion · 90% motion by 24 wk, full by 48 wk

**Delayed union** — not healed after **6 wk** · risk: seen **>2 wk** after injury

**Nonunion causes** — **pull of wrist extensors** (fragment rotates up to 180°, TO) · **intra-articular synovial fluid** · poor metaphyseal circulation / small metaphyseal fragment · instability in cast · risk ↑ **nonoperative care**

| Nonunion scenario | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Asymptomatic, isolated** | **Observation** |
| **Painful** / unstable / valgus + ulnar palsy | **ORIF of nonunion** (minimal posterior stripping) ± bone graft (✗ Wrong options: fragment excision → valgus) |
| **Painless** nonunion (OB3302) | **In situ screw + bone graft** |
| Untreated 4 months, painful | **ORIF** (✗ Wrong options: late percutaneous pins · cast · MRI) |
| **Well-established** (TO) | **Osteotomy** (varus) · avoid arthrotomy / articular realignment → stiffness, AVN |

**Nonunion goal** — union of **metaphyseal fragment**, not joint surface

**Cubitus valgus** — from **nonunion** (most common) or lateral physeal arrest · ~**10%** · less common than cubitus varus

**Tardy ulnar nerve palsy** — slow **stretch** palsy from cubitus valgus · **lateral condyle nonunion = most common cause** (TO) · counsel **ring + small finger numbness** · also after cubitus varus (nerve dislocates anterior to medial epicondyle, FCU band, TO) · treat: **supracondylar osteotomy after maturity + ulnar nerve transposition**

**AVN** — 1–3 yr after fracture · from **posterior dissection**, most often in treatment of nonunion/delayed union

**Fishtail deformity** — gap between medial + lateral ossification centres fails · usually no dysfunction · supracondylar osteotomy if needed

![Cubitus valgus after nonunion](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4009-illustration-depicting-cubitus-valgus.jpg)

### Lateral spur/overgrowth

**Incidence** — up to **50%** regardless of treatment → **counsel families in advance**

**Facts** — size **correlates with initial displacement** · **independent of age** · **does NOT affect final ROM** · gives **pseudo-varus** look, not true varus · no trimming asymptomatic spur · lateral periosteal realignment prevents it\
✗ Wrong options: caused by surgery

**Growth arrest** — rare · young → bar resection or osteotomy · older → complete epiphysiodesis + osteotomy

**Prognosis** — worse than supracondylar (articular, missed diagnosis, nonunion) · with dislocation: slower ROM, same final ROM

## X vs Y

| | **Lateral condyle** | **Medial condyle (TO)** |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 11% | **1%** |
| Main complication | **Nonunion** → cubitus **valgus** | **Missed diagnosis** (mistaken for medial epicondyle) |
| Late deformity | Valgus + tardy ulnar palsy | Nonunion → cubitus **varus**, trochlear AVN |
| Pitfall | — | Trochlea unossified until ~8 yr · complication rate up to 33% |
| Displaced | ORIF | ORIF |

✗ Wrong options: CRPP · plate

**"6-yr, displaced — best treatment?"** → **ORIF with smooth pins or cannulated screw**\
✗ Wrong options: cast 3–8 wk · plate

✗ Wrong options: medial condyle · transphyseal separation

**"3-yr, lateral condyle + posteromedial dislocation?"** → **open reduction + fixation**\
✗ Wrong options: LCL repair · CRPP

**"ORIF — avoid dissection where?"** → **posterior**
