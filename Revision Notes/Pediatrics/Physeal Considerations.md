# Physeal Considerations

*Pediatrics · 83 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Pediatric bone** — **elastic** → buckle + greenstick fractures · **remodeling**: faster **in plane of joint motion** (sagittal at wrist) and at **most active physes** · intra-articular fractures still need reduction

**Most active physes** — UE: **proximal humerus, distal radius** ("away from the elbow") · LE: **distal femur, proximal tibia**, distal tibia ("at the knee")

**Skeletal age** — differs from chronologic age by **>1 yr in 26%** · maturity ~**14 girls / 16 boys**

### Laws of the physis

| Law | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Hueter–Volkmann** | ↑ **compression slows** longitudinal growth |
| **Delpech** | ↑ **tension speeds** growth |
| **Wolff** | Bone adapts to the loads placed on it |
| ✗ Wrong option: **Maxwell** | Not a physeal law (TO) |

### Physeal zones & fracture plane (hypertrophic zone)

| Zone | Function | Disease |
|---|---|---|
| **Reserve** | Stores lipid, glycogen, proteoglycan · **low O2** | **Gaucher** · Kniest · pseudoachondroplasia |
| **Proliferative** | Chondrocyte stacking → **longitudinal growth** · **most matrix production** · ↑ O2 inhibits calcification | **Achondroplasia** · gigantism |
| **Hypertrophic** — maturation, degeneration (cells ×5), **provisional calcification** | Chondrocyte death → calcium release → matrix calcifies · **type X collagen** · PTHrP / Indian hedgehog | **SCFE (non-renal)** · **rickets (provisional calcification)** · enchondroma · MPS · Schmid · **physeal fractures** |
| **Primary spongiosa** | Vascular invasion, osteoblasts on cartilage bars → woven bone | **Metaphyseal corner fracture (abuse)** |
| **Secondary spongiosa** | Internal remodeling (lamellar bone) + funnelization | **Renal SCFE** |

**Fracture plane** — physeal fractures run through the **hypertrophic zone**, specifically the **zone of provisional calcification** (weakest in tension; calcified/uncalcified transition) · applies to SH I, II and III (OB) · chronic gymnast wrist injury → same zone

**Reserve + proliferative zones** stay with epiphysis, blood supply preserved → good prognosis in SH I–II · **TO: SH III + IV involve the reserve zone** (germinal cells) → ↑ arrest risk · SH V crush of germinal layer

### Perichondral ring (Ranvier, LaCroix)

| Structure | Role |
|---|---|
| **Groove (zone) of Ranvier** | Fibrous ring in 1st year · **↑ mechanical strength** · **appositional growth** · source of **osteochondroma** |
| **Perichondral ring of LaCroix** | Dense fibrous limiting membrane · **peripheral stability** · up to **50% of shear resistance** · injured in SH VI |
| **Perichondral artery** | Major nutrition of physis |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Salter-Harris classification

| Type | Path |
|---|---|
| I | Through physis (separation) |
| **II** | Physis → exits **metaphysis** · **Thurston-Holland fragment** · **most common (>50%)** · periosteum torn on convex (tension) side, intact on concave |
| III | Physis → exits **epiphysis** (intra-articular) |
| IV | Epiphysis → physis → metaphysis |
| V | **Crush** of physis · diagnosed late |

![Salter-Harris classification](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4002-salter.jpg)

### Physeal vs ligamentous ankle injury

**Pediatric inversion injury, normal X-ray** → **lateral ligament sprain** on MRI (more common than SH I)\
✗ Wrong options: physeal widening · bone bruise · peroneal tear

### Bone age estimation

| Method | Film / basis |
|---|---|
| **Greulich-Pyle / Tanner-Whitehouse** | **Left hand + wrist** (right more often injured; anthropology convention) (TO) |
| TW-III RUS | Distal radius, ulna, short bones · correlates with PHV |
| **Sauvegrain** | **Left elbow** · 27 points: lateral condyle, trochlea, olecranon apophysis, proximal radial epiphysis |
| Olecranon method | 6-monthly age: girls 11–13, boys 13–15 |
| **Risser** | Iliac apophysis 0–4 ossification, 5 fused · variable |
| **Peak height velocity** | Better than Risser/age · **8 cm/yr girls, 9.5 cm/yr boys** |
| **Sanders** | Phalanx, metacarpal, distal radius epiphyses · predicts scoliosis progression |

**Hand dominance** — **2–3 yr** (third year) · strong preference <2 yr → neuro deficit · persistent ambidexterity normal

## 5 Management

**Treatment** — closed reduction vs CRPP vs ORIF by pattern · **intra-articular must be anatomic**

**Displaced SH III/IV distal tibia (11-yr)** → **ORIF with fixation PARALLEL to physis** (epiphyseal or metaphyseal screw) + NWB cast · ankle physeal arrest up to **50%** → long follow-up\
✗ Wrong options: transphyseal lag screw · closed manipulation

### Growth contribution by physis

| Physis | Growth |
|---|---|
| **Distal femur** | **9–10 mm/yr** (✓ greatest LLD if arrested) |
| Proximal tibia | 6 mm/yr |
| Distal radius | 5–6 mm/yr |
| Proximal femur | 3 mm/yr |
| Distal humerus | 2–3 mm/yr |
| **Humerus** | **Proximal 80% · distal 20%** |

**12-yr boy distal femoral arrest** → 4 yr × 10 mm = **40 mm**

## 6 Complications

### Growth arrest - location, mechanism & follow-up

**Mechanism** — fracture through all zones (SH III/IV) → **vascular invasion across physis** → osteoblasts → **bony bar**\
✗ Wrong options: LaCroix proliferation · Wolff · Hueter-Volkmann

**Pattern** — complete arrest → **shortening** · partial → **angulation**

**Most common site of post-traumatic arrest** — **distal (medial) tibia** · also high: distal femur, distal ulna · rare: distal radius, proximal humerus

**Implant violation** — **<7% of physeal area unlikely to arrest** (Mäkelä: 7% arrested, 3% didn't) · retrograde nail through 5% central physis, screws above physis → **injury unlikely**

**Follow-up** — **≥2 yr** after physeal injury · multiple X-rays needed\
✗ Wrong options: one film · stop at 14/16

| Bar | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **<50%** of physis + **>2 yr or 2 cm growth left** | **Bar resection + interposition** (fat / wax) |
| **>50%** | **Complete ipsilateral arrest** ± contralateral epiphysiodesis / ipsilateral lengthening |

![Angular deformity from partial arrest](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4002-deformity.jpg)

### Little League elbow outcome — Panner disease

**Panner (osteochondrosis of capitellum)** — **age 5–10** · repetitive valgus → ischemia of whole capitellar epiphysis → flattening, fragmentation, reossification (like Perthes) · **symptomatic** (pain, swelling, ↓ motion)

**Outcome** — non-op succeeds · **full motion returns** · X-ray irregularity / sclerosis often **persists**\
✗ Wrong options: anatomic restoration · incidental · spontaneous · high failure

**vs capitellar OCD** — focal lesion, **>10 yr**

**TO points** — physeal injuries \~**20%** of skeletal injuries · SH frequency **II > I > III > IV > V** · injury near physis may **stimulate overgrowth** · ligaments stronger than bone in children · **plastic deformation** = microscopic osteon realignment without cortical break · remodelling ↑ with younger age, proximity to physis, angulation in plane of joint motion · **rotation does not remodel**

## How it's asked

**"Which physeal injury involves the reserve zone?"** → **both type III and IV** (TO)

**"Law NOT pertaining to growth plate?"** → **Maxwell's law** (TO)

**"Zone affected in SH II?"** → **hypertrophic zone**

**"5-yr SH II distal femur, injury through which layer?"** → **lower hypertrophic**

**"5-yr SH III ankle → bar a year later — layer?"** → **hypertrophic zone, provisional calcification**

**"12-yr gymnast chronic wrist pain — location?"** → **zone of provisional calcification**

**"10-yr SH I distal femur — zone?"** → **hypertrophic**\
✗ Wrong options: primary spongiosa

**"11-yr FOOSH physeal fracture — zone?"** → **hypertrophic (D)**

**"Correct match?"** → **SH III: provisional calcification**\
✗ Wrong options: Gaucher-proliferative · renal SCFE-degenerative · achondroplasia-reserve

**"7-yr large Thurston-Holland — zone?"** → **hypertrophic zone (D)**

**"12-yr SH II distal tibia — region damaged?"** → **where matrix calcification occurs**\
✗ Wrong options: LaCroix · most matrix production

**"Ranvier + LaCroix — true?"** → **↑ mechanical strength of physis**

**"Distal humerus physis contributes?"** → **20%**

**"Arrest causing greatest LLD at 12?"** → **distal femur**

**"11-yr displaced SH IV ankle?"** → **ORIF with fixation parallel to physis + NWB cast**

**"Post-traumatic arrest most common at?"** → **distal tibia**

**"Mechanism of premature arrest?"** → **vascular invasion across physis**

**"Retrograde nail violated 5% of physis — counsel?"** → **physeal injury unlikely**

**"SH IV distal femur, normal at 6 months — follow-up?"** → **at least 2 yr**

**"Twisted ankle, normal X-ray — MRI?"** → **lateral ligament complex injury**

**"8-yr pitcher, Panner disease — true?"** → **full elbow motion usually returns**

**"Hand dominance by?"** → **2–3 yr**
