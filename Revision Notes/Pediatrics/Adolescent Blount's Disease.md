# Adolescent Blount's Disease

*Pediatrics · 3 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & epidemiology

**Definition** — progressive pathologic **genu varum** centred at proximal tibia, onset **>10 years** · idiopathic proximal tibia vara

**Risk factors** — **obesity** (causative) · **African-American** descent · more common in males (OB)

**Associated** — **hypertension** (often undiagnosed, under-treated, modifiable) · vitamin D deficiency prevalence = healthy children\
✗ Wrong options: rickets

**Onset vs diagnosis** — deformity often begins 4–10 yr · diagnosed as adolescent form **after age 10** (OB)

### Pathophysiology

**Cause** — **dyschondrosis of medial proximal tibial physis** (posteromedial) · **mechanical overload** in **genetically susceptible** child

**Deformity** — genu varum + procurvatum + internal tibial torsion

**Associated deformity** — **distal femoral varus** + **distal tibial valgus** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: compensatory distal femoral valgus (that's infantile)

## 3 Presentation

**Hallmark** — **genu varum**, usually **unilateral** · obesity · "waddle"

**Other** — LLD secondary to deformity · mild–moderate **MCL laxity** · medial knee pain

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnosis – obesity & late-onset tibia vara

**Diagnosis** — clinical genu varum + radiographic **↑ metaphyseal–diaphyseal angle**

**Differentials ruled out** — congenital hypothyroidism (presents earlier) · metabolic syndrome (normal A1c, TGs) · primary varus gonarthrosis (adult) · vitamin D deficiency/rickets (**bilateral** femoral + tibial bowing)

**Physiologic** — genu varum normal **<2 years**

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **standing long-cassette AP both lower limbs**, **patellae forward** (internal tibial torsion common)

**Findings** — narrowing of tibial epiphysis · **widening of medial tibial physis** · occasional widening of lateral distal femoral physis · **metaphyseal beaking less common** than infantile

**Metaphyseal–diaphyseal (Drennan) angle** — line through metaphyseal beaks vs perpendicular to tibial axis · **>16° abnormal**

**Tibiofemoral angle** — femoral vs tibial longitudinal axes

![Metaphyseal–diaphyseal (Drennan) angle](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4051-metaphyseal-diaphyseal-angle-moved.jpg)

**Classification** — **no radiographic classification** for adolescent form (Langenskiöld = infantile) · severity graded mild / moderate / severe by **mechanical axis deviation (MAD)**

## 5 Management

**Rule** — **surgery** (TO) · observation/bracing **unlikely to work** — progressive, never resolves spontaneously · untreated → medial pain, altered kinematics, **early arthritis**\
✗ Wrong options: orthotics

```
Adolescent Blount
├─ Mild–moderate, growth remaining (>2 yr) → lateral tibial ± fibular hemiepiphysiodesis
└─ Severe / near or at maturity (<2 yr growth) → proximal tibial osteotomy
       ├─ No LLD → focal dome osteotomy at CORA
       ├─ LLD corrected by opening wedge → medial opening wedge HTO + plate
       └─ LLD too large → osteotomy + external fixator (gradual)
```

**Distal femoral varus ≥8°** → **distal femoral osteotomy or epiphysiodesis**

### Operative criteria

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Mild–moderate + significant growth remaining | **Lateral tibia + fibula hemiepiphysiodesis** (8-plate / staple / permanent) |
| Closed physes / insufficient growth (e.g. 15 yr ♀, triradiate fused) | **Osteotomy** (✗ Wrong options: growth modulation) |
| Varus + LLD 2 cm, physes closed | **Medial opening wedge HTO + plate** |
| LLD not correctable by opening wedge | Osteotomy + **external fixator** |
| Distal femoral varus **≥8°** | Distal femoral osteotomy / epiphysiodesis |
| Goal | **Neutral mechanical axis** (✗ Wrong options: overcorrection into valgus (that's infantile)) |

### Treatment – hemiepiphysiodesis (failure) & osteotomy

**Principle** — **Hueter-Volkmann law**: lateral tether slows lateral growth → medial "catches up"\
✗ Wrong options: flexible nails = secondary healing · VDRO · Ilizarov distraction · lag screw = primary healing

**Transient hemiepiphysiodesis** — **8-plate or staple** · removable · needs significant growth · watch for physeal arrest or **rebound** growth

**Permanent hemiepiphysiodesis** — lateral physeal ablation · overcorrection uncommon · no rotational correction · does not preclude later osteotomy

**Residual deformity** — up to **25%** need formal osteotomy

**Acute HTO + internal fixation** — closing, opening, dome, serrated, inclined osteotomies · immediate correction · risks **neurologic injury** (acute lengthening) + **compartment syndrome** → consider **prophylactic fasciotomy** · PWB 6–8 wk

**Gradual correction (Taylor Spatial Frame / Ilizarov)** — 12–18 wk · **limits NV compromise + compartment syndrome** · all planes + length · cons: pin-site infection, duration, bulk

### Paley osteotomy rules (tibia vara)

| Rule | Osteotomy vs ACA/CORA | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Osteotomy **through ACA-CORA** | Pure angulation, **no translation** |
| **2** | ACA through CORA, osteotomy elsewhere | Angulation + **bone-end translation**, axes **collinear** |
| **3** | Osteotomy through ACA, CORA at another level | No bone-end translation, axes **parallel but translated** |

**Best osteotomy** — **focal dome centred on CORA** → restores alignment, minimal axis translation + shortening, least bone-end translation, most bone contact\
✗ Wrong options: intra-articular (chondral/cruciate risk) · horizontal (translates ends + axes)

## 6 Complications

**Most common complication of growth modulation** — **implant failure** (screw breakage) (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: recurrence (infantile / genu valgum) · complete physeal arrest (not seen) · failure of correction (severe physeal disease, rare)

**Commonest failure site** — **tibial metaphyseal screw**

| Hardware-failure risk factor | ✓ ↑ risk | ✗ distractor |
|---|---|---|
| Deformity | **Greater severity** | — |
| Onset | **Earlier** (younger) | Later onset |
| Screws | **Cannulated** | Solid screws |
| BMI | **Higher** | Lower BMI |
| Metal | **Titanium** → use **stainless steel** | — |
| Construct | Staple = screw-plate (equal) | Staple constructs |

**Other** — undercorrection (too little growth) · **rebound deformity** · NV injury, compartment syndrome (acute osteotomy) · pin-site infection (frame)

## X vs Y

| | **Infantile Blount** | **Adolescent Blount** |
|---|---|---|
| Age | **2–5 yr** | **>10 yr** |
| Frequency | More common | Less common |
| Side | **~50% bilateral** | **Usually unilateral** |
| Risks | Early walking, large stature, obesity | **Obesity** |
| Classification | **Langenskiöld** | **None** |
| Location | Physeal / epiphyseal | Metaphyseal |
| Severity | More severe physeal change | Milder |
| Femur | **Compensatory distal femoral valgus** | **Distal femoral varus** (+ distal tibial valgus) |
| Natural history | Stage II–IV may resolve (before 4 yr) | **Progressive, never resolves** |
| Treatment | **Bracing** + surgery | **Surgery only** |
| Osteotomy goal | Overcorrect into valgus | **Neutral axis** |

## How it's asked

**"Feature of adolescent Blount?"** → **associated distal femoral varus** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: bilaterality · self-resolution · bracing first line

**"Obese hypertensive 10-year-old with unilateral bowing?"** → **adolescent Blount**\
✗ Wrong options: metabolic syndrome · vitamin D deficiency

**"15-year-old, fused triradiate, varus + 2 cm LLD?"** → **medial opening wedge osteotomy** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: hemiepiphysiodesis · bracing · lateral closing wedge

**"Closed physes, varus + 2 cm LLD?"** → **medial opening wedge HTO + plate**\
✗ Wrong options: medial closing wedge + ex-fix (worsens varus + LLD)

**"Most common complication of growth modulation?"** → **implant failure** (TO)

**"Osteotomy with least translation, most contact?"** → **focal dome at CORA**

**"Which procedure uses the Hueter-Volkmann law?"** → **hemiepiphysiodesis**

**"Broken 8-plate at 4 weeks — contributor?"** → **increased deformity severity**\
✗ Wrong options: later onset · solid screws · lower BMI · staples

**"Broken titanium plate screws — what may have prevented it?"** → **stainless steel implants**\
✗ Wrong options: cannulated screws · waiting for maturity

**"Why gradual frame correction?"** → **↓ neurovascular injury + compartment syndrome**
