# Rett Syndrome

*Pediatrics · 94 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **1 in 10,000** · **girls** · males die before term

### Genetics - X-linked dominant MECP2

**Inheritance** — **X-linked dominant**\
✗ Wrong options: AR · AD · mitochondrial · XLR

**Gene** — **MECP2** (methyl-CpG-binding protein 2, DNA methylation) · **95% de novo** · on paternal X · affects grey matter

**Associated** — developmental delay · **seizures (80%)** · screaming, loss of speech · constipation · spasticity + contractures · ataxia · hypotonia · **bruxism** · chorea-like hand movements · cold blue extremities · **coxa vara**

## 3 Presentation

**Course** — normal to **6–18 months** → rapid regression to age 3 → plateau to 10 → **half can't walk after 10** · **first sign: apraxia (loss of purposeful hand use)**

**Exam** — ↓ head circumference (from 5–6 months) · abnormal gait, breathing · dementia-like slowing · **diagnosis** clinical + developmental history (genetic testing may miss some)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Scoliosis** — **C-shaped (long) curve** · by **~age 10** · **>50%**

## 5 Management

**Non-op** — supportive (feeding, hygiene, seizures) · **PT to prevent hand contractures**

**Scoliosis** — **bracing ineffective** · **PSF** when thoracic curve hurts sitting/balance · **include entire scoliotic + kyphotic curve**

## How it's asked

**"14-yr girl, seizures, delay, C-shaped curve, MECP2 — inheritance?"** → **X-linked dominant**
