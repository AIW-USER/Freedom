# Spondyloepiphyseal Dysplasia (SED)

*Pediatrics · 100 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — abnormal **type II collagen** → vertebrae + epiphyses involved · rare

### Genes & lab markers

**Gene** — **COL2A1**, chromosome **12**

| Form | Inheritance | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| **SED congenita** | **Autosomal dominant** | More severe · LE angular deformity · myelopathy from AAI |
| **SED tarda** | **X-linked recessive** | Milder · no LE angular deformity · nephrotic syndrome |
| — | Random mutation in **50%** | — |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **cervical myelopathy** (AAI) · respiratory insufficiency (thoracic dysplasia) · **myopia / retinal detachment** · hip pain (coxa vara) · ↓ walking distance

**Exam** — **short stature** (short trunk) · **flat facies** · kyphoscoliosis · ↑ lumbar lordosis · **coxa vara** · genu valgum · waddling gait, ↓ hip ROM

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**C-spine** — AP, lateral, **open-mouth** + **flexion–extension** · **upper cervical instability** · **odontoid hypoplasia / os odontoideum**

**Thoracolumbar** — **platyspondyly** · incomplete ossification fusion · endplate irregularity, narrow discs · kyphoscoliosis · lordosis

**Hips** — horizontal acetabular roofs · delayed pubic + femoral head ossification · wide Y cartilage · **coxa vara**

**MRI** — instability / myelopathy → cord signal

### SED vs MED

| | **SED** | **MED** |
|---|---|---|
| **Spine** | **Involved** (✓ distinguishing) | Normal |
| Gene | COL2A1 (type II collagen) | COMP / type IX collagen |
| Shared | Irregular epiphyses, genu valgum, early hip OA, short stature | Same |
| Neck | **AAI, myelopathy** | — |

**Differential** — achondroplasia · diastrophic dysplasia · MED

## 5 Management

**Non-op (all)** — multidisciplinary: PT, OT, **yearly eye exam**, pulmonology, bracing mild scoliosis

### Operative criteria

| Problem | Indication | Procedure |
|---|---|---|
| **Atlantoaxial instability** | **≥8 mm** or **myelopathy** | **Posterior atlantoaxial fusion** |
| Scoliosis | **>50°** | Growing rods (young) / PSF (older) |
| **Coxa vara** | **NSA <100°**, progressive, symptomatic OA | **Valgus (+ extension) intertrochanteric osteotomy** |

### Atlantoaxial instability & myelopathy

**SEDC child, new unsteadiness, hyperreflexia, Hoffmann, ↓ dexterity** → **cervical myelopathy from AAI**\
✗ Wrong options: scoliosis · tethered cord · coxa vara gait

## 6 Complications

**List** — cervical instability · spinal deformity · eye problems · hip deformity · early OA

## How it's asked

**"10-yr SEDC, new myelopathic signs?"** → **cervical myelopathy due to atlantoaxial instability**

**"Feature distinguishing SED from MED?"** → **spine involvement**\
✗ Wrong options: irregular epiphyses · genu valgum · short stature
