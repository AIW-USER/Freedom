# Medial Epicondylar Fractures - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 65 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Frequency** — **3rd most common** pediatric elbow fracture · up to **20%** of elbow fractures (Orthobullets, OB) · 12% (OB5909) · **75% boys 9–14 yr** · rising with youth sport

**Mechanism** — **avulsion**: valgus stress + **flexor-pronator contraction** (throwers) · direct blow

**Elbow dislocation** — present in **50–60%** · **most common fracture with pediatric elbow dislocation** (33% of dislocations, Rasool) · fragment stays **incarcerated in ~15%**

### Anatomy & ulnar nerve risk

**Medial epicondyle** — **apophysis** (no longitudinal growth) · **last distal humeral centre to fuse** · origin of **flexor-pronator mass + UCL** · lies **posteromedial**, fragment displaces **ANTERIORLY** (flexor-pronator pull)

**Flexor-pronator mass** — **pronator teres** · FCR · palmaris longus · FDS · FCU\
✗ Wrong options: pronator quadratus · brachioradialis · ECRL · brachialis

**Blood supply** — anterior: inferior ulnar collateral a. · posterior: superior + inferior ulnar collateral aa.

**Ulnar nerve** — **C8–T1**, **medial cord** · splits two heads of FCU → runs **between FDP and FCU** · Guyon's canal, **superficial** to transverse carpal ligament · sensation **palmar + dorsal ulnar 1½ digits** · injury → ↓ wrist palmar flexion (FCU) + ring/small finger numbness

| Centre | Appears (Orthobullets) | Fuses |
|---|---|---|
| Capitellum | 1 | 12–14 |
| Radial head | 3 | 14–16 |
| **Medial epicondyle** | **5** | **16–18** |
| Trochlea | 7 | 12–14 |
| Olecranon | 9 | 15–17 |
| Lateral epicondyle | 11 | 12–14 |

**CRITOE (OB214100 variant)** — 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 yr · same text: medial side not ossified before ~5, trochlea complete ~9

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — medial pain · valgus instability · ecchymosis · **document ulnar nerve motor + sensory** · generalised swelling → elbow probably dislocated

**Classification** — none routine · acute (nondisplaced, minimally displaced, displaced, **entrapped**, through apophysis) vs chronic (tension stress)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Displacement assessment & imaging

**Views** — AP + lateral + **internal oblique** · **distal humeral axial view** (beam **25° anterior** to humeral axis) = **most accurate X-ray** for displacement

**Pitfall** — AP, lateral, IR views **underestimate**; fragment invisible on lateral if <10 mm

**CT** — most accurate, more radiation

**Arthrogram** — before ossification · e.g. **displaced medial condyle fracture in a 4-yr-old**\
✗ Wrong options: nondisplaced medial epicondyle at 6 · radial head at 5

**Young child (<5 yr)** — unossified epicondyle → **irreducible dislocation = incarcerated epicondyle until proven otherwise**\
✗ Wrong options: capitellum / radial head fracture (would be visible)

## 5 Management

```
Medial epicondyle fracture
├─ Incarcerated fragment / open / medial condyle extension → ORIF (absolute)
├─ Thrower or gymnast >2–5 mm, ulnar nerve dysfunction, dislocation → consider ORIF (relative)
└─ Others → long arm cast 1–3 wk at 90°, then early motion
```

### Incarcerated fragment & post-reduction incongruity

**Post-reduction incongruent ulnohumeral joint** → **interposed medial epicondyle**\
✗ Wrong options: annular ligament · brachialis · ulnar nerve

**Roberts maneuver** — **forearm supination + wrist/finger extension + valgus load** → may pull fragment out \
✗ Wrong options: Patterson (radial neck)

**Persistent incarceration** — **absolute indication for ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: repeat closed reduction · CRPP · immobilization

**With ulnar paresthesia** — **ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: observation · nerve transposition (not usually needed) · UCL reconstruction

### Operative indications & union (displacement, throwers)

| Absolute | Relative |
|---|---|
| **Incarcerated fragment** | **Ulnar nerve dysfunction** |
| **Medial condyle / articular extension** | Displacement **>2–15 mm** (controversial) · 2–10 mm (OB) |
| **Open fracture** | **>2–5 mm in throwers / gymnasts** (valgus stability) |
| — | Associated dislocation · valgus instability |

**Non-op** — long arm cast **1–3 wk**, elbow **90°** (AROM within ~1 wk) · historically **<5 mm** · RCTs: mean ~7 mm with equal outcomes · grip + ROM equal by 3 months

**Non-op vs ORIF** — **functional outcome equivalent** (displacement <15 mm) · **↑ nonunion** without surgery · **union odds 9× with surgery** \
✗ Wrong options: cubitus varus · fishtail · ↑ ulnar neurapraxia

**Throwing athlete, 7 mm or large displaced fragment** → **ORIF** (highest union, valgus stability)\
✗ Wrong options: excision (destabilises) · CRPP (ulnar nerve risk)

**ORIF technique** — **medial approach**, supine, arm abducted 90° (or prone) · interval **brachialis (musculocutaneous) / triceps (radial)** · find + protect **ulnar nerve proximal → distal** · **cannulated screw** ± washer · K-wires for small fragments / young children · avoid comminution · bicortical screw risks radial nerve \
✗ Wrong options: triceps/brachioradialis (lateral condyle) · brachialis/brachioradialis

## 6 Complications

### Asymptomatic fibrous union

**Nonunion** — common after non-op · **majority asymptomatic** · poor correlation with function\
✗ Wrong options: Tinel · valgus pain · malalignment

**Ulnar nerve injury** — **10–16%** (~10% OB) · neurapraxia after dislocation usually resolves · delayed palsy from missed fracture recovers if treated promptly

**Missed incarceration** — esp. young child with unossified epicondyle

**Stiffness** — **most common**: loss of a few degrees of **extension** · ↑ with prolonged immobilization

**Prognosis** — good to excellent with either treatment

## X vs Y

| | **Medial epicondyle** | **Medial condyle** |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 12–20% | **1–2%** |
| Age | 9–14 yr | Younger (trochlea unossified) |
| Articular | **Extra-articular** apophysis | **Intra-articular**, often SH IV |
| Risk | Incarceration, ulnar nerve | **Nonunion**, growth arrest, **AVN** (posterior terminal supply), misdiagnosis |
| Treatment | Mostly non-op | **ORIF** (anatomic) · arthrogram if unossified |

## How it's asked

**"11-yr, elbow reduced but ulnohumeral joint incongruent?"** → **interposed medial epicondyle fragment**

**"11-yr monkey bars, fragment in joint after reduction?"** → **ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: hinged brace · sling · pinning

**"ORIF indicated because?"** → **incarcerated fragment in ulnohumeral joint**\
✗ Wrong options: >5 mm · prevent cubitus valgus

**"8-yr gymnast, incarcerated after reduction — approach?"** → **ORIF through brachialis/triceps plane**\
✗ Wrong options: triceps/brachioradialis

**"12-yr, incarcerated + ring/little finger numbness?"** → **ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: nerve transposition · splint and observe · UCL reconstruction

**"Roberts maneuver fails, fragment still in joint?"** → **ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: Patterson · CRPP

**"Which X-ray most likely needs surgery?"** → **post-reduction entrapped medial epicondyle**

**"4-yr, irreducible posterolateral dislocation, no fracture seen?"** → **incarcerated medial epicondyle**\
✗ Wrong options: capitellum · radial head

**"5 mm displaced, cast — vs surgery?"** → **↑ nonunion**\
✗ Wrong options: worse function · cubitus varus

**"15-yr pitcher, 7 mm displaced — highest union?"** → **ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: brace · excision

✗ Wrong options: casting

✗ Wrong options: excision · CRPP

**"Fracture most associated with pediatric elbow dislocation?"** → **medial epicondyle**
