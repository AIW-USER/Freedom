# Tibial Tubercle Fracture

*Pediatrics · 108 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Epidemiology** — <1% of pediatric fractures · **males >> females** · **12–15 yr** (near skeletal maturity) · basketball, football, sprinting, high jump · growth hormone weakens physis

**Ossification** — **2 centers**: primary (proximal tibial physis) + secondary (tubercle apophysis, patellar tendon insertion)

**Physeal closure** — **posterior → anterior, proximal → distal** · **tubercle last to fuse** → vulnerable in older adolescents

**Vessel** — **recurrent anterior tibial artery** at **lateral border of tubercle** → lacerated → retracts under fascia → **anterior compartment syndrome**

## 2 Mechanism

### Mechanism

**Mechanism** — **knee flexion + quadriceps contraction** · concentric quad contraction at **jump take-off** · eccentric quad contraction in forced knee flexion\
✗ Wrong options: hamstring · gastrocnemius · direct blow

## 3 Presentation

**Findings** — sudden pain at jump / sprint start · can't walk · effusion / hemarthrosis (type III) · tubercle tenderness · **extensor lag / can't straight-leg raise** (II–III; retinaculum may allow some extension) · **patella alta** · palpable defect · watch **anterior compartment**

**Associated** — **compartment syndrome** · **meniscal tears with type III** · cruciate / collateral / patellar injuries uncommon (OB7708, OB210101) · patellar / quadriceps tendon avulsion, ligament tears to assess (OB1487)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — AP + lateral · **internal rotation view** (tubercle in profile) · contralateral comparison (younger) · widening / hinging of apophysis · **patella alta** · anterior swelling only → type V sleeve

**CT** — intra-articular / posterior extension · arteriogram if popliteal concern\
✗ Wrong options: delay fasciotomy

**MRI** — not routine · nondisplaced type II extension or type V

### Ogden classification (modified Watson-Jones)

| Type | Fracture |
|---|---|
| **I** | Secondary ossification center near patellar tendon insertion |
| **II** | Propagates proximally **between primary and secondary centers** |
| **III** (most common) | **Coronal**, extends posteriorly **across primary center → intra-articular** |
| **IV** | **Through entire proximal tibial physis** |
| **V** | **Periosteal sleeve avulsion** of extensor mechanism from secondary center |
| Modifier | **A** nondisplaced · **B** displaced |

## 5 Management

### Operative criteria

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Type I** / displacement **<2 mm** / acceptable after closed reduction | **Long leg cast in extension 6 wk** |
| **Displaced, types II–IV** | **ORIF** ± arthrotomy / arthroscopy (joint surface, meniscus) |
| **Type V** | **Soft-tissue (periosteal) repair** |
| Compartment syndrome | **Emergent fasciotomy + ORIF** (± prophylactic fasciotomy) |

### Treatment - ORIF

**ORIF** — midline incision · **remove interposed periosteum** (blocks closed reduction) · anatomic reduction · **4.0 cancellous partially threaded screws** (or headless compression) · smooth K-wires if >3 yr from maturity · cannulated screws **better than CRPP** (OB) · NWB long leg cast / brace **4–6 wk** · sport **≥3 months**

**Arthrotomy / arthroscopy** — intra-articular (type III) → visualize joint, evacuate hematoma, meniscus repair

**Soft-tissue repair (V)** — heavy suture of periosteum to secondary center · cast **8–10 wk**

**Traps** — ✗ Wrong options: long leg cast for displaced · patellar tendon bearing cast · CRPP · ligament reconstruction · spanning ex-fix (temporizing only) · patellar tendon repair (tendon intact, deforming) · fragment excision · MRI / CT if X-ray clear

## 6 Complications

### Compartment syndrome - anterior tibial recurrent artery

**Rate** — **4%** (Orthobullets, OB) · 4–5% (OB) · up to **10%** (OB) — read the options · high with **type III**

**Vessel** — **anterior tibial recurrent artery**\
✗ Wrong options: peroneal · posterior tibial · middle geniculate (ACL) · medial / lateral inferior geniculate · popliteal (knee dislocation)

**Signs** — rising analgesic need, anxiety, distress, swelling overnight → **emergent fasciotomies + ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: observe · vascular exploration · ORIF alone

![Recurrent branch of anterior tibial artery](../../MCQ/attachments/q857-illa-recurrent-branch-of-anterior-tibial.jpg)

**Other** — stiffness · refracture

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Bursitis** | **Most common after surgery** · screw prominence → removal |
| **Recurvatum** | Anterior arrest, posterior growth → ↓ slope · **more common than LLD** (LLD low — age) · unusual (near maturity) |
| Popliteal artery injury | Over distal metaphyseal fragment (physeal types) |
