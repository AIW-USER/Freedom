# Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy

*Pediatrics · 39 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Most common hereditary neuromuscular disease** (most common MD) · prevalence **2–3/10,000** (Orthobullets) · ~**1 in 3500 male births** (OB)

**Who** — **boys only** · onset **2–6 yr**

### Genetics – dystrophin, X-linked recessive, carrier mother & offspring risk

**Inheritance** — **X-linked recessive**\
✗ Wrong options: AD · AR · X-linked dominant · mitochondrial · Y-linked

**Gene** — **dystrophin, Xp21(.2)** · point deletion / nonsense (out-of-frame) → **absent dystrophin** (<5%)

**Spontaneous** — **one-third** new mutations

**Carriers** — mother carries · **sons 50% affected**, daughters 50% carriers · **affected male → 0% sons affected** (gives Y), all daughters carriers · **2.5–20% of carrier females symptomatic** (Lyon hypothesis) · **CK ↑ in 80% of carriers** (peak 8–12 yr)

**Confirm DMD via family** — **test mother's CK**\
✗ Wrong options: father (always normal) · unaffected brother

**Pathophysiology** — dystrophin–glycoprotein complex lost → membrane fragility, Ca²⁺ dysregulation, oxidative damage → necrosis/regeneration cycles → **fibrofatty replacement**

### Genes & lab markers

| Disorder | Gene / inheritance |
|---|---|
| **Duchenne** | **Dystrophin, Xp21** (XLR) |
| Becker | Dystrophin (in-frame, ↓ amount) (XLR) |
| Emery-Dreifuss | **Emerin** (X-linked) |
| **FSH dystrophy** | **D4Z4 deletion, 4q35** · **AD** |
| **Myotonic dystrophy** | **DMPK / CNBP**, **chromosome 19** |
| **Limb-girdle** | **AR and AD** (LGMD2 / LGMD1) · chr 2p (TO) |
| Marfan | FBN1 |
| CMT | PMP22 (AD) |
| Turner | 45,XO |

**CK** — **markedly ↑** — **10–200×** (Orthobullets) · 50–100× (TO) · often **>25,000 IU/L** (e.g. 43,000) · **peaks by age 2**, falls with fibrofatty replacement

**EMG** — myopathic (low amplitude, short, polyphasic), **normal NCV**

**Biopsy** — necrosis, fibrofatty infiltration, **absent dystrophin stain** · DNA confirms ~two-thirds · **dystrophin testing of biopsy NOT 100% confirmatory** (TO)

## 3 Presentation

**History** — delayed walking (e.g. 30 mo) · toe walking, clumsy, **waddling** · difficulty climbing stairs, hopping · falls

**Weakness** — **proximal first, starting with gluteals** · lumbar **lordosis** (gluteal weakness) · wide-based stance

**Gowers sign** — rises by **walking hands up thighs** (gluteus maximus + quadriceps weakness)

**Meryon sign** — child slips through examiner's hands when lifted under arms (shoulder girdle weakness)

**Calf pseudohypertrophy** — fat + fibrous infiltration

**Reflexes** — **present**\
✗ Wrong options: SMA · GBS

**Trendelenburg** · contractures (elbow, hip, knee, ankle) · **equinovarus** (tibialis posterior retains strength longest) · scoliosis · fractures (falls)

**Other** — cardiomyopathy · mild intellectual impairment · pharyngeal weakness · malignant hyperthermia risk

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnosis – Gowers sign, CK & Becker variant

**First test** — **serum CK** (✗ Wrong options: leg X-rays · NCS · brain/spine MRI) · screen asymptomatic at-risk boy with **CK** (✗ Wrong options: DNA, biopsy, Western blot first)

**Confirm** — **DNA testing** (± biopsy)

### Key radiology findings

**MRI muscle pattern** — **gastrocnemius early** → gluteus max/med, adductor magnus → quads etc. · **spared: sartorius, gracilis, semitendinosus, semimembranosus, tibialis posterior** · upper limb: triceps, biceps, rotator cuff; **deltoid spared**

**Echo** — **dilated cardiomyopathy in all by late teens/20s**

**X-ray** — lucent fatty soft tissue, scoliosis, cardiomegaly

### Differential

| | **Duchenne** | **Becker** | **SMA** | **Emery-Dreifuss** | **Limb-girdle** | **GBS** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dystrophin | **Absent** | **Decreased/abnormal** | Normal | Normal (**emerin** absent) | Normal | — |
| CK | **Very high** | Very high | **Normal** | Near normal | Mildly ↑ | Normal |
| Pseudohypertrophy | Yes | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Reflexes | Present | Present | **Absent + fasciculations** | — | — | **Absent** |
| Onset | 2–6 yr | **~8 yr, slower**, lives to 40s | Earlier | Teens; **early elbow + ankle contractures**, **heart block** (pacemaker) | 1st–4th decade | Acute, post-infectious |
| Key test | DNA | DNA | — | ECG | — | **CSF** (lumbar puncture) |

### Differential – Guillain-Barré syndrome

**GBS** — acute ascending flaccid weakness 1–6 wk after (GI) infection · areflexia · autonomic + diaphragm involvement · **lumbar puncture (CSF)** best test · IVIG / plasma exchange · 80% walk at 6 mo\
✗ Wrong options: C-spine MRI/CT · M-spike · stool

## 5 Management

**Corticosteroids (prednisone / deflazacort)** — age **5–7 yr** with progression · prolong ambulation (3–4 yr), ↓ **scoliosis needing fusion 92% → 20%**, delay pulmonary + cardiac decline, ↓ mortality 76% · side effects: **osteonecrosis, osteoporosis**, weight gain, cataracts, short stature · **deflazacort** — fewer glucose/bone effects, far costlier

**Exon-skipping (eteplirsen, golodirsen, viltolarsen, casimersen)** — convert out-of-frame to in-frame (**DMD → BMD-like**) · hot-spot exons 45–53 (~50%) · costly

**Cardiac** — ACE inhibitor + β-blocker · echo/ECG every 2 yr to age 10, then yearly (6-monthly with cardiomyopathy)

**Other** — nocturnal ventilation · PT, power chair · nutrition

**Soft-tissue releases (ambulatory)** — ITB (abduction contracture) · sartorius, rectus, TFL (flexion) · hamstrings · **TAL + tibialis posterior** lengthening/transfer · mobilise early

**Equinovarus** — stretching, night AFO → **TAL + PTT transfer + toe flexor tenotomies**

### Scoliosis – early fusion (20–30°, FVC), T2-pelvis & preoperative cardiopulmonary work-up

**Natural history** — **90–95%** develop scoliosis · starts when **full-time sitting (11–13 yr)** · **progresses rapidly** (1–2°/month), **small curves always progress** · **kyphoscoliosis** + pelvic obliquity · **restrictive lung disease**\
✗ Wrong options: 50%

**Bracing** — **ineffective / contraindicated** (impairs respiration)\
✗ Wrong options: TLSO · PT · observe a 25° curve

### Operative criteria

| Criterion | Value |
|---|---|
| **Cobb angle** | **20–30°** in non-ambulator (up to ~40° if responding to steroids) |
| **FVC** | Operate **before FVC ≤35%** (≤35% = indication / threshold) · **>50% = good outcomes** · <10% contraindication (some) |
| Other | **Non-ambulatory** · **rapid progression** · poor steroid response (✗ Wrong options: decreased muscle mass) |
| **Fuse to pelvis** | Curve **≥40°** · **pelvic obliquity ≥10°** · lumbar apex **below L1** |
| Construct | **T2–pelvis posterior instrumented fusion** (pedicle screws) (✗ Wrong options: T10–pelvis · growing rods · T2–L5 with obliquity · delay for steroids) |
| Anterior approach | **Avoid** (lung deflation, ↑ pulmonary complications) |

**Timing** — driven by **cardiopulmonary status, not Cobb angle** · 12-yr, 38°, FVC 50% → **as soon as possible**\
✗ Wrong options: wait for FVC 35% · cardiac decline · 50°

**Pre-op** — **echocardiogram + PFTs**, **cardiology consult** · stop ACE-I 2–3 d, taper β-blocker over weeks · TXA · beware anaesthesia-induced **rhabdomyolysis**, hypotensive anaesthesia may not be tolerated\
✗ Wrong options: hematology · neurology · rheumatology/biopsy · MRI for ectasia · baclofen trial · bracing trial

```
DMD scoliosis
├─ Ambulatory → steroids, keep walking (no brace)
└─ Non-ambulatory, curve ≥20–30° or FVC falling toward 35% → early PSF (echo + PFT first)
       ├─ Curve <40°, obliquity <10°, apex ≥L1 → T2–L5 possible
       └─ Curve ≥40° / obliquity ≥10° / apex below L1 → T2–pelvis
```

### Other muscular dystrophies (FSH, myotonic)

**FSH dystrophy** — **AD**, 4q35 · facial weakness (**can't whistle, purse lips**, wrinkle brow) · **scapular winging** — scapular stabilisers weak while **deltoid (abductors) stay strong** → can't flex/abduct arm · **scapulothoracic arthrodesis** · foot drop (AFO) · open-eyed sleeping\
✗ Wrong options: "due to weak abductors"

**Myotonic dystrophy** — **chromosome 19** (DMPK) · can't relax muscle · most common adult-onset MD · baldness, gonadal atrophy, cardiac, clubfoot, hip dysplasia, scoliosis

**Limb-girdle** — **AD (LGMD1) and AR (LGMD2)** · pelvic/shoulder girdle · CK mildly ↑

**Oculopharyngeal** — 40–70 yr, eyelids + throat

## 6 Complications & prognosis

**Untreated** — walking lost by **~10 yr** (10–12) · wheelchair by **15** · death from respiratory/cardiac failure ~**20** (Orthobullets) / ~25 (OB) / early 30s (TO) · 15-yr survival 27.9% → **78.6% with steroids**
