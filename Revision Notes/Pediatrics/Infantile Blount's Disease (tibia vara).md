# Infantile Blount's Disease (tibia vara)

*Pediatrics · 54 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — **progressive pathologic genu varum centred at the proximal tibia** · age **2–5 yr** (Orthobullets) · 0–3 yr (OB) · infantile **<8 yr** vs adolescent >8 yr (TO) — read the options

**Risk factors** — **obesity** · **early walkers (<1 yr)** · **African American / Hispanic** · never in non-ambulatory children (TO)

**Sex / side** — **M > F** (Orthobullets) · OB lists female association · **bilateral in 50%**

### Pathophysiology

**Cause** — **mechanical overload** of medial proximal tibial physis in a **genetically susceptible** child → **osteochondrosis** of medial physis + epiphysis → may progress to **physeal bar**

**Deformity components** — **varus** of proximal medial metaphysis + **procurvatum (apex anterior)** + **internal rotation** (TO) · **external rotation** is NOT a component (TO) · not recurvatum (OB) · one TO explanation says "genu recurvatum" — read the options

**Associated** — **internal tibial torsion** (✗ Wrong options: metatarsus adductus) · **compensatory distal femoral VALGUS** (✗ Wrong options: distal femoral varus (adolescent))

### Physiologic alignment

| Age | Orthobullets | TO |
|---|---|---|
| Birth–1 yr | **Varus** (bow legs, normal <2 yr) | Varus up to 10–15° |
| Neutral | **~14 months** | **18–24 months** |
| Peak valgus | **~3 yr** | **3–4 yr** (up to 15–20°) |
| Adult valgus | **~7 yr** | 5–6 yr (5–6°) |

**Physiologic bowing** — resolves by **36 months** in almost all (OB) · symmetric, femur + tibia · Brooks & Gross: varus to 18 mo → valgus peak 4 yr → adult 5–6 yr (OB)

**Tibial torsion (TO)** — 5° internal at birth → neutral by 4–5 yr → **20–25° external by 8 yr**

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — **genu varum + flexion + internal rotation** deformity · **lateral thrust on walking** (✓ abnormal finding) · internal tibial torsion · LLD · **no** tenderness, effusion or ↓ ROM

**Siffert-Katz sign (TO)** — **varus thrust during walking** · acute varus **below the knee** · obesity **> 95th percentile** · early walkers

**Pathological varum (TO)** — persists **> 2 yr** · unilateral · LLD · severe · obesity · causes: rickets, Blount, dysplasia, trauma/infection, hemimelia · **focal fibrocartilaginous dysplasia** — self-limiting, no surgery

**Not abnormal** — **internal thigh–foot angle 5°** in an infant (OB)

### Cover-up test

**Technique** — patella forward · examiner covers lower leg + foot · look at thigh–upper tibia axis (age 1–3 yr) (TO)

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Positive** (bowing in upper tibia) | X-ray · **high sensitivity** |
| Neutral (straight axis) | X-ray |
| **Negative** (slight valgus upper tibia) | Physiologic bowing · **high specificity + NPV** |

![Cover-up test](../../MCQ/attachments/q218582-illb-cover-up-test.jpg)

### When to X-ray

**Physiologic, <2 yr, symmetric** → **clinical observation** (OB)\
✗ Wrong options: X-rays · vitamin D · genetic screen

**Triggers for workup** — persists **>2 yr** (OB) · persists **>18 months** or asymmetric (Birch) · severe **>20°** · worsening · asymmetry · **varus thrust** · low height/weight percentile\
✗ Wrong options: family history alone

**Vitamin D workup** — if **<20th percentile** on growth curves (OB)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**View** — standing AP **with patellae facing forward** (ITT confounds)

**Findings** — varus focused at proximal tibia · sharp angular deformity · asymmetry · **medial + posterior sloping** of epiphysis · **metaphyseal beaking**\
✗ Wrong options: physiologic (symmetric flaring of femur + tibia)

**Pathognomonic** — **medial metaphyseal fragmentation** → progressive tibia vara (TO)

**X-ray signs (TO)** — metaphyseal flaring · **fibular physis near knee level** · physeal bar · Drennan MDA

**MRI (TO)** — ↑ thickness of medial proximal tibial chondroepiphysis · ↑ height + width of **medial meniscus** · abnormal medial femoral epiphysis

**Neglected infantile Blount (14 yr)** — **severe depression of medial tibial epiphysis**\
✗ Wrong options: adolescent Blount (metaphyseal) · septic/JRA (both sides of joint) · hemophilia (widened intercondylar notch)

### Metaphyseal–diaphyseal (Drennan) angle

**Measurement** — line through metaphyseal beaks vs line **perpendicular to tibial long axis**

| MDA | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **<10°** | **95% resolve** naturally |
| **11–16°** | **Close observation** (Orthobullets) · **>11°** warrants observation (TO) · 9–16° → brace if no correction after 24 months (OB) |
| **>16°** | **Abnormal** · **95% progress** · ✓ most reliably separates Blount from physiologic |
| **>20°** | Operative indication |

**MDA cut-offs (TO)** — **< 11° normal · 11–16° doubtful · > 16° Blount** · Levine & Drennan (TO lecture 2): **> 10° suspicious**, **> 16° high likelihood of progression**

**Tibiofemoral angle** — femoral vs tibial long axes

![Metaphyseal–diaphyseal angle](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4050-metaphyseal-diaphyseal-angle-moved.jpg)

### Langenskiöld classification

| Stage | Feature |
|---|---|
| **I–IV** | Increasing medial **metaphyseal beaking + sloping** |
| **V–VI** | **Epiphyseal–metaphyseal bony bridge** (physeal bar) |

**Use** — prognosis · stage II and IV can correct spontaneously in young children

| Langenskiöld (TO) | Feature | Treatment (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Medial metaphyseal **beak** | **Mermaid brace × 2 yr** |
| **II** | **Depression** | Mermaid brace × 2 yr |
| **III** | **Step** formation | Surgery (guided growth / osteotomy) |
| **IV** | **Ossification of step** | Surgery |
| **V** | **Separate medial fragment** | **Osteotomy + medial tibial plateau elevation** |
| **VI** | **Medial physeal bar** | Osteotomy + plateau elevation |

![Langenskiöld stages](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4050-langen.jpg)

### CT for physeal bar (TO)

**Order CT pre-op if** — age **>5 yr** · medial physeal slope **50–70°** · **Langenskiöld IV** · weight **>95th percentile** · black girls meeting the above

### Differential (pathologic genu varum)

**List** — persistent physiologic varus · **rickets** · OI · MED · SED · metaphyseal dysostosis (Schmid, Jansen) · focal fibrocartilaginous defect · TAR · physeal injury (radiation, infection, trauma)

## 5 Management

```
Infantile Blount
├─ <3 yr, Langenskiöld I–II → KAFO
│      (improvement within 1 yr; wear ~2 yr)
├─ Brace failure / progression → proximal tibia-fibula valgus osteotomy
└─ ≥3–4 yr, stage III–VI or MDA >20° → osteotomy
       └─ physeal bar (V–VI) → add bar resection / hemiepiphysiodesis
```

### Bracing (knee-ankle-foot orthosis, KAFO)

**Indication** — **stage I–II in children <3 yr** (Orthobullets) · <3 yr **and unilateral** stage I–II (TO) · TO calls it "mermaid splints"

**Duration** — ~**2 yr** for bony resolution · improvement should show **within 1 yr** · fails after **12 months** in 2–3 yr old → osteotomy (OB)

**Poor results / failure risk** — **obesity (>90th percentile)** · **bilateral** · **varus thrust** · age **≥3 yr** at start · Langenskiöld **III** · progressive deformity · age >4 (TO)

**Evidence** — **no strong consensus** on bracing efficacy (OB) · night KAFO poorly tolerated · (✗ Wrong options: AFO (doesn't address knee) · serial casting)\
✗ Wrong options: "effective" · "ineffective" · needs PT

### Proximal tibial osteotomy

### Operative criteria

| Indication for proximal tibia/fibula valgus osteotomy |
|---|
| Stage I–II in child **>3 yr** |
| **Stage III, IV, V, VI** |
| **Age ≥4 yr** (all stages) |
| **Failed brace** / progressive deformity |
| **MDA >20°** |
| Morbidly obese (OB) |

**Timing** — **before age 4** → recurrence significantly ↓ · early osteotomy (**2–4 yr**) = best results, least recurrence (TO) · best results **<4 yr** (OB)

**Technique** — **below the tibial tubercle** · **acute correction** · distal fragment into **valgus + external rotation + lateral translation** · staged for IV–VI · epiphysiolysis in V–VI · medial plateau elevation if depressed · **prophylactic anterior compartment fasciotomy**

**Osteotomy types (TO)** — closing / opening wedge · **Rab's osteotomy** (biplanar oblique + derotation) · chevron · dome · adolescent: osteotomy, fixator realignment, lateral hemiepiphysiodesis

**Focal fibrocartilaginous dysplasia (TO)** — varus **< 30° observe** (resolves) · **40–50° → osteotomy**

**Overcorrection** — **10–15° valgus** (Orthobullets, TO) · **~10° valgus** (OB) · at least **5° valgus** (OB60) · ✓ medial physeal abnormality persists\
✗ Wrong options: correction to neutral · undercorrection

**Site** — **tibia, not femur**\
✗ Wrong options: distal femur osteotomy

**Renal osteodystrophy** — **postpone** until renal status stable (osteotomy short-lived otherwise)

**Late-onset with distal femoral varus** — **LDFA >92°** → distal femoral osteotomy or guided growth (OB)

### Guided growth & Hueter-Volkmann

**Principle** — **↑ compression → slows longitudinal growth** · ↑ tension → stimulates growth

**Lateral proximal tibial hemiepiphysiodesis** — tension band plate / staples (extraperiosteal plates) · **prevents worsening, does NOT fully correct** (OB) · less morbid than osteotomy · not **medial** hemiepiphysiodesis (worsens varus)

**Caution** — young child, severe stage III → relies on injured medial physis → **severe shortening** → prefer osteotomy (OB) · lateral tibial epiphysiodesis after age **9** (TO)

**Unilateral LLD** — epiphysiodesis of uninvolved leg (TO)

### Physeal bar

**Bar resection** — needs **≥4 yr growth remaining** · with osteotomy · interposition **fat or PMMA** · **hemiepiphysiodesis if bar >50%**

**Hemi-plateau elevation** — severe uncorrected or **relapsed** disease

## 6 Complications

**Compartment syndrome** — after high tibial/fibular osteotomy → prophylactic anterior compartment release

**Recurrence** — **medial physeal bar** → progressive varus after well-done osteotomy → lateral hemiepiphysiodesis or bar resection\
✗ Wrong options: overcorrection · plate malposition · age 5 · vitamin D

**Screen for SCFE (TO)** — all Blount patients (obesity)

## X vs Y

| | **Infantile Blount** | **Adolescent Blount** |
|---|---|---|
| Age | **2–5 yr** | **>10 yr** |
| Side | **50% bilateral** | Usually unilateral |
| Risk | Early walking, large stature, obesity | Obesity |
| Classification | **Langenskiöld** | None radiographic |
| Physeal disturbance | More severe | Less severe |
| Femur | **Compensatory distal femoral valgus** | **Distal femoral varus** + distal tibial valgus |
| Natural history | Stage II/IV may resolve | Never resolves |
| Treatment | Bracing + surgery | Surgery only |

| | **Physiologic bowing** | **Infantile Blount** |
|---|---|---|
| Age | **<2 yr** | **>2 yr** |
| Shape | Symmetric, femur + tibia | Asymmetric, **proximal tibia**, sharp |
| MDA | **<10–11°** | **>16°** |
| X-ray | Symmetric flaring | **Metaphyseal beaking**, medial sloping |
| Gait | No thrust | **Lateral thrust** |
