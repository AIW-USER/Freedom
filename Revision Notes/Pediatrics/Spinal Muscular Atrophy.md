# Spinal Muscular Atrophy

*Pediatrics · 99 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Epidemiology** — **most common genetic cause of death in childhood** · 1 in 10,000

**Pathology** — progressive loss of **α-motor neurons in the anterior horn** (✓ SMN protein deficiency) · weakness **proximal > distal**, **LE > UE**, symmetric

### Genetics & pathophysiology (SMN1, SMN2)

**Inheritance** — **autosomal recessive**

**Gene** — **homozygous telomeric deletion of SMN1**, chromosome 5 · in **90%** (Orthobullets) · **95–98%** (OB) · SMN protein: RNA metabolism, apoptosis

**Severity** — all lack SMN1 protein · **number of functional SMN2 copies sets severity** (✓ SMN2 correlates with severity) · type I has 1 SMN2 copy

| Gene (distractors) | Disease |
|---|---|
| **SMN1** | **SMA** |
| Dystrophin (↑ CPK, DTRs present, calf pseudohypertrophy) | Duchenne |
| PMP22 duplication | CMT (70–80%) |
| Androgen receptor CAG repeat | Spinobulbar (Kennedy) |
| Frataxin GAA repeat | Friedreich ataxia |
| β-galactosidase | Morquio B |
| COMP / COL1A1 | Pseudoachondroplasia / OI |
| Sphingolipid (glucocerebroside) | Gaucher |
| Fetal ACh receptor, non-progressive contractures | Arthrogryposis |

**One SMN1 deletion → any of the 3 types** (✓ "any of the above")

### Types

| Type | Name | Onset | Function | Prognosis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Acute Werdnig-Hoffmann** | **<6 months** | Floppy, poor head control, feeding difficulty, **tongue fasciculations**, absent DTRs | **Death by ~2 yr** |
| **II** | Chronic Werdnig-Hoffmann | **6–12 months** | **Sits but can't walk** · LE worse | To **5th decade** |
| **III** | **Kugelberg-Welander** | **2–15 yr** | Walks as child, wheelchair as adult · proximal weakness | **Normal** life expectancy ± respiratory support |

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — **absent DTRs** · **fasciculations** · hypotonia · normal CPK + aldolase\
✗ Wrong options: Duchenne (present)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **DNA testing** (positive test usually avoids biopsy) · muscle biopsy · prenatal possible

**X-ray** — scoliosis series · pelvis

## 5 Management

### Medical therapy - nusinersen

**Nusinersen** — **antisense oligonucleotide** · alters **SMN2 splicing to include exon 7** → full-length SMN · **intrathecal** (doesn't cross BBB) · FDA-approved

**Others** — **risdiplam** (same mechanism, **oral** small molecule) · **Zolgensma** (onasemnogene, **IV gene replacement of SMN1**) · albuterol (adjunct) \
✗ Wrong options: prednisone (Duchenne)

### Scoliosis management

**Curve** — **almost universal** (100% type II) · from **age 2–3** · **long C-shaped thoracolumbar**, **very flexible** · progresses despite brace · curve size correlates with ambulatory status

**Bracing** — may **delay, not prevent** surgery (<10 yr)

**Surgery** — **PSF to pelvis** for progressive curve (>30–40°, OB; >50–60° with sitting/pulmonary decline, OB) · goals: **sitting balance, free the hands, slow pulmonary decline** · **release hip / LE contractures first** for seating · leave a **lower lumbar laminectomy window unfused** for intrathecal nusinersen

**Anterior + posterior** — only **curves >100°** or **very young (crankshaft)** · avoid with poor lungs · **typically NOT needed** · growing rods only if young\
✗ Wrong options: anterior alone (secondary progression)

**Post-op** — **temporary ↓ upper-limb function** (e.g. ↓ self-propelling wheelchair)\
✗ Wrong options: better strength · walking · contracture improvement

### Hip subluxation - observation

**Hip instability** — subluxation/dislocation in **62% of type II**, less in III · usually **painless** · open reduction redislocates

**Treatment** — **observation (leave dislocated)** = standard\
✗ Wrong options: adductor/psoas release · varus osteotomy · PAO · spica · abduction brace

**Contractures** — hip + knee (± ankle equinus in non-walkers) · PT · release controversial (rarely helps non-walkers, recurs)

## X vs Y

| | **SMA** | **Duchenne** |
|---|---|---|
| Lesion | Anterior horn cell | Muscle (dystrophin) |
| DTRs | **Absent** | Present |
| CPK | Normal | **Very high** |
| Fasciculations | Yes (tongue) | No |
| Inheritance | AR | X-linked recessive |

## How it's asked

**"Homozygous SMN1 deletion results in?"** → **any of types I–III**

**"3-month-old floppy, absent DTRs, tongue fasciculations — pathology + prognosis?"** → **anterior horn cell loss, unlikely to live past 2**\
✗ Wrong options: dystrophin · CP · CMT

**"Newborn tongue fasciculations, hypotonia, no DTRs?"** → **SMN1 mutation**

**"12-month-old sits not walks, normal CPK?"** → **SMN-1 gene**\
✗ Wrong options: dystrophin · PMP22 · frataxin

**"SMN protein deficiency leads to?"** → **anterior horn cell loss, progressive weakness**

**"Gene correlating with severity?"** → **SMN2**

**"Gene implicated in SMA?"** → **SMN-I**

**"Intrathecal SMN2 splicing modifier?"** → **nusinersen**\
✗ Wrong options: risdiplam (oral) · Zolgensma (IV gene therapy)

**"13-yr non-ambulatory, flexible 68° curve progressing in brace?"** → **posterior spinal fusion**\
✗ Wrong options: AP fusion · growing rods · hip reduction

**"Expected change after PSF?"** → **diminished ability to self-propel wheelchair**

**"SMA scoliosis — INCORRECT?"** → **anterior release + posterior fusion typically required**

**"12-yr type 3, 60° curve, night ventilation?"** → **posterior spinal fusion**\
✗ Wrong options: AP · anterior alone

**"7-yr type II, painless hip popping, subluxation?"** → **observation**

**"11-yr non-ambulatory, painless full ROM, dislocated hip?"** → **observation**
