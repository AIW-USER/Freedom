# Patella Sleeve Fracture

*Pediatrics · 79 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — cartilage **"sleeve" pulled off the ossified patella** ± small bone fleck · unique to skeletally immature

**Epidemiology** — <1% of pediatric fractures but **>50% of pediatric patella fractures** · **boys 5:1** · **8–12 yr** (ossification nearly complete)

**Mechanism** — **indirect**: powerful **quadriceps contraction on a flexed knee** (stumble, pop) · not a direct blow · superior pole: patella pushed down → intra-articular dislocation (OB)

**Anatomy** — largest sesamoid · ossifies **3–5 yr** · lateral facet larger · cartilage up to 1 cm · quadriceps 3 layers (rectus superficial, vasti middle, intermedius deep) · **blood supply mainly inferior pole** (geniculate ring)

**Location** — **inferior pole most common** · superior pole least common

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — severe pain, can't bear weight, **painful pop**

**Exam** — hemarthrosis / tense effusion · **high-riding patella or palpable gap** = extensor mechanism disrupted · **extensor lag / can't straight-leg raise**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP, lateral, tangential

**Findings** — **small bone flecks** at superior or inferior pole (easily missed) · **inferior sleeve → patella ALTA** · **superior sleeve → patella BAJA** + slight anterior tilt of superior pole

**Rule** — fragment is **much larger than it looks** (mostly cartilage)

**MRI / ultrasound** — when diagnosis unclear (soon after injury)

## 5 Management

### Treatment - ORIF

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Nondisplaced, extensor mechanism intact** (rare) · gap <2 mm | **Cylinder cast 6 wk** |
| **>2–3 mm displacement or step-off** · **disrupted extensor mechanism** | **ORIF** |

**ORIF** — medial parapatellar approach · repair retinaculum / tendon · **transosseous sutures through bone tunnels** · **modified tension band** · suture anchors · screws · K-wires if fragment large · cylinder cast in extension **2–3 wk**

**Superior sleeve** — suture between bony fragments through **tunnels in the larger distal fragment** (fracture is distal to quadriceps insertion)\
✗ Wrong options: quadriceps tendon repair · immobiliser

**Never** — **excision of the fragment** (cartilage is large) · patellar tendon repair to tubercle · partial patellectomy · knee arthroscopy · PT alone · cast with extensor lag

## 6 Complications

**List** — patella alta · extensor lag · quadriceps atrophy · malunion · nonunion · painful hardware · worse with comminution + displacement

## How it's asked

**"10-yr fell from bike, extensor lag, inferior pole avulsion?"** → **open reduction + suture fixation**\
✗ Wrong options: plate + cerclage · patellectomy · tendon midsubstance repair

**"13-yr superior pole sleeve, can't SLR, gap on US?"** → **ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: hinged brace · patellar tendon repair

**"11-yr basketball, pop, can't extend — distal pole fleck?"** → **open reduction + tension band**\
✗ Wrong options: excision · cast · tendon to tubercle

**"12-yr kicked, displaced sleeve?"** → **ORIF of patella**\
✗ Wrong options: tibial tubercle · cylinder cast

**"10-yr tripped, pop, extensor lag?"** → **ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: cast in extension · arthroscopy

**"Arthrogryposis, superior pole rim avulsed, can't straighten?"** → **ORIF with sutures through patellar bone tunnels**\
✗ Wrong options: quadriceps tendon repair · cast
