# Mucopolysaccharidoses

*Pediatrics · 68 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **13 lysosomal storage disorders** · missing/faulty lysosomal enzymes → **glycosaminoglycans (GAGs)** accumulate in chondrocytes, cartilage ECM, synovium, organs

**Most common** — **Morquio + Sanfilippo** (Orthobullets) · Morquio (OB)

### Genes & lab markers

| Syndrome | Enzyme | Urine GAG | Inheritance | Intelligence | Cornea | Prognosis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Morquio (MPS IV)** | A: galactosamine-6-sulfatase · B: β-galactosidase | **Keratan sulfate** | AR | **Normal** | **Cloudy** | Adulthood (A worse) |
| **Hurler (MPS I)** | **α-L-iduronidase** | **Dermatan** (+ heparan, TO) | AR | Progressive MR | **Cloudy** | **Death 1st decade** · most severe |
| **Sanfilippo (MPS III)** | Several | **Heparan sulfate** | AR | MR | **Clear** | Death 2nd decade |
| **Hunter (MPS II)** | Sulpho-iduronate sulfatase | Dermatan + heparan | **X-linked recessive** | MR | **Clear** | Death by 2nd decade |

**Tests** — urine **toluidine blue spot** test · **skin fibroblast** enzyme assay · **chorionic villous sampling**

### Clinical features (CTS, contractures)

**Orthopaedic** — **proportionate dwarfism** · **C1–C2 instability** · **carpal tunnel syndrome** (esp. MPS I Scheie; thick retinaculum) · trigger finger · **progressive joint contractures** (✓ most common early MSK sign — wrist, digits, hips, knees) · delayed hip dysplasia · abnormal epiphyses · **bullet-shaped phalanges/metacarpals** · **genu valgum** (lateral proximal tibial metaphyseal dysplasia) · thoracolumbar kyphosis (L1–L2 beaking, gibbus)

**Non-orthopaedic** — complex sugars in urine · visceromegaly · corneal clouding · cardiac disease · deafness · **MR (except Morquio)** · enlarged skull · coarse facies

**Not in MPS** — **dural ectasia** (Marfan, EDS, NF1, AS) · Klippel-Feil triad · sacralization of L5 · congenital ACL absence (NF1, fibular hemimelia) · block vertebrae (VACTERL) · congenital radial head dislocation

**Lysosomal CTS** — Hurler, Hunter, Scheie, pseudo-Hurler · Phalen/Tinel unreliable in children · **transverse carpal ligament release**

## 3 Presentation

### Morquio syndrome

**Onset** — **18–24 months** · ~1 in 40,000 · short-trunk dwarfism

**Features** — **normal intelligence** · waddling gait · **genu valgum** · thoracic kyphosis · corneal clouding · skin + joint laxity · hip dysplasia

**X-ray** — thick skull · wide ribs · **vertebral beaking** · **platyspondyly** · coxa vara with unossified head · bullet-shaped metacarpals · **odontoid hypoplasia → C1–C2 instability**

![Genu valgum in Morquio](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4101-xray-genu-valum-colorado-moved.jpg)

### Hurler syndrome (gargoylism)

**Features** — **gibbus (thoracolumbar kyphosis) — often first sign, ~6 months** (TO) · C1–C2 instability (25%) · severe global spinal stenosis · CTS + trigger fingers · severe hip dysplasia · genu valgum · coxa valga → subluxation · J-shaped sella, thick calvarium, platyspondyly (TO)

**Survival** — median **6.8 yr** untreated (TO)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Spine screening & atlantoaxial instability (Morquio, Hurler)

**Odontoid hypoplasia + AAI** — **Morquio** most classic · OB220568 + TO: Hurler also has odontoid hypoplasia — read the options\
✗ Wrong options: Hurler per OB2836 (Hurler instability "not from odontoid hypoplasia")

**Morquio pre-op** — **C-spine flexion–extension X-rays** (intubation risk) · GAG behind dens → stenosis

**Hurler pre-op** — **MRI of whole spine**\
✗ Wrong options: Kniest · Schmid · Turner

**Others with odontoid hypoplasia** — pseudoachondroplasia (normal urine) · diastrophic dysplasia (sulfate transporter, cervical kyphosis, cauliflower ears, hitchhiker thumb) · SED · Down (no hypoplastic dens, abnormal intelligence)

**Pre-op C-spine NOT needed** — Friedreich ataxia · Gaucher · MED · cleidocranial dysplasia

![Odontoid hypoplasia with atlantoaxial instability](../../MCQ/attachments/q4638-illa-aa-instab-odon-hypo.jpg)

## 5 Management

**Morquio** — **decompression + C1–C2 fusion** for myelopathy with instability · realignment osteotomies (malalignment recurs) · guided growth (little evidence)

**AAI surgery triggers** — unsteady gait, limb weakness, dysesthesias, urinary retention

### Treatment – bone marrow transplant

**HSCT / BMT** — ↑ **life expectancy** (Hurler if done in 1st year · Sanfilippo) · **does NOT change orthopaedic manifestations** (poor enzyme penetration of cartilage/bone)

**Age** — HSCT **<24 months** → better development · **<2 yr severe MPS I → HCT preferred** (TO)

**Graft failure** — **relatively HIGH (~30%)** + GVHD (TO) \
✗ Wrong options: "low incidence"

**Enzyme replacement (ERT)** — ↓ visceral + cardiac GAG, ↑ joint mobility, endurance, respiration · **can't cross BBB** → no help for cognition or **cord compression** · little effect on valves, bone, cornea · approved MPS I, II, IVA, VI, VII

## How it's asked

**"Lysosomal storage disease — increased rate of?"** → **carpal tunnel syndrome**\
✗ Wrong options: syringomyelia · diastematomyelia

**"Morquio — all EXCEPT?"** → **low hairline + webbed neck + ↓ C-spine ROM (Klippel-Feil)**

**"6-yr on enzyme replacement — all EXCEPT?"** → **dural ectasia**\
✗ Wrong options: kyphosis · genu valgum · CTS · AAI

**"MPS I — most common MSK abnormality?"** → **progressive joint contracture**

**"Odontoid hypoplasia AAI — which MPS?"** → **Morquio**\
✗ Wrong options: Hurler · Hunter

**"Normal intelligence, urinary keratan sulfate, unstable C1–C2?"** → **Morquio**\
✗ Wrong options: Sanfilippo · pseudoachondroplasia · Down

**"Needs C-spine X-rays pre-op?"** → **Morquio syndrome**\
✗ Wrong options: MED · Gaucher · cleidocranial

**"Genu valgum growth modulation — whole-spine MRI first?"** → **Hurler syndrome**

**"FALSE about MPS treatment?"** → **low incidence of graft failure and GVHD after HSCT** (TO)
