# Olecranon Fractures - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 74 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Epidemiology** — **uncommon** · <5% of pediatric fractures (US) · peak **5–10 yr**

**Mechanism**

| Mechanism | Result |
|---|---|
| **FOOSH, elbow FLEXED** (most common) | Triceps + brachialis tension → **transverse** fracture |
| FOOSH, elbow extended | Varus/valgus → **longitudinal** lines · varus → **radial head dislocation** · valgus → **radial neck fracture** |
| Direct blow (least common) | Shear → anterior tension failure, distal fragment anterior, posterior periosteum intact |

**Location** — **metaphyseal** (most common) · physeal · apophyseal (intra- or extra-articular)

### Association with osteogenesis imperfecta

**OI** — **olecranon (avulsion) fracture after trivial trauma = highly suspicious** ("pathognomonic" per OB) for **OI**\
✗ Wrong options: NF · abuse · ABC

### Ossification

**Olecranon apophysis** — appears **9 yr** · fuses **15–17 yr** · fuses **anterior → posterior** · **partial closure mimics fracture**

**Age 5 girl** — capitellum, radial head, medial epicondyle ossifying · trochlea, olecranon, lateral epicondyle still cartilaginous → any olecranon disruption = fracture

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — posterior swelling · pain · **can't actively extend elbow**

**Exam** — palpable gap / crepitus · **gapping → posterior periosteum torn → unstable** · abrasion suggests direct blow

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP + lateral

**Physis vs fracture** — physis is **oblique**; fracture usually **transverse** · **patella cubiti** (secondary ossification centre) ≠ fracture

**Look for** — radial neck, lateral condyle, distal radius injuries

## 5 Management

### Treatment - tension band

| Fracture | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Stress fracture / apophysitis (throwers) | **NSAIDs, rest, immobilise, avoid resisted extension** |
| **Minimally displaced**, extensor mechanism intact | Long arm splint/cast **3–4 wk** · repeat X-ray at **7 days** |
| **Displaced** · lost posterior periosteum · comminuted | **ORIF** |

**Fixation**

| Option | Note |
|---|---|
| **Tension band wiring** (AO, axial K-wires) | ✓ standard for displaced · **wires stronger** for high loads · **high hardware removal** rate |
| Tension band suture (PDS / FiberWire) ± oblique lag screw | Metaphyseal fractures |
| Plate + screws | **Comminuted** with partly fused centres / near maturity |
| Axial screw ± wire | Risk of physeal disruption with IM lag screw |

**Never in children** — ESIN · CRPP · casting a displaced fracture · **fragment excision + triceps advancement** (elderly, low-demand only)

## 6 Complications

**List** — nonunion · delayed union · compartment syndrome · **ulnar neurapraxia** (pseudarthrosis, poor fixation) · loss of reduction · stiffness

## How it's asked

**"Olecranon fracture after minimal trauma — suspect?"** → **osteogenesis imperfecta**\
✗ Wrong options: abuse · NF

**"5-yr, displaced olecranon fracture?"** → **ORIF with tension band**\
✗ Wrong options: ESIN · cast · plate

**"10-yr skateboard FOOSH, displaced?"** → **tension band construct with wires**\
✗ Wrong options: IM lag screw (physis) · CRPP · excision + triceps advancement
