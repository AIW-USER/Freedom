# Internal Tibial Torsion

*Pediatrics · 55 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Most common cause of in-toeing in toddlers** (**1–3 yr**) · often bilateral · if unilateral → **left** more often (TO) · intrauterine moulding

**Age-wise in-toeing** — infant: **metatarsus adductus** · toddler: **internal tibial torsion** · early childhood: **femoral anteversion** · OB: MA ± ITT most common at 18 months

## 3 Presentation

**History** — noticed on walking · legs "turn in" · tripping

### Rotational profile evaluation

**Staheli rotational profile (5)** — **foot progression angle** · **hip IR/ER** · **thigh–foot axis** · **transmalleolar axis** · **heel bisector**\
✗ Wrong options: **Q angle** (not part)

| Measure | Normal | ITT |
|---|---|---|
| Foot progression angle | −5° to +20° | Negative (internal) |
| **Thigh–foot angle** (prone) | Infant 5° internal (−30 to +20) · age 8: 10° external (−5 to +30) · −10° to +20° (OB) | **>10° internal** |
| Transmalleolar axis | Infant 0 to −10° → 15–23° external | **>15° internal** |
| Hip IR / ER | IR ≤ 70° | Normal |
| Heel bisector | 2nd web space | Normal |

| Thigh–foot axis | Transmalleolar axis |
|---|---|
| ![Thigh–foot axis](../../MCQ/attachments/q2852-a-thigh-foot-axis-2.jpg) | ![Transmalleolar axis](../../MCQ/attachments/q2852-b-transmalleolar-axis.jpg) |

**Red flags for work-up (X-rays)** — **pain** · **LLD** · **progression** · family history of rickets / dysplasia / MPS · rotational profile **>2 SD**\
✗ Wrong options: **metatarsus adductus** (benign)

| In-toeing cause | Key finding |
|---|---|
| Metatarsus adductus | Abnormal heel bisector, normal hindfoot |
| **Internal tibial torsion** | **TFA >10° internal** |
| Femoral anteversion | **IR >70°, ER <20°** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-rays** — not needed unless red flags · CT/MRI for surgical planning

## 5 Management

### Natural history & observation

**Observation + parental education** — resolves by **age 3–4** (Orthobullets, OB) · 6–8 yr (one OB explanation) · **bracing, Denis Browne bar, twister cables, casting, shoes don't help**

**18-month-old triplets with bowing + ITT** → **observation** (packaging)\
✗ Wrong options: vitamin D · serial alignment films · genetics

### Operative criteria

**Supramalleolar derotation osteotomy** — **>6–8 yr** (adolescent) + **functional problems** + **TFA >15°** internal · fewer complications than proximal · plate/K-wires (IM nail if mature)

## How it's asked

**"2-yr, negative foot progression angle (L > R)?"** → **internal tibial torsion** (TO)

**"In-toeing — prompts further work-up EXCEPT?"** → **metatarsus adductus**

**"Part of rotational profile EXCEPT?"** → **Q angle**

**"4-yr, TFA −10° left, normal hips?"** → **observation + education**\
✗ Wrong options: tibial/femoral osteotomy · brace · casting

**"Toddler ITT — expected outcome?"** → **resolves by 3–4 yr without treatment**

**"18-month-old, TFA 30° internal, normal hips?"** → **no treatment — resolves**

**"18-month-old triplets, bowlegs + in-toeing?"** → **observation**
