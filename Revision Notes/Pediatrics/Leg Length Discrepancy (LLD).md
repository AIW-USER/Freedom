# Leg Length Discrepancy (LLD)

*Pediatrics · 59 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **2 cm LLD in up to 2/3** of the population · most <2 cm, asymptomatic, no long-term consequence

### Hemihypertrophy & congenital causes

| Group | Causes |
|---|---|
| **Congenital** | Hemihypertrophy · dysplasias · **PFFD** · DDH · **unilateral clubfoot** |
| **Paralytic** | Spasticity (CP) · polio |
| **Physeal disruption** | **Infection** · **trauma** · tumor |

**Unilateral clubfoot** — **entire involved limb hypoplastic** (✓) → LLD, mostly **tibia** · (✗ Wrong options: hemihypertrophy of other limb) · **no bone-age difference** · **posterior tibial artery dominant** (anterior tibial hypoplastic) · casting first line

**Beckwith-Wiedemann** — hemihypertrophy + **macroglossia** → up to **10% tumor (Wilms)** → **abdominal + pelvic US and AFP 3–4×/yr until age 8**\
✗ Wrong options: thyroid · echo · spine MRI

**Associations** — **back pain** · **OA of long-leg hip** (↓ head coverage, 84%) · functional scoliosis · inefficient gait · ankle equinus · observation of small LLD not linked to scoliosis or back pain (Orthobullets)

### Classification

| Type | Example | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Static** | Malunion of femur / tibia (extra-physeal trauma) | Doesn't change |
| **Progressive** | **Physeal arrest** · congenital | Absolute LLD ↑ · **congenital: proportion stays the same** |

## 3 Presentation

### Measurement & evaluation

**Block test** — blocks under short leg until **pelvis level** · **best initial screening** · accurate, simple, cheap

**Tape** — **ASIS → medial malleolus** (inaccurate with contracture)

**Contractures** — hip, knee, ankle alter apparent length · **hip adduction contracture → apparent shortening of adducted side**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Method | Technique | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Teleoradiograph** | Single exposure, both legs, long film | Good for **angular deformity** · **magnification error** (beam parallax) |
| **Orthoradiograph** | 3 exposures (hip, knee, ankle) | Avoids magnification |
| **Scanogram** | 3 exposures, small cassette | Avoids magnification · less error if patient moves |
| **CT scanogram** | AP scout | **Most accurate with hip / knee / ankle contractures** |
| **Lateral CT scanogram** | Lateral scout | **Best with knee flexion contracture** (others are coronal) |

**Primary initial imaging (Sabharwal, OB)** — full-length standing AP, pelvis level, **magnification marker**

**Bone age** — hand X-ray

## 5 Management

### Predicting LLD from growth arrest (physeal growth rates)

**Arithmetic (Menelaus) method** — growth stops **boys 16 yr, girls 14 yr** · LLD added = **physeal rate × years left** · easiest, least accurate, best for **final years** of growth and exams

| Physis | Orthobullets / most OB | Other OB values |
|---|---|---|
| **Proximal femur** | **3 mm/yr** (1/8 in) | 4 mm/yr |
| **Distal femur** | **9 mm/yr** (3/8 in) | **10 mm/yr** · 9–11 |
| **Proximal tibia** | **6 mm/yr** (1/4 in) | — |
| **Distal tibia** | **5 mm/yr** (3/16 in) | 4 mm/yr |
| Whole leg | **23 mm/yr**, **knee 15 mm/yr** | — |

**Other physes (OB)** — **distal femur fastest** · proximal humerus 7 · distal radius 5–6 · distal tibia 4–5 mm/yr

**Dimeglio** — knee grows **2 cm/yr** (femur 1.1 + tibia 0.9) · growth stops at **bone age 15.5 (boys) / 13.5 (girls)**

| Method | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Green-Anderson tables** | Limb length for age |
| **Moseley straight-line graph** | Graph form · accounts for **skeletal vs chronologic age** · averages serial measurements |
| **Multiplier (Paley)** | Current LLD × age/sex factor · **most accurate for congenital LLD** · uses **chronological age** |
| **Half of final leg length** | **Girls at 3 yr · boys at 4 yr** |

**Best predictor in adolescence** — **skeletal age**\
✗ Wrong options: chronological age · Tanner · father's height · Paley

### Worked examples (OB)

| Scenario | Answer |
|---|---|
| 14-yr boy, distal femoral arrest | 2 yr × 10 = **2 cm** |
| 9-yr boy, distal femoral arrest | 7 yr × 9 = **~6 cm** |
| 12-yr boy, closed distal femur, LLD 2 cm now | 2 + 4 × 10 = **6 cm** |
| 8-yr girl, closed distal femur, LLD 1 cm | 6 yr × 9 + 1 ≈ **6–7 cm, uninjured (left) longer**, no angulation (central arrest) |
| 14-yr boy, DF + PT epiphysiodesis | (10 + 6) × 2 = **3.2 cm** |
| 14-yr boy, PT arrest, LLD 3 mm | 3 + 6 × 2 = **15 mm → observe** |

### Epiphysiodesis indications & timing

| Projected LLD at maturity | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **<2 cm** | **Observation ± shoe lift** |
| **2–5 cm** (OB: 2–6) | **Epiphysiodesis** of long side (femur, tibia or both) · shortening if already mature |
| **>5 cm** (OB: >5–6) | **Lengthening** of short side ± contralateral epiphysiodesis/ostectomy |
| **>20 cm** / non-reconstructable | **Amputation + prosthesis** |
| Historical (OB) | <1.5 cm none · 1.5–5 cm epiphysiodesis · >5 cm lengthening |

**Decide on LLD at maturity**, not at presentation

**Physeal bar excision** — bar **<50%** of physis + **≥2 yr growth** left

**DF + PT epiphysiodesis** — removes **1.5 cm/yr** · 13-yr boy with 4.6 cm = most equal (3 yr left)

**Timing (13-yr boy, puberty 1 month ago, 4 cm)** — **femur AND tibia** at **13 yr 4–6 months** (Menelaus 13 y 4 m · Dimeglio 13 y 6 m)

**Closed physis on short side + LLD already 2.5 cm** — close **both** long-side DF + PT\
✗ Wrong options: DF only (no differential left) · PT only

**Persistent LLD after epiphysiodesis** — most common cause = **error in timing**\
✗ Wrong options: overgrowth · joint penetration

**Small asymptomatic LLD + acute injury** — treat the injury (NSAIDs, rest)\
✗ Wrong options: epiphysiodesis

### Lengthening & distraction osteogenesis

**Corticotomy** — **metaphyseal** · periosteal supply matters more than endosteal (lengthening over nails works)

| Step | Value |
|---|---|
| **Latency** | **5–7 days** (no lengthening in week 1) |
| **Rate** | **1 mm/day** (0.25 mm ×4) |
| Too fast (>1 mm/day) | Painful neuropathy, soft-tissue problems, poor regenerate |
| **Poor regenerate** | **Slow the rate** → then **accordionization** |
| **Consolidation** | Fixator stays **as many days as lengthened** |

**Which bone** — lengthen the **femur** when the discrepancy is femoral (✗ Wrong options: tibia (asymmetric joint lines)) · 15-yr with 5.5 cm → **femoral lengthening** (✗ Wrong options: shoe lift · late epiphysiodesis · 6.5 cm femoral shortening (weak muscles))

**Pre-op stability** — need stable joints above + below · **cruciate deficiency → knee subluxation** · **hip CE angle <20° → hip subluxation**\
✗ Wrong options: Achilles contracture · 8 cm LLD

### Mechanical axis deviation

| Technique | MAD |
|---|---|
| Lengthen along **mechanical axis** (ex-fix) | **None** |
| Lengthen along **anatomic femoral axis** (nail) | **Lateral** MAD |
| Shorten along anatomic axis | **Medial** MAD |

**Angle** — ~**7°** between limb mechanical axis and femoral anatomic axis

## 6 Complications

**Epiphysiodesis** — incomplete arrest / **angular deformity** (open or percutaneous) · **persistent LLD from timing error**

**Lengthening** — pin-site infection · fracture · delayed union · premature cessation · **joint subluxation/dislocation**

| Complication | Clue | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Incomplete corticotomy** | Turning gets harder in **first week** → sudden **pop** + pain | — |
| **Premature consolidation** | Harder to turn at **~6 wk** · **bowed wires**, mature regenerate · femur + fibula most | Keep distracting until bridge ruptures · osteoclasis · repeat corticotomy |
| Causes of premature consolidation | Long latency, slow rate, exuberant bone, compliance, device failure | — |

## X vs Y

| | **Incomplete corticotomy** | **Premature consolidation** |
|---|---|---|
| When | **First days** of distraction | **Weeks** in (~6 wk) |
| Event | Acute pop as osteotomy completes | Device seizes, wires bow |

## How it's asked

**"14-yr boy, complete distal femoral arrest — LLD?"** → **2 cm**

**"9-yr boy, distal femoral arrest?"** → **6 cm**

**"Distal femur stops growing in boys at?"** → **16 yr**

**"12-yr, 2 cm LLD, closed distal femur — projected?"** → **6 cm**

**"8-yr girl, central distal femoral arrest — consequence?"** → **7 cm LLD, left femur longer**\
✗ Wrong options: varus · valgus · recurvatum

**"Same girl — LLD at maturity?"** → **6 cm**

**"Fastest-growing physis?"** → **distal femur**\
✗ Wrong options: proximal humerus

**"14-yr boy, DF + PT epiphysiodesis — correction?"** → **3.2 cm**

**"14-yr boy, PT arrest, LLD 3 mm, bone age 14?"** → **observation**

**"17-yr girl, 10 mm block levels pelvis?"** → **observation**\
✗ Wrong options: CT scanogram · epiphysiodesis

**"Epiphysiodesis at 14 with 4.2 cm, still 3 cm at 18 — why?"** → **error in timing of surgery**

**"13-yr boy, puberty 1 month ago, projected 4 cm?"** → **epiphysiodesis of left femur and tibia in 6 months**\
✗ Wrong options: immediate lengthening

**"11-yr boy, femoral LLD 4 cm at maturity?"** → **timed epiphysiodesis of longer leg**

**"13-yr, left DF closed, LLD 2.5 cm?"** → **right DF + proximal tibia/fibula epiphysiodesis**

**"DF + PT percutaneous epiphysiodesis — most equal result?"** → **13-yr boy with 4.6 cm**

**"Best predictor of remaining growth in adolescents?"** → **skeletal age**\
✗ Wrong options: Paley · Tanner

**"Distraction rate?"** → **1 mm/day**

**"15-yr, 5.5 cm femoral LLD, closed physis?"** → **right femoral lengthening**\
✗ Wrong options: tibial lengthening · femoral shortening

**"Lengthening along mechanical axis vs anatomic axis nail?"** → **no MAD vs lateral MAD**

**"Turning harder, pop on day 9 of lengthening?"** → **incomplete corticotomy with spontaneous completion**

**"Harder to turn at 6 wk, bowed wires?"** → **premature consolidation**

**"Lengthening in first week after osteotomy?"** → **none** (latency)

**"Congenital short femur — risk factor for complication?"** → **cruciate deficiency**\
✗ Wrong options: Achilles contracture · LCEA 25°

**"JRA knee flexion contracture — most accurate LLD method?"** → **CT scanogram**\
✗ Wrong options: teleoradiograph

**"Best LLD method with knee flexion contracture?"** → **lateral CT scanogram**\
✗ Wrong options: blocks · tape

**"Gymnast, 1.2 cm LLD, acute knee strain?"** → **NSAIDs, rest 1 wk**\
✗ Wrong options: epiphysiodesis · cast

**"Unilateral clubfoot — true?"** → **whole limb hypoplastic → LLD**\
✗ Wrong options: hemihypertrophy · bone-age difference · anterior tibial dominant

**"Hemihypertrophy + large tongue?"** → **abdominal/pelvic US + AFP** (Beckwith-Wiedemann)
