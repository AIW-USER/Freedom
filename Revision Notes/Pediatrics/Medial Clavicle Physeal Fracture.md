# Medial Clavicle Physeal Fracture

*Pediatrics · 64 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **pseudodislocation of the SC joint** · childhood equivalent of adult **SC separation** · rare · usually **SH I or II**

**Mechanism** — FOOSH · direct blow (tackle) · abuse rare

**Pathoanatomy** — physis = **weakest link** · periosteal sleeve + costoclavicular / SC ligaments usually intact → remodels · Lee et al: of 40 operated posterior SC injuries, **50% physeal fractures, 50% true dislocations**

### Physeal fusion age

**Clavicle** — **first bone to ossify** (5th week in utero) · central intramembranous centre <5 yr · lateral epiphysis ossifies ~18 yr

**Medial physis** — **~80% of clavicular growth** · ossifies **18–20 yr** · fuses **20–25 yr** (Orthobullets, OB) · **~20–21 yr** (TO, Schmeling) · **last physis to close** (body — Orthobullets; upper extremity — OB) → "SC dislocation" in teens/young adults is often a physeal fracture

**TO** — medial clavicle unites last with humeral head, acromion, ischial tuberosity · males 1–2 yr later than females

| Schmeling stage (TO) | Male (yr) | Female (yr) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 11–14 | 12–15 |
| 2 | 15–20 | 14–18 |
| 3 | 16–36 | 15–26 |
| 4 | 21–40 | 21–37 |
| 5 | 26–40 | 26–39 |

## 3 Presentation

| | **Anterior displacement** | **Posterior displacement** |
|---|---|---|
| Exam | **Palpable bump**, sharp metaphysis under skin · SCM pulls fragment → head tilts to affected side | Swelling, depression of medial clavicle |
| Danger | Low | **Dyspnea, dysphagia, stridor**, ↓ distal pulses, venous congestion, **paresthesias** |
| At risk | — | **Innominate artery + vein, IJV, phrenic + vagus nerves, trachea, esophagus** |

**General** — pain, ↓ arm ROM · prominence ↑ with abduction / elevation

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**AP X-ray** — unreliable

**Serendipity view** — beam **40° cephalic** · **anterior** = affected clavicle **above** opposite · **posterior** = **below**

**Axial CT** — **study of choice** · distinguishes physeal fracture from **SC dislocation** · shows mediastinal injury

## 5 Management

### Management

| Scenario | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Asymptomatic · **anterior** displacement | **Immobilization for comfort / observation** (remodels) |
| Posterior, **no mediastinal injury** | Observation |
| **Acute posterior + airway, esophageal or NV compromise** | **Closed reduction under GA, thoracic surgeon available** |
| Failed closed reduction · **chronic symptomatic posterior** | **ORIF** |
| **Late-presenting posterior** | No closed reduction (clavicle stuck to mediastinal vessels) |

**Anterior reduction** — supine, bolster, traction both arms, posterior pressure ± towel clip · fails → sling

**Posterior reduction** — supine, bolster, traction with **shoulder adducted**, posterior force on shoulder, **towel clip pulls medial clavicle forward** · fails → open

**ORIF** — horizontal incision · **sutures** clavicle → sternum / epiphysis (allow future MRI) · **no pins (migration)** · no plate · CT to confirm stability

## 6 Complications

**Persistent instability** — rare (children remodel)

**Subclavian artery/vein laceration** — rare · **rapidly expanding hematoma** · thick periosteum protective → vessel repair

**Pin migration** — avoid pins around clavicle

## How it's asked

**"9-yr tackled, CT 2 cm anterior displacement, no distress?"** → **immobilization for comfort**\
✗ Wrong options: ORIF · closed reduction with thoracic backup · pinning

**"Medial clavicle physis — true?"** → **fusion around 20–21 yr** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: fusion 15–16 · appears 10–11

**"10-yr medial clavicle physeal injury — fuses when?"** → **20–25 yr**
