# Tarsal Coalition

*Pediatrics · 104 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Prevalence** — **1–2%** (symptomatic ~1%) · **~75% asymptomatic** · bilateral ~50% · 2 coalitions in 10–20% (OB) · extra coalition on CT ~5% (Orthobullets)

### Embryology - failure of segmentation

**Cause** — **failure of mesenchymal differentiation + segmentation** (✓ both CN and TC) · bridge fibrous → cartilage → bone\
✗ Wrong options: failure of formation · ischemic necrosis · microtrauma · peroneal hypertonicity

| Interface | Name |
|---|---|
| Fibrous | **Syndesmosis** |
| Cartilaginous | **Synchondrosis** |
| Bony | **Synostosis** |

**Acquired** (less common) — trauma, degeneration, infection

### Genes & lab markers

**Non-syndromic** — **autosomal dominant** (sibling history)

**Syndromic** — **fibular hemimelia** · carpal coalition · **FGFR1/2/3 craniosynostosis** (**Apert**, Pfeiffer, Crouzon, Jackson-Weiss, Muenke) · TN coalition: Apert, Nievergelt (TO)

| Site | Incidence | Symptom age | Interface |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Calcaneonavicular (CN)** | **Most common** (Orthobullets) · ~45% (TO) | **8–12 yr** (10–14 OB) | Synchondrosis / syndesmosis |
| **Talocalcaneal (TC), middle facet** | ~45% (TO) | **12–15 yr** (12–16 TO) | Synchondrosis → **synostosis** |
| Talonavicular | 1–2% | Infancy | Synostosis |
| Calcaneocuboid · naviculocuneiform | <1% | Variable / adult | — |

**Symptoms start when the bar ossifies** (CN earlier than TC)

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanics** — subtalar joint normally internally rotates ~10° in stance → blocked → **flat arch, forefoot abduction, hindfoot valgus, peroneal spasm** ("**peroneal spastic flatfoot**" — coalition = leading cause)

**Pain sources** — ossification of bar · microfracture at interface · chondral damage · stress on other joints

## 3 Presentation

### Presentation & diagnosis (imaging, anteater sign)

**History** — **recurrent ankle sprains** · activity pain · calf pain (peroneal spasm) · uneven ground

**Pain site** — **sinus tarsi / below fibula → CN** · **distal to medial malleolus / medial foot → TC**

**Exam** — **rigid pes planus** · hindfoot valgus that **doesn't swing into varus on toe-rise** · arch doesn't reconstitute · **↓ subtalar motion** · heel cord contracture · reverse Coleman block for subtalar rigidity

**Deformity association** — **rigid pes planus**\
✗ Wrong options: cavus · rocker-bottom · varus

**Ball-and-socket ankle** — from large **neonatal TC coalition** (develops by ~5 yr)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP · standing lateral · **45° internal oblique (best for CN)** · Harris axial heel

| Coalition | Sign |
|---|---|
| **CN** | **"Anteater (nose)" sign** — elongated anterior calcaneal process (lateral/oblique) · **"comma" sign** (TO) · ± talar beaking |
| **TC** | **"C-sign"** — talar dome to posteroinferior sustentaculum (lateral) · **talar (talonavicular) beaking** — dorsal talar lip remodeling from altered motion, not OA · irregular middle facet on Harris · **dysmorphic, rounded sustentaculum** (Oestreich) |

**CT** — **pre-op**: size, location (TC % of posterior facet on coronal), **rule out 2nd coalition**, arthritis · gold standard in adults

**MRI** — **fibrous / cartilaginous coalition** (normal X-rays + suspicion) · STIR for inflammation

**Normal X-rays, rigid valgus heel, sinus tarsi pain, repeated sprains** → **suspect coalition → MRI**\
✗ Wrong options: Sever · Kohler · Iselin · flexible flatfoot surgery

## 5 Management

```
Symptomatic tarsal coalition
├─ First → short-leg walking cast 4–6 wk + NSAIDs (then orthosis / UCBL)
├─ Recurs — CN → resection + EDB or fat interposition
├─ Recurs — TC <50% → resection + fat / split FHL interposition ± deformity correction
├─ TC >50%, no arthritis → flatfoot reconstruction (OB) / subtalar fusion (Orthobullets)
└─ Failed resection / diffuse arthritis → triple arthrodesis
```

### Initial nonoperative treatment - cast immobilisation

**First line (every first presentation)** — **below-knee walking cast 4–6 wk** (6 wk Orthobullets · 4 wk OB) ± NSAIDs · up to **30% pain-free** (Orthobullets) · very high short-term relief (OB) · inserts may hurt rigid feet · **UCBL** after\
✗ Wrong options: resection first · arthrodesis · CT/MRI first if diagnosis clear · sinus tarsi injection

**Pain recurs after good cast** → **surgery**\
✗ Wrong options: repeat cast · NWB cast · orthotic · more PT · quit sport

### Calcaneonavicular coalition resection with interposition

**Procedure** — **resect bar + interpose EDB muscle or fat** (or bone wax) · resection alone ↑ recurrence · **lateral oblique incision over sinus tarsi between EXTENSOR and PERONEAL tendons** · protect superficial peroneal + sural nerves · ~1 cm gap · NWB cast 3–4 wk

**Add** — calcaneal osteotomy / lengthening for marked valgus · heel cord lengthening if DF < neutral · mild flatfoot → no osteotomy

**Outcome** — **80–85% pain relief** · **full return to sport (87%, Mubarak)** · **↑ subtalar motion (74%)**, ↑ plantar flexion (82%) · repeat resection ~5%\
✗ Wrong options: subtalar fusion · arthroereisis · manipulation

**Contraindications** — advanced degeneration · multiple coalitions → arthrodesis

### Talocalcaneal coalition - resection vs arthrodesis/reconstruction

### Operative criteria

| TC situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **≤50%** of subtalar width (coronal CT), acceptable alignment, no OA | **Resection + fat / split FHL interposition** ± valgus correction |
| **>50%**, joints healthy | **Bony flatfoot reconstruction WITHOUT resection** (OB220914) · **subtalar arthrodesis** (Orthobullets, OB3016) — read the options |
| >50% + **heel valgus >16°** | Poor results with resection (Wilde) |
| **Failed resection**, >50%, or **diffuse TN/CC arthritis** | **Triple arthrodesis** |

**TC approach** — **medial**, curved incision over **sustentaculum**, between **FDL and neurovascular bundle** (Orthobullets) · between FDL and FHL (OB) · find normal anterior + posterior facets, resect with burr, check inversion/eversion · NWB cast 3 wk

**Subtalar fusion** — avoid in young (10–12 yr) — limits foot growth (OB)

**Poor outcomes** — resecting **>50%** · **uncorrected hindfoot valgus** · degenerative change

**Adults (TO)** — usually ossified + secondary arthritis → CT · accommodative orthosis, AFO, injections · resection only if small bar and normal joints · otherwise subtalar / double / triple arthrodesis ± gastrocnemius recession

## 6 Complications

**List** — incomplete resection · **recurrence** · residual pain / stiffness (malalignment, arthritis, **missed 2nd coalition** → pre-op CT)

## X vs Y

| | **CN coalition** | **TC coalition** |
|---|---|---|
| Age | 8–12 | 12–15 |
| Pain | Sinus tarsi, lateral | Below medial malleolus |
| X-ray | **Anteater**, 45° oblique | **C-sign**, talar beak, Harris view |
| Interposition | **EDB** / fat | Fat / **split FHL** |
| Approach | Lateral (extensor/peroneal interval) | Medial (sustentaculum) |
