# Tibial Eminence Fracture

*Pediatrics · 106 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — intra-articular **avulsion of the bony ACL attachment** (tibial spine) · **ages 8–14** · 2–5% of pediatric knee injuries with effusion

**Why bone fails** — eminence **incompletely ossified** → weaker than ACL · fails through **deep cancellous bone** · may extend into plateau (medial most common)

**Anatomy** — non-articular area between plateaus · ACL → **medial spine** (lateral aspect) · insertion 9 mm behind intermeniscal ligament, beside anterior horns · **PCL doesn't attach to spines**

**Associated injuries (~40%)** — meniscal · collateral ligament · capsular · osteochondral · MRI: 15–37% intra-articular pathology

### Adult equivalent - ACL avulsion

**Equivalent in skeletally mature** — **ACL tear** · with fracture → Segond (lateral capsule)\
✗ Wrong options: PCL tear · patellar tendon rupture · lateral meniscus tear

**Adult tibial ACL avulsion** — bony fragment on MRI + arthroscopy\
✗ Wrong options: midsubstance ACL tear · PCL avulsion · bucket-handle

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanism** — rapid deceleration, **hyperextension / rotation** (sport) · fall from bike (hyperextension) · same mechanism as adult ACL tear

## 3 Presentation

**Findings** — **immediate effusion (hemarthrosis)** · knee held **flexed** · can't bear weight · +anterior drawer / Lachman · motion block after analgesia → meniscus or entrapped fragment

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — AP · **lateral (best for displacement)** · intercondylar notch · oblique (plateau extension)

**CT** — pre-op planning · displacement unclear on X-ray

**MRI** — ligament / meniscal injury (better than CT) · not routine once X-ray confirms\
✗ Wrong options: MRI as next step

### Classification & closed reduction in extension

| Modified Meyers & McKeever | Description |
|---|---|
| **I** | **Nondisplaced (<3 mm)** |
| **II** | **Anterior displacement, intact posterior hinge** |
| **III** | **Completely displaced** |
| III+ | Type III + rotation |
| **IV** | Completely displaced, rotated, comminuted |

**OB variant** — IIIa comminution · IIIb rotation

## 5 Management

```
Tibial eminence fracture
├─ Type I → long-leg cast in extension
├─ Type II → aspirate + closed reduction in extension
│      ├─ reduces → long-leg cast in extension, close X-ray follow-up
│      └─ doesn't reduce → arthroscopic / open reduction + fixation
└─ Type III → arthroscopic / open reduction + fixation
```

**Closed reduction** — aspirate tense hemarthrosis + lidocaine · **full extension / hyperextension** · cast at **0°** (or 10–20° flexion — debated) · **3–4 wk** (Orthobullets) · 4–6 wk then hinged brace (OB) · lateral X-ray to confirm + serial films · CT/MRI if unclear · **<5 mm displacement → closed** (OB)

**Traps** — cast in **extension** (✗ Wrong options: 90° flexion) · reduce before immobilizing (✗ Wrong options: knee immobilizer + NWB) · 1–2 wk cast too short\
✗ Wrong options: immediate WB · ACL reconstruction · fragment excision (ACL attached)

### Meniscal entrapment blocking reduction

**Block to reduction** — **anterior horn of medial meniscus (most common)** > intermeniscal ligament > anterior horn lateral meniscus\
✗ Wrong options: ACL · PCL · fat pad

**Kocher (80 cases)** — medial meniscus 36 · intermeniscal 6 · lateral meniscus 1 · entrapment in **26% type II**, **65% type III** · meniscal tear 3.8%

**Lowe** — lateral meniscus + ACL pull fragment in opposite directions (not entrapment)

**Associated with type II** — **meniscal entrapment**\
✗ Wrong options: compartment syndrome (tibial tubercle fracture) · patellar dislocation · growth arrest

### Operative fixation

### Operative criteria

**Indications** — **type III** · **irreducible type II** · block to extension · failed closed reduction · late displacement

**Treatment** — **arthroscopic (or open) reduction + internal fixation** of eminence · anatomic reduction\
✗ Wrong options: ACL reconstruction · tibial tubercle ORIF

| | **Suture** | **Screw** |
|---|---|---|
| Pros | Minimal physeal damage · growth breaks non-absorbable sutures · **biomechanically superior** in type II (OB) | Easier · earlier motion |
| Cons | Technically demanding | Needs large fragment · impingement · iatrogenic comminution · physeal damage · **remove only if malpositioned** |

**Post-op** — cast in extension 7–10 days (variable) → early controlled ROM

## 6 Complications

### Outcomes (laxity, arthrofibrosis)

**Loss of motion** — very common, esp. **extension** (fragment impinges in notch)

**Arthrofibrosis** — **more common after surgery** · adolescents / adults → counsel stiffness · **concomitant ACL injury = independent risk factor for return to OR for MUA**\
✗ Wrong options: nonunion · physeal closure · compartment syndrome

**ACL laxity** — **38–100%**, more after surgery · **common but rarely symptomatic** (even after anatomic fixation — ligament stretched at injury) · ACL reconstruction later 15–25%

**Growth arrest** — iatrogenic at surgery

**Prognosis** — good · **85% return to prior sport**

## How it's asked

**"Which may be treated with closed reduction in extension?"** → **McKeever type II**\
✗ Wrong options: SH II distal femur · type III · SH IV proximal tibia · tibial tubercle type IV

**"12-yr type II, tense effusion — next?"** → **closed reduction; cast if successful, surgery if not**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · immobilizer

**"7-yr successful closed reduction, +Lachman — next?"** → **immobilization in near full extension + frequent monitoring**\
✗ Wrong options: arthroscopic fixation · ACL reconstruction

**"14-yr gymnast, moderately displaced eminence?"** → **long-leg cast after successful fluoroscopic closed reduction**\
✗ Wrong options: 1–2 wk cast · ACL reconstruction

**"9-yr bike fall — type and next step?"** → **type II; closed reduction + long-leg cast in extension**\
✗ Wrong options: 90° flexion

**"Failed reduction — structure entrapped under fragment?"** → **anterior horn of medial meniscus**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral meniscus · ACL · fat pad

**"More likely with type II tibial spine fracture?"** → **meniscal entrapment**\
✗ Wrong options: compartment syndrome · growth arrest

**"14-yr obese, surgery planned — interposed structure?"** → **medial meniscus**\
✗ Wrong options: intermeniscal ligament

**"12-yr soccer, failed ER reduction — block?"** → **medial meniscus**

**"12-yr failed closed reduction?"** → **arthroscopic reduction + fixation of tibial eminence**\
✗ Wrong options: ACL reconstruction

**"12-yr displaced eminence, Lachman lax?"** → **anatomic reduction + internal fixation**\
✗ Wrong options: casting · ACL reconstruction

**"Adult equivalent of tibial spine fracture?"** → **ACL tear**

**"22-yr wakeboard, bony fragment on MRI?"** → **tibial avulsion of ACL**\
✗ Wrong options: midsubstance ACL

**"19-yr arthroscopic suture fixation — likely complication?"** → **arthrofibrosis**\
✗ Wrong options: hardware removal · SONK

**"ACL laxity after eminence fracture?"** → **common, infrequently symptomatic**

**"Concomitant ACL injury + all-epiphyseal reconstruction — outcome?"** → **return to OR for MUA**\
✗ Wrong options: physeal closure · nonunion
