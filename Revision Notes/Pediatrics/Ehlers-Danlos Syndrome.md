# Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome

*Pediatrics · 41 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Genes & lab markers

**Classic EDS** — **COL5A1 / COL5A2** (40–50%) → **type V collagen** (co-expressed with type I; skin fibril + basement membrane assembly) · ↓ type V for fibrillogenesis

**Diagnosis** — **collagen typing of skin biopsy**

### Clinical features & Beighton score

**Features** — **hyperelastic, fragile skin** · easy bruising, **poor wound healing**, widened atrophic scars · **joint hypermobility + dislocation** (shoulder, patella, ankle) · generalised laxity · early OA · chronic MSK pain (50%)

**Other** — **mitral valve prolapse** · **aortic root dilatation (up to 1/3)** · DDH · clubfoot · pes planus · **scoliosis** · high palate · gastroparesis · soft-tissue calcification

**NOT EDS** — **superior lens dislocation → Marfan** (inferior → **homocystinuria**)

### Villefranche classification

| Type | Inheritance | Former name / feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Classical** | **AD** | I (gravis), II (mitis) · COL5A1/2 · skin + scars + hypermobility |
| **Hypermobile** | **AD** | III |
| **Vascular** | **AD** | IV |
| **Kyphoscoliosis** | **AR** | VI (ocular-scoliotic) |
| Arthrochalasis | AD | VIIA, VIIB |
| Dermatosparaxis | AR | VIIC — severe fragility, sagging skin |

**Berlin** — types I–XI · **II + III most common, least disabling**

## 3 Presentation

### Beighton-Horan score (/9)

| Manoeuvre | Points |
|---|---|
| Passive **5th MCP hyperextension >90°** | 1 each side |
| Passive **thumb apposition to forearm** | 1 each side |
| **Elbow hyperextension >10°** | 1 each side |
| **Knee hyperextension >10°** | 1 each side |
| **Palms flat on floor**, knees straight | 1 |

**Hypermobility** — **≥5/9** (threshold varies: >3, 5, 6/9) · used for **EDS / joint laxity**\
✗ Wrong options: CP · myelodysplasia · arthrogryposis · Kniest

**Beighton cut-off (TO)** — adults **≥ 5** · children **≥ 6**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — subluxations/dislocations · kyphoscoliosis

**Echocardiogram** — **mandatory** (aortic root)

## 5 Management

**Mainstay** — **PT, orthotics, pain support**

### Operative criteria

| Problem | Surgery |
|---|---|
| Recalcitrant unstable/painful joint | **Arthrodesis** (soft-tissue stabilisation unlikely to hold) |
| **Progressive scoliosis** (esp. kyphoscoliosis type) | **PSF — longer fusion** (junctional problems) |

## How it's asked

**"NOT associated with EDS?"** → **superior lens dislocation** (Marfan)

**"Beighton-Horan scale used for?"** → **Ehlers-Danlos**

**"Image of thumb-to-forearm / 5th finger hyperextension — condition?"** → **Ehlers-Danlos** (TO; answer not provided by source)

**"Gene in classic EDS?"** → **COL5A1/COL5A2 (type V collagen)**

**"Mandatory investigation?"** → **echocardiogram**
