# Developmental Coxa Vara

*Pediatrics · 31 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **1 in 25,000** (US) · **M = F** · presents **from walking age to 6 yr**

**Bilateral** — **1 in 3** (Orthobullets) · **30–50%** (OB)

**Genetics** — no clear inheritance

**TO lecture** — "often **autosomal dominant**" · associations: **cleidocranial dysostosis**, metaphyseal dysplasia, spondylometaphyseal dysplasia — read the options

**Fairbank's triangle (TO)** — triangular inferior-neck (osteoid) fragment = inverted-Y defect

### Pathophysiology

**Defect** — **proximal femoral physis / ossification defect of inferior femoral neck** → ↓ neck-shaft angle, **vertical physis**

**Mechanics** — vertical physis → ↑ **shear** across physis + ↑ compression of inferomedial neck → progression

**Associated** — femoral neck **stress fractures** · LLD · **early OA**

### Etiologies of coxa vara

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Developmental** | This topic |
| **Congenital** | Congenital short femur, **PFFD** |
| **Acquired** | **SCFE**, infection, **Perthes**, trauma |
| **Dysplasia** | **OI**, Jansen, Schmid, **SED** |
| Other | **Cretinism** (hypothyroid) |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **painless** · **waddling / Trendelenburg gait** (abductor weakness from shortened lever)

**Exam** — **LLD**, limb shortening · **high-riding greater trochanter** · ↑ lumbar lordosis · non-tender ↓ hip ROM (esp. abduction, IR)

**Difficulty cross-leg sitting (TO)** · **HE angle, not neck-shaft angle, defines coxa vara** (NSA unreliable — physeal slip) (TO)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **AP hip in internal rotation** + lateral

| Finding | Value |
|---|---|
| **Neck-shaft angle** | **<120°** (varus) |
| Neck / physis | **Short neck, VERTICAL physis** (✗ Wrong options: horizontal) |
| **Hilgenreiner–epiphyseal (HE) angle** | **↑** (normal **<25°**) — Hilgenreiner line vs proximal femoral physis |
| HE angle — TO lecture | Normal "\~45°" (TO — verify: Orthobullets normal < 25°; 45° is the observe/progress threshold) |
| **Inverted-Y lucency** | **Triangular metaphyseal fragment of INFERIOR neck** (✗ Wrong options: superior · upright Y) |
| Version | **↓ femoral anteversion** (✗ Wrong options: increased) |

**Southwick angle** (epiphyseal-diaphyseal on frog lateral) — ↑ in **SCFE**, not DCV

**CT (3D)** — surgical planning, bone stock, deformity orientation

## 5 Management

### Hilgenreiner-epiphyseal angle & valgus osteotomy

| HE angle | Action |
|---|---|
| **<45°** | **Unlikely to progress → observe** |
| **45–60°** | May progress → close follow-up · **operate if symptomatic** (limp, progression) (Orthobullets) or **neck-shaft <100°** (OB) |
| **>60°** | **Valgus (derotation) osteotomy** |
| Neck-shaft angle progressively **<110°** | Osteotomy (Orthobullets) |

```
Developmental coxa vara
├─ HE <45° → observe
├─ HE 45–60° → follow; osteotomy if symptomatic / progressive / NSA <100°
└─ HE >60° → valgus derotation osteotomy (overcorrect HE <38°)
       ± adductor tenotomy, GT epiphysiodesis / transfer
```

### Operative criteria

**Valgus (derotation) intertrochanteric osteotomy** — **overcorrect** → physis **horizontal**, **HE angle <38°** · correct LLD + version · restore abductor tension · blade plate · direct lateral approach · **adductor tenotomy** · spica / abduction pillow **4–6 wk**

**Bilateral disease** → **bilateral valgus osteotomies**\
✗ Wrong options: varus osteotomy · night abduction brace · fixing the metaphyseal fragment

**Greater trochanter** — **epiphysiodesis** (prevent GT overgrowth) · **distal-lateral transfer** (tip at level of head centre)

**3 goals (TO)** — correct HE angle · **distalise GT** · prevent recurrence (epiphysiodesis)

**Intertrochanteric osteotomy principles (TO)** — most versatile level (iliopsoas stabilises proximal fragment) · CORA: **varus deformity → centre of head**, valgus deformity → base of GT · **valgus osteotomy needs adequate adduction**, varus needs abduction · fixation: varus **90° blade plate**, valgus **double-angle plate**

**Powell osteotomy (TO)** — high valgus osteotomy, fixation **crosses trochanteric physis** → vertical physis made horizontal → compression + fusion · **wedge angle = HE angle − 16°** · others: **Mosher** (multiple cuts), **Pauwels** intertrochanteric valgus

**Target HE** — **< 38°** (Orthobullets) · **\~25–30°** (TO) — read the options

## 6 Complications

**Loss of correction / recurrence** (undercorrection) · **premature proximal femoral physeal closure** · proximal femoral overgrowth · **acetabular dysplasia**

## X vs Y

| | **Developmental coxa vara** | **SCFE** |
|---|---|---|
| Key angle | **Hilgenreiner–epiphyseal ↑** (AP) | **Southwick (epiphyseal-diaphyseal) ↑** (frog lateral) |
| Defect | Inferior-neck ossification defect, **vertical physis** | Through **hypertrophic zone** |
| Version | **↓ anteversion** | — |

## How it's asked

**"Radiographic feature of developmental coxa vara?"** → **increased Hilgenreiner–epiphyseal angle**\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ anteversion · Southwick · horizontal physis · superior-neck Y

**"4-year-old waddling, HE >60° bilaterally?"** → **bilateral proximal femoral valgus osteotomies**

**"HE angle that can be observed?"** → **<45°**

**"Goal of osteotomy?"** → **overcorrect to HE <38° (horizontal physis)**
