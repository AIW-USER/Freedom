# Brachymetatarsia

*Pediatrics · 11 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Sex** — **female:male 25:1**

**Location** — **4th metatarsal** most common · **often bilateral**

### Etiology

**Cause** — **premature epiphyseal (physeal) closure** of metatarsal → congenital hypoplasia of ≥1 metatarsal

**Associated** — **Down** · **Turner** · **Larsen** · Albright · diastrophic dwarfism

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — present since birth · **discomfort with shoe wear** (e.g. heels) · forefoot pain

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **weight-bearing AP + lateral**

**Findings** — short metatarsal(s) · **broken metatarsal parabola**

## 5 Management

### Initial management – shoe modification

**First line** — **extra-depth or extra-wide shoes** → generally improves symptoms\
✗ Wrong options: immobilisation + NSAIDs · MTP fusion · ray resection · lengthening first

**Ineffective** — taping, manipulative reduction

```
Symptomatic brachymetatarsia
├─ First → extra-depth / extra-wide shoes
└─ Persistent symptoms in older child → metatarsal lengthening
       └─ Very rarely → ray amputation
```

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Metatarsal lengthening** | **Persistent symptoms in older child** after shoe modification |
| Amputation (ray resection) | **Very rarely**, persistent symptoms |
| Extensor tenotomy + capsulotomy | **None** — insufficient correction |
| Fusion (MTPJ) | **Not indicated** — complications, ineffective |

![Metatarsal lengthening](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4082-lengthening.jpg)

## How it's asked

**"18-year-old, congenital short 4th metatarsal, new forefoot pain in heels — next step?"** → **extra-depth shoe modification**\
✗ Wrong options: 4th MTPJ fusion · ray resection · lengthening

**"Most commonly affected metatarsal?"** → **4th**

**"Cause?"** → **premature physeal closure**

**"Failed shoe modification?"** → **metatarsal lengthening**
