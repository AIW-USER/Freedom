# Psoas Abscess - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 92 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Epidemiology** — **more common in the tropics** · primary form favours children

**Organism** — ***S. aureus*** most common

**Spread to hip** — via **psoas bursa** (communicates with hip joint in **15%**) · or directly between iliofemoral + iliopubic ligaments → septic arthritis

**Associated** — septic hip · **tuberculous spondylitis** · bowel source (secondary)

## 3 Presentation

**Mimics septic hip** — **fever, hip pain**, limp / refuses weight

**Exam** — **hip held in flexion** · tender iliac fossa mass · **psoas sign**: pain with **extension + internal rotation**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — AP + frog pelvis to exclude septic hip · enlarged / blurred psoas shadow

**Ultrasound** — **study of choice** (imaging section) · summary says MRI generally required — read the options

**MRI** — early changes · hard to get quickly

**CT with contrast** — mass with **ring enhancement** · may be negative early · radiation

**Bone scan** — helpful

## 5 Management

**First line** — **percutaneous US/CT-guided drainage + IV antibiotics**

**Open (extraperitoneal) drainage** — **secondary abscess** (e.g. bowel) to address source

## 6 Complications

**Septic arthritis** of hip · **sepsis**

## X vs Y

| | **Psoas abscess** | **Septic hip** |
|---|---|---|
| Hip X-ray | No effusion widening | Effusion / widening |
| Exam | Psoas sign, iliac fossa mass | Pain with all motion |
| Treatment | Percutaneous drainage | Arthrotomy / I&D |

## How it's asked

**"17-month-old, fever, hip flexed, pain on extension + IR, CT iliopsoas mass?"** → **psoas abscess**\
✗ Wrong options: septic arthritis · sacroiliitis · transient synovitis
