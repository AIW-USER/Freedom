# Humeral Shaft Fracture - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 53 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **<10%** of pediatric humerus fractures

**Mechanism** — neonates: birth (hyperextension/rotation) · adolescents: direct trauma · low energy → **pathologic** lesion · **<3 yr + spiral → abuse** · any fracture in **non-ambulator → NAT** work-up

**Growth** — **proximal 80% : distal 20%** of humeral length (distal ~3 mm/yr)

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — swelling, deformity · neonate **pseudoparalysis** (reflexes intact) · **radial nerve palsy** (no wrist/finger extension)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Full-length AP + lateral humerus** + shoulder and elbow views · transverse / oblique · look for pathologic lesion

![Pediatric humeral shaft fracture](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4005-pediatric-humerus-fracture-2.jpg)

## 5 Management

### Treatment & prognosis

**Almost all → immobilisation** (huge remodelling) · neonate: swathe / pin sleeve to shirt · young child: **sling and swathe**, collar-and-cuff · coaptation splint, hanging cast · **Sarmiento brace** (older) · ROM at 2–3 wk

| Acceptable alignment | Value |
|---|---|
| Younger child | **<35–45°** angulation (Orthobullets) · up to 40° (OB) |
| Older child | **<20°** varus/valgus · **<20°** procurvatum · **<15°** rotation · **<2 cm** shortening |

**Neonatal birth fracture** → **callus by 2 weeks, complete remodelling within 6 months**\
✗ Wrong options: malunion · nonunion · radial nerve risk

**8-month-old midshaft fracture, angulated** → **splint + sling for comfort** · consider NAT\
✗ Wrong options: ORIF · CRPP · hanging cast (child doesn't sit/stand)

![Remodelling of a pediatric humeral shaft fracture](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4005-figure-3-remodelling-humeral-shaft.jpg)

### Operative criteria

| Indication for fixation |
|---|
| **Open fracture** |
| Polytrauma (needs arms for mobilisation) |
| **Ipsilateral forearm fracture — "floating elbow"** (✓ the indication) |
| Associated shoulder injury |
| Unacceptable / unstable alignment |

**Not indications** — mechanism, age, open physes, **radial nerve palsy**

**Fixation** — **flexible IM nails** · K-wires · plate (anterior/anterolateral/posterior) 3.5/4.5 mm

## 6 Complications

**Radial nerve palsy** — **<5%**, middle + distal third · **neurapraxia** → **observe** · EMG ± explore if no return by **3–4 months**\
✗ Wrong options: immediate exploration

**Malunion** — rarely functional (shoulder ROM) · 20–30° fine · LLD common but harmless · delayed union rare (US stimulator)

## How it's asked

**"3-day-old birth humeral fracture — prognosis?"** → **callus by 2 weeks, remodelled by 6 months**

**"6-yr distal-third fracture, can't dorsiflex wrist?"** → **monitor radial nerve + sling and swathe**

**"8-month-old midshaft humerus?"** → **splint and sling for comfort**

**"10-yr humerus fracture — best indication for fixation?"** → **ipsilateral forearm fracture (floating elbow)**

**"Distal humeral physis contribution?"** → **20%**
