# Larsen's Syndrome

*Pediatrics · 57 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — ~**1 in 100,000** live births

### Genes & lab markers

| Inheritance | Gene |
|---|---|
| **Autosomal dominant** | **Filamin B** |
| **Autosomal recessive** | **Carbohydrate sulfotransferase 3** deficiency |

**Diagnosis** — family history + clinical/radiographic features · **no commercial genetic test** (many mutations)

### Orthopaedic manifestations

**Dislocations** — **hips** · **knees (bilateral)** · shoulders · **elbows (radial heads, bilateral)**

**Other** — **clubfeet** (equinovarus / equinovalgus) · scoliosis · **cervical kyphosis** (hypoplastic cervical vertebrae) · extra carpal ossification · hand deformities

**Non-ortho** — laryngeal / tracheal problems · heart valve or aortic lesions · hyperelasticity → hypotonia

## 3 Presentation

**Face** — **flattened (depressed) nasal bridge** · **prominent forehead** · hypertelorism / telecanthus

**Hands** — long cylindrical **non-tapering fingers** · wide distal phalanx of thumb

**Intelligence** — **normal**

**Hypotonia** — uncommon · may signal **cervical cord compression** · myelopathy → extremity weakness

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Cervical kyphosis screening

**C-spine AP + lateral** — **in the first year of life** · and **before any intubation** or manipulation → avoid iatrogenic cord injury

**MRI** — cervical kyphosis or myelopathy

**Findings** — vertebral **hypoplasia** · **cervical kyphosis with subluxation** · hip dislocation

**Hips** — AP pelvis + lateral · **ultrasound if <3 months**

![Cervical kyphosis in Larsen syndrome](../../MCQ/attachments/q210092-illa-cervical-spine.jpg)

## 5 Management

**Priority** — **stabilise / image the cervical spine BEFORE any hip or knee procedure**\
✗ Wrong options: hip open reduction first · knee procedures first

### Operative criteria

| Problem | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Cervical kyphosis, **no deficit** | **Posterior cervical fusion** in **first 18 months** |
| Cervical kyphosis **with deficit** | **Anterior/posterior decompression + fusion** |
| Hip dislocation | Closed reduction rarely works → **open reduction** (failed closed, contractures, unilateral) · bilateral = controversial, do early and only once |
| Knee dislocation | Closed reduction + casting rarely works → **open reduction + femoral shortening + collateral ligament excision** |

## How it's asked

**"Bilateral radial head + knee dislocations, flat facies — must check?"** → **cervical kyphosis**\
✗ Wrong options: vertical talus · trigger thumb · tibial hemimelia

**"Larsen child, hip pain, knee dislocations — next step?"** → **cervical spine radiographs**\
✗ Wrong options: hip open reduction · VDRO · tibial osteotomy · ACL reconstruction

**"Cervical kyphosis without deficit — timing of fusion?"** → **posterior fusion in first 18 months**
