# Cavovarus Foot in Pediatrics & Adults

*Pediatrics · 13 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Deformity components

**Components** — **cavus** (high arch) · **plantarflexed 1st ray + forefoot pronation** · **hindfoot varus** · forefoot adduction

### Etiology

**Neurologic** — **~67% (two-thirds)** · identifying the neuro cause is critical

**Unilateral** → rule out **tethered cord / spinal cord tumour** · **Bilateral** → most commonly **Charcot-Marie-Tooth (CMT)**, often hereditary/congenital

**Idiopathic** — subtle, bilateral (**subtle cavus**, no weakness)

**Traumatic** — talus fracture malunion · compartment syndrome · crush injury

**Conditions with cavovarus** — **CMT** · **cerebral palsy** · **Friedreich ataxia** · spinal cord lesions · polio · amniotic band syndrome

### Pathomechanics (PL vs TA, PB vs PTT) & Charcot-Marie-Tooth

| Pair | Strong (spared) | Weak | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PL vs TA** | **Peroneus longus** (hypertrophic) | **Tibialis anterior** | **Plantarflexed 1st ray** (cavus) — primary deformity |
| **PTT vs PB** | **Tibialis posterior** | **Peroneus brevis** | **Hindfoot varus** + forefoot pronation |
| Extrinsics vs intrinsics | **Long flexors + extensors** | **Lumbricals, interossei** (first affected) | **Claw toes** (intrinsic minus) |
| EHL recruited for weak TA | **EHL** | TA | **1st MTP hyperextension**, windlass drives 1st MT down |

**Sequence (forefoot-driven)** — plantarflexed 1st MT + pronated forefoot → medial forefoot strikes first → **subtalar joint supinates** for tripod contact → **compensatory hindfoot varus** → flexible at first, **rigid with time**

**Forefoot position** — **pronation**\
✗ Wrong options: supination (the EXCEPT answer)

**CMT genetics** — HMSN · **PMP22 duplication, chromosome 17** · weak TA + PB, **normal PL + PT**

**Overactive muscle with cavus** — **posterior tibialis**\
✗ Wrong options: TA (causes DF + inversion) · toe flexors/extensors

**Gait effect** — transverse tarsal joint **locks in stance** → lateral column overload

## 3 Presentation

**History** — recurrent **ankle sprains**, lateral ankle pain (peroneal pathology) · **lateral foot pain** (5th MT stress fracture) · **calluses under 1st + 5th MT heads** · **plantar fasciitis**

**Exam** — **peek-a-boo heel** (varus heel seen from front) · prominent 1st MT fat pad · **Silfverskiöld** (gastrocnemius tightness common) · unstable gait, ↑ double-limb stance

**Neuro clues** — **1st dorsal interosseous wasting** of hand → **CMT** · **scoliosis** → CMT · **spinal dysraphism** · high-steppage gait

**Tripod (TO)** — foot stands on **1st MT head, 5th MT head, calcaneus** · CMT: intrinsics weaken first; PL overpowers weak TA → 1st ray drops → hindfoot varus

**Cavus mimicking equinus (TO)** — hide the forefoot: heel may be neutral · lateral X-ray **high calcaneal pitch with normal tibiocalcaneal angle = cavus** · TAL would cause calcaneus · CORA at navicular → osteotomise **medial cuneiform** (plantar opening wedge for cavus, medial opening for adductus)

### Coleman block test – hindfoot flexibility & forefoot-driven deformity

**Used for** — **cavovarus foot** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: CVT · planovalgus · metatarsus adductus

**Tests** — **hindfoot flexibility**\
✗ Wrong options: forefoot flexibility · ankle instability · Achilles tightness (that's Silfverskiöld)

**Technique** — **1–1.5 inch block under lateral foot + heel** → 1st ray drops off medially → removes forefoot contribution

**Flexible** — heel corrects to **neutral or valgus** → forefoot-driven → **forefoot procedures** only

**Rigid** — heel **stays in varus** → **lateralising calcaneal osteotomy** + forefoot procedures

![Coleman block test — flexible hindfoot corrects](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4063-coleman-block.jpg)

**Other signs (distractors)** — "too many toes" = forefoot abduction in flatfoot · inability to single-heel-rise = PTT insufficiency

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — standing AP + lateral ankle · standing AP, lateral, oblique foot

| View | Measurement | Cavovarus value |
|---|---|---|
| **AP foot** | **Talocalcaneal (Kite) angle** | **<20°** (nl 20–45°) → hindfoot varus |
| AP foot | Talonavicular coverage | **>7°** overcoverage → forefoot adduction |
| AP foot | Metatarsal overlap | Forefoot pronation |
| **Lateral foot** | **Meary's (talo–1st MT) angle** | **>4° apex dorsal** (1st MT line passes dorsal to talus) |
| Lateral foot | **Calcaneal pitch** | **>30°** |
| Lateral foot | **Sinus tarsi see-through, double talar dome** | Hindfoot ER relative to cassette |
| Lateral foot | Bell-shaped cuboid · ↑ 5th MT base–medial cuneiform distance | — |
| Oblique | MT stress fractures, CN coalition | — |

![Meary's angle — apex dorsal break](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4063-meary.jpg)

**Calcaneal pitch** — **> 30°** (Orthobullets) · **> 25–30°** (TO) — read the options

**Half of cavus = CMT (TO)** — treat as neuromuscular until proven otherwise (TO)

### Work-up – electrodiagnostic studies

**CMT diagnostic algorithm** — **neurologic exam → EMG/NCS → genetic testing** (genetic first if family member has known CMT)

**Bilateral cavovarus, lateral pain, hand intrinsic wasting, family history** → **electrodiagnostic studies** next\
✗ Wrong options: muscle biopsy (dystrophies) · nerve biopsy (only if inconclusive) · CK/aldolase · echo

## 5 Management

```
Cavovarus foot
├─ Mild, flexible → orthotic (lateral posting + recessed 1st ray)
├─ Failed nonop, flexible hindfoot (Coleman corrects) → plantar release + 1st MT DF osteotomy ± PL→PB transfer
└─ Rigid hindfoot (Coleman stays varus) → add lateralising calcaneal osteotomy
       └─ Painful rigid arthritic → triple arthrodesis (rarely; poor long-term)
```

### Orthoses – lateral hindfoot posting & recessed first ray

**Supple adult cavovarus** — **full-length semi-rigid orthotic with 1st-ray recess + lateral hindfoot posting/wedge** · not indicated in children (Orthobullets)\
✗ Wrong options: medial posting (flatfoot) · walker boot · accommodative · lace-up

**Other orthotic matches** — hallux rigidus → **Morton's extension** · midfoot arthritis → rocker-bottom shoe · flexible planovalgus → **medial posting** · rigid cavovarus → orthoses don't work

**More severe** — **SMO** · **AFO** if dynamic equinus (equinocavovarus) · lace-up brace / high-top if rigid bracing not tolerated · accommodative shoes rarely enough

### Surgery – first metatarsal dorsiflexion osteotomy, plantar release, calcaneal osteotomy, EHL transfer

| Problem | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **Cavus** | **Plantar fascia release** · Steindler stripping (short flexors off calcaneus) |
| **Plantarflexed 1st ray** | **PL → PB transfer** (↓ 1st-ray PF without losing eversion) · **1st MT dorsiflexion osteotomy** |
| **Flexible hindfoot varus** (Coleman corrects) | **1st MT DF osteotomy** ± plantar release — calcaneus spared |
| **Rigid hindfoot varus** (Coleman fails) | **Lateralising (lateral slide / Dwyer closing-wedge) calcaneal osteotomy** + 1st MT DF osteotomy |
| PTT overpull / TA weakness | **PTT transfer to dorsum** |
| Equinus | **Gastrocnemius recession** (less weakness, with plantar release) · **TAL staged weeks after** plantar release |
| Chronic lateral ankle instability | **Broström** (fails unless deformity corrected) |
| **Clawed hallux** | **Modified Jones: EHL → 1st MT neck + IP fusion** |
| Lesser toe clawing | Extensors → 2nd–5th MT necks |
| Painful rigid arthritic hindfoot | Triple arthrodesis — **almost never** (poor long-term) |

**Clawed hallux distractors** — ✗ Wrong options: EHL to proximal phalanx / FHB tenotomy (worsen MTP hyperextension) · original Jones (EHL to neck only — inadequate) · FHL tenotomy alone · MTP + IP fusion (stiff ray) · Keller · metatarsal head resection

**Flatfoot procedures (distractors)** — ✗ Wrong options: medial calcaneal slide · lateral column lengthening · FDL transfer · 1st TMT fusion in plantarflexion

### Equinus – gastrocnemius recession (Silfverskiöld)

**Silfverskiöld** — DF ↑ with **knee flexion** → **isolated gastrocnemius** tightness → **gastrocnemius recession** · tight in both positions → **Achilles/soleus** → TAL

**Diabetic equinus after casting** — toe-drag in swing + DF improves with knee flexion → **gastrocnemius recession**\
✗ Wrong options: Thompson positive · too-many-toes · fixed dorsiflexion

## 6 Complications

**Ankle instability** — lateral ligament reconstruction **fails** if cavovarus not corrected · → **varus ankle arthritis**

**Stress fractures** — **5th MT base (Jones)** · 4th MT · **navicular** · **medial malleolus** (↑ medial compression; ↑ nonunion → cast NWB)

**Others** — **hallux sesamoiditis** (plantarflexed 1st MT) · **peroneal tendon pathology** (**PB** most common) · **plantar fasciitis** · metatarsalgia, calluses

**NOT caused by cavovarus** — **hallux valgus** (→ planovalgus)

## X vs Y

| | **Flexible hindfoot** | **Rigid hindfoot** |
|---|---|---|
| Coleman block | Heel → **neutral / valgus** | Heel **stays varus** |
| Driver | **Forefoot** (plantarflexed 1st ray) | Fixed hindfoot |
| Orthotic | **Lateral posting + recessed 1st ray** | Not effective |
| Surgery | **1st MT DF osteotomy + plantar release** ± PL→PB | **+ lateralising calcaneal osteotomy** |

## How it's asked

**"Coleman block test assesses?"** → **cavovarus foot** · **hindfoot flexibility** (TO)

**"Had lateralising calcaneal + 1st MT DF osteotomy — preop finding?"** → **heel stays in varus with block under lateral foot**

**"Calluses under 1st + 5th MT heads; heel corrects on block?"** → **flexible hindfoot varus**

**"Adult cavovarus not correcting on Coleman?"** → **1st MT DF osteotomy + lateral displacement calcaneal osteotomy**\
✗ Wrong options: LCL · medial slide

**"First-ray plantarflexion: weak X, strong Y, transfer Y to Z?"** → **TA weak, PL strong, PL → PB**

**"Meary's line dorsal to talus, neuromuscular disorder — overactive muscle?"** → **posterior tibialis**

**"PMP22 duplication — EXCEPT?"** → **forefoot supination** (it's pronation)

**"Potential result of cavovarus EXCEPT?"** → **hallux valgus** (✓ instability, 5th MT fracture, peroneal subluxation, plantar fasciitis)

**"PMP duplication chr 17 — relative strengths?"** → **weak PB, normal PT**

**"CMT child warned of higher risk of?"** → **medial malleolus stress fracture**

**"Bilateral cavovarus, hand wasting, family history — next diagnostic step?"** → **electrodiagnostic studies**

**"Orthotic for flexible adult cavovarus?"** → **lateral hindfoot posting with recessed 1st ray**

**"Pictured orthotic suits?"** → **supple adult pes cavus**

**"Coleman → 3° valgus, failed orthotic — part of surgery?"** → **1st ray DF osteotomy + plantar fascia release**\
✗ Wrong options: triple · subtalar fusion · medial slide

**"Fixed clawed hallux?"** → **EHL to MT neck + IP fusion (modified Jones)**

**"HMSN 12-year-old, block test corrects heel?"** → **plantar release + 1st MT osteotomy ± tendon transfers**\
✗ Wrong options: calcaneal osteotomy · triple

**"Clawed hallux in cavovarus, painless MTP/IP motion?"** → **1st IP fusion + EHL transfer**

**"Hindfoot corrects on Coleman, no arthritis — include?"** → **1st ray DF osteotomy**

**"PL→PB, Broström, lateralising calcaneal osteotomy — supporting exam?"** → **↑ inversion vs other side + no change on Coleman**

**"Equinus — when is gastrocnemius recession right?"** → **DF improves with knee flexion**
