# Osteomyelitis - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 76 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **1 in 5,000** children <13 yr · mean age **6.6 yr** · **boys 2.5×** · common in **healthy** children · first decade (rich metaphyseal supply, immature immunity) · mortality 50% → **<1%** with antibiotics

**Route** — **hematogenous seeding of the METAPHYSIS**\
✗ Wrong options: epiphysis · direct inoculation

**Trauma history** — **30%** (Orthobullets) · **20–40%** (OB) · bacteremia source: skin, otitis, pharyngitis, tooth brushing / dental care

**Risk factors** — diabetes · hemoglobinopathy · JRA · chronic renal disease · immune compromise · **varicella**

### Pathophysiology

**Metaphysis** — capillaries make **sharp turns** into venous sinusoids → **sluggish, turbulent flow** · low pH + low O2 near physis · spread via Haversian + Volkmann canals

**Pus through metaphyseal cortex** → **subperiosteal abscess**

**Pus through INTRA-ARTICULAR metaphysis** → **septic arthritis** · **hip, shoulder, elbow, ankle ("SHEA")** · not **knee** (extra-articular metaphysis)

**Infants** — transphyseal vessels → epiphyseal osteomyelitis + septic arthritis · **<1 yr** (Orthobullets) · **<2 yr** (TO)

![Metaphyseal osteomyelitis spreading to joint and subperiosteal space](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4031-illustration-showing-the-progression-of-hematogenous-osteomy.jpg)

### Intra-articular metaphysis & septic arthritis spread

| Bone (intra-articular metaphysis/physis) | Joint |
|---|---|
| **Proximal femur** | Hip |
| **Proximal humerus** | Shoulder |
| **Proximal radius / radial neck** | Elbow |
| **Distal fibula / distal lateral tibia** | Ankle |
| ✗ Wrong option: distal femur, proximal tibia | **Knee — least likely** |
| ✗ Wrong option: distal radius | **Wrist — not** (TO) |

### Organisms & antibiotics (MRSA, PVL, GAS, Pseudomonas)

| Setting | Organism |
|---|---|
| **All children** | ***S. aureus*** (90% Song & Sloboda · 70% Blyth) |
| **Neonate** | **Group B strep** |
| Young child | ***Kingella kingae*** (↑) · most common SA organism 3 months–5 yr · PCR-negative cases |
| **Puncture wound of foot** (through sneaker) | ***Pseudomonas*** (100% in Puffinbarger) |
| **After varicella** | **Group A β-hemolytic strep** |
| **Sickle cell** | ***Salmonella*** |
| Unvaccinated | *H. influenzae* (now rare) |
| TB | Extrapulmonary in children · spine 50%, large joints 25%, long bones 11% |

**PVL (Panton-Valentine leukocidin)** — CA-MRSA (also some MSSA) cytotoxin · **<5% of S. aureus** · complex/multifocal infection, prolonged fever, **abscess, DVT, sepsis**, necrotizing pneumonia · lyses neutrophils (indirect)\
✗ Wrong options: Strep · S. epidermidis · Klebsiella

**Coagulase** — S. aureus factor that **activates prothrombin → thrombin → fibrin clot** → DVT (✓ direct cause; PVL indirect)\
✗ Wrong options: adhesins · eDNA (biofilm) · vWF binding protein

**Other virulence (distractors)** — superantigens (toxic shock) · biofilm · protein A (binds IgG Fc)

**MRSA predictors** — **T >38°C · WBC >12,000 · Hct <34% · CRP >13 mg/L** → MRSA probability **4/4 = 92%** · 3 = 45% · 2 = 10% · 1 = 1%

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **limp / refusal to bear weight** · **usually not toxic** · ± fever · prior antibiotics mask

**Exam** — warm, swollen, **point tenderness** (check pelvis, spine) · motion limited by pain · pelvic compression pain (Gaenslen) → pelvic osteomyelitis

**Neonate** — failure to thrive, **multifocal** (shoulder + hips) → **ultrasound** of hip + shoulder\
✗ Wrong options: MRI first · Pavlik

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Workup - aspiration negative, MRI & US

**Labs**

| Test | Fact |
|---|---|
| **CRP** | **↑ in 98%** · rises in **6 h** · **best to monitor response** · should fall by **48–72 h** · normalises ~1 wk (Orthobullets) / 1 month (OB) |
| **ESR** | ↑ in **90%** · peaks **3–5 days** · falls too slowly (normal ~3 wk) · unreliable in neonates + sickle cell |
| **WBC** | ↑ in only **~25%** (✓ "often normal") |
| Procalcitonin | ↑ in **58%** · falls with effective treatment |
| **Blood culture** | + in **30–50%** (✗ Wrong options: 75–80%) |
| **Bone aspirate culture** | + in **50–70%** (51–73%, 50–85% OB) |

**Most likely abnormal test** → **CRP**

**Imaging**

| Modality | Fact |
|---|---|
| X-ray | Early normal / soft-tissue edema · periosteal new bone **5–7 days** · osteolysis **10–14 days** · metaphyseal rarefaction 1–2 wk |
| **MRI** | **Gold standard** · **T1 ↓, T2 ↑, gadolinium ↑** · sensitivity **88–100%** · shows abscess · plan surgery |
| Bone scan | **92%** sensitive · non-focal toddler, **multifocal** · **cold scan = aggressive / osteonecrosis** |
| CT | Later bone changes (✗ Wrong options: study of choice) |
| US | Neonate effusion, subperiosteal collection |

**Fever + ↑ CRP + negative joint aspirate (e.g. 1,000–9,000 cells)** → **MRI** (osteomyelitis / subperiosteal abscess)\
✗ Wrong options: arthrotomy · observe · oral antibiotics

**Septic joint excluded** — synovial WBC **<50,000** essentially rules out (Kocher 2/4 ≈ 40%)

**Suspected SA of shoulder/hip/elbow/ankle** — **pre-op MRI ↓ repeat surgery** · don't delay unstable patient\
✗ Wrong options: MRSA low risk · negative PCR rules out

### Subacute osteomyelitis vs tumour - biopsy

**Subacute** — bone pain + X-ray changes, **no systemic signs** · ↑ host resistance / ↓ virulence / prior antibiotics · afebrile, mild pain, **normal WBC, ↑ ESR**

**Classification** — IA/IB lucency · II metaphyseal with cortical loss · III diaphyseal · IV onion skin · V epiphyseal · VI spinal

**Biopsy + culture** — when diagnosis unclear / rule out tumour (Ewing, osteosarcoma, EG, lymphoma) · send tissue for pathology at every debridement

### Brodie abscess – subacute osteomyelitis

**Type** — **subacute** (TO) · age **10–20 yr** · **proximal tibia** most common · metaphysis (tibia, femur) · S. aureus

**X-ray** — solitary well-circumscribed **lucency with sclerotic rim** at metaphyseal-epiphyseal junction

**MRI** — **penumbra sign**: rim of vascular granulation tissue, **higher T1 signal** than cavity (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: hydatid · hemangioma

**Labs** — normal or mildly ↑\
✗ Wrong options: "elevated blood counts" (TO false statement)

**Treatment** — **surgical debridement / curettage (± graft) + antibiotics**\
✗ Wrong options: wide resection + chemo · empiric antibiotics alone · cast

### TB osteomyelitis

**Features** — constitutional symptoms, night sweats · normal WBC, ↑ ESR · **PPD +** · osteopenia + subchondral erosions · polyostotic

**Diagnosis** — biopsy: **caseating granulomas, giant cells** · **Ziehl-Neelsen** ("red snappers") · **Löwenstein-Jensen** culture

**Treatment** — multi-agent antibiotics **≥1 yr** · rarely debridement (sinus risk)

## 5 Management

```
Pediatric acute hematogenous osteomyelitis
├─ No abscess → aspirate / biopsy for culture → empiric IV antibiotics
│      (improves within 48 h → no surgery; IV → oral)
├─ Subperiosteal / intraosseous abscess, pus aspirated → I&D + antibiotics
└─ No improvement 48–72 h or CRP not falling → repeat MRI → I&D
```

### Surgical indications & subperiosteal abscess

**Surgery** — **subperiosteal or deep abscess** · **pus on aspiration** · **failure of appropriate antibiotics** (48–72 h) · **chronic infection / sequestrum** · adjacent septic arthritis

**Not failure** — improving at 24 h · on wrong drug (e.g. gentamicin for S. aureus) · no trial yet

**Worsening at 2–3 days on antibiotics** → **subperiosteal abscess**\
✗ Wrong options: CRMO · atypical organism · Brodie

**Subperiosteal abscess sites** — femur 23–29%, tibia 19–26%

**Septic shock + MRSA + abscess** → **fluids + antibiotics + urgent I&D**\
✗ Wrong options: wait for cultures

**Septic hip with adjacent osteomyelitis** — straight to **OR debridement** (cultures intra-op) · complication rate 38% vs <1% isolated SA

**Technique** — evacuate pus, debride, drill intraosseous collections, culture + pathology · drains or pack, re-look in 2–3 days · remove sequestrum in chronic

**Antibiotics before cultures** — only if septic / unstable

### Antibiotic choice

| Situation | Drug |
|---|---|
| Low MRSA prevalence | **Nafcillin / oxacillin** (PBP binders) |
| High MRSA prevalence | **Clindamycin** (50S) or **vancomycin** |
| **Sepsis / severe / MRSA** | **Vancomycin** — binds **D-Ala-D-Ala** |
| Gram-negative rods | + 3rd-gen cephalosporin |
| <1 month (Copley) | Ampicillin/sulbactam + gentamicin |
| 1–3 months | Vancomycin + ceftriaxone |

**Duration** — IV **4–6 wk** (Orthobullets, controversial) · 3–4 wk (OB) · 20 days = 30 days for MSSA/strep · MRSA: 24–48 h IV → 4–6 wk oral (OB)

**Oral vs PICC IV** — **equal cure** · PICC → **more ED visits / rehospitalisation**, ↑ cost

### Monitoring response - CRP

**Best early indicator** — **serial CRP** · peaks ~day 2 post-op then falls\
✗ Wrong options: ESR · WBC · MRI · X-ray · procalcitonin rise

## 6 Complications

### DVT with MRSA osteomyelitis

**Risk factors** — **CRP >6 mg/dL** · **surgical treatment** · **age >8 yr** · **MRSA**\
✗ Wrong options: fever >38.5°C

**Organism** — **MRSA** · PVL endothelial injury + coagulase\
✗ Wrong options: MSSA · Kingella · TB

**Action** — calf pain / swelling → **venous duplex US** · therapeutic anticoagulation · MRSA often needs repeat I&D

### Chronic osteomyelitis (sequestrum, involucrum)

**Sequestrum** — **necrotic bone walled off from blood supply = nidus** of chronic infection

**Involucrum** — **new periosteal bone** around sequestrum

**Brodie abscess** — chronic abscess walled by sclerosis + fibrous tissue

![Sequestrum and involucrum](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4031-sequestrum-diagram-2-moved.jpg)

### Late growth disturbance

**Physeal damage** → **LLD + angular deformity** (∝ physeal destruction) · LLD apparent ~**9 yr** average · **follow to maturity** with **standing full-length X-rays**

**Others** — septic arthritis (I&D) · meningitis · pathologic fracture · AVN · chronic / multifocal infection

## X vs Y

| | **Acute osteomyelitis** | **Subacute (Brodie)** |
|---|---|---|
| Systemic | Fever, ↑ CRP | Afebrile, normal WBC, ↑ ESR |
| Age | Mean 6.6 | 10–20 |
| X-ray | Normal early | Lucency + sclerotic rim |
| Treatment | IV antibiotics ± I&D if abscess | Curettage + antibiotics |
