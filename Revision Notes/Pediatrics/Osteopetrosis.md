# Osteopetrosis

*Pediatrics · 77 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — "marble bone disease" · **defective osteoclastic resorption** → dense but **brittle** bone, obliterated medullary canals

**Prevalence** — AD **1 in 20,000** · AR **1 in 200,000** · AD = **most common** · 75% penetrance, may skip generations

### Pathophysiology (carbonic anhydrase II, osteoclast acidification)

**Core defect** — osteoclast **cannot acidify Howship's lacuna** → no HCl → hydroxyapatite not dissolved → bone stays thick, disorganised\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ remodeling · ↓ osteoblasts · ↓ hydroxyapatite

**Carbonic anhydrase II** — CO2 + H2O → H+ + HCO3− · H+ pumped across **ruffled border**

**Causes** — **carbonic anhydrase II** loss-of-function or **chloride channel** dysfunction (Orthobullets) · CA II "most common" (OB4418, OB219284) · proton pump 60%, CLCN7 12%, CA II <5% (OB4814) — read the options

**Osteoblasts** — normal

### Classification

| Type | Inheritance | Defect | Course |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Malignant (infantile)** | **AR** (11q13) | **Proton pump / Cl channel** | Fatal early without **BMT** |
| **Intermediate** | AR | **Carbonic anhydrase II** / Cl channel | — |
| **Benign** | **AD** (most common) | **Cl channel** | Normal life · **type II = Albers-Schönberg** (anemia, fractures, early OA) · **most seen by orthopaedists** |

**ADO I vs II** — I: diffuse sclerosis, esp. cranial vault · **II: rugger jersey spine, bone-in-bone iliac wings, skull base**

**TO** — lists AD, AR **and X-linked recessive** forms

## 3 Presentation

### Clinical features & complications

**AR (malignant)** — **severe anemia** from infancy (marrow encroachment) → bleeding, infections · **hepatosplenomegaly** (extramedullary hematopoiesis) · macrocephaly · progressive **blindness + deafness** · dental abscess, **mandibular osteomyelitis** · death from bleeding / infection

**AD (benign)** — often asymptomatic until **low-energy (transverse) fracture** · anemia · **low back pain** (↑ spondylolysis) · early **hip OA** · normal height · **high risk of cranial nerve palsy**

**Cranial nerve palsies** — foraminal overgrowth · **optic > auditory > trigeminal > facial**

**Fractures** — lifelong **40–50%** (40–60% OB) · **LE > UE > axial** · transverse · delayed union, malunion

**Associated** — **coxa vara** (femoral neck nonunion / stress fractures) · **osteomyelitis** (poor marrow vascularity, ↓ WBC function) · carpal tunnel · Caffey disease (listed by Orthobullets) · seizures

**NOT associated** — **vertebral compression fractures**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**General** — ↑ cortical thickness · **↑ overall density** · loss of medullary canal · **bone-in-bone (endobones — pathognomonic, TO)**

**Signs** — **"rugger jersey" / sandwich spine** (dense endplate bands) · **Erlenmeyer flask** distal femur + proximal humerus · block femoral metaphysis · coxa vara · thick skull base, poor sinuses

**Not osteopetrosis** — **dripping candle wax** (melorheostosis) · Paget pelvis · bone infarct

**Labs** — AR: ↑ acid phosphatase, ± ↑ PTH, ± ↑ Ca · anemia ± thrombocytopenia · AD: normal

**Histology** — osteoclasts **lack ruffled border + clear zone** · islands of **calcified cartilage** in trabeculae · empty lacunae, plugged Haversian canals

### Erlenmeyer flask deformity – differential

**Definition** — constricted diaphysis + flared metaphysis (undertubulation)

**Causes** — **osteopetrosis** · **Gaucher**, Niemann-Pick · thalassemia, sickle cell · **Pyle (metaphyseal dysplasia)** · craniotubular dysplasias · **achondroplasia** · fibrous dysplasia · **Ollier** · MHE · rickets · RA · lead toxicity

**NOT** — **Langerhans cell histiocytosis** · Sprengel · melorheostosis · DEH (TO)

## 5 Management

**Medical**

| Form | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **AR malignant** | **Bone marrow transplant** (only durable cure, TO) · **high-dose calcitriol** |
| AD | **Interferon γ-1b** |
| Supportive (TO) | Erythropoietin (darbepoetin), vitamin D, corticosteroids |
| ✗ Wrong option: **alendronate** | **Inappropriate** — bisphosphonate further suppresses osteoclasts |

**Cranial nerve impingement** — neurosurgical decompression

### Fracture management

| Fracture | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Diaphyseal long bone, upper extremity** | **Non-op**: prolonged casting, NWB · slow healing, limited remodeling → malunion, refracture |
| **Proximal femur** | **ORIF with plate + screws** — **avoid IM devices** (can't pass reamer) · ↑ union, ↓ coxa vara |

**Technique** — **slow steady drilling, constant cooling, frequent drill-bit changes** · ↑ hardware failure

**Arthroplasty** — THA: cannulated reamers under fluoro, **short-stem uncemented** femur, small sharp acetabular reamers + screws · TKA: consider navigation

## 6 Complications

**Refracture** (brittle bone, hardware failure) · **infection** (↓ vascularity) · malunion · nonunion

| X-ray | Diffuse density, bone-in-bone, rugger jersey | **Dripping candle wax** cortical hyperostosis |

## How it's asked

**"Mechanism of intermediate osteopetrosis?"** → **inactive osteoclast carbonic anhydrase**\
✗ Wrong options: Gsα · RUNX2

**"22-yr distal radius fracture, dense pelvis, rugger jersey spine — etiology?"** → **defective osteoclast function**\
✗ Wrong options: FGFR3 · 1α-hydroxylase

**"Carbonic anhydrase mutation — which X-ray?"** → **Erlenmeyer flask femur**\
✗ Wrong options: Paget · melorheostosis · infarct

**"Cause of osteopetrosis?"** → **loss-of-function of carbonic anhydrase II**\
✗ Wrong options: Gsα · FGFR3

**"Can't acidify Howship's lacuna?"** → **Albers-Schönberg disease**

**"Why is the bone thicker?"** → **↓ acidification of Howship's lacuna**

**"Fractures, CN palsies, back pain — cause?"** → **osteoclast can't create acidic Howship lacunae**

**"10-yr, seizures, anemia, dense bones — defect?"** → **carbonic anhydrase II**\
✗ Wrong options: PMP22 · COMP

**"5-yr, fractures since birth, rugger jersey — inheritance?"** → **autosomal recessive**

**"10-yr, rib fracture, HSM, deafness, caries — NOT appropriate?"** → **alendronate** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: interferon γ · calcitriol · darbepoetin

**"NOT a feature of osteopetrosis?"** → **dripping candle wax** (TO)

**"18-yr, hearing + vision loss, couldn't pass reamer — spine X-ray?"** → **rugger jersey**

**"3-yr abnormal X-rays — risk of all EXCEPT?"** → **vertebral compression fractures**

**"Erlenmeyer flask in all EXCEPT?"** → **Langerhans cell histiocytosis** (TO)

**"Erlenmeyer flask of femoral metaphysis?"** → **osteopetrosis** (TO)
