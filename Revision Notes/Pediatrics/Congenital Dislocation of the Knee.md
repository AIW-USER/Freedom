# Congenital Dislocation of the Knee

*Pediatrics · 26 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Spectrum** — **positional contracture → rigid dislocation** · tibia anterior on femur

**Structural** — **quadriceps contracture** (main) · anterior subluxation of hamstrings · **absent suprapatellar pouch** · tight collaterals · ACL abnormalities

**Associated syndromes** — **myelomeningocele** · **arthrogryposis** · **Larsen** (clubfeet + hip, knee, elbow dislocations)

**Genu recurvatum (TO)** — hyperextension **> 5°** · causes: ligament laxity · **congenital knee dislocation** · quadriceps weakness · physeal injury · associations: arthrogryposis, **absent cruciates**, **hypoplastic patella** · Rx serial manipulation + casting

**Associated deformities** — **DDH** — **50%** (Orthobullets) · ipsilateral hip dislocation **70–100%** (OB) · **clubfoot** · metatarsus adductus

## 3 Presentation

**Hyperextended knee at birth** · anterior tibial translation on X-ray

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Tarek classification

| Grade | Radiology | Passive flexion | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | Simple recurvatum | **>90°** | Manipulation + casting |
| **II** | Subluxation / dislocation | **30–90°** | Casting if <1 mo; **PQR** if seen later |
| **III** | Dislocation | **<30°** | **Surgery** (open V-Y quadricepsplasty) |

![Tarek grades](../../MCQ/attachments/q4749-illa-illus-a-tarek.jpg)

## 5 Management

```
Congenital knee dislocation
├─ Newborn (Tarek I, early II) → weekly long-leg manipulation + flexion casting
│      (knee + hip dislocated → treat KNEE first, then Pavlik)
└─ Failed to gain 30° flexion by 3 mo / grade III / recurrent → surgical release
       ├─ Grade II (later) → percutaneous quadriceps recession
       └─ Older, failed casting, grade III → open V-Y quadricepsplasty / open reduction
```

### Serial casting vs quadricepsplasty/open reduction

**Knee + ipsilateral hip dislocated (2-day-old)** → **manipulate + cast knee first** — **can't fit Pavlik on a hyperextended knee**\
✗ Wrong options: Pavlik first · spica · delay · quad lengthening

**Casting failure** — **90° not attained in 4 weeks** (OB) · **30° not gained after 3 months** (Orthobullets) → surgery

**6-month-old, failed 3 mo casting, flexion 15°, hyperextension 25° (recurrent grade III)** → **open V-Y quadricepsplasty + above-knee cast**\
✗ Wrong options: cast in extension · more flexion casts · ACL division alone · percutaneous recession (for grade II at presentation)

**7-month-old, can't flex >30° after casting, hips normal** → **open reduction** — ideally **~6 months, before age 1**\
✗ Wrong options: observe · more casting · closed reduction · wait for walking

### Operative criteria

| Indication | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **<30° flexion after 3 months casting** | Soft-tissue release |
| Tarek II after 1 month, III, recurrent | Release |
| Goal | **90° flexion** |
| Options | **Percutaneous quadriceps recession (PQR)** · **V-Y quadricepsplasty / Z-lengthening** · anterior capsule release · posterior transposition of hamstrings · collateral mobilisation |
| Post-op | Cast **45–60° flexion × 3–4 weeks** |

**ACL division + quad release** — only fair results

## How it's asked

**"2-day-old hyperextended knee + subluxated ipsilateral hip — next?"** → **manipulation and casting of the knee**

**"6-month-old, failed casting, recurrent grade III?"** → **open V-Y quadricepsplasty + above-knee cast**

**"7-month-old can't flex past 30° after casting?"** → **open reduction**

**"Indication for surgery?"** → **failure to gain 30° flexion after 3 months casting**
