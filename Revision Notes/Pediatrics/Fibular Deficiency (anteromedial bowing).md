# Fibular Deficiency (anteromedial bowing)

*Pediatrics · 47 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — shortening or absence of the fibula (formerly **fibular hemimelia**; postaxial hypoplasia) · involves the **whole limb**

**Most common congenital long-bone deficiency** · 7.4–20 per million (TO) · bilateral not uncommon

**Genetics** — no known inheritance (mostly sporadic) · linked to **sonic hedgehog** · AD/AR/X-linked reported (TO)

### Three types of tibial bowing

| Bowing | Association |
|---|---|
| Anterolateral | **NF1 / congenital pseudarthrosis** (✗ Wrong options: fibular deficiency (the EXCEPT)) |
| Posteromedial | Physiologic, calcaneovalgus |
| **Anteromedial** | **Fibular deficiency** (most common cause) |

### Associated anomalies

| Region | Association |
|---|---|
| Tibia | **Anteromedial bowing** + **skin dimple** over apex (half) · **fibular anlage** (fibrocartilaginous band, 60% of complete CFD) tethers tibia |
| Foot | **Equinovalgus** (TO, Orthobullets) · talipes equinovarus less often · **absent lateral rays** · **tarsal coalition (50%)** · **ball-and-socket ankle** → instability |
| Knee | **ACL/PCL deficiency** · **lateral femoral condyle hypoplasia** → **genu valgum** · hypoplastic tibial spines, shallow notch · patella alta/hypoplasia |
| Femur / hip | **Femoral shortening (50%)** · **PFFD**, **coxa vara** · **DDH** |
| LLD | Significant (femur ± tibia) |

**NOT associated** — **quadriceps hypotonia** · **congenital vertical talus** · SCFE · scoliosis · polydactyly · MCL insufficiency · cleft hand / insufficient extensor mechanism (→ **tibial** hemimelia)

**ACL dysplasia correlates with** — **lateral femoral condyle hypoplasia** · lateral tibial spine dysplasia · absent fibula · absent lateral rays\
✗ Wrong options: medial condyle · anterolateral bowing · PCL · ↓ slope

**Foot conflict** — "pes planovalgus not associated; equinovarus is" (one OB explanation) vs **equinovalgus = classic** (Orthobullets, TO) — read the options

## 3 Presentation

**Classic** — **short limb** · **skin dimple over mid-anterior tibia** · **equinovalgus foot** · missing lateral toes · genu valgum

**Ankle sprains + ACL laxity + coalition** → look for **LLD** (fibular deficiency)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — absent/short fibula · anteromedial bow · hypoplastic tibial spines · shallow notch · **ball-and-socket ankle** (from tarsal coalition)

### Achterman & Kalamchi classification

| Type | Fibula | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **IA** | Present but short — proximal epiphysis below tibial physis, distal above talus | Heel lift (LLD >2 cm) |
| **IB** | **Partial absence (30–50%)**, distal part can't support ankle | Contralateral epiphysiodesis |
| **II** | **Complete absence** | Multiple Ilizarov lengthenings **or Syme amputation** |

**Birch classification** — by LLD + foot function · **directs treatment**

![Achterman-Kalamchi types](../../MCQ/attachments/q218919-illa-achterman-kalamchi-classification.jpg)

## 5 Management

**Treatment based on foot/ankle function + LLD** — **not** on amount of fibula

### LLD natural history

**Proportional growth inhibition** — LLD **increases slowly, limb stays in proportion** (same % at maturity) → **Paley multiplier** predicts final LLD early\
✗ Wrong options: stays 2 cm · equalises · 2 cm/yr · complete failure

### Operative criteria

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Mild LLD, functional foot | Shoe lift / orthosis |
| LLD **<5 cm or <10%**, stable plantigrade foot | **Contralateral epiphysiodesis** |
| Plantigrade stable foot, LLD **<10%** | Lengthening (resect **fibular anlage**) |
| **Moderate LLD 10–30%** | **Lengthening + contralateral epiphysiodesis** |
| **LLD >30%** / **>5 cm at birth** / **nonfunctional unstable foot** (≥3 absent rays) / can't cope with lengthenings | **Syme amputation (~1 yr)** + prosthesis |

**Syme > Boyd** — Boyd bulbous, only ~1 cm longer · satisfaction **88% amputation vs 55% lengthening**

```
Fibular deficiency
├─ Functional plantigrade foot
│      ├─ LLD <10% / <5 cm → lift or contralateral epiphysiodesis / lengthening
│      └─ LLD 10–30% → lengthening + contralateral epiphysiodesis
└─ Nonfunctional foot or LLD >30% → Syme amputation ~1 yr
```

### Fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva – palovarotene

**FOP** — **ACVR1 (R206H)** activating BMP type I receptor mutation → progressive heterotopic ossification

**Palovarotene** — oral selective **RAR-γ agonist** → inhibits BMP/SMAD 1/5/8 → ↓ HO (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: FSH dystrophy · fibromuscular dysplasia · OI

## How it's asked

**"Congenital fibular deficiency associated with all EXCEPT?"** → **anterolateral bowing** (TO)

**"Fibular hemimelia — EXCEPT?"** → **quadriceps hypotonia** (✓ absent lateral rays, ACL deficiency, ball-and-socket ankle, coxa vara)

**"Associated EXCEPT?"** → **congenital vertical talus**

**"Image associated with fibular hemimelia?"** → **ball-and-socket ankle**

**"7-yr congenital limb difference — commonly associated with?"** → **absent lateral rays**

**"Concurrent conditions?"** → **tarsal coalition + ball-and-socket ankle**

**"ACL dysplasia correlates with?"** → **lateral femoral condyle hypoplasia**

**"Recurrent sprains, TC coalition, ACL laxity — also present?"** → **leg length discrepancy**

**"12-month-old, 2 cm tibial shortening — natural history?"** → **increases slowly, staying proportional**

**"Palovarotene treats?"** → **fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva** (TO)
