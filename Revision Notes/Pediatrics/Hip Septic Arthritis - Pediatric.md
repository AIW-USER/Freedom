# Hip Septic Arthritis - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 52 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — 4–5 / 100,000 children/yr · **50% <2 yr** · **M:F 2:1** · hip and knee each **35%** of septic joints

**Risk factors** — **prematurity** · **C-section** · NICU · umbilical/venous catheters, heel puncture (transient bacteremia)

### Mechanism & pathophysiology

**Routes** — haematogenous (URI precedes ~80%) · direct inoculation · **contiguous metaphyseal osteomyelitis** — **intra-articular metaphysis: hip, shoulder, elbow, ankle (NOT knee)** · neonates: **transphyseal vessels**

**Cartilage destruction** — **matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs)** from bacteria, synovium, PMNs, cartilage → damage within **8 hours** (6 h in animal studies) · TLR → NF-κB → MMP production\
✗ Wrong options: MMPs anabolic · TLRs inhibit · activated by chelators (chelators inactivate; stromelysin is an MMP)

**↑ Intra-articular pressure** → femoral head **osteonecrosis**

### Causative organisms by age (S aureus, GBS, Kingella, gonococcus)

| Age / setting | Organism | Gram stain |
|---|---|---|
| **Neonate, community** (vaginal birth) | **Group B streptococcus** | GP cocci in chains |
| Neonate, nosocomial | **S. aureus** | — |
| **<4 yr, subacute, mild labs, negative cultures** | **Kingella kingae** — grows in **blood-culture bottles** (BACTEC); **PCR (rtxA)** · resistant to **vancomycin + clindamycin** | **GN coccobacillus** (facultative anaerobe) |
| **>2 yr / overall** | **S. aureus** — **most common** (catalase + coagulase +) · also causes **impetigo** | **GP cocci in clusters** |
| **Adolescent, sexually active** | **N. gonorrhoeae** — **migratory polyarthralgia**, maculopapular rash, tenosynovitis · F:M 3:1 · cultures negative ~50% | **GN intracellular diplococci** |
| Post-varicella | **Group A β-haemolytic strep** | — |
| Unvaccinated | H. influenzae (↓ with vaccine) — LP if meningitis signs | GN coccobacillus |
| **MRSA osteomyelitis + DVT** | **Panton-Valentine leukocidin** · CRP very high | — |

**Kingella** — "most common in children of all ages" (Orthobullets) · **S. aureus** most common overall (OB) · no organism in **up to 55%**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **acute** pain (faster than osteomyelitis) · fever, **toxic** · limp / **refusal to bear weight** · antibiotics may mask

**Exam** — hip held in **flexion, abduction, external rotation** (max capsular volume) · pain with passive motion / log-roll · pseudoparalysis · check adjacent joints + spine

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnostic criteria — Kocher

| Original Kocher criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **Fever** | **>38.5 °C (101.3 °F)** |
| **Non-weight-bearing** | ✓ |
| **ESR** | **>40 mm/h** |
| **Serum WBC** | **>12,000/mm³** |
| Modified (Caird) — **CRP** | **>2.0 mg/dL (20 mg/L)** |

| Criteria met | Probability of septic hip |
|---|---|
| 0 | **<0.2%** |
| 1 | **3%** |
| **2** | **40%** |
| **3** | **93%** |
| 4 | **99.6%** |

**Most predictive order** — **fever > CRP > ESR > refusal to bear weight > WBC** (CRP 2nd-best after fever)

**CRP >2 mg/dL + refusal to bear weight** → **74%** · **CRP <2 mg/dL (<20 mg/L)** → favours **transient synovitis** · both absent <1%

**Kocher criteria apply to the HIP only** — **not the knee** → knee: **aspirate**

**Labs** — WBC ↑ in only 30–60% (neonates may be leukopenic) · ESR may be normal early · **CRP rises in 6–8 h** · **blood cultures** if febrile (often positive when joint culture negative)

### Synovial fluid

| Diagnosis | Synovial WBC | PMN |
|---|---|---|
| Traumatic effusion | <5,000 | — |
| Transient (toxic) synovitis | 5,000–15,000 | <25% |
| Rheumatic fever | 10,000–15,000 | ~50% |
| JRA | 15,000–80,000 | ~75% (gradual onset, keeps walking) |
| **Septic arthritis** | **>50,000** | **>75%** |

**PMN % more sensitive than total WBC** (85% PMN → 88% sensitivity) · glucose ≥50 mg/dL below serum · Gram stain + culture (± synovial tissue)

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + frog)** — often normal early · **joint-space widening** (infant: **lateral displacement of proximal femur**) · subluxation/dislocation · bone lesions

**Ultrasound** — **effusion** (can't tell septic vs sterile) · guides aspiration · **neonate: scan both hips** if any septic joint

**MRI** — effusion + adjacent osteomyelitis · don't let it delay surgery

## 5 Management

### Workup & next step (US, aspiration, arthrotomy)

```
Irritable hip in a child
├─ Non-toxic, equivocal → ultrasound for effusion
├─ Effusion / high suspicion (≥2–3 Kocher, CRP ↑) → aspiration (before antibiotics)
│      └─ WBC >50,000, PMN >75%, +Gram → urgent I&D + IV antibiotics
└─ 3–4 criteria + effusion on US → emergent arthrotomy
```

**6-week-old (preterm, C-section), afebrile, widened joint space, painful motion** → **aspiration**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI (delays) · CT · observe · Pavlik

**Non-toxic 3-yr, normal X-ray, fever, ESR 50, CRP 5** → **ultrasound** next\
✗ Wrong options: NSAID trial · CT · IV antibiotics first · discharge

**Fever + refusal + ↑ CRP/ESR** → **hip aspiration**\
✗ Wrong options: IV abx · oral abx · ibuprofen · MRI · Lyme Western blot · rheumatology

**3-yr, 3/4 criteria + US effusion** → **emergent arthrotomy + I&D**\
✗ Wrong options: observe · repeat US · MRI · antibiotics before cultures in a non-septic child

**Lyme-endemic hiker, fever 38.2, WBC 14, CRP 5.5** → **aspirate** (septic more likely than Lyme)

### Operative criteria

**Urgent I&D = standard of care** — **surgical emergency** (removes chondrolytic enzymes, ↓ pressure / epiphyseal ischaemia) · err toward drainage

**Antibiotics alone** — **adolescent gonococcal** arthritis (high-dose penicillin ± aspiration)

**Technique** — anterolateral or **anterior (Smith-Petersen)** arthrotomy · remove pus, irrigate · ± **1 × 1 cm capsular window** · drain · other joints open/arthroscopic

**Antibiotics** — aspirate first, then empiric IV · **2–7 d culture-specific IV → 2–3 wk oral** · stop when CRP/ESR + clinical picture normal

| Age | Empiric |
|---|---|
| <3 mo | 1st-gen cephalosporin (GBS, S. aureus, gram-neg) |
| 3 mo–5 yr | 2nd/3rd-gen cephalosporin (Kingella, S. aureus, pneumococcus, GAS, H. flu) |
| 5–12 yr | 1st-gen cephalosporin |
| 12–18 yr | 2nd/3rd-gen cephalosporin (gonococcus) |

## 6 Complications

### Sequelae, prognosis & cartilage destruction

**Poor prognosis** — **age <6 months** · **associated osteomyelitis** · **delay >4 days** (strongest) · **hip** (vs knee)

**Sequelae** — **complete femoral head/neck destruction** (neglected neonatal sepsis) · **osteonecrosis**, articular destruction · physeal arrest → **LLD**, angular deformity · chronic osteomyelitis · persistent limp · contracture · follow **1–2 yr**

**NOT a typical sequel** — **chronic posterior instability/dislocation** (esp. after anterior approach)

**Salvage** — varus/valgus proximal femoral osteotomy · trochanteric osteotomy · modified Albee arthroplasty

## X vs Y

| | **Septic hip** | **Transient synovitis** |
|---|---|---|
| Fever | **>38.5 °C** | Low / none |
| Weight bearing | Refuses | Often limps |
| ESR / WBC | **>40 / >12,000** | Normal |
| CRP | **>2 mg/dL** | **<2 mg/dL** |
| Synovial WBC | **>50,000, >75% PMN** | 5,000–15,000 |
| Treatment | **Urgent I&D** | NSAIDs, observe |

## How it's asked

**"ESR >40, fever, refusal + which other Kocher finding?"** → **serum WBC >12,000**

**"3-yr, febrile, refuses to walk, WBC 8,000, ESR 20, CRP 1.9?"** → **arthrocentesis best confirms** (2/4 = 40%)\
✗ Wrong options: CRP is a Kocher criterion

**"8-month-old FABER hip — 2nd best predictor after fever?"** → **elevated CRP**

**"Fever + refusal, WBC 11, ESR 18 — probability?"** → **26–50%** (2/4 = 40%)

**"Afebrile 37.8, WBC 13, ESR 19, refuses — criteria / risk / organism?"** → **2/4, 40%, S. aureus**

**"Favours transient synovitis?"** → **CRP 15 mg/L** (<20)\
✗ Wrong options: ESR 43 · 38.7 °C · WBC 12,500 · leptin (SCFE)

**"Lab supporting septic hip?"** → **CRP 22 mg/L**\
✗ Wrong options: WBC 10,000 · ESR 38 · 38.2 °C · synovial 40,000

**"6-yr knee effusion, 1/4 Kocher?"** → **aspirate the knee — Kocher not valid for knees**

**"6-week-old preterm, widened joint space?"** → **aspiration**

**"18-month-old, 60,000 WBC, 95% PMN?"** → **septic arthritis**

**"3-yr, WBC 15, ESR 120, CRP 5, effusion?"** → **emergent arthrotomy + I&D**

**"Lyme-area hiker, fever, ↑ WBC/CRP, effusion?"** → **hip aspiration**

**"3-yr, 39 °C, WBC 19,000, ESR 45, effusion?"** → **hip aspiration**

**"5-yr, fever, ↓ hip ROM, normal WBC, ↑ CRP/ESR?"** → **aspiration of right hip**

**"Non-toxic 3-yr, fever, ESR 50, CRP 5, normal X-ray?"** → **ultrasound left hip**

**"Gram stain of main organism in pediatric osteomyelitis/septic arthritis?"** → **gram-positive cocci (clusters)**

**"8-day-old after vaginal birth?"** → **group B streptococcus**

**"No growth on blood agar, grew in blood-culture medium after 1 wk?"** → **Kingella kingae**

**"Osteomyelitis + DVT, positive blood culture?"** → **MRSA**

**"3-yr septic hip — most common organism's Gram stain?"** → **gram-positive cocci in clusters**

**"6-yr septic hip organism?"** → **catalase + coagulase positive GP cocci in clusters**

**"17-yr with macular rash?"** → **gram-negative diplococci**

**"19-month-old, mild labs, cultures negative at 14 days?"** → **gram-negative facultative anaerobic coccobacillus (Kingella)**

**"18-month-old, mild labs, effusion — micrograph?"** → **Kingella kingae**

**"16-yr gonococcal hip — true?"** → **usually polyarthralgia of multiple joints**

**"2-yr knee, PCR positive, culture negative?"** → **gram-negative coccobacilli (Kingella)**

**"Most common organism is also associated with?"** → **impetigo**

**"7-yr septic hip micrograph?"** → **staphylococcus (clusters)**

**"3-yr limp, prior neonatal sepsis, destroyed femoral head?"** → **residual untreated septic hip**

**"Strongest poor-prognosis predictor?"** → **delay >4 days**

**"2-yr limp, neonatal knee infection, absent femoral head?"** → **untreated neonatal hip infection**

**"MMPs — true?"** → **mediate cartilage destruction in septic arthritis**

**"LEAST likely complication of delayed treatment?"** → **chronic posterior hip instability**
