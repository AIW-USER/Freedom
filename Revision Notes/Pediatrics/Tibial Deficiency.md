# Tibial Deficiency

*Pediatrics · 105 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — congenital **longitudinal deficiency of the tibia** (tibial hemimelia), partial → complete absence

### Genes & lab markers

**Inheritance** — **autosomal dominant** → genetic counseling

**Associated MSK anomalies (75%)** — **ectrodactyly** · **preaxial polydactyly** · **ulnar aplasia**

## 3 Presentation

**Deformity** — short limb · **anterolateral tibial bowing** · prominent fibular head

**Knee** — usually flexed, **flexion contracture** · check **active knee extension** (quadriceps) · stability in all planes → **treatment based mainly on knee stability**

**Foot** — **rigid equinovarus + supination** · sole faces perineum

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — AP + lateral tib/fib → tibial deficiency · early films: small, poorly ossified **distal femoral epiphysis**

### Jones classification

| Type | Findings |
|---|---|
| **1a** | **No proximal tibia** on X-ray · **extensor mechanism absent** · hypoplastic distal femoral epiphysis |
| **1b** | Proximal tibia ossifies later, extensor mechanism works · distal femoral epiphysis normal |
| **2** | Proximal tibia present at birth, short tibia |
| **3** | Diaphysis + distal tibia present, **proximal tibia absent** |
| **4** | Short tibia, fibula migrated proximally, **distal tib-fib diastasis** |

**Alternative** — Kalamchi and Dawe (types 1–3)

![Jones classification](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4058-jones-classification.jpg)

## 5 Management

### Operative criteria

| Finding | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **Complete tibial absence** · **no active knee extension** (most cases) | **Knee disarticulation + prosthesis** |
| Proximal tibia present, **intact extensor mechanism**, minimal flexion contracture | **Tibiofibular synostosis + modified Syme** |
| **Ankle diastasis** | **Syme / Boyd amputation** |
| Brown (fibula centralized under femur) | **No longer recommended** — high failure |

### Syme amputation prognosis

**Good below-knee result** — **full functional quadriceps** + **no knee flexion contracture** · otherwise knee disarticulation\
✗ Wrong options: 35° flexion contracture · absent synostosis · bilateral · family history

## How it's asked

**"13-mo tibial hemimelia — best predictor of good Syme outcome?"** → **full functional quadriceps strength**\
✗ Wrong options: 35° flexion contracture · absent tibiofibular synostosis · bilateral
