# Cleft Foot (Lobster Foot)

*Pediatrics · TO notes*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **partial adactyly with central cleft** of the foot · "lobster foot" · split foot (TO)

**Key features** — **1st and 5th rays usually present** · variable cleft depth (TO)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Blodgett and Borisch classification (TO)

| Type | Metatarsals |
|---|---|
| **1** | **Five** metatarsals, normal |
| **2** | Five **hypoplastic** metatarsals |
| **3** | **Four** metatarsals |
| **4** | **Three** metatarsals |
| **5** | **Two** metatarsals |
| **6** | **Single** metatarsal |

## 5 Management

**Principle** — **convert the cleft into a syndactyly** for better cosmesis and shoe wear (TO)

**Wood and Shook technique (TO)** — **alternating rectangular skin flaps** · excision of fibrous tissue · **closing-wedge osteotomies** · **crossed K-wire** fixation

## How it's asked

**"Principle of cleft foot surgery?"** → **convert cleft into syndactyly** (TO)

**"Cleft foot — rays usually preserved?"** → **1st and 5th** (TO)
