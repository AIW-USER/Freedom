# Femoral Anteversion

*Pediatrics · 45 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & measurement

**Definition** — transverse-plane angle between **femoral neck axis** and the **posterior femoral condylar plane** (TO) · femoral anteversion = functional anteversion + condylar twist\
✗ Wrong options: lesser trochanter · tibial plateau · distal condylar plane

**Pathology** — ↑ neck anteversion → **compensatory internal rotation of femur** → **in-toeing** · **packaging** disorder

**Demographics** — **3–6 yr** · **girls 2:1** · hereditary · usually **bilateral** (be wary of asymmetry)

**Associated packaging** — **DDH** · **metatarsus adductus** · **torticollis** · also ↑ in clubfoot, Blount, flatfoot, CP

### Normal anteversion

| Age | Anteversion |
|---|---|
| Birth / infancy | **30–40°** (≈39–40°) |
| 2nd year | ~31° |
| To age 10 | ↓ 1–2°/yr → ~24° |
| **Adult** | **~15–16°** |
| After age 8 | Minimal change |

### Three causes of in-toeing

| Cause | Age | Resolves by |
|---|---|---|
| **Metatarsus adductus** | **Infant** | ~4 yr |
| **Internal tibial torsion** | **Toddler** | 6–7 yr |
| **Femoral anteversion** | **Early childhood (3–6)** | **~10 yr** (8–13) |

**Order of resolution** — **MA → ITT → femoral anteversion**

## 3 Presentation

**History** — in-toeing gait, tripping, awkward running · **sits in "W" position** · knee pain if with tibial torsion · more symptomatic in neuromuscular / brace-dependent walkers

### Examination findings & diagnosis

| Test (prone) | Femoral anteversion | Normal |
|---|---|---|
| **Hip IR** | **>70°** | 20–60° |
| **Hip ER** | **<20°** | 30–60° |
| **Thigh–foot angle** | **Normal** (−5° to +20°) | Infant 5° internal · age 8: 10° external |
| Heel bisector | Normal (2nd–3rd web) | MA: 3rd–4th web |
| Patella in gait | **Internally rotated** | — |
| **Trochanteric prominence test** | Hip IR at max GT prominence ≈ anteversion | — |

**Internal tibial torsion** — **TFA < −10°** (e.g. −20° on right) with normal hip rotation → ITT on that side

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**None** usually · **CT or MRI** to measure actual anteversion (pre-op)

## 5 Management

### Natural history & observation

**Observation + reassurance** — remodels in ~99% by 8–10 yr · **bracing, shoe inserts, PT, sitting restrictions do NOT change natural history**\
✗ Wrong options: night ER brace · inserts · osteotomy in a young child

### Operative criteria

| Source | Indication for derotation femoral osteotomy |
|---|---|
| Orthobullets | **ER <10°** in child **>10 yr** (rare) |
| **Staheli (TO)** | **>8 yr** · significant cosmetic + functional disability · **anteversion >50°** · **IR >85° + ER <10°** · family aware of risks |

**NOT a Staheli indication** — **medial (internal) hip rotation >35°** (TO)

**Osteotomy** — **intertrochanteric** (blade plate / screw-side plate) · subtrochanteric or supracondylar alternatives · correction ≈ **(IR − ER) / 2**

**Prognosis** — persistent anteversion **not linked to hip/knee OA** (Orthobullets) · TO lists hip OA, patellar malalignment, chondromalacia as associations · **miserable (torsional) malalignment** = anteversion + external tibial torsion

## X vs Y

| | **Femoral anteversion** | **Internal tibial torsion** | **Metatarsus adductus** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age | 3–6 yr | Toddler | Infant |
| Key exam | **Hip IR >70°, ER <20°** | **TFA internal (>10°)** | **Convex lateral border**, heel bisector lateral |
| Patella | **Points in** | Forward | Forward |
| Resolves | ~10 yr | 6–7 yr | ~4 yr |

## How it's asked

**"6-yr in-toeing — finding favouring femoral anteversion?"** → **hip IR 80°, ER 15°**\
✗ Wrong options: TFA internal (ITT) · convex lateral border (MA)

**"5-yr, in-toeing with knees in line with feet — finding?"** → **increased hip internal rotation**

**"6-yr, hips IR 60/ER 40, TFA −20° right?"** → **internal tibial torsion, right**

**"Staheli — NOT an indication?"** → **medial hip rotation greater than 35 degrees** (TO)

**"3-yr, IR 85°/ER 15°, neutral TFA?"** → **observation**\
✗ Wrong options: osteotomy · inserts · brace

**"7-yr, IR >70°, ER <20°?"** → **observation**

**"Order of resolution of in-toeing causes?"** → **metatarsus adductus → internal tibial torsion → femoral anteversion**

**"Anteversion measured relative to?"** → **posterior femoral condylar plane** (TO)
