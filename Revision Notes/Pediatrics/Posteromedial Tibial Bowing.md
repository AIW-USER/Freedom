# Posteromedial Tibial Bowing

*Pediatrics · 86 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Associated calcaneovalgus foot & aetiology

**Cause** — **intrauterine positioning** · no genetic / familial pattern\
✗ Wrong options: AR · HOXD10 (vertical talus) · Shh (anteromedial bowing/fibular hemimelia) · fracture nonunion (anterolateral)

**Site** — **middle + distal third** of tibia (distal more common) · usually unilateral · posterior bow more severe than medial · angulation 25–70° (TO)

**Associated** — **calcaneovalgus foot** (✓ most common association; together or separately) · ankle dorsiflexion contracture · distal tibial epiphyseal valgus wedging, fibular hypoplasia · shortening present at birth

**Fracture / pseudarthrosis** — **rare** (unlike anterolateral bowing)

| Tibial bowing | Association |
|---|---|
| **Posteromedial** | Packaging · **calcaneovalgus** · **LLD** |
| Anteromedial | **Fibular hemimelia** (Shh) |
| Anterolateral | **NF1, congenital pseudarthrosis** |

## 3 Presentation

**At birth** — short, bowed leg · dorsum of foot may rest on anterior shin

**Exam (TO)** — **skin dimple over apex** · **underdeveloped calf** · **↓ ankle plantarflexion** · apex **posterior on lateral, medial on AP** · differentiate from positional calcaneovalgus

**Apex** — posteromedial bowing apex in **distal tibia** · calcaneovalgus apex at **ankle** (Orthobullets) / heel (TO)

**Calcaneovalgus vs vertical talus** — **plantar-flexion lateral foot X-ray**: in CVT the talar axis stays plantar to (not collinear with) the 1st MT

**TO case** — neonate, convex posteromedial bow, calf atrophy, lateral dimple, thick anterolateral cortices, normal canal · options: constriction band / CPT / posteromedial bowing / fibular hemimelia · **answer not provided by the source**

## 5 Management

### Natural history & residual LLD

**Bowing** — **spontaneously corrects over 5–7 yr** (~80% by age 5) · **observe** · calcaneovalgus → observation + parental stretching\
✗ Wrong options: osteotomy · bracing / clamshell KAFO · serial casting (no effect; casting only for severe unresolving contracture) · genetic testing

**LLD = main problem** — proportional, ↑ with growth · ∝ amount of bow

**Remodelling (TO)** — **rapid in first year** · mainly by **physeal reorientation** · growth inhibition persists → predictable LLD · average **3.5 cm** (TO) · **no corrective osteotomy for the bow** · Rx LLD: contralateral epiphysiodesis or ex-fix lengthening

| Source | Residual LLD |
|---|---|
| Orthobullets | **Average 3–4 cm** · residual 2–5 cm |
| OB | 3–8 cm · ~4 cm · 2–5 cm |
| TO | 3–7 cm |
| Di Gennaro (OB) | 4.3 cm |

**Surgery for LLD** — **>50%** of patients (Wright 53% lengthening)

| LLD (TO) | Residual angle | Ankle | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **<2 cm** | Resolved | Normal | None |
| **2–4 cm** | Resolved | Normal | **Contralateral tibial epiphysiodesis** |
| **>4 cm** | Resolved | Normal | **Tibial lengthening** |
| >2 cm | **Residual angulation** | — | Metaphyseal lengthening + diaphyseal osteotomy |
| Any | Any | **Ankle valgus** | Supramalleolar osteotomy / medial hemiepiphysiodesis |

**Most common complication needing surgery** → **LLD** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: calcaneovalgus · bowing · pseudarthrosis fracture

**Major deformity needing correction** → **LLD** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: posterior bow · medial bow

## How it's asked

**"Newborn posteromedial bowing, normal neuro + hips?"** → **observation** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: casting · early osteotomy · bracing

**"Major deformity needing correction?"** → **LLD** (TO)

**"Most common complication requiring surgery?"** → **limb length discrepancy** (TO)

**"Infant posteromedial bow — associated condition?"** → **residual LLD**\
✗ Wrong options: pseudarthrosis · knee instability · coalition

**"2-yr posteromedial bowing — complication?"** → **LLD**\
✗ Wrong options: fibular hemimelia · NF

**"Healthy newborn — next step?"** → **observe for resolution + possible LLD**\
✗ Wrong options: AFO · NF gene counselling

**"Newborn isolated posteromedial bow?"** → **observe; bow resolves, LLD remains**\
✗ Wrong options: Syme · casting

**"3-yr — counsel family about?"** → **limb-length discrepancy**\
✗ Wrong options: NF · renal

**"2-month-old posteromedial bow?"** → **observe, revisit in 4 months**\
✗ Wrong options: clamshell · casts

**"2-yr, prognosis?"** → **resolves by ~5, will likely need LLD surgery**

**"Most common associated deformity?"** → **calcaneovalgus foot** (TO)

**"3-month-old tibial deformity — foot deformity?"** → **calcaneovalgus**\
✗ Wrong options: equinovarus · MA

**"Posteromedial bow + calcaneovalgus — etiology?"** → **intrauterine positioning**

**"2-week-old bowed leg — other finding?"** → **calcaneovalgus foot**\
✗ Wrong options: café-au-lait · limb lengthening
