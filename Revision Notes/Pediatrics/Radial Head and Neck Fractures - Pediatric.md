# Radial Head and Neck Fractures - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 93 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Epidemiology** — **5–10% of pediatric elbow fractures** · 1% of all · 3rd most common elbow fracture (OB) · median age **9–10** · M = F

**Site** — **90% physis or metaphysis (neck)** · ~50% metaphyseal, 50% SH I/II · articular head fracture only 4–7% with open physes (52–96% after closure)

**Mechanism** — FOOSH with **extension + valgus** · elbow dislocation

**Associated (30–50%)** — **elbow dislocation** · **medial epicondyle** · olecranon · condyles · **forearm compartment syndrome**

**Anatomy** — radial head ossifies **3–5 yr** (may be bipartite) · fuses 16–18 · **annular ligament covers epiphysis + physis** → stress riser at metaphysis → **neck fractures** · thick epiphyseal cartilage resists articular extension · head blood supply mainly **metaphyseal**

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — lateral swelling · pain with **pronation/supination** · may refer to wrist · **watch for forearm compartment syndrome** (rising forearm pain overnight)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP + lateral · **radiocapitellar (Greenspan) view**: elbow 90°, thumb up, beam 45° proximal

![Greenspan (radiocapitellar) view technique](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4011-shows-the-technique-to-obtain-a-greenspan-view.jpg)

**Lines** — radiocapitellar line (unreliable in young, esp. AP) · **lateral humeral line** (tangent to lateral condyle, lies lateral to radial neck) — more reliable in young children

**Pitfalls** — nondisplaced hard to see · **radial head flipped 180°** · posterior fat pad always pathologic · **neck partly extra-articular → effusion / fat pad may be absent**

**CT** — comminuted head in older child · **MRI** — rare (unossified elbow, anterior head compression with ligament injury)

### Classification

| O'Brien | Angulation |
|---|---|
| I | <30° |
| II | 30–60° |
| III | >60° |

| Judet | Angulation |
|---|---|
| I | Undisplaced |
| II | <30° |
| III | 30–60° |
| IVa | 60–80° |
| IVb | >80° |

**Chambers** — group 1 primary radial head displacement (most common; valgus: SH I/II, SH III/IV, metaphyseal; dislocation: reduction / dislocation injury) · group 2 radial neck displacement (**Monteggia variant**) · group 3 stress (**OCD**)

## 5 Management

```
Pediatric radial neck fracture
├─ <30° and <3 mm translation → long arm cast/splint, no reduction (7–21 days, then ROM)
├─ >30° → closed reduction (Patterson, Israeli, Neher-Torch, Esmarch)
│      (stable once reduced — fixation usually not needed)
├─ Residual >30° / >3–4 mm / <45° pro-supination → percutaneous reduction (K-wire joystick, Metaizeau) ± pin
└─ Can't get <45° → open reduction (lateral approach, forearm pronated)
```

### Treatment by angulation (closed, percutaneous, open)

**Closed reduction** — 45° or 35° angulation → **attempt closed reduction** · >60° unlikely to close-reduce (OB) · some tolerate up to 60° (Radomisli) · >50° often needs fixation (Nicholson & Skaggs)\
✗ Wrong options: cast without reduction · percutaneous first · open

**Techniques** — **Patterson**: elbow extended, traction, supination, **varus** + direct radial head pressure · **Israeli (Kaufman)**: elbow 90°, **pronate** the supinated forearm with thumb on head · **Neher-Torch**: extension, supination, traction, varus; assistant pushes shaft lateral, surgeon pushes head medial · **Esmarch** wrap → spontaneous reduction

![Patterson maneuver](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4011-shown-is-an-illustration-of-the-patterson-maneuver-used-in-r.jpg)

**Percutaneous** — **K-wire joystick** (push posterolateral / lever) · **Metaizeau**: retrograde flexible nail across fracture, rotate to reduce · hardware out at ~8 wk · after failed ED reduction with >30° (e.g. 62°) → **percutaneous reduction ± pin / ESIN**\
✗ Wrong options: cast 6 wk · CT · ORIF first · plate · epiphysiodesis

**Open** — widely displaced head fracture with radial subluxation → **ORIF** · **lateral approach**, **pronate forearm to protect PIN** · fix only if unstable · no **transcapitellar pins**

**Outcome** — better if younger, less angulation, isolated neck · open reduction → more stiffness, **AVN, synostosis**

## 6 Complications

### Complications & prognosis

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **↓ ROM** | **Most common (10–31%)** · pronation > supination |
| Radial head overgrowth | 20–40% · rarely matters |
| **Osteonecrosis** | 10–20% · **up to 70% after open reduction** |
| **PIN palsy** | ~15% after surgery |
| Physeal arrest | → cubitus valgus |
| **Synostosis** | **Most serious** · open reduction, extensive dissection, delay |
| Malunion (intra-articular) | Radiocapitellar instability, pain, arthritis |
| **Forearm compartment syndrome** | Increasing pain + swelling overnight |

**Worse outcome** — **age >10** · intra-articular (50% complications, 25% revision vs 0% extra-articular)\
✗ Wrong options: BMI · dominance · sex

**TO points** — \~5% of elbow fractures, **3rd commonest** (after supracondylar, lateral condyle) · head entirely cartilaginous, metaphyseal blood supply · > 30° → eccentric head, cam impingement at radiocapitellar joint · residual **< 45° after reduction may be accepted** to avoid open surgery · other methods: Jeffery, Kaufman, Monson, Orthokids · **PIN crosses \~3 finger-breadths distal to neck**; safe zone posterolateral, **pronation ↑ safety** · open reduction via **anconeus-ECU** interval, results inferior

| Angulation (other TO lecture) | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **< 15°** | Posterior slab |
| **15–30°** | Early orthopaedic referral |
| **> 30°** | Reduction · preferred fixation **Metaizeau** elastic nail |

### PIN injury

**PIN** — branch of radial nerve wrapping radial neck · **loss of thumb IP extension (EPL)**, finger extension incl. **small finger (EDM)**, partial wrist extension · supplies posterior compartment (except anconeus) + ECRB · **pins posterolateral with arm in maximal pronation**

## How it's asked

**"10-yr, 45° physeal radial neck — first step?"** → **attempt closed reduction**

**"12-yr, >30° after ED reduction?"** → **further reduction + percutaneous ESIN/pin**\
✗ Wrong options: cast 6 wk · CT · ORIF

**"10-yr, 35° within 2 hours?"** → **closed reduction in ER**\
✗ Wrong options: CRPP · open

**"9-yr, 62° residual after sedated reduction?"** → **percutaneous reduction ± pin**

**"8-yr displaced radial head fracture + anterior subluxation after reduction attempt?"** → **ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: annular ligament reconstruction · repeat closed

**"6-yr radial neck fracture, increasing forearm pain overnight?"** → **forearm compartment syndrome**

**"Factor for poor surgical outcome?"** → **age >10 yr**

**"7-yr after percutaneous pinning, can't extend thumb IP — nerve origin?"** → **radial nerve (PIN)**\
✗ Wrong options: median · ulnar

**"14-yr open reduction, nerve palsy — deficit?"** → **small finger extension**\
✗ Wrong options: thumb IP flexion (AIN) · opposition
