# Myelodysplasia (myelomeningocele, spinal bifida)

*Pediatrics · 70 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — failure of fetal neural tube closure → anatomic anomalies + neurologic deficit · incidence **0.1–0.2%**

### Prevention - folic acid

**Risk factors** — **folate deficiency** (supplement ↓ risk **70%**) · maternal hyperthermia · maternal diabetes · **valproic acid**

**Supplement** — **folic acid**\
✗ Wrong options: vitamin D · B12 · niacin · thiamine

### Genes & lab markers

**Chromosomal** — up to **10%** of fetuses: **trisomy 13, trisomy 18**, triploidy, single-gene mutations

**Maternal AFP** — **↑ in 75%** of open spina bifida · **2nd trimester** · fetal ultrasound diagnoses in utero

### Latex allergy

**Mechanism** — **IgE-mediated type 1 hypersensitivity** → anaphylaxis · **20–70%** of patients · from repeated latex exposure · **latex-free OR** · non-spina bifida latex allergy = type 4 contact dermatitis\
✗ Wrong options: IgM · IgA · complement · T cells

### Associations

**Neurosurgical** — **Chiari II** (most common associated anomaly) · **hydrocephalus 70%** · **tethered cord**

**Other** — neurogenic bladder · pathologic fractures · scoliosis / kyphosis · hip dislocation + contractures · tibial torsion · foot deformity

### Split cord malformation & dysraphism types

| Form | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Spina bifida occulta** | Arch defect, **cord + meninges confined** |
| **Meningocele** | Sac **without** neural elements |
| **Myelomeningocele** | Sac **with neural elements** · **most common dysraphism** (TO) |
| **Rachischisis** | Neural elements **exposed**, no cover |

**Open vs closed (TO)** — open = skin defect, neural tissue exposed · closed = skin-covered, ± subcutaneous mass (lipomyelocele, lipomyelomeningocele, meningocele, myelocystocele)

| Split cord (diastematomyelia, TO) | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Type 1** | **Two dural tubes**, **rigid bony / cartilaginous septum** · more symptoms |
| **Type 2** | **One dural tube**, ± non-rigid fibrous septum, **no bony septum** |
| Associated | Scoliosis, tethered cord, **hairy tuft** |

**Exam (TO)** — gaps between spinous processes / absent bony prominences in sacral triangle → **spina bifida** · step-off → spondylolisthesis · becs de perroquet → spinal TB

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Functional prognosis - motor level

**Level** — named by **lowest functioning level** · **functional motor level = most important predictor of outcome**\
✗ Wrong options: sensory level · hip dysplasia · hydrocephalus

| Level | Ambulation | Key motion (muscle) | Foot | Orthosis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1–L2 | **Non-ambulatory** | — | Equinovarus | **HKAFO** |
| **L3** | Marginal household · **high hip dislocation risk** | **Hip flexion + adduction** (iliopsoas, adductors) | Equinovarus | **KAFO** |
| **L4** | Household + · **key level (quads work)** | **Knee extension** (quads) · ankle DF + inversion (TA) | **Cavovarus** | **AFO** |
| **L5** | **Community** | Toe DF (EHL/EDL) · **hip extension + abduction** (gluteus med/min) | **Calcaneovalgus** | AFO |
| S1 | Normal | Ankle PF (gastroc-soleus) | — | Shoes |
| S2 | Normal | Toe PF (FHL) | — | — |
| S3–4 | Normal | Bowel + bladder | — | — |

**OB descriptions** — L1: hips ER + flexed, equinovarus, HKAFO · L3: hips adducted + flexed, **knee recurvatum**, equinovarus, KAFO

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — monitor scoliosis / kyphosis, hips, fractures · standing AP ankle → **tibiotalar valgus / talar tilt**

**MRI** — **any change in neuro exam or rapid curve progression → urgent MRI for tethered cord**

## 5 Management

### Hip dislocation management & functional level

**Cause** — paralytic: unopposed flexors + adductors · most common at **L3** · can be teratologic

**Rule** — function depends on **neurologic level, NOT hip status** · open reduction → high redislocation + complications, no functional gain · reduced hips may even hurt more

| Scenario | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Thoracic / L3 and above** | **Leave dislocated** · observation + **ROM to prevent contractures** (✗ Wrong options: Pavlik · spica (promote contractures) · open reduction) |
| **L4** | Most controversial · may consider if **unilateral** (OB) · L4 household ambulator with full ROM → **no intervention** |
| Contractures | **Contracture release** (improves ROM → ambulation) |

**Hip abduction contracture** — pelvic obliquity, scoliosis → **Ober-Yount** (proximal fascia lata + distal ITB release) if limits sitting/bracing

**Hip flexion contracture** — high lumbar / thoracic → anterior release (iliopsoas, sartorius, rectus, TFL) if **>40°**

### Knee

| Problem | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Weak quads | **KAFO** |
| Flexion contracture **>20°** | **Hamstring lengthening ± posterior capsulotomy** · older / failed → **supracondylar extension osteotomy** |
| Extension contracture | **Serial casting** to 90° flexion |
| Tibial torsion | **<5 yr** observe + orthotics · **>5 yr distal tibial derotation osteotomy** |

### Foot & ankle deformity

**Incidence** — **60–90%** (low nerve roots)

**Clubfoot** — **30%**, most common · **rigid + insensate** · serial casting first (high complication rate) → **posteromedial-lateral release at 12–18 months** · rigid clubfoot, no muscles below knee → **soft-tissue releases** for plantigrade AFO foot · releases beat transfers\
✗ Wrong options: tendon transfers (nothing to transfer) · Botox · triple

**Calcaneus / dorsiflexion (L5, sacral)** — unopposed tibialis anterior · bulky heel pad · **posterior transfer of tibialis anterior** if bracing can't get neutral\
✗ Wrong options: triple arthrodesis

**Planovalgus** — distal tibial valgus (mature) → **supramalleolar osteotomy** · hindfoot valgus → **calcaneal lengthening + TAL** · also calcaneal slide, subtalar arthrodesis, Grice · **tibiotalar valgus missed pre-op → poor surgical result**\
✗ Wrong options: equinus · calcaneovalgus · forefoot abduction

**Arthrodesis** — avoid (skin sores, Charcot) · triple only for severe deformity with **sensate** feet

### Spinal deformity & kyphectomy

**Scoliosis** — neurogenic or congenital · **100% with thoracic level** · rapid progression → **tethered cord** · **bracing ineffective** → **ASF + PSF with pelvic fixation** (anterior needed — dysplastic posterior elements) · high **pseudarthrosis** · **infection 15–25%** (poor soft tissue)

**Congenital kyphosis** — **10–15%** · progressive · **gibbus → recurrent skin breakdown** sitting · **kyphectomy + fusion + posterior instrumentation** · **check shunt function first** (shunt failure intra-op can kill)

**Both curves progressive** → **ASF + PSF with pelvic fixation + kyphectomy**\
✗ Wrong options: kyphectomy alone · bracing

**Multidisciplinary team** — **↓ postoperative complications**\
✗ Wrong options: ↓ deformity progression · ↓ revision

## 6 Complications

### Pathologic fracture & neuropathic joints

**Fractures** — **disuse osteopenia** · ↑ with higher level · hip + knee, age 3–7 · **fever, warmth, erythema, swelling → mimics infection / cellulitis / osteomyelitis** → **get X-rays** · not immunocompromised · treat with short **well-padded** splint/cast · **avoid long casting** (↑ osteopenia, refracture)

**Prognosis** — untreated infants 90–100% mortality · L3 and above mostly wheelchair · L5 good community ambulation

## How it's asked

**"8-yr mid-lumbar MMC, bilateral hip dislocation — true?"** → **long-term function not associated with hip status**\
✗ Wrong options: reduction improves ambulation

**"4-yr L3, unilateral dislocation, walks with walker?"** → **observation**\
✗ Wrong options: open reduction · pelvic osteotomy

**"Newborn thoracic MMC, unstable hips?"** → **observation + ROM**\
✗ Wrong options: Pavlik · spica · open reduction

**"13-yr, knee extension but no hip extension/abduction, dislocated hip?"** → **L4; no intervention**

**"6-yr, walks, strong DF, bulky heel pad, calcaneus?"** → **L5; posterior transfer of tibialis anterior**\
✗ Wrong options: triple

**"18-yr, planovalgus, distal tibia 15° valgus?"** → **supramalleolar osteotomy**

**"18-month, rigid clubfeet, nothing below knee?"** → **soft-tissue releases**\
✗ Wrong options: transfers · Botox

**"Pre-op deformity predicting poor foot surgery result?"** → **tibiotalar valgus**

**"Thoracic MMC, progressive scoliosis + kyphosis, skin ulcers?"** → **ASF + PSF with pelvic fixation + kyphectomy**

**"Benefit of multidisciplinary team?"** → **reduced postoperative complications**

**"L1 MMC, fall, febrile warm thigh?"** → **pathologic femur fracture from disuse osteopenia**\
✗ Wrong options: cellulitis · immunocompromise
