# Local Gigantism

*Pediatrics · 61 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — overgrowth of some or all tissue elements of a digit / limb · rare · mostly **nonhereditary** · classically **one toe, often the 2nd**, with **normal nails**

| Type | Causes |
|---|---|
| **Congenital** | **Neurofibromatosis** · **Proteus syndrome** · **Klippel-Trenaunay-Weber** |
| **Acquired** | Amyloidosis · elephantiasis (filariasis) · AV malformation · tumor · acromegaly |

**Growth plate layers** — resting · proliferative · hypertrophic

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain · **can't fit shoes** · enlarged digit or limb

**Diagnosis** — clinical · X-rays only for **surgical planning** · **no biopsy** needed\
✗ Wrong options: antifungals (not fungal)

## 5 Management

### Treatment - ray amputation

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Initial / mild | **Observation**, monitor growth |
| **Open physes** | **Epiphysiodesis** ± bony + soft-tissue debulking |
| **Closed physes** | **Bony reduction and/or ray amputation** |
| Recurrence prevention | **Complete ray amputation ↓ recurrence** |

**Goals** — cosmesis + function (shoe wear) · individualised by etiology, site, growth left, severity

**Cosmesis** — after ray resection often **poor** · Syme amputation = excessive\
✗ Wrong options: "favorable"

## How it's asked

**"14-yr, closed physes, enlarged 2nd toe, shoes don't fit — true?"** → **complete 2nd ray amputation ↓ recurrence**\
✗ Wrong options: biopsy · antifungals · cosmesis favorable · epiphysiodesis
