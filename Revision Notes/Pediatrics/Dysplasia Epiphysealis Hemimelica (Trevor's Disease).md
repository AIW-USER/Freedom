# Dysplasia Epiphysealis Hemimelica (Trevor's Disease)

*Pediatrics · 40 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **osteochondroma arising from the epiphysis** → **asymmetric (hemimelic) epiphyseal overgrowth** of cartilage

**Incidence** — extremely rare, **~1:1,000,000** · **male** predilection · young children

**Location** — **lower extremity**: **knee or ankle** (one side of the epiphysis, usually medial) · usually **one joint**\
✗ Wrong options: upper-limb exostoses

**Cause** — defect in the **groove of Ranvier**

### Genes & lab markers

**Genetics** — **non-hereditary**\
✗ Wrong options: AD · AR / non-viable births

**Malignancy** — **no reported transformation**

**Histology** — identical to **osteochondroma**; diagnosis rests on **epiphyseal location**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — asymmetric limb deformity · joint symptoms · ↓ motion · joint-line tenderness

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — irregular calcified mass at joint line / epiphysis

**CT** — **cortical continuity with the epiphysis**

## 5 Management

| Stage | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Asymptomatic, small | Observe |
| **Symptomatic early** | **Excision of overgrowth** |
| **Symptomatic late** | **Osteotomies** |

## 6 Complications

**Recurrence** after excision (common) · articular irregularity → **early OA**

## How it's asked

**"Woman with history of DEH, genetic counselling — consistent feature?"** → **asymmetric epiphyseal overgrowth**\
✗ Wrong options: malignant risk · upper-limb exostoses · AD inheritance

**"Most common sites?"** → **knee and ankle**

**"Symptomatic early lesion?"** → **excision**
