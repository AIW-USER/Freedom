# Proximal Humerus Fracture - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 89 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Epidemiology** — <5% of pediatric fractures · **peak age 15**

**Pattern by age** — **<5 yr: SH I** · **5–12 yr: metaphyseal** · **>12 yr: SH II**

**Deforming forces** — epiphysis **abducted + externally rotated** (rotator cuff) · shaft **anterior, adducted, shortened** (pectoralis major, deltoid)

### Humeral growth contribution

**Proximal : distal humerus = 80 : 20** (highest ratio; femur 30 : 70) · proximal physis ~7 mm/yr → **huge remodeling**

**Ossification** — epiphysis 6 months · greater tuberosity 1–3 yr · lesser tuberosity 4–5 yr · closes 14–17 (girls), 16–18 (boys)

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — shoulder pain, deformity, ecchymosis · check neck, SC joint, elbow · brachial plexus + vascular exam

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP, lateral, **axillary / scapular Y** · ± contralateral · assess max angulation · **US in newborn** (unossified)

**Differential** — **Little Leaguer's shoulder** (throwers: physeal widening, metaphyseal change — not a fracture) · pathologic fracture (**UBC, ABC**)

### Neer-Horowitz classification

| Type | Displacement |
|---|---|
| I | **<5 mm** |
| II | **<⅓** shaft width |
| III | **⅓–⅔** |
| IV | **>⅔** |

![Neer-Horowitz type IV](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4004-type-4.jpg)

## 5 Management

### Treatment by age & displacement

| Age | Acceptable alignment (non-op) |
|---|---|
| **<10 yr** | **Any angulation** |
| **10–12 yr** | Up to **60–75°** |
| **>12 yr** | Up to **45°** or **⅔ displacement** |

**Rockwood (OB)** — reduce ± fix if **>50% displaced in >11 yr**

**Non-op** — sling ± swathe, immobiliser, coaptation splint · 7-yr Neer-Horowitz IV → **sling + PT** · 9-yr displaced → **sling + follow-up X-rays**\
✗ Wrong options: CRPP · ORIF (higher complications)

**Older adolescent (≥15, <1 yr growth) Neer-Horowitz III/IV** → **closed reduction + percutaneous pinning** · 13-yr marked varus SH II → **closed/open reduction + pins** (varus = lost abduction)\
✗ Wrong options: shoulder immobiliser · hanging arm cast · spica (redisplaces) · hemiarthroplasty

**Outcome by age** — older patients with III/IV treated non-op → **↑ limitations in daily activities** vs younger · long-term (Lähdeoja, 13 yr) no impairment vs controls, op = non-op\
✗ Wrong options: nonunion · more displacement

**Reduction** — traction + **90° abduction + ER**

**Fixation** — 2–3 lateral threaded pins (avoid axillary n. laterally, musculocutaneous anteriorly) · retrograde elastic nails · cannulated screws in older teens

**Open reduction** — irreducible from **interposed long head of biceps (most common)**, capsule, periosteum, deltoid · **open fracture** · **vascular injury** · intra-articular displacement · deltopectoral approach

## 6 Complications

**Loss of reduction** — unstable fracture reduced without pins

**Axillary nerve** — <1% from injury (neurapraxia, **medially displaced shaft**) · ↑ with pins → lateral pin below nerve (~5 cm below acromion in adults; = 2× humeral head height)

**Others** — varus malunion → impingement · shortening (<3 cm tolerated <12 yr) · growth arrest rare · hypertrophic scar · **pin-site infection 5–22%**

**Prognosis** — excellent (remodeling + shoulder ROM)

## How it's asked

**"9-yr skateboard, displaced proximal humerus?"** → **sling + follow-up X-rays**\
✗ Wrong options: CRPP · ORIF

**"16-yr, <1 yr growth — which needs CRPP?"** → **Neer-Horowitz III physeal fracture**\
✗ Wrong options: type I · 10° metaphyseal

**"13-yr hockey SH II, marked varus — best motion?"** → **closed or open reduction + percutaneous pins**\
✗ Wrong options: spica

**"15-yr displaced proximal humerus?"** → **closed reduction + percutaneous pins**\
✗ Wrong options: hanging cast · hemiarthroplasty

**"7-yr Neer-Horowitz IV?"** → **sling, graduated PT, monitoring**

**"12-yr gymnast, non-op — vs younger child?"** → **↑ risk of activity limitations**

**"Proximal : distal humeral growth?"** → **80 : 20**
