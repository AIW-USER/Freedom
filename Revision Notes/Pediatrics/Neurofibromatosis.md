# Neurofibromatosis

*Pediatrics · 71 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — NF1 **1 in 3,000** (Orthobullets) · 1 in 4,000 (OB) · **~50% new mutations** · **most common genetic disorder from a new single-gene mutation** · neural crest origin

### Genetics & inheritance (NF1, neurofibromin)

**Inheritance** — **autosomal dominant**

**Gene** — **NF1 on chromosome 17q11.2** → **neurofibromin**\
✗ Wrong options: 11q17.2

**Pathway** — neurofibromin **negatively regulates Ras** → deficiency → ↑ Ras → ↑ **Ras-dependent MAPK** (osteoclast function/survival) · ↓ osteoblast activity → osteopenia

**Selumetinib** — oral **MEK1/2 inhibitor** · FDA 2020 for **symptomatic inoperable plexiform neurofibromas in children with NF1**

| Gene / protein (distractors) | Disease |
|---|---|

| FGFR3 | Achondroplasia |
| GNAS / Gs-α | **McCune-Albright** (coast-of-Maine spots, fibrous dysplasia, precocious puberty) |
| **SPRED1** | **Legius** (café-au-lait + freckling, **no neurofibromas**, no MPNST) |

## 3 Presentation

### Diagnostic criteria & clinical features

**NIH 1987 — ≥2 of**

| Criterion | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Café-au-lait** | **≥6**, **>5 mm** prepubertal / **>15 mm** postpubertal · smooth "**coast of California**" border |
| **Neurofibromas** | **≥2 any type** or **1 plexiform** |
| **Freckling** | **Axillary or inguinal** (✗ Wrong options: plantar) |
| **Optic glioma** | (✗ Wrong options: glaucoma) |
| **Lisch nodules** | **≥2** iris hamartomas |
| **Osseous lesion** | **Sphenoid dysplasia** · thin long-bone cortex ± pseudarthrosis |
| **1st-degree relative** | With NF1 |

**Orthopaedic** — **anterolateral tibial bowing / pseudarthrosis** (most common presentation, more than forearm) · **forearm (radius/ulna) bowing + pseudarthrosis** · **hemihypertrophy** / limb overgrowth · scoliosis, kyphosis · **atlantoaxial instability** · verrucous hyperplasia

**NOT NF1** — **talipes equinus** · **posteromedial tibial bowing** · **pectus excavatum** (Marfan) · radial head subluxation (MHE) · glenoid retroversion (brachial plexus palsy) · ACL absence (fibular hemimelia) · DDH · **hemivertebrae**

**Crawford (588 pts)** — spinal deformity 21% · plexiform neurofibroma 25% · LLD 7.1% · congenital tibial dysplasia 5% · pectus 4.3% · hemihypertrophy 1.4%

### Neoplasia

**Plexiform neurofibroma** — **pathognomonic** · 4% (Orthobullets) · 25% (Crawford) · limb overgrowth · → **neurofibrosarcoma / MPNST**

**MPNST** — **50% occur in NF1** · enlarging painful mass · resection · 5-yr survival ~30%

**Other** — Wilms tumor · **aortic stenosis ~2%** → new fatigue/dyspnea needs urgent cardiology · hypertension · life expectancy ↓ **8–10 yr**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Spinal deformity (dystrophic scoliosis, rib penciling, dural ectasia)

**Spine** — **most common skeletal site** in NF1 (10–60%) · not in NF2

| | **Nondystrophic** | **Dystrophic** |
|---|---|---|
| Curve | Long, gentle, like **AIS** | **Short (4–6 vertebrae), sharp, angular** **kyphoscoliosis** (thoracic; thoracolumbar per OB) |
| Recognised | Later | **Earlier** |
| Bracing | Treat like AIS | **Ineffective** |
| Surgery | Like AIS | **Early ASF + PSF** (<7 yr if dystrophic) |

**X-ray (dystrophic)** — **vertebral scalloping** (posterior) · **rib penciling** · **enlarged foramina** · rotation

**Rapid progression ("modulation")** — **penciling of ≥3 ribs** = single strongest predictor (87% progress)\
✗ Wrong options: scalloping · curve >50° · rotation

**MRI before surgery** — always · **dural ectasia** + **dumbbell lesion** (intraforaminal neurofibroma) · paraspinal masses separate from AIS\
✗ Wrong options: syrinx · hemivertebra · diastematomyelia

**Spinal manifestations** — dural ectasia, dumbbell lesions, scalloping\
✗ Wrong options: **hemivertebrae** (TO)

**Fusion** — **PSF alone 40% pseudarthrosis** · **ASF + PSF 10%** · ± repeat iliac crest graft at 6 months · anterior fusion also prevents crankshaft

**Cervical kyphosis** — **anterior + posterior fusion**\
✗ Wrong options: in situ posterior · halo + posterior · collar

## 5 Management

### Congenital pseudarthrosis of tibia

**Association** — **50% of anterolateral bowing have NF1** (55% OB) · **10% of NF1** have it · pseudarthrosis = NF1 (✗ Wrong options: NF2) · dysplastic **constricted diaphysis** (✗ Wrong options: abuse · OI II · osteopetrosis)

**Pathology** — hamartomatous fibrous tissue in pseudarthrosis · cortical thinning → fracture in first year of walking

| Tibial bowing | Association |
|---|---|
| **Anterolateral** | **NF1 / congenital pseudarthrosis** |
| **Anteromedial** | **Fibular hemimelia**, absent lateral rays |
| **Posteromedial** | Intrauterine packaging · **calcaneovalgus** · LLD |

```
Anterolateral tibial bowing (NF1)
├─ No fracture / pseudarthrosis → total contact (clamshell) orthosis
│      (no remodeling expected; osteotomy contraindicated)
├─ Fracture / pseudarthrosis → resect + bone graft + fixation (IM rod / free fibula / Ilizarov)
└─ 3 failed surgeries → Syme amputation (preferred over BKA)
```

**Infant with fractured pseudarthrosis** → **genetics referral** + brace\
✗ Wrong options: abuse workup · ORIF first · cast if not walking

**IM rod (Charnley-Williams)** — resect, antegrade through site then retrograde through heel · **<4 yr → into calcaneus** · **5–10 yr → into talus** · rod pushed proximal ~2 yr later to free ankle

**Free fibula** — usually **contralateral** (ipsilateral abnormal) · **Ilizarov** alternative

**Failed bracing/casting with pseudarthrosis** → **resection + bone graft + IM nailing**\
✗ Wrong options: IM nail alone (hamartoma left) · bone stimulator

**Amputation** — after **3 failed surgeries** · **Syme** (calf atrophic and scarred)

![Anterolateral bowing — congenital pseudarthrosis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4054-congenital-pseudo.jpg)

### Forearm pseudarthrosis

**Features** — bowing, **medullary canal obliterated in distal third**, little trauma · **~50% have NF** · early excision of fibrous tissue + bone replacement (free vascularised fibula)

**Forearm X-ray distractors** — radial club hand (VACTERL, Fanconi, Holt-Oram) · congenital radial head dislocation · Madelung (Leri-Weill) · radioulnar synostosis (Apert, Carpenter, arthrogryposis, Klinefelter)
