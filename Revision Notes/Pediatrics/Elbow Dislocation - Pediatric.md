# Elbow Dislocation - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 42 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **3–6% of pediatric elbow injuries** · **M:F 3:1** · peak **10–15 yr** · **very rare <3 yr** (→ think transphyseal fracture + NAT)

**Mechanism** — **FOOSH** · posterior = hyperextension + valgus + supination · anterior = posterior-to-anterior blow on flexed elbow

**Small coronoid** in children → can't resist posterior ulnar displacement

**Associated** — **medial epicondyle avulsion = most common** (may **incarcerate** in joint) · radial head/neck, olecranon, coronoid fractures

**Nerves** — **ulnar** (with medial epicondyle) = most common neuropraxia · **median** + **brachial artery** stretched / entrapped

### Classification

**By proximal radioulnar joint position** — **posterolateral (most common)** · posteromedial · anterior (rare) · divergent

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — swollen, painful · **held flexed** · forearm looks short · **distal humerus fullness in antecubital fossa** · NV exam (brachial artery, median, ulnar)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP + lateral (± contralateral) · loss of radiocapitellar + ulnohumeral relationship, **radius–ulna relationship maintained**

**Post-reduction** — **locate the medial epicondyle** — if not seen in normal position, assume **intra-articular**

## 5 Management

**Reduce promptly** — by the most available provider (ED) under sedation · don't delay for orthopaedics

**Technique** — supine: elbow flexed, forearm **supinated**, gradual traction · prone: forearm hanging, anterior push on olecranon · anterior dislocation: inline traction, posterior push on forearm

### Management & surgical indications

**Stable after reduction (most)** → **brief immobilisation 1–2 wk** (posterior splint ~8–12 days) → early active ROM\
✗ Wrong options: cast 6 wk · hinged ex-fix · MRI

### Operative criteria

| Absolute | Relative |
|---|---|
| **Open dislocation** | Significant instability (rare) |
| **Incarcerated medial epicondyle** (or coronoid) | — |
| **Failure to obtain/maintain closed reduction** | — |

**Medial epicondyle fracture — when to fix** — absolute: **incarcerated fragment**, open, articular extension · relative: **elbow dislocation / instability**, ulnar nerve palsy, **throwing/upper-limb athlete** · nonunion common nonoperatively (often asymptomatic) · fuses **16–18 yr** (last)\
✗ Wrong options: buckle radius fracture · <2 mm displacement · radial nerve symptoms · video games

### Entrapped medial epicondyle fragment

![Incarcerated medial epicondyle after reduction](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4013-combo.jpg)

**Incongruous joint after reduction / fragment in joint** → **open reduction (medial approach) + ORIF of medial epicondyle**\
✗ Wrong options: re-reduction attempt · lateral epicondyle · coronoid

## 6 Complications

### Loss of motion after immobilisation

**Stiffness — loss of terminal extension** — from **immobilisation >2 weeks**\
✗ Wrong options: sex · age >9 · ED reduction · no ORIF

**Others** — **heterotopic ossification** (vigorous reduction) · transient nerve injury (**median entrapment**, **ulnar with medial epicondyle**) · brachial artery (rare) · compartment syndrome (hyperflexion splint) · recurrent instability (coronoid / radial head fractures)

## X vs Y

| | **Elbow dislocation** | **Transphyseal distal humerus fracture** |
|---|---|---|
| Age | **10–15 yr** | **<3 yr** |
| Displacement | **Posterolateral** | Posteromedial |
| Radiocapitellar | Disrupted | Maintained |
| NAT | Rare | **Strong association** |

## How it's asked

**"11-yr — treated with posterior splint 8–12 days then ROM?"** → **acutely reduced elbow dislocation**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral condyle · type I supracondylar · olecranon (3–5 wk)

**"15-yr, post-reduction joint incongruous with medial epicondyle fracture?"** → **open reduction**

**"10-yr medial epicondyle fracture — indication for surgery?"** → **elbow dislocated during fall**

**"9-yr, fragment in joint after reduction?"** → **medial epicondyle — ORIF**

**"Loss of extension at 4 months?"** → **splint immobilisation for 4 weeks**
