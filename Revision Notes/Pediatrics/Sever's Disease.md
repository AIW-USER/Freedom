# Sever's Disease

*Pediatrics · 96 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **calcaneal apophysitis** · **most common cause of heel pain in growing children** (TO) · traction apophysitis + repetitive microtrauma (like Osgood-Schlatter) · ± para-apophyseal stress fracture (OB)

**Who** — running/jumping athletes (soccer, basketball, volleyball) · **just before / during peak growth** · ages **9–12** · boys ~12, girls ~11 · **bilateral ~60%** (TO)

**Forces** — heel-strike impact + opposing pull of **Achilles (gastrocsoleus)** and plantar fascia

## 3 Presentation

### Diagnosis

**Symptoms** — posterior heel pain with activity / impact, relieved by rest · ± warmth, erythema, swelling

**Exam** — **tight Achilles** (↓ dorsiflexion) · **positive squeeze test** (medial-lateral calcaneal compression) · apophyseal tenderness · pain on triceps surae stretch · difficulty single-leg heel raise · Thompson negative

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — diagnosis is **clinical** · **sclerosis** in both affected and normal · **fragmentation** more frequent in Sever · rules out osteomyelitis, cysts

![Sever's disease — sclerotic fragmented apophysis](../../MCQ/attachments/q3898-b-sever-disease-lateral-1.jpg)

**MRI** — localises apophyseal inflammation · excludes stress fracture, lytic lesion, osteomyelitis (usually unnecessary)

**Bone scan** — ↑ apophyseal uptake, not helpful

**Incidental calcaneal cyst** — treat only if full width or ⅓ of calcaneal length

![Calcaneal apophysis fragmentation](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4074-sever.jpg)

## 5 Management

### Treatment - activity modification

**Non-op** — **activity modification** · **Achilles stretching** (↓ recurrence) · ice · **heel cups/pads** · NSAIDs · **short leg cast** if persistent

**Never** — **surgery** · custom orthotics not indicated · curettage of incidental cyst\
✗ Wrong options: calcaneal epiphysiodesis · Achilles lengthening

**Prognosis** — **self-limiting**, resolves with apophyseal closure · recurrence common

## X vs Y

| Heel pain | Clue |
|---|---|
| **Sever** | Squeeze test +, apophysis, 9–12 yr athlete |
| Achilles rupture | **Thompson +** |
| Os trigonum | Posterior impingement, ballet |
| Gastrocnemius strain | Proximal myotendinous junction |
| Plantar fasciitis | Anterior heel pad, no squeeze pain |
| Baxter nerve | Arch pain like plantar fasciitis |
| Haglund | Posterolateral prominence, women 15–35 in heels |
| Kohler | Dorsomedial midfoot, navicular |
| Coalition | Recurrent ankle sprains |

## How it's asked

**"9-yr player, activity heel pain relieved by rest?"** → **Sever's disease** (TO)

**"12-yr soccer, warm tender calcaneal tuberosity, imaging?"** → **Sever's disease**\
✗ Wrong options: stress fracture · osteomyelitis · Kohler

**"12-yr, DF 5°, squeeze +, Thompson −?"** → **calcaneal apophysitis**\
✗ Wrong options: Achilles rupture · os trigonum

**"11-yr volleyball, squeeze +, etiology?"** → **Achilles traction apophysitis**\
✗ Wrong options: Baxter nerve · plantar fascia

**"14-yr peak growth, red swollen heel, squeeze +?"** → **Sever's disease**\
✗ Wrong options: Haglund · retrocalcaneal bursitis

**"11-yr basketball heel pain — image?"** → **fragmented calcaneal apophysis**\
✗ Wrong options: Kohler · Iselin · coalition

**"10-yr soccer, tight Achilles, 2-cm anterior calcaneal cyst?"** → **observation with reduced activity**\
✗ Wrong options: curettage · bone scan

**"10-yr year-round soccer, heel pain every step?"** → **activity modification**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · orthotics · epiphysiodesis
