# Lyme Disease

*Pediatrics · 62 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Organism - spirochete

**Organism** — ***Borrelia burgdorferi*** · **spirochete** · tick-borne (**Ixodes** deer tick) · zoonosis (mice, squirrels, shrews)

![Borrelia burgdorferi spirochete](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9052-borrelia.jpg)

**Transmission** — tick must stay attached **48–72 h** · bites **May–November**, most cases **June–August**

**Epidemiology** — **most common tick-borne illness in US** · bimodal: **children 5–9**, adults 55–59

**Geography** — **Northeast** (Maryland → Maine, **Connecticut**, Rhode Island, Catskills) · Great Lakes / Midwest (I. scapularis) · Pacific Northwest (I. pacificus) · deer areas

**Joint pathology** — synovial hypertrophy · vascular proliferation · mononuclear infiltrate · immune complexes · **MMPs from chondrocytes** → cartilage damage

### Stages

| Stage | Timing | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Early localized** | **1–30 days** | **Erythema migrans** (hallmark) · flu-like illness |
| **2 Early disseminated** | **Weeks–months** · 15–20% untreated | **CN VII** (bilateral 50%, unlike Bell's) · **CN VI** · **lymphocytic meningitis** · migratory poly/monoarthritis · **carditis (heart block)** — rare |
| **3 Late** | **Months–years** · **60%** untreated | **Arthritis, usually knee** · intermittent / chronic monoarthritis · **acrodermatitis chronica atrophicans** |

## 3 Presentation

**Erythema migrans** — **60–80%** · expanding **>5 cm** bull's-eye · **1–3 wk** after bite (Orthobullets) · 1 wk–1 month (OB) · itch/burn · fades ~1 month · **axillary / gluteal folds**, hairline, elastic bands

**Lyme arthritis** — **knee** effusion, **large and relatively painless** · **swelling > tenderness** · near-full ROM · **can bear weight** · recurrent, migratory · rash often already resolved

**Acrodermatitis chronica atrophicans** — **"cigarette-paper" skin** · dorsum hands/feet/knees/elbows · older patients

![Acrodermatitis chronica atrophicans](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9052-atrophica.jpg)

**Neuro** — headache, neck stiffness, encephalitis · **polyradiculoneuropathy**

![Erythema migrans](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob9052-erythema-migrans.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Presentation & diagnosis (Western blot)

**Serology (two-tier)** — **ELISA** (sensitive, not specific) → **Western blot** (specific, ✓ confirmatory)\
✗ Wrong options: Northern (RNA) · Southern (DNA) · blood/synovial culture

**Serology pitfalls** — negative early (weeks to seroconvert) · stays positive after old infection · **vaccination: ELISA +, Western blot −**

**Endemic area + EM** → **treat without blood tests**

**Blood** — WBC **normal or mildly ↑** · **ESR/CRP ↑**

**Synovial fluid** — **10,000–25,000 WBC/mm³** (lower than septic) · PMN predominance

**CSF** — ↑ protein + **lymphocytic pleocytosis**

**Other** — skin-edge punch biopsy of EM → culture on **Barbour-Stoenner-Kelly** medium · PCR

**MRI** — **large knee effusion with no other pathology**\
✗ Wrong options: hip AVN · HAGL with effusion · vertebral osteomyelitis · Kienböck

**Organism images (distractors)** — S. aureus (GPC clusters) · Pseudomonas · P. acnes · AFB (TB) · Corynebacterium (Chinese letters) · E. coli · gonococcus (GN diplococci) · Kingella (GN coccobacilli, <3 yr)

## 5 Management

### Treatment - doxycycline mechanism

| Group | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Adult / ≥8 yr, mild** | Oral **doxycycline** · amoxicillin · cefuroxime |
| **Child <8 yr** | **Amoxicillin** · cefuroxime (**no tetracyclines**) |
| **Neuro, arthritis**, failed oral therapy | **IV ceftriaxone** / cefotaxime / penicillin G |
| Chronic arthritis failing IV antibiotics | **Synovectomy** |

**Duration** — doxycycline **10 days** (Orthobullets) · **28 days** for arthritis (OB) · single **4-wk** oral course (OB) · 1–2 wk (OB) — read the options

**Doxycycline mechanism** — binds **30S** ribosomal subunit → blocks **tRNA binding** → ↓ protein synthesis (minor 50S)

| Mechanism | Drugs |
|---|---|
| **30S** | **Tetracyclines (doxycycline)** |
| **50S** | Macrolides, chloramphenicol, clindamycin, linezolid, streptogramins |
| **Cell wall** | Penicillins, cephalosporins, **vancomycin (D-ala-D-ala)**, carbapenems, aztreonam, bacitracin |
| **Folate** | Sulfonamides, trimethoprim (TMP-SMX) |
| **DNA gyrase** | Fluoroquinolones |
| **RNA synthesis** | Rifampin |

## 6 Complications

**Persistent joint pain / swelling** despite therapy · **chronic Lyme disease** (disabling MSK pain, neurocognitive symptoms, fatigue) · chronic arthritis rare

### Transfusion screening

**Not screened in donated blood** — **Lyme** · malaria · babesiosis · Chagas

**Screened** — HBV · HCV · syphilis · HIV-1/2 · **West Nile virus**

## X vs Y

| | **Lyme arthritis** | **Bacterial septic arthritis** |
|---|---|---|
| Weight bearing | **Can bear weight** | Usually can't |
| Serum WBC | **Normal** | ↑ |
| Fever / CRP | Low-grade, mild ↑ | High |
| Synovial WBC | **10–25k** | Higher |
| History | Rash + endemic travel 1–2 wk before | — |
| Treatment | **Antibiotics** | **I&D** |

## How it's asked

**"MRI best supporting B. burgdorferi?"** → **knee with large isolated effusion**

**"Bull's-eye rash, fever — organism?"** → **Borrelia burgdorferi**\
✗ Wrong options: Bartonella · Kingella · Brucella

**"10-yr, painless swollen knee, target rash in Connecticut?"** → **Lyme arthritis**\
✗ Wrong options: septic · rheumatic fever · transient synovitis

**"Confirmatory test after positive ELISA?"** → **Western blot**\
✗ Wrong options: Northern · Southern · culture

**"Catskills camp, intermittent painless knee swelling — organism image?"** → **spirochete (Borrelia)**\
✗ Wrong options: S. aureus · P. acnes · AFB

**"Connecticut camping, WBC 25,000 in knee, walking?"** → **Borrelia burgdorferi**

**"Appalachian trip, rash, warm knee, bears weight — organism?"** → **spirochete**\
✗ Wrong options: GPC clusters · GN diplococci · GN coccobacilli

**"13-yr, Rhode Island, EM + tense knee — oral drug + mechanism?"** → **doxycycline, 30S ribosomal subunit**\
✗ Wrong options: 50S · cephalexin

**"Polyarthralgia + EM — mechanism of first-line drug?"** → **blocks tRNA binding at 30S**\
✗ Wrong options: 50S translocation · DNA gyrase · D-ala-D-ala

**"Not tested in routine blood screening?"** → **Lyme disease**\
✗ Wrong options: West Nile · HCV · syphilis
