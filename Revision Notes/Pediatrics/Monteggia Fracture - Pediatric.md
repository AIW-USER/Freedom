# Monteggia Fracture - Pediatric

*Pediatrics · 67 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **radial head dislocation** + proximal ulna fracture **or plastic deformation** of ulna

**Age** — peak **4–10 yr**

**Rule** — **isolated radial head dislocation almost never occurs in children** → look for ulnar plastic deformation

**Pattern** — Bado **I most common in children** · II more common in adults · children do better than adults

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — pain, swelling, deformity · **palpate radial head with every ulna fracture** (radial head often relocates spontaneously)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Rule** — **elbow X-rays for every forearm / ulna fracture** · **forearm X-rays for every radial head dislocation** · orthogonal films of whole forearm, wrist + elbow

**Radiocapitellar line** — line along radial neck axis passes through **centre of capitellum on every view** · any deviation = Monteggia

**Ulnar shaft fracture after ED reduction** → **dedicated elbow radiographs** before anything else\
✗ Wrong options: cast · fixation

![Radiocapitellar line](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4015-rc-line.jpg)

### Bado classification

| Type | Ulna apex | Radial head | Cast position |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Anterior** | **Anterior** (most common in children) | **110° flexion + full supination** |
| **II** | Posterior | Posterior | Full extension |
| **III** | **Lateral** | **Lateral** | Full extension + **valgus mold** |
| **IV** | **Radius + ulna** fractured same level | **Anterior** (1–11%) | — operative |

![Bado type I](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4015-37-moved.jpg)

## 5 Management

```
Pediatric Monteggia
├─ Length-stable ulna (plastic / greenstick / buckle) + stable RC joint → closed reduction + long arm cast
├─ Length-unstable ulna or RC unstable → fix ulna (IM nail or plate) + closed RC reduction
├─ RC joint irreducible after anatomic ulna → open reduction (annular ligament block)
└─ Bado IV · open · ≥10 yr unstable · closed physes → operative
```

### Acute treatment - restore ulnar length

**First consideration** — **reduce + maintain ulnar length and alignment** → radial head usually reduces itself\
✗ Wrong options: open RC reduction first · annular ligament reconstruction · RC pin

**Closed reduction first** — traction · Type I: **flexion** is main maneuver (+ supination) · prompt reduction → delay makes it difficult/impossible\
✗ Wrong options: plate · pins · splint without reduction

**Why supination + 110° flexion** — **tightens interosseous membrane** + **relaxes biceps** · annular, quadrate, oblique + interosseous ligaments tighten in supination\
✗ Wrong options: tightens biceps

| Operative indication | |
|---|---|
| Types I–III with **unstable radial head** after reduction | Yes |
| **Ulnar length not maintained** (complete, comminuted, displaced) | Yes |
| **Acute Bado IV** (even if head reduces) | Yes |
| Open fracture · ≥10 yr unstable · closed physes (adult ORIF) | Yes |
| **Loss of RC reduction on follow-up** | Yes |
| Incomplete ulna fracture + reduced radial head | No (cast) |

**Ulna fixation choice** — **transverse / short oblique → IM flexible nail** · **long oblique / comminuted → open reduction + plate** (nails won't prevent shortening)

**After failed closed treatment (Bado I, lost reduction at 5 days)** — **fix ulna + closed RC reduction + cast in supination**\
✗ Wrong options: open RC · annular / LCL repair (rarely needed)

**Comminuted ulna, RC won't hold** — **closed RC reduction + ORIF plate ulna**\
✗ Wrong options: RC pin (breaks) · IM nail · repeat closed reduction

![Intramedullary nailing of ulna](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4015-show-stacked-intramedullary-nailing-of-a-monteggia-fracture.jpg)

### Irreducible radial head - annular ligament

**Block to RC reduction** — **annular ligament** (most common, trapped between capitellum and radial head) · rare: biceps, PIN, capsule

**Annular ligament function** — **stabilises the proximal radioulnar joint**\
✗ Wrong options: PIN · LUCL · MUCL · biceps

**Check** — after anatomic ulna, examine radial head in pronation **and** supination · still out → **open reduction**

### Chronic missed Monteggia

**Presentation** — pain, ↓ motion, progressive valgus after missed / delayed diagnosis

**Treatment** — **open reduction of radial head** (lateral approach, radial head still concave) ± **annular ligament repair/reconstruction**, **ulnar osteotomy**, radial osteotomy · cast in **supination + flexion**\
✗ Wrong options: closed reduction · bracing

**Acute** — annular ligament reconstruction almost never needed

## X vs Y

| | **Acute Monteggia** | **Chronic (>2–3 wk) missed** |
|---|---|---|
| Key step | **Restore ulnar length** → RC reduces closed | **Open RC reduction** |
| Annular ligament | Rarely addressed | Repair / reconstruct |
| Extra | IM nail or plate ulna | **Ulnar osteotomy** |

## How it's asked

**"6-yr, comminuted ulna + anterior RC dislocation, RC won't stay reduced?"** → **closed RC reduction + ORIF plate of ulna**\
✗ Wrong options: RC pin · IM nail

**"Surgical indication EXCEPT?"** → **incomplete ulna fracture with lateral dislocation successfully reduced**

**"Why cast at 110° flexion + full supination?"** → **tightens interosseous membrane**\
✗ Wrong options: tightens biceps

**"Lost ulnar + RC reduction at 5 days?"** → **fix ulna, closed RC reduction, cast in supination**\
✗ Wrong options: annular / LCL repair

**"5-yr monkey bars Monteggia — first consideration?"** → **reduce and maintain ulnar fracture**\
✗ Wrong options: open RC · annular reconstruction

**"5-yr trampoline Monteggia — next step?"** → **closed reduction**\
✗ Wrong options: plate · splint

**"Ulnar shaft fracture post-reduction — next?"** → **dedicated elbow radiographs**

**"Failed closed RC reduction — blocking structure?"** → **annular ligament**\
✗ Wrong options: biceps · PIN

**"Interposed structure's function?"** → **stabilises proximal radioulnar joint**

**"3-month-old radial head dislocation, closed reduction fails?"** → **open reduction + cast in supination and flexion**
