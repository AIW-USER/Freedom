# Tillaux Fractures

*Pediatrics · 109 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **Salter-Harris III** of the **anterolateral distal tibial epiphysis** · **transitional** fracture (physis closing) · entirely epiphyseal

**Epidemiology** — **3–5% of pediatric ankle fractures** · **more common in girls** · **12–14 yr** (12–15 OB) · within ~1 yr of complete distal tibial closure (18-month transitional window) · **older than triplane** (triplane ~10–12, OB)

**Associated** — distal fibula fracture (SH I/II) · ipsilateral tibial shaft fracture

### Distal tibial physeal closure pattern

**Order** — **central → anteromedial → posteromedial → lateral** (Orthobullets, TO, OB) · central → posterior → medial → anterolateral (OB3880) · central → medial → lateral (OB) · central → anteromedial → posterolateral (TO17124838)

**Last to fuse** — **anterolateral** (TO) → open lateral physis avulsed by AITFL\
✗ Wrong options: anteromedial · posteromedial · posterolateral

**Distal tibial physis** — 35–40% of tibial growth · 15–20% of lower-limb growth · 3–4 mm/yr · growth until **14 girls / 16 boys**

![Pattern of distal tibial physeal closure](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4028-pattern-of-distal-tibia-physeal-closure.jpg)

### Anatomy - AITFL avulsion

**Ligament** — **anterior inferior tibiofibular ligament (AITFL)** · Chaput tubercle (anterolateral tibial epiphysis) → **Wagstaffe tubercle** (anterior distal fibula) · part of **syndesmosis** (+ PITFL)\
✗ Wrong options: PITFL · ATFL (ankle sprain) · PTFL · CFL · deltoid

**Same ligament injured in** — **high ankle sprain** (syndesmosis)\
✗ Wrong options: low ankle sprain (ATFL) · Weber A (below syndesmosis) · Gosselin (V-shaped plafond fracture) · subtalar dislocation

**Axial CT** — displaced fragment = **anterolateral region (AITFL)**\
✗ Wrong options: posterolateral · medial malleolus

## 2 Mechanism

### Mechanism

**Mechanism** — **supination / eversion – external rotation** → AITFL tension → anterolateral avulsion\
✗ Wrong options: pronation/eversion–ER (SH II lateral tibia + suprasyndesmotic fibula) · supination–plantarflexion (posterior tibia) · axial compression (SH V) · supination–inversion (ankle sprain)

**vs triplane** — **no coronal fracture in posterior metaphysis** = Tillaux

## 3 Presentation

**Findings** — pain · can't bear weight · slight swelling · **focal anterolateral joint-line tenderness** · deformity rare (fibula blocks displacement)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — AP · lateral · **mortise = best view** · SH III anterolateral epiphysis

**CT** — fracture pattern · **articular displacement** · intramalleolar / medial variants · **after closed reduction → CT for residual step-off**\
✗ Wrong options: discharge without CT

## 5 Management

### CT & surgical indications (>2 mm step-off)

### Operative criteria

| After closed reduction | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **<2 mm** displacement (rare) | **Long leg cast** 3–4 wk → short leg cast / boot 2–4 wk |
| **>2 mm** residual step-off | **CRPP or ORIF** |

**Not indications** — age, sex, any step-off <2 mm\
✗ Wrong options: age >12 · male · any step-off

**Closed reduction** — **internal rotation** of foot (or dorsiflex pronated foot then IR) · long leg cast controls **rotation** · early X-rays

**CRPP** — K-wire joystick · fluoro / arthrogram · K-wire or cannulated screw · **crossing physis OK** (little growth left) · good outcome if residual **<2.5 mm**

**ORIF** — **anterolateral approach** · reduce with **internal rotation** · intraepiphyseal or **transphyseal** wires / screws · long leg cast 3–4 wk → short leg walking cast 2 wk

**Post-reduction care** — low compartment risk · long leg > short leg cast\
✗ Wrong options: admit + splint

## 6 Complications

**Growth arrest** — **rare** (physis already closing) · ↓ with anatomic reduction

**Early arthritis** — ↑ with articular displacement · degenerative change as early as 4 yr

## X vs Y

| | **Tillaux** | **Triplane** |
|---|---|---|
| Salter-Harris | **III** | **IV** (as described by OB) |
| Age | **Older**, nearer maturity | Younger (~10–12, OB) |
| Posterior metaphyseal coronal fracture | **Absent** | Present |

## How it's asked

**"Juvenile Tillaux — avulsed by?"** → **anterior inferior tibiofibular ligament**\
✗ Wrong options: PITFL · ATFL · CFL

**"Axial CT — Tillaux fragment region + ligament?"** → **anterolateral region; AITFL**\
✗ Wrong options: posterolateral · deltoid

**"Ligament displacing Tillaux fragment?"** → **anterior tibiofibular**\
✗ Wrong options: deltoid · calcaneofibular

**"14-yr girl, displaced fragment — attached ligament?"** → **AITFL**\
✗ Wrong options: ATFL · long plantar

**"14-yr eversion injury — ligament in figure?"** → **AITFL**\
✗ Wrong options: PITFL · PTFL · CFL

**"Tillaux — same ligament involved in?"** → **high ankle sprain**\
✗ Wrong options: low ankle sprain · Weber A · Gosselin

**"13-yr SH III anterolateral distal tibia — mechanism?"** → **supination/eversion–external rotation**\
✗ Wrong options: pronation/eversion–ER · axial compression

**"12-yr after closed reduction — next to optimize outcome?"** → **CT scan of ankle**\
✗ Wrong options: discharge in cast · pinning before CT

**"Strongest indication for fixation after reduction?"** → **articular step-off >2 mm**\
✗ Wrong options: age · male sex · any step-off

**"Last part of distal tibial physis to fuse?"** → **anterolateral** (TO)

**"Correct pattern of distal tibial physeal closure?"** → **central → anteromedial → posteromedial → lateral** (TO)
