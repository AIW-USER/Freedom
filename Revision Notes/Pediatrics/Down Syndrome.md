# Down Syndrome

*Pediatrics · 38 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Most common chromosomal abnormality** (US) · **1:700** live births

**Risk** — **advanced maternal age**: 1 in 250 if >35 yr · 1 in 5000 if <30 yr

### Genes & lab markers

**Genetics** — **trisomy 21** (maternal duplication)

**Collagen** — chr 21 codes **type VI collagen (COL6A1, COL6A2)** → generalised laxity

**Labs / medical** — **hypothyroidism** (check with SCFE) · cardiac disease **50%** (ASD, PDA) · duodenal atresia · premature aging · Alzheimer's · intellectual disability

### Orthopaedic manifestations

**Ligamentous laxity + hypotonia** · short stature · delayed walking (**2–3 yr**) · **C1–2 instability** · **occipitocervical instability** · **hip instability** · **patellofemoral instability** · scoliosis, **spondylolisthesis (6%)** · **pes planus (50%)** · metatarsus primus varus · **hallux valgus (25%)** · **SCFE**

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — flattened facies · **upward-slanting eyes** · **epicanthal folds** · **single palmar (simian) crease** · laxity · hearing loss

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **chromosomal testing** (trisomy 21)

### Key radiology findings

**C-spine** — **flexion-extension lateral** — **ADI** normal **<5 mm** (Orthobullets) / up to 4 mm (OB) · 5–10 mm tolerated in Down · **space available for cord (SAC)** threshold **14 mm**

**Occipitocervical** — **Powers ratio**

**Hip** — **acetabular retroversion** (crossover + ischial spine signs) · coxa valga

**Knee** — long-leg film (genu valgum) · sunrise/Merchant (patellar subluxation)

## 5 Management

### Atlantoaxial instability – ADI & sports clearance

![Atlantoaxial instability in Down syndrome](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4104-aa-instability-downs.jpg)

**Prevalence** — **17.5%** (Orthobullets) · ~15% (OB)

**Presentation** — subtle: change in gait, bowel/bladder

**Flexion-extension films** — **before intubation** · **Special Olympics requires** C-spine imaging before gymnastics, swimming, high jump etc.\
✗ Wrong options: AP pelvis · knee/elbow films · neurology referral · clear without work-up

```
Atlantoaxial instability in Down syndrome
├─ ADI 5–10 mm, asymptomatic, SAC >14 mm → surveillance (neuro exam + repeat imaging); avoid contact sports
└─ ADI >5 mm + myelopathy, or ADI >10 mm, or SAC <14 mm → C1–2 posterior fusion
       (complications up to 50%)
```

**Asymptomatic, ADI 6 mm** → **avoid contact sports, high jump, diving, gymnastics, butterfly**\
✗ Wrong options: clear all · collar · halo · fusion

**Asymptomatic, ADI 5 mm** → **surveillance: symptoms, neuro exam, repeat imaging**\
✗ Wrong options: O–C2 fusion · anterior fusion · soft collar · traction

### Operative criteria

| Problem | Surgery when |
|---|---|
| **C1–2 instability** | ADI >5 mm + **symptoms/myelopathy** · **ADI >10 mm** · **SAC ≤14 mm** · cord compression on MRI/CT |
| **Occipitocervical** | Progressive neuro deficit / myelopathy → occipitocervical fusion (most observed) |
| **Scoliosis** | Brace **25–30°** · fuse **>50°** (more complications than idiopathic) |
| **Hip instability** | Older symptomatic → capsulorrhaphy + pelvic (**triple / PAO** for retroversion) + femoral varus osteotomy (high complications) |
| Patellar instability | Symptomatic → lateral release, medial reefing, semitendinosus tenodesis, **TTO if mature** |
| SCFE | **Pin both hips** |
| Pes planus | Orthotics if symptomatic; surgery if refractory |

### Lower-limb problems – hip instability, patellar instability & genu valgum

**Hip instability** — **5%**, age **2–10 yr** · laxity + **acetabular retroversion**, coxa valga · young, mild subluxation, no bony change → **abduction brace**\
✗ Wrong options: observe · NSAIDs · shoe lift · osteotomy

**Patellofemoral instability** — mature, painless, knock-knees → **observation** · symptomatic → **patellar stabilising brace** → surgery (TTO in mature)\
✗ Wrong options: lateral release alone · trochleoplasty first

**10-yr with Down facies, laxity, new limp** → associated image = **acetabular retroversion**\
✗ Wrong options: foramen magnum stenosis (achondroplasia) · absent cruciates (fibular/femoral deficiency) · iliac horns (nail-patella) · absent clavicles (CCD)

## How it's asked

**"10-yr Down, high jump, ADI 6 mm, asymptomatic?"** → **avoid contact sports, high jump, diving**

**"7-yr Down, ADI 5 mm, asymptomatic?"** → **surveillance with neuro exam + repeat imaging**

**"15-yr gymnast with trisomy 21 — before participation?"** → **cervical spine imaging**

**"17-yr Down, painless knock-knees, dislocated patellae?"** → **observation**

**"3-yr Down, mild hip subluxation, no bony change?"** → **abduction brace**

**"Hip image associated with Down syndrome?"** → **acetabular retroversion**

**"Indication for C1–2 fusion?"** → **myelopathy with ADI >5 mm, ADI >10 mm, or SAC <14 mm**
