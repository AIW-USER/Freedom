# Sacral Agenesis

*Pediatrics · 95 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Clinical features & maternal diabetes

**Definition** — partial/complete absence of sacrum + lower lumbar spine · part of **caudal regression syndrome**

**Incidence** — 1–2.5 per 100,000

**Risk factor** — **maternal diabetes** (poor glucose control early pregnancy)\
✗ Wrong options: folate deficiency (myelodysplasia) · alcohol · drugs

**Neurology** — **motor deficit matches the level** · **protective sensation usually intact** (sensory doesn't match level) → **fewer decubitus ulcers**\
✗ Wrong options: like myelodysplasia

**Progression** — neurologic deficit present at birth + static (OB6072) · OB2199: neuro + MSK manifestations may be progressive — read the options

**Associated** — **imperforate anus** / GI · GU · cardiac · LE deformities · **progressive kyphosis**

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — prominent last vertebra (T12) · **buttock dimpling** · **"sitting Buddha" posture** · **popliteal webbing**, extended or flexed knees, flexed hips, **rigid equinovarus** feet · motion at spine-pelvis junction

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Renshaw classification

| Type | Defect |
|---|---|
| I | Partial/total **unilateral** sacral agenesis |
| II | Partial sacrum, **bilaterally symmetric** defect |
| III | **Ilium articulates with sides of lowest vertebra** |
| IV | Lowest vertebral endplate **rests above fused ilia** / iliac amphiarthrosis |

## 5 Management

| Type | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **I–II** | **PT** · most become community ambulators · treat foot/knee deformity |
| **III–IV** with progressive kyphosis/scoliosis | **Spinopelvic stabilisation (fusion)** → **sitting balance + frees hands** (✗ Wrong options: goal of ambulation) |
| Non-functional limbs | Amputation → better mobility |

**Untreated complete absence** → progressive spinopelvic kyphosis → **must use hands to sit**\
✗ Wrong options: progressive paralysis · hip dislocation

## How it's asked

**"6-yr, colorectal surgery at birth, sits only with hands — true?"** → **motor deficits match level, sensory deficits don't**\
✗ Wrong options: protective sensation absent · fusion for ambulation · folate

**"2-yr complete sacral + lower lumbar absence, no stabilisation — outcome?"** → **can't sit without hand support**

**"Newborn, knee webbing, rigid clubfeet, Buddha posture, absent lumbar spine — maternal condition?"** → **diabetes mellitus**
