# Syndactyly of the Toes

*Pediatrics · 103 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **1 in 2,000** · most often **2nd–3rd toes**

**Cause** — incomplete / absent **apoptosis** between digits

**Inheritance** — mostly **autosomal dominant**

### Syndromic associations

**Associated** — familial syndactyly · **Down** · **Klippel-Feil** · **Smith-Lemli-Opitz**

**Smith-Lemli-Opitz** — **AR** · **7-dehydrocholesterol reductase** deficiency (cholesterol synthesis) · **microcephaly, cleft palate, cardiac defect (ASD)**, postaxial polydactyly, **2–3 toe syndactyly**\
✗ Wrong options: Beckwith-Wiedemann (hemihypertrophy, macroglossia) · Prader-Willi (chr 15, obesity) · FSHD · Rett

| Type | Fusion |
|---|---|
| **Simple** | Soft tissue only |
| **Complex** | Bony fusion |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — painless, cosmetic · no motor delay

**X-ray** — AP foot

## 5 Management

| Type | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Simple** | **Observation** (cosmetic) · surgery risks contracture, painful scar · no toe spacers |
| **Complex** | Digit release |

## How it's asked

**"Microcephaly, cleft palate, ASD + 2–3 toe syndactyly?"** → **Smith-Lemli-Opitz syndrome**\
✗ Wrong options: Beckwith-Wiedemann · Prader-Willi · Rett
