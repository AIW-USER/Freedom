# Cerebral Palsy - Hip Conditions

*Pediatrics · 17 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Hip surveillance – risk by GMFCS level & pathogenesis

**Incidence** — progressive subluxation in up to **50% of spastic quadriparesis** · rare in ambulators

| GMFCS | Risk of subluxation **by age 7** (Orthobullets, OB) | Hip displacement risk (TO surveillance table) |
|---|---|---|
| I | **<10%** | ~0–5% |
| II | **10–15%** | ~15% |
| **III** (walks with assistive devices) | **30–40%** | ~35% |
| IV | **50–60%** | ~70% |
| **V** | **70–80%** | **~90%** |

**Pathogenesis** — hips **normal at birth** · spastic **adductors (longus, brevis, magnus, gracilis) + iliopsoas (psoas major, iliacus)** → scissoring → coxa valga, anteversion, acetabular dysplasia → subluxation → dislocation\
✗ Wrong options: sartorius · gluteus minimus · obturator externus · piriformis · rectus femoris

**Direction** — dislocation **posterior + superior (>95%)** · acetabular deficiency **posterosuperior**

**Degeneration** — femoral head cartilage erosion → **pain** · **50–75%** of dislocated hips become painful (OB) · pain by **2nd–3rd decade**

**Windswept hips** — one hip **abducted**, other **adducted** → pelvic obliquity · **adducted hip on high side** at risk of dislocation · infrapelvic obliquity → compensatory **scoliosis**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — hip/groin pain · **difficulty sitting** · **perineal care / hygiene / diaper** difficulty

**Exam** — unreliable alone · ↓ ROM, pain with motion · lever-arm gait dysfunction · thigh-length discrepancy ("**pseudo-Galeazzi**")

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **AP pelvis** + frog lateral if possible

**Reimers migration index** — % of femoral head **uncovered** lateral to Perkin line · **most accurate method** to identify and monitor stability · **<33% at risk · >33% subluxated · >100% dislocated**

**Natural history** — hips **will dislocate** untreated if MI **>60–70%**

**Acetabular index** — **>25°** (OB) / >27° (OB) → acetabular dysplasia → **add acetabuloplasty**

**CT** — **operative planning** (3D acetabular correction) · CT scanogram for femoral version before derotation

![Reimers migration index](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4130-reimers.jpg)

### Stages of hip deformity

| Stage | Characteristics | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Hip at risk** | Abduction **<45°** + partial uncovering · **MI <33%** | **Botox (<3 yr)** to delay · **adductor ± psoas release, hamstring lengthening (3–4 yr)** · **avoid obturator neurectomy** |
| **Subluxation** | **MI >33%** | Adductor tenotomy if abduction restricted · persistent → **VDRO (5–6 yr)** · pelvic osteotomy if acetabular insufficiency |
| **Spastic dislocation** | **MI >100%** | **Open reduction + VDRO + femoral shortening + pelvic osteotomy** |
| **Windswept** | One abducted, one adducted | Brace adducted hip ± tenotomy · release abduction contracture of abducted hip |

![Spastic hip dislocation](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob4130-dislocation.jpg)

## 5 Management

**Nonop** — observation (mild) · **PT never shown to prevent subluxation** · **abduction bracing alone** does not ↓ dislocation, may cause **windswept** deformity

```
CP hip
├─ Young (<4–6 yr), MI 25/33–60%, limited abduction → adductor ± psoas release
├─ >4 yr or MI >60% (or >40% failing soft tissue) → VDRO + soft-tissue release
│      └─ Acetabular index >25° → + pelvic (Dega / San Diego) osteotomy, single stage
└─ Painful, chronically dislocated, deformed head (GMFCS V) → salvage
       └─ McHale valgus-support osteotomy · Castle resection-interposition
```

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Adductor + psoas release** | Age **<6** with MI **>25/33–60%** + limited abduction (Orthobullets) · **<4** with MI **>40%** (OB) · progressive subluxation **<8 yr** · adjunct to bony surgery |
| **Psoas lengthening** | Only with **hip flexion contracture (positive Thomas)** |
| **VDRO ± Dega + soft tissue** | Age **>4** or MI **>60%** · treat all at **single stage** |
| **Add acetabuloplasty** | **Acetabular index >25°** (✗ Wrong options: age <6 · GMFCS V · neck-shaft angle (VDRO fixes that) · prior botox) |
| **Proximal femoral resection / McHale** | Painful, chronically dislocated, **deformed head**, nonambulatory (GMFCS V) |
| **Castle** | Chronic dislocation, **adults**, can't stand to transfer (GMFCS V) |
| **THA** | Ambulatory or can **stand to transfer** · 85% 10-yr survival |
| Hip arthrodesis | Young patients |
| Girdlestone | **No longer done** — uniformly painful (proximal migration, no interposition) |

### Soft-tissue releases – adductor tenotomy indications & failure risk, obturator neurectomy

**3-yr diplegic, abduction 20°, MI 20%, Thomas negative** → **bilateral adductor releases only** · one of few surgeries in CP <4 yr\
✗ Wrong options: psoas (no flexion contracture) · VDRO · pelvic osteotomy

**Technique** — adductor longus tenotomy → sequentially **gracilis**, **adductor brevis** · psoas at **lesser trochanter (nonambulatory)** or **pelvic brim MTJ (ambulatory)** · goal **>45° abduction**

**Failure risk** — **preop MI >40%** · acetabular index **>27°**\
✗ Wrong options: lack of bracing · no neurectomy · age

**Obturator nerve** — anterior branch lies on **adductor brevis/magnus** after longus release (Ludloff) · supplies **adductor longus, brevis, gracilis** · **pinch neurectomy → weak hip adduction** · neurectomy **does not improve** results and risks **abduction contracture** — avoid (Orthobullets, OB) · sometimes used in nonambulatory spastic adduction (OB)

**6-yr nonambulatory quadriplegic, subluxation >50%, abduction 20°** → **open adductor tenotomy + iliopsoas release + bilateral VDRO** (too old for soft tissue alone)\
✗ Wrong options: percutaneous tenotomy · oral baclofen · phenol · neurectomy

### Reconstruction – VDRO with pelvic (Dega) osteotomy

**VDRO** — shortening varus derotation → corrects **coxa valga + anteversion**

**Dega** — acetabular reshaping, incomplete trans-iliac · **improves posterior coverage** (neuromuscular hip needs posterior) · hinges on triradiate → **triradiate must be open** · bone graft placed · no fixation needed · **San Diego** = alternative\
✗ Wrong options: salvage (shelf/Chiari are salvage)

**Bilateral MI 70%/60%, AI 30°** → **combined bilateral VDRO + pelvic osteotomies + soft-tissue releases** — lowest resubluxation\
✗ Wrong options: soft tissue alone · VDRO alone · pelvic alone · closed reduction

**8-yr wheelchair-bound, hygiene problems, abduction 5°/15°, bilateral dislocation + dysplasia** → **bilateral adductor release + varus femoral osteotomies + acetabuloplasties**\
✗ Wrong options: botox + PT · Pavlik · abductor release + valgus osteotomy

### Salvage – proximal femoral resection

**GMFCS V** — no antigravity head/trunk control, transported

**15-yr GMFCS V, painful sitting, flattened head on CT** → **proximal femoral resection**\
✗ Wrong options: open reduction (degenerate head stays painful)

**Resection over reconstruction** → **GMFCS V, MI 100% + head deformity**\
✗ Wrong options: GMFCS IV MI 50% (reconstruct) · MI 25% (analgesia) · MI 100% normal head (osteotomy)

**McHale (valgus support)** — resect head/neck (ligamentum teres left, sutured to psoas/capsule) + **subtrochanteric valgus closing-wedge osteotomy** → **lesser trochanter articulates with acetabulum** · relieves pain, ↑ abduction

**Castle** — resect at **lesser trochanter** level · **vastus oversewn** over femur, abductors/psoas/capsule over acetabulum → large soft-tissue interposition (differs from Girdlestone)

## 6 Complications

**Osteonecrosis** — **1–11%**

**Heterotopic ossification** — **radiation on POD 2–3** better than NSAIDs

**Insufficiency fractures** — **4–29%** · **distal femur after spica**

**Abduction contracture** — **anterior obturator neurectomy**

## How it's asked

**"GMFCS level with 30–40% risk of subluxation by age 7?"** → **level III (walks with assistive devices)**

**"GMFCS V dislocated hip — responsible muscles?"** → **gracilis and psoas major**

**"After adductor tenotomy, predictor of failure?"** → **preop MI >40%**\
✗ Wrong options: AI <27° · no bracing · no neurectomy · age

**"8-yr wheelchair-bound, hygiene problems, bilateral dysplastic dislocated hips?"** → **bilateral adductor release + varus osteotomies + acetabuloplasties**

**"6-yr quadriplegic, subluxation >50%, abduction 20°?"** → **open adductor tenotomy + iliopsoas release + bilateral VDRO**

**"3-yr diplegic, abduction 20°, MI 20%, negative Thomas?"** → **bilateral adductor releases**

**"Pinch neurectomy during medial approach weakens?"** → **hip adduction**

**"Primary benefit of Dega in CP?"** → **posterior coverage**

**"MI 70%/60%, AI 30° — least resubluxation?"** → **bilateral VDRO + pelvic osteotomies + soft-tissue release**

**"Why add acetabuloplasty to VDRO?"** → **acetabular index >25°**

**"15-yr nonambulatory, painful sitting, flattened head?"** → **GMFCS V — proximal femoral resection**

**"Femoral head resection rather than reconstruction?"** → **17-yr GMFCS V, MI 100%, head deformity**
