# Kohler's Disease

*Pediatrics · 56 / 112*

## 1 Basics

### Natural history & etiology

**Definition** — idiopathic **avascular necrosis (osteochondrosis) of the tarsal navicular** in young children

**Demographics** — age **4–7 yr** (Orthobullets) · **2–9 yr** (OB) · **boys 4:1** (80%) · **bilateral up to 25%**

**Blood supply** — intraosseous flow **centripetal** → **central one-third = watershed** · primary supply = perforating branches of **dorsalis pedis** · revascularised from the **perichondral ring** → excellent prognosis

**Theory** — navicular is the **LAST tarsal bone to ossify** → **compressed between the ossified talus and cuneiforms** → central ischemia (✓ theorised etiology)\
✗ Wrong options: first

**Mimic** — often misdiagnosed as **infection**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **dorsomedial midfoot pain** + **limp** · may be asymptomatic · swelling, warmth, redness possible · point tenderness over navicular

**Exam** — antalgic gait, weight on **lateral border** of foot · no fever, no constitutional symptoms

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **sclerosis, fragmentation, flattening** of navicular · diagnosis on **plain films alone**\
✗ Wrong options: cysts · widening

**Bone scan** — **↓ uptake** · unnecessary

**MRI** — not needed initially · if pain **persists >6 months** or diagnosis unclear → exclude **(cartilaginous) tarsal coalition**

**Labs / biopsy** — unnecessary when no illness or constitutional symptoms\
✗ Wrong options: ESR/CRP/cultures · biopsy

**Classification** — none widely used

## 5 Management

```
Kohler's disease
├─ Mild / no severe pain → observation · NSAIDs · arch support orthotic
└─ Pain with activity / failed orthotic → short-leg WALKING cast 4–6 wk
       (surgery never indicated)
```

### Treatment - short leg cast

**First line** — **NSAIDs** short course · activity limitation · **shoe modification / arch support**

**Cast** — **short-leg walking (weight-bearing) cast for 4–6 wk** → **shortens duration of symptoms** · **2 wk splint is insufficient** (OB)

**Weight bearing** — **safe**, does not affect outcome · OB6130 accepts "restriction of weight bearing or short leg cast" · NWB 6 wk also relieves symptoms (OB211605)\
✗ Wrong options: NWB cast (unnecessary, hard to enforce)

**Long vs short cast** — excellent function **regardless of casting technique**\
✗ Wrong options: long leg cast quicker

**Surgery** — **not indicated**\
✗ Wrong options: core decompression · triple / subtalar fusion · curettage + graft · medial column lengthening · ORIF · I&D

**PT** — does not aid recovery

### Prognosis

**Course** — **self-limiting** · intermittent activity symptoms for **1–3 yr** (✓ resolution in 1–3 yr; ✗ Wrong options: 6 months)

**X-ray** — improves **6–48 months** from onset · navicular **reconstitutes**\
✗ Wrong options: complete absorption

**Long term** — **no long-term disability** · no midfoot collapse · **no talonavicular arthritis** · residual deformity almost always asymptomatic

## X vs Y

| | **Kohler's disease** | **Infection (mimic)** |
|---|---|---|
| History | No illness, **no constitutional symptoms** | Recent illness, constitutional symptoms |
| X-ray | **Sclerosis, flattening, fragmentation** — diagnostic | — |
| Workup | **X-ray alone** (✗ Wrong options: ESR/CRP/WBC · biopsy) | Bloods, cultures |
| Treatment | **Walking cast / NSAIDs** (✗ Wrong options: antibiotics) | Antibiotics |

## How it's asked

**"5-yr, medial foot pain, failed orthotics?"** → **short-leg walking cast**\
✗ Wrong options: core decompression · fusion · I&D

**"6-yr, limp, navicular sclerosis — treatment?"** → **short leg walking cast**\
✗ Wrong options: long leg NWB cast · biopsy

**"Splinted 2 wk, still painful — next step?"** → **weight-bearing cast**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · bone scan · NWB cast

**"5-yr, navicular tenderness + swelling?"** → **symptomatic: restrict weight bearing or short leg cast**\
✗ Wrong options: antibiotics · medial column lengthening

**"5-yr, limp, normally active, mild pain?"** → **observation or orthotic arch support**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · aspiration

**"Redness, warmth over midfoot + flattened navicular?"** → **weight-bearing short leg cast**\
✗ Wrong options: antibiotics · excision

**"Kicked at soccer, 2 months dorsal pain?"** → **walking cast**\
✗ Wrong options: ORIF · bloods · MRI

**"Natural history untreated?"** → **resolution in 1–3 yr**\
✗ Wrong options: 6 months · collapse · TN arthritis

**"Most accurate statement about etiology?"** → **compression between ossified talus and cuneiforms**\
✗ Wrong options: first to ossify · cysts + widening
