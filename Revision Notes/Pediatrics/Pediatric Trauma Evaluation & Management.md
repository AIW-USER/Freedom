# Pediatric Trauma Evaluation & Management

*Pediatrics · 81 / 112*

## 1 Basics

**Epidemiology** — trauma = **most common cause of death in children >1 yr** · polytrauma mortality **~20%** · falls + MVAs most common

**Morbidity** — **CNS injury = highest overall morbidity + mortality** · **spine fractures = highest among MSK injuries** · C-spine injury more common **<8 yr** (restraints don't fit)

### Anatomy & physiology vs adults

| System | Child |
|---|---|
| **Airway** | Smaller (obstructs easily) · large tongue, floppy epiglottis · **large occiput flexes neck supine** · **larynx higher + anterior (C2–C3 vs C6–C7)** |
| **Breathing** | **Hypoventilation = most common cause of arrest** · horizontal ribs · diaphragmatic · fewer type I fibres → tire fast · RR varies with age |
| **Circulation** | Relatively larger but absolutely small blood volume · lower SVR · **hypotension is LATE** (25–30% loss) · small veins → **IO access** |
| **Disability** | Open sutures/fontanelle · thin skull · **large head** → head/neck injury |
| **Exposure** | High BMR + surface area → **heat loss** · ↑ glucose need, small glycogen stores |

## 5 Management

### Resuscitation (airway, IO access, blood volume, transport)

**Transport** — **<8 yr: spine board with occipital cut-out or raised chest** (✓ modified board for suspected spine injury)\
✗ Wrong options: routine pelvic binder · routine IO · nasal airway in head injury

**ATLS** — Airway → Breathing → Circulation → Disability → Exposure · fix each before moving on

**Intubate** — **GCS <8** / can't protect airway (✗ Wrong options: GCS <12) · e.g. GCS 6 + SpO2 83% → **intubation first** (✗ Wrong options: splint ankle · CT · close laceration)

| Formula | Value |
|---|---|
| **Broselow tape** | Drug doses, equipment size, defibrillation energy |
| **ETT (uncuffed)** | **(age/4) + 4** or (age + 16)/4 |
| **Systolic BP** | **80 + (2 × age)** |
| **Chest tube** | **4 × ETT** |
| **Blood volume** | **75 mL/kg** (Orthobullets) · **75–80 mL/kg** (OB) |
| **First bolus** | **20 mL/kg NS** |
| **After 2 boluses** | **10 mL/kg PRBC** |

**Shock signs** — narrowed pulse pressure at **~15%** loss · hypotension after **25–30%** · 100 mL in 5 kg child ≈ 26.7% of volume

**IV access fails (even normotensive)** → **intraosseous line**\
✗ Wrong options: PICC · cutdown · subclavian · keep trying

**Triad of death** — **acidosis + hypothermia + coagulopathy** (inadequate resuscitation)

### Pediatric scoring systems

| PTS | Mortality |
|---|---|
| **<0** | **100%** |
| 1–4 | 40% |
| 5–8 | 7% |
| **≤8** | **Send to pediatric trauma centre** |

**Pediatric GCS <8** → ↑ mortality · **SpO2 at presentation + GCS at 72 h** predict long-term neuro recovery

### Injury patterns & outcomes (TBI, multiorgan failure)

**TBI** — **most common ATV injury** (✗ Wrong options: open fractures · pneumothorax) · **greatest long-term morbidity** (✗ Wrong options: spine · abdomen · open fracture) · pain ↑ ICP → **fracture fixation ↓ ICP** · **HO more common** → **↑ serum ALP heralds HO** → NSAID prophylaxis

**Peripheral nerve injury** — mostly with **closed fractures** · **EMG if no return by 2–3 months**

**Multiorgan failure** — **early after admission, all organs simultaneously** · adults: from 48 h, sequential starting with lungs · acute lung injury ~6× lower in children\
✗ Wrong options: 48 h · sequential

### Femur fixation in polytrauma with head injury

**Timing** — fix once **cerebral perfusion pressure stable** · early fixation eases transport, ↓ malunion/shortening, ↓ ICP

**9-yr TBI + transverse femoral shaft** → **retrograde flexible IM nails**\
✗ Wrong options: spica · 90-90 traction (spasticity) · piriformis reamed nail (AVN risk)

**Prognosis** — children recover from TBI better than adults

## How it's asked

**"8-yr fell from tree, GCS 6, SpO2 83%?"** → **intubation**

**"Blood volume per kg in a 5-yr?"** → **75–80 mL**

**"5-yr polytrauma, no peripheral IV, BP 110/70?"** → **intraosseous device**

**"Transporting 4-yr with fluctuating consciousness?"** → **modified spine board**\
✗ Wrong options: intubate if GCS <12 · routine binder

**"4-yr polytrauma — greatest long-term morbidity?"** → **traumatic brain injury**

**"Multiorgan failure in children?"** → **early, all organs simultaneously**

**"6-yr high-speed ATV — most likely injury?"** → **brain contusion**

**"9-yr TBI + transverse femur fracture?"** → **retrograde flexible IM nails**
