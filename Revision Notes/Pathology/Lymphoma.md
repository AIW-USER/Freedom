# Lymphoma

*Pathology · 33 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Primary lymphoma of bone (PBL)** — **very rare** · 2–3% of primary bone malignancies (OB) · 3–7% (TO, osteosarcoma chapter)

**Extranodal** — 10–35% of NHL patients

**Demographics** — **males** · all ages, **35–55** commonest (median 45–60)

**Site** — bones with **persistent red marrow**: **pelvis, spine, ribs** · knee (distal femur, proximal tibia), **proximal femur**, shoulder girdle · **diaphysis** of long bones

**Risk factors** — immunodeficiency (HIV, hepatitis) · viral/bacterial infection

### Forms

| Form | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Primary lymphoma of bone** | Solitary site, no other disease for **6 months** · mostly **non-Hodgkin B-cell** · **better prognosis** than secondary bone involvement |
| Multiple bony sites | No visceral disease |
| Bone + soft tissue | — |

**Subtype** — **diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL)** = **70–80%** of bone lymphomas

### Genes & lab markers

**Immunostains +** — **CD20**, **CD45 (leukocyte common antigen)**, CD79a, BCL-6 (B cell) · **CD3 −**

**Negative** — **CD99 −**, **no t(11;22)** (vs Ewing) · reticulin + (vs Ewing −)

| Stain | Tumour |
|---|---|
| **CD20 + CD45** | ✓ **Lymphoma** |
| CD99 + FLI-1 (EWSR1) | Ewing |
| CD138 | Myeloma |
| CD31 + CD34 | Vascular tumours |
| Desmin + MyoD1 (myogenin) | **Rhabdomyosarcoma** |

## 3 Presentation

**Pain unrelieved by rest** · **B symptoms** — fever, **night sweats**, weight loss, fatigue

**Pathologic fracture** — **25%**

**Mass** — warm, swollen, large **soft-tissue mass** · spinal cord compression

**Labs** — may mimic infection (mild ↑ ESR/CRP, lymphocytosis)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **large ill-defined, diffuse, permeative/moth-eaten lytic** lesion, subtle mottling · **diaphysis** · cortical thickening 25% · may be **sclerotic** · **"ivory" vertebra** · multiple sites · often **subtle/unremarkable**

![Diffuse lytic mottled lesion of the femur](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8025-case-a-femur-xray-parsons-moved.jpg)

**MRI** — **extensive marrow involvement + large soft-tissue mass out of proportion to bone destruction** · T1 dark, T2 bright

**Bone scan** — intensely positive · **PET/CT** — staging + follow-up

**CT chest/abdomen/pelvis** — staging

**Bone marrow aspiration + biopsy** — **required for staging** · **before any definitive bony stabilization** when no known primary\
✗ Wrong options: chemo first · resection · prophylactic nail first

**Moth-eaten (type 2) margins (TO)** — myeloma, mets, infection, EG, OS, chondrosarcoma, **lymphoma** · 45-yr-old, proximal humeral path fracture, moth-eaten → **lymphoma**

### Histology

**Mixed small round blue cells** of **different sizes and shapes** · **diffuse infiltration** of trabeculae (not nodular) · little stroma

**Needle biopsy** — often non-diagnostic (**crush artefact**)

![Mixed small round blue cell infiltrate](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8025-histology-b-high-parsons-moved.jpg)

### Differential

| | Destructive, young | Malignant, older | Small cells | Chemo alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lymphoma** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Leukaemia · Ewing | ✓ | — | ✓ | — |
| OS · EG · osteomyelitis · desmoplastic fibroma | ✓ | — | — | — |
| Metastasis | — | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| **Myeloma** | — | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| Chondrosarcoma · MFH · secondary sarcoma | — | ✓ | — | — |
| RMS · neuroblastoma | — | — | ✓ | — |

**Ewing vs lymphoma** — Ewing younger, CD99 +, t(11;22) · lymphoma older, CD20/CD45 + · TB → caseating granulomas · osteomyelitis → mixed inflammatory cells

## 5 Management

### Treatment — chemotherapy (R-CHOP) & fixation

```
Primary lymphoma of bone
├─ First → BONE MARROW BIOPSY + PET/CT (staging)
├─ All → MULTI-AGENT CHEMO (R-CHOP) ± LOCAL RT (persistent disease)
└─ Impending / actual pathologic fracture → STABILISE (IM nail / hemiarthroplasty) then chemo ± RT
```

**Chemo** — **CHOP: cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin (hydroxydaunorubicin), vincristine (Oncovin), prednisone** **± rituximab (R-CHOP)** · chemo alone effective for most · **70% 5-yr survival** in disseminated disease

**Radiation** — adjunct for local control of persistent disease · RT alone insufficient · routine RT in R-CHOP era uncertain

**Surgery** — **only to stabilise** pathologic / impending fractures (prophylactic IM nail) · surgery mustn't delay chemo

**Femoral neck lesion found at hemiarthroplasty (frozen section = lymphoma)** — **complete hemiarthroplasty, then staging + chemoradiotherapy**\
✗ Wrong options: close wound + chemo · radical resection + megaprosthesis · marrow transplant · disarticulation

**Not indicated** — wide resection / limb salvage · amputation · hemipelvectomy · marrow transplant (relapse only) · neoadjuvant chemo + resection (OS/Ewing protocol)

## 6 Complications

**Prognosis** — **primary bone lymphoma better** than secondary bone involvement

## X vs Y

| | **Lymphoma of bone** | **Ewing** | **Myeloma** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age | 35–55 | 5–25 | >50 |
| X-ray | Permeative, mottled, ± sclerotic, big soft-tissue mass | Permeative, onion skin | **Punched-out**, cold bone scan |
| Marker | **CD20, CD45** | **CD99** | **CD138** |
| Treatment | **R-CHOP ± RT** | VAC/IE + surgery ± RT | Chemo ± RT |

## How it's asked

**"45-yr-old, destructive lesion + soft-tissue mass, round blue cells, CD99 −?"** → **lymphoma of bone**\
✗ Wrong options: Ewing · chondrosarcoma · adenocarcinoma · OS

**"32-yr-old distal femur, sheets of blue cells, no t(11;22)?"** → **lymphoma**\
✗ Wrong options: Ewing · bacterial/TB osteomyelitis · OS

**"50-yr-old, tibial lesion, B symptoms, large soft-tissue mass — stains?"** → **CD20 + CD45**\
✗ Wrong options: CD31/CD34 · CD99/FLI-1 · CD138 · desmin/MyoD1

**"48-yr-old, fevers, weight loss, CD20/CD45 — histology?"** → **small round blue cells**

**"76-yr-old proximal femur, PET shows single site, prophylactic fixation + chemo — supporting pathology?"** → **large atypical lymphoid cells CD20 +, CD79a +, BCL-6 +, CD3 −**

**"Pathologic femoral neck fracture through lymphoma at hemiarthroplasty?"** → **hemiarthroplasty, then staging + chemoradiotherapy**

**"Severe thigh pain, extensive femoral lymphoma?"** → **IM nail + multi-agent chemo ± RT**\
✗ Wrong options: AKA · wide resection + prosthesis

**"Knee pain, failed antibiotics, biopsy = lymphoma?"** → **multi-agent chemo ± local RT**

**"Iliac DLBCL, PET no other lesions?"** → **CHOP chemotherapy**\
✗ Wrong options: marrow transplant · CT CAP for primary · hemipelvectomy · RT alone

**"40-yr-old, distal thigh lytic lesions, weight loss, no known primary — next?"** → **bone marrow aspiration + biopsy**\
✗ Wrong options: chemo · resection · nail first
