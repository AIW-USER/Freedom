# Epithelioid Sarcoma

*Pathology · 17 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rank** — **commonest soft-tissue sarcoma of the hand and wrist** (others in hand: MFH, synovial sarcoma, clear cell sarcoma)\
✗ Wrong options: chondrosarcoma · rhabdomyosarcoma · myxoid liposarcoma · haemangiopericytoma

**Demographics** — adolescents + young adults **10–35** (10–40) · **M:F 2:1**

**Site** — hand/wrist, fingers · forearm, buttock/thigh, knee, foot

**Depth** — superficial → **ulcerates**, mimics **skin carcinoma, rheumatoid nodule, granuloma** · deep → attached to **tendons, tendon sheaths, fascia**

### Spread

**Regional lymph nodes** — **common** · unique, more frequent than other sarcomas (lungs still the commonest overall site)

**Spreads along** lymphatics and **fascial planes** · **30% metastasized at presentation**

**Lymphatic sarcomas — "RACES"** — **R**habdomyosarcoma · **A**ngiosarcoma · **C**lear cell sarcoma · **E**pithelioid sarcoma · **S**ynovial sarcoma\
✗ Wrong options: liposarcoma · fibrosarcoma · leiomyosarcoma · UPS

### Genes & lab markers

**Genetics** — unknown

**IHC** — **keratin +**, **EMA +**, vimentin +, **CD34 +**

## 3 Presentation

**Mass** — **small (3–6 cm), firm, painless, slow-growing** nodule · upper extremity · may **ulcerate**

**History** — often a **previous "benign" excision → recurrence** (misdiagnosed)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **calcification 10–20%** · adjacent **bone erosion**

**MRI** — indeterminate · **T1 dark, T2 bright** · tendon-sheath nodule

### Histology

**Pattern** — **nodular with central necrosis** in granulomatous-looking areas → mimics **necrotizing granuloma** · chronic inflammatory cells at margins

**Cells** — epithelioid **ovoid/polygonal cells, eosinophilic cytoplasm** + spindle cells · dense hyalinized collagen · pleomorphism rare

### Differential

| Lesion | Distinguishing feature |
|---|---|
| **Epithelioid sarcoma** | Young adult, nodules with **central necrosis**, keratin + |
| Rheumatoid nodule | Similar histology · older, RA |
| Squamous cell carcinoma | **Keratin pearls** |
| Angiosarcoma (soft tissue) | Well-formed **vascular channels** |
| Epidermal inclusion cyst | Benign appearance, low recurrence |
| Clear cell sarcoma / carcinoma | **Clear cells** |
| BPOP (Nora lesion) | Reactive HO, hands/feet, 3rd decade |

## 5 Management

**Wide excision + adjuvant radiotherapy** — all operable tumours

**Sentinel lymph node biopsy** — at excision → regional nodal staging

**Inadequate excision** — high rate of **multiple recurrences** → **re-excision of tumour bed**

**Amputation** — multiple recurrences, to stop spread

### Unplanned excision — re-resection

**Unplanned ("whoops") marginal resection** — residual tumour in **50–60%** → **repeat staging + re-resection of tumour bed** (± pre/post-op RT)\
✗ Wrong options: PT · AFO · tendon transfer · nerve exploration first

## 6 Complications

**Prognosis** — **extremely poor** · lymph node + lung metastasis · recurrence

## How it's asked

**"28-yr-old, recurrent ulcerating thumb mass, nodules with necrosis?"** → **epithelioid sarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: rheumatoid nodule · SCC (keratin pearls) · angiosarcoma · inclusion cyst

**"Slowly growing finger nodule, nodular necrosis, no clear cells?"** → **epithelioid sarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: clear cell sarcoma/carcinoma · BPOP

**"Commonest soft-tissue sarcoma of the hand?"** → **epithelioid sarcoma**

**"Sarcoma most likely to spread via lymphatics?"** → **epithelioid sarcoma** (RACES)

**"Site of metastasis more common than in other sarcomas?"** → **regional lymph nodes** → **sentinel node biopsy**

**"Foot drop after outside marginal excision of popliteal epithelioid sarcoma?"** → **staging + re-resection of tumour bed**
