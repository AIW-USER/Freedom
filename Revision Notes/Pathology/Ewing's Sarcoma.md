# Ewing's Sarcoma

*Pathology · 18 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rank** — **2nd commonest primary malignant bone tumour in children** (OS 1st) · 3% of paediatric malignancies, 10% of primary malignant bone tumours · ~3/million · commonest malignant bone tumour of the **1st decade** · rhabdomyosarcoma = commonest paediatric soft-tissue sarcoma

**Age** — **5–25** · 80–90% <20 · peak **10–15**

**Sex / race** — **M:F 1.5:1** · **rare in African Americans** and Asians

**Site** — even split axial vs appendicular · **metadiaphysis of long bones ~50%** (femur, tibia, humerus) · **pelvis ~25%** · scapula, ribs · soft tissue rare

**Diaphysis myth (TO)** — only **~50% diaphyseal**, 50% metaphyseal → "predominantly diaphyseal" is false

**Origin** — unknown, probably **neuroectodermal** (PNET family) · cell of origin likely in **marrow**

### Genes & lab markers

**t(11;22)(q24;q12)** — **EWS–FLI1** fusion (aberrant transcription factor: EWS activation domain + FLI ETS DNA-binding domain) · **85–95%** · detect by **PCR / FISH**

**Variants** — **t(21;22) EWS–ERG** (10–15%) · t(7;22)

**t(11;22) presence** — does **not** alter clinical course **(TO)**

| Translocation / fusion | Tumour |
|---|---|
| **t(11;22) EWS–FLI1** | ✓ **Ewing** |
| t(X;18) SYT–SSX | Synovial sarcoma |
| t(12;16) TLS–CHOP | Myxoid liposarcoma |
| t(12;22) EWS–ATF1 | Clear cell sarcoma |
| t(9;22) EWS–CHN | Extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma |
| t(2;13) PAX3–FKHR | Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma |
| t(X;17) ASPL–TFE3 | Alveolar soft part sarcoma |
| RB, p53 | Osteosarcoma (✗ Wrong options: Ewing) |

**Labs** — **↑ ESR, ↑ WBC, anaemia, ↑ LDH** → mimics infection, marks extensive disease

**Immunostains** — **CD99 / MIC2 + (95%)** · vimentin + · **PAS +** (glycogen) · NSE +, S-100 +, Leu7 (CD57) + · **cytokeratin −**, **reticulin −** (+ in lymphoma), neurofilament − (+ neuroblastoma), myoglobin − (+ embryonal rhabdo)

**NKX2.2 +** — **highly specific** for Ewing (TO)

**Lymphoma vs Ewing (TO)** — lymphoma **PAS −, reticulin +** · Ewing PAS +, reticulin −

**CD45 (LCA)** — listed **+** in Orthobullets chart · TO/OB: **lymphoma is LCA +**, Ewing LCA − (OB calls LCA "CD34") — read the options

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — commonest, worse at night · **>50% symptomatic >6 months** (delay worse in pelvis/axial)

**Infection mimic** — **swelling, erythema, warmth, fever (~20–25%)**, weight loss, ↑ WBC/ESR

**Mass** — may be impalpable until large · limp, ↓ ROM

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — large destructive **diaphyseal/metadiaphyseal** lesion · ill-defined **permeative, moth-eaten** · lytic ± reactive bone · **"onion skin"** (lamellated) or **"sunburst" / "hair-on-end"** periosteal reaction · Codman triangle · **soft-tissue mass >80%**

![Onion-skin periosteal reaction, tibial diaphysis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8047-case-b-tibia-xray-a-parsons-moved.png)

**MRI** — **next step after suspicious X-ray** · soft-tissue extent, marrow, NV relation · T1 low–intermediate, **T2 high heterogeneous**, heterogeneous enhancement · monitors chemo response

**Bone scan** — very hot · skip/distant mets (10%) · **PET better than bone scan for Ewing mets** (vs OS)

**CT chest** — required for lung mets

### Work-up (MRI, bone marrow biopsy)

**Bone marrow biopsy** — **required for Ewing** (marrow mets), **not routine for OS**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · CT · bone scan · protein electrophoresis (done for both or neither)

**PET-CT (TO)** — staging + response · **SUV fall >50% = good response** · if **bone scan + CT chest** used instead → **must add bone marrow aspiration**

**Order** — X-ray → **MRI** → staging (CT chest, bone scan, **bone marrow biopsy**) → **biopsy by treating surgeon**

**Not appropriate** — no further work-up · arthrogram · pelvic US · chiropractor · cast / rest and repeat X-ray · IV antibiotics / I&D when ESR/CRP normal

### Histology

**Gross** — grey-white, necrosis, haemorrhage · **liquid, "pus-like"**

**Micro** — **sheets of monotonous small round blue cells**, **high N:C ratio**, scant cytoplasm · **pseudorosettes (Homer-Wright)** · no stroma

![Small round blue cells](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8047-sa-histo-moved.jpg)

### Small round blue cell tumours — by age

| Age | Likeliest |
|---|---|
| **<5 yr** | **Metastatic neuroblastoma**, leukaemia |
| 5–10 yr | Eosinophilic granuloma |
| **5–30 yr (peak 5–15)** | **Ewing** |
| >30 (15–40) | **Lymphoma** |
| >40–50 | **Myeloma**, metastatic small cell lung Ca |
| 2nd–3rd decade (rare) | Small-cell OS, mesenchymal chondrosarcoma |

**Round-cell tumours** — Ewing, PNET, neuroblastoma, embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma, EG, lymphoma, myeloma, round-cell liposarcoma · not **leiomyosarcoma = spindle cells, cigar nuclei**

**TB** — small round cells (lymphocytes) but periarticular, joint

### Staging

**MSTS** — almost all **IIB or III** · **26–28% macrometastases at presentation** · sites **lung 50%, bone 25%, marrow 20%**

### Differential

| | Destructive, young | Small round cells | Wide resection + chemo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ewing** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Osteosarcoma | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| Lymphoma · leukaemia · EG | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Osteomyelitis · desmoplastic fibroma | ✓ | — | — |
| Neuroblastoma · myeloma | — | ✓ | — |
| Dedifferentiated CS | — | — | ✓ |

**Osteomyelitis vs Ewing** — mixed inflammatory infiltrate (neutrophils, plasma cells, lymphocytes, histiocytes) → **debridement + antibiotics** · Ewing = uniform round cells, no inflammation

## 5 Management

### Treatment — chemotherapy with surgery or radiation

```
Ewing sarcoma (chemo needed in ALL — presume micromets)
├─ Resectable / expendable site → NEOADJUVANT CHEMO → WIDE RESECTION → ADJUVANT CHEMO
│      └─ Positive margin / poor necrosis / mets → + ADJUVANT RT
├─ Unresectable (spine, pelvis) or unacceptable morbidity → CHEMO + RT
└─ Huge tumour on NV structures, displaced path fracture, foot/ankle → AMPUTATION
```

**Chemo** — **VAC: vincristine, Adriamycin (doxorubicin), cyclophosphamide** (± dactinomycin) **alternating with ifosfamide + etoposide (IE)** · neoadjuvant **8–12 wk** → adjuvant **6–12 mo**\
✗ Wrong options: methotrexate-based = OS

**Surgery** — preferred local control when wide margins achievable · **negative margins → 5-yr survival ↑ >10%** · positive margin → re-resect ± RT · recurrence **5–10% with surgery** vs **35% RT alone**

**Reconstruction** — vascularized/non-vascularized autograft (fibula, scapula, iliac crest, rib, clavicle), allograft, APC, endoprosthesis, rotationplasty

**Radiation** — **radiosensitive** (most radio- + chemosensitive bone tumour) but trend **away** from RT · field = tumour + **2–3 cm**, **56–60 Gy** · standard = hyperfractionated · indications: **unresectable**, positive margins, poor chemo response, **all pulmonary mets**

**RT indications (TO)** — **positive margins** · **poor chemo response (<90% necrosis)** · **unresectable** · surgery with **major morbidity (e.g. sacrum)**

**Pathological fracture** — **not an absolute contraindication** to limb salvage (TO)

**Extensive pelvic disease + lung mets** — **chemo + radiation**

**Spine with neuro deficit, resection too morbid** — local control by **radiation**

**Outcomes** — 5-yr **82% (no mets) vs 39% (mets)** · 10-yr 63% vs 32%

### Extraskeletal Ewing sarcoma (TO)

**Histology same as skeletal** ✓ · adolescents/young adults (✗ Wrong options: <10) · **paravertebral muscles, chest wall** (✗ Wrong options: thigh) · t(11;22), MIC2 + · **chemosensitive** (✗ Wrong options: resistant) · same treatment · better prognosis than osseous (OB)

## 6 Complications

**Radiation complications (>60%)** — **LLD**, joint contracture, muscle atrophy, **pathologic fracture**, **secondary sarcoma**

### Radiation-induced sarcoma

**Secondary bone sarcoma** — ~**20% at 20 yr** · in radiation field · dose dependent (**<60 Gy → <5%**) · e.g. lytic lesion 20 yr after RT at age 10\
✗ Wrong options: recurrence · infarct · adenocarcinoma

**AML / myelodysplasia** — **1–2%**, 2–5 yr after chemo (dose-intensive regimens)

### DVT risk

**↑ VTE in paediatric sarcoma** (Ewing) — tumour activates **factor X → Xa** · no consensus on prophylaxis\
✗ Wrong options: haemangioma · MSSA osteomyelitis (MRSA ↑) · NOF · PVNS

**Recurrence** — ~20% localized, >60% metastatic · after recurrence **<10% 5-yr**

**Mets cure** — lung only **30%** · bone only 20% · lung + bone <15%

### Prognostic factors

**Most important** — **metastasis at diagnosis** · lung mets better than bone/marrow · skip mets better than distant

| Poor prognosis | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **Age** | **>10** (TO) · **>14** (Orthobullets) |
| **Size** | **>8 cm** / ≥200 mL |
| **Site** | **Pelvis/spine (worst)** > proximal > distal (best) |
| Chemo response | **<90% necrosis** · surgically treated: necrosis = most important (TO) |
| Labs | **↑ LDH (>200 IU/L)**, ↑ WBC, ↑ ESR, anaemia |
| Clinical | **Fever**, male sex |
| Marrow | Amount of marrow involvement |
| Molecular | **p53 mutation** + t(11;22), Ki-67, HER-2/neu |

**Not prognostic (TO)** — **histological grade** (all Ewing high grade) · t(11;22) presence · female sex is not a bad sign

**Good prognostic sign (TO)** — **age 7 yr**\
✗ Wrong options: 12 cm · fever · TLC 14,000

## X vs Y

| | **Ewing** | **Osteosarcoma** | **Lymphoma of bone** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age | 5–25 | 10–20, >65 | 35–55 |
| Site | Diaphysis/metadiaphysis, pelvis | Metaphysis | Diaphysis |
| X-ray | Permeative, **onion skin** | Blastic, sunburst, Codman | Moth-eaten, little periosteal reaction |
| Histology | Round blue cells | Lacy osteoid | Mixed round cells |
| Markers | **CD99, PAS +, reticulin −** | SATB2 | **LCA (CD45) +, reticulin +, PAS −** |
| Marrow biopsy | **Yes** | No | Yes |
| Chemo | **VAC/IE** | **MAP** | CHOP ± rituximab |
| RT | Radiosensitive | Radioresistant | Radiosensitive |

## How it's asked

**"13-yr-old, mid-femoral lesion, large soft-tissue mass, onion skin?"** → **Ewing sarcoma** **(TO)**

**"8-yr-old, night thigh pain, ↑ WBC + ESR, onion-skin, round blue cells?"** → **Ewing**\
✗ Wrong options: osteomyelitis · OS

**"Round cells EXCEPT?"** → **leiomyosarcoma** (spindle cells)

**"15-yr-old, thoracic small round blue cell tumour, RT for local control considered?"** → **Ewing**\
✗ Wrong options: chordoma · OS · chondrosarcoma (RT inadequate)

**"Immunostain confirming diagnosis?"** → **MIC-2 (CD99) +**\
✗ Wrong options: cytokeratin + · PAS − · reticulin + · vimentin −

**"9-yr-old, monotonous small round blue cells?"** → **Ewing**\
✗ Wrong options: neuroblastoma <5 · lymphoma · small cell lung Ca

**"t(11;22) on FISH — histology?"** → **sheets of small round blue cells, CD99 +**

**"Translocation?"** → **t(11;22)**\
✗ Wrong options: 9;22 · 12;16 · X;18 · 12;22 · 2;13

**"Fusion protein?"** → **EWS–FLI1**\
✗ Wrong options: SYT-SSX · ASPL-TFE3 · EWS-ATF1 · TLS-CHOP

**"Neoplastic properties due to?"** → **chromosomal translocation**

**"Test required for Ewing but not OS?"** → **bone marrow biopsy**

**"Which patient needs marrow biopsy in staging?"** → **5-yr-old, lytic diaphyseal femoral lesion, EWS-FLI1 +**

**"Teen runner, pelvic lesion after failed PT?"** → **MRI of the pelvis**

**"3-yr-old, febrile, normal ESR/CRP, permeative femur?"** → **MRI of the femur**\
✗ Wrong options: biopsy + culture first · antibiotics

**"Stage IIB proximal tibia Ewing?"** → **neoadjuvant chemo → wide resection → adjuvant chemo**\
✗ Wrong options: chemo + RT without surgery

**"Fibula Ewing?"** → **chemo + surgical resection**\
✗ Wrong options: resection alone · RT alone

**"Pelvic Ewing with sciatic symptoms + lung mets?"** → **chemo + radiation**

**"Best-prognosis regimen?"** → **VAC (+ dactinomycin) + wide resection ± RT**\
✗ Wrong options: methotrexate-based

**"Distal fibula Ewing, no other lesions?"** → **surgery + chemo ± RT** — chemo required in all cases

**"14-yr-old, fever, ↑ WBC/ESR, mixed inflammatory infiltrate?"** → **debridement + antibiotics** (osteomyelitis, not Ewing)

**"Lytic lesion 20 yr after RT for childhood Ewing?"** → **radiation-induced sarcoma**

**"Second commonest primary bone malignancy in children?"** → **Ewing**

**"Paediatric condition with ↑ DVT risk?"** → **bone sarcoma with t(11;22)**

**"NOT a bad prognostic sign?"** → **high grade** (all Ewing high grade) (✓ fever, ✓ ↑ LDH, ✓ age >10–12) **(TO)**

**"Good prognostic sign?"** → **age 7 yr** **(TO)**

**"True of Ewing in a 10-yr-old?"** → **raised ESR = worse prognosis** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: predominantly diaphyseal · grade prognostic · appendicular worse

**"True of extraskeletal Ewing?"** → **histology same as skeletal** **(TO)**

**"Ewing with pathological fracture — limb salvage contraindicated?"** → **No, not an absolute contraindication** (TO)
