# Melorheostosis

*Pathology · 36 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Nature** — rare, **benign, painful** developmental dysplasia of cortical bone · formation of **periosteal (or endosteal) new bone** — "flowing hyperostosis"

**Demographics** — **before age 40** · **no sex predilection**

**Genetics** — **non-hereditary**

**Site** — **lower extremities** commoner · any bone (long bones, ulna, hand, foot/metatarsals)

### Genes & lab markers

**ALP** — often **elevated**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **pain**, **reduced ROM**, **joint contractures**, swelling

**Skin** — **fibrosis with induration and erythema** over the lesion

**Incidental** — may be found on unrelated imaging (asymptomatic)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (diagnostic)** — **"dripping candle wax"** — dense **cortical hyperostosis flowing along the cortex** · may **cross joints** → mechanical block

![Dripping candle-wax hyperostosis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8043-x-ray-of-hand-melorheostosis.jpg)

**CT** — non-aggressive sclerosis, may **obliterate the medullary canal**

**Bone scan** — **hot** (↑ osteoblastic activity) — not a sign of malignancy here

### Histology

**Normal Haversian systems**, enlarged trabeculae · bland osteoid · **no atypia, no mitoses**

### Differential

| Lesion | Distinguishing |
|---|---|
| **Melorheostosis** | Flowing candle-wax cortical hyperostosis, bland bone |
| Metastatic prostate cancer | Blastic lesions, carcinoma on histology (↑ PSA is a distractor) |
| Periosteal OS | Surface sunburst, malignant osteoid |
| Spindle cell sarcoma | Malignant spindle cells |
| Stress fracture | Fracture line, callus |

**Association** — melorheostosis can underlie **secondary osteosarcoma** (rare)

## 5 Management

```
Melorheostosis
├─ Asymptomatic / incidental → OBSERVATION (X-ray diagnostic, no biopsy)
├─ Mild symptoms, adequate motion → SYMPTOMATIC CARE + BISPHOSPHONATES (zoledronate)
└─ Severe contracture, immobility, pain → HYPEROSTOTIC BONE RESECTION + CONTRACTURE RELEASE (± osteotomy)
```

**Bisphosphonates** — help **pain and swelling**\
✗ Wrong options: calcitonin · rituximab · tamoxifen (desmoid) · stereotactic radiation

## How it's asked

**"55-yr-old, foot pain, sclerotic 2nd metatarsal, hot scan, bland bone, ↑ PSA?"** → **melorheostosis**\
✗ Wrong options: metastatic prostate · periosteal OS · spindle cell sarcoma · stress fracture

**"21-yr-old, incidental flowing hyperostosis of proximal femur?"** → **observation**\
✗ Wrong options: needle/incisional biopsy · curettage · resection

**"Elbow pain, swelling, ROM 30–90°, candle-wax hyperostosis — beneficial drug?"** → **zoledronate**
