# Telangiectatic Intramedullary Osteosarcoma

*Pathology · 63 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Nature** — rare (**4% of OS**), malignant, **high-grade** OS

**Demographics** — **5–25 yr** · **M > F**

**Site** — **same as ABC** — **proximal humerus, proximal femur, distal femur, proximal tibia** · knee commonest in stems

**Risk factor** — prior **radiation**

### Genes & lab markers

**Tumour suppressors** — **Rb-1**, **p53** · **oncogenes** — **HER2/neu**, **c-myc**, **c-fos**

**Prognostic labs** — ↑ **LDH**, ↑ **ALP** → poor

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** (ABC-like), night pain, poor NSAID relief, limp · swelling, tenderness

**Pathologic fracture** — **25%**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **lytic, destructive, expansile**, eccentric, septae · **cortex may be disrupted** · periosteal reaction possible

![Lytic expansile proximal humeral lesion](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8017-case-a-prox-hum-xray-parsons-moved.png)

**MRI (all cases)** — **fluid-fluid levels** (less defined than ABC) · **extensive surrounding oedema** · soft-tissue component

![Fluid-fluid levels with oedema](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8017-case-c-distal-femur-mri-ax-parsons-moved.jpg)

**Bone scan** — ↑ uptake

### Histology

**Lakes of blood mixed with malignant cells** (not in ABC) · high-grade cells with **mitoses** in intervening cellular septa · **less osteoid** than conventional OS · gross: **"bag of blood"** · experienced MSK pathologist essential

![Blood lakes with malignant cells](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8017-histology-a-parsons-moved.png)

### ABC vs telangiectatic OS

| | **ABC** | **Telangiectatic OS** |
|---|---|---|
| Age · site | **75% <20** · same four sites | 5–25 · same four sites |
| X-ray | Expansile, eccentric, lytic, septae, **cortical thinning** | Same, **possible cortical disruption** |
| MRI | **Clear** fluid levels, may extend to soft tissue | Fluid levels **less defined**, **surrounding oedema** |
| Histology | Blood spaces, spindle cells, **benign giant cells, no malignant cells** | **Blood lakes with malignant cells + mitoses** |
| Treatment | **Curettage + graft** | **Chemo + wide resection + chemo** |

**Other distractors** — GCT (females, 3rd–5th decade, curettage) · chondroblastoma (epiphyseal, cobblestone chondroblasts) · UBC (aspiration + steroid)

## 5 Management

**Neoadjuvant chemo (8–12 wk) → wide resection (limb salvage) → adjuvant chemo (6–12 mo)** · as conventional OS

**Agents** — **doxorubicin (adriamycin), cisplatin, methotrexate, ifosfamide**

**Limb salvage** — possible in **~90%**

**✗ Wrong options** — wide resection alone · curettage + graft (ABC) · RT · steroid injection · observation · hip disarticulation first

## 6 Complications

**Local recurrence** — **~5%** · poor prognosis · ↑ with **displaced pathologic fracture**

**Good** — **≥98% necrosis** after chemo · good response + wide margins ↓ recurrence

**Poor** — **advanced stage (most important)** · ↑ LDH, ↑ ALP · **MDR gene** (25% primaries, 50% mets)

**Survival** — **more chemosensitive, same survival** as intramedullary OS · localized **~70%** · metastatic **~20%**

## How it's asked

**"21-yr-old proximal tibial mass, blood lakes with malignant cells — first step after staging?"** → **neoadjuvant chemotherapy**\
✗ Wrong options: wide excision first · curettage · RT

**"14-yr-old knee night pain, expansile lesion with blood lakes + atypia?"** → **osteosarcoma — neoadjuvant chemo + wide resection**\
✗ Wrong options: ABC (wide + RT) · GCT · wide resection alone

**"18-yr-old femur, poorly defined fluid levels, blood with mitoses + osteoid?"** → **neoadjuvant chemo → wide resection → adjuvant chemo**\
✗ Wrong options: curettage · steroid injection
