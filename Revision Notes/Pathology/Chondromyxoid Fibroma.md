# Chondromyxoid Fibroma

*Pathology · 9 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rare** — **<1%** of bone tumours · benign but painful, **locally aggressive**

**Demographics** — **males** · **2nd–3rd decades** (10–30) · up to 75 yr

**Site** — **metaphysis** of long bones · **proximal tibia** commonest (around knee: proximal tibia, proximal fibula, distal femur) · **pelvis** (ilium) · **foot/ankle**, hands, wrist (distal radius)

**Origin** — may arise from **physeal remnants**

### Genes & lab markers

**Chromosome 6 (q13)** — genetic rearrangement

## 3 Presentation

**History** — **long-standing pain, months–years** · may be **incidental**

**Symptoms** — pain + mild swelling · activity pain → rest pain

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **lytic, eccentric, metaphyseal** · sharply demarcated, **narrow zone of transition** · **scalloped sclerotic rim** · lobulated / bubbly · cortical expansion · **calcification rare** · 2–10 cm

![Eccentric lytic metaphyseal lesion with sclerotic rim](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8022-case-d-tibia-xray-b-parsons-moved.png)

**vs NOF** — radiographically often indistinguishable · CMF **larger** and **active past skeletal maturity**

**MRI** — **T1 low**, **T2 high**, lobular growth · distinct margin

**CT** — low attenuation, peripheral bone rim, subtle matrix

**Bone scan** — ↑ uptake

### Histology (diagnostic criteria)

**Histology mandatory** for diagnosis

**Low power — biphasic** — **hypercellular** lobules of fibromyxoid tissue (spindle/stellate cells, hyperchromatic nuclei; giant cells + fibrovascular tissue between lobules) + **hypocellular chondroid** areas

**High power** — myxoid stroma with **stellate cells** · may have pleomorphic bizarre nuclei

![Stellate cells in myxoid stroma](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8022-stellate-cells-moved.jpg)

### Differential

| Radiographic DD | Histologic DD |
|---|---|
| ABC · chondroblastoma · **NOF** | Chondroblastoma · enchondroma · **chondrosarcoma** |

| Lesion | Distinguishing feature |
|---|---|
| **CMF** | **Stellate cells**, spindle cells, myxoid-cartilaginous matrix |
| NOF | **Storiform** bland fibroblasts, scattered giant cells |
| Enchondroma | Calcifications · bland hyaline cartilage, pyknotic nuclei |
| GCT | Radiolucent, not bubbly · giant-cell nuclei = stromal nuclei |
| Chondrosarcoma (conventional) | **Older (50+)** · uncommon in hands/feet · cortical erosion, soft-tissue mass |
| Atypical cartilaginous tumour | (= grade 1 extremity chondrosarcoma, WHO) · like large enchondroma + endosteal scalloping |

## 5 Management

**Intralesional curettage + bone grafting (or PMMA)** — **mainstay**

**Not indicated** — observation (painful) · chemo + wide excision · radiotherapy · radical resection

## 6 Complications

**Local recurrence** — **~25%** (Orthobullets) · 20–30% · 20–25% (OB)

**Negative prognostic factors** — **children** · **more lobulated, abundant myxoid** tumour

**Metastasis** — **not reported**

## X vs Y

| | **CMF** | **Chondroblastoma** |
|---|---|---|
| Site | **Metaphysis** (proximal tibia) | **Epiphysis** |
| Histology | **Stellate cells**, lobules of fibromyxoid tissue | Polygonal chondroblasts, **chicken-wire** Ca |
| Calcification | Rare | Common (CT) |
| Lung mets | **None reported** | <1% |
| Treatment | Curettage + graft / PMMA | Curettage + adjuvant + graft / RFA |

## How it's asked

**"Adolescent wrist/foot pain, eccentric lytic metaphyseal lesion with sclerotic rim, spindle/stellate cells in myxoid matrix?"** → **chondromyxoid fibroma**\
✗ Wrong options: NOF (storiform) · enchondroma · chondrosarcoma · osteosarcoma

**"21-yr-old, large lobulated eccentric metaphyseal lesion with sclerotic rim?"** → **CMF**\
✗ Wrong options: NOF · ABC · atypical cartilaginous tumour

**"Ilium lesion, biphasic histology with stellate cells — treatment?"** → **curettage + bone grafting**\
✗ Wrong options: chemo + wide excision · radiotherapy

**"Recurrence rate?"** → **~25%** · higher in children
