# Biopsy Principles

*Pathology · 4 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Purpose & prerequisites

**Purpose** — **confirm a suspected diagnosis** · not a substitute for history, exam, labs, imaging

**Prerequisites** — **CBC, platelets, coagulation** · **cross-sectional imaging** of local anatomy · **staging before biopsy** (new lesion + prior cancer → CT chest/abdomen/pelvis + bone scan)

**Who** — the **treating (definitive) surgeon / centre** capable of diagnosis + treatment · biopsy tract resected in continuity with tumour

**Golden rule** — all tissue exposed, and any **haematoma**, is **contaminated** → must be resected at definitive surgery

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Indications for biopsy (criteria)

| Biopsy indicated | Biopsy not needed |
|---|---|
| **Aggressive** bone or soft-tissue lesion | Asymptomatic **latent** or symptomatic active bone lesion that looks **entirely benign** |
| Soft tissue **>5 cm**, **deep to fascia**, or over bone / NV structures | Soft-tissue lesion **completely benign on MRI** (lipoma, haemangioma) |
| Unclear diagnosis in a symptomatic patient · indeterminate MRI | |
| **Solitary bone lesion with history of carcinoma** | |

### Types of biopsy

| Type | Specimen | Use / fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Fine needle aspiration (FNA/FNAC)** | **Cytology only** (separated cells) · no architecture or stroma | **Carcinoma** · not **sarcoma** · **no role in bone tumours** (paucicellular, no IHC, done by pathologist unaware of incisions) **(TO)** · high false-negative |
| **Core (Tru-cut)** | Cytology **+ stroma / architecture** | **Soft-tissue sarcoma** · **85–95% accurate** · CT-guided core increasingly first-line |
| **Incisional (open)** | Large sample | **Lowest non-diagnostic rate** · after non-diagnostic core |
| **Excisional** | Whole lesion | **Small (<3 cm), superficial** (to fascia) soft-tissue masses only |

**Open vs needle** — open → more contamination → **higher local recurrence after definitive surgery** **(TO)**

**Never divide the sample** — heterogeneous tumours → discordant grades · for second opinion, review slides/blocks **(TO)**

**Frozen section** — confirms **adequate viable tissue** · definitive diagnosis not required at that time · send sample for **culture** too

## 5 Management

### Open incisional biopsy technique

| Step | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Incision** | **Longitudinal** in extremity, in line with future resection, no larger than needed | Not transverse |
| **Plane** | **Intramuscular** — through **one muscle belly / involved compartment** · biopsy the soft-tissue mass of a bone lesion | Avoid **intermuscular / internervous** planes (contaminate 2 compartments) · do not **raise flaps** **(TO)** |
| **Structures** | Stay away from NV bundle | Do not **expose neurovascular structures**, violate joint or go through quadriceps / **rectus femoris** (morbid resection) |
| **Bone** | **Oval** cortical window | |
| **Tourniquet** | **May be used** · release before closure | No **compressive exsanguination** |
| **Haemostasis** | **Meticulous** · close pseudocapsule | Haematoma = contaminated |
| **Drain** | **Exit in line with incision**, close to it | No multiple drains, no drain away from incision |

### Biopsy approach by site

| Site | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Distal femur** | **Through vastus medialis or vastus lateralis** (posteromedial/posterolateral border) — medial **or** lateral acceptable |
| Proximal humerus | **Anterior deltoid** (not deltopectoral) |
| Distal humerus | Lateral triceps / brachialis |
| Wrist | Dorsal 2nd compartment |
| Iliac wing | Through superior abductors |
| Supra-acetabular | Smith-Petersen through TFL / rectus femoris |
| Femoral head / neck | **Transtrochanteric** osseous approach |
| Tibia | **Subcutaneous** border |
| Posterior thigh mass | **Direct posterior**, drain in line |

### Non-diagnostic biopsy & referral

```
Suspected sarcoma / aggressive lesion
├─ Community hospital → REFER TO ORTHOPAEDIC ONCOLOGIST before biopsy
├─ Deep soft-tissue mass → CORE NEEDLE (CT-guided) ± CULTURE
│      └─ Non-diagnostic (necrosis) → OPEN BIOPSY, same intramuscular tract
└─ New bone lesion + prior cancer → RESTAGE, then BIOPSY
       (no nail / hemiarthroplasty / radiation before tissue diagnosis)
```

**Repeat core** — allowed but **not in a new direction** (contaminates another compartment)

**Chronic destructive monoarthritis** (endemic TB region) — **open biopsy** of joint + bone for **histology (granulomas) + AFB/fungal culture**\
✗ Wrong options: empirical antibiotics · steroid trial · fusion first

**Expanding mass with fluid + solid parts** — think **sarcoma**, not haematoma → **core biopsy**\
✗ Wrong options: compression · IR drain · excise first

## 6 Complications

### Poorly performed biopsy consequences

**Bad biopsy** — multiple tracts + **haematoma** spreading through compartments → **limb salvage impossible** → **chemotherapy + amputation** (osteosarcoma)

**Radiation** — no: osteosarcoma is radio-resistant

## How it's asked

**"True statement about bone tumour biopsy?"** → **open biopsy → higher local recurrence than needle** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: divide sample · raise flaps · FNAC preferred

**"Important factor in a proper biopsy?"** → **longitudinal incision**\
✗ Wrong options: intermuscular planes · multiple drains · expose NV structures · avoid tourniquet

**"Principle that does NOT apply?"** → **"tourniquet should not be used"** — tourniquet allowed, no exsanguination

**"Distal femur biopsy approach?"** → **through vastus medialis or lateralis (intramuscular)**\
✗ Wrong options: rectus femoris · VM–RF interval · VL–biceps interval · posterior

**"Lateral distal femur lesion?"** → **lateral longitudinal intramuscular**\
✗ Wrong options: intermuscular · arthroscopic

**"Proximal calf deep mass?"** → **longitudinal incision through the involved (medial) compartment**

**"Where to put the drain?"** → **in line with the incision**

**"Core biopsy shows necrosis only — next?"** → **open biopsy through the same lateral intramuscular tract**\
✗ Wrong options: FNA · core in new direction · resection

**"Lung-cancer survivor, new subtrochanteric lesion?"** → **biopsy**\
✗ Wrong options: prophylactic nail · radiation

**"Destructive humeral lesion at a community hospital?"** → **refer to orthopaedic oncologist**\
✗ Wrong options: biopsy there

**"Painless growing thigh mass, sarcoma-like MRI — initial management?"** → **CT-guided core needle biopsy** (+ CT chest/abdomen/pelvis)

**"High-grade OS after two outside biopsies + leg haematoma?"** → **chemotherapy + amputation**
