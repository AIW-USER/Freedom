# Periosteal Osteosarcoma

*Pathology · 53 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Nature** — extremely rare **intermediate-grade surface** osteosarcoma

**Demographics** — **15–25 yr** · **F > M**

**Site** — **diaphysis** of long bones · **femur, tibia**

### Genetics (p53, RB, Li-Fraumeni)

**p53 germ-line mutation** — **15–20%** of periosteal OS

**Li-Fraumeni** — **AD**, germ-line **p53** inactivation · breast, colon cancers, sarcomas — **50% are osteosarcoma** → **OS = commonest bone tumour with p53**\
✗ Wrong options: chondrosarcoma (p53 = aggressive behaviour) · Ewing (EWSR1–ETS) · myeloma

**Sporadic OS** — p53 mutation in **15–30%**

**Retinoblastoma (RB1)** — hereditary (bilateral) retinoblastoma → OS · RB1 linkage analysis identifies at-risk relatives

### Genes & lab markers

| Finding | Tumour |
|---|---|
| **RB + p53 alterations** | **Conventional high-grade OS** |
| **Supernumerary ring chromosomes** | Parosteal OS |
| **t(11;22)** | Ewing |
| **t(X;18)** | Synovial sarcoma |
| **Telomeric associations** | GCT of bone |

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — commonest symptom · **pathologic fracture 25%**

**Exam** — regional swelling, tenderness · may present after a minor twist

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **"sunburst" / "hair-on-end"** periosteal reaction, often in a **saucerized cortical depression** · **no medullary involvement** typically

![Sunburst periosteal reaction](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8016-osteosarcoma-radiograph-sunburst-periosteal-reaction.jpg)

**Staging** — **CT chest** (lung mets) + **bone scan** (very hot) required

### Workup — MRI of entire bone

**Bone-forming lesion, indistinct margins** → **MRI of the entire bone ASAP** before biopsy · biopsy by the treating surgeon\
✗ Wrong options: biopsy first · amputation · observation · NSAIDs

### Histology

**Grossly lobular, cartilaginous** · **osteoid + chondroblastic matrix** · **no osteoid → chondrosarcoma**

![Chondroblastic matrix with osteoid](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8016-histology-d-moved.jpg)

### Surface OS compared

| | **Parosteal OS** | **Periosteal OS** |
|---|---|---|
| Grade | Low | **Intermediate** |
| Age · sex | 30–40 · F | **15–25** · F |
| Site | Posterior distal femoral **metaphysis** | Femur/tibia **diaphysis** |
| X-ray | **Stuck-on**, lobulated, ossified | **Sunburst** in saucerized cortex |
| Histology | Spindle cells between regular trabeculae | **Chondroblastic** + osteoid |
| Genes | **MDM2**, ring chromosome | **p53** (15–20%) |
| Treatment | **Wide resection alone** | **Chemo + wide resection + chemo** |

**Other distractors** — osteochondroma (medullary continuity, not aggressive) · dedifferentiated CS (from prior cartilage tumour) · synovial sarcoma (soft tissue, spindle cells, may calcify)

### Conventional OS distractors (same module)

| Lesion | Histology |
|---|---|
| **Osteosarcoma** | **Osteoid from pleomorphic / malignant spindle cells** · Codman triangle, soft-tissue mass |
| Ewing | Uniform **small blue round cells** (reactive osteoid not prominent) |
| Osteomyelitis | Inflammatory cells, dead bone, no mass |
| EG | Histiocytes + eosinophils, rarely mass |
| MFH of bone | Lytic, **storiform**, no osteoid |
| **Osteoblastoma** | Benign stromal cells |
| Stress fracture | **T1 MRI fracture line**, no mass |

## 5 Management

### Treatment — chemotherapy & resection

**Same as intramedullary OS** — **neoadjuvant multi-agent chemo (8–12 wk) → wide resection (limb salvage) → maintenance chemo (6–12 mo)**

**Limb salvage** — allograft / megaprosthesis · rotationplasty · amputation if needed

**✗ Wrong options** — radiation (OS is not radiosensitive) · chemo alone · surgery alone · resection before chemo

## 6 Complications

**Pulmonary mets** — **20–35%**

**Prognosis** — **intermediate** between parosteal and intramedullary OS

**Good sign** — **90–98% necrosis** after neoadjuvant chemo

**Bad sign** — **MDR gene** (pumps chemo out) · in **25% of primaries, 50% of mets**

## How it's asked

**"47-yr-old knee pain/swelling, surface lesion, osteoid + cartilage?"** → **periosteal OS**\
✗ Wrong options: chondrosarcoma · Ewing · GCT · FD

**"24-yr-old softball twist, sunburst surface ossification?"** → **periosteal OS**\
✗ Wrong options: parosteal (stuck-on) · osteochondroma · dedifferentiated CS · synovial sarcoma

**"16-yr-old distal femoral bone-forming lesion, pleomorphic osteoid?"** → **osteosarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: Ewing · rhabdomyosarcoma · MFH

**"10-yr-old distal femur, destructive bone-forming, malignant spindle cells?"** → **osteosarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: osteoblastoma · Ewing

**"10-yr-old distal tibia, Codman triangle, soft-tissue mass?"** → **osteosarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: EG · osteomyelitis · trauma

**"Li-Fraumeni (p53) — commonest primary bone tumour?"** → **osteosarcoma**

**"9-yr-old, retinoblastoma, mother bilateral RB, humeral OS — treatment?"** → **chemotherapy + wide resection**\
✗ Wrong options: RT

**"16-yr-old conventional OS — genetic feature?"** → **RB + p53 alterations**\
✗ Wrong options: ring chromosomes · t(11;22) · t(X;18) · telomeric

**"13-yr-old high-grade OS — treatment?"** → **neoadjuvant chemo → resection + reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: resection then chemo · RT

**"10-yr-old proximal tibial OS?"** → **surgery + chemotherapy**

**"10-yr-old humeral OS?"** → **chemo + limb-sparing surgery with reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: amputation alone · RT

**"15-yr-old, indistinct bone-forming knee lesion — next?"** → **MRI of the entire bone**\
✗ Wrong options: biopsy first
