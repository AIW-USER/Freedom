# Intramuscular Myxomas

*Pathology · 28 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Nature** — **benign** soft-tissue tumour (mesenchymal) · **no malignant potential** · slow-growing, deep, **confined within skeletal muscle**

**Demographics** — **40–60** (40–70) · slight **female** predilection

**Site** — **thigh**, shoulder, buttock, upper arm, calf

**Origin** — primitive mesenchymal cells → benign fibroblasts that **lose the capacity to secrete collagen**

### Associations

**Mazabraud syndrome** — **multiple intramuscular myxomas + (monostotic or polyostotic) fibrous dysplasia**

**Myxoid liposarcoma** — main differential

## 3 Presentation

**Slowly growing mass** · painful or painless · firm, mobile · deep to fascia

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**MRI** — **homogeneous** · **T1 dark**, **T2 bright** (myxoid) · **intramuscular** location · well circumscribed, no oedema or NV encasement · **rind of fat** at ≥1 pole (bright T1)

![Homogeneous intramuscular myxoma on MRI](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8080-mri-intramuscular-myxoma.jpg)

### Histology

**Bland, hypocellular myxoid stroma** · undifferentiated **stellate cells** with thin collagen fibres · **no atypia**, low N:C ratio, **no atypical mitoses**

### Differential

| Lesion | Distinguishing |
|---|---|
| **Intramuscular myxoma** | **Intra**muscular, homogeneous, fat rind, little enhancement |
| **Myxoid liposarcoma** | **Inter**muscular · **intense diffuse enhancement** · "**pulmonary oedema**" pattern, lipoblasts, chicken-wire vessels |
| **Neurofibroma** | Follows nerve — **entering-and-exiting nerve sign** · ovoid/spindle cells in myxoid stroma |
| Ganglion | Dense fibrous wall around myxoid material, joint origin |
| Metastasis | Soft-tissue mets unusual |

## 5 Management

**Observation** — asymptomatic

**Marginal excision** — **symptomatic** lesions · **no neoadjuvant chemo/RT** · recurrence + metastasis uncommon\
✗ Wrong options: observation · PT when symptomatic

### Excision margin terms

| Margin | Plane | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Intralesional** | Within pseudocapsule — highest recurrence | Select benign lesions |
| **Marginal** | Through **reactive zone / capsule** | ✓ **Intramuscular myxoma** |
| **Wide** | Cuff of normal tissue | Sarcomas (**myxofibrosarcoma**), **solitary fibrous tumour** |
| **Radical** | Whole compartment | Highly aggressive, no limb-sparing margin |

**Not marginal excision** — ABC (curettage + adjuvant) · desmoid (often non-operative, infiltrative)

## How it's asked

**"40-yr-old, hip/thigh pain, homogeneous intramuscular T2-bright mass, bland myxoid histology?"** → **surgical (marginal) excision**\
✗ Wrong options: observation · neoadjuvant chemo · adjuvant RT

**"56-yr-old, slowly enlarging medial thigh mass, history of thyroid cancer?"** → **intramuscular myxoma**\
✗ Wrong options: myxoid liposarcoma · neurofibroma · ganglion · metastasis

**"Benign tumour treated with marginal excision?"** → **intramuscular myxoma**\
✗ Wrong options: desmoid · myxofibrosarcoma · solitary fibrous tumour

**"Multiple myxomas + fibrous dysplasia?"** → **Mazabraud syndrome**
