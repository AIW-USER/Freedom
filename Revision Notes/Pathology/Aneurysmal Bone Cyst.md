# Aneurysmal Bone Cyst

*Pathology · 2 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Age** — **75% <20 yr** · first 2–3 decades

**Site** — long bones **>60%** (**femur, tibia** commonest) · **metaphysis** · spine **15%** (**posterior elements** — lamina, spinous process) · pelvis posterior elements

**Foot** — **metatarsal** (metaphysis) and **calcaneus** commonest (✗ Wrong options: calcaneal UBC = incidental)

**Nature** — benign, **locally aggressive/destructive**, may extend into soft tissue · same sites as **telangiectatic OS**

### Primary vs secondary

**Primary ABC** — **neoplasm** · USP6 rearrangement

**Secondary ABC** — **not a neoplasm** (no translocation) · arises in another tumour **~30%**: **GCT**, **chondroblastoma**, fibrous dysplasia, chondromyxoid fibroma, NOF

### Genes & lab markers — USP6

**Gene** — **USP6 (TRE2/TRE17) on 17p13** ↑ (ubiquitin-specific protease) **(TO)**

**Translocation** — **t(16;17)(q22;p13)** → **CDH11 promoter** (16q22) drives USP6

**Frequency** — "invariably" found → neoplastic origin **(TO)** · only **~70%** show USP6 ↑ (OB) — read the options

**Key use** — USP6 ↑ separates ABC from **telangiectatic OS** (no USP6) and GCT

| Gene / translocation | Tumour (distractors) |
|---|---|
| **USP6 – CDH11** | ✓ **ABC** |
| ASPL–TFE3 t(X;17) | Alveolar soft part sarcoma |
| EWS–ATF1 t(12;22) | Clear cell sarcoma (OB text: "clear cell chondrosarcoma") |
| EWS–FLI1 t(11;22) | Ewing sarcoma |
| EWS–WT1 t(11;22) | Desmoplastic small round cell tumour |
| EWS–CHN t(9;22) | Extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma |
| PAX3–FKHR t(2;13) | Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **pain + swelling**, slow-growing · may present with **pathologic fracture**

**Spine** — neurologic deficit · even **cauda equina** (posterior-element ABC)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **expansile, eccentric, lytic, metaphyseal** · **bony septae ("bubbly")** · **thin rim of periosteal new bone** · **no matrix**

![Expansile septated lytic calcaneal lesion](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8036-case-c-calc-xray-parsons-moved.png)

**Width** — can expand **wider than the physis** ✓ (UBC and GCT not wider than physis)

**MRI / CT** — **multiple fluid–fluid levels** (best on axial/sagittal **T2**) · may expand into soft tissue

![Fluid–fluid levels on MRI](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8036-case-c-calc-mri-b-parsons-moved.gif)

**Fluid levels ≠ diagnostic** — also in **GCT, chondroblastoma, telangiectatic OS**, vascular tumours **(TO)** · primary ABC = fluid levels **throughout, no solid component** **(TO)**

**Spine X-ray** — **"winking owl" sign** (loss of pedicle) → **order MRI**\
✗ Wrong options: scoliosis curve alone · Scheuermann kyphosis alone

![Winking owl sign — absent pedicle](../../MCQ/attachments/q215565-illa-winking-owl.jpg)

### Histology

**Lakes of blood** — **cavernous blood-filled spaces without endothelial lining**

**Cyst walls** — **benign giant cells** + **spindle cells** · thin strands of **woven bone** · histiocytes, hemosiderin · villous projections, clefts

![Blood-filled spaces without endothelial lining](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8036-4b-moved.jpg)

**Biopsy** — required to confirm (needle biopsy acceptable)

## 5 Management

### Treatment algorithm

```
Aneurysmal bone cyst
├─ Acute pathologic fracture → NONOPERATIVE FRACTURE CARE
│      └─ Healed, cyst persists/grows → CURETTAGE + GRAFT
├─ Symptomatic, no fracture → EXTENDED CURETTAGE + ADJUVANT + GRAFT
├─ Expendable bone (fibula, rib) → RESECTION
└─ Spine with neural compression → MARGINAL RESECTION
       (avoid wide resection — risks permanent neuro injury)
```

### Curettage & adjuvants

**Standard** — **extended (aggressive) curettage + bone grafting** (allograft / substitute) **(TO — FNB 2019)**\
✗ Wrong options: aspiration + methylprednisolone · curettage + cement · aspiration + autograft

**Adjuvants** — **phenol**, **liquid nitrogen**, **argon beam**, **high-speed burr**, hydrogen peroxide, bone cement → kill residual cells, ↓ recurrence

**Sclerotherapy** — **3% polidocanol** via J-needle (aspirate, break septa, scrape wall) → "current standard of care, surpassing extended curettage" **(TO)** · OB: curettage + burr + adjuvant **or** polidocanol both suitable (>90% cure) — read the options

**Intralesional calcitonin + methylprednisolone** — high healing rates reported **(TO)**

**Embolization** — **spine and pelvis** (limited access) **(TO)** · preop → ↓ blood loss · alone may resolve lesion

**Radionuclide ablation** — evidence for axial ABCs

**Proximal femur, painful** — **curettage + grafting** (structural support)\
✗ Wrong options: observation → fracture risk · denosumab (GCT only) · proximal femoral replacement

### Not used

**Chemotherapy** — no role

**Radiation** — seldom used · **malignant transformation**, damage to physes, gonads, cord

**Phenol alone** — not used (adjuvant only) · **RFA / observation** — not for this (osteoid osteoma)

**Wide resection + chemo** — not for this (osteosarcoma) · wide/en bloc resection nearly eliminates recurrence but sacrifices function → reserve for expendable bones

## 6 Complications

### Recurrence risk factors

**Local recurrence** — **5–50%** · up to **25%** (Orthobullets) · spine 25–30%

**Risk factors** — ✓ **young age**, ✓ **open physes**, stage, type of removal / margin

**Not a factor** — chemo, radiation sensitivity, graft material, tumour necrosis (OS prognosis), giant cells, embolization

## X vs Y

| | **ABC** | **UBC** | **Telangiectatic OS** | **GCT** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Age | <20 | Child, open physis | Adolescent | **Skeletally mature** 20–40 |
| Site | **Eccentric metaphysis** | **Central** metaphysis, prox humerus / femur, calcaneus | Metaphysis | **Epiphysis**–metaphysis |
| Width | **Wider than physis** | Not wider than physis | — | Not wider than physis |
| MRI | Fluid levels, no solid part | Single fluid, no levels | Fluid levels + **solid nodular** part | ± secondary ABC |
| Histology | Lakes of blood, benign giant cells | Thin fibrous lining, giant cells, hemosiderin | Lakes of blood + **pleomorphic malignant cells, osteoid** | Giant-cell nuclei = stromal nuclei |
| USP6 | Yes | No | No | No |
| Treatment | Curettage + adjuvant + graft | Steroid injection / observe | **Chemo + wide resection** | Curettage + adjuvant · denosumab |

## How it's asked

**"Expansile eccentric metaphyseal lesion, fluid–fluid levels, lakes of blood — diagnosis?"** → **ABC**\
✗ Wrong options: GCT despite giant cells · UBC · NOF

**"Gene in ABC aetiology?"** → **USP6 (17p13)**\
✗ Wrong options: ASPL · ATF1 · EWS · PAX3

**"True of primary ABC?"** → **USP6 rearrangement on chromosome 17** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: posterior vertebral body · fluid levels confirm diagnosis

**"Management of ABC (FNB)?"** → **extended curettage + bone grafting** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: aspiration + methylprednisolone

**"ABC with acute pathologic fracture — next step?"** → **nonoperative fracture management**\
✗ Wrong options: ORIF · open biopsy

**"Spinal ABC with cauda equina in a child?"** → **marginal resection**\
✗ Wrong options: wide resection · aspiration + steroid

**"Large ABC of the fibula?"** → **resection alone**\
✗ Wrong options: + chemo/radiation

**"Pubic ramus ABC treatment?"** → **surgery alone** (± embolization)

**"Factor associated with local recurrence?"** → **open physes / young age**\
✗ Wrong options: chemo response · graft type

**"Spine X-ray needing MRI?"** → **winking owl sign**\
✗ Wrong options: scoliosis curve · Scheuermann

**"Curettage + grafting is NOT used for?"** → **osteofibrous dysplasia** (✓ for GCT, ABC, chondroblastoma, CMF)
