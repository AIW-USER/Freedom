# Radiation Therapy

*Pathology · 57 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Mechanisms** — **free radical production** · **direct genetic (DNA) damage**

### Radiosensitive tumours

| Use | Tumours |
|---|---|
| **Definitive control** (primary bone) | **Ewing / PNET** · **primary lymphoma of bone** · **haemangioendothelioma** · **solitary plasmacytoma** |
| **First-line RT alone (spinal mets — OB)** | **Leukaemia, lymphoma, myeloma/plasmacytoma, haemangioendothelioma, Ewing** |
| **Adjuvant to surgery** | **Soft-tissue sarcomas** (pre- or post-op) |
| **Palliation / after fixation** | **Metastatic bone disease** — after fixing impending/pathologic fractures |

| Metastasis sensitivity | |

| **Prostate** | **Very radiosensitive** |

**Radioresistant (OB)** — **fibrosarcoma, chondrosarcoma, melanoma, angiosarcoma** · osteosarcoma (not radiosensitive — OS chapters)

## 5 Management

### Technique

**1 rad = 1 cGy** · typical **180–200 cGy/day** in **fractions** · RT is **cumulative** (total = sum of fractions)

| Total dose | Tissue healing |
|---|---|
| **<45 Gy** | Usually uncomplicated |
| **45–55 Gy** | Heals, with problems |
| **>60 Gy** | **Unlikely to heal** |

### Preoperative vs postoperative radiation

| | **Pre-op (neoadjuvant)** | **Post-op (adjuvant)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Wound complications** | **More (up to 40%)** | Less (<20%) |
| **Oedema, fibrosis, stiffness** | **Less (<15%)** | More (>20%, often temporary) |
| Lymphoedema · fracture | **Lower** | Higher |
| Field · dose | **Smaller field**, lower dose | Larger field, higher dose |
| Pseudocapsule | **Forms** → close-margin resection | No |
| Tumour shrinkage | **Yes** | No |
| Local recurrence | **Lower** (Orthobullets) · **no difference** (OB6336) | Higher (Orthobullets) |
| Overall survival | No difference by timing | No difference |

## 6 Complications

### Radiation complications (fracture, secondary sarcoma)

**Early** — **delayed wound healing**, **infection**, **desquamation**

**Late** — **fibrosis**, **joint stiffness**, **secondary sarcoma**, **fractures**

**Post-radiation fracture** — **~25%** after STS resection + EBRT

| Fracture risk factor | Yes / No |
|---|---|
| **Dose ≥59 Gy** | Yes |
| **Weight-bearing bone, esp. femur** | Yes |
| **Female** | Yes |
| Volume of bone irradiated | Yes |
| **Anterior femoral compartment resection** | Yes |
| **Age** | Yes |
| **Periosteal stripping** (→ consider prophylactic fixation) | Yes |
| Osteoporosis | Yes |
| **Method of RT administration** | No |

**Post-radiation sarcoma** — sarcoma in a previously irradiated field · **~13%** · ↑ with prior chemo · **very poor prognosis**

**Criteria** — **≥5 yr** after RT (range **3–50 yr**) · **in the RT field** · **different histology** from the original disease

## How it's asked

**"Pre-op vs post-op RT — higher with pre-op?"** → **wound necrosis**\
✗ Wrong options: fibrosis · lymphoedema (post-op) · local recurrence (no difference)

**"Synovial sarcoma (SS18-SSX), pre-op RT (A) vs post-op RT (B)?"** → **A: more wound complications, less fibrosis**

**"NOT a risk factor for post-RT fracture?"** → **method of RT administration** (✓ female, ✓ age, ✓ dose, ✓ periosteal resection)

**"47-yr-old, clavicle lytic lesion, mantle RT 22 yr ago, atypia + bone formation?"** → **radiation-associated sarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: osteonecrosis · osteomyelitis

**"L4–5 lytic metastases — RT first-line for?"** → **lymphoma**\
✗ Wrong options: fibrosarcoma · chondrosarcoma · melanoma · angiosarcoma
