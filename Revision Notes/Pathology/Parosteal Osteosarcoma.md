# Parosteal Osteosarcoma

*Pathology · 51 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Nature** — **low-grade**, well-differentiated **surface** osteosarcoma · **<5%** (OB7521) / **~5%** (OB8282) of osteosarcomas

**Demographics** — **females > males** · **30–40 yr** (3rd–4th decades) · range in stems 20–42

**Site** — **surface of metaphysis** · **posterior distal femur** > proximal tibia > proximal humerus · **80% femur** (Orthobullets) · posterior distal femur **~75%** (OB7488) / **80%** (OB4407)

**Marrow invasion** — **25%** · does not affect long-term survival

### Genes & lab markers

**MDM2 amplification** + **ring chromosome** — high rate

## 3 Presentation

**Painless mass** · slow-growing, firm, mild/no pain · long history (>3 yr)

**↓ Knee flexion / tightness** — large posterior distal femoral lesion · tenderness on deep posterior palpation · effusion

**Labs** — ESR, CBC normal

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **heavily ossified, lobulated mass arising from cortex**, **"stuck-on"** the metaphyseal surface over a broad area · **cloud-like** ossification · may be multifocal

![Lobulated ossified mass stuck on posterior distal femur](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8015-case-a-posterior-femur-xray-parsons-moved.png)

**MRI** — **mandatory**, whole bone · soft-tissue/marrow involvement, skip lesions · **very dark** signal (heavy mineralization)

**CT chest** — **mandatory** staging (lung mets)

**Bone scan** — **mandatory** · **always hot**

**Angiography** — finds **hypervascular dedifferentiated areas** → target biopsy

**Next step** — **biopsy** after staging\
✗ Wrong options: arthroscopy · bisphosphonate · chemo/RT first

### Histology

**Low grade** — **regularly arranged normal osseous trabeculae** · **atypical spindle cells between trabeculae** = most prominent feature · bland fibroblastic stroma · some atypia needed for diagnosis

**Pitfall** — mistaken for **fibrous dysplasia** histologically (X-ray differs)

![Spindle cells between regular trabeculae](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8015-parosteal-osteosarcoma-histology.jpg)

### Differential

| | Bone surface lesion | Similar histology | Surgery alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Parosteal OS** | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| **Periosteal OS** | ✓ | — | — |
| **Periosteal chondroma** | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| **Osteochondroma / MHE** | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| Developmental defect (adductor magnus insertion) | ✓ | — | — |
| **Myositis ossificans** | ✓ | — | — |
| **Chondrosarcoma** · **adamantinoma** | — | — | ✓ |
| **Chordoma** | — | — | ✓ (controversial) |
| **FD** | — | ✓ | — |

| Lesion | Key distinction |
|---|---|
| **Osteochondroma** | **Medullary continuity** with host bone on cross-section |
| **Myositis ossificans** | Thin **egg-shell peripheral** calcification, often **not touching bone** |
| **Surface chondrosarcoma** | More cortical destruction, **rings and arcs** |
| Dedifferentiated parosteal | Hypervascular areas on angiogram |

## 5 Management

```
Parosteal osteosarcoma
├─ Low grade → WIDE RESECTION ALONE
│      (geometric osteotomy to keep native joint; no chemo or RT)
└─ Dedifferentiated (high-grade component) → NEOADJUVANT CHEMO + WIDE RESECTION
```

### Treatment — wide resection alone

**Only OS subtype treated with wide resection alone**\
✗ Wrong options: conventional · periosteal · telangiectatic · secondary (all chemo + wide + chemo)

**Low-grade intramedullary (central) OS** — also **surgery with wide margins only** · "lacey" osteoid, minimal atypia, long premorbid history

**✗ Wrong options** — curettage (high recurrence) · marginal resection · observation

**Conventional OS for contrast** — pre-op chemo **8–12 weeks**, maintenance **6–12 months** after surgery

## 6 Complications

**Prognosis** — **95% long-term survival** with local control · better than other OS

**Dedifferentiation** — **poor prognostic factor** → chemo

## How it's asked

**"21-yr-old, dense bone 'stuck-on' proximal humerus, mature bone in fibroblastic stroma?"** → **parosteal OS**\
✗ Wrong options: periosteal OS · osteochondroma · MO

**"33-yr-old knee, surface lesion, hypervascular on angiogram, high-grade areas?"** → **dedifferentiated parosteal OS** — neoadjuvant chemo + wide resection

**"34-yr-old woman, ↓ knee flexion, lobulated posterior distal femoral surface mineralization?"** → **parosteal OS**\
✗ Wrong options: chondrosarcoma (rings and arcs) · lymphoma

**"Field hockey player, ↓ knee flexion, posterior femoral surface lesion — next?"** → **surgical biopsy**\
✗ Wrong options: chemo · RT · arthroscopy

**"Low-grade intramedullary OS, no mets — treatment?"** → **surgery only**

**"Parosteal OS (biopsy) — definitive management?"** → **wide surgical resection**\
✗ Wrong options: neoadjuvant chemo (conventional OS)

**"24-yr-old, tight knee, surface OS — plan?"** → **surgical resection only**\
✗ Wrong options: chemo · RT · observation

**"42-yr-old slowly enlarging thigh mass, surface OS?"** → **wide resection**\
✗ Wrong options: curettage (recurrence)

**"Which OS subtype gets wide resection alone?"** → **parosteal**
