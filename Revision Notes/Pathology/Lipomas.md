# Lipomas

*Pathology · 31 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Common** benign tumour of **mature fat** · ~50% of soft-tissue neoplasms (OB)

**Demographics** — **40–60** · slightly **male** · sedentary individuals

**Site** — **subcutaneous** (upper back, thighs, buttocks, shoulders, arms) · deep/intramuscular (thigh, shoulder, arm) · **dorsum of foot** · **multiple in 5–10%**

### Variants

| Variant | Key features | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Spindle cell lipoma** | Men **45–65**, neck/back/shoulder · mature fat + bland spindle cells, collagen, mast cells, myxoid · **CD34 +, S-100 −** | **Marginal excision** · rarely recurs |
| **Pleomorphic lipoma** | Middle age · lipocytes, spindle cells, **atypical giant cells** → mistaken for liposarcoma | Marginal excision |
| **Angiolipoma** | **Painful on palpation** · small nodules, upper limb · fat + nests of small arborizing vessels | Excision |
| **Intramuscular lipoma** | Often symptomatic · **lipoblasts + muscle infiltration** · may have infiltrative margins (✗ Wrong options: not malignancy) · small risk → liposarcoma | **Marginal resection** |
| **Hibernoma** | **Brown fat** · younger (**20–40**) · reddish-brown (vascular, mitochondria) | Excision |

### Genes & lab markers

**Spindle cell lipoma** — **CD34 +, S-100 −**

## 3 Presentation

**Painless, mobile, soft mass**, long-standing, size plateaus · symptoms from pressure (sitting, shoe wear, tool use) · **angiolipoma painful**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**MRI (diagnostic)** — well-demarcated, **homogeneous, signal = subcutaneous fat on ALL sequences** · **T1 bright**, T2 bright, **fully suppressed on STIR / fat-sat** · **no enhancement**

![Lipoma — homogeneous fat signal on T1](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8067-lipoma-t1-axial-moved.jpg)

**X-ray** — radiolucent soft-tissue shadow · **mineralization → think synovial sarcoma**

**CT** — well-demarcated, like subcutaneous fat

**Biopsy** — usually **unnecessary** · core/incisional prone to **sampling error** in fatty tumours

**Ganglion** — fluid-bright on T2 (not fat signal) · commonest soft-tissue foot/ankle lesion

### Histology

**Mature adipocytes**, eccentric nuclei, **bland acellular stroma**, no atypia · gross soft, lobular, yellow/white, ± encapsulated

### Atypical lipomatous tumour

**Deep (subfascial) or retroperitoneal lipomas** — higher chance of being **atypical lipomatous tumour** · retroperitoneum = **well-differentiated liposarcoma** · extremity = **atypical lipoma**

**MRI iso-intense with fat on all sequences** → lipoma / ALT / low-grade liposarcoma → **no biopsy, marginal excision** · recurrence 25–50% at 10 yr · dedifferentiation rare

### Intraosseous (calcaneal) lipoma

**"Cockade sign"** — well-defined lytic **calcaneal** lesion with **central calcification** → **pathognomonic** of intraosseous lipoma **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: chondroblastoma · NOF · EG

**Site** — **calcaneus ~30%** (commonest) · subtrochanteric femur · around knee · ~40 yr **(TO)**

**MRI** — **high T1 and T2**, suppressed on fat-sat **(TO)**

**DD** — SBC/UBC, ABC, GCT, osteoblastoma, calcaneal pseudotumour **(TO)**

| Milgram stage | Histology |
|---|---|
| **I** | Viable mature lipocytes |
| **II** | Necrotic foci, foamy macrophages, reactive bone |
| **III** | Complete necrosis, **calcification**, cysts (infarct-like) |

## 5 Management

```
Fatty soft-tissue mass, MRI = fat on all sequences
├─ Painless, determinate benign → OBSERVATION
└─ Symptomatic, rapidly growing, deep/retroperitoneal → MARGINAL EXCISION (no biopsy needed)
```

**Not indicated** — core / incisional biopsy · wide/radical resection · RT · chemo · amputation

## 6 Complications

**Local recurrence** — **<5%** (Orthobullets) · 15–60% (OB review, lipomatous masses) · 25–50% at 10 yr for ALT

## X vs Y

| | **Lipoma** | **Well-diff. liposarcoma / ALT** | **Myxoid liposarcoma** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Depth | Subcutaneous mostly | **Deep**, retroperitoneal | Deep, intermuscular |
| MRI | = fat all sequences | Fat + thick septa/nodules | T2 bright, myxoid, enhances |
| Histology | Mature fat, no atypia | Atypical stromal cells, lipoblasts | Lipoblasts, chicken-wire vessels |
| Treatment | Observe / marginal | Marginal excision (extremity) | Wide resection + RT |

## How it's asked

**"55-yr-old, posterior thigh fullness worse sitting, gluteal mass T1 bright, STIR dark, lipoblasts + muscle?"** → **intramuscular lipoma**\
✗ Wrong options: dedifferentiated liposarcoma · fibrosarcoma

**"Forearm mass, T1 = fat, STIR not bright — histology?"** → **lipoma** (mature fat)\
✗ Wrong options: myxoid liposarcoma · chondrosarcoma · Ewing

**"Symptomatic intramuscular arm lipoma, CT CAP negative?"** → **surgical excision**\
✗ Wrong options: core biopsy · RT

**"Painful dorsal foot fatty mass, no enhancement?"** → **lipoma, marginal excision**\
✗ Wrong options: wide resection

**"Enlarging painful forearm intramuscular lipoma?"** → **marginal resection**\
✗ Wrong options: core/incisional biopsy

**"Large symptomatic axillary/chest wall mass iso-intense to fat?"** → **marginal excision**\
✗ Wrong options: biopsy · wide + RT

**"Shoulder mass, CD34 + spindle cells in fat?"** → **spindle cell lipoma → marginal excision**

**"Cockade sign?"** → **calcaneal (intraosseous) lipoma** **(TO)**

**"Heel pain, lucent calcaneal lesion with central calcification, T1 + T2 bright?"** → **intraosseous lipoma** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: ABC · SBC · osteoblastoma
