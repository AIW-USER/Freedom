# Myositis Ossificans

*Pathology · 40 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Diagnosis & traumatic aetiology

**Nature** — **reactive, benign** soft-tissue bone formation (heterotopic ossification) · aka **hematoma ossificans**

**Cause** — **direct trauma / contusion** → **intramuscular haematoma** · near diaphysis of long bones · ⅓ idiopathic · also paraplegia, GBS, AIDS encephalopathy, head injury, burns, coma\
✗ Wrong options: surgery · parathyroid tumour · bisphosphonates · steroids

**Demographics** — **young active males 15–35**

**Muscles** — **quadriceps, brachialis, glutei** · elbow = commonest joint · **massage / vigorous passive physio after elbow injury provokes it** **(TO)**

**Genetics** — post-traumatic, not genetic\
✗ Wrong options: COMP · type X collagen · t(9;22) · p53

### Genes & lab markers

**Early** — ↑ CRP, ↑ **CPK** (inflammation, muscle)

**Mature phase** — ↑ **ALP** · falling ALP + mature bone on X-ray = maturity **(TO)**

**FOP** — **ACVR1** mutation (see below)

## 3 Presentation

**Phases (TO)** — **acute** (days–weeks): pain, swelling, joint stiffness · **pseudotumoral** (2–3 wk): painless hard mass · **resolving** (3–6 mo)

**Mass** — grows over months to **3–6 cm**, then firm · palpable · ↓ ROM (e.g. TKA stiffness after thigh blow)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — initially **normal** · mineralization at **2–3 weeks**, matures **3–6 months** · **peripheral ossification with central lucency** ("dotted veil") · ossified, smooth · **next to but not stuck on / not sharing the cortex**

![Myositis ossificans — peripheral ossification](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8042-13-moved.jpg)

**CT** — **"eggshell"** peripheral rim

**MRI with gadolinium** — **rim enhancement in first 3 weeks** · intramuscular · surrounding oedema early

**Zonation phenomenon (Ackerman)** — matures **periphery → centre** (✓ MO) vs osteosarcoma **centre → periphery**

**Variants (TO)** — stalked (peduncle to bone) · sessile (broad periosteal base) · muscle-only

### Histology — zonal pattern

| Zone | Histology |
|---|---|
| **Periphery** | **Mature lamellar + woven bone trabeculae** (osteoblastic rimming) · **mature fibroblasts** → calcified on X-ray |
| **Centre** | **Immature, proliferating fibroblasts** (mitoses, **atypia may be seen**), ± cartilage · not calcified |

**TO key** — **mature fibroblasts at periphery, proliferating at centre** **(TO)** · Orthobullets: no cellular atypia · biopsy may mimic sarcoma → **clinical + radiographic correlation critical**\
✗ Wrong options: reverse

### Differential

| Lesion | Distinguishing |
|---|---|
| **Myositis ossificans** | **Peripheral** maturation, separate from cortex, trauma history |
| **Extraskeletal OS** | **Older**, central ossification, no zonation |
| **Parosteal OS** | **Stuck on** posterior distal femur, linear radiolucent gap, 30–40 F |
| Osteochondroma (sessile) | **Cortex + medulla continuous** with bone |
| Synovial sarcoma | Wispy irregular calcification within mass |
| Periosteal chondrosarcoma | On the bone, cartilage mineral |
| Ossifying lipoma · ossifying mets · extraskeletal chondrosarcoma | Different histology |

## 5 Management

### Management — observation

```
Myositis ossificans
├─ Acute → REST, ICE, NSAIDs, COMPRESSION, SPLINT, ACTIVE ROM (no passive stretching)
├─ Asymptomatic / maturing → OBSERVE + SERIAL X-RAYS (no biopsy if imaging diagnostic)
└─ Symptomatic after maturity (≥6 mo; ~1 yr) → SURGICAL EXCISION
```

**Passive stretching / massage** — **contraindicated** (worsens) · PT for active ROM

**Excision** — only if still a problem **after maturation** · **wait ≥6 months** · excision at **6–12 months** → recurrence (Orthobullets) · maturation ~1 yr (TO)

**Asymptomatic 13-yr-old with peripheral mature ossification after injury** — **observation**\
✗ Wrong options: NSAIDs · rheumatology · biopsy · resection

## 6 Complications

**Prognosis** — self-limiting · mass shrinks **after ~1 year**

### Fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva (ACVR1)

**Gene** — **ACVR1** (activin A type I receptor = **BMP type-1 receptor**) · **R206H** gain-of-function → uninhibited SMAD signalling (Activin A, **BMP 2/4**/6/7) · sporadic or **AD**

**Hallmarks** — **progressive HO** of muscles, fascia, tendons, ligaments, capsules after minor trauma ("**Stone Man**") + **congenital bilateral monophalangeal hallux valgus** (pathognomonic) · cervical fusions, short broad femoral necks, osteochondromas · stooped posture, trismus, recurrent "bony prominences"

**Treatment** — conservative · surgery only for refractory pain, motion block, nerve compression (excision provokes more HO)

| Gene / protein | Condition (distractors) |
|---|---|
| **ACVR1** | ✓ **FOP** |
| LMX1B | Nail-patella |
| GNAS (Gs-α) | Fibrous dysplasia |
| DTDST (sulfate transporter) | Diastrophic dysplasia |
| RUNX2 | Cleidocranial dysplasia |
| EXT1/2/3 | MHE |
| HLA-B27 | Ankylosing spondylitis |

## How it's asked

**"Groin mass a year after helmet blow, dense ossification adjacent to bone, peripheral enhancement?"** → **myositis ossificans**\
✗ Wrong options: ossifying lipoma · synovial sarcoma · periosteal chondrosarcoma

**"Stiff TKA 6 mo after baseball hit to thigh, prostate cancer history?"** → **myositis ossificans**\
✗ Wrong options: ossifying mets · parosteal OS · sessile osteochondroma

**"Most likely cause?"** → **contusion**

**"Medial arm mass after arm wrestling, peripheral mineral on CT?"** → **myositis ossificans** (zonation of Ackerman)\
✗ Wrong options: extraskeletal OS · parosteal OS

**"Thigh mass after jogging?"** → **myositis ossificans**\
✗ Wrong options: sarcoma · pseudoaneurysm · abscess

**"Quadriceps ossified mass in footballer — linked to?"** → **history of thigh trauma**

**"Asymptomatic ossified hip 'knot' 4 mo after injury?"** → **observation**

**"Stone man, trismus, bony prominences, sister affected?"** → **ACVR1 mutation**\
✗ Wrong options: EXT · HLA-B27

**"Thigh deformity since youth, ACVR1 mutation?"** → **FOP**\
✗ Wrong options: melorheostosis · Paget · FD · MHE

**"Congenital bilateral hallux valgus + stiffness — mutated protein?"** → **activin receptor type 1A**

**"True about myositis ossificans?"** → **mature fibroblasts at periphery, proliferating at centre** **(TO)**
