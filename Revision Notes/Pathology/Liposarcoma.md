# Liposarcoma

*Pathology · 32 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rank** — **2nd commonest soft-tissue sarcoma in adults** (UPS 1st) · ~20% of adult STS

**Demographics** — **50–80** · myxoid in younger adults (30–60, peak 5th decade) · **male** > female

**Site** — **deep to fascia** (almost never subcutaneous) · **75% extremities** (lower > upper) · **retroperitoneum**

**Lipomas do not predispose** to liposarcoma

### Hallmark cell

**Lipoblast** — **signet-ring** cell, indented nucleus, vacuolated cytoplasm · distinguishes from lipoma and UPS

### Subtypes & grade

| Subtype | Grade | Share | Metastasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Well-differentiated / ALT** | **Low** | 40–45% (OB) | **<1%** (ALT ~0%) |
| **Myxoid** | Low–**intermediate** | **~50% — commonest** (Orthobullets/OB) | **10–30%** |
| **Round cell** | High | (spectrum with myxoid) | >50% |
| **Pleomorphic** | High | **<5%**, most aggressive | >50% |
| **Dedifferentiated** | High | <10% · "commonest" per one OB explanation | **~50%** (highest) |

### Genes & lab markers

| Marker | Subtype |
|---|---|
| **MDM2 + CDK4 amplification** (12q13–15, **ring / giant marker chromosome 12**) | **ALT / WDL** and **dedifferentiated** · CDK4 sens 86%/spec 89%, MDM2 86%/74% |
| **t(12;16) FUS(TLS)–DDIT3(CHOP)** | **Myxoid** (± round cell) · rarely t(12;22) |
| **p53** mutation, complex karyotype | **Pleomorphic** |

**Best test to separate ALT from lipoma** — **FISH for MDM2 amplification**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · S-100 (MPNST/melanoma) · CD138 (myeloma) · CD20 (lymphoma)

**MDM2** — negative regulator of p53

| Genetic finding | Tumour (distractors) |
|---|---|
| **MDM2/CDK4** | ✓ ALT/WDL |
| **t(12;16)** | ✓ Myxoid liposarcoma |
| t(2;13) | Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma |
| t(X;18) SYT–SSX | Synovial sarcoma |
| ETV6–NTRK3 | Infantile fibrosarcoma |
| t(12;22) | Clear cell sarcoma |
| t(11;22) | Ewing |

## 3 Presentation

**Slow-growing, painless, deep mass** · often 10–20 cm · incidental · mass effect (sciatic paraesthesia, retroperitoneal)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**MRI with contrast** — best characterization (sens 100%, spec 71%, PPV 100%)

| Subtype | MRI |
|---|---|
| **ALT / WDL** | Like lipoma — fat signal (Orthobullets: **bright T1, dark T2**) · **thick (>2 mm), irregular septa / stranding**, nodules, mild enhancement, oedema → dedifferentiation focus |
| **Myxoid** | Heterogeneous, T2 bright, little fat (~10%) · **intense diffuse enhancement** (vs myxoma) |
| **High grade** | Like other sarcomas — **T1 dark, T2 bright** (only ~10% fat) |

![Myxoid liposarcoma histology](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8068-myxoid-liposarcoma.jpg)

**X-ray** — soft-tissue mass · calcification/ossification in WDL

**Biopsy** — areas **not consistent with fat** on MRI must be biopsied

### Histology

| Subtype | Histology |
|---|---|
| **ALT / WDL** | Mature fat with **atypical stromal cells**, scattered lipoblasts, minimally cellular · lipoma-like, sclerotic, inflammatory variants |
| **Myxoid** | Proliferating lipoblasts (signet ring at lobule edges) in **myxoid stroma** + **delicate plexiform "chicken-wire" capillaries** · "**pulmonary oedema**" pattern |
| **Round cell** | Small round blue cells |
| **Pleomorphic** | **Giant lipoblasts, bizarre nuclei**, eosinophilic giant cells (UPS-like + lipoblasts) |
| **Dedifferentiated** | ALT/WDL transformed to high-grade sarcoma |

### Staging

**Myxoid liposarcoma — extrapulmonary spread** — lungs + **abdomen, retroperitoneum, spine**, other extremities' soft tissue, bone marrow

**Myxoid staging/surveillance** — **CT chest/abdomen/pelvis + whole-spine MRI** + MRI of primary site\
✗ Wrong options: chest-only · PET (misses spine) · brain MRI · sentinel node · marrow biopsy

**Other STS** — chest CT

## 5 Management

```
Liposarcoma
├─ ALT (extremity), MDM2/CDK4 + → MARGINAL RESECTION, NO RADIATION
├─ Myxoid (intermediate) → WIDE RESECTION + RT
│      └─ >5% round cells (OB: ≥25%) or margins inadequate → + CHEMO (ifosfamide-based)
└─ Round cell / pleomorphic / dedifferentiated (high grade) → WIDE RESECTION + RT (pre-op 50 Gy)
```

**ALT/WDL** — **marginal resection without RT** (OB) · Orthobullets lists WDL for wide resection + RT (vs ALT marginal) — read the options ·\
✗ Wrong options: amputation · chemo · RT alone · wide resection for extremity ALT

**Myxoid** — **most chemo- and radiosensitive** liposarcoma

**Radiation** — **50 Gy preop**, **66 Gy postop** standard for STS · ↓ local recurrence

**Technique** — tourniquet without exsanguination · frozen section margins · NV resection only if bypass possible and morbidity acceptable

### Soft tissue sarcoma treatment (margins, radiation, staging)

**Most important factor preventing local recurrence** — **adequacy of surgical margin**\
✗ Wrong options: chemo · RT alone

**Large (>5 cm), deep, high-grade STS** — **wide excision + radiation** (pre- or post-op)

**Pleomorphic liposarcoma, no mets** — **neoadjuvant RT 50 Gy → wide resection**\
✗ Wrong options: RFA · resection alone · chemo-based · disarticulation

## 6 Complications

**Mass effect** — NV bundles, retroperitoneum, peritoneum

| Subtype | Local recurrence | Dedifferentiation | Survival |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ALT** | **10–50% over 10 yr** | **3–7%** (2% extremity) | ~100% |
| **WDL** (central) | High | **20%** | ~80% 5-yr |
| Intermediate/high | ~20% | — | 50–80% 5-yr |

## X vs Y

| | **ALT** | **Lipoma** |
|---|---|---|
| MDM2/CDK4 | **+** | − |
| Septa | Thick (>2 mm), nodular | Thin |
| Depth | Deep | Usually subcutaneous |
| Treatment | Marginal excision | Observe / marginal |

| | **Myxoid liposarcoma** | **Intramuscular myxoma** |
|---|---|---|
| Location | **Inter**muscular | **Intra**muscular |
| Cellularity | Cellular, **lipoblasts**, chicken-wire vessels | **Hypocellular**, bland |
| Enhancement | Intense | Little |

## How it's asked

**"Thigh fatty tumour, MDM2 + CDK4 +?"** → **atypical lipomatous tumour**\
✗ Wrong options: lipoma · RMS · LMS · myxofibrosarcoma

**"Genetic basis + treatment of thigh WDL?"** → **MDM2 amplification; marginal resection without radiation**

**"Gluteal ALT, MDM2 +?"** → **marginal resection**\
✗ Wrong options: wide · RT · chemo

**"Best test to separate ALT from lipoma?"** → **FISH for MDM2**

**"Feature allowing marginal excision alone?"** → **MDM2 + CDK4 amplification**\
✗ Wrong options: t(12;16) · t(2;13) · ETV6-NTRK3 · SYT-SSX

**"Lipomatous tumour with highest metastatic rate?"** → **dedifferentiated liposarcoma**

**"Risk of ALT dedifferentiating?"** → **3–7%**

**"Thigh mass, signet-ring lipoblasts in myxoid stroma — translocation?"** → **myxoid liposarcoma, t(12;16)**\
✗ Wrong options: alveolar RMS · synovial t(X;18)

**"33-yr-old, thigh mass, 12;16 translocation?"** → **myxoid liposarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: WDL · round cell · fibrolipomatous hamartoma

**"Posterior thigh mass with sciatic symptoms, chicken-wire vessels?"** → **myxoid liposarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: synovial sarcoma · MFH · fibrosarcoma

**"Myxoid liposarcoma after resection — extra staging?"** → **whole-spine MRI**

**"Myxoid liposarcoma surveillance?"** → **MRI of thigh + CT chest/abdomen/pelvis**

**"Slide warranting marginal resection alone?"** → **well-differentiated liposarcoma / ALT**\
✗ Wrong options: myxoid · dedifferentiated · UPS · pleomorphic

**"Thigh mass following fat with stranding + enhancement?"** → **liposarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: synovial sarcoma (cystic, no fat)

**"Most important factor against local recurrence in UPS?"** → **adequate surgical margin**

**"Large, deep, high-grade dedifferentiated liposarcoma?"** → **wide excision + radiation**

**"15 cm pleomorphic liposarcoma, p53 +, t(12;16) −?"** → **neoadjuvant RT (50 Gy) → wide resection**
