# Leukemia

*Pathology · 30 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — malignant proliferation of lymphocytes / precursors (or myeloid cells)

**Commonest malignancy of childhood** — **leukaemia (ALL)**

### Forms

| Type | Cell | Age | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ALL** | Lymphoblasts | **Peak 4 yr** · **80%** of (childhood) leukaemia | **TdT +** · bone demineralization, **periostitis**, lytic lesions · **T-ALL** teens · **B-ALL**: **t(12;21) children**, **t(9;22) adults** |
| **AML** | Myeloblasts | **Older adults (5th–6th decade)** | **Auer rods** · marrow failure · **APL t(15;17)** disrupts retinoic acid receptor → **all-trans-retinoic acid** · **megakaryoblastic** → **Down syndrome**, <5 yr · **monocytic** → **gum infiltration** |
| **CML** | Mature granulocytes | 5th–6th decade | **t(9;22) Philadelphia chromosome → BCR-ABL tyrosine kinase** (↑ division, ↓ apoptosis) · smear: immature granulocytes, basophilia, eosinophilia · **imatinib** |
| **CLL** | Naive B cells | 5th–6th decade | Insidious · **smudge cells** · lymphadenopathy |

### Genes & lab markers

| Marker | Leukaemia |
|---|---|
| **TdT +** | ALL |
| t(12;21) | B-ALL, children |
| t(9;22) BCR-ABL | CML · B-ALL adults |
| t(15;17) RARα | APL (AML) |
| Auer rods | AML |
| Smudge cells | CLL |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **recurrent infections**, **bleeding**, **fatigue**, low-grade fever, **bone pain / limp**

**Signs** — **hepatosplenomegaly**, **lymphadenopathy** (commonest in CLL)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — diffuse osteopenia · **metaphyseal lucent bands** · periosteal reaction · lytic / mixed sclerotic-lytic · permeative destruction

**MRI** — scattered ill-defined medullary lesions · mimics **LCH and chronic osteomyelitis**

**Vertebral fractures** — **thoracic** commonest, then lumbar **(TO)**

**Diagnosis** — **peripheral smear + bone marrow biopsy** (lymphoblasts)

### Differential

| Lesion | Smear / histology |
|---|---|
| **ALL** | **Lymphoblasts** |
| AML | Myeloblasts + Auer rods, older |
| Chronic osteomyelitis | Neutrophilia, left shift |
| LCH | Eosinophilia, **CD1a, CD207 (langerin)**, Birbeck granules |

## 5 Management

**Chemotherapy** — ALL (T and B) · may predispose to **pathologic fracture**

**Targeted** — **ATRA** for APL · **imatinib** for CML

## How it's asked

**"4-yr-old, fatigue, fevers, recurrent ear infections, limp, ill-defined medullary lesions, lymphoblasts on smear?"** → **ALL**\
✗ Wrong options: AML · CML · chronic osteomyelitis · LCH

**"Most common location of vertebral fracture in leukaemia?"** → **thoracic** **(TO)**

**"Philadelphia chromosome?"** → **t(9;22) BCR-ABL — CML** (imatinib)
