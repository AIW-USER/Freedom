# Extra-abdominal Desmoid Tumor

*Pathology · 19 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Nature** — benign but **most locally invasive benign soft-tissue tumour** (aggressive fibromatosis) · **no metastasis**, no malignant change (unless radiation-related)

**Demographics** — **25–35 yr** (15–40) · **F:M 3:1** · ~900/yr in US

**Site** — **shoulder (20%) > chest wall/back (17%) > thigh (13%)** · >50% extra-abdominal · **sites of previous surgery/scars** · multiple lesions in same limb → **next one appears more proximally**

### Association with FAP

**Familial adenomatous polyposis (FAP) / Gardner syndrome** — desmoid in **4–30%** (prevalence ~21%) · **10,000× risk** \
✗ Wrong options: Sinding-Larsen-Johansson · Ollier · Fanconi · McCune-Albright

**Gardner features** — colonic polyps + **jaw osteomas** + **epidermoid cysts**

**FAP** — **autosomal dominant**, **APC** tumour-suppressor gene · **~100% lifetime colon cancer** → **screen for colon cancer**\
✗ Wrong options: lung, breast, thyroid, renal

**Other associations** — **Dupuytren** contracture · **Ledderhose** disease

### Genes & lab markers

**APC mutation** — 15% of tumours (APC controls **β-catenin**)

**β-catenin ↑** — **all** tumours

**Trisomy 8 or 20**

**IHC +** — **oestrogen receptor-β (100%)**, somatostatin, cathepsin D, Ki-67, c-Kit · **IHC −** — ER-α, PR, HER2

### Cortical desmoid

**Cortical desmoid** — self-limiting benign fibro-osseous lesion of **active adolescents (10–15)** · **chronic tendon traction** · **posteromedial distal femur** at **medial gastrocnemius / adductor magnus** insertion · also humerus (pectoralis, deltoid insertions)

**Imaging** — geographic central lucency, sclerotic margin, **"bite-like" cortical irregularity** · MRI T1 low–intermediate, T2 high, heterogeneous enhancement

**Treatment** — **activity modification + PT** (self-limiting)

## 3 Presentation

**Mass** — enlarging, painless or painful · **"rock hard"**, fixed, poorly circumscribed · limits joint motion

**Nerve compression** — numbness, tingling, weakness, stabbing pain

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**MRI — best means to diagnose** · excludes multicentric lesions · **T1 low**, **T2 low–medium** (collagen) · gadolinium enhances · infiltrates muscle · 5–10 cm · may erode bone

**X-ray** — minimal role · bone erosion, **"frond-like" periosteal spicules** into tumour

### Histology

**Gross** — **white, gritty**, poorly encapsulated

**Micro** — **well-differentiated fibroblasts/myofibroblasts**, **uniform spindle cells with elongated nuclei**, occasional mitoses, **abundant collagen**, infiltrates adjacent tissue

![Uniform spindle cells in abundant collagen](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8062-extra-abdominal-desmoid-tumor.jpg)

| Histology | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| **Uniform spindle fibroblasts, elongated nuclei, collagen** | ✓ **Desmoid** |
| Fibroblasts + Schwann cells, mast cells, wavy nuclei | **Neurofibroma** |
| Stromal cells, foam cells, haemosiderin giant cells | PVNS |
| Small round blue cells | Ewing |
| Storiform fibroblasts, lipophages, giant cells | NOF |

## 5 Management

```
Extra-abdominal desmoid
├─ Asymptomatic / minimally symptomatic → ACTIVE SURVEILLANCE, SERIAL MRI (first line)
├─ Progressive, inoperable, or can't tolerate chemo → TAMOXIFEN / LOW-DOSE CHEMO
└─ Symptomatic / recurrent / failed surveillance → WIDE RESECTION + RT (50–60 Gy)
```

**Active surveillance ("watch and wait")** — primary strategy (Desmoid Tumor Working Group) · may stay stable or **regress spontaneously**

**Tamoxifen** — **oestrogen-receptor blockade** · moderate short-term success with less morbidity · for inoperable lesions / poor chemo candidates · works as lesions commoner + more aggressive in (premenopausal) women

**Wide resection + radiotherapy** — symptomatic or recurrent lesions · **50–60 Gy** EBRT · abdominal wall desmoid → surgery if surveillance fails

## 6 Complications

### Recurrence

| Margin | Surgery alone | Surgery + RT |
|---|---|---|
| **Negative** | **28%** | **6%** |
| **Positive** | **39%** | **25%** |

**Highest local recurrence** of soft-tissue tumours after wide resection without adjuvant

**Prognosis** — unpredictable · occasional spontaneous regression · **no metastasis**

## How it's asked

**"20-yr-old woman, rock-hard calf mass, ER-β +?"** → **extra-abdominal fibromatosis (desmoid)**\
✗ Wrong options: OS · leiomyosarcoma · angiosarcoma

**"Shoulder mass, family with colonic polyps, jaw osteomas, epidermoid cysts — histology?"** → **uniform spindle fibroblasts with elongated nuclei**\
✗ Wrong options: neurofibroma · PVNS · Ewing · NOF

**"Adolescent lacrosse player, posteromedial distal femoral cortical lesion from tendon traction?"** → **cortical desmoid** → activity modification + PT

**"48-yr-old, 5 cm thigh mass, biopsy → active surveillance with serial MRI?"** → **extra-abdominal desmoid**\
✗ Wrong options: lipoma · GCT · osteoid osteoma

**"Inherited condition associated with desmoids?"** → **FAP**

**"Desmoid patient — screen for?"** → **colon cancer**

**"Tumour treated by oestrogen-receptor blockade?"** → **desmoid** (tamoxifen)\
✗ Wrong options: Ewing · synovial sarcoma · myeloma · GCT
