# Synovial Sarcoma

*Pathology · 62 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Nature** — malignant, usually **high-grade** STS · **unknown cell of origin** · spreads along fascial planes\
✗ Wrong options: type II synoviocyte (misnomer from microscopic resemblance)

**Frequency** — **5–10%** of STS (Orthobullets, OB5861) · **6%** (OB7586) · **7% / 6–8%** (TO) · **4th commonest** STS (OB8312)

**Age** — **15–40 yr** · **commonest STS in young adults/adolescents** (16% of STS at that age; RMS 15%) · 30% of childhood STS · mean <30

**Sex** — M ≈ F (**1.2:1**; 1.1:1)

**Site** — **periarticular, rarely intra-articular** (only **10%** in a major joint) · **lower limb 60%** · **knee / popliteal fossa** commonest → ankle, foot · shoulder, elbow, axilla · **commonest sarcoma of the foot**

### Genetics — t(X;18) SYT-SSX

**t(X;18)(p11;q11)** in **>90%** · **SYT (SS18) on 18 + SSX1 / SSX2 / SSX4 (rare) on X** → **SYT-SSX** fusion · detect by **RT-PCR**

**Mechanism** — fusion binds **BAF complex**, displaces tumour suppressor **BAF47** → activates **Sox2**

| | **SYT-SSX1** | **SYT-SSX2** |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | **More common (60%)** | 40% |
| Histology | **Biphasic** | **Monophasic** |
| Sex | M:F 1:1 | M:F 1:2 |
| Presentation | Larger, with mets | Smaller, without mets |
| Survival | **Worse** (Orthobullets, OB213030 answer) | **Better** |

**Conflict** — TO: **monophasic histology = adverse**, biphasic favourable (and OB213030's explanation says biphasic survives better) · Orthobullets: SSX1 (biphasic) worse · **fusion type = most important prognostic factor** (Orthobullets)

| Fusion / translocation | Tumour |
|---|---|
| **SYT-SSX, t(X;18)** | **Synovial sarcoma** |
| EWS-FLI1, t(11;22) | Ewing |
| PAX3-FKHR, t(2;13) | Alveolar RMS |
| **EWS-ATF1, t(12;22)** | **Clear-cell sarcoma** |
| **FUS-CHOP / FUS-DDIT3, t(12;16)** | **Myxoid liposarcoma** |
| t(9;22) | Myxoid chondrosarcoma · BCR-ABL CML |
| COL1A1-PDGFB | DFSP |
| Ring chromosome 12 | WD liposarcoma, MFH (ring chromosomes also parosteal OS) |
| Monosomy 14, 22 | GIST |

### Genes & lab markers

**IHC** — **vimentin +**, **epithelial membrane antigen (EMA) +**, **keratin / cytokeratin +** (nearly all biphasic, many monophasic), sporadic **S-100** · only synovial sarcoma (vs MFH) stains vimentin + EMA · TO: CD56, CD99, HMW keratin (poorly differentiated)

## 3 Presentation

**Slow-growing mass near a joint** · painless or painful · mistaken for ganglion/bursitis · large deep >5 cm

**Nerve signs** — e.g. popliteal mass → plantar tingling, weak push-off, ↓ knee flexion

**Check regional lymph nodes**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnosis & features (calcification, foot, histology)

**X-ray** — **soft-tissue calcification** — **15–20%** (OB6293) · **1/3** (TO) · irregular (vs round phleboliths of haemangioma) · may mimic HO · commonest STS with calcification\
✗ Wrong options: Ewing · clear-cell · MFH

![Soft-tissue calcification in axillary synovial sarcoma](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8052-case-b-axilla-xray-parsons-moved.jpg)

**US** — non-specific hypoechoic mass

**CT** — well-circumscribed, calcification · **CT chest** staging

**MRI (gold standard)** — heterogeneous, **dark T1 / bright T2** · **T2 "triple sign"** (high: necrosis/cyst · intermediate · low: calcification/fibrosis) · cysts in up to 20% · well-defined → misread as benign

**Biopsy** — core needle or incisional · **not FNA** (no architecture)

**Workup** — **MRI of the limb + CT chest** · nodes examined · **no bone scan** · not US only

### Histology

**Biphasic** (commonest) — **epithelial cells** (gland, nest, cyst-like; pale columnar) + **spindle cells** · **monophasic** — spindle only · spindle cells small, uniform, sheets/fascicles, scant cytoplasm, dark atypical nuclei

![Biphasic glands + spindle cells](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8052-histology-c-parsons-moved.jpg)

| Histology distractor | Lesion |
|---|---|
| Round cells in fibrous septae, giant cells | Alveolar RMS |
| **Nests of clear cells**, fibrous septa, giant cells | Clear-cell sarcoma |
| Signet-ring lipoblasts, **chicken-wire** capillaries | Myxoid liposarcoma |
| Small round blue cells, rosettes | Ewing |
| Granulomatous look | Epithelioid sarcoma |
| Storiform pleomorphic | MFH |
| Haemosiderin macrophages, villi | PVNS |
| Osteoid · "blue balls of cartilage" | OS · chondrosarcoma |

## 5 Management

**Standard** — **wide resection + perioperative RT** (neoadjuvant or adjuvant — no consensus) · RT ↓ local recurrence and **improves OS + DSS** (Orthobullets)

**Chemotherapy** — **one of the more chemosensitive STS** · may improve local control + OS · reserved for **high-risk, metastatic, younger** patients · metastatic → **doxorubicin + ifosfamide (TO)**

**Large midfoot tumour** — transtibial amputation (STS chapter)

## 6 Complications

### Lymph node spread & staging

**Metastasis** — **30–60%** · **lung commonest** · **lymph nodes** (with angiosarcoma, clear-cell, RMS, epithelioid) → **sentinel node biopsy** · nodes worse than none but better than lung mets · ↑ with large, deep, high grade

**Only RACES member with SYT-SSX1** → synovial sarcoma

**vs myxoid liposarcoma t(12;16)** — synovial has **fewer bony mets** · but **more nodal mets**, **more local recurrence**, commoner in foot

**Liposarcoma mets by grade** — WD **<1%** · myxoid **10–30%** · high grade **>50%**

**Prognosis** — poor · **5-yr 50–60%** · **10-yr ~25%** · TO 5-yr ~60% (61–73%) · factors: size, grade, location, age, margins, **fusion type**
