# Neurofibroma

*Pathology · 43 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Nature** — benign peripheral nerve sheath tumour · mostly with **neurofibromatosis (NF1)**

**Age** — sporadic **20–40** · NF1 **<20**

**Site** — sporadic: peripheral nerves of extremities · NF1: near **spinal cord/brain**, unusual sites (parotid, tongue)

**Cells** — **fibroblasts predominate** + **non-myelinating Schwann cells (cell of origin)** + perineural cells, mast cells (vs schwannoma = Schwann cells only)

### Genes & lab markers

**NF1** — **autosomal dominant**, 17q11.2 · **biallelic inactivation** of NF1 tumour-suppressor in non-myelinating Schwann cells → loss of **neurofibromin** → **↑ RAS** (RAS-MAPK) activity

**MPNST transformation** — + loss of **CDKN2A or TP53**

**S-100** — **variable** (vs uniform in schwannoma)

| Schwann cell | Axons | Disease |
|---|---|---|
| Myelinating | Large-diameter, myelin | — |
| **Non-myelinating** | **Small-diameter**, cytoplasmic processes | **Neurofibromatosis** |

### Subtypes

| | **Dermal** | **Plexiform** |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | **Single** nerve | **Multiple** nerve bundles |
| Look | **Fusiform** swelling | **"Bag of worms"** |
| Association | 90% sporadic, 10% NF1 | **Pathognomonic of NF1** |
| Onset | Puberty | Early childhood |
| Malignancy | Never | **10% → MPNST** |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — asymptomatic · stinging, itching, pain, disfigurement · **exquisite tenderness** (benign nerve sheath tumour hallmark) · reduced sensation

**Cutaneous** — "**button-holes**" through skin defect with finger pressure

### NF1 diagnostic criteria (NIH — 2 of 7)

| Criterion |
|---|
| **≥2 neurofibromas or 1 plexiform** |
| **Axillary/groin freckling** (Crowe sign) |
| **≥6 café-au-lait** spots ("coast of California") — >5 mm prepubertal, >15 mm postpubertal |
| **Sphenoid wing dysplasia** or long-bone cortical thinning (**anterolateral tibial bowing**) |
| **≥2 Lisch nodules** (iris hamartomas, slit lamp) |
| **Optic glioma** |
| **First-degree relative** with NF1 |

**NF1 associations** — **dystrophic kyphoscoliosis**, **rib penciling**, intraspinal **dumbbell** neurofibromas, **dural ectasia**, meningiomas · cancer risk ~4×

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**MRI ± contrast** — T1 low, T2 very high (myxoid-type)

| Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Nerve continuity (entering-exiting)** | Fusiform tumour in continuity with NV bundle |
| **Split fat** | Thin rim of fat around mass in NV bundle |
| **Target** | High-signal periphery, low centre (STIR) |

**Neurofibroma vs schwannoma** — neurofibroma **central** to nerve (fascicles run through it, no capsule) · schwannoma **eccentric**, encapsulated

![Neurofibroma — wavy collagen](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8056-nf-histo.jpg)

**PET-CT** — **distinguishes MPNST from benign neurofibroma** (↑ SUV) in NF1 with multiple nodules\
✗ Wrong options: UPS vs MPNST · infection vs tumour

### Histology

**Hypocellular**, **fibroblasts** + Schwann cells, mast cells, lymphocytes · **elongated, wavy nuclei** · **"wire-like"/rope-like wavy collagen** · may be myxoid · corpuscle-like (Pacini/Meissner) structures

| Histology | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| **Wavy collagen, elongated cells, hypocellular** | ✓ **Neurofibroma** |
| Herringbone, atypia | Fibrosarcoma |
| Stromal cells, haemosiderin macrophages, giant cells | GCT of tendon sheath |
| Mono/biphasic epithelioid cells | Synovial sarcoma |

### MPNST risk

**MPNST in NF** — **5% (10–25% lifetime)** · usually from **plexiform** · **painful, enlarging** mass · poor prognosis

## 5 Management

**Observe** — asymptomatic

**Surgical excision** — symptomatic · tumour within nerve → **may need nerve sacrifice / grafting** (vs schwannoma shelled out)

## How it's asked

**"Forearm mass 6 yr, episodic sharp pain, fusiform on median nerve?"** → **nerve sheath tumour**\
✗ Wrong options: sarcoma · metastasis · haematoma · abscess

**"Buttock pain sitting, sciatic paraesthesia, wavy collagen?"** → **neurofibroma**\
✗ Wrong options: myxoid liposarcoma · myxoma · MFH · fibromatosis

**"Exquisitely tender long-standing leg mass in tibial nerve, rope-like collagen?"** → **neurofibroma**\
✗ Wrong options: fibrosarcoma · GCT tendon sheath · synovial sarcoma

**"NF1 with multiple nodules — PET-CT distinguishes?"** → **MPNST vs benign neurofibroma**
