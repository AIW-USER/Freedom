# Chondrosarcoma

*Pathology · 10 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rank** — **2nd commonest malignant primary bone tumour** (after OS) · commonest primary bone sarcoma in the US (OB) · commonest primary bone malignancy **of the hand** though rare there **(TO)**

**Age** — conventional **40–75**, most **>50** · peak primary **40–60**, secondary **25–45** **(TO)** · slight **male** predominance

**Site** — **pelvis (25%)**, proximal femur, distal femur, proximal humerus, scapula, ribs · **proximal/axial** · metaphyseal or diaphyseal (epiphyseal only = **clear cell**)

**Location rule** — cartilage tumours of **hands/feet mostly benign** · **flat bones (pelvis, scapula, ribs, sternum) malignant until proven otherwise** · subperiosteal or joint-lining cartilage almost always benign **(TO)**

### Primary vs secondary

**Primary** — sporadic · **conventional >90%** (central intramedullary 99%, periosteal ~1%) · dedifferentiated <10% · clear cell <5% · mesenchymal <1%

**Secondary** — from pre-existing benign cartilage lesion · **osteochondroma >80%** of secondary · younger (3rd–4th decade) · usually **low grade** · ~40% of MHE secondaries in **ilium** · histologically = conventional

### Malignant transformation risk

| Precursor | Orthobullets | OB explanation | TO |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solitary osteochondroma** | **<1%** | 1% | <1% (~1% lifetime) |
| **Solitary enchondroma** | 1–9% | 1% | 1% |
| **MHE** | **5–10%** | ~10% | 1–10% · ~5% lifetime |
| **Ollier disease** | **25–30%** | 30% | 5–50% · ~25% by age 40 |
| **Maffucci syndrome** | **>50%** | **~100%** (highest) | 25–100% |

**Maffucci** — multiple enchondromas + **soft-tissue haemangiomas** (and lymphangiomas) · also pancreatic/hepatic adenocarcinoma, ovarian, glioma/astrocytoma

**Axial/pelvic osteochondroma** — more likely to transform than peripheral

**Other precursors (TO)** — **synovial chondromatosis**, CMF, periosteal chondroma, chondroblastoma, **prior radiation**, fibrous dysplasia, **Paget** (OS + chondrosarcoma) · not **chronic osteomyelitis** → **squamous cell carcinoma** (Marjolin)

### Genes & lab markers

**IDH1 / IDH2** mutations — primary + secondary conventional chondrosarcoma **and enchondromas**

**HEY1–NCOA2** fusion — **mesenchymal** chondrosarcoma

**t(12;22)** — listed for **clear cell** (Orthobullets) · clear cell: **S-100 +, PAS +** (glycogen → clear cytoplasm)

**Telomerase activity ↑** (RT-PCR) — **directly correlates with recurrence**\
✗ Wrong options: MHC II · gadolinium uptake · apoptosis · technetium uptake

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — commonest · **pain attributable to the lesion** (night pain, not activity related) separates low-grade CS from enchondroma (vs pain from cuff tear / meniscus)

**Pelvis** — slowly growing mass · **bowel/bladder obstruction** from mass effect

**Secondary** — **new pain or growth of an osteochondroma after skeletal maturity**

**Dedifferentiated** — **pathologic fracture ~50%**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Work-up & referral

**Incidental chondroid lesion on MRI** (meniscus work-up) — **first get orthogonal radiographs** → often simple enchondroma\
✗ Wrong options: CT CAP · whole-limb MRI · core biopsy · curettage

**Suspicious lesion at community hospital** — **refer to orthopaedic oncologist**\
✗ Wrong options: observe · curettage · chemo/RT

**Staging** — **CT chest** (lungs = main site) + MRI whole bone + bone scan\
✗ Wrong options: CT abdomen/pelvis (bone sarcomas rarely go to viscera) · SPEP/UPEP (myeloma)

**Unknown lytic lesion >40 yr** — CT chest/abdomen/pelvis (carcinoma work-up)

**Biopsy** — core needle or open (gold standard) · target **area of ↓ T2 signal** (focus of dedifferentiation) · TO: needle biopsy not indicated for cartilage tumours (heterogeneous) · diagnosis = **clinical + radiographic + histologic correlation**

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — orthogonal views mandatory · usually **>4 cm** · lytic with **"rings and arcs" / "popcorn"** calcification (punctate, comma-shaped) · seen on X-ray ~70%

![Popcorn calcification](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8023-popcorn-calcifications.jpg)

**Aggressive signs** — **endosteal scalloping >2/3 cortex** · cortical destruction · periosteal reaction · bone expansion · soft-tissue mass · permeative borders · higher grade → less mineral, more lytic

**CT** — **best for low-grade cartilage lesions** (cortical involvement) · matrix seen more easily · **deep endosteal scalloping** → CS · **90% cortical breach**

**MRI** — **lobulated**, T1 low–intermediate, **T2 high** (cartilage water) with low-signal septa · **"rings and arcs" septal enhancement** · defines soft-tissue extent

**Bone scan** — very hot in all grades · **uptake > ASIS** characteristic (enchondroma < ASIS)

![Bone scan uptake greater than ASIS](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8023-bone-scan-chondrosarc.jpg)

**Secondary from osteochondroma** — **cartilage cap >2 cm** (≥2 cm) on MRI/CT in a mature patient · lysis of calcification · cortical destruction · cap **14 mm, no growth, mild symptoms → serial imaging + exam**\
✗ Wrong options: FNA · chest CT · bone scan · wide excision

### Enchondroma vs low-grade chondrosarcoma (diagnostic criteria)

| | **Enchondroma** | **Chondrosarcoma** |
|---|---|---|
| Age | **<50** | **>50** |
| Size | **<5 cm** | **>5 cm** |
| Site | **Hands/feet common** · axial/pelvis rare | **Axial/pelvis common** · hands/feet rare |
| Pain | No (or other pathology) | **Yes, attributable to lesion** |
| Soft-tissue mass | No | Yes |
| Cortical destruction | No | Yes |
| Periosteal reaction | No | Yes |
| **Endosteal scalloping** | **<2/3 cortex** | **>2/3 cortex** |
| Expansion | No | Yes |
| **Bone scan** | **< ASIS** | **> ASIS** |

### Histology & grading (conventional)

**Graded by** — **cellularity + cytological atypia** (nuclear size, hyperchromasia), myxoid stroma · mitoses rare, not used **(TO)**

**Diagnostic of malignancy** — **permeation and entrapment of pre-existing bone trabeculae** · hypercellularity, plump nuclei, >occasional **binucleate** cells

**Any malignant osteoid** — → **chondroblastic osteosarcoma**, not CS **(TO)**

![Entrapment of host trabeculae](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8023-trabeculae-entrapped.jpg)

| Grade | Share | Histology | Metastasis | 5-yr survival |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 60% | Like enchondroma · bland hyaline, low cellularity, binucleate · needs clinico-radiological correlation | Almost never | **90%** |
| **2** | 25% | Plump **bi-/tri-nucleated** cells, ↑ cellularity, atypia, myxoid change | **10–15%** | **60–70%** |
| **3** | 5% | Hypercellular permeative stroma, little hyaline, **myxoid**, pleomorphic, mitoses · "blue balls" | **>50%** | **30–50%** |
| **Dedifferentiated ("4")** | ~10% | Bimorphic | High | **<20%** (10% at 10 yr) |

**WHO 2020** — **grade 1 extremity CS = atypical cartilaginous tumour (ACT)** · axial cartilage tumours = separate, more aggressive category

### Subtypes

| Subtype | Age | Site | Imaging | Histology | Treatment / prognosis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conventional** | 40–75 | Pelvis, prox femur, humerus | Rings & arcs, scalloping | Hyaline, graded 1–3 | **Wide resection alone** |
| **Dedifferentiated** | ~60 | As conventional | **Bimorphic**: aggressive **unmineralized lytic** mass next to mineralized chondroid · path fracture | Low-grade cartilage **abruptly** next to **high-grade spindle sarcoma** (OS > fibrosarcoma > MFH) · not admixed | Wide resection ± **chemo** · **<20% 5-yr**, **10% 10-yr** |
| **Clear cell** | **20–40** | **Epiphysis** · 2/3 **humeral/femoral head** | Well-defined lytic epi-metaphyseal, thin sclerotic rim, mixed lucent/sclerotic, no periosteal reaction · mimics **chondroblastoma** | **Large vacuolated clear cells**, giant cells · ~50% low-grade CS areas · S-100, PAS + | **Wide resection** (no chemo) · 10-yr ~80% · mets ~15% |
| **Mesenchymal** | **20–40** | **Flat bones** (ilium, ribs, skull) · **30% soft tissue** | Moth-eaten / permeative, extraosseous | **Small round blue cells** + hyaline islands · **staghorn (haemangiopericytomatous) vessels** · HEY1–NCOA2 | Wide resection + **chemo ± RT** · <50% 5-yr |
| **Periosteal (juxtacortical)** | 2nd–4th decade | Surface, metaphysis femur/humerus | Juxtacortical mass, rings & arcs · **cortex eroded/saucerized** (vs periosteal chondroma) | Low grade | Wide resection · indolent |
| **Secondary** | 3rd–4th decade | Ilium (MHE) | Cap >2 cm | As conventional | As primary · usually low grade |

### Differential of chondrosarcoma

| | Malignant, older patient | Similar chondroid histology | Sacral lesion, older | Wide resection alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chondrosarcoma** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Metastasis · lymphoma · myeloma · MFH | ✓ | — | ✓ | — |
| Enchondroma / Ollier / Maffucci · periosteal chondroma · MHE | — | ✓ | — | — |
| Parosteal OS · adamantinoma | — | — | — | ✓ |
| **Chordoma** | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |

**Imaging distractors** — **bone infarct** (smoke up the chimney, cold, asymptomatic) · **fibrous dysplasia** (ground glass) · **osteosarcoma** (younger, cloud-like bone) · **plasmacytoma** (uniformly T2 bright, **cold** scan) · Ewing (lytic, no mineral)

## 5 Management

### Treatment algorithm

```
Chondrosarcoma (chemo- and radio-resistant)
├─ Grade 1 / ACT, extremity, confined to bone → EXTENDED CURETTAGE + ADJUVANT + GRAFT ± FIXATION
│      └─ Asymptomatic, no aggressive features → OBSERVE
├─ Grade 2–3, or ANY pelvic/axial lesion → WIDE RESECTION ALONE
├─ Mesenchymal → WIDE RESECTION + CHEMO (± RT)
├─ Dedifferentiated → WIDE RESECTION ± CHEMO
└─ Surgically inaccessible → RADIOTHERAPY
```

### Treatment — wide resection (chemo/radioresistant)

**Why resistant** — **low mitotic rate**, **poor perfusion / low blood supply**, chondroid/**myxoid matrix** limits drug diffusion, few cells in growth phase

**Wide excision** — better survival than marginal or intralesional · grade 2 distal femur → **wide excision**\
✗ Wrong options: curettage · marginal · chemo first · hip disarticulation

**Pelvic CS** — **internal hemipelvectomy or hindquarter amputation** (✗ Wrong options: + chemo · + RT) · **wide resection alone** (✗ Wrong options: brachytherapy)

**Proximal fibula CS** — **wide resection + reconstruct LCL and biceps femoris** (transfer to lateral tibia, staple) → prevents varus instability + flexion weakness\
✗ Wrong options: curettage + liquid N₂ + cement (GCT) · denosumab (GCT) · + chemo (OS)

**Ollier patient with CS** — **wide resection only**

**Clear cell after "benign" curettage** — **wide resection alone**\
✗ Wrong options: chemo · curettage · forequarter

**Lung metastases** — resect if possible **(TO)**

### Low-grade lesion — extended curettage

**ACT / grade 1 extremity** — **extended intralesional curettage + high-speed burr + adjuvant + bone-void graft ± prophylactic fixation** · same recurrence as wide resection, better function

**Adjuvants** — cryotherapy / **liquid nitrogen**, argon beam, phenol, ethanol, hydrogen peroxide

**Pelvis / axial grade 1** — **wide excision** (higher recurrence + metastasis, poor biopsy concordance)

**Not for ACT** — neoadjuvant RT 50–60 Gy · proximal humeral resection + megaprosthesis (old standard) · forequarter amputation

### Dedifferentiated chondrosarcoma

**Treatment** — **wide resection + endoprosthetic reconstruction** · chemo possible role (controversial) · RT palliative/adjuvant only

**IM nail through dedifferentiated CS** — seeds entire femur → **amputation**\
✗ Wrong options: proximal femoral replacement · chemo · RT

**Curettage + cement + THA, histology dedifferentiated** — resect whole bed → **hemipelvectomy**

**Pathologic fracture + high-grade CS** — next test **CT chest**

## 6 Complications

**Local recurrence** — grade 1: rare after wide resection, **5–15% after curettage + adjuvant** · grade 2: margin dependent · grade 3: **25%** recurrence, **>30%** mets · high-grade recurrence → mets + ↓ survival

### Prognostic factors

**Most important** — **histological grade** **(TO — FNB 2016)**\
✗ Wrong options: site · matrix calcification · age

**Poor prognosis** — **high grade** · **axial / proximal** (pelvis) · **advanced age** · **inadequate margins** · **↑ telomerase** (recurrence)

**Not prognostic** — gender · negative margins, younger age, tumour-centre biopsy = **favourable**

**Spine/sacrum CS** — worse local control/survival with **increasing age**

## How it's asked

**"52-yr-old, night shoulder pain, rings-and-arcs, hot bone scan, lobular T2-bright lesion?"** → **chondrosarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: infarct · OS · Ewing · FD · plasmacytoma

**"Tumour resistant to chemo AND radiation?"** → **classic intramedullary chondrosarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: Ewing · synovial sarcoma · chordoma (RT) · DFSP

**"Pelvic CS — treatment?"** → **wide resection alone** (internal hemipelvectomy or hindquarter amputation)

**"Grade 2 distal femoral CS?"** → **wide excision**

**"Symptomatic grade 1 ACT proximal humerus?"** → **extended intralesional curettage + graft ± prophylactic fixation**

**"Proximal fibula CS — best function?"** → **wide resection + LCL + biceps reconstruction**

**"Correct about dedifferentiated CS?"** → **~10% 10-yr survival**\
✗ Wrong options: de novo · homogeneous · curettage · adolescents

**"73-yr-old, pathologic fracture, high-grade spindle cells next to benign cartilage?"** → **dedifferentiated CS**\
✗ Wrong options: synovial sarcoma (keratin glands) · metastatic prostate · periosteal OS

**"Dedifferentiated CS with path fracture — staging test?"** → **CT chest**\
✗ Wrong options: CT CAP · SPEP/UPEP

**"IM nail placed through dedifferentiated CS?"** → **amputation**

**"38-yr-old, subchondral femoral head lesion, clear vacuolated cells?"** → **clear cell chondrosarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: chondroblastoma · GCT

**"Highest malignant risk?"** → **Maffucci** > Ollier > MHE > solitary lesions

**"MHE — malignant histology?"** → **low-grade chondrosarcoma** (EXT1 worst)

**"Secondary CS NOT seen in?"** → **chronic osteomyelitis** (→ SCC) (✓ Paget, ✓ synovial chondromatosis, ✓ osteochondroma) **(TO)**

**"FALSE about CS (FNB 2024)?"** → source gives no answer · **"clear cell CS is predominantly metaphyseal"** is false (it is **epiphyseal**) **(TO)**

**"Most important prognostic factor?"** → **histological grade** **(TO)**

**"Poor prognostic factor in pelvic CS?"** → **pelvic location**\
✗ Wrong options: female · negative margins · younger age

**"Recurrence most directly related to?"** → **↑ telomerase activity**

**"Incidental 'possible chondrosarcoma' on knee MRI, no images — next?"** → **orthogonal radiographs**

**"Adult osteochondroma, 14 mm cap, stable?"** → **serial imaging + exam** (cap ≥2 cm = worry)
