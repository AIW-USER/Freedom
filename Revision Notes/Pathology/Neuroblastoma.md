# Neuroblastoma

*Pathology · 42 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Nature** — malignant tumour of **sympathetic neural tissue** (neural crest) · near **adrenal gland** or spinal cord (neck, chest, abdomen, pelvis)

**Incidence** — ~1/100,000 children · slight male predominance · majority **<2 yr**

**Rank** — **commonest (malignant) solid tumour of childhood** (Orthobullets, OB) · TO: commonest **extracranial / abdominal / infancy** tumour; commonest **solid** tumour overall = **brain tumour** — read the options · leukaemia = commonest childhood malignancy (not solid)

**Small round blue cell tumour by age** — **<5 yr → metastatic neuroblastoma** (also leukaemia) · bone mets common in children

## 3 Presentation

**Systemic** — fever, malaise, weight loss, diarrhoea · **tachycardia** (adrenal)

**Exam** — **abdominal mass**, hepatosplenomegaly

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — bone mets: **permeative, lytic**, anywhere · may cause vertebra plana

**CT chest/abdomen/pelvis** — initial study, visceral extent · **MRI** — anatomy · **bone scan** — bony mets

![Metastatic neuroblastoma](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8081-neuroblastoma-xray.jpg)

**Biopsy** — needle or open incisional required

### Histology

**Small round blue cells forming rosettes** (Homer-Wright) · neurofilament + (vs Ewing −)

**Other small round blue cell tumours of childhood** — rhabdomyosarcoma, non-Hodgkin lymphoma, Ewing/PNET, blastemic Wilms

## 5 Management

```
Neuroblastoma
├─ Very young infant, low risk → OBSERVE (spontaneous regression)
├─ Low grade, resectable with wide margin → SURGICAL EXCISION
├─ High grade / metastatic, unresectable → CHEMO (cisplatin, ifosfamide) + STEM-CELL TRANSPLANT
└─ High grade near NV structures → CHEMO + SCT + EXCISION
```

## 6 Complications

**Prognosis** — **majority cured** · **bone metastasis = poor prognosis** · spontaneous regression in very young

## How it's asked

**"Most common malignant solid tumour of childhood?"** → **neuroblastoma**\
✗ Wrong options: leukaemia (not solid) · lymphoma · Ewing · EG

**"Most common tumour in children (FNB)?"** → **brain tumour** (neuroblastoma = commonest extracranial) **(TO, OS chapter)**

**"Small round blue cell tumour in a child <5?"** → **metastatic neuroblastoma**
