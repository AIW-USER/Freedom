# Non-Ossifying Fibroma

*Pathology · 45 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Commonest benign bone lesion of childhood** (with osteochondroma) · in **30–40% of skeletally immature children** · multiple in **8%**

**Demographics** — **males 2:1** · **5–15 yr**

**Site** — **metaphysis** of long bones · **80% lower limb**: **distal femur > proximal tibia > distal tibia** · uncommon proximal femur/humerus

**Synonyms** — metaphyseal fibrous defect, non-osteogenic fibroma, **fibrous cortical defect** (<2 cm), fibroxanthoma

### Pathophysiology

**Benign fibrogenic lesion** from **dysfunctional ossification** — abnormal subperiosteal osteoclastic resorption during metaphyseal remodelling

**Genetics** — somatic **RAS-MAPK activating mutations** → considered a neoplasm (RASopathy family) · mutation in a subset of cells; histiocytes + giant cells reactive

### Associated conditions

**Jaffe-Campanacci syndrome** — **multiple NOFs + café-au-lait + mental retardation/cognitive delay + heart, eye, gonadal defects + giant cell lesions of the mandible**\
✗ Wrong options: Jaffe-Lichtenstein (FD + café-au-lait) · Maffucci · Mazabraud · McCune-Albright

**Others** — **neurofibromatosis** · familial multifocal NOF · ABC

### Genes & lab markers

**RAS-MAPK** somatic mutations · no lab abnormality

## 3 Presentation

**Asymptomatic, incidental** — found after ankle/knee sprain or fall · pain from the injury, not the lesion

**Pathologic fracture** — ± stress fractures with cortical involvement

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (diagnostic)** — **eccentric, metaphyseal, cortically based "bubbly"/scalloped lytic lesion** with **thin sclerotic rim** · narrow zone of transition · cortex may be thinned/expanded · **length > width** · no periosteal reaction, no soft-tissue mass

![Eccentric bubbly metaphyseal lesion with sclerotic rim](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8027-5a-moved.jpg)

**With growth** — migrates to diaphysis, enlarges (1–7 cm) · near maturity → **sclerotic**, reossifies

**Quantitative CT** — predicts **fracture risk**

**MRI** — T1 low · T2 varies (higher when developing) · thin enhancing rim · rarely needed

**Mimic** — **adductor magnus avulsion / cortical desmoid** at posteromedial distal femur

**No biopsy, MRI, bone scan, staging** for classic lesions

### Histology

**Fibroblastic spindle cells in whorled / storiform pattern** ("helicopters in a wheat field") · fibroblastic background · **lipophages (foamy histiocytes)**, **giant cells** · **haemosiderin** · occasional ABC component

![Storiform fibroblasts with giant cells](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8027-histology-a-low-power-parsons-moved.jpg)

### Differential

| | Bubbly lytic X-ray | Haemosiderin | Observation alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NOF** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| ABC · UBC | ✓ | — | — |
| PVNS | — | ✓ | — |
| FD · enchondroma · osteochondroma · EG · Paget | — | — | ✓ |

| Lesion | Distinguishing |
|---|---|
| **NOF** | **Eccentric, scalloped, metaphyseal**, sclerotic rim |
| **UBC** | **Central**, expands to physis width, **fallen leaf / fallen fragment** sign |
| **FD** | Central, **ground glass** |
| **GCT** | Painful, skeletally mature, subchondral, no mineral, destructive |
| **Osteofibrous dysplasia** | **Diaphyseal, anterior tibial cortex** |
| **Enchondroma** | Central, mineralized, not sclerotic rim |
| **Osteosarcoma** | Painful, permeative, periosteal reaction, mineralized mass |
| **Chondroblastoma** | Epiphyseal |

### Cortical desmoid (TO)

**Posteromedial** distal femoral metaphysis (**adductor magnus** traction), boys 10–15 · cortical erosion with sclerotic base, no lytic lesion · **no biopsy**, observe · "posterolateral" is wrong **(TO)**

## 5 Management

```
Non-ossifying fibroma
├─ Incidental, asymptomatic → OBSERVE (X-ray 6 + 12 mo, then yearly; or 2–3/3–6 mo check)
├─ Pathologic fracture, non-displaced → CAST / SLING, let fracture heal, then observe
└─ Symptomatic + large (>50–75% cortex / >50% diameter) or high QCT risk → CURETTAGE + GRAFT ± PROPHYLACTIC FIXATION
```

**Observation** — first line · **reossifies spontaneously** in 2nd–3rd decade · non-urgent referral \
✗ Wrong options: biopsy · MRI · bone scan · CT staging · cast · CPS

**Pathologic fracture** — treat the fracture (casting · humerus → **sling + analgesia**) · curettage only after healing unless ORIF needed \
✗ Wrong options: IM nail without diagnosis · aspiration + injection (UBC)

**Curettage + bone grafting** — **symptomatic + large** (>50–75% cortical involvement / just over half diameter in weight-bearing bone) · persistent activity pain → **+ prophylactic internal fixation**\
✗ Wrong options: RFA · doxycycline · steroid injection · sclerotherapy · wide excision

## 6 Complications

### Pathologic fracture

**90% lower limb** · **50% distal tibia**

**Risk factors** — **>50% of transverse diameter** · **>33 mm length** in weight-bearing bones

**Prognosis** — spontaneous resolution · **no malignant or metastatic potential**\
✗ Wrong options: 5–10% (that's MHE)

## How it's asked

**"20-yr-old skier, incidental lateral distal femoral sclerotic-rimmed lesion?"** → **reassurance + WBAT**\
✗ Wrong options: biopsy · resection

**"Distal fibula lesion after sprain, asymptomatic 16-yr-old?"** → **activities as tolerated, X-ray in 3–6 mo**\
✗ Wrong options: staging · cast · curettage

**"6-yr-old, knee abrasion, tibial lesion?"** → **repeat X-ray in 3 months**

**"14-yr-old tibial lesion after ankle injury?"** → **routine follow-up**\
✗ Wrong options: endocrine (McCune-Albright) · GI cancer (Gardner) · open biopsy

**"18-yr-old soccer player — diagnosis + treatment?"** → **NOF, observation**\
✗ Wrong options: enchondroma · ABC curettage

**"Associated with NOF EXCEPT?"** → **fallen leaf sign** (UBC) (✓ stress fractures, ✓ commonest childhood lesion, ✓ Jaffe-Campanacci, ✓ haemosiderin)

**"9-yr-old, mild ankle pain, family doctor calls?"** → **symptom management, non-urgent referral**\
✗ Wrong options: urgent CT/labs · RFA · curettage

**"Incidental distal tibial / proximal tibial lesion?"** → **observation**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · bone scan · biopsy

**"15-yr-old 'shadow on the bone', eccentric sclerotic metaphyseal?"** → **NOF**\
✗ Wrong options: GCT · infection · OFD (diaphyseal) · enchondroma

**"NOF best characterized as?"** → **eccentric scalloped metaphyseal radiolucency**\
✗ Wrong options: fallen leaf · central fusiform to physis width (UBC) · ground glass (FD)

**"True of NOF?"** → **typically ossifies spontaneously**\
✗ Wrong options: trauma-related · cast first-line · high fracture risk · 5–10% malignancy

**"Trampoline ankle injury, bubbly lesion — histology?"** → **storiform fibroblasts**

**"Pathologic fracture through which lesion (lobulated, eccentric)?"** → **NOF**

**"Humeral pathologic fracture through eccentric soap-bubble lesion?"** → **sling + pain medication**

**"Outside core biopsy, no treatment needed — histology?"** → **whorled/storiform fibroblastic spindle cells**

**"11-yr-old gymnast, limp, NOF >50% diameter, daily pain?"** → **curettage + bone grafting + prophylactic internal fixation**

**"Persistent pain, 4 cm NOF over half the diameter?"** → **curettage + bone grafting**\
✗ Wrong options: observation · cast · sclerotherapy

**"Multiple NOFs, café-au-lait, cognitive delay, eye problems, mandibular lesion?"** → **Jaffe-Campanacci syndrome**

**"Cortical desmoid — all true EXCEPT?"** → **"posterolateral distal femur"** (it is posteromedial) **(TO)**
