# Chordoma

*Pathology · 11 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rank** — **commonest primary malignant spinal tumour in adults** · commonest primary malignant tumour of the **sacrum** **(TO)** · 1–4% of primary bone tumours

**Demographics** — **M:F 3:1** · **>50 yr** (5th–6th decade) · middle-aged Caucasian men

**Site** — **sacrum + coccyx 50%** (S2–S3 predilection) · **spheno-occipital (skull base) 35%** · **mobile spine 15%**

### Pathoanatomy

**Origin** — malignant transformation of **residual notochordal cells** (same lineage as **nucleus pulposus**) → **midline** location

**Behaviour** — slow-growing, locally aggressive, low-grade malignant · diagnosis often delayed

### Genes & lab markers

**Keratin (pan-cytokeratin) +** — separates from chondrosarcoma (keratin −)

**EMA +** **(TO)** · **S-100 weakly +**

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — insidious **low back / hip pain**, **worse sitting**

**Bowel/bladder** — **constipation**, faecal incontinence, bladder change · UTI symptoms (1/3) · cauda equina 10% **(TO)**

**Motor/sensory deficit** — **rare** (most lesions **S1 or distal**) · S1–S2 lesion may lose L5–S1 function · radiculopathy (sciatic) up to 1/3

**Rectal exam** — **>50% palpable**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **hard to see** (overlying **bowel gas**) · large lytic lesion **crossing the midline** (>50%) → SI joints, ilium

**CT** — **midline bone destruction + soft-tissue mass** · **calcifications** in the soft-tissue component

**MRI** — **bright on T2** · soft-tissue extension

![Sacral chordoma — T2-bright midline mass](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8032-axialmri.jpg)

### Biopsy & histology

**Biopsy required** — MRI alone insufficient (lymphoma, chondrosarcoma mimic) · **transrectal biopsy contraindicated**

**Gross** — **lobular, gelatinous**

**Histology** — **physaliferous cells**: large, **foamy, vacuolated** (eosinophilic) cytoplasm, vesicular nucleus — **pathognomonic** · cords/lobules in **mucoid matrix** separated by fibrous septa · distinct nodules

![Physaliferous cells](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8032-histology-c-parsons-moved.jpg)

### Differential

| | Sacral lesion, older patient | Keratin + | Similar X-ray | Wide resection alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chordoma** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Chondrosarcoma** | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Metastatic carcinoma | Yes | Yes | Yes | — |
| Lymphoma of bone | Yes | — | Yes | — |
| Myeloma · MFH | Yes | — | — | — |
| Adamantinoma (tibia) | — | Yes | — | Yes |
| Synovial sarcoma · epithelioid sarcoma | — | Yes | — | — |

**Other sacral tumours** — myeloma, **GCT**, ABC, metastasis

## 5 Management

### Treatment & local recurrence

**Standard** — **wide-margin en bloc resection** · **sacrifice sacral roots** to get margins · wide tumour-free margin ∝ tumour-free survival

**Radiation** — **add if margin not achieved** (positive margins) · **inoperable** tumours

**TO answer** — sacral chordoma → **wide resection alone** **(TO)** · chemo- and relatively radio-resistant; RT/SRS for recurrence\
✗ Wrong options: + EBRT · + chemo · curettage + bisphosphonate

**Mobile spine** — **en bloc corpectomy / total vertebrectomy** → best local control · needs anterior + posterior reconstruction

**Not indicated** — chemotherapy (chemoresistant) · debulking (intralesional) · RT alone · observation

### Radiosurgery (CyberKnife)

**Stereotactic radiosurgery** — recurrent/residual **chordoma** and **chondrosarcoma** after failed surgery + fractionated RT **(TO)**

**CyberKnife** — extracranial → **sacral chordoma** ✓ · **Gamma knife** — intracranial only **(TO)**

**Proton–photon beam** — may help recurrence

### Sacral nerve root sparing & function

| Roots preserved / resected | Bowel & bladder |
|---|---|
| **Bilateral S3** preserved | Near-normal (Orthobullets) · **S3 most critical** |
| **Both S2** preserved (at least) | Near-normal (OB) |
| **Unilateral S2, S3, S4 (± S5)** preserved | Near-normal ✓ (OB answer: unilateral S2–S5) |
| **Bilateral S4–S5 resected** | **Near-normal** (S2–S3 splanchnics kept) |
| **Bilateral S2–S5 resected** | **Complete loss** |
| Bilateral S1 / L5 only | Not enough |

**Pelvic splanchnic nerves** — **S2–S4** (S3 predominance) → parasympathetic to pelvic viscera

## 6 Complications

**Local recurrence** — **~50%**, margin dependent → **close surveillance**\
✗ Wrong options: nodal · liver · bone mets · malignant transformation

**Metastasis** — **30–50%**, **late** (lung, rarely bone) · OB: 30–50% already metastatic at diagnosis

**Survival** — **60% 5-yr**, **25% long-term** (25–50%) · local extension may be fatal

**Poor prognosis (TO)** — high histological grade · volume **>10 cm / 200 cm³**

**Post-op** — loss of bowel/bladder function (root sacrifice)

## X vs Y

| | **Chordoma** | **Chondrosarcoma** |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | Notochord, **midline** | Cartilage, often **eccentric** (pelvis) |
| Cells | **Physaliferous** | Atypical chondrocytes in lacunae |
| Keratin / EMA | **+** | **−** |
| S-100 | Weak + | + |
| Treatment | Wide resection ± RT (margin +) | Wide resection |

## How it's asked

**"Sacral lytic lesion, 58-yr-old, lobules with vacuolated physaliferous cells?"** → **chordoma**\
✗ Wrong options: chondrosarcoma · metastatic RCC · CMF · GCT

**"All true of sacral chordoma EXCEPT?"** → **"MRI is sufficient without biopsy"** (✓ notochordal like nucleus pulposus, ✓ mets 30–50%, ✓ motor deficit rare, ✓ resection ± RT)

**"Treatment of sacral chordoma?"** → **wide resection alone** (✗ Wrong options: + EBRT · + chemo) **(TO)** · OB: **wide surgical excision** (✗ Wrong options: chemo · debulking · RT alone)

**"L5 en bloc corpectomy is right for which histology?"** → **chordoma**\
✗ Wrong options: osteomyelitis · myeloma · LCH · lung adenocarcinoma

**"Why surveillance after resection?"** → **high local recurrence**

**"Keratin + and S-100 + sacral mass?"** → **chordoma**

**"Tumour treated by CyberKnife?"** → **chordoma** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: vertebral osteoblastoma · sacral OS · GCT

**"Near-normal bowel/bladder if which roots spared?"** → **unilateral S2–S5** (or both S2 / bilateral S3)

**"Bilateral S4–5 vs bilateral S2–5 resection?"** → **S4–5 patient near-normal**, S2–5 patient loses function
