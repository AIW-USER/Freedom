# Glomus Tumor

*Pathology · 23 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Nature** — rare **benign** tumour (hamartoma) of the **glomus body** · formerly "paraganglioma" · soft tissue and/or bone · malignant cases rare

**Age / sex** — **20–40** (3rd–4th decades) · females

**Site** — **hand 75%** · **subungual 50%** · palm, wrist, forearm, foot less often

**Bone** — **erosion of distal phalanx in 50%** · primary intraosseous rare

### Anatomy

**Glomus body** — **perivascular temperature-regulating** structure (arteriovenous shunt) at **digit tip / beneath nail**

## 3 Presentation

**Classic triad** — **paroxysmal pain** · **exquisite point tenderness** · **cold intolerance** (cold water worsens pain)

**Signs** — small **bluish nodule** (often invisible subungually) · **nail ridging / discoloration** · may show no mass or colour change

**Delay** — diagnosis often delayed for years

### Clinical tests

| Test | Method | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| **Love test** | **Pinhead pressure on the spot → excruciating pain**, none just beside it | **100% sensitive**, 78% accurate |
| **Hildreth test** | **Tourniquet inflation → pain + Love-test tenderness abolished** | **92% sensitive, 91% specific** |
| Cold sensitivity | Cold water exacerbates pain | — |

**Love test diagnostic of** — **glomus tumour** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: trochanteric bursitis · hydatid cyst · SLE

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **pressure erosion** of distal phalanx, cortical deformity · intraosseous → cystic erosive change · often **normal**

**MRI** — establishes diagnosis · **T1 low, T2 high**, small enhancing subungual nodule

![Glomus erosion into distal phalanx](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8075-glomus-erosion-into-distal-phalanx.jpg)

### Histology

**Well-defined**, **no atypia or mitoses** · uniform **small round cells with dark nuclei** and pink cytoplasm (epithelioid glomus cells) surrounding **small vascular spaces** in hyaline/myxoid stroma · gland-like nests

![Glomus cells around vascular spaces](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8075-glomus-tumor-high-power.jpg)

### Differential

| Lesion | Distinguishing |
|---|---|
| **Glomus tumour** | Triad, MRI nodule, glomus cells |
| Raynaud phenomenon | Cold sensitivity, **no mass** |
| Epidermal inclusion cyst | Prior trauma, less point tenderness / cold pain |
| Osteomyelitis | Labs, systemic, X-ray changes |
| Osteoid osteoma | Cortical nidus + sclerosis |
| Melanoma · SCC · synovial / epithelioid sarcoma | Different histology |

## 5 Management

**Marginal excision** — **curative** · for symptoms affecting quality of life · complete pain relief

**Nail bed reconstruction** — autologous **fat graft** for large defects

**Locally aggressive / intraosseous destructive** — aggressive resection, even **amputation** suggested

## 6 Complications

**Recurrence** — **~20%** (Orthobullets) · "uncommon" (benign) — usually incomplete excision

## How it's asked

**"20-yr-old, painful ecchymotic ring fingertip, eroded phalanx, dark-nucleated round cells?"** → **glomus tumour**\
✗ Wrong options: melanoma · SCC · synovial/epithelioid sarcoma

**"43-yr-old, cold-sensitive fingertip pain, ulnar nail-bed tenderness, normal X-ray, MRI nodule?"** → **glomus tumour**\
✗ Wrong options: Raynaud · inclusion cyst · osteomyelitis · osteoid osteoma

**"Little-finger tip pain worse with cold — histology vascular spaces with epithelioid cells?"** → **glomus tumour**\
✗ Wrong options: schwannoma · scleroderma · paronychia

**"Love test is diagnostic of?"** → **glomus tumour** **(TO)**

**"Treatment?"** → **marginal excision**
