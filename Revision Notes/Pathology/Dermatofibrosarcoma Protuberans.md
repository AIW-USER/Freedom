# Dermatofibrosarcoma Protuberans

*Pathology · 13 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **1% of soft-tissue sarcomas** · rare, **low-grade fibrogenic cutaneous** sarcoma arising in the **dermis**

**Demographics** — **20–50 yr** (peak 4th decade) · **male** (3:2) · **African Americans 2:1**

**Site** — **trunk (47%) > proximal extremities > head/neck (14%)**

### Variants

**Bednar tumour** — **pigmented** · **7.5× more common in African Americans** · melanin-containing dendritic cells

**Fibrosarcomatous (DFSP-FS)** — 5–15% · **herringbone** pattern · more aggressive, poor prognosis

### Genes & lab markers

**t(17;22)** — **COL1A1–PDGFB** fusion → PDGF-β overexpression → target for **imatinib**

## 3 Presentation

**Mass** — **slowly enlarging, painless**, firm irregular **nodules** · dark red / blue discoloration · early pink/violet plaques with telangiectatic skin

**Late** — ulceration, attachment to deep structures · symptoms 6 months to 30 years · often misdiagnosed and **excised before sarcoma suspected**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**MRI** — extent + depth · preop for large, atypical, recurrent lesions

**CXR** — pulmonary mets in high-risk cases (recurrence, fibrosarcomatous)

**CT** — bone involvement / mets · **US** — local + nodal monitoring · **PET** — metastatic monitoring

### Histology

**Biopsy required**

**Classic** — **uniform fibroblasts in a storiform pattern** around inconspicuous vessels · diffusely infiltrates dermis/subcutis · **interdigitates with fat ("honeycomb")** at deep margin

## 5 Management

### Treatment — wide excision

**Resectable DFSP** — **wide excision** (with frozen sections) · **2–3 cm margins** (2.5–3 cm skin cuff) **including deep fascia**

**After "lumpectomy"** — **wide tumour bed re-excision**\
✗ Wrong options: observation · marginal resection · RT alone · chemo

**Adjuvant RT (50–70 Gy)** — **positive margins**, large tumours, or when wide excision → major cosmetic/functional deficit

**Imatinib** — PDGF-receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor · **unresectable, recurrent, metastatic** adult DFSP · **65% response** · **no response without t(17;22)**

**Not indicated** — intralesional excision + RT · neoadjuvant chemo · Mohs referral (OB answer: wide excision)

## 6 Complications

**Local recurrence common** — body **15.7%** · **head/neck 51.8%**

**Distant metastasis rare (<5%)** — **lung** (haematogenous) · usually after **multiple local recurrences**

**Poor prognosis** — regional nodes · **fibrosarcomatous** change · ↑ mitoses, cellularity, aneuploidy, **TP53** overexpression · **age >50**

## X vs Y

| | **Classic DFSP** | **Fibrosarcomatous DFSP** | **Bednar** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pattern | **Storiform** | **Herringbone** | Storiform + melanin dendritic cells |
| Behaviour | Low grade | Aggressive, can metastasize | Low grade |

## How it's asked

**"45-yr-old, leg mass with skin ulceration, storiform spindle cells?"** → **dermatofibrosarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: Ewing/PNET · extraskeletal myxoid CS · myxoid liposarcoma · synovial sarcoma (deep, rarely ulcerate)

**"Shoulder skin mass after excisional biopsy shows DFSP — next?"** → **wide tumour-bed resection**

**"Abdominal wall nodular mass, t(17;22), herringbone areas?"** → **wide excision** (± RT)\
✗ Wrong options: Mohs · marginal · neoadjuvant chemo

**"Systemic agent for unresectable DFSP?"** → **imatinib** (only if t(17;22))

**"Tumour treated with chemo + RT (in a list of chemo/RT-resistant tumours)?"** → **DFSP** (vs classic chondrosarcoma resistant to both)
