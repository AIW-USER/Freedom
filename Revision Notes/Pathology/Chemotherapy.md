# Chemotherapy

*Pathology · 7 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Apoptosis mechanism

**Primary mechanism** — conventional chemo kills cancer cells by **apoptosis** (programmed cell death) · targets **rapidly dividing cells**

**Side effects** — gut lining, **bone marrow**, hair, skin (also rapidly dividing)

**Targeted agents** — tyrosine kinase inhibitors: **imatinib** (CML) · **gefitinib** (EGFR; lung, breast) · **erlotinib** (EGFR; NSCLC, pancreas)

**Role** — eliminates **lung micrometastases**

| Therapy | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Doxorubicin neoadjuvant chemo** | ✓ **Apoptosis** (blocks topoisomerase II, DNA/RNA synthesis) |
| Phenol in GCT bed | **Lysis** of residual cells (not apoptosis) |
| TNF-α inhibitor (RA) | Immune modulation |
| Colchicine (gout) | Immune / crystal inhibition |
| Vancomycin | Cell-wall synthesis inhibition |

### Genes & lab markers

**MDR (multi-drug resistance) gene** — pumps drug out of cell → **very poor prognosis** · in **25% primary**, **50% metastatic** lesions

**Necrosis >98%** after chemo — **good prognostic sign** (osteosarcoma)

## 5 Management

### Indications

**Chemo is integral (with surgery)** — **osteosarcoma** (intramedullary + periosteal) · **Ewing / PNET** · **MFH (UPS)** · **dedifferentiated chondrosarcoma**

**Controversial** — soft-tissue sarcoma

**Schedule** — **preop 8–12 weeks** → surgery → **maintenance 6–12 months**

### Agents, toxicities & antidotes

| Agent | Dose-limiting / classic toxicity |
|---|---|
| **Doxorubicin (Adriamycin)** | ✓ **Cardiomyopathy / CHF** (cumulative dose) · ~7% |
| Ifosfamide | **Encephalopathy** (neurotoxicity) |
| Cyclophosphamide | **Myelosuppression, urotoxicity** |
| Bleomycin | **Pulmonary fibrosis** |

**Doxorubicin** — **anthracycline** antibiotic, cytostatic · delayed LV wall thinning → dilated cardiomyopathy · **dexrazoxane** protective

| Extravasated agent | Antidote |
|---|---|
| Mechlorethamine / cisplatin | **Sodium thiosulfate** |
| **Doxorubicin / epirubicin** | **Dexrazoxane** |
| Vinca alkaloids (vincristine, vinblastine) | **Hot compress + hyaluronidase** |
| Other vesicants | Cold compress |

## How it's asked

**"Major (dose-limiting) side effect of doxorubicin?"** → **cardiomyopathy / cardiac toxicity**\
✗ Wrong options: pulmonary fibrosis (bleomycin) · myelosuppression/urotoxicity (cyclophosphamide) · encephalopathy (ifosfamide) · hearing loss · neuropathy

**"Pharmacologically induced apoptosis occurs with?"** → **doxorubicin neoadjuvant chemotherapy**\
✗ Wrong options: phenol in GCT · anti-TNF · colchicine · vancomycin

**"True of apoptosis?"** → **primary mechanism by which chemotherapy kills cancer cells**

**"Drug protecting against doxorubicin cardiotoxicity?"** → **dexrazoxane**
