# Chondroblastoma

*Pathology · 8 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rare** — ~**1%** of osseous neoplasms · **M:F 2:1**

**Age** — **80% <25 yr** · 10–25 typical (2nd decade) · average diagnosis 19–23 · rare after physeal closure

**Synonym** — **"Codman tumour"** **(TO)**

**Site** — **epiphysis of immature long bones** · may **cross the physis into the metaphysis** · **knee** (distal femur, proximal tibia) commonest → **proximal humerus** distant third · proximal femur\
✗ Wrong options: metaphysis **(TO)**

**Epiphyseal equivalents** — **apophysis** (greater tuberosity, greater trochanter) · **patella** · **talus, calcaneus** · flat bones

**Mnemonic "ECG"** — **E**piphysis: **C**hondroblastoma, **G**iant cell tumour (+ clear cell chondrosarcoma) **(TO)**

### Pathophysiology

**Benign, locally destructive** proliferation of **immature cartilage cells (chondroblasts)** · from epiphyseal plate / secondary ossification centre (controversial)

**Matrix** — chondroid matrix · **hyaline cartilage rarely** produced

**Natural history** — progressive bone destruction · **does not heal spontaneously**

**Secondary ABC** — up to **1/3** (15–32% on imaging)

### Genes & lab markers

**Genetics** — **histone H3.3** mutations · **chromosome 5 and 8** abnormalities

**IHC** — **S-100 +** · **SOX9 +** · vimentin, neuron-specific enolase + (GCT, ABC: no S-100)

**Bloods** — CBC, ESR, LDH normal (vs osteomyelitis, Ewing)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **progressive pain** (often night pain, not relieved by NSAIDs) · **joint stiffness** · **limp**

**Signs** — local muscle atrophy · tenderness · ↓ ROM · **effusion**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray views** — AP, lateral, oblique · **chest radiograph** (lung mets) at diagnosis

**X-ray** — **geographic, well-circumscribed round/oval lytic epiphyseal lesion** with **thin sclerotic rim** · may cross physis · 1–4 cm, lobular · expansile cortical remodelling · **metaphyseal periosteal reaction** (no periosteum over epiphysis) · effusion

![Lytic epiphyseal lesion with sclerotic rim — proximal tibia](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8021-tibia-classic-lesion.jpg)

**Calcification** — stippled matrix calcification, often **only on CT (25–45%)**

**MRI** — **extensive bone-marrow + soft-tissue oedema around the lesion** — distinguishing feature **(TO)** · T1 intermediate · T2 heterogeneous · fluid levels = secondary ABC · oedema within treated lesion → recurrence

![Peritumoral oedema on MRI](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8021-peritumoral-edema.jpg)

**Bone scan** — not required · ↑ uptake, especially adjacent uninvolved bone

**CT chest** — if chest film suspicious

**Soap bubble** — classically GCT, but chondroblastoma also in DD **(TO)** · DD: ABC, GCT, chondroblastoma, enchondroma, CMF, fibrous dysplasia, ameloblastoma, plasmacytoma, chordoma

**Benign lesions with marrow oedema (TO)** — **osteoid osteoma, osteoblastoma, chondroblastoma, LCH**\
✗ Wrong options: osteochondroma · enchondroma · GCT

### Histology

**Cells** — sheets of **polygonal mononuclear chondroblasts**, well-defined borders · **"cobblestone"** arrangement

**Nuclei** — large, central, **longitudinal groove → "coffee-bean"** · scarce mitoses

**Calcification** — fine pericellular **"chicken-wire"** dystrophic calcification

**Also** — scattered **multinucleated osteoclast-type giant cells** (almost always) · islands of amorphous **chondroid matrix**

![Chondroblasts with chicken-wire calcification](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8021-histology-b-parsons-moved.png)

**Recurrence** — may show atypia → not malignant transformation

### Differential of an epiphyseal lesion

| Lesion | Key differentiating finding |
|---|---|
| **Chondroblastoma** | **Skeletally immature**, progressive pain, matrix mineralization, **extensive peritumoral oedema** |
| **Giant cell tumour** | **Skeletally mature, 20–40**, closed physes · starts in metaphysis → epiphysis · pathologic fracture common · giant-cell nuclei = stromal nuclei · S-100 negative |
| **Clear cell chondrosarcoma** | **3rd–5th decade**, **>4 cm**, **less oedema**, malignant · clear cells |
| Brodie abscess | Immature, often metaphyseal · **penumbra** sign on MRI · neutrophils |
| Intraosseous ganglion | Middle age · no periosteal reaction · cystic, less oedema |
| Eosinophilic granuloma | Variable appearance |
| Brown tumour | >50, CKD / hyperparathyroidism |
| ABC | <20, multicystic, fluid levels · not typically epiphyseal |
| **Osteoblastoma** | 2nd–3rd decade, rarely epiphyseal, mineralization, oedema |
| Osteoid osteoma | Diaphyseal, NSAID-relieved night pain, nidus on CT |
| TB arthritis | Epiphyseal possible · **granulomas** |

## 5 Management

### Treatment algorithm

```
Chondroblastoma (does not heal spontaneously)
├─ Most lesions → EXTENDED INTRALESIONAL CURETTAGE + ADJUVANT + GRAFT
├─ Small (~1 cm) lesion → RADIOFREQUENCY ABLATION
├─ Rib / aggressive pelvic lesion with soft-tissue extension → EN BLOC RESECTION
├─ Destructive aggressive recurrence (rare) → ENDOPROSTHESIS / AMPUTATION
└─ Lung metastases
       ├─ Solitary / oligometastases → RESECTION
       └─ Numerous → DENOSUMAB
```

### Curettage & bone grafting

**Standard of care** — **intralesional curettage + local adjuvant + bone graft** (or PMMA)

**Approach** — through **cortical window** · extend with **high-speed burr** · **spare the physis** if possible

**Adjuvants** — **phenol**, **hydrogen peroxide**, **argon beam**, **cryotherapy / liquid nitrogen**

**Graft** — fill cavity · **structural osteochondral graft** for weight-bearing surfaces

**RFA** — first-line option for small lesions (~**1 cm**) **(TO)** · recurrence ≈ curettage · success on MRI = necrosis beyond margin + resolution of oedema

**Not indicated** — observation (no medical therapy, no spontaneous healing) · **denosumab** (GCT) · chemo · radiation · wide resection (non-expendable bone) · steroid injection · TKA

## 6 Complications

**Local recurrence** — **10–15%** (OB review 14–18%) · surgery and RFA alike · **purely epiphyseal lesions recur more** · due to inadequate resection · **surveillance X-rays 5 yr**

**Limb length discrepancy** — physeal damage by tumour or treatment → contralateral epiphysiodesis vs lengthening

**Degenerative joint disease** — subchondral/cartilage damage → THA/TKA in adulthood

### Lung metastasis — chest radiograph

**"Benign" pulmonary metastasis** — **<1%** (Orthobullets) · **2%** (one OB explanation) · like GCT · indolent → resect

**Chest X-ray** — the benign lesion that **needs a chest film at screening**\
✗ Wrong options: enchondroma · NOF · osteochondroma · UBC

**Malignant transformation** — exceedingly rare · median age **52** · permeative growth, atypia, **no osteoid** · wide resection

## X vs Y

| | **Chondroblastoma** | **GCT** | **Clear cell chondrosarcoma** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age | **10–25, open physis** | **20–40, closed physis** | 20–40 / 3rd–5th decade |
| Site | Epiphysis (± crosses physis) | Metaphysis → epiphysis, subchondral | Epi-metaphysis (proximal femur/humerus) |
| MRI oedema | **Extensive** | Less | **Less** |
| Histology | Chondroblasts, coffee-bean nuclei, chicken-wire Ca | Giant-cell nuclei = stromal nuclei | **Clear cells**, malignant |
| Marker | **S-100, SOX9** | **H3F3A**, RANKL | — |
| Treatment | Curettage + adjuvant + graft / RFA | Curettage + adjuvant · denosumab | **Wide excision** |

| | **Chondroblastoma** | **Chondromyxoid fibroma** |
|---|---|---|
| Site | **Epiphysis** | **Metaphysis** (anterior proximal tibia) |
| Histology | Polygonal chondroblasts, chicken-wire Ca | **Stellate cells** in chondroid/myxoid/fibrous lobules |

## How it's asked

**"Statement NOT true of chondroblastoma?"** → **"commonly involves metaphysis"** (✓ Codman tumour, ✓ RFA for small lesions, ✓ chicken-wire Ca) **(TO)**

**"15-yr-old, epiphyseal proximal tibial soap-bubble lesion?"** → **chondroblastoma** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: GCT (20–40) · CMF (metaphyseal) · osteoid osteoma (diaphyseal)

**"Benign tumour with extensive surrounding marrow/soft-tissue oedema on MRI?"** → **chondroblastoma** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: GCT · enchondroma · osteochondroma

**"Adolescent, epiphyseal lytic lesion, S-100 +, normal bloods — histology?"** → **chondroblasts with chicken-wire calcification**\
✗ Wrong options: GCT · ABC · Ewing · osteomyelitis

**"16-yr-old, medial heel / calcaneal epiphyseal-equivalent lesion?"** → **chondroblastoma**\
✗ Wrong options: GCT · CMF (stellate cells) · osteoblastoma

**"18-yr-old, greater tuberosity lesion?"** → **apophyseal chondroblastoma**

**"Best treatment?"** → **extended intralesional curettage + bone graft ± adjuvant**\
✗ Wrong options: denosumab · chemo · observation · radiation · wide resection · TKA

**"Benign lesion requiring chest radiograph at initial work-up?"** → **chondroblastoma**\
✗ Wrong options: enchondroma · NOF · osteochondroma · UBC

**"Recurrence rate after curettage?"** → **10–15%** · higher if purely epiphyseal
