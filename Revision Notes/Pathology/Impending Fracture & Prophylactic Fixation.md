# Impending Fracture & Prophylactic Fixation

*Pathology · 27 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & prognosis

**Commonest site of all bone metastases** — **spine**

**Commonest site of pathologic fracture from metastasis** — **proximal femur** (femur = commonest long bone; stress risers)\
✗ Wrong options: distal femur · humerus · thoracic spine

**Upper limb** — 20% of skeletal mets · **50% of those in the humerus**

**Shortest survival after pathologic fracture** — **lung** (and melanoma): median **<6 months**, 5-yr <5%\
✗ Wrong options: thyroid · prostate · breast · renal

### Why fix prophylactically

**Prophylactic > fixing an actual fracture** — shorter operation · less morbidity · quicker recovery

**Fracture-risk predictors** — significant **functional pain** · **>50% cortical destruction** · formal systems: **Harrington** and **Mirels**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Mirels scoring

**Purpose** — **quantify risk of impending pathologic fracture** in a neoplastic bone lesion **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: spinal operability · Scheuermann

| Score | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Site** | Upper limb | Lower limb | **Peritrochanteric** |
| **Pain** | Mild | Moderate | **Functional** |
| **Lesion** | Blastic | Mixed | **Lytic** |
| **Size** (fraction of cortical thickness) | <1/3 | 1/3–2/3 | **>2/3** |

**Threshold** — **>8 → prophylactic fixation** (Orthobullets, TO) · "**8 or higher**" (OB) · one OB explanation says ">9" — read the options · **≤8 painful → radiation**

**Worked examples**

| Case | Score | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Breast mets, proximal humerus, moderate pain, blastic, 1/3–2/3 | **1 + 2 + 1 + 2 = 6** | **Radiation** |
| RCC, humerus, severe (functional) pain, lytic, >2/3 | **1 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 10** | Fix |
| Myeloma, peritrochanteric, functional pain, lytic, >2/3 | **3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12** | **Prophylactic fixation + RT** |

### Harrington criteria

**Use** — prophylactic fixation of impending pathologic fracture of the **proximal femur** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: scoliosis · Blount stapling · proximal humeral fracture

| Criterion |
|---|
| **>50% destruction of diaphyseal cortices** |
| **>50–75% destruction of metaphysis (>2.5 cm)** |
| **Permeative destruction of subtrochanteric femur** |
| **Persistent pain after irradiation** |

**Limitation (TO)** — not validated · proximal femur only

## 5 Management

### Treatment algorithm

```
Impending pathologic fracture
├─ No proven primary with biopsy-proven bone mets → BIOPSY FIRST
│      └─ Primary bone sarcoma → CLOSE, REFER TO SARCOMA CENTRE (no nailing)
├─ Mirels ≤8, painful → RADIATION
└─ Mirels >8 → PROPHYLACTIC FIXATION (protect whole bone) → POST-OP RADIATION
```

**Biopsy** — may need a separate incision from nail incision · nailing a sarcoma **contaminates the whole bone** → loses limb salvage

**Goals of fixation** — **immediate mobilization + weight bearing** · **protect the entire bone** · implant matched to prognosis

### Prophylactic fixation by site

| Site | Fixation |
|---|---|
| **Proximal humerus (Bickel I)** | **Endoprosthesis** (✗ Wrong options: IM nail (poor hold in metaphyseal flare) · plate (thin cortex)) |
| **Humeral diaphysis (Bickel II)** | **IM nail** · resection + intercalary spacer · plate + screws (less preferred) |
| **Distal humerus (Bickel III)** | **Flexible nails** · elbow replacement |
| **Femoral neck / head** | **Cemented hemiarthroplasty** (fails by dislocation, infection) |
| **Peri-/subtrochanteric** | **Cephalomedullary (IM) nail** (fails by nonunion, AVN) (✗ Wrong options: proximal femoral replacement (dissection, weakness, infection, dislocation) · curettage alone (stress riser)) |

**Post-operative radiation** — ↓ pain, slows progression, treats residual tumour (✓ for myeloma lesion after fixation)

## How it's asked

**"Mirels criteria are used for?"** → **quantifying impending fracture risk in a neoplastic lesion** **(TO)**

**"Breast mets, proximal humerus, moderate pain, blastic, 1/3–2/3 — Mirels + next step?"** → **6 → radiation**\
✗ Wrong options: plate at 8 · nail at 7 · replacement

**"RCC humeral lytic lesion >2/3, severe pain — Mirels?"** → **10**

**"Myeloma subtrochanteric lytic lesion, functional pain?"** → **prophylactic internal fixation + radiation** (Mirels 12)\
✗ Wrong options: RT alone · curettage · proximal femoral replacement

**"Commonest site of pathologic fracture from lung mets?"** → **proximal femur**

**"Primary with shortest survival after pathologic fracture?"** → **lung**

**"Harrington criteria are used for?"** → **prophylactic fixation of impending proximal femoral fracture** **(TO)**
