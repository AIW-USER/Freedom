# Unicameral Bone Cyst

*Pathology · 66 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Diagnosis, location & natural history

**Nature** — **non-neoplastic, serous (yellow) fluid-filled** cyst · aka **simple bone cyst (SBC)** · **3%** of biopsied bone tumours / of primary tumours in first 2 decades

**Age** — **<20 yr (85%)** · peak **3–14**, mean **~9** · **M:F 2:1**

**Site** — long bones **95%**, **central metaphysis next to physis** → migrates to diaphysis with growth

| Site | Share |
|---|---|
| **Proximal humerus** | **50–60%** (Orthobullets) · **52%** (OB219296) |
| **Proximal femur** | **30%** (Orthobullets) · **25%** (OB219296) · 50% of femoral cysts in patients >17 |
| Humerus + femur | **>80%** (TO) · 90% (OB5435) |
| Calcaneus | 6% · commonest tarsal |
| Others | Distal tibia, ilium, spine (posterior elements), metacarpals, phalanges, distal radius · iliac wing, ribs, talus, radius → adults |

**Pathophysiology** — **venous stasis** in cancellous bone → resorption · ↑ pressure, inflammatory proteins, **↑ lysosomal enzymes** in fluid · "temporary failure of medullary bone formation near the physis" · resolves after losing contact with physis

**Genes / labs** — none · **no labs needed**\
✗ Wrong options: ALP (OS) · ESR · IgG (myeloma) · LDH (Ewing/OS)

### Active vs latent

| | **Active** | **Latent** |
|---|---|---|
| Distance to physis | **Adjacent, ~1 cm** (Orthobullets) · **<5 mm** (TO) | Normal bone between cyst and physis · **>5 mm** (TO) |
| Steroid injection | Indicated, but lower success | — |
| Curettage / fixation | **Avoid → growth arrest** | Symptomatic, failed injections, proximal femur |

![Latent cyst — normal bone between cyst and physis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8035-case-c-inactive-parsons-moved.png)

**Natural history** — **shrinks near skeletal maturity, may heal after growth** · Donaldson: **none fully healed** though 87% of physes closed\
✗ Wrong options: grows · malignant

## 3 Presentation

**~80% asymptomatic** · found incidentally or after fracture

**Pathologic fracture** — presents with fracture in **50–75%** · **63–87%** eventually fracture **(TO)** · **commonest cause of pathologic fracture in children** · minor trauma (throwing, sport)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (diagnostic)** — **central, lytic, well-demarcated metaphyseal** lesion, thin sclerotic rim · **unilocular**, **symmetric cortical thinning**, smooth endosteal scalloping · **no periosteal reaction** · **not wider than the physis** · oblong along long axis · 2–3% cross physis · multiloculated/trabeculated after fractures

| Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Fallen leaf / fallen fragment** | Cortical fragment in dependent part after fracture — **pathognomonic** (✗ Wrong options: before fracture) **(TO)** |
| **Trapdoor** | Fragment hinged on periosteum, folds inward **(TO)** |
| **Rising bubble** | Gas in non-dependent part — pathognomonic |
| **Doughnut** (bone scan) | Cold centre, warm rim |

![Fallen fragment sign](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8035-case-b-fallen-fragment-parsons-moved.jpg)

**CT** — spine/pelvis · wall thickness, occult fracture, fracture risk · thin wall, pseudo-septa

**MRI** — T1 dark (**better fracture-risk predictor** than X-ray) · T2 bright · **rim enhancement** · few septae/fluid levels · use when malignancy not excluded\
✗ Wrong options: double-density fluid levels (ABC)

**Bone scan / PET** — no role

### Fracture-risk predictors (TO)

| Index | High risk |
|---|---|
| **Bone cyst index** (Kaelin & MacEwen) = cyst area ÷ diaphysis diameter² | **>4 humerus** · **>3.5 femur** (MRI better than X-ray) |
| **Bone cyst diameter** (Ahn & Park) | **>85%** on AP **and** lateral |
| **Minimal cortical thickness** | **<2 mm** |

**Caveat** — poor inter/intra-observer reliability · BCI remains best

### Histology

**Thin fibrous lining** · fibrous tissue, **giant cells**, **haemosiderin** · few chronic inflammatory cells · **cementum spherules** (10%) · bland spindle cells · biopsy rarely needed (**fracture callus may mimic osteosarcoma**)

![Thin fibrous cyst lining](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8035-histology-a-parsons-moved.jpg)

### Differential

| | Bubbly lytic | Usually aspiration + injection | Occasionally curettage + graft |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UBC** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **ABC** | ✓ | — | — |
| **NOF** | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| **Enchondroma** (solitary) | — | — | ✓ |

| Eccentric vs central (TO) | Features |
|---|---|
| **SBC — central** | Least likely for an **eccentric** lytic lesion |
| **ABC — eccentric** | 1–6% of primary bone tumours · <20 · slight F · femur > tibia, spine, pelvis · expansile, bulging cortex · **wider than physis** · MRI fluid levels (CT shows them in 1/3) · secondary, solid (giant cell reparative granuloma), soft-tissue forms |
| **Fibrous cortical defect — eccentric** | 2–15 yr · commonest benign bone lesion (with NOF up to 40%) · M 2:1 · intracortical oval lucency, posteromedial distal femur |
| **GCT — eccentric** | Epiphysis → metaphysis, **closed physes**, non-sclerotic margin, subchondral · fluid levels 10–14% (ABC component) |

| Other distractor | Key feature |
|---|---|
| **Telangiectatic OS** | Expansile, matrix, Codman, soft-tissue mass · ↑ WBC, platelets, **LDH, ALP** |
| **FD** | Ground glass |
| **OFD** | Anterior tibial cortex, osteoblastic rimming |
| **EG** | Coffee-bean nuclei, **Birbeck (tennis-racket)** granules |
| ABC histology | Many RBCs, giant cells, septa |
| Chondroblastoma · CMF · osteochondroma | Epiphyseal · chondromyxoid lobules · cartilage cap |

## 5 Management

### Pathologic fracture — sling & allow healing

```
UBC with pathologic fracture
├─ Proximal humerus / non-weight-bearing → SLING (4–6 wk) — let fracture heal
│      (no biopsy, staging, MRI or ORIF)
│      └─ Cyst persists → ASPIRATION + INJECTION (steroid / marrow / DBM)
└─ Proximal femur / weight-bearing, displaced, unstable, open → CURETTAGE + GRAFT + INTERNAL FIXATION
```

**Immobilization** — sling **4–6 wk** (OB) · 3–6 wk (OB5683) · plaster cast **(TO)** · **~15% fill in** after fracture (Orthobullets) · **<10%** heal (OB6098) · **fracture healing usually does not resolve the cyst**

**✗ Wrong options (initially)** — biopsy, staging (CT chest, bone scan), MRI, ORIF, IM nail, steroid injection, curettage, RT/chemo, RFA

### Proximal femur & calcaneus — surgical treatment

**Proximal femur fracture** — high refracture + malunion/nonunion nonoperatively → **ORIF + bone grafting** (hip screw + proximal femoral plate)\
✗ Wrong options: bed rest · spica · traction

**Femoral neck** — varus malunion, AVN, growth arrest (<10%)

**Calcaneus** — surgery if symptomatic + **100% of ML width + ≥30% of AP length** → **open curettage + bone augmentation** / cannulated screws\
✗ Wrong options: MRI · bone scan · CT chest · wide excision

### Persistent cyst — curettage & injection

| Option | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Observation** | Small, asymptomatic, upper limb · X-ray at 6 mo · **42%** success |
| **Aspiration + scraping ("Q-Ropsy") ± BMA / steroid** | Symptomatic cyst (TO) |
| **Aspiration + methylprednisolone acetate** | **Active** cysts, proximal humerus · several injections (young children) |
| **Bone marrow / DBM injection** | Effective, alone or combined |
| **Curettage + bone graft (± fixation)** | Symptomatic latent cyst failing injections · displaced humeral fractures · proximal femur · **recurrent / non-responding** |
| **Curettage + ESIN + graft** | **Highest success (88%)** |
| **Decompression** | IM nail, cannulated screws, Rush rod (rarely needed in upper limb) |

**Not recommended (TO)** — **polidocanol injection** (✓ curettage, ✓ DBM injection, ✓ decompression)

**Recurrence prevention** — **intralesional corticosteroid + DBM + autologous marrow**\
✗ Wrong options: denosumab (GCT) · BMP-2 · pexidartinib (PVNS) · bisphosphonates

**Adjuvants** — **liquid nitrogen / phenol → ABC**, not UBC

| Healing after surgery | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Complete | **>95% opacification + cortical thickening + no pain** |
| Partial | 80–95% opacification ± cortical thickening |
| Incomplete | <80% opacification, no cortical thickening |

## 6 Complications

**Recurrence** — **10–30%** · risk: **age ≤10** · follow all cysts with serial X-rays

**LLD / angular deformity** — active cyst crossing physis or into epiphysis · surgery in active phase

**Prognosis** — favourable · close follow-up in active phase (recurrence, fracture, growth arrest)
