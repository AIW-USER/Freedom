# Pleomorphic Sarcoma of Bone (Malignant Fibrous Histiocytoma)

*Pathology · 56 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **<2% of primary malignant bone tumours** · **2nd commonest bone sarcoma in adults** after chondrosarcoma

**Demographics** — **20–80 yr** (peak 2nd–4th decades) · **M:F 3:2** · Caucasian

**Site** — **75% appendicular** · **metaphysis** of long bones: **distal femur, proximal tibia, proximal humerus**

**Secondary (25%)** — from **bone infarct, Paget's, prior radiation**

### Genes & lab markers

**p53** mutations — secondary MFH of bone

**Labs** — ↑ WBC · **eosinophilia** · abnormal LFTs · **hypoglycaemia**

## 3 Presentation

**New, painful mass** · **pathologic fracture** · constitutional (fever, chills, night sweats, weight loss) · swelling, limp, ↓ ROM

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + lateral)** — **purely lytic, destructive metaphyseal** lesion · **no matrix mineralization** · variable periosteal reaction · cortical destruction + soft-tissue extension · non-specific

![Lytic destructive femoral lesion](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8030-case-b-femur-xray-parsons-moved.jpg)

**MRI** — deep heterogeneous mass · internal haemorrhage · **T1 low, T2 high**

**CT** — **chest CT staging** (lung mets) · bone destruction

**Bone scan** — staging · ↑ uptake in adjacent cortex

### Histology

**Pleomorphic spindle + histiocytic cells in storiform pattern** · malignant multinucleated **giant cells with grooved/indented nuclei** · chronic inflammatory cells · variable collagen · fascicles radiating from hypocellular foci · haemorrhage/necrosis → high grade · **no osteoid** (vs OS)

![Storiform pleomorphic spindle cells](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8030-histology-b-moderate-power-parsons-moved.jpg)

### Enneking staging (OB)

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| I | Low grade |
| II | High grade |
| III | Distant metastasis |
| A / B | **Intracompartmental / extracompartmental** |

**Stage IIB spindle-cell sarcoma** → pre-op chemo + wide excision + chemo

## 5 Management

### Diagnosis & treatment

**Treat like osteosarcoma** — **neoadjuvant chemo (8–12 wk) → wide resection → adjuvant chemo (6–12 mo) ± RT**\
✗ Wrong options: wide resection alone · chemo alone · RT alone · observation

**Margin** — **wide excision or amputation** → better 5-yr survival than intralesional/marginal

**RT** — incomplete/questionable margins · adjunct

**Soft-tissue UPS for contrast** — **radiation + wide resection** (painless, slow, T1 dark/T2 bright)

### Reconstruction after resection

**Limb salvage** — endoprosthesis · resection arthrodesis · allograft · **rotationplasty**

| Proximal tibia | **Megaprosthesis (MP)** | **Allograft-prosthetic composite (APC)** |
|---|---|---|
| 10-yr implant survival | **79%** | **94%** |
| Failure rate | No significant difference | No significant difference |
| Function | Similar | Similar · **better extensor mechanism in high-demand patient** |
| Leg length | Both restore | Both restore |

## 6 Complications

**Survival** — **50–60% at 5 yr**

**Lung mets** — up to **30%**

**Prognosis** — **secondary worse than primary** · **>90% necrosis** after chemo → better · **age <40** → better DFS

## X vs Y

| Distractor (soft-tissue thigh mass) | Key feature |
|---|---|
| **UPS** | Painless, slow, deep, T1 dark / T2 bright |
| **Liposarcoma** | Lipoblasts, signet-ring cells |
| Desmoid | Upper torso, **dark T1 + T2**, fibroblasts + collagen |
| **Synovial sarcoma** (periarticular) | Biphasic, **t(X;18)**, SYT-SSX |
| Schwannoma | Homogeneous, **target sign**, Antoni A/B |

**PET** — ↓ glucose uptake = early response predictor (OS, Ewing, GIST)

## How it's asked

**"75-yr-old, slow painless 7 cm thigh mass, T1 dark/T2 bright, pleomorphic?"** → **undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: liposarcoma · desmoid · synovial sarcoma · schwannoma

**"60-yr-old, spindle-cell sarcoma of bone — optimal treatment?"** → **neoadjuvant chemo + wide resection + adjuvant chemo**\
✗ Wrong options: wide resection alone

**"12-yr-old, purely lytic distal femoral metaphyseal lesion, storiform malignant spindle cells?"** → **chemotherapy + surgery** (MFH of bone)

**"Proximal tibial resection — APC vs megaprosthesis?"** → **APC better extensor mechanism in high-demand patient**\
✗ Wrong options: MP longer survival · APC higher failure
