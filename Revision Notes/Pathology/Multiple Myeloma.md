# Multiple Myeloma

*Pathology · 39 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & eponym (Kahler disease)

**Kahler disease** = multiple myeloma **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: OS · MFH · benign fibrous histiocytoma

**Most common primary (malignant) bone tumour** **(TO)** · 3–4 per 100,000 · 1% of all malignancies

**Demographics** — **>40 yr (median 60; 65 in OB)** · **male** · **2× African Americans**

### Forms

| Form | Key features |
|---|---|
| **Multiple myeloma** | Commonest · marrow + multiple lesions · CRAB |
| **Solitary plasmacytoma** | Single skeletal site, negative marrow · **radiosensitive** · **>50% progress to MM** · MRI + FDG-PET find more lesions in 33% · **best prognosis** |
| **Osteosclerotic myeloma (POEMS)** | **P**olyneuropathy (symmetric, distal → proximal, doesn't improve) · **O**rganomegaly · **E**ndocrinopathy · **M** protein · **S**kin changes (trunk) · **sclerotic** lesions |
| MGUS / asymptomatic myeloma | Annual surveillance |

| Plasma cell dyscrasia (TO) | Criteria | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **MGUS** | M protein **< 30 g/L** · plasma cells **< 10%** · no CRAB | Observe |
| **Smouldering myeloma** | M protein **> 30 g/L** or plasma cells **10–60%** · no CRAB | **Surveillance only** |
| **Multiple myeloma** | Plasma cells **> 60%**, or **10–60% + ≥1 CRAB** | Treat |
| **Solitary plasmacytoma** | One biopsy-proven bone lesion · no systemic disease · marrow **< 60%** (TO — verify: usual criterion is normal marrow / < 10% clonal plasma cells) | **RT** |

### Pathophysiology (RANKL, osteoblast suppression)

**Osteolysis** — myeloma cells bind marrow stromal cells → **↑ RANKL**, M-CSF, **IL-6, IL-11**, MIP-1α → osteoclast activation · **OPG ↓** (decoy receptor for RANKL)

**Osteoblast suppression** — **TNF** and **DKK-1** ↓ osteoblast function → **no sclerotic rim**, **cold bone scan** · **ALP normal** despite severe destruction — key clue (TO)

**Renal failure (TO)** — **light-chain (Bence Jones) nephropathy** → tubular obstruction → glomerular damage · Bence Jones = early renal clue

**Lytic lesions (mets + myeloma)** — **RANK ligand acting on osteoclasts** (RANKL secreted by osteoblasts → RANK receptor; OPG = decoy)\
✗ Wrong options: RANKL on neoplastic cells · OPG on osteoclasts

### Genes & lab markers

| Lab | Finding |
|---|---|
| **SPEP** | **M spike** · **IgG ~50%, IgA ~25%** (heavy chains IgG 52%, IgA 21%, IgM 12%) |
| **UPEP** | **Bence Jones proteins** = **monoclonal light chains (κ, λ)** · proteinuria |
| Blood | **Anaemia**, ↑ **creatinine**, **hypercalcaemia (30%)**, ↑ ESR |
| **β2-microglobulin** | Tumour burden, **prognosis** (ISS) |
| **IHC** | **CD138 +** |

**Poor prognosis genetics** — **del 13**, **t(4;14)**, t(14;16)

**Genetics list (TO)** — hyperdiploidy · **13q14 deletion** · **t(11;14)**, t(4;14), t(14;16) · **N-RAS, K-RAS, B-RAF** mutations

**Blood (TO)** — **rouleaux** on smear · circulating plasma cells **> 2,000/mm³ → plasma cell leukaemia** · **reversed A:G ratio** · ↑ LDH, ↑ uric acid · M spike in **gamma** region

**Marrow cytology (TO)** — perinuclear halo, clock-face chromatin · **Mott cells, Russell bodies** (immunoglobulin inclusions)

## 3 Presentation

**Uncommon features (TO)** — hyperviscosity · hepatosplenomegaly · **cord compression (plasmacytoma)** · pleural effusion

**Localized bone pain** (**spine, ribs**) · **pathologic fracture** (low-energy vertebral compression) · **fatigue** (anaemia, renal failure, hypercalcaemia) · radiculopathy, fevers

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **multiple "punched-out" lytic lesions**, **no sclerotic border** · visible only after **>50% destruction** · diffuse osteopenia, moth-eaten · marrow-packing process

![Punched-out lytic lesions](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8024-case-b-femur-xray-parsons-moved.jpg)

| Skull lesion (TO) | Disease |
|---|---|
| **Punched out, no bevelling** | **Myeloma** |
| **Double bevelled** | **EG / LCH** |
| **Salt and pepper** | **Hyperparathyroidism** |
| **Cotton wool** | **Paget** |

**Spine** — **vertebra plana** may occur (also plasmacytoma) (TO)

**Skeletal survey** — **preferred screen** · **bone scan cold in ~30%** (30–50%) — no osteoblastic activity\
✗ Wrong options: bone scan ideal

**MRI** — more sensitive than X-ray (spine, pelvis) · T1 dark, T2 bright · marrow replacement · focal lesion ≥5 mm = MDE

**FDG-PET** — **93% sensitive** (> X-ray) · finds more lesions in "solitary" plasmacytoma

### Diagnostic criteria (IMWG)

**Marrow** — **clonal plasma cells ≥10%** (or biopsy-proven plasmacytoma) **+ ≥1 CRAB feature or MDE**

| CRAB | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **C**alcium | >0.25 mmol/L (>1 mg/dL) above ULN or **>2.75 mmol/L (>11 mg/dL)** |
| **R**enal | CrCl **<40 mL/min** or creatinine **>177 µmol/L (>2 mg/dL)** |
| **A**naemia | Hb >20 g/L below LLN or **<100 g/L** |
| **B**one | **≥1 lytic lesion** (X-ray, CT, PET-CT) · if marrow <10%, >1 lesion needed |

| Myeloma-defining event (MDE) |
|---|
| **≥60%** clonal marrow plasma cells |
| Involved:uninvolved **free light chain ratio ≥100** (involved ≥100 mg/L) |
| **≥1 focal MRI lesion ≥5 mm** |

**Plasma cell % — conflict** — IMWG/Orthobullets/TO: **≥10%** · OB: **plasmacytoma 10–30% vs myeloma >30%** (keyed **35%** as minimum) — read the options · normal marrow <2%

**Next test with anaemia + serum M protein** — **bone marrow aspiration + biopsy** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: UPEP (already have serum M protein) · CT head · EMG · lumbar puncture · vertebral CT biopsy

### International Staging System (ISS)

| Stage | Criteria | Median survival |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **β2-M <3.5 mg/L** + albumin ≥3.5 g/dL | 62 months |
| **II** | β2-M 3.5–5.4 mg/L | 44 months |
| **III** | **β2-M ≥5.5 mg/L** | 29 months |

### Histology

**Plasma cells** — round, **eccentric nucleus**, **"clock-face" chromatin**, prominent nucleolus, basophilic cytoplasm · **perinuclear clear zone (Hof)** = Golgi (immunoglobulin production)

## 5 Management

```
Plasma cell disorder
├─ MGUS / asymptomatic → ANNUAL SURVEILLANCE
├─ Solitary plasmacytoma → EBRT 45–50 Gy (fix if fracture/impending)
├─ Osteosclerotic (POEMS) → CHEMO + RT + PLASMAPHERESIS
└─ Multiple myeloma → MULTIAGENT CHEMO ± STEM-CELL TRANSPLANT + BISPHOSPHONATES
       ├─ Impending / complete fracture, life >3 mo → STABILISE (whole bone, cement) + EBRT
       └─ Painful VCF / instability or compression resistant to RT → KYPHOPLASTY
```

### Treatment (fixation, radiation, bisphosphonates)

**Chemo** — non-transplant (>65, comorbid): **melphalan + prednisone + thalidomide/bortezomib**, lenalidomide/thalidomide + dexamethasone · transplant candidates: **bortezomib/lenalidomide + dexamethasone** (± thalidomide) → autologous SCT (not curative, +2–3 yr DFS)

**Very radio- and chemosensitive** — RT may be primary for impending lesions · surgery shouldn't delay induction / transplant

**Bisphosphonates** — ↓ skeletal events · **zoledronic acid IV q4 wk × 2 yr**, **avoid in renal failure** (TO)

**Denosumab (TO)** — **120 mg SC** (TO lists weeks 0, 1, 2 then q4 wk) · **safe in renal failure** → preferred

**Systemic agents (TO)** — **melphalan + prednisolone/dexamethasone** · IMiDs: thalidomide, lenalidomide, pomalidomide · **proteasome inhibitors: bortezomib**, carfilzomib, ixazomib · anti-CD38: **daratumumab**, isatuximab · elotuzumab · **BCMA**: belantamab (ADC), **teclistamab** (bispecific CD3–BCMA), **CAR-T** (idecabtagene, ciltacabtagene) · **autologous SCT** best < 65 yr, not curative

**Ortho rules (TO)** — **IM nail spanning whole bone** · periarticular → arthroplasty / megaprosthesis · **post-op RT at \~3 weeks** (radiosensitive) · plasmacytoma → **RT**, fix if **Mirels > 8**, kyphoplasty for vertebrae · treat hypercalcaemia aggressively

**Surgery** — complete or impending fracture, **life expectancy >3 months** · whole-bone protection, immediate stability · **post-op RT**

| Lesion | Fixation |
|---|---|
| **Femoral neck fracture** | **Cemented hemiarthroplasty or THA** (✗ Wrong options: IM nail · percutaneous screws · DHS · radical resection) |
| Proximal femur (stem) | **Full-length cemented** stem (✗ Wrong options: press-fit (RT/chemo impair ingrowth)) |
| Peritrochanteric, Mirels 12 | **Stabilisation + RT + bisphosphonates** (✗ Wrong options: wide resection (never for myeloma) · hormone therapy · curettage + graft) |
| Distal humerus path fracture | **Internal fixation without resection** (IM rod) → RT (✗ Wrong options: megaprosthesis · chemo first) |
| Periacetabular, intact subchondral plate | **Curettage + PMMA + modified Harrington pins** |
| Periacetabular, subchondral plate violated | **Complex THA + acetabular cage** |
| Painful VCF | **Kyphoplasty/vertebroplasty** — restores height, corrects sagittal deformity |

**Prophylactic fixation** — indicated before fracture so **systemic therapy isn't delayed** (bed rest, immunocompromise)

## 6 Complications

**Fracture healing** — myeloma has **among the best healing (67%)** of metastatic-type lesions · MM vertebral fractures heal poorly, progressive height loss

**Prognosis** — **median 3 yr** · 5-yr **30%**, 10-yr 11% · **renal failure → shortest survival**

**Poor prognosis** — **del 13 / t(4;14) / t(14;16)** · circulating plasma cells · **↑ β2-microglobulin** · **↓ albumin** · ↑ marrow microvessels

## X vs Y

| | **Myeloma** | **Metastatic carcinoma** |
|---|---|---|
| X-ray | **Punched-out**, no sclerotic rim | Lytic / blastic / mixed |
| Bone scan | **Cold ~30%** → skeletal survey | Hot (except thyroid, RCC lytic) |
| Labs | M spike, Bence Jones, CRAB | Primary-specific (PSA…) |
| Marker | **CD138** | Keratin, CK7, TTF-1 |
| Wide resection | **Never** | Solitary RCC |
