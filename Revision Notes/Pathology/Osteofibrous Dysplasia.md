# Osteofibrous Dysplasia

*Pathology · 48 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Nature** — rare, **benign fibro-osseous** tumour-like lesion of the **tibial (± fibular) cortex** · aka **ossifying fibroma**

**Age** — **<10 yr** (peak 1–5) · **M > F**

**Site** — **anterior tibial cortex**, diaphysis (± metaphysis)

### Genes & lab markers

**Trisomies 7, 8, 12, 22** reported

**No Gs-α (GNAS) mutation** (vs fibrous dysplasia)

**Keratin −** (vs adamantinoma: keratin + epithelial nests)

## 3 Presentation

**Painless swelling** of the shin · may be tender when bumped

**Deformity** — **anterior / anterolateral tibial bowing (procurvatum)**, limp

**Pseudarthrosis** — **10–30%**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + lateral)** — **eccentric, intracortical, multiloculated lytic lesion of the anterior tibial cortex** · well-marginated lucencies, sclerotic borders · expands/thins anterior cortex · **no periosteal reaction** · confined to anterior cortex

![Anterior tibial cortical lesion with bowing](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8039-35a-moved.jpg)

**CT** — cortical expansion, no periosteal reaction or matrix · **bone scan** — focally hot

**Biopsy** — needed when **young adult (15–35)** or red flags (growth, change, aggressive features) → exclude **adamantinoma**

### Histology

**Like fibrous dysplasia EXCEPT osteoblastic rimming** — fibroblast proliferation (bland spindle stroma) around **woven bone islands lined by osteoblasts** (lamellar rim) · mitoses common · ± giant cells · **no epithelial nests**

![Osteoblastic rimming around woven bone](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8039-35c-moved.jpg)

### Differential

| | Tibial diaphysis | Observation alone |
|---|---|---|
| **OFD** | Yes | Yes |
| **Adamantinoma** | Yes | No (wide resection) |
| FD · EG · enchondroma · osteochondroma · NOF · Paget | — | Yes |

| | **OFD** | **Adamantinoma** | **Fibrous dysplasia** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age | **<10** | 20–40 | <30 |
| Location | **Anterior tibial cortex** | Anterior mid-tibial cortex | **Medullary**, femur/ribs/skull |
| Histology | Woven bone **with osteoblastic rimming** | **Epithelial nests, keratin +** | Woven bone **without rimming** |
| Gene | Trisomies | — | **GNAS** |
| Treatment | **Observe** | **Wide resection** | Observe / fix |

**Others** — NOF (doesn't erode cortex, storiform) · UBC/ABC (metaphyseal, femur/humerus) · Ewing (onion skin, round cells) · EG

## 5 Management

```
Osteofibrous dysplasia
├─ Most → OBSERVATION (annual follow-up) — regresses by maturity
├─ Significant deformity interfering with walking → BRACE
├─ Impending pathologic fracture / pain → CURETTAGE + GRAFT
└─ Persistent deformity after skeletal maturity → CORRECTIVE OSTEOTOMY
```

**Avoid surgery before ~15 yr** — **very high local recurrence**

**No curettage + grafting** for routine OFD · (✗ Wrong options: casting · wide resection · chemo)\
✗ Wrong options: "accepted for all EXCEPT OFD" question (see ABC chapter)

## 6 Complications

**Prognosis** — lesions **regress**, no problems in adulthood · link to adamantinoma debated (continuum?)

## How it's asked

**"3-yr-old, bowing + limp, lucent anterior proximal tibial diaphyseal lesion?"** → **osteofibrous dysplasia**\
✗ Wrong options: FD (ground glass) · UBC/ABC (metaphyseal) · NOF

**"12-yr-old, tender bump below knee, anterior cortical lucencies, osteoblastic rimming?"** → **OFD**\
✗ Wrong options: adamantinoma · NOF · ABC · low-grade CS

**"9-yr-old shin mass, trabeculae lined by osteoblasts?"** → **OFD**\
✗ Wrong options: NOF · osteoblastoma · chondroblastoma · Ewing

**"6-yr-old, tender tibial shaft lucency, hot bone scan?"** → **OFD**\
✗ Wrong options: GCT · NOF · FD · EG

**"10-yr-old painless lower-leg deformity, OFD histology — next?"** → **observation**\
✗ Wrong options: brace · cast · curettage · osteotomy

**"7-yr-old, incidental anterior tibial lesion, keratin-negative biopsy?"** → **observation with annual follow-up**\
✗ Wrong options: wide resection (adamantinoma)
