# Conventional Intramedullary Osteosarcoma

*Pathology · 12 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & genetics

**Rank** — **most common primary sarcoma of bone** · **commonest non-haematologic primary bone malignancy** · ~20% of primary bone cancers **(TO)**

**Commonest primary malignant tumour of bone overall** — **multiple myeloma** **(TO — FNB 2021)**

**Age** — **bimodal**: 1st peak **2nd decade (10–14)**, **75% <20** · 2nd peak **>65** (secondary: Paget, infarcts, prior osteochondroma/osteoblastoma, **radiation**)

**Sex / race** — **M:F 1.5:1** · ↑ Black, Hispanic

**Site** — **metaphysis** of long bones (10% diaphyseal, <1% epiphyseal) · **distal femur > proximal tibia > proximal humerus > axial** (spine/sacrum) · fastest-growing physes

**Childhood tumour facts (TO)** — commonest tumour of infancy / extracranial / abdominal: **neuroblastoma** · commonest **solid tumour** of childhood: **brain tumour** · commonest **malignancy**: **leukaemia (ALL)** · commonest benign bone lesion: **NOF** · commonest benign bone tumour: **osteochondroma** · commonest malignant bone tumour: **osteosarcoma**

### Genes & lab markers

| Association | Gene / inheritance | Other features |
|---|---|---|
| **Hereditary retinoblastoma** | **RB1** (13q14), germline · **500–1000×** risk **(TO)** | Somatic RB1 loss in **60–70%** of sporadic OS |
| **Li-Fraumeni** | **TP53** (17p13), AD | Breast, brain, adrenocortical cancer · somatic TP53 in 20–50% |
| **Rothmund-Thomson** | **RECQL4**, AR | **Poikiloderma** (sun-sensitive rash), alopecia, juvenile cataracts, dental anomalies, short stature · OS, fibrosarcoma, gastric adenoCa, BCC/SCC |
| **Bloom** | **BLM**, AR | UV facial rash, short stature · OS, leukaemia, lymphoma |
| **Werner** | **WRN**, AR | **"Adult progeria"** — premature ageing, cataracts, osteoporosis |
| Fibrous dysplasia | — | Polyostotic > monostotic |

**Pathogenesis (TO)** — **RB1 + TP53 pathway inactivation nearly universal**, via complex structural variants / **chromothripsis** · **no pathognomonic translocation** · amplification **MDM2, CDK4, MYC** · (✗ Wrong options: MDM2/CDK4 = primary driver of conventional OS (that is **low-grade central / parosteal**) · RECQL4 in sporadic elderly OS)\
✗ Wrong options: like Ewing

**Not OS genes** — EWS-FLI1 (Ewing) · EGFR, IGF-R · VEGF (angiogenesis; overexpression = poor prognosis)

**Labs** — **↑ ALP and ↑ LDH** → aggressive disease, **poor prognosis** · very high LDH may mean mets

**IHC** — **SATB2 +** (all OS subtypes) **(TO)**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **pain + swelling**, night/rest pain · often blamed on sports injury ("red herring" fall) · median **4 months** to diagnosis · may have fever, weight loss

**Exam** — mass, tenderness, ↓ ROM, NV compression · pain with axial loading

**Pathologic fracture** — ~10%

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + lateral, whole bone)** — metaphyseal **medullary + cortical destruction** · **mixed blastic/lytic** (may be purely either) · **"sunburst" / "hair-on-end"** · **Codman triangle** · **cloud-like / fluffy** osteoid mineral · soft-tissue mass · usually **doesn't cross physis**

![Codman triangle and sunburst periosteal reaction](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8014-case-e-femur-shaft-xray-ap-parsons-moved.png)

**MRI (± contrast, entire bone + joint above and below)** — soft-tissue + **NV involvement** · resection level · **skip metastases** (= stage III) · **gold standard** local study

**CT chest** — **required at presentation** (lung mets) \
✗ Wrong options: chest X-ray (less sensitive) · CT abdomen/pelvis (adult unknown-lesion work-up)

**Bone scan (Tc-99m)** — **very hot** · bone mets, skip lesions · **superior to PET in OS** (PET better in Ewing) · PET-CT misses lung nodules <5–9 mm

**FNB 2024 image (Codman triangle, distal femur)** — "least likely appearance?" — answer not given · **onion peel** (Ewing) and **stippled calcification** (chondroid) don't fit OS · sunburst + Codman do **(TO)**

### Staging work-up

**Local** — **MRI of whole involved bone** (e.g. knee + tibia) · **Systemic** — **CT chest + whole-body bone scan**

**Pre-chemo baseline** — CBC, BMP, LFTs, UA · **echocardiogram** (doxorubicin) + **hearing screen** (cisplatin)

**Not needed** — bone marrow biopsy (Ewing) · skeletal survey (myeloma / NAT) · lymph node biopsy · PFTs · contralateral MRI · angiography · reactive-zone biopsy · EXT1/2 testing (MHE)

**Biopsy** — required · by/after discussion with **definitive surgeon** · core needle preferred · sample **peripheral soft-tissue part** · (✗ Wrong options: FNA · biopsy by IR at outside hospital (path fracture → sling, analgesia, stage, refer) · empirical antibiotics without biopsy)\
✗ Wrong options: necrotic centre · Codman triangle = reactive bone

### Histology — diagnostic criteria

**Two criteria** — **tumour cells produce osteoid** + **stromal cells frankly malignant** (atypia, high N:C, abnormal mitoses)

**Hallmark** — **"lacy" osteoid / woven bone** laid down directly by pleomorphic spindle cells

![Malignant cells producing lacy osteoid](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8014-histology-a-moved.png)

| Conventional subtype | Matrix | Mimic |
|---|---|---|
| **Osteoblastic (commonest)** | Abundant **lace-like osteoid**, plasmacytoid cells **(TO)** | — |
| Chondroblastic | Malignant hyaline cartilage + **focal osteoid** | Chondrosarcoma (any osteoid → OS) |
| Fibroblastic | Herringbone/storiform collagen, sparse osteoid | Fibrosarcoma, UPS |
| Giant-cell-rich | Many giant cells | **GCT** (no atypia/osteoid) |

### MSTS staging

**Commonest** — **IIB** (high grade, extracompartmental, no mets) · **90–95% high grade**, penetrate cortex early

**Metastasis at presentation** — **20–25%** (Enneking III) · **lung ~80%** (haematogenous) > **bone 16%** · bone mets without lung mets rare (5%)

**Skip metastases** — intraosseous (same bone, usually proximal) or **trans-articular** · **2–6%** (OB) · **10%** (Orthobullets) · prognosis = lung mets

### Osteosarcoma subtypes

| Location | Grade | Subtype | Key feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intramedullary** | High | **Conventional** (this topic) | Mixed tissues |
| | High | **Telangiectatic** | Purely lytic, **ABC-like** blood lakes · **malignant cells in septa** |
| | High | **Small cell** | OS + Ewing look · **SATB2 +, CD99 − / focal**, no EWSR1 |
| | Low | **Low-grade central** (FD-like, desmoplastic-fibroma-like) | Mimics **fibrous dysplasia** · **MDM2/CDK4** amplification |
| **Surface** | Low | **Parosteal** | **Posterior distal femur**, "stuck-on" · **middle-aged females (3rd–4th decade)** · MDM2/CDK4 |
| | Intermediate | **Periosteal** | **Diaphysis femur/tibia** · **chondroblastic** lobules, sunburst |
| | High | High-grade surface | Like conventional, on surface · medullary involvement common |
| **Intracortical** | — | Rarest bony type | — |
| **Extraskeletal** | High | Soft-tissue OS | <5% · treat like STS |

### Differential

| Lesion | Imaging | Histology |
|---|---|---|
| **Osteosarcoma** | Mixed blastic/lytic, Codman, soft-tissue mass | **Lacy osteoid**, malignant stroma |
| Osteomyelitis | Lags 2 wk, lucency, sequestrum/involucrum | Neutrophils (acute), lymphocytes (chronic) |
| Eosinophilic granuloma | "Great mimicker", vertebra plana | Langerhans cells, eosinophils, no atypia |

| Dedifferentiated CS | Bimorphic | Cartilage + high-grade spindle |

| Lymphoma of bone | Diaphyseal, moth-eaten, 35–55 yr | Mixed round cells |
| **Osteoblastoma** | Posterior spine, nidus | Benign osteoblasts, giant cells |
| Myositis ossificans | **Mature periphery** | Mature bone, no atypia |
| UPS | — | Spindle cells, **no osteoid** |

## 5 Management

### Treatment — neoadjuvant chemotherapy & wide resection

```
High-grade intramedullary OS (stage IIB)
├─ First → NEOADJUVANT MAP CHEMO (8–12 wk) → RESTAGE
├─ Resectable → WIDE RESECTION (limb salvage) → ADJUVANT CHEMO 6–12 mo
│      └─ Positive margin → ADDITIONAL RESECTION ± RT
├─ Child, distal femur / epiphysis / joint contaminated → ROTATIONPLASTY
└─ Functional + disease-free limb impossible → AMPUTATION
```

**Sequence** — **neoadjuvant chemo → wide resection → adjuvant chemo** · surgery 3–4 wk after last preop dose · chemo restarts within ~2 wk of surgery\
✗ Wrong options: surgery + RT · chemo + RT

**MAP** — **high-dose Methotrexate (leucovorin rescue) + Adriamycin (doxorubicin) + cisPlatin** (US standard) · ± ifosfamide · 3-drug > 2-drug (AP) by ~10% survival despite toxicity **(TO)** · **EURAMOS-1**: adding ifosfamide + etoposide (MAPIE) for poor responders → no benefit\
✗ Wrong options: vincristine/carboplatin regimens (Ewing)

| Agent | Mechanism | Toxicity |
|---|---|---|
| **Methotrexate** | Dihydrofolate reductase inhibitor | Myelosuppression, mucositis |
| **Doxorubicin** | Topoisomerase II inhibitor | **Cardiotoxicity** → echo |
| **Cisplatin** | Covalent DNA binding | **Renal failure, hearing loss**, neurotoxicity → audiogram |
| Ifosfamide | DNA alkylating | Renal failure, **haemorrhagic cystitis** |

**Chemo effectiveness** — same preop or postop (OB)

**Radiation** — **radio-resistant** (NF-κB inhibits JNK-mediated apoptosis) · only palliation, unresectable sites, close margins (pelvis/spine) · **ECRT** — extracorporeal 50 Gy single fraction to sterilise and re-implant bone **(TO)** · **extraskeletal OS radiosensitive** (exception)\
✗ Wrong options: curative for sacral OS **(TO)**

**Pathologic fracture** — limb salvage still possible if stabilised (ex-fix/cast) and good chemo response (healing, ↓ pain/swelling, ↑ ossification, loss of tumour blush on angiogram)

### Limb salvage principles

**Biopsy tract** — **resected en bloc** with specimen, never exposed **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: routine sacrifice of popliteal vessels · keep capsule/pseudocapsule · cut at visible tumour edge

**Bone cut** — **2–3 cm beyond intramedullary extent on T1 MRI** **(TO)** · marrow sampled at cut

**Limb salvage achievable** — **85–90%** · survival **= amputation**

**Contraindications (TO)** — NV bundle encasement · path fracture with massive haematoma contaminating compartments · no clean margin · infection

**Amputation indications** — functional + disease-free limb impossible (only absolute) · NV encasement · growing on chemo next to NV bundle · bad biopsy · infected tumour

**Physeal-sparing** — not when tumour crosses physis (knee OS usually sacrifices articular surface) · transepiphyseal resection only if epiphysis free

### Resection margins (Enneking)

| Margin | Plane |
|---|---|
| **Intralesional** | Through tumour · **tumour at marrow margin** = intralesional |
| **Marginal** | Through **reactive zone / pseudocapsule** |
| **Wide** | Cuff of normal tissue 360° ("orange rind") |
| **Radical** | Whole compartment (e.g. entire femur) |

### Rotationplasty

**Indications** — distal femur / knee / proximal tibia / hip sarcomas in **young children** · **joint contamination** · epiphyseal involvement · failed salvage with intact leg · PFFD · avoids LLD

**Requirement** — **functioning, uninvolved sciatic nerve**

**Technique** — resect segment, **rotate leg 180°**, fix tibia to femur · **ankle → knee** · gastrocsoleus = quadriceps, anterior compartment = hamstrings · keep sural branches

**Winkelmann** — **A** (ankle = knee): **AI distal femur**, AII proximal tibia · **B** (knee = hip): BI proximal femur sparing hip, **BII involving hip**, BIII total femur

**Outcome** — **highest self-reported function, fewest revisions** in pre-adolescents · cosmesis is the drawback\
✗ Wrong options: endoprosthesis (loosening, LLD) · allograft (fracture, infection) · AKA (energy cost)

![Van Nes rotationplasty](../../MCQ/attachments/q3495-illa-rotationplasty-1.jpg)

### Megaprosthesis outcomes

**Modular endoprosthesis** — gold standard reconstruction · rotating hinge (ligaments sacrificed) · cemented if poor bone or early chemo · press-fit for long survivorship

**Henderson failure types (TO)** — 1 soft-tissue · 2 **aseptic loosening** · 3 structural · 4 **infection (5–15%)** · 5 tumour recurrence

**Infection** — **2–10%** (Orthobullets) · 12–19% at 5–10 yr (proximal tibia) · risks **op time ≥8 h**, **drain ≥14 d** · ~50% → amputation

**PARITY trial** — **5-day ≠ better than 1-day** antibiotics · **flaps ↓ infection** · **NPWT → ↑ reoperation, not infection** · longer op time → ↑ complications · **primary malignant tumour → higher short-term reoperation** than benign aggressive

**Aseptic loosening** — ~6% · commonest failure · **tibia** commonest mechanical failure

**Proximal tibial EPR + medial gastrocnemius flap** — good function (flexion 98–110°, lag <10°) but **infection, extensor mechanism failure (3–17%), reoperation** significant · patella alta up to 70%

**Allograft** — fracture at host–graft junction, nonunion (chemo, RT, ECRT risk factors)

### Extraskeletal osteosarcoma

**vs conventional** — <5% of OS · **older (5th–6th decade)** · nearly always **high grade** · **worse survival (~50% vs ~75%)** · chemo unproven · **radiation is part of standard treatment** (wide resection + RT, like STS)

## 6 Complications

### Prognostic factors (necrosis, MDR, margins, metastasis)

**Most important** — **stage / metastasis at diagnosis**

**Chemo response** — **>90% necrosis = good** (5-yr ~85%)\
✗ Wrong options: 60% or 70% necrosis = poor

| Huvos grade | Necrosis | Response |
|---|---|---|
| I | 0–50% | Poor |
| II | 51–90% | Poor (e.g. 88% → continue MAP) |
| III | 91–99% | Good |
| IV | 100% | Complete |

**Poor prognosis** — mets · skip lesions · **<90% necrosis** · **age >40** · male · axial/pelvic site · **large size (≥5 cm)** · **MDR / P-glycoprotein** (25% primary, 50% mets) · **VEGF** · **↑ ALP / ↑ LDH** · vascular involvement · **positive margins** · radiation-induced or Paget OS

**Not poor (TO)** — ✓ **>90% necrosis** is good · urinary heat-shock protein, serum proteoglycan C, unchanged DNA ploidy listed as poor-prognosis distractors

**Local recurrence** — most directly **surgical margins**\
✗ Wrong options: site, size, grade, neoadjuvant chemo

**Radiation-induced OS (TO)** — **worse prognosis** than conventional · **Arlen criteria**: different prior histology, in radiation field, long latency, histological proof

| Situation | 5-yr survival |
|---|---|
| Localised, standard treatment | **~65%** (extremity 65%, **pelvis 55%**) |
| Good chemo response | **85%** |
| Pulmonary mets at diagnosis | **15–20%** (resectable 15–25%) |
| Late (>1 yr) lung mets + thoracotomy | ~30% |
| Bone mets | Incurable · <5% long term |
| Paget OS | <15% long term |

**Pelvic OS** — larger, more mets, local recurrence **up to 44%** despite negative margins

**Metastasectomy** — complete resection of primary + mets → **5×** long-term survival

**Surveillance (NCCN)** — q3 mo yr 1–2 · q4 mo yr 3 · q6 mo yr 4–5 · yearly after · exam, imaging of site + chest, ± bone scan/PET, ALP
