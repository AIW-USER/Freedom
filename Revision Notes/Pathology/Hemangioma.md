# Hemangioma

*Pathology · 24 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Nature** — **benign vascular tumour of bone**

**Site** — **vertebral bodies** and **craniofacial bones** · pelvis (ilium) · may be **multifocal**

## 3 Presentation

**Usually asymptomatic** — incidental

**Symptomatic** — pain or **pathologic fracture**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings — vertebral haemangioma

| Modality | Sign |
|---|---|
| **Plain X-ray** | **"Jailhouse" / "jail-bar" / "honeycomb"** — lytic body with **vertical striations** (thickened vertical trabeculae) **(TO)** |
| **Sagittal / coronal CT** | **"Corduroy"** appearance **(TO)** |
| **Axial CT** | **"Polka dot"** (salt-and-pepper) — white dots on black fatty background **(TO)** |
| **MRI** | **"Mottled"** — **high T1** (fat) + **high T2** (vascular), black punctate/linear signal voids · retains signal on STIR/fat-sat (unlike fatty marrow) **(TO)** |
| Bone scan | Warm to hot |

![Vertical striations — jailhouse vertebra](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8033-case-c-spine-xray-parsons-moved.jpg)

**Biopsy** — almost **never required** (imaging characteristic) **(TO)**

**Why these signs** — normal cancellous bone replaced by **thickened vertical trabeculae** surrounded by **fat marrow / vascular lacunae**

### Histology

**Cavernous lesion** with numerous **thin-walled blood vessels** · small vascular channels lined by **single layer of endothelium** · no giant cells (vs ABC, Paget), no granulomas (vs sarcoid)

![Cavernous thin-walled vessels](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8033-histology-c-hig-parsons-moved.jpg)

### Differential of the spine/ilium lesion

| Lesion | Distinguishing |
|---|---|
| **Haemangioma** | **Organised vertical striations**, polka dots |
| Paget disease | Square, **enlarged** vertebra, thick cortex ("picture frame"), disorganised mineral |
| GCT | Lytic, destructive |
| Metastasis · myeloma | Lytic/sclerotic, no organisation |
| Chordoma (sacral) | Sacrum, midline, physaliferous cells |
| ABC | Fluid levels, giant cells |
| Sarcoid | Granulomas |

### Soft-tissue haemangioma (forearm)

**Phleboliths** on X-ray + intramuscular mass, **↑ T1 (fat)**, heterogeneous T2 + enhancement → **soft-tissue haemangioma**\
✗ Wrong options: synovial sarcoma · liposarcoma · rhabdomyosarcoma

## 5 Management

```
Haemangioma of bone
├─ Asymptomatic → OBSERVATION
├─ Symptomatic + accessible → CURETTAGE + BONE GRAFT (intralesional excision)
└─ Symptomatic + inaccessible → LOW-DOSE RADIATION 25–40 Gy
```
