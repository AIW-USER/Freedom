# Melanoma

*Pathology · 35 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Nature** — aggressive skin malignancy of **melanocytes** (from **neural crest**, deepest epidermal layer)

**Demographics** — **50–70** · **M:F 1.2:1**

**Hand/foot sites** — **thumb > great toe > index finger** · sun-exposed areas

**Subtype** — **acral lentiginous melanoma** (palms, subungual) · **subungual melanoma** = ALM subtype

### Risk factors

**Sun exposure** (UV suppresses immunity, DNA damage) · **family history** · fair skin, blue eyes, freckling · **xeroderma pigmentosum** · FAMM syndrome · multiple / **dysplastic naevi (precursor)** · immunosuppression

**Prevention** — sunscreen, avoid sun

### Genes & lab markers

**S-100 +** (melanoma)

**LDH ↑** — predicts poor prognosis · **AST/ALT** — liver mets · CBC

## 3 Presentation

**Pigmented lesion with recent change in shape or size** · itching, bleeding, ulceration

**ABCDE** — **A**symmetry · **B**order irregularity · **C**olour variation · **D**iameter (>6 mm suspicious; <6 mm benign) · **E**levation / enlargement

**Subungual** — dystrophic nail prone to trauma → often blamed on **injury**

**Hutchinson sign** — brown-black pigment extends from nail bed/plate onto **cuticle and nail folds** ± nail lifting

**Longitudinal melanonychia — malignant features** — band **>6 mm**, **single nail**, wider proximally, colour heterogeneity, blurry borders, pigment into nail fold

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**CXR** — lungs often first metastatic site · **US** — nodes · **PET/CT** — metastases

**Biopsy** — **full-thickness biopsy of the nail matrix** after nail plate removal\
✗ Wrong options: trephination · antibiotics · no work-up

### Histology

**Melanocytes with marked atypia + dermal invasion** · vacuolated cytoplasm · hyperchromatic nuclei, prominent nucleoli

### Breslow thickness

| Stage | Thickness |
|---|---|
| 1 | **≤0.75 mm** |
| 2 | 0.76–1.5 mm |
| 3 | 1.51–4 mm |
| 4 | **>4 mm** |

### Clark level

| Level | Invasion |
|---|---|
| **I** | Epidermis only (in situ) |
| **II** | Papillary dermis |
| **III** | Papillary dermis to papillary–reticular interface |
| **IV** | Reticular dermis |
| **V** | Subcutaneous tissue |

### Differential

**General** — naevi · seborrhoeic keratosis · basal cell carcinoma

**Subungual** — **trauma / subungual haematoma** (contained under plate, needs trauma) · **onychomycosis** (thickening, yellow-brown) · psoriasis (pitting) · SCC (no pigment) · splinter haemorrhages (endocarditis → echo) · glomus tumour (painful, blue) · DFSP (trunk)

## 5 Management

### Margins by thickness

| Lesion | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **<1 mm** | Local resection, **1 cm** margin |
| **1–2 mm** | Resection **1–2 cm** + **sentinel node biopsy** → radical node dissection if positive |
| Thick | **>3 cm** margin (OB) |
| Metastatic | Resection + node dissection + chemo |

### Subungual melanoma

**Amputation** — **thumb: IP joint disarticulation / through proximal phalanx** · finger: PIP level · **+ sentinel node biopsy**

**Distal = proximal** — distal amputation with adequate margins has **same recurrence and survival** as MCP/ray/CMC amputation → preserves function

**Not used** — neoadjuvant chemo to downsize · 1–2 **mm** margin · Mohs (controversial; for SCC/BCC) · isolated limb perfusion routinely

## 6 Complications

**Recurrence** — usually **regional lymph nodes**

**Prognosis** — **depth = most important factor** · <0.7 mm → **96%** survival · >4 mm → **47%**

**Poor prognosis** — deep lesion · **male** · **neck/scalp** · positive nodes/mets · **ulceration** · ↑ LDH

**Subungual** — **5-yr survival 40–60%** (late diagnosis, thicker, ulcerated)

**Bone metastasis** — melanoma + lung: worst survival after pathologic fracture (<6 months)

## How it's asked

**"62-yr-old, enlarging brown nail streak on ring finger — biopsy to exclude?"** → **melanoma**\
✗ Wrong options: DFSP · endocarditis · glomus · epithelioid sarcoma

**"Persistent thumbnail pigment after hammer blow, Hutchinson sign — next step?"** → **full-thickness nail matrix biopsy**\
✗ Wrong options: trephination · antibiotics · IPJ disarticulation first

**"65-yr-old, thumbnail discoloration onto folds, no trauma?"** → **melanoma**\
✗ Wrong options: SCC · onychomycosis · haematoma · psoriasis

**"Thin (0.6 mm) subungual thumb melanoma — best cure with function?"** → **IP joint disarticulation + sentinel node biopsy**\
✗ Wrong options: MCP disarticulation · ray amputation + limb perfusion · Mohs · chemo + 1–2 mm margin

**"Most important prognostic factor?"** → **depth (thickness)**
