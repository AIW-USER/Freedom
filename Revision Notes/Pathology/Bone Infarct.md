# Bone Infarct

*Pathology · 5 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Pathophysiology

**Spectrum** — same disease spectrum as **osteonecrosis** · medullary lesion

**Mechanism** — **interruption of blood supply** by intrinsic/extrinsic factors (atherosclerosis, arteritis, thrombosis)

**Site** — **metaphysis or diaphysis** of long bones (femur, tibia)

**Healing** — progressive but **incomplete resorptive replacement** → thick **calcified fibro-osseous wall**

### Risk factors

| Risk factor | Bone infarct? |
|---|---|
| **Sickle cell disease** | ✓ |
| **Thalassemia** | ✓ |
| **Gaucher disease** | ✓ |
| **Steroid use** (chronic prednisone) | ✓ |
| Connective tissue disorders | ✓ |
| Trauma | ✓ |
| **Infection** | **not a risk factor** |

## 3 Presentation

**Usually asymptomatic** — **incidental** on films taken for other reasons (twisted knee, fall)

**Pain** — if painful → **seek another aetiology**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — medullary **sheet-like central lucency + surrounding sclerosis with serpiginous border** → **"smoke up the chimney"**

![Smoke up the chimney — medullary infarct](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8078-bone-infarct-xray.jpg)

**MRI** — key: **central signal = normal marrow** · T1 **peripheral low-signal rim** (granulation tissue, sclerosis), may enhance · T2 **double line**: inner bright granulation ring + outer dark sclerotic ring · acute infarct ill-defined ↑ T2

![Bone infarct MRI — central marrow signal preserved](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8078-bone-infarct-mri.jpg)

**Bone scan** — **cold early** · **hot** in late resorptive / revascularisation phase

**Biopsy** — usually unnecessary · mineralised necrotic marrow

## 5 Management

**Observation** — asymptomatic, **no treatment** required

## 6 Complications

**Malignant transformation** — rare · **MFH / undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma** at infarct site

## X vs Y

| | **Bone infarct** | **Enchondroma** |
|---|---|---|
| Matrix | Serpiginous peripheral sclerotic rim, "smoke up the chimney" | **Rings and arcs** (chondroid) throughout |
| MRI | Central normal marrow fat | Lobulated T2-bright cartilage |
| Risk factors | Sickle, Gaucher, steroids | None |

## How it's asked

**"Incidental medullary metaphyseal lesion, serpiginous sclerotic border — diagnosis?"** → **bone infarct**\
✗ Wrong options: enchondroma · metastasis · osteosarcoma

**"NOT a risk factor for this lesion?"** → **infection** (✓ sickle cell, thalassemia, Gaucher, chronic steroids)

**"Treatment?"** → **observation**
