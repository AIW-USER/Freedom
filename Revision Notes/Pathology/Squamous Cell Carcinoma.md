# Squamous Cell Carcinoma

*Pathology · 60 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Nature** — malignant tumour of **keratinocytes** (epidermis) · elderly

**Hand** — **commonest malignancy of the hand** (skin cancers ≫ primary MSK malignancy) · **commonest subungual malignancy** · dorsum of hand + forearm

**Skin overall** — **2nd commonest skin cancer** after BCC (OB212007) · in the hand, **BCC is 2nd** (OB220030) · face/neck > hand

**Risk factors** — **UV exposure** (main) · **actinic keratosis** · Bowen's disease, keratin horns, leukoplakia · **solid-organ transplant** (immunosuppression) · **chronic osteomyelitis**

### Marjolin ulcer

**Definition** — SCC in **chronic draining sinus tracts** (osteomyelitis), **burn scars**, pressure sores

**Rate** — **~1%** malignant change · latency **~30 yr** · many low grade

**Distractors** — Mooren (corneal) · chancroid (genital) · **Curling** (peptic, burns) · **Cushing** (gastric, ↑ ICP)

**High suspicion** — biopsy chronic draining sites

## 3 Presentation

**Indurated, scaly, erythematous** plaque/lesion · pain · subungual mass mistaken for **haematoma**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + lateral)** — **lytic** lesion when bone involved (e.g. distal phalanx tuft destruction)

![Lytic destruction of distal phalanx](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8073-40a-moved.jpg)

**Biopsy** — **punch, shave or incisional**

### Histology

**Keratin nodules / squamous pearls** · tumour nests penetrating deep fascia

![Keratinizing squamous cells](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8073-40b-moved.jpg)

| Skin lesion | Feature |
|---|---|
| **SCC** | **Keratin pearls** |
| BCC | Pink/flesh papule, central ulcer · nests of basal cells |
| **Melanoma** | From naevus · ABCD changes |
| Seborrhoeic keratosis | "Stuck-on" waxy lesion · histology like SCC |
| Lentigo | Small pigmented macule |
| M. marinum | Culture 30–32°C |
| Sarcoidosis | Non-caseating granulomas |

## 5 Management

### Hand SCC — diagnosis & treatment

```
Squamous cell carcinoma
├─ Most → WIDE LOCAL RESECTION (6 mm margin to subcutaneous fat) ± SKIN GRAFT
├─ Small lesions → MOHS MICROSURGERY (highest cure rate)
├─ Bone involvement (digit) → AMPUTATION
└─ Marjolin ulcer → WIDE RESECTION / AMPUTATION + soft-tissue reconstruction
```

**Adjuvant RT (Orthobullets)** — **>2 cm** wide · **4 mm** deep · **perineural invasion** · **nodal mets** · OB: RT not needed for primary SCC, not typical for SCC ("radiation" is wrong in Marjolin and hand stems)

**Lymph node biopsy** — may be needed

**Marjolin** — ✗ Wrong options: IV antibiotics · flap or STSG alone

| Treated with wide resection alone | |
|---|---|
| **SCC** · **adamantinoma** · **chordoma** · **chondrosarcoma** · **parosteal OS** | ✓ |

## 6 Complications

**Prognosis** — **nodal metastases + high histologic grade** = poor · **more metastatic than BCC** · lymphatic → nodes, then lungs, liver, brain, bone, skin

## How it's asked

**"SCC from chronic sinus drainage or burn scars?"** → **Marjolin's ulcer**\
✗ Wrong options: Curling · Cushing · Mooren · chancroid

**"67-yr-old, chronic calcaneal osteomyelitis sinus, squamous pearls?"** → **wide resection**\
✗ Wrong options: antibiotics · flap · STSG · RT

**"Most common malignancy of the hand?"** → **SCC**\
✗ Wrong options: epithelioid sarcoma · lung mets · chondrosarcoma

**"El Paso construction worker, scaly indurated hand lesion, SCC?"** → **wide local resection**\
✗ Wrong options: transradial amputation · RT

**"Subungual 'haematoma', bone involved, digit amputated?"** → **SCC**\
✗ Wrong options: BCC · epithelioid sarcoma

**"Kidney transplant, UV exposure, dorsal hand plaque, keratin nodules?"** → **SCC**\
✗ Wrong options: BCC · melanoma · seborrhoeic keratosis
