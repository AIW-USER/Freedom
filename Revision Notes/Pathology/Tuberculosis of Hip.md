# Tuberculosis of Hip

*Pathology · TO notes*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rank** — **2nd commonest osteoarticular TB** after spine · \~**15%** of osteoarticular TB (TO)

**Age** — mostly **first three decades** (TO)

**Always secondary** — to **pulmonary / lymph node / visceral** focus · spread **haematogenous** or direct extension (TO)

**Diagnostic dilemma** — stages mimic many hip disorders (TO)

### Initial focus

| Focus (TO) | Note |
|---|---|
| **Acetabular roof** | **Commonest** · joint involvement **delayed** |
| **Epiphysis** | Intracapsular → **rapid spread to joint** |
| **Metaphysis (Babcock's triangle)** | Common in **children** |
| **Greater trochanter** | Spreads via **trochanteric bursa** |

### Pathogenesis

**Sequence (TO)** — synovial hypertrophy + congestion → **subchondral necrosis, sequestrum** → **kissing lesions** on opposing surfaces → **cold abscess** perforates capsule

**Cold abscess exit** — **weak inferior capsule** → intrapelvic · **femoral triangle** · medial / lateral / posterior thigh · **ischiorectal fossa** (TO)

## 3 Presentation

**Systemic** — fever with **evening rise**, weight + appetite loss, cough, night sweats (TO)

**Early local** — insidious pain **referred to medial knee / thigh** · stiffness → limp · fullness from cold abscess (TO)

**Night cries** — child wakes when **protective muscle spasm relaxes in sleep** (TO)

**First movement lost** — **extension** (TO)

**Late** — LLD · deformity · stiffness · **pathological dislocation** · cold abscess / sinus · untreated → **fibrous ankylosis** in deformed position (TO)

**Wandering acetabulum** — destruction of head + acetabulum, constant muscle pull **migrates head proximally** (TO)

### Clinical stages

| Stage (TO) | Pathology | Attitude | Limb length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Synovitis** | ↑ joint fluid · terminal movements restricted | **FABER** (flexion, abduction, ER) | **Apparent lengthening** |
| **2 Early arthritis** | Cartilage involved · spasm of strong flexors + adductors | **FADIR** (flexion, adduction, IR) | **Apparent shortening** |
| **3 Advanced arthritis** | Cartilage destroyed · ↓ joint space | FADIR, exaggerated | **True shortening** |
| **4 Advanced + subluxation / dislocation** | Wandering acetabulum | Gross deformity · jog of movement (fibrous ankylosis) or excess (dislocation) | **Gross shortening** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — early **haziness**, soft-tissue swelling · later **osteopenia**, **peripheral erosions** (TO)

**Phemister triad** — **juxta-articular osteoporosis + peripheral erosion + gradual joint-space narrowing** → classic osteoarticular TB (TO)

**MRI** — **most sensitive for early diagnosis** · marrow oedema, effusion (TO)

### Tissue diagnosis

**Biopsy** — histopathology · **AFB stain** · **PCR + culture** · failure of non-invasive diagnosis → **tissue diagnosis mandatory** (TO)

**AFB positivity** — synovial fluid **< 10%** · synovial tissue **< 20%** → biopsy + PCR important (TO)

**Mantoux** — high **false positives** in endemic areas (TO)

### Shanmugasundaram radiological classification

| Type (TO) | Feature | Recovery |
|---|---|---|
| **Normal** | Synovial disease only | **92%** |
| **Wandering acetabulum** | Proximal migration of head | **29%** (worst) |
| **Dislocating** | Partial dislocation, muscle spasm | **50%** |
| **Perthes type** | Head destruction, Perthes-like | **80%** |
| **Protrusio acetabuli** | Medial wall weakness | — |
| **Atrophic** | Minimal infection, small head | — |
| **Mortar and pestle** | Saucer acetabulum + small head | — |

## 5 Management

### Conservative

**Old** — hip spica 1.5 yr → "frame knee" stiffness → **obsolete** (TO)

**Modern** — **ATT + traction** + gradual mobilisation ± stage-wise surgery (TO)

**Traction** — relieves spasm · corrects deformity · maintains joint space · separates surfaces (TO)

### Stage-wise treatment

```
TB hip — by stage (TO)
├─ Synovitis → ATT + TRACTION + REST → mobilise when pain settles
├─ Early arthritis → ATT + TRACTION + SYNOVECTOMY / DEBRIDEMENT
│      (↑ vascularity → better ATT delivery; risks AVN, SCFE, iatrogenic fracture)
├─ Advanced arthritis → ARTHROLYSIS + SUBTOTAL SYNOVECTOMY (keep posterior capsule) + TRACTION
└─ Subluxation / dislocation → EXCISION ARTHROPLASTY · ARTHRODESIS · THA
       (children: ATT + traction can still heal — Sandhu)
```

### Salvage options

| | **Girdlestone excision arthroplasty** | **Arthrodesis** |
|---|---|---|
| For (TO) | **Squatting, cross-leg sitting** · one-time surgery | **Stable, painless hip** · good sitting · one-time |
| Against (TO) | **Trendelenburg gait** · **2–3 cm shortening** · stick outdoors | Secondary arthritis of **spine, opposite hip, ipsilateral knee** · no floor activities |

**Fusion position (TO)** — **flexion 30° in adults**, **1° per year of age in children** (TO — verify: usual flexion 20–30°) · **ER 5–10°** · neutral abduction–adduction

**Extra-articular fusion (TO)** — **Brittain ischiofemoral** · **Abbott–Lucas two-stage** fusion

**THA (TO)** — **safe in healed TB** · increasingly accepted in **active TB under ATT cover** · e.g. ATT 3 mo pre-op + 15 mo post-op, or 2 wk pre-op + 12 mo post-op

## X vs Y

| | **Synovitis stage** | **Arthritis stages** |
|---|---|---|
| Attitude | **FABER** | **FADIR** |
| Length | Apparent lengthening | Apparent → true shortening |
| Joint space | Preserved | ↓ |
| Treatment | ATT + traction | + debridement / arthrolysis / salvage |

## How it's asked

**"Commonest initial focus of TB hip?"** → **acetabular roof** (TO)

**"Night cries in TB hip — cause?"** → **relaxation of protective muscle spasm during sleep** (TO)

**"Attitude in synovitis stage?"** → **flexion, abduction, external rotation — apparent lengthening** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: FADIR (arthritis stages)

**"Phemister triad?"** → **juxta-articular osteoporosis, peripheral erosion, joint-space narrowing** (TO)

**"Shanmugasundaram type with worst prognosis?"** → **wandering acetabulum (29%)** (TO)

**"Salvage allowing squatting / cross-leg sitting?"** → **Girdlestone excision arthroplasty** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: arthrodesis (no floor activities)
