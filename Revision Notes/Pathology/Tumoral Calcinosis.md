# Tumoral Calcinosis

*Pathology · 64 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Diagnosis & phosphate metabolism (FGF23)

**Nature** — rare, **hereditary** dysfunction of **phosphate** regulation → massive **periarticular, extracapsular** calcinosis\
✗ Wrong options: calcium · oxalate · chloride metabolism · renal tubular insufficiency

**Demographics** — **females** · **African-Americans** · onset childhood / early adolescence (stems ~30 yr)

**Site** — **hip (trochanteric bursa commonest)** > **shoulder** (Orthobullets) · extensor surfaces: hip, **elbow (olecranon)**, shoulder, foot, wrist (OB7499)

### Genes & lab markers

**FGF-23 defect** → **familial hyperphosphataemic tumoral calcinosis**\
✗ Wrong options: IDH1 (Maffucci) · dystrophin (DMD) · ACVR1 (FOP) · 1α-hydroxylase (rickets)

**Labs** — **hyperphosphataemia** (OB) · **calcium normal** (OB7499) · Orthobullets: Ca, PO₄, uric acid, ALP **normal or slightly ↑**; renal and parathyroid function normal/slightly ↑ · PTH may ↑

**Associations** — metabolic defects, collagen vascular disorders, trauma

## 3 Presentation

**Palpable mass / swelling around a joint** · slow-growing, rarely painful · pain from compression of neighbours · ROM usually preserved

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **amorphous, cystic, lobular (round/oval), well-demarcated periarticular calcification** · bones/joints rarely involved directly

![Lobular periarticular calcification](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8077-tumoral-calcinosis-lateral-moved.jpg)

![Hip tumoral calcinosis](../../MCQ/attachments/q690-a-tumoral-calcinosis-ap.jpg)

**CT** — **fluid-fluid levels — calcium layering ("sedimentation sign")** · homogeneous → less active, less growth

**MRI** — diffuse low signal · or bright nodular pattern alternating with signal voids · fluid levels

**Bone scan** — intense uptake in the masses

### Histology

**Lobulated masses with defined capsules + thick septae** · filled with **calcareous material (calcium phosphate, carbonate, hydroxyapatite) + fluid** · loculated calcific debris in fibrous stroma

### Differential

| Lesion | Key distinction |
|---|---|
| **Calcinosis of renal failure** (secondary) | Same X-ray + histology · **CRF / dialysis**, GFR, vit D, ↑ PO₄ · ~1% prevalence · any race · **medical treatment** (diet, binders, dialysate) |
| **Calcific tendinitis** | **Within tendon** · shoulder > hip > elbow > wrist > knee · **no sedimentation** |
| **Synovial osteochondromatosis** | **Intra-articular** / tendon sheath · **rings and arcs** |
| **Myositis ossificans / HO** | Not lobular · faint calcification → bone in **6 weeks** · mature cortex + medulla |
| **Gout** | Hyperuricaemia · **focal bone erosions** |
| Osteochondroma · chondrosarcoma · BPOP (Nora) | Bone-attached lesions |

## 5 Management

```
Tumoral calcinosis
├─ Asymptomatic → OBSERVATION
└─ Symptomatic, ↓ function → COMPLETE SURGICAL EXCISION (+ normalize phosphate)
```

**Recurrence risk** — **incomplete excision** · **poorly circumscribed** lesion · excision while **actively growing**

## How it's asked

**"59-yr-old dialysis-dependent diabetic, painless calcified hip bump?"** → **calcinosis of renal failure**\
✗ Wrong options: synovial chondromatosis · MO · parosteal OS

**"30-yr-old African-American woman, hip swelling 3 yr, lobulated calcific masses?"** → **tumoral calcinosis**\
✗ Wrong options: osteochondroma · chondrosarcoma · Nora's

**"Tumoral calcinosis is a hereditary disorder of?"** → **phosphate metabolism**\
✗ Wrong options: calcium · oxalate

**"Olecranon calcified nodule — lab + gene?"** → **hyperphosphataemia; FGF-23**\
✗ Wrong options: IDH1 · ACVR1 · dystrophin
