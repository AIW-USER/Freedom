# Metastatic Disease of Spine

*Pathology · 38 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Commonest destructive bone lesion in adults** — metastasis · bone = **3rd** site (after lung, liver) · >40 yr

**Spine = commonest site of bone metastasis** — **thoracic** commonest (symptomatic: thoracic **68–70%**, lumbosacral 16–22%, cervical 8–15%) · spine > proximal femur > humerus

**Spinal disease** — up to **90%** of metastatic cancer patients, only **10–20% symptomatic** · **first sign of malignancy in 20%** · >90% of spinal tumours are metastatic

### Primary tumours metastasising to spine

| Primary | % of patients developing spine mets |
|---|---|
| **Breast** | **16–37%** |
| **Lung** | 12–15% |
| **Prostate** | 9–15% |
| **Kidney** | 3–6% |
| **Thyroid** | 4% |

**Order of spinal secondary spread** — breast, prostate, lung, kidney ≈ **80%** · plus thyroid, myeloma (~75% of bone mets)

**Rarely to spine** — **colon** · hepatic, uterine, skin, "osseous" are distractors · haemangioma doesn't metastasize\
✗ Wrong options: breast, lung, renal, thyroid

**Risk clues** — **BRCA1** (breast) · smoking (lung) · **von Hippel–Lindau / VHL** (renal) · hypercalcaemia \
✗ Wrong options: **APC + positive FOBT** (colon — least likely)

### Mechanism of metastasis

**Theories** — **seed and soil** (compatible end-organ) · **circulation** (blood flow from primary)

**Steps** — ↓ **E-cadherin** → release · immune evasion · **integrin** → endothelial attachment · extravasation · **VEGF** angiogenesis · genomic instability · ↓ apoptosis\
✗ Wrong options: osteocalcin

**Batson plexus** — **valveless** venous plexus → vertebral bodies, pelvis, skull, proximal girdles

### Osteoclast-mediated bone resorption

**Lysis** — **tumour-induced activation of osteoclasts** · tumour releases TNF-α, IL-6, IL-11, **PTHrP**, TGF-β → **osteoblasts secrete RANKL** → RANK on osteoclasts · **OPG** binds RANKL, blocks resorption\
✗ Wrong options: direct resorption by tumour cells (only osteoclasts resorb bone) · macrophages · necrosis · angiogenesis

**Sclerosis** — prostate (± breast) → **endothelin-1** → ET-A receptor on osteoblasts, ↓ **DKK-1** → WNT ↑\
✗ Wrong options: ET-1 causes lysis

### Genes & lab markers

**Labs (unknown primary)** — CBC + diff, **ESR**, BMP, LFTs, Ca, phosphate, ALP, **SPEP/UPEP**, TFTs, **PTH**, **PSA**, urinalysis (± CEA)

**Red flags → first tests** — CBC, ESR, urinalysis (+ PSA, CEA with cancer history)

**IHC** — keratin · **CK7** (breast, lung) · **TTF-1** (lung) · **vimentin** (renal) · **CDX2** (colon)

**Associated** — **hypercalcaemia 10–30%** (lung, breast) → IV fluids, loop diuretic, bisphosphonate · anaemia · hypercoagulability / VTE

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — **axial night pain** / rest pain · mechanical pain (instability) · **pain worse supine**, relieved standing

**Red flags (tumour)** — age >50, cancer history (most significant), weight loss, night pain, pain lying down

**Neurologic** — cord compression → **UMN signs: hyperreflexia**, clonus, gait ataxia, weakness, bowel/bladder · distinguish **root vs cord**

**Chemo-related** — peripheral neuropathy

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + lateral)** — **"winking owl" sign** = **unilateral absent pedicle** (pedicle destruction) · lytic or mixed · **up to 40% invisible** on X-ray · discs preserved (vs infection)

![Winking owl sign — absent pedicle](../../MCQ/attachments/q3776-illa-winking-owl-labeled.jpg)

**MRI with contrast** — **next step** for winking owl / suspected mets · dedicated region + **limited sagittal whole spine** · soft-tissue mass, ↑ T2, cortical permeation, **cord compression** · **98%** sensitive + specific

**CT** — sensitivity 66%, specificity 99% · **CT myelography if MRI contraindicated** · **CT CAP** for unknown primary

**Bone scan** — extent · **cold in myeloma + thyroid** → **skeletal survey**

**Blastic pelvis/spine in older man** → **prostate** (→ **prostate biopsy** confirms) · lung blastic only 25–30%

### Workup & diagnosis

**Known cancer, no prior mets / no cancer history** — evaluate before treating · history, exam, X-ray, labs, **CT CAP + bone scan** → identifies primary in **85%** · biopsy adds **8%**

**Staging before biopsy**\
✗ Wrong options: CT-guided biopsy first · excision · RFA · NSAIDs + PT

**Winking owl in IV drug user, normal WBC/ESR/CRP** — **CT CAP**\
✗ Wrong options: cervical MRI · antibiotics · kyphoplasty

**Multiple spinal lesions + very high PSA** — staging + diagnostic tests together (prostate biopsy, CT CAP, bone scan, CT-guided spine biopsy) \
✗ Wrong options: **bone marrow biopsy** (prostate rarely to marrow)

**Spine biopsy indications (TO)** — **life expectancy > 3 months** · **neurological deficit** · **uncertain diagnosis**

**Known metastatic disease** — biopsy not required

**Biopsy technique** — **posterior transpedicular**, wide-gauge **Jamshidi**, coring needle, image guidance · posterior (cervical) → vertebral artery risk · occult adenocarcinoma → **small lung primary**

### Histology

**Epithelial cells in clumps/glands in fibrous stroma**

### Tokuhashi (revised) score

**Six elements** — general condition · extraspinal bone mets · number of vertebral mets · visceral mets · primary tumour (0–5) · palsy · max **15**

| Total | Survival / approach |
|---|---|
| **0–8** | **<6 months** — conservative / palliative |
| **9–11 (9–12)** | **>6 months** — individualise |
| **≥12 (12–15)** | **>1 year** — excisional surgery |

**Worked example (OB)** — good health 2 + no extraspinal bone mets 2 + 2 spinal lesions 1 + removable visceral 1 + **rectal primary 4** + incomplete palsy 1 = **11** → surgery

**Tomita** — primary growth + visceral mets + bone mets \
✗ Wrong options: **general condition** **(TO)**

### NOMS framework

**NOMS** — **N**eurologic (ESCC grade) · **O**ncologic (**radiosensitivity**) · **M**echanical instability (**SINS**) · **S**ystemic illness (disease burden, comorbidity, ECOG) · most comprehensive current decision framework (FNB 2024 key not given — NOMS fits; ✗ Wrong options: Bigliani · DeWald; Tomita is a score) **(TO)**

**ESCC (epidural cord compression)** — **0–1 low grade**, **2–3 high grade**

### SINS (Spinal Instability Neoplastic Score)

| Element | Scores |
|---|---|
| **Location** | **Junctional** (occiput–C2, C7–T2, T11–L1, L5–S1) **3** · mobile (C3–C6, L2–L4) 2 · semi-rigid (T3–T10) 1 · rigid (S2–S5) 0 |
| **Pain** (mechanical — relief lying down / pain on loading) | Yes **3** · occasional non-mechanical 1 · none 0 |
| **Lesion** | **Lytic 2** · mixed 1 · blastic 0 |
| **Alignment** | **Subluxation/translation 4** · de novo deformity (kyphosis/scoliosis) 2 · normal 0 |
| **Body collapse** | >50% 3 · <50% 2 · none but >50% body involved 1 · none 0 |
| **Posterolateral involvement** (facet, pedicle, CV joint) | Bilateral 3 · unilateral 1 · none 0 |

| SINS | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **0–6** | **Stable** — no surgical consult |
| **7–12** | **Potentially unstable** → surgical referral |
| **13–18** | **Unstable** → surgical referral |

**Worked example (OB)** — T6 (1) + occasional non-mechanical pain (1) + mixed (1) + **focal kyphosis (2)** + 30% collapse (2) + no posterolateral (0) = **7** → surgical referral\
✗ Wrong options: normal-alignment combinations scoring 4–6

## 5 Management

```
Spinal metastasis (NOMS)
├─ Stable (SINS ≤6), no deficit, life <6 mo → PAIN CONTROL ± BRACE, EBRT
├─ No/stable fracture, no compression or deficit → RT / SRS ± KYPHOPLASTY / ABLATION
├─ Neuro deficit or high-grade ESCC, life >6 mo → DECOMPRESSION + STABILISATION → POST-OP RT
│      ├─ Anterior (vertebral body) compression → CORPECTOMY + ANTERIOR COLUMN RECON + POSTERIOR FUSION
│      └─ RCC / thyroid → PRE-OP EMBOLIZATION (same morning)
└─ Radiosensitive tumour (myeloma, lymphoma) → RT may suffice
```

**TO rule** — **radioresistant → decompression + stabilisation** · **radiosensitive → RT first, then stabilisation** · vertebroplasty / kyphoplasty for pain from instability (TO)

### Nonoperative & radiation

**Pain + brace** — stable fracture, no deficit, **life <6 months** (Tokuhashi)

**External beam RT** — painful lesions **without deficit or cord compression**\
✗ Wrong options: embolization · laminectomy · corpectomy

**SRS** — **88% local control** if no instability or compression

**Kyphoplasty / vertebroplasty / RFA** — no or stable fracture, **no compression, deficit, or instability** · transpedicular, fluoroscopy · kyphoplasty = balloon cavity then PMMA (partial reduction) · not with **posterior cortex disruption** or neurologic impairment

### Surgery — decompression, stabilisation & radiation

**Patchell RCT** — **direct decompression + post-op RT > RT alone** for metastatic cord compression → best for **regaining/maintaining ambulation**

**Indications** — neurologic deficit + life **>6 months** · intractable pain · instability

**Sequence** — **surgery first, then radiation** · chemo too slow for acute deficit\
✗ Wrong options: RT before surgery → **3× wound complications**

**Anterior compression (vertebral body)** — **corpectomy + anterior column reconstruction + posterior instrumented fusion** · posterior-only via **costotransversectomy / transpedicular** with expandable cage\
✗ Wrong options: **laminectomy alone** → doesn't decompress, progressive **kyphosis** · **posterior fusion alone** → continued compression

**Approach** — posterior for thoracic, lumbar, sacral · cervical anterior or posterior · cement augments screws

**Separation surgery** — decompress + stabilise to create space for **SRS** · no en bloc resection for metastases\
✗ Wrong options: goal of total resection; margin usually intralesional

**Outcomes** — **95% local control** with surgery + RT · **16%** of ASIA E with high-grade ESCC decline even with surgery \
✗ Wrong options: laminectomy + steroids alone

**Breast mets, failed chemo, T9 ventral compression, hyperreflexia** — **thoracic corpectomy + instrumented fusion + post-op RT**\
✗ Wrong options: CHOP · posterior decompression after kyphoplasty · RT alone

### Preoperative embolisation (renal, thyroid)

**Hypervascular** — **renal + thyroid** → **preop embolization** (✗ Wrong options: breast, colon, prostate, lung, liver) · ideally **morning of surgery** (✗ Wrong options: post-op) · target the **tumour capillary bed**, not main feeder (collaterals) · agents: glue, alcohol, PVA particles, Gelfoam

**RCC spine with deficit** — **embolization → decompression + stabilization → RT** · RCC radioresistant → RT alone won't relieve compression \
✗ Wrong options: kyphoplasty · steroids · cryotherapy

**Colon cancer** — no embolization needed

### Bisphosphonates

**Zoledronic acid** — **↓ skeletal-related events 30–40%** (fracture, need for RT/surgery, cord compression, hypercalcaemia) · **pain ↓ in ≥50%** · **ONJ ~2.5%** · **renal toxicity** → dose by creatinine clearance · **zoledronic acid > pamidronate**\
✗ Wrong options: 15% · 25%

## 6 Complications

**Recurrence** · **hardware failure / instability** (RT, chemo, frailty; **>6 levels** → instrumentation failure) · nonunion

### Revision surgery — soft tissue reconstruction

**Wound complications** — risk: smoking, **prior RT**, instrumentation, revision · **immediate soft-tissue reconstruction** → better outcomes (delayed = **2×** wound complications)\
✗ Wrong options: preop RT · tobacco · posterior approach

### Prognosis

**Timing** — cancer → spinal met **32 months** · spinal met → fracture **27 months**

**1-yr survival after spine-met surgery** — **50%** · epidural disease worsens

**Survival factors** — histology + prior treatment, function + neurology, age, extent, nutrition

**Median survival** — thyroid **48 mo** · prostate **40** · breast **24** · kidney variable (≥6) · **lung 6**
