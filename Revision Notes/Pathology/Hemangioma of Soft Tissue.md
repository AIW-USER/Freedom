# Hemangioma of Soft Tissue

*Pathology · 25 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Nature** — benign vascular neoplasm · **error in new formation of a vascular segment** · hormones modulate · **20% trauma-associated**

**Demographics** — **<30 yr** · M = F

**Site** — **cutaneous, subcutaneous, intramuscular** (deep, lower limb) · **hand** common · usually solitary · **diffuse haemangioma** = rare childhood form involving a whole limb

**Types** — **capillary (superficial)** vs **cavernous (deep)**

**Tissue of origin** — **vascular**\
✗ Wrong options: cartilage, bone, smooth or skeletal muscle

## 3 Presentation

**Pain varies with activity** — engorgement → **aching, heaviness, swelling** · worse when **dependent**, standing, exercising, swimming · relieved by rest, elevation, ice

**Mass size varies** — fills after minutes of standing → examine **supine and standing** · may be warm · birthmark that enlarges

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **phleboliths** (calcified thrombi in vessels) · bone erosion · may be normal

![Phleboliths in a cavernous haemangioma](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8069-benign-cavernous-hemangioma-x-ray.jpg)

**MRI with gadolinium** — **diagnostic study** · separates from **AVM and angiosarcoma** · **T1 iso- to hyperintense (fat)** · **T2 hyperintense** · low-signal foci = flow voids / calcification · heterogeneous vessels + **fatty infiltration → "bag of worms"** · no necrosis, no rim enhancement, no mass effect

![Intramuscular haemangioma on MRI](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8069-hemangioma-mri-forearm.jpg)

**Fat on T1 + calcification on X-ray** → diagnostic of haemangioma

### Histology

**No malignant cells**, no pleomorphism · multiple **dilated endothelial-lined vascular channels filled with erythrocytes** · **intramuscular type: lumens infiltrated by (striated) muscle fibres** · cavernous: large vessels + fat

**Gross** — red to tan to yellow

### Differential

| Lesion | Distinguishing |
|---|---|
| **Haemangioma** | Activity-related pain/size, phleboliths, fat on T1 |
| **Low-flow venous malformation** | Congenital, **multifocal, infiltrative**, adolescence (hormonal), compressible, phleboliths, interspersed fat |
| **AVM** | High flow · diagnose by exam + imaging (phleboliths, Doppler flow) · **multidisciplinary / IR** |
| Synovial sarcoma | Painless, calcification, different T1 |
| Lipoma | Homogeneous bright T1, suppresses |
| Desmoid | Dark T1 + T2, no fat |
| PVNS | Similar imaging, different pain · haemosiderin giant cells |
| Parosteal OS | Stuck on posterior distal femur |
| Muscle tear haematoma | Should resolve over 3 months |

## 5 Management

```
Soft-tissue haemangioma / vascular malformation
├─ First line (incl. children, incidental) → OBSERVE + NSAIDs + COMPRESSION STOCKINGS + ACTIVITY MODIFICATION
├─ Large, painful, failed conservative → IR: SCLEROTHERAPY (ethanol, US-guided) or EMBOLIZATION
├─ Small hand lesion failing conservative → MARGINAL EXCISION
└─ Recalcitrant, large → WIDE RESECTION (high local recurrence)
```

**AVM failing conservative care** — **consult interventional radiology** (coil embolization / sclerotherapy)\
✗ Wrong options: CT CAP staging · biopsy · chemo · RT

**Not used** — chemotherapy · radiation

**Recurrence after excision** — high · deep lesions abut NV structures → often intralesional

## 6 Complications

**Kasabach-Merritt syndrome** — **platelet trapping → consumptive coagulopathy**, potentially fatal

**Infantile haemangiomas** — **involute by age 7**

**Malignant transformation** — **none**

## How it's asked

**"30-yr-old, painful posterior knee mass enlarging with activity, calcification + fat on T1?"** → **haemangioma**\
✗ Wrong options: synovial sarcoma · lipoma · parosteal OS · PVNS

**"17-yr-old, enlarging hand birthmark, pain when dependent/swimming, phleboliths?"** → **benign haemangioma**\
✗ Wrong options: angiosarcoma · epithelioid sarcoma · GCT tendon sheath

**"Shoulder mass painful + warm after activity, normal X-ray, fat on MRI?"** → **intramuscular haemangioma**\
✗ Wrong options: synovial sarcoma · metastasis · haematoma · abscess

**"Tissue of origin?"** → **vascular**

**"Multiple infiltrative 'bag of worms' masses with fat in a teen?"** → **low-flow venous malformation**\
✗ Wrong options: desmoid · lipoma · MPNST · schwannoma

**"Calf mass enlarging at end of day — histology + treatment?"** → **vascular dilations with erythrocytes + striated muscle · observation**

**"Foot AVM failing stockings + activity change?"** → **consult interventional radiology**

**"Incidental intramuscular gastrocnemius haemangioma?"** → **observation**\
✗ Wrong options: wide/marginal resection · RT · chemo
