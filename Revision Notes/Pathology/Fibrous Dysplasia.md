# Fibrous Dysplasia

*Pathology · 20 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — 1 in 5,000–10,000 · ~2.5% of bone lesions, 5% of benign bone lesions

**Age** — **75% onset <30** (5–30) · monostotic 10–30 · polyostotic earlier (<10; 60% symptomatic)

**Sex** — **M = F**

**Site** — any bone · **proximal femur commonest** · monostotic: **ribs 28%**, **proximal femur 23%**, skull/craniofacial, tibia, humerus · polyostotic: often **unilateral**, femur, skull/face, pelvis, spine · craniofacial: zygomatic-maxillary complex · skull + face generalized = **"leontiasis ossea"** **(TO)**

**Location in bone** — **medullary** (metaphysis/diaphysis) — not cortical (NOF, OFD, adamantinoma are cortical) **(TO)**

### Pathophysiology

**Defect** — primitive woven bone **fails to remodel to lamellar bone** → immature trabeculae in dysplastic fibrous tissue

**Natural history** — active in childhood → **inactive at skeletal maturity** (monostotic grows with skeleton; polyostotic with disease severity)

### Genes & lab markers — GNAS / Gs-alpha

**Mutation** — **activating missense GNAS (GNAS1)** → **Gs-α subunit (20q13)** · **somatic mosaicism**, spontaneous · **no vertical transmission** · timing → extent

**Pathway** — loss of intrinsic GTPase → **constitutive adenylate cyclase → ↑ cAMP** → c-fos ↑ · ↑ **WNT/β-catenin** → osteoprogenitors fail to mature · ↑ NF-κB, IL-6 → resorption

**FGF-23 ↑** — **renal phosphate wasting → hypophosphataemia → osteomalacia** (Looser zones)

**cAMP pathway question** — ✓ **FD**\
✗ Wrong options: achondroplasia FGFR3 · hypochondroplasia · MHE EXT · NF1 · enchondroma

| Gene / defect | Condition |
|---|---|
| **GNAS / Gs-α (↑ cAMP)** | ✓ **Fibrous dysplasia, McCune-Albright** |
| DTD (sulfate transporter) | Diastrophic dysplasia |
| CBFA1 / RUNX2 | Cleidocranial dysostosis |
| COL1A1 / COL1A2 | Osteogenesis imperfecta |
| FGFR3 | Achondroplasia |
| Ring chromosome 12 (MDM2) | Low-grade osteosarcoma |
| t(11;22) | Ewing |

**Labs** — monostotic: none · polyostotic: **thyroid panel**, **IGF-1, GH, prolactin** (MAS screen) · **ALP** to monitor bisphosphonate; ↑ ALP with malignant change

### Associated syndromes

| Syndrome | Components |
|---|---|
| **McCune-Albright** | **Polyostotic FD + café-au-lait ("coast of Maine", jagged, don't cross midline, trunk) + endocrinopathy** (**precocious puberty** — 85% of girls; thyroid 66%; GH excess; ovarian cysts; neonatal hypercortisolism) · **unilateral** |
| **Mazabraud** | Polyostotic FD + **intramuscular myxomas** |
| **Jaffe-Lichtenstein** | Polyostotic FD + café-au-lait, **no endocrinopathy** |
| Jaffe-Campanacci | Multiple **NOFs** + café-au-lait, no neurofibromas |
| NF1 | Café-au-lait **"coast of California"** (smooth) + neurofibromas, 17q11.2 |

## 3 Presentation

**Usually asymptomatic** — incidental

**Pain** — high-stress sites · ↑ in **pregnancy / menses** (oestrogen receptors)

**Deformity** — **LLD (commonest skeletal deformity)** · **shepherd's crook** (coxa vara, lateral bowing, shortening) · **saber shin** (anterior tibial bowing) · **scoliosis**

**Craniofacial** — cosmetic deformity, blindness, hearing loss, airway obstruction, proptosis, sinus occlusion

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **expansile, central intramedullary**, well-defined, smooth cortices · lytic, sclerotic or mixed → **"ground glass"** · cortical thinning, endosteal scalloping · **no periosteal reaction** · fills bone over length

![Ground-glass lesion of the proximal femur](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8038-prfm1-1-moved.jpg)

**"Rind sign"** — punched-out / ground-glass lesion with **thick sclerotic rim**

**Parrot beak sign** — **fatigue fracture of the femoral neck** in FD (TO)

**Looser zones** — pseudofractures (osteomalacia)

**Shepherd's crook** — AP + lateral hip

**Spine X-rays (AP/lateral)** — in **polyostotic / MAS** (60% spinal involvement, posterior elements; scoliosis 40%) → **next step after hot spinal lesions on bone scan**\
✗ Wrong options: CT · pelvis X-ray · bisphosphonates · endocrine screen

**CT** — ground glass 56%, sclerosis 23%, cystic 21% · enhances

**MRI** — **T1 dark**, T2 variable, moderate heterogeneous enhancement · separates from malignancy

**Bone scan** — warm until adulthood · screens for **polyostotic** disease / MAS in children

**Diagnosis** — history + exam + X-ray usually enough · biopsy if polyostotic/MAS doubt or malignancy

### Histology

**"Chinese letters" / "alphabet soup"** — curvilinear irregular **woven bone trabeculae** in **fibrous stroma** (fibroblast proliferation)

**No osteoblastic rimming** (✓ rimming = osteofibrous dysplasia)

**Gross** — yellow/tan-white, **gritty/sandy** · cartilage in ~10% · cysts in older lesions

![Woven bone without osteoblastic rimming in fibrous stroma](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8038-1c-moved.jpg)

### Classification

| Type | Definition | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **Monostotic** | Single bone | **70%** |
| **Polyostotic** | Multiple bones | **30%** |

### "Touch-me-not" lesions (TO)

**Definition** — incidental, X-ray-diagnostic, need **no biopsy or intervention** · myositis ossificans, cortical desmoid, enchondroma, osteochondroma, **FD**, NOF, Paget, fibrous cortical defect, intraosseous lipoma, bone island, bone infarct · not **osteoblastoma** (painful, needs treatment)

### Differential

| | Multiple lesions, young | Observation alone | Bisphosphonates help |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FD** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| EG | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Ollier/Maffucci · MHE · NOF/Jaffe-Campanacci | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Lymphoma · leukaemia · haemangioendothelioma | ✓ | — | — |
| Paget | — | ✓ | ✓ (>50 yr, coarse trabeculae) |
| Metastasis · myeloma | — | — | ✓ |

**Osteofibrous dysplasia** — **cortical**, anterior tibia, **osteoblastic rimming**

**Adamantinoma** — anterior tibial cortex, **epithelial nests**

**Not seen in FD (TO)** — **Harrison groove** (rickets) · ✓ rind sign, Chinese letters, café-au-lait

## 5 Management

```
Fibrous dysplasia
├─ Asymptomatic, low fracture risk → OBSERVE (6-monthly exam + X-ray to maturity)
├─ Symptomatic pain, polyostotic, low fracture risk → BISPHOSPHONATE (IV, off-label)
├─ Fracture within acceptable limits (UE, tibia, child) → IMMOBILISE ± CLOSED REDUCTION
├─ Impending / pathologic fracture, weight-bearing → LONG INTRAMEDULLARY NAIL ± OSTEOTOMY
│      └─ IM nail contraindicated → TITANIUM LOCKING PLATE (whole bone) + graft
└─ Coxa vara / shepherd's crook → VALGUS (intertrochanteric) OSTEOTOMY + FIXATION
```

### Treatment (pathologic fracture, shepherd's crook, bisphosphonates)

**IM nail** — **gold standard** (load-sharing) · **long** nail to protect whole bone · elastic nails if canal small · for recalcitrant pain, impending/actual fracture, progressive deformity

**Plate** — **titanium** (modulus closer to bone) **locking**, **longest plate**, maximum screws · IM device > plate in lower limb

**Graft choice** — **calcium phosphate cement** (longest resorption) · **cortical allograft** > cancellous (slower resorption) · not **autograft** → resorbed, **converts to dysplastic bone**

**Isolated curettage + graft** — poor (resorption, fracture, reoperation) · only low-risk monostotic upper-limb lesions

**Polyostotic + femoral neck path fracture (>2/3 diameter)** — **biopsy, curettage, bone grafting + lesion-spanning internal fixation**\
✗ Wrong options: observation · RT · percutaneous screws (soft lateral cortex)

**5-yr-old, painful coxa vara** — **valgus osteotomy + internal fixation**\
✗ Wrong options: curettage + allograft · observation · spica

**Symptomatic adult, no fracture risk** — **bisphosphonate**\
✗ Wrong options: RFA · HIFU (osteoid osteoma) · steroid injection (UBC) · curettage + autograft

**Craniofacial** — recontouring / curettage · **radiotherapy contraindicated** (malignant transformation) · calcitonin unsuccessful

## 6 Complications

**Pathologic fracture** — **50% of humeral lesions ≥1 fracture** · 50% of monostotic patients · humerus risk: **>50% cortical involvement, >30 mm, cystic degeneration** · femur: large neck/trochanteric lesion

### Coxa vara classification

| Type | Region | Neck-shaft angle | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Neck + trochanter | Normal 120–140° | 24% |
| **2** | Neck + trochanter | Valgus >140° | 6% |
| **3** | Neck + trochanter | **Varus <120°** | 7% |
| **4** | **Lateral bowing** of proximal shaft | Normal | 20% |
| **5** | Lateral bowing | Valgus | 14% |
| **6** | Lateral bowing | **Varus** | **29%** |

**Course** — **types 1–2 stable**, **types 3–6 progress** · goal: NSA 120–140°, correct bowing, apex-anterior + rotation, total-bone load-sharing fixation

### Malignant transformation

**Risk ~1%** — higher in **polyostotic** (MAS, Mazabraud), after **radiotherapy**

**Tumours** — **osteosarcoma (commonest)** > fibrosarcoma > MFH · usually **diaphysis**, decades later

**Clinical** — new pain, swelling, mass, **↑ ALP** · **>50% mortality** (worse than primary sarcoma)

**Scoliosis** — 40% in polyostotic

## How it's asked

**"Genetic abnormality in cAMP signalling?"** → **fibrous dysplasia** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: achondroplasia · MHE · enchondroma

**"Gs-α point mutation — which X-ray?"** → **polyostotic FD with ground-glass proximal femur**\
✗ Wrong options: adamantinoma · enchondroma · GCT · melorheostosis

**"Gs-α (↑ cAMP) mutation causes?"** → **FD**\
✗ Wrong options: diastrophic dysplasia · cleidocranial · OI · achondroplasia

**"Aetiology of ground-glass lesion + Chinese-letter histology?"** → **Gs-α mutation → constitutive cAMP**\
✗ Wrong options: ring chromosome 12 · parathyroid · t(11;22)

**"GNAS mutation associated with?"** → **FD**\
✗ Wrong options: osteofibrous dysplasia · chondroblastoma

**"McCune-Albright EXCEPT?"** → **multiple neurofibromas**

**"Jagged café-au-lait + hip lesion in a 6-yr-old?"** → **McCune-Albright**\
✗ Wrong options: NF · Holt-Oram · Ellis-van Creveld

**"MAS, 3 hot spinal lesions — next?"** → **AP/lateral spine radiographs**

**"Polyostotic FD, serrated café-au-lait, endocrine, unilateral?"** → **McCune-Albright**\
✗ Wrong options: Jaffe-Campanacci

**"Coxa vara, ground glass, jagged trunk pigmentation — risk of?"** → **other bones + precocious puberty**

**"NOT seen in FD?"** → **Harrison groove** **(TO)**

**"Lesion NOT primarily cortical?"** → **fibrous dysplasia** (✓ NOF, OFD, adamantinoma cortical) **(TO)**

**"Touch-me-not lesions EXCEPT?"** → **osteoblastoma** **(TO)**

**"Incidental ground-glass proximal femur / humerus?"** → **FD**\
✗ Wrong options: enchondroma (rings/arcs) · UBC · infarct

**"Woven bone without osteoblastic rimming in bland stroma?"** → **FD**\
✗ Wrong options: ossifying fibroma · low-grade OS

**"16-yr-old MAS, undisplaced femoral neck path fracture?"** → **biopsy, curettage, grafting + internal fixation**

**"5-yr-old, painful coxa vara with ground glass?"** → **valgus osteotomy + internal fixation**

**"38-yr-old, symptomatic distal femoral FD, biopsy-proven?"** → **bisphosphonate therapy**

**"Polyostotic FD, new pain at 39?"** → **osteosarcoma** (malignant transformation)

**"14-yr-old, huge craniofacial ground-glass lesion on CT?"** → **maxillary fibrous dysplasia** **(TO)**\
