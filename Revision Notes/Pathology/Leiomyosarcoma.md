# Leiomyosarcoma

*Pathology · 29 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Nature** — aggressive sarcoma from **smooth muscle cells lining small blood vessels** ("leio-" = smooth muscle)

**Demographics** — **5th–6th decades**

**Bone** — <100 reported cases (extra-facial) · **metaphysis of long bones** (also diaphysis) · femur, tibia, **ilium**, humerus

### Genes & lab markers

**Immunostains** — **smooth muscle actin +**, **vimentin +**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — bone pain, palpable firm mass · ~6 months before diagnosis · weight loss

**Pelvic masses** — hard to see on inspection

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **purely osteolytic**, ill-defined, **moth-eaten / permeative** · intramedullary ± soft-tissue extension

**MRI** — **T1 dark (like muscle)** · T2 heterogeneous bright · diffuse enhancement

**Staging** — **chest X-ray + chest CT** (lung mets) · bone scan uptake

### Histology

**Spindle cell neoplasm** (osseous = soft tissue form) · **cigar-shaped (blunt-ended) nuclei** · **interlacing fascicles** with parallel myofibrils · high N:C, mitoses

![Spindle cells in fascicles with cigar-shaped nuclei](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8058-histolgy-1.jpg)

**Round cells?** — no: leiomyosarcoma is the **spindle-cell** exception among round-cell tumour lists

## 5 Management

```
Leiomyosarcoma
├─ Localized → EARLY WIDE RESECTION + RECONSTRUCTION
│      └─ Wide margin not achieved / contaminated bed → + ADJUVANT RADIATION
├─ Unplanned excision, margins unknown/positive → RE-EXCISION OF TUMOUR BED (± RT)
└─ Diffuse lung mets / unresectable pelvic mass on NV structures → CHEMOTHERAPY
```

**Chemo/RT** — variable response · chemo + surgery better than surgery alone · neoadjuvant/adjuvant role controversial

**Unplanned excision of 3 cm superficial LMS** — **repeat wide excision of tumour bed** · residual disease in up to **35%** · local recurrence **22% vs 7%**\
✗ Wrong options: observation · RT alone · chemo

## 6 Complications

**Prognosis (bone LMS)** — **25% recurrence**, **25% metastasis** · **75% survival at 3 yr**

## How it's asked

**"35-yr-old, aggressive lytic pelvic lesion, smooth muscle actin +?"** → **leiomyosarcoma** histology — interlacing spindle cells\
✗ Wrong options: GCT · myeloma · OS · hyaline cartilage

**"Outside simple excision of 3 cm thigh mass = leiomyosarcoma, margins unknown?"** → **repeat wide excision of the tumour bed**

**"Neoplasms with round cells EXCEPT?"** → **leiomyosarcoma** (spindle cells, cigar nuclei)
