# Malignant Peripheral Nerve Sheath Tumor

*Pathology · 34 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Synonyms** — **neurofibrosarcoma**, malignant schwannoma · high-grade STS from a **nerve or pre-existing neurofibroma**

**Age** — solitary **30–55** · with **NF1 20–40**

### Diagnosis & NF1 association

**NF1** — most cases · **4% of NF1 patients** develop MPNST · **autosomal dominant** (17q11.2)\
✗ Wrong options: AR · X-linked

### Genes & lab markers

**S-100 +** (diffuse) · **keratin −**

**S-100 positive tumours** — **melanoma, nerve sheath tumours, clear cell sarcoma** (± synovial sarcoma) · chondroblastoma, chondroid lesions

## 3 Presentation

**Mass** on a **large nerve** — **sciatic, brachial plexus**, femoral

**Neurologic** — motor + sensory deficit, radicular pain · **pressure → dysaesthesia** distally

**NF1 red flag** — a neurofibroma that **enlarges** and becomes **constantly painful**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — normal / non-specific soft-tissue mass

**MRI** — **T1 low, T2 high** (like other high-grade sarcomas) · traced to a major nerve · oedema, **rim enhancement (necrosis)** · serial MRI: enlargement of a benign nerve sheath lesion = malignant change

**PET** — **SUV >2.0** → malignant

![MPNST arising from the sciatic nerve](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8055-axial-mri-demonstrating-mpnst-arising-from-sciatic-nerve.jpg)

**Bone scan** — mildly positive

### Histology

**Atypical pleomorphic spindle cells with wavy nuclei** — resembles **fibrosarcoma** · hypocellular stroma areas

| | **MPNST** | **Schwannoma** | **Neurofibroma** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cells | **Pleomorphic, atypical** spindle cells | Bland, Antoni A/B | Bland, wavy nuclei, mast cells |
| MRI | Necrosis, oedema, rim enhancement | **Target sign** | Entering-exiting nerve, target sign |
| S-100 | + | + | + |

## 5 Management

**Wide resection including the entire affected nerve** + **preoperative (neoadjuvant) radiation** — treated as high-grade sarcoma

**Chemotherapy** — not useful

## 6 Complications

**5-yr survival** — **solitary 75%** · **NF1-associated 30%** (Orthobullets) · one OB-cited series: NF1 not independently worse

**Poor prognosis** — size, mitotic rate, need for amputation

## How it's asked

**"45-yr-old, radicular buttock pain, S-100 + pleomorphic spindle cells?"** → **MPNST**\
✗ Wrong options: schwannoma (bland, target sign) · liposarcoma · GCT tendon sheath

**"NF patient, enlarging painful posterior thigh mass on sciatic nerve, rim enhancement, PET SUV >2?"** → **MPNST**\
✗ Wrong options: benign PNST · MFH · liposarcoma · synovial sarcoma

**"S-100 + spindle-cell tumour of femoral nerve — associated condition's inheritance?"** → **autosomal dominant** (NF1)

**"Treatment?"** → **wide resection of tumour + entire nerve + preop RT**\
✗ Wrong options: chemo
