# Pigmented Villonodular Synovitis

*Pathology · 54 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Name** — now **tenosynovial giant cell tumour (TGCT)** · WHO 2013: **diffuse TGCT = PVNS** · **localized TGCT = GCT of tendon sheath** (commonest in **fingers**) **(TO)**

**Nature** — **locally aggressive neoplastic synovial disease** ("not a true neoplasm" — Orthobullets) · neoplastic: **aneuploidy, trisomy 7**, ~20 malignant/metastatic cases **(TO)** · "non-neoplastic inflammatory" (OB7584) · rarely metastasizes

**Incidence** — **9.2 per million/yr** (Orthobullets) · **1.8 per million/yr**, 1 in 250 arthroscopies **(TO)**

**Demographics** — **30–40 yr** (any age; stems 14–58) · **M = F** (Orthobullets) · slight **F** predominance (OB9003)

**Site** — monoarticular · **knee > hip > ankle > shoulder > elbow** · localized: **anterior knee (80%)**, patellofemoral, infrapatellar fat pad

**History of trauma** — 50%

### CSF-1 pathogenesis & pexidartinib

**Driver** — **overexpression of CSF1** → CSF1R-expressing cells cluster → synovial hyperplasia · chromosome **1p13** · **5q33** rearrangement

| Gene distractor | Lesion |
|---|---|
| **CSF1 ↑** | **PVNS / TGCT** |
| USP6 | ABC, nodular fasciitis |
| MDM2 | Parosteal / low-grade OS, ALT |
| APC | Desmoid (FAP) |
| c-myc | Aggressive GCT of bone |
| EXT | MHE |
| RB | OS (up to 35%) |
| p53 | Li-Fraumeni sarcomas, breast |
| FGFR3 · RUNX2 | Achondroplasia · cleidocranial dysplasia |
| Ki-67 · NF1 · NTRK | Ewing · neurofibromatosis · various solid tumours |

### Localized vs diffuse

| | **Localized** | **Diffuse** |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Knee > hip > ankle | Knee (75%) |
| Age | 30–50 | <40 |
| Sex | M = F | F ≥ M |
| Presentation | **Painless**, swollen, long-standing · locking/catching (meniscal mimic) **(TO)** | **Painful**, swollen, tender, ↓ motion |
| X-ray | Erosion from local pressure | Degenerative change **both sides of joint** |
| MRI | **Well-circumscribed** mass | **Ill-defined** mass |
| Recurrence after synovectomy | **8%** | **30%** |
| Treatment | **Nodule excision + partial synovectomy** | **Total synovectomy** (anterior arthroscopic + posterior open) |

## 3 Presentation

**Hallmark** — **recurrent atraumatic haemarthrosis** · insidious pain, stiffness, swelling · boggy effusion, warmth, erythema, joint-line tenderness, ↓ ROM

**Pain** — often not the main complaint **(TO)** · vague for years

**Labs** — **ESR, CRP normal**

**Aspirate** — **grossly bloody / brown** · e.g. >50,000 RBC, 1,100 WBC

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + lateral)** — soft-tissue swelling · **cystic erosions with sclerotic margins on both sides of the joint** · knee often shows advanced OA; erosions/cysts **commoner in hip + ankle** (tight joints) **(TO)** · **no mineralization**\
✗ Wrong options: synovial chondromatosis

![Cystic erosions with sclerotic margins on both sides](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8050-pvns-cystic-erosion-with-sclerotic-margins-160-on-both-sides.jpg)

**CT** — extent of cystic bone loss

**MRI (most sensitive)** — **low signal on T1 and T2** (haemosiderin) · **blooming artefact** on gradient echo (iron) · fat signal on T1 · effusion, synovial expansion, erosion · **posterior extra-articular extension** · modest enhancement · brighter foci = less haemosiderin **(TO)** · "somewhat bright on T2" (OB7420)

![Low T2 signal of PVNS](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8050-low-intensity-signal-of-pvns-on-t2-mri.jpg)

**Arthroscopy (gold standard + biopsy)** — **brownish/reddish inflamed synovium**, **frond-like papillary projections**

![Brown frond-like synovium](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8050-23-moved.jpg)

### Histology

**Haemosiderin-stained multinucleated giant cells** · mononuclear stromal cells · vascular villi lined by plump hyperplastic synoviocytes · **pigmented foam cells (lipid-laden histiocytes)** · mitoses common (few in OB stems) · gross: yellow-brown mass

![Haemosiderin-laden giant cells](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8050-q4-1d-1-moved.jpg)

| Histology distractor | Lesion |
|---|---|
| Biphasic spindle + epithelial | Synovial sarcoma |
| Lipoblasts, myxoid, signet-ring | Myxoid liposarcoma |
| Antoni A/B | **Neurilemmoma** |
| Plasma cells, lymphoid follicles | RA |
| Caseating granulomas | TB |
| Mature chondrocyte nodules | Synovial chondromatosis |
| Osteoclast-like cells + stroma + osteoid | OS / GCT of bone |

### Differential

| Lesion | Key distinction |
|---|---|
| **Synovial chondromatosis** | Nodules **low T1, high T2**, mineralized loose bodies · no haemorrhagic effusion |
| **Synovial sarcoma** | **Periarticular, not intra-articular** · T2 bright · spindle cells |
| Haemophilia | Recurrent effusions, but MRI not nodular mass |
| RA | Pannus, uniform signal · no haemorrhage |
| Gout | Punched-out erosions, overhanging edges, tophi |
| Tumoral calcinosis | Extracapsular periarticular calcification |
| GCT of bone | Eccentric epiphyseal/metaphyseal lesion in bone |
| Baker's cyst | Fluid outpouching, semimembranosus–medial gastrocnemius |
| ABC · chondroblastoma | Intra-osseous (PVNS is extra-osseous) |

**Behaviour descriptor** — **locally aggressive**\
✗ Wrong options: malignant · inflammatory · normal variant

## 5 Management

```
PVNS / TGCT
├─ Asymptomatic → OBSERVATION
├─ Localized → NODULE EXCISION + PARTIAL SYNOVECTOMY (arthroscopic if anterior)
├─ Diffuse → TOTAL SYNOVECTOMY (arthroscopic anterior + open posterior) ± EBRT
├─ End-stage joint → SYNOVECTOMY + ARTHROPLASTY (ankle → ARTHRODESIS)
└─ Recurrent / unresectable, surgery morbid → PEXIDARTINIB
```

### Surgical treatment & recurrence

**Mainstay** — **synovectomy** (open or arthroscopic) · arthroscopic → less morbidity, better ROM · open → pain, stiffness **(TO)**

**Margin** — synovectomy = **intralesional** (OB417 — only lesion where intralesional is standard) · = **marginal** excision (Orthobullets, OB7568)

**Localized** — **resection of nodule only** · arthroscopic anterior excision + partial synovectomy\
✗ Wrong options: complete synovectomy · RT · FNA (excisional biopsy)

**Diffuse knee** — lowest recurrence: **open anterior + posterior synovectomy** (morbid) · arthroscopic anterior + **open posterior** (between gastrocnemius heads — **popliteal NV bundle at risk**) · **posteromedial portal** for posterior compartment **(TO)**

**Foot/ankle** — **marginal excision** · radioisotopes poor (multi-joint spread, **skin necrosis** if extravasated) \
✗ Wrong options: amputation · wide excision

**EBRT** — **30–35 Gy / 15 fractions** or **50 Gy / 25** · with total synovectomy → recurrence **10–20%** · for recurrent/destructive disease · not RT alone

**Intra-articular Y-90 / radiocolloid** — combined with surgery historically **(TO)** · now removed/abandoned — haematomyelopoietic disease, high complications (OB)

**Not used** — **chemotherapy** **(TO)** · intra-articular steroids · plica excision · TKA in young without end-stage cartilage

### Pexidartinib

**Mechanism** — oral **CSF-1R antagonist** (TKI) · also KIT + FLT3-ITD · **first and only FDA-approved** systemic therapy (**2019**) **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: imatinib · emactuzumab · nilotinib (used, not approved)

**Indication** — **diffuse TGCT not amenable to resection** / severe morbidity from surgery / recurrent recalcitrant \
✗ Wrong options: synovial chondromatosis · GCT of bone · synovial sarcoma **(TO)**

**Efficacy** — once daily 24 wk → ~40% improve (ENLIVEN) · partial responses → surgical downstaging

**Side effects** — **cholestatic hepatotoxicity** (REMS, LFT monitoring) · **hair depigmentation** · dose taper\
✗ Wrong options: alopecia

## 6 Complications

**Recurrence** — **most common** complication · **30–50%** despite complete synovectomy (Orthobullets) · **~50%** (OB219276) · 8% localized / 30% diffuse

**Joint destruction** — early OA → arthroplasty (↑ stiffness, infection) · arthrodesis or amputation

**Radiation** — skin necrosis, **radiation-induced sarcoma**

**Uncommon:** arthrofibrosis, chondrolysis, metastasis
