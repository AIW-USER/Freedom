# Undifferentiated Pleomorphic Sarcoma

*Pathology · 65 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Name** — formerly **malignant fibrous histiocytoma (MFH)** · MFH removed from **WHO 2013** · high-grade, aggressive, malignant fibrogenic tumour · cell of origin unknown

**Commonest STS** — in **adults 55–80** (Orthobullets) · commonest STS overall / of the extremity in mature adults (OB) · **commonest radiation-associated STS** · also after **Paget's**

**Incidence** — 1–9 / 100,000

**Demographics** — **55–80 yr** (Orthobullets) · mean **59**, range 32–80 (OB) · **M > F (1.2:1)** · Caucasian > African-American

**Site** — **lower limb (~50%) > upper limb (~20%) > retroperitoneum** · from bone in **1–5%** (see Pleomorphic Sarcoma of Bone chapter)

### Genes & lab markers

**PD-1** expression in **30%**

**Labs** — **hypoglycaemia**, ↑ WBC · ± fever

**IHC — diagnosis of exclusion** — **no lineage markers**: negative for desmin, myogenin, SMA, S-100, CD34, MDM2, EMA · FISH negative for t(X;18), t(12;16)

| Positive marker | Excludes UPS → |
|---|---|
| Desmin, myogenin | **Rhabdomyosarcoma** |
| Desmin, SMA | **Leiomyosarcoma** |
| S-100 | MPNST |
| CD34 | DFSP |
| MDM2 amplification | WD liposarcoma |
| EMA, t(X;18) | Synovial sarcoma |
| t(12;16) | Myxoid liposarcoma |

## 3 Presentation

**Slow-growing, painless mass** (can grow rapidly, fungate) · trauma draws attention · no ecchymosis (vs haematoma) · mass effect → NV symptoms · tight trousers, ↓ hip flexion

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnosis (MRI, immunohistochemistry)

**X-ray** — orthogonal views first

**MRI (mandatory)** — **T1 isointense to muscle (low)** · **T2 heterogeneous high** · heterogeneous enhancement, **non-enhancing necrotic centre** (poor prognosis)

![UPS — high T2 signal](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8064-soft-tissue-sarcoma-t2.jpg)

| MRI distractor | Lesion |
|---|---|
| Isointense to fat on all sequences | Lipoma / low-grade liposarcoma |
| Well-circumscribed, **homogeneous** T2 high | Myxoid lesion |
| Intramuscular, T2 high, **peri-lesional oedema at poles**, fat rind, thin septal enhancement | **Intramuscular myxoma** (40–70, F) |

### Histology

**Spindle + histiocytic + multinucleated eosinophilic (bizarre) giant cells** · **storiform (cartwheel)** pattern around small vessels · atypia, high mitotic index · gross: nodular grey-white

| Subtype | Share |
|---|---|
| **Pleomorphic** | **80–85%** |
| Giant cell | 10% |
| Inflammatory | <10% |

![Storiform pleomorphic spindle cells](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8064-ups-low-power.jpg)

### AJCC staging (extremity / trunk)

| Stage | Size | Nodes | Mets | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IA | <5 cm | None | None | Low |
| IB | >5 cm | None | None | Low |
| II | <5 cm | None | None | Intermediate/high |
| IIIA | 5–10 cm | None | None | Intermediate/high |
| IIIB | >10 cm | None | None | Intermediate/high |
| IVA | Any | **Regional** | None | Any |
| IVB | Any | Any | **Present** | Any |

### Surgical margins (Enneking)

| Margin | Plane |
|---|---|
| **Intralesional** | Through tumour — pseudocapsule violated |
| **Marginal** | Through **pseudocapsule / reactive zone** (may leave satellite cells) |
| **Wide** | Tumour + **cuff of normal tissue** |
| **Radical** | Whole compartment / amputation |

**R classification (AJCC)** — **R0** microscopically negative (goal of curative surgery) · R1 microscopic positive · R2 gross · UICC **R+1** variant

**Myxofibrosarcoma** — infiltrative → plan **≥3 cm** margin, frozen sections

## 5 Management

### Treatment — wide resection & radiation

**Standard** — **wide resection + RT** (pre- or post-op)\
✗ Wrong options: wide resection alone · RT alone · marginal ± RT · wide + chemo · debridement · intralesional debulking

**RT dose** — **50 Gy pre-op** · **66 Gy post-op** (Orthobullets, OB217430) · **50–60 Gy** (OB213031)

**Pre-op RT** — lower dose, **~30% wound complications** · post-op → more late toxicity, **radiation-induced sarcoma**

**Chemo** — some cases · **neoadjuvant chemo improves 5-yr RFS + OS in tumours >10 cm**

**Amputation / disarticulation** — only if negative margins impossible, major nerve loss, or comorbidity · limb salvage whenever reconstruction possible

**Biopsy** — at the treating centre · longitudinal · drain distal + in line · excised en bloc with tumour (elliptical incision)

**Follow-up** — close surveillance **5–10 yr**

## 6 Complications

**Prognosis** — **5-yr survival 50–60%** · aggressive

## How it's asked

**"64-yr-old forearm mass, FISH negative for t(X;18) + t(12;16), desmin/myogenin/CD34 negative?"** → **high-grade pleomorphic undifferentiated sarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: alveolar RMS · myxoid liposarcoma · DFSP · synovial

**"UPS MRI?"** → **T1 isointense to muscle, T2 heterogeneous high**\
✗ Wrong options: fat-isointense (lipoma) · homogeneous T2 (myxoid) · polar oedema (myxoma)

**"65-yr-old deep thigh mass, pleomorphic, SMA/desmin/S100/MDM2 negative?"** → **UPS**\
✗ Wrong options: leiomyosarcoma · WD liposarcoma · MPNST · synovial

**"Fungating posteromedial leg mass, necrotic centre, pleomorphic spindle cells?"** → **wide resection + RT**\
✗ Wrong options: wide resection alone · debridement

**"63-yr-old enlarging thigh mass, UPS?"** → **wide resection + radiation**\
✗ Wrong options: wide + chemo · marginal + RT

**"Truck driver, large anteromedial thigh UPS, no mets?"** → **RT + wide surgical excision**\
✗ Wrong options: hip disarticulation · debulking · RT alone

**"Neoadjuvant RT then resection, normal tissue cuff at closest margin?"** → **wide**\
✗ Wrong options: marginal · radical
