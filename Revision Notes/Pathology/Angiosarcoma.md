# Angiosarcoma

*Pathology · 3 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Demographics** — **elderly** · **male > female**

**Origin** — malignant, aggressive tumour of **vascular endothelium**

**Bone involvement** — **<10%** · of those, **60% long bones**

### Risk factors

**Classic associations** — **chronic vascular stasis** (lymphoedema) · trauma · **polyvinyl chloride (PVC) exposure**

### Genes & lab markers

**CD31** — **endothelial marker +** · sensitive for angiosarcoma

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — insidious **pain**

**Hallmark sign** — **overlying skin changes** (central necrosis) · hand: nail-fold lesions + multiple palmar masses

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — bone invasion · **eccentric, purely lytic, metaphyseal/diaphyseal** · **no matrix**

**MRI** — **study of choice** for the soft-tissue mass

**Angiography** — diffuse multifocal involvement

**CT chest** — lung metastases

![Angiosarcoma soft-tissue mass on MRI](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8070-case-b-leg-mri-parsons-moved.jpg)

### Histology

**Pattern** — **malignant cells lining vascular channels** · variable anaplasia · abundant eosinophilic cytoplasm, large nuclei, mitoses

![Malignant cells forming vascular channels](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8070-histology-a-parsons-moved.png)

## 5 Management

**Wide surgical resection** — **treatment of choice** · relatively **insensitive to chemo + radiation** (Orthobullets summary: resection **with** radiation)

**Amputation** — for local control of a very aggressive tumour · forequarter amputation reported for the upper limb

**Adjuncts** — radiation ± chemotherapy depending on extent/metastasis

## 6 Complications

**Prognosis** — **poor** · high local failure → amputation often needed

**Spread** — **lymphatic** spread (unusual for sarcoma) · **lung metastases** common

## How it's asked

**"PVC exposure, nail-fold + palmar lesions, lung mets, CD31 + — diagnosis?"** → **angiosarcoma**\
✗ Wrong options: epithelioid sarcoma · synovial sarcoma · chondrosarcoma · osteosarcoma

**"Risk factors?"** → **chronic lymphoedema / vascular stasis, PVC, trauma**

**"Endothelial immunomarker?"** → **CD31**
