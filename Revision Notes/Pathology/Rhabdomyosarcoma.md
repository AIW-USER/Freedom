# Rhabdomyosarcoma

*Pathology · 58 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & subtypes

**Nature** — malignant tumour of **primitive mesenchyme** (mesoderm, skeletal-muscle precursors)

**Commonest soft-tissue sarcoma in children**\
✗ Wrong options: foot (synovial sarcoma) · calcifies (synovial sarcoma) · Maffucci (haemangiomas) · from myxomas

**Site** — **head/neck, genitourinary, retroperitoneum** · GU > extremities (TO, OO chapter)

| Subtype | Age | Histology | 5-yr survival |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Embryonal** | Infants, young children | **Small round blue cells**, ± cross-striations | **80%** |
| **Alveolar** | **Adolescents, young adults** (15–40) | Poorly differentiated round cells + **multinucleated giant cells**, aggregates in **dense fibrous septae** | **60%** |
| **Botryoid** ("bunch of grapes") | Infants, young children · **vagina** | — | **<30%** ("uniformly fatal") |
| **Pleomorphic** | **40–70 yr** | Multiple cell types, like other pleomorphic sarcomas | **25%** |

### Genetics — t(2;13) PAX3-FKHR

**Alveolar** — **t(2;13) → PAX3-FKHR** · **t(1;13) → PAX7-FKHR** (as named in OB8296; paired the other way round in some sources) · **high metastatic risk**

| Translocation | Tumour |
|---|---|
| **t(2;13)** | **Alveolar RMS** |
| t(11;22) EWS-FLI1 | Ewing |
| t(12;16) | Myxoid liposarcoma |
| t(16;17) | ABC |
| t(9;22) | CML |
| t(X;18) | Synovial sarcoma |
| GNAS · IDH1 | FD · Maffucci/Ollier |
| p53 · RB | Osteosarcoma |

### Genes & lab markers

**IHC** — **MyoD1 +**, **myoglobin +**, **myosin +**, **desmin +**, **vimentin +**

## 3 Presentation

**Rapidly growing painless mass** · ± **enlarged regional nodes** (e.g. axillary)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**MRI** — non-diagnostic but needed for planning · **dark T1, bright T2** (like other STS)

**CT chest** — required for staging

**Bone marrow biopsy** — **required** for staging · marrow mets → worse prognosis

**Nodes** — **sentinel node biopsy** (esp. if nodes enlarged)

### Lymph-node-metastasizing sarcomas

**"SCARE"** — **synovial sarcoma, clear-cell sarcoma, angiosarcoma, rhabdomyosarcoma, epithelioid sarcoma** \
✗ Wrong options: Ewing · myxoid liposarcoma

### Small round blue cell differential

**RMS · neuroblastoma · lymphoma · leukaemia · Ewing · metastatic disease**

## 5 Management

```
Rhabdomyosarcoma
├─ Paediatric → WIDE EXCISION + CHEMO (+ RT if no radical margin)
│      (vincristine, dactinomycin, cyclophosphamide)
├─ Adult pleomorphic → WIDE EXCISION + RT (chemo not effective in adults)
├─ Unresectable / close or positive margins → RT
└─ Widespread metastases (selected) → CHEMO ALONE
```

**Teenager, isolated limb RMS** — **wide resection + chemo + RT** · chemo has a **substantial survival benefit** · amputation rarely (only if better function) \
✗ Wrong options: observation · marginal excision

## How it's asked

**"True of rhabdomyosarcoma?"** → **commonest soft-tissue sarcoma in a child**\
✗ Wrong options: commonest in foot · calcifies · Maffucci

**"Teenager, lower-limb RMS — management?"** → **wide resection + chemo + RT**\
✗ Wrong options: hemipelvectomy · marginal

**"Alveolar RMS — genetic abnormality?"** → **PAX3-FKHR**\
✗ Wrong options: EWS-FLI1 · p53 · RB

**"9-yr-old, rapid painless arm mass, axillary nodes?"** → **t(2;13)**\
✗ Wrong options: t(12;16) · GNAS · t(11;22) · IDH1

**"Translocation of a sarcoma with lymphatic mets?"** → **t(2;13)**\
✗ Wrong options: t(16;17) (ABC) · t(9;22) (CML)
