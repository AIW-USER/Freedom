# Adamantinoma

*Pathology · 1 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rare** — <300 cases documented · **<1% of malignant primary bone tumours**

**Age** — young adults **20–40 yr** · 75% in 2nd–4th decades

**Site** — **mid-tibial diaphysis, anterior cortex** (tibia **80–90%**) · synchronous ipsilateral **fibula 10–50%** · other long bones rare

**Nature** — **low-grade malignant** · aetiology unknown · arises from **aberrant epithelial nests** (TO)

**TO profile** — tibia, **3rd decade**, **males** (TO)

### Osteofibrous dysplasia association

**OFD link** — controversial · once thought **precursor** → now doubted · some regard as a **continuum**

**Why it matters** — OFD **benign → observe** · adamantinoma **malignant → wide resection**

### Genes & lab markers

**Cytokeratin (keratin) +** — epithelial nests stain positive · marks epithelial differentiation

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **pain of months to years** · indolent course

**Signs** — **anterior tibial bowing** or **palpable firm mass** · no soft-tissue mass, no lymphadenopathy

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **multiple sharply circumscribed lucencies ("soap bubble")** with interspersed sclerosis · **eccentric, intracortical, lytic** · anterior mid-tibia

![Soap-bubble lytic lesions, anterior tibial cortex](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8048-case-b-tibia-xray-parsons-moved.png)

**Evolution** — serial films show growth and **erosion through the cortex** → favours adamantinoma over OFD

**Periosteal reaction** — typically **none** (unlike other primary bone tumours)

**MRI** — cortical erosion / destruction of anterior tibial cortex

### Histology

**Biphasic** — **epithelial + fibrous mesenchymal** cells

**Epithelial nests** — **palisading or glandular** pattern · basaloid, spindle, tubular, squamous patterns (basaloid + tubular commonest) · keratin +

**Stroma** — background **fibrous (spindle-cell) stroma** · may have nuclear atypia, mitoses

![Epithelial nests in fibrous stroma](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8048-adamantinoma-histo.jpg)

### Histology — which pattern is which?

| Histology | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| **Biphasic glandular epithelial cells + spindle cells** | ✓ **Adamantinoma** |
| Woven trabeculae **with osteoblastic rimming**, fibrous stroma ("Chinese letters") | Osteofibrous dysplasia |
| Trabeculae **without osteoblastic rimming** in fibrous stroma | Fibrous dysplasia |
| Multinucleated giant cells, stromal nuclei = giant-cell nuclei | Giant cell tumour |
| **Herringbone** pattern, pleomorphic cells | Fibrosarcoma |

### Differential of a tibial diaphyseal lesion

| | Tibial diaphysis | Wide resection alone |
|---|---|---|
| **Adamantinoma** | Yes | Yes |
| Osteofibrous dysplasia | Yes | No (observe) |
| Chondrosarcoma (conventional) | — | Yes |
| Parosteal osteosarcoma | — | Yes |

**Ewing** — medullary, aggressive periosteal reaction, small round blue cells

**Chondromyxoid fibroma** — anterior **proximal** tibia, **medullary**, thins cortex

**Osteoid osteoma** — cortical, **<1 cm**, sclerotic rim

## 5 Management

### Treatment — wide resection

```
Anterior tibial cortical lytic lesion
├─ Child, benign trabeculae with rimming (OFD) → OBSERVE
│      └─ Bowing / impending fracture → BRACE
└─ Adult, epithelial nests (adamantinoma) → WIDE EN BLOC RESECTION
       └─ Failed resection/reconstruction → AMPUTATION (salvage)
```

**Wide-margin en bloc resection** — standard of care · prevents recurrence + metastasis

**Reconstruction** — **intercalary** resection → **allograft** or intercalary megaprosthesis · amputation usually unnecessary

**Biological reconstruction preferred (TO)** — **intercalary resection + vascularised fibula** transfer

**No chemo / no radiotherapy** — low-grade, not effective for local control

**Not indicated** — observation · curettage + graft · external beam radiation · chemo + excision

## 6 Complications

**Metastasis** — **lungs ~25%** (Orthobullets) · up to **20%** (OB explanation) · **late** → long-term follow-up

**Local recurrence** — **high unless wide margin** · uncommon after negative-margin excision

## X vs Y

| | **Adamantinoma** | **Osteofibrous dysplasia** |
|---|---|---|
| Age | **Adult 20–40** | **Child** (<10) |
| Behaviour | **Low-grade malignant** | Benign |
| Imaging | Cortical, may **erode through cortex**, progresses | Eccentric intracortical lytic, anterior bowing |
| Histology | **Epithelial nests (keratin +)** in fibrous stroma | Woven bone **with osteoblastic rimming** · no epithelial nests |
| Treatment | **Wide resection** | **Observe** · brace if bowing · surgery delayed to maturity (recurs) |

## How it's asked

**"Adult, anterior mid-tibial soap-bubble cortical lesion, epithelial nests in fibrous stroma — diagnosis?"** → **adamantinoma**\
✗ Wrong options: OFD · osteomyelitis · periosteal OS (no osteoid) · fibrous dysplasia

**"Histology seen only in adamantinoma, not OFD?"** → **biphasic glandular epithelial cells surrounded by spindle cells**

**"Best treatment?"** → **wide en bloc resection + reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: observation · bracing · chemo/radiation · AKA first

**"Epithelial nests stain for?"** → **cytokeratin**

**"Site of metastasis?"** → **lungs (~25%)** · late → long follow-up
