# Neurilemmoma

*Pathology · 41 / 66*

## 1 Basics

**Synonym** — **schwannoma** · benign, **encapsulated** nerve sheath tumour of **Schwann cells** on the **surface (epineurium) of a peripheral nerve** · motor or sensory nerves

**Age / sex** — **3rd–6th decades** (tested ages 40, 45; 20–50) · M = F

**Site** — **flexor surfaces** of extremities, head and neck · large ones in pelvis · tibial nerve (posterior ankle, tarsal tunnel), median, superficial peroneal

**Malignant transformation** — **extremely rare** (vs neurofibroma)

### Genes & lab markers

**NF2** gene mutations\
✗ Wrong options: NF1 (neurofibroma)

**S-100** — **strong, uniform/diffuse** staining

## 3 Presentation

**Usually asymptomatic** · slow-growing mass · **paraesthesia** in nerve distribution · discomfort with shoe wear

**Percussion (Tinel) sign** — **radiating pain distally on tapping** = most reliable sign · sensory deficit possible, **motor weakness rare** · **rest pain = worrying for MPNST**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**MRI** — **T1 dark**, **T2 bright**, **diffuse gadolinium enhancement** · fusiform, homogeneous, in continuity with a named nerve · **eccentric** to nerve

**String sign** — nerve tapering into tumour above and below · **target sign** — central low signal

![String sign of a schwannoma](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8053-string-sign.jpg)

**Benign vs MPNST on MRI** — **well-defined capsule** favours benign\
✗ Wrong options: >2 cm peritumoral oedema · lack of enhancement · ≥2 deep lobulations · limb location irrelevant

**Hard to separate** from neurofibroma on MRI

### Histology

**Gross** — grey, **well encapsulated** in nerve sheath

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Antoni A** | **Dense, cellular** spindle cells in intersecting bundles ("spindle cell battle formation") |
| **Antoni B** | **Hypocellular, loose myxoid** areas |
| **Verocay bodies** | **Two rows of palisading nuclei** — **pathognomonic** |

![Verocay bodies — palisading nuclei](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8053-verocay-histo.jpg)

### Differential

| | **Schwannoma** | **Neurofibroma** | **MPNST** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relation to nerve | **Eccentric**, on surface, encapsulated | **Within** nerve, fusiform | From nerve/neurofibroma |
| Gene | **NF2** | **NF1** | NF1 |
| Histology | Antoni A/B, Verocay | Wavy nuclei, mast cells | Pleomorphic spindle cells |
| Excision | **Marginal, shell out, nerve preserved** | Requires nerve sacrifice | Wide + nerve |
| Recurrence / malignancy | Rare / extremely rare | Higher / can transform | — |

**Others** — ganglion (mucin-filled synovial-lined sac, near joint) · nodular fasciitis (deep fascia) · lipoma (bright T1 + T2) · haemangioma · synovial sarcoma (periarticular, heterogeneous)

## 5 Management

### Treatment — marginal excision

**Observation** — asymptomatic

**Marginal (intracapsular) excision** — symptomatic / affecting QoL · **excise parallel to nerve fascicles** → tumour extrudes, nerve preserved · recurrence **rare (<1%)** · small risk of sensory deficit

**Not indicated** — wide resection / nerve reconstruction · radiation · chemotherapy · RFA

### Fibrolipomatous hamartoma

**Intraneural lipoma** — congenital, benign · **median nerve** commonest · MRI: **"coaxial cable"** (axial) / **"spaghetti"** (longitudinal) — fat between thickened fascicles (T1 bright) · asymptomatic → observe · symptomatic → **carpal tunnel decompression ± epineurolysis**

## How it's asked

**"Posterior ankle mass, percussion radiates into foot — true?"** → **Verocay bodies on histology**\
✗ Wrong options: NF1 · mucin sac (ganglion) · MPNST transformation · high recurrence

**"Tender shoulder mass, Antoni A/B, uniform S-100?"** → **schwannoma**\
✗ Wrong options: MFH · lymphoma · synovial sarcoma · neurofibroma

**"String + target signs, palisading nuclei?"** → **schwannoma**\
✗ Wrong options: nodular fasciitis · lipoma · haemangioma

**"Medial ankle mass, Tinel over tarsal tunnel?"** → **neurilemmoma**\
✗ Wrong options: neurofibroma · ganglion

**"MRI feature favouring benign nerve sheath tumour?"** → **well-defined capsule**

**"Symptomatic arm schwannoma — treatment?"** → **marginal resection only**\
✗ Wrong options: + RT · wide + chemo · RFA

**"Which patient needs only marginal resection?"** → **62-yr-old with nerve-sheath mass + Tinel sign**\
✗ Wrong options: Ewing · OS · liposarcoma · impending fracture

**"Median-nerve schwannoma, frozen section Verocay bodies?"** → **marginal resection parallel to fascicles**

**"Superficial peroneal schwannoma with paraesthesia?"** → **marginal excision**\
✗ Wrong options: observation · wide resection

**"Fibrolipomatous hamartoma — commonest nerve?"** → **median**
