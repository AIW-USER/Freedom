# Synovial Chondromatosis

*Pathology · 61 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Diagnosis — benign metaplasia

**Nature** — **benign metaplastic** proliferation of synovium → **cartilage metaplasia** → **multiple intra-articular loose bodies** (soft synovial tissue → firm cartilage nodules → calcify → ossify) \
✗ Wrong options: neoplasia · reactive arthritis · OA · vascular

**Metaplasia** — one non-cancerous phenotype → another (cf. Barrett's oesophagus — but Barrett's has high malignant risk)

**Demographics** — **30–50 yr** (3rd–5th decades) · **M:F 2:1**

**Site** — **knee** commonest: **70%** (OB219396) · **50–65%** (TO) · then **hip (20%)**, shoulder, elbow, ankle, wrist · also tendon sheaths, **bursae** (e.g. over an osteochondroma) · not purely intra-articular

**Genetics** — occasional **chromosome 6** abnormalities

### Primary vs secondary (TO)

| | **Primary (Reichel syndrome)** | **Secondary** |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | Less common | **More common** |
| Joint | One large joint, no underlying pathology | Knee/hip/shoulder with OA, trauma, inflammation, osteonecrosis, neuropathy |
| Age · sex | 3rd–5th decade · **M >2:1** | Older |
| Bodies | Many, uniform · **"ring and arc"** calcification | **Fewer, larger, variable size** · **concentric rings** |
| Origin | Synovial metaplasia | Shed hyaline cartilage implants in synovium |

### Phases (TO)

| Phase | Pathology |
|---|---|
| **1** | Active synovial cartilage formation, **no loose bodies** |
| **2** | Synovial proliferation **+ shedding** of loose bodies (commonest MRI pattern) |
| **3** | Many loose bodies, **no active synovial disease** |

## 3 Presentation

**Slow** pain + swelling (worse with activity) · **mechanical** symptoms — catching, locking, **stiffness** · ↓ ROM (e.g. 0–40°), crepitus, effusion, quadriceps atrophy · ± warmth, erythema

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (AP + lateral)** — **multiple stippled / spheroidal calcified loose bodies** · **ring-and-arc** chondroid calcification (pathognomonic) · extrinsic bone erosion possible · **20% radiographically occult** (mineralization is time-dependent)

![Multiple calcified masses about the knee](../../MCQ/attachments/q7782-a-194a.jpg)

**MRI** — early disease: **cartilage nodules only on MRI** · lobular · nodules **low T1, high T2** · later **signal drop-out** (calcification)

**Arthroscopy** — finds **far more nodules than the X-ray shows** (unmineralized)

### Histology

**Discrete hyaline cartilage nodules** at various stages of calcification/ossification · **clustered** chondrocytes with **mild atypia**, binucleate cells, occasional mitoses

### Differential

| Lesion | Key distinction |
|---|---|
| **PVNS** | **No mineralization**, bloody effusions, low T1+T2, blooming · CSF1 |
| **Synovial sarcoma** (periarticular) | Rarely intra-articular, diffuse mineralization, no loose bodies |
| Synovial haemangioma | Diffuse mineralization (phleboliths), no loose bodies |
| Lipoma arborescens | No calcification |
| OA loose bodies | **Fewer, smaller**, mobile |
| Gout | Urate crystals, erosions, no calcified bodies |
| Septic arthritis | Acute, no cartilage on biopsy |
| **Chondroblastoma** | **Osseous**, cobblestone chondroblasts |

## 5 Management

### Treatment & recurrence

```
Synovial chondromatosis
├─ Mild symptoms, ROM preserved → OBSERVATION
└─ Pain, ↓ ROM, ↓ function → LOOSE BODY REMOVAL + SYNOVECTOMY
       └─ Arthroscopic (preferred — TO) or open (similar outcomes)
```

**Benign metaplasia** → **symptomatic treatment only**\
✗ Wrong options: wide excision · chemo · disarticulation

**Hip, young adult, joint space preserved** — **arthroscopic loose body removal** (+ synovectomy, chondroplasty, labral debridement)\
✗ Wrong options: THA · resurfacing · 600 cGy RT

**Knee** — "open, complete synovectomy" (OB7782) · complete synovectomy role **debated** (OB219396) · may prevent degenerative change

**Recurrence** — **up to 30%** (OB219396) · **3–23%** overall, arthroscopic hip **7.1–25%** (OB220967) · due to **incomplete synovial removal** · not joint-specific or predicted by ring-and-arc

## 6 Complications

### Malignant transformation (synovial chondrosarcoma)

**Incidence** — **1%** to **6.4%** (TO) · **~5%** (OB) · long-standing disease with **multiple recurrences**

**Grade** — mostly low (grade 1: 20%, grade 2: 70%)

| Feature | Recurrence | Malignancy |
|---|---|---|
| Extensive calcified mass in/around joint | Yes | Yes |
| Soft-tissue / bursal extension | Yes | Yes |
| Deep **extrinsic bone erosion** | Yes | Yes |
| **Cortical destruction + marrow invasion / permeation** | No | **✓ (most reliable)** |
| Rapid growth, rapid clinical decline | — | Suspicious → biopsy |
| **Lung mets** | No | Definitive |

**Histology of malignancy** — marked **myxoid** matrix · **sheet-like / "lining up"** chondrocytes (loss of clustering) · **spindling** · necrosis · marrow permeation · moderate–marked atypia

**Untreated** — articular destruction, chronic pain

## How it's asked

**"45-yr-old, knee trauma, nodules on X-ray, far more loose bodies at arthroscopy?"** → **synovial chondromatosis** (key absent in source; ✗ Wrong options: PVNS · OCD · OA) **(TO)**

**"47-yr-old groin pain, hip loose bodies, cartilage nodules — true statement?"** → **benign metaplastic process, symptomatic treatment**\
✗ Wrong options: wide excision · neoplastic

**"30-yr-old painful ankle, calcified loose bodies?"** → **synovial chondromatosis**\
✗ Wrong options: PVNS · lipoma arborescens · haemangioma · synovial sarcoma

**"55-yr-old swollen knee, multiple calcified masses, benign chondroid biopsy?"** → **synovial chondromatosis**\
✗ Wrong options: OA loose bodies · chondroblastoma

**"Stiff hip, arthroscopy or open debridement — underlying process?"** → **metaplasia**

**"Knee ROM 0–40°, calcified bodies — true?"** → **benign metaplastic process**\
✗ Wrong options: hip commonest · purely intra-articular · CSF1 · urate

**"Best feature separating recurrence from malignant change?"** → **marrow invasion** **(TO)**\
✗ Wrong options: soft-tissue extension · extrinsic erosion · calcified mass

**"Painful hip chondromatosis, young adult?"** → **arthroscopic loose body removal**\
✗ Wrong options: THA · 600 cGy

**"Recurrence 3 yr after arthroscopic partial synovectomy?"** → **recognized, from incomplete synovial removal**\
✗ Wrong options: pathognomonic of chondrosarcoma · hip >50%
