# Osteochondroma & Multiple Hereditary Exostosis

*Pathology · 47 / 66*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Commonest benign bone tumour (20–50%)** · commonest benign cartilage tumour · tested ages 9–24 (adolescents, young adults)

**Site** — **bone surface**, metaphysis, often at **tendon insertions** · **knee** (distal femur > proximal tibia) · proximal femur, proximal humerus · **femur 30%**, tibia + humerus 10–20% each · **2nd commonest site: pelvis (iliac crest)** **(TO)** · spine rare (posterior elements of **cervical** spine) · **never facial bones** (intramembranous) · subungual exostosis (hallux)

**Polyostotic conditions (TO)** — FD (McCune-Albright), **MHE** ("diaphyseal aclasis"), enchondroma (Ollier/Maffucci) \
✗ Wrong options: **haemangioma** (non-polyostotic)

### Pathophysiology

**Hamartomatous** bone + cartilage · physeal cartilage escapes through a **perichondral ring (node/zone of Ranvier) defect** and grows by endochondral ossification under the periosteum → **grows away from the joint**

**Secondary causes** — Salter-Harris fracture · surgery · **radiation** (**commonest benign radiation-induced bone tumour**, 6–24%, latency 3–17 yr)

### MHE genetics (EXT1/EXT2, heparan sulfate, AD)

**Inheritance** — **autosomal dominant** · penetrance **96% females, 100% males** (>95%) · child of affected parent ≥50% risk · ~15% of osteochondroma patients

**Genes** — **EXT1 (8q24), EXT2 (11p13)**, EXT3 (19) — **tumour suppressors** (exostosins) → **↓ heparan sulfate** in physeal chondrocytes · affect **prehypertrophic chondrocytes** · dysregulated **Indian hedgehog** signalling

**EXT1 vs EXT2** — EXT1 **more severe**: more exostoses, more malalignment, shorter limbs/height, less forearm + knee ROM, more pelvic/flat-bone lesions, **higher chondrosarcoma rate**\
✗ Wrong options: lower

| Gene | Condition (distractors) |
|---|---|
| **EXT1/2/3** | ✓ **MHE** |
| PHEX | X-linked hypophosphataemic rickets |
| PMP-22 | Charcot-Marie-Tooth |
| SYT-SSX | Synovial sarcoma |
| GNAS | FD / McCune-Albright |
| IDH1 | Ollier / Maffucci |
| DTDST | Diastrophic dysplasia (cauliflower ear, hitchhiker thumb) — AR |
| FGFR2 · FGFR3 | Apert · achondroplasia |
| RUNX2/CBFA1 | Cleidocranial dysplasia |

**Other inheritance contrasts** — Ollier sporadic · fibular hemimelia none · sickle (infarcts) AR

## 3 Presentation

**Solitary** — **painless mass** · mechanical symptoms, irritation (bursitis), NV compression · grows until skeletal maturity

**MHE** — family history · multiple masses (knees, wrists, shoulders)

### Forearm & ankle deformity in MHE

**Deformities** — **short stature**, **LLD**, femoral shortening · **coxa valga** (lesser trochanter lesions) · **knee valgus** + patellar dislocation and **ankle valgus** (short fibula) · **ulnar shortening + radial bowing**, **ulnar deviation of wrist**, **radial head (radiocapitellar) dislocation** · pectoral/pelvic asymmetry · premature OA · no absent thumb

| Masada | Description | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Distal ulnar exostosis → short ulna + radial bowing, radial head NOT dislocated** | **Commonest (55–61%)** **(TO)** |
| **II** | Short ulna + **radial head dislocated** · IIa proximal radial exostosis · IIb no proximal radial exostosis | 22–33% |
| **III** | **Distal radial** exostosis → relative **radial shortening** | — |

**Masada treatment (TO)** — I: conservative / excise painful lesion · II: wait to ~13 yr, then levelling ± radial head resection or ring-fixator reduction · III: excise painful lesion, ulnar shortening for positive variance

**Forearm rotation loss** — **osteochondroma excision** most effective · deformity → excision + ulnar lengthening + radial closing-wedge osteotomy\
✗ Wrong options: osteotomies · radial head resection

**Ankle valgus in a 7-yr-old** — **medial (distal tibial) hemiepiphysiodesis**\
✗ Wrong options: full epiphysiodesis · cast · curettage · resection alone

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray (diagnostic)** — **sessile (broad) or pedunculated (stalk)** surface lesion · **pedunculated points away from the joint** (in both solitary and MHE) · **cortex continuous** with host · **medullary cavity continuous** with host (**pathognomonic**) · cartilage cap radiolucent, involutes at maturity

![Medullary continuity of osteochondroma on CT](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob8020-case-d-dist-tibia-ct-parsons-moved.png)

**Cartilage cap** — **MRI best** (T1 low, T2 high) · US and CT also measure it

| Cap thickness | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Child (immature) | **1–3 cm** normal — thick cap = growth, **not** malignancy |
| Adult (mature) | **2–3 mm** normal |
| **Adult >2 cm** (≥2 cm) | **Secondary chondrosarcoma** concern (Orthobullets, TO) · OB variants: **>1.5 cm**, ">1–2 cm", ">2–3 cm" · **thickening** in adulthood (✗ Wrong options: thinning) |

**Biopsy** — not recommended for diagnosis\
✗ Wrong options: needle biopsy · bone scan · PET

### Histology

**Like a normal physis** — **hyaline cartilage cap** + **well-defined perichondrium** + linear columns of active chondrocytes + normal primary trabeculae · stalk = cortical + cancellous bone

### Differential (bony bumps)

| Lesion | Key feature |
|---|---|
| **Osteochondroma** | **Cortical + medullary continuity** |
| Subungual exostosis (Dupuytren) | Dorsal distal phalanx (hallux), **no medullary continuity** |
| **Trevor disease** (dysplasia epiphysealis hemimelica) | **Epiphyseal** osteochondromas, lower limb, M:F 3:1, no malignancy |
| **Turret exostosis** | Dome on dorsal proximal/middle phalanx, post-traumatic, no continuity |
| **BPOP (Nora lesion)** | Hand phalanges/metacarpals, bone + cartilage + fibrous, high recurrence |
| **Parosteal OS** | Posterior distal femur, dense lobulated, no continuity |
| Juxtacortical chondroma | Saucerized cortex, 20–40 |
| Subperiosteal haematoma | Smooth elliptical, trauma |
| Myositis ossificans | Doesn't share cortex, zonation |

**"Benign bone tumour presenting as a bump"** → **osteochondroma** (turret exostosis + Nora lesion = non-tumours) **(TO)**

**Cartilage tumours (TO)** — chondroblastoma, osteochondroma, CMF all cartilage → "EXCEPT" = **none**

## 5 Management

### Treatment — observation vs marginal excision

```
Osteochondroma
├─ Asymptomatic / minimal (incl. MHE child) → OBSERVATION
├─ Symptomatic, cosmetic, mechanical → MARGINAL RESECTION at stalk base + whole cartilage cap
│      (delay to skeletal maturity if possible → ↓ recurrence)
├─ MHE deformity → excision, hemiepiphysiodesis, osteotomy
└─ Growth/pain after maturity, cap >2 cm → MRI/biopsy → secondary chondrosarcoma → WIDE RESECTION
```

**Marginal resection** — resect at **base of stalk including entire cartilage cap** (↓ regrowth) · graft/fix large defect \
✗ Wrong options: chemo · RT · curettage/liquid N₂ · radical resection

**No further tests** before excising a classic symptomatic lesion (MRI already done)

**Incidental humeral / femoral sessile lesion, asymptomatic** — **observation**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI, CT, bone scan, biopsy, long-leg films if alignment normal

**MHE child** — **observe** · excision for radial head dislocation, loss of rotation, symptoms\
✗ Wrong options: biopsy largest · PET · remove all · bisphosphonates

### Subungual exostosis

**Hallux**, adolescents · reactive metaplasia (microtrauma) · painful, may ulcerate · mistaken for wart · histology: mature trabecular bone capped by **fibrocartilage**

**Treatment** — **nail removal + excision / osteotomy through base**, local wound care (protect nail matrix) · recurrence possible\
✗ Wrong options: observation if painful · amputation · chemo/RT

## 6 Complications

### Malignant transformation to chondrosarcoma

| Setting | Risk |
|---|---|
| **Solitary osteochondroma** | **<1%** |
| **MHE** | **5–10%** (Orthobullets) · 3–10% · 0.57–8.3% · ~10% · "1 in 10,000" (one OB) — read options |

**Risk factors** — **pelvic/central (proximal) location** (highest), **sessile**, MHE, **EXT1**, **older age**, cap >2 cm in adult

**Secondary chondrosarcoma** — **low grade (~90%; 67–85%)** · 3rd–4th decade (tested 50) · **pelvis** commonest · <1% in children · presents as **new pain / growth after maturity** · pelvic mass → urinary retention, perineal numbness

**Treatment** — **wide resection** (same as chondrosarcoma), curative 70–90% · pelvic → multidisciplinary (general + plastic) \
✗ Wrong options: chemo · RT · local excision

### Other complications

**Vascular** — **popliteal artery pseudoaneurysm** (rare), compression, true aneurysm, arterial/venous thrombosis

**Nerve compression (up to 25%)** — sciatic, **common peroneal** (anterior + lateral compartment atrophy), radial

**Tendon** — shoulder: cuff impingement, subscapularis tear, bicipital tendinitis

**Bursa** formation (± metaplastic cartilage nodules) · **recurrence 2–5%** after resection

## How it's asked

**"HME — EXCEPT?"** → **"exostoses grow towards the physis"** (they grow away) (✓ AD, ✓ prehypertrophic chondrocytes, ✓ EXT1-3, ✓ medullary continuity)

**"MHE — EXCEPT?"** → **"grow towards joint in MHE but away in solitary"** (both grow away)

**"NOT associated with MHE?"** → **absence of the thumb**

**"EXT1 vs EXT2 — all EXCEPT?"** → **lower sarcomatous transformation** (EXT1 is higher)

**"AD gene defect with ↑ chondrosarcoma risk?"** → **EXT-1**\
✗ Wrong options: PHEX, PMP-22, SYT-SSX, GNAS-1

**"EXT-1 defect — diagnosis + tumour risk?"** → **MHE, chondrosarcoma**

**"X-ray for EXT1/2/3 mutation?"** → **multiple osteochondromas**\
✗ Wrong options: rickets · cleidocranial · NF bowing · Madelung

**"Which X-ray shows an AD disorder?"** → **MHE**\
✗ Wrong options: infarct/sickle AR · diastrophic AR · fibular hemimelia · Ollier sporadic

**"MHE with EXT — true?"** → **↓ heparan sulfate in physis**\
✗ Wrong options: cap thinning = malignancy · EXT2 worse · 25% malignancy · distal lesions riskier

**"Father with exostoses + hemiepiphysiodesis, child with bumps — gene?"** → **EXT1**

**"Incidental lesion — true in children?"** → **secondary transformation usually low grade, cured by surgery alone**\
✗ Wrong options: cap <1 mm · 20%

**"62-yr-old, enlarging lifelong medial thigh bump, multiple bumps?"** → **EXT; secondary low-grade chondrosarcoma**

**"MHE, pelvic grade 2 chondrosarcoma with urinary retention?"** → **wide resection with multidisciplinary team**

**"Highest risk of malignant transformation?"** → **pelvic location**\
✗ Wrong options: sessile · isolated · young age · 2 mm cap

**"Non-polyostotic tumour?"** → **haemangioma** **(TO)**

**"2nd commonest site of osteochondroma?"** → **pelvis** **(TO)**

**"Benign tumour presenting as bump on long bones?"** → **osteochondroma** **(TO)**

**"Sessile medial distal femoral lesion sharing cortex + medulla?"** → **osteochondroma**\
✗ Wrong options: myositis ossificans · parosteal OS

**"Thoracic transverse-process lesion on trauma CT — aetiology?"** → **aberrant cartilaginous growth**

**"Symptomatic pedunculated tibial osteochondroma?"** → **marginal resection**\
✗ Wrong options: chemo · radical · curettage + liquid N₂

**"14-yr-old, sessile anterior femoral exostosis, painless?"** → **observation**

**"Painful, cosmetic distal femoral osteochondroma, skeletally mature?"** → **marginal resection with complete cap removal**

**"Tests before resecting classic symptomatic lesion?"** → **none**

**"7-yr-old with multiple exostoses?"** → **observation**

**"Most common forearm deformity in HME?"** → **ulnar exostosis with radial bowing (Masada I)** **(TO)**

**"Limited pronation/supination in MHE?"** → **osteochondroma excision**

**"7-yr-old, ankle valgus from fibular exostosis?"** → **medial hemiepiphysiodesis**

**"Painful hallux lesion misdiagnosed as wart?"** → **nail removal + excision** / **osteotomy through base**
