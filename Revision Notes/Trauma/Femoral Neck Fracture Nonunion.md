# Femoral Neck Fracture Nonunion

*Trauma · 20 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rate** — **10–30% after femoral neck ORIF**

| Risk factor | Note |
|---|---|
| **Vertical fracture (Pauwels III)** | Shear > compression |
| Significant initial displacement, **posterior comminution** | — |
| **Malreduction, esp. varus** | (✗ Wrong options: valgus) |
| **Older age** | — |
| **Delay to fixation > 24 h** | — |

![Pauwels classification](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12293-pauwels-classification.jpg)

### Why the neck fails to heal

**Intracapsular** — **no periosteum / extraosseous supply**, **bathed in synovial fluid**, **intra-osseous healing only (no callus)** → anatomic reduction essential

### Anatomy

**Neck-shaft angle** — **130 ± 7°** · **anteversion 10 ± 7°** · tensile + compressive trabeculae

**Blood supply** — extracapsular ring → **ascending cervical (retinacular) arteries**; **MFCA (lateral epiphyseal) = main adult supply**; LFCA anterior ring; gluteal small; **ligamentum teres insignificant**

**Load** — single-leg stance force **parallel to neck**, **4× body weight**

**Associated** — **AVN**, malunion, hardware failure, **septic nonunion**, low BMD

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — persistent groin / buttock pain, pain on WB + hip extension, referred to medial thigh / knee

**Exam** — **↓ internal rotation**, antalgic gait, **pain with log-roll**, pain on straight-leg raise; check old incision, sciatic + femoral nerves

**Nonunion signal** — **persistent pain + no radiographic healing at 3 months** → consider revision

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use |
|---|---|
| AP pelvis + hip, **cross-table lateral**, full femur | Lucent line, **settling**, **hardware backing out**, head perforation, **> 10 mm change in position**, collapse, calcar comminution, **varus** |
| **CT** | **Most definitive diagnosis**; planning |
| MRI | **Head AVN** |
| Bone scan | Uptake at nonunion; helps exclude AVN |

**Infection work-up** — **CBC, ESR, CRP** → aspirate if raised → intra-op frozen section → **staged** if infected

### Classification (descriptive)

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **1** | Inadequate fixation / non-anatomic reduction |
| **2** | Loss of fixation with displacement |
| **3** | Fibrous nonunion, no displacement, intact fixation |

## 5 Management

```
Femoral neck nonunion
├─ Young, viable head → valgus intertrochanteric osteotomy
│      └─ aligned but posterior bone loss → vascularised graft
├─ Older / active → THA (long stem past screw holes)
├─ Older / low demand / cognitive decline → hemiarthroplasty
└─ Non-ambulatory, comorbid → observe
```

### Treatment options

| Option | Indication | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Observation | Non-ambulatory, comorbid | Poor function |
| **Revision ORIF** | **Not recommended** | Bone loss, LLD, high failure |
| **Valgus intertrochanteric osteotomy** | **Young**, viable head, varus collapse, nonunion > 3 mo | **Commonest salvage**; turns **vertical → \~30° (horizontal)** line → **compression**; restores length + abductor lever; **80–90% union**; not with bone loss, incongruity, **age > 65 (relative)** |
| **Vascularised graft** (quadratus femoris pedicle, free fibula) | Aligned nonunion with **posterior bone loss**, failed fixation | Up to 90% union; not if malalignment is the cause |
| **Hemiarthroplasty** | **Low demand, comorbid, cognitive decline** | **Lower dislocation than THA**; **cemented > uncemented**; bipolar ≈ unipolar; risk of acetabular erosion |
| **THA** | **Older, active, cognitively intact**; acetabular degeneration | **\~90% 10-yr, 75% 20-yr survival** |
| Arthrodesis | Young, non-viable head, heavy labourer | **20–35° flexion, 0–5° adduction, 5–10° ER**; arthroplasty now favoured |

![Valgus intertrochanteric osteotomy](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12293-valgus-it-osteotomy-xr.jpg)

**26-yo nonunion after screws at 3 mo** — **valgus intertrochanteric osteotomy**\
✗ Wrong options: graft + revision ORIF · hemiarthroplasty · DHS without correction · core decompression

**76-yo community ambulator** — **THA gives best long-term outcome**\
✗ Wrong options: valgus malreduction causes failure (varus does) · revision ORIF + inverted triangle · valgus osteotomy · hemi ↑ dislocation (hemi is lower)

**Healed fracture but AVN collapse + screw cut-out into acetabulum (62-yo)** — **THA with long cementless stem** to bypass screw-hole stress risers\
✗ Wrong options: primary tapered stem · hardware removal · valgus osteotomy · IM revision

### Technique points

**Valgus osteotomy** — lateral approach; **blade plate or sliding hip screw**; risks: malrotation, **over-medialisation of shaft → ↓ offset + abductor weakness** (longer blade avoids)

**Conversion THA** — **longer stems** past screw holes (intra-op fracture risk), gentle cup impaction ± screws, **dual-mobility / large heads** vs dislocation; loosening ↑ if **age < 65 + BMI > 30**

**Dual mobility vs fixed bearing (< 55 yr)** — **lower dislocation** (same HHS, loosening, infection, op time)

**Failed hemi → THA** — worse than primary THA

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **AVN** | **Up to 40%** with nonunion; \~15% symptomatic; risk: displacement, poor reduction → arthroplasty |
| Acetabular cartilage wear | → THA |
| **Trendelenburg gait** | Lost offset / abductor damage |
| **Prosthetic dislocation** | **10–20% in salvage THA** vs < 10% primary; abductor insufficiency, posterior approach |

## X vs Y

| | **Valgus IT osteotomy** | **THA** |
|---|---|---|
| Patient | **Young**, viable head | **Older**, active |
| Mechanism | Shear → compression | Replace joint |
| Result | 80–90% union | \~90% 10-yr survival |

## How it's asked

**"26-yo, screws, 3-mo nonunion?"** → **valgus intertrochanteric osteotomy**

**"76-yo nonunion — true?"** → **THA gives best long-term outcome**

**"Healed but AVN collapse + screw cut-out?"** → **THA with long cementless stem**

**"Dual mobility vs fixed bearing?"** → **lower dislocation**

**"Most definitive imaging for nonunion?"** → **CT**

**"Malreduction most linked to failure?"** → **varus**
