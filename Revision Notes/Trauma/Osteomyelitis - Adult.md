# Osteomyelitis - Adult

*Trauma · 43 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & sites

| Host | Typical site |
|---|---|
| **Dialysis** | **Spine + ribs**; **sternoclavicular joint** |
| **IV drug use** | **Medial / lateral clavicle**; SC / AC joint (**Pseudomonas**) |
| Diabetes | Foot, decubitus ulcers |
| Adult haematogenous | **Vertebrae (commonest)** |

**Risk factors** — trauma / surgery, immunocompromise, IVDU, poor vascularity, DM, sickle cell, neuropathy, malnutrition

### Mechanism

| Route | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Haematogenous** | **Commonest in children**; **vertebrae in adults**; **S. aureus** |
| **Contiguous** | Surgery, trauma, wounds, **poor vascularity**; bacterial > mycobacterial / fungal |
| **Direct inoculation** | Penetrating, **open fracture**, surgical contamination |

### Organisms

| Age / setting | Organism |
|---|---|
| Neonate (< 4 mo) | S. aureus, Enterobacter, **GAS + GBS** |
| 4 mo–4 yr | S. aureus, GAS, **Kingella kingae**, Enterobacter |
| 4 yr–adult | **S. aureus (80%)**, GAS, H. influenzae |
| **Adult** | **S. aureus** ± Enterobacter / Strep |
| **Sickle cell** | **Salmonella** (S. aureus still commonest) |
| **IVDU (SC / AC joint)**, nail through rubber sole | **Pseudomonas** |
| HIV + cat scratch | **Bartonella** |
| Immunosuppressed, long IV lines, TPN | Fungal |
| Pott's disease | TB |

**Mycotic (infected) aneurysm (TO)** — **S. aureus** (+ Salmonella); "mycotic" ≠ fungal\
✗ Wrong options: H. influenzae · Strep viridans · Pseudomonas

### Biofilm & bacterial growth stages

| Stage | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Planktonic** | **Free-floating** bacteria **attach to inert substrate** + apoptosis → biofilm matrix |
| **Sessile** | **Quorum sensing** → matures; **no-growth** state; virulence genes; **antibiotic resistant** |
| Dispersion | Releases planktonic bacteria |

**Biofilm** — **extracellular polymeric substance / exopolysaccharide (glycocalyx)**; poor antibiotic penetration + ↓ metabolism · **PJI > 4 wk → explant**

### Metaphyseal osteomyelitis & joint spread

**Intracapsular metaphyses (TO)** — **proximal radius**, **proximal humerus**, **proximal femur**, **distal tibia / fibula** → septic arthritis · **NOT head of ulna**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain; **fever (acute)**; sepsis signs

**Draining sinus** — **chronic**; **probe to bone → chronic osteomyelitis** (diabetic ulcer probing bone = osteomyelitis, not Charcot — improves with elevation)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Radiographs** | Lag **\~2 wk**; need **50% bone loss**; chronic: **sequestrum** (dead bone, nidus), **involucrum** (new bone around), **Brodie abscess**, sclerosis, periosteal reaction |
| CT | Necrotic bone, planning (hardware artifact) |
| **MRI** | **Best for early diagnosis + localisation**; **negative rules out**; **may overestimate extent**; T2 oedema |
| **Penumbra sign (T1)** | **Bright rim** (vascularised membrane) around dark abscess, dark sclerotic outer rim → **intraosseous abscess → surgery** |
| **Tc bone scan** | Normal X-ray + no MRI; **sensitive, not specific**; negative rules out |
| **Gallium** | Diabetic foot / no MRI; **cellulitis false positive** |

![Penumbra sign on T1 MRI](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob1057-t1-sequence-showing-a-penumbra-sign-in-the-distal-femur.jpg)

### Genes & lab markers

| Test | Fact |
|---|---|
| WBC | **Raised in only ⅓** of acute |
| **ESR** | Raised **\~90%**; **fall = favourable** |
| **CRP** | **Most sensitive (97%)**; falls faster than ESR |
| Blood cultures | Often negative; guide haematogenous cases |
| **Sinus tract culture** | **Unreliable** (contaminants) |
| **Bone culture** | **Gold standard** for antibiotics (off antibiotics) |

**Histology** — **acute: live osteocytes + neutrophils** · **chronic: empty lacunae, marrow fibrosis, lymphocytes + plasma cells** (plasma cell = chronic)

**Most accurate diagnosis vs Ewing (TO)** — **bone biopsy** · **"biopsy all infections, culture all tumours"**\
✗ Wrong options: indium scan · MRI (next step) · LDH

### Classification

**Timing** — acute **< 2 wk** · subacute 1–several months · chronic > several months

| Cierny-Mader stage (anatomic) | Extent |
|---|---|
| **1** | **Medullary** |
| **2** | **Superficial (cortex only)** |
| **3** | **Localised** — cortex + medulla, **stable** |
| **4** | **Diffuse** — full thickness, **unstable** |

| Host | Status |
|---|---|
| **A** | Normal |
| **BL / BS** | **Locally / systemically compromised** (smoking, venous stasis) |
| **C** | **Treatment worse than infection** |

**Cierny-Mader uses** — **host status + extent of infected bone** · e.g. medullary, smoker + venous stasis → **1B**

## 5 Management

```
Adult osteomyelitis
├─ Acute → culture-directed IV antibiotics
│      └─ fails / abscess / subacute → I&D + antibiotics
├─ Chronic → debride to bleeding bone (paprika), remove sequestrum + non-essential hardware, manage dead space, stabilise
└─ Surgery not feasible → suppressive antibiotics · unsalvageable → amputation
```

### Chronic post-traumatic osteomyelitis — debridement, hardware removal & local antibiotics

**Principles** — remove **all necrotic tissue + sequestrum**; debride to **punctate bleeding ("paprika sign")**; **remove non-essential hardware**; **stability required** (**ex-fix preferred**); dead-space management; culture-directed antibiotics; often **staged**

**Dead space** — vascularised bone graft, local / free flaps, **PMMA antibiotic beads**, VAC, Masquelet, Ilizarov

**Draining sinus 18 months after GSW** — **surgical debridement + culture + IV antibiotics**\
✗ Wrong options: oral · needle biopsy + IV · tumour work-up

**Infected nonunion with hardware** — **I&D, remove hardware, over-ream canal, ex-fix + culture-directed antibiotics**\
✗ Wrong options: suppression · AKA · NPWT without stability · retain hardware + acute graft

**MRSA infected nonunion with nail + sinus** — **debride, excise sinus (rule out Marjolin), exchange to antibiotic-cement nail + 6 wk IV**\
✗ Wrong options: retain nail · larger nail · remove without stabilising · add ring fixator

**Localised, healed tibia, low-virulence S. aureus** — **curettage + absorbable antibiotic beads + IV 1–4 wk + oral 6 wk**\
✗ Wrong options: segmental resection · amputation · free flap

**Stable patient, multiple sinuses after forearm ORIF** — **scheduled (non-urgent) I&D + hardware removal**\
✗ Wrong options: suppression · sinus culture · MRI · urgent admission

**Antibiotic nail** — PMMA + **vancomycin + tobramycin**; **peak elution 24 h**; elutes **up to 4 months** · tobramycin concentration-dependent, vancomycin time-dependent

**Prognosis better** — **metaphyseal > diaphyseal** · worse: polymicrobial, **MRSA**, contralateral amputation, poor host

**Medical failure** — infection **> 3 months**, multidrug-resistant, polymicrobial\
✗ Wrong options: CRP > 20 · gram-positive · long bone

**Adjuncts** — **suppressive antibiotics** (high relapse when stopped) · **hyperbaric O₂** (refractory)

**Recurrence** — **\~30%** despite treatment

### Negative pressure dressing mechanism

**Benefits** — removes inhibitory interstitial fluid, protein + electrolytes; ↓ slime / anaerobic colonisation; arteriolar dilation → granulation; **↓ capillary afterload → ↑ inflow**; draws edges in; **VEGF release** · **EXCEPT "↑ capillary afterload"**

### Vertebral osteomyelitis & discitis

**Dialysis, back pain, ↑ ESR, MRI: disc T2 bright + T1 dark, endplate enhancement** — **osteomyelitis + discitis**\
✗ Wrong options: lymphoma · renal osteodystrophy · metastasis (disc spared) · osteoporotic fracture

**MSSA bacteraemia, L3–4, neuro intact** — **IV antibiotics + lumbar orthosis**\
✗ Wrong options: biopsy (organism known) · repeat MRI · surgery

### CRMO

**CRMO / SAPHO (TO)** — **associated with skin lesions** (palmoplantar pustulosis, pustular psoriasis); **no organism**; autoimmune; **antibiotics no role**

| Variant (TO) | Who | Site |
|---|---|---|
| **Sternocostoclavicular hyperostosis** | **Men 40–50** | Sternum, medial clavicles, spine |
| **Subacute recurrent multifocal** | **Children + adolescents** | Metaphyses (distal femur, tibia), medial clavicle |

## 6 Complications

### Marjolin ulcer (squamous cell carcinoma)

**Marjolin ulcer** — **SCC (\~90%)** in a **chronic draining sinus**; **\~1%** of chronic osteomyelitis; tibia > femur; growing tissue + **foul smell**

**Precursor** — **chronic osteomyelitis**\
✗ Wrong options: adamantinoma · OFD · EG · cortical desmoid

**Treatment** — **wide resection** (Orthobullets) · **amputation** usually (OB) — read the options

**Other** — persistence, sepsis, amputation

## X vs Y

| | **Sequestrum** | **Involucrum** |
|---|---|---|
| What | **Dead bone — nidus** | **New bone around it** |
| Treatment | **Remove** | — |

| | **Acute** | **Chronic** |
|---|---|---|
| Histology | **Neutrophils**, live osteocytes | **Plasma cells**, empty lacunae, fibrosis |
| Treatment | Antibiotics ± drainage | **Debridement** essential |
