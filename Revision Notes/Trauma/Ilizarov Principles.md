# Ilizarov Principles

*Trauma · TO notes*

## 1 Basics

### Concept

**Ilizarov apparatus** — **circular external fixator** (Gavriil Ilizarov, "magician of **Kurgan**") · mimics cylindrical bone → handles **tension, compression, bending, shear, torsion** (TO)

**Corrects** — tension (**distraction osteogenesis**) · compression (**nonunion**) · bending (angular deformity via **hinges**) · shear (axis deviation / translation) · torsion (**derotation**) (TO)

**Accordion technique** — **alternate compression + distraction** → stimulates regenerate in **nonunion** (TO)

**Indications** — nonunion · limb lengthening · deformity correction · bone defects (transport) · open fractures · congenital deformity · joint contracture (TO)

### Biological principles

**Tension-stress effect** — gradual steady traction → ↑ metabolic + biosynthetic activity → **new tissue formation** (TO)

**Piezoelectric effect** — elastic micromotion stimulates osteoblast **ion channels** (TO)

**Distraction histogenesis** — new bone + soft tissue **along the vector of applied force**; any age if healing potential persists (TO)

**Regenerate mechanism** — mechanical induction · **neovascularisation** · osteoprogenitor activation · **intramembranous ossification** (TO)

## 2 Mechanism

### Stability with micromotion

**Principle** — **controlled axial micromotion**, not rigidity; excess rigidity → **stress shielding + delayed union** (TO)

**Optimal micromotion** — **< 1 mm** under physiological load (TO)

| Micromotion type (TO) | Result |
|---|---|
| **Angular** | **"Horse-hoof" nonunion** |
| **Rotatory** | Hypertrophy |
| **Distraction / side-to-side (shear)** | **Atrophic nonunion** |
| **Dynamic axial compressive** | **Ideal for union** |

### Ilizarov vs other fixators

| Fixator (TO) | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Uniplanar** | Stable only in its own plane; **unstable at 90°**; **cantilever** bending → shear, ↓ axial motion |
| **Delta frame** | Very stiff, **abolishes axial micromotion** → "**nonunion machine**" |
| **Orthofix (monolateral)** | **Axially unstable by design**; stable in bending + rotation |
| **Ilizarov (circular)** | **Beam bending** (wire supported at both ends) → **16× less bending** under axial load; best bending, torsional + shear stability |

**Non-linear stiffness** — **low stiffness at physiological load, high stiffness when overloaded** (tensioned thin wires act as beams + cables) → **key advantage** (TO)

**Bicycle-spoke wires** — resist angular + rotational micromotion, allow axial compression / distraction (TO)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Regenerate types (TO)

| Regenerate (TO) | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Normotrophic** | Normal regenerate by **day 20** |
| **Hypertrophic** | Exuberant |
| **Hypotrophic** | Poor regenerate after **day 30** (scarring, poor vascularity) |

### Regenerate histology by week (TO)

| Week (TO) | Zone |
|---|---|
| **1** | **Fibrous interzone (FIZ)** — parallel collagen, **avascular centre**, acts as **pseudo-growth plate** |
| **2** | **Primary mineralisation front (PMF)** — osteoblast clusters, **intramembranous** ossification |
| **3** | **Microcolumn formation (MCF)** — columnar bone, sinusoidal vessels |
| End of distraction | FIZ ossifies → single MCF zone bridges gap |

**Healing index (TO)** — time from operation to full WB **per cm** of new bone (TO wording: months per cm)

### Pin-site infection – Checketts-Otterburn (TO)

| Grade (TO) | Features | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Mild erythema | Improve pin care |
| **2** | Erythema, discharge, pain | Topical / oral antibiotics |
| **3** | No improvement with grade 2 | **Remove pin** + change antibiotic |
| **4** | Soft-tissue infection, **several pins**, no bone change | Remove loose pins + antibiotics |
| **5** | **Radiological bone involvement** | **Remove whole fixator + curettage** |
| **6** | Infection **after fixator removal** | Debridement + systemic antibiotics |

## 5 Management

### Stages of treatment (TO)

**Sequence** — frame application → **corticotomy** → **latency** → **distraction** → **consolidation** → **dynamisation** → frame removal (TO)

| Phase (TO) | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Latency** | Average **5–7 days** · children **3–5 d** · adults **7–10 d** |
| **Distraction rate** | **1 mm/day as 0.25 mm × 4** · children up to **1.5 mm/day** · elderly / malnourished **0.5–0.75 mm/day** |
| **Consolidation (immobilisation)** | **2 × distraction period** |
| **Dynamisation** | **4–6 wk before removal** — loosen rod nuts → ↑ elasticity, cortical contact |
| **Removal – radiological** | **≥ 3 cortices** ossified + **recanalisation** |
| **Removal – clinical** | **Painless full WB** |

### Corticotomy (TO)

**Definition** — **low-energy subperiosteal** cut of cortex only → preserves **periosteal + endosteal (medullary)** supply; local flow ↑ **\~330%** (TO)

**Technique** — **0.5–1 cm** incision where bone is subcutaneous · **0.5 cm osteotome** · **no periosteal stripping** · **fan-shaped** cuts, avoid medulla · stop at dark fatty marrow blood · complete by **rotating osteotome / manual osteoclasis** (crack) (TO)

**Ideal cut** — **metaphyseal** · **long oblique** · no comminution · initial gap **< 2 mm** (TO)

**Confirm completion** — **AP + lateral X-ray after 5 mm distraction** (TO)

**Errors** — incomplete cut → **posterior oblique tibial fracture** · butterfly fragment · comminution if wires too close (TO)

**After proximal tibial corticotomy** — **external rotation** of distal fragment after fibular osteotomy → avoids **common peroneal nerve** stretch (TO)

| Level factor (TO) | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Anatomic** | Avoid **nutrient artery zone (mid-diaphysis)**; prefer **metaphysis / metadiaphysis** |
| **Biomechanical** | **6–7 cm from joint line** (preserve motion) |
| **Physiologic** | Avoid scars, infection, sclerosis, porosis, bone cysts |

| | **Monofocal** | **Bifocal** |
|---|---|---|
| Lengthening | **Up to 5 cm** | **10–12 cm** |
| Bone transport | 5–7 cm | **10–16 cm** |
| Use | Nonunion / pseudarthrosis stimulation, gradual deformity correction | Lengthening + deformity at 2 levels; **metabolic bone disease (Paget, OI, Ollier)** |

### Frame components (TO)

**Primary components (bone → frame)** — rings · wires (K-wire, **olive**) · wire-fixation bolts + buckles · Schanz pins · pin clamps (TO)

| Ring (TO) | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Standard ring** | **Perpendicular to bone axis** · holes **8 mm**, centre-to-centre 11 mm · bears wire tension up to 150 kg |
| **Half ring** | Joined in **handshake** position · removable intra-op · paediatric **80–140 mm**, adult **150–240 mm** |
| **Full ring** | **3 more holes** than paired half rings · cannot be detached · foot frames |
| **5/8 ring** | Around joints needing motion (proximal tibia, distal femur, elbow) · **weaker** → supplemental fixation |
| **Omega ring** | **Proximal humerus** (over deltoid) · weak |
| **Russian arch** | Older; **sciatic nerve injury** risk |
| **Italian arch** | **Slotted holes → half pins**; safer at proximal femur; uses **wide-body washer** |

**Ring size** — diameter = circumference ÷ π · **2 finger-breadths "above", 3 "below"** (anterior / posterior clearance) · or **bone diameter + 60 mm** (TO)

**Skin–ring distance** — **≥ 3 cm** (two finger-breadths) (TO)

**Rods** — **6 mm**, **pitch 1 mm** (all Ilizarov rods → predictable distraction) · 4 rods between 2 rings · telescopic rods stiffer · graduated telescopic rod (ASAMI) → **0.25 mm** steps (TO)

**Nuts** — full 6 mm (6 threads), ¾, half · **never reuse nuts or bolts** · **nylon (stopper) nut only for hinges** · paired spheric washers → 7.5–15° angulation (TO)

| Wire / pin (TO) | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Size** | Paediatric **1.5 mm** · adult **1.8 mm** · **2–3 wires per ring** |
| **Bayonet tip** | **Cancellous** (metaphysis, epiphysis) |
| **Trocar tip** | **Cortical** bone |
| **Olive (beaded) wire** | **Interfragmentary compression**, fragment translation, **internal transport**, **osteoporotic bone** |
| **Half pins (Schanz)** | 4.5, 5, 6 mm · better biomechanics than olive wires |

### Frame assembly & ring roles (TO)

| Ring (TO) | Role |
|---|---|
| **Main proximal supporting ring** | Stationary; strongest + widest metaphysis, **3–5 cm from joint line** |
| **Stabilising ring** | Distal; stationary or mobile |
| **Pusher–puller ring** | Moves for distraction / compression; **2–5 cm from corticotomy** |
| **Reference ring** | **Apex of deformity**; **no wires**; guides translation |

**Pre-assembled vs intra-operative frame** — pre-assembled saves time but needs adjustment · intra-op more accurate, longer (TO)

| Bone (TO) | Muscle pull | Ring correction |
|---|---|---|
| **Tibia** | Lateral muscles → **valgus** · posterior → **antecurvatum** | Tilt proximal ring **5°** in both planes |
| **Femur** | **Adductors → varus** · biceps → posterior | Arch **7–10° abduction + 5–7° anterior elevation** |
| **Humerus** | Coracobrachialis + triceps → **varus** | Omega ring **5–7° abduction** |

### Wire insertion & tension (TO)

**Insertion steps** — safe corridors · **1.5–2 cm** from NV bundles · **low speed (< 40 rpm), start-stop** · support skin · muscles at **maximal functional stretch** (avoid contracture) · **one wire, one hole** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: high-speed drilling (burns soft tissue, NV injury)

| Angle between wires (TO) | Stability |
|---|---|
| **< 30°** | Unstable → side-to-side displacement |
| **30–45°** | Risk of ring shearing |
| **> 45°** | Acceptable; **90° most stable** |

**Offset wires** — use washers / posts; **never bend wire down to ring** (TO)

**Capsular limits** — shoulder capsule below **surgical neck** · knee suprapatellar pouch **4–5 cm above patella** · elbow above coronoid / olecranon fossae · ankle above tendon grooves (TO)

| Wire tension (TO) | kg |
|---|---|
| Half rings | **50–70** |
| Offset wires | 50–80 |
| 2–3 wires per ring, young | **110** |
| 2–3 wires per ring, adult | **120–130** |
| Olive wires | 100–110 |
| 1.5 mm wire (tensioner) | **90–105** (≈ 50% of yield; failure \~212 kg) |
| 1.8 mm wire (tensioner) | **130–140** (failure \~305 kg) |

**Tension rule** — **50% of wire yield load**, with dynamometric tensioner (TO)

### Frame stability factors (TO)

| ↑ Stability (TO) | ↓ Stability |
|---|---|
| ↑ Pin / wire **diameter** | **↑ Pin–fracture distance** |
| ↑ Number of pins / wires | ↑ Bone–rod distance |
| ↑ **Pin spread** · ↑ planes | Wire crossing angle **< 30°** |
| ↑ Rod diameter / number | Fewer than 2 wires per ring |
