# Distal Radial Ulnar Joint (DRUJ) Injuries

*Trauma · 13 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Overview

**Why it matters** — DRUJ injury with distal radius fractures is a common, **under-appreciated cause of pain + lost rotation** → assess it **independently** after fixing the radius

**Associated** — **ulnar styloid / distal ulna fractures**, **TFCC tears**, **ulnar impaction**, **Essex-Lopresti**, **Galeazzi**

### Anatomy

**Joint** — ulnar head in **sigmoid notch** (shallow); motion = rotation + translation

**Most stable** — **supination**

**Primary stabilisers** — **volar + dorsal radioulnar ligaments** + **TFCC** (inserts at **fovea** at ulnar styloid base)

**Secondary / back-up primary** — **distal oblique bundle (DOB) of interosseous membrane** → can keep forearm stable when TFCC torn\
✗ Wrong options: annular ligament = PRUJ · central band (not DRUJ) · meniscal homologue · proximal oblique cord

**TFCC components** — central articular disc · meniscal homologue · volar + dorsal radioulnar ligaments · ulnolunate + ulnotriquetral origins · **ECU sheath floor**

**Ligament roles** — **pronation: volar ligaments block dorsal subluxation** · **supination: dorsal ligaments block volar subluxation**

## 2 Mechanism

**TFCC tear** — wrist **extension + forearm pronation**

## 3 Presentation

**Acute dislocation** — pain + instability (often with open distal radius fractures)

**Post-traumatic arthritis** — dorsal wrist pain, **↓ pronosupination**, snapping, crepitus, ↓ grip; pain on rotating with ulna compressed against radius

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| **AP** | **DRUJ widening** |
| **Lateral** | **Dorsal displacement** of ulnar head; instability = subluxed **full width** of sigmoid notch in neutral |
| **Dynamic CT** (neutral, full supination, full pronation, vs other side) | **> 50% translation = abnormal**; subtle chronic instability |
| **MRI** | **TFCC** tears |

![DRUJ instability on radiographs](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob1028-druj-instability.jpg)

### Palmer TFCC classification

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **I** | **Traumatic** |
| **II — degenerative (ulnocarpal impaction)** | **IIA** thinning · **IIB** + lunate / ulnar chondromalacia · **IIC** + perforation · **IID** + LT ligament tear · **IIE** + ulnocarpal + DRUJ arthritis |

## 5 Management

```
DRUJ after distal radius / radial shaft fixation
├─ Stable → no extra treatment (ulnar styloid ignored)
├─ Unstable, ligamentous → reduce + splint (dorsal → supination, volar → pronation) 4 wk
│      └─ highly unstable → pin 0.062 K-wires ± ligament repair
├─ Unstable + displaced styloid base / sigmoid notch / TFCC avulsion → ORIF / repair
└─ Irreducible after anatomic radius → ECU interposed → open removal
```

### Ligamentous instability

**Closed reduction + immobilise 4 wk** — **dorsal instability → supination** · **volar instability → pronation**

**Highly unstable** — **pin DRUJ with 0.062-inch K-wires** ± radioulnar ligament repair

**ECU interposition** — blocks reduction

### DRUJ instability after radius fixation – ulnar fixation & ECU

**Incongruent DRUJ after anatomic radial shaft fixation (Galeazzi)** — **interposed ECU** → extricate via dorsal approach\
✗ Wrong options: revise plating · IM nail · pronator quadratus · FCU

**Indication to fix distal ulna fracture** — **intra-operative DRUJ instability**\
✗ Wrong options: angulation, comminution, location, size alone

**Galeazzi** — distal-third radius fracture + DRUJ injury → **radial ORIF + DRUJ pinning** often; nonop (splint in supination) rarely

### Ulnar styloid fractures

**Meaning** — marker of **high initial displacement**; **base fractures → TFCC rupture + instability**

**Nonunion without instability** — **does not worsen outcome** (least likely to affect function)\
✗ Wrong options: DRUJ instability · step-off > 3 mm · radial shortening · workers' comp (all worsen)

**Operative** — **displaced base fracture + instability**, sigmoid notch fracture, Galeazzi, TFCC avulsion with unstable DRUJ → ORIF or excision **preserving TFCC ulnar attachment**

**Non-displaced, proximal** — cast

### TFCC tears

**Acute traumatic** — immobilisation + NSAIDs first → arthroscopic / open debridement or repair if persistent

**Degenerative (type II)** — debride TFCC + treat impaction: **ulnar shortening osteotomy** (no DRUJ arthrosis) or **wafer resection**

### Ulnar impaction syndrome

**Cause** — radial shortening → **positive ulnar variance** → **lunate chondromalacia + degenerative TFCC tear**

**Surgery** — TFCC debridement, radial osteotomy, **ulnar shortening**, **wafer (preserve TFCC attachment)**

### Forearm stabilisers – Essex-Lopresti

**Essex-Lopresti (longitudinal radioulnar dissociation)** — **radial head fracture + interosseous membrane tear + DRUJ disruption** → **proximal radial migration** → DRUJ pathology + ulnocarpal abutment

**Acute** — fix / replace radial head (restore radiocapitellar contact + length) + **pin DRUJ 6 wk in neutral**; failed pinning / missed → radial head replacement

**Chronic** — restore radial length, level DRUJ, **reconstruct central band of IOM**

**Ipsilateral radial head / neck + Galeazzi (functional Essex-Lopresti)** — treatment causing chronic instability = **radial head excision**\
✗ Wrong options: temporary ex-fix · radial head ORIF · arthroplasty · DR ORIF + cross-pinning

## 6 Complications

### DRUJ arthrosis – salvage

| Procedure | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **Darrach** (distal ulna excision) | **Low-demand elderly**; risk **unstable painful ulnar stump** |
| **Hemiresection-interposition** (Bowers) | Preserves **TFCC ulnar insertion**; soft tissue prevents impingement |
| **Sauvé-Kapandji** | **DRUJ fusion + proximal ulnar neck pseudarthrosis**; younger patients; keeps rotation |
| Ulnar head prosthesis | — |
| **One-bone forearm** | **Ultimate salvage**; **eliminates rotation** |

**Prevention** — **anatomic reduction of the distal radius** usually reduces the DRUJ

## X vs Y

| | **Darrach** | **Sauvé-Kapandji** |
|---|---|---|
| Patient | Elderly, low demand | Younger |
| What | Distal ulna excised | DRUJ fused + proximal pseudarthrosis |
| Risk | **Unstable ulnar stump** | Painful proximal stump |

| | **Dorsal DRUJ instability** | **Volar DRUJ instability** |
|---|---|---|
| Stable position | **Supination** | **Pronation** |

## How it's asked

**"DRUJ incongruent after anatomic radius fixation?"** → **reduce interposed ECU**

**"Indication for distal ulna fixation?"** → **intra-operative DRUJ instability**

**"Least likely to affect outcome after DR ORIF?"** → **ulnar styloid nonunion**

**"Treatment causing chronic instability in radial head + Galeazzi?"** → **radial head excision**

**"Gross forearm stability after radius fixation despite DRUJ injury — structure?"** → **distal oblique bundle**

**"Dynamic CT translation abnormal?"** → **> 50% vs contralateral**

**"Dorsal DRUJ dislocation — immobilise in?"** → **supination**

**"Essex-Lopresti — DRUJ?"** → **pin 6 wk in neutral**

**"Ultimate salvage for DRUJ?"** → **one-bone forearm**
