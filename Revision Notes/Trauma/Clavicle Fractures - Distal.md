# Clavicle Fractures - Distal

*Trauma · 8 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Clavicle fractures** — **2.6–4%** of adult fractures · **distal third = 10–25%** of clavicle fractures

**Who** — older / osteoporotic adults; less common in children (intact periosteal sleeve)

**Associated (rare)** — floating shoulder · **scapulothoracic dissociation** (if widely distracted) · rib fracture · PTX · NV injury

### Neer classification & coracoclavicular ligament anatomy

| Stabiliser | Function | Insertion from distal clavicle |
|---|---|---|
| **AC ligament** (sup, inf, ant, post) | **Anterior–posterior** stability; **superior strongest**, then posterior (posterosuperior most important) | **\~6 mm** |
| **Trapezoid (lateral)** | Vertical | **2–3 cm** (3 cm, OB); **trapezoid tubercle — anterolateral** |
| **Conoid (medial)** | **Most important for vertical stability** | **4–4.5 cm** (4.5 cm, OB); **conoid tubercle — posteromedial** |
| Dynamic | Deltoid + trapezius | — |

**✗ Conoid traps** — at 2–3 cm · anterolateral to trapezoid · trapezoid as main vertical restraint

**Displacement depends on** — **location** (extra- vs intra-articular), **pattern** (simple vs comminuted), **CC ligament integrity**, **age**

**CC ligaments** — conoid + trapezoid = primary resistance to **superior displacement of medial fragment**

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanism** — **direct compressive blow / fall onto point of shoulder** (as for midshaft)

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — anterior shoulder pain, swelling, AC deformity, **skin tenting** (impending open)

**Suprascapular nerve** — at risk → **weak ER with arm adducted**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| View | Use |
|---|---|
| **Upright AP of both shoulders** | Displacement vs normal side |
| Axillary lateral | AP displacement |
| **Zanca — 15° cephalic tilt** | Superior / inferior displacement; ± **5–10 lb weight** in hand |
| CT (3D) | Displacement, shortening, comminution, articular extension, nonunion |

**Superior displacement of medial clavicle relative to coracoid** — **CC (conoid) disruption**

### Neer classification

| Type | Fracture | CC ligaments | Stability | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Extra-articular, lateral** to CC | **Intact** | **Stable**, minimal displacement | Nonop |
| **IIA** | **Medial** to CC | **Both intact** (on distal fragment) | **Unstable**; **nonunion up to 56%** nonop | **ORIF** |
| **IIB** | (1) **Between** CC — **conoid torn, trapezoid intact** · (2) **lateral** to CC — **both torn** (proposed "IIC") | Conoid torn | **Unstable**; **nonunion 30–45%** | **ORIF** |
| **III** | **Intra-articular** into AC joint, lateral to CC | Intact | Stable → later **AC arthritis** | Nonop |
| **IV** | **Physeal** (skeletally immature); clavicle pops out of periosteal sleeve superiorly | Intact | Stable | Nonop |
| **V** | **Comminuted**; inferior fragment stays on CC | Intact | Usually **unstable** | **ORIF** |

**Stable** — **I, III, IV** · **Unstable** — **IIA, IIB, V**

**Conoid injury on X-ray** — **lateral fracture with superiorly displaced medial fragment**\
✗ Wrong options: conoid still on butterfly fragment · medial to CC · undisplaced

### Alternative classification

| Type | Definition | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Non-displaced, CC intact (A1 extra-, A2 intra-articular) | Nonop |
| **B** | Displaced, CC intact (B1 extra-articular, B2 comminuted) | Nonop vs op |
| **C** | **Displaced, CC torn** (C1 extra-, C2 intra-articular) | **Op** |

### Differential

**Mimics** — clavicle shaft fracture · paediatric medial / distal physeal injury · **AC separation**

## 5 Management

```
Distal clavicle fracture
├─ Stable (Neer I, III, IV), minimally displaced, children → sling, ROM 2–4 wk
└─ Unstable (IIA, IIB, V) / displaced in athlete → ORIF
       └─ tiny distal fragment → hook plate or CC reconstruction
```

### Treatment indications – sling vs ORIF / CC reconstruction

| Absolute | Relative |
|---|---|
| **Open / impending open (skin tenting)** | **Unstable pattern (IIA, IIB, V)** |
| **Subclavian artery / vein injury** | Brachial plexus injury (66% recover spontaneously) |
| **Floating shoulder** (+ scapular neck > 10 mm displaced) | Closed head injury, seizures, polytrauma |
| **Symptomatic nonunion** | — |

**Minimally displaced, 56-yo golfer** — **sling → gentle ROM**\
✗ Wrong options: IM pin · bone stimulator · ORIF · distal clavicle resection

**Displaced, professional motocross athlete** — **ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: sling · Kenny-Howard (skin breakdown) · smooth K-wires (migration) · Weaver-Dunn (unnecessary with good bone)

**Displaced, wants to avoid second operation** — **small-fragment plate ± CC reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: distal clavicle resection (instability) · transacromial wires (removal / migration) · CC screw (removal) · hook plate (removal for acromial osteolysis)

### Sling immobilisation

**Technique** — sling or figure-of-8 (**no difference** in function / cosmesis); **no reduction attempt**; ROM at **2–4 wk**; strengthening **6–10 wk**

**Outcomes** — nonunion **\~15%** (Neer II up to 56%); poorer cosmesis

### Open reduction internal fixation

| Construct | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Locking / precontoured / LC-DCP plate** | Needs **large enough distal fragment** for multiple locking screws; **≥ 3–4 bicortical screws medially** |
| **Hook plate** | When **distal fragment too small**; hook **posterior to AC joint, far lateral**; correct depth; **needs removal (2nd surgery)** — acromial osteolysis |
| Others | AC-spanning fixation, transacromial wires, TBW, IM screw, **CC repair / reconstruction**, modified Weaver-Dunn (excisable fragment + CA ligament) |

**ORIF advantages** — ↑ union, faster union, less overhead pain, ↓ symptomatic malunion, better cosmesis

**ORIF disadvantages** — further procedures, symptomatic hardware, infection

**Rehab** — sling 7–10 d → active motion; strengthen \~6 wk; sport \~3 mo; hardware removal after 3 mo

## 6 Complications

| Nonoperative | Operative |
|---|---|
| **Nonunion \~15%** — comminution, displacement, Z deformity, **female, older, smoker**, distal > middle third → asymptomatic: leave · symptomatic: **ORIF + graft** | **Hardware prominence** (\~30% request removal; superior plates worst) |
| **AC arthritis** — **Neer I + III** → **distal clavicle resection** | **Hardware removal** — commonest with **hook plates** |
| — | **NV injury 3%** (superior plates → subclavian penetration), **subclavian thrombosis** |
| — | Nonunion **1–5%** · infection \~4.8% · mechanical failure \~1.4% · PTX |
| — | **Adhesive capsulitis 4%** needing surgery |

## X vs Y

| | **Neer IIA** | **Neer IIB** |
|---|---|---|
| Fracture vs CC | **Medial** to CC | **Between** (or lateral to) CC |
| Conoid | **Intact** | **Torn** |
| Trapezoid | Intact | Intact (or torn) |
| Nonunion nonop | **Up to 56%** | **30–45%** |

| | **Neer IIB distal clavicle fracture** | **AC separation (type III)** |
|---|---|---|
| CC injury | **Conoid only** | **Conoid + trapezoid** |

| | **Conoid** | **Trapezoid** |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | **4–4.5 cm** | **2–3 cm** |
| Tubercle | **Posteromedial** | Anterolateral |
| Role | **Main vertical stabiliser** | Vertical |

## How it's asked

**"X-ray indicating conoid injury?"** → **lateral fracture with superiorly displaced medial fragment**

**"Conoid ligament — true?"** → **4–4.5 cm medial, posteromedial to trapezoid tubercle, main vertical stabiliser**

**"Neer IIB?"** → **between CC ligaments, conoid torn, trapezoid intact**

**"Fracture with ligament violation → vertical instability?"** → **Neer IIB**

**"Wants to avoid a second operation?"** → **small-fragment plate ± CC reconstruction**

**"Minimally displaced, golfer?"** → **sling + gentle ROM**

**"Displaced in professional athlete?"** → **ORIF**

**"RELATIVE indication for surgery?"** → **unstable fracture pattern**\
✗ Wrong options: tenting · subclavian vein injury · symptomatic nonunion · floating shoulder = absolute

**"Hook plate downside?"** → **removal needed (acromial osteolysis)**

**"Nonunion risk in IIA nonop?"** → **up to 56%**

**"AC arthritis after Neer I / III?"** → **distal clavicle resection**

**"Nerve at risk, weak ER in adduction?"** → **suprascapular**
