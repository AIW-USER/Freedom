# Tibial Plafond Fractures

*Trauma · 63 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **5–10% of tibia fractures** (Orthobullets) · 5–7% (TO) · < 10% of lower-limb injuries · age 35–45, M > F

**Associated** — **fibula fracture 75% (commonest association, TO)** · ipsilateral limb injury 30% · **open 20%** · bilateral 5–10% · **syndesmotic injury \~3.4%**

### Fracture anatomy & patterns (Chaput fragment)

**Mechanism** — **high-energy axial load** (talus driven into plafond → **articular impaction + metaphyseal comminution**) · fall from height, MVC · low-energy rotation (skiing) less common

**Posterior pilon** — **rotation + axial impaction** (plantarflexed foot) → large posterior malleolus fragment, posteromedial comminution, **double-density sign** → ORIF

**Fibula intact (TO)** — **medial (varus) impaction pattern** more likely (TO)

**Foot position (TO)** — **forced plantarflexion → posterior plafond** · **forced dorsiflexion → anterior plafond** injury (TO)

**Associated (TO)** — talar body / neck, tibial shaft, plateau, calcaneus fractures · rule out spine, head, abdominal injury · open injuries → **Ganga score** for salvage decision (TO)

**Pilon deforming forces (TO)** — talus drives plafond into comminution · distal fragment **ER**, proximal fragment IR · fibula carries **20–25%** of load (TO)

| Fragment (ligament attached) | Position |
|---|---|
| **Medial malleolar** | **Deltoid** |
| **Chaput** | **Anterolateral** — **AITFL** |
| **Volkmann** | **Posterolateral** — **PITFL** |
| Wagstaffe | **Fibular** (anterior tubercle) — other end of AITFL |

**3 fragments typical** (intact ligaments) · **4 classic** on X-ray: medial, anterior (Chaput), lateral (Wagstaffe), posterior (Volkmann)

**Syndesmosis** — AITFL (Chaput → Wagstaffe), **PITFL — strongest** (Volkmann → lateral malleolus), interosseous membrane + ligament, inferior transverse ligament

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — swelling, **fracture blisters**, open wounds, chronic skin / vascular change · **DP + PT pulses** (ABI / CTA if needed) · nerves · **compartment syndrome**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Fact |
|---|---|
| X-ray | AP, lateral, mortise + **full-length tibia / fibula + foot** · lumbar films if indicated |
| **CT** | **After spanning ex-fix** (ligamentotaxis → better planning) (✗ Wrong options: before frame) · fine cuts ± 3D · **"Mercedes-Benz" sign** on axials |
| **CT soft-tissue windows** | **Tibialis posterior tendon entrapment** (posteromedial fragment) |
| MRI | Not used — poor bony detail |

**CT impact (TO)** — **changes the surgical plan in > 60%** · obtain only after spanning + length restored ("**span, scan, plan**") (TO)

![Mercedes-Benz sign on axial CT](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob1046-screen-shot-2021-04-28-at-10-00-25-pm.jpg)

| **C** | **Complete articular** (articular dissociation from shaft) | 1 simple · 2 simple articular + multifragmentary metaphysis · 3 multifragmentary |

| Rüedi-Allgöwer | Pattern | Image |
|---|---|---|

## 5 Management

```
Pilon fracture
├─ Non-displaced / unfit / high skin risk → long leg cast 6 wk
├─ Length-unstable, high energy → splint → spanning ex-fix (± fibula) → CT → delayed ORIF (10–14 d)
│      └─ open → I&D + ex-fix
├─ Simple articular extension → percutaneous screws + IM nail
└─ Non-reconstructable / elderly / labourer → primary arthrodesis (rare)
```

### Staged management — spanning ex-fix then delayed ORIF

**Sequence** — **splint → spanning ex-fix → CT → delayed ORIF**\
✗ Wrong options: CT before ex-fix · acute ORIF · cast

**Comminuted closed pilon — next** — **spanning external fixation**\
✗ Wrong options: ORIF · percutaneous · cast · arthrodesis · CT / MRI first

**Open pilon (medial wound)** — **I&D + external fixation** · open + tibial defect → **ex-fix of tibia** only; graft at staged ORIF\
✗ Wrong options: immediate ORIF · I&D + splint · amputation

**Decreases wound complications** — **immediate spanning ex-fix ± immediate fibula fixation, delayed tibial reconstruction** (Sirkin)\
✗ Wrong options: immediate tibia · splint + ORIF at 1 wk · Ilizarov + limited fixation

**Obese smoker, swollen** — **closed reduction + spanning ex-fix**\
✗ Wrong options: splint → cast · ORIF · Blair fusion

**3 wk, swelling settled** — **ORIF tibia + fibula**\
✗ Wrong options: fibula only · walking cast · dynamise · fusion in young

**Two-stage ORIF vs LIFEF** — **two-stage ORIF preferred** (LIFEF: ↑ superficial infection, healing problems) · acute ORIF infection up to **55%** · staged \~**3%** · definitive ex-fix: pin infection, malalignment, nonunion

**Timing** — **skin wrinkles, blisters epithelialised, ecchymosis resolving (10–14 d)** · ex-fix until then

**Ex-fix** — **2 tibial half-pins + 1 transcalcaneal pin** ('delta' / 'A' frame) · **1st MT pin prevents equinus** · trans-navicular if calcaneal fracture · pins out of zone of injury / incisions

**Options (TO)** — **single-stage ORIF rare** (minimal swelling only) · **staged = most common** (stage 1 fibula fixation for length / ligamentotaxis + spanning ex-fix → stage 2 plafond) · **ex-fix + limited internal fixation** for poor soft tissue / open · **primary arthrodesis for non-reconstructable C3** · **slab** acceptable if fibula intact + alignment acceptable (TO)

**Post-op (TO)** — early ROM · **WB after 8–12 wk** (TO)

### Operative criteria

| Option | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Cast** | Stable, no articular displacement · critically ill / non-ambulatory · diabetes, PVD, neuropathy |
| **Spanning ex-fix** | **Most length-unstable** fractures (temporary) |
| **ORIF** | **Definitive for most**; outcome = **articular reduction** |
| Ex-fix / circular frame alone | Bone / soft tissue precludes ORIF · high union, pin-tract infection |
| **IM nail + percutaneous screws** | **Simple intra-articular extension** · ↑ **valgus + recurvatum** vs plate |
| **Primary arthrodesis** | Non-reconstructable, select elderly, labourers |

**Simple articular extension, swollen** — **percutaneous articular fixation + IM nail**\
✗ Wrong options: cast · immediate ORIF · uniplanar ex-fix

### Surgical technique & approaches

**Rüedi-Allgöwer principles (TO)** — **1 restore fibular length · 2 anatomic articular reduction · 3 cancellous autograft for defect · 4 medial column buttress** (now often anterolateral locking plate)

**Order after fibula plated** — **anatomic reduction + stabilisation of articular surface first** → then metaphysis (length, rotation, angulation — not anatomic) → plate to shaft · simple fibula: plate first · **comminuted fibula: after tibia**\
✗ Wrong options: plate first

**Alignment goals** — **< 5–10° varus / valgus**, **< 5–10° pro- / recurvatum** · skin bridge **> 7 cm**

**Skin bridge** — **> 7 cm** (Orthobullets) · **minimum 5–7 cm** (TO) — read the options · **curvilinear incisions** across ankle, long incisions with thick flaps (TO)

**Fragment-specific fixation (TO)** — identify anterior, medial, posterior, Chaput fragments · **reduce posterior malleolus early → converts C into B pattern** · **low-profile buttress plates (1/3 tubular / recon)** for medial + anterior shear · **avoid bulky anatomical medial plates** · **elevate impacted fragments before compressing** · graft metaphyseal voids · check syndesmosis intra-op (TO)

| Approach | Plane / fact |
|---|---|
| **Anterolateral** | **Deep ↔ superficial peroneal nerves** · in line with **4th ray** · valgus-impacted / intact fibula · **whole articular surface visible** · extensors retracted **medially** · **SPN → dorsal foot numbness (commonest nerve problem)** · DPN at risk deep |
| **Anteromedial** | **Deep peroneal ↔ tibial nerve** · **extensile access to central impaction** |
| Direct anterior | **No true internervous plane**; not extensile |
| **Posterolateral** | Fibula (separate incision for skin bridge) · **bone graft for distal tibial nonunion** |

**Fibular fixation** — not essential · **↑ overall complications + hardware removal**; **no difference in reduction, alignment, satisfaction, arthrosis** (OB) · **TO: alignment DOES differ** (EXCEPT: "no difference in alignment" is false) — read the options

**Post-op** — ankle ROM at **2 wk** · NWB **6–12 wk**

**Staging (TO)** — stage 1 **delta-frame spanning ex-fix** (length + alignment, avoid over-reduction) → stage 2 **CT by column** → stage 3 definitive once **wrinkle sign** returns · **fibular fixation helps restore tibial length** (TO)

| Approach (TO) | Fragment |
|---|---|
| **Anterolateral** | **Chaput** / anterolateral plafond |
| **Medial** | Medial malleolar fragment |
| **Posterolateral** | **Volkmann** fragment |

**Fixation (TO)** — buttress plating · **raft screws** under articular surface · graft central defect

### Fine-wire fixation vs ORIF

**43C open, FWF vs ORIF** — **FWF → higher complication rate** · same function, malunion, nonunion, PTOA · meta-analysis: frame vs ORIF — **no difference** in infection / complications (Orthobullets) — read the options

**Circular frame** — **distraction = reduction** · 2 AP half-pins proximally · **wires ≥ 1–2 cm from joint** (septic arthritis) · **60° safe arc** · HA-coated pins ↓ loosening

![Circular frame wire safe zones](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob1046-screen-shot-2021-05-20-at-8-19-48-pm.jpg)

### Primary arthrodesis

**Position** — **neutral DF, 5–10° ER, 5° hindfoot valgus, 5 mm posterior talar translation** · anterior plate · cast 8 wk, FWB 12 wk, CT at 3 months

**TTC nail** — sacrifices subtalar motion, ↑ transverse tarsal arthritis, immediate WB

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Rate / fact |
|---|---|
| **Wound slough / dehiscence** | **9–30%** · wait 1–2 wk · free flap |
| **Infection** | **5–15%** · swelling at surgery, severity · **intra-wound vancomycin powder ↓ gram +ve deep SSI** |
| Malunion | 6–14% |
| **Nonunion** | **5%**, **metaphyseal junction** · comminution, open, bone loss, **smoking, NSAIDs** → rule out infection, graft + rigid fixation |
| **PTOA** | **1–2 yr** · non-anatomic reduction · TAA / fusion |
| **Stiffness** | **Up to 33% at 3 yr** · severity, obesity, ASA ≥ 3 |
| **Tibialis posterior entrapment** | Posteromedial fragment, **↓ inversion**, gap · **CT soft-tissue windows** |

**Malunion (TO)** — **medial collapse** if impaction not addressed · **missed syndesmotic injury** (TO)

### Complications — nonunion grafting, tendon entrapment, equinus

**Distal tibial nonunion graft — approach** — **posterolateral** (best soft-tissue cover)\
✗ Wrong options: anterior · anterolateral · anteromedial · lateral

**Inversion weakness + posteromedial gap** — **tibialis posterior entrapment**\
✗ Wrong options: deltoid · FHL (more posterior) · peroneus brevis · tibial nerve (plantar numbness)

**Tendoachilles lengthening indicated** — **DF −10° with knee flexed AND extended** (Silfverskiöld)\
✗ Wrong options: only with knee extended (gastrocnemius) · subtalar 5° (arthritis)

### Post-traumatic arthritis — chondrocyte death & IL-6

**Initial chondrocyte death** — **necrosis**, **superficial zone**, **at fracture margins** · **apoptosis = delayed**, also at margins\
✗ Wrong options: deep zone · remote

**Synovial fluid** — **↑ IL-6** (+ IL-8, MMP-1, -2, -3, -9, -10) · not linked to NWB duration or compliance\
✗ Wrong options: TGF-β · RANKL · IL-2 · cAMP

### Outcomes & predictors (education level, prolonged recovery, syndesmotic injury)

**Poor outcome / no return to work** — **lower education**, comorbidities (≥ 2), **male**, **work-related**, low income, married, definitive ex-fix\
✗ Wrong options: joint line · displacement · time to ORIF · open · varus collapse · marijuana · two plates · temporary ex-fix

**Recovery** — **improves for > 2 yr** · type C → SF-36 lower than pelvic fracture, AIDS, CAD

**Syndesmotic injury** — **\~3.4%** · **↑ amputation**, deep infection, reoperation · more segmental fibula fractures · no ↑ nonunion

### Return to driving — braking time

**Brake travel time** — **↑ until 6 wk after weight bearing begins** (long-bone + articular, right leg)\
✗ Wrong options: 8 wk · normal at WB

| | **Anterolateral approach** | **Anteromedial approach** |
|---|---|---|
| Plane | **Deep ↔ superficial peroneal** | **Deep peroneal ↔ tibial** |
| Best for | Valgus impaction, intact fibula | **Central impaction** (extensile) |
| Nerve risk | **SPN — dorsal foot numbness** | Saphenous |

| | **Gastrocnemius contracture** | **Achilles contracture** |
|---|---|---|
| DF knee flexed | Normal | **Limited** |
| DF knee extended | **Limited** | **Limited** |
| Surgery | Gastrocnemius recession | **TA lengthening** |
