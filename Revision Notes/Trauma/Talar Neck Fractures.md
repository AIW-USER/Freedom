# Talar Neck Fractures

*Trauma · 60 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Frequency** — **commonest talus fracture (\~50%)** · talus fractures 0.1–2.5% of all fractures (TO) · **ipsilateral foot injuries up to 89%** (CT)

**Mechanism** — high energy · **forced dorsiflexion + axial load**

### Anatomy

**Talus** — **60–70% cartilage**, **no muscle / tendon attachments** · inferior → posterior facet of calcaneus · head → navicular + sustentaculum tali · posterior process: medial + lateral tubercles, **FHL groove**

**Neck vs body** — defined by the **lateral process** (articulates with **posterior facet of calcaneus**) · fracture **anterior → neck**, **posterior → body**

### Blood supply — artery of the tarsal canal & deltoid branch

| Source | Branch | Supplies |
|---|---|---|
| **Posterior tibial** | **Artery of tarsal canal** — **dominant** | **Most of body** (lateral ⅔ of body, OB) · forms anastomotic sling under neck with tarsal sinus artery |
| **Posterior tibial** | **Deltoid branch** (deep deltoid) | **Medial body** · **may be the only supply left in displaced fractures** |
| Anterior tibial (dorsalis pedis) | — | **Head + neck** |
| **Perforating peroneal** | **Artery of tarsal sinus** | Head + neck, lateral body |

**Extraosseous sources (TO)** — **posterior tibial, anterior tibial, peroneal** · **artery of tarsal canal = mainly intraosseous** → "NOT extraosseous" (TO)

**Retrograde supply** — so displaced neck fractures → high AVN

**Hawkins III — preserve** — **deltoid ligament / deltoid branch of artery of tarsal canal**\
✗ Wrong options: medial malleolus (**osteotomise** it) · dorsalis pedis · tarsal sinus · perforating peroneal · ATFL

**Vessel whose injury ↑ osteonecrosis** — **artery of the tarsal canal**\
✗ Wrong options: dorsalis pedis · perforating peroneal · lateral tarsal · tarsal sinus

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Fact |
|---|---|
| AP, lateral foot | Radiograph sensitivity for talar injury only **74%** (TO) |
| **Canale view** | **Best view for talar neck** — **maximum equinus, 15° pronated, beam 75° cephalad** |
| **CT** | **Displacement, comminution, congruity**; **gold standard (TO)**; confirms Hawkins I has no step-off |
| **Mortise X-ray 6–8 wk** | **Hawkins sign** |

**Hawkins II with 20–50% risk** — pick the image with **subtalar dislocation only** · posterior process fractures → little AVN

## 5 Management

```
Talar neck fracture
├─ All → emergent closed reduction in ER
├─ Hawkins I (CT: no step-off) → SLC 8–12 wk, NWB first 6 wk
├─ Displaced (II–IV), extruded → ORIF (may delay for soft tissues)
│      ├─ soft tissue / patient unfit → ex-fix until safe
│      └─ medial comminution → medial bridge plate + positional screws
└─ Open / extruded talus → I&D + reimplant + ORIF ± ex-fix
```

### Operative criteria

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Hawkins I** | Cast **8–12 wk** (NWB 6 wk) · TO: type I nonoperative · nondisplaced neck **still usually fixed** (TO) — cast for non-ambulatory / unfit |
| **Hawkins II–IV** | **ORIF** — even if closed reduction looks good (✗ Wrong options: definitive closed · pins · primary subtalar / TTC fusion) |
| **Interposed fragment** | **Open reduction before screws** (✗ Wrong options: percutaneous screws) |
| **Talar extrusion** | **Reimplant + ORIF** |

### Treatment — ORIF, fixation construct & timing

**Approaches** — **two incisions** to see medial + lateral neck (comminution **dorsal + medial**)

| Approach | Interval / key point |
|---|---|
| **Anteromedial** | **Tibialis anterior ↔ tibialis posterior** · preserve **deep deltoid** · **medial malleolar osteotomy** if needed |
| **Anterolateral** | Tibia–fibula proximally, **line of 4th ray** · elevate **EDB**, clear subtalar debris |

**Avoid** — dissection superior + inferior to the neck (remaining blood supply)

**Strongest construct (TO)** — **posterior-to-anterior parallel dual screws**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior-to-posterior · crossed

**Medial comminution (Hawkins II)** — **ORIF with medial bridge plate + lateral screw in non-lagging mode** · mini-fragment plates buttress against varus\
✗ Wrong options: lag screws (shorten medial column → **varus**) · TTC fusion · closed + pins

**Timing** — **delayed fixation does NOT ↑ AVN** · **acute fixation → ↑ infection, skin necrosis, wound dehiscence** (77% vs 2–10%, TO) · open fractures → emergent debridement\
✗ Wrong options: acute → ↓ AVN · ↓ subtalar OA · ↓ varus · ↓ dehiscence

**Post-op** — **NWB 10–12 wk**

![Talar neck ORIF](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob1048-shows-postoperative-talar-neck-fracture.jpg)

### Extruded talus — reimplantation

**Extruded body + neck fracture** — **I&D + reduce native talar body + ORIF neck**\
✗ Wrong options: cast · Steinmann pins + ex-fix definitive · allograft · remove + spacer

**Extruded talus with debris** — **bacitracin solution, irrigate, debride, reimplant**, ex-fix and / or ORIF\
✗ Wrong options: talectomy + ex-fix · tibiocalcaneal fusion · tibiotalar fusion · Syme

**Reimplantation — true** — **safe and can succeed** · **low infection** with I&D + antibiotics · **AVN \~90%, most without collapse** · \~63% need no secondary procedure\
✗ Wrong options: acute triple arthrodesis · BKA

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Rate / fact |
|---|---|
| **Subtalar arthritis** | **Most common complication — \~50%** (50–100%, OB; 54% PTA) |
| Tibiotalar arthritis | **33%** |
| **Osteonecrosis** | **31% overall** · **2nd commonest** |
| **Varus malunion** | **25–30%** (Orthobullets) · **one-third** (TO) |
| Nonunion | 10–12% |
| Symptomatic implants | \~18% |
| Wound dehiscence | \~3% |

**Order (OB)** — **subtalar arthritis > tibiotalar arthritis > osteonecrosis > varus malunion**

### Hawkins sign & osteonecrosis prognosis

**Hawkins sign** — **subchondral lucency of talar dome**, **mortise / AP at 6–8 wk** → **resorption = intact vascularity** → **AVN unlikely** → observe, progress weight bearing\
✗ Wrong options: cement · fusion · arthroscopy

**Hawkins sign positive** — **low risk of osteonecrosis**\
✗ Wrong options: sclerosis (bad) · diffuse osteopenia · medial malleolus fracture

**Absent sign** — doesn't prove AVN · up to **44% revascularise without collapse**

**Not related to Hawkins sign** — fixation construct; **reduction quality** affects AVN

### Osteonecrosis risk factors (displacement, open fracture; not surgical timing)

**Greatest impact** — **extent of initial fracture displacement**\
✗ Wrong options: fixation within 6–8 h · nicotine · PA screws · reduction quality

**↑ AVN** — displacement, **comminution**, **subtalar dislocation**, **extension into body**

**Not risk factors** — **delay > 24 h**, time to reduction > 12 h, surgery duration, polytrauma, staged ex-fix then ORIF

**Open fracture** — **independent AVN risk factor** (Orthobullets; OB — extruded talus) · **no significant difference** open vs closed (OB, Hawkins II) — read the options

**Dual-incision approach** — **associated with AVN** (Orthobullets; OB — vs lateral only) · **not linked** (OB, Hawkins II) — read the options

### Post-traumatic arthritis — most common complication

**Most likely long-term complication after anatomic ORIF** — **tibiotalar and / or subtalar arthritis**\
✗ Wrong options: osteonecrosis · nonunion · infection · forefoot supination

**Hawkins II** — **subtalar arthritis**\
✗ Wrong options: AVN · tibiotalar · talonavicular (not involved)

**Hawkins III — second commonest** — **osteonecrosis** (subtalar arthritis first)

### Varus malunion — effects & corrective osteotomy

**Cause** — **medial comminution** / non-anatomic reduction · Hawkins II treated closed

**Effects** — **↓ subtalar eversion** (TO + OB) · locked midfoot + hindfoot · **supination deformity** · **walks on lateral border** · later subtalar arthrosis\
✗ Wrong options: planovalgus (pronation)

**Treatment** — **talar neck medial opening wedge osteotomy** (tricortical ICBG)\
✗ Wrong options: TAA · lateral calcaneal closing wedge · calcaneal neck opening wedge · triple

**Dorsal impingement + ↓ ankle dorsiflexion** — **isolated dorsal malunion**\
✗ Wrong options: varus · valgus · plantar

**Hawkins sign 6 wk = poor prognosis (TO)** — false; **varus malunion ↓ eversion** = true

### Nonunion & salvage (revision ORIF, TTC fusion)

**Nonunion, viable body, no arthritis** — **revision ORIF + bone graft** (preserves motion) · vascularised graft if AVN\
✗ Wrong options: talectomy · ankle / TTC / triple fusion

**AVN + tibiotalar + subtalar arthritis** — **tibiotalocalcaneal fusion** (hindfoot nail)\
✗ Wrong options: TAA (poor talar stock) · isolated tibiotalar fusion · supramalleolar osteotomy · arthroscopic debridement

**Isolated tibiotalar arthritis** — tibiotalar fusion or TAA (needs talar bone stock)

## X vs Y

| | **Hawkins sign present** | **Absent / sclerosis** |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | **Vascularity intact** (resorption) | Possible AVN |
| Prognosis | **Low AVN risk** | Risk — but 44% revascularise |
| Action | **Observe**, progress WB | Monitor |

| | **Varus malunion** | **Dorsal malunion** |
|---|---|---|
| Effect | **↓ subtalar eversion**, lateral border WB | **Dorsal impingement**, ↓ dorsiflexion |
| Fix | **Medial opening wedge osteotomy** | — |
