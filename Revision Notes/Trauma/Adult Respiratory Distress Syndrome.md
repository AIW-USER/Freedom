# Adult Respiratory Distress Syndrome

*Trauma · 2 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Definition

**ARDS** — acute lung injury after **polytrauma / long-bone fracture** → progressive respiratory distress, **refractory hypoxaemia**, **non-cardiogenic pulmonary oedema**

### Causes – acute endothelial damage

**Triggers** — **aspiration**, **infection**, **pancreatitis**, **multiple transfusions**, **lung injury**, **sepsis / shock**, **major trauma**, **large burns**, fat emboli, thromboembolism, MOF

**Cascade** — **non-cardiogenic pulmonary oedema** → respiratory distress → **refractory hypoxaemia** → **↓ lung compliance**

### Genes & lab markers

**IL-6 (interleukin 6)** — inflammatory marker **most closely tied to systemic inflammatory response** after orthopaedic injury (**femur fracture**) **and its treatment (IM nailing)**\
✗ Wrong options: IL-1 · IL-10 (no correlation with treatment) · TNF-α · D-dimer (↑ after trauma but don't track treatment)

**Status** — research marker, **not yet clinically applicable**

### Phases

| Phase | Timing | Histology |
|---|---|---|
| **Exudative** | Initial | **Hyaline membranes (fibrin)** |
| **Proliferative** | **\~3 days** | Alveolar exudate resolves or organises |
| **Fibrotic** | **3–4 weeks** | Alveolar ducts + spaces fibrose |

## 3 Presentation

**Onset** — acute, **12–48 h** after injury

**Symptoms** — **dyspnoea**, fever, **mottled / cyanotic skin**

**Exam** — **resistant hypoxia**, intercostal retractions, rales / crackles, rhonchi, tachypnoea

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnostic criteria

| PaO₂ / FiO₂ | Grade |
|---|---|
| **< 300 mm Hg** | **Mild** (= acute lung injury, ALI) |
| **< 200 mm Hg** | **Moderate** — **ARDS threshold** |
| **< 100 mm Hg** | **Severe** |

**Other thresholds** — respiratory compliance **< 40 mL/cm H₂O** · PEEP **> 10 cm H₂O** · corrected expired minute volume **> 10 L/min**

**Hallmark** — hypoxaemia **refractory to O₂**

### Key radiology findings

**CXR** — **diffuse bilateral infiltrates**, patchy air-space oedema, **normal-sized heart** (→ CHF less likely)

**Serial CXRs** — early identification in at-risk patients

### Differential

**Mimics** — cardiogenic pulmonary oedema (CHF, MI), bilateral pneumonia, SARS

## 5 Management

**Prevention** — **early stabilisation of long-bone (femur) fractures** · monitor PEEP in at-risk patients · serial CXR

**Treatment** — **PEEP ventilation** + steroids · treat underlying cause

## 6 Complications

**Pneumothorax** — barotrauma from **high-PEEP ventilation**

**Mortality** — **\~50%** even with ICU care

## X vs Y

| | **ARDS** | **Cardiogenic oedema** |
|---|---|---|
| Oedema | **Non-cardiogenic** | Cardiogenic |
| Heart size | **Normal** | Enlarged |
| Response to O₂ | **Refractory** | Responds |

| | **ALI** | **ARDS** |
|---|---|---|
| PaO₂/FiO₂ | **< 300** | **< 200** |

## How it's asked

**"Inflammatory marker most closely tied to systemic response after orthopaedic injury and treatment?"** → **IL-6**\
✗ Wrong options: IL-1 · IL-10 · TNF-α · D-dimer

**"PaO₂/FiO₂ defining ARDS?"** → **< 200 mm Hg** (< 300 = ALI / mild, < 100 = severe)

**"Prevention in polytrauma with femur fracture?"** → **early long-bone stabilisation**

**"Complication of high-PEEP ventilation?"** → **pneumothorax**
