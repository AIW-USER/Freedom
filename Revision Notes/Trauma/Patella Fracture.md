# Patella Fracture

*Trauma · 45 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **1% of skeletal injuries** · **6–9% open** · M:F 2:1 · age 20–50

**Associated** — femoral neck fracture, **posterior wall acetabulum**, knee dislocation (dashboard)

### Anatomy

**Patella** — **largest sesamoid**; cartilage on **superior ¾** (up to 1 cm), **inferior ¼ non-articular**; **lateral facet larger**

**Function** — ↑ extensor power **30–50%** (moves line of pull anterior)

**MPFL** — medial epicondyle ↔ adductor tubercle → upper ⅔ medial patella; restrains lateral translation **0–30°**

**Quadriceps tendon layers** — superficial **rectus femoris** · middle **VM + VL** · deep **vastus intermedius**

**Retinaculum** — fascia lata, VM, VL → **repair with fixation**

### Patellar blood supply

**Ring** — genicular arteries (descending / supreme, superior + inferior medial + lateral, anterior tibial recurrent) → **mid-patellar + polar systems**

**Dominant supply** — **distal (inferior) pole**, **inferomedial** quadrant → its excision **most ↓ perfusion**\
✗ Wrong options: superior pole · superolateral (least)

![Blood supply of the patella](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob1042-blood-supply-to-patella.jpg)

## 2 Mechanism

| Mechanism | Pattern | Retinaculum |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct blow** (dashboard, fall) | **Comminuted**, chondral damage | **May be intact** |
| **Indirect eccentric quad contraction** (flexing against contracted quads) | **Transverse / inferior pole avulsion** | **Torn (typical)** |
| Child **8–10 yr** | **Sleeve fracture** | High suspicion |

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — palpable defect, haemarthrosis; **can't straight-leg raise → extensor mechanism + retinaculum disrupted** (aspirate + lidocaine if pain limits)

**Saline load** — rule out joint communication (open)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| View / study | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Lateral (30° flexion)** | **Best for transverse**; step-off |
| **Axial (sunrise / Merchant)** | **Best for vertical** |
| **Patella alta (Insall-Salvati > 1.0)** | **Patellar tendon** disruption |
| **Patella baja (< 1.0)** | **Quadriceps tendon** disruption |
| **CT** | **Distal pole comminution (sagittal)**, stress fracture, nonunion; **changes plan in 50%** |
| MRI | Not typically needed |

### Classification

| Pattern | Note |
|---|---|
| Nondisplaced | — |
| **Displaced** | **Step-off > 2–3 mm** or **gap > 1–4 mm** |
| Transverse · pole / sleeve · **vertical** · marginal · osteochondral · comminuted (stellate) | — |

**AO / OTA 34** — **A extra-articular · B partial articular · C complete articular**

![Patella fracture patterns](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob1042-shows-fracture-types.jpg)

### Bipartite patella

**Bipartite** — **failure of fusion of ossification centres**; **2–3%**; **superolateral**; **bilateral 50%**; **smooth corticated borders**; SLR intact\
✗ Wrong options: failed apoptosis (discoid meniscus) · eccentric contraction · direct blow

## 5 Management

```
Patella fracture
├─ SLR intact + step-off < 2–3 mm + gap < 3 mm (or vertical) → brace / cast in extension, WBAT
├─ SLR lost / step-off > 2–3 mm / gap > 3 mm / open / osteochondral → ORIF (keep patella)
│      └─ comminuted extra-articular inferior pole < 40–50% → partial patellectomy + tendon advancement
└─ Unsalvageable comminution / infection / tumour → total patellectomy (+ VMO advancement)
```

### Operative criteria

| Nonop | ORIF |
|---|---|
| **Intact extensor mechanism (SLR)** | **Extensor failure (no SLR)** |
| Step-off < 2–3 mm, gap < 3 mm | **Step-off > 2–3 mm**, **gap > 3 mm** (Orthobullets) · gap > 4 mm, step > 2 mm (TO) |
| **Vertical** patterns | **Open**, loose bodies, **osteochondral**, **sleeve (child)** |
| Major comorbidity | — |

**Most important factor for surgery in minimally displaced (TO)** — **ability to extend the knee**\
✗ Wrong options: > 2 mm · age · WB

**Nonop** — immobiliser / brace in extension, **WBAT**; ROM 1–2 wk; resistance at 6 wk; **> 95% good** · brace \~10° flexion avoids baja (TO)

### Fixation choice — tension band, screws & partial patellectomy

**Tension band** — **converts anterior tensile force → articular compression**; needs compressible fracture + intact opposite cortex

| Construct | Fact |
|---|---|
| **K-wires (0.062) + 18-gauge wire** | **Highest migration (K-wires bent only proximally)**, re-operation |
| K-wires + **suture** | **75% tensile strength** of wire, similar clinically, **less removal** |
| **4.0 mm cannulated screws + wire** | **Stronger than K-wires**; **less symptomatic hardware** |
| **Plate (mini-fragment / mesh)** | **Biomechanically superior**; comminution, distal pole, osteoporosis; less gapping |
| Cerclage | Alone or augment |

**Plate construct associated with** — **improved biomechanical stability**\
✗ Wrong options: less knee pain · less infection

**Technique** — midline incision; minimal stripping; retinacular repair; tension wire **superiorly** (more cover); tension in **2 places**; avoid over-tension + prominent screw tips

**Post-op** — WBAT in hinged brace, **flexion limited to 30° × 4 wk**

**Union** — **> 95%** any technique (nonunion higher if open)

**Partial patellectomy** — **comminuted extra-articular inferior pole < 40%** of height (Orthobullets) · **< 50%** (OB) when ORIF impossible · **advance tendon close to articular surface** (prevents tilt) · hyperextension, transosseous tunnels / anchors ± cerclage to tubercle\
✗ Wrong options: vertical · bipartite · stellate · chronic quad rupture

**Inferior pole, no SLR** — **ORIF or excision + patellar tendon advancement** (ORIF preferred; ✗ Wrong options: cast · brace · allograft · cerclage alone)

**Total patellectomy** — rare (unsalvageable, infection, tumour); **> 50% loss of extensor strength**; **VMO advancement** improves

**Osteochondral fracture after patellar dislocation (vertical, TO)** — **internal fixation**\
✗ Wrong options: arthroscopy · cast · patellectomy

## 6 Complications

### Symptomatic hardware & anterior knee pain

**Most common** — **symptomatic hardware (up to 50%)**; thin, open fracture, **K-wire TBW** → **remove after union**

**Removal rate** — **\~18%** elect removal (OB) · **≥ 50% after TBW** (OB) — read the options

**Painful kneeling at 1 yr, no OA** — **hardware removal**\
✗ Wrong options: PF arthritis treatment · MUA · nonunion surgery · steroid

**First 6 months** — **anterior knee pain** + quadriceps deficit = main impairment\
✗ Wrong options: osteonecrosis · refracture

**After cannulated screw TBW** — **implant removal ≥ 50%**; flexion + quad strength reduced, extensor lag may persist, pain worse than norms

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **PF osteoarthritis** | **Up to 50%** → TKA |
| **Osteonecrosis** | **Up to 25%**, **proximal pole**, asymptomatic, **revascularises by 2 yr** (Orthobullets) · < 5%, inferior pole (OB) |
| Loss of reduction | 0–22%; age, osteoporosis |
| Nonunion | **< 1–5%**; open |
| Infection | 0–5% (open **10–11%**) |
| Weakness / extensor lag | Patellectomy, poor retinacular repair |
| Stiffness | Long immobilisation |

**Poor prognosis** — comminution, open, **smoking**

## X vs Y

| | **Patella alta** | **Patella baja** |
|---|---|---|
| Insall-Salvati | **> 1.0** | **< 1.0** |
| Tendon torn | **Patellar** | **Quadriceps** |

| | **Bipartite** | **Fracture** |
|---|---|---|
| Edges | **Smooth, corticated** | Sharp |
| Site | **Superolateral** | Any |
| SLR | Intact | Often lost |

## How it's asked

**"Deciding factor for surgery (TO)?"** → **ability to extend the knee**

**"Partial patellectomy indication?"** → **severely comminuted inferior pole**

**"Inferior pole, no SLR — best?"** → **ORIF or excision + tendon advancement**

**"Highest hardware migration?"** → **K-wire TBW bent proximally only**

**"Plate construct associated with?"** → **improved biomechanical stability**

**"Painful kneeling 1 yr after TBW?"** → **remove hardware**

**"Most common complication after fixation?"** → **symptomatic implant**

**"Main impairment at 6 months?"** → **anterior knee pain**

**"Cannulated screw TBW — true?"** → **implant removal ≥ 50%**

**"Dominant arterial entry?"** → **distal pole**

**"Excision causing most ischaemia?"** → **inferomedial quadrant**

**"Superolateral smooth fragment, SLR intact?"** → **failed fusion of ossification centres**

**"Vertical osteochondral fracture after dislocation (TO)?"** → **internal fixation**
