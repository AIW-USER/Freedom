# Olecranon Fractures

*Trauma · 41 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — 12 / 100,000 / yr · **\~10% of upper-limb fractures** · mean age **\~57**; **bimodal**

**Mechanism** — **direct blow → comminuted** · **indirect (FOOSH) → transverse / oblique**

**Transolecranon fracture-dislocation** — severe axial load; **distal humerus driven through olecranon** = **anterior** dislocation; **PRUJ intact** (vs Monteggia)

### Anatomy

**Greater sigmoid (semilunar) notch** — olecranon + coronoid; articulates with trochlea (flexion-extension + stability)

**Triceps** — inserts posterior proximal ulna, blends with periosteum (**radial nerve, C7**) · **anconeus** — lateral olecranon (radial nerve)

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — posterior elbow · **palpable gap** → displaced / comminuted

**Can't extend elbow** — **extensor (triceps) mechanism discontinuous**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**True lateral** — **essential** for fracture pattern · radiocapitellar view → radial head / capitellum · CT → comminuted planning

### Classifications

## 5 Management

```
Olecranon fracture
├─ Nondisplaced, extensor intact → cast 45–90°, motion at 1 wk
├─ Displaced, transverse, no comminution → tension band (or IM screw)
├─ Comminuted / oblique past coronoid / Monteggia / fracture-dislocation → plate
└─ Elderly low-demand → nonop or excision + triceps advancement (< 50% joint)
```

### Operative criteria

| Implant | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Tension band / IM screw** | **Transverse, no comminution**, through **midpoint of trochlear notch**, **proximal to coronoid** |
| **Plate** | **Comminution**, **oblique extending distal to coronoid**, **coronoid involvement**, **Monteggia**, **fracture-dislocation**, articular impaction, **open** |
| **Excision + triceps advancement** | **Elderly osteoporotic**, **< 50% of joint**, too comminuted / small, nonunion; **no instability** |
| **Nonop cast** | Nondisplaced + intact extensor; **displaced in low-demand elderly** |

### Tension band wiring — indications & biomechanics

**Mechanism** — **converts triceps distraction (tension) into articular compression**\
✗ Wrong options: neutralisation · shear · bending

**Best for** — **transverse fracture through midpoint of trochlear notch**\
✗ Wrong options: comminuted · coronoid · Monteggia · oblique distal

**Technique** — K-wires **engaging anterior cortex** (↓ migration) but **no over-penetration**; **18-gauge wire / thick suture** figure-of-8 through drill hole

**Cons** — **hardware removal 40–80%**; **no axial stability in comminution**; can **narrow sigmoid notch** if comminuted

**Relative TBW indications** — tip fractures, transverse, osteoporotic, displaced

### Plate fixation — comminution, fracture-dislocation & open fractures

**Comminution** — **the** indication for plate over TBW\
✗ Wrong options: displacement · intra-articular · obliquity alone

**Comminuted, past coronoid (24-yr)** — **plate + screws**\
✗ Wrong options: IMN · TBW + IM screw · triceps advancement · TEA

**Articular comminution + depressed fragment (62-yr)** — **plate**

**Bridge plating** — **comminuted, non-reconstructable, young / active (45-yr)**\
✗ Wrong options: osteotomy fixation · simple transverse · 91-yr osteoporotic (excise) · child apophysis

**Transolecranon fracture-dislocation (open or closed)** — **plate** → best function\
✗ Wrong options: TBW / IM screw (need cortical contact) · excision (anterior instability) · cast

**Technique** — **dorsal (tension) side**; **lag screws for oblique**; ⅓ tubular too weak for comminution; triceps advanced over plate; **removal \~20%**

![Plate and screw fixation](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob1022-plate-and-screws.jpg)

### Elderly low-demand — nonoperative or excision with triceps advancement

**82-yr nursing home, comminuted 25% joint, osteopenic** — **cast in 90° flexion, short immobilisation then ROM** · Duckworth: good long-term results\
✗ Wrong options: 45° × 8 wk (stiffness) · pinning · TBW · locking plate

**79-yr RA + osteoporosis, comminuted proximal 30%** — **fragment excision + triceps advancement**\
✗ Wrong options: IM screw · TBW · TEA · lag + plate

**Excision ideal** — **85-yr, comminuted, < 50% of proximal joint**\
✗ Wrong options: young · > 50% · coronoid-level oblique

**Technique** — triceps reattached **adjacent to articular surface** with nonabsorbable sutures through drill holes

**Downside** — **↓ extension strength**; **instability** if ligament injury missed

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Symptomatic hardware** | **Most common** (subcutaneous; poor seating) |
| **Stiffness** | **\~50%**; rarely functional |
| **HO** | ↑ with **head injury** |
| **AIN injury** | **K-wire over-penetration of anterior (volar) cortex** — can't flex thumb IP / index DIP → observe |
| **↓ Forearm rotation** | **K-wire through volar cortex** or too radial |
| PTOA, ulnar nerve symptoms, ↓ extension strength | — |
| Nonunion | **Rare** |

### Postoperative stiffness — therapy & splinting

**Arc 40–90° at 2 months, rotation preserved** — **formal therapy + static progressive splinting** first\
✗ Wrong options: arthroscopic / open release · hardware removal · steroid + aggressive ROM

**Failed nonop** — limited open release, interposition, arthroscopy, **TEA if > 65**

## X vs Y

| | **Tension band** | **Plate** |
|---|---|---|
| Fracture | **Transverse, simple** | **Comminuted, oblique past coronoid, dislocation** |
| Principle | **Tension → compression** | Neutralisation / bridging |
| Removal | **40–80%** | \~20% |

| | **Transolecranon dislocation** | **Monteggia** |
|---|---|---|
| PRUJ | **Intact** | **Disrupted** |
| Radial head | Moves with ulna | **Dislocated** |
