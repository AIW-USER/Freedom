# Morel-Lavallee Lesion

*Trauma · 38 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & epidemiology

**Morel-Lavallée lesion (MLL)** — **closed degloving**: shear separates **dermis + subcutaneous fat from deep fascia**

**Incidence** — **up to 8% of acetabular fractures** (Orthobullets) · up to 10% (TO)

**Site** — **proximal lateral thigh (peritrochanteric) — commonest**; gluteal, lumbosacral, abdominal (hypermobile skin)

**Risk** — **high-energy shear**; **obese**; with **pelvic, acetabular, proximal femur** fractures

### Anatomy — plane of fluid collection

**Layers** — epidermis → dermis → subcutaneous fat → **[MLL] → deep fascia** → muscle

**Plane** — **deep to subcutaneous fat, superficial to deep fascia**\
✗ Wrong options: dermis (seroma) · subcutaneous (oedema) · intramuscular (haematoma) · deep to muscle

![Morel-Lavallée anatomy](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422820-morel-lavallee-anatomy-illustration.jpg)

### Pathophysiology (4 stages)

**Stages** — shear separation → **subdermal plexus** lymphatics + vessels bleed → replaced by **serosanguinous fluid** → **pseudocapsule** (chronic, untreated)

**Bacterial colonisation** — **up to 46%**

## 3 Presentation

**Missed** — **up to 33%** at index evaluation (distracting injuries, habitus)

**Exam** — **ecchymosis, swelling, fluctuance, skin hypermobility** · discoloration may take **days**

**Earliest indicator (TO)** — **paraesthesia of overlying skin**

**Chronic** — dull pain, contour deformity, cosmesis

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use / finding |
|---|---|
| Radiographs | Underlying fractures; soft-tissue swelling |
| **CT** | **Size → management**; fluid in fascial plane (soft-tissue window) |
| **Ultrasound** | **Hypoechoic** space superficial to fascia; localise for drainage |
| **MRI** | **Best extent** (avg **30 × 12 cm**); **acute: T1 low, T2 high** · **subacute: T1 + T2 high** with **capsule low on both** |

![CT soft-tissue window — Morel-Lavallée lesion of the thigh](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422820-left-thigh-mll-soft-tissue-window.jpg)

**Not to do for MLL** — MRI + core biopsy (sarcoma work-up), CT chest / abdomen / pelvis (metastasis work-up), admit for antibiotics (abscess)

## 5 Management

```
Morel-Lavallée lesion
├─ Small (< 50 cm³) → compression ± percutaneous drainage + drain
├─ Large (> 50 cm³) / failed nonop → open I&D + drain
│      └─ overlies fracture incision → dual-incision I&D, same sitting as fixation
└─ Chronic pseudocyst → open debridement + capsule resection
```

### Operative criteria

| Treatment | Indication | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Compression** (ace wraps, bike shorts) | **< 50 cm³** | Needs compliance |
| **Percutaneous drainage + drain** (US-guided, 1–2 cm incision, irrigate) | **< 50 cm³** | **Inferior** to open (can't debride necrotic tissue); **needle aspiration → high recurrence** |
| **Single-incision I&D** | **> 50 cm³** or failed nonop; **not in fracture approach** | **75% resolution** |
| **Dual-incision I&D** | **Lesion overlies fracture approach** / found intra-op | Higher infection; serial I&D |
| **Open debridement + capsule resection** | **Chronic pseudocyst** | Often multiple surgeries |

### Chronic lesion — open debridement & drain

**Large symptomatic lesion at 1 month** — **open I&D + drain** via **limited approach** (protect compromised subdermal plexus)\
✗ Wrong options: needle decompression · biopsy · antibiotics

**Trochanteric fracture + MLL (TO)** — **fix fracture + incision, open debridement at same time**\
✗ Wrong options: delay fixation · observe · fix + ambulate only

**Technique** — scrub-brush / Cobb debridement · **≥ 15 Fr drain** exiting away, remove at **< 20–30 mL/day** · primary vs delayed closure controversial (VAC + repeat I&D at **48–72 h**)

**Dual-incision** — proximal + distal ends of lesion; **close proximal primarily**, **distal: VAC → repeat I&D 48–72 h**

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Recurrence** | **Most common** — **up to 56% nonop**, **15–20% after open** → repeat I&D + drain, VAC, **sclerotherapy (talc / doxycycline)** |
| **Pseudocyst** | Chronic untreated → **resect capsule** |
| Skin necrosis | Delay, repeated I&D → skin graft |
| **SSI after pelvic / acetabular surgery** | **MLL = independent risk factor** |

**Prognosis** — better if **small, acute, away from incisions**

### Differentiation from necrotising fasciitis (LRINEC)

**LRINEC (TO)** — **CRP, WBC, haemoglobin, sodium, creatinine, glucose**; **≥ 6 = high risk** · **not included: SGPT (ALT)**

## X vs Y

| | **Morel-Lavallée** | **Haematoma / seroma** |
|---|---|---|
| Plane | **Subcutaneous fat ↔ deep fascia** | Intramuscular / dermis |
| Skin | **Hypermobile**, fluctuant | Not hypermobile |

## How it's asked

**"Plane of fluid collection?"** → **deep to subcutaneous fat, superficial to deep fascia**

**"Trochanteric fracture + thigh fullness (TO)?"** → **fixation combined with incision + open debridement**

**"Large fluctuant lesion 1 month after crush?"** → **open I&D + drain**

**"NOT in LRINEC (TO)?"** → **SGPT**
