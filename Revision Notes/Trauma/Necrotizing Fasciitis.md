# Necrotizing Fasciitis

*Trauma · 39 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & risk factors

**Necrotising fasciitis** — life-threatening infection spreading rapidly **along fascial planes**; overlying cellulitis ± (early skin deceptively benign)

**Immune suppression** — **diabetes (commonest comorbidity, 71%)**, AIDS / HIV, cancer, **obesity**, alcohol

**Bacterial entry** — **IV drug use**, injections, insect bites, **skin abrasions (turf)**, abdominal / perineal surgery

### Microbiology & empiric antibiotics (polymicrobial vs group A strep)

| Type | Organisms | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — polymicrobial (80–90%)** | **4–5 species**: **non-group A strep**, **anaerobes (Clostridia)**, facultative anaerobes, **gram-negative enteric bacilli (Enterobacteriaceae)**; synergy | **Diabetics, cancer**, post-op abdominal / perineal |
| **2 — monomicrobial (5%)** | **Group A β-haemolytic Streptococcus** (commonest monomicrobial) | **Healthy**, extremities |
| **3** | **Vibrio vulnificus** (gram-negative rods) | **Marine** exposure |
| 4 | Fungal | — |

**Commonest overall** — **polymicrobial** · **commonest single isolate** — **group A strep** (Fontes, Wong)\
✗ Wrong options: GAS · MRSA · S. aureus

**Polymicrobial culture most likely in** — **diabetic with necrotising soft-tissue infection**\
✗ Wrong options: chronic PJI (CoNS) · nail through shoe (Pseudomonas) · neonate hip (GBS) · acute PJI (S. aureus)

**MRSA** — **not** the commonest monomicrobial cause

## 3 Presentation

**Early** — rapidly progressing cellulitis / abscess, **minimal swelling**, no discoloration

**Late** — **severe pain (out of proportion)**, high fever, rigors, **tachycardia, hypotension**

**Skin** — **bullae (purple / haemorrhagic)**, ischaemic patches, cutaneous gangrene, induration, **subcutaneous emphysema (gas only 13%)**

**Red flag** — **worsening despite IV antibiotics** + systemic toxicity

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Imaging** — **not required**; **CT / MRI delay surgery** (only show oedema / thickening) · **gas → urgent debridement**

**Frozen section** — 1 × 1 × 1 cm, bedside or OR; confirms early cases; **never delay surgery** · histology: **fascial necrosis**, organisms in fascia, PMNs, **fibrinous thrombi + vessel wall necrosis**

### Diagnosis — LRINEC score & laboratory markers

| LRINEC component | Points |
|---|---|
| **CRP ≥ 150 mg/L** | **4 (largest)** |
| **WBC** 15–25 · **> 25 × 10⁹/L** | 1 · **2** |
| **Hb** 11–13.5 · **< 11 g/dL** | 1 · 2 |
| **Na < 135 mmol/L** | 2 |
| **Creatinine > 141 µmol/L** | 2 |
| **Glucose > 10 mmol/L** | 1 |

**Score** — max **13**; **≥ 6 → high risk**; original **PPV 92%** · later **sens 68%, spec 85%**

**NOT in LRINEC** — **ESR**, potassium, bicarbonate, calcium, **SGPT / ALT**

**Missing value given CRP, WBC, glucose, Cr, Na** — **haemoglobin**

**Most valuable lab (with Na 130, CRP > 200)** — **WBC > 25** → **emergent debridement ± amputation**\
✗ Wrong options: ESR · MRI

## 5 Management

```
Suspected necrotising fasciitis
├─ Resuscitate + broad-spectrum IV antibiotics immediately
├─ Emergent radical debridement (skin, fat, fascia, muscle) — don't wait for imaging / ICU / culture
│      └─ life-threatening → amputation
└─ Serial debridements; hyperbaric O₂ if anaerobes
```

### Emergent surgical debridement

**Worsening despite cefazolin, hypotension** — **urgent aggressive debridement of skin, fat + fascia**\
✗ Wrong options: triple antibiotics alone · ICU + ID consult · gentle closure · HBO / IVIG first

**Diabetic, bullae, CRP > 200, WBC 25k, Na 127** — **emergent debridement ± amputation**\
✗ Wrong options: vascular exploration · ICU + escalate antibiotics · MRI · CT

**IVDU / HIV hand, CRP 178, WBC 29k** — **emergent debridement**\
✗ Wrong options: mark margins + observe · vascular consult · oral antibiotics · next-day surgery

**Operative findings** — **liquefied fat**, **"dishwater" pus**, muscle necrosis, venous thrombosis

**Multiple debridements** — often required · **multidisciplinary team**

### Antibiotics

| Stage | Regimen |
|---|---|
| **Empiric** | **Penicillin + clindamycin + metronidazole + aminoglycoside** (clindamycin ↓ amputation odds) · or **vancomycin + clindamycin + cefepime** |
| **Empiric with CKD (avoid nephrotoxins)** | **Linezolid + meropenem** (MRSA, GAS, gram-neg, anaerobes) (✗ Wrong options: vanc + gent · vanc alone · gent alone · micafungin) |
| Strep / Clostridium | **Penicillin G** |
| Polymicrobial | **Imipenem / doripenem / meropenem** |
| MRSA | + **vancomycin or daptomycin** |

**Hyperbaric oxygen** — adjunct, esp. **anaerobes / Clostridia** (↑ leukocyte O₂ killing, perfusion, antibiotic uptake) · HBO + IVIG only after surgery\
✗ Wrong options: "contraindicated in Clostridia"

## 6 Complications

### Mortality predictors — time to surgery, vasopressors

**Mortality** — **\~30% (32%)**; **higher in older** patients; **amputation vs salvage — similar**\
✗ Wrong options: 15%

**Reduces mortality** — **shorter time from admission to surgery**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI findings · pressors · organism ID · location

**Delay > 24 h** — **↑ mortality (≈ 4×)**\
✗ Wrong options: always trial antibiotics · single debridement always enough · HBO useless · single-discipline team

**Strongest predictor of death** — **vasopressor requirement outside anaesthesia**\
✗ Wrong options: IVDU · cellulitis · ICU admission · debridement size (predicts final defect)

### Gas gangrene — clostridial toxins

**C. perfringens myonecrosis toxin (TO)** — **alpha toxin** (haemolysis, myonecrosis, gas gangrene)\
✗ Wrong options: beta · epsilon · iota

## X vs Y

| | **Type 1** | **Type 2** |
|---|---|---|
| Organisms | **Polymicrobial** | **Group A strep** |
| Host | **Diabetic / immunosuppressed** | **Healthy** |
| Frequency | **80–90%** | 5% |

| | **Necrotising fasciitis** | **Cellulitis** |
|---|---|---|
| Progression | Rapid despite antibiotics | Responds |
| Signs | **Bullae, pain out of proportion, shock** | Erythema |
| LRINEC | **≥ 6** | Low |
