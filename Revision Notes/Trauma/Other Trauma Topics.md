# Other Trauma Topics

*Trauma · 44 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Transfer to Level 1 trauma centre

**Associated with transfer** — **multiple medical comorbidities**\
✗ Wrong options: female · private insurance · Caucasian · ISS < 10

**Unnecessary transfers associated with** — **African-American race, comorbidities, Medicaid, male sex** · severe polytrauma → better outcomes at Level 1

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Associated Lisfranc injury

**Cuboid compression ("nutcracker") fracture** — twisting, midfoot swelling + ecchymosis → **look for subtle Lisfranc injury**\
✗ Wrong options: peroneal tear · lateral process talus · talar neck · deltoid

## 5 Management

### Common peroneal nerve palsy — tendon transfer

**Lacerated CPN, 1 yr, no anterior / lateral function, posterior compartments 5/5** — **split posterior tibial tendon transfer**\
✗ Wrong options: split anterior tibial (denervated) · gastrocsoleus recession (no contracture) · subtalar fusion (doesn't fix drop) · FHL transfer

### Nonunion — compression plating

**Hypertrophic forearm nonunion (1 yr)** — **compression plating alone** (stability problem, biology intact)\
✗ Wrong options: bracing · ICBG (atrophic) · reamed IMN (not used in forearm) · stimulator (atrophic adjunct)

### Masquelet induced-membrane technique

**Stage 1** — debride, **antibiotic cement spacer**, provisional fixation (ex-fix or internal) → 6 wk culture-specific antibiotics if infected, repeat stage 1 as needed

**Stage 2 at 4–6 wk** (peak **BMP, VEGF, IL-6**) — incise membrane, remove spacer, **autograft (RIA or ICBG — equivalent)**, close membrane, definitive fixation

**True** — **induced membrane prevents graft resorption**\
✗ Wrong options: large articular defects (use allograft / prosthesis) · ICBG > RIA · stage 2 at 8 wk · antibiotics disrupt membrane

**Uses** — tibia (original, 2000), femur, humerus, **forearm** (e.g. GSW defects)

## X vs Y

| | **Hypertrophic nonunion** | **Atrophic nonunion** |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | **Instability** | **Biology** |
| Treatment | **Compression plate** | Plate + **graft** ± stimulator |

## How it's asked

**"Cuboid nutcracker — problematic associated injury?"** → **Lisfranc injury**

**"CPN laceration, PTT 5/5?"** → **split posterior tibial tendon transfer**

**"Hypertrophic forearm nonunion?"** → **compression plating**

**"Masquelet — true?"** → **membrane prevents graft resorption**

**"Factor associated with Level 1 transfer?"** → **multiple medical comorbidities**
