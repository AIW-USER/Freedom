# Isolated Ulnar Shaft Fracture

*Trauma · 34 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rare** — **< 1% of upper-limb fractures** · men 10–20 yr, women > 60 yr

### Associated conditions

**Elbow / DRUJ** — **Monteggia**, **Essex-Lopresti** → image wrist + elbow

**Compartment syndrome** — up to **15%**

### Atypical (bisphosphonate-associated) fracture

**Pattern** — **isolated transverse proximal ⅓ ulna**, **thickened posterior cortex**, minimal comminution, prodromal ache → **alendronate**\
✗ Wrong options: teriparatide · semaglutide · levothyroxine · methotrexate

**Location** — **20–45% of ulnar length from olecranon tip** · ASBMR major / minor criteria (as for atypical subtrochanteric)

**Outcome** — **delayed / nonunion \~50%** → resect diseased bone + plate

### Anatomy

**Forearm axis** — **radial head (proximal) → ulnar fovea (distal)**; radius rotates around ulna; ulnar malangulation → ↓ rotation

**Interosseous membrane** — **5 ligaments**; **central band = key part to reconstruct** · accessory band, distal oblique bundle, proximal oblique cord, dorsal oblique accessory cord

![Interosseous membrane](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422969-interosseous-membrane.jpg)

## 2 Mechanism

**Direct blow ("nightstick")** · indirect: MVA, fall from height (axial load)

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — deformity, open wounds, **tense compartments**, **pain on passive finger stretch**; radial + ulnar pulses; median, radial, ulnar nerves

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **AP + lateral forearm** ± obliques + **ipsilateral wrist + elbow**

**Radial head aligned with capitellum on every view** — excludes Monteggia

| OTA (ulna shaft) | Subtypes |
|---|---|
| **A — simple** | A1 spiral · A2 oblique · A3 transverse |
| **B — wedge** | B2 intact · B3 fragmentary |
| **C — multifragmentary** | C2 intact segmental · C3 fragmentary |

![OTA type A ulnar shaft fractures](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422969-type-a.jpg)

## 5 Management

```
Isolated ulnar shaft fracture
├─ Distal ⅔, < 50% displacement + < 10° angulation → brace / Muenster cast / soft dressing
├─ Displaced distal ⅔ or any proximal ⅓ → ORIF (3.5 mm DCP, 6 cortices each side)
├─ Gustilo I–IIIA open → primary ORIF · IIIB → ex-fix
└─ Bone loss / nonunion → autograft, vascularised fibula, Masquelet
```

### Operative criteria

| Nonoperative | Operative |
|---|---|
| **Distal ⅔** (nightstick) | **Displaced distal ⅔** |
| **< 50% displacement** | **Proximal ⅓** |
| **< 10° angulation** | **Open** (I–IIIA primary ORIF; IIIB ex-fix) |
| e.g. **20% translation, < 5° angulation** | **Any adult radial shaft or both-bone fracture** (even undisplaced) |

### Nonoperative management — outcomes & acceptable displacement

**Minimally displaced** — **equivalent clinical outcomes** to surgery; **slower union**; union **> 92%**\
✗ Wrong options: long-arm cast needed · 20° rotation loss · wrist / elbow stiffness

**> 10° angulation or > 50% displacement** — **loss of forearm rotation** if treated nonop

**Options** — **functional brace**, **Muenster cast** (interosseous mould), **soft compression + immediate motion** — all similar; **above- vs below-elbow = no difference**; review at \~1 wk; ≥ 6 wk

![Muenster cast](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422969-muenster-cast.jpg)

### Operative technique

**ORIF** — **subcutaneous approach**; **3.5 mm DCP**; compression mode; **6 cortices** above + below; locking in osteoporosis; bridge if comminuted; 2.0 / 2.7 lag screws\
✗ Wrong options: **4.5 mm — refracture after removal**

**Post-op** — **early ROM**; **NWB \~6 wk** (secondary displacement)

**Ex-fix** — **Gustilo IIIB**; distal pins in **2nd + 3rd MC**, **≤ 4 mm** pins

**IMN** — **poor soft tissue**; through **posterior olecranon**; union ≈ ORIF; **7% nonunion** forearm nailing (Street)

**Grafting** — open fracture with bone loss (delayed) / nonunion; **autograft is critical** (ICBG, proximal tibia, **RIA**); **vascularised fibula — large defects, ↓ infection**

**Masquelet (induced membrane)** — stage 1: I&D + **cement spacer** + temporary fixation → stage 2: graft into membrane at **4–6 wk**

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| Infection | **3%** after ORIF; open fractures |
| **Compartment syndrome** | **Up to 15%**; crush, open, **low-velocity GSW**, vascular, **DIC** |
| **Nonunion** | **< 5%** compression plate · **up to 12%** bridge plating of comminution |
| Malunion | **Radial bow restoration ∝ function** |
| NV injury | **PIN with Monteggia**, type III open → **observe 3 months**, explore if no return |
| **Refracture** | **Up to 10%** with early removal; **remove ≥ 1 yr** (ulna, Orthobullets) · ≥ 18 months (Galeazzi note); 4.5 mm plates, comminution, lucency → **brace 6 wk + protect 3 months** after removal |

### Nonunion

| Type | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Atrophic** | **ORIF (3.5 mm plate) + autogenous cancellous graft** (✗ Wrong options: dynamic splint · graft alone · pinning · ultrasound) |
| Infected / atrophic with defect | **Masquelet** |
| Hypertrophic | **Debridement + compression plating** |

## X vs Y

| | **Isolated ulna (nightstick)** | **Radius shaft / both-bone (adult)** |
|---|---|---|
| Minimal displacement | **Nonop OK** | **ORIF always** |
| Risk if nonop | Little | Displacement, malunion, ↓ rotation |

| | **Nightstick** | **Atypical (bisphosphonate)** |
|---|---|---|
| Site | Distal ⅔ | **Proximal ⅓** |
| Pattern | Direct blow | **Transverse + thick posterior cortex**, prodrome |
| Union | > 92% | **\~50% delayed / nonunion** |

## How it's asked

**"Nonop vs operative — true?"** → **equivalent clinical outcomes**

**"Which forearm fracture can be nonop in adult?"** → **isolated mid-shaft ulna, 20% translation, < 5° angulation**

**"Atrophic nonunion after nonop at 1 year?"** → **ORIF + autogenous bone graft**

**"Proximal ⅓ transverse ulna + thick posterior cortex?"** → **alendronate**
