# Femoral Head Fractures

*Trauma · 19 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Rare** — **12% of hip dislocations**; rising (more MVAs, better resuscitation)

**With posterior dislocation** — **5–15%** (Orthobullets) · **\~15%** (TO) · **95% of head fractures accompany a dislocation (TO)**

### Blood supply

| Vessel | Fact |
|---|---|
| **MFCA** (from profunda) | **Main supply to weight-bearing head**; runs **posteromedially** → endangered by posterior approach |
| Artery of ligamentum teres (obturator or MFCA) | **10–15%**; perifoveal area |

![Femoral head blood supply](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob1036-blood-supply.jpg)

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanism** — impaction, avulsion, shear; **dashboard injury** (knee on dash, hip flexed + adducted + IR), falls, sport, industrial

**Posterior dislocation** — head **shears against posterior acetabular rim** → fracture (usually **anteromedial / anteroinferior** fragment)

**Anterior dislocation** — **impaction / indentation** fractures of head

**Associated** — femoral neck fracture, acetabular (posterior wall) fracture, **sciatic neurapraxia**, AVN, **ipsilateral knee ligament injury**

## 3 Presentation

**Posterior dislocation** — **flexed, adducted, IR**, shortened (little rotation with large wall fractures)

**Anterior dislocation** — flexed, **abducted, ER**

**Check** — **sciatic nerve**, ipsilateral knee stability

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use |
|---|---|
| AP pelvis + hip series (pre + post reduction) | — |
| Judet | Acetabular fracture |
| Inlet / outlet | Pelvic ring |
| **Post-reduction CT** | **Loose bodies / incarcerated fragments**, fragment size, plane, comminution, impaction, acetabulum |

### Pipkin classification

| Type | Definition | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Below fovea** (infrafoveal), **non-weight-bearing**, small | Nonop or **excision** (excision > ORIF) |
| **II** | **Above fovea** (suprafoveal), **weight-bearing**, larger; ligamentum stays on inferior fragment → may block reduction | ORIF if displaced (ORIF > excision) |
| **III** | I or II **+ femoral neck fracture** | **Highest AVN** → catastrophic ORIF failure |
| **IV** | I or II **+ acetabular fracture (usually posterior wall)** | Treat by acetabular injury |

![Pipkin classification](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob1036-pipkin.jpg)

### Chiron classification (TO)

| Type (TO) | Extent |
|---|---|
| 1 | Osteochondral injury |
| 2 | ¼ of head |
| 3 | ⅓ of head |
| 4 | ½ of head |
| 5 | **Cartilage impaction of weight-bearing dome** (superior collapse) |

**Subtypes (TO)** — **A** pure head · **B** + acetabulum · **C** + neck · **Brumback** — more prognostic than Pipkin (TO)

## 5 Management

```
Femoral head fracture
├─ Dislocated → reduce < 6 h → post-reduction CT
├─ Pipkin I, or II with < 1 mm step-off, stable, no fragments → TDWB 4–6 wk
├─ Pipkin II > 1 mm / loose bodies / irreducible → ORIF (anterior)
│      └─ Pipkin IV → TFO surgical dislocation (head + posterior wall)
└─ Pipkin III or older / comminuted / osteoporotic → arthroplasty
```

### Operative criteria

| Nonoperative | Operative |
|---|---|
| **Pipkin I** | **Pipkin II with > 1 mm step-off** |
| **Pipkin II with < 1 mm step-off** (Orthobullets, OB) · **< 2 mm** (TO) | **Loose bodies / incarcerated fragments** |
| No interposed fragments | **Pipkin III, IV** |
| **Stable, concentric hip** | Irreducible fracture-dislocation, polytrauma |
| — | **Older patients**, comminution, osteoporosis → **arthroplasty** |

**1 mm, not 2 mm** — femoral head cut-off differs from other articular fractures (OB)

**Strongest nonop indication on post-reduction CT** — **infrafoveal (Pipkin I) with congruent joint**\
✗ Wrong options: suprafoveal displaced · neck fracture · displaced acetabulum · incarcerated posterior wall fragment

**TO nonop indication (TO)** — **Pipkin II with 1 mm displacement**\
✗ Wrong options: Pipkin I with 3 mm · Pipkin III · nonconcentric reduction

**Displaced Pipkin II on CT (TO)** — **ORIF with Herbert (headless) screw**\
✗ Wrong options: nonop 6 wk

### Hip reduction

**Timing** — **within 6 h** → AVN **5–40%**, ↑ with delay

**Technique** — full sedation + relaxation; in-line traction, slight adduction, counter-pressure on pelvis; **no forceful manoeuvres** → **post-reduction CT**

**Nonop regimen** — **TDWB 4–6 wk**, avoid **adduction + IR**; serial X-rays

### Surgical approach

| Approach | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Smith-Petersen (anterior)** | **Internervous: superior gluteal ↔ femoral**; best view of **anteromedial** fragment; shorter, less blood loss, better reduction; **spares MFCA → lower AVN** (KL **3.2× AVN**, TO); **↑ functionally significant HO** |
| Watson-Jones (anterolateral) | TFL ↔ gluteus medius (both superior gluteal → intermuscular) |
| **Surgical hip dislocation + trochanteric flip osteotomy (TFO)** | **Whole head + acetabulum through one approach** (head + posterior wall); **preserves MFCA**; ↓ AVN + **↓ PTOA**; **↑ HO**; same function; **lateral position** |
| **Kocher-Langenbeck ± digastric (trochanteric) osteotomy** | Posterior wall (Pipkin IV); gluteus max split (no internervous plane); **plain KL → worse outcomes, more AVN** |

**Exposure** — **periacetabular capsulotomy** to protect head supply

**Pipkin IV — preserving blood supply** — **greater trochanteric osteotomy (surgical dislocation)**\
✗ Wrong options: strip gluteals off crest = extended iliofemoral · ligate LFCA ascending branches · detach piriformis (not needed) · supine (lateral position)

**TFO primary advantage** — **address all injury sites through one approach**\
✗ Wrong options: minimally invasive · minimal stripping · better union

**Irreducible posterior fracture-dislocation — TFO true?** — **lower PTOA**\
✗ Wrong options: more AVN · less HO · incomplete view · worse function

**Polytrauma, posterior dislocation + head fracture — definitive approach** — **Smith-Petersen**\
✗ Wrong options: Southern / posterior (MFCA) · Stoppa · KL · iliofemoral

**Pipkin IV with small posterior wall** — wall may be left; suprafoveal head via anterior approach

### Fixation & outcomes

**Fixation** — **≥ 2 lag screws 2.7 / 3.5 mm (mini-fragment) countersunk**, headless screws, bioabsorbable pins\
✗ Wrong options: 3.0 mm cannulated screws with washers → poor results

**Pipkin II ORIF — true?** — **mini-fragment screws suffice**\
✗ Wrong options: HO uncommon (\~15–20%) · KL optimal · excision better · 2 mm cut-off

**Post-op** — immediate ROM, **delay WB 6–8 wk**, quads + abductor strengthening; X-ray at 6 mo for AVN / OA

**Outcome** — mirrors associated injury (dislocation, neck fracture)

**Pipkin III ORIF in 51-yo — 6-mo outcome** — **fixation failure + AVN → THA**\
✗ Wrong options: posterior HO revision · pain-free · core decompression · advanced OA at 6 mo

### Arthroplasty

**Indications** — **older patients** with Pipkin I–IV displaced, osteoporotic, comminuted

**Facts** — immediate WB; **higher dislocation risk** than THA for OA; hemiarthroplasty if no acetabular fracture; **posterior approach best for posterior wall**

**Arthroscopy** — loose body removal

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Rate / fact |
|---|---|
| **HO** | **6–64%**; **anterior approach > posterior**; \~15–20% after fixation; **radiation** prophylaxis (esp. head injury) |
| **AVN** | **0–23%**; ↑ with **delayed reduction**; anterior approach **not** ↑ AVN |
| **Sciatic neurapraxia** | **10–23%**; **peroneal division**; **60–70% recover** |
| **DJD** | **8–75%** (incongruity, cartilage damage) |
| ↓ Internal rotation | Often not disabling |

## X vs Y

| | **Smith-Petersen** | **Kocher-Langenbeck** | **TFO surgical dislocation** |
|---|---|---|---|
| View | Anteromedial head | Posterior wall | **Whole head + acetabulum** |
| AVN | **Lower** | **3.2× higher** (TO) | Lower |
| HO | **Higher** | Lower | **Higher** |
| Best for | Pipkin I / II | PW only | **Pipkin IV** |

| | **Pipkin I** | **Pipkin II** |
|---|---|---|
| Location | **Infrafoveal** | **Suprafoveal (weight-bearing)** |
| Displaced → | **Excise** | **ORIF** |

## How it's asked

**"Nonop indication (TO)?"** → **Pipkin II with 1 mm displacement**

**"CT: displaced suprafoveal fragment (TO)?"** → **Pipkin II — ORIF with Herbert screw**

**"Which image is Pipkin II?"** → **suprafoveal fragment without neck / acetabulum fracture**

**"Strongest indication for nonop?"** → **Pipkin I, congruent**

**"Preserve blood supply in Pipkin IV?"** → **greater trochanteric osteotomy**

**"TFO primary advantage?"** → **all injuries through one approach**

**"Best definitive approach after posterior dislocation + head fracture?"** → **Smith-Petersen**

**"TFO — true?"** → **lower post-traumatic OA**

**"Pipkin II ORIF — true?"** → **mini-fragment screws sufficient**

**"Pipkin III ORIF at 6 months?"** → **failure + AVN → THA**

**"Reduce dislocation within?"** → **6 hours**

**"Sciatic palsy — division?"** → **peroneal; 60–70% recover**
