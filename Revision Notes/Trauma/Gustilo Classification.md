# Gustilo Classification

*Trauma · 28 / 67*

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Gustilo-Anderson classification

| | **I** | **II** | **IIIA** | **IIIB** | **IIIC** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Energy | Low | Moderate | High | High | High |
| **Wound** | **≤ 1 cm** | **1–10 cm** | Usually > 10 cm | Usually > 10 cm | Usually > 10 cm |
| Soft tissue | Minimal | Moderate | Extensive | Extensive | Extensive |
| Contamination | Clean | Moderate | Extensive | Extensive | Extensive |
| Comminution | Minimal | Moderate | Severe | Severe | Severe |
| **Periosteal stripping** | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Coverage** | Local | Local | **Local (incl. skin graft / primary closure)** | **Rotational or free flap** | Typically flap |
| **Vascular** | Normal | Normal | Normal | Normal | **Arterial injury requiring repair** |

### Grading rules

**Most accurate grading** — **intra-operatively** after debridement (definitive)

**Automatically ≥ IIIA** — **farm injuries** · **open femur** (energy needed) · **segmental fractures** · **high-velocity GSW**

**IIIA vs IIIB** — decided by **coverage**: **skin graft / primary closure = IIIA** · **flap (gastrocnemius, free) = IIIB** — **not wound size**

**IIIC** — **vascular repair needed**, regardless of soft tissue; ABI 0.6 without repair = **not IIIC**

**Not part of Gustilo** — **fasciotomy need**, closed fractures

## 5 Management

### Antibiotics by grade

| Grade | Antibiotic |
|---|---|
| **I + II** | **1st-generation cephalosporin (cefazolin)** |
| **III (A, B, C)** | **Cefazolin + aminoglycoside (gentamicin)** — gram-negative cover; **controversial** |
| **Farm injury / bowel contamination** | **+ Penicillin** (**Clostridium**, anaerobes) |
| **Fresh or salt water** | **Fluoroquinolone (ciprofloxacin)**; also for cephalosporin / clindamycin allergy |
| **Salt water** | **Doxycycline + 3rd / 4th-gen cephalosporin (ceftazidime)** |

### Timing

**Start** — **ASAP**; **infection ↑ if delayed > 3 h** from injury

**Duration** — **24–72 h after I&D**

**Tetanus booster** — if **none in last 5 years**

## X vs Y

| | **IIIA** | **IIIB** | **IIIC** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Key feature | Coverage possible locally | **Flap needed** | **Vascular repair needed** |
| Example | 8 cm avulsion **skin-grafted**; open femur closed primarily | **Gastrocnemius flap** for proximal tibia | **Pale foot after reduction** |

## How it's asked

**"Segmental tibia, 8 cm avulsion, skin graft?"** → **IIIA**\
✗ Wrong options: IIIB (no flap)

**"Which matches IIIB?"** → **proximal tibia with soft-tissue loss needing gastrocnemius flap**\
✗ Wrong options: pale foot = IIIC · 8 cm closed primarily = IIIA · fasciotomy not graded · closed pilon

**"Open femur, 3 cm wound, ABI 0.6, closed primarily?"** → **IIIA**

**"Most accurate grading?"** → **intra-operative**

**"Farm injury — add?"** → **penicillin**
