# SI Dislocation & Crescent Fractures

*Trauma · 56 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Spectrum

| Injury | Posterior SI ligaments | Stability |
|---|---|---|
| **Incomplete SI dislocation** | **Intact** | **Rotationally unstable** |
| **Complete SI dislocation** | **Ruptured** | **Rotationally + vertically unstable** |
| **Crescent fracture (SI fracture-dislocation)** | Posterior ilium stays attached to sacrum | Anterior ilium dislocates with **internal rotation** |

**Crescent fracture** — **iliac wing fracture entering the SI joint**; posterior iliac "crescent" stays with sacrum = **Young-Burgess LC-II**

### Anatomy

**SI stabilisers** — sacrospinous, sacrotuberous, anterior + **posterior SI ligaments**

**L5 root** — crosses sacral ala **\~2 cm medial to SI joint**

**Superior gluteal artery** — crosses SI joint, exits via **greater sciatic notch**

## 2 Mechanism

**Lateral compression**, high energy · iliac wing fractures may be **open** or trap **bowel**

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — haemodynamics, detailed neuro, abdominal distension, **rectal exam**, **blood at meatus**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP pelvis + **inlet / outlet** · **CT** — posterior ring + sacrum

**Classification** — within **Young-Burgess** (crescent = **LC-II**) and **Tile**

## 5 Management

| Injury | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Vertical translation | **Immediate skeletal traction** |
| **Incomplete SI + symphysis diastasis** | **Anterior ring ORIF** |
| **Complete SI dislocation** | **Anterior + posterior fixation** |
| **Crescent fracture** | **ORIF of ilium** (restores posterior ligament stability) |

**Approaches** — anterior (lateral window, elevate iliacus to SI) · **posterior** (crescent fragment to intact ilium) · plates + **iliosacral lag screws**

### Iliosacral screws — fluoroscopic views & L5 root injury

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Inlet** | **Anterior-posterior** position |
| **Outlet** | **Cephalad-caudad** position + **sacral neural foramina (best)** |
| **Lateral sacral** | Anterior alar cortex + nerve tunnel → **L5 safety** |

**In-out-in (too anterior) screw** — **L5 root → weak great toe extension / dorsiflexion (EHL)**\
✗ Wrong options: ankle DF (L4 TA) · plantarflexion (S1) · knee extension · bowel / bladder

**Positioning** — traction aids reduction; midline sacral bump

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **DVT** | **35–50%** |
| Neurological injury | L5 |
| Loss of reduction / fixation failure | — |

**Prognosis** — **accurate, stable SI joint reduction**

## How it's asked

**"Outlet view purpose during IS screws?"** → **sacral neural foramina**

**"In-out-in screw finding?"** → **no great toe extension**

**"Anterior alar penetration → weakness?"** → **great toe dorsiflexion**

**"Crescent fracture pattern?"** → **LC-II**
