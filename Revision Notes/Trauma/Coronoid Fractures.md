# Coronoid Fractures

*Trauma · 10 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Frequency** — **10–15% of elbow injuries**

**Meaning** — essentially **pathognomonic of an elbow instability episode**; isolated fractures less common than thought; usually with dislocation → recurrent instability

### Anatomy & biomechanics

**Coronoid** — **anterior buttress of greater sigmoid notch** → resists **posterior subluxation**; primary resistor of subluxation / dislocation

**Tip** — **intra-articular**; **nothing inserts on it** · **anterior capsule 6 mm** distal to tip\
✗ Wrong options: avulsion

**Anteromedial facet** — **varus stability**; critical weight-bearing ulnohumeral surface

**Sublime tubercle** — **anterior bundle of MUCL**, **18 mm** distal to tip → basal fractures amplify instability

**LUCL insertion** — **crista supinatoris** of proximal ulna

**MUCL origin** — anteroinferior **medial epicondyle**

## 2 Mechanism

**Shear** — distal humerus driven against coronoid during **severe varus stress or posterior subluxation**

| Mechanism | Fracture | Associated | Instability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Varus + posteromedial rotation** (pronation, axial load) | **Anteromedial facet** | **LCL avulsed from humerus**; **radial head + MCL spared** | **Varus PMRI** |
| **Valgus + supination (posterolateral)** | **Small tip (transverse)** | **Radial head fracture + LCL**, posterolateral dislocation | **PLRI / terrible triad** |
| Olecranon fracture-dislocation | **Large basal** fragment | Trans-olecranon / trans-ulnar basal | — |

**Anteromedial facet sequence** — **LCL fails first**, then medial coronoid compressed against / under medial trochlea

**Isolated coronoid fracture (no radial head fracture)** — think **varus PMRI**\
✗ Wrong options: PLRI · valgus anterolateral · posterior · anterior

**Trans-ulnar basal coronoid fracture-dislocation** — basal coronoid displaced from olecranon + metaphysis → **worse ROM, PROMs, more complications + reoperation**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain, swelling, deformity; forearm / wrist pain → associated injury

**PMRI clue** — **crepitus / "crispy rice cereal" + pain moving the elbow with arm abducted** (varus stress); history of dislocation, clunk, spontaneous reduction; LCL tenderness

**Gravity-assisted varus stress test** — arm **abducted 90°**, neutral rotation, flex + extend → pain, grinding, instability → **most sensitive / specific for PMRI**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral pivot shift · milking · chair rise · posterior drawer (PLRI / valgus tests)

**Exam** — varus / valgus stress (difficult but key), document ROM + crepitus, NV

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — AP + lateral; overlap makes reading hard

**PMRI signs** — **lateral joint widening, medial joint collapse** on AP · **crescent sign** on lateral (depressed anteromedial facet)

**CT** — **next step for anteromedial facet fracture** (size, comminution, articular extent)\
✗ Wrong options: EUA · traction views · immobilise 3 wk · immediate terrible-triad surgery

### Regan & Morrey classification (height)

| Type | Fragment |
|---|---|
| **I** | **Tip** |
| **II** | **≤ 50%** of height |
| **III** | **> 50%** of height |

### O'Driscoll classification (location)

| Type | Location | Association (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Tip** | **Terrible triad** (commonest) |
| **II** | **Anteromedial facet** | **Varus PMRI** |
| **III** | **Base** | **Olecranon / proximal ulna fracture-dislocation** |

| Anteromedial subtype (TO) | Involves |
|---|---|
| **1** | Anteromedial **rim** only |
| **2** | Rim + **tip** |
| **3** | Rim + **sublime tubercle** (± tip) → also **valgus instability** (MCL insertion) |

## 5 Management

```
Coronoid fracture
├─ Minimally displaced + stable elbow (any type) → brief splint, early ROM
├─ Anteromedial facet (PMRI) → medial approach ORIF + LCL repair
│      └─ still unstable → hinged ex-fix
└─ Terrible triad / olecranon fracture-dislocation → fix coronoid (lateral / posterior)
```

### Operative criteria

| Indication | Approach / fixation |
|---|---|
| **Minimally displaced, stable elbow** | **Nonop** — brief immobilisation, early ROM |
| **Type I–III with persistent instability** | **Medial ORIF** |
| **PMRI (anteromedial facet)** — most need surgery; residual subluxation / crepitus = dynamic incongruity | **Medial buttress plate / screws + LCL repair** (± sublime tubercle / MCL fix) |
| **Olecranon fracture-dislocation, terrible triad** | **Posterior approach** |
| Large fragments, poor bone, revisions | **Hinged external fixator** |

### Anteromedial facet – varus PMRI treatment

**Medial approach** — between **two heads of FCU**, or more anteriorly through **flexor-pronator split**

**Fixation by type** — **type I tip → cerclage / No. 5 suture through ulnar drill holes** · **II / III → retrograde cannulated screws or plate** · **anteromedial facet → buttress plate / pins + lateral ligament repair**

**Still unstable after coronoid ORIF** — repair **LUCL at crista supinatoris** · LUCL injured in **83% (15/18)**\
✗ Wrong options: MUCL origin · radial tuberosity = biceps · sublime tubercle (MCL rarely torn) · coronoid tip = capsule

**Splinting** — **LUCL torn (+ pivot shift) → forearm neutral** · **LUCL intact → full supination** (tightens medial side)

**Untreated / nonunion** — **varus laxity** persists → severe progressive arthritis\
✗ Wrong options: valgus laxity (MUCL intact) · milking · rotation block (radial head fine) · ulnar paraesthesia

**Rehab** — thermoplastic splint 90°, forearm neutral; **block terminal 30° extension 2–4 wk**; **avoid shoulder abduction 4–6 wk** (varus moment); early active motion (dynamic compression of joint)

### Terrible triad – coronoid tip & LCL

**Terrible triad** — **posterior / posterolateral dislocation + radial head fracture + coronoid (tip, transverse) fracture**; valgus + supination; MCL fails last if at all

**Coronoid tip fracture associated with** — **radial head fracture + posterolateral ulnohumeral dislocation**\
✗ Wrong options: PMRI · capitellum · trans-olecranon · MCL rupture

**Terrible triad, 50% coronoid, radial head replaced + LCL repaired → redislocates** — **coronoid not fixed (from lateral approach)** · fix via **ulnar bone-tunnel sutures or dorsal → volar screws**\
✗ Wrong options: immobilisation length / position (flexion + pronation stabilises lateral side) · medial buttress plate · MCL repair

### Olecranon fracture-dislocation

**Posterior approach** — mobilise olecranon fragment to reach coronoid → **fix coronoid first**, then ulna with dorsal plate

## 6 Complications

**Recurrent instability** (esp. medial) · **stiffness** · **PTOA** · **HO** · **early failure** = unrecognised / unrepaired instability

**Prognosis** — high complication + reoperation rates

## X vs Y

| | **Anteromedial facet (PMRI)** | **Tip (terrible triad, PLRI)** |
|---|---|---|
| Force | **Varus + posteromedial** | **Valgus + supination** |
| Radial head | **Intact** | **Fractured** |
| Ligament | **LCL (humeral avulsion)** | LCL ± MCL |
| Test | **Gravity-assisted varus stress** | Lateral pivot shift, chair rise, posterior drawer |
| Approach | **Medial** (buttress) + LCL repair | Lateral (± posterior) |

| | **Regan-Morrey** | **O'Driscoll** |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | **Height** (tip, ≤ 50%, > 50%) | **Location** (tip, anteromedial, base) |
| Strength | Simple | **Recognises anteromedial facet** |

## How it's asked

**"Dislocation, subtle coronoid, intact radial head, CT anteromedial — association?"** → **varus PMRI** (TO)

**"Anteromedial facet fracture, intact radial head — exam finding?"** → **varus PMRI**

**"Varus + posteromedial mechanism causes?"** → **anteromedial coronoid fracture**

**"Posteromedial instability 2 months after dislocation — fracture?"** → **anteromedial facet**

**"Isolated coronoid fracture — instability?"** → **varus posteromedial**

**"Mechanism for anteromedial facet?"** → **varus + posteromedial rotation**

**"Provocative test likely positive?"** → **gravity-assisted varus stress**

**"Valgus stress + milking positive, pivot shift negative, splinted supinated?"** → **PMRI with intact LUCL**

**"Injury most leading to PMRI?"** → **anteromedial facet fracture**

**"PMRI — fracture needing bony + soft-tissue fixation?"** → **anteromedial facet**

**"Nonunion of anteromedial facet — persistent sign?"** → **varus laxity**

**"Coronoid type in terrible triad?"** → **tip** (TO)

**"Terrible triad redislocates after RHA + LCL repair?"** → **coronoid not fixed (lateral approach)**

**"Coronoid tip on CT after valgus-supination fall — associated?"** → **radial head fracture + posterolateral dislocation**

**"Unstable after anteromedial facet ORIF — next insertion to address?"** → **crista supinatoris (LUCL)**

**"Anteromedial facet on X-ray — next step?"** → **CT**
