# Distal Radius Fractures

*Trauma · 14 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Commonest orthopaedic injury** — **17.5% of adult fractures**; lifetime risk **15% women, 2% men**; **F:M 2–3:1**

**Bimodal** — young high energy (MVC) · elderly low energy (FOOSH) = **fragility fracture → DEXA**

**Pattern** — **65% extra-articular**, 10% partial articular, 25% complete (Orthobullets) · **\~50% intra-articular** (OB)

**Predictor of later fractures** — osteoporosis, women > 50

**Associated soft-tissue injury (70%)** — **TFCC 40%**, **SL 30%**, **LT 15%** · DRUJ injury · radial styloid fracture (higher energy)

### Abbreviations

**Tendons / nerves** — EPL = extensor pollicis longus · EIP = extensor indicis proprius · FPL = flexor pollicis longus · EDC = extensor digitorum communis · PIN = posterior interosseous nerve · CTR = open carpal tunnel release

### Anatomy

**Load** — radius carries **80%** of axial load

| Column | Contents | Roles |
|---|---|---|
| **Radial** | Styloid (**volar** structure) + scaphoid fossa | BR insertion, **long radiolunate** + **radioscaphocapitate** (blocks ulnar carpal translation); buttress; ulnar-deviation load |
| **Intermediate** | **Lunate fossa** | Load transfer; **short radiolunate** origin (radiocarpal stability) |
| **Ulnar** | TFCC + distal ulna | DRUJ stability, rotation |

**Lister tubercle** — dorsal, \~1 cm above joint; **EPL just ulnar** → long volar screws rupture EPL

**Watershed line** — **most volar prominent rim** (lunate portion highest; short radiolunate) → **keep volar plate proximal**

**Volar-ulnar ("critical") corner / volar lunate facet** — radiolunate ligaments → loss → **volar carpal subluxation**

## 2 Mechanism

**FOOSH** (elderly) · MVC (young)

**Chauffeur (Hutchinson) (TO)** — **radial styloid** fracture from **scaphoid impacting styloid** (✗ ulnar styloid)

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — pain, swelling, **dinner-fork deformity** if displaced; median nerve check

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Measure | Normal | Acceptable (post-reduction) |
|---|---|---|
| **Radial height (PA)** | **13 mm** (Orthobullets) · **11 mm** (TO) | **< 5 mm shortening** |
| **Radial inclination (PA)** | **23°** (22° TO text) | **< 5° change** |
| **Articular step-off** | Congruous | **< 2 mm** |
| **Volar tilt (lateral)** | **11°** | **Dorsal angulation < 5° or within 20° of contralateral** (Orthobullets, TO) · **dorsal tilt < 10°** in healthy < 65 (OB) |
| Ulnar variance | Slightly negative | — |

**TO EXCEPT trap (TO)** — "**dorsal tilt up to 7°**" is **not** acceptable

**Radial length (TO)** — radial styloid \~1 cm distal to ulnar head

**CT** — articular involvement, planning · **MRI** — TFCC, SL (DISI), LT (VISI)

### Classifications & eponyms

**Systems** — **Fernandez** (mechanism) · **Frykman** (radiocarpal / DRUJ ± ulnar styloid) · **Melone** (4 intra-articular types by displacement) · **AO** (comprehensive, cumbersome)

| Eponym | Description |
|---|---|
| **Colles** | **Dorsally angulated, extra-articular** |
| **Smith** | **Volarly angulated, extra-articular** |
| **Volar Barton** | Radiocarpal fracture-dislocation with **volar lip** fragment |
| **Dorsal Barton** | Radiocarpal fracture-dislocation with **dorsal lip** fragment |
| **Chauffeur (Hutchinson)** | **Radial styloid** (transverse / oblique) |
| **Die-punch** | **Depressed lunate fossa** |

**Colles complications (TO)** — TFCC tear, DRUJ instability, CRPS, stiffness, **malunion (dinner fork)**, **EPL tear**, nonunion (✗ **gunstock deformity** — supracondylar humerus)

## 5 Management

```
Distal radius fracture
├─ Reduce + rigid splint → meets criteria → cast
├─ Unstable / > criteria, young → ORIF (volar locked plate)
│      └─ volar-ulnar corner fragment → fragment-specific fixation
├─ Elderly > 60–65 → closed treatment acceptable (function equal)
└─ Open / highly comminuted / unwell → ex-fix ± pins, or dorsal bridge plate
```

### Instability & indications for fixation

**LaFontaine instability predictors** — **radial shortening (most predictive)**, then **dorsal comminution**; dorsal comminution > 50%, palmar / intra-articular comminution, **dorsal angulation > 20°**, **initial displacement > 1 cm**, **shortening > 5 mm**, severe osteoporosis, associated ulnar fracture; **≥ 3 criteria → ↑ loss of reduction**

**Redisplacement after closed reduction** — **severity of initial displacement** (esp. shortening), age, metaphyseal comminution (✗ TFCC tear, ✗ open, ✗ radial head, ✗ time to reduction)

**Best stability predictor** — **pre-reduction radiographs**

**Repeat closed reduction** — **< 50%** satisfactory

| ORIF indications |
|---|
| **Dorsal angulation > 5° / > 20° vs contralateral** |
| **Intra-articular displacement > 2 mm** |
| **Radial shortening > 5 mm** |
| Volar or dorsal comminution · associated ulnar fracture (not styloid) · severe osteoporosis |
| **Barton (articular margin) fractures** · **Smith** (comminuted) · **die-punch** |
| Progressive loss of tilt / length in cast |

**Young, large dorsal articular fragment** — **ORIF** (✗ casts, ✗ ex-fix, ✗ CRPP)

**Young high-energy metaphyseal comminution** — **ORIF of radius** (locking resists shortening + dorsal angulation) (✗ cast, ✗ ex-fix + pins, ✗ styloid repair — only if DRUJ unstable / base displaced)

### Closed reduction & casting

**Technique** — anaesthesia, traction + volar / dorsal pressure; below-elbow cast / splint (**rigid / sugar tong**)

**Avoid Cotton-Loder position** — **extreme flexion + ulnar deviation** → **CTS**

**Within criteria (65-yo)** — **rigid splint + clinic follow-up** (✗ admit, ✗ ORIF, ✗ soft splint)

**75-yo dorsally angulated extra-articular — initial treatment** — **closed reduction + splint**

**Complications** — **acute CTS**, **EPL rupture**

### Elderly / osteoporotic – closed vs operative

**Meta-analyses** — **no functional difference** closed vs operative in **> 65** (Orthobullets) / **≥ 60** (OB); **radiographic** results better with surgery

**Osteoporotic, failed closed treatment** — **peri-articular locked plate**

**Volar comminution in 65-yo — contraindicated** — **Kapandji intrafocal pinning alone** (✗ cast, ✗ pinning + ex-fix, ✗ dorsal bridge plate, ✗ volar plate)

**Dorsal bridge plate** — ligamentotaxis for comminuted fractures; **polytrauma / elderly needing walker** → **immediate platform (front-wheel) walker** WB (\~22% BW) (✗ crutches \~50% BW, ✗ NWB wheelchair, ✗ no device) · must be removed

### CRPP

**Indication** — extra-articular with **stable volar cortex**; 82–90% good

**Techniques** — **Kapandji intrafocal** (pins lever fracture) · Rayhack (arthroscopic)

**Complications** — **radial sensory nerve injury**, **pin-tract infection**

### Volar plating & volar (Henry) approach

**Volar plating preferred** over dorsal; locked plates support subchondral bone

**Classic Henry** — between **BR and radial artery** (radial to artery) · **modified (FCR) Henry** — **ulnar (medial) to radial artery**, through FCR sheath (TO)

**Distal third (TO)** — FCR medial + radial artery lateral → **sweep FPL ulnarward with a finger, protecting palmar cutaneous branch** → **PQ incised along its RADIAL border** and elevated with periosteum

**TO FALSE statement (TO)** — "**PQ elevated from ulnar side**" (✗ true: FPL swept ulnarly, modified Henry medial to artery, Thompson more cosmetic for middle / distal-third shaft)

**Proximal third (TO)** — **full supination** moves PIN away; incise supinator medially · **middle third** — pronate, partially detach pronator teres

**Palmar cutaneous branch of median nerve** — leaves median **4–6 cm** above wrist crease, between median nerve + FCR → **thenar base hyperaesthesia** from retraction (✗ CRPS, ✗ Wartenberg, ✗ CTS, ✗ C7)

**Bone void** — **calcium phosphate** incorporates by \~1 yr (✗ calcium sulfate — resorbs 4–12 wk, ✗ TCP — osteolysis, ✗ OP-1, ✗ freeze-dried allograft — not synthetic)

### Volar lunate facet / volar-ulnar corner

**Volar-ulnar corner fragment** — → **volar carpal translation** (✗ die-punch, ✗ radial styloid — ulnar translation, ✗ extra-articular)

**Early failure 2 wk after volar plate** — **lunate facet fragment not supported** → **fragment-specific fixation** (small plates, TBW, K-wires) (✗ non-locking plate, ✗ volar tilt, ✗ styloid column, ✗ 3 shaft screws)

**Region risking radiocarpal instability if missed** — **volar lunate facet**

### Intra-operative fluoroscopic views

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Tilted lateral / radial inclination view (22–23° elevated)** | **Radiocarpal (intra-articular) screw penetration** |
| **Dorsal tangential (skyline)** — wrist max flexed, beam \~15° volar to radial axis | **Dorsal cortex screw penetration** |
| PA anatomic tilt (\~11°) | Articular congruity |
| Extended wrist tangential ("sigmoid notch") | DRUJ penetration |
| Standard lateral | Plate vs watershed |

**TO answer (TO)** — **dorsal tangential + radial incline view**

**Avoid extensor injury** — **pegs** for articular block, **capture \~75%** of volar–dorsal width

### External fixation

**Indications** — open, highly comminuted, unwell · **cannot reliably restore 10° palmar tilt alone** → add pins / plate

**Spanning** (small articular fragments) vs **non-spanning** (large fragments)

**Ligamentotaxis** — traction restores length + inclination; **volar translation of lunate restores volar tilt** (multiplanar, Agee)

**Rules** — open placement of radial pins (**superficial radial nerve** at "bare area" \~4 finger-breadths above styloid, dorsal to BR — **commonest nerve injured**); **avoid over-distraction (< 5 mm)**, flexion, ulnar deviation; **≤ 8 wk**; aggressive finger OT

**Pin care** — **daily showers + dry dressings**; chlorhexidine / peroxide **no better**; complication rate \~20%

**Ex-fix + K-wires vs volar plate** — **both give satisfactory results**; pin / wound complications **higher with ex-fix** (✗ tip styloid needs fixation, ✗ plate proximal to watershed ↑ flexor injury, ✗ pillar pain)

### DRUJ instability & ulnar styloid fractures

**Stable DRUJ after radius fixation + ulnar styloid fracture** — **no adverse effect without fixation**

**Shuck test positive after fixation** — **radioulnar ligaments of TFCC** (✗ thumb UCL, ✗ ulnolunate / ulnotriquetral)

**Shuck test** — elbow 90°, translate ulna sagittally in neutral, pronation, supination

**SL disruption seen intra-op** — **separate dorsal incision → repair dorsal SL ligament + K-wires** (✗ immobilise, ✗ pins alone, ✗ volar extension, ✗ arthroscopic) · results better than isolated SL injuries

### Rehabilitation & outcomes

**Formal therapy vs home programme** — **no long-term difference** (Wakefield-McQueen); **better DASH + ROM early (6 wk)** in women > 40 — improves early, not late PROMs

**DASH variability (nonop)** — **depression** (✗ neuroticism, ✗ occupation, ✗ handedness)

**Poor outcome** — workers' comp, ↓ SES, ↓ education, ↓ BMD

**Good outcome** — **articular reduction accuracy**, restored anatomy, early finger + wrist motion

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Median neuropathy / CTS** | **Commonest neurological**; **1–12% low energy, 30% high energy** (Orthobullets) · acute CTS **5.4–8.6%** (OB) |
| **EPL rupture** | Nondisplaced / nonop **0.3–5% (3–5%)**; volar plate with **proud dorsal screw** → **EIP transfer** |
| **FPL rupture** | **Volar plate distal to watershed** |
| Radiocarpal arthrosis | 2–30%; **90% of young adults with step-off > 1–2 mm** |
| Malunion / nonunion | Radial shortening → worst function |
| ECU / EDM entrapment, ulnar neuropathy | DRUJ injury |
| Compartment syndrome | — |
| **CRPS** | Vitamin C prophylaxis (AAOS) — later analyses show no benefit |

### Acute carpal tunnel syndrome

**Urgent release indications** — **progressive paraesthesia**, **weak thumb opposition**, paraesthesia not improving after reduction **> 24–48 h**

**Worsening numbness after reduction / CRPP / ORIF** — **urgent carpal tunnel release** (+ ORIF / pinning) (✗ bivalve + 1 wk, ✗ hand compartment pressures, ✗ NCS, ✗ EMG, ✗ plexus exploration, ✗ repeat closed reduction)

**Pre-op mild paraesthesia, 2PD 6 → > 10 mm day after volar plate** — **immediate CTR**

**Child SH II, worsening after bivalving** — **CTR + pinning**

**TO nuance (TO)** — **immediately after reduction in palmar flexion / ulnar deviation → ORIF + CTR (TO)** · **delayed CTS in cast → release cast + neutral wrist first (TO)**; CTR if persists · no prophylactic CTR

**Urgent-surgery sign on rounds** — **can't oppose thumb** (✗ can't extend thumb IP — EPL, ✗ initial displacement, ✗ humeral fracture, ✗ dorsal comminution)

**Mechanism** — **nerve compression** (✗ laceration, ✗ arterial, ✗ RSD)

### EPL rupture after nondisplaced fracture & EIP transfer

**Classic** — **non- / minimally displaced** fracture in cast → sudden painless loss of thumb extension at **7 wk–4 mo** (✗ PIN palsy, ✗ intersection, ✗ malunion, ✗ EPB)

**Cause** — **hypovascular zone in 3rd compartment**, attrition, callus / ischaemia (✗ displaced fragments)

**Most likely complication of nonop nondisplaced DRF** — **EPL rupture** (✗ CTS, ✗ FPL, ✗ abutment, ✗ arthritis)

**Treatment** — **EIP → EPL transfer** (✗ primary repair — attritional, ✗ EPB — too little excursion, ✗ ECRL, ✗ splinting) ± palmaris graft; pre-rupture → 3rd compartment release

**EIP** — **4th compartment** with EDC + **PIN** · EPL innervated by **PIN**

**Dorsal screw ulnar to Lister tubercle** — irritates **EDC** (4th) (✗ ECRL / ECRB — radial to Lister, ✗ ECU, ✗ EPB)

### FPL rupture – volar plate past watershed

**Risk** — plate **volar / distal to watershed (Soong) line**, prominent screws

**Soong grade** — **0** dorsal to critical line · **1** volar to line, proximal to rim · **2** on / distal to volar rim

**Flexor injury** — up to **12%** of volar plates; **FPL 57%**, FDP index next; mean **\~9 mo**

**Plate distal to watershed, asymptomatic at 2 wk** — **plan removal after union** to protect FPL

**FPL** — origin volar radius + IOM → **base of thumb distal phalanx** → rupture = **can't flex thumb IP**

### CRPS – vitamin C

**Dose** — **500 mg daily × 50 days** (✗ 200 mg, ✗ 1500 mg — no better, ✗ alendronate)

**Evidence** — AAOS 2010 / 2013 moderate support; subsequent analyses **no benefit** — read the options

### Malunion

**Nascent malunion at 2 mo in young drummer** — **acute corrective osteotomy** (easier, less structural graft) (✗ wrist fusion, ✗ USO / Darrach alone)

**Dorsal angulation > 10°** — stiffens IOM, ↓ rotation → opening-wedge osteotomy + graft

## X vs Y

| | **EPL rupture** | **FPL rupture** |
|---|---|---|
| Setting | **Nondisplaced, casted** · proud dorsal screw | **Volar plate distal to watershed** |
| Deficit | Can't extend thumb IP / "thumbs up" | **Can't flex thumb IP** |
| Fix | **EIP transfer** | Repair / graft / transfer + plate removal |

| | **Tilted lateral (22–23°)** | **Skyline** |
|---|---|---|
| Checks | **Joint penetration** | **Dorsal cortex penetration** |

| | **Classic Henry** | **Modified (FCR) Henry** |
|---|---|---|
| Interval | **BR ↔ radial artery** | **FCR ↔ radial artery** |
