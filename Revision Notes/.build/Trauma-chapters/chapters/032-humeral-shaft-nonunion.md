# Humeral Shaft Nonunion

*Trauma · 32 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — failure to heal by **6 months** (OB) · bone-stimulator criteria: **> 9 months**, or **no progress over 3 months**

**Primary nonunion** — **nonop 2–33%** (Orthobullets, OB) · 2–10% (OB) · **surgery 5–10%** · \~10% either (OB)

**Location** — **proximal ⅓** highest (pectoralis major + deltoid deforming forces; **biceps tendon interposition**)

| Risk factor | Type |
|---|---|
| **Vitamin D deficiency (commonest metabolic)**, osteoporosis | Biological |
| **Infection** (incl. **P. acnes**) | Biological |
| **Smoking**, obesity, malnutrition, **NSAIDs**, alcohol, noncompliance | Patient |
| **Open fracture**, segmental | Injury |
| **Unstable pattern**, inadequate fixation | Mechanical |
| **Shoulder / elbow stiffness** (motion → fracture site), GH arthritis | Mechanical |

**Not predictive** — radial nerve palsy, no callus at **3 weeks**, hand dominance

### Anatomy

**Nutrient artery** — **medial** aspect of mid–distal ⅓ diaphysis

![Nutrient artery of the humerus](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12767-nutrient-artery-of-humerus.jpg)

### Genes & lab markers

**Metabolic work-up — vitamin D deficiency** — **most common** correctable abnormality (✗ hypogonadism, ✗ thyroid, ✗ PTH, ✗ calcium, ✗ iron, ✗ magnesium) → correct before surgery

| Abnormality (nonunions, OB) | Attributed share |
|---|---|
| Calcium deficiency | 35% |
| Thyroid disorder | 24% |
| Central hypogonadism | 15% |
| Parathyroid disorder | 11% |

**Labs** — **CRP, ESR, CBC** (infection) · **total protein, albumin** · **vitamin D, TSH, PTH**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain with use; **gross motion** at fracture

**Exam** — brace fit, skin, atrophy, angulation; **radial nerve function**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use |
|---|---|
| AP + lateral humerus, shoulder, elbow | No consolidation, **hypertrophic callus**, **pseudarthrosis**, broken hardware |
| **CT** | **Extent of bridging**, planning; **not needed if X-ray already diagnostic** |

### Predictors of nonunion

**Most predictive** — **persistent fracture mobility at 6 weeks** (extremely sensitive + specific)

### Infection work-up

**Before any revision** — **ESR + CRP (labs)** (✗ CT, ✗ brace, ✗ revision without infection work-up) · **3–5 cultures** for atrophic · septic → **two-stage**

**Septic nonunion with bone loss** — resect + **antibiotic spacer**

**Types** — **hypertrophic, oligotrophic, atrophic** (atrophic ↑ with bone loss, open fracture, infection)

## 5 Management

```
Humeral shaft nonunion
├─ Rule out infection (ESR, CRP) + metabolic (vitamin D)
├─ Symptomatic → compression plate (4.5 mm) ± autograft (gold standard)
│      ├─ after nail → remove nail + compression plate + graft
│      └─ very proximal / distal, poor bone → dual plating
└─ Low demand, high risk, asymptomatic → brace ± stimulator
```

### Treatment options

| Option | Indication | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Brace ± **bone stimulator** | **Low demand, high risk, asymptomatic** | Stimulator ✗ if **pseudarthrosis, gap > 5 mm, poor blood supply** |
| **Compression plating + bone graft** | **Symptomatic nonunion — gold standard** | **Superior to IMN**; \~100% union; **ICBG donor morbidity 44%** |
| **Dual plating** (orthogonal) | **Very proximal / distal**, poor metaphyseal bone, micromotion after one plate | **92–100% union at 16 wk** |
| **Cortical strut allo- / autograft** (intramedullary or medial + lateral plate) | **Severe osteopenia**, bone loss, recalcitrant | **95–100%** union |
| **Free fibula** | **Recalcitrant atrophic** | — |
| Induced membrane / fibula / cancellous allograft | **Bone loss** | — |
| BMP | **Limited role** — no proven benefit | — |

**Prognosis** — **83–100%** union after surgery · plating ± graft or IMN + graft **> 85%**

### Compression plating with bone graft (after nail or brace)

**Technique** — anterior / anterolateral / **posterior (radial nerve neurolysis)**; debride fibrous tissue + bone ends; **maximal cortical contact**; **4.5 mm compression plate**

**Graft** — **ICBG autograft if atrophic** · DBM, **RIA**, local callus · **graft in hypertrophic — controversial**

**After failed IMN** — **nail removal + plate + graft**: **9/9 union vs exchange nailing 4/10 (McKee)** (✗ exchange nailing, ✗ dynamisation, ✗ MUA, ✗ nail removal + brace)

**Atrophic after nail, smoker** — **nail removal + compression plate + bone graft** (✗ augmentative plate — **hypertrophic only**, ✗ plate without graft, ✗ smoking cessation alone)

**After failed ORIF (screw pull-out), normal ESR / CRP** — **compression plating + autograft** (✗ spacer, ✗ ex-fix, ✗ brace, ✗ retain implants)

**Delayed union in brace at 16 wk (atrophic)** — **ORIF + autograft** (✗ stimulator + sling, ✗ IMN — nonunion + cuff damage, ✗ locked plate + allograft)

**Transverse, 6 months, gross motion** — **ORIF + autologous graft** (✗ ultrasound, ✗ bisphosphonates, ✗ nails)

## 6 Complications

### Postoperative nerve deficit with nonunion

**Commonest** — **radial nerve** palsy after nonunion surgery — **iatrogenic 7–19%**

| Procedure / pathology (OB) | Typical nerve palsy |
|---|---|
| **Humeral nonunion fixation** | **Radial** |
| Reverse TSA | Axillary |
| Hook of hamate fracture | Ulnar |
| Medial-entry supracondylar pins | Ulnar |
| Lunate dislocation | Median (acute CTS) |

## X vs Y

| | **Hypertrophic** | **Atrophic** |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | **Stability** | **Biology** |
| Treatment | Compression plate (± augment plate); graft controversial | **Compression plate + ICBG**; resect necrotic bone ± shorten |

| | **Plate + graft** | **Exchange nail** |
|---|---|---|
| Union after failed nail | **9/9** | 4/10 |
| Preferred | **Yes** | No (cuff damage) |

## How it's asked

**"Painful nonunion 13 months after IMN?"** → **nail removal + plate fixation**

**"Hypertrophic nonunion after nail, 9 months?"** → **nail removal, ORIF + bone graft**

**"Transverse, 6 months, gross motion?"** → **ORIF + autologous bone graft**

**"16 weeks in brace, gross motion?"** → **open plating + autograft**

**"Atrophic after nail, smoker?"** → **nail removal + compression plate + bone graft**

**"Nonunion after ORIF, normal markers?"** → **compression plating + autograft**

**"Metabolic cause of nonunion?"** → **vitamin D deficiency**

**"Broken screw 8 months after nonunion surgery — next?"** → **laboratory evaluation (ESR, CRP)**

**"Most predictive of nonunion?"** → **persistent mobility at 6 weeks**

**"Nerve palsy after humeral nonunion surgery?"** → **radial nerve**
