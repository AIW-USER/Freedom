# Galeazzi Fractures

*Trauma · 25 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & epidemiology

**Galeazzi** — **distal ⅓ radial shaft fracture + DRUJ injury**

**DRUJ instability by fracture level** — radial fracture **< 7.5 cm from articular surface → 55% unstable** · **> 7.5 cm → 6%** · closer = more unstable

### Anatomy

**Sigmoid notch** — shallow concavity on ulnar border of distal radius

**Primary DRUJ stabilisers** — **volar + dorsal radioulnar ligaments**

**DRUJ most stable in** — **supination**

![Distal radioulnar articulation](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob1029-shows-the-distal-radioulnar-articulation.jpg)

### Associated DRUJ ligament injury

**Most commonly injured** — **dorsal radioulnar ligaments** (✗ volar long radiolunate, ✗ RSC, ✗ ligament of Testut, ✗ SL)

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanism** — **FOOSH with forearm in pronation** · direct blow to dorsolateral wrist

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — point tenderness; test **pronation / supination for instability**; DRUJ stress → wrist / midline forearm pain

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP + lateral **forearm, elbow and wrist**

| Sign of DRUJ injury | View |
|---|---|
| **Widened DRUJ** | **AP** |
| **Dorsal or volar ulnar displacement** | **Lateral** |
| **Radial shortening ≥ 5 mm** | AP |
| Ulnar styloid fracture | AP |

### OTA classification (radius / ulna 22)

| Code | Description |
|---|---|
| **22-A2.3** | Simple radius fracture + DRUJ dislocation |
| 22-A3.3 | Simple both-bone (distal radius zone) + DRUJ dislocation |
| 22-B2.3 | Wedge radius + DRUJ dislocation |
| 22-B3.3 | Wedge both-bone + DRUJ dislocation |

## 5 Management

```
Galeazzi (adult) — fracture of necessity
├─ ORIF radius (volar Henry, anatomic plate, restore bow)
└─ Then assess DRUJ in supination
       ├─ stable → long-arm immobilisation in supination 6 wk
       ├─ reducible but unstable → cross-pin ulna to radius (4–6 wk)
       ├─ irreducible → open reduction (ECU interposed)
       └─ large ulnar styloid fragment → ORIF styloid + supination
```

### Treatment — radius ORIF then DRUJ assessment

**Indication for surgery** — **all adults**; **acute fixation far superior to late reconstruction**

**Radius** — **volar (Henry) approach**, **anatomic plating**, **restore radial bow**

**DRUJ assessment** — only **after rigid radius fixation**; gross ulnar motion in supination = unstable; **radius fixation alone does not guarantee DRUJ stability**

**Pinning** — **1–2 × 1.2 / 1.6 mm K-wires transversely, proximal to sigmoid notch** → **above-elbow cast in supination × 6 wk** (✗ pinning in **pronation**)

**Approach for DRUJ** — **dorsal capsulotomy**

### Irreducible DRUJ — ECU interposition

**Block to reduction after anatomic radius** — **ECU (extensor carpi ulnaris) tendon** (✗ median nerve, ✗ FCR, ✗ pronator quadratus, ✗ FCU)

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Compartment syndrome** | High-energy crush, open, vascular injury, coagulopathy; **pain on passive stretch most sensitive** |
| NV injury | Uncommon except **type III open** |
| **Refracture after plate removal** | Early removal, **large 4.5 mm plates**, comminution, persistent lucency → **remove ≥ 18 months** |
| Nonunion / malunion | — |
| **DRUJ subluxation** | Gravity, **pronator quadratus**, **brachioradialis** |

## X vs Y

| | **Galeazzi** | **Monteggia** | **Nightstick** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fracture | **Distal ⅓ radius** | **Proximal ulna** | Isolated ulnar shaft |
| Joint | **DRUJ** | **Radial head / PRUJ** | None |
| Fix | Plate radius → assess DRUJ | Plate ulna → radial head reduces | — |

| | **Rolando** | **Smith** |
|---|---|---|
| Fracture | Comminuted intra-articular **1st MC base** | Distal radius, **volar angulation** |

## How it's asked

**"Plate radius + evaluate DRUJ?"** → **Galeazzi** (✗ nightstick, ✗ Monteggia, ✗ Rolando, ✗ Smith)

**"Definitive management?"** → **ORIF radius → assess DRUJ → percutaneous pin if unstable** (✗ closed reduction + cast, ✗ PRUJ, ✗ palmaris graft — chronic only)

**"High-speed MVA, Galeazzi in adult?"** → **plate radius, closed reduction + evaluate DRUJ** (✗ open TFCC repair, ✗ fix both bones, ✗ pin in pronation)

**"Irreducible DRUJ after radius reduced?"** → **ECU**

**"Ligament most commonly injured?"** → **dorsal radioulnar ligaments**

**"DRUJ instability if fracture < 7.5 cm from joint?"** → **55%**
