# Foot Compartment Syndrome

*Trauma · 24 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & causes

**Pathophysiology** — trauma → bleeding + oedema → ↑ interstitial pressure → vascular occlusion → **myoneural ischaemia**

**Causes** — **fractures 69%** (**5–17% of calcaneus fractures**), **crush**, severe ankle sprain, GSW, tight casts / dressings, burns, post-ischaemic swelling, bleeding disorders, vascular injury

### Foot compartment anatomy

**Number** — **9** (Orthobullets, TO; controversial) · **8** (OB: lateral, medial, central, 4 interosseous)

| Compartment | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Medial** | **Abductor hallucis, flexor hallucis brevis** |
| **Lateral** | **Abductor + flexor digiti minimi brevis** |
| **Interosseous (× 4)** | **Interossei — each in its own compartment** |
| Central — **superficial** | **Flexor digitorum brevis** |
| Central — middle | **Quadratus plantae** |
| Central — **deep (calcaneal / adductor)** | **Adductor hallucis** + posterior tibial NV bundle |

**Trap (TO)** — "**adductor compartment = adductor hallucis + interossei**" = **wrong**; interossei form **4 separate** compartments

## 3 Presentation

**Symptom** — **pain out of proportion**

**Exam** — pain on **passive toe dorsiflexion (MTPJ)** (stretches intrinsics; may be absent in foot) · tense swollen foot · ↓ 2-point discrimination

**Pulses** — **present pulses do not exclude**; absent DP may be expected with severe foot trauma

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — rule out fracture only; **diagnosis is clinical ± pressures**

### Compartment pressure measurement & thresholds

**Indications** — polytrauma, obtunded / unreliable, **children**, equivocal exam (✗ needed when clinically unequivocal → straight to theatre)

| Compartment | Needle entry |
|---|---|
| **Medial** | **Base of 1st MT**, 2 cm into abductor hallucis |
| **Central** | **Base of 1st MT**, directed **lateral + plantar through abductor hallucis** (deeper than medial) |
| Interosseous | **2nd, 3rd, 4th web spaces**, plantar 2 cm through extensor fascia |
| Lateral | **Midshaft 5th MT**, 1 cm medial + plantar |

### Diagnostic criteria

| Fasciotomy if | Observe if |
|---|---|
| **Δp (diastolic − compartment) < 30 mmHg** | **Δp > 30 mmHg** |
| **Absolute 30–45 mmHg** (historic, controversial) | Exam not consistent |
| Clinical diagnosis | — |

**Intra-op** — anaesthesia lowers diastolic → **compare to pre-op diastolic**

## 5 Management

```
Suspected foot compartment syndrome
├─ Clinically unequivocal → emergent fasciotomy (no pressures)
├─ Equivocal / obtunded / child → measure pressures
│      ├─ Δp < 30 or 30–45 mmHg → fasciotomy
│      └─ Δp > 30, exam normal → observe
└─ Fasciotomy → delayed closure ± skin graft
```

### Fasciotomy technique

| Approach | Releases | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Dual dorsal (gold standard)** — **medial to 2nd MT** | 1st + 2nd interosseous, **medial**, **deep central** | Access to **Chopart, Lisfranc, TMT**; ✗ calcaneus fixation |
| Dual dorsal — **lateral to 4th MT** | 3rd + 4th interosseous, **lateral**, superficial + middle central | — |
| **+ Medial incision** | **Calcaneal compartment** | — |
| **Single medial** (along abductor hallucis in arch) | **All 9** | **Technically demanding** |

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Claw toes (fixed flexion)** | Intrinsic contracture → **release FDB + FDL at digits** |
| **Chronic pain + hypersensitivity** | Difficult to manage |

## X vs Y

| | **Dorsal incisions** | **Medial incision** |
|---|---|---|
| Access | All compartments + **midfoot fractures** | **All 9** / calcaneal |
| Downside | No calcaneus access | Technically challenging |

## How it's asked

**"Central compartment needle path + abnormal values?"** → **lateral + plantar through abductor hallucis under 1st MT base; 30–45 mmHg or Δp < 30** (✗ Δp > 30)

**"Foot compartments — EXCEPT (TO)?"** → **adductor compartment = adductor hallucis + interossei** (interossei are separate)

**"Superficial central compartment (TO)?"** → **flexor digitorum brevis**

**"Late complication?"** → **claw toes**
