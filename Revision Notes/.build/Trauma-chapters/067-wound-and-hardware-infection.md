# Wound & Hardware Infection

*Trauma · 67 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Commonest complication of orthopaedic trauma surgery** — **up to 16%** after fracture (Orthobullets) · ≥ 10% after severe injury, **< 2% after closed elective surgery** (TO) · SSI 11.8 / 100 patients (TO)

**Risk factors** — **host immunodeficiency**, extremes of age, diabetes, obesity, alcohol, **tobacco**, steroids, malnutrition, radiation, vascular insufficiency

### Biofilm — glycocalyx & quorum sensing

**Seeding** — soft-tissue, vessel + periosteal disruption lets bacteria evade defences · direct seeding of implant · haematogenous

**Biofilm** — depends on **exopolysaccharide glycocalyx** (✗ osteoclast differentiation — RANK-RANKL, ✗ metastasis, ✗ endochondral / intramembranous bone) · forms **within \~4 weeks** · resists antibiotics → chronic (> 4 wk) → **explant + antibiotics**

| Biofilm stage | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **1 Adhesion** | Individual bacteria attach to substrate (adhesins) |
| **2 Maturation** | **Quorum sensing** (cell-to-cell signalling) → **activates virulence-factor genes**, ↑ resistance (✗ creates planktonic bacteria, ✗ mediates adhesion, ✗ ↓ resistance) |

**Planktonic bacteria** — individual free-floating

### Shoulder surgery infection — C. acnes

**Indolent infection after arthroscopic acromioplasty + mini-open cuff repair** — **Propionibacterium (Cutibacterium) acnes** (✗ E. coli, ✗ Strep. viridans, ✗ Enterococcus) · arthroscopy fluid may dilute skin prep before mini-open

## 3 Presentation

**History** — soft-tissue + bone injury, hardware, previous surgery / infection at site

**Symptoms** — **pain at old fracture site** (infected nonunion) ± fever, chills, night sweats

**Exam** — erythema, **drainage / purulence**, tenderness · **gross motion = nonunion**

### Surgical site infection — definition & time window (TO)

**SSI (TO)** — infection **within 30 days** of surgery, or **within 1 year with an implant**, at incision or deep tissue

**NOT an SSI (TO)** — **no instrumentation, 3 months** (✗ implant at 3 or 6 months, ✗ no implant at 1 month — all are SSIs)

| SSI type (TO) | Features |
|---|---|
| **Superficial incisional** | Pus from superficial incision; pain, swelling, redness, heat |
| **Deep incisional / organ-space** | **30 d (1 yr with implant)** · pus from deep incision, **spontaneous dehiscence**, fever > 38 °C |

**HAI (TO)** — not present / incubating on admission; most appear **> 48 h** after admission · up to 7% (developed) / 10% (developing) of inpatients

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| **X-ray** (biplanar ± 45° obliques) | **Normal early** · subacute / chronic: **peri-implant lucency**, **involucrum** (reactive new bone), **sequestrum** (dead infected bone) |
| CT | Pre-operative planning |
| **MRI** | Extent, abscess · **98% sensitive, 78% specific** · metal artefact |
| **WBC-labelled scan** | **Infection around hardware** (MRI artefact) · distinguishes mimics |

### Labs & cultures

**WBC** — may be normal (chronic, indolent)

**ESR** — stays ↑ for **months** after injury / surgery without infection · normalises **slowly** — poor marker of cure · normal: men < 50 yr 0–15, women 0–20 mm/h · ↑ pregnancy, renal disease, cancer, anaemia · ↓ sickle cell, leukaemia, CHF, hyperviscosity

**Cultures** — **in-office swabs / sinus-tract aspirates unreliable** · **intra-operative deep cultures (multiple sites) most reliable**

### CRP / ESR kinetics after surgery & infection

**CRP after fracture fixation** — **peaks by 48 h (day 2), then falls** · **rising / not falling after 48 h → infection or haematoma** (✗ 24 h, ✗ 96 h, ✗ 7 d) · **most predictive marker in first post-op week** (> erythema, serous drainage, serial ESR)

**Cure after treated wound infection** — **normal CRP (< 3 mg/L)** even if ESR still raised (CRP 1, ESR 25 ✓) · CRP tracks clinical response

## 5 Management

```
Infection after fixation
├─ Early (< ~4 wk), deep, fracture unhealed, implant stable → I&D + retain implant + culture-specific antibiotics until union
├─ Unfit for surgery / unhealed, high surgical risk → antibiotic suppression (32% fail)
├─ Chronic (> 4 wk biofilm) or healed fracture → debride + remove hardware + antibiotics
└─ Loose / failed implant → remove, stabilise (ex-fix), staged revision
```

### Early postoperative infection — debridement with implant retention

**Purulent drainage probing to plate, 4 wk after ankle ORIF** — **I&D + keep fixation + culture-specific antibiotics until union** (✗ oral / IV suppression without surgery, ✗ remove implants + cast — instability, malunion / nonunion, ✗ office culture + suppression)

**Success** — **71%** for early acute post-op infection with debridement + antibiotics · **failure risk: IM nail, open fracture**

**Debridement** — any active infection · keep hardware if removal threatens stability · thorough excision · deep bone samples for **culture + histology**

**Irrigation** — **low-pressure normal saline** may be best during debridement (Orthobullets) · **povidone-iodine before closure; saline not recommended** for SSI prevention (TO, WHO) — read the options

**Suppression** — when surgical risk > benefit (immunosuppressed, elderly) or **infected unhealed fracture** · monitor ESR + CRP · **32% persist / worsen**

### SSI prevention (TO, WHO)

| Measure | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Skin prep | **Chlorhexidine-alcohol**, concentric circles outward |
| Hair | **Don't remove**; if needed **clippers**, never shave |
| **Antibiotic prophylaxis** | **Within 120 min before incision (ideally 30–60 min)** |
| Temperature | **Warm** — hypothermia < 36 °C; start ≥ 30 min before induction (NICE) |
| Glucose / fluids | Tight perioperative glucose · goal-directed normovolaemia |
| Drapes | Woven or non-woven; **plastic adhesive incise drapes not recommended** |
| **Laminar flow** | **Not** recommended for arthroplasty SSI prevention |
| Wound | Sterile dressing **48 h**; advanced dressings not better · drains out when indicated |
| Elective surgery | **Postpone** if remote-site infection |

## X vs Y

| | **CRP** | **ESR** |
|---|---|---|
| Post-op | **Peaks 48 h, then falls** | Raised for **months** |
| Early infection | **Most predictive (week 1)** | Poor |
| Cure | **Normalises with response** | Lags, less specific |

| | **Involucrum** | **Sequestrum** |
|---|---|---|
| What | **Reactive new bone** around infection | **Dead infected bone** (nidus) |
| Treatment | Preserve | **Excise** |

## How it's asked

**"When should CRP start to fall after ORIF?"** → **48 hours**

**"Infection cured — which labs?"** → **CRP 1 mg/L, ESR 25 mm/h**

**"Exopolysaccharide glycocalyx?"** → **biofilm creation**

**"Quorum sensing role?"** → **activates virulence-factor genes**

**"Deep infection 4 wk after ankle ORIF?"** → **I&D, retain fixation, culture-specific antibiotics until union**

**"Indolent infection after cuff repair?"** → **Propionibacterium acnes**

**"Not an SSI (TO)?"** → **no instrumentation, 3 months**
