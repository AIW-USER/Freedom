# Talus Fracture (other than neck)

*Trauma · 61 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **< 1% of all fractures** · talus = **2nd commonest tarsal fracture** (after calcaneus)

| Site | Share of talus fractures |
|---|---|
| Neck | Commonest (\~50%) |
| **Body** | **13–23%** |
| **Lateral process** | **10.4%** |
| **Head** | **Least common** |

**Mechanism** — body: high energy, hindfoot supinated or pronated · lateral process: see below

### Anatomy

**Talus** — **5 articular surfaces**, **\~70% cartilage**, **no muscle / tendon attachments** → direct extraosseous blood supply (artery of tarsal canal main; deltoid artery to body)

**Lateral process** — articulates with **posterior facet of calcaneus** + **lateral malleolus** (lateral margin of talofibular joint) · "lateral facet of calcaneus" (OB) — ✗ medial facet

**Posterior process** — **medial + lateral tubercles** separated by **FHL groove** · **os trigonum** in up to **50%** — don't call it a fracture

## 3 Presentation

**Lateral process** — anterolateral ankle pain after snowboarding, **misdiagnosed as ankle sprain**

**Posterolateral tubercle** — pain with **FHL flexion / extension**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Fact |
|---|---|
| AP + lateral | **Lateral process seen on AP**; X-rays may be **falsely negative** |
| Canale view | Talar neck (max equinus, 15° pronation, 75° cephalad) |
| **CT** | **Best for posterior, lateral, posteromedial process**; displacement, comminution, congruity; when suspicion high + X-rays negative |
| **MRI** | Confirms diagnosis when X-rays negative |

**Snowboarder, negative X-rays, can't bear weight — next** — **MRI** (OB) (✗ cast, ✗ bone scan, ✗ injection, ✗ repeat X-rays) · **CT** when suspicion high (Orthobullets) — read the options

### Lateral process fracture (snowboarder's fracture)

**Snowboarder's ankle (TO)** — **lateral process of talus fracture** (✗ ATFL avulsion, ✗ PTFL avulsion, ✗ medial process + deltoid) · **15× likelier** in snowboarders; **⅓ of their ankle fractures**

**Mechanism** — **dorsiflexion + axial load + eversion + external rotation** (Orthobullets, TO) · **inversion + ER** (OB) — read the options

**Subtalar arthritis** — **45%** regardless of treatment = **most common complication** · prognosis good with prompt treatment

**Excising a 1 cm fragment** — **lateral talocalcaneal ligament incompetent** (✗ bifurcate, ✗ peroneal retinaculum, ✗ arcuate, ✗ PTFL) · **tolerated — no ankle / subtalar instability** (✗ 5 mm)

## 5 Management

### Operative criteria

| Fracture | Nonop (**< 2 mm**) | Operative (**> 2 mm**) | Excise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lateral process** | **SLC 6 wk**, moulded to **support longitudinal arch** | **ORIF / K-wires** (lateral approach) | **Comminuted** |
| Posterior process | SLC 6 wk | ORIF posteromedial (± posterior / medial malleolar osteotomy) | **Comminuted, nonunion** |
| Head | SLC 6 wk | ORIF | — |
| **Body** | SLC 6 wk | **ORIF — combined medial + lateral** ± malleolar osteotomy | — |

**Displaced, non-comminuted, intra-articular lateral process** — **ORIF** (✗ excision, ✗ brace + PT, ✗ hard-soled shoe, ✗ cast — that is < 2 mm) · **4 mm displaced → ORIF, not cast**

**Comminuted intra-articular, failed nonop** — **fragment excision**

| Approach | Use | Interval / risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Lateral** | Lateral process | Over **tarsal sinus**, reflect EDB distally |
| **Posteromedial** | Medial tubercle / whole posterior process displaced medially | **FDL ↔ NV bundle** |
| **Posterolateral** | Lateral tubercle | **Peroneals ↔ Achilles** · **sural nerve** · NV bundle **medial to FHL** |

### Talar body fracture — articular reduction

**Improves long-term outcome** — **primary bone healing at the articular surface** (anatomic reduction, **absolute stability**) (✗ surgery < 48 h, ✗ minimally invasive, ✗ early WB, ✗ relative stability)

### Open talus fracture — staged management

**Diabetic (HbA1c 10.1), contaminated open neck fracture + body extrusion** — **urgent (< 24 h) I&D + ankle-spanning ex-fix, delayed definitive fixation** (✗ discharge + delayed single stage, ✗ Steinmann pins definitive, ✗ acute medial plating, ✗ acute TTC nail)

**Timing** — acute definitive fixation of open fractures → **up to 75% early complications** (dehiscence, skin necrosis, infection) vs **2–10% delayed** · definitive at **1–3 wk** when swelling settles

**Talus cartilage** — **> 50% articular** → limited vascular inflow · **quality of reduction = most important outcome predictor**

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **AVN** | **Hawkins sign (lucency) = revascularisation** · **sclerosis without Hawkins sign = AVN** |
| **Subtalar arthritis** | **45%** after lateral process fracture · anatomic reduction ↓ |
| **Talonavicular arthritis** | → **TN arthrodesis** |
| Malunion | — |
| Symptomatic nonunion | Pain **up to 2 yr** · posterior process → excise |

## X vs Y

| | **Shepherd fracture** | **CeDell fracture** |
|---|---|---|
| Site | **Lateral tubercle**, posterior process | **Medial tubercle** |
| Ligament | Posterior talofibular | Posterior talotibial / deltoid |
| Frequency | Commoner | Less frequent |
| Approach | Posterolateral | Posteromedial |

| | **Lateral process fracture** | **Ankle sprain** |
|---|---|---|
| Clue | Snowboarder, dorsiflexion + ER | Inversion |
| X-ray | **AP view**; may be negative | Normal |
| Next if negative | **MRI / CT** | — |

## How it's asked

**"Snowboarder's ankle (TO)?"** → **lateral process of talus fracture**

**"Snowboarder, negative X-rays — next?"** → **MRI ankle**

**"Excising 1 cm fragment → incompetent?"** → **lateral talocalcaneal ligament**

**"Displaced non-comminuted lateral process — diagnosis + treatment?"** → **lateral process fracture → ORIF**

**"Displaced lateral process fracture — treatment?"** → **operative fixation**

**"Lateral process fracture — true?"** → **subtalar arthritis commonest regardless of treatment**

**"Talar body — improves outcome?"** → **primary bone healing at articular surface**

**"Open contaminated talus, diabetic?"** → **I&D + spanning ex-fix, delayed fixation**

**"Shepherd fracture (TO)?"** → **lateral part of posterior process**
