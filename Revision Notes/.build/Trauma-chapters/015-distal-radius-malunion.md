# Distal Radius Malunion

*Trauma · 15 / 67*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Most common complication of distal radius fractures**

**Rates** — **17% overall** · **11% after operative** · **24% after definitive casting**

**Risk factors** — **post-reduction malalignment**, **osteoporosis**

### Anatomy – three columns

**Distal radius** — carries **80% of wrist axial load**

| Column | Contents | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Radial** | Radial styloid, **scaphoid fossa** | Buttress vs radiocarpal translation; load in ulnar deviation; anchors **radioscaphocapitate** (vs ulnar translation) |
| **Intermediate** | **Lunate fossa** | Main load transfer carpus → forearm |
| **Ulnar** | TFCC + distal ulna | DRUJ stability |

### Biomechanics

| Deformity | Effect |
|---|---|
| **↓ volar tilt (dorsal tilt)** | **Limits flexion + supination** |
| **↑ volar tilt** | **Limits extension + pronation** |
| **↓ radial inclination** | ↓ flexor mechanical advantage → **hand weakness** |
| **Radial shortening** | **↑ positive ulnar variance**; load shifts to ulnocarpal joint; **largest change in DRUJ kinematics** |
| Volar tilt change | DRUJ incongruity → IOM tightens → ↓ rotation + flexion–extension arc |

**Volar-angulated malunion (OB stem)** — **↓ supination + extension**, **dorsal subluxation of radius in supination** (OB) vs ↑ volar tilt limits extension + pronation (Orthobullets) — read the options

**Dorsal malunion carpal instability** — (1) **adaptive midcarpal DISI** · (2) **dorsal radiocarpal subluxation** with maintained midcarpal alignment

## 2 Mechanism

**How** — initial displacement · **secondary loss of reduction in cast** · failed fixation

| Pattern | Note |
|---|---|
| **Dorsally angulated (apex volar)** | **Most common**; DISI-like collapse |
| Volarly angulated (apex dorsal) | — |
| Radial shortening | DRUJ kinematics |
| Intra-articular | With **initial intra-articular displacement > 2 mm** |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain (localise radial vs ulnar side → guides ulnar procedures), **grip weakness**, ↓ motion with **pronation / supination worst**

**Exam** — deformity, tenderness map, ROM all planes, **bilateral grip dynamometry**, **DRUJ shuck test** (pain with instability), Allen test, CTS, CRPS

**Late DRUJ instability in adult (longshoreman)** — from untreated **childhood distal radius fracture** malunion (✗ supracondylar — cubitus varus / valgus, ✗ medial epicondyle, ✗ both-bone forearm — DRUJ preserved, ✗ elbow dislocation)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Measure | Normal |
|---|---|
| **Radial height (PA)** | **8–14 mm** |
| **Radial inclination (PA)** | **21–25°** |
| Articular step-off | Congruous |
| **Volar tilt (lateral)** | **7–15°** |
| **Ulnar variance** | Neutral (< 1 mm change) |

![Radiographic parameters of the distal radius](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob423135-radiographic-parameters-with-appropriate-measurements-obtain.jpg)

**Views** — **bilateral** PA, lateral, oblique · **anatomic (tilted) lateral** for lunate facet

**CT** — **intra-articular malunion**, axial rotation, **DRUJ instability (> 50% translation vs other side)**

**MRI** — TFCC

## 5 Management

```
Symptomatic distal radius malunion
├─ Low demand / CRPS / can't comply → therapy, NSAIDs, splint
├─ No arthritis → corrective osteotomy ± graft + plate
│      └─ ulnar positive with good radial alignment → ulnar procedure
├─ Arthritis → salvage (wrist fusion, etc.)
└─ Pain only, low demand, good function → PIN / AIN neurectomy
```

### Corrective osteotomy for symptomatic malunion

**Indications** — symptomatic after failed casting or failed fixation with **mechanical symptoms, ↓ grip, ↓ ROM, instability on stress**; **Fernandez**: active patient, extra-articular **> 25–30° angulation**, **no degenerative change**

**Ballet dancer, dorsal angulation, joint space preserved** — **corrective osteotomy** (✗ wrist arthrodesis, ✗ PRC, ✗ SLAC procedures, ✗ interposition) · arthritis → salvage

**Nonop outcomes** — **age 18–65 → more adverse outcomes** when untreated

| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Dorsal (2nd–4th compartments)** | Easy osteotomy; **tension-side** fixation; for dorsal subluxation | **Extensor tendon** irritation |
| **Volar (extended FCR)** | Align volar cortex → stability, **may avoid graft**; for volar subluxation; volar malunions | **Flexor tendon** irritation |

| Osteotomy | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Opening wedge** | **Needs bone graft** |
| **Closing wedge** | Bone contact + compression; **↑ ulnar variance → often needs ulnar shortening** |
| Intra-articular | Arthroscopy-aided; outcomes ≈ extra-articular osteotomy; **push-pull screw** restores height |

**Graft** — **structural bicortical ICBG** → union \~7 wk, better supination · **cancellous distal radius ≈ structural ICBG** · allograft, DBM, calcium phosphate options

**Complication rate** — extra-articular correction **\~50%**; **higher with distraction osteotomy**

### Denervation

**PIN ± AIN neurectomy** — debilitating pain + infection, low demand, preserved function; dorsal incision proximal to DRUJ; PIN on radial floor of **4th compartment**; AIN after dividing IOM (1 cm excised)

### Ulnar-sided procedures

**Indications** — **ulnar-positive with preserved radial alignment**, DRUJ instability, TFCC tear, styloid nonunion

| Procedure | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Ulnar shortening osteotomy (Milch)** | **Isolated radial shortening**; **✗ DRUJ arthrosis** |
| **Wafer (Feldon)** | **Intact TFCC**; angular deformities |
| **Hemiresection-interposition (Bowers)** | **Intact TFCC**; DRUJ arthrosis / instability |
| **Sauvé-Kapandji** | **Younger**, incongruity + degeneration; keeps rotation |
| **Darrach** | Salvage; don't over-resect |
| Ulnar head / DRUJ arthroplasty | Salvage |
| **One-bone forearm** | **Ultimate salvage**; no rotation |

**TFCC** — central perforations usually settle; irreparable + unstable DRUJ → **Adams reconstruction (palmaris graft)** or BR wrap; confirm reparability arthroscopically

**Styloid nonunion** — small + stable → **excise** · large + unstable → **repair** (headless screw, TBW, or excise + foveal TFCC repair)

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Tendon irritation / rupture** | **20%** of complications; **EPL commonest**; rupture \~**10 wk**; risk: **distraction osteotomy**, dorsal callus, volar plate, dorsal osteophyte, prominent hardware |
| Others | Nerve injury, CRPS, nonunion, infection, PTOA |

## X vs Y

| | **↓ volar tilt (dorsal malunion)** | **↑ volar tilt (volar malunion)** |
|---|---|---|
| Lost motion | **Flexion + supination** | **Extension + pronation** (Orthobullets) |
| Carpus | DISI / dorsal subluxation | — |

| | **Opening wedge** | **Closing wedge** |
|---|---|---|
| Graft | **Needed** | Not needed |
| Ulnar variance | Restores length | **↑ positive → ulnar shortening** |

## How it's asked

**"Dancer 11 mo after casting, dorsal angulation, no arthritis?"** → **distal radius corrective osteotomy**

**"Adult with DRUJ instability from childhood injury?"** → **distal radius fracture (malunion)**

**"Commonest malunion direction?"** → **dorsal angulation (apex volar)**

**"Deformity with biggest DRUJ kinematic change?"** → **radial shortening**

**"↓ radial inclination causes?"** → **grip weakness**

**"Ulnar shortening contraindicated with?"** → **DRUJ arthrosis**

**"Commonest tendon complication?"** → **EPL rupture (\~10 wk)**
