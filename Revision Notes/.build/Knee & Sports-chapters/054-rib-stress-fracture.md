# Rib Stress Fracture

*Knee & Sports · 54 / 61*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **uncommon** stress fracture site · elite > non-elite rowers · sweep (1 oar) = sculler (2 oars) (OB)

| Site | Where | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **First rib** | **Anterolateral** · weak point = **subclavian artery groove** | **Baseball pitching**, basketball, weightlifting, ballet |
| **Middle ribs 4–9** | **Lateral + anterolateral** | **Competitive rowers** |
| Rowing (OB) | **Ribs 4–8 (or 5–9)**, anterolateral / lateral | Rowers |
| Middle ribs 4th–9th (TO) | — | **Rowers** — **serratus anterior** traction |
| Cough (OB) | **Anterior → posterior axillary line, ribs 5–9** | Chronic cough |
| **Posteromedial ribs** | — | **Novice golfers** |

**Risk factors** — **amenorrhoea**, osteopenia / osteoporosis, extreme overuse, **repetitive coughing** · OB: low BMD, **poor technique + ↑ training volume**, low calcium, low vitamin D, eating disorders, **low testosterone**, **depot medroxyprogesterone**

**Rowing triggers (OB)** — **bigger blade** (↑ load per stroke) · ↑ volume incl. **long endurance rows at low stroke rate**

## 2 Mechanism

**Repetitive contraction** — stress on rib from attached muscle · **muscles strengthen faster than bone** during training

**Muscle fatigue** — ↓ ability to absorb + dissipate opposing forces

**First rib** — **scalenes pull superiorly** vs **serratus anterior + intercostals pull inferiorly** → fails at subclavian groove

**Rowers (OB, Karlson)** — deforming forces of **serratus anterior + external oblique** (attach ribs 5–9)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **insidious** pain · ↑ with **coughing, deep inspiration, overhead activity** · acute "**snap**" = completion of fatigued bone (throwing, batting, lifting)

**Exam** — **focal tenderness over the rib** · palpable **callus** when advanced

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use / finding |
|---|---|
| **AP chest X-ray** | **Negative in up to 60%** of rib fractures (any cause) · may show line or callus |
| Bone scan | X-ray negative + suspicion · ↑ activity · **rowing and cough fractures look identical** |
| CT | Suspected **pathologic** fracture · localises uptake at **costotransverse** region · clear fracture pattern |
| **MRI** | X-ray negative + suspicion · **no radiation** · **used more than bone scan in athletes** · marrow oedema ± line |

**Inspiration / expiration films** — for **pneumothorax**, which is **not associated** (✗)

![Bone scan — rib stress fracture](../MCQ/attachments/q4718-a-fig-a.jpg)

## 5 Management

**Nonop (majority, heal uneventfully)** — **rest, analgesia, stop inciting activity**, correct training errors / mechanics

**Duration conflict** — **\~4–6 weeks** (Orthobullets) · **1–2 weeks off rowing**, then **slow return** at low intensity, pain-free (OB) — read the options

**Rower, cough-aggravated chest pain, hot rib on bone scan** — **rest, analgesia, slow return to rowing** (✗ inspiration / expiration X-rays, ✗ operative stabilisation — only if recalcitrant, ✗ scan differs from cough fracture, ✗ **more** protraction / retraction)

**Stroke modification (OB)** — **less scapular protraction at catch + less retraction at finish** (less reach + pull-through) · proposed, **not clinically evaluated**

## 6 Complications

**Non-union** — the listed complication

## How it's asked

**"Olympic rower, bigger blade, rib uptake on bone scan — correct?"** → **rest, analgesia, slow return to rowing**
