# Osteonecrosis of the Knee

*Knee & Sports · 36 / 61*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Who** — **women : men 3 : 1** · **women < 55 yr with risk factors** · found **after knee arthroscopy in middle-aged women**

**Distribution** — usually **> 1 compartment** or even **metaphysis** · **80% bilateral** · **multifocal** common

**Prognosis** — **self-limiting** condition (Orthobullets)

### Risk factors & pathophysiology

| Group | Risk factors |
|---|---|
| **Classic** | **Alcoholism**, **steroids** (endogenous or exogenous), **sickle cell**, **hypercoagulable states**, **trauma** |
| Dysbaric | **Decompression sickness ("the bends")** |
| Marrow-replacing | **Gaucher disease** |
| Systemic | **SLE**, inflammatory bowel disease, transplant patient |
| Infective / drugs | Viruses (**CMV, hepatitis, HIV**, rubella, rubeola, varicella), **protease inhibitors** (HIV) |

**Mechanism** — ↓ blood flow to bone secondary to risk factors

**Spontaneous osteonecrosis (SONK)** — likely a **subchondral insufficiency fracture** · also linked to **meniscal root tear**

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — **with weight bearing**, especially **sitting → standing**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| X-ray (first line) | AP, lateral, Merchant · **wedge-shaped lesion** · diagnostic only in **advanced** disease |
| **MRI** | **Most useful** · **highest sensitivity + specificity** · needed for **early / subclinical** disease |
| MRI signal | **T1 dark** · **T2 bright (marrow oedema)** |

![MRI of osteonecrosis of the distal femur](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3025-mri-colorado-moved.jpg)

### Differential diagnosis

| Condition | Distinguishing fact |
|---|---|
| **Osteochondritis dissecans** | **Lateral aspect of MFC**, males **15–20 yr** (this note) · **10–15 yr**, median 13 (OCD chapter) — read the options |
| **Transient osteoporosis** | **Young–middle-aged men**; **multiple joints in 40%** (transient migratory osteoporosis) |
| Occult fracture / bone bruise | Trauma, weak bone, overuse |
| **SONK** | Subchondral insufficiency fracture; meniscal root tear |

## 5 Management

```
Osteonecrosis of the knee
├─ All → NONOP FIRST (NSAIDs, limited WB, quads, activity modification)
├─ Small unstable fragments → ARTHROSCOPIC REMOVAL
├─ Extra-articular lesion → CORE DECOMPRESSION
├─ Large symptomatic lesion, young, failed nonop → OSTEOCHONDRAL ALLOGRAFT
└─ Large area / collapse / multiple compartments → TKA
```

**Nonop outcome** — favourable, but **less so than nonop management of SONK**

**Overall** — observation + **treat the underlying systemic condition** · surgery for **advanced disease with severe subchondral collapse**

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| Diagnostic arthroscopy | **Remove small, unstable fragments** |
| **Core decompression** | **Extra-articular lesions** |
| **Osteochondral allograft** | **Large symptomatic lesions, younger patients, failed nonop** |
| **TKA** | **Large area of involvement**, **collapse**, **multiple compartments** |

### Juvenile OCD — activity modification

**12½ yr, stable small JOCD, untreated** — **activity modification** + limited WB until pain resolves (✗ arthroscopic stability check, ✗ transarticular drilling with 0.045 K-wire, ✗ excision + microfracture, ✗ mosaicplasty)

**Sport cessation** — **4–6 months** may allow healing

**Surgery for JOCD** — **unstable** lesions · no radiographic healing + **still symptomatic after 6 months** nonop · **approaching skeletal maturity** · loose body → fixation, drilling or regenerative procedure (depends on subchondral sclerosis)

**Stable in situ lesion failing nonop** — **transarticular or retroarticular drilling** → good results

**Excision alone** — **poor at 5 yr**; microfracture benefit unclear · symptomatic after excision + microfracture → **mosaicplasty**

**JOCD vs adult** — better healing potential, but **up to 50% fail to heal nonop** (Wall et al.) · 50–75% heal without fragmentation (Orthobullets, OCD chapter)

## X vs Y

| | **Osteonecrosis (risk-factor)** | **SONK** |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | **Risk factors** (steroids, alcohol, sickle cell, hypercoagulable) | **Subchondral insufficiency fracture** ± meniscal root tear |
| Extent | **Multiple compartments, metaphysis, 80% bilateral, multifocal** | — |
| Nonop result | Favourable | **Better** |

## How it's asked

**"Stable small JOCD, 12½ yr, untreated — initial?"** → **activity modification** (✗ drilling, ✗ excision, ✗ mosaicplasty)

**"Most sensitive / specific imaging?"** → **MRI (T1 dark, T2 bright)**

**"Extra-articular lesion — surgery?"** → **core decompression**

**"Collapse or multicompartment disease?"** → **TKA**
