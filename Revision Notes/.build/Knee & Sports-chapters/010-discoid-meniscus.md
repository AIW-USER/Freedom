# Discoid Meniscus

*Knee & Sports · 10 / 61*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — abnormal development → **hypertrophic, disc-shaped meniscus**

**Incidence** — **3–5%** of the population · highest in **Japanese + Korean** populations (TO)

**Side** — **lateral** ≫ medial (✗ medial commonest) · **bilateral in 25%** (Orthobullets, OB) · 25–50% (OB) — read the options (✗ > 75%) · examine the other knee

**Commonest cause** — of **symptomatic clicking / clunking in a child's knee**

**Discoid medial (TO)** — much rarer · disc-shaped · linked with **trauma / tears**

### Aetiology & pathology

**Aetiology** — **failure of apoptosis in utero** (Orthobullets, OB) · the normal meniscus develops from a cartilaginous anlage and **is never discoid** during development (OB)

**Aetiology traps** — ✗ **COL5A1** (female ACL rupture) · ✗ landing biomechanics, ✗ quadriceps dominance (ACL) · ✗ softening of cartilage with intact surface (OCD)

**Tissue** — **disorganised type I collagen** — fewer fibres, heterogeneous course, lost continuity (Atay, Papadopoulos) · TO: ↓ collagen density, **mucinous change**, ↑ thickness, enlarged surface, **↓ peripheral vascularity**

**Pathology traps** — ✗ incomplete ossification of ACL tibial insertion (tibial eminence fracture) · ✗ patellar ossification variant (bipartite patella) · ✗ proximal tibial dyschondrosis (Blount) · ✗ cartilage sleeve–ossific nucleus separation (patellar sleeve)

**Why it tears** — **↑ thickness**, **poor tissue quality**, **instability / lack of peripheral attachments**, ↓ vascularity

**Tear site + pattern** — most tears **posterior or middle** (Kramer) · commonest pattern **horizontal** (TO) · OB cases: longitudinal LM tear, radial tear

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain, clicking, **mechanical locking** · often symptomatic in **adolescence** · most children **asymptomatic**

**Snapping knee syndrome** — commonest presentation of a symptomatic discoid · classic for the **unstable (Wrisberg) variant** · clunk going **flexion → full extension** · TO: "meniscal thud" at **terminal extension (10–20°)**

**Age (TO)** — **3–4 y**: often painless snapping · **8–10 y**: pain with activity, more acute

**Exam** — mechanical symptoms **most pronounced in extension** · **loss of extension** (lacks 10°, ROM 10–85°) · lateral fullness, lateral joint line tenderness

**Painless clunk at 5–6 y, no trauma** — **discoid lateral meniscus (abnormal meniscal morphology)** (✗ ACL agenesis — hypoplastic LFC, shallow notch, absent tibial spine · ✗ medial plica — older athletes · ✗ grade IV chondral flap — rare < 9 y · ✗ PVNS · ✗ osteomyelitis — systemically ill)

**12 y, snapping, lacks 10° extension, wide lateral joint** — **discoid meniscus** (✗ OCD, ✗ medial plica — medial snapping, ✗ Osgood–Schlatter — tibial tubercle, ✗ Sinding-Larsen–Johansson — inferior pole patella)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| Radiographs (AP + lateral) | **Normal in most children** · severe discoid: **widened lateral joint space (up to 11 mm)**, **squaring of lateral femoral condyle**, **cupping of lateral tibial plateau**, **hypoplastic lateral intercondylar spine** |
| **MRI** — study of choice, most sensitive | **≥ 3 consecutive 5-mm sagittal slices with meniscal continuity ("bow-tie" sign)** — pathognomonic |
| MRI sagittal / coronal | Thick, flat meniscus across the **whole lateral compartment** · ↑ lateral meniscus-to-tibia width ratio · tear in symptomatic cases |
| MRI pitfalls (TO) | **Vacuum phenomenon** mimics a tear in a discoid · **Wrisberg type mimics a floating meniscus** |

![Discoid lateral meniscus — widened lateral joint, squared lateral condyle](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3007-ap-radiograph-of-the-knee-showing-widening-of-the-lateral-jo.jpg)

**Most likely X-ray sign** — **squaring of the lateral femoral condyle** (though radiographs are most often normal)

**X-ray traps** — ✗ joint-space **narrowing** · ✗ cupping of the **condyle** · ✗ squaring of the **plateau** · ✗ **hyperplastic** spine · ✗ shallow notch (ACL) · ✗ Segond (ACL) · ✗ patella alta · ✗ chondrocalcinosis (CPPD) · ✗ tricompartmental OA

| Imaging finding | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| **≥ 3 consecutive sagittal bow ties** | **Discoid meniscus** |
| Double anterior horn · double PCL | **Bucket-handle tear** (double PCL usually medial) |
| T2-bright cyst within meniscus | **Meniscal cyst** |
| High T2 / PD signal in patellar cartilage | **Chondromalacia patellae** |

### Diagnostic criteria

**MRI criterion** — continuity of anterior + posterior horns on **≥ 3 consecutive 5-mm sagittal images**

### Watanabe classification

| Type | Shape / coverage | Posterior attachment | Key fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I Complete** | Disc / block — **entire** lateral plateau | Normal | **Most common** (OB) · instability commoner in complete + younger |
| **II Incomplete** | Semilunar — **≤ 80%** (OB) · < 80% / up to 80% (TO) | Normal | |
| **III Wrisberg** | Near-normal size + shape | **No meniscotibial (coronary) attachment — only Wrisberg MFL** | **Least common**, hypermobile → **snapping knee** · needs **stabilization** |

![Watanabe classification](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3007-watanabe-classification.jpg)

**Frequency order** — complete > incomplete > Wrisberg (OB) · TO: complete + incomplete commoner, Wrisberg least common

**Watanabe (1969)** — arthroscopic appearance · **plateau coverage + posterior attachment** (TO) · ✗ "classified by Wrisberg" (TO)

**Neuschwander (TO)** — Wrisberg type = "**lateral meniscal variant with absence of the posterior coronary ligament**" (not truly discoid)

**Wrisberg type (TO)** — occurs **younger**, **no trauma**

**Instability (Klingele)** — **28% peripheral rim instability** · **anterior horn / anterior third commonest** > posterior horn > body · complete detachment least common (✗ instability uncommon) · Good: tears + instability each **77%**

**Tears at surgery (Klingele)** — **69% (69.5%)**

## 5 Management

### Algorithms

```
Discoid meniscus (OB / Orthobullets)
├─ Asymptomatic, no tear (incl. incidental at arthroscopy) → observe / do nothing
├─ Pain, mechanical symptoms, tear → arthroscopic saucerization ± repair
│      └─ Unstable / detached rim (Wrisberg) → saucerization + stabilization (repair)
└─ Total meniscectomy (open or arthroscopic) → avoid — early OA
```

```
Discoid lateral meniscus (TO)
├─ Asymptomatic → nonop, close follow-up
├─ Child (usually complete), mechanical symptoms → partial meniscectomy ± repair
├─ Adult, complete, mechanical symptoms → partial meniscectomy ± repair
└─ Adult, incomplete
       ├─ Pain ± mechanical symptoms → partial meniscectomy ± repair
       └─ Clicking only → nonop, close follow-up
```

### Observation — asymptomatic discoid

**Painless clicking, 8 y, incomplete, no tear** — **observation only** (✗ diagnostic arthroscopy, ✗ saucerization, ✗ + microfracture, ✗ + stabilization)

**Direct blow, 8 y, asymptomatic discoid on MRI, normal X-ray** — **observation** (✗ debridement + repair, ✗ ligament reconstruction, ✗ cast at 45°, ✗ immobiliser + NWB 6 wk)

**Incidental, untorn discoid at arthroscopy, 38 y with medial pain** — **do nothing surgically** to the lateral meniscus (✗ saucerization, ✗ meniscectomy, ✗ repair, ✗ microfracture) · not treated prophylactically

### Operative criteria

| Operate (saucerize ± repair) | Do not operate |
|---|---|
| **Persistent pain**, recurrent **locking**, swelling | **Asymptomatic** discoid without tear |
| **Mechanical symptoms / loss of extension** | Incidental finding at arthroscopy |
| **Meniscal tear** or **meniscal detachment** | Painless clicking only (TO: adult incomplete) |

### Saucerization ± repair / stabilization

**Indication for saucerization** — **symptomatic meniscal tear** (✗ asymptomatic abnormal shape, ✗ valgus instability, ✗ varus malalignment, ✗ hyperextension)

**11 y, 6 months snapping, 10° extension loss** — **arthroscopic saucerization ± meniscal repair** (✗ PT strengthening, ✗ NWB 6 wk, ✗ **open lateral meniscectomy — historical**, ✗ microfracture)

**9 y, partial central tear of stable discoid** — **arthroscopic saucerization** (partial meniscectomy) (✗ total lateral meniscectomy — poor long-term results, ✗ transplant, ✗ repair of central tear, ✗ cast 6 wk) · repair only for peripheral instability

**13 y, torn discoid, effusion, failed 4 wk PT** — **arthroscopic stabilization (repair vs partial meniscectomy) + saucerization** (✗ hinged brace, ✗ meniscectomy alone — premature OA, ✗ open saucerization)

**Watanabe I with radial tear, locking, 14 y** — **partial meniscectomy ± repair** (✗ observation, ✗ repair only — leaves discoid tissue to re-tear, ✗ complete meniscectomy, ✗ transplant — viable tissue present)

**Technique** — debride to an **anatomic-looking meniscus** · **repair if detached** (Wrisberg) · saucerization = remove central disc, keep peripheral crescent

**Rim** — saucerize to a **6–8 mm peripheral rim** — keeps function, avoids re-tear of larger remnants

**Saucerization alone vs with repair** — **comparable** ROM, pain, complications (✗ repair inferior) · Carter: stabilization doesn't change outcomes or complications

**Small knees** — **all-inside repair difficult** in young children (small knee, **deep posterior perforation** risk)

### Outcomes

**Saucerization** — successful in **93% (28 / 30 knees)**; rest needed complete meniscectomy for complex tears (Good) · full flexion > 135°

| Long-term degenerative change (OB) | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Arthroscopic saucerization** | **~20%** |
| **Partial meniscectomy** | **~40%** |
| **Complete meniscectomy** | **~90%** |

**Lee (< 15 y)** — partial meniscectomy → better radiographic results / less OA than subtotal / total (✗ similar degenerative change)

### Total meniscectomy — TO view vs OB view

**TO** — **< 16 y: total meniscectomy may give the longest symptom-free period** · **Wrisberg type → total meniscectomy** (subtotal leaves an unstable rim) or saucerization + suture rim to capsule · stable complete / incomplete → defer unless grooving / chondromalacia · hypermobile medial segment, intact periphery → subtotal meniscectomy / saucerization

**Conflict** — TO above vs OB / Orthobullets: **avoid complete meniscectomy** (~90% degenerative change); **repair / stabilize** a detached Wrisberg rim with saucerization — read the options

## X vs Y

| | **Complete / incomplete** | **Wrisberg type** |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | **Disc** / semilunar | **Near normal** |
| Posterior attachment | **Present** | **Absent** (Wrisberg MFL only) |
| Stability | Stable; symptomatic when torn | **Hypermobile** |
| Presentation | Asymptomatic until tear | **Snapping knee**, younger, no trauma |
| Treatment | Saucerize ± repair if symptomatic | Saucerize + **stabilize** (OB) · total meniscectomy (TO) |
