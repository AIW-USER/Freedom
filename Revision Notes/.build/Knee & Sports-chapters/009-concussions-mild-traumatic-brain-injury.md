# Concussions (Mild Traumatic Brain Injury)

*Knee & Sports · 9 / 61*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & epidemiology

**Definition** — subset of **mild TBI** → **acute transient impairment of neurologic function** 2° to an **impulsive force transmitted to the head** (blow to head, face, neck or elsewhere)

**Functional, not structural** — **standard CT / MRI normal** in the vast majority (✗ MRI often diagnostic)

**LOC** — **not required** for diagnosis · occurs in only **\~10%**

**Incidence** — **1.6–3.8 million** sports concussions / yr (Orthobullets) · "nearly 4 million" (OB) · **5–9% of all sports injuries** · \~6% of NCAA football injuries · rising (awareness, youth)

**Who / where** — **TBI = leading cause of sport-related death** · **American football** = majority · **competition > practice**

### Pathophysiology — neurometabolic cascade

| Step | Clinical result |
|---|---|
| Abnormal **neuronal ionic flux** | Headache, **photophobia, phonophobia** |
| Altered neurotransmitter release (**glutamate**) | Impaired cognition, **amnesia**, slowed reaction time |
| **Energy depletion** (correcting imbalance) | **Vulnerable to second injury (second-hit)** |

**After concussion** — **re-injury risk significantly ↑** (biochemistry altered, neuroprotection disrupted) (✗ risk same as pre-injury)

### Risk factors & prevention (helmets, prior concussion)

| Risk factor | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Prior concussion** | **Strongest** · **2–8×** (Orthobullets) · **2–6×** (OB) — read the options |
| **Female** | Higher risk than males (✗ male gender) — possibly oestrogen / cerebral blood flow |
| **Age < 18 (younger)** | Less "cognitive reserve" (✗ increasing age) |
| **Player-to-player contact** | **Football, wrestling, soccer, basketball** highest |
| Mood / learning disorders, migraine, anxiety / depression | **Complicate diagnosis + recovery** — not proven to predispose |
| Equipment quality | **No relationship established** (✗) |

**Helmets / headgear** — ↓ impact force · ↓ head + neck injuries in **youth hockey, alpine, equestrian, cycling, motor sports**

**Equestrian injuries** — **helmets** = greatest opportunity (✗ knee pads, wrist guards, boots, quick-release stirrups)

**Risk compensation** — protective gear may ↑ injury by enabling dangerous technique

**Rule changes** — work when one clear mechanism · **upper limb-to-head contact banned in American football (\~50% of concussions)** · red cards for high elbows (soccer)

**Preparticipation evaluation** — number of prior concussions, symptoms, recovery length · **low-impact injury + disproportionate symptoms → particularly vulnerable athlete**

### Genes & lab markers

**Apolipoprotein E** — genetic test under research (prevention)

**CTE proteins** — **tau**, **beta-amyloid**, **TDP-43** (after repetitive diffuse axonal injury)

### Consensus guidelines (International Conference on Concussion in Sport)

**Meets every 4 years** — consensus statement · 1st 2001 · **grading removed 2004** · **all classification systems removed 2008** · latest focus = assessment tools + **timing of return to play**

**SCAT origin (TO)** — **2nd conference, Prague 2004**

**Zurich 2008 (3rd)** — **no same-day RTP even if symptoms resolve** · applies to **minimal- and noncontact** athletes · applies to **children ≥ 10 yr** (younger → age-appropriate checklists) · young elite athletes → more conservative · **CT contributes little** to early diagnosis (use only if structural lesion suspected) · multidisciplinary approach

## 3 Presentation

### Symptoms & mandatory signs

**Headache** — **most common symptom — 70%** · myofascial tension (posterior neck, base of skull) vs post-traumatic (forehead / vertex pressure)

**Somatic** — dizziness, balance problems, **nausea / vomiting**, vision change, **diplopia**, **tinnitus**, photophobia, phonophobia

**Cognitive** — **"in a fog"** / slowed, poor concentration, forgetful

**Emotional** — **lability**, irritability, sadness · **sleep** — insomnia, drowsiness, more or less sleep

**Mandatory signs** — **LOC (\~10%)**, **lying motionless > 5 s**, slow to get up, **confusion / disorientation**, clutching head, **amnesia**, **vacant look / blank stare**, motor incoordination, **ataxia / gait instability** → further evaluation ± removal (sport-specific)

**Cervical muscle tenderness** — **not a concussion sign** → C-spine evaluation, but no 5–10-day concussion protocol (✓ EXCEPT answer)

**Handball player faints, plays on, progressively aggressive, can't recall last goal (TO)** — concussion → **immediately remove from play area** + **Maddocks score** (✗ counsel 15 min, ✗ electrolyte drink, ✗ "plays best aggressive")

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Sideline evaluation

**Primary survey** — ABC · **altered mental status → assume C-spine injury** (collar + board) · **never leave player alone**

**Secondary survey** — **remove from field** + immediate **cognitive evaluation by licensed healthcare provider** · no provider → remove entirely + urgent physician referral

**Diagnosis** — **clinical judgement = gold standard** · no tool diagnoses concussion alone

### Red flags — skull base fracture, diffuse axonal injury & emergent imaging

| Red flag → ER for advanced imaging | Not a reason for transport |
|---|---|
| **Deteriorating mental status**, restless, agitated, combative | **Transient unilateral UE numbness** resolving (stinger) |
| **Severe / worsening headache** | Headache alone, normal cognition |
| **Focal deficit** — unequal pupils, abnormal EOM, motor / sensory | Mild headache + shoulder pain + **prior concussion** |
| **Neck pain / tenderness** (C-spine) | **Photophobia + dizziness < 15 min**, no focal / cognitive signs |
| **Bloody otorrhea**, **mastoid ecchymosis** (skull fracture), **blepharohematoma** (eyelid haematoma) | |
| GCS < 15, LOC, amnesia / confusion (OB list) | |

**LOC + bloody discharge from external auditory meatus** — skull fracture → **transfer to ER for advanced imaging** (✗ SCAT5, ✗ graduated RTP, ✗ practice tomorrow)

**Mastoid ecchymosis + eyelid haematoma, no LOC** — **emergent transport** to ED

**Diffuse axonal injury (severe closed head injury)** — **no return to football / contact sports** (✗ return after 6 mo, 1 yr, 2 yr asymptomatic)

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Fact |
|---|---|
| Imaging overall | **Usually unnecessary** · indicated for red flags above, or **subacute / chronic trauma with persistent symptoms** |
| **CT head** | **Most used acutely** · normal in vast majority · shows fracture, haemorrhage, contusion, mass effect, herniation |
| **MRI brain** | Superior detail · **DWI most sensitive to shear injury** · standard MRI normal |
| **Functional MRI** | **↑ cerebrovascular reactivity** in acute post-concussive period |
| Negative CT | **Does not clear RTP** · not diagnostic of concussion |

**Most sensitive test for deficits after mTBI** — **neuropsychologic testing** (✗ head CT, ✗ MRI, ✗ skull radiographs, ✗ sideline assessment) · its RTP role not fully defined

### Assessment tools — SCAT

| SCAT fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Use | **Standardised tool, athletes ≥ 13 yr** · **Child SCAT for ≤ 12 yr (5–12)** (TO) |
| SCAT5 on-field | **Red flags, observable signs, Maddocks questions (memory), GCS, C-spine assessment** |
| SCAT5 off-field (private, quiet) | Background, symptom evaluation, cognitive screen (**SAC**), neuro screen, **mBESS**, delayed recall, decision |
| SCAT5 additions (TO) | On-field section, C-spine assessment, **10-word list** (≥ 5 min between immediate + delayed recall), neuro screen |
| **SCAT5 time** | **Minimum 10 min — can't be done correctly in less (TO, OB key)** · OB text: "< 10 min" / "on-field < 15 min" — read the options |
| SCAT history (TO) | SCAT 2005 → SCAT2 2009 → SCAT3 2013 → **SCAT5 2017** (5th conference) |
| SCAT6 (TO) | Ideally **within 72 h**, up to **7 days** · **> 7 days → SCOAT6** / Child SCOAT6 |
| SCAT6 immediate (TO) | Observable signs, GCS, C-spine, **coordination + ocular / motor screen**, Maddocks |
| SCAT6 off-field (TO) | Background, symptoms, cognitive screen, coordination + balance, delayed recall, decision |
| Limits | **Not stand-alone** for diagnosis, recovery or RTP · rapid screen, not a comprehensive neuro exam |
| Cost | **Free**, no technology → widely used in **high school** |

**Current SCAT** — **SCAT6** (TO, recent key) · **SCAT5** (TO, FNB 2021 key; Orthobullets "most commonly used") — read the year / options

**SCAT combines (TO)** — **GCS** (consciousness) + **modified Maddocks** (orientation) + **PCSS** (21 symptoms, 4 categories — somatic, cognitive, emotional, sleep — 7-point Likert) + **SAC** (orientation, concentration, immediate + delayed memory) + neck, balance, coordination exam

**Boxing doctor — recovery from prior concussion, fit for sport? (TO)** — **SCAT** (✗ GCS, ✗ PCSS, ✗ SAC)

**Not a documentation tool (TO)** — **SCAT** · documentation = **SOAP notes** (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan of first aid), **daily sideline injury report**, **training room treatment log**, **Daily Red Cross List** (no / limited / full participation)

### Assessment tools — ImPACT & neuropsychological tests

| Test | Assesses |
|---|---|
| **ImPACT** | **Computer-based** · **verbal + visual memory, processing speed, reaction time, impulse control, symptoms** (attention, working memory) · vs **baseline** or historical controls |
| **SAC** | **Orientation** (month?), **immediate + delayed memory** (5 words × 3 trials + 1 at end), **concentration** (5 digits backwards, months in reverse) · pencil-and-paper · ✗ reaction time, balance |
| **BESS** | Balance — **3 stances (bipedal, unipedal, tandem) × 10 s** |
| **SOT** | **Entire balance system** — vestibular, visual, somatosensory |
| **King-Devick** | **Saccadic eye movements**, language, concentration — read numbers L→R, **3 tests** · portable, **2 min** |
| **GCS** | **Depth + duration of impaired consciousness** · for **more severe** head injury |
| EyeBoxCNS | Eye movements · clinic computer — **not sideline** |
| SCORE | Smartphone app guiding parents + coaches |
| Helmet sensors | Measure head impact, **not brain** → **cannot diagnose SRC** |

**ImPACT logistics** — preseason **baseline** · off-field, quiet · **fee-based, computer / tablet, \~30 min** → collegiate / pro · **not stand-alone**; return to baseline alone ≠ RTP

**ImPACT baseline** — stable over 2 yr in **college** (no annual retest) · **retest annually in high school / youth**

**Low baseline neuropsych score** — athlete's own reference → **not** a reason to withhold play (✓ EXCEPT answer)

**Post-injury neuropsych testing** — **not required for all**; no defined role in RTP criteria · RTP stays a **clinical decision**

## 5 Management

```
Head trauma in athlete
├─ Red flag (worsening GCS, focal deficit, otorrhea, Battle sign) → ER + CT
├─ Any sign / symptom of concussion → remove, NO same-day RTP
│      └─ rest 24–48 h → graduated RTP (≥ 24 h / stage, ≥ 1 wk)
└─ Unilateral transient UE paresthesia, normal exam, no concussion signs → stinger → same-day RTP
```

### Removal criteria — no same-day return to play

| Remove from play (no same-day RTP) | ✗ Not a criterion |
|---|---|
| **Any sign / symptom** of concussion ("if concerned at all") | **Low baseline neuropsych testing** |
| No provider experienced in concussion present | Cervical muscle tenderness alone |
| **History of prior concussion** | Parental waiver / consent (doesn't permit RTP) |
| **LOC** · **amnesia** | Normal neuropsych testing or imaging |
| **Positive exertional stress test** (symptoms recur with exertion) | |
| **Symptoms > 15 min** | |

**Same-day RTP** — **contraindicated in every diagnosed / suspected concussion**, even if symptoms resolve (✗ neuro exam, ✗ SCAT5, ✗ ImPACT, ✗ K-D, ✗ SAC, ✗ SOT clears) · test to pass for same-game return → **none**

**Only same-day RTP** — **stinger**: unilateral transient UE pain / paresthesia / deltoid weakness, **complete resolution**, normal exam, no concussion signs

**Bilateral hand paresthesias / weakness** after helmet-on-helmet hit — **spinal cord work-up**, no same-day RTP

**NCAA — out ≥ 5–10 days + stepwise rehab** — **momentary LOC**, **can't recall events before tackle**, **blank stare**, **gait instability** (✗ cervical muscle tenderness)

**Lacrosse ball to head, headache + photophobia, better after minutes** — **hold out for rest of the day** (✗ RTP on resolution, ✗ CT + MRI, ✗ "≥ 2 wk rest")

### Graduated return-to-play protocol & timing

| Stage | Activity | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Symptom-limited activity** | Daily activities not provoking symptoms | Reintroduce work / school |
| **2 Light aerobic** | **Walking, swimming, stationary cycling**, HR **< 70% max** · no resistance | ↑ HR |
| **3 Sport-specific exercise** | **Running** or skating drills · **no head impact** | Add movement |
| **4 Non-contact training drills** | Complex drills (passing) · **start progressive resistance / strength training** | Exercise, coordination, cognition |
| **5 Full-contact practice** | Normal training | Confidence, functional skills |
| **6 Return to play** | Normal game play | |

**Each stage** — **≥ 24 h** (✗ 12 h, ✗ 48 h, ✗ 72 h, ✗ 96 h) → **earliest RTP = 1 week** (✗ 30 min, ✗ 24 h, ✗ 4 wk, ✗ 2 mo) · usually **7–10 days** (OB)

**Number of steps** — **6** (Orthobullets, 2012 consensus) · **5** (OB211942, counting asymptomatic day → full-contact practice) — same sequence

**Symptoms recur at a stage** — **24 h rest → drop back to previous asymptomatic stage**

**Correct sequence** — **stationary bike → running on a track → running + catching a football** (✗ squats / leg press before stage 4, ✗ track before bike)

**14-yr-old, dizzy + can't focus after head clash** — **RTP after ≥ 1-week graduated protocol** (✗ negative CT, ✗ ImPACT back to baseline, ✗ later in game, ✗ next day)

**Confirmed concussion with LOC** — **RTP after graduated protocol while symptom-free** (✗ next day, ✗ after 7 days rest alone, ✗ neuropsychiatric clearance)

**1 wk later, headache + blurred vision at rest, cognition back to baseline** — **withhold from all contact** — **symptoms at rest = absolute contraindication** (✗ graduated RTP, ✗ day-7 ImPACT, ✗ outpatient CT — symptoms may last up to 2 wk)

**Friday concussion, still symptomatic → Monday** — **sub-symptom threshold aerobic exercise after baseline symptom reassessment** (✗ strict rest until symptoms resolve, ✗ aerobic despite headache, ✗ accelerated drills, ✗ fixed 7 days regardless)

### Cognitive rest & predictors of prolonged recovery

**Treatment** — **cognitive + physical rest 24–48 h** → graduated RTP · **prolonged strict rest (weeks) ✗**

**Cognitive rest** — minimise concentration: reading, schoolwork, **video games**, texting, working online, chess → **limiting video games** ✓

**Cognitive rest** — **↓ symptom duration** ✓ · **↑ activity in post-concussion period → prolonged symptoms** (OB217826)

**Early aerobic activity (days after injury)** — **encouraged, speeds recovery** (OB217914, OB219980) — must stay **below symptom threshold** (✗ delay aerobic ≥ 1 wk)

**Prolonged recovery predictors** — **subacute headache + depression symptoms** ✓ · **ADHD no longer** thought a significant risk factor (✗)

**Natural history** — cognitive deficits, balance + symptoms **improve within the first 2 weeks** · **small % persistent** ✓

## 6 Complications

### Second impact syndrome

**Definition** — **second head trauma before first concussion's symptoms resolve** — second hit can be trivial

**Mechanism** — **catastrophic cerebral oedema** from **loss of cerebral autoregulation** (✗ venous / ✗ arterial microvascular bleeding)

**Course** — **rapid** progression (✗ gradual) · **brain stem** often involved · CT shows oedema · **poor prognosis** · **mortality \~50%**

**Prevention** — **restrict RTP until symptom-free** ✓ — the reason for no same-day RTP

**Associations** — **male**, **young age**, **American football** · ✗ leading cause of CTE · ✗ short-term memory loss

### Postconcussion syndrome

**Definition** — persistent symptoms (headache, confusion, lethargy) **> 10–14 days adults** · **> 4 weeks children** (younger → slower RTS)

**Management** — **RTP contraindicated** · formal **neuropsychiatric evaluation** · pharmacotherapy controversial · **self-limiting** (vs CTE)

### Intracranial haemorrhage

**Subdural haematoma** — **most common**

**Epidural haematoma** — **lucid interval** before decline → **neurosurgical decompression + seizure prophylaxis**

### Chronic traumatic encephalopathy

**Definition** — **progressive neurodegeneration** from **repetitive brain trauma** · **permanent** (✗ temporary neuronal derangement)

**Onset** — **years–decades later**, **midlife** · **mean 42 yr** (✗ by age 30) · boxing, football, hockey, wrestling

**Early features** — **behaviour / affect: apathy, irritability, suicidal ideation**, aggression, "short fuse", loss of impulse control · or cognitive: poor episodic memory, **executive dysfunction**, dementia

**Late** — depression, disinhibition + **parkinsonism** (bradykinesia, tremor, rigidity)

**Pathology** — tau, beta-amyloid, TDP-43 · **superficial cortical laminae II–III** (Alzheimer: layer III and deeper) · **gross changes present**: cerebral atrophy, enlarged ventricles, diffuse senile plaques (✗ no gross changes)

**Diagnosis** — **post-mortem neuropathology only** · no consensus clinical criteria

**45-yr-old ex-NFL running back, cognitive + mood + parkinsonism** — **CTE** (✗ early Alzheimer — rare in 40s, ✗ Parkinson's — usually > 60, ✗ ALS — no cognitive change, ✗ FTD)

**40-yr-old surgeon, ex-college football, worsening irritability + forgetting steps** — **CTE** (✗ postconcussion syndrome)

## X vs Y

| | **Concussion** | **Stinger** | **Cervical cord neurapraxia** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Symptoms | Cognitive, somatic, emotional | **Unilateral** UE pain, paresthesia, **C5-6 weakness** | **Bilateral** / ≥ 2 limbs |
| Same-day RTP | **Never** | **Yes** if complete resolution, full strength, painless neck ROM | **No** |
| Work-up | Clinical ± SCAT; CT if red flags | X-ray if recurrent | **MRI** |

| | **Second impact syndrome** | **Postconcussion syndrome** | **CTE** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cause | 2nd hit before recovery | Single concussion, slow recovery | **Repetitive** trauma |
| Timing | Minutes | > 10–14 d adult · > 4 wk child | Years–decades (mean 42) |
| Outcome | **\~50% mortality** | **Self-limiting** | **Progressive**, post-mortem diagnosis |

| | **ImPACT** | **SCAT5** |
|---|---|---|
| Format | **Computer**, fee, \~30 min | Paper, **free**, ≥ 10 min |
| Setting | Off-field, baseline comparison | On-field + off-field |
| Users | College / professional | High school (widely) |
