# Meniscus

*Knee & Sports · 31 / 61*

## 1 Basics

### Function

**Load transmission** — carries **50% of load in extension, 85% in flexion** · ↑ congruency → ↑ contact area → ↓ point loading

**Shock absorption** — meniscus **more elastic than articular cartilage**

**Hoop stress** — vertical compressive load → **circumferential "hoop" tension** in the circumferential fibres → load spread, ↓ femorotibial contact stress

**Stability** — deepens the tibial surface · **secondary stabiliser** · **posterior horn MM = main secondary stabiliser to anterior translation** · menisci become **primary stabilisers in the ACL-deficient knee**

**Lateral meniscus** — less stabilising · **2× the excursion of the MM**

**Other roles (OB)** — proprioception, lubrication, nutrition of the joint, cartilage protection / OA prevention

### Composition & collagen

**Tissue** — **fibroelastic cartilage (fibrocartilage)**: collagen, proteoglycan, glycoproteins, cells

**Water** — **65–75%** (Orthobullets) · **72% water + 22% collagen** (OB, TO)

**Collagen share** — **type I = 90%** of meniscal collagen (Orthobullets, OB) · **type I = 75% of ECM dry weight** (TO) · + II, III, V, VI · collagen gives **tensile strength**

**Predominant type (TO)** — **type I** (✗ type II, ✗ "type II in red-red + type I elsewhere", ✗ "type I in red-red + type II elsewhere")

**Zonal collagen (TO)** — **red-red: type I** (like ligament / tendon) · **white-white: more type II** (like hyaline cartilage) → ✗ "type II predominates in red-red"

| Collagen | Tissues |
|---|---|
| **Type I** | **Meniscus (fibrocartilage)**, **ACL**, **annulus fibrosus**, **bone**, **tendon** |
| **Type II** | **Articular (hyaline) cartilage** (✗ same as meniscus) |

| Fibre layer (OB) | Orientation | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Superficial network + lamellar layer | **Radial** | Structural integrity; tie fibres together |
| Central main layer | **Circumferential (longitudinal)**, parallel to periphery | **Converts compression → hoop stress**; captured by **vertical mattress** sutures |

![Collagen layers of the meniscus — radial superficial, circumferential central](../MCQ/attachments/q8834-illb-meniscus.jpg)

**Horizontal cleavage tear (OB)** — usually excised (avascular inner zone, degeneration) · Ahn: all-inside **vertical mattress** + **notch drilling (marrow)** → **91% healing**

### Gross anatomy

| | **Medial meniscus** | **Lateral meniscus** |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | **C-shaped**, semicircular, triangular cross-section | **Near circular** — horns close together, next to ACL |
| Length (TO) | **40–45 mm — longer / "larger"**; ~27 mm wide | **32–35 mm — shorter** |
| Width (Orthobullets) | 9–10 mm | 10–12 mm |
| Thickness | 3–5 mm | 4–5 mm |
| Plateau covered (TO) | **51–74% — less** | **75–93% — more** |
| Horns (TO) | **Posterior horn ≫ anterior** | Horns equal |
| Fixation | **Rigid peripheral (coronary) fixation → less mobile** | **Fewer capsular attachments → more mobile** |
| AP displacement (OB) | 0.43 mm | 0.66 mm |
| Artery (Orthobullets) | **Medial inferior genicular** → peripheral **20–30%** | **Lateral inferior genicular** → peripheral **10–25%** |

**Kinematics** — both menisci move **anterior with extension, posterior with flexion** · **MM: less anterior translation in extension + less posterior movement in flexion** (✗ more translation, ✗ fewer capsular attachments)

**TO "not true" trap** — "MM larger than LM" ✓ (longer) · "LM covers more surface" ✓ · "PH MM larger than anterior horn" ✓ · **"type II predominates in red-red" ✗**

### Attachments

**Transverse (intermeniscal) ligament** — joins the **anterior horns** of MM + LM

**Transverse ligament on MRI** — its junction with the **anterior horn LM mimics a tear** on sagittal images → follow it over successive images (✗ LM / MM tear, ✗ anterior meniscofemoral ligament — a posterior structure, ✗ ligamentum mucosum)

**Ligamentum mucosum (infrapatellar plica)** — best on sagittal · **intercondylar notch → anterior fat pad**

**Coronary (meniscotibial) ligaments** — fix the meniscus **peripherally** to the tibia

**Meniscofemoral ligaments** — from the **posterior horn LM** into the **PCL / MFC near the PCL insertion** · **Humphrey = anterior** to PCL · **Wrisberg = posterior** · **both in 46%, at least one in 100%** (TO)

### Blood supply & healing

**Middle genicular artery** — **posterior horns** (+ posterior capsule, ACL, PCL) → **primary supply to PH MM** (✗ medial inferior / superior genicular, ✗ lateral geniculates)

**Inferior geniculates** — medial inferior → peripheral 20–30% MM · lateral inferior → peripheral 10–25% LM · anastomose anteriorly → fat pad, synovium, patellar tendon · **lateral superior + inferior → lateral retinaculum**

**Perimeniscal capillary plexus (TO)** — from superior + inferior medial / lateral geniculates · menisci lose vessels after birth → **only peripheral 10–25% perfused at maturity** (TO; meniscal-tears TO: 10–30%)

**Central 75%** — nourished by **diffusion** from synovial fluid · limited / no intrinsic healing

**Peripheral 25% (red zone)** — heals by **fibrocartilage scar** · **fibrochondrocyte** = healing cell · **tears < 4 mm** from periphery heal best

**Innervation** — peripheral two-thirds: **type I + II nerve endings** · **posterior horns: highest mechanoreceptor density**

## 5 Management

### Meniscal root tear — tibial fixation

**Root tear** — biomechanically **= total meniscectomy** (loss of hoop tension → ↑ peak contact pressure) → chronic pain, instability, **rapid OA** · an independent tear subtype

**52 y, PH MM root tear, no arthritis on X-ray** — **fixation to the tibia (root repair)** = gold standard (✗ NSAIDs + PT, ✗ partial meniscectomy, ✗ total meniscectomy — Fairbank changes, ✗ UKA — only if arthritis)

**Nonop (Krych)** — poor outcomes, worsening OA, **> 33% arthroplasty at mean 32 months**

**Partial meniscectomy vs repair (Chung)** — repair better clinically + radiologically · **lower arthroplasty at 5 y**

**Age (LaPrade)** — transtibial double-tunnel pull-out improves **< 50 and > 50 y equally**, medial or lateral

**Pre-existing / rapid arthritis** — **UKA / TKA**

**TKA after unrepaired root tear — cross-check** — > 33% at 32 months (OB, here) · ~30% at 5 y · up to 28% at 3.2 y (OB, Meniscal Tears chapter) — read the options

## X vs Y

| | **Red zone (peripheral 25%)** | **White zone (central 75%)** |
|---|---|---|
| Blood supply | Geniculate capillary plexus | **Diffusion** only |
| Collagen (TO) | **Type I** | More **type II** |
| Healing | **Fibrocartilage scar** (fibrochondrocyte) | Little or none |

## How it's asked

**"Predominant collagen in meniscus (TO)?"** → **type I**

**"NOT true of the menisci (TO)?"** → **type 2 collagen predominates in red-red zone**

**"Medial vs lateral meniscus — true?"** → **MM: less anterior translation in extension, less posterior in flexion**

**"Primary blood supply to PH MM?"** → **middle genicular artery**

**"Structure joining anterior horns on MRI?"** → **transverse meniscal ligament**

**"Torn meniscus — same collagen as?"** → **ACL and annulus fibrosus (type I)**

**"Root tear, no arthritis — definitive treatment?"** → **fixation to the tibia**
