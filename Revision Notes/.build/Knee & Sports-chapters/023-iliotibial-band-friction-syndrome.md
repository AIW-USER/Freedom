# Iliotibial Band Friction Syndrome

*Knee & Sports · 23 / 61*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **2–15%** of knee overuse injuries · **most common cause of lateral knee pain in runners + cyclists** (TO)

**Who** — **runners, cyclists**, repetitive flexion–extension · first seen in **US Marine recruits (1975)**; skiers, hockey, basketball, soccer (TO) · **slightly more common in women** (TO)

### Anatomy

**Origin** — continuation of **tensor fascia lata** (Orthobullets) · distal fascial continuation of **TFL + gluteus medius + gluteus maximus** (TO)

**Insertion** — **Gerdy tubercle** (lateral tibial plateau) + partly **lateral femoral supracondylar ridge** (TO) · runs **superficial to vastus lateralis**

**Iliopatellar band (TO)** — anterior extension to lateral patella → **prevents medial patellar translation**

**Innervation** — **superior gluteal nerve (L4, L5, S1)** · synergists = **hip abductors**

**Function (TO)** — **knee extensor < 30° flexion**, **flexor > 30°**; moves **posterior** to lateral epicondyle with flexion · anterolateral stabiliser (OB)

![Iliotibial band over the lateral femoral condyle](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3019-itband4.jpg)

### Pathophysiology

**Mechanism** — ITB shifts forward + back over **lateral femoral condyle / epicondyle** → friction, tension, inflammation · also compression + bursal inflammation (OB)

**Impingement zone** — **30° of knee flexion** = knee angle at **foot strike** (TO)

**Pathoanatomy** — compression / irritation of tissue **deep to ITB** (TO: highly innervated **fat pad** under ITB) · cysts or **bursitis of lateral synovial recess** · condylar bone oedema · **ITB itself rarely abnormal**

**Associated** — **patellofemoral syndrome** (tight ITB) · **medial compartment OA** (↓ medial joint space → varus) · **greater trochanteric pain syndrome**

## 2 Mechanism

### Presentation & running risk factors

| Risk factors | Orthobullets | TO |
|---|---|---|
| **Training** | **Sudden ↑ intensity / mileage**, poor shoe support | Tilted surfaces, hills, abrupt training change, **downhill running** |
| **Anatomy** | **Genu varum / recurvatum**, LLD, **excessive foot pronation**, **weak hip abductors**, **tight ITB** | Tight ITB + weak hip abductors |
| **Biomechanics** | Quads–hamstring imbalance, ↑ landing force, **↑ knee flexion at heel strike** | **↑ hip adduction**, **↑ knee IR** + IR velocity, **↑ knee flexion at heel strike**, **↑ maximal foot inversion** |

**Foot conflict** — **excessive pronation** (Orthobullets) vs **↑ maximal foot inversion** (TO) — read the options

**Hockey player, lateral knee pain on running (TO)** — **increased knee flexion at heel strike** (✗ ↑ foot eversion, ✗ ↑ knee external rotation, ✗ ↑ hip abduction)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **activity-related lateral knee pain** in endurance athletes (e.g. **10 min into a run**) · worse with terrain / mileage change, **downhill** · relieved by rest

**Site** — **lateral femoral condyle / epicondyle** · tenderness **proximal to lateral joint line** (TO) · can be **insertional at Gerdy tubercle** (OB)

**26 yr woman, no trauma, pain after downhill running, normal exam (TO)** — **ITB syndrome** (✗ lateral meniscus tear, ✗ LCL tear, ✗ lateral hamstring strain)

**Exam** — swelling over ITB, **foot + knee malalignment**, crepitus, ↓ hip / knee motion, **weak hip abduction**, **pain on single-leg squat**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnosis — provocative tests

| Test | Technique | Positive |
|---|---|---|
| **Ober** (Orthobullets) | **Side-lying, symptomatic side up, knee 90°**; hip from **flexion + abduction → extension + adduction** | **Pain, tightness or clicking** over ITB |
| Ober (OB) | Lying on unaffected side; leg **abducted + extended** | **Can't adduct** the leg |
| Ober (TO) | Lateral recumbent, knees 90°; examiner abducts + extends hip, then adducts | **Restriction + lateral knee pain** |
| **Noble compression** (TO) | Supine, **knee 90°**; press **lateral epicondyle** proximal to joint line; extend knee (+ hip) to 0° | **Pain at \~30° flexion** — **specific for ITBFS** |

![Ober test positioning](../MCQ/attachments/q217836-illa-ober-test.jpg)

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| X-ray (AP, lateral ± oblique / skyline) | **Usually normal**; may show **medial JSN**, patellar malalignment, fracture |
| **MRI** | Rule out **lateral meniscus tear, LCL injury**; signal in **lateral synovial recess**, ITB or periosteum · **↑ T2 signal deep to ITB at lateral epicondyle + ITB thickening** · **negative MRI does not exclude ITBS** |
| US / MRI (TO) | **ITB thickening** |

### Differential — other lateral knee tests

| Finding | Means |
|---|---|
| **Flexion/abduction → extension/adduction pain (Ober)** | **ITB syndrome** |
| Pop on rotation + extension (McMurray) | Meniscal tear |
| Varus laxity at 30° | **Isolated LCL** (LCL inserts on **fibula**, ✗ Gerdy) |
| ER asymmetry **> 10° at 30°** (dial) | **PLC** (LCL, popliteus, popliteofibular ligament) |
| **"Clunk"**, IR + valgus, extension → flexion (pivot shift) | **ACL**; reduction at **20–30°** |

## 5 Management

```
ITB friction syndrome
├─ Acute → REST, ICE, NSAIDs (oral / topical), activity modification
│      └─ fails → CORTICOSTEROID INJECTION (ITB bursa)
├─ Then → PT: ITB / gluteal stretching, HIP ABDUCTOR strengthening, foam roller
└─ Refractory > 6 months → SURGERY (release, excision, Z-plasty, bursectomy)
```

### Diagnosis (Ober test) & stretching

**Initial treatment** — **home stretching program + cross training** (✗ bursectomy, ✗ Z-lengthening, ✗ partial ITB excision)

**PT** — stretch **ITB, lateral fascia, gluteals** · deep transverse friction massage · **hip abductor strengthening** · proprioception · **myofascial release / foam roller** (TO) · strengthening **after** inflammation settles

**Training changes** — **change shoes every 300–500 miles** · avoid sudden mileage ↑

**Return to running (TO)** — wk 1: alternate days, **flat surface** → daily fast-paced, **no downhill** → after **3–4 wk** ↑ distance / frequency → hills + cambered surfaces only if pain-free · relapse → rest + restart

**Prognosis** — **50–90% improve in 4–8 wk** nonop (Orthobullets) · most fully relieved, return in **6–8 wk** (TO)

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication / detail |
|---|---|
| **Surgery overall** | **Refractory > 6 months** nonop (TO) |
| Excision of cyst, bursa or **lateral synovial recess** | Failed nonop + soft-tissue pathology **without ITB signal change**; arthroscopic or open; may cause **chronic effusion + pain** |
| **Elliptical ITB excision** | Chronic, failed nonop · open, lateral distal femur incision · **2 × 4 cm ellipse of posterior band** over lateral epicondyle |
| **Resection of posterior half of ITB** (TO) | Resistant cases |
| **Z-plasty** lengthening | **Refractory only** |
| Others (TO) | Percutaneous / open release (triangle over LFC), **bursectomy** (bursa + fat pad), arthroscopic debridement · modern focus: **resect lateral synovial recess** + innervated synovial fat |

## X vs Y

| | **Ober test** | **Noble test** |
|---|---|---|
| Position | **Side-lying**, knee 90° | **Supine**, knee 90° |
| Manoeuvre | Hip abduct + extend → **adduct** | **Press lateral epicondyle**, extend knee |
| Positive | Tight ITB, pain, can't adduct | **Pain at 30°** |

## How it's asked

**"Runner, lateral epicondyle pain, Ober + — initial?"** → **home stretching program and cross training**

**"Cyclist, pain at Gerdy tubercle after ↑ training — exam finding?"** → **pain with hip brought from flexion/abduction to extension/adduction while side-lying** (✗ McMurray, ✗ varus at 30° — LCL, ✗ dial — PLC, ✗ pivot shift — ACL)

**"Hockey player, lateral knee pain — running risk factor (TO)?"** → **increased knee flexion at heel strike**

**"Lateral knee pain after downhill running, normal exam (TO)?"** → **iliotibial band syndrome**
