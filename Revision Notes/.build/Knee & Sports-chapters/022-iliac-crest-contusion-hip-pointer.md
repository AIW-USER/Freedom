# Iliac Crest Contusion (Hip Pointer)

*Knee & Sports · 22 / 61*

## 1 Basics

**Hip pointer** — **contusion / traumatic haematoma over the iliac wing** · direct blow or crush in **contact sports** (hockey player checked into the boards)

**Haematoma** — around iliac wing → may bleed into **hip abductor muscles**

**Muscles from the crest (any can be involved)** — sartorius, TFL, **gluteus medius**, abdominals (transversus, obliques)

**Adolescent** — **rule out iliac apophysis avulsion** (see ASIS chapter)

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — **point tenderness over iliac crest** · **contusion + haematoma** · hip weakness · **↓ ROM**

![Hip pointer: bruising and haematoma over the iliac crest](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3090-hip-1-moved.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray + MRI** — usually **unremarkable** · MRI may show a **large haematoma**

## 5 Management

**Nonoperative (mainstay)** — **ice, compression, rest** (extended, until symptoms settle), NSAIDs ± muscle relaxants

**Therapy** — stretch muscles about the crest · **leg on maximum stretch (extension)** (✗ flexion — that is quadriceps contusion)

**Corticosteroid injection** — near the crest, **some advocate**

**Return to play** — **extra padding** after acute phase

## How it's asked

**"Hockey check, point tender iliac crest, bruise (photo)?"** → **hip pointer** (✗ hip flexor strain, ✗ apophysitis, ✗ piriformis syndrome, ✗ trochanteric bursitis)
