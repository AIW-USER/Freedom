# LCL Injury of the Knee

*Knee & Sports · 27 / 61*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Isolated** — **extremely rare (< 2% of knee injuries)** · gymnasts, tennis players

**Combined** — **7–16%** of knee ligament injuries, mainly with **PLC** · also ACL, PCL

### LCL anatomy – femoral insertion relative to popliteus & epicondyle

**Shape** — tubular, cord-like · 2–3 mm thick · 4–5 mm wide · **66 mm** long (anatomy) · **\~70 mm** (graft-sizing text)

**Femoral origin** — **posterior (3.1 mm) + proximal (1.4 mm) to lateral epicondyle** · **posterior + proximal to popliteus** origin (✗ anterior, ✗ distal, ✗ directly on epicondyle)

**LCL ↔ popliteus distance** — **18.5 mm** · popliteus always attaches **anterior** to LCL (proximal + anterior fifth of popliteal sulcus) (LaPrade, 10 cadavers)

**Fibular insertion** — **anterolateral fibular head**, covers 38% of width · **most anterior structure on proximal fibula** · order **LCL → PFL → biceps femoris**

**Blood supply** — anterior tibial recurrent + **inferolateral geniculate** arteries

### Biomechanics

**Varus restraint** — **primary at 5° and 30°** · **55% at 5°**, **69% at 30°** (LCL note) · 5° (55%) and **25°** (69%) (PLC note) — read the options

**Also** — secondary restraint to posterolateral rotation at **< 50°** flexion · resists varus in full extension with ACL + PCL

**Position** — **behind the axis of rotation** → **tight in extension, lax in flexion** · tensile strength 750 N

**28 lateral stabilisers (OB)** — 3 primary: **LCL, popliteus tendon (PT), PFL** · unreconstructed PLC → ↑ force on ACL / PCL grafts → failure

| Structure (James) | Restrains | Peak |
|---|---|---|
| **LCL** | **Varus** + rotation **in extension** | **0–30°** |
| **Popliteus tendon + PFL** | **Rotation in flexion** — most rotational stability | **60–90°** |
| Lateral meniscus | 2° restraint to anterior translation | — |
| Lateral gastrocnemius | No significant rotational role | — |

| Medial stabilisers (LaPrade) | Role |
|---|---|
| **sMCL** | **Primary valgus** (proximal) + 2° rotational (distal) at all angles |
| Deep MCL | 2° valgus + rotation |
| **POL** | **Primary rotational** restraint |

## 2 Mechanism

**Traumatic** — **direct blow / force to medial knee** · varus stress, external tibial rotation, hyperextension

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — instability near full extension · stairs · **cutting / pivoting** · lateral joint line pain + swelling

**Exam** — ecchymosis · tender LCL insertion · **figure-of-4 position** → palpable cord-like LCL · **varus (lateral) thrust** or hyperextension gait · **common peroneal nerve** exam

| Test | Finding | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Varus stress 30° only** | Laxity | **Isolated LCL** |
| **Varus stress 0° + 30°** | Laxity | **LCL ± ACL / PCL** (cruciate) |
| **Dial test** | **> 10° ↑ ER at 30°** | **LCL + PLC** |
| Dial at 30° + 90° | ↑ ER | PCL + PLC |
| Lachman 1A | **< 5 mm**, firm endpoint | ACL intact |

**Varus laxity at 30° + dial + at 30° only, Lachman 1A (OB)** — **LCL + PLC** (✗ isolated LCL — no dial; ✗ ACL; ✗ PCL + PLC — + at 90°)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding / use |
|---|---|
| Weightbearing AP, lateral | Asymmetric lateral joint widening |
| **Varus stress X-ray** | Quantifies lateral opening |
| **MRI** | **Modality of choice** · **most tears off fibular insertion** · medial compartment bone contusions (hyperextension-varus) |
| MRI sensitivity | **95%** vs EUA **58%** |

![Varus stress radiographs](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob3011-varus-stress-radiographs.jpg)

### LCL grading

| Grade | Clinical (lateral opening vs other side) | MRI |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **0–5 mm** | Fluid around ligament, intact |
| **2** | **6–10 mm** | Partial fibre tear |
| **3** | **> 10 mm, no firm endpoint** | Complete tear (midsubstance or insertion) |

## 5 Management

```
LCL injury
├─ Isolated grade I / II (stable at 0°) → limited immobilisation + ROM + rehab (RTS 6–8 wk)
├─ Isolated grade III, acute (< 2 wk), avulsed from fibula → repair
├─ Grade III subacute / chronic or midsubstance with varus instability → reconstruction
└─ Rotatory / posterolateral instability → LCL + PLC reconstruction
```

### Diagnosis & treatment of LCL / PLC injury

**Nonop** — progressive ROM, quadriceps + hamstring strengthening · 6 wk casting heals but → **stiffness** · unrecognised PLC → progressive varus / hyperextension laxity

**Repair** — **acute (< 2 wk) grade III avulsion** · failure **up to 40%** · repair fails more in chronic **(> 3 wk)** (OB)

**Reconstruction** — better than repair · **6% failure at 3 yr** · **anatomic + semitendinosus autograft** best

**Chronic isolated grade 3 + peroneal paraesthesias, athlete (OB)** — **LCL reconstruction + peroneal neurolysis** (✗ PT + hinged brace — grade 1–2 only, ✗ PRP, ✗ repair — chronic, ✗ popliteus / PFL reconstruction — no PLC) · Moulton: semitendinosus graft, 43 pts, ↑ Lysholm + WOMAC

**LCL heals worse than MCL** — lower threshold to operate in grade 3 (Grawe)

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| Nonop | **Isolated grade I / II**, no instability at 0° |
| **Repair** | **Isolated acute (< 2 wk) grade III, avulsed from anatomic site (fibula)** |
| **Reconstruction** | Subacute / chronic (> 2 wk) grade III with varus instability · acute **midsubstance** grade III |
| **LCL + PLC reconstruction** | **Rotatory** or **posterolateral** instability (better if acute) |

### Techniques

**Lateral approach** — interval **ITB (superior gluteal n.) – biceps femoris (sciatic n.)** · second interval in ITB for femoral insertion · **peroneal neurolysis** · traction suture (knee extended) tests repairability · suture anchors

**Graft** — **semitendinosus autograft preferred** (closer to LCL length; patellar tendon \~50 mm; stronger than gracilis; less saphenous irritation) · alternatives patellar tendon / Achilles allograft

**Tunnels** — fibular: lateral fibular head → posteromedial styloid, distal to PFL · femoral: \~3 mm posterior to epicondyle, exit anteromedial

**LCL + PLC** — **Larson** fibular-based figure-of-eight (LCL + PFL) · transtibial double-bundle split Achilles · anatomic LCL + popliteus + PFL (bifid graft)

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Persistent varus / hyperextension laxity** | **Grade III treated nonop** · **missed PCL or PLC** |
| **Peroneal nerve injury** | **Up to 44%** of multiligament injuries with LCL / PLC |
| Stiffness | Prolonged immobilisation |
| **Physeal arrest** | Errant lateral condylar fixation in skeletally immature |
| Graft harvest | Saphenous neurapraxia, MCL injury |

## How it's asked

**"LCL femoral attachment vs popliteus?"** → **posterior and proximal**

**"Two structures giving most rotational stability in flexion?"** → **popliteus tendon + popliteofibular ligament (C + D)**

**"LCL femoral insertion vs lateral epicondyle?"** → **posterior and proximal**

**"Varus laxity at 30°, dial + at 30° not 90°, Lachman 1A?"** → **LCL + PLC**

**"Chronic isolated grade 3 LCL + peroneal symptoms, athlete?"** → **LCL reconstruction + peroneal neurolysis**
