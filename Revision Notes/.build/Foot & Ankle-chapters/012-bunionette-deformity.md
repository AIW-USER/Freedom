# Bunionette Deformity

*Foot & Ankle · 12 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Tailor's bunion** — **lateral prominence of 5th MT head** · adolescents + adults · **women 2–4×** · often **bilateral**

**Extrinsic cause** — **forefoot compression (tight shoes)** = commonest · abnormal lateral loading

**Intrinsic causes** — congenital (**splayfoot**, **brachymetatarsia**) · inflammatory arthropathy · residual surgical malalignment

**Associated** — **varus 5th MTP** · **pes planus** · forefoot widening

### Pathoanatomy & normal values

| Parameter | Normal | Bunionette |
|---|---|---|
| **4–5 IMA** | **6.5–8°** (Orthobullets, OB) · **6–8°** (OB) | **↑** |
| **Lateral deviation angle** | **0–7°** | **↑** |
| **5th MT head width** | **< 13 mm** | **↑** (> 13 mm) |
| 5th MT shaft | Straight | **Lateral bowing** |

**Lesion** — bony prominence **± bursitis** over lateral 5th MT head

![4–5 intermetatarsal angle](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7017-4-5ima.jpg)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — lateral bunion pain · **plantar callus** · **worse with constrictive shoes** · cosmetic: **medial deviation of 5th toe**, prominent 5th MT head · effect on work

**Exam** — **plantar or lateral hyperkeratosis** · widened forefoot · erythema, swollen 5th bunion · **painless passive 5th MTP ROM** usually · check shoe wear

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **clinical**: lateral 5th MT head prominence ± pain + callus

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — **weight-bearing dorsoplantar, lateral, oblique** · ↑ **4–5 IMA** · ↑ **lateral deviation angle** · ↑ **MT head width**

**CT** — rarely needed · associated **trauma or malignancy**

### Coughlin classification

| Type | Deformity | Surgery |
|---|---|---|
| **I** ![Type I](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7017-type-1.jpg) | **Enlarged 5th MT head / lateral exostosis** (head > 13 mm) | **Lateral condylectomy** |
| **II** ![Type II](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7017-type-2.jpg) | **Congenital lateral bow of 5th MT** (distal half) · **normal 4–5 IMA** | **Distal chevron osteotomy** (IMA < 12°) |
| **III** ![Type III](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7017-type-3.jpg) | **↑ 4–5 IMA** · **most common** | Distal osteotomy if < 12° · **oblique diaphyseal osteotomy if > 12°** |
| IV (OB) | **Combination** of the above | — |

**Type definitions differ** — one OB uses **1 = enlarged lateral condyle · 2 = 5th MT varus · 3 = combined** — read the stem

## 5 Management

```
Bunionette (any type)
├─ First line, all types → NSAIDs, wide shoes, orthotics, padding, callus shaving
└─ Failed nonop
       ├─ Type I (normal IMA) → lateral condylectomy
       ├─ Type II / III, IMA < 12° → distal chevron osteotomy
       └─ Type II / III, IMA > 12° → oblique diaphyseal osteotomy (+ condylectomy if head wide)
```

### Nonoperative first-line care

**First line for all types** — **NSAIDs + shoe wear modification** · orthotics (**semi-rigid inserts**, **custom orthotics** — OB) · **keratosis padding** · **callus shaving** · wide-based shoes · stretch existing shoes · **75–90% success** (✗ condylectomy, ✗ osteotomy, ✗ MT head resection as first step)

**Asymptomatic / only shoe rubbing** — nonop (✗ lateral condylectomy, ✗ condylectomy + distal osteotomy, ✗ diaphyseal osteotomy)

**✗ 1st TMT arthrodesis ± Akin** — that's **hallux valgus**, not bunionette

### Operative criteria

| Finding | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **Type I**, normal 4–5 IMA | **Lateral condylectomy (lateral eminence resection)** |
| **Type II** (lateral bow, normal IMA) | **Distal chevron osteotomy** |
| Type II / III, **4–5 IMA < 12°** (Orthobullets) · **≤ 12°** · **8–12°** · **9–12°** (OB) | **Distal MT osteotomy** (chevron) ± condylectomy + lateral capsule tightening |
| Type II / III, **4–5 IMA > 12°** | **Oblique diaphyseal rotational osteotomy** |
| **↑ IMA + MT head > 13 mm** | **Diaphyseal osteotomy + lateral condylectomy** |
| Plantar callosity | **Shave plantar 5th MT head** |
| Symptomatic after distal osteotomy | Proximal osteotomy (OB) |
| **Salvage only** | **MT head resection** |

### Procedure by type – lateral condylectomy, distal chevron, diaphyseal osteotomy (4–5 IMA)

**Lateral condylectomy** — resect **lateral ⅓** of 5th MT head (Orthobullets) · **lateral ¼–⅓** (OB) · or up to lateral articular margin · + **tighten lateral MTP capsule** · **no WB restrictions** / no prolonged immobilisation (✗ for IMA > 10°, ✗ lateral half, ✗ lowest recurrence, ✗ high metatarsalgia)

**Condylectomy downsides** — **highest recurrence** · over-resection · **MTP subluxation**, persistent pain

**MT head osteotomy for type I** — larger correction than condylectomy, but WB restrictions

**Distal chevron** — **commonest distal osteotomy** · **Type II: lateral bow, no keratotic lesion, normal IMA** (✗ type I who can't tolerate WB restriction, ✗ failed prior condylar resection, ✗ type III at 13°) · **biomechanically strongest** vs other osteotomies · fix with **K-wire or screw**

**Other distal osteotomies** — distal transverse · peg-and-slot · step-cut

**Oblique diaphyseal rotational osteotomy** — **Type II + III, IMA > 12°** · **screw** fixation · ± lateral condylectomy · **causes 5th MT shortening + metatarsalgia** · **rotational deformity** + implant problems (OB)

**Proximal osteotomy** — **avoid: poor blood supply** (Orthobullets)

**Sliding distal MIS osteotomy (S-DMMO)** — **type 3, IMA 10°, prominent condyle** → treats deformity, avoids implant complications (✗ contact casting, ✗ lateral eminence resection — type 1, ✗ open diaphyseal osteotomy, ✗ MT head resection)

**Large IMA (radiograph)** — **MT osteotomy + 5th MT head ostectomy** (✗ head excision — flail joint + transfer lesion, ✗ plantar condylectomy — ↓ plantar pressure only, ✗ Silastic implant)

**Rheumatoid, type 1, IMA 7°, congruent MTP, nodule under head** — **simple exostectomy + soft-tissue mass excision** (✗ MT head excision — no metatarsalgia, normal joint, ✗ osteotomy)

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **Recurrence** | **Condylectomy alone** — commonest / highest |
| **Transfer metatarsalgia** | **Isolated MT head resection** |
| **MTP instability / flail joint** | **MT head resection** (salvage only, poor outcomes) |
| **5th MT shortening** + metatarsalgia | **Diaphyseal osteotomy** |
| Rotational deformity | Diaphyseal osteotomy |
| **Claw toe** | — |

## How it's asked

**"Oblique diaphyseal rotational osteotomy — which radiographs?"** → **type II and type III (B and C)** with IMA > 12°

**"Planned distal chevron — which radiograph?"** → **type II, lateral bow (B)**

**"Diaphyseal 5th MT osteotomy is optimal for?"** → **widened 4–5 IMA + lateral prominence**

**"Distal chevron most indicated for?"** → **laterally bowed 5th MT, normal IMA, no keratotic lesion**

**"Distal chevron — which figure?"** → **type 2 bunionette (Figure C)**

**"Enlarged 5th MT head, failed nonop?"** → **lateral condylar resection**

**"Lateral condylectomy — true?"** → **no weight-bearing restrictions postoperatively**

**"Mid-diaphyseal osteotomy planned — pathology?"** → **type III with 4–5 IMA of 14°**

**"Large 4–5 IMA on radiograph — optimal surgery?"** → **metatarsal osteotomy + 5th MT head ostectomy**

**"IMA 13°, head 16 mm, congruent MTP?"** → **diaphyseal osteotomy + 5th MT condylectomy**

**"IMA 10°, prominent condyle, failed pads?"** → **sliding distal metatarsal MIS osteotomy**

**"RA, nodule under 5th MT head, IMA 7°?"** → **simple exostectomy + soft-tissue mass excision**

**"Painless bump, rubs on shoes — initial?"** → **NSAIDs + shoe wear modification**

**"First visit, IMA 9°, callus?"** → **shoewear modification, custom orthotics, NSAIDs**

**"Commonest complication of condylectomy?"** → **recurrence**

**"MT head resection causes?"** → **transfer metatarsalgia, MTP instability**
