# Nerves of the Foot

*Foot & Ankle · 35 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Tibial nerve branches

**Tibial nerve** — ankle **plantarflexion**, **toe flexion**, **plantar sensation** · 2 primary branches **medial + lateral plantar** → **all intrinsic foot muscles except EHB + EDB**

| Branch | Motor | Sensory |
|---|---|---|
| **Medial calcaneal n.** | — | **Plantar medial heel** (lesser role) |
| **Medial plantar n.** | **Abductor hallucis**, **FHB**, **FDB**, **lumbricals to 2nd + 3rd toes** (TO: **1st lumbrical**) | Medial plantar foot |
| **Lateral plantar n. (LPN)** | **Adductor hallucis**, **quadratus plantae**, **all interossei**, **lateral two lumbricals**, **abductor digiti minimi** (via Baxter's) | **Plantar lateral foot**, lateral 4th toe, 5th toe (TO: **lateral ⅓ plantar foot + 4th + 5th toes**) |
| **Baxter's n.** | **1st branch of LPN** = nerve to **abductor digiti quinti (ADM)** | Courses **anterior to medial tuberosity between QP and FDB** |

**Lateral / medial plantar injury** — **weak or absent MTP flexion** (**intrinsic minus**)

**Medial plantar proper branch** — at risk in **medial-plantar approach to tibial sesamoid**

### Dorsal & cutaneous nerves

| Nerve | Supplies | Most vulnerable |
|---|---|---|
| **Sural** | Dorsal sensation **4th web space (70–80%)** · lateral foot | **Extensile lateral approach to calcaneus** · **screw for proximal 5th MT stress fracture** · **Achilles repair (esp. percutaneous)** · **gastroc recession** · **peroneal tendon repair (posterolateral incision)** |
| **Deep peroneal (DPN)** | Motor **EDB + EHB** · sensation **1st dorsal web space** | Compressed **beneath inferior extensor retinaculum** (anterior tarsal tunnel) · **lateral branch**: **calcaneonavicular bar excision**, **Lisfranc reduction approach** · anterior ankle approach |
| **Superficial peroneal (SPN)** | **Most of dorsum of foot** | **Medial branch (dorsomedial cutaneous)**: **bunion surgery** · **lateral cutaneous branch**: **anterolateral arthroscopy portal**, **distal fibula ORIF (esp. Weber C)** · **ankle inversion injury** · anterior ankle approach (medial branch) |
| **Saphenous** | **Medial side of foot** | — |
| **Tibial** | Plantar foot | **Tarsal tunnel release (posteromedial)** |

**DPN injury** — **weak / absent EDB + EHB** · trauma or **CMT**

**SPN exit from deep fascia** — **12–15 cm** proximal to lateral malleolus tip (Orthobullets, lateral cutaneous branch) · **10–12 cm** (OB) — read the options · wraps **fibular head**, branch of **common peroneal**

## 3 Presentation

### Tarsal tunnel – denervation pattern

**Tarsal tunnel syndrome** — **commonest tibial nerve compression** · plantar numbness, Tinel **posterior to medial malleolus** · EMG **prolonged sensory + distal motor latencies** · untreated → intrinsic **denervation atrophy on MRI**

**Spared muscle** — **peroneus brevis** (**SPN**) — least likely to atrophy

| Coronal T1 MRI label | Muscle | Nerve |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Abductor hallucis** | Medial plantar |
| B | **Quadratus plantae** (flexes 2nd–5th toes) | Lateral plantar |
| C | **Flexor digitorum brevis** | Medial plantar |
| D | **Abductor digiti minimi** | Lateral plantar (Baxter's) |
| **E** | **Peroneus brevis** | **SPN — spared** |

## 5 Management

### Iatrogenic nerve injury by incision

| Incision | Nerve at risk |
|---|---|
| **Gastroc recession (posterior calf)** | **Sural** ✓ |
| Anterior total ankle approach | **Medial branch of SPN + DPN** (✗ tibial) |
| Tarsal tunnel release (posteromedial) | **Tibial nerve + branches** (✗ DPN) |
| Peroneal tendon repair (posterolateral) | **Sural** (✗ SPN) |
| Calcaneus ORIF | **Sural** (✗ lateral plantar) |
| Bunion (medial MTP incision) | **Dorsomedial cutaneous n.** |

**Dorsomedial cutaneous nerve** — **terminal branch of SPN** · **5–15 mm medial / plantar to EHL** · injury → **reduced sensation over medial aspect of great toe** ± painful neuroma (✗ saphenous, ✗ DPN, ✗ motor weakness)

**✗ Motor traps** — 1st MTP **abduction** weakness = **abductor hallucis (medial plantar)** · IP **extension** weakness = **EHL / EHB (DPN)** · IP **flexion** weakness = **tibial n.**

**Dorsomedial cutaneous nerve syndrome** — severe neuritic pain over medial eminence + 1st toe after bunion surgery · nerve transected / scarred → **resection + burial** if nonop fails · **positive nerve block** before surgery

![Dorsomedial cutaneous nerve isolated in a bunion incision](../MCQ/attachments/q3835-a-dmcn.jpg)

### Painful neuroma – neurectomy & burial

**Chronic SPN injury after inversion sprain** — paraesthesia dorsum, **Tinel anterolateral ankle**, no instability · failed 1 yr nonop (steroid, brace, PT) → **neurotomy + transposition to bone** (bone burial > muscle burial for pain) (✗ Semmes-Weinstein — diabetic screening, ✗ PRP — no evidence, ✗ neuroma excision + nerve grafting, ✗ Broström-Gould — no instability)

**Glass laceration, plantar lateral forefoot numbness** — **lateral plantar nerve laceration** (MRI: through abductor hallucis + LPN) (✗ foreign body, ✗ Baxter's — Tinel deep to abductor hallucis, ✗ interdigital neuroma — Mulder's click, ✗ digital nerve — more distal)

## X vs Y

| | **SPN** | **DPN** |
|---|---|---|
| Sensation | **Dorsum of foot**, dorsomedial hallux | **1st dorsal web space** |
| Motor (foot) | None | **EDB, EHB** |
| Classic injury | Bunion (medial branch), anterolateral portal, Weber C, inversion sprain | Anterior tarsal tunnel, CN bar excision, Lisfranc approach |

| | **Medial plantar** | **Lateral plantar** |
|---|---|---|
| Motor | AbH, FHB, FDB, lumbricals 2–3 | AdH, QP, all interossei, lateral 2 lumbricals, ADM |
| Sensation | Medial plantar | Plantar lateral, 4th (lateral) + 5th toes |

## How it's asked

**"Nerve looped in medial bunion incision — true?"** → **terminal branch of the superficial peroneal nerve; reduced sensation over medial aspect of great toe**

**"Correct incision–nerve pairing?"** → **gastroc recession – sural nerve**

**"Chronic SPN pain after ankle sprain, failed nonop — next?"** → **neurotomy and transposition to bone**

**"Stepped on glass, plantar lateral numbness, no Mulder's, no Tinel?"** → **lateral plantar nerve laceration**

**"Tarsal tunnel — muscle least likely to atrophy?"** → **peroneus brevis (E)**

**"First branch of LPN?"** → **Baxter's nerve (to abductor digiti minimi)**

**"Sensation to 1st dorsal web space?"** → **deep peroneal nerve**
