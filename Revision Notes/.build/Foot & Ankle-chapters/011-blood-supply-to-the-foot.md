# Blood Supply to the Foot

*Foot & Ankle · 11 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Source arteries

**Three source arteries** — **peroneal (fibular)** · **posterior tibial (PTA)** · **anterior tibial (ATA)**

| Artery | Origin | Course in leg | Ankle branches | Foot branches |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Peroneal** | **PTA, \~2.5 cm from its origin** | Pierces interosseous membrane **\~5 cm above lateral malleolus** (perforating branch) → joins **anterior lateral malleolar a.** → anterior to syndesmosis → **lateral tarsal a.** | **Posterior lateral malleolar** · communicating | **Lateral calcaneal branch** (terminal) |
| **Posterior tibial** | **Larger** terminal branch of popliteal; proximal part = **tibioperoneal trunk** | Between superficial + deep posterior compartment → medial, **palpable behind medial malleolus** | **Posterior medial malleolar** · communicating · **artery of tarsal canal** | **Bifurcates beneath sustentaculum** → medial + lateral plantar |
| **Anterior tibial** | **Smaller** terminal branch of popliteal | **Anterior to interosseous membrane**, supplies anterior compartment · **superficial at ankle midway between malleoli** | **Anterior medial + anterior lateral malleolar** | **Dorsalis pedis** |

**Peroneal supplies (leg)** — soleus, tibialis posterior, FHL, peroneals

![Arterial supply of the foot and ankle](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12114-fig-3.jpg)

### Hindfoot & talus

**Artery of tarsal canal** — branch of **PTA** · **dominant blood supply to talar body**

**Artery of sinus tarsi** — branch of **perforating peroneal a.** · with tarsal canal a. supplies **talus** (✗ heel pad)

**Lateral calcaneal branch of peroneal** — **terminal** branch · perfuses **lateral flap of extensile lateral approach to calcaneus** · one of three flap arteries with **lateral malleolar + lateral tarsal** · **minor** contribution to heel pad

![Extensile lateral calcaneal incision — flap on lateral calcaneal artery](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12114-fig10.jpg)

### Heel pad blood supply

**Heel pad** — **medial calcaneal branch of the posterior tibial artery** = major supply (✗ lateral calcaneal a. — minor, ✗ medial plantar a., ✗ artery of sinus tarsi)

**Medial calcaneal branch** — **first branch of lateral plantar a.** (Orthobullets) · "branch of posterior tibial artery" (OB) — same vessel, read the options

**Heel pad avulsion** — high-energy (industrial machine) · fibrous honeycomb septa + specialised skin → **irreplaceable** · risk **heel pad necrosis** → poor prognosis, salvage often fails · replantation with **ipsilateral saphenous vein graft** described

### Plantar branches (PTA)

| Lateral plantar a. branches (in order) | Medial plantar a. |
|---|---|
| 1st **medial calcaneal** · 2nd **abductor digiti minimi** · 3rd **digital branch to 5th toe** · terminal **plantar branch** | Given off **under flexor retinaculum** · supplies **medial side of foot** · terminal → anastomoses with **1st dorsal metatarsal a. (DPA)** · superficial digital branches → plantar metatarsal aa. of **first three** IM spaces |

### Dorsal supply (ATA)

**Dorsalis pedis** — continuation of ATA · palpable **just lateral to EHL** · branches **arcuate, lateral tarsal, medial tarsal** · ends at **1st IM space** → **1st dorsal metatarsal a.** + **deep plantar arch**

**Plantar arch** — **lateral plantar a. + dorsalis pedis** · plantar digital + plantar metatarsal aa.

**Arcuate artery** — dorsal midfoot, **deep to extensor tendons** · dorsal metatarsal aa. to **2nd, 3rd, 4th** IM spaces

![Dorsalis pedis and arcuate artery](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob12114-dorsalis-pedis-foot.jpg)

### Veins

**Dorsal venous arch** — drains into **greater + lesser saphenous** systems

## X vs Y

| | **Medial calcaneal branch (PTA)** | **Lateral calcaneal branch (peroneal)** |
|---|---|---|
| Supplies | **Heel pad (major)** | **Extensile lateral calcaneal flap** · heel pad (minor) |
| Injury context | Heel pad avulsion | Lateral wound necrosis after calcaneus ORIF |

| | **Artery of tarsal canal** | **Artery of sinus tarsi** |
|---|---|---|
| Parent | **PTA** | **Perforating peroneal** |
| Supplies | **Talar body (dominant)** | Talus |

## How it's asked

**"Heel pad avulsion — baseline blood supply of the heel pad?"** → **medial calcaneal branch of the posterior tibial artery** (✗ lateral calcaneal branch of peroneal — minor, ✗ medial plantar, ✗ sinus tarsi a.)

**"Extensile lateral calcaneal flap perfused by?"** → **lateral calcaneal branch of peroneal artery**

**"Dominant blood supply to talar body?"** → **artery of tarsal canal (PTA)**
