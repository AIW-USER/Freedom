# Tarsal Tunnel Syndrome

*Foot & Ankle · 45 / 48*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **compressive neuropathy of the tibial nerve** at the tarsal tunnel → pain + paraesthesia of the **plantar foot**

**Diagnosis** — **clinical**: burning plantar pain + **Tinel's over tibial nerve** · EMG / NCS can help confirm · **history = most useful diagnostic aid**

### Tarsal tunnel – anatomy, causes (flatfoot, space-occupying lesion) & symptoms

| Posterior tarsal tunnel | Structure |
|---|---|
| **Roof (superficial)** | **Flexor retinaculum (laciniate ligament)** |
| **Medial wall (deep)** | **Calcaneus + talus** |
| **Inferior** | **Abductor hallucis** |
| **Contents** | **Tibial nerve · posterior tibial artery · FHL · FDL · tibialis posterior** |

![Posterior tarsal tunnel — flexor retinaculum over tibial nerve, PTA and flexor tendons](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7042-tarsal-tunnel2.jpg)

**Tibial nerve course** — along fascia of **tibialis posterior in deep posterior compartment** → gives off **medial calcaneal nerve just proximal to ankle** → dives **deep to flexor retinaculum** → most often **bifurcates in the tunnel** into medial + lateral plantar nerves

**3 distal branches** — **medial plantar · lateral plantar · medial calcaneal** · bifurcation **proximal to tunnel in 5%**

**Plantar nerves** — pass through **two separate tunnels within and deep to abductor hallucis** (superficial + deep fascial layers) · can be compressed in their own sheath **distal** to the tarsal tunnel

| Intrinsic causes | Extrinsic causes |
|---|---|
| **Ganglion cyst** (classic) | Shoes |
| Tendinopathy, tenosynovitis | Trauma |
| Lipoma / tumour | Deformity — **tarsal coalition, valgus hindfoot** |
| Perineural fibrosis | Post-surgical scarring |
| Osteophytes | Systemic inflammatory disease, lower-limb oedema |

**Identifiable cause** — **80%** of cases · commonest = **space-occupying lesion in the tunnel proper** (OB)

**Heel pain triad** — **posterior tibial tendon deficiency (adult-acquired flatfoot) + plantar fasciitis + tarsal tunnel syndrome** · loss of **static + dynamic stabilisers of medial arch → traction neuropathy** of tibial nerve · **86% improvement** after surgery (Labib)

**✗ Not associated with TTS** — insertional Achilles tendinitis · hypermobile first ray · metatarsal stress fracture · gastrocnemius contracture

### Baxter's nerve (heel pain) (TO)

**Tibial nerve divisions (TO)** — **medial calcaneal nerve (MCN) · medial plantar nerve (MPN) · lateral plantar nerve (LPN) · first branch of LPN** · tibial = larger of the two sciatic divisions

**Nerve in heel pain syndrome (TO)** — **1st branch of lateral plantar nerve** (✗ medial plantar nerve, ✗ medial calcaneal nerve, ✗ 2nd branch of sural nerve)

**First branch of LPN (TO)** — = **nerve to abductor digiti minimi** · sensory branches to **calcaneal periosteum** · **supplies no skin**

**Commonest neural cause of plantar heel pain (TO)** — **entrapment of first branch of LPN**

**Entrapment sites of first branch (TO)** — **sharp edge of deep fascia of abductor hallucis** · **just distal to medial edge of calcaneus**

**Second commonest (TO)** — **medial calcaneal nerve**

**LPN (TO)** — supplies **most foot muscles** · skin of **lateral ⅓ of plantar foot + 4th and 5th toes** · entrapped **between abductor hallucis and quadratus plantae**

**MPN (TO)** — motor **abductor hallucis, FHB, FDB, 1st lumbrical** · skin **medial ⅔ of plantar foot** · entrapment **less common** than the others

### Anterior tarsal tunnel (deep peroneal nerve)

| Anterior tarsal tunnel | Orthobullets | OB |
|---|---|---|
| **Roof (most superficial)** | **Inferior extensor retinaculum** | **Inferior extensor retinaculum** |
| Floor (deep) | Fascia over **talus + navicular** | **Talonavicular capsule** |
| Medial / lateral | — | **Medial malleolus / lateral malleolus** |
| **Contents** | **DPN + branches, EHL, EDL, peroneus tertius, dorsalis pedis a.** | Same **+ tibialis anterior + dorsalis pedis vein** |

**EHB** — **not in the tunnel** (originates **distal** to it) · DPN may also be trapped **under the EHB tendon** (OB)

![Anterior tarsal tunnel — DPN under the inferior extensor retinaculum, EHL, EDL, EHB](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7042-anterior-tibial-tunnel.jpg)

**Commonest DPN entrapment site** — **under the inferior extensor retinaculum = anterior tarsal tunnel syndrome** (✗ EHB tendon, ✗ TN osteophytes, ✗ os intermetatarseum, ✗ base of 5th MT)

**Other DPN compressors (runners)** — **dorsal talonavicular osteophytes** · **os intermetatarseum** (between 1st + 2nd MT bases)

**Causes** — contusion of dorsum · **tight shoe laces / compressive shoes** (commonest) · TN osteophytes · ganglion · **pes cavus** · scar · synovial cyst · hypertrophic extensor muscles · **dorsalis pedis aneurysm** · **dorsal navicular avulsion fracture**

**Peroneal neuropathy** — **commonest compressive neuropathy of lower limb** · CPN at **fibular prominence** · SPN at **exit from lateral compartment** · DPN **under extensor retinaculum**

**Frequency** — anterior TTS **5% of foot numbness**, often misdiagnosed

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — **prolonged standing / walking** · **vague, misleading medial foot pain** · **sharp, burning** · may radiate proximally **up into the calf**

**Numbness** — **intermittent paraesthesia + numbness of plantar foot** · lateral foot burning = **lateral plantar nerve fibres** · history of trauma or surgery

**Exam** — **Tinel's over tibial nerve** · sensory exam equivocal · **pes planus** · **intrinsic wasting: abductor digiti quinti or abductor hallucis**

| Provocative test | Manoeuvre |
|---|---|
| **Compression test** | **Plantar flexion + inversion + digital pressure over tunnel** → **highly sensitive + specific** |
| **Dorsiflexion–eversion test** | **Ankle dorsiflexion + heel eversion + toe dorsiflexion** → reproduces pain / paraesthesia |
| Simple stretch | Pain with **dorsiflexion + eversion** |

**Ganglion in tunnel** — **burning + numbness on the bottom of the foot** · pain medial heel + plantar foot · affects **toe flexion** (✗ loss of toe extension)

**Nerve** — **tibial nerve** (✗ sural, ✗ saphenous, ✗ SPN, ✗ DPN — not in the triad)

**Anterior TTS** — **dull ache / vague dorsomedial pain, numbness, paraesthesia of dorsum → 1st web space** · ↑ with **plantar flexion**, inactivity, **tight shoes** · **EDB atrophy** · **Tinel's over anterior ankle** radiating between 1st + 2nd toes · ballet dancers, marathon runners, hikers

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding / use |
|---|---|
| **Weight-bearing X-ray** | Osseous structure (deformity, coalition, osteophytes) |
| **MRI** | Rules out **accessory muscle** or **soft-tissue tumour** · **ganglion cyst in tunnel** (sagittal: bright fluid lesion) |
| Ultrasound | Complements clinical diagnosis |
| **Lateral foot X-ray (anterior TTS)** | **Dorsal navicular avulsion fragment** / dorsal osteophytes compressing DPN |

![Sagittal MRI — ganglion cyst in the tarsal tunnel](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7042-6a-moved.jpg)

### Diagnostic criteria

| EMG / NCS (posterior TTS) | Positive finding |
|---|---|
| **Distal motor latency** | **≥ 7.0 ms** |
| **Sensory latency** | **> 2.3 ms** |
| **Motor amplitude** | ↓ to **abductor hallucis** or **abductor digiti minimi** |
| Which first | **Sensory (SAP) abnormal before / more often than motor** |

**Electrodiagnostics** — **not very sensitive or specific**, **false negatives** in symptomatic patients · still helpful to **localise compression** (nerve root vs tarsal tunnel vs more distal) · **EMG confirms anterior TTS**

## 5 Management

```
Tarsal tunnel syndrome
├─ Initial → NSAIDs, SSRIs, orthosis / footwear, short-leg cast
└─ Failed 3–6 months nonop
       ├─ Compressive mass (ganglion) → TARSAL TUNNEL RELEASE (best results)
       ├─ Positive EMG + reproducible exam → TARSAL TUNNEL RELEASE
       └─ Traction neuritis → release, poorer response
```

**Nonoperative** — anti-inflammatories · **SSRIs** · orthosis / footwear to correct **hindfoot alignment** · period of **short-leg cast** · usually **ineffective**

### Operative criteria

| Release indicated after **3–6 months** failed nonop **and** | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Compressive mass (ganglion cyst)** identified | **Best results** — compressing anatomic structure removed |
| **Positive EMG** | — |
| **Reproducible physical findings** | **Positive Tinel's pre-op** → good results |
| Traction neuritis | Responds less well |

**Success** — **50–90%** (Orthobullets) · **44–96%** (OB, Ahmad) — read the options · **early** operation to prevent fibrosis

### Release sites & causes of recurrence

**Technique** — **identify the nerve proximally** → release all layers

| Must release | ✗ Not a TTS site |
|---|---|
| **Flexor retinaculum** | **Medial band of the plantar fascia** |
| **Deep investing fascia of the leg** | — |
| **Superficial fascia of abductor hallucis** | — |
| **Deep fascia of abductor hallucis** | — |

![Compression sites of the tibial nerve — high tarsal tunnel, tarsal tunnel, distal sites, jogger's foot](../MCQ/attachments/q3850-illa-compression-sites.jpg)

**Anterior TTS** — nonop: **activity modification, remove compressive shoes** → surgical release · **endoscopic** for **osteophytes / isolated ganglion** · **open** for **synovitis / unknown cause**

## 6 Complications

### Recurrence

**Commonest cause** — **incomplete / inadequate release** (✗ < 5% recurrence, ✗ revision = primary outcome, ✗ motor latencies more sensitive)

**Other causes** — adhesive neuritis · **neuroma** · incorrect initial diagnosis · infection · **double crush** · bleeding + scarring · anatomic variation · pre-existing nerve damage

**Recurrence rate** — **5–40%**

**Revision** — **less successful than index operation** · **repeat release not recommended** (Orthobullets; some authors) · barrier materials ↓ adherence · **excise neuromas** · then nerve stimulators, systemic medication

**Worse prognosis** — **double crush** · post-operative scarring · revision surgery

## X vs Y

| | **Posterior TTS** | **Anterior TTS** | **Baxter's nerve** (TO) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nerve | **Tibial** | **Deep peroneal** | **1st branch LPN** |
| Roof | **Flexor retinaculum** | **Inferior extensor retinaculum** | **Deep fascia of abductor hallucis** |
| Symptoms | **Burning plantar foot** | **Dorsum → 1st web space** | Plantar heel pain |
| Motor | AbH / ADM wasting | **EDB atrophy** | **Abductor digiti minimi** |

| Heel / foot pain | Clue |
|---|---|
| **Plantar fasciitis** | **Worst first thing in the morning** · commonest misdiagnosis of TTS |
| **Tarsal tunnel** | Burning plantar numbness, Tinel's, ganglion on MRI |
| Malignant tumour (e.g. synovial sarcoma) | **Night pain** · aggressive imaging |
| Os trigonum | Impingement, **no burning / tingling** |
| **Missed foot compartment syndrome** | **Intrinsic-minus (claw) toes** from interosseous + lumbrical fibrosis · patchy paresis |
| **L5/S1 radiculopathy** | **Dermatomal** numbness, radiating pain |
| **Morton's neuroma** | **2nd / 3rd interdigital nerve** at transverse intermetatarsal ligament |
| **CRPS** | Diffuse burning pain, touch / movement intolerable |

## How it's asked

**"Flatfoot, burning lateral foot, medial heel tenderness — nerve?"** → **tibial nerve** (✗ sural, ✗ saphenous, ✗ SPN, ✗ DPN)

**"Associated with tarsal tunnel syndrome?"** → **adult-acquired flatfoot** (heel pain triad)

**"Plantar burning after failed stretching + night splints, sagittal MRI cyst?"** → **tarsal tunnel syndrome** (✗ recalcitrant plantar fasciitis)

**"Ganglion in tarsal tunnel — symptom?"** → **burning and numbness on the bottom of the foot**

**"Release sites EXCEPT?"** → **medial band of the plantar fascia**

**"Recurrent TTS — true?"** → **commonest cause is incomplete release**

**"Revision release outcome?"** → **worse than primary**

**"Sensory vs motor latencies?"** → **sensory prolonged first**

**"Best surgical results when?"** → **compressing structure (ganglion) identified + removed**

**"Ballet dancer, dorsomedial pain to 1st web space, EDB atrophy, navicular avulsion?"** → **anterior tarsal tunnel syndrome**

**"Commonest DPN entrapment site?"** → **inferior extensor retinaculum**

**"Most superficial boundary of the space (dorsal pain to 1st–2nd toes)?"** → **inferior extensor retinaculum** (✗ EHL, ✗ EDL, ✗ peroneus tertius — inside, ✗ EHB — distal)

**"Nerve involved in heel pain syndrome?"** → **1st branch of lateral plantar nerve** (TO)
