# Synovitis of 2nd MTP

*Foot & Ankle · 43 / 48*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **commonest monoarticular synovitis of the MTP joints**

**Risk factors** — **elongated 2nd MT relative to 1st (Morton foot)** · **hallux valgus**

### Plantar plate anatomy

**Role** — weight-bearing pushes proximal phalanx **dorsally** · **plantar plate + intrinsics (interossei, lumbricals)** resist → hold phalanx neutral at MTP

**Stabilisers of 2nd MTP** — **plantar plate + collateral ligaments**

![Sagittal MRI of lesser MTP plantar plate (FT flexor tendon, ET extensor tendon)](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7016-mri-plantar-plate.jpg)

## 2 Mechanism

**Cascade** — **synovitis → capsuloligamentous stretch → instability → deformity**

**Plantar plate attenuation** — MTP **extension** · **sagittal-plane deformity** · → **crossover toe**

**MTP instability** — → **dorsal dislocation of MTP** · predisposes to **hammer toe**

### Plantar plate deficiency – crossover toe deformity

**Plantar plate deficiency — commonest association** — **crossover toe deformity** (✗ Freiberg's infraction — no plantar plate pathology, ✗ Lisfranc injury — acute trauma, ✗ claw toe — weak intrinsics vs stronger EDL on extensor sling, ✗ curly toe — FDL + FDB contracture, unknown cause)

**High-grade 2nd MTP plantar plate rupture — greatest risk** — **second toe crossover** (✗ hallux varus, ✗ hallux valgus — may contribute to or follow crossover but not from rupture, ✗ Freiberg's — microtrauma → blood supply loss → 2nd MT head collapse, ✗ 2nd mallet toe — DIP flexion + lateral deviation, FDL contracture)

**Crossover toe** — 2nd toe crosses **over or under the hallux** · requires **plantar plate + collateral ligament** failure

**Order of failure** — **LCL fails first → medial deviation**, plantar plate **second → dorsal displacement** of proximal phalanx (OB) · (Orthobullets, MTP dislocations): **plantar plate first (stage 1), LCL second (stage 2)** — read the options

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain · **warm** joint · **fullness** of joint · "walking on marbles" (OB)

**Tenderness** — **plantar over plantar plate** or **over dorsal capsule** · may be in **2nd web space** (inflammation or pressure on interdigital nerve)

**Motion** — pain with ROM, **↓ with traction** · **↓ plantar flexion** · global MTP swelling

**Deformity** — **passively correctable** in predislocation stages · progresses to **crossover** (varus or valgus) when collateral + plantar plate disrupted

**Dorsal drawer test** — demonstrates instability · **dorsal subluxation possible = plantar plate attenuated**

### Diagnosis – pain worse barefoot

**Synovitis finding** — **more pain when barefoot** (✗ less pain barefoot — neuroma) · **toe ROM painful** (✗ painless ROM — neuroma)

**✗ Neuroma signs** — **pain + click with medial–lateral forefoot compression** (Mulder) · **pain with 2nd–3rd web-space compression**

**Site** — Morton's neuroma usually **3rd interspace** · pain in **2nd interspace → think 2nd MTP synovitis / metatarsalgia**

**Why it matters** — **steroid injection** helps neuroma but **weakens capsuloligamentous structures** → instability → **dorsal subluxation, crossover toe**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Weight-bearing AP + lateral foot** | **Widening / medial–lateral joint-space imbalance** of 2nd MTP |
| X-ray | **Dorsal subluxation** — looks like **joint-space narrowing** or **proximal phalanx overlapping MT head** |
| X-ray | Varus or valgus toe deformity |
| **MRI** | If **diagnosis unclear** · **quantifies plantar plate / ligament disruption** |

![Dorsal dislocation of the 2nd MTP joint — phalanx overlaps MT head](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7016-2nd-mtp-dislocation-courtesy-richardson-moved.jpg)

### Causes of metatarsalgia (TO)

**Metatarsalgia (TO)** — pain in forefoot **under one or more MT heads** · groups: **primary, secondary, iatrogenic** (after forefoot surgery)

| Primary (TO) | Secondary (TO) |
|---|---|
| Plantar soft-tissue lesions | **Iatrogenic** — 1st ray shortening, MT head resection |
| Abnormal metatarsal parabola | **Neurogenic** — referred pain, tarsal tunnel syndrome |
| **Morton's foot** | OA · RA |
| Neuroma | **Atrophied plantar fat pad** · stress fracture |
| Trauma · brachymetatarsia | Capsulitis / bursitis / tendinitis · **Freiberg's infraction** |
| Bone tumours | Pes cavus / planus · inappropriate shoegear |

| Rocker (TO) | Phase | Metatarsalgia from |
|---|---|---|
| **1st** | Heel contact → foot flat | Congenital deformity, **tight heel cord**, severe cavus |
| **2nd** | Tibia over talus; triceps surae activated at end | **Limited ankle DF**, **plantarflexed metatarsals** |
| **3rd** | Foot turns over dorsiflexed MTP joints | **MTP joint deformities** |

**Work-up (TO)** — exam of plantar foot + **weight-bearing radiographs**

## 5 Management

```
2nd MTP synovitis
├─ First line → shoe / activity change, NSAIDs, taping 10–12 wk
├─ No deformity, failed nonop → synovectomy
└─ Fixed deformity
       ├─ Long 2nd MT → Weil distal oblique shortening osteotomy
       └─ No long 2nd MT, sagittal → Girdlestone-Taylor FDL → EDL / capsular release + EDL lengthening
```

### Operative criteria

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **First line** | Activity / shoe change, NSAIDs, external MTP support (**crossover taping, Budin-type splint**) for **10–12 weeks** |
| **No deformity + failed nonop** | **Synovectomy** |
| **Fixed deformity + long 2nd MT** | **Weil (distal oblique shortening MT osteotomy)** |
| **Fixed deformity, NO long 2nd MT**, sagittal deformity | **FDL → EDL transfer (Girdlestone-Taylor)** or **MTP capsular release + extensor lengthening** |
| ✗ | **Steroid injection** — contraindicated (weakens capsule) |

### Weil osteotomy technique

**Weil used for (TO)** — **metatarsalgia** (✗ Perthes, ✗ SCFE, ✗ genu valgum) (TO)

**Nonop metatarsalgia first (TO)** — physio, orthotics, shoe modification, injections

**Definition (TO)** — **distal oblique osteotomy of MT head** · corrects **transverse + sagittal** MTP pathology · **decompresses MTP joint → relaxes soft tissues**

**Orthobullets** — preserves joint · **rebalances MT cascade** · relaxes plantar plate

**Indications (TO)** — **long or plantarflexed metatarsal** · **transverse-plane MTP deformity** · **MTP subluxation / dislocation** · **recalcitrant generalised metatarsalgia**

**Technique (TO)** — start at **dorsal articular surface** of MT head · cut **dorsal-distal → proximal-plantar**, **30° to shaft**, **parallel to weight-bearing surface** · **screw** fixation

![Weil osteotomy: dorsal-distal to proximal-plantar cut, head slides proximally, screw](../MCQ/attachments/TO19356199-2.png)

**Long 2nd MT + fixed dorsiflexion deformity, "walking on marbles" after 4 mo orthotics** — Weil · avoid commonest complication (**floating toe**) by **osteotomy parallel to plantar surface of foot** (✓)

**✗ Weil traps** — protect **collateral ligaments** (prevents **AVN** — much rarer than floating toe) · **inject plantar to intermetatarsal ligament** (steroid ↑ instability) · **shorten ≥ 4–7 mm** (over-shortening → plantar displacement → floating toe; **shorten ≤ 3 mm**) · **strict immobilisation 4 wk** (↑ stiffness; use **protected WB in surgical shoe + aggressive ROM**)

**Other floating-toe prevention** — remove a **wafer of bone** (shorten without plantar migration) · combine soft-tissue procedures

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Floating toe** | **Commonest after Weil** (OB) — **plantar shift of MT head centre of rotation** → intrinsics pass **dorsal** to axis → act as **MTP dorsiflexors** |
| **Weil (TO)** | **Dorsiflexed toe** · pain on MTP DF · **↓ MTP ROM** · **PIP contracture** (TO) |
| AVN | Collateral ligament injury · uncommon |
| Stiffness | Prolonged immobilisation |
| **Vascular compromise** | Correcting **chronic dislocation** → contracted vessels stretched → **may need to reverse procedure** to save digit |

## X vs Y

| | **2nd MTP synovitis** | **Morton's neuroma** |
|---|---|---|
| Interspace | **2nd** | **3rd** (usually) |
| Barefoot | **Worse** | Less pain |
| Toe ROM | **Painful** (↓ with traction) | Painless |
| Compression | — | **Click** (medial–lateral), web-space pain |
| Drawer | **+** if unstable | — |
| Steroid | ✗ **worsens instability** | Appropriate |

| Deformity | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Crossover toe** | **Plantar plate + collateral ligament** failure |
| Claw toe | Weak intrinsics, EDL overpull on extensor sling |
| Curly toe | FDL + FDB contracture |
| Mallet toe | FDL contracture — DIP flexion |
| Freiberg's | 2nd MT head collapse (blood supply) |

## How it's asked

**"Finding most likely in 2nd MTP synovitis?"** → **more pain when barefoot**

**"Click on medial–lateral forefoot compression?"** → **Morton's neuroma** (✗ synovitis)

**"Plantar plate deficiency most associated with?"** → **crossover toe deformity**

**"High-grade 2nd MTP plantar plate rupture — progressive deformity?"** → **second toe crossover**

**"Weil's osteotomy is used for?"** → **metatarsalgia** (TO)

**"Long 2nd MT, fixed deformity — avoid commonest Weil complication?"** → **osteotomy parallel to plantar surface of foot**

**"Commonest complication of Weil?"** → **floating toe**

**"Maximum Weil shortening?"** → **≤ 3 mm** (✗ 4–7 mm)

**"Fixed deformity without long 2nd MT?"** → **FDL → EDL transfer / capsular release + extensor lengthening**

**"No deformity, failed nonop?"** → **synovectomy**

**"Why avoid steroid injection?"** → **weakens capsule → instability, crossover**

**"Correcting chronic dislocation — feared complication?"** → **vascular compromise → reverse procedure**
