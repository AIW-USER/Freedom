# Gait Cycle

*Foot & Ankle · 22 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Definitions

**Gait cycle** — **heel strike → heel strike** of the same limb

**Stance vs swing** — **60% / 40%** (Orthobullets, OB) · **62% / 38%** (another OB) — read the options

**Stride** — consecutive initial contacts of the **same (ipsilateral) foot**

**Step** — initial contacts of **alternating (contralateral) feet**

**Stance phases (5)** — initial contact → loading response → mid-stance → terminal stance → pre-swing

**Swing phases (3)** — initial swing → mid-swing → terminal swing

| Phase (Rancho terms) | Classic term | Definition | % cycle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Initial contact** | Heel strike | Foot contacts ground | 0–2% |
| **Loading response** (1st double support) | Foot flat | Initial contact → **elevation of opposite limb**; weight transferred to stance limb | 0–10% |
| **Mid-stance** (single support) | Midstance | Opposite limb elevation → **both ankles aligned in coronal plane** | 10–30% |
| **Terminal stance** (single support) | Heel off | **Supporting heel rises** → opposite heel contacts | 30–50% |
| **Pre-swing** (2nd double support) | Toe off | Opposite initial contact → just before ipsilateral elevation | 50–60% |
| **Initial swing** | Acceleration | Limb elevation → **maximal knee flexion** | 60–73% |
| **Mid-swing** | Midswing | Knee flexion → **tibia vertical** | 73–87% |
| **Terminal swing** | Deceleration | Tibia vertical → just before initial contact | 87–100% |

### Prerequisites of gait – Gage (TO)

**Five prerequisites (TO)** — **stability in stance** · **adequate step length** · **clearance in swing** · **foot prepositioning** (in swing) · **conservation of energy**

**NOT prerequisites (TO)** — ✗ **foot clearance in stance** (clearance is a swing requirement) · ✗ **adequate stride length** (it is **step** length) (TO)

### Tibialis anterior – eccentric at heel strike / loading response

**TA eccentric** — **heel strike (initial contact) + loading response** → lowers foot to ground, controls **plantar-flexion moment**, limb loading + shock absorption

**TA concentric** — **initial swing + mid-swing** (→ terminal swing) → dorsiflexion for **foot clearance**, prepositions foot for contact

**TA inactive** — **mid-stance, terminal stance, pre-swing** (✗ eccentric at any of these)

**Gastrocsoleus** — **dormant at heel strike** → **eccentric at foot flat / mid-stance** (weight moves forward over foot) → **concentric at toe-off (push-off)** → relaxed in swing (✗ GS eccentric at heel strike, ✗ GS concentric at heel strike, ✗ GS concentric in swing, ✗ TA concentric at toe-off)

**TA weakness (peroneal nerve palsy)** — mild–moderate → **foot slap** at heel strike · in swing → **steppage gait** (↑ hip + knee flexion to clear foot)

**Primary antagonist of TA** — **peroneus longus** (PF + everts) → **superficial peroneal nerve** · TA dorsiflexes + inverts

**Other antagonist pair** — **peroneus brevis vs tibialis posterior**

**Strength** — ankle plantarflexors **6×** stronger than dorsiflexors

### Muscle activity by phase

| Phase | Active muscles |
|---|---|
| **Initial contact** | **Hip extensors** stabilise hip · **quadriceps eccentric** · **TA eccentric** |
| **Loading response** | **TA eccentric** (controls PF moment) · **quads** stabilise knee vs flexion moment |
| **Mid-stance** | **Gluteus medius + calf eccentric** |
| **Terminal stance** | **Toe flexors + tibialis posterior** most active |
| **Pre-swing** | **Hip flexors** propel advancing limb |
| **Initial swing** | **Hip flexors concentric** advance the leg · TA concentric |
| **Mid-swing** | **Ankle dorsiflexors** → foot clearance |
| **Terminal swing** | **Hamstrings** decelerate thigh |

### Swing mechanics

**Swing momentum** — generated by **ankle plantar flexors (gastrocsoleus) + hip flexors at terminal stance** (✗ hip flexors + dorsiflexors in early swing, ✗ knee flexors in early swing, ✗ knee extensors)

**Knee in swing** — flexion in early swing + extension in terminal swing are **passive**

**Main concentric contraction in swing** — **tibialis anterior**

**Activity** — **more muscle activity in stance** than swing (stabilise plantigrade foot)

**FHL** — plantarflexes hallux at **push-off**; **inactive in swing**

### Tendon function – tibialis posterior

**Origin** — **tibia + fibula + interosseous membrane** (✗ FDL = **tibia only**, ✗ FHL = **fibula only**)

**Activity** — **concentric in terminal stance + pre-swing** (✗ eccentric, ✗ initial swing) · EMG biphasic: initial contact + midstance and pre-swing

**Function** — **most powerful inverter** · dynamic stabiliser of medial arch · **inverts hindfoot + locks Chopart's joint** → gastrocsoleus acts through MT heads (**TP + GS synergistic**)

**Insertions** — anterior: **navicular tuberosity** + plantar 1st cuneiform · middle: bases **MT 2–4**, 2nd + 3rd cuneiforms, cuboid, FHB · posterior recurrent: **sustentaculum tali**

**TP loss** — GS pulls **talar head down** → stretches **spring ligament** → medial arch collapses

**TP dysfunction EMG** — **↑ activity in 2nd half of stance** (delayed / right shift)

**Rupture site** — distal to medial malleolus: flattened, fibrocartilaginous, **hypovascular** zone

### Determinants & centre of gravity

| Variable | Value / effect |
|---|---|
| **Pelvic rotation** | **4° medially (anteriorly)** on swing side → lengthens limb |
| **Pelvic tilt** | Pelvis **drops 4°** on swing side → lowers COG at midstance (Orthobullets) · contralateral drop **\~5°** minimal, gluteal-controlled (TO) |
| **Knee flexion at heel strike** | **15°** (Orthobullets) · **10°** (TO) — lowers COG, absorbs shock · read the options |
| **Ankle** | **Plantar flexion** at heel strike + first part of stance |
| **Knee at midstance** | **Extends** as ankle PF + foot supinates → restores leg length |
| **Lateral pelvic shift** | Over stance limb (COG over base of support) |
| **COG (standing)** | **5 cm anterior to S2** |
| **COG vertical** | **Highest at midstance** · **lowest at double-limb support** |
| **COG horizontal** | **5 cm** per adult male step |

### Stance & running biomechanics (TO)

**Heel at loading (TO)** — **force acceptance + shock absorption, NOT force generation** (✗ "maximum force for further movement generated by heel" = FALSE) (TO)

**Heel strike posture (TO)** — rearfoot in slight **inversion 0–5°** · foot supinates · pelvis level, **anterior tilt 10°**, internally rotated · hip **ER 5–10°, flexed 20–30°** · knee **flexed 10°** (TO)

**Shock-absorption cascade (TO)** — laterally directed GRF → rearfoot **everts** + tibia / femur **IR** + hip adduction + knee flexion (peaks **\~45°**) · all **eccentric** (TO)

**Anterior pelvic tilt up to 10° (TO)** — **acceptable** in early stance (heel strike → foot flat) · excess strains lumbar spine / hamstrings (TO)

**Slow vs fast running (TO)** — slow = **heel strikes first** · fast = **forefoot strikes first** (TRUE) (TO)

**Forefoot strike (TO)** — ↓ vertical GRF, ↓ knee + hip loads · ↑ **Achilles tendinopathy, plantar fasciopathy, MT stress fractures** (TO)

**Tibiotalar pronation (TO)** — present **when foot is on ground** (stance) = **dorsiflexion + eversion + abduction** (TRUE) (TO)

**Midstance (TO)** — ankle → **max dorsiflexion \~20°** · max pronation after max dorsiflexion · peak rearfoot eversion **\~10°**, forefoot abduction **\~5°** (TO)

## 3 Presentation

### Pathological gait – gluteus maximus paralysis (TO)

**Gluteus maximus** — **chief thigh extensor** · erect posture · lateral rotation · abduction · **inferior gluteal nerve (L5, S1, S2)** (TO)

**Gluteus maximus lurch (TO)** — at heel strike **trunk lurches backwards** · line of gravity shifts **posterior to hip** in early stance → compensates weak hip extension (TO)

**NOT seen (TO)** — ✗ **Trendelenburg gait** (gluteus medius) (TO)

### Quadriceps weakness

**Quadriceps atrophy** — affects **stance → midstance** (✗ swing phases — quads largely silent in swing)

**Compensation** — leans forward at hip → COG **anterior to knee** → knee **hyperextension**

**Hamstring / hip-flexor weakness** — affects **swing** (limits limb advancement)

## 5 Management

### Gait after TAA vs arthrodesis

**Greatest difference (level walking)** — **peak plantarflexion power generation** (↑ with TAA)

**TAA** — **\~43% more sagittal ankle ROM** (21.1° vs 14.7°) · ↑ forefoot-tibia + hindfoot-tibia motion · closer to normal but **neither restores normal gait** · differences amplified on **stair ascent**

**Arthrodesis compensations** — ✗ **↑ hip ROM** · ✗ ↑ transient force at heel strike · ✗ may have **better coronal eversion** · ✗ walking speed / cadence difference only modest

**PROMs** — similar improvement after both

## How it's asked

**"TA contracts eccentrically during?"** → **heel strike**

**"Phase of eccentric TA contraction?"** → **loading response** (✗ mid-stance, ✗ terminal stance, ✗ pre-swing, ✗ initial swing)

**"Correct muscle activity in normal gait?"** → **TA contracts eccentrically at heel strike**

**"Primary antagonist of TA innervated by?"** → **superficial peroneal nerve** (peroneus longus)

**"% of gait cycle in stance?"** → **60%**

**"Swing momentum generated by?"** → **ankle plantar flexors + hip flexors at terminal stance**

**"Quadriceps atrophy affects which phase?"** → **midstance**

**"NOT a prerequisite of gait?"** → **foot clearance in stance** (TO)

**"NOT a prerequisite of gait (Gage)?"** → **adequate stride length** (TO)

**"False statement about gait?"** → **heel generates maximum force for further movement** (TO)

**"Gluteus maximus paralysis — all true EXCEPT?"** → **Trendelenburg gait** (TO)

**"Ruptured tendon on axial MRI behind medial malleolus — true?"** → **originates tibia + fibula, concentric in terminal stance**

**"TAA vs ankle arthrodesis — greatest gait difference?"** → **peak plantarflexion power generation**
