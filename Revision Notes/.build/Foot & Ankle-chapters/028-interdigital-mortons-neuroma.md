# Interdigital (Morton's) Neuroma

*Foot & Ankle · 28 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Demographics** — **middle-aged women** · **F:M 9:1** (Orthobullets) · peak **6th decade** (OB)

**Commonest site** — **3rd web space** (3rd + 4th interdigital nerves, between 3rd + 4th MT heads) · **2nd web space = second commonest** (✗ 1st web space, ✗ 1st MTPJ — hallux rigidus / valgus, ✗ 5th MT head)

**Other OB wording** — "2nd or 3rd web space" / "2nd and 3rd interdigital nerves" — read the options

### Anatomy & pathophysiology

**Interdigital nerve** — **plantar to the transverse (deep) intermetatarsal ligament** (✗ dorsal to it) between MT heads

**3rd web space** — **confluence of medial + lateral plantar nerves** (lateral branch MPN + medial branch LPN share a common perineurium) (✗ anterior tarsal tunnel — deep peroneal nerve)

**Nature** — **compressive (entrapment) neuropathy** → **perineural fibrosis + entrapment** of the interdigital nerve

**Mechanism** — compression / tension around **transverse intermetatarsal ligament** at toe-off · **repetitive microtrauma** · traction from tethering between fixed medial + mobile lateral MTs

**Aggravators** — **high heels**, **narrow toe box**, wide feet

## 3 Presentation

### Diagnosis & location (third web space, Mulder's click)

**Pain** — **plantar forefoot**, between MT heads · worse with **weight-bearing + narrow toe-box shoes** · relieved by **removing shoes + massaging foot** · "**walking on a marble**"

**Paraesthesia** — burning / tingling into adjacent toes (3rd + 4th toes for 3rd web space) · plantar aspect of web space

**Most consistent sign** — **tenderness of involved web space** · **positive web-space compression test** · neuroma may be palpable

**Mulder's click** — squeeze forefoot (MTs together) + dorsal pressure between MT heads → painful / bursal click

**Drawer test at MTPJ** — assesses **MTPJ instability** (differential)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **clinical** (history + exam); advanced imaging generally not required

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Use / finding |
|---|---|
| **WB AP / lateral / oblique X-ray** | **Usually normal** · may show bony deformity · excludes Freiberg (MT head flattening), MTP arthritis |
| **Ultrasound** | **Non-palpable neuroma** with clear clinical picture · **oval / well-circumscribed hypoechoic mass parallel to MTs** · **operator dependent** |
| **MRI** | **Not required** (✗ "needed to confirm") · rules out other pathology / stress fracture when exam non-diagnostic |

![MRI: interdigital neuroma (arrow) between MT heads](../MCQ/attachments/q211649-illa-untitled2.jpg)

### Diagnostic criteria

| Test | Positive result |
|---|---|
| **Common digital nerve block** (diagnostic lidocaine) | **Confirms diagnosis** → numbness of lateral toe surface + **relief of pain** |
| **No relief after well-placed block** | Think **MTP synovitis** |
| **Histology** | **Perineural fibrosis + nerve entrapment, no fibrinoid necrosis** |

### Differential – rheumatoid nodule & histology

| Lesion | Histology |
|---|---|
| **Morton's neuroma** | **Perineural fibrosis**, entrapped interdigital nerve, **no fibrinoid necrosis** |
| **Rheumatoid nodule** | Subcutaneous · **central fibrinoid necrosis + palisading fibrous cells** |
| **Neurofibroma** | Hypocellular fibroblastic network, **wire-like collagen**, myxoid areas |
| **Schwannoma (benign PNST)** | Encapsulated · **Antoni A** (palisading spindle cells) + **Antoni B** (hypocellular mucinous) |
| **Plantar fibromatosis** | From **plantar fascia** · dense mature collagen + fibrocytes · not provoked by squeeze |

**Other differentials** — **MTP synovitis**, metatarsalgia, **stress fracture**, MTPJ arthritis, MT head osteonecrosis (Freiberg), neoplasm, lumbar radiculopathy · ✗ **anterior tarsal tunnel** (deep peroneal nerve) · ✗ **tarsal tunnel**

## 5 Management

```
Morton's neuroma
├─ First line → wide toe-box shoe + firm sole + metatarsal pad
│      (± short-term steroid injection, dorsal)
├─ Failed nonop → dorsal neurectomy + DTML release, bury stump
└─ Recurrent / stump neuroma → revision neurectomy (plantar or dorsal) + burial
```

### Nonoperative

**First line** — **flat shoe with wide toe box + firm sole + metatarsal pad** (✗ MRI, ✗ PT — limited role, ✗ serial steroid injections, ✗ custom orthotics before shoe change)

**Metatarsal pad** — offloads MT heads by **↑ intermetatarsal space** → ↓ nerve pressure

**Results** — unpredictable · **\~20% complete resolution** (Orthobullets) · **roughly half** eventually need surgery (OB) · "most" need surgery (another OB) — read the options · NSAIDs rarely help

**Corticosteroid injection** — **short-term relief only**, **no long-term relief** · usually **dorsal** · steroid + LA **not superior** to LA alone · \~50% still request excision · **serial injections → local tissue (fat-pad) atrophy**

**Alcohol sclerosing injection** — not recommended (no long-term data, risk to surrounding tissue)

**✗ Wrong orthoses** — **Morton extension** = hallux rigidus · **longitudinal arch support** = PTT tendinitis · **carbon-fibre insert** = midfoot arthritis · **AFO** = peroneal palsy / foot drop · ✗ "orthotics with ↑ arch height first line"

### Treatment – injection & surgical excision (dorsal vs plantar)

**Failed nonop** — **neurectomy** (most effective, most durable) · **dorsal approach most common** (✗ repeat steroid, ✗ isolated DTML release — less effective, ✗ distal oblique shortening osteotomy / MTP capsular release + extensor lengthening — these are for **MTP synovitis**)

**✗ Percutaneous nerve ablation** — not used

| Dorsal neurectomy step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Incision | **3–4 cm** just proximal to web space |
| Dissection | **Blunt** → protect **superficial peroneal nerve** branches (✗ deep peroneal) |
| Exposure | Spread MTs → tension + **transect transverse intermetatarsal ligament** (✗ "reduces success") |
| Resection | Identify nerve proximal + distal to bifurcation → resect **≥ 3 cm proximal to ligament** |
| Burial | Bury proximal stump in **intrinsic muscles** |
| Closure | **Repair DTML** → prevents intermetatarsal instability |

**Dual neuromas (2nd + 3rd web spaces)** — **resect 2nd web space + neurolysis (DTML release) of 3rd** → equal relief, protects **digital arteries** to 3rd + 4th toes (✗ resect both via two dorsal incisions — highest arterial risk, ✗ leave 3rd untreated, ✗ FDL transfer, ✗ single plantar incision)

## 6 Complications

### Complications of neurectomy

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Stump neuroma – inadequate resection** | **Commonest** cause of persistent / recurrent pain |
| **Inadequate retraction (traction neuritis)** | Plantar branches tether nerve → no retraction (✗ commonest) |
| **Painful plantar scar** | **\~5%**, with **plantar** incision |
| **Intermetatarsal instability** | DTML not repaired |
| **Wrong structure resected** | Digital artery / neurovascular bundle or **lumbrical** |
| **Toe ischaemia** | Digital artery injury in dual web-space surgery |

### Recurrent neuroma – inadequate proximal resection & revision

**Cause** — **inadequate proximal resection** → residual **plantar-directed nerve branches** tether nerve to plantar skin → no retraction out of WB zone of MT head (✗ painful scar, ✗ infection, ✗ Freiberg, ✗ MTP arthritis, ✗ tarsal tunnel, ✗ MTP synovitis)

**Presentation** — same symptoms as primary + **Tinel's sign** at prior resection site

**Resection level** — **≥ 3 cm proximal** to DTML (Orthobullets, OB) · plantar branches **absent 4 cm proximal** → cut there (another OB) — read the options · "5 mm proximal" = inadequate

**Revision** — **revision neurectomy through plantar OR dorsal approach + nerve burial** (✗ steroid + PT, ✗ fishmouth web-space incision — poor view, wound problems, ✗ lumbrical transection → MTP extension deformity, ✗ dual incisions)

**Plantar approach in revision** — preferred by most after a dorsal primary → avoids scar, **direct view of nerve bifurcation**, easier **proximal** dissection, DTML seen directly

**Dorsal approach in revision** — advantage = **lower risk of plantar scar tenderness + wound complications** · **earlier weight-bearing** but **recurrence not lower** · needs DTML release · harder proximal dissection

**Revision outcomes** — function similar · no society recommendation favouring either

## X vs Y

| | **Dorsal approach** | **Plantar approach** |
|---|---|---|
| Use | **Most common primary** | **Preferred for revision** |
| Complete excision | Less likely · revision **8–10%** | **More likely** · revision **< 5%** |
| Sensory loss | **Greater** | Less |
| Scar | No plantar scar · ✓ fewer wound problems | **Painful plantar scar** (\~5%) |
| Weight-bearing | **Earlier** | Longer NWB |
| DTML | Must be **released** | Seen directly |
| Outcomes / satisfaction / ADL limits | Comparable | Comparable |

| | **Morton's neuroma** | **MTP synovitis** |
|---|---|---|
| Site | Web space, plantar | MTP joint |
| Test | Mulder's click, web-space compression | **Drawer test** (instability) |
| Digital nerve block | **Relief** | **No relief** |
| Surgery | Neurectomy | Shortening osteotomy (long 2nd MT) · capsular release + extensor lengthening |

## How it's asked

**"Commonest location?"** → **third web space**

**"Cyclist, pain between 3rd + 4th toes, worse in shoes — next step?"** → **shoes with a wide toe box**

**"Tingling into 3rd + 4th toes, Mulder's click — true?"** → **confluence of medial + lateral plantar nerves**

**"Burning between 3rd + 4th toes, forefoot click — initial management image?"** → **metatarsal pad** (Figure D)

**"Plantar vs dorsal approach — plantar has?"** → **increased likelihood of complete neuroma excision**

**"Commonest complication of the most effective treatment?"** → **neuroma secondary to inadequate resection**

**"Failed shoe change, brief relief from steroid — next?"** → **dorsal neurectomy**

**"Walking on a marble — true of treatment?"** → **corticosteroid injections do not provide long-term relief**

**"2nd + 3rd web-space neuromas — safest plan?"** → **resect 2nd, neurolysis of 3rd by DTML release**

**"Excised 5 mm proximal to ligament, Tinel's positive — cause?"** → **inadequate proximal resection**

**"Commonest cause of persistent pain after excision?"** → **inadequate resection of an interdigital neuroma**

**"Recurrence after dorsal surgery — best step?"** → **revision neurectomy through plantar or dorsal approach with nerve burial**

**"Primary advantage of dorsal approach for revision?"** → **lower risk of plantar scar tenderness + wound complications**

**"Polyarthropathy, ANA+, perineural fibrosis without fibrinoid necrosis?"** → **Morton's neuroma** (✗ rheumatoid nodule)
