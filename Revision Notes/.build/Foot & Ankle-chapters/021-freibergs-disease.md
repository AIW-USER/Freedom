# Freiberg's Disease

*Foot & Ankle · 21 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & diagnosis (2nd metatarsal head, female predominance)

**Definition** — **infarction (infraction) + fracture of the metatarsal head** · **osteochondrosis** · = **AVN / idiopathic osteonecrosis** of MT head · rare

**Sex** — **female > male**: **\~4:1** (Orthobullets, OB) · **5:1** (OB) — read the options

**Age** — **adolescents > adults**: **13–18 yr** (Orthobullets, OB) · **11–17 yr** (OB) · **female adolescent athletes** (✗ 30s–40s)

| MT head involved | Rate |
|---|---|
| **2nd** (most common), **dorsal** aspect | **68%** |
| **3rd** (second commonest) | **27%** |
| **4th** | **3%** |
| 4th + 5th (lateral rays) | **Rare** |
| **1st** | **Rarely / not typically** — concurrent 1st involvement uncommon |

### Pathophysiology

**Cause** — disrupted blood supply from **repetitive microtrauma / stress overloading** or **idiopathic osteonecrosis** → **collapse of 2nd MT head** · multifactorial: vascular compromise, **genetic predisposition**, altered biomechanics

**Risk factors** — **long 2nd metatarsal** · **gastrocnemius contracture** (OB)

**Natural history** — **dorsal articular collapse first**, plantar surface preserved until late → **degenerative arthritis** of 2nd MTP in adulthood · early disease may **resolve spontaneously with MT head remodelling**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — forefoot pain, swelling, stiffness at **2nd MT head / base of 2nd toe**, **worse with WB** · plantar or dorsal forefoot pain · difficulty with push-off · insidious, no trauma

**Exam** — peri-articular swelling · **↓ (passive) ROM of 2nd MTP** · **pain on passive motion of 2nd toe** · pain worse with **distraction (early)** and **compaction (late)**

**Labs** — **ESR + CRP normal** → ✗ osteomyelitis

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| **X-ray AP, lateral, oblique foot** | **Early: usually normal** → **subchondral sclerosis** → **flattening of MT head** (later, ✗ early finding) → **joint destruction** (late) |
| X-ray site | Defect in **upper (dorsal) half of articular surface** |
| **MRI** | **Early disease (stage 1)**: subchondral fracture, **patchy oedema** in MT head · confirms **plantar cartilage sparing** |

![Freiberg disease — flattening of the 2nd metatarsal head](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7018-freibergflat.jpg)

### Smillie classification

| Stage | Finding | Treatment if nonop fails |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Subchondral fracture visible only on MRI** | Nonop |
| **2** | **Dorsal collapse of articular surface** on X-ray | **Debridement + MT osteotomy / DCWO** |
| **3** | **Collapse of dorsal MT head, plantar articular portion intact** (± medial + lateral bony spurs) | **Debridement + MT osteotomy / DCWO** |
| **4** | **Collapse of entire MT head**, joint-space narrowing | **DuVries partial head resection / interposition arthroplasty** |
| **5** | **Severe arthritis, joint-space obliteration** | **DuVries / interposition arthroplasty** |

![Smillie stages 1–5](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7018-sim.jpg)

### Differential diagnosis (✗ distractors)

| ✗ Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| **Morton neuroma** | **Normal X-ray**; pain at **(3rd) web space**, not MT head |
| **Osteomyelitis** | **Normal ESR / CRP** |
| **Mucous cyst** | **DIP joints of hand**, end-stage arthritis |
| **Post-traumatic arthritis** | Isolated progressive 2nd MTP OA without trauma unlikely at 17 |
| **Synovial sarcoma** | Young adults; **commonest sarcoma of foot** but commonest **around knee**; **soft-tissue calcification** |
| Benign bone tumour / normal X-ray | X-ray abnormal |

## 5 Management

```
Freiberg disease
├─ All stages first → NSAIDs, activity change, boot / cast, MT pad
├─ Failed nonop, early–mid (Smillie I–III, plantar cartilage spared) → DCWO ± debridement
└─ Late (IV–V, no plantar cartilage) → DuVries partial head resection / interposition
       (✗ MTP fusion · ✗ polyvinyl implant)
```

### Nonoperative care – metatarsal pad

**Indication** — **early stage** · first line for **all stages**

**Sequence** — **short-leg walking cast or boot 4–6 wk** (severe / orthotics fail) → **stiff-soled shoe with MT bars or pads** (after casting)

**Metatarsal pad** — **offloads 2nd MT head** → relieves pain (✗ steroid · ✗ arthrotomy · ✗ shortening osteotomy · ✗ neck dorsiflexion osteotomy — before nonop fails)

**12-yo, normal ESR / CRP** — **osteochondrosis → boot immobilisation + observation** (✗ osteomyelitis imaging · ✗ AVN → osteotomy / resection · ✗ tumour · ✗ "normal X-ray, reassure")

**Steroid injection** — **↑ intra-articular pressure → may worsen osteonecrosis** · may help **adults with secondary DJD** · short-term relief only, doesn't stop collapse

**Early surgery** — ✗ not to prevent progression; aggressive initial osteotomy ✗

### Surgery – debridement, dorsal closing-wedge osteotomy & contraindications

**Indications** — **progressive pain, joint destruction, deformity** after failed nonop

**Stage II after 2 months failed nonop (16-yo)** — **joint debridement + metatarsal osteotomy** (✗ interposition · ✗ core decompression · ✗ MTP arthrodesis · ✗ more immobilisation)

**MRI plantar cartilage spared, failed 6 wk nonop** — **dorsal closing-wedge (dorsiflexion) osteotomy of 2nd MT head** (✗ 6–8 wk more nonop · ✗ steroid · ✗ interposition — late · ✗ **debridement + cancellous grafting** — second-line, less predictable)

**DCWO goals** — **shortens → offloads MT head** · **resects collapsed dorsal bone + cartilage** · **rotates intact plantar cartilage into contact with proximal phalanx** · lesser-toe MTP approach

![Dorsal closing-wedge osteotomy of the 2nd metatarsal](../Orthobullets/attachments/ob7018-dorsiflexion-osteotomy.jpg)

| DCWO | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Contraindication** | **End-stage OA with severe joint collapse** (no plantar congruity) · end-stage destruction without plantar sparing → poor outcome |
| **Indication ✓** | **AVN of 2nd MT head** (= Freiberg) · **degenerative changes with plantar articular congruity** · **dorsal collapse with moderate arthritis** |
| **Not a contraindication** | **Age** (65-yo) |

**MTP arthrotomy + loose-body removal** — very rarely; after extensive nonop fails · ± **drilling of MT head**, **subchondral bone grafting**, **interposition arthroplasty** · effective in young + adults with DJD

**Interposition tendon** — **EDL** (Orthobullets) · **EDB** (OB, Çevik — ↑ pain, ROM, function in late disease) — read the options

**DuVries arthroplasty (partial MT head resection)** — **severe stage 4–5**, plantar cartilage insufficient · ± capsular interposition

**Full MT head resection** — **avoid → overloads adjacent MT heads**

**Polyvinyl (implant) arthroplasty** — **not approved for lesser toes** (✗ even for diffuse collapse + arthritis)

**Joint-preserving vs sacrificing** — **joint-preserving → higher satisfaction** (✗ MTP fusion superior) · **lesser-toe fusion → unacceptably high nonunion** (✗ reliable union)

## 6 Complications

**Degenerative joint disease** — **2nd MTP arthritis in adulthood** (natural history)

## X vs Y

| | **Early–mid (I–III)** | **Late (IV–V)** |
|---|---|---|
| Plantar cartilage | **Intact** | Lost |
| X-ray | Normal / sclerosis / dorsal collapse | Whole-head collapse, arthritis |
| Surgery | **DCWO ± debridement** | **DuVries / interposition** |

## How it's asked

**"15-yo girl, 2nd MT head flattening — true?"** → **greater prevalence in females**

**"Subchondral sclerosis + flattening of 2nd MT head — true?"** → **more common in females**

**"12-yo, normal ESR / CRP — pathology + next step?"** → **osteochondrosis → immobilisation in boot + observation**

**"Field-hockey player, failed walking cast — diagnosis?"** → **Freiberg's disease**

**"17-yo male, pain on passive 2nd-toe motion — true?"** → **second MTP joint most commonly affected**

**"Plantar pain at 2nd MT head, 3 months — management?"** → **metatarsal pad**

**"Stage II, failed 2 months nonop?"** → **joint debridement + metatarsal osteotomy**

**"Finding that prevents DCWO?"** → **end-stage OA with severe joint collapse**

**"Plantar cartilage spared, failed nonop?"** → **dorsal closing-wedge (dorsiflexion) osteotomy**

**"X-ray supporting polyvinyl arthroplasty?"** → **none — not approved for lesser toes**

**"Early radiographic finding?"** → **normal X-ray** (✗ flattening — late)
