# Achilles Tendonitis

*Foot & Ankle · 3 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Three conditions

| Condition | Who | Site | Key feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Insertional Achilles tendonitis** | **Middle-aged + elderly, tight heel cord** | **At insertion** | Spur, **intratendinous calcification**, progressive bony enlargement |
| **Retrocalcaneal bursitis + Haglund deformity** | **Young** | Bursa **between anterior Achilles + posterior calcaneus** · **posterosuperior calcaneal tuberosity** | Pain **anterior to + 2–3 cm proximal to insertion** |
| **Noninsertional (midportion) tendinopathy** | **Active, middle-aged men** | **2–6 cm proximal to insertion** | Thickening, fusiform swelling |

**Noninsertional** — thought **more common** · insertional **less common** (OB)

### Pathophysiology

**Insertional** — repetitive trauma → inflammation → **cartilaginous then bony metaplasia**

**Noninsertional** — **abnormal vascularity / poor blood supply 2–6 cm proximal** + **repetitive micro-tears**

**Risk factors** — overuse · **dorsiflexor / plantarflexor imbalance** · poor blood supply · genetic · **fluoroquinolones** · inflammatory arthropathy

**Inflammation?** — **degeneration without inflammation** (tendinosis; asymptomatic degeneration from microtrauma + ageing) (OB) · "inflammation and thickening" (another OB) — read the options

### Histology

**Insertional** — **disorganised collagen + mucoid degeneration**, **few inflammatory cells**

**Tendinosis** — **anaerobic degeneration** in poorly vascularised segment

## 3 Presentation

**Insertional** — **posterior heel pain**, swelling, burning, stiffness · **shoe pressure pain** (aversion to closed-heel shoes) · **midline tenderness at insertion** · bony prominence

**Retrocalcaneal bursitis** — **fullness + tenderness medial and lateral to tendon** · **pain with dorsiflexion**

**Noninsertional** — pain, swelling, warmth, **worse with activity**, difficulty running · **thickening + tenderness 2–6 cm proximal** · pain through whole ROM

**Painful arc sign** — **tender intratendinous nodule moves with active ROM** = **tendinosis** (vs **paratenonitis** — stays still)

**Dorsiflexion** — most have **limited passive dorsiflexion**

**Silfverskiöld test** — DF improves with **knee flexion** → **gastrocnemius contracture** · no improvement → **Achilles contracture**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Lateral foot X-ray** | **Bone spur + intratendinous calcification (enthesophyte)** (insertional) · **Haglund deformity** = enlarged posterosuperior tuberosity |
| **MRI** | **Intrasubstance intermediate signal** (disorganised tissue) · **thickened tendon** · % degeneration guides surgery · rarely needed for Haglund |
| **Ultrasound** | Degeneration · **colour / power Doppler ↑ intratendinous blood flow** (neovascularity) · chronic rupture = **hypoechoic gap between ends** |
| **MRI diffuse thickening, no focal area** | → **tendon transfer** indication |

### Noninsertional classification

| Type | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Achilles tendinosis** | **Tendon thickening**; anaerobic degeneration |
| **Achilles peritendonitis** | **Inflammation of tendon sheath** |
| **Paratenonitis** | **Inflammation of paratenon** |

**Brisement** — for **peritendinitis**, ✗ tendinosis

## 5 Management

```
Achilles tendinopathy
├─ First line → eccentric strengthening, NSAIDs, heel lift, shoe change
│      (✗ steroid injection — rupture)
├─ Failed nonop, < 50% degenerated → debridement ± Haglund resection
│      └─ gastrocnemius contracture → + gastrocnemius recession
└─ > 50% degenerated / diffuse / > 55 yr → debridement + FHL transfer
       └─ Achilles contracture (no Silfverskiöld change) → + Achilles lengthening
```

### Operative criteria

| Finding | Add to debridement |
|---|---|
| **Haglund deformity** | **Calcaneal exostectomy / calcaneoplasty** (prevents recurrence) |
| **< 50% degeneration** | **Side-to-side repair** / suture anchor; no transfer |
| **> 50% degeneration** (or debrided) | **FHL (or FDL, PB) transfer / augmentation** |
| **DF improves with knee flexion** | **Gastrocnemius recession** |
| **DF does not improve with knee flexion** | **Achilles lengthening** |
| **> 55 yr**, **diffuse MRI thickening**, **subacute rupture** on tendinopathy, heavier patients | **Tendon transfer** |
| Chronic symptoms **> 6 months** without improvement | Surgery considered |

### Noninsertional tendinopathy – eccentric loading & PRP

**First line** — **eccentric strengthening** (cornerstone, **heavy-load eccentric gastrocsoleus**) + rest, NSAIDs, heel lift, shoes, orthoses for overpronation, PT **3–6 months** · nonop **65–90% successful**

**Other ✗ for chronic tendinopathy** — stair climbing · rope jumping · concentric · isometric

**Eccentric = heel drop** — stand on step, heels off, **slowly lower below step** · **force remains constant as the muscle lengthens** · **closed-chain** · enhances dorsiflexion · most helpful in **later phases** of rehab / after diminished activity

![Heel drop — eccentric loading on a step](../MCQ/attachments/q215782-illa-heel-drop.jpg)

| Exercise (✗ traps) | Definition / example |
|---|---|
| **Eccentric ✓** | **Force constant while muscle lengthens** · heel drop |
| **Concentric** | **Muscle shortens**, tension ∝ external load |
| **Isometric** | **Constant length + tension**, no movement · **standing calf-raise hold**, pushing a wall |
| **Isotonic** | **Constant force through ROM** |
| **Isokinetic** | **Constant velocity (angular) through varied resistance** · Cybex |
| **Plyometric** | **Jump training** · may aggravate |
| **Single-leg stance** | **Proprioception** → ankle instability |
| **Heel walk** | **Dorsiflexor** strengthening |

**Why eccentric works (theory)** — absence of concentric phase disrupts lengthening / shortening cycle → shear in tendon + injury to collagen (exact mechanism unknown)

**Doppler neovascularity, intact tendon, 4 months** — **eccentric closed-chain exercises** (✗ **steroid** — rupture · ✗ CT chest / abdomen / pelvis · ✗ **PRP + delayed arthroscopic debridement** — level IV, not before eccentrics · ✗ resection + FHL + gastrocsoleus turndown — chronic rupture)

**PRP** — **added to eccentrics may improve outcomes in non-responders** (OB, noninsertional) · listed as nonop option (Orthobullets) · **no evidence** for PRP in tendinosis (OB, insertional) — read the options

**Orthotic + eccentrics** — **no better than eccentrics alone**

**Adjuncts studied** — orthotics, PRP, **ESWT**, **topical glyceryl trinitrate**, **polidocanol**, blood, hyaluronic acid, pulsed light, NSAIDs, steroids · GTN, **prolotherapy**, **aprotinin** = evolving, little evidence

**Steroid injection** — **avoid → Achilles rupture** (Orthobullets, OB) · one OB lists corticosteroid injection among nonop options — read the options

**Severe** — cast or removable boot

**Diagnosis from first-line eccentrics** — **Achilles tendinopathy** (✗ **Baxter nerve** — stretching, PT, NSAIDs · ✗ **calcaneal stress fracture** — NWB · ✗ **ATFL tear** — PT + proprioception · ✗ **syndesmotic sprain** — NWB cast / CAM boot)

### Insertional tendinopathy – debridement, Haglund resection & FHL transfer

**Nonop** — **heel sleeves + pads (mainstay)** · **small heel lift** · eccentric PT + gastrocsoleus stretching · **locked-ankle AFO 6–9 months** if others fail · **✗ steroid** · Haglund: ice, external padding · high nonop failure rates

**Surgery (< 50% removed)** — **retrocalcaneal bursa excision + debridement + calcaneal prominence resection** · tendon detachment → calcaneoplasty → reattachment

**Surgery (> 50% removed)** — **suture anchor repair + FHL (FDL, PB) augmentation + primary closure** (✗ **side-to-side** — for < 50% · ✗ **tibialis posterior** transfer · ✗ wound VAC / staged closure · ✗ closure alone)

**Incisions** — **midline, lateral or medial J-shaped**

**Haglund + calcifications, failed nonop, loss of function** — **debridement + calcaneal exostectomy + possible FHL transfer** (✗ debridement alone · ✗ Arizona brace · ✗ steroid · ✗ ankle arthrodesis)

**Haglund, normal DF (10°), 30% degeneration** — **debridement + exostectomy alone**

| Scenario (OB) | Correct plan |
|---|---|
| Normal heel, DF 10°, **60%** | Debridement + **FHL** (no exostectomy) |
| Normal heel, DF −10° → 10° with knee flexion, 60% | Debridement + FHL + **gastrocnemius recession** |
| **Haglund**, DF 10°, 60% | Debridement + FHL + **exostectomy** |
| **Haglund**, DF 10°, **30%** | **Debridement + exostectomy alone** ✓ |
| Haglund, DF −10° not improved with knee flexion, 30% | Debridement + **Achilles lengthening** + exostectomy |

**Haglund + gastrocnemius contracture, 40% debrided** — **calcaneoplasty / Haglund excision + gastrocnemius recession** ("I and III") (✗ FHL — < 50%)

**FHL transfer (overweight)** — **↑ ankle plantar flexion strength** (✗ ↑ AOFAS · ✗ ↑ SF-36 · ✗ **↓ great-toe plantar flexion** — not seen · ✗ no difference)

**Recovery (older, enthesopathy, debridement + ostectomy)** — **12 months (a year or more)** · young / pure tendon pathology faster

### Chronic noninsertional disease – debridement with FHL transfer

| Operation | Indication | Success |
|---|---|---|
| **Percutaneous longitudinal tenotomy** | **Mild–moderate** | — |
| Tendon stripping (suture strips anterior Achilles adhesions) | Adhesions | — |
| **Dorsal wedge calcaneal osteotomy + debridement** | **< 50%** involvement | — |
| **Open excision + tubularisation** | **Moderate–severe** | **70–100%** |
| **Tendon transfer (FHL, FDL, PB)** | **> 50% degeneration**, **> 55 yr**, **diffuse MRI thickening**, **subacute rupture** | — |

**Fusiform swelling 12 months, > 50% width** — **debridement + FHL transfer** (✗ side-to-side — inadequate strength · ✗ steroid · ✗ brisement · ✗ walking cast)

**Subacute rupture on tendinosis, 4 cm gap** — **debridement + augmentation with tendon inserting on plantar great-toe distal phalanx (FHL)** (✗ primary suture-anchor repair — fails · ✗ plantar 1st MT head · ✗ FDL plantar lesser-toe distal phalanges, not MT heads / dorsal)

**Large-gap options** — **V-Y myotendinous flap**, free tendon graft, synthetic graft, local transfer (**PB, FDL, FHL**) · small tear → debridement + primary repair

**FHL transfer outcome (Den Hartog)** — ↑ ankle–hindfoot scores, mostly good / excellent, **no hallux functional deficit**

| Tendon | Insertion |
|---|---|
| **FHL** | **Plantar aspect of great-toe distal phalanx** |
| **FDL** | **Plantar distal phalanges of lesser toes** |

## X vs Y

| | **Insertional** | **Noninsertional** |
|---|---|---|
| Site | **At insertion** | **2–6 cm proximal** |
| Age | Middle-aged + elderly | Active middle-aged |
| X-ray | **Spur, calcification, Haglund** | Often normal |
| Nonop | **Heel sleeves / pads**, heel lift, AFO | **Eccentric loading** ± PRP |
| Surgery | **Debridement + exostectomy** ± FHL | Tenotomy / excision + tubularisation ± FHL |

| | **Tendinosis** | **Paratenonitis** |
|---|---|---|
| Painful arc | **Nodule moves** with ROM | Fixed |
| Treatment | Eccentrics, debridement | **Brisement** |
