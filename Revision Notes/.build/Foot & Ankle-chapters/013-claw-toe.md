# Claw Toe

*Foot & Ankle · 13 / 48*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Distribution** — typically **multiple toes** · often **bilateral**

### Pathophysiology – EDL vs intrinsic imbalance, neurological causes

**Primary pathology** — **MTP hyperextension** → chronic MTP hyperextension → **unopposed DIP + PIP flexion by FDL** · analogous to **intrinsic minus hand**

**MTP joint** — **EDL overpowering relatively weak intrinsics** · hyperextended MTP makes intrinsics less efficient plantarflexors → deformity progresses (✗ intrinsics overpowering EDL, ✗ plantar plate overpowering EDB, ✗ lumbricals overpowering intrinsics, ✗ EDL overpowering EDB)

**IP joints** — **stronger flexors overpower weaker intrinsics** (intrinsics act as IP extensors)

**Plantar plate** — becomes **insufficient / attenuated** → **base of proximal phalanx translates dorsally**

**Interossei + lumbricals** — **move dorsally** → flexion moment arm shifts to wrong side of MTP centre of rotation

**Most common cause** — **synovitis** (Orthobullets, OB) · "occurs most commonly in **diabetes, alcoholism, neuromuscular** conditions" (another OB) — read the options

**Other causes** — trauma · **delayed / missed compartment syndrome** (deep posterior leg compartment or foot) · **cavus** · **inflammatory arthropathy** (soft-tissue attenuation → MTP instability)

**Neurological causes** — **Charcot-Marie-Tooth**, **Friedreich ataxia**, myelodysplasia, **cerebral palsy**, **multiple sclerosis**, stroke, **diabetic neuropathy** · **clawing of all 4 lesser toes → neurological abnormality**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — pain at **unstable MTP joint** · **metatarsalgia**

**Plantar** — **depressed metatarsal head with callus** + tenderness

**Dorsal / tip** — flexed IP joints with dorsal callosities · **tip-of-toe ulceration** (neuropathic / diabetic)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — clinical: **MTP hyperextension + PIP flexion + DIP flexion** of a lesser toe

## 5 Management

```
Claw toe
├─ First line → taping + shoe modification
├─ Flexible, no contracture → Girdlestone-Taylor: EDB tenotomy, EDL lengthening, FDL flexor-to-extensor transfer
│      (diabetic, tip-of-toe ulcer, no infection → isolated percutaneous FDL tenotomy)
├─ Fixed contracture → Girdlestone + MTP capsulectomy + proximal phalanx head/neck resection
└─ All four lesser toes → Girdlestone + Weil (distal MT shortening) osteotomy
```

```
Claw toe (TO)
├─ Flexible → flexor tendon transfer
└─ Fixed → MTP soft-tissue release (dorsal + medial/lateral capsule, extensor lengthening)
       ├─ Reduced → realigned toe
       └─ Not reduced → Weil osteotomy + fixed hammer-toe repair
```

**Nonoperative** — **first line** · plantar padding (**metatarsal / crest pads**, orthotics) to offload plantarly subluxed MT heads · **high toe box** · **sling** holding proximal phalanx parallel to ground

### Operative criteria

| Indication | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **Painful flexible deformity** without contracture · shoe-wear ulcers · otherwise healthy | **Girdlestone(-Taylor)**: EDB tenotomy + EDL lengthening + **FDL flexor-to-extensor transfer** |
| **Fixed contracture** | Girdlestone + **MTP capsulectomy** + **proximal phalanx head + neck resection** (tendon transfer + bony resection) |
| **Claw toe of all four lesser toes** | Girdlestone + **Weil lesser MT osteotomy** |
| **Flexible deformity, diabetic, tip-of-toe ulcer, no infection** | **Isolated percutaneous FDL tenotomy** |
| Rigid diabetic claw toe with ulcer | Arthrodesis |
| **Rigid mallet toe** (rigid DIP flexion) | Bony: **DIP fusion or resection** |
| **Hammer toe** (PIP flexion, DIP extension) | **FDL-to-EDL transfer** |

### Flexible deformity – flexor tenotomy or flexor-to-extensor transfer

**Flexible claw toe (TO)** — **flexor-to-extensor tendon transfer with extensor lengthening** · flexor split into **two slings**, transferred dorsally as for hammer toe · extensor lengthening may or may not be needed (✗ extensor lengthening + PIP arthroplasty, ✗ flexor tenotomy + MTP pinning, ✗ distal metatarsal osteotomy) (TO)

**Girdlestone-Taylor** — FDL to extensor surface → long flexor acts like **intrinsics**: **active MTP plantarflexion + IP extension** · **only for flexible deformity** (✗ PIP arthrodesis, ✗ MTP capsulotomy + PP resection arthroplasty — fixed only, ✗ isolated Weil)

**Percutaneous FDL tenotomy** — diabetic + **flexible** claw toe + **tip ulcer**, no osteomyelitis (bone not probed) → ↓ tip pressure; minimal exposure (infection, poor healing)

| Percutaneous flexor tenotomy outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| **Ulcer healing** | **98% at 4 weeks** |
| Reoperation | **\~8%** (✗ 50%) |
| Ulcer recurrence | **0–18%** (✗ 30%) |
| **Transfer ulceration** | Reported complication (✗ "no risk") |

**✗ Diabetic tip-ulcer traps** — amputation through 2nd MTP (only with infection / osteomyelitis) · repeat ulcer debridement (doesn't offload) · **DIP fusion** (rigid / arthritic) · **Weil** (all four lesser toes, non-neuropathic; floating toe + transfer metatarsalgia)

### Plantar plate repair

**Plantar plate repair** — **improves the extension deformity at the MTP joint** → toe plantigrade (✗ MTP flexion — claw has MTP hyperextension · ✗ PIP / DIP — addressed indirectly)

**Recurrence** — **persistent plantar plate dysfunction**

### Weil osteotomy – floating toe complication

**Technique** — **oblique distal MT shortening osteotomy** · translates head **proximal + plantar** · rigid internal fixation · head moved proximal to callus → axial decompression · **average shortening 4 mm**

**Indications** — metatarsalgia (2nd MT, high heels) · MTP subluxation · claw toe of all four lesser toes

**Floating toe** — **most common complication of Weil** = **dorsiflexion contracture at the MTP joint**

**Mechanism** — plantar depression of head + altered centre of rotation → **interossei move dorsal to MTP axis** → **converted from plantarflexors to dorsiflexors** (intrinsics act as MTP extensors) (✗ articular damage, ✗ disruption of dorsal capsular artery, ✗ lengthening — it shortens, ✗ ligament instability)

**Plantar translation in distal MT osteotomy (TO)** — **floating toe**: intrinsic force shifts volar → dorsal + weak toe flexors → **extension at MTP + IP joints** (✗ claw, ✗ hammer, ✗ mallet) (TO)

**Cause of 2nd toe "floating" post-op** — **technique of surgical osteotomy** (✗ immobilisation, ✗ CVA, ✗ extensor tethering, ✗ infection)

**Avoid** — osteotomy **parallel to plantar surface of foot** or **resect a wafer of bone** so head isn't depressed

**Rare after Weil** — **osteonecrosis / MTP arthritis** (constant **plantar artery** to MT head + capsule) · nonunion · significant transfer lesions

**Weil vs Helal (shaft) osteotomy** — Weil: higher satisfaction, less recurrent metatarsalgia, fewer transfer lesions

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Floating toe** (most common after Weil) | **Intrinsics migrate dorsal to joint → act as MTP extensors** |
| **Recurrence** | **Persistent plantar plate dysfunction** |
| Transfer ulceration | After percutaneous flexor tenotomy |
| Transfer metatarsalgia | Weil (not the commonest) |

## X vs Y

| | **Isolated FDL tenotomy** | **Girdlestone-Taylor transfer** | **Resection / arthrodesis** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deformity | **Flexible** | **Flexible** | **Fixed / rigid** |
| Patient | **Diabetic, tip ulcer, no infection** | Otherwise healthy | Any |
| Exposure | Percutaneous | Open | Open |

## How it's asked

**"Claw toe — muscular imbalance from which relatively strong structure?"** → **EDL overpowering relatively weak intrinsics**

**"Multiple sclerosis — associated toe deformity?"** → **extended MTP, flexed PIP + DIP** (✗ curly, ✗ mallet, ✗ hammer, ✗ crossover)

**"Plantar translation of MT head in distal MT osteotomy causes?"** → **floating toe** (TO)

**"2nd toe floats after Weil — cause?"** → **technique of surgical osteotomy**

**"Mechanism of commonest Weil complication?"** → **conversion of interossei from plantarflexors to dorsiflexors**

**"Most common complication of Weil osteotomy?"** → **dorsiflexion contracture at the MTP joint** (✗ osteonecrosis, ✗ nonunion, ✗ transfer lesions, ✗ MTP arthritis)

**"Flexible claw toe — best treatment?"** → **flexor-to-extensor tendon transfer with extensor lengthening** (TO)

**"Diabetic, flexible PIP + MTP, failed nonop — surgery?"** → **Girdlestone-Taylor flexor-to-extensor tendon transfer**

**"Who benefits most from percutaneous flexor tenotomy?"** → **diabetic (A1c 8.5), flexible PIP + DIP flexion, ulcer at tip of toe**

**"Diabetic tip ulcer, flexible 2nd claw toe, bone not probed — treatment?"** → **percutaneous flexor digitorum longus tenotomy**

**"Percutaneous flexor tenotomy — true?"** → **ulcer healing rates 98% at 4 weeks**

**"Claw toe with lateral deviation of 2nd PP — direct result of plantar plate repair?"** → **improves the extension deformity at the MTP joint**

**"Claw toes of all four lesser toes?"** → **Girdlestone + Weil osteotomy** · think **neurological cause**
