# Preiser's Disease (Scaphoid AVN)

*Hand · 68 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **idiopathic AVN of scaphoid**; Preiser 1910; not fracture

**Epidemiology** — rare, **<1/100,000/y**; mean age **45 y**

**Ranking** — scaphoid = **2nd commonest carpal AVN** after lunate (Kienböck)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — chronic, progressive **dorsoradial wrist pain**, atraumatic

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **sclerosis + fragmentation of proximal pole**, **no fracture**

**MRI** — complete vs partial involvement; STIR marrow oedema, cysts, partial collapse; contrast MRI classifications (Kalainov, Schmitt)

![Preiser's: proximal pole sclerosis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6051-scaphoid.jpg)

### Herbert & Lanzetta classification (1994)

| Stage | X-ray |
|---|---|
| 1 | Normal X-ray, **+ bone scan** |
| 2 | ↑ density proximal pole, generalised osteoporosis |
| 3 | **Fragmentation** proximal pole ± fracture |
| 4 | **Carpal collapse** ± OA |

## 5 Management

```
Preiser's disease
├─ Young, Herbert 1–2, minimal sx → NSAIDs + IMMOBILISATION (effective 20%)
├─ Fails nonop, stage 1 → ARTHROSCOPIC RESECTION / drilling
├─ Stage 2 → VASCULARISED BONE GRAFT / revascularisation
└─ Stage 3 (collapse) → PRC or scaphoid excision + 4-corner fusion (salvage)
```

**Other options** — microfracture drilling, allograft replacement

## X vs Y

| | **Preiser's** | **Kienböck's** | Trapezium AVN |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bone | **Scaphoid** | **Lunate** | Trapezium |
| Frequency | 2nd commonest | Commonest | Exceedingly rare |

## How it's asked

**"Atraumatic radial wrist pain, scaphoid sclerosis/collapse on X-ray & MRI?"** → **Preiser's disease**\
✗ Wrong options: Kienböck's · acute SL injury · basilar thumb OA · trapezium AVN

**"Herbert stage 1?"** → **normal X-ray, positive bone scan**

**"Salvage for collapse?"** → **PRC or 4-corner fusion**
