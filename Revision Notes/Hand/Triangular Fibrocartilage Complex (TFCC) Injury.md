# Triangular Fibrocartilage Complex (TFCC) Injury

*Hand · 93 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy – components, foveal insertion & radioulnar ligaments

**Function (TO)** — transmits **~20% of wrist load**; **main stabiliser of DRUJ**; cushion between ulnar head and ulnar carpus

| TFCC components | Not components |
|---|---|
| Dorsal + volar **radioulnar ligaments** (superficial + deep limbs) | **Extensor digiti minimi (EDM) sheath** (TO) |
| Central **articular disc** | LT interosseous ligament |
| **Meniscus homologue** | RSC ligament |
| **Ulnar collateral ligament** | FCU sheath |
| **ECU subsheath** (6th compartment) | |
| Origin of **ulnolunate + ulnotriquetral** ligaments | |

![TFCC components](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6047-shows-elements-of-tfcc.jpg)

**Radioulnar ligaments** — **primary restraint to dorsal/palmar translation of radius on (fixed) ulna**; span sigmoid notch margins ↔ ulna

**Deep fibres** — **ligamentum subcruentum → ulnar fovea** (key DRUJ stabiliser; repair these) · **superficial → ulnar styloid**

**Articular disc** — load bearing, almost no stabilising role

**UL / UT / ECU subsheath** — stabilise carpus to ulna; minimal DRUJ stability

| Functional zone (TO) | Role | Components |
|---|---|---|
| **Distal** | Ulnocarpal stability | TFC, meniscus homologue, UL, UT |
| **Proximal** | **DRUJ stability** | Radioulnar ligaments |
| **Functional UCL** | Dynamic stabiliser | **ECU sheath** + thickened ulnar capsule |

**Reciprocal RULs (TO)** — **supination → palmar RUL tight** · **pronation → dorsal RUL tight** · UL/UT tighten reciprocally in radio-ulnar deviation

| Ulnocarpal ligament (TO) | Origin → insertion | Key feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Ulnocapitate** | Fovea → palmar-ulnar capitate | **Most superficial** |
| Ulnotriquetral | Palmar RUL + styloid → triquetrum | Oblique |
| **Ulnolunate** | TFC disc → palmar lunate | **Motion coupling, not stability** |

**Ligamentum subcruentum (TO)** — vascular connective tissue between RUL limbs at styloid → **aids healing**

**Meniscus homologue (TO)** — C-shaped, **highly vascular + elastic**, round styloid tip · **prestyloid recess** between MH + TFC: type 1 narrow-long, type 2 wide-short, type 3 no ulnocarpal opening (connects DRUJ)

**ECU sheath (TO)** — **taut in pronation → prevents dorsal dislocation of ulnar head** · relaxes in supination

**Volar RUL injury** → **dorsal subluxation of ulna** · dorsal RUL injury → volar subluxation of ulna

**Secondary DRUJ stabiliser** — dorsal oblique band of IOM

**Blood supply** — periphery vascular (10–40%); **central disc avascular**

### Concepts of TFCC structure

| Concept | Author |
|---|---|
| 2-D structure | **Palmer & Werner** (1981) |
| **"Hammock" / suspension** (3 zones: foveal, distal hammock, UCL) | **Nakamura** |
| **"Iceberg"** (proximal foveal pc-TFCC = submerged, functional; distal dc-TFCC = visible tip, shock absorber) | **Atzei & Luchetti** (TO) |

**Iceberg (TO)** — pc-TFCC not seen through radiocarpal portals → indirect **hook sign** / DRUJ portals; pc-TFCC tear → DRUJ instability, ↓ rotation, ↓ grip, high reoperation

## 2 Mechanism

**Most common mechanism** — **fall on extended wrist + forearm pronation**\
✗ Wrong options: supination · flexion · axial load ulnar deviation

**Type 1** — traumatic; traction on ulnar wrist

**Type 2** — degenerative; **positive ulnar variance**, ulnocarpal impaction; central tears with repetitive activity

## 3 Presentation

### Diagnosis – mechanism & examination

**Symptoms** — ulnar-sided wrist pain, clicking, painful **turning a door key**

**Fovea sign** — tender **soft spot between ulnar styloid and FCU**, volar ulnar head → pisiform; **95% sensitive, 87% specific** for foveal / UT injury

**Fovea sign interpretation (TO)** — positive = **foveal avulsion (DRUJ unstable)** · negative with ulnar pain = **UT ligament tear (DRUJ stable)**

**Dimple sign (TO)** — hand flat, ulnar head shifts palmarly → dorsal dimple = **multidirectional DRUJ instability**

| Palpation (TO) | Site | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Full pronation | Dorsal radial metaphysis | EDC tenosynovitis |
| Full pronation | Proximal-ulnar corner of lunate | Ulnocarpal impaction / Kienböck |
| Full pronation | Dorsal ulnar head | DRUJ arthritis |
| **Full supination** | **Ulnar snuffbox / fovea** | **TFCC tear / DRUJ instability** |
| Palmar | Pisiform, FCU, hook of hamate | ECU tendinopathy / pisotriquetral OA |

**Provocation** — pain with **ulnar deviation** (compression) or radial deviation (tension), resisted ulnar deviation

**Piano key sign (TO)** — ballotable distal ulna = **DRUJ instability**: **TFCC tear, Galeazzi (nonunion), Madelung**; **not isolated ulnar styloid fracture**

(✗ Wrong options: **Distractors** — ECU subluxation (snap in supination + flexion + ulnar deviation; ECU tenderness)) · hook of hamate (CT / carpal tunnel view) · ulnar styloid fracture (X-ray) · Guyon's (interossei weakness, ulnar numbness)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — usually **normal**; **zero-rotation PA → ulnar variance**; pronated grip PA (dynamic); widened DRUJ / dorsal ulna on true lateral if unstable

**Arthrography** — extravasation (largely replaced by MRI)

**MRI clues (TO)** — **DRUJ effusion** · **fluid in prestyloid recess** · **"floppy" disc** · read **coronal** (axials poor) · small tears missed at 1.5 T → 3 T or MR arthrography

**MRI / MR arthrogram** — **sensitivity 74–100%**; lunate ulnar-side tear = ulnocarpal impaction

**CT (TO)** — best for **DRUJ subluxation** + bony detail · **MR arthrography** = highest sensitivity for perforations (TO)

**Arthroscopy** — **gold standard / most accurate**; after several months failed splinting

### Palmer classification

| Class 1 – Traumatic | Lesion |
|---|---|
| **1A** | **Central perforation** |
| **1B** | **Ulnar avulsion** (± styloid fx; Orthobullets: without) |
| 1C | Distal avulsion (UL/UT origin) |
| 1D | Radial avulsion |

| Class 2 – Degenerative | Lesion |
|---|---|
| 2A | Wear / thinning |
| 2B | 2A + lunate and/or ulnar chondromalacia |
| 2C | 2B + perforation |
| 2D | 2C + ligament (LT) disruption |
| 2E | 2D + ulnocarpal + DRUJ arthritis |

![Palmer classification](../../MCQ/attachments/q219370-illa-palmer-classification-tfcc.jpg)

**Defect adjacent to ulna, stable DRUJ (TO)** — **Class 1B**

**Need for surgery ↑ (TO)** — associated fractures, instability, DRUJ injury; most isolated tears no early surgery

**Ulnar wrist pain differential (TO)** — TFCC · ulnocarpal impaction · ECU · hook of hamate · LT instability · ulnar styloid impaction · **TILT (triquetral impingement ligament tear)** · DRUJ OA

**Differentials** — ulnocarpal abutment, ulnar styloid impaction, ECU tendonitis, hook of hamate fracture, ulnar tunnel, pisotriquetral OA

## 5 Management

```
TFCC injury
├─ All acute Type 1 → immobilise + NSAIDs ± steroid (first line)
│      ├─ fails, 1A central → arthroscopic debridement
│      └─ fails, 1B–1D peripheral → arthroscopic repair
└─ Type 2 → nonop first
       ├─ ulnar + variance >2 mm / LT instability → ulnar shortening osteotomy
       ├─ ulnar + variance <2 mm, 2A–C → wafer
       └─ 2D → limited ulnar head resection
```

### Treatment – immobilisation, debridement & wafer procedure with ulnar-positive variance

**Acute 1B, DRUJ stable, 5 days** — **immobilisation + NSAIDs**\
✗ Wrong options: arthroscopy yet · repair · shortening · wafer

**Conservative (TO)** — **above-elbow cast 4–6 wk**, NSAIDs, steroid or **PRP** injection, proprioceptive + grip PT

| Surgery (TO) | Procedure |
|---|---|
| Central 1A | Arthroscopic debridement |
| Peripheral 1B / 1C | Suture anchor / transosseous repair |
| **Ulnar styloid nonunion** | **Excision + TFCC reattachment** |
| **Chronic DRUJ instability** | **Foveal reattachment of RUL** / ulnar shortening |
| Approach | **Palmar for UL/UT** tears · **dorsal for RUL / foveal** repair |

**6U portal (TO)** — soft spot between **UT ligament and ECU sheath** → ulnar recess, **foveal repair**

**Rehab (TO)** — 0–4 wk immobilise neutral rotation · 4–8 wk ROM + pronosupination · 8–12 wk strengthening · full recovery **3–4 months**

**Debridement** — 1A; 3-4 + 6R portals; **keep 2 mm peripheral rim**; 80% pain relief; **not effective with ulnar-positive variance**

**Repair** — 1B–1D (peripheral, vascular); acute athletic best; outside-in / inside-out / transosseous; immobilise **6 wk**; acute (<3 mo) → **80% motion + grip**; risk ECU tendonitis (knot), dorsal sensory nerve

**DRUJ (Mulford)** — mild acute → immobilise 4–6 wk in stable position; complex reducible + styloid fx → ORIF; without fx → TFCC repair + pin DRUJ

**Ulnar diaphyseal shortening** — variance **>2 mm**, **LT instability** (tightens ulnocarpal ligaments); plate; nonunion, hardware removal

**Wafer** — removes **2–4 mm** distal ulnar head (open or arthroscopic through central defect); ulnar cortex intact, not into DRUJ; no plate

**Wafer threshold** — variance **<2 mm** (Orthobullets) · **2 mm positive variance + ulnocarpal impingement** = indication with debridement (OB) — read the options

**Darrach** — **contraindicated** (ulnar stump instability, ECU subluxation); salvage only

## 6 Complications

**Repair** — ECU tendonitis from knot, dorsal ulnar sensory nerve injury

**Ulnar shortening** — nonunion, hardware irritation

**Positive variance** — worse debridement/repair outcomes

## X vs Y

| | **Ulnar shortening osteotomy** | **Wafer** |
|---|---|---|
| Variance | **>2 mm** | **<2 mm** (2A–C) |
| Fixation | Plate | None |
| Extra benefit | Tightens UL/UT → **LT instability** | Arthroscopic via central tear |
| Complications | Nonunion, hardware | Technically difficult |

## How it's asked

**"Components of TFCC EXCEPT?"** → **EDM sheath** (TO)

**"Anatomical component of TFCC?"** → **ECU tendon sheath**\
✗ Wrong options: LTIL · RSC · FCU sheath

**"Main restraint to dorsal/palmar radius translation?"** → **dorsal + palmar radioulnar ligaments**\
✗ Wrong options: disc

**"Deep radioulnar fibres insert on?"** → **ulnar fovea**\
✗ Wrong options: ulnar styloid = superficial

**"Deep-fibre injury → dorsal ulnar subluxation — which structure?"** → **volar radioulnar ligament**

**"Fall on pronated extended wrist, tender distal to ulnar styloid, no ECU tenderness?"** → **TFCC tear**

**"Normal X-ray + CT, foveal tenderness, no snapping?"** → **TFCC tear**\
✗ Wrong options: ECU subluxation · hamate

**"Most common mechanism?"** → **wrist extension + forearm pronation**

**"Indication to add wafer to debridement?"** → **2 mm positive ulnar variance + ulnocarpal impingement**

**"Acute 1B, stable DRUJ, 5 days?"** → **immobilization with NSAIDs**

**"Defect adjacent to ulna, stable DRUJ — Palmer?"** → **Class 1B** (TO)

**"Tight radioulnar ligament in supination?"** → **palmar RUL** (TO)

**"Positive foveal sign indicates?"** → **TFCC foveal avulsion, DRUJ unstable** (TO)

**"Iceberg concept given by?"** → **Atzei & Luchetti** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: Palmer & Werner · Nakamura

**"Piano key sign NOT seen in?"** → **ulnar styloid fracture** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: TFCC tear · Galeazzi · Madelung
