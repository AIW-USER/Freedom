# Constrictive Ring Syndrome (Streeter's Dysplasia)

*Hand · 17 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Synonyms** — amniotic band syndrome (ABS), Streeter's dysplasia, **early amnion rupture sequence**

**Incidence** — **1:15,000** (Orthobullets, OB) · **1:10,000** "rare" stated true (TO) — read the options

**Sex** — M = F

**Site** — **hands/fingers 80%**, >90% distal to wrist; distal > trunk; **central digits** (long 28%, ring 27%, index 23%); average **3 limbs**

**Leg bands (TO)** — mostly **zone 2** (knee–ankle); tibia/fibula fractures may occur at band level (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: zone 1

**Risk factors** — **low birth weight (<2500 g)**, **prematurity (<37 wk)**, maternal drugs, trauma in pregnancy, attempted 1st-trimester termination; abnormal gestational history **60%**\
✗ Wrong options: late gestation / high birth weight

**Mortality** — infant 4.6%, perinatal 12.7%

### Genes & inheritance

**Inheritance** — **sporadic, not hereditary**\
✗ Wrong options: AD/AR/X-linked

## 2 Mechanism

**Extrinsic theory (most accepted)** — ruptured amnion → fibrous strands **wrap limb circumferentially**

**Distal to band** — lymphatic + NV compression → **lymphoedema**, altered circulation, neuropathy, amputation

## 3 Presentation

### Associated conditions (clubfoot, cleft lip/palate) & sporadic inheritance

**Exam** — **normal anatomy proximal to ring**; band **perpendicular** to digit/limb axis

**No amputation** — look for secondary syndactyly, bony fusion, proximal sinus tracts

**Clubfoot** — **commonest associated foot condition**; **rigid**; casting poor (6% success, 77% need band Z-plasty first)

**Clubfoot rate** — 12–56% (OB) · up to 25% (OB) · ~⅓ (Foulkes) · 38% (Koskimies)

**Associated** — **clubfoot**, **cleft lip/palate**, craniofacial defects, **cardiac defects**, **encephalocele** (off-midline), **acrosyndactyly**, pseudarthrosis, LLD, hemihypertrophy, renal, haemangioma, **lymphoedema** (TO: non-orthopaedic)

**Anterolateral tibial bowing** — listed as a manifestation (OB) · "not associated / NF1" (OB, 2 questions) — read the options

**NOT associated** — **bronchopulmonary dysplasia** (prematurity) · **complex syndactyly** (TO) · polydactyly · vertical/oblique talus · tarsal coalition · pes planovalgus · cutis aplasia · renal aplasia · dural ectasia

**Syndactyly type (TO)** — **simple incomplete, fenestrated** (slits proximally), bones not fused

### Key radiology findings

**Antenatal US** — diagnosis possible at **end of 1st trimester**; chorioamniotic membrane separation → bands

**Diagnosis** — clinical at birth

## 5 Management

```
Constriction band
├─ Shallow / incomplete, no lymphatic compromise → OBSERVE
├─ Complete band ± lymphatic/vascular compromise → BAND EXCISION + Z-PLASTY
│      └─ NV compromise → urgent release
└─ Acrosyndactyly / amputation → release / reconstruction
```

### Treatment – band release & Z-plasty

**Indication** — **complete circumferential band interfering with lymphatic drainage → excision + Z-plasty** (one-stage possible)

**Trap** — incomplete bands not affecting lymphatics → do not resect

**Acrosyndactyly + distal oedema** — **early** release → better prognosis

**Clubfoot with band** — **Z-plasty band release before** clubfoot correction

**Follow-up** — to skeletal maturity (contracture, growth disturbance)

**Prognosis** — depends on band location & severity

## How it's asked

**"Streeter band of leg — EXCEPT"** → **most in zone 1** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: ; zone 2

**"Not a feature of Streeter's dysplasia"** → **complex syndactyly** (TO)

**"Associated foot condition"** → **clubfoot (CTEV)**\
✗ Wrong options: vertical talus, coalition, equinovalgus

**"Associated EXCEPT"** → **bronchopulmonary dysplasia**

**"Inheritance"** → **sporadic**

**"Associated condition"** → **cleft palate**

**"Cleft palate + Ponseti-treated clubfoot + syndactyly — expect"** → **amniotic band syndrome of hand**

**"True statement"** → **complete band blocking lymphatics → excision + Z-plasty**\
✗ Wrong options: trunk > limbs · late gestation/high BW · tibial bowing
