# Jersey Finger

*Hand · 46 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **FDP avulsion** from base of distal phalanx (**zone I**); aka rugby/rugger jersey finger

**Ring finger** — **75%**; ring tip 5 mm more prominent in grip (~90%) → ↑ pull-away force

**FDP innervation** — ulnar n. + AIN

**Zone I** — distal to FDS insertion

**Blood supply** — **vincula**: short (breve) near insertion · long (longum) from FDS chiasma region; palm retraction shears **both** → tendon necrosis

**Quadriga basis (TO)** — FDP of long, ring, little share **common muscle belly**

## 2 Mechanism

**Forced DIP extension during max FDP contraction** — grabbing a jersey/flag in a tackle

## 3 Presentation

### Diagnosis, retraction level & urgency

**Rest** — finger in **slight extension** at DIP vs cascade

**Key sign** — **no active DIP flexion**; MCP/PIP normal

**Palpable mass** — retracted tendon in palm / distal palmar crease (Type I) — MRI can show level

**Paradoxical PIP extension** — on attempted flexion

![Jersey finger: loss of DIP flexion in ring finger](../../MCQ/attachments/q1132-a-jersey-finger.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — may show **avulsion fragment** (level of fragment = level of retraction)

**MRI** — level of proximal retraction

### Modified Leddy & Packer classification

| Type | Retraction / fragment | Vincula | Repair window | Image |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Into palm** (lumbrical origin, ≥A1); no fragment | **Both ruptured** → **most threatening** (TO) | **7–10 d** | ![Types](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6015-types-of-jesey-finger.jpg) |
| **II** | **PIP level** (A3 / Camper's chiasma), ± small fragment; **most common** (TO) | Short ruptured, **long intact** | Several weeks (Orthobullets) · **6 wk** (OB) · up to **3 mo** reported (TO) | |
| **III** | **Large fragment** held at **DIP / A4 pulley** (Orthobullets, TO) · held at **A2** in one TO stem — read the options | **Both intact** → best prognosis | Weeks; **ORIF** (screw, K-wire, pull-out wire) | ![Type III](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6015-jerseybony2.jpg) |
| **IV** | **Double avulsion**: fragment + tendon off fragment → tendon usually into palm (Smith 1981) | As I/II | **ORIF fragment first, then tendon** | ![Type IV](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6015-type-4.jpg) |
| V | Avulsion + **comminuted distal phalanx** (Va extra-, Vb intra-articular) (Al-Qattan 2001) | Minimal damage (TO) | Fix if possible, else tendon-to-bone ± DIP stabilisation | |

**Urgency depends on** — **degree of tendon retraction**\
✗ Wrong options: presence or size of fragment · PIP ROM · athletic level

## 5 Management

```
Jersey finger (all need surgery)
├─ Acute (<3 wk; ideally <1 wk) → tendon repair / reinsertion (button or anchor)
│      └─ bony (III/IV) → ORIF fragment (IV: + tendon reinsertion)
├─ Chronic (>3 mo) + full DIP PROM → TWO-STAGE TENDON GRAFT
└─ Chronic (>3 mo) + stiff DIP → DIP ARTHRODESIS
```

**No fracture on X-ray (TO)** — Type I/II → **immediate surgical repair**\
✗ Wrong options: splint in extension/flexion · observe months

**No nonop** — extension block splint, buddy taping, early ROM → no DIP function

### Acute repair technique – advancement limit & anchor placement

**Fixation** — direct repair or reinsertion with **dorsal (nail) pull-out button** or **suture anchor** (anchors replacing buttons: button irritation) (TO)

**Advancement limit (TO)** — **≤1 cm** (excursion balance of common FDP belly) → >1 cm = **quadriga** / DIP flexion contracture

**2.5 mm advancement** — **acceptable**

**Anchor** — **45° retrograde** (distal-volar → proximal-dorsal) in **proximal metaphysis** of distal phalanx\
✗ Wrong options: perpendicular · dorsal cortex breach · antegrade (more gap) · articular surface

**DIP pinning** — optional, not required

**No DIP arthrodesis acutely** — repair instead

**Do not delay** — don't finish 8-week season before repair

**Rehab** — **Duran** (patient-assisted passive) or **Kleinert** (dynamic splint passive)

**After repair** — check **symmetric resting cascade** (TO: over-tension → quadriga)

### Chronic injury – two-stage grafting

**Chronic (>3 mo) + full PROM** — **excise palmar mass + 2-stage grafting** (Hunter: silicone rod → pseudosheath → PL/plantaris graft)

**Avoid single-stage graft** — adhesions in fibro-osseous tunnel

**Avoid active silicone rod** — not effective

**No direct repair late** — too much tension, tendon won't reach

**DIP arthrodesis** — chronic + **stiff** DIP; young guitarist with supple finger → **grafting**

**Outcome** — reconstruction poorer than acute repair, stiffness

## 6 Complications

**Quadriga** — over-advancement (>1 cm) → adjacent fingers' flexion & grip ↓

**DIP flexion contracture** — over-advancement

## X vs Y

### Differential – central slip injury

| | **Jersey finger** | **Central slip / boutonnière** (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| PIP | Flexes normally | **Flexed, can't extend** |
| DIP | **Can't flex** | **Hyperextended, can't flex** |
| Test | No active DIP flexion with PIP blocked | **Elson** (rigid DIP = +), modified Elson, Boyes (late) |
| Acute Rx | **Surgery** | **PIP extension splint 4–6 wk**, DIP free |

**Boutonnière pathology (TO)** — central slip rupture → lateral bands volar (transverse retinacular lig. contract) → PIP flexion + DIP hyperextension; may appear **10–14 d** after injury

**Chronic boutonnière (TO)** — supple: **terminal tendon tenotomy (Fowler/Dolphin)**, lateral band relocation, Littler; stiff/RA/arthritic: **PIP arthrodesis**

**Pseudo-boutonnière (TO)** — PIP flexion contracture **without** DIP hyperextension

| Other diagnoses (wrong) | Finding |
|---|---|
| FDS avulsion | PIP flexion lost |
| Distal extensor (mallet) | DIP extension lost |
| Sagittal band | MCP extensor subluxation |

## How it's asked

**"Football tackle, can't flex DIP, X-ray normal — Rx"** → **immediate surgical repair** (TO)

**"Most threatening Leddy-Packer type"** → **Type I** (TO)

**"Large fragment trapped at pulley, ring finger, 5 d"** → **Type III**, vincula intact (TO)

**"Ring finger after tackle, can't flex DIP — Dx"** → **FDP avulsion**

**"Rugby, 1 wk, distal palm tender, can't flex DIP"** → **acute tendon repair**

**"Urgency of fixation depends on"** → **degree of tendon retraction**

**"Max advancement to avoid quadriga"** → **1 cm** (TO)

**"Correct statement about repair"** → **2.5 mm advancement acceptable**

**"Suture anchor position"** → **45° retrograde in proximal metaphysis**

**"5 mo, palmar mass, full PROM"** → **excise mass + 2-stage grafting**

**"Guitarist, 6 mo, supple DIP"** → **two-stage flexor reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: arthrodesis

**"DIP hyperextended can't flex, PIP flexed"** → **central slip injury** (TO)
