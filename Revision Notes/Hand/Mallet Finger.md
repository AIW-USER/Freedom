# Mallet Finger

*Hand · 54 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — disruption of **terminal extensor tendon distal to DIP** (tendinous or bony avulsion); "baseball / drop finger"; extensor **zone 1** (Kleinert & Verdan)

**Demographics** — young–middle-aged ♂, older ♀; work/sport (ball sports)

**Digits** — **long (commonest) > ring > small**, dominant hand (Orthobullets, TO) · "ulnar three fingers" (TO)

## 2 Mechanism

**Commonest** — **sudden forced flexion** (impaction) of extended fingertip → DIP hyperflexion avulses tendon

**Bony mallet** — **axial load** on extended finger → P3 abuts P2 head

**Less common** — dorsal sharp/crush laceration

**Untreated** — terminal tendon retracts → central slip overpulls P2 → **swan neck** (PIP hyperextension + DIP flexion) (TO)

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — painful swollen DIP; fingertip rests **~45° flexion**; **no active DIP extension** (TO)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — AP + lateral finger: **bony avulsion at dorsal P3 base** or normal bones (tendinous); **assess volar subluxation of P3** (key decision)

**US** — not needed; loss of tendon motion, tears, fluid at insertion

## 5 Management

```
Mallet finger
├─ Tendinous / bony, P3 NOT subluxed → FULL-TIME DIP EXTENSION SPLINT (6–8 wk)
│      └─ chronic, supple, congruent → still SPLINT TRIAL
├─ Volar subluxation of P3 (even after splinting) → CRPP (extension block pinning) / ORIF
└─ Painful stiff arthritic DIP → DIP ARTHRODESIS
       └─ swan neck → Fowler central slip tenotomy / lateral band or FDS tenodesis
```

### Nonoperative – continuous DIP extension splinting (bony & tendinous, delayed, residual lag)

**Treatment of choice (TO)** — **extension splinting of DIP only**; **no PIP immobilisation**; DIP in extension / slight hyperextension\
✗ Wrong options: PIP extension block · buddy strap

**Partial articular avulsion, no subluxation (TO)** — **splint distal phalanx in extension**\
✗ Wrong options: ORIF

**Displaced fragment but joint reduced** — still **full-time DIP extension splint** (surgery ↑ complications)

**Regimen** — **24 h/day** (✗ Wrong options: 12 h), **6–8 wk** (✗ Wrong options: 2–4 wk); PIP free; then night splint

| Splint duration | Source |
|---|---|
| 6–8 wk full-time, then **4 wk nocturnal** (controversial) | Orthobullets |
| **6 wk** full-time, then **night 2–6 wk** | TO |
| **Bony 6 wk · tendinous ≥8 wk** | OB218821 |
| Bony 6–8 wk · tendinous **10–12 wk** | OB219900 |

**Splint details** — **volar** splint fewer complications than dorsal; avoid hyperextension (skin necrosis); swan-neck posture → add **dorsal blocking PIP splint**; stack splint; flexion exercises from 6 wk; extra night splinting no proven benefit

**Delayed presentation** — splint trial still works: **chronic >12 wk** supple, congruent, non-arthritic (Orthobullets) · **>4 wk** without fixed deformity or fragment >30% (TO) · 1 month (OB) → **chronic >6 wk ≠ surgery** (TO)

**Compliance** — key to success

**Outcome** — op = nonop for satisfaction & lag; ~80% satisfactory

**Residual lag <10°** — up to **40%** after op or nonop → **no functional deficit** (TO, OB)\
✗ Wrong options: dexterity loss · arthritis · swan neck/boutonnière

### Operative indications – volar subluxation & pinning; swan-neck risk

| Indication | Source |
|---|---|
| **Volar subluxation of P3 = absolute** | Orthobullets, OB |
| >50% articular surface · >2 mm articular gap/step-off · open injury (relative) | Orthobullets, TO |
| **All open injuries + large fragment >30% with DIP subluxation** | TO |
| Chronic failing splint trial | TO, OB |
| Chronic >12 wk without contracture, segmental loss → **tendon reconstruction** (~50% complications) | Orthobullets |
| Painful stiff arthritic DIP → **arthrodesis** | All |

**Fragment threshold conflict** — **>50%** (Orthobullets) · **>30% with subluxation** (TO) — read the options

**Bony mallet not fixed at earliest** (TO) — splint first; re-X-ray in splint before deciding

**Subluxed after closed reduction + splint** → **reduction and pinning**\
✗ Wrong options: repeat splint · PIP+DIP splint · fusion in young

**~50% articular + volar subluxation (athlete)** → **CRPP**\
✗ Wrong options: play in cast · fusion · fragment excision + dermatotenodesis · 12 wk splint

**Extension block pinning sequence (TO, Ishiguro)** — **DIP flexion → K-wire through extensor tendon into middle phalanx (dorsal block) → DIP extension (reduces fragment) → K-wire across DIP**

![Extension block pinning](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6014-dorsal-blocking.jpg)

**Pinning hierarchy (TO)** — simple K-wire < **dorsal extension block (Ishiguro)**

**ORIF options** — dorsal midline; pins, dorsal blocking pin, screw, plate, suture-button

**Tendon repair** — direct repair + trans-articular pin; tenodermodesis; spiral ORL reconstruction

**Swan neck correction (TO)** — **Fowler central slip tenotomy**, **lateral band tenodesis**, FDS tenodesis; mild may correct with DIP treatment alone

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Extensor lag** | <10° in 40%, no deficit; **1 mm terminal tendon lengthening → 25° lag** |
| ↓ DIP flexion | **1 mm shortening**; adhesions |
| **Swan neck** | Terminal retraction → central slip overpull + **volar plate & transverse retinacular lig. attenuation** → dorsal lateral bands → PIP hyperextension; **triangular ligament contracture maintains** (TO) |
| Skin (maceration, ulcer, nail) | **~70%** with splinting; dehiscence/infection after surgery |

| PIP | Hyperextension | Flexion | Paradoxical extension |

## How it's asked

**"True regarding mallet finger?"** → **extension splinting of DIP only** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: PIP + DIP · fix bony mallet early · >6 wk = surgery

**"Partial articular avulsion, no subluxation?"** → **splint distal phalanx in extension** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: buddy splint · ORIF · PIP extension block

**"Sequence of extension block pinning?"** → **DIP flexion → wire through tendon into P2 → DIP extension → wire across DIP** (TO)

**"Non-displaced bony mallet, 2 days?"** → **DIP extension splint 6–8 wk**

**"15 y, displaced fragment, joint reduced?"** → **full-time DIP extension splint**

**"Tendinous mallet regimen?"** → **24 h/day DIP splint for 6–8 wk**\
✗ Wrong options: 12 h · 2–4 wk · PIP

**"7° lag after 8 wk splint — outcome?"** → **no functional effect**

**"Bony mallet, 1 month, no subluxation?"** → **trial of extension splint 6 wk**\
✗ Wrong options: pinning · screw · fusion

**"Subluxed P3 after closed reduction + splint?"** → **reduction and pinning**

**"Untreated mallet risks which deformity?"** → **swan neck**\
✗ Wrong options: boutonnière · lumbrical plus · Dupuytren · scissoring

**"~50% fragment + volar subluxation, athlete?"** → **CRPP**
