# De Quervain's Tenosynovitis

*Hand · 19 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — **stenosing tenosynovitis of 1st dorsal compartment** (APL + EPB)

**Incidence** — very common, ~1/1000/y

**Demographics** — **♀ > ♂ (6×, OB)**; **30–50 y** (Orthobullets) · 5th–6th decade (OB); **dominant wrist**

**Risk factors** — **overuse** (golf, racquet sports), post-traumatic, **postpartum / lactating** (classic: mother of 2-month-old)

**Occupation** — **overuse syndrome**; typist, bowler, cell-phone use (TO, OB) · **no causal link with occupational risk factors** (OB) — read the options

### Pathology

**Pathoanatomy** — thickened, swollen **extensor retinaculum** → ↑ tendon friction

**Not inflammatory** — **NOT an inflammatory process**; **mucopolysaccharide** accumulation (Orthobullets) · **myxoid degeneration** + sheath thickening (TO)

**Tendon mechanics (TO)** — typist's graph = **hysteresis / energy-dissipation curve** of cyclically loaded tendon

| Viscoelastic term (TO) | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Creep** | ↑ deformation under **constant load** |
| **Stress relaxation** | ↓ stress under **constant deformation** |
| **Hysteresis** | Unloading curve ≠ loading curve; area = energy lost as heat |
| Cyclic loading | Curve shifts right, hysteresis ↓ → reproducible = **pseudo-elasticity** |
| Toe region | **Un-crimping** of collagen fibrils (low stiffness) → linear → yield/failure |

### Anatomy – APL/EPB & injection site

| Dorsal compartment | Contents | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **APL, EPB** | **De Quervain's** |
| 2 | ECRL, ECRB | **Intersection syndrome** (1st/2nd crossing) |
| 3 | EPL | — |
| 4 | EIP, EDC | — |
| 5 | EDM | Vaughan-Jackson |
| 6 | ECU | Snapping ECU |

**Nerve** — APL & EPB innervated by **PIN**\
✗ Wrong options: radial n. proper · AIN · median · deep ulnar

**EPB** — **more dorsal** than APL · **thinner** · single tendon · **absent in 7%** · often in **own subsheath** (dorsal) → missed at release; separate EPB compartment ↑ failure of nonop

**APL** — **thicker**, usually **≥2 slips**, may have own fibro-osseous compartment

**Separate compartment for EPB or APL** — **24–34%** of wrists

**1st compartment** — **most numerous tendon variations** in upper limb

**Radial artery** — dives **deep** to 1st compartment distal to scaphoid\
✗ Wrong options: superficial to APL

**Not EPL** — 3rd compartment, never in 1st

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — gradual **radial-sided wrist pain**, worse gripping/lifting with wrist neutral

**Tenderness** — 1st compartment **at radial styloid** / **1–2 cm proximal to radial styloid**

**Motion** — usually normal; pain with resisted radial deviation

**NV exam** — **normal**

| Test | Manoeuvre | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Finkelstein** | Examiner grasps thumb, quickly **ulnar-deviates** hand → pain at radial styloid | More indicative of **EPB > APL**; TO and OB also apply the name to the fist-around-thumb version |
| **Eichhoff** | Thumb clenched in fist + wrist ulnar-deviated → pain, **relieved when thumb extended** | Often confused with Finkelstein |

**Bowler/typist (TO)** — radial wrist pain ↑ by **thumb flexion + ulnar deviation** → **De Quervain's**\
✗ Wrong options: tennis/golfer's elbow · lunate AVN · UCL injury · scaphoid insufficiency #

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Diagnosis** — **clinical**; advanced imaging not needed

**X-ray** — AP + lateral; **usually not indicated**; rule out **thumb CMC (basilar) arthritis**, **carpal arthritis**

**Ultrasound** — thickened retinaculum over 1st compartment tendons

**Differentials** — thumb CMC OA, intersection syndrome, FCR tendinitis

## 5 Management

```
De Quervain's
├─ First line → rest + NSAIDs + thumb spica splint
├─ Second step → steroid injection into 1st dorsal compartment
│      (near radial styloid; 50–80% success after 1–2)
└─ Failed nonop ~6 mo / severe → surgical release of 1st compartment
       └─ recurrence → re-explore for unreleased EPB subsheath
```

### Nonoperative

**Injection site** — **1st dorsal compartment near radial styloid**\
✗ Wrong options: CMC joint · carpal tunnel · A1 pulley · 1st/2nd crossing

**Steroid vs splint** — **steroid superior**; adding splint/NSAID after injection does not improve outcome

**Immobilisation** — high failure rate, **except lactating women** (nonop highly effective)

**Septum between APL and EPB (TO)** — may need **injection on both sides** of the septum (MRI/US shows it)

**TO conservative list** — activity modification, NSAIDs, ice massage, thumb spica, **steroid into APL/EPB sheath** → release if fails

### Surgical release

**Incision** — radial-based, proximal to wrist; transverse

**Release on DORSAL side** of sheath → prevents **volar subluxation** of tendons

**Protect** — **superficial radial sensory nerve**

**Release all** — APL slips + **EPB subsheath**

### Recurrence – unreleased EPB subsheath

**Classic** — recurrent pain, **+ Finkelstein**, tender 1st compartment, **– Tinel**, normal skin → **failure to decompress EPB subsheath**\
✗ Wrong options: neuroma: Tinel – · CRPS: no skin change · EPL/APB: not in 1st compartment

**Prognosis** — most resolve nonop; **high recurrence rate**

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Clue |
|---|---|
| **Superficial radial nerve injury / neuroma** | **+ Tinel**; no positive Finkelstein |
| **Incomplete release → recurrence** | **Unreleased EPB / separate APL compartment** |
| **Volar tendon subluxation** | Avoided by dorsal-side release |
| CRPS | Skin changes |

## X vs Y

| | **De Quervain's** | **Intersection syndrome** | Thumb CMC OA | Snapping ECU |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Site | Radial styloid | **More proximal + more dorsal** | CMC joint | Ulnar wrist |
| Structures | APL + EPB | **1st crossing 2nd** (APL/EPB over ECRL/ECRB) | Basal joint | ECU (6th) |
| Clue | Finkelstein + | Crepitus, pain on **resisted wrist extension** | **Grind test**, older | Snap pronation → supination |

## How it's asked

**"Postpartum radial wrist pain — inject where?"** → **1st dorsal compartment near radial styloid**

**"True during 1st compartment release?"** → **EPB more dorsal than APL**\
✗ Wrong options: EPB thicker · EPB multiple slips · radial artery superficial · EPL subsheath

**"Exam finding that localises to APL + EPB?"** → **radial pain on Finkelstein**\
✗ Wrong options: resisted extension = intersection · Phalen = CTS · grind = CMC OA

**"Recurrence 3 mo after release, Finkelstein +, Tinel –?"** → **unreleased EPB subsheath**

**"Nerve supplying affected tendons?"** → **PIN**

**"Bowler: pain ↑ thumb flexion + ulnar deviation?"** → **De Quervain's** (TO)

**"Typist, dorsolateral wrist pain + hysteresis curve?"** → **De Quervain's** (TO)

**"Which side of sheath to incise?"** → **dorsal** (prevents volar subluxation)
