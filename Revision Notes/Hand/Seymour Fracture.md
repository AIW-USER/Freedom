# Seymour Fracture

*Hand · 83 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & epidemiology

**Definition** — **displaced distal phalanx physeal fracture + nailbed injury** in **children** (TO) = **open fracture**

**Not** — adult thumb hyperextension sports injury (TO)

**Physeal involvement** — 20–30% of paediatric phalangeal fractures

**Commonest digit** — **middle finger** (TO)

| Fracture types (TO) | Nailbed injury types |
|---|---|
| **SH I** | Nailbed laceration |
| **SH II** | **Nail plate subluxation** (superficial to eponychial fold) |
| **Juxta-epiphyseal metaphyseal**, 1–2 mm distal to physis | **Interposed germinal matrix** at fracture |

### Anatomy – why it flexes

**Extensor** — inserts **dorsal epiphysis** (with volar plate) → extension force on epiphysis

**FDP** — inserts **volar metaphysis** → flexion force on shaft

**Result** — **flexion moment at physis** → **apparent mallet deformity** (TO)

## 2 Mechanism

**Direct trauma / crush** — **finger caught in door** (TO), hammer, heavy object, ball, sport

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — apparent **mallet deformity**; swelling, ecchymosis, subungual haematoma; **nail plate superficial to eponychial fold**

**Delayed** — tender swollen DIP, infection

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — PA (**may look normal**) + **lateral** (flexion at fracture)

**Signs** — **widened physis** (→ **interposed tissue**) or epiphysis–metaphysis displacement

**vs mallet fracture** — paediatric mallet = **SH III/IV** avulsion, line **enters DIPJ**; Seymour line **traverses physis**, not DIPJ

## 5 Management

```
Seymour fracture
├─ Closed, minimally displaced, no interposition → CLOSED REDUCTION (hyperflexion) + SPLINT
├─ Closed, displaced/unstable → CLOSED REDUCTION + PIN ACROSS DIPJ
│      └─ reduction difficult → assume interposition → OPEN
└─ Open (nail plate above eponychial fold) → I&D + REMOVE INTERPOSED TISSUE + REDUCE + NAILBED REPAIR ± K-wire
```

### Anatomy & management – nail bed repair, remove interposed tissue, reduce

**Open Seymour** — **nail plate removal + nailbed repair + removal of interposed soft tissue (germinal matrix) + open reduction**

**Must include** — **removal of interposed soft tissue** (pinning optional adjunct)

**Pinning (conflict)** — open reduction + **retrograde K-wire across fracture + DIPJ** (Orthobullets, Abzug) · pinning **not required**; nail plate replaced under fold = sufficient stability (OB)

**Avoid DIPJ-sparing pin** — poor purchase in epiphysis

**Avoid closed reduction + pin** in interposition → locks germinal matrix in fracture

**Avoid mallet splint in situ / buddy taping** — growth & nail disturbance

**Nailbed** — repair every nailbed injury; absorbable (6-0) suture or Dermabond

**Post-op** — extension splint **4–6 wk**; IV Abx → short oral course

**Open vs closed** — **open management fewer complications**; Abzug: surgery for open **and** closed

**ED management** — 89% managed in ED; OR mainly for failed closed reduction (Lin)

**Antibiotics** — **within 24 h → infection 6.9%** vs **>24 h → 76.5%** (Rask; overall 27.3%); **clindamycin** for delayed (bone penetration)

**Delayed treatment** — infection 0% (acute appropriate) · 15% (partial) · **45% (delayed)** (Reyes & Ho)

## 6 Complications

### Complication – osteomyelitis

**Highest risk, delayed presentation** — **osteomyelitis**\
✗ Wrong options: growth arrest · septic arthritis · extension deformity

**List** — **nail dystrophy**, **growth disturbance / physeal arrest**, secondary displacement, **chronic osteomyelitis** (not treated as open), **flexion deformity**

**Nail growth disturbance** — 11/24 despite full motion in 23/24 (Krusche-Mandl)

## X vs Y

| | **Seymour fracture** | Paediatric mallet fracture |
|---|---|---|
| Fracture | **SH I/II or juxta-epiphyseal** | **SH III/IV** |
| DIPJ involvement | No | Yes |
| Nailbed | **Injured (open)** | Usually intact |
| Key treatment | **Debride, remove matrix, repair nailbed** | Splint |

## How it's asked

**"True of Seymour fracture EXCEPT"** → **adult thumb hyperextension in sports** (TO)

**"12-y crushed middle finger, mallet posture, subungual haematoma — next?"** → **nail plate removal, nailbed repair, remove interposed tissue, open reduction**\
✗ Wrong options: closed pin · mallet splint

**"Surgery must include?"** → **removal of interposed soft tissue**\
✗ Wrong options: pinning

**"Anatomic cause of deformity?"** → **extensor on dorsal epiphysis, flexor on volar metaphysis**

**"Delayed Seymour, highest-risk complication?"** → **osteomyelitis**
