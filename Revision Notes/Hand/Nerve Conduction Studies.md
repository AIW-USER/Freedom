# Nerve Conduction Studies

*Hand · 59 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Uses

**Components** — **NCV + EMG** (= EDS)

**Uses** — localise **compression**/neuropathy; **UMN vs LMN**; root vs plexus vs peripheral; **severity & prognosis** (neurapraxia good; axonotmesis/neurotmesis poor); denervation/reinnervation; **worker's compensation**/secondary gain

**Indications** — CTS, cubital tunnel, cervical & lumbar radiculopathy, shoulder nerve dysfunction (winging)

**Pretest probability** — EDS most useful when nerve injury **likely** (severity, location, prognosis); low probability + nonspecific sx → inconclusive/misleading; adjunct after Hx + Ex

**Fibres tested** — **large myelinated** motor + sensory (position, vibration, some light touch)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Nerve conduction velocity – measures

![NCV technique](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6113-nerve-conduciton-velocity.jpg)

**Stimulus** — **0.1–0.2 ms square wave**; motor studies add a 2nd proximal stimulus (cancels NMJ delay)

**NCV** — **distance ÷ latency** (cathode → recording electrode); NCV ∝ 1/latency

**Latency** — stimulus onset → response onset (incl. NMJ transmission + muscle propagation)

**NCV determinants** — **myelin thickness**, internode distance, temperature, age

| Age | NCV (% adult) |
|---|---|
| Newborn | **50%** |
| 1 y | **75%** |
| 5 y | **100%** |

**Amplitude** — baseline → negative peak (mV) = **number of functioning axons/muscle fibres**

**Duration** — spread of CVs; ↑ in acute demyelination

**Late responses (proximal lesions, e.g. GBS)** — **F-wave**; **H-reflex** (Iα at knee → soleus, **S1 root**; tibial/sciatic neuropathy)

### Demyelination – increased latency

**Demyelination** — **↑ latency** + **↓ conduction velocity**; amplitude normal/↓; evoked response absent/prolonged\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ amplitude · normal evoked response · unchanged CV/latency

**Velocity vs latency** — velocity **less specific** than latency; **<52 m/s abnormal**

**Clinical focal demyelination** — ↓ DTRs, ↓ vibration, intermittent pain/paraesthesia; **no motor strength/sensibility loss**

### Interpreting NCS in carpal tunnel syndrome

**Abnormal for CTS (Orthobullets NCS note)** — **distal sensory latency >3.2 ms** · **motor latency >4.3 ms**

**Mild–moderate CTS** — **↑ latency + ↓ NCV, normal amplitude** = focal demyelination, axon number intact

**Severe/chronic CTS** — **↓ amplitude** (SNAP/CMAP) = axonal loss from ischaemia → surgery less predictable

**Prognosis** — mild–moderate + temporary relief from steroid injection → **CTR predictably relieves**

**EDS in CTS** — not needed for diagnosis; only EDS grades mild → severe; ambiguous cases, outcome prediction

**SNAP vs CMAP** — sensory nerve action potential vs compound motor action potential

### Electromyography

**Purpose** — root vs nerve vs muscle; acute vs chronic; reinnervation; nerve continuity

**Insertional activity** — **↑ (>300–500 ms) = early denervation** (polymyositis, myotonia, myopathy); **↓ = prolonged denervation** (fibrosis)

**Normal spontaneous** — **end plate potentials & spikes**

**Fibrillations** — **single** muscle fibre; denervated fibre membrane oscillation; appear **3–5 wk** after lesion; also muscular dystrophy

**Fasciculations** — **group** of fibres; **ALS**, spinal muscular atrophy, polio, syringomyelia; "**bag of worms**"

**Other abnormal** — positive sharp waves, complex repetitive discharges, myokymic discharges

| Condition | Insertional | Spontaneous | Minimal activity | Interference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | N | Silent | Bi/triphasic | Complete |
| **Axonal neuropathy** | ↑ | **Fibs / PSW** | Bi/triphasic | Incomplete |
| **Demyelinating neuropathy** | N | **Silent** | Bi/triphasic | Incomplete |
| Anterior horn cell | ↑ | Fibs/**fasciculations**, PSW | **Large polyphasic** | Incomplete |
| Inflammatory myopathy | ↑ | Fibs, myotonia | **Small polyphasic** | Early |
| Noninflammatory myopathy | N | N | Small polyphasic | Early |
| NMJ disorder | N | N | Bi/triphasic (↓ amplitude/duration) | Early/N |
| **Neurapraxia** | N | **Silent** | None | None |
| Axonotmesis / neurotmesis | ↑ | **Fibs / PSW** | None | None |

## How it's asked

**"Demyelination on NCS?"** → **increased latency**\
✗ Wrong options: ↑ amplitude · unchanged velocity

**"↑ latency, ↓ NCV, equal amplitude + transient injection relief — counsel?"** → **mild–moderate CTS; surgery predictably relieves**

**"Amplitude reflects?"** → **number of functioning axons**

**"NCV of 1-year-old?"** → **75% of adult**

**"Fasciculations seen in?"** → **ALS / anterior horn disease**

**"Fibrillations appear after?"** → **3–5 weeks**

**"H-reflex tests?"** → **S1 root**
