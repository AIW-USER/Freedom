# PIN Compression Syndrome

*Hand · 64 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — compressive neuropathy of **PIN** (deep motor radial n.) → extensor compartment weakness, **no sensory loss**

**Incidence** — ~3/100,000/yr; manual labourers, ♂, bodybuilders

**Causes** — repetitive pronosupination microtrauma · trauma (Monteggia, radial head) · **space-occupying (lipoma = commonest lesion, ganglion)** · **RA radiocapitellar synovitis** · iatrogenic

### Anatomy – arcade of Frohse, innervated muscles & recovery order

**Origin** — radial n. splits at **anterior radiocapitellar joint**, just proximal to supinator

**Course** — between 2 heads of **supinator**, around **radial neck**, over APL origin → posterior interosseous membrane → **floor of 4th compartment** (dorsal wrist capsule sensation)

**Cutaneous** — **none**

| PIN innervates | Not PIN |
|---|---|
| **Supinator, EDC, EDM, ECU, APL, EPB, EPL, EIP** (± ECRB) | **ECRL, BR** (± ECRB) = radial n. proper; **APB** = median recurrent motor |

**EMG fibrillations** — **EPL / supinator / APL**\
✗ Wrong options: any set with ECRL, BR, APB

**Last to recover** — **EIP** (most distal)

| 5 sites of compression | Note |
|---|---|
| Fibrous bands anterior to radiocapitellar jt | Between brachialis & BR |
| **Leash of Henry** | Recurrent radial vessels at radial neck |
| **ECRB** edge | Medio-proximal |
| **Arcade of Frohse** | Proximal edge superficial supinator — **commonest spontaneous site** |
| Distal supinator edge | |

**Not lateral intermuscular septum** — compresses **radial nerve** proper, not PIN

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — insidious; forearm/wrist pain (site-dependent; just distal to lateral epicondyle = arcade); **weak finger MCP, thumb, wrist extension**

**Wrist** — extends with **radial deviation** (ECRL intact, ECU lost); can't extend in neutral/ulnar deviation

**Thumb** — **can't extend thumb** (EPL)

**Preserved** — PIP extension (intrinsics, ulnar) · thumb flexion (AIN) · sensation

**Resisted supination** — ↑ pain

**Tenodesis** — **normal**\
✗ Wrong options: tendon rupture

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — clinical

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray / MRI** — not routinely needed; **MRI** to locate a **mass** + plan resection

**EMG** — level of compression, rule out other neuropathy

### Differential – radial tunnel syndrome & rheumatoid extensor rupture

| Condition | Key finding |
|---|---|
| **PIN syndrome** | Pain distal to lateral epicondyle (mobile wad) + **extensor weakness** + **intact sensation** |
| **Radial tunnel syndrome** | Mobile wad pain, **no motor deficit** |
| **RA extensor tendon rupture** | **Abnormal tenodesis** |
| **RA PIN palsy** | **Peri-elbow synovitis** → normal tenodesis, can't initiate or hold extension |
| **Sagittal band rupture** | Can't initiate extension from fist but **can maintain** after passive extension |
| Wartenberg | Superficial radial n. → dorsal thumb numbness, normal motor |
| C7 radiculopathy | Sensory loss, triceps + wrist flexor weakness |
| Intersection syndrome | 1st/2nd compartment crossing pain |

**Others** — brachial plexus, peripheral neuropathy

## 5 Management

```
PIN compression
├─ All → rest, splint, NSAIDs, activity modification
│      └─ tender distal to lat epicondyle, no mass → injection 3–4 cm distal
├─ Mass on imaging → SURGICAL DECOMPRESSION
└─ Symptoms >3 mo despite nonop → SURGICAL DECOMPRESSION
```

**Approach** — **anterolateral elbow** (commonest) or posterior

**Release** — brachialis–BR bands → leash of Henry → ECRB edge → arcade of Frohse + distal supinator

**Outcomes** — variable; spontaneous motor recovery **75–97%** (non-traumatic); improves up to **18 mo**

## 6 Complications

**Neglected** — fibrosis of PIN muscles → **tendon transfers**

**Chronic pain**

## How it's asked

**"EMG fibrillations in PIN compression"** → **EPL / supinator / APL**

**"Commonest site of spontaneous PIN entrapment"** → **arcade of Frohse**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral intermuscular septum

**"Lesion at arcade of Frohse — exam finding"** → **can't extend thumb**\
✗ Wrong options: can't flex thumb · PIP extension lost

**"Last muscle to recover"** → **EIP**

**"RA, can't extend fingers, normal tenodesis, wrist radially deviates"** → **peri-elbow synovitis compressing PIN**

**"Exam corroborating PIN syndrome"** → **pain distal to lateral epicondyle, extensor weakness, intact sensation**
