# Scaphoid Fracture

*Hand · 78 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology – most common carpal fracture

**Most common carpal fracture (TO)** — **scaphoid (60%) > triquetrum > trapezium**; 15% of acute wrist injuries

**Who** — ♂ 2:1, 3rd decade, contact sport

| Location | Adult | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Waist** | **65–70%** | Commonest |
| Proximal third | 25% (5–10% TO) | **AVN / nonunion** |
| Distal third | 10% (10–20% TO) | Heals well |
| Tubercle | 5% | — |

**Children** — historically **distal pole/tubercle** (ossifies distal → proximal; 52% tubercle TO) · **now waist (middle ⅓) most common** (OB) → read options

### Anatomy & blood supply

**Bone** — largest of proximal row; **>75% articular cartilage**; links proximal & distal rows

**Major supply** — **dorsal carpal branch of radial artery** → enters **dorsal non-articular ridge** → **retrograde** to **proximal 80%**

**Minor supply** — **superficial palmar arch (volar radial a.)** → **distal tubercle** → distal 20%

**AVN by level** — **proximal ⅕ → 100%** · **proximal ⅓ → 33%** (untreated)

![Retrograde scaphoid blood supply](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6034-blood-supply-ttc-moved.jpg)

## 2 Mechanism

### Wrist loading biomechanics

**Mechanism** — **axial load on hyper-dorsiflexed, pronated, ulnar-deviated wrist** (FOOSH)

**Force transmission** — neutral: scaphoid fossa ≈ lunate fossa (slight scaphoid predominance) · **extension → ↑ force through scaphoid fossa**

**Stability** — transverse > vertical/oblique

**Humpback** — distal fragment **flexes**, proximal **extends** (pulled by SLIL) → DISI

## 3 Presentation

### Diagnosis – snuffbox tenderness, scaphoid view & MRI

**Signs** — **snuffbox tenderness** · **scaphoid tubercle tenderness** · **scaphoid compression** (axial thumb load) · pain on **resisted pronation**

**All 3 positive in 24 h** — sensitivity 87–100%, specificity 74%

![Anatomic snuffbox](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6034-snuffbox.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Imaging | Fact asked |
|---|---|
| X-ray series | PA, lateral, **45° semi-pronated oblique**, **scaphoid view** |
| **Scaphoid view** | **PA in 20° ulnar deviation** → waist best |
| Initial X-ray negative | **27%** → **repeat in 14–21 d** (immobilise meanwhile) |
| **MRI** | **Most sensitive for occult # <24 h** (~100%); **proximal pole vascularity (T1)** |
| Bone scan | Occult # at **72 h** (sens 100%, spec 98%) |
| **CT (1 mm along scaphoid axis)** | **Displacement, humpback, union/nonunion after fixation**; not for occult # |

| Measurement / sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Displacement >1 mm** | Unstable → fix |
| **Intrascaphoid angle >35°** / humpback | Flexion malalignment → ORIF (volar) |
| **Radiolunate angle >15°** | **DISI** → ORIF |
| Cysts, fracture widening | **Delayed union** (Herbert C) |
| Sclerosis / ↓ T1 signal proximal pole | **AVN** (MRI T1 best) |

✗ Wrong options: **Wrong views** — 30° supinated oblique = **pisiform**; hyperextension axial = **carpal tunnel view**; clenched fist = **SL ligament**

**High suspicion + negative X-ray + wants to RTP** — **MRI**

## 5 Management

```
Scaphoid fracture
├─ Suspected, X-ray negative → immobilise + repeat X-ray 14–21 d  OR  MRI
├─ Nondisplaced waist/distal → cast (≥8–12 wk)  OR  percutaneous screw (faster union/RTW)
├─ PROXIMAL POLE (any) → ORIF via DORSAL approach, headless compression screw
└─ Displaced >1 mm / humpback / DISI / perilunate / comminuted → ORIF
       ├─ waist/distal + humpback → VOLAR approach
       └─ delayed (≥6 wk) with cysts → ORIF + bone graft (volar for waist)
```

**Nonop union** — **<1 mm displacement → 90%** (88–95% if started <3 wk); ↑ nonunion if immobilisation delayed **>4 wk**

**Cast** — **wrist alone sufficient** (thumb & elbow add nothing but stiffness — OB); long vs short arm: no consensus; ≥8 wk, up to 12–14 wk; RTP only when healed

**PEMF** — may help delayed union

### Operative vs nonoperative – percutaneous fixation of nondisplaced waist fractures

**Percutaneous vs cast** — **↓ time to union (6–7 vs 10–12 wk)**, **faster RTW/sport**; similar total cost; no higher union rate; no better 2-y grip/ROM

**Union after surgery** — 90–95%

**Strongest reason to fix (stem)** — **fracture location (proximal pole)**\
✗ Wrong options: age, job, dominance, chronicity at 1 wk

### ORIF indications & approach (displacement, proximal pole, delayed presentation)

**Operative criteria**

| Treatment | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Cast** | Stable, **nondisplaced (<1 mm)** waist/distal; start **<3–4 wk** |
| **Percutaneous screw** | Nondisplaced waist (faster union/RTW) · displacement >1 mm **without** significant angulation |
| **ORIF** | **Displaced**, humpback, DISI, perilunate, comminuted, unstable vertical oblique, **proximal pole** |
| ORIF + graft | **Delayed presentation with cysts** |
| Vascularised graft | **Nonunion with cysts/AVN** only |

**ORIF indications** — **displacement >1 mm** · **intrascaphoid angle >35°** · **radiolunate angle >15° (DISI)** · humpback 15° · **perilunate** · comminuted · unstable vertical oblique · **proximal pole**

| | **Volar approach** | **Dorsal approach** |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | **Waist & distal ⅓, humpback** | **Proximal pole** |
| Interval | **FCR – radial artery** | Limit to proximal half of scaphoid |
| Blood supply | **Spared** (TO ✓) | Risk to dorsal ridge vessels |
| At risk | — | **EPL**; proximal pole cartilage |
| Weakness | Reduction harder (TO); not for oblique/proximal pole (can't be perpendicular) | Poor distal ⅓ access |
| Must close | RSC ligament | — |

**TO: volar better because** — **(1) less vascular injury, (3) distal ⅓ easier**\
✗ Wrong options: easier reduction · oblique fractures

**Proximal pole** — **open reduction + headless differential-pitch screw via dorsal approach** (100% healing reported)\
✗ Wrong options: cast · K-wires · percutaneous · vascularised graft acutely

**K-wires** — only if proximal fragment too small for screw head

**Displaced waist with cysts at 6 wk** — **ORIF + bone graft, volar**

### Screw fixation technique – central axis position

**Most important** — **long screw in central axis** (into subchondral bone) → max rigidity

**Center-center** on AP & lateral; **volar approach → excise proximal trapezium** to get dorsal-ulnar start point (else too volar & radial → nonunion)

**Open vs percutaneous dorsal** — open: **↓ risk of screw prominence** above subchondral bone (direct view)

**Don't violate STT cartilage**

![Percutaneous screw fixation](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6034-orif.jpg)

### Paediatric scaphoid fractures

**Most common now** — **middle ⅓ (waist)**; unicortical up to 21%

**Treatment** — nondisplaced → cast **4–6 wk**; displaced → ORIF

## 6 Complications

### Persistent pain after fixation – CT assessment

**Nonunion definition** — no union **6 mo** after cast/fixation

**Pain months after fixation, hardware OK on X-ray** — **fine-cut CT along scaphoid axis**\
✗ Wrong options: MRI (metal) · bone scan (nonspecific) · clenched-fist view

| Complication | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **Nonunion** | 5–10% after cast; ↑ proximal pole, **vertical oblique**, displacement >1 mm, age, **smoking** |
| **AVN** | 13–50%; proximal ⅕ ≈ 100% |
| Malunion | **Humpback** (flexed distal fragment) |
| Screw prominence | Subchondral penetration → arthrosis |
| **SNAC** | End result of nonunion |

### Vascularised bone graft (medial femoral condyle)

**Indication** — nonunion with **cysts / AVN / collapse**

**MFC graft pedicle** — **longitudinal branch of descending geniculate artery** (superomedial genicular in 11–20%)

**MFT (medial femoral trochlea)** — **transverse branch** of DGA → **proximal pole** (osteochondral)

**1,2 ICSRA** — distal radius pedicled; smaller graft

**Not for acute fractures** — vascularised graft only for nonunion

## X vs Y

| Imaging question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Occult fracture <24 h | **MRI** |
| Occult at 72 h (no MRI) | Bone scan |
| Displacement / union after fixation | **CT** |
| Proximal pole AVN | **MRI T1** |

| Graft | Pedicle | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **MFC** | **Longitudinal br. DGA** | **Waist nonunion with collapse** |
| **MFT** | **Transverse br. DGA** | **Proximal pole** (needs cartilage) |
| 1,2 ICSRA | Radial artery branch | Smaller dorsal graft |
