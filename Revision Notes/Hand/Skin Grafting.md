# Skin Grafting

*Hand · 84 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **avascular** dermal tissue covering a **well-perfused** bed; usually autologous

**Nutrition** — diffusion from wound bed (STSG & FTSG)

**Goals** — cover deep structures · bacterial barrier · restore function · prevent contracture

**Indication** — well-perfused bed over **muscle or subcutaneous tissue**

**Contraindication** — exposed **bone, tendon, nerve, vessel** → flap

**Palmar vs dorsal skin** — palm: **no hair or sebaceous glands**, **Meissner + Vater-Pacini** corpuscles; dorsum = like rest of body → STSG adequate

## 5 Management

### STSG vs FTSG

| | **STSG** | **FTSG** |
|---|---|---|
| Use | **Dorsal hand**; beds where contraction won't limit motion | **Volar hand & fingertips** |
| Donor | **Anterolateral thigh** | **Proximal forearm, medial upper arm, hypothenar eminence** |
| Content | Keratinocytes + variable dermis | **Full epidermis + dermis**, hair follicles, sweat glands; **no subcutaneous fat** |
| Contracture | More (thicker = less) | **Less** |
| Sensation / durability | Less | **Better reinnervation, more shear-resistant** |

**STSG thickness** — thin **0.005–0.012 in** · intermediate **0.012–0.018** · thick **0.018–0.030**

**Meshed STSG** — ↑ surface area, ↓ haematoma & infection → **better take**

**Defat FTSG** — fat = barrier to vascular ingrowth → graft failure

**FTSG technique** — gentle tension, multiple **tie-over sutures** (↓ shear), medicated gauze + moist cotton, dressing **5–7 d**

**FTSG cons** — haematoma/seroma → failure; revascularisation **2–3 d**

### Palmar defect coverage

**Palm defect (post-resection)** — **flap + full-thickness skin coverage** (e.g. posterior interosseous artery island flap)

(✗ Wrong options: **Traps** — VAC alone) · wet-to-dry alone · STSG alone · flap + meshed STSG

![Palmar defect after mass resection](../../MCQ/attachments/q109-a-palm-cancer-met.jpg)

### Other grafts

**Allograft** — **temporary** bed preparation for autograft

**Xenograft** — **biologic dressing**

## How it's asked

**"Palmar soft-tissue defect after mass excision — coverage?"** → **flap with full-thickness skin**\
✗ Wrong options: meshed STSG

**"Graft for dorsal hand"** → **STSG**

**"Why remove fat from FTSG?"** → **barrier to vascularisation**

**"Meshing advantage"** → **↓ haematoma/infection, better take**
