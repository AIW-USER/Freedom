# Hook of Hamate Fracture

*Hand · 40 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — **2–4% of carpal fractures**; ♂:♀ 2:1; often missed (mean **4 wk** to diagnosis)

**Sports** — **golf, baseball, hockey**, racquet sports; **non-dominant hand** in batters

### Anatomy

**Articulations** — 4th & 5th MC, capitate, triquetrum

**Flexor retinaculum** — hook = palpable attachment

**Deep motor branch ulnar n.** — lies **under the hook**

**Blood supply** — radial + ulnar foramina at base; **ulnar vessel absent 29%** → high nonunion

| Guyon's canal border | Structure |
|---|---|
| Roof | Superficial palmar carpal ligament |
| Floor | Deep flexor retinaculum, hypothenar muscles |
| Ulnar | Pisiform, pisohamate lig. |
| Radial | **Hook of hamate** |

**Not a border** — **capitate**

**Ulnar tunnel zones** — 1 (motor + sensory) & 2 (motor): **ganglion, hook of hamate fracture**; 3 (sensory): **ulnar artery thrombosis**

## 2 Mechanism

**Direct blow to volar proximal palm** — grounding golf club, **checking a bat**, fouling a pitch, racket/oar strike; shear from ring/small flexors; FOOSH

**Associated** — **bipartite hamate** (smooth cortices = normal variant, not fracture) · ring/small **flexor tendonitis/rupture** · **ulnar neuropathy in Guyon** (often motor) · other carpal fracture

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **ulnar wrist pain** (commonest), hypothenar pain, pain with tight grip; ± ring/small paraesthesia

**Exam** — **hook tenderness (80% sensitive)**; ↓ ulnar deviation, ↓ grip; Tinel at Guyon; intrinsic weakness

**Hook of hamate pull test** — ulnar deviation + resisted DIP flexion ring/small → pain (70% sens)

**Ulnar nerve clue** — **dorsal ulnar sensation normal** → lesion at wrist\
✗ Wrong options: cubital tunnel

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Fact |
|---|---|
| PA/lateral | 10% sensitivity; PA: **absent "eye sign"** (cortical ring) |
| **Carpal tunnel view** | **Best/most sensitive radiograph**, **first test** (40%) |
| Semi-supinated oblique | Adjunct |
| **CT** | Confirms if X-rays negative (92%; missed if cuts >1 mm) — **next step** |
| **MRI** | **Most accurate** (100%) with high suspicion |
| Bone scan | Confirms with CT (OB) · not typically indicated (OB) — read the options |
| ✗ Wrong option: clenched fist | SL ligament |
| ✗ Wrong option: MRA | Hypothenar hammer |

## 5 Management

```
Hook of hamate fracture
├─ Acute nondisplaced, low demand → ulnar gutter cast 6 wk
│      (nonunion 40–50%, mostly painless)
├─ Chronic symptomatic / nonunion / ulnar neuritis / tendon irritation → EXCISION
├─ High-level athlete → EXCISION (RTP ~6 wk)
└─ Acute displaced, can't lose grip → ORIF (countersunk headless screw)
```

### Treatment – fragment excision & return to play

**Excision** — **surgical treatment of choice**; fastest return; **RTP 6 wk** (some 3 mo)

**Indications** — chronic symptomatic nonunion, **ulnar neuritis**, flexor tendon irritation/grating, high-level athlete

✗ Wrong options: **Traps** — Guyon release alone, carpal tunnel release, pisiform excision, cast for chronic nonunion\
✗ Wrong options: tendon

**Technique** — incision along **ulnar border of ring finger**; release Guyon; **subperiosteal** removal (protect deep motor br.)

**Excision cost** — small finger FDP strength ↓ **10–15%**; FDP shifts 5 mm ulnar

**ORIF** — ~100% union, theoretically better grip; screws **countersunk** (deep motor br.); neurolysis if neuritis

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Nonunion** | **Commonest**, ~50%; motion + poor supply |
| **Ulnar neuritis** | 20% → excision |
| **Closed rupture ring/small flexors** | **Hook nonunion** (fulcrum); rare |
| Weak grip | Excision of large fragment |
| Recurrence | Pro baseball; prevent with extraperiosteal excision |

**Other closed ruptures** — volar plate DRF → **FPL** · closed DRF → **EPL** · scaphoid spur → radial flexors

## How it's asked

**"Baseball player, hypothenar pain + ulnar paraesthesia — view?"** → **carpal tunnel view**

**"Chronic golfer wrist pain, initial test"** → **carpal tunnel view**\
✗ Wrong options: bone scan, EMG, MRA

**"Mechanic, interossei weak, dorsal sensation normal, X-ray"** → **hamate nonunion**

**"Not a border of Guyon's canal"** → **capitate**

**"Rower, oar into palm, carpal tunnel view"** → **hook of hamate fracture**

**"Pro baseball, 1 yr hypothenar pain, CT fracture — Rx"** → **hook excision**

**"Best literature outcome / RTP"** → **surgical excision**

**"Ulnar nerve sx + grating fingers 6 mo after FOOSH"** → **excision of hook**

**"Return to play after excision"** → **6 wk**

**"True of CT finding"** → **excision → excellent RTP**\
✗ Wrong options: normal variant · APB weakness · high union nonop

**"Closed small-finger flexor rupture from"** → **hook of hamate nonunion**
