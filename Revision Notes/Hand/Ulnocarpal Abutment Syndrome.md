# Ulnocarpal Abutment Syndrome

*Hand · 98 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Load transmission

| Ulnar variance | Ulna load | Radius load |
|---|---|---|
| **Neutral** | **20%** | **80%** |
| **+2 mm** | **40%** | 60% |

**Definition** — excessive impact between **ulnar head and carpus (primarily lunate)** → common ulnar-sided wrist pain

**Positive variance causes/associations** — SL dissociation, **TFCC tears**, LT tears, **radial shortening after Colles**, **distal radius physeal fracture** in adolescence (TO)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — ulnar/dorsal DRUJ pain; ↑ with **ulnar deviation**, **axial load**, rotation (jar opening, wringing towel, door opening, punching, weight bearing on extended wrist); relief with rest

| Test | Technique / meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ballottement** | Dorsal–palmar ulna displacement in ulnar deviation → pain |
| **Nakamura ulnar stress** | Pronated wrist ulnar deviated + axial load, flex/extend → pain |
| **Fovea test** | Palpate between styloid + FCU → **TFCC / ulnotriquetral ligament tear** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Required view** — **PA/AP in neutral rotation** (zero rotation) to measure variance

**Pronated grip view** — **↑ impaction** (dynamic positive variance)

**X-ray signs** — **positive ulnar variance** · **sclerosis/cysts of lunate + ulnar head**

**MRI** — TFCC tear (changes treatment); T1/T2 lunate signal change

**Arthrography** — TFCC + LT tears

### Differential – benign variant

**Differentials** — ulnar styloid impaction, DRUJ instability/arthritis, TFCC tear, LT tear, pisotriquetral arthritis, ECU tendinitis/instability, **LT coalition**

**Lunotriquetral coalition** — **uncommon normal variant, no significant sequelae**; incidental; commoner in African descent; persistent pain → arthrodesis\
✗ Wrong options: takedown for ROM · scaphoid fracture risk · abnormal TFCC · inflammatory arthritis

**Coalition syndromes** — arthrogryposis, diastrophic dwarfism, Turner, Ellis-van Creveld

## 5 Management

```
Ulnocarpal abutment
├─ Mild → rest, splint, supportive
├─ Positive variance ± DRUJ incongruity (young, active) → ulnar shortening osteotomy
│      └─ variance <4 mm → arthroscopic wafer
└─ DRUJ arthritis → salvage
       ├─ laborer → hemiresection + TFCC / Sauvé-Kapandji
       └─ low demand → Darrach
```

### Ulnar shortening osteotomy vs wafer – indications & outcomes

**Ulnar shortening osteotomy** — **treatment of choice** for most positive variance + **DRUJ incongruity** in young adults (TO: MMA student after adolescent DRF → **distal ulnar osteotomy**; ✗ Wrong options: TFCC repair alone · interposition · Darrach)

**USO advantages** — preserves ulnar dome cartilage + DRUJ; **tightens TFCC + ulnocarpal ligaments**; may improve DRUJ congruity; **carpal cysts can resolve**

**USO approach** — **subcutaneous border of ulna**; ± arthroscopic TFCC repair/débridement

**USO pitfalls** — protect **dorsal sensory branch of ulnar n.**; **dorsal plate → ECU tendinitis**

**USO contraindications** — **DRUJ arthrosis** · **reverse oblique DRUJ inclination** (↑ radioulnar contact)\
✗ Wrong options: "results encouraging with DRUJ degeneration"

**Wafer** — arthroscopic removal of **2–4 mm** under TFCC; variance **<4 mm**

| At 1 y | USO vs wafer |
|---|---|
| Outcome scores, grip, ROM, pain | **Equal** |
| **Reoperation / complications** | **↑ USO** (symptomatic hardware) |
| Return to work | **Wafer faster** |

### DRUJ arthritis – hemiresection arthroplasty

| Procedure | Indication / key fact |
|---|---|
| **Hemiresection arthroplasty** | Post-traumatic DRUJ arthritis; **needs intact or reconstructed TFCC** (prevents ulnar instability); **heavy laborer** |
| **Sauvé-Kapandji** | DRUJ fusion + proximal ulnar **pseudarthrosis**; **manual laborers**; preserves ulnocarpal support |
| **Darrach** (ulnar head resection) | **Low-demand / elderly**; risk **painful proximal stump instability** |
| Ulnar head replacement | Severe arthrosis; **salvage failed Darrach** |

**Laborer with DRUJ arthritis + TFCC tear** — **hemiresection + TFCC repair/reconstruction**\
✗ Wrong options: Darrach · complete head resection · isolated TFCC reconstruction · styloid repair

## How it's asked

**"MMA puncher, positive variance after adolescent DRF — best treatment?"** → **distal ulnar (shortening) osteotomy** (TO)

**"USO most indicated for?"** → **young, ulnar positive + DRUJ incongruity**\
✗ Wrong options: neutral/negative · elderly with DRUJ arthritis

**"USO — all true EXCEPT"** → **good results despite DRUJ arthritis**

**"USO vs wafer at 1 y?"** → **↑ reoperation with USO**\
✗ Wrong options: better ROM/grip · earlier RTW

**"Construction worker, DRUJ arthritis + TFCC tear?"** → **hemiresection arthroplasty + TFCC repair**

**"Fused lunate–triquetrum on X-ray?"** → **benign variant, no sequelae**

**"Load through ulna with +2 mm variance?"** → **40%**
