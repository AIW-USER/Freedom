# Quadriga Effect

*Hand · 71 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Pathoanatomy

**Definition** — **active flexion lag of fingers adjacent** to a digit with injured/repaired **FDP**; injured finger reaches palm

**Common belly** — FDP **long, ring, little** share a common muscle belly + distal cross-connections (forearm, carpal tunnel sheaths) → excursion = shortest tendon (TO)

**Index FDP** — often **independent belly** → can be shortened without quadriga

**FDS** — **separate muscle bellies** → FDS repair/advancement **no quadriga**

**Name** — reins of 4 chariot horses: one tight rein → slack in the others

**Zone** — most quadriga injuries **zone I**

## 2 Mechanism

### FDP over-advancement or tethering – quadriga

**Causes** — **FDP over-advancement/over-tensioning** at repair · **adhesions** (Neu: mostly after crush) · FDP **retraction** · **"over-the-top" FDP repair / flexor–extensor tenodesis after DIP disarticulation** · too-short tendon graft

**Threshold** — **>1 cm** advancement (Orthobullets; Malerich: 1 cm tolerable, **1.5 cm → ulnar 3 digits lose 40° flexion**) · OB7866 option states **>1.5 cm** → risk of quadriga — read the options

**FDP avulsion reinserted (button) despite proximal migration** → tight, adhesed, improperly tensioned FDP → **quadriga** (TO)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — can't close fingers tightly (e.g. over cricket ball, TO); **weak grip**

**Exam** — fist: **fingers adjacent to injured digit don't reach palm**; **full passive motion**; ↓ grip

**Radiographs** — usually not required

## 5 Management

```
Quadriga
├─ Mild, QoL unaffected → OBSERVATION
└─ Severe, function-limiting → RELEASE FDP of injured digit
       (or tendon lengthening / tenolysis / release of intertendinous connections)
```

**FDP release** — 85% resolution (Neu)

**Prevention** — don't advance FDP **>1 cm**

## X vs Y

| | **Quadriga** | **Lumbrical plus** |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | FDP **too short/tethered** (over-advanced, tenodesed, adhesions) | FDP **lax/detached** (retracts after amputation at/proximal to DIP, long graft) |
| Finding | Injured finger reaches palm; **adjacent fingers lag** | **Paradoxical PIP/IP extension** on attempted fist |

| Distractor | Why wrong |
|---|---|
| Elson test (DIP hyperextends with PIP 90°) | Central slip injury |
| Can't actively extend MCP but holds it once placed | Sagittal band rupture |
| Intrinsic tightness | ↓ PIP flexion with MCP extended |
| AIN injury | Thumb IP + index DIP flexion lost |
| Ulnar nerve injury | Intrinsic minus / claw ulnar 2 digits |
| FDP repair rupture | Affected finger can't flex, not adjacent |

## How it's asked

**"FDP avulsion reinserted despite proximal migration, can't grip cricket ball at 6 mo?"** → **quadriga effect** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: lumbrical plus · swan neck · intrinsic tightness

**"Ring FDP repaired, long & small can't flex fully — intraoperative cause?"** → **overtensioning of FDP**

**"Shortened 12 mm, adjacent fingers can't reach palm, full passive motion — factor?"** → **common flexor muscle belly**

**"Injured finger flexes fully, others don't — which injury?"** → **FDP laceration with repair**\
✗ Wrong options: FDS repair · nail bed · P1 fracture · dorsal MCP dislocation

**"Shortening that causes it?"** → **2 cm**\
✗ Wrong options: 0.5 cm

**"FDP advanced to stump after DIP amputation; only ring tip reaches palm?"** → **quadriga**\
✗ Wrong options: lumbrical plus

**"Flexor–extensor tenodesis at revision amputation → 3-mo finding?"** → **can't make full fist, adjacent fingers ↓ ROM**

**"True about FDP avulsion?"** → **advancement >1.5 cm risks quadriga**\
✗ Wrong options: timing irrelevant to retraction · chronic → tenodese FDP to FDS · repair method irrelevant

**"Treatment of symptomatic quadriga?"** → **release FDP of injured digit**
