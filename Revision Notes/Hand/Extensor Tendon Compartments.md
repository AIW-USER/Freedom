# Extensor Tendon Compartments

*Hand · 27 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Dorsal compartment contents

| Dorsal wrist compartment | Contents |
|---|---|
| **1** | **APL, EPB** |
| **2** | **ECRL, ECRB** |
| **3** | **EPL** only (✗ Wrong options: ECRL) |
| **4** | **EDC, EIP, PIN** (floor) |
| **5** | **EDM** |
| **6** | **ECU** |

![Six dorsal wrist compartments](../../MCQ/attachments/q3522-illa-extensor-cpts2.jpg)

**PIN** — **only nerve** in the dorsal compartments; consistently at **base (floor) of 4th**

**Septa** — 1/2 radial surface thickest; **3/4 septum thinnest**; **5th compartment lowest resistance to failure** (Iwamoto)

**Extensor retinaculum** — check rein against **bowstringing** → **repair** at surgery (Palmer)

**PIN ± AIN block** — PIN alone: **1 cm ulnar to proximal Lister's tubercle**; PIN + AIN single injection: 1 cm ulnar + 3 cm proximal to Lister's (Grutter, 100% accurate)

### Number of compartments (hand)

**Hand compartments (TO)** — **10**

| Hand compartment (TO) | Number |
|---|---|
| Dorsal interossei | 4 |
| Palmar (volar) interossei | 3 |
| Thenar + hypothenar | 2 |
| Adductor pollicis | 1 |
| **Total** | **10** |

**11th (TO)** — some authors add **mid-palmar** compartment

## How it's asked

**"Compartment incorrectly paired with contents?"** → **3rd: EPL + ECRL** (ECRL is in 2nd)

**"Only nerve in dorsal compartments?"** → **PIN, floor of 4th**

**"How many compartments in the hand?"** → **10** (TO)
