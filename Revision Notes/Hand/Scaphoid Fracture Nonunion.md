# Scaphoid Fracture Nonunion

*Hand · 79 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & risk factors

**Incidence** — **5–10% after immobilisation** (Orthobullets) · "up to 10%" (OB) · **~25% after surgical fixation** (some studies)

**Demographics** — ♂:♀ **2:1**; 3rd decade

**Risk factors** — **proximal pole** fracture · **vertical oblique** pattern · **displacement >1 mm** · age · **nicotine** · **delayed diagnosis/treatment**

**Why prone** — retrograde supply, limited cancellous bone, few soft-tissue attachments, delayed Dx

**Missed fracture** — fracture line may need **1–2 wk** to appear on X-ray

### Anatomy – blood supply

**Shape** — boat / twisted peanut; oblique to long axis; **largest proximal-row bone**; **>75% articular cartilage**

**Articulations** — radius, lunate, trapezium, trapezoid, capitate

| Vessel | Entry | Supplies |
|---|---|---|
| **Dorsal carpal branch of radial a.** (major) | Dorsal non-articular ridge | **Proximal 80%, retrograde** |
| Superficial palmar arch (volar radial a.) | Distal tubercle | Distal 20% |

**AVN rate** — **15–50%** of scaphoid fractures; ↑ proximally; **proximal 1/5 → ~100%**

**AVN site** — **proximal** pole\
✗ Wrong options: distal

![Scaphoid blood supply](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422868-illustration-of-scaphoid-blood-supply.jpg)

## 2 Mechanism

**Humpback deformity** — proximal pole **extends** with lunate (SL ligament) · distal pole **flexes** (scaphotrapezial lig.) · hinge = **volar radioscaphocapitate ligament**

**Natural history** — untreated → carpal derangement, pain, cartilage loss → **SNAC**

## 3 Presentation

**History** — remote fall / "sprain"; get prior op notes & imaging

**Symptoms** — wrist pain with motion, ↓ grip; some asymptomatic

**Exam** — swelling, **snuffbox / fracture-site tenderness**, ↓ ROM

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Fact |
|---|---|
| X-ray | PA, lateral, 45° semi-pronated oblique, **scaphoid view**; cysts, sclerosis, resorption, hardware failure, **humpback on lateral**, SNAC |
| **CT** | **Best to evaluate nonunion & plan surgery**; **1 mm cuts in scaphoid plane**; gap, angulation, prior technical error, SNAC cysts |
| MRI | **Osteonecrosis**: ↓ T1 signal **proximal fragment** (different signal between fragments); inconsistent utility, gadolinium helps · distal-fragment oedema also dark on T1 → compare FS images (TO) |
| **Perfusion (dynamic contrast) MRI** (TO) | **Zero proximal-pole enhancement = necrosis** → dead pole not simply fixed · viable pole → freshen + compression screw |
| CT vs MRI (TO) | Weeks-old fracture with pain: **CT for union, MRI for viability** |

**Intra-op punctate bleeding** — **highly predictive** of vascular proximal pole; absent → vascularised graft

![Scaphoid view](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422868-scaphoidview.jpg)

### Diagnosis – nonunion, humpback deformity & proximal pole osteonecrosis

**Diagnosis** — history + exam + X-ray + **CT**; MRI for AVN

| MRI picture | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| Fracture line + **different signal between fragments** | **Scaphoid nonunion + osteonecrosis** |
| Most/all of scaphoid necrotic | **Preiser's** |
| Lunate | Kienböck |
| Fluid area with dense rim | Intraosseous ganglion |
| SL ligament injury, widened interval | SL dissociation |

**Sequela of nonop Rx** — **humpback deformity**\
✗ Wrong options: distal pole AVN · capitolunate OA = SLAC III · radiolunate OA

![Humpback deformity](../../MCQ/attachments/q211952-illa-humpback-deformity.jpg)

### Classification – stability

| Type | Features |
|---|---|
| **Stable** | Length & alignment kept, **fibrous union** |
| **Unstable** | Loss of length/alignment, carpal instability or chondral change |

## 5 Management

```
Scaphoid nonunion
├─ No AVN, no SNAC → ORIF + non-vascularised graft + screw
│      ├─ no humpback → inlay (Russe)
│      └─ humpback / carpal collapse → interposition wedge (Fisk), VOLAR approach
├─ Proximal pole AVN / no punctate bleeding, no pancarpal OA → vascularised graft
│      ├─ pedicled distal radius (1,2-ICSRA dorsal · Mathoulin volar · Sotereanos)
│      └─ free MFC (volar, humpback) · MFT if cartilage lost · iliac crest
└─ Advanced SNAC / arthritis → salvage (PRC, 4-corner / scaphoid excision + intercarpal fusion, arthrodesis)
```

### Operative criteria

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| No union by **6 mo**, technical error (screw position, implant failure, distraction) | **ORIF + graft** |
| Inadequate prior immobilisation / refuses surgery | Cast (up to 6 mo) ± **PEMF** — 69% union by 3 mo (fixed, no error) |
| Technical error / displacement | **no cast** |

### Without osteonecrosis – ORIF with non-vascularised graft (volar approach for humpback)

**Standard** — **ORIF + autologous bone graft**; union **92%**; best when failure = simple technical error\
✗ Wrong options: cast · arthroscopy · 4CF / arthrodesis in young w/o SNAC

**Humpback, no AVN, no prior surgery** — **volar approach + standard (non-vascularised) graft + screw**\
✗ Wrong options: K-wire · VBG · dorsal

**Volar advantages** — corrects humpback (K-wire joysticks), grafts volar defect, **preserves dorsal blood supply**

**Dorsal approach** — most acute waist fractures; proximal pole visualisation; **↑ devascularisation risk**

**Screw direction** — volar: **distal → proximal** · dorsal: proximal → distal; plate = volar buttress; K-wire removable

**Casting nonunion** — 4–6 mo, high failure; thumb spica / above-elbow **does not improve union**

| Graft | Type | Indication | Union |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Russe (inlay)** | Non-vasc corticocancellous, volar (FCR–radial a.) | **No collapse/humpback** | **92%** |
| **Fisk (interposition wedge)** | Non-vasc, distal radius (Fernandez: iliac crest) | **Humpback / carpal collapse** | 72–95% |
| BMP / PRP | Adjunct | No SNAC; avoids free flap | High (series) |

### Proximal pole AVN – vascularised (MFC) graft

**Indications (VBG)** — waist nonunion + **proximal pole AVN** · **no punctate bleeding** · not with pancarpal OA / collapse · failed non-vasc graft

**Relative contraindication** — no intact cartilage shell · advanced collapse with OA (e.g. SNAC + scaphocapitate arthrosis → arthrodesis)

| Graft | Pedicle / approach | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **1,2-ICSRA (Zaidemberg)** | Branch of **radial a.**, **dorsal** (1st–2nd compartments) | **71% nonunion / 50% AVN**; ↑ failure with DISI/humpback |
| Mathoulin | **Volar carpal a.**, volar | Wedge for humpback |
| Sotereanos | Artery of **4th dorsal compartment**, dorsal capsular pedicle | Inlay |
| Local VBG overall | — | 82% good/excellent |
| **Free MFC** | **Descending genicular a.** (longitudinal br., from **SFA**) or superomedial genicular; **volar**, anastomosis to **radial a.** | **100% by 13 wk** (1 study) |
| **Free MFT** | Osteochondral; DGA periosteal branches | AVN + **cartilage loss** of proximal pole; >90% |
| Free iliac crest | **Deep circumflex iliac a.** | 76% |

**AVN + no bleeding + collapse** — **free vascularised MFC via volar approach**\
✗ Wrong options: non-vasc · 1,2-ICSRA dorsal = inferior union

**VBG vs non-vasc — conflict** — **88% vs 47%** union with proximal pole AVN (OB8865) · **no difference** in type/source on meta-analysis for waist nonunion (OB219567) · no clear superiority (Orthobullets) — read the options

**Graft source** — no single source superior (iliac crest = distal radius); young patients **85–100%** union

**MFC uses** — **proximal scaphoid AVN / nonunion**\
✗ Wrong options: lunate OCD · acute humpback · SNAC with arthrosis · Kienböck stage I (4+5 ECA there)

## 6 Complications

**Osteonecrosis** — proximal fractures

**Graft failure → SNAC** — radioscaphoid (styloid) → capitolunate arthrosis

![SNAC wrist](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422868-snac-moved.jpg)

## X vs Y

| | **Russe** | **Fisk** | **1,2-ICSRA** | **Free MFC** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vascularised | No | No | ✓ pedicled | ✓ free |
| Approach | Volar | Volar | **Dorsal** | **Volar** |
| Humpback correction | No | **Yes** | Poor | **Yes** |
| Proximal pole AVN | No | No | Yes (inferior) | **✓ best** |

## How it's asked

**"MRI 18 mo after fall — fracture line, different fragment signal"** → **scaphoid nonunion + osteonecrosis**\
✗ Wrong options: Preiser, Kienböck

**"Radiographic sequela of nonop Rx"** → **humpback deformity**

**"Waist nonunion 6 mo, young"** → **ORIF + autologous bone graft**

**"Humpback, no AVN — best surgery"** → **volar, standard graft, screw**

**"Army medic, nonunion >1 y, no SNAC"** → **ORIF + bone graft**\
✗ Wrong options: cast, PRC, fusion

**"Delayed Dx humpback on CT"** → **ORIF single screw, volar approach**\
✗ Wrong options: dorsal · cast · 4CF

**"MFC VBG + fixation best for"** → **proximal scaphoid AVN**

**"AVN, no bleeding at surgery, collapse"** → **free vascularised femoral graft, volar approach**

**"Waist nonunion with AVN — true statement"** → **vascularised = non-vascularised union rates**\
✗ Wrong options: dorsal corrects humpback · iliac crest superior
