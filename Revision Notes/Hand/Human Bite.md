# Human Bite

*Hand · 41 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Frequency** — ~2% of bite wounds; **3rd after dog & cat**; ♂

**Location** — **dorsal 3rd/4th MCP** = **"fight bite"**

### Pathoanatomy

**Mechanism** — clenched fist → tooth → **MCP capsule penetrated** → fist opens → bacteria **trapped under extensor tendon/capsule**

**Also** — direct bite (child biting child)

**Associated** — **extensor tendon laceration** (missed — proximal retraction)

### Microbiology – polymicrobial & Eikenella

**Most common isolate** — **polymicrobial** (~4 isolates/culture; saliva >600 species)

**Commonest single organisms** — **α-haemolytic strep (S. viridans)** + **S. aureus** (S. aureus = overall most common pathogen)

**Eikenella corrodens** — **facultative anaerobic gram-neg rod**; **characteristic of human bites** (rare in dog/cat); 7–29% (Orthobullets) · 17–30% / ~⅓ (OB)

**Eikenella resistance** — **aminoglycosides, 1st-gen cephalosporins, clindamycin**

| Bite | Infection rate | Signature organism |
|---|---|---|
| Dog | 5–15% | Pasteurella canis, Capnocytophaga |
| Cat | **30–50%** | Pasteurella multocida |
| **Human** | High | **Eikenella corrodens** |

## 3 Presentation

**History** — punch to mouth, often **concealed** → treat as fight bite until proven otherwise; delayed presentation

**Exam** — small **transverse, irregular** dorsal MCP wound; erythema, warmth, ± pus; pain on passive MCP ROM; **check extensor function**; volar side & NV usually spared

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **tooth fragment (FB)** + fracture (MC head)

**Culture** — not in ED (contaminated); **deep aerobic + anaerobic in OR**

## 5 Management

```
Fight bite
├─ All fight bites / joint or sheath involved → I&D + IV AMPICILLIN-SULBACTAM
│      └─ discharge → PO AMOX-CLAVULANATE 5-7 d
└─ PIP joint bite → SERIAL DEBRIDEMENT + prolonged Abx
```

**I&D** — debride wound + **joint capsule**; **leave open**; Gram stain + culture

**MCP approach** — **tendon-splitting** (Shewring)

**IV Abx** — cover **Staph, Strep, gram-neg** → **ampicillin/sulbactam**; alternatives: cephalosporin, gentamicin + penicillinase-resistant penicillin, **penicillin + metronidazole**

**Outcome** — MCP injuries satisfactory/good; **PIP injuries → 42% poor**

## X vs Y

| | Human bite | Dog / cat bite |
|---|---|---|
| Characteristic organism | **Eikenella corrodens** | **Pasteurella** |
| Also | S. viridans, S. aureus, anaerobes | Capnocytophaga (dog), Corynebacterium, Moraxella |
| Typical site | Dorsal MCP (fist) | Any (cat → joints/sheath) |
| Oral Abx | Amox-clav | Amox-clav |

## How it's asked

**"Fight bite — most common culture result?"** → **polymicrobial**\
✗ Wrong options: Eikenella alone · Pasteurella

**"Organism more frequent in human than animal bites?"** → **Eikenella corrodens**\
✗ Wrong options: Capnocytophaga, P. canis, S. aureus

**"IV antibiotic for fight bite?"** → **ampicillin-sulbactam**

**"Eikenella resistant to?"** → **clindamycin, 1st-gen cephalosporins, aminoglycosides**
