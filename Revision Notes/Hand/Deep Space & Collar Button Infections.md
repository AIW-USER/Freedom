# Deep Space & Collar Button Infections

*Hand · 20 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Definitions

**Deep space infection** — **thenar** (**most commonly infected**) · hypothenar · **midpalmar** (rare)

**Collar button abscess** — **web space** abscess between fingers, usually from a **volar puncture**

### Anatomy – deep spaces

| Space | Boundaries |
|---|---|
| **Thenar** | Potential space **palmar to adductor pollicis**, dorsal to flexor tendons; fascial septum from midpalmar |
| **Midpalmar** | **Dorsal and radial to hypothenar space** |
| **Hypothenar** | Palmar to 5th MC, dorsal-radial to hypothenar fascia, ulnar to hypothenar septum |

![Deep spaces of the hand](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6106-deep-spaces.jpg)

### Ulnar & radial bursae communications

**Ulnar bursa (TO)** — proximal continuation of **little finger (5th) sheath**

**Radial bursa (TO)** — proximal continuation of **thumb (FPL) sheath**

**Extent (TO)** — **2–3 cm above wrist**

**Communications (TO)** — ulnar ↔ **radial bursa** · ± **Parona's (forearm) space** · not **4th (ring) finger sheath**

## 2 Mechanism

**Collar button spread** — volar puncture → **dorsal through sinus in superficial transverse metacarpal ligament**; proximal spread **blocked by palmar aponeurosis**

**Organisms** — Staph & Strep; **CA-MRSA ~30%** of surgically treated hand infections, ↑ yearly; only independent risk factor **IV drug use**

## 3 Presentation

**History** — ± penetrating trauma; pain, swelling

| Space | Pain on flexion of |
|---|---|
| Thenar | **Thumb** |
| Hypothenar | **Small finger** |
| Midpalmar | **Middle, ring, small** |

**Thenar & midpalmar** — **loss of palmar concavity**

**Collar button** — web-space tenderness + **abduction of adjacent digits (V-sign)**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — if **foreign body** suspected

**MRI** — defines **extent** of infection

## 5 Management

```
Deep space / collar button infection
└─ I&D + IV antibiotics (standard of care)
       └─ Collar button → DORSAL + VOLAR longitudinal incisions, avoid web skin ± Penrose drain
```

### Collar-button abscess drainage

**Incisions** — **two (dorsal + palmar)**, **avoid web space** → prevents **scar contracture & loss of abduction**

(✗ Wrong options: **Traps** — Brunner incisions) · incision across web space · oral antibiotics alone

## How it's asked

**"Child, palmar abrasion, web space swelling, digits abducted — incisions?"** → **dorsal + palmar, avoiding web**\
✗ Wrong options: Brunner · web incision

**"Ulnar bursa does NOT communicate with"** → **4th finger sheath** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: 5th sheath, Parona, radial bursa

**"Most commonly infected deep space"** → **thenar**

**"Pain with middle/ring/small flexion + loss of concavity"** → **midpalmar space**
