# Blood Supply to Hand

*Hand · 7 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Source arteries

**Radial artery** — between **brachioradialis & FCR** → dorsum via **snuffbox** (between FCR and APL/EPB) → gives **superficial palmar branch** → dives between **2 heads of 1st dorsal interosseous** → **deep palmar arch**

**Ulnar artery** — under FCU; **lateral (radial) to ulnar nerve** at wrist → **Guyon's canal** → lies on TCL

**Supplemental** — anterior interosseous, posterior interosseous, median artery (occasional)

### Vascular anatomy – deep palmar arch & digital neurovascular relationship

| | **Superficial arch** | **Deep arch** |
|---|---|---|
| Main supply | **Ulnar artery** | **Deep (terminal) branch of radial artery** |
| Minor supply | Superficial palmar br. of radial a. | Deep palmar br. of ulnar a. |
| Depth | Deep to palmar fascia | **Deep to flexor tendons**, on MC bases & interossei, under oblique adductor pollicis |
| Position | **Distal** | **Proximal** (1 fingerbreadth proximal to superficial) |
| Surface marking | Line across palm from distal edge of fully abducted thumb | 1 fingerbreadth proximal to that line |
| From Kaplan's cardinal line | 15 mm distal | 7 mm distal |
| From distal wrist crease | 50 mm distal | 40 mm distal |
| Complete | **80%** | **97%** |
| Direct digital branch | **Ulnar digital a. of little finger** | **Radial digital a. of index** |
| Other branches | Deep branch (to deep arch), **common palmar digital aa.** | **Princeps pollicis** (between 1st DI & adductor pollicis), common digital aa. to 2nd–4th webs |

**Deep arch main contributor** — **deep dorsal (terminal) branch of radial a.**\
✗ Wrong options: superficial palmar br. radial · princeps pollicis · ulnar a.

**Wrist course of radial a.** — between radial collateral ligament & 1st dorsal compartment → between heads of 1st DI → anastomoses with deep palmar br. of ulnar

### Digital arteries

**Origin** — common digital aa. from **superficial arch** → divide into proper digital at web spaces → dorsal branches distal to PIP

**Palm** — digital artery **volar** to nerve

**Finger** — **artery DORSAL to nerve** (nerve palmar: pads feel, nail refill)\
✗ Wrong options: artery palmar · nerve dorsal

**Ligament relations** — NV bundle **volar to Cleland's** (not in Dupuytren's) · Grayson's volar (involved in Dupuytren's)

![Digital artery dorsal to digital nerve in the finger](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6007-digital-artery-and-nerve.jpg)

|---|---|
| Rule | **Side closer to midline (median side)** |
| Index | **Ulnar** |
| Little | **Radial** |
| Middle / ring | Ulnar / radial (less obvious) |

### Dorsal arteries & veins

**Dorsal carpal arch** — from posterior interosseous + dorsal perforating br. of anterior interosseous → **dorsal metacarpal aa.** (flaps; 1st & 2nd most consistent)

**Veins** — deep = **venae comitantes**; superficial dorsal → basilic & cephalic

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Allen test

**Purpose** — patency of collateral (dual) circulation before **radial artery cannulation/harvest** (thrombosis risk) (TO)

**Positive Allen (TO)** — colour returns quickly = **adequate dual supply** → **indication** for cannulation

**Negative Allen (TO)** — pallor persists = **no patent dual supply** → **contraindication** to radial cannulation

| | **Original Allen (TO)** | **Modified Allen (TO)** |
|---|---|---|
| Hands | **Both hands**, one artery at a time | **One hand** at a time |
| Method | Clench both fists 1 min, compress **both radial aa.** simultaneously, open → repeat with ulnar | Raise hand, clench 30 s, compress **radial + ulnar together**, open (blanched) → **release ulnar** |
| Normal | Pale → pink quickly | Pink **within 6 s** |

## How it's asked

**"Main contributor to deep palmar arch, between heads of 1st DI?"** → **deep dorsal (terminal) branch of radial artery**

**"Main supply of superficial arch?"** → **ulnar artery**

**"Artery–nerve relation in the finger?"** → **artery dorsal to nerve**

**"True about Allen test?"** → **negative = no patent dual supply** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: positive is a contraindication · original compresses radial + ulnar of one hand · modified compresses them one by one

**"Dominant digital artery of index?"** → **ulnar**

**"Which arch is more complete?"** → **deep (97%)** vs superficial 80%
