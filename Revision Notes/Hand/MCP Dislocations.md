# MCP Dislocations

*Hand · 55 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — rare, **<1/100,000/y**; less common than IPJ dislocations

**Direction** — **dorsal** most common

**Digit** — **index** > **thumb**

**Associated** — base P1 / MC head fractures in up to **50%**

### Anatomy

**Osteology** — condyloid; **cam-shaped MC head**; shallow concave P1 base; sesamoids in volar plate (thumb: FPB, APB)

**Proper collateral** — **primary stabiliser**; dorsal MC head → volar P1 base; **tight in flexion**

**Accessory collateral** — volar to proper → volar plate; **tight in extension**

**Volar plate** — thick fibrocartilage distally, membranous proximally; **A1 pulley attaches to it**; attaches to **deep** transverse MC ligament\
✗ Wrong options: superficial

**Dorsal capsule** — weak, thin, loose

**Sagittal bands** — keep extensor central during MCP flexion\
✗ Wrong options: interposed

## 2 Mechanism

**Dorsal** — FOOSH → **hyperextension** → **volar plate avulsed from MC neck/head**\
✗ Wrong options: hyperflexion · rotation

**Volar** — hyperextension or hyperflexion

## 3 Presentation

| | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Simple dorsal** | **P1 hyperextended** on MC head, **PIP flexed** |
| **Complex dorsal** | **Bayonet** P1 (dorsal to MC shaft), **skin dimpling proximal palmar crease**, palpable MC head in palm, digit in extension deviated toward central digits, can't flex MCP |
| **Volar** | **Extensor lag**, dorsal skin depression proximal to P1 base |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP, **lateral (best view)**, oblique

**MCP volar plate (TO)** — attached to **P1, not the metacarpal** → displaced dorsally · collaterals taut in flexion · **tendon noose round MC neck** blocks reduction · complex: P1 **not markedly hyperextended**

**Complex signs** — **joint space widening** (volar plate interposed) · **sesamoid in joint = diagnostic of complex**

## 5 Management

```
MCP dislocation
├─ Simple → CLOSED REDUCTION
│      └─ fails / complex / delayed → OPEN REDUCTION
└─ Complex (sesamoid in joint, widening, dimpling) → OPEN REDUCTION (dorsal approach, split volar plate)
```

### Complex dorsal MCP dislocation – volar plate interposition & reduction manoeuvre

**Commonest block** — **volar plate**\
✗ Wrong options: flexor tendon · extensor · lumbrical · collaterals · 1st DI · natatory lig.

**Cadaver (Afifi)** — only **volar plate division** allows reduction; flexors move ulnar, lumbricals radial, radial digital nerve superficial

**Dorsal reduction manoeuvre** — **wrist flexion** (relax intrinsic & extrinsic flexors) + **direct dorsal-to-volar (distal & volar) pressure on P1 base** (same for thumb, index, small)

**Avoid longitudinal traction** — pulls volar plate into joint → **converts simple → irreducible complex**\
✗ Wrong options: wrist extension tightens noose

**Traction source conflict** — avoid traction (Orthobullets, OB) · "gentle" traction with dorsal pressure (OB219826) — read the options

**Volar reduction** — pressure on volar P1 with MCP flexed → immobilise **30° flexion 2 wk** → AROM in dorsal blocking splint

**After dorsal reduction** — early ROM + dorsal blocking splint

**Noose (dorsal complex)**

| Digit | Radial side | Ulnar side |
|---|---|---|
| **Index** | **Lumbrical** | **Flexor tendons** |
| Thumb | Intrinsics | **FPL** |
| Small | Flexors + lumbrical | ADM + FDM |

![Index noose: lumbrical and flexor tendon](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6115-buttonhole.jpg)

**Volar dislocation blocks** — **dorsal capsule**, avulsed volar plate / collateral ligament, **juncturae tendinum (4th–5th EDC) in small finger**, **1st DI in thumb**

| Open approach | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Dorsal** (midline, split extensor/capsule; thumb EPL–EPB interval; **split volar plate longitudinally**) | **↓ NV injury**, easy **MC head fracture fixation**, 14 min shorter, no 2nd approach | — |
| Volar (oblique, skin only; release **A1**) | Better volar plate access | **NV bundle at risk** (radial digital n. tented), hard osteochondral fixation; 42% needed 2nd dorsal approach |

### CMC fracture-dislocations – fixation

**Mechanism** — axial load (punch), hyperflexion; ~1% of hand/wrist injuries; often **5th ± 4th**, dorsal **hamate fracture**

| Scenario | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **All 5 CMC fracture-dislocations** | **Closed vs open reduction + internal fixation** (✗ Wrong options: cast · splint) |
| Open contaminated | External fixation |
| **Acute 4th CMC fracture-dislocation** | **CRPP** (✗ Wrong options: IM fixation · fusion · splint) |
| **Subacute >2 wk, still dislocated** | **Open reduction + fixation** (scar blocks closed reduction) |
| Acute (<3 d) isolated ulnar CMC dislocation | Closed reduction + splint can work (Zhang, Storken) |
| Severely comminuted MC base | CMC arthrodesis |
| Chronic painful | Fusion / arthroplasty |

**4th CMC fracture-dislocation** — **contraindicates 5th CMC suspension arthroplasty** (ECU slip)

## 6 Complications

**Stiffness** — injury trauma, prolonged immobilisation

**Post-traumatic arthritis / osteonecrosis** — repeated closed attempts, prolonged dislocation, traumatic open reduction

**Premature physeal closure** — rare

## How it's asked

**"Irreducible dorsal index MCP — interposed structure?"** → **volar plate**

**"Mechanism + cause of failed closed reduction?"** → **hyperextension → volar plate entrapped dorsal to MC head**\
✗ Wrong options: flexor–lumbrical buttonhole

**"Simple dorsal MCP subluxation — next?"** → **wrist flexion + distal/volar pressure on P1**\
✗ Wrong options: axial traction · surgery first

**"Reduction manoeuvre for 5th MCP / thumb MCP dorsal dislocation?"** → **dorsal pressure on P1 with wrist flexed**\
✗ Wrong options: wrist extension · longitudinal traction

**"True of interposed structure?"** → **A1 pulley attaches to it**\
✗ Wrong options: prevents bowstringing (A2/A4) · superficial TML · midline tracking (sagittal band)

**"Cross-section — which structure blocks?"** → **volar plate**\
✗ Wrong options: DTML, sagittal band, proper collateral, dorsal capsule

**"Fracture-dislocation of all 5 CMC joints?"** → **closed vs open reduction + internal fixation**

**"4th/5th CMC dislocation seen at 3 wk?"** → **open reduction + fixation**

**"Acute 4th CMC fracture-dislocation?"** → **CRPP**
