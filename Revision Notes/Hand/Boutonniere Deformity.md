# Boutonniere Deformity

*Hand · 8 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Anatomy – extensor mechanism

**Central slip** — central part of extensor hood → inserts **base of middle phalanx** → **extends PIP**

**Lateral bands** — deep head dorsal interossei + volar interossei → insert **base of distal phalanx** → **extend DIP**

**Lumbricals** — origin **FDP** → insert on **lateral bands**

**Triangular ligament** — spans the 2 lateral bands dorsally → **prevents volar subluxation**

**Transverse retinacular ligament** — **prevents dorsal subluxation** of lateral bands

### Epidemiology

**Rheumatoid** — up to **50% of RA** patients → boutonniere in ≥1 digit

**Definition** — **Zone III** extensor injury → **PIP flexion + DIP hyperextension** ("buttonhole": PP head buttons through extensor)

## 2 Mechanism

### Pathophysiology – central slip injury & lateral band relocation

**Causes of central slip failure** — **laceration** · **traumatic avulsion (jammed finger)** · **PIP synovitis / capsular distension (RA)**

**Primary trigger (TO)** — **attenuated central slip + volar displacement of lateral bands**\
✗ Wrong options: dorsal subluxation of lateral bands · ORL shortening · volar plate rupture

**Chronic injury to** — **central slip** of extensor tendon\
✗ Wrong options: terminal tendon → mallet · sagittal band → MCP extensor subluxation · volar plate → swan neck · FDP insertion → jersey

| Step | Event | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Central slip rupture/attenuation** | EDC extension of PIP lost |
| 2 | **Triangular ligament attenuates** | Lateral bands free to slide volarly |
| 3 | **Lateral bands drop volar to PIP axis** | Become **PIP flexors** (TO); all extensor force → **DIP hyperextension** |

![Boutonniere pathoanatomy](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6012-lineage-medical-500-moved.jpg)

**Lumbricals** — unopposed pull through lateral bands → flex PIP + extend DIP

**Pseudo-boutonniere** — PIP flexion contracture **without DIP extension**

## 3 Presentation

**Deformity** — **PIP flexion + DIP hyperextension**; RA patient or jammed/lacerated finger

![Boutonniere clinical](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6012-clinical-photo-surgery-missouri-moved.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnostic test – Elson

**Elson test** — **most reliable** for central slip injury **before deformity evident**

**Technique** — PIP 90° over table edge → extend middle phalanx against resistance

**Positive** — **weak PIP extension + rigid DIP** (lateral bands fire)

**Negative (intact slip)** — **DIP floppy**

![Elson test](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6012-elson.jpg)

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **not required** for diagnosis; shows **avulsion fracture** of middle phalanx base → ORIF/repair

### Rheumatoid staging (TO)

| Stage | Findings | Surgery |
|---|---|---|
| **I (mild)** | **Passively correctable** (PIP extends, DIP flexes) | **Dolphin-Nalebuff terminal tenotomy** |
| **II (moderate)** | **Fixed PIP flexion contracture**, joint space preserved | Matev lateral band transfer / central slip reattachment |
| **III (severe)** | Fixed PIP + **radiographic destruction** or severe soft-tissue contracture | **PIP arthrodesis** (most reliable) |

## 5 Management

```
Boutonniere
├─ Acute closed (<4 wk) → PIP extension splint 6 wk
│      └─ then part-time splint 4–6 wk more
├─ Displaced avulsion fx / open wound → primary central slip repair
├─ Chronic, supple (after full PROM) → tenotomy / lateral band relocation / reconstruction
└─ RA stiff arthritic PIP → PIP arthrodesis
```

### Nonoperative

**Splint** — **PIP in full extension 6 wk** (continuous) + part-time 4–6 wk; **acute closed <4 wk**

**In splint** — **active DIP flexion/extension** → prevents **ORL contracture**

**Traumatic vs RA (TO)** — traumatic responds well to splinting; **RA tissue "sick" → splinting less successful**, progresses

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Primary central slip repair** | **Acute displaced avulsion fx** · open wound needing I&D (ORIF of attachment fractures successful) |
| **Lateral band dorsal relocation** | ~**40° active flexion contracture with full passive extension** |
| **Terminal tendon tenotomy** (modified Fowler / **Dolphin**) | Chronic, after FROM; PIP flexible but can't flex DIP; **never central slip tenotomy** |
| **Matev** (TO) | One lateral band → central slip; other extends DIP |
| Secondary reconstruction | Tendon graft, Littler, Matev, triangular ligament reconstruction |
| **PIP arthrodesis** | **RA**, painful stiff arthritic PIP; **stage III** (TO) |
| Silastic arthroplasty (TO) | Occasionally ulnar digits; high recurrence |

**PIP fusion angles (TO)** — **ring/small 40–50°**; **index 25–30°**

**RA outcome (TO)** — boutonniere surgery **less predictable than swan-neck**

## X vs Y

| (TO) | **Swan-neck** | **Boutonniere** |
|---|---|---|
| PIP | Hyperextension | **Flexion** |
| DIP | Flexion | **Hyperextension** |
| Primary defect | **Volar plate / FDS** | **Central slip** |
| Lateral bands | Subluxate **dorsally** | Subluxate **volarly** |

| Structure injured | Deformity |
|---|---|
| **Central slip** | **Boutonniere** |
| Terminal extensor tendon | Mallet |
| Sagittal band | Extensor subluxation at MCP |
| Volar plate (chronic) | Swan neck |
| FDP insertion avulsion | Jersey finger |

## How it's asked

**"Primary trigger of boutonniere cascade?"** → **attenuated central slip + volar lateral bands** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: dorsal lateral bands · ORL

**"Surgery relocates lateral bands dorsally — which deformity?"** → **boutonniere**\
✗ Wrong options: swan neck · mallet · jersey · lumbrical plus

**"Chronic injury to which structure → boutonniere?"** → **central slip**\
✗ Wrong options: terminal tendon · sagittal band · volar plate · FDP

**"Detect central slip injury before deformity?"** → **Elson test** (rigid DIP + weak PIP extension)

**"Acute closed central slip injury — treatment?"** → **PIP extension splint 6 wk, DIP free**

**"Stage III RA boutonniere?"** → **PIP arthrodesis** (TO)

**"Tenotomy for chronic boutonniere?"** → **terminal tendon (Dolphin/Fowler)**\
✗ Wrong options: central slip
