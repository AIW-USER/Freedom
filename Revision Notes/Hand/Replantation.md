# Replantation

*Hand · 76 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Epidemiology** — 90% of UL amputations traumatic; **♂:♀ 4:1**; most at **digit** level

**Mechanisms** — **sharp** (best) · blunt · avulsion · crush

**Assess** — timing, level, number of digits, preservation of part, **associated injuries**, PMH; stump: zone of injury, viability, contamination

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Indications & contraindications by level (thumb, multiple digits, zone)

| Indications (primary / "absolute") | Relative indications |
|---|---|
| **Thumb at any level** (even proximal to FPL insertion) | Single digit **distal to FDS insertion (zone I)** — good function |
| **Multiple digits** | Ring avulsion (Urbaniak 2A) |
| **Through palm** | Through/above elbow |
| **Wrist or proximal** (distal forearm/wrist = excellent function) | — |
| **Almost any part in a child** | — |

| Primary contraindications | Relative contraindications |
|---|---|
| Severe vascular disease | **Single digit proximal to FDS insertion (zone II)** |
| **Mangled / crush** | Medically unstable |
| **Segmental / multilevel** | Disabling psychiatric illness |
| Ischaemia **>6 h with large muscle** | Contamination |
| — | Ischaemia **>12 h, no muscle** |

**Zone II single digit** — FDS insertion = mid-middle phalanx = distal border of zone II → **PIP stiffness**, ↓ sensation, FDP–FDS adhesions → patient **bypasses stiff index**, uses long finger

**Zone II exceptions** — **thumb, multiple digits, children**

**Distal limit** — need **~4 mm intact skin proximal to nail fold** for veins; distal to nail fold → vessels bi/trifurcate → do not replant (TO)

**Proximal phalanx level (TO)** — poor PIP motion → do not replant (single digit)

**Ring avulsion (Urbaniak)** — **2A** (vascular only) → repair · **2B** (bone/tendon/nerve) or **3** (degloving) → **amputation**

**Not reasons to refuse** — age, workers' comp, malingering

### Ischaemia time criteria

| Level | Warm | Cold |
|---|---|---|
| **Proximal to carpus** (muscle) | **<6 h** | **<12 h** |
| **Digit** (distal to carpus) | **<12 h** (Orthobullets/OB) · **≥8 h** (TO) | **<24 h** (Orthobullets/OB) · **up to 30 h** (TO) |

**Muscle necrosis (TO)** — irreversible after **6 h warm**; cooling to **4 °C** → 12 h

**Wrist with 8 h warm** — **relative contraindication**

**Forearm with 10 h warm** — do not replant (TO; good only if <6 h)

### Prognostic factors – mechanism & ischaemia time

**Most important factor** — **mechanism of injury** (sharp > crush/avulsion)

**Favourable survival** — **sharp**, **female**, **age <13**, **non-smoker**, warm <12 h / cold <24 h (digit)

**Unfavourable** — **crush**, avulsion, **ribbon sign** (corkscrew artery = intimal avulsion → needs segmental resection + vein graft)

**Level** — DIP–PIP replants good · **PIP–MCP poor** (flexion contracture)

## 5 Management

### Transport of amputated part

**Part** — rinse; wrap in **gauze moist with RL/saline** → **sealed plastic bag** → **ice water**; cool to **~4 °C** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: direct ice · dry ice

**Stump** — moist gauze, compression

### Surgical sequence & major limb replantation

**Shunt first** — proximal replants with large muscle (↓ warm ischaemia)

| Source | Sequence |
|---|---|
| **TO** | **Bone → extensor → flexor → artery → nerve → vein → skin** |
| OB | Bone → extensor → flexor → arteries → nerves → veins (veins may precede nerves) → skin |
| Orthobullets | Bone ± shortening → extensor → artery → veins → flexor → nerve → skin ± fasciotomy |

**Always** — **bone first**; **arteries before veins**; vascular repair = greatest priority (TO); artery right after bone if ischaemia >3–4 h

**Vein grafts** — use **generously**; bone shortening avoids grafts

**Wrist level, ischaemia approaching 6 h (TO)** — **restore arterial flow first** (muscle mass, reperfusion risk) · **cold ischaemia better than warm** (TO)

**Finger order** — **thumb → long → ring → small → index**

**Multiple digits** — **structure-by-structure** (fastest) · **digit-by-digit = longest**

**Palm amputation, digits intact, clean** — **anatomic replantation of hand**\
✗ Wrong options: transpositional (for multidigit loss)

### Ray resection vs replantation – function & palmar width

**Ring finger amputated at proximal phalanx** — **early 4th ray resection + 5th ray transposition** → best cosmesis & grip\
✗ Wrong options: delayed → ↓ grip, pronation, cosmesis · revision amputation

**Transposition** — after **central ray** (long/ring) resection → prevents gap & **scissoring**

**Ray resection** — **↓ palmar width** → ↓ grip; good cosmesis; most **return to work**

| Ray resected | Greatest loss |
|---|---|
| **Long** | **Grip** |
| **Index** | **Pinch** |

**Contaminated avulsion-crush small finger + ribbon sign** — **ray resection**\
✗ Wrong options: replant even in child

### Postoperative monitoring – arterial vs venous insufficiency, leeches & antibiotics

**Environment** — warm room (**80 °F**); avoid caffeine, chocolate, nicotine; hydration; aspirin, dipyridamole, LMW dextran, heparin

**Monitoring** — **skin temperature most reliable**: drop **>2 °C in <1 h** or **<30 °C** · **pulse oximetry <94%**

| | **Arterial insufficiency** | **Venous congestion** |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | **<12 h** post-op | **>12 h** |
| Cause | **Vasospasm → thrombosis** (commonest early failure) | Outflow failure |
| Treatment | **Release bandages**, **dependent position**, heparin, **stellate ganglion block**, early exploration | **Elevate**, **leeches**, heparin pledgets |
| Avoid | **Leeches** | — |

**Leeches** — *Hirudo medicinalis* → **hirudin** (100× heparin) → venous congestion

**Leech infection** — ***Aeromonas hydrophila*** (Gram −ve rod, leech gut) → **ciprofloxacin** (**DNA gyrase inhibitor**) first line · alt **TMP-SMX / 3rd-gen cephalosporin**

### Reperfusion injury – allopurinol

**Mechanism** — **hypoxanthine → xanthine** (xanthine oxidase)

**Agent** — **allopurinol** (xanthine oxidase inhibitor) improves outcome\
✗ Wrong options: tPA, heparin, COX-2, leeches

### WALANT anaesthesia

**WALANT (TO)** — wide-awake local anaesthesia **no tourniquet**; lidocaine/bupivacaine + **epinephrine**; no sedation; **Lalonde (2005)**

**Purpose (TO)** — proposed to **↓ waiting time** for surgery

**Advantages (TO)** — **↓ adhesions** (immediate mobilisation; collagen from day 3) · **intraoperative active testing** (tendon transfer tension) · comorbid patients / tourniquet to avoid

**Incorrect (TO)** — "not suitable for **tendon transfers**" → it **is** suitable for complex procedures

## 6 Complications

**Early failure** — **arterial thrombosis from vasospasm** (<12 h)

**Stiffness** — replanted digit ~**50% total motion**; **tenolysis = commonest secondary surgery** (**47.2%** of secondary procedures)

**Major limb** — **myonecrosis**, **myoglobinuria → renal failure** (fatal), acidosis, **hyperkalaemia** (TO)

**Others** — infection, **cold intolerance**

## How it's asked

**"Best indication — healthy 35-y"** → **wrist-level amputation** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: thumb distal to nail bed · index at PP · forearm 10 h warm

**"Relative contraindication"** → **ring finger through proximal phalanx shaft**

**"Replantation indicated"** → **thumb through proximal phalanx**\
✗ Wrong options: multilevel, crush, Urbaniak 2B, long finger at PP

**"Indications EXCEPT"** → **long finger through proximal phalanx**

**"Absolute indication"** → **thumb proximal to FPL insertion**

**"Absolute indications EXCEPT"** → **single digit distal to FDS** (relative)

**"Index cut proximal to FDS, 15 h cold — why not replant?"** → **local anatomy (zone II stiffness)**\
✗ Wrong options: delay · age

**"Most important factor in outcome"** → **mechanism of injury**

**"Unfavourable for survival"** → **crushed digit**

**"Wrist amputation — true"** → **8 h warm ischaemia = relative contraindication**\
✗ Wrong options: veins before arteries · bone last

**"Repair sequence"** → **bone, extensor, flexor, artery, nerve, vein, skin** (TO)

**"Increases surgical time"** → **digit-by-digit**

**"Clean palm amputation, digits intact"** → **anatomic replantation of hand**

**"Temp drop 33 → 29 °C, SpO₂ 87% — EXCEPT"** → **leeches**

**"Leech prophylaxis MOA"** → **DNA gyrase inhibition** (ciprofloxacin)

**"Inhibits xanthine formation"** → **allopurinol**

**"Ring finger GSW at PP — best grip/cosmesis"** → **early 4th ray resection + 5th ray transposition**

**"Ray resection — true"** → **↓ palmar width**

**"Avulsion-crush small finger, ribbon sign, contaminated"** → **ray resection**

**"WALANT — incorrect"** → **unsuitable for tendon transfers** (TO)
