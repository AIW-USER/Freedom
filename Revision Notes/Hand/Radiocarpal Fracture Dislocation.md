# Radiocarpal Fracture Dislocation

*Hand · 74 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — **total loss of contact between carpus and radius**

**Incidence** — **0.2%** of wrist injuries; ♂ > ♀; high energy

**Direction** — **dorsal > volar**

### Anatomy – radiocarpal ligaments

| Ligament | Attachment (radius) | Restrains |
|---|---|---|
| **Short radiolunate** | **Volar lunate facet** (volar-ulnar "critical corner") | **Volar translation** of carpus (primary) |
| **Radioscaphocapitate (RSC)** | **Radial styloid** | **Ulnar translation** |
| Ulnolunate, ulnotriquetral | Volar TFCC (→ base ulnar styloid) | — |

**Distal radius** — biconcave triangular surface; apex = radial styloid; base = sigmoid notch

![Volar wrist ligaments](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob422863-ligaments-of-the-wrist.jpg)

## 2 Mechanism

**Force** — high-energy shear / **rotation on hyperextended, pronated wrist** (falls from height, MVC)

**Associations** — open #; **radial styloid avulsion (RSC)**; **volar lunate facet # (short RL)**; ulnar styloid #; scaphoid/lunate/trapezium #; **median > ulnar nerve injury**; **irreducible DRUJ (soft-tissue interposition)**; SL/LT dissociation

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — deformity (dorsal/volar), swelling, **pale cold hand** (radial/ulnar artery occlusion common), numbness; note crepitus

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| View | Look for |
|---|---|
| **PA** | Radiolunate alignment (normally **⅔ of lunate on radius**; complete disruption → **ulnar translation**), intercarpal widening, SL/LT dissociation, **broken Gilula arcs** |
| **Lateral** | Dorsal/volar dislocation; loss of lunate–radius colinearity |
| **Teardrop view** | **10° proximal tilt** on lateral → **lunate facet fracture** |
| CT | Comminution, articular depression |
| MRI | SL and LT integrity |

**Differentials** — perilunate/lunate dislocation, distal radius fracture

### Moneim classification

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Radiocarpal fracture-dislocation **without** intercarpal dissociation |
| 2 | **With** intercarpal dissociation |

## 5 Management

```
Radiocarpal fracture-dislocation
├─ Stable after closed reduction / unfit → cast up to 6 wk
└─ Irreducible / unstable after reduction → ORIF + radiocarpal pinning + ligament repair
       └─ still unstable → ex-fix / radiolunate pin 4–6 wk or dorsal bridge plate
```

### Operative technique – three-column fixation

| Column | Fixation |
|---|---|
| Radial (styloid) | K-wire / compression screw / plate; anchor repair if small/ligamentous |
| **Intermediate (lunate facet)** | **Screw or tension-band wire**; **repair short RL + RSC** |
| Ulnar (controversial) | DRUJ injury / persistent instability → ulnar styloid fixation, DRUJ pinning in **mid-supination** |

**Also** — decompress NV structures; volar ± dorsal approach

### Ligament injury – short radiolunate

**Loss of reduction after casting** — unfixed **volar lunate facet fragment** → **short radiolunate ligament** → volar carpal subluxation\
✗ Wrong options: SL · RSC · LT · ulnolunate

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **Acute CTS** | Risk: delay → **CTR** |
| Stiffness | Loss **30–40%** of F/E arc; MUA, hardware removal |
| Post-traumatic OA | Non-anatomic articular reduction |
| Chronic radiocarpal instability | — |
| Late intercarpal disruption | Occult SL/LT injury |

## How it's asked

**"Carpus subluxes volarly after casting — ligament on displaced fragment?"** → **short radiolunate (volar lunate facet)**

**"Ligament restraining ulnar translation?"** → **RSC (radial styloid)**

**"View for lunate facet fracture?"** → **teardrop (10° proximal lateral)**

**"Commoner nerve injury?"** → **median**
