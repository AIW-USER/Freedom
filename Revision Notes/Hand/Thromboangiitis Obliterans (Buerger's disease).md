# Thromboangiitis Obliterans (Buerger's disease)

*Hand · 89 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & epidemiology

**Definition** — **non-atherosclerotic, segmental, inflammatory** disease of **small + medium vessels** of hands and feet

**Demographics** — 12.6/100,000 (US); **♂:♀ 3:1**; **<45 y**

**Risk factors** — **smoking**, chewing tobacco

**Pattern** — **distal vessels first**, proximal last

## 2 Mechanism

| Phase | Histology |
|---|---|
| **Acute** | Thrombus with **neutrophils + giant cells** occludes lumen, **wall spared** |
| Subacute | Progressive thrombus organisation |
| Chronic | Inflammation gone; organised thrombus + vascular fibrosis |

## 3 Presentation

**Early** — **intermittent claudication** (feet, legs, hands, arms), numbness/tingling

**Late** — critical ischaemia → **rest pain**; frequently **painful**\
✗ Wrong options: painless

**Exam** — ulcers, **necrotic distal digits**, large erythematous superficial vessels, cold hands/feet, ↓ pulses, sensory findings up to 70%

**Allen test** — **positive in young smoker with digital ischaemia** → suggestive

![Necrotic digits](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6099-necrotic-fingers.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Arteriography** — **study of choice** if diagnosis uncertain; excludes mimics

**Signs** — **"corkscrew" vessels** (diagnostic in right setting) · collaterals → **"spider leg"**

![Corkscrew arteriogram](../../MCQ/attachments/q4486-illa-arteriogram.jpg)

**Echocardiogram** — exclude **proximal embolic source**

**Labs** — exclude alternative diagnoses (autoimmune/vascular mimics)

## 5 Management

```
Buerger's disease
├─ All tobacco users → smoking cessation + symptomatic care
├─ Refractory pain / digital ischaemia → sympathectomy (controversial)
└─ Gangrene / non-healing ulcer / refractory pain → amputation
```

**Smoking cessation** — **only treatment that ↓ amputation risk** (education, pharmacotherapy, groups)

**Symptomatic** — avoid cold, gentle exercise, daily **aspirin**, vasodilators\
✗ Wrong options: shown to ↓ amputation or halt progression

**Sympathectomy** — cut nerves to affected area; controversial

**No prophylactic amputation** of unaffected digits — not described

**Prognosis** — **quit → 94% avoid amputation**; continue → **43% amputation within 8 y**

## How it's asked

**"45-y male smoker, digital ulcers — true statement?"** → **arteriography useful for diagnosis**\
✗ Wrong options: aspirin ↓ amputation · prophylactic amputation · painless · proximal first

**"Only treatment that reduces amputation?"** → **smoking cessation**

**"Arteriogram sign?"** → **corkscrew vessels**

**"Acute-phase histology?"** → **occlusive thrombus, vessel wall spared**
