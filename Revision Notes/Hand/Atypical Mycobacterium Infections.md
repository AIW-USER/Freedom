# Atypical Mycobacterium Infections

*Hand · 4 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & organisms

**Incidence** — rare; **0.05 / 100,000 / y**

**Who** — **marine workers**, fishermen; **immunocompromised** host (most have some immunosuppression)

**Site** — **hand & wrist in 50%**

**Incubation** — avg **2 wk** (up to 6 mo); time to diagnosis often **>1 y** (missed Dx)

**Prior steroid injection** — **poorer outcome**

| Organism | Habitat | Culture |
|---|---|---|
| ***M. marinum*** | **Most common**; stagnant fresh/salt water, **aquariums**, fish nets | **Löwenstein-Jensen at 30–32 °C** |
| *M. kansasii* | Soil | — |
| *M. terrae* | **Soil** | **37 °C** |
| *M. avium intracellulare* | **Terminal AIDS** (also non-HIV) | **Room temperature** |

### Mycobacterium marinum

**Classic** — fisherman/aquarium cut → dorsal hand swelling weeks later → grows on **Löwenstein-Jensen at 30 °C** after ~2 wk → ***M. marinum***

**Other aquatic pathogens** — *Vibrio* spp., *Aeromonas hydrophila*

### Fishmonger's disease (erysipeloid)

**Organisms (TO)** — commonest: ***Streptococcus iniae*, *Vibrio vulnificus*, *M. marinum*** · others: ***Erysipelothrix rhusiopathiae***, *Aeromonas hydrophila*, *Vibrio damsela*, *Proteus vulgaris*, *Morganella morganii*, *Lactococcus garvieae*, *M. abscessus*, *Streptococcus halichoeri*

**Mechanism (TO)** — penetration by sharp instruments or **fish bony spines**; immunosuppressed → **high morbidity & mortality**

**NOT a cause (TO)** — ***Pseudomonas aeruginosa***

### Compound palmar ganglion (TB tenosynovitis)

**Definition (TO)** — **tuberculous tenosynovitis** of wrist/hand flexor tendons; **radial + ulnar bursae**

**Shape (TO)** — **hourglass / dumb-bell** swelling **proximal & distal to flexor retinaculum** (across wrist crease) → **positive cross-fluctuation**

**Bursal anatomy (TO)** — ulnar bursa communicates with radial bursa in **80%** → **horseshoe** tenosynovitis

**Spread (TO)** — **little finger → ulnar bursa → Parona's space** (more commonly associated) · thumb → radial → ulnar bursa

**Causes (TO)** — **TB** (commonest comorbidity) · **RA** · infection · SLE · gout · PVNS of tendon sheath · fungal

**NOT a cause (TO)** — **malignancy**

**Diagnosis (TO)** — clinical, **always confirmed by histopathology**

**Treatment (TO)** — progresses to gross destruction → **excision without delay**

## 3 Presentation

**Early** — **papules, ulcers, nodules** on hand; cutaneous rash; often **single nodule → lymphatic spread**

**Late** — **tenosynovitis, septic arthritis, osteomyelitis**

**Look-alike** — indistinguishable from TB infection

![M. marinum nodules and ulcers](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6107-mycobacterium-infection.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — clinical + **culture** (cultures & sensitivities = key)

**Histology** — **granulomas ± acid-fast bacilli** on AFB stain

**Culture** — **Löwenstein-Jensen**; *M. marinum* **30–32 °C** · MAI room temp · *M. terrae* 37 °C

![M. marinum on Löwenstein-Jensen agar](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6107-image-of-lowenstein-jensen-agar-growing-m-marinum.jpg)

**Differentials** — herpetic whitlow, fungal infection, psoriasis

## 5 Management

```
Atypical mycobacterial hand infection
├─ Early (papules / nodules) → ORAL ANTIMYCOBACTERIALS
│      └─ osteomyelitis → ADD RIFAMPIN
└─ Late (tenosynovitis / joint / bone) → DEBRIDEMENT + combination Abx 3–6 mo
```

**Oral agents** — **ethambutol, tetracycline, TMP-SMX, clarithromycin, azithromycin**; **+ rifampin** if osteomyelitis

**Mortality** — **32%** with sepsis / untreated

## X vs Y

| Organism | Classic exposure |
|---|---|
| ***M. marinum*** | Fish nets, aquarium; LJ 30 °C |
| *M. terrae* | Soil; 37 °C |
| *Sporothrix schenckii* | **Thorn** (rose gardener) — fungus |
| *Bartonella henselae* | **Cat scratch**; epitrochlear lymphadenopathy; endocarditis |
| *Eikenella corrodens* | **Human bite**; Gram −ve |
| *Erysipelothrix* / *S. iniae* / *Vibrio* | Fishmonger's disease |

## How it's asked

**"Fisherman cut, LJ medium at 30 °C"** → ***M. marinum***\
✗ Wrong options: *M. terrae*, *Sporothrix*, *Bartonella*, *Eikenella*

**"MAI culture temperature"** → **room temperature**

**"Add for osteomyelitis"** → **rifampin**

**"Fishmonger's disease NOT caused by"** → ***Pseudomonas aeruginosa*** (TO)

**"Compound palmar ganglion — true"** → **more commonly with little finger infection** (TO)

**"Dumb-bell wrist swelling, cross-fluctuation — comorbidity?"** → **tuberculosis** (TO)

**"Compound palmar ganglion NOT seen in"** → **malignancy** (TO)
