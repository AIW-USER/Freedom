# Wrist Arthroscopy

*Hand · 101 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Indications

**Diagnostic + therapeutic** — **TFCC** · **SL / LT interosseous ligaments** · reduction aid (**distal radius, scaphoid**, perilunate (TO)) · **ulnocarpal impaction** / distal ulnar resection · chondral debridement · loose bodies · **synovectomy** · **dorsal ganglion** · SNAC/SLAC · **septic wrist I&D** · unexplained mechanical pain

### Set-up

**Position** — supine, **elbow 90°**, traction tower **10 lb** to fingers

**Landmarks** — Lister's tubercle, scaphoid, lunate, DRUJ, ECU

**Scope** — **2.7 mm, 30°**

**Portal creation** — **skin incision only → blunt (haemostat) dissection**

### Portals & nerves at risk

**Naming** — by adjacent **extensor compartments**; **≥11 dorsal**: 5 radiocarpal, 4 midcarpal, 2 DRUJ (TO: 9 radiocarpal/intercarpal + 2 DRUJ) + volar portal radial to FCR at proximal crease (TO)

| Radiocarpal portal | Location / use | At risk |
|---|---|---|
| **3-4** | **1 cm distal to Lister's**, EPL–EDC; **first portal, primary viewing ("workhorse")** | EPL, EDC; distal **PIN** (TO) |
| **4-5** | Ring MC line, EDC–EDM; instrumentation, **TFCC view** | EDC, EDM; distal PIN (TO) |
| **6R** | **Radial to ECU**; TFCC, UL/UT/LT ligaments, SLIL (TO) | **Dorsal sensory branch ulnar n.** (**~8 mm**) |
| **6U** | **Ulnar to ECU**; ulnar TFCC repair, irrigation | **Dorsal sensory branch ulnar n.** (± ulnar n./a.) |
| **1-2** | **APL–ECRB**, dorsal snuffbox; radial styloid, inflow, styloidectomy | **SBRN + radial artery** |

| Midcarpal / CMC portal | Location / use | At risk |
|---|---|---|
| **MCR** | 1 cm distal to 3-4, radial border middle MC, ECRB–EDC; **SL, SC, STT** joints | ECRB, EDC |
| **MCU** | 1 cm distal to 4-5, ring MC, EDC–EDM; **LC, LT, TH** joints | EDC, EDM |
| STT | Index MC axis, ulnar to EPL | ECRB, ECRL |
| TH (TO) | Ulnar, distal to triquetrum; inflow/outflow | — |
| **1U / 1R** | Ulnar / radial to **EPB** at thumb CMC; CMC DJD debridement | **SBRN** (portals straddle 1st branch); dorsal radial a. |

**Midcarpal portals** — needed for full carpal view & **instability** assessment

**SBRN distance from 3-4 — conflict** — **16 mm** (Orthobullets/OB) · **9 mm** closest branch (Kiliç cadaver study, OB) → superficial incision + blunt dissection

### Arthroscopic anatomy – visible structures

**Seen (radiocarpal)** — **SL & LT ligaments** · **superficial TFCC** over ulnar head · **radioscaphocapitate** (discrete volar capsular band)

**Not seen** — **ECU tendon** (extra-articular) · **dorsal intercarpal lig.** (dorsal)

**RSC ligament** — radial styloid → scaphoid waist → proximal radial capitate; **prevents ulnar translocation**; seen from 3-4 looking volar-radial

**Palmar oblique ligament** — 1st–2nd MC bases\
✗ Wrong options: wrist

## 6 Complications

**Overall** — **1–2%**

**Most common overall (TO)** — **failure to achieve the procedure**

**Most common set-up (TO)** — finger traction → oedema, transient digital nerve neurapraxia

**Nerves** — **dorsal sensory ulnar** (6R/6U, esp. 6U for TFCC sutures → small open incision before knot tying) · **SBRN** (1-2, 1U, 1R) · **distal PIN** (3-4, 4-5; less mobile, 4th compartment floor) \
✗ Wrong options: **motor branch of ulnar n.** (TO)

**Dorsoradial wrist/thumb hyperaesthesia after scope** — **1-2 portal** (SBRN)

**Radial artery** — 1-2 portal (styloidectomy); dorsal branch with CMC portals

**Tendon injury — conflict** — **EDC** (index & small) most common (TO) · **EPL & EDM** from improper portal placement (Orthobullets); late **EPL rupture**, ECRL tenosynovitis (TO)

**Other** — chondral injury, portal infection, stiffness, **MCPJ pain (over-distraction)**

## Misfiled – kinematic TKA balancing

**Kinematic alignment (TO)** — restores **pre-arthritic** alignment & joint lines; **ligament-sparing** (bone cuts, no releases); femur positioned first, tibia adjusted

| KA imbalance (TO) | Correction |
|---|---|
| Tight extension & flexion | Remove more tibia |
| Tight extension, stable flexion | Remove posterior osteophytes, strip posterior capsule, ↓ posterior slope |
| Tight flexion, stable extension | ↑ posterior tibial slope |
| **Extension: tight medial, loose lateral** | **Remove medial osteophytes · recut tibia 2° more varus · thicker liner** (✗ Wrong options: **medial release**) |
| Extension: tight lateral, loose medial | Remove lateral osteophytes · recut 2° more valgus · thicker liner |
| Fixed valgus | Release arcuate complex + popliteus · recut 2° more varus |

## How it's asked

**"Most commonly injured tendon"** → **EDC** (TO)

**"Nerve injury NOT common"** → **motor branch of ulnar nerve** (TO)

**"Dorsoradial wrist/thumb hyperaesthesia after scope — portal?"** → **1-2**\
✗ Wrong options: 6U, 6R, MCU, 4-5

**"Initial portal"** → **3-4, 1 cm distal to Lister's**

**"Incorrect portal–nerve pair"** → **4-5 ; dorsal sensory ulnar**\
✗ Wrong options: correct: 1U/1-2 SBRN, 6U/6R DSBUN

**"Structure seen from 3-4 looking volar-radial"** → **radioscaphocapitate ligament**

**"Cannot be seen at radiocarpal arthroscopy"** → **extensor carpi ulnaris tendon**

**"KA TKA tight medial / loose lateral in extension — NOT recommended"** → **medial release** (TO)
