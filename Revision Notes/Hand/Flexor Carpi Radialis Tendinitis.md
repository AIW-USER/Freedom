# Flexor Carpi Radialis Tendinitis

*Hand · 32 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — rare; <1 / 100,000

**Risk** — **repetitive wrist flexion**: **golf, racquet sports**, manual labour

**Primary** — stenosing tenosynovitis in fibro-osseous tunnel

**Secondary** — scaphoid fracture/cyst, DRF, **STT arthritis**, thumb CMC arthritis

### Anatomy – FCR tunnel & insertion

**Muscle** — **bipennate**

**Sheath** — origin → trapezium; no fibrous sheath distal to trapezium

**Tunnel entry** — **proximal border of trapezium**

| Tunnel wall | Structure |
|---|---|
| Radial | Body of trapezium |
| Palmar | Trapezial crest, TCL |
| Ulnar | Retinacular septum (separates from carpal tunnel) |
| Dorsal | Septum reflection on trapezium |

| Zone | Tendon fills | Prone to |
|---|---|---|
| **Within tunnel** | **90%** (on rough trapezium) | Constriction, tendinitis, **attrition, rupture** |
| Proximal to tunnel | 50–65% | Irritation from **osteophytes** |

**Insertion** — 1–2 mm slip → trapezial crest; **80% → 2nd MC**; **20% → 3rd MC**

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — **volar radial wrist**; tender along FCR at distal wrist crease

**Provocative** — **resisted wrist flexion** · resisted radial deviation

**No** — numbness/tingling

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — clinical ± **MRI**

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — normal in primary; secondary: healed scaphoid/DRF, STT/CMC arthritis, exostosis

**MRI (T2)** — **↑ fluid/signal around FCR sheath**; ganglion, scaphoid cyst

![T2 MRI fluid in FCR sheath](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6033-fcr-mri.jpg)

**Diagnostic injection** — LA along FCR sheath relieves pain

**Differentials** — thumb CMC OA, scaphoid cyst, ganglion, De Quervain's, CTS

## 5 Management

```
FCR tendinitis
├─ First line → REST / PT / ACTIVITY MODIFICATION, splint, NSAIDs
│      └─ fails (e.g. 6 wk) → FCR SHEATH STEROID INJECTION (not into tendon)
└─ Recalcitrant → SURGICAL RELEASE of FCR sheath
```

### FCR sheath injection

**Technique** — near, **not into tendon** (rupture risk)

**Outcome** — effective in **primary**; fails in **secondary** (osteophytes)

### Surgical release

**Approach** — volar longitudinal, proximal to wrist crease → proximal thenar eminence; reflect thenar muscles radially

**Technique** — open sheath distal forearm → trapezial crest; in tunnel **incise along ulnar margin**; mobilise tendon from groove (release trapezial insertion)

**Nerves at risk** — **palmar cutaneous br. median** · **lateral antebrachial cutaneous** · **superficial radial**

## 6 Complications

**Disease** — **FCR attrition/rupture**

**Surgery** — cutaneous nerve injury · deep palmar arch · **FPL** (lies superficial to FCR) · **FCR laceration within tunnel**

**Poor prognosis** — overuse, **workers' comp**, **no response to injection**, long duration

## How it's asked

**"Golfer, volar-radial wrist pain, T2 fluid round FCR, failed 6 wk PT — next?"** → **FCR sheath injection**\
✗ Wrong options: more PT · CTR · SRN decompression · FCU debridement

**"Provocative test"** → **resisted wrist flexion**

**"FCR main insertion"** → **2nd metacarpal base**
