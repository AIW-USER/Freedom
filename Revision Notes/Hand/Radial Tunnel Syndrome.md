# Radial Tunnel Syndrome

*Hand · 73 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — compressive neuropathy of **PIN** in the **proximal forearm (radial tunnel)** → **pain only**, no motor or sensory deficit

**Incidence** — rare, **~3/100,000/y**; ♂ > ♀

**Lateral epicondylitis** — hard to distinguish; **coexists in 5%**

**Risk** — constant prono-supination with 1 kg force, elbow 0–45° flexion

### Anatomy – radial tunnel & PIN

**Radial tunnel** — **~5 cm**; radiocapitellar joint → past proximal edge of supinator

| Boundary | Structure |
|---|---|
| Lateral | **Brachioradialis, ECRL, ECRB** (mobile wad) |
| Medial | **Biceps tendon, brachialis** |
| Floor | Radiocapitellar capsule |

**Nerve origin** — radial nerve ← **posterior cord**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral/medial cord · anterior division

**PIN course** — between **2 heads of supinator** · contacts radial neck · over APL origin · along posterior interosseous membrane

**PIN motor** — **ECRB** (often radial n. proper), **EDC, EDM, ECU**, supinator, APL, EPB, EPL, EIP

**PIN sensory** — **dorsal wrist capsule** via terminal branch in **floor of 4th extensor compartment**; not cutaneous

**Wrist extension in PIN palsy (TO)** — **radial deviation** (ECRL intact, ECU lost)

## 2 Mechanism

### Diagnosis & compression site (arcade of Frohse, posterior cord origin)

| Compression site (proximal → distal) | Note |
|---|---|
| Fibrous bands **anterior to radiocapitellar joint** (between brachialis & BR) | |
| **Leash of Henry** (radial recurrent vessels fanning over PIN at radial neck) | |
| **Medial (medio-proximal) edge of ECRB** | |
| **Arcade of Frohse** = proximal aponeurotic edge of **superficial** supinator | **Most common** |
| **Distal edge of supinator** | |

**Arcade of Frohse naming (TO)** — compression here sometimes designated **PIN / supinator syndrome**

**NOT PIN sites (TO)** — **ligament of Struthers** (supracondylar process → medial epicondyle, <1%, **median** n.) · **Gantzer muscle**

**Tendinous supinator** — next to bone → does not compress PIN

**Pressures** — radial tunnel normal **50 mmHg** → **250 mmHg** with supinator stretch (forced wrist flexion + pronation)

![Compression sites](../../MCQ/attachments/q407-illa-radtunnelsitescompression.jpg)

## 3 Presentation

**Pain** — **deep aching dorsoradial proximal forearm**, lateral elbow → wrist; ↑ rotation, lifting; ± night pain

**Weakness** — **due to pain**, not denervation (subtle at most)

**Tenderness** — mobile wad over supinator arch, **3–5 cm distal to lateral epicondyle** (~4 cm; TO: 5 cm)

**Sensory** — **none**; 1st dorsal web changes → think more proximal

| Provocative test | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Resisted long (3rd) finger extension** | Pain at radial tunnel |
| **Resisted supination** (elbow & wrist extended) | Pain |
| **Passive pronation + wrist flexion** | Pain (↑ pressure to 250 mmHg) |
| **Radial tunnel injection** | **PIN palsy + pain relief = diagnostic** |

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **clinical** (history + exam)

**EMG/NCV** — **inconclusive / normal**: pain carried by **unmyelinated group IV (C) + small myelinated IIA** fibres; large myelinated fibres normal

**Diagnostic LA injection** — into point of tenderness; avoid spread to lateral epicondyle

### Key radiology findings

**MRI** — usually **negative**; may show **denervation oedema/atrophy** supinator/extensors · thick ECRB edge · prominent leash of Henry · PIN swelling

**MRI other causes** — tumour, ganglion, radiocapitellar synovitis, bicipital bursitis, radial head fracture/dislocation

![Denervation oedema](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6024-edema.jpg)

## 5 Management

```
Radial tunnel syndrome
├─ First line ≥1 year → activity modification + PT + splint/night brace + NSAIDs
│      └─ 2nd line → steroid injection (diagnostic + therapeutic)
└─ Refractory to exhaustive nonop → radial tunnel release
       (arcade of Frohse + distal supinator + fibrous bands over radiocapitellar joint)
```

### First-line treatment – therapy & activity modification

**First line** — **PT + activity modification** (± night bracing) **≥1 year**\
✗ Wrong options: injection first · surgery

**Avoid** — prolonged elbow extension + forearm pronation + wrist flexion

**Steroid injection** — **2nd line**; **70% improve at 6 wk**, **60% pain-free at 2 y**; TO: if associated lateral epicondylitis

### Radial tunnel release

**Releases** — **arcade of Frohse**, **distal edge of supinator**, fibrous bands over radiocapitellar joint

| Approach | Interval |
|---|---|
| Dorsal (Henry / Kaplan) | **ECRB–EDC** |
| Dorsal | **BR–ECRL** |
| Dorsal | **Transmuscular BR-splitting** |
| **Anterior** | **BR–biceps** → used for **mass on MRI** |

**Posterior approach** — preferred; safe access to supinator + distal edge

**Outcomes — conflict** — **50–90% good/excellent, "disappointing"**, max recovery **9–18 mo** (Orthobullets) · **70–90%** success (Orthobullets techniques) · "excellent in most" (TO)

| Lower success group | Success |
|---|---|
| Multiple entrapment neuropathies | 60% |
| **Concomitant lateral epicondylitis** | 40% |
| **Workers' compensation** | **30%** |

## X vs Y

| | **Radial tunnel** | **PIN syndrome** | **Wartenberg** | Lateral epicondylitis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pain | **Yes** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Motor | **No** | **✓ finger/thumb/wrist ext weak** | No | No |
| Sensory | **No** | No | **✓ dorsoradial hand** (superficial radial) | No |
| Tenderness | **3–5 cm distal** to epicondyle | — | Distal forearm | **Directly over** epicondyle |
| EMG | Normal | Abnormal | — | Normal |

**Other mimics** — **intersection syndrome** (1st/2nd compartment, crepitus with wrist extension; ECRL/ECRB tenosynovitis) · **pronator syndrome** (numb radial 3 digits) · **AIN** (thumb IP flexion weak) · **C6–7 radiculopathy** (EDS may show denervation) · radiocapitellar arthritis

## How it's asked

**"PIN compressed at all EXCEPT"** → **ligament of Struthers** (TO)

**"Does NOT cause PIN entrapment"** → **Gantzer muscle** (TO)

**"Most common site of PIN compression"** → **arcade of Frohse (free proximal aponeurotic supinator margin)** (TO)

**"RTS — all true EXCEPT"** → **weakness of MCP extension** (TO)

**"RTS — NOT true"** → **motor weakness** (TO)

**"Pain 4 cm distal to lateral epicondyle, pain on resisted long finger extension"** → **radial tunnel syndrome**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral epicondylitis, intersection

**"Arcade of Frohse compression — symptom?"** → **dorsoradial forearm pain worse with passive pronation**\
✗ Wrong options: dorsoradial numbness = Wartenberg · thumb IP weakness = AIN

**"Nerve's origin in brachial plexus"** → **posterior cord**

**"Untreated, normal EMG — first line"** → **PT + activity modification**\
✗ Wrong options: steroid injection · release

**"Diagnostic injection result"** → **PIN palsy + pain relief**
