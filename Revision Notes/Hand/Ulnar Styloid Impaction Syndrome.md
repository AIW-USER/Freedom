# Ulnar Styloid Impaction Syndrome

*Hand · 95 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & pathoanatomy

**Definition** — **impaction of ulnar styloid tip on triquetrum** → ulnar-sided wrist pain; **long ulnar styloid** or **ulna-positive** wrist

**Demographics** — common; **Asians > Whites** (more positive variance)

**Causes of ulnar positivity** — normal variant · trauma / **radial malunion** · congenitally short radius · **premature radial physeal closure** · **Madelung deformity** (volar-ulnar distal radial physis dyschondrosis)

**Ulnar impaction (OB)** — degenerative; excess ulnocarpal load → TFCC degeneration → chondromalacia lunate/triquetrum/ulnar head → **LT instability** → ulnocarpal & DRUJ OA; mostly ulna +ve, may occur neutral/negative

### Anatomy – ulnocarpal load

**Ulnocarpal load** — **~20%** of wrist load

**Pronation & grip** — **↑ ulnar variance** & load

### Ulnar variance

| Variance | Ulna − radius | Radius load | Ulna load | Image |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — ulnar wrist pain, **worse with pronation / grip**

**Exam** — swelling, tenderness **ulnar styloid / TFCC**, painful ↓ ROM

**Ulnar stress test** — **max ulnar deviation + axial load + supination → pronation** → reproduces pain

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| View / test | Fact |
|---|---|
| **PA wrist** | **Positive ulnar variance** + **subchondral sclerosis / cysts** ulnar styloid ± triquetrum |
| **Styloid process index** | (ulnar styloid length − ulnar variance) ÷ ulnar head width; **<0.22 normal** |
| **Pronated grip PA** | **Dynamic** ulnar variance |
| Contralateral views | Comparison |
| MRI | **TFCC** and **LTIL** |

### Diagnosis & failed treatment – positive ulnar variance, styloid impaction

**Failed OT + steroid + isolated TFCC debridement** — cause = **unaddressed positive ulnar variance**\
✗ Wrong options: inadequate debridement · SL tear · scaphoid fracture

**TFCC debridement alone** — **90%** improve & return to work (when variance not the problem)

**Differentials** — TFCC injury · **ulnocarpal abutment** · DRUJ injury · pisotriquetral OA · ECU tendinitis · LT tear

## 5 Management

```
Ulnar styloid / ulnar impaction
├─ First line → rest, NSAIDs, splint, steroid injection ≥6–12 wk
│      (may be definitive in non-athletes; athletes often recur → finish season)
└─ Refractory → ulnar shortening osteotomy (gold standard)
       ├─ positivity >2 mm → diaphyseal USO
       └─ positivity <2 mm → wafer resection (open / arthroscopic), aim 2–3 mm ulnar minus
```

### Ulnar shortening osteotomy – nonunion risk

**USO** — **gold standard**; preferred in athletes after failed cast + PT

**Wafer (partial ulnar head/styloid resection)** — <2 mm positivity; encouraging early results, **not proven superior**; post-op immobilisation ~6 wk

**USO nonunion** — **4–8%**; **~6% in smokers**

| Factor | Nonunion effect |
|---|---|
| **Smoking** | **↑ (≈6%)** |
| **Low BMD** | **↑** |
| Transverse vs oblique | **No difference** (4.16% vs 3.86%) |
| Volar vs ulnar plate | **No difference** |
| BMI >30 | **No association** |

## 6 Complications

**USO complications** — **nonunion** · tendon rupture · **persistent pain / hardware irritation** · infection

## How it's asked

**"Failed OT, injection, TFCC debridement — why still pain?"** → **positive ulnar variance** (needs USO)

**"Martial artist, failed cast + PT, USO recommended — pathology"** → **ulnar impaction** (MRI: ulnar lunate/triquetral changes)

**"USO — true statement"** → **nonunion ~6% in smokers**\
✗ Wrong options: oblique worse · BMI · plate position · BMD irrelevant

**"Gold-standard operation"** → **ulnar shortening osteotomy**

**"Load through ulna in neutral variance"** → **20%**

**"View for dynamic variance"** → **pronated grip PA**
