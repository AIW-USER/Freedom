# Acute Subclavian Artery Thrombosis

*Hand · 1 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & risk factors

**Definition** — acute UE ischaemia from **proximal occlusion at subclavian artery** level

**Incidence** — rare, <1%

**Risk factors** — **aortic arch abnormality**, **aortic arch syndrome**, **trauma**, arterial catheterisation, pre-existing stenosis/atheroma at subclavian ostium

**Patterns** — slow occlusion (atherosclerosis) · **blue finger syndrome** (repeated microemboli) · acute global ischaemia (large emboli)

### Anatomy

**Left subclavian** — **3rd branch of aortic arch**

**Right subclavian** — branch of **brachiocephalic (innominate)** artery

**Vertebral arteries** — arise from subclavian → join as **basilar** → cerebellum → risk of **vertebrobasilar insufficiency / basilar stroke**

## 2 Mechanism

**Commonest cause of UE thromboembolism** — **atrial fibrillation** (80% of UE thromboembolism)

**Cardiac embolism** — up to **93%** of acute UE ischaemia; AF → loss of atrial contraction → stasis → thrombus

**Other cardiac sources** — cardiomyopathy, valvular disease, ASD, aortic arch abnormality; endocarditis → septic emboli

**Hand arterial occlusion (general)** — usually **post-traumatic ulnar artery**; venous occlusion → hypercoagulable state

## 3 Presentation

**History** — sudden onset, no inciting event

**Symptoms** — **arm pain**, **paraesthesia**, nausea, exertional pain/fatigue

**Exam** — **cyanotic/pale, cold** limb; **pulses absent** (axillary, brachial, radial, ulnar); **neuro exam often normal**

**Non-occlusive** — pulses absent but **Doppler signal present** (monophasic)

**BP** — differs between arms

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Arteriography** | **Recommended diagnostic test**; allows **percutaneous treatment** at same sitting |
| CT angiography | Fast; rules out other causes |
| MRA | cannot treat at diagnosis |
| Doppler US | Signal may persist in non-occlusive disease |
| Echocardiography | Rules out **cardiac embolus** after treatment |

![Arch angiogram, subclavian thrombus, stent with vertebral protection balloon](../../MCQ/attachments/q5818-illa-illus-a.jpg)

### Labs

**Hypercoagulable panel** — rule out underlying disorder; labs usually unremarkable

## 5 Management

```
Acute subclavian thrombosis
├─ Symptomatic limb ischaemia → percutaneous or open revascularisation
│      ├─ percutaneous → thrombolysis / PTA / aspiration / stent
│      └─ open → embolectomy-thrombectomy / bypass
└─ Adjunct (all) → IV + oral anticoagulation
```

### Arterial thromboembolism – treatment & anticoagulation

**Percutaneous** — **intra-arterial thrombolysis**, **PTA**, **manual aspiration**, **stenting** (protect vertebral artery with balloon)

**Open** — embolectomy/thrombectomy, bypass grafting

**Medical** — heparin, aspirin, clopidogrel, IV thrombolysis, GP IIb/IIIa antagonists; limited role alone if limb-threatening

**✗ Wrong option: aortic root replacement** — for **ascending thoracic aortic dissection**, not subclavian disease

**AF prophylaxis** — **anticoagulants**\
✗ Wrong options: antiplatelets = mechanical valves · statin = ischaemic cardiomyopathy · antibiotics = endocarditis · β-blocker = rate only

| AF anticoagulation (AHA/ACC/HRS 2014) | Factors |
|---|---|
| **High risk** (any 1 → anticoagulate) | Prior stroke/TIA, systemic thromboembolism, **age ≥75** |
| **Moderate** (>1 → anticoagulate) | Age 65–74, ♀, **HTN**, **DM**, heart failure, arterial disease |

## 6 Complications

**Limb ischaemia** — gangrene, limb loss; **delayed diagnosis** → amputation

**Basilar stroke** — prolonged ischaemia, or **iatrogenic emboli** if vertebrobasilar system unprotected during endovascular Rx

**Prognosis** — favourable with prompt Rx; untreated → stroke + amputation

## How it's asked

**"Absent pulses + Doppler signal — treatment EXCEPT"** → **aortic root replacement**\
✗ Wrong options: thrombolysis, PTA, aspiration, stent

**"Best diagnostic test"** → **arteriography** (diagnose + treat)

**"Commonest cause of UE thromboembolism"** → **atrial fibrillation**

**"AF patient — reduce hand thromboembolism"** → **anticoagulants**

**"Feared complication of delay / endovascular Rx"** → **basilar stroke**
