# Fingertip Amputations & Finger Flaps

*Hand · 31 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Goals

**Goals** — **sensate** tip · **durable** tip · **bone support for nail growth**; improper Rx → **stiffness**

**Reconstructive ladder** — primary closure → secondary intention → STSG → FTSG → random local flap → axial local → island local → distant random → distant axial → free tissue

### Fingertip anatomy

| Structure | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Eponychium** | Dorsal skin proximal to nail (covers nail fold) |
| Paronychium | Lateral nail folds |
| **Hyponychium** | Keratin plug under distal free nail edge |
| **Lunula** | White proximal nail; **divides sterile from germinal matrix** |
| **Sterile matrix** | Distal; **nail adheres** here |
| **Germinal matrix** | Proximal; **90% of nail growth** |

![Fingertip anatomy](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6060-fingertip-anatomy.jpg)

### Digital arterial arches

**Proper digital aa.** — radial + ulnar communicate via **proximal, middle, distal transverse arches**

**Middle transverse arch** — at **C3 pulley**, deep to flexors; supplies digital aa. between PIP & DIP → **required for retrograde digital artery flap**

## 3 Presentation

**Mechanism** — avulsion, laceration, crush

**Exam** — **laceration geometry** (transverse / dorsal oblique / volar oblique) guides Rx; **exposed bone?**; flexor/extensor involvement

**X-ray** — AP + lateral for **bony involvement**

## 5 Management

```
Fingertip amputation
├─ No bone/tendon exposed, <2 cm loss → SECONDARY INTENTION
│      └─ >2 cm loss, no bone → FTSG (hypothenar)
├─ Child, even with exposed bone → SECONDARY INTENTION (ointment + dressing)
└─ Exposed bone/tendon (adult)
       ├─ can rongeur without losing nail support → REVISION AMPUTATION + primary closure
       └─ can't → FLAP by region
```

**Not replantation** — not indicated for distal fingertip amputation

### Fingertip amputation – healing by secondary intention (children, small defects)

**Indications** — adult/child, **no bone or tendon exposed, <2 cm skin loss**; **children with exposed bone**

**6-y-old, exposed P3** → **no change: ointment + dressing**\
✗ Wrong options: homodigital island · thenar · volar advancement · V-Y

**6-y-old, lawn mower, 6 mm distal to lunula, contaminated, no bone, <1 cm** → **I&D + soft dressing + review within 1 wk**\
✗ Wrong options: replant · DIP revision · Moberg · Z-plasty

**Adult 1 cm defect, no bone, sterile matrix intact** → **local wound care alone**\
✗ Wrong options: primary closure · revision · V-Y · digital island

**Child >1 cm soft-tissue loss** — controversial: V-Y vs dressings

**Technique** — irrigation + soft dressing → **day 7–10 water–peroxide soaks** → heals **3–5 wk** (Orthobullets) · ~4 wk (Neustein) · semiocclusive 2–8 wk (Germann)

**Revision amputation outcomes** — better **static 2PD & arc of motion** than local flaps; commonest complaint **cold intolerance**

### FTSG & revision amputation

**FTSG** — **hypothenar** donor, closed primarily; bolster (cotton ball) 7 d → ROM after 7 d

**Avoid STSG** — **contractile, tender, less durable**

**No skin graft on bare tendon without paratenon** → adhesions

**Revision amputation steps** — **ablate remaining nail matrix** (avoid nail remnants/cysts) · tendon insertions lost → **disarticulate DIP** · **transect digital nerves & tendons proximally** (traction neurectomy) · volar skin over bone to dorsal

### Flap selection by region

![Flap options by region](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6060-hand-master-moved.jpg)

| Region | Flap(s) |
|---|---|
| Tip – **transverse / dorsal oblique** | **V-Y advancement**, digital island artery |
| Tip – **volar oblique** | **Cross-finger (>30 y)** · **thenar (<30 y)** · digital island artery |
| Nail bed sterile matrix + eponychial loss | **Reverse cross-finger** |
| Volar proximal finger | Cross-finger (>30 y), axial flag (from long finger) |
| Dorsal proximal finger & MCP | Reverse cross-finger, axial flag |
| **Volar thumb** | **Moberg <2 cm** · **FDMA >2 cm** · **neurovascular island up to 4 cm** |
| **Dorsal thumb** | **FDMA (Kite)** |
| **1st web space** | **Z-plasty 60°**, **posterior interosseous fasciocutaneous** (if >75%) |
| **Dorsal hand** | **Groin flap** (superficial circumflex iliac a.) |

### Advancement flaps – V-Y & volar (Moberg) advancement

**V-Y** — **transverse / dorsal oblique** tip (more dorsal than volar loss); can't advance over phalanx to cover eponychium

**Transverse ring tip, exposed bone** → **shorten P3 + nail bed removal + V-Y** (nail bed removal prevents **hook nail**)\
✗ Wrong options: PIP amputation · cross-finger · groin · thenar

**Moberg (volar advancement)** — **volar thumb pulp ≤2 cm**

**Island volar advancement (thumb)** — divide all but NV bundles → covers up to **3.5 cm** · up to 4 cm by elevating whole volar thumb skin; IP crease → nail bed\
✗ Wrong options: <2.5 cm options

**Thumb defect size source note** — neurovascular island **up to 4 cm** (Orthobullets) · island volar advancement **2.5–3.5 cm** (OB4508)

| Flap | Thenar | Cross-finger |
|---|---|---|
| Indication | **Volar oblique, index/long, <30 y** | Volar oblique / volar proximal, **>30 y** |
| Pros | **Cosmesis**, more fat/bulk, colour match, primary donor closure | **Less stiffness** |
| Cons | **PIP flexion contracture** (finger sutured to palm 2–3 wk), **donor tenderness**, limited size | — |
| Contraindications | **RA, Dupuytren's, age >30, DJD** | — |

**Thenar positioning** — flex **MCP & DIP** maximally to ↓ PIP flexion

### Cross-finger & reverse cross-finger flaps

**Cross-finger** — full-thickness; **volar** loss distal to PIP with **intact tenosynovium**; hinge adjacent to defect

**IVDU ring finger volar defect after abscess I&D (exposed tendon)** → **cross-finger flap**

**Reverse cross-finger (RXFF)** — hinge **opposite** defect; dermis level (below follicles, above veins); **subcutaneous (adipofascial) flap turned over + FTSG**; donor paratenon left; divide **2–3 wk**; flap **5 mm wider** than defect; no graft over germinal matrix

**RXFF indications** — **eponychial fold loss + exposed extensor**, **sterile matrix loss + bare P3**, tendon without paratenon, dorsal PIP (boutonnière/burn), total nail-complex avulsion, burns

**RXFF donor** — **dorsum of middle/proximal phalanx of adjacent finger**\
✗ Wrong options: distal phalanx (too little tissue, endangers donor germinal matrix) · over DIP/PIP (skin too thin)

**Blood supply RXFF** — dorsal cutaneous branches of proper digital a.

**Index total nail complex avulsion alternative** — cross-thumb-to-index flap

**Other dorsal options (wrong here)** — toe nail bed graft (nail bed only, not eponychium) · volar cross-finger (rare, distal thumb) · heterodigital reversed NV island (extensive defects, alternative to microsurgery)

### First dorsal metacarpal artery (Kite) flap for thumb defects

**Indication** — **dorsal thumb** with exposed tendon/bone (no paratenon/tenosynovium); also volar thumb >2 cm, web space

**Vessel** — **FDMA, branch of radial a. at snuffbox**; **antegrade**, axial pattern; over index MC; ± 2nd DMA for large defects

**Flap** — island from dorsoproximal index (over MCP/P1) + radial nerve branches + 1st DI fascia; pedicle **up to 7 cm**; covers **up to 3 × 5 cm**; donor FTSG; sensate via dorsal digital nerve branches

**2 × 4 cm dorsal thumb, exposed tendon & bone** → **FDMA**\
✗ Wrong options: cross-finger · Moberg · FTSG · V-Y

**Dorsal thumb avulsion after EPL repair/transfer** → **Kite flap**\
✗ Wrong options: VAC · wet-to-dry

![Kite flap](../../MCQ/attachments/q8837-illa-kite-flap.jpg)

| Flap | Vessel / flow |
|---|---|
| **FDMA (Kite)** | **First dorsal MC a., antegrade** |
| Dorsoulnar/radial collateral (thumb) | Retrograde, distal thumb |
| Posterior interosseous | PIA, antegrade or retrograde; web space, dorsal hand, elbow |
| Homodigital / heterodigital NV island | Digital a., antegrade or reverse |
| **Retrograde digital artery** | **Needs middle transverse arch** |
| Axial flag | Dorsal MC aa. |
| Groin | Superficial circumflex iliac a. |

### Littler neurovascular island flap (TO)

**Flap (TO)** — pulp skin from **ulnar side of middle** or **radial side of ring** finger (\~2.5 × 1 cm) on its digital NV bundle → dissected to palm → **tunnelled to thumb**

**Use (TO)** — **sensate cover for distal thumb / thumb pulp** (slicing, bike-chain injuries) · donor closed primarily (graft distally)

**Complications (TO)** — commonest **venous congestion** (tight tunnel) · patient must **relearn localisation (cortical reorientation)**

**Blast index unsalvageable beyond PIP (TO)** — **amputate at PIP level** + cover (keep functional length)

### Homodigital retrograde island flap

**Retrograde flow digital artery flap** — pulp, dorsal & lateral defects **P3 → PIP**; requires **patent digital a. + middle transverse anastomotic arch**\
✗ Wrong options: axial flag · groin · PIA · RXFF

**Digital island artery** — **best axial pattern flap**; straight, dorsal oblique or volar oblique tip

### Z-plasty principles

| Z-plasty angle | Gain in length |
|---|---|
| 30° | **25%** |
| 45° | **50%** |
| **60°** | **75%** |

**Use** — scar contracture revision (1st web burn, volar finger scar), prophylaxis; **60° = standard**\
✗ Wrong options: 25° limbs · STSG · Moberg · V-Y

## 6 Complications

### Nail bed injury – germinal matrix

**Split nail** — **germinal matrix** scar → excise scar + matrix graft (adjacent finger / 2nd toe)\
✗ Wrong options: eponychium · hyponychium · lunula · sterile matrix

**Nail inclusion cyst** — incomplete germinal matrix ablation at revision · or eponychial fold not splinted open → scars to sterile matrix

**Nail bed repair** — remove plate, repair (chromic), splint eponychial fold (nail/foil)

**Hook nail** — **tight tip closure / pulp overtightened** + **insufficient bony support** → prevent by nail bed removal when shortening

![Hook nail deformity](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6060-hook-nail-deformity.jpg)

### Complications – digital neuroma

**Symptomatic neuroma** — <10%; **highest risk: avulsion mechanism** and **index finger** (nerve density)

**Not risk factors** — single digit, proximal level, ED vs theatre, small finger

**Flap failure** — inadequate arterial flow (**vasospasm → anastomotic thrombosis**) or venous outflow

**Thenar flap** — **recipient PIP flexion contracture**\
✗ Wrong options: flap failure · colour mismatch · motor branch injury · recipient sensitivity (donor tenderness)

## X vs Y

| | **Cross-finger** | **Reverse cross-finger** |
|---|---|---|
| Defect | **Volar** | **Dorsal** (eponychium, nail bed, bare extensor) |
| Hinge | Adjacent to defect | **Opposite** defect |
| Tissue | Full-thickness skin + fat | Subcutaneous turnover + FTSG |

| | **Moberg** | **FDMA (Kite)** | NV island |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thumb defect | Volar **<2 cm** | **Dorsal**, volar >2 cm | Volar up to 4 cm |

## How it's asked

**"Child, fingertip amputation, exposed bone?"** → **ointment + dressing (no flap)**

**"Child, contaminated tip, no bone, <1 cm?"** → **I&D, soft dressing, review in 1 wk**

**"Adult 1 cm defect, no bone, sterile matrix intact?"** → **local wound care**

**"Transverse tip, exposed bone?"** → **shorten P3 + nail bed removal + V-Y**

**"Moberg flap suits?"** → **volar thumb defect**

**"Largest defect for island volar advancement?"** → **2.5–3.5 cm**

**"Distal thumb amputation needing sensate cover?"** → **Littler neurovascular island flap** (TO)

**"Thenar flap candidate?"** → **28 y, volar oblique index defect**\
✗ Wrong options: 66 y · RA · dorsal · thumb

**"Volar finger defect with exposed tendon, 29 y IVDU?"** → **cross-finger flap**

**"Commonest complication of thenar flap?"** → **recipient PIP flexion contracture**

**"Nail + eponychium avulsed, bare extensor?"** → **reverse cross-finger flap**\
✗ Wrong options: FTSG · toe nail bed · volar cross-finger

**"RXFF for index sterile matrix loss — donor?"** → **dorsum of middle phalanx of middle finger**

**"Dorsal thumb defect with exposed tendon/bone?"** → **FDMA (Kite) flap**

**"Blood supply of Kite flap?"** → **antegrade, first dorsal metacarpal artery**

**"Flap needing middle transverse anastomotic arch?"** → **retrograde flow digital artery flap**

**"75% lengthening of web scar?"** → **two-flap Z-plasty, 60°**

**"Split nail after revision amputation?"** → **germinal matrix**

**"Neuroma after revision amputation — biggest risk?"** → **avulsion mechanism**
