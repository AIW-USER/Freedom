# Pyogenic Flexor Tenosynovitis

*Hand · 70 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — **2.5–9.4%** of hand infections

**Severity (TO)** — **most serious hand infection** → sheath gliding destruction, tendon necrosis, osteomyelitis, amputation

**Commonest digits (TO)** — **ring, middle, index**

**Risk factors** — **diabetes**, **IVDU**, immunocompromise

### Anatomy – sheaths & spaces

| Digit | Sheath extent |
|---|---|
| Index, middle, ring | **DIP → just proximal to A1** (MC neck); **isolated** (✗ Wrong options: horseshoe) |
| **Thumb (FPL)** | IP → **radial bursa** |
| **Little** | DIP → **ulnar bursa** |

**Parona's space** — **between pronator quadratus fascia and FDP conjoined tendon sheaths** → radial–ulnar bursa communication → **horseshoe abscess** (thumb ↔ little finger)

![Flexor sheaths and bursae](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6105-flexor-sheaths-of-the-hand.jpg)

### Microbiology – MRSA in IV drug users

| Organism | Setting |
|---|---|
| **Staph aureus** (40–75%) | **Most common** (gram + cocci; not gram + bacilli) |
| **MRSA** (29%) | **IV drug users** |
| Staph epidermidis, β-haemolytic strep, Pseudomonas | Skin flora |
| Mixed / gram-negative | Immunocompromised |
| **Eikenella** | **Human bite** / fight bite; IVDU licking needles |
| **Pasteurella multocida** | Animal bite |

**Distractors** — HSV → **herpetic whitlow** (vesicles, healthcare workers, **do not debride**, acyclovir) · Candida → **chronic paronychia** · E. coli → uncommon

## 2 Mechanism

**Route** — **penetrating trauma** (commonest) · direct spread from felon, septic joint, deep space · rarely haematogenous

## 3 Presentation

**Timing** — presents **24–48 h** after onset; palmar aspect of one digit

### Kanavel signs & diagnosis

| Kanavel sign | Note |
|---|---|
| **Flexed (partially flexed) resting posture** | Not hyperextended |
| **Tenderness along entire flexor sheath** | not extensor sheath (TO) · not localised to A1 (= trigger finger) |
| **Pain on passive extension** | **Most constant + first sign** (TO) |
| **Fusiform swelling** | 4th sign **added later, not by Kanavel** (TO) · not discrete swelling |

**NOT a Kanavel sign** — **pain on passive flexion** (TO ×2, OB) · tenderness over extensor sheath (TO ×2)

**✗ Wrong option** — inability to flex DIP → **FDP rupture**

![Kanavel signs](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6105-pft1.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — **clinical (Kanavel)**; labs may show normal WBC with mildly ↑ ESR/CRP

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Fact |
|---|---|
| X-ray | Not required; exclude **foreign body** |
| MRI | **not needed for diagnosis**; cannot separate infective vs inflammatory; extent if equivocal |

**Differential** — felon, cellulitis, deep space infection, collar-button abscess, herpetic whitlow, septic arthritis, gout

## 5 Management

```
Suspected PFT
├─ Early (<24–48 h), no purulence → ADMIT, IV ANTIBIOTICS, SPLINT, ELEVATE
│      └─ no improvement in 24 h → I&D
└─ Late presentation / purulence / ischaemia → URGENT I&D + CULTURE-SPECIFIC IV ANTIBIOTICS
       ├─ Early disease → CLOSED CATHETER IRRIGATION (A1 → A5)
       └─ Advanced / necrosis → OPEN I&D (BRUNER / MIDLATERAL)
```

### Surgical drainage – indications, technique & stiffness risk

**Best outcome predictor** — **early IV antibiotics** correlate most closely with good outcome

**Empirical (OB)** — **vancomycin + piperacillin/tazobactam**

**Surgery indicated by** — **delayed presentation (>24–48 h)**, purulence, failed 24 h antibiotics\
✗ Wrong options: immunocompromise · age · malignancy · all four Kanavel signs alone

**Closed irrigation** — catheter **proximal to A1** → out at **A5 / DIP**; thumb exits just distal to carpal tunnel; little finger with ulnar bursa → 2nd catheter A1 → wrist

**Open vs closed** — **equally effective**; open → trend to more complications/reoperation; closed largely replaced open\
✗ Wrong options: two-incision superior

**Incision error** — **volar midline incision perpendicular to creases** → **flexion contracture** → Z-plasty revision (✓ Bruner zig-zag, ✓ midlateral)

**A1 release** — does not reduce contracture risk

**Poor-outcome risk factors** — **age >43 y**, **DM**, PVD, renal failure, **subcutaneous purulence**, **digital ischaemia**, **polymicrobial** infection

### Spread – thenar space & Parona's space

**Thumb PFT spreads first to** — **thenar space**\
✗ Wrong options: central · hypothenar · carpal tunnel (not a palmar space; posterior adductor = secondary)

**Palmar spaces** — thenar, central (midpalmar), hypothenar

**Thumb + little finger + distal forearm swollen** — **Parona's space** → horseshoe abscess

## 6 Complications

**List** — **stiffness** · tendon/pulley rupture · spread · soft-tissue loss · **osteomyelitis** · amputation

## X vs Y

| | PFT | Herpetic whitlow | Felon | Trigger finger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Site | Whole volar digit | Fingertip vesicles | Pulp abscess | A1 nodule |
| Posture | Flexed | Normal | Normal | Locking |
| Treatment | **I&D + IV abx** | **Acyclovir; do not debride** | Incise | Injection / A1 release |

## How it's asked

**"Kanavel signs seen in?"** → **tenosynovitis** (TO)

**"Not a Kanavel sign?"** → **pain on passive flexion** (TO)

**"All true except?"** → **tenderness over extensor sheath** (TO)

**"Most constant / earliest sign?"** → **pain on passive extension** (TO)

**"IVDU index finger PFT — true statement?"** → **early IV antibiotics correlate with good outcome**\
✗ Wrong options: index → horseshoe · MRI confirms

**"Purulence found at surgery — exam finding?"** → **pain on passive extension**

**"IVDU hand infection — commonest pathogen?"** → **MRSA**

**"Advanced PFT with ischaemia — next step?"** → **open I&D**

**"Contracture after I&D — technical error?"** → **volar midline incision perpendicular to creases**

**"Indication for urgent surgery?"** → **delayed presentation**

**"Risk of stiffness/amputation?"** → **age >43**

**"Thumb PFT spreads first to?"** → **thenar space**

**"Thumb PFT + swollen little finger + forearm?"** → **Parona's space** (PQ fascia–FDP sheaths)
