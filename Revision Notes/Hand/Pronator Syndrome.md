# Pronator Syndrome

*Hand · 69 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — compressive neuropathy of **median nerve proper at the elbow / proximal forearm** (proximal median nerve entrapment, PMNE)

**Incidence** — **rare, <1/100,000/y**

**Demographics** — ♀ > ♂; **5th decade**

**Risk** — **well-developed forearm muscles** (weightlifters, climbers, MMA / jiu-jitsu); repetitive pronosupination

**Association** — **medial epicondylitis**

### Compression sites

| Site | Note | Provocative test |
|---|---|---|
| **Supracondylar process** | Osseous remnant distal humerus, **1%** | Elbow X-ray |
| **Ligament of Struthers** | Supracondylar process tip → medial epicondyle (✗ Wrong options: arcade of Struthers = ulnar) | — |
| **Lacertus fibrosus** (bicipital aponeurosis) | Biceps tendon → deep volar forearm fascia | **Resisted elbow flexion + supination** |
| **Between humeral & ulnar heads of pronator teres** | **Most common site** | **Resisted pronation with elbow extended** |
| **FDS aponeurotic arch** | Rare; FDS = medial epicondyle → volar P2 digits 2–5 | **Resisted FDS of middle finger** |
| Gantzer muscle (accessory FPL head) | Listed by Adler & Wolf (OB) | — |

**Pronator teres attachments** — humeral head **medial epicondyle** + ulnar head **coronoid** → **mid-lateral radial shaft**

### Median–ulnar anastomoses (Berrettini)

| Anastomosis (TO) | Site | Fibres / direction | Clue |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Martin–Gruber** | Forearm, distal to medial epicondyle | **Median/AIN → ulnar**, motor | Intrinsics work despite ulnar lesion |
| **Marinacci** | Forearm | **Ulnar → median** ("reverse MG"); **rarest** | Ulnar stimulation at elbow → **larger CMAP** than at wrist |
| **Riche–Cannieu** | **Palm** | **Recurrent median ↔ deep ulnar**; motor ± sensory | Normal thenar function despite advanced CTS; all-ulnar hand |
| **Berrettini** | **Hand** | **Common digital nerves** of ulnar + median (sensory) | **Most frequent** anomaly |

## 3 Presentation

**Paraesthesia** — thumb, index, middle, radial ½ ring (as CTS), **worse with repetitive pronosupination**

**Differentiators from CTS** — **aching proximal volar forearm pain** · numbness of **palm/thenar eminence (palmar cutaneous branch**, arises 4–5 cm proximal to carpal tunnel) · **no night symptoms**

**Tinel** — **+ proximal volar forearm / medial antecubital fossa**; **– at wrist**; no Phalen/Durkan

**Motor** — weakness of median-innervated forearm + hand muscles (e.g. **FPL, FDP index, FCR**; trouble using a key)

**Other** — pain on palpation of lacertus fibrosus; **scratch collapse +**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — **elbow films mandatory** → **supracondylar process**

**EMG/NCV** — usually **inconclusive/negative**; help exclude other sites or **double crush**

**Differentials** — AIN syndrome, CTS, pronator teres strain

![Supracondylar process on elbow X-ray](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6020-sp.jpg)

## 5 Management

```
Pronator syndrome
├─ Mild–moderate → rest + splint (avoid forearm rotation) + NSAIDs 3–6 mo
└─ Failed 3–6 mo nonop → decompress median nerve at elbow, ALL 5 sites
       (≈80% relief; lacertus release possible under local anaesthesia)
```

**After failed CTR + forearm signs** — **decompress median nerve at elbow**\
✗ Wrong options: revision CTR · AIN decompression: AIN purely motor · ulnar decompression · more night bracing

## X vs Y

| | **Pronator syndrome** | **CTS** | **AIN syndrome** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sensory | Radial 3½ **+ palm/thenar eminence** | Radial 3½, not palm | **None** |
| Night sx | **No** | **Yes** | No |
| Tinel | **Proximal forearm** | Wrist | — |
| Pain | **Proximal volar forearm ache** | — | — |
| Worse with | **Pronosupination** | Wrist flexion | — |
| NCS | Often negative | Diagnostic | — |

| Nerve (OB distractors) | Supplies |
|---|---|
| **Median proper** | **Pronator syndrome** source |
| AIN | Radial FDP, FPL, PQ; **no sensory** |
| PIN | Forearm extensors; no sensory |
| Recurrent motor median | OP, APB, superficial FPB |
| Ulnar | FCU, FDP ring/small, adductor pollicis, deep FPB, interossei, hypothenar |

## How it's asked

**"Failed CTR, no night sx, forearm pain, lacertus tender — next step?"** → **decompress median nerve at elbow**

**"Jiu-jitsu, numb radial digits worse with pronosupination, Tinel – at wrist — nerve?"** → **median nerve proper**

**"Pain with resisted pronation, elbow extended — structure's attachments?"** → **medial epicondyle, coronoid, radial shaft (pronator teres)**\
✗ Wrong options: FDS · lacertus · brachioradialis · TCL

**"Pain with resisted elbow flexion + supination?"** → **lacertus fibrosus**

**"Pain with resisted middle-finger FDS?"** → **FDS arch**

**"Connection between common digital nerves of median and ulnar?"** → **Berrettini** (TO)

**"Reverse Martin–Gruber?"** → **Marinacci** (TO)

**"Mandatory imaging?"** → **elbow X-ray (supracondylar process)**
