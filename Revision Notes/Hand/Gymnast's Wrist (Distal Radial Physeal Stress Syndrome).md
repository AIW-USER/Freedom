# Gymnast's Wrist (Distal Radial Physeal Stress Syndrome)

*Hand · 38 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Incidence** — up to **25% of non-elite gymnasts**; radiographic physeal stress changes in **up to 85%** of gymnasts studied

**Dose-response** — training intensity correlates with ulna–radius length discrepancy

**Load** — distal radius bears **80% of axial load** on the extended wrist

## 2 Mechanism

**Pathophysiology** — wrist used as weight-bearing joint → supraphysiological repetitive load → **physeal inflammation** → microtrauma → **premature closure of distal radial physis** → **secondary ulnar overgrowth**

**Associations** — **distal ulnar overgrowth**, **positive ulnar variance**

**Commonest injuring skills** — front/back handsprings, saltos/flips

### Distal radial physeal stress – growth arrest & Madelung deformity

**Growth arrest risk in gymnasts** — **distal radius** > scaphoid, distal humerus, clavicle, shoulder

**Upper-limb weight bearers** — stress fractures occur **distal to the elbow**

**Physeal bar** — forms in **ulnar aspect of distal radial physis** → relative ulnar overgrowth → ulnocarpal abutment → **Madelung deformity** (depends on growth remaining)

(✗ Wrong options: **Distractors** — distal radial *overgrowth*) · compression pseudofractures (Looser zones = osteomalacia/rickets) · radiocapitellar instability (nursemaid's) · ulnar longitudinal deficiency (club hand)\
✗ Wrong options: — radius is short

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — chronic wrist pain, usually **radial-sided**

**Exam** — **tenderness over distal radius**, ± swelling, ↓ flexion/extension from pain

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — clinical + radiographs

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Finding |
|---|---|
| **X-ray (AP + lateral)** | **Widened distal radial physis with ill-defined, sclerotic borders**; chronic → **positive ulnar variance** |
| MRI (chronic / non-responders) | **Paraphyseal oedema**, early physeal bridging, radial bone bruise |
| CT | **Physeal bar** (ulnar side of distal radial physis) |

## 5 Management

```
Gymnast's wrist
├─ First line → NSAIDs + REST + IMMOBILISATION 3–6 WK
└─ Premature physeal closure
       ├─ Small bar → PHYSEAL BAR RESECTION
       └─ Large closure (~50% physis) → ULNAR EPIPHYSIODESIS ± SHORTENING ± RADIAL OSTEOTOMY
```

**Prevention** — proper technique, wrist guards/braces, ↓ repetitions during growth spurts, shoulder flexibility + thoracic mobility, core stabilisation

**Return to play** — pain resolved, normal wrist function, completed progressive rehab, proper technique

**Prognosis** — good with early treatment

## 6 Complications

**Premature distal radial physeal closure** — ulnar overgrowth → ulnocarpal abutment → **Madelung deformity**, DRUJ instability\
✗ Wrong options: PRUJ
