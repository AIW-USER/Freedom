# Congenital Radial Ulnar Synostosis

*Hand · 15 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Aetiology & associations (failure of segmentation, XXY)

**Embryology** — single cartilaginous anlage divides **distal → proximal** into radius + ulna in **7th week** in utero; proximal ends share common perichondrium

**Cause** — **failure of segmentation / differentiation** → proximal bony bridge\
✗ Wrong options: failure of formation · trauma · transverse forearm deficiency

**Demographics** — **♂ > ♀ (3:2)**; **~60% bilateral** (TO: "more than half")

**Family history** — **~20%**; familial cases **autosomal dominant**

**Associated syndromes (~30%)** — **Apert** · Carpenter · **arthrogryposis** · mandibulofacial dysostosis · **Klinefelter**

**Associated anomaly** — posterior **radial head dislocation**

### Genes & lab markers

**Chromosomes** — **sex chromosome duplication** → **Klinefelter 47 XXY** (most common chromosomal association)

| ✗ Distractor karyotype | Associations |
|---|---|
| 45 XO (Turner) | Malignant hyperthermia, sexual infantilism, scoliosis, **short 4th MC**, webbed neck |
| Trisomy 21 | C1–2, hip, patellofemoral instability, scoliosis |
| Trisomy 18 | Cardiac defects; 5% survive 1 y |
| Partial 15 deletion (Prader-Willi) | Obese hypotonia, insatiable appetite, hip dysplasia, scoliosis |

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **painless**; usually **asymptomatic**, noticed by parents/teachers

**Age at diagnosis** — average **6 y** (unilateral may go unnoticed until adolescence)

**Exam** — **fixed forearm pronation** (average **30°**); **elbow flexion/extension preserved**; no active or passive rotation

**Compensation** — **shoulder abduction → for lost pronation** · **shoulder adduction → for lost supination** · wrist hypermobility

**Tasks** — keyboard/tabletop ← pronation deficit; eating, washing face, catching ball ← supination deficit

**Toddler clue** — rotates elbow in front of body to bring bottle to mouth; abducts shoulder to pick up blocks

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — AP + lateral forearm and elbow; **X-ray confirms**\
✗ Wrong options: CT · MRI · arthrocentesis (no extra info)

**Findings** — **proximal radioulnar synostosis** · **radius wide and bowed** · **ulna narrow and straight** · radial head ± dislocated/malformed

![Proximal radioulnar synostosis](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6071-ru-synostosis.jpg)

**Fall + fixed pronation** — synostosis is congenital, not from acute trauma 3 days ago

### Cleary & Omer classification

| Type | Synostosis | Radial head | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cartilaginous** (no osseous) | Normal, reduced | ![Type 1](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6071-type1.jpg) |
| 2 | Osseous | Normal, reduced | ![Type 2](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6071-type2.jpg) |
| **3** | **Long osseous** | **Hypoplastic, posteriorly dislocated** (TO) | ![Type 3](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6071-type3.jpg) |
| 4 | **Short osseous** | **Mushroom-shaped, anteriorly dislocated** | ![Type 4](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6071-type4.jpg) |

**Cleary vs Wilkie (TO)** — Cleary & Omer = the better classification

### Wilkie classification (TO)

| Type | Features |
|---|---|
| 1 | Medullary canals of radius + ulna joined; usually **bilateral**; radial head **not** dislocated |
| 2 | Radial head **dislocated** (anterior or posterior); usually **unilateral** |

## 5 Management

```
Congenital RU synostosis
├─ Asymptomatic / unilateral → observation
├─ ADL-limiting / pronation >60° / bilateral → derotational osteotomy (age 3–6 y)
│      └─ goal: functional position, NOT motion
└─ Goal = restore motion → excise + vascularised fascio-fat interposition
```

### Management – observation vs osteotomy/mobilisation indications

**Default** — **observation** (most cases; surgery rarely needed) — 14-mo-old with ↓ supination → **observe**

**Slight pronation** — well tolerated (keyboard position)

### Operative criteria

| Indication | Type |
|---|---|
| Deformity **limiting activities** (sport, hygiene, eating) | Absolute |
| **Pronation >60°** | Relative |
| **Bilateral** | Relative (✓ answer over 45° pronation, 30° supination, age <3, affected sibling) |

**Optimal age** — **3–6 y** (average ~5 y)

### Derotational osteotomy (procedure of choice)

**Goal** — **functional resting position**; does **not** restore motion

**Sites** — at synostosis (proximal diaphysis; narrow space → tightness, loss of correction, NV risk, **PIN palsy**) · **distal to synostosis at different levels** (distributes rotation → safer) · radius distal diaphysis alone

**Timing** — immediate · **delayed at 10 days** (Lin: percutaneous drill osteotomy + cast, manipulate in plaster at 10 d) (TO) · gradual with **Ilizarov** (**lowest NV complications**)

| Position | Orthobullets | OB explanation | TO |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unilateral** | **0–30° pronation** | 30° pronation | 0–30° pronation |
| **Bilateral – dominant** | **0–15° pronation** | 10–20° pronation | 20° pronation |
| **Bilateral – nondominant** | **Neutral** | Neutral | Neutral |

**Old teaching** — nondominant in 10–15° (Orthobullets) / 20° (TO) **supination** → no longer recommended (keyboards, phones)

**Bilateral older OB goal** — one forearm modest pronation, other modest supination

### Synostosis excision (mobilisation)

**Goal** — restore active rotation; gain usually **slight**, unsatisfactory results

**Prevent recurrence** — **interpose material between radius and ulna**: **vascularised fascio-fat graft** (vascularised > free fat) · free/pedicled fat, muscle, Gelfoam, Micropore

**Do not prevent recurrence** — soft-tissue reconstruction (capsule, annular ligament, biceps) · post-op casting · post-op radiation (not described) · **anconeus interposition**

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **Recurrence of synostosis** | **~100%** with excision alone or **anconeus** interposition; **0%** (negligible, TO) with vascularised fascio-fat graft |
| **Loss of correction** | Casting after osteotomy → **15–20°** (Orthobullets) · up to 20° (TO, Lin method) |
| **Compartment syndrome** | Up to **36%**; corrections **>60°**; ± prophylactic fasciotomy |
| **Nerve palsy** | **PIN** (proximal osteotomy), AIN, radial; most resolve in 3 mo |

## How it's asked

**"Fixed pronation, delayed milestones, obese uncoordinated boy — karyotype?"** → **47 XXY**\
✗ Wrong options: 45 XO · trisomy 21/18 · 15 deletion

**"Proximal RU synostosis is caused by?"** → **failure of segmentation**\
✗ Wrong options: formation · trauma · transverse deficiency · always needs surgery

**"Indication for future surgery?"** → **bilateral involvement**\
✗ Wrong options: 45° pronation · 30° supination · age <3 · sibling

**"Excision to restore motion — step to prevent recurrence?"** → **interposition between radius and ulna**\
✗ Wrong options: soft-tissue reconstruction · radiation · casting

**"14-month-old, ↓ supination, abducts shoulder — next step?"** → **observation**\
✗ Wrong options: CT · MRI · aspiration · reduction

**"Long osseous synostosis, hypoplastic posteriorly dislocated radial head — Cleary & Omer type?"** → **Type 3** (TO)

**"Short synostosis, mushroom radial head dislocated anteriorly?"** → **Cleary Type 4**

**"Shoulder abduction compensates for?"** → **loss of pronation**
