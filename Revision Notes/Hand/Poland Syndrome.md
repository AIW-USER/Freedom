# Poland Syndrome

*Hand · 66 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & epidemiology

**Triad** — **unilateral chest wall hypoplasia (absent sternocostal head of pectoralis major)** + ipsilateral hand/forearm hypoplasia + **symbrachydactyly**

**Incidence** — **1 in 32,000** live births; **♂ 3×**; **right side ~75%**; sporadic (no family history)

**Cause** — **subclavian artery hypoplasia / disruption in utero**

**Syndactyly link** — Poland = **10% of syndactyly** cases

### Associations – syndactyly & absent pectoralis major

**Most common hand malformation** — **symbrachydactyly** = short central digits (absent/short **middle phalanx**) + syndactyly ± nubbins

![Symbrachydactyly](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6077-symbrachy.jpg)

**Syndactyly type** — **simple complete** of short digits (Orthobullets) · usually **simple incomplete** (Ireland) — read the options; can involve all digits ± thumb

| Syndactyly associated | Not associated |
|---|---|
| **Poland** (chest wall + limb hypoplasia) | **Tay-Sachs** |
| **Apert** (AD, craniosynostosis, complex syndactyly) | |
| **Holt-Oram** (heart–hand) | |
| **Carpenter** (acrocephaly) | |

**Not seen in Poland** — **polydactyly**, **macrodactyly**, **lower-limb** anomalies (pes planus, accessory navicular)

## 3 Presentation

**Chest** — hypoplasia/absence pec major, pec minor, deltoid, serratus anterior, external oblique, latissimus; **breast/nipple aplasia**; hypoplastic ribs/scapula; **Sprengel**; scoliosis; **dextrocardia**; renal/GU anomalies

**Hand / forearm** — syndactyly, hypoplastic/absent MC or phalanges, absent flexors/extensors, carpal coalition, **radioulnar synostosis** (no forearm rotation), nail agenesis

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**CT chest** — **absent pectoralis major** / unilateral chest wall hypoplasia

**X-ray hand** — hypoplastic digits, short/absent middle phalanges, malformed MCs, absent carpal ossification

![CT chest: absent pectoralis major](../../MCQ/attachments/q214241-c-fig-c-chest-in-poland.jpg)

**✗ CT distractors** — **pectus excavatum** (Marfan), **pectus carinatum**, rib osteochondroma

**Classification (Catena)** — severity ↑ with more proximal upper-limb involvement

## 5 Management

**Syndactyly release** — most patients, **early (by ~1 y)**; some need revision releases or 3-finger hand

**Technique** — complete release → **skin graft** needed; **one side of a digit at a time** (vascular safety); local commissure flap + interdigitating **zigzag dorsal/palmar flaps**

**Other** — **first web release** (opposition), toe-to-hand transfer; chest wall reconstruction / muscle transfer near **sexual maturity**

**Outcome** — function improves but **hand stays hypoplastic**; deficits persist vs idiopathic syndactyly (Kramer)

## 6 Complications

**After release** — skin graft failure, excessive tension, poor flap planning, **digital artery injury**, **web creep**, nail deformity

## X vs Y

| | **Poland** | **Apert** |
|---|---|---|
| Syndactyly | **Simple**, short central digits | **Complex**, bilateral |
| Other | Absent pec major, unilateral | **Craniosynostosis**, AD |
| Side | Unilateral (R) | Bilateral |

## How it's asked

**"Syndactyly associated EXCEPT?"** → **Tay-Sachs**\
✗ Wrong options: Apert, Poland, Holt-Oram, Carpenter

**"Hand malformation most associated with Poland?"** → **symbrachydactyly**\
✗ Wrong options: Apert complex syndactyly · constriction band · radial club hand · thumb polydactyly

**"Absent pec major in 3-year-old — associated?"** → **syndactyly**\
✗ Wrong options: polydactyly · macrodactyly · foot anomalies

**"Symbrachydactyly + forearm lacks rotation — which CT?"** → **unilateral absent pec major / chest wall hypoplasia**\
✗ Wrong options: pectus excavatum/carinatum

**"Symbrachydactyly X-ray — also found?"** → **absence of pectoralis major**\
✗ Wrong options: craniosynostosis · Lisch nodules · ash-leaf · leukocoria
