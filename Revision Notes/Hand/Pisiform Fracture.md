# Pisiform Fracture

*Hand · 65 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & anatomy

**Incidence** — **<1% of carpal fractures**; rare, often missed

**Associated** — **50% isolated**, 50% with other carpal or distal radius fractures

**Pisiform** — pea-shaped **sesamoid within FCU**; most **ulnar & palmar** bone of proximal row; stabilises ulnar column (prevents triquetral subluxation)

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanism** — **direct impact / FOOSH**; or sudden **FCU contraction**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — **ulnar-sided wrist pain** after fall, ↓ grip

**Exam** — **hypothenar tenderness** + swelling; rule out other carpal / DR injuries

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Fact |
|---|---|
| X-ray | PA, lateral, ER oblique, carpal tunnel; **best seen on ER oblique or carpal tunnel view** (routine views miss it) |
| **CT** | Delineates pattern, guides treatment; shows nonunion |
| MRI | Suspected fracture with negative X-rays → **marrow oedema** |

![Pisiform fracture on CT](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6112-fracture.jpg)

**Differential** — hamate body fracture · **hook of hamate fracture** · FCU tendonitis

## 5 Management

```
Pisiform fracture
├─ Acute → SHORT ARM CAST 30° FLEXION + ULNAR DEVIATION, 6–8 WK
└─ Severely displaced symptomatic / painful nonunion → PISIFORMECTOMY
```

### Symptomatic nonunion – pisiform excision

**Cast position** — **30° wrist flexion + ulnar deviation**, 6–8 wk (relaxes FCU)

**Painful nonunion / pisotriquetral incongruity / arthritis** — **pisiform excision**\
✗ Wrong options: ORIF pisiform · hook of hamate excision · more splinting · SL repair · LT fusion

**Outcome** — reliable pain relief; **no loss of wrist flexion strength**, grip or motion (75% complete relief)

## 6 Complications

**List** — malunion · **nonunion** · chronic ulnar wrist pain · ↓ grip

## X vs Y

| | Pisiform fracture | Hook of hamate fracture |
|---|---|---|
| Best view | ER oblique / carpal tunnel | Carpal tunnel / CT |
| Symptomatic nonunion | **Pisiform excision** | Hook excision |
| Bone | Sesamoid in FCU | Radial border of Guyon's canal |

## How it's asked

**"Ulnar wrist pain 6 mo after fall, pisiform nonunion on CT — treatment?"** → **excision of pisiform**\
✗ Wrong options: ORIF pisiform

**"Hypothenar pain 9 mo, splint failed, displaced pisiform fracture?"** → **pisiform excision**

**"Best view?"** → **ER oblique / carpal tunnel view**

**"Cast position?"** → **30° flexion + ulnar deviation**
