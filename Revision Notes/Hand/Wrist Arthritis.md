# Wrist Arthritis

*Hand · 100 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Patterns by location

| Pattern | Rank / key fact |
|---|---|
| **SLAC** | **Most common** |
| **STT arthrosis** | **2nd most common** |
| SNAC | Scaphoid nonunion |
| DRUJ arthrosis | Post-traumatic, RA |
| Pisotriquetral arthrosis | Ulnar-sided pain |

**Causes** — primary OA · **post-traumatic → SLAC/SNAC/DRUJ** · RA · congenital (**Madelung**) · idiopathic (**Kienböck, Preiser**)

## 2 Mechanism

**SLAC** — SL tear → **palmar rotary subluxation of scaphoid** → radiocarpal OA → **capitolunate** OA; **radiolunate spared**

**SNAC** — proximal pole stays with lunate, distal scaphoid flexes → early **radial styloid–distal scaphoid** OA; radiolunate spared

**RA wrist** — **supinated, palmarly dislocated, radially deviated, ulnarly translocated**; early DRUJ loss → **caput ulnae** (dorsal ulna)

### Golf – trailing-wrist dorsal rim impaction

| Wrist (TO) | Motion | Injuries |
|---|---|---|
| **Trailing** | Extends in backswing, flexes in follow-through | **Dorsal rim impaction syndrome** |
| **Leading** (more vulnerable) | Radial → ulnar deviation | Hamate stress injury, **hook of hamate #**, **ECU synovitis/subluxation**, TFCC |

**Dorsal rim impaction (TO)** — repetitive **axial load in dorsiflexion** → pinpoint radiocarpal pain on **loaded extension**; MRI: synovitis ± loose bodies; Rx training modification, strengthening, brace limiting dorsiflexion → arthroscopy if fails\
✗ Wrong options: passive

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Standard** — hand series + joint-specific views

**Pisotriquetral** — **supinated oblique, 30° supination** (profile of PT joint); **Garraud view** = 30° supination + slight extension

| View | Used for |
|---|---|
| **30° supinated oblique** | **Pisotriquetral arthritis** |
| Clenched-fist PA | **SL widening** |
| **Carpal tunnel view** | **Hook of hamate #** |
| Standard AP / lateral | not preferred for PT joint |

## 5 Management

**First line** — **NSAIDs, bracing, intra-articular steroid**

### Radioscapholunate fusion – distal scaphoid & triquetrum excision

**Post-traumatic radiocarpal OA (after DRF)** — **RSL fusion + excise triquetrum + distal pole scaphoid** → frees midcarpal joint → ↑ radial deviation + F/E arc; alternative to total wrist fusion

(✗ Wrong options: **Traps** — AIN neurectomy (volar; fusion is dorsal)) · capitolunate interposition (capitolunate preserved) · dorsal intercarpal ligament section · ulnar shortening (unnecessary once triquetrum excised)

### Ulnar-sided wrist arthritis

**Pisotriquetral** — refractory → **pisiform excision**

| DRUJ arthrosis / abutment | Note |
|---|---|
| **Darrach** | Distal ulna resection |
| **Sauvé-Kapandji** | — |
| Partial resection + interposition | — |
| **Ulnar head replacement** | Primary or **salvage of failed Darrach**; early results promising |

## X vs Y

| | SLAC | SNAC |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | SL ligament tear | Scaphoid nonunion |
| First joint | Radiocarpal → capitolunate | Radial styloid–**distal** scaphoid |
| Radiolunate | **Spared** | **Spared** |

## How it's asked

**"Add to RSL fusion for post-DRF arthrosis?"** → **excise triquetrum + distal pole scaphoid**

**"Best image for pisotriquetral arthritis?"** → **30° supinated oblique view**\
✗ Wrong options: lateral · clenched fist · AP · carpal tunnel view

**"Trailing-wrist injury in golf?"** → **dorsal rim impaction syndrome** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: ECU synovitis · hook of hamate · scaphoid #

**"Joint spared in SLAC/SNAC?"** → **radiolunate**
