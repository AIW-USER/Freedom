# Base of Thumb Fractures

*Hand · 5 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & anatomy

**Incidence** — **80% of thumb fractures involve the MC base**; commonest pattern = **extra-articular epibasal**

**CMC joint** — **saddle-shaped**, trapezium + 1st MC base; flex–ext ~53°, abd–add ~42°, very limited axial rotation

**Volar beak ligament** — = **anterior oblique ligament (AOL)**; trapezial tuberosity → volar edge 1st MC → **keeps volar-ulnar Bennett fragment attached to trapezium**

**Dorsoradial ligament** — dorsoradial trapezial tubercle → dorsal MC base; important for CMC stability but does not hold Bennett fragment

**Do not hold the fragment** — anterior/posterior intermetacarpal ligaments (no trapezial attachment), posterior oblique ligament

![CMC ligaments: AOL (volar beak) holds the Bennett fragment](../../MCQ/attachments/q213993-illa-screen-shot-2020-02-23-at-11-58-16-am.jpg)

### Deforming forces (APL, adductor pollicis, EPL) & reduction manoeuvre

| Muscle | Nerve | Pull on shaft fragment |
|---|---|---|
| **Abductor pollicis longus** (main force, TO) | **PIN** | **Proximal, dorsal, radial** |
| **Adductor pollicis** | **Ulnar** | **Supination + adduction** (ulnar angulation) |
| Extensor pollicis longus | PIN | Proximal, dorsal, radial; adds ulnar angulation (less important) |
| EPB | PIN | Small contribution |

**Primary deforming pair** — **APL + adductor pollicis** (OB, TO) · three-muscle answer = **APL + AdP + EPL** (Orthobullets) — read the options

**Nerves of deforming muscles** — **ulnar + PIN**\
✗ Wrong options: AIN · recurrent median

**Not deforming** — **APB**, FPB, opponens (thenar, recurrent median), FPL, PQ, **brachioradialis** (distal radius)

**Shaft displacement (TO)** — Bennett: shaft **lateral, proximal, dorsal**; Rolando: diaphyseal fragment **usually not displaced**

**Reduction manoeuvre** — **longitudinal traction + palmar abduction + pronation** + pressure on MC base

![Deforming forces on Bennett fracture](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6036-bennett.jpg)

## 2 Mechanism

**Mechanism** — **axial load on partially flexed (adducted) thumb**, e.g. clenched-fist punch (TO)

**Pathoanatomy** — imperfect reduction → ↑ joint contact pressure → **early arthritis**; excessive angulation → **MCP hyperextension deformity** (malunion)

## 3 Presentation

**Findings** — base-of-thumb pain, swelling, ecchymosis, CMC tenderness, painful ROM

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **Roberts view** (true AP: full pronation, dorsum of thumb on cassette) + **true lateral (hand pronated 30°, beam 15° distal)** + oblique

**Best view** — **30° pronated view**

**Bennett** — small MC base fragment still articulating with trapezium

**Rolando** — **Y sign** = base split into volar + dorsal fragments

**Traction view** — Rolando / comminuted pattern

**CT** — complex patterns, fragment detail

![Rolando Y sign](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6036-y-sign.jpg)

**Rolando (TO)** — **intra-articular** fracture base 1st MC\
✗ Wrong options: extra-articular · "adductor longus" · commonest thumb fracture-dislocation

### Treatment criteria

| Pattern | Accept / nonop | Operate |
|---|---|---|
| Extra-articular | **<30° angulation** after CR | >30° or can't hold in spica → **CRPP** |
| Bennett | **<1 mm** step-off / displacement | **>1 mm** → CRPP or ORIF |
| Rolando | — | <1 mm → CRPP; >1 mm → ORIF / ex-fix; comminution dictates strategy |

**Open reduction threshold** — **>1 mm** displacement (Orthobullets, Soyer) · **>2 mm** step-off despite CR (Carlsen) — read the options

## 5 Management

```
1st MC base fracture
├─ Extra-articular <30° → CR + thumb spica
│      └─ >30° / lost reduction → CRPP
├─ Bennett / Rolando ≤1 mm → spica (Bennett) / CRPP (Rolando)
└─ >1 mm displaced
       ├─ large fragments → ORIF (screws / T-plate)
       ├─ small fragments → CRPP
       └─ soft-tissue injury / too small / impacted → distraction ex-fix
```

**Nonop** — CR (traction, palmar abduction, pronation) + **thumb spica**; >3 wk old with no motion at fracture → accept step-off + cast

**Small avulsion (TO)** — CR + thumb spica **6 wk**; unstable → closed/open reduction + K-wires + spica **4–6 wk** (TO)

**CRPP** — transverse extra-articular → **transarticular** K-wire; oblique → **intermetacarpal** K-wire; risk **loss of reduction**

**ORIF** — **volar Gedda–Moberg approach**; thenar reflected, longitudinal capsulotomy; screws or T-plate; risk **SBRN injury**, wound problems if oedema

**Ex-fix** — two 3 mm pins dorsoradial MC + two in radius ± 2nd MC; risk pin-site infection

**Key outcome factor** — **anatomic articular congruity** > fixation method; reduction predicts **radiographic** arthritis\
✗ Wrong options: symptomatic

## 6 Complications

**Post-traumatic CMC arthritis** — risk ↑ with comminution, major step-off, multiple small fragments

**Malunion** — MCP hyperextension deformity, long-term disability

**Poor prognosis** — Bennett, Rolando, comminuted, delayed treatment · good: acute, extra-articular

## X vs Y

| | **Bennett** | **Rolando** |
|---|---|---|
| Articular | **Partial** intra-articular | **Complete** intra-articular |
| Shape | Oblique, single volar-ulnar fragment | **Y / T / V**, volar + dorsal fragments |
| Held by | AOL (volar beak) | AOL holds volar fragment |
| Shaft | Displaced proximal/dorsal/radial | Usually not displaced (TO) |
| X-ray sign | Fragment stays with trapezium | **Y sign** |

## How it's asked

**"Primary deforming forces on Bennett / Rolando?"** → **APL + adductor pollicis**\
✗ Wrong options: adductor pollicis longus + abductor pollicis

**"Three deforming muscles overcome by reduction?"** → **APL, adductor pollicis, EPL**\
✗ Wrong options: APB · brachioradialis

**"Nerves to the deforming muscles?"** → **ulnar + PIN**\
✗ Wrong options: AIN · recurrent median

**"Structure holding fragment to trapezium?"** → **anterior oblique (volar beak) ligament**\
✗ Wrong options: dorsoradial · intermetacarpal · posterior oblique

**"Reduction manoeuvre?"** → **traction + palmar abduction + pronation**

**"True of Rolando?"** → **intra-articular fracture base 1st MC** (TO)

**"Best radiographic view?"** → **30° pronated true lateral / Roberts view**
