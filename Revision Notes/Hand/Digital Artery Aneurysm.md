# Digital Artery Aneurysm

*Hand · 21 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — permanent dilation with **50% ↑ in normal diameter**

**Incidence** — rare; **<1 / 100,000**

| Type | Cause | Wall | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **True aneurysm** | **Blunt** trauma | Weakened arterial wall | **Uniform** |
| **False (pseudoaneurysm)** | **Penetrating** trauma | Organised haematoma + fibrous wall | **Sac-like** |
| Non-traumatic | Inflammatory, atherosclerotic | — | — |

**Palmar arch pseudoaneurysm** — after laceration or **endoscopic CTR**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — slow-growing **painful mass** after hand trauma; **paraesthesia** (digital nerve compression)

**Pulsatile** — ~**50%**

**Digit** — **thumb > index > ring**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — usually normal; only if destructive lesion suspected

**Doppler US / CT angiography** — pre-op confirmation: size, location, **thrombus**, **collateral circulation**

**MRA** — false aneurysm of superficial palmar arch

![MRA: false aneurysm of superficial palmar arch](../../MCQ/attachments/q219-a-coronalmra.jpg)

**Differentials** — AV fistula, neurilemmoma, foreign body granuloma, ganglion, epidermoid cyst

## 5 Management

```
Digital / palmar artery aneurysm
├─ Small, asymptomatic → OBSERVATION + analgesia
├─ Symptomatic + adequate collaterals → EXPLORATION + LIGATION (prox & distal) / excision
└─ Symptomatic + inadequate collaterals → EXCISION + INTERPOSITION GRAFT
```

**Thrombin injection (US-guided)** — reported for more proximal hand/wrist lesions

**Repair** — end-to-end anastomosis or **autogenous vein/arterial interposition graft**

**Not appropriate** — aspiration, compression bandage, steroid injection, elevation/observation (symptomatic false aneurysm)

## 6 Complications

**Complications** — digital ischaemia, chronic pain

## How it's asked

**"Pulsatile palmar mass + paraesthesia after laceration, MRA shown — treatment?"** → **surgical exploration**\
✗ Wrong options: aspiration, compression, steroid, observation

**"Blunt vs penetrating"** → **blunt = true (uniform) · penetrating = false (sac-like)**
