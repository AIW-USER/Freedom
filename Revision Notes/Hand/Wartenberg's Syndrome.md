# Wartenberg's Syndrome

*Hand · 99 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Definition** — **"cheiralgia paresthetica"** = compressive neuropathy of the **superficial sensory radial nerve (SRN)** (TO); **no motor deficit**

**Incidence** — rare, **<1/100,000/yr**

**Demographics** — **♂:♀ 1:4**; 20–70 y

**Association** — **De Quervain's in 20–50%**

### Anatomy – superficial radial nerve

**Origin** — radial nerve divides anterior to lateral epicondyle → **PIN (motor)** + **SRN (sensory)** (TO)

**Course** — deep to **brachioradialis** in forearm

**Emerges subcutaneous** — between **brachioradialis & ECRL**, **9 cm** proximal to radial styloid (Orthobullets, TO) · **~8 cm** (OB explanation) — read the options

**Dorsal branch** — 1–3 cm radial to **Lister's tubercle** → 1st & 2nd web spaces

**Palmar branch** — within 2 cm of 1st dorsal compartment, over EPL → dorsolateral thumb

**Overlap** — with **LACN** → hypoaesthesia may be absent

## 2 Mechanism

### Compression site & sensory-only presentation

**Site** — **scissoring of brachioradialis + ECRL tendons in forearm PRONATION** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: supination · ECRL/ECRB · APL/EPB

**Other** — **fascial bands** at subcutaneous exit

**Causes** — forearm fracture, **handcuffs**, tight **wristwatch**/bracelet/cast; repetitive circumduction (knitting)

![Scissoring of brachioradialis and ECRL in pronation](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6025-scissor.jpg)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — burning, ill-defined pain + paresthesia/numbness **dorsoradial hand**, dorsal 1st web & thumb; dislikes watch

**Aggravated by (TO)** — **wrist flexion + ulnar deviation + pronation**

**Motor** — **none** (TO)

| Exam | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Tinel over SRN** | **Most common finding** |
| Wrist flexion + UD + pronation 1 min | Provocative |
| **Finkelstein** | ↑ symptoms in **96%** (nerve traction), even without 1st compartment tenosynovitis (TO) |
| Diagnostic wrist block | Temporary relief |

![Provocative test: flexion, ulnar deviation, pronation](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6025-flexion.jpg)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — clinical

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — limited value; old forearm fracture

**EMG/NCS** — limited value; often **normal**

## 5 Management

```
Wartenberg's syndrome
├─ First line → rest, activity modification, remove watch band, NSAIDs, splint
│      └─ non-responders → (US-guided) steroid injection
└─ Persists >6 mo → SRN decompression between BR & ECRL
```

### Radial sensory nerve decompression

**Nonop** — most improve (TO); **spontaneous resolution common**; steroid injection evidence limited

**Surgery** — **after 6 mo** of failed nonop (Orthobullets); OB MCQ answered decompression after 6 wk failed nonop

**Procedure** — **SRN decompression in BR–ECRL interval**: neurolysis + release of fascia\
✗ Wrong options: LACN · ECRL–ECRB · BR–ECRB · APL–EPB

**Incision** — longitudinal, **volar to Tinel** → protects **LACN**, avoids scar tethering over SRN

**Outcome** — **74%** success

## 6 Complications

**Complications** — failed decompression, persistent pain/numbness, wound dehiscence, infection

## X vs Y

| | **Wartenberg's syndrome** | **De Quervain's** | **Intersection syndrome** | LACN neuritis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | SRN | 1st compartment (APL, EPB) | 1st crossing 2nd compartment | LACN |
| Site | BR–ECRL, ~8–9 cm above styloid | Radial styloid | **4–6 cm proximal to Lister's** (TO) | Lateral forearm |
| Pronation ↑ pain | **Yes** | **No** | No | — |
| Sign | Tinel over SRN | Finkelstein | **"Wet leather" crepitus**, swelling | Tinel over LACN (mimics) |

**Wartenberg's sign (TO)** — **abducted little finger** in **ulnar nerve palsy**: unopposed radial-innervated EDM/EDC; more with fingers extended\
✗ Wrong options: Wartenberg's syndrome

## How it's asked

**"Wartenberg syndrome NOT characterised by?"** → **compression of motor branch of radial nerve** (TO)

**"Compression takes place between?"** → **brachioradialis & ECRL in pronation** (TO)

**"Failed nonop — decompress?"** → **radial sensory nerve between BR & ECRL**

**"Most common exam finding?"** → **Tinel over SRN**

**"Finkelstein positive in?"** → **96%**

**"Incision placed where?"** → **volar to Tinel** (protect LACN)

**"Abducted little finger in ulnar palsy?"** → **Wartenberg's sign** (TO)
