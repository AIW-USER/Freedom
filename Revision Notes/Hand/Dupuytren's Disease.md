# Dupuytren's Disease

*Hand · 25 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & risk factors

**Who** — **white ♂ of northern European (Scandinavian/Celtic) descent**, 40–60 y; ♂ earlier & more severe

**Sex ratio** — **2:1** (Orthobullets) · **10:1** (TO sources) — read the options

**Genetics** — **autosomal dominant, variable penetrance**; sporadic commoner

**Digits** — **ring > small > middle > index**; joints **MCP > PIP > DIP**

**Bilateral** — **~45%** but **rarely symmetrical**

**TO EXCEPT** — "**index & middle most common, asymmetrical**" = false

**Risk factors** — **DM, alcohol (cirrhosis), smoking**, antiepileptics/epilepsy, HIV, TB, COPD

**NOT a risk factor (TO)** — **occupation with vibrating tools/earth movers** (not confirmed)

**Ectopic** — **Garrod knuckle pads** (dorsal PIP, 40–50%) · **Ledderhose** (plantar, 10–30%) · **Peyronie** (penis, 2–8%)

**Shoulder link (TO)** — adhesive capsulitis associated with DM, thyroid, **Dupuytren**

**Collagen** — **↑ type III : type I** ratio; **nodules deposit type III** (TO)

**Cytokines** — **TGF-β1** (also blocks myofibroblast apoptosis), bFGF, PDGF, EGF, CTGF

**NOT implicated** — **CBFA-1** (osteoblast transcription factor)

**Diathesis** — **age <50, ♂ white, bilateral, family history, ectopic (Garrod, Ledderhose, Peyronie)** → aggressive & recurrent

**Bad prognostic factors (TO)** — **family history, ♂, DM, smoking, alcohol, bilateral, knuckle pads, plantar nodules**

**NOT a bad prognostic factor (TO)** — **epilepsy** (not associated per Geoghegan)

**Garrod pads alone + mild contracture (MCP 5°)** — **observe**; **do not resect dorsal pads**

### Cord anatomy – spiral cord, neurovascular displacement & natatory ligament

**Rule** — **bands = normal, cords = diseased**

| Cord | From | Contracture | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pretendinous** | Pretendinous band | **MCP** | Palmar pits (superficial fibres) |
| **Central** | Distal pretendinous; inserts flexor sheath at PIP | **MCP** (± PIP) | Midline; **does not displace NV bundle** |
| **Spiral** | **Pretendinous band + spiral band + lateral digital sheet + Grayson lig** | **PIP** (TO) | **Displaces NVB central & superficial** |
| **Lateral** | Lateral digital sheet | PIP / DIP | — |
| **Retrovascular** | Dorsal to NVB; P1 → P3 | **DIP** (± hyperextension with lateral cord) | Displaces nerve palmar & toward midline |
| **Natatory** | Natatory lig | **Web space** | — |
| Isolated digital | — | PIP | — |

**Spared (TO)** — **Cleland ligament** · **transverse lig of palmar aponeurosis**

**Spiral cord position** — runs **dorsal to NVB proximally, volar distally**; cord **lateral & deep** to NVB → NVB pulled **central & superficial** → **at risk**

**Spiral cord predictors** — **PIP flexion contracture (PPV 77%)** · **interdigital soft-tissue mass (71%)** · **nodule distal to MCP**

**Ring finger radial digital nerve** — displaced **palmarly & ulnarly** (toward midline)

## 2 Mechanism

| Luck stage | Histology |
|---|---|
| **Proliferative** | **Nodule**; hypercellular, large myofibroblasts, vascular, gap junctions; minimal matrix |
| **Involutional** | Myofibroblasts align along **tension lines**; ↑ type III collagen → contraction |
| **Residual** | **Acellular**; fibrocytes; dense collagen cord (type I predominant) |

**Surgery in proliferative stage** — ↑ stiffness & recurrence

## 3 Presentation

**Signs (TO)** — palmar nodules · **pitting** · skin tethering · **tabletop test positive** (can't place hand flat) = surgical need

**History** — **painless** nodule at **distal palmar crease** → cord → fixed flexion MCP/PIP

**Exam** — palmar pits, skin blanching on extension, **Hueston tabletop test** + (can't lay palm flat)

**Nodule beyond MCP** — clue to **spiral cord** (nerve midline & superficial)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

**Diagnosis** — clinical

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — not needed; only to exclude arthritis/bony cause of stiffness

**Ultrasound** — not needed; shows **thickened palmar fascia + nodules**

| Differential | Distinguishing clue |
|---|---|
| Locked trigger finger | **Painful, tender A1** |
| Pulley rupture | Bowstringing, trauma |
| Ulnar claw | MCP **hyperextension** + IP flexion, ulnar sensory loss |
| Volkmann | Post-compartment syndrome; wrist flexion, thumb adduction, **MCP extension**, IP flexion |

## 5 Management

```
Dupuytren
├─ Nodule only / no function loss / painful nodule → observe (± steroid for painful nodule)
├─ Palpable cord, MCP-predominant, wants to avoid surgery → CCH or needle aponeurotomy (equal at 1 y)
└─ MCP >30° or ANY PIP contracture (>15°), recurrence, wants lowest recurrence → partial fasciectomy
       └─ recurrent / diathesis / skin involved → dermofasciectomy + FTSG
```

### Operative criteria

| Treatment | Indication |
|---|---|
| Observation | Nodules only, no functional loss |
| Steroid injection | **Painful nodule** (✗ Wrong options: surgery for painful nodule) |
| **CCH** | Adult with **palpable cord**, MCP-predominant; max 2 cords/visit |
| **PNA** | Mild MCP contracture, **medically unfit / elderly** |
| **Partial fasciectomy** | **MCP ≥30°** · **any PIP (>15°)** · +ve tabletop · wants lowest recurrence |
| Total fasciectomy | Rare: diffuse, multiple joints, **recurrence** |
| **Dermofasciectomy + FTSG** | **Recurrent / diathesis** / skin involved |
| Arthrodesis / amputation | Salvage — severe small-finger PIP contracture |

**Surgical indication** — **MCP ≥30°** · **PIP any (>15°)** · positive tabletop

**NOT an indication (TO)** — **painful nodule**

**NOT a treatment (TO)** — **Hyalase injection** · **high-dose radiotherapy** (only **low dose <30 Gy** for early disease)

**Steroid injection** — painful nodule; recurrence 50%; fat atrophy, depigmentation, tendon rupture

### Collagenase & needle fasciotomy – mechanism, complications & outcomes

**CCH (Xiaflex)** — metalloprotease from *Clostridium histolyticum*; lyses **type I & III**

**Why NV safe** — **low activity vs type IV** (basement membrane of vessels/nerves)

**Technique** — 0.58 mg per cord; **max 2 cords/joints per visit**; manipulate 24–72 h; up to 3 injections/cord at 4-wk intervals

**Manipulation at day 1 vs day 7** — **equivalent correction** (pain, skin tears same)

**Meals & Hentz protocol (TO)** — 4-step manipulation after CCH: flex PIP + extend MCP → flex MCP + extend PIP → extend both → press residual cord; max 3 attempts; stop if skin tears; night splint up to 4 mo

**CCH-only side effects** — **pruritic rash, axillary lymphadenopathy**

**CCH major (1%)** — **flexor tendon rupture**, CRPS, pulley rupture

**Both CCH & PNA** — **skin tears** (from manipulation; ↑ with age & degree of correction)

**CCH vs PNA** — **outcomes equal at 1 y** (90% full MCP extension); CCH costlier ($5,952 vs $1,540) & not fewer treatments

| Reintervention | PNA | CCH |
|---|---|---|
| 2 y | 24% | **41%** |
| 5 y | **61%** | 55% |

**Both better at MCP than PIP**; **PIP recurrence more severe**

**PNA** — office, 22–25 G needle; elderly/comorbid; risk **nerve/tendon injury**; recurrence up to 58%

### Fasciectomy – indications, technique & complications

**Partial (selective) fasciectomy** — **gold standard**; removes diseased tissue only; **lowest recurrence**; best for **severe PIP**

**Incisions** — Brunner zig-zag, V-Y, **sequential Z-plasty → allows skin closure**\
✗ Wrong options: recurrence

**McCash open palm** — transverse DPC wound left open → **↓ haematoma, ↓ stiffness**; slower healing

**Total/radical fasciectomy** — rarely; diffuse/recurrent; high complications, no ↓ recurrence

**Dermofasciectomy + FTSG** — recurrent/diathesis; **recurrence rare under graft**; grafts take even over NVB

**Dermofasciectomy vs fasciectomy** — **no proven difference** in ROM/recurrence/satisfaction

**Skoog (TO)** — selective fasciectomy of **pretendinous fibres only**

**Luck (TO)** — **subcutaneous fasciotomy** (3 puncture wounds; elderly/unfit)

**Staged** — mini-ex-fix across PIP 6 wk → fasciectomy

**Amputation** — severe small finger PIP contracture

**Do CTR on separate occasion** (TO)

**Correction principles (TO)** — **MCP contractures correct more easily than PIP** · **PIP < 30° fares better** · persistent PIP tightness after cord excision = **joint contracture → capsulotomy** · protect digital nerves near spiral band · PNF: higher recurrence, for elderly / low demand · dermofasciectomy + graft for recurrent aggressive disease

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Rate / key fact |
|---|---|
| **Wound (dehiscence, necrosis, skin tear)** | **23% — commonest after fasciectomy** |
| Scar pain | 17% |
| Infection | **Fasciectomy 7% · PNA 1% · CCH 0%** |
| CRPS | 5.8%; ♀ |
| Flare reaction | 3.5%; ↓ by delaying splint to 1st visit |
| NV injury | **2–3% primary · 20% recurrent**; **spiral cord**; PNA |
| Haematoma | 2%; ↓ with McCash |
| CCH skin tear | 12% |

| Recurrence | Rate |
|---|---|
| **CCH** | ~50% at 5 y (35% in some stems) |
| **PNA** | ~50% at 3 y |
| **Fasciectomy** | **3.5–20%** at 4–5 y (long-term up to 50% TO) |

**Recurrence risk factors** — **PIP disease**, small finger, **diathesis**, PNA/CCH > fasciectomy

**NOT a recurrence risk factor (TO)** — **MCP joint contracture**

**PIP secondary structures** — volar plate, accessory collaterals, flexor sheath → may need release
