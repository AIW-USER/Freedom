# Giant Cell Tumor of Tendon Sheath

*Hand · 37 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Synonyms** — pigmented villonodular tumour of tendon sheath · **tenosynovial giant cell tumour (TGCT)**

**Nature** — **benign** nodular tumour from synovial lining of tendon sheath; idiopathic; **no metastasis, no malignant transformation reported**

**Epidemiology** — **2nd commonest hand soft-tissue tumour after ganglion** · **commonest SOLID soft-tissue mass** in hand · 3rd–5th decade

**Site** — **palmar radial 3 digits near DIPJ**; also foot/ankle tendon sheaths (dorsum, web space)

**GCTTS vs PVNS** — **histologically identical**; GCTTS almost always **extra-articular** · PVNS diffuse, intra-articular, larger joints

### Genes & lab markers

**Histology** — **multinucleated giant cells**, sheets of **polygonal/histiocyte-like mononuclear cells**, **hemosiderin-laden** & **lipid/foam** macrophages, bland fibrous/spindled stroma, lymphocytes

**Hemosiderin** — present, typically **less than PVNS**

**Gross** — **lobulated, multicoloured: yellow-brown**, red, grey

**CSF1R** — CSF1R inhibitors for **diffuse-type TGCT** when surgery too morbid\
✗ Wrong options: routine after excision

![GCTTS histology: giant cells and hemosiderin](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6092-gctts-histo.jpg)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — slowly enlarging mass (months–years); pain with activity / **shoe wear** (foot); mechanical block, ↓ joint flexion

**Exam** — **firm nodular mass, does not transilluminate** (transilluminates = ganglion); may be fixed to bone

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — soft-tissue swelling; **pressure erosion of bone** in up to **5%**

**US** — relation to tendon; homogeneously **hypoechoic**; **internal vascularity**

**MRI** — **modality of choice**; **↓ signal on T1 AND T2** (hemosiderin; Orthobullets, OB) · OB213042/OB219922: T1 iso/hypo, T2 may be **hyperintense**, enhances — read the options

![MRI: low-signal GCTTS](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6092-gctts-mri.jpg)

**Differential** — ganglion (cystic, transilluminates), PVNS, desmoid, fibroma/fibrosarcoma, glomangioma

## 5 Management

```
GCTTS
├─ Primary → MARGINAL EXCISION (complete, not piecemeal)
│      └─ excised → ROUTINE FOLLOW-UP
├─ Local recurrence → REPEAT EXCISION
└─ Diffuse, unresectable → CSF1R INHIBITOR
```

**Not indicated** — radical/wide excision, ray resection, chemotherapy, routine radiation (lower recurrence reported but wound complications/fibrosis)

### Local recurrence after excision

**Commonest complication of incomplete excision** — **recurrence**\
✗ Wrong options: metastasis · malignant degeneration · pathological fracture · infection

**Recurrence rate** — **5–50%** (Orthobullets) · 4–44% (Lanzinger) · 27% at mean 27 mo (Reilly)

| Recurrence risk factors |
|---|
| **Incomplete/piecemeal excision** |
| **Poorly encapsulated / diffuse**, satellite lesions |
| **Intra-articular** extension, deep to volar plate |
| **Flexor/extensor tendon** involvement |
| **Intraosseous involvement / osseous erosion** |
| Adjacent **degenerative joint disease** |
| **Distal location** (DIP finger / IP thumb) |
| Not: age, sex, size, volar/dorsal, histology |

## X vs Y

| | **GCTTS** | Ganglion | Epithelioid sarcoma | Gouty tophus | Synovial sarcoma |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Key | Solid, yellow-brown, giant cells + hemosiderin | **Transilluminates**, cystic | **Commonest hand sarcoma**; epithelioid nodules, central necrosis/ulceration | **White chalky**, negatively birefringent crystals | **Commonest foot sarcoma**; mineralised 30%, heterogeneous MRI |

## How it's asked

**"Painful firm foot mass with shoe wear, GCTTS histology — true?"** → **marginal resection**\
✗ Wrong options: like GCT of bone · transilluminates · metastasis · radical excision

**"Enlarging hand mass, multicoloured lobulated, giant cells?"** → **GCTTS**\
✗ Wrong options: ganglion · synovial sarcoma

**"Foot web-space mass, hypointense T1 & T2, brown specimen?"** → **GCTTS**\
✗ Wrong options: melanoma · neuroma

**"Finger mass eroding phalanx cortex, yellow-brown, hemosiderin?"** → **GCTTS**\
✗ Wrong options: epithelioid sarcoma · tophus

**"Commonest complication of incomplete excision?"** → **recurrence**

**"After marginal excision — next step?"** → **routine follow-up**\
✗ Wrong options: wide re-excision · radiation · chemo

**"Intraosseous, poorly encapsulated — counsel?"** → **↑ local recurrence**\
✗ Wrong options: CSF1R inhibitor · radiation

**"Histologically identical to?"** → **PVNS**
