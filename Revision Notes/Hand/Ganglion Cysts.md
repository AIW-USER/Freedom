# Ganglion Cysts

*Hand · 36 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology & sites

**Commonest** — **most common hand/wrist mass (60–70%)**\
✗ Wrong options: 40%

**Age** — 20s–40s (TO); trauma history ≥10% (TO)

**Lower limb** — 15–20% of all ganglia; ⅓ of these around **knee**

| Site | % | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| **Dorsal carpal** | **70%** (Orthobullets) · 60–70% (TO) | **Scapholunate ligament / SL articulation** (most common stalk); over 3rd/4th compartments, between EDC & EPL |
| Volar carpal | 20% (Orthobullets) · 13–20% (TO) | Radiocarpal or **STT** (Orthobullets, OB) · **radioscaphoid–SL interval > scaphotrapezial > metacarpotrapezial** (TO, in that order) |
| Volar retinacular (flexor sheath) | 10% | Herniated tendon-sheath fluid; A1/A2 pulley |
| **Dorsal DIP (mucous cyst)** | — | DIP OA, **Heberden's nodes** |

**Children** — **volar > dorsal (1.2:1)**; higher ♀:♂

### Pathology & origin

**Causes** — trauma, **mucoid degeneration**, **synovial herniation**

**Contents** — **mucin** from tendon sheath or joint; clear yellow viscous gel

**Lining** — **no true epithelial lining**; **no true synovial lining**, fibrous layer (Orthobullets, OB4424) · "**synovial cells with mucin accumulation**" (OB8790 answer) — read the options\
✗ Wrong options: epithelial

**Histology** — **mucin-filled space with occasional spindled fibroblasts**

**Associations (volar)** — median/ulnar nerve compression; hand ischaemia (vascular occlusion)

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — usually asymptomatic; cosmesis; may wax & wane

**Exam** — **firm, well circumscribed**, **transilluminates**; fixed to deep tissue, **not fixed to skin**

**Volar wrist** — **Allen's test** (radial artery adjacent)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Finding |
|---|---|
| X-ray | **Normal** |
| **MRI** | Not routine; well-marginated, **homogeneous fluid signal: T1 low, T2 high**; diagnostic if uncertainty remains |
| **Ultrasound** | **Homogeneous anechoic**, well-defined; **differentiates cyst from aneurysm**/vascular malformation; guides aspiration away from artery |
| Biopsy | Not routine; specimen always sent post-op |

**Most definitive confirmation (OB)** — **needle aspiration + cytology** (excludes myxoid neoplasm)\
✗ Wrong options: observe 1 y · gadolinium MRI · X-ray · manual rupture

![Ganglion MRI: homogeneous T2-bright mass](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6086-wrist-ganglion-mri.jpg)

### Histology vs MRI of look-alikes

| Lesion | Histology | MRI |
|---|---|---|
| **Ganglion** | **Mucin + spindled fibroblasts** | **T1 low / T2 high** |
| GCT tendon sheath | **Uniform stromal + giant cells** / histiocytes with giant cells | **Low T1 + low T2**; recurs |
| Spindle-cell lipoma | Mature fat + spindle cells | High T1 + T2 |
| Nerve sheath tumour | Fibroblasts + Schwann, mast cells, lymphocytes | T1 hypo / T2 hyper |
| Pyogenic granuloma | Lobular vascular proliferation + inflammation | T1 iso to muscle / T2 high |
| Abscess | PMNs | — |

## 5 Management

```
Ganglion
├─ Child → observation (± splint)
│      (76–80% resolve within 1 y)
├─ Adult, asymptomatic → observation (1st line)
├─ Adult dorsal, wants Rx → aspiration (2nd line; avoid volar — radial artery)
└─ Severe sx / NV compression / recurrence → marginal excision of stalk + capsule cuff
```

### Recurrence rates

| Treatment | Recurrence |
|---|---|
| Closed rupture | High (home remedy) |
| **Aspiration** | **50%** (Orthobullets, OB4424) · **40–60%** (OB211501); repeat aspiration success only **30–50%** (TO) |
| Observation | Recurrence 50–60% (OB) |
| **Open excision** | **15–20%** (volar higher than dorsal) |
| Arthroscopic excision | **Lower** than open (OB) |
| Intralesional excision | High |

**TO adjuncts** — steroid after aspiration / hyaluronidase before aspiration → **do not improve success**

### Surgical excision

**Gold standard (TO)** — surgical excision of **cyst + pedicle + cuff of joint capsule**; recurrence from missed tortuous capsular ducts

**Margin** — **marginal excision**\
✗ Wrong options: wide · intralesional

**Arthroscopic (TO)** — viewing **6R**, working **3-4** (through ganglion); excise root; if stalk not found → resect wide dorsal capsule

**Mucous cyst** — excise cyst + **resect DIP osteophyte**

### Paediatric ganglia – observation & spontaneous resolution

**Resolution** — **76% within 1 y** (Orthobullets, OB217981) · **79%** (OB2880) · nearly 80% (OB211160) → **observe**\
✗ Wrong options: aspiration · biopsy · MRI · excision

**Aspiration in children** — recurrence 13–85%, worse if off tendon; excision recurrence 6–35%

**Volar palm base of index, 3 y** — tendon-sheath ganglion → **reassure, will resolve**

### Special locations – retinacular cyst, mucous cyst, Guyon's canal, proximal tibiofibular joint

**Retinacular cyst** — **2–8 mm, pea-sized**, from **A1/A2 pulley**, at **MCP flexion crease**; **does not move with tendon**; aspiration may work; excise with small portion of sheath/pulley

**Mucous cyst** — DIP ganglion in **OA** (dorsal osteophyte), **nail deformity** → excise + debride osteophyte\
✗ Wrong options: inclusion cyst · synovial sarcoma · metastasis · felon

![Mucous cyst with nail groove](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6086-mucous-cyst.jpg)

**Guyon's canal ganglion** — ulnar to hook of hamate → ulnar motor palsy (**Froment**, **adductor pollicis sharp waves**, no numbness) → **excise**; ulnar **nerve dorsal + ulnar to artery**; DDx hypothenar hammer (jack-hammer)

**Proximal tibiofibular ganglion, recurrent** — **PTFJ arthrodesis = lowest recurrence**\
✗ Wrong options: interposition arthroplasty · phenol / liquid nitrogen (ABC adjuvants) · aspiration

## 6 Complications

**Aspiration** — infection (rare), NV injury

**Excision** — infection, **radial artery injury (most common NV)**, **SL ligament injury**, stiffness, recurrence

## How it's asked

**"Dorsal wrist ganglion stalk origin?"** → **scapholunate ligament** (TO, OB)\
✗ Wrong options: STT · radioscaphoid–SL interval · CMC

**"True of this dorsal wrist mass?"** → **lacks a true epithelial lining**\
✗ Wrong options: aspiration low recurrence · STT origin · wide excision

**"Best way to confirm dorsal wrist ganglion?"** → **needle aspiration + cytology**

**"Volar wrist ganglion — resection & histology?"** → **marginal excision; synovial cells with mucin**\
✗ Wrong options: histiocytes + giant cells = GCT

**"Histology of post-sprain dorsal wrist mass?"** → **mucin-filled space with spindled fibroblasts**

**"10-year-old, transilluminating dorsal wrist mass?"** → **observation**

**"Counsel parents of child with volar finger-base cyst?"** → **will likely resolve with observation**

**"True of paediatric ganglion?"** → **>75% resolve within 1 y**

**"True of volar wrist ganglion management?"** → **open excision ~20% recurrence**\
✗ Wrong options: aspiration 33% · 40% of masses · open better than arthroscopic

**"DIP mass + nail deformity + dorsal osteophyte?"** → **mucous cyst**

**"Ganglion in Guyon's canal — nerve position?"** → **ulnar nerve ulnar and dorsal to ulnar artery**

**"Inconsistent with retinacular cyst?"** → **moves with the tendon**

**"Recurrent proximal tibiofibular ganglion — lowest recurrence?"** → **PTFJ arthrodesis**
