# Madelung's Deformity

*Hand · 53 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Who** — **adolescent females**; commonly **bilateral**; ♀ > ♂ (TO); common in **gymnasts**

**History (TO)** — described by Malgaigne, then **Otto Madelung 1878**

### Aetiology – volar-ulnar physis & SHOX (Léri-Weill)

**Cause** — **impaired growth of the volar (palmar) & ulnar part of the distal radial physis**\
✗ Wrong options: physeal mismatch radius/ulna · DRUJ fusion · nutritional · unrecognised trauma

**Physis asked** — **ulnar volar physis of distal radius**\
✗ Wrong options: radial dorsal · ulnar dorsal · radial volar · distal ulna

**Vickers ligament** — thick **radiolunate** band (volar), tethers lunate to distal radius **proximal to the physis** → restricts ulno-palmar growth; 0.5–1.0 cm diameter; ± anomalous palmar radiotriquetral ligament

**Result** — **↑ radial inclination** (coronal) + **↑ volar tilt** (sagittal) + **ulnocarpal impaction**; **dorsal subluxation of distal ulna** (TO)

### Genes & lab markers

| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Inheritance** | **Autosomal dominant** (Orthobullets, TO) · SHOX on X & Y → **sex-linked dominant** (OB) — read the options |
| **Gene** | **SHOX** (short stature homeobox), **pseudoautosomal region / tip of sex chromosomes** (TO) |
| **Léri-Weill dyschondrosteosis** | SHOX mutation → **mesomelic dwarfism + bilateral Madelung** |
| Other associations (TO) | **Turner syndrome**, achondroplasia |
| Madelung-like (TO) | **Multiple hereditary exostoses**, **Ollier disease** |

**SHOX gene distractors (TO)** — radial club hand → **Sonic hedgehog** · CPT → **NF1** · Blount → no named gene

**Other dysplasias (OB)** — cleidocranial → **CBFA-1** (AD) · pseudoachondroplasia → **COMP** chr 19 (AD) · Ellis-van Creveld → **EVC** (AR) · achondroplasia → **FGFR3** (AD)

## 3 Presentation

**Course** — **asymptomatic until late childhood/adolescence**; deformity **painless**, worsens with growth; advanced → pain limits function (TO)

**Symptoms** — **ulnar impaction**, **median nerve irritation**

**Exam** — **ulnar + volar displacement of hand**, prominent distal ulna, **restricted forearm rotation**, family history +

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

| Modality | Finding |
|---|---|
| **X-ray** | Undergrowth of **volar-ulnar corner of radius**; **↑ radial inclination**, **↑ volar tilt**; **lunate uncovered**; carpus **wedge-shaped with lunate at apex**, subluxed ulnar + palmar (TO) |
| X-ray (TO) | ↑ radius–ulna width; **positive ulnar variance**; **↓ carpal angle**; triangular distal radial epiphysis; carpus migrates proximally |
| X-ray | ± proximal synostosis |
| **MRI** | **Thick Vickers ligament** distal radius → lunate |

## 5 Management

```
Madelung's deformity
├─ Asymptomatic → OBSERVATION  (stop repetitive wrist loading if painful)
├─ Skeletally immature, pain / ↓ ROM → PHYSIOLYSIS + VICKERS LIGAMENT RELEASE
│      └─ marked deformity → + DOME OSTEOTOMY ± ULNAR SHORTENING
└─ Skeletally mature → RADIAL OSTEOTOMY ± ULNAR PROCEDURE
       └─ painful DRUJ instability → DRUJ ARTHROPLASTY (controversial)
```

### Operative options

| Procedure | Indication / note |
|---|---|
| **Physiolysis + Vickers release** (volar approach; bar resection + fat graft) | Pain / ↓ ROM in immature; **83% radiographic improvement**, no ROM loss (OB); prophylactic release in mild cases — efficacy unknown |
| **Radial corrective (dome) osteotomy ± ulnar shortening** | Pain, ↓ ROM, cosmesis, function; dome corrects coronal + sagittal; severe → staged distraction ex-fix |
| Adolescents (TO) | **Physiolysis + ligament resection + dome osteotomy** preferred |
| Mature (TO) | Ligament resection + dome osteotomy · closing wedge + ulnar shortening (**Milch**) · opening wedge · + **Darrach** · + **Sauvé-Kapandji** |
| DRUJ arthroplasty | Painful DRUJ instability + ↓ pronosupination; controversial |

**Acute/recent cases (TO)** — dorsiflexion in **full arm plaster 4 wk**

## 6 Complications

**List** — incomplete physiolysis / premature arrest · violation of radiocarpal/ulnocarpal joint · under-correction · recurrence · osteotomy nonunion · **continued ulnar impaction if radial osteotomy alone**

## How it's asked

**"Inheritance pattern?"** → **autosomal dominant** (TO)

**"SHOX mutation → which deformity?"** → **Madelung** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: radial club hand · Blount · CPT

**"SHOX sex-linked mutation → which dysplasia?"** → **Léri-Weill dyschondrosteosis**

**"Cause of Madelung?"** → **impaired growth of volar-ulnar distal radial physis**

**"Which physis is disrupted?"** → **ulnar volar physis of distal radius**

**"Tethering structure?"** → **Vickers ligament** (radiolunate)

**"Radial osteotomy alone — complication?"** → **continued ulnar impaction**
