# Ulnar Variance

*Hand · 97 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Definition & demographics

**Ulnar variance (UV)** — length of ulna vs radius at the wrist; **neutral (ulnar zero) = difference <1 mm**

**Demographics** — UV **lower in males**; **UV ↑ with age**

**Child gymnasts** — **positive UV** (distal radial physeal arrest)

### Load distribution & radiographic measurement

| Ulnar variance | Ulna − radius | Load through radius | Load through ulna | Image |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Positive | **+2 mm** | 60% | **40%** | |
| Positive | +1 mm | 70% | 30% | ![Positive UV](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6046-positive.jpg) |
| **Neutral** | 0 (<1 mm) | **80%** | **20%** | ![Neutral UV](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6046-neutral.jpg) |
| Negative | −1 mm | 90% | 10% | |
| Negative | **−2 mm** | **95%** | **5%** | ![Negative UV](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6046-negative.jpg) |

**Palmer & Werner** — +2.5 mm → ulnocarpal load **18% → 42%**; −2.5 mm → **4.3%**

**Dynamic** — UV **more positive in pronation** and **with grip**; **↓ in supination**

## 2 Mechanism

### Causes

| Cause | Positive UV | Negative UV |
|---|---|---|
| Congenital | **Madelung deformity** | Reverse Madelung |
| Trauma | DRF with radial shortening, **growth arrest** (SH injury), **Galeazzi, Essex-Lopresti** | Ulnar fracture with shortening |
| Iatrogenic | **Radial head resection** | Joint-levelling (ulnar shortening / radial lengthening) |

### Carpal instability adaptive (CIA) after radius malunion

**CIA (TO)** — carpal malalignment **secondary to extrinsic deformity**; carpal bones + intrinsic/extrinsic ligaments **normal**

**Commonest cause (TO)** — **DRF malunion with dorsal tilt** (normal volar tilt ≈ 11°) → **lunate extends (tilts dorsally)** to realign with radial surface → **adaptive DISI**\
✗ Wrong options: lunate flexes · ulnar translocation

**Key difference from CID (TO)** — in CIA **intrinsic interosseous ligaments intact**; CID = primary intrinsic ligament failure

**Other causes (TO)** — **scaphoid humpback malunion** · metacarpal malunion · **Madelung (↑ volar tilt → adaptive VISI)**

**Clinical (TO)** — pain, stiffness, ↓ grip, **↓ extension**, midcarpal prominence; **Watson + ballottement negative**

**X-ray (TO)** — DR malunion (↓ volar tilt, ↓ inclination, shortening, DRUJ incongruity); **↑ SL angle but SL gap normal**

**Treatment (TO)** — **correct skeletal deformity**: **opening wedge DR osteotomy + structural graft + volar locking plate** → carpus resets; scaphoid malunion → volar wedge graft (iliac crest / vascularised MFC) + headless screw; **no ligament reconstruction or intercarpal fusion**

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**View** — **PA wrist, shoulder abducted 90°, elbow flexed 90°, neutral rotation**

**PA vs AP** — UV **more positive on PA** (pronation); more negative on AP (supination)\
✗ Wrong options: radial inclination differs · AP better for scaphoid · DRUJ/lunate facet better on one view

**Measurement** — line tangential to **ulnar articular surface** ⟂ shaft vs line tangential to **lunate fossa of radius** ⟂ shaft; ulnar line distal = positive, proximal = negative; normal 0 mm

**MRI** — estimates but **cannot quantify** UV (wrist position not reproducible)

## 5 Management

**Principle** — treat the condition: ulnar abutment, TFCC tear, Kienböck

## 6 Complications

### Associated conditions

| Positive UV | Negative UV |
|---|---|
| **Ulnar abutment (impaction) syndrome** | **Kienböck disease** |
| **TFCC tears** | **Ulnar impingement** (ulna hits radius proximal to sigmoid notch) |
| LT ligament tears, SLD | |
| Arthrosis ulnar head / lunate / triquetrum | |

## X vs Y

| | CIA | CID |
|---|---|---|
| Intrinsic ligaments | **Intact** | Torn (e.g. SL) |
| SL gap | Normal | Widened |
| Watson test | Negative | Positive |
| Treatment | **Correct osteotomy** → carpus resets | Ligament repair / reconstruction / fusion |

## How it's asked

**"−2.5 mm UV — load distribution?"** → **95% radius / 5% ulna**

**"Neutral UV load?"** → **80% radius / 20% ulna**

**"PA vs AP view?"** → **UV more positive on PA**

**"UV more positive in?"** → **pronation and grip**

**"Negative UV associated with?"** → **Kienböck**

**"CIA after dorsally tilted DR malunion — lunate?"** → **extends (dorsal tilt)** (TO)

**"CIA vs CID fundamental difference?"** → **intrinsic ligaments intact in CIA** (TO)
