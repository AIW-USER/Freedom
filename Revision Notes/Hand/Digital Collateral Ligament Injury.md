# Digital Collateral Ligament Injury

*Hand · 22 / 103*

## 1 Basics

**Incidence** — rare, <1 per 100,000/yr

**Anatomy** — collaterals on lateral aspect of **DIP, PIP, MCP**; key to **pinch stability**

**Index RCL** — **critical for pinch**; deficient → MCPJ deviates **ulnarly** on pinch; 5.8 mm wide × 6.4 mm long

## 2 Mechanism

**Jammed finger** — dorsal/volar dislocation tears one or both collaterals; direct blow

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — joint pain, swelling, deformity; **pinch instability** after pain settles

**Exam** — varus/valgus stress; **endpoint** decides grade

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — PA, lateral, oblique ± **varus/valgus stress views**

**MRI** — if exam equivocal

![MRI collateral ligament](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6039-mri-collateral-lig.jpg)

### Index RCL grading

| Grade | Exam | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| I | Tender, stable | Immobilise **4–6 wk** |
| II | Laxity, **definite endpoint** | Immobilise **4–6 wk** → excellent |
| **III** | **No endpoint** = unstable | Early (<4 wk): **repair** (or strict immobilisation); late: **reconstruction / fusion** |

## 5 Management

```
Digital collateral injury
├─ Simple/partial tear → buddy tape 3 wk
├─ Complete tear (non-index-RCL) → buddy tape 6 wk
└─ Index MCP RCL, unstable → surgery
       ├─ early (<4 wk) → PRIMARY REPAIR
       └─ late → reconstruction / fusion (arthritic)
```

### Index MCP radial collateral ligament – repair

**Unstable index RCL** — **best outcome = early surgical repair**

**Traps** — RCL not key to pinch · ✗ Wrong options: stable → surgery (immobilise instead) · early vs late equivocal (late = poor) · early **reconstruction** (reconstruction = late, irreparable)

**Late presentation** — universally poor; chronic instability → OA

### Bowler's thumb (ulnar digital nerve neuropathy)

**Bowler's thumb (TO)** — **neuropathy of ulnar digital nerve of thumb** from friction of ball

**Pathology (TO)** — **perineural fibrosis**\
✗ Wrong options: true neuroma

**Presentation (TO)** — pain/tenderness **1st web space**, numbness/paraesthesia along medial thumb, tender small mass, hyperaesthesia

**Treatment (TO)** — conservative: rest, pressure relief, avoid activity; refractory → **neurolysis, neurectomy, transposition**

**Not (TO)** — thumb UCL injury · mallet thumb/index · 1st MC neck fracture

## How it's asked

**"Index MCP, no endpoint on ulnar stress, weak pinch — true?"** → **early surgical repair best**

**"Stable collateral injury"** → **immobilise / buddy tape**\
✗ Wrong options: surgery

**"Bowler's thumb is"** → **ulnar digital nerve neuropathy of thumb** (TO)
