# Thumb Collateral Ligament Injury

*Hand · 91 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**UCL vs RCL** — **UCL 10× more common**; UCL = **86%** of athletic thumb injuries; most common injury at thumb MCP (TO)

**Skier's thumb** — **acute** (ski pole); most common UE skiing injury (7–32%)

**Gamekeeper's thumb** — **chronic** attenuation from repetitive valgus (Scottish gamekeepers breaking rabbits' necks) (TO)

### UCL anatomy – proper vs accessory ligament & testing positions

| Ligament | Course | Taut / restrains radial stress in |
|---|---|---|
| **Proper UCL (pUCL)** | **Dorsal third of MC head** → **volar P1** | **Flexion** (+ dorsal capsule) |
| **Accessory UCL (aUCL)** | Palmar to pUCL → **volar plate** | **Extension** (+ volar plate) |

![Proper vs accessory UCL](../../MCQ/attachments/TO17141908-1.png)

**Static stabilisers** — bony shape (flat MC condyles), collaterals, volar plate, dorsal capsule

**Dynamic stabilisers** — **adductor pollicis** (inserts P1, **superficial to UCL**, ulnar n.); EPL, EPB, FPL, APB, FPB

**RCL side** — covered by **abductor aponeurosis** → Stener-like RCL lesion rare; RCL tear → **subluxation** rather than overt instability

## 2 Mechanism

**UCL** — **radially directed force → hyperabduction / extension** = **valgus** (ski pole, FOOSH)

**RCL** — **axial load** (adduction)

**Site of UCL rupture** — **distal (P1 base)** in most; bony avulsion **20–30%** (TO)\
✗ Wrong options: **avulsion from MC head** (TO)

**RCL rupture site** — evenly proximal / midsubstance / distal

### Stener lesion – adductor aponeurosis

**Stener lesion** — completely torn UCL (pUCL + aUCL) retracts **proximal + superficial to adductor aponeurosis** → interposed aponeurosis **blocks reduction & healing** → **surgery**

**Blocking structure** — **adductor pollicis aponeurosis** (**ulnar n.**)\
✗ Wrong options: opponens, FPB, APB (median thenars) · volar plate · FPL · lumbricals · anterior oblique ligament

**Incidence** — **65–85%** of complete ruptures (OB) · **64–87%** of grade III (TO)

**Second bony fragment** — may mark distal UCL end → Stener lesion

![Stener lesion](../../MCQ/attachments/TO17141908-2.png)

| ✗ Distractor | Where it blocks |
|---|---|
| Volar plate, sesamoids, FPL | Thumb MCP **dorsal dislocation** |
| Lumbricals | Finger MCP (2–5) dorsal dislocation |
| Anterior oblique (beak) ligament | Holds **Bennett** fragment; CMC arthritis/instability |

## 3 Presentation

**UCL** — **ulnar MCP pain** worse with **pinch/grasp**; tender **distally**; **palpable ulnar mass = Stener**; haematoma

**RCL** — **radial MCP pain**; tender **proximally**; presents late (**>10 wk**) vs UCL (2–10 wk)

### Stress examination

**Technique** — X-ray **first** (don't displace avulsion); stress in **extension and 30° flexion**; prevent P1 rotation; ± local anaesthetic

| Laxity | Injured |
|---|---|
| **30° flexion only** | **Isolated proper UCL** (torn off **P1**) — partial |
| **Extension (and flexion)** | **Proper + accessory UCL ± volar plate** — complete |

**RCL** — anterior/posterior drawer → volar subluxation, no endpoint

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — PA, lateral, oblique; **avulsion at ulnar base of P1** / condylar fx; **sag sign** (P1 **supination**, UCL) · P1 **pronation** (RCL) · **volar P1 subluxation** on lateral (dorsal capsule/extensor injury)

**Stress views** — controversial; only after bony avulsion excluded

**MRI** — equivocal exam; **gold standard**, **100% sensitivity + specificity** for tear/Stener (TO)

**US** — operator-dependent; **76–88% sens, 81–83% spec**, 81% accuracy

![Stener lesion MRI](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6040-626c6c7fa32a771f1ff764297c8745-big-gallery.jpg)

### UCL/RCL instability grading

| Grade | Findings | Tear |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sprain, no instability | Incomplete |
| 2 | Asymmetric laxity, **endpoint present** | Incomplete |
| **3** | **No endpoint**; **30–35° opening** or **10–15° > contralateral** | **Complete** |

**Complete tear thresholds** — **>35° opening and/or >15° vs other side** (TO) · 30–35° (Orthobullets) · criteria range 15–45°; many use **>35°** (Newland, OB) — read the options

## 5 Management

```
Thumb UCL injury
├─ Grade 1–2 / <15° side difference → immobilise 4–6 wk (spica)
├─ Grade 3 / Stener / displaced fragment → acute repair
│      └─ ± adductor advancement
└─ Chronic (>3–8 wk) / poor tissue → tendon graft reconstruction
       └─ failed / arthritic → MCP fusion 15° flexion
```

### Operative criteria

| Nonoperative (4–6 wk; TO partial: spica 4 wk) | Operative repair |
|---|---|
| **Partial tear** (unstable in flexion only) | **Complete tear** (unstable flexion + extension) |
| **<15° side-to-side** difference | **>15° side-to-side** |
| Bony skier's thumb, **stable + undisplaced** fragment | **>30–35° opening** |
| | **Stener lesion** |
| | Fragment **displaced >1 mm**, **malrotated**, or **>⅓ joint surface** (TO) |

**Not a surgical indication (TO)** — **ipsilateral metacarpal fracture**

**Reconstruction** — chronic **>3 wk** (TO) / **>3–8 wk** (Orthobullets); **PL graft** through bone tunnels (screw, button, anchor); 92% satisfaction

**Adductor advancement** — with acute repair (100% RTS) or chronic salvage

**MCP fusion** — salvage; **15° flexion**

### Repair technique

**UCL** — S/chevron incision; transosseous sutures / anchors; immobilise **IP free**; strength 4–6 wk; >90% excellent; RTP ~100%

**RCL** — straight radial incision; may resect **abductor aponeurosis**; spare **dorsal radial sensory branches**; anchors/pull-out; ± K-wire; **96% good–excellent**

### RCL vs UCL – delayed repair

**RCL** — **more amenable to primary repair even with delayed presentation**

| | **UCL** | **RCL** |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | **10×** | 1× |
| Mechanism | **Abduction moment** | Axial load / adduction |
| Rupture site | **Distal** | Even proximal/mid/distal |
| Overlying aponeurosis | **Adductor** → Stener; may need removal | Abductor → Stener rare |
| Presentation | 2–10 wk | **>10 wk** |
| Delayed repair | Reconstruct if chronic | **Still repairable** |
| X-ray | P1 supination (sag) | P1 pronation |

## 6 Complications

| Complication | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Stiffness** (MCP, IP) | **Most common** after repair |
| Persistent instability | **15%** of grade 3 treated nonop → reconstruct |
| **Skin necrosis** | Pull-out suture technique |
| Superficial radial neurapraxia | Observe |
| ↓ pinch strength | |

## How it's asked

**"Role of proper vs accessory UCL?"** → **proper: radial stress in flexion; accessory: radial stress in extension**

**"Skier, laxity in 30° flexion but not extension — MRI?"** → **proper UCL torn off proximal phalanx**

**"Skier's thumb — all EXCEPT"** → **avulsion from 1st MC head** (TO; it's P1 base)

**"Not an indication for surgery in gamekeeper's thumb?"** → **ipsilateral metacarpal fracture** (TO)

**"Structure blocking UCL reduction — muscle & nerve?"** → **adductor pollicis, ulnar nerve**

**"Lettered figure — what blocks reduction?"** → **adductor aponeurosis (E)**

**"Palpable ulnar nodule, UCL retracted on MRI — blocking structure?"** → **adductor aponeurosis**\
✗ Wrong options: anterior oblique ligament · volar plate · FPL · lumbricals

**"RCL vs UCL — true of RCL?"** → **more amenable to delayed repair**\
✗ Wrong options: adductor removal · abduction moment · distal rupture · presents <2 wk

**"Most common complication after repair?"** → **stiffness**
