# Wrist Ligaments & Biomechanics

*Hand · 102 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Wrist motion

**Joints** — radiocarpal + intercarpal; axes: flexion–extension, radial–ulnar deviation, prono-supination (TO)

| Motion | Normal | Functional | Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flexion** | 65° | 10° | **40% radiocarpal / 60% midcarpal** |
| **Extension** | 55° | 35° | **66% radiocarpal / 33% midcarpal** |
| **Radial deviation** | 15° | 10° | **90% midcarpal** |
| Ulnar deviation | 35° | 15° | 50 / 50 |

### Carpal rows (TO)

**PCR (TO)** — scaphoid, lunate, triquetrum, pisiform; mobile between bones, moves as a row; **intercalated segment**\
✗ Wrong options: tendon attachments

**DCR (TO)** — trapezium, trapezoid, capitate, hamate; tightly bound → **single functional unit**

**Lunate–capitate (TO)** — **no ligamentous connection**

### Biomechanic concepts

| Concept | Key points |
|---|---|
| **Link** | Radius–lunate–capitate chain; **capitate head = centre of rotation**; ✓ efficient, strong volar ligaments; (✗ Wrong options: more links → instability)|
| **Column** | **Lateral (mobile)**: scaphoid, trapezium, trapezoid; **central (FE)**: lunate, capitate, hamate (lunocapitate = centre); **medial (rotation)**: triquetrum + distal row |
| **Row** | Proximal + distal rows; **scaphoid bridges** rows |
| Others (TO) | Row-column theory, **ring theory** |

**Scaphoid** — resting force / **radial deviation → scaphoid flexes**, triquetrum extends; **ulnar deviation → scaphoid extends**

### Carpal relationships

| Ratio | Normal |
|---|---|
| **Carpal height / MC3 length** (collapse) | **0.54** |
| **Ulna–capitate / MC3** (ulnar translation) | **0.30** |

**Load** — **radius 80% / ulna 20%**; ulnar **lengthening ↑** ulnar load, **shortening ↓**

**Lunate alignment (PA)** — ≥ **2/3 of lunate** over radius (lunate fossa)

### Ligament overview

**Extrinsic** — bridge carpus to radius / metacarpals; volar + dorsal

**Intrinsic** — carpal-to-carpal; most important **SLIL + LTIL**

**Extrinsic vs intrinsic failure (TO)** — extrinsic: **stiffer, lower yield strength → rupture midsubstance** · intrinsic: **avulse** (not rupture)

**No dorsal ulnocarpal ligaments (TO)** — extrinsics mostly volar · **ulnocapitate arises from ulnar styloid** · **scaphocapitate + triquetrocapitate cross the midcarpal joint**

**Distal row instability (TO)** — occurs **only as axial / longitudinal** instability (distal row = fixed unit)

**Volar vs dorsal** — **volar stronger**; volar = secondary stabilisers of scaphoid; **dorsal converge on triquetrum**

**Space of Poirier** — weak area, **floor of carpal tunnel at proximal capitate**, between **RSC and long radiolunate**; ↑ in dorsiflexion, disappears in palmar flexion; perilunate: distal row separates from lunate · **lunate dislocation: lunate escapes into it**

![Space of Poirier](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6005-space-of-poirier.jpg)

### Extrinsic ligaments – radioscaphocapitate & radiocarpal dislocation

| Ligament | Key fact |
|---|---|
| Radial collateral | Volar radiocarpal |
| **Radioscaphocapitate (RSC)** | Radial styloid → capitate; **sling for scaphoid waist**; **main restraint to ulnar translation**; **preserve in PRC** (primary stabiliser, prevents ulnar drift); at risk with large radial styloidectomy |
| **Long radiolunate** (radiolunotriquetral, volar RL) | Counteracts ulnar-distal lunate translocation; **abnormal in Madelung = Vickers ligament** |
| **Radioscapholunate (Testut & Kuentz)** | **Neurovascular conduit only**; no mechanical strength; mesocapsule with **AIN branches + radial arcade** rami (TO); extrinsic (TO) |
| **Short radiolunate** | Stabilises lunate; from **volar-ulnar "critical corner"** → **primary restraint to volar carpal translation**; disrupted in stage IV perilunate |
| Volar ulnocarpal | Ulnotriquetral, ulnolunate, ulnocapitate |
| **Dorsal radiotriquetral (DRC)** | Must be torn **with LTIL** for **VISI** |
| **Dorsal intercarpal (DIC)** | With DRC forms lateral **"V"** → secondary stabiliser vs **DISI** |
| Dorsal radiolunate, radioscaphoid | Dorsal extrinsic |

**Arthroscopy from 3-4 portal (EPL–EDC)** — volar extrinsics **radial → ulnar: RSC, long RL, short RL**; RSL (Testut) just radial to SLIL

![Arthroscopic volar extrinsic ligaments 1–3 radial to ulnar](../../MCQ/attachments/q5913-a-arthroscopic-volar-wrist-ligaments.jpg)

**Radiocarpal dislocation** — rare, high-energy; **dorsal > volar**; ORIF essential

| Ligament not repaired | Late deformity |
|---|---|
| **RSC** | **Ulnar translocation** of carpus (multidirectional instability) |
| **Short RL** (critical corner) | **Volar subluxation** of carpus |
| SLIL | DISI (Terry Thomas sign) |

**Surgical steps (Ilyas)** — provisional reduction → NV decompression → joint debridement → intercarpal injuries → fracture fixation / soft-tissue repair

**SL injury suspicion** — radial styloid fracture exiting at **scaphoid–lunate fossa junction**

**Classifications** — **Moneim** (intercarpal dissociation; type II & open → worse) · **Dumontier** (radial styloid involvement)

**RSC reconstruction** — **brachioradialis** tendon

### Intrinsic & extrinsic ligament function (LT, RSL)

| SLIL part | Role |
|---|---|
| **Dorsal** | **Thickest, strongest**; prevents **translation**; primary SL stabiliser |
| Volar | Prevents **rotation** |
| Proximal | No significant strength |

| LTIL part | Role |
|---|---|
| **Volar** | **Strongest** |
| Dorsal / proximal | Weaker |

**SLIL torn** — scaphoid flexes, lunate extends → **DISI**

**LTIL torn (+ dorsal radiotriquetral)** — lunate flexes with normal scaphoid → **VISI**; **volar LTIL** = most important contributor (TO)

**Isolated DRC or RSC injury (TO)** — no VISI

**Distal row** — trapeziotrapezoid, trapeziocapitate, capitohamate

**Palmar midcarpal** — STT, scaphocapitate, triquetrocapitate, triquetrohamate

### Six features of carpal instability (TO)

| Feature | Categories |
|---|---|
| **Chronicity** | **Acute 0–1 wk** · **subacute 1–6 wk** · **chronic > 6 wk** |
| Aetiology | Traumatic · inflammatory |
| Location | Radiocarpal · proximal row · midcarpal · distal intercarpal |
| **Severity** | **Predynamic** (pain, no malalignment) · **dynamic** (malalignment on stress views) · **static** (malalignment on plain X-ray) |
| Direction | DISI · VISI · dorsal · volar · radial / ulnar translation |
| **Pattern** | **CID** (SL, LT, scaphoid fracture, Kienböck) · **CIND** (radiocarpal, midcarpal) · **CIC** (perilunate, axial dislocations) |

| View (TO) | Purpose |
|---|---|
| PA neutral + lateral | Neutral confirmed by **clear 3rd CMC joint space** |
| **Clenched fist** | Load → SL gap (Terry Thomas) |
| PA with **10° angulation from ulna** | Mid-SL interval |
| Radial / ulnar deviation PA | Scaphoid flexion / extension |
| Flexion / extension lateral | Lunate tilt |
| **Stress (volar / dorsal drawer)** | **Midcarpal instability** |

### Secondary stabilisers against DISI – dorsal intercarpal ligament

**SL instability** — **most common carpal instability**; FOOSH

**Secondary SL stabilisers** — **DIC + DRC** (lateral V) → missed injury → persistent DISI → **SLAC**

**DIC** — assess and repair/reconstruct with SLIL surgery (Mitsuyasu)

**SLIL cut alone** — <2 mm translation; need SLIL + volar or dorsal ligaments for significant translation

## X vs Y

| | **DISI** | **VISI** |
|---|---|---|
| Intrinsic | **SLIL (dorsal)** | **LTIL (volar)** |
| Extrinsic needed | DIC / DRC secondary | **Dorsal radiotriquetral** |
| Lunate | Extended | Flexed |
| Test | Watson scaphoid shift | Ballottement / shuck |

| Ligament | Classic MCQ association |
|---|---|
| RSC | PRC preservation · ulnar translocation |
| Long RL | Madelung (Vickers) |
| Short RL | Critical corner · volar subluxation · stage IV perilunate |
| RSL (Testut) | Neurovascular conduit |
| Dorsal radiotriquetral | VISI with LTIL |
| DIC | Secondary DISI stabiliser |

## How it's asked

**"Volar extrinsic disrupted → ulnar translation after radiocarpal dislocation?"** → **RSC**\
✗ Wrong options: short RL, SLIL, radiotriquetral, RSL

**"Arthroscopic structures 1, 2, 3 from 3-4 portal?"** → **RSC, long RL, short RL**

**"RSC repaired — without surgery what deformity?"** → **ulnar translocation of carpus** (Figure D)\
✗ Wrong options: volar subluxation = short RL · SLIL · perilunate · Madelung

**"PRC — preserve which ligament?"** → **RSC**

**"Secondary stabiliser preventing DISI?"** → **dorsal intercarpal ligament**

**"VISI, positive ballottement — ligament?"** → **volar lunotriquetral interosseous** (TO)

**"RSL all except?"** → **provides mechanical strength** — false (TO)

**"Ligament abnormal in Madelung?"** → **long radiolunate (Vickers)**

**"Lunate escapes into?"** → **space of Poirier**

**"Radial deviation is mostly?"** → **midcarpal (90%)**

**"Extrinsic vs intrinsic wrist ligaments — mode of failure?"** → **extrinsic rupture midsubstance; intrinsic avulse** (TO)

**"Subacute carpal instability — time window?"** → **1–6 weeks** (TO)
