# Flexor Pulley System

*Hand · 33 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Pulley anatomy – annular/cruciform sequence, A2 & A4, thumb oblique pulley

**Finger sequence** — **A1, A2, C1, A3, C2, A4, C3** (prox → distal); 5 annular + 3 cruciate

**Joint pulleys** — **A1, A3, A5** → arise from **palmar plates** of MCP, PIP, DIP

**Bone pulleys** — **A2** = periosteum **proximal half of proximal phalanx** · **A4** = **midportion of middle phalanx**

**Most important** — **A2 + A4** → prevent **bowstringing**; minimum to preserve/reconstruct in flexor tendon surgery

**Ideal** — a pulley **proximal and distal to each joint**; preserve/reconstruct **≥3 pulleys**

**Cruciate (C1–C3)** — thin, collapsible; prevent sheath collapse/expansion; let annular pulleys **approximate in flexion**

**Trigger finger** — **A1** most commonly involved

![Finger pulleys](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6004-finger-pulleys-2.jpg)

### Thumb pulleys

**Thumb sequence** — **A1 – Av – oblique – A2** (2 annular + Av + oblique)

**Oblique pulley** — **most important in thumb**; analogue of finger C1 position; full FPL excursion, prevents bowstringing

**Oblique origin** — proximal half of proximal phalanx (Orthobullets) · ulnar side of PP base → radial side of DP base, 4 mm wide (Bayat, OB)

**A1 (thumb)** — at MCP volar plate; ~6 mm; **radial digital nerve closest (2.7 mm)** · ulnar 5.4 mm

**A2 (thumb)** — 5–10 mm (Orthobullets) · 8 mm (Bayat); contributes **least to arc of motion**

**Bowstringing rule** — if **A2 intact**, cutting **A1 OR oblique** alone → no bowstringing; cutting **both A1 + oblique** → **bowstringing**

**Restore kinematics (A2 intact)** — repair **oblique OR A1**

**Bayat caveat** — once **A1 + Av** both cut, oblique can no longer prevent bowstringing

## 2 Mechanism

### Pulley rupture – climbers, grading & sequelae

**Mechanism** — **extending force on flexed (crimped) digit**: **rock climbers**, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, baseball, lifting crimped; or laceration

**Climbers** — 75% get UE injury; **30–50%** of these = closed pulley injury

**Thumb pulley rupture** — rare; e.g. after **steroid injection** for FPL tenosynovitis → pain, clicking, ↓ ROM

**Sequential ruptures** needed for bowstringing; isolated rupture → early functional rehab

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Closed injury** — **MRI or US** to diagnose

**MRI sign** — **volar subluxation of flexor tendon (FPL) away from phalanx** (normally apposed)

**Clinical** — visible **bowstringing**; FPL intact (bowstringing needs tendon fixed at both ends → not FPL avulsion)

![Thumb FPL bowstringing after oblique pulley rupture](../../MCQ/attachments/q5681-a-fig-a.jpg)

### Schöffl classification

| Grade | Injury | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Pulley strain** | Nonop |
| II | **Complete rupture A4** or **partial A2/A3** | Nonop, **taping** |
| III | **Complete rupture A2 or A3** | Nonop |
| **IV** | **Multiple ruptures** (A2/A3, A2/A3/A4) or single A2/A3 + **lumbrical/collateral lig trauma** | **Surgical reconstruction** |

**Outcome (Schöffl)** — 92% nonop returned to previous climbing level

## 5 Management

```
Pulley injury
├─ Grade I–III → NONOP (taping, functional rehab)
└─ Grade IV → PULLEY RECONSTRUCTION (not repair)
       ├─ A2 (proximal phalanx) → 3 LOOPS around bone, DEEP to extensor
       └─ A4 (middle phalanx) → 2 LOOPS around bone, SUPERFICIAL to extensor
```

### Pulley reconstruction

**Goal** — prevent bowstringing → **flexion contracture** & ↓ ROM; preserve/reconstruct ≥3 pulleys, **A2 important**; A4 may be sacrificed acutely (unclear)

**Thumb** — reconstruct **oblique pulley alone** = enough (tendon graft)

**First step** — **excise all scar dorsal to flexor tendon**

**Graft** — **extensor retinaculum** (synovialised → **least gliding resistance**) · excised tendon · PL/plantaris · FDS · allograft

| Technique | Examples | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Around-the-bone (encircling)** | Bunnell single loop · **Okutsu triple loop** | **Biomechanically strongest** (triple loop) |
| Non-encircling | Kleinert ever-present rim · Karev belt loop · Lister extensor retinaculum · Doyle-Blythe PL through volar plate · Pulvertaft weave | Weaker |

**Hemostasis** — prevents adhesions; check excursion after reconstruction

![Okutsu triple loop](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6004-okutsu-triple-loop.jpg)

## 6 Complications

**Untreated grade IV** — **bowstringing → painful flexion contracture**

**Encircling reconstruction** — **phalangeal fracture** (most worrisome) · stiffness · persistent bowstringing (inadequate tension, **scar left dorsal to tendon**)

## X vs Y

| Effect | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Bowstringing → flexion contracture** | Neglected multi-pulley (A2–A4) injury |
| **Quadriga** | FDP **repaired short** → flexor lag of adjacent digits |
| **Lumbrical plus** | FDP injury **distal to lumbrical** → paradoxical IP extension on MCP flexion |

## How it's asked

**"Annular pulleys arising from palmar plates"** → **A1, A3, A5**

**"Proximal → distal sequence"** → **A1, A2, C1, A3, C2, A4, C3**

**"Two most important pulleys"** → **A2 and A4**

**"Thumb, A2 intact — repair which to restore kinematics?"** → **oblique or A1**

**"Thumb clicking after FPL steroid injection, bowstringing on MRI"** → **pulley rupture**\
✗ Wrong options: FPL avulsion, RCL, gout

**"Grade II pulley injury"** → **complete A4 rupture**\
✗ Wrong options: A2/A3 complete = III; multiple = IV

**"Laceration through A2–A4, tendons intact, not reconstructed — sequel?"** → **flexion contracture**\
✗ Wrong options: quadriga, lumbrical plus

**"Strongest reconstruction"** → **Okutsu triple loop**

**"Graft with least gliding resistance"** → **extensor retinaculum**
