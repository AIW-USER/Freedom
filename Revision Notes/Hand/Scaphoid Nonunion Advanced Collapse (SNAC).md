# Scaphoid Nonunion Advanced Collapse (SNAC)

*Hand · 81 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Scaphoid anatomy

**Main blood supply** — **dorsal carpal branch of radial a.** → dorsal nonarticular ridge → **proximal 80%, retrograde**

**Minor supply** — **superficial palmar arch branch** (volar radial) → distal tubercle → distal 20%

**Motion** — flexes with wrist flexion + radial deviation; extends with extension + ulnar deviation

## 2 Mechanism

### Natural history – radiolunate preserved

**Sequence** — **radioscaphoid (radial styloid) first** → scaphocapitate/midcarpal → pancarpal

**Radiolunate** — **preserved** until very end stage → **least likely** in natural progression

**Poor union predictors** — nonunion **>5 y**, **proximal pole necrosis**, heavy work, prior radial styloidectomy, immobilisation duration

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — ↓ grip & pinch, stiffness in **extension + radial deviation**

**Exam** — tender **radioscaphoid** joint; ↓ extension & radial deviation

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Views** — **AP + lateral wrist**

**Findings** — scaphoid nonunion + radioscaphoid arthritis progressing per stage; **capitolunate & lunate fossa preserved** until late

**Differential** — SLAC wrist, scaphoid nonunion without arthrosis

## 5 Management

```
SNAC wrist
├─ Frail / low demand → OBSERVATION
├─ Stage I → RADIAL STYLOIDECTOMY + scaphoid repair/SL stabilisation, or DISTAL POLE EXCISION
└─ Stage II–III → PRC / 4-CORNER FUSION / wrist arthrodesis
       ├─ capitate head arthritis → no PRC → 4-CORNER FUSION
       └─ capitate head intact → PRC or 4CF
```

**Union attempt** — only if **no arthrosis or stage I** (with styloidectomy); no arthroscopic debridement with arthrosis

### Salvage – four-corner fusion, PRC & distal pole excision (contraindications, RSC ligament)

| Procedure | Indication | Key facts / contraindications |
|---|---|---|
| **Distal pole excision** | Stage I (SNAC I–III not contraindications) | **Pre-existing DISI = contraindication** (↑ SL angle, destabilises proximal row); not for STT OA |
| **PRC** | Stage II–III | Needs intact **capitate head + lunate fossa**; **capitolunate/capitate head arthritis = contraindication**; ↓ motion & grip; **preserve RSC** |
| **Scaphoid excision + 4-corner fusion** (or capitolunate arthrodesis) | Stage II–III (incl. capitate arthritis) | Retains **60% motion, 80% grip** |
| Wrist arthrodesis | Stage II–III | Final salvage |

**RSC ligament** — most **volar-radial** ligament; radial styloid → carpus obliquely, radial to long & short RL ligaments → excision at PRC → **ulnar translocation of carpus**

![RSC ligament at PRC](../../MCQ/attachments/q211226-illa-rsc.jpg)

**62 y, stage III SNAC, failed splint/NSAIDs** → **scaphoid excision + 4-corner fusion**\
✗ Wrong options: debridement · ORIF · bone graft ± vascularised

## 6 Complications

**PRC + RSC excised** → **ulnar translocation**\
✗ Wrong options: capitate AVN (that is a contraindication, not complication) · DRUJ instability

**Distal pole excision** → ↑ SL angle / DISI, worsening arthritis in half (Malerich)

### Prognosis

**Punctate bleeding at surgery** — union **92%** obvious · **71%** questionable · **0%** none

## X vs Y

| | **PRC** | **4-corner fusion** |
|---|---|---|
| Needs capitate head cartilage | **Yes** | No |
| Stability depends on | **RSC ligament** | Fusion |
| Motion / grip | ↓ | **60% / 80%** |

## How it's asked

**"Untreated scaphoid nonunion — least likely arthritis?"** → **radiolunate**

**"Stage III SNAC, failed nonop?"** → **scaphoid excision + 4-corner fusion**

**"Motion-sparing salvage on capitate head; most volar-radial ligament excised — outcome?"** → **ulnar translocation of carpus**

**"Contraindication to distal pole excision?"** → **DISI**\
✗ Wrong options: SNAC I–III arthrosis

**"Arthritis between which bones contraindicates PRC?"** → **capitate & lunate (capitate head)**

**"Main blood supply of scaphoid?"** → **dorsal carpal branch of radial artery (retrograde, proximal 80%)**
