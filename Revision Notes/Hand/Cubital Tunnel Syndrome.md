# Cubital Tunnel Syndrome

*Hand · 18 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Frequency** — **2nd most common** upper-limb compressive neuropathy (after CTS); ~30/100,000/y

**Demographics** — **♂ > ♀** (♀ present younger); ↑ with age

**Associations** — cubitus varus/valgus, medial epicondylitis, burns, elbow contracture release

**Children** — rare; trauma or repetitive use; nonop works in up to 50%

### Anatomy – ulnar nerve course & cubital tunnel

**Ulnar nerve** — **medial cord (C8–T1)** → posteromedial to brachial artery → pierces IM septum at **arcade of Struthers 8 cm proximal** to medial epicondyle → behind medial epicondyle (cubital tunnel) → **between 2 heads of FCU** → between FCU & FDP → **superficial to TCL** at wrist

| Cubital tunnel | Structure |
|---|---|
| **Roof** | **FCU fascia + Osborne's ligament** (cubital tunnel retinaculum; medial epicondyle → olecranon) |
| **Floor** | **Posterior bundle (posterior oblique ligament) of MCL** + transverse band + capsule |
| Walls | Medial epicondyle + olecranon |

**✗ Floor distractors** — Osborne (= roof) · anterior/posterior band of anterior bundle UCL · transverse bundle

**MUCL bundles** — **anterior bundle** = primary valgus stabiliser (anterior band isometric; posterior band tight in flexion) · **posterior bundle** = greatest tension change, **tight in flexion**, **floor of tunnel** · transverse = no stability (doesn't cross joint)

### Compression sites (Osborne's ligament, anconeus epitrochlearis)

| Site (proximal → distal) | Note |
|---|---|
| **Arcade of Struthers** | Hiatus in medial IM septum, 8 cm proximal |
| Medial IM septum / medial head triceps | |
| Medial epicondyle | Nonunion, osteophyte |
| **Osborne's ligament–MCL** (cubital tunnel) | |
| **Anconeus epitrochlearis** | Accessory muscle medial epicondyle → olecranon, **replaces Osborne's** |
| **Two heads of FCU / aponeurosis** | = humeral head forms **ulnar border of flexor-pronator mass** |
| Deep flexor-pronator aponeurosis / FDS proximal edge | |

**Most common site** — **between 2 heads of FCU** (Orthobullets, OB) · **Osborne's ligament** (one OB explanation) — read the options

**Anconeus epitrochlearis** — ~**10% of population** · **13–30%** of surgically treated CuTS; static/dynamic compression; usually found at surgery → **excise + in situ decompression ± transposition**; intra-op: muscle over nerve posterior to medial epicondyle

![Anconeus epitrochlearis intra-op](../../MCQ/attachments/q219430-a-epitrochlearis.jpg)

**External causes** — fracture/medial epicondyle nonunion, osteophytes, **HO**, tumours, ganglia

| NOT ulnar nerve compression sites | Nerve |
|---|---|
| **Ligament of Struthers** (supracondylar process → medial epicondyle) | **Median** (pronator syndrome) |
| Supracondylar process (1%) | Median |
| **Lacertus fibrosus** | Median |
| **Arcade of Frohse** + FLEAS (fibrous bands over radial head, leash of Henry, ECRB edge, supinator) | **PIN / radial** |
| Ulnar artery thrombosis/aneurysm | Ulnar at **Guyon zone III** (no dorsal sensory loss) |

## 2 Mechanism

**Compression + traction** — elbow flexion ↑ tension; superficial nerve; **subluxation = ↑ traction**

**Exacerbating** — **phone use (flexion)**, repetitive flexion + valgus (throwers); **night symptoms** (sleeping flexed)

### Tardy ulnar nerve palsy (TO)

**Cause (TO)** — **nonunion of paediatric lateral condyle fracture → cubitus valgus → chronic stretch**; also medial epicondyle nonunion, **varus** malunion of supracondylar fracture, arthritis, cysts, recurrent dislocation, Monteggia

**Onset (TO)** — delayed; months to **up to 25 years** after injury; even minimal valgus can cause it

**Pathology (TO)** — tension neuritis + intraneural fibrosis; V-kinking, FCU fibrous band

**Treatment (TO)** — **surgical**: **anterior transposition** ± corrective osteotomy (osteotomy only if elbow complaint)

**Trap (TO)** — ulnar nerve supplies **adductor pollicis**, **not abductor pollicis brevis** (median)

## 3 Presentation

**Sensory** — paraesthesia **small + ulnar ring + ulnar dorsal hand**

**Motor (intrinsics)** — adductor pollicis, deep FPB, interossei, lumbricals 3–4 → **weak grasp** (MCP flexion) · **weak pinch** (up to 70% lost) · ↓ index abduction (1st DI)

**Inspection** — interosseous + **1st web atrophy**, ring/small **clawing**, **subluxation** over medial epicondyle on flexion–extension

### Clinical signs (Wartenberg, Froment)

| Sign | Finding | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Froment** | **Thumb IP flexion** during key pinch | **FPL (AIN/median)** compensates weak **adductor pollicis** |
| **Jeanne** | Thumb **MCP hyperextension** + adduction on key pinch | **EPL (radial)** compensates |
| **Wartenberg** | **Small finger abducted** | Weak **3rd palmar interosseous** + small lumbrical; **accessory slip of EDM (radial n.) to abductor digiti minimi tendon** |
| Masse | Flat palmar arch, lost ulnar hand elevation | Weak ODQ |
| **Pollock** | Can't flex DIP ring/small | Weak **FDP** (extrinsic → proximal lesion) |

![Froment sign](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6021-1-s2-0-s0749071211000904-gr3.jpg)

**Froment = which nerve?** — **ulnar** (cubital tunnel or Guyon's)

**Wartenberg wrong explanations** — ✗ Wrong options: tetanic ADM (ADM is ulnar too) · RCL insufficiency · unopposed FDP (→ clawing) · Martin-Gruber

**Wartenberg causes (TO)** — ulnar palsy (commonest) · **avulsion of 3rd volar interosseous insertion** · **cervical cord compression** · mild hemiparesis ("digiti quinti sign") · hemiplegic migraine \
✗ Wrong options: **median nerve palsy**

**Wartenberg (TO)** — unopposed EDM + EDC (radial) abduct small; more pronounced **in extension**

**Provocative** — **Tinel over cubital tunnel** · **elbow flexion test >60 s** · direct compression

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Diagnostic criteria – electrodiagnostics

**EMG/NCS** — diagnosis + **prognosis**; **NCV <50 m/s across elbow**; ↓ SNAP + CMAP amplitude

**Repeat EDS** — not when clinical diagnosis clear; only for new inconsistent symptoms

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray elbow** — look for **osteophyte / HO** (→ changes procedure: medial epicondylectomy), cubitus valgus/varus, lateral condyle nonunion

### McGowan & Dellon classification

| Type | Findings |
|---|---|
| 1 | Subjective sensory; no objective 2PD loss or atrophy |
| 2A | Sensory + **weak pinch/grip, no atrophy** |
| 2B | Sensory + **atrophy**, intrinsic strength ≤3 |
| 3 | **Profound atrophy** + sensory disturbance |

### Ulnar tunnel (Guyon's canal) compression

**Dorsal ulnar sensory branch** — arises **proximal** to Guyon → **dorsal hand spared** in Guyon/distal lesions

| Guyon zone | Content | Deficit |
|---|---|---|
| I (proximal to bifurcation; palmar carpal ligament) | Mixed | Sensory volar ring/small + intrinsic weakness; dorsal spared |
| II (deep branch, hook of hamate) | Motor | **Intrinsic weakness only** |
| **III (superficial branch, pisohamate ligament)** | Sensory | **Volar ring/small numbness, normal motor**; ulnar artery thrombosis/aneurysm (**cyclist**) |

## 5 Management

```
Cubital tunnel syndrome
├─ Mild → night extension splint 45° + NSAIDs + activity mod
│      └─ fails (~3 wk adequate trial) → surgery
├─ Stable nerve, idiopathic → in situ decompression
├─ Subluxing nerve in thrower / young / failed in situ / poor bed → anterior transposition
└─ Osteophyte/HO, thin patient with subluxation → medial epicondylectomy
```

### Nonoperative

**Regimen** — NSAIDs, activity modification, **night splint 45° extension, forearm neutral**; effective **~50%**

**Not indicated** — **daytime splinting** · **cortisone injection** (nerve risk) · continuing night splint another 6 wk in thrower

### Operative criteria

| Procedure | Indications |
|---|---|
| **In situ decompression** | Failed nonop; **before denervation**; idiopathic, stable nerve |
| **Anterior transposition** (subcut / intramuscular / submuscular) | **Failed in situ** · **throwing athlete** · **poor nerve bed (tumour, osteophyte, HO)** · **unstable/subluxing nerve** (snapping), esp. young · tardy palsy |
| **Medial epicondylectomy** | Visible symptomatic subluxing nerve · **thin patient** · **osteophyte/HO** on X-ray |

### In situ decompression vs anterior transposition

**In situ = transposition outcomes** — **no difference** (motor or sensory); in situ **fewer complications** → choice for idiopathic

**Subluxation** — not a contraindication to in situ (Keiner) · but **throwers/snapping nerve → transposition**

**Two "snaps"** — ulnar nerve + **medial triceps** dislocating → transposition

**Subcutaneous transposition** — not for idiopathic (↑ complications, equal outcomes)

**In situ technique** — 4 cm incision between olecranon + epicondyle; release **Osborne's + FCU fascia** distally, triceps–IM septum fascia proximally; **avoid circumferential dissection** (devascularisation, hypermobility); endoscopic option

**Transposition technique** — circumferential dissection, **excise medial IM septum**, secure with subcut tissue / fascial sling / within or under flexor-pronator mass

![Ulnar nerve transposition with fascial sling](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6021-ulnar-nerve-transposition.jpg)

**Medial epicondylectomy** — oblique osteotomy; **preserve MCL insertion** (risk medial instability)

### Prognosis

**Good** — **80–90%** good if intermittent + no denervation; **ulnar nerve instability (subluxation) in young = good prognostic factor**

**Poor** — **intrinsic muscle atrophy** (strongest)

**NCV** — returns to baseline within ~2 wk; distal latency recovers better with early decompression

## 6 Complications

### Medial antebrachial cutaneous nerve injury

**MABC** — crosses field **~3 cm (3.1) distal** to medial epicondyle; minor branches at/proximal → **painful neuroma**, posteromedial elbow pain, **↓ sensation ulnar (anteromedial) forearm**

**Medial brachial cutaneous** — crosses **7 cm proximal** to epicondyle

| ✗ Deficit distractor | Nerve |
|---|---|
| Can't flex thumb IP | AIN |
| Can't extend thumb IP | PIN |
| Can't hold fingers abducted | Ulnar |
| ↓ radial forearm sensation | Lateral antebrachial cutaneous |

**Recurrence** — inadequate decompression, scarring, tethering at IM septum/FCU; **higher than after CTR**

**Transposition** — risk of **new point of compression**

## X vs Y

| | **Cubital tunnel** | **Guyon's canal (ulnar tunnel)** |
|---|---|---|
| Clawing | **Less** (FDP also weak) | More |
| Dorsal ulnar hand sensation | **Lost** | **Spared** |
| Extrinsics (FDP ring/small, FCU) | **Weak** | Normal |
| Tinel / flexion test | **Elbow** | Wrist |

| | **Cubital tunnel** | **C8 radiculopathy** |
|---|---|---|
| Sensory | Ring + small | C8 dermatome |
| Motor | Ulnar intrinsics only | **+ APB, FPB, OP, lateral lumbricals** (median C8) |
| Shoulder abduction | — | **Relieves** symptoms |

## How it's asked

**"Thrower, medial elbow pain, small-finger paraesthesia — structure?"** → **anconeus epitrochlearis**\
✗ Wrong options: supracondylar process · ulnar artery · lacertus · arcade of Frohse

**"Ulnar nerve compression sites EXCEPT"** → **ligament of Struthers** (median)

**"Idiopathic CuTS failed splint/NSAIDs, no subluxation, no osteophyte — treatment & site?"** → **in situ release; FCU aponeurosis**

**"Floor of cubital tunnel?"** → **posterior bundle of UCL**\
✗ Wrong options: Osborne = roof

**"Froment sign + EMG compression proximal to wrist — release?"** → **border of flexor-pronator mass (FCU heads)**\
✗ Wrong options: FLEAS radial tunnel structures

**"Black-dot muscle over nerve behind epicondyle?"** → **anconeus epitrochlearis**

**"Wartenberg sign not caused by"** → **median nerve palsy** (TO)

**"Froment sign — nerve?"** → **ulnar**

**"Why is little finger abducted in ulnar palsy?"** → **accessory EDM slip to ADM tendon**

**"Surgery that minimises complications?"** → **simple in situ decompression**

**"In situ vs subcutaneous transposition with subluxation?"** → **no difference in outcome**

**"Good prognostic factor, adolescent gymnast?"** → **ulnar nerve subluxation**\
✗ Wrong options: atrophy = poor

**"Snapping medial elbow + Jeanne sign, ex-pitcher?"** → **decompression + anterior transposition**

**"Javelin thrower, two snaps, failed 6 wk splint?"** → **release + anterior transposition**\
✗ Wrong options: more splinting · daytime splint · cortisone

**"Painful paraesthesia near incision after transposition?"** → **medial antebrachial cutaneous** nerve

**"Nerve cut 3 cm distal to epicondyle — deficit?"** → **↓ ulnar forearm sensation**

**"Medial cord branch at risk 3 cm distal?"** → **MABC (label C)**

**"Cyclist, volar ring/small numbness, normal strength, dorsal spared?"** → **superficial ulnar branch at pisohamate ligament (zone III)**

**"False about tardy ulnar palsy nerve"** → **supplies APB** (TO)
