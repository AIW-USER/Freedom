# Extremity Flap Reconstruction

*Hand · 29 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Definitions & indications

**Flap** — tissue unit transferred **with its own vascular supply**

**Pedicle** — vascular portion; usually 1 artery + ≥1 veins

**Indication** — exposed **bone, tendon, cartilage, implant**\
✗ Wrong options: STSG/FTSG on exposed tendon/bone/prosthesis (no viable bed, poor gliding)

### Reconstructive ladder

| Rung (simple → complex) |
|---|
| Primary closure |
| Secondary closure / secondary intention (± NPWT) |
| Skin graft |
| **Local flap** |
| Regional / distant flap |
| Free tissue transfer |

**Rule** — choose the **lowest rung** that covers the defect (local before distant or free)

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Blood supply classification

| Type | Supply | Indication |
|---|---|---|
| **Axial pattern** | Single named AV pedicle | Length:width **>2:1** |
| **Random pattern** | Microcirculation, no named pedicle | Length:width **<2:1** |
| Venous | Veins as arterial inflow + outflow | |

### Tissue type classification

| Type | Note |
|---|---|
| Cutaneous | Skin + subcut |
| Fascial | Fascia only (temporoparietal) |
| **Muscle** | Needs **skin graft** or musculocutaneous (composite); **no motor nerve → atrophy to 50%** |
| Bone | **Free fibula (peroneal a.) → diaphysis** · **free iliac crest (deep circumflex iliac) → metaphysis** |
| Composite | e.g. radial forearm (fasciocutaneous) |

### Mobilisation classification

| Type | Use |
|---|---|
| **Local** | Tissue **adjacent** to defect |
| Distal random | Non-contiguous site; surrounding tissue inadequate; ratio <2:1 |
| Distal axial | Surrounding tissue inadequate; ratio >2:1 |
| **Free** | Local/distant tissue insufficient |

## 5 Management

```
Leg defect over tibia (by third)
├─ Proximal 1/3 / knee / TKA → gastrocnemius rotational flap
│      └─ medial (anteromedial, larger) or lateral (smaller)
├─ Middle 1/3 → soleus / hemisoleus flap
└─ Distal 1/3 → free flap (or reverse sural)
       └─ also proximal/middle if gastroc/soleus damaged
```

### Gastrocnemius flap – sural artery supply; knee & tibia coverage

**Pedicle** — **medial / lateral sural arteries** (branches of **popliteal**); single source 85%; **Mathes I**

**✗ Gastroc distractors** — genicular arteries · anterior/posterior tibial · peroneal · saphenous

**Medial head** — **medial + midline** defects, proximal 1/3 tibia; **preferred for anteromedial TKA wounds** (larger, more mobile)

**Lateral head** — **lateral** defects; smaller, less excursion; lateral sural artery

**Reach** — defects **up to 15 cm** from knee joint; fascial pie-crusting or proximal release (island) → **+5 cm**

**TKA wound necrosis / PJI with exposed prosthesis** — **gastrocnemius flap first**\
✗ Wrong options: latissimus/gracilis free (higher on ladder, donor morbidity) · STSG/FTSG

![Medial gastrocnemius flap](../../MCQ/attachments/q12-illa-medial-gastroc.jpg)

**Soleus supply** — PT (medial) + peroneal (proximal) + popliteal trunk branches (Orthobullets) · **peroneal proximally, PT distally** (OB) · **PT medially, peroneal distal/lateral** (OB) — read the options\
✗ Wrong options: anterior tibial perforators

**Free flap** — distal 1/3 tibia; **gracilis = most common free muscle donor** (**medial femoral circumflex a.**, anterior division obturator n.)

**ALT free flap** — **lateral femoral circumflex a.**\
✗ Wrong options: popliteal

### Upper-extremity flaps – lateral arm, radial forearm, latissimus & groin flaps (pedicles)

| Flap | Pedicle | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Lateral arm** | **Posterior radial collateral a.** (branch of **profunda brachii**), 3–5 septocutaneous perforators in lateral IM septum | Max width 8 cm, pedicle 8 cm; supine; primary donor closure; **posterior antebrachial cutaneous n. sacrificed** (forearm hypoaesthesia); protect **radial n.**; osteo/sensate/fascial variants |
| **Radial forearm** (rotational) | Radial a. | Local flap for dorsal hand/forearm when **PIA disrupted** by dorsal wound |
| Posterior interosseous | PIA (septum EDM–ECU) | not if dorsal wound disrupted septum |
| **Latissimus dorsi** myocutaneous | **Thoracodorsal a.** (branch of **subscapular**) | **Large defects around elbow**; Mathes V (TO) |
| **Groin** (axial, distant) | **Superficial circumflex iliac a.** | Mainstay UE coverage (thumb amputation); **LFCN at risk** (pierces iliac fascia medial to ASIS) |

**Radial collateral a.** — runs with radial n. in spiral groove → pierces lateral IM septum → anterior + **posterior** radial collateral (posterior to septum)

**✗ Lateral arm distractors** — brachial, recurrent interosseous, posterior ulnar recurrent, middle collateral, radial recurrent, anterior radial collateral

**Dorsal hand wound (MC + radius fx, exposed tendon/bone)** — **radial forearm rotational flap** (local) > groin (distant) > free lateral arm (free)

### Bone flaps – free fibula (peroneal artery)

**Free fibula** — **peroneal a.** (1.5–4 mm) + 2 venae; pedicle 4–6 cm; ± skin paddle; keep distal **5–6 cm** (syndesmosis); defects **>6 cm** (trauma, infection, congenital, AVN) → 10 cm radius defect

| Bone flap | Pedicle | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Free fibula** | **Peroneal** | Long diaphyseal defects |
| Free iliac crest | Deep circumflex iliac | Metaphyseal |
| **Medial femoral condyle** | **Longitudinal br. descending geniculate** | Scaphoid nonunion |
| Medial femoral trochlea | Transverse br. descending geniculate | Scaphoid proximal pole nonunion |
| Distal radius vascularised graft | **1,2-intercompartmental supraretinacular a.** (radial a.) | Scaphoid AVN/nonunion |
| Lateral arm osteo | PRCA | Small bone only |

**Vascularised bone nonunion** — up to **32%** without additional bone graft

### Moberg flap limitations in the thumb

**Moberg** — volar advancement; medium defects **1.8–3 cm**; >1.5 cm → ↑ dehiscence, **parrot-beak nail**, ↓ padding → modifications (V-Y, Z-plasty, Burrow triangles, **O'Brien island + graft**)

**3.5 cm pulp defect** — **Moberg contraindicated**: needs **IPJ flexion >45° → IP stiffness**

**Large thumb pulp options** — **Foucher / Holevich FDMA flap** · **Littler neurovascular island** · **free great toe pulp**

### Ray transposition after digit amputation

**Problem** — central ray loss → objects fall through gap + **scissoring**

| Ray amputated | Gap closure |
|---|---|
| **Middle** | **Index metacarpal transposition** (straight or step-cut; K-wire or plate) |
| **Ring** | **Little (small) metacarpal transposition** |
| Benign, ligaments intact | Suture deep transverse intermetacarpal ligaments / remove MC base fully |

(✗ Wrong options: **Traps** — ring→long transposition leaves new gap) · DTML suture impossible if resected with **tumour** · 2nd toe transfer = multiple digit / thumb loss · iliac crest graft = thumb reconstruction

![Index metacarpal transposition](../../MCQ/attachments/q4518-illa-transposition.jpg)

### Negative pressure wound therapy settings

**Most efficient pressure** — **125 mmHg** → 4× blood flow; granulation ↑63% continuous, **↑103% intermittent**; <50 or >250 → ↓ granulation

**NPWT ≠ coverage** — temporary; no infection reduction vs soft dressing (**WOLFF 2018**)

### Timing

**Free transfer** — **within 7 days** of severe UE trauma → ↓ complications (Orthobullets) · coverage **within 7 days of definitive fixation** (OB) — orthoplastic team

**Fasciocutaneous vs muscle flaps** — equivalent bone healing, salvage, infection

## 6 Complications

### Flap failure as infection predictor

**Strongest predictor of wound infection** — **flap failure**; 2nd = **time from definitive fixation to flap coverage**\
✗ Wrong options: age · temporary ex-fix, time from debridement weaker

| Failure | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Arterial insufficiency** | **Immediate return to OR** |
| **Venous congestion** | Loosen dressings, remove sutures → OR if unrelieved |

**Donor site** — cosmesis, graft pain, **seroma → aspirate** (excise if encapsulated)

## How it's asked

**"Type IIIB proximal 1/3 tibia — medial gastroc dominant supply?"** → **sural artery**\
✗ Wrong options: genicular · AT · PT · saphenous

**"Sural artery supplies which flap?"** → **gastrocnemius**\
✗ Wrong options: soleus

**"Lateral defect over revision TKA, exposed deep tissue?"** → **gastrocnemius flap**\
✗ Wrong options: lat dorsi/gracilis free · skin graft

**"Anteromedial TKA wound — label for dominant supply?"** → **medial sural artery**\
✗ Wrong options: popliteal · geniculates

**"Correct flap–artery pair?"** → **lateral gastroc – lateral sural**\
✗ Wrong options: hemisoleus–AT · medial gastroc–PT/peroneal · ALT–popliteal

**"Latissimus dorsi vascular pattern?"** → **single dominant + secondary segmental (Mathes V)** (TO)

**"Lateral arm flap perforators arise from?"** → **posterior radial collateral artery**

**"Dorsal hand wound, exposed tendon/bone, dorsal forearm wound — coverage?"** → **radial forearm rotational flap**\
✗ Wrong options: STSG · PIA flap · groin · free lateral arm

**"Groin flap artery & nerve at risk?"** → **superficial circumflex iliac a.; LFCN**

**"Latissimus flap for posterior elbow defect — artery?"** → **thoracodorsal**\
✗ Wrong options: subscapular · circumflex scapular

**"10 cm radius defect — anastomosis artery?"** → **peroneal** (free fibula)

**"3.5 cm thumb pulp — contraindicated flap?"** → **Moberg** (IP stiffness)

**"Middle ray resected with DTML — prevent scissoring?"** → **index metacarpal transposition**\
✗ Wrong options: ring transposition · ligament suture

**"Most efficient NPWT pressure?"** → **125 mmHg**

**"Strongest predictor of infection after flap for open tibia?"** → **flap failure**
