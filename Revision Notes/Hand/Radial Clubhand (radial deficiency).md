# Radial Clubhand (radial deficiency)

*Hand · 72 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition (TO)** — longitudinal **failure of formation** along pre-axial (radial) border; hand **perpendicular** to forearm + **absent thumb**

**Incidence** — 1 / 100,000 (Orthobullets) · 1 / 50,000–100,000 · 1 / 30,000–100,000 (OB)

**Sex** — ♂ predilection (**3:2**)

**Bilateral** — **50–72%** (Orthobullets) · **38–58%** (OB)

**Syndromic** — **30–44%** associated with syndromes

**Bilateral = usually syndromic · unilateral often idiopathic (TO)** · mesenchymal defect: radius, thumb, thenar muscles, radial vessels, **radial half of median nerve**, radial forearm muscles (TO)

### Aetiology – apical ectodermal ridge defect

**Embryology** — **defect of apical ectodermal ridge**\
✗ Wrong options: teratogen · AD · FGF excess (OB)

**Isolated RCH** — **spontaneous mutation** (most common cause; vs cleft hand, Madelung, syndactyly, symphalangism = AD)

**Teratogens (TO)** — **thalidomide, valproic acid, phenobarbital** (conflicts with OB "not linked to specific teratogens")

### Associated syndromes & work-up (TAR, Fanconi, Holt-Oram, VACTERL)

**Mandatory screen** — **CBC + renal US + echocardiogram**

**NOT associated** — **osteogenesis imperfecta**

| Syndrome | Key features | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **TAR** | **AR**; **thumb PRESENT**; bilateral absent radii; platelets <50/nL, **transient** (improves in 1st year); lower limbs involved; NICU | **CBC**; platelet transfusion |
| **Fanconi** | **AR**; **aplastic anaemia** at **6–9 y** (1st decade); **most common inherited aplastic anaemia**; **lethal**; ↑ malignancy (**AML**, solid tumours); café-au-lait, microcephaly, triangular face, short stature, abnormal kidneys, ↓ fertility (TO); **↑ MCV, ↓ platelets** (TO) | **CBC, smear, chromosomal breakage** (diepoxybutane / mitomycin C) → **bone marrow transplant** |
| **Holt-Oram** | **AD**, **TBX5**; **ASD/VSD** (75%); heart block/conduction disease; absent/triphalangeal thumb → phocomelia; **radioulnar synostosis**; abnormal carpal bone in **all**; clavicle/scapula abnormality (TO) | **Echocardiogram** |
| **VACTERL** | Vertebral, Anal, Cardiac, TE fistula, oEsophageal atresia, Renal, Limb (TO: + radial) | **Spine X-rays**, **bronchoscopy** (TEF) |
| VATER | Vertebral, Anal, TE fistula, oEsophageal atresia, Radial, Renal (TO) | — |
| Heart-hand II (Tabatznik) | AD; short thumb DP, **arrhythmias** (✗ Wrong options: malformation), no gene | — |

**Life-threatening AR condition** — **Fanconi** → chromosomal breakage before pancytopenia

## 3 Presentation

**Exam** — hand perpendicular to forearm, **absent thumb**, short forearm; **examine elbow** (flexion, biceps)

**Thumb predicts radius** — **thumb hypoplasia severity = greatest predictor of RLD severity** → Blauth V thumb → **Bayne-Klug IV (absent radius)** (Orthobullets) · **severity of radial deficiency does NOT correlate with thumb hypoplasia** (TO) — read the options

**Subtle (TO)** — mild hypoplastic thumb, missing/fused carpals · **severe** — total radial absence, short humerus, hypoplastic glenoid

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**X-ray** — radius partly/entirely absent, thumb often absent; ulna may be curved (TO)

**Carpal ossification** — **capitate 1–2 mo, lunate 2–4 mo** → no carpal ossification = <12 mo old

**TAR X-ray** — absent radius **with thumb**

![TAR: absent radius, thumb present](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6067-tar-xray-colorado-moved.jpg)

### Bayne & Klug classification

| Type | Orthobullets | TO | Modified (OB / TO table) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | — | — | Thumb absent/hypoplastic, **normal radius** |
| **I** | Deficient distal radial epiphysis | Radius **shorter than ulna**, distal physis present | **>2 mm short**; ± synostosis / radial head dislocation |
| **II** | Deficient distal + proximal epiphyses | Small, thin; **no growth plate** | **Hypoplasia proximal + distal** |
| **III** | **Present proximally** (partial aplasia) | Small radial segment only | **Absent distal physis** + proximal hypoplasia |
| **IV** | **Completely absent** (total aplasia) | Completely absent, ulna curved | **Absence proximal + distal** |

**Type IV** — **most common** (and most severe); Heikel type 4 most common (TO)

![Bayne & Klug](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6067-classification-def.jpg)

### Blauth thumb hypoplasia (TO)

| Type | Findings (TO) | Treatment (TO) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Minor generalised hypoplasia | **No surgery** / 1st web lengthening |
| II | **Absent thenar intrinsics**, narrow 1st web, **UCL insufficiency** | **Opponensplasty + UCL reconstruction** |
| IIIA | II + extrinsic abnormalities, **stable CMC** | **Opponensplasty + UCL reconstruction** |
| **IIIB** | **Unstable CMC** (Manske) | **Pollicization** |
| IIIC | Remnant MC head only (Buck-Gramcko) | Pollicization |
| IV | **Pouce flottant** (floating thumb) | **Pollicization** |
| V | **Aplasia** | **Pollicization** |

**Pollicization prerequisites (TO)** — **flexible, functional index finger** (side-to-side grip) · wrist oriented on forearm · **after 2 y**

## 5 Management

```
Radial clubhand
├─ Stiff elbow / no elbow flexion / biceps absent → OBSERVATION (radial deviation = reach to mouth)
├─ Mild (I–II) → STRETCHING + SPLINTING
└─ Severe (III–IV), good elbow flexion → CENTRALIZATION ± tendon transfers
       └─ thumb absent / IIIB–V → POLLICIZATION (later)
```

**Infant (TO)** — stretch & splint from early infancy; **above-elbow splint / serial casting 0–6 mo** · stretching + night splint 6–18 mo · operate **2–3 y** (TO)

**Avoid (TO)** — aggressive early surgery → **distal ulnar growth arrest**; address **fibrous anlage**

### Treatment – centralisation indications, contraindications & thumb reconstruction

**Centralization** — carpus on ulna → ↑ reach, wrist tendon balance, ± ↑ limb length

**Timing** — **6–12 mo** (Orthobullets, TO) · not before **12 mo** for anaesthesia safety (OB) · 2–3 y (TO age guide)

**Technique** — resect carpus, **shorten ECU**, ± ulnar angular osteotomy (**spare distal ulnar physis**); ± 2-stage with **distraction ex-fix**; then **tendon transfers**

**Current approach (TO)** — **bilobed flap + anlage resection + ulnar osteotomy + temporary longitudinal wire** carpus → ulna

**Others (TO)** — **radialization** (distraction, carpus on ulna, radial tendons transferred ulnarly), Ilizarov, **vascularised epiphyseal transfer** (MTPJ — OB)

**Thumb** — reconstruction ~**18 mo** (Orthobullets) · pollicization **after 2 y** (TO)

**Riordan casting (TO)** — first 6 mo · Ponseti-like · lift **2nd metacarpal**, ulnar-deviate with pressure over distal ulna · above-elbow casts

**Distraction ex-fix (TO)** — after 6 mo · uniplanar · **2 metacarpal + 2 ulnar pins** · **1 mm/day**

**Procedures (TO)** — **Buck-Gramcko tendon transfers** (milder) · **centralisation (Sayre 1893)** — ulna into carpal notch, overcorrect · **radialisation** — ulna under radial carpus, ↓ recurrence · **ulnarisation (Paley)** — **FCU transfer** augments wrist extension · **centralisation = stability · radialisation = mobility** (TO)

**Pollicisation (TO)** — ideal **12–18 mo** (lecture A) · up to **3 yr** (brain plasticity, lecture B) · after 2 y (existing TO) — read the options · shorten + **derotate 160°** + extend · **EIP → EPL · EDC → APL · 1st dorsal interosseous → APB · 1st palmar interosseous → adductor pollicis** · **index MP joint becomes thumb CMC** · above-elbow splint 4–5 wk, K-wires out 4–6 wk

**Adolescents (TO)** — wrist flexion needed → **no wrist fusion**; otherwise **fusion + partial correction**

### Operative criteria

| Centralization | Factor |
|---|---|
| ✓ Indication | **Good elbow flexion + biceps intact**, types III/IV |
| Contraindication | **Lack of elbow flexion** (<90°) → can't reach mouth |
| Contraindication | **Older child with good function** |
| Contraindication | Elbow extension contracture relying on radial deviation (Orthobullets) |
| Contraindication | Proximate terminal condition · severe soft-tissue contracture |
| Relative contraindication | **Age <12 mo** (OB) |
| Not contraindications | Cardiac defects, ↓ grip, **unstable carpus**, scoliosis, TEF, imperforate anus, hypoplastic thumb |

## How it's asked

**"Radial clubhand — associated EXCEPT"** → **osteogenesis imperfecta**

**"Tests to order"** → **CBC + renal US + echo**

**"Rule out AR lethal condition"** → **CBC, smear, chromosomal breakage (Fanconi)**

**"Absent radius, thumb present"** → **TAR**

**"ASD/VSD, TBX5, AD family history"** → **Holt-Oram**

**"Bilateral RLD + radioulnar synostosis + syndactyly — add which test?"** → **echocardiogram**

**"Congenital UL anomaly most often from spontaneous mutation"** → **radial clubhand**

**"Aetiology of isolated type IV"** → **apical ectodermal ridge defect**

**"Pollicization treatment of choice"** → **Blauth IIIB + flexible index finger** (TO)

**"Relative contraindication to centralization in infant (no carpal ossification)"** → **age**\
✗ Wrong options: limited elbow extension, cardiac defect

**"7-mo, good elbow flexion, has thumb — surgery?"** → **centralization ± tendon transfers**\
✗ Wrong options: pollicization · free fibula

**"Contraindication to centralization"** → **lack of elbow flexion**

**"Blauth V thumb — radius?"** → **absent proximal + distal (type IV)**

**"SHOX mutation"** → **Madelung** (TO)
