# Macrodactyly (local gigantism)

*Hand · 52 / 103*

## 1 Basics

### Epidemiology

**Definition** — **nonhereditary congenital digit enlargement**; all tissues (skin, subcut fat, **nerve, joint, bone**) (TO)

**Incidence** — very rare; no known risk factors

**Laterality** — **90% unilateral** (Orthobullets + TO)

**Multiple digits** — **70%** involve >1 digit; majority >1 digit (TO)

**Commonest digit** — **index** > long > thumb > ring > small; hand or foot

**Distribution** — follows **nerve territory**; **median nerve** commonest

### Genes & lab markers

**Genetics** — **somatic (post-zygotic) gain-of-function PIK3CA mutation**, mTOR pathway (TO) · "no genetic correlation known" (Orthobullets) — read the options

### Associations & growth pattern (lipofibromatous hamartoma)

**Lipofibromatous hamartoma of median nerve** — seen in **macrodactyly** (TO); **adult homolog**\
✗ Wrong options: syndactyly, polydactyly, idiopathic CTS

**Median nerve in carpal tunnel** — enlarged in majority → **significant CTS** (TO)

**Most common associations (TO)** — **neurofibromatosis**, hemangiomatosis, **congenital lipofibromatosis**

**Other associations** — **Proteus**, **Bannayan-Riley-Ruvalcaba**, **Maffucci**, **Ollier**, **Milroy** (Orthobullets + TO)

**Foot pattern (TO)** — overgrowth **greatest plantarly and distally** → convex sole, dorsiflexed toes\
✗ Wrong options: dorsal

**Bone involvement (TO)** — usually limited to **phalanges**

## 3 Presentation

**Symptoms** — asymmetry at birth or over time, pain, ↓ use, cosmesis; foot: pain, **shoe fitting** (TO)

**Exam** — thick **fibrofatty** tissue; ↓ ROM from soft-tissue constraint

## 4 Diagnosis & Classification

### Key radiology findings

**Radiographs** — biplanar hand views → **enlarged phalanges** ± joint malalignment, angled phalanges

**CT / MRI** — not typically needed; **angiography** only for surgical planning

![Radiograph: enlarged phalanges](../../Orthobullets/attachments/ob6080-f21-large.jpg)

### Functional classification

| Type | Feature | Prognosis |
|---|---|---|
| **Static** | Present at birth; grows **linearly** with other digits | Asymmetry **does not worsen** |
| **Progressive** | Less obvious at birth; **disproportionate growth** | Asymmetry **worsens** |

## 5 Management

```
Macrodactyly
├─ Mild, 1–2 digits, not progressive → observe
├─ Single digit, length issue → epiphysiodesis when = same-sex parent's digit
│      └─ circumferential overgrowth → debulking (often incl. digital nerve) at physeal closure
├─ Thumb / multiple digits / severe deformity → osteotomy + shortening
└─ Massive / non-reconstructable / severe recurrence → (ray) amputation
```

### Epiphysiodesis

**Timing** — when digit reaches **adult length of same-sex parent's same digit** (father for a boy) (TO + OB)\
✗ Wrong options: now · debulk now · ray amputation · osteotomy at maturity

**Level (TO)** — **proximal & middle phalanges** (± metacarpal) → stops **longitudinal** growth

**Most common approach** — epiphysiodesis for single digit

**Debulking (TO)** — for **circumferential** growth; removes enlarged digital nerve + skin/subcut; done **at time of physeal closure** unless massive

**Angulation (TO)** — osteotomy, can be at level of physeal closure → combining procedures ↓ number of operations

**Avoid nerve resection** — hypertrophic nerve resection fails to prevent overgrowth (Ishida)

**Avoid compression wrapping**

**Ray amputation (TO)** — massive bone + soft-tissue enlargement; **procedure of choice for severe recurrence** after debulking/reduction syndactyly; "often required" = ✓ true (TO)

**Foot options (TO)** — amputation, ray amputation, epiphyseal ablation, osteotomies, nerve stripping, defatting

**Post-op** — soft-tissue care, early ROM

## 6 Complications

**Complications** — **digital stiffness**, chronic pain / oedema; recurrence

## How it's asked

**"10-y boy with macrodactyly — advice?"** → **epiphysiodesis of PIP when finger = father's** (TO)

**"Best intervention for proportional finger?"** → **epiphysiodesis when finger reaches father's adult length**\
✗ Wrong options: nerve resection

**"All except — macrodactyly?"** → **max overgrowth dorsal & distal** (false — it is plantar & distal) (TO)

**"Lipofibromatous hamartoma of median nerve seen in?"** → **macrodactyly** (TO)

**"Commonest digit?"** → **index**

**"Severe recurrence after debulking?"** → **ray amputation** (TO)
