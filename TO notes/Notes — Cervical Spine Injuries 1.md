Target Ortho Quick Revision Notes – Cervical Spine Injuries
Faculty: Dr. C S Vishnu Prasath

### OVERVIEW
- Cervical spine injuries involve the region from C3 to C7 (subaxial cervical spine).
- Movement in this region: Flexion, extension, and lateral bending.
- Rotation occurs mainly at the atlantoaxial joint (50%), rest in subaxial cervical spine.
- Key aim: Evaluate stability, identify mechanism, and classify injury using SLIC (Subaxial Injury Classification). “Always ensure that the cervical X-ray includes up to C7–T1 before declaring it normal.” – Dr. C S Vishnu Prasath

RADIOLOGICAL EVALUATION
### X-ray Evaluation
- Lateral view must visualize C7–T1 junction.
- Evaluate four smooth lines for continuity:
  1. Anterior vertebral body line
  2. Posterior vertebral body line
  3. Spinolaminar line
  4. Posterior spinous process line
Any break = possible fracture or dislocation.
### Prevertebral Soft Tissue Thickness

| Level | Normal Limit |
|---|---|
| C2 | ≤7 mm |
| C7 | ≤20 mm |

- Increased thickness → suspect hematoma, abscess, or ligamentous injury.

CLASSIFICATION SYSTEMS
### Older Systems:
- Allen & Ferguson, Anderson, Harris – mechanism-based.
- Limitations: Lack of neurological component, difficult reproducibility.
Modern System – SLIC (Subaxial Injury Classification, Vaccaro et
al. 2007)
- Replaced older mechanism-based systems.
- Focuses on morphology, discoligamentous complex (DLC), and neurological status.
- Scores used to guide management decisions.

ROLE OF STEROIDS
### Historical (NASCIS Trials)
- Early studies claimed benefit within 3 hours (minor sensory improvement).
- Later studies failed to reproduce results.
### Current Consensus
- Not standard of care.
- May be used within 8 hrs in selected young, non-diabetic cases.
- Risks: Infection, myopathy, pneumonia. “Use steroids only after informed consent – they’re optional, not essential.” – Dr. C S Vishnu Prasath

### KEY EXAM & VIVA PEARLS
- Always check C7–T1 on X-ray.
- Four lines rule – discontinuity = instability.
- Prevertebral soft tissue: C2 ≤7 mm, C7 ≤20 mm.
- SLIC Components: Morphology, DLC, Neurology.
- Translation >3.5 mm or rotation >11° = Unstable.
- Central cord = most common, anterior cord = worst prognosis.
- Steroids not standard – only optional within 8 hrs.
