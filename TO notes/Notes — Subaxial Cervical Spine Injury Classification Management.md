Target Ortho Quick Revision Notes – Subaxial Cervical Spine Injury:
Classification & Management
Faculty: Dr. C S Vishnu Prasath

### OVERVIEW
- Subaxial cervical spine = C3–C7.
- Accounts for most flexion–extension movement in the cervical region.
- Common injury mechanisms: hyperflexion, hyperextension, axial compression, distraction, rotation/translation.
- Important for understanding SLIC (Subaxial Injury Classification) system, neurological grading, and management protocols. “If C7–T1 is not visible on X-ray, don’t assume the cervical spine is normal.” – Dr. C S Vishnu Prasath

RADIOGRAPHIC EVALUATION
### X-ray Evaluation
- Lateral X-ray should include C7–T1 junction.
- Draw four alignment lines:
  1. Anterior vertebral body line
  2. Posterior vertebral body line
  3. Spinolaminar line
  4. Posterior spinous process line
Breaks or interruptions = subluxation or dislocation.
### Prevertebral Soft Tissue Thickness

| Level | Normal Limit |
|---|---|
| C2 | ≤7 mm |
| C6–C7 | ≤20 mm |

- Thickening = possible hematoma, abscess, or ligament injury.

CLASSIFICATION SYSTEMS
### 1⃣ Allen & Ferguson (1982)
- Mechanism-based: Flexion, Compression, Extension, Distraction.
- Limitations: No neurology; poor reproducibility.
2⃣ SLIC (Subaxial Injury Classification – Vaccaro et al., 2007)
- Based on injury morphology, disco-ligamentous complex (DLC) integrity, and neurology.
- Replaced mechanism-based systems; validated for treatment decision-making.

### ROLE OF STEROIDS IN SPINAL CORD INJURY
- NASCIS II trial: Claimed benefit if given within 3 hrs (minor sensory improvement).
- Later trials: No functional benefit → not standard of care.
- Methylprednisolone only optional within 8 hrs for selected young, non-diabetic patients.
- Risks: Infection, myopathy, pneumonia. “Use only if there’s no alternative and after informed consent.” – Dr. C S Vishnu Prasath

### EXAM & VIVA PEARLS
- Subaxial = C3–C7.
- Four alignment lines – break = instability.
- Prevertebral soft tissue: C2 ≤7 mm, C6–C7 ≤20 mm.
- SLIC Components: Morphology, DLC, Neurology.
- Incomplete neurology → more points (3).
- Central cord syndrome = most common.
- Anterior cord = worst prognosis.
- Steroids not standard – only optional within 8 hrs.
- Translation >3.5 mm or rotation >11° → Unstable.
