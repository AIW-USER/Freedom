Target Ortho Quick Revision Notes – Doping (Part 1)
Faculty: Dr. Shikha Dhaundiyal

OVERVIEW
Definition:
Doping is the use of prohibited substances or methods by athletes to enhance
performance artificially, giving them an unfair advantage.
Dr. Shikha’s Quote:
“True performance comes from discipline and training — not from a syringe or
a pill.”
Authority:
Governed globally by the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA), which maintains the
Prohibited List updated annually.
WADA Objectives: 1. Promote fair play and athlete health.
2. Prevent unethical enhancement of performance.
3. Maintain the integrity of sports.
WADA Founded: 1999
HQ: Lausanne, Switzerland (moved to Montreal).
Indian Agency: NADA (National Anti-Doping Agency).

STIMULANTS
Common Examples: Amphetamine, Ephedrine, Caffeine (in excess), Cocaine,
Methylphenidate.
Effects: - ↑ Heart rate, BP, alertness. - ↓ Fatigue perception. - ↑ Aggression &
risk-taking.
Adverse Effects: - Anxiety, tremors, insomnia.
- Arrhythmias, stroke, addiction.
MCQ Tip: Ephedrine is banned when urine concentration >10 µg/mL.
Caffeine: - Removed from banned list (since 2004).
- Still monitored; performance enhancement >9 mg/kg can be penalized.

<!-- page 3 -->

NARCOTIC ANALGESICS
Examples: Morphine, Fentanyl, Methadone, Buprenorphine.
Purpose: Mask pain → allows athlete to perform despite injury.
Risks: - Dependency, respiratory depression, impaired coordination.
MCQ: Codeine may appear positive due to morphine metabolism —
confirm via ratio test.

ANABOLIC AGENTS
Definition: Substances that mimic testosterone to build muscle mass and strength.
Types: 1. Exogenous anabolic steroids – Synthetic analogues (e.g., Nandrolone,
Stanozolol).
2. Endogenous anabolic steroids – Naturally produced (e.g., Testosterone, DHEA).
Mechanism: - ↑ Protein synthesis → muscle hypertrophy.
- ↓ Recovery time.
- ↑ Aggressiveness (“roid rage”).
Side Effects: - Hepatotoxicity, infertility, gynecomastia, mood changes.
- Premature epiphyseal closure in adolescents.
Detection: Ratio of testosterone:epitestosterone (T:E) > 4:1 → suspicious.
MCQ Tip: Nandrolone (19-NorTestosterone) metabolites detectable for
up to 18 months.

DIURETICS & MASKING AGENTS
Examples: Furosemide, Acetazolamide, Spironolactone, Mannitol.
Purpose: - Rapid weight loss (boxing, wrestling).
- Dilute urine → hides other drugs.
Adverse Effects: Dehydration, cramps, arrhythmia.
Note: Even small doses → adverse analytical finding (AAF).

BETA-2 AGONISTS
Examples: Salbutamol, Terbutaline, Clenbuterol.
Mechanism: - Relax bronchial smooth muscle → better oxygenation.
- At high doses → anabolic effect.
Therapeutic Use: Asthma (TUE required).
Allowed Inhaled Limits: - Salbutamol: ≤1600 µg/24 hr
- Formoterol: ≤54 µg/24 hr
Side Effects: Tremor, tachycardia, hypokalemia.
MCQ: Salbutamol urine concentration >1000 ng/mL → presumed
abuse.

BETA-BLOCKERS
Examples: Propranolol, Atenolol, Metoprolol.
Banned In: Archery, Shooting, Golf, Billiards.
Mechanism: Block β-receptors → ↓ HR, ↓ tremors, calming effect.
Therapeutic Use: Hypertension, anxiety, essential tremor.
Risk: Bradycardia, fatigue, hypotension.
MCQ Tip: Propranolol banned only in precision sports (reduces hand
tremor).

### SANCTIONS
- 1st Offense: 2–4 years suspension.

- 2nd Offense: Lifetime ban (depending on severity).

- Support Staff Involvement: Coaches/doctors also penalized.
Common Defenses: - Contaminated supplement.
- Therapeutic use.
- Lab error.
MCQ: Under WADA Code, strict liability applies — athlete is responsible
regardless of intent.

### EXAM & VIVA PEARLS
- WADA formed: 1999; HQ Montreal.

- Indian equivalent: NADA.

- TUE: Allows medical use of banned drugs.

<!-- page 6 -->

- Commonly abused: Anabolic steroids, EPO, stimulants.

- Masking agents: Diuretics.

- Salbutamol: >1000 ng/mL urine = violation.

- T:E Ratio >4:1: Suggests exogenous testosterone.

- Propranolol: Banned in shooting/archery only.

- Nandrolone: Detectable up to 18 months.

### TAKE-HOME SUMMARY
- Doping undermines fairness, endangers health, and damages sport credibility.

- Substances banned for enhancing oxygen delivery, muscle growth, or masking.

- WADA/NADA ensure compliance and regular updates.

- Detection uses GC-MS, CIR, and biological passport.

- Education remains key to prevention. Dr. Shikha’s Closing Note: “If it comes from discipline, it’s strength. If it comes from a drug, it’s deceit.”
