OVERVIEW
Performance Enhancing Drugs (PEDs) are substances or methods used by athletes
to improve performance, strength, endurance, or recovery beyond natural capability.
Dr. Shikha’s Quote:
“Performance is earned, not injected. The moment you take a shortcut, you
lose the essence of sport.”
Definition – Ergogenic Aid:
Any substance, treatment, or technique that tends to increase work performance by
improving physiological variables or removing factors limiting performance.

### Types of Ergogenic Aids
1. Psychological – mental techniques improving focus and motivation.

2. Mechanical – equipment or clothing that enhances performance.

3. Pharmacological – drugs or supplements affecting physiology.

4. Physiological – techniques that alter body function (blood/gene doping).

5. Nutritional – diet or supplements improving energy or recovery.

PHARMACOLOGICAL ERGOGENIC AIDS
Categories: - Legal: Caffeine, Creatine Monohydrate.
- Banned: Amphetamines, Anabolic Steroids, GH, Ephedra, Beta-2 Agonists, Diuretics.

Over-the-Counter Drugs:
Caffeine, Nicotine, Melatonin, Amphetamine.

Recreational Drugs:
Alcohol, Marijuana, Cocaine.

Prescription Drugs:
Anabolic Steroids, Benzodiazepines, Beta-adrenergic agents.

CAFFEINE
Most widely consumed ergogenic drug in North America & Europe.

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| IOC Ban History | Banned 1962 → Removed 1972 |
| Ergogenic Dose | ~330 mg (≈4 cups strong coffee) |
| IOC Violation | Urinary caffeine >12 µg/mL (≈12 cups) |
| Timing | Taken 2–3 hrs before performance |
| Mechanism | CNS stimulant → ↓ fatigue, ↑ alertness |
| Side Effects | Tremors, palpitations, insomnia, GI upset, dehydration |

Not advised in hot & humid conditions – may impair heat tolerance.
MCQ Tip: IOC limit = 12 µg/mL urine; ~5–6 cups coffee ≈ 4 Vivarin tablets.
ALCOHOL
Category: Depressant with paradoxical ergogenic effects.

| Effect | Details |
|---|---|
| Low dose (BAC 0.02) | May ↑ balance, accuracy (dart throwing) |

High dose (BAC >0.05) ↓ coordination, reaction time, vision
Legal/Ethical Banned in modern pentathlon,
shooting, archery, diving, skating
Physiologic Effects ↑ HR, BP, blood lactate; impairs
hand-eye coordination

MCQ: Alcohol banned in sports demanding precision & coordination (archery,
shooting, pentathlon).

CREATINE MONOHYDRATE
Legal supplement improving performance & muscle recovery.

<!-- page 4 -->

| Mechanism | Details |
|---|---|
| Composition | 3 amino acids (Glycine, Arginine, Methionine) + 3 enzymes (AGAT, GAMT, MAT) |
| Transport | Via receptor Creatine Transporter 1 (CREAT1) |
| Effect | ↑ Strength & fat-free mass after ~12 weeks |
| Mechanism | ↑ ATP recycling, ↑ Ca²⁺ reuptake, faster actomyosin detachment |
| Training Requirement | Works best with resistance training |

### Dosage Protocol

| Phase | Dosage | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Loading | 20 g/day (4×5 g) | 5–7 days |
| Maintenance | 3–5 g/day | ≥12 weeks |

Side Effects: Muscle cramps, bloating → hydrate well.
Precheck: Urine R/M + KFT before starting supplementation.
MCQ: Creatine improves strength only after 12 weeks + resistance training.
ANABOLIC STEROIDS (AAS)
Type: Synthetic derivatives of testosterone
Effects: ↑ Protein synthesis, muscle mass, aggression.

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Mechanism | ↑ Protein synthesis, ↓ recovery time |
| Forms | Exogenous (synthetic) & Endogenous (natural) |
| T–E Ratio | >4:1 in urine → Doping offense (WADA) |
| Testing | IRMS (Isotope Ratio Mass Spectrometry) |
| Major Effects | ↑ Muscle mass, ↓ body fat, ↑ nitrogen retention |

<!-- page 5 -->

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Side Effects | Acne, hair loss, gynecomastia, liver dysfunction, mood swings, CV risk |

GROWTH HORMONE (GH / rHuGH)

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Structure | Polypeptide, 191 amino acids |
| Natural Isoforms rHuGH | 22 kDa (main), 20 kDa (10%) Only 22 kDa form → high 22:20 ratio indicates doping |
| Class (WADA) | S2 – Hormones & related substances |

### Physiologic Actions
- ↑ Lean body mass, ↓ fat.

- ↑ Protein synthesis (partitioning agent).

<!-- page 6 -->

- Therapeutic Dose: 1–2 IU/day SC every evening.

- Doping Dose: 10–25 IU, 3–4×/week → ↑ risk of acromegaly, DM, HTN.
Side Effects: Carpal tunnel, edema, arthralgia, cardiomyopathy, osteoporosis.
MCQ Tip: GH detection uses 22:20 ratio; serum GH alone not reliable.

Clenbuterol: Illegal β₂-agonist → ↑ muscle mass, ↓ fat (Type II fiber hypertrophy).
Adverse Effects: Tachycardia, tremors, nephrotoxicity.

### TAKE-HOME SUMMARY
- Ergogenic aids enhance performance via psychological, mechanical, or pharmacological means.

- Legal aids: Caffeine, creatine, altitude training, carbohydrate loading.

- Banned substances: AAS, GH, EPO, Diuretics, Clenbuterol.

- Detection: Urine/blood testing; ratio-based methods (T:E, 22:20).

- Ethics: Use of banned PEDs violates WADA/NADA code. Dr. Shikha’s Closing Note: “The true edge in sport isn’t chemical — it’s character.”
