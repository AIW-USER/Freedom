Faculty: Dr. Himanshu Champaneri

OVERVIEW
This lecture covers the Trigeminal (V), Facial (VII), and Hypoglossal (XII) Nerves,
focusing on their nuclei, course, branches, functional components, and clinical
relevance.
Dr. Himanshu’s Quote:
“Cranial nerves are not isolated wires — they are networks of reflexes,
sensations, and motor control that define the face we show to the world.”

### COURSE & GANGLION
- Emerges from pons (large sensory + small motor root).

- Forms the Trigeminal (Gasserian) Ganglion in Meckel’s Cave (petrous apex).

- Divides into 3 branches: Ophthalmic (V1), Maxillary (V2), Mandibular (V3).
Relations
- Lies over foramen lacerum (does not pass through).

- Related to cavernous sinus (medially).

### COURSE
1. Origin: Pontomedullary junction.

2. Internal acoustic meatus → Petrous temporal bone.

3. Forms Internal Genu around abducens nucleus (→ facial colliculus).

4. Forms Geniculate Ganglion (external genu).

5. Exits via stylomastoid foramen → passes through parotid gland (forms pes anserinus).
Relations
- Traverses near cochlea & middle ear cavity.

- Gives nerve to stapedius and chorda tympani (through petrotympanic fissure → joins lingual nerve).

### CLINICAL CORRELATION

| Lesion Site | Features |
|---|---|
| UMN (Supranuclear) | Contralateral paralysis of lower face only |
| LMN (Nuclear/Infranuclear) | Ipsilateral paralysis of entire face (Bell’s palsy) |
| At Geniculate Ganglion | Loss of taste, dry eyes, hyperacusis |
| At Stylomastoid Foramen | Pure facial palsy |

HYPOGLOSSAL NERVE (CN XII)
Type: Pure Motor (GSE)

### Nucleus & Origin
- Nucleus: In medulla, between pyramid & olive.

- Emerges as 10–12 rootlets → forms single trunk → exits via hypoglossal canal.
### Course & Relations
- Crosses lingual artery loop in the neck.

- Joined by C1 fibers → supply geniohyoid & thyrohyoid (via ansa cervicalis).

- Enters tongue → supplies intrinsic & extrinsic muscles (except palatoglossus → CN XI via X).
### Lesion Effects

| Lesion Type | Findings |
|---|---|
| LMN | Tongue deviates toward lesion, atrophy, fasciculations |
| UMN | Tongue deviates away from lesion, no atrophy |

### VIVA & EXAM PEARLS
- Trigeminal: 4 nuclei (chief, spinal, mesencephalic, motor); Gasserian ganglion in Meckel’s cave.

<!-- page 7 -->

- Corneal reflex: V1 afferent – VII efferent.

- Facial nerve: 4 nuclei; forms 2 genu (internal & external).

- Taste: Anterior 2/3 via VII; Posterior 1/3 via IX.

- Bell’s palsy: LMN lesion of VII → total ipsilateral facial paralysis.

- Hypoglossal: Supplies all tongue muscles except palatoglossus.

- Parasympathetic ganglia (4): Ciliary, Otic, Submandibular, Pterygopalatine.

### TAKE-HOME SUMMARY
- CN V: Sensory to face, motor to mastication, 4 nuclei, 3 divisions.

- CN VII: Mixed nerve, 4 nuclei, complex intraosseous course, key reflexes (blink, taste, salivation).

- CN XII: Pure motor to tongue, joined by C1 fibers for neck muscles. Dr. Himanshu’s Closing Remark: “Every facial movement, every blink, every smile is an orchestra led by these three nerves — know their anatomy, and you know human expression.”
