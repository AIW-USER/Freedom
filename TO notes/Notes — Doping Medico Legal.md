OVERVIEW
Medico-Legal Aspects of Doping involve understanding the legal, ethical, and
professional responsibilities of doctors, coaches, and athletes when it comes to the
prescription or use of prohibited substances.
Dr. Shikha’s Quote:
“Ignorance is not an excuse in doping. If you prescribe it, you’re responsible for
it.”

### WADA SANCTIONS

| Offense | Sanction |
|---|---|
| First Sanction | 4-year ban |
| Second Sanction | Lifetime ban |
| If Doctor Involved | Possible termination of medical license |

### Reduction or Elimination of Sanctions
- Unintentional Use: If athlete proves no intent or negligence → sanction reduced (0–4 years).

- Assistance Provided: If athlete helps expose others (doctor, coach, or co-athlete) → WADA can suspend or lift ban completely.

DOCTOR’S LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY
Key Roles Involving Medical Professionals: 1. As a Physician: Must verify whether
a prescribed drug is banned.
2. As a Psychiatrist: Must avoid causing drug dependency or addiction.
3. As a Toxicologist: Must identify and report narcotic misuse.
4. As a Forensic Pathologist: Determines cause of death in doping-related fatalities.

### Liability Scenarios

| Role | Liability Type |
|---|---|
| Team Physician | Under NADA, WADA, and Medical Council of India |
| Private | Under Medical Council only (not WADA/NADA) |

Practitioner

Penalties:
- 1st Offense: 4-year ban + ₹50,000 fine (doctor + athlete).
- If athlete consented: Not punishable by court of law, but still under Medical Council
ethics.

Coach asks team doctor to inject steroids for shoulder pain before a match.
Action: Refuse — punishable under NADA, WADA, and Indian Law.

NDPS ACT, 1985 (Narcotic Drugs & Psychotropic Substances
Act)
Purpose: Regulates production, possession, and consumption of narcotic or
psychotropic substances.

| Type | Punishment |
|---|---|
| Small quantity | Up to 1 year imprisonment or ₹10,000 fine |
| < Commercial but > Small | Up to 10 years + ₹1 lakh fine |
| Commercial quantity | 10–20 years + ₹1–2 lakh fine |

NDPS Act applies to both doctors and athletes found handling, prescribing,
or consuming banned narcotics.

NEGLIGENCE IN DOPING PRACTICE
### Section 304A: Death by Negligence
- Rash or negligent act not amounting to culpable homicide.

- Punishment: Up to 2 years imprisonment or fine or both.

- Non-bailable offense.
### Section 336–338: Negligence Endangering Life
- 336: Endangering life/safety → 3 months jail or ₹250 fine.

- 337: Causing hurt → 6 months jail or ₹500 fine.

- 338: Causing grievous hurt → 2 years jail or fine.

DISCRETIONARY FUNCTIONAL IMMUNITY
Definition: Doctors acting within professional duty & standard care are protected from
personal liability.

ETHICAL ASPECTS IN DOPING
“EPO is not dangerous; it’s the abuse that is.” – Dr. Michele Ferrari (1994)
### Core Medical Ethics for Physicians:
1. Refuse to prescribe or administer performance-enhancing drugs.

2. Educate athletes about harmful effects.

3. Obtain informed consent before any procedure.

4. Uphold confidentiality of athlete data.

5. Avoid involvement in “quick fix” performance enhancement.
### Violation of Medical Ethics During Anti-Doping:

| Ethical Breach | Example |
|---|---|
| Loss of Autonomy | Athletes legally compelled to provide samples |
| Breach of Privacy | Urine/blood samples collected under observation |
| Loss of Confidentiality | Results made public (no informed consent) |

increase oxygen capacity.

| Method | Description |

### ETHICAL CONTROVERSY: PRIVACY & AUTONOMY
- Athletes are obliged by law to give samples; no informed consent required.

- Public announcement of results violates confidentiality.

- Sample collection under observation violates personal privacy.

KEY QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

- Q: Can analysis of urine for doping be done by any lab? A: No. Only WADA-accredited labs.

- Q: Is an OTC supplement always safe? A: No. Many contain banned substances.

- Q: Can an injured athlete avoid testing? A: No. Must still submit whereabouts.

- Q: If banned in one sport, can athlete compete in another? A: No. Ban applies across all sports.

- Q: Can association with banned coach lead to sanction? A: Yes. Considered prohibited association.

### TAKE-HOME SUMMARY
- Doctors must know banned substances before prescribing.

- Athletes and physicians are equally liable for doping violations.

- Consent does not absolve liability under Medical Council ethics.

- NDPS Act & IPC sections apply to medical negligence and substance misuse.
