### MAJOR CATEGORIES OF DOPING SUBSTANCES
1. Anabolic Androgenic Steroids (AAS) – Muscle growth & recovery.

2. Stimulants (S6) – CNS activation, ↓ fatigue, ↑ alertness.

3. Blood Manipulation / EPO – ↑ Oxygen carrying capacity.

4. Peptide Hormones & Analogues – GH, GnRH, IGF-1.

ANABOLIC ANDROGENIC STEROIDS (S1)
### 1⃣ Boldenone (Equipoise / Parenabol)
- Used illegally in racehorses to enhance performance.

- Not FDA-approved for humans.
Case: Robert Farah (World No. 1 Tennis Doubles)
- Tested positive for Boldenone.
- Claimed inadvertent doping after eating contaminated beef injected with boldenone.
- Received a reduced sanction after investigation.

Modafinil
- Originally for narcolepsy, later abused for alertness, endurance, and cognitive
enhancement.
- Added to WADA Prohibited List (2004) under Stimulants – S6.
MCQ: Modafinil was banned after 6 athletes tested positive in the 2003 US Track &
Field Championships.

### 4⃣ Testosterone Gel
- Approved (2000) for male hypogonadism.

- Misused for anabolic effects & recovery enhancement.
Detection Markers: 1. T/E ratio > 4:1
2. 5α-androstane-3α,17β-diol / epitestosterone ratio
3. Confirmed via GC/C/IRMS testing.

### Urine Concentration Limits

| Substance | Limit |
|---|---|
| Cathine | >5 µg/mL |
| Ephedrine / Methylephedrine | >10 µg/mL |
| Pseudoephedrine | >150 µg/mL |
| Phenylpropanolamine | >25 mg dose |

Exceptions (Allowed Local Use):
- Clonidine, Bupropion, Caffeine, Nicotine, Phenylephrine, Oxymetazoline,
Xylometazoline.
MCQ Tip: Pseudoephedrine is prohibited if urine concentration >150 µg/mL.
### MELDONIUM (Mildronate / Quaterine)

| Type | Fatty acid oxidation inhibitor |
|---|---|
| Medical Use | Angina, Heart failure, MI |
| Mechanism | Alters carnitine metabolism, ↑ endurance |
| Ban Date | 1 Jan 2016 (Non-specified substance) |
| Threshold | >15 µg/mL = recent intake |

Case: Maria Sharapova – Tested positive (2016), banned for 2 years.
Meldonium improves endurance & recovery by increasing oxygen efficiency.
