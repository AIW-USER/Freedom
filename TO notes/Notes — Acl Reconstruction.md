Target Ortho Quick Revision Notes – ACL Reconstruction
Faculty: Dr. Mukul Mohindra

OVERVIEW
Anterior Cruciate Ligament (ACL) injuries are among the most common causes of
knee instability in active individuals. ACL Reconstruction (ACLR) aims to restore
stability, kinematics, and proprioception while preventing secondary meniscal or
chondral damage.
Quote: “Anatomical ACL reconstruction is not just about putting a graft — it’s
about reproducing what nature made.” – Dr. Mukul Mohindra

### ANATOMY
- Dense Type I collagen band.
- Length: 31 mm (range 29–35 mm)
- Width: 9 mm (range 7–11 mm)
- Blood supply: Middle genicular artery.
- Nerve supply: Posterior articular nerve (branch of tibial nerve).
- Proprioceptors: Golgi tendon–type mechanoreceptors at insertion sites.
Bundles:

| Bundle | Function |
|---|---|
| Anteromedial (AM) | Controls anterior translation of tibia under femur |
| Posterolateral (PL) | Controls rotational stability (pivoting, twisting) |

AM bundle = anterior stability; PL bundle = rotational control.

### FUNCTION
- Prevents anterior translation of tibia on femur.
- Provides rotational stability.
- Key stabilizer in pivot shift and cutting movements.

INJURY MECHANISM & RISK FACTORS
Mechanism:
- Tibia moves forward under femur (anterior translation) ± rotation.
- Often non-contact pivoting, deceleration, or twisting injury.

<!-- page 2 -->

### Predisposing Factors:
- Environmental: Hard surfaces, poor footwear.
- Anatomical: High Q-angle, genu valgum, increased tibial slope, narrow notch.
- Neuromuscular: Abnormal hamstring–quadriceps co-activation.
- Hormonal: Relaxin effect on ACL in females.
### Notch Width Index (Souryal & Freeman):
- Normal: 0.231; narrow notch predisposes to ACL tear.

### CLINICAL & RADIOLOGICAL FINDINGS
- Lachman Test: Most sensitive for ACL tear.
- Pivot Shift Test: Assesses anterolateral rotational instability.
- Anterior Drawer Test: Less sensitive (hamstring guarding).
- Empty Notch Sign (MRI): Absence of ACL fibers.

### WHY RECONSTRUCT THE ACL?
- Restores normal knee mechanics.
- Prevents instability & secondary meniscal/chondral injury.
- Improves proprioception.
- Decreases risk of osteoarthritis. OA develops in up to 3× higher frequency in ACL-deficient knees.
OA Compartment: Anteromedial (most affected).

### KARLSON PRINCIPLES OF ANATOMICAL ACLR
1. Restore native insertion sites.
2. Recreate two functional bundles (AM & PL).
3. Maintain appropriate graft tension.
4. Use correct graft diameter & type.

### TIMING OF SURGERY

| Timing | Indications / Risks |
|---|---|
| Early (<3 weeks) | Risk: Arthrofibrosis |
| Late (>1 year) | Risk: Meniscal & chondral damage |

Perform once inflammation subsides & ROM restored to avoid stiffness.

### GRAFT INCORPORATION STAGES

| Stage | Process | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Inflammatory | Partial graft necrosis | 3–6 weeks |
| Proliferative | Neovascularization | 6–8 weeks |
| Matrix Synthesis | Collagen formation | 8–12 weeks |
| Remodeling | Tendon–bone healing | 6–9 months |

### FIXATION METHODS

| Type | Mechanism | Example / Site |
|---|---|---|
| Aperture fixation | At tunnel opening | Interference screws (Bio/HA/Titanium) |
| Suspensory fixation | Remote from joint | Buttons / Suture discs (EndoButton) |

Femoral side: Suspensory fixation preferred.
Tibial side: Aperture fixation preferred (reduces tunnel widening).

SURGICAL PRINCIPLES
### Tibial Tunnel Placement:
- 15 mm posterior to anterior tibial edge.
- 7 mm anterior to PCL.
- In line with posterior horn of lateral meniscus.
### Femoral Tunnel Placement:
- Centered between AM & PL footprints.
- Transportal technique = more anatomic (vs. transtibial).
Fixation:
- Graft fixed at 30° knee flexion.
- Avoid anterior tunnel placement → impingement & limited ROM.

### SPECIAL TECHNIQUES & ADVANCEMENTS
1. Remnant Preservation (SAMBBA Concept)
- Preserves native ACL stump for proprioception & biological healing.
2. All-Inside ACLR
- Closed-socket tunnels, dual suspensory fixation, smaller incisions.
- ↓ pain, ↓ bone removal, faster rehab.

<!-- page 5 -->

3. Double Bundle ACLR
- Separate AM & PL grafts.
- Improves rotational stability & reduces pivot shift.
- Man behind Double Bundle: Friedi Fu.
4. Internal Brace Augmentation
- Fiber tape adds protection during graft remodeling phase.
- ↓ dynamic elongation by 50%; ↑ peak load by 64%.

### HEALING AUGMENTATION (PRP / BRACING)
- PRP gel accelerates graft incorporation on MRI (~179 days vs. 369 days).
- No major clinical outcome difference proven yet.

### COMPLICATIONS

| Category | Common Causes |
|---|---|
| Infection | Graft contamination (use chlorhexidine soak) |
| Recurrent instability | Vertical tunnels, missed injuries, tension errors |
| Loss of motion | Cyclops lesion, anterior tunnel, arthrofibrosis |
| Extensor dysfunction | BTB graft harvest issues |
| Hardware problems | Screw back-out, tunnel widening |

Cyclops lesion: Fibrous nodule anterior to graft → loss of extension.

### FAILURE MECHANISMS
- Re-rupture: Fresh trauma / malalignment.
- Tensioning errors / fixation failure.
- Arthrofibrosis: Loss of >10° extension or >25° flexion.
- Nutcracker knee: Anterior tibial tunnel impingement → extension loss.

REHABILITATION & RETURN TO SPORT
### Key Guidelines:
- Avoid early open-chain quadriceps isotonic exercises.
- Begin ROM & closed-chain strengthening early.
### Return-to-Play Criteria:

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Time since surgery | >8–12 months |

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Isokinetic quadriceps strength | ≥85% of contralateral |
| One-leg hop test | ≥85% of contralateral |
| Vertical jump / Drop test | No dynamic valgus |
| Lachman & Pivot shift | Negative / Grade I |
| No effusion or pain | – |

### EXAM PEARLS & TAKEAWAYS
- Anteromedial bundle: anterior stability; PL bundle: rotational.
- Anteromedial compartment OA post-ACL injury.
- BTB autograft – gold standard in athletes.
- Weakest graft period: 8–12 weeks post-op.
- Femoral fixation: Suspensory; Tibial: Aperture.
- Cyclops lesion → loss of extension.
- Double bundle ACLR – better rotational control.
- Friedi Fu – key name in double bundle concept.
- Return to play: ≥8–12 months + 85% strength recovery.
