OVERVIEW
Definition:
Early Onset Scoliosis (EOS) refers to any spinal deformity developing before 10
years of age.
Includes idiopathic, congenital, syndromic, and neuromuscular scoliosis.
Importance:
EOS directly affects spinal growth, thoracic development, and lung capacity.
Untreated cases can lead to restrictive lung disease and severe deformities.

### PATHOPHYSIOLOGY
- Deformity develops early due to asymmetrical vertebral growth or intrauterine malposition.
- Infants have a natural right-sided turning tendency → mild left thoracic curve.
- Early onset deformities affect thoracic cage volume → restrictive lung disease.

### ETIOLOGICAL FACTORS
1. Mechanical: Intrauterine crowding, oligohydramnios, abnormal posture.
2. Genetic: Chromosomal & connective tissue anomalies.
3. Environmental: Maternal diabetes, CO exposure, valproate use, hyperthermia.
4. Postnatal: Recurrent respiratory infections, neuromuscular imbalance.

CLINICAL EVALUATION
History
- Antenatal scan: vertebral anomalies.
- Maternal exposure: teratogens, diabetes, CO, infection.
- Family history of scoliosis.
Examination
- Head-to-toe inspection for neurocutaneous markers.
- Neurologic exam: Reflexes, tone, abdominal reflex, power.
- Chest wall: Deformity, symmetry, respiratory pattern.
- Systemic evaluation: Cardiac, renal, neurological.
### Common Associated Anomalies

|---|---|---|

| Cardiac | 25% | 2D Echo |
| Neurological | 10–35% | MRI spine |

THORACIC ASSESSMENT
Goals Before Non-Growth-Friendly Surgery:

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Weight | >40 kg |
| Vital Capacity | >50% |
| Thoracic Spine Length | 22 cm |
| T1–S1 Height | 30 cm |

- 50% lung development achieved by 10 years.
- Thoracic spine height progression: 11 cm (birth) → 18 cm (5 yrs) → 22 cm (10 yrs).

### RADIOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT
- Cobb’s angle: Measure curve magnitude.

- RVAD (Rib–Vertebral Angle Difference) – Mehta’s Index
 20° → likely to progress → needs intervention.
  - <20° → observe.

### Radiologic Growth Assessment:
- Hand X-ray for Sanders staging (growth potential).
- Olecranon apophysis & phalangeal fusion stages.
- CT chest to assess thoracic volume.
- MRI spine – mandatory before surgery.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES
Goals:
- Maintain spinal & thoracic growth.
- Prevent curve progression.
- Maintain pulmonary function.
- Minimize number of fusions.
### 1⃣ Observation

| Indication | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Mild deformity | Cobb’s <25°, RVAD <20° |
| Follow-up | Every 6 months (X-ray) |
| Intervention threshold | Progression >5° |

### 2⃣ Casting (Mehta EDF Technique)
- Indicated for Cobb’s >35° or RVAD >20°.
- Start as early as 1 year, under GA for muscle relaxation.
- Technique: Elongation–Derotation–Flexion (EDF) casting.
- Limited for apices above T9.
### 3⃣ Bracing

| Type | Best For | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Milwaukee | Curves above T8 | Full body brace (neck to pelvis) |
| Boston (TLSO) | Curves below T8 | Daytime brace |
| Charleston | Nighttime brace | Used post-casting |

THORACIC INSUFFICIENCY SYNDROME (TIS)
Definition: Inability of the thorax to support normal respiration or lung growth.
Classification:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Absent ribs + scoliosis |
| 2 | Fused ribs + scoliosis |
| 3A | Shortened thorax (Jarcho-Levin) |
| 3B | Transversely narrowed thorax (Jeune syndrome) |

Treatment:
- VEPTR (Vertical Expandable Prosthetic Titanium Rib): Expands thoracic cavity.

<!-- page 7 -->

- Attachments: Rib–Rib / Rib–Spine / Rib–Pelvis.
  - High complication rate → not routine.

### COMPLICATIONS
- Skin breakdown, implant back-out.
- Flat-back syndrome (overcorrection).
- Fusion loss, metallosis (MCGR).
- Lung injury (VEPTR).
- Curve progression in syndromic cases (NF, Marfan, EDS).

### VIVA & EXAM PEARLS
- Definition: EOS = scoliosis <10 yrs (GSSG).
- Most common congenital defect: Hemivertebra.
- Most progressive: Unilateral unsegmented bar + contralateral hemivertebra.
- RVAD >20°: Likely to progress.
- EDF Casting: Best for <T9 curves.
- Growth-friendly techniques: Used till 10 yrs or thoracic height ≥22 cm.
- Avoid early fusion: Wait till vital capacity ≥50%.

### TAKE-HOME SUMMARY
- EOS = spinal deformity <10 yrs, affects lung and spine growth.
- Always evaluate C–M–S–I causes.
- MRI, renal USG, 2D echo are mandatory before any intervention.
- Observation → Casting → Bracing → Growth-friendly → Fusion.
- Thoracic Insufficiency Syndrome needs special attention (VEPTR).
- Multidisciplinary management is essential for best outcomes.
