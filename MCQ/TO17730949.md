## Question

Surgical indication in scheuermann’s disease is (spine- scheuermann’s disease)

## Options

1. Kyphotic curve more than 70 degree
2. Pain and rigid deformity
3. Unacceptable cosmetic appearance
4. All of the above

## Correct option

**4. All of the above**

## Answer explanation

Surgery is rarely indicated in patients with Scheuermann's disease. Indications for surgery are spinal pain not relieved by conservative management, kyphotic curvature more than 70-75 degrees and unacceptable cosmetic appearance.

 Answer: D
