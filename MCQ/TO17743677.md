Chapter: FNB 2019 — MCQ 2019

## Question

True regarding PCL is (arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- PCL)

## Options

1. Intra Articular extrasynovial
2. Intra Articular intrasynovial
3. Intrasynovial and extracapsular
4. Intrasynovial intracapsular

## Correct option

**1. Intra Articular extrasynovial**

## Answer explanation

- Collaterals are Extra articular and Extra synovial

- Cruciates are Intra articular but Extra Synovial (Extra synovial because they don’t derive their nutrition from synovial fluid as they hv their own blood supply)

- Menisci are Intra articular and Intra synovial (Intra synovial as they derive their nutrition from synovial fluid)

Answer: 1
