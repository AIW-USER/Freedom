## Question

Which does not form extraosseous blood supply to talus? (general orthopedics- anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. Branches from posterior tibial artery
2. Peroneal artery
3. Anterior tibial artery
4. Artery of tarsal canal

## Correct option

**4. Artery of tarsal canal**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: artery of the tarsal canal mainly forms intraosseous blood supply to the talar body.

Answer: 4
