## Question

Shown below is MRI of LS spine. The likely end result of this etiology would be

![[TO17906143-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Bony ankylosis
2. Fibrous ankylosis
3. Non union
4. Pathological fracture

## Correct option

**1. Bony ankylosis**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

MRI is showing changes of tuberculosis. The L1 and L2 vertebral bodies are showing intensity changes due to bone marrow edema and inflammation. The arrows are marking the subligamentous soft tissue spread of abscess.

Now, the end result of Tuberculosis that involves joints mostly tends to be a fibrous ankylosis. However, in spine the end result is mostly Bony ankylosis for the reasons cited below: -

- The vertebral column is a weight bearing structure and loading encourages development of bony trabeculae leading to bony ankylosis
- The degeneration of disc leads to cancellous vertebral ends with large surface area opposing each other which further promotes the bony union.
