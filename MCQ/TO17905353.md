## Question

Vo2 Max should be measured in [Exercise Physiology]

## Options

1. l/kg/min
2. ml/kg/min
3. ml/g/min
4. ml/ kg/hr

## Correct option

**2. ml/kg/min**

## Answer explanation

answer-b

VO2 max (also maximal oxygen consumption, maximal oxygen uptake, peak oxygen uptake, or maximal aerobic capacity) is Maximal aerobic capacity. It refers to the maximum amount of oxygen that an individual can take in and use during intense or maximal exercise. VO2 max is typically measured in milliliters of oxygen consumed in a minute per kilogram of body weight (mL/kg/min). Having a higher VO2 max number typically means you’re in good cardiovascular shape, but you could also increase your VO2 max by losing body fat.

There are various methods to measure the aerobic capacity

- Direct methods
- Indirect methods.

The direct methods include using the treadmill, ergometer, and step tests, while the indirect methods include charts and formulas of Astrand and physiological (e.g., heart rate [HR]) and subjective (e.g., rating of perceived exertion [RPE]) variables. Direct methods are more accurate but more expensive which need training technicians for setting up and using equipments while time consuming; the indirect methods (heart beat (HR) as an indirect method for assessing the aerobic capacity) are useful and effective for assessing aerobic capacity in the industries as to have no such limitations.

Calculation of VO2max:-

To perform the test based on the Astrand protocol, worker with light clothing pedals the ergometer for 6 minutes to reach his HR over 120 beats per minute then, 25 watt per minute is added to the workload (heaviness of pedal) as well as, at the end of each stage, the HR using the sports tester and the RPE (according to the Borg scale) are measured during the ending 15 seconds. The borg scale starts from number 6 to 20, and each is labeled with its own individual's RPE and physical activity.

Finally, HR and the Borg scale relative to 6-20 RPE will be determined for each subject. Next, VO2 max will be calculated in ml.kg-1.min-1 using ACSM formula.
