## Question

Spinal manifestations of neurofibromatosis include all except (general orthopedics- orthopedic manifestations of different diseases)

## Options

1. Dural ectasia
2. Dumbbell lesions
3. Vertebral scalloping
4. Hemivertebrae

## Correct option

**4. Hemivertebrae**

## Answer explanation

Neurofibromatosis: NF1 (von Recklinghaussen disease) and NF2. NF2 is associated with bilateral vestibular schwannomas

Orthopedic manifestations of NF1: anterolateral bowing of tibia due to pseudoarthrosis, radial bowing, sphenoid dysplasia, thinning of long bone cortex with or without pseudoarthrosis and scoliosis. X ray may show vertebral scalloping, enlarged foramina and penciling of ribs. MRI of the spine may show dural ectasia and dumbbell lesions (neurofibroma at nerve ends).

Criteria for diagnosis of NF1 (2 or more should be met for diagnosis)

-six or more café-au-lait macules over 5 mm in greatest diameter in prepubertal individuals and over 15 mm in postpubertal individuals.

-two or more neurofibromas of any type or one plexiform neurofibroma.

-freckling in the axillary or inguinal region.

-optic glioma.

-two or more Lisch nodules (iris hamartomas).

-a distinctive osseous lesion such as sphenoid dysplasia or thinning of the long bone cortex with or without pseudarthrosis.

-a first-degree relative (parent, sibling, or offspring) with NF-1 by the above criteria.

 Answer: D
