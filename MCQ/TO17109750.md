Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Total Shoulder Arthroplasty

## Question

Total shoulder arthroplasty, absolute contraindication [FNB 2022]

## Options

1. Upper brachial plexus palsy
2. Supraspinatous deficiency
3. Poor glenoid bone stock
4. Retroverted glenoid

## Correct option

**1. Upper brachial plexus palsy**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

In glenoid deficiency one can build up glenoid with graft and retroverted glenoid can be tackled with eccentric reaming. Isolated Supraspinatous deficiency is also a relative contraindication only (risk of glenoid loosening is high, so hemiarthroplasty or reverse shoulder is preferred).

Also know, common complications associated with various types of Shoulder replacements: -

HEMIARTHROPLASTY SHOULDER

Most common complication- Overall progressive glenoid erosion but if done for fracture case then tuberosity fragment displacement

Most common cause of failure and subsequent revision- Glenoid erosion

TOTAL SHOULDER ARTHROPLASTY

Most common complication- Glenoid component loosening

Most common cause of failure and subsequent revision- Glenoid component loosening

Most common nerve injury- Axillary (Neuropraxia)

REVERSE SHOULDER ARTHROPLASTY

Most common complication- Scapular notching > Instability/ Dislocation (mechanism is Extension, Internal rotation and Adduction as in getting up from a chair)

Most common cause of failure and subsequent revision- Glenoid component loosening

Most common nerve injury- Axillary (Neuropraxia)
