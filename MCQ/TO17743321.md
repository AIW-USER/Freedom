## Question

Recommended treatment after 1st patellar dislocation is( Regional orthopedics- knee- patellofemoral instability)

## Options

1. MPFL reconstruction
2. Conservative
3. Operative only in case of osteochondral fracture
4. MPFL and retinaculum repair

## Correct option

**3. Operative only in case of osteochondral fracture**

## Answer explanation

Non-operative treatment is indicated after a first-time patellar dislocation in a vast majority of patients. Surgical treatment is indicated primarily in case of relevant concomitant injuries such as osteochondral fractures, and secondarily for recurrent dislocations

Answer: 3
