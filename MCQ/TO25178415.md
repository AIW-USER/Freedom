## Question

Fosfomycin (often referred to as Phosphomycin) is an increasingly popular "off-label" addition to bone cement. Which option does not appropriately describes property of fosfomycin in bone cement?

## Options

1. It is highly thermostable and easily survives the exothermic polymerization process of bone cement.
2. It dramatically reduces the elution rate of the accompanying antibiotic ( gentamycin or vancomycin)
3. On its own, Fosfomycin has an excellent elution profile, with a high initial burst followed by a sustained release that can last for several weeks.
4. As the Fosfomycin dissolves out of the cement, it leaves behind large micro-voids causing mechanical degradation of the cement.

## Correct option

**2. It dramatically reduces the elution rate of the accompanying antibiotic ( gentamycin or vancomycin)**

## Answer explanation

Fosfomycin (often referred to as Phosphomycin) is an increasingly popular "off-label" addition to bone cement, especially in Europe and parts of Asia, due to its unique chemical properties and its activity against multi-drug resistant (MDR) organisms.

In the context of PMMA, it is regarded as a powerful synergistic agent.

1. Antibacterial Spectrum

Fosfomycin has a very broad spectrum of activity, including:

 - Gram-positives: Highly effective against MRSA, MRSE, and Vancomycin-resistant Enterococci (VRE).

 - Gram-negatives: Active against ESBL-producing Enterobacteriaceae and some strains of Pseudomonas aeruginosa.

 - Biofilm Activity: One of its most valuable traits in orthopedics is its ability to penetrate biofilms better than many standard antibiotics.

2. Properties in Bone Cement (PMMA)

Fosfomycin is unique because of how it interacts with the cement matrix and other antibiotics.

 - Thermal Stability: It is highly thermostable and easily survives the exothermic polymerization process of bone cement.

 - The "Synergy King":
 Fosfomycin is most famous for the "Spacer Effect." When Fosfomycin is combined with another antibiotic (like Gentamicin or Vancomycin), it significantly increases the porosity of the cement as it elutes. This dramatically "boosts" the elution rate of the accompanying antibiotic.

 - Elution Profile: On its own, Fosfomycin has an excellent elution profile, with a high initial burst followed by a sustained release that can last for several weeks.

3. Impact on Mechanical Strength

While it elutes well, Fosfomycin has a more significant impact on the mechanical properties of cement than Gentamicin.

 - Mechanical Degradation: Because it is highly soluble, as the Fosfomycin dissolves out of the cement, it leaves behind large micro-voids.

 - Clinical Application: For this reason, high-dose Fosfomycin (e.g., >2g per 40g bag) is generally restricted to temporary antibiotic spacers and is not recommended for permanent, load-bearing fixation.
