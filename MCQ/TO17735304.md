## Question

For a combined ACL and MCL tear, the most appropriate test would be? (arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- multiligamentous knee injury)

## Options

1. Anterior drawer test with foot in internal rotation
2. Anterior drawer test at 30 and 90 degree knee flexion
3. Anterior drawer test with foot in external rotation
4. Anterior drawer test with foot in neutral rotation

## Correct option

**3. Anterior drawer test with foot in external rotation**

## Answer explanation

Combined ACL and MCL tear produce anteromedial rotatory instability. The anterior drawer test with tibia in external rotation is used to detect anteromedial rotatory instability. With this combined ACL and MCL injury, anterior drawer laxity in external rotation will be greater than laxity in neutral and internal rotation.

Answer : C
