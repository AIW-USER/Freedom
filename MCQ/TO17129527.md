## Question

Which is not included in Kocher’s criteria to differentiate septic arthritis from transient synovitis of the hip joint

## Options

1. Fever > 38.5°C
2. Erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR) > 40
3. White blood cell (WBC) count > 12,000/mm3t
4. CRP > 2.5 mg/lt

## Correct option

**4. CRP > 2.5 mg/lt**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Kocher’s criteria are used to differentiate septic arthritis of the hip joint from transient synovitis hip in children. It has four components; 1 point is assigned to each component.

Originally CRP was not a part of Kocher’s criteria. It was added later by Caird et al. CRP is the most sensitive of all lab criteria.

Order of sensitivity of above criteria

fever > CRP > ESR > refusal to bear weight > WBC

Answer: 4
