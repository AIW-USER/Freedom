## Question

An army recruit after 6 weeks of training complained of activity related pain at the posteromedial aspect of leg. On examination there is point tenderness at the junction of distal and medial third of tibia. The most likely diagnosis is [FNB 2014]

## Options

1. Stress fracture
2. Posteromedial tibial stress syndrome
3. Gastrocnemius strain
4. Bone contusion

## Correct option

**1. Stress fracture**

## Answer explanation

Activity related pain, history of recent strenuous unaccustomed activity, and point tenderness all suggest diagnosis of stress fracture. In posteromedial tibial stress syndrome pain is usually described as dull pain that is aggravated by activity and relieved by rest. In contrast to point tenderness of stress fracture, posteromedial tibial stress syndrome is characterized by diffuse tenderness over the posteromedial border of the tibia.

Answer: 1
