Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Cerebral Palsy (CP)

## Question

A patient with Hemiplegic CP (Winters Type IV) exhibits significant 'Stiff Knee Gait' and internal rotation of the lower limb during swing. 3D Gait Analysis shows a 'double-bump' pattern on the EMG of the rectus femoris and excessive femoral anteversion of femur. Which surgical combination addresses the primary pathomechanics of both the swing phase and the rotational deformity?

## Options

1. Femoral Derotation Osteotomy (FDRO) and Rectus Femoris Transfer (RFT).
2. Tibial Tubercle Osteotomy and anterior branch Obturator neurectomy.
3. Distal Femoral Extension Osteotomy and Hip Adductor release.
4. Strayer procedure and Femoral Derotation Osteotomy.

## Correct option

**1. Femoral Derotation Osteotomy (FDRO) and Rectus Femoris Transfer (RFT).**

## Answer explanation

FDRO corrects the bony rotational
malalignment, while RFT converts the rectus from a knee extensor to a hip
flexor, improving knee clearance in swing.

Answer: 1
