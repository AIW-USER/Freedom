Chapter: Spine MCQs — Thoracic Disc Herniation

## Question

The most common site for a Thoracic disc prolapse is

## Options

1. T7-8
2. T11-12
3. T9-T10
4. T6-7

## Correct option

**2. T11-12**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

Thoracic spine has relatively less mobility compared to cervical and lumbar spine and is further supported by rib cage, hence, prevalence of symptomatic disc prolapse in Thoracic spine tends to be very low (0.1% to 3%).

75% of disc prolapse in Thoracic spine tend to occur below T7- T8 segments because the mobility of Thoracic segments increases below T7- T8 level. Since, T11 - T12 has got maximum mobility and thin posterior longitudinal ligament the most common level of Thoracic disc prolapse tends to be T11- T12. It’s also the most common symptomatic level for a thoracic herniated disc.

In Lumbar spine also disc herniation corresponds to range of motion (mobility). L4-L5 has got maximum mobility so frequency is as follows: L4-L5 >L5-S1>L3-L4.

Cervical disc prolapse is relatively commoner than Thoracic and most common levels for cervical disc are C5-C6 > C6-C7.
