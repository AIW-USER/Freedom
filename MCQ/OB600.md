## Question

You are seeing a 53-year-old female for 1 year of increasing knee pain. She is otherwise healthy. Based on the imaging studies below (Figures A-D), what is your diagnosis?

![[q600-a-renal-cell-ct.jpg]]

![[q600-b-renal-cell-mri.jpg]]
*Figure B*

![[q600-c-renal-cell-bone-scan.jpg]]
*Figure C*

![[q600-d-renal-histo.jpg]]
*Figure D*

## Options

1. Chondrosarcoma
2. Giant cell tumor
3. Multiple myeloma
4. Chordoma
5. Renal Cell Carcinoma

## Correct option

**5. Renal Cell Carcinoma**

## Answer explanation

The history and imaging studies, specifically the high power histology slide, suggest a metastatic renal cell carcinoma. Specifically the histology slides show the characteristic clear cell change and the pseudoalveolar pattern of renal cell carcinoma. The histology is not characteristic of chondrosarcoma, giant cell tumor, or multiple myeloma. Chordoma, while similiar in histologic appearance, occurs from cellular remnants of the notochord, not in the proximal tibia.
