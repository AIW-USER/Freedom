## Question

Which is not a marker of bone formation (general orthopedics- anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. Serum bone specific alkaline phosphatase—serum osteocalcin
2. Serum carboxy terminal extension peptide of procollagen-1
3. Serum type I collagen extension peptide
4. Urine deoxypyridinoline

## Correct option

**4. Urine deoxypyridinoline**

## Answer explanation

With bone formation and resorption the levels of following substances increase in urine or serum:

Markers of bone resorption:

• Urine and serum cross-linked N telopeptides

• Urine and serum cross-linked C telopeptides

• Urine hydroxyproline

• Urine deoxypyridinoline

• Urine hydroxylysine glycosides

• Serum TRAP (tartrate-resistant acid phosphatase—serum bone

sialoprotein

Markers of bone formation:

• Serum bone specific alkaline phosphatase—serum osteocalcin

• Serum carboxy terminal extension peptide of procollagen-1

• Serum type I collagen extension peptide

Answer: 4
