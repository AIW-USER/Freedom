## Question

A 34-year-old patient with suspected spinal tuberculosis has a CT-guided biopsy. The Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra result indicates 'MTB detected, Rifampicin resistance detected.' Following the latest WHO and NTEP guidelines, what is the most appropriate next step in the diagnostic algorithm before finalizing the treatment regimen?

## Options

1. Wait 6-8 weeks for the results of the Lowenstein-Jensen (LJ) solid culture for definitive drug susceptibility.
2. Initiate the standard 4-drug ATT (HRZE) immediately while waiting for solid culture results.
3. Perform a Second-Line Line Probe Assay (S-LPA) or phenotypic DST to check for Fluoroquinolone resistance.
4. Start empirical Bedaquiline and Linezolid without further testing since Rifampicin resistance is confirmed.

## Correct option

**3. Perform a Second-Line Line Probe Assay (S-LPA) or phenotypic DST to check for Fluoroquinolone resistance.**

## Answer explanation

Differentiating between MDR-TB and Pre-XDR-TB requires checking resistance to fluoroquinolones, which dictates the choice between shorter and longer oral regimens.

Answer: 3
