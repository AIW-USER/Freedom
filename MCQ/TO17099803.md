Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Hip Arthroplasty (Miscellaneous)

## Question

Contraindication for THR is all except

## Options

1. Morbid obesity
2. Asymptomatic bacteriuria
3. Dementia
4. Abductor deficiency

## Correct option

**2. Asymptomatic bacteriuria**

## Answer explanation

Absolute contraindications for total hip arthroplasty include active infection of the hip joint or any other region and any unstable medical illnesses that would significantly increase the risk of morbidity or mortality. Other relative contraindications include morbid obesity, severe dementia, tobacco use, severe osteoporosis, untreated skin conditions such as psoriasis, and absence or relative insufficiency of the abductor musculature.

Asymptomatic bacteriuria has not been associated with postoperative surgical site infections and should not be considered a contraindication. Although perioperative glycemic control seems important, hemoglobin A1c levels are also not reliable for predicting postoperative infection. According to Charnley, total hip arthroplasty can be done in the presence of a chronic, low-grade infection in the opposite hip

Answer: 2
