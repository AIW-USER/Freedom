## Question

Normal prevertebral space above cricoid is (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. < 7 mm
2. <12 mm
3. <18 mm
4. <21 mm

## Correct option

**1. < 7 mm**

## Answer explanation

The upper limits of normal thickness of the PVST are 6 mm at C2, 7 mm at C3, 18 mm at C6, and 18 mm at C7. The upper limit of normal has not been determined for C4 and C5 levels due to variable position of the esophagus and larynx. Some studies have determined the upper safe limit of prevertebral space at C6 level to be 21 mm.

Adults: no > than 7 mm at C2 or 21-22 mm at C6;

Child: no > than 14 mm at C6

Now, Cricoid is present at C6 level. Here it has been asked about the pre vertebral space ABOVE Cricoid (which could mean any level proximal to C6 level), so the best answer is 7 mm.

Ans: A
