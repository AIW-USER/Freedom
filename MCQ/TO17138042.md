## Question

Spinal AV malformation, which factor responsible for aggressive lesion

## Options

1. Location
2. Severity of symptoms
3. Presence of Leptomeningeal vein

## Correct option

**3. Presence of Leptomeningeal vein**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

In spinal vascular malformations, the location or presentation of symptoms do not determine the outcome, but the presence of fistulous communication determines the outcome. Presence of large leptomeningeal vein differentiates a malformation into nidal based or fistulous lesion.
