## Question

A patient has come with saddle anesthesia but there is no other neurological deficit noted in lower limbs. Likely site of lesion is [SPINE-ANATOMY]

## Options

1. Below S2
2. L4
3. L5
4. S1

## Correct option

**1. Below S2**

## Answer explanation

S3 dermatome corresponds to the supply of the inner thighs, buttocks and perianal region.
