## Question

Boxer’s fracture is ( trauma- fractures around wrist and hand- metacarpal fractures)

## Options

1. Short oblique fracture of head of 5th MC l
2. Fracture of neck of 5th MC
3. Comminuted fracture of head of 5th MC
4. Fracture of shaft of 5th MC

## Correct option

**2. Fracture of neck of 5th MC**

## Answer explanation

Boxer’s fracture: It is a transverse or short oblique fracture of the neck of usually, the fifth or less commonly, the fourth metacarpal. It is the most common metacarpal fracture and commonly occurs in young people while punching with a clenched fist. AP and lateral X-rays of hand show apex dorsal angulation of fracture. It can mostly be treated by closed reduction and cast immobilization or K-wire fixation

Answer: 2
