## Question

Which is a non polyostotic tumor (orthopedic oncology- benign tumors/tumor like lesions- Osteochondroma and multiple hereditary exostosis )

## Options

1. Fibrous dysplasia
2. Enchondroma
3. Osteochondroma
4. Hemangioma

## Correct option

**4. Hemangioma**

## Answer explanation

Polyostotic tumor/syndrome

- McCune Albright syndrome: It refers to the triad of polyostotic fibrous dysplasia, precocious puberty and skin pigmentation in the form of café-au-lait spots
- Hereditary multiple exostosis (HME)” (synonym diaphyseal aclasis) is an autosomal dominant condition with multiple bony osteochondromas

- Multiple enchondromatosis (nonhereditary disorder) is referred to as Ollier’s disease and if along with multiple enchondromas there are multiple cavernous hemangiomas and phleboliths, then the condition is called as Maffucci syndrome (hereditary disorder).

Answer: 4
