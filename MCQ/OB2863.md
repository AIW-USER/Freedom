## Question

Which of the following radiographs demonstrates a tumor that would be best treated by curettage with a power burr and packing with cement?

![[q2863-a-adamantinoma-tibia.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q2863-b-fibrous-dysplasia-tibia.jpg]]
*Figure B*

![[q2863-c-osteochondroma-tibia.jpg]]
*Figure C*

![[q2863-d-osteosarcoma-tibia.jpg]]
*Figure D*

![[q2863-e-tibia-giantcell.jpg]]
*Figure E*

## Options

1. Figure A
2. Figure B
3. Figure C
4. Figure D
5. Figure E

## Correct option

**5. Figure E**

## Answer explanation

Figure E is representative of a giant cell tumor and appropriate treatment includes curettage with a power burr and packing with cement.

*Bini et al conducted a Level 4 review of 38 patients treated with curettage through a cortical window and adjuvant packing of the defect with cement.* **They found** a 95% good to excellent result with an 8% recurrence rate.

Turcotte discussed that giant cell tumors are primarily treated with extensive curettage and reconstruction with adjuvant treatment.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Figure A demonstrates an adamantinoma (or osteofibrous dysplasia) which is typically treated with surgical wide excision.
Answer 2: Figure B is fibrous dysplasia which is typically treated with nonoperative management unless a fracture is present.
Answer 3: Figure C is an osteochondroma which is typically treated with surgical resection.
Answer 4: Figure D is a parosteal osteosarcoma which is typically treated with wide surgical excision.
