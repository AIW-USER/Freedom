## Question

True about Mueller-Weiss syndrome is all except (regional orthopedics- foot and ankle- Mueller-Weiss syndrome)

## Options

1. Navicular osteonecrosis
2. Paradoxical pes planovarus
3. Pseudo hindfoot valgus
4. Pseudo hindfoot varus

## Correct option

**4. Pseudo hindfoot varus**

## Answer explanation

Mueller-Weiss syndrome, also known as Brailsford disease, is a rare disease that historically refers to spontaneous adult-onset tarsal navicular osteonecrosis.

Mueller-Weiss syndrome usually affects adults between 40 and 60 years of age, is more common in women and is frequently bilateral.

Etiology: In Mueller-Weiss syndrome, the chronic loading forces are unevenly distributed, preferentially causing compression of the lateral half of the navicular. Any biomechanical precondition—such as, subtalar varus, first ray brachymetatarsia, and mild forms of club-foot that leads to lateral displacement of the net compressive force on the navicular and lateral incompetence of the acetabulum pedis may contribute to the development of Mueller-Weiss syndrome.

Clinical features: Most patients present with chronic mid and hindfoot pain in the fourth or fifth decades, as the navicular collapses and secondary osteoarthritis develops. With further navicular collapse, pes planovarus develops, and the tibia is forced into external rotation, which may lead to altered biomechanics and osteoarthritis of the knee.

Physical findings include swelling and tenderness over the dorso-medial aspect of the midfoot and heel varus, but a prominent navicular tuberosity may give the false clinical impression of hindfoot valgus, or pseudo hindfoot valgus. The medial longitudinal arch can be markedly flattened depending on the severity of the disease. This combination of pseudo hindfoot valgus and pes planus deformity is also known as paradoxical pes planovarus.

Treatment: It is mostly surgical by fusion of symptomatic degenerative joints and restoration of the plantar and medial longitudinal arches by correcting the Meary-Tomeno line. Talonavicular Cuneiform arthrodesis with bone grafting satisfies these two goals and provides the most satisfactory results

Answer: 4
