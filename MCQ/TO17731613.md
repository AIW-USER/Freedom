## Question

Napoleon Hat sign indicates( spine- spondylolisthesis)

## Options

1. Facet dislocation of L4-L5
2. Retrolisthesis of L5-S1
3. Anterolisthesis of L5-S1
4. Sacralization of L5 vertebra

## Correct option

**3. Anterolisthesis of L5-S1**

## Answer explanation

In spondylolisthesis an AP X-ray shows “Inverted Napoleon Hat sign” which occurs due to superimposition of sacrum and L5 and indicates marked anterolisthesis of L5 over S1.

Answer: 3
