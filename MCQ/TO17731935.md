## Question

Main blood supply of humeral head is ( general orthopedics-anatomy basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. PCHA
2. ACHA
3. Axillary artery
4. Profunda brachii artery

## Correct option

**1. PCHA**

## Answer explanation

BLOOD SUPPLY OF HUMERAL HEAD

![[TO17731935-1.jpg]]

Anterior circumflex humeral artery (ACHA): It arises from the 3rd part of the axillary artery and passes deep to coracobrachialis & both heads of biceps. It gives rise to the Arcuate artery (called as antero-lateral branch by some authors) that courses laterally along the IT sulcus and finally passes into the bone around surgical NOH area to go and anastomose posteriorly with PCHA. The anterolateral branch gives off a small branch that runs superiorly to the rotator cuff.

As per old consensus it (specifically its antero lateral branch) was considered the main blood supply of the humeral head.

Posterior Circumflex Humeral Artery (PCHA): It arises from the 3rd part of the axillary artery. It passes through the quadrilateral space (between subscapularis, teres major, long head triceps, & humerus) with axillary nerve above it. As per current consensus (Hettrich et al. and Keough et al.), it supplies 64% of humeral head (Superior, Lateral and Inferior parts, posterior portion of the greater tuberosity & small portion of the posteroinferior articular segment)

Ans: A
