## Question

True about meniscal cyst is? (Arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- meniscus injury)

## Options

1. Most common associated lesion is meniscal tear
2. Common on lateral side
3. Equally affect medial and lateral menisci
4. Common in cases with ACL tear

## Correct option

**1. Most common associated lesion is meniscal tear**

## Answer explanation

Meniscal cyst: Until recently it was believed that meniscal cysts are more common on lateral side, but many recent radiological reviews have shown that medial meniscal cysts are more common than lateral meniscal cysts (references given below). Most accepted etiology of meniscal cysts is extrusion of synovial fluid through the meniscal tear, so meniscal cysts are almost always associated with an underlying meniscal tear.

Anderson JJ, Connor GF, Helms CA. New observations on meniscal cysts. Skeletal Radiol. 2010;39(12):1187–1191.

Campbell SE, Sanders TG, Morrison WB. MR imaging of meniscal cysts: incidence, location, and clinical significance. AJR Am J Roentgenol. 2001;177(2):409–413

De Smet AA, Graf BK, del Rio AM. Association of parameniscal cysts with underlying meniscal tears as identified on MRI and arthroscopy. AJR Am J Roentgenol. 2011;196(2):W180–186.

Answer: 1
