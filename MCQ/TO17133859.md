## Question

Not a cause of pediatric atlanto-axial instability

## Options

1. Os odontoideum
2. Rotatory atlantoaxial subluxation
3. JRA
4. Morquio's Syndrome

## Correct option

**1. Os odontoideum**

## Answer explanation

ANSWER-A

Os odontoideum is cause of atlanto-axial instability in adults not in children.

Adult causes of atlanto-axial instability

- degenerative
- Down's syndrome
- Rheumatoid Arthritis
- Os odontoideum
- traumatic
- Type I odontoid fracture (very rare)
- Atlas fractures
- Transverse ligament injuries

Pediatric causes

- degenerative
- JRA
- Morquio's Syndrome
- lysosomal storage disorder
- trauma/infection
- rotatory atlantoaxial subluxation
