## Options

1. 4-6 weeks
2. 3 months
3. 7 months
4. 12 months
5. 16 months

## Correct option

**3. 7 months**

## Answer explanation

Adolescent patients who undergo open treatment for femoroacetabular impingement (FAI) should expect to return to sports at a median of 7 months.

Femoroacetabular impingement (FAI) is becoming a more recognized pathology of the hip in adolescent athletes. FAI can be subdivided into two types: cam and pincer. Cam type impingements are a femoral based disorder (results from cam morphology of the femur) that is more common in young athletic males, while pincer type impingements are an acetabular based disorder (results from pincer morphology of the acetabulum) that is more common in active middle-aged women. Treatment of FAI includes observation, arthroscopic procedures, and open procedures. For symptomatic patients with mechanical symptoms, open procedures have been the gold standard of treatment, with patients being able to return to sport at a median of 7 months. Some recent studies have shown arthroscopic procedures to have equivalent outcomes to open hip surgery in select patients.

*Fabricant et al. performed a retrospective study on arthroscopic treatment of FAI in adolescent athletes.* **They found** short-term improvements in clinical outcome scores with no complications for arthroscopic treatment of FAI in their cohort of adolescent athletes. **They concluded** that arthroscopic treatment of FAI by an experienced hip arthroscopist should be considered in selected patients when treating athletically active adolescents for whom nonoperative management fails.

*Novais et al. published a study on return to play following open treatment of FAI in adolescent athletes.* **They found** that the median timing of return was 7 months after treatment for FAI and that 90% of patients returned at a level that was equivalent to or greater than their level of play before surgery.

Figure A is an AP radiograph of the pelvis demonstrating bilateral cam deformities. Figure B is a Dunn view radiograph of the left hip demonstrating the same cam deformity.

Answers 1, 2, 4, and 5: The expected time for adolescents to return to sports after open treatment of FAI is 7 months.
