## Question

A 12-year-old boy was diagnosed with Li–Fraumeni syndrome. The origin of his syndrome is related to a germ-line mutation of the tumor suppressor p53 gene. What is most common primary bone tumor associated with this gene mutation?

## Options

1. Chondrosarcoma
2. Osteosarcoma
3. Spindle cell sarcoma of bone
4. Ewing's sarcoma
5. Multiple myeloma

## Correct option

**2. Osteosarcoma**

## Answer explanation

Osteosarcoma is the most common primary bone tumor associated with p53 gene mutations.

Li–Fraumeni is a familial cancer syndrome with a spectrum of primary cancers, including breast cancer, colon cancer and sarcomas - of which 50% are osteosarcoma. It has an autosomal dominant inheritance pattern so multiple members of a family can (tragically) be affected. Li–Fraumeni syndrome is related to germ line mutations resulting in deactivation of the tumor suppressor gene p53. In the general population, 15-30% cases of osteosarcoma are found to be due to sporadic mutation of the p53 gene.

*Toguchida et al. analyzed the spectrum of mutations of the p53 gene seen in 127 bone and soft tissue sarcomas.* **They found** that mutations of the p53 gene in sarcomas are quite heterogeneous both in their distribution in the gene and in the type of genetic alteration. **They showed** that 50% of osteosarcoma cases of allelic deletions on 17p chromosome for p53 gene.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: p53 mutations are associated with aggressive behavior of chondrosarcoma. It is not common in Li–Fraumeni syndrome.
Answer 3: Spindle cell sarcoma of bone is very rare and therefore not a common primary bone tumor associated with Li–Fraumeni syndrome.
Answer 4: Most cases of Ewings sarcoma harbor somatic chromosomal translocations that fuse the EWSR1 gene on chromosome 22 with members of the ETS family.
Answer 5: Mutations of the p53 gene are rare events in patients with newly diagnosed multiple myeloma.
