## Question

In the infratentorial supracerebellar approach to the pineal region, which of the following veins are usually sacrificed?

- Superior version vein
- Posterior pericallosal vein
- Precentral cerebellar vein
- Basal vein of Rosenthal

## Options

1. I, II, III
2. I, III
3. II, IV
4. All

## Correct option

**2. I, III**

## Answer explanation

The superior vermian vein and the precentral cerebellar vein are usually sacrificed during the infratentorial supracerebellar approach to the pineal region.

The basal vein of Rosenthal is a very important draining vein from the deep venous system and cannot be sacrificed.

The posterior pericallosal vein usually does not come in the field for this approach.

![[TO17138013-1.jpg]]

Also remember:

The pineal region, also known as the quadrigeminal region or cistern, or posterior third ventricular region has a crucial location.

The surgical approaches to this region are:

- infratentorial supracerebellar approach
- Occipital trans tentorial approach
- Interhemispheric transcallosal approach
- Lateral suboccipital approach
- Combined supratentorial intratentorial approach
![[TO17138013-2.gif]]

The veins in this region are:

The basal vein of Rosenthal and the Internal Cerebral Vein, from both the sides, combine to form the Vein of Galen.

This is also joined by a precentral cerebellar vein and a superior vermian vein. Both of which can be sacrificed in the approach.

answer: b
