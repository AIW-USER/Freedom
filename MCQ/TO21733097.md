Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Dupuytren’s Contracture

## Question

Which is not a risk factor for recurrence in Dupuytren’s contracture?

## Options

1. MCP joint contracture
2. PIP joint contracture
3. Dupuytren diathesis
4. Partial fasciectomy > CCH injection

## Correct option

**1. MCP joint contracture**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 1

EXPLANATION: -
Reported incidence of recurrence in Dupuytren’s contracture is
CCH: approximately 50% at 5 years
PNA: approximately 50 % at 3 years
Fasciectomy: approximately 3.5%-20% at 4 years
Reported risk factors for recurrence in dupuytren’s contracture are-
Higher recurrence is seen with non-operative measures (needle aponeurotomy and collagenase injection) compared to fasciectomy. Patients with Dupuytren’s diasthesis and PIP disease have high recurrence.
