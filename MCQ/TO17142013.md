Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Cavovarus Foot

## Question

Coleman block test is used for assessment of:

## Options

1. Congenital vertical talus
2. Cavovarus foot
3. Planovalgus foot
4. Congenital metatarsus adductus

## Correct option

**2. Cavovarus foot**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Coleman block test is used to assess flexibility in a cavovarus foot deformity. Place 1inch block under lateral foot. Flexible deformity gets corrected to neutral or valgus on making a patient stand on a block.
