## Question

In resistance training periodization refers to (sports medicine- principles of training and recovery)

## Options

1. Manipulation of training variables in order to maximize training adaptations
2. Interval of rest and recovery in between spells of intense training
3. Gradual increase of load and repetitions to increase the endurance
4. Gradual decline in load and repetitions to prevent overtraining syndrome

## Correct option

**1. Manipulation of training variables in order to maximize training adaptations**

## Answer explanation

Periodisation is a form of structuring training to achieve performance results, and involves dividing the annual training plan into phases and training units. Periodization is further divided into macro, meso and microcycles. In other words it is planned manipulation of training variables (load, sets, and repetitions) in order to maximize training adaptations and to prevent the onset of overtraining syndrome. Within Periodisation there are several different models, including:

Linear Periodisation – focusing on one particular aspect of fitness, for example working on strength training for four weeks.

Non-linear Periodisation – focusing on several particular attributes at once, for example strength, power and muscular endurance fitness.

Also know other types of sports training:

Interval training : In interval training, short to moderate periods of exercise are alternated with short to moderate periods of recovery.

Fartlek training: It is used for improving aerobic capacity in distance athletes. Fartlek combines the aerobic requirements of continuous exercise with the anaerobic requirements of interspersed speed intervals, and is often undertaken in the countryside. The concept of fartlek is to run or cycle at a steady pace for a set distance and then to sprint or exercise intensely for a short distance before going back to the steady pace. Fartlek training is a good way to enhance aerobic power and capacity.

Circuit training: In circuit training a series of different activities is performed in a given sequence. A circuit normally has at least six stations wherein the individual will exercise for a given period before resting (or not) and moving on.

Answer: 1
