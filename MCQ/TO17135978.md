Chapter: Sports Medicine MCQs — Female Athlete

## Question

A young sportswoman lands up with Female Athlete TRIAD. An advice not suitable for her would be

## Options

1. Avoid OCPs
2. Improve nutrition
3. Increase Body Fat
4. All the above

## Correct option

**1. Avoid OCPs**

## Answer explanation

FEMALE ATHLETE TRIAD

Under nutrition in female athletes leads to decreased IGF-1 levels and leptin levels. This is associated with decreased appetite stimulation, menstrual problems and problems with bone remodeling leading to osteopenia and stress fractures.

Components:

- Low energy balance state in body due to eating disorder
- Disordered menstruation (Amenorrhea)
- Low BMD (Stress fractures)

Management involves Use of OCPs to normalize menstruation, increasing body fat to improve energy balance by dietary modifications and prompt management of stress fractures if they develop.

Answer: 1
