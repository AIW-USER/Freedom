## Question

Choose the correct option for the axial CT given in the picture

![[TO17137007-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Fracture of Wagstaffe tubercle and insufficiency of PITFL
2. Fracture of Chaput tubercle and insufficiency of AITFL
3. Fracture of Volkmann tubercle and insufficiency of PITFL
4. Fracture of Wagstaffe tubercle and insufficiency of AITFL

## Correct option

**2. Fracture of Chaput tubercle and insufficiency of AITFL**

## Answer explanation

This is the fracture of Chaput tubercle of tibia. It is anterolateral tubercle of tibia where AITFL originates. Tillaux fracture is avulsion fracture of Chaput tubercle due to AITFL pull seen in adolescents.

Distal tibiofibular syndesmosis consists of the following ligaments

- anterior-inferior tibiofibular ligament (AITFL)
- originates from Chaput tubercle (anterolateral tubercle of tibia)
- inserts on Wagstaffe tubercle (anterior tubercle of fibula)
- posterior-inferior tibiofibular ligament (PITFL)
- originates from Volkmann tubercle (posterior tubercle of tibia)
- inserts on posterior part of lateral malleolus
- strongest component of syndesmosis
- interosseous membrane
- interosseous ligament (IOL)
- distal continuation of the interosseous membrane
- inferior transverse ligament (ITL)

Answer: 2
