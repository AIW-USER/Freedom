## Question

What is not true about a large diameter head in total hip replacement?

## Options

1. Will increase the ROM
2. Will decrease the ROM
3. Will increase the jump distance
4. Will be more stable for same neck length

## Correct option

**2. Will decrease the ROM**

## Answer explanation

For a given neck diameter, the use of a larger femoral head increases the head-neck ratio and the range of motion before the neck impinges on the rim of the socket will be greater. When this impingement does occur, the femoral head is levered out of the socket. The “jump distance” is the distance the head must travel to escape the rim of the socket and is generally approximated to be half the diameter of the head. For both of these reasons, a larger-diameter head is theoretically more stable than a smaller one.

Answer: 2
