## Question

True about femoral rollback in knee arthroplasty

## Options

1. the posterior translation the femur with progressive flexion, prevents posterior impingement during deep flexion
2. the posterior translation the tibia with progressive flexion, prevents posterior impingement during deep flexion
3. the posterior translation the tibia with progressive flexion, causes posterior impingement during deep flexion
4. the posterior translation the femur with progressive flexion, causes posterior impingement during deep flexion

## Correct option

**1. the posterior translation the femur with progressive flexion, prevents posterior impingement during deep flexion**

## Answer explanation

Femoral rollback is the posterior translation the femur with progressive flexion of the knee joint. It improves quadriceps function and range of knee flexion by preventing posterior impingement during deep flexion. In the knee joint femoral rollback is controlled by cruciate ligaments. After knee replacement with PCL substituting implants femoral rollback is caused by cam and post mechanism where tibial post contacts the femoral cam causing posterior displacement of the femur.

Answer : a
