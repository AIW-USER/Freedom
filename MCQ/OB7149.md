## Question

Type I Charcot-Marie-Tooth (CMT) disease is a disorder of which of the following structures?

## Options

1. Myelin sheath
2. Perineural vasculature
3. Neuronal cell body
4. Dendrite
5. Neuromuscular junction

## Correct option

**1. Myelin sheath**

## Answer explanation

Charcot-Marie-Tooth type 1 (CMT1) affects approximately 80% of CMT patients and is the most common type of CMT. This is an autosomal-dominant genetic disorder that causes demyelination, which can be detected by measuring nerve conduction velocities. Symptoms usually start in early adulthood, including foot drop, hammer toe, and muscle wasting in the feet with a high arch.
