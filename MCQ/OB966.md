## Question

Absence of the enzyme glucocerebrosidase leads to what clinical entity?

## Options

1. Niemann-Pick disease
2. Fabry disease
3. Gaucher's disease
4. Tay-Sachs
5. Krabbe disease

## Correct option

**3. Gaucher's disease**

## Answer explanation

Gaucher disease is caused by a deficiency of the enzyme glucocerebrosidase.

Gaucher disease is an autosomal recessive lipid storage disease, characterized by the deposition of glucocerebroside in cells of the macrophage-monocyte system. Deficiency of a specific lysosomal hydrolase, acid beta-glucocerebrosidase, causes the condition. Three clinical subtypes exist and are inherited as autosomal recessive traits.

*Pastores et al review the bone and joint complications of Gaucher disease.* **They report** glucosylceramide accumulation in the bone marrow causes thinning of the cortex, pathologic fractures, bone pain, bony infarcts, and osteopenia. This may lead to the characteristic Erlenmeyer flask deformity of the distal femur that is associated with Gaucher's disease.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Niemann-Pick disease is caused by defective sphingomyelinase.
Answer 2: Fabry's disease is caused by alpha-galactosidase deficiency.
Answer 4: Tay-Sachs disease is casued by hexosaminidase A deficiency
Answer 5: Krabbe disease is caused by galactocerebrosidase deficiency.
