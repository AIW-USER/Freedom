Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Hallux Valgus Bunion

## Question

During a lateral release in hallux valgus surgery, which structure is typically released from its insertion on the lateral sesamoid and proximal phalanx?

## Options

1. Flexor hallucis longus
2. Extensor hallucis brevis
3. Adductor hallucis tendon
4. Abductor hallucis tendon

## Correct option

**3. Adductor hallucis tendon**

## Answer explanation

The adductor hallucis pulls the hallux into valgus and must be released to allow the toe to return to a neutral position.

Answer : C
