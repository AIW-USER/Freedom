## Question

Bones are weakest in (general orthopedics- anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. Shear
2. Compression
3. Tension
4. Twisting force

## Correct option

**1. Shear**

## Answer explanation

Bones are strongest in compression, weakest in shear forces and intermediate in tension forces. Shearing forces are unaligned forces pushing one part of a body in one direction, and another part of the body in the opposite direction. With shearing force fracture occurs parallel to the direction of shear force. To apply tensile force one end of the bone must be in a fixed position and the other part is pulled away from the fixed end.

Answer: 1
