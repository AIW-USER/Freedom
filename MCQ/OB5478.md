## Question

Figure A is the axial T2 MRI of a 56-year-old man with right leg pain radiating to the lateral leg and dorsum of the right foot. He also has significant low back pain. He has attempted non-operative management for several months. Which of the following treatments is associated with the lowest incidence of recurrent back pain two years after treatment?

![[q5478-a-synovial-cyst-radiopaedia.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Broad-spectrum intravenous antibiotic administration in consultation with an infectious disease specialist
2. CT-guided aspiration
3. Decompression with unilateral hemilaminectomy
4. Decompression with bilateral laminectomy
5. Decompression with facetectomy and instrumented fusion

## Correct option

**5. Decompression with facetectomy and instrumented fusion**

## Answer explanation

This patient has a facet cyst causing lumbar spinal stenosis with leg and back pain. Rate of recurrent back pain is lowest when fusion is employed.

Intra-spinal synovial cysts may cause radiculopathy, back pain and neurogenic claudication. These symptoms are reliably relieved with decompression but instrumented fusion is associated with a lower rate of recurrent back pain compared with laminectomy alone. Back pain is multifactorial but the association between fusion and reduced back pain may be due to reduced rates of cyst recurrence or the ability of fusion to address concomitant pathology such as instability.

*Singh et al. present a surgical technique review of lumbar laminectomy, lumbar laminoplasty and limited laminotomy, with a focus on laminectomy.* **They recommend** pre-operative flexion-extension films to evaluate for instability to include a potential fusion.

*Xu et al. retrospectively reviewed the surgical management of 195 symptomatic synovial cysts undegoing unilateral hemilaminectomy, bilateral laminectomy, facetectomy with in situ fusion or facetectomy with instrumented fusion.* *Patients undergoing laminectomy without fusion had higher incidence of recurrent back pain and recurrent cyst formation.*

Figure A shows a right-sided synovial facet cyst compressing the neural elements.

Incorrect answers:
Answer 1. There is no evidence of an infectious process in this case
Answer 2. Cyst aspiration is associated with high failure rate compared to surgical decompression.
Answers 3 and 4. Laminectomy alone is an appropriate treatment but fusion is associated with lower rate of recurrent back pain.
