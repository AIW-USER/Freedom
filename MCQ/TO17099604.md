## Question

10 In X ray AP view of the hip joint positive crossover sign indicates

## Options

1. Anterior over coverage of the hip
2. Retroverted acetabulum
3. Deficient posterior coverage
4. Any of above three in isolation or combination

## Correct option

**4. Any of above three in isolation or combination**

## Answer explanation

The crossover sign is present when the anterior wall outline crosses over the posterior wall below the superior lateral margin of the acetabulum. The crossover sign is indicative of either isolated anterior over coverage of the hip or retroversion of the entire acetabulum with deficient posterior coverage.

Answer: 4
