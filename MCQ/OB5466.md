## Question

You treat a skeletally immature patient for aspiration-proven septic arthritis of the right hip with an incision and debridement through an open anterior approach. Appropriate antibiotics were given following the I&D. Which of the following is the LEAST likely complication of delayed management of this condition:

## Options

1. Limb length discrepancy with growth
2. Persistent limping due to pain or muscle imbalances
3. Chronic posterior hip instability and dislocations
4. Osteonecrosis of the epiphysis and destruction of the articular cartilage
5. Chronic osteomyelitis of the proximal femur

## Correct option

**3. Chronic posterior hip instability and dislocations**

## Answer explanation

While outcomes are poor, delayed management of septic arthritis of the hip is not associated with chronic posterior hip instability and dislocations, especially when an anterior surgical approach is performed.

Even with prompt treatment of septic arthritis, complications can occur. Injury to the articular surface can occur within 8 hours of onset of symptoms, and necrosis of the physis and epiphysis is thought to result from excessive intracapsular pressures during the inflammatory process. Similar to other conditions that cause avascular necrosis, the timeline of clinical presentation and radiographic findings may differ from the acute vascular insult. Reconstructive osteotomies may better position the less affected portions of the femoral head in the acetabulum, improving pain and mobility, and delaying salvage procedures such as arthrodesis or arthroplasty.

*Montgomery et al. cited discusses the pathophysiology and treatment of pediatric septic arthritis.* While prompt surgical debridement and irrigation of the joint in addition to appropriate antibiotic therapy are the mainstays of early treatment, delayed complications can present after initial recovery. It is important to discuss these with the patients' families to establish expectations and appropriate monitoring.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1, 2, 4, 5: All of these can be consequences of septic arthritis in skeletally immature patients
