## Question

Protrusio acetabuli is defined by (arthroplasty- hip arthroplasty-protrusio acetabuli)

## Options

1. Migration of femoral head past ilio-ischial line
2. Migration of femoral head past ilio-pectineal line
3. Center-edge angle above 25 degrees
4. Decreased center-edge angle

## Correct option

**1. Migration of femoral head past ilio-ischial line**

## Answer explanation

Protrusio acetabuli is defined by medial displacement of acetabulum and femoral head into the pelvis. Medialisation of the medial wall of the acetabulum past the ilioischial line (Kohler's line) and center-edge angle of Wiberg greater than 40 degrees on the anteroposterior radiograph of the pelvis define the protrusio acetabuli. Kohler’s line is defined as line connecting most lateral aspect of pelvic brim and most lateral aspect of obturator foramen on AP of pelvis

The Center edge angle of Wiberg is formed between two lines, one vertical line that passes through the center of the femoral heads and a second line connecting the center of the femoral head and the superior border of the acetabulum

Common causes of unilateral protrusion acetabulum are: Osteoarthritis, septic arthritis, tubercular arthritis, AVN, central fracture dislocation(trauma), fibrous dysplasia and total hip replacement surgery

Common causes of bilateral protrusio acetabuli are rheumatoid arthritis, osteomalacia, ankylosing spondylitis,Paget's disease, chronic renal osteodystrophy, rachitism, osteoporosis, and marfan syndrome.
