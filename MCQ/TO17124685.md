## Question

What is the main source of nutrition to cells within a disc in adults (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Diffusion of nutrients through porous central concavity of vertebral end plates
2. Diffusion of nutrients from CSF
3. Blood vessels contained within annulus fibrosus
4. Diffusion of nutrients across annulus

## Correct option

**1. Diffusion of nutrients through porous central concavity of vertebral end plates**

## Answer explanation

Disc is the largest avascular structure in the body and nutrition to disc is via diffusion of nutrients through vertebral end plates.

Answer: 1
