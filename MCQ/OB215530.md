## Question

You are called to the trauma bay to evaluate a patient for a suspected pelvis injury. You order a series of x-rays to evaluate the pelvic ring. Which of the following injury patterns, if present, is most likely to be seen on the radiographic view depicted in figure A?

![[q215530-a-screen-shot-2021-04-30-at-7-04-43-am.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Vertical translation of the hemipelvis
2. Anterior translation of the hemipelvis
3. Posterior translation of the hemipelvis
4. Internal rotation of the hemipelvis
5. External rotation of the hemipelvis

## Correct option

**1. Vertical translation of the hemipelvis**

## Answer explanation

The radiograph in figure A demonstrates a pelvic outlet view of the pelvis. Pelvic outlet radiographs demonstrate vertical translation of the hemipelvis.

When evaluating for a pelvic ring injury, pelvic inlet and outlet views are useful adjuvants to AP pelvis x-rays to further delineate fracture lines and pelvis deformity. These X-rays are obtained by aiming the beam 45 degrees cephalad (outlet) and caudal (inlet) compared to a traditional AP pelvis. A pelvic outlet view demonstrates vertical translation, flexion/extension deformity, and fractures through the sacral foramina. A pelvic inlet view aids in visualization of anterior/posterior translation, internal/external rotation, and widening of the SI joint.

*Ricci et al. compared traditional pelvic inlet and outlet x-rays with CT scans to determine the optimal angle of these x-rays to best evaluate posterior pelvic anatomy.* **They found** optimal inlet angle to visualize S1 is 21 degrees and the optimal angle to visualize the S2 body was 57 degrees. **They conclude** inlet and outlet radiographs made at 25 and 60 degrees, respectively, were best to visualize posterior pelvic anatomy.

*Young et al. reviewed pelvic radiographs of 142 severely traumatized patients in order to develop a classification scheme to help guide treatment.* **They identified** four distinct patterns of force: anteroposterior compression, lateral compression, vertical shear, and complex pattern. **They determined** utilization of this classification scheme correlates with associated injuries and is useful in surgical correction of these deformities.

Illustration A is an outlet x-ray demonstrating vertical displacement of the left hemipelvis.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 2-5: These deformities are best seen on pelvic inlet x-rays, not outlet
