Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Subacromial Impingement

## Question

What does a positive tangent sign on the MRI predict? (arthroscopy- shoulder arthroscopy- rotator cuff and subacromial impingement)

## Options

1. Irreparable supraspinatus due to fatty atrophy
2. Fatty atrophy of subscapularis muscle indicating irreparability
3. Retracted cuff tear medial to glenoid
4. Biceps dislocation due to subscapularis tear

## Correct option

**1. Irreparable supraspinatus due to fatty atrophy**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: The tangent sign is useful in helping to quantify supraspinatus muscle belly fatty atrophy with a positive sign implicated with an irreparable tear or poorer outcome after supraspinatus tendon tear repair.

On a sagittal oblique plane, a line is drawn between the upper surface of the scapular spine and the upper surface of the coracoid process (medial aspect). A normal supraspinatus muscle belly lies above this line (negative sign) and an abnormal supraspinatus muscle belly (small from fatty atrophy) lies below this line (positive sign)

Left side arrow indicates supraspinatus tendon below tangent level indicating severe fatty atrophy and irreparability of supraspinatus tendon. On the right side the arrow indicates supraspinatus tendon above the tangent indicating a repairable tear.

Answer: 1
