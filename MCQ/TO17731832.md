## Question

True about Sinding-Larsen-Johansson syndrome is all except( regional orthopedics-knee- patellar tendinitis)

## Options

1. Osteochondrosis of the inferior pole of the patella
2. Overuse injury
3. Pain at lower end of patellar tendon
4. Commonly seen in active adolescents

## Correct option

**3. Pain at lower end of patellar tendon**

## Answer explanation

Sinding-Larsen-Johansson syndrome is an osteochondrosis of the inferior pole of the patella and is often bilateral in nature. This syndrome is caused by repetitive traction (overuse injury) by the patellar tendon on the lower pole of the patella, (still partly cartilaginous in adolescents) during contraction of the quadriceps muscle. This leads to cartilage damage, swelling and pain, especially after exertion of force, and later to tendon thickening and fragmentation of the lower pole of the patella and sometimes to bursitis, i.e. inflammation of a bursa situated between the tendon and the patella causing inflammation at the insertion of the proximal tendon into the inferior pole of the patella.

Clinical features: Sinding-Larsen-Johansson syndrome is commonly seen in active adolescents between 10 and 14 years of age. Clinically it is characterized by pain and tenderness localized to the lower pole of the patella, which increases when the patellar is loaded during flexion, subpatellar edema and functional limitation

Answer: 3
