Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Chondroblastoma

## Question

Which of the following statements about chondroblastoma is not true

## Options

1. Also Known as “Codman Tumour”
2. Smaller Lesions can be treated with Radio frequency Ablation
3. Chicken wire Calcification is seen of histopathology
4. Commonly involves metaphysis of long bones

## Correct option

**4. Commonly involves metaphysis of long bones**

## Answer explanation

Chondroblastoma is also known as “Codman Tumour. Smaller lesions up to size approx. 1cm can be treated with radiofrequency ablation. It has characteristic “chicken wire calcification” seen in histopathology.

Chondroblastoma involves the epiphysis of long bones. Other tumours involving the epiphysis are Giant Cell Tumour and clear cell chondrosarcoma

Answer D
