Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Patellofemoral Instability

## Question

Most important static stabilizer of patella in a structurally normal knee is [FNB-2014]

## Options

1. VMO
2. MPFL
3. Q angle
4. TT:TG ratio

## Correct option

**2. MPFL**

## Answer explanation

Stabilizers of patellofemoral joint:

Static stabilizers: These are the shape of the trochlea (it is groove shaped with lateral edge elevated to contain the patella once it is fully seated, i.e. after 30° knee flexion), shape and position of the patella, the medial retinaculum and the presence of a medial patellofemoral ligament (MPFL) that binds medial side of the patella to the medial epicondyle of femur (and stabilizes in the first 30° knee flexion when patella is not fully engaged in the trochlear groove).In a structurally normal knee MPFL is the most important static stabilizer of patella.

Dynamic stabilizer: Vastus medialis [its oblique fibers called vastus medialis obliquus (VMO)] stabilizes patella during initial phases of knee flexion in conjunction with static stabilizers.

Answer: 2
