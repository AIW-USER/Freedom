Chapter: Spine MCQs — Miscellaneous (Spine)

## Question

Which of the following is not a cause of absent ankle jerks and upgoing plantars?

## Options

1. Vitamin B12 deficiency
2. Motor neuron disease
3. Friedrich’s ataxia
4. Multiple sclerosis

## Correct option

**4. Multiple sclerosis**

## Answer explanation

Vitamin B12 deficiency leads to subacute combined degeneration of the spinal cord that leads to both LMN and UMN features and hence can present with the above scenario.

Motor neuron diseases also consist of a mixture of UMN and LMN findings. In Friendrick’s ataxia both cord and peripheral nerve degeneration accompany cerebellar ataxia.

Multiple sclerosis may cause a mixture of UMN signs along with ataxia (cerebellum involvement) and sensory loss (dorsal column involvement) but never LMN signs.

Answer: 4
