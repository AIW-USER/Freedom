## Question

True about pseudo subluxation of cervical spine is all except

## Options

1. More common in female children
2. Seen in children less than 8 years
3. C2 on C3 is most common
4. Reduction of subluxation with extension x rays

## Correct option

**1. More common in female children**

## Answer explanation

Pseudo subluxation of cervical spine

A physiologic (normal) pseudo subluxation of cervical vertebrae of up to 40% (or 4 mm) seen in young children. It is seen in children below 8 years due to the horizontal nature of the facet joints at younger ages. (facet joints become more vertical with age)

Epidemiology: around 20% of children admitted for polytrauma will demonstrate this incidental finding. No associations with gender, trauma, intubation status or injury severity have been demonstrated

Location: C2 on C3 is most common. C3 on C4 is second most common

Factors that support pseudo subluxation as opposed to true traumatic subluxation include

- reduction of subluxation with neck extension
- spinolaminar line within 1.5mm of C2
- no history or physical findings of significant trauma
- absence of anterior soft-tissue swelling

Answer: 1
