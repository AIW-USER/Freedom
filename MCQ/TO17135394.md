Chapter: Spine MCQs — Spinal Infections

## Question

True about disc space infection in children is all except

## Options

1. Staph aureus is most common cause
2. Lumbar spine is most commonly involved
3. Loss of disc space height is the earliest sign
4. Bed rest, immobilization, and antibiotics for 4-6 weeks is the treatment for early disease

## Correct option

**3. Loss of disc space height is the earliest sign**

## Answer explanation

Radiological Signs of disc space infection in children:

- loss of lumbar lordosis is the earliest radiographic sign
- disc space narrowing (10-21 days after infection begins)
- endplate erosion (10-21 days after infection begins)

Answer: 3
