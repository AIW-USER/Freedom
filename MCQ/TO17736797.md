## Question

Cause of Hallux Varus after fibular sesamoid resection is all except: [REGIONAL ORTHOPEDICS-Foot & Ankle -Hallux Varus]

## Options

1. MTP joint hyperextension
2. Medial dislocation of tibial sesamoid
3. Buttonholing of metatarsal head
4. Unopposed pull of adductor hallucis

## Correct option

**4. Unopposed pull of adductor hallucis**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 4

Hallux varus is caused by unopposed pull of abductor hallucis not adductor. Rest all form a role in pathology
