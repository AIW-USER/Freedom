## Question

Which Vitamin is responsible for bone mineralisation and deficiency of which leads to stress fracture?

## Options

1. Vitamin B
2. Vitamin C
3. Vitamin K
4. Vitamin A

## Correct option

**3. Vitamin K**

## Answer explanation

answer-c

Apart from 25(OH)D, vitamin K is also important for the bones . As with calcium, vitamin K works synergistically with 25(OH)D to regulate bone resorption, bone activation, and bone distribution . Vitamin K carboxylates the newly formed osteocalcin proteins, which are produced in mature bone cells and are strictly regulated by 25(OH)D. Once the protein is carboxylated, it interacts with calcium ions in bone tissue and has a significant impact on bone mineralization, bone formation, preventing bone loss and possibly preventing fractures.

Osteocalcin production is not suppressed if the vitamin K levels are insufficient. This situation facilitates the build-up of non-carboxylated (inactive) osteocalcin proteins in the bone, which leads to a potential increase in calcium release from the bone and the deposition of calcium in soft tissues such as hardening of the arteries. Therefore, 25(OH)D toxicity can occur in the absence of adequate vitamin K level.

Answer : c
