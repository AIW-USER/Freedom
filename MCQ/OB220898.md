## Question

A 65-year-old female presents with a painless thigh mass. A core needle biopsy is performed, which stains positive for MDM2 amplification. In discussing with the patient's family, you explain benign, intermediate, and malignant tumors in this class. Which of the following lipomatous tumors has the highest rate of metastasis?

## Options

1. Atypical lipomatous tumor
2. Dedifferentiated liposarcoma
3. Lipoma
4. Myxoid liposarcoma
5. Well-differentiated liposarcoma

## Correct option

**2. Dedifferentiated liposarcoma**

## Answer explanation

Of the answer choices, high-grade liposarcomas have the highest rate of metastatic potential (Answer choice 5, dedifferentiated liposarcoma). Dedifferentiated liposarcoma has a metastasis rate of approximately 50%.

Liposarcomas are a class of soft tissue sarcomas with differentiation towards adipose tissue. They are typically seen in patients aged 50–80 years and often present as a slow-growing, painless mass. There are several subtypes of liposarcoma: atypical lipomatous tumors, myxoid, round cell, pleomorphic, and dedifferentiated. Treatment involves marginal excision for atypical lipomatous tumors, and wide excision with radiation for intermediate and high-grade liposarcoma. High-grade liposarcomas have the highest metastatic potential.

*Yun et al. published a retrospective review of patients presenting with dedifferentiated liposarcoma to characterize their MRI findings.* **The authors found** a wide spectrum of MRI findings associated with dedifferentiated liposarcoma, concluding that while the typical finding of a fatty mass next to a non-fatty mass was common, dedifferentiated liposarcoma can also present as a non-fatty mass without a fatty component.

*Amer et al. queried the Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results (SEER) and the Canadian Institute for Clinical Evaluative Sciences databases to determine liposarcoma survivorship by subtype.* **The authors conclude** that the worst survival was seen among the de-differentiated, pleomorphic, and mixed subtypes.

Incorrect Answer Choices:
Answer choice 1: Atypical lipomatous tumors have metastatic potential similar to benign lipomatous masses.
Answer choice 3: Lipomas are benign tumors without metastatic potential.
Answer choice 4: Myxoid liposarcoma has a metastasis rate of 10-30%.
Answer choice 5: Well-differentiated liposarcoma does have the potential for malignant transformation; however, the chance of this occurring is low.
