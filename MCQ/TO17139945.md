Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Adhesive Capsulitis

## Question

Which of the following is a known risk factor for the development of adhesive capsulitis of the shoulder?

## Options

1. Epilepsy.
2. Diabetes mellitus.
3. Renal disease.
4. All of the above

## Correct option

**2. Diabetes mellitus.**

## Answer explanation

Adhesive capsulitis affects an estimated 2–5% of the population. The reported incidence in diabetics is between 10% and 36%. Patients with insulin-dependent diabetes tend to have more severe limitation of movement and are more resistant to non-surgical treatment than non-insulin diabetics. Risk factors for adhesive capsulitis include female sex, age over 40 years, preceding trauma, HLA-B27 positivity and prolonged immobilization of the glenohumeral joint. Adhesive capsulitis is associated with diabetes, thyroid disease, cerebrovascular disease, coronary artery disease, autoimmune disease and Dupuytren’s disease.

answer : b
