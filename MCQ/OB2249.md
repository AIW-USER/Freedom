## Question

Which of the following is true as it relates to scoliosis in patients with Neurofibromatosis (NF)?

## Options

1. Dystrophic curves are treated similar to idiopathic scoliosis
2. Rib penciling is prognostic for rapid progression
3. Scoliosis is associated with NF-Type 2
4. Evidence of Vertebral scalloping is the greatest risk factor for progression
5. Non-dystrophic curves are usually short segments

## Correct option

**2. Rib penciling is prognostic for rapid progression**

## Answer explanation

In patients with neurofibromatosis and dystrophic scoliosis, penciling of 3 or more ribs is prognostic for rapid progression and is the greatest risk factor for rapid progression.

Neurofibromatosis results from a mutation of either the NF1 or NF2 gene. However, NF2 is not associated with scoliosis, but rather bilateral vestibular schwannomas. Scoliosis in patients with neurofibromatosis can be classified as either non-dystrophic or dystrophic. Non-dystrophic curves tend to be longer curves and treatment is more similar to idiopathic scoliosis. Dystrophic curves are characterized by short segments (usually 4-6 vertebrae) and have a sharp angular curve. Rapid progression is associated with rib penciling (3 or more ribs).

Crawford and Schorry review Neurofibromatosis. They review the diagnostic criteria and common findings including scoliosis, tibial bowing, pseudoarthrosis, and developmental delays.

*Durrani et al. performed a retrospective chart and radiographic review of 457 patients between 1982 and 1995 with the diagnosis of NF 1.* *A total of 128 patients had a spinal deformity and 91 were ultimately included in the study.* **They found** that rib penciling to be the only factor associated with progression and in those with 3 or more, 87% showed progression.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Dystrophic curves are not treated similarly to idiopathic scoliosis. Rather, non-dystrophic curves are Treated similarly.
Answer 3: Scoliosis is not associated with NF Type 2
Answer 4: Vertebral scalloping is a risk factor for rapid curve progression, but penciling of 3 or more ribs is the greatest risk factor for rapid progression
Answer 5: Non-dystrophic curves are usually longer segments
