## Question

In a patient of lateral ankle pain a tendon was found torn. Intraoperative picture is given below. What are the most common patterns and sites of such tears?

![[TO17142183-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Longitudinal tear and tip of fibula
2. Radial tear and retro malleolar
3. Radial tear and base of fifth metatarsal
4. Longitudinal tear and base of fifth metatarsal

## Correct option

**1. Longitudinal tear and tip of fibula**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: This is a tear of the peroneus brevis tendon. This is rare but commonly seen in athletes especially those with ankle instability. Most common pattern of tendon tear is longitudinal and centered at the level of the tip of the fibula. At this location, the tendon acutely changes direction, increasing the mechanical stress on the tendon. In addition, compression of the brevis between the peroneus longus tendon and the fibula also increases the mechanical stress on the tendon.

Answer: 1
