Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Pes Planus - Flat Foot

## Question

Which of the following etiological processes represents the primary embryological failure resulting in congenital rigid pes planus secondary to tarsal coalitions?

## Options

1. Ischemic necrosis of the cartilaginous precursors of the tarsal bones during the first trimester
2. Repetitive microtrauma during early childhood ambulation causing periosteal bridging across tarsal bones
3. Neurogenic hypertonicity of the peroneus brevis tendon leading to secondary adaptive osseous fusion
4. Failure of differentiation and segmentation of the primitive mesenchyme during fetal development

## Correct option

**4. Failure of differentiation and segmentation of the primitive mesenchyme during fetal development**

## Answer explanation

Congenital tarsal coalitions result from an in-utero failure of complete segmentation of the mesenchymal syncytium, leaving persistent tissue bridges that later fibrously transform, chondrify, or ossify.

Answer: 4
