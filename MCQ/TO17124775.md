## Question

What is true about bone remodeling?

## Options

1. In cancellous bone remodeling occurs through cutting cones
2. Bisphosphonate therapy fasten the remodeling rate
3. Cortical bone takes less time in remodeling than cancellous bone
4. In cortical bones remodeling occurs on the surface of lamellae

## Correct option

**3. Cortical bone takes less time in remodeling than cancellous bone**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Bone remodeling takes place in what Frost termed the Basic Multicellular Unit (BMU), which comprises the osteoclasts, osteoblasts, and osteocytes within the bone-remodeling cavity. In cancellous bone remodeling occurs on the surface of trabeculae and lasts about 200 days in normal bone.

In cortical bone remodeling proceeds in tunnels with osteoclasts forming “cutting cones” removing damaged bone followed by refilling by osteoblasts in the “closing cone” occurring behind the osteoclasts. In normal bone the duration of the remodeling cycle in cortical bone is shorter than in cancellous bone with a median of 120 days

The remodeling cycle can be as short as 100 days in thyrotoxicosis and primary hyperparathyroidism and exceed 1,000 days in low turnover states like Myxedema and after bisphosphonate treatment

Answer: 3
