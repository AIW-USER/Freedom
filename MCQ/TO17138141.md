Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Streeter's Dysplasia

## Question

All are true about Streeter band of soft tissue of leg except:

## Options

1. Is a rare condition 1:10000
2. Associated with other anomalies
3. Most were present in zone 1 of lower limb
4. Fractures of tibia and fibula may be present at the level of the band

## Correct option

**3. Most were present in zone 1 of lower limb**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: most of the bands are present in zone 2 of the leg (between knee and ankle)

ANSWER-C
