## Question

Which among the following statements regarding progression of curve in idiopathic scoliosis is wrong?

## Options

1. In infantile scoliosis the chance of progression is more if Mehta’s RVAD is more than 20deg.
2. Infantile curves with rib phase 1 (convex rib at the apex does not overlap the vertebral body) carries worse prognosis compared to curves with rib phase 2 (convex rib at apex overlaps the vertebral body).
3. In juvenile scoliosis, thoracic curves inevitably progressed to surgery despite bracing, whereas lumbar and thoracolumbar curves rarely progressed to the surgical range.
4. In juvenile scoliosis, thoracic curves inevitably progressed to surgery despite bracing, whereas lumbar and thoracolumbar curves rarely progressed to the surgical range.

## Correct option

**2. Infantile curves with rib phase 1 (convex rib at the apex does not overlap the vertebral body) carries worse prognosis compared to curves with rib phase 2 (convex rib at apex overlaps the vertebral body).**

## Answer explanation

- earlier and more rapid curve progression than other curve types (Lenke types 2, 4, 5, and 6).

Rib phase in infantile scoliosis:

Curves in rib phase 1- slow progression

Curves in rib phase 2- rapid progression

The rib–vertebral angle of Mehta= the difference formed by the apical vertebral body’s perpendicular and the two rib heads. Most (83%) resolving curves have an RVAD less than 20°, whereas most (again 83%) progressive curves have an RVAD of more than 20°.

answer: b
