## Question

A 30-year-old female presents with an enlarging mass in her right arm. An MRI was performed and can be seen in Figure A. She underwent a biopsy shown in Figure B. The tumor was also found to express estrogen receptor-beta. Which other neoplasm should this patient be screened for?

![[q210147-b-desmoid-tumor-histology.jpg]]

## Options

1. Colon cancer
2. Lung cancer
3. Breast cancer
4. Thyroid cancer
5. Renal cancer

## Correct option

**1. Colon cancer**

## Answer explanation

This patient has an extra-abdominal desmoid tumor which has been associated with Gardner syndrome and familial adenomatous polyposis (FAP). These patients are at high risk for colon cancer.

FAP is an autosomal dominant syndrome characterized by profuse adenomatous polyposis in the colon and rectum with nearly a 100% lifetime risk of colon cancer. This is caused by a mutation of the APC gene. Desmoid tumors are rare and histologically benign mesenchymal tumors, which grow aggressively and most are sporadic in nature. In patients with FAP, the prevalence is upwards of 21% and the risk is increased when there is a family history of desmoid tumors. Desmoid tumors may be found intra or extra-abdominally. The most common locations for extra-abdominal desmoid tumors are the shoulder, chest wall/back, and thigh. Desmoid tumors are also positive for estrogen receptor-beta expression.

*Koskenvuo et al. performed a study to evaluate the risk of FAP among patients with desmoid tumors by using endoscopy and an APC gene mutation test.* *Of 106 patients, 10 already had a diagnosis of FAP.* **They found** that the overall risk of FAP among patients with desmoid tumor was 4.8%. **They conclude** that patients with desmoid tumor have an elevated risk of FAP and should be screened accordingly.

*Slowik et al. wanted to identify the genetic mutations that put patients with FAP at high risk for desmoid tumors by performing a database study to identify patients with FAP and desmoid tumors.* **They found** that mutations in the APC gene from codons 1310 to 2011 were the most common region encompassing 48% of published desmoid cases. **They conclude** that there is an increased risk for desmoid tumors in patients with APC mutations between codons 1310-2011.

Figure A is a T2-weighted MRI slice demonstrating a desmoid tumor encasing the neurovascular bundle. Figure B is a photomicrograph demonstrating bland sheets of spindle cells in dense collagen stroma, characteristic of a desmoid tumor. Also note, that there is an absence of mitoses or nuclear atypia.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 2, 3, 4, & 5: Lung, breast, thyroid, or renal cancer have not been associated with desmoid tumors. These cancers are likely to metastasize to bone.
