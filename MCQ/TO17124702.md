## Question

Which muscle in the posterior compartment arises from the fibula only? (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. Tibialis posterior
2. FHL
3. Soleus
4. FDL

## Correct option

**2. FHL**

## Answer explanation

Muscles of anterior compartment of leg

Origin
Insertion

Tibialis anterior
shaft of tibia and interosseous membrane
medial cuneiform & base of first metatarsal

Extensor digitorum
shaft of fibula and interosseous membrane

Peroneus tertius
shaft of fibula & interosseous membrane
base of 5th metatarsal bone

Extensor hallucis longus
shaft of fibula & interosseous membrane
base of distal phalanx of big toe

Origin
Insertion

shaft of fibula

peroneus brevis
shaft of fibula

Origin

Flexor digitorum longus
shaft of tibia

Flexor hallucis longus
shaft of fibula

Tibialis posterior
shafts of tibia and fibula & interosseous membrane
tuberosity of navicular bone

Remember:

- In the anterior compartment of leg all muscles arise from fibula except tibialis anterior.
- In the lateral compartment all muscles arise from fibula
- In the posterior compartment only FHL arises from fibula. Soleus and tibialis posterior arise from both tibia and fibula.

Answer: 2
