Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Arthrogryposis Multiplex Congenital

## Question

What is correct sentence for treatment of club foot in arthrogryposis multiplex congenital?

## Options

1. Ponseti method is as effective as in idiopathic club feet
2. Ponseti method is effective but greater number of casts are required
3. Ponseti method is not effective due to severely rigid foot and require posteromedial release
4. Ponseti method is not used due to high relapse rate

## Correct option

**2. Ponseti method is effective but greater number of casts are required**

## Answer explanation

The Ponseti method of clubfoot casting has been used successfully in patients with arthrogryposis for clubfeet treatment, but a greater number of casts are required than for idiopathic clubfeet, and the relapse rate is high (27% at 2-year follow-up in one study).

Answer: 2
