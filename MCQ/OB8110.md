## Question

The peroneus brevis is the primary antagonist to which of the following structures?

## Options

1. Anterior tibialis
2. Posterior tibialis
3. Peroneus longus
4. Flexor hallucis longus
5. Extensor hallucis longus

## Correct option

**2. Posterior tibialis**

## Answer explanation

The primary function of the peroneus brevis is eversion of the foot, thus acting as the primary antagonist of the posterior tibialis, which inverts the foot, and secondarily plantar flexes the ankle. The anterior tibialis secondarily inverts the foot and only acts as a partial antagonist of the posterior tibialis. The peroneus longus plantar flexes the first ray.
