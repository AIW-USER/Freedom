Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Streeter's Dysplasia

## Question

Which of the following is not a feature of Streeters dysplasia?

## Options

1. Cleft palate
2. Clubfoot
3. Lymphedema
4. Complex syndactyly

## Correct option

**4. Complex syndactyly**

## Answer explanation

Orthopedic features of Constriction band syndrome include

• Cleft palate

• Cleft lip

• Club foot

• Cranio-facial defects

• Acro syndactyly

Lymphedema is a non-orthopedic manifestation of Constriction band syndrome Syndactyly in Constriction band syndrome is fenestrated / incomplete, and has slits between the digits proximally. It is also not complex because the bones are not fused. It is a simple incomplete Syndactyly.

Answer – d
