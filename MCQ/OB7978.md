## Question

Which of the following tumors is considered highly vascular?

## Options

1. Thyroid
2. Breast
3. Colon
4. Prostate
5. Lung

## Correct option

**1. Thyroid**

## Answer explanation

Preoperative embolization is often used for highly vascular lesions such as renal and thyroid metastases.
