## Question

Post spinal decompression surgery via TLIF done for L4-5 disc, a patient develops paraesthesia and weakness in right leg on day 3. Likely Cause is [FNB 2023]

## Options

1. Meningitis
2. Haematoma
3. Battered root syndrome
4. Infection

## Correct option

**2. Haematoma**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B
EXPLANATIONS: -
Postoperative neurological deficit after TLIF surgery can be classified based on timing of deficit: -
• Immediate post op period
Battered root syndrome- excessive root manipulation/ retraction during surgery.
Iatrogenic injury while putting screws / while doing decompression.
• Delayed onset within few days
Hematoma formation and root compression.
Gel foam related complications.

Post operative Neuro deficit due to Hematoma can occur in cervical/ thoracic and lumbar region.

Steps to prevent Hematoma related root compression:
• Achieving good hemostasis after surgery before closure
• Keeping a drain after surgery
• Remedial measures involve immediate re exploration and Hematoma evacuation
