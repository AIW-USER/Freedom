## Question

Which osteoporosis therapy has been uniquely associated with a reduction in all-cause mortality following a recent hip fracture?

## Options

1. Denosumab
2. Teriparatide
3. Zoledronic acid
4. Romosozumab

## Correct option

**3. Zoledronic acid**

## Answer explanation

The HORIZON-RFT (Recurrent Fracture Trial) demonstrated that a annual infusion of Zoledronic acid within 90 days of hip fracture repair reduced the risk of new fractures and all-cause mortality by.

So remember

Ibandronate does not reduce the risk of hip fractures.

Zoledronic acid is the only bisphosphonate that reduces the overall risk of mortality.

Answer: 3
