## Question

A lesion affecting the left optic tract will be manifested by a deficit in the

## Options

1. Nasal half of the visual field of both eyes
2. Nasal half of the right visual field and temporal half of the left visual field
3. No deficits unless the right optic tract was also affected
4. Temporal half of the right visual field and nasal half of the left visual field

## Correct option

**4. Temporal half of the right visual field and nasal half of the left visual field**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D

After the optic chiasma, all the field defects affect the homonymous field of the opposite side.

Meaning that with a lesion in left optic tract, a person will not be able to see the right half of visual fields in both the eyes. This field on right side is the temporal field and the left side is the nasal field.

Hence the above answer.
