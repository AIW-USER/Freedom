Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — Ankle Arthroscopy

## Question

True regarding ankle arthroscopy

## Options

1. 4th toe flexion is used to protect CPN while making anterolateral portal in ankle arthroscopy
2. Most common complication is neurapraxia of CPN
3. 4th toe extension is used to keep the CPN away from the field while making anterolateral portal
4. Dorsal medial cutaneous branch of CPN is most commonly injured nerve

## Correct option

**1. 4th toe flexion is used to protect CPN while making anterolateral portal in ankle arthroscopy**

## Answer explanation

4th toe flexion is used to protect CPN while making anterolateral portal in ankle arthroscopy. Neurapraxia of CPN is the most common neurovascular injury, specifically, the dorsal intermediate cutaneous branch. Most common complication of ankle arthroscopy is cartilage injury.

Answer: 1
