## Question

Which of the following is the greatest risk factor for a paradoxical embolus from a venous air embolus (VAE) when performing complex cervical spine deformity operations in the beach-chair position?

## Options

1. Nitrous oxide anesthesia
2. Persistent foramen ovale (PFO)
3. Severe hypotension
4. Obesity
5. Emphysema

## Correct option

**2. Persistent foramen ovale (PFO)**

## Answer explanation

Cervical spine procedures performed in the beach-chair, or seated position as compared to the prone position are associated with a greater risk of venous air embolus (VAE). The presence of a persistent foramen ovale (PFO) increases the risk of a paradoxical embolus from a VAE.

A paradoxical embolism refers to an embolus which is carried from the arterial to the venous side of circulation, or vice versa. Embolism of a thrombus, air, tumor, fat, or amniotic fluid of venous origin travels to the arterial side through an opening in the heart, such as a PFO.

*Engelhardt et al.* **found** that VAE occurred in 8 of 80 patients with a PFO in sitting or semi-sitting position. **They recommend** preoperative evaluation and detection of PFO in patients with surgery planned in the seated position, and if surgery is performed, additional monitoring and special care.

*Matjasko et al. reviewed the overall incidence of VAE in 554 patients undergoing neurosurgical procedures in the seated position.* **They found** VAE occurred in 3 of 554 patients, and are of the opinion that surgery in the seated position is safe in experienced hands with proper patient selection. They advised caution in patients with right to left intracardiac shunts (PFO), atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, severe hypertension, and cervical stenosis.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Nitrous oxide has not been shown confer a greater risk.
Answer 3: Caution with performing surgery in the seated position should be taken regarding patients with severe hypertension rather than hypotension.
Answer 4: Obesity itself has not been shown to have a greater risk.
Answer 5: Emphysema has not been shown to have a greater risk.
