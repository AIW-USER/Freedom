## Question

Most important static stabilizer of shoulder with arm in 45° of abduction and external rotation is (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. MGHL
2. IGHL
3. SGHL
4. Subscapularis

## Correct option

**1. MGHL**

## Answer explanation

Static stabilizers of shoulder:

Anterior static shoulder stability is provided by

Anterior band of IGHL (main restraint): provides static restraint with arm in 90° of abduction and external rotation

MGHL: provides static restraint with arm in 45° of abduction and external rotation

SGHL: provides static restraint with arm at the side

Answer: 1
