## Options

1. He has a mutation in alpha-1 type I collagen
2. He expresses a class I surface antigen encoded by the B locus in the major histocompatibility complex on chromosome 6
3. He has immunohistochemical positivity of cluster of differentiation 20
4. He has a mutation in exostosin-1
5. He expresses human leukocyte antigen-B8

## Correct option

**2. He expresses a class I surface antigen encoded by the B locus in the major histocompatibility complex on chromosome 6**

## Answer explanation

This patient is presenting with findings of ankylosing spondylitis. HLA-B27 is a class I surface antigen encoded by the B locus in the major histocompatibility complex on chromosome 6. Gene testing for HLA-B27 is positive in nearly 95% of patients with this disease.

Ankylosing spondylosis (AS) is a seronegative inflammatory disease characterized by inflammation of the axial skeleton. Up to 95% of patients are HLA-B27 positive. The pathognomonic feature of AS is a combination of inflammation and bony destruction at the sites of tendon insertions. Inflammatory cells invade the bone adjacent to entheseal attachments which cause increased bone formation and ankylosis of the affected joints. Ankylosis and motion loss of zygapophyseal joints leads to syndesmophyte formation which gives the appearance of the bamboo spine. Enthesopathy may also occur in peripheral joints as well. Other manifestations of AS may include inflammatory bowel disease, psoriasis, uveitis, iritis, pulmonary fibrosis, aortitis, and genitourinary problems.

*Kubiak et al. report that 15-20% of patients with AS have a positive history of the disease.* Carriers of the HLA-B27 gene have a 16-50% increased risk of developing AS. **They report** that patients with AS who are HLA-B27 positive develop the disease at a younger age. **They also report** that HLA-B27 status is not required to make a diagnosis of AS, however, it is helpful in the workup.

*Gensler et al. performed a review to discuss the classification, diagnosis, and management of juvenile onset spondyloarthritis.* Like its adult counterpart, HLA-B27 is positive. **They report** that bilateral grade 2 sacroiliitis or unilateral grade 3 or 4 sacroiliitis seen in radiographs assist in making the diagnosis. **They report** that the appearance of sacroiliac joint and spinal disease usually takes 5-10 years after initial symptom presentation. **They conclude** that patients with juvenile-onset ankylosing spondylitis appear to have poorer outcomes.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: An alpha-1 type I collagen mutation is found in osteogenesis imperfecta.
Answer 3: CD20 immunohistochemical positivity is suggestive of lymphoma.
Answer 4: A mutation in EXT-1 can lead to multiple hereditary exostoses.
Answer 5: HLA-B8 is strongly associated with DISH in patients with diabetes.
