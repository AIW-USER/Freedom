## Question

A 72-year-old female presents to the emergency department for evaluation of right arm pain after catching herself following a trip over her rug. The patient reports antecedent achy pain in her arm that has been slowly worsening for the past six months. Radiographs are obtained, which demonstrate an isolated, transverse proximal one-third ulnar shaft fracture in the setting of a thickened posterior ulnar cortex. A history of consuming which of the following medications would most likely be present?

## Options

1. Teriperatide
2. Alendronate
3. Semaglutide
4. Levothyroxine
5. Methotrexate

## Correct option

**2. Alendronate**

## Answer explanation

This 72-year-old female sustained a rather innocuous isolated transverse proximal one-third ulnar shaft fracture with a thickened posterior cortex. This suggests an atypical ulna fracture secondary to prolonged bisphosphonate use (Answer 2).

Atypical fractures stemming from chronic bisphosphonate use are notoriously associated with subtrochanteric femur fractures. However, recent literature has identified isolated proximal ulna fractures to correlate with chronic bisphosphonate use in a manner similar to subtrochanteric fractures. The fractures must exhibit multiple characteristics, which can be categorized into major and minor criteria, as described by the American Society for Bone and Mineral Research task force (Illustration 1). Additional findings suggesting a bisphosphonate-related fracture include its location, which has been found to occur at approximately 20 to 45% of the length of the ulna from the tip of the olecranon (Illustration 2). Outcomes for these fractures are similarly poor to subtrochanteric femur fractures, with a high rate of delayed or nonunion (50%) following fixation, which typically includes diseased bone resection with plate fixation.

*Heo et al. provide a multicenter case series of ten patients (12 fractures), detailing the demographics and outcomes of atypical ulna fractures.* All of the fractures occurred in females who were on bisphosphonates for an average of 9.8 years and occurred at approximately 35.1% of the ulnar length from the olecranon tip. All fractures were treated surgically, with 50% experiencing either delayed or nonunion. **The authors conclude** that resection of sclerotic bone with plate fixation is the ideal treatment to mitigate the risk of complications.

*Tan et al. similarly performed a systematic review of the at-the-time (2015) case reports (seven patients, eight fractures) on atypical ulnar shaft fractures in those on bisphosphonates.* **The authors noted** a predilection for the dominant limb to be affected, where the span of time on bisphosphonates ranged from seven to 15 years. **They also noted** that the fractures appeared similarly to subtrochanteric fractures and possessed multiple of the aforementioned major and minor characteristics.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Teriparatide is a recombinant parathyroid hormone and is the only anabolic osteoporotic agent (when given intermittently) currently available in the US
Answer 3: Semaglutide is a glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor antagonist purposed for diabetes and weight loss. No association between atypical bone fractures and the medication has been established.
Answer 4: Levothyroxine is a synthetic form of thyroxine used to treat hypothyroidism and does not have any association with atypical bone fractures.
Answer 5: Methotrexate is a folate analogue that is used for a number of conditions (rheumatoid arthritis, lupus) and does not have an association with atypical bone fractures.
