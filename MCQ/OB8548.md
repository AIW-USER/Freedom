## Question

Where is the physis with the highest growth rate (in mm per year) located?

## Options

1. Proximal humerus
2. Distal femur
3. Distal tibia
4. Distal radius

## Correct option

**2. Distal femur**

## Answer explanation

The distal femur averages 9 mm to 11 mm in growth per year, the proximal humerus 7 mm per year, the distal tibia 4 mm to 5 mm per year, and the distal radius 5 mm to 6 mm per year.
