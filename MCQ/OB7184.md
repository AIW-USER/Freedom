## Question

An osteoprogenitor cell is expected to commit to a bone lineage in the presence of what transcription factor?

## Options

1. PPARy2
2. MyoD
3. Sox 9
4. C/EBPa
5. Runx2

## Correct option

**5. Runx2**

## Answer explanation

Mesenchymal stem cells are pluripotent cells that can differentiate into many lineages including osteoblasts, adipocytes, myoblasts, chondroblasts, and fibroblasts. Runx2 and Osx appear to be required for differentiation to osteoblastic lines. PPARy2 and C/EBPa are transcription factors leading to adipocytes, MyoD promotes myoblasts, and Sox 9 corresponds to chondroblasts.
