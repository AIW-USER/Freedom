## Question

What is the inheritance pattern for Duchenne's muscular dystrophy?

## Options

1. autosomal recessive
2. autosomal dominant
3. X-linked recessive
4. X-linked dominant
5. random

## Correct option

**3. X-linked recessive**

## Answer explanation

Duchenne's muscular dystrophy is an X-linked recessive disorder which is caused by a mutation in the dystrophin gene. X-linked recessive inheritance is a mode of inheritance in which a mutation in a gene on the X chromosome causes the phenotype to be expressed (1) in males (who are necessarily hemizygous for the gene mutation because they have only one X chromosome) and (2) in females who are homozygous for the gene mutation (i.e., they have a copy of the gene mutation on each of their two X chromosomes). This means that if women are carriers (XX) then their sons (XY) have a 50% chance of inheriting the disease. Other common X-linked recessive disorders include color blindness, hemophilia A, hemophilia B, and Becker's Muscular dystrophy. *The referenced article by Sussman et al is a review articles summarizing the pathophysiology, evaluation, and treatment of Duchenne muscular dystrophy.* Illustration A depicts X-linked recessive inheritance.
