## Question

A 36 years female patient presents with complaints of posterior knee pain and difficulty in knee bending. She gives a history of knee injury 1 year back. She also finds heaviness in the knee on climbing stairs and prolonged standing. A Sagittal view of the MRI is given below. Which is the correct statement?

![[TO17118643-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Treatment is arthroscopic reconstruction of ACL
2. Treatment is arthroscopic debulking
3. Treatment is close and then open kinetic chain exercises for partial ACL tear
4. Treatment is arthroscopic PCL reconstruction

## Correct option

**2. Treatment is arthroscopic debulking**

## Answer explanation

This is a case of Mucoid degeneration of the ACL. Treatment is arthroscopic debulking of the ACL.

Mucoid degeneration of ACL: Mucoid degeneration (MD) of the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) is a rare pathological entity with disputed theories of origin. It is characterized by infiltration of mucoid like substance (glycosaminoglycans) interspersed within the substance of ACL causing knee pain and limited motion.

The most common and consistent symptom is knee pain, mostly posterior. Hypothetically, it is caused due to mechanical impingement on the PCL and posterior capsule or by causing bone erosions. Knee pain during flexion is probably due to the tightening of the anteromedial bundle. Knee pain during terminal extension has also been described. Debridement or debulking of ACL relieves posterior impingement.

Answer: 2
