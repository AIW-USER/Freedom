## Question

Correct sequence of graft fixation in combined ACL and PCL fixation is (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. PCL femoral tunnel, ACL femoral tunnel, PCL tibial tunnel, ACL tibial tunnel
2. PCL tibial tunnel, PCL femoral tunnel, ACL tibial tunnel, ACL femoral tunnel
3. ACL femoral tunnel, PCL femoral tunnel, ACL tibial tunnel, ACL femoral tunnel
4. PCL femoral tunnel, PCL tibial tunnel, ACL femoral tunnel, ACL tibial tunnel

## Correct option

**1. PCL femoral tunnel, ACL femoral tunnel, PCL tibial tunnel, ACL tibial tunnel**

## Answer explanation

Sequence of graft fixation in combined ACL and PCL reconstruction

- Pass PCL graft through tibial tunnel and fix in femoral tunnelHICH
- Pass ACL graft through tibial tunnel and fix in femoral tunnel
- Fix PCL graft on tibia at 90° of flexion with anteromedial step off
- Fix ACL graft on tibia at 30-degree flexion.

Answer: 1
