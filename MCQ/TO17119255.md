Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — Multiligamentous Knee Injuries

## Question

What is the management for MCL grade 1 injury with ACL tear? (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. ACL reconstruction with MCL repair
2. Brace for 1 month in 30 degrees of flexion
3. Conservative
4. Knee brace for 2 weeks then ACL reconstruction.

## Correct option

**4. Knee brace for 2 weeks then ACL reconstruction.**

## Answer explanation

Management of ACL plus MCL tear

Complete ACL tear in young patients require ACL reconstruction. MCL tear management depends on the grade of MCL tear and associated injuries.

Indications of acute repair of MCL

• Presence of intra articular ligamentous entrapment

• A large bony avulsion

• Associated tibial plateau fracture

• MRI finding of complete tibial side avulsion in athletes

• Presence of anteromedial rotational instability

 • Presence of valgus instability in 0 degrees of flexion in an underlying valgus knee alignment

Indication of delayed repair of MCL

- Combined with anterior cruciate or other ligament reconstruction if the examination under anesthesia shows valgus laxity in 0 degrees of flexion

Indication for MCL reconstruction

- Chronic valgus instability

Conservative management of MCL

MCL has high healing potential and conservative management is successful in more than 90 % of cases. Grade 1 MCL tear requires a hinged knee brace locked at 30 degrees for 1-2 weeks. Grade 2 MCL tear requires hinged knee brace locked at 30 degrees for 2-3 weeks and grade 3 tear for 3-4 weeks. Knee ROM exercises are gradually started and once pain free knee movement up to 90 is achieved, then ACL reconstruction is done.

Answer: 4
