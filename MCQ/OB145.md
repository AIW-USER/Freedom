## Question

Salter-Harris type I fractures typically occur through which zone of the physis?

## Options

1. Resting zone
2. Proliferative zone
3. Zone of maturation
4. Zone of degeneration
5. Zone of provisional calcification

## Correct option

**5. Zone of provisional calcification**

## Answer explanation

Physeal fractures occur in zone of provisional calcification, which is a part of the hypertrophic zone. The transition from the soft cartilage to to the hard calcified metaphysis puts this zone at risk for fracture.
