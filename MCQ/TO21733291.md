#TO #hand #congenitaltriggerthumb
Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Miscellaneous (Hand  Wrist)

## Question

First treatment for a flexible trigger thumb in a 1 year old girl

## Options

1. release of A1 pulley
2. extension splitting
3. passive thumb extension exercises
4. passive thumb extension exercises plus Intermittent extension splinting

## Correct option

**4. passive thumb extension exercises plus Intermittent extension splinting**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 4
EXPLANATION: -
Treatment of pediatric trigger thumb
Nonoperative: with passive thumb extension exercises 30-60% flexible deformities (which are not fixed) will resolve spontaneously before the age of 2 years.

Intermittent extension splinting is more successful than exercise alone. Along with passive extension exercises it is the first line of treatment for flexible deformities in children before 2 years. 50-60% deformities resolve before the age of two years.

Operative: A1 pulley release is indicated for fixed deformity beyond age of 12 months of age and for failed conservative treatment.
