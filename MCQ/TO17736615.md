## Question

Most common site for Osteoblastoma (orthopaedic oncology- benign tumors and tumor like lesions-osteoblastoma)

## Options

1. Thoracic spine
2. Lumbar spine
3. Sacrum
4. Cervical spine

## Correct option

**4. Cervical spine**

## Answer explanation

Ans : D

A definitive consensus regarding the most frequent spinal location of osteoblastoma is absent in the existing literature. Nevertheless, the following information may be consulted pertinent to this inquiry.

Almost 40-50% of Osteoblastomas are located in the spine (predominantly involving the posterior elements). Cervical > Lumbar spine are predominant spinal segments involved followed by thoracic spine and then sacrum. Some authors have reported equal frequency in cervical, thoracic and lumbar spine with sacrum being involved in almost 17% cases. About long bone lesions, they are mostly diaphyseal in location and femur is the most common site. Rarely metaphyseal lesions can also be seen but they are never epiphyseal.
