## Question

True regarding screw home mechanism is (regional orthopedics- knee- kinematics)

## Options

1. With knee flexion the normal tibia rotates externally
2. With knee flexion the normal tibia rotates internally
3. With knee extension the normal tibia rotates internally
4. With knee extension the normal femur rotates externally

## Correct option

**2. With knee flexion the normal tibia rotates internally**

## Answer explanation

With knee flexion, the normal tibia typically internally rotates relative to the femur and conversely, externally rotates with knee extension (i.e., screw home mechanism)

Answer : B
