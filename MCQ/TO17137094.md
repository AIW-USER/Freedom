Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Fracture Dislocations of Hand

## Question

Indications for surgical fixation of metacarpal fracture include all except

## Options

1. Malrotation of index finger 15 degree
2. Sagittal angulation of 15 degree in ring finger
3. Shortening of 7 mm
4. Open fracture

## Correct option

**2. Sagittal angulation of 15 degree in ring finger**

## Answer explanation

Angulation in the sagittal plane (e.g., apex volar/dorsal) has variable tolerability based on each metacarpal bone. The tolerability is decreased moving from ulnar to radial with the index and middle metacarpal shaft only able to tolerate about 10 degrees. The ring finger can tolerate close to 25 degrees while the small finger can tolerate up to 45 degrees. Malrotation or scissoring is the least tolerated of the deformities. Any deformity greater than 10 degrees, which causes the adjacent finger to impinge or cross over, requires surgical intervention.

Indications for metacarpal fracture fixation

- Malrotation >10 degrees or any scissoring
- Apex dorsal angulation of the shaft

Index: >10

Middle finger >10

Ring finger >25

Small finger >45

- Open fractures
- Multiple fractures
- Associated CMC dislocation
- Intra-articular fracture
- Extensor lag
- Shortening >5 to 6 mm

Answer: 2
