## Question

You're asked to review an article relating to outcomes following various fixation techniques for distal femur fractures. The authors report a statistically significant p-value of 0.03. Which of the following represents the meaning of a p-value?

## Options

1. Probability of a type I error
2. Probability of a type II error
3. Probability that test results will be positive in patients with disease
4. Probability test result will be negative in patients without disease
5. Probability a study will be able to detect a true effect of the intervention

## Correct option

**1. Probability of a type I error**

## Answer explanation

The p-value is the probability of a type I error occurring.

The p-value is a number, calculated from a statistical test, that describes how likely you are to have found a particular set of observations if the null hypothesis were true. The probability of making a type I error is represented by your significance or alpha level (α), which is the p-value below which you reject the null hypothesis.

*Kocher et al. review clinical epidemiology and biostatistics.* **They report** that the p-value is the probability of a type I error occurring. **They conclude** that by convention, the alpha level of significance is set at 0.05, which means that we accept the finding of a significant association if there is less than a one in twenty chance that the observed association was due to chance alone.

*Staffa et al. review statistical power and sample size.* *They report on a general 5-step approach for performing a sample size justification and statistical power analysis.* **They conclude** that understanding sample size considerations and statistical power in the surgical research community will improve the quality of published articles.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: Power represents one minus the probability of a type II error.
Answer 3: Sensitivity is the probability that test results will be positive in patients with the disease.
Answer 4: Specificity is the probability test result will be negative in patients without the disease.
Answer 5: Power is an estimate of the probability a study will be able to detect a true effect of the intervention.
