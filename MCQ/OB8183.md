## Question

Figures 47a and 47b are the radiograph and CT scan of a 45-year-old man who was involved in a high-speed motor vehicle accident. What is the most appropriate treatment?

![[q8183-a-47a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q8183-b-47b.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Subtalar arthrodesis
2. Percutaneous screw fixation
3. Closed reduction and cast application
4. Open reduction and internal fixation
5. Non-weight-bearing and early range of motion

## Correct option

**4. Open reduction and internal fixation**

## Answer explanation

The radiograph and CT scan show a displaced talar neck fracture that is best treated with anatomic reduction and stable internal fixation. Neither non-weight-bearing and early range of motion nor closed reduction and cast application address the unstable fracture or restore articular congruity. The fracture is displaced with an interposed fragment in the fracture line and therefore requires open reduction prior to screw placement. Subtalar arthrodesis should be reserved for talar fractures with severe injury to the subtalar joint.
