## Question

Which is not a position for elbow arthroscopy

## Options

1. Elbow flexed, Prone
2. Elbow flexed, Supine
3. Elbow extended, prone
4. Lateral decubitus

## Correct option

**3. Elbow extended, prone**

## Answer explanation

Lateral position with the elbow freely hanging on the support (Elbow flexed position) is the most commonly used position for elbow arthroscopy.
