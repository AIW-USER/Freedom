## Question

Which of the following is associated with a mutation in the GNAS gene?

## Options

1. Fibrosarcoma
2. Chondroblastoma
3. Fibrous dysplasia
4. Osteoblastoma
5. Osteofibrous dysplasia

## Correct option

**3. Fibrous dysplasia**

## Answer explanation

Fibrous dysplasia is associated with a mutation in the GNAS gene.

Fibrous dysplasia is a disorder where normal bone and marrow is replaced with fibrous tissue, resulting in the formation of bone that is weak and prone to expansion. It is caused by a spontaneous mutation in the GNAS gene, which affects the alpha subunit of the Gs protein that regulates adenylate cyclase. This mutation leads to the constitutive activation of adenylate cyclase, resulting in an overproduction of cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cAMP). In bone, constitutive Gs-alpha signaling results in impaired differentiation and proliferation of bone marrow stromal cells, leading to the replacement of normal bone and marrow with fibrous tissue.

*Weinstein published a review on G(s)alpha mutations in fibrous dysplasia and McCune-Albright syndrome.* **He reported** that fibrous dysplasia results from increased cAMP in bone marrow stromal cells, leading to increased proliferation and abnormal differentiation.

*Blanco et al. performed a study on mutations of the GNAS1 gene, stromal cell dysfunction, and osteomalacic changes in non-McCune-Albright fibrous dysplasia of bone (non-MAS FD).* **They found** that occurrence of GNAS1 mutations, previously noted in individual cases of FD, is a common and perhaps constant finding in non-MAS FD. **They concluded** that FD, MAS, and nonskeletal isolated endocrine lesions associated with GNAS1 mutations represent a spectrum of phenotypic expressions of the same basic disorder

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1, 2, 4, and 5: These conditions are not associated with a mutation in the GNAS gene.
