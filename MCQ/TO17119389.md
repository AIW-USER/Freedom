## Question

A footballer injured his knee while playing. He landed with knee flexion and a foot that was plantar flexed. Ligament that would likely get injured in this scenario (FNB 2022)

## Options

1. ACL
2. PCL
3. PLC
4. LCL

## Correct option

**2. PCL**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

The mechanism described in the question is classically one of a dashboard type injury and is well simulated in the picture below.

![[TO17119389-1.png]]

In above picture, the athlete can be seen slipping as the foot gets caught on ground in plantar flexion and one falls with flexed knee hitting the ground. The posterior force on tibia would lead to tibia sub luxating posteriorly thereby injuring the PCL.
