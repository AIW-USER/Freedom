## Question

Which of the following is true regarding Anterior approach to Cervical spine? (FNB Patter Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Left sided approach is preferred as there are more chances of damaging Recurrent laryngeal nerve in a right sided approach
2. Either approach can be used depending on site of lesion and surgeon comfort as chances of nerve damage are similar
3. Right sided approach is preferred as there are more chances of damaging Recurrent laryngeal nerve in a left sided approach
4. Any approach may be used, the chances of damage are always more to the opposite sided recurrent laryngeal nerve

## Correct option

**1. Left sided approach is preferred as there are more chances of damaging Recurrent laryngeal nerve in a right sided approach**

## Answer explanation

It is well known that the recurrent laryngeal nerve is often damaged during or following an anterior approach to the cervical vertebral column. Reports in the literature suggest that the right-sided approach gives rise to more complications of the recurrent nerve than the left-sided approach and that the right recurrent nerve is in a less protected position.

Answer: 1
