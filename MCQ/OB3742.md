## Question

Figure A is axial CT scan of the lumbar spine. The line from X to Y represents the AP canal diameter. The line from Z to W represents the right-to-left diameter of the canal. The circle represents cross-sectional area of the canal. Which of the following statements would define critical stenosis?

![[q3742-a-area-xy.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. The distance from X to Y is less than 10mm
2. The distance from Z to W is less than 20mm
3. The area of the blue circle is 130mm2
4. The combined distance from X to Y and Z to W is less than 30mm
5. None of the above

## Correct option

**1. The distance from X to Y is less than 10mm**

## Answer explanation

Critical lumbar spinal stenosis is defined by an AP canal diameter of less than 10 mm or a cross-sectional area of less than 100 mm2, as measured on an axial CT.

Lumbar spinal stenosis is a common degenerative condition that leads to the symptoms of neurogenic claudication characterized by bilateral buttock and posterior thigh pain with walking that improve with sitting. The diagnosis is usually made based on sagittal and axial MRI images. Ocassionally in patients that dont tolerate an MRI a CT scan or CT myelogram is peformed.

*Alsaleh et al. compared the reliability of quantitative and qualitative assessment of lumbar central stenosis between MRI and CT imaging modalities.* Historically, CT and CT myelogram was utilized to define and diagnose critical stenosis, however, in this study, by also taking into account the soft tissue structures, MRI has a higher inter-observer reliability and lower rate of stenosis over-estimation, when compared to CT.

Cowley in this systematic review, discusses the superiority of MRI in diagnosing central spinal stenosis, however, also discusses the utility of CT and CT myelogram (CTM). Especially in regards to a concomitant ossified posterior longitundinal ligament as well as in clinical scenarios where MRI is contraindicated (i.e. pacemaker, defibrillator), the author urges the reader to not forget the use of CT and CTM to guide proper treatment.
