Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Simple Unicameral Bone Cyst

## Question

Which is not a recommended treatment of simple bone cyst? (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Curettage
2. Injection of polidocanol
3. Injection of DBM
4. Surgical decompression

## Correct option

**2. Injection of polidocanol**

## Answer explanation

Simple bone cyst or unicameral bone cyst (UBC) mostly affects children and adolescents.

X ray features: mildly expansile, lytic thin-walled bone lesion without periosteal reaction. Most common site is the metaphysis adjacent to the growth plate of the tubular bones, mostly the humerus and femur. Based on the distance between the cyst and the growth plate, UBC is classified as active when the distance is less than 5 mm, and latent when the distance is larger than 5 mm

Treatment of UBC includes

- Injection: methylprednisolone acetate/ autologous bone marrow/demineralized bone matrix/ combination
- Curettage and bone grafting (autologous/allograft)/calcium sulfate pallets.
- Surgical decompression: intramedullary nail/CC screws etc.

Answer: 2
