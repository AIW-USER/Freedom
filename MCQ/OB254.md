## Question

What is the most common site of metastasis for osteosarcoma?

## Options

1. Bone
2. Liver
3. Lung
4. Kidney
5. Lymph nodes

## Correct option

**3. Lung**

## Answer explanation

The most common site of metastasis for osteosarcoma is the lung via hematogenous spread. When a patient is diagnosed with osteosarcoma, a chest CT is recommended as part of the initial staging process. Bone is the second most common site.
