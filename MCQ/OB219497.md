## Question

While you are perusing the literature, you have encountered multiple studies evaluating the potential benefit of incisional negative pressure wound therapy (iNPWT) for total joint arthroplasty. Which of the following procedures most clearly benefits from iNPWT?

## Options

1. Unicompartmental knee arthroplasty
2. Primary total knee arthroplasty
3. Primary total hip arthroplasty using direct anterior approach
4. Primary total hip arthroplasty using posterior approach
5. Revision total knee arthroplasty

## Correct option

**5. Revision total knee arthroplasty**

## Answer explanation

Incisional negative pressure wound therapy (iNPWT) reduces the rate of wound complications after revision knee and hip arthroplasty (Answer 5).

Revision knee and hip arthroplasty are at an elevated risk of complications compared to primary arthroplasty. Wound complications are at particular risk due to prior surgical incisions, other host factors, and revision arthroplasty often being a more lengthy surgery compared to primary arthroplasty. In the setting of revision hip/knee arthroplasty, iNPWT has been shown to reduce wound complications and should be employed accordingly.

*Newman et al. performed a randomized controlled trial (RCT) evaluating the efficacy of incisional negative pressure wound therapy (iNPWT) after revision total hip arthroplasty (THA) and total knee arthroplasty (TKA) in patients at high risk for infection.* **They found** that the control group had a higher rate of wound complications and a higher reoperation rate, but there was no difference in the rate of readmission. **They concluded** that iNPWT may decrease the rate of wound complications for patients who are at high risk.

*Ailaney et al. performed a systematic review and meta-analysis of iNPWT for THA and TKA.* **They found** that was associated with a lower rate of surgical site infections for revision arthroplasty, but that iNPWT increased the rate of noninfectious complications such as wound blistering. iNPWT was also associated with decreased hospital length of stay and reoperation rate. **They concluded** that iNPWT plays a beneficial role in revision arthroplasty but that additional RCTs are required.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1 and 2: UKA and primary TKA have not been found to have the same wound complication benefit that revision TKA has.
Answer 3 and 4: Primary THA, regardless of surgical approach, has not been found to have the same benefit in wound complications that revision THA has.
