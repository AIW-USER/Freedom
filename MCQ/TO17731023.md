## Question

Shoulder approximation in front of chest is characteristic of (Congenital and developmental bone disorders- Cleidocranial Dysplasia (Dysostosis)

## Options

1. Ectodermal dysplasia
2. Multiple epiphyseal dysplasia
3. Cleidocranial dysplasia
4. Glenoid dysplasia

## Correct option

**3. Cleidocranial dysplasia**

## Answer explanation

Cleidocranial dysplasia is an autosomal dominant genetic skeletal dysplasia in which hypoplasia of the clavicles and deficient ossification of the anterior fontanelle are the major features. The defective clavicles permit undue mobility of the shoulders, which can often be approximated anteriorly. Affected persons have a characteristic facial appearance with a bulky forehead, hypertelorism, pointed jaw and midfacial hypoplasia (central depression).

General health is usually good and the intellect is unimpaired. Supernumerary teeth (hyperdontia) in the primary and secondary dentition may lead to dental crowding and malocclusion. Skeletal abnormalities may also occur, including short stature, short terminal phalanges, scoliosis, kyphosis, genu valgum (knock knees), and pes planus. Progressive scoliosis or syringomyelia may be observed.

Answer: 3
