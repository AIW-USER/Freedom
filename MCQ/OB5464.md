## Question

A 47-year-old diabetic female with long-standing peripheral neuropathy presents with worsening foot pain over the past 3 days with associated redness and swelling. A lateral foot radiograph is shown in Figure A. Which imaging modality is the most sensitive to diagnose soft tissue infection?

![[q5464-a-figure-a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Radiographs alone
2. Bone scan
3. Ultrasound
4. MRI with and without contrast
5. CT scan with and without contrast

## Correct option

**4. MRI with and without contrast**

## Answer explanation

In patients with Charcot arthropathy, the most sensitive test to diagnose soft tissue (and bone) infection is MRI.

Regarding advanced imaging modalities, MRI remains the most sensitive in diagnosing soft tissue and bone infections in the diabetic foot. In those studies that are equivocal, adding bone scan may help.

*Anakwenze et al. offer the most recent review and guidelines in this JAAOS management article.* While CT and radiographs may offer signs of infection (i.e. air, eroding bony architecture), MRI remains the gold standard in assessing and diagnosing osteomyelitis as well as infection of the surrounding soft tissue structures. In equivocal studies, bone scan may also offer assistance, but when used independently remains inferior to MRI for sensitivity and specificity.

*Lipsky et al. offer the most recent guidelines from the infectious disease standpoint and mirror the guidelines set forth by the AAOS.* *They also cite level 1 evidence that reports that MRI is more specific for diagnosis of infection in the diabetic foot, when compared to bone scan.*

Figure A exhibits a lateral foot radiograph with significant Charcot arthropathy.

Incorrect answers:
Answer 1: Radiographs alone may offer advanced changes of infection (i.e. erosion, bone loss), but these findings are not specific for infection.
Answer 2: Ultrasound may help find fluid collections but is not as specific as MRI.
Answer 3,5: Other advanced imaging studies may be helpful and diagnostic, however, MRI is more sensitive and specific.
