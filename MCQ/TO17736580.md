## Question

Most commonly fractured carpal bone is (trauma- Fractures around wrist and hand-scaphoid fracture)

## Options

1. Scaphoid
2. Lunate
3. Triquetral
4. Pisiform

## Correct option

**1. Scaphoid**

## Answer explanation

Scaphoid is most commonly fractured carpal bone in the hand followed by triquetrum and then trapezium.

 Answer: A
