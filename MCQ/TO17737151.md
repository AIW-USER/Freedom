## Question

A sportsman is on Propranolol for high blood pressure. The sport that will be allowed for him would be [DRUGS & DOPPING IN SPORTS]

## Options

1. Billiards
2. Hockey
3. Dart/ Archery
4. Shooting

## Correct option

**2. Hockey**

## Answer explanation

Beta blockers cause decreased heart rate, reduction in hand tremor, and anxiolysis. These effects may be performance-enhancing in sports in which it is beneficial to have increased steadiness, such as archery, shooting, and billiards.

Answer: 2
