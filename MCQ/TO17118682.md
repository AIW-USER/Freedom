Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — ACL

## Question

BEAR implant is meant for

## Options

1. Ankle syndesmotic repair
2. ACL repair
3. AVN femoral head with collapse
4. A focal chondral defect in knee

## Correct option

**2. ACL repair**

## Answer explanation

Bridge-Enhanced Anterior Cruciate Ligament Repair [BEAR]

ACL traditionally has been reconstructed owing to poor cellularity in the ligament that limits the healing potential of a native repair. However, need for a graft and fixation implant are the main negative points associated with a reconstruction.

Hence, scientific interest in ACL repair has always been high.

BEAR has come up as a recent development to make an ACL repair a success story. Bridge-enhanced anterior cruciate ligament repair (BEAR) combines suture repair of the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) with a special extracellular matrix scaffold (the BEAR scaffold) that is placed in the gap between the torn ends of the ACL to facilitate ligament healing.

During the BEAR procedure, stitches are placed in the torn ends of the ACL using suture passers. Then a sponge-like BEAR implant (that is injected with the patient's own blood to introduce growth factors) is placed in the gap. The BEAR Implant acts as a bridge to help the torn ends of the ACL heal together. In about 8 weeks of implantation, the BEAR implant gets absorbed by the body and gets replaced by native cells that go on to become the natural ligament with the natural properties.
