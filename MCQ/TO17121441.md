## Question

Detachment of anterior inferior labrum with medially stripped but intact periosteum is called as

## Options

1. Bankart’s lesion
2. GLAD lesion
3. ALPSA lesion
4. Perthes lesion

## Correct option

**4. Perthes lesion**

## Answer explanation

Ans : (D)

Bankart’s lesion: Detachment of anterior inferior labrum from the glenoid rim. The medial periosteum

hinge is undisrupted.Perthes lesion: Detachment of anterior inferior labrum with medially stripped (but intact) periosteum.

ALPSA: Anterior labral periosteal sleeve avulsion where there is grossly medially displaced labrum and capsule ligamentous complex with just an intact hinge medially and fluid collection underneath

GLAD: Glenohumeral articular disruption represent a labral tear with glenoid articular cartilage disruption
