Chapter: Sports Medicine MCQs — Female Athlete

## Question

Female athlete triad include all except (FNB Pattern Paper 2019]

## Options

1. Dysmenorrhea
2. Stress fracture
3. Low energy availability with or without eating disorder
4. Low bone density

## Correct option

**2. Stress fracture**

## Answer explanation

ANSWER-B

Female athlete triad:

The female athlete triad (the triad) refers to a constellation of 3 clinical entities: menstrual dysfunction, low energy availability (with or without an eating disorder), and decreased bone mineral density (BMD).

Many athletes with low bone density and/or menstrual irregularity suffer from stress fractures, but stress fracture is not a part of the female athlete triad. The most frequent site of stress fracture is the tibia. Amenorrheic athletes have 2 to 4 times greater risk for stress fracture than their eumenorrheic counterparts.
