## Question

Which is not a positioning goal of the component in TKR in kinematic alignment?

## Options

1. Neutral rotation relative to PCL
2. Tibial slope parallel to medial plateau
3. Axial rotation of tibial component in line with tibial tubercle
4. Axial rotation of tibial component parallel to long axis of lateral tibial plateau

## Correct option

**3. Axial rotation of tibial component in line with tibial tubercle**

## Answer explanation

Goals of component position in mechanical and kinematic alignment:

 Mechanical alignment
 Kinematic alignment

 Flexion of femoral component
 Similar

 Distal femoral cut
 Perpendicular to femoral mechanical axis
 Parallel to distal femoral articular surface after correcting for cartilage wear

 Posterior femoral cut
 3° external rotation relative to PCL
 Neutral rotation (0°) relative to PCL

 Tibial cut
 Perpendicular to tibial mechanical axis
 Parallel to frontal tibial articular surface after correcting for cartilage wear

 Tibial slope
 2° to 7° posterior slope
 Parallel to medial plateau

 Axial rotation of tibial component
 Aligned with tibial tubercle
 Parallel to long axis of lateral tibial plateau
