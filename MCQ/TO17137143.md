Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Lunate and Perilunate Dislocation

## Question

Which of the following is true regarding a Mayfield Stage I injury?

## Options

1. There is not always a scaphoid fracture.
2. There is a lunotriquetral ligament injury.
3. The lunate is extruded.
4. There is a radio-scapho-capitate ligament detachment.

## Correct option

**1. There is not always a scaphoid fracture.**

## Answer explanation

ANSWER-A

To understand carpal instability, it is essential to appreciate the ligamentous attachments

both between the individual carpal bones as well as the extrinsic ligaments that support the

wrist. The Mayfield staging is thus summarized:

![[TO17137143-1.png]]

Stage I – scapholunate dissociation/scaphoid fracture/ scaphoid rotation

Stage II – lunocapitate dislocation/ perilunate dislocation

Stage III – lunotriquetral disruption/triquetrum dislocation

Stage IV – lunate dislocation
