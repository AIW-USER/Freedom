## Question

Post-operative protocol after Q-snip exposure in TKR is (Arthroplasty- knee arthroplasty- surgical approaches)

## Options

1. Rehabilitation is slow as immobilization is needed
2. Rehabilitation is fast compared to midline parapatellar approach
3. Rehabilitation is as same as midline parapatellar approach
4. Rehabilitation is slow and some extension lag may occur due to quadriceps weakness

## Correct option

**3. Rehabilitation is as same as midline parapatellar approach**
