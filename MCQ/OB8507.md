## Question

Figure 49a is the photograph of the axilla and Figure 49b is the radiograph of the spine of a 13-year-old child referred for scoliosis treatment by a school screening program. What is the most likely diagnosis?

## Options

1. Idiopathic scoliosis
2. Nondystrophic scoliosis in neurofibromatosis I (NF1)
3. Nondystrophic scoliosis in NF2
4. Dystrophic scoliosis in NF2

## Correct option

**2. Nondystrophic scoliosis in neurofibromatosis I (NF1)**

## Answer explanation

Scoliosis is the most common skeletal manifestation of NF1. Nondystrophic scoliosis is less common than dystrophic scoliosis. Its clinical appearance and behavior mimic idiopathic scoliosis. Dystrophic scoliosis is recognized earlier than nondystrophic. It is characterized by a sharp angular curve involving 4 to 6 vertebrae. It is progressive and more difficult to treat. Scoliosis is not found in NF2. Axillary freckling is common in NF1 or NF2.
