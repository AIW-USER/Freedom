Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Cerebral Palsy (CP)

## Question

Dynamic EMG in a patient with crouch gait shows hamstring activity persisting throughout the stance phase. What does this 'stance phase spasticity' signify?

## Options

1. An indication that hamstring lengthening will likely fail
2. That the primary pathology is at the ankle (Equinus)
3. A normal compensatory mechanism for weak hip extensors
4. That the patient is a candidate for Rectus Femoris Transfer

## Correct option

**1. An indication that hamstring lengthening will likely fail**

## Answer explanation

Rectus femoris transfer addresses swing phase pathology (stiff knee), not stance phase crouch.

Answer: 1
