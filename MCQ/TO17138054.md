## Question

Each of the following statements is true about AVMs, except

## Options

1. Higher pressures have been measured in the feeding arteries of smaller as compared to larger AVMs.
2. Smaller AVMs are more likely to bleed than larger AVMs.
3. The annual risk of death from a ruptured AVM is 1%.
4. The risk of re bleed in the first year after hemorrhage is highest in the first 2 weeks.

## Correct option

**4. The risk of re bleed in the first year after hemorrhage is highest in the first 2 weeks.**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D

The risk of re bleed from an AVM in the first year after hemorrhage is as high as 6 to 18% but this risk is evenly distributed throughout the first year.
