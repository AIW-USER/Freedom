## Question

What is the indication of upper limb lengthening?

## Options

1. Bilateral affection
2. Unilateral affection (Limb length discrepancy)
3. Forearm length affection
4. None

## Correct option

**1. Bilateral affection**

## Answer explanation

Bilateral affection such as in Achondroplasia with significant shortening of the humerus which limits a person from personal perineal hygiene is one of the limited indications of upper extremity lengthening.

LLD in the upper is not a usual concern as it is not a weight bearing limb and is mainly a cosmetic issue. Limb lengthening in upper limb is associated with high rate of complications (25-43%) such as Fractures, Early consolidation, Non-union, Stiffness.

Answer: 1
