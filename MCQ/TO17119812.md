## Question

Cause of pain in 50 years person after 6 weeks of immobilization with shoulder immobilizer applied after an episode of anterior shoulder dislocation (FNB 2022)

## Options

1. Rotator cuff tear
2. Entrapment of long head of biceps tendon
3. Loose body

## Correct option

**1. Rotator cuff tear**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

Anterior traumatic shoulder dislocations carry an inherent risk for rotator cuff tears especially in patients over the age of 40 years as there is already some pre-existing age-related tendon degeneration. Mostly these tears are supraspinatus tears and are often missed initially. Prompt identification can allow greater functional return and prevent a further instability from a deficient cuff. Hence, any patient who finds pain in lifting the shoulder in the scapular plane (a movement called Scaption) must be subjected to Ultrasound/ MRI evaluation for cuff.
