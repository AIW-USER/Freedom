## Question

Earliest sign of Potts’s spine is (musculoskeletal infections- tuberculosis of spine)

## Options

1. Stiffness of back
2. Back pain
3. Paraspinal muscle spasm
4. Swelling due to cold abscess

## Correct option

**3. Paraspinal muscle spasm**

## Answer explanation

Earliest symptom is back pain and the earliest sign is paraspinal muscle spasm.

Anwer: C
