## Question

which of the following is not a prerequisite of Gait?

## Options

1. Stability in stance
2. Adequate step
3. Foot clearance in stance
4. Energy conservation

## Correct option

**3. Foot clearance in stance**

## Answer explanation

Foot Clearance is needed in swing phase and not the stance phase of the gait cycle.

Answer: 3
