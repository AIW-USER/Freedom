## Question

A patient with shoulder pain has a positive dynamic labral shear test. His MRI shows a labrum tear from 11 to 1 o'clock position with intact biceps. Probable diagnosis is? (arthroscopy- shoulder arthroscopy- SLAP )

## Options

1. SLAP tear type 2
2. SLAP tear type 3
3. Bankarts lesion
4. Bankart's lesion with biceps tendinopathy

## Correct option

**2. SLAP tear type 3**
