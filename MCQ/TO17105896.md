Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Ligament Balancing in TKR

## Question

Which statement is true for soft tissue balancing in TKR

## Options

1. For tight extension space posterior capsule and semimembranosus should be released in sequence
2. For tight extension space anterior half of the superficial medial collateral and the pes anserinus insertion should be released in sequence
3. For tight flexion space posterior capsule and semimembranosus should be released in sequence
4. For tight flexion space posterior capsule and superficial MCL should be released in sequence

## Correct option

**1. For tight extension space posterior capsule and semimembranosus should be released in sequence**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 1

In general, release of the posterior structures from the posterior oblique ligament to the posterior capsule and semimembranosus insertion on the tibia affects the extension space more than the flexion space, and release of the anterior half of the superficial medial collateral and the pes anserinus insertion affects the flexion space more.
