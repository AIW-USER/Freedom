## Question

The MRI scan of a patient with symptomatic shoulder pain reveals subacromial bursitis. What markers have been shown to be significant contributors to this pain?

## Options

1. Metalloproteases
2. Alpha fetoprotein
3. Prostate-specific antigen (PSA)
4. Carcinoembryonic antigen (CEA)
5. CA-125

## Correct option

**1. Metalloproteases**

## Answer explanation

Several inflammatory markers have been shown to be elevated in subacromial bursitis. These include metalloproteases, tumor necrosis factors, and cyclooxygenase 1 and 2. The other answers provided are all tumor markers and not typically present in routine subacromial bursitis ("impingement syndrome").
