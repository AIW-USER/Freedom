Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Slipped Capital Femoral Epiphysis (SCFE)

## Question

Which is not a predictive factor for contralateral slip in SCFE

## Options

1. Young age
2. Posterior sloping” angle of more than 12 degrees
3. Obesity with endocrinal abnormalities
4. Continued slipping after fixation

## Correct option

**4. Continued slipping after fixation**

## Answer explanation

Bilateral slips are present in 20% to 30% of patients at initial presentation. Predictive factors for contralateral slip are young age, a “posterior sloping” angle of more than 12 degrees and obesity with endocrinal abnormalities in young children.

Answer: 4
