## Question

Unhappy triad of O’Donoghue is(Arthroscopy - knee Arthroscopy- meniscus injuries)

## Options

1. Rupture of the medial collateral ligament, damage to the medial meniscus, and rupture of the anterior cruciate ligament
2. Rupture of the Lateral collateral ligament, damage to the medial meniscus, and rupture of the anterior cruciate ligament
3. Rupture of the lateral collateral ligament, damage to the lateral meniscus, and rupture of the anterior cruciate ligament
4. Rupture of the medial collateral ligament, damage to the medial meniscus, and rupture of the posterior cruciate ligament

## Correct option

**1. Rupture of the medial collateral ligament, damage to the medial meniscus, and rupture of the anterior cruciate ligament**

## Answer explanation

The unhappy triad was described by American orthopedic surgeon D H O'Donoghue, in 1950. He described simultaneous occurrence of tear of ACL, MCL and medial meniscus tear when lateral force applied to knee while the foot is fixed to the ground. However later based on the arthroscopic studies this association was questioned as lateral meniscus tear is more commonly associated with acute ACL tears

Answer: 1
