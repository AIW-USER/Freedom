Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Anatomy ( Knee )

## Question

True regarding lateral structures of the knee

## Options

1. Iliotibial band is lax in flexion and tense in extension
2. Iliotibial band moves forward in extension and backward in flexion
3. During flexion Iliotibial band and LCL remain parallel to each other
4. Iliotibial band and biceps tendon cross each other during flexion.

## Correct option

**2. Iliotibial band moves forward in extension and backward in flexion**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: The iliotibial band moves forward in extension and backward in flexion but is tense in both positions. During flexion, the iliotibial band, the popliteal tendon, and the lateral collateral ligament cross each other, whereas the iliotibial band and biceps tendon remain parallel to each other as in extension, all serving to enhance lateral stability.

ANSWER-B
