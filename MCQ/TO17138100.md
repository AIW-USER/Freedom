Chapter: Nerve Injury MCQs — Carpal Tunnel Syndrome

## Question

The most specific method to diagnose Carpal tunnel syndrome from among the following would be

## Options

1. Durkan’s test
2. Hand diagram
3. Phalen’s test
4. Monofilament test

## Correct option

**2. Hand diagram**

## Answer explanation

The tests that have high sensitivity in diagnosis of CTS: Durkan’s direct nerve compression test, Hand diagram, History of night pains, Semmes Weinstein monofilament test The tests that have high specificity: Hand diagram, Tinel sign

Electro-diagnostic study findings in CTS: > 4.5 ms motor latency, > 3.5 ms sensory latency, Asymmetry of conduction velocity > 1 m/s.

Answer: 2
