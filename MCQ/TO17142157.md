Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Hallux Valgus Bunion

## Question

The most common complication following correction of juvenile hallux valgus is which of the following?

## Options

1. Hallux varus
2. Recurrent hallux valgus
3. First metatarsophalangeal (MTP) degenerative disease
4. Avascular necrosis
5. Physeal arrest

## Correct option

**2. Recurrent hallux valgus**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 2

Recurrence of the disease is the most common complication post-surgery mainly due to inadequate release of lateral contracture, insufficient medial plication, failure to identify metatarsus adducts and treat, failure to recognize joint congruency. TMT hypermobility may also be a cause.
