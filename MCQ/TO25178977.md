Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Hallux Valgus Bunion

## Question

Which of the following describes the most common rotational deformity found in the first metatarsal in severe hallux valgus cases?

## Options

1. Supination (external rotation) of the first metatarsal
2. Inversion of the first ray
3. Pure sagittal plane dorsiflexion
4. Pronation (internal rotation) of the first metatarsal

## Correct option

**4. Pronation (internal rotation) of the first metatarsal**

## Answer explanation

High-grade hallux valgus often involves a 3D deformity where the first metatarsal rotates into pronation, leading to sesamoid displacement.

Answer: 4
