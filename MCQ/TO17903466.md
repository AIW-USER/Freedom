## Question

Most common tendon rupture in RA hand

## Options

1. EPB
2. EPL
3. EDM
4. EDC

## Correct option

**3. EDM**

## Answer explanation

Answer-c

In RA distal ulna dorsal subluxation contributes to extensor tendon rupture because the extensor tendons usually glide between the arthritic distal ulnar head and the tight, intact dorsal carpal ligament. The small finger usually is involved first and subsequently the ring (Vaughn-Jackson syndrome) and then sequentially more radial digital extensors. The long extensor tendon of the thumb, because of its tortuous course, frequently ruptures at Lister’s tubercle
The flexor pollicis longus is the most common flexor tendon to be affected by RA. Erosion of the volar capsule and ligaments over radial osteophytes contribute to flexor pollicis longus rupture in the carpal tunnel (Mannerfelt lesion).
So most common extensor tendon involved by RA is extensor digiti minimi and most common flexor tendon involved is flexor pollicis longus.
