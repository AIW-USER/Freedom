## Question

What is the primary function of an extracellular bone protein with an Arg-Gly-Asp sequence?

## Options

1. Allows reversible osteoclast binding to bone
2. Allows irreversible osteoblast binding to bone
3. Binds RANKL on osteoblast preventing RANK activation
4. Forms osteoblasts under low strain and high oxygen tension
5. Forms cartilage under intermediate strain and low oxygen tension

## Correct option

**1. Allows reversible osteoclast binding to bone**

## Answer explanation

Osteoclasts bind reversibly to bone via integrins located on the osteoclast's surface. An Arg-Gly-Asp (RGD) sequence of extracellular bone proteins binds directly to the integrins.

Osteoclast-bone attachment occurs when the osteoclast attaches to bone matrix at the sealing zone. The osteoclast reversibly attaches to the bone surface via integrins on the osteoclast surface. Specifically, integrin avß3 binds to RGD-containing proteins, located on the bone surface. This binding signals the insertion into the plasma membrane of lysosomal vesicles containing cathepsin K, which is an acid protease.

*Ono et al. reviewed the repeating cycle of osteoclastic bone resorption and osteoblastic bone formation.* **They reported** that the osteoclast is differentiated from monocyte/macrophage lineage cells by macrophage colony-stimulating factor (M-CSF) and receptor activator of nuclear factor-κB ligand (RANKL). **They concluded** that RANKL/RANK signaling allows for the regulation of osteoclastogenesis, which may lead to bone diseases such as osteoporosis and osteopetrosis.

*Drissi et al. reviewed the function and importance of the osteoclast.* *They reported on the concept of clastokines and osteoclast precursor-mediated bone formation.* **They concluded** that advances in osteoclast biology have provided insight to new roles of osteoclasts.

*Boyce et al. reviewed osteoclasts.* **They reported** that all aspects of osteoclast formation and functions are regulated by macrophage-colony-stimulating factor (M-CSF) and receptor activator of NF-κB ligand (RANKL). **They concluded** that more recent studies indicate that osteoclasts and their precursors regulate immune responses and osteoblast formation and function by means of direct cell-cell contact through ligands and receptors, and through expression of clastokines.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: Integrins allow for REVERSIBLE binding of osteoCLASTS to bone
Answer 3: Osteoprotegerin is involved in osteoclastogenesis by binding to RANKL and acting as an antagonist to bony turnover
Answers 4&5: Osteocytes become osteoblasts under low strain and high oxygen tension, cartilage under intermediate strain and low oxygen tension, and fibrous tissue under high strain
