## Question

Most commonly injured ligament is ( arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- ACL)

## Options

1. ACL
2. MCL
3. LCL
4. ALL

## Correct option

**2. MCL**

## Answer explanation

Most commonly injured knee ligament is the MCL followed by the ACL. ACL is the most commonly operated knee ligament as most of the MCL injuries are managed conservatively.

Answer: 2
