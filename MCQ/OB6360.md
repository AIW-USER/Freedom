## Question

Mutations of what gene are associated with the subsequent development of osteosarcoma?

## Options

1. RB
2. EWS-FLI1
3. EGF-R
4. IGF-R
5. VEGF

## Correct option

**1. RB**

## Answer explanation

Patients with a mutation of the retinoblastoma gene (RB) have an increased likelihood for the development of osteosarcoma. The EWS-FLI1 gene is the fusion product of a chromosomal translocation of 11:22. EGF-R and IGF-R are growth factor receptors, but mutations in these genes have not been correlated with osteosarcoma. VEGF is a proangiogenic molecule that is involved in tumor formation in multiple sites but not the development of osteosarcoma.
