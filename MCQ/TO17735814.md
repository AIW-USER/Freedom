## Question

A young patient met an accident and sustained severe crush injury to his leg with a 6 cm long and 4 cm wide dirty and contaminated wound over posterior aspect of distal tibia. Fractured bone is exposed through a wound with periosteal stripping. What is his Gustilo-Anderson grading? (trauma- management of open fractures)

## Options

1. IIIA
2. IIB
3. IIIB
4. None of above

## Correct option

**3. IIIB**

## Answer explanation

The Gustilo and Anderson system is based upon size of the traumatic wound—

Type II
greater than 1 cm but less than 10 cm
moderate contamination
moderate soft tissue injury, no periosteal stripping

Type III
greater than 10 cm
high contamination
wounds with extensive periosteal stripping/ vascular injury

IIIA—a
 adequate soft-tissue coverage of a fractured bone despite extensive soft-tissue laceration or flaps, or high-energy trauma irrespective of the size of the wound;
high contamination
extensive soft tissue damage with periosteal stripping but local soft tissue coverage possible

IIIB
extensive soft-tissue injury loss with periosteal stripping and bone exposure, which is usually associated with massive contamination
high contamination
extensive soft tissue damage with periosteal stripping but soft tissue coverage not possible

(rotational or free flap required to cover the wound)

 type IIIC
open fracture associated with arterial injury requiring repair
extensive soft tissue damage with periosteal stripping. The wound requires flap coverage. arterial injury that requires repair.

Special categories in Type III are gunshot injuries, and any open fracture caused by a farm injury.

Disadvantages of Gustilo and Anderson's Classification of Open Fractures

- Definition has undergone many modifications and does not have uniformity in application
- Includes wide spectrum of injuries in type IIIB injuries
- Mainly depends on size of the skin wound Does not evaluate the severity of injury to skin, bone, and musculotendinous units separately
- Does not address the question of salvage
- Poor interobserver reliability

Answer: 3
