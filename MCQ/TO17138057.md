## Question

Factors that predispose to the subclavian steal syndrome include

## Options

1. Occlusion of the left subclavian artery proximal to the origin of left vertebral artery
2. Occlusion of the left subclavian artery distal to the origin of the left vertebral artery
3. Occlusion of the left vertebral artery
4. All of the above

## Correct option

**1. Occlusion of the left subclavian artery proximal to the origin of left vertebral artery**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

The subclavian steal syndrome is associated with the symptoms of vertebrobasilar insufficiency.

It occurs when increased activity of the left arm results in the stunting of blood into the left subclavian, which is occluded proximal to the origin of left vertebral artery.

The occlusion of left vertebral artery will cause symptoms of vertebrobasilar insufficiency, but it has not got any relation with the subclavian artery, hence not subclavian steal phenomenon.
