## Question

Miller’s asthma is seen in (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Marathon runners
2. Football players
3. Infants
4. Elderly athletes

## Correct option

**3. Infants**

## Answer explanation

Millar’s asthma (aka Spasmodic croup) is also called Asthma of Children. It occurs as a result of spasm of glottis (closure of glottis) producing a crowning noise during inspiration. It is common in children under the age of 2 years and symptoms predominate at night.

Also know:

Miller’s asthma: Millers' asthma or Mill-workers' asthma or Millers' cough or Grain worker's asthma: it is allergic responses to the grain weevil (Sitophilus granarius).

Answer: 3
