## Question

In the radiograph shown in Figure 42, the fracture pattern around this well-fixed stem is classified as Vancouver type

![[q6032-a-42.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. A.
2. B1.
3. B2.
4. B3.
5. C.

## Correct option

**2. B1.**

## Answer explanation

The Vancouver classifications describes periprosthetic hip fractures in the following way. Type A fractures are in the trochanteric region. Type B1 fractures occur around the stem or at the tip in the face of a well-fixed stem. These are usually treated with open reduction and internal fixation, usually including struts, cable, and/or cable plates. Type B2 fractures occur in the same region with a loose stem. Type B3 fractures occur with a loose stem where the proximal bone is of poor quality and/or severely comminuted. Type C fractures occur well below the stem.
