## Question

What is andrew’s lesion ?

## Options

1. Anterior and superior labral tear
2. Posterior and superior labral tear
3. Anterior and posterior labral tear
4. IGHL avulsion from posterior glenoid

## Correct option

**1. Anterior and superior labral tear**

## Answer explanation

Andrew’s lesion- detachment of the anterior and superior labrum without extension posterior to the biceps brachii insertion

Andrew's lesion is mainly found in throwers. It consists of pure superior labrum detachment without extension posterior to the biceps footplate (thus not to be confused with SLAP type-II).

Answer : a
