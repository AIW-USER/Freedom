Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Chondromalcia Patella  Patellofemoral Pain Syndrome

## Question

When designing a rehabilitation program for a patient recovering from a complex knee reconstruction who concurrently exhibits symptomatic patellofemoral chondrosis, the clinician must carefully manage patellofemoral joint (PFJ) reaction forces. Which of the following exercise prescriptions best minimizes PFJ stress while still allowing for necessary quadriceps strengthening?

## Options

1. Exclusively utilizing open kinetic chain exercises through a full 90° to 0° range of motion to completely eliminate weight-bearing compressive forces.
2. Closed kinetic chain exercises restricted to the 0° to 45° flexion arc and open kinetic chain exercises restricted to the 90° to 45° flexion arc.
3. Closed kinetic chain exercises in the 60° to 90° flexion arc and open kinetic chain exercises in the 30° to 0° extension arc.
4. Exclusively utilizing closed kinetic chain deep squats (0° to 90°), relying on the increased patellar contact area at high flexion angles to reduce overall joint stress.

## Correct option

**2. Closed kinetic chain exercises restricted to the 0° to 45° flexion arc and open kinetic chain exercises restricted to the 90° to 45° flexion arc.**

## Answer explanation

Limiting closed-chain movements to shallow flexion and open-chain movements to deeper flexion arcs successfully avoids the specific ranges where patellofemoral joint reaction forces are mathematically highest.

Answer: 2
