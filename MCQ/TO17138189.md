## Question

A 3 years old child with moderate metatarsus adductus is brought to your clinic by his parents. His parents find difficulty in putting shoes on his feet. They give a history of serial stretching and casting when the child was 6 months old. What is the appropriate treatment at this age?

## Options

1. Observation
2. Stretching and serial casting
3. Metatarsal osteotomies and then serial casting
4. Tarsometatarsal capsulotomies

## Correct option

**4. Tarsometatarsal capsulotomies**

## Answer explanation

treatment of metatarsus adductus:

In a young child, surgery is not indicated until conservative treatment has failed. When a child passes the appropriate age for serial stretching and casting, surgery becomes a reasonable option. Indications for surgery include pain, objectionable appearance, and difficulty in fitting shoes because of residual forefoot adduction
