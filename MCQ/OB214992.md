## Question

A 24-year-old male presents to your office for evaluation of a recurrent proximal tibiofibular ganglion cyst. He had the previous cyst excised approximately one year ago. Which of the following is true regarding the treatment of recurrent proximal tibiofibular ganglion cysts?

## Options

1. Interpositional arthroplasty results in the highest patient reported outcomes
2. Proximal tibiofibular joint arthrodesis has the lowest recurrence rate
3. Phenol should be used as an adjuvant following repeat excision
4. Liquid nitrogen should be used as an adjuvant following repeat excision
5. Aspiration should be performed prior to excision of recurrent lesions

## Correct option

**2. Proximal tibiofibular joint arthrodesis has the lowest recurrence rate**

## Answer explanation

Proximal tibiofibular fusion has the lowest reported recurrence rate and is the most reliable options for the treatment of this rare condition.

In general, 15-20% off all ganglion cysts occur in the lower extremity, with one third of those occurring around the knee. While simple excision is the most common initial treatment for proximal tibiofibular ganglion cysts, this has proven to be ineffective for recurrent lesions. While numerous treatments have been explored for recurrent cysts, proximal tibiofibular fusion results in the lowest recurrence rate and the most predictable results.

*Miskovsky et al review the treatment and outcomes of proximal tibiofibular joint ganglion cysts.* **They report** a 13% recurrence rate following primary excision with a 100% recurrence rate following secondary resection. No recurrence was noted in any patient undergoing primary or secondary arthrodesis for the treatment of these cysts. **They conclude** that proximal tibiofibular arthrodesis serves a valuable role in the treatment of proximal tibiofibular ganglion cysts.

*Vatansever et al review the presentation of proximal tibiofibular joint ganglion cysts.* **They report** a variable presentation with the most common symptoms being pain, the presence of a palpable mass that varies in size, and dysesthesias of the common peroneal nerve. **They conclude** that MRI should be used to aid in the diagnosis of these rare lesions.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Interposition arthroplasty has proven to be ineffective in the treatment of recurrent cysts.
Answer 3-4: Phenol and liquid nitrogen may be used as an adjuvant for the treatment of aneurysmal bone cysts (ABC), but have no role in the treatment of recurrent ganglion cysts.
Answer 5: Aspiration is not part of the treatment algorithm for proximal tibiofibular ganglion cysts.
