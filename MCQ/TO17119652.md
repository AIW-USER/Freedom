## Question

Sourcil sign on X ray is suggestive of (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Rotator cuff arthropathy
2. Calcific tendinopathy of supraspinatus
3. SLAP tear
4. Shoulder instability

## Correct option

**1. Rotator cuff arthropathy**

## Answer explanation

Chronic cuff disease may be accompanied by sclerosis of the undersurface of the acromion (the "sourcil" or eyebrow sign). However, its Sensitivity, specificity, positive predictive value and negative predictive value all are poor for the radiological signs as predictors of rotator cuff pathology, regardless of age.

Reference: The Sourcil Sign: A Useful Finding on Plain X-ray? Chris Smith et al. Shoulder and elbow January 1, 2010 https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1758-5740.2009.00034.x

Answer: 1
