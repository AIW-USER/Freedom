Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Proximal Humerus Fractures

## Question

Which is the best predictor of humeral head ischemia after proximal humeral fracture?

## Options

1. 4-part fracture
2. Fracture line involving anatomical neck
3. Quality of reduction
4. Calcar length along with fracture fragment

## Correct option

**4. Calcar length along with fracture fragment**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: The most relevant predictors of ischemia of humeral head after proximal humeral fracture are the length of the dorsomedial metaphyseal extension (8< mm calcar length attached to articular fragment) and the integrity of the medial hinge.

Indicators of AVN humeral head after proximal humeral fracture:

- calcar length (<8 mm calcar length attached to articular fragment),
- medial hinge integrity
- anatomic neck fracture
- increasing fracture complexity
- displacement >10mm
- angulation >45 degree

Answer: 4
