Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Clinical Examination (Shoulder)

## Question

Lift off test is specific for (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Internal rotation deficit
2. Abduction strength
3. External rotation deficit
4. Anterior shoulder instability

## Correct option

**1. Internal rotation deficit**

## Answer explanation

Tests to assess subscapularis tear/strength or internal rotation deficit are

 Lift-off test, Belly press test, Napoleon test and Bear Hug test.

Answer: 1
