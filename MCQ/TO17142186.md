Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Posterior Tibial Tendon Insufficiency

## Question

Johnson and strom classification is used for which tendon involvement?

## Options

1. Tibialis anterior
2. Tibialis posterior
3. Peroneus longus
4. FHL

## Correct option

**2. Tibialis posterior**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Jhonson staging is used for adult acquired flatfoot secondary to tibialis posterior tendon dysfunction.

Stage 1: Tenosynovitis – no deformity

Pain in tendon- normal function- able to repeat tiptoes

Stage 2: Flexible deformity present Tendon rupture fully o/e reduced strength of tendon and unable to repeat tip toes

Stage 3 : Rigid flat foot Valgus heel

Loss of subtalar motion Forefoot Varus

Absent heel raise test Antero-lateral impingement Secondary OA

Answer: 2
