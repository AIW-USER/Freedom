## Question

7 yrs. old girl with juvenile idiopathic thoracic scoliosis of magnitude 45 degrees. All the following are treatment options except

## Options

1. Growth rods
2. Stapling technique
3. Scoliosis cast application
4. Posterior instrumented correction and fusion

## Correct option

**4. Posterior instrumented correction and fusion**

## Answer explanation

- Early onset scoliosis- scoliosis less than 10 yrs.
- Curve magnitude - 25deg – 40 deg- cast application.
- More than 40deg – surgical – fusion less surgeries.
- Posterior instrumented correction and fusion in this early onset scoliosis would lead to decreased thoracic cavity growth and crankshaft phenomenon due to continued anterior column growth.

answer: d
