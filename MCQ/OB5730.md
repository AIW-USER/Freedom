## Question

The cadaveric dissection in Figure A identifies anatomic structures that are at risk with both an anterior lumbar interbody fusion (ALIF) and trans-psoas lateral approaches. Which of the following clinical findings would be expected if the anatomic structure labelled Structure A in Figure A is injured?

![[q5730-a-chain-2.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Urinary incontinence
2. Ipsilateral lower extremity anhidrosis
3. Decreased patellar reflex
4. Decreased sensation to mons pubis or scrotum
5. Isolated loss of proprioception

## Correct option

**2. Ipsilateral lower extremity anhidrosis**

## Answer explanation

Structure A is the sympathetic chain. Injury to this structure would lead to ipsilateral lower extremity anhidrosis.

Anterior lumbar interbody fusion (ALIF) and trans-psoas approach are commonly used procedures to obtain interbody fusion in the lumbar spine. The sympathetic chain, which runs on the anterior lateral aspect of the spine, can be injured with either of these approaches. When injured, post-sympathectomy (post-SE) dysfunction syndrome, a debilitating condition, can occur. Post-SE dysfunction syndrome is characterized by increased temperature, reduced perspiration, and reduced sympathetic skin responses in the ipsilateral lower extremity.

*Kasliwal et al. discussed a case where a patient experienced anhidrosis after an L4-L5 artificial disc replacement using the anterior retroperitoneal approach.* **They discussed** that anhidrosis can be an indication of sympathetic injury, though retrograde ejaculation is a much more common and better known complication from this approach with injury to the superior hypogastric plexus. **The authors also concluded** that despite injury being described more often at the level of L5-S1, it is important to recognize that damage to the sympathetic chain can happen at other levels.

*Schulte et al. investigated sympathetic function after anterior scoliosis instrumentation in 24 patients.* **They found** that the patients with instrumentation down to L3 had more sympathetic dysfunction than patients who had instrumentation down to T12, supporting the idea that the the L2 ganglion plays a significant role of sympathetic outflow from the spinal cord. **They also found** that lesions of the sympathetic chain lead to increases in skin temperature due to unbalanced vasodilatation, reduction of perspiration, and reduced sympathetic skin responses.

Figure A shows a cadaveric dissection of the lumbar spine from an anterior view. Illustration A is a drawing of the hypogastric plexus in isolation. Illustrations A and B are drawings showing the neurovascular anatomy of the retroperitoneal space with the lumbar sympathetic trunk and the hypogastric plexus, respectively, highlighted.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Urinary incontinence would be seen with injury to S3-S4
Answer 3: Decreased patellar reflex would be caused by injury to the L4 nerve root.
Answer 4: Sensation to the mons pubis or scrotum would be altered by damage to the genitofemoral nerve.
Answer 5: Isolated loss of proprioception occurs with posterior cord syndrome, which is a condition caused by injury to the spinal cord.
