## Question

Which of the following factors is least predictive of a poor outcome after whiplash injury?

## Options

1. Headache
2. Immediate pain
3. Pain isolated to neck
4. Severe pain in first week

## Correct option

**3. Pain isolated to neck**

## Answer explanation

The term ‘whiplash’ is used to denote the mechanism of an indirect flexion-extension soft-tissue injury to the neck resulting from a rear-end motor-vehicle collision. Symptoms associated with a worse outcome are rapid onset of pain, severity of neck pain, acute hospital admission, radiation of pain to the upper limb and headache

Answer: 4
