## Options

1. Sulfate transport protein gene mutation affecting cartilage matrix formation
2. Mutation in the gene CBFA-1 affecting osteocalcin formation
3. Defect in lysosomal-storage
4. Defect in lipid-storage
5. Mutation in gene FGFR-3 resulting in growth plate growth abnormality

## Correct option

**1. Sulfate transport protein gene mutation affecting cartilage matrix formation**

## Answer explanation

Diastrophic dysplasia is a condition caused by a defect in the SLC26A2 gene, which encodes for a sulfate transporter. The impaired function leads to under-sulfation of proteoglycans in cartilage matrix and abnormalities in the hydraulic properties of cartilage. Multiple cartilaginous structures are affected including the trachea, ear, and ligaments. The pinnae of the ears have a characteristic swelling referred to as cauliflower ear. Neonates can develop tracheomalacia which can be fatal. An abducted hitchhiker thumb and symphalangism (generalized finger stiffness) are both common hand manifestations of this disease. Kyphosis in the c-spine occurs early in life but frequently spontaneously resolves.

*Horton et al describe the phenotypic variability of diastrophic dysplasia in a retrospective study from their institution.* **They identified** 4 patients with "diastrophic variant" out of their cohort of over 60 patients with diastrophic dysplasia. Diastrophic variant is a milder form of diastrophic dysplasia with less physical malformations and a more normal lifespan. Such variants allow for examination of the variable phenotype associated with the mutations known to cause diastrophic dysplasia.

*Remes et al studied cervical kyphosis in 120 patients with diastrophic dysplasia.* **They found** that much like the other phenotypic manifestations of diastrophic dysplasia, the presence and severity of cervical kyphosis in diastrophic dysplasia was variable. In most cases however, cervical kyphosis in diastrophic dysplasia resolves spontaneously and doesn't need treatment.

2) CBFA-1 mutation is associated with cleidocranial dysplasia
3) Hunter and Hurler mucopolysaccharidoses are examples of a lysosomal-storage diseases
4) Gauchers is an example of a lipid-storage disease
5) FGFR-3 mutation is the genetic defect associated with achondroplasia
