## Question

Figures 34a and 34b show the axial and sagittal MRI scans of a 36-year-old man who reports the insidious onset of pain in the right shoulder. What is the most appropriate description of the acromial morphology?

![[q6629-a-34a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q6629-b-34b.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Type I acromion
2. Type III acromion
3. Meso os acromiale
4. Meta os acromiale
5. Pre os acromiale

## Correct option

**3. Meso os acromiale**

## Answer explanation

The MRI scans reveal a meso os acromiale with edema at the site in a skeletally mature patient.
