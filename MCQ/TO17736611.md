## Question

Vacuum phenomenon in intervertebral disc is seen in all except ( General orthopedics- imaging and investigations)

## Options

1. Vertebral osteomyelitis
2. Osteoporotic collapse of vertebra
3. Avulsion of endplates
4. Degenerative disc disease

## Correct option

**3. Avulsion of endplates**

## Answer explanation

Vacuum phenomena: It is accumulation of gas (nitrogen) within the crevices of the intervertebral discs or adjacent vertebrae. It is commonly observed in intervertebral degenerative disc disease (on X ray/ CT and MRI). Other common causes include osteoporotic vertebral fracture, multiple myeloma, schmorl node formation, osteonecrosis of vertebral body or vertebral body collapse and rarely in spinal infection.

Answer: 3
