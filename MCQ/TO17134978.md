## Question

Filum extends till? (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. L2
2. S1
3. First coccygeal segment
4. Lower border of L1

## Correct option

**3. First coccygeal segment**

## Answer explanation

Spinal cord is 45 cm long in adults. Neural structure (cord) extends from the foramen magnum to the lower border of L1 as conus medullaris. The fetus cord is as long as the vertebral column. Cauda equina refers to hanging nerve roots in the canal that are arising from respective spinal cord segments. Filum terminal is the fibrous septum that extends from the distal end of the conus and then adhering to dura mater, it inserts into the first coccygeal segment.

Answer: 3
