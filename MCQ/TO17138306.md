Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Arthrogryposis Multiplex Congenital

## Question

Classical Arthrogryposis commonly involves

## Options

1. Abduction of thumb
2. Flexed elbows
3. Wrist flexed with ulnar deviation
4. Finger extension

## Correct option

**3. Wrist flexed with ulnar deviation**

## Answer explanation

Abduction of thumb – not classically seen in Arthrogryposis, Adducted thumb, thumb in palm deformity is seen

Flexed elbows not commonly seen in Arthrogryposis, extension contractures of elbow are commonly seen

Wrist flexed with ulnar deviation is commonly seen in Arthrogryposis

Finger extension is not commonly seen in AMC, IP joint flexion contracture and MCP joint relative extension contractures are commonly seen in AMC

Answer: 3
