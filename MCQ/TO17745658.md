## Question

What is the internervous plane in the direct anterior approach to hip (regional orthopedics- hip and pelvis - surgical approaches)

## Options

1. Femoral and obturator
2. Femoral and superior gluteal nerve
3. Sciatic and superior gluteal nerve
4. Obturator and superior gluteal nerve

## Correct option

**2. Femoral and superior gluteal nerve**

## Answer explanation

The direct anterior approach goes through an internervous plane in its superficial and deep interval. The superficial interval is between the tensor fasciae latae (TFL) and the sartorius. The deep interval resides between the gluteus medius and the rectus femoris.

The TFL is innervated by the superior gluteal nerve and Sartorius by the femoral nerve. Gluteus medius is innervated by superior gluteal nerve and rectus femoris by femoral nerve. So the internervous plane lies between the superior gluteal nerve and femoral nerve.

Answer : b
