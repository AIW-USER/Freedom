## Question

Figures 8a and 8b are the preoperative radiographs of a 47-year-old woman who is being treated for a supple pes plano abductovalgus deformity. She is unable to perform an ipsilateral single leg heel raise. Which of the following is the most likely soft-tissue procedure performed in combination with the bony surgery?

![[q8030-a-8a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q8030-b-8b.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Flexor digitorum longus tendon transfer
2. Extensor hallucis longus tendon transfer
3. Spring ligament release
4. Peroneus longus tendon repair
5. Lateral collateral ligament reconstruction

## Correct option

**1. Flexor digitorum longus tendon transfer**

## Answer explanation

Flexor digitorum longus tendon transfer and augmentation has similar dynamic function to the posterior tibial tendon. Other reported tendon transfers for this procedure include flexor hallucis longus and peroneus brevis, but not extensor hallucis longus nor peroneus longus. Spring ligament release accentuates the flatfoot deformity whereas conversely, spring ligament repair/reconstruction is another recognized soft-tissue procedure that may be combined with bony surgery for treatment of flexible acquired flatfoot deformity. Lateral collateral ligament reconstruction addresses lateral ankle instability which the patient does not have.
