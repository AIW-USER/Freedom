## Question

You are attending a grand rounds lecture on fracture healing after orthopedic trauma. The lecturer begins to discuss the importance of bone growth factors. Which of the following growth factors is most appropriately characterized by promoting the differentiation of mesenchymal cells into chondrocytes and osteoblasts?

## Options

1. Transforming Growth Factor Beta (TGF-B)
2. Bone morphogenetic proteins (BMP)
3. Fibroblast Growth Factor (FGF)
4. Insulin-like Growth Factor (IGF)
5. Platelet-derived Growth Factor (PDGF)

## Correct option

**2. Bone morphogenetic proteins (BMP)**

## Answer explanation

Bone morphogenic protein (BMP) helps promote the differentiation of mesenchymal cells into chondrocytes and osteoblasts.

Fracture healing begins with hematoma formation. During this process, numerous cell lines are present and contribute to healing. Mesenchymal stem cells (MSC) are pluripotent cells that respond to a variety of growth factors and cytokines that dictate the future of that cell. Growth factors TGF-ß, BMP, FGF, IGF, and PDGF are important to drive these cells to perform many healing functions. BMPs, specifically, promote MSC differentiation to chondrocytes and osteoblasts. BMPs are part of the TGF-B family and transduce their signals through serine-threonine kinase transmembrane receptors.

*Schlickewei et al. reviews bone regeneration.* *They reported on the clinical implications of impaired bone regeneration.* **They concluded** that recent advances in understanding fracture healing have resulted in the identification and development of novel therapeutic approaches for affected patients.

*Schlundt et al. reviewed the developmental progression of fracture healing at the tissue, cellular and molecular levels.* **They reported** that innate and adaptive immune processes are a component of the injury response, as are environmental factors, such as the extent of injury to the bone and surrounding tissue, fixation, and the contribution of vascular tissues. **They concluded** that the biophysical and biological basis of the molecular actions of various therapeutic approaches, including recombinant human bone morphogenetic proteins and parathyroid hormone therapy, may influence this.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: TGF-B is a pleiotropic growth factor stimulating undifferentiated mesenchymal cell proliferation.
Answer 3: FGF is mitogenic for mesenchymal cells, chondrocytes, and osteoblasts.
Answer 4: IGF promotes the proliferation and differentiation of osteoprogenitor cells.
Answer 5: PDGF is a mitogen for mesenchymal cells and osteoblasts; macrophage chemotaxis.
