## Question

Most common property for a bone graft is (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Osteogenesis
2. Osteoinduction
3. Osteoconduction
4. . Osseointegration

## Correct option

**3. Osteoconduction**

## Answer explanation

Osseointegration (not osteointegration), is not a property of bone graft. It’s rather a concept that originated in context of dental implants and was pioneered by Professor Per-Ingvar Brane mark (Institute of Biotech, Goteborg Univ, 1969). He defined it as a process where a direct connection develops between living bone and surface of a load carrying end osseous implant (visible under light microscopy). There is no interposition of any non-bone tissue between bone and implant.

Osteoconduction is the ability of the bone graft to provide a scaffold for bone formation and is the most important property. Osteoinduction refers to the ability of graft to induce the host's osteoblasts (via growth factors like BMPs) to form bone while Osteogenesis correlates with the capability of graft’s osteoblasts to contribute in bone formation in the host.
