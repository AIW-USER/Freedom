## Question

A 36-year-old female is in your office for a left-sided pelvic mass. An outside provider performed an excisional biopsy and a specimen from this is shown in Figure A. Her radiograph prior to this procedure is shown in Figure B. Which of the following risk factors is associated with a poorer prognosis for this type of tumor?

![[q3753-b-chondrosarc-xray.jpg]]

## Options

1. Female gender
2. Negative resection margins
3. Pelvis location
4. Younger patient age
5. Biopsy performed at a tumor center

## Correct option

**3. Pelvis location**

## Answer explanation

Out of the above options, pelvic tumor location is the only negative prognostic factor for chondrosarcomas.

Chondrosarcomas are a malignant primary bone tumor most commonly found in the axial skeleton. The majority of these lesions require large tissue en bloc resections given their resistance to radiation. Chondrosarcomas carry a poor survival rate that is inversely correlated with high-grade tumor histology and patient age. Additionally, pelvic tumors and those with inadequate surgical margins do poorly as well. De-differentiated chondrosarcomas have even worse survival outcomes; however, the adjuvant use of chemotherapy following resection has shown promise but has yet to show significant improvements in recurrence or survival.

*Lee et al. followed 227 patients with chondrosarcoma over 6 years,* **noting** an overall 87% survival rate. High-grade lesions and older patients had higher rates of local recurrence and metastasis. Additionally, tumor size over 100 cubic centimeters, positive surgical margins, and pelvic location were all also poor prognostics factors.

*Bergh et al. examined 69 patients with chondrosarcoma of the axial skeleton.* The local recurrence rate was 27%, and the 5- and 10-year survival rates were 72% and 67%, respectively. Factors associated with a worse prognosis were high histologic tumor grade, increasing patient age, primary surgery outside of a tumor center, incisional biopsy versus a noninvasive diagnostic procedure, and inadequate surgical margins.

Figure A shows the characteristic histology of chondrosarcoma, with enlarged chondrocytes surrounded by cartilage. Figure B shows a radiograph of the pelvis with a radiolucent mass involving the left anterior hemipelvis.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1- Gender does not have any effect on prognosis
Answers 2, 4, 5: All of these are positive prognostic factors for local recurrence and overall survival.
