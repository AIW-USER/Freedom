## Question

Most safe ankle position to avoid leg compartment syndrome after long leg casting for tibial fractures is (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. 30-degree dorsiflexion
2. Neutral
3. Neutral to 30-degree dorsiflexion
4. Neutral to 30-degree plantar flexion

## Correct option

**4. Neutral to 30-degree plantar flexion**

## Answer explanation

Most common compartment involved in leg compartment syndrome is anterior compartment. Resting pressure is minimum in the anterior compartment with the ankle in neutral position. Whereas resting pressure in the deep posterior compartment is minimum with the ankle in plantar flexion.

Answer: 4
