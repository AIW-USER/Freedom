## Question

In osteoarticular tuberculosis of foot, after biopsy ATT is started, first sign of healing on x ray is (musculoskeletal infections- Tuberculosis of peripheral joints)

## Options

1. Reappearance of joint space
2. Remineralization of bones
3. Removal of sequestrated bone
4. Bony ankylosis

## Correct option

**2. Remineralization of bones**

## Answer explanation

Signs of healing of bone TB after treatment are-

The remineralization of the bone (normal trabecular pattern, correction of osteopenia) is the first sign of healing.

Signs of healing in spine TB

Remineralization of the vertebra, sharpening of disc margins, reduction of pre-/paravertebral shadows, and osseous/fibro-osseous fusion are signs of healing on X-rays.

Answer: 2
