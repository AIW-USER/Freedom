## Question

Which of the following may be observed in the histological section of the chronic condition depicted in Figure A?

![[q2279-a-screen-shot-2020-03-19-at-1-12-48-pm.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Vascular invasion in the zone of proliferation
2. Increased thickness of the perichondral ring
3. Increase in undulations in the growth plate
4. Normal amount of proteoglycans
5. Granulation tissue between columns in the zone of hypertrophy

## Correct option

**5. Granulation tissue between columns in the zone of hypertrophy**

## Answer explanation

In chronic slipped capital femoral epiphysis (SCFE), histological sections may reveal granulation tissue between the columns in the hypertrophic zone, decreased thickness of the perichondral ring, decrease in physeal undulations, and abnormal accumulation of proteoglycans.

In SCFE, the hypertrophic zone of the physis is most commonly injured. It is theorized that SCFE occurs from microtrauma in this zone, which results in vascular invasion (histologically similar to granulation tissue when chronic) and an increase in the height of the hypertrophic zone. Endocrine abnormalities, obesity, as well as adolescent growth spurts also increase the height of the physeal hypertrophic zone, and are known risk factors for SCFE.

*Chung et al. investigated gross and microscopic morphologies of post-mortem pediatric patients with SCFE.* **They reported** that the shear strength of the epiphysis was significantly reliant on the surrounding perichondrial complex in infancy and early childhood. **They observed** that the forces needed to cause a slippage were within the physiological range generated in overweight children. **They concluded** that mechanical factors may play a significant role in the etiology of SCFE.

Figure A demonstrates a chronic SCFE.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: In chronic SCFE, vascular invasion in the hypertrophic zone (not proliferative zone) resembles granulation tissue.
Answer 2: In chronic SCFE, there is decreased thickness of the perichondral ring.
Answer 3: In chronic SCFE, there is a decrease in physeal undulations.
Answer 4: In chronic SCFE, there is an abnormal accumulation of proteoglycans.
