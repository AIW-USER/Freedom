## Question

Which is not a method of measuring anteversion of the acetabular component on AP plain radiographs?

## Options

1. The method of Liaw et al.
2. The method of Woo and Morrey
3. The method of Lewinnek et al.
4. The method of Hassan et al.

## Correct option

**2. The method of Woo and Morrey**
