## Question

Which is not attached to medial femoral condyle?( General orthopedics- anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. MPFL
2. PCL
3. Popliteus
4. Adductor magnus

## Correct option

**3. Popliteus**

## Answer explanation

Popliteus muscle originates from the lateral surface of the lateral condyle of femur (popliteal notch) by a rounded tendon, the fibers pass downward and medially and insert on the posterior surface of tibia above the soleal line.

Attachments on the medial femoral condyle: MCL, MPFL, Adductor magnus, PCL.

Answer: 3
