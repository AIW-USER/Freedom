## Question

What is the most common finding of the acetabulum in patients with bladder exstrophy?

## Options

1. Anteversion
2. Retroversion
3. Protrusio
4. Neutral version
5. Hypoplasia

## Correct option

**2. Retroversion**

## Answer explanation

Acetabular retroversion is the most common finding of the acetabulum in patients with bladder exstrophy.

Bladder exstrophy occurs due to the failure of the abdominal wall to close during fetal development and results in protrusion of the posterior bladder wall through the lower abdominal wall. The goals of the treatment for urogenital pathology are to provide urinary continence with preservation of normal renal function, to obtain a secure closure of bladder and abdominal wall, and to reconstruct functional external genitalia. The success of the reconstructive surgery of the lower urogenital tract and abdominal wall depends on the reconstruction of a stable anterior pelvic ring. Pelvic deformities include pubic diastasis, shortened pubic rami, and acetabular retroversion.

*Yazici et al. performed a study to investigate the natural outcome of the rotational profile of the lower extremities and to define the risk factors for early degenerative arthritis of the hip and knee in exstrophy patients with a widened anterior pelvis.* *They analyzed 14 patients over age 7 and found all patients had a pubic diastasis of >2cm.* All acetabuli were retroverted with anteversion of the femur. **They concluded** that over time, neither cosmetic deformity nor functional limitation last after late childhood and no hip dysplasia develops.

*Nordin et al. performed a study to determine the hip configuration and function in bladder exstrophy treated without a pelvic osteotomy.* *They analyzed 9 children clinically and radiologically at a mean age of 13.* **They report** that the acetabulum was retroverted by an average of 5°, but was balanced by increased femoral anteversion of 10-20° higher than normal. All femoral heads were spherical and none of the patients had dysplasia. **They concluded** that the balanced deformities resulted in a normal range of hip movements and a normal gait in later childhood.

*Nehme et al. performed a study to determine acetabular morphology in bladder exstrophy.* *They retrospectively reviewed 62 hips with bladder exstrophy, which had no prior surgery.* **They reported** an average lateral center-edge angle, acetabular index, and head extrusion index of 32°, 5.7°, and 0.26%, respectively. **They found** 58% of hips had a retroverted acetabulum and 42% were neutral or anteverted. **They conclude** that most patients will not have early arthritis and the majority of patients have acetabular retroversion.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1, 3, 4, & 5: Patients with bladder exstrophy most commonly exhibit acetabular retroversion.
