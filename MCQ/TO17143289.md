Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Fibrous Dysplasia

## Question

A genetic abnormality in the cyclic AMP signaling pathway is involved in which of the following conditions? [FNB-2014]

## Options

1. Achondroplasia
2. Multiple osteochondromatosis
3. Fibrous dysplasia
4. Enchondroma

## Correct option

**3. Fibrous dysplasia**

## Answer explanation

The genetic defect in fibrous dysplasia involves a G protein that affects the cAMP signaling pathway

Answer: 3
