## Question

Identify the pathology [Regional Orthopedics-Foot & Ankle-Tarsal Coalition]

![[TO17747146-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Talocalcaneal coalition
2. Calcaneonavicular coalition
3. Spring ligament avulsion
4. Calcaneocuboid dislocation

## Correct option

**2. Calcaneonavicular coalition**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: calcaneo-navicular coalition seen as anteaters sign and talar beaking

Answer: 2
