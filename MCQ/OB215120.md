## Question

Which of the following has been implicated in the process of cartilage degeneration?

## Options

1. Transformation growth factor beta (TGF-B)
2. Aggrecan
3. Osteoprotegerin
4. Stromelysin
5. Insulin-like growth factor-1 (IGF-1)

## Correct option

**4. Stromelysin**

## Answer explanation

Stromelysin is a matrix metalloproteinase that is involved in cartilage degeneration.

Matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs) are a family of proteolytic enzymes which utilize metal as a co-factor (i.e zinc) during the catalytic process to break down cartilage extracellular matrix including proteoglycans. Stromelysin is secreted by chondrocytes and fibroblasts, and secretion is regulated by several growth factors including PDGF (induces) and TGF-B (inhibits). It is believed that these MMPs play a role in articular degeneration in multiple forms of arthritis to include osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis. Tissue inhibitor of metalloproteinase (TIMPs) inhibits the degradative action of stromelysin.

*Tetlow et al. performed an immunohistochemical experiment on the cartilage in osteoarthritis specimens.* **They found** multiple MMPs including Stromelysin were found preferentially in the superficial layers of osteoarthritic cartilage cells. Non-arthritis specimens demonstrated near-zero MMPs in the superficial layers. This study supports the concept that cytokine-MMP associations reflect and an intrinsic process of cartilage degradation in OA.

*Li et al. wrote an extensive review of the age-related changes in cartilage and osteoarthritis.* Within the review, they discuss the epidemiology, cellular changes in chondrocytes, and changes in the cartilage matrix. Included in these sections are the effects of cytokines and MMPs on a genetic and cellular biology level.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: TGF-B stimulates proteoglycan and extracellular matrix synthesis in cartilage.
Answer 2: Aggrecan is an essential proteoglycan in cartilage that provides compressive strength and the hydrophilic behavior of cartilage
Answer 3: Osteoprotegerin is a RANKL sequestering protein that inhibits osteoclast differentiation and activation.
Answer 5: IGF-1 stimulates extracellular matrix production and cartilage matrix synthesis in adult articular cartilage.
