## Question

At 90o abduction, main stabilizer of shoulder against anterior dislocation is (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Anterior-inferior glenohumeral ligament
2. Postero-inferior glenohumeral ligament
3. Middle glenohumeral ligament
4. Superior glenohumeral ligament

## Correct option

**1. Anterior-inferior glenohumeral ligament**

## Answer explanation

The IGHL forms a hammock-like support when the shoulder is abducted to 90o with the antero-inferior and posterior-inferior bands forming the anterior and posterior supports respectively.

Answer: 1
