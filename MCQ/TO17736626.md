## Question

Not a posterior instrumentation in spine

## Options

1. Harrington rod system
2. Steffe plate
3. Dwyer Zielke instrumentation
4. Luque rod system

## Correct option

**3. Dwyer Zielke instrumentation**

## Answer explanation

Dwyer Zielki instrumentation is an anterior instrumentation used in cases of scoliosis. Rest all are posterior instrumentation systems. The commonest posterior instrumentation used nowadays is the Pedicle screw system (a pedicle screw spans through all the three columns of spine). This was first introduced in 1959 by H.H Boucher. More recently the use of plate systems (Steffe plates) with pedicle screws has become popular to provide the best of stability.

Answer: 3
