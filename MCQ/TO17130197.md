## Question

Metaphyseal bands are not seen in (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Lead poisoning
2. Methotrexate therapy
3. Hypervitaminosis D
4. Sickle cell disease

## Correct option

**3. Hypervitaminosis D**

## Answer explanation

All options given can result in formation of Dense metaphyseal bands. Hypervitaminosis D is the most uncommon cause so the best pick

Metaphyseal bands:

![[TO17130197-1.png]]

Common causes of metaphyseal bands are:

-Chronic anemia, e.g. sickle cell disease, thalassaemia

-Chemotherapy, e.g. methotrexate

-Growth acceleration lines following growth arrest due to systemic illness or stress in infancy or childhood, e.g. asthma, diabetes, cystic fibrosis, juvenile chronic arthritis, juvenile rheumatoid arthritis, malnutrition

Heavy metal poisoning: lead (most common), bismuth, arsenic, phosphorus, fluoride, mercury etc.

Leukemia: treated

Renal osteodystrophy (secondary hyperparathyroidism): healing

Rickets: healing

Bisphosphonate therapy

Normal variant: especially in a neonate - dense zone of provisional calcification

Answer: 3
