## Question

What type of physical therapy is most effective for chronic noninsertional Achilles tendinopathy?

## Options

1. Stair climbing
2. Eccentric strengthening
3. Concentric strengthening
4. Isometric strengthening
5. Rope jumping

## Correct option

**2. Eccentric strengthening**

## Answer explanation

Eccentric gastrocsoleus strengthening (especially with heavy loads) consistently has been shown to be superior in the management of Achilles tendinopathy. Decreases in pain and increases in strength have been demonstrated despite the frequently refractory nature of this condition.
