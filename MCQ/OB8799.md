## Question

## Options

1. Antibiotics
2. Wide resection with megaprosthesis
3. Radiation therapy
4. Internal fixation without resection
5. Chemotherapy

## Correct option

**4. Internal fixation without resection**

## Answer explanation

This patient has left distal humeral metastases from multiple myeloma with a pathologic fracture. Internal fixation is indicated, followed by postoperative radiation.

Multiple myeloma occurs at a median age of 65 years and has a male predominance. Surgery is indicated for impending or pathologic fracture. Surgical principles include preoperative imaging of the entire involved bone, surgery to create an immediately stable and durable construct, and postoperative radiation.

*Evans et al. reviewed the Mirels' rating system for fracture prediction in humeral metastases.* *They presented case histories and plain radiographs to physicians with varying training and experience.* Participants scored each case using Mirels’ criteria and provided a fracture prediction. The sensitivity and specificity for determining the likelihood of pathologic humeral fracture were 14.5% and 82.9%, respectively. **They recommend** a score of 7 or greater for identifying impending pathologic fractures of the humerus.

*Scharschmidt et al. reviewed multiple myeloma.* **They state** that pathologic fractures are common in patients with multiple myeloma and can be the presenting symptom. **They recommend** that the orthopedic surgeon and oncology team work together to develop an individualized treatment plan.

Figures A and B show a lytic lesion of the distal humerus. Anterior cortical thinning is present, and there is a fracture best identified on the lateral radiograph. Figure C is a bone scan showing increased activity in the right distal humerus and proximal left tibia. Figure D depicts the classic histologic presentation of multiple myeloma including characteristic round plasma cells with an eccentric nucleus, prominent nucleolus, and clock face organization of chromatin, and a characteristic clear area (Hoffa clear zone) next to the nucleus representing the prominent Golgi apparatus involved in immunoglobulin (protein) production.

Incorrect Answers
Answer 1: Antibiotics would be indicated in infection, but not for pathological fractures from multiple myeloma.
Answer 2: Wide resection may be considered in multiple myeloma involving periarticular regions, followed by a mega-prosthesis. This is not necessary in this case.
Answer 3: Radiation is important for pain relief and to reduce tumor bulk (if there is considerable soft tissue involvement) prior to surgical stabilization. Surgical stabilization should be prioritized in this case. There is no large soft tissue component on CT. An intramedullary rod will avoid dissection into the tumor.
Answer 5: While chemotherapy is the mainstay of treatment for multiple myeloma, surgical stabilization should be prioritized in this case.
