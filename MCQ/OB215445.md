## Question

Which of the following is the biggest risk factor for the development of venous thromboembolism (VTE) in a pediatric population undergoing surgery?

## Options

1. nephrotic syndrome
2. obesity
3. oral contraceptive use
4. history of VTE
5. spinal cord injury

## Correct option

**4. history of VTE**

## Answer explanation

History of prior venous thromboembolism (VTE) is considered to be a very high risk factor associated with the development of VTE in a pediatric population undergoing surgery.

Although rare, the incidence of VTE in pediatric patients undergoing surgery has increased over the years. Age-related risk factors such as lifestyle changes, initiation of oral contraceptives (OCPs), smoking, gastrointestinal and renal issues, and the rising obesity epidemic have been identified as important etiologic factors for adolescent VTE and may be important to identify and discuss with patients before an elective orthopedic procedure. The highest overall risk factors have been identified as an acquired or inherited thrombophilia, a history of a VTE, or a strong family history of VTE.

*Ellis et al. developed a simple screening tool to identify VTE risk factors and determine which pediatric patients necessitate preoperative hematology referral in the elective surgery population.* *They stratified these risk factors into three categories to include very high risk, major risk, and minor risk (Illustration A).* **They found** that 32.5% of adolescent patients undergoing elective arthroscopy had risk factors for VTE, and that a screening tool can identify up to 30% more risk factors.

*Baker et al. reviewed 14,776 pediatric patients who underwent orthopedic procedures to determine the incidence of VTE.* Overall, **they found** a low rate of VTE in this pediatric population and noted hyponatremia, abnormal partial thromboplastin time, elevated aspartate transaminase level, and gastrointestinal, renal, and hematologic disorders were associated with the development of VTE. No patients in the review died from their VTE or its complications.

Illustration A is a table of VTE risk factors by Ellis et al. in a pediatric and adolescent population undergoing elective lower extremity surgery.

Incorrect Answers
Answers 1,2,3,5: Nephrotic syndrome, obesity, OCP use, and spinal cord injury, are all considered to be risk factors associated with the development of VTE in a pediatric population, however, they are not considered to be very high risk factors.
