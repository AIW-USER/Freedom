## Question

All are true regarding RUNNER’S NIPPLES Except

## Options

1. Warm weather precipitates the condition
2. Occur due to irritation by rubbing against clothing
3. Common in cycling sports
4. Taping and use of Sports Bra is the standard recommendation

## Correct option

**1. Warm weather precipitates the condition**

## Answer explanation

Runner’s Nipples occur due to irritation caused by rubbing against clothing.

They are mostly associated with cold weather sports. Cold exposure makes nipples hard and prominent causing greater irritation.

The condition is common in Cyclists due to exposure to cold and perspiration.

Treatment involves taping nipples to restrict movement and application of petroleum jelly, moisturizers and Vitamin E soaks. Use of a Sports Bra to restrict breast movements is also recommended.

SPORTS BRA: Special bra that provides support against excessive movement during sports in young women. It is firm (but elastic enough to accommodate protective padding), Non Abrasive and has good absorptive properties. Ideally it should have crossed Y shaped back with no hooks/ fasteners. No seams should be there in the nipple area. Preferably sizing should be personally fitted.

Answer: 1
