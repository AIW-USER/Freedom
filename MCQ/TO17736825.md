## Question

True about complications of total ankle arthroplasty is [Arthroplasty-Total Ankle Arthroplasty]

## Options

1. The most frequent intraoperative complication of total ankle arthroplasty is fracture of the medial or lateral malleolus
2. The most frequent intraoperative complication of total ankle arthroplasty is fracture of the talus dome
3. Wound dehiscence is most common postoperative complication
4. Heterotopic ossification is rare after total ankle arthroplasty

## Correct option

**1. The most frequent intraoperative complication of total ankle arthroplasty is fracture of the medial or lateral malleolus**

## Answer explanation

Complications of total ankle arthroplasty

- The most frequent intraoperative complication of total ankle arthroplasty is fracture of the medial or lateral malleolus.
- Most of the published series show aseptic loosening as the most common cause for revision
- In some series arthrofibrosis/impingement has been reported as most common cause for revision surgery
- Debridement of heterotopic bone has also been reported as the most common cause of reoperation in some published series.
- Wound dehiscence and heterotopic ossification are major reported complications.

Answer: 1
