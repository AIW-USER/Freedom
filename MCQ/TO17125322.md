## Question

Ground reaction Ankle Foot Orthosis ( GRAFO)

## Options

1. Works on Newton’s third law
2. Accelerates knee extension at mid stance
3. Compensates for weak Gastrocnemius/soleus
4. All of the above

## Correct option

**4. All of the above**

## Answer explanation

GRAFO works on Newton's third law and thus the ‘reaction’ to the sum of body weight and center of mass acting downwards during stance”. So in case of weak quadriceps it accentuates knee extension in mid stance and compensates for weak plantar flexors.

Answer: 4
