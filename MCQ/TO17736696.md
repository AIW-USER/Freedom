## Question

Most common complication of arthroscopic surgery is (arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- ACL)

## Options

1. Cartilage injury
2. Hemarthrosis
3. Infection
4. Joint stiffness

## Correct option

**2. Hemarthrosis**

## Answer explanation

Complications for arthroscopic surgeries are as follows: in decreasing order of incidence:

Hemarthrosis, infection, thromboembolic disease, anesthetic complications, instrument failure, reflex sympathetic dystrophy, ligament injury, and fracture and neurologic injuries,

Answer : B
