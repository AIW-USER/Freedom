## Question

Figure 8 shows the radiograph of a 76-year-old man who has knee pain and swelling. History reveals that he underwent total knee arthroplasty 18 years ago. What is the most likely diagnosis?

![[q6173-a-08.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Loose femoral component
2. Loose tibial component
3. Particle-mediated osteolysis
4. Polyethylene failure
5. Infection

## Correct option

**4. Polyethylene failure**

## Answer explanation

The radiograph reveals complete loss of joint space with particulate metal debris consistent with total polyethylene failure and metal-on-metal articulation. The components appear to be well fixed and minimal osteolysis is evident.
