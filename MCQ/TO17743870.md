## Question

Not true about chauffeur’s fracture is (trauma- fractures around hand and wrist- chauffeur’s fracture)

## Options

1. Fracture of ulnar styloid process
2. Fracture of radial styloid process
3. Caused by scaphoid impaction against radial styloid
4. Hutchinson fracture

## Correct option

**1. Fracture of ulnar styloid process**

## Answer explanation

Chauffeur's fracture, also known as Hutchinson fracture, is fracture of the radial styloid process. The injury is typically caused by compression of the scaphoid bone of the hand against the styloid process of the distal radius. It can be caused by falling onto an outstretched hand. Treatment is often open reduction and internal fixation.

Answer: 1
