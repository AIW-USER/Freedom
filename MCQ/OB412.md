## Question

A 7-year-old male presents to the orthopedic clinic with leg pain that has been present for the past 6 months. The patient recently immigrated to the United States from Somalia. On physical exam, the patient appears malnourished. There is tenderness to palpation of the distal femur. Additionally, the gums have a bluish-purple hue with areas of hemorrhages. A radiograph of the patient's knee is demonstrated in Figure A. Given the most likely diagnosis, which of the following is true regarding this condition?

![[q412-a-scurvy1-1.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Result of decreased osteoclast function
2. Result of Vitamin D deficiency
3. Result of increased chondroitin sulfate and collagen synthesis
4. Primarily effects the epiphysis
5. Causes widening of the zone of provisional calcification

## Correct option

**5. Causes widening of the zone of provisional calcification**

## Answer explanation

The clinical presentation and radiograph are consistent with scurvy. Scurvy leads to widening of the zone of provision calcification which is demonstrated on radiograph as a dense band (White line of Frankl) at the growing metaphysis.

Scurvy is a disease resulting from a deficiency of vitamin C (ascorbic acid), which is required for the synthesis of collagen in humans. This leads to decreased chondroitin sulfate synthesis which causes defective collagen growth and repair in the metaphyseal region of the long bone. This defect in type I collagen synthesis primarily affects osteoblastic function. As a result, this decreased osteoblastic activity leads to defective primary and secondary spongiosa of the growing long bone.

Histologically, the primary trabeculae of the bone is replaced with granulation tissue causing thin cortices. Additionally, impaired collagen synthesis in scurvy leads to fragile capillaries, resulting in abnormal bleeding, ecchymosis, and joint effusions. The treatment for scurvy is prompt administration of vitamin C.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Decreased osteoclast function is the primary pathology seen in osteopetrosis
Answer 2: Scurvy is caused by vitamin C deficiency, not vitamin D
Answer 3: Chondroitin sulfate and collagen synthesis is decreased in scurvy
Answer 4: The physeal region of the metaphysis is primarily affected in scurvy.
