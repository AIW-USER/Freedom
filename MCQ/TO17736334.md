## Question

After closed reduction and percutaneous fixation with medial and lateral pins of a supracondylar fracture in a 6 years old girl, she developed partial ulnar nerve palsy with motor and sensory deficit in the immediate postoperative period. What is the next step in the treatment? (Trauma- fractures /dislocations around elbow- pediatric supracondylar fracturs)

## Options

1. Observation and remove the medial pin at 3 weeks if no improvement is seen
2. Remove the medial pin immediately
3. Exploration and anterior transposition of ulnar nerve
4. Observation and make sure the elbow is not hyper flexed to more than 90 degrees

## Correct option

**4. Observation and make sure the elbow is not hyper flexed to more than 90 degrees**

## Answer explanation

Medial pin insertion is associated with ulnar nerve palsy. Most patients improve and palsy completely resolves with no intervention in a couple of months (1 to 7 months).There is no evidence that pin removal, exploration, or transposition is necessary at this stage. Hyperflexion in a splint may cause the nerve to be opposed to the pin and should be corrected.

Answer: 4
