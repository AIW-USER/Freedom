## Question

Which of the following is the most common site for compression and burst fractures?

## Options

1. L2 level
2. L3 level
3. L4 level
4. L5 level
5. S1 level

## Correct option

**1. L2 level**

## Answer explanation

Thoracolumbar compression and burst fractures occur most frequently at the thoracolumbar junction (between T12 and L2) and represent more than 50% of all thoracic and lumbar fractures because the thoracolumbar junction represents a transition from the stiff thoracic spine to the more flexible lumbar spine.
