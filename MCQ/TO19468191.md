Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Anatomy ( Shoulder)

## Question

Ratio of glenohumeral to scapulothoracic motion is (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. 1:2
2. 2:1
3. 2:5:1
4. 3:1

## Correct option

**2. 2:1**

## Answer explanation

In the first 30 degrees of shoulder elevation the movement is largely glenohumeral. Scapulothoracic movement is small and inconsistent. After the first 30 degrees of shoulder elevation the glenohumeral and scapulothoracic joints move simultaneously. Overall ratio of glenohumeral to scapulothoracic movement is 2:1.

Answer: 2
