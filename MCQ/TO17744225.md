## Question

Ideal anticoagulant for preparation of PRP is [General Ortho.-Platelet rich plasma]

## Options

1. EDTA
2. Heparin
3. Acid citrate dextrose
4. Hirudin

## Correct option

**3. Acid citrate dextrose**

## Answer explanation

With regard to the type of anticoagulant for use, most authors agree on not using EDTA because it could damage the platelet membrane. Citrate based anticoagulants are preferable for PRP procurement, such as acid citrate dextrose (ACD) solution A or B, citrate-theophylline-adenosine-dipyridamole ( CTAD) and sodium citrate (CIT). They are ideal anticoagulants for PRP because they are able to prevent platelet activation and maintain the integrity and functional reserve of these cells.

Answer : c
