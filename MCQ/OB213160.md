## Question

A 30-year-old man presents to the ED following a motor vehicle collision (MVC). He is found to have a Gustillo IIIB fracture of the tibia. He is initially treated with wound debridement and placement of an external fixator. A free flap was used for soft tissue coverage of the defect. Two weeks later, he is treated with intramedullary nail stabilization and placement of an antibiotic spacer measuring 18cm in length. He is planned to undergo staged surgery. Which of the following statements is true regarding this staged procedure?

## Options

1. Excision of the spacer only should be performed at that time
2. Debridement of the membranous tissue and definitive fixation is recommended
3. One-month membranous tissue has the highest expression of IL-6
4. Two-month membranous tissue has the highest expression of VEGF
5. Three-month membranous tissue has the highest expression of type-I collagen

## Correct option

**3. One-month membranous tissue has the highest expression of IL-6**

## Answer explanation

The second stage of the Masquelet technique involves placement of bone graft into "induced membrane" and definitive fixation. This induced membrane has osteogenesis-improving capabilities that appear to decrease over time.

The Masquelet technique is a surgical technique in which a previously placed cement spacer is removed and bone grafted within the induced membrane to treat large segment defects in the bone. This induced membrane is a well-vascularized membrane that is rich in proteins such as BMP-2, VEGF, IL-6, and type-I collagen (Col-1), which promotes bony healing. Peak concentrations of these proteins occur at 4 weeks and return to baseline by around 6 months.

*Taylor et al. reviewed the Masquelet technique.* **They reported** that this technique allows for the development of a biologic membrane that appears to mature biochemically and physically 4 to 8 weeks after spacer placement. **They concluded** that the placement of cancellous autograft within this membrane prevents graft resorption, promotes revascularization and consolidation of new bone.

*Aho et al. reviewed the induced membrane characteristics of the Masquelet technique.* **They reported** that vascularization is greatest at one month but decreases to <60% at three months. Additionally, one-month-old membranes have the highest expression of VEGF, IL-6, and Col-1, whereas two-month-old membranes express <40% of the levels of one-month-old membranes. **They concluded** that the induced membranes have osteogenesis-improving capabilities that appear to decrease over time.

Incorrect Answer:
Answers 1&2: At the time of second-stage surgery, the spacer should be excised, the membranous tissue should be preserved, and the cavity should be bone grafted
Answers 4&5: One-month-old membranes have the highest expression of VEGF, IL-6, and Col-1, which decrease over time
