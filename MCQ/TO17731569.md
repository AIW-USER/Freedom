## Question

Most common site of osteochondritis dissicans is( Arthroscopy -knee Arthroscopy- cartilage injury) -

## Options

1. Lateral femoral condyle femur
2. Medial femoral condyle femur
3. Anteromedial corner of talus
4. Capitellum

## Correct option

**2. Medial femoral condyle femur**

## Answer explanation

Osteochondritis dissecans:

This is a special type of osteochondritis in which an osteochondral fragment separates from the rest of the bone and forms a loose body in the joint. Knee (lateral surface of medial femoral condyle) is the most common site affected, but a few other areas can also be involved like talus (anteromedial corner), femoral head (superomedial part), elbow (capitellum) and first metatarsal head.

Answer: 2
