## Question

A clinical study for lateral epicondylitis allocates 1 group to receive physical therapy for 4 weeks and another group a new oral medication for 4 weeks. Then the 2 groups immediately switch therapies with one another for the next 4 weeks. The half-life of the medication used in the study is 2 weeks. Which of the following best describes the bias that is present in this study design?

## Options

1. Recall bias
2. Verification bias
3. Washout period bias
4. Detection bias
5. Incorporation bias

## Correct option

**3. Washout period bias**

## Answer explanation

In a crossover study design, the washout period is the time between therapies, receiving no therapy, so that the effect of the first therapy is allowed to wear off. In this scenario, the medication's long half-life may continue to have effects after the first group (receiving the medication) has switched to the physical therapy treatment.

*The review article by Kocher et al describes the basics of biostatistics for clinicians.*

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Recall bias occurs when patients who experience an adverse outcome have a different likelihood of recalling an exposure than others that don't have an adverse outcome.
Answer 2: Verification bias occurs when results of a diagnostic test influence whether patients are assigned to a treatment group.
Answer 4: Detection bias occurs when one looks more carefully for an outcome in one specific group more than the other group(s).
Answer 5: Incorporation bias occurs when someone studies a diagnostic test that includes features of the target outcome.
