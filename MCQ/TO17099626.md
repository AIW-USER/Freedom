## Question

Exact size of Charnley’s head?

## Options

1. 22 mm
2. 22.225 mm
3. 22.240 mm
4. 22.28 mm

## Correct option

**2. 22.225 mm**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Sir John Charnley used the head size of 7/8 inch in his first THR. 7/8 inch = 22.2

Answer: 2
