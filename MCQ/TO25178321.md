Chapter: General Orthopedics MCQs — Bone Cement

## Question

Which mechanical property of PMMA is most negatively affected by the 'voids' created when high volumes of antibiotic powder are added?

## Options

1. Thermal conductivity
2. Compressive strength
3. Radio-opacity
4. Elution

## Correct option

**2. Compressive strength**

## Answer explanation

The voids act as stress risers, significantly reducing the ability of the cement to withstand the crushing forces of weight-bearing.

Answer: 2
