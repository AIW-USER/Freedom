Chapter: Spine MCQs — Complications (Fractures of Cervical Spine)

## Question

A patient is having a trans-articular C1/C2 screw fixation. Which structure is most at risk?

## Options

1. C2 nerve root
2. Hypoglossal nerve
3. Carotid artery
4. Vertebral artery

## Correct option

**4. Vertebral artery**

## Answer explanation

The indications for C1-C2 trans articular screw fixation include traumatic injuries to the atlantoaxial complex, instability resulting from inflammatory disease (rheumatoid arthritis), and congenital abnormalities (os odontoideum). The structure that is most at risk is the vertebral artery in the C2 vertebral artery groove, this may be at risk in almost 10% of patients based on CT planning but actual injury is thankfully much rare.

Answer: 4
