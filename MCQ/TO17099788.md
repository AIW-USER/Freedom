## Question

Most common source of hematoma in total hip replacement is

## Options

1. Branches of obturator vessels
2. Branches of femoral vessels
3. Branch of profunda femoris
4. Branches of superior and inferior gluteal vessel

## Correct option

**1. Branches of obturator vessels**

## Answer explanation

Common sources of bleeding in THR are-

- Branches of the obturator vessels near the ligamentum teres, transverse acetabular ligament, and inferior acetabular osteophytes,
- The first perforating branch of the profunda femoris deep to the gluteus maximus insertion
- Branches of the femoral vessels near the anterior capsule, and Branches of the inferior and superior gluteal vessels.

Answer: 1
