## Question

Which of the following does not cause PIN entrapment syndrome

## Options

1. Arcade of Froshe
2. ECRB origin
3. Gantzer muscle
4. Radial artery fanning

## Correct option

**3. Gantzer muscle**

## Answer explanation

Five potential sites of posterior interosseous nerve compression sites in the forearm include

- fibrous tissue anterior to the radiocapitellar joint (between the brachialis and brachioradialis)
- “leash of Henry” (these are recurrent radial vessels that fan out across the PIN at the level of the radial neck)
- extensor carpi radialis brevis edge- medio-proximal edge of the extensor carpi radialis brevis
- "arcade of Fröhse" - which is the proximal edge of the superficial portion of the supinator
- supinator muscle edge- distal edge of the supinator muscle

Answer : c
