Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Polytrauma Patients

## Question

A 29-year-old soldier steps on a landmine on the battlefield. He starts to bleed profusely from a mangled left leg. What is the initial treatment?

## Options

1. A battlefield tourniquet should be applied to the limb
2. A clean dressing with a military pressure bandage
3. A haemostatic dressing should be applied immediately
4. Immediate evacuation from the battlefield

## Correct option

**1. A battlefield tourniquet should be applied to the limb**

## Answer explanation

Early use of a tourniquet in combat situations has led to a reduction in morbidity from haemorrhagic limb injuries.

Answer: 1
