## Question

What will be true more for a 22 mm head than a 32 mm head

## Options

1. Higher Linear Wear rate
2. Lower Dislocation chances
3. Higher ROM
4. Periprosthetic fracture chances are low

## Correct option

**1. Higher Linear Wear rate**

## Answer explanation

Answer a)

As per literature, the mean rate of linear wear is higher in the 22-mm femoral head, whereas the volumetric wear is higher in the 32-mm femoral head

The “22 mm” head
 - produces too much linear wear or creep
 - smaller diameter head (22 mm) allow less stress but may result in increased central acetabular wear and dislocation

 The ”32 mm” head
 -32 mm allow increased ROM before it impinges against acetabulum, and therefore theoretically has less dislocation;
 - main disadvantage is that it has less net wall thickness, and produces too large a volume of wear debris

Other options

Answer b): decreasing femoral head size increases (not decreases) the risk of dislocation.

Answer c): decreasing femoral head size decreases (not increases) range of motion.

Answer d): Increasing femoral head size has not been shown to increase the rate of periprosthetic fracture.
