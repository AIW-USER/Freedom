## Question

Which of the following cytokines has been shown to promote scar tissue formation in lacerated skeletal muscle?

## Options

1. Fibroblast growth factor (FGF)
2. IGF-1
3. Nerve growth factor (NGF)
4. VEGF
5. TGF-Beta

## Correct option

**5. TGF-Beta**

## Answer explanation

In lacerated skeletal muscle the production of fibrous scar tissue is stimulated by the presence of TGF-beta.

Once laceration of skeletal muscle has occurred, regeneration and reinnervation is unpredictable. Regeneration may occur within 7-10 days after injury. This process is facilitated by the cytokines: IGF-1, FGF, and NGF. At the same time fibrous scar tissue forms to bridge the lacerated muscle segments. This process is mediated by TGF-Beta, which stimulates the differentiation and proliferation of myofibroblasts.

*Pereira et al. studied the expression of inflammatory cytokines in lacerated skeletal muscle.* In muscle samples where the nerve had been cut there was increased fibrosis. Additonally, there was increased production of TGF-Beta.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1, 2, 3: FGF, IGF-1 and NGF are implicated in the regeneration of damaged skeletal muscle.
Answer 4: VEGF is not implicated in the formation of fibrosis in lacerated skeletal muscle.
