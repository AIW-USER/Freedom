## Question

Amount of blood to be collected from an athlete for dope testing ideally is

## Options

1. 20 ml once divided into two samples
2. 10 ml twice sent in two samples
3. 15 ml twice and sent in two samples
4. 10 ml once and sent in two samples

## Correct option

**1. 20 ml once divided into two samples**

## Answer explanation

Ans : A

Amount of Blood taken in each vial is not fixed but approximately more than 5 ml should be collected in each tube, so around 15-17 ml for both tubes.

Process to take Blood

- Confirm athlete’s Identity (Are you…..?)
- Explain the doping control process to athlete as needed/requested
- Be sure the athlete has been sitting non-stop feet on the floor (not laying down) for at least 10 minutes. You can use this time to answer the questions on the BCF and fill in the declaration of medication/supplements section.
- Athlete chooses a BEREG kit (1 from 3 kits to choose from).
- Athlete checks and opens the kit and removes all the contents.
- DCO/BCO and athlete check the numbers on the plastic bottle, lid and the stickers to make sure they are all the same.
- DCO/BCO (Doping Control Officer/ Blood Control Officer) should not touch any of the materials though at this point.
- Athlete places one sample code sticker on the vacutainer(s) longitudinally.
- The BCO chooses the most suitable arm for venipuncture. This will always be the non-dominant arm, unless the BCO assesses the other arm to be more suitable or the athlete requests a specific arm.
- BCO applies a tourniquet to the athlete’s upper arm.
- BCO inserts the needle into the athlete’s vein then inserts the vacutainer into the needle. Be sure to hold the vacutainer firmly in place as it will push back and you can lose the vacuum.
- BCO releases the tourniquet as soon as blood starts to flow into the vacutainer.
- The BCO collects the amount of blood needed for the test.
- Ask if athlete has taken any medication, vitamins or supplements in the last 7days. And let the athlete fill out “declaration of medication/supplements” part of BCF
