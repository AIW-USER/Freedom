## Question

Which of the following deformity is not commonly seen in the foot in cerebral palsy?

## Options

1. Equinocavovarus foot
2. Equinovalgus foot
3. Hallux valgus
4. Cavus

## Correct option

**4. Cavus**

## Answer explanation

First three deformities are seen in the cerebral palsy foot. The hemiplegic commonly present with an equinocavovarus foot whereas the diplegic as they go into crouch present with an equinoplanovalgus / Planovalgus foot. Hallux valgus is also a common problem in the foot with cerebral palsy which causes issue in wearing of the orthosis too. In fact Hallux valgus recalcitrant to non operative management in cerebral palsy is preferably managed with arthrodesis, especially in the non ambulatory child.

Isolated Cavus deformity is infact a sign of underlying neurological disorder, spinal dysraphism and CMT being the common ones.

Answer: 4
