Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Ligament Balancing in TKR

## Question

Tight flexion gap in CR knee, options

## Options

1. Cut more proximal tibia
2. Cut PCL
3. Increase implant size (femoral)
4. Cut distal femur

## Correct option

**2. Cut PCL**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B
EXPLANATION
Increasing implant size would rather tighten the gap so not an option at all. Cutting more distal femur will also not provide any benefit as it will widen extension (not flexion) gap. Cutting more of proximal tibia would certainly widen the flexion gap but would also have a detrimental effect of further widening the extension gap simultaneously. So, cutting the PCL would be best pick.
Flexion gap tightness is particularly an issue that more commonly occurs with CR knee. This causes tightness of the PCL and eventually impedes flexion. In this scenario, transacting the PCL and changing to PS knee is most appropriate. Other methods that can be opted include increasing the posterior tibial slope (this has a limit) or down sizing the femoral component (that would require removing more bone from posterior femoral condyles) which could necessitate change of referencing method used for bone cutting (changing from posterior to anterior referencing).
