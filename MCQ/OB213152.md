## Question

An 11-year-old boy falls on an outstretched hand and presents with the injury depicted in Figure A. What portion of the physis in Figure B does this injury most commonly occur?

![[q213152-a-img-7138.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q213152-b-presentation1.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. A
2. B
3. C
4. D
5. E

## Correct option

**4. D**

## Answer explanation

In the skeletally immature population, Salter-Harris fractures occur through the hypertrophic zone of the physis, which is labeled D in Figure B.

Pediatric patients are at risk for fractures of the physis. Physeal fractures most commonly propagate through the hypertrophic zone because this is the weakest zone in tension. The more ductile chondral elements of the proliferative zone interface with the calcified cartilage of the hypertrophic zone. The reserve and proliferative zones remain attached to the epiphysis, with preserved circulation. As a result, reduction almost always leads to reliable healing but with the possibility of residual growth disturbance with increasing grades of injury.

*Caine et al. reviewed potentially serious injuries in the pediatric athlete.* **They reported** that sudden and gradual onset physeal injury is a unique injury to the pediatric population with growth threatened if not managed correctly. **They highlighted** that physeal stress injuries are thought to develop when repetitive loading of the extremity disrupts metaphyseal perfusion in the hypertrophic zone, which in turn inhibits ossification of the chondrocytes in the zone of provisional calcification. **They concluded** that for physeal stress injuries, management should consist of rest from loading of the extremity. However, in cases involving growth disturbance, corrective surgery may be required.

*DiFiori et al. reviewed wrist pain and distal radial physeal injuries in the young gymnast.* **They reported** that since gymnasts typically begin gymnastics at a very young age and train for several years prior to skeletal maturity, the wrist physis is at risk for injury. **They concluded** that early detection and treatment of wrist pain in young gymnasts may serve to limit the extent of the injury, accelerate recovery, and reduce the prevalence of chronic pain and wrist injury in older gymnasts.

Figure A depicts a Salter-Harris I fracture of the distal radius. Figure B illustrates the histology of the physis.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: A is the epiphysial cap.
Answer 2: B is the reserve zone.
Answer 3: C is the proliferative zone.
Answer 5: E is the primary spongiosa of the metaphysis.
