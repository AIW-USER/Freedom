## Question

Investigation of choice for stress fracture is (general orthopedics- stress fractures)

## Options

1. CT scan
2. MRI
3. Clinical examination
4. Bone scan

## Correct option

**2. MRI**

## Answer explanation

Even at early onset, MRI has the best combined specificity and sensitivity for bone stress injuries and is the recommended diagnostic imaging test to assess the spectrum of bone stress injury among symptomatic patients.

Grading is based on signs seen at MRI:

Grade 1: mild - moderate periosteal edema on STIR, no marrow changes

Grade 2: moderate - severe periosteal edema on STIR + marrow changes on T2WI

Grade 3: Grade 2 + marrow changes on T1WI

Grade 4: fracture line visible

Answer: 2
