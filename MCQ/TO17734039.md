## Question

Iselin disease is? (regional orthopedics- foot and ankle- iselin disease)

## Options

1. Osteochondritis Of 5th metatarsal head
2. Osteochondritis Of 2nd Metatarsal head
3. Osteochondritis Of 5th Metatarsal base
4. Osteochondritis Of Lower Pole Of patella

## Correct option

**3. Osteochondritis Of 5th Metatarsal base**

## Answer explanation

Iselin disease is a benign and self-limiting condition, defined as apophysitis of the base of the 5th metatarsal. It is most commonly seen in young and adolescent sportspersons.

- Osteochondritis of the lunate bone: kienbock's disease

- Osteochondritis of lateral part of medial femoral condyle: osteochondritis dissecans
- Osteochondritis of the tibial tubercle: osgood schlatter’s disease
- Osteochondritis of lower part of patella: sinding larsen johansson disease
- Osteochondritis of the calcaneal tuberosity: sever’s disease
- Osteochondritis of the navicular bone: kohler’s disease
- Osteochondritis of 2nd metatarsal head: freiberg’s disease
- Osteochondritis of 5th metatarsal base: islene disease
- Osteochondritis of the ring epiphysis of the vertebrae: scheurmann’ s disease
- Osteochondritis of the central bony nucleus of vertebral body: calve’s disease
