## Question

A mental health professional is giving a grand rounds lecture on physician burnout. All medical and surgical residents are in attendance. With respect to physician suicide, which of the following is true?

## Options

1. Orthopedic surgeons account for the highest number of suicides among surgical subspecialties at 28%
2. Orthopedic surgeons are 25% more likely to die by suicide than the general population
3. Orthopedic surgeons have the third highest number of suicides among medical specialties at 15%
4. Orthopedic surgeons have the lowest number of suicides among surgical subspecialties
5. Orthopedic surgeons have a equal likelihood to die by suicide to that of the general population

## Correct option

**1. Orthopedic surgeons account for the highest number of suicides among surgical subspecialties at 28%**

## Answer explanation

Among surgical subspecialties, orthopedic surgeons accounted for the highest number of suicides at 28.2%.

Burnout (exhaustion syndrome and professional burnout) is a condition characterized by emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, and a sense of loss of personal accomplishment. Diagnosis is made using validated questionnaires such as the Maslach Burnout Inventory and Professional Fulfillment Index. Treatment involves a combination of leave of absence, medications, therapy, and workplace modifications.

*Elkbuli et al. tried to identify trends and patterns in physician/surgeon suicide and the key factors influencing physician suicide.* **They reported** that the prevalence of physician suicide increased over the 2003 to 2017 timeframe with over a third of deaths occurring from 2015 to 2017. **They concluded** that among surgeons, orthopedics has the highest prevalence of reported suicide.

*Peterson et al. reviewed the suicide rate among the U.S. working-age population (persons aged 16–64 years).* **They reported** that the suicide rate has increased 34%, from 12.9 per 100,000 population to 17.3. **They concluded** that the largest suicide rate increase among males from 2012 to 2015 (47%) occurred in the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media occupational group and among females, in the Food Preparation and Serving Related group, from 6.1 to 9.4 (54%).

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 2&5: Surgeons were found to be 6% more likely to die by suicide when compared with the general population.
Answers 3&4: Among surgical subspecialties, orthopedic surgeons accounted for the highest number of suicides at 28.2%.
