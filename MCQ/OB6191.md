## Question

What structure is located at the tip of the arrow in Figure 18?

![[q6191-a-18.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Left L3 nerve root
2. Right L3 nerve root
3. Right L4 segmental artery
4. Right L4 nerve root
5. Left lateral disk herniation

## Correct option

**2. Right L3 nerve root**

## Answer explanation

The structure shown is the exiting nerve root at the L3-4 disk, which is the right L3 root.
