Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Carpal Instability

## Question

A patient presents with a 'clunk' during ulnar deviation. Fluoroscopy shows a sudden jump of the proximal row from flexion to extension at the midcarpal joint. There is no SL or LT dissociation. What is the most likely classification?

## Options

1. CID - Scapholunate dissociation
2. CIA - Madelung deformity
3. CIND - Midcarpal instability (MCI)
4. CIC - Perilunate Injury

## Correct option

**3. CIND - Midcarpal instability (MCI)**

## Answer explanation

Midcarpal CIND typically involves a loss of the normal transition from flexion to extension, resulting in a symptomatic 'clunk' as the row shifts.

Answer: 3
