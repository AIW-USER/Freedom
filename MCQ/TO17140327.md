## Question

All are true regarding pelvic incidence(PI) except

## Options

1. PI is Relation between the sacral plate and the femoral heads.
2. PI is Constant morphological parameter for one person and Does not change after skeletal maturity
3. PI is posture dependant
4. Linear correlation between age and PI and PI stabilizes in early adolescence

## Correct option

**3. PI is posture dependant**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

![[TO17140327-1.png]]

Pelvic incidence is not posture dependent while pelvic tilt and sacral slope are posture dependent. The PT(pelvic tilt) and LL(lumbar lordosis) increase with age during childhood to provide adequate anterior displacement of the body’s center of gravity. The SS is not significantly influenced by age and is independently achieved with standing posture heads.

Pelvic incidence = Pelvic tilt + sacral slope
