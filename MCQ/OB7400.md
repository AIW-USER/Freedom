## Question

Figure 12a shows a cross section of the pelvis at the level of the greater trochanters. What structure is marked with the arrow?

![[q7400-a-12a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Adductor magnus
2. Obturator internus
3. Obturator externus
4. Pectineus
5. Adductor brevis

## Correct option

**2. Obturator internus**

## Answer explanation

In Figure 12b, the arrow marks the obturator internus muscle which projects posteriorly and banks around the ischium, inserting on the posterior aspect of the proximal femur, just below the piriformis. The other structures are labeled. The obturator externus is more anterior and is seen anterior to the ischium. The adductor magnus is not seen in this image, and is more distal. The adductor longus is just starting to appear anteriorly (with the adductor brevis just posterior), and the pectineus is seen posterior and just deep to the femoral vessels.
