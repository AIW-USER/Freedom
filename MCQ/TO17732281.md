## Question

What is the level of evidence for meta-analysis of cohort study?( General orthopaedics- research and statistics)

## Options

1. 1
2. 2
3. 3
4. 4

## Correct option

**2. 2**

## Answer explanation

Level of evidences:

Level
Type of evidence

1
Systemic review or meta-analysis of RCTs, High quality randomized trial, prospective study, All or none study.

2
Lesser quality RCT , Systemic review or meta-analysis of cohort studies, Individual cohort study, prospective comparative study, retrospective study

3
Systemic review of case control study, Case control study (therapeutic and prognostic studies); retrospective comparative study

4
Case series

5
Case report (a report of a single case)

Expert opinion

Personal observation

Answer: 2
