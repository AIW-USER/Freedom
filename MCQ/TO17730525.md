## Question

Which is not a complication of colles fracture? (traumatology- fractures around wrist and hand-fracture of distal end radius)

## Options

1. Nonunion
2. EPL tear
3. Complex regional pain syndrome
4. Gunstock deformity

## Correct option

**4. Gunstock deformity**

## Answer explanation

Complications of colles fractures are-

Early complications: TFCC tear and DRUJ instability, Complex regional pain syndrome,

Late complications: wrist stiffness, malunion (DINNER FORK DEFORMITY), tendon injury (EPL tear)

Answer: 4
