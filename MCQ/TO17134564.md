## Question

The following lesion is also known as “glomus AVMs”

## Options

1. Type I spinal AVMs
2. Type II spinal AVMs
3. Type III spinal AVMs
4. Type IV spinal AVMs

## Correct option

**2. Type II spinal AVMs**

## Answer explanation

Type II or glomus AVMs are intramedullary spinal vascular malformations.

Type I or spinal dural AVF are the most common type of spinal AVMs. They are believed to be ACQUIRED. They present with a SLOW PROGRESSIVE course.

The other types II, III & IV are CONGENITAL.

Type III or Juvenile AVMs, are intramural. They are true AVMs with rapid blood flow and at risk for SAH or intramedullary hemorrhage.

Type IV AVMs are intramural extramedullary or peri medullary.
