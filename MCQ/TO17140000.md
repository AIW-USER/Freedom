Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Anatomy ( Shoulder)

## Question

Which is not a stabilizer of intra articular part of the long head of the biceps? (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. Subscapularis tendon
2. SGHL
3. CHL
4. Transverse humeral ligament

## Correct option

**4. Transverse humeral ligament**

## Answer explanation

Transverse humeral ligament is stabilizer of extra articular portion of long head of biceps

Answer: 4
