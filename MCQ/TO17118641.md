## Question

Post ACL reconstruction, a patient has > 10-degree loss of extension and > 25-degree loss of flexion, with tight medial and lateral capsular structures. Patellar movements and position of patella are normal. Which type of arthrofibrosis is it? (FNB Pattern Paper 2020)

## Options

1. Type 4
2. Type 3
3. Type 2
4. Type 1

## Correct option

**3. Type 2**

## Answer explanation

There is no universally accepted definition of arthrofibrosis. Historically, arthrofibrosis can be defined as a loss of motion after a knee trauma or reconstructive surgery. Based on literature, its overall prevalence is about 4-38%. Shelbourne and associates classified arthrofibrosis based on the pattern and severity of knee stiffness.
