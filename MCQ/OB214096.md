## Question

A 7-year-old boy presents with an angulated extra-physeal distal radial fracture that is reduced and placed in a well-molded short arm cast by the orthopedic resident. Post-reduction radiographs reveal residual dorsal angulation of 8 degrees with no shortening. The child is discharged by the emergency room. Which of the following statements below is most accurate?

## Options

1. Application of a short arm cast was inappropriate given his age and fracture characteristics
2. As long as the short arm cast index is 0.9 or greater, closed treatment will be successful
3. Lack of communication can adversely affect post-discharge care transitions
4. It is the sole responsibility of the child's parents to locate an orthopedist for followup
5. It is the sole responsibility of the primary care physician to locate an orthopedist for followup

## Correct option

**3. Lack of communication can adversely affect post-discharge care transitions**

## Answer explanation

Of the above statements, the most accurate is that lack of communication has clearly been shown to adversely affect post-discharge care transitions.

Transition of care between inpatient and outpatient settings have significant patient safety and quality implications. Studies have demonstrated that discharge summaries often lack important information such as diagnostic test results, treatment or hospital course, discharge medications, test results pending at discharge, patient or family counseling, and follow-up plans. Individuals with no clear followup plans are unsure of where to go and often return to the emergency department, which results in increased costs and delay in care. Communication between hospital physicians and primary care or subspecialty physicians must be established to create a loop of continuous care and diminish morbidity and mortality.

*Snow et al. developed consensus standards to address the quality gaps in the transitions between inpatient and outpatient settings.* **They reported** that at every point of transition, the patient and/or their family and caregivers need to know who is responsible for their care at that point. **They recommended** that a high standard be maintained in the domains of coordinating clinicians, care plans/transition record, communication infrastructure, standard communication formats, transition responsibility, timeliness, community standards, and measurement.

*Chugh et al. reviewed methods in the comprehension of discharge instructions during care transitions.* **They reported** that inadequate health literacy and unrecognized cognitive impairment are two contributing factors to poor care transitions. **They concluded** that better care transitions may impact the frequency of adverse events and decrease the likelihood of readmissions.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: For this patient and his fracture characteristics, there is no increased risk of loss of reduction with good (i.e. well molded) short arm vs. long arm casting.
Answer 2: A cast index higher than 0.8 is associated with loss of fracture reduction (not treatment success).
Answer 4 and 5: It is not the sole responsibility of the child's parents or PCP to locate an orthopedist for followup, however the discharging institution/provider should provide a list of options for followup.
