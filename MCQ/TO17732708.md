## Question

Most common tumor in children is (orthopedic oncology- malignant tumors- osteosarcoma)

## Options

1. Neuroblastoma
2. Osteosarcoma
3. Ewing's tumor
4. Brain tumor

## Correct option

**4. Brain tumor**

## Answer explanation

Most common tumor of infancy: Neuroblastoma

Most common abdominal tumor of childhood: Neuroblastoma

Most common extracranial tumor of childhood: Neuroblastoma

Most common solid tumor of childhood: Brain tumor

Most common malignancy of childhood: Leukemia (ALL)

Most common benign bone lesion in children: Non ossifying fibroma

Most common benign bone tumor in children: Osteochondroma

Most common malignant bone tumor in children: Osteosarcoma

Answer: 4
