## Question

Commonest CPAK type is

## Options

1. neutral aHKA and apex distal JLO
2. varus aHKA and apex distal JLO
3. valgus aHKA and apex distal JLO
4. neutral aHKA and apex proximal JLO

## Correct option

**1. neutral aHKA and apex distal JLO**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 1
CPAK Type II knees (neutral aHKA and apex distal JLO) are the commonest knee type, comprising nearly 40% of knees in the normal population.
