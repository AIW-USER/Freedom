## Question

After normal menses has begun and in the absence of pregnancy, secondary amenorrhea is defined as which of the following?

## Options

1. Increase in menstrual volume for 3 months
2. Absence of menstrual bleeding for 6 months
3. Excessive pain during menstrual bleeding for 2 months
4. Absence of menstrual bleeding in the month following peak training intensity
5. Decrease in menstrual volume for 2 consecutive months

## Correct option

**2. Absence of menstrual bleeding for 6 months**

## Answer explanation

Secondary amenorrhea is defined as the absence of menstrual bleeding for 6 months or the absence of three to six consecutive menstrual cycles after normal menses has begun. The prevalence of amenorrhea among female athletes is estimated at 10% to 20% in women who exercise vigorously and as high as 40% to 66% in elite runners and professional ballet dancers.
