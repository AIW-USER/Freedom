## Question

Miller’s Asthma is due to( general orthopaedics-anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. Hypersensitivity to pollen grains
2. Temperature variation seen in athletes
3. Hypersensitivity to granary weevil
4. Coal hypersensitivity

## Correct option

**3. Hypersensitivity to granary weevil**

## Answer explanation

Miller’s asthma is also known as Grain worker's asthma or Mill-workers' asthma. It is hypersensitivity pneumonitis to granary weevil.

Answer: 3
