Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Chondromalcia Patella  Patellofemoral Pain Syndrome

## Question

Which exercise is likely to increase pain in a patient of patellofemoral pain syndrome?

## Options

1. Leg press
2. Leg extension
3. Squat
4. Lateral step

## Correct option

**2. Leg extension**

## Answer explanation

At present, the best exercises to strengthen the quadriceps in patients with patellofemoral dysfunction in the intermediate and advanced phases of the treatment, are the techniques of closed kinetic chain (minisquatting, lateral step, bicycling with a high saddle, etc.) in the last degrees of extension (from 0° to 30°), as the joint is subject to the minimal pressures. Furthermore, the strengthening of the VMO is favored if the mini-squatting is associated to an adduction of the hips, which can be achieved with the use of a balloon. The clinical experience shows that patients with PFJ problems seem to tolerate best the exercises of leg press (closed kinetic chain) through the functional mobility range (less contact pressure [force times area] upon the PFJ), but they tend to present an increase of the symptoms during the leg extension exercises in open kinetic chain against resistance, in the functional mobility range (greater contact pressure [force times area] upon the PFJ). Additionally, many patients without problems in the PFJ develop symptoms after leg extension.

Answer: 2
