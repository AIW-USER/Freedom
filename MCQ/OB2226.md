## Question

A 3-year-old male is evaluated in your office. On examination he is found to have several pigmented cutaneous lesions and hamartomas of the iris. His right leg was previously treated with both a knee-ankle-foot orthotic and long-leg casting for approximately 9 months. His most recent radiographs are seen in Figures A and B. What is the next best treatment?

## Options

1. Bone stimulator
2. Continue bracing
3. Syme amputation
4. Resection of pseudoarthrosis with bone grafting and surgical fixation
5. Below knee amputation

## Correct option

**4. Resection of pseudoarthrosis with bone grafting and surgical fixation**

## Answer explanation

This patient has clinical findings concerning for Neurofibromatosis. His radiographs demonstrate anterolateral tibial bowing with a pseudarthrosis and surgical intervention is indicated.

In this vignette, the pigmented cutaneous lesions are referring to café au lait spots, which suggests an underlying diagnosis of neurofibromatosis. Neurofibromatosis is associated with extremity deformities such as hemihypertrophy, congenital anterolateral bowing, and pseudarthrosis of the lower leg and forearm. Anteromedial and posteromedial tibial bowing are associated with fibular hemimelia and physiologic tibial bowing, respectively. Treatment is influenced by the presence of a fracture or pseudarthrosis. Total contact orthosis is indicated for anterolateral bowing without a fracture or pseudarthrosis to prevent further bowing or occurence of a fracture. Bone grafting and surgical fixation is indicated with a fracture or pseudarthrosis. Amputation is typically reserved following 3 failed surgical attempts.

*Crawford et al. investigated the osseous manifestations of neurofibromatosis in over 150 patients <12 years of age and reported an incidence of one in 3,000 live births.* **They concluded** that orthopedic manifestations should warrant an NF-1 evaluation.

*Feldman et al. investigated the clinical manifestations of NF-1.* *They reviewed clinical and orthopedic manifestations, neoplasms, neurologic, and cardiovascular features.* **They concluded** that NF-1 affects multiple organ systems, and a multidisciplinary approach to treatment is required.

Figures A and B are AP and lateral radiographs demonstrating anterolateral tibial bowing with a pseudarthrosis of the tibia.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1: A bone stimulator is likely to be ineffective after 9 months of conservative management.
Answer 2: Bracing is indicated with anterolateral bowing without a fracture or pseudarthrosis.
Answers 3 and 5: Amputation is indicated after 3 failed surgical attempts. Syme amputation is favored over BKA due to atrophic and scarred calf musculature in these patients.
