## Question

Which of the following is true of the fibrochondrosseous structure at the periphery of the physis that includes the Zone of Ranvier and the Ring of LaCroix in the growing physis?

## Options

1. Zone responsible for longitudinal growth
2. Increases mechanical strength of the physis
3. Location of chondrocyte maturation, hypertrophy, and calcification
4. Dysfunction results in Achondroplasia
5. Osteoblast align on cartilage bars to produce physeal expansion

## Correct option

**2. Increases mechanical strength of the physis**

## Answer explanation

The Zone of Ranvier and the Ring of LaCroix are part of the peripheral ring that, during the first year of life, mainly provides mechanical strength to the growing physis.

During the first year of life, the Zone of Ranvier forms a fibrous circumferential ring around the physis that bridges from the diaphysis to the epiphysis. This structure provides the first mechanical strength to the physis. Secondarily, it provides a region of appositional growth and provides chondrocytes to the periphery, which in turn, further adds to the ability to resist mechanical forces. From a pathologic standpoint, the Zone of Ranvier is the region that produces osteochondromas, a benign chondrogenic lesion.

*Shapiro et al review the organization and cellular biology of the Zone of Ranvier in rabbits.* **They report** three groups of cells were identified in this part of the physis: 1. Progenitor cells of osteoblasts, 2. Undifferentiated mesenchymal cells and fibroblasts, and 3. Fibroblasts amongst organized collagen fibers. **They conclude** that this part of the physis is anchors the periosteum to the epiphysis and provides strength as bone growth proceeds.

*Burkus et al review the microscopic development of the distal femoral physis.* *They report on 97 human specimens ranging in age from 9 prenatal weeks to 16 postnatal years, and how growth developed.* **They conclude** that interstitial chondrogenesis became regionally limited, with appositional chondrogenesis being restricted to the Zone of Ranvier.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Longitudinal growth occurs through the Proliferative Zone.
Answer 3: Chondrocyte maturation, hypertrophy, and calcification all occur within the Hypertrophic Zone, not the Zone of Ranvier.
Answer 4: Achondroplasia occurs due to a gain of function mutation that inhibits chondrocyte proliferation within the Proliferative Zone.
Answer 5: The alignment of osteoblasts on cartilage bars to produce physeal expansion occurs within the primary spongiosa.
