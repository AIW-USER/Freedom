Chapter: Spine MCQs — Intervertebral Disc

## Question

Main blood supply of intervertebral disc is through (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Diffusion through pores in the endplates
2. Diffusion through pores in the annulus
3. Capillaries of sinuvertebral artery
4. Capillaries of vertebral artery

## Correct option

**1. Diffusion through pores in the endplates**

## Answer explanation

Vascular and neural supply of intervertebral discs:

Blood supply: The intervertebral discs are avascular structures and have no blood supply. Blood capillaries terminate at the end plates only. Annulus is a tough structure and not porous enough to allow nutrition through diffusion. Nucleus pulposus gets nutrition through diffusion through pores in the endplates.

Nerve supply: The intervertebral disc is innervated through the sinuvertebral nerves which arise from dorsal root ganglion.

Normal nucleus pulposus and inner annular zones are devoid of nerves. Only the superficial layers of the normal annulus have sensory nerve endings.

In degenerative discs, nerves extend deeper, even up to the inner third in 50% of painful degenerative discs. Many of these nerves were observed in a perivascular position, suggesting that they arise from granulation tissue growing into the degenerative disc, neo-innervation.

Recently some studies have shown that the lumbar vertebral end-plate, particularly in its central area adjacent to the nucleus pulposus, is supplied with a neural pattern similar to that of the outer annulus.
