## Question

Which is not an indication of ALL reconstruction?

## Options

1. Explosive pivot
2. Revision ACL reconstruction
3. High grade ACL tear in a young active man
4. Segond fracture

## Correct option

**3. High grade ACL tear in a young active man**

## Answer explanation

Biomechanical studies have shown that the ALL is an important stabilizer during anterolateral tibial rotation and affects pivot shift in cases of ACL failure. The main aim of the associated ACL and ALL reconstruction would be a greater rotational control and prevention of ACL retear. Thus, the best indications for ALL reconstruction are clinical conditions that present greater rotational instability and greater risk of retear.

Indications for anterolateral ligament reconstructions are:

Explosive or pivot shift grade 3, Active sports person with pivot mechanism, or Segond fracture, revision ACL reconstruction, subjective rotational laxity, chronic ACL injury, high level of sports activity, radiographic signs of lateral femoral condyle depression and Telos value >10 mm.

Answer: 3
