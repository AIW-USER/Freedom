## Question

Spinal shock is characterized by all except?[SPINE-SPINAL CORD INJURY]

## Options

1. flaccid areflexic paralysis
2. Absence of bulbocavernosus reflex
3. Neurological assessment can’t be done in stage of spinal shock
4. Bulbocavernosus reflex returns in all cases of Injury to cauda equina when spinal shock is over

## Correct option

**4. Bulbocavernosus reflex returns in all cases of Injury to cauda equina when spinal shock is over**

## Answer explanation

Spinal shock is characterized by:

- flaccid areflexic paralysis
- bradycardia & hypotension (due to loss of sympathetic tone)
- absent bulbocavernosus reflex - reflex characterized by anal sphincter contraction in response to squeezing the glans penis or tugging on an indwelling Foley catheter

Evaluation of spinal shock is important because one cannot evaluate neurologic deficit until the spinal shock phase has resolved. End of spinal shock indicated by return of the bulbocavernosus reflex. Conus or cauda equina injuries may lead to permanent loss of the bulbocavernosus reflex.

Answer: 4
