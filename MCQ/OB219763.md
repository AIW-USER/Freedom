## Question

A physician in your practice is investigated after a long string of patient complaints and reports to patient affairs. As a result, you are asked to attend the ethics board's discussion regarding this colleague. Which of the following terms is incorrectly paired with its definition?

## Options

1. Beneficence: The duty to do good for the patient
2. Nonmaleficence: The duty to not harm the patient
3. Respect for autonomy: The right of the patient for self-determination
4. Justice: The duty to ensure everyone gets the same treatment
5. Confidentiality: A patient's right and the doctor's duty to protect patient's personal information

## Correct option

**4. Justice: The duty to ensure everyone gets the same treatment**

## Answer explanation

Justice represents more than a duty to treat everyone equally; the physician's responsibility is to ensure that each patient receives fair, equitable, and appropriate treatment.

The Hippocratic oath, typically taken by medical students, strongly emphasizes nonmaleficence or “do no harm” but this definition should also be expanded to include actively seeking to help others (beneficence). Furthermore, the oath encourages providers to be empathetic, allow for choice (autonomy), and provide equitable treatment to all (justice). These four definitions represent the four pillars of medical ethics. Bioethicists use these principles to help determine the merits and conflicts of procedures and decision-making in medical care. Medical practices should strive to respect all four pillars to be considered ethical.

Gillon provides a unique commentary on the four pillars of medical ethics. He argues that, despite protests that they are overly simplified, they provide a universal lens through which all physicians can accept a basic moral language. Gillon concludes that this universal language allows for a moral mission statement for all practicing medicine.

*Johnson et al. present a comprehensive review of medical errors in hand surgery.* *They report on the incidence and legal, financial, and ethical considerations and provide recommendations to prevent and address these errors.* In their conclusion, **they emphasize** surgeon response as the best target for intervention.

Incorrect answers:
Answers 1-3, 5: Each answer is correctly paired with its definition.
