## Question

When using the Ponseti technique of clubfoot correction, the foot is manipulated in

## Options

1. supination and abduction.
2. supination and adduction.
3. supination and dorsiflexion.
4. pronation and abduction.
5. pronation and dorsiflexion.

## Correct option

**1. supination and abduction.**

## Answer explanation

The Ponseti method is widely used for the correction of clubfeet. Its advantages include simplicity, reliability, and excellent long-term results. When applying a cast according to the Ponseti method, the first cast is applied with the foot supinated. This dorsiflexes the first ray and helps correct the cavus deformity of the foot. Foot adduction is corrected by abducting the forefoot in supination and rotating the forefoot around the head of the talus. Long leg casts are used and are typically changed on a weekly basis. After about 6 weeks of casting, an Achilles tenotomy will be required in 90% of patients.
