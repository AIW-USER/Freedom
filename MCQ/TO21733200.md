Chapter: Spine MCQs — Scoliosis Kyphosis

## Question

For a 13 years female coming first time to you with DORSOLUMBAR scoliosis, what x ray would you take for evaluation of scoliosis

## Options

1. AP and Lateral whole spine (standing)
2. PA and Lateral whole spine (standing)
3. Scoliosis segment alone lateral view
4. AP and Lateral whole spine (supine)

## Correct option

**2. PA and Lateral whole spine (standing)**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

EXPLANATION: -

Standing films are better than supine.
In PA view, the radiation is less as compared to AP; but AP view gives better quality. PA may be preferred in girls to reduce risk of radiation to breasts .
