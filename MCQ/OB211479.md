## Question

Botulinum toxin has demonstrated which of the following outcomes in the application toward vasospastic disorders of the hand?

## Options

1. Healing of digital ulcers
2. Decreased pain
3. Decreased cold intolerance
4. Increased digital blood flow
5. All of the above

## Correct option

**5. All of the above**

## Answer explanation

Botulinum toxin (BTX) has been shown to facilitate healing of digital ulcers, reduction in pain, increased digital blood flow, and decreased cold intolerance.

Vasospastic disease of the hand is common and is reported among 3-5% of the US population. Up to 40-50% of these patients may develop painful digital ulcers. In addition to digital ulcers, patients with vasospastic disease often have cold intolerance, pain, stiffness, and skin color changes. Conservative therapy consists of lifestyle modifications, and medical therapy may include calcium channel blockers, phosphodiesterase inhibitors, and BTX. BTX acts to decrease neurovascular vasospasm and improve blood flow to the digits by inhibiting acetylcholine release from terminal neurons. Secondarily, BTX may reduce pain by inhibiting substance P and glutamate release by pain nociceptors. In addition, BTX inhibits the RhoA kinase pathway, which deactivates actin/myosin, thereby reducing vasoconstriction of smooth muscle.

*Fonseca et al. review neuronal regulators and vascular dysfunction in Raynaud's phenomenon.* **The authors noted** that this condition chiefly involves a vasospastic response to cold temperatures or emotional stress. **They highlight** the role of the sympathetic nerves in the regulation of vasoconstriction by way of adrenergic, REDOX, and RhoA signaling.

*Neumeister reviewed the utility of botulinum toxin in vasospastic disorders of the hand.* **The authors noted** that treatment begins with activity modifications and may often progress to medical management should symptoms persist. However recalcitrant cases may be treated with digital sympathectomies. They hold that botulinum toxin is an excellent surgical alternative, and often times a single treatment resolves pain, ulcers, and improves digital perfusion.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: BTX has been shown to improve healing of digital ulcers
Answer 2: BTX has been shown to improve pain
Answer 3: BTX has been shown to improve cold sensitivity
Answer 4: BTX has been shown to increase digital blood flow by Doppler examination
