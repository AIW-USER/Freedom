## Question

Most common muscle injury in athletes (sports medicine- miscellaneous)

## Options

1. Adductor longus
2. Gastrocnemius
3. Rectus femoris
4. Biceps femoris

## Correct option

**4. Biceps femoris**

## Answer explanation

Muscles which span across two joints are more subject to acceleration and deceleration forces. These muscles are prone to injury during sports and accidents. The muscles most commonly affected are the hamstrings, quadriceps and gastrocnemius. The three hamstring muscles are the semimembranosus, semitendinosus, and the biceps femoris. The biceps femoris is further divided into a long head and a short head, and is the most commonly injured muscle of the three.

Answer : d
