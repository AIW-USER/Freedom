## Options

1. Starting point A and two 2.5 mm nails
2. Starting point B and two 2.5 mm nails
3. Starting point A and two 4.0 mm nails
4. Starting point B and two 4.0 mm nails
5. Starting point B and one 2.5 mm nail and one 5.5 mm nail

## Correct option

**4. Starting point B and two 4.0 mm nails**

## Answer explanation

During flexible nailing of pediatric femoral shaft fractures the ideal starting point is approximately 2.5 cm proximal to the distal femoral physis and the ideal combined nail size is 80% of the femoral canal at its narrowest diameter.

Flexible intramedullary nailing of pediatric femoral shaft fractures is associated with a high rate of union and low rate of complications. Intramedullary nailing is a treatment option in patients 5 to 11 years of age with simple length stable fractures. Smaller nail diameters create weaker constructs. Larger nail diameters lead to stiffer constructs however present a higher risk of fracture malreduction, posterior gapping, and rotational malalignment. Therefore most surgeons use nails of equal diameter to mitigate risks of oversized nails. Avoiding transphyseal nailing is important to reduce the risk of angular deformity or growth arrest.

*Lascombes et al. review the variety of uses of flexible nails in treating a variety of pediatric fractures.* In general, nail sizes should be roughly 40% of the narrowest femoral diameter (80% combined). **They conclude** good results are achievable using flexible intramedullary nailing in pediatric patients when surgeons have an understanding of the principle of the correction of the fracture and its stability.

*Hosalkar et al. reviewed intramedullary nailing of femoral shaft fractures in pediatric patients.* **They state** multiple fixation methods are available and decision making is driven by fracture type, patient age, skeletal maturity, and body mass index.

Figure A shows a midshaft femoral shaft fracture and Figure B shows the same patient with possible starting points and the narrowest diameter of the femoral canal. Illustration A shows a postoperative image of flexible nailing of a femoral shaft fracture in a skeletally immature patient.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1, 2, 3, and 5: These combinations do not represent the ideal starting point or nail diameter for this patient.
