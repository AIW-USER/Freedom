## Question

A 42-year-old mechanic sustained an injury to his arm while lifting an engine block. At the time, he felt a pop in his arm that was accompanied by swelling and ecchymosis. Several weeks later he presents to your office with an MRI that was obtained by his primary care provider. A selected cut of the sagittal T2 sequence is depicted in figure A. If the patient opts to treat his injury nonsurgically, what activity will the patient have the most difficulty performing?

![[q217985-a-mri-distal-biceps-tear.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Pushing up from a chair
2. Bench press
3. Using a screw driver
4. Wrist extension
5. Elbow extensions

## Correct option

**3. Using a screw driver**

## Answer explanation

A distal biceps tendon rupture will compromise the forearm supination strength in the affected extremity. Forearm supination is performed when using a screwdriver.

Distal biceps tendon ruptures account for 10% of all biceps injuries. The distal insertion is on the bicipital groove of the radius which provides greater supination strength contributions than elbow flexion. Rupture of the distal biceps tendon will result in significant impairment in forearm supination strength. Surgical repair is recommended in higher-functioning individuals.

*Baratz et al. reviewed the repair of distal biceps tendon ruptures.* **They stated** patients treated nonoperatively have a 40% loss in supination strength and a 30% loss in flexion strength. **They concluded** that nonsurgical treatment of distal biceps tendon injuries can result in chronic weakness and functional limitations, especially in higher-functioning patients.

*Sutton et al. reviewed the surgical treatment of distal biceps tendon ruptures.* **They stated** that the biomechanical data supports the suspensory button has the highest peak load to failure of the studied repair techniques. **They highlighted** common complications with surgical treatment including lateral antebrachial cutaneous nerve neurapraxia, infection, and heterotopic ossification.

Figure A is a sagittal T2 MRI of the elbow demonstrating a distal biceps tendon tear with retraction of the tendon stump.

Incorrect answers
Answer 1: A lateral ulnar collateral ligament tear will affect elbow stability when pushing up from a chair.
Answer 2: A pectoralis major tear will affect bench press strength.
Answer 4: Extensor carpi radialis brevis and longus muscles function to perform wrist extension. With a rupture of the common tendon, wrist extension strength can be impaired.
Answer 5: A triceps tendon tear will affect elbow extension strength.
