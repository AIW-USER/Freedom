Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Periprosthetic Fractures

## Question

A 75 years female underwent a THR 10 years ago. Now she sustained a trauma following a fall. Her X ray shows a fracture line around the stem starting below the trochanter with a loose stem. What is the correct Vancouver classification and treatment? (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. B1, fixation with plate
2. B2, Long stem revision and fixation with plate
3. B3, Long stem revision and fixation with plate and graft
4. C, fixation with plate

## Correct option

**2. B2, Long stem revision and fixation with plate**

## Answer explanation

Vancouver classification of postoperative periprosthetic femur fractures
