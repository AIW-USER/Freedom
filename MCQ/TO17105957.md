## Question

. A 82/F had THR done 5 years ago. She fell and developed severe pain and inability to stand on the operated hip. X ray shows periprosthetic femur fracture. What is the best management?

![[TO17105957-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. ORIF with Cable Plate
2. Toe touch weight bearing
3. Revision with cemented long stem
4. Revision with uncemented long stem

## Correct option

**4. Revision with uncemented long stem**

## Answer explanation

![[TO17105957-2.png]]

High rates of non-union coupled with mid-term loosening caused cemented stems to fall out of favor in treatment of periprosthetic fractures. Fully porous coated implants became the gold standard following cemented stems. Currently most surgeons choose tapered, modular, fluted titanium stems in the setting of periprosthetic fracture specifically. This is type B 2 fracture and best treatment is revision with long uncemented stem and fixation of fracture with cable wires and plate.

Answer: 4
