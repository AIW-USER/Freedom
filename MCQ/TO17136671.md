## Question

What is the Caprini Index used for?

## Options

1. Risk assessment of amputation in crush injury
2. Risk assessment of DVT in trauma patient
3. Risk assessment of non-union in compound fractures
4. Risk assessment of failure of conservative trial in young patients of rotator cuff tear

## Correct option

**2. Risk assessment of DVT in trauma patient**

## Answer explanation

The Caprini Index is a valuable tool that can be used to help establish a composite,

individualized risk profile for a trauma patient.

This combines a series of clinical variables and outputs a single value to stratify patients into low, moderate, high, or highest risk of DVT. Notably, major lower extremity fractures place patients in the highest risk category by definition. Patients with scores of 3 of

higher (moderate risk) merit DVT/VTE prophylaxis.
