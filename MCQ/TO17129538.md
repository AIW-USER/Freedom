Chapter: Musculoskeletal Infections MCQs — Tuberculosis of Hip,Knee and Peripheral Joints

## Question

Spina Ventosa is (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Tubercular discitis
2. Tubercular dactylitis
3. Tubercular arthritis
4. Tubercular tendinitis

## Correct option

**2. Tubercular dactylitis**

## Answer explanation

“Spina Ventosa” is the name given to “tubercular dactylitis” that involves the phalanges of the hand. The affected phalanx shows destruction with a lot of new bone formation. The surrounding soft tissues may also markedly swell up and the patient presents with a painful spindle-shaped swelling of the affected finger.
