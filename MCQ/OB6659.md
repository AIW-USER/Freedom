## Question

A 20-year-old college pitcher reports medial elbow pain after 3 innings of hard throwing. He recalls no injury and reports no pain with light throwing. The examination shown in the clinical photograph in Figure 48 reproduces the elbow pain. What is the most likely diagnosis?

![[q6659-a-48.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Flexor-pronator avulsion
2. Ulnar nerve subluxation
3. Medial collateral ligament injury
4. Lateral ulnar collateral ligament rupture
5. Triceps tendon subluxation

## Correct option

**3. Medial collateral ligament injury**

## Answer explanation

The milking test, as seen in the photograph, elicits pain when a tear is present in the medial collateral ligament. Complete rupture is possible but unlikely when there is no history of trauma and the patient is able to throw pain-free for several innings. Subluxation of the ulnar nerve and triceps tendon subluxation present as a painful snapping over the medial aspect of the elbow.
