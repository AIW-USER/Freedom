Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Total Ankle Arthroplasty

## Question

Not a contraindication to the procedure shown in the X ray below is

![[TO17109968-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. AVN talus
2. Ankle instability
3. RA
4. A heavy industrial laborer

## Correct option

**3. RA**

## Answer explanation

Commonly cited contraindications to total ankle arthroplasty include age younger than 50 years, history of poor patient compliance, a heavy industrial laborer, a heavy smoker, uncontrolled diabetes with neuropathy, significant ankle instability, angular deformity of more than 10 to 15degrees, vascular insufficiency, obesity (over 250 lb.), significant bone loss, osteonecrosis, and active or previous infection.

Indication: The ideal candidate for ankle arthroplasty has been described as an older, thin, low- demand individual with minimal deformity, retained ankle range of motion and with degenerative/inflammatory or post traumatic ankle arthritis.

Answer: 3
