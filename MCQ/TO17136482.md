## Question

Best way to assess iron status in a female athlete would be

## Options

1. Ferritin
2. Transferrin saturation
3. Ferritin/ Transferrin ratio
4. Serum Hepcidin levels

## Correct option

**3. Ferritin/ Transferrin ratio**

## Answer explanation

Ferritin is considered a marker of iron status.
It is a protein that stores iron in the body and is commonly used to assess iron levels. In non-athletes, it is considered the best marker for evaluating iron stores because its levels directly correlate with iron availability.
However, ferritin also acts as an acute-phase reactant, meaning its levels rise in response to inflammation, stress, or infection. In athletes, intense physical activity and training can cause systemic inflammation, leading to elevated ferritin levels—even when iron stores are normal or low. This makes ferritin a less reliable indicator in athletes compared to non-athletes.
Female athletes experience higher rates of iron depletion due to factors such as menstrual blood loss, exercise-induced hemolysis (red blood cell breakdown), and sweat loss, which further complicates the interpretation of ferritin levels.
Therefore, the Ferritin/Transferrin ratio is the preferred marker for assessing iron status in female athletes.

Answer: 3
