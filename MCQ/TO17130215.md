Chapter: Metabolic Bone Disorder MCQs — Osteomalacia

## Question

Biochemical profile of tumor induced Osteomalacia is characterized by

## Options

1. Low serum phosphate, normal calcium, low Vit. D, high FGF23
2. Low serum phosphate, high calcium, low Vit. D, high FGF23
3. High serum phosphate, high calcium, low Vit. D, low FGF23
4. Low serum phosphate, normal calcium, normal to low Vit. D, low FGF23

## Correct option

**1. Low serum phosphate, normal calcium, low Vit. D, high FGF23**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Tumor-induced Osteomalacia (TIO), also known oncogenic Osteomalacia, is a rare paraneoplastic syndrome of abnormal phosphate and vitamin D metabolism caused by typically small endocrine tumors that secrete the phosphaturic hormone, fibroblast growth factor 23 (FGF23). Many bone tumors also secrete FGF23 and may produce TIO. These include

- Nonossifying fibroma
- Osteoblastoma
- Giant cell tumor
- Fibrous dysplasia

Biochemical hallmarks of the disorder are hypophosphatemia due to renal phosphate wasting, inappropriately normal or low 1,25-dihydroxy vitamin D, and elevated or inappropriately normal plasma FGF23.

FGF23 acts by binding to target cells via an FGF receptor (probably FGFR1), but signaling requires the co- receptor Klotho. Activation of FGFR leads to renal phosphate excretion. Phosphaturic action of FGF23 is to some extent PTH-dependent. Subjects with hypoparathyroidism, who have very low or undetectable PTH levels, have high serum phosphorus in the setting of high serum FGF23, which is consistent with the need for PTH for the full phosphaturic effect of FGF23.

Clinically patients present with bone pain, muscle weakness, and fractures.

Metabolic profile include-Low serum phosphate, low Tubular reabsorption of phosphate, normal to low serum Vit D3 and very high FGF23.

When suspected diagnosis should involve tumor localization: Functional imaging: FDG-PET/CT+ Octreoscan /CT Anatomical imaging: MRI/CT

Treatment: tumor excision and medical treatment with phosphate and Vit. D supplementation.

Answer: 1
