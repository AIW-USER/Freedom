Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Wear And Osteolysis (Hip)

## Question

Which of the following is not a determinant of wear of implant?

## Options

1. Wear factor
2. Sliding distance
3. Load applied across bearing surface
4. All are independent determinants

## Correct option

**4. All are independent determinants**

## Answer explanation

Explanation:

All of the above are independent determinants of implant wear.

Answer: 4
