## Question

Laboratory examination of a 5-year-old boy who toe-walks and recently began tripping and falling revealed a creatine phosphokinase level 10 times higher than the normal level. He has one 11-year old brother who walks normally. To confirm the diagnosis of Duchenne muscular dystrophy, a creatine phosphokinase test should be ordered on which family member?

## Options

1. Father
2. Mother
3. Brother
4. Sister

## Correct option

**2. Mother**

## Answer explanation

Creatine phosphokinase levels are elevated in this patient and the mother is the carrier. The father's test result is always negative because this is a sex-linked disease. The brother's test result will be normal because he does not have the disease.
