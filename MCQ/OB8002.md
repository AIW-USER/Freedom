## Question

What is the prevalence of the radiographic finding shown in Figure 89 in the adult population in North America?

![[q8002-a-89.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Less than 2%
2. 4% to 6%
3. 8% to 10%
4. 12% to 14%
5. Greater than 15%

## Correct option

**2. 4% to 6%**

## Answer explanation

The pars interarticularis is a thin bicortical region of the posterior arch of the lumbar vertebra, where the lamina and inferior articular process intersect with the pedicle and superior articular process. A fibrocartilaginous cleft in this region is referred to as a spondylolysis or a pars defect. This finding is present in 4% to 6% of the North American adult population. *Buetler and associates conducted a prospective study of 500 children and followed them through young adulthood.* **They found** the prevalence of spondylolysis to be 4.4% in children aged 6 years. An additional eight subjects developed spondylolysis between the ages of 12 and 25 years for an adult prevalence of 6%. Ninety percent of the pars defects occurred at the L5-S1 level, with two-thirds of the defects occurring in males.
