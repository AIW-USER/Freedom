## Question

Carpal tunnel syndrome is not common in( peripheral nerve injuries- compression neuropathies- carpal tunnel syndrome)

## Options

1. Pregnancy
2. Hypothyroidism
3. Hyperparathyroidism
4. Addison’s disease

## Correct option

**4. Addison’s disease**

## Answer explanation

Carpal tunnel syndrome is common in middle-aged females and is associated with rheumatoid arthritis, hypothyroidism (second most common cause), pregnancy, acromegaly, diabetes, amyloidosis and hyperparathyroidism. However, the most common cause is idiopathic.
Ans: D
