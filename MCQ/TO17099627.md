## Question

Which approach was used in original Charnley technique of total hip replacement

## Options

1. Posterior
2. Anterolateral
3. Anterior
4. Lateral

## Correct option

**2. Anterolateral**

## Answer explanation

The original Charnley technique used the anterolateral surgical approach with the patient in supine position, osteotomy of the greater trochanter, and anterior dislocation of the hip.

Answer: 2
