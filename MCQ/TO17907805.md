## Question

Neural plate formation is seen by

## Options

1. 18 days
2. 30 days
3. 4 weeks
4. 3 months

## Correct option

**1. 18 days**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

The neural plate is a thickened portion of ectoderm along the midline of the embryo. The neural plate development begins at approximately day 18/19 after fertilization.

See the flowchart below to know the Embryological sequence of Neural Development: -

Sperm + Egg fuse to form Zygote.

Zygote develops into Morula (16 celled stage) that further develops into Blastula (128 cells).

Blastula develops a cavity inside to become Blastocyst (has outer Trophoblast and an Inner Cell Mass).

Blastocyst will develop into Gastrula around 3 weeks of gestation. Gastrula is the stage where the three layers (viz. Endoderm, Mesoderm and Ectoderm are separated and identifiable).

FATE OF ECTODERM:

Ectoderm forms Notochord (which remains surrounded within ectoderm) and Para-axial Mesoderm.

NOTOCHORD: Goes on to form Nucleus pulposus of IV disc and a structure called NEURAL PLATE. Neural plate divides to form a Neural tube (that develops into Brain and Spinal cord) and Neural crest cells (that are precursors of Neurons and Glial cells).

PARA AXIAL MESODERM: This goes on to form SOMITES (42 pairs, arranged cranio-caudally) that divide into DERMATOME/ MYOTOME (Dermatome forms skin dermis and Myotomes develop into muscles) and SCLEROTOME (which develop into Vertebral body and Annulus fibrosus).
