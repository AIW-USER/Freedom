## Question

A young male has come with Neck pain and weakness of both upper and lower limbs after sustaining trauma. X ray shows fracture dislocation at C4-C5. However, the patient is stable. The most appropriate next line of management would be

![[TO21733250-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Reduction with skull traction
2. MRI
3. Hard collar and Observe
4. Immediate surgery

## Correct option

**2. MRI**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

EXPLANATION: -

![[TO21733250-2.png]]

In any spinal cord injury patient with neurological deficit: -

• MRI is mandatory to find out the condition of spinal cord/ extent of injury / amount of compression/ presence of disc prolapse.

• Patient can be shifted to MRI once the pt is hemodynamically stable.

• Following MRI patient should be taken up for surgery as early as possible if there is compression & instability.

• Role of traction:

When the MRI is not available, without wasting time we can apply traction to reduce.

When the surgery is delayed because of pts condition we can apply traction

If the pt presents late – Per op reduction will be difficult and per op traction helps in reduction.
