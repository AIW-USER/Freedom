Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Congenital Pseudoarthrosis Of Clavicle

## Question

Congenital pseudarthrosis of clavicle occurs most commonly on which side

## Options

1. Bilateral
2. Right
3. Left
4. On the side of cervical rib

## Correct option

**2. Right**

## Answer explanation

Answer: Ninety percent of cases are noted on the right side. Ten percent of cases are bilateral and have been associated with bilateral cervical ribs. Only a few cases of left-sided pseudarthrosis have been described and have been associated with dextrocardia.

ANSWER-B
