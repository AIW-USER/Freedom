## Question

The sural artery provides the vascular supply to which musculocutaneous flap?

## Options

1. soleus
2. gastrocnemius
3. latissimus dorsi
4. tibialis anterior
5. peroneus longus

## Correct option

**2. gastrocnemius**

## Answer explanation

The sural artery supplies the both heads of the gastrocnemius and is the pedicle for rotational flaps. Eighty-five percent of the time there is a single vascular source.
