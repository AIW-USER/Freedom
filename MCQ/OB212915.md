## Question

Which of the following statements regarding acute hematogenous osteomyelitis in pediatric patients is true?

## Options

1. Group A Streptococcus is the most common infecting organism
2. A history of trauma is reported in 20-40% of patients
3. It typically occurs in the epiphysis via hematogenous seeding
4. CT scan is the advanced imaging study of choice
5. Blood cultures are positive in 75-80% of patients

## Correct option

**2. A history of trauma is reported in 20-40% of patients**

## Answer explanation

A history of trauma may be present in 20-40% of pediatric patients with osteomyelitis.

Bone and joint infections in children may be difficult to diagnose. The surgeon must look for physical findings such as skin changes and fluctuance for abscess formation. Osteomyelitis in pediatric patients typically affects the metaphysis via hematogenous seeding, with Staphylococcus aureus being the most common infecting organism. A history of trauma may be present in 20-40% of patients, while blood cultures may be positive in only 30-50% of patients.

*Morrissy et al. published a study using rabbits to model trauma as an etiology for acute hematogenous osteomyelitis.* *They divided the rabbits into three different groups: Group I received an injury to the proximal tibial physeal plate; Group II received standardized bacteremia with Staphylococcus aureus; and Group III received both the fracture and the bacteremia.* **They found** that the animals with fracture and bacteremia developed significant osteomyelitis in almost all cases.

*Pendleton et al. published a review on methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus bone and joint infections in children.* **They report** that early use of MRI helps delineate the extent of the infection, aids in the consideration of surgery, and provides valuable information for surgical planning. **They also report** that trauma increases the chance of development of osteomyelitis when there is concurrent bacteremia.

*Dich et al. published a study on osteomyelitis in infants and children.* **They found** that a history of trauma was reported in 35% of patients. **They also found** that boys were twice as often affected than girls, and that Staphylococcus aureus was the major etiologic agent, accounting for 61% of infections.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Staphylococcus aureus, not Group A Streptococcus, is the most common infecting organism.
Answer 3: Osteomyelitis in pediatric patients typically affects the metaphysis, not the epiphysis, via hematogenous seeding.
Answer 4: CT scans are not the advanced imaging study of choice in acute hematogenouso steomyelitis.
Answer 5: Blood cultures are positive in only 30-50% of patients, not 75-80%.
