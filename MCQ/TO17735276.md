## Question

Lipohemarthrosis is most commonly associated with (trauma- fractures/dislocations around knee joint-Lipohemarthrosis )

## Options

1. Occult fracture of patella
2. Missed knee ligament injury
3. Recurrent patellar instability secondary to traumatic MPFL tear
4. Tibial plateau fracture

## Correct option

**4. Tibial plateau fracture**

## Answer explanation

Lipohaemarthrosis results from an intra-articular fracture with escape of fat and blood from the bone marrow into the joint. It is most commonly seen in fractures around the knee joint, like tibial plateau fracture and distal femoral fractures. CT and MRI have high sensitivity and diagnostic accuracy for lipohaemarthrosis, showing three layers of lipohaemarthrosis (fat above, serum/synovial fluid middle, red blood cells below) along with intra articular fractures.

Answer : D
