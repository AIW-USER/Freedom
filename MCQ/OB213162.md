## Question

A 42-year-old female presents with a history of polyarthropathy and a diagnosis of rheumatoid arthritis. After discussing pharmacologic treatment options, the plan is to begin a disease-modifying antirheumatic drug (DMARD) that works by binding to CD80 and CD86 inhibiting T-cell activation. What drug has this mechanism of action?

## Options

1. Abatacept
2. Methotrexate
3. Rituximab
4. Adalimumab
5. Anakinra

## Correct option

**1. Abatacept**

## Answer explanation

Abatacept is a disease-modifying antirheumatic drug (DMARD) that works by inhibiting T cell (T lymphocyte) activation by binding to CD80 and CD86. It is primarily used in moderate-to-severe rheumatoid arthritis and polyarticular juvenile idiopathic arthritis.

Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) is a chronic autoimmune disease that presents with a multitude of orthopedic manifestations. It is the most common inflammatory arthritis with a predilection for females. The pathophysiology of RA involves chronic inflammation of the synovial membrane, which can destroy articular cartilage and juxta-articular bone. Pharmacologic treatment is the mainstay of treatment to prevent the chronic disabling joint disease associated with untreated RA. Occasionally, surgery in the form of arthroplasty, fusion, or synovectomy may be indicated when the disease is advanced.

*Benjamin et al. outline the common disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs used in the treatment of rheumatoid arthritis and other inflammatory arthritides.* Specifically, they describe the mechanism of action, adverse event profile, pharmacology, monitoring, and relevant interactions of DMARDs.

*Blair et al. review abatacept and its indications for patients with rheumatoid arthritis.* **They conclude** that the current evidence suggests that abatacept is a useful treatment option for patients with rheumatoid arthritis, particularly in patients with highly active and progressive disease.

Incorrect Answers
Answer 2: Methotrexate is a folate antagonist, which inhibits dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR), the enzyme that reduces folic acid to tetrahydrofolic acid.
Answer 3: Rituximab is a monoclonal antibody that targets the CD20 surface antigen present on B cells.
Answer 4: Adalimumab is a monoclonal antibody that specifically binds to tumor necrosis factor (TNF)-alpha.
Answer 5: Anakinra blocks the biologic activity of IL-1 by competitively inhibiting IL-1 binding to the interleukin-1 type I receptor (IL-1RI), blocking proinflammatory signaling.
