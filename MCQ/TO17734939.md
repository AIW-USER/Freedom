## Question

Which type of JRA has the highest risk of iritis and blindness? (arthritis- Juvenile idiopathic arthritis)

## Options

1. Systemic JRA
2. Pauciarticular JRA
3. Polyarticular JRA
4. Spondyloarthropathy

## Correct option

**2. Pauciarticular JRA**

## Answer explanation

Patients with pauciarticular juvenile rheumatoid arthritis (JRA) have the highest risk of developing iritis, cataracts, and blindness. Polyarticular JRA has the second highest risk. Systemic-onset JRA has the lowest risk of uveitis.

Answer: 2
