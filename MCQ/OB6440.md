## Question

An 11-month-old boy is seen in the emergency department at 3:00 AM with swelling and limited movement of the left elbow. The infant's stepfather reports that the injury occurred after a fall off the bed at home. Further examination reveals some mild swelling of the right ankle. ## Options

1. Initiate a metabolic work-up.
2. Discuss home safety measures with the stepfather to prevent further injury.
3. Splint the left elbow and right ankle and see the patient in your office the next afternoon.
4. Restrain the stepfather and notify the police.
5. Admit the patient to the hospital.

## Correct option

**5. Admit the patient to the hospital.**

## Answer explanation

The infant has two fractures with a history of trauma that may not be adequate to explain the injuries. These factors suggest a strong possibility of child abuse. Also, the bucket-handle fracture of the distal tibial metaphysis is highly specific for abuse. In a corner or bucket-handle fracture, there is no gross displacement of the epiphysis, but a chip is seen at the edge of the involved metaphysis. Once child abuse is suspected, hospital admission allows the patient to be placed in a safe, protected environment, allows further diagnostic evaluation to proceed, and facilitates evaluation of the family's social situation by qualified personnel. Early recognition of abuse by the orthopaedist is crucial because children who are returned to their homes following an unrecognized episode of abuse have a significant risk of reinjury or death.
