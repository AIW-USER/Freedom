## Question

An 18-year-old girl sustains a closed tibial fracture when she is pinned between the bumpers of two cars. You suspect that she has compartment syndrome and you prepare her for fasciotomies in the operating room. Which of the following muscles is not released by a standard medial-sided incision?

## Options

1. Tibialis posterior
2. Extensor hallucis longus
3. Soleus
4. Flexor digitorum longus
5. Popliteus

## Correct option

**2. Extensor hallucis longus**

## Answer explanation

The tibialis posterior, flexor digitorum longus, and popliteus are all located in the deep posterior compartment. The soleus is in the superficial posterior compartment. All are easily released through a medial surgical approach. The extensor hallucis longus is located in the anterior compartment and therefore cannot be released through a medial-sided incision.
