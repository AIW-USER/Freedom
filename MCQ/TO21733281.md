Chapter: Spine MCQs — Thoracolumbar Fracture-Dislocation

## Question

Perched facets is seen in

## Options

1. Vertical compression
2. Compressive flexion
3. Distractive flexion
4. Extension and distraction

## Correct option

**3. Distractive flexion**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 3
Perched facets are seen in stage 3/4 of distractive flexion injury of cervical spine.
Distractive flexion injuries may range from facet sprains and perched facets to unifacet or bifacet dislocations
● Stage 1: Failure of the posterior ligamentous complex.
● Stage 2: Unilateral facet dislocation.
● Stage 3: Bilateral facet dislocations.
● Stage 4: Full anterior displacement of the vertebral body, resulting in a floating vertebra.
Facet sprains (distractive-flexion [DF] Stage 1) involve posterior ligament and facet capsule disruption. These patients can be treated with rigid external immobilization for 8–12 weeks, with serial radiographs to look for sagittal malalignment.
Unifacet (DF Stage 2)/bifacet dislocations (DF Stage 3 and 4) are more severe injuries involving a tear of the supraspinous and interspinous ligaments, the ligamentum flavum and the facet capsule. The PLL may be disrupted or stripped off the posterior surface of the vertebral body. The presence of a facet fracture reduces the probability of a successful closed reduction. Occasionally, the inferior lip of the inferior facet of the cranial vertebra locks onto the tip of the superior lip of the superior facet of the caudal vertebra resulting in significant kyphosis and distraction. This condition is known as perched facets.
