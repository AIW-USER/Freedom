## Question

Minimum flexion required to get up from the chair after TKR? (arthroplasty- knee arthroplasty- coronal plane balancing)

## Options

1. 95
2. 70
3. 50
4. 30

## Correct option

**1. 95**

## Answer explanation

Many studies have shown that 90-degrees of motion is needed to go up and down stairs. Getting up from a chair requires just slightly more motion (95-100 degrees). And lifting an object requires at least 120-degrees of flexion. Patients requiring more squatting, kneeling, and sitting cross-legged need between 110 and 165 degrees of knee flexion to use these positions.

Answer: 1
