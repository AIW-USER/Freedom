Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Clinical Examination (Shoulder)

## Question

Correct statement for the test shown in below diagram is (FNB Pattern Paper 2014)

![[TO17140078-1.png]]

## Options

1. Jones test for supraspinatus weakness
2. O brian’s test for SLAP tear
3. Hornblower’s test for infraspinatus and teres minor
4. Internal rotation lag sign for subscapularis

## Correct option

**3. Hornblower’s test for infraspinatus and teres minor**

## Answer explanation

Hornblower’s sign

Examiner will put the patient's arm in 90 degree abduction in the scapular plane. The elbow is flexed at 90 degrees. The patient will try to externally rotate the forearm against the resistance of the examiner. Test is positive if the patient can’t externally rotate his arm and assume a position of a positive hornblower’s sign. This sign predicts more accurately the integrity of teres minor.

Answer: 3
