## Question

At the level of tibial bone resection in total knee arthroplasty, where does the common peroneal nerve lie?

## Options

1. Deep to the arcuate ligament
2. Closer to bone in larger legs
3. On the muscle belly of the popliteus
4. On the bony posterolateral corner of the tibia
5. Superficial to the lateral head of the gastrocnemius

## Correct option

**5. Superficial to the lateral head of the gastrocnemius**

## Answer explanation

At the level of tibial bone resection in total knee arthroplasty, the common peroneal nerve lies superficial to the lateral head of the gastrocnemius and is therefore protected by this structure. In an MRI study of 60 knees, the mean distance from the bony posterolateral corner of the tibia to the nerve was 1.49 cm, with no distance less than 0.9 cm. The distance from the bone to nerve was greater in larger legs.
