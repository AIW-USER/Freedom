## Question

Most common type of spinal dysraphism is

## Options

1. Meningocele
2. Myelomeningocele
3. Spina bifida occulta
4. Open myelomeningocele

## Correct option

**2. Myelomeningocele**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Types of spinal dysraphism include myelomeningocele (also known as spina bifida Aperta or open spina bifida), spina bifida occulta, split cord malformation (diastematomyelia), spinal cord lipoma (lipomyelomeningocele), dermal sinus tract, tight filum terminale, and tethered spinal cord. Myelomeningocele is the most common type.

Answer: 2
