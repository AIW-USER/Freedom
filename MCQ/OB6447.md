## Question

The lateral Coleman block test for the evaluation of the cavus foot is used to diagnose

## Options

1. lateral ankle instability.
2. medial ankle instability.
3. hindfoot deformity flexibility.
4. forefoot deformity flexibility.
5. gastrocsoleus contracture.

## Correct option

**3. hindfoot deformity flexibility.**

## Answer explanation

The lateral Coleman block test is performed by placing a 1-1.5 inch block under the lateral border of the forefoot. This block helps support the lateral border of the foot and corrects a flexible heel varus deformity to neutral if the heel varus is compensatory for a plantar flexed first ray. If the heel deformity does not correct to neutral, it has at least some fixed deformity and therefore will most likely require a calcaneal osteotomy.
