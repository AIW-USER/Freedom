## Question

A complication unique to computer navigation of total knee arthroplasty (TKR) is [Arthroplasty-Knee Arthroplasty-Computer Navigation,Robotics & Latest Trends]

## Options

1. Femoral shaft fracture.
2. Intercondylar femur fracture.
3. Ligament disruption.
4. Nerve palsy.

## Correct option

**1. Femoral shaft fracture.**

## Answer explanation

Threaded pins are frequently inserted into the femoral shaft and tibial shafts or proximal tibia to attach arrays for tracking devices. There have been case reports of fractures propagating through the pin tracks, which is a complication unique to computer navigation. Intercondylar fractures can occur following posterior stabilized TKR. Vascular injury, ligament disruption, and nerve palsy are rare complications following TKR performed with or without computer navigation.

Answer: 1
