## Question

Which is not an advantage of BPTB graft over hamstring graft

## Options

1. Low revision rate
2. No loss of range of motion
3. No flexor torque deficiency
4. Low level of post operative laxity

## Correct option

**2. No loss of range of motion**

## Answer explanation
