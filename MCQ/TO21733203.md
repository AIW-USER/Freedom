Chapter: Nerve Injury MCQs — Ulnar Nerve Palsy

## Question

Which intrinsic muscle is supplied by the superficial branch of the ulnar nerve?

## Options

1. adductor policis
2. palmeris brevis
3. flexor digiti minimi brevis
4. abductor digiti minimi,

## Correct option

**2. palmeris brevis**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

EXPLANATION: -

The majority of the intrinsic hand muscles are innervated by the deep branch of the ulnar nerve:
• Hypothenar muscles (flexor digiti minimi brevis, abductor digiti minimi, opponens digiti minimi)
• Medial two lumbricals
• Adductor pollicis
• Palmar and dorsal interossei of the hand
• Medial head of the flexor pollicis brevis.

The palmaris brevis is an exception to this rule and is innervated by the superficial branch of the ulnar nerve.
