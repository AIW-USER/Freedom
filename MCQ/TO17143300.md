Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Fibrous Dysplasia

## Question

All of the following are examples of “Touch Me Not “Lesions except

## Options

1. Myositis Ossificans
2. Fibrous Dysplasia
3. Enchondroma
4. Osteoblastoma

## Correct option

**4. Osteoblastoma**

## Answer explanation

Touch me not lesions are those lesions which are usually picked up incidentally and have characteristic radiological features to diagnose the lesion on X-rays and which do not usually require any further intervention (not even Biopsy).

Examples include Myositis Ossificans, Cortical desmoid, Enchondroma, Osteochondroma, Fibrous dysplasia, non-ossifying fibroma, Paget’s disease, Fibrous cortical defect, intra osseous lipoma, Bone islands and Bone infarcts.

On the other hand, Osteoblastoma is painful and requires intervention.
