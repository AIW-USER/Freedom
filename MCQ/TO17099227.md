Chapter: Arthritis MCQs — Inflammatory and Miscellaneous Arthritis

## Question

True about HIV arthropathy is all except

## Options

1. Mostly nonerosive
2. Symmetrical polyarthritis is the most common form
3. New bone formation
4. Mucocutaneous involvement is rare

## Correct option

**2. Symmetrical polyarthritis is the most common form**

## Answer explanation

HIV-associated arthritis can occur at any stage of HIV illness. It presents as an asymmetric oligoarthritis, symmetrical polyarthritis or as a monoarthritis. The asymmetric, oligoarthritis variant is the most common form, has a male preponderance, and predominantly affects the knees and ankles . The symmetrical polyarthritis variant closely mimics RA, with patients exhibiting similar deformities to rheumatoid patients, including ulnar deviation. It is characterized however by greater acuity at onset and is usually nonerosive. The presence of Jaccoud arthropathy as part of an HIV-associated arthritis has also been described occasionally. HIV-associated arthritis tends to be short lived with its peak intensity occurring in 1 to 6 weeks . However, some patients develop a chronic destructive arthropathy, associated with marked functional disability. Features of mucocutaneous involvement or enthesopathy are rare. Radiological changes can occasionally mimic RA, with joint space narrowing, erosions and periarticular osteopenia. However, some patients demonstrate new bone formation - a radiological finding unusual in RA.

Answer: 2
