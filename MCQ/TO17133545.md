## Question

The major risk factor for nonunion in a type 2 odontoid fracture is (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Age >35 years old
2. Posterior displacement >5 mm
3. Anterior displacement >5 mm
4. Smoking history

## Correct option

**2. Posterior displacement >5 mm**

## Answer explanation

A posterior displacement >5 mm has the greatest risk of nonunion. Type 2 odontoid fractures have the highest rate of nonunion of the 3 types and type 2 should be treated with halo immobilization. Nonunion rates also increase with age more than 65years.

Answer: 2
