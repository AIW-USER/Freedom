## Question

Allis test is not done for (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. DDH
2. Perthes disease
3. SCFE
4. Polio affection of lower limb

## Correct option

**4. Polio affection of lower limb**

## Answer explanation

Galleazzi/Allis Test: This test demonstrates whether the shortening is in the femur or tibia. Patient is positioned supine with both hips flexed to 45 degree, both knees flexed to 90 degrees and malleoli touching together. In a normal person both knees lie at the same level.

Normally both knees lie at the same level. When the normal knee projects farther forwards than the affected, the affected femur is shorter and when the affected knee is lower than the other knee, the tibia is shorter.
