## Question

A 9-year-old boy falls from a scooter and sustains the injury shown in the radiographs in Figure 26. After closed reduction and cast immobilization, what is the most likely complication that can result?

![[q6930-a-26.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Growth arrest of the distal ulna
2. Growth arrest of the distal radius
3. Compartment syndrome
4. Radioulnar synostosis
5. Entrapment of the extensor pollicis longus (EPL) tendon

## Correct option

**1. Growth arrest of the distal ulna**

## Answer explanation

The radiographs show a fracture of the distal radius and ulna physis. The most likely complication is growth arrest of the distal ulna. In contradistinction to physis fractures of the radius (growth arrest incidence of less than 5%), the incidence of growth arrest in the ulna is between 30% and 40%. Entrapment of the EPL tendon and cross union between the two bones is extremely rare.
