## Question

Figure A is the radiograph of a 12-year-old male baseball pitcher that presents with a 6-month history of worsening right shoulder pain. On examination, he has point tenderness over the proximal humeral physis but has full range of motion of the shoulder with no neurologic deficits. What portion of the physis is affected in this condition as depicted in Figure B?

## Correct option

**3. C**

## Answer explanation

This patient has little Leaguer's shoulder. Injury occurs at the hypertrophic zone of the physis which is the weakest the most susceptible to repetitive stress

Little Leaguer's shoulder is an overuse injury occuring in young baseball pitchers resulting in epiphysiolysis of the proximal humerus (a Salter Harris Type 1 injury). Diagnosis is made with radiographs of the shoulder showing a widened proximal humerus physis in comparison to contralateral shoulder. Treatment is usually cessation of throwing, followed by physical therapy and progressive throwing program after sufficient rest.

*Carson et al. reviewed 23 cases of Little Leaguer's shoulder* **They reported** that all patients demonstrated radiographic widening of the proximal humeral physis of the throwing arm on internal and external rotation comparison anteroposterior radiographs of the shoulder. **They concluded** that rest from throwing for at least 3 months is recommended, followed by a gradual return to throwing in an asymptomatic shoulder.

*Fleming et al. reviewed a case of Little Leaguer's shoulder in a skeletally immature patient.* **They reported** that this entity manifests as widening of the proximal humeral physis. **They concluded** that there is little in the current radiologic literature describing Little Leaguer's shoulder.

*Chen et al. reviewed sports-related shoulder injury in children and adolescents participating in recreational and organized sports.* **They reported** that understanding the relevant functional anatomy, biomechanics of throwing, and pathophysiology of injury can help the clinician manage common acute traumatic injuries, some of which may result in chronic problems. Over-use injuries occur more frequently than do acute, traumatic injuries, and early recognition, coupled with appropriate treatment or prevention, can help restore and maintain normal shoulder and elbow function.

Figure A is the AP radiograph of the right shoulder demonstrating widening of the proximal humeral physis consistent with Little Leaguer's shoulder. Figure B is the unlabeled illustration of the physis. Illustration A is the labeled illustration of the physeal zones.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: This illustrates the metaphysis, which is not a physeal zone
Answer 2: This illustrates the provisional zone of calcification of the physis
Answer 4: This illustrates the proliferative zone of the physis
Answer 5: This illustrates the reserve zone of the physis
