Chapter: Congenital & Developmental Musculoskeletal Disorders MCQs — Muscular Dystrophies

## Question

Chromosome implicated in development of Myotonic dystrophy is

## Options

1. 19
2. 20
3. X
4. 16

## Correct option

**1. 19**

## Answer explanation

Chromosomes involved in different muscular dystrophies:

Myotonic Dystrophy
Mutation on chromosome 19.

DMD
Mutations of the DMD gene on the X chromosome

Becker muscular dystrophy
X-linked recessive inherited disorder

Facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy
Long (q) arm of chromosome 4.

Limb-girdle muscular dystrophy
Chromosome 2p.

Answer: 1
