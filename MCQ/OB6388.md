## Question

The spring ligament complex of the foot connects the

## Options

1. calcaneus and cuboid.
2. calcaneus and navicular.
3. calcaneus and talus.
4. talus and navicular.
5. medial cuneiform and first metatarsal.

## Correct option

**2. calcaneus and navicular.**

## Answer explanation

The spring ligament complex consists of strong plantar ligaments that connect the calcaneus and navicular. These ligaments help support the medial longitudinal arch and head of the talus. Injuries to this ligament complex are commonly associated with posterior tibial tendon dysfunction. If this ligament complex is significantly disrupted, reconstruction is required.
