Chapter: Spine MCQs — Complications (Fractures of Cervical Spine)

## Question

All are true regarding halo vest immobilization except?

## Options

1. Most rigid form of cervical spine external immobilization
2. Contraindicated in patient with cranial fractures
3. Oculomotor is most commonly injured cranial nerve
4. Ideal for upper cervical injury patient

## Correct option

**3. Oculomotor is most commonly injured cranial nerve**

## Answer explanation

Abducens nerve is most common to get involved in halo vest immobilization

Answer: 3
