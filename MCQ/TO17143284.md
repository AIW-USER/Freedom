Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Enchondroma And Multiple Enchondroma

## Question

Which of the following is false regarding chondroid lesions

## Options

1. Grade 1 chondrosarcoma of appendicular skeleton can be treated with extended curettage
2. Mesenchymal variant of Chondrosarcoma requires chemotherapy as well in addition to surgery
3. Ollier’s disease (Multiple Enchondromatosis) is transmitted as Autosomal Dominant Gene
4. Chondrosarcoma arising from enchondroma is usually of low grade

## Correct option

**3. Ollier’s disease (Multiple Enchondromatosis) is transmitted as Autosomal Dominant Gene**

## Answer explanation

Low grade chondrosarcomas in the appendicular skeleton can be treated with extended curettage, however low grade chondrosarcoma in pelvis and axial skeleton should be treated with resection only.

Mesenchymal variant of chondrosarcoma is a highly aggressive variant requiring chemotherapy similar to Ewing Sarcoma protocol.

Ollier’s disease (Multiple Enchondromas) does not have hereditary transmission. It is Multiple Osteochondromatosis (Hereditary Multiple Exostosis) that has Autosomal Dominant inheritance.

Secondary transformation from enchondroma or osteochondroma is of low grade.

Answer C
