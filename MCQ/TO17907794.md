## Question

After RTA a patient was found lying unconsciousness in lateral position on road (see pic). There is a bruise on face, elbow and lateral side of knee. Which nerve has maximum chances of injury in this position?

![[TO17907794-1.png]]

## Options

1. Tibial nerve
2. Trigeminal
3. Ulnar
4. CPN

## Correct option

**4. CPN**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D

Out of mentioned nerves, CPN is the most fixed nerve with minimum tolerance to mobility. It is fixed at its origin under the biceps femoris and then over a short course, fixes to neck of fibula (as it winds around, see pic below). Hence, pressure over the nerve in the shown position is well likely to injure it.
