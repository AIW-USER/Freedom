## Question

Precautions while reaming the acetabulum in protrusio acetabuli case? (arthroplasty- Hip arthroplasty- protrusio acetabuli)

## Options

1. One size large reamer is used midway of reaming
2. Use one size small reamer throughout
3. Only peripheral reaming
4. Horizontal reaming

## Correct option

**3. Only peripheral reaming**

## Answer explanation

Acetabular Protrusio may be occasionally seen in arthritic hips secondary to rheumatoid arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, previous trauma, osteomalacia and Paget’s disease.

The presence and degree of protrusio is determined by drawing Kohler’s line on an anteroposterior (AP) radiograph of pelvis showing both hips.

Technical tips of acetabular reaming in case of acetabular protrusion:

After the neck osteotomy, The acetabular preparation is performed in two stages : that of the periphery and then that of the medial floor. Unlike the common case in standard reaming, reaming is not started with the smallest reamers with the aim of preparing the floor first. Instead, the peripheral cartilage is reamed down at first, albeit preserving the subchondral bone using larger reamers, to one or two sizes smaller than the final cup size template. The aim is to achieve firm contact between the peripheries of the cup and as wide as a band of the acetabular rim as possible. The acetabular rim should be remained shallow circumferentially only inside the rim, with the decided cup abduction angle to obtain a perfect shape of the rim.

Second, in preparation of the medial floor, the synovial tissue and soft tissue in geodes in the acetabulum are gently resected using curette and then only exposed sclerotic subchondral bone is drilled and multiple holes are made using Kirschner wire. The cup should be in contact with at least 50% of good quality acetabular bone for adequate stability of the cup to facilitate bone ingrowth into the shell and provide sufficient mechanical support to the underlying graft.

The morselized graft is then introduced into the prepared acetabulum and impacted against the floor using hemispherical impactors making sure that no graft should be present at the rim to cause obstruction to the peripheral fit of the acetabular component. The final acetabular component typically 1 mm to 2 mm larger in diameter than the last reamer used is then impacted into place.

Answer : c
