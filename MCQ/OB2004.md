## Question

A 70-year-old man presents with intermittent right lower extremity pain over several years. He denies any history of trauma. Physical examination reveals mild tenderness over the proximal third of right tibia with slight tibial bowing. A routine blood test shows an elevated serum alkaline phosphatase. Radiographs of the right tibia are shown in Figures A and B. A total body bone scan is shown in Figure C. What malignant transformation is most commonly associated with this patients underlying condition?

## Options

1. Osteosarcoma
2. Fibrosarcoma
3. Multiple myeloma
4. Giant cell tumor
5. Osteoid Osteoma

## Correct option

**1. Osteosarcoma**

## Answer explanation

This patients has clinical and radiographic features of Paget's disease. Secondary osteosarcoma is the most common malignant transformation associated with this condition.

Secondary osteosarcoma is a rare complication in patients with long-standing Paget's disease. It has an overall incidence of less than 1% in this population. The average age of onset is typically within the seventh decade of life. The 5-year survival after diagnosis is reported as less than 5%. A molecular basis for the association of osteosarcoma with Paget's disease is unclear.

*Hansen et al. reviewed the incidence of osteosarcomas complicating Paget's Disease.* *Of the typical sites that are usually involved in Paget's disease (e.g., spine, pelvis, femur, tibia and humerus), secondary osteosarcoma tends to spare the spine.*

*Shaylor et al. reported the mortality rates in 26 patient with osteosarcoma secondary to Paget' s disease.* There was a 47% mortality at 1 year and 75% at 2 years from diagnosis. In their series, no patient survived for 5 years. All patients died of metastatic disease.

Figures A and B show the AP and lateral radiographs of a right tibia. There is evidence of sclerosis along with "flamed shaped" appearing margins in the proximal tibia. Figure C shows a total body bone scan demonstrative of multiple foci of increased signal uptake. These findings are consistent with Paget's disease.

Illustration A is a schematic image that shows the common distribution of osteosarcoma.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: Osteosarcoma is the most common secondary malignancy, followed by fibrosarcoma and chondrosarcoma.
Answers 3,4: There is no causal association between multiple myeloma, giant cell tumors and Paget's disease
Answer 5: Osteoid osteoma is a benign bone tumor. It does not have metastatic properties.
