## Question

MC Intramedullary tumor in spine

## Options

1. Ependymoma
2. Astrocytoma
3. Meningioma
4. Medulloblastoma

## Correct option

**2. Astrocytoma**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

Most common intramedullary tumor in children and adoloscents: Astrocytoma

In Adults: Ependymoma

Overall: Astrocytoma

Also know some facts about BRAIN TUMORS: -

- Most common brain tumor - Metastases (>50%)
- Most common primary brain tumor – Ganglioblastoma Multiforme (GBM)
- Most common primary non-glial tumor - Meningioma.
- Most common primary CNS tumor in adults - GBM
- Most common primary CNS tumor in children - Gliomas (more specifically astrocytomas 50%), and the second most common is Medulloblastoma
- Most common pediatric brain malignancy - Medulloblastoma
- Most common pediatric vermian tumor - Medulloblastoma
- Most common supra-tentorial tumor in pediatrics (as in adults) - Astrocytoma
