Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Complications of THR

## Question

Most common complication of press fit technique in acetabular component fixation is

## Options

1. Acetabulum fracture
2. Implant loosening
3. Implant migration
4. Dull pelvic ache

## Correct option

**1. Acetabulum fracture**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Acetabular fracture is the most common complication of press fit technique of acetabular implantation. Under reaming is the most common cause for this fracture.

Answer: 1
