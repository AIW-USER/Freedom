## Question

A 10 year old boy is brought to your clinic by his parents for hand deformity. Picture of the hand is given below. What would be appropriate advice here?

![[TO17141421-1.png]]

## Options

1. Epiphysiodesis of PIP joints now
2. Surgical debulking now and epiphysiodesis later if recurrence occurs
3. Epiphysiodesis of PIP joints when the finger reaches the length of same finger of father
4. Ray amputation

## Correct option

**3. Epiphysiodesis of PIP joints when the finger reaches the length of same finger of father**

## Answer explanation

Macrodactyly is a rare condition in which growth of the finger(s), hand or limb is dysregulated by a somatic (post-zygotic) gain-in-function mutation in PIK3CA, a component of the mTOR pathway. Peripheral nerves in the involved ‘territory’ are usually markedly enlarged.

Associated conditions: The most common associated conditions are neurofibromatosis, Ollier’s disease, hemangiomatosis, Maffucci syndrome, congenital lipofibromatosis. Proteus syndrome, Banayan-Riley-Ruvalcabe's disease and Milroy’s disease.

Most of the cases are unilateral (90%) and index finger is most commonly involved. In majority of the cases more than one digits are involved.

Treatment: When enlargement is limited to one or two fingers, is not excessive and is not progressive, observation is appropriate. Surgery is delayed until the finger(s) reaches the same length as the same digit in the hand of the parent of the same gender. Epiphysiodeses, usually of proximal and middle phalanges but also of metacarpals if necessary, can be used to stop longitudinal growth. Circumferential growth, however, needs to be addressed by debulking procedures that often require removal of the underlying involved digital nerve as well as skin and subcutaneous overgrowth of an enlarged soft-tissue segment. If possible, debulking is performed at the time of physeal closures unless massive overgrowth demands earlier attention. Angulation can be treated with appropriate osteotomy(ies), at times at the level of the physeal closure. The combination of these procedures decreases the number of operations inflicted on the child.

Ray amputation is indicated in patients with massive enlargement of the bone and soft tissues, and is also the procedure of choice for severe recurrence after reduction syndactyly or soft-tissue debulking.

Answer: 3
