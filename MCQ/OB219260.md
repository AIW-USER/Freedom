## Question

Much research has been performed into the microbiological cause and treatment of periprosthetic joint infections (PJI). Which of the following best describes quorum as it relates to PJI?

## Options

1. Quorum refers to the lowest concentration of an antibiotic that inhibits the growth of a given strain of bacteria
2. Quorum refers to the critical density of bacteria needed to establish a biofilm colony
3. Quorum refers to the minimal concentration of bacteria needed in order for a colony to cohesively shift towards replication
4. Quorum refers to the number of treatment options available for a given bacterial strain
5. Quorum refers to the dose of antibiotic which would be be fatal in one-half of a group receiving said dose

## Correct option

**2. Quorum refers to the critical density of bacteria needed to establish a biofilm colony**

## Answer explanation

Quorum is the critical density of bacteria needed to overwhelm the immune system and establish a biofilm (Answer 2).

Quorum sensing (or quorum signaling) (QS) is a bacterial colony's ability to detect and respond to population density by gene regulation. It is a form of paracrine signaling but also includes traits of autocrine signaling. Many bacterial species utilize QS to coordinate gene expression according to the density of their local population. Once a colony of bacteria reaches a critical population density (quorum), a signaling cascade is activated, which induces multiple changes in both bacterial behavior and gene expression. During biofilm formation, the colony collectively shifts away from replication, and instead expresses genes that code for virulence factors. The formation of biofilms is an effective survival strategy for bacteria. Biofilms are resistant to antibiotics, disinfectant chemicals, phagocytosis, and other adaptive defense systems of the body.

The composition of biofilm is roughly 15% bacterial cells and 85% polysaccharides (glycocalyx). Concerning PJI, glycocalyx allows for the biofilm to adhere to prostheses and seal off the bacterial cells from the host immune system. As mentioned above, biofilm eradication is nearly impossible, and infections that have been present for longer than 4 weeks often require explantation of implants secondary to biofilm formation.

Ng and Bassler provide a review of bacterial quorum-sensing networks. The authors note that QS allows for synchronization of gene expression and that various forms of QS exist. The authors argue that the study of these networks has revealed some of the fundamental understandings of the evolution of collective behavior.

Rutherford and Bassler provide an overview of bacterial quorum sensing and how it relates to virulence. The authors highlight that QS enables bacteria to express energetically expensive processes only when the impact of those processes is of a net benefit to the colony. The authors note that inhibition of QS may represent a pathway for the development of novel antimicrobial therapies.

Miller and Bassler provide a review of QS in bacteria. The authors note that the detection of a minimal threshold stimulatory concentration of an autoinducer serves as the inciting step of the alteration of gene expression once quorum is achieved. The authors note that at this stage, the colony can shift from replication to survival and virulence. The authors also note that QS may have served as one of the early steps in the development of multicellular organisms.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: This answer refers to the minimum inhibitory concentration.
Answer 3: Quorum is the concentration of bacteria needed to shift collectively away from replication
Answer 4: This answer refers to the empiric options index (EOI), which varies from 0 to "the number of treatment options available" and measures the empiric value of the current stock of antibiotics as a depletable resource.
Answer 5: This answer refers to the median lethal dose (LD50).
