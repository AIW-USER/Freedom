## Question

A 55 years male was counselled by you for a Unicondylar Knee Arthroplasty (UKA) pertaining to unicompartmental disease and you went ahead for the procedure as he agreed. However, on opening the joint you found the ACL to be torn. Best next step from here would be [FNB 2023]

## Options

1. Continue with intended plan and intervene later as may be required
2. Convert to TKR and convey the reason to patient post surgery
3. Reconstruct ACL and proceed with intended plan of UKA
4. Simply do a Fixed bearing UKA

## Correct option

**3. Reconstruct ACL and proceed with intended plan of UKA**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C
EXPLANATION: -
ACL insufficiency is considered a relative contraindication for UKA. With improvement in techniques, most surgeons now advocate to continue with UKA when conditions are appropriate. A deficient UKA in such scenarios is simultaneously reconstructed.
