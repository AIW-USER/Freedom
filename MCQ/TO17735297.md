## Question

A 20 years old soccer player comes to you with a history of injury to his knee 2 months ago while playing. He notes that knee swelling has subsided with rest but he is not able to run and his knee occasionally gives way. Which test can confirm a combined PCL and PLC injury? (arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- multiligamentous knee injury)

## Options

1. Dial test positive at 0 degree and 90 degree
2. Positive posterior drawer test
3. Dial test positive at 30 degree and 90 degree
4. Pivot shift test and positive dial test at 30 degree

## Correct option

**3. Dial test positive at 30 degree and 90 degree**

## Answer explanation

Positive dial test at 30 degree signals PLC injury. If it is also positive at 90 degrees then it suggests combined PLC and PCL injury.

Answer: 3
