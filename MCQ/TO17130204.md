Chapter: Metabolic Bone Disorder MCQs — Miscellaneous (Metabolic Bone Disorders)

## Question

Looser’s zone not seen in (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Osteomalacia
2. Paget’s disease
3. X-linked hypophosphatemia
4. Led poisoning

## Correct option

**4. Led poisoning**

## Answer explanation

Conditions with Looser’s zones:

- Osteomalacia (characteristic)
- Renal osteodystrophy
- Fibrous dysplasia
- Hyperthyroidism
- Paget’s disease of bone
- X-linked hypophosphatemia
- Osteogenesis imperfecta

Answer: 4
