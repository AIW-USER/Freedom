## Question

Rhizomelic dwarfism refers to

## Options

1. short proximal part of the limbs
2. short distal part of the limbs
3. disproportionately short hands or feet
4. short trunk

## Correct option

**1. short proximal part of the limbs**

## Answer explanation

-Rhizomelic Dwarfism: Arms or thighs are short relative to the entire limb
- Mesomelic Dwarfism: Disproportionately short forearms or legs
- Acromelic Dwarfism: Disproportionately short hands or feet
