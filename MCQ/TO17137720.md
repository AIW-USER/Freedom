Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Intertrochanteric Fractures

## Question

Which types of reduction according to cortical support classification?

## Options

1. Positive
2. Negative
3. Neutral
4. Zero

## Correct option

**1. Positive**

## Answer explanation

![[TO17137720-1.jpg]]

Explanation: Medial cortical support classification- Positive – Proximal fragment is Medial (Mn: ppm) Negative – Proximal fragment is lateral

Neutral- Anatomical reduction. Most Acceptable is Positive.

Answer: 1
