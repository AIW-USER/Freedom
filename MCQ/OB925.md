## Question

Which term used in rehabilitiation describes a strengthening exercise performed at a constant speed?

## Options

1. Open chain
2. Closed chain
3. Isotonic
4. Isometric
5. Isokinetic

## Correct option

**5. Isokinetic**

## Answer explanation

This question is purely definition-based. Isokinetic strengthening involves movement at a constant speed (often controlled by a dynamometer). The references define the terms. Isotonic strengthening involves movement against a constant force. Isometric exercise is a resisted exercise in which the muscle contracts without an appreciable change in the length of the muscle or visible joint motion. Open chain exercises are when the distal part of extremity (i.e. hand or foot) are free to move (e.g. hamstring curls). Conversely, closed chain is when the hand/foot are not free to move (e.g. squats).
