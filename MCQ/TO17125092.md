Chapter: General Orthopedics MCQs — C Arm and Radiation Exposure in Orthopaedics

## Question

Respective annual permissible radiation exposure to body, thyroid and hands are

## Options

1. 20 mSv for the body, 150 mSv for thyroid or eyes, and 500 mSv for hands
2. 200 mSv for the body, 1500 mSv for thyroid or eyes, and 5000 mSv for hands
3. 10 mSv for the body, 100 mSv for thyroid or eyes, and 1000 mSv for hands
4. 150 mSv for the body, 500 mSv for thyroid or eyes, and 1500 mSv for hands

## Correct option

**1. 20 mSv for the body, 150 mSv for thyroid or eyes, and 500 mSv for hands**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: The International Commission on Radiological Protection has established the standards for radiation protection including the dosage limits. The maximum annual permissible upper dose limit is 20 mSv for the body, 150 mSv for thyroid or eyes, and 500 mSv for hands (International guidelines, ICRP)

Also know:

Average amount of radiation received at 18 inches from the primary beam is only 0.1% of that measured directly over the femoral head. Scatter radiation outside the 2-meter zone of a C Arm unit is less than 1 mSv. Within 2 meters of the C Arm unit, the lead protection is a must. Radiation exposure by C-arm is (in beam) 1,200 mrem/min to 4,000 mrem/min.

Answer: 1
