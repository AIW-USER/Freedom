## Question

A 4-year-old child has a 3-cm limb-length discrepancy, hemi-hypertrophy, and a large tongue. Additional tests should include which of the following?

## Options

1. Thyroid function studies
2. CT scan of the hip, knee, and ankle to measure torsion
3. Echocardiogram and EKG
4. MRI scan of the spine and CBC with differential
5. Abdominal and pelvic ultrasounds and alpha-fetoprotein levels

## Correct option

**5. Abdominal and pelvic ultrasounds and alpha-fetoprotein levels**

## Answer explanation

The child likely has Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome and up to a 10% chance for the development of a tumor, especially a Wilm's tumor. Therefore, studies consisting of surveillance abdominal and pelvic ultrasounds and alpha-fetoprotein levels, three to four times per year until age 8, are recommended. An echocardiogram is not needed in this population nor is thyroid function studies, MRI scan of the spine, or a CT scan to address torsion.
