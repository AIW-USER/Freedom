## Question

Ligamentum flavum origin and insertion are (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Origin from the lower half of the anterior surface of the lamina above and insertion on the posterior surface and upper margin of the lamina below
2. Origin from the upper half of the anterior surface of the lamina above and insertion on the posterior surface and lower margin of the lamina below
3. Origin from the lower half of the posterior surface of the lamina above and insertion on the anterior surface and upper margin of the lamina below
4. Origin from the upper half of the posterior surface of the lamina above and insertion on the anterior surface and lower margin of the lamina below

## Correct option

**1. Origin from the lower half of the anterior surface of the lamina above and insertion on the posterior surface and upper margin of the lamina below**

## Answer explanation

The ligamentum flavum arises from the lower half of the anterior surface of the lamina above and attaches to the posterior surface and upper margin of the lamina below.

Answer: 1
