## Question

“Cockade Sign” is diagnostic of Orthopedic Oncology-Benign Tumor & Tumor like lesions-Lipomas]

## Options

1. Calcaneal Lipoma
2. Chondroblastoma
3. Non Ossifying Fibroma
4. Eosinophillic Granuloma

## Correct option

**1. Calcaneal Lipoma**

## Answer explanation

Cockade sign is characteristic of intraosseous lipoma. It refers to central calcification within a well defined lytic area in the calcaneum secondary to lipoma.
