## Question

Identify the splint shown in the image below

![[TO17907808-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Cock up splint
2. Turn buckle splint
3. Knuckle bender splint
4. None of the above

## Correct option

**2. Turn buckle splint**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

Turn buckle splint is used to produce gradual stretching over a contracted joint by its turn buckle mechanism. It significantly decreases the rehabilitation time and also carries a very good patient compliance. Its commonly used for stretching tissues in case of Volkmann’s ischemic contractures and stiff elbow.
