## Question

Most common complication of triple arthrodesis is( regional orthopedics- foot and ankle- arthrodesis)

## Options

1. Mid foot arthritis
2. Hardware irritation
3. Talo-navicular nonunion
4. Ankle arthritis

## Correct option

**3. Talo-navicular nonunion**

## Answer explanation

Complications of triple arthrodesis

1. Nonunion remains the most commonly early and midterm complication following triple arthrodesis. Most nonunions are noted at the TN joint, followed by CC joint.

2. Development of ankle arthritis remains a concern and has been reported to occur in a few as 40% and as many as 100% of patients with long enough follow-up (Most common long term complication)

3. Midfoot arthritis is also common and has been reported to occur in nearly 50% of patients

4. Other complications such as hardware irritation, infection, osteonecrosis, malunion, deep-vein thrombosis, and iatrogenic nerve injury remain quite rare

Answer: 3
