## Question

Most common complication after high tibial osteotomy for varus knee deformity is

## Options

1. Patella baja
2. Recurrence of varus
3. Peroneal nerve injury
4. Knee stiffness and loss of extreme range of motion

## Correct option

**2. Recurrence of varus**

## Answer explanation

Complications after high tibial osteotomy:Recurrence of a varus deformity is the most common complication. (overcorrection beyond the normal 5 degrees of anatomic valgus decreases the frequency of this complication)

- Peroneal nerve injury most often is related to fibular osteotomy
- Popliteal artery injury is rare but devastating. keeping something substantial between the proximal
- Most patients develop significant patella baja after proximal tibial osteotomy. Several factors may cause this, including shortening of the patellar tendon after prolonged immobilization, new bone formation at the site of the osteotomy in the area of the insertion of the patellar tendon, and fibrosis of the patellar tendon.

Answer: 2
