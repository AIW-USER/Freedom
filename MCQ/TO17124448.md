## Question

Chances of direct anatomical extension from metaphysis to the joint is least likely in which joint? (FNB Pattern Paper 2014)

## Options

1. Hip
2. Knee
3. Ankle
4. Shoulder

## Correct option

**2. Knee**

## Answer explanation

The metaphysis of the proximal humerus, radial head, distal lateral tibia and proximal femur lies partially within the joint. Only knee metaphysis is extra articular, so chances of direct anatomical extension are most unlikely in the knee joint.

Answer: 2
