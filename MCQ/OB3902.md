## Answer explanation

This patient had an ankle arthrodesis performed. The dorsiflexion/plantarflexion of the ankle may be mimicked with a rocker-bottom shoe as shown in Figure C.

Arthrodesis of the ankle may be detrimental to maintaining a normal gait pattern. Compared to normal walking, ankle arthrodesis has been reported to result in an estimated 9-15R% increase in metabolic energy expenditure. To restore walking function, a rocker-bottom sole is usually prescribed. They have been found to redistribute plantar foot pressures, improve gait kinematics, and functional performance.

*Vanderpool et al. performed a study to test whether the metabolic energy cost of walking with the ankles immobilized can be comparable to normal walking.* *They measured oxygen consumption, kinematics, and ground reaction forces in six volunteers.* Ankle fixation with walking boots caused the total rate of energy expenditure for walking to increase by 4.1% compared to normal but differed by an insignificant amount compared to walking with equivalent ankle weight. **They conclude** that with a lightweight apparatus and curved rocker bottom surface, loss of ankle motion does not increase energy expenditure for walking.

*van Schie et al. tested 9 different rigid rocker shoe designs in 17 symptom-free male subjects and compared them with the control condition of a flexible, non-rockered extra-depth shoe with the same flat insole.* **They found** that peak pressure was reduced at most forefoot locations by rocker shoes, but increased in the midfoot and heel. Most configurations of the rocker shoes were superior to the control shoe, but no single configuration was optimal. **They conclude** that gait training should be performed to ensure the correct use of the shoe.

Figure A is a lateral radiograph of an ankle arthrodesis with screws. Figure B is a photograph of an Arizona brace. Figure C is a photograph of a shoe with a rocker-bottom sole. Figure D is a photograph of a rigid ankle-foot orthosis (AFO). Figure E is a photograph of a hinged AFO. Figure F is a photograph of a carbon fiber insert with a Morton's extension.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: An Arizona brace is meant to immobilize the ankle and may be used in the conservative treatment of ankle arthritis.
Answers 3 & 4: AFOs also immobilize the ankle. They have many uses including the treatment of a drop-foot.
Answer 5: A carbon fiber insert with a Morton's extension would not improve gait. It is used in the conservative treatment of hallux rigidus.
