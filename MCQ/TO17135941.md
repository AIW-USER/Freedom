## Question

In which sport does the Magnus effect play an important role? (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Tennis
2. Sprints
3. Swimming
4. D

## Correct option

**1. Tennis**

## Answer explanation

Magnus effect is seen in the curved flight path of balls that are given a spin due to hit, throw or kick in different ball sports like cricket, soccer, baseball, golf, tennis etc.

 When a spinning ball moves through the air, it spins a boundary layer of air that clings to its surface. On one side of the spinning ball high pressure area is generated due to deceleration of the boundary layer air which is traveling along the spinning ball and collides with the air passing by. On the opposing side, both airs move in the same direction so no collision occurs between the boundary layer and the air passing by creating a low-pressure area. This pressure differential generates a lift force (the Magnus force) that bends the flight path of the ball in the direction of the pressure differential (i.e., from high to low).
