## Question

False statement about ACL reconstruction is all except

## Options

1. Better IKDC score with bioabsorbable screw
2. Bioabsorbable screws have less revision rate compared to metal screws
3. Bioabsorbable screws have more screw breakage and failure rate compared to metal screws
4. Tunnel widening is less with bioabsorbable screws compared to metal screws

## Correct option

**3. Bioabsorbable screws have more screw breakage and failure rate compared to metal screws**

## Answer explanation

Answer: Metal versus bioabsorbable interference screws

Cochrane reviews and meta-analysis have shown that surgical and functional outcomes (IKDC, Lysholm, Tegner, KT and other scores) are the same with both screws.
