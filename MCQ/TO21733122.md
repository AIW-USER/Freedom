Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Post THR Infection

## Question

A patient who underwent THR, 10 years ago has come to you with complaint of groin pain. X ray was done that depicted loosening signs. Which of the following investigation will help you best to differentiate between septic and aseptic loosening

## Options

1. Radiolabelled WBC scan
2. Radionuclide bone scan
3. USG of hip
4. Aspiration of hip

## Correct option

**1. Radiolabelled WBC scan**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

Radiolabelled WBC scan 99 mTc-labelled WBC scintigraphy (especially with associated bone scan) is the gold standard especially when it comes to distinguishing between septic and aseptic loosening.
Symptoms of septic and aseptic loosening can be very non-specific with pain on weight bearing and reduced mobility. Clinical signs, routine blood tests, and X-rays mostly fail to distinguish septic from aseptic loosening.
Fine needle aspiration is characterised by Sensitivity (SE) of 67% and a low negative predictive value & has to be performed exclusively where there is high suspicion level with increased ESR and CRP.
Nuclear medicine techniques have a high SE but a lower SP (Specificity); bone scan, using TC99-bisphosphonate, is a bone metabolism marker with an SE of 100% but a very low SP (30%). The use of 99 mTc-labelled WBC scintigraphy allows partial avoidance of this problem, achieving an SP of 86% and thus has achieved gold standard status.
Nevertheless, it has several other problems including high cost, dangers related to blood manipulation, and a longer procedure.
Currently, 99 mTc-labelled WBC scintigraphy is still today the gold standard especially when associated with bone scan.
Positron emission tomography based on 2-fluoro-2-deoxy-D-glucose could be a valid option if research is able to find an uptake pattern specific for septic and aseptic loosening. The exact role of FDG PET/CT is not yet fully established. A normal scan can rule out PJI with high certainty. Differentiation between PJI and loosening or inflammation is difficult. This is particularly the case in patients with signs of polyethylene and metal wear- induced chronic inflammation. On the other hand, the high sensitivity of FDG PET/CT for small particle disease could be of high clinical value as an early predictor of loosening. Combined reading of FDG PET/CT and the bone scan could be helpful to differentiate PJI from abrasion-caused inflammation. The potential value of FDG-PET is confirmed because it offers several benefits above traditional methods: good accuracy, a faster procedure, lower cost, as well as lower contamination risk for technicians.
