## Question

Each of the following are complications or late deformities associated with clubfoot surgery EXCEPT:

## Options

1. Dynamic supination
2. Dorsal Bunion
3. Intoeing
4. Tarsal tunnel syndrome.

## Correct option

**4. Tarsal tunnel syndrome.**

## Answer explanation

Dynamic supination is one of the common gait issue after correction of a child with clubfoot deformity. The anterior tibialis muscle is the antagonist of the peroneus longus muscle and its integrity or overactivity is required for the development of a dorsal bunion. An abnomality of its bone insertion or its retraction can be found in congenital clubfoot and partly explains the natural tendency of these feet to develop a dorsal bunion. Internal tibial torsion is a known problem with CTEV. Tarsal tunnel and its association with CTEV is not known.

Amswer: d
