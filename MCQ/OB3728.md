## Question

A patient of yours with known metastatic breast cancer recently recovered from a vertebral compression fracture that you treated non-operatively. She asks you about zoledronic acid, a drug that one of her friends takes for multiple myeloma. You tell her that there is good evidence that:

## Options

1. She has a 25% chance of developing osteonecrosis of the jaw while taking zoledronic acid
2. Zolendronic acid will not reduce her pain but may have other benefits
3. With zoledronic acid treatment, she will have a significant reduction in risk of pathologic fractures, need for radiotherapy, need for orthopaedic surgery, spinal cord compression, and hypercalcemia
4. Renal impairment will not affect her ability to take zoledronic acid
5. Zoledronic acid is significantly less effective than pamidronate at reducing skeletal related events

## Correct option

**3. With zoledronic acid treatment, she will have a significant reduction in risk of pathologic fractures, need for radiotherapy, need for orthopaedic surgery, spinal cord compression, and hypercalcemia**

## Answer explanation

Zoledronic acid treatment results in a 30 to 40% decrease in skeletal related events (risk of pathologic fractures, need for radiotherapy, need for orthopaedic surgery, spinal cord compression, and hypercalcemia).

Bisphosphonates such as zoledronic acid reduce skeletal morbidity in patients with metastatic bone disease. Other benefits include correction of hypercalcemia and treatment of malignancy-induced osteopenia. Fifty percent of patients experience a clinically-significant reduction in pain.

Body reviews the role of bisphosphonates in the treatment of metastatic bone disease related to breast cancer. They note that metastatic bone disease can significantly adversely affect quality of life in cancer patients. In breast cancer, the skeleton is the most common site of metastasis and the most common first site of distant relapse. Patients with bony metastases alone have a higher rate of skeletal-related events than those with bony and visceral metastases, and may benefit from bisphosphonate therapy.

*Bedard et al. provide a commentary on the role of bisphosphonates in breast cancer in light of the publication of the results of the Austrian Breast and Colorectal Cancer Study Group (ABCSG)-12 trial.* **The results of this study suggest** that the addition of a nitrogen-containing bisphosphonate improves long-term outcome in early-stage breast cancer, potentially opening the door to new roles for bisphosphonate treatment.

Incorrect answers:
Answer 1: There is a 2.5% chance of jaw osteonecrosis
Answer 2: 50% of patients will experience a decrease in pain
Answer 4: Renal toxicity is a known issue with zoledronic acid. Renal function should be assayed prior to treatment and dosing should be adjusted based on creatinine clearance
Answer 5: Zoledronic acid was found to be significantly more effective than pamidronate in a randomized trial
