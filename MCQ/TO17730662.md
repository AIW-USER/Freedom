## Question

A young male comes in with a complaint of hip pain on left side. On examination he has pain in external rotation. MRI of the left hip shows subchondral collapse with maintained sphericity. Which is the stage of AVN according to Ficat-Arlet staging system? (regional orthopedics- Hip- avascular necrosis of hip)

## Options

1. 2A
2. 2B
3. 3
4. 4

## Correct option

**2. 2B**

## Answer explanation

Ficat-Arlet staging system:

STAGE
Radiograph findings

0
None(bone scan may show decreased uptake)

1
Normal (bone scans show cold spot)

2A
Diffuse or localized osteoporosis, cyst or sclerosis

2B
Crescent sign (subchondral collapse with maintained sphericity, flattening

3
Loss of sphericity

4
Joint space narrowing, arthritic changes

Answer : B
