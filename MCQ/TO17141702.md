Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Tendon Injuries of Hand

## Question

How many compartments are there in the hand

## Options

1. 4
2. 5
3. 8
4. 10

## Correct option

**4. 10**

## Answer explanation

Anatomically, the hand can be divided into 10 separate compartments and includes the hypothenar, thenar, adductor pollicis compartment, 4 dorsal interosseous compartments, and 3 volar interosseous compartments.

Answer: 4
