## Question

Which is not a maneuver to avoid nerve injury in elbow arthroscopy? (arthroscopy- elbow arthroscopy- portals and complications)

## Options

1. Pronation and 90 degree flexion to protect PIN
2. Joint distension
3. More distal lateral portal
4. Low flow infusion

## Correct option

**3. More distal lateral portal**

## Answer explanation

Answer : C
