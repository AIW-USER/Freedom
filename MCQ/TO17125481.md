Chapter: General Orthopedics MCQs — Pathological Fractures

## Question

Mirels criteria are used for (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Operability in spine secondaries
2. To quantify the risk of impending fracture in case of neoplastic lesion of the bone
3. Pathological fracture management
4. Indications of surgery in scheuermann's disease

## Correct option

**2. To quantify the risk of impending fracture in case of neoplastic lesion of the bone**

## Answer explanation

Prophylactic internal fixation of bone is warranted in case of large neoplastic lesion, if the risk of fracture is high. Mirels criteria are used to quantify the risk of impending fracture in such cases and thereby plan prophylactic fixation in high-risk fractures.
