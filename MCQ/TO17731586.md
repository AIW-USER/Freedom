## Question

ACL does not derive its blood supply from ( Arthroscopy- knee Arthroscopy- ACL)

## Options

1. Synovium
2. Middle geniculate artery
3. Inferior lateral genicular artery
4. Superior genicular artery

## Correct option

**4. Superior genicular artery**

## Answer explanation

Anterior cruciate ligament vascularization arises primarily from the middle genicular artery and the adjacent synovium. After branching from the popliteal artery, the middle genicular artery courses along the dorsal aspect of the ACL, while giving rise to ligamentous branches to the ligament. Additional blood supply to the ACL comes from the inferior medial and lateral genicular arteries.

Answer: 4
