Chapter: Congenital & Developmental Musculoskeletal Disorders MCQs — Achondroplasia

## Question

Achondroplasia is associated with an aberration in which zone of the growth plate?

## Options

1. The zone of provisional calcification.
2. The resting zone
3. The zone of hypertrophy
4. The zone of proliferation

## Correct option

**4. The zone of proliferation**

## Answer explanation

Achondroplasia is a defect of endochondral ossification. It is an autosomal dominant disorder with mutation in the fibroblast growth factor receptor 3 gene. The disease has its predominant effect at the zone of proliferation, where it causes a relative lack of cartilage production. The term achondroplasia is actually a misnomer because there is not a complete absence of cartilage production.

In achondroplasia intramembranous bone production, conversely, proceeds normally. Thus, the diaphysis of long bones has relatively normal width despite their altered length. The proximal segments of the limbs are most severely affected (rhizomelic micromelic).

Answer: 4
