## Question

Figure 31 shows an MRI scan of a left shoulder. The arrow is pointing to what structure?

![[q6457-a-31.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Long head of the biceps
2. Supraspinatus
3. Subscapularis
4. Coracoacromial ligament
5. Middle glenohumeral ligament

## Correct option

**1. Long head of the biceps**

## Answer explanation

The rotator interval is an area bounded superiorly by the suprapinatus, inferiorly by the subscapularis, and medially by the glenoid. On a sagittal MRI image, the long head of the biceps can be seen in cross section in this interval between the subscapularis and supraspinatus. It extends from the superior labrum laterally, before it turns inferiorly and exits into the bicipital groove. If seen on a sagittal image, the middle glenohumeral ligament will extend obliquely, in an anteroinferior direction, from the superior labrum, and will be deep, or posterior to the subscapularis.
