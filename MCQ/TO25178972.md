Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Cerebral Palsy (CP)

## Question

What is the 'Rule of 15' when considering DFEO in Cerebral Palsy?

## Options

1. The osteotomy should be fixed with at least 15 hole plates
2. The maximum wedge size should not exceed 15mm
3. Only perform DFEO if the patient is older than 15 years
4. DFEO is generally indicated when FKFD is greater than 15 degree

## Correct option

**4. DFEO is generally indicated when FKFD is greater than 15 degree**

## Answer explanation

Below 15 degree soft tissue procedures or
guided growth are often sufficient; above 15 degree , bony correction is
typically necessary.

Answer: 4
