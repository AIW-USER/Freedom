# TO17135445

#TO #pathology #hemangioma
Chapter: Spine MCQs — Spinal Tumors

## Question

Polka dot sign on CT is seen in (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Osteomyelitis of clavicle
2. Vertebral hemangioma
3. Cortical osteosarcoma
4. bony lymphoma

## Correct option

**2. Vertebral hemangioma**

## Answer explanation

The polka-dot sign is seen in the axial cut of CT scan of the vertebral hemangioma. It results from replacement of the normal cancellous bone by thickened vertical trabeculae surrounded by fat marrow or vascular lacunae.

In the sagittal and coronal images corduroy sign is seen in vertebral hemangioma. On CT scan the dots are white on a black fatty background, whereas on MRI they are black dots on a white background (on non-fat-suppressed T1 or T2-weighted images).

Jailhouse appearance due to striations is seen on plain X rays in case of bony hemangiomas.
