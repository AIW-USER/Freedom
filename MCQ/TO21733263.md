## Question

Which is not a recommended technique for gap balancing in kinematically aligned TKR for tight medial and loose lateral extension?

## Options

1. Removal of medial osteophytes
2. Recut tibia in 2 degrees more varus
3. Thicker liner
4. Medial release

## Correct option

**4. Medial release**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 4
KA aims to restore the patient’s pre-arthritic limb alignment and joint lines by resurfacing the distal femur and proximal tibia. KA is considered a “ligament sparing” technique as it relies on bone cuts to recreate pre-arthritic knee laxities and compartment forces. Femoral component is positioned first at intended place and tibial cuts are adjusted to create the desired joint line orientation and ligament tensioning.
