## Question

Which is the main stabilizer of shoulder in abduction and external rotation (regional orthopedics- shoulder- anatomy)

## Options

1. SGHL
2. CHL
3. IGHL
4. MGHL

## Correct option

**3. IGHL**

## Answer explanation

Shoulder ligaments:

The coracohumeral ligament (CHL): It originates from the proximal third of the horizontal part of the coracoid, and has a variable insertion. Superﬁcial ﬁbers insert into the greater tuberosity; only 15–50 % of the ﬁbers insert into the lesser tuberosity; they blend into muscular fibers of the supraspinatus and subscapularis tendons. Deep ﬁbers blend into expansions of the subscapularis more similar to capsular tissue.

 Glenohumeral Ligaments: These are reinforcements of the capsule

 Superior Glenohumeral Ligament (Flood ligament): it arises from the superior tubercle of the glenoid. It inserts into the fovea capitis of the humerus, just above the lesser tuberosity. The gap between the superior and the middle glenohumeral ligament is called the Weitbrecht foramen. It is considered one of the most important stabilizers of the LHB in the bicipital groove.

 Middle Glenohumeral ligament (MGL): MGL originates just beneath or just medial to the SGL and inserts just medial to the lesser tuberosity. Its fibers blend with subscapularis tendon. It is separated from the inferior glenohumeral ligament by a recess or by the so-called Rouviere foramen.

 Inferior Glenohumeral Ligament (IGL): it has two bands, anterior and posterior which make a hammock-like structure. The anterior band originates from the anterior margin of glenoid from 2 to 4 o’clock and the posterior band originates from the posterior margin of glenoid from 7 to 9 o’clock. Between them there is the axillary pouch or recess. Both bands insert into the humeral head like a collar just under the articular margin.

 Rotator Interval

 The space between the upper border of the subscapularis tendon and the anterior margin of the supraspinatus tendon is called the rotator interval (RI).

The rotator interval consists of four layers:

The superﬁ cial layer of the CHL • Supraspinatus and subscapularis ﬁ bers • The deep layer of the CHL ligament
