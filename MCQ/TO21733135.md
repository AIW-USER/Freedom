## Question

Which of the following is false regarding foot posture index (FPI)

## Options

1. It is a dynamic biomechanical assessment
2. Congruency and height of the medial longitudinal arch is assessed
3. Talar head is palpated
4. Calcaneal position in frontal plane is assessed

## Correct option

**1. It is a dynamic biomechanical assessment**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

EXPLANATION: -

Foot Posture Index is a static assessment of foot done by a clinician. It’s a measure of standing foot posture and helps you to quantify how much a sportsman’s foot is supinated, neutral or pronated which could help in injury prevention.

The patient has to stand in the relaxed stance position with double limb support for approximately two minutes for scoring to be complete. It contains 6 items each of which is individually scored as follows: -

Individual item scoring:

-2 highly supinated
-1 supinated
 0 neutral
+1 pronated
+2 highly pronated

Total scoring: This is calculated by adding individual scores for each above item. Final outcome is then rated as follows: -

-5 to -12 highly supinated
-1 to -4 supinated
 0 to +5 neutral
+6 to +9 pronated
> +10 highly pronated

The applications of the FPI include studies of biomechanical risk factors for neuropathic ulceration in diabetes, identifying foot type as a basis for screening subjects as inclusion or exclusion criteria in clinical research, investigating the relationship between foot types and risk factors for sports and training injuries, investigating whether foot posture is associated with falls in older people and as a means of assessing age-related differences in foot structure. The index is considered by many as more valid approach for foot posture assessment instead of many weightbearing and non-weightbearing goniometric methods available.
