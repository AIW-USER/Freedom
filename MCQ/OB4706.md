## Question

## Options

1. Observation alone
2. Chemotherapy alone
3. Radiation therapy alone
4. Neoadjuvant chemotherapy, wide resection, adjuvant chemotherapy
5. Wide resection alone

## Correct option

**4. Neoadjuvant chemotherapy, wide resection, adjuvant chemotherapy**

## Answer explanation

Based on the MRI and the histology, this patient has a spindle cell sarcoma (undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma) of bone. The optimal treatment is wide resection and chemotherapy

Spindle cell sarcoma (undifferentiated pleomorphic) is treated similarly to osteosarcoma. Preoperative chemotherapy is given for 8-12 weeks prior to surgical resection. Chemotherapy is continued for 6-12 months postoperatively. Staging of these lesions may be completed with the Enneking system. Stage I is low grade, Stage II is high grade and Stage III is distant metastatic. These stages are subclassifed based on whether the lesion is intracompartmental (A) or extra compartmental (B). Stage 2B spindle cell lesions are treated with preoperative chemotherapy, wide excision followed by chemotherapy

*Jeon et al. compared treatment outcomes between spindle cell sarcoma of bone and osteosarcoma.* **They noted** that the former presented in older patients with a greater osteolytic effect. In response to chemotherapy, **the authors found** comparable survival rates and histologic responses for both pathologies.

Figure A is a coronal MRI sequence of an spindle cell sarcoma of bone. Figures B and C are low and high power figures, respectively. They are histologic representations of a pleomorphic spindle cell sarcoma of bone, with subtle storiform pattern and scattered atypical mitoses.

Incorrect Answers
Answer 1: Observation is not indicated for this high grade lesion
Answers 2, 3: Chemotherapy and radiation alone are not indicated for this lesion
Answer 5: Wide resection alone is not used for spindle cell sarcomas of bone
