## Question

A 16-year-old basketball player is running down the court and suddenly falls to the ground, becoming unresponsive. He is found to be pulseless and is not breathing. You begin chest compressions per American Heart Association (AHA) guidelines. He has no significant cardiac or other past medical history. What is the most common cause of cardiac arrest in his age group?

## Options

1. Aortic rupture
2. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
3. Long QT Syndrome
4. Myocarditis
5. Wolff-Parkinson-White Syndrome

## Correct option

**2. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy**

## Answer explanation

Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy is the most common cause of sudden cardiac death in young athletes.

Sudden cardiac death in young athletes is becoming an increasingly recognized problem, with rates up to 1 in 40,000. Young men, African-Americans, and participants of certain sports, like basketball, appear to be at higher risk. The most commonly identified causes of sudden cardiac death include hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, left ventricular hypertrophy, and coronary artery anomalies. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy is a condition causing abnormal thickening of the left ventricle walls, which can significantly decrease cardiac output, and can be exaggerated with exercise. In the event of a sudden cardiac event, the American Heart Association (AHA) recommends immediate cardiopulmonary resuscitation techniques with compression-to-ventilation ratio of 30:2 in addition to other life-saving measures.

*Harmon et al. performed a meta-analysis to better quantify the incidence of sudden cardiac death in young athletes.* **They found** high variability in the reporting of these rates, with most higher-power studies describing incidence between 1:40,000 and 1:80,000. **They conclude** that this incidence is likely higher than traditionally appreciated, which should drive improved prevention measures.

*Peterson et al. also reviewed the incidence of sudden cardiac arrest in young athletes, but also describe the most common etiologies.* **They note** a high incidence in high school males who play football or basketball. They also describe the most common causes of sudden cardiac death, headlined by hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, which makes up about 21% of cases.

Answers 1,3-5: While these cardiac conditions can cause sudden cardiac death, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy has been shown to be the most common cause.
