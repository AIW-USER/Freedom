## Question

What is not a component of the terrible triad in damage control surgery? (trauma- polytrauma patients )

## Options

1. Coagulopathy
2. Hypothermia
3. Hemorrhage
4. Acidosis

## Correct option

**3. Hemorrhage**

## Answer explanation

Damage control surgery is used in critically ill trauma patients to prevent further progression (second hit) to already existing physiological insult. In critically ill trauma victims hemorrhage is the leading cause of death. Severe hemorrhage causes a lethal triad of damage control surgery including metabolic acidosis, hypothermia and coagulopathy. Damage control surgery focuses on prevention of further bleeding, correction of shock and wound care to reduce contamination. Surgery is limited to external fixation of the bone/ pelvis and emergency removal of the unsurvivable severely crushed part of the limb. Definitive anatomical fixation is delayed until stabilization of the patient.
