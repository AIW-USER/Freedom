Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Lisfranc Injury

## Question

Preferred treatment of purely ligamentous lisfranc injury is[FNB 2016]

## Options

1. Non weight bearing below knee cast
2. Anatomical fixation with screws
3. Medial tarso-metatarsal joint arthrodesis
4. Weight bearing as tolerated in below knee cast.

## Correct option

**3. Medial tarso-metatarsal joint arthrodesis**

## Answer explanation

Most preferred treatment for the purely ligamentous lisfranc injury is the medial tarso-metatarsal fusion.

Answer: 3
