Chapter: Spine MCQs — Kyphoplasty Vertebroplasty

## Question

All are true regarding kyphoplasty and vertebroplasty, in the treatment of osteoporotic compression fractures except?

## Options

1. Chances of cement leakage is less in kyphoplasty compared to vertebroplasty
2. Healed vertebral compression fracture with kyphosis can be treated with vertebroplasty/Kyphoplasty
3. Kyphoplasty technique is superior in terms of deformity correction compared to vertebroplasty
4. Vertebroplasty cement has high viscosity and more radiopacity compare to Arthroplasty cement

## Correct option

**2. Healed vertebral compression fracture with kyphosis can be treated with vertebroplasty/Kyphoplasty**

## Answer explanation

Healed compression fracture with kyphosis cannot be treated with cementing techniques.

The cement applying pressure is less in kyphoplasty compared to vertebroplsty because we create space before applying cement in kyphoplasty. This is done by inflating ballon to restore vertebral height that creates a void. Hence cement inserting pressure goes low. However, pain relief is comparable in both procedures

Answer: 2
