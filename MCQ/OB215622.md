## Question

A 55-year-old presents to the Emergency Department with atraumatic knee pain, intermittent fevers, and an inability to bear weight. Inflammatory markers are significantly elevated. Given the signs and symptoms, the junior resident on call performs an arthrocentesis, which confirms septic arthritis. The patient promptly undergoes a thorough irrigation and debridement followed by hospital admission. The infectious disease team is consulted and recommends starting an antibiotic that inhibits transpeptidation by binding to D-alanyl-D-alanine residues of the bacterial cell wall. What antibiotic has this mechanism of action?

## Options

1. Clindamycin
2. Vancomycin
3. Daptomycin
4. Ciprofloxacin
5. Sulfamethoxazole/trimethoprim

## Correct option

**2. Vancomycin**

## Answer explanation

Vancomycin is the antibiotic being described which inhibits cell-wall biosynthesis by binding to the D-Ala-D-Ala terminal of the growing peptide chain during cell wall synthesis. This results in transpeptidase inhibition, which prevents further elongation and cross-linking of the peptidoglycan matrix.

Septic arthritis is an orthopedic emergency that requires a high level of suspicion for diagnosis. It is caused by joint inflammation secondary to an infectious etiology, most commonly affecting the knee, hip, and shoulder. Clinical presentation varies but can include monoarticular joint pain, effusion, warmth, difficulty bearing weight, and fevers. Treatment is generally urgent surgical irrigation and debridement to prevent permanent cartilage damage followed by culture-directed antibiotics.

*Chen et al. review the use of intrawound antibiotics in the prevention of surgical site infections in various orthopedic and non-orthopedic surgeries.* The purpose of these topical antibiotics (irrigation solutions, ointments, pastes, beads, sponges, and fleeces) is to provide supratherapeutic dosage with minimal systemic exposure and fewer adverse effects. **They conclude** that the addition of local antibiotics may further reduce the incidence of infections, however, the current evidence available in the orthopedic literature is poor.

*Eyler et al. describe the pharmacology of antibiotics to include descriptions of antibiotic drug classes, their spectrum of activity, mechanisms of action, and the common infectious diseases in which they treat.* Specific to vancomycin, they describe it as a glycopeptide antibiotic with activity against the majority of Gram-positive bacteria and a first-line agent for the treatment of serious methicillin-resistant S. aureus infections.

Incorrect Answers
Answer 1: Clindamycin inhibits bacterial protein synthesis by binding to the 50S subunit of the bacterial ribosome.
Answer 3: Daptomycin works by binding to bacterial cell membranes causing rapid depolarization and ultimate bacterial death.
Answer 4: Ciprofloxacin inhibits DNA replication by inhibiting bacterial DNA topoisomerase and DNA-gyrase.
Answer 5: Sulfamethoxazole/trimethoprim, also known as Bactrim, interferes with folic acid metabolism.
