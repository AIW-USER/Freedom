## Question

A patient presents with longstanding and worsening foot pain and deformity. On examination, he has a claw-toe deformity, and radiographs of the foot demonstrate lateral deviation of the proximal phalanx of the 2nd toe. What would be the most direct result of a plantar plate repair in this patient?

## Options

1. Improves the flexion deformity at the MTP joint
2. Improves the extension deformity at the MTP joint
3. Improves the flexion deformity at the PIP joint
4. Improves the extension deformity at the PIP joint
5. Improves the extension deformity at the DIP joint

## Correct option

**2. Improves the extension deformity at the MTP joint**

## Answer explanation

A plantar plate repair improves the extension deformity at the MTP joint, allowing the toe to assume a plantigrade position.

Claw toe is a lesser toe deformity characterized by MTP hyperextension and resulting PIP and DIP flexion. Diagnosis is made clinically with the presence of MTP hyperextension, PIP flexion, and DIP flexion of a lesser toe. Treatment is a trial of nonoperative management with shoe modification and taping. Surgical management is indicated for progressive deformity, fixed contractures, and dorsal toe ulcerations. A plantar plate repair improves the extension deformity at the MTP joint, allowing the toe to assume a plantigrade position.

*Nery et al. prospectively reviewed the results obtained in the treatment of a group of patients with plantar plate tears by direct repair through a dorsal approach combined with a Weil metatarsal osteotomy with a minimum follow-up of 12 months.* **They reported** that the direct plantar plate repair combined with a Weil osteotomy and lateral soft tissue reefing can restore the normal alignment of the MTP joint. **They concluded** that the anatomic repair of the plantar plate can correct the deviation of the affected toe (medial, dorsal, dorsomedial, or dorsolateral), which leads to diminished pain with improved functional scores.

*Doty et al. reviewed lesser toe metatarsophalangeal joint instability.* **They reported** that some surgeons now advocate the incorporation of a primary repair of the plantar plate into the surgical plan for correction of metatarsophalangeal joint deviation in the sagittal and transverse planes. **They concluded** that dorsal and plantar approaches have both been used successfully to repair the plantar plate and tears of the plantar plate can be repaired primarily or advanced on the base of the proximal phalanx through bone tunnels.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Claw toe has MTP hyperextension.
Answer 3: While a claw toe does have PIP flexion, a plantar plate repair directly addresses the MTP hyperextension.
Answer 4: Claw toe has PIP flexion.
Answer 5: Claw toe has DIP flexion.
