A 38-year-old woman fell from a ladder onto her right hip. The radiographs and CT scan are shown in Figures 52a through 52d. What is the best surgical approach for this fracture?

## Options

1. Kocher-Langenbeck
2. Iliofemoral
3. Ilioinguinal
4. Extended iliofemoral
5. Triradiate approach

## Correct option

**3. Ilioinguinal**

## Answer explanation

The fracture is an associated both column fracture. The best approach for this fracture is the ilioinguinal. The Kocher-Langenbeck is best for posterior injuries to the acetabulum and some transverse fractures. The iliofemoral alone is limited to high anterior column injuries. The extended iliofemoral and triradiate approaches although useful for this fracture, have a higher rate of complications.
