## Question

Following statements are true about Highly cross linked Polyethylene used as an insert in TKR surgery, except

## Options

1. Made by Compression moulding
2. Sterilized by ether
3. It has Vitamin E to scavenge free radicals
4. There is less chance of delamination

## Correct option

**2. Sterilized by ether**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

Sterlization of Highly cross-linked PE is done by Ethylene oxide or plasma gas treatment or gamma radiation (not by ether).

HOW A TKR SPACER PE INSERT IS MADE: -

Polyethylene is crosslinked by gamma radiation treatment to generate ULTRA HIGH MOLECULAR WEIGHT POLYETHYLENE [UHMWPE]. This product is basically a resin powder that is machined into desired shape (such as a TKR insert/ spacer) by following processes: -

- Compression moulding
- Ram Extrusion
- Isostatic pressing

The final manufactured product is sterilized via radiation treatment (Gamma or Electron beam), Plasma Gas or Ethylene oxide treatment.

Now, currently what is being used is HIGHLY CROSS-LINKED POLYETHYLENE [HCPE] which is more advanced than UHMWPE. Here additional cross linking is done via higher radiation (gamma) dose treatment to further lower the wear rates. However, this leads to free radicals getting entrapped in PE that eventually cause accelerated wear.

Hence, the HCPE is further subjected to some processes to scavenge these free radicals which include: -

- Vitamin E soaks
- Remelting
- Annealing
