## Question

A patient with suspected XDR-TB of the spine has already been confirmed to have Rifampicin-resistant TB via Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra. According to the latest WHO diagnostic algorithm, which rapid molecular tool is now recommended to specifically identify resistance to both Fluoroquinolones and the newer Group A drugs (like Bedaquiline and Linezolid) to confirm a diagnosis of XDR-TB?

## Options

1. First-line Line Probe Assay (FL-LPA)
2. Repeat Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra on a larger tissue sample.
3. Second-line Line Probe Assay (SL-LPA) combined with target gene sequencing (e.g., mmpL5, rplC).
4. Phenotypic Drug Susceptibility Testing (DST) using the Proportion Method on LJ media.

## Correct option

**3. Second-line Line Probe Assay (SL-LPA) combined with target gene sequencing (e.g., mmpL5, rplC).**

## Answer explanation

While SL-LPA identifies Fluoroquinolone resistance, defining XDR-TB now requires confirming resistance to Group A drugs (Bedaquiline/Linezolid), which necessitates targeted molecular sequencing of specific gene regions.

Answer: 3
