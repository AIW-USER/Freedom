## Question

Which of the following accurately describes the function of osteoprotegerin?

## Options

1. Binds to RANK receptor and stimulates osteoclastic bone resorption
2. Inhibits activation of adenylyl cyclase leading to inhibition of osteoclast differentiation
3. Binds to and sequesters RANKL preventing osteoclastic differentiation
4. Binds directly to osteoclasts and prevents osteoclast differentiation
5. Binds directly to osteoblasts to stimulate production of RANKL

## Correct option

**3. Binds to and sequesters RANKL preventing osteoclastic differentiation**

## Answer explanation

Osteoprotegerin (OPG) binds to and sequesters RANK ligand (RANKL) preventing its attachment to the RANK receptor on osteoclasts and blocking osteoclastic differentiation.

Understanding the molecular pathogenesis of bone signaling is central in understanding disorders of bone metabolism. The RANKL/RANK pathway plays a critical role in this process. Bone resorption is stimulated by RANKL binding to the RANK receptor on osteoclasts leading to osteoclast differentiation/activation. There are many molecules that lead to the activation of this pathway (and thus stimulate bone resorption) through a variety of different mechanisms and they include PTH, IL-1, Vitamin D, IL-6, and in some models, prostaglandins. In contrast, there are many molecules that inhibit the activation of this pathway (and thus prevent bone resorption) and these include OPG, calcitonin, estrogen, IL-10, and TGF-B.

*Liu et al. describe the RANKL/RANK/OPG system and their role in osteoclastogenesis, bone disease and tissues beyond bone.* *They describe that disorders of the RANKL/RANK/OPG system are associated with postmenopausal osteoporosis, rheumatoid arthritis (RA), bone tumors, and certain bone metastatic tumors.* **They also noted** that the RANKL/RANK/OPG system may be a key regulator in the formation of lymph nodes and in RA, which further suggests that the immune system may interact with the RANKL/RANK/OPG system.

*Kang et al. describe the expression of OPG in osteoclasts and its biological functions during osteoclastogenesis in a rat model.* **The authors determined** the function of OPG produced by osteoclasts during osteoclastogenesis by silencing the OPG gene. **The authors noted** that OPG expression was significantly increased during the differentiation of osteoclasts and that that OPG small interfering (si)RNA treatment during osteoclastogenesis enhanced the sizes of osteoclasts, but attenuated their bone-resorbing activity. **The authors concluded** that OPG, may play an auto-regulatory role in the late phase of osteoclastogenesis through the induction of apoptosis.
