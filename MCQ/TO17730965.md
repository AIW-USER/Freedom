## Question

Swimmer’s view is used to see fractures of (spine- miscellaneous)

## Options

1. Cervicothoracic junction of spine
2. Bony avulsion of inferior labrum
3. Hill sachs lesion of humeral head
4. Atlanto-occipital junction

## Correct option

**1. Cervicothoracic junction of spine**

## Answer explanation

Cervical spine swimmer's lateral view is a modified lateral projection of the cervical spine to visualize the C7/T1 junction.

Answer: 1
