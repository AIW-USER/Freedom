Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Post TKR Infection

## Question

Sonification is a process useful in (NEW QUE)

## Options

1. Management of overuse injuries in athletes by the use of high intensity ultrasound
2. Treatment of acute muscle strains
3. Making diagnosis in prosthetic joint infections by isolating organisms that colonize the prosthesis
4. Transdermal delivery of steroids in overuse tendon injuries

## Correct option

**3. Making diagnosis in prosthetic joint infections by isolating organisms that colonize the prosthesis**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

Sonication is used for dislodging adherent microorganisms from the biofilms on surface of prosthetic joints during revision surgeries. It thus helps to isolate accurately the bacteria that have colonized the prosthesis, enabling appropriate culture sensitivity reports.

Process of sonication: Briefly, sterile Ringer solution is added to the container in a laminar airflow biosafety cabinet to cover 85–90% of the volume of a big sized prosthesis or the entire volume of small sized components. The container with the implant is vortexed for 30 sec, followed by sonication for 1 min (at a frequency of 40 kHz and power density of 0.22 W/cm2).

The sonication fluid culture proves the higher sensitivity and specificity than periprosthetic tissue culture which is also valid at the patient who has received antibiotic treatment before surgery. Discontinued antibiotic therapy within 14 days will have higher sensitivity. Sonication can also help to isolate the organism even if the patient has received antibiotics in the past. The sonication fluid in BCB also reduces the culture time because it detects all bacteria in only 5 days.
