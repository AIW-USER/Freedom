## Question

Figure A is the AP pelvis radiograph of an 11-year-old female who has a 23° right thoracic scoliotic curve. The curve magnitude at which time point is the best indicator of curve progression?

![[q2203-a-11-year-old-pelvis-xr.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Menarche
2. Peak Height Velocity
3. Risser Stage 3
4. Tanner-Whitehouse Stage 2
5. 9 years of age in females, 10 years of age in males

## Correct option

**2. Peak Height Velocity**

## Answer explanation

The magnitude of the scoliotic curve at peak height velocity (PHV), which occurs just before menarche in females and before Risser 1, is the best indicator of curve progression. Patients with >30° curves before PHV have a strong likelihood of requiring surgery.

Predicting whether or not a scoliotic curve will progress is one of the key elements in treating adolescent idiopathic scoliosis. The curve magnitude at PHV is the best predictor of curve progression; with curves >30° at that time having a high propensity for requiring surgery. PHV occurs just before menarche in females, prior to Risser stage 1, and most closely correlates with the Tanner-Whitehouse Stage III. Risser staging (Illustration A) is a measure of the progression of ossification over the iliac wings. The Tanner-Whitehouse method of measuring skeletal maturity uses the distal radial and ulnar epiphyses and the metacarpal and phalangeal epiphyses of the first, third and fifth digits for determination of skeletal age.

*Little et al reviewed the relationship of peak height velocity to other maturation indicators in patients with idiopathic scoliosis.* **They reported** that clinical height measurements can accurately predict PHV. In their study, 88 patients had a progression of their primary curves, and of these, 60 had curves of >30° at PHV. **They conclude** that knowing the timing of PHV provides valuable information in determining if a curve will progress to require arthrodesis.

*Anderson et al reviewed the normal trunk growth of boys and girls during the second decade of life.* *They reported on the normal growth in sitting height at successive levels of maturity in normal spines.* **They concluded** that the trends in this data will help evaluate children who have some interference with normal spinal growth.

Figure A is the AP pelvis radiograph of a skeletally immature, Risser Stage 0, patient. Illustration A is an AP pelvis radiograph that outlines the Risser stages.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: PHV occurs prior to menarche in girls.
Answer 3: Risser is useful for judging skeletal maturity, however, PHV occurs during Risser 0.
Answer 4: Tanner-Whitehouse stage 3 most closely correlates with PHV, not stage 2
Answer 5: Chronological age varies greatly and is not consistently associated with PHV.
