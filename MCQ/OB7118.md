## Question

Oxazolidinone antibiotics (eg, linezolid), used to treat methicillin-resistant staphylococcus aureus (MRSA), exert their effectiveness by targeting bacterial

## Options

1. ribosomal activity.
2. cell wall synthesis.
3. DNA replication.
4. DNA-dependent RNA polymerase.
5. cell membrane.

## Correct option

**1. ribosomal activity.**

## Answer explanation

Linezolid is a synthetic antibacterial agent of a class of antibiotics, the oxazolidinones, that has clinical effectiveness against MRSA. This group of antibiotics affects bacterial protein synthesis through a unique inhibition of the bacterial ribosome. Linezolid binds to a site on the 23S ribosomal RNA of the 50S subunit, preventing formation of the 70S initiation complex and interrupting the translational process. Linezolid is bacteriostatic against enterococci and staphylococci and bactericidal against streptococci. Penicillin and its derivatives inhibit cell wall synthesis. Fluoroquinolones inhibit DNA gyrase and DNA replication. DNA-dependent RNA polymerase is inhibited by rifampin. Bacterial cell membrane depolarization interrupting DNA, RNA, and protein synthesis is the mechanism of action of daptomycin.
