## Question

Ground Reaction Ankle Foot Orthoses (GRAFOs) are often used post-operatively in CP knee correction. What is their primary biomechanical function?

## Options

1. To allow for calf muscle hypertrophy
2. To prevent internal tibial torsion recurrence
3. To create an extension moment at the knee by controlling the floor reaction force
4. To provide a swing-phase dorsiflexion assist

## Correct option

**3. To create an extension moment at the knee by controlling the floor reaction force**

## Answer explanation

By holding the ankle in slight
plantarflexion or neutral, the GRAFO shifts the ground reaction force anterior
to the knee axis, promoting extension.

Answer: 3
