## Question

Osteoarthritic changes in cartilage include all except(general orthopedics- anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. Decreased proteoglycan content
2. Decreased water content
3. Decreased collagen content,
4. Decreased modulus of elasticity

## Correct option

**2. Decreased water content**

## Answer explanation

Osteoarthritic cartilage in a cartilage include- decreased proteoglycan content, increased water content and chondroitin sulfate concentration, decreased collagen content, modulus of elasticity, and keratin sulfate concentration.

Answer B
