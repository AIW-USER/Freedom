## Question

A 43-year-old male presents to the trauma bay following a MVC. Radiographs reveal that he has an acetabular fracture. Which of the following acetabular fractures is the only elementary fractures that involves both columns?

## Options

1. Associated both columns
2. Transverse
3. Transverse + Posterior wall
4. Posterior column + Posterior wall
5. T-type

## Correct option

**2. Transverse**

## Answer explanation

The transverse fracture pattern is the only elementary fracture pattern that involves both columns.

Acetabulum fractures are pelvis fractures that involve the articular surface of the hip joint and may involve one or two columns, one or two walls, or the roof within the pelvis. Diagnosis can be made radiographically with dedicated pelvis radiographs (including Judet views) but frequently requires a CT of the pelvis for surgical planning. The Judet and Letournel classification is used to describe these fractures. There are 5 elementary fractures (anterior wall, anterior column, posterior wall, posterior column, and transverse) and 5 associated fractures (associated both column, transverse-posterior wall, T-type, posterior wall-posterior column, and anterior column-posterior hemitransverse). A transverse fracture is the only elementary fracture that involves both columns.

Beaulé et al. assessed the interobserver and intraobserver reliability of Letournel's acetabular fracture classification and the effect of computed tomography on its reliability. They report that Letournel's acetabular classification with the use of plain radiographs with or without supplemental computed tomography scans has substantial reliability when used by surgeons who have been taught how to interpret the images or by those who treat acetabular fractures on a regular basis. They concluded that the value of computed tomography scans in the evaluation of acetabular fractures has been well established for the identification of loose bodies and articular impaction; however, they do not appear to be essential for the classification of acetabular fractures.

*Letournel et al. reviewed displaced acetabular fractures that were treated surgically.* **They report** that the type of fracture dictates the choice of approach, but the indication for operative treatment as well as the result of acetabular fracture treatment is the degree of congruence achieved between the acetabulum and the femoral head. **They concluded** that congruency should try to be obtained whenever possible to optimize outcomes.

Incorrect Answer:
Answer 1: Associated both column fractures is an associated fracture that is characterized by dissociation of the articular surface from the innominate bone
Answer 3: Transverse + Posterior wall is the most common associated fracture
Answer 4: Posterior column + Posterior wall is the only associated fracture that does not involve both columns
Answer 5: T-type is an associated fracture
