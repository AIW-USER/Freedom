## Question

A Rectus Femoris Transfer (RFT) is specifically indicated for 'stiff-knee gait.' What is the primary objective of this procedure during the swing phase?

## Options

1. To improve peak knee flexion by removing a swing-phase restraint
2. To stabilize the knee against buckle during terminal swing
3. To increase the knee extension moment in late swing
4. To convert the rectus femoris into a pure hip extensor

## Correct option

**1. To improve peak knee flexion by removing a swing-phase restraint**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 1
