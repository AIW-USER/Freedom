Chapter: Sports Medicine MCQs — Drugs And Dopping In Sports

## Question

You are a doping control officer for world tennis tournament. During doping control procedure which among the following is not done by an athlete?

## Options

1. Selection of collection vessels
2. Sample division
3. Selection of doping control kit
4. Measuring Specific Gravity of Urine

## Correct option

**4. Measuring Specific Gravity of Urine**

## Answer explanation

Specific gravity of urine is measured by doping control officer (DCO).

Doping control procedure

- notification – by chaperone

2. reporting to the doping control station- by athlete

- selection of collection vessels by athlete
- provision of sample – by DCO/DCA same gender

4. selection of doping control kit by athlete

- sample division by athlete
- sealing of sample by athlete

6. measuring specific gravity by DCO

- documentation by DCO
- analysis by WADA accredited laboratory

DCO- doping control officer

- Ensuring appropriate number of supplies (equipment and paperwork) for sample collection. Three A&B sample collection kits and three urine collection vessels per sample are required.
- Setting-up the Doping Control Station (DCS)

- Ensuring that each athlete is properly notified and chaperoned to the DCS
- Ensuring that each sample is properly collected, identified and sealed, and that all samples have been properly stored and dispatched to a WADA-accredited Laboratory.

BCO- blood control officer

- The BCO will prepare the athlete for the blood collection, answer any relevant questions from the athlete, collect the blood sample(s), and advise the athlete of aftercare procedure
- Where blood is collected, the DCO is still responsible for the overall sample collection session with the BCO having specific responsibility for venipuncture and athlete care (i.e., first aid if needed).
- Minimum amount of blood for serum sample analysis to be drawn from the Athlete is 20 ml (10 ml for each tube) (range 10-15 ml)

Chaperone

The individual who is suitably trained and authorized by the SCA to carry out specific duties, including one or more of the following:

- notification of the athlete selected for sample collection
- accompanying and observing the athlete until arrival at the DCS
- accompanying and/or observing athletes who are present in the DCS
- witnessing and verifying the provision of a urine sample where the training specifically qualifies them to do so.

Answer: 4
