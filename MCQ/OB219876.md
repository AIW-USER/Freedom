## Question

A 46-year-old female with a history of diabetes presents to the clinic today for evaluation of isolated right knee lateral compartment osteoarthritis. She has previously attempted physical therapy, which had a limited impact on her symptoms. She is interested in pursuing other nonoperative means. The treatment decided upon results in the attenuation of cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2) and nuclear factor-kappa B (NF-kB) pathways for therapeutic relief, as well as increasing hyaluronic acid production. Which of the following therapies correlates with these mechanisms of action?

## Options

1. non-steroidal anti-inflammatories
2. corticosteroids
3. hyaluronic acid
4. platelet-rich plasma
5. opioids

## Correct option

**4. platelet-rich plasma**

## Answer explanation

The therapy that has been found to attenuate COX-2 and NF-κB pathways, as well as increase hyaluronic acid production in knee osteoarthritis (OA) is platelet-rich plasma (PRP; Answer 4).

Platelet-rich plasma, the most recognizable orthobiologic agent, continues to receive significant attention for its impact on combating/slowing OA, soft tissue/tendon/ligament healing, and fracture healing. Studies have repeatedly delineated its physiological impact and have discovered the therapy to downregulate COX-2 and nuclear factor-kappa B (NF-κB) pathways, while increasing hyaluronic acid production by synoviocytes, decreasing cartilage degradation and increasing chondrocyte proliferation in knee OA. Despite these proposed mechanisms, the existing literature remains unable to fully characterize its impact clinically.

*Su et al. reviewed the current status of various cell-based orthobiologic agents, including PRP and mesenchymal stromal cells (MSCs).* PRP is believed to ameliorate symptoms stemming from OA by downregulating the COX-2 and NF-κB pathways while also promoting chondrocyte proliferation and hyaluronic acid secretion from synoviocytes. Despite the therapies' purported impact on various inflammatory mechanisms, its impact clinically has yet to be fully elucidated, which can be partially attributed to the lack of uniform methodology regarding PRP preparations.

*Chahla et al. similarly review the number of orthobiologic therapies currently available as they relate to the knee and elbow.* *The authors cite several growth factors present within PRP, which in turn upregulate mechanisms resulting in cell proliferation, cell migration, angiogenesis, and extracellular matrix production.* **The authors note** the current amalgamation of literature surrounding PRP's clinical effect on the knee is controversial at best, despite positive findings within laboratory and animal studies.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Although NSAIDs primarily inhibit COX-1 and COX-2 pathways, preventing the synthesis and release of prostaglandins, and do modulate the NF-κB pathway, they do not upregulate hyaluronic acid production.
Answer 2: Corticosteroids impact several mechanisms, most notably by acting directly on nuclear steroid receptors.
Answer 3: Hyaluronic acid is a glycosaminoglycan naturally produced within the body, but is harvested from rooster combs or via bacterial fermentation and injected into the knee to act as a viscous lubricant.
Answer 5: Opioids exert their function by acting principally on central and peripheral nerve system opioid receptors, inhibiting the release of neurotransmitters substance P and glutamate.
