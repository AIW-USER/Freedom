## Question

Most common organism causing foot deformity given in the picture below is

![[TO17129494-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Actinomadura madurae
2. Pseudallescheria boydii
3. Actinomadura pelletieri
4. Madurella mycetomatis

## Correct option

**1. Actinomadura madurae**

## Answer explanation

Mycetoma (Madura foot) is a chronic granulomatous infection of skin and subcutaneous tissues caused by fungi (eumycetoma) or bacteria (actinomycetes). Bacteria account for about 60% of cases.
Common organisms causing Madura foot are as follows:
Actinomycetes:
Actinomadura madurae
Actinomadura pelletieri
Streptomyces somaliensis
Nocardia species
Eumycetes:
Pseudallescheria boydii
Madurella mycetomatis

Answer: 1
