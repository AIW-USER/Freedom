## Question

A 3-day-old female exhibits limited use of her right arm following birth. Figure A exhibits her right upper extremity radiograph. If nonoperative treatment is provided, what is the expected prognosis?

![[q5283-a-figure-a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Limited range of motion, malunion, need for corrective osteotomy
2. High risk of radial nerve palsy, minimal deformity, no need for corrective osteotomy
3. Reliable healing with callus by 2 weeks, complete remodeling within 6 months
4. High risk of asymptomatic fibrous nonunion
5. Cubitus varus with limb length discrepancy

## Correct option

**3. Reliable healing with callus by 2 weeks, complete remodeling within 6 months**

## Answer explanation

Humeral shaft fractures can occur during delivery. However, in patients this young, typically, a gentle swath or even using a safety pin to pin the arm to the contralateral shoulder can facilitate comfort during an uneventful healing period, followed by complete remodeling.

The majority of humeral shaft fractures in infants, toddlers, and most children with open physes go onto uneventful healing and remodeling, not requiring operative intervention. The older the children get, however, temporary immobilization may be required.

*Caviglia et al. divide treatment for humeral shaft fractures into age groups: less than a week old, less than 3-years-old, 3-10-years-old and older than 10 years old.* Emphasis is placed on those under 3, where abuse is commonly associated and must be ruled out. **The authors note** that while the majority go on to uneventful union, they warn that proximal third fractures as well as the older than 10-years-old patients are more prone to deformity and must be watched closely.

Figure A exhibits the typical, transverse mid-shaft humerus fracture typically seen during difficult deliveries.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1,2,4,5: This early in age, nearly all humeral shaft fractures go onto uneventful healing within 2 weeks and complete remodeling by 6 months. There is no need for the interventions noted in these answer choices.
