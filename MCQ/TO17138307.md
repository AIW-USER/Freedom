Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Arthrogryposis Multiplex Congenital

## Question

Most common deformity seen in Classical Arthrogryposis multiplex congenita?

## Options

1. Ankle and foot-based deformity – CVT
2. Knee based deformity – Flexion contracture
3. Ankle and foot-based deformity – Talipes Equinovarus
4. Hand based deformity – Flexion contracture of IP joint

## Correct option

**3. Ankle and foot-based deformity – Talipes Equinovarus**

## Answer explanation

Most common deformity seen in AMC is an Ankle and foot-based deformity that is Talipes Equinovarus

Answer: 3
