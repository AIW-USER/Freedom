## Question

Minimum bone marrow density required for proximal femoral implant fixation is (in mg/cm3) [General Ortho.-Anatomy, Basic Science and Pathology]

## Options

1. 250
2. 350
3. 200
4. 300

## Correct option

**1. 250**

## Answer explanation

The risk of implant fixation failure is directly related to bone density. The minimum BMD below which fixation is expected to fail has been defined for several osteoporotic fracture locations.

Location
BMD for Implant Stability (mg/cm3)

Proximal femur
250

Proximal humerus
95

Vertebral body (pedicle)
95–150

Vertebral body
220

 Answer: A
