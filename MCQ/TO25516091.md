Chapter: Metabolic Bone Disorder MCQs — Rickets

## Question

A 35-year-old female presents with recent onset of severe bone pain, muscle weakness, and radiographic evidence of pseudofractures. Laboratory evaluation reveals hypophosphatemia, renal phosphate wasting, and elevated intact FGF23. Genetic testing confirms Autosomal Dominant Hypophosphatemic Rickets (ADHR). Which of the following systemic conditions is uniquely recognized as a trigger for the phenotypic expression of this specific disorder?

## Options

1. Primary hyperparathyroidism
2. Vitamin C deficiency
3. Chronic kidney disease
4. Iron deficiency

## Correct option

**4. Iron deficiency**

## Answer explanation

ADHR involves a mutation at the FGF23 cleavage site. Iron deficiency upregulates FGF23 transcription. In ADHR, the mutant FGF23 resists normal cleavage, leading to a sudden accumulation of intact, active FGF23 and the delayed onset of clinical symptoms.

Answer: 4
