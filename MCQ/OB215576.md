## Question

An inherited mutation of the p53 tumor suppressor gene is the primary cause of which of the following?

## Options

1. Multiple Myeloma
2. Maffucci syndrome
3. Li-Fraumeni syndrome
4. Fibrous dysplasia
5. McCune Albright syndrome

## Correct option

**3. Li-Fraumeni syndrome**

## Answer explanation

Li-Fraumeni syndrome is associated with a germ-line deactivating mutation of the p53 tumor suppressor gene.

Li-Fraumeni is a hereditary cancer predisposition syndrome caused by a germ-line mutation in the p53 gene. Given this mutation, patients are at risk for a spectrum of primary cancers including osteosarcoma, soft tissue sarcomas, acute leukemia, breast cancer, brain cancer and colon cancer. This condition primarily has an autosomal dominant heritance pattern, and often affects several members of a single family. Osteosarcoma is the second most common cancer identified in these patients (20%), secondary only to breast cancer (25%).

*Huby et al review the management of bone lesions in patients with Li-Fraumeni syndrome.* **They report** that sarcomas account for more than 30% of malignant tumors found in these pediatric patients. **They conclude** that whole-body MRI be used as a screening tool for patients with germline mutations of p53.

*Wu et al review the clinical significance of the p53 protein in patients with osteosarcoma.* **They report** that p53 expression was significantly lower in osteogenic osteosarcoma than in nonosteogenic osteosarcoma. P53 expression was further associated with a poor overall prognosis. **They conclude** that p53 expression may be a useful prognostic biomarker for overall survival in patients with osteosarcoma.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: The specific etiology of multiple myeloma is poorly understood, but has not been linked to a p53 mutation.
Answer 2: Maffucci syndrome is caused by a mutation in the IDH-1 and IDH-2 genes.
Answer 4: Fibrous dysplasia is associated with an activating mutation of the GS alpha protein (GNAS).
Answer 5: McCune Albright syndrome is caused by a mutation in the GNAS1 gene.
