## Question

Which of the following is seen in a child with adolescent blount’s disease?

## Options

1. Bilaterality
2. Self resolution
3. Associated distal femoral varus
4. Bracing is first line of management

## Correct option

**3. Associated distal femoral varus**

## Answer explanation

Adolescent blount’s disease though having similar affection of the proximal tibia to that of an infantile blount’s disease, does have significant differences.
