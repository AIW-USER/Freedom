## Question

316L material is used in orthopedic implants. What does it make up of? (general orthopedics- material properties and orthopedic implants)

## Options

1. Iron, carbon, lead, nickel.
2. Iron, carbon, chromium, nickel.
3. Iron, carbon, Cobalt, chromium
4. Iron, carbon, chromium, nickel, molybdenum.

## Correct option

**4. Iron, carbon, chromium, nickel, molybdenum.**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: 316 L is a type of stainless steel which contains Iron, Carbon, Chromium, Nickel and Molybdenum.

L means low carbon

Chromium – protect against rusting by forming a layer of Chromium oxide. Nickel – facilitates manufacturing process

Molybdenum- prevents degradation of chromium oxide layer

Answer: 4
