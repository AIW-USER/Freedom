## Question

When comparing the overall outcomes of surgical versus nonsurgical treatment of stable thoracolumbar burst fractures in patients without neurologic injury, 5 years following injury, the principle differences lie in

## Options

1. fracture kyphosis.
2. reduction of retropulsed bone.
3. pain reduction.
4. incidence of complications.
5. return to work.

## Correct option

**4. incidence of complications.**

## Answer explanation

When patients are compared at 5 years follow-up, there are no statistically significant differences between the two groups with respect to kyphosis, the degree of retropulsed bone resorption, pain and function levels, or the ability to return to work. Nonsurgical management of stable neurologically intact burst fractures has a very low incidence of complications.
