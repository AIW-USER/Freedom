Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Ligament Balancing in TKR

## Question

During TKR after bone cuts the surgeon finds loose flexion gap and balanced extension gap. All are recommended option to tackle this situation except (FNB Pattern Paper 2014)

## Options

1. Cut additional distal femur and use thicker tibial spacer
2. Increase size of femoral component and use same size tibial spacer
3. Recut tibia with less posterior slope and use thicker tibial spacer
4. Down size femoral component and use thicker tibial spacer

## Correct option

**4. Down size femoral component and use thicker tibial spacer**

## Answer explanation

In gap balancing, ligaments are released before femoral bone cuts are made.

Steps of gap balancing:

-Remove all osteophytes

- Precise tibial cut

- Ligaments are released before femoral cut

 Remember:

- Distal femoral cut affects only extension gap

- Posterior femoral cut affects flexion gap

- Tibial cut affects both flexion and extension cut

- Increasing/decreasing the size of the femoral component only changes the AP diameter and therefore affects the flexion gap only.

Flexion and extension gaps should be rectangular and equal to each other.
