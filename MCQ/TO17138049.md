## Question

The following muscles are supplied by facial nerve, except:

## Options

1. Orbicularis oris
2. Orbicularis ocularis
3. Lateral pterygoid
4. Buccinator

## Correct option

**3. Lateral pterygoid**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

The facial nerve supplies the muscles of facial expression. These are to be remembered as the muscles around the three orifices of the face:
![[TO17138049-1.jpg]]

- Eyes — orbicularis ocularis
- Nostrils - levator nasi, depressor nasi, nasalis
- Mouth - orbicularis oris, labial muscles, mentalis, risorius, zygomaticus muscles, buccinator

The other muscles supplied are frontalis and platysma.

The lateral pterygoid is a muscle of mastication (chewing). There are four muscles of mastication:

The masseter, temporalis, medial and lateral pterygoid. All the muscles of mastication are supplied by the trigeminal nerve.

Also remember: The trigeminal nerve is a mixed nerve and NOT a sensory nerve.

The motor component of trigeminal nerve is supplied via MANDIBULAR BRANCH (V3) of the trigeminal nerve.
