## Question

Figure 58 shows the radiograph of a 5-year-old boy who sustained an injury to his left proximal humerus. Based on these radiographic findings, what is the next most appropriate step in management?

![[q6526-a-58.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Application of a shoulder immobilizer
2. Open biopsy, curettage, and bone grafting
3. Bone and CT scans of the chest
4. MRI of the lesion
5. Oncology consultation

## Correct option

**1. Application of a shoulder immobilizer**

## Answer explanation

The radiograph shows a pathologic fracture through a unicameral bone cyst of the proximal humerus. There is a fallen flake sign representing a portion of the cortical bone that has been fractured into the cavity of the cyst. This location and radiographic appearance are classic for a cyst. Treatment should consist of managing the fracture according to standard fracture protocols. A fracture will occasionally result in healing of the cyst. Persistent, large cysts can be treated by a variety of methods after the fracture has healed.
