## Question

A 45-year-old male presents with right foot pain that has progressively worsened since he fell down a flight of stairs. At the time of injury, he remembers having a large bruise on the plantar surface of his foot, but was diagnosed with an ankle sprain. His foot has never felt the same since the injury. He now has large prominence over the dorsal aspect of his midfoot. Radiographs reveal midfoot arthritis with a large dorsal osteophyte. A decision is made to perform an arthrodesis, which will include iliac crest autograft for the procedure. What is the purpose for the use of this graft?

## Options

1. Reduced surgical cost
2. Need for fewer implants
3. Improved fusion rate
4. Improved midfoot alignment at long term follow-up
5. Decreased donor site morbidity

## Correct option

**3. Improved fusion rate**

## Answer explanation

The use of iliac crest autograft has been associated with improved fusion rates for midfoot arthrodesis.

Midfoot arthritis is most often the result of post-traumatic articular damage. Cases of misdiagnosed Lisfranc injuries can lead to progressive flat foot deformity and midfoot arthrosis. In these instances, salvage treatment is limited to midfoot arthrodesis to realign and stabilize the deformity. The use of autograft has been associated with improved fusion rates in these patients.

*Buda et al. retrospectively reviewed 189 patients undergoing tarsometatarsal fusion and evaluated fusion rate effects influenced by fixation and bone graft.* **They reported** an overall nonunion rate of 11.4% with plate fixation alone, with perioperative smoking, and postoperative nonanatomic alignment being associated negative risk factors. **They also concluded** autograft use significantly decreased the risk of nonunion.

*Patel et al. reviewed the diagnosis and treatment of midfoot arthritis.* *The authors mentioned post-traumatic arthritis remains the most common etiology for midfoot arthrosis.* **They stated** medial midfoot fusion with mobility preservation of the lateral midfoot remains the goal of surgical treatment.

Incorrect answers
Answer 1: Use of iliac crest autograft is not associated with decreased surgical costs.
Answer 2: Iliac crest autograft included in an arthrodesis construct does not have an effect on the needed number of implants or fixation.
Answer 4: Midfoot alignment following arthrodesis is dependent on the surgical technique and rigidity of the fixation used. The choice of bone graft does not impact the long-term alignment.
Answer 5: Harvesting autograft from the iliac crest is associated with significant donor pain.
