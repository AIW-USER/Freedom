## Question

Intramembranous bone formation does not occur in which of the following?

## Options

1. Compression plating
2. Distraction osteogenesis
3. Embryonic flat bone formation
4. Callus formation during fracture healing

## Correct option

**4. Callus formation during fracture healing**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Endochondral bone formation or ossification is bone formation on a cartilage model. It is seen in

- Longitudinal Physeal growth
- Embryonic long bone formation
- Non-rigid fracture healing (secondary healing)
- Epiphyseal secondary center of ossification formation
- Callus formation during fracture healing
- Degenerating cartilage of osteoarthritis, Calcifying cartilage tumors, and
- Bone formed with use of demineralized bone matrix.

Examples of intramembranous ossification

- Embryonic flat bone formation (skull, maxilla, mandible, pelvis, clavicle, subperiosteal surface of long bone)
- Distraction osteogenesis bone formation
- Fracture healing with rigid fixation (compression plate)
- Perichondrial bone formation
- One component of healing with intramedullary nailing
