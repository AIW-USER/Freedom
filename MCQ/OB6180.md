## Question

The attachments of the transverse carpal ligament include which of the following structures?

## Options

1. Scaphoid and the ulna
2. Trapezium and the hook of the hamate
3. Trapezium and the triquetrum
4. Trapezoid and the hook of the hamate
5. Trapezoid and the pisiform

## Correct option

**2. Trapezium and the hook of the hamate**

## Answer explanation

The transverse carpal ligament is the volar boundary of the carpal tunnel. It attaches to the scaphoid and trapezium radially and the pisiform and the hook of the hamate ulnarly. The ulna and trapezoid do not receive attachments of the transverse carpal ligament.
