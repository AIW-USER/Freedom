## Question

A study is performed comparing the mean operative times of total knee arthroplasty performed with traditional instrumentation versus robotic-assisted. The data is found to be normally distributed. Which of the following tests is most appropriate when comparing these two means?

## Options

1. Student t-test
2. Analysis of variance (ANOVA)
3. Mann-Whitney U Test
4. Chi-squared
5. Fisher's exact test

## Correct option

**1. Student t-test**

## Answer explanation

The best test for comparing normally distributed continuous data in two groups is the Student t-test.

Experimental data can be classified into two types: continuous and discrete data. Continuous data consists of measurements over a continuous range such that they can be graphed on a curve. Examples include height, weight, time, and age. When comparing means of two groups that follow a normal distribution, one can utilize a student t-test. This may be further broken down into an independent t-test or a dependent t-test depending on if the groups/samples are independent or related, respectively.

*Kocher et al. review basic clinical epidemiology and biostatistics relevant to orthopedic surgery.* Amongst other things, the authors describe that data can be summarized in terms of measures of central tendencies, such as mean, median, and mode, and in terms of measures of dispersion, such as range, standard deviation, and percentiles.

*Kuhn et al. describe the difference between continuous and discrete data.* *The authors further characterize the Chi-squared test and Fisher's exact test and the utility of these tests in evaluating discrete data.* **They state** the Chi-square test should be used when there are at least 50 occurrences among all groups and at least 10 occurrences per group.

Incorrect answers:
Answer 2: Analysis of variance (ANOVA) is used to evaluate means of parametric data between three or more groups when the data is normally distributed.
Answer 3: The Mann-Whitney U test is used to compare the means of two independent groups with non-parametric data.
Answer 4: The Chi-square test is used to compare proportions for categorical or ordinal data (non-parametric).
Answer 5: The Fisher's exact test is used to compare proportions for non-parametric data when the expected frequency is small (less than five per group).
