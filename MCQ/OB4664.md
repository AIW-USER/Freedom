## Question

A 4-year-old girl is involved in a tobogganing accident. She has a fluctuating level of consciousness and requires urgent transport to the local pediatric trauma center. When transporting a pediatric trauma patients, which of the following factors should be considered?

## Options

1. A pelvic binder is required in all pediatric trauma patients
2. Intraosseous cannulation is the first choice for access in patients with hypovolemia
3. Modified spinal boards are required for pediatric trauma patients with a suspected spine injury
4. Intubation is required in pediatric patients when Glosgow Coma Score <12
5. Nasal cannula airways are required in pediatric patients with head injuries

## Correct option

**3. Modified spinal boards are required for pediatric trauma patients with a suspected spine injury**

## Answer explanation

Young children have a disproportionate head and chest, forcing the neck into flexion if placed on a standard spine board. To avoid excessive flexion, pediatric trauma patients less than 8 years of age with a suspected spine injury should be immobilized on a modified spine board that either raises the chest or lowers the occiput.

Trauma is the most common cause of death in children. Pediatric polytrauma has a mortality rate of 20%. Spine injuries are associated with the highest morbidity and mortality among musculoskeletal injuries. It is essential to immobilize the spine immediately and initiate rapid evaluation and management according to ATLS protocol.

*Schouten et al. discussed the management of spine-injured patient.* Initial management should focus on evaluation and management of (A) Airway, (B) Breathing, and (C) Circulation according to ATLS protocol. An accurate, complete spine and neurologic assessment should be performed once the ABCs are secured.

*Kay and Skaggs reviewed the management of pediatric polytrauma.* **They highlight** the need for thorough evaluation for neurological and musculoskeletal injuries, and orthopaedic care to facilitate early mobilization.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Pelvic binders are only required if there is a suspected pelvic injury. They are not routinely placed in pediatric patients.
Answer 2: Intraosseous cannulation is required when there is peripheral access difficulties or hemodynamic instability. They are not routinely placed in pediatric trauma patients.
Answer 4: Intubation is only required when pediatric patients have airway issues or cannot protect their airway. As a general rule, intubation is required when GCS<8.
Answer 5:Nasal cannula airways should not be used in patients with head injuries.
