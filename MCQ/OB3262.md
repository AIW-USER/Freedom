## Question

A 24-year-old female presents with a transverse midshaft humerus fracture. Which of the following implants would create the most compression on both the far and near cortices?

## Options

1. Compression plate with concave bend (ends bowed towards bone)
2. Large fragment locking plate with 3 bicortical locking screws proximal and distal to the fracture
3. Dynamically locked intramedullary nail
4. Compression plate with convex bend (ends bowed away from the bone)
5. Sarmiento style fracture brace

## Correct option

**1. Compression plate with concave bend (ends bowed towards bone)**

## Answer explanation

Placing a concave bend in the plate during compressive plating results in compressive forces at both the near and far cortices (Illustration A). As originally described in the AO manual of fracture fixation, this technical pearl helps to ensure that osteosynthetic forces occur on both sides of the fracture. This technique is most useful in transverse fractures and can be used in any transverse fracture, not just humeral shaft fractures.
