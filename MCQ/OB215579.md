## Question

Which of the following is true as it relates to thromboembolic events in patient with Factor V Leiden mutations undergoing THA?

## Options

1. Increased risk of developing a DVT in the first 3 months postoperatively
2. Increased risk of developing a DVT in the first 2 weeks postoperatively
3. Increased risk of developing a PE in the first 7 days postoperatively
4. Increased risk of developing a PE in the first 30 days postoperatively
5. No increased risk of developing a PE or DVT postoperatively

## Correct option

**5. No increased risk of developing a PE or DVT postoperatively**

## Answer explanation

Factor V Leiden mutations result in a hypercoagulable state, however, an increased risk of DVT and/or PE has not been reported following arthroplasty procedures.

Normal factor V serves as a cofactor to Factor Xa, and together they produce thrombin and drive coagulation. Protein C helps regulate this process by inhibiting factor V to prevent hypercoagulability. The mutation seen in patients with Factor V Leiden prevents factor V from being inhibited by protein C and thus results in a hypercoagulable state. Despite this mechanism, no increased risk of DVT or PE has been reported in these patients following THA compared to normal age matched controls.

*Woolson et al. review the risk of proximal venous thrombosis after THA in patients with Factor V Leiden mutations.* **They report** no statistical difference in the rate of thromboembolic events in patients with Factor V Leiden compared to those without the mutation following THA. **They conclude** that Factor V Leiden is not associated with DVT prophylaxis failure in patients undergoing THA.

*Bowler et al review the prevalence and thromboembolic complications in patients with Factor V Leiden mutations undergoing THA in Ireland.* Half of the patients developing a DVT/PE in this study had previous history of thromboembolic events, however, none had Factor V mutations. **They conclude** that the prevalence of the mutation in 2% but this was no a risk factor for acute symptomatic DVT/PE following THA.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1-4: No increased risk of postoperative DVT/PE has been reported in patients with Factor V Leiden mutations compared to aged matched controls undergoing THA.
