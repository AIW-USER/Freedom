Chapter: Musculoskeletal Infections MCQs — Acute and Chronic Osteomyelitis

## Question

A 15 years old patient comes with history of high-grade intermittent fever since a month. ESR, CRP are raised. Swelling and redness are present clinically. X ray picture shows bone destruction with periosteal reaction in lower diaphyseal femur. Investigation that will be most accurate in clinching the diagnosis (FNB 2022)

## Options

1. Indium labelled bone scan
2. Bone Biopsy
3. MRI
4. Serum LDH levels

## Correct option

**2. Bone Biopsy**

## Answer explanation

The clinical history raises the possibility of Hematogenous Osteomyelitis but it is well known that Ewing sarcoma can have raised inflammatory markers and can well simulate Osteomyelitis. Hence it becomes imperative to investigate such patients to reach a conclusion.

In above scenario, the next best step would be MRI but a conclusive confirmatory investigation would be taken as the bone biopsy.

Ans: B
