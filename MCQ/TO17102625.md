## Question

In osseointegration ideal micomotion between bone and implant is

## Options

1. > 150 micrometer
2. between 40 and 150 micro meter
3. <20 micro meter
4. No micromotion

## Correct option

**3. <20 micro meter**

## Answer explanation

In 1981, on the basis of human retrieval studies, Albrektsson et al. described ‘‘osseointegration’’ as the attachment of lamellar bone to implants without intervening fibrous tissue. Both animal studies and human retrieval analyses of implants have led to a better understanding of this process, which takes approximately four to twelve weeks after implantation and may continue for up to three years. Adequate osseous contact and firm fixation of the implant minimize micro motion. Micro motion of >150 micro meter leads to fibrous tissue formation, between 40 and 150 micro meter leads to a combination of bone and fibrous tissue formation, and fibrous tissue formation, and <20 micro meter results in predominantly bone formation.

Answer : C
