## Question

Ideal fracture for MIPO is (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Comminuted metaphyseal fracture of long bones
2. Comminuted diaphyseal fracture with skin abrasions
3. Compound metaphyseal fracture
4. Comminuted fracture in a diabetic patient

## Correct option

**1. Comminuted metaphyseal fracture of long bones**

## Answer explanation

MIPO is ideal for comminuted metaphyseal fractures of long bones.

Answer: 1
