Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Dupuytren’s Contracture

## Question

Which structure is usually spared in Dupuytren disease?

## Options

1. Neurovascular bundle
2. Grayson ligament
3. Clelend ligament
4. Lateral digital sheet

## Correct option

**3. Clelend ligament**

## Answer explanation

Longitudinally oriented fascia located dorsal to the neurovascular bundle, (the retrovascular cord),

The pretendinous cord attached to the distal palmar crease skin, base of the proximal phalanx, or the tendon sheath at this level, or it may extend to attach to the flexor tendon sheath over the middle phalanx or the skin in this area.

A spiral cord occurs when four normally existing structures (pretendinous band, spiral band, lateral digital sheet, and Grayson ligament) become diseased. The spiral cord runs dorsal to the neurovascular bundle proximally and volar to it distally. When the spiral cord is contracted, the neurovascular bundle is drawn toward the midline of the finger.

The Cleland ligament generally is believed to be spared.

Answer : c
