## Question

A 12-year-old soccer player was kicked in the left knee while his foot was stuck in the mud. He reported immediate pain and swelling. AP and lateral radiographs are shown in Figures 13a and 13b. Treatment should consist of

## Options

1. open reduction and internal fixation of the patella.
2. open reduction and internal fixation of the tibial tubercle.
3. repair of the patella tendon.
4. repair of the quadriceps tendon.
5. extension casting in a cylinder cast.

## Correct option

**1. open reduction and internal fixation of the patella.**

## Answer explanation

The patient has a patella sleeve fracture. This fracture is unique to skeletally immature children and represents a sleeve of articular cartilage that is pulled off of the patella. This sleeve of cartilage may detach with a fragment of bone (as seen in this patient) or without an osseous fragment. Displaced fractures require open reduction and internal fixation, which is typically done using either large nonabsorbable sutures passed through drill holes in the patella, or a tension band wire technique.
