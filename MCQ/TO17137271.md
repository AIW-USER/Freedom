Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Pediatric Lateral and Medial Condyle Fracture

## Question

Tardy ulnar nerve palsy is seen after [FNB-2014]

## Options

1. Nonunion fracture lateral condyle
2. Malunited lateral condyle fracture
3. Elbow dislocation
4. Gunstock deformity

## Correct option

**1. Nonunion fracture lateral condyle**

## Answer explanation

Typically, tardy ulnar nerve palsy occurs as a consequence of non-union of lateral condyle fracture in a child resulting in cubitus valgus deformity which ultimately is the cause of ulnar nerve palsy. However tardy ulnar nerve palsy may also develop after post traumatic cubitus varus deformity due to dislocation of the nerve anterior to the medial epicondyle and entrapment of the nerve by the fibrous band of the flexor carpi ulnar is muscle. Overall nonunion lateral condyle fracture is the most common cause of tardy ulnar nerve palsy.

Answer: 1
