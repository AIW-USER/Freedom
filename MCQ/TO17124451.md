## Question

Lateral quadruple complex consists of all except (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Iliotibial tract
2. Fabellofibular ligament
3. Popliteus
4. Lateral collateral ligament

## Correct option

**2. Fabellofibular ligament**

## Answer explanation

Lateral quadruple complex is made up of

- Iliotibial tract b. Lateral collateral ligament c. Popliteus tendon d. Biceps muscle.

Answer: 2
