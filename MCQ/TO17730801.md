## Question

A young 35 years male comes to you with complaints of wrist pain and reduced strength of grip. He gives the history of fall onto outstretched hands. On examination pain is noted on the radial side of the wrist with tenderness around the lister tubercle. His X-ray PA view shows scapholunate distance 3.7 mm. The lateral x-ray shows a radio-lunate angle as 25 degrees in extension. What is the diagnosis? (regional orthopedics- hand and wrist injuries- scapholunate instability)

## Options

1. Scapholunate dissociation without DISI
2. VISI
3. Scapholunate instability with DISI
4. SLAC

## Correct option

**3. Scapholunate instability with DISI**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 3
