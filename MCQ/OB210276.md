## Question

Which arrow(s) in Figure A demonstrate an appropriate path to perform a biopsy of this patient's distal femur mass?

![[q210276-a-biopsy.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. A only
2. B only
3. C only
4. D only
5. A or B

## Correct option

**5. A or B**

## Answer explanation

This patient's distal femur can be biopsied through a medial or lateral intramuscular approach with minimal morbidity.

Biopsy of this patient should be completed by the final treating surgeon. Biopsy of the distal femur from either a medial or lateral approach is acceptable. At the time of the definitive surgery, the entire biopsy tract should be resected in continuity with the tumor, therefore, the surgeon should decide if he/she would prefer to resect from a medial or lateral approach prior to biopsy. As seen in Illustration A (different patient), the entire biopsy tract should be removed as the malignant tumor can grow in the tract. Intermuscular or internervous planes should be avoided during biopsies of malignant tumors due to the potential to contaminate multiple compartments.

*Mankin et al. reported on the results of 597 patients at multiple institutions that underwent biopsy at a referring institution instead of the treating center and* **found** a diagnostic error of 17.8%. The study also found that there was a 19.3% rate of the treating surgeon needing to do a different, more complex procedure, or requiring additional chemotherapy or radiation due to an incorrect biopsy at a referring institution.

*Traina et al. performed a systematic literature review of 21 articles discussing the topic of biopsy of bone and soft-tissue primary tumors.* **They found** that core needle biopsy had greater diagnostic accuracy than fine needle aspiration. Additionally, **they found** biopsies of myxoid, infection, and round cell histologies generally have the least accuracy.

Figure A is an axial MRI of a distal femur osteosarcoma. Illustration A is an axial MRI post-chemotherapy and pre-surgical demonstrating osteosarcoma growing in the contaminated biopsy tract.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 3: The extensor mechanism should be avoided during biopsy due to the need for future resection of the biopsy tract.
Answer 4: Intermuscular and internervous planes should be avoided due to the risk of contaminating both compartments requiring resection.
