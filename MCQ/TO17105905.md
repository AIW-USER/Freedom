## Question

In total knee arthroplasty, the extension gap refers to

## Options

1. The distance between the medial and lateral femoral condyles
2. The distance between the proximal tibial cut and the anterior femoral cut
3. The distance between the distal femoral cut and the proximal tibial cut
4. The distance between the posterior femoral Cut and the proximal tibial Cut

## Correct option

**3. The distance between the distal femoral cut and the proximal tibial cut**

## Answer explanation

The flexion gap is the distance between the posterior femoral cut and the proximal tibial cut (measured in flexion). The extension gap is the distance between the distal femoral cut and the proximal tibial cut (measured in extension).

Answer: 3
