Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Chondroblastoma

## Question

Extensive edema of the bone marrow and/or soft tissues surrounding the lesion is a distinguished MRI feature of which benign bone tumor

## Options

1. chondroblastoma
2. osteochondroma
3. enchondroma
4. GCT

## Correct option

**1. chondroblastoma**

## Answer explanation

Bone marrow oedema is associated with a wide variety of pathological processes including both benign and malignant bone tumours. This imaging finding in relation to intraosseous tumours can aid in providing a more focused differential diagnosis. Benign lesions commonly associated with bone marrow oedema include osteoid osteoma, osteoblastoma, chondroblastoma and Langerhan's cell histiocytosis. Metastases and malignant primary bone tumours such as osteosarcoma, Ewing's sarcoma and chondrosarcoma may also be surrounded by bone marrow oedema.

Answer: 1
