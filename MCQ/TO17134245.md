Chapter: Spine MCQs — Cervical Disc Herniation And Myelopathy

## Question

For single level cervical spondylotic myelopathy the best approach is (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Corpectomy and anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF)
2. Posterior cervical canal decompression
3. Laminoplasty and laminectomy
4. Endoscopic fusion

## Correct option

**1. Corpectomy and anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF)**

## Answer explanation

For single-level cervical spondylotic myelopathy, ACDF is the gold standard.

Answer: 1
