## Question

Which of the following fractures has the highest total indemnity following a malpractice claim?

## Options

1. Femur
2. Tibia
3. Radius or ulna
4. Metatarsal or tarsal
5. Humerus

## Correct option

**2. Tibia**

## Answer explanation

The ten most prevalent orthopaedic patient conditions for malpractice suits are: fracture femur, fracture tibia/fibula, displacement of intervertebral disk, osteoarthrosis, fracture radius/ulna, disorder of joint, fracture of medial malleolus, tear of the medial meniscus, back disorders, and fracture of the humerus. Although there were 879 femur cases and only 760 tibia/fibula cases, over 2 million dollars more was paid out for tibia claims.
