## Question

A positive Reverse pivot shift test indicates

## Options

1. PCL injury
2. PLC injury
3. LCL injury
4. ALL injury

## Correct option

**2. PLC injury**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

PIVOT SHIFT TEST of Macintosh (Knee moved from extension to flexion)

With the patient supine, and knee fully extended the examiner lifts the leg and internally rotates the tibia. The knee is gradually flexed along with application of valgus force. A sudden posterior movement of the tibia is seen and felt as the joint is fully relocated. A positive test is suggestive of anterolateral instability (signifies torn ACL and lateral capsular laxity/ALL tear).

JERK TEST of Hughston and Losse (Knee moved from flexion to extension)

Reverse of Pivot Shift test.

Tests Antero lateral instability

REVERSE PIVOT SHIFT TEST

This is again same as a pivot shift test, but the tibia is externally rotated and knee moved from extension to flexion to feel the clunk. A positive test is suggestive of posterolateral instability (signifies torn PCL and the PLC structures of the knee, specifically PLC).
