Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Clavicle Fractures

## Question

False about clavicle fracture are all except

## Options

1. Fall on outstretched hand is the most common mode of injury
2. Brachial plexus injuries are most common in middle third fracture
3. Most common associated fracture is fracture of ipsilateral humerus
4. Highest incidence of nonunion is seen in fracture middle third of clavicle

## Correct option

**4. Highest incidence of nonunion is seen in fracture middle third of clavicle**

## Answer explanation

Clavicle fracture:

Mechanism of injury: Falls onto the affected shoulder leading to a bending force account for most (87%) of clavicular fractures, with direct impact accounting for only 7% and falls onto an outstretched hand accounting for 6%.

Brachial plexus injuries are most commonly seen with medial third clavicle fracture. Rib fracture is the most commonly associated fracture. The incidence of nonunion following clavicle fractures ranges from 0.1% to 13.0%, with 85% of all nonunion occurring in the middle third.

Answer: 4
