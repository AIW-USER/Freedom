Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Hallux Valgus Bunion

## Question

The 'round sign' on a dorsoplantar radiograph of the first metatarsal head indicates what specific pathological feature?

## Options

1. Presence of a subchondral cyst
2. Complete sesamoid obliteration
3. Pronation of the first metatarsal
4. Avascular necrosis of the metatarsal head

## Correct option

**3. Pronation of the first metatarsal**

## Answer explanation

The 'round sign' occurs when the metatarsal rotates, making the lateral profile of the head appear more rounded on an AP view.

Answer: 3
