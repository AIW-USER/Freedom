Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Compound Palmar Ganglion

## Question

Most common site of origin of ganglion stalk of dorsal wrist ganglion is

## Options

1. scaphotrapezial joint
2. scapholunate ligament
3. radio scaphoid- scapholunate interval
4. metacarpotrapezial joint

## Correct option

**2. scapholunate ligament**

## Answer explanation

Ganglion cysts are benign soft tissue tumours most commonly encountered in the wrist, but which may occur in any joint. They may affect any age group; however they are more common in the twenties to forties. A history of trauma is elicited in at least 10% of cases and is considered a causative factor although the pathogenesis remains unclear.

Site- Sixty to seventy percent of ganglion cysts are found in dorsal aspect of wrist & communicate with joint via a pedicle. This pedicle usually originates at the scapholunate ligament (most common site) but also may arise from a number of other sites over the dorsal aspect of the wrist capsule. Thirteen to twenty percent of ganglia are found on volar aspect of wrist, arising via a pedicle from radio scaphoid- scapholunate interval, scaphotrapezial joint, or metacarpotrapezial joint, in that order of frequency. Ganglia arising from a flexor tendon sheath in the hand account for approximately 10%. Occurrence in other joints as well as intraosseus and intratendinous ganglia are much less common.

Treatment: Aspiration is the mainstay of non-surgical treatment. Studies have shown remarkably variable rates of success with high recurrence rate. Even with repeat aspirations, success rate is only 30–50%. Steroid injection after aspiration, instillation of hyaluronidase prior to aspiration do not improve the success rate.

Surgical excision remains the gold standard for treatment of ganglion cysts. Generally, higher reported rates of recurrence are attributed to inadequate dissection in which the tortuous duct system located at the joint capsule is not fully excised.

Since the adoption of surgical techniques that include excision of the entire ganglion complex, including cyst, pedicle, and a cuff of the adjacent joint capsule, recurrence rates have improved significantly.

Arthroscopic excision: Viewing portal is 6R; working portal is through the ganglion, typically 3-4. An essential part of surgical treatment is to excise the attachment or root of ganglion. If the ganglion stalk could not be identified, then a wide area of dorsal capsule is resected.

Answer: 2
