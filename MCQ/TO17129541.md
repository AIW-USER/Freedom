## Question

Aneurysmal phenomenon in dorsal pott’s spine is seen duside (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Vertebral plexus
2. Long standing abscess
3. Vacuum phenomenon in vertebral bodies
4. Vertebral ankylosis

## Correct option

**2. Long standing abscess**

## Answer explanation

In anterior type of pott’s lesion, the collection of tuberculous granulation tissue and necrotic material leads formation of paravertebral abscess. In the region of thoracic spine, it is visible on plain radiographs as a fusiform or globular radio dense shadow called the bird nest appearance. Long standing abscesses may produce concave erosions around the anterior margins of the vertebral bodies producing a scalloped appearance called the aneurysmal phenomenon.

Answer: 2
