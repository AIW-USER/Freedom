## Question

In cement less porous implants what should be the ideal pore size/ range to allow for optimal bony ingrowth? (Arthroplasty- hip arthroplasty- implants and prosthesis design)

## Options

1. <5 micron
2. <500 micron
3. 500-1000 micron
4. 1000-10000 micron

## Correct option

**2. <500 micron**

## Answer explanation

The range of 50 to 400 microns is the optimal pore size for cementless porous implants to allow for optimal bony ingrowth.

Answer: 2
