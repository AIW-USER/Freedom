## Question

Which of the following bisphosphonates failed to demonstrate a statistically significant reduction in hip fracture risk in its primary pivotal clinical trial?

## Options

1. Zoledronic acid
2. Risedronate
3. Alendronate
4. Ibandronate

## Correct option

**4. Ibandronate**

## Answer explanation

In the BONE study, Ibandronate significantly reduced vertebral fractures, but it did not show a significant reduction in non-vertebral or hip fractures in the overall study population.

Answer: 4
