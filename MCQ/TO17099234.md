## Question

Correct operating sequence for RA patient for upper limb reconstructive surgery is

## Options

1. Hand > shoulder > elbow
2. Elbow > shoulder > hand
3. Shoulder > elbow > hand
4. Most painful first

## Correct option

**1. Hand > shoulder > elbow**

## Answer explanation

Sequence of reconstruction in lower limb is: lower limb arthroplasty should be done before upper limb.
Order of reconstructive procedures in upper limb is – Hand and wrist>shoulder>elbow
Order of reconstructive procedures in lower limb is- forefoot>hip> knee> hindfoot> ankle.

Answer : a
