Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Hip Arthrodesis

## Question

Ideal position for hip arthrodesis is

## Options

1. 20 to 30 degrees of flexion, 0 to 5 degrees of adduction, and 0 to 15 degrees of external rotation
2. 10 degrees of flexion, 0 to 5 degrees of adduction, and 20 degrees of external rotation
3. 10 degrees of flexion, 0 to 5 degrees of abduction, and 0 to 15 degrees of external rotation
4. 20 to 30 degrees of flexion, 10 degrees of abduction, and 0 to 15 degrees of external rotation

## Correct option

**1. 20 to 30 degrees of flexion, 0 to 5 degrees of adduction, and 0 to 15 degrees of external rotation**

## Answer explanation

Regardless of the technique selected, the ideal fusion position is 20 to 30 degrees of flexion, 0 to 5 degrees of adduction, and 0 to 15 degrees of external rotation.

Answer: 1
