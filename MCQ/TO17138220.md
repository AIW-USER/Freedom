## Question

Which of the following statement is False?

## Options

1. Unilateral clubfoot children develop a difference in the girth of the calf
2. Parental education level is related to the recurrence of clubfoot in maintenance phase
3. Bracing recommendation of clubfeet after walking age is night time shoes connected by bar and daytime AFO/ Customized shoes
4. Half of the cases are bilateral.

## Correct option

**3. Bracing recommendation of clubfeet after walking age is night time shoes connected by bar and daytime AFO/ Customized shoes**

ANSWER-C
