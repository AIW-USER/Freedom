Chapter: Musculoskeletal Infections MCQs — Tuberculosis Of Spine

## Question

Which among the following MRI is not suggestive of a tubercular etiology?

![[TO17129551-1.png]]

## Options

1. a
2. b
3. c
4. d

## Correct option

**4. d**

## Answer explanation

TB spine features in MRI

- Multiple vertebral body involvement
- Skip lesions
- Vertebral body collapse
- Paraspinal abscess

In the option D, Abscess is confined to disc space in the lumbar spine which is more a feature of pyogenic spondylodiscitis.

Answer: 4
