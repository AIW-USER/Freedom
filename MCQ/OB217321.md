## Question

While on your orthopedic tumor rotation, you see a patient with a presumed benign bone lesion. You are then asked about Enneking staging by your attending surgeon. Which of the following benign bone lesions would be consistent with an Enneking stage 1 lesion?

## Options

1. Aneursymal bone cyst (ABC)
2. Chondromyxoid fibroma
3. Chondroblastoma
4. Enchondroma
5. Giant cell tumor of bone

## Correct option

**4. Enchondroma**

## Answer explanation

Enneking stage 1 benign bone lesions are latent. These include enchondromas and non-ossifying fibromas.

Enchondromas are benign chondrogenic tumors composed of hyaline cartilage that typically occur in the medullary cavity of the diaphysis or metaphysis, most commonly in the hands. Patients typically present between the ages of 20-50 with an asymptomatic lesion, discovered incidentally on radiographs. Diagnosis is made radiographically with the presence of a well-defined, lucent, central medullary lesion that is 1-10 cm, and often associated with "pop-corn" stippling, arcs, whorls, or rings. Treatment is observation as most lesions are asymptomatic.

*Marco et al. reviewed cartilage tumors including enchondromas.* **They reported** that enchondromas are benign tumors that are usually asymptomatic and should be treated nonoperatively. **They concluded** that symptomatic enchondromas are often treated by intralesional excision.

*Mulligan et al. reviewed enchondromas and* **reported** that these lesions are among the most common benign tumors seen in the skeleton. **They concluded** that it is important to understand the radiological characteristics of these lesions to confidently make the correct diagnosis and distinguish it from other entities such as a bone infarct, bone graft, and low-grade chondrosarcoma.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1-3: ABCs, chondromyxoid fibromas, and chondroblastomas are stage 2 (active lesions) based on the Enneking classification of benign bone lesions
Answer 5: Giant cell tumor of bone is a stage 3 (aggressive lesion) based on the Enneking classification of benign bone lesions
