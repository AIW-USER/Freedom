## Question

A patient being treated for XDR-Tuberculosis of the hip joint is on a regimen including Bedaquiline and Linezolid. Which monitoring protocol is most essential to screen for the specific 'high-risk' adverse effects of these two medications?

## Options

1. Frequent ECG for QTc prolongation and weekly Complete Blood Counts (CBC) for myelosuppression.
2. Monthly Audiometry and Serum Creatinine monitoring.
3. Visual acuity and color vision testing every two weeks.
4. Periodic Liver Function Tests (LFT) and Uric acid levels only.

## Correct option

**1. Frequent ECG for QTc prolongation and weekly Complete Blood Counts (CBC) for myelosuppression.**

## Answer explanation

Bedaquiline is known to prolong the QTc interval, while Linezolid carries a significant risk of bone marrow suppression, particularly anemia and thrombocytopenia.

Answer: 1
