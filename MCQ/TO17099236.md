Chapter: Arthritis MCQs — Osteoarthritis

## Question

Most common cause/source of loose body in knee (FNB Pattern Paper 2020)

## Options

1. Osteophytes
2. Synovial chondromatosis
3. Damaged menisci
4. OCD

## Correct option

**1. Osteophytes**

## Answer explanation

The most common cause of loose bodies in the knee joint is OA of the knee. However, the most common cause of loose body in the knee joint in young patients is osteochondritis dissecans of the knee. And the most common cause of multiple loose bodies in the knee joint is synovial chondromatosis.

Answer: 1
