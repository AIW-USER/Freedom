## Question

Which of the following decreases pull out strength of screw?

## Options

1. Increasing outer diameter
2. Decreasing inner diameter
3. Increase thread density
4. Decrease thickness of cortex

## Correct option

**4. Decrease thickness of cortex**

## Answer explanation

The aim is to maximize the friction between the screw and the bone. So increasing the thickness of cortex will increase the pull out strength

Answer: 4
