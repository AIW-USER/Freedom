## Question

Which is not an indication for consideration for treatment of osteoporosis

## Options

1. History of hip fracture
2. T score -2.5 or below
3. patients who have a T-score of between −1.0 and −2.5 at the femoral neck or spine and a ten-year hip fracture risk of ≥3%
4. ten-year risk of a major osteoporosis-related fracture of ≥10% as assessed with the FRAX

## Correct option

**4. ten-year risk of a major osteoporosis-related fracture of ≥10% as assessed with the FRAX**

## Answer explanation

According to the 2008 National Osteoporosis Foundation recommendations, treatment of osteoporosis should be considered for (1) patients with a history of hip or vertebral fracture, (2) patients with a T-score of −2.5 or lower at the femoral neck or spine, and (3) patients who have a T-score of between −1.0 and −2.5 at the femoral neck or spine and a ten-year hip fracture risk of ≥3% or a ten-year risk of a major osteoporosis-related fracture of ≥20% as assessed with the FRAX.

Answer : d
