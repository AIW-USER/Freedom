## Question

Which one of the following would be the most suitable test to detect a difference between the Grade of Perthes’ disease (Herring classification) in two different population groups belonging to the rural or urban areas?

## Options

1. Paired t-test
2. Mann-Whitney U test
3. Wilcoxon signed rank test
4. Kruskal-Wallis test

## Correct option

**2. Mann-Whitney U test**

## Answer explanation

Here we are comparing two different groups that are not paired or matched. The Herring classification system is an ordinal measurement (i.e. Grade A, B, or C, with increasing severity).

Answer: 2
