## Question

A 65-year-old female with history of rheumatoid arthritis (RA) is being scheduled for a right total hip arthroplasty. She currently takes Stelera (ustekinumab) every 12 weeks. You advise her that she should not take her next dose and wait an additional week thereafter before having surgery. You also recommend that she not restart this medication until 14 days postoperatively. What is the basis of your recommendation?

## Options

1. Irreversible effect on bleeding time
2. Increased risk of postoperative thromboembolic events
3. Black box warning for the use of ustekinumab with general anesthetic agents
4. Increased risk of wound related complications
5. Increased risk of postoperative fibrosis

## Correct option

**4. Increased risk of wound related complications**

## Answer explanation

Stelera (ustekinumab) is a biologic agent that can inhibit the healing of surgical sites, thus its recommended that patients wait a total of 13 weeks from their last dose prior to surgery and that it is held for at least 14 days postoperatively.

Anti-rheumatic disease medications are categorized as agents believed to modify disease activity (DMARDS) or as biologics, which target specific mediators (cytokines) of the inflammatory response. Stelera (ustekinumab) is a monoclonal antibody biologic medication that blocks IL-12 and IL-23 used in the treatment of RA. Owing to their anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive effects, it's recommended that these medications be stopped preoperatively and held postoperatively to prevent wound complications. An increased risk of infection has also been suggestive of those biologic medications that block TNF-alpha.

*Goodman et al. review the results from a patient panel that was constructed between members of the American College of Rheumatology and the American Association of Hip and Knee Surgeons prior to drafting their clinical practice guidelines.* **They conclude** that patients placed a high importance on avoiding postoperative infection rather than a disease flare.

*MacKenzie et al. review the perioperative medication management in rheumatic diseases.* **They report** that biologic agents should be avoided in the acute perioperative period to decrease the risk of wound complications and infections. **They conclude** that Stelara (ustekinumab) should be withheld on the 13th-week following a 12-week cycle prior to surgery, and should not be restarted until at least 14 days postoperatively.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1 & 2: Biologics have no effect on bleeding time or thromboembolic events.
Answer 3: There is no black box warning for the use of Stelara and general anesthetics
Answer 5: Postoperative fibrosis following THA has not been associated with biologic agents.
