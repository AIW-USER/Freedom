Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — Shoulder Instability[Unidirectional and Multidirectional]

## Question

A wrestler presents to OPD with complaints of recurrent shoulder dislocations. He gives a history of 30 dislocations and dislocations while sleeping. His MRI shows Bankart tear and CT scan shows 40% bone loss from the posterolateral aspect of the head of the humerus. What is the appropriate management here? (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Arthroscopic Bankart repair
2. Latarjet procedure
3. Mclaughlin procedure
4. Remplissage procédure

## Correct option

**2. Latarjet procedure**
