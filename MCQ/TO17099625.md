## Question

Flexion deformity of both hip and knee joint is present in a patient. Right sequence of correction is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. First hip then knee
2. First knee then hip
3. Both hip and knee simultaneously
4. Treat knee first and hip after 3 weeks

## Correct option

**1. First hip then knee**

## Answer explanation

In combined hip and knee flexion deformities hip flexion deformity should be corrected first for 5 reasons

1. Once FFD at hips is corrected FFD at knee decreases.

2. It is easier to do knee with mobile hips rather than stiff hips

3. Referred pain from hip arthritis decrease after THR

4. Knee should be done after new center of rotation of head is achieved for better mechanical alignment

5. Rehabilitation is quicker after correction of hip FFD.

Answer: 1
