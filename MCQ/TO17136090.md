## Question

Why Prithwi Shaw was banned from cricket for 8 months

## Options

1. He consumed cough syrup containing terbutaline
2. for taking combination of steroids with formoterol
3. Forgot to take exemption certificate for taking salmeterol inhaler for asthma
4. Consumed 7 up drink containing illegal amount of caffeine

## Correct option

**1. He consumed cough syrup containing terbutaline**

## Answer explanation

Prithwi Shah- Case of INADVERTENT DOPING

India cricketer Prithvi Shaw was suspended for eight months for a anti-doping rule violation (ADRV). He admitted, he had taken Terbutaline inadvertently to treat a respiratory tract Infection and not as a performance-enhancing drug.

Sample was subsequently tested and found to contain Terbutaline. Terbutaline, a specified substance, is prohibited both In & Out of Competition prohibited at all times (even in therapeutic use) by the WADA in the WADA Prohibited List of Substances.

Beta 2 agonists: Salbutamol and terbutaline are short-acting beta 2 agonists (SABAs) while salmeterol and formoterol are long acting beta 2 agonists (LABAs). Terbutaline is a beta 2 agonist ( SABA) I.e Short Acting Beta 2 agonist approved to prevent and treat bronchospasm (narrowing of airways) associated with asthma, bronchitis, and emphysema. Terbutaline is on the list of substances that are because it has been shown to enhance time to exhaustion and, among other things, the muscle power output in cyclists. It helps to open the airways and thereby ventilate the lungs effectively. It may be beneficial to cyclists, runners, etc, but wouldn't really help a cricketer perform better.

An athlete can use a beta 2 agonist if she has a therapeutic-use exemption (TUE) certificate confirming that she is suffering from asthma and needs to use an inhaler with SABA/LABA. Now, the WADA has set thresholds for beta 2 agonist (Salbutamol, salmeterol and formoterol) intake but not for terbutaline.

 BCCI accepted Mr. Shaw’s explanation of the cause of his ADRV, & sanctioned him a period of ineligibility of eight months. In 2018 Yusuf Pathan was also tested positive for terbutaline and was handed a back dated 5 months ban.
