Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Ewings Sarcoma

## Question

13-year-old boy presenting with a mid-femoral lesion with a large associated soft tissue swelling. Xray and biopsy were done. What's the most likely diagnosis?

## Options

1. Chondrosarcoma.
2. Osteosarcoma.
3. Ewing’s sarcoma.
4. Giant cell tumor.

## Correct option

**3. Ewing’s sarcoma.**

## Answer explanation

![[TO17142753-1.png]]

![[TO17142753-2.jpg]]

The clinical presentation and biopsy image are consistent with Ewing's sarcoma.

Ewing's sarcoma is a pediatric round cell bone tumor resulting from a translocation of chromosomes 11 and 22 which leads to the formation of a fusion protein (EWS-FLI1).

Radiographs demonstrate a lytic lesion with typical lamellated periosteal reaction (“onion skin” type).

An MRI is useful to evaluate soft-tissue involvement.

Histologically, uniform sheets of small round blue cells are seen with round or oval nuclei of uniform size (PNET type biopsy).

Prognostic factors: In surgically treated patients, the most important prognostic factor is chemotherapy-induced necrosis. Other poor prognostic factors in surgically treated patients are old age, fever, anemia, male sex, raised WBC count, raised ESR, and high serum LDH level.

Prognostic factors associated with survival include metastasis at diagnosis, large tumors (volume ≥ 200 ml or largest diameter ≥ 8 cm), primary tumors located in the axial skeleton, especially pelvic and a histological response of less than 100%.

Answer: 3

Explanation 2:

Chondrosarcomas typically occur in middle age and have a predominance for the pelvis and shoulder. Osteosarcomas have a bimodal age distribution with a peak in childhood and the elderly. They most commonly occur in the distal femur and proximal tibia.

Giant cell tumors generally occur after skeletal maturity. It usually appears as an eccentric, lytic, expanding lesion in the distal metaphysis/epiphysis. It is locally aggressive but rarely malignant.

Neoadjuvant chemotherapy, wide surgical resection, and adjuvant chemotherapy.

Answer: 3
