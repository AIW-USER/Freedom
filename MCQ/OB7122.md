## Question

A child with bilateral fractures of the olecranon apophysis should be evaluated for which of the following disorders?

## Options

1. X-linked hypophosphatemic rickets
2. Idiopathic osteoporosis
3. Osteopetrosis
4. Osteogenesis imperfecta
5. Renal osteodystrophy

## Correct option

**4. Osteogenesis imperfecta**

## Answer explanation

Fractures of the olecranon apophysis are rare in most children but are relatively common in children with osteogenesis imperfecta. The fracture should be treated surgically with open reduction and internal fixation if there is displacement of the interarticular fracture fragments and a work-up for osteogenesis should be considered.
