Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Bipolar Tripolar Dual Mobility Hip

## Question

A young diabetic patient had a dual mobility right hip prosthesis implanted 5 years ago. He presented with limp and acute limb shortening after a minor fall. He somehow manages to walk with limp. X ray right hip AP view is given below. Choose the most appropriate answer explaining the situation

![[TO17099763-1.png]]

## Options

1. Its due to dislocation of the head from liner
2. Its due to wear of the PE liner’s retentive chamfer
3. Its due to central fracture dislocation of hip joint
4. Wear around metallic shell causing osteolysis and instability

## Correct option

**2. Its due to wear of the PE liner’s retentive chamfer**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 2

X ray right hip shows intraprosthetic dislocation of the dual mobility hip, as suggested by eccentric location of the neck in the cup. It is caused by wear of the PE liner’s retentive chamfer.
