## Question

On average, which of the following vertebrae has the greatest pedicle isthmic width in a nondeformity patient?

## Options

1. C7
2. T4
3. T11
4. T12
5. L1

## Correct option

**4. T12**

## Answer explanation

Of the pedicles listed, T12 has the largest diameter on average.

Pedicle diameter is an important anatomic factor when stabilizing the spine with pedicle screw instrumentation. While pedicle diameter varies by individual and ethnicity, on average the following trends include: T1 is the largest pedicle diameter in the upper spine, T4 is the smallest pedicle diameter in the upper spine, T12 has the largest diameter in the thoracolumbar junction (larger than L1).

*Ofiram et al. used MRI data to look at pedicle characteristics in the thoracolumbar spine in 100 patients.* **They found** the smallest pedicle isthmic width was at L1. T12 had the largest pedicle width. Although smaller in diameter than T12, both T10 and T11 had larger pedicle width than L1. Pedicle widths were larger in males compared with females.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: C7 has the largests pedicle width of the cervical vertebrae.
Answer 2: T4 is the smallest pedicle diameter in the upper spine.
Answer 3: Although smaller in diameter than T12, both T10 and T11 had larger pedicle width than L1.
Answer: L1 has a smaller diameter than T12.
