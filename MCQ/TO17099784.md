Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Complications of THR

## Question

False statement regarding acetabular component position in total hip replacement is

## Options

1. In horizontal cup dislocation occurs posteriorly with hip flexion
2. In vertical cup dislocation occurs superiorly with hip adduction
3. In excessively anteverted cup anterior dislocation occurs during hip flexion, adduction, and internal rotation.
4. In the retroverted cup, dislocation occurs posteriorly with flexion, adduction, and internal rotation.

## Correct option

**3. In excessively anteverted cup anterior dislocation occurs during hip flexion, adduction, and internal rotation.**

## Answer explanation

If the cup is excessively anteverted, anterior dislocation can occur during hip extension, adduction, and external rotation. If the cup is retroverted, dislocation occurs posteriorly with flexion, adduction, and internal rotation. Excessive inclination of the cup can lead to superior dislocation with adduction, especially if there is a residual adduction contracture, or if the femur impinges on osteophytes left along the inferior margin of the acetabulum. Conversely, if the cup is inclined almost horizontally, impingement occurs early in flexion and the hip dislocates posteriorly; this is accentuated if the cup also is in less anteversion.

Answer: 3
