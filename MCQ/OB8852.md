## Answer explanation

The patient has a large posterior thigh mass that is most amenable to removal through a direct posterior approach with drain placement in line with this incision. The mass was later confirmed to be a schwannoma.

Guidelines for biopsying soft tissue masses are made with the assumption that all exposed tissues could be contaminated and may require excision. As such, the following principles attempt to limit the extent of involved structures: incisions should be made in a longitudinal fashion, dissection should stay within one compartment and avoid neurovascular structures when possible, and drains should be placed in line with the incision for ease of excision.

*Traina et al. reviewed basic biopsy principles.* **They note** the importance of an incision mindful of the definitive procedure, a dissection which does not break anatomic planes or compartments and avoiding post-operative hematoma. Although the method of biopsy may vary from fine needle aspiration, core biopsy, or incisional biopsy, **they note** that these principles should be followed to optimize patient outcome.

*Mankin et al. compiled cases from 25 musculoskeletal tumor surgeons to investigate whether rates of complication, errors, or deleterious effects of biopsy had changed after the Musculoskeletal Tumor Society recommended performing procedures at a treatment center rather than at the referring institution.* **They found** little improvement from their prior study, with 17% rate of diagnostic error, 19% rate of having to perform a more difficult procedure due to previous biopsy, and a 10% rate of patients having a change in outcome due to previous biopsy. **Their study highlights** the needs for biopsy to be done by the treating surgeon rather than the referring surgeon.

*Rougraff et al. performed a systemic review to evaluate the timing and location of biopsies, who should perform the procedure, and which tumors are the most difficult to diagnose.* Though there were a limited number of studies addressing these, infection, paraspinal tumors, and round and myxoid tumors were the most difficult to diagnose. Frozen specimen analysis following incisional biopsy yielded the best results regardless of who was performing the procedure (interventional radiologist vs. surgeon).

Figures A through C are sagittal and axial MR images of a large posterior thigh mass contained within the posterior compartment with the neurovascular structures in close proximity to the mass. Illustration A shows the characteristic histology of schwannoma with spindle-like cells.

Incorrect Answers
Answers 1 & 2: Both paths would involve the neurovascular bundle while path 1 would also violate the anterior compartment. If the patient's mass is malignant, the entire neurovascular bundle would be contaminated.
Answer 3: This path could contaminate the medial compartment unnecessarily.
Answer 5: This path could possibly contaminate the anterior compartment unnecessarily.
