## Question

Surgical treatment was recommended, but the patient wishes to attempt medical treatment. ## Options

1. Chimeric monoclonal antibody which targets tumor necrosis factor-alpha
2. Human monoclonal antibody which targets receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-beta ligand
3. Human monoclonal antibody which targets receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-beta
4. Decoy receptor which targets receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-beta
5. Decoy receptor which targets receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-beta ligand

## Correct option

**2. Human monoclonal antibody which targets receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-beta ligand**

## Answer explanation

Figures A and B are radiographs demonstrating a lytic lesion in the proximal tibia. Figure C is a histopathologic slide of giant cell tumor (GCT) of bone. Denosumab, which may be used in the treatment of GCT is a human monoclonal antibody which targets receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-Β ligand (RANKL).

Denosumab is a fully human monoclonal antibody which targets RANKL, preventing it from binding to the surface of osteoclast precursors and osteoclasts. It, therefore, inhibits osteoclast differentiation, survival, and osteoclast-mediated bone destruction. GCT is characterized by the presence of numerous multinucleated osteoclast-type giant cells. The mononuclear stromal cell is the neoplastic cell of this tumor. Giant cells, which are responsible for the osteolytic nature of this tumor, have been found to express RANKL. Therefore, inhibition of RANK-L by denosumab has been found to be beneficial in the treatment of GCT.

*Xu et al. performed a review of denosumab as a treatment of GCT.* **They state** that no available treatment option is definitively effective in curing GCT. **They report** that several studies of denosumab in patients with advanced or unresectable GCT have shown objective changes in tumor composition, reduced bony destruction, and improved the clinical condition. **They conclude** several large phase III studies have shown that denosumab is more effective than bisphosphonates in the treatment of GCT.

*Park et al. wrote a case report of denunsomab in the treatment of GCT of the distal radius.* **They report** that juxta-articular GCT can pose major challenges as they often require complex procedures that may be associated with complications. They presented a case of a 25-year-old man with GCT of the distal radius that was treated successfully with denosumab without surgery. At 3.5 year follow-up, he did not have a recurrence. **They conclude** with denosumab therapy, patients may avoid surgery and have a successful outcome.

Figures A and B are AP and lateral radiographs, respectively, demonstrating an osteolytic lesion in the epiphysis and metaphysis of the proximal tibia. Illustration C is a histologic image demonstrating multiple giant cells, characteristic of GCT.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Infliximab is a chimeric monoclonal antibody which targets tumor necrosis factor-alpha.
Answer 3: There are currently no human monoclonal antibodies which target receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-beta.
Answer 4: There are no decoy receptors which target receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-beta.
Answer 5: Osteoprotegerin is a decoy receptor which targets receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-beta ligand.
