## Question

Most common site of stress fracture is

## Options

1. Distal third of tibia
2. Second metatarsal
3. Fibula
4. Femoral neck

## Correct option

**1. Distal third of tibia**

## Answer explanation

Tibia: Posteromedial aspect of the tibia. Junction of the middle and distal third is the most common site for stress fractures

-Shaft of second/third metatarsal (March fracture, second most common stress fracture)

-Other metatarsals

-Medial malleolus: In jumpers and runners

-Distal fibula (Runner’s fracture)

-Femoral neck : Inferomedial cortical breaks (compression side) are more common than superior (tension side). However, tension side breaks are more dangerous and often progress to complete fracture without fixation.

-Femoral shaft: Posteromedial or medial cortical breaks.

- Navicular: In the central third of bone in runners/sprinters, ballet dancers, football players

-Calcaneus: Tuberosity fractures in military recruits and in runners

- Ribs: Fractures of middle ribs (4th–9th) common in rowers due to traction by serratus anterior

-Pubic ramus stress fractures in female long distance runners
