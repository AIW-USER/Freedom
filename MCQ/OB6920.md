## Question

A 19-year-old collegiate baseball player injures the ring finger on his dominant hand while sliding headfirst into second base. He reports that he is unable to actively flex or extend the distal interphalangeal joint of the finger. Radiographs are shown in Figures 19a and 19b. What is the anatomic lesion leading to this injury?

![[q6920-a-19a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q6920-b-19b.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Rupture of the terminal extensor tendon
2. Avulsion of the volar plate
3. Rupture of the sagittal bands
4. Rupture of the spiral oblique retinacular ligaments
5. Rupture of the profundus insertion

## Correct option

**5. Rupture of the profundus insertion**

## Answer explanation

The radiographs reveal a bony avulsion of the flexor profundus insertion (Jersey finger). The large bony fragment classifies this as a Leddy type III injury. The bony fragment has retracted to the level of the annular pulley (A4).
