Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Congenital Vertical Talus (CVT)

## Question

What is the status of the heel in a child with congenital vertical talus?

## Options

1. Calcaneus
2. Equinovarus
3. Equinus
4. Supple

## Correct option

**3. Equinus**

## Answer explanation

Infact CVT has a rigid equinovalgus deformity of the hindfoot.

Contrary to the common logic it is important to understand the deformity and its etiology. Hypertrophy and overactivity of the tendoachilless causes an equinus of the hand foot and it is the forefoot which is dorsiflexed causing a resultant rocker bottom appearance of the foot.

The Axis of the muscles and tendons of the foot is altered because of the deformity. As a result, the posterior tibial and peronei act as dorsiflexors of the foot.
