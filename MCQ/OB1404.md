## Question

Which of the following is a side-effect from anabolic steroid use?

## Options

1. Renal failure with secondary hyperpararthyroidism
2. Decrease in low density lipoprotein
3. Decreased blood pressure
4. Increase in fat
5. Decrease in high density lipoprotein

## Correct option

**5. Decrease in high density lipoprotein**

## Answer explanation

Anabolic steroids decrease high-density lipoprotein levels. They may either increase or have no effect on low-density lipoprotein levels. Elevations in blood pressure have been observed in athletes who use anabolic steroids. Abnormally low high-density lipoprotein levels may suggest steroid use in an athlete.

*Hartgens et al along with Blue et al showed that steroid use led to decreases in HDL in bodybuilders.*

Hartgens and Kuipers outline in a review article the effects of anabolic steroids including their effect on the immune, endocrine, and cardiovascular system.

*In the recent review by Achar et al, the effect of steroids on the cardiovascular system are discussed, especially in regard to the education of athletes and their evaluations by physicians.*
