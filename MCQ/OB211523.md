## Question

A 40-year-old male presents to your clinic 4 weeks after punching a wall after his alma mater lost in the championship game. Figure A shows the injury films. You attempt a closed reduction but this is unsuccessful so you recommend an open reduction and internal fixation through a standard dorsal ulnar approach to the wrist. Figure B is a intra-operative image during this procedure. The structure identified by the arrow is a branch of which structure?

## Options

1. Lateral cord
2. Medial cord
3. Medial antebrachial cutaneous nerve
4. Posterior cord
5. Posterior cutaneous nerve of the forearm

## Correct option

**2. Medial cord**

## Answer explanation

The patient has sustained a 5th CMC fracture-dislocation and given the subacute nature of this fracture, he is indicated for open reduction which is done via a dorsal ulnar approach. The dorsal branch of the ulnar nerve is seen in the clinical photo. This nerve is a direct branch off of the ulnar nerve which comes off the medial cord.

The dorsal branch of the ulnar nerve supplies sensation from the dorsal aspect of the ulnar wrist to the dorsal small finger and dorsal ulnar half of the ring finger. It branches off the ulnar nerve around 6 cm proximal to the ulnar styloid. The nerve is encountered during approaches to the distal ulna as well as dorsal ulnar approaches to the carpus and ulnar metacarpals and should be retracted from the field to avoid injury and painful neuromas.

*Botte et al. performed an analysis of the dorsal branch of the ulnar nerve in 24 cadaveric specimens.* **They noted** that the nerve arose at an average distance of 6.4 cm from the distal aspect of the ulnar head and 8.3 cm from the proximal border of the pisiform. **They noted** that the nerve passed dorsal to the FCU and pierced the deep fascia to become subcutaneous on the medial aspect of the forearm at a mean distance of 5.0 centimeters from the proximal edge of the pisiform. **The authors believe** a more complete understanding of the anatomy of this nerve may help prevent injury during surgical procedures.

*Ruchelsman and Vitale reviewed the management of acute and chronic ECU subsheath injury.* In addition, the standard approach used for subsheath reconstruction was highlighted. The authors underscore the importance of protecting the dorsal branch of the ulnar nerve during this dorsal ulnar approach.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: The lateral cord gives off the musculocutaneous nerve which terminates with the lateral antebrachial cutaneous nerve.
Answer 3: The medial antebrachial nerve branches from the medial cord but is not encountered during this approach.
Answer 4: The posterior cord gives off the axillary nerve and radial nerve.
Answer 5: The posterior cutaneous nerve of the forearm is a branch of the radial nerve and supplies sensation to the dorsal aspect of the middle and index finger.
