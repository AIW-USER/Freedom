Chapter: Sports Medicine MCQs — Sudden Cardiac Death And Sports Concussion

## Question

Incorrect statement is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Most common cause of sudden cardiac death in athletes is HOCM
2. Sudden cardiac death is more common in adolescent athlete than adult athletes
3. SCD are more common in white American male athletes than other athletes
4. Most important factor affecting survival in case of SCD is time interval from arrest to defibrillation

## Correct option

**3. SCD are more common in white American male athletes than other athletes**

## Answer explanation

Sudden cardiac death in athletes is more common in black American African athletes. Immediate defibrillation after cardiac arrest is the single most important factor affecting survival. One shock should be followed by immediate CPR and chest compression should be started airway management in a collapsed athlete. So, the recommended sequence of intervention in an athlete with cardiac arrest is “C-A-B” or chest compression, airway and breathing.

 Answer: C
