## Question

Clostridium perfringens toxin which causes myonecrosis is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Alfa
2. Beta
3. Epsilon
4. Iota

## Correct option

**1. Alfa**

## Answer explanation

The major toxins used for strain classification of clostridium perfringens are alpha toxin, beta toxin, epsilon toxin, and iota toxin. Alpha toxin is responsible for hemolysis, myonecrosis and gas gangrene in the infected tissue.

Answer: 1
