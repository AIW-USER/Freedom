## Question

A patient is undergoing a lumbar fusion for degenerative spondylolisthesis. During the operation, the surgeon decides to use allograft in addition to locally harvested autograft. Which of the following graft materials has the highest theoretical risk of disease transmission?

## Options

1. Freeze-dried cancellous allograft
2. Fresh frozen allograft
3. Bone morphogenic protein
4. Demineralized bone matrix
5. Fresh allograft

## Correct option

**5. Fresh allograft**

## Answer explanation

Fresh allograft has the highest immunogenicity and highest risk of disease transmission due to the fact there has been the least amount of processing compared to other grafting options.

Bone grafting in orthopedic surgery provides structural support and fusion material. Allograft material is often used due to the abundant supply for larger surgical needs. However, there have been historical concerns regarding disease transmission due to historical reports. The most recent disease transmitted from tissue translation occurred in 2001 involving clostridium. Regardless, allograft materials that undergo less processing and sterilization have a higher theoretical risk of disease transmission. Fresh allograft is one type of material that falls into this category. However, fresh allograft has the added advantage of increased cellular viability, which may provide greater long-term biomechanical strength.

*McAllister et al. provided an update on allograft tissue regulation, procurement, processing, and sterilization.* **The authors stated** manufacturers had made processing changes to sterilize allograft tissue in response to well-publicized infections associated with transplants. They addressed the general concerns of orthopedic surgeons regarding the effects of sterilization on graft tissue biology and biomechanics.

Incorrect answers
Answer 1: Freeze-dried cancellous allograft has the least risk of disease transmission than fresh allograft.
Answer 2: Fresh frozen allograft has a higher risk of disease transmission than freeze-dried allograft but less than fresh allograft.
Answer 3: Bone morphogenic protein has near a zero percent risk of disease transmission as it is synthetic.
Answer 4: Demineralized bone matrix has an extremely low risk of disease transmission compared to fresh allograft.
