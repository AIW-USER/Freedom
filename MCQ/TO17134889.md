Chapter: Spine MCQs — Lumbar Disc Herniation

## Question

Which cannot cause a weak great toe dorsiflexion (FNB Pattern Paper -2016)

## Options

1. L4-L5 disk extrusion
2. Far lateral disc at L5-S1
3. A laceration at dorsum of foot
4. Morton’s neuroma

## Correct option

**4. Morton’s neuroma**

## Answer explanation

A central disc at L4-L5 level, a far lateral disc at L5-S1 level, tendon injury due to laceration at dorsum of foot all can cause weak thumb dorsiflexion (weak EHL).

Answer: 4
