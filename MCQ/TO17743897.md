## Question

A diabetic foot ulcer is given in the picture. There is no bone involvement or abscess formation. Which grade correctly describes it according to Wagner’s classification? (regional orthopedics- foot and ankle- diabetic foot and amputations)

![[TO17743897-1.png]]

## Options

1. Grade 1
2. Grade 2
3. Grade 3
4. Grade 4

## Correct option

**2. Grade 2**

## Answer explanation

![[TO17743897-2.png]]

Wagner classification is most commonly used to grade diabetic foot ulcers. Superficial ulcers above superficial fascia are classified as grade 1. In grade 2 ulcers break through the superficial fascia deep to bone, tendons and deep fascia. So ulcers with exposed bone or tendon are grouped as grade 2.

Answer: 2
