## Question

A 50-year-old male presents with a painless thigh mass that has been growing in size. A representative MRI sequence, gross specimen and histologic evaluation are shown in Figures A, B and C respectively. What is the most likely diagnosis and associated corresponding chromosomal translocation?

## Options

1. Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma; t(2;13)
2. Fibrosarcoma; t(11;22)
3. Pleiomorphic undifferentiated sarcoma; t(12;16)
4. Synovial sarcoma; t(X;18)
5. Myxoid liposarcoma; t(12:16)

## Correct option

**5. Myxoid liposarcoma; t(12:16)**

## Answer explanation

The translocation t(12;16) is most commonly associated with myxoid liposarcoma.

Myxoid liposarcomas are the most common form of liposarcomas. They are associated with a translocation between chromosomes 12 and 16. This gives rise to the fusion protein CHOP-TLS. These sarcomas are an intermediate grade with a 10-30% chance of metastasis. On histology, proliferating lipoblasts are seen with a myxoid stromal matrix.

*Krishnan et al. completed a systematic review to identify the clinical relevance of molecular genetics in management of musculoskeletal tumors.* **They note** that the TLS-CHOP fusion protein seen in myxoid liposarcomas is highly sensitive and specific for this tumor.

*Bode-Lesniewska et al. evaluated the correlation between translocation type and prognosis in myxoid liposarcoma.* Although patients with type 2 TLS-CHOP fusion proteins had the longest period of disease-free survival, they were unable to conclude if this type of translocation has an effect on prognosis.

Figure A is an axial MRI cut demonstrating a myxoid lipsoarcoma of the thigh. Figure B is a gross specimen of myxoid liposarcoma. Figure C is a histologic evaluation of myxoid liposarcoma. Note the multivacuolated cells, indented nuclei of lipoblasts, "chicken wire" capillaries and myxoid background.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma is associated with a t(2;13) translocation, but the indented nuclei of lipoblasts seen in Figure C are hallmark for liposarcoma
Answer 2: Ewing’s sarcoma is associated with a t(11;22) translocation.
Answer 3: Myxoid liposarcoma is associated with t(12;16).
Answer 4: Synovial sarcoma is associated with a t(X;18) translocation. The signet ring lipoblasts seen in Figure C are hallmark for liposarcoma
