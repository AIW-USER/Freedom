## Question

In distal tibial physis last portion to fuse is?

## Options

1. Anteromedial
2. Posterolateral
3. Anterolateral
4. Posteromedial

## Correct option

**3. Anterolateral**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Fusion starts in the middle of the physis- then progresses anteromedially-then posterolateral. The last portion of the physis to fuse is anterolateral

Answer: 3
