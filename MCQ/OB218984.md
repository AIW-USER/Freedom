## Question

You are at an orthopedic grand rounds lecture, and a segment of the lecture discusses treatments to promote fracture healing. Which of the following most accurately defines the concept of reverse dynamization as it relates to fracture healing?

## Options

1. Allowing micromotion to promote callus formation during early healing, followed by changing to more rigid fixation to accelerate the progression toward union
2. Alternating between distraction and compression to promote healing of regenerate bone
3. Gradually destabilizing the fixation in an effort to train the bone to accept more load
4. Skeletal elements are strategically placed to optimize strength in relation to the distribution of applied load
5. Remodeling of bone in response to electric charges

## Correct option

**1. Allowing micromotion to promote callus formation during early healing, followed by changing to more rigid fixation to accelerate the progression toward union**

## Answer explanation

Reverse dynamization allows micromotion to promote callus formation during early healing, followed by changing to more rigid fixation to accelerate the progression toward union.

Fracture healing involves a complex and sequential set of events to restore the injured bone to pre-fracture condition. Fracture stability, specifically strain, dictates the type of bone healing that will occur. Mechanical stability in the fixation construct governs strain; when the strain is <2%, primary bone healing will occur. When the strain is between 2% and 10%, secondary bone healing will occur. Reverse dynamization accelerates fracture healing by allowing for early micromotion, which promotes callus formation; the fixation is then converted to a more rigid construct which protects the vascularity of the new bone and allows it to mature and remodel more quickly.

*Glatt et al. reviewed several concepts regarding fracture healing.* **They reported** that the mechanical environment around the fracture site significantly influences the way bone heals, or if it heals at all. **They concluded** that the role of the various biological factors, the timing, and spatial relationship of their introduction, and how the mechanical environment orchestrates this activity, are all crucial aspects to consider.

*Glatt et al. reviewed reverse dynamization.* **They reported** that it is a mechanical manipulation regimen designed to accelerate bone healing and remodeling based on the hypothesis that a fracture that is initially stabilized less rigidly allows micromotion to encourage initial cartilaginous callus formation. Once a substantial callus has formed, the stabilization should then be converted to a rigid configuration to prevent the disruption of neovascularization. **They concluded** that tibial osteotomies under reverse dynamization healed faster, healed objectively better, and were considerably stronger, all suggesting an accelerated healing and remodeling process.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: The accordion method describes alternating between distraction and compression to promote the healing of regenerate bone.
Answer 3: Dynamization describes the concept of gradually destabilizing the fixation in an effort to train the bone to accept more load.
Answer 4: Wolff's law refers to the principle that skeletal elements are strategically placed to optimize strength in relation to the distribution of applied load.
Answer 5: Piezoelectric charges refers to bone that remodels in response to electric charges; the compression side is electronegative and stimulates osteoblast formation, while the tension side is electropositive and simulates osteoclasts.
