## Question

Most important factor determining final ROM after TKR in arthritic knees with flexion deformity is (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. Post operative CPM and supervised rehabilitation
2. Preoperative range of motion
3. Tibial slope and femoral capsular release
4. Soft tissue balancing

## Correct option

**2. Preoperative range of motion**

## Answer explanation

Factors that correlate with postoperative range of motion include: preoperative values for flexion, flexion arc, and tibiofemoral angle. Better range of motion before the surgery with a lower tibiofemoral varus/valgus angle are more likely to result in a better range of motion after the surgery. Preoperative range of motion is the most important factor that determines postoperative flexion range.

Answer: 2
