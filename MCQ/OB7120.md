## Question

What collagen type is considered the major cartilage collagen, representing 90% to 95% of the total?

## Options

1. I
2. II
3. III
4. IX
5. X

## Correct option

**2. II**

## Answer explanation

The major cartilage collagen is known as type II. The articular cartilage matrix also contains types V, VI, IX, X, and XI. Collagen I is the most abundant form found in tendon and bone, and collagen III is found in smaller quantities in connective tissues.
