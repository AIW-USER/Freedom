## Question

True about patellar clunk syndrome is [ FNB 2023 ]

## Options

1. Occurs at superior pole of patella when knee flexes from an extended position
2. Occurs at superior pole of patella when knee extends from a flexed position
3. Occurs at inferior pole of patella when knee flexes from an extended position
4. Occurs at inferior pole of patella when knee extends from a flexed position

## Correct option

**2. Occurs at superior pole of patella when knee extends from a flexed position**

## Answer explanation

Answer : B
Patellar clunk syndrome: It is characterized by formation of a nodular car at the superior pole of the patella which gets caught in the box of the femoral component as the knee moves from flexion into extension. Scar catches in the box, then releases with a clunk. Typically, a clunk is felt between low range of flexion (between 30 and 45 degrees).

Risk factors:

Preoperative factors: Preop patella baja, valgus knee, revision surgery,

Patellar component factors: small patellar component, low lying patellar component, exposed cut bone of patella not covered by patellar component, patellar over resection and thin patellar button

Femoral component: PS design, increased posterior femoral condylar offset, smaller femoral component, femoral component in flexed position, femoral component with higher intercondylar box ratio (trochlear groove extended more proximal and anterior)

Thick polyethylene insert

Treatment is arthroscopic removal of suprapatellar scar nodule
