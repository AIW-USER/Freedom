## Answer explanation

Figure A demonstrates a hemiepiphysiodesis to correct Blount’s disease. This technique utilizes the Heuter-Volkmann law, which states that compression across the physis slows longitudinal growth.

Adolescent Blount’s disease is a genu varum deformity that can be seen in infants and adolescents. The diagnosis is made both clinically and radiographically by the presence of an increased metaphyseal-diaphyseal angle. For mild to moderate cases, a lateral tibial hemiepiphysiodesis may be performed to correct the varus deformity by slowing growth laterally, but not medially.

*Funk et al performed a retrospective review to compare implants used for hemiepiphysiodesis in late-onset Blount’s disease.* **They identified** 25 patients with 38 hemiepiphysiodeses performed. The only difference between staples and plates was that plates were up to 3-5 times more expensive. **They noted** higher implant failure rates with greater BMI, more severe deformity, and younger age. **They suggest** a cutoff of BMI < 35 and mechanical axis deviation < 80 mm of varus to minimize complications.

*Stokes reviewed the mechanisms and previous literature on the Hueter-Volkmann law.* **They state** that growth occurs longitudinally by endochondral ossification. This process can be slowed by 40% or more with continuous compression of the growth plate. To a lesser extend it can be accelerated with sustained distraction.

Figure A demonstrates the use of a plate for a lateral hemiepiphysiodeses to correct Blount’s deformity.
Figure B demonstrates the use of flexible nailing for a tibia fracture.
Figure C demonstrates the use of a fixed angle plate for a varus derotational osteotomy.
Figure D demonstrates the surgical correction of tibial bowing.
Figure E demonstrates the use of a lag screw to fix a Tillaux fracture.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: Flexible nailing utilizes the principle of secondary bone healing.
Answer 3: Varus derotational ostetomies do not utilize the Heuter-Volkmann law.
Answer 4: Ilizarov frames can be utilized for distraction osteogenesis and deformity correction, but generally not the Heuter-Volkmann law.
Answer 5. Lag screws utilize the principle of primary bone healing.
