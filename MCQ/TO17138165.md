Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Perthes Disease

## Question

Most common age group affected by perthes disease is (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. 10-15 years
2. 5 to 10 years
3. 4 to 8 years
4. 8 to 15 years

## Correct option

**2. 5 to 10 years**

## Answer explanation

Age groups for pediatric hip diseases:

-SCFE: Boys of age 12–15 years and girls of age 11–13 years are commonly affected

- Transient synovitis of the hip joint- 4–10 years (mean age at onset is 6 years)

- Perthes disease- The condition affects children in the age range of 4–10 years

Answer: 2
