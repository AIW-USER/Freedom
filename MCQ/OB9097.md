## Question

A 69-year-old woman is in the recovery room following the procedure seen in Figure A completed through a deltopectoral approach. Following surgery, the patient is given instructions on restrictions to protect a tendon that was repaired at the end of the case. The nerves that innervate the repaired tendon originate directly from which portion of the brachial plexus?

![[q9097-a-total-shoulder.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Lateral cord
2. Medial cord
3. Posterior cord
4. Superior trunk
5. C5 nerve root

## Correct option

**3. Posterior cord**

## Answer explanation

The upper and lower subscapular nerves come off the posterior cord of the brachial plexus and innervate the subscapularis muscle.

Postoperative rehabilitation management after total shoulder arthroplasty follows different protocols depending on surgeon preference. One consistency, however, is most surgeons do not recommend active strengthening exercises in the immediate postoperative period to protect the subscap repair. During surgery, the subscapularis tendon is reflected either through: a peel directly off its insertion, a trans-tendon release, or a lesser tuberosity osteotomy. The subscapularis is innervated by the upper and lower subscapular nerves which originate off the posterior cord of the brachial plexus.

*Miller et al. reviewed seven patients with symptomatic rupture of their subscapularis tendon repair following total shoulder arthroplasty.* **They found** factors associated with subscapularis rupture included previous surgery through the subscapularis tendon or lengthening procedures of the subscapularis tendon. **They concluded** that subscapularis rupture following total shoulder arthroplasty required surgical repair and did lead to worse functional outcomes.

*Norris et al. prospectively reviewed 176 shoulders with osteoarthritis undergoing total shoulder arthroplasty.* **They found** significant improvements in all self-assessed outcome parameters. **They conclude** prosthetic arthroplasty leads to dramatic improvement in pain, function, and patient satisfaction.

Figure A shows an anatomic total shoulder arthroplasty. Illustration A shows a brachial plexus diagram.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: The medial cord gives rise to three branches - the medial pectoral nerve, the medial brachial cutaneous nerve, and the medial antebrachial cutaneous nerve.
Answer 2: The lateral cord gives rise to a single branch, the lateral pectoral nerve.
Answer 4: The superior trunk gives rise to the suprascapular nerve and the nerve to subclavius.
Answer 5: The C5 nerve root gives rise to the dorsal scapular nerve which innervates the rhomboids and levator scapulae.
