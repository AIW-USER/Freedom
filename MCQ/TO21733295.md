Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Peroneal Tendon Tear And Instability

## Question

What is most common pathoanatomy of peroneal tendon dislocation?

## Options

1. Superior peroneal retinacula(SPR) detachment from the fibrocartilaginous ridge(FCR) and fibula with the fibular anterior periosteum.
2. Collectively avulsion and dislocation of FRC, SPR, and periosteum on the anterior surface of the fibula
3. Avulsion fracture of the bone of the anterior surface of the fibula with combined FCR and SPR
4. Tear of SPR and peroneus longus dislocation

## Correct option

**1. Superior peroneal retinacula(SPR) detachment from the fibrocartilaginous ridge(FCR) and fibula with the fibular anterior periosteum.**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 1
EXPLANATION: -

![[TO21733295-1.png]]

Eckert and Oden classification. Type 1: dislocation occurs when the SPR continuously detaches from the FCR and fibula with the fibular anterior periosteum. This type is the most reported one, accounting for 51% of all cases.
Type 2: this is a dislocation with the FRC, SPR, and periosteum on the anterior surface of the fibula collectively avulsed and dislocated. This type is reported in 33% of the cases.
Type 3: this is a type of avulsion fracture of the bone of the anterior surface of the fibula with combined FCR and SPR, with an incidence rate of 13%.
Type 4: this is a rare type with the SPR itself is ruptured, later added by Oden et al. (12); its frequency is unknown.
