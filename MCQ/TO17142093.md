Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Foot and Ankle Arthrodesis

## Question

Most common joint to have pseudoarthrosis after triple arthrodesis?

## Options

1. Talocalcaneal
2. Calcaneocuboid
3. Talonavicular
4. Lisfranc

## Correct option

**3. Talonavicular**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: The most difficult joint for achieving arthrodesis is Talonavicular.

Answer: 3
