## Question

While doing Z lengthening of tendo achilles in a child of CTEV, the distal horizontal limb of Z should be towards

![[TO17138202-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Medial
2. Lateral
3. Superolateral
4. Superomedial

## Correct option

**1. Medial**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: In a varus foot, distal horizontal limb should be medial to correct residual varus deformity.

Similarly, in valgus foot, distal horizontal limb be directed laterally to correct residual valgus.

Answer: 1
