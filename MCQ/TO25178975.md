## Question

A surgeon is performing a repair on a complex bucket-handle tear that extends from the mid-body into the posterior horn of the medial meniscus. Which of the following statements correctly identifies the most appropriate technical choice and its associated anatomical risk?

## Options

1. The outside-in technique is preferred for this posterior horn tear because it allows for the highest tension when tying knots manually over the capsule.
2. The all-inside technique is the only viable option for the mid-body component to avoid the necessity of a secondary skin incision.
3. An all-inside device is superior for the mid-body repair because the risk of iatrogenic chondral damage is lower than that of the outside-in technique.
4. An inside-out repair for the posterior horn requires a 'safety' incision to protect the saphenous nerve and vessels, as needles are passed from the joint to the exterior.

## Correct option

**4. An inside-out repair for the posterior horn requires a 'safety' incision to protect the saphenous nerve and vessels, as needles are passed from the joint to the exterior.**
