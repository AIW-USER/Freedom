## Question

The most common cause of early revision total knee arthroplasty is associated with a predominant activation of which cell lineage?

## Options

1. Macrophage
2. Neutrophil
3. Lymphocyte
4. Plasma Cell
5. Fibroblast

## Correct option

**2. Neutrophil**

## Answer explanation

The most common cause of early total knee arthroplasty (TKA) implant revision is infection. Neutrophils are the predominant inflammatory cell lineage seen in periprosthetic infections.

Total knee arthroplasty remains one of the greatest surgical innovations of the last century. It provides lasting pain relief and improved function for patients with advanced osteoarthritic disease. The positive results are ubiquitous, reproducible, and continue to improve over time. There is an expected increase in demand for primary knee arthroplasty over the next 30 years because of increasing population size, longer life expectancies, and widespread access to healthcare. With the rising number of primary TKAs performed each year, there is a reflective rise in the absolute number of revision procedures performed. Polyethylene wear used to be considered the most common cause for TKA failure, however, improvements in manufacturing have negated these potential failures. Large database registries, which capture both academic institutions as well as community hospitals, have identified periprosthetic infections as the most common cause for revision TKA overall. Institution-specific studies found that infection was specifically the most common cause for early revision (<2 years), while aseptic loosening was the most common cause for late revision (>2 years).

*Bozic and colleagues utilized the Nationwide Inpatient Sample (NIS) database to evaluate the most common cause for revision TKA in the United States.* After analyzing over 60,000 revision TKA procedures, **they found** that infection (25.2%) was the most common cause followed by mechanical loosening (16.1%). **The authors conclude** that their findings highlight focused areas for improvement in TKA procedures.

*Delanois and colleagues performed a similar, updated epidemiology study using the Nationwide Inpatient Sample (NIS) database.* After analyzing over 337,000 revision TKA procedures, **they found** infection (20.4%) was the most common cause for failed primary TKA followed closely by mechanical loosening (20.3%). **The authors conclude** by highlighting the economic burden of revision TKA and the need to continue improving outcomes and primary TKA survivability.

*Sharkey and colleagues utilized their institutional, prospectively-collected database to determine the most common cause for revision TKA over a 10-year period.* **They found** that infection was the most common cause of early revision (<2 years), while aseptic loosening was the most common cause of late revision (>2 years.) Importantly, they reported that polyethylene wear was no longer the major cause of failure as compared to their previously reported data 10 years prior.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Macrophage activation is associated with osteolysis, which is historically due to polyethylene wear over time. However, the markedly improved wear rates seen in highly cross-linked polyethylene have shifted osteolysis to an uncommon condition experienced with newer implants.
Answer 3 and 4: Lymphocytes serve as the dominant cell type associated with metal hypersensitivity/allergy. Plasma cells will also be found in abundance on synovial tissue analysis seen in chronic synovitis associated with metal hypersensitivity.
Answer 5: Fibroblasts are the predominant cells associated with arthrofibrosis.
