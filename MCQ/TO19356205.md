## Question

Which of the following would cause iatrogenic instability following surgery for lumbar canal stenosis? [FNB 2023]

## Options

1. Removing BL inferior facets
2. Removal of near total disc
3. Laminotomy with spinous process removal
4. Medial facet removed 1/4th

## Correct option

**1. Removing BL inferior facets**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

EXPLANATION: -

There are many factors which can cause iatrogenic instability following lumbar spine surgery.

- Doing Complete laminectomy

- Removing up to 1/4 th of facet joints

- Laminotomy and removal of spinous process

- Near total discectomy- Usually does not create significant instability
All above mentioned things do not create significant instability

As a general rule, for unilateral decompressions usually a maximum of up to half of the facet joint can be excised and for bilateral decompression one third of the facet joint can be excised on each side.
