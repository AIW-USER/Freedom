Chapter: Sports Medicine MCQs — Sports Psychology

## Question

Not a feature of Dysthymia

## Options

1. Lack of concentration
2. Bulemia and anorexia nervosa
3. Lack of interest
4. Low mood

## Correct option

**3. Lack of interest**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C
EXPLANATION: -

The Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition (DSM-5), the latest version of the DSM, has removed the word "(dysthymia)" from the diagnosis.
It has rather merged dysthymic disorder with chronic major depressive disorder into a single diagnosis called persistent depressive disorder (PDD).
The DSM-5-TR criteria for PDD includes:
• A. depressed mood that lasts for most of the day, for more days than not, for at least two years in adults. For children and adolescents, the mood may be irritable and the duration must be at least one year
• B. The presence of at least two of the following while depressed:
o Poor appetite or overeating
o Insomnia or hypersomnia
o Low energy or fatigue
o Low self-esteem
o Poor concentration and/or difficulty making decisions
o Feelings of hopelessness
C. During a 2-year period (1 year for children or adolescents), the individual has never been without the symptoms in Criteria A and B for more than 2 months at a time.
D. Criteria for a major depressive disorder may be continuously present for 2 years.
E. There has never been a manic or hypomanic episode.
F. The disturbance is not better explained by a schizophrenia spectrum or other psychotic disorder.
G. The symptoms are not attributable to the physiological effects of a substance or another medical condition.
H. The symptoms cause clinically significant distress or impairment in social, occupational, or other important areas of functioning.

PDD is a chronic mood disorder that can begin early in life and often becomes chronic. Symptoms may persist or fluctuate but never completely resolve.
