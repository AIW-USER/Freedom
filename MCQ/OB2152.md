## Question

A 20-year-old college running back sustains a knee injury after being tackled from the medial aspect of his right knee. On examination, he has a knee effusion and tenderness laterally. He has a 1A Lachman and a normal posterior drawer test. He has symmetric rotation with his knee flexed at 90 degrees, but 20 degrees of increased external rotation with his knee flexed to 30 degrees. Which of the following choices is the appropriate surgical treatment?

## Options

1. ACL reconstruction with bone-tendon-bone autograft
2. Combined ACL and posterolateral corner reconstruction
3. PCL reconstruction
4. Posterolateral corner reconstruction
5. Combined PCL and posterolateral corner reconstuction

## Correct option

**4. Posterolateral corner reconstruction**

## Answer explanation

The above clinical scenario describes an isolated posterolateral corner (PL corner) injury, and this can be an indication for PL corner repair or reconstruction.

A positive Dial test (demonstrating increased external rotation) at 30 degrees but not at 90 degrees is consistent with an isolated PL corner injury. His Lachman's exam to test the ACL is normal (1A). The normal posterior drawer and Dial at 90 degrees effectively rule out a PCL injury. For a PL corner injury in a high level athlete, PL corner repair or reconstruction would be the suggested treatment.

*Veltri et al discussed the treatment of isolated and combined PCL injuries.* **They support** nonoperative treatment for isolated acute and chronic PCL tears with less than 10 mm of posterior laxity. PCL injuries with greater than 10 mm of posterior laxity and those with combined ligamentous injuries should be reconstructed.

Incorrect answers:
1- Would be indicated for ACL tear, and would have positive Lachman's and anterior drawer signs
2- Would be indicated for combined ACL and PLC injuries
3- Woud be indicated for PCL tear, and would have positive posterior drawer and increased leg external rotation test at 90 degrees
5- Would be indicated for combined PCL and PLC injuries
