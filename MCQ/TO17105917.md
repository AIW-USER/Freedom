Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Patellar Maltracking

## Question

Lateral malt racking of patella is caused by all except

## Options

1. Internal rotation of the tibial component
2. Internal rotation of the femoral component
3. Medial translation of the femoral component
4. Medial translation of the patellar component

## Correct option

**4. Medial translation of the patellar component**

## Answer explanation

Any factor that increases the Q angle of the extensor mechanism can cause lateral malt racking of the patella. These factors include-

- Internal rotation of the tibial component
- Internal rotation of the femoral component
- Medial translation of the femoral component: it can increase lateral patellar subluxation by moving the trochlea medially.
- Centralization or lateralization of patellar component: the prosthetic patella should be medialized to approximate the median eminence of the normal patella, rather than simply centering the prosthetic button on the available bone.

Answer: 4
