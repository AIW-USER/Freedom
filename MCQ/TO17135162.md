Chapter: Spine MCQs — Scoliosis Kyphosis

## Question

Which of the following syndromes has the highest incidence of scoliosis as a percentage of affected patients:

## Options

1. Marfan syndrome
2. Down syndrome
3. Loeys-Dietz syndrome
4. Rett syndrome

## Correct option

**1. Marfan syndrome**

## Answer explanation

Marfan syndrome, due to a mutation in fibrillin-1, causes spinal deformity in two-thirds of patients. Rett syndrome, caused by a mutation in MEC P-2, results in neurological balance disorder and may cause scoliosis in up to half of affected patients. Scoliosis is

seen in smaller numbers of patients with Prader-Willi, Down, and Loeys-Dietz syndromes

answer: A
