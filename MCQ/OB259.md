## Question

Which treatment factor has been shown to be most important for five-year disease-free survival in patients with subcutaneous sarcomas?

## Options

1. Dose of chemotherapy
2. Addition of adjuvant radiation
3. Size of operative margin
4. Length of chemotherapy course
5. Addition of radiofrequency ablation

## Correct option

**3. Size of operative margin**

## Answer explanation

Basically, there are studies which show microscopic residual tumor when a marginal resection is done. The size of the operative margin is the most important treatment-related factor. If the proper treatment was not done initially for a subcutaneous high-grade sarcoma the patient should be taken back to provide adequate margins.

*Gibbs et al* **also concluded** that size is the most important tumor-related factor, while operative margin is the most important treatment-related factor. Multivariate analysis revealed that a large tumor (greater than five centimeters), a marginal excision, and adjuvant radiation therapy were associated with a worse prognosis with subcutaneous sarcomas.
