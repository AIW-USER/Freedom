## Question

A 28-year-old female with a Campanacci Grade III, sacral giant cell tumor undergoes 6 months of neoadjuvant Denosumab therapy. A follow-up biopsy is performed prior to definitive surgery. Which histological transformation is characteristically observed in GCT specimens following Denosumab administration?

## Options

1. Diffuse liquefactive necrosis and massive aneurysmal cystic changes throughout the entire tumor bed.
2. Complete replacement of the tumor architecture by primitive small round blue cells with a high mitotic index.
3. Elimination of osteoclast-like giant cells accompanied by dense fibro-osseous tissue synthesis, new woven bone formation, and stromal cell spindling.
4. An abundant influx of lipid-laden xanthoma cells and foreign-body type giant cells overriding the native stroma.

## Correct option

**3. Elimination of osteoclast-like giant cells accompanied by dense fibro-osseous tissue synthesis, new woven bone formation, and stromal cell spindling.**

## Answer explanation

Denosumab
characteristically eliminates the giant cells, leading to a marked reduction in
osteolytic activity and inducing a fibro-osseous remodeling response that
mimics a low-grade fibro-osseous lesion.

Answer:
C
