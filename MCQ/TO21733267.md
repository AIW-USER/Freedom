Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Cerebral Palsy (CP)

## Question

CROW walker is most suitable for

## Options

1. Equino varus foot deformity in Polio
2. Charcot foot
3. Calcaneovalgus foot in spinabifida
4. Flexible equinus deformity in cerebral palsy

## Correct option

**2. Charcot foot**

## Answer explanation

Answer : B
Medical management of charcot foot: It can be done by immobilizing the affected limb with a case or protective boot and only permitting non-weight-bearing mobilization with a walking aid.
Contact casting can be done with the casts being changed every 2-4 weeks for 2-4 months.
Total contact casts (TCC) with a controlled ankle motion (CAM) walker can provide protected
weight-bearing. TCC redistributes and reduces pressures on the plantar foot while allowing
ambulation. The hyperemic phase of the charcot’s arthropathy can last for weeks. By
using these types of protective measures, the foot may heal the fractures in a stable position
if the stress does not exceed the rate of healing. The orthotics that can be prescribed is a
Charcot restraint orthotic walker (CROW) boot that can be used after contact casting. A few
modifications to footwear include a double rocker shoe adjustment that can be used in stage
3 patients that will reduce the risk of ulceration at the plantar apex of the deformity.
Bisphosphonates can help in decreasing the bone resorption and activity levels of the
osteoclasts. Pamidronate can help to decrease the symptoms and temperature levels of the
affected limb. Calcitonin has a direct effect on the activity of the osteoclasts and the RANK-L
pathway. This helps to cause a reduction in bone turnover markers.
