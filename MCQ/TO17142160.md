Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Hallux Valgus Bunion

## Question

Which does not indicate a severe Hallux valgus deformity

## Options

1. HVA 45 degree
2. IMA 18 degree
3. DMAA 5 degree
4. HVI 14 degree

## Correct option

**3. DMAA 5 degree**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

Distal metatarsal articular angle up-to 6 degrees is acceptable. HVA>40, IMA> 16, HVI>10 suggest severe deformity
