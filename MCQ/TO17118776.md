## Question

Gold standard to make diagnosis of chondromalacia patellae (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. X ray
2. MRI
3. Arthroscopy
4. CT scan

## Correct option

**3. Arthroscopy**

## Answer explanation

Arthroscopy is the gold standard in making diagnosis of chondromalacia patella.

Answer: 3
