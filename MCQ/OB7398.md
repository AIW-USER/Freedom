## Question

The finding at L3 in the sagittal CT scan shown in Figure 10 is characteristic of which of the following conditions?

![[q7398-a-10.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Paget's disease
2. Hemangioma
3. Giant cell tumor
4. Metastatic disease
5. Chordoma

## Correct option

**2. Hemangioma**

## Answer explanation

The L3 vertebral body shows the classic vertical striations of a vertebral hemangioma. On axial images, these would appear as a collection of bony spots as they are seen end-on. Paget's disease may have areas of sclerotic trabeculae but the vertebrae appear square and enlarged with thickened cortex and demonstrate no organization in the mineral density. Giant cell tumors are more lytic and locally destructive. Metastatic disease can be lytic or sclerotic but does not organize in this pattern. Similarly, chordoma does not have this organization. Chordomas more commonly occur in the sacrum, but may occur in the lumbar vertebrae.
