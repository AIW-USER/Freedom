## Question

A surgeon is evaluating a new anchoring device developed for the reattachment of a distal biceps rupture. The estimate for the weight of the arm is 20 Newtons (N) acting 15 cm from the elbow, and the biceps acts 5 cm from the elbow. To have a safety factor of 3 for the anchor strength at static equilibrium immediately following repair, what is the minimum acceptable pullout strength?

## Options

1. 20N
2. 60N
3. 80N
4. 180N
5. 240N

## Correct option

**4. 180N**

## Answer explanation

The biceps force at equilibrium with a 20N force for the forearm is 60N (Moments about elbow = 20N x 15cm - yN x 5cm; yN = 20N x 15cm/5cm = 60N), multiplied by the safety factor of 3 is 180N.
