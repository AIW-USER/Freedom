## Question

Reverse Hill Sachs lesion involves which part of the humeral head? (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Postero lateral
2. Postero medial
3. Antero lateral
4. Antero medial

## Correct option

**4. Antero medial**

## Answer explanation

While the Hill Sachs lesion is a bone defect on the Posterosuperior-lateral surface of the humeral head, Reverse Hill Sachs lesions involve the Antero-medial surface.

Answer: 4
