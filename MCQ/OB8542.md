## Question

Plain radiographs do not provide an accurate assessment of bone mineral density (BMD) until what percentage of mineral has been lost?

## Options

1. 5%
2. 20%
3. 40%
4. 90%

## Correct option

**3. 40%**

## Answer explanation

Radiographic evidence of BMD loss is not apparent until 40% reduction. Osteopenia should not be ruled out based on an apparently normal mineralized bone.
