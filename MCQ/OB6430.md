## Question

Automated external defibrillators (AEDs) are primarily used for what cardiac rhythm?

## Options

1. Atrial fibrillation
2. Bradycardia
3. Ventricular fibrillation
4. Tachycardia
5. Asystole

## Correct option

**3. Ventricular fibrillation**

## Answer explanation

Automated external defibrillators administer an electric counter shock. Their use may increase survival rates by 90%. They are of little use in other cardiac arrhythmias other than ventricular fibrillation.
