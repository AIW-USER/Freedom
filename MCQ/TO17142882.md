## Question

A 65-year-old female has a 8 month history of low back pain with left side radiculopathy. On examination she has thoracic vertebral tenderness and with 4/5 weak EHL. Reduced sensation is also noted on the dorsum of the left foot. Her blood investigations show anemia and presence of serum monoclonal protein. What would be the next appropriate investigation?
(FNB Pattern Paper 2014)

## Options

1. Bone marrow biopsy
2. Urine electrophoresis
3. CT guided biopsy from thoracic vertebra
4. Lumbar puncture

## Correct option

**1. Bone marrow biopsy**

## Answer explanation

This is a case of multiple myeloma. On suspicion plasma cells> 10% in bone marrow aspiration biopsy confirms the diagnosis.

International myeloma working group criteria for diagnosis of MM (2015)
