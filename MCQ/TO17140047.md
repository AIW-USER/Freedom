## Question

Not true about the tubercle of Assaki?

## Options

1. Cartilage thinning
2. Normal anatomical variants
3. Elevation of glenoid cartilage
4. Depression of humeral head cartilage

## Correct option

**4. Depression of humeral head cartilage**

## Answer explanation

The tubercle of Assaki is a ridge (focal zone of elevation) at the subchondral bone in the center of the glenoid cavity. It is a normal anatomical variant associated with a focal thinning of the overlying cartilage. This should not be mistaken for a cartilage defect.
