Chapter: Sports Medicine MCQs — Sudden Cardiac Death And Sports Concussion

## Question

Most common cause of sudden death in athletes is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Brugada syndrome
2. Coronary artery disease
3. Cardiomyopathy
4. Channelopathy

## Correct option

**3. Cardiomyopathy**

## Answer explanation

Sudden cardiac death (SCD) in sports:

 Causes – In athletes > 35 years, more than 80% of all SCDs are due to atheromatous coronary artery disease. In young athletes’ Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and arrhythmogenic right ventricular cardiomyopathy are most common causes (>50%). Congenital coronary artery anomalies and premature coronary artery atherosclerosis account for 20 % of cases of SCD in young sports people. In structurally normal heart ion channelopathies like congenital long OT syndrome, Brugada syndrome, catecholaminergic polymorphic ventricular tachycardia are the main causes.

High risk sports: Basketball (max deaths reported), American football, soccer.

Marker for high risk for SCDs: Unheralded syncope, LVH> 30mm), non-sustained ventricular tachycardia, family history of SCD, decreased blood pressure in response to exercises.

Answer: 3
