Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Dupuytren’s Contracture

## Question

Which of the following is not true of Dupuytren’s contracture?

## Options

1. The long-term recurrence rate is 50%.
2. Painful nodules are an indication for surgery.
3. Metacarpophalangeal joint (MCPJ) contracture of greater than 30º is an indication for surgery.
4. Myofibroblasts are the offending cells in the aetiology of the disease.

## Correct option

**2. Painful nodules are an indication for surgery.**

## Answer explanation

The disease is usually in its early phases. The stages, according to Luck’s classification, are proliferative, involutional and finally residual. Early surgery will certainly lead to recurrence and can stimulate the disease process. Carpal tunnel surgery must be performed at a separate occasion for a similar reason. Unfortunately in the long term recurrence rates are high (50%).

Answer: 2
