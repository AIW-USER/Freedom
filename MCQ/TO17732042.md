## Question

Main stabilizer of C1-C2 joint is ( general orthopedics- anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. Apical ligament
2. Transverse ligament
3. Paired alar ligament
4. Tectorial membrane

## Correct option

**2. Transverse ligament**

## Answer explanation

Intrinsic ligaments are located within the spinal canal, providing most of the ligamentous stability to the cervico-atlas and atlanto axial joint. They include

- Transverse ligament : It is primary stabilizer of atlantoaxial joint. It connects the posterior odontoid to the anterior atlas arch, inserting laterally on bony tubercles.
- Paired alar ligaments : They contribute to occipital cervical stability. They connect the odontoid to the occipital condyles.
- Apical ligament : It is midline vertical ligament which connects odontoid to foramen magnum.
- Tectorial membrane : It connects the posterior body of the axis to the anterior foramen magnum and is the cephalad continuation of the PLL.

Answer: 2
