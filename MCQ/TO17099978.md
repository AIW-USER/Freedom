Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Periprosthetic Fractures

## Question

A 75 years female sustained a trauma to her right hip following a fall. She had undergone a THR on right side 10 years ago. Her X ray shows a fracture line around the stem starting below the trochanter with a loose stem. What is the correct Vancouver classification and treatment? (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. B1, fixation with plate
2. B2, Long stem revision and fixation with plate
3. B3, Long stem revision and fixation with plate and graft
4. C, fixation with plate

## Correct option

**2. B2, Long stem revision and fixation with plate**

## Answer explanation

Vancouver classification of postoperative periprosthetic femur fractures

Type
Description
Treatment

AL

Fracture at lesser trochanter
Conservative (consider ORIF if large segment of medial cortex involved)

AG
Fracture at greater trochanter
Conservative with abduction precautions (consider ORIF if displaced > 2.5 cm)

B1
Fracture around the stem or extending just below it with well fixed stem
ORIF with or without cortical strut allograft

B2
Fracture around the stem or extending just below it with loose stem
Revision THA with long-stem prosthesis and fixation of fracture fragment with cerclage wire or locking plate

B3
Fracture around the stem or extending just below it with poor bone stock in proximal femur
Revision THA and augmentation of bone stock with allograft versus tumor prosthesis

C
Fracture distal to stem
ORIF

 Answer: B
