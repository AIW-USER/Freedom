## Question

Sequence of clamp removal after carotid endarterectomy

## Options

1. ECA, CCA, ICA
2. ECA, ICA, CCA
3. CCA, ECA, ICA
4. CCA, ICA, ECA

## Correct option

**1. ECA, CCA, ICA**

## Answer explanation

After closure of the arteriotomy, the superior thyroid artery vessel loop is removed to

allow back bleeding while the final prolene suture is placed. Then ECA and CCA clamps

are removed to clear any remaining debris through the external system and then the

ICA clamp is removed.

Answer: 1
