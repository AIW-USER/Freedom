## Correct option

**2. Figure B**

## Answer explanation

Figure B demonstrates congenital vertical talus (CVT) in a 2-year-old patient, which is best treated with serial casting to stretch the dorsolateral soft tissues followed by surgery.

CVT can be difficult to correct and maintain. Treatment typically begins with gentle manipulation and serial casting to stretch the anterior structures. Surgery is typically required and the technique can vary depending on age and ease of reduction. Percutaneous or open reduction and retrograde pinning of the talonavicular joint with percutaneous achilles tenotomy is a standard form of treatment.

*Miller et al. review the management of CVT and discuss the technique for initial serial manipulation and casting.* The head of the talus must be identified and the foot is then plantarflexed and adducted with one hand while the talus is pushed dorsally and laterally with the other. A long leg cast is applied in this position. The talonavicular joint should be reduced after about the 5th weekly cast and then surgical stabilization of the talonavicular joint can be completed.

*Yang et al. performed a retrospective review of 42 feet with CVT.* *24 feet were treated with a minimally invasive approach and 18 feet were treated with a traditional open approach with extensive soft tissue release.* *At an average of 7 year follow-up, the minimally invasive group had significantly superior ROM and function scores.* **They conclude** that a minimally invasive approach is actually superior to an extensive soft tissue release approach for function and ROM.

Incorrect answers:
Figure A: Calcaneovalgus foot, which typically resolves with passive stretching alone
Figure C: Congenital curly toe has no indication for serial casting. Treatment is FDL tenotomy
Figure D: Posteromedial tibial bowing is typically observed and resolves spontaneously, but can cause a limb length discrepancy
Figure E: Fibular deficiency treatment is variable depending the length of the limb and present structures, however, serial casting is typically not part of the treatment
