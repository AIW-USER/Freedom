## Question

A 70 years old male presented with reduced sensation over the dorsal aspect of the first web space of right foot. Which is the most likely site of pathology? (spine- lumbar disc herniation)

## Options

1. L4-5 disc prolapse
2. L3-4 disc prolapse
3. L5-S1 disc prolapse
4. None of the above

## Correct option

**1. L4-5 disc prolapse**

## Answer explanation

Traversing nerve root is generally involved in case of disc (as most commonly prolapses are postero-lateral). The Dorsal aspect of first web space is supplied by L5 dermatome and hence, L4-5 disc prolapse will lead to reduced sensation over dorsal aspect of first web space.

Answer: 1
