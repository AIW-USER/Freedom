## Question

True about preoperative total hip arthroplasty templating are all except (arthroplasty – hip arthroplasty- Anatomy, biomechanics and templating )

## Options

1. AP is taken with 15 degree internal rotation of proximal femur in 20% magnification
2. Placing the new femoral head center of rotation inferior to the acetabular center of rotation will shorten the limb
3. Placing the new femoral head center of rotation inferior to the acetabular center of rotation will lengthen the limb
4. Acetabular COR medial to femur COR will decrease the offset.

## Correct option

**3. Placing the new femoral head center of rotation inferior to the acetabular center of rotation will lengthen the limb**

## Answer explanation

THA templating:

 - X ray of hip AP view is taken with 10-15 degrees of internal rotation of proximal femur. Normally X-rays are taken with 20% magnification.

 - Acetabular center of rotation is the fixed part while templating.

 - Femoral center of rotation will move towards the Acetabular center of rotation

 - if one wants to lengthen the limb ,then while templating femoral center of rotation will be kept higher than Acetabular center of rotation, So that intra operatively Femoral center of rotation moves toward the Acetabular center of rotation giving desired lengthening.

 Hence, Placing the new femoral head center of rotation superior to the acetabular center of rotation will lengthen the limb and vice-versa. Similarly, Acetabular COR medial to femur COR will decrease the offset and vice-versa.
