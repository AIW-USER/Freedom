## Question

Position of humeral stem and glenoid in TSA is [FNB-2014]

## Options

1. Stem in 25–45-degree retroversion with glenoid component in neutral or slight retroversion
2. Stem in slight retroversion with glenoid component in neutral
3. Stem in 15-degree retroversion with glenoid component in neutral or slight retroversion
4. Stem in neutral or slight retroversion with glenoid in slight anteversion or neutral

## Correct option

**1. Stem in 25–45-degree retroversion with glenoid component in neutral or slight retroversion**

## Answer explanation

In the TSA, the stem should be placed in 25–45-degree retroversion. The Glenoid component is placed in neutral or slight retroversion. If glenoid retroversion is required then humeral stem retroversion is reduced to avoid posterior dislocation.

Answer: 1
