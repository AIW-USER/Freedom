## Question

A 65-year-old male underwent a left total hip arthroplasty 9 years ago with an ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene socket and 44 mm cobalt-chromium head. He is presenting with worsening left hip pain. An ultrasound is ordered and reveals a large fluid collection adjacent to the joint. Which of the following is likely to be present?

## Options

1. Intramedullary bone pedestal distal to the femoral stem
2. Increased macrophage activation in biopsied tissue
3. Radiographs with bone formation in the gluteus minimus and capsule
4. Elevated serum cobalt levels
5. Limb length discrepancy

## Correct option

**4. Elevated serum cobalt levels**

## Answer explanation

Trunnionosis presents with asymmetrically elevated cobalt levels and local tissue reactions with fluid collections.

Corrosion of the metal taper between the stem component and the femoral head can lead to local adverse tissue reactions. The proposed mechanism is fretting corrosion, where micromotion between two materials leads to wear. Patients will often present with hip pain and imaging can reveal a fluid associated with an adverse local tissue reaction. Serum levels of cobalt are often substantially elevated, especially with respect to chromium.

*Cooper et al. retrospectively studied ten cases of trunnion corrosion of metal-on-polyethylene total hip prostheses.* **They reported** patients presented with hip pain and swelling with disproportionately elevated serum cobalt ions. **They concluded** the adverse local tissue reaction to trunnionosis is similar to the reaction seen with metallosis from metal-on-metal bearings.

*Nam et al. systematically reviewed the reported advantages and disadvantages in the literature regarding advanced imaging modalities to assess wear-related corrosion problems.* **They reported** that ultrasound is relatively inexpensive but lacks the sensitivity of MRI and CT scans whereas MRI has the potential to predict the presence and severity of adverse local tissue reactions. **They concluded** all three imaging modalities pose a role in the diagnosis of adverse local tissue reactions.

Incorrect answers
Answer 1: Femoral stem loosening can radiographically manifest as a pedestal distal to the femoral stem. This functions to "stabilize" the prosthesis in theory.
Answer 2: Adverse local tissue reactions are characterized by lymphocytic activation by elevated metal particles. Typically, macrophage activation is seen with elevated polyethylene wear particles.
Answer 3: Heterotopic bone formation can manifest with pain and stiffness, but usually does not accompany a fluid collection.
Answer 5: A limb length discrepancy does not often manifest with a fluid collection adjacent to a prosthetic joint.
