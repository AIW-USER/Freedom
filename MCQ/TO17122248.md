## Question

9-month-old boy has short height for age, like his elder brother. He has frontal bossing on Radiograph has champagne like pelvis and Xray changes in the spine. His parents are concerned regarding the deformity at the back, what is the management?

## Options

1. Will Resolve by adolescence
2. Will resolve by 1.5 to 2 years age
3. Will require laminectomy and fusion
4. Will require growth rod insertion

## Correct option

**2. Will resolve by 1.5 to 2 years age**

## Answer explanation

This clinical profile suggests a diagnosis of achondroplasia. Resolution of thoraco-lumber kyphosis (TLK) occurs because of the patient walking and gaining truncal strength. This is achieved after 1 year of age as the motor milestones in a child with Achondroplasia are normal. Bracing is initiated if TLK develops a fixed component >30 degrees, and surgery is reserved for patients with neurologic manifestations or fixed kyphosis >50 degrees.

Answer: 2
