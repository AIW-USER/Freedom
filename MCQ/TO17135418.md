## Question

Causes of vertebra plana are all except ( FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Eosinophilic granuloma
2. Ewing’s sarcoma
3. Lymphoma
4. Lyme’s disease

## Correct option

**4. Lyme’s disease**

## Answer explanation

Answer: Causes of Vertebra Plana (Flat Vertebrae)

Langerhans cell histiocytosis (LCH): Most common cause is Eosinophilic granuloma, a subtype of LCH.

Ewing’s sarcoma

Lymphoma/Leukemia

Gaucher’s disease

Aneurysmal bone cyst

Infection: Spondylitis

Answer: 4
