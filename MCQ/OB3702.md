## Question

A 65-year-old male presents for postoperative follow up after undergoing spinal surgery. His preoperative and postoperative radiographs are shown in Figure A and B respectively. His past medical history is significant for osteoarthritis, hypertension, and smoking 1.5 packs per day for greater than 35 years. Which variable in this patient’s history has been reported to be associated with lower functional outcomes after this surgery?

*Figure A*

![[q3702-b-psf.jpg]]

## Options

1. Age > 60 years old
2. Gender
3. Hypertension
4. Osteoarthritis
5. Smoking

## Correct option

**5. Smoking**

## Answer explanation

The clinical presentation is consistent for degenerative spondylolisthesis that has been treated with an L4/5 fusion. Based on the patient’s history, the risk factor most associated with lower functional outcomes scores after his spinal surgery is smoking.

Smoking is the leading cause of morbidity and mortality in the United States. Nicotine has been shown to increase aggregation of platelets. Carbon monoxide (also contained in cigarette smoke) displaces oxygen from hemoglobin resulting in a lower oxygen tension in tissues. Subsequently, rates of post-operative infections and pseudoarthrosis are elevated. This leads to decreased post-operative functional outcomes in patients who smoke.

*Mok et al. studied 89 patients to identify patient specific factors predicting complications after fusion for adult spinal deformity.* **They found** that in the patients undergoing reoperations, rate of smoking was significantly higher versus those who did not undergo revision surgery.

*Slover et al. studied 3482 patients undergoing lumbar spine surgery to identify medical and psychosocial factors that could potentially influence clinical outcomes.* **They found** that smoking negatively impacted SF-36 and Owestry disability index scores.

*Emery et al. studied the effect of steroid administration on rate of delayed extubation following multiple anterior corpectomy procedures with anterior strut graft reconstruction.* Only female gender was a statistically significant predictor of delayed extubation in this study.

Figures A and B show preoperative and post-operative lateral radiographs of a lumbar spine. Figure A shows degenerative spondylolisthesis and Figure B shows the posteroperative correction after a posterolateral spinal fusion with inter body cage placement.

Incorrect Answers
Answers 1, 2, 3, 4: Only smoking has been shown to be associated with lower functional outcomes.
