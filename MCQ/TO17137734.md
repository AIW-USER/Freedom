## Question

PFN A2 as compared to PFN A has

## Options

1. Less chances of perforation of lateral cortex
2. Smaller proximal diameter
3. Smaller blade diameter
4. All of the above

## Correct option

**4. All of the above**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D

THE PROXIMAL FEMORAL NAIL (PFN) SYSTEM

A number of cephalomedullary devices have been introduced for unstable intertrochanteric (IT) fracture fixations over the years (see pic below) by various companies. Proximal Femoral Nail (PFN by AO) has particularly been a common implant used across India.

![[TO17137734-1.jpg]]

PFN: There are three types of implants

- Conventional PFN
- PFN A
- PFN A2

Conventional PFN is the one with two screws - Compression screw and anti-rotation screw. Although PFN was designed for unstable IT fractures, conventional PFN was met with high failure rates, especially due to screw migrations: The Z effect and the Reverse Z effect.

The Z-effect involved the lateral migration of the lag screw, varus collapse and perforation of the femoral head by the superior de-rotation screw. The reverse Z-effect involved the lateral migration of the superior de-rotation screw accompanied by the medial migration of the lag screw.

![[TO17137734-2.png]]

![[TO17137734-2.png]]

The screws that required drilling/ reaming removed lots of bone that was already osteoporotic limiting purchase and causing unwanted migrations so the concept of single helical blade was introduced. Not only does the single blade makes technique of insertion easier, since it’s inserted unreamed so less of bone is removed so more stock left in osteoporotic bone.

![[TO17137734-3.jpg]]

Fig: PFN A with helical blade (introduced by AO in 2003)

So, PFN A and PFN A2 came up as two design variants of Synthes BOTH WITH SINGLE HELICAL BLADE SCREW. Difference between them lies in geometric proportions of nails as PFN A2 has been specifically designed to better fit the Asian population.

In PFN A2, the following adaptions were made to better fit to the small trochanteric area and narrow IM canal of the Asian population: -

- Lateral flat surface to ease insertion, and reduce pressure on the lateral cortex
- Higher bending point resulting in a straighter tip part
- 5° Medial-Lateral Bending
- Smaller proximal diameter (©️ 16.5 vs 17.0 mm) and smaller blade diameter
