## Question

Byrd C sign is seen in (regional orthopedics- pelvis and hip- miscellaneous)

## Options

1. Intra Articular Hip pathology
2. Intra Articular knee pathology
3. Intra Articular ankle pathology
4. Intra Articular shoulder pathology

## Correct option

**1. Intra Articular Hip pathology**

## Answer explanation

Byrd has described the classic "C" sign of intra-articular hip pathology where the patient places their index finger over the anterior aspect of the hip and thumb over the posterior trochanteric region to indicate the location of their pain.
