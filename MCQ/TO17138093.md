Chapter: Nerve Injury MCQs — Radial Nerve Palsy

## Question

A young man developed wrist drop and finger drop after a humeral shaft fracture. Patient was managed with ORIF with anterolateral plating. Nerve was explored during surgery and found to be intact. If spontaneous recovery occurs which motor function is expected to recover first?

## Options

1. Forearm supination
2. Wrist extension
3. Elbow extension
4. MCP joint extension

## Correct option

**2. Wrist extension**

## Answer explanation

In radial nerve palsy recovery occurs in a proximal to distal direction. Wrist extension in radial deviation is expected to be recovered first and MCP joint extension last. Brachioradialis function recovers first and extensor indicis in the last.

Answer: 2
