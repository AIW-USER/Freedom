## Question

During total knee replacement after implantation and final reduction the surgeon finds lateral patellar subluxation with knee flexion. What cannot be the likely reason? (arthroplasty- knee arthroplasty- patellar maltracking)

## Options

1. Internal femoral component rotation
2. Residual valgus alignment
3. Medial shift of the femoral component
4. Medial placement of patellar component

## Correct option

**4. Medial placement of patellar component**

## Answer explanation

Causes of patellar maltracking after TKR

- Femoral component internal rotation
- Femoral component medial placement
- Internal rotation of tibial component
- Flexed femoral component
- Residual valgus alignment
- Preoperative malalignment- excessive femoral anteversion, tibial internal rotation etc.
- Lateral placement of patellar button- Placement of the patellar component in a more medial position relative to the center of the retropatellar surface is considered beneficial because it replicates the medialized position of the native median eminence.
- Asymmetrical patellar thickness

Answer: 4
