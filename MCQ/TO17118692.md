## Question

A 32 years male has constant knee pain which makes it difficult to carry out his routine work. He has a history of injury to his knee joint 1 month ago. On examination medial joint line tenderness is present. His MRI showed a 5 cm2 osteochondral defect of medial femoral condyle. Which is the best procedure for him? (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Microfracture
2. ACI
3. Unicondylar TKR
4. Mosaicplasty

## Correct option

**2. ACI**

## Answer explanation

Cartilage repair procedures and their indications:

- Marrow stimulation techniques: These include abrasion chondroplasty and microfracture technique. These techniques are preferred in chondral defect <2 cm2
- Osteochondral grafting: Defects of up to 5 cm can be grafted
- Autologous chondrocyte implantation (ACI): Large grade IV osteochondral defects (2 to 10 cm2) can be treated by ACI.

Answer: 2
