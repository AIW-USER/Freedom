## Question

Which law is not pertaining to growth plate? [FNB 2023]

## Options

1. Hueter–Volkmann law
2. Delpeche’s law
3. Wolff’s law
4. Maxwell’s law

## Correct option

**4. Maxwell’s law**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 4
Laws of the physis
Hueter–Volkmann law: ‘Increased compression at the growth plate slows longitudinal growth.’
Delpeche's law: ‘Increasing tension on the growth plate speeds growth.’
Wolff's law: ‘Bone adapts according to loads under which it is placed.’
