Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Aneurysmal Bone Cyst

## Question

Which of the following statements is correct in relationship to primary aneurysmal bone cyst?

## Options

1. Posterior half of vertebral body is the most common involved area in spine
2. Current standard of care is Extended Curettage plus bone grafting
3. Presence of fluid levels on MRI confirms the diagnosis of primary Aneurysmal Bone Cyst
4. It is associated with USP6 gene rearrangement on Chromosome 17

## Correct option

**4. It is associated with USP6 gene rearrangement on Chromosome 17**

## Answer explanation

ABC most commonly involves posterior elements ( lamina , spinous process etc ) of the vertebra.
 Percutaneous Sclerotherapy is the current standard of care (surpassing extended curettage) for treating ABC where in a J needle is inserted into the cavity, contents aspirated, septa broken and walls of cavity scraped with the bevelled tip of J needle. 3% polidocanol is then injected into the lesion which damages the cystic lining of the cavity causing a healing response.
Fluid levels are seen in aneurysmal bone cyst on MRI but can also be seen in Giant Cell Tumours, Chondroblastoma, Telangiectatic Osteosarcoma, vascular tumours etc. In primary ABC, lesion only has fluid levels throughout but in other lesions there will be solid component along with the fluid levels.
Invariably USP6 gene rearrangement is found in all cases of ABC underlying it’s neoplastic origin.
Answer: 4
