## Question

A 20-year-old female sustains an open tibia fracture and is set to undergo intramedullary nail insertion. Before the start of the case, the attending asks you about the mechanism of bone morphogenetic protein-2 (BMP-2) in anticipation of possible utilization of rhBMP-2. What protein complexes with Smad1, Smad5, and Smad8 to affect gene transcription?

## Options

1. Smad 2 and 3
2. Smad 6 and 7
3. Smad 4
4. Runx2
5. TGF-ß

## Correct option

**3. Smad 4**

## Answer explanation

Smads 1, 5, and 8 form complexes with a co-mediating Smad (Smad4), which can then enter the nucleus and affect gene transcription (Answer 3).

The Smad signaling pathway is essential in osteoblastic and osteoclastic differentiation during skeletal development, bone formation and homeostasis. BMP signaling is initiated when an extracellular BMP-2 protein binds BMP receptors on a cell's surface. The intracellular domain of these cell surface receptors can form complexes with regulatory Smad1, 5, and 8 through phosphorylation. These regulatory Smads then form complexes with Smad4, which is neither phosphorylated nor bound by cell membrane BMP receptors, allowing the complex to enter the nucleus and influence the transcription of genes.

*Zou et al. detailed the Smad signaling pathway, specifically, to summarize the role of Smad proteins in the mediation of the BMP and transforming growth factor-β (TGF-β) signaling pathways to better understand their critical role in the regulation of osteoblast and osteoclast function.* Ultimately, to facilitate the understanding of Smad modulation as a potential target in treating osteoporosis.

*Rahman et al. reviewed the critical role of the TGF-β/BMP pathway.* *They determine that both pathways are crucial for bone organogenesis, with disruptions linked to various clinical outcomes such as skeletal anomalies, autoimmune diseases, cancer, and cardiovascular conditions.* Activation of the TGF-β/BMP receptors leads to Smad phosphorylation, with interactions involving pathways like mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK), Wingless-related integration site (Wnt), Hedgehog, mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR), and microRNA (miRNA) contributing to the complex regulation of bone dynamics.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Smad 2 and 3 do not form a mediating complex with Smad 1,5 and 8
Answer 2: Smad 6 and 7 are inhibitory proteins
Answer 4: Runx2 is a transcription factor that is not known to interact with BMP receptors
Answer 5: TGF-β binds to its independent cell surface receptor and is not known to interact directly with the Smad 1, 5, and 8 complex
