Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Elbow Arthrodesis

## Question

Optimal position of unilateral elbow arthrodesis is

## Options

1. 90-degree flexion with slight valgus
2. 90-degree flexion with slight varus
3. 110-degree flexion with slight valgus
4. 110-degree flexion with slight varus

## Correct option

**1. 90-degree flexion with slight valgus**

## Answer explanation

Optimal position for elbow arthrodesis:

In a unilateral arthrodesis elbow should be fixed in 90 degree of flexion and neutral to slight valgus

In a bilateral arthrodesis one elbow should be fixed in 110 ° of flexion for feeding and one elbow in 65 ° of flexion for perineal hygiene.

Answer: 1
