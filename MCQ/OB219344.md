## Question

You are attending a lecture on autoimmune conditions. Rheumatoid factor is discussed in great detail with respect to rheumatoid arthritis. Which of the following most appropriately describes rheumatoid factor?

## Options

1. IgM antibody against native IgG antibodies
2. IgM antibody against native IgA antibodies
3. IgA antibody against native IgG antibodies
4. IgG antibody against native IgM antibodies
5. IgG antibody against native IgA antibodies

## Correct option

**1. IgM antibody against native IgG antibodies**

## Answer explanation

Rheumatoid factor (RF) is an IgM antibody against native IgG antibodies.

Rheumatoid arthritis is a chronic systemic autoimmune disease caused by IgM cell-mediated immune response against soft tissues, cartilage, and bone. Patients present with insidious onset of morning joint stiffness, polyarthropathy, and subcutaneous nodules, with progressive hand and wrist deformity. Diagnosis is made with a combination of physical examination, characteristic periarticular erosions on radiographs, and labs to evaluate for the presence of RF and anti-CCP antibodies. Treatment is primarily medical management with NSAIDS, disease-modifying anti-rheumatic drugs (DMARDS), biologics, antimalarials, and corticosteroids.

*Saleh et al. reviewed rheumatoid arthritis as an autoimmune disease mediated by a widespread chronic systemic inflammatory process that causes joint deterioration, which leads to pain, disability, and poor quality of life.* **They reported** that the increased use of disease-modifying anti-rheumatic drugs has been shown to markedly slow disease progression, which has translated into a decrease in the need for orthopedic intervention in this population. **They concluded**, however, that a substantial percentage of patients with the disease fail optimal pharmacologic treatment and still require surgical intervention.

*Howe et al. reviewed the treatment of rheumatoid arthritis.* **They reported** that despite advances in medications, in some patients, inflammation is not diminished sufficiently to prevent irreversible musculoskeletal damage, thus requiring surgical intervention to reduce pain and improve function. **They concluded** that consultation with a rheumatologist is recommended, but the surgeon also should be aware of these medications that could potentially affect surgical outcomes.

*McInnes et al. reviewed the pathogenesis and treatment of rheumatoid arthritis.* **They report** that the introduction of targeted biologic and synthetic disease-modifying anti-rheumatic drugs have transformed clinical outcomes. However, many patients experience partial response and non-response to treatment, failure to achieve immune homeostasis or drug-free remission, and inability to repair damaged tissues. **They concluded** that RA results in inflammation and immune reactivity that are primarily localized to the synovium leading to pain and articular damage but is also associated with a broader series of comorbidities.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 2-5: Rheumatoid factor is an IgM antibody against native IgG antibodies.
