## Question

After 4 weeks of ACL reconstruction the patient complains of knee swelling, pain and fever. ESR is 105 and CRP is 44. What should be the next step? (arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- ACL)

## Options

1. Arthroscopic debridement, graft retention and IV antibiotics
2. Removal of ACL graft and synovectomy and IV antibiotics
3. Open synovectomy and removal of graft and IV antibiotics
4. Graft retention and IV antibiotics

## Correct option

**1. Arthroscopic debridement, graft retention and IV antibiotics**

## Answer explanation

Treatment options for infection after ACL surgery include long-term intravenous antibiotics, arthroscopic or open washout and debridement, constant joint irrigation, and graft retention or removal with or without re-implantation. There is no consensus for optimal treatment and decision regarding retention or removal of graft. The most frequently used treatment is joint irrigation and debridement with graft retention. Some authors have suggested removal of graft but most others advocate graft removal only in cases of persistent infection. Decision regarding open or arthroscopic debridement is based on Gaechter grading system for septic arthritis of the knee. For Gaechter grades 1 and 2, arthroscopic treatment is suggested. For grades 3 and 4, medial or lateral arthrotomy is suggested.

Gachter criteria :

Stage 1 Blurred effusion, hyperemia synovium

Stage 2 Purulent effusion, fibrin deposits, hypertrophic synovium

Stage 3 Synovial adhesions, necrotic areas in the synovium and cartilage

Stage 4 Diffuse necrosis in the cartilage, bone erosion and osteolysis

Answer: 1
