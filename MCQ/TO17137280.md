Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Pediatric Lateral and Medial Condyle Fracture

## Question

Treatment of choice for well-established lateral condyle nonunion is

## Options

1. Observation till maturity
2. Open reduction, bone grafting and fixation
3. Varus osteotomy
4. Watchful neglect

## Correct option

**3. Varus osteotomy**

## Answer explanation

Surgery for well-established nonunion of lateral condyle fracture is difficult, and the goals should be to restore a more anatomic alignment of the elbow. Arthrotomy and realignment of the articular surface should be avoided because of the high risk of further elbow stiffness and osteonecrosis; osteotomy generally is a better option. Rigid internal fixation should be used to promote early motion. Tardy ulnar nerve palsy can accompany lateral condyle nonunion and can be treated with ulnar nerve transposition and correction of the cubitus valgus

Answer: 3
