## Question

Figure A is the radiograph of a 62-year-old female that presents with complaints of worsening shoulder pain. You discuss with your attending physician several potential causes for this patient's advanced joint destruction. Which of the following is the most common etiology of neuropathic arthropathy of the upper extremity?

![[q217931-a-shoulder-1.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Chronic alcoholism
2. Diabetes
3. Neurosyphilis
4. Hansen's disease (leprosy)
5. Syringomyelia

## Correct option

**5. Syringomyelia**

## Answer explanation

Syringomyelia is the most common etiology of neuropathic arthropathy of the upper extremity.

Neuropathic (charcot) shoulder is a chronic and progressive joint disease most commonly caused by syringomyelia leading to the destruction of the shoulder joint and surrounding structures. Arnold-Chiari malformation is the most common cause of syringomyelia. Diagnosis is made with radiographs of the shoulder and supplemented with cervical spine MRI to assess for a syrinx. A syrinx formation (fluid-filled cavity) in spinal cord causes damage to the decussating fibers of the lateral spinothalamic tract leading to loss of pain and temperature sensation. Treatment should be individualized based on degree of functional limitation and underlying neurological condition. Neurosurgical decompression is indicated in the presence of a syrinx.

*Drvaric et al. reviewed a case of neuropathic arthropathy of the shoulder secondary to syringomyelia.* **They reported** that pain, loss of motion, and instability are most common findings. **They concluded** that the use of MRI in evaluation is important in the diagnosis.

*Hatzis et al. retrospectively reviewed patients with neuropathic arthropathy of the shoulder.* **They reported** that the etiology of the neuropathic condition was syringomyelia in five patients (six shoulders) and chronic alcoholism in one patient. **They concluded** that while radiographs revealed destruction of the shoulder joint and marked resorption of the humeral head, MRI revealed a syrinx of the central cord in all of the patients except for the one who had chronic alcoholism.

Figure A depicts a neuropathic (charcot) shoulder that was caused secondary to syringomyelia.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Chronic alcoholism is an associated condition but is not the most common cause of advanced shoulder destruction.
Answer 2: Diabetes is the most common cause of foot and ankle neuropathic joints.
Answer 3: Neurosyphilis usually affects the knee and can be polyarticular.
Answer 4: Hansen's disease is the second most common cause of upper extremity neuropathic arthropathy.
