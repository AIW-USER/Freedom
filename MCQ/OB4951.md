## Question

An 10-year-old African-American boy presents to the emergency department with complaint of right hip pain and limp. Symptoms have been present for one week. His medical history is positive for hypothyroidism, and his weight exceeds the 99th percentile for his age. Figure A is an AP pelvis and Figure B is a frog-leg lateral or the right hip. All of the following factors would lead you to recommend prophylactic treatment of the left hip EXCEPT:

![[q4951-a-scfe-ap-severe.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q4951-b-scfe-frog-severe.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Age 10 years
2. Severity of displacement
3. History of hypothyroidism
4. Obesity
5. Symptoms for one week

## Correct option

**5. Symptoms for one week**

## Answer explanation

Age less than 10 years in girls and 12 years in boys, severity of displacement, hypothyroidism, and obesity, are all risk factors for slippage and indications for prophylactic treatment of the hip opposite of that with presenting symptoms. The chronicity of the injury is not a known risk factor for subsequent slipped capital femoral epiphysis (SCFE) of the contralateral hip. Thus, having symptoms for one week would not encourage prophylactic fixation of the left hip.

Prophylactic fixation of the contralateral hip remains a controversial subject. However, given the morbidity of a displaced SCFE, many consider it appropriate to prophylactically fix the contralateral hip in patients who are considered high risk, accepting the disease risks are higher than risk for prophylactic fixation.

*Yildirim et. al. present a retrospective review of children who presented and were treated for a unilateral SCFE and followed for 24 months.* **They found** that a subsequent slip of the contralateral side occurred in 36% of patients, and correlated with younger age at presentation and severity of the initial slip.

*Riad et al evaluated chronological age at presentation, sex, race and status of triradiate cartilage and a modified Oxford bone age were assessed.* In this analysis, chronological age was the only significant predictor for developing a contralateral slip

Figures A and B demonstrate a SCFE of the right hip.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1-4: Age 10 years, severity of displacement of the right hip, hypothyroidism, and obesity, are all risk factors for slippage and potential indications for prophylactic treatment.
