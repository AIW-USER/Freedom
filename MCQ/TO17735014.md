## Question

What is true about dural tears ? (spine- spinal cord injury)

## Options

1. Posterior tear are commonest
2. Fibrin glue is the best treatment
3. Suturing should be done whenever possible
4. Small tear can be left as such

## Correct option

**3. Suturing should be done whenever possible**

## Answer explanation

Most common location of the dural injury is the lateral lesion, followed by injuries of the root sheath and root axilla.

Small tears not amenable to suturing should be managed with tight fascial and skin closures but sutured wherever possible as Suturing is the best way to treat dural tears. If this is not possible, there are various sealants to be used our of which Tachosil and fibrin glue are the best.

Answer C
