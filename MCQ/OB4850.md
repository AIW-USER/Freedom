## Question

A 12-year-old male previously presented with 4 days of left groin pain and inability to bear weight. Radiographs taken preoperatively, 1 week and 8 months postoperatively are seen in Figures A through C. What complication has occurred?

![[q4850-b-fig-b.jpg]]
*Figure B*

![[q4850-c-fig-c.jpg]]
*Figure C*

## Options

1. Hardware migration
2. Secondary loss of fixation
3. Slip progression
4. Avascular necrosis
5. Chondrolysis

## Correct option

**4. Avascular necrosis**

## Answer explanation

This patient underwent fixation of a left SCFE. There is late AVN on follow-up radiographs at 8 months.

SCFE is usually fixed with cannulated screws to prevent further slippage and promote physeal closure. Factors believed to be responsible for the development of AVN in SCFE include unstable SCFE, over-reduction of an acute slip, attempted reduction of a chronic slip, pins in the superolateral quadrant, and femoral neck osteotomy.
 
*Lubicky reviewed chondrolysis and AVN in SCFE.* Chondrolysis is believed to be related to hardware penetration into the joint. An immune mechanism has likewise been proposed, as there is reported incidence without hardware use. AVN is believed to be related to impeded blood supply by initial displacement or forceful reduction and improper placement of fixation devices.

*Sankar et al. reviewed the development of AVN in 70 unstable SCFE patients.* **They found** AVN was more likely to develop in younger patients (11.7 vs 12.8 years) with a shorter duration of prodromal symptoms (17.5 vs 65.9 days).

Figure A is an AP radiograph showing acute unstable SCFE of the left hip. Figure B is an AP and frog-leg lateral radiograph showing fixation with with 2 cannulated screws. Figure C shows sclerosis and collapse of the left femoral head characteristic of AVN.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: There is no migration of hardware.
Answer 2: There is no secondary loss of fixation.
Answer 3: Slip progression has not occurred. It may occur following secondary loss of fixation as existing hardware loses purchase of the epiphysis. Slip progression following hardware removal is also known as secondary SCFE.
Answer 5: There is no evidence of chondrolysis.
