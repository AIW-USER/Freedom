## Question

A 62-year-old patient with rheumatoid factor positive rheumatoid arthritis presents with complaints of left elbow pain. Radiographs reveals significant joint destruction. The patient is scheduled to undergo total elbow arthroplasty. Which of the following most accurately describes rheumatoid factor?

## Options

1. IgM antibody against native IgG antibodies
2. IgG antibody against native IgE antibodies
3. IgM antibody against native IgA antibodies
4. IgA antibody against native IgG antibodies
5. IgG antibody against native IgM antibodies

## Correct option

**1. IgM antibody against native IgG antibodies**

## Answer explanation

Rheumatoid factor is an IgM antibody against native IgG antibodies.

Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) is a chronic systemic autoimmune disease caused by IgM cell-mediated immune response against soft tissues, cartilage, and bone. Patients present with insidious onset of morning joint stiffness, polyarthropathy, subcutaneous nodules, with progressive hand and wrist deformity. Diagnosis is made with a combination of physical examination, characteristic radiographs, and labs to evaluate for the presence of RF and anti-CCP antibodies. Treatment is primarily medical management with NSAIDS, DMARDS, biologics, antimalarials, and steroids.

*Gill et al. reviewed patients with rheumatoid arthritis who were managed with a Coonrad-Morrey total elbow arthroplasty (TEA).* **They reported** that the rate of survival of the prosthesis was 92.4%. **They concluded** that patients had 86% good or excellent and 14% fair or poor results.

*Larsen et al. reviewed radiographic evaluations of RA.* **They reported** that standard reference films are very important in evaluating elbow destruction related to RA. **They concluded** that a numerical evaluation of arthritis may be applied to patients with RA.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 2-5: Rheumatoid factor is an IgM antibody against native IgG antibodies.
