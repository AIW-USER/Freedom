## Question

Figure 72 is the MRI scan of a 61-year-old man who had left shoulder pain with a massive rotator cuff tear. Active forward elevation was 120 degrees. Arthroscopic examination revealed that the rotator cuff tear was irreparable. The articular surfaces of the glenohumeral joint have a normal appearance without significant degenerative changes. What is the most appropriate treatment option?

![[q8633-a-72.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Biceps tenotomy
2. Loose body removal
3. Latissimus dorsi transfer
4. Reverse total shoulder arthroplasty

## Correct option

**1. Biceps tenotomy**

## Answer explanation

The MRI scan shows medial subluxation of the biceps tendon. Biceps tenotomy has been an effective treatment option for patients with large to massive rotator cuff tears when the tear is irreparable and pain is the main symptom. The MRI scan does not show a loose body. Patients with severe external rotation deficit and a deficient teres minor may experience a better functional result with latissimus dorsi transfer. Reverse total shoulder arthroplasty is an option in patients with cuff tear arthropathy and pseudoparalysis.
