## Question

In pediatric forearm fracture which deformity limits the rotation maximum (trauma- fractures of radius and ulna)

## Options

1. Malrotation
2. Angulation
3. Bayonet apposition
4. Hypertrophic Nonunion

## Correct option

**2. Angulation**

## Answer explanation

Pediatric forearm fracture:

10 degree of malrotation restricts 10 degrees of rotation, 10 degree of angulation restricts 20 degrees of rotation whereas bayonet apposition (bones uniting side by side with some overlapping) does not limit rotation.

Acceptable angular deformity after femoral shaft in fracture reduction

In children up to 2 years, anterior –posterior angulation up to 30-40 degrees is acceptable. It decreases to 10 degrees in older children and adolescents. Varus angulation in infants and children should be less than 15 degrees. Acceptable valgus angulation is up to 30 degrees in infants, 20 degrees in children up to 5 years, and 10 degrees in older children.
