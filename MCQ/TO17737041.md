## Question

In KD IV injuries most common pattern of cruciate injury include [TRAUMA-Knee Dislocation]

## Options

1. ACL mid substance tear and PCL femoral avulsion
2. ACL tibial avulsion and PCL tibial avulsion
3. Both midsubstance tear
4. ACL midsubstance tear and PCL Tibial avulsion

## Correct option

**1. ACL mid substance tear and PCL femoral avulsion**

## Answer explanation

Most anterior cruciate ligament injuries associated with knee dislocations are midsubstance tears (45%), followed by femoral avulsions (34%) and tibial avulsions (21%). The posterior cruciate ligament injury is most often a femoral avulsion (76%), followed by midsubstance tear (17%) and tibial avulsion (7%).

Also know: Non-contact ACL injury (that occur without physical contact between athletes) are more common than contact ACL injuries. Most common mechanisms of ACL tear are solid foot plant with rotation of the knee, and solid foot plant with valgus stress on the knee.

Answer: 1
