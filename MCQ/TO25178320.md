Chapter: General Orthopedics MCQs — Bone Cement

## Question

In the context of 'high-dose' antibiotic cement used for non-weight-bearing spacers, what is the typical threshold where mechanical strength begins to compromise significantly?

## Options

1. When the antibiotic is pre-mixed by the manufacturer rather than hand-mixed.
2. When the antibiotic concentration exceeds 2g per 40g of cement powder.
3. Only when multiple different antibiotics are used simultaneously regardless of weight.
4. When the antibiotic concentration exceeds 3.6g to 4g per 40g of cement powder.

## Correct option

**4. When the antibiotic concentration exceeds 3.6g to 4g per 40g of cement powder.**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 4

At these therapeutic levels, the cement becomes too brittle for permanent load-bearing fixation but is ideal for antibiotic spacers.
