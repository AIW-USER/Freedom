## Question

A new physician is seeking employment at a hospital. Based on the proposed employment contract, the physician will be an independent contractor. Which of the following statements are true regarding independent contractor employment?

## Options

1. The employer must pay social security and federal income taxes on earnings
2. The employer is required to provide training related to the employment
3. The employee determines the result of the work
4. The employee determines the means and methods of the work
5. The employer must provide retirement benefits to the employee under this agreement

## Correct option

**4. The employee determines the means and methods of the work**

## Answer explanation

An independent contractor is a person who determines the means and methods of the result and the employer determines the result itself.

The internal revenue service (IRS) defines an independent contractor based on the control the employer has over the individual. If the individual owns the means or methods of the work then they are considered an independent contractor. From a tax standpoint, the employers of independent contractors are not required to pay for social security, federal or state income taxes, offer health insurance, offer pension or retirement plans, or vacation pay.

The IRS discusses the definitions of behavior and financial control in employees versus independent contractors. They define behavioral control as a means for a business to direct and control how work is completed. Information can be found at [https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc762](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc762)

The IRS further defines behavioral control, financial control, and relationships regarding independent contractors. They define training related to the intended job to be the responsibility of the independent contractor and receive payment for their services at a flat rate. More information can be found at [https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/understanding-employee-vs-contractor-designation](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/understanding-employee-vs-contractor-designation)

Incorrect answers
Answer 1: Independent contractor employers are not required to pay social security and federal income taxes for the employee.
Answer 2: Employers of independent contractors are not required to provide job-related training.
Answer 3: Employers of independent contractors determine the result of the work, whereas the employee determines the means and methods to achieve the result.
Answer 5: Employers of an independent contractor are not required to provide retirement benefits to the employee.
