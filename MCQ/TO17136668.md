## Question

Which is a major criteria for fat embolism? (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. Tachycardia over 110 beats/minute
2. Pyrexia > 38.50 C
3. Retinal changes of fat or petechiae
4. Petechial rash

## Correct option

**4. Petechial rash**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 4

Gurd and Wilson's Diagnostic criteria for Fat embolism Syndrome.

Major criteria

- Respiratory symptoms and signs (hypoxemia)and radiographic changes.
- Cerebral signs unrelated to head injury or other conditions.
- Petechial rash.

Minor criteria

- Tachycardia over 110 beats/minute
- Pyrexia > 38.50 C
- Retinal changes of fat or petechiae
- Renal changes
- Jaundice
- Laboratory:
- Acute fall in hemoglobin
- Sudden thrombocytopenia
- High ESR
- Fat macroglobulinemia

One major and four minor criteria, and fat macroglobulinemia are required for diagnosis.
