## Question

Functions of sternocleidomastoid are all except (General orthopedics- anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. Rotate the neck to opposite side
2. Rotate the neck to same side
3. Flexion of neck and extension of head
4. Help in lifting head from pillow while getting up in bed

## Correct option

**2. Rotate the neck to same side**

## Answer explanation

SCM pulls the mastoid process down toward the same side which causes the chin to turn up toward the opposite side. By this action it rotates the head to the opposite side and flexes the head slightly to the same side. When both SCM contract together they flex the neck and extend the head. It is also an accessory muscle of inspiration.

Answer: 2
