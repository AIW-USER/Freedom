Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Cerebral Palsy (CP)

## Question

In the context of Single-Event Multilevel Surgery (SEMLS), a patient undergoes a Supracondylar Extension Osteotomy (SCEO) for a fixed knee flexion deformity. To optimize the 'Extensor Power' and prevent the recurrence of crouch, which concomitant procedure is often mandatory if the Preoperative Physical Exam revealed a Migration Percentage of 30% and a positive Coleman block test?

## Options

1. Fractional lengthening of the iliopsoas at the pelvic brim.
2. Medial hamstrings lengthening (Semimembranosus and Semitendinosus).
3. Distalization and plication of the patellar tendon.
4. Sub-talar Arthroereisis to stabilize the foot.

## Correct option

**3. Distalization and plication of the patellar tendon.**

## Answer explanation

Chronic crouch leads to patella alta and a functionally 'long' extensor mechanism; shortening the tendon is vital to restore the quadriceps' mechanical advantage after bony correction.

Answer: 3
