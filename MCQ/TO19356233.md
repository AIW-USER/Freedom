## Question

Critical period of delay of recovery in Radial nerve palsy [FNB 2023]

## Options

1. 9 months
2. 12 months
3. 10 months
4. 15 months

## Correct option

**4. 15 months**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D
EXPLANATION: -
Critical period of recovery is the time zone beyond which return of function (particularly motor) is not to be expected in case nerve repair is delayed beyond this time zone. Table below gives the critical period of delay for some important peripheral nerves from upper and lower limbs: -
Radial nerve: 15 months
PIN: 9 months
Ulnar nerve high lesions: 9 months
Ulnar nerve low lesions: 15 months
Median nerve high lesions: 9 months
Median nerve low lesions: 12 months
Sciatic nerve: 12-15 months
CPN: 12 months
