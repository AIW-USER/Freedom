## Question

A patient with bladder problem presents with urinary urgency, urge incontinence and nocturia. On investigation, he has normal bladder volume and pressures. There is some detrusor over activity but no detrusor hypertrophy, no detrusor-sphincter dyssynergia. There is loss of voluntary micturition control. The likely location of the lesion is?

## Options

1. pelvic nerves
2. below the S2 spinal segment
3. above the S2 spinal segment
4. above the pons

## Correct option

**4. above the pons**

## Answer explanation

The micturition centre lies within the pons in the locus cereuleus. The micturition is initiated with

the sensation of bladder filling, it coordinates the detrusor contraction and sphincter relaxation.

The cortex controls the voluntary inhibition of micturition reflex, thus preventing the reflex

urination when the circumstances are not favorable.

For lesions above the pons

There is loss of voluntary inhibition of micturition.

There is mild detrusor over activity (DO) but no detrusor-sphincter dyssynergia (DSD).

There is no bladder hypertrophy and the bladder volume and pressures are normal.

For lesions above the S2

There is significant DSD.

Bladder pressures are high and rise in bladder volumes.

Results in Functional Outlet Obstruction.

For lesions below the S2 (incl pelvic nerves)

There is loss of detrusor activity.

No DSD.

Bladder volumes are high and pressures are reduced.
