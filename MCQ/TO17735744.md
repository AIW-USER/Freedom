## Question

Advantages of tight body cloths in athletes are all except: (sports medicine- principles of training and recovery)

## Options

1. Decreased turbulence
2. Increased speed
3. Decreased surface drag
4. Decreased buoyancy

## Correct option

**4. Decreased buoyancy**

## Answer explanation

Newer paper-thin body tight designer fabric cuts surface drag. In these clothes seams and zip also follow the contour of the body and are bonded smoothly to the fabric. In sports like swimming this is important because seams may increase the turbulence and thus drag if they cut across the flow of water. In swimmers these body tight suits design decreases drag, increases buoyancy and thus may enhance the performance.

Answer: 4
