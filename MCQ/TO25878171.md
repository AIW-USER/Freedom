Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Unicondylar and Patellofemoral Arthroplasty

## Question

A patient with medial compartment osteoarthritis undergoes a lateral stress radiograph that demonstrates full thickness preservation of the lateral joint space. However, the lateral view shows the wear is located on the posterior third of the medial tibial plateau. Which of the following is the most likely surgical implication?

## Options

1. The PCL is likely incompetent.
2. The ACL is likely incompetent.
3. The patient has global OA.
4. The MCL is likely contracted.

## Correct option

**2. The ACL is likely incompetent.**

## Answer explanation

PCL deficiency typically leads to posterior subluxation of the tibia relative to the femur, but in the context of medial OA, posterior tibial erosion is specifically associated with the loss of the ACL's restraining force against posterior femoral translation during flexion.

In Anteromedial Osteoarthritis (AMOA), the ACL must be functional to keep the femur positioned anteriorly on the tibia; thus, wear is typically central or anterior. Posterior erosion is a classic surrogate sign for chronic ACL deficiency, which is a contraindication for mobile-bearing UKA.

Answer : B
