## Question

Which of the following materials has both osteoconductive and osteoinductive properties?

## Options

1. Calcium phosphate
2. Demineralized bone matrix
3. Bone morphogenic protein
4. Calcium carbonate
5. Freeze dried bone allograft

## Correct option

**2. Demineralized bone matrix**

## Answer explanation

Only demineralized bone matrix (DBM) has both osteoconductive and osteoinductive properties of the options listed.

Bone graft comes in a variety of options including autograft, allograft, synthetics, and DBM. All bone grafts have aspects of one or more of the following properties: osteoinductive, osteoconductive, and osteogenic. An osteogenic substance has bone-forming stromal stem cells. An osteoinductive substance has bone-stimulating molecules serving to activate bone-forming osteoblasts. Lastly, an osteoconductive substance contains bone scaffolding materials that act as an inert scaffolding to facilitate the induction and genesis of bone. Autograft has components of all three properties while allograft and synthetics primarily have osteoconductive properties. DBM is the acidic extraction of bone matrix from allograft which removes the minerals and leaves the collagenous and noncollagenous structure and protein. DBM has both osteoconductive and osteoinductive properties as it contains collagen, bone morphogenic proteins (BMPs), transforming growth factor-beta (TGF-β), and residual calcium.

*Sen et al. reviewed the use of bone grafting for the treatment of fracture non-union.* **They note** that while traditionally autograft has been the gold standard for the management of nonunions, given the associated donor-site morbidity, alternatives such as bone marrow aspirate, platelet-rich plasma, allograft, and ceramics have been increasingly used. **They conclude** that combinations of allograft, DBM, and BMA can provide the osteogenic, osteoinductive, and osteoconductive properties needed for fracture healing without the associated donor site morbidity associated with autograft.

*Mahendra et al. reviewed the available bone graft substitutes for the treatment of non-union.* *The authors review the benefits of BMA, DBM, allograft, growth factors, and synthetics including calcium phosphate and calcium sulfate.* **The authors noted** that while DBM provides no structural strength, it provides a structurally stable environment. **They state** that Hydroxyapatite, autograft, allograft, or BMA may be added to DBM to improve its handling characteristics and mechanical properties.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Calcium phosphate is a ceramic that only has osteoconductive properties
Answer 3: Bone morphogenic protein only has osteoinductive properties
Answer 4: Calcium carbonate is a ceramic that only has osteoconductive properties
Answer 5: Freeze-dried bone allograft only has osteoconductive properties.
