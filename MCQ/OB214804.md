## Question

After years of research, you discover a compound that induces overexpression of Osteoprotegerin (OPG) in humans. In what group of patients would this medication likely be beneficial?

## Options

1. Osteopetrosis
2. Familial hypophosphatameic rickets
3. Achondroplasia
4. Osteoporosis
5. Scurvy

## Correct option

**4. Osteoporosis**

## Answer explanation

Overexpression of OPG would decrease osteoclast function, increasing bone mineral density by preventing bone resorption.

Osteoprotegerin (OPG) binds the receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa beta (RANK) and inhibits its binding to RANK ligand (RANKL) on osteoclasts. This results in the decreased bone resorption by preventing the activation of osteoclasts. The result is increased bone mineral density. Basic science studies have shown that OPG knock-out mouse models develop osteoporosis. Other disease states in which OPG may be beneficial include Paget's disease of bone and malignant metastatic disease.

*Bucay et al. performed an animal study to evaluate the physiological role of OPG.* **They found** that OPG knock-out mice exhibit a decrease in total bone density characterized by severe trabecular and cortical bone porosity, marked thinning of the parietal bones of the skull, and a high incidence of fractures. These findings demonstrated that OPG is a critical regulator of postnatal bone mass. **They also found** that, unexpectedly, OPG-deficient mice also exhibit medial calcification of the aorta and renal arteries, suggesting that regulation of OPG, its signaling pathway, or its ligands may play a role in the long-observed association between osteoporosis and vascular calcification.

*Kanematsu et al. performed an animal study to clarify the role of altered B lymphopoiesis and of the receptor activator of nuclear factor-kappa B ligand (RANKL), a key molecule in osteoclastogenesis, in the bone loss associated with estrogen deficiency.* **They found** that an increase in the number of B220+ cells in bone marrow may play an important role in accelerated bone resorption in estrogen deficiency because B220+ cells exhibit RANKL on the cell surface in the presence of PGE2, thereby leading to accelerated osteoclastogenesis.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Osteopetrosis is an osteosclerotic disease most often due to decreased or nonfunctional carbonic anhydrase. This would not benefit from OPG over-expression.
Answer 2: Rickets is a disorder of decreased mineralization of bones during growth. Familial hypophosphatemic rickets should be treated with calcitriol to increased the mineralization of bones.
Answer 3: Achondroplasia is a disease of chondrocyte proliferation due to mutations most often of the fibroblast growth factor receptor 3 (FGFR 3) gene. OPG would likely not be beneficial in these patients.
Answer 5: Scurvy is caused by Vitamin C deficiency results in altered bone formation due to abnormal hydroxylation of collagen peptides. OPG overexpression would likely not be beneficial in these patients.
