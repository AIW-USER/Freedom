## Question

In planning resection of a sacral chordoma in a young man, near normal bladder and bowel function are expected if

## Options

1. bilateral S1 nerve roots are spared.
2. bilateral S1 and unilateral S2 nerve roots are spared.
3. bilateral L5 and unilateral S1 nerve roots are spared.
4. unilateral S2, S3, S4, and S5 nerve roots are spared.
5. unilateral S3, S4, and S5 nerve roots are spared.

## Correct option

**4. unilateral S2, S3, S4, and S5 nerve roots are spared.**

## Answer explanation

Bowel and bladder function are controlled by the sacral nerve roots. Resection of a chordoma often requires resection of one or more of these nerve roots to achieve an adequate margin. To preserve near normal bowel and bladder function, both S2 nerve roots (at least) must be spared. Alternatively, if the tumor is predominantly on one side of the sacrum, preservation of the S2, S3, and S4 nerve roots on one side can yield near normal function.
