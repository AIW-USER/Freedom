## Question

Best investigation to see healing of osteochondritis dissecans lesion (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. MRI
2. CT scan
3. Bone scan
4. SPECT

## Correct option

**1. MRI**

## Answer explanation

MRI is the best investigation to see progress of OCD lesions. MRI criteria of healing of an OCD lesion are

(1). A discrete round area of homogenous high signal intensity 5 mm or more in diameter beneath the lesion;

(2). A focal defect with a width of 5 mm or more in the articular surface of the lesion; and

(3). A line of high signal intensity running through the articular cartilage and subchondral bone plate into the lesion.

CT scan is not sensitive to assess the healing potential of OCD lesions. Before invention of MRI bone can was used to assess the OCD lesion and to plan the course of the treatment. Lesion with normal finding on bone can but visible on X ray were considered to be fully healed or unhealed but without osteoblastic activity. So, bone scans can’t differentiate between healed and unhealed lesions. Lesions with high uptake of technetium in the femoral condyle were believed to have potential for repair.

Reference: Musculoskeletal Imaging E-Book, pg. 406, chapter 29: Internal derangement of the knee, cartilage and osteochondral lesion.

By Thomas Pope, Hans L. Bloem, Javier Beltran, William B. Morrison, David John Wilson. 2nd ed. Elsevier Saunders

Answer: 1
