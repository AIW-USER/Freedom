Chapter: Sports Medicine MCQs — Chronic Exertional Compartment Syndrome

## Question

What is NOT true regarding chronic exertional compartment syndrome? (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Pre-exercise resting compartment pressure > 10 mm of Hg
2. Compartment pressure > 30 mm Hg after 1 minute of exercise
3. Compartment pressure > 20 mm Hg after 5 minute of exercise
4. Anterior and lateral compartments of the leg are most commonly involved.

## Correct option

**1. Pre-exercise resting compartment pressure > 10 mm of Hg**

## Answer explanation

Chronic exertional compartment syndrome: Chronic exertional compartment syndrome (CECS) is an exercise or activity induced compartment syndrome that causes cramping pain in the involved limb. Anterior and lateral compartments of legs are most commonly involved. Patient who usually is a long distance runner or a military recruit complains of moderate to severe leg pain after some time of starting exercises. Pain relieves after rest. Exact pathophysiology is not clear but increased muscle mass during exercises in a non compliant close compartment may give rise to this condition. Diagnosis is confirmed by measuring intracompartmental pressure in the leg.

Diagnostic criteria for chronic exertional compartment syndrome (CECS)

 • Pre-existing resting compartment pressure > 15 mm Hg

 • Compartment pressure > 30 mm Hg × 1 minute after exercise

• Compartment pressure > 20 mm Hg × 5 minutes after exercise

Treatment is initially conservative with rest,ice and stretching exercises of the involved compartment. Fasciotomy is required in non responders.

Answer: 1
