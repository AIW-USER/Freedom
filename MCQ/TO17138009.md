## Question

A 60 years old patient has 5mm ACOM aneurysm. True about its rupture and ISUIA trial is

## Options

1. In phase 1 cut off for rupture is 8 mm
2. Aneurysms are more common in posterior circulation
3. For aneurysms > 10 mm, the risk of rupture is approximately 5% per year
4. In phase 2 cut off for small aneurysm is 10 mm

## Correct option

**3. For aneurysms > 10 mm, the risk of rupture is approximately 5% per year**

## Answer explanation

In phase 1 ISUIA study, aneurysms < 10 mm had remarkably low aneurysm rupture rates. T Aneurysms are more common in anterior circulation than posterior circulation but rupture of aneurysm is more common in posterior circulation location. The risk of rupture for aneurysms between 7-12mm is 2.6%, 13-24mm is 14.5% and 25mm or larger is 40%. Various studies have quoted the risk of 5% rupture for > 10 mm aneurysms. In phase 2, the cut off for small aneurysms is 7 mm.

Answer: 3
