## Question

A cook has accidently cut his wrist in the kitchen. He has clawing of ring and little finger, thumb hyperextension and inability to pinch and inability to abduct middle finger. Which nerve is injured? (peripheral nerve injuries- nerve injuries of the upper limb- ulnar nerve palsy)

## Options

1. Ulnar
2. Median
3. PIN
4. Radial

## Correct option

**1. Ulnar**

## Answer explanation

Its case of ulnar nerve palsy. In the hand deep branch of the ulnar nerve supplies three hypothenar muscles—the abductor digiti minimi, flexor digiti minimi, and opponens digiti minimi (palmaris brevis the fourth hypothenar muscle is supplied by the superficial branch). Amongst the thenar muscles, it again supplies one and a half muscles—the adductor pollicis and deep head of flexor pollicis brevis. It innervates all the interossei muscles. The interossei bring about adduction and abduction of the fingers (PAD, palmar interossei are adductors and DAB, dorsal interossei are abductors), and hence this movement is brought about by the ulnar nerve. Lumbricals originate from the tendons of FDP only, so just like FDP, they have a dual nerve supply. So, only the medial two lumbricals get supplied by the ulnar nerve.

So ulnar nerve injury at wrist level will cause loss of finger abduction and adduction (interossei), clawing of ring and little finger(lumbricals), and inability to pinch (adductor pollicis) and .

Answer: 1
