Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Fracture Of Distal End Radius

## Question

Which x-rays views are most useful to assess screw penetration of the dorsal cortex and articular surface in volar plating?

## Options

1. Wrist dorsiflexion and 20-degree ulnar tilted view
2. Dorsal tangential view and radial incline joint view
3. Volar tangential view and ulnar incline joint view
4. Dorsal tangential view and ulnar incline joint view

## Correct option

**2. Dorsal tangential view and radial incline joint view**

## Answer explanation

Dorsal tangential view and radial incline joint view are generally used to assess for screw penetration in volar plating of distal radius fracture. The dorsal tangential view is easily obtained by flexing the wrist and getting a view with the forearm tangential to the beam of C-arm. This method has been shown in a study to be a reliable method for detecting dorsal cortical screw penetration in volar plating of distal radius fractures. The radial incline view is taken on the lateral view with the C-arm adjusted to match the radial inclination and provides a good view of the articular surface of the radius.
