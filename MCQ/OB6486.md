## Question

Which of the following metastatic cancers to bone is least likely to respond to external beam radiation therapy?

## Options

1. Breast
2. Prostate
3. Lymphoma
4. Myeloma
5. Renal

## Correct option

**5. Renal**

## Answer explanation

Radiation therapy is often used to treat pain associated with metastatic disease to bone. The response to radiation therapy is dependent on a number of factors including the radiosensitivity of the primary tumor. Breast cancer, prostate cancer, lymphoma, and myeloma are considered among some of the most radiosensitive tumors. Renal and thyroid carcinomas are notoriously radioresistant.
