## Question

Isolated resection of a talocalcaneal tarsal coalition in an 11-year-old girl is best indicated for which of the following conditions?

## Options

1. 20% involvement of the width of the subtalar joint, 35 degrees of forefoot abduction
2. 25% involvement of the width of the subtalar joint, 30 degrees of forefoot adduction
3. 30% involvement of the width of the subtalar joint, 25 degrees of heel valgus
4. 40% involvement of the width of the subtalar joint, no increased hindfoot valgus
5. 80% involvement of the width of the subtalar joint, neutral heel alignment

## Correct option

**4. 40% involvement of the width of the subtalar joint, no increased hindfoot valgus**

## Answer explanation

The indication for resection of an isolated talocalcaneal tarsal coalition is a coalition of less than or equal to 50% of the width of the subtalar joint as viewed on a coronal CT view (this typically involves 100% of the middle facet of the subtalar joint), and acceptable foot alignment. Therefore, 40% involvement of the width of the subtalar joint and no hindfoot valgus deformity meets this criteria. All the other responses do not meet the criteria because they are either greater than 50% of the subtalar joint or have significant foot deformity. Resection of a coalition greater than 50% has been associated with poor outcomes.
