## Question

Neurologic injury is the most common complication of ankle arthroscopy. The nerve most commonly injured is at risk during placement of which portal?

## Options

1. Anterolateral
2. Anterocentral
3. Anteromedial
4. Posteromedial
5. Posterolateral

## Correct option

**1. Anterolateral**

## Answer explanation

The superficial peroneal nerve (SPN) is the most common nerve injured during ankle arthroscopy and is associated with anterolateral portal placement.

Ankle arthroscopy is an important therapeutic tool to manage a variety of pathologies including osteochondral defects and impingement syndrome. The average complication rate is 6-20% and the most common complication is neurologic injury. The SPN is most often injured, in part due to its variation in anatomic location. Identifying and marking important structures, as well as proper portal placement are vital to lessen the risk of neurovascular (NV) injury.

*Carlson et al* **stated** that technical aspects of portal placement are important to minimize injury to NV structures. **They recommended** (1) Marking important structures, (2) Utilization of a “nick and spread” technique with careful spreading of the subcutaneous tissues prior to penetration of the capsule, and (3) Usage of a protective interchangeable cannula to avoid repetitive soft tissue injury.

*Zengerink et al prospectively evaluated 33 patients undergoing ankle arthroscopy using the dorsiflexion method with intermittent soft tissue distraction.* The complication rate was 3.5%, lower than the published rate of 10.3% with continuous distraction. The authors attribute this to anterior NV structures being in a relaxed/mobile position when the ankle is dorsiflexed and therefore able to move away when a blunt instrument is introduced, making these structures less prone to injury.

Incorrect Responses:
Answer 2: At the anterocentral portal, the dorsalis pedis artery and deep peroneal nerve are at risk.
Answer 3: At the anteromedial portal, the great saphenous vein and saphenous nerve are at risk.
Answer 4: At the posteromedial portal, the posterior tibial artery and tibial nerve are at risk.
Answer 5: At the posterolateral portal, the small saphenous vein and sural nerve are at risk.
