Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Multiple Myeloma

## Question

Which of the following is also known as Kahler’s Disease?

## Options

1. Osteosarcoma
2. Malignant Fibrous Histiocytoma
3. Benign Fibrous Histiocytoma
4. Multiple Myeloma

## Correct option

**4. Multiple Myeloma**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Kahler’s disease is another name for Multiple myeloma. It’s a type of blood cancer arising from plasma cells of bone marrow, characterized by overproduction of immunoglobulins.

Answer: 4
