Chapter: Metabolic Bone Disorder MCQs — Rickets

## Question

Low circulating level of FGF 23 is associated with

## Options

1. Hypophosphatemia
2. Osteomalacia
3. Low serum level of 1,25 dihydroxyvitamin D
4. hyperostosis

## Correct option

**4. hyperostosis**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D

EXPLANATION: -

The transport of inorganic minerals to and from the skeletal tissues necessarily requires interaction with, and has effects on, other organs—particularly the kidneys. One factor that is central to the regulation of the bone–kidney axis is the phosphaturic factor FGF-23. High circulating levels of FGF-23 can cause disorders that are characterized by hypophosphatemia, decreased production of 1,25 dihydroxyvitamin D, and osteomalacia. By contrast, low circulating levels can be associated with hyperphosphatemia, increased production of 1,25 dihydroxyvitamin D, soft tissue calcification, and hyperostosis.
