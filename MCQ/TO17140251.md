## Question

Ratio of body weight lever arm to that of abductor musculature is

## Options

1. 1:2
2. 2:1
3. 2.5: 1
4. 1:2.5

## Correct option

**3. 2.5: 1**

## Answer explanation

Explanation:

The ratio of the length of the lever arm of the body weight to that of the abductor musculature is about

2.5 : 1. The force of the abductor muscles must approximate 2.5 times the body weight to maintain the pelvis level when standing on one leg.

Answer : C
