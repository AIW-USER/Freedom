Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Tendon Injuries of Hand

## Question

How many compartments are there in the hand?

## Options

1. 10
2. 12
3. 09
4. 07

## Correct option

**1. 10**

## Answer explanation

In the hand, there are 10 separate osteofascial compartments including dorsal interossei (4), Palmar interossei (3), Thenar and Hypothenar (2), and adductor pollicis (1)

Few authors also mention 11th compartment as mid-palmar compartment.

Answer : A
