## Question

Which is not a correct constellation of markers for post arthroplasty prosthetic joint infection ( Arthroplasty- knee arthroplasty- post TKR infection)

## Options

1. ESR,CRP, WBC count, IL-1
2. CRP, IL-6, D-dimer, ICAM-1
3. TNF- alpha, procalcitonin, CRP
4. CRP, ESR, Neutrophil count of synovial fluid

## Correct option

**1. ESR,CRP, WBC count, IL-1**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 1
