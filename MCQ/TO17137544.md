## Question

choose the correct diagnosis and management protocol for the given CT image

![[TO17137544-1.png]]

## Options

1. Pipkin type 1 femoral head fracture, ORIF with Herbert screw
2. Pipkin type 2 femoral head fracture, ORIF with Herbert screw
3. Pipkin type 1 femoral head fracture, non-operative immobilization for 6 weeks
4. Pipkin type 2 femoral head fracture, non-operative immobilization for 6 weeks

## Correct option

**2. Pipkin type 2 femoral head fracture, ORIF with Herbert screw**

## Answer explanation

This is CT axial section of Pipkin type 2 displaced femoral head fracture. Ideal treatment for displaced weight bearing fracture fragment is accurate reduction and fixation.

Answer: 2
