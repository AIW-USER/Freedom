## Question

You work as an Olympic team physician and you are asked to assist in exercise programming for multiple athletes. Athlete A is a marathon runner, while athlete B is a powerlifter. When considering their respective training regimens, which of the following is true regarding likely adaptions in skeletal muscle in these athletes?

## Options

1. Increased cross-sectional area of muscle in Athlete A
2. Increased capillary density in Athlete A
3. Increased mitochondrial number and density in Athlete B
4. Increased utilization of fatty acids and glycogen in Athlete B
5. Increased percentage of type I muscle fibers in Athlete B

## Correct option

**2. Increased capillary density in Athlete A**

## Answer explanation

In endurance athletes, capillary density is increased in order to improve circulation and muscle metabolism.

Adaptation of skeletal muscle is an important consideration when designing exercise programming. The type of exercise being performed plays an important role in the muscle changes that take place in order to optimize performance. Endurance athletes tend to have increased circulation due to increased capillary density, increases in mitochondrial size/density, and increases in the amount of fatty acids and glycogen that are recruited. There is also an increase in the percentage of type I muscle fibers, which are highly oxidative. Strength training, or anaerobic activity, tends to result in muscle hypertrophy with increased motor unit recruitment and muscle fiber synchronization. For this reason, the type of muscle adaptation desired is important to understand when designing exercises programs.

*Holloszy and Coyle reviewed the adaptation of muscle with endurance exercise training.* **They note** significant increases in mitochondrial content and respiratory capacity of muscle fibers which provide a smaller metabolic disturbance than in untrained muscle. **They also discuss** that with improvements in muscle endurance adaptation, muscles are slower to utilize glycogen and fatty acids and rely less on lactate production, making them more efficient and able to perform prolonged activity.

*Kirkendall et al. reviewed the effects of aging and training of skeletal muscle noting that aging leads to a predictable decrease in muscle mass.* **They also note** that there is an increased percentage of type I muscle fibers with age, and aging skeletal muscle produces less force. However, **they note** that continued training can minimize these losses, with endurance training improving aerobic muscle capacity and resistance training improving recruitment of muscle and muscle mass.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Athletes participating in more anaerobic activities, such as strength training, see increased muscle cross-sectional area due to muscle hypertrophy.
Answer 3: Mitochondrial density, number, and size increase with aerobic, not anaerobic activities.
Answer 4: Fatty acids and glycogen are utilized more commonly in endurance athletes after several minutes of activity, compared to anaerobic strength training activities which utilize more immediate sources of energy.
Answer 5: The percentage of type I muscle fibers would be expected to increase in endurance athletes, not those performing strength training activities.
