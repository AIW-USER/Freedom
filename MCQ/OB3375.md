## Question

An 83-year-old female presents to your office following a DEXA scan, in which her T-score was -3.0. You counsel the patient that she is at risk for fragility fractures. Which of the following is most common?

## Options

1. Distal radius fracture
2. Vertebral compression fracture
3. Femoral neck fracture
4. Intertrochanteric femur fracture
5. Proximal humerus fracture

## Correct option

**2. Vertebral compression fracture**

## Answer explanation

Vertebral compression fractures (VCF) are the most common fragility fracture with an incidence of approximately 70,000 per year in the United States.

In osteoporosis, the bone is of normal quality, but decreased quantity. The WHO defines this as a T-score below -2.5. VCFs are the most common fragility fractures, occurring in 50% of people over the age of 80. Additionally, patients are at a 20% increased risk of having another fragility fracture. The 1 and 2-year mortality rates following VCF are 15% and 20% respectively, which is less than that of a hip fracture at 1 year but equivalent at 2 years. It is important to always rule out metastatic cancer to the spine, specifically in fractures abover T5 or in younger patients. Bisphosphonates should be started in an attempt to decrease the risk of future fragility fractures.

*Gass et al reviewed the best methods for preventing osteoporosis-related fractures.* **They report** that 1.5 million fragility fractures occur each year in the United States, resulting in 12-18 billion dollars in direct healthcare costs. **They conclude** that a pyramidal approach starting with calcium and vitamin D supplementation, physical activity, and fall prevention is the first line in fracture prevention.

*Lindsay et al reviewed the risk of new vertebral fractures in the year following a previously identified fracture.* **They report** that 19.2% of postmenopausal women who developed an incidental vertebral fracture sustained a second fracture in the following year. **They conclude** that there is a 5-fold risk of additional fracture in patients who are found to have a VCF at baseline.

*Kado et al reviewed the incidence and mortality of older women following VCF.* *They report on 7233 women, age >65-years-old, who were diagnosed with osteoporosis-related fractures.* **They conclude** that women with at least one new VCF had an age-adjusted 32% increased risk of mortality during the 8-year follow-up period.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1,5: While distal radius fractures and proximal humerus fractures are the most common fragility fractures of the upper extremity, they are seen less commonly than VCF.
Answer 3,4: Femoral neck and intertrochanteric femur fractures may also be considered fragility fractures, but both have a lower incidence than VCF.
