## Question

A 25-year-old tennis player has shoulder pain and weakness to external rotation. MRI scans are shown in Figures 16a and 16b. What is the most likely cause of his weakness?

![[q6186-a-16a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q6186-b-16b.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Supraspinatus tear
2. Infraspinatus tear
3. Suprascapular nerve compression
4. C5 radiculopathy
5. Subacromial impingement

## Correct option

**3. Suprascapular nerve compression**

## Answer explanation

The MRI scans show a paralabral cyst, which is most commonly associated with labral tears. Compression of the suprascapular nerve results in weakness of the supraspinatus and/or infraspinatus depending on the level of compression.
