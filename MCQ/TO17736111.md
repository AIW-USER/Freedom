## Question

Crab meat lesions on MRI seen in (arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- cartilage injury)

## Options

1. Grade III chondromalacia patellae
2. Grade II chondromalacia patellae
3. Mucoid degeneration of ACL
4. Mucoid degeneration of PCL

## Correct option

**1. Grade III chondromalacia patellae**

## Answer explanation

Outerbridge Classification for Chondromalacia Patella

Modified Outerbridge Classification for Chondromalacia Patella

Initially, Outerbridge classification was based on arthroscopic findings. Later, it was modified based on MRI findings.

MRI-based Classification

 Grade
 MRI features

 I
 FS PD focal areas of hyperintensity within normal contour

 II
 FS PD fissure or blister-like swelling of articular cartilage extending to surface

 III
 FS PD focal ulcerations and "crab meat" lesions

 IV
 FS PD full-thickness cartilage loss with underlying bone reactive changes

Arthroscopy-based Classification

 Grade
 Pathology

 I
 Softening and swelling of articular cartilage

 II
 Fragmentation and fissuring of articular cartilage affecting an area of less than 0.5 inches

 III
 Fragmentation and fissuring of articular cartilage affecting an area greater than 0.5 inches

 IV
 Cartilage erosion to bone

Note: Tram track appearance is seen in mucoid degeneration of PCL.
