## Question

Ulnar bursa does not communicate with which space? General orthopaedics-anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. Digital sheath of 4th finger
2. Digital sheath of 5th finger
3. Forearm space of parona
4. Radial bursa

## Correct option

**1. Digital sheath of 4th finger**

## Answer explanation

The digital synovial sheath of the little finger continues proximally in the palm as the ulnar bursa while that of the thumb as radial bursa. The two bursae extend proximally for 2–3 cm above the wrist and communicate with each other and may communicate with the forearm space of “parona”.

Answer: 1
