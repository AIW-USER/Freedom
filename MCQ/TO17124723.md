## Question

Which nerve injury causes loss of opposition (peripheral nerve injury- nerve injuries of upper limb- median nerve palsy)

## Options

1. Median
2. Ulnar
3. Radial
4. Medial division of median nerve

## Correct option

**1. Median**

## Answer explanation

The median nerve enters the hand deep to the flexor retinaculum (area called the Carpal tunnel). Immediately below the retinaculum the nerve divides into lateral (stouter branch) and medial divisions. The lateral division primarily supplies the two and a half thenar muscles (superficial head of FPB, abductor pollicis brevis and opponens pollicis) and first lumbrical (sensory supply is only to thumb and lateral border of index finger) while the medial division is primarily sensory (supplies the left-over index, middle and half of ring finger to complete up the sensory supply—the lateral three and a half fingers; it also supplies second lumbrical).

Answer: 1
