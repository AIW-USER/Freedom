Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Reverse Shoulder Arthroplasty

## Question

What is the biomechanical advantage of a reverse total shoulder arthroplasty compared to a standard arthroplasty?

## Options

1. Centre of rotation is superior.
2. Centre of rotation more medial.
3. Centre of rotation more lateral.
4. Increased lateral humeral offset.

## Correct option

**2. Centre of rotation more medial.**

## Answer explanation

Biomechanically, the reverse prosthesis works by changing the direction of pull of the deltoid muscle. With standard prostheses, absence of the rotator cuff allows the humeral head to subluxate superiorly during deltoid muscle contraction. The reverse prosthesis corrects this abnormal vector by moving the center of rotation of the arm medially and distally and reestablishing a fulcrum around which the deltoid can pull to restore forward elevation. It decreases the forces at the implant–bone interface and provides a mechanical advantage to the deltoid muscle to substitute for the deficient rotator cuff muscles to provide shoulder abduction.

Answer : b
