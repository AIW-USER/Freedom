## Question

## Options

2. CR type
3. CCK type
4. Hinge knee

## Correct option

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

TYPES OF TKR PROSTHESIS

- TOTAL CONDYLAR DESIGN: PCL sacrificing design. No Cam-post either. Knee flexion could only go to 90o (as there was no provision for femoral roll back). Historical as of now.

![[TO17105627-2.png]]

- DUO-PATELLAR PROSTHESIS (KINEMATIC DESIGN): PCL retaining design also called as CR type. PCL is retained for femoral rollback to increase knee flexion beyond 90o.

![[TO17105627-3.jpg]]

POSTERIOR STABILIZED PROSTHESIS (PCL SUBSTITUTING DESING) by Insall and Burnstein: Functional PCL cannot be there in every knee, especially those with advanced stage of arthritis (specifically flexion deformities). In this variety, PCL was substituted by “CAM-POST” mechanism that effected the posterior femoral roll back. The disadvantage of Posterior stabilized knee is that more bone has to be removed as compared to PCL retaining prosthesis (as notch has to be cut to adjust cam and post mechanism). So, when PCL is functional, PCL retaining knee should be chosen else Posterior stabilized knee is opted. However, an advantage of PS type is that they can tolerate changes in joint line after TKR better.

![[TO17105627-4.jpg]]

![[TO17105627-5.jpg]]

- LOW CONTACT STRESS (ROTATING PLATOFORM) DESIGNS: The higher segment in PS type implants. In these, there is rotational motion of tibial polyethylene base plate over stem that allows stresses to be dissipated even in case of few degrees of mal-alignment and imperfect ligament balancing, prolonging longevity of implant.
- MEDIAL PIVOT KNEE: Here the medial articulation is made to functional like a ball and socket joint allowing rotational motion as the femoral condyle (most designs medial femoral condyle, some lateral) pivots over the tibial base plate.
- ULTRACONGRUANT and DEEPDISH DESINGS: These are evolutions of the PCL retaining designs. Here the polyethylene spacer has been built in such a manner that it provides the femoral roll back even if PCL is sacrificed (the anterior flange of PE is raised to provide femoral roll back). This allows the knee to flex beyond 90o even without an intact PCL and even without the need to remove extra bone from notch to adjust Cam and post.
- CONSTRAINED DESIGNS: These are meant for cases where there is deficiency of collaterals. These designs are further categorized into two varieties: -

- HINGED KNEE (CONSTRAINED KNEE): All the above varieties need intact collateral ligaments to work. When the collaterals are damaged or deficient a hinged implant is chosen. These invariably are rotating platform in nature. Even in case MCL gets transacted on table, ideal is to move ahead with hinge knee.
- Constrained Condylar Knee (CCK): This is a semi hinge design as there is no true locking mechanism like in a hinge knee. However, it has an exaggerated (longer) post/ keel on the insert for extra medio lateral stability. CCK is ideally designed for cases with collateral attenuation like high degree of varus where a significant amount of collateral stripping will be required. It can be kept as a low budget back up in collateral transactions on table.

Note: A bicruciate retaining design has also been developed preserving both cruciate ligaments and hence more bone and soft tissue conserving allowing much natural restoration of knee kinematics. It’s particularly scores in providing much better strength and stability on stairs, and better performance in single-leg weight-bearing activities. However, availability in India is not there.
