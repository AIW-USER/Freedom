## Question

Taping method in runner with ACUTE ankle injury

## Options

1. Medial patellar tapinga.
2. Open basket weave taping
3. Closed basket weave taping
4. Low dye taping

## Correct option

**3. Closed basket weave taping**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

EXPLANATION: -

Closed basket weave is a taping technique is used for an acute ankle injury.

• Purpose
Acute Injuries for maximum support.
• How to do it
Here are some steps for applying a closed basket weave tape:
1. Start with two anchors at the top, overlapping each by half. One anchor below calf and another at top of foot, behind metatarsals
3. Start with a stirrup that runs from the medial side to the lateral side
4. Horseshoe goes behind heel.
4. Alternate or weave between stirrups and horseshoe as you work your way up the foot
5. Close the entire tape job with C strips
6. Finish with heel locks

Open basket weave is a taping technique that can also be used to treat an acute ankle injury, when there is presence of massive swelling.
• Purpose
Provides compression and support to the ankle, and helps minimize inflammation
• When to use
Can be used for acute sprains with a lot of swelling
• How it differs from a closed basket weave
The main difference is that the tape doesn't overlap in front, which allows for swelling and ankle movement
• How to do it
Similar to a closed basket weave, but the crisscrossing heel locks are applied with elastic tape instead of nonelastic tape. A horseshoe can also be used to help reduce swelling.

Here are some steps for applying an open basket weave:
1.Start with two or three anchors at the top, overlapping each by half
2. Place an anchor across the top of the foot, just behind the metatarsal heads
3. Start with a stirrup that runs from the medial side to the lateral side
4. Alternate between vertical and horizontal stirrups as you work your way up the foot
5. Overlay the tape by a half each time
6. Finish with anchors, one underneath and one at the top

Low dye taping makes use of Zinc Oxide (ZnO) tape for mid foot, plantar fascia related pain or heel pain. It is not used for ankle sprains.
