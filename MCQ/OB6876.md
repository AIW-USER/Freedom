## Question

What is the typical axial plane transverse angulation of the thoracic pedicles?

## Options

1. 5 degrees medial at T1 and T2; 10 degrees from T3 to T10
2. 5 degrees lateral at T1; neutral at T2; 5 degrees medial from T3 to T12
3. 10 degrees medial from T1 to T10; 15 degrees medial at T11 and T12
4. 10 degrees medial from T1 to T12
5. 25 degrees medial at T1; 15 degrees at T2; and 10 degrees medial from T3 to T10

## Correct option

**5. 25 degrees medial at T1; 15 degrees at T2; and 10 degrees medial from T3 to T10**

## Answer explanation

Thoracic pedicles typically are angled 25 degrees medially at T1 so the starting point is more lateral. T2 angles about 15 degrees, and then the pedicles average about 5 to 7 degrees down to T10. At T11 and 12, the angulation is minimal.
