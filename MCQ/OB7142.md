## Question

Fluoroquinolones work by inhibiting what enzyme?

## Options

1. DNA gyrase
2. RNA gyrase
3. DNA polymerase
4. Ribosomal transferase
5. DNA telomerase

## Correct option

**1. DNA gyrase**

## Answer explanation

Fluoroquinolones are commonly used antibiotics used to treat orthopaedic infections and are popular because of their equivalency in IV or oral formulations and their gram-positive and gram-negative antibacterial activity. It is part of a larger class of antibiotics called quinolones that interfere with DNA metabolism. Fluoroquinolones are synthetic derivatives of nalidixic acid. Its mechanism of action involves interfering with the function of DNA gyrase. DNA gyrase ia a type-II topoisomerase, and "unwinds" DNA to allow replication.
