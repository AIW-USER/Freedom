Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Dupuytren’s Contracture

## Question

Not a risk factor for the disease shown in the picture:

![[TO17141359-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Smoking
2. Occupation involving heavy vibrating tool and earth movers
3. Diabetics
4. Alcoholics

## Correct option

**2. Occupation involving heavy vibrating tool and earth movers**

## Answer explanation

Dupuytren’s contracture is commonly seen in men of Scandinavian and Celtic origin (Male:Female = 10:1) in their fourth to sixth decade of life. People who are diabetics, alcoholics, smokers and who are on antiepileptic drugs are at greater risk of affection. Although the exact cause remains unknown, hand trauma and heavy manual labor performed by an individual may be a contributing factor. Hereditary predisposition is present in some families.

Occupation is not a confirmed associated factor with Dupuytren’s contracture and best choice among given options.

Answer: 2
