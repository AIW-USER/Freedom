## Question

Which of the following paediatric patients should undergo cervical spine radiographs as part of their standard pre-surgical screening?

## Options

1. Friedreich's ataxia
2. Gaucher Disease
3. Morquio syndrome
4. Multiple Epiphyseal Dysplasia
5. Cleidocranial Dysplasia

## Correct option

**3. Morquio syndrome**

## Answer explanation

Morquio syndrome is an inherited mucopolysaccharidose storage disease, also referred to as MPS4. It is associated with atlanto-axial instability. Cervical spine radiographs are required in the pre-surgical workup of this patient to avoid complications during intubation.

Morquio syndrome is a rare (1 in 40, 000) inherited cause of short-trunk dwarfism. Skeletal manifestations include severe growth retardation, odontoid hypoplasia with atlanto-axial instability, thoraco-lumbar kyphosis, hip dysplasia, genu valgum and marked skin and joint laxity. Mental function is usually normal.

*White et al. reviewed Morquio syndrome and* **note** this is an autosomal recessive mucopolysaccharide (also known as lysosomal) storage disorder caused by defective activity of N-acetyl-galactosamine-6-sulfatase (GALNS). Lower extremity malalignment (genu valgum), hip dysplasia, weakness and joint laxity contribute to the pathological gait pattern with these patients. There may also be associated myelopathic gait (broad based gait with decreased cadence) due to spinal cord compression.

*Baratela et al. looked retrospectively at patients with Morquio syndrome, who had undergone decompression and fusion of the cervicothoracic spine.* Half of the patients had previously undergone occipitocervical fusion for atlanto-axial instability. **They recommend** careful neurological assessment, including whole spine MR imaging, as separate and distinct sites of cord compression can occur in this population of patients.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Friedreich's ataxia is a genetic neuromuscular disorder characterized by spinocerebellar degeneration. It is not associated with C1-C2 instability.
Answer 2: Gaucher disease is a rare genetic disorder characterized by the deposition of glucocerebroside in cells of the macrophage-monocyte system. It is not associated with C1-C2 instability.
Answer 4: Multiple Epiphyseal Dysplasia (MED) is caused by failure of formation of the secondary ossification center. It can be differentiated from Spondyloepiphyseal Dysplasia (SED), which may be associated with C1-C2 instability.
Answer 5: Cleidocranial dysplasia is a skeletal dysplasia affecting bones formed by intramembranous ossification. It is not associated with C1-C2 instability.
