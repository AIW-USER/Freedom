## Question

Patient with compressive myelopathy has Spinal Dural AV fistula, All true except

## Options

1. Recurrent bleeding is cause of symptoms
2. Treatment is ligation of proximal leptomeningeal vein
3. Multiple lesions

## Correct option

**1. Recurrent bleeding is cause of symptoms**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

Explanation: Spinal Dural AVF are the result of an anomalous connection between the epidural segment of a radicular artery, before it dives into the dural sleeve of a nerve root, and the epidural venous plexus. With an extensive network of veins in the epidural space, this type of fistula can result in marked venous engorgement and compression on the thecal sac and nearby nerve roots. As with any fistula, vascular steal phenomena also ensue. Intraspinal bleeding is rare in spinal Dural AVF. So recurrent bleeding is the cause of symptoms is false.

Other options, treatment include ligation / embolization of proximal leptomeningeal vein. There are multiple arterial feeders in dural AVF and ligation of one will lead to formation of collaterals. Hence, ligation/embolization of the draining vein is essential.

15-20% are associated with other AVMs.

(ref: Yoemen’s Neurological Surgery, Greenberg Handbook of Neurosurgery)
