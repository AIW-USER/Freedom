## Question

Which of the following is likely to be associated with the abnormality shown in Figure A?

![[q2264-a-screen-shot-2020-02-02-at-11-42-11-am.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Crankshaft phenomenon
2. Progressive sagittal deformity and paralysis
3. Progressive coronal defortmity and paralysis
4. Progressive sagittal deformity unlikely to result in paralysis
5. Favorable response to bracing

## Correct option

**2. Progressive sagittal deformity and paralysis**

## Answer explanation

Anterior failure of formation is associated with progressive kyphotic deformity and paralysis.

Congenital scoliosis results from the failure of normal vertebral development. This can take the form of failure of formation, failure of segmentation, or both. Anterior failure of segmentation is less likely to progress to neurologic compromise compared to failure of formation. Deformity progression is most rapid during the first few years of life. Congenital kyphosis does not respond to bracing and requires operative intervention in the form of fusion with or without osteotomies.

*McMaster et al. reviewed the natural history of congenital kyphosis and kyphoscoliosis.* *They reviewed records of 112 patients, 68 of which had failure of anterior vertebral body formation (type I).* **They found** that the severity of a type-I kyphosis was proportional to the severity of the anterior failure of formation demonstrated on lateral radiographs; two adjacent type-I defects progressed the most rapidly and resulted in greater deformity compared to the other type-I defects.

*Chang et al. performed a retrospective study looking at the outcomes of posterior hemivertebra resection and short segment fusion for congenital scoliosis.* *All 18 children were less than 10 at the time of surgery and the average follow-up was 11.4 years.* **They found** that the procedure was safe and effective and suggest an early surgical intervention in this population to minimize complications.

Figure A is a drawing showing failure of anterior formation. Illustration A is an MRI of a patient with this condition. Illustration B is a drawing and corresponding CT reconstruction of a patient with anterior failure of segmentation.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: This term describes the twisting of the spine resulting from posterior-only fusion in younger patients with anterior element growth remaining.
Answer 3: Anterior failure of formation is mainly associated with a kyphotic deformity and spinal cord compression resulting from the spinal cord draping over the kyphotic segment; coronal deformity can co-exist.
Answer 4: This describes anterior failure of segmentation.
Answer 5: Bracing is not effective in the treatment of congenital scoliosis.
