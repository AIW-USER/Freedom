## Question

For a patient with a type II odontoid fracture, which of the following factors best predicts the development of a nonunion with nonsurgical management?

## Options

1. Frontal oblique pattern
2. Magnitude of fracture displacement
3. Degree of posterior angulation
4. Age
5. Patient history of diabetes mellitus

## Correct option

**2. Magnitude of fracture displacement**

## Answer explanation

All five factors have been found to be associated with nonunion for type II odontoid fractures. Of these, initial fracture displacement of greater than 6 mm has the greatest association with the development of fracture nonunion.
