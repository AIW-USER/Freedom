## Question

During arthroscopic mobilization of a chronic, retracted Lafosse Type IV subscapularis tear, which anatomical structure must be released from the base of the coracoid to improve lateral excursion?

## Options

1. Coracoacromial ligament
2. Transverse humeral ligament
3. Coracohumeral ligament
4. Conjoined tendon origin

## Correct option

**3. Coracohumeral ligament**

## Answer explanation

The coracohumeral ligament (CHL) and superior glenohumeral ligament (SGHL) complex often tether a retracted subscapularis to the coracoid; releasing this 'medial' constraint is essential for excursion.

Answer: 3
