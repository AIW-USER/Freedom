Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Unicondylar and Patellofemoral Arthroplasty

## Question

Anterior medial knee pain after medial compartment mobile bearing UKA would most commonly result from

## Options

1. Bearing dislocation
2. Implant loosening
3. Instability
4. Anterior impingement

## Correct option

**4. Anterior impingement**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D
EXPLANATION: -

Medial UKA can be separated into fixed-bearing or mobile-bearing (MB) based on if the polyethylene liner is designed to move in the anterior-posterior direction on the tibial tray with ROM or stay in place. The original design for the UKA first used in 1969 was a fixed-bearing all-polyethylene tibial component that demonstrated high wear rates. The MB-UKA was designed to have better wear patterns and increased conformity with the femoral component thereby maintaining normal knee kinematics and better ROM. However, the unique design with the highly constrained contact between the mobile inlay and the femoral component in MB-UKA requires accurate alignment and size selection of the implant. Inaccuracies in surgical execution increase the risk of different complications, notably anterior impingement. The reasons resulting in an anterior impingement are multifactorial but most likely related to faulty surgical technique and the implant mal-positioning.
