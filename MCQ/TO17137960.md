Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Open Fracture Management

## Question

Most common source of infection in an open fracture is

## Options

1. hospital (hospital acquired)
2. haematogenous spread
3. site of injury
4. aerobic flora from soil

## Correct option

**1. hospital (hospital acquired)**

## Answer explanation

Although infection does result from wound contamination especially if the debridement has been poor, there is now firm evidence that most acute infections after open injuries are the result of pathogens acquired in the hospital rather than from the site of injury.

Answer: 1
