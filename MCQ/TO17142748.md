## Question

Which of the following factors is a good prognostic sign for this patient?

![[TO17142748-1.jpg]]
![[TO17142748-2.jpg]]

## Options

1. Age of patient 7 years
2. Size of lesion 12 cm
3. Fever (T= 390 C)
4. TLC = 14000

## Correct option

**1. Age of patient 7 years**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Skeletally immature patient, aggressive bone lesion involving diaphysis of femur→ Ewing's sarcoma

Bad Prognostic factors-

- Age > 10 years

- Size > 8cm

- Fever,

- Hb ↓

- ESR ↑

- TLC ↑

- LDH ↑

Answer: 1
