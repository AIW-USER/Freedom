Chapter: Spine MCQs — Spinal Tumors

## Question

MC Intramedullary tumour in spine (FNB 2022)

## Options

1. Ependymoma
2. Astrocytoma
3. Meningioma
4. Medulloblastoma

## Correct option

**2. Astrocytoma**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

Most common intramedullary tumour in children and adolescents: Astrocytoma

In Adults: Ependymoma

Overall: Astrocytoma

Also know some facts about BRAIN TUMORS: -

- Most common brain tumour - Metastases (>50%)
- Most common primary brain tumour – Ganglioblastoma Multiforme (GBM)
- Most common primary non-glial tumour - Meningioma.
- Most common primary CNS tumour in adults - GBM
- Most common primary CNS tumour in children - Gliomas (more specifically astrocytoma’s 50%), and the second most common is Medulloblastoma
- Most common paediatric brain malignancy - Medulloblastoma
- Most common paediatric vermian tumour - Medulloblastoma
- Most common supra-tentorial tumour in paediatrics (as in adults) - Astrocytoma
