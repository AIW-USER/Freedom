## Question

. A hypertensive patient who is a chronic smoker is to undergo a foot amputation. He has forefoot gangrene with necrosis going till navicular. Ideal amputation for the patient would be (FNB 2022)

## Options

1. Pirogoff
2. Chopart
3. Syme
4. Lisfranc

## Correct option

**2. Chopart**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 2: Chopart Amputation

Explanation: -

Chopart amputation Chopart’s amputation is the ultimate gesture for the conservation of heel support and has great and distinct advantages over both the Syme and Pirogroff / below knee amputation.

- Technically much easier
- Patient can wear a shoe with AFO rather than knee high prosthesis
- Does not produce much shortening of the limb
- Distal surface is covered with tough weight bearing surface of heel skin so it’s definitely a weight bearing amputation
- Easier to plan and do muscle balancing and avoid equinus contracture (which is a common problem with most foot amputations, even Chopart) that leads to amputation failure in Syme’s. In Chopart with equinus, we can transfer Tibialis anterior proximally along with TA lengthening as a balancing procedure. In Syme’s, even if slight equinus is there, it can disturb ambulatory settings leading amputation failure and then we have to rely only on TA lengthening as above-mentioned balancing transfer cannot be used.
