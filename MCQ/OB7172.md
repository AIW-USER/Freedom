## Question

Osteoarthritis (OA) affects the matrix of articular cartilage, which includes changes in collagen type and content. During early arthritis, which collagen type is found to be increased?

## Options

1. I
2. IX
3. X
4. XI
5. XII

## Correct option

**3. X**

## Answer explanation

Type X collagen is seen in articular cartilage in the region of the interface with subchondral bone in most OA patients. The threshold of chondrocyte sensitivity to inducing type X collagen mRNA production is more than 2.5% local strain. Increased local strains above the threshold results in an increase of Col X mRNA expression. Such quantitative analysis has important implications for our understanding of mechanosensitivity of cartilage and mechanical regulation of chondrocyte gene expression. None of the other collagen types are shown to increase in osteoarthritic articular cartilage.
