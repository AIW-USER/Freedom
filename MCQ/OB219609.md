## Question

A retired orthopedic surgeon is newly diagnosed with breast cancer. Throughout her career, she recalls wearing lead to protect from radiation. Which area of the breast is the most common site of cancer?

## Options

1. Bottom half
2. Inner half
3. Lower inner quadrant
4. Upper half
5. Upper outer quadrant

## Correct option

**5. Upper outer quadrant**

## Answer explanation

The upper outer quadrant of the breast is the most common location of breast cancer (Answer 5).

The use of fluoroscopy presents an occupational risk to orthopedic surgeons. Radiation places surgeons at elevated risk of cataracts and multiple forms of solid cancers. Precautions taken to reduce radiation exposure include, but are not limited to: radiation shields (including lead aprons), lead glasses, and conscientious C-arm positioning and management. Recent literature has evaluated the protective effect of lead garments and found the upper outer quadrant of the breast inadequately protected by conventional lead vests. This same area also happens to be the overall (not just orthopedic surgeons) most common location for the development of breast cancer.

*Valone et al. performed a study using dosimeters and anthropomorphic torso phantoms to examine breast radiation exposure with different lead aprons.* **They found** that the upper outer quadrant (UOQ) received the most radiation, cross-back aprons provided greater protection than vests, and cross-table lateral images exposed the breast to greater radiation than AP images. **They concluded** that proper lead wear is vital to reduce radiation exposure.

*Hayda et al. reviewed radiation exposure and health risks for orthopedic surgeons.* **They reported** that orthopedic surgeons have a 2.9 relative risk of breast cancer. **They recommended** proper precautions to minimize radiation exposure: radiation shields (including lead aprons), lead glasses, and conscientious c-arm positioning and management.

Incorrect answers:
Answer 1-4: The breast is typically defined in quadrants (not halves), and the upper outer quadrant is the most common location of breast cancer.
