## Question

Femoral offset in THR is ( arthroplasty- hip arthroplasty- ( arthroplasty- hip arthroplasty- anatomy, biomechanics and templating)

## Options

1. Distance from the center of rotation of the femoral head to a line bisecting the long axis of the femur.
2. Distance between the center of the femoral head and the true floor of the acetabulum.
3. Distance between the true floor of the acetabulum to a line bisecting the long axis of the femur.
4. Distance between the most prominent point on the trochanter and the center of the femoral head.

## Correct option

**1. Distance from the center of rotation of the femoral head to a line bisecting the long axis of the femur.**

## Answer explanation

Femoral offset is the distance from the center of rotation of the femoral head to a line bisecting the long axis of the femur. Increased femoral offset increases hip stability and decreases joint reaction force and impingement. Decreased offset may lead to instability, abductor weakness and gluteus medius lurch.

Acetabular offset: Acetabular offset (AO) is the distance between the center of the femoral head and the true floor of the acetabulum.

Answer: 1
