## Question

Most common diagnosis with recurrent fractures with hearing loss in children is (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. Osteogenesis imperfecta type II
2. Osteogenesis imperfecta type I
3. Paget’s disease
4. Osteogenesis imperfecta type IV

## Correct option

**2. Osteogenesis imperfecta type I**

## Answer explanation

Hearing loss is commonly found in OI with prevalence rates ranging from 50 to 92% in some studies. Hearing loss in OI may be conductive, mixed, or sensorineural and is more common by the second or third decade, but also seen in children. Hearing loss varies with the type of OI and is reported to be most common in type I OI and rarely reported in type IV OI

Answer: 2
