## Question

Harington’s criteria are used in [General Orthopedics-Pathological Fractures]

## Options

1. Decision making in surgical correction of idiopathic scoliosis
2. Prophylactic fixation of impending pathological fixation of proximal femur
3. Prophylactic stapling in blount’s disease
4. Decision making in proximal humeral fracture fixation

## Correct option

**2. Prophylactic fixation of impending pathological fixation of proximal femur**

## Answer explanation

Harrington’s criteria are used in prophylactic fixation of impending pathological fracture of proximal femur. These are

> 50% destruction of diaphyseal cortices

 lesion > 2.5 cm

Permeative destruction of the subtrochanteric femoral region

Persistent pain following irradiation

Major limitation of these criteria is that these are not validated and these can be applied to proximal femur only.

Answer : b
