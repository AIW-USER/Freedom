## Question

Which of the following conditions are associated with an increased risk of deep vein thrombosis in pediatric patients?

## Options

1. Lesion with with soft tissue calcifications and carvernous blood filled structure
2. Lytic bone lesion with methicillin-susceptible gram positive cocci in clusters
3. Bone sarcoma with a t(11: 22) translocation
4. Distal femur eccentric lytic lesion with a sclerotic rim and fibroblastic spindle cells in a whirled storiform pattern
5. Recurrent knee effusions with hemosiderin staining giant cells

## Correct option

**3. Bone sarcoma with a t(11: 22) translocation**

## Answer explanation

Sarcomas in pediatric patients, including Ewing's sarcoma, are associated with an increased risk of deep vein thrombosis.

Venous thromboembolism (VTE) is a potentially lethal complication in many orthopedic conditions. However, there has been a higher association of VTE in patients, even the pediatric population, with sarcomas. The proposed mechanism is tumor activation of factor X to factor Xa, leading to clot formation. Despite the higher association in pediatric patients, there still does not appear to be a consensus amongst specialists regarding appropriate prophylaxis.

*Paz-Priel et al. performed a retrospective study of thromboembolic events in 122 pediatric patients treated for sarcoma.* Nineteen patients experienced 23 thromboembolic events with a higher rate of events in patients treated for metastatic disease and asymptomatic 43% of cases. **They concluded** pediatric sarcoma patients should be monitored closely for thrombosis.

*Crocco et al. evaluated the consensus of venous thromboembolism (VTE) prophylaxis in pediatric sarcoma patients through a 5-question questionnaire.* **They reported** a large variation in specialty preference in VTE prophylaxis with orthopedic surgeons having the highest preference to treat(60%). **The authors concluded** a lack of consensus exists for VTE prophylaxis in pediatric sarcoma patients.

Incorrect answers
Answer 1: Hemangiomas can present with soft tissue calcifications on radiographs and blood-filled cavernous structures on histology. There is no association between hemangiomas and VTEs.
Answer 2: Osteomyelitis with methicillin-susceptible Staphylococcus aureus is not a risk factor for VTE events. However, MRSA infections have a higher risk of an associated VTE event.
Answer 4: Non-ossifying fibromas are characterized by metaphyseal eccentric lytic lesions with sclerotic rims. Histologically, there are fibroblastic spindle cells with a whirled storiform pattern and are not associated with an increased VTE risk.
Answer 5: Recurrent knee effusions with hemosiderin-staining multinucleated giant cells are characteristic of pigmented villonodular synovitis. These lesions are not associated with an increased VTE risk.
