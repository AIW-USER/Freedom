## Question

## Options

1. Complex regional pain syndrome
2. Osteoarthritis
3. Osteosarcoma
4. Osteonecrosis
5. Inflammatory arthritis

## Correct option

**4. Osteonecrosis**

## Answer explanation

The clinical exam and MRI studies are consistent with osteonecrosis of the knee.

Osteonecrosis of the knee occurs less frequently than of the hip or shoulder. In this case the tibial plateau is predominantly affected however there are smaller lesions on the femoral condyles as well. The symptoms are similar to those of idiopathic osteonecrosis of the medial femoral condyle and include pain and tenderness of the medial aspect of the knee and a slight synovitis. The range of motion of the knee remains within normal limits, and no gross deformity is present. Osteonecrosis of the tibial plateau is easily misdiagnosed as degenerative meniscus or osteoarthritis of the compartment of the knee. Review of lateral radiographs may reveal an osteopenic area in the subchondral bone of the medial tibial plateau. The diagnosis is more easily established with MRI studies with surrounding marrow hypointensity on T1-weighted images and corresponding surrounding marrow T2 hyperintensity on T2-weighted images. Treatment depends on severity of symptoms, age and functional status of the patient with knee arthroplasty having shown great outcomes in end-stage disease.

*Pareek et al. performed a retrospective review on 223 patients with a diagnosis of subchondral insufficiency fractures of the knee, also referred to as spontaneous osteonecrosis of the knee.* *Of the 223 patients, 154 had femoral involvement (69%) and 123 tibia (55%), with medial compartment involvement in 198 knees (89%).* *The rates of conversion to surgical intervention and arthroplasty was 47% and 37% respectively, in patients with >5 years of follow-up.* **The authors conclude** subchondral insufficiency fractures predominantly involve the medial compartment of the knee and approximately one-third of patients progressed to total knee arthroplasty.

*Ecker and Lotke performed a review of 15 patients with spontaneous osteonecrosis of the knee.* *All patients presented with acute pain in the knee and tenderness to palpation over the medial aspect of the tibial plateau.* *Plain x-rays initially showed a radiolucent area at the site of the tenderness in 9/16 knees with follow-up x-rays eventually showing the the typical lesion in all 16 knees.* Progression of the symptoms led to a TKA in nine knees and to a unicompartmental replacement in three, 2 refused treatment and resolution of symptoms in two knees.

Figures A and B are MRI studies revealing T1 and T2 weighted images with predominantly a large medial tibial plateau AVN lesion. You can see the characteristic serpiginous border of the lesion.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: MRI findings in complex regional pain syndrome are normal
Answer 2: In osteoarthritic involvement of the medial compartment, uptake is over both the medial femoral condyle and the medial tibial plateau.
Answer 3: Osteosarcoma has a characteristic radiographic appearance of a bone-forming tumor.
Answer 5: Inflammatory osteoarthritis will generally involve the entire knee
