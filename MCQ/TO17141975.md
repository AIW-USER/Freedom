## Question

Strongest tibiotalar ligament (FNB Pattern Paper 2020)

## Options

1. Posterior tibiotalar ligament
2. Talonavicular ligament
3. Anterior deep tibiotalar ligament
4. Posterior deep tibiotalar ligament

## Correct option

**4. Posterior deep tibiotalar ligament**

## Answer explanation

The posterior talofibular ligament (PTFL) is the strongest and most deeply seated ligament of the lateral complex. Deep posterior tibiotalar ligament is the strongest part of the medial deltoid complex.

Lateral ligament complex: The lateral collateral ligament complex (LCL) consists of the anterior talofibular, the calcaneofibular, and the posterior talofibular ligaments.

Medial Deltoid complex: can roughly be divided into a superficial and deep group of fibers.
