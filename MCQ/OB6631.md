## Question

What is the most likely cause of the lesion shown in Figures 35a and 35b?

![[q6631-a-35a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q6631-b-35b.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Surgery
2. Contusion
3. Parathyroid tumor
4. Bisphosphonate use
5. Corticosteroid use

## Correct option

**2. Contusion**

## Answer explanation

The most common cause of myositis ossificans is contusion. Certain regions, including the quadriceps and brachialis, are more commonly affected. The mechanisms of development have not been clearly established.
