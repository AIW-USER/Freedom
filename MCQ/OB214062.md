## Question

Which of the following drugs works by indirect inhibition of factor X in the clotting cascade?

## Options

1. Rivaroxaban
2. Fondaparinux
3. Apixaban
4. Dabigatran
5. Edoxaban

## Correct option

**2. Fondaparinux**

## Answer explanation

Fondaparinux works by indirect inhibition of factor X in the clotting cascade by potentiating the effects of antithrombin III on factor X.

The coagulation cascade comprises of a series of reactions that lead to the formation of fibrin, which leads to platelet activation and clot formation. It is in this cascade that several pharmaceutical medications have been created to help prevent venous thromboembolism after total hip (THA) and total knee (TKA) arthroplasty. There are a wide variety of anticoagulation medications ranging from oral to subcutaneous to intravenous. Fondaparinux is a fully synthetic pentasaccharide that precisely corresponds to the heparinoid binding site on antithrombin III. It provides highly selective indirect inhibition of the clotting cascade at the level of factor X.

*Bawa et al. evaluated the trends in postoperative DVT prophylaxis within 6 months of total joint arthroplasty (TJA) in a large cohort of 369,473 patients.* **They noted** that warfarin was the most commonly prescribed anticoagulant and it was most commonly prescribed to patients with a hypercoagulable diagnosis. A multivariate regression analysis showed that patients prescribed aspirin, fondaparinux, and rivaroxaban were less likely than those prescribed warfarin or enoxaparin to have a DVT within 6 months.

*Runner et al. analyzed 22,072 patients having undergone TJA to determine efficacy, complication rates, and prescribing patterns for different DVT prophylactic strategies.* *The authors defined "less aggressive" prophylaxis patterns as aspirin and/or sequential compression devices and "more aggressive" prophylaxis as enoxaparin, warfarin, rivaroxaban, or fondaparinux.* **They noted** that the use of a less aggressive prophylaxis strategy was associated with patients having no complications (95.5% vs 93.0%). **They concluded** that more aggressive strategies were associated with higher rates of bleeding and thrombotic complications and less aggressive strategies were not associated with a higher rate of thrombosis.
