## Question

Cephalosporins work by blocking the transpeptidase enzyme; this has which of the following actions on bacteria?

## Options

1. Inhibits cell wall synthesis
2. Inhibits DNA metabolism
3. Increases cell membrane permeability
4. Mimics metabolite substrate
5. Increases RNA synthesis

## Correct option

**1. Inhibits cell wall synthesis**

## Answer explanation

Penicillins and cephalosporins block the transpeptidase enzyme that is responsible for cross-linking of polysaccharide molecules in the bacterial cell wall.

The cephalosporins are semisynthetic derivatives of the cephalosporin-C antibiotic isolated from the fungus Cephalosporium acremonium and have been divided into first-, second-, and third-generation cephalosporins. The quinolone group of antibiotics inhibit DNA metabolism. Amphotericin and nystatin bind sterols in the cell membrane, increasing permeability. Trimethoprim and the sulfonamides in combination block folic acid metabolism. Aminoglycosides bind ribosomal subunits and inhibit RNA synthesis.
