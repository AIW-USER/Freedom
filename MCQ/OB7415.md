## Question

Which of the following surgical approaches to the knee has the greatest potential for denervation of the quadriceps muscle?

## Options

1. Subvastus
2. Midvastus
3. Quadriceps sparing
4. Median parapatellar
5. Tibial tubercle osteotomy

## Correct option

**2. Midvastus**

## Answer explanation

Several studies have demonstrated excellent functional results and recovery after total knee arthroplasty (TKA) with a variety of minimally invasive approaches; however, studies have demonstrated abnormal electromyographic (EMG) studies in a significant number of TKAs performed using a midvastus exposure. Patients whose vastus medialis intervals were developed bluntly were significantly more likely to fully recover normal EMG activity than if the intervals were developed with sharp dissection.
