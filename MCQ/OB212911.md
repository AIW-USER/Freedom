## Question

Which of the following correctly describes multiorgan failure in pediatric trauma patients?

## Options

1. Occurs early after admission; all organ systems affected simultaneously
2. Occurs early after admission; cardiovascular system affected first
3. Occurs within 12-24 hours after admission; cardiovascular system affected first
4. Occurs around 48 hours after admission; all organ systems affected simultaneously
5. Occurs around 48 hours after admission; cardiovascular system affected first

## Correct option

**1. Occurs early after admission; all organ systems affected simultaneously**

## Answer explanation

Multiorgan failure (MOF) in pediatric trauma patients usually occurs early (within the first 24 hours) after admission and affects almost all organ systems simultaneously.

The timing of MOF in pediatric trauma patients differs from that in adult trauma patients. MOF failure in children often occurs early after admission during resuscitation and affects all organs almost simultaneously, whereas organ failure in adults begins 48 hours after injury and occurs sequentially, starting with the lungs. Additionally, the rate of acute lung injury has been found to be almost six times lower in children than in adults.

*Pandya et al. performed a review of the pediatric polytrauma patient.* **They report** that the orthopaedic surgeon plays an important role in managing hemodynamic instability, vascular insult, and neurologic damage in the child with multiple injuries. **They conclude** that the pattern of MOF in children differs than that of adults.

*Calkins et al. performed a retrospective study to determine the incidence, course, and severity of MOF in children.* **They found** that the incidence of postinjury MOF is rare in children (3%). **They also found** that the mortality rate was 17% for this cohort. **They conclude** that the incidence of postinjury MOF in the child is less than the adult, and it is less severe when it occurs.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 2, 3, 4, & 5: MOF in pediatric trauma patients occurs early after admission and affects all organ systems simultaneously.
