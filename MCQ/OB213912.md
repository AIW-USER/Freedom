## Question

A 35-year-old female presents to your clinic complaining of pain and swelling of her right elbow. She does not remember the onset and denies any trauma to the elbow. She has also noticed recently that she can't flex her right elbow as much as the left. On exam, her right elbow range of motion is limited from 30 to 90 degrees. Radiographs are shown in Figure A. Which of the following has been shown to be a beneficial treatment for the patient?

![[q213912-a-melorheostosis.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Zoledronate
2. Calcitonin
3. Rituximab
4. Tamoxifen
5. Stereotactic radiation

## Correct option

**1. Zoledronate**

## Answer explanation

The patient has a diagnosis of melorheostosis given the "dripping candle wax" appearing, corticated lesions noted on the radiographs. Treatment with bisphosphonates has been shown to be beneficial for both pain and swelling of the elbow.

Melorheostosis is an extremely rare benign painful disorder characterized by the formation of periosteal new bone. There is no known sex predilection and no known hereditary pattern of transmission. Patients will generally present with joint pain, decreased range of motion, and the appearance of joint swelling. Radiographs often show “dripping candle wax” appearance with dense hyperostosis that flows along the cortex. Bone hyperostosis may flow across the joints leading to mechanical blocks in range of motion. Serum alkaline phosphatase is often elevated. Treatment is generally conservative with pain control and the use of bisphosphonates having been shown to be of some benefit with pain and swelling. In refractory cases with severe joint immobility, hyperostotic bone resection with contracture release can be performed.

*Donath et al. presented a case report of a 38-year-old male with extensive melorheostosis with progressive deformity of the left hand, left leg, and right foot.* The patient was treated with a bisphosphonate (30 mg/day of pamidronate for 6 days) infusion had an improvement in both pain and swelling of his right foot. The patient also had a reduction in his serum alkaline phosphatase following treatment.

*Slimani et al. reported a case report of an adult patient with very painful melorheostosis who had significant symptomatic improvement after treatment with zoledronate.* **The authors conclude** that zoledronate can be a medical treatment in cases of refractory pain due to this very rare disorder.

Figure A: lateral radiograph of the elbow revealing diffuse cortical hyperostosis with characteristic "dripping candle wax" appearance of periosteal bone that crossed the elbow joint.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: Calcitonin binds to membrane receptors on osteoclasts to inhibit resorption and is used for treatment in osteoporosis.
Answer 3: Rituximab is a monoclonal antibody to CD20 antigen which is used for the treatment of rheumatoid arthritis.
Answer 4: Tamoxifen is an estrogen receptor blocker that has been beneficial for the treatment of desmoid tumors.
Answer 5: Radiation has not been shown to be beneficial for the treatment of melorheostosis.
