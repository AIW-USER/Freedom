## Question

An athlete sustained obvious ankle fracture dislocation on field. After assessing airway, breathing and circulation, What is the next best step? (FNB Pattern Paper 2020)

## Options

1. Immediate shift to hospital
2. Oral analgesic
3. Splint the limb in situ in as it is position
4. Gross reduction of dislocation

## Correct option

**3. Splint the limb in situ in as it is position**

## Answer explanation

Answer : C

In mentioned scenario, the ideal time for assessment of athlete should be less than 60 seconds and assessment should be done inside the Field of Play (FOP).

Quick decisions by the doctor have to be taken regarding: -

- If the injury is serious and removing the athlete from FOP is mandatory
- Or to let the athlete continue while playing

Now, suppose the doctor decides on allowing athlete to stay in FOP, managing non life threatening injuries like a minor cut isn’t allowed on field. For that athlete needs to be taken out of field. Exception to this scenario is when it’s a life threatening situation and immediate resuscitation is required on filed (as in case of sudden cardiac arrest.

Now, in an obvious fracture/dislocation, athlete has to be moved out of FOP. Recommendation is that on field limb should be placed in a splint or immobilization boot/ shoe, and the shoe be left in place while the athlete is being transported to the hospital and until the doctor has obtained radiographic confirmation of the injury and one has ruled out any fractures, which are commonly associated with this dislocation.

So ideal response here should be situation C. For reducing the dislocation, athlete has to be transferred out of the FOP.
