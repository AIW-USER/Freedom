## Question

A patient who had FFD of 15o at knee is undergoing TKR. During gap balancing you find that the knee is tight in extension on medial side with flexion balanced, structure to be released should be

## Options

1. Superficial MCL
2. POL
3. Pes insertions
4. PCL

## Correct option

**2. POL**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

STEPS OF MEDIAL SOFT TISSUE RELEASE (for tight medial gap)

- Deep MCL and Postero medial capsule from tibia and Medial osteophytes removal
- Posterior Oblique ligament
- Semimembranosus (and transect PCL if preserved)
- Pie crust Superficial MCL and release Pes anserinus insertions
- Strip off periosteum and superficial MCL from tibia distally for additional 4-5 cms

STEPS OF LATERAL RELEASE (for tight lateral side)

- Remove lateral osteophytes and postero lateral capsule
- PCL to be always released
- Pie crust IT band (if tight in extension) at joint line or release at Gerdy tubercle insertion
- Strip off LCL and Popliteus (if tight in flexion) from LFC
- Strip off Lateral head of Gastrocnemius and postero lateral capsule off the femur

STEPS OF POSTERIOR RELEASE (for FFD cases)

- Posterior tibial and femoral osteophytes to be removed
- Posterior capsule stripped off a short distance from femoral condyles posteriorly
- Release tendinous origins of medial and lateral Gastrocnemius muscles
