## Question

A 33-year-old male presents to your office for a follow-up evaluation of a mass in his right hand. Biopsy results are consistent with epithelioid sarcoma. Compared to other sarcoma subtypes, what site of metastasis does this type of tumor more commonly spread?

## Options

1. Bone
2. Brain
3. Lungs
4. Lymph nodes
5. Liver

## Correct option

**4. Lymph nodes**

## Answer explanation

Epithelioid sarcoma, one of the most common soft tissue sarcomas of the hand, has a propensity to metastasize to regional lymph nodes.

While relatively rare overall, epithelioid sarcoma represents one of the most common soft tissue sarcomas found in the hand and often presents in young adults with a 2:1 male to female predominance. Most patients present with a small, firm, slow-growing mass, which may ulcerate through the skin and be confused with skin carcinomas. Treatment consists of wide excision with adjuvant radiotherapy. Sentinel node biopsy should also be considered to evaluate for metastasis, as this represents a unique and common site of metastasis for this subtype of sarcoma.

*Pradhan et al review the management of 63 patients found to have soft tissue sarcomas of the hand.* **They report** clear cell (17.4%), epithelioid (17.4%), and synovial sarcoma (16%) to be the most commonly encountered in the hand. **They conclude** that both epithelioid and clear cell sarcomas tend to metastasize to regional lymph nodes more often than other sarcomas.

*McPhee et al. review the evaluation and treatment of 24 patients diagnosed with primary soft tissue sarcoma of the hand.* **They report** that epithelioid sarcoma (25%) was among the most common diagnoses. **The authors concluded** that tumor grade and surgical margin most affected local recurrence and survival.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1, 2, 5: Epithelioid sarcoma most commonly metastasizes to the regional lymph nodes, not the bone, brain, or liver.
Answer 3: While the lungs are the most common site of metastasis overall, this question is testing your knowledge that regional lymph node metastases in epithelioid sarcoma are more common compared to other sarcoma subtypes.
