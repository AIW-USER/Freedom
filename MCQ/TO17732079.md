## Question

Best indication for meniscus repair is( Arthroscopy- knee Arthroscopy- meniscus injuries)

## Options

1. Red-white zone tear
2. Capsule-meniscal junction tear
3. White-white zone tear
4. Fresh radial tear in young patient

## Correct option

**2. Capsule-meniscal junction tear**

## Answer explanation

Capsulo-meniscal junction is the most peripheral part of the red –red zone of a meniscus. It has the maximum healing potential due to rich vascular supply.

Answer: 2
