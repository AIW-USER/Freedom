## Question

Which is not a function of Parathyroid hormone (PTH)?

## Options

1. Increases renal hydroxylation of 25-hydroxycholecalciferol
2. Increases resorption of calcium and decreases resorption of phosphate in kidneys
3. Increases osteoclastic resorption of bone
4. Increases osteoblastic resorption of bone

## Correct option

**4. Increases osteoblastic resorption of bone**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 4

PTH stimulates osteoblasts that finally stimulate osteoclasts leading to bone resorption. There is no term like Osteoblastic bone resorption; bone resorption is osteoclastic.
