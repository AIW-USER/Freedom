Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Anatomy ( Knee )

## Question

Not true about fabella [FNB 2019]

## Options

1. Can be seen in medial head of gastrocnemius
2. Can be seen in both medial and lateral heads of gastrocnemius
3. Can be bony or cartilaginous
4. No bony attachment

## Correct option

**4. No bony attachment**

## Answer explanation

The fabella is an accessory ossicle most always found in the lateral head of the gastrocnemius, rarely can occur at the medial head of gastrocnemius . It occurs in ~20% (range 10-30%) of the population . It often articulates directly with the lateral femoral condyle. The fabella can be bony or cartilaginous.

The fabella’s main function is thought to be stabilization of the medial femoral condyle and the fabella complex, which is made up of the plantaris and gastrocnemius muscles and the arcuate, fabellofibular, fabello popliteal, and oblique popliteal ligaments.

Answer : d
