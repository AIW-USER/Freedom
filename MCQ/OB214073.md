## Question

Which of the following shows the correct order of decreasing shelf life for bone allografts?

## Options

1. Freeze-dried > Fresh-Frozen stored at -20 degrees C > Fresh-Frozen stored at -70 degrees C > Fresh
2. Freeze-dried > Fresh > Fresh-Frozen stored at -70 degrees C > Fresh-Frozen stored at -20 degrees C
3. Fresh > Fresh-Frozen stored at -20 degrees C > Fresh-Frozen stored at -70 degrees C > Freeze-dried
4. Freeze-dried > Fresh-frozen stored at -70 degrees C > Fresh-Frozen stored at -20 degrees C > Fresh
5. Fresh-frozen stored at -70 degrees C > Fresh-Frozen stored at -20 degrees C > Freeze-dried > Fresh

## Correct option

**4. Freeze-dried > Fresh-frozen stored at -70 degrees C > Fresh-Frozen stored at -20 degrees C > Fresh**

## Answer explanation

The shelf-life of allograft bone is considered to be highest in Freeze-dried bone (5 years to infinite) followed by fresh-frozen bone stored at -70 degrees C (up to 5 years), followed by fresh-frozen bone stored at -20 degrees C (1-2 years) followed by fresh bone (hours-days).

Allograft bone has had increasing use as a bone substitute over the last 30 years. The specific properties of the allograft bone depends on its size, location (cortical or cancellous), and its method of preservation.The optimal method of preparing allograft is debridement of all soft tissue, washing the bone with ethanol (removes live cells), and subsequent gamma irradiation (sterilization). Freezing the bone will lead to longer preservation but at the cost of loss of osteoinductive properties. The lower the temperature of storage, the longer the shelf-life of the bone graft with freeze-dried bone theoretically having an infinite shelf-life.

*Shelton et al. reviewed the use of allograft bone for knee reconstruction.* *They cite the advantages of allogeneic bone use to include less surgical morbidity, shorter surgical time, smaller incisions, and the wider selection of graft sizes and types of tissue while the disadvantages include the risk of disease transmission, a slower biologic remodeling process, and the potential for a subclinical immune response.* Allografts can be obtained in several forms, including fresh, fresh-frozen, freeze-dried, and cryopreserved. The preservation is critical in determining the shelf-life of the graft and for appropriate use prior to expiration. **The authors conclude** that the physician should thoroughly understand the structural and biologic influence of the preservation technique used for the allograft they plan to use.

*Folsch et al. review the effect of the storage temperature of allograft bone on its biomechanical and biological behavior.* **The authors noted** that short term storage up to 6 months is recommended with −20 °C and storage for 1-2 years is recommended at −70 °C. They cite that duration of storage at −20 °C can be maintained at least for 2 years. **They also note** that studies have shown no clinically relevant impairment of biomechanical properties of cancellous bone due to different storage temperatures.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1, 2, 3, 5: The correct order of shelf-life in decreasing order is Freeze-dried > Fresh-frozen stored at -70 degrees C > Fresh-Frozen stored at -20 degrees C > Fresh
