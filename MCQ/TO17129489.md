Chapter: Musculoskeletal Infections MCQs — Brodi’s Abscess (Subacute Osteomyelitis)

## Question

Penumbra sign on MRI is seen in (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. Hydatid cyst
2. Brodie's abscess
3. Degeneration of a hydatid cyst
4. Hemangioma

## Correct option

**2. Brodie's abscess**

## Answer explanation

The penumbra sign represents a rim of vascularised granulation tissue around a bone abscess cavity that has a higher T1 signal intensity than the cavity itself.

Answer: 2
