## Question

On an axial CT image, which of the following dimensions is considered to be indicative of a critical amount of lumbar spinal stenosis?

## Options

1. AP canal diameter of less than 20 mm
2. AP canal diameter of less than 50 mm
3. Cross-sectional area of less than 100 mm2
4. Cross-sectional area of less than 50 mm2
5. Circumferential area of less than 25 mm

## Correct option

**3. Cross-sectional area of less than 100 mm2**

## Answer explanation

Lumbar central stenosis is defined by an AP canal diameter of less than 10 mm or a cross-sectional area of less than 100 mm2 as measured on CT.
