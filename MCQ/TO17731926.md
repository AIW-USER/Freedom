## Question

Which phase of bowling exerts maximum valgus force to elbow during bowling? ( Sports medicine- biomechanics of common sports [phases of throwing])

## Options

1. Acceleration
2. Wind-up
3. Late cocking
4. Deceleration

## Correct option

**3. Late cocking**

## Answer explanation

There are 5 main phases of throwing (Baseball throwing) are:

wind up, cocking, acceleration, deceleration and follow-through

1. Wind Up: Rotator cuff muscles are inactive during this phase, so minimal force is exerted on the shoulder.

2. Cocking

early cocking- Deltoid is most active muscle

late cocking- Rotator cuff muscles are the most active muscles. It is a high torque phase in which maximal shoulder external rotation occurs. Valgus stress to the elbow joint is greatest in the late cocking phase. Repeated injury in this phase is responsible for Internal Impingement and GIRD (glenohumeral internal rotation defect).

3. Acceleration: This phase extends from maximal external rotation in the late cocking phase to the release of the ball. Active muscles are triceps, pectoralis major, latissimus dorsi, and serratus anterior

4. Deceleration : In this phase eccentric contraction of all muscles occurs to slow down arm motion. It is the highest torque phase and also the most harmful phase of throwing. Associated injuries to this phase are SLAP lesion, biceps tendon injury ,brachialis injury and teres minor injury.

5. Follow- through: In this phase muscle activity returns to resting levels

Answer: 3
