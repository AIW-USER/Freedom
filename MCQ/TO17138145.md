Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Sprengel's Deformity

## Question

Woodward procedure is used for which of the following diseases?

## Options

1. Osteochondroma
2. Klippel Feil syndrome
3. Sprengel’s shoulder
4. Pseudarthrosis of clavicle

## Correct option

**3. Sprengel’s shoulder**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Sprengel's shoulder – Failure of descent of scapula. Scapula lies proximally. Classification – Cavendish

Treatment- Release of scapular muscles and mobilization of scapula (Woodward procedure)

Answer: 3
