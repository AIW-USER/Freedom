Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Wear And Osteolysis (Hip)

## Question

In an optimally positioned acetabular shell wear is mostly seen in which quadrant?

## Options

1. Posterosuperior
2. Anterosuperior
3. Central
4. Anteroinferior

## Correct option

**2. Anterosuperior**

## Answer explanation

In in vitro studies edge-wear has been most prevalent in the superior anterior quadrant of the acetabular components. Disregarding wear coverage extending towards the pole, the distribution of edge-wear is seen most prevalent on the superior edge of the acetabular bearing surface.

Comparable to the in vivo location of edge-wear, acetabular cartilage degradation is also found to be most severe at the anterosuperior portion of native hip joints.

Acetabular component malpositioning increases the prevalence of edge-wear, particularly with excessive angles of inclination. In greatly anteverted acetabular wear scar is centred at -90° (posterior). This may be explained, however, by posterior impingement of the femoral neck on the cup rim during extension.

Answer : b
