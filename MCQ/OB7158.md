## Question

Forty grams of polymethylmethacrylate (PMMA) is mixed for implantation of a cemented total hip arthroplasty. Due to significant patient risk factors for infection, vancomycin is added to the cement. What is the maximum amount of antibiotic that can be added that optimizes antibiotic delivery and does not have deleterious effects on the mechanical properties of the cement?

## Options

1. 500 mg
2. 1 g
3. 2 g
4. 4 g
5. 8 g

## Correct option

**3. 2 g**

## Answer explanation

The maximal amount of antibiotic to be added to PMMA for optimizing antibiotic elution without deleterious effects on mechanical strength is 5% of the total weight of the PMMA. In this example, this amounts to 5% of the 40 grams, or 2 grams of antibiotic. Commercially prepared antibiotic-impregnated PMMA typically contains 1 g of antibiotic per 40 g of PMMA. The antibiotic should be added as a powder, never as a liquid to avoid affecting the binding and handling properties of the preparation.
