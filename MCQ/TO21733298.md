## Question

A young marathon runner complains of vague pain in right foot while running. He gives history of injury to his right foot 6 months ago. What is the correct diagnosis and treatment? His X ray scan of weight bearing right foot is given below.

![[TO21733298-1.png]]

## Options

1. Metatarsus adductus and corrective osteotomy
2. Lisfranc injury and screw fixation of medial cuneiform and second metatarsal
3. Lisfranc injury and arthrodesis of medial column
4. Metatarsus adductus, corrective osteotomy and medial column arthrodesis

## Correct option

**3. Lisfranc injury and arthrodesis of medial column**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 3
EXPLANATION: -
This is a case of neglected pure ligamentous Lisfranc injury with more than 5mm diastasis and tarsometatarsal joints is preferred treatment in purely ligamentous lisfranc injuries, delayed treatment, chronic deformity and complete Lisfranc fracture dislocations (Type A or C2).
