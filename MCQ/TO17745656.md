## Question

All are true about stingers except (sports medicine- burners and stingers)

## Options

1. Resolution of symptoms in 6-8 weeks
2. Bilateral arm weakness and paresthesia
3. Traction of brachial plexus
4. Precludes participation in present contest

## Correct option

**2. Bilateral arm weakness and paresthesia**

## Answer explanation

Burners or stingers: These are mild traction injuries of brachial plexus in sports persons. These are common injuries in collision sports (e.g., football, rugby, ice hockey, wrestling) and most common nerve injury in a sports person.

Mechanism : Common mechanisms of injury include distraction of shoulder and head in opposite direction, cervical compression and extension to the same side the head is flexed toward, and direct trauma to the exiting nerve root or brachial plexus.

Signs and symptoms: After traction injury sports people develop immediate pain, weakness, and paresthesia (burning, stinging) symptoms radiating from the shoulder down the upper extremity. Muscle weakness mostly occurs in more than one major muscle group acting on the shoulder (e.g., external rotators, flexors, and abductors). Cervical ROM often is reduced in chronic cases.

In most cases, burner stinger syndrome is an acute mild injury with symptoms lasting only several minutes. However, 5% to 10% of those affected have symptoms lasting up to several weeks. Symptoms are never bilateral and may preclude participation in sports.

Answer : b
