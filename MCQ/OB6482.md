## Question

A 62-year-old man reports hip pain. A radiograph is shown in Figure 40a, and MRI scans are shown in Figures 40b and 40c. A biopsy specimen is shown in Figure 40d. Staging studies show no other sites of disease. What is the best treatment for this patient?

## Options

1. Chemotherapy and radiation therapy
2. Chemotherapy and wide resection
3. Curettage and cement fixation
4. Radiation therapy
5. Wide resection

## Correct option

**5. Wide resection**

## Answer explanation

The patient has a typical chondrosarcoma of the pelvis. This is a destructive lytic lesion with a large soft-tissue mass of the pubis and acetabulum. Metastatic carcinoma would be a logical first choice, but the biopsy specimen clearly defines a chondroid lesion with cellular atypia. This is an intermediate grade chondrosarcoma. Chondrosarcomas are not typically sensitive to chemotherapy or radiation therapy. Surgery is the mainstay of treatment. Wide resection is the treatment of choice for this patient.
