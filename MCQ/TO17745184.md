## Question

A 9 yr old player having activity related pain in heel, relieved on rest. Diagnosis (regional orthopedics- foot and ankle- sever’s disease)

## Options

1. Sever's disease
2. OCD talus
3. Achilles tendinopathy
4. Tarsal coalition

## Correct option

**1. Sever's disease**

## Answer explanation

Sever’s disease (calcaneal apophysitis) is the most common aetiology of heel pain in growing children. It is postulated to be caused by repetitive microtrauma due to increased traction by Achilles tendon on its insertion site. Bilateral involvement is present in approximately 60% of cases. Pain increases with activity and often there is a preceding history of sports or dance. Plain x-ray of the heels may demonstrate sclerotic changes and fragmentation at the insertion of TA to the calcaneus. It is usually managed conservatively with anti-inflammatory medications and restriction of physical activity.

Answer: 1
