## Question

A 45-year-old distance runner has a hyaluronic acid injection to his knee because of degenerative arthritis. He immediately develops a severe rash and a systemic hypersensitivity reaction. This patient most likely is also allergic to which of the following?

## Options

1. Penicillin
2. Sulfur
3. Shellfish
4. Chicken or eggs
5. Lidocaine

## Correct option

**4. Chicken or eggs**

## Answer explanation

Preparations of hyaluronic acid can be divided into low and high molecular weight compounds. Contraindications to intra-articular hyaluronic acid include joint or skin infection, overlying skin disease, and allergies to chicken or egg products if using a preparation derived from rooster comb.
