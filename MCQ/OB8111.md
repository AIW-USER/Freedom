## Question

Lisfranc's ligament connects which of the following structures?

## Options

1. Base of the first metatarsal to the base of the second metatarsal
2. Base of the first metatarsal to the middle cuneiform
3. Base of the second metatarsal to the middle cuneiform
4. Base of the second metatarsal to the medial cuneiform
5. Medial cuneiform to the middle cuneiform

## Correct option

**4. Base of the second metatarsal to the medial cuneiform**

## Answer explanation

The stability of the Lisfranc joint complex results from bony and ligamentous contributions. The metatarsal cuneiform articulations form a Roman arch configuration. The second metatarsal is recessed between the medial and lateral cuneiforms, adding more stability. Strong intermetatarsal ligaments are present between each of the lateral four metatarsals but are absent between the first and second metatarsals. In this region, the base of the second metatarsal is joined to the medial cuneiform by Lisfranc's ligament.
