## Question

Contracture or tightness of the triangular ligament of the finger is associated with which of the following conditions?

## Options

1. Sagittal band insufficiency
2. Volar subluxation of the lateral bands
3. Swan-neck deformity
4. Volar plate contracture
5. Boutonniere deformity

## Correct option

**3. Swan-neck deformity**

## Answer explanation

Swan-neck deformity may result from contracture/tightness of the triangular ligament. Anatomically, the triangular ligament is on the dorsal aspect at the base of the middle phalanx just distal to the central slip. It keeps the lateral bands dorsal. With a boutonniere deformity, the lateral bands move volar to the central axis resulting in a flexion deformity of the proximal interphalangeal joint and extension of the distal interphalangeal joint. Sagittal band insufficiency results in subluxation of the extensor tendon(s) at the metacarpophalangeal joint level. Volar plate contracture will not cause swan-neck deformity.
