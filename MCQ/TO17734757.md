## Question

A 32 years old male presents with severe left side radiculopathy and backache. On examination he has saddle anesthesia. What are the likely diagnosis and other signs and symptoms? (spine- cauda equina/conus medullaris)

## Options

1. Cauda equina syndrome, Urinary retention, absent ankle jerk
2. Cauda equina syndrome, Perianal anesthesia, areflexia
3. Conus medullaris syndrome, Symmetrical flaccid paralysis
4. Conus medullaris syndrome, urinary incontinence, areflexia

## Correct option

**1. Cauda equina syndrome, Urinary retention, absent ankle jerk**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 1
