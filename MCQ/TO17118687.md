# TO17118687

#TO #kneesports #acltear
Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — ACL

## Question

True about Delhi active test is

## Options

1. A standing manoeuvre for complete ACL tear
2. A standing manoeuvre for complete PCL tear
3. A supine manoeuvre for complete ACL tear
4. A supine manoeuvre for complete PCL tear

## Correct option

**1. A standing manoeuvre for complete ACL tear**

## Answer explanation

This is a clinical test to detect complete ACL tear. In this new method, the patient remains standing throughout and performs the test actively as instructed by the examiner.

![[TO17118687-1.png]]

The affected limb is abducted and the foot is in firm contact with the examination table to ensure the internal rotation of the leg. To begin with the test, the patient is asked to flex slowly the extended knee and then to extend the flexed knee. Patient is asked to repeat these maneuvers. With gradual knee flexion, at approximately 15 to 20 degrees, the anterolaterally subluxed lateral tibial condyle relocates with a visible ‘shift’ or ‘clunk’. As the patient extends the flexed knee, the lateral tibial condyle subluxates anterolaterally with a visible ‘jerk’ of the knee. The examiner, standing preferably at the front, watches these signs of anterolaterally unstable knee, which confirms the complete tear of ACL.

Answer : a
