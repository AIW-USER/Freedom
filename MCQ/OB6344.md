## Question

Acral metastases are most commonly seen in what type of carcinoma?

## Options

1. Renal cell
2. Lung
3. Thyroid
4. Breast
5. Ovarian

## Correct option

**2. Lung**

## Answer explanation

Metastatic lesions to bone are usually located in the axial and proximal appendicular skeleton. Metastases below the elbow and knee are rare, but when they do occur they are most commonly from lung carcinoma.
