## Question

A positive Ober test for contracture of the tensor fascia lata and iliotibial band is characterized by which of the following?

## Options

1. Limited hip adduction when flexed
2. Limited hip adduction when extended
3. Limited hip abduction when flexed
4. Limited hip abduction when extended
5. Bow legged gait

## Correct option

**2. Limited hip adduction when extended**

## Answer explanation

The Ober test is positive with contracture of the tensor fascia lata or iliotibial band which limits adduction of the hip while in an extended position. The tensor fascia lata flexes and abducts the hip so a contracture would limit both extension and adduction.

Gautam designed a new exam maneuver that quantifies the contracture by stabilizing the pelvis and maintain hip extension using a prone position.
