Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Congenital Vertical Talus (CVT)

## Question

Which of the following is correct to be diagnosed for a child to have congenital vertical talus?

## Options

1. PF stress view TAMBA > 35
2. DF Stress view TAMBA > 35
3. PF stress view TAMBA < 35
4. DF Stress view TAMBA < 35

## Correct option

**1. PF stress view TAMBA > 35**

## Answer explanation

Hamanishi had studied in detail the radiology of congenital vertical talus. He described the angles related to the CVT i.e. TAMBA and CAMBA which are the Talus- metatarsal base angle ad Calcaneal Metatarsal base angle. The TAMBA is used to diagnose CVT in a stress plantar flexion view. I.e. If, even on plantar flexion, the angle remains high)

Metatarsal base is used as a surrogate till the navicular ossification center appears which is usually around 3 years of age.
