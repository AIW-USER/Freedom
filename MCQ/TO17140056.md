Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Anatomy ( Shoulder)

## Question

Most useful landmark to locate the superior border of subscapularis during total shoulder arthroplasty is

## Options

1. Coracoid process
2. Biceps tendon
3. Humeral insertion of pectoralis major tendon
4. Anterolateral aspect of acromion

## Correct option

**2. Biceps tendon**

## Answer explanation

To perform subscapularis tenotomy identification of superior aspect of subscapularis is essential. Intact rotator cuff makes this identification and exposure difficult. Biceps tendon can be easily located just medial to the humeral insertion of the pectoralis major and followed superiorly to identify the rotator interval. Rotator interval is opened to visualize the superior aspect of the subscapularis tendon. In the event that the biceps tendon is ruptured or dislocated, the base of the coracoid process can be used to identify the medial aspect of the rotator interval.

Answer: 2
