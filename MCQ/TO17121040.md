Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — Shoulder Instability[Unidirectional and Multidirectional]

## Question

Kim’s lesion is (FNB Pattern Paper 2014)

## Options

1. Root avulsion of anterior horn of medial meniscus
2. Incomplete and concealed avulsion of posteroinferior labrum
3. Anterior labral periosteal sleeve avulsion along with IGHL tear
4. Root avulsion of posterior horn of medial meniscus

## Correct option

**2. Incomplete and concealed avulsion of posteroinferior labrum**

## Answer explanation

Kim's lesion: It is an incomplete and concealed avulsion of the posteroinferior labrum in posterior or multidirectional posteroinferior instability of the shoulder. It is usually diagnosed on arthroscopy and requires arthroscopic repair to prevent posterior shoulder dislocation.

Answer: 2
