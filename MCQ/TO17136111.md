## Question

Kobe bryant is an american professional basketball player. He came to limelight about taking orthokine therapy for his ailing knee . All are true with respect to orthokine therapy except

## Options

1. Contain IL-1 anatagonist
2. contain excessive amount of growth factors
3. contain anti-inflammatory properties
4. to be stored at 5 degree temperature

## Correct option

**4. to be stored at 5 degree temperature**

## Answer explanation

ORTHOKINE THERAPY is a variation of blood spinning, are also prohibited by WADA. Orthokine is different from platelet rich plasma that its being cultured at higher temperature.

The Orthokine procedure involves taking 20 cc of the patient’s blood and putting it in a tube with ‘factors”. it includes heating the cells and culturing with glass beads.The blood is spun down and injected into the patient where the platelets and lymphocytes help the body heal itself quickly.

Per FDA regulations, this manipulates the blood sample too much, and would now be considered a drug that has not been approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA). Orthokine is similar to PRP in that it is preparation of your own blood.

Difference is in preparation:

- Orthokine has the ability to increase the number of growth factors per procedure compared with PRP, and also has the advantage of blocking IL-1 and therefore blocking the inflammatory response.
