## Question

Which is a major criteria for fat embolism? (trauma- fat embolism syndrome/ VTE/PE)

## Options

1. Tachycardia over 110 beats/minute
2. Pyrexia > 38.50 C
3. Retinal changes of fat or petechiae
4. Petechial rash

## Correct option

**4. Petechial rash**
