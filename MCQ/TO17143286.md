Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Enchondroma And Multiple Enchondroma

## Question

Most common tumor of hand

## Options

1. Squammous cell carcinoma
2. Enchondroma
3. Chondrsarcoma
4. Osteochondroma

## Correct option

**1. Squammous cell carcinoma**

## Answer explanation

Most common tumor of bone: Metastasis.

Most common primary tumor of bone: Multiple myeloma > Osteosarcoma > Chondrosarcoma.

Most common benign bone tumor: Osteochondroma.

Most common true benign bone tumor: Osteoid osteoma.

Most common lesion of bone: Fibrous cortical defect.

Most common malignant tumor of bone found in first decade of life: Ewing’s sarcoma.

Most common radiation-induced tumor: Osteosarcoma > Fibrosarcoma > Malignant fibrous histiocytoma.

Most radiosensitive and chemo sensitive bone tumors: Ewing’s sarcoma most common tumor of jaw is squamous cell carcinoma of oral mucosa but most common bone tumor of mandible is Ameloblastoma.

Most common bone tumor of hand bones: Enchondroma (benign tumor).

Most common malignant bone tumor of hand bones: Chondrosarcoma (otherwise, most common malignant tumor of hand-squamous cell carcinoma).

Most common malignant bone tumor of chest wall: Chondrosarcoma.

Answer: 1
