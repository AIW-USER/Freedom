## Question

Which of the following factors is most associated with malrotation during antegrade or retrograde femoral nailing? (trauma- femoral shaft fracture)

## Options

1. Entry point
2. Fracture level
3. Fracture comminution
4. Surgeon’s experience

## Correct option

**3. Fracture comminution**

## Answer explanation

In femoral intramedullary nailing length changes and malrotation are significantly associated with fracture comminution. Increased comminution increases malrotation rates.

Answer: 3
