## Question

Most common meniscal injury associated with chronic ACL tear is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Posterior horn of MM
2. Anterior horn of LM
3. Anterior horn of MM
4. Posterior horn of LM

## Correct option

**1. Posterior horn of MM**

## Answer explanation

Medial meniscus tears are more common in patients with chronic ACL insufficiency, lateral meniscus tears are predominantly found in acute ACL injuries. Posterior horn tears are more common than anterior horn tears.

 Answer: A
