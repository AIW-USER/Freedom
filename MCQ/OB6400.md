## Question

What is the most common complication associated with the reconstruction shown in Figures 10a through 10c?

![[q6400-a-10a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q6400-b-10b.jpg]]
*Figure B*

![[q6400-c-10c.jpg]]

## Options

1. Infection
2. Sciatic nerve injury
3. Heterotopic ossification
4. Osteonecrosis of the femoral head
5. Posttraumatic arthritis

## Correct option

**3. Heterotopic ossification**

## Answer explanation

The reconstruction of this very unstable T type acetabular fracture required two surgical approaches as demonstrated by hardware on both the anterior and posterior columns. Whereas the incidence of nerve injury and infection are elevated with an extended surgical approach or combined surgical approaches, heterotopic ossification is by far the greatest risk. Osteonecrosis is related to the injury and may be higher with a dislocation. Posttraumatic arthritis is related to the injury and the quality of the reduction.
