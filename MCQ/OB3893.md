## Options

1. Talonavicular joint only
2. Talonavicular and naviculocuneiform joints
3. Talonavicular and subtalar joints
4. Naviculocuneiform and subtalar joints
5. Talonavicular, naviculocuneiform, and subtalar joints

## Correct option

**2. Talonavicular and naviculocuneiform joints**

## Answer explanation

The patient has avascular necrosis of the navicular bone (also known as Mueller-Weiss syndrome). Surgical management should consist of arthrodesis of the talonavicular and the naviculocuneiform joints.

Spontaneous osteonecrosis of the navicular is a rare disease with many considering it related to Kohler's disease. It is often seen in middle-aged patients in the fourth to fifth decade of life (in contrast to Kohler’s which is seen in the pediatric population) who present with chronic midfoot pain. The pain is generated by abnormal motion at the talonavicular and naviculocunieform joints. This diagnosis must be differentiated from a navicular stress fracture which also can present with chronic midfoot pain. The radiographs of navicular osteonecrosis show a narrow navicular bone with lateral sclerosis. There can also be a splitting of the navicular known as the "double navicular sign." Initial treatment consists of semirigid insoles with surgical management consisting of talonavicular and naviculocuneiform fusion being reserved for patients who fail conservative management.

*Fernandez de Retana et al. performed a study to review arthrodesis of the talonavicular and naviculocuneiform joints in osteonecrosis of the talus.* **They report** that conservative treatment is usually effective in most patients, but in those who fail conservative treatment, surgical options exist. **They conclude** that talonavicular and naviculocuneiform arthrodesis can lead to good results while alleviating pain in patients with talus osteonecrosis.

*Kitaura et al. reported on 2 cases of spontaneous osteonecrosis of the tarsal navicular, or Mueller-Weiss syndrome.* **They reported** that both patients had failed conservative management and underwent double (talonavicular and naviculocuneiform joints) arthrodesis with locking plates and iliac crest autograft. **The authors conclude** that a double arthrodesis procedure is a reliable treatment for the management of spontaneous osteonecrosis of the tarsal navicular bone.

Figure A is a lateral radiograph of the ankle demonstrating sclerosis of the navicular with abnormality of the talonavicular articulation. Figures B and C are sagittal T-2 weighted MRI cuts demonstrating high signal within the navicular and disruption of the talonavicular joint.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Isolated fusion of the talonavicular joint only will not address the pathology at the naviculocuneiform joint.
Answers 3, 4, and 5: The subtalar joint is spared in this disease process.
