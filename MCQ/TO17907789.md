## Question

Incorrect statement regarding the use of S2 alar iliac screws in comparison to direct iliac screws for sacropelvic fixation is

## Options

1. Alar screws were introduced to overcome the difficulties of iliac screws
2. Alar screws have deeper starting point so problem of prominence is not there
3. An additional connector is not needed as they are better aligned with lumbar screws
4. They cause less violation of sacroiliac joint

## Correct option

**4. They cause less violation of sacroiliac joint**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D

Sacropelvic (SP) fixation is the immobilization of the sacroiliac joint to attain lumbosacral fusion and prevent distal spinal junctional failure. SP fixation is indicated in numerous spinal conditions (e.g. scoliosis, multilevel spondylolisthesis, spinal/sacral trauma, tumors, or infections). Many SP fixation techniques have been described in the literature. Currently, the most used surgical techniques for SP fixation are direct iliac screws and sacral-2-alar-iliac screws.

Direct Iliac screws traditionally have had following disadvantages: -

- Entry point is posterior iliac crest in line with S1 sacral foramen (near PSIS) and their trajectory goes towards ASIS. Its prominent causing pain in supine position and sometimes even wound complications and hence the screws often need removal.
- The screws don’t line up well with lumbar screws so additional connector rods are often required
- Their placement often requires extensive devascularization of iliac muscles.

To overcome these difficulties, S2 alar iliac screws were introduced in 2007 by Kebaish et al. The S2 alar screw is a modification of the iliac fixation technique using the space between the neuroforamina of S1 and S2 as an insertion point to fix the sacrum to the ilium.

- They have deeper starting point (15 mm deeper) so problem of prominence is not there, and hence a revision surgery for screw removal is seldom indicated.
- They better align up with lumbar screws so additional connectors are not required.
- Their main disadvantage is that they cause more damage to sacroiliac joint.
