## Question

A 16-year-old male is seen in your clinic complaining of recently worsening knee pain and swelling with the MRI and histology imaging shown in Figures A & B, respectively. Given this patient's diagnosis, the presence of which of the following would be the most concerning predictor of a negative prognosis?

![[q215505-a-osteosarc-distal-femur.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q215505-b-osteosarc-histo.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Enneking stage Ia classification
2. 95% tumor necrosis after chemotherapy
3. Use of multiple chemotherapy agents
4. Expression of multi-drug-resistant (MDR) gene
5. Decreased alkaline phosphatase

## Correct option

**4. Expression of multi-drug-resistant (MDR) gene**

## Answer explanation

This patient has been diagnosed with osteosarcoma, and the expression of the multi-drug resistant (MDR) gene is consistent with a poorer prognosis, as the tumor cells are able to pump the therapeutic chemotherapy out of the cells.

Osteosarcoma has a long-term survival rate between 50-75%. It is typically treated with a combination of chemotherapy pre- and post-operatively, combined with wide surgical resection. Current chemotherapy regimens usually include methotrexate, doxorubicin, and cisplatin. Risk factors for poor prognosis include MDR positivity, metastatic spread, poor chemotherapeutic response, vascular involvement, and poor surgical margins.

*Smeland et al. performed a randomized controlled trial evaluating outcomes and prognostic indicators in patients with osteosarcoma.* **They noted** 3 and 5 year event-free survival rates of 59% and 54% respectively. Further, **they conclude** poor prognostic factors include pulmonary metastases, non-pulmonary metastases, and axial skeleton tumor site.

*Allison et al. reviewed osteosarcoma outcomes over the past 50 years and* **noted** the curious lack of improvement in survival rates in this population in the last 30 years. **They suggest** that we must invest in research and drug therapies and focus more attention on targeted molecular therapies, rather than cytotoxic drugs moving forward. **They conclude** by reiterating that improvements in osteosarcoma treatments have plateaued and we must continue to be diligent in future research efforts.

Figure A depicts a coronal T2 MRI image with a large osteosarcoma invading the local soft tissues. Figure B represents a histologic slide showing osteosarcoma which its classic "lacey osteoid."

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Enneking stage Ia tumors are low grade and intracompartmental and do not represent the most concerning predictive factor for poor prognosis.
Answer 2: 95% tumor necrosis would be predictive of a good, not a negative prognosis.
Answer 3: Use of chemotherapy is commonplace and multiple agents are commonly employed to improve effectiveness.
Answer 5: Increased alkaline phosphatase is representative of a poorer prognosis.
