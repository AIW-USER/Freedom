## Question

Which of the following are present in osteoarthritic articular cartilage but not in normal aging articular cartilage?

## Options

1. Increased water content, increased proteoglycan degradation
2. Decreased water content, decreased proteoglycan concentration
3. Decreased water content, decreased collagen concentration
4. Increased water content, decreased collagen concentration

## Correct option

**1. Increased water content, increased proteoglycan degradation**

## Answer explanation

Relative to the normal aging process in articular cartilage, osteoarthritic changes include increased water content, a relative increase in collagen concentration as a result of loss of proteoglycans through increased degradation, increase in chondroitin sulfate, decrease in keratin sulfate, and decrease in elastic modulus.

Answer: 1
