## Question

Which of the following is an injury that occurs in the trailing wrist in golf

## Options

1. Dorsal rim impaction syndrome
2. Extensor Carpi ulnaris Synovitis
3. Hook of Hamate fractures
4. Scaphoid fractures

## Correct option

**1. Dorsal rim impaction syndrome**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

EXPLANATION: -

The leading wrist is more vulnerable to injuries in golfers as compared to the trailing wrist. The injuries of the leading wrist occur as a result of moving from radial deviation at the top of back swing to ulnar deviation during follow through. The common injuries of the leading wrist are:

- Stress injuries of hamate
- Hook of hamate fractures
- ECU synovitis / subluxation
- TFCC injuries

The trailing wrist extends during backswing and flexes during follow through. Dorsal rim impaction syndrome is common in them. This is caused by axial loading of the wrist in dorsiflexion on a repetitive basis. Players complain of pinpoint radio-carpal joint pain on loaded extension but not on passive extension. Chronically inflamed synovium with loose bodies maybe present on MRI. Treatment is primarily conservative and involves modification of training programs, wrist strengthening exercises couple with intermittent use of wrist guards/ braces to prevent excessive dorsiflexion. Arthroscopy may be required in non responders.
