## Correct option

**3. C**

## Answer explanation

Figure A is a T2-weighted MRI demonstrating the characteristic bone bruising pattern seen in a tear of the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL). The ACL's major blood supply comes from the middle genicular artery which corresponds to letter C in Figure B.

The ACL has been described as a hypovascular structure. Disruption of the blood supply to the ACL has been described as the cause of the large effusion and hemarthrosis after injury. The main blood supply to the ACL is the middle genicular artery from the back of the knee as it comes off of the popliteal artery. The inferior medial and inferior lateral genicular arteries have also been found to supply the distal aspect of the ACL directly.

*Toy et al. performed a study to determine the arterial supply to the ACL by injecting a fresh cadaver knee with a pigment.* *They subsequently dissected the knee for examination.* A second specimen was evaluated by computed tomography. **They found** that a majority of the ACL vascularization arises from the middle genicular artery and vessels of the infrapatellar fat pad and adjacent synovium. Terminal branches of the inferior medial and lateral genicular arteries supply the distal portion of the ACL directly. **They conclude** that the proximal and distal portion of the ACL are better vascularized than the middle portion and the proximal portion has the highest vascular density.

*Scapinelli et al. performed a review to describe the arterial supply of the ACL.* **They report** that the major supplying vessel to the ACL is the middle genicular artery. **They also report** that fetal and neonatal connections between the branches of this artery and the vascular network of the infrapatellar fat pad, mainly nourished by the terminal branches of the inferior genicular arteries, are usually lost in the adult.

Figure A is a T2-weighted MRI demonstrating bone bruising in the middle third of the lateral femoral condyle and the posterior third of the lateral tibial plateau, which is seen in a rupture of the ACL. Figure B is an unlabeled illustration of the blood supply to the knee. Illustration A is a labeled version of the same image.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Letter A corresponds to the lateral superior genicular artery.
Answer 2: Letter B corresponds to the medial superior genicular artery.
Answer 4: Letter D corresponds to the lateral inferior genicular artery, and though it contributes to the ACL, it is not the major blood supply.
Answer 5: Letter E corresponds to the medial inferior genicular artery, and though it contributes to the ACL, it is not the major blood supply.
