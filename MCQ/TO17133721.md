## Question

A young adult presents with anterior wedge compression fracture of L1 vertebra with 40% loss of vertebral height. What factor is an indication for surgery? (FNB Pattern Paper 2020)

## Options

1. Left leg radiculopathy since injury
2. Posterior ligamentous complex injury on MRI
3. Middle column injury
4. Kyphosis > 15 degree

## Correct option

**2. Posterior ligamentous complex injury on MRI**

## Answer explanation

Thoracolumbar Injury Severity Score (TLISS) system.

- This system incorporates the neurologic examination of the patient in a more direct way than previous systems and uses this information with the fracture morphology and the integrity of the posterior ligamentous complex to derive a numeric score for a given injury.
- The numeric value is then used to guide treatment options, and these treatment options are based on consensus opinions.
- This system was subsequently modified to become the Thoracolumbar Injury Classification and Severity Score (TLICS) by the original author in an effort to improve the reliability of classifying injuries
