## Question

Medial quadruple complex consists of all except? (FNB Pattern Paper 2017

## Options

1. Medial collateral ligament
2. Medial meniscus
3. Posterior oblique ligament
4. Semimembranosus

## Correct option

**2. Medial meniscus**

## Answer explanation

Medial quadruple complex is made up of MCL, semimembranosus, pes anserinus tendons (semitendinosus, gracilis, Sartorius) and posterior oblique ligament

Answer: 2
