## Question

True about the given X-Ray

## Options

1. Operated case of ACL plus ALL reconstruction
2. Operated case of all inside ACL reconstruction
3. Operated case of ACL reconstruction with medial meniscal root repair
4. Operated case of revision ACL reconstruction with allograft

## Correct option

**2. Operated case of all inside ACL reconstruction**

## Answer explanation

This is a case of all inside ACL reconstruction. In ACL reconstruction common fixation methods of graft into tunnels include

- Femoral cortical button (inside out) and tibial interference screw (outside-in)

![[TO17118661-2.jpg]]

- Femoral interference screw (inside out) and tibial interference screw(outside-in)

![[TO17118661-3.png]]

- Femoral cortical button (inside out) and tibial cortical button (inside-out)- this is all inside technique as shown in the given X ray.
- Femoral cross pin(outside-in) and Tibial interference screw (bio screw which is not seen in the X ray) and staple

![[TO17118661-4.png]]

Answer: 2
