## Question

Which of these values reflects the normal tibio- femoral axis for a child aged three years? [Perdiatric Orthopedic-Tibial Bowing]

## Options

1. Varus of 7°
2. Varus of 10°
3. Neutral alignment
4. Valgus of 10°

## Correct option

**4. Valgus of 10°**

## Answer explanation

During development, the tibio-femoral alignment in children changes during early years. At birth it is 10° to 15° of varus, which remodels to neutral at about 24 months of age and then becomes 10° of valgus by the age of three years. Over the next four years it gradually goes to normal alignment of 7°.

Answer: 4
