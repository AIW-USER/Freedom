## Question

Incorrect statement regarding the knee Cruciate Ligaments is

## Options

1. Antero-medial ACL bundle is tight in flexion
2. Antero-lateral PCL bundle is tight in flexion
3. ACL bundles are named after their positions on femoral footprints while PCL bundles are named after their positions on tibial footprint
4. Tibial insertion of PCL is wider than the femoral insertion

## Correct option

**3. ACL bundles are named after their positions on femoral footprints while PCL bundles are named after their positions on tibial footprint**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

ANTERIOR CRUCIATE LIGAMENT [ACL]

- 31 to 38mm is average length
- Tibial insertion is 120% of femoral insertion

TWO BUNDLES (Named after their position on Tibial footprint): -

A] AM Bundle (Tight in flexion)

 -Femoral insertion cross section area: 47 ± 13mm2

 -Tibial insertion cross section area: 56 ± 21mm2

 -Length 38mm on average

B] PL Bundle (Tight in Extension)

 -Femoral insertion- 49 ± 13mm2

 -Tibial insertion- 53 ± 21mm2

 -Length 18mm average

Overall AM bundle is longer and has slightly larger diameter than PL bundle.

POSTERIOR CRUCIATE LIGAMENT [PCL]

- Avg. length 38mm
- Diameter: 10/11mm
- Tibial insertion wider than femoral

TWO BUNDLES (Named after their position on femoral footprint)

A] AL Bundle (55%)- Bulkier -Tight in flexion

B] PM Bundle (45%)-Tight in extension
