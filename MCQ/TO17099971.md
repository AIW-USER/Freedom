## Question

Which is not a distally fixed cementless stem?

## Options

1. Type 2
2. Type 3b
3. Type 4
4. Type 3c

## Correct option

**1. Type 2**

## Answer explanation

Cementless stems can be categorized according to distinct geometries that govern where fixation is obtained. Commonly used classification system is based on the amount of osseous contact and the progression of stem fixation from proximal to distal. In this classification, Types 1 through 4 are straight stems, and as the number increases so does the fixation area. Types 1, 2, and 3 are tapered, designed to obtain more proximal fixation, and Type 4 is fully coated to obtain distal fixation. Type 5 is a modular prosthesis, and Type-6 stems are curved, anatomic designs and are used less commonly

Answer: 1
