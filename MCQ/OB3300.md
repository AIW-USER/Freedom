## Question

The chi-square test is considered the most appropriate statistical test to analyze categorical data, but is unreliable if there are less than 5 events in any of the groups or the sum of all cells is less than 50. Which test is preferred in place of the chi-square test when these small sample sizes are encountered?

## Options

1. Fisher exact test
2. Regression analysis
3. Two-sample t-test
4. Mann-Whitney test
5. Analysis of variance (ANOVA)

## Correct option

**1. Fisher exact test**

## Answer explanation

In the situation where there are relatively few total cases (the sum of all cells is less than approximately 50 or less than 5 events in a cell), the Fisher exact test is the most appropriate substitute for the chi-square test.

Fisher's exact test is a statistical significance test used in the analysis of contingency tables. Although in practice it is employed when sample sizes are small, it is valid for all sample sizes. It is one of a class of exact tests, so called because the significance of the deviation from a null hypothesis (e.g., P-value) can be calculated exactly, rather than relying on an approximation that becomes exact in the limit as the sample size grows to infinity, as with many statistical tests. The chi-square test is a simple method of comparing two proportions, such as a difference in nonunion rates(%) between two groups of fracture patients.

Incorrect answers:

2) Regression analysis is used to estimate the association between a response variable and a series of known explanatory variables (includes simple, multiple, and logistic regression).

3) The two-sample t-test is a parametric test that compares two means.

4) A Mann-Whitney test, considered a non-parametric statistical test, compares the mean ranks of two independent groups, essentially evaluating whether one group tends to have higher ranked values than the other, making it useful when data is not normally distributed; it does not compare raw means but instead analyzes the ranks of the data within each group after combining them all together.

5) Analysis of variance (ANOVA) compares one dependent variable among three or more groups.
