## Question

The Achilles tendon is mainly supplied from( FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Anterior surface
2. Posterior surface
3. Medial surface
4. Lateral surface

## Correct option

**1. Anterior surface**

## Answer explanation

The Achilles tendon is mainly supplied by arteries from its anterior and deep surface, with branches either looping around or passing transverse through the tendon to gain access to its posterior superficial surface.

Answer: 1
