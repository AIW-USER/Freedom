## Question

Which of the following statements about nutritional supplements for sports and exercise is correct?

## Options

1. Creatine supplementation primarily helps in endurance performance rather than high-intensity, short-duration activities.
2. Branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs) are essential amino acids that play a role in muscle protein synthesis and reducing exercise-induced fatigue.
3. Sodium bicarbonate acts as an acidifier in the blood, making exercise performance less efficient.
4. Caffeine has no proven effects on exercise performance and does not enhance endurance.

## Correct option

**2. Branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs) are essential amino acids that play a role in muscle protein synthesis and reducing exercise-induced fatigue.**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

EXPLANATION: -

Nutritional supplements/ Ergonic aids for Sports and Exercise

The law defined a "dietary supplement" as a product taken by mouth that contains a "dietary ingredient" intended to supplement the diet. "Dietary ingredients" may include vitamins, minerals, herbs or other botanicals, amino acids, and substances (e.g., enzymes, organ tissues, glandular, and metabolites). Dietary supplements may also be extracts or concentrates from plants or foods. Dietary supplements are typically sold in the form of tablets, capsules, soft gels, liquids, powders, and bars. These ergogenic aids purport to improve the capability of an athlete to improve their performance. Their use is considered legal because they are regulated as food or dietary supplements, not drugs. Most nutrient and dietary supplements are presumed to be safe also if consumed in recommended dosages. Excess amounts of various nutrients and dietary supplements may pose health risks to some individuals.

As far as the question of these being effective is concerned, the supplements are grouped in in to following categories: -

• Apparently effective: Creatine, Ephedra, Sodium bicarbonate, Beta Alanine

• Possibly effective: Branch chain amino acids, Green tea extracts, Calcium, Hydroxymethyl butyrate

• Apparently ineffective: Glutamine, Carnitine

However, the practicality of the classification is bit debated and seemingly its too early to say the classification is correct. More research is required as of the day.

For purpose of use they can also be grouped in to muscle building, weight loss supplements and performance and recovery enhancing supplements.

• Muscle building: Creatine, Essential amino acids, Hydroxymethyl butyrate, Glutamine

• Weight loss inducing: Green tea extracts, Carnitine, Ephedra, Caffeine

• Performance enhancing: Creatine, Sodium bicarbonate, Caffeine, Beta alanine, Glutamine

• Recovery enhancing: Antioxidants
