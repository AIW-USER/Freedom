## Question

With knee in terminal extension (15-20 degree) if tibia is medially rotated, which muscle will show maximum activity (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. VMO
2. VL
3. VI
4. RF

## Correct option

**1. VMO**

## Answer explanation

It’s well known that PF knee pain responds to VMO strengthening. Now which is best way to strengthen VMO?

Studies hv shown that in open kinetic chain mode (non weight bearing/ sitting position) when tibia is medially rotated with knee in terminal extension, VMO shows maximum activity.

During closed chain mode (when tibia is bearing weight), squats with external rotation generate maximum activity in VMO.

Answer: 1
