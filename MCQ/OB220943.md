## Question

A 33-year-old female sustains a closed bimalleolar ankle fracture-dislocation after a slip on ice. She undergoes closed reduction in the emergency room under sedation, and a post-reduction CT scan is obtained. Based on the axial slice shown in Figure A, which of the following fracture characteristics would most likely be present?

![[q220943-a-orthobullets.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Comminuted fibula fracture above the level of the syndesmosis
2. Short oblique fibula fracture
3. Spiral fibula fracture proximal to the level of the tibiotalar joint
4. Transverse medial malleolus fracture
5. Vertical medial malleolus fracture

## Correct option

**5. Vertical medial malleolus fracture**

## Answer explanation

Figure A shows an axial CT scan slice at the level of the tibiotalar joint. Anteromedial impaction is noted on the selected slice, consistent with a supination-adduction (SAD). SAD injuries present most commonly with distal fibular avulsion fractures and vertical medial malleolus fractures (Answer choice 5).

Rotational ankle fractures are common injuries that are frequently classified using the Lauge-Hansen system. This classification attempts to characterize ankle fractures based on the position of the foot and the direction of the force applied at the time of injury. The four fracture patterns that are classified include SAD, supination-external rotation, pronation-abduction, and pronation-external rotation. SAD injuries can present with anteromedial impaction of the plafond. Reduction of the impaction is an important goal during operative intervention.

*Tartaglione et al. published a review article highlighting the Lauge-Hansen classification.* In the authors' review, the characteristics of each fracture pattern and the treatment methods are discussed.

*McConnell and Tornetta conducted a retrospective review of SAD injuries that presented to their institution over five years.* **They noted** a total of 44 SAD injuries with anteromedial impaction in eight patients (18 percent). **The authors note** the importance of reduction and stabilization in cases of impaction and conclude that suspicion for this finding should be high when treating SAD injury patterns.

*Rammelt and Boszczyk conducted a literature review highlighting the importance of osteoligamentous complexes in ankle fractures.* **The authors highlight** several points and conclude that a ligament-based view of ankle fractures is preferred for its ability to deepen understanding of the mechanism of injury.

Figure A demonstrates an axial CT scan slice of an SAD at the level of the tibiotalar joint. Anteromedial impaction can be visualized.

Incorrect Answer Choices:
Answer choice 1: This is representative of pronation-abduction injury.
Answer choice 2: This is representative of a supination-external rotation injury.
Answer choice 3,4: These are representative of a pronation-external rotation injury.
