## Question

A surgeon is planning the application of a single plane external fixation construct as provisional fixation for an unstable knee dislocation. There are two pin sizes available, one with twice the diameter of the other. The bending stiffness of the larger pin is greater than that of the smaller by a factor of which of the following?

## Options

1. 2
2. 4
3. 8
4. 16
5. 32

## Correct option

**4. 16**

## Answer explanation

The increase in bending stiffness is proportional to the radius to the 4th power, so a doubling results in an increase by a factor of 16.
