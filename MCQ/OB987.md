## Question

Which of the following is true regarding apoptosis?

## Options

1. It is the primary mechanism by which conventional chemotherapy kills cancer cells
2. It is the primary mechanism by which cancer metastasis occurs
3. It is the primary mechanism by which cancer causes organ failure
4. It is the primary mechanism by which cancer forms lytic lesions in bone
5. None of the above

## Correct option

**1. It is the primary mechanism by which conventional chemotherapy kills cancer cells**

## Answer explanation

Apoptosis is the process of programmed cell death, where intra- or extra-cellular signals induce a signaling cascade that causes the cell to die. While there are many mechanisms by which chemotherapy acts, the primary mechanism by which this treatment kills cancer cells is via apoptosis. Chemotherapy targets rapidly dividing cells, such as those found in neoplasms, which offers some specificity in cancer treatment. The side-effects of chemotherapy however are often due to the lack of complete specificity as other rapidly dividing cells like the lining of the gut, bone marrow, hair, and skin are also affected by chemotherapy.
