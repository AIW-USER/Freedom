## Question

In wear of acetabular cup which is the initial wear pattern?

## Options

1. fibrils
2. foldings
3. ripples
4. ditches

## Correct option

**2. foldings**

## Answer explanation

Wear mode and pattern:

There are different wear modes between PE cups that clinically have been worn rapidly and slowly. The PE wear process has three steps: (1) foldings are generated first, (2) ripples are formed, and (3) when the PE cup is exposed to more severe wear condition, ripples decrease and fibrils increase.

Foldings are generated by the loading force on the PE surface; ripples are formed by the sequential loading-shear force; and fibrils are produced from the surface and delaminated, leading to the formation of wear debris. Foldings are also seen on cups without oscillation, and this may prove that at least foldings can be generated regardless of the abrasive force.
