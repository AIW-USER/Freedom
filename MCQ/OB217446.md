## Question

A 27-year-old female presents to your office for evaluation of an ulnar shaft fracture. She is not willing to state how the injury happened, but does mention that she lives alone. During the encounter the patient's boyfriend answers every question for her. On exam, you notice several bruises to the patients arms and legs. Which of the following is true as it relates to intimate partner violence?

## Options

1. Highest prevalence in patients age 16-49 years old
2. Lifetime prevalence is 5% for women
3. Higher prevalence in long term relationships
4. Higher prevalence in couples who do not cohabitate
5. Physicians are required to report suspected intimate partner violence in all states

## Correct option

**1. Highest prevalence in patients age 16-49 years old**

## Answer explanation

Intimate partner violence is most common in patients age 16-49 years-old.

As many as 35% of female patients presenting to the ED with injuries may be a result of intimate partner violence. Risk factors that physicians should be aware of include: age (16-49 years most common), female gender, pregnant women, those involved in short-term relationships, and individuals of low socioeconomic status. Patients characteristically have a changing affect, constantly seek their partner's approval, do not speak for themselves, and repeatedly visit the ED.

Stöckl et al review the physical and mental health symptoms associated with intimate partner violence. They report that female patients from age 16 to 49 had 33% higher prevalence of intimate partner violence than female patients in other age ranges. They conclude that these patients may present with women's health symptoms, such as gastrointestinal, psychosomatic, and psychological symptoms, and pelvic problems. They conclude that individuals involved with screening patients should be aware of these symptoms.

*Bhandari et al review the incidence of intimate partner violence in patients presenting to an orthopedic fracture clinic.* **They report** that the overall prevalence of intimate partner violence (emotional, physical, and sexual abuse) within the last twelve months was 32%. **They conclude** that surgeons and healthcare personnel in fracture clinics should consider intimate partner violence when interacting with injured women.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: A female's lifetime prevalence of being involved with intimate partner violence is about 30%.
Answer 3 & 4: Intimate partner violence is more common in short-term relationships and in those couples who do cohabitate.
Answer 5: Physicians are obligated to report elder abuse, but not suspected domestic abuse in most states. They should, however, inquire about patient safety and encourage patients to file reports with the appropriate authorities.
