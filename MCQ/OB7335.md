## Question

Early failure of arthroscopic rotator cuff repair most commonly occurs by which of the following mechanisms?

## Options

1. Anchor pull-out
2. Anchor fracture
3. Suture rupture
4. Knot failure
5. Tissue failure

## Correct option

**5. Tissue failure**

## Answer explanation

Arthroscopic repair of the rotator cuff is becoming increasingly popular. Unfortunately, recent objective evaluations have indicated high failure rates even in patients who are clinically improved. Early failure can occur by failure of the suture anchor, suture, or knot. However, the most common cause of failure is when the suture pulls through the tendon. This results in "stretching" of the repair that can lead to gap formation between the repaired tendon and the osseous insertion, and subsequently, poor tendon-to-bone healing.
