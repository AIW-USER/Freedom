## Question

Morquio's syndrome is associated with all of the following anomalies EXCEPT:

## Options

1. Accumulation of keratan sulfate
2. Genu valgum
3. Short stature
4. Odontoid hypoplasia
5. Triad of low hairline, webbed neck, and limited cervical spine range of motion

## Correct option

**5. Triad of low hairline, webbed neck, and limited cervical spine range of motion**

## Answer explanation

Morquio’s syndrome is an autosomal recessive mucopolysaccharidosis. Mucopolysaccharidoses are a class of dwarfism that produce proportionate dwarfism and are a result of deficient or defective lysosomal enzymes. The 4 main types are Morquio’s, Hurler’s, Hunter’s, and Sanfilippo’s. Morquio’s is due to accumulation of keratan sulfate and presents around 2 years of age with waddling gait, genu valgum, thoracic kyphosis, cloudy corneas, and normal intelligence. C1–C2 instability (due to odontoid hypoplasia) can be seen with Morquio’s and can present with myelopathy. Klippel-Feil syndrome presents with a classic triad of low hairline, webbed neck, and limited cervical spine range of motion due to congenitally fused cervical segments. The review article by Copley and Dormans reviews the presentation and management of congenital cervical spine disorders.
