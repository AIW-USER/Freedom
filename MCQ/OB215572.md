## Question

When harvesting mesenchymal stem cells from the iliac crest, posterior aspiration is associated with which of the following compared to anterior aspiration?

## Options

1. Increased number of stem cells
2. Increased postoperative pain
3. Increased risk of superficial infection
4. Increased viability of stem cells
5. Increased gait abnormalities

## Correct option

**1. Increased number of stem cells**

## Answer explanation

Posterior iliac crest harvesting is associated with an increased number of stem cells without a change in viability compared to anterior harvest sites.

Bone marrow contains osteoblast progenitor cells that can be obtained with aspiration and appear to arise from a population of pluripotential connective-tissue stem cells. These cells ultimately differentiate into cells with a mature osteoblast phenotype, which can be helpful in fractures with difficulty healing. Anterior and posterior iliac crest sites have both been reported in the literature. Posterior harvest sites are reported to be less traumatic, have a lower overall complication rate and produce an increased number of stem cells. Viability of the obtained cells, however, is not different between harvest sites.

*Pierini et al review the differences in the samples obtained from posterior and anterior iliac crest harvest sites.* **They report** a 1.6x increase in colony founding connective tissue progenitors in posterior samples. Further, **they report** that the biologic potential of the cells was equivalent between sites. **They conclude** that whenever possible, posterior iliac crest harvest is preferred.

*Muschler et al review aspiration volume as it relates to obtaining osteoblastic progenitor cells.* **They report** that an increase in the aspiration volume from one to four mL caused a decrease of approximately 50 per cent in the final concentration of alkaline phosphatase-positive colony-forming units. **They conclude** that aspiration should be limited to 2 mL at any one site to maximize the concentration of progenitor cells.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: Posterior harvesting is less traumatic, and thus, patients report less postoperative pain.
Answer 3: Infection rates are not increased utilizing a posterior approach.
Answer 4: Viability is unchanged between cells obtained from each harvest site.
Answer 5: Increased gait abnormalities are associated with the more traumatic anterior technique.
