## Question

Which of the following pharmacologic treatments for osteoporosis decreases osteoclast activity and number by binding directly to osteoclasts?

## Options

1. Calcitonin
2. Denosumab
3. Romosozumab
4. Teriparatide
5. Vitamin D

## Correct option

**1. Calcitonin**

## Answer explanation

Calcitonin is a second-line pharmacologic treatment for osteoporosis that exerts its effect by decreasing osteoclast activity and number.

Calcitonin is a peptide hormone secreted from the parafollicular cells of the thyroid gland. When calcitonin binds its receptors on osteoclasts, there is a loss of the ruffled border, followed by cell retraction and arrest of bone resorption. Calcitonin's effects on osteocytes include inhibition of apoptosis and increased expression of sclerostin (a Wnt-signaling inhibitor).

*Tu et al. review treatment options for osteoporosis.* *They discuss non-pharmacologic management (calcium and vitamin D, weight-bearing exercise, smoking cessation, limitation of alcohol/caffeine consumption, and fall-prevention techniques), and pharmacologic treatments.* **The authors recommend** bisphosphonates as first-line treatment and either teriparatide or denosumab as second-line treatment. **They note** that calcitonin is an option for those who have failed first-line treatments, but that it does not have evidence for reduction of nonvertebral insufficiency fractures.

*Naot et al. review the role of calcitonin in inhibiting bone resorption.* **They conclude** that while calcitonin is an effective bone resorption inhibitor, its use has declined in recent years for conditions like Paget's disease and osteoporosis due to the development of more potent bone resorption inhibitors.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: Denosumab is a monoclonal IgG2 antibody against RANKL (receptor activator of nuclear factor-kappa B ligand).
Answer 3: Romosozumab is a monoclonal Ig2 that activates Wnt pathway by binding sclerostin
Answer 4: Teriparatide is a recombinant protein form of PTH (parathyroid hormone) which exerts its anabolic effect on bone by activating osteoblasts.
Answer 5: Vitamin D stimulates the terminal differentiation of osteoclasts and promotes the mineralization of osteoid matrix produced by osteoblasts.
