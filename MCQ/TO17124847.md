## Question

All of the following muscles have dual nerve supply except

## Options

1. Flexor Digitorum profundus
2. Adductor magnus
3. Gracilis
4. Brachialis

## Correct option

**3. Gracilis**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Composite muscle (Hybrid muscle) – having more than one set of fibers and performing the same function supplied by different nerves. FDP- ulnar and median nerve

Adductor magnus- obturator and sciatic nerve Brachialis- musculocutaneous and radial nerve Pectineus- femoral and obturator nerve

Flexor pollicis brevis- Median and ulnar nerve

Answer: 3
