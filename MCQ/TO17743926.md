## Question

Which is not an indication for negative pressure wound therapy (Trauma- open fractures)

## Options

1. Necrotizing fasciitis
2. Large diabetic foot ulcer
3. Ulcer with necrotic wound bed
4. Post surgical wound dehiscence

## Correct option

**3. Ulcer with necrotic wound bed**
