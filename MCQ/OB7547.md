## Options

1. Chemotherapy and wide resection
2. Radiation therapy alone
3. Extended curettage and radiation therapy
4. Wide surgical resection alone
5. Observation with repeat scans in 3 months

## Correct option

**4. Wide surgical resection alone**

## Answer explanation

This is a classic presentation of chondrosarcoma of the pelvis. Wide surgical resection is the treatment of choice. Though chemotherapy may be offered for patients with dedifferentiated chondrosarcoma, the histology of this lesion is consistent with an intermediate to high-grade myxoid chondrosarcoma. Chondrosarcomas are radioresistant, and should be aggressively treated with surgery. Extended intralesional resection of low-grade chondrosarcoma is acceptable in the appendicular skeleton; it is inappropriate in patients with intermediate or high-grade lesions and lesions that occur in the pelvis and other axial sites.
