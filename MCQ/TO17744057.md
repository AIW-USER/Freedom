## Question

Red flag signs for backache are all except [Spine-Lumbar Disc Herniation]

## Options

1. Unexplained fever
2. Age less than 20 years
3. History of significant trauma
4. Lack of family support

## Correct option

**4. Lack of family support**

## Answer explanation

Presence of red flags suggests presence of a serious underlying pathology as the genesis factor for LBA and must be thoroughly investigated. Evolution of chronic LBA is rather complex, with physiological, psychological and psychosocial influences. In no way should it be viewed as just an extension of acute back pain. Work-up for chronic back pain (and acute LBA) should include a screen for “Yellow Flag Signs” that indicate risk for problems ending up in long-term disability.
