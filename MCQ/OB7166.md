## Question

PTHrP-positive metastatic breast cancer cells in bone promote osteolysis via

## Options

1. osteoblastic production of receptor activator of nuclear factor-kappa B ligand (RANKL).
2. osteoclastic production of matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs).
3. inhibition of osteoblastic alkaline phosphatase activity.
4. capillary end artery obliteration.
5. inhibition of TGF-B release.

## Correct option

**1. osteoblastic production of receptor activator of nuclear factor-kappa B ligand (RANKL).**

## Answer explanation

PTHrP-positive breast cancer cells activate osteoblastic production of receptor activator of nuclear factor-kappa B ligand (RANKL). This promotes the activation of osteoclast progenitor cells and subsequently, osteoclastic bone resorption. Osteoclastic resorption induces a positive feedback loop on the tumor cells by release of TGF-B from the bone matrix to produce more PTHrP. This destructive loop is inhibited by bisphosphonate therapy, anti RANKL, and osteoprotegerin.
