Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Miscellaneous (Hand  Wrist)

## Question

Notta’s node is characteristic of

## Options

1. Pediatric trigger thumb
2. radial longitudinal deficiency
3. radioulnar synostosis
4. constriction ring syndrome

## Correct option

**1. Pediatric trigger thumb**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 1

EXPLANATION: -

Prominence of the flexor tendon nodule in pediatric trigger thumb is referred to as "Notta's node".
