Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Post TKR Infection

## Question

Which factor is not associated with increased chances of postoperative infection in a knee arthroplasty patient?

## Options

1. Rheumatoid arthritis
2. Asymptomatic bacteriuria
3. Use of a hinged knee prosthesis
4. Psoriasis

## Correct option

**2. Asymptomatic bacteriuria**

## Answer explanation

Preoperative factors associated with a higher rate of infection after TKA include rheumatoid arthritis (especially in seropositive men), skin ulceration, previous knee surgery, use of a hinged-knee prosthesis, obesity, concomitant urinary tract infection, steroid use, renal failure, diabetes mellitus, poor nutrition, malignancy, and psoriasis. Many studies have shown that asymptomatic bacteriuria is not associated with increased rate of postoperative infection.

Answer: 2
