## Question

28 Which of the following is false regarding olfaction:

## Options

1. Unmyelinated nerves
2. Olfactory nerves pass through cribiform plate
3. Entorhinal cortex is a part of primary olfactory cortex
4. The fibres relay to the VPL nucleus of thalamus

## Correct option

**4. The fibres relay to the VPL nucleus of thalamus**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D

The olfactory fibres do not relay in the thalamus. In fact, olfaction is the only sensory modality which do not relay in the thalamus. All other modalities of sensations, from cranial nerves or the spinal nerves, relay in the thalamus, before going to their respective sensory cortices.
