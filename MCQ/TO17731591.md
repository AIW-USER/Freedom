## Question

Which is most commonly injured ligament in knee?

## Options

1. ACL
2. MPFL
3. MCL
4. ATFL

## Correct option

**3. MCL**

## Answer explanation

MCL is the most commonly injured ligament. The ACL is the most commonly injured ligament which requires surgical intervention.

Answer: 3
