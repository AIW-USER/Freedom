## Question

Which of the following associated diagnoses is more likely to occur in a young adolescent with a displaced type III tibial tubercle fracture that occurred as a result of a noncontact basketball injury?

## Options

1. Compartment syndrome
2. Peroneal nerve palsy
3. Patella dislocation
4. Anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) and lateral collateral ligament (LCL) injuries
5. ACL and medial collateral ligament (MCL) injuries

## Correct option

**1. Compartment syndrome**

## Answer explanation

Most tibial tubercle fractures occur as a result of a noncontact injury often while a skeletally immature athlete lands from a jump. The resulting zone of soft-tissue injury often exceeds that of the tibial tubercle itself, leading to the development of a compartment syndrome. Typically, tibial tubercle fractures are not associated with ACL, MCL, LCL, or patella injuries.
