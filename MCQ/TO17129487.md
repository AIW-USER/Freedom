Chapter: Musculoskeletal Infections MCQs — Brodi’s Abscess (Subacute Osteomyelitis)

## Question

Brodie’s abscess is categorized under which type of osteomyelitis? (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Acute
2. Sub acute
3. Chronic
4. Non suppurative

## Correct option

**2. Sub acute**

## Answer explanation

Brodie’s abcess is long standing localized form of osteomyelitis seen in cases 10-20 years old where either the virulence of the infective organism is low or the immunity of the host is good, such that the host is able to localize the infection within the metaphysis. Proximal tibia (metaphysis) is the most common site. The label “Sub acute osteomyelitis” was added by Billroth based on the clinical presentation.

Answer: 2
