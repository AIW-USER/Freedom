## Question

Advancements in targeted gene therapies, immunomodulation, and hormone regulation have collectively changed the paradigm of cancer treatments. Selumetinib is a novel oral selective inhibitor of the mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase (MEK). In which of the following clinical scenarios is selumetinib indicated?

## Options

1. Long term management of t(9;22) chronic myeloid leukemia
2. Inoperable plexiform neurofibromas in children with type 1 neurofibromatosis
3. Aggressive pigmented villonodular synovitis recalcitrant to multiple surgical debridement
4. Giant cell tumors where surgery alone would result in significant residual functional deficits
5. Initial medical management of a minimally symptomatic extra-abdominal desmoid tumor

## Correct option

**2. Inoperable plexiform neurofibromas in children with type 1 neurofibromatosis**

## Answer explanation

In April 2020, Selumetinib was given FDA approval for use in pediatric patients with type 1 neurofibromatosis (NF1) who have symptomatic, inoperable plexiform neurofibromas (Answer 2).

Type 1 neurofibromatosis (NF-1) is an autosomal dominant genetic disorder characterized by multiple progressive tumor and nontumor manifestations. These patients demonstrate a predilection to forming plexiform neurofibromas, which are benign peripheral nerve sheath tumors that can undergo malignant transformation. These tumors can be notably large, resulting in disfigurement, pain, and even motor dysfunction. Due to their size and location, some of these tumors are considered inoperable (Illustration A). In such cases, selumetinib has been shown to decrease plexiform neurofibroma tumor burden via tumor volume reduction significantly. This results in improved quality of life and decreased tumor-related pain while maintaining a low-risk side effect profile (asymptomatic creatine kinase elevation, acneiform rash, gastrointestinal upset).

*Dombi and colleagues performed a phase 1 trial of selumetinib in NF1 children with inoperable plexiform neurofibromas.* *Treatment resulted in confirmed partial responses (tumor volume decrease >20%) in 17 of the 24 children (71%) with no cases of tumor progression.* **The authors further report** that patients endorsed decreased tumor-related pain and disfigurement with improved functional status. **They concluded** that this early phase data suggests significant benefit from long-term dose-adjusted treatment with selumetinib without excess side effects.

*Gross and colleagues conducted an open-label, phase 2 trial of selumetinib in NF1 children with inoperable plexiform neurofibromas.* *Of the 50 children enrolled, 70% had a partial response with a clinically meaningful reduction in child-reported tumor pain intensity scores.* Five patients discontinued treatment because of toxic effects (nausea/vomiting/diarrhea), and six patients had disease progression. **The authors concluded** that selumetinib demonstrated durable tumor shrinkage and clinical benefit in this patient population.

Illustration A demonstrates one patient’s drastic response to selumetinib with a clinically apparent tumor response on both MRI and physical examination.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Imatinib is indicated for the long-term management of t(9;22) chronic myeloid leukemia. It is a tyrosine kinase inhibitor that prevents the BCR-ABL protein, found in t(9;22) chronic myeloid leukemia, from exerting its downstream oncogenic effects.
Answer 3: Pexidartinib is a colony-stimulating factor (CSF-1) inhibitor indicated for the treatment of recalcitrant or inoperative (due to patient health reasons) pigmented villonodular synovitis (PVNS).
Answer 4: Denosumab is a monoclonal antibody against osteoblast-expressed RANK-L, preventing the activation of osteoclasts. It has been shown to decrease the size of giant cell tumors and result in the reconstitution of cortical bone, both of which serve to make resection surgery more feasible.
Answer 5: Tamoxifen is a selective estrogen receptor inhibitor shown to halt or slow the progression of desmoid tumors. It is generally indicated when the desmoid tumor is minimally symptomatic or inconsequential to overall function. More aggressive desmoid tumors are treated with chemotherapy and surgery.
