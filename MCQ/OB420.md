## Question

All of the following are contents of the carpal tunnel EXCEPT:

## Options

1. Flexor pollicis longus (FPL)
2. Flexor digitorum sublimis (FDS)
3. Flexor digitorum profundus (FDP)
4. Flexor carpi radialis (FCR)
5. Median nerve

## Correct option

**4. Flexor carpi radialis (FCR)**

## Answer explanation

The only neurovascular structure that runs in the carpal tunnel is the median nerve. Flexor carpi radialis is (FCR) is not a tendon within the carpal tunnel. In summary, the carpal tunnel contains the median nerve, FPL and 4 tendons each of the FDP and FDS. Of note, with respect to the FDS tendons, the FDS tendons to the 3rd and 4th digit are volar to the FDS tendons to the 2nd and 5th digit.
