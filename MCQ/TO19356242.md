## Question

A young male has come with Neck pain and weakness of both upper and lower limbs (Asia C) after sustaining trauma. X ray and MRI shows fracture dislocation at C4-C5. Vitals of the patient are stable. The most appropriate next line of management in this patient would be [FNB 2023]

## Options

1. Reduction with skull traction
2. Halo waist brace and immobilization
3. Hard collar and Observe
4. Immediate surgery with reduction and stabilization

## Correct option

**4. Immediate surgery with reduction and stabilization**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D
EXPLANATION: -

Here there is subaxial cervical spine injury and hence the SLIC scoring can be used to plan the management.

Here the SLIC scoring is
Injury morphology- 4
DLC status- 2
Neurology- 3+1
Total=10 (Indicator for surgery)
