## Question

Which bone tumor is treated by cyberknife surgery? (orthopaedic oncology- malignant tumors- chordoma)

## Options

1. Vertebral osteoblastoma
2. Osteosarcoma of sacrum
3. Chordoma
4. GCT

## Correct option

**3. Chordoma**

## Answer explanation

Chordomas are malignant bone tumors that arise from the sacrococcygeal region (50%), the base of the skull (35%) and vertebral column (15%). En bloc excision is the treatment of choice for chordoma. However, local recurrence is frequent because complete surgical removal of the tumor is difficult. Given their tendency for local recurrence despite aggressive measures stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS) has become a standard management option for recurrent or residual chordomas and chondrosarcomas after failed surgical resection and fractionated radiation therapy

Gamma knife and cyberknife are two forms of stereotactic radiosurgery. While gamma knife can’t be used at extra cranial sites cyberknife is used to treat sacral chordomas and chondrosarcomas.

Answer: 3
