## Question

PASTA lesion of the shoulder occurs on the( Arthroscopy- shoulder Arthroscopy- rotator cuff)

## Options

1. Bursal side
2. Articular side
3. Bursal side is more common than articular side
4. Both side but articular side is more common

## Correct option

**2. Articular side**

## Answer explanation

The partial articular supraspinatus tendon avulsion (PASTA) lesion is a type of partial thickness rotator cuff tear and is far more common than bursal-sided or intratendinous partial tears.

Answer: 2
