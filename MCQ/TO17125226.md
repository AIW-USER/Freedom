Chapter: General Orthopedics MCQs — Material Properties And Orthopedic Implants

## Question

On a stress- strain curve

## Options

1. Young’s modulus is stress proportional to strain(linear elasticity)
2. Strain is X axis
3. Strain is Y axis
4. Stress is X axis

## Correct option

**2. Strain is X axis**

## Answer explanation

Young’s modulus of elasticity – stress/strain (N/m2 or Pascal )

Hooke’s law: Stress is proportional to strain (linear elasticity) within elastic limits only

Ans: B
