Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — PCL

## Question

MRI is the best investigation for all except? (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Cartilage injury
2. ACL tear
3. Meniscal tear
4. PCL tear

## Correct option

**4. PCL tear**

## Answer explanation

Although MRI is an investigation of choice for all options. However, diagnosis of PCL tear is mainly clinical based on the posterior drawer test.

Answer: 4
