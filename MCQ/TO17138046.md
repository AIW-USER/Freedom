## Question

Which cranial nerve is supplied by ECA

## Options

1. II
2. V
3. VI
4. VII

## Correct option

**4. VII**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D

The external carotid artery is the artery of head and neck. It supplies facial nerve (CN VII).
