Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Calcaneus Fractures

## Question

Which of the following is not an aim in the management of calcaneus fracture?

## Options

1. Correct articular incongruity (posterior facet)
2. Restore shape- widening/shortening/loss of height
3. Correct talar declination angle
4. Correct hindfoot varus

## Correct option

**3. Correct talar declination angle**

## Answer explanation

Correcting talar declination angle is important for talar neck fracture.

Answer: 3
