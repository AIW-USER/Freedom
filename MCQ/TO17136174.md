Chapter: Sports Medicine MCQs — Drugs And Dopping In Sports

## Question

Recently Indian women's discus thrower and a medallist from the 2018 Gold Coast Commonwealth Games, Navjeet Dhillon, has been banned for three years by the Athletics Integrity Unit (AIU) for doping. It has been reported that Dhillon maintained that she consumed the substance unknowingly as part of a supplement she was using. What was the banned substance?

## Options

1. dehydrochloromethyltestosterone (dhmct)
2. androstenedione
3. epitestosterone
4. hydrochlorothiazide

## Correct option

**1. dehydrochloromethyltestosterone (dhmct)**

## Answer explanation

She consumed DHMCT which comes under S1 Class of Anabolic Agents.

All prohibited substances in this class are non-Specified Substances.

Anabolic agents are prohibited.

Answer: 1
