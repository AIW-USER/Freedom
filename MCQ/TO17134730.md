Chapter: Spine MCQs — Lumbar Canal Stenosis

## Question

In which position the space in the central canal is maximum in a patient of lumbar canal stenosis? (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Axial loading
2. Extension
3. Flexion
4. Side lying

## Correct option

**3. Flexion**

## Answer explanation

Posture-related pain in LCS is due to the fact that the available space in the central canal decreases in axial loading and extension and increases in axial distraction and flexion. This also explains why patients get more relief in lying on the side (allowing flexion posture) than lying flat. For the same reason patients find walking uphill easier and pushing a shopping cart gives them relief (positive shopping cart sign).

Answer: 3
