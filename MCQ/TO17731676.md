## Question

True about screw home mechanism is ( regional orthopedics- knee- kinematics)

## Options

1. During the last 30 degrees of knee extension, the tibia internally rotates to about 10 degrees, when the foot is in the air (open chain)
2. When the foot is planted on the ground (close chain), during the last 30 degrees of knee extension, the tibia internally rotates to about 10 degrees.
3. Screw home mechanism is brought by cruciate ligaments
4. The tibia must internally rotate slightly to allow for knee flexion in an open chain.

## Correct option

**4. The tibia must internally rotate slightly to allow for knee flexion in an open chain.**

## Answer explanation

Screw home mechanism: The knees have a "Screw Home" rotation that allows for full knee extension and flexion.

When the foot is in the air (open chain): During the last 30 degrees of knee extension, the tibia externally rotates to about 10 degrees.

When the foot is planted on the ground (close chain): During the last 30 degrees of knee extension, the femur internally rotates to about 10 degrees.

In order to unlock the knee from extension, the popliteus muscle must work to initiate internal or external rotation. The tibia must internally rotate slightly to allow for knee flexion in an open chain, and the femur must externally rotate for knee flexion in a closed chain.

This is called screw home mechanism. It is due to the specific bony anatomy of the knee (condyles are unequal) and relative differences in lengths of the two cruciate ligaments.

Answer : D
