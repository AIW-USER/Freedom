Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Femoral Neck Fractures

## Question

Best hip position for the diagnosis of the impacted fracture of neck of the femur is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Abduction and internal rotation
2. Abduction and external rotation
3. Flexion and abduction
4. Neutral rotation

## Correct option

**1. Abduction and internal rotation**

## Answer explanation

In AP view of the both hip joints, either both patellae should be facing forward or lower extremities should be internally rotated by 15°-20° to accommodate femoral anteversion.

Answer: 1
