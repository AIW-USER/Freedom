Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Humerus Shaft Fractures

## Question

What is the maximum deformity that is acceptable for the fracture shaft of the humerus after reduction in adults? (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Shortening < 3cm, rotation <30 degrees, angulation <20 degree
2. Shortening < 2cm, rotation <20 degrees, angulation <20 degree
3. Shortening < 3cm, rotation <20 degrees, angulation <30 degree
4. Shortening < 2cm, rotation <30 degrees, angulation <20 degree

## Correct option

**1. Shortening < 3cm, rotation <30 degrees, angulation <20 degree**

## Answer explanation

Adequate reduction of fracture shaft of humerus in adults defined as

Shortening < 3cm, rotation <30 degrees, angulation <20 degree

Also know: acceptable reduction for fracture shaft of humerus in pediatric age group

20° to 30° of varus, 20° of sagittal plane angulation (apex anterior/procurvatum), 15° of internal rotation, and up to 2 cm of shortening is acceptable.

Answer: 1
