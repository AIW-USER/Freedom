## Question

False about blood supply of tendo Achilles is( regional orthopedics-foot and ankle- tendoachilles injury)

## Options

1. The posterior tibial artery providing the major contribution
2. Some contribution comes from peroneal artery
3. The most abundant blood supply zone in the tendon is the mid section between muscle-tendon junction and the bone-tendon junction
4. The area of the tendon approximately 2 to 6 cm above the insertion into the calcaneus is the least vascularized zone

## Correct option

**3. The most abundant blood supply zone in the tendon is the mid section between muscle-tendon junction and the bone-tendon junction**

## Answer explanation

Blood supply to the tendo achilles is from three areas: the musculotendinous and osteotendinous junctions and the paratenon (the most important blood supply to the tendon), with the posterior tibial artery providing the major contribution. Peroneal artery also gives little contribution to the blood supply of the mid section between muscle-tendon junction and the bone-tendon junction. The most abundant blood supply zone in the tendon is at the tendon insertion. The area of the tendon approximately 2 to 6 cm above the insertion into the calcaneus is the least vascularized zone at all ages , resulting in limited reparative ability at times of stress or injury.

Answer: 3
