## Question

Which of the following is the commonest post operative complication of knee arthroplasty?

## Options

1. Haemarthrosis
2. Infection
3. Compartment syndrome
4. Tourniquet palsy

## Correct option

**1. Haemarthrosis**

## Answer explanation

Out of the above the commonest postoperative complication after TKR is Haemarthrosis occurring particularly in cases where a lateral retinacular release or liberal synovectomy has been done. It occurs in a little over 1% of cases.

Answer: 1
