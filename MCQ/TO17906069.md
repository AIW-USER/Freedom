## Question

Worst prognosis for a case of congenital scoliosis is when patient has

## Options

1. Hemivertebra
2. Block vertebra
3. Hemivertebra with contra lateral bar
4. Segmented hemivertebra

## Correct option

**3. Hemivertebra with contra lateral bar**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

In context of congenital scoliosis: -

- A fully segmented hemivertebra is most common anomaly (38%)
- A fully segmented hemivertebra with contralateral bar (deformity progresses on average at 6 degree per year) causes most severe deformity followed by a unilateral bar followed by fully segmented hemivertebra
- Progression of deformity is minimum with block vertebra
