Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Carpal Instability

## Question

A patient with carpal instability adaptive (CIA) due to a shortened radius (e.g., following an old fracture) develops ulnocarpal impaction. What is the fundamental difference between this and CID?

## Options

1. CIA cannot be treated surgically
2. The CID patient has normal radial length
3. In CIA, the intrinsic interosseous ligaments are typically intact
4. CID only occurs on the radial side

## Correct option

**3. In CIA, the intrinsic interosseous ligaments are typically intact**

## Answer explanation

The 'instability' in CIA is a secondary
response to extrinsic geometry, while carpal instability dissociative (CID) is
a primary failure of the intrinsic row ligaments.

Answer: 3
