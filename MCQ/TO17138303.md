## Question

What is the most common complication with growth modulation in Adolescent blount?

## Options

1. Recurrence
2. Implant failure
3. Complete physeal arrest
4. Failure of correction

## Correct option

**2. Implant failure**

## Answer explanation

Recent reports have suggested screw breakage as one of the complication to growth modulation using 8 plated in adolescent blount disease.

Recurrence is common in infantile blounts and after growth modulation in Genu valgum. Failure of correction may be seen in cases with severe physeal affections which are rare in adolescent blounts. Complete physeal arrest is not known after growth modulation.

Answer : b
