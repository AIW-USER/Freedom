Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Patella Fracture

## Question

A truck driver presents with minimally displaced fracture patella after dashboard injury. Which factor is most important in deciding the need for surgery? (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. More than 2mm displacement
2. Ability to extend the knee
3. Age
4. Ability to bear weight

## Correct option

**2. Ability to extend the knee**

## Answer explanation

Nonoperative management is indicated for minimally displaced fractures (less than 2 to 3 mm of step-off and less than 1 to 4 mm fracture gap) with the extensor mechanism in continuity. In minimally displaced fracture patella ability to extend knee fully indicates intact retinaculum and extensor mechanism except patella fracture. This can be managed with a cylindrical cast or long knee brace. If a patient with minimally displaced patella has extension lag it suggests disruption of extensor mechanism and warrants for surgery.

Answer: 2
