## Question

Functional position of wrist and hand is (trauma- fractures around wrist and hand- phalanx fractures)

## Options

1. Wrist in neutral, MCP 70 degree flexion, fingers full extension
2. Wrist in 20 degree flexion, MCP 70 flexion, fingers full extension
3. Wrist in 20 degree extension , MCP 70 degree flexion, finger 10 degree flexion
4. Wrist 20 flexion, MCP 20 degree flexion, finger full extension

## Correct option

**3. Wrist in 20 degree extension , MCP 70 degree flexion, finger 10 degree flexion**

## Answer explanation

Position of safe immobilization (POSI)

Wrist in 10-45 degree of extension, MCP joint in 60-90 degree flexion, fingers in slight flexion to full extension, thumb in 45 degree of abduction.

Answer : c
