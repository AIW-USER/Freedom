## Question

For Muscle bulking and cutting, approximate time (weeks before competition) would be

## Options

1. Gain 4 weeks before, cutting after competition
2. Gain 8 weeks before, cut 4 weeks after competition
3. Gain 12 weeks before, cut 4 weeks after competition
4. Gain 12 weeks before and continue cutting after competition

## Correct option

**3. Gain 12 weeks before, cut 4 weeks after competition**

## Answer explanation

answer-c

Bulking is about adding weight and muscle mass. Cutting is about trimming body fat while maintaining muscle mass. Best way to gain a bulk of muscle is to take high carbs and protein diet to topped up muscle glycogen levels. While cut down is " Calorie restriction" ( not losing muscle mass). This difference in their body energy's balance leads to mutual incompatibility between two.

It is not physiologically reconcilable to do muscle bulking and cutting at the same time. Usually the Bulking Cutting Ratio is 3:1. Ideal time for bulking is usually 8-12 weeks. So if period of bulking is 12 weeks cutting would be 4 weeks.
