## Question

Which is not a component of blount’s disease [Pediatric Ortho-Blount’s Disease]

## Options

1. Varus angulation of proximal tibial metaphysis
2. Procurvatum with apex anteriorly
3. Internal rotation
4. External rotation

## Correct option

**4. External rotation**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: blount’s disease is multiplanar tibial deformity with sharp varus angulation of the proximal medial metaphysis, procurvatum (apex anteriorly) and internal rotation. External rotation is not seen as a component

Answer: 4
