## Question

The muscle responsible for internal rotation of the humerus and inserting on the medial lip of the intertubercular sulcus is innervated by what peripheral nerve labeled in Figure A?

![[q210271-a-bp.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. A
2. B
3. C
4. D
5. E

## Correct option

**4. D**

## Answer explanation

Letter D corresponds to the lower subscapular nerve of the posterior cord of the brachial plexus. The lower subscapular nerve innervates the teres major muscle.

The lower subscapular nerve arises from the posterior cord of the brachial plexus, along with the upper subscapular nerve and the thoracodorsal nerve. The lower subscapular nerve receives contributions from C5 and C6 and also innervates the lower part of the subscapularis muscle. Teres major receives innervation from the thoracodorsal nerve in 20% of cases. An understanding of the neurovascular anatomy of this muscle is critical for performing safe tendon transfers, sometimes utilized in the treatment of massive rotator cuff tears.

*Dancker et al. reported on the neuromuscular anatomy of the teres major muscle.* *They examined 30 upper extremities in 15 cadavers.* **They concluded** that the lower subscapular nerve innervated the teres major in 86.6% of shoulders, whereas the thoracodorsal nerve innervated the remaining shoulders; the neurovascular pedicle was found in the center of the muscle belly.

*Pearle et al. provided a review of surgical techniques and an anatomic overview of latissimus dorsi and teres major muscle transfers.* *They dissected out these muscles, along with the posterior cord of the brachial plexus, in 12 cadavers.* *They quantified and described the anatomic relationship of neurovascular structures in an effort to make these transfers safer.*

Figure A and Illustration A are unlabeled and labeled drawings of the brachial plexus, respectively. Illustration B demonstrates the muscle insertions about the proximal humerus.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Letter A corresponds to the nerve to subclavius
Answer 2: Letter B corresponds to the long thoracic nerve
Answer 3: Letter C corresponds to the upper subscapular nerve
Answer 5: Letter E corresponds to the medial cutaneous nerve of the arm
