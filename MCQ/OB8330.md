## Question

A study is proposed in which 2 groups of patients are randomized to treatment with bisphosphonates or placebo. This is an example of what study type?

## Options

1. Crossover design trial
2. Parallel design trial
3. Cohort study
4. Case series

## Correct option

**2. Parallel design trial**

## Answer explanation

A study in which 2 groups of patients are randomized to treatment with bisphosphonates or placebo would be an example of a parallel design trial.

In a parallel design trial is a form of randomized clinical trial where participants are randomized to 2 or more groups, each of which receives a different treatment or intervention (most common form of RCT). For example, Group A receives the drug and Group B receives the placebo. This type of design allows for comparison between groups.

Incorrect Answers:

Answer 1: In a crossover design clinical trial, both groups receive both interventions over a defined time period. For example, Groups A and B both receive the drug as well as the placebo. This allows for within-participant comparisons.

Answer 3: In a cohort study, patient groups are followed over time on the basis of having or not having received an exposure. Cohort studies are not randomized.

Answer 4: In a case series, patients often receive a particular treatment and the outcomes are then examined.
