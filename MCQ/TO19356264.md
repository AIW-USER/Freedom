## Question

An athlete complains of anterolateral leg pain and paresthesia every time after running. He gets relief after rest. Which of the following parameters points towards chronic exertional compartment syndrome? [FNB 2023]

## Options

1. Pre exercise CP 18 mm
2. Pressure at 1 minute post exercise 58 mm
3. Pressure at 5 minute post exercise 33mm
4. All the above

## Correct option

**4. All the above**

## Answer explanation

Answer : D

Chronic exertional compartment syndrome: Chronic exertional compartment syndrome (CECS) is an exercise or activity induced compartment syndrome that causes cramping pain in the involved limb. Anterior and lateral compartments of legs are most commonly involved. Patient who usually is a long distance runner or a military recruit complains of moderate to severe leg pain after some time of starting exercises. Pain relives after rest. Exact pathophysiology is not clear but increased muscle mass during exercises in a non compliant close compartment may give rise to this condition. Diagnosis is confirmed by measuring intracompartmental pressure in the leg.
Diagnostic criteria for chronic exertional compartment syndrome (CECS)
 • Pre-existing resting compartment pressure > 15 mm Hg
 • Compartment pressure > 30 mm Hg × 1 minute after exercise
• Compartment pressure > 20 mm Hg × 5 minutes after exercise
Treatment is initially conservative with rest, ice and stretching exercises of the involved compartment. Fasciotomy is required in non responders.
