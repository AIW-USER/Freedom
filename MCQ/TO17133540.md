## Question

A 58-year-old male who sustained traumatic fractures to the upper cervical spine following a motor vehicle accident. After a detailed evaluation of the fracture characteristics and imaging studies, you recommend posterior instrumentation and fusion from the occiput to C2. You show a similar X-ray of another patient to him (given below). The patient asks you how this would affect his range of motion. You should inform him that he would probably experience which one of the following? (NEW)

![[TO17133540-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. A decrease in cervical spine rotation of 50%.
2. A decrease in cervical spine flexion-extension of 50%.
3. Decrease of cervical spine rotation of 50% and a decrease of cervical spine flexion-extension of 50%.
4. Decrease of cervical spine rotation of 50% and a decrease of cervical spine flexion-extension of 10%.

## Correct option

**3. Decrease of cervical spine rotation of 50% and a decrease of cervical spine flexion-extension of 50%.**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

Explanation: -

• The Occipito-axial joint (occiput-C1) provides about 50% of cervical spine flexion-extension, the patient can anticipate the loss of this plane of motion.

• The atlantoaxial joint (C1-C2) provides about 50% of cervical spine rotation, the patient can anticipate the loss of this plane of motion.

• The Occipito-axial joint (occiput-C1) provides about 50% of cervical spine flexion-extension, and the atlantoaxial joint (C1- C2) provides about 50% of cervical spine rotation. The patient can anticipate a loss of half the motion in both of these planes upon fusion from the occiput to C2.
