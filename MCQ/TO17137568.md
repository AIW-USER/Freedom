## Question

Which is not a predictor of poor outcome after hip fracture? (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Intracapsular fracture
2. High ASA score
3. Cognitive impairment
4. Non-weight bearing status

## Correct option

**1. Intracapsular fracture**

## Answer explanation

Predictors of functional outcomes after hip fracture:

Factors
Outcome

Age
Poor outcomes with older age

Place of residence
poor outcomes for those who are not living at their own home

Socioeconomic status
Poor outcomes with poverty

Marital status
Poor outcome with no marriage

Pre-fracture functional status
 Poor outcome with Low pre-fracture functional status

Cognitive impairment
Poor outcomes with cognitive impairment

Presence of comorbidities
Poor outcomes with presence of comorbidities

American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA)
Poor outcomes with higher ASA scores

Hand grip strength
Poor outcomes with low grip strength

Body Mass Index (BMI)
Outcomes not related to high BMI

Depression
Poor outcomes with depression

Serum albumin and folic acid level
Poor outcomes with low serum albumin or folic level

Visual impairment
Poor outcomes with visual impairment

Fracture type
Poor outcomes with extracapsular fracture types

Delay to Surgery
Poor outcomes with delay to surgery > 48 h

Weight-bearing status
Poor outcomes with non-weight bear status post-op

Process of care
Poor outcomes with poor process of care

Length of hospital stay
Poor outcomes with longer length of stay

Answer: 1
