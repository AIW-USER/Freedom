## Question

True regarding tibial retro screw is all except (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. Used in ACL reconstruction
2. Less tunnel widening is a potential advantage
3. Proximal to distal insertion
4. Tunnel widening is more compared to antegrade screw

## Correct option

**4. Tunnel widening is more compared to antegrade screw**

## Answer explanation

Tibial retro screw is a type of ACL fixation screw which is inserted in retrograde fashion from proximal to distal in tibia. It is claimed to have less tibial tunnel dilatation as the screw head blocks the tunnel mouth and prevents synovial fluid inflow into the tunnel.

Answer: 4
