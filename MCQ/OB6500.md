## Question

For patients with ankle arthritis and angular deformity, which of the following procedures provides the greatest change in forces at the ankle?

## Options

1. Deltoid ligament reconstruction
2. Lateral ankle ligament reconstruction
3. Calcaneal osteotomy
4. Tibial osteotomy
5. Peroneus longus to peroneus brevis tendon transfer

## Correct option

**4. Tibial osteotomy**

## Answer explanation

The procedure that is able to create the largest change in the forces across the ankle joint is the tibial osteotomy. The other procedures are important adjunctive procedures for given deformities, but alone are not as powerful. Whether a tibial osteotomy or other procedure(s) are used for treatment of ankle arthritis depends on the severity of the arthritis as well as the magnitude and location of the deformity.
