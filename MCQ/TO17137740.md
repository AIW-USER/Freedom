Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Pediatric Hip Fractures

## Question

Which types of pediatric hip fracture has the maximum chance of AVN? (trauma- fracture/dislocations around hip joint- pediatric hip fractures)[FNB 2016]

## Options

1. Type a
2. Type b
3. Type c
4. Type d

## Correct option

**1. Type a**

## Answer explanation

Delbet classification of pediatric hip fractures

Type I: Trans-physeal (epiphyseal separation). These are fractures through the proximal femoral physis, and represent Salter-Harris type I fractures of the proximal femur (<10%). Subtypes are IA (without dislocation) and IB (with dislocation).

Type II: Transcervical fracture. This is the most common type of pediatric hip fracture (40%–50%). It extends through the mid-portion of the femoral neck.

Type III: Cervicotrochanteric fracture. This fracture occurs through the base of the femoral neck (25%–35%).

Type IV: Intertrochanteric fracture. This fracture between the greater and lesser trochanters accounts for 6%–15% of all pediatric hip fractures and has the best outcome.

Chances of AVN are maximum in the transphyseal, then in transcervical, and cervicotrochanteric fractures.
