## Question

A 63-year-old male is seen in the clinic for left hip arthritis. He has undergone physical therapy and injections but remains symptomatic and he is subsequently scheduled for total hip arthroplasty. A Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index score is obtained during the pre-surgical visit regarding his self-reported symptoms. What is the benefit of patient-reported outcome measures compared to physician-reported outcome measures?

## Options

1. More cost-effective care
2. Better assessment of patient satisfaction
3. Improved estimate of ambulating distance post operatively
4. Determination of discharge home versus rehab post-operatively
5. Estimation of morphine equivalent requirements for post-operative analgesia

## Correct option

**2. Better assessment of patient satisfaction**

## Answer explanation

The use of patient-reported outcome measures is associated with a better assessment of patient satisfaction.

Patient-reported outcome measures provide a means to assess treatment interventions and the relationship with patient satisfaction. Physician-reported outcomes, such as radiographic outcomes, do have importance in determining the effectiveness of a treatment modality. However, outcomes measures that correlate with patient quality of life are most useful when used to effectiveness of treatment modalities.

*Schnadig et al. retrospectively studied patient and physician differences in patient performance assessment during the treatment of advanced colorectal and lung cancer.* **The authors stated** physicians were more likely to rate patients being better than the patients themselves. **The authors concluded** that patient-physician assessment differences were associated with mortality and highlights the need to avoid physician-only functional assessments.

*Mokkink et al. performed a four-round Delphi study to develop a consensus-based checklist for assessing the quality of studies reporting outcome measures.* **The authors concluded** the COnsensus-based Standards for the selection of health status Measurement INstruments (COSMIN) checklist can be useful for selecting measurement tools, peer-reviewing a manuscript, or designing a study.

*Gagnier reviewed patient-reported outcome measures in orthopedic research.* **The authors stated** that patient-reported outcome measures can provide means to assess, screen, diagnose, and prognosticate treatments based on patient-centered care.

Incorrect answers
Answers 1, 3, 4, and 5: The use of patient-reported outcome measures has been associated with the improved assessment of patient satisfaction. It has not been associated with cost-effective care, improved estimate of ambulation post-op, post-operative disposition, and analgesia requirements.
