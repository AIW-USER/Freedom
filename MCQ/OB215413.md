## Question

Compared with a soft tissue sarcoma with a chromosomal translocation of t(12,16), a soft tissue sarcoma with a chromosomal translocation of t(X,18) has a lower rate of what?

## Options

1. Bony metastases
2. Local recurrence following radiation and wide resection
3. Presence within the foot
4. Regional lymph node metastases
5. SYT-SSX fusion transcript production

## Correct option

**1. Bony metastases**

## Answer explanation

Compared with myxoid liposarcoma t(12,16), synovial sarcoma t(X,18) has a lower rate of bony metastases.

Synovial sarcoma is a malignant soft tissue sarcoma which arises near joints, but rarely within the joint, and is the most common sarcoma found in young adults (15-40 years). Metastasis may develop in 30-60% of patients and, like other sarcomas, the lung is the most common site of metastasis. Synovial sarcoma is one of the rare soft tissue sarcomas which can metastasize to lymph nodes. Conversely, liposarcoma's metastasis risk correlates with grade/sub-type of the liposarcoma, with low grade (well-differentiated) having a metastasis rate of < 1%, intermediate grade (myxoid) having a metastasis rate of 10-30%, and high grade having a metastasis rate of > 50%.

*Moreau et al. retrospectively reviewed oncologic outcomes for the management of primary myxoid (MLS) and round cell liposarcoma (RCLS).* **They report** an overall 10-year local control rate of 93% following surgical resection and radiotherapy with no differences between MLS and RCLS. **They noted** that radiotherapy was significant in preventing local relapse and reducing tumor diameter, while improving microscopic margin status, but did not impact survival. **They concluded** that MLS and RCLS showed different metastatic risk but equally good local control, and that radiotherapy was effective in preventing local recurrence and should be delivered as neoadjuvant.

*Scully et al. reviewed 14 patients with synovial sarcoma of the foot and ankle.* **They found** that patients tended to be younger than patients with other soft tissue sarcomas (30 years) and had a median duration of symptoms of 14 months. **They noted** that all patients who had metastasis develop died of their disease and that tumor size was not observed to be a prognostic variable. **They concluded** that patients with a prolonged duration of symptoms before diagnosis had a better outcome, presumably because these tumors were less aggressive biologically.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: Synovial sarcoma has a higher local recurrence rate following radiation and wide resection compared with myxoid liposarcoma
Answer 3: Synovial sarcoma is the most common malignant soft tissue sarcoma of the foot
Answer 4: Synovial sarcoma has a higher rate of regional lymph node metastases compared with myxoid liposarcoma
Answer 5: Chromosomal translocation t(X;18) is observed in more than 90% of synovial sarcoma cases which forms the SYT-SSX1, 2, or 4 fusion protein
