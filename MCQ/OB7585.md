## Question

Acral bone metastases (to the hands and feet) are most likely the result of a primary solid organ tumor in which of the followings structures?

## Options

1. Liver
2. Lung
3. Breast
4. Thyroid
5. Prostate

## Correct option

**2. Lung**

## Answer explanation

Lung cancer is the most likely solid organ cancer to metastasize distal to the elbow and the knees. The most common sites for metastatic disease are the vertebral bodies, pelvis, and long bones. Genitourinary tumors may also metastasize to distal sites.
