## Question

Surgeon removes the compression screws from failed surgery for fracture neck femur and converts it to the THR. What is recommended to prevent stress risers from screw holes? (FNB Pattern Paper 2020)

## Options

1. Use long cemented femoral stem bypassing the last screw hole by 2 cortical diameters
2. Plug the holes with bone graft and use long cemented stem
3. Uncemented femur stem + locking plate fixation
4. Long uncemented stem and additional cerclage

## Correct option

**4. Long uncemented stem and additional cerclage**

## Answer explanation

During conversion of failed proximal hip fracture surgery to THR, Long-stem implants are inserted to bypass the most distal screw hole by two cortical diameters, combined with or without a prophylactic cable to avoid creation of a stress riser.

In above case, patient is expected to be young active as he had fracture neck femur. So uncemented stem will be preferred. Cemented stem will have issue of cement leaking from screw holes.

Answer: 4
