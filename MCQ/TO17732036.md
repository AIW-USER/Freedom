## Question

Which structure is at maximum risk in inside-out lateral meniscal repair( Arthroscopy- knee Arthroscopy- meniscus injury)

## Options

1. Popliteus and
2. Peroneal nerve
3. Lateral inferior genicular artery
4. Saphenous nerve

## Correct option

**2. Peroneal nerve**

## Answer explanation

Inside-out repair of meniscal tears:

 Indications: Posterior horn tears, middle-third tears, peripheral capsule tears, bucket-handle tears

Contraindications: Root tears, severe degenerative meniscal changes, avascular zone tears

Structures at risk in inside-out repair:

Lateral meniscal posterior horn tear repair: Peroneal nerve, popliteus and lateral inferior genicular artery. To avoid peroneal nerve injury, keep the posterolateral incision anterior to the biceps and lateral head of the gastrocnemius tendons.

Medial meniscus posterior horn repair: Saphenous nerve (avoid placement of the medial incision too posteriorly)

Answer: 2
