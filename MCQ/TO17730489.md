## Question

Acid phosphatase enzyme is contained in (general orthopedics- anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. Osteoblast
2. Osteoclast
3. Osteocytes
4. Lamellae of cancellous bone.

## Correct option

**2. Osteoclast**

## Answer explanation

Osteoclasts are well known for containing a large amount of TRAP (tartrate resistant acid phosphatase) activity

Answer: 2
