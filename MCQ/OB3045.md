## Question

Which nerve innervates the rectus femoris muscle?

## Options

1. Tibial
2. Peroneal
3. Obturator
4. Femoral
5. Sartorial

## Correct option

**4. Femoral**

## Answer explanation

The rectus femoris muscle is innervated by the femoral nerve.
