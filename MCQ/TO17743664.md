## Question

Wall test is useful in ( arthritis- ankylosing spondylitis)

## Options

1. Lumbar spine involvement in AS
2. Cervical spine involvement in RA
3. Cervical spine involvement in AS
4. SI joint involvement in AS

## Correct option

**3. Cervical spine involvement in AS**

## Answer explanation

In severe cases of AS, the cervical spine involvement may also be present. It can be detected by the Fleche wall test. The patient is asked to stand against a wall and to simultaneously touch the heels, the back and the back of head against the wall. If the patient is unable to touch the back of head against the wall, it indicates cervical spine involvement.

Answer: 3
