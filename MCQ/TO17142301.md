Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Tendoachilles Injury

## Question

Incorrect statement for TA rupture is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Thomas test is a reliable test
2. Antithrobolytics have a role
3. Haglund deformity is associated with increased incidence of TA rupture
4. Incidence of re-rupture is more in conservatively managed cases

## Correct option

**2. Antithrobolytics have a role**

## Answer explanation

Thomas test for TA rupture: Patient is placed in a prone position with both feet hanging at the edge of the couch. Examiner now squeezes the musculature of the posterior calf. A positive Thompson test reveals little or no plantar flexion of the foot relative to the contralateral leg.

Risk factors for TA rupture: steroid therapy(both local and systemic), hypercholesterolemia, gout, rheumatoid arthritis, long-term dialysis, and renal transplantation, Chronic tendinopathy with Haglund’s disease, Fluoroquinolones, Malalignment of foot and/or ankle, Training errors, Degenerative changes in tendon, etc.

Also know:

Simmonds or Thomas’ test is a reliable diagnostic tool with a sensitivity of between 0.89-0.93

Most commonly involved patients are males in 30 to 39 age group

The commonest site of rupture is in a region 3 to 6 cm above the os calcis which corresponds to a watershed region of poor vascularization

Answer: 2
