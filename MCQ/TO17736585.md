## Question

Which is not a feature of Klippel –Feil syndrome (Spine- klippel-Feil syndrome)

## Options

1. Short webbed neck
2. Restricted neck movements
3. Fused cervical vertebrae
4. B/L sternocleidomastoid contractures

## Correct option

**4. B/L sternocleidomastoid contractures**

## Answer explanation

Klippel feil syndrome: This is characterized by congenital fusion of cervical spine due to failure of segmentation.

Clinical Features: Classical triad consists of short-webbed neck, low posterior hairline and restriction of neck movements. Segment of spine near the fused part becomes hypermobile, causing degenerative changes, which may lead to radiculopathy or myelopathy in young adult life. Torticollis is present. Scoliosis (congenital or idiopathic) occurs in 60% of patients. Sprengel deformity of the shoulder accompanies the syndrome in 50% cases. Other associated conditions include Small cord size, absent ribs, cleft palate, skeletal abnormalities of ear, nose, mouth and larynx, digital hypoplasia, webbing of digits, genitourinary abnormalities (structural abnormalities like double collecting system, horseshoe-shaped kidney, renal aplasia, etc.), congenital heart defects, hearing loss, synkinesis, ocular anomalies and cervical canal stenosis.

Answer: 4
