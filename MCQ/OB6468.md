## Question

When evaluating a CT scan of a calcaneus fracture, the constant fragment shown in Figure 35 represents the

![[q6468-a-35.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. sustentaculum tali.
2. tuberosity.
3. anterior process.
4. lateral wall.
5. posterior facet.

## Correct option

**1. sustentaculum tali.**

## Answer explanation

The constant fragment refers to the sustentaculum tali fragment that stays attached to the talus through ligamentous attachments. It is the nondisplaced fragment in relation to the rest of the calcaneus. During surgical reduction, the lateral articular fragment(s) and the tuberosity are reduced and fixed to this constant fragment.
