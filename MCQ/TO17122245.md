## Question

In Achondroplasia, what region of the physis is affected and what is defective?

## Options

1. Zone of hypertrophy, FGF,3
2. Zone of proliferation, FGFR 3
3. Zone of hypertrophy, FGFR 3
4. Zone of proliferation, COMP

## Correct option

**2. Zone of proliferation, FGFR 3**

## Answer explanation

Achondroplasia is an autosomal dominant condition that results from sporadic mutations in FGFR3 on chromosome 4 leading to a gain of function of FGFR3 (turned on). This causes increased tyrosine kinase activity in the proliferative zone during enchondral bone formation at the physis, resulting in the inhibition of chondrocyte proliferation and differentiation.

Inheritance is by sporadic mutation in majority of cases. It has an Autosomal dominant pattern of transmission

It is the most common type of dwarfism (80-90% of all dwarfism)

![[TO17122245-1.png]]

- Patients classically have rhizomelic dwarfism.
- Frontal bossing of the forehead,
- Trident hands (All finger same size. Divergence between middle and ring finger)
- Bowing of the legs, (Genu Varum)
- Thoracolumbar kyphosis and hyper lordosis are some of the characteristic clinical findings in this patient population.

FGFR 3 receptor mutation; Increased tyrosine kinase activity - Achondroplasia

Type I collagen abnormalities - Osteogenesis imperfecta,

Type II collagen abnormalities - Spondyloepiphyseal dysplasia and Kneist syndrome.

COMP is defective - Multiple epiphyseal dysplasia

Mutation in DTDST gene (SLC26A2) on chromosome 5; Sulphate transport defects - Diastrophic dysplasia.

Radiology:

The pelvis is low and broad with narrow sciatic notches and ping-pong paddle-shaped iliac wings. This is often called a champagne glass pelvis. Inverted V shaped physis, Genu varum are common features.

Orthopedic Problems:

Short height: Cosmetic lengthening

Genu Varum: May need osteotomy > Growth modulation

Thoracolumbar Kyphosis: Mostly resolves

Lumbar Stenosis: May require intervention

Foramen magnum stenosis: Sleep apnea; Clonus; Quadriparesis

Answer: 2
