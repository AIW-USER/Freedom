## Question

What is the most common type of skeletal dysplasia to present with thoracolumbar kyphosis?

## Options

1. Trevor disease
2. Multiple epiphyseal dysplasia
3. Larsen syndrome
4. Achondroplasia
5. Diastrophic dysplasia

## Correct option

**4. Achondroplasia**

## Answer explanation

Thoracolumbar kyphosis is a common spinal deformity (T12, L1 apical wedging) in Achondroplasia.

Achondroplasia is the most common skeletal dysplasia with an incidence of 1 per 30,000 births. It is inherited as an autosomal dominant trait with 80% spontaneous mutation. It occurs as a result of fibroblast growth factor receptor (FGFR)-3 mutations, which results in abnormal enchondral bone formation and failure in proliferative zone of physis.

*Shirley et al. reviewed the manifestations of Achondroplasia, and states that thoracolumbar kyphosis will usually resolve in 90% of patients after walking age.* Other spinal manifestations include spinal stenosis (due to short thick pedicles, narrow interpedicular distance), scoliosis and narrow foramen magnum.

Illustration A shows a lateral spine X-Ray of a patient with achondroplasia. Note the thoracolumbar kyphosis, short thick pedicles, reduced interpedicular distance, posterior scalloping of the vertebral bodies and bullet shaped vertebrae.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Trevor disease is a rare skeletal developmental disorder of childhood. It is characterized by an overgrowth of cartilage in the epiphyses of the long bones of the arms and legs, as well as the carpal and tarsal bones.
Answer 2: Compared to Spondyloepiphyseal Dysplasia (SED), patients with Multiple Epiphyseal Dysplasia (MED) usually have a normal spine.
Answer 3: Larsen syndrome is characterized by multiple dislocated joints, including hip, knee, elbow/radial head, foot deformities, scoliosis, and potentially lethal cervical kyphosis.
Answer 5: Diastrophic dysplasia is inherited as an autosomal recessive trait. Spine deformity is most common in the cervical spine.
