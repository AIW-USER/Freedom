## Question

Which of the following neurovascular structures is closest to the posterior capsule of the knee?

## Options

1. Popliteal vein
2. Popliteal artery
3. Tibial nerve
4. Common peroneal nerve
5. Sural nerve

## Correct option

**2. Popliteal artery**

## Answer explanation

The order from superficial to deep (as well as from lateral to medial) is tibial nerve, popliteal vein, and popliteal artery. The sural nerve is subcutaneous in the posterior calf and the peroneal nerve is posterolateral.
