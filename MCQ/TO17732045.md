## Question

True about Jefferson’s fracture is ( spine- fractures of cervical spine - Fractures of ATLAS and AXIS)

## Options

1. Treatment of Landells type 2 fracture with intact transverse ligament is C1-C2 fusion
2. Greater occipital neuralgia
3. Treatment of Landells type 1 fracture is halo vest
4. High incidence of neurological injury

## Correct option

**2. Greater occipital neuralgia**

## Answer explanation

Jefferson Fracture:

This is a burst fracture of C1 vertebra (Atlas) caused by an axial loading force. There is fracture involving both anterior and posterior rings of the atlas. It has an extremely high association (up to 50%) with other cervical spine fractures. Patients will often complain of severe suboccipital discomfort and a sense of instability. At C1 level only 35% of the canal is occupied by the cord with plenty of space as opposed to 50% occupancy in the lower spine, hence, neurologic injury is uncommon.

However, when it occurs, the greater occipital nerve is most frequently injured (it emerges below the posterior ring of atlas), followed by the lower cranial nerves.

 Landells Atlas Fractures Classification and treatment:

Type
Description
Treatment

Type 1
Isolated anterior or posterior arch fracture.
Treat with a hard collar.

Type 2
Burst fracture, bilateral fractures of anterior and posterior arch resulting from axial load.
Hard collar- if transverse ligament is intact

C1-C2 fusion- if intrasubstance tear of transverse ligament

Halo vest- if transverse ligament is disrupted with bony avulsion

Type 3
Unilateral lateral mass fracture
Hard collar- if transverse ligament is intact

Halo vest- if transverse ligament is disrupted
