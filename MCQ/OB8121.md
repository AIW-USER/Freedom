## Question

Which of the following factors predisposes patients undergoing lateral ankle reconstruction to surgical failure?

## Options

1. Low functional demand
2. Anatomic reconstruction
3. Cavovarus foot deformity
4. Planovalgus foot deformity
5. History of acute instability (preoperative)

## Correct option

**3. Cavovarus foot deformity**

## Answer explanation

Bony malalignment such as hindfoot varus, first ray plantar flexion, or cavovarus foot deformity renders patients more susceptible to chronic lateral ankle sprains and can cause failure of isolated lateral ankle reconstruction. Planovalgus foot deformity principally strains the medial ankle ligamentous structures. Low functional demand patients, as well as patients with a preoperative history of acute traumatic ankle instability and patients undergoing anatomic reconstruction all have decreased rates of surgical failure with lateral ankle ligament reconstruction.
