Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Elbow Dislocation

## Question

Most common nerve injured in elbow dislocation?

## Options

1. Median nerve
2. radial nerve
3. Ulnar nerve
4. Posterior interosseous nerve

## Correct option

**3. Ulnar nerve**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Ulnar nerve is the most common nerve to be injured in elbow dislocations.

Answer: 3
