## Question

A patient underwent L4-5 surgery. Which specific neuromonitoring system will you use [FNB 2023]

## Options

1. SSEP
2. TcMEP
3. D waves
4. Triggered EMG

## Correct option

**4. Triggered EMG**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D
EXPLANATION: -
SSEO and MEP are for spinal “cord” monitoring. Most surgeries at this level involve laminectomy and pedicle screw insertion. A pedicle breach leading to nerve root injury would best be detected by Triggered EMG.
