## Question

Looser’s zone not seen in( metabolic bone disorders-miscellaneous)

## Options

1. Osteomalacia
2. Paget’s disease
3. X-linked hypophosphatemia
4. Led poisoning

## Correct option

**4. Led poisoning**

## Answer explanation

Conditions with Looser’s zones:

––Osteomalacia (characteristic)

––Renal osteodystrophy

––Fibrous dysplasia

––Hyperthyroidism

––Paget’s disease of bone

––X-linked hypophosphatemia

––Osteogenesis imperfecta

Answer: 4
