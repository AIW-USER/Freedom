## Question

To prevent pivoting, position of ACL graft in the knee joint is (arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- ACL)

## Options

1. Horizontal
2. Vertical
3. Roof top
4. 11’o o'clock

## Correct option

**1. Horizontal**

## Answer explanation

Concept of femoral tunnel placement has been changed from isometric to anatomical one. Isometric graft is a vertically oriented non-anatomic graft, which is able to control anterior tibial translation but not the rotational component of the instability so it does not prevent pivot shift. Femoral tunnel obliquity mainly determines rotational stability. To improve the obliquity of graft, an anatomical ACL reconstruction should be attempted. Anatomical insertion of ACL on the femur lies very low in the notch, at 10 'clock position. Vertical position or 11 o'clock position is unable to restore rotational stability. The 10 o' clock position results in a more oblique graft placement, avoiding problems related to PCL impingement during knee flexion.

Answer: 1
