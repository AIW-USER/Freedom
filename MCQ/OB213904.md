## Question

Type II collagen is the most abundant type of collagen in all the following EXCEPT?

## Options

1. Articular cartilage
2. Nucleus pulposus
3. Elastic cartilage
4. Hyaline cartilage
5. Blood vessels

## Correct option

**5. Blood vessels**

## Answer explanation

The most abundant type of collagen found in blood vessels is type III collagen.

Collagen, which is made up of elongated fibrils formed by fibroblast cells, is a naturally-occurring family of proteins. This triple helix structure is the most abundant type of protein in humans. Multiple forms of collagen exist, each with differing biomechanical properties suited to its environment. Type III collagen is found primarily in the skin and blood vessels. Type III collagen is abnormal in such conditions as Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome Type IV (vascular). It is also the predominant collagen contributing to the pathophysiology of Dupuytren’s disease.

*Jeffery et al. reviewed the three-dimensional architecture of bovine articular cartilage collagen.* **They report** that the middle and superficial zones consist of a fine network of predominantly type II collagen fibrils. **They conclude** that the orientation shifts from vertical to horizontal and parallel as it transitions to the superficial zone. The components of the articular cartilage matrix interact closely, and the organization of the collagen is important to function and the processes of cartilage growth, injury, and repair.

*Hope et al. reviewed the processes of tendon healing and tendon-to-bone.* **They report** that tendons and ligaments are primarily composed of type I collagen. However, following tendon and ligament injury, type III collagen production predominates during the inflammatory and early regenerative phases of healing. **They conclude** that during the later regenerative phase, type I collagen production is initiated and predominates.

Answer 3: Elastic cartilage is predominately type II collagen
