Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Wear And Osteolysis (Hip)

## Question

Osteolysis in fully porous coated stem occurs

## Options

1. Around proximal stem
2. Around distal stem
3. Along entire length
4. In patches

## Correct option

**1. Around proximal stem**

## Answer explanation

In fully coated and proximally coated stems osteolysis occurs around proximal stem. In patch coated stem osteolysis occurs distally.

Answer: 1
