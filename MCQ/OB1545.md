## Question

A 9-year-old boy presents to your clinic for evaluation of his scoliosis. He has large bilateral calves and has been in a wheelchair since the age of 7. He has a forced vital capacity (FVC) of 35% of predicted for his height and weight. His thoracic curve from T4 to T10 measures 30 degrees with his lumbar curve measuring 35 degrees from T12 to L5. He has been treated with prednisone since the age of 7 with his lumbar curve measuring 25 degrees 8 months prior. Which of the following would be an indication for surgical management in this particular patient?

## Options

1. FVC of 35%
2. Non-ambulatory patient
3. Lumbar curve > 30 degrees
4. Answers 2 and 3
5. Answers 1, 2 and 3

## Correct option

**5. Answers 1, 2 and 3**

## Answer explanation

The patient has Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD) with decreased FVC, is non-ambulatory and has a progressively growing curve. Early arthrodesis of scoliosis is recommended for patients with FVC <= 35%, and those with curves > 20-30 degrees who are non-ambulatory.

Posterior spinal fusion is the treatment of choice for scoliosis in patients with DMD once they are no longer able to walk. This treatment improves the quality of life and upright wheelchair positioning. Its effect on pulmonary function is less clear, as the pulmonary function will continue to decline because of the underlying muscle disease. While bracing and wheelchair modifications may slow the progression of the curve, progression will continue. Surgical intervention at this stage does not have to include the pelvis, which, in general, is indicated in curves of greater than 40 degrees, and when pelvic obliquity is greater than 10 degrees. Surgical intervention is now recommended when FVC drops to 35% since surgery has shown to provide some benefit to pulmonary function. Some surgeons will consider FVC < 10% to be a contraindication to surgery.

*Mubarak et al. performed a review of 22 wheel-chair bound patients with DMD who underwent instrumentation and fusion.* **They noted** that 12 patients were instrumented to the sacropelvis, while 10 were instrumented to L5. **The authors concluded** that the recommendation for operation in such patients should be made when their curve is > 20 degrees and if initiated early, instrumentation and fusion to the fifth lumbar vertebra should be sufficient.

*Smith et al. performed a review of 51 boys with DMD with scoliosis who had not had surgical treatment of the spine and had been followed until death.* **The authors noted** that the following variables were not useful in predicting which curves would become severe: the age at ambulation, age when they ceased walking, age at onset of spinal collapse, or age at the time of death. **The authors concluded** that when a patient's curve exceeded 35 degrees, the FVC usually was less than 40 percent of the predicted normal value. **They also concluded** that when walking becomes impossible for boys with DMD, routine spinal arthrodesis should be considered.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1-4: Surgical intervention is recommended for the DMD patient with scoliosis when FVC <= 35% and for curves > 20-30 degrees in the non-ambulatory patient.
