Chapter: Metabolic Bone Disorder MCQs — Rickets

## Question

Which is not an example of FGF23 mediated hypophosphatemic rickets

## Options

1. α-klotho translocation
2. Dent’s syndrome
3. X linked hypophosphatemic rickets
4. hereditary hypophosphatemic rickets with hypercalciurea

## Correct option

**4. hereditary hypophosphatemic rickets with hypercalciurea**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 4

![[TO21733260-1.png]]

FGF23 dependent hypophosphatemia: X-linked hypophosphatemic rickets (XLH), autosomal dominant hypophosphatemic rickets (ADHR), autosomal recessive hypophosphatemic rickets (ARHR), epidermal nevus syndrome (ENS), tumor-induced osteomalacia (TIO), Osteoglophonic dysplasia (OGD), sodium phosphate cotransporter 2a (NPT2a) mutation, sodium hydrogen exchange regulator factor 1 (NHERF1) mutation, Iron infusion induced hypophosphatemia, fibrous dysplasia, kidney transplant and hepatic resection associated hypophosphatemic rickets.
FGF23 independent hypophosphatemia: Fanconi syndrome (FS), hypophosphatemic rickets with hypercalciuria (HHRH).
