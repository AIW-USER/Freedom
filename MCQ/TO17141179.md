Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Patellar Tendinitis

## Question

True about jumper’s knee are all except (FNB Pattern Paper 2017]

## Options

1. Involves inferior pole of patella
2. Involves superior pole of patella
3. Tooth sign
4. Common in jumpers

## Correct option

**2. Involves superior pole of patella**

## Answer explanation

Jumper’s knee is the tendinitis of the knee extensor mechanism usually occurring at the tendo-osseous junction of the lower pole of patella. Tooth sign is the periosteal reaction on the anterior surface of the patella. It is seen in the jumper's knee. Treatment is largely conservative and involves eccentric quadriceps strengthening, avoidance of provoking activity, taping, deep transverse friction massage of the patellar tendon, cryotherapy etc.

Answer: 2
