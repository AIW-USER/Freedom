## Question

Snow Storm appearance is not seen in? (general orthopedics- imaging and investigations)

## Options

1. Secondaries in the lung
2. Gout
3. Synovial chondromatosis
4. Hydatidiform mole

## Correct option

**1. Secondaries in the lung**

## Answer explanation

Snowstorm sign in obstetric imaging is classically seen in complete hydatidiform mole.

Snowstorm appearance on X ray is seen in silicosis

Snowstorm appearance of synovial fluid on musculoskeletal ultrasound is seen in GOUT

Snowstorm appearance on arthroscopy is seen in synovial chondromatosis.

Answer A
