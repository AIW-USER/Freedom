## Question

Figures 20a and 20b show the AP and lateral radiographs of a 62-year-old man who has had hip pain for the past 3 weeks. Figure 20c shows a CT scan of the abdomen and pelvis. A needle biopsy specimen is shown in Figure 20d. Preoperative management should include which of the following?

*Figure D*

## Options

1. Lymphocentigraphy
2. Colonoscopy
3. Bronchoscopy
4. Embolization of the femoral lesion
5. Bone marrow aspiration

## Correct option

**4. Embolization of the femoral lesion**

## Answer explanation

The histology shows findings consistent with metastatic renal cell carcinoma. Renal cell carcinoma metastases are extremely vascular. Preoperative embolization helps minimize the amount of blood loss during curettage of these lesions.
