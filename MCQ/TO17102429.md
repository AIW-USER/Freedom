Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Wear And Osteolysis (Hip)

## Question

Which is not true about polyethylene wear in THA

## Options

1. A 22 mm head will have more linear wear than 32 mm head
2. A 32 mm head will have less volumetric wear than 22 mm head
3. Most common type of wear is adhesive wear
4. Endosteal scalloping of femoral cortex is most common finding due to osteolysis around femoral stem

## Correct option

**2. A 32 mm head will have less volumetric wear than 22 mm head**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Osteolysis in Total hip arthroplasty:

Types of polyethylene wear:

- Adhesive wear —This is the most important mechanism in the osteolysis process. Microscopically PE sticks to the prosthesis and debris gets pulled off the PE.
- Abrasive wear—A rough femoral head surface causes mechanical scratching of PE surface with loss of PE material (cheese grater effect).
- Third-body particles—Particles within the joint space get between the head and PE cup, causing abrasion. These particles cause PE to be removed from the cup surface. Third-body particle sources include Cement debris, Metal debris shed from cup or stem, Metal debris from metal corrosion at modular metal-metal interfaces (i.e., modular junctions), and Hydroxyapatite debris shed from implant surfaces.
- Linear wear is measured by the distance the prosthesis has penetrated into the linerVolumetric wear is the main determinant of the number of particles created. It is directly related to the square of the radius of the head.

Small heads (22 mm) will have higher linear wear and lower volumetric wear than large (32-mm head). Failure is more likely to result from wear through the PE cup. Large head (32 mm) Will have lower linear and higher volumetric wear than 22-mm head. Failure is more likely to result from osteolysis. Compromise head (28 mm) will have less volumetric wear than 32-mm head and Less linear wear than 22-mm head. They also have better stability than a 22-mm head. Thus, most hip systems use 28-mm head size.

X ray findings of osteolysis around THR include:

- Endosteal scalloping in the femoral endosteal canal is hallmark finding.
- Round lytic lesions behind an acetabular cup with screw holes is a common finding.
- Round lytic lesion surrounding acetabular screw is also a typical finding.

Answer: 2
