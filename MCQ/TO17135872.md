Chapter: Spine MCQs — Thoracic Disc Herniation

## Question

True regarding thoracic disc herniation is all except (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Less than 1 % of all disc herniation cases
2. Most common symptom is dorsal back ache
3. Disc herniation is common in lower thoracic vertebrae than upper thoracic vertebrae.
4. Most cases require surgical decompression.

## Correct option

**4. Most cases require surgical decompression.**

## Answer explanation

Thoracic disc bulgings/herniations are relatively uncommon, compared to lumbar and cervical disc pathologies. It is estimated that only 0.25 to 1% of all disc herniations take place in the thoracic region. Most common symptom is dorsal back ache. In thoracic spine disc bulging is more common below T7 vertebrae. Surgical decompression is required only in a few cases and indications for surgery include progressive myelopathy and stable myelopathy with radicular pain that is not improving with conservative treatment.

Answer: 4
