Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Tendon Injuries of Hand

## Question

Which structure is involved in Drummer’s palsy ( FNB Pattern Paper 2014)

## Options

1. Median nerve
2. Ulnar nerve
3. EPL tendon
4. Flexor tendon origin at medial epicondyle of elbow

## Correct option

**3. EPL tendon**

## Answer explanation

Drummer’s boy palsy is a chronic tendinitis of the extensor pollicis longus (EPL) tendon. In drummer boy, it is an overuse injury to EPL due to repetitive motion injury (repetitive palmar flexion and ulnar deviation). Tendon rubs against the dorsal tubercle of the radius and with cumulative trauma, it may get attenuated or in extreme cases rupture may occur. Treatment is usually conservative with activity modification. Transfer of extensor indicis to substitute EPL may be required in cases of rupture

Answer: 3
