Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Periprosthetic Fractures

## Question

Not a risk factor for periprosthetic fracture:

## Options

1. Anterior femoral notching
2. Osteopenia
3. Parkinson’s disease
4. Rheumatoid arthritis

## Correct option

**1. Anterior femoral notching**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 1
Causative factors for periprosthetic fractures:
Osteopenia appears to be an important predisposing factor contributing to periprosthetic fractures. Other risk factors include old age, chronic use of corticosteroids, inflammatory arthropathy including rheumatoid arthritis, local osteolysis and stress risers from screw holes, as well as significant deformity or previous surgery. Rheumatoid arthritis is a risk factor in itself although associated osteopenia and use of corticosteroids may contribute to the increased incidence. Patients with pre-existing neurologic problems like epilepsy, Parkinson’s disease, and poliomyelitis also appear to be at a higher risk of periprosthetic fractures. Anterior femoral notching is controversial. Although deep anterior notches more than 3 mm have been shown to reduce flexural and torsional strength in biomechanical studies, this does not seem to be reflected in the clinical setting. Age above 70 years and female gender have also been associated with a higher risk of periprosthetic fractures.
