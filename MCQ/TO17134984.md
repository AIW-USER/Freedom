## Question

Smallest diameter of the pedicle is seen at which level? (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. C2
2. C3
3. C4
4. C5

## Correct option

**2. C3**

## Answer explanation

Transverse pedicle angle or coronal angulation decreases as one descends caudally in the spine until the lumbar region. The angle increases as the lumbar spine is descended.

The size of the vertebrae increases from cephalad to caudad, with the cervical vertebrae being the smallest and the lumbar being the largest. The shape of the vertebrae also varies within the different regions:

- In the lumbar spine, vertebrae are longer in the sagittal plane than the coronal plane.
- The cervical vertebrae are wider in the coronal plane.
- Within the thoracic spine, the upper vertebrae are shaped akin to the cervical vertebrae, while the lower thoracic levels are more similar to lumbar vertebrae.
- The middle thoracic spine contains vertebrae that are nearly equal in width for both sagittal and coronal planes.
- The middle thoracic spine is also the area where the spinal canal is the narrowest, from approximately T4 to T9

According to the anatomic studies the cross-sectional area of the spinal column is largest at C2 averaging 374 mm. The thoracic spine has the narrowest canal, with the cross-sectional area being the smallest between T3 and T8 (ranging between 189 and 199 mm2). T4 has the narrowest pedicle diameter in the spine.

The transverse pedicle diameter is largest at L5 (14.95 mm) and smallest at C3 (5.1 mm). The pedicle is longest at L5 (19.9 mm) and shortest at T10 (15.7). The vertebral body is largest at L5 (34.9 mm) and smallest at C3 and C5 (15.6 mm). The vertebral body width is largest at L5 (46.6 mm) and smallest at C4 (22 mm). The intervertebral disc space height is largest at L2-3 (10 mm) and smallest at T1-2 (2.85 mm).

Cervical pedicles:

In the cervical spine, the C2 pedicle is largest and the C3 pedicle is smallest, with pedicle sizes increasing from C3 to C7.

Lumbosacral pedicles:

Pedicles angulate more medially as one moves distally. L1 has smallest diameter in lumbar spine (T4 has smallest diameter overall.

Thoracic pedicle anatomy

T4 has the narrowest pedicle diameter (on average). Pedicle length decreases from T1 to T4 and then increases again as you move distal in the thoracic spine
