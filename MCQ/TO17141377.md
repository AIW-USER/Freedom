Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Dupuytren’s Contracture

## Question

Which is not a bad prognostic factor for dupuytren disease

## Options

1. Epilepsy
2. Diabetes mellitus
3. Positive family history
4. Bilateral disease

## Correct option

**1. Epilepsy**

## Answer explanation

Prognostic factors in dupuytren disease

Bad prognostic factors in dupuytren disease: positive family history, male sex, diabetes mellitus, smoking, alcoholism, bilateral disease, presence of knuckle pads and nodules in the plantar fascia

Despite earlier statements positively associating Dupuytren contracture with epilepsy, Geoghegan et al. concluded that neither epilepsy nor antiepileptic medications were associated with the disease.

Answer : a
