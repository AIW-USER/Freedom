Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Osteochondroma And Multiple Hereditary Exostosis

## Question

2nd most common site for osteochondroma

## Options

1. Pelvis
2. Scapula
3. Forearm
4. Spine

## Correct option

**1. Pelvis**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Most common site is the ends of long bones. The 2nd common site is iliac crest.

Forearm osteochondroma are classified by MASADA classification.

Answer: 1
