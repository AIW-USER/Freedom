## Question

Blood flow ratio Internal carotid artery: External carotid artery is [General Ortho-Anatomy, Basic Science and pathology]

## Options

1. 60:40
2. 70:30
3. 80:20
4. 90:10

## Correct option

**3. 80:20**

## Answer explanation

Most studies describe the blood flow velocities in ICA or CCA as a determinant of

stenosis. Studies have found blood flow ration between ICA/CCA as 0.8,

meaning the flow between ICA: ECA would be 80:20.

answer: c
