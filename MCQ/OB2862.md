## Question

What is the genetic cause of the dwarfism characterized by a hitchhikers thumb, cauliflower swelling of the ears, and severe clubfeet?

## Options

1. FGFr-2
2. FGFr-3
3. Sulfate transport protein
4. CBFA-1
5. Gs Alpha Protein

## Correct option

**3. Sulfate transport protein**

## Answer explanation

Diastrophic Dysplasia is a form of dwarfism characterized by hitchhikers thumb, cauliflower swelling of the ears, and severe clubfeet. It is an autosomal recessive condition caused by a mutation in DTD gene, which leads to a defective sulfate transporter protein, and undersulfation of cartilage proteoglycan.

Associated conditions include short stature ("twisted dwarf"), cleft palate (59%), cauliflower ears (80%), hitchhikers thumb, scoliosis, poorly developed UE, cervical kyphosis, hip dislocations, and rigid clubfeet (equinocavovarus).

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Mutations in FGFr-2 results in Apert's Syndrome
Answer 2: Mutations in FGFr-3 results in Achondroplasia
Answer 4: Mutations in CBFA-1 results in Cleidocranial Dysplasia
Answer 5: Mutations in Gs Alpha Protein results in Fibrous Dysplasia.
