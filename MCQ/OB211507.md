## Question

Which of the following adjuvants to fracture healing contains osteogenic, osteoconductive, and osteoinductive properties?

## Options

1. Bone marrow aspirate (BMA)
2. Demineralized bone matrix (DBM)
3. Fibular strut allograft
4. Bone from a reamer irrigator aspirator (RIA)
5. Bone morphogenic proteins (BMP)

## Correct option

**4. Bone from a reamer irrigator aspirator (RIA)**

## Answer explanation

Bone obtained from the reamer irrigator aspirator (RIA) is autogenic and contains osteogenic, osteoinductive and osteoconductive properties.

Autogenous bone grafts remain the gold standard source for grafting procedures due to their many beneficial properties. When discussing graft choices, it’s important to understand each of these properties. Osteoconductive adjuvants are those which provide structural framework. Osteoinductive materials provide factors that stimulate bone growth and induction of stem cells down a bone-forming lineage (bone morphogenic proteins). Lastly, the osteogenic property refers to the ability of a material to directly provide cells that will produce bone including primitive mesenchymal stem cells, osteoblasts, and osteocytes. The RIA is one such method of obtaining autogenous bone graft that provides each of these properties and is considered one of the gold standards for grafting procedures.

*Sen et al. review the bone graft options available for the treatment of nonunions.* **They report** that bone autograft is the only available graft to include osteoconductive, osteoinductive, and osteogenic properties. Based on this, the group concludes that autograft remains the gold standard for the treatment of nonunions.

*Mahendra et al. review the available biological treatments for complex nonunions.* *They report on the differences between bone autograft, bone allograft, bone marrow aspirate, demineralized bone matrix, and bone morphogenic proteins.* **They conclude** that given the biologic profile, autogenous iliac crest bone graft is the gold standard for the treatment of nonunions.

*Taylor et al. review the technique for obtaining femoral bone graft via retrograde use of RIA.* **They report** that this technique provides an alternative for traditional iliac crest bone grafting as it provides the same biologic properties. **They conclude** that retrograde femoral acquisition of bone graft using the RIA should be considered in patients in which a large amount of bone graft may be needed.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: BMA is only osteogenic and osteoinductive as it provides no structural support.
Answer 2: DBM has osteoconductive and osteoinductive properties only
Answer 3: Fibular strut allograft only has osteoconductive properties.
Answer 5: BMPs are purely osteoinductive.
