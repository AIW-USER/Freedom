## Question

Altered BMP-4 signal transduction is known to be associated with which of the following diseases?

## Options

1. Fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva
2. Mazabraud's syndrome
3. Ollier's syndrome
4. Osteopetrosis
5. Osteopoikilosis

## Correct option

**1. Fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva**

## Answer explanation

Altered BMP-4 signal transduction is known to be associated with Fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva (FOP), a rare disease also called "stone man syndrome." Patients with this disease develop massive spontaneous heterotopic bone, so much so, that their bodies literally ossify. There is no known cure for this disease however surgical removal of the heterotopic bone can help with patient mobility and seating (Illustration A).

Shore and Kaplan review FOP and the genetic defect in BMP-4 signal transduction which leads to the massive formation of heterotopic bone in these patients. Their review provides an excellent discussion of the normal cell-signaling cascade driven by the interactions of various BMP proteins and highlights the consequences when this process doesn't function properly. Illustration B shows the complex interaction of BMP acting through trans-membrane proteins to stimulate SMAD and SMURF intracellular proteins, necessary to transcribe various BMP dependent gene products.

Incorrect answers:
2) Mazabraud's syndrome - characterized by poly-ostotic fibrous dysplasia and multiple intramuscular myxomas, known to be associated with mutations in the GNAS1 gene
3) Ollier's syndrome - multiple enchondromas - likely caused by mutations in the Parathyroid hormone-related protein and Indian Hedgehog protein.
4) Osteopetrosis - caused by abnormal number or function of osteoclasts - associated with mutations in the CLCN7 gene
5) Osteopoikilosis - aka "spotted bone disease" - caused by defective LEMD3 gene
