## Question

A 67-year-old female is seeing you as a second opinion for her right knee osteoarthritis. She has many questions about how you perform a total knee arthroplasty. She is very concerned about the alignment of her knee. Which implement is associated with the greatest implant positioning precision?

## Options

1. Spinal anesthesia
2. Robotic assistance
3. Tranexamic acid
4. Intramedullary cutting guides
5. Extramedullary cutting guides

## Correct option

**2. Robotic assistance**

## Answer explanation

Robotic assistance in total knee arthroplasty (TKA) is a useful tool that helps surgeons precisely place implants and reduce outliers (Answer 2).

There is an inherent amount of error when using traditional manual instrumentation. It has been thought that the outliers caused by this error could contribute to the 5-20% dissatisfaction rate patients have after their TKA. Technologies like computer navigation and robotic assistance have been developed and have been demonstrated to reduce the number of outliers. This has not been demonstrated to have an impact on patient outcomes measures.

*Cho et al. performed a retrospective study in which they followed randomized patients to either conventional or robotic TKA and followed patients for a mean of 11 years.* **They found** no significant difference in outcomes between groups. **They found** the conventional TKA group showed a significantly higher number of outliers. **They concluded** that both robotic and conventional TKA have excellent long-term outcomes.

*Liow et al. performed a prospective randomized study assessing joint line restoration in conventional versus robotic TKA.* *They defined joint line outliers as being >5mm.* **They found** that the robotic group had 3.23% joint line outliers, while the conventional group had 20.6%. **They also found** that the robotic group had no mechanical axis outliers or notching, while the conventional group had 19.4% and 10.3%, respectively. **They concluded** that robotic-assisted TKA reduces joint-line deviation outliers compared to conventional TKA.

Incorrect answers:
Answer 1: Spinal anesthesia is an option for anesthesia for total joint arthroplasty that has been variably associated with improved outcomes after surgery. It has not been associated with reducing implant positioning outliers.
Answer 3: Tranexamic acid (TXA) is a synthetic lysine analog that reduces perioperative blood loss by competitively inhibiting the activation of plasminogen. TXA has been associated with improved outcomes after surgery, but it has not been directly linked with reducing implant positioning outliers.
Answers 4 and 5: Both intramedullary (IM) and extramedullary (EM) cutting guides are options with manual instrumentation. Both have their advantages and disadvantages. Neither has been associated with a reduction in outliers like robotic assistance has.
