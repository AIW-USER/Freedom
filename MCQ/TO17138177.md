Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Obstetric Brachial Plexopathy (Erb s, Klumpke_s Palsy)

## Question

Most common presentation of Shoulder sequelae according to Zancolli classification in Neonatal Brachial plexus palsy?

## Options

1. Internal rotation adduction contracture without joint deformity or dislocation
2. Internal rotation adduction contracture with joint deformity and posterior subluxation or dislocation of shoulder
3. External rotation abduction contracture with anteroinferior subluxation or dislocation
4. Shoulder and rotation flaccid paralysis and elbow flexion paralysis

## Correct option

**2. Internal rotation adduction contracture with joint deformity and posterior subluxation or dislocation of shoulder**

## Answer explanation

Internal rotation adduction contracture with joint deformity and posterior subluxation or dislocation of shoulder is the most common residual shoulder deformity occurring in more than 60% children.

Answer: 2
