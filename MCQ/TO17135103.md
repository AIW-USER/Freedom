Chapter: Spine MCQs — Pediatric Cervical Spine

## Question

All are true regarding Pediatric Spinal Cord Injury except?

## Options

1. Cervical spine injuries are more common in children aged <8 years due to small head-to-body ratio.
2. RTA is most common cause
3. Pediatric spinal column can stretch up to 5 cm without rupture.
4. Normal retropharyngeal space is < 6 mm at C2 and < 14 mm at C6

## Correct option

**1. Cervical spine injuries are more common in children aged <8 years due to small head-to-body ratio.**

## Answer explanation

Cervical spine injuries are more common in children aged <8 years due to large head to body (neck) ratio

![[TO17135103-1.jpg]]

Normal retro pharyngeal space
at C2 is < 6mm for both adults and children. At C6 it’s < 22mm for adults and < 14mm in children.

Answer: 1
