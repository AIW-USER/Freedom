Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Hallux Valgus Bunion

## Question

Which is not a recommended treatment for severe hallux valgus with high IMA?

## Options

1. Chevron’s osteotomy
2. Scarf’s osteotomy
3. 1st MTPJ fusion
4. Lapidus procedures

## Correct option

**1. Chevron’s osteotomy**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: chevron osteotomy is indicated for mild to moderate deformity

Answer: 1
