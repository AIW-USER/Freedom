## Question

Position of elbow in valgus stress test is( regional orthopaedics-elbow- Medial ulnar collateral ligament (MUCL) injury)

## Options

1. Full extension and supination
2. 20-30 degrees flexion
3. 20-30 degrees flexion and supination
4. 45 degrees flexion and supination

## Correct option

**3. 20-30 degrees flexion and supination**

## Answer explanation

In the valgus stress test, the elbow is placed in 20° to 30° of flexion with the forearm supinated and a valgus stress is applied. The valgus stress test is 66% sensitive and 60% specific.

The moving valgus stress test, described by O’ Driscoll is performed with the patient upright; the shoulder is placed in 90° of abduction and maximal external rotation with the elbow in maximal flexion. A constant valgus force is applied as the elbow is extended to 30° of flexion. A positive test must reproduce pain over the MCL between 70° and 120° of flexion, which is reminiscent of throwing between the late cocking and the early acceleration phases. This test is 100% sensitive and 75% specific.

Answer: 3
