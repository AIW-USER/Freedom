## Question

Which of the following ganglion/s are associated with the Trigeminal Nerve?

## Options

1. Ciliary ganglion
2. Otic ganglion
3. Sphenopalatine ganglion
4. All of the above

## Correct option

**4. All of the above**

## Answer explanation

![[TO17138010-1.jpg]]

There are 4 parasympathetic ganglia in the head & neck:

The ciliary ganglion, otic ganglion, sphenopalatine (or pterygopalatine) ganglion & the submandibular ganglion.

All the 4 ganglia are associated with the trigeminal nerve.

The ciliary ganglion via ophthalmic division.

The otic and sphenopalatine ganglia via the maxillary division.

The submandibular ganglion via the mandibular division.

Also remember: the Trigeminal ganglion is a SENSORY ganglion.

The parasympathetic system has peripheral ganglia like the above ones.

The sympathetic system has ganglia in the sympathetic chain, which in head & neck are the sympathetic cervical ganglia.
