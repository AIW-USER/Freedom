## Question

When using the Ponseti technique of clubfoot correction, the foot is manipulated in which position?

## Options

1. Supination abduction
2. Supination adduction
3. Pronation abduction
4. Pronation adduction

## Correct option

**1. Supination abduction**

## Answer explanation

The first Ponseti cast is applied with the foot supinated. This dorsiflexes the first ray and helps correct the cavus deformity of the foot. Foot adduction is corrected by abducting the forefoot in supination and rotating the forefoot around the head of the talus.

Answer: 1
