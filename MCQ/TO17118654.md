Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — ACL

## Question

Which of the following is NOT a risk factor for ACL injury

## Options

1. High tibial slope
2. Female sex
3. Hamstring dominance
4. Foot pronation

## Correct option

**3. Hamstring dominance**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

Important risk factors for ACL injury: Hard field surface, Inappropriate footwear, high BMI, Knee valgus and high Q angle, narrow intercondylar notch dimensions, high tibial slope and female sex. Factors that make ACL injury almost 8 times more common in females: differences in level of conditioning and skill (leading to altered movement patterns), femoral notch size, ACL dimensions, degree of knee laxity, Q angle, Quadriceps dominance and hormonal differences.
