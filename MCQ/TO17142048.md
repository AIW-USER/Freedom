Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Claw Toe

## Question

Plantar translation of the metatarsal head during a distal metatarsal osteotomy results in which of the following complications?

## Options

1. Claw toe
2. Hammer toe
3. Mallet toe
4. Floating Toe

## Correct option

**4. Floating Toe**

## Answer explanation

Translation of metatarsal head ( weil’s osteotomy) leads to change in force of intrinsics from volar to dorsal and weak toe flexors leading to extension at both MTPJ and IP joints called as floating toe. Girdlestone and distal MT shortening osteotomy(Weil lesser MT osteotomy) is done for claw toe deformity of all four lesser toes.

Answer: 4
