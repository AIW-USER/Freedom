## Question

Which of the following statement is true? (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Physis acts as a barrier for spread of infection to epiphysis in all age groups
2. Physis may allow spread of infection to epiphysis in children below the age of 2 years
3. Physis may allow spread of infection in children to epiphysis once they are above the age of 2 years
4. Acute hematogenous osteomyelitis is commonly seen in adults

## Correct option

**2. Physis may allow spread of infection to epiphysis in children below the age of 2 years**

## Answer explanation

In children younger than 2 years, the common blood supply of the metaphysis and epiphysis crosses the physis and can allow spread of a metaphyseal abscess into the epiphysis and eventually into the joint.

Answer: 2
