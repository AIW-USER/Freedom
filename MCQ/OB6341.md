## Question

What is the most common site of metastases from a soft-tissue sarcoma?

## Options

1. Liver
2. Bone
3. Lymph nodes
4. Lungs
5. Muscle

## Correct option

**4. Lungs**

## Answer explanation

The most common site of metastases from a soft-tissue sarcoma is the lungs and occurs in 40% to 60% of patients. The second most common site of metastases in soft-tissue sarcomas is the lymph nodes. Nodal metastases are seen with regularity in synovial sarcoma, epithelioid sarcoma, and rhabdosarcoma. The liver, brain, bone, and muscle are occasional sites of spread, but the occurrence is very rare.
