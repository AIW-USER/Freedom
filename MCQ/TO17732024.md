## Question

Investigation of choice for occult fracture neck of femur within 24 hours of injury is (general orthopedics-imaging and Investigations)

## Options

1. MRI
2. CT
3. PET-CT
4. X ray

## Correct option

**1. MRI**

## Answer explanation

The NICE Hip Fracture Management Guideline (published in 2011) clearly states that MR imaging is the investigation choice for an occult hip fracture, with CT as second line if MRI is contraindicated or cannot be obtained within 24 hours.

Answer: 1
