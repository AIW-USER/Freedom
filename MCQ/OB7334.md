## Question

Emergent management of acute tooth displacement (luxation) includes

## Options

1. delaying replantation until a dentist is present.
2. scrubbing the root of the tooth clean with hydrogen peroxide.
3. transporting the tooth in a carbonated beverage.
4. emergency root canal.
5. immediate repositioning or replantation of the tooth.

## Correct option

**5. immediate repositioning or replantation of the tooth.**

## Answer explanation

Avulsed teeth must be replanted immediately to enhance viability of the periodontal ligament cells on the root. With the tooth in place, the athlete should bite down on a towel to maintain stability. The athlete should be taken emergently to a dentist's office or emergency room. The avulsed tooth should not be handled by the root or scrubbed to remove debris. If immediate replantation is not possible, the tooth should be transported in saline solution, milk, or saliva on gauze.
