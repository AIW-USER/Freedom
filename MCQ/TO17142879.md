## Question

Not a component of TOMITA Scoring that is adopted for prognosticating patients with Spinal metastasis [FNB 2022]

## Options

1. Presence of visceral metastasis
2. General condition of patient
3. Number of bony metastasis lesions
4. Type of primary lesion

## Correct option

**2. General condition of patient**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

General condition of the patient is taken into consideration by Modified Tokuhashi score and not Tomita score.

SCORING SYSTEMS FOR SPINAL METASTASIS

The prognosis of patients with spinal metastasis is never easy to predict. Although the primary site from where metastasis arose can help determine the aggressiveness of the disease, it still is very difficult to predict patient prognosis. Hence, two scoring systems have become popular for same: -

- TOMITA score
- Modified TOKUHASHI score

These scores intend to predict patient prognosis in people with spinal metastasis and help physicians to choose the optimal plan of management.

TOMITA SCORE

The Tomita score was introduced in 2001, and is composed of three parameters based on tumour growth, presence of visceral metastases and number of bone metastasis lesions.

![[TO17142879-1.jpg]]

Suggestions as per scores: -

Score 2-4: Good prognosis- Pursue aggressive surgery

Score 5-7: Moderate prognosis- Surgery intent is palliative

Score 8-10: Poor prognosis- Non operative and supportive treatment should be mainstay

MODIFIED TOKUHASHI SCORE

The Tokuhashi score was first introduced in 1990 and revised in 2005 and is alternative to Tomita score. This scoring system is based on five parameters, including patient condition, location of metastasis and the site of primary cancer. The modified Tokuhashi score is believed to have better accuracy in determining actual survival.

![[TO17142879-2.jpg]]

Suggestions as per this scoring system: -

Score 12-15: Good prognosis- Pursue aggressive surgery

Score 9-11: Moderate prognosis- Surgery intent is palliative

Score 0-8: Poor prognosis- Non operative and supportive treatment should be mainstay
