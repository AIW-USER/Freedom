## Question

Which of the following is NOT part of the Joint Commission Universal Protocol to prevent wrong-site surgery?

## Options

1. Displaying labeled radiographic images before and during the procedure
2. Involving the patient in the site marking process when possible
3. Only the staff surgeon should speak during the time out
4. Procedure site marking by a licensed independent practitioner who will be present during the case
5. Verifying availability of all required blood products, implants and special equipment.

## Correct option

**3. Only the staff surgeon should speak during the time out**

## Answer explanation

All active members of the procedure team should actively communicate during the time, NOT only the staff surgeon.

The Joint Commission has created a Universal Protocol with the goal to prevent wrong site, wrong procedure, and wrong person surgery. There are three parts to it: conduct a pre-procedure verification, mark the procedure site, and perform a time-out. Each of these steps has guidelines and substeps to ensure that the correct surgery is performed every time.

*Conley et al. demonstrated in a qualitative analysis that the effectiveness of surgical checklists relies heavily on its implementation.* **They found** that when the checklist was implemented by leaders who persuasively explained why and demonstrated the use of the checklist there were high rates of adherence and continued utilization.

*Haynes et al. used the modified operating-room version Safety Attitudes Questionnaire (SAQ) to evaluate how safety attitudes changed after implementation of a surgical safety checklist.* **They found** that there was a correlation between the reduction in postoperative complications and the SAQ score, and a significant improvement in SAQ score after checklist implementation. **They noted** that 93.4% of polled clinicians reported wanting the checklist performed if they were to undergo surgery.

Illustrations A, B and C are the three sections of the Universal Protocol as outlined above.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1, 2, 4, 5: These are all parts of the universal protocol that contribute to correct patient, procedure, and site.
