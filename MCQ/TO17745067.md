## Question

Intramedullary femur hole in TKR is not required to make in (arthroplasty- knee arthroplasty- implants and prosthesis design)

## Options

1. TKR –patient specific instrumentation
2. PS
3. CR
4. Conventional TKR

## Correct option

**1. TKR –patient specific instrumentation**

## Answer explanation

Patient specific instrumentation(PSI)/blocks: PSI uses preoperative imaging(CT scan with 3 D reconstruction/MRI) to generate custom bone cutting guides that fit precisely on the unique articular surface of the bone of each patient to allow for more precise bony cuts. The proposed benefits of the PSI system include improved mechanical alignment of the limb, a decrease in surgical time, a reduction in blood loss and a decrease in the number of surgical trays used.

Alignment of the knee can be restored by using PSI and computer assisted navigation without using the IM rod. This theoretically reduces the chance of fat emboli due to avoidance of intramedullary invasion.

Answer: 1
