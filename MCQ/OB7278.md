## Question

Abnormal autosomal-dominant genes typically result in what type of defect?

## Options

1. Structural
2. Enzymatic
3. Biochemical
4. Translocations
5. Deletions

## Correct option

**1. Structural**

## Answer explanation

Autosomal-dominant gene defects usually cause structural deformities. An example is achondroplasia where the dominant gene codes for fibroblast growth factor receptor 3, a structural protein that results in the quantitative decrease in cartilage formation. The gene defects are inherited from one of the parents or are sporadic mutations. Autosomal-recessive genes code for enzymatic and biochemical defects. Translocations and deletions relate to chromosomal abnormalities.
