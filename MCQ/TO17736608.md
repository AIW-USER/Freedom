## Question

Most important sign of compartment syndrome is (trauma- leg compartment syndrome)

## Options

1. Tight compartment
2. Pain out of proportion of injury and pain on passive movement
3. Increased compartment pressure
4. Loss of pulse and paresthesia

## Correct option

**2. Pain out of proportion of injury and pain on passive movement**

## Answer explanation

Compartment syndrome: Compartment syndrome results when pressure rises within the closed compartment of a limb, causing a vascular compromise. Apart from limbs it can also develop into buttocks, abdomen and paraspinal muscles.

While most common cause is trauma (fracture) other causes include crush injuries of limb, tight circumferential bandaging, snake bite, burns, Surgery to blood vessels of an arm or leg, a blood clot in a blood vessel in an arm or leg, anabolic steroids, vigorous eccentric exercises, and re-establishment of blood flow after blocked circulation. Diagnosis is based on clinical background and signs and symptoms include six “P”s; Puffiness (swelling), pain on passive stretch of the fingers (passive stretch test), pallor, pulselessness, paresthesia and paralysis. Pain disproportionate to injury or pain on passive stretch is the most early and reliable sign. Pulse is an unreliable sign and distal pulses may be present or absent. Development of paresthesia and paralysis are late features. Normal compartment pressure is below 10 mm of Hg. Pressure more than 30 mm Hg indicates need for urgent fasciotomy. Compartment pressure can be measured by Wick or slit catheter technique or an arterial line pressure monitor system.

Prevention: Injured limb should be kept elevated to decrease soft tissue swelling and edema. It is important to note that in impending compartment syndrome limb should be kept at heart level because limb elevation also reduces arterial blood flow and thus oxygen delivery to tissues.

Supracondylar fracture humerus is the most common cause of compartment syndrome in children. Proximal tibial fracture is the most common cause of compartment syndrome in adults and overall.

Answer: 2
