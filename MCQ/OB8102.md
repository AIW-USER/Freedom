## Question

What is the most common organism in osteomyelitis of the foot that results from a puncture wound in a non-diabetic patient?

## Options

1. Proteus
2. Clostridia
3. Pseudomonas
4. Streptococcus
5. Staphylococcus aureus

## Correct option

**3. Pseudomonas**

## Answer explanation

Although Staphylococcus aureus is the most common causative agent for soft-tissue infection following puncture wounds of the foot, pseudomonas is the most common organism found when osteomyelitis occurs.
