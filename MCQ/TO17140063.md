## Question

According to Tuoheti classification most common type of attachment of long head of biceps on the glenoid labrum is

## Options

1. Completely posterior
2. Predominantly posterior
3. Completely anterior
4. Predominantly anterior

## Correct option

**2. Predominantly posterior**

## Answer explanation

long head of biceps tendon most commonly has 50% fibers attaching to labrum and 50% to supraglenoid tubercle

biceps tendon attachment on glenoid (Tuoheti classification)

Type 1
Completely posterior

Type 2
Predominantly posterior (most common)

Type 3
Anterior plus posterior

Type 4
Anterior (least common)

Answer : b
