## Question

Q angle is increased by which of the following parameter

## Options

1. Increased femoral anteversion
2. Medialization of tuberosity
3. Internal tibial torsion
4. Genu varum

## Correct option

**1. Increased femoral anteversion**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

Quadriceps angle (Q-angle): It may be defined as the angle subtended between a line drawn from the anterior superior iliac spine to the center of the patella, and a line from the center of the patella to the tibial tubercle (Fig). Normally the angle is 14° in males and 17° in females (on a standing radiograph). More is the Q angle (as when the tibial tuberosity is laterally shifted), more would be the lateral pull of the quadriceps on patella and more unstable would be the patellofemoral joint.

![[TO17906043-1.jpg]]

Increases in q-angle are associated with: -

- increased femoral anteversion
- external tibial torsion
- laterally displaced tibial tubercle
- genu valgum
