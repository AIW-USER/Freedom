Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Proximal Humerus Fractures

## Question

Which is the initial site of osteonecrosis in humeral head after damage to its blood supply?

## Options

1. Superolateral aspect
2. Superomedial aspect
3. Supero-posterior aspect
4. Supero-anterior aspect

## Correct option

**2. Superomedial aspect**

## Answer explanation

The superomedial aspect of the humeral head has been noted to be almost void of large intraosseous blood vessels and may receive its supply via a more indirect or diffusive route (Keough et al., 2019). This also suggests that damage to the main intraosseous network may compromise the blood flow to this already high-risk zone, which corresponds to the initial starting location of osteonecrosis. This poorly vascularized area may be more susceptible to perioperative damage to the intraosseous network and/or damage to the ACHA and its anterolateral branch.

Answer: 2
