## Correct option

## Answer explanation

This football player with recurrent shoulder instability has undergone a coracoid transfer (Latarjet procedure) to restore bony instability. The musculocutaneous nerve (Label E) is the most commonly injured nerve and occurs due to instrumentation around the conjoint tendon.

The Latarjet procedure has gained increased popularity for use in anterior glenoid defect reconstruction after with recurrent anterior glenohumeral instability. The general surgical technique involves transferring the coracoid process to the anterior glenoid through a split in the subscapularis. The most common nerve at risk during this procedure is the musculocutaneous nerve, followed by the axillary nerve. Most neurologic injuries following the Latarjet procedure are neuropraxias that resolve spontaneously without intervention.

*Hovelius et al. reviewed the Latarjet procedure for recurrent dislocation of the shoulder.* **They reported** a 6% incidence of redislocation after the procedure, however, no redislocation in patients who achieved osseous or fibrous union. **They concluded** that increased redislocation events were associated with more than 1.5 centimeters of graft migration or with placement of the graft 1 cm or more medial to the glenoid rim.

*Griesser et al. reviewed the complications and re-operations after the Latarjet stabilization procedure.* **They reported** a 30% total complication rate, of which 2.9% were redislocations, with most dislocations occurring within the first year postoperatively. **They observed** a 7% unplanned reoperation rate, seen mostly in open Latarjet procedures compared to arthroscopic.

Figures A-C demonstrate significant anteroinferior bone loss of the glenoid. Figure D is an unlabeled schematic of the brachial plexus. Illustration A is the labeled version of Figure D.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Label A is the long thoracic nerve.
Answer 2: Label B is the suprascapular nerve.
Answer 3: Label C is the axillary nerve, which is the second most common neuropraxia.
Answer 4: Label D is the radial nerve.
