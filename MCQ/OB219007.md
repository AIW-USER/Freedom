## Question

A patient with a history of narcotic abuse is complaining of ongoing pain after having open reduction and internal fixation (ORIF) of an ankle fracture. He was involved in a motor vehicle accident. Which of the following is true regarding morphine milligram equivalents (MME) of various narcotic medications?

## Options

1. Hydrocodone is equal to oxycodone
2. Hydromorphone is more than double oxycodone
3. Oxycodone is half of hydrocodone
4. Hydromorphone is double hydrocodone
5. Oxycodone is triple hydrocodone

## Correct option

**2. Hydromorphone is more than double oxycodone**

## Answer explanation

The correct morphine milligram equivalents (MME) for hydrocodone, oxycodone, and hydromorphone are 1, 1.5, 4, respectively.

Opiates change the perception and modulation of pain. Oral morphine milligram equivalents (MME) allow physicians to understand the strengths of various opioids. While oxycodone is more potent than hydrocodone with 1.5 MME per dose versus hydrocodone (1 MME), both have been found to have equal efficacy for acute fracture-related pain. Hydromorphone has 3 to 7 times the efficacy of morphine, whereas oxycodone is only 1.5 times greater.

*Hagedorn et al. reviewed oral morphine milligram equivalents (MME).* **They reported** that the use of MME is especially helpful when transitioning patients with chronic pain between opioid medications. **They concluded** that although oral MME serves as an excellent tool, it should not be used solely when judging how to transition between opioids.

*Marco et al. randomized 73 patients >12 years of age to receive either oxycodone or hydrocodone after an acute fracture.* **They reported** that there was no difference in reported pain between the two at 30 minutes and 60 minutes.

*Chang et al. performed a prospective, randomized, double-blind trial of non-elderly adults with extremity pain in the emergency department.* **They reported** that at 24 hours after discharge, they found no clinically or statistically significant difference in analgesic efficacy between 5 mg of oxycodone plus 325 mg of acetaminophen versus 5 mg of hydrocodone plus 325 mg of acetaminophen. **They concluded** that while oxymorphone and oxycodone are about equally effective in treating pain, more adverse events are seen with oxymorphone because of its higher potency.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1,3-5: The correct morphine milligram equivalents (MME) for hydrocodone, oxycodone, and hydromorphone is 1, 1.5, 4, respectively.
