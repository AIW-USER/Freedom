Chapter: General Orthopedics MCQs — Platelet Rich Plasma

## Question

In buffy coat method of PRP preparation which layer contains PRP

## Options

1. Bottom layer
2. Middle layer
3. Top layer
4. Superficial part of top layer

## Correct option

**2. Middle layer**

## Answer explanation

Buffy coat method of PRP preparation

1. Whole blood should be stored at 20°C to 24°C before centrifugation.

2. Centrifuge whole blood at a ‘high’ speed.

3. Three layers are formed because of its density: The bottom layer consisting of RBCs, the middle layer consisting of platelets and WBCs and the top Platelet poor plasma (PPP) layer.

4. Remove supernatant plasma from the top of the container.

5. Transfer the buffy-coat layer to another sterile tube.

6. Centrifuge at low speed to separate WBCs or use leucocyte filtration filter.

Answer : b
