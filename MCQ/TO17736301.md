## Question

Not seen in fibrous dysplasia (orthopedic oncology- benign tumors and tumor like lesions-fibrous dysplasia)

## Options

1. Rind sign
2. Harrison groove
3. Chinese letter pattern
4. Café-au-lait spots

## Correct option

**2. Harrison groove**

## Answer explanation

Fibrous dysplasia: It is a nonfamilial disorder due to mutation of the gene (GNAS1) which encodes for α subunit of stimulatory G protein (Gsα). This results in inhibition of the differentiation and proliferation of bone forming stromal cells causing replacement of mature bone by fibrous tissue and woven bone.

Age group
site of affection
Most common site
Forms
Important Radiological Signs

5–30 years.
Metaphysis and diaphysis of the the proximal femur, tibia, humerus, ribs, and craniofacial bones
Femur is the most common site. (zygomatic-maxillary complex is the most common site in the craniofacial area).
Monostotic(most common form) -affecting a single bone

polyostotic -a number of bones are affected.

 -Generalized fibrous dysplasia involving the face and skull is called “Leontiasis ossea.”
1. Ground Glass appearance-multiloculated translucent (loss of trabecular pattern due to replacement of bony tissue by solid fibro-osseous mass) lesion expanding the cortex of the bone

2. Rind sign: when ground glass lesion is surrounded by a sclerotic rim.

3. Shepherd Crook Deformity- hook shaped deformity of proximal femur. It occurs due to micro fractures in the proximal femur, which becomes hook shaped as the medial wall collapses due to continued weight bearing.

Also know:

 Diagnosis is confirmed on biopsy that shows the well-recognized “Chinese Letter Pattern”

Most common non-skeleton manifestation of fibrous dysplasia is café-au-lait spots.

McCune Albright syndrome: It refers to the triad of polyostotic fibrous dysplasia, precocious puberty and skin pigmentation in the form of Café-au-lait spots. These spots are hallmarks of neurofibromatosis but in NF, these are smoothly marginated (Coast of California) in contrast to the irregular margins (Coast of Maine) these spots have when found in McCune Albright syndrome.

Mazabraud syndrome: Includes polyostotic fibrous dysplasia with intramural myxomas.

Answer : B
