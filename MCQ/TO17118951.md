Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — Meniscus Injuries

## Question

A young cricket player comes to you with knee pain and inability to extend it fully. He gives a history of twisting injury to his right knee when another player struck his fixed leg with a flexed knee. His MRI shows a double PCL sign. What is the appropriate management? (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Arthroscopic repair
2. Arthroscopic partial meniscectomy
3. Icing, rest, compression and elevation for 2 weeks and then isometric and knee bending exercises
4. Icing, rest, compression and elevation for 2 weeks and then gradual return to sports.

## Correct option

**1. Arthroscopic repair**

## Answer explanation

Double PCL sign on MRI and inability to fully extend the knee suggest a locked knee due to entrapped bucket handle meniscal tear. Treatment of acute bucket handle meniscal tear in young athletes is arthroscopic repair.

Answer: 1
