## Question

Current recommendation to assess neuropathic foot for sensation of touch is to use(FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. 10 gm monofilament
2. 1 gm monofilament
3. 5 gm monofilament
4. 1 gm polyfilament

## Correct option

**1. 10 gm monofilament**

## Answer explanation

To assess the neuropathy a 10 g monofilament test is currently recommended. With both eyes closed, the monofilament is touched at 90° to the skin and pressed till it buckles to 1 cm, and the patient is asked to say yes when a touch is felt. This test is repeated 3 times. If a patient fails to perceive touch all the three times then neuropathy is confirmed.
