## Question

## Options

1. EWS-FLI1
2. SYT-SSX1
3. PAX3-FKHR
4. EWS-ATF1
5. FUS-DDIT3

## Correct option

**2. SYT-SSX1**

## Answer explanation

The patient's imaging and histology specimen are most consistent with synovial sarcoma. SYT-SSX1 and SYT-SSX2 are the transcription products characteristic of synovial sarcoma's cytogenetic profile.

Synovial sarcoma is a malignant soft tissue tumor with an unknown cell of origin. These are generally deep, high-grade extremity tumors. The classic t(x:18) translocation is present in 90% of cases and the resultant fusion protein is typically either SYT-SSX1(most common), SYT-SSX2, or SYT-SSX4 (least common). Like most high-grade sarcomas, treatment involves wide resection and radiation. Synovial sarcomas are one of the more chemo-sensitive sarcomas, therefore chemotherapy may also be considered in some cases.

*Deshmukh et al. discuss the effect of synovial sarcoma size and location on survival.* *They retrospectively reviewed 135 patients treated over a period of 35 years and report the 10 year survival to be 88%, 38%, and 8% for tumors of sizes <5 cm, 5-10 cm, and over 10 cm, respectively.*

Figure A demonstrates a T1 sagittal MRI with a large soft tissue mass posterior to the distal femur. Figure B is a T2 sagittal MRI showing the same heterogenous mass. Figure C is a T2 axial MRI demonstrating the hyperintensity of the mass on T2-weight imaging. Figure D demonstrates the biphasic variant histology of synovial sarcoma with sheets of spindle cells and occasional epithelial cells in gland-like formations.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: EWS-FLI1 is the fusion protein for Ewing's sarcoma
Answer 3: PAX3-FKHR is the fusion protein for alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma
Answer 4: EWS-ATF1 is associated with clear cell sarcoma
Answer 5: FUS-DDIT3 is associated with myxoid liposarcoma
