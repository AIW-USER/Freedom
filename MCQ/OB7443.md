## Question

Which of the following arteries is the pedicle supply to the lateral arm flap?

## Options

1. Radial recurrent
2. Profunda brachii
3. Interosseous recurrent
4. Anterior radial collateral
5. Posterior radial collateral

## Correct option

**5. Posterior radial collateral**

## Answer explanation

The posterior radial collateral artery provides the vascular supply to the lateral arm flap. The radial collateral artery travels with the radial nerve in the spiral groove until both penetrate the lateral intermuscular septum. It then divides into the anterior and posterior radial collateral arteries. The posterior branch passes posterior to the lateral intermuscular septum.
