## Options

1. Incisional biopsy
2. Allograft reconstruction
3. Vascularized fibula reconstruction
4. Nonvascularized fibular autograft
5. Intralesional curettage and polymethylmethacrylate (PMMA) packing

## Correct option

**1. Incisional biopsy**

## Answer explanation

Whereas the imaging studies show a benign giant cell tumor of bone, an incisional biopsy is still the first surgery that should be performed. After a tissue diagnosis is confirmed, then the reconstructive options can be discussed. A malignancy may present like a benign, aggressive giant cell tumor.
