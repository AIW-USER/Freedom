## Question

Most specific test for carpal tunnel syndrome is

## Options

1. Wrist flexion test
2. Durkan test
3. Tinel sign
4. Hand diagram

## Correct option

**4. Hand diagram**

## Answer explanation

Durkan nerve compression, the hand diagram score, night pain, and Semmes-Weinstein testing after a Phalen test have the highest sensitivity. The most specific tests are the hand diagram and Tinel sign. A patient with an abnormal hand diagram, a positive Durkan test, abnormal Semmes-Weinstein sensibility testing, and night pain has a very high probability of having carpal tunnel syndrome

Semmes-Weinstein monofilament pressure testing is the most accurate in determining early nerve compression.

According to some authors, electrodiagnostic studies including nerve conduction velocities and electromyography are reliable confirmatory tests. A distal motor latency of more than 4.5 milliseconds (ms) and a sensory latency of more than 3.5 ms are considered abnormal.

Answer: 4
