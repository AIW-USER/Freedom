Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Fracture of Femur and Tibia Shaft

## Question

What is true about checkrein deformity of great toe (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. FHL tear
2. Fracture of distal third of tibia
3. Fracture involving IP joint of great toe
4. FHL tethering at first MTP joint due to injury

## Correct option

**2. Fracture of distal third of tibia**

## Answer explanation

Checkrein deformity of the great toe may occur following a fracture of the distal third of the tibia. Flexor hallucis longus adheres to the callus at the fracture site so that its tendons form a bowstring between this point and the point of insertion on the tendon into the great toe. With ankle dorsiflexion, the great toe undergoes flexion and with ankle plantar flexion IP joint of great toe extends.

Answer: 2
