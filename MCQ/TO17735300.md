## Question

Risk factors for patellofemoral pain include all except (Regional orthopedics- knee- patellofemoral instability)

## Options

1. Flat feet, Gastrocnemius tightness, pronated feet
2. Ligamentous laxity without patellar instability
3. Patellar instability without ligamentous laxity
4. Excessive femoral anteversion and tibia vara

## Correct option

**2. Ligamentous laxity without patellar instability**

## Answer explanation

Ligamentous laxity without patellar instability is not necessarily associated with knee pain

Answer : B
