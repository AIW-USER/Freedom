## Question

Spinal AV malformation, which factor responsible for aggressive lesion

## Options

1. Location
2. Severity of symptoms
3. Presence of Leptomeningeal vein
4. Age

## Correct option

**3. Presence of Leptomeningeal vein**

## Answer explanation

In spinal vascular malformations, the location or presentation of symptoms do not determine the outcome, but the presence of fistulous communication determines the outcome. Presence of large leptomeningeal vein differentiates a malformation into nidal based or fistulous lesions.

Answer: 3
