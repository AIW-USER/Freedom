Chapter: General Orthopedics MCQs — Bone Cement

## Question

What happens to the 'dough time' of bone cement when high amounts of antibiotic powder are added during manual mixing?

## Options

1. The dough time increases, making the cement stay sticky for a longer period.
2. The dough time is unaffected because the antibiotic is chemically inert.
3. The dough time decreases, causing the cement to flash-set instantly.
4. The cement becomes too hot to handle during the dough phase.

## Correct option

**1. The dough time increases, making the cement stay sticky for a longer period.**

## Answer explanation

Adding extra powder can disrupt the ratio of monomer to polymer, often extending the time it takes for the cement to reach a workable, non-sticky state.

Answer: 1
