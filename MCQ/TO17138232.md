Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Congenital Vertical Talus (CVT)

## Question

Which of the following condition is not associated with Congenital vertical talus?

## Options

1. Arthrogryposis
2. Spinal dysraphism
3. Spinal muscular atrophy
4. Amniotic band syndrome

## Correct option

**4. Amniotic band syndrome**

## Answer explanation

Below is a list of associations of conditions and syndromes with congenital vertical talus.

Because of its large association it is important to do a neuraxial screening and syndromic evaluation in all such children.

HOX D10 mutations have now been found in many of the familial positive cases which has been found to be associated with previously considered idiopathic cases.
