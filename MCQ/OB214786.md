## Question

Which of the following pathological processes is correctly matched to the physeal zone it most commonly affects?

## Options

1. Gaucher's disease; proliferative zone
2. Saltar Harris type II fracture; maturation zone within the hypertrophic zone
3. Renal SCFE; degenerative zone within the hypertrophic zone
4. Saltar Harris type III fracture: zone of provisional calcification within the hypertrophic zone
5. Achondroplasia; reserve zone

## Correct option

**4. Saltar Harris type III fracture: zone of provisional calcification within the hypertrophic zone**

## Answer explanation

Saltar harris (SH) fractures most commonly occur through the zone of provisional calcification within the hypertrophic zone.

The physis of the developing skeleton has 5 distinct zones: reserve zone, proliferative zone, hypertrophic zone, primary spongiosa, and secondary spongiosa. The hypertrophic zone is further subdivided into 3 distinct zones: the maturation zone, the degenerative zone, and the zone of provisional calcification. The hypertrophic zone is the weakest zone in tension of the 5 zones. Furthermore, the zone of provisional calcification is a transition point between calcified and noncalcified extracellular matrix proteins and is weaker than the surrounding bone and ligaments. As such, it is within the zone of provisional calcification that physeal fractures most commonly occur.

*Ogden et al. performed a detailed radiologic and histologic analysis to determine the failure patterns of the developing skeleton.* **They noted** that in the physis, specific fracture propagation varied, usually going through the non-calcified portion of the hypertrophic zone adjacent to the metaphysis. **They also noted** that there may be microscopic propagation at oblique angles from the primary fracture plane, splitting cell columns apart longitudinally. **The authors concluded** that the cartilage canals supplying the hypertrophic zone appear to be "weak" areas with the least tensile strength.

*Salter published a review of injuries involving the epiphyseal plate.* **He reported** that physeal fractures almost invariably occur on the diaphyseal side of the epiphyseal plate through the zone of hypertrophy and that the fracture line occasionally deviates into the shaft, leaving a triangular fragment of the metaphysis attached to the epiphysis.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Gaucher's disease primarily affects the reserve zone of the physis
Answer 2: SH fractures primarily fracture through the zone of provisional calcification within the hypertrophic zone.
Answer 3: Renal SCFE primarily affects the secondary spongiosa of the physis.
Answer 5: Achondroplasia primarily affects the proliferative zone of the physis.
