Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Dupuytren’s Contracture

## Question

What is the cause of PIP contracture in Dupuytren’s disease?

## Options

1. Retrovascular cord
2. Central cord
3. Spiral cord
4. Natatory cord

## Correct option

**3. Spiral cord**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C
EXPLANATION: -
Causes of contractures in Dupuytren’s disease
spiral cord It inserts distally into the lateral digital sheet then into Grayson's ligament and consists of pretendinous band, spiral band,
lateral digital sheet and
Grayson's ligament. cause of PIP contracture
central cord from disease involving pretendinous band
inserting into flexor sheath at PIPJ level and
forms palmar nodules and pits between distal palmar crease and palmar digital crease causes MCP contracture
retrovascular cord runs dorsal to the neurovascular bundle distally

originates from proximal phalanx, inserts on distal phalanx
causes DIP contracture

natatory cord (from natatory ligament) causes web space contracture
NOT involved in Dupuytren's disease Cleland's ligament
transverse ligament of the palmar aponeurosis
