## Question

Waddell signs include all the following except

## Options

1. Nonorganic tenderness
2. Simulation tests
3. Neuroanatomic motor and/or sensory compromise
4. Overreaction

## Correct option

**3. Neuroanatomic motor and/or sensory compromise**

## Answer explanation

Waddell signs are clinical findings that are typically elicited from patients who are experiencing pain of nonphysical (supratentorial) origin.

 The five classic signs of Waddell are as follows:

- Nonorganic tenderness (such as superficial tenderness to light touch),
- Simulation tests (designed to suggest to the patient that a specific provocative maneuver is being performed when, in fact, it is not),
- Overreaction to non-noxious stimuli,
- Regional (non neuroanatomic) motor and/or sensory compromise, and
- Distraction tests (e.g., performing a seated straight leg raise while seemingly focusing on the knee).

Answer: 3
