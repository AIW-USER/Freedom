## Question

After residency, a new orthopedic surgeon contemplates how they will perform total knee arthroplasty (TKA). To achieve the best long-term results, current literature would support which of the following practices?

## Options

1. Midvastus Approach
2. Mini-medial parapatellar
3. Subvastus approach
4. Standard medial parapatellar approach
5. The approach the surgeon is most comfortable with

## Correct option

**5. The approach the surgeon is most comfortable with**

## Answer explanation

There is significant variation in surgeon approach preference in primary total knee arthroplasty. Several minimally invasive or “muscle-sparing” techniques have been described and include the midvastus, mini-medial parapatellar, and subvastus approach. While many advertise significant differences in outcomes or cosmesis, no large-scale studies or meta-analyses have demonstrated long-term benefits to any of these techniques. Given this, recommendations generally advise performing the approach the surgeon is most comfortable with (Answer choice 5).

Bouché et al. conducted a systematic review and network meta-analysis to compare surgical approaches in primary total knee arthroplasty. Their series reports the result of 60 randomized control trials including 5042 patients and 5107 TKAs. They found no significant differences between different approaches for the KSS assessment or the WOMAC at 6 months, but the mean range of motion (ROM) at 6 months post-surgery was higher in the subvastus group than in all the other surgical approaches. They concluded that until future studies can demonstrate a long-term benefit, the outcomes between all studied surgical approaches to perform a TKA are clinically equivalent.

*Mora et al. reviewed TKA approaches and clinically relevant literature comparing post-operative results.* **They reported** short-term benefits in a range of motion with minimally invasive approaches that become insignificant at longer-term follow-up. **The authors conclude** that surgeons can achieve satisfactory and equal results with each approach, and should utilize the approach with which they are most comfortable.

Illustrations: A: Standard medial parapatellar approach. B: Quad-sparing approach. C: Subvastus approach. D: Midvastus approach.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1-4: Several small series have demonstrated improved ROM at short-term follow-up with minimally invasive techniques, but large studies and meta-analyses have demonstrated no difference in long-term outcomes across approaches.
