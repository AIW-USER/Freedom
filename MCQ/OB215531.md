## Question

Which of the following is an associated condition with amniotic constriction band syndrome?

## Options

1. Cleft palate
2. Cutis aplasia of the scalp
3. Pes planovalgus
4. Polydactyly
5. Renal aplasia

## Correct option

**1. Cleft palate**

## Answer explanation

This patient has amniotic band syndrome (ABS). Of the choices listed above, cleft palate has a known association with ABS.

Amniotic band syndrome is a condition involving fetal entrapment in strands of amniotic tissue. These bands most frequently affect the distal segments, including the hand. Several theories of ABS have been postulated and include a developmental abnormality attributed to vascular disruption, intrauterine disruption during pregnancy, or intrauterine trauma. ABS is associated with several conditions including clubfoot deformity, acrosyndactyly, cleft lip and/or cleft palate, and hemangiomas. Treatment is individualized, and the timing of repair and surgical planning are important in improving functional outcome. In patients with distal edema and acrosyndactyly, early repair portends better prognosis.

*Bernal et al. reviewed the features and associated defects of ABS.* **They noted** that this congenital anomaly has an unknown etiology. **They concluded** that treatment is aimed at recovery of function and prevention of the sequelae rather than prevention of the syndrome.

*Graf et al. reviewed use of sonographic detection of chorioamniotic membrane separation (CMS).* **They noted** that following membrane separation, amniotic bands formed, resulting in fetal demise in some cases. **They concluded** that membrane separation may be associated with amniotic band formation and in the setting of cord compromise, emergent delivery or fetoscopic release of the strangulating amniotic band may be required.

*Foulkes et al. reviewed 71 cases of ABS.* **They noted** that the average patient had three involved limbs, with a predilection for distal, central digits of the upper extremity. Furthermore, **they noted** that abnormal gestational histories were found in 60% of the cases; 50% had concurrent diagnoses, and nearly one-third had club-feet. **They concluded** that the term "early amnion rupture sequence" more accurately reflects the true pathogenesis of ABS.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2-5: These conditions are not associated with ABS
