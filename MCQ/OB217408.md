## Question

A 10-year-old male with a known spontaneous genetic mutation of chromosome 17 sustained a distal tibial shaft fracture approximately 3 years prior. He was treated with several attempts at casting but developed a pseudarthrosis. Skin examination reveals cafe-au-lait spots. Which type of tibial bowing was likely present before the fracture in this patient?

## Options

1. Anteromedial
2. Anterolateral
3. No bowing is expected
4. Posteromedial
5. Posterolateral

## Correct option

**2. Anterolateral**

## Answer explanation

This patient has Neurofibromatosis type 1 (NF1) with the development of tibial pseudarthrosis. Anterolateral tibial bowing is associated with NF1.

Anterolateral bowing and congenital pseudarthrosis of the tibia are congenital conditions, most commonly associated with NF1. Patients with NF1 may have physical manifestations such as café-au-lait spots. With NF1, the gene encoding the protein neurofibromin found on chromosome 17 most often undergoes a spontaneous mutation. Diagnosis is confirmed with radiographs of the tibia. Treatment is nonoperative with bracing for patients who are weight-bearing without pseudarthrosis or fracture. Surgical correction is indicated in the presence of pseudarthrosis or fracture

*Feldman et al. reviewed NF1.* **They reported** that café-au-lait macules and peripheral nerve sheath tumors (ie, neurofibromas) are the most commonly recognized manifestations of NF-1. **They noted** that the most complex orthopedic manifestations are scoliosis (dystrophic and non dystrophic), congenital pseudarthrosis of the tibia, and problems related to soft-tissue tumors. **They concluded** that congenital pseudarthrosis of the tibia is a long-bone dysplasia that afflicts patients with NF-1.

*Van Royen et al. reviewed the relationship between congenital pseudarthrosis of the tibia (CPT) and NF1.* **They reported** that the prevalence of NF1 in patients with CPT might be higher than what is reported. **They concluded** that a repeat meticulous examination and a multidisciplinary approach with clinical genetic counseling is paramount for CPT patients.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Anteromedial bowing is seen in fibular hemimelia
Answer 3: NK1 is associated with anterolateral tibial bowing
Answer 4: Posteromedial bowing is thought to develop from abnormal intrauterine packaging and is associated with calcaneovalgus and limb length discrepancy
Answer 5: Posterolateral bowing is not associated with any particular underlying condition
