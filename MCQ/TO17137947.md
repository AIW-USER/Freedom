Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Microvascular Surgery

## Question

What is the maximum acceptable time delay for vascular reconstruction if confirmed vascular impairment exists in a lower limb?

## Options

1. 8 hours of cold ischaemia
2. 6 hours of warm ischaemia
3. 3–4 hours of warm ischaemia
4. It depends on coexisting soft/bone tissue injury

## Correct option

**2. 6 hours of warm ischaemia**

## Answer explanation

Six hours of warm ischaemia represents the amount of time it takes for irreversible ischaemic damage to occur in muscle tissue.

Answer: 2
