## Question

A patient with neck pain has weak wrist dorsiflexion and a sluggish supinator reflex. The nerve root likely involved in the case is

## Options

1. C7
2. C5
3. C8
4. C6

## Correct option

**4. C6**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D

Wrist extensors (dorsiflexors) are supplied by C6 spinal nerve (see table below).

![[TO17907803-1.jpg]]

Supinator reflex is also controlled by C6 spinal segment (nerve). In this reflex, the hammer is meant to strike the styloid process of radius to stimulate “Brachioradialis (BR) muscle” and elicit its contraction causing the forearm to flex in mid prone position. Since the old name of BR was supinator longus, the reflex is often termed Supinator reflex.
