## Question

Not an operative indication for fracture shaft of the humerus (trauma- humerus shaft fractures)

## Options

1. Nonunion
2. Close fracture with radial nerve palsy
3. Floating elbow
4. Pathological Fracture with secondaries

## Correct option

**2. Close fracture with radial nerve palsy**

## Answer explanation

Radial nerve palsy is the most commonly encountered nerve deficit associated with humeral shaft fractures. However in most of the cases it is neurapraxia only and spontaneous recovery over a period of 4 months occurs in 100 % patients with low energy injuries. Therefore, radial nerve palsy in close humeral shaft fracture is not an indication for open management and nerve exploration.

Exploration of the nerve is warranted if function has not returned in 3 to 4 months and the fracture has healed. However in case of open fracture shaft of humerus exploration should be done at the time of debridement of wound.

Also know:

Acceptability criteria for closed reduction of shaft of humerus:

Shortening <3 cm

Rotation <30 degrees

Angulation <20 degrees

Answer: 2
