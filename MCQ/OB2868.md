## Question

What is the function of the homeobox gene products in the axial skeleton?

## Options

1. Create cross links between collagen fibers
2. Regulate somatization of the axial skeleton
3. Regulates limb bud formation
4. Controls proximal-distal growth
5. Controls dorsal-ventral growth

## Correct option

**2. Regulate somatization of the axial skeleton**

## Answer explanation

The function of homeobox (Hox) gene products in the axial skeleton regulates the somatization of the axial skeleton (answer choice 2).

For vertebrates, the axial skeleton derives from the somites, segmental units organized in pairs on both sides of the developing neural tube. Somites are formed in a rostral-to-caudal sequence by the epithelialization of mesenchymal cells at the rostral end of the presomitic mesoderm. The function of the Hox genes is to regulate the somitogenesis of the axial skeleton, essentially patterning digit formation. They play a crucial role in embryonic development and regulate the formation of structures in an anterior-to-posterior pattern. It is generally accepted that a specific combination of Hox genes expressed at a particular somatic level determines the axial identity of the resulting structures.

*Iimura et al. published a review article discussing the regulation and role of Hox gene activation and its contribution to limb formation.* **They found** that overexpression of Hox(b) genes in the epiblast of somites during the gastrula stage resulted in anterior to posterior limb development.

*The review article by Turnpenny et al. highlights abnormal vertebral segmentation (AVS) and other congenital malformations and their association with gene signaling pathways.* **They note** axial skeleton development is susceptible to genetic alterations of typical somitogenesis and a variety of dysmorphic syndromes express AVS in different forms.

Incorrect answer choices:
Answer choice 1: COL1A1 is an example gene that is involved in creating cross-links between collagen fibers.
Answer choice 3: The Sonic Hedgehog (Shh) gene regulates limb bud formation.
Answer choice 4: Proximal to distal growth is stimulated by FGF10 stimulation, not homeobox genes.
Answer choice 5: Wnt genes regulate dorsal-ventral growth.
