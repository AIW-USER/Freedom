Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Malleolar Fractures

## Question

Correct inference from picture given below is

![[TO21733294-1.png]]

## Options

1. ATFL avulsion
2. Superior peroneal retinaculum injury
3. Tillaux-Chaput fracture
4. wagstaffe fracture

## Correct option

**2. Superior peroneal retinaculum injury**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 2
EXPLANATION: -
This is fleck sign in the ankle and it refers to a cortical avulsion fracture of the distal fibula (lateral malleolus), indicating superior peroneal retinaculum injury. This fleck sign should raise the possibility of peroneal tendon subluxation or dislocation.
