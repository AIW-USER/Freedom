## Question

Which of the following condition is not associated with NF type 1?

## Options

1. Nerve sheath tumor
2. Psudoarthrosis of tibia
3. Scoliosis
4. None of the above

## Correct option

**4. None of the above**

## Answer explanation

Café-au-lait macules and peripheral nerve sheath tumors (ie, neurofibromas) are the most commonly recognized manifestations of NF-1. Orthopedic manifestations are scoliosis (dystrophic and non-dystrophic), congenital pseudarthrosis of the tibia, and problems related to soft-tissue tumors.
