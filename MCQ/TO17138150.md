Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Slipped Capital Femoral Epiphysis (SCFE)

## Question

Southwick’s angle is associated with

![[TO17138150-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Perthes disease
2. DDH
3. SCFE
4. Coxa vara

## Correct option

**3. SCFE**

## Answer explanation

Southwick's angle – It is used to measure the severity of slip of SCFE. It is measured as an angle between the femur shaft axis and a line perpendicular to the epiphysis base.

Answer: 3
