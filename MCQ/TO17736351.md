## Question

In transportal ACL, correct statement is (arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- ACL)

## Options

1. Femoral tunnel is drilled through tibial tunnel
2. Femoral tunnel is drilled through anteromedial portal
3. Femoral tunnel is drilled through anterolateral portal

## Correct option

**2. Femoral tunnel is drilled through anteromedial portal**

## Answer explanation

Femoral tunnel is placed vertically at roof top position
In transportal ACL reconstruction the femoral tunnel is drilled through an anteromedial portal. Anterolateral portal is used to put an arthroscope into the joint. In transtibial ACL reconstruction the femoral tunnel is drilled through a tibial tunnel which is made above 11 o'clock position (or roof top 12 o clock) and graft has vertical orientation. In transportal technique femoral tunnel is drilled in anatomical position at 10 o'clock position and graft takes horizontal orientation.

Answer: 2
