## Question

A 34-year-old active-duty male presents with worsening lower back pain and increasing radicular symptoms over the past year. He has undergone a reasonable length of treatment with physical therapy, NSAIDs, and lifestyle modifications. However, his symptoms are recalcitrant to these conservative measures and therefore indicated to undergo a fusion procedure. When comparing anterior interbody lumbar fusion (ALIF) with supplemental posterior instrumented fusion (PF) to transforaminal lumbar interbody fusion (TLIF) alone, which of the following advantages does ALIF+PF provide?

*Figure A*

## Options

1. Greater sagittal plane alignment correction
2. Lower risk of sexual dysfunction, including retrograde ejaculation
3. Improved rate of fusion with lower risk of nonunion
4. Direct visualization of nerve roots for decompression
5. Lower risk of retroperitoneal hematoma

## Correct option

**1. Greater sagittal plane alignment correction**

## Answer explanation

For the treatment of adult isthmic spondylolisthesis, anterior lumbar interbody fusion (ALIF) with posterior instrumented fusion (PF) has demonstrated improved segmental lordosis and disc height compared to transforaminal lumbar interbody fusion (TLIF; Answer 1). This improvement is purportedly due to the anterior longitudinal ligament's direct release during the ALIF approach.

Adult isthmic spondylolisthesis is characterized by pars interarticularis defects that result in subluxation of one vertebral body anterior to the adjacent inferior vertebral body. It most commonly occurs at the L5/S1 junction and may lead to worsening pain and radiculopathy. In a healthy spine, lumbar lordosis is primarily generated by the lower lumbar segments, and the segmental alignment at the lumbosacral junction can have a significant impact on global spinal alignment. Therefore, the central goal of surgically treating isthmic spondylolisthesis is maintaining or improving sagittal balance. ALIF+PF has been shown to significantly increase the resultant post-operative disc height and segmental lordosis compared to TLIF. These findings result in greater indirect neural decompression and could (1) substantially affect global sagittal balance over time and (2) reduce the progression of adjacent segment degeneration. While prospective randomized control trials are lacking, contemporary findings suggest ALIF+PF provides better radiographic and patient-reported outcomes for the treatment of L5-S1 isthmic spondylolisthesis compared to TLIF.

*Tye and colleagues retrospectively reviewed their institutional results, comparing the clinical, radiographic, and financial differences between ALIF+PF and TLIF for L5-S1 adult isthmic spondylolisthesis.* **They reported** no difference in the total cost between both procedures. Further, **they found** that ALIF+PF significantly improved segmental lordosis, disc height, and outcome scores at one year. **The authors concluded** that the superior radiographic outcomes achieved with ALIF may have contributed to improved clinical outcomes.

*Lightsey and colleagues performed a similar retrospective review comparing the outcomes of ALIF+PF and TLIF for treating L5-S1 adult isthmic spondylolisthesis.* **They reported** that ALIF generated greater segmental and L4-S1 lordosis, greater disc height, and more improved patient-reported outcome scores at final follow-up. **The authors concluded** that ALIF+PF generates multiple improved radiographic outcomes and patient-reported outcomes relative to TLIF patients.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: ALIF’s direct approach carries a HIGHER risk of injury of the superior hypogastric plexus, resulting in sexual dysfunction (i.e., retrograde ejaculation). The TLIF’s approach does not place patients at risk for sexual dysfunction.
Answer 3: ALIF+PF and TLIF have similar, high rates of successful fusion.
Answer 4: The TLIF approach involves direct visualization and decompression of nerve roots. Since the ALIF approach does not allow visualization, neural decompression is achieved indirectly by restoring disc height space and/or correcting anterolisthesis.
Answer 5: Given that its approach requires dissection through the abdomen, ALIF has the added risk of post-operative retroperitoneal hematomas. However, given that its dissection is away from the spinal cord and nerve roots, the ALIF component of treatment imparts a lower risk of injury to the dura and neural structures than posterior-based approaches.
