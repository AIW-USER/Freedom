## Question

The femoral insertion of the lateral collateral ligament maintains what consistent relationship relative to the lateral epicondyle of the femur?

## Options

1. Anterior and distal
2. Anterior and proximal
3. Posterior and distal
4. Posterior and proximal
5. The lateral collateral ligament inserts directly on the lateral epicondyle

## Correct option

**4. Posterior and proximal**

## Answer explanation

The femoral insertion of the lateral collateral ligament maintains a proximal and posterior relationship relative to the lateral femoral epicondyle. *In a cadaveric study, LaPrade and associates described the consistent anatomic relationship between the lateral collateral ligament insertion and the lateral epicondyle of the femur.* On average, the lateral collateral ligament inserts 1.4 mm proximal and 3.1 mm posterior to the lateral epicondyle. The lateral collateral ligament inserts proximal and posterior to the popliteus insertion on the femur. The average distance between the femoral insertions of the lateral collateral ligament and popliteus tendon was 18.5 mm.
