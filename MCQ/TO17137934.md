## Question

Which is the critical transcutaneous oxygen tension value below which the likely end result for an ischemic limb is amputation

## Options

1. Less than 40 mmHg
2. Less than 30 mmHg
3. Less than 20 mmHg
4. Less than 10 mmHg

## Correct option

**3. Less than 20 mmHg**

## Answer explanation

Just as MES score predicts the chances of amputation after a crush injury to the limb, transcutaneous oxygen tension would do that for a case of peripheral vascular disease.

Transcutaneous oxygen tension measurement is basically the partial pressure of oxygen measured in an extremity to detect the presence of a vascular insufficiency from a peripheral vascular disease. When the value is above 40 mmHg, the chances of an ulcer or wound present to heal would be normal. However, as the value decreases the wound healing starts getting affected and with values lower than 20 mmHg, the usual end result has been seen to be an amputation only.

Answer : (C)
