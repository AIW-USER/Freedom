## Question

A 17-year-old male patient presents to your clinic for evaluation of his forefoot pain. The pain is worse with weight bearing. Passive motion of his second toe elicits pain. His radiograph can be seen in Figure A. Which of the following is true of this condition?

![[q218896-a-freiberg.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. It is more common in adults than adolescents
2. It is more common in males
3. The second MTP joint is the most commonly affected
4. MTP joint fusion results in superior function to joint-preserving procedures
5. Fusion results in reliable union rate

## Correct option

**3. The second MTP joint is the most commonly affected**

## Answer explanation

Freiberg's disease most commonly affects the second MTP joint.

Freiberg’s disease is an osteochondrosis, typically of the head of the second metatarsal. It is thought to be a result of repetitive microtrauma. It is most common in adolescent females. The diagnosis can usually be made radiographically. An MRI can be useful in the early stages of the disease. Conservative management with either a cast or stiff-soled shoe is the first-line management. When surgical management is undertaken, joint preserving procedures have been shown to have higher satisfaction than those that sacrifice the joint.

*Kim et al. retrospectively reviewed the outcomes of a modified Weil osteotomy for the treatment of Freiberg’s disease.* **They found** improvements in VAS and AOFAS scores with improvements also noted in range of motion. **They concluded** that shortening the metatarsal improves pain and restores MTP joint congruency.

*Trnka et al. reviewed Frieberg’s disease and commented on the surgical options to address it.* **They state** that most of these patients are female, and it most commonly begins between the 11th and 17th year of age. **They reported** that 70% of joint destructive procedures result in the resolution of pain, and 90% of joint preserving procedures result in the resolution of pain.

Figure A is an AP radiograph of the foot demonstrating flattening and cystic changes of the second metatarsal head.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Adolescents are more commonly impacted than adults.
Answer 2: Freiberg’s disease more commonly impacts females in a 4:1 ratio.
Answer 4: Joint preserving procedures have higher patient satisfaction than joint sacrificing procedures.
Answer 5: Fusion of the lesser toes is notorious for an unacceptably high nonunion rate.
