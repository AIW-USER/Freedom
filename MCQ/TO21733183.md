## Question

Drop metastasis from GBM are of what type

## Options

1. Intradural extramedullary
2. Extra medullary
3. Extra dural
4. Intra medullary

## Correct option

**1. Intradural extramedullary**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

EXPLANATION: -

• Drop metastasis is a rare event that occurs when glioblastoma multiforme (GBM) spreads from the brain to the spinal cord.
• Definition: Intradural extramedullary spinal metastasis that originates from intracranial lesions
• Incidence : 1–2% of patients with GBM
• The primary mechanism of drop metastasis is believed to be tumor extravasation through the cerebrospinal fluid (CSF). Surgical debulking can increase the likelihood of tumor seeding.
• Other factors that may predispose to metastasis include: Lymphatic dissemination, Hematogenous dissemination, and Immunosuppression caused by chemoradiation.
• Symptoms of spinal metastasis may include: Nodular type drop metastasis, Diffuse leptomeningeal metastasis, and Meningeal signs.
• Steroid therapy can decrease pain and neurological dysfunction in patients with rapidly-progressive symptoms of cord compression.
• There is typically an average lag time of 8.5 months after GBM diagnosis prior to detection of spinal drop metastasis
