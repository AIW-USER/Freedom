## Question

Which of the following correctly pairs the anatomical space of the shoulder with its contents?

## Options

1. Quadrangular space; radial nerve and profunda brachii artery
2. Quadrangular space; axillary nerve and scapular circumflex artery
3. Quadrangular space; axillary nerve and posterior humeral circumflex artery
4. Triangular space; axillary nerve and posterior humeral circumflex artery
5. Triangular space; radial nerve and profunda brachii artery

## Correct option

**3. Quadrangular space; axillary nerve and posterior humeral circumflex artery**

## Answer explanation

The quadrangular (quadrilateral) space of the shoulder contains the axillary nerve and posterior humeral circumflex artery.

The shoulder contains three anatomically importance spaces: the quadrangular (quadrilateral) space, the triangular space, and the triangular interval. The quadrangular space contains the axillary nerve and posterior humeral circumflex artery, and is bordered by the long head of the triceps medially, humeral shaft laterally, teres minor superiorly, and teres major inferiorly. The triangular space contains the scapular circumflex artery, and is bordered by the long head of the triceps laterally, teres minur superiorly, and teres major inferiorly. Finally, the triangular interval contains the radial nerve and profunda brachii artery, and is bordered by the long head of the triceps medially, lateral head of the triceps or humerus laterally, and teres major superiorly.

*Flynn et al. published a review on quadrilateral space syndrome (QSS).* **They reported** that QSS is characterized by poorly-localized shoulder pain that worsens with shoulder abduction and external rotation, and paresthesia over the lateral aspect of the shoulder and arm in a nondermatomal pattern. **They state** that no definitive diagnostic imaging exists; however, pain relief after lidocaine block of the axillary nerve within the QS is a useful finding in the evaluation of patients with suspected QSS.

*Aval et al. published a review on neurovascular injuries in the athlete’s shoulder.* **They reported** that the cause of QSS is thought to be the result of abnormal fibrous bands and/or muscular hypertrophy of the borders of the quadrilateral space. **They state** that most patients improve with nonoperative treatment, but those who do not improve after a 3-6 months should be considered for surgical decompression.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1 and 2: The quadrangular space of the shoulder contains the axillary nerve and posterior humeral circumflex artery.
Answers 4 and 5: The triangular space contains the scapular circumflex artery.
