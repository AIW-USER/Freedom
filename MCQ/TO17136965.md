## Question

A 12 years old female child complains of pain and inability to bear weight also presenting slight swelling and focal tenderness at the Antero lateral joint line. The patient underwent for radiographic examination and the X ray is shown below:

Assertion: The condition is associated with forced lateral rotation of the foot or medial rotation of the leg on a fixed foot.

Reason: This rotational injury results in avulsion of the anterior tibia-fibular ligament at the lateral epiphysis

![[TO17136965-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A
2. Both A and R are correct but R is not the correct explanation of A
3. A is correct but R is incorrect
4. A is incorrect but R is correct

## Correct option

**1. Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A**

## Answer explanation

The fracture shown above is Tillaux Chaput fracture that results from avulsion of anterior-inferior tibio-fibular ligament from tibia (chaput tubercle) leading to a type III physeal injury in children (usually 12-14 years old). It’s caused by forced lateral rotation of the foot or medial rotation of leg on a fixed foot (supination external rotation injury). > 2mm displacement is an indication for ORIF. The injury comprises almost 5% of pediatric ankle fractures.

Its counterpart is Lefort Wagstaffe fracture that results when there is avulsion of anterior-inferior tibio fibular ligament from fibula.

Answer: 1
