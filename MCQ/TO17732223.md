## Question

Which is not a cause of meniscal calcification?( Arthroscopy- knee Arthroscopy- meniscus injury)

## Options

1. Trauma
2. Pseudogout
3. Ochronosis
4. Rheumatoid arthritis

## Correct option

**4. Rheumatoid arthritis**

## Answer explanation

Causes of meniscal calcification:

- Trauma
- Ochronosis
- Pseudogout
- Degenerative arthritis

Answer: 4
