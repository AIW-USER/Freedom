Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Jersey Finger

## Question

Which is the most threatening form of Jersey finger?

## Options

1. Leddy and Packer Type 1
2. Leddy and Packer Type 2
3. Leddy and Packer Type 4
4. Leddy and Packer Type 5

## Correct option

**1. Leddy and Packer Type 1**

## Answer explanation

Leddy and Packer Type 1 - FDP tendon retracts to the palm at the lumbrical origin. As there is loss of nutrient supply of tendon with rupture of both short and long vinculum, this is the most threatening type of Jersey finger. It requires early surgery to prevent necrosis of tendon (Leddy and Parker 1977)

Leddy and Packer Type 2 - FDP tendon retracts to the A3 pulley at the proximal interphalangeal (PIP) joint. In this injury there is less significant loss in nutrient supply due to preservation of long vinculum

Leddy and Packer Type 4 - Avulsion of a large bony fragment with an accompanying rupture of the FDP tendon off the bony fragment. Since the avulsed FDP is not attached to the bony fragment, the FDP retracts into the palm. Minimal damage to blood supply and minimal retraction.

Leddy and Packer Type 5 - Avulsion of a large bony fragment, accompanied by another significant fracture of the distal phalanx. Minimal damage to blood supply and minimal retraction.
