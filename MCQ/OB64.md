## Question

During total hip arthroplasty, which of the following interventions increases the risk of pulmonary ventilation-perfusion mismatch the greatest?

## Options

1. Acetabular reaming
2. Cement pressurization of the femoral canal
3. Use of a modular femoral stem
4. Intra-operative sequential compressive device on the non-operative leg
5. Posterior approach

## Correct option

**2. Cement pressurization of the femoral canal**

## Answer explanation

Cement pressurization of the femoral canal increases methylmethacrylate (MMA) and fat emboli into the venous system that can travel to the lungs.

*Elmaraghy et al examined the roles of MMA monomer and cementing technique in the formation, and hemodynamic outcome, of pulmonary fat emboli in an animal model.* Embolic particles were detected in femoral vein samples when cement pressurization was used. Intramedullary lavage before cementing had no significant effect on the embolic particle level. Hemodynamic evidence of pulmonary embolism was noted with reaming and intramedullary canal preparation.

*Pellegrini et al reviewed more than 1000 cases of THA to evaluate the efficacy of post-operative venograms to diagnose DVT.* Greater than 20% of patients had evidence of DVT although few of these were symptomatic. **The authors suggested** that post-operative venogram may be useful to shorten the time period of DVT chemoprophylaxis following THA.
