Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Fracture Dislocations of Hand

## Question

What structure is most often injured in a volar proximal interphalangeal joint dislocation?

## Options

1. Sagittal bands
2. Central slip
3. Lumbrical
4. Juncturae tendinum

## Correct option

**2. Central slip**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Closed ruptures of the central slip of the extensor tendon may occur with volar proximal interphalangeal joint dislocation, forced flexion of the proximal interphalangeal joint, or blunt trauma to the dorsum of the proximal interphalangeal joint. The other structures are not typically injured in proximal interphalangeal joint dislocations. Treatment typically requires static splinting of the proximal interphalangeal joint. In the more common dorsal proximal interphalangeal joint dislocation, the volar plate is injured, and early range of motion may be started after reduction.

Answer: 2
