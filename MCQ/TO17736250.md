## Question

In inversion injuries of ankle, sequence of ligament disruption is (regional orthopedics- foot and ankle-lateral ankle ligament injuries)

## Options

1. Anterolateral capsule, ATFL, CFL, and PTFL
2. ATFL,CFL,anterolateral capsule and PTFL
3. ATFL,PTFL,CFL and anterolateral capsule
4. ATFL, anterolateral capsule, CFL and PTFL

## Correct option

**1. Anterolateral capsule, ATFL, CFL, and PTFL**

## Answer explanation

In inversion injuries of the ankle classically, the first structures to tear are the anterolateral ankle joint capsule followed by the anterior talofibular, calcaneofibular and posterior talofibular ligaments.

Reference: Zehava S. Rosenberg, Javier Beltran, and Jenny T. Bencardino. MR Imaging of the Ankle and Foot https://doi.org/10.1148/radiographics.20.suppl_1.g00oc26s153

Answer : A
