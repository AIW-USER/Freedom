Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Radial Head and Neck Fracture

## Question

Degree of angulation acceptable in radial neck fracture in ten-year-old child is (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. <10-degree angulation and <2mm translation
2. < 20-degree angulation and <3mm translation
3. <30-degree angulation and <2mm translation
4. <15-degree angulation and <3mm translation

## Correct option

**3. <30-degree angulation and <2mm translation**

## Answer explanation

In children up to 30-degree angulation and 3 mm translation in fracture neck of radius is acceptable.

Fracture neck of radius: points to remember-

Radio capitellar or Greenspan view is oblique lateral view of the elbow to see the radial head/neck fracture

Treatment: No reduction and only long arm cast immobilization is required if angulation is less than 30 degrees and translation is less than 2-3mm.

Close reduction and cast immobilization if angulation are more than 30 degrees and translation is more than 2-3mm.

Reduction technique:

- Israeli (Kaufman) technique: elbow flexed to 90 degrees, apply direct pressure on the radial head and pronate the supinated forearm
- Patterson maneuver: distal traction on the fully extended elbow with forearm in supination. pull the forearm into varus while applying direct pressure over the radial head
- Nehar and Torch technique: elbow held in extension and supination with distal traction and varus force with assistant pushing laterally on radial shaft and surgeon pushing medially on radial head

Close reduction and percutaneous pinning:

- Metaizeau technique: retrograde pinning technique.
- Joystick technique with k wire

Answer: 3
