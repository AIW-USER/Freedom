## Question

A 14-year-old hurdler has the immediate onset of right hip pain after his trailing limb struck a hurdle and he falls down. Figure A demonstrates a right hip AP and lateral radiograph. The muscle originating at the injured structure is innervated by which of the following nerves?

![[q4390-a-avulsion-fracture-of-aiis-0-1.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Genitofemoral nerve
2. Femoral nerve
3. Pudendal nerve
4. Obturator nerve
5. Superior gluteal nerve

## Correct option

**2. Femoral nerve**

## Answer explanation

The clinical presentation is consistent with rectus femoris avulsion of the anterior inferior iliac spine (AIIS). The rectus femoris muscle is innervated by the femoral nerve.

Apophyseal avulsion injuries most often occur in adolescent athletes. The most common sites of avulsion include the ischium (hamstring/ adductor muscles), the anterior superior iliac spine (sartorius), the anterior inferior iliac spine (rectus femoris), the iliac crest (abdominals), and the lesser trochanter (iliopsoas).

*Rossi et al. performed a Level 4 review of pelvic apophyseal fractures.* **They reported** 203 avulsion fractures over a period of 22 years. The ischial tuberosity was involved in approximately 50% of the cases followed by the AIIS(25%), anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS)(20%), superior corner of pubic symphysis(4%)and iliac crest (1%).

*Holden et al. present a Level 5 review of pediatric pelvic high energy trauma.* *They summarized that avulsion fractures, iliac wing fractures (type II), and iliac apophyseal separations can be appropriately treated symptomatically.* This regimen consists of protected weight bearing for 2 to 4 weeks, followed by a stretching and strengthening program. **They suggest** that children can resume normal activity at approximately 2 months.

Figure A demonstrates AP and frog lateral radiographs of the hip with an AIIS avulsion. Illustration A depicts the locations of the common avulsion sites and their muscle associations.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: The genitofemoral nerve innervates the cremaster muscle in males.
Answer 3: The pudendal nerve innervates the levator ani muscles. It also innervates the bulbocavernosus and ischiocavernous muscle in males.
Answer 4: The obturator nerve innervates obturator externus, adductor longus, adductor brevis, and gracilis.
Answer 5: The superior gluteal nerve nerve innervates the gluteus medius,
gluteus minimus, and tensor fascia lata.
