## Question

Which of the following translocations is commonly associated with a sarcoma that is noted to have lymphatic metastasis?

## Options

1. t(16;17)
2. t(11;22)
3. t(2;13)
4. t(12;16)
5. t(9;22)

## Correct option

**3. t(2;13)**

## Answer explanation

The t(2;13) translocation is associated with alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma which has been known to have a high risk of lymphatic metastasis.

Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma occurs most commonly in patients 15 to 40 years of age. It is very commonly associated with the t(2;13) translocation which forms Pax3-FKHR fusion protein. The histology generally consists of poorly differentiated round cells with multinucleated giant cells. The treatment generally consists of wide resection + chemotherapy with a 5-year survival of approximately 60%. Rhabdomyosarcoma is one of 5 sarcomas that have a tendency to metastasize to lymph nodes. The others include clear cell sarcoma, angiosarcoma, epitheliod sarcoma, and synovial sarcoma.

*Dall'Igna et al. reviewed the importance of sentinel lymph node biopsy (SLNB) for staging in soft tissue sarcomas.* **They note** that the soft tissue sarcomas most commonly seen to metastasize to lymph nodes are rhabdomyosarcoma, synovial sarcoma, epithelioid sarcoma, angiosarcoma, and clear cell sarcoma. The authors report on 29 patients who were evaluated with SLNB in a 12-year span and **they noted** that SLNB was a feasible and reliable technique that allowed a targeted sampling of regional lymph nodes. This technique avoids aggressive operations or random biopsies, leading to an accurate staging without complications.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: The t(16;17) translocation is the most commonly reported translocation in an aneurysmal bone cyst which is not a sarcoma.
Answer 2: The t(11:22) translocation is associated with Ewing's sarcoma which does not generally metastasize to lymph nodes
Answer 4: The t(12;16) translocation is associated with myxoid liposarcoma which does not generally metastasize to lymph nodes
Answer 5: The t(9:22) translocation is associated with chronic myeloid leukemia (CML)
