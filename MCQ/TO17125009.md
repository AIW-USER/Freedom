## Question

Correct pattern of closure of distal tibial physis is

## Options

1. Anteromedially- posteromedially- laterally- centrally
2. Centrally - posteromedially- laterally- anteromedially
3. Centrally - anteromedially- posteromedially-laterally
4. Anteromedially- posteromedially- centrally- laterally

## Correct option

**3. Centrally - anteromedially- posteromedially-laterally**

## Answer explanation

Physeal closure occurs in a predictable pattern beginning centrally and progresses anteromedially, posteromedially, and finally laterally.

Answer: 3
