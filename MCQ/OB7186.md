## Question

Doxorubicin is often used as a chemotherapeutic agent for treatment of bone and soft-tissue sarcomas. Which of the following side effects limits its use?

## Options

1. Cardiotoxicity
2. Neurotoxicity
3. Cystitis
4. Mucositis
5. Ototoxicity

## Correct option

**1. Cardiotoxicity**

## Answer explanation

Doxorubicin is an anthracycline that intercalates in DNA base pairs and inhibits topoisomerase II, preventing DNA from unwinding and initiating transcription. Cardiotoxicity is common with this agent, especially with cumulative doses of more than 450 mg/m2. Patients should have a cardiac Multiple Uptake Gated Acquisition (MUGA) scan prior to initiating therapy and in follow-up to assess and monitor for possible cardiotoxicity. Doxorubicin is often combined with other agents in the treatment of sarcomas, due to inferior efficacy as single agent treatment. Cisplatin is associated with ototoxicity and neurotoxicity. Methotrexate is associated with mucositis. Ifosfamide is associated with cystitis.
