## Question

Burk schaffers approach is for (regional orthopedics- knee- surgical approaches)

## Options

1. ACL avulsion fixation
2. PCL avulsion
3. TA repair
4. ACL reconstruction

## Correct option

**2. PCL avulsion**

## Answer explanation

Burk and Schaffers approach is a posteromedial approach for PCL avulsion fixation.

The patient is positioned in a prone position. Skin incision is made over the posterior aspect of the knee with the horizontal limb over the popliteal crease and vertical limb on the medial aspect of gastrocnemius. The deep fascia over the medial gastrocnemius is incised and the interval between medial gastrocnemius and semimembranosus tendon is identified. The dissection is carried bluntly with finger until the posterior capsule of the knee joint is reached. The middle geniculate artery is ligated wherever necessary. The motor branch of the tibial nerve to the medial head of gastrocnemius is preserved. The medial gastrocnemius is retracted laterally, thus protecting the neurovascular structures. Capsule is incised vertically to reach the fracture.

Answer: 2
