## Question

Which of the following design features is most helpful in preventing osteolysis around distal femoral uncemented stem?

## Options

1. Metaphyseal filling
2. Diaphyseal filling
3. Circumferential porous coating
4. Collared stem with tapered end

## Correct option

**3. Circumferential porous coating**

## Answer explanation

Explanation:

How to prevent distal femur osteolysis

•⁠ ⁠Long stem
•⁠ ⁠Proximal coated stem
•⁠ ⁠Collared stem
•⁠ ⁠Fully coated stem

Regarding femoral stem characteristic to avoid distal femur osteolysis- Proximal femur circumferential coating (proximal 33-40% of stem) should be done. This will prevent migration of PE wear particles to lower canal limiting osteolysis.

PE particles travel anywhere within the effective joint space by the simple rule of path of least resistance. PE particles will move along any smooth surface no matter how tight the implant fills. Circumferential porous coating tends to “seal” the implant-bone interface. This protects against pumping of PE particles to the stem tip region.

Answer: 3
