## Question

Following fixation of a comminuted both-bone forearm fracture, the patient has weakness of the flexor pollicis longus and flexor digitorum profundus to the index finger. Which of the following structures has most likely been injured?

## Options

1. Anterior interosseous nerve
2. Posterior interosseous nerve
3. Radial nerve
4. Ulnar nerve
5. Lateral antebrachial cutaneous nerve

## Correct option

**1. Anterior interosseous nerve**

## Answer explanation

The anterior interosseous nerve innervates the flexor pollicis longus (FPL) and the flexor digitorum profundus (FDP) to the index finger. It branches posteriorly from the median nerve deep to the pronator teres where it is susceptible to injury. The posterior interosseous nerve, radial nerve, or ulnar nerve do not innervate the FPL or FDP to the index finger. The lateral antebrachial cutaneous nerve is a sensory nerve.
