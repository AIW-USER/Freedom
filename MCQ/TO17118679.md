Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — ACL

## Question

How long after ACL graft placement in bony tunnel fibrovascular interface is seen between graft and bone?

## Options

1. 6 weeks
2. 12 weeks
3. 6 months
4. 3 weeks

## Correct option

**2. 12 weeks**

## Answer explanation

The findings of the systematic reviews indicate that a fibrovascular interface is formed between tendon graft and bone tunnel at an early stage of 3 months. A fibrous interface in continuous contact with the bone tunnel can be expected at around 6 months. An indirect insertion, characterizing Sharpey-like fibers, tended to be matured after 10 months.

Answer: 2
