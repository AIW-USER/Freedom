## Question

Which of the following AV malformations do not have intervening brain parenchyma

## Options

1. Cryptic AV malformation
2. Cavernous hemangioma
3. Capillary telangiectasia
4. Developmental venous anomalies

## Correct option

**2. Cavernous hemangioma**

## Answer explanation

Cavernous Hemangioma or cavernoma do not have intervening brain parenchyma.

Answer: 2
