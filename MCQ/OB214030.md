## Question

DNA methylation in periarticular cells, such as chondrocytes and synoviocytes, may play a role in the development and progression of osteoarthritis by modulating the expression of cartilage constituents, such as proteases. These changes, which do not involve DNA mutation, are an example of which of the following:

## Options

1. Chromosome translocation
2. Hybridization
3. Transformation
4. Transgenesis
5. Epigenetics

## Correct option

**5. Epigenetics**

## Answer explanation

Epigenetic changes result in phenotype alterations that do not involve modifications to the underlying DNA sequence.

Epigenetic influences include genetic alterations from developmental or environmental cues that do not involve DNA mutations. These mechanisms include DNA methylations, histone modification, nucleosome location, and noncoding RNA. Epigenetics is thought to play a role in the development and progression of osteoarthritis. In addition, genomic imprinting is an epigenetic phenomenon that causes genes to be expressed in a specific manner (i.e., Prader-Willi and Angelman syndromes).

*Shen et al. reviewed recent progress in osteoarthritis research and* **note** growing evidence that suggests the gene expression profile can be largely regulated by epigenetic machinery. DNA methylation signatures in both synoviocytes and chondrocytes have been revealed in patients with osteoarthritis and have been observed during the development and progression of the disease. **The authors conclude** that these findings indicate a role of epigenetic changes in the pathophysiology, susceptibility, and severity of osteoarthritis.

*Delgado-Calle et al. revealed differentially methylated regions in osteoporosis and osteoarthritis using genome-wide profiling.* **The authors note** that epigenetic mechanisms, specifically DNA methylation, modulates expression of proteases and other cartilage constituents. This suggests these mechanisms may be involved in osteoarthritis. **They conclude** these findings suggest the existence of a developmental component in the predisposition of these disorders.

Incorrect Answers
Answer 1: Chromosome translocations involve the rearrangement of chromosomes. This results in expression or non-expression of genes and the creation of fusion proteins.
Answer 2: Hybridization refers to the binding or annealing of complementary DNA or RNA sequences.
Answer 3: Transformation is the genetic alteration of a cell as a result of the incorporation of exogenous genetic material.
Answer 4: Transgenesis is the process of artificially incorporating genes from one organism into the genome of another organism with the aim of the transgenic organism to exhibit a new property or characteristic.
