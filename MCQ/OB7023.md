## Question

With respect to the safety of allogeneic transfusions, which of the following infectious diseases is not tested for during routine laboratory blood screening?

## Options

1. Human immunodeficiency virus (HIV)
2. Hepatitis C
3. Lyme disease
4. Syphilis
5. West Nile virus encephalitis

## Correct option

**3. Lyme disease**

## Answer explanation

Routine screening of donated blood usually includes screening for Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, syphilis, HIV-1 (responsible for AIDS), HIV-2, and West Nile virus. Several bloodborne diseases that are not routinely screened for during blood donation include Lyme disease, malaria, babesiosis, and Chagas disease. Because routine blood bank practices do not include screening for all potential bloodborne diseases, surgeons should be aware of the possibility of disease transmission.
