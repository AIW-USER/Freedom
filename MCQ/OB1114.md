## Question

Which of the following is a phenomenon whereby the symptoms of a genetic disorder become apparent at an earlier age as it is passed on to the next generation?

## Options

1. Genetic drift
2. Expansion
3. Mendelian inheritance
4. Anticipation
5. Phenotypic plasticity

## Correct option

**4. Anticipation**

## Answer explanation

Anticipation is a phenomenon whereby the symptoms of a genetic disorder become apparent at an earlier age as it is passed on to the next generation. In most cases, an increase of severity of symptoms is also noted. Anticipation is common in trinucleotide repeat disorders such as Huntington's disease, myotonic dystrophy, Friedreich ataxia, and Fragile X syndrome.
