## Question

The peroneus tertius is a commonly used landmark for arthroscopic portal placement. What is the function of this tendon?

## Options

1. Dorsiflexion
2. Eversion
3. Dorsiflexion and eversion
4. Fifth toe extension
5. Lesser toe extension

## Correct option

**3. Dorsiflexion and eversion**

## Answer explanation

The peroneus tertius, although absent in 10% of the population, originates on the distal third of the extensor surface of the fibula and inserts onto the base of the fifth metatarsal, possibly extending to the fascia over the fourth interosseous space. The muscle is located in the anterior compartment of the leg and is innervated by the deep peroneal nerve. The tendon produces dorsiflexion and eversion when walking and can be used as an insertion point during tendon transfers to assist dorsiflexion. This tendon is peculiar to humans and is a proximally migrated deep extensor of the fifth toe.
