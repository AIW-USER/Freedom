## Question

Which of the following materials below exhibits solely osteoinductive properties?

## Options

1. Freeze dried allograft
2. Platelet rich plasma
3. Calcium triphosphate
4. Iliac crest autograft
5. Demineralized bone matrix

## Correct option

**2. Platelet rich plasma**

## Answer explanation

Of the above materials, the only one that exhibits osteoinductive properties only is platelet rich plasma (PRP).

PRP is solely osteoinductive, with the advantage of using the host as a donor source, which avoids bone graft donor site morbidity. Furthermore, PRP provides numerous growth factors needed for bone healing, including those that are normally released at the fracture site by degranulating platelets. Unfortunately, many clinical studies have not demonstrated the same success with the use of PRP as has been seen with other osteoinductive agents like BMP.

*Sen et al. investigated reviewed the use of iliac crest bone graft still as the gold standard for treating nonunions.* **They reported** that nonunion treatment has traditionally included iliac crest autograft to augment biology. **They however noted** the associated morbidity with harvesting of the graft, and highlighted alternatives such as bone marrow aspirate, platelet-rich plasma, and allograft.

*Mahendra et al. reviewed available biological treatments for complex nonunions.* **They reported** that most cases of aseptic nonunions require some degree of biological augmentation, of which autologous iliac crest bone graft remains the gold standard. **They highlighted** that various scaffolds,
demineralized bone matrices (DBM) and BMPs are commercially available but
need further refining and that stem cell and gene therapy are evolving and likely to impact significantly in the next few decades.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Freeze dried allograft (i.e. croutons) are purely osteoconductive.
Answer 3: Calcium triphosphate is a good graft when trying to resist compressive forces given its osteoconductive property.
Answer 4: Iliac crest autograft exhibits osteoinductive, osteogenic, AND osteoconductive properties.
Answer 5: Demineralized bone matrix exhibits both osteoinductive AND osteoconductive properties.
