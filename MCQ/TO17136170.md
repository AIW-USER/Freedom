## Question

Which process of doping control program is shown below?

![[TO17136170-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. sealing of partial sample urine
2. measuring specific gravity
3. provision of sample
4. sealing of urine sample

## Correct option

**1. sealing of partial sample urine**

## Answer explanation

Sample Processing

After the sample is secured, the athlete and the DCO or chaperone will proceed back to the doping control station to begin the sample processing part of sample collection.

If there is enough urine provided, the player goes back to the doping control room and collects the interim sealing device, checks the number, opens the bottle and pours the additional urine sample into the initial sample, mix it with the previous urine.

The athlete will first be asked to choose a sample collection kit containing the bottles in which the sample will be sent to the lab. Similar to the way the vessel was chosen, the athlete will choose from a choice of sample collection boxes and ensure that the container is not damaged or tampered with. The athlete will open the sample collection kit and inspect the contents of the package. Both the DCO and athlete will ensure that all sample code numbers on the bottles and sample collection kit box match.The athlete will then divide the sample between the B Sample and A Sample bottles and seal them. The DCO will not handle any of the equipment during the procedure unless requested to do so by the athlete. The athlete is to maintain control of the sample until the sample is sealed.

Partial urine sample:

White lid is usually applied when sample of Urine is Partial i.e less than 90 ml. So whatever sample athlete had provided we asked him to fill it in vial B ( out of 2 vial i.e given A & B) Athlete will fill urine in sample b and then select partial kit and take out the contents. This white Cork/lid is one of the components of Partial Sample.

So that vial used or partial sample won't seal the sample. Vial name is “BERLINGER “ vial which gets locked if we closed it rotating in circular motions for 5-6 times
