## Question

Burosumab has fundamentally altered the management of X-linked hypophosphatemia (XLH). What is the precise molecular target and mechanism of action of this biologic agent?

## Options

1. It acts as a pharmacological chaperone, restoring the function of the mutated PHEX enzyme.
2. It is a fully human monoclonal antibody that binds to and neutralizes circulating Fibroblast Growth Factor 23.
3. It is an agonist at the Klotho-FGFR1 complex in the proximal renal tubule.
4. It upregulates the sodium-phosphate cotransporter (NaPi-IIa) directly via intracellular signaling.

## Correct option

**2. It is a fully human monoclonal antibody that binds to and neutralizes circulating Fibroblast Growth Factor 23.**

## Answer explanation

Burosumab directly binds to excess circulating FGF23, neutralizing it. This lifts the inhibition on renal phosphate reabsorption and allows for normal 1-alpha-hydroxylase activity.

Answer: 2
