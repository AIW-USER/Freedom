Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Avascular Necrosis Of Hip (Avn)

## Question

True about safe dislocation of the hip is

## Options

1. Anterior dislocation and posterior arthrotomy
2. Posterior dislocation and anterior arthrotomy
3. Posterior dislocation and posterior arthrotomy
4. Anterior dislocation and anterior arthrotomy

## Correct option

**4. Anterior dislocation and anterior arthrotomy**

## Answer explanation

Answer : D

Ganz is Posterior approach -> Trochanteric osteotomy -> Anterior arthrotomy -> Anterior dislocation of hip

The approach preserves the deep branch of medial circumflex femoral artery allowing for a safe surgical dislocation
