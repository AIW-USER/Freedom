## Question

A 40-year-old female has carpal tunnel syndrome with motor and sensory symptoms of long duration. There is weakness in the abduction of the thumb. You decided for surgery, carpal tunnel release and tendon transfer. Which tendon transfer will you prefer?

## Options

1. Burkhalter
2. Riordan
3. Camitz
4. Jones

## Correct option

**3. Camitz**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Burkhalter- Extensor Indicis Proprius to Abductor pollicis brevis

Riordan- FDS (ring finger slip) transfer for opposition

Camitz- Palmaris longus to Abductor pollicis brevis (preferred in carpal tunnel syndrome, ease of taking palmaris longus tendon through same incision)

Jones- for radial nerve palsy

Answer: 3
