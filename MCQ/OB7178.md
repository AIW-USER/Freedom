## Question

What modulator of inflammatory is most closely associated with a herniated nucleus propulsus?

## Options

1. TNF-alpha
2. TGF-beta
3. IL-10
4. MMP-1
5. IF-gamma

## Correct option

**1. TNF-alpha**

## Answer explanation

Proinflammatory cytokines play an important role in the pathophysiology of pain associated with a herniated disk. Several cytokines have been detected in both in vitro and in vivo models including TNF-alpha, IL-1, and IL-8 among others. Of these, TNF-alpha has been consistently implicated as the main offender among inflammatory modulators potentially responsible for nerve root pain in the setting of a herniated disk.
