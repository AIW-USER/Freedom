Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Congenital Muscular Torticollis

## Question

Treatment of choice for congenital muscular torticollis in infancy is

## Options

1. Unipolar release
2. Bipolar release
3. Bipolar release with SCM Z lengthening
4. Passive stretching

## Correct option

**4. Passive stretching**

## Answer explanation

Treatment for congenital muscular torticollis in infancy is passive stretching. 90% respond to passive stretching of the sternocleidomastoid in the first year of life. Bipolar release of SCM or Z plastic lengthening is indicated after failed response to at least 1 year of stretching

Answer: 4
