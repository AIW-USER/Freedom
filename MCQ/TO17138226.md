Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Congenital Vertical Talus (CVT)

## Question

Which approach gives the least incidence of avascular necrosis in congenital vertical talus? (FNB Pattern Paper 2014)

## Options

1. Extensile
2. Sinus tarsi
3. Posteromedial
4. Dorsal

## Correct option

**4. Dorsal**

## Answer explanation

Compared to the posteromedial approach, least chances of avascular necrosis have been reported with the dorsal approach. Extensile and sinus tarsi approaches are not used for vertical talus.

Answer: 4
