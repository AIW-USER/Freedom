## Question

What is the most common form of child maltreatment (abuse)?

## Options

1. Physical
2. Neglect
3. Sexual
4. Emotional
5. Financial desertion

## Correct option

**2. Neglect**

## Answer explanation

Unfortunately, child maltreatment is common and has potentially deadly consequences. A common presentation of child abuse is fractures, and therefore the orthopaedic surgeon must remain vigilant. Although physical abuse may potentially have the highest morbidity, neglect is the most common form of child maltreatment.
