## Question

The usual presentation of traumatic subscapularis tears is most often seen after forced

## Options

1. internal rotation.
2. external rotation.
3. extension.
4. abduction.
5. forward flexion.

## Correct option

**2. external rotation.**

## Answer explanation

The typical mechanism of injury is a fall and the patient grasps something to prevent the fall. This maneuver forces the arm into external rotation against resistance.
