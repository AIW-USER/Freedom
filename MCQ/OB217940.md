## Question

A 52-year-old patient with a bleeding disorder is scheduled to undergo a total knee arthroplasty. Due to his known disorder, pneumatic compression without chemical venous thromboembolic prophylaxis is used postoperatively. Which of the following inheritance patterns would be consistent with the diagnosis of factor VIII deficiency in this patient?

## Options

1. Autosomal dominant
2. Autosomal recessive
3. Spontaneous
4. X-linked dominant
5. X-linked recessive

## Correct option

**5. X-linked recessive**

## Answer explanation

Hemophilia is a congenital bleeding disorder that results from an X-linked recessive mutation leading to a decrease in clotting factor VIII or factor IX.

Hemophilia patients present with excessive bleeding into joints, most commonly the knee. The diagnosis is made with a plasma factor VIII or IX inhibitor assay. Treatment is medical management with pain management and clotting factor administration. For patients with hemophilia, a pneumatic compression device alone is recommended over pharmacoprophylaxis to minimize risk for excessive bleeding and wound complications. In addition to factor VIII deficiency (hemophilia), active liver disease is another condition for which support is strongest to withhold anticoagulation.

*Mont et al. reviewed the various aspects of patient screening, risk factor assessment, and prophylactic treatment against venous thromboembolic (VTE) disease.* **They reported** that patients should be assessed for known bleeding disorders, such as hemophilia, and for the presence of active liver disease. **They concluded** that patients who are not at elevated risk of VTE or for bleeding should receive pharmacologic prophylaxis and mechanical compressive devices for the prevention of VTE.

*Falck-Ytter et al. reviewed the optimal prophylaxis to reduce postoperative pulmonary embolism and DVT.* *They reported on the use of several antithrombotic prophylaxis combinations for patients undergoing total hip arthroplasty or total knee arthroplasty.* **They concluded** that for patients without bleeding disorders, the optimal strategies for thromboprophylaxis after major orthopedic surgery include pharmacologic and mechanical approaches.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1-4: Factor VIII deficiency (Hemophilia) is an X-linked recessive mutation.
