Chapter: Spine MCQs — Cervical Disc Herniation And Myelopathy

## Question

A cervical disk herniation that causes weakness in the wrist extensors will likely produce sensory changes in the:

## Options

1. Lateral arm
2. Radial forearm
3. Ulnar forearm
4. Hypothenar eminence

## Correct option

**2. Radial forearm**

## Answer explanation

The wrist extensors are innervated by C 6. Weakness would likely be the result of a C 5-C 6 cervical disk herniation, thus causing a C6 radiculopathy. The dermatome of C 6 includes the radial side of the forearm and radial digits.

Answer: 2
