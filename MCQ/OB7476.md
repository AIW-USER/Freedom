## Question

An axial MRI scan of an ankle is shown in Figure 88. The arrow indicates what tendinous structure?

![[q7476-a-88.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Posterior tibial
2. Peroneus longus
3. Peroneus brevis
4. Flexor digitorum longus
5. Flexor hallucis longus

## Correct option

**5. Flexor hallucis longus**

## Answer explanation

The image shown is a transverse cut of the left ankle that shows the distal tibia metaphysis and the proximal medial malleolus, the lateral malleolus, and the surrounding soft tissues. The structure highlighted by the arrow is the flexor hallucis longus tendon that travels along the posterior tibia, just anterior to the Achilles tendon. The peroneal tendons lie behind the fibula, and the posterior tibial and flexor digitorum longus tendons travel posterior to the medial malleolus.
