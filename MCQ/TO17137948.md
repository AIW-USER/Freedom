## Question

Vascular supply of Latissimus dorsi flap is characterized by

## Options

1. Single major vascular pedicle
2. One dominant pedicle with additional minor pedicles
3. Single dominant pedicle with multiple secondary segmental pedicles
4. Two dominant pedicles

## Correct option

**3. Single dominant pedicle with multiple secondary segmental pedicles**
