## Question

A patient has a humeral shaft fracture and is scheduled to undergo open reduction and internal fixation with a plate. What surgical approach will provide the greatest amount of exposure?

## Options

1. Modified posterior approach with elevation of the medial and lateral heads of the triceps
2. Posterior triceps-splitting approach
3. Posterior triceps-splitting approach with radial nerve mobilization
4. Posteromedial approach
5. Lateral approach with radial nerve mobilization

## Correct option

**1. Modified posterior approach with elevation of the medial and lateral heads of the triceps**

## Answer explanation

The modified posterior approach with elevation of the medial and lateral heads of the triceps can provide exposure of 94% of the humeral shaft. The traditional posterior triceps-splitting approach exposes 55% of the humeral shaft.
