Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Knee Kinematics

## Question

In the screw home mechanism (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. When foot plantigrade- Femur rotates externally during flexion
2. When foot plantigrade- tibia rotate internally during flexion
3. When foot in the air- tibia rotates externally during flexion
4. When foot in the air- femur rotates internally during flexion

## Correct option

**1. When foot plantigrade- Femur rotates externally during flexion**

## Answer explanation

Screw home mechanism: “Screw Home” rotation allows full knee extension and flexion. In open chain (foot in the air) knee extension during the last 30 degrees of knee extension, the tibia rotates externally (about 10 degrees) while in close chain (foot on the ground) extension femur rotates internally (about 10 degrees) to lock the knee. In order to unlock or flex the knee from extension, the popliteus muscle initiates internal rotation of tibia (in open chain) or external rotation of femur (in close chain).

Factors causing screw home mechanism:

1. Medial femoral condyle has a 30 degree lateral curve and the length of the medial femoral condyle is longer than the length of the lateral condyle this allows for 10-15 degree external rotation of the tibia during the last 20° of extension

2. The lateral pull of the quadriceps causes an anterior translation of the tibia on the femur. This creates tension in the ACL which contributes to external rotation.

Answer: 1
