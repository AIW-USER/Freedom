## Question

Grand piano sign is related to

## Options

1. Hip
2. Knee
3. Humerus
4. Tibia

## Correct option

**2. Knee**

## Answer explanation

The appearance of the 'grand-piano sign' on the anterior resected surface of the femur has been considered to be a marker for correct femoral rotational alignment during total knee replacement.
