## Question

A 15-year-old male presents 6 months after undergoing below-knee amputation for synovial sarcoma involving neurovascular structures. The patient has been doing extremely well postoperatively and has started using a prosthesis for the past few months. His mother is concerned, however, because he has pain over his lateral knee and medial distal residual limb at the end of a long day of wear. This pain prevents him from wearing the prosthesis the following day. What adjustment is likely needed to improve prosthesis alignment?

## Options

1. Increase lateral offset of the foot piece
2. Reduce lateral offset of the foot piece
3. Increase anterior positioning of the foot
4. Decreasing anterior position of the foot
5. This is normal and resolves with continued prosthesis use

## Correct option

**2. Reduce lateral offset of the foot piece**

## Answer explanation

This patient's presentation is consistent with a prosthesis with too much lateral offset and would be improved by medializing the foot.

Prosthesis alignment is critical for patient comfort and the proper function of a prosthesis. There are several different styles of foot design for below-knee prosthesis, each with its own advantages and disadvantages, that need the proper placement to have the maximal function of the prosthesis. An inset (medialized) foot causes varus strain and a circumduction gait. An outset (lateralized) foot causes valgus strain and a broad-based gait. An anterior foot causes increased knee extension and patellar pain, while a posterior foot can cause knee buckling and unstable increased knee flexion.

*Davenport et al. performed a systematic review examing transtibial prosthetic socket pressures as device alignment changes.* *They provide an exhaustive description and understanding of the eight different alignment adjustments possible.* **They conclude** there is greater sensitivity to angular changes than translational, pressure response to alignment changes are patient specific, and changes in one direction are not necessarily symmetric to changes in the opposite direction.

*Armitage et al. investigated the clinical utility of pressure feedback.* **They found** that pressure feedback data helped reduce pressure in three of four anatomic locations tested. Additionally, it created more consistent pressure measurements between individual prosthetists involved in the study. **They conclude** that pressure measurements provide clinically useful data in socket design and fabrication.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Increasing lateral offset would exacerbate the current issue with prosthetic alignment
Answer 3 and 4: This patient's malalignment is in the coronal plane, not in the sagittal plane, which would be addressed with these answer choices.
Answer 5: Prosthesis fit is important for use and proper function. A painful prosthesis needs to be evaluated and adjusted to improve patient comfort.
