## Question

Which of the following is NOT true regarding Internal Acoustic Meatus?

## Options

1. Nervus intermedius passes along with the facial nerve through the IAM
2. Cochlear nerve passes through superior & medial compartment of IAM
3. Bill’s bar seperates the facial and vestibular compartments of the IAM
4. Bill’s bar lies deeper in the IAM than the transverse crest

## Correct option

**2. Cochlear nerve passes through superior & medial compartment of IAM**

## Answer explanation

answer-b

The cochlear nerve passes through the inferior and medial (aka anterior) compartment of IAM. The internal acoustic meatus (or Porus acousticus) is located in the petrous part of the temporal bone. It is divided into superior and inferior compartments by a transverse crest.

The superior compartment is further divided into medial (or anterior) and lateral (or posterior) compartments by a bony bar known as Bill’s bar (after Dr William House). The Bill’s bar lies deeper in the IAM than the transverse crest. The vestibular nerves occupy the lateral (posterior compartments). The superior vestibular nerve passes above and the inferior vestibular nerve passes below the transverse crest. The medial (anterior) compartments contain the Facial nerve and Nervus Intermedius in the superior compartment, and Cochlear nerve in the inferior compartment. The mnemonic is ‘7 up and Coke down’.
