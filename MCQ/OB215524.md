## Question

Which of the following is associated with reduced mortality and complication rates in the treatment of hip fractures?

## Options

1. Preventing patient fall events by delaying full postoperative weightbearing
2. Surgical treatment within 24 hours of admission
3. Preoperative echocardiogram and cardiology clearance
4. Use of general anesthesia rather than neuraxial anesthesia
5. Avoidance of tranexamic acid (TXA) perioperatively

## Correct option

**2. Surgical treatment within 24 hours of admission**

## Answer explanation

Surgical treatment of hip fractures with 24 hours of admission has been associated with decreased mortality and complication rates.

Hip fractures are common injuries amongst the geriatric population due to ground-level falls. Mortality rates as high as 45% have been noted in patients with comorbid renal disease. Several factors have been associated with decreased mortality in the treatment of these injuries, including early surgical intervention (<24 hours from admission), use of neuraxial anesthesia, perioperative use of tranexamic acid, and early mobilization post-op.

*Pincus et al. performed a retrospective study of 42,320 patients undergoing hip fracture surgery in Ontario, Canada.* *Patients experiencing surgical delay greater than 24 hours had significantly increased incidence of 30-day mortality, 90-day mortality, and 365-day mortality, as well as pulmonary embolism, myocardial infarction, and pneumonia.* **They concluded** that 24 hours appears to be a threshold to reduce complications and mortality related to hip fracture care.

*Simunovic et al. performed a systematic review and meta-analysis of 16 studies including 13,478 patients assessing mortality and complication rates of hip fractures treated with early surgical intervention.* With early intervention, one-year mortality was reduced by 45%, pneumonia was reduced by 41%, and pressure sores reduced by 52% using the unadjusted data from the included studies. **The authors concluded** that surgery performed within 24-72 hours for hip fractures was associated with lower mortality and certain complication rates.

Incorrect answers
Answer 1: Early ambulation following surgery is the goal of hip fracture care. Several studies have correlated early ambulation with decreased mortality rates and complications.
Answer 3: Preoperative cardiology clearance and echocardiogram have not been proven to decrease mortality rates in hip fracture care. Rather, it has been shown to delay treatment and potentially increase mortality and other adverse events.
Answer 4: The use of general anesthesia has been associated with increased mortality rates following hip fracture surgery.
Answer 5: Some studies have shown decreased 30-day mortality rates with the use of tranexamic acid.
