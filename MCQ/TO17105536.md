## Question

In severe valgus deformity, Which TKR prosthesis allows lateralization of femoral and tibial components to improve patellar tracking? (FNB Pattern Paper 2020)

## Options

1. AS
2. PS
3. CR
4. OS

## Correct option

**2. PS**

## Answer explanation

Posterior stabilized design is inherently more stable than a cruciate-retaining design as a result of the post-cam mechanism and joint surface conformity. Posterior stabilized design also allows for greater lateralization of the femoral and tibial components, which greatly improves patellar tracking and minimizes the need for lateral retinacular releases.

Answer: 2
