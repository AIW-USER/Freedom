## Question

All are included in unhappy triad of O’ Donoghue except ( Arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- multiligamentous knee injuries)

## Options

1. ACL
2. MM
3. MCL
4. PCL

## Correct option

**4. PCL**

## Answer explanation

The unhappy triad was described by American orthopaedic surgeon D H O'Donoghue, in 1950. He described simultaneous occurrence of tear of ACL, MCL and medial meniscus tear when lateral force applied to knee while the foot is fixed to the ground. However later based on the arthroscopic studies this association was questioned as lateral meniscus tear is more commonly associated with acute ACL tears.

Answer : d
