## Question

During the treatment of a trimalleolar ankle fracture, the decision is made to use Allgöwer-Donati sutures to close the wounds. What is the proven benefit of this technique compared to vertical or horizontal mattress techniques?

## Options

1. Faster closure time
2. Lower wound complications
3. Higher incision perfusion
4. Improved wound cosmesis
5. Decreased need for rotational flaps

## Correct option

**3. Higher incision perfusion**

## Answer explanation

Studies have demonstrated that Allgöwer-Donati suture techniques provide improved incisional perfusion for wound closure of ankle ORIF.

Ankle fractures requiring surgical fixation are prone to extensive wound complications. The thin soft tissue envelope compounded with extensive swelling and poor soft tissue perfusion is the proposed mechanism behind healing complications. There are multiple soft tissue closure techniques with minimal differences identified with respect to cosmesis, wound complications, infections, and the need for rotational flaps. However, Allgöwer-Donati sutures have been associated with improved incisional perfusion.

*Shannon et al. performed a prospective randomized study of 30 patients undergoing ankle ORIF with wounds closed with either Allgöwer-Donati or vertical mattress sutures.* **They reported** higher skin perfusion with Allgöwer-Donati sutures via fluorescence units (51 vs 28, p <0.0001), but with one wound complication in each group. **They concluded** that Allgöwer-Donati suture techniques afford better incisional perfusion which theoretically can reduce wound healing complications.

*Shorten et al. performed a prospective randomized study of 75 patients undergoing ankle ORIF and were closed with five different suture techniques.* **They reported** the running subcuticular pattern resulted in the highest incision perfusion and lowest mean wound perfusion impairment without differences in wound cosmesis between all techniques. **They concluded** the running subcuticular closure technique has the highest mean incisional perfusion following ankle fracture ORIF closure.

Incorrect answers
Answer 1, 2, 4, and 5: The benefit of wound closure time, wound complications, wound cosmesis, and need for rotational flaps has not been established for Allgöwer-Donati sutures.
