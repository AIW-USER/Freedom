Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Coronoid Fractures

## Question

Most common type of coronoid fracture associated with terrible triad fracture-dislocations is

## Options

1. Fracture of anteromedial facet
2. Fracture of tip of coronoid
3. Fracture of base of coronoid
4. Fracture of anterolateral facet

## Correct option

**2. Fracture of tip of coronoid**

## Answer explanation

The O'Driscoll classification of coronoid fractures includes: type I, fractures of the tip; type II, fractures involving the anteromedial facet; and type III, basal fractures.

Type I fractures are most commonly associated with terrible triad fracture-dislocations, type II is associated with varus posteromedial rotatory instability, and type III are associated with olecranon and proximal ulna fracture-dislocations.

Answer: 2
