Chapter: FNB 2017 — 2017 MCQs

## Question

Kienbock’s disease involves (regional orthopedics- hand and wrist joint- kienbock’s disease)

## Options

1. Scaphoid
2. Radial head
3. Lunate
4. Capitulum

## Correct option

**3. Lunate**

## Answer explanation

Osteochondrosis includes a group of conditions that are disorders of the growing epiphysis and hence also called as epiphysitis

Crushing Type

- Kohler’s disease: Navicula

- Kienbock’s disease: Lunate

- Panner’s disease: Capitulum

- Scherman’s disease: Ring epiphysis of vertebrae

- Calve’s disease: Central bony nucleus of vertebrae.

- Theeman’s disease: Phalangeal epiphysis.
- Freiberg’s disease: Metatarsal heads

Traction/Pulling Type

- Base of 5th metatarsal: Iselin’s disease.

- Medial epicondyle of humerus: Adam’s disease.

- Tibial tuberosity: Osgood-Schlatter’s disease

- Calcaneal epiphysis: Sever’s disease

- Patellar ligament (at its lower pole patellar attachment): Johansson-Larsen syndrome

Splitting Type

Osteochondritis dissecans

Answer: 3
