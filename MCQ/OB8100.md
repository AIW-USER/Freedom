## Question

Recurrence of hallux valgus deformity after corrective surgery has been shown to be related to which of the following?

## Options

1. Inversely correlated with presence of bipartite fibular sesamoid
2. Associated with residual increased tibial sesamoid displacement
3. Associated with squared lateral first metatarsal shape
4. Unrelated to preoperative 1-2 intermetatarsal angle
5. Unrelated to preoperative hallux valgus angle

## Correct option

**2. Associated with residual increased tibial sesamoid displacement**

## Answer explanation

*Okuda and associates have studied the factors associated with the recurrence of hallux valgus deformity following correction with proximal first metatarsal osteotomy.* The factors that **they found** associated with recurrence of deformity postoperatively are a rounded shape to the lateral first metatarsal head, severe lateral displacement of the tibial sesamoid, an increased preoperative 1-2 intermetatarsal angle, and an increased preoperative hallux valgus angle. The reported rate of recurrence of deformity after proximal first metatarsal osteotomy is 4% to 11%.
