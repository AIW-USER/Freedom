Chapter: Sports Medicine MCQs — Sports Equipment

## Question

Best clothes you would recommend to children playing in summers in Delhi

## Options

1. Light weight dry clothes
2. Moisture wicking garments
3. Light weight cotton fabrics
4. Dark and heavy clothes

## Correct option

**2. Moisture wicking garments**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

EXPLANATION: -
For children playing outside in summers in Delhi, moisture wicking fabrics will be best.
The moisture management character is extremely important for the clothing for sportswear where the high amount of dissipated moisture vapours and liquid moisture needs to be transported from the skin to the outer environment for dry feel. Moisture-wicking fabrics achieve this by virtue of "capillary action" transporting liquid moisture from the human skin to the outer environment. As the fibres keep the sweat on the surface, the sweat readily evaporates in the air and this maintains high level of comfort to the wearer. Examples of such material are polyester, polypropylene, nylon and merino wool.
Cotton fabrics on the other hand behave differently. In a T shirt that is made from a natural fabric like cotton, the moisture is absorbed into the fibres. This makes the t-shirt feel damp and heavy. Cotton is an especially bad option for a t-shirt or workout gear, as it can absorb up to 7 percent of its own weight. All that dampness held close to the body is not only uncomfortable, but it can also make it harder to cool off while exercising in the heat. And on cool, rainy days, that dampness can make one even colder.
So, in hot humid environments choosing moisture wicking fabrics is recommended as it would benefit in two ways: one is quickly moving (or wicking) sweat to the fabric's outer surface, and the other is drying rapidly so that your sweat doesn't saturate the fabric.
Here are some details of some of the best choices for moisture-wicking T-shirt materials:
• Polyester: This synthetic material is a go-to for moisture-wicking tops. And for good reason: It's durable, lightweight and breathable. Polyester (or polyester blended with nylon or elastane) is a safe choice for a wide variety of workouts and environments.
• Polypropylene: Polypropylene is another top moisture-wicker for men and women. It's lightweight, yet it offers thermal properties, making it a great choice for cold-weather gear.
• Nylon: Nylon is a breathable synthetic material that gives well for a comfortable fit. These qualities make it another common pick for moisture-wicking tops. Like polyester, nylon (or nylon blended with other sweat-wicking materials) is great for most workouts.
• Merino wool: Wool is unique. It absorbs the highest percentage of moisture for its weight, and yet, the wool won't feel damp on your skin when you sweat. The explanation lies in the shape of the fibres. While other fabrics have smooth fibres that lie flat against your skin, wool fibres are naturally crimped. This crimping creates space between the fabric and your skin, which cuts back on that damp, heavy sensation you often feel from other natural fabrics. Combine the wool fibre's shape with its warming abilities, and you've got a great material for workouts, especially cold-weather ones. Merino wool is an especially good choice as its fibres are thinner than traditional wool. Clothing and apparel made from Merino wool will be lighter than those made from other types of wool.
