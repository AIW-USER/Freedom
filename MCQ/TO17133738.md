## Question

During an anterior instrumented fusion of the lumbar spine through a left-sided retroperitoneal approach, all of the following statements are correct except?

## Options

1. The ureter is adherent to the posterior peritoneum and falls away from the psoas through the dissection.
2. The sympathetic trunk, lying longitudinally along the lateral border of the psoas, is at risk during this procedure.
3. The ilioinguinal nerve emerges from the lateral border of the psoas and travels to the quadratus lumborum.
4. A cold and pale right foot is a recognized post-operative examination finding.

## Correct option

**2. The sympathetic trunk, lying longitudinally along the lateral border of the psoas, is at risk during this procedure.**

## Answer explanation

The sympathetic trunk lies longitudinally along the medial border of the psoas. An injury to

the sympathetic chain results in a warm and red foot on the ipsilateral side to the approach

(left in this case), giving the appearance that the normal foot is cold and pale.

Answer: 2
