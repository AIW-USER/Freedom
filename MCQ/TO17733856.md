## Question

Most common location of vertebral fracture in leukemia (Spine- miscellaneous)

## Options

1. Lumbar
2. Thoracic
3. Sacral
4. Cervical

## Correct option

**2. Thoracic**

## Answer explanation

In leukemia pathological vertebral fractures are most common in thoracic followed by lumbar vertebrae.

 Answer: b
