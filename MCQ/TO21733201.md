## Question

In a 2 years old child, Osteomyelitis of bone will lead to septic arthritis in all these places except

## Options

1. Head of ulna
2. Distal end tibia
3. Neck femur
4. Radial neck

## Correct option

**1. Head of ulna**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

EXPLANATION: -
Osteomyelitis at following locations may concurrently end up with septic arthritis owing to intracapsular metaphysis: -
• Metaphysis of proximal radius
• Proximal humerus
• Proximal femur
• Distal tibia and fibula

![[TO21733201-1.jpg]]

Osteomyelitis in these locations may decompress into the joint, producing concomitant septic arthritis [From Tachdjian]
Metaphyses of the proximal radius (A), proximal humerus (B), proximal femur (C), and distal tibia-fibula (D) are intra-articular.
