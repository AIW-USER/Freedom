## Question

With regard to Footwear assessment, which of the following is false

## Options

1. Pitch of the shoe is the difference between heel height to forefoot height
2. It is advisable to increase pitch for athletes with achilles tendon issues
3. Increasing the pitch to above 12mm can help marathon runners have a midfoot strike
4. The forefoot sole flexion point should ideally line up with the first MTP joint

## Correct option

**3. Increasing the pitch to above 12mm can help marathon runners have a midfoot strike**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

EXPLANATION: -

Choice D is correct. The forefoot sole flexion point should ideally align with the first MTP joint. If too proximal it impairs the stability of the shoe. If it is too distal, it affects the sagittal plane motion of the first MTP joint.

![[TO21733139-1.png]]

So, in this question one has to evaluate choices A to C, and this requires understanding of “Shoe Pitch”.

Pitch of the shoe or heel drop is the difference between heel height to forefoot height (see diagram). The lower the pitch or the heel drop of the shoe, the closer an athlete is to bare foot running with a forefoot strike.

![[TO21733139-2.jpg]]

Nomrally, in an athlete the pitch would vary from 0-14 mm or sometimes even higher. A pitch of about 6 mm can help with midfoot strike, while a pitch as high as 12 mm will lead to heel strike pattern during running.

![[TO21733139-3.jpg]]

In athletes with Achilles tendon issues, it is advisable to give a slightly higher heel height (pitch) in order to reduce the stretch load on the Achilles. Meanwhile, one must also work on the flexibility and eccentric strength of the Achilles.
