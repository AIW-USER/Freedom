## Question

In a patient with rheumatoid arthritis of the wrist, which of the following extensor tendons is most at risk of rupture?

## Options

1. Extensor digiti quinti
2. Abductor pollicis longus
3. Extensor pollicis longus
4. Extensor carpi radialis brevis
5. Extensor carpi ulnaris

## Correct option

**1. Extensor digiti quinti**

## Answer explanation

The tendon most prone to rupture in a patient with rheumatoid arthritis of the wrist is the extensor digiti quinti. It can be a silent injury since the extensor digitorum communis can provide extension to the fifth finger. The extensor digiti quinti is at high risk since it is overlying the ulnar head where it is prone to attritional rupture (Vaughan-Jackson syndrome).
