## Question

Most common instability in RA is (spine- Rheumatoid arthritis involvement of cervical spine)

## Options

1. Glenohumeral
2. Atlanto axial
3. Subaxial
4. Lumbosacral

## Correct option

**2. Atlanto axial**

## Answer explanation

After peripheral joints, cervical spine is the 2nd most common area to be involved in RA. Chronic inflammation of the cervical spine initially leads to bony erosion and ligamentous laxity which may subsequently lead to cervical spinal instability in the form of atlantoaxial instability (AAI), cranial settling (CS), and subaxial subluxation (SAS) or a combination of the three. Additionally, RA can cause an inflammatory discitis and atraumatic odontoid erosion or fracture.

Cranial settling is caused by C1–C2 instability, leading to upward migration and/or rotation of the C2 complex into the cranial vault.

Answer: 2 (see Q 73 FNB pattern paper 1)
