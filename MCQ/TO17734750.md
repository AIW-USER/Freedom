## Question

False about hematoma block is ( general orthopedics- complications of anaesthesia)

## Options

1. Local anesthetic is injected around nerves at target site
2. It is directly given into fracture site
3. Commonly used for fracture distal end radius
4. Can be used for ankle fracture dislocation

## Correct option

**1. Local anesthetic is injected around nerves at target site**

## Answer explanation

Hematoma block is used for fracture distal end radius and fracture dislocation of ankle joint. In this local anesthetic is directly injected into the fracture site.

Answer: 1
