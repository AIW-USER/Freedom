## Question

A 32-year-old male presents as a polytrauma after being involved in a motor vehicle accident. After undergoing primary and secondary surveys, the trauma service decides to transfer the patient to a local Level 1 trauma center. Which of the following factors are associated with transfer to a Level 1 trauma center?

## Options

1. Female gender
2. Private insurance
3. Multiple medical comorbities
4. Caucasian race
5. Injury severity score < 10

## Correct option

**3. Multiple medical comorbities**

## Answer explanation

The presence of multiple medical comorbidities has been associated with transfer to a Level 1 trauma center.

Polytrauma patients presenting to level II and level III trauma centers may benefit from transfers to level I centers. Several studies have reported improved outcomes in severely traumatized patients that are transferred to level 1 trauma centers. However, unnecessary transfers to level 1 centers have been associated with African-American race, presence of medical comorbidities, Medicaid insurance, and male gender.

*Koval et al. performed a retrospective case-control national database study to assess factors associated with trauma patient transfer to level 1 centers.* **They reported** African-American race, presence of medical comorbidities, Medicaid insurance, and male gender are predictors for transfer of patients to a trauma center that have ISS scores less than 9. **They concluded** further prospective studies are needed to assess for medical and nonmedical patient factors associated with the transfer.

*Nathens et al performed a retrospective study evaluating factors associated with trauma transfer from level III/IV trauma centers to level I trauma centers.* **They found** that lack of insurance was an independent predictor for transfer to a trauma center after adjusting for differences in injury severity. An injury severity score of 36 represents a patient that has sustained life-threatening polytrauma and should be transferred to a Level 1 trauma center.

Incorrect answers
Answers 1, 2, 4, and 5: Female gender, private insurance, caucasian race, and injury severity score <10 are not associated with transfers to level 1 trauma centers.
