## Question

An athlete has a strong belief in his ability to perform successfully in his sport. This is referred to as? [Sports Medicine-Sports Psychology]

## Options

1. Vicarious Experience
2. Motivational behaviour
3. . Emotional Arousal
4. Athletic Self-efficacy

## Correct option

**4. Athletic Self-efficacy**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D

In the last few decades, sport psychology has become an important part of sports. One of the applications of psychology in sport is to help improve performance, to learn and to execute more correctly and more easily the skills, using different techniques such as mental training, self-confidence and self-efficacy.

Athletic self-efficacy essentially means feeling competent and able to deal with the problems that arise in athletic competition. Athletic self-efficacy is defined as the athlete’s belief in the ability to perform a successful operation to achieve a specific outcome in athletic performance. While on one hand, athletic self-efficacy includes the athlete’s abilities, physical and mental fitness, leadership and coaching; on the other hand, it influences the athletic performance, success, and the ability to cope with stress. In simple terms, Self-efficacy reflects athlete’s confidence in the ability to exert control over one's own motivation, behaviour, and social environment.
