## Question

In modified Thomas test if Posterior thigh (of test knee) touches the table and knee extends then what will be the interpretation

## Options

1. Shortness of two joint hip flexors- positive test
2. Shortness of one joint hip flexors- positive test
3. Shortness of both two-joint and one joint hip flexors- positive test
4. Negative test

## Correct option

**1. Shortness of two joint hip flexors- positive test**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A
EXPLANATION: -
Modified Thomas test: The patient is placed in a supine position with the knees bent over the edge of the examination table. He or she is instructed to flex one knee to the chest and hold it. At the same time, the angle of the test knee (opposite of the knee held to the chest) is to remain at 90°, and the hip and posterior thigh of the test leg are to remain in a stationary position and flat against the examination table. The assessment is scored as a pass if the test knee remained in a stationary 90° position. The assessment is scored as a fail if the test knee extended and moved to a position of less than 90°.
