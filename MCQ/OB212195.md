## Question

A 19-year-old volleyball player presents with chronic pain at the distal aspect of her patella without antecedent trauma. Examination reveals full range of motion without evidence of instability. She is diagnosed with patellar tendinopathy. Which of the following rehabilitation protocols is best indicated?

## Options

1. Plyometric
2. Isotonic with muscle lengthening
3. Isotonic with muscle shortening
4. Isometric with increasing tension
5. Isometric with decreasing tension

## Correct option

**2. Isotonic with muscle lengthening**

## Answer explanation

This patient is presenting with chronic patellar tendinopathy. Nonsurgical treatment of patellar tendinopathy focuses on eccentric exercises, which are characterized by isotonic contraction with muscle lengthening.

Patellar tendinopathy is not uncommon in athletes who jump repetitively, such as volleyball and basketball players. Initial management is conservative in nature, with eccentric exercises providing the most reliable clinical results. Eccentric exercises have been found to stimulate the remodeling process of the collagen fibers in the patellar tendon, enabling the muscle-tendon unit to better adapt to physical stresses.

*Kongsgaard et al. investigated eccentric training, heavy slow resistance training, and steroid injections in a randomized study of treatments for patellar tendinopathy.* **The authors concluded** that each of the modalities studied demonstrated good short-term effects. However, at long-term follow-up, **they found** that only the eccentric training and resistance groups maintained the effects, with steroid injections having no beneficial effects at 6-months.

*Figueroa et al. reviewed the diagnosis and treatment of patellar tendinopathy.* *They characterized the tendinosis as a degenerative rather than inflammatory disorder.* **The authors highlighted** that nonsurgical treatment is focused on eccentric exercises and is most often met with good results. **They recommend** that surgical management be reserved for cases that were refractory to nonsurgical treatment.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Plyometric exercises involve rapid eccentric-concentric shortening and can result in further injury and exacerbation of symptoms.
Answer 3: Isotonic contraction with muscle shortening refers to concentric exercise.
Answer 4 and 5: Isotonic, specifically eccentric, exercises, and not isometric exercises, are most appropriately indicated.
