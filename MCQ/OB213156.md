## Question

You are running a research meeting in which residents review their yearly projects. In reviewing one of the studies, a first-year resident plans to improperly utilize a statistical test. Which of the following best describes data that should be evaluated using the chi-squared test?

## Options

1. Categorical data of two small groups
2. Categorical data of two large groups
3. Continuous unpaired parametric data of two groups
4. Continuous paired parametric data of two groups
5. Continuous paired non-parametric data of two groups

## Correct option

**2. Categorical data of two large groups**

## Answer explanation

The chi-squared test is best used for comparing categorical data of two large groups.

Statistical inference is used to test specific hypotheses about associations or differences among groups of subjects/sample data. Various tests can be utilized based on the type of data being evaluated. The chi-squared test and Fisher's exact test can assess for independence between two variables when the groups are independent and not correlated. The chi-squared test applies an approximation assuming the sample is large, while Fisher's exact test runs an exact procedure, especially for small-sized samples.

*Guyatt et al review principles for applying evidence-based medicine to patient care.* **They report** that an evidence-based practitioner must also understand the patient's circumstances or predicament, identify knowledge gaps and frame questions to fill those gaps, conduct an efficient literature search, critically appraise the research evidence, and apply that evidence to patient care. **They conclude** that clinicians need to be able to distinguish high from low quality in primary studies, systematic reviews, practice guidelines, and other integrative research focused on management recommendations.

*Kim et al review statistical notes for clinical researchers.* **They report** that the chi-squared test, as well as the Fisher exact test, can both be used to evaluate categorical data. **They conclude** that the chi-squared test should be used for large groups, whereas the Fisher exact test is utilized for smaller samples.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Fisher exact test is used to compare categorical data of two small groups.
Answer 3: Independent t-test is used to study continuous unpaired parametric data.
Answer 4: The Dependent (paired) t-test is used to compare continuous paired parametric data of two groups.
Answer 5: The Wilcoxon Rank-Sum test is used to compare continuous paired non-parametric data.
