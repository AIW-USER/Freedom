## Question

Which of the following is the most common soft-tissue sarcoma of the hand?

## Options

1. Chondrosarcoma
2. Rhabdomyosarcoma
3. Epithelioid sarcoma
4. Myxoid liposarcoma
5. Hemangiopericytoma

## Correct option

**3. Epithelioid sarcoma**

## Answer explanation

The hand is a rare location for soft-tissue sarcomas. The most common histologic subtypes seen there are epithelioid sarcoma, malignant fibrous histiocytoma, synovial sarcoma, and clear cell sarcoma.
