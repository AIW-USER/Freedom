## Question

Correct statement regarding the use of titanium implant over a cobalt chromium for uncemented stem in THR

## Options

1. Titanium has high modulus of elasticity
2. Titanium is stiffer than Co-Cr
3. Titanium has lower tensile strength and fatigue resistance
4. Titanium has lower biocompatibility than Co-Cr

## Correct option

**3. Titanium has lower tensile strength and fatigue resistance**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C
EXPLANATION: -
Young’s modulus of Elasticity (Y) is mathematically given by a ratio of stress over strain. Higher the value of “Y”, stiffer is the implant. Following materials are arranged in their increasing order of “Y” i.e. stiffness  Polymers < Bone cement < Bone (cortical) < Titanium < Stainless steel < Cobalt-chromium < Ceramics. Hence, Titanium has lower “Y” and is less stiff than Co-Cr. Tensile strength and fatigue resistance of titanium is also lower than Co-Cr.
However, most studies have documented higher osseointegration with titanium stems (although practical benefit is debatable) and titanium is considered to be more biocompatible than Co-Cr.
