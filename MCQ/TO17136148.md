## Question

Minimum amount of urine required for sample collection during doping control program is?

## Options

1. 90ml
2. 120ml
3. 150ml
4. 70ml

## Correct option

**1. 90ml**

## Answer explanation

- Once athlete is notified about doping control procedure athlete should report to station “as soon as possible” within 60 min time frame.

- Delay in activities allowed for- Press conference

 - Training Session

Athlete from “time of notification till completion of sample collection process (SCP)”---- will be accompanied at all the times by chaperone.

Selection of collection vessel

- Athlete will choose any 1 individually sealed collection vessels.
- Athlete should verify that equipment is intact and has not been tampered.
- Athlete maintains control of collection vessels at all times.

 Provision of sample

- Athlete & DCO of the same gender permitted in the washroom during sample provision. [ total no of people -2]

- 3rd person is representative [* not allowed to view sample provision]

Volume of sample

- > 90 Ml
- 150 ml for a diluted urine [ instruct athlete during notification not to OVERHYDRATE]

- Athlete divide sample [ representative for disabled]

Athlete’s urine sample should be collected in a manner that ensures:

a) The Sample should meet the suitable specific gravity for analysis and the suitable volume of urine for analysis.

Failure of a Sample to meet these requirements in no way invalidates the suitability of the Sample for analysis. The determination of a Sample’s suitability for analysis is the decision of the relevant laboratory, in consultation with the testing authority for the sample collection session in question.

b) The sample should not be manipulated, substituted, contaminated or otherwise tampered with in anyway

c) The sample should be clearly and accurately identified

e) The sample should be securely sealed in a tamper evident kit

The requirement for accepting a sample with a suitable specific gravity for analysis is either:

- A sample with a minimum volume of 90mL and less than 150mL, with a specific gravity of 1.005 or higher with a refractometer, or 1.010 or higher with lab sticks
- A sample with a volume of at least 150mL, with a specific gravity of 1.003 or higher with a refractometer only

Answer: 1
