## Question

A 54-year-old woman has several months of plantar forefoot pain. The pain is worse with walking, particularly in her high heels. She can feel a palpable mass between her toes and she has a positive web-space compression test. What is the most common location for this pathology to take place?

## Options

1. Fifth metatarsal head
2. First metatarsophalangeal joint
3. First web space
4. Second web space
5. Third web space

## Correct option

**5. Third web space**

## Answer explanation

Morton's neuroma most commonly affects the third webspace at the confluence of the lateral and medial plantar nerves.

Morton's neuroma is a compressive neuropathy caused by perineural fibrosis and entrapment of the interdigital nerve. Symptoms include pain between the metatarsal heads that are exacerbated with weight-bearing, particularly while wearing narrow toe box shoes (i.e high heels). On examination, the neuroma may be palpable and compression of the web-space reproduces the symptoms. MRI is not usually required but may be useful if the diagnosis is in question. Nonsurgical options include a wide shoebox with firm soles and a metatarsal pad and corticosteroid injections. Surgical treatment consists of neurectomy via a dorsal approach. The most common complication is recurrent pain secondary to inadequate nerve resection.

*Pomeroy et al. described Morton neuroma as being an entrapment neuropathy of the interdigital nerve most commonly in the third webspace and only occasionally in the second webspace.* **The authors discuss** that to confirm the diagnosis one may inject local anesthetic 2 cm proximal to the metatarsal heads and patients will report pain relief. They go on to describe conservative and surgical management to include accommodative shoes and dorsal neurectomy, respectively.

*Johnson et al. performed a case series on 37 inadequately resected digital nerves requiring repeat operation.* **The authors noted** that at 76 months follow up following revision surgery, 67% had complete relief or marked improvement in pain, 9% had improvement but persistent pain and 24% had no improvement or worse pain. **They concluded** that the number of previously unsuccessful attempts that had been made to excise the neuroma did not adversely affect the results of reoperation in this group of patients.

Illustration A shows the confluence of the lateral and medial plantar nerves to end at the 3rd webspace of the foot.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: The fifth metatarsal head is not the most common site of Morton's neuroma.
Answer 2: The 1st MTPJ is the site of hallux rigidus and hallux valgus. The 1st MTPJ is not implicated in Morton's neuromas.
Answer 3: The 1st webspace is not the most common site of Morton's neuroma.
Answer 4: The 2nd webspace is the second most common site of Morton's neuroma.
