## Question

Sourcil sign is seen in which condition? (arthroscopy- shoulder arthroscopy- rotator cuff and subacromial impingement)

## Options

1. Rotator cuff tear arthropathy
2. Supraspinatus tendinitis
3. Adhesive capsulitis
4. Calcific tendinitis

## Correct option

**1. Rotator cuff tear arthropathy**

## Answer explanation

Sourcil sign refers to erosion of inferior surface of acromion and humeral head migrates superiorly to abut against the same and is seen with cuff tears leading to anterior-superior escape of humeral head.

Answer: 1
