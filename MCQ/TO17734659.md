## Question

Ball sign in ankle fracture indicates (Trauma- fractures/dislocations around foot and ankle-uni/bimalleolar fractures)

## Options

1. Malreduced fibula
2. Deltoid ligament tear
3. ATFL avulsion
4. Syndesmotic injury

## Correct option

**1. Malreduced fibula**

## Answer explanation

In the ankle fractures the most useful radiographic signs of fibular length are the talocrural angle and the “ball sign.” On the AP view the “ball” or “dime sign” is described as an unbroken curve of the ball connecting the recess in the distal tip of the fibula and the lateral process of the talus when the fibula is out to length. If the fibula is malreduced in a shortened position, the ball sign is absent.
