Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Foot and Ankle Arthrodesis

## Question

Recommended position for ankle arthrodesis is?

## Options

1. 10 degree external rotation/ 5 degree valgus/neutral plantar and dorsiflexion
2. 5 degree internal rotation/ neutral heel/ 5 degree equinus
3. No rotation/ neutral heel/ neutral plantar and dorsiflexion
4. 10 degree external rotation/5 degree Varus/neutral plantar and dorsiflexion

## Correct option

**1. 10 degree external rotation/ 5 degree valgus/neutral plantar and dorsiflexion**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 1
