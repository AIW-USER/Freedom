Chapter: Spine MCQs — Spinal Infections

## Question

After discectomy, patient developed severe pain and paraspinal spasm. There is no fever. TLC and ESR are normal but CRP is highly elevated. What is the diagnosis?

## Options

1. Spinal instability
2. Infective discitis
3. Pseudo meningocele
4. Residual disc compressing nerve root

## Correct option

**2. Infective discitis**

## Answer explanation

This picture is suggestive of Infection. The complications associated with standard disc excision and micro lumbar disc excision are similar. These include- wound infection, postoperative discitis, dural tear, thrombophlebitis, nerve root injury and pulmonary embolism.

Dural tears with CSF leaks, pseudo meningocele formation, CSF fistula formation, and meningitis are more likely after reoperation.

Answer: 2
