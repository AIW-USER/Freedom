## Question

What is Wallerian degeneration? (peripheral nerve injuries- wallerian degeneration)

## Options

1. Proximal to severed nerve
2. Distal to severed nerve
3. Degeneration of Adjacent nerve
4. Degeneration at both ends

## Correct option

**2. Distal to severed nerve**

## Answer explanation

![[TO17747051-1.png]]

Any part of a neuron detached from its nucleus degenerates and is destroyed by phagocytosis. This process of degeneration distal to a point of injury is called secondary, or Wallerian degeneration. The reaction proximal to the point of detachment is called primary, traumatic, or retrograde degeneration

Answer : b
