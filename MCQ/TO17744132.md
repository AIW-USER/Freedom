## Question

Second line investigation after pelvic X ray for suspected/occult fracture neck of femur in trauma victims is [TRAUMA- Femoral Neck Fracture]

## Options

1. Clinical Examination
2. MRI
3. X ray in internal rotation of the hip
4. Thin slice multidetector CT

## Correct option

**4. Thin slice multidetector CT**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: CT and MRI are comparable at detecting occult femoral neck fractures. Given the increased availability, improved patient tolerance and speed of CT imaging, we advocate its use as the main second-line imaging modality. MRI remains a valuable problem-solving tool in a select few cases following review with a musculoskeletal radiologist. When interpreted by experienced radiologists, MDCT has both sensitivity and specificity of 100%

Answer: 4
