## Question

A 40-year-old man sustains a fracture-dislocation of C4-5. Examination reveals no motor or sensory function below the C5 level. All extremities are areflexic. The bulbocavernosus reflex is absent. The prognosis for this patient’s neurologic recovery can be best determined by

## Options

1. myelography with CT.
2. spinal cord-evoked potentials.
3. repeat physical examinations.
4. MRI.
5. electromyography and nerve conduction velocity studies.

## Correct option

**3. repeat physical examinations.**

## Answer explanation

The patient has spinal shock. MRI is an appropriatediagnostic procedure. Myelography with CT is of little value unless there is an unusual skeletal variant. Spinal cord-evoked potentials have no value. The best method to determine the patient’s neurologic recovery is repeated physical examinations over the first 48 to 72 hours.
