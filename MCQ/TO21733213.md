Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Complications of THR

## Question

Likely cause of shortening observed on post op day 1 after a THR would be

## Options

1. Plus 8 sized femoral head
2. Low femur neck shaft angle
3. High femur neck shaft angle
4. Size 3 stem is used in place of size 4

## Correct option

**2. Low femur neck shaft angle**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B
EXPLANATION: -
Plus 8 sized femoral head would increase the vertical offset and would lengthen the limb not shorten. Size 3 stem (where size 4 is appropriate) would subside over time not on day 1 and shortening caused will come with ambulation.
Now, concerning change in femoral neck shaft angle (NSA) : if the implant has higher neck shaft angle compared to native NSA, this would increase vertical offset (decreasing horizontal offset rather) and would lengthen limb not shorten. Decreasing neck shaft angle would decrease vertical offset on table which could result in shortening getting observed on post op day 1.
