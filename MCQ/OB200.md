## Question

A minimal-incision technique with an incision no more than 10 centimeters has which of the following advantages compared to a standard incision for a total hip replacement?

## Options

1. lower post-operative visual analogue pain score
2. less transfusion requirement
3. shorter length of stay
4. better cosmetic result
5. less pain medication requirement

## Correct option

**4. better cosmetic result**

## Answer explanation

*Ogonda et al randomized patients to standard (16cm) versus MIS incision (<10 cm).* **They found** no significant difference with respect to postoperative hematocrit, blood transfusion requirements, pain scores, or analgesic use. There were also no differences in early walking ability or length of hospital stay and no differences in component alignment.
