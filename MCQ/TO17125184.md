## Question

Length of the long barrel of DHS plate?

![[TO17125184-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. 22 mm
2. 34 mm
3. 38 mm
4. 42 mm

## Correct option

**3. 38 mm**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: There are two types of DHS plate available according to barrel length – Standard barrel (38 mm) and short barrel (25 mm).

Thread length of the lag screw is 22mm.

Answer: 3
