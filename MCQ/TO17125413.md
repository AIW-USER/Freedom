Chapter: General Orthopedics MCQs — Orthosis And Prosthesis

## Question

Which is not a feature of bone anchored implant system for prosthetic attachment in an above knee amputee

## Options

1. Perception of terrain changes
2. Prevention of osteoporosis in the residual stump
3. Useful in amputee who have limited residual limb length
4. No donning/doffing of the prosthetic limb is required

## Correct option

**4. No donning/doffing of the prosthetic limb is required**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 4
