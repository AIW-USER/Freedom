## Question

Parathyroid hormone is thought to have which of the following effects on cartilage?

## Options

1. Increased expression of matrix metalloprotease-13 (MMP-13)
2. Increased water content and elasticity
3. Overproduction of type X collagen
4. Reduced proteoglycan content
5. Decreased cartilage degradation

## Correct option

**5. Decreased cartilage degradation**

## Answer explanation

Parathyroid hormone (PTH) has demonstrated chondroprotective effects in vivo, decreasing cartilage degradation.

Cartilage consists of chondrocytes and extracellular matrix (collagen, water, proteoglycans). Cartilage is prone to fissuring and ulceration with damage. Research exploring ways to stimulate cartilage growth and repair in these settings is ongoing and parathyroid hormone has demonstrated chondroprotective properties. Although this mechanism is not fully understood, it is thought to involve stimulation of the Indian Hedgehog (IHH) signaling pathways that are regularly associated with chondrocyte growth. Additionally, growth factors like basic fibroblast growth factor (FGF) and insulin-like growth factor (IGF) may increase the usefulness of PTH when given together.

*Martin et al. reviewed the physiologic role of PTH in different tissues.* **They note** that PTH is important in regulating bone and cartilage formation and bony remodeling. They specifically review the use of PTH as anabolic therapy for bone and cartilage growth.

*Morita et al. used a rat model to look at subchondral bony fragility and its association with osteoarthritis progression as well as to evaluate the role PTH played on associated cartilage degeneration.* **They noted** that cartilage degradation increased significantly with underlying subchondral bone microfractures and meniscal pathology and the administration of PTH delayed further progression of cartilage damage. **They conclude** based on these effects that PTH may have some therapeutic utility in decreasing the progression of osteoarthritis, although use is not widespread at this time.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Increased MMP-13 would cause increased cartilage degradation.
Answer 2: There is no evidence that PTH directly increases cartilage water content.
Answer 3: Type X collagen is thought to be increased in worsening osteoarthritis as it is though to represent end-stage hypertrophic chondrocyte differentiation.
Answer 4: PTH would not decrease proteoglycan content, as this would decrease cartilage quality and would not be chondroprotective.
