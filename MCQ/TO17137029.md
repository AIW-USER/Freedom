## Question

The reported rate of osteonecrosis in a 21-year-old female athlete who sustained a displaced talar neck fracture with displacement of both the subtalar and ankle joint is?

## Options

1. Less than 30%
2. 59%
3. 70%
4. 90%

## Correct option

**3. 70%**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

 OSTEONECROSIS RATES

 Hawkins Type 1: 0-50%

 Hawkins Type 2: 20-50%

 Hawkins Type 3: 50-80%

 Hawkins Type 4: 90-100%
