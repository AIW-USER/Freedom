## Question

Best hip position for the diagnosis of the impacted fracture of neck of the femur is (trauma- fractures/dislocations around hip - fracture neck of femur)

## Options

1. Abduction and internal rotation
2. Abduction and external rotation
3. Flexion and abduction
4. Neutral rotation

## Correct option

**1. Abduction and internal rotation**

## Answer explanation

In AP view of the both hip joint, either both patellae should be facing forward or lower extremities should be internally rotated by 15°-20° to accommodate femoral anteversion.

Answer: 1
