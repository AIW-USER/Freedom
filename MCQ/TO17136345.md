## Question

Which is not a tool for documentation of Sports Injuries

## Options

1. SOAP
2. SCAT
3. Daily Red Cross List
4. Training Room Treatment Log

## Correct option

**2. SCAT**

## Answer explanation

FOR DOCUMENTATION/ REPORTING OF SPORTS INJURIES, following formats are used

- SOAP NOTES
- S- Subjective statement of athlete
- O- Objective finding observed by trainer
- A- Assessment of extent and nature of injury
- P- Plan of First Aid
- Daily Sideline Injury Report
- Training Room Treatment Log
- Daily Red Cross List- Filled by practitioner to inform coaches as to whether the athlete is allowed no participation, limited participation or full participation

SCAT : stands for Sports Concussion and Assessment tool. It is a chart used to assess if a patient has sustained a clinically significant Sports concussion.

Answer: 2
