Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Anatomy ( Knee )

## Question

Correct statement about tibial plateau anatomy is

## Options

1. Medial is more convex and larger than lateral
2. Lateral is more convex and larger than medial
3. Medial is more concave and larger than lateral
4. Lateral is more concave and larger than medial

## Correct option

**3. Medial is more concave and larger than lateral**

## Answer explanation

Medial tibial plateau has a more concave shape and is larger in both length and width than the lateral tibial plateau, which has a slightly convex shape. The lateral tibial plateau lies proximal to the medial plateau. The convexity of the lateral plateau helps differentiate it from the medial plateau on a lateral radiograph of the proximal tibia.

Answer: 3
