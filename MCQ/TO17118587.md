## Question

In trans portal ACL, correct statement is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Femoral tunnel is drilled through tibial tunnel
2. Femoral tunnel is drilled through anteromedial portal
3. Femoral tunnel is drilled through anterolateral portal
4. Femoral tunnel is placed vertically at roof top position

## Correct option

**2. Femoral tunnel is drilled through anteromedial portal**

## Answer explanation

In trans portal ACL reconstruction, the femoral tunnel is drilled through an anteromedial portal. Anterolateral portal is used to put an arthroscope into the joint. In transtibial ACL reconstruction the femoral tunnel is drilled through a tibial tunnel which is made above 11 o'clock position (or roof top 12 o clock) and graft has vertical orientation. In trans portal technique femoral tunnel is drilled in anatomical position at 10 o'clock position and graft take horizontal orientation.

Answer: 2
