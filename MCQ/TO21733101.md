Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Lisfranc Injury

## Question

Below is the picture of a foot. What is the likely diagnosis?

![[TO21733101-1.png]]

## Options

1. Mycetoma foot
2. Lisfranc fracture dislocation
3. Hydatid cyst of the talus
4. Vertical talus

## Correct option

**2. Lisfranc fracture dislocation**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

EXPLANATION: -

Plantar ecchymosis is highly suggestive of Lisfranc fracture dislocation. Plantar bruises can also be seen in calcaneal fracture. Among the given options, Lisfranc fracture dislocation is the most appropriate answer.
