## Question

What is the most common deformity associated with the deformity shown in picture?

![[TO17138261-1.png]]

## Options

1. CTEV
2. Metatarsus Adductus
3. Calcaneovalgus foot
4. A and C

## Correct option

**3. Calcaneovalgus foot**

## Answer explanation

Congenital posteromedial bowing of the tibia-fibula, in which both bones are medially and posteriorly bowed at either the middle or distal third of the shaft. It is also associated with dorsiflexion contracture of the ankle. It is an uncommon anomaly of unknown etiology and pathogenesis.

 A calcaneovalgus alignment of the ipsilateral foot usually coexists.

The distal third of tibia is more commonly affected than the middle third. The posterior bow is the more severe deformity than the medial bow.

Unlike anterolateral bowing of the tibia, these bows are less prone to fractures and pseudoarthrosis formation. This condition is usually benign and rarely associated with other underlying conditions.

Answer: 3
