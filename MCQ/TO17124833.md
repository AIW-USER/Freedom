## Question

Following are correct for compartment of foot except

## Options

1. Central compartment contains flexor digitorum brevis
2. Adductor compartment content- adductor hallucis, interosseous muscle
3. Medial compartment – abductor hallucis, flexor hallucis brevis
4. Lateral compartment: flexor digiti minimi, abductor digiti minimi

## Correct option

**2. Adductor compartment content- adductor hallucis, interosseous muscle**

## Answer explanation

Explanation:

Foot muscles are divided in 9 compartments:

- medial
- abductor hallucis
- flexor hallucis brevis
- lateral
- abductor digiti minimi
- flexor digiti minimi brevis
- interosseous (x4)
- central (x3)
- superficial
- flexor digitorum brevis
- central
- quadratus plantae
- deep
- adductor hallucis
- posterior tibial neurovascular bundle

 interosseous muscles form separates 4 interosseous compartments
