Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Periprosthetic Fractures

## Question

In Vancouver type B3 femoral fracture after THR which is not a management option?

## Options

1. Allograft prosthesis composite
2. Tumor prosthesis
3. Revision with long porous coated stem with plate and cable wire construct
4. All of the above

## Correct option

**3. Revision with long porous coated stem with plate and cable wire construct**

## Answer explanation

![[TO17099985-1.jpg]]
In type B3 fractures, the proximal femur is so deficient that it cannot be treated with open reduction and internal fixation or support a new femoral component. The femur can be reconstructed with an allograft prosthesis composite to restore bone stock. Alternatively, the revision can be done with a proximal femoral replacement prosthesis, such as that used for tumor reconstructions.

Answer: 3
