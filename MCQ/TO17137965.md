## Question

True about open fracture debridement is

## Options

1. Delayed debridement after golden hours significantly increases chances of infection
2. Antibiotics should be started only after debridement to get a positive culture
3. Early debridement within 6 hours significantly decrease the chances of infection
4. Delayed debridement does not increase the chances of infection provided that appropriate antibiotic cover is established

## Correct option

**4. Delayed debridement does not increase the chances of infection provided that appropriate antibiotic cover is established**

## Answer explanation

Surgical debridement is considered one of the most important procedures for open lower limb fractures.Traditionally, debridement has been performed within 6 hours of presentation. The rationale behind the figure is believed to date back to early studies on micro-organism load following contamination. In current literature, the 6-hour wait does not show a clear evidence base. Many studies show insignificant differences in the incidence of infection whether debridement is performed early following presentation or is delayed, given that appropriate antibiotic cover is established immediately.

Answer : d
