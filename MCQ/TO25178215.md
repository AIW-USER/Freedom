Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Chronic Regional Pain Syndrome (CRPS)

## Question

Graded Motor Imagery (GMI) is a mainstay of CRPS rehabilitation. Which of the following describes the correct physiological sequence of these stages?

## Options

1. Mirror Therapy → Desensitization → Laterality Restoration
2. Stress Loading → Mirror Therapy → Implicit Motor Imagery
3. Laterality Restoration → Mirror Therapy → Explicit Motor Imagery
4. Laterality Restoration → Explicit Motor Imagery → Mirror Therapy

## Correct option

**4. Laterality Restoration → Explicit Motor Imagery → Mirror Therapy**

## Answer explanation

Graded Motor Imagery (GMI): A 3-step process to "retrain the brain": Graded Motor Imagery (GMI) is a structured, three-stage rehabilitation program designed to "retrain the brain" in patients with chronic pain conditions like CRPS, phantom limb pain, or severe focal dystonia.

The core theory is that in chronic pain, the brain's cortical map (the "software" that represents your body) becomes distorted or "smudged." GMI seeks to reorganize these neural pathways sequentially without triggering the body's protective pain response.

 - Laterality Training: Identifying left vs. right limbs.

 - Explicit Motor Imagery: Mentally visualizing movement without moving.

 - Mirror Therapy: Using a mirror box to trick the brain into seeing the "painful" limb move normally.

Answer: 4
