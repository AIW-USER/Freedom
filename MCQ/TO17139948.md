Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Anatomy ( Shoulder)

## Question

false about version of glenoid and humeral head is (FNB Pattern Paper 2014)

## Options

1. Normal version of glenoid is near 0 degree or slightly retroverted
2. Normal version of glenoid is near 0 degree or slightly anteverted
3. Normally humeral head is retroverted
4. Both glenoid and humeral head are anteverted.

## Correct option

**4. Both glenoid and humeral head are anteverted.**

## Answer explanation

Normal glenoid version in most studies has been reported close to 0°, sometimes with slight anteversion but more often slight retroversion with values typically less than 10° in either direction. Normal humeral head is also retroverted with the average angle of retroversion being 26° ± 11°.

Answer: 4
