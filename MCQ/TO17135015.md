## Question

All are true regarding spinal anatomy except?

## Options

1. C5 nerve root exits above the C5 pedicle
2. L5 nerve root exits below L5 pedicle
3. Filum terminale is modification of pia mater
4. Vertebral artery travels through transverse foramen of C1 to C7 vertebral body

## Correct option

**4. Vertebral artery travels through transverse foramen of C1 to C7 vertebral body**

## Answer explanation

C1 to C7 have a transverse foramen. Vertebral artery travels through transverse foramen of C1 to C6. C7 despite having a transverse foramen, the vertebral artery does NOT travel through it in the majority of individuals.

Answer: 4
