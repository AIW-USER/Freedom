## Question

Patients with Rothmund-Thomson syndrome are most commonly predisposed to develop which of the following tumors?

## Options

1. Osteosarcoma
2. Ewing's sarcoma
3. Synovial sarcoma
4. Chondrosarcoma
5. Chondromyxoid fibroma

## Correct option

**1. Osteosarcoma**

## Answer explanation

Rothmund-Thomson syndrome (RTS) is characterized by growth retardation, thin eyebrows and lashes, juvenile cataracts, sunlight sensitivity, hypogonadism, and teeth abnormalities. RTS is associated with an increased risk for cancer, such as cutaneous epitheliomas (basal, squamous), gastric adenocarcinoma, fibrosarcoma, and osteosarcoma. Osteosarcoma is often the second most common malignancy found in these patients. Therefore, patients with RTS need careful survey of any bony lesion.
