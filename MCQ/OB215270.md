## Question

Rates of recurrence for patients with chondrosarcoma are most directly related to which of the following?

## Options

1. Increased telomerase activity
2. Expression of major histocompatibility complex type II
3. Gadolinium uptake on MRI
4. Increased tumor apoptosis on histology
5. Technetium activity on bone scan

## Correct option

**1. Increased telomerase activity**

## Answer explanation

Rates of recurrence for patients with chondrosarcoma are most directly related to increased telomerase activity in tumor samples.

Telomerase is a nuclear enzyme that elongates chromosomes leading to cellular growth and proliferation. Expression of the telomerase reverse transcriptase subunit telomerase reverse transcriptase gene is associated with most human malignancies. Because telomerase reverse transcriptase is rarely expressed in normal tissue, its presence in pathologic specimens is considered a marker of transformed cells. Moreover, high levels of expression have been correlated with poor prognosis in many cancers.

*Martin et al. performed a study using immunohistochemistry to compare telomerase reverse transcriptase expression in a collection of 61 tumors consisting of malignant chondrosarcomas of varying grades and benign enchondromas.* **They found** that the relative abundance of telomerase reverse transcriptase-expressing cells correlated significantly with grade and recurrence.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: Expression of major histocompatibility complex type II has not shown to be a risk factor for increased rates of recurrence of chondrosarcoma.
Answer 3: Gadolinium uptake on MRI has not shown to be a risk factor for increased rates of recurrence of chondrosarcoma.
Answer 4: Increased tumor apoptosis on histology has not shown to be a risk factor for increased rates of recurrence of chondrosarcoma.
Answer 5: Technetium activity on bone scan has not shown to be a risk factor for increased rates of recurrence of chondrosarcoma.
