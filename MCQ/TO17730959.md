## Question

Correct sequence of extensor block pinning for mallet finger is (trauma- fractures around wrist and hand-mallet finger)

## Options

1. DIP flexion, k wire through extensor tendon into middle finger, DIP extension, k wire across the DIP joint
2. DIP extension, k wire through extensor tendon into middle finger, DIP flexion, k wire across the DIP joint
3. DIP extension, k wire through extensor tendon into middle finger, DIP flexion then extension, k wire across the DIP joint
4. DIP extension, k wire through extensor tendon into middle finger, second k wire across the DIP joint

## Correct option

**1. DIP flexion, k wire through extensor tendon into middle finger, DIP extension, k wire across the DIP joint**

## Answer explanation

Correct sequence of extensor block pinning for mallet finger

Flexion of DIP, K wire through the extensor tendon into middle phalanx, full extension of DIP, K wire across the DIP
