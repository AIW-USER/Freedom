Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — Shoulder Instability[Unidirectional and Multidirectional]

## Question

Which of the following describes the 'Sling Effect' achieved during a Latarjet procedure?

## Options

1. The transfer of the coracoacromial ligament to the capsule
2. The increase in the glenoid bone surface area (articular arc)
3. The conjoint tendon providing dynamic stability to the anteroinferior capsule in abduction and external rotation
4. The tenodesis of the long head of the biceps

## Correct option

**3. The conjoint tendon providing dynamic stability to the anteroinferior capsule in abduction and external rotation**

## Answer explanation

The Latarjet (coracoid transfer) works via the "Triple Effect":

 - Bone Effect: Increases the glenoid surface area/width.

 - Sling Effect: The conjoint tendon stabilizes the inferior capsule in abduction/external rotation.

 - Capsular Effect: Using the remaining CA ligament to repair the capsule.

The conjoint tendon (short head of biceps and coracobrachialis) acts as a dynamic brace across the lower third of the joint during vulnerable positions.

Answer: 3
