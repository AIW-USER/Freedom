## Question

After the discectomy, the patient developed severe pain and paraspinal spasm. There is no fever. TLC and ESR are normal but CRP is highly elevated. What is the diagnosis? (spine- miscellaneous)

## Options

1. Spinal instability
2. Infective discitis
3. Pseudomeningocele
4. Recurrence

## Correct option

**2. Infective discitis**

## Answer explanation

complications of spinal surgeries:

This picture is suggestive of Infection. The complications associated with standard disc excision and micro lumbar disc excision are similar. These include- wound infection, postoperative discitis, dural tear, thrombophlebitis, nerve root injury and pulmonary embolism.

Dural tears with CSF leaks, pseudomeningocele formation, CSF fistula formation, and meningitis are more likely after reoperation.

Answer : b
