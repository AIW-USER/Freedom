## Question

Most consistent finding in Klippel Feil syndrome is

## Options

1. Shortening of neck
2. Low hair line
3. Limitation of neck motion
4. Elevation of scapula

## Correct option

**3. Limitation of neck motion**

## Answer explanation

The classic clinical presentation of Klippel-Feil syndrome is the triad of a low posterior hairline, a short neck, and limited neck motion. This triad indicates almost complete cervical involvement and may be clinically evident at birth; however, fewer than half of patients with Klippel-Feil syndrome have all parts of the triad. Shortening of the neck and a low posterior hairline are not constant findings and may be overlooked; webbing of the neck (pterygium colli) is seen in severe involvement. The most constant clinical finding is limitation of neck motion.

Answer: 3
