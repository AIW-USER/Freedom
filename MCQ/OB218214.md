## Question

Figure A is the CT scan of a 17-year-old male is involved in an MVC. Upon presentation to the ED the patient is found to have difficulty breathing. Advanced trauma life support is started. Which of the following is the primary restraint for anterior-posterior stability for the injury sustained?

![[q218214-a-sc-posterior.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Anterior sternoclavicular capsular ligament
2. Posterior sternoclavicular capsular ligament
3. Anterior fasciculus of the costoclavicular ligament
4. Posterior fasciculus of the costoclavicular ligament
5. Intra-articular disk ligament

## Correct option

**2. Posterior sternoclavicular capsular ligament**

## Answer explanation

The posterior sternoclavicular capsular ligament is the primary restraint for anterior-posterior stability (Answer 2).

The stenoclavicular joint is a diarthrodial saddle joint that is incongruous (~50% contact). It is fibrocartilage (not synovial cartilage) and contains an intra-articular disc. The posterior sternoclavicular capsular ligament is the primary restraint for anterior-posterior stability, while the anterior sternoclavicular ligament is the primary restraint to superior displacement of medial clavicle. It is important to note that most injuries at this location involve the medial clavicular physis, the closure of which typically occurs at 25 years of age. Injuries to this region are associated with motor vehicle collision or contact sports. Posterior dislocation can be life-threatening, and ATLS protocol and rapid transport to the emergency department is recommended.

*Wirth et al. reviewed acute and chronic traumatic injuries of the sternoclavicular joint.* **They reported** that posterior dislocation of the sternoclavicular joint requires prompt closed or open reduction, as posterior displacement of the medial clavicle has been associated with numerous complications, including respiratory distress, venous congestion or arterial insufficiency, brachial plexus compression, and myocardial conduction abnormalities. **They concluded** that surgical resection of the medial clavicle, with maintenance, repair, or reconstruction of the costoclavicular ligaments is recommended when surgery is indicated.

*Waters et al. reviewed posterior sternoclavicular joint dislocations and posteriorly displaced physeal fractures of the medial clavicle.* **They reported** that patients with posterior dislocations who underwent ligament repair and/or open reduction and suture stabilization had excellent functional outcomes. **They concluded** that skeletally immature patients may expect excellent functional outcomes following surgery for posterior sternoclavicular joint dislocations or posteriorly displaced physeal fractures of the medial clavicle.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: The anterior sternoclavicular capsular ligament is the primary restraint to superior displacement of the medial clavicle.
Answer 3: The anterior fasciculus of the costoclavicular ligament resists superior rotation and lateral displacement.
Answer 4: The posterior fasciculus of the costoclavicular ligament resists inferior rotation and medial displacement.
Answer 5: The intra-articular disk ligament prevents medial displacement of the clavicle and is the secondary restraint to superior clavicle displacement.
