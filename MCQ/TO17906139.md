## Question

A patient with back pain has presented with his MRI of LS spine. You notice the MRI is showing Spondylodiscitis. L3-4 vertebrae were involved and collection is there but disc space is relatively preserved. Likely etiology is

## Options

1. Tubercular
2. Pyogenic
3. Fungal
4. Viral

## Correct option

**2. Pyogenic**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

Two most common spinal infections are

1. Tubercular

2. Pyogenic

Pyogenic infections are more acute and more painful. So, the patients present relatively earlier in the course of the disease. In pyogenic infections, the disc spaces are relatively spared even though disc space abscess will be there. This is in contrast to Tuberculosis where presentation is relatively delayed, and most often patients present with collapsed disc spaces. Basically, the chronic course in TB damages discs. As the vertebral end plates are destroyed, nutrition to discs suffers and leads to their degeneration with time. As presentation is delayed, by the time the disease is diagnosed, the changes become noticeable.
