## Question

A 28-year-old woman who is an avid runner reports pain about the left hip with activities. Nonsurgical management has failed to provide relief. An MRI arthrogram is shown in Figure 47. What is the most likely diagnosis?

![[q6038-a-47.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Osteonecrosis
2. Transient osteoporosis
3. Loose chondral fragment
4. Labral tear
5. Femoral neck stress fracture

## Correct option

**4. Labral tear**

## Answer explanation

The MRI arthrogram reveals dye extravasation into the labrum, consistent with a labral tear. The MRI findings are not typical of osteonecrosis, stress fracture, or transient osteoporosis. There is no increase in bone marrow edema in the neck or femoral head.
