## Question

Which is not a part of Lateral Quadruple Complex?

## Options

1. Iliotibial Band
2. LCL
3. Biceps femoris
4. Popliteo fibular [PF] ligament

## Correct option

**4. Popliteo fibular [PF] ligament**

## Answer explanation

Answer-D

Lateral Quadruple Complex includes : IT band, LCL, Popliteus Tendon & biceps femoris.
