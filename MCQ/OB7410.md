## Question

A 20-year-old man has a dorsal metacarpophalangeal dislocation of the index finger. Multiple attempts to reduce the dislocation in the emergency department have not been successful. What structure is most likely preventing the joint from being reduced?

## Options

1. First dorsal interosseous
2. Radial collateral ligament
3. Ulnar collateral ligament
4. Natatory ligament
5. Volar plate

## Correct option

**5. Volar plate**

## Answer explanation

The volar plate is the structure that usually prevents the finger metacarpophalangeal joint from reducing. Blockage by the first dorsal interosseous is not a common reason for an irreducible metacarpophalangeal joint dislocation. None of the other structures commonly prevent metacarpophalangeal joint reduction.
