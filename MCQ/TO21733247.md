## Question

A professional soccer player is preparing for a 90-minute match in hot and humid conditions. Which of the following is the best hydration strategy during the match?

## Options

1. Drink only water throughout the match to prevent dehydration and avoid excess sugar intake.
2. Consume an isotonic sports drink containing 6–8% carbohydrates and electrolytes.
3. Drink a hypertonic sports drink to maximize energy intake.
4. Skip hydration during the match and focus on replenishing fluids only after the game to prevent stomach discomfort.

## Correct option

**2. Consume an isotonic sports drink containing 6–8% carbohydrates and electrolytes.**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B
EXPLANATION: -
Sports drinks are water-based flavored beverages specially made to provide superiority to drinking water. They are designed to consume before or during the workout to prevent possible dehydration. They consist of large amounts of electrolytes such as sodium, calcium, potassium, magnesium, etc. They can be categorized as shown in table below: -

Type Carbohydrate Content Purpose Best For
Hypotonic Low (<6%) Rapid hydration, minimal energy Short-duration workouts (<60 min)
Isotonic Moderate (6–8%) Hydration + energy Endurance & team sports
Hypertonic High (>8%) Energy replenishment, slower absorption Post-exercise recovery

Isotonic sports drink:
Isotonic sports drinks provide rapid fluid absorption, electrolyte replacement, and carbohydrates for sustained energy, making them ideal for prolonged, high-intensity exercise in hot conditions.
Key Characteristics of Isotonic Sports Drinks:
Carbohydrate Content: 6–8% (6-8g per 100mL)
Electrolytes: Sodium (Na⁺), Potassium (K⁺), Magnesium (Mg²⁺), and Chloride (Cl⁻)
Osmolarity: Similar to blood plasma (~300 mOsm/kg) → Fast absorption
Purpose: Hydration, energy replenishment, electrolyte balance
Examples:
Commercial: Gatorade, Powerade, Lucozade Sport
Homemade Recipe:
• 500 mL water
• 30-40g sugar (6-8%)
• ¼ tsp salt (sodium)
• 200 mL orange juice (potassium source)
Why Are Isotonic Sports Drinks Used?
Rapid Rehydration: Replaces lost fluids quickly, preventing dehydration.
Energy Supply: Provides quick-absorbing carbohydrates for endurance and prolonged activity.
Electrolyte Replacement: Restores sodium & potassium lost through sweat, preventing cramps.
Maintains Performance: Prevents fatigue during long-duration sports (>60 min).
When to Use an Isotonic Sports Drink?
• Endurance Sports (Marathon, Cycling, Soccer, Basketball) → Helps sustain energy and hydration.
• Intense Workouts (>60 min, High Sweat Loss) → Prevents dehydration & electrolyte imbalance.
• Hot & Humid Conditions → Replaces heavy sweat losses more effectively than water alone.
