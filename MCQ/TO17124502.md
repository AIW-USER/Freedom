## Question

Miller’s Asthma is seen in (FNB Pattern Paper 2016,2018)

## Options

1. Children
2. Athletes sensitive to cold
3. Grain workers
4. Coal workers

## Correct option

**3. Grain workers**

## Answer explanation

Miller’s asthma is also known as Grain worker's asthma or Mill-workers' asthma. It is hypersensitivity pneumonitis to granary weevil.

Answer: 3
