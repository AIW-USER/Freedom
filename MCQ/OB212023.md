## Correct option

**1. Both are a result of failure of segmentation**

## Answer explanation

Figures A and B depict a calcaneonavicular and a talocalcaneus coalition, respectively. Both forms of coalition are the result of an embryological failure of mesenchymal segmentation between tarsal bones.

Tarsal coalitions are a structural anomaly between two or three tarsal bones causing a rigid flatfoot. The age of symptom onset for a calcaneonavicular and talocalcaneal coatlition are usually between 8-12 and 12-15 years old, respectively. The pathophysiology of this condition occurs at the embryological level when there is a failure of mesenchymal segmentation leading to coalition between two or three tarsal bones. This develops into a fibrous coalition (syndesmosis), cartilagenous coalition (synchondrosis), or an osseous coalition (synostosis). These coalitions are initially treated non-operatively, but may require resection with possible graft interposition following failed nonoperative management.

*Mubarak et al. retrospectively reviewed symptomatic calcaneonavicular coalitions treated with resection and fat graft interposition.* **They reported** that only 5% had symptomatic regrowth requiring repeat resection. **They concluded** that reossification and reoperation rates with fat graft interposition were lower than in most published reports of EDB interposition.

*Gantsoudes et al. retrospectively reviewed outcomes of patients treated for symptomatic talocalcaneal coalition with resection and interposition of fat graft.* **They report** only a 3% recurrence, which was treated with repeat excision. **They concluded** that a symptomatic talocalcaneal coalition can be treated with excision and fat graft interposition, and achieve good to excellent results.

Figure A is the radiograph of the foot demonstrating a calcaneonavicular coalition. Figure B is a radiograph of the ankle demonstrating a talocalcaneal coalition. Illustration A depicts a talocalcaneal coalition "C-sign," which is formed by the medial outline of the talar dome and the inferior outline of the sustentaculum tali.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 2-5: Both talocalcaneal and calcaneonavicular coalitions are the result of a failure of segmentation
