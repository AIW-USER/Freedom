## Question

A 3-year-old male presents to the ED with acute onset right hip pain. His parents report he has been ill for the past 3 days. Beginning this morning he was unable to bear weight on his right side secondary to pain. Oral temperature is 102.1 F. An ultrasound of the right hip demonstrates an effusion and an aspiration was subsequently performed. Which of the following is true of the most common bacterial organism?

## Options

1. Gram positive cocci in clusters
2. Gram positive cocci in chains
3. Gram negative cocci in clusters
4. Gram negative cocci in chains
5. Unidentifiable on gram stain

## Correct option

**1. Gram positive cocci in clusters**

## Answer explanation

Staphylococcus aureus is the most common bacteria isolated in pediatric septic hips in patients >2 years of age.

While many organisms may cause septic arthritis in various populations of pediatric patients, staph aureus remains the most common overall. Staph aureus is identified on gram stain as being a gram-positive bacteria in clusters. Prior to the advent of vaccination, H. flu was the most common causative organisms in children between the ages of 2-5 years. Neonates, on the other hand, have a higher propensity of group B streptococcus and E. coli infections. Recently, infections with Kingella species has also increased in the United States, however, staph aureus remains the most common cause of septic arthritis in pediatric patients. In adolescent, sexually active patients, neisseria gonerhoeae is the most common cause of septic arthritis.

*Young et al review the etiology and responsible organisms of septic arthritis in children.* *They report on 13 cases of septic arthritis in which the hip was the most commonly infected joint (6/15), followed by the elbow (3/15), knee (2/15), and ankle (2/15).* **They conclude**, that overall, the most commonly isolated bacteria was methicillin-sensitive staph aureus.

*Montgomery et al review septic arthritis in pediatric patients.* **They report** that the organism most frequently identified is staphylococcus aureus. **They conclude** that treatment consists of open arthrotomy with decompression of the joint, irrigation and debridement, and treatment of current infections.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: Staph aureus is found in clusters, not in chains.
Answer 3-4: Staph aureus is a gram-positive organism, not gram-negative.
Answer 5: Staph aureus is identified as a gram-positive bacteria found in clusters on gram stain.
