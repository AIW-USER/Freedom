## Question

Normal thumb flexor tendon kinematics are restored by repairing which of the following pulleys when the A-2 is intact?

## Options

1. Av-2
2. Av-1
3. Oblique or A-1
4. A-3
5. Palmar aponeurotic

## Correct option

**3. Oblique or A-1**

## Answer explanation

When the A-2 pulley remains intact, dividing either the A-1 or the oblique pulley will not alter thumb mechanical efficiency or joint angular displacement. If both the oblique pulley and A-1 pulley are cut, significant bow stringing will occur. Studies showed that repair or reconstruction of either the oblique pulley or the A-1 pulley after injury will restore thumb kinematics as long as the A-2 pulley is intact.
