## Question

## Options

1. Hip disarticulation
2. Infectious diseases consultation and long-term suppressive antibiotics
3. Incision and drainage, removal of hardware, excision of heterotopic bone, and culture-directed antibiotics
4. Excision of heterotopic bone and radiation therapy to prevent recurrence
5. Excision of heterotopic bone and administration of nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs to prevent recurrence

## Correct option

**3. Incision and drainage, removal of hardware, excision of heterotopic bone, and culture-directed antibiotics**

## Answer explanation

This is a case of chronic osteomyelitis with infected hardware and a healed fracture. Limb salvage should be attempted in the patient. The presence of a chronic draining sinus requires surgical debridement. Removal of the implant and as much of the infected heterotopic bone will reduce the bacterial load. Culture-directed antibiotics are started after deep cultures are obtained. Infectious diseases consultation is obtained following surgical intervention. Long-term suppressive antibiotics are not the treament of choice in healthy patients who can tolerate a surgical procedure.
