## Question

Which structure is not at risk of injury during bone grafting from iliac crest?

## Options

1. Lateral cutaneous nerve of thigh
2. Superior cluneal nerve
3. Superior gluteal vessel
4. External iliac artery

## Correct option

**4. External iliac artery**

## Answer explanation

During iliac crest graft harvesting, the lateral femoral cutaneous nerve and ilioinguinal nerves are at risk as they are at close proximity from the anterior ilium. The superior cluneal nerves are at risk if dissection is carried farther than 8 cms lateral to the posterior superior iliac spine. The superior gluteal vessels can be damaged by retraction against the roof of the sciatic notch.

Answer : d
