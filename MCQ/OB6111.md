## Question

A 14-year-old girl has a painful hallux valgus deformity that has not responded to shoe modifications. Figure 21 shows a standing AP radiograph. What is the most appropriate surgical procedure?

![[q6111-a-21.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Distal soft-tissue realignment
2. Distal first metatarsal osteotomy with distal soft-tissue realignment
3. Proximal first metatarsal osteotomy with distal soft-tissue realignment
4. Proximal and distal first metatarsal osteotomy
5. Osteotomy of the proximal phalanx

## Correct option

**4. Proximal and distal first metatarsal osteotomy**

## Answer explanation

The radiograph reveals an increased first-second intermetatarsal angle and a congruent metatarsophalangeal joint with an abnormal distal metatarsal articular angle. Correction of both of these abnormalities requires a proximal and distal first metatarsal osteotomy.
