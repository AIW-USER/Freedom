## Question

A 22-year-old male from South Tyrol was traversing a mountain pass when his alphorn slipped and a dorsally directed force was sustained at his right thumb carpometacarpal joint. A local hand surgeon diagnoses the reducible CMCJ dislocation and recommends surgical fixation with a ligament reconstruction using flexor carpi radialis autograft. What structure will the Alpine hand surgeon reconstruct?

## Options

1. MCPJ ulnar collateral ligament
2. STT capsule
3. Volar- radial CMCJ capsule
4. Abductor Pollicis Longus
5. Volar oblique ligament

## Correct option

**5. Volar oblique ligament**

## Answer explanation

The volar oblique ligament is a primary stabilizer of the thumb CMCJ and is reconstructed in the CMCJ ligament reconstruction technique.

In acute thumb CMCJ instability, the volar oblique ligament usually tears off the first metacarpal in a subperiosteal fashion. The volar oblique ligament is a key structure in maintaining thumb CMCJ stability, which is the premise of the reconstruction first reported by Eaton and Littler. Cadaveric analyses have demonstrated that the dorsal radial ligament complex to be the primary restraint to dorsal CMCJ instability. Ligament reconstructions for CMCJ instability reconstruct the volar oblique ligament.

Eaton and Littler report on 18 patients who underwent a ligament reconstruction for unstable CMCJ. The authors noted that results were superior if the reconstruction was completed in non-arthritic thumbs.

*Simonian and Trumble compared 8 patients who underwent closed reduction and pinning with 9 patients who underwent ligament reconstruction for unstable thumb CMCJ.* The reconstruction group had slightly better abduction and pinch strength. Patients in both groups developed arthritic changes.

*Strauch et al. performed a cadaveric biomechanical analysis of CMCJ stability.* A dorsal dislocating force simulated a clinical dislocation. The primary restraint to dorsal dislocation was the dorsal radial ligament. The joint was most stable in pronation and extension which served to tighten the anterior oblique ligament. **The authors concluded** that the dorsal radial ligament is the primary restraining force to dorsal dislocation of the thumb CMCJ.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: The MCPJ ulnar collateral ligament is reconstructed in chronic ruptures with symptomatic instability of the MCPJ.
Answer 2: STT capsule is reconstructed in the 3LT reconstruction for chronic SL tears.
Answer 3: The Dorsal-radial capsule is also a primary stabilizer of the CMCJ.
Answer 4: This structure is used in the Eaton Littler reconstruction, but is not the structure being reconstructed.
