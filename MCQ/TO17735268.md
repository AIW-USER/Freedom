## Question

Most common cause of revision of total knee replacement is (arthroplasty- knee arthroplasty- revision TKR)

## Options

1. Aseptic loosening
2. Infection
3. Periprosthetic fracture
4. Instability

## Correct option

**2. Infection**

## Answer explanation

Most common cause of early failure is infection. Aseptic loosening is the most common cause of late failure. Overall 4 most common causes for total knee arthroplasty revision are-

1. Infection

2. Aseptic loosening

3. Periprosthetic fracture

4. Instability of the joint

ANSWER : B
