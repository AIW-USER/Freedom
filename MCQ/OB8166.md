## Question

A starting point entry portal that is too lateral on a trochanteric femoral nail will result in what deforming force?

## Options

1. Varus
2. Valgus
3. Flexion
4. Extension
5. Excessive hoop stress

## Correct option

**1. Varus**

## Answer explanation

The trochanteric entry portal for femoral nail insertion is increasingly being used by orthopaedic surgeons both for cephalomedullary implants and standard femoral nailing. In contradistinction to the piriformis fossa, the tip of the trochanter is not co-linear to the diaphyseal isthmus and an errant start can lead to the introduction of malalignment and/or iatrogenic comminution at the fracture site. The desired starting point should be at the tip or slightly medial to the tip of the greater trochanter to avoid varus malalignment and blow out of the lateral wall.
