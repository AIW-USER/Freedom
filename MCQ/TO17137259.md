Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Distal Humerus Fractures

## Question

Anterior transposition of ulnar nerve during ORIF of distal humerus fracture leads to [ FNB 2014]

## Options

1. Decreased incidence of ulnar neuritis
2. Increased incidence of ulnar neuritis
3. No effect on the incidence or increased incidence of ulnar neuritis
4. Increased fibrosis and decreased range of motion of elbow

## Correct option

**3. No effect on the incidence or increased incidence of ulnar neuritis**

## Answer explanation

Anterior transposition of ulnar nerve during ORIF of distal humerus fracture does not reduce the incidence of ulnar nerve neuritis, on the contrary many studies are available which show increased incidence of ulnar neuritis following anterior transposition of ulnar nerve. Literature does not support decreased ulnar nerve symptoms with transposition.

Answer: 3
