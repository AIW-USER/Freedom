## Question

Lateral quadruple complex consists of all except?(Regional orthopedics- knee- anatomy)

## Options

1. Iliotibial tract
2. Fabellofibular ligament
3. Popliteus
4. Lateral collateral ligament

## Correct option

**2. Fabellofibular ligament**

## Answer explanation

Lateral quadruple complex is made up of Iliotibial tract, Lateral collateral ligament , Popliteus tendon and Biceps muscle.

Answer: 2
