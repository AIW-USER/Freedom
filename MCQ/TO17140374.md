## Question

The lateral radiograph of the lumbosacral junction and pelvis of a 67-year-old woman with a lumbar deformity who is being evaluated for surgery. Angle “x” refers to which radiographic parameter?

![[TO17140374-1.png]]

## Options

1. Sacral slope
2. Pelvic tilt
3. Pelvic incidence
4. Slip angle

## Correct option

**3. Pelvic incidence**

## Answer explanation

![[TO17140374-2.png]]

PI is Relation between the sacral plate and the femoral heads. PI is Constant morphological parameter for one person and Does not change after skeletal maturity . PI is not posture dependent.

![[TO17140374-3.png]]

answer: c
