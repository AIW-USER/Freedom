## Question

In an effort to reduce costs, a limited MRI sequence is planned to detect a possible occult hip fracture. What is the anticipated fracture signal?

## Options

1. Bright on T1 and T2
2. Dark on T1 and T2
3. Dark on T1, bright on T2
4. Bright on T1, dark on T2
5. Enhancement by gadolinium

## Correct option

**3. Dark on T1, bright on T2**

## Answer explanation

At present, radiologists perform multiple MRI images to rule out all possible diagnoses. The ability to specify the anticipated changes on MRI should become more important as a means of reducing costs. MRI is sensitive to changes in free water (or hemorrhage) and thus this will appear dark on T1 and bright on T2.
