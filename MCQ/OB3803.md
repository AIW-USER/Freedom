## Options

1. MRI spine and brain
2. Obtain skeletal survey
3. SPEP and UPEP
4. Mammogram
5. CT chest

## Correct option

**5. CT chest**

## Answer explanation

Work up of giant cell tumor should include a radiograph or CT scan of the chest to evaluate for pulmonary metastasis.

Giant cell tumor is most commonly seen in females between the ages of 30-50. Giant cell tumors are considered a benign process that may metastasize to the lungs. Lesions of the wrist and distal radius have the highest rates of lung metastasis. Lung metastasis are considered benign as they have the same characteristics as the primary tissue. Metastases to the lungs overexpress the c-myc oncogene. The recommended treatment if pulmonary metastases are found is surgical resection yielding a 76% disease-free survival rate.

*Raskin et al reviewed the diagnosis and treatment giant cell tumors.* **They state** that CT of the chest should be performed prior to biopsy. **They state** that lung metastasis occur in about 2% of cases in giant cell tumor. **They state** lung metastasis are treated with surgical resection.

*Turcotte et al review giant cell tumor diagnosis and treatment.* **They discussed** that giant cell tumors are primarily treated with extensive curettage and reconstruction with adjuvant treatment. **They state** lung metastases occur more commonly in patients with distal radius or sacrum, stage 3 lesions, and multiple local recurrences.

Figure A shows an eccentric lytic epiphyseal lesion that borders subchondral bone consistent with a giant cell tumor. Figure B and C show a T1 and T2 image respectively of the same image that shows decreased signal intensity on T1 and increased signal intensity on T2. Figure D shows numerous giant cells which are hallmark of giant cell tumors.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: MRI of the spine and brain are not indicated based on the information given.
Answer 2: Skeletal survey may be done if suspicion for multiple myeloma is present.
Answer 3: SPEP/UPEP may be included in the work up for an elderly patient with a single bone lesion with an unknown primary.
Answer 4: Mammogram is indicated as screening for breast cancer of asymptomatic women.
