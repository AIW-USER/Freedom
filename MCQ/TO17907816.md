## Question

A Thalasemic child with Lateral condyle humerus fracture underwent k wire fixation. Post fixation he landed up with non union. Which Weiss classification type is most likely to result in the mentioned complication

## Options

1. Type 1
2. Type 2
3. Type 3
4. Type 4

## Correct option

**3. Type 3**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

Lateral condyle fractures have a higher risk of nonunion when treated non-operatively compared to other pediatric elbow fractures. This may be due to minimal metaphyseal bone on the distal fragment, intra-articular location, fracture rotation of 180o by the pull of the extensor tendons, tenuous blood supply, or instability in a cast. Displaced lateral condyle fractures >2mm are recommended to be treated operatively to achieve anatomic articular reduction and pinning. Other less common complications are stiffness, osteonecrosis, tardy ulnar nerve palsy, and fishtail deformity.

This classification system based on fracture displacement and articular congruity predicts the risk of complications, which were seen to be more than 3 times as likely to occur in type 3 fractures as in type 2 fractures (thus type 3 should have highest risk of non union).

As mentioned in the index article, operatively treated displaced lateral condyle fractures with the articular surface intact (type 2) had half the complications of displaced fractures without articular continuity (type 3). The higher complication rate in type 3 fractures may be attributable to the articular displacement and increased extent of initial energy.
