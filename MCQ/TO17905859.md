## Question

Permissible levels for Salbutamol in a 24 hours urine sample collected from an athlete

## Options

1. 1600 ng/ml
2. 1200 ng/ml
3. 800 ng/ml
4. 1000 ng/ml

## Correct option

**3. 800 ng/ml**

## Answer explanation

Ans 1: C

The WADA prohibited list indicates that Salbutamol use is allowed in inhaled dosages up to 1600

micrograms over 24 hours in divided doses, which should not exceed 600 micrograms (earlier it was

800) over 12 hours starting from any dose, which is considered the maximum therapeutic dose for

athletes with Therapeutic Use Exemption (TUE).

Regarding urine concentration, the presence in 24 hour urine sample of Salbutamol in excess of 1000

ng/ml or Formoterol in excess of 40 ng/ml is NOT consistent with therapeutic use of the substance

and will be considered as an Adverse analytical finding.

Choice D could be borderline scenario, so best answer here is choice C (800 ng/ml will be totally

safe).
