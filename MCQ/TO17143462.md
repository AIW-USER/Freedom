## Question

Which is a recommended indication for use of Pexidartinib

## Options

1. Synovial chondromatosis
2. Pigmented villonodular synovitis
3. Unresectable giant cell tumor of bone
4. Synovial sarcoma

## Correct option

**2. Pigmented villonodular synovitis**

## Answer explanation

Tenosynovial giant cell tumor can present as single nodules, also known as localized TGCT (L-TGCT), which is most frequently seen in the fingers. TGCT may also occur as multiple nodules, also known as diffuse-type TGCT (D-TGCT), most commonly occurring in the knee but also observed in the hip, ankle, wrist, shoulder or elbow. In 2013, the WHO reorganized the types of TGCT as L-TGCT and D-TGCT, replacing the terms giant cell tumor of the tendon sheath (GCT-TS) and pigmented villonodular synovitis (PVNS), respectively.

Pexidartinib, a colony stimulating factor 1 receptor (CSF-1R) antagonist, is the first approved systemic therapy for patients with tenosynovial giant cell tumor. Current indication is diffuse tenosynovial giant cell tumors which are not amenable to complete resection.

Answer : b
