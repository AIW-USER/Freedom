Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Tendoachilles Injury

## Question

Which of the following nerves is at risk while performing a surgical repair of the tendo Achillis?

## Options

1. Sural nerve
2. Saphenous nerve
3. Tibial nerve
4. Lateral plantar nerve

## Correct option

**1. Sural nerve**

## Answer explanation

The sural nerve passes lateral to the tendo Achilles and can be injured during repair of the tendon. Making a skin incision slightly medial to the tendo Achilles when performing a surgical repair can reduce this risk.

Answer: 1
