## Question

What does the arrow indicate in the picture given below? (FNB Pattern Paper 2014)

![[TO17137352-1.png]]

## Options

1. Sail sign
2. Codman’s sign
3. Posterior fat pad sign
4. Gull sign

## Correct option

**1. Sail sign**

## Answer explanation

The sail sign or anterior fat pad sign indicates the elbow effusion and elevation of anterior fat pad due to intra articular fracture. The anterior fat pad is usually concealed within the coronoid fossa. When there is a joint effusion, the anterior fat pad (which is intra-articular, but extra synovial) becomes elevated. Most common cause of sail or anterior fat pad sign is supracondylar fracture in children and radial head fracture in adults. Where a fat pad sign is present but no fracture is seen on X ray, occult fracture should be suspected.

Answer: 1
