## Question

Which of the following Figures represents the exercise type that strengthens muscle most efficiently?

![[q1385-a-muscle-concentric-contraction.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q1385-b-eccentric.jpg]]
*Figure B*

![[q1385-c-muscle-isotonic-contraction.jpg]]
*Figure C*

![[q1385-d-muscle-contraction-cybex-labeled.jpg]]
*Figure D*

![[q1385-e-muscle-plyometric.jpg]]
*Figure E*

## Options

1. Figure A
2. Figure B
3. Figure C
4. Figure D
5. Figure E

## Correct option

**2. Figure B**

## Answer explanation

Figure B represents eccentric exercise and is the excercise that most efficiently strengthens skeletal muscle. Eccentric exercise is a type of isotonic exercise that includes constant muscle tension while the muscle is lengthening. Figure A represents a concentric exercise, also a type of isotonic exercise, as the muscle is shortening and the tension is proportional to the external load. Figure C represents isometric exercise since there is constant muscle length and tension that is proportional to the external load and no movement. A similar movement would include pushing against an immovable object such as a wall. Figure D displays isokinetic strengthening on a Cybex machine and involves movement at a constant speed (often controlled by a dynamometer). Figure E is an example of plyometrics and includes exercises using explosive movements to develop muscular power (such as bounding, hopping, and jumping).
