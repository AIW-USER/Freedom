Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Polytrauma Patients

## Question

Swan Ganz catheter is used in management of

## Options

1. Suprapubic catheterization in pelvic fracture with urethral injury
2. In management of cardiogenic shock after spinal injury
3. In management of neurogenic bladder after spinal injury
4. In management of raised intracranial pressure after head injury

## Correct option

**2. In management of cardiogenic shock after spinal injury**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 2
Swan-Ganz catheter or the pulmonary artery catheter are used for hemodynamic monitoring modality in critical care. This invasive method involves placement of a central venous catheter into the internal jugular vein. As the flow-directed catheter is advanced, waveforms are tracked that reveal the location of the catheter tip as well as the pressures associated with that particular part of the heart or pulmonary vasculature. These pressures can then be used to help differentiate between different forms of shock (i.e. cardiogenic versus neurogenic shock), but require correct interpretation.
A complete set of hemodynamic data includes the direct measurement of CVP (discussed above), right atrial pressure, right ventricular pressures, pulmonary artery pressures, pulmonary artery occlusion or wedge pressure (PAWP), and cardiac output by the thermodilution or Fick method (requiring actual measurement of oxygen consumption and the arteriovenous oxygen concentration difference). Additional parameters such as the indirect Fick cardiac output (based on assumed values of metabolism/oxygen consumption, arterial saturation, and body surface area that are unlikely to be valid in the critically ill), systemic vascular and pulmonary vascular resistance are calculated.
As an estimate of left heart filling pressures, the PAWP can help to differentiate between cardiogenic shock and distributive or hypovolemic shock, as it will be elevated in the former and decreased in the latter.
