Chapter: Spine MCQs — Kyphoplasty Vertebroplasty

## Question

Which of the following statements is false when comparing vertebroplasty with kyphoplasty?

## Options

1. In kyphoplasty one of the goals is to restore vertebral height and this is not the case in vertebroplasty
2. PMMA bone cement is injected at a higher pressure in kyphoplasty when compared with vertebroplasty
3. Kyphoplasty requires additional equipment compared with vertebroplasty
4. Improvement in the level of pain is comparable in both procedures

## Correct option

**2. PMMA bone cement is injected at a higher pressure in kyphoplasty when compared with vertebroplasty**

## Answer explanation

Direct comparisons between vertebroplasty and kyphoplasty are not possible because of a lack of prospective data comparing the two approaches. Indirect comparisons suggest that vertebroplasty and kyphoplasty reduce pain comparably. Both procedures appear to improve patient function in most series, although available data usually cannot be pooled because of a wide variety of measurement scales. Vertebral height restoration and reduction of kyphotic deformity are also similar for both procedures.

The cement applying pressure is less in kyphoplasty compared to vertebroplsty because we create space before applying cement in kyphoplasty. This is done by inflating ballon to restore vertebral height that creates a void. Hence cement inserting pressure goes low. However, pain relief is comparable in both procedures.

Answer: 2
