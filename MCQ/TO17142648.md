Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Tennis Elbow

## Question

A 55-year-old patient has chronic pain over the lateral aspect of the elbow,exacerbated when playing backhand tennis strokes. On examination she has pain with resisted middle finger extension. Which muscle attachment is most likely involved?

## Options

1. Brachioradialis.
2. Extensor digitorum communis.
3. Extensor carpi radialis longus.
4. Extensor carpi radialis brevis.

## Correct option

**4. Extensor carpi radialis brevis.**

## Answer explanation

Tennis Elbow is an overuse injury caused by eccentric overload at the origin of the common extensor tendon, leading to tendinosis and inflammation of the ECRB. Precipitated by repetitive wrist extension and forearm pronation

ECRB). Histologically the lesion that was consistently identified at surgery was immature fibroblastic and vascular infiltration (Angio fibroblastic

dysplasia) of the origin of ECRB.

Resisted movements of the wrist and finger extensors are usually painful.

Cozen’s test: The test is said to be positive if a resisted wrist extension triggers pain to the lateral aspect of the elbow owing to stress placed upon the tendon of the extensor carpi radialis brevis muscle. The test is performed with an extended elbow.

ECRB > ECRL are the 2 muscles that get involved in lateral epicondylitis.

Treatment: - Non-Operative activity modification, ice, NSAIDS, physical therapy, counterforce brace (strap)

Answer: 4
