Chapter: Spine MCQs — Spinal Infections

## Question

A 25 years male has Mid-Backache and a spinal lesion at T7-9. He is also having history of progressive neurological deficit. On imaging there is mild osteopenia, mild discitis, mild signal intensity changes in cord but no paravertebral collection. Ideal management option for the case would be

## Options

1. Anterior decompression and fusion with antibiotic coverage
2. Antibiotics and observe
3. Posterior decompression and stabilisation
4. CT guided biopsy and broad spectrum antibiotics

## Correct option

**3. Posterior decompression and stabilisation**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

EXPLANATION: -

According to the case scenario the pt is suffering from spinal infection (discitis) with progressive neurological deficit. It could be either progenitor or TB infection.

In any infection the indication of surgery is

1. Progressive significant neuro deficit

2. Significant instability (Ganga TB spine score >3 /spine at risk sign >2)

3. Significant Kyphosis (lumbar >30 deg/ thoracic > 40 deg)

4. Lack of response to ATT /AKT or antibiotics in case of pyogenic infections.

If there is no neurological deficit/ significant deformity/ instability in this case then the best option will be CT guided biopsy and antibiotics.

The specimen should be sent to investigate TB, Pyogenic and fungal infections. If the investigations are negative then we have to decide on clinical and radiological parameters.

Treat as pyogenic if: -

• The presentation is acute with septicaemia features and severe pain.

• Lumbar spine involvement with predominant disc space involvement without significant paraspinal abscess.

• Older individuals with immunocompromised status.

Treat as TB if: -

• The presentation is subacute / chronic

• Multiple lesions / skip lesions in spine

• Paraspinal abscess collection

• Predominant thoracic / thoracolumbar spine.

MUST NOTE,

There is no role for empirical antibiotics in spinal infection without biopsy unless the patient is not willing for biopsy.

Role of anterior surgery in infection is limited now a days. It is best suited for cases where the anterior cord compression is significant and to relieve that compress one can go for anterior decompression. But the fixation points are limited and the screws has to be put in involved vertebra where the hold will be good.
