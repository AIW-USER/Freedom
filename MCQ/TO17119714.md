## Question

What does a positive tangent sign on the MRI predicts?

## Options

1. Irreparable supraspinatus due to fatty atrophy
2. Fatty atrophy of subscapularis muscle indicating irreparability
3. Retracted cuff tear medial to glenoid
4. Biceps dislocation due to subscapularis tear

## Correct option

**1. Irreparable supraspinatus due to fatty atrophy**

## Answer explanation

The tangent sign is useful in helping to quantify supraspinatus muscle belly fatty atrophy with a positive sign implicated with an irreparable tear or poorer outcome after supraspinatus tendon tear repair.

On a sagittal oblique plane, a line is drawn between the upper surface of the scapular spine and the upper surface of the coracoid process (medial aspect). A normal supraspinatus muscle belly lies above this line (negative sign) and an abnormal supraspinatus muscle belly (small from fatty atrophy) lies below this line (positive sign)

Answer: 1
