## Question

Ideal glenoid component position in TSA would be

## Options

1. Neutral to 10 degree retroversion
2. 10-20 degree anteversion
3. 10-20 degree retroversion
4. 20-30 degree retroversion

## Correct option

**1. Neutral to 10 degree retroversion**

## Answer explanation

In the TSA, the stem should be placed in 25-45 degree retroversion. The Glenoid component is placed in neutral or slight retroversion. If glenoid retroversion is required then humeral stem retroversion is reduced to avoid posterior dislocation.

Answer -A
