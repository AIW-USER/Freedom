## Question

A healthy 2-year-old boy falls from a swing and sustains a displaced midshaft femoral fracture with 1 cm of shortening. What is the most appropriate treatment?

## Options

1. Pavlik harness
2. Skeletal traction for 3 weeks followed by a spica cast
3. Skin traction for 3 weeks followed by a spica cast
4. Closed reduction and spica casting
5. Closed reduction and an intramedullary pin

## Correct option

**4. Closed reduction and spica casting**

## Answer explanation

For children between the ages of 1 and 6 years, closed reduction and early spica casting is recommended. In some instances, associated injuries or body habitus may preclude cast treatment. Pavlik harness treatment of femoral fractures is for infants younger than 1 year of age. Rarely is there an indication for traction. Internal fixation is reserved in general for children older than age 6 years or with confounding factors.
