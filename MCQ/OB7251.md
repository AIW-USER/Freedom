## Question

Posttraumatic physeal arrest is most common at which of the following locations?

## Options

1. Proximal tibia
2. Proximal humerus
3. Distal radius
4. Distal humerus
5. Distal tibia

## Correct option

**5. Distal tibia**

## Answer explanation

Posttraumatic physeal arrest occurs most commonly in the distal medial tibia. *Using MRI, Echlund and associates confirmed this finding.* Arrest of the distal radius and proximal humerus are rare after trauma. Traumatic injuries of the distal femoral and distal ulnar physis have a high incidence of growth arrest as well.
