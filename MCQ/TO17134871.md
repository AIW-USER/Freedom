Chapter: Spine MCQs — Lumbar Disc Herniation

## Question

Far lateral disc herniation at L5-S1 level will cause (FNB Pattern Paper 2014)

## Options

1. Absent plantar reflex
2. Weakness of hip abduction
3. Exaggerated EHL
4. Trendelenburg gait

## Correct option

**4. Trendelenburg gait**

## Answer explanation

A far lateral disc will be involving an existing nerve (L5 in this case). Now, L5 nerve supplies Extensor hallucis longs (EHL) and hence loss of function of EHL would have been the most appropriate clinical scenario, however, it’s not an option.

From the options given the best pick will be Trendelenburg gait, due to weakness in gluteus Medius.

Although this can affect abduction at hip but the effect in this aspect may be minimal. Hip abduction to be specific is achieved by a number of other contributors also apart from Glutes Medius viz. Gluteus minim, TFL, Piriformis, Sartorius, Superior fibers of Gluteus maximus which have an innervation spanning from L5-52.

Answer: 4
