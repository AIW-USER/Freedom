## Question

During which period of gait does the tibialis anterior contract eccentrically?

## Options

1. Mid-stance
2. Heel strike
3. Pre-swing
4. Initial swing
5. Mid-swing

## Correct option

**2. Heel strike**

## Answer explanation

At the beginning of heel strike, the tibialis anterior contracts eccentrically while the foot is lowered to the ground.

In order to understand pathologic gait disturbances, a thorough understanding of normal gait is necessary. During initial heel strike, the tibialis anterior contracts eccentrically to assist in limb loading and shock absorption. With mild to moderate weakness in the tibialis anterior, as seen in a peroneal nerve palsy, the foot is not allowed to gradually lower leading to a "foot slap." During the swing phase, weakness in the tibialis anterior causes a "steppage gait" (increased hip and knee flexion) in order to clear the foot while it moves forward.

*Winter et al. analyze the gait patterns of 15 normal adults.* Through their biochemical analysis, **they concluded** that rehabilitation of lower extremity injuries should focus on eccentric knee extensor and concentric plantarflexor exercises.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: At mid-stance, the tibialis anterior is inactive.
Answer 3: During pre-swing, the tibialis anterior is inactive.
Answer 4: During initial swing, the tibialis anterior contracts concentrically.
Answer 5: During mid-swing, the tibialis anterior contracts concentrically.
