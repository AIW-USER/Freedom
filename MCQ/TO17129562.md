## Question

Regarding TB spine, most common pattern among the following would be (FNB 2022)

## Options

1. Central
2. Anterior
3. Posterior
4. Facetal

## Correct option

**2. Anterior**

## Answer explanation

Ans: B

The main route of infection in skeletal TB is through hematogenous spread from the primary source (usually lungs/ lymph nodes). The mycobacterium is deposited in the end arterials in the vertebral body adjacent to the anterior aspect of the vertebral end plate. Thus, the anterior portion of the vertebral body is most commonly involved. The lower thoracic region is the most common segment; next in decreasing order of frequency are the lumbar, upper thoracic, cervical, and sacral regions.
