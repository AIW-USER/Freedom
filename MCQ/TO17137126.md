Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Fracture Of Distal End Radius

## Question

Patient presented with distal end radius fracture in the emergency. Emergency doctor did close
reduction and cast was given in slight palmar flexion and ulnar deviation. Immediately after the cast
application patient developed complaints of numbness and pain in thumb and index finger. What is
the next step in management?

## Options

1. Immediate release of cast and re reduction
2. Urgent carpal tunnel release
3. ORIF with plate and carpal tunnel release
4. Observation only

## Correct option

**3. ORIF with plate and carpal tunnel release**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C
This is a case of acute carpal tunnel syndrome and likely so the nerve is entrapped at fracture site
and hence warrants urgent exploration and carpal tunnel release.
