Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Complications of THR

## Question

Which structure is at risk from placement of the Hohmann retractor along the anterior rim of the acetabulum?

## Options

1. Femoral artery and nerve
2. Psoas tendon
3. External iliac artery and vein
4. Obturator artery

## Correct option

**1. Femoral artery and nerve**

## Answer explanation

Explanation:
Placement of a curved cobra or Hohmann retractor in the interval between the anterior rim of the acetabulum and the psoas tendon puts the femoral nerve and vessels at risk. Erroneous placement of this retractor over the psoas muscle can cause injury to the femoral nerve or adjacent vessels. The risk increases with a more inferior placement of the retractor. The safest position is near the level of the anterior superior iliac spine.

Answer: 1
