## Question

True about bone cement is all except

## Options

1. Acts as space filling grout aiding load transfer
2. Strong in shear
3. Strong in compression
4. Young’s modulus -2.2GPa

## Correct option

**2. Strong in shear**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: bone cement is weak in shear and strong in compression

Answer: 2
