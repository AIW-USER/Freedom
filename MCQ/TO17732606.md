## Question

What is not true about the Kanavel sign? (regional orthopedics-hand and wrist- pyogenic flexor tenosynovitis )

## Options

1. Fusiform swelling of finger
2. Pain on passive flexion
3. Tenderness along finger
4. Pain on passive extension

## Correct option

**2. Pain on passive flexion**

## Answer explanation

Cardinal signs of flexor tenosynovitis are commonly known as Kanavel signs. These are 4 signs:

- Flexed posture of finger
- Exquisite pain on extension
- Exquisite tenderness along the course of the flexor sheath
- Fusiform swelling of finger(4th cardinal sign was not given by canavel and added later)
