## Question

Which of the following is associated with achondroplasia?

## Options

1. Atlanto-axial instability
2. Central sleep apnea
3. Dural ectasia
4. Genu valgum
5. Cauliflower ear

## Correct option

**2. Central sleep apnea**

## Answer explanation

Stenosis of the foramen magnum may be present during infancy in a patient with achondroplasia, causing central sleep apnea.

Foramen magnum stenosis is a known cause of sudden death in young children with achondroplasia, as it can result in central sleep apnea and cessation of breathing. As a result, it is recommended that all infants patients with achondroplasia undergo screening with a sleep study. Physical examination findings associated with foramen magnum stenosis include clonus, hypotonia, hyperreflexia, clumsiness, and difficulty swallowing. MRI or CT is indicated if there are abnormal clinical findings or sleep study.

Shirley and Ain. review the orthopedic manifestations of achondroplasia. They note that foramen magnum stenosis, spinal stenosis, thoracolumbar kyphosis, short stature, congenital radial head dislocations and genu varum are orthopaedic manifestations of this dysplasia.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Atlanto-axial instability is associated with Down Syndrome and skeletal dysplasias such as the mucopolysaccharidoses.
Answer 3: Dural ectasia is associated with neurofibromatosis and Marfan Syndrome.
Answer 4: Genu varum is common in achondroplastic patients, not genu valgum.
Answer 5: Cauliflower ear may occur patients with diastrophic dysplastic.
