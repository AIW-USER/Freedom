## Question

What is the most frequent location of entrapment of the deep peroneal nerve?

## Options

1. Tendon of the extensor hallucis brevis
2. Inferior extensor retinaculum
3. Osteophytes of the talonavicular joint
4. Os intermetatarseum
5. Base of the fifth metatarsal

## Correct option

**2. Inferior extensor retinaculum**

## Answer explanation

The most frequently described entrapment of the deep peroneal nerve is the anterior tarsal tunnel syndrome. This syndrome refers to entrapment of the deep peroneal nerve under the inferior extensor retinaculum. Entrapment can also occur as the nerve passes under the tendon of the extensor hallucis brevis. Compression by underlying dorsal osteophytes of the talonavicular joint and an os intermetatarseum (between the bases of the first and second metatarsals) have previously been described in runners.
