## Question

What percentage of the gait cycle is spent in the stance phase?

## Options

1. 20%
2. 40%
3. 60%
4. 80%
5. Over 90%

## Correct option

**3. 60%**

## Answer explanation

Normal gait is a combination of postures and muscle activities and is divided into two phases: swing and stance. The swing phase accounts for 40% and the stance phase accounts for 60% of the normal gait cycle. The swing phase is divided into three phases: initial, mid-swing, and terminal swing. The stance phase is the longer phase and is divided into five different activities: initial contact, loading response, mid-stance, terminal stance, and pre-swing.
