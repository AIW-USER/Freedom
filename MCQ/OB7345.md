## Question

A 57-year-old man who plays recreational sports reports pain in his dominant shoulder. An MR arthrogram is shown in Figure 57. During arthroscopy of the shoulder, what pathology is most likely to be found?

![[q7345-a-57.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Complete disruption of the transverse humeral ligament
2. Acromioclavicular joint arthritis
3. Absent coracohumeral ligament
4. Subscapularis tear and biceps subluxation
5. Complete rupture of the short head of the biceps

## Correct option

**4. Subscapularis tear and biceps subluxation**

## Answer explanation

The MR arthrogram shows medial subluxation of the biceps tendon out of the bicipital groove and a subscapularis tendon tear. Biceps tendon subluxation is almost always associated with subscapularis tears. Whereas other diagnoses can be associated, none of them is directly related to this finding or seen on the MR arthrogram.
