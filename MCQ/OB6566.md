## Question

A 40-year-old woman underwent an arthroscopic acromioplasty and mini-open rotator cuff repair 4 weeks ago. At follow-up examination, the incision is painful, erythematous, and draining fluid. The patient is febrile and has an elevated WBC count. What infectious organism should be under high suspicion of causing this outcome?

## Options

1. Escherichia coli
2. Streptococcus viridans
3. Oxalophagus oxalicus
4. Proprionobacter acnes
5. Enterococcus faecalis

## Correct option

**4. Proprionobacter acnes**

## Answer explanation

Proprionobacter acnes has been a leading cause of indolent shoulder infections. During shoulder arthroscopy, the arthroscopic fluid may actually dilute the shoulder preparation and lead to a higher rate of infection during subsequent mini-open rotator cuff repair surgery. The remaining bacteria listed are rarely associated with shoulder infections after arthroscopy.
