Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — ACL

## Question

Which imaging feature is not suggestive of ACL injury

## Options

1. Bone bruise of medial tibial plateau
2. Segond fracture
3. Lateral femoral notch sign
4. Anterior translation of tibia more than 5mm

## Correct option

**1. Bone bruise of medial tibial plateau**

## Answer explanation

Imaging of ACL injury:

- Bone bruising is very common in ACL tears. Most common site of bone bruise associated with ACL injury is posterior lateral tibial plateau and lateral femoral condyle.

![[TO17118666-1.jpg]]

- The deep lateral femoral notch sign (curved arrow in fig. below), although uncommon, is quite specific for ACL tear and is due to impaction injury of the lateral femoral condyle onto the tibia. A notch depth of over 2 mm is diagnostic of ACL tear.

![[TO17118666-2.jpg]]

- If there is ≥ 5 mm anterior translocator of the tibia relative to the femur, this would be indicative of ACL tear, while an anterior tibial translation > 7 mm is fully diagnostic of ACL tear.

![[TO17118666-3.jpg]]

- PCL buckling is caused by anterior subluxation of the tibia in ACL tears, considering that hyper buckling disappears after ACL reconstruction. Therefore, a PCL buckling that is present after ACL reconstruction is suggestive of ACL laxity.
- Segond fracture results from internal rotation and varus stresses of the knee in flexion. This is an avulsion injury of the lateral tibial plateau and mid lateral capsule (anterolateral ligament) that frequently accompanies ACL rupture.

![[TO17118666-4.png]]

- Empty notch sign: a fluid signal at the site of femoral attachment at the intercondylar notch, denotes avulsion at the femoral attachment.
- Abnormal anterior cruciate ligament orientation relative to intercondylar (Blumensaat's) line-

ACL fibers are subjectively less steep than a line tangent to the intercondylar roof (Blumensaat's line)

ACL angle (angle between the intercondylar line and ACL) >15° with the apex of the angle located anteriorly, indicating a less steep ACL line - this indicates a ruptured and collapsed ligament

Answer: 1 (bone bruise of lateral tibial plateau is suggestive of ACL injury)
