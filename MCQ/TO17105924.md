Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Periprosthetic Fractures

## Question

Ogden construct can be used for (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Distal femur fractures
2. Physeal injuries in children
3. Periprosthetic fractures in femur
4. Neck femur fracture fixation

## Correct option

**3. Periprosthetic fractures in femur**

## Answer explanation

The Ogden plate and cable construct is useful in stabilizing periprosthetic fractures (Vancouver type B) after Total Hip Arthroplasty. Originally before the invention of locking plates Ogden system consisted of plates with standard non locking screw distally and cable proximally. After emergence of locking screws unicortical locking screws proximally and bicortical locking screw distally were added to augment cable and nonlocking screws.

Answer: 3
