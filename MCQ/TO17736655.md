## Question

Which is the most consistent injury seen after a lateral patellar dislocation (regional orthopaedics- knee- patellofemoral instability)

## Options

1. MPFL tear
2. Patello tibial and patello meniscal ligaments
3. Lateral femoral condyle trochlea fracture
4. VMO disruption

## Correct option

**1. MPFL tear**

## Answer explanation

MPFL (Medial PatelloFemoral Ligament) tear is an integral part of lateral patellar dislocation. The ligament is the primary restraint against a lateral dislocation between 0-30o knee flexion.

Answer: 1
