Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — Meniscus Injuries

## Question

Best zone for meniscal repair is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Red-red
2. White-white
3. Red-white
4. Central zone

## Correct option

**1. Red-red**

## Answer explanation

From prenatal development until shortly after birth, the meniscus is fully vascularized. Afterwards, vascularization decreases with age. At 10 years of age, vascularization is present in around 10-30% of the meniscus, and at maturity only the peripheral 10–30% of the meniscus is vascularized (red-red zone). Meniscal tears are divided on the basis of their location in the meniscus. From out inwards menisci have an outer red-red zone which has rich blood supply, middle red-white zone which has a few blood vessels and inner white-white zone with no blood vessels. A peripheral meniscal tear in the red-red zone has the maximum healing chances whereas central meniscal tear in the white-white zone almost never heals.

Answer: 1
