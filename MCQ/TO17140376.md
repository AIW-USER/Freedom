## Question

True about pelvic movement in sitting and standing position in a person with normal mobile spine is

## Options

1. Pelvis is anteverted in sitting upright position and retroverts during sitting to stand motion
2. Pelvis is retroverted in the sitting upright position
3. Lumbar losrdosis increases in sitting upright position
4. Pelvis is retroverted in the sitting upright position and further retroverts during sit-to-stand motion

## Correct option

**2. Pelvis is retroverted in the sitting upright position**

## Answer explanation

The pelvis is retroverted in the sitting upright position and gradually anteverted during sit-to-stand motion. Lumbar lordosis decreased in the sitting upright position, temporarily decreased further (sitting anteflexed), and then increased in the standing position (standing anteflexed and standing upright). The mechanical loads on lumbosacral segments were greater after seat-off due to the reverse movement between upper lumbar and pelvic segments.

Answer : b
