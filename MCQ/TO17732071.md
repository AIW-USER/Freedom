## Question

Which is not seen in adhesive capsulitis?( Regional orthopedics-shoulder- adhesive capsulitis)

## Options

1. Joint capsule thickening
2. Obliteration of fat in subcoracoid space
3. Thickened CHL
4. Widening and thickening of axillary pouch

## Correct option

**4. Widening and thickening of axillary pouch**

## Answer explanation

Rotator interval:

 Boundaries of rotator interval: Coracoid process (COR) at its base, superiorly by anterior margin of supraspinatus tendon (SST) and inferiorly by superior margin of subscapularis tendon (SSC).

Contents of rotator interval include long head of biceps tendon (BT), coracohumeral ligament (CHL), superior glenohumeral ligament (SGHL), and rotator interval capsule.

Rotator interval changes (On MRI) in adhesive capsulitis:

- Thickening of the axillary recess: may show thickening ≥1.3 cm
- Joint capsule thickening : anterior capsule thickness >3.5 mm and abnormal hyperintensity
- Abnormal soft tissue thickening within the rotator interval with signal alteration
- Abnormal soft tissue encasing the biceps anchor

- Thickening of the coracohumeral ligament
- Subcoracoid triangle sign: Obliteration of fat signal in subcoracoid space.

Answer: 4
