Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — PCL

## Question

Most common mechanism of PCL injury is (FNB Patter Paper 2014)

## Options

1. Hyper flexion non-contact injury
2. Hyperextension non-contact injury
3. Blow on the front of upper tibia while knee is flexed and foot is plantar flexed
4. Blow on the back of upper tibia while knee is flexed and foot is plantar flexed

## Correct option

**3. Blow on the front of upper tibia while knee is flexed and foot is plantar flexed**

## Answer explanation

PCL tears are typically produced by external trauma such as the classic “dashboard injury” resulting from a posteriorly directed force on the anterior aspect of the proximal tibia with the knee flexed and foot plantar flexed. In athletics, the typical mechanism of isolated PCL tears is a direct blow to the anterior tibia or a fall onto the knee with the foot in a plantar flexed position. Football, soccer, rugby and skiing are among the sports with the highest incidence of PCL tears. Non-contact mechanisms, such as hyperflexion or hyperextension, are less common. Hyperflexion injuries are more common than hyperextension injuries.

Answer: 3
