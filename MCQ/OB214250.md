## Answer explanation

Quadriceps strengthening with the feet unsupported (open chain) should be avoided following ACL reconstruction as it places unnecessary force across the graft.

Rehabilitation following ACL reconstruction begins immediately with early passive extension and weight-bearing. Many exercises are utilized during rehab with specific attention to not increase the force across the reconstruction. Eccentric quadriceps strengthening in the earlier postoperative period has been shown to increase quadriceps volume and strength. Special attention is given to open vs closed chain exercises for quadriceps strengthening. Open chain maneuvers, such as the seated leg extension, allows the quadriceps to pull the tibia anteriorly, which can stress the graft. Closed chain quadriceps exercises (leg press) and isometric bodyweight exercises (straight leg raise) are encouraged to help regain quadriceps strength without compromising the reconstruction.

*Beutler et al reviewed EMG activity in quadriceps following 1-legged, closed chain exercises in healthy patients.* *They report on both 1-legged squats and step-ups in an attempt to see if these exercises are suitable for post-ACL reconstruction patients.* **They conclude** that single leg, closed chain exercises provide effective quadriceps activation to gain strength, while also protecting the ACL reconstruction.

*Beynnon et al review biomechanical effects of rehabilitation exercises on ACL strain.* **They report** that weighted, open chain knee extension placed a significantly increased strain across the normal ACL compared to a non-weighted active range of motion. **They conclude** that exercises that are primarily hamstring driven (isometric hamstring contractions at any angle), involve quadriceps strengthening with the knee flexed >60°, or involve active knee motion between 35-90° do not place excessive force across the ACL.

Figure A is the demonstration of a patient performing a body-weight squat. Figure B depicts a closed-chain leg press. Figure C shows a seated leg extension. Figure D demonstrates prone hamstring curls. Figure E shows short arc knee extensions.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1-2: Bodyweight squats and leg presses are both closed chain quadriceps maneuvers and are encouraged following ACL reconstruction.
Answer 4: Hamstring curls, while open chain, result in isolated hamstring contraction and have been shown not to increase force across the ACL.
Answer 5: Short arc knee extension, while technically open chain, is an acceptable exercise following ACL reconstruction if performed without weight and limited to 0-30 degrees.
