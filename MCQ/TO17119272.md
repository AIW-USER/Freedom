## Question

What is the optimal position to access the knee collateral ligaments? (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. 90-degree knee flexion
2. Full extension of knee
3. 30-degree knee flexion
4. 30-degree knee flexion with slight external rotation of the leg

## Correct option

**3. 30-degree knee flexion**

## Answer explanation

Collateral ligaments are best tested by the stress tests. Examiner keeps one hand on the knee of the patient and with the other hand holds the leg to give a varus or valgus stress to the knee. Performing the tests at 30° knee flexion relaxes other structures in the knee and one can specifically isolate the testing to the collateral ligaments. More than 10 mm opening and soft end point indicates complete ligament tear.

In 30-degree flexion
In full knee extension

Varus stress test
If lateral joint opening occurs, it indicates LCL injury
If lateral joint opening occurs, it indicates PLC and PCL injury in addition to LCL injury

Valgus stress test
If medial joint opening occurs, it indicates MCL injury
If medial joint opening occurs, it indicates concomitant posteromedial capsule, posterior oblique ligament (POL) and PCL injury in addition to MCL injury

Answer: 3
