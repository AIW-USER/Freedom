## Question

A patient with a grade 2 L5-S1 isthmic spondylolisthesis reports low back pain and bilateral lower extremity pain. Nonsurgical management has failed to provide relief, and the patient is now a candidate for surgical intervention. The surgeon elects to proceed with L5-S1 laminectomy and posterior instrumented fusion after reduction of the spondylolisthesis. If a postoperative neurologic deficit develops, what structure has most likely been affected?

## Options

1. L4 nerve root
2. L5 nerve root
3. S1 nerve root
4. Genitofemoral nerve
5. Cauda equina

## Correct option

**2. L5 nerve root**

## Answer explanation

The L5 nerve root is especially vulnerable and prone to injury after the reduction of spondylolisthesis in patients with mid-and high-grade isthmic spondylolisthesis. The genitofemoral nerve is more commonly injured during anterior retroperitoneal approaches to the lumbar spine. Injury to the cauda equina often leads to bowel and bladder dysfunction and lower extremity weakness and is uncommon after reduction maneuvers.
