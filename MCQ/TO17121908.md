Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — Wrist Arthroscopy

## Question

Which nerve injury is not common in wrist arthroscopy

## Options

1. dorsal sensory branch of the ulnar nerve
2. sensory branch of radial nerve
3. motor branch of ulnar nerve
4. PIN

## Correct option

**3. motor branch of ulnar nerve**

## Answer explanation

3 nerves, dorsal sensory branch of the ulnar nerve, sensory branch of radial nerve and PIN are at risk of getting injured during wrist arthroscopy through dorsal portals.

Answer: 3
