Chapter: Spine MCQs — Scoliosis Kyphosis

## Question

Physical examination findings suggesting possible non-idiopathic scoliosis are all except

## Options

1. Hairy patches
2. Subcutaneous tumors/ Pigmented lesions
3. Excessive flexibility/Joint contractures
4. Truncal shift

## Correct option

**4. Truncal shift**

## Answer explanation

Physical examination findings suggesting possible non-idiopathic scoliosis

- Hairy patches - Spina bifida or diastematomyelia
- Subcutaneous tumors - Neurofibromatosis
- Pigmented lesions - Neurofibromatosis
- Excessive flexibility - Ehlers–Danlos or Marfan syndrome
- Joint contractures- Cerebral palsy or arthrogryposis
- Craniofacial abnormalities- Syndromic scoliosis
- Weakness of extremities- Neuromuscular scoliosis.

Truncal shift signifies loss of coronal balance and it is seen is large idiopathic scoliosis

answer: d
