## Question

Preferred prophylactic antibiotic in case of penicillin allergy in orthopedic surgery is [General Ortho.-Pharmacology & Perioperative Workup]

## Options

1. Vancomycin
2. Cefazolin
3. Clindamycin
4. Teicoplanin

## Correct option

**1. Vancomycin**

## Answer explanation

Most common organisms causing postoperative infection are Staphylococcus aureus, Staphylococcus epidermidis, and Streptococcus species, the usual choice of prophylactic antibiotic is a first-generation cephalosporin, such as cefazolin. In patients with significant penicillin allergy, vancomycin or clindamycin may be used.

Answer: 1
