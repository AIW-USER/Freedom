## Question

Which of the following variables is a risk factor for graft failure following ACL reconstruction?

## Options

1. Concomintant MCL injury
2. Early accelerated reharbilitation protocol
3. Graft-screw divergence of 35 degrees
4. Presence of a Segond fracture of preoperative radiographs
5. Tibial tunnel placement 10mm in front of the anterior border of the PCL

## Correct option

**3. Graft-screw divergence of 35 degrees**

## Answer explanation

Graft-screw divergence exceeding 30 degrees is a risk factor for ACL graft failure. Participation in early accelerated rehabilitation protocol does not have an increased risk of reinjury compared with nonaccelerated rehabilitation programs.

The most common cause of ACL failure is tunnel malposition. However, other causes of reinjury include inadequate graft fixation and missed concomitant posterolateral corner (PLC) injuries. Graft-screw divergence >30 degrees from the trajectory of the femoral tunnel may lead to failure of fixation and early ACL failure. Conversely, recent randomized clinical trials that compared early accelerated versus nonaccelerated rehabilitation programs demonstrated no significant differences in function, reinjury, or return to play.

*Beynnon et al. compared rehabilitation following ACL reconstruction with either an accelerated or nonaccelerated program.* An accelerated program was completed within 19 weeks compared to a 32-week nonaccelerated program. **They found** that at the 2-year follow-up, the groups were similar in terms of clinical assessment, patient satisfaction, function, proprioception, and isokinetic thigh muscle strength. **They concluded** that rehabilitation with the accelerated and nonaccelerated programs have the same clinical assessment, functional performance, proprioception, and thigh muscle strength which returned to normal levels after healing was complete.

*Lemos et al. performed a biomechanical evaluation in 12 pairs of fresh intact bovine knees of femoral interference screws placed divergently and parallel relative to the bone block and its tunnel.* **They report** failure was evident with divergent constructs. **They concluded** that pullout strength remains high even with divergence of up to 15 degrees between the bone plug and femoral interference screws placed endoscopically.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: MCL injuries are commonly associated with ACL ruptures. They do not post a significant risk of reinjury
Answer 2: Early accelerated rehabilitation protocols have been shown to be equivocal with nonaccelerated programs with respect to reinjury
Answer 4: A Segond fracture is an avulsion of the ALL. Many authors advocate for the repair of the ALL, but its presence has not been shown to be a significant risk factor for ACL reinjury
Answer 5: This is normal positioning of the tibial tunnel in ACL reconstruction
