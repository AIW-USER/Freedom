## Question

In compound grade 3 tibia fracture how long antibiotics should be continued?

## Options

1. For 24 hours
2. Until wound culture becomes negative or 5 days whichever is sooner
3. For 72 hours
4. For 48 hours

## Correct option

**3. For 72 hours**

## Answer explanation

Recent studies suggest commencement of broad spectrum antibiotics within 3 hours of injury, which should continue until first debridement. Antibiotics should continue to be administered until primary closure of the wound, or for 72 hours, whichever is sooner. A recent meta-analysis of comparative studies on antibiotic protocols demonstrated insignificant differences in the incidence of septic complication with prolonged therapy (> 72 hours) or short-course (< 72 hours). This is seen irrespective of the fracture severity and sub-group analysis.

Answer: 3
