## Question

Law governing remodeling is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Devil’s law
2. Wolf’s law
3. Davis low
4. Both c and b

## Correct option

**4. Both c and b**

## Answer explanation

Wollf’s law: Bones adapt to the loads under which they are placed. Increase in load causes the adaptive changes in the trabeculae and external cortex and bone becomes stronger to bear that load. In other words, change in the function of the bone is followed by change in its internal architecture and its external conformation.” On the other hand, if the loading on a bone decreases, the bone will become osteopenia and weaker due to the lack of the stimulus required for continued remodeling. Osteocytes are sensitive to the mechanical load and upon loading they regulate bone remodeling by signaling to other cells with signaling molecules or direct contact.

Also know: Davis’s low is applied to soft tissues and states that soft tissues heal according to the manner in which they are mechanically stressed

Answer: 4
