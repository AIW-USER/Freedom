## Question

What is the respective prevalence of HLA B 27 in ankylosing spondylitis, juvenile spondyloarthropathy and in rheumatoid arthritis?

## Options

1. 90%, 70% and 6%
2. 95%, 90% and 75%
3. 95%, 70% and <10%
4. . 80% ,90% and 70%

## Correct option

**1. 90%, 70% and 6%**

## Answer explanation

Prevalence of HLA B27 in patients of ankylosing spondylitis is approximately 90%. In juvenile spondyloarthropathy 70% children test positive for HLA B27. Approximately 60-80% of individuals with rheumatoid arthritis (RA) have RF present during the course of their disease. However, RF results are positive in less than 40% of patients with early RA.

Answer: 1
