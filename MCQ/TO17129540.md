Chapter: Musculoskeletal Infections MCQs — Tuberculosis Of Spine

## Question

Spine at risk signs in TB spine include all except (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Presence of retropulsion (posterior displacement) of the vertebra
2. Posterior toppling of the vertebra
3. Kyphotic angle more than 55 degrees
4. Subluxation of the facet joint at the apex of the kyphosis deformity

## Correct option

**3. Kyphotic angle more than 55 degrees**

## Answer explanation

Spine at Risk” signs: Prof Rajasekaran (India) described “spine at risk signs” (X-ray signs) in TB to caution against a progression of the tuberculous deformity. These include:

- Subluxation of the facet joint at the apex of the kyphosis deformity
- Presence of retropulsion (posterior displacement) of the vertebra
- Posterior toppling of the vertebra
- Lateral translation of the vertebra.

Answer: 3
