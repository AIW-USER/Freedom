## Question

An athlete is on a vegan diet predominantly and is deficient in some amino acids. You plan to give CARNOSINE for same. The compound is composed of (FNB 2022)

## Options

1. Combination of Beta alanine and L Histidine
2. Combination of Glutamine, Glutathione and Alanine
3. L-Arginine
4. Combination of Methionine and cysteine

## Correct option

**1. Combination of Beta alanine and L Histidine**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

Carnosine is a dipeptide synthesized in our body from beta alanine and L-Histidine that was discovered by Dr. Vladimir Gulevic at University of Charkow in 1900. Vitamin B6 is an essential precursor and deficiency can lower the levels of carnosine.

It is found in high concentrations in our brain, skeletal and cardiac muscles and GI tissues. It mainly functions as an antioxidant and hence can benefit the athlete in both physical and mental health. It is also thought to have anti-aging effects and can benefit in glycaemic control.

This animal origin product is available to the athletes as a nutritional supplement (so not under doping), and it is capable of enhancing the recovery of fatigue and prevent chronic diseases such as diabetes, atherosclerosis, and cognitive impairments. Infact, it has been documented that carnosine loading can benefit in high intensity exercise. However, many aspects of the supplement, such as the potential side effects and the mechanism of action, require additional and thorough investigation by the sports science community before wide recommendation.
