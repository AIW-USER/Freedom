## Question

Which of the following structures attaches to the fracture fragment shown in Figure 49?

![[q6507-a-49.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Extensor hallucis brevis
2. Extensor digitorum brevis
3. Calcaneofibular ligament
4. Anterior tibiofibular ligament
5. Anterior talofibular ligament

## Correct option

**4. Anterior tibiofibular ligament**

## Answer explanation

The anterior tibiofibular ligament, which is attached to the anterior lateral portion of the distal tibial epiphysis, may avulse the anterior lateral quarter of the epiphysis, resulting in a Tillaux fracture. The Tillaux fracture is a transitional fracture of the distal tibia that occurs during the normal sequence of closure of the distal tibial physis. The anterior lateral portion of this physis is the last to close. The other structures listed do not have an attachment to the anterior lateral portion of the distal physis of the tibia.
