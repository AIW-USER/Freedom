## Question

Which of the following is true regarding scoliosis in patients with untreated Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD)?

## Options

1. Small curves are likely to remain stable over time
2. 50% of all patients with DMD will develop scoliosis
3. Spinal fusion should be offered when the Cobb angle reaches 20 to 30 degrees
4. Scoliosis in DMD is responsive to bracing
5. Scoliosis surgery is ideally performed at skeletal maturity patients with DMD

## Correct option

**3. Spinal fusion should be offered when the Cobb angle reaches 20 to 30 degrees**

## Answer explanation

Once identified, scoliosis should be treated with posterior instrumented fusion in all patients with untreated DMD once curve Cobb angle reaches 20 to 30 degrees.

Scoliosis develops in 90 to 95% of patients with DMD, usually between ages 11 and 13 when patients have progressed to full time sitting. Surgery should not be delayed because curves in DMD are progressive, not responsive to bracing, and patients have usually reached sufficient trunk height. The older the patient is, the greater the risk of postoperative pulmonary complications. In the setting of steroid treatment of DMD, there is less consensus on indications for spinal fusion, with some practitioners using more traditional Cobb angle measures to indicate fusion.

Sussman reviews DMD and its orthopaedic implications by stage (1-5). He states that spinal deformity is the most critical orthopaedic issue for patients with DMD. Stage 4, age 12 to 16 years, is the time when patients are involved in full time sitting and develop spinal deformity. He recommends starting the discussion of fusion with parents early, when the child is 5 to 10 years old, in order to prepare them for the necessity of surgery later, which will greatly improve their quality of life and survival.

Illustrations A and B show pre- and post-operative radiographs of a patient with DMD. This patient and his family deferred surgery until age 15.

Incorrect answers:
Answer 1: Unlike AIS, in which small curves may remain stable over time after maturity, small curves in DMD inevitable progress.
Answer 2: 90 to 95% of patients with DMD will develop scoliosis.
Answer 4: Scoliosis in DMD is not responsive to bracing.
Answer 5: Scoliosis surgery should be performed before pulmonary deteriorates significantly. Patients whose forced vital capacity is <35% of normal (which typically occurs at 15 years of age in DMD) are at high risk of pulmonary complications.
