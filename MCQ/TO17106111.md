Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Post TKR Infection

## Question

Minimum recommended duration of IV antibiotics after debridement and retention of implant with change of polyethylene in a case of acutely infected TKR is

## Options

1. 2-6 weeks
2. 4 weeks
3. 6 weeks
4. 8 weeks

## Correct option

**1. 2-6 weeks**

## Answer explanation

There is no consensus about duration of IV antibiotics after debridement and retention of implant with change of polyethylene in a case of acutely infected TKR but most of the studies recommend 2 to 6 weeks of culture guided IV antibiotic therapy.

Answer: 1
