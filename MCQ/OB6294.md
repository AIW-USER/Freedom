## Question

Which of the following is most associated with local recurrence of the lesion seen in the radiograph and MRI scan shown in Figures 27a and 27b?

![[q6294-a-27a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q6294-b-27b.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Effectiveness of chemotherapy
2. Effect of local adjuvant
3. Open physes
4. Presence of giant cells
5. Effectiveness of embolization

## Correct option

**3. Open physes**

## Answer explanation

The lesion is an aneurysmal bone cyst. These lesions are known to have a local recurrence rate of 5% to 50%. Young age, open physes, stage, and type of surgical removal and resulting margin have all been shown to affect the recurrence rate. Chemotherapy is not used in the treatment of aneurysmal bone cysts.
