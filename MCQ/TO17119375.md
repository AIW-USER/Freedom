Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — PCL

## Question

True regarding PCL is [ FNB 2019 ]

## Options

1. Intra Articular extra synovial
2. Intra Articular intrasynovial
3. Intrasynovial and extracapsular
4. Intrasynovial intracapsular

## Correct option

**1. Intra Articular extra synovial**

## Answer explanation

- Collaterals are Extra articular and Extra synovial

- Cruciate are Intra articular but Extra Synovial (Extra synovial because they don’t derive their nutrition from synovial fluid as they hv their own blood supply)

- Menisci are Intra articular and Intra synovial (Intra synovial as they derive their nutrition from synovial fluid)

Answer: 1
