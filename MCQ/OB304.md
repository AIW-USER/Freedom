## Question

During a minimally invasive total knee arthroplasty with a quadriceps-sparing approach, the exposure is found to be limited and causing difficulties with jig alignment. What is the optimal next step?

## Options

1. Conversion of the exposure to a subvastus approach
2. Tibial tubercle osteotomy
3. Conversion of the exposure to a two-incision approach
4. Conversion of the exposure to a standard parapatellar arthrotomy
5. Ligament release to improve exposure

## Correct option

**4. Conversion of the exposure to a standard parapatellar arthrotomy**

## Answer explanation

As with difficulties in any minimally invasive exposure, conversion to a larger or more traditional exposure is indicated when the exposure is causing difficulties or potential complications. The referenced study by Tria mentions this, but the paper focuses on the importance of having appropriate instrumentation to be able to successfully perform minimally invasive procedures.
