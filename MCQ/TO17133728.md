## Question

Investigation of choice for burst fracture is (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. X ray
2. CT scan
3. MRI
4. MR angiography

## Correct option

**3. MRI**

## Answer explanation

Burst and chance fractures: Burst fracture is caused by vertical compression. Anterior and middle column are crushed and stability depends on the status of the posterior column. In unstable burst fracture posterior column also fail in distraction. In unstable burst fracture there is a high chance of neural compression due to retropulsion of bony fragments into the canal.

Chance fracture: these are flexion –distraction injuries characterized by horizontal avulsion of the vertebral bodies caused by flexion around an axis anterior to the anterior longitudinal ligament. Combined flexion and posterior distraction cause the lumbar spine to jack knife around an axis placed anterior to the vertebral column. There is no crushing of the vertebral body but the posterior and middle column fail in distraction.

Investigation of choice in spine injuries is MRI to see intervertebral disc, ligamentum flavum and neural structures.

Answer: 3
