## Correct option

**2. The exercises shown in Figures B and D are most likely to relieve pain and return him to his previous activity level**

## Answer explanation

This patient has chronic non-insertional Achilles tendinosis (with visible swelling of the Achilles tendon). Eccentric exercises (depicted in Figures B and D) are best for pain relief and return to activity.

Achilles tendinosis arises from asymptomatic degeneration without inflammation caused by microtrauma and aging. Tendinosis can be differentiated from paratenonitis by the mobility of the tender intratendinous nodule during active range of motion (painful arc sign). Initial treatment includes NSAIDs, rest, bracing, flexible/semi-rigid orthoses (to treat overpronation), and physical therapy (PT) for 3-6 months. PT should concentrate on enhancing dorsiflexion (i.e. eccentric training), as most patients have limited limited passive dorsiflexion. Surgical treatment involves removing the areas of degenerated tendon or, if > 50% of the tendon is involved, FHL/FDL/allograft reconstruction should be performed.

*Mafi et al. performed a prospective randomized multi-center study comparing eccentric and concentric exercises for the treatment of painful chronic Achilles tendinosis.* *Patients in the eccentric training group had a higher rate of satisfaction and return to previous activity compared with the concentric training group.* **They recommend** eccentric training for this condition.

*Rowe et al. performed a systematic review for the treatment of midportion achilles tendinopathy which included 47 studies.* The only treatment recommendations with strong recommendations were eccentric loading exercises and extracorporeal shockwave therapy.

Figure A is a sagittal T2-weighted fat-suppressed MRI image showing Achilles tendinosis with mucoid degeneration and fusiform enlargement of the Achilles tendon critical zone with abnormal, high intrasubstance signal. Figures B and D show examples of ECCENTRIC exercises. Figures C and E show examples of CONCENTRIC exercises.
