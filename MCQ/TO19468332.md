## Question

True about patellofemoral joint reaction force (PFJRF) is( FNB Pattern Paper 2014)

## Options

1. Ascending order of PFJRF is Walking<stair ascent<stair descent< jump< running< deep squat
2. Ascending order of PFJRF is Walking< jumping< <stair ascent<stair descent<running< deep squat
3. PFJRF is more during going the stair up than going down
4. More the flexion of knee, lesser will be the PFJRF

## Correct option

**2. Ascending order of PFJRF is Walking< jumping< <stair ascent<stair descent<running< deep squat**

## Answer explanation

Patellofemoral joint reaction force is .5 to 1 times the body weight during walking on level surface. The knee experiences mean peak loadings of 2.4-4.6 × body weight at the patellofemoral joint in vertical jumping. PFJRF is 3.5 to 6 times the body weight during ascending and descending the stairs. It is more in going down than going up the stairs. In running PFJRF increases up to 4.5 to 7 times the body weight. It increases up to 7 times during squats. With increase in knee flexion angle PFJRF increases and it can be as high as 20 times the body weight during deep squat.

So correct sequence of PFJRF is

Walking< jumping<stairs ascent<stairs descent<running< squat<deep squat

Answer: 2
