## Question

Which of the following is true about Ewing Sarcoma in a 10-year-old?

## Options

1. Predominantly diaphyseal
2. Raised ESR at presentation is associated with worse prognosis
3. Grading of tumor has got prognostic importance
4. Tumor in appendicular skeleton has worse prognosis that Ewing Sarcoma in axial skeleton

## Correct option

**2. Raised ESR at presentation is associated with worse prognosis**

## Answer explanation

Surgery is the preferred form of local treatment for Ewing’s Sarcoma if the tumor can be resected with good margins. Although Ewing’s sarcoma is radiosensitive, radiation therapy is reserved for unresectable tumors. Radiation therapy is also given after surgery, if chemotherapy response is not good or margins are positive after resection.

Radiation therapy avoids surgery but has lesser recurrence free and overall survival as compared to surgery. Also, it kills the growing cells of growth plate in children causing deformities later on and also weakens the bone increasing the chances of radiation induced fractures later on and also increases the risk of radiation induced sarcoma later in life

Another misconception about Ewing’s sarcoma is that it is predominantly diaphyseal. 50% of the patients have diaphyseal involvement while the remaining 50 % involve the metaphysis.

Worse prognostic factors for Ewing’s include

-Raised ESR, TLC, LDH levels

- larger disease volume

- unresectable tumors or tumors in axial skeleton

- poor response to chemotherapy

- older age at presentation

- presence of metastasis at presentation

GRADE OF EWING’s Sarcoma IS NOT A PROGNOSTIC FACTOR SINCE EWING’S SARCOMA IS ANYWAYS A HIGH-GRADE TUMOUR

High dose methotrexate is used in the management of osteosarcoma.

The chemotherapy drugs given in Ewing’s sarcoma are Vincristine, Adriamycin, Cyclophosphamide (VAC) alternating with Ifosfamide and etoposide (IE)
