## Question

During walking which metatarsal bears most of the weight? (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. 1st
2. 2nd
3. Head of all metatarsals bear weight equally
4. 5th

## Correct option

**2. 2nd**

## Answer explanation

During standing maximum weight is borne by the first metatarsal. However, during gait it is the second metatarsal which bears most of the weight.

Answer: 2
