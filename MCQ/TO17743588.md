## Question

Which is not a component of triad of DCO (trauma- polytrauma patients)

## Options

1. Hypothermia
2. Acidosis
3. Coagulopathy
4. Hypoxia

## Correct option

**4. Hypoxia**

## Answer explanation

This relatively new concept aims at reducing mortality by advocating a temporary management of the fractures and soft tissue injury initially in a multiply injured patient till the patient is stable enough to tolerate a definitive procedure. The concept advocates that a multiple injured patient has already sustained a “hit” in the form of high energy impact during trauma. Subjecting him to “second hit” in the form of a major surgery (for definitive fracture management)

DCO is a surgical strategy that focuses on control of hemorrhage, soft tissue management, early temporary fracture stabilization(External fixator) and optimization of the patient's physiology, avoidance of the 'second hit' and the triad of hypothermia, acidosis and coagulopathy.

 Answer D
