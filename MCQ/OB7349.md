## Question

The sublime tubercle of the elbow serves as the insertion site of the

## Options

1. anterior bundle of the medial collateral ligament.
2. posterior bundle of the medial collateral ligament.
3. transverse bundle of the medial collateral ligament.
4. annular ligament.
5. lateral collateral ligament.

## Correct option

**1. anterior bundle of the medial collateral ligament.**

## Answer explanation

The anterior bundle originates on the anteroinferior medial humeral epicondyle and inserts on the medial portion of the coronoid, known as the sublime tubercle.
