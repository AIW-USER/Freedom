## Question

A 55-year-old male presents with left hip pain and stiffness. Radiographs are shown in Figures A and B. A biopsy of the left femur is performed and shown in Figure C. Which of the following medications is contraindicated in this patient?

![[q3617-b-paget-femur-xr.jpg]]
*Figure B*

![[q3617-c-paget-disease-of-bone-high-mag.jpg]]
*Figure C*

## Options

1. Teriparatide
2. Alendronate
3. Ergocalciferol
4. Zoledronic acid
5. Calcitonin

## Correct option

**1. Teriparatide**

## Answer explanation

The radiographs and histology are consistent with Paget's disease. Figure A shows the skull with areas of lysis, areas of sclerosis (‘cotton-wool spots’), and calvarial thickening. Figure B demonstrates coarsened trabeculae, cortical thickening, and enlargement of the left femur. The histology in Figure C shows abnormal bone formation with a mosaic pattern of woven bone and irregular sections of thickened trabecular bone and numerous cement lines.

Teriparatide (Forteo) is a recombinant form of parathyroid hormone, used in the treatment of some forms of osteoporosis. Administration is contraindicated in the patient with Paget's disease. Other contraindications to the use of teriparatide include any hypercalcemic disorder, osteosarcoma, metastatic bone disease, pregnancy, and radiation therapy to the skeleton or soft tissue in which a skeletal port is exposed.

*Tastekin and Zateri authored a letter to the editor that discusses the use of teriparatide in patients with Paget's disease.* **They report** that the FDA issued a black box warning on teriparatide due to the increased rate of osteosarcoma development in rats that have been given very high doses of teriparatide. The FDA limits the duration of teraparatide administration in humans to a duration of 18–24 months.
