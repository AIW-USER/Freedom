Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Developmental Dysplasia Of Hip (DDH)

## Question

The alpha angle on the ultrasound of an infant’s hip is defined as

## Options

1. The angle between the acetabular roof and the midline of the pelvis
2. The acute angle between the lateral wall of the ilium and the bony acetabular roof
3. The angle between the center of the femoral head and the lateral wall of the ilium
4. The angle of the thigh required to produce subluxation of the hip on the sonogram

## Correct option

**2. The acute angle between the lateral wall of the ilium and the bony acetabular roof**

## Answer explanation

The alpha angle is used most commonly as a measurement of acetabular concavity, and it is calculated as the angle between the lateral wall of the ilium (vertical cortex of the ilium) and the roofline (acetabular roof). A normal alpha angle is 60 degrees or greater. This measurement is vital to understanding the morphology of the immature acetabulum in developmental dysplasia.

The beta angle is the angle formed between the vertical cortex of the ilium and the triangular labral fibrocartilage (echogenic triangle). Normal value is 55 degrees or less.

Graf classification of DDH is based on the ultrasound value of these angles.
