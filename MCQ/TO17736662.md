## Question

In young children physeal infection can lead to septic arthritis in all the areas except (general orthopaedics- anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. Distal fibula
2. Hip joint
3. Wrist joint
4. Radial neck

## Correct option

**3. Wrist joint**

## Answer explanation

In children younger than 2 years, the common blood supply of the metaphysis and epiphysis crosses the physis and can allow spread of a metaphyseal abscess into the epiphysis and eventually into the joint leading to septic arthritis. Hip joint is most common site of septic arthritis is young children. Other sites where physis is intra-articular are the radial neck, proximal humerus and distal fibula. Infection in these areas can lead to septic arthritis.

Answer: 3
