## Question

A 50-year-old male presents to the clinic with progressive low back for 3 months and 20 lbs of unintentional weight loss. He is neurovascularly intact. Imaging studies confirm lytic lesions to the L4 and L5 vertebral bodies without cord compression. Additional diagnostic studies confirm metastatic disease. Radiation therapy could be considered first-line treatment for which of the following diagnosis?

## Options

1. Fibrosarcoma
2. Lymphoma
3. Chondrosarcoma
4. Melanoma
5. Angiosarcoma

## Correct option

**2. Lymphoma**

## Answer explanation

Lymphoma is considered widely sensitive to radiation and is often used as first-line treatment.

Metastatic disease to the spine is the most common reason for a destructive bone lesion in adults. Treatment is guided towards local disease control, reducing pain and decreasing disease burden. Treatment will vary by the pathology and can be a combination of radiation therapy, chemotherapy, and surgery. Isolated radiation therapy can be considered first-line treatment for leukemia, lymphoma, multiple myeloma (AKA plasmacytoma of bone), hemangioendothelioma and Ewing's disease which are considered very radiosensitive.

*Rose et al. performed a review of metastatic lesions to the spine.* **The authors note** that understanding the epidemiology of metastatic spine disease and its presentation is essential to developing a diagnostic strategy. Authors noted that the most radiation-sensitive tumors are lymphoma, myeloma, leukemia, and seminoma with most other solid organ malignancies being relatively radioresistant. **The authors conclude** that understanding the sensitivity of tumors to radiation and chemotherapy will help create a rational treatment plan that can help improve quality of life, preserve neurologic function, and prolong survival.

*Messina et al. discussed and reviewed different forms of bony lymphomas and different treatments.* *They discuss primary bone lymphoma, consisting of a single bone lesion with or without regional lymphadenopathies; the polyostotic lymphoma, consisting of multifocal disease exclusively involving the skeleton; and the disseminated lymphoma with secondary infiltration of the skeleton.* **The authors conclude** that the first two forms exhibit a good prognosis. The authors give several recommendations on the management of these 3 forms of bone lymphoma and stress the importance of radiation therapy as a mainstay in these treatment options.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Fibrosarcoma is generally considered radiation resistant
Answer 3: Chondrosarcoma generally considered radiation resistant
Answer 4: Metastatic melanoma is generally considered radiation resistant
Answer 5: Angiosarcoma is generally considered radiation resistant
