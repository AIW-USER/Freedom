## Question

A 6 months old child is brought to the OPD by her parents with complaints of left leg deformity. AP and lateral X rays of the leg are given below. Which is not a true statement for this case?[Pediatric Ortho.-Tibial Bowing]

![[TO17744251-1.png]]

## Options

1. Diagnosis is pseudoarthrosis of tibia and protective bracing is indicated
2. Observation is the only treatment required at this stage
3. Leg length discrepancy is a known complication
4. Calcaneovalgus feet is the most common association.

## Correct option

**1. Diagnosis is pseudoarthrosis of tibia and protective bracing is indicated**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: This is a case of posteromedial bowing of tibia and fibula. It is physiologic bowing of the tibia thought to be a result of intrauterine positioning. Calcaneovalgus feet is the most common association. Usually, it involves the middle and distal third of the tibia. Treatment is observation as spontaneous correction occurs in the majority of the cases over 5-7 years. Most common squeal of posteromedial bowing is average leg-length discrepancy of 3-4 cm.

In pseudoarthrosis tibial bowing is mostly anterolateral.

Answer: 1
