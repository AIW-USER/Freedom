## Question

Force platform is used for(sports medicine- biomechanics of common sports-force platform)

## Options

1. To measure spinning velocity of a ball
2. To measure ground reaction force
3. To measure jump force
4. To measure frictional force of sports shoes

## Correct option

**2. To measure ground reaction force**

## Answer explanation

Force platforms are measuring platforms which are used to measure ground reaction force when a person stands or walks across them. These are used to study biomechanics of gait while walking or running in sports.

Answer : B
