## Question

A 15-year-old male high school football player presents with a brief loss of consciousness and decreased orientation following a tackle. His symptoms quickly recover and he wishes to return to the game. Which of the following is true regarding this scenario?

## Options

1. The patient can return to play immediately
2. Symptom duration is not associated with physical activity
3. Cognitive rest decreases the duration of symptoms
4. MRI is often diagnostic of the condition
5. Long-term sequelae are minimal

## Correct option

**3. Cognitive rest decreases the duration of symptoms**

## Answer explanation

Cognitive rest during the post-concussion period is associated with decreased symptom duration.

Sports-related concussions are mild traumatic brain injuries which is due to a direct blow to the face of the head. Patients often present with loss of consciousness, cognitive slowing, dizziness, emotional lability, vision changes, balance issues, dizziness, and light/sound sensitivity. Initial treatment consists of immediate cessation from the sport with a graduated return to play protocol. During this period, the patient should rest from physical activity and rest from cognitive activities to reduce the duration of symptoms. Early return to play after a concussion can pose a risk for second-impact syndrome due to cerebral edema from loss of autoregulation.

*Shirley et al. reviewed the management of sports-related concussions.* **They stated** concussion risk factors are age <18 years, female sex, history of previous concussion and the initial treatment consists of reduced cognitive activity and physical rest. **They concluded** the long-term consequences of concussions are not fully understood.

*Brown et al. evaluated the effect of cognitive activity on the duration of post-concussion symptoms.* **They reported** an average symptoms duration of 43 days with total symptom burden at the initial visit and cognitive activity level associated with symptom duration. **They concluded** cognitive rest is supported for the recovery of post-concussion symptoms.

*Majerske et al. evaluated the effect of post-concussion activity levels on symptoms and neurocognitive performance.* **They reported** a strong correlation between symptom duration and activity intensity. **They concluded** activity levels following concussions can affect neurocognitive recovery.

Incorrect answers
Answer 1: Patients with concussions cannot return to play until they have completed a graduated return-to-play protocol while remaining symptom-free.
Answer 2: Increased activity level during the post-concussion period is associated with a prolonged duration of symptoms.
Answer 4: Brain MRI is often normal in patients presenting with concussions.
Answer 5: Chronic traumatic encephalopathy can manifest many years later following repetitive concussions leading to memory and mood disturbances.
