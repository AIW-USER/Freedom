## Question

True about medial physis of clavicle-Age of ossification? (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Complete fusion by 15-16 years
2. Appear at 10-11 years
3. Fusion around 20-21 years
4. Scar disappears between 20-25 years

## Correct option

**3. Fusion around 20-21 years**

## Answer explanation

In human skeletons, the secondary ossification center of medial clavicular epiphysis is the last to be unified (i.e., approximately 20 years), along with the humeral head, scapular acromion process, and ischial tuberosity. Males mature 1 to 2 years later than females.
