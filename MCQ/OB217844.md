## Question

A 59-year-old obese female presents to your office for follow up. You performed a right sided TKA approximately 6 years ago. The patient reports falling last weekend at a fourth of July party, landing directly on her right knee. Following the injury she was unable to actively extend her leg. You determine that she needs an extensor mechanism reconstruction. Which of the following is decreased when synthetic mesh reconstruction is performed rather than allograft reconstruction?

## Options

1. Complication rates
2. Knee Society Scores
3. Postoperative flexion
4. Cost
5. Postoperative length of stay

## Correct option

**4. Cost**

## Answer explanation

Extensor mechanism reconstruction utilizing synthetic mesh, rather than allograft, are much more cost effective.

Extensor mechanism disruption following TKA is arguably the most devastating complication one can experience. There are two main reconstruction techniques utilized today, allograft and synthetic mesh. Regardless of the technique selected, patients can expect to have a 10-20 degree extensor lag following the operation. Outcomes between the two techniques in regards to range of motion, reoperation rates, and infection have not been different. Cost, however, has been found to be much lower with synthetic mesh reconstruction. Additionally, appropriate allografts are more difficult to locate when compared to the readily available synthetic mesh, leading most surgeons towards mesh reconstructions.

*Abdel et al review extensor mechanism reconstructions with the use of Marlex mesh.* **They report** that 84% of their mesh reconstructions were in place and the functional outcomes were excellent at final follow up (mean, 4 years). Further **they report** an final extension lag of 9 degrees, which represented a 26 degree improvement on average. **They conclude** that extensor mechanism reconstruction with Marlex mesh is a viable options for patients with failed extensor mechanisms following TKA.

*Shau et al review synthetic mesh vs. allograft extensor mechanism reconstruction in TKA.* **They report** that reconstruction success rates (76% allograft vs. 74% mesh), average time to diagnosis/treatment, Knee Society Scores (KSS), knee range of motion/extensor lag, and complication rates yielded no statistical difference between the groups. **They conclude**, however, that synthetic reconstruction was associated with more widely available products and a much lower cost.

*Wood et al review synthetic graft vs allograft reconstruction of the extensor mechanism in a multicenter review.* **They report** that the allograft cost was significantly higher compared with the synthetic graft cost (P = 0.001). The mean total cost was 4,733.08 CAD for the synthetic group and 24,050.40 CAD for the allograft group (P = 0.17). **They conclude** that synthetic graft reconstruction provides many benefits when compared to those performed with allograft.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Complication rates are similar between groups in most studies.
Answer 2 & 3: Functional outcomes are similar between groups and patients can expect to have 10-20 degrees of extensor lag postoperatively.
Answer 5: Postoperative length of stay has not been linked to graft choice for extensor mechanism reconstruction.
