## Question

Cause of lytic lesion in malignant secondaries in spine is (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Renal carcinoma
2. Medulloblastoma
3. Prostate carcinoma
4. Carcinoid

## Correct option

**1. Renal carcinoma**

## Answer explanation

Most common primary sites producing metastasis are:

 •  In males: Prostate followed by lung

•  In females: Breast followed by lung

•  In children: Neuroblastoma

•  Overall: Breast followed by prostate and then lung

Most common causes of Osteoblastic metastases—prostate, seminoma, carcinoids, medulloblastoma

Most common causes of Osteolytic metastases—kidney, thyroid, lung

  Mixed—breast (more commonly lytic)
