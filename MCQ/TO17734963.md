## Question

Skin biopsy is helpful in diagnosis of ( congenital and developmental musculoskeletal disorders-osteogenesis imperfecta)

## Options

1. Pyknodysostosis
2. Osteogenesis imperfecta
3. Pigmented villonodular synovitis of tendon sheath
4. Achondroplasia

## Correct option

**2. Osteogenesis imperfecta**

## Answer explanation

Skin biopsy is diagnostic in osteogenesis imperfecta. Histology findings include a large area of woven bone with crowded osteocytes within bone. Severe forms lack an organized trabecular pattern.

Answer: 2
