## Question

Which nerve root supplies the dermatome of the little finger? (spine- cervical disc herniation and myelopathy)

## Options

1. C6
2. C7
3. C8
4. T1

## Correct option

**3. C8**

## Answer explanation

Sensation over the little finger is carried by the C8 nerve. The C8 cervical spine nerve root supplies sensation to the medial border of the forearm and little finger.
