Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Humerus Shaft Fractures

## Question

Acceptable reduction of humeral shaft in adult include all except

## Options

1. Shortening <3 cm
2. Angulation <30 degrees
3. Rotation <30 degrees
4. No segmental fracture fragments

## Correct option

**2. Angulation <30 degrees**

## Answer explanation

Surgical indications in fracture shaft of humerus Failure to obtain and maintain adequate closed reduction

Shortening > 3 cm Rotation > 30 degrees Angulation > 20 degrees

- Segmental fracture
- Pathologic fracture
- Intraarticular extension (shoulder joint, elbow joint)

Answer: 2
