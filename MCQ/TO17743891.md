Chapter: FNB 2019 — MCQ 2019

## Question

True regarding tibial retroscrew is all except (arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- ACL)

## Options

1. Used in ACL reconstruction
2. Less tunnel widening is a potential advantage
3. Proximal to distal insertion
4. Tunnel widening is more compared to antegrade screw

## Correct option

**4. Tunnel widening is more compared to antegrade screw**

## Answer explanation

Tibial retroscrew is a type of ACL fixation screw which is inserted in retrograde fashion from proximal to distal in tibia. It is claimed to have less tibial tunnel dilatation as the screw head blocks the tunnel mouth and prevents synovial fluid inflow into the tunnel.

Answer: 4
