## Question

Figure 6 shows a sagittal oblique MRI scan. The arrow is pointing to what structure?

![[q6168-a-06.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Bucket-handle tear of the medial meniscus
2. Ligament of Humphrey
3. Ligament of Wrisberg
4. Posterior intermeniscal ligament
5. Partial tear of the posterior cruciate ligament

## Correct option

**2. Ligament of Humphrey**

## Answer explanation

The meniscofemoral ligaments connect the posterior horn of the lateral meniscus to the intercondylar wall of the medial femoral condyle. The ligament of Humphrey (arrow) passes anterior to the posterior cruciate ligament, whereas the ligament of Wrisberg passes posterior to the posterior cruciate ligament. One or the other has been identified in 71% to 100% of cadaver knees, with the ligament of Wrisberg being more common.
