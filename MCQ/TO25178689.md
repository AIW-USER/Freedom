## Question

When performing distal femoral extension osteotomy (DFEO), what is the most common reason for post-operative 'extensor shift' and anterior knee pain?

## Options

1. Over-tightening of the medial hamstrings
2. Failure to address patella alta in the presence of a shortened femur
3. Inadequate resection of the bone wedge
4. Excessive posterior tilting of the distal fragment

## Correct option

**2. Failure to address patella alta in the presence of a shortened femur**

## Answer explanation

The osteotomy effectively shortens the femur, increasing the functional length of the extensor mechanism and worsening patellar tracking/pressure.

Answer: 2
