## Question

Which of the following statement correctly describes the latest Ponseti method of corrective casting?

## Options

1. Weekly long leg cast followed by tenotomy and 3 week cast
2. Weekly short leg cast followed by tenotomy and 3 week long leg cast
3. Weekly short leg cast followed by tenotomy and 3 week short leg cast
4. 5 day long leg cast followed by tenotomy and 3 week cast.

## Correct option

**1. Weekly long leg cast followed by tenotomy and 3 week cast**

## Answer explanation

The Ponseti method is based on the following points:

1. Above knee corrective cast. This is because it prevents the cast from sliding off. Also, the tibial torsion issue which exists in these patients is corrected by an above knee corrective cast.

2. Weekly cast change. Though recent papers have tried to study rapid ponseti casting, the recommendation by the Ponseti foundation still remains a weekly cast change until tenotomy.

3. Tenotomy is used to address the residual equinus after correction of cavus, Adduction and Varus.

4. The end point of corrective cast before tenotomy is reduction of talo navicular joint and a passive abduction of 60*.

Answer : a
