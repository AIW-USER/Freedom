## Question

All of the following are known steps in the development of a malignant tumor with the ability to metastasize EXCEPT?

## Options

1. Increased apoptosis
2. Sustained angiogenesis
3. Tumor cell intravasation
4. Avoidance of immune surveillance
5. Genomic instability

## Correct option

**1. Increased apoptosis**

## Answer explanation

All of the listed options are involved in the development of a malignant tumor with the ability to metastasize EXCEPT increased apoptosis. Apoptosis is programmed cell death, and tumor cells are known for their decreased rate of apoptosis. This allows for tumor cell sustained growth even after induction of cellular signals known to induce apoptosis in non-neoplastic cells.
