## Question

You are consulted on a 30-year-old female who sustained a wrist fracture. She is also found to have several bruises about her face and thorax and a nasal fracture. Intimate partner violence (IPV) is suspected. Which of the following is true regarding IPV?

## Options

1. A physician has the authority to provide protection to abused spouses in most states
2. 30-40 years-old are the most common ages of IPV
3. Equally common in low and high-income families
4. Head and neck injuries are the most common physical manifestations
5. More common in long-term than short-term relationships

## Correct option

**4. Head and neck injuries are the most common physical manifestations**

## Answer explanation

There is concern of IPV in this patient. Unwitnessed head, neck, or facial injuries are significant markers for IPV

As many as 35% of women presenting to the ER with injuries are a result of domestic violence and approximately 25% of women experience domestic violence. It is important to be aware that child abuse is present in nearly 50% of homes where domestic violence occurs. Known risk factors include female, age 19-29 years, pregnant, short-term relationship, cohabitation, and low-income families/low socioeconomic status.

*Bhandari et al. reviewed the characteristics of abused women, the prevalence of musculoskeletal injuries, and the variables associated with increasing frequency of physical violence against women.* **They reported** that among women presenting to a domestic violence therapy program seeking counseling, head and neck and musculoskeletal injuries were most common. Frequency of physical abuse was most likely to be associated with younger women who are in short-term relationships, have chemical and alcohol dependency, and concomitant emotional, psychological, and sexual abuse. **They concluded** that recognizing musculoskeletal injuries in women as a potential result of intimate partner violence is warranted. Moreover, identifying children exposed to abusive situations may further alert treating surgeons to the potential for intimate partner violence in the mother.

*Wu et al. reviewed the patterns of physical injury associated with intimate partner violence (IPV) among women presenting to emergency room departments.* **They reported** that the association between head, neck, or facial injuries and IPV was higher among studies that excluded women with verifiable injuries such as witnessed falls or motor vehicle collisions. Moreover, thoracic, abdominal, or pelvic injuries were nonspecific for IPV. **They concluded** that among women presenting to emergency room departments, unwitnessed head, neck, or facial injuries are significant markers for IPV. Conversely, extremity injuries are less likely to have been the consequence of IPV.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: A physician does NOT have authority to provide protection to abused spouses in most states
Answer 2: IPV is most common in females aged 19-29 years
Answer 3: IPV is more common in low-income families
Answer 5: IPV is more common in short-term relationships
