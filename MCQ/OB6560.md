## Question

In assessing the knee after acute injury, increased tibial external rotation at 30 degrees of knee flexion indicates injury to what structure?

## Options

1. Medial collateral ligament
2. Anterior cruciate ligament
3. Posterior cruciate ligament
4. Posterolateral corner
5. Posteromedial capsule

## Correct option

**4. Posterolateral corner**

## Answer explanation

Increased external rotation at 30 degrees indicates injury to the posterolateral corner of the knee. Increased external rotation at both 30 degrees and 90 degrees indicates injury to both the posterolateral corner and the posterior cruciate ligament.
