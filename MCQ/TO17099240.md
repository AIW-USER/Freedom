Chapter: Arthritis MCQs — Osteoarthritis

## Question

Incorrect statement regarding Disease modifying Osteoarthritis Drugs is

## Options

1. They are monoclonal antibodies targeting fibroblast growth factor
2. Tanezumab and Fasinumab are examples of DMOADs
3. Long term use may predispose to abnormalities of nerve conduction
4. DMOADs being investigated will be available for use by both IV and subcutaneous routes but not oral

## Correct option

**1. They are monoclonal antibodies targeting fibroblast growth factor**

## Answer explanation

A
Explanation:
Disease-modifying osteoarthritis drugs (DMOADs)

Monoclonal antibodies mAbs targeting different pathological mechanisms of OA, including ADAMTS (Aggrecanase activity, most notably ADAMTS-5 > ADAMTS-4), Never growth factor and Vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF).
Treatment with anti-nerve growth factors monoclonal antibodies (anti-NGFs) has specifically been found to improve joint pain and physical function, and human trials for the same have been started.
These anti-NGFs include Tanezumab, Fulranumab, and Fasinumab. In 2016, development was discontinued for fulranumab. Currently ongoing trials are focusing on Fasinumab and Tanezumab for hip or knee OA. As tanezumab is the most advanced in clinical development among all three anti-NGFs, Tanezumab is expected to be reviewed by the US FDA with “fast track” designation for OA and chronic low back pain. For hip and knee OA, tanezumab is being studied for both subcutaneous and intravenous (IV) administrations.
Safety: adverse events involving abnormalities of peripheral sensation such as paresthesia, dysesthesia, and others were noted for all three anti-NGFs compared with the placebo.
Ans 1: A
