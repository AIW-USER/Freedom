## Question

A patient presents to OPD with complaints of left leg pain which increases on standing and walking. On examination he has numbness over the dorsum of the foot. MRI shows severe compression of nerve root by herniated posterolateral disc. Which is the correct statement? (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. L4L5 herniated disc with positive Trendelenburg test
2. L5-S1 herniated disc with positive Trendelenburg test
3. L5-S1 herniated disc with negative Trendelenburg test
4. L4-L5 herniated disc with negative Trendelenburg test

## Correct option

**1. L4L5 herniated disc with positive Trendelenburg test**

## Answer explanation

L4-L5 herniated discs usually compress L5 nerve root (L5 is traversing nerve root at L4-L5 level). L5 nerve root supplies dorsum of foot, extensor of great toe (EHL) and hip abductors (positive Trendelenburg test).

Answer: 1
