Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Periprosthetic Fractures

## Question

Su classification is used for (FNB Pattern Paper 2019)

## Options

1. Periprosthetic fracture of patella
2. Periprosthetic fracture of humerus
3. Periprosthetic fracture of femur
4. Periprosthetic fracture of tibia

## Correct option

**3. Periprosthetic fracture of femur**

## Answer explanation

Su and associates' classification is used for supracondylar periprosthetic fractures of the distal femur.

Type I Fracture is proximal to the femoral component

Type II Fracture originates at the proximal aspect of the femoral component and extends proximally

Type III Any part of the fracture line is distal to the upper edge of anterior flange of the femoral component

Most commonly used classification for distal femoral periprosthetic fracture is Lewis and Rorabeck Classification .

Type I Nondisplaced; component intact

 Type II Displaced: component intact

Type III Displaced; component loose or failing

Answer: 3
