## Question

Which of the following statements regarding osteogenesis imperfecta is FALSE?

## Options

1. It is most commonly caused by a mutation in the COL1A1 or COL1A2 genes
2. It can be inherited in an autosomal dominant pattern
3. It can be inherited in an autosomal recessive pattern
4. It can be inherited in an x-linked recessive pattern
5. It can be secondary to a glycine substitution disrupting the collagen triple helix

## Correct option

**4. It can be inherited in an x-linked recessive pattern**

## Answer explanation

Osteogensis imperfecta (OI) is not inherited in an x-linked recessive pattern.

OI is a genetic disorder characterized by a decrease in the amount of normal Type I collagen leading to insufficient osteoid production. Manifestations of OI can include short stature, scoliosis, ligamentous laxity, blue sclera, hearing loss, dental involvement, and frequent fractures. There are four main types of OI, two of which are inherited by an autosomal dominant pattern, and two by an autosomal recessive pattern.

*Burnei et al. present a review on the diagnosis and treatment of OI.* *They discuss the four main types as well as three newer types more recently described.* *They comment on future directions of research involving bone marrow transplantation to increase osteoblast density.*

Krakow presents a review on skeletal dysplasias, and focuses on the need for a multidisciplinary team approach to caring for children with these conditions given their numerous medical and surgical challenges. In regards to OI, the author notes that the recessive forms of the disorder are commonly more severe and potentially even lethal in infancy.

*Harrington et al. provide an update on the treatment of OI.* **They note** that 90% of cases are due to an autosomal dominant mutation in type I collagen. **They emphasize** that treatment of appendicular fractures must limit the time and extent of immobilization in order to avoid disuse osteopenia.

Incorrect answers:
Answer 1: OI is most commonly is caused by a mutation in the COL1A1 or COL1A2 genes
Answers 2 and 3: OI can be inherited by an autosomal dominant or recessive pattern depending on the type.
Answer 5: A mutation of COL1A1 or COL1A2 often leads to a substitution of glycine with a different amino acid. This alters the structure of the collagen triple helix and creates a qualitative defect within the type I collagen produced.
