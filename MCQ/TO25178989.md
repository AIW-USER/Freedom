## Question

In the mechanism of a perilunate dislocation (CIC), the Mayfield Classification Stage II involves the disruption of which space?

## Options

1. Lunotriquetral ligament
2. Capitate-Lunate (Radiocapitate) joint through the Space of Poirier
3. Scapholunate ligament
4. Dorsal Radiocarpal ligament

## Correct option

**2. Capitate-Lunate (Radiocapitate) joint through the Space of Poirier**

## Answer explanation

Stage II involves the capitate dissociating
from the lunate, typically through the volar Space of Poirier.

Answer: 2
