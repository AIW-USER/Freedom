## Question

An orthopedic intern is trying to design a Level 1 randomized controlled trial. The attending surgeon recommends utilizing a computer random number generator for patient randomization. What is the primary purpose of this?

## Options

1. Enhance study blinding
2. Increase study power
3. Obtain statistical significance
4. Prevent selection bias
5. Prevent crossover

## Correct option

**4. Prevent selection bias**

## Answer explanation

A randomized controlled trial that utilizes a computer random number generator for patient randomization is done to prevent selection bias.

A randomized controlled trial is a study in which patients are randomly assigned to the treatment or control group and are followed prospectively; they are by definition an experimental study because there is an intervention. These studies provide the most compelling evidence that the study treatment causes the expected effect on human health. Randomization minimizes study selection bias.

*Spindler et al. reviewed the principles of evidence-based medicine.* **They reported** that careful, systematic review of the literature should be performed to appropriately value the merit of studies. **They concluded** that sufficiently valid evidence-based information ultimately will help in making decisions regarding patient care.

*Poolman et al. performed a systematic review to examine the reporting of outcome measures in orthopedic trials, to determine the feasibility of blinding in published orthopedic trials, and to examine the association between the magnitude of treatment differences and the blinding of outcome assessors.* **They reported** that only 31% of randomized controlled trials used a modified outcome instrument. Moreover, many studies did not report blinding of outcome assessors when blinding would have been possible. **They concluded** that future orthopedic randomized controlled trials should emphasize detailed reporting of outcome measures to facilitate generalization and the outcome assessors should be blinded, when possible, to limit bias.

*Fischgrund et al. reviewed conflict of interest in orthopedic journals.* **They reported** that readers of articles published in medical journals are entitled to a full disclosure of all financial conflicts of interest of the authors of those articles. **They noted** that in the fall of 2010, the International Committee of Medical Journal Editors, as a part of their “Uniform Requirements for Manuscripts Submitted to Biomedical Journals,” approved a Form for Disclosure of Potential Conflicts of Interest ([www.icmje.org](http://www.icmje.org/)). **They concluded** that use of this form will (1) clarify the reporting responsibilities of all authors; (2) facilitate the reporting process, as a common form will be employed by all of the journals listed; and (3) lead to a fuller and clearer understanding of potential author conflicts of interest on the part of our readers.

Incorrect Answer:
Answer 1: Blinding is an important component of conducting high quality research. Generally speaking, there are four parties who may be subject to blinding. It is important to clearly state in the study methodology who is blinded because of the various types of bias.
Answer 2: Meta-analysis are a systematic review that summarizes results of other studies. They may be used to increase the statistical power of several under-powered studies.
Answer 3: Statistical significance refers to the claim that a result from data generated by testing or experimentation is not likely to occur randomly or by chance but is instead likely to be attributable to a specific cause.
Answer 5: A crossover study is one that administers two or more therapies, one after the other, in a random order susceptible to bias if washout period is inadequate.
