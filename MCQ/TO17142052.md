## Question

Correction of a flexible claw-toe deformity is best treated with which of the following?

## Options

1. Extensor lengthening with a proximal interphalangeal (PIP) arthroplasty
2. Flexor tenotomy and pinning of the MTP jointMTP capsulotomy, extensor lengthening, and PIP arthrodesis
3. Flexor-to-extensor tendon transfer with extensor lengthening
4. Distal metatarsal osteotomy

## Correct option

**3. Flexor-to-extensor tendon transfer with extensor lengthening**

## Answer explanation

Answer : C

![[TO17142052-1.jpg]]

For flexible claw toes flexor tendon is divided into its two slings and transferred dorsally just like for hammer toe correction. Extensor lengthening may or may not be needed.

So best answer is (C)
