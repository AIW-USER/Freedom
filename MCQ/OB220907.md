## Question

A researcher is looking to study the impact of altering the concentrations of specific articular cartilage components. Which of the following best describes the effect on articular cartilage of downregulating the expression of genes responsible for producing aggrecan?

## Options

1. Decreased production of advanced glycosylation end-products (AGEs)
2. Decreased water content
3. Increased collagen content
4. Increased compressive strength
5. Increased osmolality

## Correct option

**2. Decreased water content**

## Answer explanation

Aggrecan is a component of articular cartilage responsible for attracting water through its hydrophilic properties and effects on increasing articular cartilage osmolality. Decreasing its production will decrease the water content of articular cartilage (Answer 2).

Aggrecan is a proteoglycan found in articular cartilage that is composed largely of chondroitin sulfate and keratin sulfate chains. Its overall negative charge and ability to increase osmolality ultimately contribute to its hydrophilic properties, which lead to the attraction of water to articular cartilage and resultant increase in compressive strength. Reducing the production of aggrecan will lead to diminished water content and compressive strength of articular cartilage.

*Roughley and Mort reviewed the role of aggrecan in articular cartilage and osteoarthritis.* *They discuss the molecular structure of aggrecan, its role in articular cartilage, and the biochemical changes that occur in pathologies such as osteoarthritis.* **They conclude** that aggrecan’s role in articular cartilage function supports the development of agents designed to reinstate more normal concentrations of this molecule in osteoarthritis.

*Zhang et al. reviewed the role of decorin in the extracellular matrix.* *They report on its molecular structure and its functionality with regard to anti-fibrotic, pro-inflammatory, anti-tumor growth, and angiogenic processes.* **They conclude** that decorin’s anti-tumor properties are particularly promising and warrant additional studies building upon the results of promising pre-clinical research.

*Ulrich-Vinther et al. reviewed the biology of articular cartilage, the pathology affecting this structure, and potential avenues for future therapeutic modalities.* *They cover the anatomic structure, molecular components, metabolic and regulatory processes, response to various degrees of injury, the basis for current osteoarthritis treatment strategies, and targets for further research.* **They conclude** that the lack of an innate ability for cartilage to regenerate or repair itself necessitates the development of interventions to address osteoarthritis, which requires a strong molecular understanding of articular cartilage.

Incorrect answers:
Answer 1: Reducing the concentration of aggrecan does not directly alter the production of AGEs.
Answer 3: The relationship between aggrecan and collagen production is minimal, and as such, downregulating aggrecan production will not greatly impact collagen production.
Answer 4: Reducing the concentration of aggrecan diminishes, not increases, the compressive strength of articular cartilage.
Answer 5: Aggrecan contributes to the higher osmolality of articular cartilage. Downregulating the production of aggrecan would decrease, not increase, the osmolality of articular cartilage.
