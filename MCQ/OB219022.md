## Question

A study is designed in which 100 patients are randomly assigned to receive either an experimental biologic drug for the treatment of avascular necrosis of the hip or the current standard of care. The data is collected prospectively and analyzed with a minimum of 5-year follow-up. Which of the following is true of this type of study?

## Options

1. An odds ratio is typically used to report the results
2. It is the best method to determine causality of an intervention
3. May be used to increase statistical power of several under-powered studies
4. Relative-risk is typically used to report the results
5. Useful for generating hypotheses for additional studies

## Correct option

**2. It is the best method to determine causality of an intervention**

## Answer explanation

A well-designed, prospective, randomized controlled trial (RCT) has long been viewed as the gold standard study for determining causality between an observed outcome and a tested or experimental intervention.

Clinical trial design can broadly be divided into two groups of studies: observational and experimental. Observational studies can be either prospective or retrospective in nature and provide either descriptive or analytic results. Descriptive studies include case reports, case series, and cross-sectional studies, and are considered useful for obtaining background information for more advanced studies. Analytic studies, on the other hand, explore the association between a given outcome and a potentially related variable and include case-control studies, cohort studies, and meta-analyses. Experimental studies, by comparison, are studies in which researchers specifically allocate treatment, preferably in a blinded, prospective, and random fashion in order to investigate the efficacy of a studied therapeutic intervention.

Zabor, et al published an overview of study design and statistical considerations in which the authors note that randomized controlled trials (RCTs) are considered the highest level of evidence to establish causal associations in clinical research. They also provide a set of guidelines that should be considered when planning an RCT or reviewing RCTs submitted for publication in peer-reviewed academic journals.

Morshed, et al provide an analysis of observational studies, which the authors state are an important source of information when randomized controlled trials cannot or should not be undertaken. The concepts of bias and confounding in particular are reviewed as obstacles to drawing
valid conclusions from an observational study.

Incorrect Answers:
1. An odds ratio is typically used to report the results of a case-control study, which is an analytic observational study, not an RCT.
3. A meta analysis is usually performed to increase the statistical power of several under-powered studies and represents an analytic observational study.
4. Relative-risk is typically used to report the results of a cohort study, which again represents an analytic observational study.
5. Case series are descriptive observational studies and are viewed as useful for generating hypotheses for additional studies.
