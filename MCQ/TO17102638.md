Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Wear And Osteolysis (Hip)

## Question

Not a risk factor for trunnionosis

## Options

1. modularity
2. small size head
3. metal on metal arthroplasty
4. taper design of stem

## Correct option

**2. small size head**

## Answer explanation

Trunnionosis is defined as the wear of the femoral head–neck interface and has been acknowledged as a source of total hip arthroplasty (THA) failure. This phenomenon appears to have gained prevalence with newer THA implant designs, particularly when modularity was introduced.

Diagnosis can be suspected in a patient with painful hip arthroplasty with elevated serum metal ions with cobalt levels being elevated more substantially than chromium levels. MRI studies can be helpful to assess for pseudotumors.

Risk factors for trunnionosis: modular designs, large cobalt chrome femoral heads (more torsional forces on the trunnion), taper design of stem, mixed alloy components and metal on metal arthroplasty.

Answer : b
