## Question

Mortise view ankle is taken in (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. 15 degree external rotation of foot
2. 15 degree internal rotation of foot
3. 10 degree supination and internal rotation
4. 15 degree cephalad beam with foot in supination and internal rotation

## Correct option

**2. 15 degree internal rotation of foot**

## Answer explanation

Mortise view or anteroposterior view of the ankle in 15° internal rotation of the ankle (to offset the posterior shadow of the fibula) is the best view to see the ankle articular surfaces.

Answer: 2
