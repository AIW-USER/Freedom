Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Slipped Capital Femoral Epiphysis (SCFE)

## Question

Most dreaded complication of SCFE surgery is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Chondrolysis
2. AVN
3. Hip dislocation
4. Coxa vara

## Correct option

**2. AVN**

## Answer explanation

Most common complication of SCFE surgery is AVN. Chondrolysis is the next common complication.

Answer : B
