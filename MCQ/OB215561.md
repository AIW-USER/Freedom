## Question

Which of the following is the best confirmatory test for Lyme disease following a positive ELISA?

## Options

1. Northern blot
2. Southern blot
3. Western blot
4. Blood culture for Borrelia burgdorferi
5. Synovial fluid culture for Borrelia burgdorferi

## Correct option

**3. Western blot**

## Answer explanation

The Western blot assay is the best confirmatory test for Lyme disease as it is specific.

Serologic testing for Lyme disease is a two-tiered approach. First, an ELISA(enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay) test is used to screen patients. This test is sensitive, but not specific for Lyme antibodies. Follow up testing after a positive or indeterminant ELISA test should consist of a Western blot assay, as this is a much more specific test. The Western blot assay tests for proteins specific for Lyme disease. It is important to note, that patients with a previous diagnosis of Lyme disease may have persistent positive tests.

*Smith et al review orthopedic implications of lyme disease.* **They report** that lyme arthritis is a common clinical manifestation of Lyme disease and should be considered in the evaluation of patients with monoarticular or pauciarticular joint complaints in a geographic area in which Lyme disease is endemic. **They conclude** by discussing treatment, which consists of antibiotic therapy, and arthroscopic synovectomy for recalcitrant cases.

*Long et al review lyme arthritis.* **They report** that lyme disease is the most common vector-borne illness in North America, namely in the Northeast and upper Midwest. **They conclude** that diagnosis should be obtained utilizing 2-tier lyme serologies.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Northern blot tests for RNA and is not used as part of the workup for lyme disease.
Answer 2: Southern blot tests for DNA and is not used as part of the workup for lyme disease.
Answer 4 & 5: Blood and/or synovial fluid cultures are not routinely used as a confirmatory test following a positive ELISA.
