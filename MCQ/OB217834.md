## Question

A 17-year-old female soccer player experiences sudden onset knee pain and swelling after a noncontact injury during a game. Imaging reveals a complete ACL tear in addition to another intra-articular injury. Which of the following injuries has the greatest propensity to heal without requiring additional surgery when treated at the time of ACL reconstruction?

## Options

1. Chondral defect
2. Meniscal tear
3. Grade III MCL tear
4. PLC tear
5. PCL tear

## Correct option

**2. Meniscal tear**

## Answer explanation

A meniscus tear has a >90% healing rate and a low risk of requiring additional surgery when treated simultaneously with an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) reconstruction.

Meniscus tears can commonly occur in the setting of an ACL tear with lateral meniscus tears occurring in the acute setting and medial meniscus tears occurring with chronic ACL tears. Surgical repair of meniscus tears with an intact ACL has modest results with a 60% success rate reported. However, meniscus repair with concomitant ACL reconstruction has a reported success rate of >90%. This is believed to be from the post-op hemarthrosis after drilling the graft tunnels for the ACL reconstruction which provides growth factors for meniscal healing.

*Toman et al. retrospectively evaluated the effectiveness of meniscal repair in the setting of ACL reconstruction in 82 patients.* **They reported** concomitant meniscal repairs at the time of ACL reconstruction had a 96% success rate at 2-year follow-up. **They concluded** repairable meniscal tears can be successfully treated at the time of ACL reconstruction.

*Westermann et al. retrospectively determined the effectiveness of concomitant meniscal repair at the time of ACL reconstruction at 6-year follow-up.* **They reported** a 14% failure rate with medial repairs failing sooner than lateral repairs. **They concluded** patients experience improvement in patient-oriented outcomes that were sustained at 6-year follow-up.

Incorrect answers
Answer 1: Treatment of chondral defects at the time of ACL reconstruction has a success rate of 60-70%.
Answer 3: Surgical treatment of grade III MCL tears at the time of ACL reconstruction has a 19% reoperation rate for arthrofibrosis.
Answer 4: Surgical treatment of posterolateral corner injuries at the time of ACL reconstruction is associated with a 90% treatment success based on objective stability.
Answer 5: Posterior cruciate ligament reconstruction at the time of an ACL reconstruction has a success rate of 85-90%.
