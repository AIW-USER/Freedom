Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — ACL

## Question

False about ACL anatomy is (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. AM bundle is longer than PL bundle
2. AM bundle is taut in flexion
3. PL bundle is taut in extension
4. PL bundle is bulkier and longer than AM bundle

## Correct option

**4. PL bundle is bulkier and longer than AM bundle**

## Answer explanation

The ACL is an intra-articular but extra synovial ligament. Proximally, it takes origin from the posteromedial aspect of the lateral femoral condyle. It runs inferomedial to get inserted on the tibia between two spines. The ACL has two bundles: the anteromedial (AM) bundle and the posterolateral (PL) bundle. They are named according to their relative insertion sites onto the tibia. In extension, the AM and PL bundles are parallel and both are relatively taut. With knee flexion AM bundle remains taut but PL bundle relaxes.

The AM bundle is longer and bulkier than the PL bundle. Primarily ACL prevents anterior tibial translation on the femur. The ACL is a secondary restraint to internal rotation of the tibia and resist varus displacement at full extension.

Answer: 4
