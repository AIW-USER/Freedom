## Question

What does type 5 in AAOS classification of acetabular bone loss indicates?

## Options

1. Segmental loss
2. Cavitary plus segmental loss
3. Pelvic discontinuity
4. Arthrodesis

## Correct option

**4. Arthrodesis**

![[TO26583579-1.png]]

Also know: AAOS classification of periprosthetic femur fracture

![[TO26583579-2.png]]

Modified 2003 Paprosky and Della Valle classification for periprosthetic acetabular fractures : most commonly used classification for periprosthetic acetabular fractures.

![[TO26583579-3.png]]

Answer : d
