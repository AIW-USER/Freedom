## Question

To achieve the best outcome from an acute, purely ligamentous Lisfranc dislocation/subluxation, treatment should consist of

## Options

1. closed reduction and percutaneous pin fixation.
2. open reduction and pin fixation.
3. open reduction and internal metallic cortical screw fixation.
4. open reduction and bioabsorbable screw fixation.
5. open reduction and primary arthrodesis with screw fixation.

## Correct option

**5. open reduction and primary arthrodesis with screw fixation.**

## Answer explanation

Anatomic reduction is critical to increasing the treatment success of a displaced Lisfranc injury. Recent studies have shown a high rate of radiographic evidence of posttraumatic arthritic changes at the tarsometatarsal joint following screw fixation of Lisfranc injuries. A recent randomized controlled trial of Lisfranc injuries demonstrated superior results of primary fusion over open reduction and internal fixation and screw fixation of the purely ligamentous Lisfranc injury.
