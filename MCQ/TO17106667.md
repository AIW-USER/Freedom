Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Shoulder Hemi Arthroplasty

## Question

Best results of shoulder hemiarthroplasty are seen in

## Options

1. Post traumatic arthrosis
2. Humeral head AVN
3. Rheumatoid arthritis
4. Glenohumeral osteoarthritis

## Correct option

**2. Humeral head AVN**

## Answer explanation

The best results of shoulder hemiarthroplasty are in patients with osteonecrosis, in whom hemiarthroplasty has been reported to provide consistently good pain relief in 90%, with an almost normal range of motion . Results are not quite as good in patients with rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, glenoid dysplasia, or posttraumatic glenohumeral arthrosis but are satisfactory in most patients, although range of motion is decreased.

Answer : b
