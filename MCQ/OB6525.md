## Question

The area moment of inertia of a structure such as a bone is influenced most by the

## Options

1. density of the bone.
2. bone's elastic modulus.
3. cross-sectional volume of the bone.
4. distance of the bone from its center of mass.
5. cross-sectional shape of the bone.

## Correct option

**4. distance of the bone from its center of mass.**

## Answer explanation

The area moment of inertia is a function of the distance squared of the mass distribution from its center of mass. Density of a material and its elastic modulus do not affect the area moment of inertia. The cross-sectional volume and shape of the bone has some effect, but two objects with the same cross-sectional volume or shape can have very different area moments of inertia (ie, a cylinder and a tube).
