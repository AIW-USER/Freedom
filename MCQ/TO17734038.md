## Question

Most specific test for spinal infection is (musculoskeletal infections- tuberculosis of spine)

## Options

1. Biopsy
2. MRI
3. PET can
4. Blood investigation along with history.

## Correct option

**1. Biopsy**

## Answer explanation

Although MRI is the gold standard in diagnosis of spinal infection, there is no pathognomonic finding on MRI that dependably discriminates between infection and possible neoplasm. Since positive blood cultures and imaging findings associated with clinical symptoms do not automatically confirm spinal infection, the definite diagnosis can only be attained by microscopic or bacteriological examination of the affected tissue.

Answer: 1
