Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Macrodactyly

## Question

Lipofibromatous hamartoma of median nerve is commonly seen in

## Options

1. Syndactyly
2. Macrodactyly
3. Polydactyly
4. Idiopathic carpal tunnel syndrome

## Correct option

**2. Macrodactyly**

## Answer explanation

Peripheral nerves in the involved ‘territory’ are usually markedly enlarged in patients with macrodactyly. The majority of patients of macrodactyly of fingers have enlargement of the median nerve in the carpal tunnel (lipofibromatous hamartoma) that can lead to significant carpal tunnel syndrome.

Answer: 2
