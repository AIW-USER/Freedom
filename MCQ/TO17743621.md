## Question

Fishmonger’s disease is not caused by (general orthopedics- anatomy, basic science and pathology)

## Options

1. Erysipelothrix rhusiopathiae
2. Streptococcus iniae
3. Mycobacterium marinum
4. Pseudomonas aeruginosa

## Correct option

**4. Pseudomonas aeruginosa**

## Answer explanation

Fishmonger’s disease: A fishmonger is a person or store that sells fish for food. In the world of the seafood business, fishmongers are at risk of severe acquired infections due to the handling and processing offish.3Acquired infections can result from penetrating sharp instruments to the skin or from the fish or seafood's sharp bony structures. Although infections can occur in both the immunocompetent and the immunosuppressed, the latter will always demonstrate a high morbidity and mortality. The common organisms that cause acquired infections in the processing and handling of fish include Streptococcus species, Mycobacterium species, Vibrio species, Aeromonas species, Erysipelothrix species, and Lactococcus species.

The most common bacteria involved are Streptococcus iniae , Vibrio vulnificus , and Mycobacterium marinum. Other pathogens reported are Erysipelothrix rhusiopathiae, Aeromonas hydrophila, Vibrio damsela, Proteus vulgaris, Morganella morganii, Lactococcus garvieae, Mycobacterium abscessus, and Streptococcus halichoeri.

Answer: 4
