## Question

Which change is not suggestive of loosening of cemented femoral stem

## Options

1. Parallel radiodense line around stem
2. Radiolucency in Gruen zone 1
3. Fracture of stem
4. Stem subsidence

## Correct option

**1. Parallel radiodense line around stem**

## Answer explanation

Following is a list of changes in the stem and the cement around it suggestive of loosening of the femoral component.

- Radiolucency between the superolateral one third of the stem (Gruen zone 1) and the adjacent cement mantle, indicating debonding of the stem from the cement and possible early stem deformation
- Radiolucency between the cement mantle and surrounding bone
- Subsidence of the stem alone or in combination with the surrounding cement mantle
- Change of the femoral stem into a more varus position
- Fragmentation of the cement, especially between the superomedial aspect of the stem and the femoral neck (Gruen zone 7)
- Fracture of the cement mantle, most commonly near the tip of the stem (Gruen zone 4)
- Deformation of the stem
- Fracture of the stem

Answer : a
