## Question

The addition of which of the following food supplements may lead to a decrease in neural tube defects?

## Options

1. Vitamin D-1,25
2. Vitamin B-12
3. Niacin
4. Folic Acid
5. Thiamine

## Correct option

**4. Folic Acid**

## Answer explanation

The use of folic acid in developed countries has lead to a decrease in neural tube defects. The incidence of neural tube defects is increased in third world countries.
