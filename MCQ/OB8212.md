## Question

Figures 73a through 73c are the radiographs of a 57-year-old woman who twisted her ankle stepping off a curb and now has lateral foot pain. Examination reveals intact skin and point tenderness over the base of the fifth metatarsal. What is the most appropriate treatment?

## Options

1. Fragment excision
2. Intramedullary screw fixation
3. Non-weight-bearing cast for 6 weeks
4. Percutaneous Kirschner wire fixation
5. Immediate weight bearing with a postoperative shoe

## Correct option

**5. Immediate weight bearing with a postoperative shoe**

## Answer explanation

The patient has an avulsion fracture of the fifth metatarsal base. Unlike a true Jones-type fracture, this is amenable to immediate weight bearing. Intramedullary screw fixation may be indicated in select patients with a Jones fracture but not with this fracture type. Fragment excision can be considered in the presence of a symptomatic nonunion of this fracture, but is not indicated acutely. Percutaneous pinning is not indicated.
