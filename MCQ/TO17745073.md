## Question

Regarding ALVAL in MOM, All are true except (arthroplasty- Hip arthroplasty- complications)

## Options

1. B lymphocytes
2. Macrophage depletion
3. Diagnosis is not made by macroscopic appearance
4. Lymphoid aggregates

## Correct option

**3. Diagnosis is not made by macroscopic appearance**

## Answer explanation

ALVAL leads to solid pseudo tumor mass formation that can be seen macroscopically.

The metal debris are cytotoxic to macrophages, leading to macrophage depletion and lymphocyte predominant lesions.

Metal-on-metal pseudotumor or aseptic lymphocyte-dominated vasculitis-associated lesions (ALVAL) are large focal solid or semi-liquid masses around metal-on-metal THR bearing prostheses. These are sterile inflammatory lesions.

ALVAL is a hypersensitivity reaction causing pseudotumor formation around the prosthesis, eventually leading to failure. The mechanism is thought to be a type IV delayed hypersensitivity reaction.

Metal ions released from prosthetic surfaces trigger a delayed immune response, characterized by lymphocytes, histiocytes, and multinucleated giant cells. Cytotoxic T cells and activated monocyte/macrophages contribute to tissue damage.

Histopathologically, ALVAL exhibits a dense perivascular inflammatory infiltrate centered on small vessels.

Clinical Features: Nonspecific pain and potential pseudotumor development. MRI may show periprosthetic cystic and solid masses. Diagnosis is confirmed via histological examination.

Elevated levels of metal ions such as Chromium and Cobalt are often found in blood and urine samples of affected patients.

Answer: 3
