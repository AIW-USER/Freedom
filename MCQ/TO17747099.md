## Question

a 30 year male patient with diabetes mellitus has bilateral saddle anesthesia, bladder bowel involvement and absent bulbocavernosus reflex. What is the diagnosis?(spine- cauda equina/conus medullaris syndrome)

## Options

1. Conus medullaris
2. Central cord syndrome
3. Brown sequard syndrome
4. Mixed cord syndrome

## Correct option

**1. Conus medullaris**

## Answer explanation

Conus medullaris syndrome, or injury of the sacral cord (conus) and lumbar nerve roots within the spinal canal, usually results in areflexic bladder, bowel, and lower extremities. Most of these injuries occur between T11 and L2 and result in flaccid paralysis in the lower extremities and loss of all bladder and perianal muscle control. The irreversible nature of this injury to the sacral segments is evidenced by the persistent absence of the bulbocavernosus reflex and the perianal wink. Motor function in the lower extremities between L1 and L4 may be present if nerve root sparing occurs.

Answer : a
