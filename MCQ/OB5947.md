## Question

You are called by a family physician asking to review the x-ray images shown in Figures A and B. She provides background information about a 9-year-old competitive soccer player complaining of mild, activity related, right ankle pain that started 1 week ago. What would be your recommendation?

![[q5947-a-screen-shot-2016-07-17-at-10-20-15-am.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q5947-b-screen-shot-2016-07-17-at-10-20-30-am.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Activity restriction, complete laboratory work-up, urgent CT chest and extremity, and referral to clinic in less than 2 weeks
2. Non-urgent extermity CT scan and referral to clinic in less than 4 weeks
3. Referral to clinic in less than 4 weeks and surgical planning for radiofrequency ablation
4. Referral to clinic in less than 4 weeks and surgical planning for biopsy, curettage and grafting
5. Symptom management and non-urgent referral to clinic

## Correct option

**5. Symptom management and non-urgent referral to clinic**

## Answer explanation

Figures A and B are AP and lateral ankle radiographs showing a osteolytic lesion with well-defined sclerotic margin over medial aspect of right distal tibial cortex, consistent with a diagnosis of nonossifying fibroma. The best recommendation would be symptom management and non-urgent referral to clinic.

Nonossifying fibromas are benign processes. Laboratory and imaging methods other than conventional radiography generally are not required. Surgery is not necessary. Follow-up with these patients is usually non-urgent, unless you suspect pathologic fracture. The pain would usually be constant and focused over the bone involved. Ankle pain is the presenting complaint and the lesion was found incidentally.

*Steffner et al. reviewed benign bone lesions.* **They state** that NOF is most commonly found in the metaphysis of long bones, with 80% in the lower extremity. Common locations include the knee (distal femur and proximal tibia) and distal tibia.

*Smith et al. reviewed nonossifying fibromas.* Classic histologic characteristics are (1) fibroblastic spindle cells in whirled or storiform pattern; (2) fibroblastic connective tissue background; (3) numerous lipophages and giant cells; (4) hemosiderin pigmentation

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1,2: Urgent referrals would be considered for aggressive/malignant appearing lesions, such as osteosarcoma or Ewing's sarcoma.
Answers: 2,3: A semi-urgent referral would be considered for benign aggressive lesions that are symptomatic or pending pathologic fracture, such as osteoid osteoma or uncameral bone cyst.
