## Question

Which of the following bone sarcomas has the worst prognosis?

## Options

1. Malignant fibrous histiocytoma
2. Conventional osteosarcoma
3. Ewing's sarcoma
4. Clear cell chondrosarcoma
5. Sarcoma arising in Paget's disease

## Correct option

**5. Sarcoma arising in Paget's disease**

## Answer explanation

Paget's sarcoma is a high-grade sarcoma that occurs most commonly in the elderly in the context of preexisting Paget's disease. Paget's sarcoma of bone typically is diagnosed in the pelvis and proximal skeleton of patients with polyostotic Paget's disease. Because the disease is usually metastatic at diagnosis, in a poor anatomic location for resection, and because the patients are too old and ill to receive chemotherapy, 5-year survival for this disease is less than 10%. In the absence of metastatic disease, these patients have a 5-year survival of greater than 50%.
