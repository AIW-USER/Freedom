## Question

Which of the following is true of the following anti-osteoporosis medications?

## Options

1. Calcitonin is anabolic in nature
2. Denosumab upregulates osteoclastic activity
3. Estrogen receptor modulators are anabolic in nature
4. Intermittent parathyroid hormone is anabolic in nature
5. Teriparatide downregulates osteoclastic activity

## Correct option

**4. Intermittent parathyroid hormone is anabolic in nature**

## Answer explanation

Intermittent parathyroid hormone (PTH) is an anabolic agent for the treatment of osteoporosis.

Unlike bisphosphonates, estrogen, estrogen receptor modulators, denosumab, and calcitonin, all of which downregulate osteoclastic activity and are antiresorptive, parathyroid hormone (PTH) is an anabolic agent. The receptor for PTH is present on osteoblasts and preosteoblasts. Intermittent dosing of PTH stimulates bone formation at a higher rate than that of bone resorption, while sustained dosing activates osteoclasts and can lead to bone resorption.

*Canalis et al. reviewed skeletal anabolic agents and bone formation.* **They report** that teriparatide and abaloparatide induce IGF-I, increase bone mineral density (BMD), reduce the incidence of vertebral and non-vertebral fractures and are approved for the treatment of postmenopausal osteoporosis. **They concluded** that cell and molecular studies have formed the foundation for the development of new anabolic therapies for osteoporosis with proven efficacy on the incidence of new fractures

*Gambacciani et al. reviewed several anti-osteoporosis medications in the treatment of osteoporosis.* **They report** that bisphosphonates are first-line therapy for patients with established osteoporosis at high risk of fracture but have some serious, yet rare, adverse events have been associated with their long-term administration. **They concluded** that in patients with established osteoporosis, often occurring in elderly women, bisphosphonates or, in more severe and selected cases, anabolic agents (teriparatide) are of benefit.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 1-3: Estrogen and estrogen receptor modulators, denosumab (a RANKL inhibitor), and calcitonin all downregulate osteoclastic activity and are therefore antiresorptive
Answer 5: Teriparatide, a PTH analog, is an anabolic anti-osteoporosis drug
