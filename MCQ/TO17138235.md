Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Congenital Vertical Talus (CVT)

## Question

Which of the following is the ideal management of a child at 10 months age who has been diagnosed with congenital vertical talus?

## Options

1. Corrective casting
2. Casting followed by mini open reduction.
3. Open soft tissue release and reductio
4. Subtalar arthrodesis.

## Correct option

**2. Casting followed by mini open reduction.**

## Answer explanation

Literature now has shown that only casting may be a treatment for oblique talus but not vertical talus. Vertical talus benefits by casting by stretching of the dorsal soft tissue. A paper which reviewed his own previous technique by Matthew Dobbs describes the mini open reduction technique which is preceded by serial casting for treatment of CVT is now the standard of care.

Answer: 2
