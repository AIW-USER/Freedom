## Question

Most commonly fused vertebrae in Klippel feil syndrome is

## Options

1. C3-C4
2. C2-C3
3. C5-C6
4. C6-C7

## Correct option

**2. C2-C3**

## Answer explanation

In KFS, most commonly the cervical fusion is between C2-C3 vertebras (71.0 %), followed by C5-C6 (67.7 %), C6-C7 (67.6 %), and C3-C4 (29.0 %)

Klippel – Feil syndrome: KFS, also known as synostosis of cervical spine is a rare condition, presentedby fusion of the cervical vertebrae due to failure of normal segmentation of the cervical somites during embriological development. KFS is characterized by classical triad found in around 50% of the patients and consisting of a short neck, low posterior hairline and restricted neck mobility.

Etiology: Maternal alcoholism -50% incidence of cervical vertebral fusions has been found on radiographs of infants with fetal alcohol syndrome. Familial Klippel-Feil syndrome gene locus has been identified on the long arm of chromosome 8.

Cervical spine involvement: apart from cervical fusion occipitalization of the atlas, hemivertebrae, and basilar impression occur frequently in patients with Klippel-Feil syndrome.

Other associations: Scoliosis is seen in 60 % of cases (Most frequent orthopaedic complication) renal abnormalities, ventricular septal defect and other cardiac anomalies, deafness, synkinesis (mirror movements), Respiratory Anomalies (Failure of lobe formation, Ectopic lungs, Restriction of lung function by shortened trunk, scoliosis, rib fusion, or deformed costovertebral joints). Sprengel deformity is seen in 20 % cases.

Treatment: in symptomatic patients surgical stabilization (posterior fusion of C3-C7 ) with or without decompression may be required.

Answer: 2
