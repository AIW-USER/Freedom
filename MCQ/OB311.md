## Question

Pharmacologically induced apoptosis occurs during the medical therapy of which of the following disorders?

## Options

1. Phenol adjuvant resection bed therapy in giant cell tumors
2. TNF-alpha inhibitor treatment in rheumatoid arthritis
3. Colchicine treatment for gout
4. Vancomycin therapy for MRSA infection
5. Doxorubicin neoadjuvant chemotherapy for cancer

## Correct option

**5. Doxorubicin neoadjuvant chemotherapy for cancer**

## Answer explanation

Apoptosis is defined as programmed cell death, and is seen during doxorubicin neoadjuvant chemotherapy for cancer. Doxorubicin inhibits DNA and RNA synthesis and blocks the function of topoisomerase II, which causes cellular apoptosis. TNF-alpha inhibition is an example of immune modulation in the treatment of rheumatoid arthritis. While the exact mechanism of action of colchicine is unknown but it is thought to inhibit the immune response and inhibit crystal formation in gout. Vancomycin inhibits proteoglycan cell wall synthesis and functions as a bacteriostatic or bacteriocidal antibiotic depending on the organism. Phenol treatment in the resection bed of a resected giant cell tumor lyses the residual tumor cells, a different mechanism of action than apoptosis.
