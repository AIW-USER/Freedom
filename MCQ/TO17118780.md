## Question

Crab meat lesions on MRI seen in (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Grade III chondromalacia patellae
2. Grade II chondromalacia patellae
3. Mucoid degeneration of ACL
4. Mucoid degeneration of PCL

## Correct option

**1. Grade III chondromalacia patellae**

## Answer explanation

Modified Outerbridge classification for chondromalacia patella:

Initially Outerbridge classification was based on the arthroscopic findings. Later it was modified based on the MRI findings

![[TO17118780-1.png]]

Outerbridge classification based on the arthroscopy findings:

Grade Pathology

I Softening and swelling of articular cartilage

II Fragmentation and fissuring of articular cartilage affecting an area of less than 0.5 inches

III Fragmentation and fissuring of articular cartilage affecting an area greater than 0.5 inches

IV Cartilage erosion to bone

Tram track appearance is seen in mucoid degeneration of PCL.

References: 1. E. Carlos Rodriguez-Merchan, Primitivo Gomez-Cardero. The Outerbridge Classification Predicts the Need for Patellar Resurfacing in TKA

Clin Ortho Relate Res. 2010 May; 468(5): 1254–1257.

2. Bhawan K Paunipagar and DD Rasalkar. Imaging of articular cartilage. Indian J Radial Imaging. 2014 Jul-Sep; 24(3): 237–248.

Answer: 1
