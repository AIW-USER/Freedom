Chapter: Spine MCQs — Spinal Cord Injury

## Question

A 33-year-old male is involved in a road traffic accident sustaining a fracture dislocation of the cervical spine. He has absent motor function, absent sensation and anal tone. The bulbocavernosus reflex is intact. Which of the following best describes this spinal cord injury pattern?

## Options

1. Central cord syndrome.
2. Incomplete spinal cord injury.
3. Complete spinal cord injury.
4. Spinal shock

## Correct option

**3. Complete spinal cord injury.**

## Answer explanation

An intact bulbocavernosus reflex indicates that the patient is not in a state of spinal shock and therefore the cord injury can be classified as a complete injury pattern in this scenario.

Answer: 3
