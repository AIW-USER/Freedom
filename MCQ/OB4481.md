## Question

A 20-year-old male sustains a right ankle injury after landing awkwardly catching a rebound in a basketball game. The next day he has swelling and ecchymosis present about the lateral ankle. ## Options

1. Short leg cast for 4 weeks followed by physical therapy
2. Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the ankle
3. Brostrom reconstruction with Gould modification
4. Functional bracing as needed and physical therapy
5. Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the ankle with intra-articular contrast

## Correct option

**4. Functional bracing as needed and physical therapy**

## Answer explanation

The clinical presentation is consistent with a lateral ankle ligament sprain. Following a period of initial ice and elevation, functional bracing as needed and physical therapy would be the most appropriate treatment.

Lateral ankle ligament sprains present with variable amounts of swelling, ecchymosis, tenderness, and loss of function. The anterior talofibular ligament {ATFL} is the most common ligament injured with ankle sprains and occurs most often with inversion with the foot in slight plantar flexion. Plantar flexion of the foot causes the ATFL to become parallel with the axis of the foot and become taut. Initial treatment consists of rest, ice, compression, and elevation (RICE). Early weight bearing and use of a protective brace during functional activities facilitates recovery better than non–weight bearing or immobilization.

*Digiovanni et al. present a level 5 review article discussing that a functional ankle rehabilitation program is the primary initial treatment for ankle sprains.*

Figure A demonstrates the area of the ATFL with the white arrow. Figure B demonstrates the anterior drawer test with ankle instability typically revealing greater than 3mm of translation. Figure C demonstrates the syndesmosis squeeze test to diagnosis high ankle sprains. The video V is a link to a video demonstrating the anterior drawer exam maneuver.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Cast immobilization is not indicated with lateral ankle sprain treatment.
Answer 2&5: MRI is reasonable for patients with recalcitrant ankle pain following a course of conservativie management or evidence of osteochondral defect on radiographs. Intra-articular contrast is not typically required.
Answer 3: The modified Brostrom procedure is an anatomic reconstruction of the lateral ankle ligaments (ATFL and/or CFL) and augmentation with the inferior extensor retinaculum.
