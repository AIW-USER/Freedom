Chapter: Congenital & Developmental Musculoskeletal Disorders MCQs — Osteogenesis Imperfecta

## Question

For which disease so field procedure is used?

## Options

1. Osteogenesis imperfecta
2. Pseudarthrosis tibia
3. Fibular hemimelia
4. Tibia hemimelia

## Correct option

**1. Osteogenesis imperfecta**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: so, field procedure or seekh kabab technique is used for correcting deformities in osteogenesis imperfecta using intramedullary rod.

Answer: 1
