## Question

A 13-year-old male presents with severe cognitive impairment, choreoathetosis, and self-mutilating behavior. He has sustained multiple fractures that have been treated conservatively and progressive scoliosis that is currently treated with TLSO bracing. The mother states he was diagnosed with Lesch-Nyhan syndrome as a child. What laboratory finding would likely be present in this patient?

## Options

1. Increased keratan sulfate in urine
2. Elevated serum uric acid
3. High serum calcium, low serum phosphate
4. Elevated serum glucocerebrosides
5. Missing portion of chromosome 15 on genetic testing

## Correct option

**2. Elevated serum uric acid**

## Answer explanation

The patient is presenting with Lesch-Nyhan syndrome which is characterized by deficient hypoxanthine-guanine phosphoribosyltransferase (HRPT) enzyme leading to elevated serum uric acid.

Lesch-Nyhan syndrome is an X-linked recessive disorder due to a deficient HPRT enzyme leading to impaired purine metabolism and elevated serum uric acid. Patients present with severe cognitive delays, choreoathetosis, self-mutilating behavior. Often, patients can sustain fractures, hip dysplasia, and progressive scoliosis. Patients can tolerate surgical treatment with outcomes similar to the cerebral palsy population.

Guidera, et al. studied eight congenitally insensate patients including congenital insensitivity to pain, Riley-Day syndrome, and Lesch-Nyhan syndrome. They reported these patients lacked pain avoidance behavior and often sustained self-inflicted injuries. They recommended conservative treatment for fractures, but operative intervention for progressive scoliosis, Charcot joint, and osteomyelitis.

Sponseller, et al. reviewed the treatment and outcomes of orthopedic manifestation of Lesch-Nyhan syndrome in nine patients. They reported Lesch-Nyhan patients can safely undergo orthopedic procedures with outcomes similar to the cerebral palsy population. They concluded awareness of the increased incidence of heterotopic ossification and femur fracture fixation failure.

Incorrect answers
Answer 1: Elevate urine keratan sulfate is associated with Morquio syndrome.
Answer 3: High serum calcium and low serum phosphate would be present in hyperparathyroidism.
Answer 4: Elevated glucocerebrosides are associated with Gaucher disease.
Answer 5: A missing portion of chromosome 15 is associated with Prader-Willi syndrome.
