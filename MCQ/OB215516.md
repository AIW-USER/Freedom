## Question

Which of the following is true regarding the anatomic structure whose relationship distinguishes body and neck fractures in the talus?

## Options

1. Articulates with the posterior facet of calcaneus
2. Articulates with the navicular
3. Articulates with the sustenaculum tali
4. Consists of the medial and lateral tubercle
5. Separated by the groove for FHL

## Correct option

**1. Articulates with the posterior facet of calcaneus**

## Answer explanation

The relationship to the lateral process of the talus distinguishes fractures of the talus body and neck. The lateral process of the talus articulates with the posterior facet of the calcaneus.

There are five articulating surfaces of the talus and nearly 70% of the talus is covered by cartilage. The inferior surface articulates with posterior facet of calcaneus. The talar head articulates with navicular bone and the sustenaculum tali. The lateral process articulates with posterior facet of calcaneus. Also, the lateral malleolus of the fibula forms the lateral margin of the talofibular joint. The posterior process consist of the medial and lateral tubercle separated by the groove for FHL. Fractures posterior to the lateral process of the talus defines a talar body fracture. Conversely, fractures anterior to the lateral process of the talus defines a talar neck fracture.

*Inokuchi et al. reviewed fractures of the talar neck (extra-articular) and body (intra-articular) fractures.* **They noted** that reported ratios between neck fractures and body fractures varied widely in the literature due to the difficulty in differentiating fractures crossing the anteromedial aspect of the trochlea. **They concluded** that classifying fractures of the talus based on the inferior (not superior) surface fracture line is a more reliable method of diagnosis.

*Vallier et al. reviewed talus fractures.* **They report** that posttraumatic arthrosis occurs frequently after talar neck and body fractures. Osteonecrosis is reported in up to 50% of talar neck fractures, although many of these injuries will revascularize without collapse of the talar dome. **They concluded** that initial fracture displacement and presence of open fractures increase the risk of osteonecrosis.

*Vallier et al. reviewed talar body fractures.* **They report** that fractures of both the talar body and neck led to development of advanced arthritis more frequently than did fractures of the talar body only. **They concluded** that worse functional outcomes are seen in association with advanced posttraumatic arthritis and osteonecrosis that progresses to collapse.

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 2 and 3: The talar head articulates with both the navicular and sustenaculum tali.
Answers 4 and 5: The posterior process of the talus consist of medial and lateral tubercle separated by groove for FHL.
