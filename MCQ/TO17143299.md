Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Fibrous Dysplasia

## Question

Which of the following bone lesions does not primarily involve the cortex of the bone?

## Options

1. Non-Ossifying Fibroma
2. Fibrous Dysplasia
3. OsteoFibrous Dysplasia
4. Adamantinoma

## Correct option

**2. Fibrous Dysplasia**

## Answer explanation

Non-Ossifying Fibroma, Osteofibrous dysplasia and Adamantinoma are all cortical lesions which do not involve the medulla unlike fibrous dysplasia which involves the medulla of bone.
