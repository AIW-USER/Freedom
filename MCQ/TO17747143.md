## Question

True about patella-femoral kinematics is[Regional Orthopedics-knee-knee kinematics]

## Options

1. At 20 to 30 degrees of flexion, the extensor lever arm is greatest and the quadriceps force required for knee extension is maximum
2. At 20 to 30 degrees of flexion, the extensor lever arm is smallest and the quadriceps force required for knee extension is minimum
3. At 20 to 30 degrees of flexion, the extensor lever arm is greatest and the quadriceps force required for knee extension is minimum
4. At 20 to 30 degrees of flexion, the extensor lever arm is smallest and the quadriceps force required for knee extension is maximum

## Correct option

**3. At 20 to 30 degrees of flexion, the extensor lever arm is greatest and the quadriceps force required for knee extension is minimum**

## Answer explanation

![[TO17747143-1.png]]
The primary function of the patella is to increase the lever arm of the extensor mechanism around the knee, improving the efficiency of quadriceps contraction

The extensor lever arm is greatest at 20 degrees of flexion and the quadriceps force required for knee extension is minimum. Quadriceps force increases significantly in the last 20 degrees of extension as less of patella comes in touch with trochlear groove and lever arm decreases.
