## Question

The most frequently involved spinal segment in rheumatoid arthritis is (spine- cervical spine- Rheumatoid arthritis involvement of cervical spine)

## Options

1. C7-C1
2. C1-C2
3. C5-C6
4. D12-L

## Correct option

**2. C1-C2**

## Answer explanation

C1-C2 joint is a synovial joint and most common target of RA in cervical spine.

Answer: 2
