## Question

A young athlete presents with burning pain over the shoulder with atrophy of supraspinatus and infraspinatus muscles. Abduction and external rotation are weak. Nerve conduction studies are likely to reveal supraspinatus nerve entrapment at (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Spinoglenoid notch
2. Suprascapular notch
3. Supraglenoid notch
4. Superior angle of scapula

## Correct option

**2. Suprascapular notch**

## Answer explanation

![[TO17138115-1.jpg]]

The suprascapular nerve arises from the upper trunk of the brachial plexus. It passes across the posterior triangle of the neck and then runs along the superior border of the scapula, passes through the suprascapular notch inferior to the superior transverse scapular ligament and enters the supraspinous fossa. Here it innervates the supraspinatus. After passing beneath the supraspinatus, it curves around the lateral border of the spine of the scapula through a spinoglenoid notch to the infraspinous fossa. In the infraspinous fossa it innervates infraspinatus muscle. Compression in the suprascapular notch will result in palsy of both supra and infraspinatus muscles while compression in the infraglenoid notch will cause palsy of infraspinatus only.

Answer: 2
