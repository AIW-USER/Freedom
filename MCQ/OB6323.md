## Options

1. Chondrosarcoma
2. Osteoblastoma
3. Chondroblastoma
4. Giant cell tumor of bone
5. Subchondral cyst

## Correct option

**1. Chondrosarcoma**

## Answer explanation

The pathology demonstrates a very cellular chondroid matrix with multinucleated forms, atypia, and myxomatous regions. This is most consistent with a myxoid chondrosarcoma. The radiograph shows a well-circumscribed lesion in the superior and medial aspect of the right acetabulum. The CT and MRI scans confirm these same findings with no evidence of matrix mineralization or significant surrounding edema. Unfortunately, in this location with this appearance, the radiographic differential diagnosis includes all the diagnoses listed.
