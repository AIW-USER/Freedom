## Question

During the posterolateral approach to the hip, the sciatic nerve is most frequently identified passing between which of the following structures?

## Options

1. Obturator internus and superior gemellus
2. Obturator internus and inferior gemellus
3. Piriformis and superior gemellus
4. Piriformis and gluteus minimus
5. Inferior gemellus and obturator externus

## Correct option

**3. Piriformis and superior gemellus**

## Answer explanation

In most (> 80%) patients, the sciatic nerve lies anterior to the piriformis as it exits the pelvis through the greater sciatic notch and then runs through the interval between the piriformis and the superior gemellus to continue its course posterior to the remainder of the short external rotators. Other variations include passing superior to or piercing the piriformis.
