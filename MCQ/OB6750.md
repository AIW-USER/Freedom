## Question

When the great toe deviates into a valgus position, the action of the abductor hallucis muscle becomes one of

## Options

1. increased abduction.
2. pronation.
3. flexion.
4. flexion and pronation.
5. extension.

## Correct option

**4. flexion and pronation.**

## Answer explanation

The abductor hallucis muscle inserts together with the medial tendon of the flexor hallucis brevis into the medial base of the proximal phalanx of the great toe. When the hallux assumes a valgus position, the action of the abductor becomes one of flexion and pronation of the first metatarsal.
