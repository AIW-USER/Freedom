Chapter: FNB 2020 — 2020 MCQ

## Question

Spine at risk sign includes (Musculoskeletal infections- TB of spine)

## Options

1. Kyphosis
2. Abscess
3. Facetal subluxation
4. Sudden neurological worsening

## Correct option

**3. Facetal subluxation**

## Answer explanation

Spine at Risk” signs: Prof Rajasekaran (India) described “spine at risk signs” (X-ray signs) in TB to caution against a progression of the tuberculous deformity. These include:

––Subluxation of the facet joint at the apex of the kyphus deformity

––Presence of retropulsion (posterior displacement) of the vertebra

––Posterior toppling of the vertebra

––Lateral translation of the vertebra.

Answer: 3
