Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Patellofemoral Instability

## Question

Fulkerson osteotomy is done for (FNB Pattern Paper 2020)

## Options

1. Habitual patella dislocation
2. Grade 1 and 2 Chondromalacia patella
3. Grade 3 and 4 Chondromalacia patella and recurrent patellar dislocation
4. Osgood Schlatter disease

## Correct option

**3. Grade 3 and 4 Chondromalacia patella and recurrent patellar dislocation**

## Answer explanation

Fulkerson osteotomy: it is a slightly oblique osteotomy of the tuberosity that transfers the tuberosity anteriorly and medially. This procedure is indicated when grade 3 or 4 chondromalacia (lateral facet or distal pole arthritis) is associated with recurrent dislocations of patella and TT-TG >20 mm. Proximal/medial facet arthritis is a contraindication.

 Answer: c
