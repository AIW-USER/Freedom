## Question

Which of the following pathologic entities is most often encountered in association with the clinical diagnosis of internal impingement of the shoulder?

## Options

1. Bankart lesion
2. SLAP tear
3. Anterior capsular contracture
4. Full-thickness rotator cuff tear
5. Humeral avulsion of glenohumeral ligaments (HAGL) lesion

## Correct option

**2. SLAP tear**

## Answer explanation

A SLAP tear with posterior extension of the labral detachment is felt to be an important aspect of pathology in internal impingement. Whether this is the cause of condition or a result of the altered glenohumeral mechanics is still debated. While described as instability after repetitive microtrauma, it is not associated with Bankart or HAGL lesions as with gross dislocations. The current theories do not associate it with anterior capsular contracture. An articular-sided partial-thickness tear of the posterior supraspinatus can be seen but full-thickness tears have not been described.
