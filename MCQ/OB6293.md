## Question

Which of the following malignant tumors most commonly contains soft-tissue calcifications seen on radiographs or CT?

## Options

1. Hemangioma
2. Ewing’s sarcoma
3. Clear cell sarcoma
4. Malignant fibrous histiocytoma
5. Synovial sarcoma

## Correct option

**5. Synovial sarcoma**

## Answer explanation

Focal calcifications causing small radiopacities are found in 15% to 20% of synovial sarcomas. Their irregular contours differentiate them from the phleboliths found in a benign hemangioma. Ewing’s sarcoma, clear cell sarcoma, and malignant fibrous histiocytoma do not commonly have calcifications within the lesions.
