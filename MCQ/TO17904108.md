## Question

Broadman area 4 is associated with which of the following:

## Options

1. Primary motor cortex
2. Primary sensory cortex
3. Primary visual cortex
4. Premotor cortex

## Correct option

**1. Primary motor cortex**

## Answer explanation

answer-a

Broadman divided the cerebral cortex into various areas based on microscopic structure of the

cortex. They are known as cytoarchitectonic fields. Remember, these areas are not functional classification, but microscopic classification based on the microscopic structure of the cerebral cortex.
