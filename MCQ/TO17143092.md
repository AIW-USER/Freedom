Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Soft Tissue Sarcomas

## Question

Herringbone pattern on histology is seen in

## Options

1. Fibrosarcoma
2. Ewing's sarcoma
3. Synovial cell sarcoma
4. Malignant fibrous histiocytoma

## Correct option

**1. Fibrosarcoma**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 1 Fibrosarcoma

Very rare entity; in fact, it has become a diagnosis of exclusion. The characteristic microscopic appearance of fibrosarcoma consists of spindle cells arranged in a herringbone pattern. The typical presentation is a 5–10-cm, slow-growing, painless mass in the deep soft tissues of the lower extremity in adults aged 30–50 years old
