Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Compound Palmar Ganglion

## Question

True about compound palmar ganglion is (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. More commonly associated with Infection of the thumb
2. More commonly associated with Infection of the little finger
3. Common in gouty arthritis
4. Hourglass swelling on both side of radial and ulnar bursa

## Correct option

**2. More commonly associated with Infection of the little finger**

## Answer explanation

Infections of the thumb can spread to radial and then to ulnar bursa while the infections of the little finger spread to involve the ulnar bursa primarily and then the forearm space of “parona”. This results in an hourglass swelling both proximal and distal to the wrist (flexor retinaculum) producing the “compound palmar ganglion”, a clinical picture more commonly seen in patients with rheumatoid arthritis and tuberculosis.

Answer : B
