## Question

Which of the following types of muscle contractions demonstrates the length-tension relationship while keeping muscle length constant?

## Options

1. Isotonic
2. Isokinetic
3. Concentric
4. Eccentric
5. Isometric

## Correct option

**5. Isometric**

## Answer explanation

Isometric contractions demonstrate the length-tension relationship while keeping muscle length constant.

Isometric contractions are those in which muscle length remains constant during force generation. They illustrate the length-tension relationship, which states that force generation is wholly dependant on the length of the muscle while contracting. If the length is too long, the sarcomeres generate little or no active tension. If a muscle is overstretched, which may occur during certain surgical procedures, then the patient may generate less than 30% maximal force.

*Gordon et al. performed a study demonstrating the variation in isometric tension with sarcomere length in vertebrate muscle fibers.* **They demonstrated** the length-tension relationship and found that it was based on the sliding-filament theory (i.e. isometric tension generation in skeletal muscle is a function of the magnitude of overlap between actin and myosin filaments).

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Isotonic contractions demonstrate the force-velocity relationship while keeping muscle force constant.
Answer 2: Isokinetic contractions demonstrate the force-velocity relationship while keeping muscle velocity constant.
Answers 3 and 4: Concentric and eccentric contractions are types of isotonic contraction, which demonstrates the force-velocity relationship.
