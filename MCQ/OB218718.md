## Question

While on your sports medicine rotation, you have a high school athlete who follows up in the office after sustaining a concussion one week prior. The attending surgeon starts discussing the various assessment tools related to concussions. Which of the following is true regarding the various assessment tools?

## Options

1. ImPACT is designed to assess the depth and duration of impaired consciousness
2. Glasgow coma scale is a computer-based neurocognitive test given to athletes prior to participation in contact sports
3. EyeBoxCNS is a commonly used sideline assessment tool
4. SCAT5 must take at least 10 minutes to perform
5. SCAT5 and ImPACT are applied to more severe head injuries

## Correct option

**4. SCAT5 must take at least 10 minutes to perform**

## Answer explanation

The SCAT5 is the most commonly used sideline assessment tool. SCAT5 is a neuropsychological test for attention and memory. SCAT5 can be performed on the sideline in less than 10 minutes.

Concussions are a subset of mild traumatic brain injury (mTBI) characterized by acute transient impairment of neurologic function secondary to an impulsive force transmitted to the head. Diagnosis is made by careful neurological evaluation of an athlete following head trauma with a focus on cognitive function. Treatment is immediate removal from play followed by cognitive and physical rest for 24-48 hours, and subsequent completion of a graduated return to play protocol.

*Echemendia et al. reviews the Sport Concussion Assessment Tool 5th Edition (SCAT5).* **They reported** that this tool is the most recent revision of a sport concussion evaluation tool for use by healthcare professionals in the acute evaluation of suspected concussions. **They concluded** that the SCAT5 is intended for use in those who are 13 years of age or older, whereas the Child SCAT5 is a tool for those aged 5-12 years.

*Davis et al. reviewed the Child Sport Concussion Assessment Tool 5th Edition (Child SCAT5).* **They reported** that this tool was developed for children aged between 5 and 12 years. **They concluded** that the current revision of the test, the Child SCAT5, was edited after a review of the literature.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: ImPACT is a computer-based neurocognitive test given to athletes prior to participation in contact sports and the office after an injury to guide treatment.
Answer 2: The Glasgow coma scale is designed to assess the depth and duration of impaired consciousness. Many concussions do not have impaired consciousness.
Answer 3: The EyeBoxCNS test is a neurodiagnostic test that measures eye movements. The test can be helpful for concussion management and is done in the clinic on a computer but not on the sideline.
Answer 5: The Glasgow scale is applied to more severe head injuries.
