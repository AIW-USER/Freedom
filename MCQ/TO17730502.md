## Question

Which is not true about Stove in the chest? (Traumatology- polytrauma patients)

## Options

1. Paradoxical respiration
2. Flail chest injury
3. Penetrating injury causing multiple broken ribs at multiple places
4. Flail segment collapses into chest

## Correct option

**3. Penetrating injury causing multiple broken ribs at multiple places**

## Answer explanation

A stove-in chest is a rare and complex type of flail chest injury where the flail segment collapses into the chest. It is caused by severe blunt trauma to the chest wall and not due to penetrating injury.

Flail chest occurs when multiple adjacent ribs are broken at multiple places (2 or 3 ribs in at least 2 places), separating a segment, so a part of the chest wall moves independently.

Paradoxical breathing: It may occur with flail/stove in chest. The flail segment moves in the opposite direction to the rest of the chest wall, because of the ambient pressure in comparison to the pressure inside the lungs, it goes in while the rest of the chest is moving out, and vice versa.

Answer: 3
