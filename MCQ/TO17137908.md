Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Proximal Humerus Fractures

## Question

Which combined cortical thickness (medial plus lateral) in proximal humeral fracture precludes internal fixation?

## Options

1. 5mm
2. 6mm
3. 4mm
4. 7mm

## Correct option

**3. 4mm**

## Answer explanation

The combined cortical thickness is the average of the medial and lateral cortical thickness at two levels. Level 1, most proximal aspect of humeral diaphysis, is at level in which endosteal borders of medial and lateral cortices are parallel. Level 2 is 20 mm distal to level 1.

Generally, a cortical thickness of less than 4 mm precludes internal fixation because adequate screw purchase cannot be obtained; sling immobilization, trans osseous suture, or hemiarthroplasty may be better options.
