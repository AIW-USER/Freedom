## Question

Bending cantilever fatigue and stem failure in THR is caused by

## Options

1. Proximal loosening in uncemented stem
2. Distal loosening in cemented stem
3. Proximal loosening in cemented stem
4. Distal loosening in uncemented stem

## Correct option

**3. Proximal loosening in cemented stem**

## Answer explanation

Bending cantilever fatigue is caused by proximal loss of stem support while distally the stem is securely fixed. Although it can occur in uncemented stems but it is mainly seen in cemented stem when proximal cement breaks up, but distal portion of stem remains tightly fixed in place. This leads to medial migration or deformation of the stem and may cause stem breakage.

Answer : c
