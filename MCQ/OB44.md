## Question

A 13-year-old girl presents with an isolated distal femur osteosarcoma that extends into the soft tissue. Work-up is negative for metastasis, but biopsy reveals a high grade lesion. What is the stage of this tumor by the Musculoskeletal Tumor Society system?

## Options

1. I A
2. II A
3. I B
4. II B
5. III

## Correct option

**4. II B**

## Answer explanation

This patient has a osteosarcoma that is high grade, extracompartment, and without metastases, so it is a IIB by the MSTS Staging System. This is the most common MSTS stage for an osteosarcoma to present. Here is a review of the stages in the MSTS (Enneking) Staging System:
(I-A = Low Grade, intracompartmental, No Metastases);
(I-B = Low Grade, extracompartment, No Metastases);
(II-A = High Grade, intracompartmental, No Metastases);
(II-B = High Grade, extracompartment, No Metastases);
(III = Any Grade, Distant Metastases).
