Chapter: Nerve Injury MCQs — Miscellaneous Nerve Injuries

## Question

Bowler’ thumb is (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Injury of ulnar collateral ligament of thumb
2. Neuropathy of the ulnar digital nerve of the thumb
3. Mallet injury of the thumb
4. Mallet injury of the index finger

## Correct option

**2. Neuropathy of the ulnar digital nerve of the thumb**

## Answer explanation

Bowler's thumb is a traumatic neuropathy in which the ulnar digital nerve of the thumb undergoes chronic frictional irritation that becomes symptomatic. Thickening (Perineural fibrosis) of the nerve occurs due to constant friction as the ball rubs against the lateral thumb surface during bowling.

Patients typically present with localized pain and tenderness at the first web space, numbness, and/or paresthesias that extend distally from the first web space along the medial thumb surface. On physical examination, a small mass may be palpable and will often be tender with hyperesthesia in the surrounding areas.

Treatment of the bowler's thumb is typically conservative, with recommended rest, relief of pressure, and avoidance of the inciting activity. Surgery including neurolysis, neurectomy, and transposition of the ulnar digital nerve is required in refractory cases.

Answer: 2
