## Question

Occlusion of the anterior choroidal artery results in

- Contralateral hemiplegia
- Hemi hypesthesia
- Homonymous hemianopia
- Impaired cognition

## Options

1. I, II, III
2. I, III
3. II, IV
4. All of the above

## Correct option

**1. I, II, III**

## Answer explanation

The anterior choroidal artery is the most distal branch of the internal carotid artery. It arises after the origin of the posterior communicating artery and courses posterolaterally.

It supplies the genu and posterior limb of the internal capsule, the lentiform nucleus (medial part), the lateral thalamus, and the optic tract.

AChA infarct causes contra lateral hemiplegia, hemi hypoesthesia and homonymous hemianopia. But it does not cause any impairment of cognition
