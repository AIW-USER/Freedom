## Question

The structure most at risk during lateral mass screw placement is the:

## Options

1. Vertebral artery
2. Spinal cord
3. Nerve root
4. Thoracic duct

## Correct option

**3. Nerve root**

## Answer explanation

Lateral mass screws placed in the cervical spine for plate fixation are directed 30Â° lateral and 15Â° cephalad from a point 1 mm medial to the mid-portion of the particular pillar. The nerve root exits at the anterolateral portion of the facet joint and is at risk of injury. The vertebral artery may be injured in screws placed too medially. The spinal cord is essentially free of danger with
accepted techniques.
