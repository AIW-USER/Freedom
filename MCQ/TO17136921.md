Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Calcaneus Fractures

## Question

Most strong association of calcaneal fracture with foot compartment syndrome is seen with (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Compromised soft tissue envelope
2. Sander’s type 4fracture
3. Delayed management
4. Crush injury foot

## Correct option

**2. Sander’s type 4fracture**

## Answer explanation

Compartment syndrome is seen in 3 to 10 % of calcaneal fractures. Most commonly it is seen with sanders type 4 fractures.

Answer: 2
