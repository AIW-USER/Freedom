## Question

Figure 85a shows the radiograph of a 10-year-old boy who has back pain. A representative electron microscopy specimen from a biopsy is shown in Figure 85b. What is the structure outlined by the black arrow in Figure 85b?

![[q7173-a-85a.jpg]]
*Figure A*

![[q7173-b-85b.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Langerhans cell
2. Plasma cell
3. Birbeck granule
4. Kupffer cell
5. Antibody

## Correct option

**3. Birbeck granule**

## Answer explanation

The patient has Langerhans cell histiocytosis (LCH). The radiograph shows classic vertebra plana. The electron micrograph specimen demonstrates tennis-racquet shaped cytoplasmic organelles called Birbeck granules that are diagnostic of LCH. The function of these organelles is not entirely known. LCH is also confirmed by CD1a immunohistochemical staining. Langerhans cells are the neoplastic cell in LCH but are not the structure seen in the electron micrograph image. Plasma cells are mature B cells that secrete antibodies and are found in multiple myeloma. Kupffer cells are specialized macrophages located in the liver.
