Chapter: Regional Orthopedics MCQs — Patellofemoral Instability

## Question

Maximum contact between patella and trochlear groove occurs at what degree of knee flexion

## Options

1. 30
2. 60
3. 90
4. 120

## Correct option

**3. 90**

## Answer explanation

Patellofemoral biomechanics: patellofemoral contact area increases by increasing flexion angle from 30 to 90 degrees. Beyond 90 degree posterior surface of quadriceps tendon makes contact with the trochlear facet of the femur. Between 90 and 135 degrees odd facet of the patella makes contact with the medial condyle of the femur.

Answer : c
