Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Giant Cell Tumor

## Question

Common site of extraarticular GCT is (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Pelvis
2. Extremities
3. Spine
4. Abdomen

## Correct option

**2. Extremities**

## Answer explanation

Giant cell tumor of tendon sheath: This is also known as giant cell synovioma. It is a firm nodular tenosynovitis of tendons of hands and wrist. Flexor tendons are more commonly involved. Usually, it is painless but may cause pressure symptoms and cortical erosion. Treatment is surgical excision.

Answer: 2
