## Question

A 23-year-old female presents complaining of bilateral hip pain. She was recently married and would like to conceive soon. On physical exam, she has dolichostenomelia, pes planus, and a scoliotic deformity of the spine. Imaging is shown in Figure A. What test or evaluation should the patient be referred for prior to conception?

![[q3891-a-bilateral-protrusio.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Genetic testing
2. CT of the pelvis
3. Cardiovascular evaluation
4. MRI of the spine
5. MRI of the brain

## Correct option

**3. Cardiovascular evaluation**

## Answer explanation

This patient has protrusio acetabuli and Marfan syndrome. Prior to conception, she should be sent for a cardiovascular examination as patients may have aortic dissection or mitral valve prolapse, which can be fatal during pregnancy.

Marfan syndrome is a connective tissue disorder that is inherited in an autosomal dominant fashion. It has no ethnic or gender predilection. It is associated with a mutation in the fibrillin-1 gene located on chromosome 15 (locus CH 15q21). Patients may exhibit dolichostenomelia (arm span greater than height), arachnodactyly, scoliosis, protrusio acetabuli, ligamentous laxity, pes planovalgus, and dural ectasia. Cardiac abnormalities including aortic root dilatation, aortic dissection, and mitral valve prolapse may cause sudden cardiac death during periods of exertion or pregnancy. The differential diagnosis of protrusio acetabuli includes: Marfan syndrome, Paget disease, rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthropathy, ankylosing spondylitis, infection (including tuberculosis), osteomalacia, fibrous dysplasia, and idiopathic causes.

*Van de Velde et al. performed a review of protrusio acetabuli in Marfan syndrome.* **They report** that protrusio acetabuli is a criterion for the diagnosis of Marfan syndrome and it may result in secondary osteoarthritic changes of the hip. Radiographic criteria are an abnormally positioned acetabular line, a center-edge angle of Wiberg of >40°, and crossing of the teardrop by the ilioischial line. In skeletally mature patients, valgus intertrochanteric osteotomy and eventually total hip arthroplasty are the only methods available for correction of the protrusio acetabuli.

*Bitterman et al. performed a review of Marfan syndrome.* **They report** that patients with Marfan syndrome may elect to undergo preimplantation genetic testing before conception. Pregnant women with Marfan syndrome require strict surveillance throughout pregnancy. The hormonal and hemodynamic changes of pregnancy put women at high risk of aortic dilation, dissection, and sudden death. **They conclude** that avoiding hypertension during pregnancy is essential, and beta blockers are an appropriate first-line therapy.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Though the patient may elect for genetic testing prior to conception, a cardiac evaluation should be obtained first.
Answer 2: CT of the pelvis may be necessary to evaluate the protrusio acetabuli, however, a cardiac evaluation should be obtained prior to conception.
Answer 4: An MRI of the spine may be obtained to evaluate the patient's scoliotic deformity, however, a cardiac evaluation should be obtained prior to conception.
Answer 5: An MRI of the brain may be obtained to evaluate the possibility of dural ectasia, however, a cardiac evaluation should be obtained prior to conception.
