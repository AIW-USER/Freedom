## Question

Fallen fragment sign is pathognomonic of (orthopedic oncology- benign tumors and tumor like lesions- simple bone cyst)

## Options

1. Aneurysmal bone cyst pathological fracture
2. Simple bone cyst before fracture
3. Fibrous dysplasia
4. Fracture of unicameral bone cyst

## Correct option

**4. Fracture of unicameral bone cyst**

## Answer explanation

The fallen fragment sign refers to a bony fragment which is seen resting at the dependent part of a simple bone cyst after pathological fracture. It is pathognomonic of simple /unicameral bone cyst.
