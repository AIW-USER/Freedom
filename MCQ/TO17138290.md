## Question

Hoke’s lengthening for equinus contracture of a spastic hemiplegic foot occurs in what zone of the Gastrosoleus complex?

## Options

1. Zone 1
2. Zone 2
3. Zone 3
4. Zone 2 + 3

## Correct option

**3. Zone 3**

## Answer explanation

In spastic diplegic cerebral palsy, isolated gastrocnemius contracture is usually causative for the deformity whereas in hemiplegics the gastrosoleus complex is tight. The Silfverskiold test is used to differentiate isolated gastrocnemius contracture from a Gastrosoleus contracture.

Hoke’s lengthening is performed in the tendinous portion of the tendoachilles where a triple hemisection helps in slide lengthening of the tendoachilles thereby addressing the equinus. The hemisection direction are decided based on the preoperative varus or valgus deformity at the heel.
