## Question

Fallen fragment sign is seen in( orthopedic oncology- benign tumor and tumor like lesions-simple bone cyst)

## Options

1. Giant cell tumor
2. Simple bone cyst
3. Aneurysmal bone cyst
4. Fibrous dysplasia

## Correct option

**2. Simple bone cyst**

## Answer explanation

Diagnostic of the SBC is the “fallen fragment/leaf sign”. A small fragment of cortex sometimes breaks and the piece settles to the most dependent part of the cyst, confirming its empty cystic nature. However, in some cases the broken fragment does not fall as it remains attached to the periosteum but it folds inwards. This is called a “trapdoor sign”.
