## Question

While on a mission trip, a 3-year-old boy was referred to the clinic by a local pediatrician for evaluation of a left upper extremity deformity. The patient remains in the waiting room for radiographs and has yet to be seen. When considering the broad differential associated with congenital upper extremity deformities, which of the following conditions has been found to most commonly arise from spontaneous mutations?

## Options

1. Cleft hand
2. Madelung's deformity
3. Syndactyly
4. Radial clubhand
5. Symphalangism

## Correct option

**4. Radial clubhand**

## Answer explanation

Radial clubhand, while commonly associated with syndromes, has been found to be a result of spontaneous mutations when found in isolation.

Radial clubhand (radial deficiency) is a rare birth defect, occurring in approximately 1 in 50,000-100,000, and having a predilection for males. Patients are often diagnosed in utero and present shortly thereafter for evaluation. In isolation, the anomaly is believed to most commonly stem from spontaneous mutation. A thorough evaluation and work-up are necessary, however, due to the condition's high rate of association with syndromes (30-44%), namely thrombocytopenia-absent radius (TAR), Fanconi's anemia, Holt-Oram, and VACTERL/VATER syndromes.

*Dy et al. performed a review examining the embryology, diagnosis, and evaluation of several congenital hand anomalies, including radial & ulnar longitudinal deficiency, central deficiency (cleft hand), thumb hypoplasia, syn- and polydactyly, and amniotic constriction band.* **The authors note** their rarity but highlight the importance of high rates of association with medical syndromes and the need for thorough history-taking/physical examination with possible referrals for further diagnostic workup.

*Wall et al. review current concepts concerning the management of radial longitudinal deficiency.* **The authors highlight** a thorough work-up, including a thorough physical examination, a complete blood count, an echocardiogram, abdominal ultrasound, and possible referral to genetic counseling for syndromic evaluation. Treatment of radial clubhand centers around improving hand function and is largely dependent on the degree of radial hypoplasia, which can include tendon transfers, pollicization, and/or hand centralization.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: cleft hand inheritance is autosomal dominant with variable penetrance, with 70% inheriting the gene demonstrating the malformation
Answer 2: Madelung's deformity exhibits autosomal dominant inheritance
Answer 3: syndactyly most commonly exhibits autosomal dominant inheritance with reduced penetrance and only 10-40% experience the deformity
Answer 4: symphalangism expresses autosomal dominance inheritance
