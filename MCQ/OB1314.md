## Question

What is the most important factor when choosing an optimal lower limb prosthesis for an adult patient?

## Options

1. Prosthesis cost
2. Patient comorbidities
3. Patient functional status
4. Patient gender
5. Patient age

## Correct option

**3. Patient functional status**

## Answer explanation

The most important factor when choosing an optimal lower limb prosthesis for an adult patient is the current and potential functional requirements of the patient.

*Pinzur reviewed 90 patients treated with lower extremity amputations for peripheral vascular insufficiency and* **found** the current and potential functional status of the patient were more important than any other factor in creating the best lower limb prosthesis for the patient.
