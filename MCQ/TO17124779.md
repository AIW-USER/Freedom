## Question

The brachial plexus can be found exiting between which two muscles?

## Options

1. Scalenus Medius and levator scapulae
2. Scalenus Medius and scalenus posterior
3. Scalenus anterior and scalenus Medius
4. Teres major and teres minor

## Correct option

**3. Scalenus anterior and scalenus Medius**

## Answer explanation

Answer- C
