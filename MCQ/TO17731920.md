## Question

Piano key sign is not seen in( regional orthopedics-hand and wrist- TFCC injury)

## Options

1. TFCC tear
2. Ulnar styloid fracture
3. Nonunion Galeazzi fracture dislocation
4. Madelung deformity

## Correct option

**2. Ulnar styloid fracture**

## Answer explanation

Piano key sign: Balloting (up and down movements as in pressing a piano key) of distal ulnar end can be demonstrated in instability of the DRUJ. It is seen in Galeazzi fracture dislocation, Madelung deformity of distal end radius (DER) and tear of triangular fibrocartilage complex (TFCC) injury.

The "grand-piano sign" is a well-known indicator of proper rotational femoral alignment during TKR.

Answer: 2
