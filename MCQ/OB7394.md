## Question

Figure 6 shows a sagittal oblique MRI scan of a right shoulder. The asterisk indicates what anatomic structure?

![[q7394-a-6.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Subscapularis
2. Supraspinatus
3. Infraspinatus
4. Teres minor
5. Teres major

## Correct option

**1. Subscapularis**

## Answer explanation

The asterisk indicates the subscapularis. The sagittal oblique MRI image shown is taken at the level of the coracoid, which allows orientation of the anterior and posterior aspects of the shoulder. Evaluation of the rotator cuff in this plane is important to determine the amount of muscle atrophy and fatty infiltration, which may determine prognosis for rotator cuff repair. The supraspinatus is superior to the glenoid and the infraspinatus; teres minor and teres major are posterior.
