Chapter: Musculoskeletal Infections MCQs — Tuberculosis of Hip,Knee and Peripheral Joints

## Question

Melon seed bodies are seen in [FNB 2014]

## Options

1. Pigmented villonodular synovitis
2. Tubercular synovitis
3. Rheumatoid arthritis
4. Madura foot

## Correct option

**2. Tubercular synovitis**

## Answer explanation

Operative findings of "rice bodies, millet seeds, or melon seeds" are highly suggestive of tuberculous tenosynovitis
