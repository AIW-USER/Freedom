## Question

Ulnar paradox is due to( peripheral nerve injuries- nerve injuries of upper limb- ulnar nerve palsy)

## Options

1. FDP
2. FDS
3. FCU
4. FCR

## Correct option

**1. FDP**

## Answer explanation

Ulnar paradox: Higher is the injury of the ulnar nerve, lesser will be the clawing of fingers. Usually one would assume that more proximal ulnar nerve injury would produce more clawing but what is seen is opposite; hence the name paradox. This happens in case of ulnar nerve injury because the ulnar nerve also supplies the medial half of flexor digitorum profundus. Lesion at or above the elbow would also cause palsy of this muscle so flexion of the IP joint is weakened producing less clawing. Whereas in distal lesion, clawing is more due to sparing of FDP..

Answer: 1
