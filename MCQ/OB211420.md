## Question

A 28-year-old male presents to the ED for evaluation of left hand pain and swelling. He's ultimately diagnosed with an abscess and undergoes incision and drainage in the ED. Cultures are found to be positive for methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) that is resistant to erythromycin. You wish to start the patient on clindamycin. Which test can be obtained to determine if this drug will be effective?

## Options

1. Phenylalanine deaminase
2. ß lactamase
3. Citrate
4. D-zone
5. Decarboxylase

## Correct option

**4. D-zone**

## Answer explanation

The D-zone test is used to determine clindamycin resistance in patients with erythromycin resistant MRSA.

Isolates of MRSA that are resistant to erythromycin have been shown to become resistant to clindamycin, a process called inducible resistance, which is conferred by a plasmid that alters the 50S ribosome binding site for both clindamycin and erythromycin. In patients who are found to have erythromycin resistant MRSA a D-zone test should be performed prior to starting clindamycin to confirm that there is no resistance. If a positive D-zone test is obtained, clindamycin should not be selected as an antibiotic.

*Marcotte et al. review community-acquired methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (CA-MRSA) in orthopedic surgery.* **They report** that CA-MRSA is increasingly prevalent, particularly among athletes, children in day care, homeless persons, intravenous drug users, men who have sex with men, military recruits, certain minorities (ie, Alaskan Natives, Native Americans, Pacific Islanders), and prison inmates. **They conclude** that when the patient is a member of an at-risk group or has any of the risk factors for CA-MRSA, beta-lactam antibiotics (eg, methicillin) are no longer a reasonable choice for treatment.

*Steward et al. performed a study to determine the efficacy of testing for induced clindamycin resistance in erythromycin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus.* **They reported** that resistance to erythromycin and clindamycin can occur through methylation of their ribosomal target site (25), which is typically mediated by erm genes. **They found** that disk diffusion is the preferred method for testing S. aureus isolates for inducible clindamycin resistance.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: The Phenylalanine deaminase tests the ability of an organism to produce the enzyme deaminase, which can be used to differentiate members of the proteus genera.
Answer 2: The β lactamase test is used to detect the enzyme β-lactamase, which confers penicillin resistance to various bacterial organisms. This test would not be useful in this scenario.
Answer 3: Citrate agar is used to test an organism’s ability to utilize citrate as a source of energy.
Answer 5: The decarboxylase test is utilized to differentiate decarboxylase producing Enterobacteriaceae from other gram negative rods.
