## Question

A 12-year-old female presents with increasing thigh pain over the past month. Radiographs and biopsy are demonstrated in Figures A and B. She is started on chemotherapy and undergoes surgical resection of the tumor. Which of the following factors places the patient at greatest risk for local recurrence?

*Figure A*

![[q5939-b-screenshot-2026-01-10-at-10-07-39-am.jpg]]
*Figure B*

## Options

1. Location of tumor in the distal femur
2. Surgical margins
3. Large tumor size
4. Use of neoadjuvant chemotherapy
5. High histologic grade

## Correct option

**2. Surgical margins**

## Answer explanation

The patient has osteosarcoma of the distal femur treated with chemotherapy and resection. Local recurrence of primary malignant bone tumors is most directly related to surgical margins.

The goal of orthopaedic oncologic surgery is to achieve local cure of primary malignant bone tumors with complete resection. Margins can be defined as intralesional, marginal, wide, or radical. Intralesional excision is through the tumor. Marginal excision is through the reactive tissue that surrounds the tumor, Wide excision is through normal tissues. The size of the normal tissue remains a topic of debate within the literature. Radical excision involves the entire compartment.

*Biermann et al provided guidelines on the evaluation and management of primary bone cancers.* **The authors note** the importance of complete surgical resection to reduce the risk for local recurrence.

*Li et al retrospectively reviewed cases of conventional osteosarcoma to investigate the impact of close margins on local recurrence and overall survival for osteosarcoma patients treated with neoadjuvant therapy.* *Patients with positive margins had increased risk of local recurrence (57.1%) compared with patients with wide margins and close margins.* There was no significant difference in local recurrence between close margins and wide margins (8.3% vs. 10.7%).

Figure A demonstrates osteosarcoma of the distal femur. Figure B shows the histologic appearance of osteosarcoma with irregular woven bone and atypical cells.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1, 3, 4, and 5: Anatomic location, tumor size, duration of chemotherapy, and tumor grade all influence patient prognosis and function, but are not predictive of local recurrence.
Answer 2: Wide surgical resection would decrease the risk for local recurrence.
