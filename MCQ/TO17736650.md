## Question

Which is the most common muscle ruptured after a total shoulder arthroplasty? (arthroplasty- shoulder)

## Options

1. Subscapularis
2. Supraspinatus
3. Deltoid
4. External rotators

## Correct option

**1. Subscapularis**

## Answer explanation

Subscapularis is the most commonly torn muscle after a TSA. Factors leading to the same include: Multiple previous operations, over stutting of joint, aggressive physiotherapy, Tendon length compromise owing to poor surgical technique.

Answer: 1
