## Question

Figure 29 shows the radiograph of a 10-year-old boy who injured his knee playing football. What is the most appropriate initial treatment?

![[q6937-a-29.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Closed reduction and casting
2. Flexible nailing
3. Blade plate fixation
4. Anatomic reduction and smooth pin fixation with supplemental casting
5. Open or closed reduction and screw fixation

## Correct option

**4. Anatomic reduction and smooth pin fixation with supplemental casting**

## Answer explanation

Salter type I fractures of the distal femur are quite unstable; therefore, closed reduction and cast immobilization can be expected to result in high rates of redisplacement. Optimal treatment consists of open or closed reduction and smooth pin fixation, and supplemental casting is required to ensure fracture stability. Screw fixation may increase rates of growth plate injury. Blade plate or flexible nail fixation will be challenging to apply and is not necessary.
