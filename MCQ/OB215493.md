## Question

Which of the following is associated with increased taper corrosion in total hip arthroplasty?

## Options

1. Mixed alloy taper and bore components
2. Use of a 4,000 N impaction force during taper assembly
3. Low length femoral heads
4. Dry taper assembly
5. Low offset femoral stems

## Correct option

**1. Mixed alloy taper and bore components**

## Answer explanation

Mixed alloy components of the taper and bore in total hip arthroplasty (THA) have been associated with increased corrosion rates.

Trunnion corrosion, also known as trunnionosis, is the chemical degradation of the femoral stem taper in total hip arthroplasty implants. There are several mechanisms by which corrosion occurs including galvanic, fretting, and crevice corrosion. The end result is the release of metal ions and debris that deleteriously affect the local tissues. Several factors have been associated with an increased corrosion rate including assembly force <4,000 N, wet taper assembly, high offset stems, high length femoral heads, and mixed alloy components.

*Lum et al. reviewed the history, engineering, and biomechanics of tapers in total hip arthroplasty.* From the available literature, mixed-alloy components, insufficient taper assembly force, wet taper during assembly, lower flexural rigidity of the stem, and previous taper damage are associated with corrosion. **The authors recommended** using a 4,000 N impaction force during the taper assembly to enhance taper-bore contact.

*Mroczkowski et al. performed a biomechanical study of electrochemical potentials at taper junctions under various loading and assembly conditions.* With increasing load across the taper, **they found** a decrease in open circuit potential (OCP) and an increase in fretting current compared to standard calomel electrode (SCE) with specimens assembled in the air having a higher load to the onset of corrosion. **The authors concluded** environmental conditions and assembly load affect the stability of modular taper junctions.

*Del Balso et al. performed a retrospective retrieval study of 56 femoral heads and 17 femoral stems.* **They found** increased fretting damage in increased offset femoral stems and high length femoral heads with most damage occurring in the central portion of the female bore.

Incorrect answers:
Answer 2: Taper assembly with a 4,000 N force is recommended for optimal contact between the taper and the bore components.
Answer 3: Increased corrosion has been associated with longer-length femoral heads.
Answer 4: Wet taper conditions have been associated with increased taper corrosion.
Answer 5: Higher offset femoral stems have been associated with increased taper corrosion.
