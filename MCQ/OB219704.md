## Question

This study concluded, when compared to nonoperative management, surgery was associated with which of the following?

## Options

1. Greater improvement of pain
2. Greater improvement of pain and function
3. An increase in adjacent level fractures
4. An improvement in sagittal balance

## Correct option

**1. Greater improvement of pain**

## Answer explanation

This meta-analysis provides evidence in favor of the surgical treatment of OVFs and found surgery was associated with greater improvement of pain.
