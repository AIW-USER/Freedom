## Question

Which is not a sequel of fracture neck of femur in children (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. Coxa vara
2. Chondrolysis
3. Physeal arrest
4. Osteonecrosis

## Correct option

**2. Chondrolysis**

## Answer explanation

Complications related to fracture neck of femur in children include in decreasing frequency:

Osteonecrosis (most common), coxa vara, premature physeal closure causing LLD, Nonunion, infection , chondrolysis (rare).

Answer: 2
