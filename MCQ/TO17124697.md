## Question

Medial scapular muscles are innervated by (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Superior subscapular
2. Inferior subscapular
3. Dorsal scapular
4. Thoraco dorsal

## Correct option

**3. Dorsal scapular**

## Answer explanation

Muscles which originate on the medial border of scapula are-

 Teres major- It originates from the medial border of the scapula (anterior surface) and inserts laterally on the humerus. (teres minor originates from lateral border of scapula)

On the posterior surface, the deep extrinsic muscles (levator scapulae, rhomboid major, and rhomboid minor) originate on the medial border. Levator scapulae inserts on the transverse processes of the upper cervical vertebrae. Rhomboids muscles attach to the spinous processes of the upper thoracic vertebrae.

Supraspinatus, infraspinatus and subscapularis take their origin from supraspinatus, infraspinatus and subscapular fossa respectively.

Nerves supply of muscles around scapula:

- the dorsal scapular nerve (levator and rhomboid muscles) is from the anterior ramus of C5;
- the suprascapular nerve (supraspinatus and infraspinatus muscles) is from the superior trunk;
- the four other nerves to this region (the superior and inferior subscapular, thoracodorsal, and axillary) are branches of the posterior cord and supply the subscapularis, teres major, latissimus dorsi, deltoid, and teres minor muscles.

So medial scapular muscles are innervated by dorsal scapular nerve, suprascapular nerve,

Answer: 3
