## Question

## Options

1. Figure B
2. Figure C
3. Figure D
4. Figure E
5. Figure F

## Correct option

**2. Figure C**

## Answer explanation

The patient's age, history, and imaging studies are consistent with chondroblastoma. The knee radiograph shows a radiolucent lesion with sclerotic margins centered in the epiphysis of the proximal tibia.

Figure C is representative of chondroblastoma and has chondroblasts arranged in a "cobblestone" or "chickenwire calcification" pattern. The chondroblasts have an oval shaped nuclei with a prominent groove which has been likened to a coffee bean.

Incorrect Answers:
Figure B has many blue, small round cells consistent with Ewings sarcoma. Ewing's sarcoma can be found in patients of this age cohort, but the radiographs will demonstrate a destructive metaphyseal or diaphyseal lesion that shows "onion skinning" periosteal reaction and a "moth-eaten" or mottled appearance.

Figure D is representative of a Grade 2 chondrosarcoma with chondrocytes that have increased cellularity, atypia, and mitoses.

Figure E is consistent with malignant fibrous histiocytoma(MFH) showing pleomorphic spindle cells in a storiform pattern .

Figure F shows a high grade dedifferentiated chondrosarcoma with a chondroid component and spindle cell component. Chondrosarcomas and MFH are typically found in older adults.
