## Question

At what age does longitudinal growth usually arrest in boys at the distal femur physeal growth plate?

## Options

1. 18
2. 16
3. 14
4. 12
5. 10

## Correct option

**2. 16**

## Answer explanation

Longitudinal growth usually arrest at the distal femur physeal growth plate occurs in boys at 16 years of age and girls at 14 years of age.

There are four common methods to determine leg length remaining: 1. Growth remaining method. 2. Arithmetic method. 3. Straight line graphic method. 4. Multiplier method.

The arithmetic method of growth estimation is based on the principle that boys typically reach skeletal maturity at 16 years of age and girls at 14 years of age. The arithmetic method assumes a certain contribution from each physis to longitudinal annual growth: proximal femur (4 mm/yr), distal femur (10 mm/yr), proximal tibia (6 mm/yr), and distal tibia (5 mm/yr). The arithmetic method is particularly helpful to predict leg length difference for a test question.

*Paley et al. present the multiplier method of leg length discrepancy determination.* **They report** that the multiplier method is comparable with or more accurate than the Moseley method of limb-length prediction.
