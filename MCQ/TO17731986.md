## Question

Serum ALP increases in( metabolic bone disorders-miscellaneous)

## Options

1. Renal osteodystrophy
2. Osteopetrosis
3. Multiple myeloma
4. Hypophosphatasia

## Correct option

**1. Renal osteodystrophy**

## Answer explanation

Serum ALP is elevated in rickets (all forms), osteomalacia, Paget’s disease, primary hyperparathyroidism and chronic renal failure (CRF) with secondary hyperparathyroidism, renal osteodystrophy and lytic bone neoplasms. The levels are normal in osteoporosis, osteopetrosis, fibrous dysplasia, multiple myeloma and hypoparathyroidism. Decreased levels may be seen in achondroplasia, hypophosphatasia and cretinism.

Answer: 1
