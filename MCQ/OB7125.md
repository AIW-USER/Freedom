## Question

A study to determine the relationship between the symptoms of carpal tunnel syndrome and occupational repetitive trauma is initiated and performed over 5 years following 500 workers at a local factory. Questionnaires identified 100 workers with symptoms of carpal tunnel syndrome at the outset of the study. At the 5-year conclusion, 100 previously asymptomatic workers reported the onset of carpal tunnel syndrome symptoms during the study period. What is the incidence of carpal tunnel syndrome symptoms in this 5-year study?

## Options

1. 10%
2. 20%
3. 25%
4. 40%
5. Insufficient information to calculate

## Correct option

**3. 25%**

## Answer explanation

Incidence is a measure of the risk of developing some new condition within a specified period of time. In this example, it is the number of new patients developing carpal tunnel symptoms (100 workers) divided by the number of at risk individuals (400 workers) for an incidence of 25%. In contrast, prevalence is a measure of the total number of cases of disease in a given population. At the conclusion of 5 years, the prevalence of carpal tunnel symptoms in this group of 500 workers is 40% or 200 workers.
