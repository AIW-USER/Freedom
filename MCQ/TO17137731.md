Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Intertrochanteric Fractures

## Question

Optimal position of lag screw in case of trochanteric fracture fixation is

## Options

1. central on the AP radiograph and central on the lateral view
2. central on the AP radiograph and posterior on the lateral view
3. inferior on the AP radiograph and posterior on the lateral view
4. low to central on the AP radiograph and posterior on the lateral view

## Correct option

**1. central on the AP radiograph and central on the lateral view**

## Answer explanation

Clinical studies on lag screw positioning have all indicated that the correct lag screw position is low to central on the AP radiograph and central on the lateral view. Essentially, peripheral placement of the lag screw should never be accepted. Some early surgeons had suggested a posterior position was acceptable but this was not found to be the case in clinical studies. A posterior screw may lead to a rotational force on the femoral head, such that the head rotates leaving the lag screw in a superior position in which cut-out occurs.

Answer: 1
