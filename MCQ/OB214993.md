## Answer explanation

Posterior antiglide plating for distal fibula fractures may cause peroneal tendon irritation.

Several fixation methods are available for distal fibula fractures. Fractures that are orientated from anteroinferior to posterosuperior are often treated with antiglide plating. This plating technique allows the plate to be used as a reduction tool and directly buttress against distal fracture fragment displacement. Posterolateral antiglide plating of unstable AO-type B lateral malleolar fractures has been shown to be biomechanically stronger than lateral plating. Due to the positioning of the plate on the posterior aspect of the distal fibula, the peroneal tendons are at risk for irritation and attritional rupture.

*Weber et al. retrospectively reviewed 70 patients to determine hardware position and identify peroneal tendon lesions with antiglide plating.* **They report** that 43% of patients had the plate removed because of discomfort or signs of peroneal tendinitis and peroneal tendon lesions were identified intraoperatively in 30% of patients. **They concluded** that placement of the distal end of the plate distal to the proximal third of the lateral malleolus with a screw in the most distal hole of the plate and a prominent screw head strongly correlates with peroneal tendon lesions.

*Schaffer et al. performed a biomechanical study comparing neutralization and antiglide plating for distal fibula fracture stabilization.* **They report** that antiglide plating has several advantages over lateral plating including dissection of a smaller area, minimum bending of the plate, and no potential for penetration of a screw into the joint.

Figure D illustrates a lag screw with neutralization plating. Figure E illustrate an intramedullary screw. Figure F are three consecutive coronal MRI images of the peroneal tendons

Incorrect Answers:
Answers 2-5: All of these are reasonable treatment options for distal fibula fractures, but none are associated with a higher rate of peroneal tendon irritation than posterior antiglide plating
