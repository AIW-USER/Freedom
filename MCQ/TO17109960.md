Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Outcome Measure Tools in Arthroplasty

## Question

WOMAC score is used for which joint

## Options

1. Hip
2. Knee
3. Ankle
4. Shoulder

## Correct option

**2. Knee**

## Answer explanation

Answer : b

WOMAC score (Western Ontario and McMaster Universities’ Arthritis Index) is an assessment tool used in evaluation of patients having osteoarthritis (OA) in hip and knee. It’s a self administered questionnaire having 24 items divided into three subscales:

- Pain (5 items)

- Stiffness (2 items)

- Physical function (17 items)

Since the scoring is applied in OA, and in India OA is more common in Knee than hip, knee has been chosen as answer.
