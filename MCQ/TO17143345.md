Chapter: Orthopedics Oncology MCQs — Glomus Tumor

## Question

Love test is diagnostic is

## Options

1. Trochanteric bursitis
2. Hydatid cyst of quadriceps
3. Glomus tumor
4. SLE

## Correct option

**3. Glomus tumor**

## Answer explanation

Explanation: Love test is diagnostic in glomus tumor. Pain, cold sensitivity, and point tenderness are the characteristic symptoms of a glomus tumor.

Love test: Direct pressure on the tumor by a small, firm object causes excruciating pain whereas pressure applied slightly to one side of it elicits no pain.

Answer : C
