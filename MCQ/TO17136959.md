## Question

Maisonneuve fracture is (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. External rotation pronation injury
2. External rotation abduction injury
3. Supination internal rotation injury
4. Supination adduction injury

## Correct option

**1. External rotation pronation injury**
