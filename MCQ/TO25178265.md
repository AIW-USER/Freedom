Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — Shoulder Instability[Unidirectional and Multidirectional]

## Question

What is the primary technical goal of the 'Remplissage' procedure?

## Options

1. To repair the anterior labrum more securely
2. To increase the diameter of the humeral head
3. To increase the glenoid track width
4. To turn an intra-articular Hill-Sachs lesion into an extra-articular one

## Correct option

**4. To turn an intra-articular Hill-Sachs lesion into an extra-articular one**

## Answer explanation

In the remplissage procedure infraspinatus is filled into the hill sachs lesion using suture anchors. By filling the defect with the infraspinatus, the lesion can no longer engage the glenoid rim during external rotation.

Answer : D
