## Question

A high school basketball player dies suddenly on the basketball court. An autopsy is most likely to find which of the following conditions to be the cause of death?

## Options

1. Hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy
2. Dilated cardiomyopathy
3. Mitral valve prolapse
4. Aortic root rupture
5. Ventricular dysplasia

## Correct option

**1. Hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy**

## Answer explanation

There is a differential diagnosis list for sudden cardiac death in the athlete. The Garrick chapter lists the most common cause in the US as hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (36%), followed by coronary anomalies (19%), and increased cardiac mass (10%). The other choices were much less common causes: Ruptured aorta accounted for 5%, dilated cardiomyopathy 3%, and MVP 2%.

The Basilico article reviews cardiovascular diseases that may limit an athlete's participation in sports and that may put an athlete at risk for sudden cardiac death. It also reviews the extent and limitations of the cardiovascular preparticipation screening examination.
