Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Total Shoulder Arthroplasty

## Question

Recommended glenohumeral mismatch to decreases the glenoid component loosening rate is

## Options

1. <2mm
2. Anatomically conformed glenohumeral components give best survival rates
3. <4 mm
4. >8mm

## Correct option

**3. <4 mm**

## Answer explanation

In shoulder arthroplasty greater prosthetic mismatches can lead to increased joint translation, accelerated polyethylene wear, and fracture. The upper limit of mismatch has not been conclusively determined but current opinion seems to suggest that a glenoid with a radius curvature of 2 to 4 mm larger than the humeral head allows normal translation during rotation without rim loading or risk of loosening.

Answer: 3
