## Question

Perthes lesion of shoulder is (arthroscopy- shoulder arthroscopy- instability)

## Options

1. Anteroinferior labral tear with intact periosteum
2. Posterosuperior labral tear
3. Associated with recurrent shoulder dislocation
4. Avulsion of inferior glenohumeral ligament

## Correct option

**3. Associated with recurrent shoulder dislocation**

## Answer explanation

Perthes lesion is variation of the Bankart lesion, in which the labrum is avulsed (not torn) from the glenoid but remains attached to the scapula by the intact periosteum. Periosteum albeit remains intact but is stripped medially. Labrum may remain in normal position so it is difficult to diagnose arthroscopically but its function as stabilizer may be lost producing recurrent dislocation.

Answer: 3
