## Question

Iontophoresis, as commonly used in physical therapy, is a modality best defined by which of the following descriptions?

## Options

1. Induction of muscle contraction facilitating metabolic byproduct removal
2. Transcutaneous delivery of medication with electrical current
3. Transmission of high frequency acoustic waves to soft tissues
4. A low level laser treatment
5. Utilization of pulsed magnetic fields for recalcitrant muscle pain

## Correct option

**2. Transcutaneous delivery of medication with electrical current**

## Answer explanation

Iontophoresis is the use of an electrical current to drive charged molecules of medicine through the skin to the deeper tissues (Illustration A). This technique has been used with a variety of medications including steroids, local anesthetics, salicylates, and non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs. When ultrasound, rather than electrical current, is used to deliver medicine it is called phonophoresis.

*The article by Chapman et al is a review of different physical therapy modalities.* These include transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation (answer 1), ultrasound (answer 3), laser treatments (answer 4), and magnet therapy (answer 5).
