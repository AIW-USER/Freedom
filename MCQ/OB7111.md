## Question

Two metal bone plates are loaded to failure in a bending test, producing the load-displacement curves shown in Figure 23. Based on these results, what can be said about plate B?

![[q7111-a-23.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Plate B is stronger.
2. Plate B is tougher.
3. Plate B is stiffer.
4. Plate B is more brittle.
5. Plate B has less fatigue strength.

## Correct option

**2. Plate B is tougher.**

## Answer explanation

Toughness refers to the amount of energy that a material can absorb before failing. On a load-displacement plot, the toughness is equal to the area under the stress-strain curve, which, in this case, is substantially greater for plate B. Strength refers to the magnitude of the load at the point where the plate breaks, which is greater for plate A. Brittleness is the opposite of toughness - a brittle plate breaks at a lower value of displacement (plate A). Fatigue strength refers to breaking after many load cycles, which was not evaluated in this test.
