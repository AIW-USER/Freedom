## Question

False statement regarding the Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA) diagnostic criteria [FNB 2023]

## Options

1. Score of more than 8 is required to label RA
2. DIP joints and First MTP joints are excluded from criteria
3. ESR, CRP, RA factor and Anti CCP are important part of criteria
4. Number and type of joint involvement is given the greatest weightage

## Correct option

**1. Score of more than 8 is required to label RA**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A
EXPLANATION: -
In 2010, the American College of Rheumatology and the European League against Rheumatism jointly agreed to the criteria depicted in table below as standard diagnostic criteria for Rheumatoid arthritis to establish a confirmatory diagnosis.
A score of 6 is enough to ascertain diagnosis not 8. Patients with a score of < 6 can be reassessed; they may meet the criteria for rheumatoid arthritis cumulatively over time.
Although in RA small joints of the hand and foot are involved, hand DIP joints, first carpometacarpal (basilar) joint and the first MTP joint of foot are excluded from the assessment.
ESR, CRP, RA factor and Anti CCP are important part of criteria whereby “Low positive” indicates levels between 1 and 3 times the upper limit of normal while “High positive” indicates levels at least 3 times the upper limit of normal.
