## Question

Identify the condition

![[TO17118915-1.jpg]]

## Options

1. Pellegrini stieda lesion
2. Segond fracture
3. Reverse segond fracture
4. Stieda process

## Correct option

**1. Pellegrini stieda lesion**

## Answer explanation

In chronic cases of MCL tear one may spot calcification at the femoral insertion of MCL, called as Pellegrini Stieda lesion

A segond fracture is associated with ACL tear,seen on plain x ray as an avulsion fracture of lateral proximal tibia, actually a bony avulsion of anterolateral ligament

Reverse Segond fracture is a cortical avulsion fracture off the medial tibial plateau of the knee, associated with tears of the posterior cruciate ligament (PCL) mainly, may also be seen in medial collateral ligament (MCL), medial meniscus, and probably the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL)

Stieda process (not to be confused with a Pellegrini-Stieda lesion) refers to an elongated lateral tubercle of the posterior process of the talus . It is often considered an anatomical variant
