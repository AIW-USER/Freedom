## Question

Figure 68 shows the view from a posterosuperior shoulder arthroscopic portal. The muscle associated with the tendinous structure shown is innervated by what nerve?

![[q7456-a-68.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Axillary
2. Median
3. Musculocutaneous
4. Radial
5. Ulnar

## Correct option

**3. Musculocutaneous**

## Answer explanation

The structure shown in the arthroscopic image is the tendon of the long head of the biceps, originating from the supraglenoid tubercle. The biceps brachii muscle is innervated by the musculocutaneous nerve. The long head of the biceps brachii is not innervated by the axillary, median, radial, or ulnar nerves.
