## Question

In gluteus maximus paralysis all are true except: (FNB Pattern Paper 2017)

## Options

1. Trunk lurches backwards
2. In early stance phase line of gravity shifts posterior to hip joint
3. Inferior gluteal nerve palsy may cause it
4. Trendelenberg gait Is seen

## Correct option

**4. Trendelenberg gait Is seen**

## Answer explanation

Functions of gluteus maximus-

- Chief extensor of the thigh
- Essential for maintaining an erect posture
- Lateral rotation of the thigh
- Abduction of the thigh

Gluteus maximus palsy: when the gluteus maximus is weak, at the heel strike, the trunk lurches backwards (extension of the trunk) to prevent the forward motion of the trunk on the weak side. This compensates for the weak hip extension. G. Maximus is supplied by inferior gluteal nerve (L5, S1,S2).

Answer: 4
