## Question

X ray pelvis with both hips AP view is given below. True about the X ray is

![[TO17099605-1.png]]

## Options

1. Coxa profunda
2. Protrusio acetabuli
3. Retroverted acetabulum
4. Coxa magna

## Correct option

**1. Coxa profunda**

## Answer explanation

The presence of coxa profunda is seen on the anteroposterior pelvic radiograph when the medial aspect of the acetabular fossa extends medial to the ilioischial line. Coxa profunda may be present in patients with acetabular over coverage, though it can also be a normal variant, particularly in women. Protrusio acetabuli exists when the medial aspect of the femoral head is projected crossing the ilioischial line and usually indicates excessive acetabular depth and possible acetabular over coverage with pincer impingement morphology.

Protrusio acetabuli: both head and medial aspect of acetabular fossa extends medial to ilioischial line.

![[TO17099605-2.png]]

Coxa profunda: when only medial aspect of acetabular fossa and not the head extends medial to ilioischial line

![[TO17099605-3.png]]

Answer: 1
