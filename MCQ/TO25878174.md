Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Unicondylar and Patellofemoral Arthroplasty

## Question

A surgeon is performing a mobile-bearing UKA. After seating the components, the bearing is found to be unstable during mid-flexion but tight in full extension. Which technical error most likely occurred?

## Options

1. Over-resection of the tibial plateau.
2. Inadequate posterior femoral resection.
3. Excessive tibial slope.
4. MCL over-release during exposure.

## Correct option

**2. Inadequate posterior femoral resection.**

## Answer explanation

MCL release is generally avoided in UKA. If the MCL were incompetent, the joint would be lax in both extension and flexion, likely leading to failure of the procedure.

In UKA, the flexion gap is primarily determined by the posterior femoral cut. If the posterior cut is insufficient, the flexion gap will be too tight; conversely, if the flexion gap is lax compared to extension, it suggests issues with the posterior condyle or component positioning relative to the ligamentous tension.

Answer: 2
