## Question

A 53-year-old female with left knee osteoarthritis presents to your clinic for evaluation. Her condition has been refractory to non-steroidal anti-inflammatory medications, exercise, and steroid injections. She is not yet ready for arthroplasty. She is wondering what you can tell her about "gel" injections. What recommendation does the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons (AAOS) Clinical Practice Guidelines (CPG) provide for hyaluronic acid?

## Options

1. Strong Recommendation For
2. Strong Recommendation Against
3. Limited Recommendation For
4. Moderate Recommendation Against
5. Consensus Recommendation For

## Correct option

**4. Moderate Recommendation Against**

## Answer explanation

Hyaluronic acid (HA) intra-articular injections are not recommended for routine use in treating symptomatic knee osteoarthritis.

The 2021 American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons (AAOS) Clinical Practice Guidelines (CPG) provide a moderate recommendation against HA injections for osteoarthritis of the knee. This was in contrast to the 2013 edition which provided a strong recommendation against. In formulating their guidelines, the authors included 29 studies (17 high strength and 11 moderate strength). Some studies demonstrated a statistical benefit with the use of HA but could not reach the significance for a minimally clinically meaningful difference. The number needed to treat to see a tangible benefit from HA was 17 patients.

*Carlson et al. performed a survey of members of the American Association of Hip and Knee Surgeons (AAHKS).* **They found** that compliance with AAOS guidelines was high for Kellgren-Lawrence stages 0, 1, and 4 (80%, 82%, and 98%, respectively), but low for stages 2 and 3 OA (21% and 50%, respectively). The most commonly selected intervention not recommended by the AAOS was HA injection.

*Meiyappan et al. retrospectively reviewed 1096 outpatient encounters with the primary diagnosis of knee OA.* **They found** that injection with steroids or HA was the most common intervention (32%). The most expensive treatment was HA injection. **They concluded** that adherence to AAOS CPGs was modest.

Incorrect answers:
Answers 1-3 and 5: The AAOS CPGs provide a Moderate Recommendation Against the use of intra-articular HA injections.
