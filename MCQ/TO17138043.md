## Question

Blood flow ratio Internal carotid artery: External carotid artery

## Options

1. 60:40
2. 70:30
3. 80:20
4. 90:10

## Correct option

**3. 80:20**

## Answer explanation

Ans: C

Most studies describe the blood flow velocities in ICA or CCA as a determinant of stenosis. In one study, I have found the blood flow ration between ICA/CCA as 0.8, meaning the flow between ICA: ECA would be 80:20.
