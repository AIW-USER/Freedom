## Question

A 70 years old female patient undergoes total knee revision replacement for loose femoral component. Patient is a known case of rheumatoid arthritis. Surgery goes uneventful and routine intraoperative cultures are submitted that show growth of coagulase negative staphylococci at 48 hours in 3 out of 5 samples. Patient is afebrile and her wound is dry. What is the next line of management?

## Options

1. IV antibiotics
2. Irrigation, debridement and change of tibial insert
3. Observation
4. Irrigation, debridement, removal of components and antibiotic cement spacer

## Correct option

**1. IV antibiotics**
