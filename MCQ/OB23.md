## Question

A 27-year-old patient comes in for a new prescription for his below knee amputation prosthesis because it is not fitting properly. All of the following are complaints and examination findings consistent with a prosthetic foot that is placed too far inset EXCEPT:

## Options

1. Varus strain
2. Socket-related pain located medial-proximal
3. Increased knee extension during stance
4. Socket-related pain located lateral-distal
5. Circumducted gait

## Correct option

**3. Increased knee extension during stance**

## Answer explanation

Increased knee extension during stance can be a result of a heel that is too soft or a foot that placed is too anterior. Posterior placement of the prosthetic foot or a heel that is too hard will increase knee flexion/instability at heel strike.

A prosthetic foot that is too far inset produces varus strain, pain (proximal/medial and distal/lateral) at the socket, and a circumducted gait.

A prosthetic foot that is too far outset will produce valgus strain, socket pain that is proximal/lateral and distal/medial, and a broad-based gait.
