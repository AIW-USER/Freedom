## Question

A 63-year-old female sustains a subtrochanteric femoral fracture after a fall in her home. Five years ago she underwent resection of a left thigh leiomyosarcoma with adjuvant radiotherapy. Which of the following is not a known risk factor for development of pathologic fracture post radiotherapy?

## Options

1. Female
2. Age
3. Dose of radiotherapy
4. Periosteal resection during sarcoma removal
5. Method of radiotherapy administration

## Correct option

**5. Method of radiotherapy administration**

## Answer explanation

The method of radiotherapy administration has not been shown to increase the risk of post-radiation fracture.

*The study by Holt et al* **found** a 26% incidence of post-radiation fracture in 374 patients following combined surgical resection and external beam irradiation of soft tissue sarcomas. **They also found** that fracture risk was increased in females, patients older than 50 years of age, and those with higher doses of irradiation (>60 Gy).

*The study by Lin et al of 205 patients found a 29% incidence of fracture at 5 years followup.* **They found** that periosteal resection was a risk factor for fracture occurrence. The approximate calculated risk of a femoral fracture for this patient is 25%.
