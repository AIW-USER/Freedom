Chapter: Spine MCQs — Neuromonitoring

## Question

The following are the steps to be taken when we lose neuromonitoring signals while doing spine surgery except

## Options

1. Check the neuromonitoring equipment and stimulate again.
2. Check for anaesthetic agents’ delivery
3. Decrease the blood pressure to reduce the blood loss
4. Reverse the manipulation and do wider decompression of the cord.

## Correct option

**3. Decrease the blood pressure to reduce the blood loss**

## Answer explanation

Answer: 3

Steps to be taken when we lose neuromonitoring signals:

- Check equipment:

*Electrode dislodgement from the scalp (SSEPs) or the muscle (MEPs) would have occurred during surgery - mechanical manipulation of the spine and positioning

*Check the wire circuit for discontinuity.

*Monitoring equipment - stimulation devices and recording boxes,

•Exposure to irrigation and blood.

•Electrical interference from digital equipment - warming blankets, fluoroscopy units, electrocautery equipment, large magnetic devices, and the operating microscope

2.Check for anaesthetic agents’ delivery- inadvertent usage of neuromuscular blockade agents or inhalation agents will interfere with neuromonitoring

3.Monitor blood pressure: Hypotension would lead to spinal cord ischemia and can lead to loss of signals in MEP & SSEP. Increase in mean arterial pressure will lead to recovery of lost signals in 50% of cases.

To ensure adequate cord perfusion

•Mean arterial pressure should be elevated to 80– 90 mm Hg

•Any anemia with hemoglobin below 8 mg/dL should be reversed,

•Patient oxygenation should be increased.

 •Any hypocapnic alkalosis and hypothermia should be reversed.

- Reverse manipulation and widen decompression:

Mechanical manipulation of the spine.

•It is important to address, or if possible, reverse, the offending step.

•compression or distraction across a spinal level -reverse that manipulation.

•It may take >/=10 min after reversing the mechanical manipulation for the signals to return.

•However, if the signals still do not return- do further decompression

•Three-column osteotomies- closing the osteotomy -decompresses the spinal cord

- If the signals have not returned- do a stagnara wake up test.
