## Question

In Mannerfelt syndrome most commonly involved tendon is

## Options

1. FPL
2. FDP
3. Extensor digital tendons from ulnar to radial
4. Extensor digital tendon from radial to ulnar

## Correct option

**1. FPL**

## Answer explanation

Mannerfelt syndrome is characterized by rupture of FPL (most common flexor rupture) in carpal tunnel due to scaphoid spur.

Also know:

Vaughan- Jackson syndrome: Vaughan-Jackson syndrome is characterized by the disruption of the digital extensor tendons, beginning on the ulnar side with the extensor digiti minimi and extensor digitorum communis tendon of the small finger. It is most commonly associated with rheumatoid arthritis.

Extensor digiti minimi (EDM) and extensor digitorum communis (EDC) tendon of the small finger are involved first. If the underlying pathology is not treated, sequential rupture of the EDC tendons of the ring, long, and index fingers occurs; ultimately, rupture of the extensor indicis proprius (EIP) may follow.

Answer: 1
