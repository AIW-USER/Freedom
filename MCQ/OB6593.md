## Question

A patient sustained a sharp laceration to the base of his left, nondominant thumb 4 months ago. Examination reveals no active flexion but full passive motion of the interphalangeal joint. What is the best treatment option?

## Options

1. Interphalangeal joint fusion
2. Intercalary tendon graft
3. Silicone rod placement
4. Primary flexor pollicis longus repair
5. Flexor digitorum superficialis transfer

## Correct option

**5. Flexor digitorum superficialis transfer**

## Answer explanation

The patient has a chronic flexor tendon laceration. There are options to restore motion and strength; therefore, fusion is not necessary. Full range of motion is present so the soft tissues are suitable for a tendon transfer. A transfer of the flexor digitorum superficialis of the ring finger to the insertion of the flexor pollicis longus on the distal phalanx provides good results with a one-stage operation.
