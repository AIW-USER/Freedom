## Question

Petro clival meningioma, True is

## Options

1. Arterial feeders are from Internal carotid artery
2. Embolization by small particles cause more complications
3. Liquid embolization is preferred than small particles

## Correct option

**1. Arterial feeders are from Internal carotid artery**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A

The arterial feeders of Petro clival arise from the dural branches of ICA (the cavernous part, the meningohypophyseal trunk).

The embolization using polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) particles provide permanent occlusion by adherence to the vessel wall, causing stagnation of flow. They result in an inflammatory reaction and focal angionecrosis. The major disadvantage is their tendency to aggregate, occluding vessels. more proximally. These agents harbour immediate risks similar to the liquid agents. (NOT more complications)

Liquid embolic agents are N-butyl 2-cyanoacrylate (NBCA) glue and ethylene vinyl alcohol copolymer (EVOH) branded as Onyx. They form a vessel cast and incite inflammatory reaction thereby occluding the vessels. Disadvantages of NBCA is that it requires expertise and that of Onyx is that it requires compatible catheters and syringes. Onyx is supplied with DMSO (dimethyl sulfoxide) as a solvent. These agents have limited penetration into the tumor vasculature, hence there are infrequently used as the primary agent for tumor embolization.
