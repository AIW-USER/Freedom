## Question

True about popliteus muscle

## Options

1. Bipartite muscle with main proximal attachment to lateral femoral condyle
2. Tripartite muscle with main proximal attachment to medial femoral condyle
3. Tripartite muscle with main proximal attachment to lateral femoral condyle
4. Bipartite muscle with main proximal attachment to medial femoral condyle.

## Correct option

**3. Tripartite muscle with main proximal attachment to lateral femoral condyle**

## Answer explanation

Popliteus muscle:

Popliteus muscle
Proximal attachments (tripartite tendon)
Distal attachment
Action

1. Lateral femoral condyle

2. Head of fibula

3. Posterior aspect of lateral meniscus

Posteromedial aspect of upper tibia
External rotation of femur (foot plantigrade)

Internal rotation of tibia( foot free)

“Unlocking of the knee”

Answer: 3
