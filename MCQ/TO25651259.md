Chapter: General Orthopedics MCQs — Bone Graft and Graft Substitutes

## Question

A surgeon is reconstructing a 6-cm atrophic tibial nonunion and wishes to optimize the biological milieu according to Giannoudis’ "Diamond Concept" of fracture healing. The surgeon utilizes rigid locking-plate fixation and fills the defect with a construct consisting of a biphasic calcium phosphate (HA/beta-TCP) scaffold, recombinant human Bone Morphogenetic Protein-2 (rhBMP-2), and leukocyte-rich Platelet-Rich Plasma (LR-PRP).

Based on the distinct properties of these chosen components, which fundamental biological pillar of the Diamond Concept is profoundly deficient in this construct, and why?

## Options

1. Osteoconduction; the biphasic HA/beta-TCP scaffold undergoes overly rapid, passive dissolution in vivo, failing to provide a durable trellis for creeping substitution across a critical-sized defect.
2. Osteoinduction; while LR-PRP provides high concentrations of TGF-beta and PDGF, the overall construct lacks the specific capability to induce the differentiation of perivascular mesenchymal stem cells into osteoblasts.
3. Osteogenesis; neither the biphasic ceramic, rhBMP-2, nor the LR-PRP contains a viable population of multipotent mesenchymal stem cells or committed osteoprogenitor cells.
4. Mechanical Stability; rhBMP-2 profoundly stimulates osteoclastic activity prior to osteoblastic bone formation, which will prematurely degrade the biphasic ceramic scaffold before rigid fixation can be achieved.

## Correct option

**3. Osteogenesis; neither the biphasic ceramic, rhBMP-2, nor the LR-PRP contains a viable population of multipotent mesenchymal stem cells or committed osteoprogenitor cells.**

## Answer explanation

The Diamond Concept: Successful bone regeneration relies on four intersecting elements: an osteoconductive scaffold, osteoinductive mediators, osteogenic cells, and mechanical stability.

Component Breakdown:

 - Rigid fixation provides the mechanical stability.

 - HA/beta-TCP is a purely synthetic ceramic that provides a highly effective osteoconductive scaffold
 (a physical trellis for new bone). It does not dissolve instantly; it provides a reliable, lasting framework, making A incorrect.

 - rhBMP-2 is one of the most potent osteoinductive
 agents available. It binds to serine/threonine kinase receptors and acts via the intracellular SMAD pathway to force undifferentiated mesenchymal cells down the osteoblastic lineage. Therefore, osteoinduction is well-supplied, making B incorrect. (Note: PRP does not provide true osteoinduction, only mitogenic and angiogenic growth factors).

 - The Missing Link: Osteogenesis
 requires living, viable cells capable of forming bone (osteoblasts or mesenchymal stem cells). Ceramics are synthetic and acellular. rhBMP-2 is a synthesized protein. PRP is derived from centrifuged peripheral blood and contains concentrated platelets and growth factors, but it does not
 contain mesenchymal stem cells. To supply osteogenesis, the surgeon would need to add autograft or Bone Marrow Aspirate Concentrate (BMAC), which is rich in mesenchymal and hematopoietic stem cells.

Answer: 3
