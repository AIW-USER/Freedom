## Question

A 12-year-old patient presented to your clinic after developing knee pain two weeks ago when she fell off of the school bleachers. The pain has not improved in this time, however, she is able to ambulate and attend school. X-rays obtained in your office are shown in Figure A. After proper workup to include a biopsy, the patient is diagnosed with osteosarcoma. Which of the following factors suggests a poor prognosis?

![[q215417-a-ap-knee2.jpg]]
*Figure A*

## Options

1. Tumor size of 4.5 cm in largest diameter
2. Serum lactate dehydrogenase within normal limits
3. Distal femoral location for primary tumor site
4. Tumor necrosis of 60% after neoadjuvant chemotherapy
5. No evidence of metastatic disease at diagnosis

## Correct option

**4. Tumor necrosis of 60% after neoadjuvant chemotherapy**

## Answer explanation

Tumor necrosis of greater than 90% or greater is considered a good response and correlates with a better prognosis.

*Friebele et al performed a meta-analysis and literature review of osteosarcoma.* **They found** overall survival of osteosarcoma patients was 63% when including both non-metastatic and metastatic patients. Based upon their literature review, tumor necrosis of 90% or greater resulted in overall survival approaching 75%.

*Marina et al published a review on osteosarcoma which includes clinical features, epidemiology, radiographic workup, histopathology, and treatment.* *They discuss the importance of neoadjuvant chemotherapy on overall survival with one cited study showing 66% relapse-free survival compared to 11% without neoadjuvant chemotherapy at 6-years with both groups including surgical resection.* Poor prognostic factors included clinically detectable metastatic disease, axial skeleton location of the primary tumor, elevated LDH and alkaline phosphatase levels.

*Chen et al performed a meta-analysis evaluating the prognostic significance of LDH serum levels on osteosarcoma.* **They concluded** that a high LDH level was a poor prognostic indicator for osteosarcoma patients. Studies with levels between 213-440IU/L as "high" cut-off were included in this study, however, a specific cut-off was not determined based upon the meta-analysis.

Figure A demonstrates an AP x-ray image of the distal femur with a metaphyseal "moth-eaten" appearing mass involving the lateral cortex with cortical disruption, periosteal reaction (Codman's triangle proximally), and soft-tissue invasion with ossification/calcification of the tumor matrix. Illustration A is a diagram of common locations for different masses that often involve the distal femur.

Incorrect answers:
Answer 1: Tumor size less than 5cm in greatest diameter portends a better prognosis than larger tumors
Answer 2: Elevated lactate dehydrogenase is a poor prognostic indicator, not normal levels of serum LDH
Answer 3: Primary tumor location outside of the axial skeleton do better than tumors of the spine or pelvis
Answer 5: Metastatic disease is a poor prognostic indicator, and patients without evidence of it at initial diagnosis and workup have better outcomes with current treatment modalities.
