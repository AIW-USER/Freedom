Chapter: Pediatric Orthopedics MCQs — Sprengel's Deformity

## Question

A 6 years old girl with Sprengel Deformity of shoulder has presented to you. Her shoulder abduction is restricted to only 100 degrees apart from the cosmetic issue and hence she has been planned for surgery. Not true regarding the surgical intervention in this case is

## Options

1. Surgery should preferably be deferred for 2 more years as after the age of 8 years the chances of an iatrogenic brachial plexus palsy are rare
2. Surgery would involve detachment and reattachment of medial parascapular muscles at spinous process origin to allow scapula to move inferiorly and rotate into more shoulder abduction
3. Clavicle should also be morselized in her case
4. Despite surgery, abduction can only be benefitted to around 40 degrees

## Correct option

**1. Surgery should preferably be deferred for 2 more years as after the age of 8 years the chances of an iatrogenic brachial plexus palsy are rare**

## Answer explanation

Ans: A
