## Question

Which of the following diseases is characterized by an Erlenmeyer flask deformity of the femoral metaphysis?

## Options

1. Sprengel deformity
2. Melorheostosis
3. Osteopetrosis
4. Dysplasia epiphysealis hemimelica

## Correct option

**3. Osteopetrosis**

## Answer explanation

Erlenmeyer flask deformity (EFD), also known as metaphyseal flaring, refers to a radiographic appearance typically on a femoral radiograph demonstrating relatively reduced constriction of the diaphysis and flaring of the metaphysis as a result of undertubulation.

The name refers to the resemblance to a flat bottomed titration flask used by chemists, (conical flask)
