## Question

After ACL reconstruction, which exercise is not recommended in the early rehabilitation period? (arthroscopy- knee arthroscopy- ACL)

## Options

1. Low degree squat
2. Stationary bicycle
3. Seated leg raising
4. Leg press

## Correct option

**3. Seated leg raising**

## Answer explanation

In the early rehabilitation period after ACL reconstruction only close kinetic chain exercises (CKCE) are recommended. In CKC exercises, the foot remains in contact with the ground or footstep of a machine. CKC exercises can safely be instituted in early rehab period as they are safer for the patellofemoral joint, replicate functional tasks, cause coactivation of hamstrings and do not contribute to increased risk of anterior tibial displacement. CKC exercises are mainly used to train the quadriceps muscle group to improve muscle strength, coordination, and proprioception, while putting the least amount of tensile strain on the ACL. Squat, stationary bicycle, leg press all are closed kinetic chain exercises. Open chain extension exercises, such as seated leg extensions, are generally not allowed in the first 6 weeks of rehabilitation after reconstruction of the ACL because they put increased stress on the graft.

Answer : C
