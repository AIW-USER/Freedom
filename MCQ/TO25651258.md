Chapter: General Orthopedics MCQs — 3D Printing in Orthopaedics and Patient Specific Implants

## Question

A primary advantage of utilizing Direct Metal Laser Sintering (DMLS) or Selective Laser Melting (SLM) to produce custom oncologic reconstruction implants rather than using traditional subtractive manufacturing (CNC milling) of solid titanium is:

## Options

1. The ability to eliminate the risk of fatigue failure under cyclic physiological loading entirely.
2. The capacity to deliberately engineer a gradient porosity that reduces the effective Young's modulus of the implant, thereby mitigating stress shielding.
3. The complete elimination of anisotropic mechanical properties inherent to all forged metallic alloys.
4. The automatic optimization of the hydrophobic properties of the implant surface to prevent bacterial biofilm formation.

## Correct option

**2. The capacity to deliberately engineer a gradient porosity that reduces the effective Young's modulus of the implant, thereby mitigating stress shielding.**

## Answer explanation

Stress Shielding: Solid titanium possesses a Young’s modulus of elasticity (around 110 GPa) that is significantly higher than that of human cortical bone (10–20 GPa) or cancellous bone (less than 3 GPa). This stiffness mismatch causes the implant to carry the bulk of the physiological load, shielding the adjacent bone from mechanical stress and leading to bone resorption and aseptic loosening.

Additive Solution: Powder bed fusion technologies like DMLS and SLM allow engineers to design implants with a solid structural core transitioning into a highly porous outer lattice. By altering the density, pore size, and strut thickness, the effective modulus of elasticity of the titanium component can be dropped significantly to match regional bone properties, encouraging functional remodeling.

Why others are incorrect: Additive manufacturing does not completely eliminate fatigue risk (A); surface roughness and internal micro-voids can actually act as stress concentrations if post-processing (such as Hot Isostatic Pressing or chemical polishing) is inadequate. Layer-by-layer printing introduces its own microstructural anisotropy along the build axis (C). Unmodified printed titanium surfaces do not inherently possess anti-biofilm properties (D) without specific secondary surface modifications or coatings.

Answer: 2
