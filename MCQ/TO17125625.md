## Question

In which two muscles the surgical plan is made in the posterolateral approach to tibial plateau?[FNB 2014]

## Options

1. Popliteus and peroneus group
2. Gastrocnemius ,soleus group and peroneus group
3. ITB and peroneals
4. FHL and soleus

## Correct option

**2. Gastrocnemius ,soleus group and peroneus group**

## Answer explanation

In the posterolateral approach to tibial plateau surgical plan is made between the gastrocnemius and soleus (posterior group) and peroneal muscles (lateral group).

Answer: 2
