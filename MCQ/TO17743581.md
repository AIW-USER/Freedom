## Question

Terrible triad of shoulder include all except (trauma- fractures/dislocations around shoulder- terrible triad of shoulder)

## Options

1. Brachial plexus injury
2. Rotator cuff (RC) tear
3. Proximal humeral fracture
4. Anterior shoulder dislocation

## Correct option

**3. Proximal humeral fracture**

## Answer explanation

The shoulder terrible triad (STT) is a traumatic anterior shoulder dislocation, associated with rotator cuff (RC) tear and nerve injury from the brachial plexus. Mostly there is a complete tear of rotator cuff along with axillary nerve injury and anterior shoulder dislocation.
