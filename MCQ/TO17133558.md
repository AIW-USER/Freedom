## Question

The typical anterior approach to the cervical spine uses the plane between which of the following structures?

## Options

1. The carotid artery (laterally) and jugular vein (medially)
2. The carotid sheath (laterally) and the trachea and esophagus (medially)
3. The inferior thyroid artery (medially) and the carotid sheath (laterally)
4. The brachial plexus (laterally) and the carotid sheath (medially)

## Correct option

**2. The carotid sheath (laterally) and the trachea and esophagus (medially)**

## Answer explanation

There is no true internervous plane during the anterior approach to the cervical spine.

The surgical plane lies between carotid sheath (laterally) and the trachea and esophagus (medially).

Answer: 2
