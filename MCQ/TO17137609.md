Chapter: Trauma MCQs — Hip Dislocation

## Question

A 18-year-old boy suffers a hip dislocation in a motor vehicle accident. It is reduced by closed means and he is immobilized in abduction for a month. Radiographs at that time demonstrate a normal appearance of the hip. You recommend:

## Options

1. Weight baring as tolerated, exercise and jogging by 8 weeks, running by 3 months
2. Traction for 2 weeks then weight bearing walk and exercises, running by 6 months
3. Hip abduction orthosis for 2 weeks, nonweight bearing walk for 2 weeks and exercises, running by 6 months
4. Weight bearing walk, exercises, and return to running/sports after 2months.

## Correct option

**1. Weight baring as tolerated, exercise and jogging by 8 weeks, running by 3 months**

## Answer explanation

Athletes recovering from hip dislocations must follow a strict physical therapy regimen to ensure complete recovery of function. Weight bearing walk as tolerated can be started after reduction with walker support. Stretching and range-of-motion exercises are important early in the recovery process, advancing to walking on crutches when the patient's pain fully resolves. Strengthening exercises of the muscles around the hip are important during the rehabilitation to take stress off the injured joint. The athlete should advance his or her rehabilitation regimen over time as tolerated, with light jogging by 6-8 weeks post injury, and regain full function in high-performance athletes by 3-4 months post injury.

Answer: 1
