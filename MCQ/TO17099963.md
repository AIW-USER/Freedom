Chapter: Arthroplasty MCQs — Implants And Prosthesis Design (Hip)

## Question

Not true about MOM hip bearings

## Options

1. High carbon content decreases wear rate
2. ALVAL is associated with MOM bearings
3. Large diametral clearance is associated with better fluid film lubrication.
4. optimal diametral clearance is in range of 200-500 mm

## Correct option

**3. Large diametral clearance is associated with better fluid film lubrication.**

## Answer explanation

Both C and D are incorrect options. Diametral clearance has to be ideal (100-200) for best lubrication film. Both large and inadequate clearances are problems. So, choice (C) is incorrect. However, since ideal clearance for a particular case actually depends on head size used, choice (D) is not as incorrect.

Characteristics of metal-on-metal bearings

- Carbon content: High-carbon (>0.20% carbon) cobalt-chromium alloy has been demonstrated to have lower wear rates than low carbon alloys.
- Diametral clearance: It refers to the gap between the two implants at the equator of the articulation and may be the most important variable affecting wear of the couple. Smaller clearances tend to produce fluid film lubrication and reduced wear. Inadequate clearance leads to equatorial contact between the implants, and high loosening rate. Too large a clearance leads to reduced surface contact, loss of effective lubrication, and more rapid wear. Current implants promote primarily polar contact with a clearance in the range of 100 to 200 mm, depending on the diameter of the head.

Metal reactions: Aseptic lymphocytic vasculitis associated lesion (ALVAL) is a delayed-type hypersensitivity reaction seen in approximately 0.3% of patients with metal-on-metal hip arthroplasties. Patients present with pain localized around the hip with or without evidence of loosening. There may be periarticular fluid accumulation, solid mass formation (or so-called pseudotumor), and, rarely, extensive tissue necrosis, including the hip abductors.

Answer: 3
