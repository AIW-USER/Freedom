## Question

Figure A and Figure B show two distinct hyperostotic conditions of the spine. Which of the following statements is true?

## Options

1. Patients with the condition shown in FIGURE A have higher rates of neurologic injury with trauma than patients with the condition in FIGURE B
2. Patients with the condition shown in FIGURE A have a lower rate of surgical treatment with trauma than patients with the condition in FIGURE B
3. Patients with the condition shown in FIGURE A have a higher mortality rate with trauma than patients with the condition in FIGURE B
4. Patients with the condition shown in FIGURE A have a higher association with HLA-B8 than patients with the condition in FIGURE B
5. Patients with the condition shown in FIGURE A have an increased rate isolated disease on the right side of the thoracic spine compared to patients with the condition in FIGURE B

## Correct option

**1. Patients with the condition shown in FIGURE A have higher rates of neurologic injury with trauma than patients with the condition in FIGURE B**

## Answer explanation

Figures A and B show patients with ankylosing spondylitis (AS) and diffuse idiopathic skeletal hyperostosis (DISH), respectively. Patients with AS have a higher rate of neurologic injury with spine trauma than patients with DISH.

All patients with hyperostotic conditions of the spine are at a high risk of neurologic injury following even minor trauma, which often leads to unstable three-column injuries. Aggressive surgical stabilization can help minimize risk of delayed neurological deficit, but rates of complications are quite high. Patients with AS are also at high risk of developing epidural hematoma after spine trauma compared to those with normal spines due to tethering of epidural vessels.

*Whang et al. performed a retrospective comparison of 12 patients with AS and 18 patients with DISH.* **The authors found** that although the rate of neurologic injury was high for both groups, AS patients were more likely to exhibit neurologic deficits and undergo operative management. However, **they determined** that there were no statistically significant differences between the mortality and outcomes in the AS and DISH groups.

*Westerveld et al. reviewed the literature regarding spinal fractures in ankylosing spinal disorders.* *On review of data from 90 articles,* **the authors found** higher rates of neurologic injury and complications in AS patients as compared to DISH patients. Mortality rates were otherwise similar between the two groups.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 2: Patients with AS have a HIGHER rate of surgical treatment than patients with DISH.
Answer 3: Patients with AS have an EQUIVALENT mortality rate than patient with DISH.
Answer 4: Patients with AS have a higher association with HLA-B27 while patients with DISH have a higher association with HLA-B8.
Answer 5: Patients with DISH have an increased rate isolated disease on the right side of the thoracic spine compared to patients with AS.
