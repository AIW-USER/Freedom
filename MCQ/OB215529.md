## Question

A 9-month-old male is brought to the emergency department by his babysitter with a left leg injury. According to the babysitter, he injured his leg during an unwitnessed fall. You have a high-index of suspicion for child abuse. Which of the following findings, if present, is the most common manifestation of child abuse?

## Options

1. Multiple bruises in various stages of healing
2. Posterior rib fractures
3. Metaphyseal corner fracture
4. Bucket handle fracture
5. Spiral femur fracture

## Correct option

**1. Multiple bruises in various stages of healing**

## Answer explanation

Unfortunately, there are more than one million victims of child abuse each year in the United States. Soft-tissue injuries, such as bruises in various stages of healing, are the most common manifestation of child abuse.

Orthopedic surgeons are very likely to evaluate and treat these patients, as fractures are the second most common lesion present in child abuse victims. Fractures involving the diaphysis are four times more common than metaphyseal fractures, and the humerus, tibia, and femur are the most commonly injured long bones. Physicians are mandated reporters of child abuse and may be charged with a crime for failure to report.

*McMahon et al. reviewed 371 patients who were suspected victims of abuse.* **They found** soft tissue injuries were present in 92% of these patients and ecchymosis was the most common lesion present. **They also found** that fractures were only present in 9% of abuse patients. **They conclude** the presence of soft tissue injury, especially bruises in various stages of healing, warrant a child abuse workup.

*Sugar et al. performed a cross-sectional survey evaluating the normal frequency and pattern of bruising observed in children.* **They found** that normal bruising occurred in approximately 20% of children who could walk and most commonly involved the anterior knee secondary to falls. **They concluded** bruising present in non-ambulatory children, or infants <9 months old, is highly suspicious for abuse.

Incorrect answers:
Answers 2-5 are fracture patterns that are highly suggestive of pediatric abuse. However, bruising and other soft tissue injuries are more common than any fracture.
