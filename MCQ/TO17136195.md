## Question

Indian Sprinter Dhanalakshmi Sekar, who burst onto the Indian athletics scene last year after beating top Indian sprinters Hima Das and Dutee Chand in 100m and 200m races was banned for failing a dope test.

Her sample collected contained a performance-enhancing steroid which falls under prohibited substances, as per the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA)

What was the substance ?

## Options

1. Stanozolol
2. Nandrolone
3. Metandienone
4. Androstenedione

## Correct option

**3. Metandienone**

## Answer explanation

She consumed metandienone which comes under S1 ANABOLIC AGENTS. These are prohibited all the times. All prohibited substances in this class are non-Specified Substances.

Anabolic agents are prohibited.

Answer: 3
