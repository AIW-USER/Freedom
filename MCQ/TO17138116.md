Chapter: Nerve Injury MCQs — Miscellaneous Compression Neuropathies

## Question

Wartenberg syndrome is not characterized by (FNB Pattern Paper 2015)

## Options

1. Compression of motor branch of radial nerve
2. Compression of sensory branch of radial nerve
3. Compression neuropathy between brachioradialis and ECRL tendon
4. Symptoms increases on wrist flexion, ulnar deviation and pronation

## Correct option

**1. Compression of motor branch of radial nerve**

## Answer explanation

Wartenberg syndrome or cheiralgia paresthetica is a compressive neuropathy of the superficial sensory radial nerve (SRN). There are no motor symptoms. SRN is vulnerable to get compressed between brachioradialis and ECRL tendons specially during forearm pronation.

Patient complains of pain, paresthesia and numbness over the dorsoradial hand. Pain increases in wrist flexion, ulnar deviation and pronation. Finkelstein test increases symptoms in most of the patients due to traction on the nerve.

Treatment: most of the patients improve with rest, activity modification and NSAIDS. Surgical decompression is required in a few cases.

Answer: 1
