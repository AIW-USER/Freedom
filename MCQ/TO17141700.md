## Question

Most common finger involvement in syndactyly is

## Options

1. Ring-small
2. Long- ring
3. Thumb-index
4. Index-long

## Correct option

**2. Long- ring**

## Answer explanation

Ray involvement in syndactyly–

- 50% long-ring finger
- 30% ring-small finger
- 15% index-long finger
- 5% thumb-index finger

Answer : b
