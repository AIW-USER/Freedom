## Question

A healthy 70-year-old male is scheduled to undergo right total knee arthroplasty (TKA) and presents for his preoperative appointment. The patient inquires about the differences between postoperative physical therapy (PT) protocols, specifically, self-directed home PT and formal outpatient PT (OPPT). What is the most appropriate response?

## Options

1. Self-directed home PT after TKA is associated with increased cost
2. Self-directed home PT after TKA is associated with decreased knee range of motion
3. Self-directed home PT after TKA provides similar clinical outcomes to formal outpatient PT
4. 50% of patients undergoing self-directed PT will eventually require formal outpatient PT
5. There are substantial cost savings associated with formal outpatient PT

## Correct option

**3. Self-directed home PT after TKA provides similar clinical outcomes to formal outpatient PT**

## Answer explanation

Current literature indicates that self-directed home PT yields similar clinical outcomes and significant cost savings when compared to formal outpatient PT in properly selected patients (Answer 3).

After total joint arthroplasty, the surgeon must ensure that patients pursuing self-directed home PT meet range of motion, gait, and strength milestones. The treating surgeon should also understand that up to one-third of patients in self-directed therapy programs may require formal outpatient services. It is imperative that clinicians select the appropriate patients for self-directed PT, as these patients should be highly motivated, have access to basic equipment, and be able to complete home exercises without any additional risk.

*Wang et al. completed a comparative analysis of patients undergoing a home exercise program (HEP) and patients attending OPPT after TKA.* **Their results suggest** that 65.8% percent of patients in the HEP group avoided needing OPPT, with comparable outcomes in range of motion and manipulation under anesthesia rates between HEP and OPPT groups. Additionally, patients receiving OPPT incurred average cost increases of $1,340.87 and $1,893.42 for Medicare and private insurers, respectively.

*Klement et al. reviewed 296 patients who underwent TKA and enrolled in a web-based self-directed physical therapy program.* **Their results indicate** that 65.9% of patients did not require OPPT. Subsequent multivariate analysis indicated that a higher body mass index, higher preoperative SF-12 mental score, and loss of flexion at 2 weeks were independent predictors of the need for OPPT.

*Zachwieja et al. completed a retrospective review of 701 patients to determine the utilization trends of Web-based physical therapy (WBPT) versus OPPT after TKA.* *In their review, 49.8% of patients utilized WBPT, 34.7% utilized a combination of WBPT and OPPT, and 23% utilized neither source of therapy.* **They found** no differences in postoperative outcomes based on the number of WBPT logins.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1&5: Home physical therapy is less expensive in comparison to OPPT
Answer 2: In properly selected patients, self-directed home PT is associated with comparable outcomes in regard to range of motion
Answer 4: Approximately one-third of patients participating in self-directed therapy will require formal outpatient physical therapy
