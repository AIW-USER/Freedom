## Question

What type of collagen is initially produced during the inflammatory phase of tendon and ligament healing?

## Options

1. I
2. II
3. III
4. IV
5. V

## Correct option

**3. III**

## Answer explanation

Tendons and ligaments are primarily composed of type I collagen. However, following tendon and ligament injury, type III collagen production predominates during the inflammatory and early regenerative phases of healing. During the later regenerative phase, type I collagen production is initiated and predominates.
