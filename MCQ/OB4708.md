## Question

A 36-year-old construction worker sustained an ankle fracture 4 years ago after falling off a roof. Postoperative radiographs are seen in Figure A. The hardware is removed 2 years later. He now returns with ankle pain and intermittent swelling but has no difficulty with uneven surfaces. Examination reveals 5 degrees of gastrocnemius equinus contracture, pain with passive plantar and dorsiflexion, but no pain with hindfoot inversion and eversion. Recent radiographs are seen in Figure B. CT scan shows no degenerative changes in the hindfoot. What is the best treatment option?

## Options

1. Arthroscopic debridement of the tibiotalar joint and corticosteroid injection
2. Tibiotalar arthrodesis with screws
3. Total ankle arthroplasty
4. Tibiotalocalcaneal arthrodesis with an intramedullary device
5. Tibiotalocalcaneal arthrodesis with an extramedullary device

## Correct option

**2. Tibiotalar arthrodesis with screws**

## Answer explanation

This patient has post-traumatic ankle arthritis, and anterior bony impingement. The subtalar joint is spared. Tibiotalar arthrodesis is preferred to ankle arthroplasty because of his age and occupation.

Ankle arthrodesis is most commonly performed for post-traumatic ankle arthritis. Other indications include AVN, primary OA, infection, chronic instability and inflammatory arthropathy. The optimal position for fusion is neutral dorsiflexion, 5-10° of external rotation and 5° of hindfoot valgus. Common techniques for open arthrodesis include screws alone, plate and screws, and external fixation.

*Hendrickx et al. examined the medium-long term results following ankle arthrodesis.* Ninety-one percent of patients achieved fusion, and 91% of patients were satisfied with their result at 9 years. However, there was significant radiographic progression of OA in the subtalar, talonavicular and calcaneocuboid joints.

*Bai et al. compared total ankle arthroplasty for primary ankle osteoarthritis and posttraumatic ankle arthritis.* **They found** that while clinical and radiographic characteristics were comparable, there was a higher incidence of complications in the post-traumatic arthritis group.

Figure A shows open reduction and internal fixation of a trimalleolar ankle fracture. Figure B shows late post-traumatic arthritis. Ilustration A shows an example of ankle fusion using screws.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Ankle arthroscopy may be performed as an adjunct to fusion. However, arthroscopy alone will not have any impact on end-stage arthritis.
Answer 3: Total ankle arthroplasty is acceptable for elderly, less active patients. In a young construction worker, ankle fusion is preferred.
Answers 4 and 5: Subtalar involvement is not present and there is no indication to fuse the subtalar joint.
