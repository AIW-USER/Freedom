## Question

Which of the following is the best predictor of mortality after a patient has sustained a pelvic ring injury?

## Options

1. Gender
2. Comorbidities
3. Fracture pattern
4. Use of angiography
5. Shock on presentation

## Correct option

**5. Shock on presentation**

## Answer explanation

*Starr and associates* **demonstrated** that age and shock on presentation were predictors of mortality after pelvic ring injury. *Smith and associates* **showed** that the amount of blood transfusions in the first 24 hours was also predictive of mortality. Gender, fracture pattern, use of angiography, and comorbidities do not correlate directly with mortality.
