## Question

Treatment of Giant Cell Tumor of Bone can include a monoclonal antibody directed at what?

## Options

1. Receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-B
2. Tumor necrosis factor-alpha
3. CD-20
4. human epidermal growth factor receptor 2
5. Receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-B ligand

## Correct option

**5. Receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-B ligand**

## Answer explanation

Denosumab is a monoclonal antibody against receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-Β ligand used in the treatment of Giant Cell Tumor of Bone (GCT).

GCTs account for 5% of all bone tumors and typically present in the third decade of life. Typically they occur around the knee (distal femur and proximal tibia), but also are commonly seen in the distal radius and the sacrum. The RANKL molecular pathway is implicated in the pathogenesis of GCT, where osteoclast-like giant cells express high levels of RANK and drive osteolysis. Denosumab is a monoclonal antibody designed to bind RANKL, therefore preventing the binding to RANK and activation of these osteoclast-like giant cells.

*Singh et al. review the current treatment options for GCTs and the role of denosumab in treatment.* The mainstay of treatment has been excision, but can be limited in areas such as the pelvis and spine. Denosumab treatment in GCT patients can reduce the stromal cell and giant cell density, decrease the chance of progression, and decrease tumor metabolic activity on FDG PET scan.

*Savvidou et al. review the current uses of denosumab in primary bone tumors.* *They breakdown its effect on the RANKL-RANK-OPG pathway, where it binds RANKL and inhibits osteoclast activation.* In GCTs denosumab can decrease tumor size and help provide a peripheral margin for excision. Further discussion in the paper is over the possible use of denosumab in the treatments of multiple myeloma, aneurysmal bone cysts, and osteosarcoma.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: RANKL is the target of denosumab, not the receptor itself.
Answer 2: TNF-alpha monoclonal antibodies include adalimumab and infliximab and are often used in the treatment of rheumatologic diseases.
Answer 3: CD-20 is found on B cells and is the target of rituximab, which is used in the treatment of B-Cell mediated diseases included non-hodgkins lymphoma and CLL.
Answer 4: HER2/neu is the target of trastuzumab and is used in the treatment of breast cancer.
