## Question

ALVAL lesion is seen with (FNB Pattern Paper 2018)

## Options

1. Metal on metal THR
2. Ceramic on ceramic
3. Metal on Poly
4. Ceramic on Poly

## Correct option

**1. Metal on metal THR**

## Answer explanation

Metal-on-metal pseudotumor or aseptic lymphocyte-dominated vasculitis-associated lesions (ALVAL) are large focal solid or semi liquid masses around metal-on-metal THR bearing prostheses. These are sterile inflammatory lesions. ALVAL is a hypersensitivity reaction leading to formation of a pseudotumor around the prosthesis that eventually results in subsequent failures. The mechanism involved is thought to be a type IV delayed hypersensitivity reaction. In ALVAL, it is hypothesized that metal ions are slowly released from the prosthetic bearing surfaces as a by-product of normal wear.

MRI may show Periprosthetic cystic and solid masses. The clinical diagnosis can reliably be made only after histological examination. Metal ions, Chromium and cobalt in particular have been demonstrated in elevated quantities in the blood and urine of patients with loose prostheses.

Answer: 1
