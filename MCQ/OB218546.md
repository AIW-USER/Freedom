## Question

A 4-year-old male presents to the clinic with multiple orthopedic manifestations. Examination reveals blue sclera and genetic analysis reveals COL1A1 and COL1A2 gene defects. Which of the following abnormalities might be associated with these findings?

## Options

1. Activating mutation of a receptor gene
2. Glycine substitution in a precursor protein
3. Abnormal intracellular sulfate concentrations
4. Basement membrane abnormalities
5. Defective bone remodeling

## Correct option

**2. Glycine substitution in a precursor protein**

## Answer explanation

Osteogenesis imperfecta (OI) is associated with type 1 collagen defects from a glycine amino acid substitution in the procollagen protein.

Osteogenesis imperfecta is the abnormal type 1 collagen production in the body leading to a lack of tensile strength of bone. This clinically manifests as recurrent fractures and osteoporosis. In severe cases, this can lead to neonatal death. Substitution of a single amino acid for glycine in the procollagen protein leads to abnormal collagen crosslinking of the final protein.

*Gwynne-Jones retrospectively reviewed eight olecranon fractures in three patients with OI.* **They reported** all fractures were treated with tension band wiring with fractures of the contralateral elbow occurring within 12 months in all patients. **They concluded** there is a high risk of refracture following hardware removal and contralateral fractures in this population.

*Peddada et al. retrospectively reviewed 29,101 pediatric fractures to determine fracture patterns between OI-associated and routine fractures.* **They reported** OI-associated fractures typically presented at younger ages and were often transverse, diaphyseal, and involved bilateral long bones. **They concluded** transverse and diaphyseal humerus fractures and olecranon fractures were most likely to indicate OI-associated fractures.

*Burnei et al. reviewed OI diagnosis and treatment.* **They stated** medical treatment consists of bisphosphonate use even in patients younger than 2 years and surgical treatment involving internal splinting of long bones.

Incorrect answers
Answer 1: Achondroplasia is associated with an activating mutation of the fibroblast growth factor receptor gene.
Answer 3: Diastrophic dysplasia is the result of a defective sulfate transporter gene.
Answer 4: Ehlers-Danlos syndrome is due to defective type V collagen leading to basement membrane defects.
Answer 5: Osteopetrosis is due to a defective carbonic anhydrase gene leading to defective bone remodeling and brittle bone formation.
