## Question

A patient has been prescribed “Short Foot Exercises” by the treating physician. The most relevant indication from the options would be

## Options

1. Plantar fasciitis
2. Tennis elbow
3. After triple arthrodesis done for a case of CTEV
4. For Flat foot

## Correct option

**4. For Flat foot**

## Answer explanation

Ans: D
EXPLANATIONS: -
SHORT FOOT EXERCISES are employed for improving the foot alignment and medial longitudinal arch of the foot in patients with flat foot (plano valgus). They are known to strengthen the foot intrinsics, decrease navicular drop in flat feet individuals and improve postural stability.
Exercise basically requires the patient to use the muscles of the medial arch to pull the big toe towards the heel (see pic). Starting initially in sitting position, can be done in squatting or single leg stance positions later.
