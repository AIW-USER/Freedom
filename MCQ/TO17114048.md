Chapter: Arthroscopy MCQs — Elbow Arthroscopy

## Question

which is most commonly used working portal in elbow

## Options

1. Anterolateral
2. Proximal anterolateral
3. Anteromedial
4. Proximal anteromedial

## Correct option

**4. Proximal anteromedial**

## Answer explanation

Portals of elbow arthroscopy:

Anterolateral portal- viewing portal

The proximal anteromedial portal (or supracondylar anteromedial portal): most common working portal

Anterolateral portals: First portal to be made. Many anterolateral portals have been described:

- Anterolateral or distal anterolateral portal: It is located 3 cm distal and 1 cm anterior to the LE just anterior to the radial neck and head. Many surgeons don’t favor this portal due to its close proximity to the radial nerve.
- The mid anterolateral portal: It is Located directly anterior to the radio capitellar joint, and made just proximal and approximately 1 cm anterior to the palpable radio capitellar joint.
- The proximal anterolateral portal is 2 cm proximal and 1 cm anterior to the lateral epicondyle. It is the safest anterolateral portal.

Anteromedial portals:

- Anteromedial portal: 2 cm anterior and 2 cm distal to the ME. The anteromedial portal is placed through the flexor pronator musculature and the median and medial antebrachial cutaneous nerves (MABCN) are at risk.
- The proximal anteromedial portal (or supracondylar anteromedial portal): This portal is located 2 cm proximal and anterior to the medial epicondyle. The PAMP is the most commonly used medial portal.

Posterior portals:

- The posterolateral portal is located 2 to 3 cm proximal to the olecranon tip and just lateral to the border of the triceps tendon along the lateral supracondylar ridge.
- Direct Posterior Portal: The direct posterior portal or posterior central or straight posterior or “trans triceps” portal. It is located 3 cm proximal to the tip of the olecranon and pierces through the tendinous portion of the triceps.

Answer: 4
