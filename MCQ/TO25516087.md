## Question

A 4-year-old child presents with bowing of the lower limbs, marked hypophosphatemia, and hyperphosphaturia. Biochemical evaluation reveals low intact FGF23 levels, elevated serum 1,25(OH)2D, and an elevated 24-hour urinary calcium excretion. Which of the following genes is most likely mutated in this patient?

## Options

1. CYP27B1
2. PHEX
3. SLC34A3
4. FGF23

## Correct option

**3. SLC34A3**

## Answer explanation

SLC34A3 mutations cause Hereditary Hypophosphatemic Rickets with Hypercalciuria (HHRH) by directly impairing the NaPi-IIc cotransporter. This is FGF23-independent; the body compensates for phosphate loss by upregulating 1,25(OH)2D, which in turn causes intestinal hyperabsorption of calcium and subsequent hypercalciuria.
