## Question

A 35-year-old male undergoes intramedullary fixation for a left femoral shaft fracture, with postoperative films shown in Figures A and B. He is subsequently lost to follow-up and returns to your clinic eight months later. Which of the following best describes the process seen in Figures C and D?

*Figure A*

![[q220919-b-screenshot-2025-11-10-at-1-44-26-pm.jpg]]
*Figure B*

![[q220919-c-screenshot-2025-11-10-at-1-41-21-pm.jpg]]
*Figure C*

![[q220919-d-screenshot-2025-11-10-at-1-42-01-pm.jpg]]
*Figure D*

## Options

1. Catastrophic failure
2. Cyclic fatigue
3. Mismatch of modulus of elasticity
4. Pitting corrosion
5. Static Failure

## Correct option

**2. Cyclic fatigue**

## Answer explanation

Figures C and D demonstrate a bend in the intramedullary rod, which is due to cyclic fatigue (Answer choice 2).

Cyclic fatigue is the process by which fatigue failure occurs when a material weakens under repeated loading (stress cycles). When performing biomechanical testing of fracture constructs, repeated stress loading is the testing method that most closely replicates the post-fixation environment. Static, or catastrophic failure, in contrast, occurs due to a single supraphysiological load. This is not a typical mode of failure after fracture fixation.

*Gardner et al. published a review article on biomechanical testing of fracture fixation constructs.* **They discuss** that cyclic fatigue is the most representative biomechanical testing model of the environment after fracture fixation.

*Frankel et al. conducted a review of experiments performed in the 1960s to demonstrate the viscoelastic and fracture mechanics of bone.* **The authors conclude** that understanding of the methods for biomechanical testing of bone is paramount to the interpretation of fracture mechanics and energy in injury.

Figures A and B demonstrate intraoperative fluoroscopic films of a femoral shaft fracture with intramedullary rod fixation. Figures C and D demonstrate a nonunited fracture with a bend of the intramedullary rod that is not seen in the fluoroscopic images.

Incorrect answer choices:
Answer choices 1 and 5: Static loading leads to catastrophic failure. This is typically not seen as a mode of failure after fracture fixation.
Answer choice 3: Mismatch of modulus of elasticity refers to differences in Young’s modulus between the implant and bone. This would present as stress shielding.
Answer choice 4: The radiographs are not consistent with findings from pitting corrosion.
