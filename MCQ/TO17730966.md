## Question

Which is not a poor prognostic factor in synovial sarcoma? (orthopedic oncology- malignant tumors- synovial sarcoma)

## Options

1. Old age
2. Large tumor size
3. Biphasic histology
4. Axial tumor

## Correct option

**3. Biphasic histology**

## Answer explanation

Synovial sarcomas (SS) are rare malignant mesenchymal soft tissue tumors (STS), accounting for approximately 6-8% of all malignant STS. They are considered high grade with poor prognosis, due to a high degree of local invasiveness and propensity to metastasise. It commonly occurs in adolescents and young adults, mostly within the age range of 15-40 years. Contemporary 5-year overall survival (OS) rates for SS have been reported as ranging from 61% to 73%. The translocation of (X;18) (p11;q11), results in either of three possible gene fusions, SYT-SSX1, SYTSSX2 or rarely SYT-SSX4 and is highly specific for SS, present in 95% cases.

Prognostic factors in synovial sarcoma:

- Age: Younger patients having improved survival compared to those of older age.
- Tumor size: Generally, it appears that larger tumor size, especially ≥5cm predicts poor outcome, whilst size<5 cm is linked to more favorable outcome.
- Tumor depth: Superficial SS conferring better prognosis than deep tumors
- Tumor site: Axial sites confer the worst prognosis, and distal extremity sites confer more favorable outcomes.
- Tumor stage: At the time of diagnosis higher stage is linked to worse prognosis.
- Histological subtype: Monophasic histology confers adverse survival outcome compared to biphasic histology.
- Higher grade tumor(Fedération Nationale des Centres de Lutte le Cancer (FNCLCC) criteria)

as either Grade II or Grade III have inferior outcomes.

- Nuclear expression of CXCR4 and IGF-1R are independent adverse prognostic factors for

patient survival.

- Surgical margins: Inadequate margins confer higher risk of local recurrence and metastases compared to clear wide margins.
- Increasing mitotic activity has been implicated as conferring worse outcome in a number of studies

Answer: 3
