## Question

Progression of which of the following disease processes is associated with an ipsilateral increased adductor moment during gait?

## Options

1. Cervical myelopathy
2. Medial compartment knee arthritis
3. Hip arthritis
4. Arthritis of the anterior tibiotalar joint
5. Levoscoliosis >30 degrees

## Correct option

**2. Medial compartment knee arthritis**

## Answer explanation

Knee adductor moment is a surrogate measure for medial tibiofemoral contact force and is associated with a progression of osteoarthritis in a varus knee.

External knee adductor moment reflects the medial-to-lateral knee joint load distribution during gait. In varus knees, the mechanical axis of the limb passes medial to the knee center, thus the force vector creates an adductor moment, resulting in increased load on the medial compartment. This increase load seen by the medial compartment is thought to lead to the progression of knee osteoarthritis in varus knees.

*Chehab et al. review the baseline adduction and flexion moments during walking.* **They report** that changes in the femoral medial-to-lateral cartilage thickness ratio were most closely related to the peak knee adduction moment. **The authors conclude** that knee adductor moment should be considered when assessing the risk progression in knee osteoarthritis.

*Cole et al. review degenerative arthritis of the knee in active patients.* **The authors report** that deformities of greater than 10 degrees are associated with a varus thrust and lateral laxity. **They conclude** that the evaluation of a patient for arthritis must include both standing weightbearing radiographs as well as dynamic evaluation of the gait cycle.

Incorrect Answers:
Answer 1: Patients with cervical myelopathy have difficulty with heel-toe walking, they do not develop an increased adductor moment.
Answer 3: Patients with hip OA commonly walk slowly and lean forward, dropping the pelvis on the affected side.
Answer 4: Patients with tibiotalar arthritis commonly have longer double support times, but do not have an increased adductor moment.
Answer 5: The timing of individual gait phases is not altered by levoscoliosis.
