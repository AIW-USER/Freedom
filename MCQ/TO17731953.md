## Question

Picture frame vertebrae are seen in ( metabolic bone disorders- paget's disease)

## Options

1. Vitamin D resistant rickets
2. Fluorosis
3. Multiple epiphyseal dysplasia
4. Pagets’s disease

## Correct option

**4. Pagets’s disease**

## Answer explanation

Radiographic features of Pagets’s disease

Radiographs initially show flame-shaped osteolysis and later on bone becomes deformed and expanded with a thickened and sclerotic cortex. Some characteristic X-ray signs of this disease include:

• Ivory vertebrae (diffusely sclerotic vertebrae)

• Picture frame vertebrae

• Osteoporosis circumscripta/osteoporotic patch/cotton wool spots seen in the skull

• Brim sign: Sclerotic iliopectineal line

• Blade of grass appearance: It is “V”-shaped radiolucency seen in the diaphysis of long bones

• Biopsy of bone characteristically shows the mosaic pattern, but is rarely indicated.

• A banana fracture or incremental fracture refers to a complete, horizontally oriented pathological fracture seen in deformed bones affected by Paget’s disease
