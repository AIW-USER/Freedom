## Question

An orthopedic resident is conducting a study with his attending on surgical site infections of the spine. While the resident is submitting the manuscript for publication he is prompted to submit a conflict of interest statement for each of the authors. What is the purpose of this statement?

## Options

1. To state which orthopedic surgeons the authors may have differing opinions with
2. To disclose all financial interests the authors may have
3. To discuss literature that reports contentious findings to the current study
4. Disclosure of what each author contributed to the manuscript or study
5. Reveal potential bias present in the study design

## Correct option

**2. To disclose all financial interests the authors may have**

## Answer explanation

A conflict of interest statement in orthopedic literature is to disclose all financial interests the authors may have surrounding the research and/or publication.

Orthopedic research provides useful evidence to guide treatment decisions and improve the quality of care. In order to fully appreciate the findings reported, a systematic and evidence-based approach is necessary. The conflict of interest is a critical aspect to consider when interpreting the results reported in the publication. This statement discloses the authors' financial interests.

*Spindler et al. reviewed how to interpret the orthopedic literature in a systematic and evidence-based approach.* **They stated** that approaching the literature in a systematic way allows the reader to find true merit to study results as it pertains to patient care. **They concluded** that evidence-based information will aid in decision-making related to patient care.

Poolman, et al. retrospective reviewed 32 randomized, controlled trials to assess the effect of blinded outcomes assessors. They reported that with studies with dichotomous outcomes reported, the unblinded assessments were associated with a significantly greater treatment effect than the blinded assessments (p < 0.001). The authors emphasized blinding the outcome assessors in future orthopedic trials to limit bias.

*Fischgrund presented a consensus statement of 20 journal editors on conflict of interest statements in the orthopedic literature.* **The author stated** the American Academy of Orthopedic Surgeons (AAOS) intends to adopt the International Committee of Medical Journal Editors (ICMJE) form for conflict of interest statements. **The author concluded** this should clarify the reporting responsibilities, facilitate the reporting process, and lead to a clearer understanding of potential conflicts of interest.

Incorrect answers
Answers 1, 3, 4, and 5: The conflict of interest statement is submitted at the time of publication submission to disclose all financial interests of the authors as it relates to the publication and research.
