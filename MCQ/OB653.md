## Question

Risk factors for a motor nerve palsy following primary total hip arthroplasty include all of the following EXCEPT?

## Options

1. Developmental dysplasia of the hip
2. Limb lengthening
3. Posttraumatic arthritis
4. Obesity
5. Posterior approach

## Correct option

**4. Obesity**

## Answer explanation

Certain pre-operative risk factors may place patients at increased risk for the development of nerve injury during primary total hip arthroplasty (THA). These include a history of developmental dysplasia of the hip (DDH), limb lengthening, posttraumatic arthritis, cementless femoral fixation, and the posterior approach.

*Farrell et al. reviewed more than 27,000 patients who underwent THA between 1970 and 2000.* Forty-seven patients developed a nerve palsy (0.17%) with 29 complete palsies and 18 incomplete palsies. DDH, post-traumatic arthritis, posterior approach, use of a cementless femoral implant, and limb-lengthening were all found to have an increased risk of nerve palsy. Body-mass index (BMI) was not associated with increased risk of nerve palsy.

Of note, the average time to maximal recovery for both incomplete and complete nerve palsies was approximately 2 years. Patient should be counseled regarding this complication especially if one of the above risk factors is present.
