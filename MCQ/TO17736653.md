## Question

After a Posterior Lumbar Fusion surgery, which motion is still most preserved? (spine- lumbar disc herniation)

## Options

1. Flexion
2. Extension
3. Axial rotation
4. Lateral bending

## Correct option

**3. Axial rotation**

## Answer explanation

Axial rotation is still most preserved as rotation is mostly at thoracic vertebrae and not at lumbar levels.

Answer: 3
