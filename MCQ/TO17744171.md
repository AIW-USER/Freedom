## Question

In shoulder hemiarthroplasty recommended stem position in canal is [Arthroplasty-Shoulder Arthroplasty-Shoulder Hemi Arthroplasty]

## Options

1. 30 degree retroversion
2. Neutral position
3. 10 degree anteversion
4. Neutral to 10 degree retroversion

## Correct option

**1. 30 degree retroversion**

## Answer explanation

In shoulder hemiarthroplasty proper position of the broaches is 30 degrees of retroversion during canal reaming. Stem is also tapped in 30 degrees of retroversion to prevent component malposition.

Answer: 1
