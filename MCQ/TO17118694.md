## Question

Treatment of juvenile osteochondritis dissecans of femoral condyle is (FNB Pattern Paper 2016)

## Options

1. In Situ fixation
2. Microfracture
3. Activity modification
4. Arthroscopic excision

## Correct option

**3. Activity modification**

## Answer explanation

Majority of JOCD lesions are found in the “classic” area of the posterolateral aspect of the medial femoral condyle. Prognosis of these lesions is good and most of the cases heal with rest and activity modification.

Answer: 3
