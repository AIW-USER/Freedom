@@AFTER **Transfer counselling** — pain relief more reliable than function (Omid & Lee)
**TO age rule** — **<60 y → tendon transfer / SCR** · **>70 y → RSA** · posterosuperior → **LD**, anterosuperior → **pec major**

**Gerber transfer principles (TO)** — donor **expendable** · similar **excursion + tension** · matching **line of pull** · replaces at least one lost function

| Transfer detail (TO) | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Pec major** | Deltopectoral; **sternal head** under conjoined → **lesser tuberosity** · pectoral nerves **~12 cm** from insertion · thoracoacromial (pectoral branch) · line of pull differs → **anterior humeral translation** risk |
| **Latissimus dorsi** | **Gerber 1988** · origin T7–L5, iliac crest, ribs 10–12 → intertubercular groove · passed between deltoid + cuff → GT · **thoracodorsal nerve (C6–C8)** · **subscapularis intact = prerequisite** · contraindicated: SSc tear, pseudoparalysis, deltoid dysfunction, stiffness |
| **Lower trapezius (Elhassan)** | Origin **C7–T12** → medial scapular spine · line of pull **matches infraspinatus**, synergistic → **now preferred** · **spinal accessory nerve 2 cm medial** to scapular border |

**SCR (TO)** — **Mihata 2013**, **TFL autograft = gold standard** (donor morbidity) · dermal allograft costly · graft superior glenoid → GT · tension to restore **AHI ~8 mm**

**LHB-based options (TO)** — **biceps augmentation / anterior cable reconstruction** (LHB tenotomised distally, transposed to SS footprint, glenoid attachment kept) · **biceps rerouting (Kim)**: LHB intact proximally, moved to posterior groove at footprint, cinch anchors → **all-inside, no donor morbidity**, internal splint + head depressor

**Subacromial spacer balloon (InSpace) (TO)** — biodegradable saline balloon, **resorbs ~1 y** · **low-demand, pain-dominant, motion preserved** · **not for pseudoparalysis or GH arthritis** · overinflation → deltoid tension · underinflation → **escape**

| Rehab after transfer / SCR (TO) | Protocol |
|---|---|
| **0–4 wk** | Immobilisation (USI brace) |
| 3 days–6 wk | Passive ROM (30° flexion, 80° IR, ER to neutral) |
| 6 wk–3 mo | Active ROM all planes |
| >3 mo | Strengthening |
@@END
