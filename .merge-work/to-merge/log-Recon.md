### Revision Notes/Knee & Sports/Femoroacetabular Impingement (FAI).md — under "Key radiology findings"
**First radiological sign (TO)** — cam → **anterolateral migration of femoral head** · pincer → **posteroinferior joint-space narrowing** (counter-coup)

**Coxa profunda (TO)** — acetabular floor **touches Kohler's (ilioischial) line** · protrusio → head **medial** to Kohler's line

**Posterior wall sign (TO)** — **head centre lateral to posterior wall** → acetabular retroversion

**AP pelvis for FAI (TO)** — hip **internally rotated 15°**

**Alpha angle — where to measure (TO, radiology)** — **Dunn / modified Dunn 45°, false profile or MRI oblique axials along the neck** · **not on AP or orthogonal MRI axials** · abnormal **> 55°** (some use **60° or 65°**, activity-dependent) · **MRI coronals often miss FAI**

**False profile pelvis rotation** — **65°** (current chapter) · **about 60°** (TO radiology) — read the options

**GRE axial pincer clue (TO)** — posterior acetabular wall should lie **lateral** to a line from the anterior wall · **in line → think pincer**

**FAI + sports groin (TO)** — young athletes with FAI often also show pubic symphysis irregularity, sclerosis, instability

### Revision Notes/Knee & Sports/Hip Arthroscopy.md — under "Portals & nerve injury (LFCN, sciatic)"

### Revision Notes/Pediatrics/Slipped Capital Femoral Epiphysis (SCFE).md — under "Key radiology findings"
**Age pattern (TO, radiology)** — hip pain without sport at **11–12 yr → SCFE** · **7–8 yr → Perthes** · frog-leg shows irregular physis · MRI: **physeal widening + fluid**, effusion, head slipped backwards

### Revision Notes/Pathology/Osteoid Osteoma.md — under "Key radiology findings"
**Femoral neck OO in young athlete (TO)** — marrow oedema + effusion but **no fracture line**, thickened neck cortex → **CT for the nidus** · **intracapsular** location explains the large effusion · can occur in the 20s

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Acetabular Fractures.md — under "Key radiology findings"
**Column fracture (TO)** — **always crosses the quadrilateral plate** and usually the inferior pubic ramus · anterior vs posterior direction decides the column (Judet views)

### Revision Notes/Pediatrics/Pelvis Fractures - Pediatric.md — under "Apophyseal avulsion fractures"
**Iliac crest apophyseal avulsion (TO)** — adolescent (e.g. 16-yr-old cricketer), sudden crest pain · unfused apophysis pulled off by **core muscles** = **type I physeal injury** · often missed

### Revision Notes/Basic Science/Thromboembolism (PE & DVT) Prophylaxis.md — under "Prophylaxis guidelines (AAOS, ACCP, mechanical, combined) (TO)"
**THR prophylaxis (TO, arthroplasty lecture)** — start immediately post-op, continue **4–6 weeks** · LMWH (enoxaparin) / fondaparinux **3 weeks** or **aspirin 75 mg daily × 6 weeks (NICE)** · **stop LMWH before removing epidural catheter / drain** (spinal haematoma) · untreated THR: **~70% DVT, ~2% fatal PE** (TO)
