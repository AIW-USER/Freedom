### On-field emergency – BLS, ACLS & collapsed athlete (TO)

**Event sequence (TO)** — preparation (drills, AED location, ambulance route) → triage → **primary survey ABCDE** → resuscitate → **AMPLE** history → secondary survey → definitive care

| Triage colour (TO) | Category |
|---|---|
| **Red** | **P1** — immediate, life-threatening but treatable |
| Yellow | P2 — urgent, can wait briefly |
| Green | P3 — minor |
| Blue | Expectant — poor prognosis |
| Black | Dead |

| High-quality CPR (TO) | Value |
|---|---|
| **Rate** | **100–120 / min** |
| **Depth (adult)** | **5–6 cm** · child **2 inches** (one hand) · infant **1.5 inches** (two-thumb) |
| Ratio | **30 : 2** · child with 2 rescuers **15 : 2** |
| Interruptions | **< 10 s** · full recoil · avoid over-ventilation |
| Rescue breaths | 1 every **5–6 s** adult · 3–5 s child |
| Pulse check | Carotid **≤ 10 s** |

**AED (TO)** — pads right upper chest + left lateral chest · shock → **resume CPR 2 min** · survival **↓ 10% per minute** of delay · paediatric pads 1–8 yr (or adult pads antero-posterior) · **biphasic 120–200 J** / monophasic 360 J

**Airway (TO)** — head tilt–chin lift · **jaw thrust if C-spine injury** · OPA sized corner of mouth → earlobe · NPA tip of nose → earlobe · LMA supraglottic · **cricothyroidotomy = last resort (CICO), avoid in children < 5–12 yr** · cuff pressure < 20 cm H₂O · **waveform capnography = gold standard tube confirmation** (ETCO₂ 0 = misplaced; ≥ 20 during CPR; **< 10 after 20 min → poor CPR**; abrupt ↑ to **≥ 40 = ROSC**)

**ET-tube drugs (TO)** — **NAVEL**-type: naloxone, atropine, epinephrine (vasopressin), lidocaine at **2–2.5× IV dose** · **not amiodarone**

| ACLS drug (TO) | Dose |
|---|---|
| **Epinephrine** (arrest) | **1 mg IV/IO every 3–5 min** (1:10,000) |
| **Amiodarone** (VF / pulseless VT) | **300 mg**, then **150 mg** |
| Lidocaine (no amiodarone) | 1–1.5 mg/kg, then half |
| Adenosine (PSVT) | 6 mg → 12 mg |
| Atropine | 1 mg, max 3 mg |
| **Magnesium** | **1–2 g IV — torsades** |

**Rhythms (TO)** — shockable: **VF, pulseless VT** (150–250 bpm, QRS > 0.12 s) · non-shockable: **asystole, PEA** → CPR + epinephrine + reversible causes (**Hs & Ts**: hypoxia, hypovolaemia, H⁺, hypo / hyperkalaemia, hypothermia, hypoglycaemia · tension pneumothorax, tamponade, thrombosis PE / MI, toxins)

**Post-arrest (TO)** — 10 breaths/min, **SpO₂ 92–98%**, PaCO₂ 35–45 · SBP > 90, **MAP > 65** · **TTM 32–36 °C**

**Anaphylaxis (TO)** — **IM adrenaline 1:1000**: adult **0.5 mg** · 6–12 yr 0.3 mg · < 6 yr 0.15 mg · + O₂, hydrocortisone 200 mg, chlorpheniramine, salbutamol

**Choking (TO)** — encourage cough → **5 back blows + 5 abdominal thrusts** → unconscious = BLS

**Spinal / head injury on field (TO)** — assume C-spine injury · collar (chin → suprasternal notch) · neutral head · **log roll ≥ 4 rescuers** · imaging by **NEXUS ("NSAID")**: Neuro deficit, Spinal tenderness, Altered alertness, Intoxication, Distracting injury · GCS **13–15 mild, 9–12 moderate, ≤ 8 severe**
