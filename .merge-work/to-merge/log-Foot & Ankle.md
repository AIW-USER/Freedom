### Revision Notes/Trauma/Ankle Fractures.md — under "### Other classifications" (after the Danis-Weber table)
**Weber stability (TO)** — **A** = most stable → conservative · **B** = variable → ORIF often required · **C** = most unstable → ORIF + syndesmotic fixation (TO)

**Ring concept (TO)** — ankle = closed ring · **single break = stable** · **≥ 2 breaks = unstable → surgery** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Ankle Fractures.md — under "### Radiographic parameters" (after the "Weber B stable fracture on AP" line)
**Radiological signs of instability (TO)** — ↑ medial clear space · ↓ tibiofibular overlap · **break in mortise arc** · **proximal fibular migration** · talar tilt / subluxation (TO)

**TF overlap / clear space** — overlap **> 6 mm AP** (Orthobullets) · **> 10 mm AP** (TO) · clear space **< 6 mm** (Orthobullets) · **< 5 mm** (TO) — read the options

**CT (TO)** — **not routine**; comminuted, posterior malleolar, suspected syndesmotic, pre-op planning, **post-op reduction assessment** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Ankle Fractures.md — under "### Lateral malleolus fixation" (after the "Plate modes" line)
**Weber B reduction (TO)** — fibula displaced **posteriorly + externally rotated** → **traction + internal rotation**; contour plate with an **internal-rotation bend** (TO)

**Plate choice (TO)** — anatomical distal fibular or 1/3 tubular · **hook plate if distal fragment small** · unstable Weber A → plate or **tension band** (TO)

**Fixation sequence (TO)** — **fibula first** (length + rotation) → posterior malleolus → medial malleolus (antiglide / tension band) → **Cotton test** for syndesmosis (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Ankle Fractures.md — under "### Syndesmosis – reduction technique, fixation & outcomes" (after the "Fixation fact" table)
**Syndesmotic fixation indications (TO)** — PER injuries · suprasyndesmotic fractures · **MCS widening > 2 mm after fibular fixation** · screw **3.5 mm, 3–4 cm above talar dome, 3 cortices, routine removal at 3 months** · tightrope needs no removal (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Ankle Fractures.md — under "### Soft tissue – fracture-dislocation, external fixation, open fracture" (after the "Fracture blisters" line)
**Timing (TO)** — urgent: dislocations, open fractures · operate before swelling or after **wrinkle sign (\~10–14 days)** · delay if blisters on incision line · **deep dirty wounds > 12–24 h = contraindication** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Ankle Fractures.md — under "## 6 Complications" (after the complications table)
**Prolonged immobilisation (TO)** — capsular adhesions · cartilage degeneration · myostatic contracture · bone atrophy · **CRPS** → early mobilisation (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Calcaneus Fractures.md — under "## 3 Presentation"

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Calcaneus Fractures.md — under "### Key radiology findings" (after the Böhler / Gissane table)
**Böhler normal** — **20–40°** (Orthobullets) · **25–40°** (TO) — read the options

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Calcaneus Fractures.md — under "### Operative criteria" (after the table)
**Nonop indications (TO)** — extra-articular undisplaced · Sanders I · **severe PVD** · **uncontrolled diabetes** · elderly minimally ambulatory · **extensive open wounds** · **life-threatening polytrauma** (TO)

**Nonop protocol (TO)** — BK slab → cast / boot after swelling · **ROM at 4 wk** · **NWB 6–12 wk** (TO) · NWB cast 10–12 wk (Orthobullets) — read the options

**Operative share (TO)** — **70–80%** of calcaneal fractures need surgery (TO)

**Talar inclination** — "correct talar declination" = **not** an aim (TO question) · **restore talar inclination; horizontal talus post-op = inadequate reduction** (TO lecture) — read the options

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Calcaneus Fractures.md — under "### Approaches & techniques" (after the approaches table)
**Sinus tarsi timing (TO)** — can be done **day 1** · after **7–10 days** fragments become sticky → reduction difficult · best for **Sanders II + III** (TO)

**Medial approach (TO)** — **sustentacular involvement** · combined medial + lateral for complex fractures (TO)

**Reduction sequence (TO)** — posterior facet → medial sustentacular alignment → varus / valgus → height → tuberosity → CC joint (TO)

**Lateral wall osteotomy (TO)** — remove lateral wall → elevate depressed facet from **neutral triangle** → fix (TO)

**Extensile flap handling (TO)** — thick full-thickness flap · **no toothed forceps** · minimal retractors · **Allgöwer-Donati** closure (TO)

**Primary subtalar fusion (TO)** — Sanders IV not reconstructable · **chronic malunion with arthritis** · **poor bone stock** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Calcaneus Fractures.md — under "### Malunion sequelae & subtalar distraction bone-block arthrodesis"
**Malunion pain sources (TO)** — subfibular impingement · lateral wall blowout · loss of height → **horizontal talus** · heel varus · **facet incongruity = least common source of pain** (TO)

**Fast union (TO)** — calcaneus unites quickly → **after 3 wk treat as malunited** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Pediatrics/Tarsal Coalition.md — under "### Key radiology findings" (after the "MRI" line)
**C-sign (TO)** — continuous posterior talus + sustentaculum on lateral X-ray = **diagnostic of talocalcaneal coalition** · MRI: cysts, hypertrophy, stress oedema · **CT classification of TC coalition: types 1–5** (TO)

**Anterolateral (anterior) process fracture of calcaneum (TO)** — look for an **underlying calcaneonavicular coalition** · CN coalition may be **fibrous or osseous**; elongated anterior process ("anteater") or navicular, dorsal talar beak, pes planus (TO)

### Revision Notes/Pediatrics/Flexible Pes Planovalgus (Flexible Flatfoot).md — under "### Nonoperative management & observation"

**EOTTS / sinus tarsi stent (TO)** — extra-osseous talotarsal stabilisation · corrects **talar plantarflexion + inversion** · for **selected adolescent flexible flatfeet** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Pediatrics/Congenital Vertical Talus.md — under "## 3 Presentation"
**CVT features (TO)** — detected at birth · **convex (rocker-bottom) sole with deep dorsolateral crease** · rounded medial + plantar prominence (talar head) · forefoot dorsiflexed at midtarsal joint · with growth: **"hourglass" talus**, callosities under anterior calcaneus + medial border, severe forefoot abduction, **heel off ground in stance** · ↑↑ Meary angle (TO)

### Revision Notes/Pediatrics/Cavovarus Foot in Pediatrics & Adults.md — under "### Key radiology findings"
**Calcaneal pitch** — **> 30°** (Orthobullets) · **> 25–30°** (TO) — read the options

**Cavus X-ray signs (TO)** — talar **overcoverage by navicular** · Meary positive · **double-dome sign (sinus tarsi)** (TO)

**Half of cavus = CMT (TO)** — treat as neuromuscular until proven otherwise (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Tibial Plafond Fractures.md — under "### Fracture anatomy & patterns (Chaput fragment)" (after the "Posterior pilon" line)
**Fibula intact (TO)** — **medial (varus) impaction pattern** more likely (TO)

**Foot position (TO)** — **forced plantarflexion → posterior plafond** · **forced dorsiflexion → anterior plafond** injury (TO)

**Associated (TO)** — talar body / neck, tibial shaft, plateau, calcaneus fractures · rule out spine, head, abdominal injury · open injuries → **Ganga score** for salvage decision (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Tibial Plafond Fractures.md — under "### Key radiology findings" (after the table)
**CT impact (TO)** — **changes the surgical plan in > 60%** · obtain only after spanning + length restored ("**span, scan, plan**") (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Tibial Plafond Fractures.md — under "### Staged management — spanning ex-fix then delayed ORIF"
**Options (TO)** — **single-stage ORIF rare** (minimal swelling only) · **staged = most common** (stage 1 fibula fixation for length / ligamentotaxis + spanning ex-fix → stage 2 plafond) · **ex-fix + limited internal fixation** for poor soft tissue / open · **primary arthrodesis for non-reconstructable C3** · **slab** acceptable if fibula intact + alignment acceptable (TO)

**Post-op (TO)** — early ROM · **WB after 8–12 wk** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Tibial Plafond Fractures.md — under "### Surgical technique & approaches" (after "Alignment goals")
**Skin bridge** — **> 7 cm** (Orthobullets) · **minimum 5–7 cm** (TO) — read the options · **curvilinear incisions** across ankle, long incisions with thick flaps (TO)

**Fragment-specific fixation (TO)** — identify anterior, medial, posterior, Chaput fragments · **reduce posterior malleolus early → converts C into B pattern** · **low-profile buttress plates (1/3 tubular / recon)** for medial + anterior shear · **avoid bulky anatomical medial plates** · **elevate impacted fragments before compressing** · graft metaphyseal voids · check syndesmosis intra-op (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Tibial Plafond Fractures.md — under "## 6 Complications" (after the table)
**Malunion (TO)** — **medial collapse** if impaction not addressed · **missed syndesmotic injury** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Talar Neck Fractures.md — under "### Blood supply — artery of the tarsal canal & deltoid branch"
**Deltoid artery (TO)** — **most important**, supplies **\~70% of talar body** · displaced neck / body fractures disrupt it · **do not remove small medial neck fragments attached to deltoid** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Talar Neck Fractures.md — under "## 2 Mechanism" (or "## 1 Basics" if no stage 2)
**Aviator's fracture (TO)** — **forced dorsiflexion** → talar neck impacts anterior tibia · body fracture = **axial load** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Talar Neck Fractures.md — under "### Key radiology findings" (after the table)
**CT (TO)** — for **all suspected talar fractures** · detects **medial comminution** (present in nearly all neck fractures → varus malunion if compressed) · subtalar / tibiotalar involvement (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Talar Neck Fractures.md — under "### Treatment — ORIF, fixation construct & timing" (after the approaches table)
**Dual approach (TO)** — **standard of care**; avoids rotational errors · anteromedial: **tip of MM → 1st ray**, protect **saphenous vein** · anterolateral: **tip of fibula → 4th MT base**, split **EDB** (TO)

**Fixation by comminution (TO)** — non-comminuted: **2 compression screws ⟂ fracture** · comminuted (medial): **lateral screw compressive, medial screw positional** ± small medial buttress plate ± bone graft (TO)

**Medial malleolar osteotomy (TO)** — **only for body fractures, never for neck** · **short oblique**; **crack the posterior cortex** rather than cutting through (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Talar Neck Fractures.md — under "### Hawkins sign & osteonecrosis prognosis"
**Absent Hawkins sign (TO)** — no subchondral lucency **after 12 wk → MRI** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Talar Neck Fractures.md — under "## 6 Complications" (after the table)
**Arthritis rates (TO)** — **subtalar up to 81%** long-term · **tibiotalar \~18%** · infection up to **25% in open** fractures · **varus malunion = commonest complication** (TO), from medial comminution + overcompression → osteotomy, subtalar fusion if advanced (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Talar Neck Fractures.md — under "## How it's asked" (append)
**"Medial comminution in talar neck — medial screw mode?"** → **positional (non-compressive)** (TO)

**"Medial malleolar osteotomy is used for?"** → **talar body fractures only, never neck** (TO)

### Revision Notes/Trauma/Talus Fracture (other than neck).md — under "### Operative criteria" (after the table)
**Lateral + posterior process (TO)** — **large fragments → fix · small → excise** · posterior process: forced inversion in slight DF or PF, **commonly misdiagnosed as ankle sprain** · lateral process: sudden inversion or eversion (TO)
