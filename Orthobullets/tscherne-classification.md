# Tscherne Classification

Source: https://www.orthobullets.com/trauma/1002/tscherne-classification

Updated: May 27 2021


## Closed Fractures

- The Oestern and Tscherne classification for closed fractures

**Oestern and Tscherne classification of soft tissue injury in closed fractures**

|  |  |
|---|---|
| Grade 0 | Minimal soft tissue damage<br>Indirect injury to limb (torsion)<br>Simple fracture pattern |
| Grade 1 | Superficial abrasion or contusion<br>Mild fracture pattern |
| Grade 2 | Deep abrasion<br>Skin or muscle contusion<br>Severe fracture pattern<br>Direct trauma to limb |
| Grade 3 | Extensive skin contusion or crush injury<br>Severe damage to underlying muscle<br>Compartment syndrome<br>Subcutaneous avulsion |


## Open Fractures

- The Oestern and Tscherne classification for open fractures
  - uses wound size, level of contamination, and fracture pattern to grade open fractures

**The Oestern and Tscherne classification for open fractures**

|  |  |
|---|---|
| Grade I | Open fractures with a small puncture wound without skin contusion<br>Negligible bacterial contamination<br>Low-energy fracture pattern |
| Grade II | Open injuries with small skin and soft tissue contusions<br>Moderate contamination<br>Variable fracture patterns |
| Grade III | Open fractures with heavy contamination<br>Extensive soft tissue damage<br>Often, associated arterial or neural injuries |
| Grade IV | Open fractures with incomplete or complete amputations |
