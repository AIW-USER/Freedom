## Summary


**Tibialis posterior**

|  |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| **Origin** | Posterior aspect of interosseous membrane, superior 2/3 of medial posterior surface of fibula, superior aspect of posterior surface of tibia, and from intermuscular septum between muscles of posterior compartment and deep transverse septum | ![[ob10089-tibialis-posterior.jpg]]<br>![[ob10089-ll4-moved.jpg]] |
| **Insertion** | Splits into two slips after passing inferior to plantar calcaneonavicular ligament;<br>**Superficial slip:** inserts on the tuberosity of the navicular bone and sometimes medial cuneiform;<br>**Deeper slip:** divides again into slips inserting on plantar surfaces of metatarsals 2 - 4 and second cuneiform |  |
| **Action** | Principal invertor of foot; also adducts foot, plantar flexes ankle, and helps to supinate the foot |  |
| **Innervation** | **Tibial nerve (L4, L5)** |  |
| **Arterial supply** | Muscular branches of sural, peroneal and posterior tibial arteries |  |
