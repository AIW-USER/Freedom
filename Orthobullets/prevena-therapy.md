## Summary

- Solventum™ Prevena™ Therapy is a closed-incision negative pressure wound therapy (ciNPT) system designed to help protect surgical incisions, optimize the healing environment, and reduce the risk of certain postoperative complications in appropriate patients.
- It delivers continuous negative pressure over a closed incision to help hold incision edges together, remove fluid, reduce swelling, and create a barrier against external contamination.
- Prevena Therapy is commonly used for patients at higher risk of surgical site complications following procedures such as orthopedic, cardiovascular, general, plastic, and vascular surgeries.
