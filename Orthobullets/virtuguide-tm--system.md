## Summary

- The VIRTUGUIDE™ System includes a pre-operative AI powered planning software that suggests a patient-matched Virtual Alignment Correction (VAC) Guide used for Lapidus arthrodesis procedures.
- The VIRTUGUIDE™ pre-operative planning software utilizes AI to analyze the patient’s foot anatomy by identifying specific landmarks.
- The software generates a plan of the surgery and helps in the selection of a patient matched VAC Guide.
  - The VAC Guide incorporates the correction Intermetatarsal (IM) and Frontal Plane (FP) angles for the patient to allow wire placements in the specific deformity positions required for the correction.

## General Lapidus Indications

- 1st TMT arthrodesis (Lapidus)
  - indications
    - severe deformity (HVA > 40 degrees, IMA > 20 degrees)
    - arthritis at TMT joint or ligamentous laxity
      - note: VIRTUGUIDE System is for hallux valgus only

## Open Anatomy

- Osteology
  - first tarsometatarsal (TMT) joint
    - articulation between the medial cuneiform and base of the first metatarsal
    - primary stabilizer of the medial column and first ray
- Osseous
  - medial cuneiform
  - base of the first metatarsal
  - relationship to the second metatarsal contributes to midfoot stability
- Ligaments
  - dorsal and plantar TMT ligaments
  - plantar restraints stronger than dorsal restraints
- Nerves
  - deep peroneal nerve adjacent to dorsalis pedis artery
  - saphenous and superficial fibular nerves at risk during dorsal approach
- Blood Supply
  - dorsalis pedis artery traverses dorsal aspect of the first TMT joint
- Reference
  - Donantoni M, Santini S, Martinelli D, Marinozzi A. Surgical Techniques for Lapidus Arthrodesis: Approaches, Indications, and Outcomes. J Clin Med. 2025 Jun 28;14(13):4591

## Preoperative Planning

- Imaging parameters
  - the VIRTUGUIDE™ Software is configured to accept weight bearing CT scans and AP X-rays of the entire foot
  - weight bearing may influence the deformed anatomy and therefore provide a more accurate analysis of the deformity correction needed
- Imaging
  - x-ray
    - must be calibrated before being analyzed by the software
    - scaling factors must be defined using an option in the software
    - must be calibrated prior to software analysis using X-ray scaling factors to enable AI-automated measurements in mm
      - this will allow AI-automated analysis of the measurements in mm
    - if you do not have the magnification factor, you can set a default value
      - if you do so, the software will not display any measurements in mm and will show only the calculations for the various angles
  - CT
    - while Standard CT scans can be uploaded, they may result in a less desired deformity correction
    - when performing scans and gathering files, ensure the images meet noted image requirements in the adjacent table

## Surgical Approach

- Dorsomedial incision
  ![[ob423331-dorsomedial-incision.jpg]]
  - create a dorsomedial incision that is approximately 20 degrees from direct dorsal and medial to the extensor hallucis longus
  - the incision should be 3-4cm in length
  - retract the skin incision laterally
  - create a small direct dorsal stab incision into the TMT joint capsule to expose access to the joint
    ![[ob423331-stab-incision.jpg]]
    - the size of this incision should be just large enough to fit the paddle of the Joint Seeking Pin Guide
      ![[ob423331-place-joint-seeking-guide.jpg]]
  - do not expose and mobilize the entire joint capsule and do not place an osteotome into the joint
    - the joint space should still be tight

## Surgical Technique

- Placement of joint seeking pin and VAC Guide
  - place Joint Seeking Pin Guide into the TMT joint where the stab incision was made
  - the Joint Seeking Pin can be inserted either using the Wire Driver of the Power Tool or a Mallet
    ![[ob423331-verify-position-of-joint-seeking-pin.jpg]]
  - assemble distal Insert into the VAC Guide
    ![[ob423331-assemble-vac-guide.jpg]]
  - slide the VAC Guide over the Joint Seeking Pin through the central hole until the Guide is in apposition with the foot
    ![[ob423331-position-vac-guide.jpg]]
  - in the Cuneiform, place a long 2.0mm Wire first proximally (1), then a short 2.0mm Wire distally (2)
    ![[ob423331-insert-wires.jpg]]
  - in the Metatarsal, place a short 2.0mm Wire proximally (3), then a long 2.0mm Wire distally (4)
- Cut guide for bone resections
  - dissect the joint capsule to expose the joint
  - place the Cut Guide over the Metatarsal Wires through the holes marked “0”
    ![[ob423331-cut-guide-placement-on-metatarsal.jpg]]
  - apply the Cut Guide on the proximal Wires and resect the bone of the Cuneiform
    ![[ob423331-cut-metatarsal.jpg]]
- Realignment and compression with RAC Block
  - apply pressure to the medial aspect of the first Metatarsal squeezing towards the 2nd Metatarsal to allow ease of placement of the RAC Block
    ![[ob423331-slide-rac-block-over-wires.jpg]]
  - slide down RAC Block over all 4 Wires to compress joint space and generation correction and alignment
  - verify desired correction has been achieved by assessing Intermetatarsal and Frontal Plane angles under fluoroscopy
    ![[ob423331-apply-plantar-counter-pressure.jpg]]
  - verify if desired compression is achieved under AP and lateral fluoroscopy by verifying apposition of joint surfaces
    ![[ob423331-rac-block-compression.jpg]]
    - if additional compression is needed, remove 0 - RAC Block (Standard) and replace with a 1 - RAC or 2 - RAC Block for greater compression until apposition is achieved
      ![[ob423331-screenshot-2026-02-02-at-2-43-19-pm.jpg]]
- Fixation
  - the VIRTUGUIDE™ Instruments are compatible with a range of DePuy Synthes Lapidus fixation options
    ![[ob423331-screenshot-2026-04-23-at-4-36-21-pm.jpg]]

## Technical specifications

- VAC Guide
  - patient-matched to deformity and incorporates IM and FP angles for wire placement in correction-specific positions
  - 3D printed and supplied sterile packed
  - single patient use only and must never be reused
  - instrument kits are available as left- and right-foot options across multiple IM and frontal plane angle combinations
- Disposable and reusable instrumentation
  - disposable instruments include patient-matched VAC guide, 2.0 mm K-wires with trocar in 102 mm and 152 mm lengths, long sawblade, and Joint Seeking Pin
  - reusable instruments include the cut guide, Universal Handle, and RAC blocks, supplied via a modular tray system
- Realignment and Compression (RAC) Block options
  - 0, 1, and 2 levels of compression
  - 0, 5, and 10 degrees of additional frontal plane rotation, with left, right, and universal configurations depending on the block
