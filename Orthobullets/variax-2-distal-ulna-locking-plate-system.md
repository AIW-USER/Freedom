## Related Implants

- The VariAx® 2 series contain 3 plate types including
  - VariAx 2 Compression Plating System
  - VariAx 2 Distal Ulna Locking Plate System
  - VariAx 2 Distal Radius Locking Plate System

## Design

- VariAx 2 Distal Ulna Plating System provides you with a comprehensive range of anatomic plating options that work with a common instrument and screw platform
- Plates utilize SmartLock technology which permits polyaxial screw placement
- You can angle locking screws up to 15˚in each direction for a total range of 30˚ providing you with the flexibility to position screws based on the patient, not a plate’s pre-existing design
  ![[ob423223-screenshot-2025-03-27-at-10-24-33-am.jpg]]

## Indications

- The VariAx 2 Distal Ulnar System is indicated for the treatment of:
  - Fractures
  - Non-unions
  - Malunions
  - Deformities
- The DU Hook Plate may be used for DU fractures with a fractured or displaced ulnar styloid in order to apply compression on the fracture site
  ![[ob423223-screenshot-2025-03-28-at-4-32-34-pm.jpg]]
- The DU Base Plate may be used for DU neck fractures and neck fractures that are extending into the shaft of the ulna
  ![[ob423223-screenshot-2025-03-28-at-4-32-37-pm.jpg]]

## Approach

- DU Hook Plates
  - A longitudinal incision is made over the palpable ulna
  - Expose the distal ulna in the region between the tendons of the flexor carpi ulnaris and extensor carpi ulnaris toward the ulnar styloid
  - Fracture fragments can be visualizedand reduced. The fractured ulna styloid should be reduced and temporarily stabilized with a K-wire
- DU Base Plates
  - A longitudinal incision is made over the palpable ulna
  - Expose the distal ulna in the region between the tendons of the flexor carpi ulnaris and extensor carpi ulnaris toward the ulnar styloid
  - Fracture fragments can be visualized and reduced

## Placement

- DU Hook Plates
  - The plate is slipped over the K-wire and placed along the medial aspect of the ulna, ensuring the distal hook is secured around the ulnar styloid
  - Plate placement can be adjusted by sliding the plate along the K-wire through the K-wire slot
- DU Base Plates
  - Place the plate on the medial aspect of the ulna
  - Temporary plate fixation can be achieved by inserting a K-wire through the proximal K-wire hole or by placing an olive-stop K-wire through two of the screw holes

## Specific Plates

- Distal Ulna Hook Plate
  ![[ob423223-screenshot-2025-03-27-at-9-19-08-am.jpg]]
  - SOMA designed
  - 1.5mm thickness
  - Distal hook allows for fragment reduction by K-wire prior to plate fixation and plate can be slid over K-wire for final reduction
- Distal Ulna Base Plate
  ![[ob423223-screenshot-2025-03-27-at-9-19-17-am.jpg]]
  - SOMA designed
  - 1.7mm thickness
  - Distal screw cluster offers more fixation options in a narrow footprint

## Technical specifications

- Locking screws are laser marked with a ‘dot’ and ‘ring’ marking on the screw head to differentiate them from non-locking screws
- Locking and Non-Locking screws can be used in any round hole
- Allows for ±15 degrees of angulation
- The 2.0mm drill guide for T8 Screws facilitates drilling a 2.0mm pilot hole for a 2.4 or 2.7mm T8 screw or a 2.0mm peg centrically for locking or non-locking screws
